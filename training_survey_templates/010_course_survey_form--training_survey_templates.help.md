# Course Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about a course they have completed. The information collected will help us understand their experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your honest and constructive feedback about the course.
2. Answer all the questions in this form.
3. Submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Title** (`course_title`, text, optional): Please provide a brief title of the course you have completed.
* **Course Description** (`course_description`, text, optional): Describe your experience with the course, including any notable events or circumstances that impacted your engagement.
* **Course Rating** (`course_rating`, number, optional): Rate the course on a scale of 0-10, where 0 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **Course Rating 2** (`course_rating_2`, number, optional): This is a second rating for specific aspects of the course. Please rate the following: (insert options here, e.g., "content", "instruction", "organization")
* **What did you like?** (`what_did_you_like`, text, optional): Tell us what you enjoyed or appreciated about the course.
* **What did you dislike?** (`what_did_you_dislike`, text, optional): Share any frustrations or difficulties you experienced with the course.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions you have for improving the course or future versions.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide specific examples to help us better understand your feedback.
* If you have any comments or suggestions that are not captured in the fields above, please add them in the "Suggestions" field.
