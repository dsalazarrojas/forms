# Module Completion Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from learners after they have completed a module. The form is crucial in helping us improve the module and provide a better learning experience for future learners.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select "Yes" or "No" for "Have you completed the module?" and "Email sent" fields. If you have not completed the module, please provide a reason for not completing it in the "Comments" field.
2. Rate the module by selecting a number from 1 to 10 for "How would you rate this module?" and "How confident do you feel about the module content?" fields.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select the date of submission from the calendar.
6. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Email CC" field.
7. Enter your name in the "Submitted by" field.
8. Enter the module ID, module name, and module version in the respective fields.
9. Enter the timestamp of submission in the "Submission Timestamp" field.
10. Select the module status from the drop-down menu.
11. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.
12. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Email sent" field.
13. Enter the name of the person who sent the email in the "Email sent by" field.
14. Enter the date when the email was sent in the "Email sent on" field.
15. Select the CC list for the email in the "Email CC list" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Learner Feedback**: This field allows you to provide feedback on the module, such as its difficulty level and your level of confidence in the content.
	+ Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have completed the module or not.
* **How would you rate this module?**: This field allows you to rate the module on a scale of 1 to 10, with 1 being very poor and 10 being excellent.
* **Have you completed the module?**: This field allows you to indicate whether you have completed the module or not. If you have not completed the module, please provide a reason for not completing it in the "Comments" field.
* **How much time did you spend on this module?**: This field allows you to enter the amount of time you spent on the module.
* **How difficult was this module?**: This field allows you to rate the module on a scale of 1 to 10, with 1 being very easy and 10 being very difficult.
* **How confident do you feel about the module content?**: This field allows you to rate your confidence level on a scale of 1 to 10, with 1 being not confident at all and 10 being very confident.
* **Do you have any suggestions for this module?**: This field allows you to provide any suggestions you have for improving the module.
* **Do you have any additional comments?**: This field allows you to provide any additional comments or feedback on the module.
* **Email**: This field allows you to enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: This field allows you to enter your phone number.
* **Date of submission**: This field allows you to select the date when you submitted the form.
* **Time of submission**: This field allows you to select the time when you submitted the form.
* **Submitted by**: This field allows you to enter your name.
* **Module ID**: This field allows you to enter the ID of the module you completed.
* **Module Name**: This field allows you to enter the name of the module you completed.
* **Module Version**: This field allows you to enter the version of the module you completed.
* **Submission Timestamp**: This field allows you to enter the timestamp when you submitted the form.
* **Module Status**: This field allows you to select the status of the module.
* **Comments**: This field allows you to provide any additional comments or feedback on the module.
* **Email sent**: This field allows you to select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether an email was sent to you or not.
* **Email sent by**: This field allows you to enter the name of the person who sent the email to you.
* **Email sent on**: This field allows you to enter the date when the email was sent to you.
* **Email CC**: This field allows you to select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the email was CCed to anyone else or not.
* **Email CC List**: This field allows you to enter the list of people who were CCed on the email.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all the required fields.
* Please select the correct options from the drop-down menus.
* Please enter accurate and valid information in the text fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
