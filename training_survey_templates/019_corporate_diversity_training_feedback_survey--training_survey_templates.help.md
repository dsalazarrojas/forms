# Corporate Diversity Training Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to gather your thoughts and experiences from the diversity training workshop you just attended. It will help us improve our training sessions to better support and include everyone.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions on each page, following the instructions provided.
2. Use specific examples or details when answering the questions where possible.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields marked as "true" and "required".
4. If you're unsure about any of the questions, please feel free to ask your manager or the training facilitator for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was your overall experience in the workshop?** (`training_survey`, text): Describe your overall experience in the workshop. What did you find engaging, informative, or useful? What could be improved?
* **Which diversity topic was covered during the workshop?** (`diversity_topic_id`, select_one): Choose the diversity topic that was covered during the workshop. This will help us understand which topics need more emphasis or attention.
* **What did you learn from the workshop?** (`feedback`, text): Share what new insights, knowledge, or skills you gained from the workshop. Be as specific as possible, and include any action plans you plan to implement.
* **What action plan will you implement based on what you learned?** (`action_plan`, text): Describe a concrete action plan or plan of action you will take based on what you learned during the workshop. Be specific about what you will do, by when, and by whom.
* **Would you like to provide additional feedback to your manager?** (`feedback_to_manager`, select_multiple): If you'd like to share more thoughts or suggestions with your manager, choose "True". If you're not interested, choose "False".
* **Manager's name** (`manager_name`, text): If you answered "True" to the previous question, please enter your manager's name.
* **Manager's email** (`manager_email`, text): If you answered "True" to the previous question, please enter your manager's email.
* **Manager's phone** (`manager_phone`, text): If you answered "True" to the previous question, please enter your manager's phone number.
