# Employee English Language Training Needs Assessment Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess employees' English language training needs, aiming to identify areas for improvement in their English language skills. The goal is to collect data that will help in planning and implementing language training programs to enhance employee performance and engagement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the options that best describe your English language training needs:
   * On the "English Language Training Needs" page, select all the options that apply to your current level of English language proficiency.
   * On the "English Language Programs" page, write your desired English language programs or courses you would like to participate in.
   * On the "Training Goals Priority" page, select the goal(s) that you would like to achieve through language training.
   * On the "English Language Learning Goals" page, select all the skills you would like to improve in your English language skills.
   * On the "Review Submissions" page, provide any additional comments or feedback about your submissions.
2. Enter the date and time you completed the form:
   * On the "Date Submitted" page, enter the date you submitted this form.
   * On the "Time Submitted" page, enter the time you submitted this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **English Language Training Needs**: This field asks you to select all the options that best describe your current English language proficiency level. Please choose one or more options that apply to you.
* **English Language Programs**: Here, you can write the name(s) of English language programs or courses you would like to participate in. This will help us to understand your preferences for language training.
* **English Language Needs Assessment**: Select the option that best describes your current English language proficiency level. This will help us to understand your current level of English language skills.
* **Training Goals Priority**: This field asks you to select the goal(s) that you would like to achieve through language training. Choose one or more options that apply to you.
* **English Language Learning Goals**: This field asks you to select all the English language skills you would like to improve. Please choose one or more options that apply to you.
* **Review Submissions**: Here, you can provide any additional comments or feedback about your submissions. If you have any questions or concerns, you can share them with us in this field.
* **Date Submitted**: Enter the date you completed this form in the format (month/day/year).
* **Time Submitted**: Enter the time you completed this form in the format (hours:minutes AM/PM).

## Tips
* Please be honest in your answers, as this data will be used to plan language training programs that are beneficial for you and your team.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* If you have any other language training needs that are not listed on this form, please let us know in the "Review Submissions" field.
