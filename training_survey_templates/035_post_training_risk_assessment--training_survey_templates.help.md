# post_training_risk_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The post-training risk assessment form is used to evaluate the overall risk associated with a training program after it has been completed. It helps to identify areas of improvement and gather feedback for future training programs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Review the form carefully** before answering the questions. Ensure that you have a good understanding of the context and the training program.
2. **Read each question carefully** and answer them accurately and concisely.
3. **Provide as much detail as possible** in the text fields.
4. **Select the relevant options** in the select_one and select_multiple fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **page_1 (introduction)**: A short text field to provide a brief introduction to the training program.
* **page_2 (risk_areas)**: Select the relevant risk areas that are applicable to your training experience. Choose one option that best describes the risk area.
* **page_3 (risk_areas_2)**: Select multiple risk areas that are applicable to your training experience.
* **page_4 (risk_factors)**: Describe the risk factors that you have observed or experienced during the training program. This field is for general comments and observations.
* **page_5 (risk_factors_2)**: Describe another risk factor that you have observed or experienced during the training program.
* **page_6 (risk_factors_3)**: Describe another risk factor that you have observed or experienced during the training program.
...
* **page_24 (risk_factors_21)**: Describe another risk factor that you have observed or experienced during the training program.
* **page_25 (review)**: Provide your overall review and summary of the training program.
