# Retail Warehouse Training Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from warehouse trainers on their experience with the Retail Warehouse Training program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. **Enter the trainer's name** into the "Trainer" field.
2. **Select the name of the training program** you attended from the dropdown menu in the "Training Program" field.
3. **Rate your overall satisfaction** with the training program from the dropdown menu in the "Overall Satisfaction" field.
4. **Provide any comments or feedback** you have in the "Feedback" field (optional).
5. **Enter the date** of the training session using the calendar picker in the "Date" field.
6. **Enter the time** of the training session using the time picker in the "Time" field.
7. **Submit the form** to complete the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trainer** (`Trainer`, text, required): Enter the name of the trainer who provided the training.
* **Training Program** (`Training Program`, select_one, required): Select the name of the training program you attended from the dropdown menu. The options are:
	+ Retail Warehouse Training
	+ Safety Training
	+ Quality Control Training
	+ Compliance Training
	+ Other
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`Overall Satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the training program from the dropdown menu. The options are:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, note, optional): Provide any comments or feedback you have about the training program.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required): Enter the date of the training session using the calendar picker.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, required): Enter the time of the training session using the time picker.
