# Training Pre Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from trainees to assess their current knowledge and skills before participating in training programs. It helps identify areas of improvement and potential challenges trainees may face during training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your information, ensuring you provide accurate and honest responses.
2. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
3. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
4. Enter your Email in the Email field, using the correct format (e.g., [yourname]@domain.com).
5. Select your Department from the options provided in the Department field.
6. Choose your Level of Training from the options provided in the Level of Training field.
7. Select the Areas of Growth relevant to your training needs in the Areas of Growth field.
8. Enter your Date of Training in the Date field, using the correct format (e.g., 2022-01-01).
9. Enter your Time of Training in the Time field, using the correct format (e.g., 10:00).
10. Add any relevant Note about your training needs or challenges in the Note field.
11. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
12. If applicable, select additional Areas of Growth 2 relevant to your training needs in the Areas of Growth 2 field.
13. If applicable, select your Level of Training 2 from the options provided in the Level of Training 2 field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., `[yourname]@domain.com`).
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Select your department or team from the options provided.
* **Level of Training** (`level_of_training`, select_one, required): Choose your current level of training from the provided options (e.g., Training Level 1).
* **Areas of Growth** (`areas_of_growth`, select_multiple, required): Select the areas that you would like to improve during training (e.g., Area 1, Area 2, etc.).
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of your training (e.g., 2022-01-01).
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of your training (e.g., 10:00).
* **Note** (`note`, note, required): Add any relevant information about your training needs or challenges.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Areas of Growth 2** (`areas_of_growth_2`, select_multiple, optional): If applicable, select additional areas of growth relevant to your training needs (e.g., Area 1, Area 2, etc.).
* **Level of Training 2** (`level_of_training_2`, select_one, optional): If applicable, select your current level of training from the provided options (e.g., Training Level 1).
