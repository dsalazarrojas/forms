# Skincare Training Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skincare Training Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from trainees on the workshops they attend. This feedback will be used to improve future training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the title of the workshop you attended.
2. Indicate whether or not you attended the workshop.
3. Provide any feedback you have on the workshop.
4. Suggest any improvements to the training session.
5. Enter the date of the workshop you attended.
6. Enter the time of the workshop you attended.
7. Rate your overall experience with the workshop.
8. Enter your email address.
9. Enter your phone number.
10. Optionally, provide any additional comments for the trainer.
11. Optionally, provide any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Title** (`workshop_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the workshop you attended.
* **Have you attended this workshop** (`attended_workshop`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether or not you attended the workshop. Choose 'Yes' or 'No' from the dropdown options.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required): Provide any feedback you have on the workshop.
* **Suggest improvements** (`suggest_improvements`, `text`, required): Suggest any improvements to the training session.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the workshop you attended in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the workshop you attended in the format HH:MM.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required): Rate your overall experience with the workshop on a scale of 1-5.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Trainer Comments** (`trainer_comments`, `text`, optional): Optionally, provide any comments or feedback for the trainer.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Optionally, provide any additional comments.
