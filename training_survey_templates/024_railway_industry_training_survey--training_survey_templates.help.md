# Railway Industry Training Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from participants about their experiences and evaluations of the railway industry training programs. The data collected will help us understand your feedback and preferences, and inform future training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant information (Survey Participant Info)
2. Select the training programs you have attended or are interested in (Training Programs)
3. Describe your training experiences (Training Experiences)
4. Evaluate the training programs (Program Evaluations)
5. Rate your satisfaction with the programs (Participant Satisfaction)
6. Provide your date of completion (Date)
7. Provide your time of completion (Time)
8. Enter your email address (Email)
9. Enter your phone number (Phone)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Survey Participant Info** (`text`, required: false): This is the first section where you will enter your participant information. Please provide your name and any other relevant details.
- **Training Programs** (`select_one`, required: false): Select the training programs you have attended or are interested in. You can select only one option.
- **Training Experiences** (`text`, required: false): Describe your training experiences, including any relevant details or feedback.
- **Program Evaluations** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Evaluate the training programs by selecting multiple options.
- **Participant Satisfaction** (`select_one`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the programs. You can select only one option.
- **Date** (`date`, required: false): Provide the date of completion. This should be in a format that can be easily understood (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
- **Time** (`time`, required: false): Provide the time of completion. This should be in a format that can be easily understood (e.g., 12:30 PM).
- **Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address. This will be used to contact you for further communication.
- **Phone** (`text`, required: false): Enter your phone number. This will be used for further communication.

## Tips
- Please fill in the form accurately and honestly.
- You can save your progress and come back later to finish the form.
- If you have any issues or questions, contact the administrators of this survey.
