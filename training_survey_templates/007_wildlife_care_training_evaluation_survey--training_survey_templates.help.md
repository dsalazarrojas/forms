# wildlife_care_training_evaluation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with wildlife care training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field (please use the format `example@example.com`).
4. Rate your overall experience with the training by selecting a number from 1 to 5.
5. Provide any comments you may have in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, **required**): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, **required**): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **required**): Please enter your email address in the format `example@example.com`.
* **Overall Rating** (`rating`, `number`, **required**): Rate your overall experience with the training by selecting a number from 1 to 5.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, **optional**): Please provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the training.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Be as detailed as possible in the "Comments" field.
* If you have attended multiple training sessions, please only fill out this form once for each session.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please reach out to us at [support email].
