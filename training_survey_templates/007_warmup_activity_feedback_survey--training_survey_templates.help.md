# Warmup Activity Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants after a warm-up activity. It aims to gauge their subjective experience and opinions about the activity, helping to refine future sessions to better engage the audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions honestly, based on your personal experience.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the option that best describes your response.
3. For open-ended questions, provide specific and concrete answers that relate to your experience.
4. Be as detailed as possible, but avoid writing excessively long answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, text, required): This field is where you can briefly describe the session, mention any notable moments, or express your overall impression of the activity.
* **How did you feel about the session?** (`How did you feel about the session?`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the options that best describes your emotional response to the session. The options are:
	+ Very Positive
	+ Positive
	+ Negative
	+ Very Negative
	+ Not Applicable
* **Rate the activity** (`Rate the activity`, select_one, optional): Rate your experience with the activity on a scale from Very High to Very Low.
* **What did you find most engaging about the session?** (`What did you find most engaging about the session?`, text, required): Describe the activities or moments that you found most captivating or engaging. Be specific about what stood out to you.
* **Was the activity well-paced?** (`Was the activity well-paced?`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the options that best describes your experience with the pacing of the activity.
* **Were any of the following activities engaging?** (`Were any of the following activities engaging?`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the activities that you found engaging, from the options provided. 
* **Would you like to see more of this type of activity in the future?** (`Would you like to see more of this type of activity in the future?`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the options that best describes your preference for future sessions of this type.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Avoid vague or unclear answers.
* Be specific and concrete in your responses.
* If unsure, try to interpret the field based on your experience and memory.
* Do not assume or speculate about other participants' experiences.
* Complete the form in a quiet and private space where you can think clearly.
