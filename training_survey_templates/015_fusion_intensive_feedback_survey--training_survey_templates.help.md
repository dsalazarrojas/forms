# Fusion Intensive Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from participants after a training session, aiming to improve future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Rate your overall experience with the training session.
2. Provide a brief summary of the key takeaways you gained from the session.
3. Share your improvement suggestions for future sessions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Rating**: This field is a numeric scale from 1 to 5, where:
	+ 1: Not satisfactory
	+ 2: Average
	+ 3: Good
	+ 4: Satisfactory
	+ 5: Excellent
	Please rate your overall experience with the session.
* **Key Takeaways**: Provide a brief summary (about 100-150 words) of the main points you took away from the session.
	+ This field is a text area where you can type in your thoughts.
* **Improvement Suggestion**: Share your idea for improving future sessions.
	+ This field is also a text area for you to type in your thoughts.
## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your feedback.
* Only provide feedback on one session at a time.
* If you're unsure about any field, just type "Not Applicable" to move on.
