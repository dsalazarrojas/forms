# Principles Of Management Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from employees about their current management insights, training needs, and years of service. The purpose of this form is to gather data for the organization's training and development programs. It will help management identify areas where employees need additional support or training to improve their performance and job satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee Name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field. Please ensure that your email address is formatted correctly (e.g., "john.doe@example.com").
3. If applicable, select your Role from the available options in the "Role" field. If not applicable, please leave this field blank.
4. Enter your department in the "Department" field (optional).
5. Enter the number of years you have been with the company in the "Years Of Service" field.
6. Provide a brief description of your management insights in the "Management Insights" field. This is a free-text field for you to express your thoughts on your management style and experiences.
7. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.
8. Finally, enter your secondary email address in the "Email 2" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Role** (text, optional): If applicable, select your job title or role in the organization.
* **Department** (text, optional): Enter your department or team name (if applicable).
* **Years Of Service** (number, required): Enter the number of years you have been with the company.
* **Training Needs** (text, required): Describe the areas where you feel you need additional training or support.
* **Management Insights** (note, required): Share your thoughts on your management style and experiences.
* **Additional Comments** (note, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about your role or the organization.
* **Email 2** (email, optional): Enter your secondary email address (if you have one).
