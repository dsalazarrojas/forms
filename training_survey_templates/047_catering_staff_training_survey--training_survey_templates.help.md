4. Choose the type of training you attended.
5. Rate the trainer's effectiveness and the overall content quality.
6. Rate the pace of the training and the difficulty level (if applicable).
7. Select the amount of knowledge you gained from the training.
8. Choose how confident you feel about your new skills.
9. Indicate how often you will use what you learned in your job.

11. Indicate whether you would recommend the training to colleagues.
12. Select the topics covered well and mention any missing topics.

14. Indicate if you need additional training in any area.
15. Rate your overall satisfaction with the training.
16. Enter any additional comments or feedback you have.
