# Solo Performer Training Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your training and background as a solo performer. It will help us better understand your needs and preferences and provide more effective support and training programs for you.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Select your dance discipline**: Choose the type of dance that you specialize in from the dropdown menu.
2. **Enter your years of experience**: Provide the number of years you have been training in your chosen discipline.
3. **Choose your training frequency**: Indicate how often you train, whether it's rarely, occasionally, regularly, or daily.
4. **Select your training type**: Choose one or more ways you usually train, such as private lessons, group lessons, workshops, online courses, or residential programs.
5. **Specify your support needs**: Tell us what kind of support you need from your studio or dance organization.
6. **Describe your training habits**: Write a brief description of what you typically do during your training sessions.
7. **Enter your contact information**: Provide your email address, phone number, and website (if applicable).
8. **Add any additional comments**: If you have any further information or comments to share, use this field to add them.
