# Job Training Employment Impact Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Training Employment Impact Assessment form is designed to evaluate the effectiveness of job training programs on employment outcomes. It assesses the impact of training on employment, improvement areas, and scales, and gathers data on participant demographics and program details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Select the appropriate answers for each question**, indicating the impact of job training on employment outcomes and improvement areas. Make sure to answer every question, as they all contribute to the assessment's overall accuracy.
2. **Use the provided select options** for questions with multiple choices, such as "Yes" or "No".
3. **Enter numerical values** for questions with number type fields, such as "Job Training Employment Impact Scale" and "Job Training Outcome Scale 2".
4. **Submit the form** to generate the assessment report.

* **Job Training Outcomes** (`job_training_outcomes`): Indicate whether job training has led to employment outcomes, such as finding a job or getting promoted. Select all relevant options.
* **Job Training Employment** (`job_training_employment`): Choose whether job training has resulted in employment. This is a required field.
* **Job Training Employment Impact Scale** (`job_training_employment_impact_scale`): Rate the impact of job training on employment, using a scale of 1-10.
* **Job Training Employment Outcome Scale** (`job_training_employment_outcome_scale`): Rate the employment outcomes of job training, using a scale of 1-10.
* **Job Training Employment Outcome Scale 2** (`job_training_employment_outcome_scale_2`): Rate the employment outcomes of job training, using a scale of 1-10.
