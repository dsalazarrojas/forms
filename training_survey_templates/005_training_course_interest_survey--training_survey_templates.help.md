# Training Course Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant to be a quick and easy way for users to express their interests in training courses. Your input will help us create more relevant and engaging training content.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the topic that interests you the most from the available options.
2. Choose your preferred schedule for the training course.
3. Enter your contact email address so we can reach out to you with more information.
4. If you have a phone number, please enter it so we can follow up with a call.
5. Select the date and time when you are interested in taking the course.
6. Add any additional notes or comments about your training interests.
7. Once you've filled out all the required fields, click the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Interest** (`training_course_interest_survey`, `number`, not required): This field is where you can express your interests in specific training courses. Choose one of the available options.
* **Select Topics** (`topic_selection`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select the topics that interest you the most. You can choose one, multiple, or all of the available options.
* **Preferred Schedule** (`preferred_schedule`, `select_one`, not required): Choose the schedule that fits your needs best: Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
* **Contact Email** (`email_address`, `email`, not required): Enter your contact email address so we can follow up with you about your training interests.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, not required): If you have a phone number, please enter it so we can reach out to you for more information.
* **Date Of Interest** (`date_of_interest`, `date`, not required): Choose the date when you are interested in taking the training course.
* **Time Of Interest** (`time_of_interest`, `time`, not required): Select the time when you would like to take the training course.
* **Additional Notes** (`note`, `note`, not required): Add any additional comments or notes about your training interests.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, not required): Once you've filled out all the required fields, click the "Submit" button to complete the form.
