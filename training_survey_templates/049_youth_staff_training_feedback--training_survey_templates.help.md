# Youth Staff Training Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from youth staff on their training experience. This feedback will help us improve the quality of our training sessions and better meet the needs of our staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the session you are providing feedback on from the "Session Rating" field.
2. Rate the trainer's effectiveness in delivering the session using the "Trainer Effectiveness" field.
3. Provide suggestions for future training sessions using the "Training Suggestions" field.
4. Select areas of improvement for the session using the "Areas to Improve" field.
5. Add any additional comments about the session using the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Feedback** (session_feedback, text): Provide a brief description of your experience with the training session.
* **Training Suggestions** (training_suggestions, text): Suggest ways to improve future training sessions.
* **Trainer Effectiveness** (trainer_effectiveness, number): Rate the trainer's effectiveness on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not effective" and 5 is "very effective".
* **Session Rating** (session_rating, select_one): Select the session you are providing feedback on from the list.
* **Trainer Rating** (trainer_rating, number): Rate the trainer's performance on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not effective" and 5 is "very effective".
* **Areas to Improve** (areas_to_improve, select_multiple): Select all relevant areas for improvement.
* **Comments** (comments, text): Add any additional comments or suggestions for the trainer or future sessions.
* **Trainer Name** (trainer_name, text): If you are providing feedback for a specific trainer, please add their name here.
* **Session Date** (session_date, date): Select the date of the session you are providing feedback on.
* **Session Time** (session_time, time): Select the time of the day the session took place.
* **Additional Tips**:
	+ Please be specific and concise in your feedback.
	+ If you have any concerns or suggestions, please prioritize them by importance.
	+ If you have any additional comments or questions, please add them in the "Comments" field.
