# Wool Felting Training Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wool Felting Training Survey is designed to gather information about your training session. This form helps us understand the details of your course, such as the trainer's name, session date and start time, duration, felting skills level, and areas covered during the session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of your course in the **Course Details** field. This is a brief description of your training session.
2. Provide your name as the **Trainers Name** in the corresponding field.
3. Specify the date of your training session in the **Training Session Date** field. Use the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Select the start time of your session in the **Session Start Time** field. Use the time format 12:00 AM.
5. Enter the duration of your session in the **Session Duration** field. This is the number of hours or minutes of your training session.
6. Choose your felting skills level from the **Felting Skills Level** dropdown menu: High or Low.
7. In the **Areas Covered** field, list the topics or areas you covered during your training session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Details**: Enter a brief description of your course, e.g., "Introduction to Felting" (optional)
* **Trainers Name**: This field is for the name of the trainer or facilitator (optional)
* **Training Session Date**: Enter the date of your training session (e.g., 05/01/2024) (optional)
* **Session Start Time**: Enter the start time of your session (e.g., 09:00 AM) (optional)
* **Session Duration**: Enter the duration of your session in hours or minutes (e.g., 2 hours) (optional)
* **Felting Skills Level**: Choose your felting skills level: High or Low (optional)
* **Areas Covered**: List the topics or areas you covered during your session, e.g., "Basic Felting Techniques, Felting for Beginners" (optional)

Tips:
* Please ensure that you provide accurate information for the duration and date fields.
* If you are not sure about the felting skills level, please select the one that best describes your skills.
* For the Areas Covered field, be as detailed as possible to help us understand your training session better.

Note: This form is optional and is meant to assist us in improving our training resources. If you have any issues or concerns, please reach out to us.
