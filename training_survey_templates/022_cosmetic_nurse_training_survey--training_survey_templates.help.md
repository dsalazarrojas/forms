# Cosmetic Nurse Training Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from cosmetic nurse training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out each field carefully, as each one is essential for providing accurate and complete information.
2.  Review each field for any additional guidance or instructions provided.
3.  Make sure to provide detailed and accurate information in each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Form Title** (`form-title`, text, optional):
    Enter a brief title of the survey.
*   **Form Category** (`form-category`, text, optional):
    Enter the category of the survey, for instance, "Cosmetic Nursing".
*   **Form Description** (`form-description`, text, optional):
    Provide a detailed description of the survey.
*   **Form ID** (`form-ids`, number, optional):
    Enter the unique ID of the form.
*   **Assigned Tool** (`assigned-tool`, text, optional):
    If a tool was assigned to you during the training session, enter the name of the tool.
*   **Output File** (`output-file`, text, optional):
    You do not need to fill out this field.
