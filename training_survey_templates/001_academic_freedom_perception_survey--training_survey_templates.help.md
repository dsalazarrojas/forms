# Academic Freedom Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Freedom Perception Survey is designed to collect feedback on the perceptions of academic freedom at your institution. This guide will walk you through each field to ensure you understand what we're asking and how to provide valuable feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer truthfully based on your experience.
2. Select options that best describe your current situation or perception.
3. Be as detailed as possible in your answers, especially in the 'additional comments' section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your primary role at the university** (`respondent_role`): Please select the option that best describes your current position at the institution. This helps us understand who's answering the survey and provides insights specific to your group's experience.
* **Department or College** (`department`): Enter the name of your department or college. This helps us understand the context of your feedback.
* **How many years have you been at this institution** (`years_at_institution`): Choose the option that best describes your tenure at the institution. This helps us understand your level of experience and perspective.
* **How would you rate the level of academic freedom in teaching at this institution** (`freedom_teaching_rating`): Rate the level of academic freedom in teaching at this institution on a scale from 1 (very restricted) to 5 (fully free). This helps us gauge the extent to which academic freedom is exercised in teaching.
* **How would you rate the level of academic freedom in research at this institution** (`freedom_research_rating`): Rate the level of academic freedom in research at this institution on a scale from 1 (very restricted) to 5 (fully free). This helps us understand the level of freedom in research activities.
* **How comfortable do you feel expressing viewpoints that may be unpopular on campus** (`freedom_expression_rating`): Select the option that best describes your comfort level with expressing viewpoints that may be unpopular on campus. This helps us gauge the level of freedom in expressing opinions.
* **Have you ever self-censored your academic work or speech due to concerns about institutional consequences** (`self_censorship`): Choose the option that best describes your experiences with self-censorship.
* **What barriers to academic freedom have you experienced or observed** (`barriers_to_freedom`): Select all that apply from the given options, as this helps us understand the barriers to academic freedom on campus.
* **How well do institutional policies support academic freedom** (`institutional_policies`): Rate the level of support from institutional policies for academic freedom on a scale from 1 (very poorly) to 5 (very well).
* **Are you aware of your institution's written policies on academic freedom** (`awareness_of_policies`): Choose the option that best describes your awareness of these policies.
* **What areas need the most improvement to enhance academic freedom on campus** (`improvement_areas`): Select all that apply from the given options, as this helps us identify areas for improvement.
* **Overall, how satisfied are you with the state of academic freedom at this institution** (`overall_satisfaction`): Rate your level of satisfaction with the state of academic freedom on campus on a scale from 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **Please provide any additional comments or suggestions regarding academic freedom on campus** (`additional_comments`): Share any further thoughts or suggestions you have about enhancing academic freedom on campus. Your feedback is valuable and will be kept confidential.
