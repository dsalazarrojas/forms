# Counseling Training Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from trainees who have participated in a counseling training session. The goal is to gather information about the trainees' perceptions of the session outcomes, engagement, trainer support, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Read through each page of the form to understand the questions and prompts.
2. Answer all questions and select all applicable options. You can submit the form once you have completed all pages.
3. Be honest and thorough in your responses, as your feedback is valuable in helping us improve the training program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Introduction
* **introduction** (text): This is a brief description of the form and its purpose.
### Page 2: Session Outcomes
* **session_outcomes** (select_one): This question asks you to select the primary outcome that you achieved from the training session. The options are:
	+ Outcome 1
	+ Outcome 2
	+ Outcome 3
Please select the one that best describes your experience.
### Page 3: Engagement
* **engagement** (number): This question asks you to rate your engagement level during the training session. Please select a number to indicate how engaged you felt:
	+ 1 (not engaged)
	+ 2 (somewhat engaged)
	+ 3 (engaged)
	+ 4 (very engaged)
	+ 5 (extremely engaged)
### Page 4: Improvement Areas
* **improvement_areas** (select_multiple): This question asks you to select all areas where you feel you need improvement. The options are:
	+ Outcome 1
	+ Outcome 2
	+ Outcome 3
	+ Outcome 4
	+ Outcome 5
Please select all that apply.
### Page 5: Trainer Support
* **trainer_support** (select_multiple): This question asks you to select the areas where you felt you received sufficient support from the trainer. The options are:
	+ Outcome 1
	+ Outcome 2
	+ Outcome 3
	+ Outcome 4
Please select all that apply.
### Page 6: Time Management
* **time_management** (number): This question asks you to rate how well the training session was time-managed. Please select a number to indicate:
	+ 1 (poor)
	+ 2 (fair)
	+ 3 (good)
	+ 4 (very good)
	+ 5 (excellent)
### Page 7: Session Length
* **session_length** (number): This question asks you to rate how well the training session length was managed. Please select a number to indicate:
	+ 1 (too short)
	+ 2 (short)
	+ 3 (just right)
	+ 4 (too long)
	+ 5 (much too long)
### Page 8: Feedback
* **feedback** (text): This is an open-ended question for you to provide any additional feedback or comments about the training session.
### Page 9: Overall Experience
* **overall_experience** (select_one): This question asks you to rate your overall experience with the training session. The options are:
	+ Outcome 1
	+ Outcome 2
	+ Outcome 3
	+ Outcome 4

## Tips
* Be thorough and honest in your responses, as your feedback is valuable in helping us improve the training program.
* If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the "feedback" field to share them.
* If you feel that any of the questions or options do not apply to your experience, please select "Not Applicable" or "Not Applicable (None of the above)".
