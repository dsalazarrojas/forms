# Retirement Webinar Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees of a retirement webinar. Your input will help us evaluate the effectiveness of the session and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the session title.
2. Rate your overall experience with the session.
3. Select all the ways you think the session can be improved.
4. Provide more information about how the session can be improved.
5. If you want to leave additional comments, you can use the comment field.
6. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
7. Enter your phone number for contact purposes (optional).
8. Specify the date of the webinar session you attended.
9. Specify the time of the webinar session you attended.
10. Specify the date you attended the webinar session.
11. Rate the length of the session (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Title** (`session_title`, text, required): The title of the session you attended.
* **Rating** (`session_rating`, number, required): A scale from 1 to 5 to rate your overall experience with the session.
* **How** (`session_rating_how`, text, required): Briefly describe why you gave the rating you did.
* **Improve** (`session_improve`, select_multiple, required): Select all the ways you think the session can be improved. For example, "more hands-on exercises" or "more examples".
* **How to Improve** (`session_improve_how`, text, required): Provide more information about how the session can be improved.
* **Comments** (`session_comments`, note, required): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please enter them here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further discussion.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Session** (`date_of_session`, date, required): Specify the date of the webinar session you attended.
* **Time of Session** (`time_of_session`, time, required): Specify the time of the webinar session you attended.
* **Date Attended** (`date_attended`, date, required): Specify the date you attended the webinar session.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, number, required): The length of the webinar session you attended, in minutes.
