# Gym Trainer Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the skills and performance of gym trainers, focusing on four key areas: Professionalism, Communication, Motivation, and Overall Satisfaction. The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback from clients or trainees to help trainers improve their services and create a better training experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the questions carefully and answer them honestly.
2.  For each question, provide a detailed response that reflects your experience with the trainer.
3.  Use the text fields to provide as much detail as needed.
4.  Submit the form once you have completed all questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Professionalism** (`training_survey_templates_professionalism`, text, optional):
	+ Explain how the trainer's professionalism impacted your experience. This could include their punctuality, organization, and respect for boundaries.
* **Communication** (`training_survey_templates_communication`, text, optional):
	+ Describe how the trainer's communication skills were during training sessions. This could include their ability to explain exercises, provide feedback, and answer questions.
* **Motivation** (`training_survey_templates_motivation`, text, optional):
	+ Discuss how the trainer motivated you during training sessions. This could include their energy, encouragement, and support.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`training_survey_templates_satisfaction`, text, optional):
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction with the trainer's service, providing any comments or suggestions you may have.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your responses, as your feedback will help the trainer improve.
* Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your answers.
* If you're unsure about a question, feel free to ask the trainer for clarification.
