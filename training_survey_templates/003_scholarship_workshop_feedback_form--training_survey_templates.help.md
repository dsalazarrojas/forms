# Scholarship Workshop Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants on the scholarship workshop. Your input will help us understand what worked well and what could be improved.

## How To Complete This Form

- To complete this form, simply fill out each section as instructed below.
- Make sure to provide your honest and thoughtful feedback.
- If you have any difficulty or questions, contact us at your organization's contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name_input`, text, optional): This is your name, which we will use to reference your feedback.
* **Feedback Form** (`feedback_form`, text, optional): This is the main feedback section. Please describe what you liked most about the workshop, including any specific sessions, activities, or speakers that stood out to you.
* **What did you dislike about the workshop?** (`feedback_input_2`, text, optional): This is the space to share any concerns, issues, or areas for improvement.
* **How many participants attended?** (`name_of_participants_attending`, number, optional): This is the number of participants that attended the workshop. If you are unsure, please estimate or provide a range.
* **Contact Email** (`email_input`, email, optional): This is your email address, which we will use to contact you with follow-up questions or to share additional information.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_input`, text, optional): This is your phone number, which we will use to contact you with follow-up questions or to share additional information.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your feedback.
