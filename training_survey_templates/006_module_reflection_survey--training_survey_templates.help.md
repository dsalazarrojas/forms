# Module Reflection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Module Reflection Survey is a form used by trainers to gather feedback and reflections on a module or session they have completed. This form is designed to help gather information about the session's effectiveness and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form once you have finished the module or session.
2. Ensure that you have read and understand the questions before providing feedback.
3. Answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
4. If you encounter any issues or have questions, please contact our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (intro_page, note, Not Required): This is a brief introduction to the survey. Please provide a short description of the session or module you have just completed.

- **Comments** (comments_field, text, Not Required): If you have any additional comments or observations about the session or module, please provide them in this text field.
- **Trainer Name** (trainer_name, text, Not Required): If you wish to provide your name as the trainer, please do so in this text field. This will help us to identify your feedback.
- **Email** (email, email, Not Required): If you wish to provide your email address, please do so in this text field. This will help us to contact you if we need more information.
- **Training Date** (training_date, date, Not Required): If you can recall the date of the session or module, please provide it in this date field.
- **Start Time** (start_time, time, Not Required): If you can recall the start time of the session or module, please provide it in this time field.
- **End Time** (end_time, time, Not Required): If you can recall the end time of the session or module, please provide it in this time field.
- **Training Duration** (training_duration, number, Not Required): If you can estimate the duration of the session or module, please provide it in this number field.
- **Module Name** (module_name, text, Not Required): If you can recall the name of the module or session, please provide it in this text field.
- **Session Type** (session_type, select_one, Required): Choose the type of session or module that you have just completed.
	* Online
	* In-person
	* Hybrid

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team. 
* This form is only intended for trainers who have completed a specific module or session.
* Please make sure to fill out the form once you have finished the module or session and not for other purposes. 

This guide will help you in understanding how to fill out the Module Reflection Survey form and provide the best possible feedback for improvement. 
If you have any issues or concerns while filling out the form, feel free to contact us.
