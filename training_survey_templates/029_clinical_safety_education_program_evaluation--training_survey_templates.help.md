# Clinical Safety Education Program Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help evaluate the effectiveness and satisfaction of the clinical safety education program. It will help identify areas of strength and weakness in the program, allowing for adjustments and improvements to be made for future training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Read each question carefully and answer them based on your honest feedback.
2.  Select the relevant options for each field. For fields with a 'select_multiple' type, select all that apply.
3.  Provide as much detail as possible for fields with a 'text' type.
4.  If a question doesn't apply to you, select 'Not Applicable' or 'Not Relevant' where possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Name**: This field is optional and is for your identification purposes only.
    *   **Hint:** You can leave this field blank if you're not comfortable sharing your name.
*   **Participant ID**: This field is optional and is for your identification purposes only.
    *   **Hint:** You can leave this field blank if you're not comfortable sharing your ID.
*   **Department**: Select the department where you took the training.
    *   **Hint:** Check the provided options and select the one that best represents your department.
*   **Your Role**: Select the title of your role within the organization.
    *   **Hint:** Check the provided options and select the one that best represents your role.
*   **Training Completion Date**: Enter the date you completed the training.
    *   **Hint:** This should be the date you actually attended the training session.
*   **Type of Safety Training**: Choose the type of safety training you attended.
    *   **Hint:** Select the one that best describes the type of training you received.
*   **Training Format**: Choose the format of the training.
    *   **Hint:** Select the one that best describes the way the training was delivered.
*   **Training Duration**: Choose how long the training session was.
    *   **Hint:** Select the one that best describes the length of the training session.
*   **Relevance**: Rate how relevant the content was to your role.
    *   **Hint:** Select the one that best describes your experience with the content.
*   **Clarity**: Rate how clear the content was presented.
    *   **Hint:** Select the one that best describes your experience with the content presentation.
*   **Material Quality**: Rate the quality of the training materials.
    *   **Hint:** Select the one that best describes your experience with the materials provided.
*   **Instructor Knowledge**: Rate how knowledgeable the instructors were of the subject matter.
    *   **Hint:** Select the one that best describes your experience with the instructors.
*   **Instructor Effectiveness**: Rate the effectiveness of the instructor's teaching.
    *   **Hint:** Select the one that best describes your experience with the instructors.
*   **Pace of Training**: Rate how fast or slow the training was.
    *   **Hint:** Select the one that best describes your experience with the pace of the training.
*   **Difficulty Level**: Rate how difficult or easy you found the training was.
    *   **Hint:** Select the one that best describes your experience with the difficulty level of the training.
*   **Engagement**: Rate how engaging you found the training to be.
    *   **Hint:** Select the one that best describes your experience with the engagement level of the training.
*   **Knowledge Gained**: Rate how much you learned from the training.
    *   **Hint:** Select the one that best describes your experience with the amount you learned.
*   **Confidence Improvement**: Rate how much your confidence improved after the training.
    *   **Hint:** Select the one that best describes your experience with the confidence improvement level.
*   **Practical Application**: Rate how likely you are to apply what you learned.
    *   **Hint:** Select the one that best describes your experience with applying what you learned.
*   **Barriers to Application**: Select all the barriers that prevent you from applying what you learned.
    *   **Hint:** Select all that apply.
*   **Assessment Fairness**: Rate how fair the knowledge assessment was.
    *   **Hint:** Select the one that best describes your experience with the assessment.
*   **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the training.
    *   **Hint:** Select the one that best describes your experience with the training.
*   **Met Expectations**: Rate how well the training met your expectations.
    *   **Hint:** Select the one that best describes your experience with the training meeting your expectations.
*   **Recommend to Colleagues**: Would you recommend this training to your colleagues?
    *   **Hint:** Select the one that best describes your experience with recommending this training.
*   **Most Valuable Aspect**: Describe the most valuable aspect of the training.
    *   **Hint:** Provide as much detail as possible.
*   **Least Valuable Aspect**: Describe the least valuable aspect of the training.
    *   **Hint:** Provide as much detail as possible.
*   **Topics for Improvement**: Suggest topics that should be improved or added.
    *   **Hint:** Provide as much detail as possible.
*   **Additional Training Needed**: Suggest any additional training you may need.
    *   **Hint:** Provide as much detail as possible.
*   **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments about the training.
    *   **Hint:** Provide as much detail as possible.

## Tips

*   Be honest in your feedback. Your input will help us improve the training sessions.
*   Select the options that best describe your experience with each question.
*   If you're unsure about a question, select the one that you think best describes your experience.
*   Provide as much detail as possible for fields with a 'text' type.
*   If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use the 'Additional Comments' field.
