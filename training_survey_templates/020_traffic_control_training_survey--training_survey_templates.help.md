# Traffic Control Training Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information from participants on their experiences with the traffic control training program. It aims to evaluate the effectiveness of the program and gather insights for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your information, making sure to answer all required fields.
2. Select the correct departments that were relevant to your experience with the traffic control training program.
3. Indicate whether you completed the program and your overall score.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (first_name, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (last_name, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter your work email address.
* **Participant ID** (participant_id, number, optional): Enter your participant ID if you have one.
* **Department(s)** (department, select_multiple, optional): Select all departments that were relevant to your experience with the traffic control training program.
	+ **Yes**: The department was relevant to your experience.
	+ **No**: The department was not relevant to your experience.
* **Date Completed** (date_completed, date, optional): Enter the date you completed the traffic control training program.
* **Time Completed** (time_completed, time, optional): Enter the time you completed the traffic control training program.
* **Comments** (comments, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about the program.
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter your work phone number.
* **Score** (score, number, optional): Enter your overall score of the program, out of 10.
* **Completed** (completed, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you completed the program.
	+ **Yes**: You completed the program.
	+ **No**: You did not complete the program.
* **Category** (category, select_one, optional): Select the category that best describes your experience with the program.
* **Comments Required** (comments_required, select_one, optional): Indicate whether comments are required for your submission.
	+ **Yes**: Comments are required.
	+ **No**: Comments are not required.
* **Date** (date, date, optional): Enter the date of your submission.
* **Time** (time, time, optional): Enter the time of your submission.
* **Department 2**, **Department 3**, ..., **Department 11**, **Department 12** (department_2 to department_12, select_multiple, optional): Select all departments that were relevant to your experience with the traffic control training program. These fields are similar to "Department", but with more options.
