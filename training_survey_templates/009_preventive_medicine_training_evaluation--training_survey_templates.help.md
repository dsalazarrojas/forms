# Preventive Medicine Training Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to evaluate the effectiveness of preventive medicine training programs. It assesses various aspects of the training, including course content, delivery, and impact on trainees. The evaluation results will inform future program development and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Introduction**: Briefly describe the context of the training, including the course title, location, and duration.
2. **Course Name**: Enter the name of the training course.
3. **Training Location**: Specify the location where the training was conducted.
4. **Training Duration**: Indicate the length of the training program.
5. **Training Start Date**: Enter the date the training began.
6. **Training End Date**: Enter the date the training ended.
7. **Training Instructor**: Identify the person who delivered the training.
8. **Submitter**: Select who submitted this evaluation (Trainee, Instructor, or Evaluator).
9. **Course Level**: Describe the level of the course (if applicable).
10. **Form Submission Type**: Indicate if the training was conducted online, offline, or both.
11. **Form Completion Status**: Select the status of the form completion (Completed, In Progress, or Not Started).
12. **Note**: Add any additional comments or notes about the training.
13. **Comments**: Provide any comments or feedback on the training.
14. **Email**: Enter your email address (if you have one).
15. **Phone**: Enter your phone number (if you have one).
16. **Submitter Name**: Enter your name.
17. **Submitter Title**: Enter your job title or position.
18. **Training Facility**: Specify the location where the training took place.
19. **Form Completion Date**: Enter the date this form was completed.
20. **Course ID**: Enter the course ID (if applicable).
21. **Evaluation Score**: Enter a numerical score to evaluate the training (if applicable).
22. **Submitter Institution**: Enter your institution or organization.
23. **Evaluation Score Range**: Enter the range of scores (if applicable).
24. **Evaluation Comment**: Add any additional comments about the evaluation.
25. **Form ID**: Enter the ID of the form (if applicable).
