# vlan_knowledge_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vlan_knowledge_assessment form is designed to evaluate the knowledge of users on VLAN (Virtual Local Area Network) concepts, ensuring they have a good understanding of VLAN configuration, challenges, and setup.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Start on the "introduction" page, which is just a note to remind you that this form is not meant for users without prior knowledge of VLAN concepts.
2. Proceed to the "vlan_configuration" field, where you will need to provide your knowledge on VLAN configuration in text format.
3. Select the challenges related to VLAN configuration that you are familiar with from the options provided in the "challenges" field.
4. Choose your level of skills in VLAN from the "skills" field.
5. Indicate your level of readiness for learning VLAN-related concepts, with options for "High" or "Low", in the "learner_readiness" field.
6. Enter any additional comments or feedback, if needed.
7. Finally, add any VLAN setup details that you would like to share in the "vlan_setup" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### VLAN Configuration (vlan_configuration)

* **vlan_configuration** (text, required): Enter your knowledge on VLAN configuration in text format.

### Challenges (challenges)

* **challenges** (select_multiple, optional): Select the challenges related to VLAN configuration that you are familiar with. Options:
	+ VLAN configuration challenges
	+ Other (please describe)

### Skills (skills)

* **skills** (select_one, required): Choose your level of skills in VLAN from the options provided.
	+ Basic configuration skills
	+ Medium complexity skills
	+ High complexity skills

### Learner Readiness (learner_readiness)

* **learner_readiness** (select_multiple, required): Indicate your level of readiness for learning VLAN-related concepts.
	+ High
	+ Low

### VLAN Setup (vlan_setup)

* **vlan_setup** (number, optional): Enter any additional VLAN setup details. Note: This field is not currently supported.

### Comments (comments)

* **comments** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback, if needed.

### Additional Comments (additional_comments)

* **additional_comments** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback, if needed.
