# Anti Bribery And Corruption Training Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Anti Bribery And Corruption Training Feedback Survey is designed to capture participant feedback after the training session. The form is used to evaluate the effectiveness of the training, identify areas of improvement, and provide feedback on the relevance, presenter's engagement, and overall satisfaction of the participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the training details section with the date of the session you attended.
2. Provide your name (optional).
3. Select your job role or title (optional).
4. Rate the relevance of the content (scale: 1-5).
5. Rate the presenter's effectiveness (scale: 1-5).
6. Choose the level of usefulness of the training materials (optional).
7. Specify the key takeaways or improvements you can apply from this training.
8. Provide your overall satisfaction rating for the training experience (scale: 1-5).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Section: Training Details** (`section_training_details`)
* **Training date** (`training_date`): This field is required to track when you attended the training session.
* **Job role or title** (`role`): This field helps us understand who participated in the training. Select your job role or title from the provided options.

### **Section: Participant Information** (`participant_name`, `role`)
* **Participant name** (`participant_name`): If you prefer to provide your name, please do so here. Note that this field is optional.
* **Job role or title** (`role`): This field is used to segment responses by job role or title. Select your job role or title from the provided options.

### **Section: Feedback** (`relevance_rating`, `presenter_effectiveness`, `materials_usefulness`, `key_takeaways`, `overall_satisfaction`)
* **Relevance of content** (`relevance_rating`): Rate the relevance of the content on a scale from 1 (not relevant) to 5 (highly relevant).
* **Presenter effectiveness** (`presenter_effectiveness`): Rate the presenter's engagement and clarity on a scale from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Usefulness of training materials** (`materials_usefulness`): Choose the level of usefulness of the training materials from the provided options (Not useful, Somewhat useful, Useful, Very useful).
* **Key takeaways or improvements** (`key_takeaways`): Specify the key takeaways or improvements you can apply from this training.
* **Overall satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`): Provide your overall satisfaction rating for the training experience on a scale from 1 (not satisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).

### **Tips**
* Be as specific as possible when rating the presenter's effectiveness and overall satisfaction.
* If you have any suggestions or improvements for future trainings, please provide them in the "Key takeaways or improvements" field.
