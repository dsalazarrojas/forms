# Continuous Improvement Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your knowledge of continuous improvement concepts, including Kaizen, PDCA, lean methodology, Six Sigma, and other relevant topics. It will help identify areas where you need further review and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your information and answers to the questions.
2. Answer all questions with the correct options provided.
3. For the "Topics Needing Review" field, select any topics you feel need further review or study.
4. Finally, select your level of confidence in applying these concepts.
