**Thermal Management Training Survey - Help Guide**
## Purpose
The Thermal Management Training Survey is designed to collect feedback and ratings from trainees about their recent thermal management training experience. This form is used to gather information on the course's effectiveness, rating, and any additional feedback or comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the **Course Name**, the title of the training session or course.
2. Enter the name of the **Trainer(s)** who conducted the training session.
3. Enter the **Course Date** in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
4. Enter the **Course Start Time** and **Course End Time** in the format `HH:MM`.
5. Rate the course by selecting **Course Rating** from the options provided.
6. Enter any **Additional Feedback** or comments you may have about the course.
7. Select how strongly you **Course Recommendation** by choosing from the options provided.
8. Enter your **Email** address for contact purposes.
9. Enter your **Phone** number for contact purposes.
10. Add any additional **Comments** about the training session, if desired.
11. If applicable, enter the **Course Name 2**, **Course Start Time 2**, and **Course Date 2** for a secondary course.
12. Enter the **Reporting Person** and **Reported To** for reporting purposes.
13. Enter the name of the **Reporting Organization**.
