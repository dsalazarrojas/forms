# Adult Return On Investment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adult Return On Investment (ROI) Survey is a tool used to measure the effectiveness of a training program in generating revenue and promoting career advancement opportunities. It is designed to help evaluate the return on investment of a training program by gathering feedback from participants on its impact on their productivity, skill application, revenue generation, and other relevant aspects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information required to complete each field accurately.
2. Enter your participant information, including your name and any other relevant details.
3. Provide information about your job title, department, and department/division (if applicable).
4. Answer questions about your training experience, including the name of the training program, the date you completed it, and the total hours spent in training.
5. Rate your productivity improvement, skill application frequency, revenue impact, cost savings, promotion opportunities, likelihood of recommending the training, and overall ROI rating using the provided scales.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`Participant Name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, `text`, optional): Enter your current job title.
* **Department** (`Department`, `text`, optional): Enter your department or division (if applicable).
* **Training Program Name** (`Training Program Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the training program you attended.
* **Training Completion Date** (`Training Completion Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you completed the training in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Training Duration (Hours)** (`Training Duration (Hours)`, `number`, required): Enter the total hours spent in training.
* **Training Cost** (`Training Cost`, `number`, required): Enter the total cost of the training program.
* **Productivity Improvement** (`Productivity Improvement`, `select_one`, required): Rate the improvement in your productivity since the training.
* **Skill Application Frequency** (`Skill Application Frequency`, `select_one`, required): Rate how often you apply skills learned in the training.
* **Revenue Impact Rating** (`Revenue Impact Rating`, `number`, required): Rate the impact of training on revenue generation (1-10 scale).
* **Cost Savings Identified** (`Cost Savings Identified`, `select_one`, required): Have you identified any cost savings as a result of the training?
* **Promotion Opportunities** (`Promotion Opportunities`, `select_one`, required): Has the training led to any promotion opportunities?
* **Recommendation Likelihood** (`Recommendation Likelihood`, `number`, required): Rate how likely you are to recommend this training to others (1-10 scale).
* **Overall ROI Rating** (`Overall ROI Rating`, `number`, required): Rate the overall return on investment of this training (1-10 scale).
