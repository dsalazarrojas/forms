# Report Writing Workshop Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your honest feedback on the Report Writing Workshop you just attended. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form and provide your input to help us improve future workshops.

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on the next page button (>>) at the bottom of each page to proceed to the next page.
- Fill out each field according to the instructions provided below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Writing Feedback (Workshop Feedback)**
	+ (**label**: `workshop_feedback`, **type**: `text`, **required**: `false`):
		- Please share your overall impression of the workshop.
* **Participant Rating (Participant Rating)** (Optional)
	+ (**label**: `workshop_participant_rating`, **type**: `number`, **required**: `false`):
		- If you attended this workshop, please rate your level of engagement and enjoyment on a scale of 1-5.
* **Additional Comments (Additional Comments)**
	+ (**label**: `workshop_comments`, **type**: `text`, **required**: `false`):
		- Share any other thoughts, opinions, or suggestions you have about the workshop.
* **Overall Workshop Rating (Overall Workshop Rating)** (Optional)
	+ (**label**: `workshop_rating`, **type**: `select_one`, **required**: `false`):
		- Rate the overall quality of the workshop (1-5).
* **Relevance (Relevance)**
	+ (**label**: `workshop_relevance`, **type**: `select_multiple`, **required**: `false`):
		- Indicate whether you think the workshop topics are relevant to your needs (Relevant, Not Relevant, Not Applicable).
* **Areas for Improvement (Areas for Improvement)**
	+ (**label**: `workshop_improvement`, **type**: `text`, **required**: `false`):
		- Share any areas you think could be improved in future workshops.
* **Trainer Performance (Trainer Performance)** (Optional)
	+ (**label**: `trainer_performance`, **type**: `select_one`, **required**: `false`):
		- Rate the quality of the trainer's presentation (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Overall Value (Overall Value)** (Optional)
	+ (**label**: `workshop_value`, **type**: `number`, **required**: `false`):
		- Rate the value of the workshop to you (Optional).
* **Would you recommend this workshop? (Would you recommend this workshop?)** (Optional)
	+ (**label**: `workshop_would_recommend`, **type**: `select_one`, **required**: `false`):
		- Indicate whether or not you would recommend this workshop to others.
* **Additional Comments (Additional Comments)**
	+ (**label**: `additional_comments`, **type**: `text`, **required**: `false`):
		- Share any other comments or suggestions you have about the workshop.
