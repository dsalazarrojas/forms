# Neurodiversity Program Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from attendees about their experience with the Neurodiversity Program. The information collected will help us understand the effectiveness and relevance of the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all the questions truthfully and concisely.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
3. You can skip optional fields, but your feedback will be more valuable if you answer all questions.
4. Review and proofread your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as you would like to be addressed in the feedback.
* **Session ID** (`session_id`, number, required): Enter the ID of the session you attended.
* **Attended Sessions** (`attended_sessions`, select multiple, required): Check all the sessions you attended. You can select multiple sessions if applicable.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the program on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Share any suggestions for improvement for the program.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share any other feedback about your experience.
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, select one, required): Choose the type of session you attended.
* **Session Topic** (`session_topic`, text, optional): Enter the session topic or theme.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the session you attended.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the session you attended.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of the session you attended.
* **Rating Sessions** (`rating_sessions`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the sessions on a scale of 1-5.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, optional): Share any suggestions for improvement for the program or sessions.
* **Attended By** (`attended_by`, text, optional): Share who attended the session with you.
* **Comments on Sessions** (`comments_on_sessions`, text, optional): Share any comments about the sessions.
* **Rating Attendee Satisfaction** (`rating_attendee_satisfaction`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the program on a scale of 1-5.
* **Rating Instructor Satisfaction** (`rating_instructor_satisfaction`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the instructor on a scale of 1-5.
* **Rating Materials Provision** (`rating_materials_provision`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the materials provided on a scale of 1-5.
* **Suggestions for Improvement Attendee** (`suggestions_for_improvement_attendee`, text, optional): Share any suggestions for improvement for the attendee experience.
* **Suggestions for Improvement Instructor** (`suggestions_for_improvement_instructor`, text, optional): Share any suggestions for improvement for the instructor.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback.
