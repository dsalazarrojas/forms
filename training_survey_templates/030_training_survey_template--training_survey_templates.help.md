## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your honest feedback and ratings.
2. Answer each question carefully, providing detailed responses to help us improve.
3. If you need more time or space to express yourself, use the "Comments" field to add any additional thoughts or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the purpose of the training session?** (`purpose`, text, optional): This is a text field where you can briefly describe the purpose or objective of the training session.
* **How would you rate the overall quality of the training session?** (`quality_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the quality of the training session using the scale: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Which of the following topics were covered during the training session?** (`topics_covered`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the topics that were covered during the training session from the list provided.
* **How many hours did you spend on the training session?** (`training_hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you spent on the training session. This will help us calculate the duration and effectiveness of the training.
* **On what date did the training session take place?** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the training session took place.
* **What time did the training session start and end?** (`start_end_time`, time, optional): Enter the start and end time of the training session in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **How would you rate the training facilitator's performance?** (`facilitator_performance`, select_one, optional): Rate the facilitator's performance using the scale: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Are there any other comments you would like to provide?** (`comments`, note, optional): Use this field to add any additional thoughts or concerns you may have about the training session.
* **How would you rate the overall relevance of the training session to your job requirements?** (`relevance`, select_one, optional): Rate the relevance of the training session using the scale: Very relevant, Somewhat relevant, Not very relevant.
* **How would you rate the overall effectiveness of the training session?** (`effectiveness`, select_one, optional): Rate the effectiveness of the training session using the scale: Highly effective, Somewhat effective, Not very effective.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your responses to help us improve future trainings.
* If you're unsure about any part of the form, ask for clarification or guidance.
* We appreciate your feedback and look forward to using it to better serve our training needs.
