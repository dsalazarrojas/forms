# Diversity And Inclusion Workshop Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants in a workshop on diversity and inclusion, aimed at providing valuable insights for further improvement and enhancing the overall diversity and inclusion experience in the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Review the form sections: The form is divided into several sections, each covering different aspects of the workshop. Please ensure you provide feedback for all required fields.
2. Provide clear and concise answers: Please answer each question or prompt with clear and concise responses. Avoid vague or incomplete answers, as they might not provide sufficient feedback.
3. Review and submit: Once you have completed all the fields, review your answers carefully, and submit the form when you're ready.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Workshop Details** (`workshop_details`, `text`, not required)
This section allows you to provide any additional context or details about the workshop you attended.

### 2. **Session Attendance** (`session_attendance`, `number`, not required)
In this field, please report whether you attended any sessions during the workshop.

### 3. **Demographics** (`demographics`, `select_one`, required)
Please select one of the three options to describe your gender identity: Male, Female, or Non-Binary.

### 4. **Feedback** (`feedback_session`, `text`, not required)
Here, you can provide any general feedback about the workshop, including suggestions for improvement.

### 5. **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, not required)
This section is for you to add any additional comments or notes about the workshop.

### 6. **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required)
Please enter your email address so that we can follow up with you regarding your feedback.

### 7. **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required)
You can provide your phone number to enable us to contact you for further discussion or clarification.

### 8. **Rating (1-5)** (`rating`, `number`, not required)
Please rate your overall experience in the workshop on a scale of 1 to 5.

### 9. **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `note`, not required)
Here, you can provide suggestions for improving the workshop.

### 10. **Submit** (`submit`, `select_multiple`, required)
Please select the reasons why you want to submit your feedback:
  * I want to improve this course further
  * It is perfect the way it is
  * Other, please specify

### 11. **Additional Comments** (`submit_comments`, `note`, not required)
This is an optional space for any additional comments or suggestions you might have. 

Note: Please make sure to submit your feedback for it to be counted.
