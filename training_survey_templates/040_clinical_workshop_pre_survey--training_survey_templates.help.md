# Clinical Workshop Pre Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your background, experience, and expectations from the workshop. Your input will help us tailor the workshop content to meet your needs and learning objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. For each field, use the provided options or enter a detailed description.
3. If a field is optional, you may skip it unless you feel it is relevant to your response.
4. If you are unsure about a field or have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Clinical Workshop Pre-Survey** (`survey_intro`, `note`, required: false):
	+ This is an introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully to understand the purpose and scope of the form.
* **Your Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Employee or Student ID** (`participant_id`, `text`, required: false):
	+ If you are employed or a student, enter your ID number or name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required: true):
	+ Enter your institutional email address.
* **Department or Unit** (`department`, `select_one`, required: true):
	+ Select your department or unit from the provided options.
* **Your Role** (`role`, `select_one`, required: true):
	+ Choose your role from the provided options.
* **Years of Clinical Experience** (`years_experience`, `select_one`, required: true):
	+ Select the number of years you have been in clinical practice.
* **Workshop Topic** (`workshop_topic`, `text`, required: true):
	+ Enter the topic you would like to learn about in the workshop.
* **Current Knowledge Level on This Topic** (`current_knowledge_level`, `select_one`, required: true):
	+ Choose your current knowledge level regarding the topic.
* **Have You Had Previous Training on This Topic** (`previous_training`, `select_one`, required: true):
	+ Select your experience with previous training on this topic.
* **Previous Training Details** (`previous_training_details`, `text`, required: false):
	+ If you have had previous training, describe it in detail.
* **Current Confidence Level** (`confidence_level`, `select_one`, required: true):
	+ Choose your current confidence level in applying the knowledge.
* **What Are Your Learning Goals** (`learning_goals`, `text`, required: true):
	+ Describe what you hope to gain from this workshop.
* **Specific Topics You Want Covered** (`specific_topics`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter specific topics you would like to be covered in the workshop.
* **How Do You Plan to Apply This Knowledge** (`clinical_application`, `text`, required: true):
	+ Describe how you plan to use the knowledge gained from the workshop.
* **What Challenges Do You Currently Face** (`current_challenges`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Describe the challenges you face related to the topic.
* **Patient Population You Serve** (`patient_population`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Select the population you serve.
* **Practice Setting** (`practice_setting`, `select_one`, required: true):
	+ Choose your practice setting from the provided options.
* **Size of Your Clinical Team** (`team_size`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Select the size of your clinical team.
* **Resources Available at Your Facility** (`resources_available`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select all the resources available at your facility.
* **Preferred Learning Style** (`learning_preference`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Choose your preferred learning style.
* **Potential Barriers to Implementation** (`barriers_to_implementation`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Describe the potential barriers to implementing new skills.
* **What Support Would You Need** (`support_needed`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Describe what support you would need to implement new knowledge.
* **Are You Interested in Certification** (`certification_interest`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Select your interest in certification related to the topic.
* **How Did You Hear About This Workshop** (`how_did_you_hear`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Choose how you heard about this workshop.
* **Accessibility Requirements** (`accessibility_needs`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Describe any accessibility requirements.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter any additional comments you would like to share.

Note: This help guide is a general explanation of the form. Please review the form carefully to ensure you understand each field and its purpose. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
