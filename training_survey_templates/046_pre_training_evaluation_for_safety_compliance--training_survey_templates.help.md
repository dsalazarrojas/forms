# Pre Training Evaluation For Safety Compliance - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the safety compliance aspects of a training program, ensuring that all necessary safety measures are in place and that incident reporting processes are effective.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill in the required fields accurately and thoroughly.
3. If you are unsure about a particular field, please refer to the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Safety Training Program** (`safety_training_program`, text, optional)
 Enter a brief description of the safety training program offered by your organization.
### 2. **Risk Assessment** (`risk_assessment`, text, optional)
 Describe the risk assessment process used to identify potential hazards and hazards in your workplace.
### 3. **Training Program** (`training_program`, text, optional)
 Provide details about the training program offered by your organization, including its objectives and goals.
### 4. **Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment`, text, optional)
 List the safety equipment available for use in your workplace.
### 5. **Safety Procedure** (`safety_procedure`, text, optional)
 Describe the safety procedures in place for responding to emergencies and incidents.
### 6. **Safety Equipment Inspection** (`safety_equipment_inspection`, text, optional)
 Outline the frequency and method of safety equipment inspection and maintenance.
### 7. **Training Certification** (`training_certification`, text, optional)
 Explain the process for obtaining and maintaining training certifications.
### 8. **Safety Meeting** (`safety_meeting`, text, optional)
 Describe the safety meetings held by your organization, including their frequency and purpose.
### 9. **Emergency Contact Information** (`emergency_contact_information`, text, optional)
 Provide contact information for emergency situations, including phone numbers and email addresses.
### 10. **Training Location** (`training_location`, text, optional)
 List the locations where training is offered, including addresses and room numbers.
### 11. **Safety Equipment Inspection Frequency** (`safety_equipment_inspection_frequency`, text, optional)
 Outline the frequency of safety equipment inspection and maintenance.
### 12. **Reporting Frequency** (`reporting_frequency`, text, optional)
 Describe the frequency of reporting incidents, including how often and who is responsible.
### 13. **Reporting Method** (`reporting_method`, select_one, optional)
 Select one of the following methods for reporting incidents:
	* Email
	* Phone
	* Text Message
	* In Person
### 14. **Reporting Supervisor** (`reporting_supervisor`, text, optional)
 Identify the supervisor responsible for receiving incident reports.
### 15. **Reporting Frequency Unit** (`reporting_frequency_unit`, text, optional)
 Describe the unit or team responsible for reporting frequency.
### 16. **Reporting Unit** (`reporting_unit`, text, optional)
 List the unit or team responsible for reporting incidents.
### 17. **Reporting Supervisor Name** (`reporting_supervisor_name`, text, optional)
 Enter the name of the supervisor responsible for receiving incident reports.
### 18. **Incident Reporting** (`incident_reporting`, select_one, optional)
 Select one of the following options for incident reporting:
	* Yes
	* No
### 19. **Incident Reported To** (`incident_reported_to`, select_one, optional)
 Select one of the following options for where incidents are reported:
	* Yes
	* No
### 20. **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, optional)
 Enter the date of the incident, if applicable.
### 21. **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, optional)
 Enter the time of the incident, if applicable.
### 22. **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, optional)
 Provide a detailed description of the incident, including any injuries or damage.
### 23. **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, optional)
 Describe the location of the incident.
### 24. **Incident Injured Participants** (`incident_injured_participants`, text, optional)
 List the individuals injured in the incident.

Note that some fields are optional and should be filled only when necessary. Please review the form carefully to ensure you have all the required information.
