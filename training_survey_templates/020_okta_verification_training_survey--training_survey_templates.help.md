# okta_verification_training_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the quality, clarity, and confidence of an Okta verification training program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant ID on page 1.
2. Answer each of the 8 questions on pages 2-9, rating the quality, clarity, and confidence of the training program for each question.
3. Submit the form to provide your feedback.
