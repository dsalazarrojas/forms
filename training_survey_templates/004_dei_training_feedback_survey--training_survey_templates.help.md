# DEI Training Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The DEI Training Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback from attendees about their experiences with the Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI) training sessions. Your feedback will help us evaluate the effectiveness of the training sessions and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the session for which you are providing feedback.
2. Answer all the questions in the form.
3. Provide detailed and specific feedback where possible.
4. Submit your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was the main purpose of this training session?** (`survey_purpose`, text, optional): This question helps us understand the main objective of the training session.
* **Which of the sessions did you attend?** (`session_attendance`, select multiple, optional): Please select all the sessions you attended.
* **What date did you attend the session?** (`session_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the session you attended.
* **What time did you attend the session?** (`session_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the session you attended.
* **How many hours did the session last?** (`session_length`, select multiple, optional): Select the duration of the session from the available options.
* **What was the name of the trainer?** (`trainer_name`, text, optional): Provide the name of the trainer.
* **Where was the session held?** (`session_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the session.
* **What was the main content of the session?** (`session_content`, text, optional): Describe the main content of the session.
* **How would you rate this session?** (`feedback`, select one, optional): Rate the session from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Do you have any comments about the session?** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions about the session.
* **How would you rate the trainer's performance?** (`trainer_performance`, select one, optional): Rate the trainer's performance from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **How would you rate the quality of the materials?** (`material_quality`, select one, optional): Rate the quality of the materials from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **What would you suggest for future improvements?** (`session_improvement`, text, optional): Provide suggestions for future improvements.
* **How satisfied were you with this session?** (`overall_satisfaction`, select one, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the session from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **How often would you like to see this session offered again?** (`session_frequency`, select multiple, optional): Select how often you would like to see this session offered.
* **Do you want to continue with this type of session?** (`session_continuation`, select multiple, optional): Select whether you want to continue with this type of session.
* **How relevant was the session to your work?** (`session_relevance`, select one, optional): Rate how relevant the session was to your work from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Would you attend this session again?** (`session_repetition`, select one, optional): Select whether you would attend this session again.
* **Would you recommend this session to others?** (`session_recommend`, select one, optional): Select whether you would recommend this session to others.
* **Was this survey completed on your own or with assistance?** (`survey_completed`, select multiple, optional): Select how you completed the survey.
* **How long did it take you to complete this survey?** (`survey_duration`, select multiple, optional): Select the duration it took you to complete the survey.
* **What device did you use to complete this survey?** (`survey_device`, select multiple, optional): Select the device you used to complete the survey.
* **Where did you complete this survey?** (`survey_location`, text, optional): Provide the location where you completed the survey.
* **Is there anything else you would like to tell us?** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
