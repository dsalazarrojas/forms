# Cross Training Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Cross Training Feedback Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from trainees about their training experience. This feedback is essential for trainers and training managers to evaluate the effectiveness of the training programs and make necessary improvements. Please provide your honest feedback to help us improve our training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in the text box (page 1).
2. Select your response to "How was the training?" using the options provided (page 2).
3. Add any additional comments or feedback in the text box (page 3).
4. Enter your email address (page 4).
5. Enter your phone number (page 5).
6. Rate your overall experience with a number between 1 and 5 (page 6).
7. Provide the name of the training program you attended (page 7).
