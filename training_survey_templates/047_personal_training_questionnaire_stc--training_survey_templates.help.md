# Personal Training Questionnaire STC - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users about their personal training goals, habits, and preferences. The goal is to help trainers understand the individual's requirements and create a tailored training plan. The information collected will be kept confidential and not shared without your consent.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your personal details.
2. For fields with options, select only one option that best describes your current state.
3. For fields with text input, enter your answer in the corresponding field.
4. You can skip fields that are not relevant to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, Optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, Optional): Enter your current address.
* **Birth Date** (`birthdate`, `date`, Optional): Enter your birth date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, Optional): Enter your age.
* **Goal 1** (`goal_one`, `text`, Optional): Enter your primary training goal.
* **Goal 2** (`goal_two`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select one or more training goals (e.g., Running, Swimming, Cycling, etc.).
* **Height** (`height`, `number`, Optional): Enter your height in centimeters.
* **Weight** (`weight`, `number`, Optional): Enter your weight.
* **Marital Status** (`marital_status`, `select_one`, Optional): Select your marital status.
* **Education** (`education`, `select_one`, Optional): Select your level of education completed.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, `select_one`, Optional): Select your current occupation.
* **Training Goals** (`training_goals`, `select_one`, Optional): Select your training goals (e.g., Strength Training, Cardio, Endurance, etc.).
* **Training Frequency** (`training_frequency`, `select_one`, Optional): Select your training frequency.
* **Training Intensity** (`training_intensity`, `select_one`, Optional): Select your training intensity level.
* **Training Type** (`training_type`, `select_one`, Optional): Select your preferred training type.
* **Training Level** (`training_level`, `select_one`, Optional): Select your training level.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, Optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_name`, `text`, Optional): Enter your emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_phone`, `text`, Optional): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, `select_one`, Optional): Select your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_address`, `text`, Optional): Enter your emergency contact's address.
* **Goal 3** (`goal_three`, `text`, Optional): Enter another goal or additional information.

Note: The fields marked as Required are mandatory fields, while the Optional fields can be skipped.
