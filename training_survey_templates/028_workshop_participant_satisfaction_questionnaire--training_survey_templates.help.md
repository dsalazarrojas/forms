# Workshop Participant Satisfaction Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from workshop participants about their experience with the workshop session they attended.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant options and provide your feedback in the text fields. You can skip any questions that do not apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Session** (`workshop_session`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you attended the workshop session (Yes/No).
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of session you attended (Yes/No).
* **Session Length** (`session_length`, number, required/optional): Enter the length of the session (if applicable).
* **Workshop Facilitator** (`workshop_facilitator`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether the workshop facilitator was effective (Yes/No).
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, required/optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the workshop on a scale of 0-5.
* **Content Learning** (`content_learning`, number, required/optional): Rate how much you learned from the workshop content on a scale of 0-5.
* **Facilitation Skills** (`facilitation_skills`, number, required/optional): Rate the workshop facilitator's skills on a scale of 0-5.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required/optional): Enter any information about the organization that hosted the workshop.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, required/optional): Enter any suggestions you have for improving the workshop.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments about your experience with the workshop.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide constructive feedback.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, please enter them in the "Suggestions" field.
* If you have any additional comments, please enter them in the "Comments" field.
