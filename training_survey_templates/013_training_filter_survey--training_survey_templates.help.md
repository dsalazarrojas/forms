# Training Filter Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Training Filter Survey is designed to help you categorize and narrow down training sessions based on a series of filters. This form asks you to indicate which filters apply to your training needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the provided filters and read each label carefully.
2. For each filter, select "Yes" if it applies to your training needs, or "No" if it does not.
3. Use the notes section to add any additional information or context if needed.
4. Review your selections before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Filter 1**: "Filter 1" - A binary choice between "Yes" and "No".
* **Filter 2**: "Filter 2" - A binary choice between "Yes" and "No".
* **Filter 3**: "Filter 3" - A binary choice between "Yes" and "No".
* ...
* **Filter 19**: "Filter 19" - A binary choice between "Yes" and "No".
* **Notes**: "Notes" - A text field for any additional information or context.
* **Submit**: "Submit" - Submit the form once you have finished selecting all applicable filters and have added any necessary notes.

**Tips**:
* Make sure to review each filter carefully to ensure you're selecting the correct options.
* Use the notes field to add any additional context or clarification.
* Review your selections before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any filter, you can always ask for clarification or guidance.
