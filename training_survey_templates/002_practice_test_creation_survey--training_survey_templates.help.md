# Practice Test Creation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the creation and administration of practice tests and training programs within an institution or organization. It helps to identify challenges, improve the quality of practice tests, and optimize training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Practice Test Creation" checkbox if you are involved in creating or administering practice tests.
2. Indicate whether educators, tutors, or training teams are involved in the process.
3. Choose the status of practice exams in your institution or organization.
4. Identify any challenges you've encountered while creating practice tests.
5. Prioritize improvements for future practice tests.
6. Provide additional comments or information about your experience with practice tests.
7. Determine if a specific tool has been assigned for creating practice tests.
8. Decide on the use of JotForm survey templates for practice tests.
9. Report the status of practice tests, including whether they've been assigned challenges.
10. Provide a detailed description of any challenges identified during practice test creation.
