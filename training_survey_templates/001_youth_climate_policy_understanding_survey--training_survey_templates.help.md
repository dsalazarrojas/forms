# Youth Climate Policy Understanding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your views and engagement with climate policy. Please take a few minutes to complete it, and we will use your feedback to better understand your perspective.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read the introduction carefully, which provides context for the questions that follow.
2. Answer each question honestly, and feel free to elaborate on your responses if you'd like.
3. Select all the options that apply to your situation for multiple-choice questions.
4. You can skip any questions that do not apply to you or your situation.
5. When finished, review your answers to ensure they accurately reflect your views.
