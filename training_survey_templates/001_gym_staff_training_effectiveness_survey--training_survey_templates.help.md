# Gym Staff Training Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Gym Staff Training Effectiveness Survey is a tool used to evaluate the effectiveness of training sessions provided to gym staff. It helps to identify areas of improvement and gather feedback from staff members to make training more engaging, relevant, and valuable to their jobs. By completing this survey, you will be contributing to the betterment of our training program and helping us improve your professional development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the relevant option that best matches your experience with the training sessions.
3. For questions that allow text responses, provide a brief but descriptive answer.
4. Make sure to complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Quality of Training** (`staff_training_overall_quality`): This question asks about your general impression of the quality of the training sessions. Please select the option that best describes your experience.
	+ Good: The training sessions were well-structured, engaging, and provided valuable information.
	+ Fair: The training sessions were somewhat satisfactory, but could be improved.
	+ Poor: The training sessions were not effective or useful.
* **How often do you attend training sessions?** (`training_frequency`): This question is about how frequently you participate in training sessions. Choose the option that best represents your attendance.
	+ Every week: You regularly attend training sessions.
	+ Every 2 weeks: You occasionally attend training sessions.
	+ Every month: You infrequently attend training sessions.
	+ Rarely: You rarely or never attend training sessions.
* **What topics do you typically attend training on?** (`training_topics`): Select all topics that you usually attend training on.
	+ Management: Training on management-related topics, such as leadership and communication.
	+ Operations: Training on operational-related topics, such as workflows and processes.
	+ Technical: Training on technical-related topics, such as software and equipment usage.
	+ Other: Select this option if you typically attend training on other topics.
* **How would you rate the relevance of the training to your job?** (`training_relevance`): This question is about how relevant the training sessions are to your job role. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
	+ Good: The training sessions are directly applicable to your job.
	+ Fair: The training sessions are somewhat relevant, but could be more focused.
	+ Poor: The training sessions are not relevant to your job.
* **How often do you apply the skills learned from training in your work?** (`skill_application`): This question is about how frequently you apply the skills and knowledge gained from training in your work.
	+ Every day: You regularly use the skills learned from training.
	+ Every week: You occasionally use the skills learned from training.
	+ Every 2 weeks: You infrequently use the skills learned from training.
	+ Rarely: You rarely or never use the skills learned from training.
* **What feedback do you receive on your performance after training?** (`feedback_performance`): This question is about the feedback you receive on your performance after attending training sessions. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
	+ Regularly: You receive regular feedback on your performance.
	+ Occasionally: You receive occasional feedback on your performance.
	+ Rarely: You rarely or never receive feedback on your performance.
	+ Never: You have never received feedback on your performance.
* **How would you rate the effectiveness of the training in improving your job performance?** (`effectiveness_improvement`): This question is about how effective the training sessions are in improving your job performance. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
	+ Good: The training sessions were effective in improving your performance.
	+ Fair: The training sessions were somewhat effective, but could be more effective.
	+ Poor: The training sessions were not effective in improving your performance.
* **How often do you get to practice what you learned from training?** (`practice_opportunity`): This question is about how frequently you get to practice the skills and knowledge gained from training. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
	+ Every day: You frequently get to practice what you learned from training.
	+ Every week: You occasionally get to practice what you learned from training.
	+ Every 2 weeks: You infrequently get to practice what you learned from training.
	+ Rarely: You rarely or never get to practice what you learned from training.
* **What is the most valuable aspect of the training provided?** (`value_aspect`): This question is about the most valuable part of the training sessions. Please provide a brief but descriptive answer.
* **How often do you get to use the training materials after training?** (`materials_use`): This question is about how frequently you use the training materials after attending training sessions. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
	+ Every day: You frequently use the training materials.
	+ Every week: You occasionally use the training materials.
	+ Every 2 weeks: You infrequently use the training materials.
	+ Rarely: You rarely or never use the training materials.
* **What would you recommend to improve the training?** (`recommendations`): This question is about suggestions you have to improve the training sessions. Please provide a brief but descriptive answer.
* **Do you think the training is worth your time?** (`worth_time`): This question is about your overall perception of the training sessions. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
	+ True: You think the training is worth your time.
	+ False: You do not think the training is worth your time.
