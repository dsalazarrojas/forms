# Training Effectiveness Insight Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather your feedback on the effectiveness of our training programs. Your input will help us understand what works well and what areas need improvement, allowing us to refine our training initiatives and better serve our learners. Your participation is optional and anonymous. We want to ensure that you're not duplicating efforts by taking this survey if you've already provided similar feedback in the past. If you're unsure if this survey is relevant to your training experience, please refer to your training schedule or recent communication from your manager or L&D department.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide accurate feedback, please take your time to answer each question thoroughly. You can navigate through the form using the "Next" and "Previous" buttons. If you need to go back to a previous page, you can click on the "Back" button. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your manager or L&D department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. What is your role? 
**Role** (`HR`, `L&D`, `Operations`, `Admin`, `IT`): Please select the role that best describes your position within the organization.

### 2. Which training category do you belong to?
**Category** (`Compliance Training`, `Leadership Development`, `Soft Skills`, `Technical Skills`, `Mandatory`): Please choose the category that corresponds to the type of training you've undergone.

### 3. How would you rate the quality of this training program?
**Program Quality** (`1-5`): Rate the quality of the training program using the provided scale, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".

### 4. What was the outcome of this training?
**Outcome** (`Improved skills`, `Changed behavior`, `Increased motivation`, `Enhanced job satisfaction`, `Increased productivity`): Select all the outcomes that apply to your experience with this training.

### 5. Do you want to provide feedback on this training?
**Feedback** (`True` or `False`): If you'd like to provide feedback, please select "True". If not, please select "False".

### 6. What was your experience with this training?
**Experience** (Note): Share your thoughts on your experience with this training. This field is optional, but your feedback will help us better understand your perspective.

### 7. What value did this training add to your work?
**Value** (`Improved performance`, `Increased efficiency`, `Enhanced creativity`, `Better collaboration`, `Better time management`): Select all the values that apply to your experience with this training.

### 8. How satisfied are you with this training?
**Satisfaction** (`1-5`): Rate your satisfaction with the training program, where 1 is "Very dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very satisfied".

### 9. Do you have any additional comments about this training?
**Additional Comments** (Note): If you have any thoughts or suggestions that weren't covered by the previous questions, please share them here.

### 10. What is your Email address?
**Email Address** (`Your email address`): Please enter your work email address for contact purposes.

### 11. What is your phone number?
**Phone** (`Your phone number`): Enter your work phone number for communication purposes.

### 12. Submit your survey
**Submit** (`Submit`): Once you've completed the survey, click "Submit" to send your feedback.
