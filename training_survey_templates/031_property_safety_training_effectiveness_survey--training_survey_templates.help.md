# Property Safety Training Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from property managers on the effectiveness of the property safety training provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer all the questions truthfully and in detail.
2. For each question, provide feedback on the provided training.
3. Do not skip any questions as all fields are important to evaluate the effectiveness of the training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Property Manager Rating (`property_manager_rating`, number, optional):** 
    This question asks how you rate the overall quality of the training provided as a property manager.
    
- **Training Quality Rating (`training_quality_rating`, number, optional):** 
    This question asks how you rate the quality of the training provided.
    
- **Relevance Rating (`relevance_rating`, number, optional):** 
    This question asks how relevant you found the training to be to your work as a property manager.
    
- **Confidence Level (`confidence_level`, number, optional):** 
    This question asks how confident you are in your ability to apply the knowledge and skills learned during the training.
    
- **Training Effectiveness (`training_effectiveness`, time, optional):** 
    This question asks how long you think the training was effective in helping you perform your duties as a property manager.
    
- **Property Safety Training Recommendation (`property_safety_training_recommendation`, select one, required):** 
    This question asks for your recommendation on how effective you think the training was.
    
- **Additional Comments (`additional_comments`, text, optional):** 
    This question gives you an opportunity to add any additional comments or suggestions.
    
- **Additional Information (`property_safety_training_effectiveness_survey_additional_info`, text, optional):** 
    This question gives you an opportunity to add any additional information about the training.
    
- **Confidence (`property_safety_training_effectiveness_survey_confidence`, number, optional):** 
    This question asks for your confidence level in your ability to apply the knowledge and skills learned during the training.
