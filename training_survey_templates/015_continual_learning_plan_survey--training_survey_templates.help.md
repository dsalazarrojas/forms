# Continual Learning Plan Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather information on your learning needs and preferences to help us create a comprehensive plan for your development and growth within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name and employee ID in the first two fields.
2. Select your department and job title from the dropdown menus.
3. Choose your years of service and current skill level.
4. Specify your learning goals, areas of interest, and preferred learning methods.
5. Indicate your available time for learning and your estimated training budget.
6. Express your interest in certifications and specify the type if applicable.
7. Share your career aspirations in 5 years and indicate any barriers to your learning.
8. Choose your manager's level of support for your development.
9. Select a timeline for your learning plan.
10. Add any additional comments or thoughts about your development and growth.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or business card.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): Provide your employee identification number as per your company's ID system.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Choose the department you belong to (Human Resources, Information Technology, Marketing, Sales, Operations, Finance, Customer Service, Research and Development, Other).
* **Position Title** (`position_title`, text, required): Enter your job title as it appears on your ID or business card.
* **Years with Organization** (`years_with_organization`, select_one, required): Choose the correct tenure category (Less than 1 year, 1-3 years, 3-5 years, 5-10 years, 10+ years).
* **Current Skill Level** (`current_skill_level`, select_one, required): Select your self-assessed proficiency level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Expert).
* **Learning Goals** (`learning_goals`, text, required): Briefly describe what you want to learn or develop.
* **Skill Areas of Interest** (`skill_areas_of_interest`, select_multiple, required): Select all the areas you want to develop or improve (Leadership, Communication, Technical skills, Project management, Data analysis, Customer service, Sales techniques, Software proficiency, Creative skills, Industry knowledge).
* **Preferred Learning Method** (`preferred_learning_method`, select_multiple, required): Choose how you prefer to learn (Online courses, In-person training, Workshops, Conferences, Mentoring, Self-study, On-the-job training, Webinars).
* **Learning Time Availability** (`learning_time_availability`, select_one, required): Select the number of hours you can dedicate to learning per week (1-2 hours, 2-5 hours, 5-10 hours, 10+ hours).
* **Training Budget Needed** (`training_budget_needed`, select_one, required): Estimate the budget needed for your development (Under $500, $500-$1000, $1000-$2500, $2500-$5000, Over $5000).
* **Certification Interest** (`certification_interest`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you are interested in certifications (Yes, actively pursuing, Yes, in the future, No, not interested).
* **Certification Type** (`certification_type`, text, optional): If you expressed interest in certifications, specify the type.
* **Career Aspirations** (`career_aspirations`, text, required): Describe where you see yourself in 5 years.
* **Development Support Needed** (`development_support_needed`, select_multiple, required): Select all the support you need for your development (Time off for training, Financial assistance, Access to resources, Mentor assignment, Flexible schedule, Study materials).
* **Previous Training Completed** (`previous_training_completed`, text, optional): List any recent training or development activities you have completed.
* **Training Barriers** (`training_barriers`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate any barriers preventing you from learning (Lack of time, Cost, No available courses, Workload, Family commitments, Lack of motivation, None).
* **Manager Support** (`manager_support`, select_one, required): Choose your manager's level of support for your development (Very supportive, Somewhat supportive, Neutral, Not supportive).
* **Learning Timeline** (`learning_timeline`, select_one, required): Select when you want to start your learning plan (Immediately, Within 1 month, Within 3 months, Within 6 months, Within 1 year).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any other thoughts or comments about your development and growth.

Note: The fields with optional labels are not required to be filled in. 

Tips: Please be honest about your needs and preferences, and provide detailed answers whenever possible. The information collected will help us create a comprehensive learning plan that suits your development and growth within the organization. 

This guide is not intended to be a replacement for the actual form but a supporting resource for understanding the fields and their purpose. If you have any further questions or need clarification on any field, please reach out to us.
