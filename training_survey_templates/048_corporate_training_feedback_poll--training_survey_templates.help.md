# Corporate Training Feedback Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from employees after attending a training session. The feedback will help in evaluating the effectiveness of the training and making necessary improvements.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields with the relevant information.
2.  Choose the correct option from the dropdown menu when prompted.
3.  Provide feedback comments if you have any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Corporate Training Feedback Poll** (`title`, `text`, `required=true`): This is the title of the form and is a required field.
*   **course_id** (`course_id`, `number`, `required=true`): Enter the ID of the course you attended.
*   **Course Title** (`course_title`, `text`, `required=true`): Enter the title of the course.
*   **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, `required=true`): Choose the department you belong to.
*   **Training Facilitator** (`training_facilitator`, `text`, `required=true`): Enter the name of the training facilitator.
*   **Course Date** (`course_date`, `date`, `required=true`): Enter the date of the course.
*   **Rating (1-5)** (`rating`, `number`, `required=true`): Rate the course on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Feedback Comments** (`feedback_comments`, `note`, `required=false`): Provide any additional comments about the course.
*   **Department Lead** (`department_lead`, `text`, `required=true`): Enter the name of your department lead.
*   **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `text`, `required=true`): Enter your employee ID.
*   **Training Session** (`training_session`, `select_one`, `required=true`): Choose the type of training session you attended (Workshop, Course, or Workshop and Course).
*   **Session ID** (`session_id`, `text`, `required=true`): Enter the ID of the training session.
*   **Session Start Date** (`session_start_date`, `date`, `required=true`): Enter the start date of the training session.
*   **Session End Date** (`session_end_date`, `date`, `required=true`): Enter the end date of the training session.
*   **Session Location** (`session_location`, `text`, `required=true`): Enter the location of the training session.
*   **Course Length** (`course_length`, `number`, `required=true`): Enter the length of the course.
*   **Attended** (`attended`, `select_one`, `required=true`): Choose if you attended the course (Yes or No).
*   **Attended Session** (`attended_session`, `select_one`, `required=true`): Choose if you attended the training session (Yes or No).
*   **Attended Training** (`attended_training`, `select_one`, `required=true`): Choose if you attended the training (Yes or No).
*   **Attended Workshop** (`attended_workshop`, `select_one`, `required=true`): Choose if you attended the workshop (Yes or No).
*   **Attended Course** (`attended_course`, `select_one`, `required=true`): Choose if you attended the course (Yes or No).
*   **Attended Workshop and Course** (`attended_workshop_course`, `select_one`, `required=true`): Choose if you attended both workshop and course (Yes or No).
*   **Attended Workshop and Course** (`attended_workshop_course_training`, `select_one`, `required=true`): Choose if you attended both workshop and course (Yes or No).
*   **Session Evaluations** (`session_evaluated`, `number`, `required=true`): Enter the number of session evaluations.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, `required=false`): Provide any additional comments about the session.

## Tips

*   Please make sure to fill in all the required fields.
*   Choose the correct option from the dropdown menu when prompted.
*   If you have any additional comments or feedback, provide them in the "Comments" field.
*   Be as specific as possible when filling in the form to ensure accurate feedback.
