# Corporate Training Material Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on corporate training material to help improve its quality and relevance.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the provided instructions and guidelines for each field.
2. Complete all required fields (indicated by a star symbol) with accurate and clear information.
3. Fill out the form completely to ensure that the feedback collected is comprehensive.

* **Corporate Training Material Feedback Questionnaire (1)** (`form_title`, required, *): This is the title of the training material feedback questionnaire.
* **Category (2)** (`form_category`, required, select_one): Select the category to which this feedback pertains, either 'Training Survey Templates' or 'Other'.
* **Description (3)** (`form_description`, required, text): Provide a brief description of the training material being reviewed.
* **Assigned Tool (4)** (`form_assigned_tool`, required, select_one): Choose the tool assigned to this training material, either 'chatjimmy' or 'other'.
* **File (5)** (`form_file`, optional, text): If applicable, provide the file name or URL of the training material being reviewed.
* **Email (6)** (`form_email`, optional, email): If applicable, provide the email address associated with this feedback.
* **Phone (7)** (`form_phone`, optional, text): If applicable, provide the phone number associated with this feedback.
* **Comments (8)** (`form_comments`, optional, text): Provide any additional comments or suggestions regarding the training material.
* **Form ID (9)** (`form_form_id`, required, number): Enter a unique identifier for this form, which helps track the feedback.
* **Form Title (10)** (`form_form_title`, required, text): Enter the title of the form being reviewed.
* **Form Description (11)** (`form_form_description`, required, text): Enter a brief description of the form being reviewed.
* **Form Category (12)** (`form_form_category`, required, select_one): Select the category to which this form belongs, either 'Training Survey Templates' or 'Other'.
* **Form Category (13)** (`form_form_category_other`, optional, text): If 'Other' is selected for the form category, provide a brief description of the category.
* **Form Assigned Tool (14)** (`form_form_assigned_tool`, required, select_one): Choose the tool assigned to this form, either 'chatjimmy' or 'other'.
* **Form File (15)** (`form_form_file`, optional, text): If applicable, provide the file name or URL of the form being reviewed.
* **Form Email (16)** (`form_form_email`, optional, email): If applicable, provide the email address associated with this feedback.
* **Form Phone (17)** (`form_phone`, optional, text): If applicable, provide the phone number associated with this feedback.
* **Form Comments (18)** (`form_comments`, optional, text): Provide any additional comments or suggestions regarding the form.
