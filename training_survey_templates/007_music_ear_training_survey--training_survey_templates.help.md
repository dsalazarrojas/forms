# Music Ear Training Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Ear Training Survey is a general survey to gauge the state of music ear training in the community. It aims to gather information about your current skills and methods used for ear training, as well as any challenges you face and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section with your responses to the questions asked. The form is divided into several sections, each with its own set of questions. You can fill out the form on one page at a time, and review your responses before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Which skills do you currently use?** (`skills_question`, `select_multiple`): This question asks about your current skills used for music ear training. You can select one or multiple options from the list provided.
* **What methods do you currently use?** (`methods_question`, `select_multiple`): This question asks about your current methods used for music ear training. You can select one or multiple options from the list provided.
* **What challenges do you face?** (`challenges_question`, `text`): This question asks about any challenges you face during music ear training. Type your answers in the text field provided.
* **Any suggestions for improvement?** (`suggestions_question`, `text`): This question asks for any suggestions you may have for improving music ear training. Type your answers in the text field provided.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `email`): This question asks for your contact information, which will help us reach out to you with further information or opportunities related to music ear training. Type your email address in the field provided.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`): This is an optional section where you can add any additional comments or thoughts about music ear training. Type your answers in the text field provided.
