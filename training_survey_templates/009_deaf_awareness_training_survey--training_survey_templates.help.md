# Deaf Awareness Training Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback from participants on their experiences with deaf awareness training. Your input is valuable in helping us understand how to improve our training programs and better serve the deaf community.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form by providing your name and email address (if you choose to provide it).
2.  Select the type of training you participated in.
3.  Indicate how many hours you spent on the training.
4.  Rate your overall satisfaction with the training.
5.  Answer if your knowledge about deaf awareness has changed since the training.
6.  Provide any feedback or comments about your experience.
7.  Optionally, provide additional comments or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name to help us identify you in our records.
*   **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address to receive notifications or follow up from us.
*   **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): This field is optional, but if you have a preferred phone number, you can enter it here.
*   **Which type of training did you participate in?** (`training_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of deaf awareness training you participated in from the list.
*   **How many hours did you participate in?** (`training_length`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of hours you spent on the training.
*   **Overall satisfaction with training** (`satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the training on a scale from High, Medium, or Low.
*   **Has your knowledge about Deaf Awareness changed since the training?** (`knowledge_change`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Answer whether your knowledge about deaf awareness has changed since the training.
*   **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): Share any feedback or comments about your experience.
*   **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or information you think would be helpful.
*   **Category** (`category`, `text`, required: false): Enter a category or label for your entry.
*   **Tag** (`tag`, `text`, required: false): Enter a tag or keyword for your entry.
*   **Tag 2** (`tag_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter another tag or keyword for your entry.
*   **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter a participant ID if you have one.
*   **Created At** (`created_at`, `time`, required: false): The date and time this entry was created.
*   **Updated At** (`updated_at`, `time`, required: false): The date and time this entry was updated.
*   **Deleted At** (`deleted_at`, `time`, required: false): The date and time this entry was deleted.
*   **Participant Status** (`participant_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the status of the participant.
*   **Training Status** (`training_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the status of the training.
*   **When was training completed?** (`training_completed`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date and time the training was completed.
*   **When was training ended?** (`training_ended`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date and time the training ended.
*   **When did participant end?** (`participant_ended`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date and time the participant ended.
*   **When did training start?** (`training_start`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date and time the training started.
*   **When did training end?** (`training_end`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date and time the training ended.
*   **How did you hear about this survey?** (`survey_response`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the way you heard about this survey.

Note: The fields marked as "required: false" are optional and you can skip them if you're not comfortable providing the information.
