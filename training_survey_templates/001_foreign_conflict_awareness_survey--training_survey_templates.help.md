# Foreign Conflict Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Foreign Conflict Awareness Survey is a form designed to gather information about users' awareness and perceptions of conflicts in various contexts. This form aims to assess your understanding of conflicts, their sources, and potential resolutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you're filling out the form for the correct purpose and that your responses are original.
2. Read each field explanation below and fill out the form accordingly.
3. Complete all required fields (marked as `required` in the form).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Conflict Awareness (Field 1)
* **Conflict Awareness** (`title`, `number`, `required`): This is the first section of the form, which asks you about your awareness of conflicts in general. You will be asked how many conflicts you are aware of.

### Information Sources (Field 2)
* **Information Sources** (`information_sources`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Please select all sources of information you use to be aware of conflicts:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3

### Perceived Consequences (Field 3)
* **Perceived Consequences** (`perceived_consequences`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the option that best describes your perception of the consequences of conflicts:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### Public Perception (Field 4)
* **Public Perception** (`public_perception`, `date`, `required`: This is a date field. Enter the date when you last became aware of public perception on conflicts.

### Public Perception Hint (Field 5)
* **Public Perception Hint** (`public_perception_hint`, `note`, `optional`): Please add any additional information about public perception.

### Phone Number (Field 6)
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, `required`): Enter your phone number where you can be reached if needed.

### Email Address (Field 7)
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, `optional`): Enter your email address where you can be reached if needed.

### Conflict Location (Field 8)
* **Conflict Location** (`conflict_location`, `select_multiple`, `optional`): Please select all conflict locations that apply:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4

### Conflict Type (Field 9)
* **Conflict Type** (`conflict_type`, `select_one`, `optional`): Select the type of conflict you are aware of:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### Conflict Description (Field 10)
* **Conflict Description** (`conflict_description`, `text`, `required`): Describe the conflict you are aware of in detail.

### Conflict Date (Field 11)
* **Conflict Date** (`conflict_date`, `time`, `optional`): Enter the date of the conflict, if applicable.

### Resolution Type (Field 12)
* **Resolution Type** (`resolution_type`, `select_one`, `optional`): Select the resolution type you think is most likely for the conflict:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### Tips
* Ensure you fill out all required fields accurately.
* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out for clarification.
* This form is for original assessments, so please do not duplicate your submissions.
