# Fitness Performance Training Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help evaluate an individual's fitness performance training goals, progress, and habits. It aims to collect information on their motivation, workout frequency, and additional comments. This data will be used to help create a personalized training plan to achieve their fitness goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Start by filling out the "Main Page" section with your answers to the following questions:
   - How many times do you work out per week?
   - Do you do strength training exercises?
2. If you want to provide more information, you can add additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.
3. Review your answers and make sure to provide accurate and detailed information to ensure that your training plan is tailored to your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### What was your motivation for starting your fitness journey?
* **What was your motivation for starting your fitness journey?** (`select_one`, required/optional): This question helps us understand what drives you to start your fitness journey. Please choose one of the provided options that best describes your motivation.

### How many times do you work out per week?
* **How many times do you work out per week?** (`select_one`, required): This question helps us understand your workout frequency. Please choose how many times you work out in a week from the provided options.

### Do you do strength training exercises?
* **Do you do strength training exercises?** (`select_one`, required): This question helps us understand your workout type. Please choose if you do or do not do strength training exercises.

### How many days do you work out per week?
* **How many days do you work out per week?** (`select_one`, required): This question is similar to the "How many times do you work out per week?" question, but provides more specific information on the number of days worked out.

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`note`, optional): This field is for you to provide any additional comments or information that you think will help us create a more accurate training plan for you. Please be as detailed as possible.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions. This will help us create a training plan that meets your needs.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask your trainer for guidance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to reach out to us.
