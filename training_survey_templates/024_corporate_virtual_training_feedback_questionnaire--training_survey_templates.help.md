# Corporate Virtual Training Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from users about a corporate virtual training session. It is used by the training organizers to evaluate and refine their programs to better meet the needs of their participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the training session you are providing feedback on from the "training_session_id" dropdown menu.
2. Choose the department you belong to from the "department" dropdown menu.
3. Provide any feedback you may have about the training session in the "feedback" text field.
4. Enter the date and time of the training session in the "date" and "time" fields.
5. Enter your email address and phone number in the "email" and "phone" fields.
6. If applicable, provide any additional comments about the session in the "comments" text field.
7. Select the user who submitted the feedback from the "submitted_by" dropdown menu.
8. Choose the department of the user who submitted the feedback from the "submitted_by_department" dropdown menu.
9. Enter the date and time when the feedback was submitted in the "submitted_on" and "submitted_at" fields.
10. Enter the name of the person who submitted the feedback in the "submitted_by_name" text field.
11. Enter the title of the person who submitted the feedback in the "submitted_by_title" text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **introduction** (`1`, `note`, required): This field is a note that serves as an introduction to the form. Please do not complete this field.
* **training_session_id** (`2`, `select_one`, required): Choose the training session you are providing feedback on from the dropdown menu.
* **department** (`3`, `select_one`, required): Choose the department you belong to from the dropdown menu.
* **feedback** (`4`, `text`, required): Provide any feedback you may have about the training session.
* **date** (`5`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the training session.
* **time** (`6`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the training session.
* **email** (`7`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (`8`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **comments** (`9`, `text`, required): If applicable, provide any additional comments about the session.
* **submitted_by** (`10`, `select_one`, required): Select the user who submitted the feedback from the dropdown menu.
* **submitted_by_department** (`11`, `select_one`, required): Choose the department of the user who submitted the feedback from the dropdown menu.
* **submitted_on** (`12`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the feedback was submitted.
* **submitted_at** (`13`, `time`, required): Enter the time when the feedback was submitted.
* **submitted_by_name** (`14`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person who submitted the feedback.
* **submitted_by_title** (`15`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the person who submitted the feedback.
* **session_name** (`16`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the session.
* **session_id** (`17`, `text`, required): Enter the ID of the session.
* **session_time** (`18`, `text`, required): Enter the time of the session.
* **session_location** (`19`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the session.
* **session_facilitators** (`20`, `text`, required): Enter the facilitators of the session.
* **session_goals** (`21`, `text`, required): Enter the goals of the session.
* **session_notes** (`22`, `text`, required): Enter any notes about the session.
* **session_topics** (`23`, `text`, required): Enter the topics covered in the session.
* **submitted_by_phone** (`24`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the feedback.
* **session_name** (`25`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the session.
