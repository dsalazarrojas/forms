> # Medical Simulation Training Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from medical professionals who have participated in a simulation-based training program. The purpose of this form is to understand your experience, satisfaction, and opinions about the simulation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the following questions as honestly and thoroughly as possible. You may choose to skip questions that do not apply to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What was your experience in the simulation?** (`simulation_training_evaluation_form_part_1`): This question is a free-text field where you can describe your overall experience with the simulation. Please be as detailed as possible, but try to keep your answer concise.
- **Simulation Experience** (`simulation_training_evaluation_form_part_2`): This question asks you to rate your experience in the simulation on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is "not satisfied" and 10 is "very satisfied".
- **How would you rate your overall satisfaction with the simulation?** (`simulation_training_evaluation_form_part_3`): Similar to the previous question, this one also asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with the simulation on a scale of 1-10.
- **Was the simulation relevant to your work or field?** (`simulation_training_evaluation_form_part_4`): Please choose "Yes" if the simulation was relevant to your work or field, or "No" if it was not.
- **Simulation Training Evaluation Form Part 5** (`simulation_training_evaluation_form_part_5`): This is another free-text field where you can provide any additional comments, feedback, or suggestions about the simulation.

## Tips
- Answer questions thoroughly and honestly.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Simulation Training Evaluation Form Part 5" field.
- If you need help or have questions about this form, contact us.
- Please keep your answers concise and clear.
