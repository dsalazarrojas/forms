# Creative Software Training Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Creative Software Training Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from participants about their recent training session. This form is intended to help us improve our training sessions and better serve our participants in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Read each field carefully and answer accordingly.
2.  Fill out the form completely, but only answer questions that apply to you.
3.  If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What did you learn from this training session?** (`participant_feedback`, text, required/optional): Please describe what you learned or took away from the training session. This will help us understand what we need to focus on for future sessions.
* **Do you have any suggestions for future training sessions?** (`training_suggestion`, text, required/optional): If you have any ideas or suggestions for improving future training sessions, please share them with us. This will help us make the training more relevant and useful for you and other participants.
* **How can we improve future training sessions?** (`session_improvement`, text, required/optional): Suggest ways we can improve the training session, such as providing more examples, using different teaching methods, or improving the content.
* **How would you rate the instructor?** (`instructor_feedback`, select_one, required/optional): Please rate the instructor's performance, selecting one of the options below:
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Excellent
* **Overall satisfaction with the training session?** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required/optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the training session, selecting one of the options below:
	+ Very dissatisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Very satisfied
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Please enter the date of the training session you attended.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Please enter the time of the training session you attended.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): If you'd like to be contacted with the results of this survey, please enter your email address.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text, required/optional): If you'd like to be contacted with the results of this survey, please enter your phone number.
* **Additional comments** (`note`, note, required/optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please share them with us. 

## Tips

* Please answer all questions thoroughly, but only what you feel comfortable sharing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey! Your feedback is invaluable to us.
