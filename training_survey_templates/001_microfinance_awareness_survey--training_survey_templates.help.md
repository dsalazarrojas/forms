# Microfinance Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to assess your understanding and interest in microfinance services. It will help us to better understand your knowledge, interest, and interaction methods regarding microfinance services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the "Introduction" section, which contains a brief overview of the survey.
2. Proceed to the "Type of Institution" section, where you can select the type of institution you represent (e.g., Banks, Microfinance Institutions, NGOs, etc.).
3. In the "What microfinance services do you offer?" section, select all the services your institution offers (e.g., Savings Accounts, Loans, Payment Services, etc.).
4. For the "How familiar are you with microfinance services?" section, select how familiar you are with the services (e.g., Very Familiar, Somewhat Familiar, Not Familiar).
5. Next, select how interested you are in microfinance services (e.g., Very Interested, Somewhat Interested, Not Interested).
6. Then, choose the method you usually use to contact microfinance services (e.g., Online, Mobile Phone, Bank Branch, etc.).
7. Fill in the "Name of institution" section with the name of your institution.
8. Enter the name of your institution's location.
9. In the "Email" section, enter your institution's email address (optional).
10. Enter your institution's phone number (optional).
11. If you have any comments, please write them in the "Comments" section.
12. Finally, submit the survey by clicking "Submit".


## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (note): This is a brief introduction to the survey.
- **Type of Institution** (select_one): Select the type of institution you represent (e.g., Banks, Microfinance Institutions, NGOs, etc.).
  * Required: No
  * Options: Banks, Microfinance Institutions, NGOs, Other
- **What microfinance services do you offer?** (select_multiple): Select all the services your institution offers (e.g., Savings Accounts, Loans, Payment Services, etc.). 
  * Required: Yes
  * Options: Savings Accounts, Loans, Payment Services, Insurance, Microinsurance, Mobile Money, Payment Gateway
- **How familiar are you with microfinance services?** (select_one): Select how familiar you are with microfinance services (e.g., Very Familiar, Somewhat Familiar, Not Familiar). 
  * Required: Yes
  * Options: Very Familiar, Somewhat Familiar, Not Familiar
- **How interested are you in microfinance services?** (select_one): Select how interested you are in microfinance services (e.g., Very Interested, Somewhat Interested, Not Interested). 
  * Required: Yes
  * Options: Very Interested, Somewhat Interested, Not Interested
- **How do you usually contact microfinance services?** (select_multiple): Select the method you usually use to contact microfinance services (e.g., Online, Mobile Phone, Bank Branch, etc.). 
  * Required: Yes
  * Options: Online, Mobile Phone, Bank Branch, ATM, Other
- **Name of institution** (text): Enter the name of your institution.
  * Required: No
  * Options: None
- **Location** (text): Enter the name of your institution's location.
  * Required: Yes
  * Options: None
- **Email** (email): Enter your institution's email address (optional).
  * Required: No
  * Options: None
- **Phone** (text): Enter your institution's phone number (optional).
  * Required: No
  * Options: None
- **Comments** (note): If you have any comments, write them here.
  * Required: No
  * Options: None
- **Submit** (text): Submit the survey.
  * Required: Yes
  * Options: None
