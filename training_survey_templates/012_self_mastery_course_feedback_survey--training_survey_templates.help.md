# self_mastery_course_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from course participants about their experience with the self-mastery course.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please follow the instructions below.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as required in the form.
3. Be as detailed as possible when answering the feedback questions.
4. Make sure to submit your feedback before leaving this page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Title** (`survey_title`, text, required/optional): This is a title for your feedback. Please enter a short and descriptive title for your feedback.
* **How would you rate the course overall?** (`rating_scale`, number, required/optional): This question will help us understand your overall satisfaction with the course.
* **What did you find most valuable about the course?** (`feedback_question_1`, text, required/optional): Share what you found most helpful or valuable during the course.
* **What would you like to see improved in future courses?** (`feedback_question_2`, text, required/optional): Suggest areas where the course could be improved.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Please enter your email address so we can follow up with you about your feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for our records.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you completed the course.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you completed the course.
* **How would you rate the course content?** (`rating_scale_2`, number, required/optional): Rate the quality of the course content.
* **How would you rate the instructor?** (`rating_scale_3`, number, required/optional): Rate the instructor's performance.
* **How would you rate the materials?** (`rating_scale_4`, number, required/optional): Rate the quality of the course materials.
* **How would you rate the overall experience?** (`rating_scale_5`, number, required/optional): Rate your overall experience with the course.

## Tips
* Be honest and detailed in your feedback.
* Please submit your feedback only once per course completion.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, we welcome them.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
