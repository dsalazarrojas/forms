# manikin_training_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The manikin_training_survey is designed to collect feedback and evaluation from participants after a training session. This form helps our organization to evaluate and improve our training programs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant session to provide feedback for.
2. Provide the participant's name and rating score for the training session.
3. Enter any comments or feedback.
4. Select the facilitator and session type from the provided options.
5. Enter the session's start and end dates and times.
6. Enter the training facilitator's name.
7. Provide the training location (optional).
8. Select if you attended the training session (True or False).
9. Select the participant's name.
10. Enter any additional comments.
11. Select the training outcome (Good, Fair, Satisfactory).
12. Enter the participant's rating score.
13. Enter the overall rating score.
14. Enter any additional comments.
15. Enter the session date and time.
16. Select the training outcome (Good, Fair, Satisfactory).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Name** (`session_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the training session you are providing feedback for.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a participant.
* **Rating Score** (`rating_score`, number, required): Enter a rating score between 1-5.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required): Enter any comments or feedback you have for the training session.
* **Rating Score 2** (`rating_score_2`, number, not required): Enter another rating score between 1-5.
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of the training session.
* **Session Start Date** (`session_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the training session in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Session Start Time** (`session_start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the training session in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Session End Date** (`session_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the training session in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Session End Time** (`session_end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of the training session in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Training Facilitator** (`training_facilitator`, select_one, required): Select the facilitator of the training session.
* **Session Location** (`session_location`, text, not required): Enter the location of the training session (optional).
* **Comments 2** (`comments_2`, text, not required): Enter any additional comments.
* **Training Attendance** (`training_attendance`, select_multiple, required): Select if you attended the training session (True or False).
* **Training Participant** (`training_participant`, select_one, required): Select your name as a participant.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, not required): Enter any additional comments.
* **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, select_multiple, not required): Select any comments or feedback you have as a participant.
* **Training Outcome** (`training_outcome`, select_one, required): Select the outcome of the training session (Good, Fair, Satisfactory).
* **Participant Rating** (`participant_rating`, number, required): Enter a rating score between 1-5.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, required): Enter an overall rating score between 1-5.
* **Comments 3** (`comments_3`, text, not required): Enter any additional comments.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, not required): Enter any additional information.
* **Training Session Date** (`training_session_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the training session in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Training Session Time** (`training_session_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the training session in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
