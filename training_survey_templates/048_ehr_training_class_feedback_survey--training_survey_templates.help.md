# ehr_training_class_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ehr_training_class_feedback_survey form is designed to gather feedback from trainees who have attended a training class. The information collected will help us assess the quality of the class and make improvements for future training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you have the required information ready.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
3. Be honest in your feedback, as it will help us understand your experience and make improvements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a note field where you can provide any additional comments or context about your experience with the class.
* **session_date**: Enter the date of the session you attended.
* **session_attended**: This is a numerical value indicating how many sessions you attended.
* **Rating**: Select all the options that apply to your experience with the class (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4, Option 5).
* **comments**: Enter your detailed comments about your experience with the class.
* **email**: Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for further feedback.
* **name**: Enter your name so we can address you correctly.
* **contact_number**: Enter your contact number so we can contact you if needed.
* **session_facilitator**: Enter the name of the session facilitator.
* **session_location**: Enter the location of the session.
* **class_name**: Enter the name of the class you attended.
* **class_length**: Enter the length of the class (in hours).

Note: Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as "required") to ensure your feedback is complete and accurate.
