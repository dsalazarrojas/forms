# Dog Training Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dog Training Evaluation Form is a tool used to assess the progress and effectiveness of a dog training program. It collects information about the dog's behavior, training goals, and the owner's information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "First Page" section, which is optional but can be used to provide any additional information about the training session.
2. Move on to the "Dog Information" section, where you can enter details about the dog, such as its breed, age, and size.
3. In the "Owner Information" section, enter your contact information as the dog's owner or trainer.
4. Describe the "Training Goals" section by specifying what the dog is being trained for (e.g., basic obedience, agility, or behavioral issues).
5. The "Behavior Assessments" section is for evaluating the dog's behavior before and after training, using a scale from 1-5, where 1 is "Not a problem" and 5 is "Severe problem".
6. In the "Training Needs" section, provide a brief description of what the dog needs to improve its behavior or training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Page** (optional): This section allows you to provide any additional comments or information about the training session that is not covered in the other fields.
* **Dog Information**:
	+ *<label>*: Enter your dog's breed, age, and size.
* **Owner Information**:
	+ *<label>*: Enter your name, phone number, and email address as the dog's owner or trainer.
* **Training Goals**:
	+ *<label>*: Specify what the dog is being trained for (e.g., basic obedience, agility, or behavioral issues).
* **Behavior Assessments**:
	+ *<label>*: Evaluate the dog's behavior before and after training using a scale from 1-5, where 1 is "Not a problem" and 5 is "Severe problem".
* **Training Needs**:

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible when describing the dog's behavior and training goals.
- Use the "Behavior Assessments" scale to provide a clear and consistent evaluation of the dog's behavior.
