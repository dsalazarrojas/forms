# Online Professional Skills Course Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and insights on various aspects of online professional skills courses, including course creators, training teams, learner needs, delivery preferences, course planning, learner preferences, delivery frequency, and future course planning. This information will be used to inform decisions or improve the development of online professional skills courses.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the options that best fit your experiences and preferences for each section. Please answer honestly and accurately.
2. Make sure to review each section carefully before submitting the form.
3. You can select multiple options for most fields, so feel free to choose as many as apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Course Creators Survey (Select Multiple)
* **Yes**: You have created online courses in the past or are currently a course creator.
* **No**: You have never created online courses or are not a course creator.

### 2. Training Teams Survey (Select One)
* **Option 1**: You work with or are part of a training team.
* **Option 2**: You are not part of a training team or do not work with one.

### 3. Learner Needs (Select Multiple)
* **Yes**: You have helped learners with development needs in this area before.
* **No**: You do not have experience helping learners with development needs in this area.

### 4. Delivery Preferences (Select One)
* **Yes**: You prefer delivering courses through video.
* **No**: You do not prefer delivering courses through video.

### 5. Course Planning (Number)
* A number between 1-10, indicating how often you plan to deliver your courses.

### 6. Learner Preferences (Select Multiple)
* **Yes**: You typically teach learners with this preference.
* **No**: You do not typically teach learners with this preference.

### 7. Delivery Frequency (Select Multiple)
* **Yes**: You deliver courses frequently (e.g., daily, weekly).
* **No**: You do not deliver courses frequently.

### 8. Future Course Planning (Select Multiple)
* **Yes**: You plan to improve or change your future offerings in this area.
* **No**: You do not plan to improve or change your future offerings.
