# fire_department_orientation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from new recruits during the fire department orientation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the new recruit's information in the "New Recruit Information" section.
2. Provide your emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact" section.
3. Enter your name and phone number in the "Name" and "Phone" fields, respectively.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Select your department from the "Department" dropdown menu.
6. Select all applicable locations from the "Location" dropdown menu.
7. Select all applicable emergency services from the "Emergency Services" dropdown menu.
8. Select all applicable safety prevention options from the "Safety Prevention" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Recruit Information** (`New Recruit Information`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for new recruits to enter their information. Please fill in the required details.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required/optional): Provide emergency contact information for yourself or someone who can reach you in case of an emergency.
* **Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your department from the available options (Emergency Medical Services, Fire Safety and Prevention, Training and Development).
* **Location** (`location`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all locations where you will be working.
* **Emergency Services** (`emergency_services`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all emergency services you are trained in (Fire Suppression, First Aid and Rescue, Hazmat, Investigation).
* **Safety Prevention** (`safety_prevention`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all safety prevention options you are trained in (Safety and Equipment, Fire Safety, Emergency Preparedness, Prevention and Response).

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submission.
* Ensure the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your department's orientation officer.
