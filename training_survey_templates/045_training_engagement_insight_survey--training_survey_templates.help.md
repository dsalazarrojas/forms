# Training Engagement Insight Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide provides an overview of the Training Engagement Insight Survey form and its fields.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the Survey Header page.
2. Enter your name in the Name field.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field.
4. Enter the date of the training session in the Training Date field.
5. Enter the time of the training session in the Training Time field.
6. Enter the duration of the training session in the Duration field.
7. Enter the location of the training session in the Location field.
8. Enter a brief description of the training content in the Training Content field.
9. Enter the type of training received in the Training Type field.
10. Provide feedback on the training session in the Feedback field.
11. Enter the goals and objectives of the training session in the Training Goals field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Header** (id: 1, name: survey_header, type: note): This page serves as a title and introduction to the form.
* **Name** (id: 2, name: name, type: text, required: false): Enter your name to identify yourself as the trainee.
* **Email** (id: 3, name: email, type: email, required: false): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you for further clarification or follow-up.
* **Training Date** (id: 4, name: training_date, type: date, required: false): Enter the date of the training session.
* **Training Time** (id: 5, name: training_time, type: time, required: false): Enter the time of the training session.
* **Duration** (id: 6, name: duration, type: number, required: false): Enter the duration of the training session.
* **Location** (id: 7, name: location, type: text, required: false): Enter the location of the training session.
* **Training Content** (id: 8, name: training_content, type: text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the training content.
* **Training Type** (id: 9, name: training_type, type: text, required: false): Enter the type of training received.
* **Feedback** (id: 10, name: feedback, type: text, required: false): Provide feedback on the training session.
* **Training Goals** (id: 11, name: training_goals, type: text, required: false): Enter the goals and objectives of the training session.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form as thoroughly and accurately as possible to provide valuable insights and feedback.
* If you have already submitted a survey, your responses will not be recorded.
* This form is designed to gather feedback and insights, not to record or store personal or sensitive data.
* All responses will be treated as confidential and will only be used to improve the training programs.
