# Veterinary Continuing Education Course Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from veterinarians about their interests and preferences for continuing education courses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your profession?** (`Yes, No`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the professions that you identify with.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your name for reference purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications about new courses.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Optional field to enter a phone number if you prefer contact via phone.
* **Which topic of the course are you interested in?** (`Yes, No`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the topics you are interested in.
* **Course Topic** (`Yes, No`, select_one, required): Please select the topic that interests you the most.
* **Which format of the course are you interested in?** (`Yes, No`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the formats you are interested in (e.g., online, in-person, online + in-person).
* **Course Format** (`Yes, No`, select_one, required): Please select the format you prefer the most.
* **What is your preferred date of completion?** (`date`, `date`, required): Please enter the date you prefer to complete the course.
* **Date Completion** (`date`, `date`, optional): Optional field to enter an alternate date.
* **What time of day do you prefer to complete?** (`time`, `time`, required): Please enter the time you prefer to complete the course.
* **Time Completion** (`time`, `time`, optional): Optional field to enter an alternate time.
* **How do you prefer to take the course?** (`Yes, No`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the ways you prefer to take the course.
* **Course Mode** (`Yes, No`, select_one, required): Please select the mode you prefer the most.
* **Do you prefer online or on-site training?** (`Yes, No`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the formats you prefer.
* **Mode of Training** (`Yes, No`, select_one, required): Please select the option you prefer.
* **How many hours do you prefer to take?** (`number`, `number`, optional): Optional field to enter the number of hours you prefer.
* **Hours Taken** (`number`, `number`, optional): Optional field to enter the number of hours.
* **How do you prefer to receive feedback?** (`Yes, No`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the ways you prefer to receive feedback.
* **Feedback Preferred** (`Yes, No`, select_one, optional): Please select the preferred method of feedback.
* **How often would you like to receive emails or updates?** (`Yes, No`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the frequencies you prefer.
* **Email Frequency** (`Yes, No`, select_one, optional): Please select the preferred frequency.
* **Preferred email address to send updates to?** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your preferred email address to receive updates.
* **Email Updated** (`text`, `text`, optional): Optional field to enter a preferred email address.
* **Any additional comments or questions?** (`text`, `text`, optional): Please enter any additional comments or questions you may have.

Note: All required fields are marked with an asterisk (*) in the field labels.
