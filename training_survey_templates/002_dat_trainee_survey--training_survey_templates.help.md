# DAT Trainee Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The DAT Trainee Survey is a form designed to collect information from trainees who are undergoing training programs. This form helps in tracking progress, identifying areas for improvement, and providing a record of trainee performance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Email address to ensure we can contact you about your training progress.
3. Select the training program you are currently enrolled in.
4. Enter the date and time you started your training.
5. Add any notes or comments about your training experience.
6. If you have any comments or feedback, please provide them in the "Comments" field.
7. Enter the date and time you submitted this form.
8. If you have a Supervisor, enter their name, email, and phone number.
9. If you have a Reviewer, select their name from the options provided and enter their comments.
10. Finally, enter your Reviewer's name, email, and phone number, and provide their rating of your performance (1-10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Training Program**: Select the training program you are currently enrolled in.
* **Training Date**: Enter the date you started your training.
* **Training Time**: Enter the time you started your training.
* **Note**: Add any notes or comments about your training experience.
* **Comments**: If you have any comments or feedback, provide them here.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Submission Time**: Enter the time you submitted this form.
* **Trainee ID**: Enter your trainee ID number.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor Email**: Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Supervisor Phone**: Enter your supervisor's phone number.
* **Supervisor Comments**: Select any comments from the options provided.
* **Reviewer Name**: Select the name of your reviewer from the options provided.
* **Reviewer Email**: Enter your reviewer's email address.
* **Reviewer Phone**: Enter your reviewer's phone number.
* **Reviewer Comments**: Select any comments from the options provided.
* **Reviewer Rating**: Enter a rating of your performance (1-10).

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your supervisor or reviewer.
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact the training administration.
* If you have completed this form before, please do not resubmit, as this may cause errors in the training system.
