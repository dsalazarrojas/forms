# pet_training_program_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user feedback and satisfaction data from participants of the pet training program. It aims to gather information about the program's effectiveness and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions thoroughly and honestly.
2. Select your ratings and responses carefully, as they will contribute to the overall assessment of the pet training program.
3. Provide your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number, to allow us to reach out to you for further clarification or follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### What is your experience with our pet training program?
* **What is your experience with our pet training program** (`introduction`, text, optional): A text field where you can describe your experience with the pet training program. This field is optional, and you can skip it if you don't have anything to share.

### How would you rate your overall satisfaction?
* **How would you rate your overall satisfaction** (`question1`, select_one, optional): This question asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with the pet training program. You can select from the following options:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied

### What will we improve?
* **What will we improve** (`question2`, text, optional): A text field where you can suggest areas for improvement for the pet training program.

### Contact Us
* **Contact Us** (`question3`, email, optional): This field is for your contact information, where you can provide your email address to allow us to reach out to you for further clarification or follow-up.

### How did you hear about us?
* **How did you hear about us** (`question4`, select_multiple, optional): This question asks you to select the source from which you heard about the pet training program. You can select from the following options:
	+ Google Search
	+ Facebook
	+ Instagram
	+ Referral
	+ Other

### How long have you been using our pet training program?
* **How long have you been using our pet training program?** (`question5`, select_one, optional): This question asks you to rate your tenure with the pet training program. You can select from the following options:
	+ 1-2 years
	+ 2-5 years
	+ 5+ years

### Question 6

### Question 7
