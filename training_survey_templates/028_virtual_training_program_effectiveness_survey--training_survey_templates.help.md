# Virtual Training Program Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the effectiveness of a virtual training program. The purpose of this form is to help the training team evaluate the program's strengths and weaknesses, identify areas for improvement, and make data-driven decisions to enhance future programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field in the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Answer all questions honestly and provide specific examples or explanations when requested.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Title**: This field is where you can provide the title of the virtual training program you attended. This will help us track which program you're providing feedback on.
* **Session Date**: This field is for the date of the virtual training session you attended. Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Trainer Name**: This field is for the name of the trainer who led the virtual session. If you don't remember their name, you can leave this field blank.
* **Session Attendance**: This field is for the number of people who attended the virtual session. Please provide the total number of attendees.
* **Session Location**: This field is for the location of the virtual session (e.g., online platform, conference room, etc.). Please provide the specific location.
* **Session Rating**: This field is for your rating of the session's overall effectiveness. Please select one of the provided options (Very Good, Good, Average, Fair, Poor).
* **Feedback**: This field is for any additional comments or feedback you'd like to provide about the session. Please be specific and constructive in your feedback.
* **Program Effectiveness**: This field is for your rating of the overall effectiveness of the virtual training program. Please select one of the provided options (Very Good, Good, Average, Fair, Poor).
* **Improvement Area**: This field is for any areas you feel the program could improve in. Please select all that apply from the provided options (content, delivery, engagement, relevance).

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
* Be specific when providing feedback or explanations.
* If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to the training team for clarification.
* We appreciate your feedback and look forward to improving our virtual training programs based on your input.
