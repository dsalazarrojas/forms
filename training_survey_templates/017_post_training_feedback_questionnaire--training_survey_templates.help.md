# Post Training Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the training session, ensuring that trainers can improve future sessions and provide a better experience for participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions and answers carefully.
2. Select the correct response for each question.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the text fields.
4. Ensure that you have filled out all required fields.
5. Click the "Submit" button to send in your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Form (1)**: A space to input any additional comments or feedback about the training.
* **Session Date (2)**: The date of the training session.
* **Session Start Time (3)**: The start time of the training session.
* **Session End Time (4)**: The end time of the training session.
* **Trainer (5)**: The name of the trainer who conducted the session.
* **Feedback Question 1.1 (6)**: Rate your satisfaction with the training session.
*   *Not Satisfied*: You did not feel satisfied with the training session.
*   *Somewhat Satisfied*: You felt somewhat satisfied with the training session.
*   *Very Satisfied*: You felt very satisfied with the training session.

* **Feedback Question 2.1 (8)**: Rate your satisfaction with the overall content of the training.
