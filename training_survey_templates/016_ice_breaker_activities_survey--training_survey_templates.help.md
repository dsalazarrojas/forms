# ice_breaker_activities_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ice breaker activities survey form is designed to collect information about the participant's willingness to participate in various activities during the training program. This data will help us understand the participants' interests and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all the ice breaker activities that you would be interested in participating in.
2. Enter your name as it appears on your official documents (e.g. ID, driver's license, passport).
3. Enter your age.
4. Select your gender.
5. Enter your email address.
6. Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Ice Breaker Activities** (`ice_breaker_activities`, select_multiple, required): Select all the activities that you would be interested in participating in during the training program. You can select multiple activities from the list provided.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents (e.g. ID, driver's license, passport).
* **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, number, required): Enter your age.
* **Participant Gender** (`participant_gender`, select_one, required): Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.

## Tips

* Make sure to select all activities that you are interested in participating in.
* Double-check your name, age, and contact information for accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to the support team for assistance.
