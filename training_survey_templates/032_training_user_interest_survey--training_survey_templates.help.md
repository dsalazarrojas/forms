# Training User Interest Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Training User Interest Survey. This form is designed to collect information from users to help tailor training programs to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Select the relevant options** from the select_one, select_multiple, and select_one fields to answer the questions. For number fields, enter the number.
2.  **Provide additional comments** if you have any further information to provide.
3.  **Submit the form** after filling out all the necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What are your interests?**: Select all the interests that apply to you.
    *   *Type:* select_multiple
    *   *Example:* Select 'Yes' for each interest that applies to you.
*   **Skills Level**: Enter the number of years of experience you have with the relevant skill.
    *   *Type:* number
    *   *Example:* Enter 5 for 5 years of experience.
*   **What type of training program are you interested in?**: Select the type of training program that you are interested in.
    *   *Type:* select_one
    *   *Example:* Select 'Yes' for the type of program that interests you.
*   **Have you taken a training session in the last 2 years?**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' accordingly.
    *   *Type:* select_one
    *   *Example:* Select 'Yes' if you have taken a training session in the last 2 years.
*   **Additional Comments**: Type any additional comments or information you would like to provide.
    *   *Type:* text
    *   *Example:* Provide any information you feel is necessary to help tailor the training program to your needs.
*   **How many times have you taken a training session in the last 6 months?**: Enter the number of times you have taken a training session in the last 6 months.
    *   *Type:* number
    *   *Example:* Enter 2 for 2 times you have taken a training session in the last 6 months.
*   **Training Location**: Select all the locations you are interested in.
    *   *Type:* select_multiple
    *   *Example:* Select 'Yes' for each location that you are interested in.
*   **What language do you prefer for the training session?**: Select the language you prefer for the training session.
    *   *Type:* select_one
    *   *Example:* Select 'Yes' for the language you prefer.
*   **What media do you prefer for the training session?**: Select the media you prefer for the training session.
    *   *Type:* select_one
    *   *Example:* Select 'Yes' for the media you prefer.
*   **What format do you prefer for the training session?**: Select the format you prefer for the training session.
    *   *Type:* select_one
    *   *Example:* Select 'Yes' for the format you prefer.
*   **Would you like to train with a group or individual?**: Select whether you would like to train with a group or as an individual.
    *   *Type:* select_one
    *   *Example:* Select 'Yes' for the preference you have.
*   **How long would you like the training session to be?**: Enter the length of the training session in number of hours.
    *   *Type:* number
    *   *Example:* Enter 2 for a 2-hour training session.
*   **How many times would you like to attend this training session?**: Enter the number of times you would like to attend the training session.
    *   *Type:* number
    *   *Example:* Enter 2 for 2 sessions.
*   **Is there any additional information you would like to provide?**: Type any additional information you would like to provide.
    *   *Type:* text
    *   *Example:* Provide any information you feel is necessary to help tailor the training program to your needs.
*   **Trainer**: Type the name of the trainer you would like to work with.
    *   *Type:* text
    *   *Example:* Type the name of the trainer you would like to work with.
*   **Training Facility**: Select the preferred training facility.
    *   *Type:* select_one
    *   *Example:* Select 'Yes' for the facility you prefer.
*   **Additional Information**: Type any additional information you would like to provide.
    *   *Type:* text
    *   *Example:* Provide any information you feel is necessary to help tailor the training program to your needs.
*   **Email**: Type your email address.
