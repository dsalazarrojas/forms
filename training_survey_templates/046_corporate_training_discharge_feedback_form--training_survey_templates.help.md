# Corporate Training Discharge Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from you after a corporate training session. It should take around 10-15 minutes to complete.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions as accurately as possible.
2. Please ensure that you only answer the questions once.
3. You can type your answers directly into the form, without any formatting or attachments.
4. Once you've completed the form, click the submit button to send it to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Form Section 1
* **Form Section 1** (`form_section_1`, text, not required): This is the first section of the form. You can write any comments or feedback you have about the training session. Keep it as specific and detailed as possible.

### Form Section 2
* **Form Section 2** (`form_section_2`, text, not required): This section is similar to the first one. It's another chance to provide any comments or feedback you have about the training session.

### Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, not required): This section is meant for you to provide your contact information, so we can get back to you with any follow-up questions or to thank you for your feedback.

### Training Info
* **Training Info** (`training_info`, text, not required): Here, you can provide any information about the training session you attended. This could include the session title, date, or any other relevant details.

### Form Section 4
* **Form Section 4** (`form_section_4`, text, not required): This section is another opportunity for you to provide any comments or feedback about the training session. 

### Form Section 5
* **Form Section 5** (`form_section_5`, text, not required): Similar to Section 4, this section is meant for comments and feedback. Try to be as specific as possible. 

### Form Section 6
* **Form Section 6** (`form_section_6`, text, not required): This section is where you can provide any final thoughts or feedback about the training session.

### Form Section 7
* **Form Section 7** (`form_section_7`, text, not required): Another chance to provide any comments or feedback about the training session.

### Form Section 8
* **Form Section 8** (`form_section_8`, text, not required): This section is similar to the others, where you can provide any final thoughts or feedback about the training session.

### Form Section 9
* **Form Section 9** (`form_section_9`, text, not required): This is the last section of the form. Take the time to provide any final comments or feedback about the training session.
