# Educational Institution Training Needs Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the training needs of educational institutions. Please answer the questions to the best of your ability, providing general information about your institution's requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question with your best effort, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for questions that ask for a simple confirmation.
3. Use numbers to answer scale questions.
4. In the "Comments" field, provide any additional information that you think is relevant to your institution's training needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Educational Institution Staff** (`staff_input_form_1`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter the name of your institution's staff member(s) completing this form.
* **Educational Institution** (`educational_institution`, text, optional): Enter the name of your educational institution.
* **Training Needs Priority** (`training_needs_priority`, select_one, optional): Please select "Yes" if your institution has a specific training need, "No" otherwise.
* **Priority 1** (`priority_scale_1`, number, optional): Rate the priority of your institution's training need on a scale of 1 (low) to 10 (high).
* **Priority 2** (`priority_scale_2`, number, optional): Rate the priority of your institution's training need on a scale of 1 (low) to 10 (high).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Use this field to provide any additional comments or information about your institution's training needs.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
* If you're unsure about any question, please ask for clarification or guidance.
* Remember that this form is a starting point for identifying potential areas of improvement, not a comprehensive needs assessment.
