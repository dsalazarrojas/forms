# Youth Work Methods Training Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and evaluations from participants who have attended a Youth Work Methods training session. The survey aims to collect information on their overall satisfaction, session attendance, facilitator performance, and areas for improvement. Please ensure that you complete this survey if you have attended the training session.


## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Review the Survey

Please read the following survey carefully and answer the questions accordingly. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the facilitator or training team.


### Step 2: Answer the Survey Form

Complete the form by providing your feedback on the following fields:

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Session Date** (`Session Date`, date, required: false)

* The date of the session you attended.

### 2. **Session Attendance Count** (`Session Attendance Count`, number, required: false)

* The number of people who attended the session.

### 3. **Overall Satisfaction** (`Overall Satisfaction`, select_one, required: false)

* Please rate your overall satisfaction with the session on a scale of 1-5.

### 4. **Suggestions** (`Suggestions`, text, required: false)

* Share any suggestions or comments you have about the training session.

### 5. **Session Topic** (`Session Topic`, select_multiple, required: false)

* Select all the topics that were covered during the session.

### 6. **Session Facilitator** (`Session Facilitator`, select_multiple, required: false)

* Select all the facilitators who conducted the session.

### 7. **Participant Feedback** (`Participant Feedback`, text, required: false)

* Share your feedback about the session, such as what you liked, disliked, or found challenging.

### 8. **Trainer Evaluation** (`Trainer Evaluation`, text, required: false)

* Share your evaluation of the trainer's performance.

### 9. **Training Outcome** (`Training Outcome`, text, required: false)

* Share your thoughts on what you achieved or learned from the session.

### 10. **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, text, required: false)

* Share any additional comments or thoughts about the session.

### 11. **Participant Agreement** (`Participant Agreement`, text, required: false)

* Share any agreement or commitments you made during the session.

### 12. **Participant Dissatisfaction** (`Participant Dissatisfaction`, text, required: false)

* Share any dissatisfactions or concerns you have about the session.

### 13. **Session Facilities** (`Session Facilities`, select_one, required: false)

* Rate the facilities provided for the session.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the facilitator or training team.
