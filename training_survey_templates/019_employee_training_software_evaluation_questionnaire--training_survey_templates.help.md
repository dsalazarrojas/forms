# employee_training_software_evaluation_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from employees on their experience with the training software used in the company. The goal is to understand the effectiveness of the software and identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the training platform you used from the options provided in the "Training Platform" dropdown menu.
2. Rate your satisfaction level with the software in the "Satisfaction Level" field. Please use the rating scale as follows: Low, Medium, High.
3. If applicable, choose one or more of the options in the "Training Adoption" field to indicate your level of adoption of the software.
4. Type your feedback in the "Feedback" field. This is an open-ended question where you can provide any comments or thoughts about your experience with the software.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, which will be used to contact you if needed.
6. If you have any additional comments, please use the "Additional Comments" field.
7. Finally, select the date you used the software from the calendar in the "Date" field.
8. If you have any other comments, you can enter them in the "Time" field, which will allow you to specify when you used the software.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Platform** (`training_software`, `select_one`, required): Select the training platform you used from the dropdown menu.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction`, `number`, required): Rate your satisfaction level with the software as Low, Medium, or High.
* **Training Adoption** (`adoption`, `select_multiple`, optional): If applicable, choose one or more of the options to indicate your level of adoption of the software.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required): Type your feedback and comments about your experience with the software.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address, which will be used to contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number (this field is not currently used, but it may be used in the future).
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Select the date you used the software from the calendar.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): If you want to specify the time you used the software, enter it here (this field is not currently used, but it may be used in the future).
* **Additional Comments** (`note`, `note`, optional): Provide any other comments or thoughts you have about your experience with the software.
