# internal_training_impact_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback on internal training programs to help improve their effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. On page 1, rate the usefulness of the training program on a scale of 1-5.
2. On page 2, provide your satisfaction level with the training program.
3. On page 3, list any skills or knowledge you gained from the training program.
4. On page 4, add any additional comments or recommendations you have for the training program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How useful was this training?** (`training_usefulness`, number, required: false): Please rate the usefulness of the training program on a scale of 1-5.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction_level`, text, required: false): Describe your level of satisfaction with the training program.
* **What skills did you gain from this training?** (`skills_gained`, text, required: false): List any new skills or knowledge you acquired from the training program.
* **Do you have any additional comments or recommendations for the training?** (`recommendations`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have for the training program.
