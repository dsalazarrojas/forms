# Post Session Reflection Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants after a training session to improve the effectiveness and quality of future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

To provide accurate and useful feedback, please answer the questions to the best of your ability. You can select all that apply for survey participants.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Survey Participants
* **Survey Participants** (Select all that apply):
	+ Yes: This session was attended by participants.
	+ No: The session was not attended by participants.

### Session Details
* **Session Details**:
	+ A numerical value representing the number of hours spent during the session.

### Session Notes
* **Session Notes**:
	+ A text field where you can provide a brief summary of the session, any notable events that occurred, or any additional comments.

### Facilitator Comments
* **Facilitator Comments**:
	+ A text field for the facilitator to provide feedback or comments about the session.

### Participant Experience
* **Participant Experience**:
	+ A numerical value representing the participant's experience with a scale (e.g., 1-5).

### Participant Comments
* **Participant Comments**:
	+ A text field where the participant can provide their personal comments or feedback.

### Suggestions for Improvement
* **Suggestions for Improvement**:
	+ A text field where the participant can suggest areas for improvement.

### Next Steps
* **Next Steps**:
	+ A text field where the participant can provide suggestions for next steps or follow-up actions.

### Evaluation
* **Evaluation**:
	+ A numerical value representing the participant's evaluation of the session (e.g., 1-5).

### Rating
* **Rating**:
	+ A numerical value representing the participant's rating of the session (e.g., 1-5).

### Rating Scale
* **Rating Scale**:
	+ A dropdown selection with four options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.

### Comments
* **Comments**:
	+ A text field where the participant can provide additional comments or feedback.
 
## Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Select all that apply for survey participants.
* For numerical fields, provide a value between 1 and 5, unless otherwise specified.
* Keep your answers concise and focused on providing actionable feedback.
