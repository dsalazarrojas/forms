# Community Impact Framework Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate your understanding of community impact evaluation concepts. It will ask you to provide information about your organization, your understanding of outcomes, and your approach to evaluation methods.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the instructions and questions carefully.
2. Answer the questions to the best of your ability.
3. If you're unsure about any of the questions, don't hesitate to ask for help.
4. Fill in your participant name and organization (if applicable).
5. Choose the correct answers to the multiple-choice questions.
6. Provide a short answer to the open-ended questions.
7. Select all applicable evaluation methods.
8. Enter your contact email for training follow-up (if you want to receive additional support).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Impact Framework Quiz --** (`quiz_intro`, note, not required): This is an introduction to the form. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Participant name** (`participant_name`, text, not required): Please enter your name as a participant in the community project or initiative.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, not required): If you're representing an organization, please enter your organization's name.
* **Understanding of outcomes definition** (`understanding_of_outcomes`, select_one, required): This question asks about your understanding of outcomes. Choose one of the options:
	+ Clear and measurable outcomes
	+ Outputs that are activities
	+ Inputs or resources
* **Which component explains causal relationship** (`logic_model_component`, select_one, required): Choose the component that explains the causal relationship:
	+ Outputs
	+ Outcomes
	+ Activities
* **Preferred evaluation methods** (`evaluation_method_preference`, select_multiple, not required): Select all applicable evaluation methods:
	+ Surveys
	+ Interviews
	+ Case studies
	+ Administrative data
* **Example of an indicator** (`indicators_example`, text, required): Provide a short answer to this question.
* **Confidence in scoring impact** (`scoring_confidence`, select_one, not required): Rate your confidence in scoring impact:
	+ Very confident
	+ Somewhat confident
	+ Not confident
* **Training needs related to impact evaluation** (`training_needs`, text, not required): This question is open-ended. Provide your thoughts about any training needs you may have related to impact evaluation.
* **Contact email for training follow up** (`follow_up_contact`, email, not required): If you want to receive additional support, enter your contact email address here.

## Tips

* Take your time to answer the questions carefully.
* If you're unsure about any question, don't hesitate to ask for help.
* This form is intended to be a tool for self-assessment and improvement.
