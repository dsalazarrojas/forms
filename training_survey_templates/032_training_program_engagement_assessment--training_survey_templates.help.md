# Training Program Engagement Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the engagement and effectiveness of the training program. It collects feedback and ratings from the participants, which can be used to identify areas for improvement and make informed decisions about future training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly, based on your personal experiences and perceptions of the training program.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for each field, and do not leave any fields blank unless they are explicitly marked as optional.
3. Use the space provided for any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
