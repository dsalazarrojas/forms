# Simulation-Based Training Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Simulation-Based Training Survey Form is designed to gather feedback from participants after a simulation-based training session. This form helps assess the quality and effectiveness of the training session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the title of the session in the `session_title` field.
2.  Select the type of session you attended from the `session_type` dropdown menu.
3.  Enter the start and end dates of the session using the `session_start_date` and `session_end_date` fields.
4.  Enter the start and end times of the session using the `session_start_time` and `session_end_time` fields.
5.  Rate your satisfaction with the session on a scale of 1-5 in the `session_rating` field.
6.  Select your level of satisfaction with the session from the `session_satisfaction` dropdown menu.
7.  Provide feedback on what can be improved for the next simulation session in the `session_improvement` text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **session_title** (text, optional): Enter a brief description of the session title to help identify the specific session being referenced.
*   **session_type** (select_one, optional): Choose the type of session you attended, such as "Simulation" or "Training".
*   **session_start_date** (date, optional): Enter the date the session started.
*   **session_start_time** (time, optional): Enter the start time of the session.
*   **session_end_date** (date, optional): Enter the date the session ended.
*   **session_end_time** (time, optional): Enter the end time of the session.
*   **session_rating** (number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the session on a scale of 1-5.
*   **session_satisfaction** (select_one, optional): Select your level of satisfaction with the session, such as "Very Satisfied" or "Somewhat Unsatisfied".
*   **session_improvement** (text, optional): Provide any feedback on what can be improved for the next simulation session.
