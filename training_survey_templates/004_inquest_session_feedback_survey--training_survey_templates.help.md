# inquest_session_feedback_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants after a training session. It helps us evaluate the effectiveness of our training programs and make improvements for future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question truthfully.
2. Be as detailed as possible when providing feedback, especially in the "Additional Comments" field.
3. If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, please share them in the "Suggestions" field.
4. If you have any follow-up questions or need further clarification, please feel free to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (Text, Optional): Provide a brief introduction to the session or training program. This can include any background information or context that may be helpful for our evaluation.
* **Facilitator Rating** (Select One, Optional): Rate the effectiveness of the facilitator. Choose from the following options: Good, Fair.
* **Training Content** (Text, Optional): Share your thoughts about the training content. Were there any areas that you found particularly helpful or challenging?
* **Feedback** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about the session. This is a great place to share your thoughts and suggestions.
* **Session Rating** (Select One, Optional): Rate the overall quality of the session. Choose from the following options: Good, Fair.
* **Participant Experience** (Text, Optional): Share your thoughts about your experience as a participant. Were there any areas that you found particularly challenging or enjoyable?
* **Suggestions** (Text, Optional): Share any suggestions or ideas you have for improving future sessions.
* **Participant Rating** (Select Multiple, Optional): Rate the participant's experience. Choose from the following options: Good, Fair.
* **Follow Up** (Text, Optional): Share any additional comments or questions you have about the session.
* **Participant Follow Up** (Text, Optional): Share any additional comments or questions you have about your follow-up with the facilitator.
* **Session Follow Up** (Text, Optional): Share any additional comments or questions you have about the session follow-up.
* **Additional Comments** (Text, Optional): Share any further thoughts or comments about the session.
* **Session Improvement** (Text, Optional): Share any suggestions or ideas you have for improving future sessions.
* **Participant Rating** (Select Multiple, Optional): Rate the participant's experience. Choose from the following options: Good, Fair.
* **Session** (Text, Optional): Share any additional comments or questions you have about the session.
