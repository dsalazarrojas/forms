# Training Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Training Satisfaction Survey is a tool designed to help us understand the effectiveness of our training programs. Your feedback is invaluable in helping us improve future training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this survey, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly, and to the best of your ability.
2. For questions that ask you to select an option, choose one that best describes your experience.
3. You can leave fields blank if you are unsure or do not have an answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What were your initial purpose for attending?** (`training_satisfaction_survey`, text, optional)
  This question is meant to understand your initial reason for joining the training session. Try to recall what motivated you to sign up for the training in the first place.
* **Which training session were you attending?** (`session_attended`, select_one, optional)
  Please select the training session where you actually attended. This will help us track progress and attendance.
* **How satisfied are you with your instructors?** (`satisfaction_with_instructors`, select_multiple, optional)
  Indicate how satisfied you are with the instructors of your training session. If you were satisfied with all or some of the instructors, select all or some of the options that apply.
* **How relevant was the content to your needs?** (`content_relevance`, number, optional)
  Rate how relevant the training content was to your needs. On a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not relevant at all" and 5 is "very relevant".
* **What were your learning outcomes for attending?** (`learning_outcome`, select_one, optional)
  Select one of the outcomes that best describe your experience after attending the training session.
* **Was time sufficient?** (`time_sufficient`, select_multiple, optional)
  Indicate how you perceive the time allocated for the training session. If it was too long, too short, or just right for your needs, select the corresponding options.
