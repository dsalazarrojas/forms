# Hospital Ethics Committee Workshop Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants on the effectiveness and quality of the hospital ethics workshop. The feedback will help the workshop organizers improve the content, presentation, and overall experience for future participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions and fields below.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Provide specific examples or comments to support your answers where possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Presentation Style** (`workshop_presentation`, Select Multiple, **Required**): Rate the style of the workshop presentation, selecting from the options Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Instructor Facilitation Technique** (`instructor_facilitation_technique`, Select One, **Not Required**): Rate the instructor's facilitation technique, selecting from the options Very good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Materials Provided** (`material_provided`, Select Multiple, **Required**): Indicate whether the materials provided were satisfactory, selecting from the options True or False.
* **Workshop Length** (`workshop_length`, Select Multiple, **Required**): Rate the workshop length, selecting from the options Too long, Just right, or Too short.
* **Workshop Evaluation Score** (`workshop_evaluation_score`, Number, **Not Required**): Evaluate the overall quality of the workshop on a scale of 1-5.
* **Workshop Attendance Rating** (`workshop_attendance_rating`, Number, **Not Required**): Rate the workshop's attendance engagement, selecting from 1-5.
* **Workshop Content Rating** (`workshop_content_rating`, Number, **Not Required**): Evaluate the workshop's content, selecting from 1-5.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, **Not Required**): Provide any additional comments or suggestions about the workshop.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, **Not Required**): Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
