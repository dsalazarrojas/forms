# Vehicle Insurance Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vehicle insurance claim form is used to collect information about a vehicle-related incident and its associated claim. This form is designed to be completed by the policy holder or claimant to facilitate the processing of the claim.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling in your vehicle claim details, including the policy holder's information, vehicle details, and incident information.
2. Provide your claimant's information, including name, address, phone number, and email.
3. Specify the claim amount, claim date, and claim status.
4. Attach any relevant documentation or evidence to support your claim.
5. Review and submit the form to initiate the claim process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Vehicle Claim
* **Vehicle Claim** (`vehicle_claim`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the vehicle-related incident, including the date, time, and location of the incident.

### Policy Holder Information
* **Policy Holder Information** (`policy_holder_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter the policy holder's name, address, and contact information.

### Vehicle Details
* **Vehicle Details** (`vehicle_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter the vehicle's make, model, year, and registration number.

### Incident Information
* **Incident Information** (`incident_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter a detailed description of the incident, including any injuries or damages involved.

### Claimant Information
* **Claimant Information** (`claimant_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name, address, phone number, and email.

### Claimant Contact
* **Claimant Contact** (`claimant_contact`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact information, including phone number and email.

### Claim Details
* **Claim Details** (`claim_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional details about the claim, such as the type of claim and any supporting documentation.

### Attachments
* **Attachments** (`attachments`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Attach any relevant documentation or evidence to support your claim, such as photos, medical records, or repair estimates.

### Claim Amount
* **Claim Amount** (`claim_amount`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total amount claimed for the incident.

### Claim Date
* **Claim Date** (`claim_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the claim was made.

### Claim Status
* **Claim Status** (`claim_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Specify the current status of the claim, such as "Active", "Inactive", or "Pending".

### Assigned Tool
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the tool or equipment assigned to process the claim.

### Claim Type
* **Claim Type** (`claim_type`, `text`, required: false): Enter the type of claim, such as "Accident" or "Theft".

### Policy Holder
* **Policy Holder** (`policy_holder`, `text`, required: false): Enter the policy holder's name and contact information.

### Vehicle Registration
* **Vehicle Registration** (`vehicle_registration`, `text`, required: false): Enter the vehicle's registration number.

### Incident Date
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the incident.

### Incident Location
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of the incident.

### Incident Description
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, `text`, required: false): Enter a detailed description of the incident.

### Claimant Name
* **Claimant Name** (`claimant_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name.

### Policy Provider
* **Policy Provider** (`policy_provider`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the policy provider.

### Policy Number
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the policy number.

### Claimant Address
* **Claimant Address** (`claimant_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your address.

### Claimant Phone
* **Claimant Phone** (`claimant_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.

### Policy Effective Date
* **Policy Effective Date** (`policy_effective_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the effective date of the policy.

### Claimant Email
* **Claimant Email** (`claimant_email`, `text`, required: false): Enter your email.
