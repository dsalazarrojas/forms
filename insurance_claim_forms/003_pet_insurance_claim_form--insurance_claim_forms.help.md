# Pet Insurance Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Insurance Claim Form is a document used to submit a claim for your pet's medical expenses under your pet insurance policy. To fill out this form accurately and efficiently, please read the following instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please fill out all the required fields marked as "required". This will ensure that your claim is processed quickly and effectively. If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask for assistance or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Claimant Name** (`claimant_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your insurance policy.
* **Email** (`claimant_email`, email, required): Please enter your email address as it is registered with the insurance company.
* **Phone** (`claimant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any further communication or clarification.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): If you are unsure about your date of birth, please leave this field blank.
* **Pet Name** (`pet_name`, text, required): Enter your pet's name as it appears on your insurance policy.
* **Pet Species** (`pet_species`, select_one, required): Select the species of your pet from the options provided.
* **Veterinary Clinic Name** (`veterinary_clinic_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the veterinary clinic where your pet received treatment.
* **Medical Expenses** (`medical_expenses`, number, required): Please enter the total medical expenses for your pet's treatment, including all costs and fees.
* **Veterinary Certificate** (`veterinary_certificate`, text, required): Attach a copy of the veterinary certificate or treatment report to support your claim.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, optional): If additional supporting documents are required, please attach them here.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Please sign this form to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your form for processing.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, select_one, optional): This field will be updated by the insurance company after reviewing your claim.
* **Review Comment** (`review_comment`, text, optional): If the insurance company needs further clarification or information, they may leave a comment here.
* **Claim Status** (`claim_status`, select_one, optional): This field will be updated by the insurance company after reviewing your claim.
* **Reason for Denial** (`reason_for_denial`, text, optional): If your claim is denied, this field will contain the reason.
* **Claimant Address** (`claimant_address`, text, optional): Enter your address as it appears on your insurance policy.
* **Claimant City** (`claimant_city`, text, optional): Enter your city as it appears on your insurance policy.
* **Claimant State** (`claimant_state`, text, optional): Enter your state as it appears on your insurance policy.
* **Claimant Zip** (`claimant_zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip code as it appears on your insurance policy.
* **Veterinary Clinic Address** (`veterinary_clinic_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the veterinary clinic as it appears on your insurance policy.
* **Veterinary Clinic City** (`veterinary_clinic_city`, text, optional): Enter the city of the veterinary clinic as it appears on your insurance policy.
* **Veterinary Clinic State** (`veterinary_clinic_state`, text, optional): Enter the state of the veterinary clinic as it appears on your insurance policy.
* **Veterinary Clinic Zip** (`veterinary_clinic_zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of the veterinary clinic as it appears on your insurance policy.
* **Veterinary Clinic License** (`veterinary_clinic_license`, text, optional): Enter the license number of the veterinary clinic as it appears on your insurance policy.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required): Please enter the assigned tool number as given to you by the insurance company.
