# Claim Processing Efficiency Audit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Claim Processing Efficiency Audit Form is designed to help auditors assess and identify areas of improvement in the claims processing efficiency of an insurance company. This form is used to collect data and insights on the current state of claim processing, including bottlenecks, compliance issues, and recommendations for improvement. The form is an essential tool for auditors to evaluate and optimize the claims processing process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, please start by providing your name as the auditor.
2.  Enter the start and end dates of the audit period in the "Audit period start" and "Audit period end" fields, respectively.
3.  Enter the total number of claims reviewed in the "Total claims reviewed" field.
4.  If applicable, enter the mean processing time in days for the claims reviewed in the "Average turnaround days" field.
5.  In the "Common bottlenecks identified" field, describe any recurring issues that hinder claim processing efficiency.
6.  If any compliance issues were found during the audit, note them in the "Compliance issues found" field.
7.  Provide actionable steps and priorities for improvement in the "Recommendations for improvement" field.
8.  If you wish to specify the risk level for the current process, select a risk level from the "Risk level for current process" dropdown menu. 
8.  Lastly, enter the date of the audit's finalization in the "Audit completion date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Auditor name**: Please enter your full name as the auditor performing the audit.
* **Audit period start**: Enter the start date of the audit period, in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Audit period end**: Enter the end date of the audit period, in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Total claims reviewed**: Enter the total number of claims sampled for the audit.
* **Average turnaround days**: If applicable, enter the mean processing time in days for the claims reviewed. If not applicable, leave this field blank.
* **Common bottlenecks identified**: Describe any recurring issues that hinder claim processing efficiency.
* **Compliance issues found**: Note any policy deviations found during the audit.
* **Recommendations for improvement**: Provide actionable steps and priorities for improvement.
* **Risk level for current process**: Select the risk level for the current process from the options: Low, Medium, High.
* **Audit completion date**: Enter the date of the audit's finalization.
