# Damage Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Damage Claim Form is a tool used to submit a claim for damage to a property. It helps the insurance company gather the necessary information to process the claim and provide a resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit a Claim" button to open the form.
2. Fill in the required information:
	* **Claimant Name** (`claimant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person who has experienced the loss.
	* **Email** (`claimant_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the claimant (optional but recommended for correspondence).
	* **Phone** (`claimant_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the claimant (optional).
	* **Date of Loss** (`date_of_loss`, date, optional): Enter the date of the incident (if known).
	* **Time of Loss** (`time_of_loss`, time, optional): Enter the time of the incident (if known).
	* **Loss Description** (`loss_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the damage or loss.
	* **Damaged Property Type** (`damaged_property_type`, text, optional): Describe the type of property that was damaged.
	* **Damaged Property Value** (`damaged_property_value`, text, optional): Enter the value of the damaged property.
	* **Damage Amount** (`damage_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the damage (if applicable).
3. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Claimant Name**: This field is used to capture the name of the person who has experienced the loss. Please enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Claimant Email**: This optional field is for the email address of the claimant. If you have an email address, please provide it for communication purposes.
* **Claimant Phone**: This optional field is for the phone number of the claimant. If you have a phone number, please provide it for communication purposes.
* **Date of Loss**: This field is used to record the date of the incident. If known, please enter the date to help with tracking and processing the claim.
* **Time of Loss**: This field is used to record the time of the incident. If known, please enter the time to help with tracking and processing the claim.
* **Loss Description**: This field is used to describe the damage or loss in detail. Please provide as much information as possible to help with processing the claim.
* **Damaged Property Type**: This field is used to identify the type of property damaged. Please provide a brief description of the property (e.g., building, vehicle, etc.).
* **Damaged Property Value**: This field is used to enter the value of the damaged property (if applicable). Please provide the actual value of the property.
* **Damage Amount**: This field is used to enter the amount of the damage (if applicable). This will help with processing the claim and providing a resolution.
