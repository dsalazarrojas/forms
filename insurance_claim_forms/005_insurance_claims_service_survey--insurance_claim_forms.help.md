# Insurance Claims Service Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with our insurance claims service. Your input will help us understand where we excel and where we can improve to better serve you in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question according to your experience with our insurance claims service.
2. For questions with a scale (e.g., select_one), select one of the options that best represents your experience.
3. For open-ended questions (e.g., comments), provide as much detail as possible about your experience.
4. Ensure all required fields are filled before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied are you with our claims service?** (claim_satisfaction, select_one, required)
Select one of the following options:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
This question helps us understand how you feel about our service.
* **How quickly were your claims resolved?** (claim_speed, select_one, required)
Select one of the following options:
	+ Fast
	+ Medium
	+ Slow
This question helps us understand how long it took to resolve your claim.
* **How would you rate the communication with your claims adjuster?** (communication, select_one, required)
Select one of the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Poor
This question helps us understand how well our adjuster communicated with you.
* **How easy was the claims process?** (claim_process, select_one, required)
Select one of the following options:
	+ Easy
	+ Difficult
	+ Neutral
This question helps us understand how smooth the claims process was for you.
* **How would you rate the outcome of your claim?** (claim_outcome, select_one, required)
Select one of the following options:
	+ Satisfactory
	+ Unsatisfactory
	+ Neutral
This question helps us understand how your claim turned out.
* **Comments about your claim experience?** (claim_comments, text, optional)
Please provide any additional thoughts or comments about your experience. This is an opportunity to share any positive or negative feedback.
* **Your contact details** (contact_details, email, required)
Please enter your email address so we can contact you about your feedback.
* **What date did you file your claim?** (claim_date, date, required)
Please select the date you filed your claim.
* **What time did you file your claim?** (claim_time, time, required)
Please select the time you filed your claim.
* **Your phone number** (contact_phone, text, required)
Please enter your phone number so we can contact you about your feedback.
* **Your email** (contact_email, email, required)
Please enter your email address so we can contact you about your feedback.
