# medical_expense_claim_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The medical expense claim form is used to gather information from claimants for new medical expense claims. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth claim process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review all fields carefully to ensure accuracy.
2. Fill in the required fields marked as required (e.g., medical insurance provider, claimant's address, etc.).
3. Select options from dropdown fields (e.g., medical insurance provider, claim status, etc.).
4. Click "Submit Claim" to submit your claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Claimant Details**: Enter your name and contact information, ensuring accuracy and completeness.
  - Label: claimant_details
  - Type: text
  - Required: false

- **Medical Expenses**: Describe your medical expenses, including dates and amounts, in detail.
  - Label: medical_expenses
  - Type: text
  - Required: false

- **Billing Information**: Enter your billing information for the medical expenses.
  - Label: billing_info
  - Type: text
  - Required: false

- **Medical Insurance Provider**: Indicate if you have medical insurance provider for the claim.
  - Label: medical_insurance_provider
  - Type: select_one
  - Options: 'Yes', 'No'
  - Required: true

- **Claimant Address**: Provide your address for correspondence.
  - Label: claimant_address
  - Type: select_multiple
  - Options: None, Other
  - Required: false

- **Medical Expense Date**: Enter the date of the medical expense.
  - Label: medical_expense_date
  - Type: date
  - Required: true

- **Medical Expense Amount**: Enter the total amount of the medical expense.
  - Label: medical_expense_amount
  - Type: number
  - Required: true

- **Medical Insurance Claim Number**: Enter your medical insurance claim number.
  - Label: medical_insurance_claim_number
  - Type: text
  - Required: true

- **Submit Claim**: Select "Submit Claim" to send in your claim.
  - Label: submit_claim
  - Type: select_one
  - Options: None, Other
  - Required: true

- **Claim Status**: Choose the status of your claim.
  - Label: claim_status
  - Type: select_one
  - Options: None, Other
  - Required: true

- **Claim Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about your claim.
  - Label: claim_notes
  - Type: note
  - Required: false

- **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient.
  - Label: patient_name
  - Type: select_one
  - Options: None, Other
  - Required: true

- **Claimant Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact.
  - Label: claimant_phone
  - Type: text
  - Required: true

- **Claimant Email**: Enter your email for contact.
  - Label: claimant_email
  - Type: email
  - Required: true

- **Claimant Address Street**: Enter your street address.
  - Label: claimant_address_street
  - Type: text
  - Required: true

- **Claimant Address City**: Enter your city.
  - Label: claimant_address_city
  - Type: text
  - Required: true

- **Claimant Address State**: Enter your state or province.
  - Label: claimant_address_state
  - Type: text
  - Required: true

- **Claimant Address Zip**: Enter your zip code.
  - Label: claimant_address_zip
  - Type: text
  - Required: true

- **Medical Expense Date Time**: Enter the date and time of the medical expense (if applicable).
  - Label: medical_expense_date_time
  - Type: date
  - Required: false

- **Medical Expense Description**: Describe your medical expenses in detail.
  - Label: medical_expense_description
  - Type: text
  - Required: false

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled before submitting the claim.
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submission.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your insurance provider or administrator.
