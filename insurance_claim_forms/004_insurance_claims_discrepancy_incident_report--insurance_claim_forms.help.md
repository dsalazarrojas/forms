Insurance Claims Discrepancy Incident Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Insurance Claims Discrepancy Incident Report form is used to report discrepancies or errors in an insurance claim. This form will help identify and document the issue, allowing for it to be investigated and resolved by the relevant parties.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the Claimant information, including their name, email, and phone number.
2. Enter the Claimant Date of Birth and Date of the incident.
3. Enter the address of the Claimant, including Line 1, Line 2, City, State, Postal Code, and Country.
4. Enter the Claimant's Insurance Company information, including their Policy Number, Policy Type, Policy Status, Start Date, and End Date. This may require contacting the Insurance Company to obtain this information.
5. Enter the Insurance Company address, including Line 1, Line 2, City, State, and Postal Code.
6. Finally, review the form for accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claimant**: This field is required for the person making the claim.
* **Claimant Email**: This field is optional, but recommended for communication purposes.
* **Claimant Phone**: This field is optional, but recommended for communication purposes.
* **Claimant Date of Birth**: This field is required to validate the age of the Claimant.
* **Incident Date**: This field is required to document the date of the incident.
* **Incident Time**: This field is optional, but recommended for time of the incident.
* **Claimant Address Line 1**, **Claimant Address Line 2**: These fields are required for the Claimant's address.
* **Claimant City**: This field is required for the Claimant's city of residence.
* **Claimant State**: This field is required for the Claimant's state of residence.
* **Claimant Postal Code**: This field is required for the Claimant's zip code.
* **Claimant Country**: This field is required for the Claimant's country of residence.
* **Insurance Company**: This field is required to identify the Insurance Company involved.
* **Claimant Policy Number**: This field is required to identify the Claimant's Policy Number.
* **Claimant Policy Type**: This field is required to identify the type of policy the Claimant has.
* **Claimant Policy Status**: This field is required to identify the status of the Claimant's policy.
* **Claimant Policy Start Date**: This field is required to document the start date of the Claimant's policy.
* **Claimant Policy End Date**: This field is required to document the end date of the Claimant's policy.
* **Claimant Policy Deductible Amount**: This field is optional, but recommended for financial purposes.
* **Claimant Policy Provider**: This field is optional, but recommended for policy provider information.
* **Insurance Company Address Line 1**, **Insurance Company Address Line 2**, **Insurance Company City**, **Insurance Company State**: These fields are required for the Insurance Company's address.
* **Insurance Company Postal Code**: This field is required for the Insurance Company's zip code.
* **Insurance Company Country**: This field is required for the Insurance Company's country of residence.

**Tips**:
* Please make sure to enter the correct information for both the Claimant and Insurance Company.
* Double-check the form for accuracy before submitting it.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your supervisor or HR for assistance.
