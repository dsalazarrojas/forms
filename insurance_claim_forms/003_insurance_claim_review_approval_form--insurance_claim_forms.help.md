# insurance_claim_review_approval_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Insurance Claim Review and Approval Form** is a tool used by authorized personnel to review and approve or deny insurance claims. It helps to ensure that all required information is verified and accurate before making a decision on a claim.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **Policyholder Name** of the person or company filing the claim.
2.  Provide the **Email** address associated with the claimant's account.
3.  Enter the **Phone** number associated with the claimant's account.
4.  Select the **Claim Date** when the claim was filed.
5.  Enter the **Claim Amount** being requested.
6.  Choose the current **Claim Status** of the claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Policyholder Name** (`policyholder_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or company filing the claim.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address associated with the claimant's account.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number associated with the claimant's account.
*   **Claim Date** (`claim_date`, date, optional): Select the date when the claim was filed.
*   **Claim Amount** (`claim_amount`, number, optional): Enter the total amount being requested for the claim.
*   **Claim Status** (`claim_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the current status of the claim. Options are:
    *   Approved
    *   Denied
    *   Pending

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
*   Only authorized personnel can use this form to review and approve or deny claims.
*   This form is not intended for claimants to submit new claims.
