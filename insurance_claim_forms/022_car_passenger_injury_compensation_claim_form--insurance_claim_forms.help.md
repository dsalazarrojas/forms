# Car Passenger Injury Compensation Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who have been injured as a passenger in a vehicle accident. The form will gather details about the accident, the claimant's injuries, medical treatment, and compensation sought.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review all fields carefully and answer each question accurately and thoroughly.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. If you are completing the form on behalf of someone else, please make sure they are aware of the information provided.
4. If you are unsure about any field or have questions, please contact the relevant authorities or a representative of your insurance company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Claimant Information (ID 1)** (`Claimant Information`, `text`, required): This section is used to collect personal details about the claimant. Please provide your first name, last name, date of birth, and email address for contact purposes.
* **First Name (ID 2)** (`First Name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name (ID 3)** (`Last Name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth (ID 4)** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, required): Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address (ID 5)** (`Email Address`, `email`, required): Please provide your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number (ID 6)** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required): Please enter your contact phone number.
* **Street Address (ID 7)** (`Street Address`, `text`, required): Please enter your street address.
* **City (ID 8)** (`City`, `text`, required): Please enter your city.
* **State (ID 9)** (`State`, `text`, required): Please enter your state.
* **ZIP Code (ID 10)** (`ZIP Code`, `text`, required): Please enter your ZIP code.
* **Accident Information (ID 11)** (`Accident Information`, `text`, required): Please provide details about the accident, including location and description.
* **Date of Accident (ID 12)** (`Date of Accident`, `date`, required): Please enter the date the accident occurred.
* **Time of Accident (ID 13)** (`Time of Accident`, `time`, required): Please enter the time the accident occurred.
* **Location of Accident (ID 14)** (`Location of Accident`, `text`, required): Please enter the full address or description of the accident location.
* **Type of Accident (ID 15)** (`Type of Accident`, `select_one`, required): Please select the type of accident that occurred. Options include Rear-end collision, Side-impact collision, Head-on collision, Rollover, Single vehicle, Hit and run, and Other.
* **Accident Description (ID 16)** (`Accident Description`, `text`, required): Please describe what happened in the accident.
* **Vehicle Information (ID 17)** (`Vehicle Information`, `note`, required): This section is used to collect information about the vehicle you were in at the time of the accident. Please provide details about the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year (ID 18)** (`Vehicle Year`, `text`, required): Please enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make (ID 19)** (`Vehicle Make`, `text`, required): Please enter the manufacturer of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model (ID 20)** (`Vehicle Model`, `text`, required): Please enter the model of the vehicle.
* **VIN (ID 21)** (`VIN`, `text`, required): This is the Vehicle Identification Number.
* **Your Seating Position (ID 23)** (`Your Seating Position`, `select_one`, required): Please select your seating position in the vehicle at the time of the accident. Options include Front passenger seat, Rear seat - left, Rear seat - center, Rear seat - right, and Other.
* **Were You Wearing a Seatbelt? (ID 24)** (`Were You Wearing a Seatbelt?`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether you were wearing a seatbelt at the time of the accident. Options include Yes, No, and Unsure.
* **Did Airbags Deploy? (ID 25)** (`Did Airbags Deploy?`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether airbags deployed in the accident. Options include Yes, No, and Unsure.
* **Driver Information (ID 26)** (`Driver Information`, `text`, required): This section is used to collect information about the driver. Please provide details about the driver, including their name and relationship to you.
* **Driver Name (ID 27)** (`Driver Name`, `text`, required): Please enter the driver's name.
* **Driver Relationship (ID 28)** (`Driver Relationship`, `select_one`, required): Please select how you know the driver. Options include Family member, Friend, Co-worker, Rideshare driver, Bus/taxi driver, Stranger, and Other.
* **Driver Insurance (ID 29)** (`Driver Insurance`, `text`, required): Please enter the insurance company of the driver.
* **Driver Policy Number (ID 30)** (`Driver Policy Number`, `text`, required): This is the policy number of the driver's insurance.
* **Other Party Information (ID 31)** (`Other Party Information`, `text`, required): This section is used to collect information about the other party involved in the accident. Please provide details about the other party.
* **Was Another Vehicle Involved? (ID 32)** (`Was Another Vehicle Involved?`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether another vehicle was involved in the accident. Options include Yes and No.
* **Other Driver Name (ID 33)** (`Other Driver Name`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of the other driver.
* **Other Driver Insurance (ID 34)** (`Other Driver Insurance`, `text`, required): Please enter the insurance company of the other driver.
* **Other Driver Policy (ID 35)** (`Other Driver Policy`, `text`, required): This is the policy number of the other driver's insurance.
* **Injuries Sustained (ID 37)** (`Injuries Sustained`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select all injuries sustained in the accident. Options include Whiplash, Head injury, Neck injury, Back injury, Chest injury, Arm injury, Leg injury, Knee injury, Facial injury, Internal injuries, and Psychological trauma.
* **Injury Description (ID 38)** (`Injury Description`, `text`, required): Please describe your injuries in detail.
* **Medical Treatment (ID 39)** (`Medical Treatment`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether you received medical treatment. Options include Yes - emergency room, Yes - hospital admission, Yes - doctor visit, Yes - urgent care, No - declined treatment, and No - not needed.
* **Treating Facility (ID 40)** (`Treating Facility`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of the treating facility.
* **Treating Physician (ID 41)** (`Treating Physician`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of the treating physician.
* **First Treatment Date (ID 42)** (`First Treatment Date`, `date`, required): Please enter the date of the first treatment.
* **Ongoing Treatment (ID 43)** (`Ongoing Treatment`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether you are still receiving treatment. Options include Yes - ongoing care, Yes - physical therapy, No - treatment complete, and No - not needed.
* **Medical Expenses (ID 44)** (`Medical Expenses`, `number`, required): Please enter the total medical expenses to date.
* **Lost Wages (ID 45)** (`Lost Wages`, `number`, required): Please enter the income lost due to the injury.
* **Pain and Suffering (ID 46)** (`Pain and Suffering`, `select_one`, required): Please rate your pain level. Options include Mild, Moderate, Severe, and Debilitating.
* **Impact on Daily Life (ID 47)** (`Impact on Daily Life`, `text`, required): Please describe how the accident has affected your daily life.
* **Was a Police Report Filed? (ID 48)** (`Was a Police Report Filed?`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether a police report was filed. Options include Yes and No.
* **Police Report Number (ID 49)** (`Police Report Number`, `text`, required): Please enter the police report number.
* **Were There Witnesses? (ID 50)** (`Were There Witnesses?`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether there were witnesses. Options include Yes and No.
* **Witness Details (ID 51)** (`Witness Details`, `text`, required): Please enter the names and contact information of the witnesses.
* **Photos and Evidence (ID 52)** (`Photos and Evidence`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether you have photos and evidence. Options include Yes - photos, Yes - videos, Yes - both, and No.
* **Do You Have an Attorney? (ID 53)** (`Do You Have an Attorney?`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether you have an attorney. Options include Yes, No, and Considering.
* **Attorney Name (ID 54)** (`Attorney Name`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of your attorney.
* **Attorney Contact (ID 55)** (`Attorney Contact`, `text`, required): Please enter the contact information of your attorney.
* **Compensation Sought (ID 56)** (`Compensation Sought`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select the types of compensation sought. Options include Medical expenses, Lost wages, Pain and suffering, Emotional distress, Loss of enjoyment, Future medical care, Rehabilitation costs, and Other damages.
* **Estimated Claim Amount (ID 57)** (`Estimated Claim Amount`, `number`, optional): This is the estimated claim amount.
* **Additional Comments (ID 58)** (`Additional Comments`, `text`, optional): Please enter any other information about the claim.
* **Signature Confirmation (ID 59)** (`Signature Confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether you confirm that the information provided is accurate. Options include I certify this information is true and accurate and I cannot certify.
* **Claim Date (ID 60)** (`Claim Date`, `date`, required): Please enter the current date.

Note: Please review each field carefully and ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form. If you are unsure about any field or have questions, please contact the relevant authorities or a representative of your insurance company.
