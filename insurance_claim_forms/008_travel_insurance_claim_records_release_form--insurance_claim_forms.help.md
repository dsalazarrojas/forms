# Travel Insurance Claim Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Travel Insurance Claim Records Release Form" is used to collect information from policyholders to release their travel insurance claim records. It is essential for insurance companies to have this information for accurate and legitimate claim processing.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your policyholder name in the "Policyholder Name" field.
2. Provide your claim number in the "Claim Number" field, if applicable.
3. Enter the date of your claim in the "Claim Date" field, if applicable.
4. Describe your claim details in the "Claim Details" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Policyholder Email" field.
6. Provide your phone number in the "Policyholder Phone" field, if applicable.
7. Sign the "Authorized Signature" field to confirm that you are releasing your claim records.
8. Sign the "Policyholder Signature" field to confirm that you are the policyholder.
9. Declare your intention of releasing your claim records by signing the "Policyholder Declaration" field.
10. Select one of the options under "Policyholder Acknowledgement" to confirm your understanding of releasing your claim records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Policyholder Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your policy.
* **Claim Number**: Enter your claim number, if you have been assigned one. This is not required but helps insurance companies to identify your claim.
* **Claim Date**: Enter the date of your claim if it's applicable. This is not required but helps insurance companies to track your claim timeline.
* **Claim Details**: Describe your claim in this field. Be as detailed as possible to ensure that your claim is processed accurately.
* **Policyholder Email**: Enter your email address so that insurance companies can contact you regarding your claim.
* **Policyholder Phone**: Enter your phone number, if you wish to be contacted via phone. This is not required but helps insurance companies to contact you.
* **Authorized Signature**: Sign and date this field to confirm that you are releasing your claim records. This is required to process your claim.
* **Policyholder Signature**: Sign and date this field to confirm that you are the policyholder. This is required to process your claim.
* **Policyholder Declaration**: Sign and date this field to declare your intention of releasing your claim records. This is required to process your claim.
* **Policyholder Acknowledgement**: Select one of the options to confirm your understanding of releasing your claim records. This is required to process your claim.
