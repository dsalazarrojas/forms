# Health Insurance Claim Cost Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Insurance Claim Cost Tracker is a form used to gather information about health insurance claims. This form helps in tracking and processing claims in an efficient and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Provide your personal details in the User Information field.
2.  Fill in the Claim Details, including the claim amount, date, and description.
3.  Select the current status of the claim from the Claim Status options.
4.  Submit the form once all the required fields are filled in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (Text, Optional): This field is used to enter your personal information, including your name and contact details.
* **Claim Details** (Text, Optional): This field is used to enter the specifics of the claim, including the claim amount, date, and description.
* **Claim Status** (Select One, Optional): This field is used to select the current status of the claim from the available options: Insurance Company, Denied, or Pending.
* **Claim Amount** (Number, Optional): This field is used to enter the amount of the claim.
* **Claim Date** (Date, Optional): This field is used to enter the date of the claim.
* **Claim Description** (Text, Optional): This field is used to enter a brief description of the claim.
