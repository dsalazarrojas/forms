# Student Insurance Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to file an insurance claim for medical expenses or other incidents. Please fill out this form carefully and completely to help us process your claim efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Step 1:** Review the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
- **Step 2:** Fill out the required fields (Student ID, Claim Type, and Description).
- **Step 3:** For each field, enter the requested information as accurately and completely as possible.
- **Step 4:** Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID**: Enter your student ID as issued by the institution or organization providing this form. This is a required field.
* **Claim Type**: Choose the type of claim you are filing (Claim for accident, Claim for illness, or Claim for theft). This field is required.
* **Claim Date**: Enter the date when the incident occurred. This field is not required, but it helps us track the timeline of your claim.
* **Description**: Provide a detailed description of the incident or reason for your claim. This field is not required, but it helps us process your claim efficiently.
* **Amount Claimed**: Enter the total amount you are claiming for your expenses or losses. This field is not required.
* **Amount Paid**: Enter the amount that has already been paid or reimbursed. This field is not required.
* **Reason for Visit**: If applicable, provide a brief explanation of the reason why you visited a healthcare professional. This field is not required.
* **Medical Condition**: If applicable, describe the medical condition you were treated for. This field is not required.
* **Insurance Provider**: If you have an existing insurance policy, enter the name of your insurance provider. This field is not required.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your contact information in case we need to follow up with you. This field is not required.
* **Submitted By**: This field is not editable and will be filled out by our staff. You can ignore it. 

Please note that some fields are not required, but we recommend filling them out to help us process your claim efficiently. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
