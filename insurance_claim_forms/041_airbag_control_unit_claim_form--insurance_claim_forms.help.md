# Airbag Control Unit Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to file an insurance claim for an Airbag Control Unit issue. The claimant must provide accurate and relevant information to support their claim.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately, making sure to include all required information.
2. Review each field for relevance and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Claimant name** (`claimant_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person filing the claim.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for claim follow-up and contact.
* **Vehicle VIN** (`vehicle_vin`, `text`, required): Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the affected vehicle.
* **Incident date** (`incident_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the Airbag Control Unit issue occurred.
* **Fault description** (`fault_description`, `text`, required): Describe the observed Airbag Control Unit issue in detail.
* **Supporting documents** (`supporting_documents`, `text`, optional): Attach repair invoices or inspection reports that support your claim.
* **Claim amount requested** (`claim_amount_requested`, `number`, optional): Enter the estimated cost for repair or replacement of the Airbag Control Unit.
* **Insurer policy number** (`insurer_policy_number`, `text`, required): Enter your insurance policy number for processing the claim.
* **Declaration of truth** (`declaration`, `note`, required): Confirm that all information provided is accurate and true.

## Tips

* Double-check the accuracy of the information you provide, as this will affect the processing of your claim.
* Ensure that you have attached all necessary supporting documents.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the insurance provider for assistance.
