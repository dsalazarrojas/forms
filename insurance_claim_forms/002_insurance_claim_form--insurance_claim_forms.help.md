# insurance_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information for an insurance claim from a claimant, the individual submitting the claim. The form gathers data about the claimant, policy details, and claim specifics for the insurance provider to process the claim.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name and contact information in the fields provided.
2.  Enter your address, phone number, and email address.
3.  Enter your date of birth.
4.  Describe your relationship to the policyholder.
5.  Enter your policy number, if applicable.
6.  Provide details about the claim, including supporting documents.
7.  Enter the name and address of the healthcare provider.
8.  Enter the provider's phone number, license number, and license status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claimant Information**: Enter your name, address, phone number, and email address.
* **Claimant Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Claimant Email**: Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Claimant Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Claimant Address**: Enter your current address.
* **Claimant Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Claimant Relationship**: Describe your relationship to the policyholder (e.g., policyholder, dependent, etc.).
* **Claimant Policy Number**: Enter your policy number, if applicable.
* **Claim Details**: Describe the claim in detail, including any supporting documents.
* **Claim Documents**: Attach any relevant documents to support your claim.
* **Provider Name**: Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
* **Provider Address**: Enter the address of your healthcare provider.
* **Provider Phone**: Enter the phone number of your healthcare provider.
* **Provider License Number**: Enter the license number of your healthcare provider.
* **Provider License Status**: Enter the status of your healthcare provider's license.
* **Claimant Signature**: Enter your digital signature, confirming you submitted the form accurately.
