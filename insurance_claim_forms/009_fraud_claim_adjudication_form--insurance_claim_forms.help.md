# Fraud Claim Adjudication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fraud Claim Adjudication Form is used to process and adjudicate insurance claims that have been reported as potentially fraudulent. It is intended for use by employees of the insurance company who need to review and investigate claims that may be suspect or require further attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the claim documentation thoroughly to understand the claimant's circumstances and the reason for the claim.
2. Enter the claimant's first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter the claimant's surname in the "Surname" field.
4. Enter the claimant's address in the "Address" field.
5. Enter the claimant's phone number in the "Claimant's Phone" field.
6. Enter the claimant's date of birth in the "Date of Birth", "Claimant's DOB", "Claimant's DOB (Date and Time)" fields. Note that these fields are all related to the same date of birth, but are formatted differently.
7. If necessary, enter the claimant's date of birth time in the "Claimant's DOB Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`name_1`, `text`, required): Enter the claimant's first name.
* **Surname** (`surname_1`, `text`, required): Enter the claimant's surname.
* **Address** (`address_1`, `text`, required): Enter the claimant's address.
* **Claimant's Phone** (`claimant_phone_1`, `text`, required): Enter the claimant's phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth_1`, `date`, required): Enter the claimant's date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Claimant's DOB** (`claimant_dob_date`, `date`, required): Enter the claimant's date of birth in the format DD MM YYYY.
* **Claimant's DOB Time** (`claimant_dob_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the claimant's date of birth time in the format HH:MM:SS.
* **Claimant's Date of Birth** (`claimant_dob_date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter the claimant's date of birth in the format DD MM YYYY. Note: This field is redundant and may be removed in future updates.
* **Claimant's DOB (Date and Time)** (`claimant_dob_date_time`, `date`, optional): Enter the claimant's date of birth in the format DD MM YYYY HH:MM:SS. Note: This field is redundant and may be removed in future updates.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have accurate and up-to-date information about the claimant before completing the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please seek guidance from an authorized representative of the insurance company.
* Please note that this form is intended for internal use only and is restricted to authorized personnel.
