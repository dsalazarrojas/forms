# Life Insurance Early Claim Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit an early claim request for a life insurance policy. It gathers information from the policyholder or beneficiary to process the claim.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your valid **Email** in the "Email" field, as this will be used to contact you regarding your claim.
4. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the "Date of Birth" field in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
5. Select the type of **Claim** from the "Claim Type" dropdown list.
6. Enter the **Claim Date** if applicable, in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
7. If you have an **Insurance Provider**, enter the name in the "Insurance Provider" field.
8. Enter your **Contact Phone** number if you'd like to be contacted regarding your claim.
9. Enter the **Policy Number** associated with your life insurance policy.
10. Enter the **Claim Amount** you are requesting.
11. Select the current **Claim Status** from the dropdown list.
12. Add any additional **Notes** regarding your claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Claim Type**: Select the type of claim you are submitting (Life Insurance, Other, Annuity, or Accident).
* **Claim Date**: If applicable, enter the date the claim event occurred.
* **Insurance Provider**: Enter the name of the insurance provider (if applicable).
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted regarding your claim.
* **Policy Number**: Enter the policy number associated with your life insurance policy.
* **Claim Amount**: Enter the amount you are requesting for your claim.
* **Claim Status**: Select the current status of your claim (Pending, Approved, Denied, Rejected, or Pending Re-Submission).
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments regarding your claim.
