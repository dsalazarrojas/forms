# Retail Store Insurance Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about incidents that occurred within the store, including details about the incident, employee contact information, and supporting documents.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Store Name** of the incident, which is the name of the retail store where the incident occurred.
2. Provide a brief **Incident Description** of what happened, including any relevant details.
3. Enter the **Incident Date** when the incident occurred.
4. Fill in the **Contact Person**'s name and **EMail** address and **Phone** number, so that we can reach out to them for more information.
5. If necessary, **Supporting Documents** may be uploaded to help with the claim process. Please attach any relevant receipts, invoices, or other documentation related to the incident.
6. Repeat the above steps for any additional **Supporting Documents 2** that may be required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store Name** (`Store Name`, required): Enter the name of the retail store where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, required): Provide a brief description of the incident.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, optional): Upload any supporting documents like receipts, invoices, etc.
* **Store Location** (`store_location`, optional): Enter the location of the store where the incident occurred.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, required): Enter your name and EMail address and Phone number so we can reach out to you for more information.
* **EMail** (`email`, required): Enter your EMail address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, required): Enter the date when the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, optional): Provide a brief description of the incident.
* **Supporting Documents 2** (`supporting_documents_2`, optional): Upload any additional supporting documents like receipts, invoices, etc.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields with a red asterisk (\*) are mandatory.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* Make sure to attach all relevant documents to support your claim.
* Please provide a detailed and accurate description of the incident.
