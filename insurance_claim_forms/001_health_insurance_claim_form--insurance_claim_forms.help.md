# Health Insurance Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to submit a new health insurance claim. This form will help gather information about the patient and the claim they are submitting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information, including First Name, Last Name, and Date of Birth.
2. Select the type of claim you are submitting (Inpatient, Outpatient, or Urgent Care).
3. Enter the details of your claim, including Date of Service and Claim Amount.
4. Provide your insurance information, including Insurance ID, Insurance Plan, and Social Security Number.
5. Enter your contact information, including Phone Number and Email Address.
6. Provide any additional claim notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of your insurance provider.
* **Claim Type** (`claim_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of claim you are submitting:
	+ Inpatient
	+ Outpatient
	+ Urgent Care
* **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, date, optional): Enter the date of service for your claim.
* **Insurance ID** (`insurance_id`, number, optional): Enter your insurance ID number.
* **Claim Status** (`claim_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of your claim:
	+ New
	+ Pending
	+ Closed
	+ Reopened
* **Reason for Denial** (`reason_for_denial`, text, optional): Enter a reason for denial if your claim has been denied.
* **Claim Amount** (`claim_amount`, number, optional): Enter the claim amount.
* **Address** (`patient_address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, text, optional): Enter your social security number.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, text, optional): Enter your medical record number.
* **Billing Provider** (`billing_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of your billing provider.
* **Claim Number** (`claim_number`, text, optional): Enter your claim number.
* **Claim Date** (`claim_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your claim.
* **Insurance Plan** (`insurance_plan`, select_one, optional): Select your insurance plan:
	+ Gold
	+ Silver
	+ Bronze
* **Claim Notes** (`claim_notes`, text, optional): Enter any claim notes or comments.

## Tips
Please make sure to complete all required fields and review your information carefully before submitting the form.
