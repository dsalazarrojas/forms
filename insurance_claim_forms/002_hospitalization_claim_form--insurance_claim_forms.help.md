# Hospitalization Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from the claimant and medical provider to process the hospitalization claim. It is essential to fill out the form accurately to ensure that the claim is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the claimant's personal information, including name, date of birth, insurance ID, policy number, and relationship with the claimant.
2. Provide details about the hospitalization event, such as date of hospitalization, provider name, diagnosis, procedure description, and medical record numbers.
3. Enter information about the claimant's insurance plan and medical treatment.
4. Sign and date the form as the claimant, attending physician, and hospital staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Claimant Name** (`claimant_name`, text, optional): Enter the claimant's full name.
* **Date of Hospitalization** (`date_of_hospitalization`, date, optional): Enter the date of the hospitalization event.
* **Claimant Address** (`claimant_address`, text, optional): Enter the claimant's address.
* **Claimant Email** (`claimant_email`, email, optional): Enter the claimant's email address.
* **Claimant Phone** (`claimant_phone`, text, optional): Enter the claimant's phone number.
* **Treating Provider Name** (`treating_provider_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the treating provider.
* **Diagnosis** (`diagnosis`, text, optional): Enter the diagnosis or condition treated during the hospitalization.
* **Procedure Description** (`procedure_description`, text, optional): Enter a description of the procedure performed.
* **Hospitalization Date** (`hospitalization_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the hospitalization event.
* **Hospital Name** (`hospital_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the hospital where the claimant was hospitalized.
* **Attending Physician** (`attending_physician`, text, optional): Enter the name of the attending physician.
* **Medical Chart Notes** (`medical_chart_notes`, text, optional): Enter any relevant medical chart notes.
* **Claimant's ID** (`claimant_insurance_id`, text, optional): Enter the claimant's insurance ID.
* **Claimant's Policy Number** (`claimant_policy_number`, text, optional): Enter the claimant's policy number.
* **Claimant's Date of Birth** (`claimant_date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the claimant's date of birth.
* **Claimant's SSN** (`claimant_ssn`, text, optional): Enter the claimant's Social Security Number.
* **Claimant's Insurance Plan** (`claimant_insurance_plan`, text, optional): Enter the claimant's insurance plan.
* **Medical Record Numbers** (`medical_record_numbers`, text, optional): Enter any relevant medical record numbers.
* **Claimant's Relationship** (`claimant_relationship`, text, optional): Enter the claimant's relationship to the claimant (e.g., patient, guardian, etc.).
* **Hospital Location** (`hospital_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the hospital.
* **Attending Physician Signature** (`attending_physician_signature`, text, optional): Enter the signature of the attending physician.
* **Hospital Signature** (`hospital_signature`, text, optional): Enter the signature of the hospital staff.
* **Claimant Signature** (`claimant_signature`, text, optional): Enter the signature of the claimant.

Note: The fields marked as optional are not required, but it's recommended to fill them out to provide a complete and accurate account of the claimant's hospitalization event.
