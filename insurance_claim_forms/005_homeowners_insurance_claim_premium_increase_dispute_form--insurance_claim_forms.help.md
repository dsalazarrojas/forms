# Homeowners Insurance Claim Premium Increase Dispute Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for homeowners to dispute an increase in their insurance premium. It helps to gather essential information and supporting documents to facilitate a fair review of the claim.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully review the form to understand what is required.
2. Fill out the form with your information and any supporting documents.
3. Submit the form to initiate the claim process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claimant Information** (`home_claimant_info`, text, optional):
	+ This section is for you to provide any additional information about your claim. You can enter any details you think are relevant to your claim.
* **Claimant Name** (`claimant_name`, text, optional):
	+ Please enter your full name as it appears on your policy documents.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your contact number where you can be reached for further questions or updates.
* **Claimant Email** (`claimant_email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter your email address where you can be reached for further questions or updates.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your policy number as it appears on your policy documents.
* **Claim Type** (`claim_type`, select_one, optional):
	+ Indicate whether you are disputing the increase in a 'yes' or 'no' answer.
* **Dispute Date** (`dispute_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date when you first noticed the premium increase.
* **Dispute Amount** (`dispute_amount`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the amount you are disputing as a numeric value.
* **Reason For Increase** (`reason_for_increase`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Indicate the reasons for the premium increase by selecting 'yes' or 'no' for the following options:
		- 'Yes' - Select this if the increase was unjustified.
		- 'No' - Select this if the increase was justified.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, note, optional):
	+ Attach any supporting documents that can aid in understanding your claim. This could be a scan or digital copy of your policy documents, or any other relevant attachments.

## Tips
- Ensure you provide accurate information to facilitate a smooth claim process.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your insurance provider for further assistance.
- Attach any necessary supporting documents to aid in your claim.
