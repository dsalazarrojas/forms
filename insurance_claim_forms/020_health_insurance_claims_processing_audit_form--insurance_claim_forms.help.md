# health_insurance_claims_processing_audit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The health insurance claims processing audit form is used to collect and review claims data in an organized and systematic way. This helps to ensure that all necessary information is gathered and validated for accurate and efficient claims processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by entering the **Claim Date** (1) in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
2. Select the **Claim Status** (3) as either Active or Inactive to indicate the current status of the claim.
3. Enter the **Claim Amount** (4) in the format of a number.
4. Select the **Claim Type** (5) as either 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the type of claim.
5. In the **Claim Details** (6) field, provide a brief description of the claim.
6. Enter your **Auditor Comments** (7) to add any additional notes about the claim.
7. Enter your **Auditor Name** (8) and **Auditor E-mail** (12) and **Auditor Phone** (13) if you are the auditor.
8. Select the **Submission Status** (9) as either Active or Inactive to indicate the status of the submission.
9. Enter the **Submission Date** (10) and **Submission Time** (11) for the submission.
10. Enter your **Submitter E-mail** (14) and **Submitter Phone** (15) if you are the submitter.
11. In the **Submission Notes** (16) field, provide any additional notes or comments about the submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Claim Date (1)** (date): This field is used to enter the date of the claim.
* **Claim Status (3)** (select_one): Select the current status of the claim as either Active or Inactive.
* **Claim Amount (4)** (number): Enter the amount of the claim.
* **Claim Type (5)** (select_one): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the type of claim.
* **Claim Details (6)** (text): Provide a brief description of the claim.
* **Auditor Comments (7)** (text): Enter any additional notes about the claim.
* **Auditor Name (8)** (text): Enter your name as the auditor.
* **Submission Status (9)** (select_one): Select the status of the submission as either Active or Inactive.
* **Submission Date (10)** (date): Enter the date of the submission.
* **Submission Time (11)** (time): Enter the time of the submission.
* **Auditor E-mail (12)** (email): Enter your email as the auditor.
* **Auditor Phone (13)** (text): Enter your phone number as the auditor.
* **Submitter E-mail (14)** (email): Enter your email as the submitter.
* **Submitter Phone (15)** (text): Enter your phone number as the submitter.
* **Submission Notes (16)** (text): Provide any additional notes or comments about the submission.
