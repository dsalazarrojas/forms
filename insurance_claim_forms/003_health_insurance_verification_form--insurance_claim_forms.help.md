# Health Insurance Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Insurance Verification Form is used to collect information about a patient's insurance plan, healthcare provider, and claim details. This form is essential for processing insurance claims correctly and ensuring that all necessary information is collected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information:
	* **patient_info**: Enter the patient's name.
2. Verify the insurance plan:
	* **insurance_plan_info**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the patient's insurance plan is active or inactive.
3. Identify the healthcare provider:
	* **healthcare_provider**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the patient's healthcare provider is associated with the claim.
4. Enter claim details:
	* **claim_number**: Enter the claim number.
	* **claim_date**: Enter the date of the claim.
	* **claim_status**: Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate the status of the claim.
	* **claim_type**: Enter the type of claim (e.g., medical, dental, etc.).
	* **claim_amount**: Enter the claim amount.
	* **claim_details**: Enter any additional claim details.
5. Provide insurance company information:
	* **insurance_company**: Enter the name of the insurance company.
6. Provide contact information:
	* **insurance_id**: Enter any unique identifier for the insurance company.
	* **claimant_id**: Enter any claimant ID.
	* **insurance_number**: Enter the insurance number.
7. Enter claim location:
	* **claim_address**: Enter the claim address.
	* **claim_zip**: Enter the zip code of the claim address.
	* **claim_city**: Enter the city of the claim address.
	* **claim_state**: Enter the state of the claim address.
	* **claim_country**: Enter the country of the claim address.
8. Contact information:
	* **claim_email**: Enter the email address of the claimant.
	* **claim_phone**: Enter the phone number of the claimant.
	* **claim_fax**: Enter the fax number of the claimant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info**: Enter the patient's name.
* **Insurance Plan Info**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the patient's insurance plan is active or inactive.
* **Healthcare Provider**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the patient's healthcare provider is associated with the claim.
* **Claim Number**: Enter the claim number.
* **Claim Date**: Enter the date of the claim.
* **Claim Status**: Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate the status of the claim.
* **Claim Type**: Enter the type of claim (e.g., medical, dental, etc.).
* **Claim Amount**: Enter the claim amount.
* **Claim Details**: Enter any additional claim details.
* **Insurance Company**: Enter the name of the insurance company.
* **Insurance ID**: Enter any unique identifier for the insurance company.
* **Claimant ID**: Enter any claimant ID.
* **Insurance Number**: Enter the insurance number.
* **Claim Address**: Enter the claim address.
* **Claim Zip**: Enter the zip code of the claim address.
* **Claim City**: Enter the city of the claim address.
* **Claim State**: Enter the state of the claim address.
* **Claim Country**: Enter the country of the claim address.
* **Claim Email**: Enter the email address of the claimant.
* **Claim Phone**: Enter the phone number of the claimant.
* **Claim Fax**: Enter the fax number of the claimant.
* **Claim Note**: Enter any additional claim notes.
* **Claim Other**: Enter any additional claim information.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that the claim is processed correctly.
* Double-check all fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact the insurance company or claimant for clarification.
* This form is for verification purposes only and does not guarantee claim approval.
