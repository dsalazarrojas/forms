# Health Insurance Verification Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help verify the details of a health insurance policy. It collects personal and policy information to ensure accuracy and validity of the policyholder's identity and coverage.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the format of MM/DD/YY or MM/DD/AAAA.
3. Input your **Insurance Number** as per your policy details.
4. Provide your **Phone Number** and **Email** for contact purposes.
5. Enter your **Policyholder ID**, which is usually provided by your insurance provider.
6. Mention your **Insurance Provider** name.
7. Enter your **Group Number**, which is specific to your policy.
8. Select the **Effective Date**, **Expiration Date**, and **Policy Start Date** for your policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **required**): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **required**): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, **required**): Enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YY or MM/DD/AAAA.
* **Insurance Number** (`insurance_number`, text, **required**): Enter your insurance number or policy ID provided by your insurance provider.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, **required**): Enter your contact phone number for verification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **required**): Enter your contact email for verification purposes.
* **Policyholder ID** (`policyholder_id`, text, **required**): Enter your policyholder ID provided by your insurance provider.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, **required**): Mention your insurance provider name.
* **Group Number** (`group_number`, text, **required**): Enter your group number, which is specific to your policy.
* **Effective Date** (`effective_date`, date, **required**): Select the effective date of your policy.
* **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, date, **required**): Select the expiration date of your policy.
* **Policy Start Date** (`policy_start_date`, date, **required**): Select the policy start date.
* **Tips**: Make sure to double-check your details for accuracy to ensure smooth verification.
