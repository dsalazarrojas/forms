# Home Insurance Claim Records Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect details from policyholders to release their insurance claim records. It's a crucial step in the insurance claim process, and accurate information is required to ensure the release is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and any other details as requested in the Policy Holder Details section.
2. Enter your policy number and claim date.
3. Provide a brief reason for releasing your claim records.
4. If applicable, include details about any third parties involved in the claim.
5. Enter your email address (if applicable).
6. Sign and date the release (you can add multiple signatures if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Policy Holder Details** (`policyholder_details`, text, optional): This section is where you should enter your name and any other relevant details as requested.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, number, optional): Enter your policy number as issued by your insurance provider.
* **Claim Date** (`claim_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when your claim was filed.
* **Release Reason** (`release_reason`, text, optional): Provide a brief explanation for why you're releasing your claim records.
* **Third Party Details** (`third_party_details`, text, optional): If any third parties were involved in your claim, include their details here.
* **Third Party Name** (`third_party_name`, text, optional): Enter the name(s) of the third party involved.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address (if applicable) so we can contact you regarding your released claim.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when your claim records will be released.
* **Sign Off** (`sign_off`, note, optional): This is where you sign to confirm your release of claim records. Please print your name and date to sign.
* **Sign Off 2** (`sign_off_2`, note, optional): This is another signature line (if required). Print your name and date as above.
* **Sign Off 3** (`sign_off_3`, note, optional): A third signature line (if required). Print your name and date as above.
* **Sign Off 4** (`sign_off_4`, note, optional): A fourth signature line (if required). Print your name and date as above.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and fill out the form carefully.
* If you're unsure about any fields, please contact your insurance provider.
* Ensure accuracy in filling out the form to avoid any issues.
* Keep a copy of the completed form for your records.
* If you need to add more signatures, please use the additional signature lines as needed.
