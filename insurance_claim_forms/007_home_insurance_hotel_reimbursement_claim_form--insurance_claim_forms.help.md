# Home Insurance Hotel Reimbursement Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Insurance Hotel Reimbursement Claim Form is used by guests to submit claims for reimbursement for expenses incurred due to incidents that occurred while they were staying in a hotel. This form will collect information about the guest, the incident, and other relevant details to process the claim.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your guest name and contact information in the "Guest Details" section.
2. Describe the incident that occurred during your stay in the "Incident Details" section.
3. Provide information about your lodging arrangements, including the dates of stay and room number, in the "Lodging Details" section.
4. Enter the total amount of claimable expenses in the "Claim Amount" field.
5. Specify the reason for your claim in the "Claim Reason" field.
6. If applicable, attach any supporting documents related to your claim in the "Claim Documents" section.
7. The hotel's insurance team will review your claim and provide comments or updates in the "Insurer Comments" section.
8. Finally, select the current status of your claim from the options provided in the "Claim Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Details (Guest Details)**: Enter your name, address, email, and phone number so we can contact you with updates about your claim.
* **Incident Details (Incident Details)**: Describe the incident that occurred during your stay, including what happened, where it happened, and any relevant circumstances.
* **Lodging Details (Lodging Details)**: Provide the dates of your stay, room number, and any other relevant details about your accommodation.
* **Claim Amount (Claim Amount)**: Enter the total amount you are claiming for expenses related to the incident.
* **Claim Reason (Claim Reason)**: Specify the reason for your claim, such as "accident" or "theft".
* **Claim Documents (Claim Documents)**: If you have supporting documents related to your claim, upload them here (e.g. police report, medical records, etc.).
* **Insurer Comments (Insurer Comments)**: The hotel's insurance team will provide updates or comments about your claim here.
* **Claim Status (Claim Status)**: Select the current status of your claim from the options provided (Pending, Approved, Denied, In Progress).
