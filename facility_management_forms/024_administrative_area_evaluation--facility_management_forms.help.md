# Administrative Area Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate the condition and quality of administrative areas within an organization, providing a comprehensive assessment of various aspects of these areas.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have a good understanding of the areas being evaluated.
2. Choose the appropriate options for each field based on your observation and experiences.
3. Complete all fields that are marked as required.
4. Provide as much detail as possible in text fields (e.g., positive aspects, areas for improvement, maintenance issues, and improvement suggestions).
5. Select the correct priority level for improvements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluator Name** (`Evaluator Name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the evaluator.
* **Evaluator Department** (`Evaluator Department`, `select_one`, required): Choose the department you belong to.
* **Evaluation Date** (`Evaluation Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Area Evaluated** (`Area Evaluated`, `select_one`, required): Choose the administrative area being evaluated.
* **Location/Room Number** (`Location/Room Number`, `text`, required): Specify the location or room number of the area.
* **Cleanliness Rating** (`Cleanliness Rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the cleanliness of the area as:
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Maintenance Condition** (`Maintenance Condition`, `select_one`, required): Rate the maintenance condition of the area as:
	+ Excellent

	+ Poor
* **Lighting Quality** (`Lighting Quality`, `select_one`, required): Rate the lighting quality in the area as:

	+ Poor
* **Temperature Comfort** (`Temperature Comfort`, `select_one`, required): Rate the temperature comfort in the area as:
	+ Excellent

	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Noise Level** (`Noise Level`, `select_one`, required): Rate the noise level in the area as:

## Tips
* Be thorough and objective when evaluating the areas.
* Use the provided options to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the form maintainers.
* This form is a tool to help improve the condition and quality of administrative areas. Your feedback is valuable in making improvements.
