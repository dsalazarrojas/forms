# Transit Station Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about the safety of transit stations. It helps identify potential hazards and ensure that corrective actions are taken to maintain a safe environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Station ID number to identify the specific station being inspected.
2. Provide the name of the station in the `station_name` field.
3. Check the `walkway_clearance` field to report any clearance issues on walkways.
4. Select all safety equipment that are present and functioning properly in the `safety_equipment` field.
5. Choose the relevant safety procedures in place at the station in the `safety_procedures` field.
6. Report the location of safety equipment in the `safety_equipment_location` field.
7. List all hazards found during the inspection in the `hazards_found` field.
8. Describe the corrective actions taken or planned in the `corrective_actions` field.
9. Select the current status of the station in the `station_status` field.
10. Add any additional remarks in the `remarks` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Station ID (1)**: Enter the unique identifier for the station being inspected.
* **Station Name (2)**: Enter the name of the station being inspected.
* **Walkway Clearance (3)**: Check this field if there are any clearance issues on walkways.
* **Safety Equipment (4)**: Select all equipment that are present and functioning properly.
* **Safety Procedures (5)**: Choose the safety procedures followed at the station.
* **Safety Equipment Location (6)**: Report the location of safety equipment.
* **Hazards Found (7)**: Select all hazards detected during the inspection.
* **Corrective Actions (8)**: Describe the actions taken or planned to address hazards.
* **Station Status (9)**: Select the current status of the station.
* **Remarks (10)**: Add any additional comments or observations.
