# Mailbox Usage Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track the usage of a facility's mailboxes. It allows users to log their entry and exit times, the facility's location, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your User Email address in the corresponding field.
2. Select the Start Date and End Date of your mailbox usage.
3. Enter your User Name for identification purposes.
4. Select the Facility ID of the facility where you used the mailbox.
5. Choose your User Type (Yes or No) to indicate your affiliation with the facility.
6. Choose the Access Type (Yes or No) to indicate whether you accessed the mailbox.
7. Choose the Facility Location (Yes or No) to indicate the location of the facility.
8. Choose the Facility Floor (Yes or No) to indicate the floor of the facility where the mailbox is located.
9. Record the Access Duration (in minutes) for how long you used the mailbox.
10. Record the Access Count (a count of the number of times you accessed the mailbox).
11. Enter any additional Notes about your mailbox usage.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Email**: Enter your email address that is associated with your facility account. This is required for identification purposes.
* **Start Date**: Select the date and time you started using the mailbox.
* **End Date**: Select the date and time you finished using the mailbox.
* **User Name**: Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Facility ID**: Enter the ID of the facility where you used the mailbox.
* **User Type**: Select whether you are a regular user (Yes) or a guest (No).
* **Access Type**: Check whether you accessed the mailbox (Yes) or not (No).
* **Access Duration**: Record the time you spent using the mailbox (in minutes).
* **Access Count**: Record the number of times you accessed the mailbox.
* **Facility Location**: Check whether the mailbox is located in the main facility (Yes) or another location (No).
* **Facility Floor**: Check whether the mailbox is located on the main floor (Yes) or another floor (No).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information about your mailbox usage.
