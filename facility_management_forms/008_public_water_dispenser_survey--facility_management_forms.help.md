# Public Water Dispenser Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Water Dispenser Survey is a form designed to collect feedback and ratings from users about public water dispensers. It helps facility managers to understand how well-maintained and accessible the water dispensers are, and to identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. Provide clear and concise answers.
4. If you need help or clarification, contact the facility management team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Public Water Dispenser** (`water_dispenser_survey_1`, text, optional): Describe the location of the water dispenser (e.g., building name, room number).
* **How would you rate this water dispenser's cleanliness?** (`water_dispenser_survey_2`, number, optional): Rate the cleanliness of the water dispenser on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is very dirty and 5 is very clean.
* **How would you rate this water dispenser's maintenance status?** (`water_dispenser_survey_3`, number, optional): Rate the maintenance status of the water dispenser on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not maintained and 5 is well-maintained.
* **How would you rate this water dispenser's accessibility?** (`water_dispenser_survey_4`, text, optional): Describe how accessible you found the water dispenser (e.g., easy to reach, wheelchair accessible).
* **Water temperature** (`water_dispenser_survey_5`, number, optional): Record the current water temperature in degrees Celsius or Fahrenheit.
* **Is water temperature suitable for drinking?** (`water_dispenser_survey_6`, select_one, optional): Answer 'True' or 'False' to indicate if the water temperature is suitable for drinking.
* **What type of water is this dispenser providing?** (`water_dispenser_survey_7`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of water the dispenser is providing (e.g., Drinking water, Non-potable water, Other).
* **Would you like to report a maintenance request for this water dispenser?** (`water_dispenser_survey_8`, select_one, optional): Answer 'True' or 'False' to indicate if you want to report a maintenance request.
* **What issue do you report for this water dispenser?** (`water_dispenser_survey_9`, text, optional): Describe any issues you have with the water dispenser (e.g., leaky faucet, no paper available).
* **Date** (`water_dispenser_survey_10`, date, optional): Record the date you visited or used the water dispenser.
* **How long did you have to wait for the water dispenser?** (`water_dispenser_survey_11`, time, optional): Record the time it took for you to get to the water dispenser.
