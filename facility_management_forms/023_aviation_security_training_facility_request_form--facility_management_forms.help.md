# Aviation Security Training Facility Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a training facility for security-related programs. Please fill it out accurately to provide the necessary information for facility coordination.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in the required information for your training request, including your name, email address, and organization name.
- Choose the type of training you need.
- Select the number of attendees and preferred facility or lab.
- Specify the training start and end dates.
- Choose the preferred time slot for the training.
- If necessary, select any special equipment needed.
- Indicate the minimum security clearance level required for the area.
- Confirm the security badge status of attendees.
- Add any additional requirements or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Requester Full Name** (requester_name, text, required): Your full name as the person requesting the training.
- **Requester Email Address** (requester_email, email, required): Your email address associated with the training request.
- **Organization or Company Name** (requester_organization, text, required): The name of your organization or company requesting the training.
- **Employee ID** (requester_employee_id, text, required): Your employee ID, required for facility access.
- **Type of Training** (training_type, select_one, required): Choose the primary training category (e.g., X-ray Screening Procedures, Dangerous Goods (DG) Awareness, etc.).
- **Number of Attendees** (attendee_count, number, required): Enter the approximate number of people joining the training.
- **Preferred Facility or Lab** (requested_facility, select_one, required): Choose the preferred facility or lab for the training (e.g., Classroom A (Large), Screening Lab (Hands-on), etc.).
- **Training Start Date** (training_start_date, date, required): Enter the start date of the training session.
- **Training End Date** (training_end_date, date, required): Enter the end date of the training session.
- **Preferred Time Slot** (preferred_time_slot, select_one, required): Choose the preferred time slot for the training (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Full Day, etc.).
- **Special Equipment Needed** (special_equipment, select_multiple, optional): Select any special equipment needed for the training.
- **Security Clearance Level of Attendees** (security_clearance_level, select_one, required): Choose the minimum security clearance level required for the area.
- **Security Badge Status** (badge_status, select_one, required): Confirm if attendees have valid airport ID badges or if temporary or visitor passes are needed.
- **Additional Requirements or Comments** (additional_requirements, text, optional): Add any additional information for the facility coordinator.
- **I Agree to Comply with All Facility Security Rules and Regulations** (rule_agreement, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to comply with all facility security rules and regulations.
