# Equipment Storage Space Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book storage space in a facility for equipment, supplies, or tools.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Person" field.
2. Fill in the "Facility Contact Person" field with the name of the person responsible for facility management.
3. Type in the name of the facility where the storage space is to be booked in the "Facility Name" field.
4. Provide the location of the facility in the "Facility Location" field.
5. Select the type of equipment, supplies, or tools to be stored in the "Equipment Type" field.
6. Enter the size of the storage unit in square feet in the "Storage Unit Size" field.
7. Select the start and end dates of the booking in the "Booking Start Date" and "Booking End Date" fields, respectively.
8. Choose the booking status as "Active" or "Inactive" in the "Booking Status" field.
9. Choose the facility booking status as "Active" or "Inactive" in the "Facility Booking Status" field.
10. Enter the facility booking ID in the "Facility Booking ID" field.
11. Enter the storage unit capacity in square feet in the "Storage Unit Capacity" field.
12. Enter any remarks about the booking in the "Remarks" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Person**: Enter your name and contact information to be used as a reference for facility management.
* **Facility Contact Person**: The name of the person responsible for facility management.
* **Facility Name**: The name of the facility where the storage space is to be booked.
* **Facility Location**: The location of the facility where the storage space is to be booked.
* **Equipment Type**: Select the type of equipment, supplies, or tools to be stored.
* **Storage Unit Size**: Enter the size of the storage unit in square feet.
* **Booking Start Date**: The date when the storage space is to be booked.
* **Booking End Date**: The date when the storage space is to be returned.
* **Booking Status**: Choose the booking status as "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Facility Booking Status**: Choose the facility booking status as "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Facility Booking ID**: Enter the facility booking ID.
* **Storage Unit Capacity**: Enter the storage unit capacity in square feet.
* **Submit**: Enter any remarks about the booking.
* **Remarks**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the booking.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Enter accurate and complete information to ensure smooth processing of the booking request.
* Review and submit the form carefully before submitting it for approval.
