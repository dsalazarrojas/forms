# Family Room Guidelines Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a agreement for visitors to understand and confirm the guidelines for family rooms in the facility. It is a required form for all visitors to complete before entering the family rooms.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the form link to access the form.
2. Fill in the required fields, including visitor name and date.
3. Read and agree to the family room guidelines by signing the visitor signature field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **visitor_information** (visitor information, text, optional): Please provide your name and any other relevant information that is not included in the other fields.
* **visitor_signature** (visitor signature, text, optional): Sign your name to confirm that you have read and agree to the family room guidelines.
* **date** (date, date, optional): Enter the date you visited the family room.
# Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the facility management.
* This form is for internal use only and should not be shared or distributed without permission.
