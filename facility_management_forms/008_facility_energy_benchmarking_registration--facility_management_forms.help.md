# Facility Energy Benchmarking Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Facility Energy Benchmarking Registration" form is designed to help facilities collect and track their energy consumption and benchmarking status. This information is essential for understanding a building's energy usage, identify areas of improvement, and meet regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide facility details such as title, address, and contact information.
2. Describe the building's characteristics, including energy consumption patterns and energy source.
3. Choose the energy benchmarking participation status and programs involved.
4. Specify the energy consumption data collection methods and sources.
5. Provide information on facility ownership and management.
6. Select the building type and energy use types (e.g., heating, cooling, lighting).
7. Choose the energy benchmarking status and start/end dates (if applicable).
8. Select the energy consumption source (e.g., electricity, gas, water).
9. Choose the facility ID from the provided options.
10. Finally, provide a program pledge and confirm participation in energy benchmarking programs.
