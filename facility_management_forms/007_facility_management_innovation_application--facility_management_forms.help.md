# Facility Management Innovation Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and manage innovation ideas for facility management. Users can submit ideas for various stakeholders, including staff, vendors, and innovators, and specify the status of the idea.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name and email address to provide identification information.
2. Choose the submitter type (e.g., Staff, Vendors, Innovators).
3. Enter your facility name.
4. Add your idea title and description.
5. Select the status of your idea (e.g., Active, Pending, Archived).
6. Select the priority level of your idea.
7. Add any necessary attachments (e.g., images, documents).
8. Provide a reason for any status changes.
9. Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
