# Facility Upgrade Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for facility management voting, allowing users to provide their preferences and opinions on potential facility upgrades. It helps to gather valuable feedback from stakeholders about the upgrade's type, location, timing, budget, and other relevant aspects.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer according to your preferences and opinions.
2. Select the options that best match your views on the facility upgrade.
3. For open-ended questions, provide detailed and concise answers.
4. Ensure you answer all questions, if possible, to provide comprehensive feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of facility upgrade would you prefer?** (Select One)
    - This question helps us understand what kind of upgrade you think is best for the facility.
    - Please select one of the options: "Yes" or "No".
* **Which location would you prefer for the upgrade?** (Select One)
    - This question helps us determine the preferred location for the upgrade.
    - Please select one of the options: "Yes" or "No".
* **How many people would be impacted by the upgrade?** (Number)
    - This question helps us understand the potential impact of the upgrade on people.
    - Please enter a numerical value (0-100).
* **When would you like the upgrade to happen?** (Date)
    - This question helps us determine the preferred timeline for the upgrade.
    - Please enter a date in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-01-01).
* **What time of day would you prefer for the upgrade?** (Time)
    - This question helps us understand the preferred timing for the upgrade.
    - Please select one of the options: "Yes" or "No".
* **How would you like to receive updates about the upgrade?** (Select One)
    - This question helps us decide on the best method of communication for the upgrade.
    - Please select one of the options: "Yes" or "No".
* **What benefits do you think the upgrade would bring?** (Text)
    - This question helps us understand the expected benefits of the upgrade.

* **How do you feel about the upgrade's budget?** (Select One)
