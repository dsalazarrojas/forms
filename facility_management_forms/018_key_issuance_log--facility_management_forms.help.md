# Key Issuance Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Key Issuance Log is a record-keeping form used to track the issuance and return of keys within the organization. Its main purpose is to ensure that all key-related information is accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in the form title at the top of the page.
- Enter the date keys were issued.
- Enter the key serial number or description.
- Specify the condition of the key.
- Choose the status of the key (Active or Inactive).
- Enter the name of the employee or staff member responsible for the key.
- Enter the department or team the key is associated with.
- If keys are returned, fill in the date they were returned and add any comments.
- Fill in any additional comments about the key issuance or return.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Key Issuance Log** (form_title, text, required: false): This is the title of the form, which should be brief and descriptive.
- **Date Issued** (date_issued, date, required: false): The date the key was issued or returned.
- **Key Serial Number** (key_serial_number, text, required: false): A unique identifier or description of the key.
- **Key Description** (key_description, text, required: false): A detailed description of the key.
- **Key Condition** (key_condition, text, required: false): The current condition of the key.
- **Key Status** (key_status, select_one, required: false): The status of the key (Active or Inactive).
- **Employee Name** (employee_name, text, required: false): The name of the employee or staff member responsible for the key.
- **Department** (department, text, required: false): The department or team the key is associated with.
- **Date Returned** (date_returned, date, required: false): The date the key was returned (if applicable).
- **Comments** (comments, text, required: false): Additional comments about the key issuance or return.
