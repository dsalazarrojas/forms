# Digital Facility Management Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information for facilities within an organization's management system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Facility Details" section with the necessary information about the facility.
2. In the "Service Needs" section, describe the services required or needed for the facility.
3. In the "Registration Information" section, provide any additional details relevant to the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility Details (Facility Details)**: Enter a brief description of the facility, including its name, location, and any other relevant details.
* **Service Needs (Service Needs)**: Describe the services that are currently provided or required for the facility, such as maintenance, utilities, or administrative support.
* **Registration Information (Registration Information)**: Provide any additional information relevant to the registration process, such as the registration date or contact person.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you complete all required fields correctly to prevent duplication of efforts.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to your organization's facility management guidelines or contact the facility manager for clarification.
* Review your submission carefully before submitting it for review and approval.
