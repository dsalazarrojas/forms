# Facility Waste Recycling Approval Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be submitted by facilities to request approval for waste recycling activities. It ensures that the necessary information is provided to facilitate a smooth and efficient recycling process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter the title of your facility in the "Facility Waste Recycling Approval Request" field.
2. Select all the waste types that apply to your request.
3. Attach any supporting documentation that may be required for your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility Waste Recycling Approval Request** (Text Field, Required): This field is used to enter the title of your facility. Please provide a clear and concise name that will help us identify your facility.
* **Waste Types** (Select Multiple Field, Not Required): Please select all the waste types that are applicable to your request. This will help us to assess the type of waste and ensure that it is disposed of correctly.
* **Supporting Documentation** (Text Field, Not Required): If you have any supporting documentation, such as photos or videos, of the waste or the facility, please attach them to this field. This will help us to review your request more efficiently.
