# Cabinet Cleaning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the cleaning of cabinets within properties. It captures the essential details of each cleaning task, ensuring accurate record-keeping and reporting for facility management teams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Cleaner Name** field with the name of the person performing the cleaning task.
2. Select the **Cleaning Date** to record the date the task was performed.
3. Choose the **Cabinet Type** from the list of options to specify the type of cabinets cleaned.
4. Select the **Exterior Cleaned**, **Interior Cleaned**, **Doors and Frames Cleaned**, **Handles and Knobs Cleaned**, **Hinges Checked**, **Drawers Cleaned**, and **Shelves Wiped** options to report on the cleaning status of each component.
5. If any damage was found, select the **Damage Found** option and provide a brief description in the **Damage Description** field.
6. If any additional supplies are needed, type them in the **Supplies Needed** field.
7. Choose the **Time Spent** option to report on the duration of the cleaning task.
8. Select the **Task Completed** option to indicate whether the task was fully or partially completed, and if not completed, provide a reason in the **Reason if Incomplete** field.
9. Add any additional comments or observations in the **Additional Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Cleaner Name** (Required)
* Name of person performing the cleaning task.

### 2. **Cleaning Date** (Required)
* Date the cleaning task was performed.

### 3. **Cleaning Time** (Optional)
* Time taken to complete the cleaning task.

### 4. **Property Name** (Required)
* Name or address of the property.

### 5. **Property Location** (Required)
* Building or area within the property.

### 6. **Cabinet Type** (Required)
* Type of cabinets cleaned (e.g. Kitchen, Bathroom, Storage, etc.).

### 7. **Cabinet Material** (Optional)
* Type of material the cabinets are made of (e.g. Wood, Laminated, Metal, etc.).

### 8. **Exterior Cleaned** (Required)
* Was the cabinet exterior cleaned?

### 9. **Interior Cleaned** (Required)
* Was the cabinet interior cleaned?

### 10. **Doors and Frames Cleaned** (Required)
* Were all cabinet doors and frames cleaned?

### 11. **Handles and Knobs Cleaned** (Required)
* Were all cabinet handles and knobs cleaned?

### 12. **Hinges Checked** (Required)
* Were hinges inspected and cleaned?

### 13. **Drawers Cleaned** (Required)
* Were all drawers cleaned?

### 14. **Shelves Wiped** (Required)
* Were all shelves wiped down?

### 15. **Damage Found** (Required)
* Was any damage discovered during the cleaning process?

### 16. **Damage Description** (Optional)
* Brief description of the damage found.

### 17. **Cleaning Products Used** (Optional)
* List of cleaning products used (e.g. All-purpose cleaner, Glass cleaner, etc.).

### 18. **Supplies Needed** (Optional)
* Additional supplies needed for future cleaning tasks.

### 19. **Time Spent** (Optional)
* Duration of the cleaning task (e.g. Under 15 minutes, 15-30 minutes, etc.).

### 20. **Task Completed** (Required)
* Was the cleaning task fully or partially completed?

### 21. **Reason if Incomplete** (Optional)
* Reason why the task was not completed.

### 22. **Additional Notes** (Optional)
* Any additional comments or observations.
