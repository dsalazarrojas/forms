# Site And Equipment Management Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help facility management teams collect and analyze data on site and equipment status. It's essential to complete this form accurately to provide valuable insights for informed decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read the introductory page to understand the purpose and scope of the survey.
2. Fill out the "Site Information" field with relevant details about the site, such as its name, location, and any notable features.
3. Indicate whether or not you observed any equipment during the visit by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Equipment List" field.
4. Select the maintenance status of any observed equipment from the options provided in the "Maintenance Status" field.
5. Describe any safety practices you observed or did not observe during the visit in the "Safety Practices" field.
6. Add any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.
7. Enter the submitter's email address in the "Email" field.
8. Enter the submitter's phone number in the "Phone" field.
9. Enter the submitter's name in the "Submitter Name" field.
10. Enter the submitter's title in the "Submitter Title" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Site Information** (text, required/optional): Enter details about the site, such as its name, location, and any notable features.
* **Equipment List** (select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate whether or not you observed any equipment during the visit by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Maintenance Status** (select_one, required): Select the maintenance status of any observed equipment from the options provided.
* **Safety Practices** (text, required/optional): Describe any safety practices you observed or did not observe during the visit.
* **Comments** (text, required/optional): Add any additional comments or feedback.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter the submitter's email address.
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter the submitter's phone number.
* **Submitter Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the submitter's name.
* **Submitter Title** (text, required/optional): Enter the submitter's title.

Note: Completing this form is an essential part of the facility management process, and any inaccuracies or omissions may impact the quality of the collected data. Be thorough and accurate when filling out each field to provide valuable insights for informed decision-making.
