# Wheelchair Accessible Door Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wheelchair Accessible Door Request Form is a tool used to submit requests for making facilities more accessible to people with mobility impairments. This form helps staff to identify and prioritize requests for wheelchair-accessible doors and ensure that necessary modifications are made in a timely and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of facility that the request applies to (e.g., "Building", "Office", etc.).
2. Enter the location of the facility where the request is being made (e.g., "Room 101", "Main Lobby", etc.).
3. Choose the type of door you are requesting modifications for (e.g., "Automatic", "Manual", etc.).
4. If applicable, select any requested modifications (e.g., "Threshold Reduction", "Automatic Door Operator", etc.).
5. Enter any additional comments or information about the request.
6. Provide your name and contact information for follow-up purposes.
7. Select the staff member responsible for handling your request (e.g., "Facilities", "Security", etc.).
8. Choose a date and time for the visit to confirm the requested modifications (this will ensure that the requested modifications are made promptly).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submit Request**: Enter a brief description of your request.
* **Facility Type**: Choose the type of facility where you would like to request modifications (e.g., "Building", "Office", etc.). This will help staff prioritize requests based on facility type.
* **Facility Location**: Enter the exact location of the facility where you would like to make the request (e.g., "Room 101", "Main Lobby", etc.). This will help staff locate the facility quickly and efficiently.
* **Door Type**: Select the type of door you are requesting modifications for (e.g., "Automatic", "Manual", etc.). This will help staff understand the nature of the request.
* **Requested Modifications**: If applicable, select any requested modifications for the door (e.g., "Threshold Reduction", "Automatic Door Operator", etc.). This will help staff understand the specific modifications needed.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional information or comments about the request.
* **Submitter Information**: Enter your name and contact information for follow-up purposes.
* **Submitter Contact Info**: Enter your email address or contact phone number for staff to reach out to you for more information or clarification.
* **Date of Visit**: Enter the date you would like to visit the facility to confirm the requested modifications.
* **Time of Visit**: Enter the time you would like to visit the facility to confirm the requested modifications.
* **Staff Responsible**: Choose the staff member responsible for handling your request (e.g., "Facilities", "Security", etc.). This will help staff know who to direct the request to.
