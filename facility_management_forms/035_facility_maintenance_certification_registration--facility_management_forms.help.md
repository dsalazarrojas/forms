# facility_maintenance_certification_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The facility maintenance certification registration form is used to register facility maintenance certification. It captures information about the participant, employer, and certification session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant details in the **Participant Details** section.
2. Provide your employer details in the **Employer Details** section.
3. Enter session details in the **Session Details** section.
4. Select your certification status in the **Participant Certification** section.
5. Update your certification status in the **Certification Session Status Update** section.
6. Enter your facility manager's name and email in the **Facility Manager** section if applicable.
7. Select your schedule and certification session status in the **Schedule** and **Certification Session Status** sections.
8. Enter your availability in the **Availability** section.
9. Select your training provider and employer type in the **Training Provider** and **Employer Type** sections.
10. Update your certification status in the **Certification Status** section.
11. Select your participant status in the **Participant Status** section.
12. Select the type and status of the certification session in the **Certification Session Type** and **Certification Session Status Update** sections.
13. Enter your facility manager's name if applicable.
14. Enter notes for the certification session status update in the **Certification Session Status Update Notes** section.
15. Enter any additional comments in the **Participant Comments**, **Employer Comments**, and **Certification Session Status Update Notes** sections.
