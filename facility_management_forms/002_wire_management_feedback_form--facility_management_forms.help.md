# Wire Management Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wire Management Feedback Form is a tool used to gather feedback and issues related to wire management within an organization. This form is intended to capture specific problems or concerns related to wire management, ensuring that they are documented and addressed promptly. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Cable Organization" page and enter a brief description of the cable or wire management issue you are experiencing.
2. Choose the "Problem Area" where the issue is located or affecting.
3. Provide a detailed description of the problem in the "Problem Description" field.
4. Indicate the recurrence of the issue using the "Recurrence" select option (e.g., Cable Organization, Cable Management, Not Applicable).
5. Choose the impact of the issue using the "Impact" select option (e.g., Critical, Minor, Not Applicable).
6. Describe any action taken to address the issue in the "Action Taken" field.
7. Share any additional feedback or comments in the "Feedback" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email" field for contact purposes.
9. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field for further discussion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Cable Organization** (`cable_organization`, text, required): Enter the specific cable or wire management issue you are experiencing (e.g., faulty connection, damaged wire).
- **Problem Area** (`issue_area`, text, required): Choose the area where the issue is located or affecting (e.g., specific room, building, or system).
- **Problem Description** (`issue_description`, text, required): Describe the issue in detail, including any steps leading to the problem.
- **Recurrence** (`recurrence`, select_one, required): Indicate the recurrence of the issue (e.g., recurring problem, occasional issue, not applicable).
- **Impact** (`impact`, select_multiple, required): Select the impact of the issue (e.g., critical, minor, not applicable).
- **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, text, required): Describe any action taken to address the issue.
- **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): Share any additional feedback or comments regarding the issue.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for further discussion.
