# Facility Energy Monitoring Program Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to apply for the Facility Energy Monitoring Program, which helps facilities reduce their energy consumption and meet their sustainability goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct "Application Type" based on your affiliation with the facility.
2. Fill in the "Energy Usage Details" section with information about your facility's energy usage.
3. Provide the start and end dates of your energy usage period.
4. Choose the frequency of your energy usage (e.g., weekly, monthly, quarterly, or yearly).
5. Enter any additional notes about your energy usage (optional).
6. Enter the name, phone number, and email of the contact person responsible for this application.
7. Enter the utility company or service provider associated with your facility.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Type**: 
  * Select the type of application:
    - **Utility**: If you are applying on behalf of a utility company.
    - **Agency**: If you are applying on behalf of an agency or government organization.
    - **Sustainability**: If you are applying for the program to help reduce your facility's energy consumption.
* **Energy Usage Details**: 
  * Describe your facility's energy usage, including the amount of energy consumed (in units, e.g., kWh) and any relevant energy-saving initiatives or projects.
* **Energy Usage Start Date**: 
  * Enter the start date of your energy usage period (e.g., 01/01/2022).
* **Energy Usage End Date**: 
  * Enter the end date of your energy usage period (e.g., 12/31/2022).
* **Energy Usage Frequency**: 
  * Choose the frequency of your energy usage:
    - **Weekly**: If your energy usage is measured weekly.
    - **Monthly**: If your energy usage is measured monthly.
    - **Quarterly**: If your energy usage is measured quarterly.
    - **Yearly**: If your energy usage is measured yearly.
* **Energy Usage Notes** (optional): 
  * Enter any additional notes about your energy usage, such as specific energy-saving projects or initiatives.
* **Energy Usage Additional Notes** (optional): 
  * Enter any additional notes or comments about your energy usage.
* **Contact Name**: 
  * Enter the name of the contact person responsible for this application.
* **Contact Phone**: 
  * Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (optional): 
  * Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Utility**: 
  * Enter the name of the utility company or service provider associated with your facility.
* **Agency** (optional): 
  * Enter the name of the agency or government organization associated with your facility.
* **Sustainability** (optional): 
  * Enter any relevant sustainability initiatives or projects related to your facility's energy usage.

Note: This help guide is a general explanation of the form. Please refer to the specific requirements and rules provided by the Facility Energy Monitoring Program administrators for any specific requirements or guidelines.
