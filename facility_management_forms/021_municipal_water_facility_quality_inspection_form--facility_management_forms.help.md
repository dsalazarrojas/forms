# municipal water facility quality inspection form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the details of a quality inspection at a municipal water facility. It captures the essential information about the inspection, including the date and time of the inspection, the duration of the inspection, and the details of the facility being inspected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the details of the water facility being inspected, including the facility's name or ID.
2. Enter the date and time of the inspection, using the format MM/DD/YY HH:MM AM/PM.
3. Enter the duration of the inspection, in minutes.
4. Enter the name of the inspector conducting the inspection.
5. Enter the contact information of the inspector, such as their name and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Water Facility Details
* **Water Facility Details**: Enter the name or ID of the water facility being inspected.

### Inspection Date
* **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the inspection, in the format MM/DD/YY.

### Inspection Time
* **Inspection Time**: Enter the start time of the inspection, in the format HH:MM AM/PM.

### Inspection Duration
* **Inspection Duration**: Enter the duration of the inspection, in minutes.

### Inspector Name
* **Inspector Name**: Enter the name of the inspector conducting the inspection.

### Inspector Contact
* **Inspector Contact**: Enter the contact information of the inspector, such as their name and phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete inspection records.
* Double-check the information entered to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Save a copy of the form for future reference.
