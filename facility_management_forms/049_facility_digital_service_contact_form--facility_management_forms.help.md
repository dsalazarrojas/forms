# Facility Digital Service Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facility Digital Service Contact Form is designed to collect information from users about facility digital services. It helps facility management teams to identify areas that require assistance and allocate resources accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select the location** of the facility where the digital service is needed. Choose the correct location from the dropdown menu.
2. **Enter the contact person** responsible for the digital service. This is an optional field, but providing this information will help us to contact the right person for follow-up.
3. **Describe the service request**. This is also an optional field, but it will help us to understand the nature of the service required.
4. **Choose the priority** of the service. Select from Low, Medium, or High to indicate the urgency of the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **location**: Select the facility location where the digital service is needed.
	+ Options: Location 1, Location 2
* **contact_person**: Enter the person responsible for the digital service.
	+ Optional, but helps us to contact the right person for follow-up.
* **service_request**: Describe the digital service needed.
	+ Optional, but helps us to understand the nature of the service required.
* **service_priority**: Choose the priority of the service.
	+ Low, Medium, or High to indicate the urgency of the service.
* **comment**: Provide any additional comments or information about the service.
