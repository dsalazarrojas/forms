# Medical Device Maintenance Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a structured way of recording and tracking medical device maintenance records, which helps ensure that medical devices are kept in good working condition and are serviced or repaired promptly when needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **User ID**, which is your unique identifier for this facility.
2. Enter the **Device ID**, which is the unique identifier for the medical device being maintained or serviced.
3. Provide the **Service Date** and **Service Start Time** and **Service End Time** for the maintenance activity.
4. Enter the **Technician ID**, which is the name of the technician who performed the service or maintenance.
5. Provide a brief **Description** of the maintenance activity.
6. Enter the **Facility ID**, which is the unique identifier for the facility where the maintenance took place.
7. Select the **Status**, which indicates the maintenance status (Active, Maintenance, Repair, or Decommissioned).
8. Enter the **Category**, which is the category or type of maintenance activity.
9. Provide any additional **Notes** about the maintenance activity.
10. If relevant, enter the **Email**, **Phone**, **Address**, **Location**, **Contact Name**, **Contact Email**, and **Contact Phone** details.
11. Optionally, provide **Equipment Model**, **Equipment Serial Number**, **Asset Tag**, **Manufacturer**, and **Model Year** details if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User ID**: A unique identifier for the user filling out this form.
* **Device ID**: A unique identifier for the medical device being maintained or serviced.
* **Service Date**: The date the maintenance or service took place.
* **Service Start Time** and **Service End Time**: The start and end times of the maintenance or service activity.
* **Technician ID**: The name of the technician who performed the service or maintenance.
* **Description**: A brief description of the maintenance activity.
* **Facility ID**: A unique identifier for the facility where the maintenance took place.
* **Status**: The status of the device during the maintenance or service activity (Active, Maintenance, Repair, or Decommissioned).
* **Category**: The category or type of maintenance activity.
* **Notes**: Any additional comments or notes about the maintenance activity.
* **Email**: The email address of the contact person.
* **Phone**: The phone number of the contact person.
* **Address**: The address of the contact person or facility.
* **Location**: The location where the maintenance took place.
* **Contact Name**: The name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email**: The email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: The phone number of the contact person.
* **Department**: The department or section of the facility where the maintenance took place.
* **Service Type**: The type of service or maintenance activity.
* **Equipment Model**: The model number of the medical device.
* **Equipment Serial**: The serial number of the medical device.
* **Asset Tag**: The asset tag number of the medical device.
* **Manufacturer**: The manufacturer of the medical device.
* **Model Year**: The year the medical device was manufactured.
