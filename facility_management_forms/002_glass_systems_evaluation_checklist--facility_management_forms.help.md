# Glass Systems Evaluation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Glass Systems Evaluation Checklist is a form designed to evaluate the condition and performance of glass systems in buildings or facilities. This form helps assessors gather information on the systems' condition, inspection frequency, performance, and maintenance needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page of the form carefully and follow the instructions provided.
2. For each field, provide the requested information based on your observation and assessment.
3. If a field is marked as required, please ensure you complete it accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Glass Systems Evaluation Checklist Label** (`glass_systems_evaluation_checklist_label`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the glass systems being evaluated.
* **Inspection Frequency** (`inspection_frequency`, text, optional): Record the frequency of inspections performed on the glass systems.
* **Glass Condition** (`glass_condition`, text, optional): Describe the overall condition of the glass systems, including any issues or concerns.
* **Hardware Performance** (`hardware_performance`, text, optional): Report on the performance of the glass systems' hardware components.
* **Maintenance Needs** (`maintenance_needs`, text, optional): Indicate any necessary maintenance or repairs required for the glass systems.
* **Glass Condition Observations** (`glass_condition_observations`, text, optional): Provide any specific observations or notes about the glass systems' condition.
* **Hardware Performance Observations** (`hardware_performance_observations`, text, optional): Record any notable observations or issues related to the hardware performance.
* **Maintenance Needs Observations** (`maintenance_needs_observations`, text, optional): Note any specific maintenance needs or concerns.
* **Glass Systems Checklist Form Comments** (`glass_systems_checklist_form_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or remarks about the evaluation process.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you complete all required fields accurately.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or refer to your standard operating procedures.
* This form is intended to be used for general information gathering and evaluation purposes only. If you need to report any issues or concerns, please use the appropriate channels and procedures in your organization.
