# Mail Service Disruption Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about incidents of mail service disruptions that have occurred in the mail service department. It helps to track and record these incidents for analysis and resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by selecting the "Service Disruption Report" page. This is the starting point for reporting an incident.
2. On the "Service Disruption Report" page, select the type of disruption that occurred:
	* Postal Delay: Select this option if the mail service was delayed due to reasons such as weather, high volume, or equipment failure.
	* Mailroom Issue: Choose this option if there was a problem within the mailroom, such as a mechanical failure or staff shortage.
	* Other: Select this option if the disruption is of an unknown or miscellaneous nature.
3. Move on to the "Disruption Description" page, where you will provide a detailed description of the service disruption that occurred. This field is free text, so provide as much information as needed to thoroughly describe the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Disruption Report** (`mail_service_disruption_report_form_page_1`, text, optional): This is the first page of the form where you select the type of service disruption that occurred. Please choose one of the options provided to categorize the incident accurately.
* **Disruption Type** (`mail_service_disruption_report_form_page_2`, select_one, optional): This field helps to further specify the type of disruption that occurred. Please select one of the options: Postal Delay, Mailroom Issue, or Other.
* **Disruption Description** (`mail_service_disruption_report_form_page_3`, text, optional): This is the detailed description of the service disruption that occurred. Please provide as much information as possible, including the cause, location, and effects of the incident.

## Tips
* Please provide as much detail as possible in the "Disruption Description" field to ensure that the report is accurate and thorough.
* If you are unsure of the correct type of disruption, please choose "Other" and provide an explanation in the "Disruption Description" field. 
* Ensure that the form is only completed for incidents that have not been previously reported to avoid duplication of effort.
