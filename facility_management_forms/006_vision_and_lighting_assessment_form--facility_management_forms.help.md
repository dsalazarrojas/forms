# Vision And Lighting Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vision and Lighting Assessment Form is a tool used by facility managers to evaluate the lighting conditions within their facilities. This form helps identify any issues with the lighting and outlines steps for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully to provide accurate information about your facility's lighting conditions.
2. Enter your position within the organization to provide context to your responses.
3. Fill out the form completely, as indicated by the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. What is your position in the organization? * (Text Field)
* This field is used to identify your role within the organization.
* Please enter your job title or position to help us understand your perspective.

### 2. What is the name of your facility? * (Text Field)
* This field collects the name of the facility you are assessing.
* Please ensure that the name is accurate and complete.

### 3. What is the overall condition of the lighting at your facility? * (Text Field)
* This field asks for a general description of the lighting conditions at your facility.
* Please provide a brief but thorough assessment of the lighting's overall state.

### 4. What is the current lighting level at your facility? (Select One)
* This field asks you to choose the current lighting level at your facility from the following options:
	+ Adequate
	+ Inadequate

### 5. What is the intensity of the lighting at your facility? (Select One)
* This field asks you to choose the intensity of the lighting at your facility from the following options:
	+ Sufficient
	+ Insufficient

### 6. Please note any observations about the lighting conditions at your facility (Text Field)
* This field is for any additional comments or observations you have about the lighting conditions.
* Please be as detailed as possible.

### 7. If there is an issue with the lighting, please report it (Text Field)
* This field is for reporting any specific issues with the lighting.
* Please provide a clear description of the problem.

### 8. What action will be taken to address the issue? (Text Field)
* This field asks for a description of the planned action to address the issue.
* Please be specific about the steps that will be taken.

### 9. What is the next action due date? (Date Field)
* This field asks for the date when the next action is due.
* Please enter the date in a standard format (e.g., MM/DD/YY).

### 10. What is the status of the follow-up? (Select One)
* This field asks you to choose the status of the follow-up from the following options:
	+ Completed
	+ Ongoing
	+ Scheduled

### 11. What is the follow-up frequency? (Select One)
* This field asks you to choose the frequency of the follow-up from the following options:
	+ Weekly
	+ Bi-Weekly
	+ Monthly

### 12. How will the follow-up be conducted? (Select One)
* This field asks you to choose the method of the follow-up from the following options:
	+ Phone Call
	+ Email
	+ Meeting

### 13. Please enter any follow-up comments (Text Field)
* This field is for any additional comments about the follow-up.
* Please provide any further information that may be helpful. 

## Tips

* Please answer each question carefully and accurately.
* If unsure about any field, please reach out to your administrator or facility manager for clarification.
* Complete the form in a single session, as saving progress may not be supported.
* The form is for facility maintenance and upkeep purposes only. If used in other contexts, please ensure it's adapted and modified accordingly. 

This user-facing help guide is designed to be concise and concrete, helping end-users complete the form efficiently and accurately. Each field is explained in a way that minimizes confusion and ensures a thorough understanding of the form's purpose and requirements.
