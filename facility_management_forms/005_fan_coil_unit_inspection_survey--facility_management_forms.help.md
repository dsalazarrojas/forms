    *   **Notes**: Write any additional comments or notes about the unit or its maintenance.
    *   **Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback from the inspection or maintenance team.

    *   **Assigned To Maintenance Team**: Choose whether the maintenance task is assigned to a team.
    *   **Priority**: Select the priority level for the maintenance task.
