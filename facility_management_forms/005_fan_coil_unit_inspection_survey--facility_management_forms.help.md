## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Fan Coil Unit ID**: A unique identifier for the fan coil unit. Enter the unit's ID number.
*   **Building Location**: The location of the building where the unit is installed. Type the building's name or address.
*   **Room Number**: The room number where the unit is located. Enter the correct room number.
*   **Air Handler Type**: Check if the unit has an air handler type (Yes or No). Select 'Yes' or 'No' accordingly.
*   **Fan Coil Unit Capacity**: Enter the fan coil unit's capacity rating. This will help track the unit's performance and maintenance needs.
*   **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the most recent inspection. This is essential for tracking the unit's history and schedule.
*   **Next Inspection Date**: Schedule the next inspection date for the unit.
*   **Last Inspection By**: Record the name of the person who performed the last inspection.
*   **Equipment Last Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the last equipment inspection.
*   **Maintenance Due Date**: Schedule the next maintenance due date for the unit.
*   **Maintenance Performed**: Check if maintenance was performed on the unit (Yes or No). Select 'Yes' or 'No' accordingly.
*   **Maintenance Status**: Choose the maintenance status of the unit (Not Started, In Progress, or Completed).
*   **Last Maintenance By**: Record the name of the person who performed the last maintenance.
*   **Notes**: Write any additional comments or notes about the unit or its maintenance.
*   **Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback from the inspection or maintenance team.
*   **Status**: Select the current status of the unit (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
*   **Reason for Status Change**: Enter the reason for any status changes.
*   **Next Inspection**: Schedule the next inspection date for the unit.
*   **Assigned To Maintenance Team**: Choose whether the maintenance task is assigned to a team (Yes or No).
*   **Priority**: Set the priority level for the maintenance task (High, Medium, or Low).
*   **Category**: Enter the category of the fan coil unit (e.g., air conditioning, heating, or ventilation).
*   **Sub Category**: Enter the subcategory of the fan coil unit (e.g., air handler, fan coil, or coil cleaning).
*   **Last Inspection By**: Record the name of the person who performed the last inspection.
*   **Assigned To**: Enter the name of the person or team assigned to perform the maintenance.
*   **Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback from the maintenance team.

## Tips

*   Be accurate when filling out the fields, especially the inspection and maintenance dates.
*   Ensure you assign the correct priority level and maintenance status for each unit.
*   Keep the notes and comments up-to-date to track any issues or concerns.
*   Use the provided fields to track and monitor the fan coil units effectively.
