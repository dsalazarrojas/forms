# Maintenance Service Company Information Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect company information from maintenance service providers. Please complete this form to provide your company's details and service information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the company information section with your company's details.
2. Enter the vendor name, email, phone number, and address in the respective fields.
3. Add any additional notes about your company in the vendor notes field.
4. Provide the maintenance service information in the maintenance service field.
5. If applicable, enter the vendor's service start date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Information**: This field is used to collect your company's name.
* **Vendor Name**: This field is used to collect your company's name.
* **Vendor Email**: This field is used to collect your company's email address. Please enter a valid email address.
* **Vendor Phone**: This field is used to collect your company's phone number.
* **Vendor Notes**: This field is used to collect any additional information about your company.
* **Maintenance Service**: This field is used to collect the service information provided by your company.
* **Vendor Address**: This field is used to collect your company's address.
* **Vendor Service Start Date**: This field is used to collect the start date of your company's service. If applicable, please enter a valid date.
* **Vendor License Number**: This field is used to collect your company's license number. If applicable, please enter a valid license number.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant department for guidance.
* Please use a valid email address for the vendor email field.
* Please use a valid phone number for the vendor phone field.
* Please use a valid date for the vendor service start date field.
* Please use a valid license number for the vendor license number field if applicable.
