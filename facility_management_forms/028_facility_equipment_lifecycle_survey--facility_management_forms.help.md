# Facility Equipment Lifecycle Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the maintenance and status of facility equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all applicable equipment from the "Equipment List" field.
2. Provide general information about the selected equipment in the "General Info" field.
3. Report the equipment's maintenance history in the "Maintenance History" field.
4. Indicate any replacement needs in the "Replacement Needs" field.
5. Describe the asset's condition in the "Asset Condition" field.
6. Specify the equipment's type, model, year purchased, cost, and location in their respective fields.
7. Provide contact information for the equipment's owner or operator in the "Contact" field.
8. Select the current status of the equipment in the "Facility Equipment Status" field.
9. Enter any relevant comments or additional information in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (`general_info`, text, optional): Enter general information about the equipment, such as its purpose or any relevant notes.
* **Equipment List** (`equipment_list`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable equipment from the list provided.
* **Maintenance History** (`maintenance_history`, text, optional): Describe the equipment's maintenance history, including any repairs or upgrades.
* **Replacement Needs** (`replacement_needs`, select_one, optional): Indicate if the equipment needs replacement or not.
* **Asset Condition** (`asset_condition`, text, optional): Describe the current condition of the asset.
* **Type** (`asset_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of asset (e.g., machinery, vehicle, etc.).
* **Model** (`asset_model`, text, optional): Enter the model of the asset.
* **Year Purchased** (`asset_year_purchased`, number, optional): Enter the year the asset was purchased.
* **Cost** (`asset_cost`, number, optional): Enter the original purchase cost of the asset.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Identify the location where the asset is used.
* **Contact** (`contact`, text, optional): Provide contact information for the equipment's owner or operator.
* **Facility Equipment Status** (`facility_equipment_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable statuses (e.g., operational, non-operational, etc.).
* **Asset Tag** (`asset_tag`, text, optional): Enter the asset's tag or ID number.
* **Asset Serial Number** (`asset_serial_number`, text, optional): Enter the asset's serial number.
* **Asset Vendor** (`asset_vendor`, text, optional): Identify the asset's vendor or manufacturer.
* **Asset Description** (`asset_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the asset.
* **Asset Category** (`asset_category`, text, optional): Select the category of the asset (e.g., machinery, vehicle, etc.).
* **Subcategory** (`asset_subcategory`, text, optional): Select the subcategory of the asset (e.g., machinery - HVAC, etc.).
* **Facility Equipment Condition** (`facility_equipment_condition`, text, optional): Describe the current condition of the facility equipment.
* **Maintenance Frequency** (`maintenance_frequency`, number, optional): Indicate how often the equipment is maintained.
* **Last Maintenance Date** (`last_maintenance_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last maintenance.
* **Next Maintenance Date** (`next_maintenance_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the next maintenance.
* **Last Maintenance Cost** (`last_maintenance_cost`, number, optional): Enter the cost of the last maintenance.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the equipment.

## Tips

* Ensure all applicable equipment is selected in the "Equipment List" field.
* Be accurate and detailed when providing information in each field.
* Review and update the form regularly to ensure the equipment's lifecycle information is up-to-date.
