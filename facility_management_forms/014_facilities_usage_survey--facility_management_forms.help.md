# Facilities Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facilities Usage Survey is a form designed to collect information about employee usage patterns and preferences of various facilities within an organization, aiming to improve facilities management by optimizing scheduling, resource allocation, and maintenance decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above, ensuring that you provide accurate and honest answers to each field. This will help management gather valuable insights into your usage habits and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Usage Survey Form**: Please enter your name and any other relevant details.
	+ This field is for your identification and any other information you'd like to share.
* **Usage Patterns**: Select the time of day you most often visit the facilities.
	+ This field will help us understand when you visit the facilities, whether it's during morning, afternoon, evening, or on weekends.
* **Satisfaction Level**: Indicate how satisfied you are with the facilities' overall condition and services.
	+ This field measures your satisfaction level with the facilities, ranging from Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied.
* **Usage Frequency**: Choose how often you visit the facilities.
	+ This field helps us understand how frequently you use the facilities, whether it's daily, weekly, monthly, or rarely.
* **Facilities Used**: Select the facilities you have used in the past month.
	+ This field lists the facilities you have used, such as the gym, office, classroom, or community space.
* **Facilities Used (Other)**: If any other facilities have been used, please specify them in this field.
	+ This field is for any other facilities not listed in the previous field.
* **Comments**: Share any additional comments or suggestions you have about the facilities.
	+ This field is for any feedback or suggestions you have about the facilities, services, or management.
* **Email**: If you have any email address where you can be reached for follow-up questions or comments.
	+ This field is for any email address where you can be contacted for further discussion or clarification.
* **Phone**: If you have any phone number where you can be reached for follow-up questions or comments.
	+ This field is for any phone number where you can be contacted for further discussion or clarification.
* **Date Last Visited**: Please enter the date of your last visit to the facilities.
	+ This field records the date of your last visit to the facilities.
* **Time Last Visited**: Enter the time of your last visit to the facilities.
	+ This field records the time of your last visit to the facilities.

## Tips
- Please answer the questions honestly and accurately to help us improve facilities management.
- If you have any further questions or need clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us at the email or phone number provided.
