# Facility Cleaning Service Benchmark Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from users about the cleanliness of a facility. It helps the facility management team to assess the quality of cleaning services and make informed decisions about how to improve them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully and make sure you have a good understanding of the facilities you are rating.
* Answer all questions accurately and honestly.
* For the `facility_name` field, enter the name of the facility you are rating.
* For the `facility_rating`, `satisfaction`, and `facility_cleanliness` fields, rate the cleanliness of the facility on a scale of 1 to 10.
* For the `improvement_feedback` field, provide any comments or suggestions you have for improving the cleaning services.
* If you are the facility manager or have contact information for the manager, please enter their name, email, and phone number in the `manager_name`, `manager_email`, and `manager_phone` fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Facility Name
* **facility_name** (`facility_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the facility you are rating.

### Facility Rating
* **facility_rating** (`facility_rating`, number, required/optional): Rate the cleanliness of the facility on a scale of 1 to 10.

### Cleanliness
* **facility_cleanliness** (`facility_cleanliness`, number, required/optional): Rate the cleanliness of the facility on a scale of 1 to 10.

### Satisfaction
* **satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, number, required/optional): Rate your satisfaction with the cleaning services on a scale of 1 to 10.

### Improvement Feedback
* **improvement_feedback** (`improvement_feedback`, text, required/optional): Provide any comments or suggestions you have for improving the cleaning services.

### Manager Contact Information
* **manager_name** (`manager_name`, text, required/optional): If you are the facility manager or have contact information for them, enter their name.
* **manager_email** (`manager_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the manager's email address.
* **manager_phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the manager's phone number.
* **manager_contact** (`manager_contact`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional contact information for the manager.
