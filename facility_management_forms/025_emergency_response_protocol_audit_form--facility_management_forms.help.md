# Emergency Response Protocol Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and track emergency response protocol audits at various locations. It is designed to collect information about the visit, the response protocol, and follow-up actions taken after the visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the location's information, including the name of the location, date of the visit, and response protocol in place.
2. Describe the follow-up action taken after the visit, including any next steps or actions to be taken.
3. Provide a review from the location manager, including any notes or comments.
4. Document the follow-up date and status.
5. Enter the name, phone number, and email of the location manager.
6. Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Location Info (1)**: Enter the name of the location where the emergency response protocol audit took place.
* **Date Visited (2)**: Enter the date of the visit in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Response Protocol (3)**: Describe the emergency response protocol in place at the location.
* **Follow Up Action (4)**: Describe the follow-up action taken after the visit.
* **Location Manager Review (5)**: Enter a review or comment from the location manager, including any notes or observations.
* **Follow Up Date (6)**: Enter the date for the next follow-up visit or check-in.
* **Follow Up Status (7)**: Select the status of the follow-up, Active or Inactive.
* **Follow Up Next Action (8)**: Describe the next step or action to be taken.
* **Follow Up Notes (9)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the follow-up action.
* **Location Manager Name (10)**: Enter the name of the location manager responsible for the protocol.
* **Location Manager Phone (11)**: Enter the phone number of the location manager.
* **Location Manager Email (12)**: Enter the email address of the location manager.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields correctly.
* Enter accurate and complete information to ensure that records are maintained correctly.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all fields are filled out correctly.
