# Facility Energy Data Publishing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facility Energy Data Publishing Consent Form is designed to gather user consent for the publishing of facility energy data. This form is used to ensure that the facility has given explicit consent for the publication of their energy data, which will be used for reporting and analytics purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, select the appropriate option for each question and provide your consent.
2. You will need to select one of the options for each question, as indicated by the label next to each field.
3. If you are unsure about any of the questions, you can refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Energy Data Publishing Consent** (`facility_energy_data_publishing_consent_form`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Select all options that apply to the facility energy data that you are consenting to publish.
* **Facility Energy Data Publishing Consent Form 1** (`facility_energy_data_publishing_consent_form_1`, text, optional):
	+ This field is an optional text field where you can provide additional information or context about your consent.
* **Facility Energy Data Publishing Consent Form 2** (`facility_energy_data_publishing_consent_form_2`, text, optional):
	+ This field is also an optional text field where you can provide additional information or context about your consent.
* **Facility Energy Data Publishing Consent Form 3** (`facility_energy_data_publishing_consent_form_3`, text, optional):
	+ This field is another optional text field where you can provide additional information or context about your consent.
* **Facility Energy Data Publishing Consent Form 4** (`facility_energy_data_publishing_consent_form_4`, text, optional):
	+ This field is another optional text field where you can provide additional information or context about your consent.
* **Facility Energy Data Publishing Consent Form 5** (`facility_energy_data_publishing_consent_form_5`, select_one, required):
	+ Select one option to indicate your consent for the publication of the facility energy data.
* **Facility Energy Data Publishing Consent Form 6** (`facility_energy_data_publishing_consent_form_6`, select_one, optional):
	+ This field is an optional select-one field where you can select one option to indicate your consent.
* **Facility Energy Data Publishing Consent Form 7** (`facility_energy_data_publishing_consent_form_7`, select_one, optional):
	+ This field is another optional select-one field where you can select one option to indicate your consent.
* **Facility Energy Data Publishing Consent Form 8** (`facility_energy_data_publishing_consent_form_8`, select_one, optional):
	+ This field is another optional select-one field where you can select one option to indicate your consent.

Tips:
* Make sure to read each question carefully and select the appropriate option for your facility.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, you can refer to the field-by-field explanation above.
