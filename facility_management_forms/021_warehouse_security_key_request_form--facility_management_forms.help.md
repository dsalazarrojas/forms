# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for facility management, covering various aspects of facility operations and management. It captures essential information from users, including facility managers, shift managers, and other stakeholders. The form helps track and record facility-related activities, requests, and approvals in a structured and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all the required fields as prompted.
2. Ensure all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
3. Review and proofread your submissions before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title** (title): Enter the title of the facility or project being managed.
* **User Input** (user_input): Provide a brief description of the user's input or request.
* **Date** (date): Enter the date of the user's input or request.
* **Purpose** (purpose): Describe the purpose or reason for the user's input or request.
* **Authorized By** (authorized_by): Enter the name of the authorized person or entity.
* **Requestor** (requestor): Provide the name of the person or entity making the request.
* **Department** (department): Identify the department involved or responsible for the facility management.
* **Shift Manager** (shift_manager): Enter the name of the shift manager or supervisor.
* **Shift Manager Approval** (shift_manager_approval): Check the box to indicate the shift manager's approval.
* **Policy Acknowledgment** (policy_acknowledgment): Confirm that the user has read and understood the policies and procedures.
* **Reason For Request** (reason_for_request): Provide a detailed explanation for the user's request.
* **Date Approved** (date_approved): Enter the date when the request was approved.
* **Approved By** (approved_by): Enter the name of the person or entity that approved the request.
* **Signature** (signature): Provide a digital or physical signature to authenticate the user's identity.
* **Facility Manager Approval** (facility_manager_approval): Check the box to indicate the facility manager's approval.
* **Facility Manager Name** (facility_manager_name): Enter the name of the facility manager.
* **Facility Manager Title** (facility_manager_title): Enter the title or position of the facility manager.
* **Facility Manager Phone** (facility_manager_phone): Enter the contact phone number of the facility manager.
* **Facility Manager Email** (facility_manager_email): Enter the contact email of the facility manager.
* **Facility Manager Comments** (facility_manager_comments): Provide additional comments or feedback from the facility manager.
* **Facility Approvals** (facility_approvals): List the facility approvals or approvals from other facility managers.
* **Reason For Rejection** (reason_for_rejection): Provide a reason for rejection if the request was not approved.
* **Signature Facility** (signature_facility): Provide a digital or physical signature to authenticate the facility manager's identity.
* **Date Facility** (date_facility): Enter the date when the facility manager approved or rejected the request.
* **Facility Manager Approval 2** (facility_manager_approval_2): Check the box to indicate the facility manager's second approval.
* **Facility Manager Signature** (facility_manager_signature): Provide a digital or physical signature to authenticate the facility manager's identity.

## Tips

* Make sure to review and proofread your submissions before submitting.
* Ensure all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
* Use clear and concise language in all your submissions.
* If you encounter any issues or need assistance, contact the form's administrators or support team.
