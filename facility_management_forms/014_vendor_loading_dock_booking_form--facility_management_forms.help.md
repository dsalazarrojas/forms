# Vendor Loading Dock Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a vendor loading dock appointment. To complete this form, please follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the contact information section with your name, email, phone number, and company name.
- Provide vehicle details, including the year, make, model, and color of your vehicle.
- Schedule your appointment by selecting a date and time that works for you.
- Choose a time zone that aligns with your location.
- Add any additional notes about your appointment.
- Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit your booking request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Information (Contact Information)**: Enter your name, phone number, and company name to allow us to contact you about your appointment.
- **First Name (Contact First Name)**: Enter your first name.
- **Last Name (Contact Last Name)**: Enter your last name.
- **Email (Contact Email)**: Enter your email address.
- **Phone (Contact Phone)**: Enter your phone number.
- **Company (Contact Company)**: Enter your company name.
- **Vehicle Details (Vehicle Details)**: Enter the year, make, model, and color of your vehicle.
- **Year (Vehicle Year)**: Enter the year of your vehicle.
- **Make (Vehicle Make)**: Enter the make of your vehicle.
- **Model (Vehicle Model)**: Enter the model of your vehicle.
- **Color (Vehicle Color)**: Enter the color of your vehicle.
- **Appointment Details (Appointment Details)**: Enter any additional details about your appointment.
- **Date (Appointment Date)**: Select a date for your appointment.
- **Time (Appointment Time)**: Select a time for your appointment.
- **Time Zone (Appointment Time Zone)**: Select a time zone that aligns with your location.
- **Note (Appointment Note)**: Add any additional notes about your appointment.
- **Submit (Submit)**: Click to complete and submit your booking request.
