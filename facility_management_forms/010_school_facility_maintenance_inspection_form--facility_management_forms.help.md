# school-facility-maintenance-inspection-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for facility maintenance inspections in schools. It captures information about the inspection, including building address, contact details, facility type, condition, and issues identified. The form helps track the status of maintenance and follow-up activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the building address where the inspection was conducted.
2. Select the date of the inspection.
3. Enter the name and contact email of the person conducting the inspection.
4. Enter the phone number of the contact person (if applicable).
5. Choose the type of facility inspected (e.g., Building, Parking Lot, Playground, or Other).
6. Evaluate the condition of the facility and select all that apply (e.g., Good, Fair, Poor, Critical).
7. Describe any issues identified during the inspection.
8. Add any comments about the inspection.
9. Schedule a follow-up date (if necessary).
10. Mark the completed status of the inspection (True, False, or Pending).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Building Address** (required): Enter the complete address of the school facility, including street, building number, and postal code.
* **Inspection Date** (required): The date on which the inspection was conducted.
* **Contact Name** (required): The name of the person conducting the inspection.
* **Contact E-mail** (required): The email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (optional): The phone number of the contact person (if applicable).
* **Facility Type** (required): Select the type of facility inspected (e.g., Building, Parking Lot, Playground, Other).
* **Building Condition** (optional): Evaluate the condition of the facility, selecting all that apply (e.g., Good, Fair, Poor, Critical).
* **Issues Identified** (optional): Describe any issues or problems identified during the inspection.
* **Comments** (optional): Add any additional comments about the inspection.
* **Follow-up Date** (optional): Schedule a follow-up date if necessary.
* **Completed Status** (required): Mark the completed status of the inspection (True, False, or Pending).
