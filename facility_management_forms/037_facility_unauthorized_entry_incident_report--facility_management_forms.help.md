# Facility Unauthorized Entry Incident Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document incidents of unauthorized entry into facilities. This helps us keep track of and analyze these incidents, ensuring we can provide accurate and timely information to relevant stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all relevant fields, as they are indicated as required.
2. If the incident type is "Other", please provide a brief description in the "incident_type_other" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **incident_date** (Date): Enter the date of the incident in dd/mm/yy format.
- **incident_time** (Time): Enter the time of the incident in 24h:mn format (e.g., 13:00 for 1 PM).
- **incident_location** (Location): Describe the location where the incident happened.
- **incident_description** (Description): Briefly describe what happened during the incident.
- **incident_reporter** (Reporter): Enter your name and contact information, if available.
- **facility_name** (Facility Name): Enter the name of the facility.
- **facility_address** (Facility Address): Enter the address of the facility.
- **incident_type** (Type of Incident): Select the type of incident that occurred (Unauthorized Entry, Unauthorized Access, or Unauthorized Trespass). If "Other" is selected, please fill in the "incident_type_other" field.
- **incident_type_other** (Other): If "Other" is selected in "incident_type", describe the type of incident that occurred.
- **facility_unauthorized_entry_incident_report** (Facility Unauthorized Entry Incident Report): This field is automatically filled with a default value, you can leave it as is.

## Tips
- Ensure that you fill in all relevant fields for accurate reporting.
- If you are unsure about the type of incident, please select "Other" and provide a brief description in the "incident_type_other" field.
- Re-submission of the same incident report will be treated as a new submission.
- Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information for the incident report. Incomplete or inaccurate information may lead to difficulties in investigating or resolving the incident.
