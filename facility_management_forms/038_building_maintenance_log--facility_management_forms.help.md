# Building Maintenance Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track maintenance activities and repairs in a building. It captures details about the maintenance activity, including the date, type of maintenance, and specific details about the work performed. The form helps building management or maintenance personnel to keep a record of maintenance activities and to identify any safety concerns or issues that need to be addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the maintenance activity.
2. Select the type of maintenance activity (e.g., Preventive maintenance, Routine maintenance, Emergency repair, etc.).
3. Enter the building's information, including the building's name or identifier and address.
4. Specify the floor or room number where the maintenance activity was performed.
5. Enter the detailed description of the work performed.
6. Select whether the work was completed, and if not, the reason why.
7. Select whether a follow-up visit is needed.
8. Enter any safety concerns or issues that were identified.
9. Enter any additional issues or problems that were found.
10. Enter any recommendations for future maintenance.
11. Confirm that the work was tested and verified, and enter the results of any testing performed.
12. Enter the status of the building after the maintenance activity (e.g., fully operational, partially operational, etc.).
13. Confirm that photos or documentation were attached or taken.
14. Enter any additional maintenance notes or comments.

* **Maintenance description** (`maintenance_description`): Enter a detailed description of the work performed.
