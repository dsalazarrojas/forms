> # Fitness Center Health Compliance Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help fitness center health and safety inspectors conduct regular audits to ensure compliance with relevant laws, regulations, and standards. It is intended to be a comprehensive guide for identifying and reporting potential health and safety risks and providing recommendations for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the corresponding option for each section based on the actual conditions at the fitness center.
2. Fill in the necessary information for each field, including your name and position.
3. Ensure that you have completed all sections carefully and accurately.
4. Once completed, review your submission for any errors or missing information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gym Sanitation** (`gym_sanitation`, `text`, required): This section is used to report on the overall cleanliness and hygiene conditions of the fitness center. Please describe any issues or concerns related to sanitation, such as cleanliness of restrooms, showers, and locker rooms.
* **Equipment Checks** (`equipment_checks`, `text`, required): This section is used to report on the condition and maintenance of exercise equipment, such as treadmills, free weights, and exercise machines. Please describe any issues or concerns with equipment functionality, cleanliness, or maintenance.
* **Safety Protocols** (`safety_protocols`, `text`, required): This section is used to report on safety procedures and policies in place, such as emergency exits, fire safety, and emergency contact procedures. Please describe any issues or concerns with safety protocols, including procedures for reporting incidents or accidents.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required): This section is used to report on the layout and organization of the fitness center, including accessibility features and clear signage. Please describe any issues or concerns with navigation, accessibility, or clarity of signage.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): This section is used to record the date of the audit. Please enter the date of the audit in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): This section is used to record the time of the audit. Please enter the time of the audit in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30 for 2:30 PM).
* **Auditor Name** (`auditor_name`, `text`, required): This section is used to record your name and position. Please ensure that you have signed the audit form accurately and honestly.
* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, `select_one`, required): This section is used to report the overall compliance status. Please select one of the following options:
	+ Compliant
	+ Non-Compliant
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): This section is used for any additional comments or explanations for your findings.
* **Submission Date and Time** (`submission_date_and_time`, `date`, required): This section is used to record the date and time of submission of this form. Please ensure that you enter the correct date and time.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, `select_one`, required): This section is used to report the status of your submission. Please select one of the following options:
	+ Submitted
	+ Pending
	+ Rejected
* **Auditor's Signature** (`auditors_signature`, `text`, required): This section is used to authenticate your submission. Please sign your name as the auditor.
* **Manager's Signature** (`manager_signature`, `text`, required): This section is used to authenticate your submission. Please sign your name as the manager.
* **Supervisor's Signature** (`supervisors_signature`, `text`, required): This section is used to authenticate your submission. Please sign your name as the supervisor.
* **HR's Signature** (`hr_signature`, `text`, required): This section is used to authenticate your submission. Please sign your name as the HR representative.
* **Submission Date and Time 2** (`submission_date_and_time_2`, `date`, required): This section is used to record the date and time of submission of this form. Please ensure that you enter the correct date and time.
* **Submission Status 2** (`submission_status_2`, `select_one`, required): This section is used to report the status of your submission. Please select one of the following options:
	+ Submitted
	+ Pending
	+ Rejected
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, `note`, optional): This section is used for any additional comments or explanations for your findings.

Note: Please ensure that you have signed all required sections accurately and honestly.
