# Shipping Dock Operational Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the safety status of your shipping dock during a shift, ensuring a safe and secure environment for employees.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form in the order of the pages. Each page is a continuation of the previous one.
- Make sure to fill out every required field, and if a field is not required, fill it out if you think it is relevant to your inspection.
- Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the facility being inspected.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Shift Start Time** (`shift_start_time`, time, not required): Enter the start time of the shift.
* **Shift End Time** (`shift_end_time`, time, not required): Enter the end time of the shift.
* **Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment`, select_multiple, not required): Check if you used safety equipment during this shift. Options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Safety Equipment Options** (`safety_equipment_options`, text, not required): Provide any additional information about the safety equipment used.
* **Hazardous Hazards** (`hazardous_hazards`, select_multiple, not required): Check if you found any hazardous hazards during this inspection. Options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Hazardous Hazards Options** (`hazardous_hazards_options`, text, not required): Describe the hazardous hazards found.
* **Finding Follow-up Status** (`finding_followup_status`, select_one, not required): Select if this finding needs follow-up and what the status is. Options are:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ In Progress
	+ Completed
* **Finding Follow-up Status Options** (`finding_followup_status_options`, text, not required): Provide any additional information about the finding follow-up status.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required): Enter any additional notes about this inspection.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, not required): Enter the name of the person assigned to follow up on this finding.
