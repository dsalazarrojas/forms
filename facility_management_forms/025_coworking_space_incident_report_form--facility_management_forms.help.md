# Coworking Space Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents that occur within the coworking space, including accidents, injuries, or other events that may be of concern to facility management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a detailed description of the incident in the **Report Title** field.
2. If applicable, select the date of the incident in the **Report Date** field.
3. Enter the time of the incident in the **Report Time** field.
4. Provide the location where the incident occurred in the **Report Location** field.
5. Enter a detailed description of what happened in the **Report Details** field.
6. Specify the action taken to address the incident in the **Action Taken** field.
7. If applicable, select the person assigned to investigate or address the incident in the **Assigned To** field.
8. Enter your contact information in the **Contact Number**, **Contact Email**, and **Contact Phone** fields.
9. If applicable, select the person or group that was affected by the incident in the **Reportee Details** field.
10. Enter any relevant details about the person or group affected in the **Reportee Number** field.
11. Enter the Facility Manager's contact information in the **Facility Manager** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Title** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title summarizing the incident.
* **Report Date** (`report_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the incident, if known.
* **Report Time** (`report_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the incident, if known.
* **Report Location** (`report_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Report Details** (`report_details`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of what happened.
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, text, required): Specify the action taken to address the incident.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, optional): Select the person assigned to investigate or address the incident.
* **Reported By** (`reported_by`, text, optional): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email.
* **Facility Manager** (`facility_manager`, text, required): Enter the Facility Manager's contact information.
* **Facility Manager Email** (`facility_manager_email`, email, optional): Enter the Facility Manager's email address.
* **Facility Manager Phone** (`facility_manager_phone`, text, optional): Enter the Facility Manager's phone number.
* **Facility Manager Number** (`facility_manager_number`, text, required): Enter the Facility Manager's number.
* **Facility Manager Position** (`facility_manager_position`, text, optional): Enter the Facility Manager's position.
* **Facility Manager Extension** (`facility_manager_extension`, text, optional): Enter the Facility Manager's extension.
* **Facility Manager Alternate Phone** (`facility_manager_alternate_phone`, text, optional): Enter the Facility Manager's alternate phone number.
* **Facility Manager Alternate Number** (`facility_manager_alternate_number`, text, optional): Enter the Facility Manager's alternate number.
* **Witness** (`witness`, select_one, required): Select the option that best describes the witness.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click to submit the form.

**Tips**

* Please provide as much detail as possible when completing the form.
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date contact information.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor for guidance.
* This form is intended to be used for reporting incidents, not for reporting routine matters.
