# ประก Feedback On New Facility - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from users regarding a new facility. It is designed to gather information about the user's experience at the facility and provide valuable insights for the facility management team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the facility you are providing feedback on.
2. Select the type of feedback you want to provide (positive, neutral, or negative).
3. If you want to share your contact details, enter them in the "Contact Details" field.
4. If you want to share your email address, enter it in the "Email" field.
5. If you want to provide additional comments, type them in the "Comments" field.
6. Select the date of your visit to the facility.
7. If you have a facility number, enter it in the "Facility" field.
8. Choose your rating of the facility (very satisfied, neutral, or not satisfied).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Facility Name** (`new_facility_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the facility you are providing feedback on.
* **Feedback Type** (`feedback_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of feedback you want to provide (positive, neutral, or negative).
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): If you want to share your contact details with the facility management team.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you want to share your email address with the facility management team.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): If you want to provide additional comments about your experience at the facility.
* **Facility Visit Date** (`facility_visit_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your visit to the facility.
* **Facility** (`facility`, number, optional): If you have a facility number, enter it here.
* **Facility Rating** (`facility_rating`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your rating of the facility (very satisfied, neutral, or not satisfied).

## Tips
* Please be honest and specific when providing feedback.
* If you have any concerns or suggestions, please share them in the "Comments" field.
* If you want to receive a response from the facility management team, please provide your email address in the "Email" field.
