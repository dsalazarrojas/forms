# department_condition_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the condition of a department within an organization. It collects information about the department's location, condition, and maintenance status to help facility management teams make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the contact information of the person submitting the assessment.
2. Provide the department's location.
3. Describe the condition of the department.
4. Select the level of condition (High, Medium, or Low).
5. Provide a brief description of the condition.
6. Select the status of the maintenance (Completed, Not Completed, or Ongoing).
7. Enter the date the maintenance was completed.
8. Add any comments or notes about the department's condition.
9. Upload any relevant photos or attachments.
10. Add any additional comments or notes about the department's condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information**: Enter your name and contact information so that we can contact you for further questions or clarification.
* **Department Location**: Provide the physical address of the department, including the building number and room number if applicable.
* **Department Condition**: Describe the current state of the department, including any issues or concerns.
* **Condition Level**: Select the level of condition from High, Medium, or Low to indicate the department's overall state.
* **Condition Description**: Provide a brief explanation of the department's condition, including any relevant details.
* **Maintenance Status**: Select the status of the maintenance from Completed, Not Completed, or Ongoing to indicate the current status.
* **Completed Date**: Enter the date the maintenance was completed, if applicable.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the department's condition.
* **Photos**: Upload any photos that support your assessment of the department's condition.
* **Attachments**: Upload any relevant attachments that support your assessment, such as maintenance reports or schedules.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any further comments or notes about the department's condition.
