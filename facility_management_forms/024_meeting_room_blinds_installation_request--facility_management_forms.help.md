# Meeting Room Blinds Installation Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a request for installing blinds in a meeting room. Please fill out this form to provide necessary information about your meeting room's blinds installation request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information in the "Contact" section.
2. Select the meeting room that requires blind installation.
3. Enter the name of the contact person for the meeting room.
4. Enter the contact person's phone number.
5. Choose the type of blind to be installed (Roller, Vertical, or Horizontal).
6. Select the size of the blind (Small, Medium, or Large).
7. Choose the color of the blind (White, Black, or Brown).
8. Enter the installation date and start and end times.
9. Select the preferred day for the installation (Monday to Sunday).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Room Number** (`meeting_room_number`, text, optional): Enter the number of the meeting room where the blind installation is required.
* **Meeting Room** (`meeting_room`, text, optional): Select the meeting room where the blind installation is required.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person for the meeting room.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Blind Type** (`blind_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of blind to be installed. Options are: Roller, Vertical, and Horizontal.
* **Blind Size** (`blind_size`, select_one, optional): Choose the size of the blind to be installed. Options are: Small, Medium, and Large.
* **Blind Color** (`blind_color`, select_one, optional): Choose the color of the blind to be installed. Options are: White, Black, and Brown.
* **Installation Date** (`installation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the blind installation is required.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the blind installation.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the blind installation.
* **Preferred Installation Day** (`preferred_installation_day`, select_one, optional): Select the preferred day for the blind installation. Options are: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, and Sunday.
