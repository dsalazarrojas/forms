# IT Room Weekly Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IT Room Weekly Inspection Checklist is a form designed to facilitate the inspection of IT equipment and facilities on a weekly basis. It is crucial to maintain accuracy and efficiency in reporting issues that need attention.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete the form thoroughly, ensuring you answer all questions accurately and clearly.
2. Start by providing details about yourself, including your name, position, and date of inspection.
3. Next, check the condition of the equipment and report on any issues or concerns you may have.
4. Record the current temperature in the room and note any variations from the expected range.
5. Check for any security issues, such as unlocked doors or open windows, and note the dates and details of these issues.
6. Add any additional comments or observations you have during your inspection.
7. If you identify any security issues, note the dates of these issues in the security_issues_date field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspector Details** (`inspector_details`, Text, Required): This section is for you to input your name and position, as well as the date of the inspection. This information is crucial for tracking and auditing purposes.
* **Equipment Condition** (`equipment_condition`, Text, Optional): Describe the current condition of the equipment, including any issues or concerns you may have.
* **Cleanliness** (`cleanliness`, Text, Optional): Report on the general cleanliness of the area, including any areas of concern.
* **Temperature** (`temperature`, Number, Optional): Record the current temperature in the room and note any variations from the expected range.
* **Security Issues** (`security_issues`, Text, Optional): Identify any security issues you've encountered during your inspection, such as unlocked doors or open windows.
* **Dates** (`dates`, Date, Optional): Note the dates of any security issues you've identified.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, Note, Optional): Add any additional comments or observations you have during your inspection.
* **Security Notes** (`security_notes`, Text, Optional): If you have any security notes or concerns, report them here.
* **Security Issues Date** (`security_issues_date`, Date, Optional): Note the dates of any security issues, as previously mentioned.
* **Security Issue 2** (`security_issues_2`, Text, Optional): Report on any secondary security issues, if applicable.
* **Security Issue 3** (`security_issues_3`, Text, Optional): Report on any tertiary security issues, if applicable.

## Tips
Please remember to be thorough and accurate when completing this form, and don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or concerns.
