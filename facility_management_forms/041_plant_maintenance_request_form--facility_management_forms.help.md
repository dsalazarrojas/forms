# Plant Maintenance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Plant Maintenance Request Form is a tool for requesting maintenance or repair for facilities within a plant. It is used to gather information about the request, including the type of maintenance or repair needed, the asset involved, and the priority of the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Facility Management Form" section with any relevant information about the facility that needs maintenance or repair.
2. Enter the "facility_name" field with the name of the facility where the maintenance or repair is needed.
3. Enter the "asset_id" field with the ID of the asset that needs maintenance or repair.
4. Select the type of maintenance or repair needed from the "request_type" field.
5. Enter the date the request is submitted in the "request_date" field.
6. Enter a brief description of the maintenance or repair request in the "description" field.
7. Select the priority of the request from the "priority" field.
8. Enter the name of the user or personnel assigned to handle the request in the "assigned_to" field.
9. Select the date when the request is due from the "due_date" field.
10. Check the status of the request in the "status" field.
11. Enter any additional notes about the request in the "note" field.
12. Enter the email and phone number of the contact person for the facility in the "email", "phone" fields.
13. Enter the name, email, and phone number of the facility manager in the "facility_manager_name", "facility_manager_email", "facility_manager_phone" fields.
14. Enter the name of the asset involved in the "asset_name" field.
15. Enter the location of the asset in the "location" field.
16. Enter the name of the category and type of asset in the "facility", "asset_category", "asset_type" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Management Form** (text, required): This field is a brief description of the facility that needs maintenance or repair.
* **facility_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the facility where the maintenance or repair is needed.
* **asset_id** (text, required): Enter the ID of the asset that needs maintenance or repair.
* **request_type** (select one, required): Select the type of maintenance or repair needed from the options: Repair, Maintenance, Scheduling.
* **request_date** (date, required): Enter the date the request is submitted.
* **description** (text, required): A brief description of the maintenance or repair request.
* **priority** (select one, required): Select the priority of the request from the options: High, Medium, Low.
* **assigned_to** (text, required): Enter the name of the user or personnel assigned to handle the request.
* **due_date** (date, required): Select the date when the request is due.
* **status** (select multiple, required): Check the status of the request from the options: Completed, In Progress, Scheduled.
* **note** (note, required): Enter any additional notes about the request.
* **email** (email, required): Enter the email of the contact person for the facility.
* **phone** (text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person for the facility.
* **facility_manager_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the facility manager.
* **facility_manager_email** (text, required): Enter the email of the facility manager.
* **facility_manager_phone** (text, required): Enter the phone number of the facility manager.
* **asset_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the asset involved in the request.
* **location** (text, required): Enter the location of the asset.
* **facility** (text, required): Enter the name of the facility where the asset is located.
* **asset_category** (text, required): Enter the category of the asset.
* **asset_type** (text, required): Enter the type of the asset.

Note: The fields marked "required" are mandatory fields that must be filled out to submit the request.
