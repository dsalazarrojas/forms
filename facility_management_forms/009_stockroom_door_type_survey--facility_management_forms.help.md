# Stockroom Door Type Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the condition and accessibility of stockroom doors, as well as provide comments and recommendations for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the stockroom door type from the options provided.
2. Evaluate the stockroom performance by choosing Excellent, Fair, or Poor.
3. Report on the stockroom condition by entering the date.
4. Enter the time of access to the stockroom.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about the stockroom.
6. Rate the staff and manager's perception of the stockroom.
7. Leave comments for the staff and manager.
8. Choose the manager's rating scale (1-100).
9. Enter the stockroom door ID and name.
10. Enter the facility name, address, contact information, and website.
11. Report on the number and condition of stockroom doors.
12. Enter the access time for the stockroom doors.
13. Leave any additional comments or recommendations for the stockroom.
14. Enter the stockroom floor plan information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Stockroom Door Type**: Choose the correct type of door for the stockroom from the available options.
* **Stockroom Performance**: Evaluate the stockroom performance as Excellent, Fair, or Poor.
* **Stockroom Condition**: Report on the current condition of the stockroom on the date.
* **Stockroom Access**: Enter the time of access to the stockroom.
* **Stockroom Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the stockroom.
* **Staff Rating**: Rate the stockroom from the staff's perspective as Very Good, Good, Neutral, or Poor.
* **Manager Rating**: Rate the stockroom from the manager's perspective as Very Good, Good, Neutral, or Poor.
* **Manager Comments**: Leave comments for the manager about the stockroom.
* **Staff Comments**: Leave comments for the staff about the stockroom.
* **Manager Rating Scale**: Choose a rating from 1-100 for the stockroom.
* **Stockroom Door Type ID**: Enter the ID of the stockroom door.
* **Stockroom Door Name**: Enter the name of the stockroom door.
* **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the facility.
* **Facility Address**: Enter the address of the facility.
* **Facility Contact**: Enter the contact information for the facility.
* **Facility Email**: Enter the email of the facility.
* **Facility Website**: Enter the website of the facility.
* **Stockroom Doors Number**: Report on the number of stockroom doors.
* **Stockroom Doors Condition**: Report on the condition of the stockroom doors.
* **Stockroom Doors Access**: Enter the time of access to the stockroom doors.
* **Stockroom Doors Notes**: Leave any additional comments or recommendations for the stockroom doors.
* **Stockroom Floor Plan**: Enter the stockroom floor plan information.
* **Stockroom Comments**: Leave comments about the stockroom.
* **Stockroom Recommendations**: Leave comments or recommendations for the stockroom.
