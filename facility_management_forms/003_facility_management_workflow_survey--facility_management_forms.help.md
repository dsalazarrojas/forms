# Facility Management Workflow Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facility Management Workflow Survey is a form designed to gather information from facility management staff about their current workflow, challenges, and improvement ideas. This survey aims to improve the facility management process by gathering feedback and insights from staff members who are directly involved in the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review each section carefully and ensure you understand the questions being asked.
* Fill out the form completely, as indicated by the required and optional fields.
* Use the provided options for select_one and select_multiple fields.
* Provide a clear and detailed response to the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Location Name** (`location_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the location you are currently working with.
* **Workflow Type** (`workflow_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of workflow you are currently performing:
	+ General
	+ Administrative
	+ Maintenance
	+ Facilities
	+ Engineering
	+ Security
* **Staff Feedback on Satisfaction** (`staff_feedback_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate your level of satisfaction with the current workflow:
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
* **Challenges** (`challenges`, text, optional): Describe any challenges you are currently facing with the workflow.
* **Improvement Ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, text, optional): Share any ideas you have for improving the current workflow.
* **Location Address** (`location_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the location you are currently working with.
* **Location Contact** (`location_contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact information for the location you are currently working with.
* **Workflow Description** (`workflow_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the current workflow, including any relevant details or procedures.

## Tips
* Be honest and detailed in your responses, as this information will be used to improve the facility management process.
* If you are unsure about a particular field, contact your supervisor or a member of the facility management team for guidance.
* Please complete the form in a timely manner to ensure your feedback is received and reviewed promptly.
