# Facility Location Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The facility location evaluation form is used to collect and track information about a facility's location. This includes details about the facility, its address, contact information, and evaluation metrics. The form is a crucial tool for facilities staff and managers to assess the status and performance of facilities within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Location Details**: Enter the location name and description in the text field.
2. **Location Address**: Provide the facility's address in the text field.
3. **Location Contact**: Select the facilities manager(s) responsible for the facility from the options provided.
4. **Facilities Staff**: Select the facilities staff members assigned to the facility from the options provided.
5. **Facilities Equipment**: Enter the total number of equipment present in the facility.
6. **Facilities Condition**: Evaluate the condition of the facility by selecting one of the three options (Good, Fair, or Poor).
7. **Facilities Rating**: Enter a numerical rating for the facility's overall performance.
8. **Facility Location Date**: Enter the date of the last facility evaluation.
9. **Facility Location Time**: Enter the time of the last facility evaluation.
10. **Facility Comments**: Enter any additional comments or observations about the facility.
11. **Facility Email**: Enter the facility's email address.
12. **Facility Phone**: Enter the facility's phone number.
13. **Location Surveyed By**: Select the person(s) who conducted the facility survey from the options provided.
14. **Facility Surveyed Date**: Enter the date when the facility was surveyed.
15. **Facility Surveyed Time**: Enter the time when the facility was surveyed.
16. **Facility Location Rating**: Evaluate the facility's location rating by selecting one of the three options (High, Medium, or Low).
17. **Facility Location Rating Scale**: Enter a numerical score for the facility's location rating.
18. **Facility Location Comments**: Enter any additional comments or observations about the facility's location.
19. **Facility Location Rating Comments**: Enter any comments or observations about the facility's location rating.
20. **Facility Location Score**: Enter a numerical score for the facility's overall performance.
21. **Facility Location Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the facility's location.
22. **Facility Location Status**: Evaluate the facility's status by selecting one of the two options (Active or Inactive).
23. **Facility Location Rating Scale Notes**: Enter any additional comments or observations about the facility's location rating scale.
