# Equipment Sanitization Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request equipment sanitization for a specific piece of equipment. This is to be used by authorized personnel to submit requests for equipment to be sanitized.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your contact information in the Contact Information section.
2. Provide your contact phone number in the Contact Phone section.
3. Choose the sanitization date for the equipment in the Sanitization Date section (if necessary).
4. Briefly describe the equipment in the Equipment Description section.
5. Indicate the location of the equipment in the Equipment Location section.
6. Choose how frequently the equipment needs to be sanitized in the Sanitization Frequency section (e.g., Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
7. If necessary, prioritize the request in the Priority section (e.g., Critical, High, or Medium).
8. Enter any additional information about the request in the Additional Information section.
9. Select the department or team that should perform the sanitization in the Submitter section.
10. Enter your phone number in the Submitter Phone section.
11. Enter your email address in the Submitter Email section.
12. Enter your department or team in the Submitter Department section.
13. Enter your title or position in the Submitter Title section.
14. Enter any comments or notes about the request in the Submitter Comments section (if necessary).
