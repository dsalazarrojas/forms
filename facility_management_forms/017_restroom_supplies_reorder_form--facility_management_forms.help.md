# Restroom Supplies Reorder Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restroom Supplies Reorder Form is a tool used to request the reordering of restroom supplies for your facility. This form should only be completed by authorized personnel to ensure accurate and timely ordering.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the location of the facility for which you are requesting supplies.
2. Choose your delivery preference:
	* Same Day: Select this option for urgent requests.
	* 2-5 Business Days: Select this option for standard delivery times.
	* 1-2 weeks: Select this option for less urgent requests.
3. Select the items you wish to reorder from the list:
	* Paper Towels
	* Toilet Paper
	* Air Freshener
	* Soap
4. Enter the quantity of each item you wish to reorder.
5. Add any additional comments or instructions in the notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Location**: Enter the location of your facility. This helps ensure that supplies are delivered to the correct location.
* **Delivery Preference**: Choose how quickly you would like to receive your ordered supplies. This helps our team prioritize and schedule delivery accordingly.
* **Reorder Item**: Select all the items you need to reorder from the provided list. This helps us ensure that we have the correct quantities of each item.
* **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of each item you need to reorder. This helps us accurately process your order.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or instructions to help us process your order efficiently.
