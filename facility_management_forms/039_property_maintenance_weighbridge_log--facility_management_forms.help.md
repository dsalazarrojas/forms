# Property Maintenance Weighbridge Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track the weights of vehicles on a property during maintenance activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the `site_id` to identify the property where the weights were recorded.
2. Enter the `site_name` to identify the property name.
3. Enter the `vehicle_id` to identify the vehicle.
4. Enter the `vehicle_number` to identify the vehicle number.
5. Enter the weights of the vehicle at different points (`weight_1` to `weight_16`).
6. Add any additional notes about the weights or the maintenance activity in the `notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Site ID (`site_id`, number, required)
The `site_id` field is used to enter the identification number of the property where the weights were recorded.

### Site Name (`site_name`, text, optional)
The `site_name` field is used to enter the name of the property.

### Vehicle ID (`vehicle_id`, text, optional)
The `vehicle_id` field is used to enter the identification number of the vehicle.

### Vehicle Number (`vehicle_number`, text, optional)
The `vehicle_number` field is used to enter the vehicle number.

### Weight 1-16 (`weight_1` to `weight_16`, number, optional)
The `weight_1` to `weight_16` fields are used to enter the weights of the vehicle at different points. Each weight field represents a separate point in time or location where the weight was recorded.

### Notes (`notes`, text, optional)
The `notes` field is used to add any additional comments or notes about the weights or the maintenance activity.

**Tips**
- Make sure to accurately enter the correct site and vehicle information to ensure that the weights are properly attributed.
- Use the weights fields to record weights at specific points in time or location.
- Use the `notes` field to add any additional comments or explanations about the weights or the maintenance activity.
