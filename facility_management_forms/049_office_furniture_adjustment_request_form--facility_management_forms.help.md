# Office Furniture Adjustment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Office Furniture Adjustment Request Form is a tool used to report and request adjustments to office furniture. This form helps ensure that any issues with office furniture are addressed promptly, and the office remains a comfortable and productive space for all employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Be as specific as possible when describing the issue with the furniture.
3. Provide any relevant details or photos of the issue.
4. Submit the form for review and action.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Field 1 (Text)** (`form_field_1`, Text, Not Required): This field is for any additional comments or notes about your request. Please include as much detail as possible.
* **Form Field 2 (Number)** (`form_field_2`, Number, Not Required): If applicable, please specify the exact quantity of furniture items that are damaged or missing.
* **Form Field 3 (Date)** (`form_field_3`, Date, Not Required): The date you first noticed the issue or when it occurred.
* **Form Field 4 (Time)** (`form_field_4`, Time, Not Required): The time you first noticed the issue or when it occurred.
* **Form Field 5 (Text)** (`form_field_5`, Text, Not Required): This field can be used to provide further details or context about the issue, such as location or description.
