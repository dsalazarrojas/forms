# Facility Design Studio Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Facility Design Studio Contact Form is intended to gather information from users for the purpose of inquiry, contact, or feedback. This form is usually used to address various facility management needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Project Inquiry** 
    This field is for providing a brief description of your project or concern.
2.  **Facility Manager** 
    Select the appropriate facility manager from the available options: Manager 1, Manager 2, or Manager 3.
3.  **Design Studio** 
    Choose one or more design studios from the available options: Studio 1, Studio 2, or Studio 3.
4.  **Contact Method** 
    Select your preferred contact method from the available options: Phone or Email.
5.  **Email** 
    Please enter your email address for contact purposes.
6.  **Phone** 
    Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
7.  **Message** 
    Provide a detailed description of your project or concern.
8.  **Submission Details** 
    Enter any additional comments or details regarding your submission.

## Tips

*   Be clear and concise when filling out the form.
*   Make sure to select the correct facility manager and design studio for your needs.
*   If you have any additional questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
