# Shade Structure Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shade Structure Feedback Survey is used to collect feedback from users about their experiences with shades structures. The purpose of this form is to gather information on various aspects of the shade structure, such as its canopy, maintenance, and overall satisfaction. This feedback is crucial for improving the design and functionality of shade structures in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the option that best describes your experience with the shade structure from the "shade-structure-feedback-survey" drop-down menu.
2.  Provide a rating of the canopy quality on the "shade-canopy-rating" field (optional).
3.  Share your feedback on the covered area by typing in the "covered-area-feedback" field (optional).
4.  Rate your comfort level with the shade structure on the "comfort-rating" field (optional).
5.  Indicate the maintenance planning status for the shade structure by selecting all that apply from the "maintenance-planning" drop-down menu (optional).
6.  Choose your overall satisfaction with the shade structure by selecting one option from the "overall-satisfaction" drop-down menu (optional).
7.  Share any additional comments or feedback on the "comments" field (optional).
8.  Enter your email address on the "email" field (optional).
9.  Provide your phone number on the "phone" field (optional).
10. Upload a photo of the shade structure's canopy on the "shade-canopy-photo" field (optional).
11. Upload a photo of the covered area on the "covered-area-photo" field (optional).
12. Upload a photo of the shade structure itself on the "shade-structure-photo" field (optional).
13. Upload a photo of the shade structure's plan on the "shade-structure-plan-photo" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **shade-structure-feedback-survey** (`shade_structure_feedback_survey`, select_one, required): Select the option that best describes your experience with the shade structure.
*   **shade-canopy-rating** (`shade_canopy_rating`, number, optional): Rate the quality of the shade structure's canopy on a scale of 1-5.
*   **covered-area-feedback** (`covered_area_feedback`, text, optional): Provide feedback on the covered area of the shade structure.
*   **comfort-rating** (`comfort_rating`, number, optional): Rate your comfort level with the shade structure on a scale of 1-5.
*   **maintenance-planning** (`maintenance_planning`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the maintenance planning status for the shade structure by selecting all that apply.
*   **overall-satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Choose your overall satisfaction with the shade structure by selecting one option.
*   **comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback about the shade structure.
*   **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact.
*   **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for contact.
*   **shade-canopy-photo** (`shade_canopy_photo`, text, optional): Upload a photo of the shade structure's canopy.
*   **covered-area-photo** (`covered_area_photo`, text, optional): Upload a photo of the covered area.
*   **shade-structure-photo** (`shade_structure_photo`, text, optional): Upload a photo of the shade structure itself.
*   **shade-structure-plan-photo** (`shade_structure_plan_photo`, text, optional): Upload a photo of the shade structure's plan.
