# Energy Consumption Benchmark Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information for energy consumption benchmarking purposes. This form is intended to gather data about the energy consumption of a facility to establish a baseline for future comparisons. Please ensure you are filling out this form for the first time to avoid duplicating previous submissions.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Ensure you have all necessary information before starting the form.
*   Enter the facility name and the building name accurately.
*   Enter a valid email address for the contact person.
*   Select the correct energy type.
*   Enter the benchmark year for the energy consumption data.
*   Enter a value for the energy consumption rate.
*   Enter a value for the energy consumption intensity (if applicable).
*   Provide any additional comments or details about the energy consumption.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Facility Name** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to enter the name of the facility. Enter the name of the facility where the energy consumption data is to be collected.
*   **Building Name** (`2`, `text`, required: true): This field is used to enter the name of the building. Enter the name of the building where the energy consumption data is to be collected.
*   **Contact Person** (`3`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to enter the contact person's name. Enter the name and title of the person who can be contacted for further information.
*   **Contact Email** (`4`, `email`, required: false): This field is used to enter the contact person's email. Enter the contact email of the person who can be contacted for further information.
*   **Energy Type** (`5`, `text`, required: true): This field is used to select the type of energy being consumed. Enter the type of energy being consumed (e.g., electricity, gas, etc.).
*   **Energy Source** (`6`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to enter the energy source. Enter the source of the energy being consumed (e.g., solar, wind, etc.).
*   **Energy Usage** (`7`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to enter the energy usage details. Enter any additional information about the energy usage.
*   **Energy Consumption Benchmark** (`8`, `number`, required: false): This field is used to enter the energy consumption benchmark. Enter the energy consumption benchmark (e.g., kWh).
*   **Benchmark Year** (`9`, `number`, required: true): This field is used to enter the benchmark year. Enter the year for which the energy consumption data is being collected.
*   **Energy Consumption Rate** (`10`, `number`, required: false): This field is used to enter the energy consumption rate. Enter the energy consumption rate (e.g., energy per square foot).
*   **Energy Consumption Intensity** (`11`, `number`, required: false): This field is used to enter the energy consumption intensity. Enter the energy consumption intensity (e.g., energy per square foot).
*   **Comments** (`12`, `note`, required: false): This field is used to enter any additional comments or details about the energy consumption. Enter any additional information that may be helpful for the energy consumption assessment.

## Tips

*   Ensure all fields are accurate and complete.
*   Check the form before submitting for any errors.
*   Contact the contact person if you have any questions or need further information.
