# University Campus Facility Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help university staff and students report issues or concerns related to university campus facilities. Please use this form to provide a brief description of the facility's issue or concern.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please select the "Facility Type" that best describes the facility in question.
- Enter the name of the campus facility in the "campus_name" field.
- If you wish to report a concern, select the corresponding "facility_condition" and provide a brief description in the "Description" field.
- If you have any contact information, please enter your name, email, and phone number in the "Contact Name", "email", and "phone" fields, so the facilities management team can get back to you for further information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **campus_name** (`campus_name`): Enter the name of the campus facility that needs attention.
  * This field is used to help the facilities management team identify the location of the facility in question.

- **Facility Type** (`facility_type`): Select the type of facility that best describes the issue or concern.
  * This field helps the facilities management team understand the type of facility that needs attention.

- **facility_condition** (`facility_condition`): Select the condition that best describes the current state of the facility.
  * This field is used to help the facilities management team understand the severity of the issue or concern.

- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): If you have any contact information, please enter your name.
  * This field is used to enable the facilities management team to contact you for further information.

- **email** (`email`): Enter your email address so the facilities management team can get back to you.
  * This field is used to enable the facilities management team to contact you for further information.

- **phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number so the facilities management team can get back to you.
  * This field is used to enable the facilities management team to contact you for further information.

- **Description** (`description`): Briefly describe the issue or concern you are reporting.
  * This field is used to provide a clear description of the facility's condition.

- **Submit** (`submit`): Click this button to submit your report.
  * This field is used to send the report to the facilities management team for review and action.
