**Facility Virtual Inspection Consent Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain your consent for a remote facility inspection.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your contact details in the first field.
2.  Select your preferred time for the virtual inspection in the second field.
3.  Choose your consent option in the third field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information so that we can reach out to you for the inspection.
*   **Preferred Inspection Time** (`preferred_inspection_time`, `date`, required): Select a date and time for the virtual inspection that is most convenient for you.
*   **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether or not you consent to the virtual inspection. If you select "I consent to remote facility inspections", our team will proceed with the inspection as scheduled. If you select "I do not consent to remote facility inspections", we will not proceed with the inspection.

**Tips**

*   Make sure to enter accurate contact information so that we can reach out to you for any further questions or updates.
*   If you need to change your preferred inspection time, please let us know as soon as possible to avoid any conflicts.
*   If you have any concerns or questions about the virtual inspection, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
