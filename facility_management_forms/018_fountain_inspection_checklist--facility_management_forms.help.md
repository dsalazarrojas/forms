# Fountain Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help facilities management teams conduct regular inspections of fountains and record their findings. It ensures that the fountain is in good working order and identify potential maintenance needs or safety issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the "General Information" section with the fountain's ID number or a description to help track its location and history.
2. Select the "Water Quality" option that best describes the current water quality of the fountain.
3. Choose all the maintenance needs that apply to the fountain, such as a leaking pump or low water level.
4. Select "Safety Issue Present" if you've found any safety concerns during the inspection.
5. Add any additional comments or notes about the inspection in the "Notes" field.
6. Enter the date the inspection was completed.
7. Enter your name and title for reference.
8. Enter the email contact information for follow-up communication.
9. Enter the phone contact number for immediate communication.
10. Provide any additional comments or feedback about the fountain in the "Comments" field.
11. Finally, enter the date the form was submitted and your name for tracking purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (Page 1): Enter the fountain's ID number or a brief description to help track its location and history.
* **Water Quality** (Page 2): Select the option that best describes the current water quality of the fountain. Options are:
	+ Water Quality Good
	+ Water Quality Fair
	+ Water Quality Poor
* **Maintenance Needs** (Page 3): Choose all the maintenance needs that apply to the fountain, such as:
	+ Water Pump is Leaking
	+ Water Level is Low
	+ Filter Needs Replacement
* **Safety Issues** (Page 4): Select "Safety Issue Present" if you've found any safety concerns during the inspection.
* **Notes** (Page 5): Add any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Date Completed** (Page 6): Enter the date the inspection was completed.
* **Completed By** (Page 7): Enter your name and title for reference.
* **Email Contact** (Page 8): Enter the email contact information for follow-up communication.
* **Phone Contact** (Page 9): Enter the phone contact number for immediate communication.
* **Comments** (Page 10): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the fountain.
* **Date Submitted** (Page 11): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Submitted By** (Page 12): Enter your name for tracking purposes.
