# Warehouse Cooling Maintenance Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for requesting maintenance or repair of the warehouse cooling system. This form is used by authorized personnel to report issues or concerns with the cooling system and ensure that they are addressed in a timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Describe the problem with the cooling system in the "What is the problem with the cooling system?" field.
2. Enter the ID of the equipment affected in the "Cooling equipment id" field.
3. Select the priority level of the issue (Critical, Low, Medium, High) in the "Priority" field.
4. Provide a detailed description of the issue in the "Description of the issue" field.
5. Enter the location where the issue is occurring in the "Location" field.
6. (Optional) Enter the date the issue was discovered in the "Date" field.
7. (Optional) Enter the time the issue was discovered in the "Time" field.
8. Select the person or team responsible for addressing the issue in the "Assigned to" field.
9. Enter the contact email of the person submitting the form in the "Contact email" field.
10. Provide any additional comments in the "Comments" field.
11. Select the category of the person submitting the form (Admin, Maintenance, Staff, Other) in the "Submitted by" field.
12. Enter any comments from the submitter in the "Submitter comments" field.
13. Enter your name in the "Submitter name" field.
14. (Optional) Enter your phone number in the "Submitter phone" field.
15. (Optional) Enter your email in the "Submitter email" field.
16. (Optional) Enter your department in the "Submitter department" field.
