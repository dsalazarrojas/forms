# Facility Usage Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facility Usage Form is used to request access to a facility for official business or personal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the date of your planned access to the facility.
2.  Enter the time you will be accessing the facility.
3.  Enter the name of the facility you plan to visit.
4.  Enter the purpose of your visit to the facility.
5.  If you will be accessing the facility at specific times, enter the start and end times.
6.  Enter the name of the facility user (if required).
7.  Enter the name of the facility administrator or facility manager (if required).
8.  Select "Facility Admin" if you are visiting the facility as an administrator, or "Facility Manager" if you are visiting as a facility manager.
9.  If you are requesting facility admin approval, select the relevant options.
10.  Indicate whether facility access is requested or not.
11.  Add any additional comments you would like to provide about your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Facility Access** (`facility_usage_form_1`, text, required): This is the main purpose of the form. Describe your reason for accessing the facility.
* **Date of Access** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to visit the facility.
* **Time of Access** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time you plan to visit the facility.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the facility you plan to visit.
* **Purpose of Access** (`purpose`, text, required): Enter the reason for your visit to the facility.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of your visit, if applicable.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of your visit, if applicable.
* **Facility User** (`facility_user`, text, optional): Enter the name of the facility user, if required.
* **Facility Admin** (`facility_admin`, text, optional): Enter the name of the facility administrator, if required.
* **Facility Manager** (`facility_manager`, text, optional): Enter the name of the facility manager, if required.
* **Facility Admin Approval** (`facility_usage_form_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select the facility administrators who need to approve your access request.
* **Facility Access Requested** (`facility_usage_form_3`, select_one, optional): Check if you are requesting facility access.
* **Comments** (`facility_usage_form_4`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your visit.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the information you enter before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the relevant facility team or administrator.
* This form is designed to facilitate easy and accurate submission, but accuracy is key to ensuring your access request is processed smoothly.
