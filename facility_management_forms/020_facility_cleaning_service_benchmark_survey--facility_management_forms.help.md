# Facility Cleaning Service Benchmark Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information on the quality of the facility cleaning services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections carefully and answer each question according to your experience with the facility cleaning services.
2. For numerical questions, such as "cleaning services score", please enter a number between 1 and 10.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select the answer that best describes your experience.
4. If you have any suggestions or comments, please use the "Improvement requests" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you rate the overall cleaning quality (`facility_cleaning_quality`)**: This question is a multiple-choice question that asks you to rate the overall cleaning quality from Excellent to Poor.
* **Cleaning services score (`cleaning_services_score`)**: Please enter a number between 1 and 10 to score the cleaning services.
* **How frequently are cleaning services performed (`cleaning_frequency`)**: Select the frequency with which you think cleaning services are performed, from Daily to Quarterly.
* **How would you rate cleanliness standards (`cleanliness_standards`)**: This question is a multiple-choice question that asks you to rate the cleanliness standards from High to Low.
* **Satisfaction score (`satisfaction_score`)**: Please enter a number between 1 and 10 to score your satisfaction with the cleaning services.
* **Is there anything we can improve on? (`improvement_requests`)**: This question allows you to provide any suggestions or comments about the cleaning services.
* **Facility ID (`facility_id`)**: Please enter a unique facility ID if you have one.
* **Facility name (`facility_name`)**: If you have a facility name, please enter it.
