# Building Signage Change Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Building Signage Change Request Form is a standard document used to submit requests to update or modify building signage. This form helps to ensure that all necessary information is collected and presented to the relevant stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the request date in the "Request Date" field.
2.  Enter your name in the "Requestor Name" field.
3.  Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5.  Select your role from the options provided in the "Your Role" field.
6.  Enter the building address in the "Building Address" field.
7.  Enter the unit or space number in the "Unit or Space Number" field (if applicable).
8.  Describe the current signage in the "Description of Current Signage" field.
9.  Describe the proposed signage in the "Description of Proposed Signage" field.
10. Enter the exact text to be displayed on the sign in the "Text or Content of Signage" field.
11. (Optional) Enter the sign dimensions in the "Sign Dimensions" field.
12. (Optional) Select the sign material from the options provided in the "Sign Material" field.
13. (Optional) Check if you have a design or mockup of the sign in the "Design or Mockup Attached" field.
14. (Optional) Enter any business or tenant information in the "Business or Tenant Information" field.
15. (Optional) Select the purpose of the signage from the options provided in the "Purpose of Signage" field.
16. (Optional) Check if the signage adheres to building branding guidelines in the "Adheres to Building Branding Guidelines" field.
17. (Optional) Check if the signage complies with local codes and regulations in the "Sign Complies With Local Codes" field.
18. (Optional) Select any approvals already obtained in the "Approvals Already Obtained" field.
19. (Optional) Enter an estimated cost for the signage in the "Estimated Cost" field.
20. (Optional) Select a desired installation timeline from the options provided in the "Desired Installation Timeline" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Date**: Date when the request was submitted.
* **Requestor Name**: Name of the person submitting the request.
* **Email Address**: Contact email of the person submitting the request.
* **Phone Number**: Contact phone number of the person submitting the request.
* **Your Role**: The role of the person submitting the request.
* **Building Address**: Location of the building where the signage is located.
* **Unit or Space Number**: Specific location within the building if applicable.
* **Current Signage Description**: Description of the current signage.
* **Proposed Signage Description**: Description of the proposed signage.
* **Text or Content of Signage**: Exact text to be displayed on the sign.
* **Sign Dimensions**: Height and width of the sign in inches or cm (if applicable).
* **Sign Material**: Material of the sign (if applicable).
* **Design or Mockup Attached**: Check if a design or mockup of the sign is attached.
* **Business or Tenant Information**: Any relevant business or tenant information.
* **Purpose of Signage**: Purpose of the signage.
* **Adheres to Building Branding Guidelines**: Check if the signage adheres to building branding guidelines.
* **Sign Complies With Local Codes**: Check if the signage complies with local codes and regulations.
* **Approvals Already Obtained**: Select any approvals already obtained.
* **Estimated Cost**: Estimated cost of the signage.
* **Desired Installation Timeline**: Desired installation timeline for the signage.
