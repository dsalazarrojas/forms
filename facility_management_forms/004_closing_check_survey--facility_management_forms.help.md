# Closing Check Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to ensure that all necessary tasks have been completed before a facility is closed for the day.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the staff member's name who is closing the facility
2. Enter the closing date
3. Select the closing time
4. Select the department that was closed
5. Answer the questions on the cleaning and maintenance of the facility (e.g. were the floors cleaned and mopped, was trash and waste removed, etc.)
6. Answer the security questions (e.g. were all doors and windows locked, is the alarm system armed)
7. Answer the equipment and systems questions (e.g. were all lights turned off, is the HVAC set to the right temperature, etc.)
8. Enter any additional comments or notes as needed

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Staff Member Name** (`staff_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the staff member who is closing the facility.
- **Closing Date** (`closing_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date on which the facility is being closed.
- **Closing Time** (`closing_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time at which the facility is being closed.
- **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): Enter the department or area that was closed.
- **Floors Cleaned** (`floors_cleaned`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the floors were cleaned and mopped during the closing process.
- **Trash Removed** (`trash_removed`, `select_one`, required): Select whether all trash and waste were removed from the facility.
- **Surfaces Wiped** (`surfaces_wiped`, `select_one`, required): Select whether desks and surfaces were wiped down during the closing process.
- **Bathrooms Checked** (`bathrooms_checked`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the bathrooms were cleaned and restocked during the closing process.
- **Bathroom Supplies** (`bathrooms_supplies`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the restrooms were adequately stocked with necessary supplies.
- **Doors Locked** (`doors_locked`, `select_one`, required): Select whether all doors and windows were locked.
- **Windows Secured** (`windows_secured`, `select_one`, required): Select whether all windows were secured.
- **Alarm System Armed** (`alarm_system_armed`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the alarm system is armed.
- **Lights Off** (`lights_off`, `select_one`, required): Select whether all interior lights were turned off.
- **HVAC Set** (`hvac_set`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the HVAC system is set to the appropriate temperature.
- **Appliances Off** (`appliances_off`, `select_one`, required): Select whether all appliances and equipment were powered off.
- **Maintenance Issues** (`maintenance_issues`, `text`, optional): Enter any maintenance issues or concerns found during the closing process.
- **Follow-up Needed** (`follow_up_needed`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether follow-up action is required.
- **Follow-up Description** (`follow_up_description`, `text`, optional): Enter a description of the follow-up action needed.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all required fields before submitting the form.
- Double-check your answers to ensure that they are accurate.
- Enter any additional comments or notes as needed in the "Additional Notes" section.
