# Recycling Facility Audit Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from the facility management team to conduct an efficient and effective audit of the recycling facility.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully to ensure you provide accurate and complete information.
2. Fill out your facility's details, including its name, address, and contact information.
3. Indicate the preferred dates for the audit by selecting multiple dates from the provided options.
4. Determine whether you have authorized representatives available for the audit and select the correct option.
5. Confirm whether you give formal consent for the audit by selecting the correct option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
1. **Facility Details** (`facility_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your facility's name, address, and any other relevant details.
2. **Preferred Audit Dates** (`preferred_audit_dates`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose one or more dates from the provided options for when you would like the audit to take place.
	* Please select a date that works best for you.
3. **Authorized Representatives** (`authorized_representatives`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether you have authorized representatives available for the audit. Select 'Yes' if you have people who can be present during the audit, or 'No' otherwise.
4. **Formal Consent** (`formal_consent`, select_one, required/optional): Confirm your formal consent for the audit by selecting 'True' or 'False'.
	* Select 'True' to confirm your consent, or 'False' otherwise.
5. **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your facility's name for easy identification.
6. **Facility Address** (`facility_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your facility's address for easy location.
7. **Facility Contact** (`facility_contact`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact person's name and title for easy communication.
8. **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact person's phone number for quick communication.
9. **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the contact person's email address for easy correspondence.
10. **Preferred Audit Date** (`preferred_audit_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you prefer for the audit, if applicable.
11. **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the preferred start time for the audit, if applicable.
12. **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the preferred end time for the audit, if applicable.
