# Elevator Maintenance Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and record maintenance activities and inspection history of elevators. It helps ensure the equipment is in good working condition and meets safety standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go to the first page.
2. Answer the Equipment Details question: select 'Yes' if the elevator is available and functional, or 'No' if it's not.
3. Enter the Inspection Date of the most recent inspection.
4. Enter the Maintenance Date of the maintenance activity.
5. Provide any additional comments or notes about the maintenance activity in the Maintenance Notes field.
6. Schedule the Next Inspection Date if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment Details** (`Select One`, `Yes/No`, required: false):
	+ Select 'Yes' if the elevator is available and functional, or 'No' if it's not.
* **Inspection Date** (`Date`, required: true):
	+ Enter the date of the most recent inspection of the elevator.
* **Maintenance Date** (`Date`, required: true):
	+ Enter the date of the maintenance activity performed on the elevator.
* **Maintenance Notes** (`Note`, required: false):
	+ Provide any additional comments or notes about the maintenance activity.
* **Next Inspection Date** (`Date`, required: false):
	+ Schedule the date of the next inspection of the elevator.

## Tips

* Make sure to accurately record the inspection and maintenance dates and details to ensure accurate tracking of the elevator equipment's status.
* Schedule regular inspections to prevent accidents and ensure the equipment is in good working condition.
* If the equipment is not functional, do not schedule a maintenance activity until it's restored to service.
