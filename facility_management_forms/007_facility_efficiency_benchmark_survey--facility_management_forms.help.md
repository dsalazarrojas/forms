# Facility Efficiency Benchmark Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Facility Efficiency Benchmark Survey is a tool for organizations to assess and compare their facility performance, sustainability practices, and data collection methods with industry standards. This guide will walk you through each section of the form to help you fill it out accurately and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer each question briefly and concisely. If a field has additional questions or instructions, read them carefully before providing your response.
2.  Fill out all the required fields (indicated by a *). If you're unsure about a field, try to provide as much information as possible, but do not leave it blank if you can avoid it.
3.  Use the hints provided for each page if you're unsure about the question or need clarification.
4.  Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Building Performance (Page 1)
*   **Building Performance**: *This field is intended to gather information about the overall performance of your facility, such as its energy efficiency, water usage, and other key performance indicators.* 

### Efficiency Practices (Page 2)
*   **Efficiency Practices**: *This field is meant to collect information on the practices your facility employs to improve efficiency, such as implementing recycling programs, using renewable energy sources, and reducing waste.* 

### Sustainability Challenges (Page 3)
*   **Sustainability Challenges**: *This field asks about the obstacles you face in achieving sustainable practices within your facility, such as funding constraints, lack of resources, or regulatory issues.* 

### Performance Indicators (Page 4)
*   **Performance Indicators**: *This field requires you to provide key performance indicators that measure your facility's progress, such as energy consumption, water usage, and waste reduction.* 

### Benchmarking Data (Page 5)
*   **Benchmarking Data**: *This field is for you to provide data points for comparison with industry standards, such as energy usage per square foot or water usage per capita.* 

### Data Collection (Page 6)
*   **Data Collection**: *This field is for describing how you collect data, including methods, frequency, and tools used.* 

Note that the form has six pages, each with fields related to different aspects of facility efficiency. Please ensure you fill out all pages accurately and comprehensively for a thorough assessment.
