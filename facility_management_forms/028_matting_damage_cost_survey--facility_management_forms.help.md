# Matting Damage Cost Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report a potential matting damage issue. The goal of this form is to gather information about the issue and its impact on the facility.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information (Name, Email, Phone) in the "Contact Information" field.
2. Enter the date and time of the event in the "Event Date" and "Event Time" fields.
3. Enter the name of the supplier involved in the "Supplier Name" field.
4. Describe the damage in detail in the "Damage Description" field.
5. Provide an estimate of the damage cost in the "Damage Estimate" field.
6. Select the cost categories affected by the damage in the "Cost Categories" field (if applicable).
7. Enter the actual cost amount in the "Cost Amount" field.
8. Choose the unit of measurement for the cost in the "Cost Unit" field.
9. Enter your title and other contact information in the "Submitter" fields.
10. Click the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (user_contact, required): Enter your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Event Date** (event_date, required): Enter the date of the event that caused the damage.
* **Event Time** (event_time, required): Enter the time of the event that caused the damage.
* **Supplier Name** (supplier_name, required): Enter the name of the supplier involved in the damage.
* **Damage Description** (damage_description, required): Describe the damage in as much detail as possible.
* **Damage Estimate** (damage_estimate, required): Provide a rough estimate of the cost of the damage.
* **Cost Categories** (cost_categories, optional): Select any applicable cost categories affected by the damage.
* **Cost Amount** (cost_amount, required): Enter the actual cost amount of the damage.
* **Cost Unit** (cost_unit, required): Select the unit of measurement for the cost amount.
* **Submitter** (submitter, required): Enter your name as the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Title** (submitter_title, optional): Enter your title or position.
* **Submitter Email** (submitter_email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (submitter_phone, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Comment** (submitter_comment, optional): Add any additional comments about the submission.
* **Submitter Company** (submitter_company, optional): Enter your company name.
* **Submitter Address** (submitter_address, optional): Enter your work address.
* **Submitter Department** (submitter_department, optional): Enter your department name.
* **Submitter Job Title** (submitter_job_title, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Submitter Notes** (submitter_notes, optional): Add any additional notes about the submission.

## Tips

* Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* Make sure to submit the form only once for the same issue.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact your supervisor or facility manager for guidance.
