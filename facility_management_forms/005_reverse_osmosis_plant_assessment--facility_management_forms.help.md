# reverse_osmosis_plant_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for facility management to assess the state of plants and equipment. It collects information on performance, maintenance, and other operational issues, and assigns tasks to specific operators.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your operator name in the "Operator Name" field.
2. Select the date of the assessment from the "date_of_assessment" field.
3. If necessary, provide performance indicators for the plant in the "Plant Performance Indicators" field.
4. Indicate the current status of the plant in the "Osmosis Plant Status" field.
5. Document any maintenance findings in the "Maintenance Findings" field.
6. Add any comments or notes from the operator in the "Operator Comments" field.
7. Rate the plant's performance in the "Plant Rating" field.
8. Leave comments or feedback from the facility manager in the "Facility Manager Comments" field.
9. Select the operator who is assigned to perform the next task in the "Assigned To" field.
10. Select the operator who submitted the assessment in the "Submitted By" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Operator Name** (`operator_name`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the name of the operator completing the form.
* **date_of_assessment** (`date_of_assessment`, date, optional): This field is used to select the date of the assessment.
* **Plant Performance Indicators** (`plant_performance_indicators`, text, optional): This field is used to collect performance indicators for the plant.
* **Osmosis Plant Status** (`osmosis_plant_status`, text, optional): This field is used to indicate the current status of the plant.
* **Maintenance Findings** (`maintenance_findings`, note, optional): This field is used to document any maintenance findings or issues.
* **Operator Comments** (`operator_comments`, text, optional): This field is used to collect any comments or notes from the operator.
* **Plant Rating** (`plant_rating`, number, optional): This field is used to rate the plant's performance.
* **Facility Manager Comments** (`facility_manager_comments`, text, optional): This field is used to collect comments or feedback from the facility manager.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required): This field is used to select the operator who is assigned to perform the next task.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required): This field is used to select the operator who submitted the assessment.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Use the correct format for date fields.
* Select the correct operators for assignment and submission.
* Be concise and clear in your comments and notes.
* Ensure that the form is completed accurately and thoroughly to ensure reliable data collection.
