# Cabinet Inventory Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide provides a step-by-step explanation of the Cabinet Inventory Checklist form to help users complete it accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have the necessary information about the cabinet being inspected (location or asset tag).
2.  Enter the inspector's name.
3.  Record the date of the inventory check.
4.  Enter the total number of items counted inside the cabinet.
5.  Evaluate and select the condition of the items inside (Good, Fair, or Poor).
6.  If items are missing, describe them in the "Missing items details" field.
7.  If any actions are required (e.g. repair, re-order, disposal), describe them in the "Action required" field.
8.  If applicable, schedule the next inventory date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Inspector name (inspector_name)**: The person performing the inventory check.
*   **Inventory date (inventory_date)**: The date of the inventory check.
*   **Cabinet identifier (cabinet_id)**: The unique identifier for the cabinet being checked.
*   **Number of items counted (item_count)**: The total number of items inside the cabinet.
*   **Condition summary (condition_summary)**: A brief evaluation of the items inside the cabinet (Good, Fair, or Poor).
*   **Missing items details (missing_items)**: A list of any items found missing from the cabinet.
*   **Action required (action_required)**: A description of any actions needed for the cabinet (e.g. repair, re-order, disposal).
*   **Next inventory date (next_inventory_date)**: The scheduled date for the next inventory check (if applicable).
