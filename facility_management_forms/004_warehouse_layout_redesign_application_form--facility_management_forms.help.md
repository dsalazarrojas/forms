# Warehouse Layout Redesign Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your warehouse's current state and goals, allowing you to propose improvements and redesign the layout to optimize efficiency and productivity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide a brief description of the warehouse's current state.
2. List any layout issues you've noticed in the warehouse.
3. Define the goals and objectives for the warehouse.
4. Suggest improvements and ideas for the layout.
5. Upload any relevant files or images related to your proposals.
6. Choose a layout design approach for your proposal.
7. Add a label and color to your layout design.
8. Choose the size of your layout design.
9. Select the position of your layout design (e.g., left, right, top, bottom).
10. Choose where to position your layout design within the warehouse (e.g., center, corner).
11. Select whether or not to snap elements to the grid.
12. Define the goals and objectives for the facility.
13. List any areas for improvement in the facility.
14. Add any notes or comments about your proposal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Warehouse Details**: Describe the current state of the warehouse, including its size, layout, and any notable features.
* **Layout Issues**: List any issues you've noticed with the current layout, such as bottlenecks or inefficiencies.
* **Goals and Objectives**: Define the goals and objectives for the warehouse, such as increased efficiency or improved product flow.
* **Improvements Suggestions**: Suggest improvements and ideas for the layout, such as reconfiguring shelves or adding new equipment.
* **Upload 1/2/3**: Upload relevant files or images to support your proposals.
* **Layout Design** (*Yes/No*): Choose whether or not to use a layout design approach for your proposal.
* **Label** (*Text*): Add a label to your layout design, such as "Warehouse Aisle".
* **Color** (*Text*): Choose a color for your layout design.
* **Size** (*Text*): Select the size of your layout design.
* **Position** (*Yes/No*): Choose whether or not to position your layout design at a specific location within the warehouse.
* **Position** (*Text*): Select the position of your layout design within the warehouse (e.g., center, corner).
* **Snap** (*Yes/No*): Choose whether or not to snap elements to the grid in your layout design.
* **Facility Goals**: Define the goals and objectives for the facility, such as increased efficiency or improved product flow.
* **Facility Objectives**: List any specific objectives for the facility, such as reducing waste or improving productivity.
* **Facility Improvement Areas**: Suggest areas for improvement in the facility, such as adding new equipment or improving lighting.
* **Notes**: Add any notes or comments about your proposal.
