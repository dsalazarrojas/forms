# Irrigation System Sanitation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the sanitation status of an irrigation systems site inspection, operation, and cleanliness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start on the first page, "Site Inspection," where you will record your observations about the site.
2.  If applicable, move on to the "System Operation" page, where you will select the current operational status of the irrigation system. Choose one of the options provided.
3.  Next, move to "System Cleanliness," which is a text field where you can describe the overall cleanliness of the irrigation system.
4.  Finally, on the "Inspection Notes" page, provide any additional comments or notes about your inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site Inspection (site_inspection)**: This field is a text area where you can record any observations about the site, such as its condition, layout, or any notable features.
* **System Operation (system_operation)**: This field is a dropdown menu where you can select the current operational status of the irrigation system. Please choose from the provided options: Water Source, Pump, Meter, Valve, or Filter.
* **System Cleanliness (system_cleanliness)**: This field is a text area where you can describe the overall cleanliness of the irrigation system, including any areas that are dirty or require maintenance.
* **Inspection Notes (inspection_notes)**: This field is a text area where you can add any additional comments or notes about your inspection, including any recommendations or action items for maintenance or improvement.
