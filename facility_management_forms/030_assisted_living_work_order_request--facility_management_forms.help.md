# Assisted Living Work Order Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Assisted Living Work Order Request form is used to submit a work request for assisted living facilities. It captures essential information about the person making the request, the facility or building, and the nature of the issue or maintenance required.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your full name and contact information as the person making the request.
2.  Select your role in the facility (resident, family member, staff member, or administrator).
3.  Enter your contact phone number.
4.  Enter the date of the issue.
5.  Enter the name of the facility or building affected.
6.  If applicable, enter the resident's name and room or unit number.
7.  Select the type of maintenance required.
8.  Provide a detailed description of the problem.
9.  Indicate the priority level (routine, non-urgent, urgent, or emergency).
10.  Determine if the issue poses a safety concern.
11.  Decide if the resident's safety is affected.
12.  If available, enter a temporary solution description.
13.  If applicable, estimate the cost of the repair.
14.  Specify the preferred work dates.
15.  If required, enter special access details or materials needed.
16.  If applicable, select a preferred vendor or contractor.
17.  If applicable, specify the resident's availability.
18.  Enter any pet or special considerations.
19.  Check if the item is under warranty or if the warranty status is unknown.
20.  If applicable, describe the previous service history.
21.  Decide if photos of the issue have been attached.
22.  Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Requestor Name** (1): Enter your full name as the person making the request.
*   **Requestor Role** (2): Select your role in the facility (resident, family member, staff member, or administrator).
*   **Requestor Phone** (3): Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Request Date** (4): Enter the date of the issue.
*   **Facility or Building Name** (5): Enter the name of the facility or building affected.
*   **Resident Name** (6): If applicable, enter the resident's name.
*   **Room or Unit Number** (7): If applicable, enter the room or unit number.
*   **Type of Work Request** (8): Select the type of maintenance required.
*   **Description of Issue** (9): Provide a detailed description of the problem.
*   **Priority Level** (10): Indicate the priority level (routine, non-urgent, urgent, or emergency).
*   **Is This a Safety Concern?** (11): Determine if the issue poses a safety concern.
*   **Is Resident Safety Affected?** (12): Decide if the resident's safety is affected.
*   **Is Temporary Solution Available?** (13): If applicable, enter a temporary solution description.
*   **Estimated Cost** (15): If applicable, estimate the cost of the repair.
*   **Preferred Work Dates** (16): Specify the preferred work dates.
*   **Special Access Requirements** (17): If required, enter special access details or materials needed.
*   **Materials or Parts Needed** (18): If applicable, list the materials or parts needed.
*   **Preferred Vendor or Contractor** (19): If applicable, select a preferred vendor or contractor.
*   **Resident Availability** (20): If applicable, specify the resident's availability.
*   **Pets or Special Considerations** (21): Enter any pet or special considerations.
*   **Warranty Status** (22): Check if the item is under warranty or if the warranty status is unknown.
*   **Previous Service History** (23): If applicable, describe the previous service history.
*   **Photos of Issue Attached** (24): Decide if photos of the issue have been attached.
*   **Form Submission Date** (25): The date the form is submitted.

**Tips**

*   Be as detailed as possible when describing the issue to ensure the maintenance team can address it effectively.
*   If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to contact the facility's maintenance team for guidance.
*   Attach photos or documentation if necessary to support your request.
*   Ensure the form is submitted correctly to prevent duplication and ensure timely attention to your maintenance request.
