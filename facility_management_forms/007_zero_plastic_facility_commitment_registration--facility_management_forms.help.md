# Zero Plastic Facility Commitment Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help facilities commit to reducing plastic usage and register their zero-plastic initiatives. It is a tool for facilities to declare their commitment to sustainability and reduce plastic waste.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*), and provide as much detail as possible.
2. Review each field for accuracy before submitting the form.
3. Make sure to review the form in its entirety to ensure all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Details** (Facility details*, required): Please enter your facility's name and other relevant information.
* **Zero Plastic Initiatives** (zero_plastic_initiatives*, required): Describe the initiatives your facility is taking or plans to take to reduce plastic usage.
* **Authorized Commitments** (authorized_commitments*, required): List the individuals or departments that have authorized this commitment.
* **Sustainability Programs** (sustainability_programs*, required): Mention any existing sustainability programs or plans in place within your facility.
* **Authorized By** (authorized_by*, required): Enter the name of the person who authorized this commitment.
* **Commitment Date** (commitment_date*, required): Enter the date of your commitment to reduce plastic usage.
* **Commitment Duration** (commitment_duration*, optional): If applicable, provide the duration of your commitment (e.g., short-term, long-term, ongoing).
* **Commitment Notes** (commitment_notes*, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your commitment.
