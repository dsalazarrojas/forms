# Vehicle Database Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Database Form is used to create and manage vehicle database entries. It provides a structured format for users to input and store vehicle information, making it easier to track and maintain accurate records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Vehicle Make, Year Made, and Vehicle Model in their respective fields.
2. Select the correct Year Made from the dropdown list.
3. Enter the Vehicle License, Vehicle Tag Number, and Vehicle Description in their respective fields.
4. Choose the Vehicle Color, Vehicle Type, Vehicle Category, and Vehicle Location from their respective dropdown lists.
5. Select multiple Vehicle Status options to accurately represent the vehicle's condition.
6. Enter the Vehicle Owner's contact information, including email and phone number.
7. Optionally, add Vehicle Images and Notes for further documentation.
8. Review all fields carefully and ensure accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Make**: Enter the make of the vehicle, e.g., Toyota, Honda, or Ford.
* **Year Made**: Select the year the vehicle was made from the dropdown list.
* **Vehicle Model**: Enter the model of the vehicle, e.g., Corolla or Camry.
* **Vehicle Year**: Enter the model year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle License**: Enter the license plate number of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Tag Number**: Enter the vehicle's tag number (if applicable).
* **Vehicle Description**: Briefly describe the vehicle's features and condition.
* **Vehicle Color**: Select the vehicle's color from the dropdown list.
* **Vehicle Type**: Select the type of vehicle, e.g., sedan, truck, or SUV.
* **Vehicle Category**: Select the category of vehicle, e.g., passenger, commercial, or off-road.
* **Vehicle Location**: Select the location where the vehicle is currently parked or stored.
* **Vehicle Status**: Select multiple status options that accurately represent the vehicle's condition.
* **Vehicle Owner**: Enter the owner's name and contact information.
* **Vehicle Image**: Add an optional image of the vehicle for further documentation.
* **Vehicle Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Notes 2-7**: Additional fields for further notes or comments about the vehicle.
