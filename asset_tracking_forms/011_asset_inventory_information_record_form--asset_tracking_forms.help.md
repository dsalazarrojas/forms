# Asset Inventory Information Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Asset Inventory Information Record Form is a comprehensive tool used to collect and record detailed information about assets, including their condition, location, and maintenance history. The form is designed to aid in accurate asset tracking, ensuring that assets are properly accounted for and their value is accurately assessed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the organization's name as it is officially recognized.
2. Provide the date of the inventory count.
3. Select the category of the asset from the dropdown list.
4. Enter the unique tracking identifier for the asset.
5. Provide a brief description of the asset.
6. Enter the brand or manufacturer's name.
7. Enter the model or version number.
8. Enter the serial number (if applicable).
9. Enter the asset's color.
10. Enter the date the asset was purchased.
11. Enter the original purchase price.
12. If applicable, enter the name of the supplier.
13. If applicable, enter the warranty expiration date.
14. Enter the current location of the asset.
15. Identify the person assigned to the asset (employee or department).
16. Evaluate the asset's current condition.
17. Indicate if the asset has had regular maintenance.
18. Select the current maintenance status of the asset.
19. If applicable, enter the date of the last maintenance.
20. Select the current status of the asset.
21. Enter the estimated remaining useful life of the asset (if applicable).
22. If applicable, enter the current book value of the asset.
23. Add any additional notes about the asset.
24. Enter the name of the person conducting the inventory.
25. Enter the date the inventory was verified.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your organization as it is officially recognized.
* **Inventory Date** (`inventory_date`, date, required): Provide the date of the inventory count.
* **Asset Category** (`asset_category`, select_one, required): Select the category of the asset from the dropdown list.
* **Asset ID Code** (`asset_id_code`, text, required): Enter the unique tracking identifier for the asset.
* **Asset Name** (`asset_name`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the asset.
* **Asset Description** (`asset_description`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the asset.
* **Manufacturer** (`manufacturer`, text, optional): Enter the brand or manufacturer's name.
* **Model Number** (`model_number`, text, optional): Enter the model or version number of the asset.
* **Serial Number** (`serial_number`, text, optional): Enter the unique serial number of the asset.
* **Color** (`color`, text, optional): Enter the color of the asset.
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, date, required): Enter the date the asset was purchased.
* **Purchase Price** (`purchase_price`, number, required): Enter the original cost of the asset.
* **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the supplier.
* **Warranty Expiration Date** (`warranty_expiration`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the warranty expiration date.
* **Current Location** (`asset_location`, text, required): Enter the current location of the asset.
* **Assigned To (Employee/Department)** (`assigned_to`, text, required): Identify the person or department responsible for the asset.
* **Current Condition** (`asset_condition`, select_one, required): Evaluate the current condition of the asset.
* **Maintenance Status** (`maintenance_status`, select_one, required): Indicate if the asset has had regular maintenance.
* **Last Maintenance Date** (`last_maintenance_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the date of the last maintenance.
* **Asset Status** (`asset_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the asset.
* **Estimated Remaining Useful Life (years)** (`estimated_remaining_life`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the estimated remaining useful life of the asset.
* **Current Book Value** (`book_value`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the current book value of the asset.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any other relevant information about the asset.
* **Inventory Checked By** (`inventory_checked_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the person conducting the inventory.
* **Verification Date** (`verification_date`, date, required): Enter the date the inventory was verified.

## Tips

* Be thorough when filling out this form to ensure accurate asset tracking and inventory management.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and consistency.
* Ensure that the information you provide is up-to-date and relevant.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid any errors or discrepancies.
