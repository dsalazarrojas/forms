# Records Inventory Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Records Inventory Form is a simple tool used to collect information about records inventory, including contact details and inventory comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Records Inventory Form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide a title for the records inventory form.
2.  Enter the contact details of the person or team responsible for the inventory.
3.  Select the supervisor in charge of the inventory.
4.  Select the coordinator in charge of the inventory.
5.  Enter the contact person's name and their email address.
6.  Enter the contact phone number.
7.  Enter any relevant comments about the inventory.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Records Inventory Form (1)**:
    *   A text field for entering a title for the records inventory form. 
* **Contact Details (2)**:
    *   A text field for entering contact details, e.g., the person or team responsible for the inventory.
* **Supervisor (3)**:
    *   A select-one dropdown menu where you can choose the supervisor in charge of the inventory among the options: supervisor1, supervisor2, and supervisor3.
* **Coordinator (4)**:
    *   A select-one dropdown menu where you can choose the coordinator in charge of the inventory among the options: coordinator1, coordinator2, and coordinator3.
* **Contact Person (5)**:
    *   A text field for entering the contact person's name.
* **Contact Email (6)**:
    *   An email field for entering the contact person's email address.
* **Contact Phone (7)**:
    *   A text field for entering the contact person's phone number.
* **Inventory Comments (8)**:
    *   A text field for entering any relevant comments about the inventory.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
*   Use the select-one dropdown menus to select the correct supervisor and coordinator.
*   Enter the contact person's name, email address, and phone number accurately.
*   Use the inventory comments field to provide any additional information about the inventory.
