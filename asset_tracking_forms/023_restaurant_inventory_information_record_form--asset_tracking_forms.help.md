# Restaurant Inventory Information Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Restaurant Inventory Information Record Form is designed to track and record inventory levels, stock checks, and other relevant details within a restaurant setting. This form is used to collect information about the restaurant's inventory, perform stock checks, and document any issues or concerns.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the general information about the restaurant.
2. Enter the date of the stock take (inventory take).
3. Record the current stock levels.
4. Enter the date and time of the stock check.
5. Enter any additional notes about the stock check.
6. Optionally, sync the form submissions with other popular apps.
7. Enter the restaurant's phone number and email for contact purposes.
8. Click the "Submit" button to finalize and save the data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: Enter the general information about the restaurant.
* **Inventory Takes**: Enter the date of the stock take (inventory take).
* **Stock Levels**: Enter the current stock levels.
* **Stock Check Date**: Enter the date of the stock check.
* **Stock Check Time**: Enter the time of the stock check.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the stock check.
* **Sync Submissions**: Sync the form submissions with other popular apps.
* **Restaurant Phone**: Enter the restaurant's phone number.
* **Restaurant Email**: Enter the restaurant's email address.
* **Submit**: Click this button to finalize and save the data.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information about the restaurant's inventory levels and stock checks.
* Use the "Notes" field to capture any specific issues or concerns that need to be addressed.
* Ensure that the "Sync Submissions" feature is enabled to integrate with other restaurant management systems.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
