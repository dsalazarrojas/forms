# Office Supplies Inventory Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Office Supplies Inventory Form is a tool for updating and managing the quantity and status of office supplies within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form with the required information**. The form is divided into several fields that require different types of information. Please make sure to fill out each field accurately and completely.
2. **Check your answers**. Double-check your entries to ensure that they are correct and up-to-date.
3. **Submit the form**. Once you have completed the form, click the submit button to save your changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Item Number** (`item_number`, `number`, optional): This field is used to identify the specific office supply item being updated. If you know the item number, please enter it here. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Item Name** (`item_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the office supply item being updated. This field helps identify the specific item in the inventory.
* **Category** (`category`, `text`, optional): Enter the category of the office supply item being updated (e.g. paper products, writing supplies, etc.). This helps with searching and filtering the inventory.
* **Number of Items on Hand** (`number_of_items_on_hand`, `number`, optional): Enter the current quantity of the office supply item on hand. If you're unsure, you can estimate or leave this field blank.
* **Quantity to Be Ordered** (`quantity_to_be_ordered`, `number`, optional): Enter the quantity of the office supply item that needs to be ordered. If you're unsure, you can estimate or leave this field blank.
* **Unit Price** (`unit_price`, `number`, optional): Enter the unit price of the office supply item. If you're unsure, you can estimate or leave this field blank.
* **Checked By** (`checked_by`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person who last checked the inventory. This helps track who last updated the inventory.
* **Approver** (`approver`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person who approved the inventory update. This helps track who approved the changes.
