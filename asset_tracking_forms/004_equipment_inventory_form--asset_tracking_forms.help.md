# Equipment Inventory Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equipment Inventory Form is used to collect and store information about the equipment in use within the organization. This includes details such as the equipment type, description, manufacturer, and other relevant details. The form helps to maintain a comprehensive record of the equipment, making it easier to track and manage it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the correct "Equipment Type" from the options provided.
2. Enter a brief description of the equipment in the "Description" field.
3. If the equipment has a manufacturer, enter the name of the manufacturer in the "Manufacturer" field.
4. If the equipment has a model, enter the model number in the "Model" field.
5. Check the "Year of Manufacture" field to indicate if the equipment was manufactured within the past year.
6. Enter the serial number of the equipment in the "Serial Number" field.
7. Enter the purchase price of the equipment in the "Purchase Price" field.
8. Provide the current location of the equipment in the "Current Location" field.
9. If applicable, enter the date of purchase in the "Purchase Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment Type** (Select One): Indicates if the equipment is new or used. This field helps to classify the equipment for tracking and management purposes.
* **Description**: A brief description of the equipment, including its purpose and any notable features.
* **Manufacturer**: The company that manufactured the equipment.
* **Model**: The model number of the equipment.
* **Year of Manufacture** (Select One): Indicates if the equipment was manufactured within the past year.
* **Serial Number**: A unique identifier assigned to the equipment by the manufacturer.
* **Purchase Price** (Number): The price paid for the equipment upon purchase.
* **Current Location**: The current whereabouts of the equipment.
* **Purchase Date** (Date): The date the equipment was purchased.
