# Equipment Inventory Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about your assets to maintain a comprehensive inventory and track the status of your equipment.

## How To Complete This Form

- Fill out this form for every asset in your possession.
- Complete all the required fields marked with `*`. Optional fields can be filled out voluntarily.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: General Information
* **Asset Name (<name>, <type>, required: true)**: Enter the name of the asset, for example, "Desk Chair" or "Computer Monitor".
* **Asset Type (<type>, required: true)**: Select from the provided options: Asset, Equipment, Vehicle, Tool, or Other.

### Page 2: Additional Details
* **Asset Tag (<name>, <type>, required: false)**: Enter the asset tag if available (not required but can be helpful for identification purposes).
* **Serial Number (<name>, <type>, required: true)**: Enter the serial number of the asset (e.g., for laptops or other serial-numbered assets).
* **Location (<name>, <type>, required: true)**: Indicate where the asset can be found. Be as specific as possible.

### Page 3: Purchase Information
* **Purchase Date (<name>, <type>, required: true)**: Enter the date the asset was purchased (this helps track purchase history).
* **Purchase Cost (<name>, <type>, required: true)**: Fill out the cost incurred during the purchase of the asset.
* **Last Inspection Date (<name>, <type>, required: false)**: Record the date of the last inspection or maintenance performed on the asset.
* **Next Inspection Date (<name>, <type>, required: true)**: Schedule the next inspection or maintenance date for the asset.

### Page 4: Ownership and Maintenance
* **Asset Owner (<name>, <type>, required: true)**: Identify the owner of the asset for accountability.
* **Notes (<name>, <type>, required: false)**: Add any additional comments or notes relevant to the asset's status.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled out to ensure accurate and comprehensive asset records.
- Use the provided options for Asset Type to ensure correct classification of your assets.
- The serial number and purchase date are crucial for tracking and identifying assets.
