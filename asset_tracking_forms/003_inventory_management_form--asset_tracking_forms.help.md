# Inventory Management Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inventory Management Form is a tool for keeping track of items in your inventory. It's designed to help you collect and record information about the items in your stock, including their quantities, prices, and purchase/sale dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the general information about the item at the top of the page.
2. Choose whether the item is included in the list or not.
3. Enter a brief description of the item.
4. Enter the quantity of the item in your inventory.
5. Enter the price of the item.
6. Choose the date when you received the item.
7. Choose the date when you sold the item (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional): This field is for entering any general information about the item. You can enter any relevant details that will help you identify the item.
* **Item List** (`item_list`, select multiple, optional): This field is for selecting whether the item is included in the list or not. Choose 'Yes' if the item is part of your inventory, 'No' otherwise.
* **Item Name** (`item_name`, text, optional): This field is for entering the name of the item. Enter a brief name that will help you identify the item quickly.
* **Item Description** (`item_description`, text, optional): This field is for entering a brief description of the item. Enter any details that will help you understand what the item is.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): This field is for entering the quantity of the item in your inventory. Enter a whole number.
* **Item Price** (`item_price`, number, optional): This field is for entering the price of the item. Enter the price you paid for or expect to sell the item.
* **Received Date** (`date_received`, date, optional): This field is for choosing the date when you received the item.
* **Sold Date** (`date_sold`, date, optional): This field is for choosing the date when you sold the item (if applicable). If the item is still in your inventory, leave this field blank.
