# Art Inventory Information Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Inventory Information Record Form is used to collect and record information about art pieces in your collection. This form helps to ensure accurate and up-to-date information about each artwork, making it easier to manage and maintain your inventory.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about the art piece before starting the form.
2. Fill in the required fields (marked as required) with accurate and relevant information.
3. For fields marked as optional, use this information if available, but don't feel pressured to fill in if you don't have it.
4. Be specific and detailed when filling in fields like 'condition_status' and 'notes', as they help to provide a comprehensive understanding of the artwork.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Record Date**: This is the date when the artwork's information was recorded in the form. Enter the current date in the format DD/MM/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2023).
* **Artwork ID**: A unique identification number for the artwork, often assigned by the museum or collection.
* **Artwork Title**: A brief title or description of the artwork.
* **Artist Name**: The full name of the artist who created the work.
* **Artist Birth Year**: If available, the year the artist was born (optional).
* **Artist Nationality**: The country of origin of the artist (optional).
* **Creation Date**: The date the artwork was created or when it was finished (if known).
* **Artwork Category**: Select the category of the artwork from the provided options (e.g., Painting, Sculpture, etc.).
* **Medium**: A brief description of the materials or medium used to create the artwork.
* **Support**: A description of the surface or base material used to create the artwork (if applicable).
* **Dimensions Height**: The height of the artwork in inches.
* **Dimensions Width**: The width of the artwork in inches.
* **Dimensions Depth**: The depth of the artwork in inches (if applicable).
* **Weight**: The weight of the artwork in pounds (if applicable).
* **Edition Info**: If the artwork is an editioned piece, provide the edition number (if applicable).
* **Signature Location**: Where the artist signed or inscribed the artwork.
* **Condition Status**: Select the current condition of the artwork from the options provided (e.g., Excellent, Very Good, etc.).
* **Condition Notes**: Additional details about the condition of the artwork.
* **Acquisition Date**: The date the artwork was acquired (if applicable).
* **Acquisition Method**: Select how the artwork was acquired (e.g., Purchase, Donation, etc.).
* **Acquisition Cost**: The cost of acquiring the artwork (if applicable).
* **Current Value**: The estimated current value of the artwork (if applicable).
* **Appraisal Date**: The date of the last appraisal (if applicable).
* **Current Location**: The location where the artwork is currently stored or displayed.
* **Storage Requirements**: Special storage needs or requirements for the artwork (if applicable).
* **Display History**: A list of previous exhibitions or displays the artwork has been a part of (if applicable).
* **Conservation History**: Any conservation work done on the artwork (if applicable).
* **Insurance Status**: Select whether the artwork is insured.
* **Insurance Value**: The insured value of the artwork (if applicable).
* **Copyright Status**: Any copyright information related to the artwork (if applicable).
* **Reproduction Rights**: Who holds the reproduction rights to the artwork (if applicable).
* **Provenance**: A brief description of the ownership history of the artwork (if applicable).
* **Exhibition History**: A list of previous exhibitions or displays the artwork has been a part of (if applicable).
* **Bibliography**: A list of publications featuring the artwork (if applicable).
* **Related Works**: A list of related artworks (if applicable).
* **Notes**: Additional notes or comments about the artwork (if applicable).
* **Recorded By**: The name of the person recording the artwork's information.
