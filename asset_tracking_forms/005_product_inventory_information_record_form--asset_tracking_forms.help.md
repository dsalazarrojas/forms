# product_inventory_information_record_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Select the required fields you need to fill in, as indicated by the "required" label on each field.
3. Fill in the fields with the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Product Inventory Form** (1, product_inventory_form, Optional)
This field is the main form section for recording product information.

### **Product Information** (2, product_info_form, Optional)
Enter a brief description of the product. This field is not required.

### **Product Location** (3, product_location_form, Optional)
Enter the location where the product is stored. This field is not required.

### **Product Description** (4, product_description_form, Optional)
Enter a brief description of the product. This is the same field as **Product Information** but in a different format.

### **Product Quantity** (5, product_quantity_form, Optional)
Enter the quantity of the product. This field is not required.

### **Product Price** (6, product_price_form, Optional)
Enter the price of the product. This field is not required.

### **Product Date** (7, product_date_form, Optional)
Enter the date the product was received. This field is not required.

### **Product Supplier** (8, product_supplier_form, Optional)
Choose whether the product was supplied or not. Select either "Yes" or "No".

### **Product Status** (9, product_status_form, Optional)
Choose the status of the product. Select either "Active" or "Inactive".

### **Product Notes** (10, product_notes_form, Optional)
Enter any additional notes about the product. This field is not required.

### **Contact Email** (11, contact_email_form, Optional)
Enter the contact email for the product. This field is not required.

### **Contact Phone** (12, contact_phone_form, Optional)
Enter the contact phone number for the product. This field is not required.
