# Inventory Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and organize inventory data in a structured and efficient manner. This form is designed to gather information about the inventory, including product description, quantity, unit price, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the general information section (Fields 1-2) with the necessary data about the product.
2. Select the product category (Field 5) that best describes the product.
3. Choose the product tags (Field 6) that are relevant to the product.
4. Enter the date and time the product was added (Fields 7-8).
5. Add any additional notes or comments about the product (Field 9).
6. Enter the email and phone number of the product's owner or supplier (Fields 10-11).
7. Select the product status (Field 12) as active or inactive.
8. Choose the supplier (Field 13) that provided the product.
9. Select the buyer (Field 14) who purchased the product.
10. Enter the date and time the product was sold (Fields 15-16).
11. Enter the quantity and unit price of the product sold (Fields 17-18).
12. Select the currency (Field 19) in which the product was sold.
13. Enter the quantity of the product bought (Field 20).
14. Select the product weight (Field 21) that best describes the product.
15. Enter the date and time the product was bought (Fields 22-23).
16. Add any supplier notes (Field 24) about the product.
17. Add any buyer notes (Field 25) about the product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Title** (1): Enter the title or name of the product.
  - This is the name of the product being tracked in the inventory.
- **Product Description** (2): Briefly describe the product being tracked.
  - Provide a brief summary of the product's features and characteristics.
- **Quantity** (3): Enter the number of the product in inventory.
  - Enter the quantity of the product currently available.
- **Unit Price** (4): Enter the price of one unit of the product.
  - Enter the current market price of the product.
- **Product Category** (5): Select the category that best describes the product.
  - Choose a category from the list provided to classify the product.
- **Product Tags** (6): Select the tags that are relevant to the product.
  - Choose tags from the list provided to describe the product's characteristics.
- **Date Added** (7): Enter the date the product was added to the inventory.
  - Enter the date the product was first added to the inventory.
- **Time Added** (8): Enter the time the product was added to the inventory.
  - Enter the time the product was first added to the inventory.
- **Note** (9): Add any additional notes or comments about the product.
  - Enter any relevant information about the product.
- **Email** (10): Enter the email address of the product's owner or supplier.
  - Enter the email address of the person responsible for the product.
- **Phone** (11): Enter the phone number of the product's owner or supplier.
  - Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the product.
- **Product Status** (12): Select the status of the product as active or inactive.
  - Choose the status of the product as active or inactive.
- **Supplier** (13): Select the supplier that provided the product.
  - Choose the supplier from the list provided.
- **Buyer** (14): Select the buyer who purchased the product.
  - Choose the buyer from the list provided.
- **Date Sold** (15): Enter the date the product was sold.
  - Enter the date the product was sold.
- **Time Sold** (16): Enter the time the product was sold.
  - Enter the time the product was sold.
- **Quantity Sold** (17): Enter the quantity of the product sold.
  - Enter the quantity of the product that was sold.
- **Unit Price Sold** (18): Enter the unit price of the product sold.
  - Enter the price at which the product was sold.
- **Currency** (19): Select the currency in which the product was sold.
  - Choose the currency from the list provided.
- **Quantity Bought** (20): Enter the quantity of the product bought.
  - Enter the quantity of the product that was purchased.
- **Product Weight** (21): Select the weight of the product.
  - Choose the weight of the product from the list provided.
- **Date Bought** (22): Enter the date the product was bought.
  - Enter the date the product was purchased.
- **Time Bought** (23): Enter the time the product was bought.
  - Enter the time the product was purchased.
- **Supplier Notes** (24): Add any notes or comments from the supplier.
  - Enter any information from the supplier about the product.
- **Buyer Notes** (25): Add any notes or comments from the buyer.
  - Enter any information from the buyer about the product.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Double-check the accuracy of the information entered.
- Review the form before submitting to ensure all fields are complete and correct.
