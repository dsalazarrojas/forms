# Sneaker Inventory Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sneaker Inventory Record Form is a tool used to document and track information about individual sneakers in the inventory. This form helps manage and maintain accurate records of the sneaker collection.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out all required fields**, which are marked as "required" in the form.
2. Enter your contact information in the "first_name", "last_name", "email", and "phone" fields. 
3. For "shoe_type", select one or more shoe types from the options provided.
4. Assess the condition of the sneaker from the options "excellent", "good", or "bad".
5. Enter the size of the sneaker.
6. Enter the number of the sneaker in stock, if applicable.
7. Record the location where the sneaker is stored.
8. If available, enter the date and price of purchase.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name** (text, required): Enter your name.
* **last_name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **email** (email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **phone** (text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **shoe_type** (select multiple, required): Select the type of shoe (e.g., Air Jordan, Adidas).
* **condition** (select one, required): Assess the condition of the sneaker (e.g., excellent, good, bad).
* **size** (number, required): Enter the size of the sneaker.
* **quantity** (number, optional): Enter the number of the sneaker in stock.
* **location** (note, required): Describe where the sneaker is stored (e.g., shelf, box, closet).
* **purchase_date** (date, optional): If available, enter the date the sneaker was purchased.
* **purchase_price** (number, required): Enter the price at which the sneaker was purchased.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
- Review and double-check the information before submitting the form.
- Update the form regularly to reflect any changes in the sneaker's status.
- Consider adding more details about the sneaker's condition or other attributes that may help with tracking and inventory management.
