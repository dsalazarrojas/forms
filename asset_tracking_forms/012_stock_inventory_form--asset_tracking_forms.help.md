## Purpose
This form is designed to track and manage stock inventory of a company or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the stock type from the provided options.
2. Enter a brief description of the stock item.
3. Enter the quantity of the stock item.
4. Select the date the stock item was received.
5. Choose the status of the stock item (e.g., New, Used, Damaged, Sold).
6. Enter the location of the stock item.
7. Select the category of the stock item.
8. Enter the subcategory of the stock item.
9. Select the condition of the stock item (e.g., Good, Fair, Bad).
10. Enter the price of the stock item.
11. Enter the name of the stock item vendor.
12. Add any additional notes about the stock item (optional).
