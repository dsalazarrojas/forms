# Food Storage Inventory Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to aid in monitoring and managing the food storage inventory levels of your organization. It's used to collect information on the current stock levels of food items, helping you to keep track of what's available, what's needed, and what's purchased.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form fields below and ensure you have all the required information at hand.
2. Fill out the form with the requested details.
3. Submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Food Item** (<font size="small">`food_item`</font>): Enter the name or description of the food item being tracked.
* **Amount On Hand** (<font size="small">`amount_on_hand`</font>): Enter the current quantity of the food item available in storage.
* **Amount Needed** (<font size="small">`amount_needed`</font>): Enter the amount of the food item needed or required.
* **Purchases** (<font size="small">`purchases`</font>): Record any purchases or transactions related to the food item.

Note: This form is meant to be filled out as and when needed, and it's not required to complete it.
