# Wrestling Match Prediction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect users' predictions and insights on wrestling matches. It's intended for a specific group of users who are passionate about wrestling and want to share their opinions on upcoming matches.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address for contact information.
2. Select your picks for each match, choosing between "Yes" and "No" options.
3. Provide a confidence level for each match, selecting a number from 0-100.
4. Add a brief reasoning or note for each match to explain your picks and confidence levels.
5. Repeat steps 2-4 for each match.
6. Once you've completed all matches, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Match 1**: 
	+ **Match 1**: Select your picks for the first match, choosing between "Yes" and "No" options.
	+ **Confidence Level Match 1**: Provide a confidence level for the first match, selecting a number from 0-100.
	+ **Reasoning For Match 1**: Add a brief note explaining your picks and confidence level for the first match.
	+ **Confidence Note (Match 1)**: Add a separate note for your confidence level, if needed.
* **Match 2**: 
	+ **Match 2**: Select your picks for the second match, choosing between "Yes" and "No" options.
	+ **Confidence Level Match 2**: Provide a confidence level for the second match, selecting a number from 0-100.
	+ **Reasoning For Match 2**: Add a brief note explaining your picks and confidence level for the second match.
	+ **Confidence Note (Match 2)**: Add a separate note for your confidence level, if needed.
* **Form Submission**: Enter any additional comments or information about your submissions.
* **Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (will be used for contact purposes).

## Tips

* Please fill out all fields for each match to ensure accurate and complete data collection.
* Select the correct options for each match according to your personal insights and analysis.
* Use clear and concise notes for each match to explain your picks and confidence levels.
* Make sure to proofread your submission for errors and completeness before submitting.
