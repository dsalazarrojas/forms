# Villain Character Profile - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Villain Character Profile form is used to collect and store information about a villain character's identity and attributes. This form is essential for creating, editing, or managing character profiles in your game world.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the character's **Full Name** in the Full Name field. This is the primary name by which the character is known.
2. Provide an **Alias** (optional) for the character, if applicable.
3. Enter the character's **Real Name** (optional) for internal reference or secret identity purposes.
4. Choose a **Title** (optional) that reflects the character's position or role.
5. Select the character's **Species** from the options provided. This can be Human, Alien, Robot, or Monster.
6. Optionally, enter the character's **Powers** if they have any.
7. List any **Weaknesses** the character may have.
8. Enter the character's **Email** address, which will be used for internal communication or notifications.
9. Optionally, enter the character's **Phone** number for further contact.
10. Add a **Bio** to describe the character's background, personality, or story.
11. Upload or reference the character's **Image** (optional) for visual representation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (text, required): This is the character's primary name by which they are known.
- **Alias** (text, optional): An alternative name or nickname used for the character.
- **Real Name** (text, optional): The character's true, secret, or internal name used for reference.
- **Title** (text, optional): A descriptive term for the character's position or role.
- **Species** (select multiple, required): The character's species or type (Human, Alien, Robot, or Monster).
- **Powers** (text, optional): A list of abilities or powers possessed by the character.
- **Weaknesses** (text, optional): A list of vulnerabilities or limitations of the character.
- **Email** (email, required): The character's email address for internal communication or notifications.
- **Phone** (text, optional): The character's phone number for further contact.
- **Bio** (text, optional): A descriptive background, personality, or story about the character.
- **Image** (text, optional): A reference or upload link to the character's image.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately.
- Be creative when choosing the character's title and species.
- Remember to keep the character's email and phone number for internal use only.
- Use the bio section to add depth to the character's story and personality.
- The image can be a reference to a public image or a uploaded image file. 

Note that this guide assumes the form fields are self-explanatory, and the provided explanations are based on the best-effort understanding of the field types. If any field label is unclear or weak, please clarify or modify the guide accordingly.
