# Esports Broadcaster Licensing Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Esports Broadcaster Licensing Application form is intended for individuals or organizations seeking to obtain a license for broadcasting esports events. This form is used to gather necessary information to process and evaluate your license request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the required and optional sections as prompted.
2. Ensure that you have read and understood the instructions and requirements before proceeding.
3. Review each field carefully to ensure the information provided is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **League Name** (`league_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the league you are affiliated with or the event you are planning to broadcast.
* **Tournament ID** (`tournament_id`, number, optional): Enter the ID number associated with the league or event.
* **Broadcaster Name** (`broadcaster_name`, text, optional): Enter your name or the name of your organization.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for your license request.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your or your organization's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your or your organization's phone number.
* **License Details** (`license_details`, note, optional): Enter any additional details about your license request, such as event dates or times.
* **Broadcast Schedule** (`broadcast_schedule`, date, optional): Enter the dates and times you plan to broadcast the event.
* **Broadcast Duration** (`broadcast_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of your broadcast, in hours or minutes.
* **License Request** (`license_request`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you are requesting a license for your broadcast.
* **Broadcast Media** (`broadcast_media`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable media types (TV, Radio, Digital, Social Media, or Other) you plan to use for your broadcast.
* **License Agreement** (`license_agreement`, text, optional): Enter the version of the license agreement you are familiar with.
* **Agreement Version** (`agreement_version`, text, optional): Enter the version number of the license agreement you are familiar with.

## Tips
* Ensure that you answer all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field or have questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will review your application and respond to your inquiry promptly.
