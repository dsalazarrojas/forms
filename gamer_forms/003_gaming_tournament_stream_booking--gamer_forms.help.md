# Gaming Tournaments Stream Booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Gaming Tournaments Stream Booking" form is designed to collect information from streamers about their streaming schedule and preferences. This information is necessary for the form owner to understand and manage the streamers' streaming setup.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the start time for your stream from the time picker.
2. Enter your name as the streamer.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter the title of the game being streamed.
5. Enter the ID of the game being streamed.
6. Add any additional notes about yourself as the streamer.
7. Select the start date for your stream.
8. Select the end date for your stream.
9. Specify how long your stream will last.
10. Enter the ID of your streamer's agenda.
11. Select whether you have any slots available for streaming.
12. Select whether you have a stream type preference.
13. Select whether you have a preferred stream format.
14. Select your stream quality preference.
15. Enter the link to your stream.
16. Select the type of your stream link.
17. If required, enter the password for your stream.
18. Enter any notes about your stream password.
19. Select whether your stream password is required.
20. Enter the timezone for your stream.
21. Enter any additional notes about your timezone.
22. Select whether your stream timezone is required.
23. Select the form owner as the person responsible for your streaming setup.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, required): Select the time when you want your stream to start.
- **Streamer Name** (`streamer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the streamer.
- **Streamer Email** (`streamer_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
- **Game Title** (`game_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the game being streamed.
- **Game ID** (`game_id`, `text`, required): Enter the ID of the game being streamed.
- **Streamer Phone** (`streamer_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional phone number for your contact.
- **Streamer Notes** (`streamer_notes`, `note`, required): Add any additional notes about yourself as the streamer.
- **Streamer Agenda** (`streamer_agenda`, `date`, required): Select the start date for your stream.
- **Stream Start Date** (`stream_start_date`, `date`, required): Select the start date for your stream.
- **Stream End Date** (`stream_end_date`, `date`, required): Select the end date for your stream.
- **Stream Duration** (`stream_duration`, `number`, required): Specify how long your stream will last.
- **Streamer Agenda ID** (`streamer_agenda_id`, `text`, required): Enter the ID of your streamer's agenda.
- **Streamer Slots** (`streamer_slots`, `select_multiple`, required): Select whether you have any slots available for streaming.
- **Streamer Stream Type** (`streamer_stream_type`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you have a stream type preference.
- **Streamer Stream Format** (`streamer_stream_format`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you have a preferred stream format.
- **Streamer Stream Quality** (`streamer_stream_quality`, `select_one`, required): Select your stream quality preference.
- **Streamer Stream Link** (`streamer_stream_link`, `text`, required): Enter the link to your stream.
- **Streamer Stream Link Type** (`streamer_stream_link_type`, `text`, required): Select the type of your stream link.
- **Streamer Stream Password** (`streamer_stream_password`, `text`, optional): If required, enter the password for your stream.
- **Streamer Stream Password Note** (`streamer_stream_password_note`, `note`, optional): Enter any notes about your stream password.
- **Streamer Stream Password Required** (`streamer_stream_password_required`, `select_one`, required): Select whether your stream password is required.
- **Streamer Stream Timezone** (`streamer_stream_timezone`, `text`, required): Enter the timezone for your stream.
- **Streamer Stream Timezone Note** (`streamer_stream_timezone_notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes about your timezone.
- **Streamer Stream Timezone Required** (`streamer_stream_timezone_required`, `select_one`, required): Select whether your stream timezone is required.
- **Form Owner** (`form_owner`, `text`, required): Select the form owner as the person responsible for your streaming setup.
