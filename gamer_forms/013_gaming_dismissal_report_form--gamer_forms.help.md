# Gaming Dismissal Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a player's dismissal from a team or game.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you're submitting a report for a player who has been dismissed from a team or game.
2. Fill in all the required fields, indicated by the label.
3. Double-check the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dismissal Reason** (Dismissal Reason, text, required): Please provide a short description of the reason for the player's dismissal.
* **Player ID** (Player ID, number, required): Enter the player's unique ID.
* **Player Name** (Player Name, text, required): Enter the player's name.
* **Dismissal Date** (Dismissal Date, date, required): Enter the date the player was dismissed.
* **Dismissal Time** (Dismissal Time, time, required): Enter the time the player was dismissed.
* **Dismissal Cause** (Dismissal Cause, text, required): Provide a detailed explanation of the reason for the player's dismissal.
* **Dismissal Notes** (Dismissal Notes, text, required): Add any additional comments or notes about the player's dismissal.
* **Other Dismissal Reason** (Other Dismissal Reason, text, optional): If the player was dismissed for a reason not listed, please provide a brief explanation.
* **Team ID** (Team ID, text, required): Enter the team's ID.
* **Team Name** (Team Name, text, required): Enter the team's name.
* **Player Status** (Player Status, select one, required): Choose the player's status after dismissal (Active, Inactive).
* **Division** (Division, select one, required): Choose whether the player was part of a division or not.

Please submit this form carefully and accurately to ensure that the player's dismissal is properly recorded. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to our support team.
