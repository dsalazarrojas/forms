# Discord Role Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to apply for a role within a Discord server, gathering essential details from users to assess their eligibility for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above: fill out the User Info field, select your desired role and experience level, choose your primary and secondary languages, and provide your motivation for applying.
