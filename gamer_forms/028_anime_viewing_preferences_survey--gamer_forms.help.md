# Anime Viewing Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your anime viewing habits and preferences. It will help us understand your individual tastes and provide you with tailored recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select all applicable answers to the questions and fill in the necessary fields. Make sure to answer honestly, and if you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask!

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Your Name (required)
	+ Please enter your name as you would like it to be referred to in our responses.
* **Email Address**: Email Address (required)
	+ Enter your email address for us to communicate with you.
* **Age Group**: Age Group (optional)
	+ Select your age range to help us understand your demographic.
* **Hours of Anime You Watch Per Week**: Hours of Anime You Watch Per Week (required)
	+ Approximate the number of hours you spend watching anime per week.
* **Preferred Viewing Format**: Preferred Viewing Format (required)
	+ Choose how you prefer to watch anime (e.g., dubbed English, subtitled Japanese, either, or no preference).
* **Streaming Platforms You Use**: Streaming Platforms You Use (optional)
	+ Select all the streaming platforms you use to watch anime.
* **Other Ways You Watch Anime**: Other Ways You Watch Anime (optional)
	+ Choose how you watch anime besides streaming (e.g., purchased DVD or Blu-ray, anime convention events, etc.).
* **Top 3 Favorite Genres**: Top 3 Favorite Genres (required)
	+ Select your three favorite anime genres.
* **Genres You Dislike**: Genres You Dislike (optional)
	+ Choose genres you prefer to avoid.
* **Preferred Series Length**: Preferred Series Length (required)
	+ Choose the ideal number of episodes for a series.
* **Story Type**: Story Type (optional)
	+ Pick the type of plot you enjoy (e.g., standalone stories, ongoing plot, etc.).
* **Pacing Preference**: Pacing Preference (optional)
	+ Choose how fast or slow you like your anime to be.
* **Current Anime You are Watching**: What anime are you currently watching? (optional)
	+ List any anime you're currently watching.
* **Would You Like Personalized Recommendations**: Would you like personalized recommendations? (required)
	+ Answer yes, maybe, or no to receiving recommendations.
* **Special Content Interests**: Special Content Interests (optional)
	+ Select any special interests you'd like to see in anime (e.g., LGBTQ+ representation, strong female characters, etc.).
* **Additional Feedback or Suggestions**: Additional Feedback or Suggestions (optional)
	+ Share any comments or suggestions you have about anime selection.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
- Select all applicable options for each question.
- If you have any comments or suggestions, feel free to add them at the end.
- You can always ask if you have any questions or need clarification on any field.
