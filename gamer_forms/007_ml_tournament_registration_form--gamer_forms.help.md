# ML Tournament Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for Machine Learning (ML) tournament participants to register their teams. It collects essential information about the team and its participants.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Team Information**: Fill out the Team Information field with your team's details.
2.  **Participant Information**: Provide information about each participant in your team.
3.  **Contact Information**: Fill out the Contact Information field with your contact details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Team Information**: 
    *   <label>Team Information</label> (text, required)
    *   This field is where you will provide information about your team.
*   **Participant Information**: 
    *   <label>Participant Information</label> (text, required)
    *   In this field, you will list each participant of your team, one at a time.
*   **Contact Information**: 
    *   <label>Contact Information</label> (text, required)
    *   Here, you will provide a convenient contact method, like email or phone number, to get updates about the tournament and communicate with organizers if necessary. 

## Tips

*   Be thorough when filling out each field to avoid errors in your team's entry.
*   Double-check that each required field is filled out accurately.
*   Review each field's label for any clarification needed. 
*   If you have any doubts or issues, contact the tournament organizers for assistance.
