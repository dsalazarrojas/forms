# Game Name Assignment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Game Name Assignment Survey is designed to gather information from gamers about the titles of games they have played or are interested in playing. This survey is used to understand the diversity of game titles and genres in the gaming community. It is intended for gamers who have played a significant number of games and are familiar with various game titles and genres.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the "Game Title" field, where you can enter the title of the game you have played or are interested in playing.
2. Next, choose the genre of the game from the "What Genre" dropdown list by selecting one or more options.
3. Then, select the "Name Ideas" field, where you can choose one or more options for how you like to name games.
4. Finally, click "Submit" to complete the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Game Title**: Enter the title of the game you have played or are interested in playing. This could be the official title, a common nickname, or a personal preference.
* **What Genre**: Select the genres of games you have played or are interested in. You can choose one, multiple, or all options from the list to better categorize your game experience.
* **Name Ideas**: Choose how you like to name games. Options include "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you prefer traditional names, names that are not traditional, or names that are a mix of both.
* **Submit**: Click this button to complete the survey and submit your responses.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and accurately about the games you have played or are interested in playing.
* You can choose all, some, or only one option for "What Genre" and "Name Ideas".
* If you have any other comments or suggestions, feel free to include them in your response.
