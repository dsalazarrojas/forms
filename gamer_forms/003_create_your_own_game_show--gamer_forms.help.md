# create-your-own-game-show - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users create their own game show by inputting relevant information about their game show concept, rules, audience, prizes, and other details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form's pages to understand the different sections and fields.
2. Enter a brief description of your game show concept in the **Concept** field.
3. Define the rules of your game show in the **Rules** field.
4. Identify your target audience in the **Audience** field by selecting one of the options provided.
5. Enter the expected number of people in the audience for your game show in the **Audience Number** field.
6. Choose the type of game show you are creating (e.g., Trivia, Music, Dance, or Other) in the **Game Type** field.
7. Enter a unique and creative name for your game show in the **Game Name** field.
8. Select the type of prizes your game show will offer in the **Prizes** field by choosing multiple options.
9. Determine the duration of each show segment in the **Duration** field by selecting one of the options provided.
10. Indicate the number of segments your game show will have in the **Segments Count** field.
11. Enter a unique name for your game show in the **Game Show Name** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Concept** (`title`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your game show concept.
* **Rules** (`rules`, `text`, required: false): Define the rules of your game show in this field.
* **Audience** (`audience_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Identify your target audience by selecting one of the options provided.
* **Audience Number** (`audience_number`, `number`, required: false): Enter the expected number of people in the audience for your game show.
* **Game Type** (`game_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of game show you are creating (e.g., Trivia, Music, Dance, or Other).
* **Game Name** (`game_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter a unique and creative name for your game show.
* **Prizes** (`prizes`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the type of prizes your game show will offer by choosing multiple options.
* **Duration** (`duration`, `select_one`, required: false): Determine the duration of each show segment by selecting one of the options provided.
* **Segments Count** (`segment_count`, `number`, required: false): Indicate the number of segments your game show will have.
* **Game Show Name** (`game_show_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter a unique name for your game show.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide clear and concise information for each field to avoid confusion.
- Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and consistency.
- If you need further clarification on any field, please refer to the field explanations above.
