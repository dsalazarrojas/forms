# Educational Game Beta Trial Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Educational Game Beta Trial Consent Form is designed to gather information from participants in a beta trial for an educational game. This form is a critical part of the study process, as it helps researchers understand who, what, and why data is being collected. The form is required to ensure that participants have provided their informed consent and agreement to participate in the study.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your **Email** address to ensure we can contact you about your participation in the study.
3. Enter your **Phone Number** if you wish to be contacted about the study.
4. If applicable, enter your **Date of Birth** to ensure we can verify your identity.
5. Enter your **Participant ID** if you have been provided one.
6. Confirm your **Participant Informed Consent** by selecting the option that you have read and understand the participant information sheet.
7. Confirm your **Participant Agreement** by selecting the option that you agree to participate.
8. If you have any additional comments or concerns about your participation, enter them in the **Participant Notes** field.
9. If you are a researcher involved in the study, enter your **Researcher ID** and **Researcher Name**, and provide any relevant **Researcher Notes**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: A valid email address where we can contact you about your participation in the study.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted about the study. This will be used to follow up with you about your participation.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth to verify your identity. This is only required if you have not been previously identified in the study.
* **Participant ID**: Enter the participant ID provided to you, if any.
* **Participant Informed Consent**: Confirm that you have read and understand the participant information sheet. Please select one of the options provided.
* **Participant Agreement**: Confirm that you agree to participate in the study. Please select one of the options provided.
* **Participant Notes**: Enter any comments or concerns you have about your participation in the study.
* **Researcher ID**: Enter the researcher ID provided to you, if any.
* **Researcher Name**: Enter the name of the researcher who is conducting the study.
* **Researcher Notes**: Enter any relevant notes or comments for the researcher.

## Tips

* Ensure that your participant information sheet is reviewed before proceeding.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the study, please contact the researcher.
* This form is intended to be completed by one person only.
* Please ensure that you select one of the options for participant informed consent and participant agreement.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask the researcher for clarification.
