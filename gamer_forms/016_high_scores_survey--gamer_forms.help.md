# High Scores Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The High Scores Survey is designed to collect information from gamers about their performance, feedback, and contact information. This form helps developers, administrators, or moderators track player progress and gather valuable insights for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the High Scores Survey, please follow these steps:

1. Start by reading through the introduction page, which provides a brief overview of the form's purpose and instructions.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3. Type your player score in the "Player Score" field.
4. Rate your experience with a number in the "Rating" field.
5. Provide feedback or comments about your experience in the "Feedback" and "Comment" fields.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
7. If you'd like to be contacted, enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
8. Check the "Submit" field to save your progress or indicate you're not done yet.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Player Score**: Type your current score in the game or tournament.
* **Rating**: Rate your experience with a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
* **Feedback**: Share any feedback or suggestions you have about your experience.
* **Comment**: Provide additional comments about your experience.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted directly.
* **Game Name**: Type the name of the game or tournament you're participating in.
* **Submit**: Check this box to save your progress or indicate you're not done yet.
