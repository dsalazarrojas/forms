# Character Sheet Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Character Sheet Template is a tool to help you create a comprehensive character sheet for your RPG character. The form is designed to guide you through the process of providing essential details about your character, such as name, class, level, background, and abilities.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling in the required fields for **Player Name** and **Character Name**, which are essential details about your character.
2.  Select the appropriate **Class**, **Level**, and **Race** for your character from the provided options.
3.  Choose the **Background** and **Alignment** that best fit your character's personality and backstory.
4.  Enter the **Ability Scores** (STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, and CHA) and **Proficiency Bonus** for your character.
5.  For **Hit Points**, enter the current and maximum health points for your character.
6.  In **Armor Class**, enter the AC value for your character.
7.  List your character's **Proficient Skills** and **Equipment** in the respective fields.
8.  If applicable, list the **Known Spells** your character has access to.
9.  Provide a brief description of your character's **Class Features and Abilities**.
10. Enter a brief **Character Backstory**, if you desire.
11. Optionally, provide **Character Goals** and **Additional Notes** about your character.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Player Name** (Required): Enter your real name, which will be used as a reference for the character sheet.
*   **Character Name** (Required): Enter the name of your character, which can be a nickname or a full title.
*   **Character Title** (Optional): Enter any titles or honorifics your character may have.
*   **Class** (Required): Select the class that best fits your character's profession or abilities.
*   **Level** (Required): Enter the current level of your character.
*   **Race** (Required): Select the race or species of your character.
*   **Background** (Required): Choose the background that suits your character's origin and history.
*   **Alignment** (Required): Select the moral alignment of your character.
*   **Strength** (Required): Enter your character's Strength score.
*   **Dexterity** (Required): Enter your character's Dexterity score.
*   **Constitution** (Required): Enter your character's Constitution score.
*   **Intelligence** (Required): Enter your character's Intelligence score.
*   **Wisdom** (Required): Enter your character's Wisdom score.
*   **Charisma** (Required): Enter your character's Charisma score.
*   **Hit Points** (Required): Enter both the current and maximum health points for your character.
*   **Armor Class** (Required): Enter the AC value for your character.
*   **Proficiency Bonus** (Required): Enter the proficiency value for your character.
*   **Proficient Skills** (Required): List the areas your character is proficient in.
*   **Equipment** (Required): List the items and equipment your character has.
*   **Known Spells** (Optional): List the spells you have access to, if applicable.
*   **Class Features and Abilities** (Required): Provide a brief description of your character's class features and abilities.
*   **Character Backstory** (Optional): Enter a brief origin story for your character.
*   **Character Goals** (Optional): Enter your character's motivations and objectives.
*   **Additional Notes** (Optional): Enter any other details about your character.
