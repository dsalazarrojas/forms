# esports_junior_tag_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register junior teams for esports events. It requires general information about the team, team details, and information about the players participating in the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the general information section with the team's name, location, and contact details.
2. Provide team details, including the team's name, logo, and tag.
3. Add player information, including player names, ages, and grades for each player participating in the event.
4. Enter event details, such as the event name and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (`1`, `text`, required):
  * Enter the team's general information, such as the team's name, location, and contact details.
* **Team Info** (`2`, `text`, required):
  * Enter the team's details, including the team's name.
* **Team Representative Info** (`3`, `text`, optional):
  * Enter the team representative's name and contact information (if available).
* **Team Contact Info** (`4`, `text`, required):
  * Enter the team's contact person's name and contact details.
* **Team Experience** (`5`, `text`, optional):
  * Briefly describe the team's experience and background.
* **Team Tourney Info** (`6`, `text`, optional):
  * Enter any additional tourney information, such as previous events participated in.
* **Player 1 Info** (`7`, `text`, required):
  * Enter the first player's name and other relevant details.
* **Player 2 Info** (`8`, `text`, optional):
  * Enter the second player's name and other relevant details (if available).
* **Player 3 Info** (`9`, `text`, optional):
  * Enter the third player's name and other relevant details (if available).
* **Player 4 Info** (`10`, `text`, optional):
  * Enter the fourth player's name and other relevant details (if available).
* **Player 5 Info** (`11`, `text`, optional):
  * Enter the fifth player's name and other relevant details (if available).
* **Team Logo** (`12`, `text`, optional):
  * Upload or provide a link to the team's logo.
* **Team Banner** (`13`, `text`, optional):
  * Upload or provide a link to the team's banner.
* **Team Club** (`14`, `text`, optional):
  * Enter the team's club affiliation (if any).
* **Team Tag** (`15`, `text`, optional):
  * Enter a unique tag or identifier for the team.
* **Tourney** (`16`, `text`, optional):
  * Enter any additional tourney information, such as previous events participated in.
* **Event Name** (`17`, `text`, optional):
  * Enter the event name.
* **Event Date** (`18`, `text`, required):
  * Enter the event date in date format.
* **Event Time** (`19`, `time`, optional):
  * Enter the event time (if applicable).
* **Player 1 Name** (`20`, `text`, required):
  * Enter the first player's name.
* **Player 1 Age** (`21`, `number`, required):
  * Enter the first player's age.
* **Player 1 Grade** (`22`, `number`, required):
  * Enter the first player's grade level.
* **Player 2 Name** (`23`, `text`, required):
  * Enter the second player's name.
* **Player 2 Age** (`24`, `number`, required):
  * Enter the second player's age.
* **Player 2 Grade** (`25`, `number`, required):
  * Enter the second player's grade level.
