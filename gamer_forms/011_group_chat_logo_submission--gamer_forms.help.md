# Group Chat Logo Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a new group chat logo for processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Group Chat Logo Submission" field with a brief description of your submission.
2. Upload a logo file for your group chat.
3. Enter the name of the owner of the logo.
4. Enter the email address of the owner of the logo.
5. Select the usage permission for your logo.
6. Enter any additional notes about the logo.
7. Enter the phone number of the owner of the logo.
8. Enter the street address of the owner of the logo.
9. Enter the city of the owner of the logo.
10. Enter the state of the owner of the logo.
11. Enter the zip code of the owner of the logo.
12. Enter the country of the owner of the logo.
13. Select the submission status of your logo.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Group Chat Logo Submission** (`Group Chat Logo Submission`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your group chat logo submission.
* **logo_file** (`logo_file`, text, optional): Upload a logo file for your group chat.
* **owner_name** (`owner_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the owner of the logo.
* **owner_email** (`owner_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the owner of the logo.
* **usage_permission** (`usage_permission`, select one, required): Select the usage permission for your logo.
* **notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the logo.
* **phone_number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the owner of the logo.
* **street_address** (`street_address`, text, optional): Enter the street address of the owner of the logo.
* **city** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city of the owner of the logo.
* **state** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state of the owner of the logo.
* **zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of the owner of the logo.
* **country** (`country`, text, optional): Enter the country of the owner of the logo.
* **submission_status** (`submission_status`, select one, required): Select the submission status of your logo.

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
