# XP/FR Points Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to update a user's experience points (XP) and forum reputation (FR) points for any discrepancies or errors in the past. It's intended for internal use by moderators or support staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and reason for adjustment to provide context for the update.
2. Specify the amount of XP/FR points to adjust.
3. Select the date when the adjustment was requested.
4. Provide a support contact email address and phone number for follow-up.
5. Optionally, add any additional notes for support or operations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitter** (`user_xp_fr_points_update`, text, optional): Enter your name and title to submit the form.
* **Reason for Adjustment** (`reason_for_adjustment`, text, required): Provide a brief explanation for the XP/FR points adjustment.
* **XP/FR Points Adjustment** (`user_xp_fr_points`, number, required): Enter the amount of XP/FR points to adjust.
* **Date Adjustment Requested** (`date_adjustment_requested`, date, required): Select the date when the adjustment was requested.
* **Reason for Adjustment 2** (`reason_for_adjustment_2`, text, optional): This field is not required, but provides additional context for the adjustment.
* **Support Contact** (`support_contact`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact information for support.
* **Support Email** (`support_email`, email, required): Enter the support email address for follow-up.
* **Support Phone** (`support_phone`, text, required): Enter the support phone number for follow-up.
* **Support Notes** (`support_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes for support.
* **Operations Notes** (`operations_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes for operations.
* **HR Notes** (`hr_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes for HR.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, required): Enter the name of the person to assign the adjustment to.

## Tips

* Please ensure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered.
* Use this form for legitimate adjustments only, and avoid unnecessary updates.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or support staff for guidance.
