# Virtual Reality Production Grant Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Virtual Reality Production Grant Application form is designed to collect information for a virtual reality project grant application. This form is used to gather necessary details about the project, its budget, and the applicant's contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter a brief description of your project in the "Grant Application" field (page 1).
3. Enter your budget for the project in the "Budget" field (page 2).
4. Attach any supporting documents for your project in the "Supporting Documents" field (page 3).
5. Provide a detailed production plan for your project in the "Production Plan" field (page 4).
6. Enter your project budget in the "Project Budget" field (page 5).
7. Choose the supporting documents that are relevant to your project in the "Supporting Documents Uploader" field (page 6).
8. Indicate whether your project is eligible for a grant fund in the "Grant Fund" field (page 7).
9. Choose the production status of your project in the "Production Status" field (page 8).
10. Enter your production schedule in the "Production Schedule" field (page 9).
11. Enter your project lead's name and contact information in the "Project Lead" field (page 10).
12. List your team members in the "Team Members" field (page 11).
13. Provide the contact person's name and contact information in the "Contact Person" field (page 12).
14. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (page 13).
15. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (page 14).

17. Enter your website in the "Website" field (page 16).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Grant Application** (`grant_application`, text, optional):
	+ A brief description of your project.
* **Budget** (`budget`, number, optional):
	+ Your estimated budget for the project.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, note, optional):
	+ Attach any relevant documents that support your project proposal.
* **Production Plan** (`production_plan`, text, optional):
	+ A detailed plan for the production of your project.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, number, optional):
	+ Your actual project budget.
* **Supporting Documents Uploader** (`supporting_documents_uploads`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose the supporting documents that are relevant to your project:
		+ Budget and timeline
		+ Project proposal
		+ Production plan
		+ Project deliverables
* **Grant Fund** (`grant_fund`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Indicate whether your project is eligible for a grant fund:
		+ Yes
		+ No
* **Production Status** (`production_status`, select_one, optional):
	+ The current production status of your project:
		+ Active
		+ Inactive
* **Production Schedule** (`production_schedule`, date, optional):
	+ The planned production schedule for your project.
* **Project Lead** (`project_lead`, text, optional):
	+ The name and contact information of your project lead.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, text, optional):
	+ A list of team members involved in the project.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional):
	+ The name and contact information of the contact person.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
	+ Your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional):
	+ Your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional):
	+ Your project's address.
* **Website** (`website`, text, optional):
	+ Your project's website.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have attached all required supporting documents to your application.
* Double-check that your project budget and schedule are realistic and feasible.
* Clearly describe your production plan and project deliverables.
* Be prepared to provide any additional information that may be requested as part of the application process.
