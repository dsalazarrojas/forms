# kids_monopoly_game_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The kids' Monopoly game registration form is designed to collect essential details from users and their guardians for game participation. This information will be stored securely for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:
1. Scroll through the pages to find the relevant section.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
3. Submit your registration for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details** (`user_details`, text, optional): This field collects the user's basic details, such as their name and contact information. Please enter the user's name, email, or phone number as applicable.
* **Guardian Details** (`guardian_details`, text, optional): This field is for the guardian's or parent's information, including their name and contact details.
* **Game Sessions** (`game_sessions`, text, optional): This field is intended to track the user's game sessions, including dates, times, and any notable events or outcomes.

## Tips
* Ensure you fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy.
* Submit your form once you have completed all necessary fields.
* If you need help or have questions, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
