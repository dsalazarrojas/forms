# game_level_design_checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and guidance on the quality and design of a game level. It will help identify areas of improvement, highlight strengths and weaknesses, and support the overall development and polish process of the game.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Go through each page of the form carefully and ensure you understand each field's purpose.
2.  Fill out the form with your feedback and guidance on the game level's title, gameplay, visuals, and other relevant aspects.
3.  Choose the correct answers for each field based on your assessment of the game level.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Game Level Title** (`game_level_title`, `text`, required: false): This is the title of the game level. Enter a short and descriptive title that summarizes the game level.
*   **Gameplay Issues** (`gameplay_issues`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose 'Yes' if the game level has significant issues with gameplay mechanics, such as unclear objectives, frustratingly difficult sections, or poorly implemented features.
*   **Visuals Issues** (`visuals_issues`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all issues related to the game level's visuals, such as poor graphics, unclear UI, or poorly optimized performance.
*   **Technical Issues** (`technical_issues`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all issues related to the game level's technical aspects, such as lag, glitches, or crashes.
*   **Player Guidance** (`player_guidance`, `text`, required: false): Describe any guidance or feedback you would provide to players to improve their experience with the game level.
*   **Player Experience** (`player_experience`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose 'Yes' if the game level provides an enjoyable experience for players, or 'No' if it's not.
*   **Level Length** (`level_length`, `number`, required: false): Enter the estimated length of the game level, in minutes.
*   **Difficulty Level** (`difficulty_level`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the level of difficulty of the game level, such as High, Low, or Medium.
*   **Overall Score** (`overall_score`, `number`, required: false): Enter an overall score out of 100 for the game level, based on your assessment.
*   **Level Description** (`level_description`, `text`, required: false): Enter a short description of the game level, highlighting its unique features and mechanics.
*   **Game Level Title Comment** (`game_level_title_comment`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or feedback on the game level's title.
*   **Gameplay Achievements** (`gameplay_achievements`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all achievements and rewards that the game level offers.
*   **Level Designers** (`level_designers`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all individuals involved in the level's design, such as level creators, reviewers, or game developers.
*   **Level Reviewers** (`level_reviewers`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all individuals involved in reviewing the level, such as QA, game testing, or game development team.
*   **Game Developers** (`game_developers`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all developers involved in the game's development, such as game creators, artists, or engineers.

## Tips
*   Always fill out the form carefully and thoroughly, providing detailed and specific feedback.
*   Use clear and concise language in each field to ensure accurate understanding.
*   Be respectful and constructive in your feedback, providing actionable suggestions for improvement.
*   Review the form before submitting it to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
