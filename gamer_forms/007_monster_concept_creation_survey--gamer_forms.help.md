# Monster Concept Creation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Monster Concept Creation Survey form, which is designed to help collect and document your monster concept ideas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name for reference purposes only.
2. Enter the name of your monster concept.
3. Choose the type of creature you are envisioning (e.g., Dragon, Beast, etc.).
4. Describe your monster in as much detail as possible.
5. Select the features that you think your monster has (e.g., sharp claws, scales, etc.).
6. Choose the abilities you think your monster has (e.g., super strength, super speed, etc.).
7. If you have an image of your monster, you can upload it as proof of concept.
8. Summarize your monster concept in your own words.
9. Enter your contact information (email and phone number) so we can get back to you.
10. If you have any additional notes or comments, you can add them here.
11. Finally, click submit to send us your monster concept.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Monster Concept Name** (`monster_concept_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your monster concept. This is the name of your monster, and it should be unique.
* **Creature Type** (`creature_type`, select multiple, required): Choose the type of creature you are envisioning. This can be a general category, such as "Dragon" or "Beast".
* **Creature Description** (`creature_description`, text, required): Describe your monster in as much detail as possible. This is your chance to tell us what makes your monster unique.
* **Creature Features** (`creature_features`, select one, required): Select the features you think your monster has. These can include things like "Sharp Claws" or "Scales".
* **Creature Abilities** (`creature_abilities`, select multiple, required): Choose the abilities you think your monster has. These can include things like "Super Strength" or "Super Speed".
* **Upload Image** (`file_upload`, text, required): If you have an image of your monster, you can upload it here as proof of concept.
* **Monster Concept Submission** (`monster_concept_submission`, text, required): Summarize your monster concept in your own words. This is your chance to tell us what makes your monster unique and interesting.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can get back to you with any questions or follow-up information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like us to contact you directly.
* **Additional Notes** (`submission_notes`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, you can add them here.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): This field will be automatically populated with the date and time of your submission.
