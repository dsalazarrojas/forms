# poker_game_sign_up - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to sign up for a poker game. Fill out the form to provide your details and preferences, and the system will use this information to manage your sign-up and potentially pair you with other players.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Sign Up" field with your preferred username or handle.
2. Provide your "Player Name" as you'd like it to be displayed in the game.
3. If you're feeling lucky, enter a "Buy In" amount to secure your seat.
4. Add any relevant "Player Info" in the "Player Info" field for the organiser's knowledge.
5. Select the type of game you'd like to play, such as a "Tournament" or "Cash" game.
6. Choose a date and time for the game from the calendar picker or enter manually.
7. Select a location where you'd like to play, either "Online" or in person.
8. Indicate the number of players you'd like to play with.
9. Enter a "Buy In Amount" to confirm your commitment.
10. Add any additional "Notes" that might help the organiser understand your preferences.
11. Finally, provide your contact "Phone" and "Email" details so the organiser can reach out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sign Up** (1, text, optional): Enter your preferred username or handle for the game.
* **Player Name** (2, text, optional): This is your real name that will be displayed in the game.
* **Buy In** (3, number, optional): The amount you're willing to pay to participate in the game.
* **Player Info** (4, note, optional): Add any relevant information about yourself that the organiser might find useful.
* **Game Type** (5, select_one, optional): Choose between "Tournament" or "Cash" game types.
* **Date** (6, date, optional): Select a date for the game from the calendar picker or enter manually.
* **Time** (7, time, optional): Choose a time for the game.
* **Location** (8, select_multiple, optional): Select where you'd like to play, either "Online" or in person.
* **Player Count** (9, number, optional): Indicate the number of players you're comfortable playing with.
* **Buy In Amount** (10, number, optional): Enter the amount you're willing to pay to participate.
* **Notes** (11, note, optional): Add any additional comments or requests you have for the organiser.
* **Phone** (12, text, optional): Provide your phone number so the organiser can contact you.
* **Email** (13, email, optional): Enter your email address for further communication.
