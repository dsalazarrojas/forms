# Twitch Mod Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help us understand your qualifications, experience, and fit for becoming a moderator on Twitch. This will help us assess your suitability for the role and make informed decisions about your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question accurately and thoroughly. Remember to:

* Be honest about your experience and background
* Choose the correct options for each question
* Don't hesitate to elaborate on your answers if you need to provide more information
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as you would like to be known on Twitch.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name as you would like to be known on Twitch.
* **Twitch Username** (`twitch_username`, text, required): Please enter your Twitch username.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number (optional).
* **Bio** (`bio`, note, required): Please share a brief bio about yourself, including your interests and experience on Twitch.
* **How long have you been streaming on Twitch?** (`how_long_have_you_been_streaming_on_twitch`, number, required): Please enter the number of months you've been streaming on Twitch.
* **What games do you stream?** (`what_games_do_you_streamer`, select multiple, required): Choose all the games you've streamed on Twitch.
* **Why do you want to become a moderator?** (`why_do_you_want_to_become_a_moderator`, text, required): Please explain why you're interested in becoming a moderator.
* **What would you like to do as a moderator?** (`what_would_you_like_to_do_as_a_moderator`, text, required): Please describe the role you'd like to take on as a moderator and what you'd like to achieve.
* **How many months per year do you plan to mod on Twitch?** (`how_many_months_per_year_do_you_plan_to_mod_on_twitch`, number, required): Please enter the number of months per year you plan to dedicate to moderating on Twitch.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select multiple, required): Choose your availability for moderating, including weekeday mornings, afternoon, evenings, and weekend times.
* **Availability time** (`availability_time`, time, required): Please enter your availability time in 24-hour format (e.g., 09:00-17:00).
* **What would you like to gain more experience with?** (`what_would_you_like_to_gain_more_experience_with`, select multiple, required): Choose what you'd like to gain more experience with, including moderation, streaming, chat management, and leadership.
* **How do you plan to improve Twitch as a platform?** (`how_do_you_plan_to_improve_twitch_as_a_platform`, text, required): Please explain how you plan to contribute to improving Twitch as a platform.
* **Have you been banned from Twitch?** (`have_you_been_banned_from_twitch`, select one, required): Please choose whether you've been banned from Twitch.
* **Why were you banned from Twitch?** (`why_were_you_banned_from_twitch`, text, required): Please explain why you were banned from Twitch.
* **What is your current rank?** (`what_is_your_twitch_rank`, text, required): Please enter your current rank on Twitch.
* **What is your current status?** (`what_is_your_current_twitch_status`, text, required): Please enter your current status on Twitch.
* **What is your current ID number?** (`what_is_your_current_twitch_id`, text, required): Please enter your current ID number on Twitch.
* **How long have you been muted on Twitch?** (`how_long_have_you_been_muted_on_twitch`, select one, required): Please choose whether you've been muted on Twitch.
* **Have you been timeouted on Twitch?** (`have_you_been_timeouted_on_twitch`, select one, required): Please choose whether you've been timeouted on Twitch.
* **Have you been banned from another Twitch streamer's channel?** (`have_you_been_banned_from_another_twitch_streamer`, select one, required): Please choose whether you've been banned from another streamer's channel.
* **Have you been kicked from another Twitch streamer's channel?** (`have_you_been_kicked_from_another_twitch_streamer`, select one, required): Please choose whether you've been kicked from another streamer's channel.
* **Have you been terminated from another Twitch streamer's channel?** (`have_you_been_terminated_from_another_twitch_streamer`, select one, required): Please choose whether you've been terminated from another streamer's channel.
