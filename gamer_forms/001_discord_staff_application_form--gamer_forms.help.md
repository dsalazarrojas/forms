# Discord Staff Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from potential staff members about their availability and timezone offset to coordinate with the server's time zone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form with your Discord username.
2. Provide a brief explanation of why you wish to become a staff member.
3. Share your experience in the community.
4. Specify your availability for work, including your timezone offset and offset hours.
5. Set your timezone offset.
6. Select your timezone.
7. Choose how you are aware of your timezone offset (True or False).
8. Choose how you are aware of your timezone offset hours (True or False).
9. Choose how you are aware of your timezone offset minutes (True or False).
10. Choose how you are aware of your timezone offset seconds (True or False).
11. Choose if you are aware of your timezone offset hours offset (True or False).
12. Choose if you are aware of your timezone offset minutes offset (True or False).
13. Choose if you are aware of your timezone offset seconds offset (True or False).
14. Choose if you are aware of your timezone offset AM/PM (True or False).
15. Choose if you are aware of your timezone offset UTC (True or False).
16. Choose if you are aware of your timezone offset (True or False).
17. Select the Discord channel where you would like to be contacted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): This is the title of the form, and it's expected to be "Discord Staff Application Form".
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): This is your first name.
* **Discord Username** (`discord_username`, text, required): This is your Discord username.
* **Discord Tag** (`discord_tag`, text, optional): This is your Discord tag (a nickname used in the server).
* **Why Apply** (`why_apply`, text, optional): This is a brief explanation of why you want to become a staff member.
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, optional): Share your experience in the community.
* **Availability** (`availability`, text, optional): Specify your availability for work.
* **Timezone** (`timezone`, text, optional): This is your timezone.
* **Timezone Offset** (`timezone_offset`, number, optional): This is your timezone offset.
* **Timezone Offset Hours** (`timezone_offset_hrs`, number, optional): This is your timezone offset hours.
* **Timezone Offset Minutes** (`timezone_offset_mins`, number, optional): This is your timezone offset minutes.
* **Timezone Offset Seconds** (`timezone_offset_secs`, number, optional): This is your timezone offset seconds.
* **Timezone Offset Hours Offset** (`timezone_offset_hrs_offset`, number, optional): This is your timezone offset hours offset.
* **Timezone Offset Minutes Offset** (`timezone_offset_mins_offset`, number, optional): This is your timezone offset minutes offset.
* **Timezone Offset Seconds Offset** (`timezone_offset_secs_offset`, number, optional): This is your timezone offset seconds offset.
* **Timezone Offset AM/PM** (`timezone_offset_am_pm`, select_one, optional): Choose if you are aware of your timezone offset AM/PM.
* **Timezone Offset UTC** (`timezone_offset_utc`, select_one, optional): Choose if you are aware of your timezone offset UTC.
* **Timezone Offset Aware** (`timezone_offset_aware`, select_one, optional): Choose if you are aware of your timezone offset.
* **Timezone Offset Hours Aware** (`timezone_offset_hrs_aware`, select_one, optional): Choose if you are aware of your timezone offset hours.
* **Timezone Offset Minutes Aware** (`timezone_offset_mins_aware`, select_one, optional): Choose if you are aware of your timezone offset minutes.
