# Gamer Life Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the gamer's preferences, interests, and availability for a gamer community or online platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2. Select your game name, interests, and platform preferences from the corresponding dropdown menus.
3. Enter your player name, email, phone number, and age (if applicable).
4. Choose your availability and availability 2 status from the dropdown menus.
5. Select your interests 2 and hobbies from the corresponding dropdown menus.
6. Enter any additional comments or comments 2 if you have any specific information about yourself that you'd like to share.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Game Name** (`game_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the game you are interested in.
* **Interests** (`game_interests`, select_multiple, required): Select the interests that best describe you as a gamer. Options include Gaming, Role Playing, and Strategy.
* **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, required): Enter your real name as it appears on your account.
* **Email** (`player_email`, email, required): Enter your email address to stay connected with us.
* **Phone** (`player_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Availability** (`player_availability`, select_one, required): Choose whether you are Available, Not Available, or Unavailable for upcoming events or offers.
* **Player Interests** (`player_interests`, select_multiple, required): Select the interests that best describe you as a player. Options include Gaming, Role Playing, and Strategy.
* **Comments** (`player_comment`, note, required): Enter any additional comments or information about yourself.
* **Game Genre** (`game_genre`, select_one, required): Select the genre of games you are interested in. Options include Action, Adventure, and Strategy.
* **Platform** (`player_platform`, select_one, required): Choose the platform(s) you play on. Options include PC, Console, and Mobile.
* **Game Language** (`game_language`, select_one, required): Select the language of games you are comfortable playing. Options include English, Spanish, and French.
* **Age** (`player_age`, number, required): Enter your age (if applicable).
* **Platforms** (`player_platforms`, select_multiple, required): Select the platforms you play on. Options include PC, Console, and Mobile.
* **Game Type** (`game_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of games you are interested in. Options include Single Player, Multi Player, and Online Player.
* **Gender** (`player_gender`, select_one, required): Choose your gender. Options include Male, Female, Other, and Non-Binary.
* **Country** (`player_country`, select_one, required): Select your country of residence. Options include United States, Canada, Mexico, Europe, Asia, and Australia.
* **Language** (`player_language`, select_one, required): Select the language you speak fluently. Options include English, Spanish, French, German, and Other.
* **Region** (`player_region`, select_one, required): Select the region you reside in. Options include North America, South America, Europe, Asia, Africa, and Australia.
* **Occupation** (`player_occupation`, text, required): Enter your occupation or profession.
* **Hobbies** (`player_hobbies`, select_multiple, required): Select your hobbies. Options include Reading, Gaming, Music, and Other.
* **Education** (`player_edu`, select_one, required): Select your educational background. Options include High School, Bachelor, Master, and PhD.
* **Experience** (`player_experience`, number, required): Enter your experience in years (if applicable).
* **Availability 2** (`player_availability2`, select_one, required): Choose your availability for another event or offer. Options include Available, Not Available, and Unavailable.
* **Interests 2** (`player_interests2`, select_multiple, required): Select your interests 2. Options include Gaming, Role Playing, and Strategy.
* **Comments 2** (`player_comment2`, note, required): Enter any additional comments or information about yourself.
* **Game Type 2** (`player_game_type`, select_one, not required): Not a required field.
* **Platform 2** (`player_platforms2`, select_multiple, not required): Not a required field.
