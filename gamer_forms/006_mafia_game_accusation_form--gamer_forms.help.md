# Mafia Game Accusation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for players in a Mafia Game to submit accusations against other players.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill in the form title and your player name.
2. Select the type of accusation you are making (Player 1, Player 2, or Player 3).
3. Provide a brief reason for your accusation.
4. Describe any supporting details or evidence for your accusation.
5. Repeat steps 2-4 for up to 4 players.
6. Click the "Submit" button to submit your accusation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for your accusation.
* **Accusation** (`accusation`, text, optional): Briefly describe the accusation you are making.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): Provide a clear and concise reason for your accusation.
* **Supporting Details** (`supporting_details`, text, optional): Describe any supporting details or evidence for your accusation.
* **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, optional): Enter your player name.
* **Accusation Type** (`accusation_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of accusation you are making (Player 1, Player 2, or Player 3).
* **Evidence** (`evidence`, text, optional): Describe any evidence supporting your accusation.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your accusation.
* **Player Name 2** (`player_name_2`, text, optional): If you are making an accusation against multiple players, enter the name of the second player.
* **Accusation Type 2** (`accusation_type_2`, select_one, optional): Select the type of accusation you are making for the second player (Player 1, Player 2, or Player 3).
* **Evidence 2** (`evidence_2`, text, optional): Describe any evidence supporting your accusation for the second player.
* **Submit 2** (`submit_2`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your second accusation.
* **Player Name 3** (`player_name_3`, text, optional): If you are making an accusation against multiple players, enter the name of the third player.
* **Accusation Type 3** (`accusation_type_3`, select_one, optional): Select the type of accusation you are making for the third player (Player 1, Player 2, or Player 3).
* **Evidence 3** (`evidence_3`, text, optional): Describe any evidence supporting your accusation for the third player.
* **Submit 3** (`submit_3`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your third accusation.
* **Player Name 4** (`player_name_4`, text, optional): If you are making an accusation against multiple players, enter the name of the fourth player.
* **Accusation Type 4** (`accusation_type_4`, select_one, optional): Select the type of accusation you are making for the fourth player (Player 1, Player 2, or Player 3).
* **Evidence 4** (`evidence_4`, text, optional): Describe any evidence supporting your accusation for the fourth player.
* **Submit 4** (`submit_4`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your fourth accusation.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all relevant fields for each accusation.
* Ensure that your accusations are clear and concise.
* Use the evidence section to provide any supporting details.
* Review your form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact form@gic.mx.
