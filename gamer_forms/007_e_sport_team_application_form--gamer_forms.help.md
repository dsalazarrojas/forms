# E_Sport_Team_Application_Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your e-Sport team to help us evaluate your application for support.

## How To Complete This Form
Please fill out this form with the following information:

1. Team name: Enter the name of your team.
2. Team leader: Enter the name and contact information of your team's leader or main contact person.
3. Email: Enter your team's email address.
4. Website: Enter your team's website URL.
5. Discord: Enter your team's Discord tag or invite link.
6. Twitch: Enter your team's Twitch channel URL.
7. Team members: List the names and roles of your team members (e.g., players, coaches, managers).
8. Game: Enter the game you're competing in (e.g., Fortnite, League of Legends).
9. Platform: Enter the platform you're competing on (e.g., Steam, Xbox).
10. Streamer: If applicable, enter your streamer's name and URL.
11. YouTube: Enter your YouTube channel URL.
12. Social media: List your team's social media handles (e.g., Twitter, Instagram).
13. Team logo: Upload your team's logo (optional).
14. Description: Briefly describe your team and its goals.
15. Social media links: List any social media links not already included in the previous field.
16. Discord tag: Enter your team's Discord tag (if applicable).
17. Twitter: Enter your team's Twitter handle.
18. Instagram: Enter your team's Instagram handle.
19. Stream URL: Enter your team's stream URL (if applicable).
20. Platform URL: Enter your team's platform URL (if applicable).
