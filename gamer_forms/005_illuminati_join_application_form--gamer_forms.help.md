# Illuminati Join Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Illuminati Join Application Form is a application process for users to join a community with a group of like-minded individuals. The form is designed to gather information about the applicants, their gaming habits, and their experience in moderating communities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your gamer tag in the field "What is your gamer tag?"
2. Select whether or not you have a gamer tag in the field "Select your gamer tag"
3. Enter your Discord username in the field "Discord username"
4. Enter your email address in the field "Email"
5. Write a brief explanation for why you want to join the community in the field "Why do you want to join?"
6. Answer whether you are a moderator on Discord in the field "Are you a Discord moderator?"
7. If you are a moderator, describe your moderating experience in the field "What is your moderating experience?"
8. Agree to the community's coding policy by filling in the field "How do you accept the Illuminati coding of your account"

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your gamer tag?** (`gamer_info`, text, required): This field is where you enter your username for gaming platforms such as Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo, or Steam. It's how we identify you as a gamer.
* **Select your gamer tag** (`gamer_tag`, select_one, required): This field is asking you to confirm whether you have a gamer tag. Please select "Yes" if you have one.
* **Discord username** (`discord_username`, text, required): This is your username on the Discord platform.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
* **Why do you want to join?** (`why_join`, text, required): In this field, please explain why you are interested in joining The Illuminati. Be as brief as possible, while still providing enough information for us to understand your application.
* **Are you a Discord moderator?** (`moderator_info`, select_one, required): If you have experience moderating on Discord, please select "Yes".
* **What is your moderating experience?** (`moderator_experience`, text, required): If you selected "Yes" to the previous question, please describe your experience as a moderator on Discord.
* **How do you accept the Illuminati coding of your account?** (`join_requirements`, text, required): This is a field where you agree to our community's policies, including how your account will be "coded" (likely referring to a specific type of moderation or coding system). Please be aware that this field is required and should be filled with care.
