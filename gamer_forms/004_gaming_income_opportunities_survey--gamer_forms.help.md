# Gaming Income Opportunities Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gaming Income Opportunities Survey is designed to collect information about gaming-related income opportunities from end-users. This data will help us better understand the gaming industry's dynamics and identify trends.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form's fields and understand what each section represents.
2. Select the relevant options for each field based on your gaming income opportunities.
3. Enter your responses accurately and completely.
4. Submit the form when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What do you earn?** (`main_page_earnings`): Select one or more ways you earn money from gaming, such as "Game Developer", "Pro Gamer", "Streamer", "YouTuber", or "Other (please specify)".
- **Earnings (average per month)** (`main_page_earnings_2`): Enter your average monthly earnings from gaming-related activities, if applicable.
- **Moments that changed your gaming income** (`main_page_moments`): Select one or more moments that contributed to a change in your gaming income, such as "New game release", "Game update", "Twitch partnership", or "Other (please specify)".
- **Date of the most significant moment** (`main_page_moments_2`): If you selected a specific moment, please enter the date it occurred.
- **Time of the most significant moment** (`main_page_moments_3`): If you selected a specific moment, please enter the time of day it happened.
- **Most significant moment details** (`main_page_moments_4`): Briefly describe the moment that contributed to the change in your gaming income.
- **Email for further questions** (`main_page_moments_5`): Enter your email address if you're willing to be contacted for further questions or clarification on your responses.
