# Gaming Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect anonymous gaming-related data from players or gamers. The form aims to gather demographic information and opinions on gaming experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully to understand what information is being collected.
2. Fill out each field with the requested information.
3. Submit the form when you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Gaming Survey Form** (`gaming_survey_form`, text, optional): 
This field is a text field where you can enter any information you'd like to share about your gaming experience.
* **Gamer Data** (`gamer_data`, text, optional): 
This field is a text field where you can enter any demographic information you'd like to share about yourself as a gamer.
* **Gaming Survey Form Response** (`gaming_survey_form_response`, text, optional): 
This field is another text field where you can enter any additional comments or responses you'd like to share about your gaming experience.

## Tips
* Please keep in mind that this form is anonymous, so your responses will not be linked to your personal identity.
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact form@gic.mx.
