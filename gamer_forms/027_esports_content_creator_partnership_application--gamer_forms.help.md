# Esports Content Creator Partnership Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help us get to know you and your content as a potential partner. Please fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information to help us assess your eligibility for partnership.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form in its entirety, but only answer the required field, "Social Media Links", which is field 6.
2.  For the "Social Media Links" field, select up to 5 social media platforms where you have a presence. You can choose from the following options:
    *   Twitch
    *   YouTube
    *   Twitter
    *   Instagram
    *   Facebook
    *   TikTok
3.  For the remaining fields, feel free to provide as much information as you'd like, but keep in mind that it will not be used for any other purpose than to get to know you and your content better.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional):
    *   A brief title of your content or a description of your brand.
*   **Creator Details** (`creator_details`, text, optional):
    *   A short description of yourself or your team, including your name, team name, or brand name.
*   **Audience Size** (`audience_size`, number, optional):
    *   An estimate of the number of viewers or followers you have on your social media platforms.
*   **Collaboration Goals** (`collaboration_goals`, text, optional):
    *   A brief description of your goals for the partnership, such as what type of content you'd like to create together.
*   **Creator Experience** (`creator_experience`, text, optional):
    *   A brief description of your experience as a content creator, including any notable achievements or credentials.
*   **Social Media Links** (`social_media_links`, select_multiple, required):
    *   A list of up to 5 social media platforms where you have a presence, such as Twitch, YouTube, Twitter, etc.
*   **Collaboration Requests** (`collaboration_requests`, note, optional):
    *   A note about any specific requests or requirements you have for the collaboration, such as equipment or software needs.
*   **Team Size** (`team_size`, number, optional):
    *   An estimate of the number of people on your team, including yourself.
*   **Audience Type** (`audience_type`, text, optional):
    *   A brief description of your target audience, such as age group, interests, or demographics.
*   **Audience Size** (`audience_size`, number, optional):
    *   An estimate of the number of viewers or followers you have on your social media platforms.

## Tips

*   Please keep your answers concise and accurate.
*   Only answer the required field, "Social Media Links", and provide as much information as you'd like for the other fields.
*   If you're unsure about any field, feel free to reach out to us for clarification.
