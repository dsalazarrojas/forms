# Gaming Strategy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information about your gaming habits and preferences. Your responses will help us better understand what makes you tick when it comes to gaming.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, simply navigate through each page using the provided buttons.
2. Fill in the required and optional fields as prompted.
3. Make sure to review each field for any hints or explanations to ensure you understand what is being asked.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Gamer Demographics** (`gamer_demographics`, text, required: false): Please enter some basic information about yourself, such as your name and age. This will help us get a better understanding of who is participating in the survey.
- **Gaming Platforms** (`gaming_platforms`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the gaming platforms you use to play games on (e.g., PC, Console, Mobile, etc.).
- **Gaming Genres** (`gaming_genres`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the gaming genres you enjoy playing (e.g., Action, Adventure, Role Playing, etc.).
- **Strategies** (`gamer_strategies`, text, required: false): Describe your favorite gaming strategies or techniques.
- **Skill Ratings** (`skill_ratings`, number, required: false): Rate your skills in various areas, such as critical thinking, problem-solving, or creativity.
- **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, required: false): Enter your player name or alias.
- **Player Age** (`player_age`, number, required: false): Enter your age.
- **Player Email** (`player_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
- **Player Phone** (`player_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, please don't hesitate to ask.
* Remember to review each field carefully to ensure you understand the question before answering.
