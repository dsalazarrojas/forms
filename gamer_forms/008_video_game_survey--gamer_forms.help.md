# video_game_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to collect information about video games from users. Your responses will help us understand your preferences, habits, and opinions about games.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the video game you would like to report about.
2. Choose the platform on which you play the game (PS4, Xbox, PC, or Mobile).
3. Select the genre(s) of the game (Action, Adventure, Role-Playing, etc.).
4. If you know, enter the release date of the game (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
5. Estimate the average hours you spend playing the game per week.
6. If applicable, mention your favorite game among the ones you've played.
7. If applicable, mention your favorite character in any game you've played.
8. Provide any additional feedback or comments about the game or the form itself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Game Title** (text, required): Enter the title of the video game you would like to report about.
* **Game Platform** (select_one, required): Choose the platform on which you play the game (PS4, Xbox, PC, or Mobile).
* **Game Genre** (select_multiple, required): Select the genre(s) of the game (Action, Adventure, Role-Playing, etc.).
* **Release Date** (date, optional): If you know, enter the release date of the game (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Hours Spent per Week** (number, optional): Estimate the average hours you spend playing the game per week.
* **Favorite Game** (text, optional): If applicable, mention your favorite game among the ones you've played.
* **Favorite Character** (text, optional): If applicable, mention your favorite character in any game you've played.
* **Feedback** (note, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about the game or the form itself.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
* If you are unsure about the platform or genre, you can always ask for clarification.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your time and effort in filling out this form.
