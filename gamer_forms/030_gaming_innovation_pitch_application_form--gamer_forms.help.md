# Gaming Innovation Pitch Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for game developers to submit their game ideas for the game jam, providing a brief overview of their innovation pitch.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the Game Jam option that best represents your game idea.
2. Fill in the Game Title and Game Description fields with a concise and brief overview of your game concept.
3. Attach any relevant supporting files, such as prototypes, designs or documents.
4. Enter your Contact Details, including your name, email, and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Game Jam** (`game_jam`, `select_one`): Select the game jam that best represents your game idea. Please choose one of the available options: Game Jam 1, Game Jam 2, or Game Jam 3.
* **Game Concept** (`game_concept`, `text`): Describe your game concept in 100-200 words. This should be a brief overview of your game idea, including its core mechanics and features.
* **Supporting Files** (`supporting_files`, `note`): Attach any relevant files that support your game concept, such as prototypes, designs or documents.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `email`): Enter your contact information, including your name and email. This will help us get in touch with you if we need more information.
* **Game Title** (`game_title`, `text`): Enter a concise title for your game, that summarizes your concept.
* **Game Description** (`game_description`, `text`): Enter a brief description of your game, that includes its core features and objectives.
* **Game Jam Submission** (`game_jam_submission`, `number`): Please enter the game jam number that you are submitting your game for.
* **Game Jam Submission Date** (`game_jam_submission_date`, `date`): Enter the date you are submitting your game for the game jam.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`): Enter your name, that we can address you by.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`): Enter your email address, that we can get in touch with you.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`): Enter your phone number, that we can get in touch with you.

## Tips
* Make sure to choose the correct game jam option that represents your game idea.
* Keep your Game Concept brief and concise, 100-200 words.
* Attach relevant files to support your game concept.
* Fill in your contact information accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
