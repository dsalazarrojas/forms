# Online Game Demo Highlight Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect gamer's details, their game highlights, and their consent for featuring their gameplay on the platform. It is used to determine which game highlights to feature and ensure the gamer's privacy and consent are respected.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the "Gamer Details" section with your information as it appears on your profile.
2. In the "Highlight Uploads" section, select the game highlight you want to feature.
3. Check the boxes in the "Permissions" section to indicate which aspects of your gameplay you are willing to feature.
4. In the "Gamer Acceptance" section, choose whether you accept or decline to feature your gameplay.
5. In the "Consent" section, enter a yes or no answer to confirm your acceptance.
6. In the "Agreement" section, enter your agreement to the terms and conditions.
7. (Optional) In the "Terms and Conditions" section, review the terms and conditions of featuring your gameplay.
8. Fill out the "Gamer Consent" section with a number indicating how many game highlights you are willing to feature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gamer Details** (`gamer_details`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other details as it appears on your profile.
* **Highlight Uploads** (`highlight_uploads`, text, optional): Select the game highlight you want to feature. You can select multiple highlights if you have multiple game highlights.
* **Permissions** (`permissions`, text, required): Check the boxes to indicate which aspects of your gameplay you are willing to feature. These include gameplay, gameplay footage, and gameplay screenshots.
* **Gamer Acceptance** (`gamer_acceptance`, select_one, required): Choose whether you accept or decline to feature your gameplay.
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, required): Enter a yes or no answer to confirm your acceptance. If you answer "yes", you must enter a number indicating how many game highlights you are willing to feature.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, text, required): Enter your agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, optional): Review the terms and conditions of featuring your gameplay.
* **Gamer Consent** (`gamer_consent`, number, required): Fill out a number indicating how many game highlights you are willing to feature.
