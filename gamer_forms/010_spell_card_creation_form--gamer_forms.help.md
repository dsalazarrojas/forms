# Spell Card Creation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spell Card Creation Form is a digital tool for game developers to create and manage magical spell cards for their game worlds.

## How To Complete This Form
- Enter the **Spell Name** field with the name of the spell card. 
- Enter the **Effects** field with a description of the spell card's effects. 
- Enter the **Casting Requirements** field with any specific conditions that must be met before casting the spell. 
- Choose the **Rarity** level of the spell card (Common, Uncommon, Rare, or Legendary). 
- Enter the **Image URL** field with a link to the image of the spell card. 
- Select the effects the spell card should have from the **Effects Select** field. 
- If any effects are selected, enter additional information about those effects in the **Other Effects** field. 
- Upload an **Image** of the spell card. 
- Enter a **Description** of the spell card. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Spell Name** (`form_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the spell card.
* **Effects** (`effects_text`, `note`, required: false): Describe the effects of the spell card.
* **Casting Requirements** (`casting_requirements`, `text`, required: false): Specify any casting requirements for the spell card.
* **Rarity** (`rarity`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose the rarity level of the spell card (Common, Uncommon, Rare, or Legendary).
* **Image URL** (`image_url`, `text`, required: false): Provide the URL of the image associated with the spell card.
* **Image** (`image`, `text`, required: true): Upload an image of the spell card.
* **Effects Select** (`effects_select`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select which effects the spell card should have.
* **Other Effects** (`effects_select_other`, `text`, required: false): If any effects are selected, enter additional information about those effects.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): Enter a description of the spell card.
