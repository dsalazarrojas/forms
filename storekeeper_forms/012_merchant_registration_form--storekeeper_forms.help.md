# Merchant Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register vendors for a store or service. It gathers essential information about the vendor, including their contact details, preferences, and registration status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: Registration Type, Sponsorship Level, Meal Preperation, and Vendor Email.
2. Fill in any optional fields as needed, such as Early Bird Coupon, Representative Name, and Vendor Name.
3. Select the relevant options for Meal Preperation and Sponsorship Level.
4. Click submit to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Email** (text, required): Enter the vendor's email address.
* **Sponsorship Level** (select_multiple, required): Select one or more of the following options: Bronze, Silver, Gold, or VIP.
* **Meal Preperation** (select_multiple, required): Select one or more of the following options: veg or non-veg.
* **Registration Type** (select_one, required): Select one of the following options: early bird or standard.
* **Early Bird Coupon** (text, optional): Enter the coupon code if you have it.
* **Representative Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the representative (if any).
* **Vendor Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the vendor.
* **Vendor Phone** (text, optional): Enter the vendor's phone number.
* **Send an invite to vendors** (text, optional): Enter the invite message (if any).
