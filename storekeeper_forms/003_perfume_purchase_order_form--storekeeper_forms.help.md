# Perfume Purchase Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Perfume Purchase Order Form is a tool used by storekeepers to place orders with our company. It helps us to collect and process order information accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your storekeeper information:
	* Enter your name in the "Storekeeper Name" field.
	* Enter your phone number in the "Storekeeper Phone" field.
	* Enter your email address in the "Storekeeper Email" field.
2. Select the order date.
3. Enter your store address in the "Storekeeper Address" field.
4. Enter the product ID, name, price, and quantity.
5. Select the order status (Pending, Confirmed, or Cancelled).
6. Select the shipping status (Shipped or Pending).
7. Fill in customer information:
	* Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
	* Enter the customer's phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
	* Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field.
	* Enter the customer's address in the "Customer Address" field.
8. Enter any order comments in the "Order Comments" field.
9. Select the total price.
10. Choose the payment method (Credit/Debit Card or PayPal).
11. Enter any order notes in the "Order Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Storekeeper Name (1)**: Enter the name of the storekeeper placing the order.
    *   Type: text
    *   Required: true
*   **Storekeeper Phone (2)**: Enter the storekeeper's phone number.

    *   Required: true

    *   Type: date
    *   Required: true
