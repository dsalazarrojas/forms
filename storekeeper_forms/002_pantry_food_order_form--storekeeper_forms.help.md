# Pantry Food Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pantry Food Order Form is a data collection tool used to record and track food items stored in a pantry or inventory system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the food item ID (if provided).
2. Provide the name of the food item.
3. Specify the quantity of the food item.
4. Enter the date the food item was received.
5. Enter the store ID (if applicable).
6. Add any additional notes about the food item, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Food Item ID** (`food_item_id`, text, optional): If you have a unique ID for the food item, enter it here.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the food item.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of the food item stored.
* **Date Received** (`date_received`, date, optional): Enter the date the food item was received.
* **Store ID** (`store_id`, text, optional): If you have a store ID for the food item, enter it here.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the food item, if necessary.
