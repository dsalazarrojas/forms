# Black Friday Shopping List Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Black Friday Shopping List Form Template is a tool to help you plan your shopping strategy, organize your list, and save time on the actual shopping day.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your information, including your name, email address (if you want to save and share your list), and the date you plan to shop.
2. Set your total budget for the shopping day.
3. Choose where you will shop (online, in-store, or both).
4. List the stores you plan to visit.
5. Identify your top 3 priority items to buy.
6. Set a budget for each of your priority items.
7. If you're buying gifts, list them here.
8. If you're buying items for yourself, list them here.
9. Choose how you will pay for your items (credit card, debit card, cash, etc.).
10. List any companions you'll be shopping with.
11. Select how you will get to the stores (personal vehicle, public transit, ride share, etc.).
12. Add any additional notes you'd like to remember.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shopping List Planner** (`shopping_list_intro`): This is a note field where you can write a brief introduction to your shopping plan.
* **Shopper Name** (`shopper_name`): Enter your name as the shopper.
* **Email Address** (`email`): Enter your email address if you want to save and share your shopping list with someone.
* **Shopping Date** (`shopping_date`): Select the date you plan to shop.
* **Total Budget** (`total_budget`): Set your total budget for the shopping day.
* **Shopping Location** (`shopping_location`): Select where you will shop: online, in-store, or both.
* **Stores to Visit** (`store_list`): List all the stores you plan to visit.
* **Priority Item 1** (`priority_item_1`): Enter the item you consider most important to buy.
* **Priority Item 1 Budget** (`priority_item_1_budget`): Set the budget for your priority item 1.
* **Priority Item 2** (`priority_item_2`): Enter the item you consider second most important to buy.
* **Priority Item 2 Budget** (`priority_item_2_budget`): Set the budget for your priority item 2.
* **Priority Item 3** (`priority_item_3`): Enter the item you consider third most important to buy.
* **Priority Item 3 Budget** (`priority_item_3_budget`): Set the budget for your priority item 3.
* **Gift Items** (`gift_items`): List items you are buying as gifts.
* **Personal Items** (`personal_items`): List items you are buying for yourself.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select how you will pay: credit card, debit card, cash, store credit, etc.
* **Shopping Companions** (`shopping_companions`): List anyone shopping with you.
* **Transportation** (`transportation`): Select how you will get to the stores: personal vehicle, public transit, ride share, etc.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`): Add any other planning notes you'd like to remember.
