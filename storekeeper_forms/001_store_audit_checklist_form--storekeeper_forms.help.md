# Store Audit Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Store Audit Checklist Form is designed to collect and document essential information about a store's condition and maintenance needs. This form helps storekeepers to identify areas requiring attention and ensures accurate reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the store's name in the **store name** field.
2. Provide the store's address in the **store address** field.
3. Enter the store's contact information in the **store contact info** field.
4. Select the **maintenance needs** of the store from the provided options.
5. Add any comments or observations about the store's condition in the **comments** field (optional).
6. Enter the date of the audit in the **date** field.
7. Enter the time of the audit in the **time** field.
8. Select the store's **store status** by choosing one or multiple options.
9. Identify the type of store by selecting one option from the **store type** field.
10. Optionally, provide details about the store's condition in the **store condition** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store name** (`store name`, text, required): Enter the name of the store being audited.
* **Store address** (`store address`, text, required): Enter the store's physical address.
* **Store contact info** (`store contact info`, text, required): Enter the store's contact information, including phone number and email.
* **Maintenance needs** (`maintenance needs`, select_one, required): Select the specific maintenance needs of the store, such as repairs or replacement of equipment.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any comments or observations about the store's condition.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the audit.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of the audit.
* **Store status** (`store status`, select_multiple, required): Select one or multiple options to describe the store's current status, such as "operational" or "closed".
* **Store type** (`store type`, select_one, required): Select the type of store, such as "physical" or "online".
* **Store condition** (`store condition`, note, optional): Add any additional information about the store's condition, if necessary.
* **Store type** (id 10) should be renamed to **Store Category** for clarity
