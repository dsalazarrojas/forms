# Supermarket Product Pricing Tracker Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track product pricing data for a supermarket. It helps store managers and administrators to keep track of products, their prices, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Product Id** of the product you want to track, which is a unique identifier for the product in your store.
2. Enter the **Store Id**, which is also a unique identifier for the store that carries the product.
3. Enter the price of the product using the **Price** field.
4. Enter the **Shelf Id**, which is a reference to the shelf where the product is stored.
5. If desired, add more details about the product such as its **Product Name**, **Product Category**, and **Store**.
6. If you need to track changes in the price, enter the **Price Date** and **Price Time** when the price change occurred.
7. Enter the **User Id** of the person who made the price change.
8. Enter the reason for the price change in the **Price Change Reason** field.
9. You can also upload a **Product Image** and **Store Image** to provide more visual information about the product.
10. Finally, review and confirm the entered data before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Id (Required)**: A unique identifier for the product.
* **Store Id (Required)**: A unique identifier for the store that carries the product.
* **Price (Required)**: The current price of the product.
* **Shelf Id (Required)**: The reference to the shelf where the product is stored.
* **Product Name (Optional)**: The name of the product.
* **Product Category (Optional)**: The category of the product.
* **Store (Optional)**: The store where the product is sold.
* **Price Date (Optional)**: The date when the price change occurred.
* **Price Time (Optional)**: The time when the price change occurred.
* **User Id (Optional)**: The person who made the price change.
* **Created By (Optional)**: The person who created the form entry.
* **Updated By (Optional)**: The person who updated the form entry.
* **Product Code (Optional)**: A code for the product.
* **Store Code (Optional)**: A code for the store.
* **Price Change (Optional)**: The amount of price change.
* **Price Change Type (Optional)**: The type of price change.
* **Price Change Reason (Optional)**: The reason for the price change.
* **Price Change User (Optional)**: The person who made the price change.
* **Product Image (Optional)**: An image of the product.
* **Store Image (Optional)**: An image of the store.
* **Store Image Url (Optional)**: The URL of the store image.
* **Store Image Url Type (Optional)**: The type of store image URL.
* **Price Image (Optional)**: An image of the price.
* **Price Image Url (Optional)**: The URL of the price image.
* **Price Image Url (Optional)**: This field appears to be a duplicate, please ignore it.
