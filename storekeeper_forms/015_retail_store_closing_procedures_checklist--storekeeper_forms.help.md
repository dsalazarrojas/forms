# retail_store_closing_procedures_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The retail_store_closing_procedures_checklist form is used to document the status of a store's closing procedures. It helps store managers or staff ensure that all tasks are completed before leaving the store at the end of a shift.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Choose the current status of the store opening from the `store opening status` field.
2. Indicate whether you have completed all closing tasks for the shift by answering `yes` or `no` in the `closing tasks completed` field.
3. Sign and date the form in the `manager signature` field.
4. List the names of employees who have signed the form in the `employee signatures` field.
5. Enter the time the store was closed and the time it was opened in the `store closing time` and `store opening time` fields, respectively.
6. Add any additional notes about the store's opening time in the `store opening time note` field.
7. Add any additional notes about the store's closing status in the `store opening status note` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **store opening status**: Select the current status of the store opening. Choose from the options: `open`, `closing`, or `closed`.
* **closing tasks completed**: Indicate whether you have completed all closing tasks for the shift by selecting `yes` or `no`.
* **manager signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm that you have reviewed and confirmed the store's closing procedures.
* **employee signatures**: List the names of employees who have signed the form to confirm that they have reviewed and completed closing tasks.
* **store closing time**: Enter the time the store was closed.
* **store opening time**: Enter the time the store was opened.
* **store opening time note**: Add any additional notes about the store's opening time.
* **store opening status note**: Add any additional notes about the store's closing status.
