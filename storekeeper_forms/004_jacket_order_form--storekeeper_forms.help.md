# Jacket Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Jacket Order Form is used by storekeepers to record orders from customers, capturing essential information about each sale.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's name.
2. Enter the quantity of items ordered.
3. Record the price of the order.
4. Enter any additional comments about the order.
5. Choose the payment method used.
6. Enter the customer's email address.
7. Enter the customer's phone number.
8. Enter the storekeeper's name.
9. Record any notes for the storekeeper.
10. Choose the sale method.
11. Select the order status.
12. Enter any customer notes.
13. Record the sale date and time.
14. Enter the employee assigned to handle the order.
15. Enter the customer's address details (city, state, zip, country).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the customer's full name.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Record the number of items ordered.
* **Price** (`price`, number, optional): Enter the total price of the order.
* **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, note, optional): Add any comments about the order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose the method used for payment (e.g., cash, credit card, etc.).
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Storekeeper** (`storekeeper`, text, required): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Storekeeper Notes** (`storekeeper_notes`, note, optional): Add any notes for the storekeeper.
* **Sale Method** (`sale_method`, select_one, required): Choose the method used for sale (e.g., in-store, online, etc.).
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the order (e.g., pending, shipped, etc.).
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, note, optional): Add any notes about the customer.
* **Sale Date** (`sale_date`, date, optional): Record the date of the sale.
* **Sale Time** (`sale_time`, time, optional): Record the time of the sale.
* **Assigned** (`assigned`, text, required): Enter the employee assigned to handle the order.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee handling the order.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, required): Enter the customer's address.
* **Customer City** (`customer_city`, text, required): Enter the customer's city.
* **Customer State** (`customer_state`, text, required): Enter the customer's state or region.
* **Customer Zip** (`customer_zip`, text, required): Enter the customer's zip code.
* **Customer Country** (`customer_country`, text, required): Enter the customer's country.
* **Storekeeper Notes** (`storekeeper_notes`, note, optional): Add any notes for the storekeeper.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Record the date of the order.
* **Storekeeper** (`storekeeper`, text, required): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Storekeeper Phone** (`storekeeper_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.

## Tips

* Be thorough when filling out the form to avoid errors.
* Double-check the customer's information for accuracy.
* Use the form to improve communication between storekeepers and customers.
* Store the completed form for reference and record-keeping purposes.
