# Product List Order Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a product list order, collecting relevant information from the end user to facilitate the ordering process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the first page, enter a list of products you want to order into the "Product List" field.
2. Enter the subtotal of your order in the "Subtotal" field.
3. If applicable, select the tax rate from the "Tax" field.
4. Enter your shipping address or other shipping information into the "Shipping" field.

* **Product List** (`product_list`, `text`, required): Enter a list of products you want to order. This field is for free-form text entry.

* **Tax** (`tax`, `select_one`, required): Select the tax rate applied to your order. You can choose between 5.13% and 6.13%.
