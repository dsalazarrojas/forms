# Catalog Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Catalog Request Form is used to request a catalog for various purposes, such as personal or business use. It is intended for users who need to obtain a catalog and do not already have one.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name and company name (if applicable) in the respective fields.
2.  Enter your email address and phone number so we can contact you with more information.
3.  Provide your mailing address so we can send the catalog to you.
4.  Specify your city and state or province where you are located.
5.  Enter your ZIP or postal code to ensure accurate delivery.
6.  Choose the country where you are located.
7.  Select the type of catalog you are interested in.
8.  Specify the quantity of catalog copies you need.
9.  Indicate the purpose of your request.
10.  Choose the industry related to your work or business.
11.  Specify your preferred format for the catalog.
12.  Select the urgency of your request.
13.  If you have any additional requests, please specify them in the field provided.
14.  Finally, indicate how you heard about us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Requester Name** (`requester_name`, **text**, required): Enter your full name.
*   **Company Name** (`company_name`, **text**, optional): Enter your company or organization name if applicable.
*   **Email Address** (`email`, **email**, required): Enter your email address where we can contact you.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone`, **text**, required): Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Mailing Address** (`address`, **text**, required): Provide the address where we should send the catalog.
*   **City** (`city`, **text**, required): Enter your city name.
*   **State or Province** (`state`, **text**, required): Enter your state or province name.
*   **ZIP or Postal Code** (`zip_code`, **text**, required): Enter your ZIP or postal code.
*   **Country** (`country`, **select_one, required): Choose your country of residence.
*   **Catalog Type Requested** (`catalog_type`, **select_one, required): Choose the type of catalog you are interested in.
*   **Quantity Needed** (`quantity`, **number, required): Specify how many catalog copies you need.
*   **Purpose of Request** (`purpose`, **select_one, required): Indicate the purpose of your request.
*   **Industry** (`industry`, **select_one, optional): Choose the industry related to your work or business.
*   **Preferred Format** (`preferred_format`, **select_one, required): Choose your preferred format for the catalog.
*   **Urgency** (`urgency`, **select_one, optional): Select the urgency of your request.
*   **Additional Requests** (`additional_requests`, **text, optional): If you have any special requirements, specify them here.
*   **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_heard`, **select_one, optional): Indicate how you heard about us.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, **text, optional): Enter any additional comments or requests you have.
