# gardening_shop_visitation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record visitation details for customers at a gardening shop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the `user_name` field.
2. Provide your email address in the `email` field.
3. Enter your phone number in the `phone` field.
4. Choose the date of your visit from the calendar in the `visit_date` field.
5. Select the time of your visit from the time options in the `visit_time` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name to associate it with your visit details.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you back.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for future reference.
* **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, date, required/optional): Choose the date of your visit from the calendar.
* **Visit Time** (`visit_time`, time, required/optional): Select the time of your visit from the available options.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately to maintain data quality.
* If you have any issues or need assistance, do not hesitate to contact us.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please let us know.
