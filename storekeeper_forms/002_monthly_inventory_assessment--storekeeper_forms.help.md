# Monthly Inventory Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Monthly Inventory Assessment form is used to track and record the stock levels of different locations within the warehouse on a monthly basis. This form is essential for maintaining accurate and up-to-date inventory records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the locations for which you want to report stock levels.
2. Enter the current stock counts for each location.
3. Record any issues or discrepancies you have encountered.
4. Specify the date of the stock count issuance.
5. Enter the name of the warehouse.
6. Enter the name of the item(s) being counted.
7. Enter the quantity of stock being reported.
8. Provide any remarks or comments regarding the stock count.
8. Enter the stock count for the warehouse.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **stock_counts** (`Stock Counts`, `number`, optional): Enter the current stock count for the selected location.
* **locations** (`Locations`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the locations for which you want to report stock levels. Choose from the list of available locations provided in the dropdown menu.
* **issues** (`Issues`, `text`, optional): If you encounter any issues or discrepancies during the stock count, use this field to record them.
* **stock_count_issuance** (`Stock Count Issuance Date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when the stock count was conducted or will be conducted.
* **warehouse_name** (`Warehouse Name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the warehouse.
* **item_name** (`Item Name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the item(s) being counted.
* **quantity** (`Quantity`, `number`, optional): Enter the quantity of stock being reported.
* **issued_by** (`Issued By`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the person who conducted the stock count or issued the stock.
* **remarks** (`Remarks`, `note`, optional): Provide any remarks or comments regarding the stock count.
* **stock_count_warehouse** (`Stock Count Warehouse`, `text`, optional): Enter the stock count for the warehouse.
