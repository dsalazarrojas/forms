# Sports Apparel Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Apparel Order Form is a form designed for customers to provide information about their order of sports apparel. The form is used to collect customer details, order information, and payment details for processing the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer details, including name and contact information.
2. Provide information about your order, such as the type of sports apparel you are ordering.
3. Add any additional order notes if necessary.
4. Enter your payment information, including payment method and amount.
5. Enter your email address and phone number.
6. Select the order status and shipping information for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, optional): Enter your name and contact information for our records.
* **Order Information** (`order_info`, text, optional): Provide information about your order, such as the type of sports apparel you are ordering.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your order if necessary.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, optional): Enter your payment method and amount for processing the order.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication and order confirmation.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication and order updates.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your order, either "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Shipping Information** (`shipping_info`, text, optional): Enter your shipping information, including address and any other relevant details.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly to avoid delays in processing your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our customer support team for assistance.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
