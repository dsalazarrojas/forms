# Black Friday Inventory Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Black Friday Inventory Checklist is designed to help store managers and staff members ensure that their store is ready for the Black Friday sales event by verifying stock levels, preparing display areas, and updating inventory management systems. This checklist helps identify areas of concern and ensures that all necessary steps are taken to provide a smooth and successful shopping experience for customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the store location from the list of options.
2. Enter the name of the store manager responsible for this checklist.
3. Choose the date when the checklist was completed.
4. For each section, select the status of your inventory management:
	* Complete: All stock has been counted and verified.
	* In Progress: Stock is being counted and verified but not yet complete.
	* Not Started: Stock counting and verification has not begun.
	* Not Applicable: Not applicable to this section (e.g., not a Black Friday section).
5. Identify any low stock items that need restocking before Black Friday.
6. Confirm that all necessary reorder requests have been submitted to suppliers.
7. Check that the warehouse space is clear and prepared for incoming stock.
8. Verify that all Black Friday prices have been correctly labeled.
9. Confirm that all display areas are prepared for the event.
10. Check that high-value items are secured with security tags.
11. Test and confirm that the point of sale systems are working correctly.
12. Verify that adequate staffing is in place for inventory management.
13. Confirm that backup suppliers have been contacted (if necessary).
14. Ensure that inventory software is up-to-date with the latest version.
15. Enter any additional notes or concerns.

	+ Not Started: Electronics stock counting and verification has not begun.

	+ Complete: All clothing stock has been counted and verified.

* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional notes or concerns.
* **Manager Signature**: Enter the store manager's digital signature or typed name.
