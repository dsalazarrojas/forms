# Store Visit Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Store Visit Report" form is used to record and document visits to stores. It captures key details about the visit, including the date, time, employee name, store location, and specific hours of operation for each day of the week.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the **Store Visit Date**: The date you visited the store. 
2. Enter the **Store Visit Time**: The time of your visit.
3. Enter **Employee Name**: The name of the storekeeper who assisted you during your visit.
4. Enter **Store Location**: The address or name of the store.
5. Enter **Store Hours Open**: The hours the store is open on Monday. If you don't know the hours, enter 'Not Applicable' or leave blank.
6. Enter **Store Hours Close**: The hours the store closes on Monday. If you don't know the hours, enter 'Not Applicable' or leave blank.
7. Enter **Store Hours Open (Monday)**, **Store Hours Open (Tuesday)**, **Store Hours Open (Wednesday)**, **Store Hours Open (Thursday)**, **Store Hours Open (Friday)**, **Store Hours Open (Saturday)**, **Store Hours Open (Sunday)**: The hours the store is open on each day of the week. If you don't know the hours, enter 'Not Applicable' or leave blank.
8. Enter **Store Hours Close (Monday)**, **Store Hours Close (Tuesday)**, **Store Hours Close (Wednesday)**, **Store Hours Close (Thursday)**, **Store Hours Close (Friday)**, **Store Hours Close (Saturday)**, **Store Hours Close (Sunday)**: The hours the store closes on each day of the week. If you don't know the hours, enter 'Not Applicable' or leave blank.
9. Enter **Store Visit Notes**: Any additional comments or notes you took during your visit.
10. Enter **Storekeeper Name**: The name of the storekeeper who assisted you during your visit.
11. Enter **Storekeeper Email**: The email address of the storekeeper. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Store Visit Date** (`store_visit_date`, `text`, required): Enter the date you visited the store.
* **Store Visit Time** (`store_visit_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of your visit.

* **Store Location** (`store_location`, `text`, required): Enter the address or name of the store.

* **Store Hours Open (Tuesday)** (`store_hours_open_tuesday`, `text`, optional): Enter the hours the store is open on Tuesday. If not known, enter 'Not Applicable'.
* **Store Hours Open (Wednesday)** (`store_hours_open_wednesday`, `text`, optional): Enter the hours the store is open on Wednesday. If not known, enter 'Not Applicable'.
* **Store Hours Open (Thursday)** (`store_hours_open_thursday`, `text`, optional): Enter the hours the store is open on Thursday. If not known, enter 'Not Applicable'.
* **Store Hours Open (Friday)** (`store_hours_open_friday`, `text`, optional): Enter the hours the store is open on Friday. If not known, enter 'Not Applicable'.
* **Store Hours Open (Saturday)** (`store_hours_open_saturday`, `text`, optional): Enter the hours the store is open on Saturday. If not known, enter 'Not Applicable'.
* **Store Hours Open (Sunday)** (`store_hours_open_sunday`, `text`, optional): Enter the hours the store is open on Sunday. If not known, enter 'Not Applicable'.
* **Store Hours Close (Monday)** (`store_hours_close_monday`, `time`, optional): Enter the hours the store closes on Monday. If not known, enter 'Not Applicable'.
* **Store Hours Close (Tuesday)** (`store_hours_close_tuesday`, `time`, optional): Enter the hours the store closes on Tuesday. If not known, enter 'Not Applicable'.
* **Store Hours Close (Wednesday)** (`store_hours_close_wednesday`, `time`, optional): Enter the hours the store closes on Wednesday. If not known, enter 'Not Applicable'.
* **Store Hours Close (Thursday)** (`store_hours_close_thursday`, `time`, optional): Enter the hours the store closes on Thursday. If not known, enter 'Not Applicable'.
* **Store Hours Close (Friday)** (`store_hours_close_friday`, `time`, optional): Enter the hours the store closes on Friday. If not known, enter 'Not Applicable'.
* **Store Hours Close (Saturday)** (`store_hours_close_saturday`, `time`, optional): Enter the hours the store closes on Saturday. If not known, enter 'Not Applicable'.
* **Store Hours Close (Sunday)** (`store_hours_close_sunday`, `time`, optional): Enter the hours the store closes on Sunday. If not known, enter 'Not Applicable'.
* **Store Visit Notes** (`store_visit_notes`, `note`, required): Enter any additional comments or notes you took during your visit.
* **Storekeeper Name** (`storekeeper_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the storekeeper who assisted you during your visit.
* **Storekeeper Email** (`storekeeper_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the storekeeper.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have accurate and up-to-date information about the store's hours of operation before filling out this form.
* If you are not sure or do not know the store's hours of operation for a particular day, enter 'Not Applicable'.
