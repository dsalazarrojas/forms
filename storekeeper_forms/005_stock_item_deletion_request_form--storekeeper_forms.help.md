# Stock Item Deletion Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stock Item Deletion Request Form is a form used by storekeepers to request the deletion of stock items from the inventory management system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the name of the stock item to be deleted in the "Stock Item Name" field.
2.  Enter the quantity of the stock item to be deleted in the "Stock Item Quantity" field.
3.  Provide the reason for deleting the stock item in the "Reason for Deletion" field.
4.  Select the storekeeper who will be responsible for the deletion from the "Select Storekeeper" dropdown list.
5.  Enter any additional comments or notes about the deletion in the "Storekeeper Form Comment" field.
6.  Enter the date of the request in the "Date of Request" field.
7.  Select the status of the form from the "Storekeeper Form Status" dropdown list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Stock Item Name** (`stock_item_name`): Enter the name of the stock item you want to delete from the inventory.
* **Stock Item Quantity** (`stock_item_quantity`): Enter the quantity of the stock item to be deleted.
* **Reason for Deletion** (`reason_for_deletion`): Provide a brief explanation for why you are requesting the deletion of the stock item.
* **Select Storekeeper** (`select_storekeeper_form`): Select the storekeeper who will be responsible for the deletion. You can choose one or multiple storekeepers from the list.
* **Storekeeper Form Comment** (`storekeeper_form_comment`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the deletion.
* **Date of Request** (`date_of_request`): Enter the date of the request for deletion.
* **Storekeeper Form Status** (`storekeeper_form_status`): Select the status of the form, typically "New", "In Progress", or "Completed".
