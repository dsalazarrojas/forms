# Food Storage Inventory Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to track food storage inventory, which includes the quantity of food items available and their purchase history.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Start" button to begin filling out the form.
2. In the "Food Item" field, enter the name of the food item being tracked.
3. In the "Quantity On Hand" field, enter the current quantity of the food item available.
4. In the "Quantity Needed" field, enter the quantity of the food item needed to be purchased or ordered.
5. In the "Purchases" field, enter any relevant purchase information or notes about the food item.
6. In the "Food Storage Form Notes" field, enter any additional notes or comments about the food item or its storage.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Food Item** (`id: 1`): Enter the name of the food item being tracked.
* **Quantity On Hand** (`id: 2`): Enter the current quantity of the food item available. This number should be a whole number and does not need to include any units (e.g., 10 is the correct entry for 10 items).
* **Quantity Needed** (`id: 3`): Enter the quantity of the food item needed to be purchased or ordered. Like the "Quantity On Hand" field, this should be a whole number.
* **Purchases** (`id: 4`): Enter any relevant purchase information or notes about the food item, such as the date and time of the purchase or any notable details about the purchase.
* **Food Storage Form Notes** (`id: 5`): Enter any additional notes or comments about the food item or its storage. This can be used to record any relevant information that does not fit in the other fields.
