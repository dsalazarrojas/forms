# Inventory Adjustment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Inventory Adjustment Form is used to update the inventory levels of items in the system. This form allows you to add, remove, or modify inventory quantities, as well as record adjustments and reasons for changes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the current inventory levels before making any changes.
2. Select the item you wish to adjust.
3. Enter the new inventory level (quantity).
4. Choose the reason for the adjustment.
5. Add any relevant notes or comments.
6. Review and submit the form for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_1_label** (Text, Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the adjustment (e.g., "Received new shipment" or "Moved to different location").
* **form_2_label** (Text, Number, Optional): Enter the new inventory quantity.
* **form_3_label** (Text, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the reason for the adjustment (e.g., "Received Shipment", "Moved to Different Location", "Destroyed or Damaged", etc.).
* **form_4_label** (Text, Date, Optional): Enter the date of the adjustment (if applicable).
* **form_5_label** (Text, Time, Optional): Enter the time of the adjustment (if applicable).
* **form_6_label** (Text, Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the adjustment.
* **form_7_label** (Text, Email, Optional): Enter your email address for tracking and audit purposes (if applicable).
* **form_8_label** (Text, Text, Optional): Enter the current inventory level of the item.

Note: Fields are not required, but entering accurate information is crucial for maintaining accurate inventory records. Ensure that you enter the correct information for the selected item and reason for adjustment.
