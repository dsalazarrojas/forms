# Stationary Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stationary Payment Form is used to record payments made by storekeepers for stationary products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have a legitimate reason to fill out this form. This form is not required for every transaction, so only fill it out when necessary.
2. Fill in the following fields:
	* **storekeeper_forms**: Enter any additional information or comments about the storekeeper's forms or transactions (text field).
	* **stationary_products**: Select all the stationary products that were purchased or paid for (multiple select field).
	* **amount**: Enter the total amount paid for the stationary products (number field).
	* **payment_date**: Enter the date the payment was made (date field).
	* **payment_time**: Enter the time the payment was made (time field).
	* **store_name**: Enter the name of the store (text field).
	* **store_address**: Enter the address of the store (text field).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Storekeeper Forms
* **storekeeper_forms**: (`storekeeper_forms`, text, optional) - Enter any additional information or comments about the storekeeper's forms or transactions.

### Stationary Products
* **stationary_products**: (`stationary_products`, select_multiple, optional) - Select all the stationary products that were purchased or paid for.

### Amount
* **amount**: (`amount`, number, optional) - Enter the total amount paid for the stationary products.

### Payment Date
* **payment_date**: (`payment_date`, date, optional) - Enter the date the payment was made.

### Payment Time
* **payment_time**: (`payment_time`, time, optional) - Enter the time the payment was made.

### Store Name
* **store_name**: (`store_name`, text, optional) - Enter the name of the store.

### Store Address
* **store_address**: (`store_address`, text, optional) - Enter the address of the store.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out only the fields that are relevant to your transaction.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
