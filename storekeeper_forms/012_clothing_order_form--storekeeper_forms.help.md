## Purpose
This form is for customers to provide their personal and order details to place a clothing order. It collects customer information, order items, and shipping options to ensure prompt and accurate order fulfillment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number** in the required fields.
2.  Select the **Item Category**, **Item Name/Description**, **Size**, and **Color** for each order item.
3.  Specify the **Quantity** and **Unit Price** for each item.
4.  Choose your preferred **Delivery Method** (Standard, Express, Overnight, or Local Pickup).
5.  Optionally, select **Gift Wrapping** if required.
6.  Enter any **Special Instructions or Notes** if necessary.
