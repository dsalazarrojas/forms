# Liquor Inventory Information Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Liquor Inventory Information Record Form is designed to help storekeepers track and record information about liquor sales and purchases. This form will help you keep accurate records of your inventory, allowing you to make informed decisions about ordering more supplies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form for each sale or purchase made in your store.
2. Ensure you fill out all required fields (indicated by *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sales Amount** (`sales_amount`, number, false): Enter the total amount of liquor sold.
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, date, false): Enter the date the liquor was purchased.
* **Purchase Cost** (`purchase_cost`, number, false): Enter the total cost of the liquor purchased.
* **Store Name** (`store_name`, text, false): Enter the name of your store.
* **Sales Date** (`sales_date`, date, false): Enter the date the liquor was sold.
* **Purchase Amount** (`purchase_amount`, number, false): Enter the total amount of liquor purchased.
* **Sales Person Name** (`sales_person_name`, text, false): Enter the name of the sales person responsible for the sale.
* **Store Address** (`store_address`, text, false): Enter the address of your store.
* **Sales Total** (`sales_total`, number, false): Enter the total amount of liquor sold on this day.
* **Store Phone** (`store_phone`, text, false): Enter the phone number of your store.

Note: If a field has no required attribute in the YAML, it is considered optional.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to fill out each field carefully and accurately, as this information will help you keep track of your liquor sales and purchases.
* Use this form for every sale and purchase made in your store.
* If you are unsure about any information, ask your store manager for clarification.
* Review this form regularly to ensure your inventory is up-to-date and accurate.
* Keep this form in a safe and secure location to prevent tampering or loss.
