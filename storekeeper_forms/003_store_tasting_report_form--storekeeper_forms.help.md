# Store Tasting Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Store Tasting Report Form is designed to gather feedback from store employees on their interactions with customers during tasting events. This information helps improve customer service and inform store operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions on each page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Store Tasting Event** (`store_tasting_event`, text, not required)
Enter a brief description of the tasting event, such as "Wine and cheese tasting on Friday evening".

### **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, not required)
Enter the date of the tasting event.

### **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, not required)
Enter the time of the tasting event.

### **Store Tasting Event Type** (`store_tasting_event_type`, select_one, not required)
Select the type of tasting event that occurred:

*   Store Manager
*   Staff

### **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, not required)
Enter the customer's name.

### **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, not required)
Enter the customer's email address.

### **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, not required)
Enter the customer's phone number.

### **Store Tasting Note** (`store_tasting_note`, note, not required)
Enter any relevant notes on the customer's experience.

### **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`, text, not required)
Enter any additional comments or feedback from the customer.

### Tips
*   Please make sure to enter accurate information to ensure the form is complete and helpful.
*   You can add any additional comments or feedback that will assist the store in providing better customer service.
*   The form is intended for internal use only, so please do not share it with customers.
