# Supermarkets Cabinet Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience at your store. Your input will help us understand our strengths and weaknesses, enabling us to improve our service and provide a better experience for you and our other customers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly, and to the best of your knowledge.
2. Select the most suitable option for each question from the provided options.
3. You can skip any question that doesn't apply to your experience.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the 'Do you have any suggestions?' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Layout (1)** (`Number`, `Number`, Not Required): Rate the store's layout. This helps us understand how easy or difficult it is to navigate our store.
* **Organization (2)** (`Number`, `Number`, Not Required): Rate the store's organization. This includes how well our products are arranged and easy to find.
* **Product (3)** (`Number`, `Number`, Not Required): Rate the store's product selection. This helps us understand how appealing and useful our products are to you.
* **Shelf Stock (4)** (`Select One`, `Select One`, Not Required): How full are the shelves? Select one of the options: A lot, Moderately full, A little full, or Barely any items left.
* **Product Offers (5)** (`Select Multiple`, `Select Multiple`, Not Required): Which products do you usually look for while visiting our store? Select all that apply: Beer, Canned Goods, Baking Supplies, Dairy, Snacks, Meat, Frozen Foods, Bread, Nonperishable Goods, Produce, Seafood, Health and Beauty, Baby Products, or Household Goods.
* **Store Experience (6)** (`Select One`, `Select One`, Not Required): How would you rate our store experience? Choose one of the options: Exceptional, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Customer Service (7)** (`Select Multiple`, `Select Multiple`, Not Required): How would you rate our customer service? Select all that apply: Friendly, Unfriendly, or Neutral.
* **Communication (8)** (`Select One`, `Select One`, Not Required): How clear is our communication? Choose one of the options: Very clear, Mostly clear, or Not very clear.
* **Store Cleanliness (9)** (`Select One`, `Select One`, Not Required): How clean is our store? Choose one of the options: Very clean, Moderately clean, or Not very clean.
* **Product Quality (10)** (`Select One`, `Select One`, Not Required): What's the quality of our products? Choose one of the options: High, Medium, or Low.
* **Store Location (11)** (`Select One`, `Select One`, Not Required): Is our store easily accessible? Choose one of the options: True or False.
* **Store Hours (12)** (`Select One`, `Select One`, Not Required): How do you rate our store hours? Choose one of the options: Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Store Staff (13)** (`Select Multiple`, `Select Multiple`, Not Required): How helpful are our store staff? Select all that apply: Very helpful, Somewhat helpful, or Not very helpful.
* **Store Price (14)** (`Select One`, `Select One`, Not Required): How reasonable are our prices? Choose one of the options: Reasonable, Not reasonable.
* **Store Value (15)** (`Select One`, `Select One`, Not Required): What's the value of our store? Choose one of the options: High, Moderate, or Low.
* **Overall Experience (16)** (`Select One`, `Select One`, Not Required): What's your overall experience at our store? Choose one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Do you have any suggestions? (17)** (`Note`, `Note`, Not Required): If you have any ideas on how we can improve, please share them here.
* **Can we contact you? (18)** (`Select One`, `Select One`, Not Required): Are you willing to be contacted about your feedback? Choose one of the options: True or False.
