# Uniform Allocation Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Uniform Allocation Record Form is used to track the allocation of uniforms to staff members, ensuring accurate inventory management and preventing over-issuing of uniforms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the staff member to whom the uniform was issued from the "Issued to" dropdown menu.
2. Enter the date the uniform was issued using the calendar feature.
3. Enter the uniform item number, quantity issued, and quantity returned.
4. If the uniform was returned, select the reason for return from the dropdown menu.
5. Enter any additional comments or notes about the transaction.
6. Enter the contact information for the storekeeper (email and phone number).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Issued to** (`issued_to`, `select_one`, required): Select the staff member to whom the uniform was issued.
* **Date issued** (`date_issued`, `date`, required): Enter the date the uniform was issued using the calendar feature.
* **Uniform Item Number** (`uniform_item_number`, `number`, required): Enter the unique identifier for the uniform item.
* **Quantity Issued** (`quantity_issued`, `number`, required): Enter the number of uniforms issued to the staff member.
* **Quantity Returned** (`quantity_returned`, `number`, required): Enter the number of uniforms returned from the staff member.
* **Reason Returned** (`reason_returned`, `select_one`, required): Select the reason for returning the uniform from the dropdown menu.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the transaction.
* **Email Contact** (`email_contact`, `email`, optional): Enter the contact email of the storekeeper.
* **Phone Contact** (`phone_contact`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the storekeeper.
* **Date Received** (`date_received`, `date`, required): Enter the date the uniform was received from the staff member.
* **Storekeeper Name** (`storekeeper_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the storekeeper responsible for the transaction.
