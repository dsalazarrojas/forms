# Black Friday Product Reservation Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Black Friday Product Reservation Form Template is used to reserve products on Black Friday sales. It collects customer information and the details of the product to be reserved.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the customer's full name and contact email.
2. Enter the customer's contact phone number (optional).
3. Enter the product's SKU (Internal Stock Keeping Unit).
4. Enter the product name.
5. Enter the quantity to reserve.
6. Select the preferred pickup date.
7. Choose the store branch for pickup.
8. Add any special instructions for fulfillment.
9. Submit the form for review and confirmation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer name**: Enter the customer's full name for reservation purposes.
* **Contact email**: Enter the customer's email for confirmation and communication.
* **Contact phone**: Enter the customer's phone number for optional contact.
* **Product SKU**: Enter the internal stock keeping unit for the product.
* **Product name**: Enter the name of the product to be reserved.
* **Quantity to reserve**: Enter the number of products to reserve.
* **Preferred pickup date**: Choose a date for the customer to pick up the product.
* **Store branch**: Select the location where the product will be picked up.
* **Special instructions**: Enter any relevant notes for product fulfillment.
