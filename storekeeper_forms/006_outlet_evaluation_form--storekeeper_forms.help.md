# Outlet Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Outlet Evaluation Form is designed to collect information about storekeepers' outlets, including their address, contact details, social media links, and ratings.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by navigating through the form's pages, which are designed to be completed in a logical and easy-to-follow sequence.
2. Fill out each page carefully, ensuring you provide accurate and complete information about your outlet.
3. If you're unsure about any field or have questions, refer to the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section below for more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Storekeeper Outlets List** (`storekeeper_outlets_list`, select_one): Choose the type of outlet you represent (e.g., storekeeper_outlets_list).
* **Storekeeper Comments** (`storekeeper_comments`, note): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your outlet.
* **Storekeeper Outlet Address** (`storekeeper_outlet_address`, text): Enter your outlet's address.
* **Storekeeper Outlet Name** (`storekeeper_outlet_name`, text): Enter your outlet's name.
* **Storekeeper Contact** (`storekeeper_contact`, email): Enter your email address or a contact number for your outlet.
* **Storekeeper Phone** (`storekeeper_phone`, text): Enter your phone number.
* **Storekeeper Outlet Email** (`storekeeper_outlet_email`, email): Enter your outlet's email address.
* **Storekeeper Outlet Website** (`storekeeper_outlet_website`, text): Enter your outlet's website URL.
* **Storekeeper Outlet Facebook** (`storekeeper_outlet_facebook`, text): Enter your outlet's Facebook link.
* **Storekeeper Outlet Instagram** (`storekeeper_outlet_instagram`, text): Enter your outlet's Instagram link.
* **Storekeeper Outlet Twitter** (`storekeeper_outlet_twitter`, text): Enter your outlet's Twitter link.
* **Storekeeper Outlet YouTube** (`storekeeper_outlet_youtube`, text): Enter your outlet's YouTube link.
* **Storekeeper Outlet Other Social Media** (`storekeeper_outlet_other_social_media`, text): Enter any other social media links that your outlet has.
* **Storekeeper Outlet Rating** (`storekeeper_outlet_rating`, number): Rate your outlet's performance (e.g., 1-5).
* **Storekeeper Outlet Comments** (`storekeeper_outlet_comments`, note): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your outlet.
* **Storekeeper Outlet Facebook Comments** (`storekeeper_outlet_facebook_comments`, note): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your Facebook page.
* **Storekeeper Outlet Instagram Comments** (`storekeeper_outlet_instagram_comments`, note): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your Instagram page.
* **Storekeeper Outlet Twitter Comments** (`storekeeper_outlet_twitter_comments`, note): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your Twitter page.
* **Storekeeper Outlet YouTube Comments** (`storekeeper_outlet_youtube_comments`, note): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your YouTube channel.
* **Storekeeper Outlet Other Social Media Comments** (`storekeeper_outlet_other_social_media_comments`, note): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your other social media pages.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text): Enter the tool assigned to your outlet (if any).

Note: Fields with a note type are for providing additional comments or feedback, while fields with a select_one type are for selecting options from a list.
