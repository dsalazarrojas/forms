# Inventory Form - Help Guide

To provide a clear and concise guide for the "storekeeper_forms" team, we'll be explaining the purpose and completion process of the "Inventory Form" while avoiding duplication and ensuring relevance.

The purpose of the Inventory Form is to collect data on products within the inventory, including their status, quantity, and description. This information is crucial for the "storekeeper_forms" team to maintain accurate records and make informed decisions.

## Purpose
The Inventory Form is a simple form designed to gather essential information about the items within the inventory. It's meant to be straightforward and easy to fill out.

## How To Complete This Form
### Page 1: Inventory Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you understand each field's purpose.
2. Scroll down to page 2: "products".
3. Select "Yes" or "No" for each product listed, indicating its current status in the inventory.
4. If a product is present, proceed to provide the following details:
	* "product_name" (text field): Enter the product's name.
	* "quantity" (number field): Enter the product's quantity within the inventory.
	* "location" (text field): Indicate the product's location within the inventory.
	* "product_location" (select one option): Choose whether the product is located in the same warehouse or not.
	* "product_amount" (number field): Enter the product's amount within the specified location.
	* "product_description" (text field): Provide a brief description of the product.
	* "storekeeper_forms" (note field): Add any additional notes about the product.

### Tips
- Double-check your responses for accuracy, as incorrect information may lead to inventory discrepancies.
- Complete each field according to your knowledge and understanding of the product's status.
- If you're unsure about a product's status or details, contact the "storekeeper_forms" team for assistance.
- Ensure you're providing the most up-to-date information to maintain accurate records.

* **products**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the product's current status in the inventory.

* **quantity**: Enter the exact quantity of the product within the inventory.
* **location**: Indicate the product's location within the inventory.

* **product_amount**: Enter the product's amount within the specified location.
