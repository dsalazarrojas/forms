# Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shirt Order Form is used to document and track customer orders for shirts, ensuring accuracy and consistency in recording order details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the customer information section with the customer's name, email, and phone number.
2. In the "Order Items" field, list the shirts ordered by the customer, including the type and quantity of each shirt.
3. Choose the payment method for the order, whether it's "Yes" for cash or "No" for other payment methods.
4. Enter the total amount of the order.
5. Select the status of the order as "Active" or "Inactive".
6. You can add any additional notes about the order in the "Order Notes" field.
7. Once you have filled in all the fields, click on "Submit" to save the order information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shirt Order** (`shirt_order_form`, Text): Enter a brief description of the order, for example, "Shirt Order for John Doe on 01/01/2023".
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, Text): Fill in the customer's name, email, and phone number for easy reference.
* **Order Items** (`order_items`, Text): List the shirts ordered by the customer, including the type and quantity of each shirt.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, Select One): Choose "Yes" for cash or "No" for other payment methods.
* **Total Amount** (`total_amount`, Number): Enter the total amount of the order.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, Select One): Select "Active" for orders that are being processed or "Inactive" for orders that are closed.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, Email): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, Text): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, Note): Add any additional comments or notes about the order.
* **Submit** (`submit`, Text): Click this button to save the order information.
