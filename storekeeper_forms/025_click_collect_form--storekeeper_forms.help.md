# Click & Collect Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Click & Collect Form is designed for customers to provide information for their click & collect orders. This form allows customers to input their personal details, order information, and preferred collection time and location. By filling out this form, customers can ensure that their orders are ready for collection at the agreed-upon time and location.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by reviewing the form carefully and ensuring you have all the required information ready.
2. Fill in your personal details, including your full name, email address, and mobile number.
3. Provide your preferred collection date and time slot.
4. Select your preferred store location for collection.
5. Choose your preferred payment method (pre-paid online, pay on collection with card, or pay on collection with cash).
6. Confirm that you understand that items are subject to availability.
7. Click the "Submit" button to send your order information for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Customer Name, Text, Required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, Email, Required): Enter your valid email address where you can receive order updates and confirmations.
* **Mobile Number** (Mobile Number, Text, Required): Provide your mobile number for order status updates.
* **Products and Quantities** (Text, Required): List the items you wish to purchase, e.g. "2x Milk, 1x Bread, 5kg Rice".
* **Online Order Number** (Online Order Number, Text, Optional): If you have already placed an order online, enter the order number for reference.
* **Any Special Instructions** (Text, Optional): If you have specific requirements or requests for your order, e.g. "Please ensure that the bread is gluten-free".
* **Collection Date** (Date, Required): Select a date for collection of your order.
* **Collection Time Slot** (Select One, Required): Choose a time slot for collection of your order, e.g. 9:00 - 11:00 AM or 5:00 - 7:00 PM.
* **Store Location** (Select One, Required): Select the store location where you would like to collect your order, e.g. Main Street Branch or West Side Mall.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (Select One, Required): Choose how you would like to pay for your order, e.g. pre-paid online, pay on collection with card, or pay on collection with cash).
* **I understand and Agree** (Select One, Required): Confirm that you understand that items are subject to availability and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Confirmation Message** (Note): A thank you message will be sent to you once your order is ready for collection.

This guide is designed to ensure that customers can easily fill out the Click & Collect Form with confidence and accuracy. Please review the form carefully and follow the steps outlined above to ensure that your order is processed correctly.
