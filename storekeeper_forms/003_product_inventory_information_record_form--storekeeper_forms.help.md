# Product Inventory Information Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Inventory Information Record Form is used to collect and record information about a product in the inventory. This form is designed to be filled out for one product at a time, and it helps to ensure that all relevant product details are accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Product Description field with a brief description of the product.
2. Enter the Product Name.
3. Select the Product ID, if applicable.
4. Enter the Quantity of the product.
5. Enter the Location of the product.
6. Enter the Product Price.
7. Select the Product Color.
8. Select the Product Size.
9. Enter the Product Weight.
10. Enter the Product Height.
11. Enter the Product Length.
12. Enter the Product Width.
13. Enter the Product Depth.
14. Select the Product Category.
15. Select the Product Subcategory.
16. Select the Product Status.
17. Enter the Product Notes.
18. Select the Product Tags.
19. Enter the Product Date.
20. Enter the Product Time.
21. Enter the Product Email.
22. Enter the Product Phone.
23. Enter the Product Fax.
24. Enter the Product Address.
25. Enter the Product Comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, optional): A brief description of the product.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, optional): The name of the product.
* **Product ID** (`product_id`, select_one, optional): The ID of the product, if applicable.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): The quantity of the product.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): The location of the product.
* **Product Price** (`product_price`, number, optional): The price of the product.
* **Product Color** (`product_color`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more colors of the product.
* **Product Size** (`product_size`, select_one, optional): The size of the product.
* **Product Weight** (`product_weight`, number, optional): The weight of the product.
* **Product Height** (`product_height`, number, optional): The height of the product.
* **Product Length** (`product_length`, number, optional): The length of the product.
* **Product Width** (`product_width`, number, optional): The width of the product.
* **Product Depth** (`product_depth`, number, optional): The depth of the product.
* **Product Category** (`product_category`, select_one, optional): The category of the product.
* **Product Subcategory** (`product_subcategory`, select_one, optional): The subcategory of the product.
* **Product Status** (`product_status`, select_one, optional): The status of the product.
* **Product Notes** (`product_notes`, note, optional): Additional notes about the product.
* **Product Tags** (`product_tags`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more tags for the product.
* **Product Date** (`product_date`, date, optional): The date of the product.
* **Product Time** (`product_time`, time, optional): The time of the product.
* **Product Email** (`product_email`, email, optional): The email of the product.
* **Product Phone** (`product_phone`, text, optional): The phone number of the product.
* **Product Fax** (`product_fax`, text, optional): The fax number of the product.
* **Product Address** (`product_address`, text, optional): The address of the product.
* **Product Comments** (`product_comments`, text, optional): Any additional comments about the product.
