# Store Rota Management Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Store Rota Management Form is used to manage the storekeepers' schedules and inform store managers of their availability and work schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your storekeeper name in the "storekeeper_name" field.
2. Select your role as a storekeeper from the "storekeeper_role" dropdown menu (Assistant Store Manager, Store Manager, or Sales Assistant).
3. If you have a start date, select it in the "start_date" field.
4. If you have an end date, select it in the "end_date" field.
5. Enter your start time in the "start_time" field.
6. Enter your end time in the "end_time" field.
7. Enter the length of your shift in the "shift_length" field.
8. If you have any other information about your shift, you can enter it in the "store_name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store Rota Form** (`store_rota_form`): This field is a free-text field where you can enter any information about your store rota.
* **storekeeper_name** (`storekeeper_name`): Enter your name as a storekeeper.
* **storekeeper_role** (`storekeeper_role`): Select your role as a storekeeper from the dropdown menu (Assistant Store Manager, Store Manager, or Sales Assistant).
* **start_date** (`start_date`): Enter the start date of your shift if you have one.
* **end_date** (`end_date`): Enter the end date of your shift if you have one.
* **start_time** (`start_time`): Enter the start time of your shift.
* **end_time** (`end_time`): Enter the end time of your shift.
* **shift_length** (`shift_length`): Enter the length of your shift.
* **store_name** (`store_name`): Enter any other information about your shift if needed.
