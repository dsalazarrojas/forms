# Outlet Auditing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Outlet Auditing Form is used to gather information about outlets, including their name, address, type, and status. This form helps ensure that outlets are properly labeled and maintained, which is essential for safe and efficient operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the outlet in the "Outlet Name" field.
2. Enter the address of the outlet in the "Address" field.
3. Select the type of the outlet from the dropdown menu in the "Outlet Type" field.
4. Enter the voltage rating of the outlet in the "Voltage" field.
5. Enter the current rating of the outlet in the "Current" field.
6. Select the phases that the outlet is currently using in the "Phase" field.
7. Enter the power factor of the outlet in the "Power Factor" field.
8. Select the status of the outlet from the dropdown menu in the "Outlet Status" field.
9. Add any additional comments about the outlet in the "Comments" field.
9. If applicable, enter the tool assigned to the outlet in the "Assigned Tool" field and the date it was assigned in the "Assigned Date" field.
10. Enter the name of the person who last checked the outlet in the "Checked By" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Outlet Name** (`outlet_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the outlet in this field.
- **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the outlet in this field.
- **Outlet Type** (`outlet_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of the outlet from the dropdown menu, such as Outlet or Socket.
- **Voltage** (`voltage`, number, required/optional): Enter the voltage rating of the outlet, typically measured in volts (V).
- **Current** (`current`, number, required/optional): Enter the current rating of the outlet, typically measured in amperes (A).
- **Phase** (`phase`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the phases that the outlet is currently using, such as 1, 2, or 3.
- **Power Factor** (`power_factor`, number, required/optional): Enter the power factor of the outlet, a value between 0 and 1.
- **Outlet Status** (`outlet_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the status of the outlet from the dropdown menu, such as Functional or Non-Functional.
- **Comments** (`comments`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments about the outlet in this field.
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required/optional): If applicable, enter the tool assigned to the outlet in this field.
- **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, date, required/optional): If applicable, enter the date the tool was assigned to the outlet in this field.
- **Checked By** (`checked_by`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who last checked the outlet in this field.
