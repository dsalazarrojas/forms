# received_inventory_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "received_inventory_form" is used to track and record inventory received from various vendors. This form ensures that all items are properly documented, including their description, quantity, and unit price, to help with stock management and accurate accounting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Vendors Name** in the text field.
2. Describe the **Item Description** in the text area.
3. Enter the **Quantity Received**.
4. Enter the **Unit Price**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Vendors Name** (`vendor_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the vendor who provided the item(s).
- **Item Description** (`item_description`, text, required/optional): Briefly describe the item being received, including any relevant details.
- **Quantity Received** (`quantity_received`, number, required/optional): Enter the total quantity of items received from the vendor.
- **Unit Price** (`unit_price`, number, required/optional): Enter the price per unit of the item being received.

## Tips
* Ensure you have all necessary information before completing the form.
* If unsure about any field, check with your inventory manager or the vendor.
* This form should be completed for every item received from a vendor.
* Review and update your inventory records regularly to ensure accuracy.
