# Stock Inventory Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stock Inventory Form is a tool for storekeepers to report stock status and other relevant details about products in stock. This form is designed to help storekeepers accurately track and manage inventory levels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*). These fields are:
	* Quantity: Enter the current quantity of the product in stock.
	* Product ID: Enter the ID of the product being reported.
2. Select the correct stock status from the dropdown menu:
	* In stock
	* Out of stock
3. If necessary, select one or more storekeeper roles from the dropdown menu:
	* Admin
	* Manager
	* Staff
4. Enter the date and time of the stock update.
5. Add any additional notes about the stock update.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Store Inventory Form** (`store_inventory_form`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the stock update.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the current quantity of the product in stock.
* **Product ID** (`product_id`, number, optional): Enter the ID of the product being reported.
* **Stock Status** (`stock_status`, select_one, optional): Select the correct stock status from the dropdown menu:
	+ In stock
	+ Out of stock
* **Storekeeper ID** (`storekeeper_id`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more storekeeper roles from the dropdown menu:
	+ Admin
	+ Manager
	+ Staff
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the stock update.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the stock update.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the stock update.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the person reporting the stock update (if applicable).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person reporting the stock update (if applicable).
