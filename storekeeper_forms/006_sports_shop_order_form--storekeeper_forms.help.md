2. Select the type of order you are placing in the "Order Type" section.
3. Indicate if you have any items ordered in the "Items Ordered" section.
4. Enter your payment information in the "Payment Info" section.
5. Provide the order total in the "Order Total" section.
6. Enter the date and time of the order in the "Order Date" and "Order Time" sections.
7. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Order Comments" and "Customer Comments" sections.
