# Sports Shop Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Shop Order Form is designed to collect customer and order information for sports-related products. This form is used to gather details about the customer, the order, and payment information. It is part of a sales process that involves online ordering and customer service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer information, including name, email, and phone number in the "Customer Details" section.
2. Select the type of order you are placing in the "Order Type" section.
3. Indicate if you have any items ordered in the "Items Ordered" section.
4. Enter your payment information in the "Payment Info" section.
5. Provide the order total in the "Order Total" section.
6. Enter the date and time of the order in the "Order Date" and "Order Time" sections.
7. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Order Comments" and "Customer Comments" sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details**: Enter your name, email, and phone number to ensure that we can contact you regarding your order.
* **Order Type**: Select the type of order you are placing (e.g., Team Uniforms, Sports Fan Gear).
* **Items Ordered**: Indicate if you have any items ordered, if applicable.
* **Payment Info**: Choose your preferred payment method (e.g., Card, Cash, PayPal).
* **Order Total**: Enter the total cost of your order.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date of your order.
* **Order Time**: Enter the time of your order.
* **Order Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Customer Comments**: Add any comments or notes from the customer.
* **Customer Agreement**: Select if you agree to the terms and conditions of the order.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Address**: Enter your customer's address.
* **Order Status**: Select the status of your order (e.g., New, In Progress, Complete, Cancelled).
* **Order Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the order.
* **Customer Agreement**: Select if you agree to the terms and conditions of the order.
* **Customer Note**: Add any additional comments or notes from the customer.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as required).
* Enter accurate and complete information to ensure efficient processing of your order.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact us.
