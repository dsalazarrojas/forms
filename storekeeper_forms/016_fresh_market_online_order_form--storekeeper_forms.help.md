# Fresh Market Online Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fresh Market Online Order Form is used to collect customer information and preferences for online orders, ensuring accurate and efficient order management within the system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the product details to confirm your order.
2.  Provide your contact information to enable communication and updates.
3.  Choose your delivery and payment options.
4.  Fill in the payment information to proceed with your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Product Details (product_details_label)** (`product_details`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Select the product details to confirm your order.
*   **Contact Information (contact_info_label)** (`contact_info`, `select_one`, **Required**): Provide your contact information to enable communication and updates.
*   **Delivery Information (delivery_info_label)** (`delivery_info`, `date`, **Optional**): Choose your delivery options (not applicable if not required).
*   **Payment Information (payment_info_label)** (`payment_info`, `text`, **Optional**): Fill in the payment information to proceed with your order.

## Tips
*   Review each field carefully to ensure accurate submission.
*   Double-check your product and delivery options.
*   Enter accurate payment information for secure and efficient order processing.
*   Submit the form once all required fields are filled in correctly.
