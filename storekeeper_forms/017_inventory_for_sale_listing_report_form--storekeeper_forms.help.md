# Inventory For Sale Listing Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inventory For Sale Listing Report Form is used to gather information on products available for sale in the inventory system. It helps users quickly scan and analyze sales data, making it easier to make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the product name in the **Product Name** field.
2. Enter the product price in the **Product Price** field.
3. Enter a brief description of the product in the **Product Description** field.
4. Enter the inventory quantity of the product in the **Inventory Quantity** field.
5. Enter the sale price of the product in the **Sale Price** field.
6. Select the **Category** of the product from the dropdown menu (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
7. Select the **Vendor** of the product from the dropdown menu (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
8. Select any applicable tags for the product in the **Tags** field.
9. Enter the date the product was created in the **Created At** field.
10. Enter the date the product was last updated in the **Updated At** field.
11. Select the status of the product from the dropdown menu (e.g., 'Active', 'Inactive').
12. Enter any assigned tool information in the **Assigned Tool** field.
13. Enter the ID of the assigned tool in the **Assigned Tool Id** field.
14. Enter the timestamp of when the product was created in the **Created At Timestamp** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product, e.g., "Apple Watch".
* **Product Price**: Enter the price of the product in currency (e.g., "10.99").
* **Product Description**: Briefly describe the product (e.g., "Smartwatch for fitness tracking").
* **Inventory Quantity**: Enter the number of products available in inventory (e.g., "50").
* **Sale Price**: Enter the price at which the product is sold (e.g., "12.99").
* **Category**: Select the category of the product (e.g., "Electronics").
* **Vendor**: Select the vendor of the product (e.g., "Apple").
* **Tags**: Select any applicable tags for the product (e.g., "Fitness", "Gadgets").
* **Created At**: Enter the date the product was created in the format "YYYY-MM-DD" (e.g., "2022-01-01").
* **Updated At**: Enter the date the product was last updated in the format "YYYY-MM-DD" (e.g., "2022-01-01").
* **Status**: Select the status of the product (e.g., 'Active', 'Inactive').
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter any assigned tool information.
* **Assigned Tool Id**: Enter the ID of the assigned tool.
* **Created At Timestamp**: Enter the timestamp of when the product was created in the format "HH:MM:SS" (e.g., "12:00:00").
