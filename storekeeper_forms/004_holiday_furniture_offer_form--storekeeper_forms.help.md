# Holiday Furniture Offer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Holiday Furniture Offer Form" is designed to gather information about customers' holiday offers or preferences related to furniture.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions as accurately as possible to provide detailed information about the offers or preferences you are interested in or have received.
2. If a field is marked as "optional", feel free to fill it out if you have relevant information or would like to provide more details.
3. Use the provided options for select fields to choose the most suitable answer.
4. Enter your email address and phone number so we can contact you with any further questions or for confirmation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Get Offers From Your Customers**: This is the main title of the form, and it's an opportunity for you to describe the offers or preferences you are looking for or have received. Please provide a clear and concise description.
* **item_list**: Select all the items you are offering or interested in from the list provided. You can choose one or multiple items.
* **item name**: Enter the name of the item you are offering or interested in. This could be a specific furniture piece or a group of items.
* **offer_date**: If you are offering a specific date for the offer, enter it in the correct format (e.g., 2024-02-25). If not, leave it blank.
* **offer_time**: If you are offering a specific time for the offer, enter it in the correct format (e.g., 14:00). If not, leave it blank.
* **offer description**: Describe the offer or preference you are interested in, including any relevant details.
* **item price**: If you are offering a specific price for the item, enter it here. If not, leave it blank.
* **customer message**: Enter any additional information or notes about the offer or preference you are providing.
* **customer email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with any further questions or for confirmation.
* **customer phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you with any further questions or for confirmation.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to help us understand your needs better.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us directly.
* We will review and follow up with you based on the information you provide in this form.
