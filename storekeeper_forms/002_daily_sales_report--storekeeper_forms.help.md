# Daily Sales Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Sales Report is a form used to track and review the sales activity of a given day. This report captures key information about the date of the sales, total sales amount, product categories, and order details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Daily Sales Report, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have access to the form and have selected the correct date for the report.
* Enter the date of the sales report in the `daily_sales_date` field.
* Enter the total sales amount for the day in the `sales_amount` field.
* Select the product category for the sales in the `product_category` field.
* Enter any notes for the salesperson in the `sales_person` field.
* Enter any notes for the customer in the `customer_note` field.
* Enter any sales notes in the `sales_note` field.
* Enter any product notes in the `product_note` field.
* Enter any order notes in the `order_note` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Daily Sales Date (`daily_sales_date`)**: Enter the date of the sales report.
* **Sales Amount (`sales_amount`)**: Enter the total sales amount for the day.
* **Product Category (`product_category`)**: Select the category of the product sold (e.g., Electronics, Clothing, etc.).
* **Employee ID (`employee_id`)**: Enter the salesperson's ID number.
* **Sales Person (`sales_person`)**: Enter notes about the salesperson's performance.
* **Customer ID (`customer_id`)**: Enter the customer's ID number.
* **Order ID (`order_id`)**: Enter the order number.
* **Order Date (`order_date`)**: Enter the date of the order.
* **Order Time (`order_time`)**: Enter the time of the order.
* **Order Status (`order_status`)**: Select the status of the order (e.g., Pending, Shipped, Cancelled).
* **Payment Type (`payment_type`)**: Select the method of payment (e.g., Payment by Cash, Payment by Card, etc.).
* **Total Orders (`total_orders`)**: Enter the total number of orders made.
* **Storekeeper (`storekeeper`)**: Enter notes for the storekeeper.
* **Storekeeper Note (`storekeeper_note`)**: Enter any additional notes for the storekeeper.
* **Customer Note (`customer_note`)**: Enter notes for the customer.
* **Sales Note (`sales_note`)**: Enter any additional notes about sales.
* **Product Note (`product_note`)**: Enter notes about the product.
* **Order Note (`order_note`)**: Enter any additional notes about the order.
