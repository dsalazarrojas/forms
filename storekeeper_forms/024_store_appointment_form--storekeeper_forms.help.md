# Store Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to schedule appointments with customers for various services or meetings at the store. It requests basic information from customers, including name, email, phone number, and appointment details, to facilitate efficient communication and planning.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, email address, and phone number in the respective fields.
2. Choose the correct store location for the appointment.
3. Select the date and time for the appointment.
4. Provide any additional details about the appointment in the text field.
5. Confirm your appointment by selecting "Yes" from the dropdown menu.
6. Confirm the appointment time by selecting "Yes" from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for the store staff to contact you.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required): Select the date for the appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required): Select the time for the appointment.
* **Store Location** (`store_location`, select_one, required): Choose the store where the appointment is scheduled.
* **Appointment Details** (`appointment_details`, text, required): Provide any additional information about the appointment, such as the reason for the visit or specific products/services being discussed.
* **Confirm Appointment** (`confirm_appointment`, select_multiple, required): Confirm that you have scheduled this appointment by selecting "Yes".
* **Confirm Appointment Time** (`confirm_appointment_time`, select_multiple, required): Confirm the appointment time by selecting "Yes".

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all the required fields to ensure the store staff can process your appointment correctly.
* Double-check the store location, date, and time to avoid any confusion.
* Be clear and concise when providing appointment details to ensure the store staff understand your needs.
