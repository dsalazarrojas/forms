# Inventory Management Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Inventory Management Form is used by storekeepers to add, update, or remove items from their store's inventory. It helps to ensure accurate and up-to-date inventory records, making it easier to manage stock levels and make informed business decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information for each field.
2. Enter the item name in the "Item Name" field (Field id: 2).
3. Provide a brief description of the item in the "Item Description" field (Field id: 3).
4. Enter the quantity of the item in stock (Field id: 4).
5. Enter the price of the item (Field id: 5).
6. Choose the category of the item (Field id: 6).
7. Select one or multiple subcategories for the item (Field id: 7).
8. Enter the location where the item is stored (Field id: 8).
9. Add the date and time of the last update (Fields id: 9 and 10).
10. Add any additional notes about the item (Field id: 11).
11. Enter the employee's name, email, and phone number who is responsible for the item (Fields id: 12-14).
12. Enter the customer's name, email, and phone number (Fields id: 16-18).
13. Enter the employee's ID (Field id: 20).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Item Name** (Field id: 2): Enter the name of the item you want to add to or update in the inventory.
* **Item Description** (Field id: 3): Provide a brief description of the item to help identify it.
* **Quantity** (Field id: 4): Enter the current quantity of the item in stock.
* **Price** (Field id: 5): Enter the current price of the item.
* **Category** (Field id: 6): Choose the main category of the item (e.g., Electronics, Books, Sportswear, or Toys).
* **Subcategory** (Field id: 7): Select one or multiple subcategories for the item (e.g., Gadgets, Novelty Books, Sportswear, or Dolls).
* **Location** (Field id: 8): Enter the location where the item is stored.
* **Date** (Field id: 9): Enter the date of the last update of the item.
* **Time** (Field id: 10): Enter the time of the last update of the item.
* **Note** (Field id: 11): Add any additional notes about the item.
* **Employee Name** (Field id: 12): Enter the name of the employee responsible for the item.
* **Employee Email** (Field id: 13): Enter the email of the employee responsible for the item.
* **Employee Phone** (Field id: 14): Enter the phone number of the employee responsible for the item.
* **Employee Location** (Field id: 15): Enter the location of the employee responsible for the item.
* **Customer Name** (Field id: 16): Enter the name of the customer who bought the item.
* **Customer Email** (Field id: 17): Enter the email of the customer who bought the item.
* **Customer Phone** (Field id: 18): Enter the phone number of the customer who bought the item.
* **Customer Location** (Field id: 19): Enter the location of the customer who bought the item.
* **Employee ID** (Field id: 20): Enter the ID of the employee who is responsible for the item.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check the information you enter for accuracy.
* Use the form to update existing items in the inventory.
* Use the form to add new items to the inventory.
* Use the form to remove items from the inventory.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field's label for clarification.
