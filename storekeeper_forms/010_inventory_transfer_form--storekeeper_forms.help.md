# inventory_transfer_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the process of tracking and recording inventory transfers between members of a team or organization. It collects metadata and notes related to the transfer, such as the quantity of items, date, and time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the member from whom the item is being transferred (transfer_from).
2. Select the member to whom the item is being transferred (transfer_to).
3. Enter a brief description of the item being transferred (item_description).
4. Enter the quantity of items being transferred.
5. Enter the date and time of the transfer.
6. Add any additional notes or comments about the transfer (notes).
7. Enter the email address of the team member involved in the transfer (email).
8. Optionally, enter a phone number for the team member involved (phone_number).
9. Enter a unique identifier for the form (form_id).
10. Select a submit option to finalize the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **transfer_from**: Select the member from whom the item is being transferred.
* **transfer_to**: Select the member to whom the item is being transferred.
* **item_description**: Enter a brief description of the item being transferred.
* **quantity**: Enter the quantity of items being transferred.
* **date**: Enter the date of the transfer.
* **time**: Enter the time of the transfer.
* **notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the transfer.
* **email**: Enter the email address of the team member involved in the transfer.
* **phone_number**: Enter a phone number for the team member involved (optional).
* **form_id**: Enter a unique identifier for the form.
* **submit**: Select this option to finalize the form and complete the transfer process.
* **assigned_tool**: This field appears to be redundant and has no clear purpose. Please disregard this field.
* **category**: This field has no apparent purpose and seems to be redundant. Please disregard this field.
* **description**: This field is likely a duplicate of the **item_description** field. Please use **item_description** instead.
* **form_ids**: This field has no apparent purpose and seems to be redundant. Please disregard this field.
