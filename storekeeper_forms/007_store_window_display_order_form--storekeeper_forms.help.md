# store_window_display_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "store_window_display_order_form" is a form designed to collect information about store window display requests. It is intended for store window display requesters to provide their details and requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the "store_window_request" field and enter your name as the requester.
2. Click on the "store_window_display_request_date" field and select the date when you would like to display your window.
3. If you have any additional comments or information, you can type them in the "store_window_requester" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **store_window_request** (`store_window_request`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the requester. This field is required.
* **store_window_display_request_date** (`store_window_display_request_date`, `date`, required): Select the date when you would like to display your window. This field is required.
* **store_window_requester** (`store_window_requester`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or information, you can type them in this field. This field is optional.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (store_window_request and store_window_display_request_date) before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx.
