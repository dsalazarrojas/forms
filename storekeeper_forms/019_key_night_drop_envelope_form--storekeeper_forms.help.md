# Key Night Drop Envelope Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Key Night Drop Envelope Form is used to collect and document information related to vehicle drops within a store. This form is essential for tracking and recording vehicle details, client information, and consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the vehicle's unique number, which is used to identify the vehicle.
2. Select the type of vehicle from the provided options.
3. Enter the vehicle's year of manufacture.
4. Enter the vehicle's make.
5. Enter the vehicle's model.
6. Enter any necessary client signature information.
7. Enter relevant client details and information.
8. Provide any required consent information.
9. Enter the storekeeper's signature to verify completion and accuracy.
10. Read and agree to the form's terms of consent if desired, although this is an optional field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Number**: <vehicle_number> (`vehicle_number`, text, required) - Enter the unique vehicle number for accurate tracking.
* **Vehicle Type**: <vehicle_type> (`vehicle_type`, select_one, optional) - Select the type of vehicle from the provided options.
* **Vehicle Year**: <vehicle_year> (`vehicle_year`, number, required) - Enter the vehicle's year of manufacture.
* **Vehicle Make**: <vehicle_make> (`vehicle_make`, text, required) - Enter the vehicle's make.
* **Vehicle Model**: <vehicle_model> (`vehicle_model`, text, required) - Enter the vehicle's model.
* **Client Signature**: <client_signature> (`client_signature`, text, required) - Enter the client's signature for verification.
* **Client Info**: <client_info> (`client_info`, text, optional) - Enter any additional client details or information.
* **Damage Info**: <damage_info> (`damage_info`, text, optional) - Enter any relevant damage information, if applicable.
* **Consent Info**: <consent_info> (`consent_info`, text, required) - Enter any consent information or agreements related to the vehicle drop.
* **Storekeeper Signature**: <storekeeper_signature> (`storekeeper_signature`, text, required) - Enter the storekeeper's signature to verify completion and accuracy.
* **Consent**: <consent> (`consent`, text, optional) - Read and agree to the form's terms of consent, if desired.
