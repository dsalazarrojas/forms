# Retail Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers about their visit to your retail store. This information will help us understand how to better serve our customers and improve our products.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please fill out this form completely and honestly to provide us with valuable feedback.**
2. **Use the options provided for each question to select your answers.**
3. **Double-check your responses before submitting the form.**

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on our records.
* **Prophets Count** (`prophets_number`, number, required: false): This question is intended to measure how many prophets you have encountered during your visit.
* **Price Range** (`price_range`, number, required: false): Please select the price range that best reflects your purchasing experience.
* **Product Name** (`product_id`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Choose all products that apply to your visit:
		- Yes
		- No
* **Store Location** (`store_address`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose where you shopped:
		- Yes
		- No
* **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, date, required: false): Please enter the date of your visit in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Visit Time** (`visit_time`, time, required: false): Please enter the time of your visit in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted, please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted, please enter your phone number.
* **Store Manager Name** (`store_manager_name`, text, required: false): If you spoke with a store manager, please enter their name.
* **Store Manager Email** (`store_manager_email`, email, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted by a store manager, please enter their email address.
* **Store Manager Phone** (`store_manager_phone`, text, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted by a store manager, please enter their phone number.
* **Store Location** (`store_location`, text, required: false): If you can remember the store's location, please enter it.
* **Store City** (`store_city`, text, required: false): If you can remember the city where the store is located, please enter it.
* **Store State** (`store_state`, text, required: false): If you can remember the state where the store is located, please enter it.
* **Store Zip** (`store_zip`, text, required: false): If you can remember the zip code where the store is located, please enter it.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, text, required: false): Please select the type of product(s) you were looking for.
* **Price** (`price_range`, number, required: false): If you can remember the price range of the product(s) you purchased, please enter it.
* **Rating** (`rating`, text, required: false): Please rate your overall experience (e.g., 1-5 stars).
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): If you have any additional feedback or comments, please enter them here.

## Tips
* Please keep your responses concise and honest.
* If you're unsure about any question, please answer "Not Applicable" or "Not Sure".
* If you have any further questions or need help, please don't hesitate to contact us.
