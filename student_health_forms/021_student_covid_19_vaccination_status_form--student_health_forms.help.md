# Student COVID 19 Vaccination Status Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Student COVID 19 Vaccination Status Form" is used to track and document the COVID-19 vaccination status of students. Please complete this form to report your current vaccination status and any additional details as required.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Select your current vaccination status from the provided options (Active or Inactive).
2. If you have been vaccinated, enter the date of vaccination.
3. If you have been vaccinated, enter the location where you received your vaccination.
4. If applicable, provide any vaccination certificates or related notes.
5. Add any comments or notes from teachers or staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student COVID 19 Vaccination Status Form** (student_covid_19_vaccination_status_form, select_multiple): Select your current vaccination status as Active or Inactive.
* **Vaccination Status** (vaccination_status, select_one): Select your current vaccination status as Active or Inactive.
* **Vaccinated On** (vaccinated_on, date): Enter the date you were vaccinated if you are Active.
* **Vaccinated Location** (vaccinated_location, text): Enter the location where you received your vaccination.
* **Vaccination Certificate** (vaccination_certificate, text): Upload or attach your vaccination certificate (if applicable).
* **Student Vaccination Status Notes** (student_vaccination_status_notes, note): Add any additional notes or comments about your vaccination status.
* **Teacher Comments** (teacher_comments, text): Add any comments or notes from teachers or staff.
* **Last Updated** (student_vaccination_status_last_updated, date): This field is automatically updated when you submit the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to select your correct vaccination status.
* If you have been vaccinated, provide accurate dates and locations for your vaccination.
* If you have a vaccination certificate, attach it to the form.
* Keep your comments and notes up to date and accurate.
* This form will be reviewed and updated regularly.
