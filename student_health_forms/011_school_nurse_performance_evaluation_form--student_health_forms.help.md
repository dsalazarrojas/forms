# school_nurse_performance_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The school nurse performance evaluation form is designed to assess the performance of school nurses in providing health services and maintaining a safe environment. This form will help in identifying areas of improvement and recognizing strengths to support professional development and accountability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you are evaluating a school nurse's performance.
2. Select the answer options that best match your evaluation for each field. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Nurse Performance** (`school_nurse_performance`, select_multiple): This field is used to evaluate whether or not a school nurse has performed well in their school duties.
	+ Select 'Yes' for excellent performance or 'No' for a less than satisfactory performance.
* **School Staff** (`school_staff`, select_multiple): This field is used to evaluate whether or not school staff have been well managed by the school nurse.
	+ Select 'Yes' for well-managed staff or 'No' for a less than satisfactory performance.
* **School** (`school`, select_one): This field is used to evaluate whether or not the school environment is conducive to the nurse's performance.
	+ Select 'Yes' for a conducive environment or 'No' for a less than satisfactory environment.
* **Overall Performance Rating** (`overall_performance_rating`, select_one): This field is used to rate the overall performance of the school nurse.
	+ Select 'Good' for a satisfactory performance or 'Fair' for a less than satisfactory performance.
* **Evaluation Frequency** (`evaluation_frequency`, select_multiple): This field is used to determine how often the school nurse's performance is evaluated.
	+ Select 'Yes' for regular evaluations or 'No' for infrequent evaluations.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, select_multiple): This field is used to provide comments on the performance evaluation.
	+ Select 'Yes' to indicate comments have been provided or 'No' if no comments have been given.
* **Student Health Clinics** (`student_health_clinics`, select_multiple): This field is used to evaluate the provision of student health clinics.
	+ Select 'Yes' for good service or 'No' for a less than satisfactory service.
* **Student Health Services** (`student_health_services`, select_multiple): This field is used to evaluate the provision of student health services.
	+ Select 'Yes' for good service or 'No' for a less than satisfactory service.
* **School Environment** (`school_environment`, select_multiple): This field is used to evaluate the school environment's impact on the performance of the school nurse.
	+ Select 'Yes' for a conducive environment or 'No' for a less than satisfactory environment.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date): This field is used to record the date of the performance evaluation.
	+ Enter the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time): This field is used to record the time of the performance evaluation.
	+ Enter the time in the format HH:MM.
* **Evaluation Time of Day** (`evaluation_time_of_day`, time): This field is used to record the time of day of the performance evaluation.
	+ Enter the time in the format HH:MM.
* **Evaluation Time of Year** (`evaluation_time_of_year`, time): This field is used to record the time of year of the performance evaluation.
	+ Enter the time in the format HH:MM.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, select_one): This field is used to record who submitted the performance evaluation form.
	+ Select 'Yes' if the form was submitted by someone or 'No' if not.
* **Form Submitted On** (`form_submitted_on`, date): This field is used to record the date the form was submitted.
	+ Enter the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

## Tips
* Please answer each field based on your actual experience and observations.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy.
* This form is for evaluating school nurses' performance, not students' evaluations.
