# parent_consent_and_medical_history_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The parent_consent_and_medical_history_form is a form designed to collect essential information about a child's medical history, allergies, and emergency contact information from parents or guardians.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions regarding the child's medical history, including any allergies, medical conditions, and previous medical procedures.
2. Provide information about any medical devices or equipment the child is using.
3. List any medical supplies that the child may need in case of an emergency.
4. Enter the contact information for any emergency contacts.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm that you have provided accurate and up-to-date information.

* **Parent Consent**: This field is for the parent or guardian to provide consent for their child to participate in activities and receive medical care.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No

	+ Required: No

	+ Required: No

* **Medical Device**: This field is for listing any medical devices or equipment the child is using.

* **Medical Equipment**: This field is for listing any medical equipment the child may need in case of an emergency.

## Tips
This form is a critical tool for ensuring the child's safety and well-being. Please take the time to fill it out accurately and thoroughly. If you are unsure about any information, please consult with a healthcare professional.
