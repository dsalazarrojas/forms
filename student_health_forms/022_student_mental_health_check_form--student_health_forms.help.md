# Student Mental Health Check Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help students provide accurate and timely information about their mental health status, including their health concerns, the services they require, and the support they have in place.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, providing as much detail as possible in the text fields.
2. Choose the correct services and supports you require from the select_one and select_multiple fields.
3. Make sure to submit the form by choosing a submit date and time.
4. If a support staff member reviews the form, they will receive a copy of the submitted form and will follow up with you regarding your mental health status.
