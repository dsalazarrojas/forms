# Student Program Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant to be a comprehensive feedback collection tool for students to express their thoughts about a specific program. It will help the program administrators and developers to identify areas of strength and areas that need improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Choose the relevant options for each field.
3. Use the text fields for any additional comments or explanations.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Info** (`general_info`, text, required/optional): Enter any general information you'd like to share about your experience with the program.
- **Program Info** (`program_info`, text, required/optional): Provide details about the program you're giving feedback on.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Share your thoughts about the program, including both positive and negative aspects.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you attended the program.
- **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you attended the program.
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as a student.
- **Program Rating** (`program_rating`, number, required/optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the program, on a scale from 0 to 10.
- **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, required/optional): Choose one of the options provided to indicate your level of satisfaction with the program.
- **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose multiple options that best describe your experience with the program.
- **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or reminders about the program.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for further contact.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for further contact.
- **Date 2** (`date_2`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you attended the program (again).
- **Time 2** (`time_2`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you attended the program (again).
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as a student (again).
- **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the program you're giving feedback on.
- **Submit** (`submit`, note, required/optional): Click this button to submit your feedback.
- **Cancel** (`cancel`, note, required/optional): Click this button to cancel your feedback without submitting.
- **Email 2** (`email_2`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for further contact (again).
- **Phone 2** (`phone_2`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for further contact (again).
- **Date 3** (`date_3`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you attended the program (again).
- **Time 3** (`time_3`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you attended the program (again).
- **Comments 2** (`comments_2`, text, required/optional): Share your thoughts about the program (again).
- **Program Rating 2** (`program_rating_2`, number, required/optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the program (again).
- **Select One 2** (`select_one_2`, select_one, required/optional): Choose one of the options provided to indicate your level of satisfaction with the program (again).
