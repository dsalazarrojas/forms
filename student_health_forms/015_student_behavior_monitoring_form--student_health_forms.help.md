# Student Behavior Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information on a student's behavior, behavior history, clinical assessment, and services provided to them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the general information section with the student's basic details.
2. Provide information on the student's behavior history, including any incidents or observations.
3. Conduct a clinical assessment of the student's behavior, including any notable behaviors or issues.
4. List any parent contact information.
5. List any student referrals or recommendations for services.
6. List any teacher referrals or recommendations for services.
7. List any school referrals or recommendations for services.
8. List any parent services provided to the student.
9. List any additional student services provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`general_info`, `text`, required):
	+ A place to fill in basic student details, such as name and date of birth.
* **Student Information** (`student_info`, `text`, required):
	+ Provide information about the student, such as student ID or student number.
* **Behavior History** (`behavior_history`, `text`, required):
	+ Describe any incidents or observations of the student's behavior, including any relevant dates and details.
* **Behavioral and Clinical Assessment** (`behavioral_clinical_assessment`, `text`, required):
	+ Conduct a clinical assessment of the student's behavior, including any notable behaviors or issues.
* **Parent Contact** (`parent_contact`, `text`, required):
	+ List any contact information for the parent or guardian of the student.
* **Student Referral** (`student_referral`, `text`, required):
	+ List any referrals or recommendations for services for the student.
* **Teacher Referral** (`teacher_referral`, `text`, required):
	+ List any referrals or recommendations for services from teachers or educators.
* **School Referral** (`school_referral`, `text`, required):
	+ List any referrals or recommendations for services from school staff or administrators.
* **Parent Services** (`parent_services`, `text`, required):
	+ List any services provided to the student by parents or guardians.
* **Student Services 2** (`student_services_2`, `text`, required):
	+ List any additional services provided to the student. 

# Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Be as detailed as possible when providing information in each field.
* If you're unsure about any field, please consult with the student's teacher or school staff for guidance.
