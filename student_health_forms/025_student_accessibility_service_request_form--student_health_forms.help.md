# Student Accessibility Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Accessibility Service Request Form is a tool used to request accessibility services for students with diverse needs and abilities. To complete this form, please follow the instructions below.


## How To Complete This Form
1. Determine if you need accessibility services by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the **Student Accessibility Service Request** field.
2. If you need services, select the types of accommodations you require in the **Accommodation Request** field.
3. In the **Accommodation Request Type** field, choose the specific types of services you need.
4. Provide your contact details in the **Contact Details** field for follow-up communication.
5. If you have a preferred method of contact, enter your phone number in the **Contact Phone** field.
6. Enter your email address in the **Contact Email** field.
7. Select "Yes" or "No" in the **Student Support Type** field to indicate the type of support you need.
8. In the **Support Request Type** field, choose the specific type of support you require.
9. Enter the date and time you need support in the **Support Request Date and Time** field.
10. Write any additional information or comments in the **Student Support Note** field.
11. Confirm your request in the **Student Support Request** field by selecting "Yes" or "No".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Accessibility Service Request**: Please select "Yes" if you need accessibility services. This will help us identify your needs and allocate resources accordingly.
* **Student Accessibility Support Request**: This field is for students with diverse needs and abilities who require support beyond regular classes. Please select "Yes" if you need special assistance.
* **Accommodation Request**: Indicate if you require specific accommodations such as extra time, extra space, or other support needs.
* **Accommodation Request Type**: Please select from the options provided to specify what type of accommodation you require.
* **Contact Details**: Provide your preferred contact information so we can reach out to you about your request.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number if you have one.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address if you have one.
* **Student Support Type**: Select "Yes" if you need support for a specific reason.
* **Support Request Type**: Choose from the options provided to specify what type of support you require.
* **Support Request Date and Time**: Enter the date and time you need support.
* **Student Support Note**: Write any additional information or comments you think we should know about your request.
* **Student Support Request**: Confirm your request by selecting "Yes" or "No".
