# Student Emergency Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Emergency Contact Form is used to collect contact information for students in case of an emergency. This information will be used by authorized personnel to reach out to the student or their emergency contact in a crisis situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and student information in the "Student Information" section.
2. Provide contact information for your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact" section.
3. Specify your relationship with your emergency contact in the "Relation" section.
4. Enter your emergency contact's name in the "Name" section.
5. Enter your emergency contact's address in the "Address" section.
6. Enter your emergency contact's phone number in the "Phone" section.
7. Enter your emergency contact's email address in the "Email" section.
8. Enter any additional emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact Information" section.
9. If applicable, provide a second emergency contact's contact information in the "Contact 2" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Information** (`Student Information`, text, required): Please enter your name and student ID number to identify yourself as a student.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact, such as a parent or guardian.
* **Relation** (`Relation`, number, required): Select the type of relationship you have with your emergency contact (e.g. 0 for parent/guardian, 1 for spouse/partner, 2 for friend, etc.).
* **Name** (`Name`, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, required): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Email** (`Email`, text, required): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Information** (`Emergency Contact Information`, text, required): Enter any additional information you think would be helpful in contacting your emergency contact.
* **Contact 2** (`Contact 2`, text, optional): If you have a secondary emergency contact, enter their information here.
