# School Health Center Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Health Center Discharge Form is used to document a student's health information after a visit to the school health center.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring that all required fields are filled out.
2. Use the "Student Health Center Visit" page to capture the necessary information about the student's health center visit.
3. Use the "Discharge Instructions" page to capture any discharge instructions provided by the school health center.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Health Center Visit** (`student_health_center_visit`, `note`, required: false): This page captures information about the student's visit to the school health center. You can enter any relevant details about the visit, such as the date and time of the visit, the student's symptoms, or any treatment provided.
* **Discharge Instructions** (`discharge_instructions`, `text`, required: false): This page captures any discharge instructions provided by the school health center for the student. Please enter the instructions accurately and thoroughly.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the student's name as it appears on their school record.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the student's grade from 1 to 8. This will help us understand the student's grade level and adjust the information accordingly.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the school's name where the student attends.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the student's health center visit.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the student's health center visit.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the student's contact phone number.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the school's contact phone number.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the school's email address.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the school's address.
* **Student Health Counselor** (`student_health_counselor`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the name and title of the student health counselor.
* **Student Health Center** (`student_health_center`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the name of the student health center.
* **Student Health Counselor Phone** (`student_health_counselor_phone`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the student health counselor's phone number.
* **Student Health Counselor Email** (`student_health_counselor_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the student health counselor's email address.
* **Student Health Counselor Title** (`student_health_counselor_title`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the title of the student health counselor.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any information, please do not hesitate to contact the school health center or the student's health counselor.
* This form is for internal use only, please do not share it with anyone outside of the school.
