# Back To School Health Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect health-related information from students returning to school. It's essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure the student's health and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant sections to fill out, which are marked with `--`.
2. Fill out each field as prompted, providing accurate and complete information.
3. Use the provided options for select fields, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's full name, including first, middle, and last names.
* **Date of Birth** (`student_dob`, date, required): Enter the student's date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Grade** (`student_grade`, select_one, required): Select the student's grade level from the provided options (Kindergarten to 8th Grade).
* **Known Allergies** (`known_allergies`, text, optional): List any known allergies, including food, medication, and environmental allergies.
* **Chronic Health Conditions** (`chronic_conditions`, text, optional): List any chronic health conditions, such as asthma, diabetes, or epilepsy.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, text, optional): List any current medications, including dosage and frequency.
* **Immunizations Are Current** (`immunizations_current`, select_one, required): Confirm whether the student's immunizations are current, according to school requirements.
* **Requesting Vaccination Exemption** (`vaccination_exemption`, select_one, optional): Select the reason for requesting a vaccination exemption (No, Yes - Medical, Yes - Religious, Yes - Philosophical).
* **Date of Last Physical Exam** (`last_physical`, date, optional): Enter the date of the student's last physical exam.
* **Primary Care Physician Name** (`physician_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the student's primary care physician.
* **Physician Phone Number** (`physician_phone`, phone, required): Enter the primary care physician's phone number.
* **Parent/Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the student's parent or guardian.
* **Parent Phone Number** (`parent_phone`, phone, required): Enter the parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_phone`, phone, required): Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Declaration Date** (`parent_signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of declaration.
* **I Confirm This Information is Accurate and Complete** (`accuracy_confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm that the provided information is accurate and complete.

**Tips**

* Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* Review each section carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask for help or clarification.
