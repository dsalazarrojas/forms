## Student Wellbeing Research Network Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from students who want to join the Student Wellbeing Research Network, a platform for sharing knowledge and resources related to student wellbeing research. This information will be used to verify the identity of students and ensure that they are eligible to join the network.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your student details in the "Student Details" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Confirm your email address in the "Confirm Email" field.
6. Enter your researcher's phone number in the "Researcher Phone" field.
7. Enter your research institution's name in the "Research Institution" field.
8. Enter your research institution's location in the "Location" field.
9. Enter your researcher's email address in the "Researcher Email" field.
10. Enter any additional notes in the "Researcher Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Student Details
• **Student Details**: Enter your student ID number, student number, or any other unique identifier that identifies you as a student.

### Email
• **Email**: Enter your student email address, which will be used to communicate with you about your registration and any network updates.

### Name
• **Name**: Enter your full name as it should be displayed in the network.

### Phone
• **Phone**: Enter your mobile or landline phone number, which will be used to contact you if needed.

### Confirm Email
• **Confirm Email**: Re-enter your email address to verify that it is correct.

### Researcher Phone
• **Researcher Phone**: Enter your researcher's phone number, which will be used to contact your research team if needed.

### Research Institution
• **Research Institution**: Enter the name of the institution where you are conducting your research.

### Location
• **Location**: Enter the location of your research institution, which will be used to determine your network affiliation.

### Researcher Email
• **Researcher Email**: Enter your researcher's email address, which will be used to communicate with your research team.

### Researcher Note
• **Researcher Note**: Enter any additional information about your research or any relevant notes that you think would be helpful for the network administrators.

### Tips
- Make sure to enter all required fields correctly to avoid any issues with your registration.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the network administrators for assistance.
- Please note that your email address will be used to send you updates and notifications about the network, so make sure to use a valid email address.
