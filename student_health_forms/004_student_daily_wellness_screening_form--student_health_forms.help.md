# student_daily_wellness_screening_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Daily Wellness Screening Form is used to gather information about a student's health and wellness, including symptoms, travel history, and exposure to potential health risks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your student ID number in the "Student ID" field.
2. Enter your name in the "Student Name" field.
3. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
4. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
5. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the question "Fever" and enter the duration of symptoms in the "Symptoms Duration" field if applicable.
6. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the question "Symptoms" and enter the start and end dates of symptoms in the "Symptoms Start Date" and "Symptoms End Date" fields.
7. Enter the travel history in the "Travel History" field.
8. Enter any exposure to potential health risks in the "Exposure" field.
9. Enter the date of travel history in the "Travel History Date" field.
10. Enter the date of exposure in the "Exposure Date" field.
11. Enter the location of travel history in the "Travel History Location" field.
12. Enter the location of exposure in the "Exposure Location" field.
13. Enter the name of your healthcare provider in the "Healthcare Provider" field (optional).
14. Enter any test results in the "Test Result" field (optional).
15. Enter the date of the test in the "Test Date" field (optional).
16. Enter your response in the "Student Response" field.
17. Enter your response in the "Parent Response" field.
18. Enter any comments in the "Student Response Comments" and "Parent Response Comments" fields (optional).
19. Select "Yes" or "No" to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student ID**: Enter your student ID number.
* **Student Name**: Enter your name.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Fever**: Answer "Yes" if you have a fever.
* **Symptoms**: Answer "Yes" if you have any symptoms. Enter the start and end dates of symptoms in the "Symptoms Start Date" and "Symptoms End Date" fields.
* **Travel History**: Enter any travel history.
* **Exposure**: Enter any exposure to potential health risks.
* **Exposure Date**: Enter the date of exposure.
* **Travel History Date**: Enter the date of travel history.
* **Symptoms Duration**: Enter the duration of symptoms (optional).
* **Travel History Location**: Enter the location of travel history.
* **Exposure Location**: Enter the location of exposure.
* **Healthcare Provider**: Enter the name of your healthcare provider (optional).
* **Test Result**: Enter any test results (optional).
* **Test Date**: Enter the date of the test (optional).
* **Student Response**: Enter your response.
* **Parent Response**: Enter your parent's response.
* **Student Response Comments**: Enter any comments from the student (optional).
* **Parent Response Comments**: Enter any comments from the parent (optional).
* **Submit**: Select "Yes" to submit the form.
* **Signature**: Enter your signature.
