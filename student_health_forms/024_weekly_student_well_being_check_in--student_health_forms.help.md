# weekly_student_well_being_check_in - Help Guide

## Purpose

The "weekly_student_well_being_check_in" form is designed to help school staff and administrators understand students' mental health and support needs. By completing this form, students can share their feelings about their mood, stress levels, and experiences with school climate.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, fill in your student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2.  Select your current mood from the "Mood" dropdown menu. Choose one that best describes how you feel at the moment.
3.  Indicate your current stress level using the "Stress Level" field. Select a number that accurately represents your stress level.
4.  Select all the support sources that you currently rely on, such as classmates, teachers, or family members, in the "Support Sources" field.
5.  Check all the self-care actions you've taken during the week, such as exercise, meditation, or reading, in the "Self-Care Actions Taken" field.
6.  Provide any additional comments or thoughts you'd like to share about your well-being in the "Additional Comments" field.
7.  Finally, indicate your overall perception of the school climate, selecting one of "Positive", "Negative", or "Neutral" from the "School Climate" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on school records.
    *   Type: Text field
*   **Mood**: Select one option that best describes how you feel right now:
    *   Type: Select one
    *   Options: Happy, Sad, Angry, Bored, Stressed, Focused, Anxious, Confused, Afraid, Excited
    *   Purpose: Help staff understand your current emotions.
*   **Stress Level**: Indicate your current stress level by selecting a number from 1-10:
    *   Type: Number
    *   Purpose: Help staff assess your current stress level.
*   **Support Needs**: Select all support sources that you currently rely on:
    *   Type: Select multiple
    *   Options: School Support, Family Support, Friend Support, Other
    *   Purpose: Help staff understand who you turn to for support.
*   **Self-Care Actions Taken**: Check all activities you've done for self-care:
    *   Type: Select multiple
    *   Options: Exercise, Meditation, Journaling, Reading, Listening to Music
    *   Purpose: Help staff see how you're taking care of yourself.
*   **Support Sources**: Select all support sources that you've used:
    *   Type: Select multiple
    *   Options: Classmates, Teachers/Staff, Family Members, Friends
    *   Purpose: Help staff understand who you're relying on for support.
*   **Additional Comments**: Share any thoughts or comments about your well-being:
    *   Type: Text field
    *   Purpose: Allow staff to gain a deeper understanding of your experiences.
*   **School Climate**: Indicate your perception of the school climate:
    *   Type: Select one
    *   Options: Positive, Negative, Neutral
    *   Purpose: Help staff understand your overall school experience.
