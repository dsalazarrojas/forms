# Health And Consent Form For Summer Camp - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect health and consent information for summer camp participants. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Camp Title**: Provide a brief title for the summer camp program.
2. **Camp Category**: Choose the category of the summer camp (e.g. "Day Camp", "Residential Camp", etc.).
3. **Camp Description**: Briefly describe the summer camp program and its activities.
4. **Parent Category**: Confirm the category of your child's camp (e.g. if you have selected "Day Camp", select "Day Camp" here as well).
5. **Parent Name**: Enter your name as the parent or guardian.
6. **Camp Parent Contact**: Provide your contact information, including your name and phone number/email.
7. **Medical Condition**: List any pre-existing medical conditions that may affect your child's participation.
8. **Medication Information**: List any prescribed medications your child may be taking, including dosages and administration instructions.
9. **Emergency Contact**: Provide emergency contact information, including the name, phone number, and relationship to the camper.
10. **Medical Record**: Choose whether to share medical information with camp staff or not.
11. **Medical Record Sharing Consent**: Choose who to share medical information with (e.g. camp staff, camp director, etc.).
12. **Allergy Information**: List any allergies or sensitivities your child has, including severity.
13. **Special Needs**: Confirm any special needs or requirements your child may have.

## Tips

* Please complete this form accurately and truthfully to ensure the safety and well-being of all campers.
* If you have any medical conditions, please list them in detail to help our staff provide proper care.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us directly.
* Please make sure to sign and date this form to confirm your consent.
