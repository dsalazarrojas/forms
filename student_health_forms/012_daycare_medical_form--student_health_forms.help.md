# Daycare Medical Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daycare Medical Form is used to gather medical information from students, ensuring their health and well-being while in our care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Fill in all required fields (marked as "required") with accurate and relevant details.
3. For select fields, choose the correct options that apply to the student's situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Health Form (student_health_form, note, required: false):** This is the main form section where you can enter any relevant health information about the student. Please keep this section concise and focused on the student's health status.
* **Health Info (health_info, text, required: false):** Enter any additional health information about the student, such as medical conditions, allergies, or special needs.
* **Files (files, select_multiple, required: false):** Select all files that are relevant to the student's health, such as medical records or test results. You can choose multiple files from the available options.
* **Files2 (files2, select_one, required: true):** Choose one file that is most relevant to the student's health, such as a primary medical condition.
* **Files3 (files3, select_multiple, required: true):** Select all relevant files related to the student's health, but only choose one file that is most critical. You can choose multiple files from the available options.
* **Files4 (files4, select_one, required: false):** Choose one file that is most relevant to the student's health, such as a secondary medical condition.
* **Files5 (files5, select_multiple, required: true):** Select all relevant files related to the student's health, but only choose one file that is most critical. You can choose multiple files from the available options.
* **Files6 (files6, select_one, required: false):** Choose one file that is most relevant to the student's health, such as a medical test result.
* **Healthcare Provider (healthcare_provider, text, required: false):** Enter the name of the healthcare provider responsible for the student's care, if applicable.

## Tips
* Be thorough but concise when filling out the form.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact your supervisor or support team for assistance.
