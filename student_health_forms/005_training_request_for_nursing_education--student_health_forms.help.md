# Training Request For Nursing Education - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request training for nursing education. It's a collection of fields that gather information about the requested training, including the name of the trainee, date of birth, contact number, education level, and other relevant details. This form is typically used by nursing students or professionals to provide their training requirements and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "name" field.
2. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field, using the format MM/DD/YY.
3. Provide your contact number in the "contact_number" field.
4. Select your education level from the options provided in the "Education Level" field.
5. Enter the training area of interest in the "training area of interest" field.
6. Choose the date and time for the training session in the "training_session_date" and "training_session_time" fields.
7. Set the duration of the training session in the "training_session_duration" field.
8. Name your chosen training instructor in the "training_instructor" field.
9. Specify the topic of the training in the "training_topic" field.
10. Enter the training venue in the "training venue" field.
11. Provide a brief description of the training objectives in the "training Objectives" field.
12. List the attendees attending the training in the "attendees_attending" field.
13. Enter the total number of attendees for the training session in the "total_attendees" field.
14. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
15. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
16. Confirm that the information is correct by selecting "Yes" in the "Confirm" field.
17. Finally, select "Yes" or "No" in the "Submit" field to submit your training request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (name, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Contact Number** (contact_number, text, required): Enter your phone number or other contact information.
* **Education Level** (education_level, select_multiple, required): Select your current education level (bachelor, master, or doctor).
* **Training Area of Interest** (training_area_of_interest, text, required): Enter the area of interest for the training (e.g., pediatrics, cardiology, etc.).
* **Training Session Date** (training_session_date, time, required): Choose the date for the training session.
* **Training Session Time** (training_session_time, time, required): Select the time for the training session.
* **Training Session Duration** (training_session_duration, number, required): Enter the duration of the training session in hours or minutes.
* **Training Instructor** (training_instructor, text, required): Name your chosen training instructor.
* **Training Topic** (training_topic, text, required): Specify the topic of the training.
* **Training Venue** (training_venue, text, required): Enter the location of the training.
* **Training Objectives** (training_Objectives, text, required): Provide a brief description of the training objectives.
* **Attendees Attending** (attendees_attending, text, required): List the attendees attending the training.
* **Total Attendees** (total_attendees, number, required): Enter the total number of attendees for the training session.
* **Email** (email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Notes** (notes, note, required): Add any additional comments or notes about the training.
* **Confirm** (confirm, select_one, required): Confirm that the information is correct by selecting "Yes".
* **Submit** (submit, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" to submit your training request.
