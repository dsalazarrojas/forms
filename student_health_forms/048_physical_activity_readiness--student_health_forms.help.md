# Physical Activity Readiness - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess a student's physical activity readiness. The information collected will help the student's health care provider evaluate their suitability for physical activities. The form will be reviewed and discussed with the student's parent/guardian.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Read the form carefully and understand the questions.
* Answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* For questions with options, select the answer that best describes your situation.
* For open-ended questions, please provide clear and concise answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Physical Activity Readiness** (`student_health_form_1`, text, optional): This is the title of the form. Please enter a brief description of the form purpose.
* **Do you have any heart problems?** (`student_health_form_2`, select_one, optional): Please select 'True' if you have any heart problems, or 'False' if you do not.
* **Have you had any of the following?** (`student_health_form_3`, select_multiple, optional): This question allows you to select one or more conditions that apply to you. Please choose the conditions that are relevant to your situation. If none of the options apply, select 'Other' and provide a brief description.
* **Please describe** (`student_health_form_4`, text, optional): Please provide a clear and concise description of your health situation.
* **Please contact** (`student_health_form_5`, email, optional): Enter your email address or phone number where you can be reached for follow-up questions.
