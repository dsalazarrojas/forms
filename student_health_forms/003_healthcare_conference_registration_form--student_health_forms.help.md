# healthcare_conference_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register students for a healthcare conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in your name.
- Enter your email address.
- Specify the conference topic you are interested in.
- Enter your phone number.
- Provide your address.
- Enter your medical ID number (if applicable).
- Select if you will attend the conference.
- Specify your arrival and departure times.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name** (Text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your official ID or documentation.
* **email** (Email, required: false): Enter your official email address.
* **conference_topic** (Text, required: false): Choose the conference topic you are interested in attending.
* **phone_number** (Text, required: false): Enter your primary phone number.
* **address** (Text, required: false): Enter your current address.
* **medical_id_number** (Number, required: false): Enter your medical ID number (if applicable).
* **will_attend** (Select One, required: false): Choose whether you will attend the conference.
* **arrival_time** (Time, required: false): Enter your estimated arrival time.
* **will_arrive_at** (Time, required: false): Enter the time you will arrive at the conference location.

## Tips
- Please ensure your contact information is up-to-date and accurate.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, please specify them in the 'Additional Comments' field (not shown in this form).
- Review your information carefully before submitting the form to avoid errors.
