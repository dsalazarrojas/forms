# municipal_office_machinery_requisition_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The municipal office machinery requisition form is a tool for requesting equipment from the municipal office. This form will guide you through the necessary steps to submit your request accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information and permissions.
2. Select the department that corresponds to your request (Yes/No).
3. Choose the type of equipment you are requesting (Yes/No).
4. Specify the quantity of equipment you need.
5. Indicate the priority of your request (Yes/No).
6. Provide a brief description of the equipment you are requesting.
7. Enter your budget allocation for the equipment (if applicable).
8. Identify any departmental budget allocations for the equipment (if applicable).
9. Specify the status of the equipment you are requesting (Active/Inactive).
10. Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the equipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **department** (`department`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the department that corresponds to your request.
* **equipment_type** (`equipment_type`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose the type of equipment you are requesting.
* **quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional):
	+ Specify the number of equipment you need.
* **priority** (`priority`, select_one, optional):
	+ Indicate the priority of your request (High, Medium, or Low).
* **description** (`description`, text, optional):
	+ Provide a brief description of the equipment you are requesting.
* **budget** (`budget`, number, optional):
	+ Enter your budget allocation for the equipment (if applicable).
* **department_budget** (`department_budget`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Identify any departmental budget allocations for the equipment (if applicable).
* **equipment_status** (`equipment_status`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Specify the status of the equipment you are requesting (Active/Inactive).
* **contact_person** (`contact_person`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the person responsible for the equipment.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter the email address of the person responsible for the equipment.
* **phone_number** (`phone_number`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the equipment.

## Tips
- Ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals before submitting this form.
- Review your previous requests to avoid duplication.
- Contact the municipal office if you have any questions or need further clarification.
- Fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth process.
