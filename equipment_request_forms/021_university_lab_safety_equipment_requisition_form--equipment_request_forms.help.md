# University Lab Safety Equipment Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Lab Safety Equipment Requisition Form is designed to facilitate the request process for equipment needs in your lab department. It helps ensure that the procurement team gets the necessary information to process your requests efficiently and correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your department from the provided options.
2. Enter your name and contact information for follow-up purposes.
3. Choose the type of equipment you need.
4. Specify the quantity and purpose of the equipment required.
5. Check the departmental and lab manager's approval status.
6. Select the date you need the equipment by.
7. Enter any departmental or lab manager comments.
8. Provide a detailed description of the equipment you are requesting.
9. Enter your contact email and phone number for easy communication.
10. Review and submit the form to initiate the request process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lab Department (1)**: Choose the department you belong to (e.g., Department 1, Department 2, Department 3).
* **Contact Name (2)**: Enter your name to ensure we can contact you for follow-up questions or updates.
* **Equipment Type (3)**: Select the type of equipment you are requesting (e.g., Type 1, Type 2, Type 3).
* **Quantity (4)**: Enter the number of units of the equipment you need.
* **Purpose (5)**: Describe the purpose of the equipment you are requesting.
* **Departmental Approval (6)**: Select "Approve" if you have the departmental approval for this request.
* **Lab Manager Approval (7)**: Select "Approve" if you have the lab manager's approval for this request.
* **Date Submitted (8)**: Enter the date you are submitting this request.
* **Date Needed (9)**: Enter the date you need the equipment by.
* **Departmental Approval Date (10)**: Enter the date you received departmental approval.
* **Lab Manager Approval Date (11)**: Enter the date you received lab manager approval.
* **Comments (12)**: Enter any comments related to your request.
* **Equipment Description (13)**: Provide a detailed description of the equipment you are requesting.
* **Contact Email (14)**: Enter your email address for easy communication.
* **Contact Phone (15)**: Enter your phone number for easy communication.
* **Contact Phone Extension (16)**: Enter any phone extension if applicable.
* **Created Date (17)**: This is automatically generated and not editable.
* **Updated Date (18)**: This is automatically generated and not editable.
* **Assigned To (19)**: Enter the person or department assigned to handle your request.
* **Status (20)**: This is automatically generated and not editable.
* **Equipment ID (21)**: This is automatically generated and not editable.
* **Category (22)**: Enter the category of the equipment you are requesting.
* **Location (23)**: Enter the location where the equipment will be used.
* **Lab Manager Comments (24)**: Enter any comments from the lab manager.
* **Departmental Comments (25)**: Enter any comments from the departmental staff.
