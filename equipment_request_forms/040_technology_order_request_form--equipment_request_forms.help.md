# Technology Order Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Technology Order Request Form is used to gather information for technology-related orders. This includes requests for new equipment, upgrade, or replacement of existing technology.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Request Details" field with a brief description of the technology order request.
2. Select the type of equipment needed from the "Equipment Type" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the model of equipment you are requesting in the "Equipment Model" field.
4. Specify the quantity of the equipment needed in the "Quantity" field.
5. Provide a detailed description of the technology order in the "Description" field.
6. Enter the budget for the request in the "Budget" field.
7. Enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email.
8. Optionally, specify your organization, job title, and department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Details** (text): A brief description of the technology order request.
* **Equipment Type** (select_one, options: Laptop, Desktop, Other): Select the type of equipment needed.
* **Equipment Model** (text): Enter the model number of the equipment you are requesting.
* **Quantity** (number): Specify the quantity of the equipment needed.
* **Description** (text): A detailed description of the technology order, including any specific requirements or constraints.
* **Budget** (number): Enter the budget for the request.
* **Contact Name** (text): Your name and title for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (text): Your phone number for easy contact.
* **Contact Email** (email): Your email address for correspondence.
* **Contact Organization** (text): Your organization's name for reference.
* **Department** (text): Your department or team name for internal tracking.
* **Job Title** (text): Your job title for reference.
* **Ext Phone** (text): An alternative phone number for contact purposes.

Note: This form is not designed for immediate purchasing or procurement process. The information collected will be reviewed and processed by the relevant teams, and a follow-up will be made to discuss the request in more detail.
