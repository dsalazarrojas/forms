# Public Safety Equipment Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for public safety department employees to request equipment for their department. It allows you to specify the type of equipment you need, the quantity, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the department that the requested equipment will be used for from the list of available options.
2. Type the name of the equipment you are requesting.
3. Specify the quantity of the equipment you need.
4. Enter the location where the equipment will be used.
5. Describe the purpose of the request.
6. Budget is optional, if applicable.
7. Prioritize your request by selecting "High", "Medium", or "Low" from the list.
8. Add any additional comments or notes about your request.
9. Specify a contact person in case additional questions or information is needed.
10. Select who from your department needs to approve this request.
11. Select if your manager has approved the request.
12. Choose the date when you first requested the equipment.
13. Choose the date when the equipment will be delivered.
14. Provide a status update on the request.
15. Enter your name, so we know who submitted the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Public Safety Equipment Requisition** (`public_safety_equipment_requisition_form`): This is the title of the form, which should not be completed by the user.
* **Equipment Request Form** (`equipment_request_form`): This is a description of the form, which should not be completed by the user.
* **Department** (`department`): Select the department that the requested equipment will be used for. Choose from the list of available options.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`): Type the type of equipment you are requesting, if applicable.
* **Equipment Name** (`equipment_name`): Type the name of the equipment you are requesting.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`): Specify the quantity of the equipment you need. For example, if you need 5 fire extinguishers, enter "5".
* **Location** (`location`): Enter the location where the equipment will be used.
* **Purpose** (`purpose`): Describe the purpose of the request.
* **Budget** (`budget`): If the equipment has a specific budget, enter the amount.
* **Priority** (`priority`): Select the priority level of your request. You can choose from "High", "Medium", or "Low".
* **Remarks** (`remarks`): Add any additional comments or notes about your request.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`): Specify a contact person in case additional questions or information is needed.
* **Departmental Approvals** (`departmental_approvals`): Select who from your department needs to approve this request. You can choose from John, Jane, or Other.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`): Select if your manager has approved the request. You can choose from Approve, Reject, or Pending.
* **Date Requested** (`date_request`): Choose the date when you first requested the equipment.
* **Date Delivered** (`date_delivered`): Choose the date when the equipment will be delivered.
* **Status** (`status`): Provide a status update on the request.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`): Enter your name, so we know who submitted the request.
