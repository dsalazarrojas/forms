# Payment Card Reader Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about payment card readers, including contact details and payment information. It's essential to fill this form out accurately and truthfully to ensure that your request is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide Company Name**: Enter your company's name as it appears on your business cards or letterhead.
2. **Specify Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person we can contact for further information or assistance.
3. **Enter Contact Email**: Enter your company's email address where you can be reached.
4. **Provide Phone Number**: Enter your company's phone number where you can be contacted.
5. **Enter Company Address**: Enter your company's physical address where you can be reached.
6. **Specify Date**: Enter the date you would like to be contacted or when you expect delivery of the equipment reader.
7. **Specify Time**: Enter the time you would like to be contacted or when you expect delivery of the equipment reader.
