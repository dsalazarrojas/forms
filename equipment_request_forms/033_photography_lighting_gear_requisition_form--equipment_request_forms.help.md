# photography_lighting_gear_requisition_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "photography_lighting_gear_requisition_form" is a form used to request photography lighting equipment from a rental service. This form is for photographers who require specialized lighting equipment for a photo shoot and need to rent it for a short-term or long-term period.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the request form with accurate and complete information.
2. Provide a valid contact person's name, phone number, and email address.
3. Answer the questions about the equipment you need, including the type and quantity of equipment, and any specific details or requirements.
4. Provide a desired delivery and pickup date and time.
5. Confirm the return date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester Information (1)**: Enter your name, phone number, and email address as the contact person for the request.
* **Equipment (2)**: Select 'Yes' to indicate that you are requesting equipment, and choose the specific type(s) of equipment needed from the options provided.
* **Equipment Details (3)**: Enter any additional information or details about the equipment requested, such as specifications or special requirements.
* **Desired Delivery Date (4)**: Enter the date you would like to receive the equipment.
* **Desired Time (5)**: Enter the time you would like to receive the equipment.
* **Pickup Date (6)**: Enter the date you would like to return the equipment.
* **Pickup Time (7)**: Enter the time you would like to return the equipment.
* **Return Date (8)**: Enter the date you would like to return the equipment.
* **Return Time (9)**: Enter the time you would like to return the equipment.
* **Return Notes (10)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the return process.
* **Contact Person (11)**: Enter the name, phone number, and email address of the contact person for the request.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure that the equipment is delivered correctly and on time.
* Confirm the return date and time to avoid any penalties or late fees.
* Be prepared to provide additional information or clarification if needed.
