# Lighting System Power Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a lighting system power request, enabling the efficient and accurate fulfillment of your project's power needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide a clear and concise description of your project, including its title, type, and purpose.
2. Specify the power needs of your project, including the amount of power required.
3. Identify the location where the power system will be used.
4. Provide the dates when the power system will be used.
5. If applicable, mention any facilities team, engineering team, or event team involved in the project.
6. Indicate the status of your power request.
7. Specify the tool assigned to fulfill your power needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **project_details** (`project_details`, text, required): This field is used to describe the project in a brief and concise manner, including its title, type, and purpose.
* **power_needs** (`power_needs`, text, required): This field is used to specify the power requirements of your project.
* **location** (`location`, text, required): This field is used to identify the location where the power system will be used.
* **usage_dates** (`usage_dates`, text, required): This field is used to provide the dates when the power system will be used.
* **facilities_team** (`facilities_team`, text, required): This field is used to mention any facilities team involved in the project.
* **engineering_team** (`engineering_team`, text, required): This field is used to mention any engineering team involved in the project.
* **event_team** (`event_team`, text, required): This field is used to mention any event team involved in the project.
* **event_date** (`event_date`, date, required): This field is used to specify the date of the event.
* **event_time** (`event_time`, time, required): This field is used to specify the time of the event.
* **power_draw** (`power_draw`, text, optional): This field is used to specify any additional power requirements.
* **power_notes** (`power_notes`, text, optional): This field is used to provide any additional comments or notes about your power needs.
* **power_request_date** (`power_request_date`, date, optional): This field is used to specify the date when you first submitted this form. 
* **power_request_status** (`power_request_status`, select one, required): This field is used to indicate the status of your power request.
* **assigned_tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required): This field is used to specify the tool assigned to fulfill your power needs.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out this form accurately and completely to avoid any confusion or delays in fulfilling your power needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please contact the form creators at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
