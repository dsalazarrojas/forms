# University Lab Safety Gear Order Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by university lab staff to request safety gear for use in various laboratories.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant laboratory(s) for your request.
2. Choose the relevant equipment type(s) you need.
3. Select the desired fulfillment date for your request.
4. Provide any additional comments or information that may be helpful in fulfilling your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Laboratory Information** (page 2): This field allows you to select all relevant laboratories where you will be using the requested equipment. Please choose all applicable laboratories from the list. If you need equipment for a lab not listed, please contact [your contact information].
* **Laboratory Equipment Details** (page 3): This field allows you to select all relevant equipment types you require. Please choose all applicable equipment from the list. If you need equipment not listed, please contact [your contact information].
* **Ordering Information** (page 4): This field allows you to select the desired fulfillment date for your request. Choose the date that best fits your needs.
* **Additional Comments** (page 4): This field is for any additional information you would like to provide about your request. Please use this space to explain any further details that may be helpful in fulfilling your request.

## Tips
* Please ensure you are authorized to submit a request on behalf of your lab.
* Ensure you have included all relevant information to help fulfill your request accurately and efficiently.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact [your contact information].
