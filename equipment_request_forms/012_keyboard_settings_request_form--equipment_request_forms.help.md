# Keyboard Settings Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect user information for keyboard settings requests. It helps us understand your keyboard needs and preferences, ensuring we can provide the right support and resources to meet your requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your User Name to identify yourself.
* Select your department from the drop-down menu.
* Choose the type of keyboard you are requesting (e.g., Mechanical, Laptop, or Desktop).
* If you have any special accessibility needs, please describe them in the note field.
* Select the configuration of your preferred keyboard (e.g., QWERTY, Dvorak, or Colemak).
* Indicate your request's priority level (High, Medium, or Low).
* Add any additional notes about your request.
* Enter the date you submitted the request (this will be populated automatically).
* Enter your submitted by name and email address (this will be populated automatically for users).
* Enter your Employee ID (for internal purposes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name**: Enter your full name as it appears in our system.
* **Department**: Select your department from the provided options. This will help us understand where you work.
* **Keyboard Type**: Choose the type of keyboard you are requesting (Mechanical, Laptop, or Desktop).
* **Accessibility Needs**: If you have any special accessibility needs or requirements, describe them here. This will help us understand your needs and provide support accordingly.
* **Keyboard Configuration**: Select the configuration of your preferred keyboard (QWERTY, Dvorak, or Colemak).
* **Priority**: Indicate your request's priority level. If you need urgent support, select High. Otherwise, Medium or Low will do.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information about your request. This can be used to clarify your needs and preferences.
* **Date**: This will be populated automatically once you submit the form. It helps us track the timeline of your request.
* **Submitted By**: This will be populated automatically for users. It's not editable.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted about your request.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your Employee ID for internal purposes.

## Tips
* Please make sure to submit a new request if you need to update or change your previous request.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or IT department for assistance.
