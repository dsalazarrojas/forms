The purpose of this form is for faculty and staff to request academic equipment for their research, teaching, or other academic purposes. The form ensures that these requests are properly reviewed and approved, and that there is a clear understanding of the equipment's intended use and funding source. This helps to prevent duplication of requests and ensures that equipment is allocated fairly and efficiently.

This form will help guide you through the process of submitting a request for academic equipment. To ensure that your request is processed correctly, please follow the instructions below.

## Academic Equipment Requisition Form - Help Guide
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling in your contact information, including your name and email address.
2.  Select the department and your position within that department.
3.  Choose the type of equipment you are requesting (e.g., Laboratory Equipment, Computer Hardware, etc.).
4.  Provide specific details about the equipment, including its name and manufacturer.
5.  Specify the quantity of equipment required.
6.  Enter an estimated cost per unit of equipment.
7.  Describe how you plan to use the equipment.
8.  Indicate your priority level, which will help us understand its urgency.
9.  Finally, confirm if special approval is required for this request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Academic Equipment Requisition Request**: This is the introduction note for the form.
*   **Requester Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your business card or ID badge.
*   **Requester Email**: Enter your institutional email address.
*   **Department**: Choose the academic department or unit you belong to.
*   **Position/Title**: Indicate your role within the department.
*   **Equipment Category**: Choose the type of equipment you are requesting from the given options.
*   **Equipment Name**: Enter the specific name or model of the equipment.
*   **Manufacturer**: If known, enter the company that makes the equipment.
*   **Model Number**: If known, enter the specific model number of the equipment.
*   **Quantity Needed**: Enter the number of units of equipment you require.
*   **Estimated Cost Per Unit**: Enter an approximate cost for one unit of equipment.
*   **Funding Source**: Choose where the funds for the equipment will come from.
*   **Justification for Request**: Describe why this equipment is necessary for academic purposes.
*   **Intended Use**: Describe how the equipment will be used in your research, teaching, or other academic activities.
*   **Priority Level**: Choose the urgency level of your request from the given options.
*   **Approval Required**: If your request requires special approval, choose the relevant option.

Note: This guide is intended to be a practical help for end-users. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us for further assistance.
