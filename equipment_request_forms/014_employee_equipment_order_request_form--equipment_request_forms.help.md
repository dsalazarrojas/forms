# Employee Equipment Order Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Equipment Order Request Form is a tool for employees to submit requests for new equipment. This form is used by the equipment team to determine the need for new equipment, allocate resources, and ensure that the right equipment is delivered to the right people.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the new Hire Equipment Request field with your employee ID or a brief introduction to your role or project.
2. Select the type of equipment you are requesting (e.g., Desk, Laptop, Chair, Paper, or Other).
3. Indicate the quantity of equipment you need.
4. Choose the specific equipment you are requesting (e.g., Desk, Laptop, Chair, Paper, or Other).
5. Provide a brief reason for your equipment request.
6. Enter your contact information, including your email address.
7. Select the department you belong to (e.g., Finance, Marketing, Sales, or Other).
8. If necessary, add a project title related to your equipment request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hire Equipment Request** (`new_hire_equipment_request`): A brief introduction to your role or project.
	+ Please enter your employee ID or a brief introduction to your role or project.
* **New Equipment Type** (`new_equipment_type`): Select the type of equipment you are requesting.
	+ Choose from the options: Desk, Laptop, Chair, Paper, or Other.
* **Quantity of Equipment** (`quantity_of_equipment`): Indicate the number of equipment you need.
	+ Enter a numerical value.
* **Equipment Requested** (`equipment_requested`): Choose the specific equipment you are requesting.
	+ Select from the options: Desk, Laptop, Chair, Paper, or Other.
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`): Provide a brief explanation for your equipment request.
	+ Please be specific about why you need the equipment.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`): Enter your contact information, including your email address.
	+ This will help the equipment team contact you for further discussion or clarification.
* **Department** (`department`): Select the department you belong to.
	+ Choose from the options: Finance, Marketing, Sales, or Other.
* **Project Title** (`project_title`): If related to a specific project, enter the project title.
	+ This will help the equipment team understand the project context.

## Tips

* Please be specific and accurate when filling out the form to ensure that your request is fulfilled correctly.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with the equipment team for guidance.
* Make sure to submit your request with all required fields filled in to avoid delays.
