# University Sports Equipment Order Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Sports Equipment Order Request Form is designed to collect information about the equipment needed for sports activities and events. This form helps the university to manage and allocate equipment effectively, ensuring that the right equipment is available for use when needed. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in your user information in the User Information page (page 1)
* Select one or more equipment types from the list on the Equipment Type page (page 2)
* Enter a brief description of the equipment needed on the Description page (page 3)
* Indicate the quantity of equipment required on the Quantity page (page 4)
* Choose a pickup date for the equipment on the Pickup Date page (page 5)
* Add any additional comments on the Comments page (page 6)
* Choose the assigned tool from the dropdown list on the Assigned Tool page (page 7)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (page 1)** (`user`, `text`, optional): Enter your name and contact information to allow the equipment team to contact you about your request.
* **Equipment Type (page 2)** (`equipment_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the type of equipment you need (Tennis Racket, Basketball Hoop, Volleyball Net, or Other). You can choose one or multiple equipment types as needed.
* **Description (page 3)** (`description`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of the equipment needed, including any specific requirements or special requests.
* **Quantity (page 4)** (`quantity`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of equipment items needed.
* **Pickup Date (page 5)** (`pickup_date`, `date`, optional): Choose a date for the equipment to be picked up.
* **Comments (page 6)** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or requests related to the equipment.
* **Assigned Tool (page 7)** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, optional): Select the person or department responsible for the equipment (Chatjimmy, or Other).

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when selecting the equipment type and quantity needed.
* If you need equipment for a recurring event or long-term use, please indicate the start and end dates of the event or the duration of use.
* If you have any special requests or requirements for the equipment, please include them in the comments field.
* Review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required information is provided. 

Note: This guide is intended to be a basic explanation of the form's fields. If you need further assistance or clarification, please contact the relevant department or the form administrator.
