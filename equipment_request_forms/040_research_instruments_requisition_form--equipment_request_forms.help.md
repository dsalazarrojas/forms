# Research Instruments Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Research Instruments Requisition Form is a tool to request and manage equipment or instruments needed for research projects. This form helps ensure that all necessary information is collected and approved before the procurement process begins.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete the form, please fill out each section carefully and completely.
- Select the correct category for your request (Equipment Request Forms or Other).
- Provide a brief description of the equipment or instrument needed.
- Choose whether you are requesting the form for Research Instruments Requisition Form or Other (if applicable).
- Specify the date and time when you would like to purchase or use the equipment or instrument.
- If applicable, indicate if you are requesting the equipment or instrument for a specific project or purpose.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category (Equipment Request Forms/Other)**: Choose the category that best fits your request. If unsure, select "Other" for general inquiries.
* **Description**: Please provide a brief description of the equipment or instrument needed, including its intended use and any specific features or requirements.
* **Research Instrument Request Form**: Select "Research Instruments Requisition Form" if this form is for research purposes only.
* **Research Instrument Request Form 2**: Select "Yes" if you are requesting the form for research purposes only, or "No" if not.
* **Purchase Request Date**: Enter the date when you would like to purchase the equipment or instrument.
* **Purchase Request Time**: Enter the time when you would like to purchase the equipment or instrument.
* **Research Instrument Request Form 2**: This field is used to further categorize your request. If you are unsure, select "Other".
