# Tech Conference AV Equipment Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tech Conference AV Equipment Requisition Form is designed to collect information from presenters and organizers about the audio-visual equipment needed for a tech conference. This form helps ensure that all equipment requests are properly reviewed and approved before the conference, reducing duplication of requests and meeting presenters' needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and fill out all required fields.
2. Provide accurate and clear information about your equipment needs.
3. Submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title** (`Tech Conference AV Equipment Requisition Form`): This is the title of the form. You can ignore this field and move on to the next section.
* **Presenter Details** (`presenter_details`): In this field, please provide a brief description of your presentation, including any necessary context or background information.
* **Equipment Requests** (`equipment_requests`): This field allows you to specify if you need any AV equipment for your presentation. Please choose "Yes" if you need equipment, and "No" if you don't.
* **Conference Details** (`conference_details`): If you have any specific requests or requirements for your presentation, please include them in this field.
* **Support Requirements** (`support_requirements`): If you need any technical support for your presentation, please provide details on what kind of support you require.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): Please provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number, so we can reach out to you with any further questions or concerns.
* **Confirmation** (`confirmation`): Please confirm that the information you've provided is accurate.
* **Presenter Confirmation** (`presenter_confirmation`): If you are a presenter, please confirm that you are indeed a presenter.
* **Submit** (`submission`): Once you've completed all the required fields, click "Submit" to send your form.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your needs are met.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We will review and approve your equipment requests based on your form submission.
