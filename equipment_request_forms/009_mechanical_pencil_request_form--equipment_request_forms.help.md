# Mechanical Pencil Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mechanical Pencil Request Form is used for requesting mechanical pencils for various purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Provide your name in the User field.
3. Select your department from the Department dropdown menu.
4. Enter the quantity of mechanical pencils you are requesting.
5. Specify the reason for your request in the Reason field.
6. Choose the date you would like to pick up your pencils from the Date field.
7. Select the time you would like to pick up your pencils from the Time field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User (1)**: Enter your name, which will help us identify you.
* **Department (2)**: Select the department you belong to so that we can process your request correctly.
* **Quantity (3)**: Enter the number of mechanical pencils you need.
* **Reason (4)**: Describe briefly why you need mechanical pencils. This helps us understand your request.
* **Date (5)**: Choose a date when you would like to pick up your mechanical pencils. This ensures timely pick-up.
* **Time (6)**: Select the time when you would like to pick up your mechanical pencils.
