# Food Innovation Lab Equipment Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Innovation Lab Equipment Order Form is a tool for requesting equipment from the food innovation lab. To use this form, please follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Complete each field according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information (Page 1)
* **General Information** (`General Information`, `text`, required/optional: False):
	+ This field is for providing any relevant general information about your request.
* **Equipment Details** (`Equipment Details`, `text`, required/optional: False):
	+ This field is for describing the specific equipment you are requesting.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required/optional: False):
	+ This field is for providing your contact information so that we can reach out to you regarding your request.

## Tips
- Please be as clear and concise as possible when providing information in each field.
- If you have any questions or need help with the form, don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
- Ensure you have all necessary information before submitting the form to avoid multiple submissions.
