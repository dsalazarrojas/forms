The "Video Game Workstation Requisition Form" is used to submit a request for equipment related to video game workstations, such as video game consoles, chairs, and other gaming accessories. This form is a mandatory part of the equipment request process, and all fields must be filled out accurately for the request to be processed.

## Purpose
This form is used to provide a standardized way for departments to request equipment related to video game workstations. The form ensures that all necessary information is gathered in a clear and concise manner, making it easier for the equipment team to process and fulfill the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Choose the department that will be using the equipment.
2. Select the type of equipment you are requesting (e.g., video game console, chair, etc.).
3. Enter the number of units you need (if applicable).
4. Choose the request date for the equipment.
5. Indicate the priority level of the request (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
6. Enter the email address of the department contact person.
7. Check the status of the equipment (e.g., Available, In use, Reserved, Lost, Broken).
8. Add any additional comments or information about the request.
9. Approve or decline the request by choosing the correct option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Department** (`Department of Information Technology`, `Department of Human Resources`, `Department of Management`, `Department of Finance`, `Department of Marketing`, `Department of Sales`, `Department of Operations`, `Department of Customer Support`, `Department of Production`, `Department of Quality Assurance`): Choose the department that will be using the requested equipment.
* **Equipment Type** (`Video Game Console`, `Video Game Chair`, `Gaming Keyboard`, `Gaming Mouse`, `Gaming Monitor`, `Gaming Headset`, `Gaming Controller`, `Gaming Desk`): Select the type of equipment you are requesting.
* **Equipment Quantity** (`Number of units of equipment`): Enter the number of units of equipment you need (if applicable).
* **Request Date** (`date`): Choose the date when you need the equipment to be available.
* **Priority Level** (`High`, `Medium`, `Low`): Indicate the priority level of the request (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
* **Departmental Contact** (`email`): Enter the email address of the department contact person.
* **Equipment Status** (`Available`, `In use`, `Reserved`, `Lost`, `Broken`): Check the status of the equipment being requested.
* **Comments** (`Any additional information about this request.`): Add any additional comments or information about the request.
* **Approved by Manager** (`Approved`, `Declined`, `Pending Review`): Approve or decline the request by choosing the correct option.
