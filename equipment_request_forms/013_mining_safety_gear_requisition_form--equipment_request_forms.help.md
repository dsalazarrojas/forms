# Mining Safety Gear Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mining Safety Gear Requisition Form is used to request the use of specific safety gear for work assignments. This form ensures that the equipment is tracked and accounted for, and is a requirement for all work assignments that involve the use of safety gear.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Determine if you need to use safety gear for your work assignment.
2. Answer "Yes" to the "employee_request" field if you need to use safety gear.
3. If you need to use safety gear, enter the item number in the "item_number" field.
4. Describe the item you are requesting in the "item_description" field.
5. Select the category of the item you are requesting in the "item_category" field.
6. Answer "Yes" to the "requested_by" field if you are requesting the gear.
7. Answer "Yes" to the "supervisor_review" field if your supervisor needs to review your request.
8. Answer "Yes" to the "submitted_by" field if you are submitting this form.
9. Enter the date you are submitting this form in the "submit_date" field.
10. If submitting at a specific time, enter it in the "submit_time" field.
11. Select the equipment location if you know where the equipment will be located.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_request** (Yes/No):
	+ This field asks if you need to request safety gear for your work assignment.
* **item_number**:
	+ This field is for entering the item number of the safety gear you need.
* **item_description**:
	+ Describe the safety gear you are requesting in this field.
* **item_category** (Yes/No):
	+ This field asks if the item you are requesting falls into one of the listed categories.
* **requested_by** (Yes/No):
	+ This field indicates that you are requesting the safety gear.
* **supervisor_review** (Yes/No):
	+ This field indicates that your supervisor needs to review your request.
* **submitted_by** (Yes/No):
	+ This field indicates that you are submitting this form.
* **submit_date**:
	+ Enter the date you are submitting this form in this field.
* **submit_time**:
	+ If submitting at a specific time, enter it in this field.
* **equipment_location** (Yes/No):
	+ This field indicates if you know where the equipment will be located.
