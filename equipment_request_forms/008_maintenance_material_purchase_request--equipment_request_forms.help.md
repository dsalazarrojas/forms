# maintenance_material_purchase_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "maintenance_material_purchase_request" form is used to submit a request for the purchase of maintenance materials or equipment. Fill out this form to submit a request to the responsible department or team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*). These fields are: Requestor Name, Contact E-mail, Contact Phone, Purchase Type, Quantity, and Request Date.
2. Select the appropriate option for "Purchase Type" from the dropdown menu. You can choose either "Supplies" or "Equipment".
3. Enter the quantity of the material or equipment being requested.
4. If you are requesting an equipment, provide a detailed description of the equipment you are requesting.
5. Enter the unit cost of the material or equipment.
6. Select the date you would like to submit the request.
7. Select who the request is being made on behalf of ("John", "Jane", or "Jim").
8. Enter the department related to the request.
9. If applicable, enter the location where the material or equipment will be used.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requestor Name**: Enter your name as the person submitting the request.
* **Contact E-mail**: Enter your work or contact email address.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your work or contact phone number.
* **Purchase Type**: Select either "Supplies" or "Equipment" to indicate what type of item you are requesting.
* **Equipment Description**: If you are requesting equipment, provide a detailed description of the item being requested.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of items you are requesting.
* **Unit Cost**: Enter the cost per unit of the item being requested.
* **Request Date**: Enter the date you would like to submit the request.
* **Requested By**: Select who the request is being made on behalf of ("John", "Jane", or "Jim").
* **Department**: Enter the department related to the request.
* **Location**: If you are requesting equipment, enter the location where the item will be used.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the request.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the responsible department or team for assistance.
* Please note that this form is for internal use only and is not intended for external requests.
