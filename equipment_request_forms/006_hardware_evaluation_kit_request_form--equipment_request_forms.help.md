# Hardware Evaluation Kit Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from users regarding their requests for hardware evaluation kits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have a legitimate reason for requesting the hardware evaluation kit.
2.  Review the form sections to understand what information is required for each.
3.  Complete each field accurately and thoroughly.
4.  Submit the form once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`General Information`, `text`, optional): This field is for providing a brief description of your request.
* **User Info** (`User Info`, `text`, optional): Please enter your name and any other relevant user information.
* **Product Information** (`Product Information`, `text`, optional): Describe the product you are requesting evaluation for.
* **Request Details** (`Request Details`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional details or context regarding your request.
* **Delivery Information** (`Delivery Information`, `text`, optional): Specify the delivery address and any other relevant shipping details.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact information for follow-up and communication.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes regarding your request.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, `text`, optional): This is the final step to submit your request.

## Tips
- Be sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
- Ensure that your request is for a legitimate reason, and not for personal use or commercial purposes.
