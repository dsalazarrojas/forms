# Stage Microphone Replacement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stage Microphone Replacement Request Form is a tool for reporting and addressing stage microphone issues that require replacement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information.
2. Provide a clear description of the issue with the microphone.
3. Attach any relevant photos or screenshots if required.
4. Select the urgency level of your request.
5. Specify the technician or staff member you would like to handle your request.
6. Add any additional staff notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **request_urgency** (Required): Choose the level of urgency for your request:
	+ Low: Non-urgent issue that can be addressed at a later stage.
	+ Medium: Urgent issue that needs attention within a few hours or days.
	+ High: Emergency or critical issue that requires immediate attention.
* **attach_photos** (Optional): Attach a photo or screenshot of the faulty microphone for better understanding.
* **describe_the_issue** (Required): Provide a detailed description of the microphone issue you are experiencing.
* **requesting_technician** (Required): Specify the name of the technician or staff member you would like to handle your request.
* **staff_notes** (Optional): Add any additional notes or comments for the staff member handling your request.
