# Laptop Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for employees to submit requests for a laptop for work-related purposes. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill in your employee's name in the "employee" field.
2. Indicate your department in the "employee_department" field.
3. Select the type of laptop you need for your work in the "laptop_type" field.
4. If necessary, provide the level of urgency for the laptop request in the "urgency" field.
5. If you have a specific delivery date in mind, enter it in the "delivery_date" field.
6. If you have any comments regarding your request, type them in the "comments" field.
7. Enter the tool or software that you are assigning the laptop to in the "assigned_tool" field.
8. Optionally, provide additional comments for the form in the "laptop_requisition_form_comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **employee**:
	+ Enter your employee ID or name as required.
- **employee_department**:
	+ Select "Yes" if your department is requesting the laptop. 
- **laptop_type**:
	+ Select multiple types of laptops you need, separated with commas, e.g., "Laptop A, Laptop B".
- **urgency**:
	+ If you need the laptop urgently, select the number indicating the level of urgency (e.g., 1-5).
	+ This field is optional and will help the requester and approvers understand the level of priority.
- **delivery_date**:
	+ Enter the date you would like to receive the laptop, in "YYYY-MM-DD" format.
	+ This field is optional and will help the requester and approvers understand when they need the laptop.
- **comments**:
	+ Provide any additional comments or information about your request.
	+ This field is optional and will help the requester and approvers understand your needs.
- **assigned_tool**:
	+ Enter the tool or software that you are assigning the laptop to as required.
- **laptop_requisition_form_comments**:
	+ Provide any additional comments or information about your request.
	+ This field is optional and will help the requester and approvers understand your needs.

## Tips
- Be specific about the type and quantity of laptop you need.
- Attach supporting documentation if necessary.
- Be clear about your request's purpose and urgency.
