# Medical Equipment Loan Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Medical Equipment Loan Request Form is used to request the loan of medical equipment from other facilities. This form helps the loaner understand the borrower's needs and ensure the equipment is loaned to the right person.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the request details, including your name and contact information.
2. Select the type and location of equipment needed.
3. Specify the loan duration and reason for the request.
4. Enter the loaner's name and contact details.
5. Add any additional comments or requests.
6. Select the loan status and who the equipment was loaned to.
7. Select whether the loan was returned on time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Request Details** (`request_details`): <i>Enter a brief description of the equipment you are requesting to help the loaner understand your needs.</i>
- **Borrower Name** (`borrower_name`): <i>Enter your name as the person requesting the loan.</i>
- **Loan Date** (`loan_date`): <i>Enter the date on which you would like the loan to start.</i>
- **Equipment Requested** (`equipment_requested`): <i>Select the type of equipment you need from the list of available options.</i>
- **Equipment Location** (`equipment_location`): <i>Select all relevant locations where the equipment will be used.</i>
- **Loan Duration** (`loan_duration`): <i>Enter the duration of the loan you are requesting in days.</i>
- **Reason for Loan** (`reason_for_loan`): <i>Briefly explain why you are requesting the loan.</i>
- **Loaner Name** (`loaner_name`): <i>Enter the name of the person or department responsible for the loan.</i>
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`): <i>Enter any contact information for the person or department requesting the loan.</i>
- **Comments** (`comments`): <i>Add any additional comments or requests.</i>
- **Loan Status** (`loan_status`): <i>Select the current status of your loan request.</i>
- **Loaned To** (`loaned_to`): <i>Select who the equipment was loaned to (Borrower or Loaner).</i>
- **Loan Returned** (`loan_returned`): <i>Select whether the loan was returned on time.</i>
