# Medical Equipment Loan Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Medical Equipment Loan Request Form is used to request the loan of medical equipment from other facilities. This form helps the loaner understand the borrower's needs and ensure the equipment is loaned to the right person.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the request details, including your name and contact information.
2. Select the type and location of equipment needed.
3. Specify the loan duration and reason for the request.
4. Enter the loaner's name and contact details.
5. Add any additional comments or requests.
6. Select the loan status and who the equipment was loaned to.
7. Select whether the loan was returned on time.
