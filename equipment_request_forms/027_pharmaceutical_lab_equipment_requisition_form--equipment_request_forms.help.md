# Pharmaceutical Lab Equipment Requisition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used by lab staff or researchers to request lab equipment, provide details for inventory tracking, and help manage equipment quantity.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the question of whether the equipment is requested or not by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "lab_equipment_request" field.
2. If the equipment is requested, enter the quantity of the equipment needed in the "quantity_issued" field.
3. Provide the date the equipment was issued in the "date_issued" field.
4. Select the location where the equipment will be used from the options provided in the "location" field.
5. Check the equipment status by selecting "Active" or "Inactive" in the "equipment_status" field.
6. If necessary, enter any additional information in the "equipment_request_form_2" field.
7. If additional quantity information is required, enter it in the "quantity_issued_2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lab Equipment Details**: Please enter a brief description of the equipment being requested, including its type and any additional details. (text)
* **Lab Equipment Request**: Select "Yes" if the equipment is requested, otherwise select "No". (select_one)
* **Quantity Issued**: Enter the quantity of the equipment being requested. (number)
* **Date Issued**: Enter the date the equipment was issued. (date)
* **Location**: Select the location where the equipment will be used. (select_one)
* **Equipment Status**: Select "Active" if the equipment is functional and in use, otherwise select "Inactive". (select_one)
* **Lab Equipment Request Form 2**: Please enter any additional information about the request, if necessary. (text)
* **Quantity Issued 2**: Enter any additional quantity information, if necessary. (number)

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or contact the lab equipment manager for assistance.
* This form is used for internal purposes only, please do not submit it without approval from a supervisor or manager.
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
