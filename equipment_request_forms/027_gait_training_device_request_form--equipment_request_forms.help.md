# Gait Training Device Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to streamline the process of requesting a gait training device. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your request is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please complete this form in its entirety, ensuring all required fields are filled out.
- If you are requesting a gait device for yourself, please fill out the patient information section.
- If you are a healthcare provider requesting a gait device for a patient, please fill out the patient information and submitter information section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (First Name, text, optional): Enter your first name.
  - This field is optional but recommended for identification purposes.
- **Last Name** (Last Name, text, optional): Enter your last name.
  - This field is optional but recommended for identification purposes.
- **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
  - This field is optional but recommended for medical history purposes.
- **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address.
  - This field is optional but recommended for communication purposes.
- **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
  - This field is optional but recommended for communication purposes.
- **Gait Device Type** (Gait Device Type, select_one, required): Select the type of gait device you are requesting.
  - Please choose the correct gait device type from the options provided.
- **Gait Device Model** (Gait Device Model, text, optional): Enter the model of gait device you are requesting.
  - This field is optional but recommended for detailed information purposes.
- **Medical History** (Medical History, note, optional): Enter any relevant medical history.
  - This field is optional but recommended for medical history purposes.
- **Request Date** (Request Date, date, optional): Enter the date you are requesting the gait device.
  - This field is optional but recommended for tracking purposes.
- **Submitter** (Submitter, text, optional): Enter your name.
  - This field is optional but recommended for identification purposes.
- **Submitter Phone** (Submitter Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
  - This field is optional but recommended for communication purposes.
- **Submitter Email** (Submitter Email, email, optional): Enter your email address.
  - This field is optional but recommended for communication purposes.
- **Reviewer** (Reviewer, text, optional): Enter the reviewer's name.
  - This field is optional but recommended for identification purposes.
- **Review Phone** (Review Phone, text, optional): Enter the reviewer's phone number.
  - This field is optional but recommended for communication purposes.
- **Review Email** (Review Email, email, optional): Enter the reviewer's email address.
  - This field is optional but recommended for communication purposes.
- **Clinic** (Clinic, text, optional): Enter the name of the clinic.
  - This field is optional but recommended for location tracking purposes.
- **Therapist** (Therapist, text, optional): Enter the name of the therapist.
  - This field is optional but recommended for identification purposes.
- **Department** (Department, text, optional): Enter the department name.
  - This field is optional but recommended for location tracking purposes.
- **Location** (Location, text, optional): Enter the location name.
  - This field is optional but recommended for location tracking purposes.
- **Submitted By** (Submitted By, select_multiple, required): Select the option that best describes who submitted the request.
  - Please choose the correct option from the options provided.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled out to process your request efficiently. If you have any questions or need clarification, please contact the relevant contact person.
