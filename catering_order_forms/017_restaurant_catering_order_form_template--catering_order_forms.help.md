# Restaurant Catering Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Catering Order Form Template is designed to collect information from customers for catering orders. This form helps restaurant staff gather the necessary details about the customer, their preferences, and order requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of food the customer would like to order from the "Main" section.
2. Enter the customer's full name in the "Customer Name" field.
3. Enter the customer's contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
4. Choose the time for the order in the "Order Time" field.
5. Select the food choices from the "Food Choice" field.
6. Enter the quantity of the order in the "Quantity" field.
7. If necessary, add any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
8. Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main**: Select the type of food the customer would like to order.
	+ Please choose from the options: Apple, Banana, or Orange.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's full name.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the customer's contact number.
* **Order Time**: Choose the time for the order.
* **Food Choice**: Select the food the customer would like to order.
	+ Please choose from the options: Chicken Wings, Salad, or Soup.
* **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the order.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the order.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email address.
## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly to avoid any issues with order processing.
- Double-check the accuracy of the customer information before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact the restaurant staff.
