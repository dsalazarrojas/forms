# Catering Service Operation Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a catering business's operation details to ensure compliance with local regulations and safety standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the applicant's information, including their name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide the business's information, including the business name, business license number, and business registration date.
3. Select the type of catering business (e.g., full-service, buffet, food truck, etc.).
4. Provide the proposed operating location, operating days, and operating hours.
5. Specify the expected daily customers (if applicable).
6. Confirm if the food handler certificate is on file, if the recent health inspection passed, and if liability insurance coverage is in place.
7. Confirm compliance with local sanitation standards, food safety procedures, and emergency procedures.
8. Finally, confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the applicant.
* **Email Address** (`applicant_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the applicant.
* **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the applicant.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the business.
* **Business License Number** (`business_license_number`, text, required): Enter the business license number.
* **Business Registration Date** (`business_registration_date`, date, required): Enter the business registration date.
* **Type of Catering Business** (`business_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of catering business (e.g., full-service, buffet, food truck, etc.).
* **Proposed Operating Location** (`proposed_operating_location`, text, required): Provide the full address of the proposed operating location.
* **Type of Operation** (`operation_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of operation (e.g., on-site, off-site, combined).
* **Operating Days** (`operating_days`, select_multiple, required): Select the days on which the business will operate.
* **Operating Hours** (`operating_hours`, text, required): Enter the operating hours of the business.
* **Expected Daily Customers** (`expected_daily_customers`, number, optional): Enter the expected daily customers (if applicable).
* **Food Handler Certificate on File** (`food_handler_certificate`, select_one, required): Confirm if the food handler certificate is on file.
* **Recent Health Inspection Passed** (`health_inspection_pass`, select_one, required): Confirm if the recent health inspection passed.
* **Liability Insurance Coverage** (`liability_insurance`, select_one, required): Confirm if liability insurance coverage is in place.
* **Comply with local sanitation standards** (`sanitation_standards_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm if the business complies with local sanitation standards.
* **Food Safety Procedures** (`food_safety_procedures`, select_one, required): Confirm if food safety procedures are in place.
* **Emergency Procedures Documented** (`emergency_procedures`, select_one, required): Confirm if emergency procedures are documented.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_acceptance`, select_one, required): Confirm that all information provided is accurate and complete and you agree to the terms and conditions.
