# Catering Contact Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate communication between you and our catering team. It's meant to gather essential information about your event, allowing us to tailor our services to your specific needs and preferences. Please fill out this form to start a conversation with our team and we'll be in touch within 48 business hours to discuss your event details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the `Full Name` field.
2. If applicable, provide your company name in the `Company Name (if applicable)` field.
3. Enter your email address in the `Email Address` field. Please ensure it's a valid email address to which you can be reached.
4. Enter your phone number in the `Phone Number` field.
5. Fill out the `Date of Event (if confirmed)` field with the date of your event if you have it confirmed.
6. Provide an estimated guest count in the `Estimated Guest Count` field.
7. Write a detailed description of your inquiry, menu interests, or questions in the `How can we help you with your event` field.
8. Choose your preferred method of contact in the `Preferred Method of Contact` field. Our team will reach out to you in the method you prefer.
9. If you prefer, select the best time of day to contact you in the `Best Time of Day to Reach You` field.
10. Indicate where you found our contact information in the `Where did you find our contact information` field.
11. Enter an approximate budget range for your event in the `Approximate Budget Range` field (optional).
11. Finally, confirm that you understand our 48-hour response time in the `I understand that my inquiry will be responded to within 48 business hours` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification or business cards.
* **Company Name (if applicable)**: If you're representing a company, please enter your company name. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached. Our team will use this to communicate with you.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further discussions or questions.
* **Date of Event (if confirmed)**: If you have a confirmed event date, please enter it here. This helps us plan ahead.
* **Estimated Guest Count**: Provide an estimated number of guests attending your event.
* **How can we help you with your event**: Describe your event details, menu preferences, or questions you have for our team.
* **Preferred Method of Contact**: Choose how you'd like to be contacted by our team: Email, Phone, or Text Message.
* **Best Time of Day to Reach You**: If you're available, select a time of day when you'd prefer to be contacted by our team.
* **Where did you find our contact information**: Indicate where you found our contact information: Online Search, Social Media, Referral, or Billboard/Signage.
* **Approximate Budget Range** (Optional): If you have a rough idea of your event budget, provide it here.
* **I understand that my inquiry will be responded to within 48 business hours**: Confirm that you're aware of our response time.
