# Corporate Event Catering Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather necessary information about your corporate event so that we can provide you with an accurate quotation for our catering services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the event details, including the event name, date, and location.
2. Provide contact information, including the contact person's name, contact number, and email.
3. Specify the number of guests attending the event.
4. Indicate your menu preferences, such as vegetarian or non-vegetarian options.
5. Add any special requests or requirements for the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Details**: <br>
  Enter the brief details of the event, including any specific details that would be helpful for us to know.
- **Corporate Event Details**: <br>
  Describe the corporate event, including any relevant background information or context.
- **Event Name**: <br>
  Enter a short name for the event that will be used to identify it.
- **Event Date**: <br>
  Enter the date of the event in a standard date format (e.g., 2023-02-15).
- **Event Time**: <br>
  Enter the start and end time of the event in a 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00).
- **Event Location**: <br>
  Enter the location of the event, including the building name, room number, or a specific address.
- **Contact Person**: <br>
  Enter the name and title of the person we should contact about the event.
- **Contact Number**: <br>
  Enter the contact person's phone number that we can reach them at.
- **Email**: <br>
  Enter the contact person's email address.
- **Number of Guests**: <br>
  Enter the estimated number of guests attending the event.
- **Menu Details**: <br>
  Enter any specific menu details, including any dietary restrictions or requirements.
- **Menu Preferences**: <br>
  Select the type of menu you would prefer, such as vegetarian, non-vegetarian, or special requests.
- **Special Requests**: <br>
  Enter any special requests or requirements for the event, such as equipment, decorations, or catering services.
- **Payment Terms**: <br>
  Enter any special payment terms or requirements, such as payment method or due date.
