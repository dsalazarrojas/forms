# Catering Services Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an event or occasion that requires catering services. It helps us understand your needs and preferences to provide the best possible experience and services for you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your contact information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide details about your event, such as the date, type, and location.
3. Select your preferred cuisine type and service style.
4. If you have any special dietary needs or restrictions, please let us know.
5. Estimate your budget per guest, if you have one.
6. Indicate how soon you need our catering services.
7. If you're interested in additional services, select the ones that apply to you.
8. Finally, confirm that you consent to receive follow-up contact from us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Your full name as we should address you.
* **Email Address**: Your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number where we can reach you for further information or questions.
* **Company or Organization**: Your company or organization name (if applicable).
* **Event Date**: The date of your event.
* **Type of Event**: Select the type of event you're planning (e.g., wedding, corporate event, birthday party).
* **Event Location**: The full address of your event location.
* **Expected Number of Guests**: The estimated number of guests attending your event.
* **Preferred Cuisine Type**: Select the type of cuisine you prefer (e.g., American, Italian, Mexican).
* **Preferred Service Style**: Select the service style you prefer (e.g., buffet, plated service, family style).
* **Special Dietary Needs or Restrictions**: If you have any dietary restrictions or preferences, please specify them.
* **Budget Estimate**: If you have an estimated budget per guest, please provide it.
* **Interested in Additional Services**: If you're interested in any additional services, select them from the list.
* **Event Start Time**: The start time of your event (optional).
* **Event End Time**: The end time of your event (optional).
* **Top Priorities**: Select the top priorities for your event (e.g., food quality, variety of options, cost efficiency).
* **Special Requests or Comments**: Any special requests or comments you'd like to share with us.
* **Inquiry Urgency**: Indicate how soon you need our catering services.
* **Consent to Follow-up**: Confirm that you consent to receive follow-up contact from us.

Note: Please answer all required fields to ensure we can provide the best possible services for your event.
