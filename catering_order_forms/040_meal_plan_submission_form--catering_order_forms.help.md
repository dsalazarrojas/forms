# Meal Plan Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meal Plan Submission Form is a tool used to collect information about a client's meal plan preferences, dietary restrictions, and allergies. This form is essential for catering businesses to understand the client's needs and requirements accurately and provide an exceptional dining experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name as the client, ensuring it is spelled correctly to avoid any issues with order processing.
2.  Provide detailed information about your meal plan, including any dietary restrictions and allergies you may have.
3.  Select your preferred meal options and any special instructions for your meal plan.
4.  Enter your contact information so that we can contact you with any questions or updates.
5.  Enter your email address for easy communication and order updates.
6.  Select the person who submitted the order and the date and time of submission for record-keeping purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name as the client for accurate order processing.
  - **Meal Plan Details** (`meal_plan_details`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of your meal plan requirements and preferences.
  - **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, required: false): Select any dietary restrictions you may have, such as vegetarian, gluten-free, or dairy-free.
  - **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_one, required: true): Select any allergies you have, such as shellfish or soy, to ensure your safety.
  - **Dietary Preferences** (`dietary_preferences`, select_multiple, required: true): Select your preferred dietary options, such as vegan, keto, or low-carb.
  - **Meal Plan** (`meal_plan`, text, required: false): Enter any specific meal plan requirements or preferences.
  - **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you would like your meal plan to start.
  - **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you would like your meal plan to end.
  - **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, required: false): Enter any special instructions or requests for your meal plan.
  - **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information for easy communication and order updates.
  - **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for easy communication and order updates.
  - **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about your meal plan.
  - **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required: true): Select the person who submitted the order, such as yourself or another representative.
  - **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of submission for record-keeping purposes.
  - **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of submission for record-keeping purposes.

## Tips
- Double-check your contact information and email address for accuracy to ensure easy communication and order updates.
- Clearly describe your meal plan requirements and preferences to provide the best possible dining experience.
- Selecting the correct submitted by field ensures that the order is attributed to the correct person.
- Enter the correct submission time and date for record-keeping purposes.
