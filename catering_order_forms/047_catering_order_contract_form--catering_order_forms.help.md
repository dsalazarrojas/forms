# Catering Order & Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect event details and contract agreements from clients for catering services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out all required fields, marked with a red asterisk (*).
2. Enter your event date and venue details accurately.
3. Confirm the menu package selected.
4. Provide the guaranteed minimum guest count for the event.
5. Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the contract.
6. Acknowledge the caterer's licenses and insurance status.
7. Understand and agree to the cancellation policy and liability waiver.
8. Confirm your left over food policy.
9. Provide the non-refundable deposit amount.
10. Confirm the final payment due date.
11. Add any additional special requests or agreements if necessary.
12. Sign the form electronically.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Please enter your full legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event for which the catering service is required.
* **Event Venue**: Provide the name of the venue where the event will take place.
* **Confirmed Menu Package**: Select the menu package agreed upon with the client.
* **Guaranteed Minimum Guest Count**: Enter the expected number of guests for the event.
* **License Insurance Acknowledgment**: Confirm that you understand the caterer holds all required licenses and insurance.
* **Cancellation Policy Acknowledgment**: Read and agree to the cancellation policy.
* **Liability Waiver and Indemnification**: Understand and agree to the terms of the liability waiver.
* **Leftover Food Policy**: Understand that leftover food remains the property of the caterer.
* **Non-Refundable Deposit Amount**: Enter the amount to be paid as a deposit.
* **Final Payment Due Date**: Enter the date when the final payment is due.
* **Additional Special Requests or Agreements**: Add any special requests or agreements not already covered in the form.
* **Client Electronic Signature**: Sign the form electronically with your full legal name.
* **Catering Representative Name**: Enter the name of the catering representative.
* **Date of Agreement**: Enter the date when this contract is signed.
