# Chicken Biryani Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chicken Biryani Order Form is designed to collect information for ordering chicken biryani from a catering service. This form is intended for customers who wish to place an order for delivery or pickup.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name, email address, and phone number to contact you for your order.
2. Select your preferred delivery date.
3. Choose your preferred delivery time.
4. Select the type of chicken biryani you would like to order.
5. Choose the spice level of your biryani (Mild, Medium, Hot, Very Hot, or Extra Hot).
6. Enter the number of biryani plates you would like to order.
7. Select the size of your biryani plates (Regular, Medium, Large, or Extra Large).
8. Choose any add-on items you would like to include with your order.
9. Select a beverage to accompany your order (if desired).
10. If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, please enter them in the provided field.
11. If you have any special requests or preferences, please enter them in the provided field.
12. Enter your delivery address, city, and postal code.
13. Select your preferred delivery option (Home delivery, Restaurant pickup, or Office delivery).
14. Enter the estimated total cost of your order.
15. Choose your payment method (Cash on delivery, Credit card, Debit card, Mobile payment, or Bank transfer).
16. Finally, confirm that you agree to the cancellation and delivery policies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (2, text, required): Enter your name as it should be spelled out on your order receipt.
* **Email address** (3, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **Phone number** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you about your order.
* **Preferred delivery date** (5, date, required): Select the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Preferred delivery time** (6, time, required): Select the time you would like to receive your order.
* **Type of chicken biryani** (7, select_one, required): Choose the type of biryani you would like to order (Hyderabadi, Lucknowi, Kolkata, Sindhi, Dum pukht, or Mixed).
* **Spice level preference** (8, select_one, required): Choose the spice level of your biryani (Mild, Medium, Hot, Very hot, or Extra hot).
* **Number of biryani plates** (9, number, required): Enter the number of biryani plates you would like to order.
* **Plate size** (10, select_one, required): Choose the size of your biryani plates (Regular, Medium, Large, or Extra Large).
* **Add-on items** (11, select_multiple, optional): Select any add-on items you would like to include with your order (Raita (yogurt), Pickle (achaar), Salad, Pappad (wafers), Naan bread, Roti, Dessert, Beverages, or None).
* **Beverage choice** (12, select_one, optional): Select a beverage to accompany your order (Water, Soft drink, Iced tea, Mango lassi, Sweet lassi, or No beverage).
* **Dietary restrictions or allergies** (13, text, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, please enter them here.
* **Special requests or preferences** (14, text, optional): If you have any special requests or preferences, please enter them here.
* **Delivery address** (15, text, required): Enter the address where you would like to receive your order.
* **City** (16, text, required): Enter the city where you are located.
* **Postal code** (17, text, required): Enter the postal code for your delivery address.
* **Delivery option** (18, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to receive your order (Home delivery, Restaurant pickup, or Office delivery).
* **Estimated order total** (19, number, required): Enter the estimated total cost of your order.
* **Payment method** (20, select_one, required): Choose your payment method (Cash on delivery, Credit card, Debit card, Mobile payment, or Bank transfer).
* **I agree to the cancellation and delivery policies** (21, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to the cancellation and delivery policies.
