# Curbside Pickup Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Curbside Pickup Order Form is designed to collect information for curbside pickup orders. This form helps us understand your order preferences, including pickup details and customer information. It's recommended to complete this form carefully to ensure accurate processing of your order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "order_details" page and choose between "Yes" and "No" options to specify your order details.
2. On the "customer_info" page, enter your name and any additional customer information as prompted.
3. On the "delivery_time" page, choose a delivery time for your order.
4. On the "delivery_date" page, select a date for your order delivery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details** (`order_details`, select_one, required): Choose whether you have any special order details, such as dietary restrictions or special requests.
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant customer information to help us process your order efficiently.
* **Pickup Time** (`delivery_time`, time, optional): Select a suitable time for your curbside pickup. This can be a convenient time for you and our staff to collect your order.
* **Pickup Date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): Select a date for your curbside pickup. This is the date you'd like to receive your order.
