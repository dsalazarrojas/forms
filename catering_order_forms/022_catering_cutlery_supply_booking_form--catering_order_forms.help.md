# Catering Cutlery Supply Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Catering Cutlery Supply Booking Form is designed to collect information about catering events and ensure that the Catering Cutlery Supply company has the necessary cutlery to provide for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields with your contact information, including your name, company/organization, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the date of the event and the date cutlery is needed.
3. Choose the type of event (e.g. wedding, corporate event, etc.).
4. Enter the expected number of guests and the number of cutlery items needed (e.g. forks, knives, spoons, etc.).
5. Select the cutlery style and any specialty items needed.
6. Choose whether delivery is needed and select the preferred pickup time.
7. Enter any special instructions or requirements.
8. Indicate whether a deposit has been paid.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Name** (1): Your full name as the contact person for the event.
* **Company Name** (2): Your company or organization name.
* **Email Address** (3): Your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (4): Your contact phone number.
* **Booking Date** (5): The date of the event.
* **Event Date** (6): The date cutlery is needed.
* **Return Date** (7): The date when the cutlery will be returned.
* **Event Type** (8): The type of event (e.g. wedding, corporate event, etc.).
* **Number of Guests** (9): The expected number of guests.
* **Forks Needed** (10): The number of forks needed.
* **Knives Needed** (11): The number of knives needed.
* **Spoons Needed** (12): The number of spoons needed.
* **Dessert Forks** (13): The number of dessert forks needed.
* **Serving Spoons** (14): The number of serving spoons needed.
* **Serving Forks** (15): The number of serving forks needed.
* **Specialty Cutlery** (16): Any specialty cutlery items needed.
* **Cutlery Style** (17): The preferred style or finish of the cutlery.
* **Delivery Needed** (18): Whether delivery is needed.
* **Delivery Address** (19): Where to deliver the cutlery.
* **Preferred Pickup Time** (20): When to pick up the cutlery.
* **Special Instructions** (21): Any special requirements or instructions.
* **Deposit Paid** (22): Whether a deposit has been paid.
* **Additional Comments** (23): Any other information about the event.

Note: All fields marked as required are mandatory and must be filled in by the user. Fields marked as optional can be left blank if not applicable.
