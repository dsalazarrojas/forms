# Friday Dinner Menu - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a catering order for the Friday dinner menu.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the order in the "Friends And Family" field.
2. Provide the address for collection in the "Collection Address" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Enter your PayPal email address or transfer details in the "PayPal Transfer" field.
5. Select the collection time for your order from the options in the "Collection Time" field (if available).
6. Provide the collection date for your order in the "Collection Date" field (if applicable).
7. Enter the name of the person ordering the food in the "Order By" field (if desired).
8. Enter the URL of your order in the "Order Link" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Friends And Family
* Friends And Family (`name`, `text`, optional): Enter a title or label for the order.
### Collection Address
* Collection Address (`id`, `text`, optional): Provide the address where you want the order to be collected from.
### Email Address
* Email Address (`id`, `text`, optional): Enter your email address to allow communication.
### PayPal Transfer
* PayPal Transfer (`id`, `text`, optional): Enter your PayPal email address or transfer details.
### Collection Time
* Collection Time (`id`, `text`, optional): Select the time for collection from the available options.
### Collection Date
* Collection Date (`id`, `text`, optional): Provide the date for collection.
### Order By
* Order By (`id`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person ordering the food.
### Order Link
* Order Link (`id`, `text`, optional): Provide the URL of your order.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all relevant fields to ensure accurate order processing.
- If you have any difficulties or questions, contact the form's administrators.
