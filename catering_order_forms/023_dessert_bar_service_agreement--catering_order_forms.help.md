# dessert_bar_service_agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "dessert_bar_service_agreement" form is used to collect information for event catering services. It helps the service team to gather the necessary details for efficient service provision, ensuring a smooth and enjoyable experience for clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure completeness and consistency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **event name** (text, required): Enter the name of the event where the catering service will be provided.
* **event date** (date, not required): Enter the date of the event, if applicable.
* **event time** (time, not required): Enter the time of the event, if applicable.
* **client name** (text, required): Enter the name of the client for whom the catering service will be provided.
* **client email** (email, not required): Enter the email address of the client (if available).
* **client phone** (text, not required): Enter the phone number of the client (if available).
* **client notes** (note, not required): Enter any additional notes or comments from the client.
* **dessert types** (select_multiple, required): Select the types of desserts that will be served at the event (e.g., "Yes" for cake, "No" for cupcakes).
* **num guests** (number, required): Enter the estimated number of guests attending the event.
* **num servers** (number, required): Enter the estimated number of servers required for the event.
* **Approvals** (select_multiple, required): Approve or decline the terms and conditions of the service.
* **Terms and Conditions** (note, required): Review and acknowledge the terms and conditions of the service.

Please note that some fields are required to ensure accurate and complete service provision.
