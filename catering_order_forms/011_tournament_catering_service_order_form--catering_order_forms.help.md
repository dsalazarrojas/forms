# Tournament Catering Service Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about the tournament catering service you are interested in ordering for your event. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure that we can meet your catering needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your personal details, such as your name and email address.
2. Provide the date and time of your event.
3. Choose the number of guests attending the event and select your catering preferences.
4. Choose from the available menu options.
5. Enter any special requests you may have.
6. Provide contact information for the person handling your order.
7. Confirm that you have reviewed and accepted the event details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your full name as it will appear on the catering order confirmation.
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, required: `true`): Enter your email address where you would like to receive communication about your order.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required: `true`): Enter the date of your event in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, required: `true`): Enter the time of your event in the format `HH:MM`.
* **Event Duration** (`event_duration`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the duration of your event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, `text`, required: `true`): Enter the location of your event.
* **Catering Preferences** (`catering_preferences`, `select_one`, required: `true`): Answer `Yes` if you need catering services.
* **Catering Choices** (`catering_choices`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select one or more menu options that you would like to order.
* **Number of Guests** (`num_guests`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the estimated number of guests attending your event.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter any special requests or instructions you may have for your catering order.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the name of the person handling your order.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the phone number of the person handling your order.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the email address of the person handling your order.
* **Contact Message** (`contact_message`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter any message for the person handling your order.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the type of event (e.g. corporate meeting, wedding, etc.).
* **Special Requests 2** (`special_requests_2`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter any other special requests.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, `radio`, required: `true`): Select `Yes` to confirm that you have reviewed and accepted the event details.

Note: If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
