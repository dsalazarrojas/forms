# Catering Service Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Catering Service Recommendation Form is designed to help us better understand your event needs and preferences, allowing us to provide personalized catering recommendations for your upcoming event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information: Fill out the "Full Name", "Email Address", and "Phone Number" fields with your accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Event Details: Enter the "Event Date", "Type of Event", "Expected Number of Guests", and "Event Location" fields with the correct information.
3. Food and Beverage Preferences: Select your preferred "Cuisine", "Special Dietary Requirements", and "Service Style" for your event.
4. Budget and Services: Choose your budget range per guest and select any additional services you may need (e.g., bar service, beverage service, etc.).
5. Priorities and Preferences: Select your top priority for the event and provide any special themes or concepts you'd like to incorporate.
6. Confirm Contact: Finally, confirm that you agree to be contacted with catering recommendations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your active email address where you can be contacted for updates and recommendations.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number where we can contact you for urgent matters.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of your event (e.g., 2023-02-20).
* **Type of Event** (`event_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of event (e.g., Wedding, Corporate Event, Birthday Party).
* **Expected Number of Guests** (`guest_count`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the estimated number of guests attending the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location of your event (e.g., Hotel Ballroom, Backyard, Park).
* **Cuisine Preferences** (`cuisine_preference`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose your preferred cuisine(s) for the event (e.g., American, Italian, Mexican).
* **Special Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_restrictions`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select any special dietary requirements you may have (e.g., Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free).
* **Service Style Preference** (`service_style_preference`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your preferred service style for the event (e.g., Buffet, Plated Service, Family Style).
* **Budget Range Per Guest** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your budget range per guest (e.g., Under 25 dollars, 50-75 dollars).
* **Additional Services Needed** (`additional_services`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose any additional services you may need (e.g., Bar Service, Beverage Service, etc.).
* **Bar Service Type** (`bar_service_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): If you've selected "Bar Service", choose the type of bar service you need (e.g., Full Bar, Beer and Wine Only, etc.).
* **Equipment Rental Needed** (`equipment_rental_needed`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select if you need equipment rental services for the event.
* **Top Priority for This Event** (`top_priority`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your top priority for the event (e.g., Food Quality, Variety of Options, etc.).
* **Special Themes or Concepts** (`special_themes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any special themes or concepts you'd like to incorporate into the event.
* **Preferred Setup Timing** (`setup_timing`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your preferred setup timing for the event (e.g., 1 hour before, 2 hours before).
* **Have you used catering services before?** (`previous_vendor`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select if you've used our catering services before.
* **Additional Comments or Special Requests** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or special requests you may have.
* **I agree to be contacted with catering recommendations** (`contact_for_recommendations`, `select_one`, required/optional): Confirm that you agree to be contacted with catering recommendations.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure we can provide the best possible experience for your event.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Our team will review your form and provide personalized catering recommendations for your event.
