# Catering Service Menu Selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to select a catering service menu for your event. Please provide your contact information, event details, and preferences for our menu offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your contact information (name, email, and phone number) in the "Customer Details" section.
2. Select the date and time of your event in the "Event Information" section.
3. Enter the number of guests attending your event in the "Event Information" section.
4. In the "Cuisine Selection" section, choose your preferred cuisine type.
5. In the "Entree Selection" section, choose your first and second entree options, and select the quantity for each.
6. In the "Sides and Salads" section, select your side dish and salad preferences.
7. In the "Beverages" section, choose your beverage service type and select non-alcoholic beverages.
8. In the "Desserts" section, select your dessert preference.
9. In the "Special Requests" section, enter any special requests or dietary accommodations you may have.
10. Finally, confirm your menu selections in the "Menu Confirmation" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter your name for contact purposes.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for quick contact.
* **Event Date**: Select the date of your event.
* **Event Time**: Select the time of your event.
* **Number of Guests**: Enter the number of guests attending your event.
* **Preferred Cuisine Type**: Choose a cuisine that suits your taste.
* **First Entree Choice**: Select your first entree option.
* **Quantity for First Entree**: Enter the quantity you'd like for your first entree.
* **Second Entree Choice**: Select your second entree option (if desired).
* **Quantity for Second Entree**: Enter the quantity you'd like for your second entree (if desired).
* **Salad Selection**: Select your preferred salad options.
* **Side Dishes**: Select your preferred side dishes.
* **Beverage Service Type**: Choose your beverage service type.
* **Non-Alcoholic Beverage Selections**: Select your non-alcoholic beverage preferences.
* **Dessert Selection**: Select your dessert preference.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests or dietary accommodations.
* **Menu Confirmation**: Confirm your menu selections.
