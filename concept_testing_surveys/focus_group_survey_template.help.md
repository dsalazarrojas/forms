# focus_group_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from a focus group on a new concept, gathering insights on user satisfaction, perceptions, and recommendations for the idea or innovation presented.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly, providing your genuine thoughts and opinions.
2. For the "Overall satisfaction" question, select the option that best describes your level of satisfaction with the concept.
3. For the "Aspects influencing your opinion" question, select all the options that apply to you.
4. For the "Recommendation" question, simply mark whether you would or would not recommend the concept to others.
5. In the "Improvement suggestions" field, describe specific areas where you think the concept could be improved.
6. In the "Additional comments" field, share any further thoughts or feedback you might have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Overall, how satisfied are you with the concept?** (`satisfaction`, single): This question asks for your overall level of satisfaction with the concept presented. Select one of the five options: "Very satisfied", "Somewhat satisfied", "Neutral", "Somewhat dissatisfied", or "Very dissatisfied".
- **Which aspects of the concept most influenced your opinion?** (`aspects`, multiple): Choose all the options that apply to you, including "Overall idea", "Potential benefits", "Ease of use", "Design aesthetics", "Innovativeness", and "Perceived value".
- **Would you recommend this concept to others?** (`recommend`, single): Simply mark whether you would or would not recommend the concept to others.
- **What would you improve or change about the concept?** (`suggestions`, text): Provide specific, concrete ideas on how the concept could be improved or changed.
- **Do you have any additional comments or feedback?** (`comments`, text): Share any further thoughts or feedback you might have about the concept.
