# market_research_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on new concepts or ideas, allowing stakeholders to understand how well their proposal resonates with the target audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the options that best describe your feelings about the concept:
	* Rate your satisfaction with the concept.
	* Choose the aspects of the concept that appeal to you the most.
2. Express your initial impression of the concept:
	* Select your reaction to the concept.
3. Consider using the concept:
	* Indicate your likelihood of considering a product or service built around it.
4. Provide your thoughts on improving the concept:
	* Share your suggestions for changes or improvements.
5. Add any additional feedback:
	* Type in any other thoughts or comments about the concept.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How satisfied are you with the concept?**
	* This question gauges your overall satisfaction level with the proposed concept.
	* Please select one of the five options: Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.
- **Which aspects of the concept appeal most to you?**
	* Choose all that apply from the list of options: Innovation, Practicality, Potential benefit, Ease of use, Design/Aesthetics, Value proposition.
	* This question helps to understand which features or benefits of the concept resonate with you the most.
- **What is your initial impression of the concept?**
	* Select one of the five options: Excited, Interested, Neutral, Unsure, Disinterested.
	* This question aims to capture your immediate reaction to the concept.
- **Would you consider using a product or service built around this concept?**
	* Choose one of the five options: Definitely, Likely, Maybe, Unlikely, Definitely not.
	* This question assesses your likelihood of adopting a product or service built on the concept.
- **What improvements or changes would you suggest?**
	* Type in any suggestions you have for improving the concept.
	* Be as specific as possible to help refine the concept.
- **Do you have any additional thoughts or feedback?**
	* Share any other thoughts or comments about the concept.
	* This is your chance to provide any further insights or suggestions that didn't fit in the previous questions.
