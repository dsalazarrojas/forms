# product_research_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather users' initial reactions and feedback on a new product concept. It aims to collect information on users' satisfaction, the aspects of the product that influenced their opinion, their willingness to consider using the product, and their overall feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question based on your honest opinion or reaction to the product concept.
2. Select all that apply when asked about the aspects that influenced your opinion.
3. Write your suggested improvements and any other thoughts about the product concept.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How satisfied are you with this product concept?** (`Very satisfied`, `Somewhat satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Somewhat dissatisfied`, `Very dissatisfied`): This question asks for your level of satisfaction with the product concept, with the options ranging from very positive to very negative.
* **Which aspects of the product influenced your opinion?** (`Design`, `Potential features`, `Price point`, `Performance potential`, `Ease of use`, `Innovation`, etc.): This question asks you to select all the aspects that influenced your opinion about the product concept. You can select multiple options.
* **How likely are you to consider using this product if it were available?** (`Very likely`, `Somewhat likely`, `Neutral`, `Somewhat unlikely`, `Very unlikely`): This question gauges your willingness to use the product if it were available.
* **What improvements would you suggest for this product concept?**: This is an open-ended question where you can provide any suggestions or improvements you have for the product concept.
* **Do you have any other feedback or thoughts on this concept?**: This question gives you an opportunity to share any other thoughts or comments about the product concept.

## Tips
* Be honest and sincere in your responses, as this will help in gathering accurate and valuable feedback.
* Select all that apply when asked about the aspects that influenced your opinion, so you can provide a comprehensive view of your thoughts.
* If you have any suggestions or improvements, be specific and provide concrete ideas.
* Remember, this form is a starting point for product development and improvement, so every comment and suggestion counts!
