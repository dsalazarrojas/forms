# Price sensitivity survey template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Price sensitivity survey template is designed to help researchers and product developers understand user willingness to pay and perceived value for a new concept or product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
2. Consider the provided options carefully and choose the ones that best reflect your opinions.
3. Provide additional comments or thoughts as needed in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Value Rating** (`Excellent value`, `Good value`, `Fair value`, `Poor value`, `Very poor value`): This question asks you to rate the overall value-for-money of the concept or product.
* **Price Range Consideration** (`Below $25`, `$25 - $50`, `$51 - $75`, `Over $75`): This question inquires about the price range that you would consider most appealing for the concept or product.
* **Factors Influencing Perceived Value** (`Quality`, `Features`, `Brand reputation`, `Design`, `Performance`, `Quality`, `Features`, `Brand reputation`, `Design`, `Performance`): This question requests that you select all the factors that most influence your perception of the concept's value.
* **Purchase Likelihood at $40** (`Very likely`, `Likely`, `Neutral`, `Unlikely`, `Very unlikely`): This question asks how likely you would be to purchase the product or concept if it were priced at $40.
* **Additional Thoughts on Pricing or Value**: This open-ended question allows you to provide any additional thoughts or comments about the concept or product's pricing or value.
* **Additional Comments on Value**: This open-ended question allows you to provide any additional thoughts or comments about the concept or product's value.

## Tips

* Please provide honest and thoughtful responses to help ensure the accuracy of the results.
* If you need more information or clarification about any question, please refer to the concept or product's description provided earlier.
