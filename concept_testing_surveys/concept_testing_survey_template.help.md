# concept_testing_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather initial impressions and feedback on a presented concept.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the space provided.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply.
3. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you like.
4. Submit your responses when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Very satisfied
* A question to gauge your overall satisfaction with the concept. Select the option that best describes your sentiment.
* **How satisfied are you with the concept presented?**: 
  * Very satisfied: You're very pleased with the concept and the idea is well-executed.
  * Somewhat satisfied: You're somewhat pleased, but there's room for improvement.
  * Neutral: You're neither satisfied nor dissatisfied.
  * Somewhat dissatisfied: You're somewhat unhappy with the concept.
  * Very dissatisfied: You're very unhappy with the concept.
* Please select one of the options above that best describes your sentiment.

### Which aspects of the concept influenced your overall opinion the most? (Select all that apply)
* A multiple-choice question to help us understand what specifically influenced your opinion.
* Please select all the options that apply:
  * Design: The concept's appearance and aesthetics.
  * Functionality: The concept's features and capabilities.
  * Value proposition: The concept's value and benefits.
  * Ease of use: How easy or hard it is to use or understand the concept.
  * Problem solved: How well the concept addresses a specific problem.
* Select all that apply.

### How likely would you be to use a product or service built from it?
* A question to gauge your likelihood of using the concept.
* Please select one of the options:
  * Very likely: You'd highly likely use a product or service based on the concept.
  * Somewhat likely: You'd somewhat likely use a product or service based on the concept.
  * Neutral: You're not sure or neutral about using a product or service based on the concept.
  * Not very likely: You'd not likely use a product or service based on the concept.
  * Not at all likely: You'd not likely use a product or service based on the concept.

### What do you find most appealing about the concept?
* A question to understand what specifically appeals to you about the concept.
* Please provide a brief description of what you like most about the concept.

### What would you improve or change about the concept?
* A question to help us refine the concept.
* Please provide a brief description of what you'd change or improve about the concept.

### Do you have any additional comments or feedback regarding the concept?
* A question for any final thoughts or ideas you'd like to share.
* Please provide any additional comments or feedback you'd like to offer.
