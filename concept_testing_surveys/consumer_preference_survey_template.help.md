# consumer_preference_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from consumers on a new concept, aiming to understand their preferences, opinions, and likelihood of adoption.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the first question about your level of satisfaction with the concept.
2. Select all that apply in the second question to identify which aspects of the concept influenced your opinion.
3. Answer the third question about your likelihood of adopting or using the concept.
4. Type any improvements or changes you'd like to see made to the concept in the fourth question.
5. Share any additional comments or feedback you have about the concept in the final question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied are you with this concept?**
	+ (options: Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied): Please choose the one that best describes your overall satisfaction with the concept. 
* **Which aspects influenced your opinion of this concept?**
	+ (options: Design, Price, Performance, Features, Support, Usability): Select all that apply from the list. 
* **How likely are you to adopt or use this concept?**
	+ (options: Very likely, Somewhat likely, Neutral, Not very likely, Not at all likely): Choose the option that best describes your likelihood. 
* **What would you improve or change about this concept?**
	+ (text): Please provide specific ideas or suggestions for improvement.
* **Do you have any additional comments or feedback?**
	+ (text): Share any further thoughts or suggestions you have about the concept.
