# Competitor Research Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey template is designed to gather insights into competitor offerings and market perceptions to inform future product development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question based on your genuine experience and perceptions about competitors in the market.
2. Be honest and thorough in your responses.
3. Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
4. Provide detailed answers for text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How familiar are you with the main competitors in this market?**
  (`options`: Very familiar, Somewhat familiar, Neutral, Not very familiar, Not at all familiar):
  This question helps us understand how well you know the key players in the market.
* **How would you rate the overall quality of competitor products/services?**
  (`options`: Excellent, Good, Average, Below average, Poor):
  This question helps us gauge your opinion on the quality of competitor offerings.
* **Which of the following aspects do competitors generally excel at? (select all that apply)**
  (`options`: Pricing, Feature set, User experience, Customer support, Innovation, Brand recognition):
  This question helps us identify areas where competitors have a competitive edge.
* **Which areas do competitors struggle with or have weaknesses in? (select all that apply)**
  (`options`: High cost, Missing features, Complex UI, Slow support, Outdated tech, Poor reputation):
  This question helps us pinpoint areas where competitors are lagging behind.
* **What key features or benefits do competitor products offer that you find most compelling?**
  (`type`: Text field):
  This question invites you to share any standout features or benefits you find attractive about competitor offerings.
* **What frustrates you most about current competitor solutions?**
  (`type`: Text field):
  This question helps us understand the pain points or limitations you face with current competitor solutions.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your responses.
- Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
- Provide detailed answers for text fields.
- This survey is designed to be anonymous, so feel free to share your genuine opinions without fear of judgment.
