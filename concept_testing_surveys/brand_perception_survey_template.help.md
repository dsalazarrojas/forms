# Brand Perception Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather end-users' initial perceptions of a brand concept. It aims to collect feedback on various aspects of the brand, such as design, messaging, values, uniqueness, price perception, and reputation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly, providing your genuine thoughts and opinions.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. Use the text field to provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.
4. Submit your completed form to share your perceptions and help refine the brand concept.

	+ Reputation
