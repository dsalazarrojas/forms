# Extracurricular Class Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students who wish to register for an extracurricular class or activity that is not part of the standard curriculum.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Student Information section with your name and any other relevant details.
2. Select all activities related to your passion or hobbies from the Extracurricular Class Selection dropdown menu.
3. In the Course Details section, enter the course ID number and course name.
4. Enter the course start and end dates if applicable.
5. Choose the class time from the available options.
6. Enter the teacher's name (if applicable).
7. Enter the room number (if applicable).
8. In the Contact Information section, enter your email and phone number.
9. Confirm your parent or guardian's email and phone number.
10. Add any additional comments you may have about the registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information (1)** (Text, Optional): This section is where you'll enter your name and any other relevant details. 
* **Parent Information (2)** (Text, Optional): If you're under 18, you might need to provide your parent or guardian's contact information.
* **Extracurricular Class Selection (3)** (Select Multiple, Required): Select all activities related to your passion or hobbies.
* **Course Details (4)** (Text, Optional): This section is for additional course information. 
* **Course ID (5)** (Number, Required): Enter the unique ID number for the course you are registering for.
* **Course Name (6)** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the course.
* **Course Start Date (7)** (Date, Optional): Enter the start date of the course.
* **Course End Date (8)** (Date, Optional): Enter the end date of the course.
* **Class Time (9)** (Time, Optional): Choose the time of the class.
* **Teacher Name (10)** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the teacher.
* **Room Number (11)** (Text, Optional): Enter the number of the room where the class will be held.
* **Student Email (12)** (Email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Student Phone (13)** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Parent Email (14)** (Email, Optional): If you're under 18, enter your parent or guardian's email address.
* **Parent Phone (15)** (Text, Optional): If you're under 18, enter your parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Comments (16)** (Note, Optional): Add any additional comments about the registration.
* **Course Status (17)** (Select One, Required): Choose the status of the course (Active or Inactive).
* **Class Type (18)** (Select One, Required): Choose whether you are 'Yes' or 'No' for this class type.
