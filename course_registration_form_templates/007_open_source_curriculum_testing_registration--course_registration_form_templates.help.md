# open_source_curriculum_testing_registration - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from users interested in participating in a pilot program for open source curriculum testing. The form will help us understand your preferences and requirements to better serve your needs and ensure a smooth testing experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "What is your name?" field.
2. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the question "Are you interested in participating in a pilot program for open source curriculum testing?".
3. Enter the course number and name you are interested in, in the respective fields.
4. Choose the start and end dates for the course you selected.
5. Enter your email address in the "What is your email address?" field.
6. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "What is your phone number?" field (this is optional).
7. Provide any additional notes or comments you may have in the "Do you have any additional notes?" field (this is optional).
8. Indicate whether you are interested in receiving email notifications by selecting "Yes" or "No".
9. Choose whether you want to be part of a community group by selecting "Yes" or "No" (optional).
10. If you have any decimal or integer values, enter them in the respective fields.
11. Select your available time slot (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name? (Text)**: This is the field where you can enter your full name. Please use the format "First Name Last Name" for best results.
* **Are you interested in participating in a pilot program for open source curriculum testing? (Select One)**: Answer "Yes" if you are interested, "No" otherwise. This will help us understand your willingness to participate.
* **What is the course number you are interested in? (Text)**: Enter the course number assigned to the course you are interested in.
* **What is the course name you are interested in? (Text)**: Enter the name of the course you selected in the previous field.
* **When would you like to start the course? (Date)**: Choose the start date for the course you selected.
* **When would you like to end the course? (Date)**: Choose the end date for the course you selected.
* **What is your email address? (Email)**: Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications about the course.
* **What is your phone number? (Text)**: Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone. This is optional.
* **Do you have any additional notes? (Text)**: Provide any additional comments or notes you would like to share with us.
* **Are you interested in receiving email notifications? (Select One)**: Select "Yes" if you want to receive email updates, "No" otherwise.
* **Are you interested in being part of a community group for the course? (Select One)**: Select "Yes" if you want to be part of a community group, "No" otherwise.
* **What is the decimal value? (Number)**: Enter a decimal value if you need to provide one. This field is optional.
* **What is the integer value? (Number)**: Enter an integer value if you need to provide one. This field is optional.
* **What is your available time slot? (Time)**: Select your available time slot for the course. This field is optional.
