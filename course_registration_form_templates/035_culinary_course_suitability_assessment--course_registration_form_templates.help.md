# Culinary Course Suitability Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the suitability of a prospective student for a culinary course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Prospective Student Info** field with your name and any other relevant contact information.
2. Select your **Experience Level**, which indicates your level of expertise in the culinary field (No Experience, Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced).
3. Choose your **Learning Needs**, which indicates what type of learning experience you are looking for (Basic, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert).
4. Select your **Availability**, which indicates how many hours you are available to dedicate to the course per week (Full-Time, Part-Time, or Online).
5. Provide additional details about your **Availability** in the **Availability Details** field.
6. Choose your **Preferred Culinary Field**, which indicates the type of culinary field you are interested in (Pastry, Meat, Seafood, etc.).
7. Provide additional details about your **Preferred Culinary Field** in the **Preferred Culinary Field Details** field.
8. Fill out the **Culinary School Info** field with any relevant information about your previous educational background or experience with culinary schools.
9. Provide information about your instructor preferences in the **Instructor Info** field.
10. Choose a **Course Recommendation** that reflects your desired course level (Basic, Intermediate, Advanced).
11. Select a specific **Assessment Date** and **Assessment Time** for your suitability assessment.
12. Enter your **Email** address.
13. Provide your **Phone** number.
14. Finally, click the **Submit** button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Prospective Student Info**: Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Experience Level**: Select your level of expertise in the culinary field.
* **Learning Needs**: Choose the type of learning experience you are looking for.
* **Availability**: Select your availability to dedicate to the course per week.
* **Availability Details**: Provide additional details about your availability.
* **Preferred Culinary Field**: Choose the type of culinary field you are interested in.
* **Preferred Culinary Field Details**: Provide additional details about your preferred culinary field.
* **Culinary School Info**: Fill out any relevant information about your previous educational background or experience with culinary schools.
* **Instructor Info**: Provide information about your instructor preferences.
* **Course Recommendation**: Choose a course recommendation that reflects your desired course level.
* **Assessment Date**: Select a specific assessment date.
* **Assessment Time**: Select a specific assessment time.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Submit**: Click the submit button to complete the form.

## Tips
* Be honest about your experience and availability to ensure an accurate suitability assessment.
* Provide specific and detailed information about your background and preferences.
* Make sure to choose the correct course recommendation to reflect your desired level of study.
