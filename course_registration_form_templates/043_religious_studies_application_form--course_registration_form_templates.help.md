# Religious Studies Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Religious Studies Application Form is used to gather information from individuals applying for religious studies programs or activities. This form will help the administrators evaluate your application and provide you with the necessary guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by providing your personal details in the **Applicant Details** section.
2.  If you have a referral from a member of our organization, please provide the **Referral Information** section.
3.  In the **Church Affiliation** section, select the denomination that you are affiliated with (e.g., Church of Christ, Baptist, etc.). If your denomination is not listed, select "Other (please specify)" and provide the name of your church.
4.  In the **Educational History** section, briefly describe your educational background.
5.  In the **Employment History** section, list your work experience, if any.
6.  In the **Health History** section, report any health issues that might be relevant to your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Details**: Please enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Referral Information**: If you have a referral from a member of our organization, please provide their name and title/position within the organization.
* **Church Affiliation**: Select your denomination from the list or choose "Other (please specify)" if your denomination is not listed. If you choose "Other (please specify)", please provide the name of your church.
*   **Educational History**: Briefly describe your educational background, including any degrees earned and relevant courses taken.
*   **Employment History**: List your work experience, including job titles, company names, and dates of employment.
*   **Health History**: Report any health issues that might be relevant to your application. Please provide detailed information to ensure that your application is processed correctly.

## Tips

*   Please fill out each field accurately and completely to avoid errors in your application.
*   If you're unsure about any of the fields, contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
*   Make sure to proofread your application before submitting to catch any mistakes.
