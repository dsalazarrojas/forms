# kilkenny_ppn_online_training_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The kilkenny_ppn_online_training_survey form is designed to collect user information for online training purposes. It is used to streamline the registration process for online training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your user details in the User Details section.
2. Select the type of online training you are interested in.
3. Choose the type of course you prefer.
4. Select a start date for the training.
5. Select an end date for the training.
6. Choose the location where you would like to attend the training.
7. Select your preferred time of day for the training.
8. Enter your contact name.
9. Enter your contact email address.
10. Enter your contact phone number (optional).
11. Add any additional comments you have about the training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. User Details
* **User Details** (`text`, required, false): This section is for entering general user information. Please fill in your details accurately and completely.

### 2. What type of online training do you want?
* **What type of online training do you want?** (`select_one`, required, true): Please select the type of online training you are interested in from the available options (Option A, Option B, Option C).

### 3. What type of course do you want?
* **What type of course do you want?** (`select_one`, required, true): Choose the type of course you prefer from the available options (Online Course, Face-to-Face).

### 4. When would you like to start?
* **When would you like to start?** (`date`, required, true): Select a start date for the training. This will help us schedule the training accordingly.

### 5. When would you like to end?
* **When would you like to end?** (`date`, required, true): Select an end date for the training. This will help us schedule the training accordingly.

### 6. What location would you like to attend?
* **What location would you like to attend?** (`select_multiple`, required, true): Choose one or more locations where you would like to attend the training.

### 7. What time of day do you prefer?
* **What time of day do you prefer?** (`time`, required, true): Select your preferred time of day for the training.

### 8. Contact Name
* **Contact Name** (`text`, required, true): Enter your name for contact purposes.

### 9. Contact E-mail
* **Contact E-mail** (`email`, required, true): Enter your email address for contact purposes.

### 10. Contact Phone
* **Contact Phone** (`text`, optional, false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes (if applicable).

### 11. Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`note`, optional, false): Add any additional comments you have about the training (if applicable).

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have selected all required fields before submitting the form.
* Review the form for accuracy before submitting to avoid errors.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
