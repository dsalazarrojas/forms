# Computer Tuition Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students who wish to register for computer tuition programs offered by the institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your registration ID, which is a unique identifier for your registration.
2. Enter your registration date, which is the date you are registering for the program.
3. Provide your full name and date of birth.
4. Select your gender from the options provided.
5. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
6. Provide your mailing address, city, and state or province.
7. Enter your ZIP or postal code.
8. Select your country from the options provided.
9. If you are under 18, provide your parent or guardian's name and contact information.
10. If applicable, provide your current school name and grade level.
11. Select the tuition program you wish to enroll in from the options provided.
12. Choose the program duration from the options provided.
13. Select your preferred class schedule.
14. Choose your preferred learning mode.
15. Select your prior computer experience level.
16. Describe your learning goals.
17. Enter the total tuition amount.
18. Select your payment plan from the options provided.
19. Choose your payment method from the options provided.
20. If applicable, indicate if you are applying for a scholarship.
21. Enter your preferred start date.
22. Accept the terms and conditions of the program.
23. Select your current registration status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration ID** (`registration_id`, `text`, **required**): A unique identifier for your registration, which is used to track your progress.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, **required**): The date you are registering for the program.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, **required**): Your full name as you wish it to be recorded.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, **required**): Your birth date.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, **required**): Your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, **required**): Your contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, `text`, **required**): Your complete mailing address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, **required**): Your city of residence.
* **State or Province** (`state_province`, `text`, **required**): Your state or province of residence.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, `text`, **required**): Your ZIP or postal code.
* **Country** (`country`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your country from the options provided.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, `text`, **optional**): If you are under 18, provide your parent or guardian's name.
* **Parent or Guardian Contact** (`parent_guardian_contact`, `text`, **optional**): If you are under 18, provide your parent or guardian's contact information.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, **optional**): If you are currently enrolled in a school, provide the name.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, `text`, **optional**): If you are currently enrolled in a school, provide your grade level.
* **Tuition Program** (`tuition_program`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the tuition program you wish to enroll in.
* **Program Duration** (`program_duration`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose the program duration.
* **Class Schedule** (`class_schedule`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your preferred class schedule.
* **Learning Mode** (`learning_mode`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose your preferred learning mode.
* **Prior Computer Experience** (`prior_experience`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your prior computer experience level.
* **Learning Goals** (`learning_goals`, `text`, **required**): Describe your learning goals.
* **Tuition Fee** (`tuition_fee`, `number`, **required**): Enter the total tuition amount.
* **Payment Plan** (`payment_plan`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your preferred payment plan.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose your payment method.
* **Scholarship Application** (`scholarship_application`, `select_one`, **required**): If applicable, indicate if you are applying for a scholarship.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter your preferred start date.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_accepted`, `select_one`, **required**): Accept the terms and conditions of the program.
* **Registration Status** (`registration_status`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your current registration status.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you are under 18, please ensure that your parent or guardian has provided their consent and contact information.
* Review and understand the terms and conditions before accepting them.
* Ensure that you select the correct program duration and schedule that suits your needs.
