# course_enrollment_contract_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The course_enrollment_contract_form is a tool used to collect essential information and agreements from students and staff when enrolling in a course. It helps to ensure a smooth and transparent enrollment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the Course Terms section for the course details and requirements.
2. Provide a brief description of the course in the Course Description field.
3. Enter the student's details in the Student Details field.
4. Enter the date the student signed the contract in the Student Sign Date field and sign with your name in the Student Signature field.
5. Enter the date the staff signed the contract in the Staff Sign Date field and sign with your name in the Staff Signature field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Terms (Note)**: This is a read-only field that displays the terms of the course. Please review and agree to the terms before proceeding.
* **Course Description (Text)**: Provide a brief and concise description of the course in this field.
* **Student Details (Text)**: Enter the student's name, contact information, and any other relevant details required for the course.
* **Student Sign Date (Date)**: Enter the date the student signs this contract. This field will automatically format the date according to the selected date format.
* **Student Signature (Text)**: Sign this field with your name to confirm your agreement with the course terms.
* **Staff Sign Date (Date)**: Enter the date the staff signs this contract. This field will automatically format the date according to the selected date format.
* **Staff Signature (Text)**: Sign this field with your name to confirm your agreement with the course terms.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Make sure to review and agree to the course terms before signing.
* Keep a record of the signed contracts for future reference.
