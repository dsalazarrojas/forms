# Customer Journey Optimization Course Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for registering for a Customer Journey Optimization course. It collects information about the participant, their learning goals, session preferences, and agreements related to the course and session facilitators.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Participant Details, including your name.
2. Specify your learning goals.
3. Indicate your session preferences.
4. Enter your Participant Email address.
5. Select your preferred Session Date.
6. Choose your preferred Session Time.
7. Provide your Participant Phone number.
8. Enter your Participant Address.
9. Add any Participant Notes you'd like to share.
10. Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions.
11. Choose a Session Facilitator to work with.
12. Confirm that you agree to the Session Facilitator's terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of yourself.
* **What are your learning goals** (`learning_goals`, `text`, required: false): Specify what you hope to learn from the course.
* **Session preferences** (`session_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose how you prefer to participate in the session (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your home or office address.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any notes you'd like to share with the facilitator.
* **Participant Agreed** (`participant_agreed`, `select_one`, required: false): Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions (I agree or I do not agree).
* **Participant Agreement Date** (`participant_agreement_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Participant Agreement Time** (`participant_agreement_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Session Facilitator** (`session_facilitator`, `text`, required: false): Choose a facilitator to work with.
* **Session Facilitator Agreed** (`session_facilitator_agreed`, `select_one`, required: false): Confirm that you agree to the Session Facilitator's terms and conditions (I agree or I do not agree).
* **Session Facilitator Agreement Date** (`session_facilitator_agreement_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you agree to the Session Facilitator's terms and conditions.
* **Session Facilitator Agreement Time** (`session_facilitator_agreement_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you agree to the Session Facilitator's terms and conditions.

## Tips
- Be as accurate and truthful as possible when completing the form.
- Double-check your answers before submitting.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the course administrators.
