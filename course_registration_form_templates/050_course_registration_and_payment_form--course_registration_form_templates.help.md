# Course Registration And Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Registration And Payment Form is a user-facing form designed to collect relevant details from attendees who wish to register and pay for courses. This form is used to process course registrations and payments in a seamless and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields. Fields marked as required are necessary to process your registration and payment.
2. Ensure all fields are completed accurately to avoid any discrepancies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Name** (name, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Attendee Email** (attendee_email, email, required): Enter your email address. This field is used for course communication and updates.
* **Attendee Phone** (attendee_phone, text, required): Enter your phone number for any urgent course-related communications.
* **Course Name** (course_name, text, required): Select the name of the course you wish to register for.
* **Date** (date, date, required): Choose the date you wish to attend the course.
* **Time** (time, time, required): Select the time slot you prefer for the course.
* **Price** (price, number, required): Review the price for the course, which will be displayed here.
* **Payment Method** (payment_method, select_one, required): Choose how you wish to pay for the course (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
* **Payment Details** (payment_details, note, not required): A text field for you to input any additional payment information if needed.

Please note that payment method and price fields are subject to change based on the course and payment terms. Review and confirm payment details before completing the form.
