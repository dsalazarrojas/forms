# Tutor Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from students about their needs and preferences for a tutor request.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk \*.
2. Enter a brief description of your needs or preferences in the "User Request" field.
3. Select the subjects you are interested in or need help with in the "Subjects" field.
4. Select your preferred contact method in the "Contact Preference" field.
5. Select a time and date for the tutor session.
6. Enter your preferred location for the tutor session.
7. Select the subjects you prefer the tutor to specialize in in the "Tutor Preference" field.
8. Enter any other relevant details about your preferences in the "Other Tutor Preference" field.
9. Enter the student's name, email, phone number, and other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input**: A field where you can enter any information you'd like to share about yourself. This could be a description of your needs or preferences. (Required)
* **User Request**: Enter a brief description of your needs or preferences. (Not Required)
* **Subjects**: Select the subjects you are interested in or need help with. (Required)
* **Contact Preference**: Select how you'd like to be contacted (Phone Call, Email, Chat). (Required)
* **Time**: Select a time for the tutor session. (Required)
* **Date**: Select a date for the tutor session. (Required)
* **Location**: Enter the location for the tutor session. (Required)
* **Tutor Preference**: Select the subjects you prefer the tutor to specialize in. (Required)
* **Other Tutor Preference**: Enter any other relevant details about your preferences. (Required)
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name. (Required)
* **Student Email**: Enter the student's email. (Required)
* **Student Phone**: Enter the student's phone number. (Required)
* **Student Description**: Enter any other relevant details about the student. (Required)
* **Student Time**: Select a time for the student's session. (Required)
* **Student Date**: Select a date for the student's session. (Required)
* **Student Location**: Enter the location for the student's session. (Required)
* **Student Other**: Enter any other relevant details about the student. (Required)
* **Student Other Details**: Enter any other details about the student. (Required)
* **Tutor Name**: Enter the tutor's name. (Required)
* **Tutor Code**: Enter the tutor's code. (Required)
* **Tutor Email**: Enter the tutor's email. (Required)
* **Tutor Phone**: Enter the tutor's phone number. (Required)

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk \*.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the tutor or administrator for assistance.
* This form is intended for tutors to gather information about students, so please be honest and clear in your responses.
