# health-services-class-registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The health-services-class-registration form is used to register users for health services classes. This form collects information such as class date, class time, class description, and other relevant details to track and manage class enrollment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user information, including your class date.
2. Select your preferred class time.
3. Choose your schedule preferences.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Provide a phone number (optional).
6. Enter a description of the class you wish to register for.
7. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (Text, Optional): This field is used to enter your user information.
* **Class Date** (Date): Enter the date you wish to attend the class.
* **Class Time** (Time): Select the time of day you wish to attend the class.
* **Schedule Preferences** (Select One): Choose whether you can attend the class at any time or have specific schedule preferences.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter a phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Class Description** (Text, Required): Enter a description of the class you wish to register for.
* **Submit** (Text, Required): Click this button to complete the form and register for the class.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information to ensure you receive important class reminders and updates.
* If you have specific schedule preferences, make sure to select them correctly to avoid conflicts.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact the form administrator or instructor for assistance.
