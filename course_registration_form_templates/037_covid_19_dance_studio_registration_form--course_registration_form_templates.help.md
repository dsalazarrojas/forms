# COVID 19 Dance Studio Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 Dance Studio Registration Form is used to collect information from dancers to ensure a safe and healthy class environment. This form helps the studio management to understand the dancers' health status, attendance, and other important details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the dance class for which you are registering.
2. Choose the start time of the dance class.
3. Enter the name of the dance instructor.
4. Select the type of dance style you will be attending (e.g., Ballet, Jazz, etc.).
5. Choose the level of dance class you will be attending (e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, etc.).
6. Provide your full name.
7. Enter your age (if applicable).
8. If you are a minor, provide your parent's or guardian's name.
9. Enter your contact phone number.
10. Enter your email address (if applicable).
11. Indicate if you will be attending the class (Yes, No, Late Arrival, etc.).
12. Confirm if you have completed a COVID-19 health screening (Yes, No, Not completed, etc.).
13. Report if you have a fever (100.4 degrees F or higher) (Yes, No, Not checked, etc.).
14. Describe any COVID-19 symptoms you are experiencing (No symptoms, Mild symptoms, Moderate symptoms, etc.).
15. Report if you have had COVID-19 exposure in the last 14 days (No exposure, Possible exposure, Confirmed exposure, etc.).
16. Indicate your vaccination status (Fully vaccinated, Partially vaccinated, Not vaccinated, etc.).
17. Confirm if you are currently under quarantine or isolation (Yes, No, Unsure, etc.).
18. Choose if you will wear a mask during class (Yes, No, As needed, etc.).
19. Select the payment method for the class (Class Pack, Monthly Membership, Drop-in Fee, etc.).
20. Enter the amount paid for the class (if applicable).
21. Provide the name of an emergency contact person.
22. Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person (if applicable).
23. Describe any medical conditions you have that the studio should be aware of (injuries, allergies, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Class Date** (1, `date`, required): Enter the date of the dance class for which you are registering.
* **Class Time** (2, `time`, required): Choose the start time of the dance class.
* **Instructor Name** (3, `text`, required): Enter the name of the dance instructor.
* **Dance Style** (4, `select_one`, required): Select the type of dance style you will be attending (e.g., Ballet, Jazz, etc.).
* **Class Level** (5, `select_one`, required): Choose the level of dance class you will be attending (e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, etc.).
* **Dancer Name** (6, `text`, required): Provide your full name.
* **Age** (7, `number`, optional): Enter your age (if applicable).
* **Parent/Guardian Name** (8, `text`, optional): If you are a minor, enter your parent's or guardian's name.
* **Contact Phone Number** (9, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email Address** (10, `email`, optional): Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Attendance Status** (11, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you will be attending the class (Yes, No, Late Arrival, etc.).
* **Health Screening Completed** (12, `select_one`, required): Confirm if you have completed a COVID-19 health screening (Yes, No, Not completed, etc.).
* **Fever Present** (13, `select_one`, required): Report if you have a fever (100.4 degrees F or higher) (Yes, No, Not checked, etc.).
* **COVID-19 Symptoms** (14, `select_one`, required): Describe any COVID-19 symptoms you are experiencing (No symptoms, Mild symptoms, Moderate symptoms, etc.).
* **COVID-19 Exposure** (15, `select_one`, required): Report if you have had COVID-19 exposure in the last 14 days (No exposure, Possible exposure, Confirmed exposure, etc.).
* **Vaccination Status** (16, `select_one`, optional): Indicate your vaccination status (Fully vaccinated, Partially vaccinated, Not vaccinated, etc.).
* **Currently Under Quarantine or Isolation** (17, `select_one`, required): Confirm if you are currently under quarantine or isolation (Yes, No, Unsure, etc.).
* **Will you wear a mask during class?** (18, `select_one`, optional): Choose if you will wear a mask during class (Yes, No, As needed, etc.).
* **Payment Method** (19, `select_one`, optional): Select the payment method for the class (Class Pack, Monthly Membership, Drop-in Fee, etc.).
* **Amount Paid** (20, `number`, optional): Enter the amount paid for the class (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (21, `text`, optional): Provide the name of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (22, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person (if applicable).
* **Any Medical Conditions** (23, `text`, optional): Describe any medical conditions you have that the studio should be aware of (injuries, allergies, etc.).

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, ask for clarification from the studio staff.
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
