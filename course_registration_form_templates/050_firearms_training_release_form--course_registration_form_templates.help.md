# Firearms Training Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain a participant's release and consent for firearms training, which can include training, certification, or qualification programs. This form ensures that participants understand their responsibilities and liabilities before participating in the training, and it serves as a record of their release.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select the relevant training type (e.g., "Training", "Certification", or "Qualification") that applies to you.
3. Enter your name, course title, and organization (if applicable).
4. Sign and date the form to acknowledge your understanding of the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Info (id: 1)**: This is the personal information section where you will provide your name, details that will be kept confidential. Please ensure that it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Release Date (id: 2)**: This is the date on which you are releasing the liability for participating in the firearms training.
* **Training Type (id: 3)**: Select the type of training you are participating in, which can be "Training", "Certification", or "Qualification".
* **Course Title (id: 4)**: Enter the title of the specific course or program you are participating in.
* **Trainer Name (id: 5)**: If applicable, enter the name of the trainer or instructor who will be providing the training.
* **Organization (id: 6)**: Enter your organization's name, if you are participating through your workplace or institution.
* **Participant Signature (id: 7)**: Sign and date this section to acknowledge that you have read, understand, and agree to the terms and conditions of the release.
* **Witness Signature (id: 8)**: If possible, have a witness sign this section to verify your understanding and agreement with the terms and conditions.
