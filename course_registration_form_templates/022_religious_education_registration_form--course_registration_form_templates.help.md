# religious_education_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Religious Education Registration Form" is a form designed to collect necessary information from students to register them in various religious education courses. The form is essential for the efficient and accurate registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the "Course Name" of the course you wish to enroll in.
2.  Provide your "Student Name" accurately.
3.  Enter your valid "Email" address.
4.  (Optional) Enter your "Phone Number" if you prefer to be contacted through this method.
5.  Enter your "Date of Birth".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Name (Text)**: This is the name of the course you wish to register in. Please enter the exact name as it appears in the course catalog or description.
* **Student Name (Text)**: This is your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Email (Email)**: This is your valid email address where we will communicate with you regarding the course.
* **Phone Number (Text)**: If you have a preferred phone number where we can contact you, please enter it here. This is optional but recommended.
* **Date of Birth (Date)**: This is your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
