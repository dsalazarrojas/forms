# Course Substitution Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Course Substitution Form is designed for students to request a course substitution, allowing them to switch from one course to another. This form gathers necessary information from the student to facilitate a smooth and efficient course substitution process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Begin by filling out your information, including your name and email address.
2.  Then, enter the details of the course you wish to substitute, including the new and old courses.
3.  Provide a brief explanation for why you're requesting the course substitution.
4.  Add any additional comments or notes from your instructor.
5.  Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Name** (Student Name, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your student records.
*   **Student Email** (Student Email, email, required): Enter your university email address to ensure that your instructor can contact you regarding your substitution request.
*   **New Course** (New Course, text, required): List the name of the new course you wish to substitute into.
*   **New Course Code** (New Course Code, text, required): Enter the course code for the new course you've selected.
*   **Old Course** (Old Course, text, required): List the name of the course you currently have.
*   **Old Course Code** (Old Course Code, text, required): Enter the course code for the course you are leaving.
*   **Reason for Substitution** (Reason for Substitution, text, required): Provide a brief explanation for why you want to substitute courses.
*   **Instructor Comments** (Instructor Comments, text, required): Add any notes or comments from your instructor regarding your substitution request.
*   **Submit** (Submit, text, required): Click this button to complete and submit your course substitution request.
