# Learning Design Certification Pathway Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering progress in a learning design certification pathway. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your personal details and course information.
2. Make sure to accurately report your status (Completed, Not Completed, or In Progress).
3. If you have any comments or feedback about your experience, feel free to include them in the comments field.
4. Enter your contact information (email and phone number) so we can get in touch with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or certificate.
* **Course Title** (`course_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the course you are registering for.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the program associated with the course.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, optional): Enter the date you completed the course, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you started the course, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you finished the course, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Total Hours** (`total_hours`, number, optional): Enter the total number of hours you spent on the course.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ Completed
	+ Not Completed
	+ In Progress
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **User ID** (`user_id`, text, optional): Enter your User ID associated with the course.
* **Course ID** (`course_id`, text, optional): Enter your Course ID associated with the course.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields (if any).
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
* If you have any feedback about this form, let us know!
