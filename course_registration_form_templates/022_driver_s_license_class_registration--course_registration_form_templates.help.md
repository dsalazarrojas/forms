1. Fill out the **General Information** page with your basic details.
2. On the **Schedule Preferences** page, select your preferred schedule options.
3. Proceed to the **Payment Details** page to enter your payment information.
4. Continue to the **Student Details** page to provide your student-specific information.
5. Move on to the **Emergency Contact** page to enter your emergency contact details.
6. Next, provide your **Driver License Details** on the respective page.

9. Confirm your payment on the **Confirm Payment** page.
10. Review and confirm your details on the **Confirm Details** page.
11. Finally, select your **Course Selection** and upload your **Driver License** on the last two pages.
