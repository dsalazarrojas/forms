# Empowered Thinking Mindset Coaching Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for individuals who want to register for the Empowered Thinking Mindset Coaching program. It is a registration form that collects basic contact information and demographic data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with your relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (first_name, text, optional): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Last Name** (last_name, text, optional): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted for further information.
- **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter a contact phone number where you can be reached for additional details.
- **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, date, optional): Please enter your date of birth in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
- **Time Zone** (time_zone, time, optional): Please select your local time zone for scheduling purposes.

## Tips
- Ensure you have already checked that this is the correct form for your intended purpose.
- Enter accurate and up-to-date information for a successful registration.
