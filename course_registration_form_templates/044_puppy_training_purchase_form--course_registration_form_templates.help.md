# Puppy Training Purchase Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from customers for purchasing puppy training services. Please fill it out thoroughly to help us understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form completely and accurately**: Each field in this form is designed to help us understand your details and preferences. Please ensure that you fill out every field, including user input, payment information, contact details, schedule preferences, and any additional notes.
2. **Provide clear and concise answers**: Use clear and concise answers in each field to help us understand your needs and preferences accurately.
3. **Submit the form**: Once you have filled out the form, click the submit button to send your details to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input (`title`: User Input, `type`: text, `required`: false):
	+ This field is used to collect any relevant information about you that you want us to know for your puppy training.
* **Payment Info (`title`: Payment Info, `type`: text, `required`: false):

* **Contact Info (`title`: Contact Info, `type`: text, `required`: false):
	+ This field is used to collect your contact details, including your name and contact method.
* **Schedule (`title`: Schedule, `type`: text, `required`: false):
	+ This field is used to collect your schedule preferences for your puppy training sessions.
* **Notes (`title`: Notes, `type`: text, `required`: false):
	+ This field is used to collect any additional information you might have about your puppy training.
* **Payment Terms (`title`: Payment Terms, `type`: text, `required`: false):
	+ This field is used to collect any specific payment terms or instructions you may have.
