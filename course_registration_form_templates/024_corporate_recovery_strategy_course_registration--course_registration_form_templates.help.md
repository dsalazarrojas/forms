# corporate_recovery_strategy_course_registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for course registration. It is intended for participants and/or their representatives to provide necessary information to secure a spot in the corporate recovery strategy course.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields have been filled out.
3. Review the form for any errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participants Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the participant(s) enrolling in the course.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): If applicable, please enter the company name associated with the participant(s).
* **Course Title** (`course_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the course being registered for.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the course.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address for the participant(s) or their representative.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter a valid phone number for the participant(s) or their representative.
* **Submit and Save** (`submit_and_save`, text, optional): Click to submit and save the registration once all required information has been entered.
* **Confirm Registration** (`confirmation`, note, optional): This field will display a confirmation message once the registration is submitted.
