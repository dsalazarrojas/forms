# lash_technician_course_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The lash_technician_course_registration_form is used to register students for a Lash Technician course. Fill out this form to provide your details and course preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your personal details, such as First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Provide your Email and Phone number for contact purposes.
3. Select the course date and start time from the provided options.
4. Indicate if you already hold a Lash Technician Certificate, if so, please provide the Certificate ID.
5. Select the course type, which can be Full Course or Partially Full Course.
6. Enter the course fee and total fees.
7. Choose your preferred Payment Method from the options provided.
8. Enter the payment date.
9. Select the instructor(s) for the course.
10. Choose the course level and language.
11. Enter the total number of classes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address in this format: `example@example.com`.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number in the format `XXX XXX XXXX`.
* **Course Start Date** (`course_date`, date, required): Select the date of the course start from the calendar options provided.
* **Course Start Time** (`course_time`, time, required): Select the start time of the course from the options provided.
* **Lash Technician Certificate** (`lash_technician_certificate`, select_multiple, required): Check if you already hold a Lash Technician Certificate. If yes, provide the Certificate ID.
* **Lash Technician Certificate ID** (`lash_technician_certificate_id`, text, optional): If you have a Lash Technician Certificate, enter the ID number.
* **Course** (`course`, select_one, required): Select the type of course you want to enroll in, either Full Course or Partially Full Course.
* **Lash Technician Course Fee** (`lash_technician_course_fee`, number, required): Enter the course fee for the selected course.
* **Total Fees** (`total_fees`, number, required): Enter the total fees for the course.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required): Choose the payment method you will use, such as Cash, Credit Card, or PayPal.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required): Enter the payment date.
* **Instructor(s)** (`instructors`, select_multiple, optional): Select the instructor(s) for the course.
* **Course Level** (`course_level`, select_one, required): Select the level of the course, either Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Course Language** (`course_language`, select_one, required): Select the language of the course, either English, Spanish, or French.
* **Total Classes** (`total_classes`, number, required): Enter the total number of classes in the course.

Please note that some fields may have specific formatting or selection options. Be sure to follow the required formats and selection options to ensure accurate and correct submission.
