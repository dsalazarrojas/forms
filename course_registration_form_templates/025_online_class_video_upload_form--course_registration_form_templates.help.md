# online_class_video_upload_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online_class_video_upload_form is designed to help instructors upload videos for online classes. This form gathers essential information about the video, such as its title, description, and upload date, as well as details about the student and course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide a title for the video.
2. Enter the name of the student who will be using the video.
3. Enter the email address of the student.
4. Enter the name of the course the video is associated with.
5. Select the date the video was uploaded.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Upload Video** (`video_info`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the video.
* **Title** (`video_title`, text, required: false): Enter a clear and concise title for the video.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the student who will be using the video.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the student.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the course the video is associated with.
* **Upload Date** (`upload_date`, date, required: false): Select the date the video was uploaded.
