# General Knowledge Examination Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The General Knowledge Examination Registration Form is designed to gather user information and course registration details for general knowledge examination purposes. The form is divided into several sections, each with its specific purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your **User Information**:
    *   Please provide your personal details, including your name and any other required information as prompted.
    *   The **Contact Us** field is for additional notes or messages that you may want to send to us.
2.  Select your **Course Information**:
    *   Choose whether you are taking the general knowledge examination by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'. This will help us understand your course interest.
3.  Provide **Test Information**:
    *   Choose whether you have taken the general knowledge examination by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'. This will help us understand your examination experience.
4.  Enter **Registration Information**:
    *   If applicable, provide any additional notes or information about your examination registration.
5.  Enter **Date** and **Time**:
    *   Select a date and time that you can be available to attend the examination.
6.  Add any **Notes**:
    *   If you have any specific notes or comments for the examination team, use this section.
7.  Enter **Email** and **Phone**:
    *   These will be used to contact you if we need further details or clarification.
8.  Confirm **Submission Status**:
    *   Select 'Yes' or 'No' if you have submitted your form or not.
9.  Confirm **Submission Date**:
    *   Select 'Yes' or 'No' if you have submitted your form on a specific date.
10. Confirm **Submission Time**:
    *   Select 'Yes' or 'No' if you have submitted your form on a specific time.
11. Confirm **Submitted Notes**:
    *   Select 'Yes' or 'No' if you have submitted any additional notes along with your form.
