# Flight School Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Flight School Training Registration Form is used to register for the Flight School Training course. This form collects basic contact information and course preferences from students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Student Name in the designated field.
2. Provide your Email address to help us contact you with course updates and information.
3. Enter your Phone number so we can reach out to you if needed.
4. Choose the date you would like to attend the course (if available).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in course materials and communication.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for course updates, materials, and other important information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Course Date** (`course_date`, date, optional): Choose the date you would like to attend the course (if available).
