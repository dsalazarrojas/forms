# Hunter Education Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering for a Hunter Education course. It will help us gather your demographic information and verify your identity.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. If you have a date of birth, enter it in the "Date Of Birth" field.
6. If you have a social security number, enter it in the "Social Security Number" field. Please note that this field is not required.
7. Select your Utah license status from the options in the "Utah License" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your given name (e.g., John).
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname (e.g., Doe).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (e.g., 555-123-4567).
* **Email**: Enter your email address (e.g., johndoe@example.com). This field is for communication purposes only.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Social Security Number**: If you have a social security number, enter it here. This field is not required.
* **Utah License**: Select whether you are a Utah resident or a non-resident.
  * **Utah resident**: You live in Utah.
  * **Non-resident**: You do not live in Utah.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled out all required fields correctly before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
