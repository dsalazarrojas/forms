# Grow Children Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Grow Children Registration Form is a tool to help course administrators collect information from students and their parents for efficient course management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the required fields marked as `required` in the form.
2. Ensure you provide accurate and correct information for each field.
3. If you are registering multiple children, fill out the form for each child separately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, `text`, required: false): This is the title of the form, which will be displayed on the screen. You can leave this field blank if you don't want to add a title.
* **Student First Name** (`student_first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the first name of the student registering.
* **Student Last Name** (`student_last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the last name of the student registering.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Parent Name** (`student_parent_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the student's parent or guardian.
* **Student Class** (`student_class`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether the student has a class.
* **Student Parent Email** (`student_parent_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the student's parent or guardian email address.
* **Student Parent Phone** (`student_parent_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's parent or guardian phone number.
* **Student Parent Address** (`student_parent_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's parent or guardian address.
* **Student Parent Phone Type** (`student_parent_phone_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of phone number provided (home, work, or mobile).
* **Student Parent Phone Number** (`student_parent_phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's parent or guardian phone number.
* **Relationship with Student** (`student_parent_relationship`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the relationship between the student and the parent or guardian.
* **Relationship with Student (Other)** (`student_parent_relationship_other`, `text`, required: false): If the relationship is not listed, enter the relationship in this field.
* **Student Notes** (`student_notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes about the student.
* **Course Title** (`course_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter the title of the course.
* **Course Start Date** (`course_start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date of the course.
* **Course End Date** (`course_end_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date of the course.
* **Course Start Time** (`course_start_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the start time of the course.
* **Course Days** (`course_days`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the days of the course.
* **Course Location** (`course_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of the course.
* **Course Duration** (`course_duration`, `text`, required: false): Enter the duration of the course.
* **Course Instructors** (`course_instructors`, `text`, required: false): Enter the instructors of the course.
* **Course Fees** (`course_fees`, `text`, required: false): Enter the fees for the course.
* **Course Additional Fees** (`course_additional_fees`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional fees for the course.
* **Course Notes** (`course_notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes about the course.

## Tips
1. Double-check the accuracy of the information before submitting.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Be thorough and provide any additional information that may be useful for course management.

This help guide is designed to be a concrete resource for filling out the Grow Children Registration Form. Please follow these steps and tips for a smooth and successful registration process.
