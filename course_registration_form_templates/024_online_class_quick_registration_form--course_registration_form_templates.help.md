# Online Class Quick Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Online Class Quick Registration Form" is designed to collect information from students for registering for online classes. This form is typically used on educational institutions' websites to gather details from students who want to join online courses. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your relevant information.
2. Choose your preferred course type.
3. Select the areas you're interested in.
4. Choose how you'd like to register (Self, Assisted, or Coached).
5. Select the topics you're interested in.
6. Choose the facilitators and teachers you'd prefer.
7. Select your level of expertise (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
8. Finally, choose the duration of your course registration (Full-time or Part-time).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as you'd like to be listed in the course registration records.
- **Email Address** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address for communication.
- **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any further communication.
- **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date of your course start or completion.
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Choose the time of your course start or end.
- **Additional Info** (`note`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or information, please add them here.
- **Course Type** (`select_one`, select, optional): Choose the type of course you're interested in: Class, Tutorial, or Workshop.
- **Interested Areas** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required): Select the areas of interest (Math, Science, History, English, Computer Science).
- **Start Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the start date of your course.
- **Start Time** (`time`, time, optional): Choose the start time of your course.
- **Registration Method** (`select_one`, select, optional): Choose how you'd like to register: Self, Assisted, or Coached.
- **Select Topics** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required): Select the topics of interest (Data, Algorithms, Database, Networking).
- **Select Facilitators** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the facilitators you'd prefer (John, Mary, Doe).
- **Select Teachers** (`select_one`, select, optional): Choose the teachers you'd prefer (Jane, Joe, Doe).
- **End Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the end date of your course.
- **Select Level** (`select_one`, select, optional): Choose your level of expertise (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
- **Select Duration** (`select_one`, select, optional): Choose the duration of your course registration (Full-time or Part-time).

## Tips
- Please ensure you're selecting the correct options for each field.
- Double-check that you've filled in all the required fields.
- If you have any issues or questions, contact your educational institution's support team.
