# Digital Empowerment For Educators Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register educators for a program or initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your personal details such as your first name, last name, school name, district name, and email.
2. Select your interests from the list provided.
3. Choose your motivation for the program.
4. Select your district interests.
5. Enter any additional suggestions you may have for the program.
6. Fill in the date and time you are available to be registered.
7. Add any notes or comments you may have.
8. Select the user name and tool assigned to you.
9. Fill in any other details as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name (1)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last name (2)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **School name (3)**: Enter the name of your school.
* **District name (4)**: Enter the name of your district.
* **Email (5)**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone (6)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Interests (7)**: Select all the interests that apply to you from the list provided.
* **Motivation (8)**: Choose your motivation for the program.
* **District interests (9)**: Select all the district interests that apply to you from the list provided.
* **Suggestions (10)**: Enter any additional suggestions you may have for the program.
* **Date (11)**: Enter the date you are available to be registered.
* **Time (12)**: Enter the time you are available to be registered.
* **Note (13)**: Add any notes or comments you may have.
* **User (14)**: Select the user name assigned to you.
* **Assigned tool (15)**: Select the tool assigned to you.
* **Output file (16)**: Fill in any other details as required.
* **Form ID (17)**: Fill in the form ID.
* **Category (18)**: Fill in the category.
* **Description (19)**: Fill in the description.
* **Assigned tool 1 (20)**: Select the first tool assigned to you.
* **Assigned tool 2 (21)**: Select the second tool assigned to you.
* **Assigned tool 3 (22)**: Select the third tool assigned to you.
* **Submitted by (23)**: Select the user name who submitted the form.
* **Form IDs (24)**: Fill in the form IDs.
* **Description (25)**: Fill in the description.
