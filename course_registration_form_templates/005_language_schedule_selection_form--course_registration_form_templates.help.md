# Language Schedule Selection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by learners to express their language preferences and schedule constraints for a course.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  The form is divided into three sections: Language Schedule Selections, Course Selection, and Schedule Selection.
2.  Select your language preference and schedule constraints using the options provided in each section.
3.  Ensure that you are familiar with the options before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Language Schedule Selections** (`lang_pref_selections`, select_multiple, **not required**): Select one or more language options that apply to you. You can choose from "Yes" or "No" for each language.
*   **Course Selection** (`course_selection`, select_one, **not required**): Select the course that best fits your interests and schedule.
*   **Schedule Selection** (`schedule_selection`, select_one, **not required**): Select your schedule preference for the course.

## Tips
*   Be honest when answering the questions to ensure that the course experience is tailored to your needs.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the course administrator.
*   The form is optional, but providing accurate and relevant information will help the course administrators better understand your needs.
