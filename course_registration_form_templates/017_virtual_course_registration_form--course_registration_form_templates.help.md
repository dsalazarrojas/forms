# Virtual Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students to register for a virtual course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the Personal Details section with your name, email address, and any additional information you would like to provide.
2. Select the course details you are interested in from the Course Enquiry section.
3. Provide your contact information in the Contact Info section.
4. If necessary, provide additional information in the Additional Info section.
5. Select the status of your course in the Course Status section.
6. Choose the type of course you are registering for in the Course Type section.
7. Click the Submit button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details** (`Personal Details`, text, optional): Fill out this section with your personal details such as name, email address, and any other relevant information.
* **Course Enquiry** (`Course Enquiry`, select_multiple, optional): Select the courses you are interested in from the available options.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, text, optional): Provide your contact information, such as phone number and address, if necessary.
* **Additional Info** (`Additional Info`, text, optional): If there is any additional information you would like to provide about yourself or the course, enter it here.
* **Course Status** (`Course Status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your course application.
* **Course Type** (`Course Type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of course you are registering for from the available options.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, text, optional): Click this button to complete your course registration.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Time of Birth** (`Time of Birth`, time, optional): Enter the time of your birth.
* **Note** (`Note`, note, optional): Enter any additional note about yourself or the course.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your birth.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your birth.
* **Integer Field** (`Integer Field`, number, optional): Enter an integer value.
* **Decimal Field** (`Decimal Field`, number, optional): Enter a decimal value.
* **Select One Field** (`Select One Field`, select_one, optional): Select one option from the available choices.
* **Select Multiple Field** (`Select Multiple Field`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple options from the available choices.
* **Date Range** (`Date Range`, text, optional): Enter a date range if necessary.
Note: Some fields in this form are optional and can be left blank if you do not have the information. Please ensure that you fill out all required fields and provide accurate information to complete your course registration process.
