# traffic_control_supervisor_course_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information, work experience, course sessions, and contact details from individuals registering for a traffic control supervisor course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the participant information on page 1.
2. Provide your work experience details on page 2.
3. Specify your course sessions on page 3.
4. Enter your contact information on page 4.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, required: false)
 Enter your basic participant details.

### **Work Experience Information** (`experience_info`, text, required: false)
 Provide a brief description of your relevant work experience.

### **Course Sessions** (`course_session`, text, required: false)
 Specify the course sessions you have attended or wish to attend.

### **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false)
 Enter your contact details so we can get in touch with you.

## Tips
- Please make sure to provide accurate and clear information in each field.
- If you are unsure about any field, you can ask your supervisor for assistance.
- You can also contact us at [insert contact info] for any questions or clarifications.
