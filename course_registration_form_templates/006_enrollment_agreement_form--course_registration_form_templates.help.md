# Enrollment Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Enrollment Agreement Form is used to collect information from parents/guardians to ensure proper student registration and to facilitate communication between the institution and the family.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and fill out all the required fields.
2. Ensure you have the necessary information and documents prior to completing the form.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enrollment Agreement Form** (`enrollment_agreement_form`, text, required: false): This field is a general text input for any additional comments or agreements related to the student's enrollment.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the parent/guardian completing this form.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the student being registered.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the school where the student will be enrolled.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the school where the student will be enrolled.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the school where the student will be enrolled.
* **School Policy** (`school_policy`, text, required: false): Enter any specific policies or rules that apply to the student's enrollment.
* **School Rules** (`school_rules`, text, required: false): Enter any specific rules that apply to the student's enrollment.
* **School Goals** (`school_goals`, text, required: false): Enter any specific goals or objectives for the student's enrollment.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, text, required: false): Sign and date this field to confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions of the student's enrollment.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, text, required: false): Sign and date this field to confirm the student's understanding of the terms and conditions of their enrollment.

## Tips
* Ensure you have all the necessary information and documents before completing the form.
* Double-check the form for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting it.
* Keep a copy of the completed form for your records.
