# Certified Phlebotomy Training - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Certified Phlebotomy Training form is designed to collect necessary information from students for the phlebotomy training program. This form is used to determine the student's eligibility for the program and to provide a seamless registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2.  Select the relevant options for each field according to your needs.
3.  Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Legal Name (`full_legal_name_registration`, Text, Required)**: Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Student Email Address (`student_email_registration`, Email, Required)**: Enter the email address you want to use for course materials and official communication.
* **Student Phone Number (`student_phone_registration`, Text, Required)**: Enter your phone number for emergency and program updates.
* **Mailing Address (`mailing_address_student`, Text, Required)**: Enter your mailing address (Street, City, State, ZIP).
* **Phlebotomy Course Session (`phlebotomy_course_session`, Select One, Required)**: Select the 4-week program cycle you wish to join (Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter).
* **Training Format (`training_format_selected`, Select One, Required)**: Select your preferred learning format (Full-Time, Part-Time, Hybrid).
* **Diploma Type Earned (`diploma_type_earned`, Select One, Required)**: Select your completed level of schooling (High School Diploma, Associate Degree, Bachelor Degree, Master Degree, Current Healthcare Professional).
* **Previous Healthcare Experience (`healthcare_background_experience`, Select One, Required)**: Answer if you have prior experience in a medical setting (Yes - Clinical, Yes - Administrative, No - Entry Level Student).
* **Immunization Status Confirmed (`immunization_status_confirmed`, Select One, Required)**: Confirm that all required healthcare vaccinations are current (Yes - All Current, No - In Progress).
* **TB Test Status Current (`tb_test_status_current`, Select One, Required)**: Confirm if you have a negative result within the last 12 months for clinical training (Yes - Negative Result, No - Pending Appointment).
* **Background Check Authorization (`background_check_authorization`, Select One, Required)**: Agree to undergo a background check for clinical placement (I Consent and Authorize, Other).
* **Mandatory Attendance Agreement (`mandatory_attendance_agreement`, Select One, Required)**: Confirm that you understand 100% attendance is required for program completion (I Understand and Agree, Other).
* **Exam Fee Policy Acknowledgement (`exam_fee_policy_acknowledgement`, Select One, Required)**: Understand that tuition covers only the first attempt of the National Exam (I Understand and Agree, Other).
* **Course Registration Date (`course_registration_date`, Date, Required)**: Enter today's date.
* **Additional Comments or Information (`additional_comments_registration`, Text, Optional)**: Enter any additional comments or information you would like to provide.
