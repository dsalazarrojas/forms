# Skills Class Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skills Class Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from participants about their skills class registration. This form helps us understand your preferences and requirements for the training session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant details in the first field.
2. Select your class preferences in the second field.
3. Choose a date and time for your training in the third field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details**: This field is used to collect your name, which is essential for registration purposes.
* **Class Selections**: This field allows you to choose which skills class you're interested in. Select one or multiple options to express your preferences.
* **Preferred Date**: This field is for selecting a date and time for your training. Please choose a date that suits you best, taking into account the training schedule.

## Tips
* Ensure you fill in your participant details accurately to avoid any discrepancies in the registration process.
* Be specific with your class selection to ensure you get matched with the right training.
* Choose a date and time that works for you to make the most out of your skills class training.
