# musical_training_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The musical training application form is designed to collect information from applicants about their musical skills, experience, and background for the purpose of evaluating and processing their applications for musical training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the applicant info (page 1) with your personal details such as name and contact information.
2. Provide information about your musical skills and experience on page 2.
3. Select your preferred appointment and slot on page 3.
4. Choose your preferred skills and instruments on page 4.
5. Provide information about your musical education and background on pages 5-6.
6. Confirm your agreement on page 7.
7. Fill out any additional pages if required.
