# musical_training_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The musical training application form is designed to collect information from applicants about their musical skills, experience, and background for the purpose of evaluating and processing their applications for musical training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the applicant info (page 1) with your personal details such as name and contact information.
2. Provide information about your musical skills and experience on page 2.
3. Select your preferred appointment and slot on page 3.
4. Choose your preferred skills and instruments on page 4.
5. Provide information about your musical education and background on pages 5-6.
6. Confirm your agreement on page 7.
7. Fill out any additional pages if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **applicant_info** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Fill out your personal details such as name and contact information.
* **personal_info** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Provide information about yourself.
* **contact_info** (`id: 3`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Provide your contact information.
* **musical_skills** (`id: 4`, `type: text`, `required: true`): List your musical skills.
* **musical_history** (`id: 5`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Provide information about your musical history.
* **appointment** (`id: 6`, `type: select_one`, `required: true`): Select your preferred appointment.
* **slot** (`id: 7`, `type: select_multiple`, `required: true`): Select your preferred slots.
* **agreement** (`id: 8`, `type: select_one`, `required: true`): Confirm your agreement.
* **email** (`id: 9`, `type: email`, `required: true`): Fill out your email address.
* **phone_number** (`id: 10`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Fill out your phone number.
* **note** (`id: 11`, `type: note`, `required: false`): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **skills_list** (`id: 12`, `type: select_multiple`, `required: false`): Select your musical skills.
* **instruments** (`id: 13`, `type: select_multiple`, `required: true`): Select your instruments.
* **experience** (`id: 14`, `type: number`, `required: false`): Provide information about your musical experience.
* **musical_history_details** (`id: 15`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Provide information about your musical history details.
* **musical_education** (`id: 16`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Provide information about your musical education.
* **music_style** (`id: 17`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Describe your music style.
* **musical_instruments** (`id: 18`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Provide information about your musical instruments.
* **musical_formation** (`id: 19`, `type: select_multiple`, `required: false`): Select your musical formation.
* **musical_background** (`id: 20`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Provide information about your musical background.
* **musical_references** (`id: 21`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Provide information about your musical references.
* **musical_goals** (`id: 22`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Describe your musical goals.
* **musical_formation_background** (`id: 23`, `type: select_multiple`, `required: false`): Select your musical background.
* **agreement2** (`id: 24`, `type: select_one`, `required: true`): Confirm your agreement.
* **note2** (`id: 25`, `type: note`, `required: false`): Add any additional comments or notes.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
- Double check your information for accuracy.
- Use clear and concise language when providing information.
- Attach any supporting documents if required.
- Review your application before submission.
- Contact the musical training staff if you have any questions or concerns.
