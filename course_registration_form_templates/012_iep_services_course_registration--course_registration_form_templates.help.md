# iep_services_course_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect participant information and preferences for course registration in the context of Individualized Educational Plan (IEP) services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by entering the **Participant Details** in the first page.
2. Move on to the **Course Preferences** page and select your preferences.
3. You may add any additional **Participant Notes** on the second page if necessary.
4. On the fourth page, **Course Preferences 2**, select whether you want to participate or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Details** (Required/Optional: False, Type: text)
  This section is where you can enter your basic participant details.
  Enter your name, email, or any other relevant information that you feel is necessary to complete your registration.
- **Course Preferences** (Required/Optional: False, Type: text)
  This section allows you to select your course preferences.
  Choose the courses you are interested in and describe your preferences in detail.
- **Participant Notes** (Required/Optional: False, Type: note)
  If you have any additional comments or notes that you would like to share with the course organizers, this is the place to do so.
  Use this field to provide any further information that may be helpful in understanding your preferences.
- **Course Preferences 2** (Required/Optional: False, Type: select_one)
  Please select whether you want to participate or not.
  Select "Yes" if you want to participate, and "No" if you do not.
