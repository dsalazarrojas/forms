# Bilingual Certification Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bilingual Certification Registration form is used to collect information from candidates who are interested in pursuing a bilingual certification. The form helps us understand your preferences and requirements, ensuring that the certification process is tailored to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the **Candidate Information** section.
2. Provide your **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number**.
3. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
4. In the **Language Certification Details** section, select your **Source Language** and **Target Language**.
5. Choose your preferred **Certification Level** and **Specialization Area**.
6. Select your preferred **Exam Date**, **Exam Time**, and **Exam Location**.
7. Specify if you need any **Special Accommodations**.
8. Choose your level of **Prior Translation Experience** and **Highest Education Level**.
9. Select your **Payment Method**.
10. If you have a **Referral Source**, let us know how you heard about us.
11. Finally, read and agree to the **Terms and Conditions**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Candidate Information

* **First Name**: Enter your given name. (Required)
* **Last Name**: Enter your family name. (Required)
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary email for certification correspondence. (Required)
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact number for appointment reminders. (Required)
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the mm/dd/yyyy format. (Required)

### Language Certification Details

* **Source Language**: Select your native or primary language. (Required)
* **Target Language**: Select the language you want to be certified in. (Required)

### Certification and Specialization

* **Certification Level**: Select the level of certification you seek. (Required)
* **Specialization Area**: Choose a field of expertise, if applicable. (Required)
* **Prior Translation Experience**: Choose your level of translation experience. (Required)
* **Highest Education Level**: Choose your highest completed education level. (Required)

### Exam and Accommodation

* **Exam Date**: Choose your preferred exam date. (Required)
* **Exam Time**: Choose your preferred exam time. (Required)
* **Exam Location**: Choose where you want to take the exam. (Required)
* **Special Accommodations**: Specify if you need any testing accommodations. (Required)

### Payment and Referral

* **Payment Method**: Choose how you will pay for the certification. (Required)
* **Referral Source**: Let us know how you heard about us, if you prefer. (Optional)

### Terms and Conditions

* **Terms and Conditions**: Read and agree to the certification policies. (Required)

### Tips

* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your honesty in providing accurate information to ensure a smooth certification process.
