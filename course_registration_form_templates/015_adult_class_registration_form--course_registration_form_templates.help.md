# Adult Class Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from adults interested in registering for classes. It collects essential details about the student, including name, contact information, and background. The form also allows students to select their preferred course and session schedule, as well as provide information about their payment method and agreement to the attendance policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Student Information**: Start by filling out the "Student Full Name" field with your full name.
2. **Contact Information**: Provide your contact phone number and email address in the corresponding fields.
3. **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. **Course Selection**: Choose the course you wish to enroll in from the list provided.
5. **Session Preference**: Select your preferred session schedule (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
6. **Days Available**: Choose all the days that work for your schedule (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, or Saturday).
7. **Background and Goals**: Briefly describe any relevant skills or classes you have completed in the past.
8. **Primary Learning Goal**: State what you hope to achieve by the end of this course.
9. **How Did You Hear About Us**: Select how you discovered our adult classes (Social Media, Website, Friend or Referral, Print Ad, or Other).
10. **Payment and Confirmation**: Select your payment method (Paid online, Pay in person, or Request scholarship).
11. **Agree to Attendance Policy**: Confirm that you agree to attend at least 80% of classes to be eligible for certification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Student Full Name
Student Full Name (`student_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official government-issued identification.

### Date of Birth
Date of Birth (`date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Contact Phone
Contact Phone (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.

### Email Address
Email Address (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for registration confirmation.

### Course Title
Course Title (`course_title`, `select_one`, required): Select the course you wish to enroll in from the provided options.

### Session Preference
Session Preference (`session_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred session schedule (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).

### Days Available
Days Available (`days_available`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the days that work for your schedule.

### Previous Experience in Topic
Previous Experience in Topic (`previous_experience_in_topic`, `text`, optional): Describe any relevant skills or classes you have completed.

### Primary Learning Goal
Primary Learning Goal (`primary_learning_goal`, `text`, required): State what you hope to achieve by the end of this course.

### How Did You Hear About Us
How Did You Hear About Us (`how_did_you_hear_about_us`, `select_one`, optional): Select how you discovered our adult classes (Social Media, Website, Friend or Referral, Print Ad, or Other).

### Registration Fee Status
Registration Fee Status (`registration_fee_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose your payment method (Paid online, Pay in person, or Request scholarship).

### Agree to Attendance Policy
Agree to Attendance Policy (`agree_to_attendance_policy`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you agree to attend at least 80% of classes to be eligible for certification.
