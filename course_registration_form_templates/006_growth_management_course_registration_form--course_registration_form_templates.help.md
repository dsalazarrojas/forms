# growth_management_course_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who are interested in enrolling in a growth management course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name in the "name" field.
2. Enter your date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field.
3. Fill in your contact information (email and phone number) if you don't want to use the existing email address from our records.
4. Select the course you are interested in from the "course" dropdown menu.
5. Review your details in the "confirm" section to make sure everything is correct.
6. Fill in your confirm email address if you don't use the existing one from our records.
7. Click "submit" to proceed with the course registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name** (`name`, `text`, required): Fill in your full name as it should appear in our records.
* **date_of_birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YY".
* **email** (`email`, `text`, optional): If you have an existing email address in our records, you can use it. Otherwise, enter a new email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **course** (`course`, `select_one`, required): Select the course you are interested in from the dropdown menu.
* **submit** (`submit`, `text`, optional): This is a confirmation button. Please review your details before clicking it.
* **confirm** (`confirm`, `note`, optional): Review your details to ensure everything is correct.
* **confirm_email** (`confirm_email`, `email`, optional): If you don't use the existing email address from our records, enter a new email address here.
* **confirm_phone** (`confirm_phone`, `text`, optional): If you don't use the existing phone number from our records, enter a new phone number here.
