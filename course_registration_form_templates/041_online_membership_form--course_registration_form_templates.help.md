# Online Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Membership Form is used to collect personal and contact information from learners who want to register for a course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2. Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
3. Select the correct options for each dropdown field.
4. If you have any allergies, please enter them in the "Allergies" field.
5. If you have a medical condition, please select it in the "Medical Condition" field.
6. If you have an emergency contact, please fill out the corresponding fields with their contact information.
7. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`3`, `email`, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`4`, `text`, required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`5`, `text`, required): Please enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Relationship with Emergency Contact** (`6`, `select_one`, required): Please select your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Student Grade** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select your student grade.
* **Payment Plan** (`8`, `select_one`, required): Please select your payment plan.
* **Class Level** (`9`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select your class level.
* **Start Date** (`10`, `date`, required): Please select the start date of the course.
* **End Date** (`11`, `date`, required): Please select the end date of the course.
* **Classes per Week** (`12`, `number`, required): Please enter the number of classes per week.
* **Class Time** (`13`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether the class has a time.
* **Student Name** (`14`, `text`, required): Please enter your name.
* **Parent's Name** (`15`, `text`, required): Please enter your parent's name (if applicable).
* **Parent's Email** (`16`, `email`, required): Please enter your parent's email (if applicable).
* **Medical Condition** (`17`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select your medical condition.
* **Allergies** (`18`, `text`, required): Please enter any allergies you have.
* **Emergency Contact Note** (`19`, `note`, optional): Please enter any note about the emergency contact (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`20`, `text`, optional): Please enter the emergency contact's phone number (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`21`, `email`, optional): Please enter the emergency contact's email (if applicable).

## Tips
* Make sure to review your information carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the course administrator.
* If you need help with the form, please ask the course administrator for assistance.
