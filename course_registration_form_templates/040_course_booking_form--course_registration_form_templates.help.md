# Course Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a course for a student, ensuring that you provide accurate and complete information to facilitate a smooth booking process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the course you wish to book.
2. Enter the student's name, ensuring it is spelled correctly.
3. Enter the student's email address, using a valid email format.
4. Enter the student's phone number, without any spaces or special characters.
5. If needed, enter any comments or additional information about the booking.
6. Review your inputs before submitting the form to avoid any errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the course you wish to book.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the student's name, ensuring it is spelled correctly.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter the student's email address, using a valid email format (`example@example.com`).
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter the student's phone number, without any spaces or special characters.
* **Course Start Date** (`course_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the course in the standard date format (`YYYY-MM-DD`).
* **Course End Date** (`course_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the course in the standard date format (`YYYY-MM-DD`).
* **Booking Comments** (`booking_comments`, note, optional): If needed, enter any comments or additional information about the booking.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check all fields before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
- If you're unsure about any field, ask for assistance.
- Be as accurate and detailed as possible to ensure a smooth booking process.
