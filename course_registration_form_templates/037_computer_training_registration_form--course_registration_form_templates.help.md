# Computer Training Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants for computer training sessions. It includes fields for registration details, course selection, and preferences. This form helps the training team to understand participant requirements and allocate resources effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Registration ID**: Enter a unique identifier for your registration.
2. **Registration Date**: Select the date of your registration.
3. **Participant Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
4. **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company.
5. **Job Title**: Enter your job title at your company.
6. **Email Address**: Enter your work email address.
7. **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
8. **Department**: Select the department you belong to from the list.
9. **Training Course**: Select the course you wish to attend.
10. **Training Level**: Choose your current skill level for the selected course.
11. **Preferred Date**: Select a preferred date for the training session.
12. **Preferred Time**: Choose a preferred time slot for the training session.
13. **Training Format**: Select your preferred format of training (in-person, online live, or online self-paced).
14. **Current Skill Level**: Rate your current skill level for the course.
15. **Learning Objectives**: Describe what you hope to learn from the training session.
16. **Special Requirements**: If you have any special needs or accommodations, please mention them here (optional).
17. **Dietary Requirements**: Select any dietary requirements for in-person training catering (optional).
18. **Payment Method**: Choose how you will pay for the training session.
19. **Number of Participants**: Enter the number of people attending the training session (if applicable).
20. **Group Discount Eligible**: If you are part of a group of 5 or more, select 'True'.
21. **How Did You Hear About Us**: Select the source through which you heard about us (optional).
22. **Terms and Conditions**: Accept the terms and conditions of the training session.
23. **Registration Status**: Select the current status of your registration (submitted, confirmed, waitlisted, or cancelled).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration ID** (`registration_id`, `text`, required): A unique identifier for your registration.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, required): The date you registered for the training session.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Your full name as it appears on your ID.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): The name of your company.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Your job title at your company.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Your work email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Your contact phone number.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): The department you belong to.
* **Training Course** (`training_course`, `select_one`, required): The course you wish to attend.
* **Training Level** (`training_level`, `select_one`, required): Your current skill level for the selected course.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, `date`, required): The preferred date for the training session.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, `select_one`, required): The preferred time slot for the training session.
* **Training Format** (`training_format`, `select_one`, required): The preferred format of training (in-person, online live, or online self-paced).
* **Current Skill Level** (`current_skill_level`, `select_one`, required): The level of your current skill for the course.
* **Learning Objectives** (`learning_objectives`, `text`, required): The goals and expectations from the training session.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, `text`, optional): Any special needs or accommodations you may require.
* **Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, `select_one`, optional): Your dietary requirements for in-person training catering.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): The method of payment for the training session.
* **Number of Participants** (`number_of_participants`, `number`, required): The number of people attending the training session.
* **Group Discount Eligible** (`group_discount_eligible`, `select_one`, required): If you are part of a group of 5 or more, select 'True'.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_did_you_hear`, `select_one`, optional): The source through which you heard about us.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_accepted`, `select_one`, required): Accept the terms and conditions of the training session.
* **Registration Status** (`registration_status`, `select_one`, required): The current status of your registration (submitted, confirmed, waitlisted, or cancelled).

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Review your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact the training team.
* If you have any special requirements, please mention them in the 'Special Requirements' field.
* If you are part of a group of 5 or more, select 'True' in the 'Group Discount Eligible' field.
