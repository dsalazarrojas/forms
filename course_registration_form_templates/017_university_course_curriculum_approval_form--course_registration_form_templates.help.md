# University Course Curriculum Approval Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for instructors to submit and committee members to review course curricula. It provides a structured process for collecting and managing course information and committee feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting the form, please ensure that you have the necessary information ready. The form has two main sections: Course Information and Committee Feedback.

1. Fill out the "Course Name" field with the title of the course being submitted.
2. Enter the "Curriculum Approval Date" if necessary, or leave blank for future reference.
3. Provide the "Course Instructor" name and contact information.
4. In the "Committee Members" field, select "Yes" if the course will be reviewed by the committee, and "No" otherwise.
5. In the "Committee Chair" field, select "Yes" if the committee chair will be involved in the review process, and "No" otherwise.
6. The "Faculty Feedback" and "Committee Feedback" fields are optional and provide space for comments.
7. In the "Changes" field, list any changes or updates to the course curriculum.
8. Select the "Approval Status" field to indicate the final approval decision.
9. If necessary, fill out the "Approval Date" and "Approval Time" fields with the date and time of approval.
10. In the "Comments from Committee", "Comments from Instructor", and "Comments from Faculty" fields, provide any additional comments or feedback.
11. Finally, enter the "Course Registration Date" and "Course Institution" for course registration information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Name**: Enter the title of the course being submitted.
* **Curriculum Approval Date**: Enter the date of approval if necessary (optional).
* **Course Instructor**: Enter the instructor's name and contact information.
* **Committee Members**: Select "Yes" if the course will be reviewed by the committee, and "No" otherwise.
* **Committee Chair**: Select "Yes" if the committee chair will be involved in the review process, and "No" otherwise.
* **Faculty Feedback**: Provide any comments or feedback from faculty members.
* **Committee Feedback**: Provide any comments or feedback from committee members.
* **Changes**: List any changes or updates to the course curriculum.
* **Approval Status**: Select the final approval decision.
* **Approval Date**: Enter the date of approval if necessary (optional).
* **Approval Time**: Enter the time of approval if necessary (optional).
* **Comments from Committee**: Provide any additional comments or feedback from committee members.
* **Comments from Instructor**: Provide any additional comments or feedback from the instructor.
* **Comments from Faculty**: Provide any additional comments or feedback from faculty members.
* **Course Registration Date**: Enter the date of course registration.
* **Course Institution**: Enter the name of the institution where the course will be held.
