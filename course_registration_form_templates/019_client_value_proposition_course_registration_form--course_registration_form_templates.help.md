# Client Value Proposition Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about you, the client, and your preferences for the course. It will help us tailor the course experience to your needs and ensure that you get the most out of your learning journey.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your personal details in the **Personal Information** section.
2. Choose your preferred course format (In-person, Online, Hybrid, or Self-paced) in the **Course Selection** section.
3. Select your experience level with the topic (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert) in the **Experience Level** section.
4. Describe your goals for taking the course in the **Learning Goals** section.
5. Explain why you're interested in this course in the **Motivation and Interest** section.
6. Indicate how committed you are to completing the course (Very Low, Low, Moderate, High, or Very High) in the **Motivation and Interest** section.
7. If you have any additional questions or comments, you can enter them in the **Additional Questions** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address that we will use to communicate with you about the course.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number that we will use to contact you about the course.
* **Organization/Company** (`organization`, text, optional): If you are registering on behalf of your organization, enter the company name here.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title or position if you are registering on behalf of your organization.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required): Select the name of the course you are interested in.
* **Preferred course format** (`course_format`, select_one, required): Choose how you want to take the course (In-person, Online, Hybrid, or Self-paced).
* **Preferred start date** (`start_date`, date, required): Choose the date you prefer to start the course.
* **Your experience level with this topic** (`experience_level`, select_one, required): Choose your level of experience with the topic (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert).
* **Relevant background or skills** (`background`, text, optional): If you have any relevant background or skills, enter them here.
* **What do you hope to achieve from this course?** (`course_goals`, text, required): Describe your goals for taking the course.
* **Specific topics you want to cover** (`specific_topics`, text, optional): If there are specific topics you would like to cover in the course, enter them here.
* **Why are you interested in this course?** (`reason_for_course`, text, required): Explain why you're interested in this course.
* **How committed are you to completing this course?** (`commitment_level`, select_one, required): Indicate how committed you are to completing the course (Very Low, Low, Moderate, High, or Very High).
* **Hours per week available for coursework** (`time_availability`, number, optional): If you have a certain amount of time available per week for coursework, enter it here.
* **Additional questions or comments** (`additional_questions`, text, optional): If you have any additional questions or comments, enter them here.
