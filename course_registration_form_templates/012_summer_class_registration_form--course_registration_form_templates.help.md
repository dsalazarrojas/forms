# Summer Class Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Class Registration Form is designed for registering students for summer classes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the title of the class you are registering for.
2.  Provide your student name.
3.  Enter your student email address.
4.  Enter your student phone number.
5.  Enter your student ID number.
6.  Provide the course title.
7.  Enter the date and time of the course.
8.  Select the course duration.
9.  Enter the name of the course instructor.
10.  Choose the level of the course (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
11.  Enter the location of the course.
12.  Provide a brief description of the course.
13.  Enter the date and time of your registration.
14.  Enter any comments you have about your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Summer Class Registration**: Enter the title of the class you are registering for.
* **Student Name**: Enter your student name.
* **Email**: Enter your student email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your student phone number.
* **Student ID**: Enter your student ID number.
* **Course Title**: Enter the course title.
* **Course Date**: Enter the date of the course.
* **Course Time**: Enter the time of the course.
* **Course Duration**: Select the duration of the course.
* **Course Instructor**: Enter the name of the course instructor.
* **Course Level**: Choose the level of the course (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Course Venue**: Enter the location of the course.
* **Course Description**: Enter a brief description of the course.
* **Student Registration Date**: Enter the date and time of your registration.
* **Student Registration Time**: Enter the time of your registration.
* **Student Registration Comments**: Enter any comments you have about your registration.
