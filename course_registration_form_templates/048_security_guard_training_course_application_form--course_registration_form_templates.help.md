# Security Guard Training Course Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from security guards who wish to register for a training course. It will guide you through the necessary steps to provide required details and course selection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill out all required fields (marked as **Required**) with your personal and course selection information.
- Enter your first name, last name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and security guard license number accurately.
- Select one training option as "Training Completed" and choose one or more courses you wish to register for under "Security Guard Training Course".
- Ensure that all fields are correctly filled before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Email** (`email`, email, **Required**): Enter your valid email address that you access frequently.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, **Required**): Enter your phone number where you can be reached easily.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, **Required**): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **Course Start Date** (`course_start_date`, date, **Required**): Enter the date you wish to start the training course.
- **Security Guard License Number** (`security_guard_license_number`, text, **Required**): Enter your valid security guard license number.
- **Training Completed** (`training_completed`, select_one, **Required**): Select "True" if you have completed the security guard training before, otherwise select "False".
- **Security Guard Training Course** (`security_guard_training_course`, select_multiple, **Optional**): Choose one or more courses you wish to register for from the provided options.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields for accuracy before submission to avoid errors.
- Make sure to select "True" or "False" correctly for "Training Completed".
- If you have any issues or concerns, please contact our support team for assistance.
