# Enterprise Change Strategy Course Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to register participants for the Enterprise Change Strategy course. It collects participant details, session preferences, and other relevant information to ensure a successful and smooth experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out each field with the required information for your participant.
2. Verify that you have entered accurate and up-to-date information for all required fields.
3. Make sure to select the correct course for your participant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details**: Enter the participant's name and other required details in this field. 
* **Session Preferences**: Enter any specific preferences or requirements for your participant's session in this field.
* **Policy Agreements**: Confirm that your participant has read and agreed to the course policies.
* **Select Course**: Choose the course that best suits your participant's needs.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of your participant's course session.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of your participant's course session.
* **Participant E-mail**: Enter your participant's email address.
* **Session E-mail**: Enter the email address of your participant's session facilitator or contact person.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your participant's phone number.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes for your participant's session. (This field is not required.)

## Tips

* Double-check all required fields before submitting the form.
* Ensure that the participant's course selection aligns with their needs and preferences.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.
