# Tennis Session Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tennis Session Registration Form is designed to collect attendee information for a tennis session. Please use this form to register attendees for your tennis session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the attendee's name in the "Attendee Name" field.
2. Provide the attendee's email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter the attendee's phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter the attendee's address in the "Address" field.
5. Select the attendee's availability for the session (morning, afternoon, or evening) in the "Availability" field.
6. Enter the preferred time slot for the session in the "Time Slot" field (optional).
7. Enter the start date of the session in the "Start Date" field (optional).
8. Enter the end date of the session in the "End Date" field (optional).
9. Enter the time of day for the session in the "Time of Day" field (optional).
10. Enter any additional attendee details in the "Attendee Details" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the attendee's name in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the attendee's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the attendee's phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the attendee's address.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the attendee's availability for the session (morning, afternoon, or evening).
* **Time Slot** (`time_slot`, `text`, required: false): Enter the preferred time slot for the session (optional).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date of the session (optional).
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date of the session (optional).
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of day for the session (optional).
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional attendee details.
