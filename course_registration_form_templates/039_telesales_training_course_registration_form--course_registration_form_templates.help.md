# Telesales Training Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from individuals interested in taking the Telesales Training Course. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
5. Enter the Date you would like to take the course in the Date field.
6. Select the Session Date for the course from the Session Date field.
7. Choose the Session Time for the course from the Session Time field.
8. Enter the Session Duration in hours for the course in the Session Duration field.
9. Enter the name of the Trainer for the course in the Trainer Name field.
10. Select any sessions you would like to attend from the Training Session field.
11. Select any additional sessions you would like to attend from the Training Session 2, Training Session 3, or Training Session 4 fields.
12. If you have any additional comments or information, please enter them in the Comments field.
