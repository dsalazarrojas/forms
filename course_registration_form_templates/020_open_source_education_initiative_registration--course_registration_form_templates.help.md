# Open Source Education Initiative Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Open Source Education Initiative is a program designed to promote open-source education and provide opportunities for individuals to develop their skills in this field. By registering for this initiative, you will be able to access a range of resources and services to help you achieve your educational goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please fill out all the required fields (indicated by the asterisk \* symbol).
* Enter your information accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
* Once you have completed the form, submit it to proceed with your registration.

### Participant Consent
* **Participant Consent** (3): \* This field is not required, but it is essential to confirm your consent to participate in the initiative. Please provide a brief statement indicating that you understand and agree to the terms of the initiative.
### Participant Email
* **Participant Email** (4): \* Please enter your valid email address. This will be used to communicate with you about the initiative and any related opportunities.
### Participant Phone
* **Participant Phone** (5): \* This field is not required, but please provide your phone number if you would like to be contacted by the initiative team.
### Education Program Details
* **Education Program Details** (6): \* This field is not required, but please provide any relevant details about your education program, such as the name of the program, its focus, or any specific goals you have.
### Nonprofit Details
* **Nonprofit Details** (7): \* This field is not required, but please provide any relevant details about the nonprofit organization you are affiliated with, such as its name and location.
### Community Group Details
* **Community Group Details** (8): \* This field is not required, but please provide any relevant details about the community group you are part of, such as its name and location.
