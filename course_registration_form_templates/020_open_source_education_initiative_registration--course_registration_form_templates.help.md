# Open Source Education Initiative Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Open Source Education Initiative is a program designed to promote open-source education and provide opportunities for individuals to develop their skills in this field. By registering for this initiative, you will be able to access a range of resources and services to help you achieve your educational goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please fill out all the required fields (indicated by the asterisk \* symbol).
* Enter your information accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
* Once you have completed the form, submit it to proceed with your registration.
