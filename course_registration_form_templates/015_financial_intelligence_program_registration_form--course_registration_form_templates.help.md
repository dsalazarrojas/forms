# Financial Intelligence Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Intelligence Program Registration Form is designed to collect participant information and preferences for the Financial Intelligence Program. This form is used to ensure a tailored experience for each participant and to facilitate a smooth program progression.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand the required and optional fields.
2. Complete each field according to the instructions provided.
3. Double-check your responses for accuracy and relevance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name and Last Name (participant_name)**: Enter your first and last names as they appear on your official identification.
* **Select a module (module_selection)**: Choose one of the available options to select a module for the program. This will help tailor the program to your needs.
* **Program Date (program_date)**: Enter the date on which you would like to start the program.
* **Program Consent (consent)**: Read and agree to the program terms and conditions.
* **Participant Email (email)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Participant Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Module 1 (module_1)**: Enter the module number you selected earlier (from the module_selection field).
* **Module 2 (module_2)**: Enter the module number you selected earlier (from the module_selection field).
* **Module 3 (module_3)**: Enter the module number you selected earlier (from the module_selection field).
* **Additional Information (participant_note)**: Enter any additional comments or notes you would like to provide.
* **Submission Confirmation (submission_confirmation)**: Confirm your submission by selecting one of the provided options.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly before submitting the form. Optional fields are provided for your convenience and may be used to enhance your program experience.
