# Alcohol Awareness Course Submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you register for an Alcohol Awareness Course. It will guide you through the necessary information required for course enrollment, ensuring you get the best possible experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
3. If you need assistance or have questions, contact the support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`participant_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`participant_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Select your gender from the provided options.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address for course materials and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, text, required): Enter your full mailing address for certificate delivery.
* **Course Type** (`course_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of course you are registering for.
* **Reason for Course** (`course_reason`, select_one, required): Select the reason for taking the course.
* **Court Case Number** (`court_case_number`, text, optional): If court-ordered, enter the court case number.
* **Preferred Course Date** (`preferred_course_date`, date, required): Select a date for the course you would like to attend.
* **Preferred Course Time** (`preferred_course_time`, select_one, required): Select the preferred time for the course.
* **Course Format** (`course_format`, select_one, required): Select how you would like to take the course.
* **Language Preference** (`language_preference`, select_one, required): Select your preferred language for instruction.
* **Special Accommodations Needed** (`special_accommodations`, select_multiple, optional): If you need any special accommodations, select the options that apply to you.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select how you will pay for the course.
* **Course Fee** (`course_fee`, number, required): Enter the total amount to be paid.
* **How Did You Hear About Us?** (`referral_source`, select_one, optional): Let us know how you heard about us.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm you agree to the course terms and conditions.
* **Cancellation Policy Acknowledgment** (`cancellation_policy_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Confirm you understand the course cancellation policy.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Tips
* Double-check the required fields for accuracy and completeness.
* Review your information before submitting the form.
* Contact the support team if you need assistance or have questions.
