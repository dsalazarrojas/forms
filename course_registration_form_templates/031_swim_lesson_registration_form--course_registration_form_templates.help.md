# Swim Lesson Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Swim Lesson Registration Form is used to collect essential information from students registering for swim lessons. This form helps the instructor and management team schedule and prepare for the lessons.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address to ensure we can contact you.
3. Provide your Phone number for any further communication.
4. Enter your Date of Birth for age verification.
5. Select your Swim Level, from Beginner to Advanced.
6. Choose your preferred Swim Style from the options provided.
7. Select your preferred Swim Frequency, from Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
8. Enter the name of your assigned Instructor.
9. Provide the Instructor's Experience for better assessment.
10. Enter the Course Length you plan to take.
11. Choose your preferred Course Schedule for the lessons.
12. Select your Swim Type, from Lessons, Group Classes, or Private Classes.
13. Enter the time and duration of each Lesson.
14. Enter the expected number of Students for the Lesson.
15. Provide the Student Fee for each Lesson.
16. Enter the Instructor Fee for each Lesson.
17. Select the Instructor's Availability, from Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
18. List any additional Instructor Qualifications for better understanding.
19. Confirm if you have any Student Contracts or not.
20. Confirm if you have any Instructor Contracts or not.
21. Enter any additional Notes for your Lesson.
22. Click on Save and Continue or Save and Exit to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name. This is a required field.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name. This is a required field.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This is required for communication.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for any further communication.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth for age verification. (Optional)
* **Swim Level**: Choose your swim level from Beginner to Advanced. (Required)
* **Swim Style**: Choose your preferred swim style from the options provided. (Optional)
* **Swim Frequency**: Select how often you wish to swim, from Daily, Weekly, or Monthly. (Optional)
* **Instructor Name**: Enter the name of your assigned Instructor. (Optional)
* **Instructor Experience**: Enter the instructor's experience for better assessment. (Optional)
* **Course Length**: Enter the length of the course you plan to take. (Optional)
* **Course Schedule**: Choose your preferred course schedule. (Optional)
* **Swim Type**: Select your preferred swim type from Lessons, Group Classes, or Private Classes. (Optional)
* **Lesson Time**: Enter the time of each lesson. (Optional)
* **Lesson Duration**: Enter the duration of each lesson. (Optional)
* **Student Number**: Enter the expected number of students for the lesson. (Optional)
* **Student Fee**: Enter the student fee for each lesson. (Optional)
* **Instructor Fee**: Enter the instructor fee for each lesson. (Optional)
* **Instructor Availability**: Select the instructor's availability from Morning, Afternoon, or Evening. (Optional)
* **Instructor Qualifications**: Provide any additional qualifications for the instructor. (Optional)
* **Student Contracts**: Confirm if you have any student contracts. (Optional)
* **Instructor Contracts**: Confirm if you have any instructor contracts. (Optional)
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes for your lesson. (Optional)

## Tips

* Please fill in the required fields with accurate information.
* Select the best fit option for your swim style and frequency.
* Ensure to provide accurate and complete notes for your lesson.
* Review the information carefully before selecting Save and Continue or Save and Exit.
