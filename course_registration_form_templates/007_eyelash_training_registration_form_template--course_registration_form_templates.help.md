# Eyelash Training Registration Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Eyelash Training Registration Form Template is a generic form designed to collect basic information from students for various training programs, including eyelash training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the following fields:
	* **Student Details**: Provide your personal details, such as your name, email, and phone number.
	* **Course Details**: Enter the course name and any additional comments about your course selection.
	* **Contact Details**: Provide your contact information, including your address and any other relevant contact details.
	* **Payment Details**: Enter any payment-related information, such as payment method and any other relevant payment details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Details** (Student Details, text, required): Enter your personal details, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Course Details** (Course Details, text, required): Enter the course name and any additional comments about your course selection.
* **Contact Details** (Contact Details, text, required): Provide your contact information, including your address and any other relevant contact details.
* **Payment Details** (Payment Details, text, optional): Enter any payment-related information, such as payment method and any other relevant payment details.
* **Submit** (Submit, text, required): Click the submit button to send your registration information to the training provider.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the training provider for support.
