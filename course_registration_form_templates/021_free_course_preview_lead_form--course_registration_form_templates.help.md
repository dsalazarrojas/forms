# free_course_preview_lead_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form captures lead information for a free course preview.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
2. Provide accurate and truthful information.
3. Select the most suitable options for your course interest and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Free Course Preview**: Enter a brief description of the course you are interested in.
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Course Preview**: Select the course you are interested in.
* **Course Name**: Enter the name of the course you are interested in.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the course.
* **Course Interest**: Indicate your level of interest in the course (High, Medium, Low).
* **Course Type**: Select the type of course you are interested in (Online, In-person, Hybrid).
* **Course Format**: Select the format of the course (Video, Live, Interactive).
* **Lead Capture**: Indicate if you are interested in capturing leads for this course.
* **Course Status**: Indicate if the course is available or not.
* **Course Description**: Enter a brief description of the course.
* **Course Link**: Enter the link to the course.
* **Submit**: Submit the form after filling out all fields.

Note: This form should only be submitted once to prevent duplication. Please ensure all fields are accurate and complete before submitting.
