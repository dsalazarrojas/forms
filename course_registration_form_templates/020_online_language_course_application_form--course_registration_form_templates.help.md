# Online Language Course Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from applicants interested in registering for an online language course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Type your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select your answer to "Has the course been helpful?" in the "Purpose" field.
5. Select your preferred languages in the "Preferred Language" field.
6. Choose your level for the course in the "Course Level" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Name (1)
* **Name**: (`text`, `Name`, required)
  This field is for your full name, which will be used to address and contact you.

### Email (2)
* **Email** (`email`, `Email`, required)
  Enter your email address that we can use to communicate with you about your application.

### Phone (3)
* **Phone** (`text`, `Phone`, required)
  This is your phone number that we can use to contact you in case of any urgent matters.

### Purpose (4)
* **Has the course been helpful?** (`select_one`, `Purpose`, required)
  Please select "Yes" if the language course has helped you in the past or will benefit you in the future.

### Preferred Language (5)
* **Preferred Language** (`select_multiple`, `Preferred Language`, required)
  Select all the languages you are interested in learning or would prefer as the course medium.

### Course Level (6)
* **Course Level** (`select_one`, `Course Level`, required)
  Choose the level of the course you need: High for more challenging courses or Low for beginner-friendly courses.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth application process.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact us for assistance.
* We will assess your application based on the information provided.
