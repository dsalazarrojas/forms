# Process Hazard Simulation Course Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Process Hazard Simulation Course Registration form is designed to collect necessary information from trainees and instructors for the process hazard simulation course. This includes contact information, employee type, role, course participation, simulation details, location, and contact information for the training manager and instructor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact details, including name, email, and phone number.
2. Select your employee type from the list of options (Manager, Supervisor, or Employee).
3. Choose your role from the available options.
4. Indicate whether you will be participating in the course.
5. Select the simulation details (Yes or No).
6. Choose the location for the course (Yes or No).
7. Select the training facility from the available options (Yes or No).
8. Enter the training facility address.
9. Enter any additional information or notes about the training.
10. Confirm that the submitted information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Details
* **Contact Details**: Enter your name, email, and phone number to provide contact information.
* **Employee Type**: Select your role from the available options (Manager, Supervisor, or Employee).
* **Role**: Choose your role in the organization.
* **Course**: Indicate whether you will be participating in the course.
* **Simulation**: Select the simulation details (Yes or No).
* **Start Date**: Enter the date when you expect the course to start.
* **Start Time**: Enter the time when you expect the course to start.
* **End Date**: Enter the date when you expect the course to end.
* **End Time**: Enter the time when you expect the course to end.
* **Location**: Choose the location of the training (Yes or No).
* **Training Facility**: Select the training facility from the available options (Yes or No).
* **Training Facility Address**: Enter the address of the training facility.
* **Training Facility Map**: Enter any additional information about the training facility.
* **Training Manager**: Indicate whether the training manager is involved (Yes or No).
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Training Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Training Email**: Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information or notes about the training.
* **Submitted By**: Indicate whether you have submitted the form.
* **Submitted By Role**: Choose your role at the time of submission (Yes or No).
* **Submitted By Title**: Enter your title at the time of submission.
* **Submitted Date**: Enter the date when you submitted the form.

#Tips

* Make sure to accurately provide all required fields.
* Double-check for spelling and formatting errors before submission.
* If you need help or have questions, contact the training manager or instructor.
