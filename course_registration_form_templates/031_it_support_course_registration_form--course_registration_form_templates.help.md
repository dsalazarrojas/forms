# it_support_course_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information for an IT support course. It is essential to provide accurate and complete data to ensure smooth enrollment and effective support during the course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "participant_info" field with your name and any other relevant personal details.
2. Select multiple participant info fields that apply to you (if any).
3. Provide course information in the "course_info" field.
4. Add any additional enrollment details in the "enrollment_details" field.
5. Finally, click the "submit_button" to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant_info** (`participant_info`, `text`, required): Your name and any other personal details.
* **participant_info_fields** (`participant_info_fields`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select multiple participant info fields that apply to you (if any).
* **course_info** (`course_info`, `text`, optional): Provide information about the course you are registering for.
* **enrollment_details** (`enrollment_details`, `text`, optional): Add any additional enrollment details.
* **participant_info** (participant_info, `text`, required): This is the same field as participant_info_1. 
* **participant_info_3** (`participant_info_3`, `text`, optional): Not used in this form.

Note: Please fill out fields with correct and accurate information to ensure a smooth experience. 

## Tips
- Make sure to proofread your form before submission to avoid any errors.
- If you have any issues with the form, contact your support team.
