# exam-date-voting-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather user input for an exam date voting process. Users can choose their preferred date from the available options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the available dates for the exam.
2. Select your preferred date for the exam.
3. Optionally, choose an additional preferred date, if desired.
4. Submit your response to be included in the voting process.
