# photography_course_payment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The photography_course_payment_form is a payment form for registering students for a photography course. It is used by institutions or organizations to collect payment details from students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the `Course Name` field with the name of the photography course you are registering for.
* Fill in the `Student Name` field with the actual name of the student.
* Fill in the `Student Phone` field with a valid phone number.
* Choose a payment method from the `Payment Method` field.
* If credit card payment is selected, fill in the `Credit Card Number` field with the credit card number.
* Fill in the `Email` field with the student's email address.
* If applicable, fill in the `Course Fees` field with the course fees amount.
* Optionally, you can add notes to the `Notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, not required): The name of the photography course you are registering for.
* **Course Duration** (`course_duration`, number, not required): The duration of the course in units of time (e.g., hours, days, weeks, etc.).
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, not required): The actual name of the student.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, not required): Choose a payment method (e.g., Credit Card, PayPal).
* **Credit Card Number** (`credit_card_number`, text, not required): Fill in the credit card number if credit card payment is selected.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): The student's email address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, not required): A valid phone number for the student.
* **Course Fees** (`course_fees`, number, not required): The amount of course fees.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required): Any additional information you would like to add.
