# Continuing Education Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect student information for continuing education purposes. It is designed to ensure accurate and complete information is collected from students to process their enrollment in courses offered by our institution.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Student Full Name field with your full name.
2. Enter your Student ID number (if applicable).
3. Enter your primary Email Address.
4. Enter your Phone Number for contact purposes.
5. Enter your Date of Birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
6. Enter your Mailing Address.
7. Enter your City.
8. Enter your State.
9. Enter your ZIP Code.
10. Select the Course Selection that you wish to enroll in.
11. Select the Course Section that suits your schedule.
12. Select the enrollment period that you prefer.
13. Choose your Enrollment Type (Full-time or part-time).
14. Select your Prior Education level.
15. If your employer is sponsoring your education, select True. Otherwise, select False.
16. Choose your Payment Method for tuition fees.
17. If you require special accommodations, enter the details in the Special Accommodations field (this field is optional).
18. Enter the Emergency Contact Name and their Phone Number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Full Name** (Required): Enter your full name for our records.
* **Student ID** (Optional): Enter your student ID number (if you have one).
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter your primary email for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (Required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Mailing Address** (Required): Enter your street address for our records.
* **City** (Required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (Required): Enter your state of residence.
* **ZIP Code** (Required): Enter your zip code.
* **Course Selection** (Required): Select the course you wish to enroll in from the list provided.
* **Course Section** (Required): Select the course section that best suits your schedule.
* **Semester or Term** (Required): Select the enrollment period that you prefer.
* **Enrollment Type** (Required): Select whether you are enrolling as a Full-time or part-time student.
* **Prior Education** (Required): Select your highest educational level completed.
* **Employer Sponsorship** (Required): If your employer is sponsoring your education, select True. Otherwise, select False.
* **Payment Method** (Required): Select how you will pay for your tuition fees.
* **Special Accommodations** (Optional): If you require any special accommodations, enter the details here.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Terms Agreement** (Required): Select I agree to the terms and conditions to complete your enrollment.
