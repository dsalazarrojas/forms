# Training Request For Nursing Education - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request for nursing education training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the training you are requesting (e.g. "Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation Training").
2. Enter the name of the course you are interested in (e.g. "Basic Life Support").
3. Choose the type of course you are looking for (e.g. "Basic", "Advanced", or "Intermediate").
4. Enter the start and end dates of the course (e.g. "2022-01-01" and "2022-01-31").
5. Choose the location where you would like to attend the course (e.g. "Hospital A").
6. Enter the time of the course (e.g. "08:00-16:00").
7. Enter the fees for the course (e.g. "100").
8. Choose the frequency of the fees (e.g. "Once", "Recurant", or "One-time").
9. Choose the currency of the fees (e.g. "USD", "AUD", or "EUR").
10. If other currency is chosen, enter the alternative currency.
11. Enter the name of the instructor who will lead the course (e.g. "John Doe, RN").
12. Enter a brief description of the course (e.g. "This course covers basic life support skills").
13. Enter the name of the course organizer (e.g. "Jane Smith, Training Department").
14. Enter the email and phone number of the trainee (e.g. "jane.smith@example.com", "123-456-7890").
15. Enter the email and phone number of the trainee's supervisor (e.g. "john.doe@example.com", "123-456-9870").
16. Add any additional notes about the training request (e.g. "Please ensure the course is available for nurses with a level 2 certification").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Request Form (1)**: Enter the title of the training you are requesting.
* **Course Name (2)**: Enter the name of the course you are interested in.
* **Course Type (3)**: Choose the type of course you are looking for (e.g. "Basic", "Advanced", or "Intermediate").
* **Course Level (4)**: Choose the level of course you are looking for (e.g. "Basic", "Advanced", or "Intermediate").
* **Start Date (5)**: Enter the start date of the course (e.g. "2022-01-01").
* **End Date (6)**: Enter the end date of the course (e.g. "2022-01-31").
* **Location (7)**: Enter the location where you would like to attend the course (e.g. "Hospital A").
* **Time (8)**: Enter the time of the course (e.g. "08:00-16:00").
* **Fees (9)**: Enter the fees for the course (e.g. "100").
* **Fees Frequency (10)**: Choose the frequency of the fees (e.g. "Once", "Recurant", or "One-time").
* **Currency (11)**: Choose the currency of the fees (e.g. "USD", "AUD", or "EUR").
* **Other Currency (12)**: If other currency is chosen, enter the alternative currency.
* **Instructor (13)**: Enter the name of the instructor who will lead the course (e.g. "John Doe, RN").
* **Description (14)**: Enter a brief description of the course (e.g. "This course covers basic life support skills").
* **Organizer (15)**: Enter the name of the course organizer (e.g. "Jane Smith, Training Department").
* **Trainee Email (16)**: Enter the email of the trainee (e.g. "jane.smith@example.com").
* **Trainee Phone (17)**: Enter the phone number of the trainee (e.g. "123-456-7890").
* **Trainee Address (18)**: Enter the address of the trainee (e.g. "123 Main St").
* **Trainee Department (19)**: Enter the department of the trainee (e.g. "Pediatrics").
* **Trainee Job Title (20)**: Enter the job title of the trainee (e.g. "Registered Nurse").
* **Other Job Title (21)**: If the trainee has another job title, enter it here (e.g. "Nurse Practitioner").
* **Trainee Supervisor (22)**: Enter the name of the trainee's supervisor (e.g. "Jane Smith, RN").
* **Supervisor Email (23)**: Enter the email of the trainee's supervisor (e.g. "jane.smith@example.com").
* **Supervisor Phone (24)**: Enter the phone number of the trainee's supervisor (e.g. "123-456-7890").
* **Notes (25)**: Add any additional notes about the training request (e.g. "Please ensure the course is available for nurses with a level 2 certification").
