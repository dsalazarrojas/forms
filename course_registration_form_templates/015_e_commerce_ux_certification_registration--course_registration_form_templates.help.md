# E Commerce UX Certification Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information for E Commerce UX Certification Registration. The form gathers data on the user's background, experience, and goals related to e-commerce and UX certification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your User Details
2. Select your Experience Levels (choose all that apply)
3. Share your Motivations for taking the certification
4. Provide your Education Background (if applicable)
5. Describe your Work Experience (if applicable)
6. Outline your E-commerce Background (if applicable)
7. Set your Certification Goals
8. Enter your Contact Info (email address)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details (Text)**: Please enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Experience Levels (Select Multiple)**: Choose all the levels that apply to your current level of experience in e-commerce and UX.
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
* **Motivations (Text)**: Share why you are interested in taking the E Commerce UX Certification.
* **Education Background (Text)**: If you have a degree or relevant education in a field related to e-commerce and UX, please provide details.
* **Work Experience (Text)**: If you have relevant work experience in e-commerce or UX, describe it briefly.
* **E-commerce Background (Text)**: Provide any relevant background or experience in e-commerce that may be helpful to understand your qualifications.
* **Certification Goals (Text)**: Specify what you hope to achieve or gain from taking the E Commerce UX Certification.
* **Contact Info (Email)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for any communication related to your registration.
