# Organizational Capability Course Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Organizational Capability Course Registration" form is designed to collect participant information, payment details, and course registration data for organizational capabilities courses. This form helps to ensure that participants provide accurate and complete information, allowing us to efficiently process their course registrations and maintain records.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Participant Information section with your personal details, including name, email, phone number, address, and other relevant information.
2. Review and select the payment details, including payment amount and method.
3. Provide any additional notes or comments in the Participant Notes field.
4. Read and accept the terms and conditions by selecting the correct option in the Terms Accepted field.
5. Sign and date the form by providing your signature in the Participant Signature field.
6. If applicable, provide your date of birth, driver's license number, ID number, social security number, gender, ethnicity, race, marital status, language, and any additional notes in the corresponding fields.
7. Finally, select the Form Completion option to indicate that you have read and understood the form and its contents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, text, required/optional): This section requires your personal details, including name, email, phone number, address, and any other relevant information.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, required/optional): Review and select the payment amount and method to ensure accurate payment processing.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, select_one, required/optional): Read and accept the terms and conditions by selecting the correct option.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name to associate with your course registration.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address to receive important updates and notifications.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you efficiently.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your address to ensure accurate delivery of course materials and updates.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the course you are registering for.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the payment amount for the course.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required/optional): Select the payment method to ensure accurate processing.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about your course registration.
* **Terms Accepted** (`terms_accepted`, select_one, required/optional): Select the option to indicate that you have read and accepted the terms and conditions.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, select_one, required/optional): Select the option to indicate your agreement with the course terms.
* **Form Completion** (`form_completion`, select_one, required/optional): Select the option to indicate that you have read and understood the form and its contents.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, required/optional): Sign and date the form to confirm your participation.
* **Participant DOB** (`participant_dob`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth, if applicable.
* **Participant DHID** (`participant_dhid`, text, required/optional): Enter your driver's license number, if applicable.
* **Participant ID Number** (`participant_id_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your ID number, if applicable.
* **Participant SSN** (`participant_ssn`, text, required/optional): Enter your social security number, if applicable.
* **Participant Gender** (`participant_gender`, select_one, required/optional): Select your gender, if applicable.
* **Participant Ethnicity** (`participant_ethnicity`, text, required/optional): Enter your ethnicity, if applicable.
* **Participant Race** (`participant_race`, text, required/optional): Enter your race, if applicable.
* **Participant Marital Status** (`participant_marital_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select your marital status, if applicable.
* **Participant Language** (`participant_language`, text, required/optional): Enter your language, if applicable.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your course registration.

## Tips

* Please review each field carefully to ensure accurate and complete information.
* Enter all required fields to avoid delays in processing your course registration.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
