# Online Physio Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for online physio courses. It collects essential information from users, including their personal details and course preferences. Please ensure you have the necessary permissions to register for the course before filling out this form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you are registering for the correct course.
2. Fill in your First name in the "First name" field.
3. Fill in your Last name in the "Last name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Enter your Date of birth in the "Date of birth" field.
7. Select the Physio course you wish to register for from the "Physio course" field.
8. Choose the duration of the course you want to register for in the "Duration" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Fill in your first name as it is displayed on your identification documents.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Fill in your last name as it is displayed on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your valid email address. This will be used for further communication regarding the course.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Physio course** (`physio_course`, select_one, required: false): Select the Physio course you wish to register for. The available options are:
	+ Physiotherapy 101
	+ Physiotherapy 202
	+ Physiotherapy 303
* **Duration** (`physio_course_duration`, number, required: false): Choose the duration of the Physio course you wish to register for.
