# Videographer Training Course Selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users who are interested in video training courses. Fill out this form to provide your personal details and course selection preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Personal Details" section:
	* Enter your name in the "Name" field.
	* Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
2. Select your training interest in the "Training Interest" dropdown menu.
3. Select your training availability in the "Training Availability" dropdown menu.
4. Select the "Training Dates" you are interested in.
5. Enter your "Training Location" in the text field.
6. Select the duration of the course in the "Training Duration" field.
7. Enter the training price in the "Training Price" field.
8. Select your preferred payment method.
9. Leave any additional comments or notes about your training interest or preferences in the "Notes" field.
10. You can also enter any other relevant details about your course interest or preferences in the "Phone", "Course ID", "Course Name", "Workshop ID", "Studio ID", "School ID", "Organizer ID", "School Name", "Studio Name", "Organizer Name" fields if needed.
