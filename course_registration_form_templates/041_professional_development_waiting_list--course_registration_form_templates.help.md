# Professional Development Waiting List - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Professional Development Waiting List" form is used to collect information from potential participants about their interest in professional development programs. The form helps program administrators or coordinators to manage the waiting list for these programs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information:
	* Participant Name: Enter your name and any other name(s) you are interested in enrolling.
	* Email: Enter your email address.
	* Phone: Enter your phone number.
	* Program of Interest: Select one or more programs you are interested in.
	* Location: Choose one or more locations where you would like to participate in the program.
	* Date: Choose a date when you would like to participate in the program.
	* Time: Choose a time when you would like to participate in the program.
	* Follow-up Intensity: Choose how often you would like to be followed up with regarding the program (Low, Medium, High).
	* Follow-up Method: Choose how you would like to be followed up (Phone, Email, Chat).
	* Follow-up Frequency: Choose how frequently you would like to be followed up (Once, Twice, Weekly, Monthly).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (text): Enter your name and any other name(s) you are interested in enrolling.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Program of Interest** (select_one): Select one program that interests you.
* **Location** (select_multiple): Choose one or more locations where you would like to participate in the program.
* **Date** (date): Choose a date when you would like to participate in the program.
* **Time** (time): Choose a time when you would like to participate in the program.
* **Follow-up Intensity** (select_one): Choose how often you would like to be followed up with regarding the program (Low, Medium, High).
* **Follow-up Method** (select_one): Choose how you would like to be followed up (Phone, Email, Chat).
* **Follow-up Frequency** (select_multiple): Choose how frequently you would like to be followed up (Once, Twice, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Note** (note): Add any additional notes or comments about your interest or preferences.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields.
* If you select multiple options for "Program of Interest" and "Location", separate them with a comma.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, use the "Note" field to add them.
