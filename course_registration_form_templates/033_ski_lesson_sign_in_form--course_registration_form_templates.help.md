# ski_lesson_sign_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ski lesson sign-in form is a crucial tool for registering students for ski lessons. This form helps instructors and administrators gather essential information about each student, including their parent's contact details and lesson preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to provide the necessary information for lesson planning and parental contact.
2. Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting the form to avoid any errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): This field is for the parent or guardian's name to be recorded.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, optional): Enter the parent or guardian's email address for communication purposes.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, optional): Provide the parent or guardian's phone number for easy contact.
* **Lesson Details** (`lesson_details`, note, optional): Describe the specific lesson type, level, or any special requirements for the student.
* **Ski Date** (`ski_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the planned ski lesson.
* **Rental Details** (`rental_details`, note, optional): If applicable, specify any rental equipment needed for the student.
* **Sign Up Date** (`signup_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the student was signed up for the lesson.

## Tips
* Double-check the form for any missing or incorrect information before submitting it.
* Make sure to enter accurate contact information for the parent or guardian.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact the ski school or resort administration for assistance.
