# SEO Training Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SEO Training Enrollment Form is designed to gather information from users to provide a tailored course experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your Last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field. Make sure to use a valid email address.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone number" field.
5. Select one or multiple courses from the "Course choice" field.
6. Select the date of the course from the "Course date" field.
7. Select the time of the course from the "Course time" field.
8. Select the day of the week the course will be held from the "Course day of the week" field.
9. Select the duration of the course from the "Course duration" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This will help the course organizers to contact you.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number. This will help the course organizers to contact you.
* **Course choice**: Select one or multiple courses you are interested in. This will help the course organizers to tailor the course content to your needs.
* **Course date**: Select the date of the course. This will help the course organizers to schedule the course accordingly.
* **Course time**: Select the time of the course. This will help the course organizers to schedule the course accordingly.
* **Course day of the week**: Select the day of the week the course will be held. This will help the course organizers to schedule the course accordingly.
* **Course duration**: Select the duration of the course. This will help the course organizers to prepare the course content and schedule accordingly.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate information in all fields.
* Selecting the correct course options will help us to provide the best course experience for you.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [email address].
