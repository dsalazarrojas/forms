# Online Class Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from registrants for online classes or tutorials. It will likely be used to identify course preferences or registration status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please select or enter the following information:
	* **Course Selection**: Choose "Yes" if you are interested in registering for this course, "No" otherwise.
	* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact details, including your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
	* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the required format (mm/dd/yyyy).
	* **Time of Day**: Enter the time of day you are most available to participate.
	* **Email Address**: Enter your email address to receive communications and updates about the course.
	* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in the format: XXX-XXX-XXXX.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Selection** (`Yes`, `No`, optional): This field is used to determine if you are interested in registering for the course. Select "Yes" to express your interest.
* **Contact Information** (`text`, optional): This field collects your contact details, allowing us to reach out to you about the course.
* **Date of Birth** (`date`, optional): Enter your date of birth to help us better understand your demographic information.
* **Time of Day** (`time`, optional): Enter your time of day to help us schedule events or communications more effectively.
* **Email Address** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address to receive important updates and notifications about the course.
* **Phone Number** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number to be contacted in case of emergencies or updates.
