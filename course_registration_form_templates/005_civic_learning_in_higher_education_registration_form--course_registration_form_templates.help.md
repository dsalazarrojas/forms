# Civic Learning In Higher Education Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for students to register for various programs offered by the Civic Learning program in higher education. It collects information on student demographics, academic status, program preferences, and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill in this form, please follow these steps:

- Read each field's label and hint carefully.
- Fill in all mandatory fields (those marked as 'required' in the field labels).
- Select all relevant options for each field, such as selecting your academic year and preferred schedule.
- Double-check that you have filled in all fields correctly before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name (student_name)** (Text, Required): Enter your full name, including first and last names.
* **Student ID Number (student_id)** (Text, Required): Enter your university-issued student ID number.
* **University Email Address (email_address)** (Email, Required): Enter your university email address.
* **Phone Number (phone_number)** (Text, Required): Enter your personal contact number.
* **Enrollment Status (enrollment_status)** (Select One, Required): Select your current status (Full-time Undergraduate, Part-time Undergraduate, Full-time Graduate, Part-time Graduate, Non-degree Student).
* **Academic Year (academic_year)** (Select One, Required): Select your current academic year (First Year, Sophomore, Junior, Senior, Graduate Student, Other).
* **Major or Field of Study (major_field)** (Text, Required): Enter your primary major or field of study.
* **Civic Learning Program (program_selection)** (Select One, Required): Select the program you wish to register for (Community Engagement Certificate, Public Policy Internship, Service Learning Course, Civic Research Project, Leadership Development Program, Voter Education Initiative).
* **Semester or Term (semester_term)** (Select One, Required): Select when you want to start (Fall Semester, Spring Semester, Summer Session, Winter Term).
* **Desired Credit Hours (credit_hours)** (Select One, Required): Select how many credits you wish to enroll for (1 credit, 2 credits, 3 credits, 4 credits, Non-credit).
* **Prior Experience (prior_experience)** (Select One, Required): Select your prior experience level (Extensive experience, Some experience, Limited experience, No experience).
* **Describe Your Civic Experience (experience_description)** (Text, Optional): Briefly describe any relevant experience.
* **Learning Goals (learning_goals)** (Text, Required): State your learning goals for the program.
* **Community Issues of Interest (community_interest)** (Select Multiple, Required): Select all issues that interest you the most (Education Equity, Environmental Justice, Food Security, Housing and Homelessness, Healthcare Access, Criminal Justice Reform, Economic Development, Youth Development, Senior Services, Disability Rights).
* **Preferred Schedule (schedule_preference)** (Select Multiple, Required): Select your preferred schedule options (Morning classes, Afternoon classes, Evening classes, Weekend activities, Flexible schedule).
* **Transportation Method (transportation_method)** (Select One, Required): Select your primary transportation method (Personal Vehicle, Public Transportation, Bicycle or Walking, University Shuttle, Carpool, Need transportation assistance).
* **Academic Advisor Name (advisor_name)** (Text, Optional): Enter your academic advisor's full name.
* **Emergency Contact Name (emergency_contact)** (Text, Required): Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (emergency_phone)** (Text, Required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Additional Information (additional_comments)** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional information or special needs you have.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you are not duplicating a registration.
- Fill in all mandatory fields.
- Check your information carefully before submission.
