# Global Online Academy Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help students register for online courses offered by Global Online Academy. Fill out this form to provide information about the course you're interested in and your personal details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each section carefully and provide the necessary information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
3. Double-check your information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Details** (`1`, text, required/optional): Provide the title and description of the course you're interested in.
* **Student Details** (`2`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, email, and phone number to create an account.
* **Sign-up Preferences** (`3`, text, required/optional): Specify your preferences for course enrollment, including start and end dates.
* **Course Enrollment** (`4`, text, required/optional): Enter the course you're enrolled in and other relevant details.
* **Time Zone** (`5`, select_one, required/optional): Select your time zone to ensure you're in the correct time zone for the course.
* **Date of Birth** (`6`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth for verification purposes.
* **Course Start Date** (`7`, date, required/optional): Specify the start date of the course you're enrolled in.
* **Course End Date** (`8`, date, required/optional): Specify the end date of the course you're enrolled in.
* **Course Duration** (`9`, number, required/optional): Enter the duration of the course.
* **Student Name** (`10`, text, required/optional): Enter your name for account creation.
* **Student Email** (`11`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Student Phone** (`12`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Course Title** (`13`, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the course you're enrolled in.
* **Course Description** (`14`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the course.
* **Student Address** (`15`, text, required/optional): Enter your address.
* **Course Price** (`16`, number, required/optional): Enter the price of the course.
* **Student Level** (`17`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your student level (High or Low).
* **Course Language** (`18`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the language of the course.
* **Time Zone** (`19`, select_one, required/optional): Select your time zone for the course.
* **Payment Method** (`20`, select_one, required/optional): Select the payment method you'd like to use.
* **Payment Status** (`21`, select_one, required/optional): Select the status of your payment (Active or Inactive).
* **Course Start Time** (`22`, time, required/optional): Enter the start time of the course.
* **Course Days** (`23`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the days you'd like to attend the course.
* **Course Price Currency** (`24`, select_one, required/optional): Select the currency of the course price.
* **Course Price** (`25`, number, required/optional): Enter the price of the course.

Note: Please be sure to fill out all required fields accurately, and double-check your information before submitting the form.
