# mentoring_program_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in participating in a mentoring program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form structure and content to ensure it aligns with your needs.
2. Complete each field as prompted, providing accurate and relevant information.
3. Use the "Attachments" field to upload any supporting documents or materials required for your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Information** (`personal_information`, `text`, required: false): Enter your personal details such as name, email, and other relevant contact information.
* **Educational Background** (`educational_background`, `text`, required: false): Describe your educational background, including relevant coursework, degrees, or certifications.
* **Financial Information** (`financial_information`, `text`, required: false): Provide information about your financial situation, including any relevant income or financial aid information.
* **Simple Questions** (`simple_questions`, `text`, required: false): Answer a series of short questions to help us understand your motivations and goals.
* **Essay** (`essay`, `text`, required: false): Write a short essay explaining why you are interested in the mentoring program and how you think you can benefit from it.
* **Attachments** (`attachments`, `text`, required: false): Upload any supporting documents or materials that you think are relevant to your application.

## Tips
- Ensure you provide accurate and complete information in each field.
- Use clear and concise language in the essay field.
- Attach only relevant and required documents to the "Attachments" field.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
