# Virtual Classroom Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from students for a virtual classroom. The form is used to gather data such as name, email, phone number, date of birth, time zone, and course selection for each student. This form is not a substitute for official registration processes, but rather a supporting tool to aid the administration of virtual classrooms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your **Email** address to allow us to contact you regarding the course.
3. Provide your **Phone** number if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
4. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the format MM/DD/YY.
5. Select your **Time Zone** from the provided options.
6. Choose the **Course** you are interested in by selecting all applicable options.
7. Add any **Notes** regarding your registration.
8. You are not required to fill in **Time** or **Submission Date** and **Submission Time** fields.
9. Finally, click the submit button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address to receive communication from us.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to receive calls from us.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YY format.
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, select_one, optional): Select your time zone from the options provided.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you are available in 24-hour format (00:00-24:00).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or information regarding your registration.
* **Course** (`course`, select_multiple, required): Select all courses you are interested in by clicking the checkboxes.
* **Registered** (`registered`, time, optional): Enter the time you are registered in 24-hour format (00:00-24:00).
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you submitted your registration.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you submitted your registration in 24-hour format (00:00-24:00).
* **Course ID** (`course_id`, number, optional): Enter the course ID if known.
