# Masoretic Studies Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Masoretic Studies Registration form is designed to capture essential information from individuals interested in enrolling in a program at Masoretic Studies. This form ensures that we have accurate and complete contact information and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the required information before starting.
2. Enter your contact details, including your email, phone number, and address.
3. Select your preferred study format (online, on-campus, or part-time).
4. Select your preferred study level (bachelor, master, or PhD).
5. For the second point of contact, select your preferred contact method (email or phone).
6. Enter your preferred language (English, French, Spanish, or other).
7. Confirm your consent for both the primary and secondary points of contact.
8. Enter any additional contact information for the secondary contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter Program Details**: This field allows you to enter any additional program details not captured by the other fields.
* **Student Details**: This field is not currently required and is intended for additional student information.
* **Study Format**: Select one of the three options: 
    + Online: This format allows you to complete your studies online.
    + On-campus: This format requires you to physically attend classes on campus.
    + Part-time: This format allows you to complete your studies part-time.
* **Select Study Level**: Choose one or more of the three options: 
    + Bachelor: This is an undergraduate degree.
    + Master: This is a postgraduate degree.
    + PhD: This is a doctoral degree.
* **How long do you plan to study?**: Enter the number of years you plan to study. 
* **Other**: This field is for when the number of years is other than what is listed in the options.
* **Program length other details**: Enter any additional information about your program length.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Consent**: Confirm your consent for contact by checking this box.
* **Consent other**: Enter any additional details about your consent.
* **Contact Method**: Select one of the two options: 
    + Email: This is the preferred contact method for email.
    + Phone: This is the preferred contact method for phone.
* **Contact Method 2**: Select one of the two options: 
    + Email: This is the preferred contact method for email.
    + Phone: This is the preferred contact method for phone.
* **Contact Details**: Enter any additional contact details for your contact method.
* **Address**: Enter your physical address.
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **State**: Enter your state or province.
* **Zip**: Enter your postal code.
* **Country**: Enter your country.
* **Preferred Language**: Select one of the four options: 
    + English: This is the primary language of instruction.
    + French: This is a secondary language of instruction.
    + Spanish: This is a tertiary language of instruction.
    + Other: This is any other language you prefer.
* **Language Other**: Enter any additional information about your preferred language.
* **Language Other Details**: Enter any additional information about your language preference.
* **Contact Method 2**: Select one of the two options: 
    + Email: This is the preferred contact method for email.
    + Phone: This is the preferred contact method for phone.
* **Contact Details 2**: Enter any additional contact details for your contact method.
* **Consent 2**: Confirm your consent for the secondary point of contact.
* **Address 2**: Enter any additional address details for the secondary contact method.
* **City 2**: Enter any additional city details for the secondary contact method.
* **State 2**: Enter any additional state details for the secondary contact method.
* **Zip 2**: Enter any additional postal code details for the secondary contact method.
* **Country 2**: Enter any additional country details for the secondary contact method.
* **Preferred Language 2**: Select one of the four options: 
    + English: This is the primary language of instruction.
    + French: This is a secondary language of instruction.
    + Spanish: This is a tertiary language of instruction.
    + Other: This is any other language you prefer.
* **Language Other 2**: Enter any additional information about your preferred language.
* **Language Other Details 2**: Enter any additional information about your language preference.

## Tips
1. Be accurate and clear when entering your contact information.
2. Ensure you select the correct study format and level.
3. Confirm your consent for both the primary and secondary points of contact.
4. Enter any additional information required for the secondary contact method.
5. Review the form carefully before submitting it.
