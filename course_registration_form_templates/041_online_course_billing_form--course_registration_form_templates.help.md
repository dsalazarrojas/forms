# Online Course Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to provide billing information and complete the online course registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with the requested information.
2. Select the payment method for your transaction.
3. Confirm your submission by choosing the "submit" option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer (customer)** (`Text`): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Billing info (billing_info)** (`Text`): Enter your billing address and any other relevant details.
* **Submit (submit)** (`Select One`): Choose "submit" to confirm your registration.
* **Other fields (other_fields)** (`Text`): This is an optional field for any additional information you'd like to provide.
* **Course info (course_info)** (`Text`): Enter any relevant course details or notes.
* **Submit 2 (submit_2)** (`Select One`): This field is likely intended for further confirmation or additional submission status.
* **Payment method (payment_method)** (`Select Multiple`): Choose how you'd like to pay for the course (e.g., credit card, PayPal, or bank transfer).
* **Payment details (payment_details)** (`Text`): Enter any additional payment details or instructions.
* **Submit 3 (submit_3)** (`Select One`): This is another "submit" field, likely for final confirmation or completion of the process.

## Tips
* Double-check all required fields before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you've selected a valid payment method and entered accurate payment details.
* If you encounter any issues during the submission process, contact the course administrators for assistance.
