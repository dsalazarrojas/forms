# Geology Course Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect participant information for a geology course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your participant information, including your details, academic status, and workshop preferences.
2. Ensure you select the correct geology class you are interested in.
3. Enter your registration date.
4. Provide your email and phone number.
5. Write any additional notes or comments in the text area.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Geology Course Registration** (`user_input`): This is the title of the form. Simply enter your name as it appears on your participant record.
* **Participant Details** (`user_input_2`): Enter any relevant details about your participant status.
* **Academic Status** (`user_input_3`): Select your current academic status from the dropdown list.
	+ **Active**: You are currently enrolled in a geology class.
	+ **Inactive**: You are not currently enrolled in a geology class.
* **Geology Classes** (`user_input_4`): Select the geology class you are interested in. 
	+ **geology class 1**
	+ **geology class 2**
	+ **geology class 3**
* **Session Preferences** (`user_input_5`): Enter any specific session preferences you have.
* **Registration Date** (`user_input_6`): Enter the date you registered for the course.
* **Geology Workshop Preferences** (`user_input_7`): Enter any specific geology workshop preferences you have.
* **Participant Email** (`user_input_8`): Enter your participant email address.
* **Geology Workshop Preferences 2** (`user_input_9`): Enter any additional geology workshop preferences you have.
* **Participant Phone Number** (`user_input_10`): Enter your participant phone number.
* **Participant Note** (`user_input_11`): Enter any additional notes or comments you have about your participation in the course.
* **Geology Workshop Preferences 3** (`user_input_12`): Enter any final geology workshop preferences you have.

## Tips
* Be sure to enter your correct email address and phone number so we can contact you with course updates and information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to enter them in the participant note field.
* If you have any additional workshop preferences, be sure to enter them in the geology workshop preferences fields.
