# Admission Form Healthcare - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Admission Form Healthcare is designed to collect personal and educational background information from applicants to assess their eligibility and suitability for healthcare-related programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your personal details such as name, email, phone number, and address.
2.  Select the program you are applying for and provide your degree level and preferred start term.
3.  Provide your educational background, including your date of birth and any relevant education history.
4.  Share your healthcare experience, work experience, volunteer experience, and standardized test scores.
5.  Consent to a background check and drug screening as part of the admission process.
6.  Provide contact information for your emergency contact person.
7.  Sign and date the application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`applicant_full_name`, text, required): Enter your legal name.
* **Email Address** (`applicant_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address** (`applicant_address`, text, required): Enter your current residential address.
* **City** (`applicant_city`, text, required): Enter the city of your residence.
* **State/Province** (`applicant_state`, text, required): Enter the state or province of your residence.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (`applicant_zip`, text, required): Enter the postal code of your residence.
* **Country** (`applicant_country`, text, required): Enter the country of your residence.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your birth date.
* **Program Applying For** (`program_applying_for`, text, required): Select the healthcare program you are applying for.
* **Degree Level** (`degree_level`, select_one, required): Select the level of degree you are pursuing (e.g., Certificate, Diploma, etc.).
* **Start Term** (`start_term`, select_one, required): Select the term you wish to start.
* **Start Year** (`start_year`, number, required): Enter the year you wish to start.
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, text, required): Describe your educational background.
* **Previous Institution** (`previous_institution`, text, optional): Enter the name of your previous educational institution.
* **GPA** (`gpa`, number, optional): Enter your cumulative GPA.
* **Standardized Test Scores** (`standardized_test_scores`, text, optional): Enter your standardized test scores.
* **Healthcare Experience** (`healthcare_experience`, text, optional): Describe any relevant healthcare experience.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): Describe any other relevant work experience.
* **Volunteer Experience** (`volunteer_experience`, text, optional): Describe any volunteer experience.
* **Personal Statement** (`personal_statement`, text, optional): Write a personal statement about your goals in healthcare.
* **References** (`references`, text, optional): Provide contact information for references.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Enter the relationship to your emergency contact person.
* **Medical Clearances** (`medical_clearances`, select_one, required): Select whether you have required medical clearances.
* **Background Check Consent** (`background_check_consent`, select_one, required): Select whether you consent to a background check.
* **Drug Screen Consent** (`drug_screen_consent`, select_one, required): Select whether you consent to a drug screening.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, required): Enter the date of submitting this application.
* **Applicant Signature** (`applicant_signature`, text, required): Sign and date the application to confirm your submission.
