# Course Curriculum Overview - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Curriculum Overview form is used to create a detailed summary of a course, including its title, description, outcomes, and instructor information. This form helps ensure that all relevant details are captured, making it easier to review and compare courses.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each section of the form, working from top to bottom.
2. Enter the course title in the "Course Details" section.
3. Provide a brief description of the course in the "Course Description" section.
4. Select the relevant course outcomes in the "Course Outcomes" section.
5. List any specific requirements for the course in the "Course Requirements" section.
6. Enter your name, email, and phone number in the "Instructor Details" section.
7. Upload a photo of the instructor in the "Instructor Photo" section (if required).
8. Upload an image representing the course in the "Course Image" section (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Details (Required)**: Enter the title of the course.
* **Course Description (Required)**: Provide a brief description of the course.
* **Course Outcomes (Required)**: Select all relevant outcomes for the course.
* **Course Requirements (Required)**: List any specific requirements for the course.
* **Instructor Details (Required)**: Enter your name and email. You can also add your phone number if applicable.
* **Instructor Photo (Optional)**: Upload a photo of yourself as the instructor.
* **Course Image (Optional)**: Upload an image representing the course. This can be a photo, logo, or other visual representation of the course.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out each section accurately and completely.
* You can save your progress and come back to the form later if needed.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please consult with the relevant department or support team.
