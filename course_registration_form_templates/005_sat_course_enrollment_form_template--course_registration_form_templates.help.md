# SAT Course Enrollment Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SAT Course Enrollment Form Template is designed to collect student registration details for SAT courses. This form helps administrators track and manage course enrollments efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "student_name" field.
2. Provide your valid email address in the "student_email" field.
3. For the "select_course" field, choose "Yes" if you're interested in enrolling in a course or "No" if not.
4. If you're enrolling in a course, enter the course start date in the "course_start_date" field.
5. If applicable, enter the course start time in the "course_start_time" field.
6. Enter the course price in the "course_price" field.
7. For "payment_method", select your preferred payment method (if you're enrolling).
8. In the "submit" field, confirm whether you want to submit your course enrollment application.
9. Finally, confirm your payment status as "Active" or "Inactive" in the "payment_status" field.
10. If you have any other payment details, provide the "order_id" number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **student_name (Text)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **student_email (Email)**: Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **select_course (Select One)**: Choose "Yes" if you're interested in enrolling in a course or "No" if not. This helps us track your interest in the course.
* **course_start_date (Date)**: If you're enrolling in a course, enter the start date of the course.
* **course_start_time (Time)**: Enter the start time of the course if it's applicable.
* **course_price (Number)**: Enter the price of the course you're interested in.
* **payment_method (Select One)**: Choose your preferred payment method.
* **submit (Select Multiple)**: Confirm whether you want to submit your course enrollment application.
* **payment_status (Select Multiple)**: Confirm your payment status as "Active" or "Inactive" if applicable.
* **order_id (Number)**: If you have any other payment details, provide the "order_id" number.

**Tips**

* Make sure your email address is valid and easily accessible to ensure you receive important updates and notifications from our institution.
* Ensure you select the correct payment method to avoid any delays in course processing.
* If you have any issues or concerns during the course registration process, contact us for assistance.
