# Mentorship And Investment Course Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants interested in signing up for a mentorship and investment course. Its purpose is to collect essential information about the participant's details, course preferences, and consent.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start on the Main Page**: Begin by filling out the first field, which is the Main Page. This section is not optional and is a required field.
2. **Participant Details**: On the second page, fill out the Participant Details field, which is optional but recommended for providing more specific information about yourself.
3. **Course Choice**: On the third page, select your preferred course from the options provided. This field is also optional.
4. **Mentorship and Investment Choice**: On the fourth page, choose your preferred mentorship and investment option from the options provided. This field is optional.
5. **Consent**: On the last page, fill out the Consent field, which is optional but necessary for processing your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Page (page 1, required)**: This is the initial page where you'll begin filling out the form. Please ensure you're in the right place before proceeding.
* **Participant Details (page 2, optional)**: Provide more specific information about yourself to help us better understand your needs and preferences.
* **Course Choice (page 3, optional)**: Select your preferred course from the options provided. This helps us tailor the course content to your interests.
* **Mentorship and Investment Choice (page 4, optional)**: Choose your preferred mentorship and investment option from the options provided. This helps us match you with the right mentor.
* **Consent (page 5, optional)**: This is the last field, and it's necessary for us to proceed with your application. Please take a moment to review and understand what you're consenting to.
 
## Tips
* Please ensure each field is filled out accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this help guide.
* Take your time to fill out the form, and don't hesitate to contact us if you have any questions or concerns.
