# International Student Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The International Student Application Form is designed to collect essential information from international students applying to our institution. The form helps us understand your background, interests, and qualifications to assess your eligibility for our programs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Complete all required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, refer to the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your passport or ID.
* **Date Of Birth** (`Date Of Birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your official email address.
* **Nationality** (`Nationality`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if you are a citizen of the country where you are applying from, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Passport Details** (`Passport Details`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information about your passport (e.g., passport number, expiration date).
* **Contact Data** (`Contact Data`, `text`, optional): Include any relevant contact information (e.g., address, phone number).
* **Degree** (`Degree`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the degrees you have completed or are pursuing.
* **Institution Name** (`Institution Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the institution where you studied or are currently studying.
* **Field of Study** (`Field of Study`, `text`, required): Enter your field of study.
* **Motivation** (`Motivation`, `text`, required): Explain why you are interested in our program or institution.
* **Supporting Documents** (`Supporting Documents`, `text`, required): Upload any relevant supporting documents (e.g., transcript, letter of recommendation).
* **Select Course** (`Select Course`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the courses you are interested in pursuing.
* **Further Comments** (`Further Comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or information not included in the other fields.
* **Height** (`Height`, `number`, required): Enter your height in centimeters.
* **Weight** (`Weight`, `number`, required): Enter your weight in kilograms.

Note: All fields marked as required must be completed for a successful submission.
