# Online Class Attendance - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to record and keep track of online class attendance. Students are expected to fill out their own form to report their presence and provide any comments or feedback if needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name as the student.
2. Enter your student ID number (if applicable).
3. Select the date you attended the class.
4. Choose the time you attended the class (24-hour format).
5. Indicate whether you attended the class by selecting "Yes" or "No".
6. If you have any comments or feedback, type them in the "Comments" field.
7. Finally, sign your name as the instructor to confirm.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the student.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id_number`, number, optional): Enter your student ID number if you have one.
* **Attendance Date** (`attendance_date`, date, optional): Select the date you attended the class.
* **Attendance Time** (`attendance_time`, time, optional): Choose the time you attended the class using the 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 for 2:00 PM).
* **Attended** (`attended`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you attended the class by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): If you have any comments or feedback, type them in this field.
* **Instructor Signature** (`instructor_signature`, text, optional): Sign your name as the instructor to confirm.
