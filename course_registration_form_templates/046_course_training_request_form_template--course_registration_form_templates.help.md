# Course Training Request Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect information from employees about their training requests. It's essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that the training provided meets your needs and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Employee Details page.
2. Provide your name and any other relevant employee information.
3. Move to the Training Details page.
4. Describe the training you're interested in, including the type of training, duration, and any specific goals or objectives you have.
5. Proceed to the Submission page.
6. Summarize your training request, including any additional comments or questions you have.
7. Review your form to ensure you've answered all the required questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Details**:
    • **Employee Name** (`<name>`, `text`, required): Please enter your name.
    • **Employee ID** (`<id>`, `text`, optional): If you have an employee ID, please enter it.
    • **Job Title** (`<title>`, `text`, optional): If you have a job title, please enter it.
* **Training Details**:
    • **Training Type** (`<type>`, `text`, required): Describe the type of training you're interested in (e.g., classroom, online, on-the-job, etc.).
    • **Training Duration** (`<duration>`, `text`, required): Specify the length of time you're interested in dedicating to the training (e.g., one day, one week, etc.).
    • **Training Goals** (`<goals>`, `text`, required): Define your specific objectives for the training (e.g., skill development, knowledge enhancement, etc.).
* **Submission**:
    • **Training Request** (`<request>`, `text`, required): Provide a brief summary of your training request, including any additional comments or questions you have.

## Tips

- Be as detailed as possible when filling out this form to ensure your training needs are met.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask your supervisor or training coordinator.
- Please review your form before submission to ensure all required fields are completed.
