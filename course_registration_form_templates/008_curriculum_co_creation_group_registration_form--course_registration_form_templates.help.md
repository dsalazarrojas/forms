# Curriculum Co Creation Group Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Curriculum Co-Creation Group Registration Form is a crucial tool for registering and managing teams that work on creating new curricula or courses. By filling out this form, you'll be able to:

* Provide details about your group's name, contact information, and meeting schedules.
* Ensure that your group's information is accurately recorded in our system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Group Name** in the first field. This is the name of your team.
2. Enter the **Group Email** in the second field. This is the email address of your team.
3. If applicable, enter the **Group Leader's Name** in the third field. This is the name of the person leading your team.
4. Choose the **Group Role** from the dropdown menu. This indicates the purpose of your team.
5. Enter the **Department** where your group is working from the fourth field. If not applicable, leave this field blank.
6. Enter the **Course Title** in the fifth field. If not applicable, leave this field blank.
7. Add any additional comments about your group in the **Comment** field. This is optional.
8. Describe the **Group Purpose** in the seventh field. This helps us understand the objectives of your team.
9. Enter the **Group Size** in the eighth field. This indicates the number of people in your team.
10. Choose the **Group Meeting Time** if applicable. This is the time of your team's meetings.
11. Choose the **Group Meeting Date** if applicable. This is the date of your team's meetings.
12. Finally, enter the **Group Location** where your team meets. If not applicable, leave this field blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Group Name**: Enter the name of your group.
* **Group Email**: Enter the email address of your group.
* **Group Leader's Name**: Enter the name of the person leading your group (if applicable).
* **Group Role**: Choose the purpose of your group from the dropdown menu:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Department**: Enter the department where your group is working (if applicable).
* **Course Title**: Enter the title of the course your group is working on (if applicable).
* **Comment**: Add any additional comments about your group.
* **Group Purpose**: Describe the objectives of your group.
* **Group Size**: Enter the number of people in your group.
* **Group Meeting Time**: Choose the time of your group's meetings (if applicable).
* **Group Meeting Date**: Choose the date of your group's meetings (if applicable).
* **Group Location**: Enter the location of your group's meetings (if applicable).
