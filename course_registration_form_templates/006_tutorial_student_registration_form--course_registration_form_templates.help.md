# Tutorial Student Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tutorial Student Registration Form is designed to collect information from students registering for a tutorial.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, follow the steps below.
2. Read each field carefully to ensure you provide the required and optional information accurately.
3. Fill out the form according to the guidelines below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Course Name** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the course you're registering for.
* **Student ID** (`5`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your student ID number.
* **Course ID** (`6`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the course ID number.
* **Start Date** (`7`, `date`, required/optional): Select the start date of the tutorial.
* **End Date** (`8`, `date`, required/optional): Select the end date of the tutorial.
* **Start Time** (`9`, `time`, required/optional): Select the start time of the tutorial.
* **End Time** (`10`, `time`, required/optional): Select the end time of the tutorial.
* **Registered By** (`11`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the person or department registering you.
* **Registered On** (`12`, `date`, required/optional): The date you will be registered on will be filled in automatically.

Note: The fields marked 'required/optional' may or may not be required based on specific circumstances. Please consult your course instructor or relevant guidelines for confirmation.
