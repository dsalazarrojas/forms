# university_student_course_load_check_in - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to check a university student's course load for a specific semester. This includes tracking the student's status in a particular semester and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the semester for which you want to check the student's course load.
2. Enter the student ID number to identify the student.
3. Enter the course ID number to specify the course being checked.
4. Enter the credit earned by the student in that course.
5. Enter the checked credit to track the student's progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Semester**: Select the semester for which you want to check the student's course load. This will help you track the student's progress over different semesters.
* **Student ID**: Enter the student's unique identifier number to identify them accurately.
* **Course ID**: Enter the course identifier number to specify which course is being checked.
* **Credit**: Enter the total credit earned by the student in that course.
* **Checked Credit**: Enter the credit checked or verified by the student in that course.
