# Mandarin Speech And Drama Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register students for the Mandarin Speech and Drama course at a school or institution. Please fill in the required fields to provide accurate information and complete the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary information ready before filling out the form.
2. Fill in the student's personal details: Student Name, Parent Name, Contact Email, Contact Phone, and Contact Address.
3. Provide the course schedule details: Class Schedule, Start Date, End Date, and Signup Date.
4. Confirm the student's registration details: Student Name, Student Age, Student Level (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced), and Student Interest (Dance, Music, or Theater).
5. Choose the preferred payment method (optional) and agree to the terms of payment.
6. Review all filled-in information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, required): Enter the student's full name as it appears on their identification document.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, required): Enter the parent/guardian's full name as it appears on their identification document.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, required): Enter a valid email address where the student can be contacted.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, required): Enter the student's or parent's phone number.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, required): Enter the student's or parent's address.
* **Class Schedule** (`schedule_class`, required): Select the date and time of the course schedule you prefer.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, required): Enter the date the course will start.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, required): Enter the date the course will end.
* **Signup Date** (`signup_date`, required): Enter the date you want to start attending the course.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, optional): Choose how you want to pay for the course (Bank Transfer, Credit Card, or Cash).
* **Terms of Payment** (`payment_terms`, optional): Read and agree to the payment terms and conditions.
* **Student Age** (`student_age`, required): Enter the student's age.
* **Student Level** (`student_level`, optional): Choose the student's level of experience (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
* **Student Experience** (`student_experience`, optional): Select the student's prior experience (Dance, Music, or Speech).
* **Student Interest** (`student_interest`, optional): Choose the student's interest (Dance, Music, or Theater).

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any information, contact the school or institution for assistance.
* Review the payment terms and conditions carefully to understand the payment process.
