# EKG Online Training Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The EKG Online Training Registration Form is designed to collect essential information about courses, participants, and training sessions. This form is used for registration and management purposes, ensuring that all required information is collected accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the course name in the "Course Name" field.
2. Fill in the participant's name in the "Participant Name" field.
3. Provide the participant's email address in the "E-mail" field, following the correct email format (YYYY-MM-DD).
4. Enter the participant's phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select the session start and end dates in the "Session Start Date" and "Session End Date" fields, respectively.
6. Enter the session time in the "Session Time" field.
7. Choose the training provider from the "Training Provider" field.
8. Select the training location from the "Training Location" field.
9. Choose the training date in the "Training Date" field.
10. Enter the participant count in the "Participant Count" field.
11. Enter the training duration in the "Training Duration" fields.
12. Choose the course category from the "Course Category" field.
13. Select multiple course categories from the "Course Category List" field.
14. Choose the participant category from the "Participant Category" field.
15. Select multiple participant categories from the "Participant Category List" field.
16. Choose the course status from the "Course Status" field.
17. Select multiple course statuses from the "Course Status List" field.
18. Choose the training provider status from the "Training Provider Status" field.
19. Select multiple training provider statuses from the "Training Provider List" field.
20. Choose the participant status from the "Participant Status" field.
21. Select multiple participant statuses from the "Participant Status List" field.
22. Enter participant notes in the "Participant Notes" field.
23. Select multiple participant notes from the "Participant Notes List" field.
24. Enter training provider notes in the "Training Provider Notes" field.
25. Select multiple training provider notes from the "Training Provider Notes List" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the course.
* **Participant Name** (2, text, required): Enter the name of the participant.
* **E-mail** (3, email, required): Enter the participant's email address in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-01-01).
* **Phone** (4, text, required): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Session Start Date** (5, date, required): Select the date when the session starts.
* **Session End Date** (6, date, required): Select the date when the session ends.
* **Session Time** (7, time, required): Enter the session time.
* **Training Provider** (8, text, required): Choose the training provider.
* **Training Location** (9, text, required): Select the training location.
* **Training Date** (10, date, required): Choose the training date.
* **Participant Count** (11, number, required): Enter the number of participants.
* **Training Duration** (12, number, required): Enter the duration of the training.
* **Course Category** (13, select_one, required): Choose the course category.
* **Course Category List** (14, select_multiple, required): Select multiple course categories.
* **Participant Category** (15, select_one, required): Choose the participant category.
* **Participant Category List** (16, select_multiple, required): Select multiple participant categories.
* **Course Status** (17, select_one, required): Choose the course status.
* **Course Status List** (18, select_multiple, required): Select multiple course statuses.
* **Training Provider Status** (19, select_one, required): Choose the training provider status.
* **Training Provider List** (20, select_multiple, required): Select multiple training provider statuses.
* **Participant Status** (21, select_one, required): Choose the participant status.
* **Participant Status List** (22, select_multiple, required): Select multiple participant statuses.
* **Participant Notes** (23, note, required): Enter notes about the participant.
* **Participant Notes List** (24, select_multiple, required): Select multiple participant notes.
* **Training Provider Notes** (25, note, required): Enter notes about the training provider.
* **Training Provider Notes List** (26, select_multiple, required): Select multiple training provider notes.

## Tips
* Double-check the required fields before submitting the form.
* Ensure that all selected options are accurate and relevant to the specific field.
* If you are unsure about a field, contact the form administrator for assistance.
