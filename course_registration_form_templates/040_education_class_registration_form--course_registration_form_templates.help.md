# Education Class Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for students to provide their details and register for an education class.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the course details in the first field.
2. Provide your student information in the second field.
3. Select the courses you want to enroll in from the third field.
4. Enter your contact information in the fourth field.
5. Add any additional notes in the fifth field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Details (text)**:
	+ Enter the name of the course you wish to register for.
* **Student Information (text)**:
	+ Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant student information.
* **Enrollment Information (select multiple)**:
	+ Choose one or more courses from the list to indicate your enrollment status.
* **Contact Information (text)**:
	+ Enter your contact details, such as your address and phone number.
* **Additional Notes (text)**:
	+ Add any additional comments or information about your registration.
