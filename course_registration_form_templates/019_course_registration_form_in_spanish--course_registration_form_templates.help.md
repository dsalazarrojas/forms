# Course Registration Form In Spanish - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a course registration form designed to collect information from students, including course information, personal details, and relevant contact data. It is intended for students who want to register for a course in Spanish.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the course information section.
2. Enter your student name in the "Student Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Fill out the "Address" field with your physical address.
6. Select the desired course code from the "Course Code" dropdown menu.
7. Choose the relevant course level from the "Course Level" select multiple menu.
8. Optionally, enter the date of registration in the "Date of Registration" field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Information (course_info)**: Enter the title of the course you want to register for.
* **Student Name (student_name)**: Enter your full name as a student.
* **Date of Registration (date_registration)**: If you have already registered for the course, enter the date you registered.
* **Email (email)**: Enter a valid email address for correspondence.
* **Phone Number (phone_number)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address (address)**: Enter your physical address for delivery or other purposes.
* **Course Code (course_code)**: Choose the course you want to register for by selecting one of the options.
* **Course Level (course_level)**: Choose the relevant course level that matches your requirements.
  Note: You can select multiple levels if applicable.
