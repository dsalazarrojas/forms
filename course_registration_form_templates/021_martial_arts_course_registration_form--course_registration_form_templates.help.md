# Martial Arts Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for students to register for martial arts courses. It requires accurate and complete information to process successful registrations.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the Personal Info section with your name, contact information, and belt ranking (if applicable).
- In the Contact Info section, enter your email address and phone number.
- Select one of the available course packages in the Course Packages section.
- In the Payment section, choose your preferred payment method (credit card or bank transfer).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Info (Name)**: Enter your full name, as it appears on your identification.
* **Personal Info (Phone)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Personal Info (Belt Ranking)**: If applicable, enter your current belt ranking.
* **Contact Info (Email)**: Enter your valid email address for communication and updates.
* **Contact Info (Phone)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Belt Ranking**: Enter your current belt ranking (e.g., white, black, etc.).
* **Course Packages**: Select one of the two course options:
	+ **Martial Arts Course**: General martial arts course.
	+ **Special Course**: Advanced or specialized martial arts course.
* **Payment**: Select your preferred payment method:
	+ **Credit Card**: Pay using your credit card.
	+ **Bank Transfer**: Pay using a bank transfer (e.g., online or bank draft).
* **Name**: Enter your full name, as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Emergency Info**: Enter any relevant emergency contact information.
* **Health Medical**: Enter any health or medical information that is relevant to your martial arts training.
* **Health Medical**: Enter any medical information that is relevant to your martial arts training.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- Double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you have any issues with the form, contact the course administrators for assistance.
