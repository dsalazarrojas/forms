# Computer Short Course Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Computer Short Course Admission Form is a simple and organized form designed to collect information from applicants interested in taking a computer short course. This form is not affiliated with any form platforms and was generated by AI using public metadata.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Select your preferred payment method from the available options.
3. Provide a brief description of your prior experience in coding.
4. Choose your preferred start date for the course.
5. Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant name** (`applicant_name`, Text, **Required**): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email address** (`email`, Email, **Required**): Enter your email address for communication with the course organizers.
* **Prior experience** (`prior_experience`, Text, **Optional**): Briefly describe your prior experience in coding (if any).
* **Preferred start date** (`preferred_start_date`, Date, **Optional**): Choose your preferred start date for the course.
* **Payment method** (`payment_method`, Select One, **Required**): Select how you will pay for the course: Credit card, Bank transfer, or Invoice.
* **Emergency contact** (`emergency_contact`, Text, **Optional**): Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact person.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately.
- If you have prior experience, provide a brief description.
- If you need to change your start date, contact the course organizers.
