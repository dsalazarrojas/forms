# Health And Movement Science Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect participant information for the "Health And Movement Science" program. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure the participant's health and contact information is up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Participant Information" section with your name and any relevant identifying information.
2. Provide your "Health Details" in the specified section.
3. Enter your "Contact Information" in the specified fields.
4. Confirm your consent to participate in the program by marking "Yes" in the "Consent" section.
5. Enter your emergency contact's information and select your relationship with them in the "Emergency Contact" and "Emergency Contact Relationship" sections.
6. If you have any health information relevant to your participation in the program, enter it in the "Participant Health Info" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information (Page 1)**: This section is where you'll enter your personal details, such as your name and any other relevant information. Please ensure that this information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Health Details (Page 1)**: In this section, please provide any health details that are relevant to your participation in the program. This will help the program staff understand your health conditions better.
* **Contact Information (Page 1)**: Enter your contact information, such as your address and phone number, so that the program staff can reach out to you.
* **Consent (Page 1)**: Please confirm your consent to participate in the program by marking "Yes" in this section. This is essential for your participation in the program.
* **Emergency Contact (Page 1)**: Enter the information of the emergency contact person who will be reached in case of any issues during your participation in the program.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship (Page 1)**: Select "Yes" if the emergency contact person is a family member, friend, or other established relationship. Otherwise, select "No".
* **Participant Health Info (Page 2)**: This section is optional but still valuable for the program's understanding of your health conditions. If you have any health information relevant to your participation, please enter it in this section.
* **Participant Notes (Page 2)**: This is an optional section where you can provide any additional comments or information about yourself that is not covered in the other fields.
