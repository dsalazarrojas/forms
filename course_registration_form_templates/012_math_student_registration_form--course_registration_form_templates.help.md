# Math Student Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from math students. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure that you receive the correct math course materials and information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select a date for your registration in the "Date" field.
5. Select a time for your registration in the "Time" field.
6. If you have any notes or comments, enter them in the "Note" field.
7. Select one or more math courses you wish to register for from the "Math Courses" field.
8. Select your math tutor from the "Math Tutor" field.
9. Confirm your phone number in the "Confirm Phone" field.
10. Confirm your email address in the "Confirm Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your record.
* **Email**: Enter your email address associated with your school account.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in the format "XXX-XXX-XXXX".
* **Date**: Select a date for your registration. This will help us schedule your math course.
* **Time**: Select a time for your registration. Please choose a time that works best for you.
* **Note**: If you have any notes or comments, enter them here.
* **Math Courses**: Select one or more math courses you wish to register for.
* **Math Tutor**: Select your math tutor from the list. This will be assigned to you based on your preferences.
* **Confirm Phone**: Re-enter your phone number to confirm its accuracy.
* **Confirm Email**: Re-enter your email address to confirm its accuracy.
* **Math Courses**:
	+ Select one or more math courses you wish to register for.
	+ Please select at least one course to register for.
* **Math Tutor**:
	+ Select your math tutor from the list.
	+ Please choose a tutor that you feel comfortable with.
* **Confirm Phone**:
	+ Re-enter your phone number to confirm its accuracy.
	+ Please ensure it matches the original phone number you entered.
* **Confirm Email**:
	+ Re-enter your email address to confirm its accuracy.
	+ Please ensure it matches the original email address you entered.
