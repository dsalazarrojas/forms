# Kayaking Safety Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather important information about each participant and instructor for a kayaking safety course. It helps us ensure everyone's safety and well-being during the activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the `participant_details` section with your personal information, if required.
2. Provide details about the course in the `course_details` section.
3. If you are a participant, provide emergency contact information in the `emergency_contacts` section.
4. Read and sign the `participant_signature` field to acknowledge that you have read and understood the course's terms and conditions.
5. If you are an instructor, sign the `instructor_signature` field.
6. If you are the course leader, sign the `course_leader_signature` field.
7. Enter your name in the `course_leader_name` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details**: Enter your name, date of birth, and emergency contact information (if applicable).
* **Course Details**: Provide the course's name, description, and any other relevant information about the course.
* **Emergency Contacts**: Enter emergency contact names and their relationship to you, as well as their phone number and email.
* **Waivers**: This field is currently not applicable.
* **Emergency Contact Details**: Provide emergency contact information for the person listed in the `Emergency Contacts` field, including their name, phone number, and email.
* **Participant Signature**: Sign your name below to acknowledge that you have read and understood the course's terms and conditions.
* **Instructor Signature**: Sign your name below if you are an instructor, indicating that you have read and will adhere to the course's guidelines.
* **Course Leader Signature**: Sign your name below if you are the course leader, indicating that you have read and will adhere to the course's guidelines.
* **Course Leader Name**: Enter your name as the course leader.

## Tips
Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure that we have up-to-date information about each participant and instructor. If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
