# Student Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Course Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from students about their course registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Scroll through the form pages and fill in the required details for each page.
2. Provide accurate and clear responses to each question or field.
3. Review and submit the form when you have completed all necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Information (Student Information)** (`student_info`, text, optional):
  This field is used to capture the student's personal details. Please enter your name, email, and any other relevant information as required.

- **Course Information (Course Information)** (`course_info`, text, optional):
  This field is used to gather information about the course the student is registering for. Please provide the course title, description, and any other relevant details.

- **Payment Information (Payment Information)** (`payment_info`, text, optional):
  This field is used to collect payment-related information from students. Please enter your payment method and any other necessary details.

- **Student Enrollment (Student Enrollment)** (`student_enrollment`, text, optional):
  This field is used to capture the student's enrollment status and any other relevant information. Please provide your enrollment date and any other necessary details.

## Tips
- Ensure you fill in all necessary fields before submitting the form.
- If you encounter any issues while completing the form, feel free to contact form@gic.mx for assistance.
