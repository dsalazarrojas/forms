# Course Change Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Change Request Form is used to submit changes to a course in the system. This form is designed to gather relevant information about the change, including the reason behind it and the person requesting the change.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: **Course ID** and **Reason for change**.
2. If necessary, select the **Submitted to** field with the person or department the change was submitted to.
3. Enter the **Submitted on** date of when you made the request.
4. Select the person who **Approved by** from the list.
5. If applicable, add any **Comments** about the change.
6. Fill in the **Old Course ID** if the change is regarding a course that was previously offered.
7. Finally, fill in your **Submitted by** and select the **Submitted on** date to record the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course ID**: This field is where you enter the ID of the course you are requesting to change.
* **Old Course ID**: If the change is related to a previously offered course, enter the ID of that course.
* **Reason for change**: Please provide a brief explanation of why you are requesting the course change.
* **Submitted by**: Enter your name or username, so we know who submitted the request.
* **Submitted on**: Select the date you submitted the request.
* **Approved by**: Select the person who approved the change from the list.
* **Approved on**: Select the date the change was approved.
* **Comments**: If there's anything important we need to know about the change, enter it here.
* **Submitted to**: Select the person or department the change was submitted to.
