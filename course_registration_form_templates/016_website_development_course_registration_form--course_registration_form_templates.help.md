# Website Development Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from participants for a website development course. The collected data will be used to keep track of course attendees, their contact details, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your participant name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Participant Email" field.
3. Enter your participant name (again, this is a duplicate field with a different label, please fill it in with the same information as in the "Participant Name" field).
4. Enter your participant email (again, this is a duplicate field with a different label, please fill it in with the same information as in the "Participant Email" field).
5. Enter your participant phone number in the "Participant Phone" field.
6. Specify the name of the course you want to register for in the "Course Name" field.
7. Choose the start date of the course from the calendar in the "Start Date" field.
8. Choose the end date of the course from the calendar in the "End Date" field.
9. Provide your participant address in the "Participant Address" field.
10. If you have any special notes or comments, you can include them in the "Participant Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (1, text, required/optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on course materials and communication.
* **Participant Email** (2, email, required/optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached for course updates and information.
* **Participant Name** (3, text, required/optional): This is a duplicate field for your name, enter the same information as in the "Participant Name" field above.
* **Participant Email** (4, email, required/optional): This is a duplicate field for your email, enter the same information as in the "Participant Email" field above.
* **Participant Phone** (5, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Course Name** (6, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the course you want to register for.
* **Start Date** (7, date, required/optional): Choose the start date of the course from the calendar.
* **End Date** (8, date, required/optional): Choose the end date of the course from the calendar.
* **Participant Address** (9, text, required/optional): Enter your address where you can be reached.
* **Participant Note** (10, note, required/optional): Enter any special notes or comments about your registration.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your registration is complete.
* If you have any issues filling out the form, please contact us for assistance.
* Please review your information carefully before submitting the form to ensure it is accurate.
