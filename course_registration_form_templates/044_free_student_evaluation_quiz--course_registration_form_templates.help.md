# free_student_evaluation_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The free_student_evaluation_quiz form is a tool for students to provide their evaluation and feedback on their performance in a quiz.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter your instructor's name in the "Instructor Name" field.
3. Select the course you are evaluating by entering the "Course ID" number.
4. Choose the date and time of the quiz from the "Quiz Date Time" field.
5. Select the score you received in the "Score" field.
6. Optionally, provide any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (if you want to receive feedback).
8. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (if you want to receive feedback).
9. If applicable, select the tools used during the quiz from the "Assigned Tool" field and the "Selected Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the evaluation.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your instructor.
* **Course ID** (`course_id`, number, required): Enter the unique ID of your course.
* **Quiz Date Time** (`quiz_datetime`, date, required): Choose the date and time of the quiz.
* **Quiz Date** (`quiz_date`, date, required): Choose the date of the quiz.
* **Quiz Time** (`quiz_time`, time, required): Choose the time of the quiz.
* **Score** (`score`, number, required): Enter your score for the quiz.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about the quiz.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you want to receive feedback from your instructor.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to receive feedback from your instructor.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of your student (if different from yours).
* **Instructor Email** (`instructor_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of your instructor (if different from yours).
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select the tool that was assigned to you for the quiz.
* **Selected Tool** (`selected_tool`, select_multiple, optional): Select the tools you used during the quiz.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your evaluation is complete and accurate.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you have any feedback or comments, use the "Notes" field to provide them.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your instructor for assistance.
