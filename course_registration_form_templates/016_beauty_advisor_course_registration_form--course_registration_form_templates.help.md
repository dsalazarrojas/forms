# Beauty Advisor Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from students who are interested in taking a beauty-related course. The form is divided into several sections, including "Student Information," "Course Selection," and "Payment Information." 

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the "Student Information" section, which includes your first name, last name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and street address.
2.  In the "Course Selection" section, choose the course you are interested in, select the preferred course format (in-person, online, or hybrid), and choose your preferred start date.
3.  In the "Payment Information" section, select your preferred payment method (credit card, debit card, bank transfer, or installments) and payment plan (full payment, monthly installments, or quarterly installments).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): This is your first name.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): This is your last name.
*   **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): This is your email address where you can be reached.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): This is your phone number where you can be reached.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): This is your date of birth.
*   **Street Address** (`address`, text, required): This is your street address.
*   **City** (`city`, text, required): This is your city of residence.
*   **State/Province** (`state`, text, required): This is your state or province of residence.
*   **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): This is your ZIP code.
*   **Highest Level of Education** (`highest_education`, select_one, required): Choose your highest level of education (high school, associate degree, bachelor degree, master degree, or other).
*   **Previous Beauty Training or Experience** (`previous_beauty_training`, text, optional): If you have previous beauty training or experience, please describe it in this field.
*   **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required): This is the name of the course you are interested in.
*   **Course Type** (`course_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of course you are interested in (beauty advisor certification, makeup artistry, hair styling, esthetics, nail care, or advanced techniques).
*   **Preferred Course Format** (`course_format`, select_one, required): Choose the preferred course format (in-person, online, or hybrid).
*   **Preferred Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Choose your preferred start date for the course.
*   **Schedule Preference** (`schedule_preference`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred schedule preference (weekday mornings, weekday evenings, weekends, or flexible).
*   **Career Goals in Beauty Industry** (`career_goals`, text, required): Describe your career goals in the beauty industry.
*   **Current Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): If you have previous work experience, please describe it in this field.
*   **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method (credit card, debit card, bank transfer, or installments).
*   **Payment Plan** (`payment_plan`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment plan (full payment, monthly installments, or quarterly installments).
*   **I Agree to the Terms and Conditions** (`terms_accepted`, select_one, required): Select "yes" if you agree to the terms and conditions.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out all required fields carefully and accurately.
*   If you have any additional information or questions, please use the "Additional Information or Questions" field.
*   Please note that the preferred payment method and plan are subject to change based on course availability and other factors.
