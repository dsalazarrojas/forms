# Music Conservatory Spring Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students and parents/guardians for music lessons at the conservatory.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in the "Student Name" field with the student's name.
2.  Enter the "Parent Name" field with the parent/guardian's name.
3.  Choose the student's "Grade Level" in the "Student Grade Level" field.
4.  Choose the student's music "Instrument" field.
5.  Select the student's music "Style" (e.g., Jazz, Classical, Folk, Pop, Rock).
6.  Enter the number of hours the student will "Practice Time per Week".
7.  Enter the number of lessons the student will have per week.
8.  Enter the "Tuition" amount for the lessons.
9.  Select the method of payment (e.g., Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer).
10.  Enter the "Emergency Contact Name" field with the emergency contact's name.
11.  Enter the "Emergency Contact Relationship" field with the relationship to the student.
12.  Enter the "Contact Phone" field with the contact's phone number.
13.  Enter the "Contact Email" field with the contact's email address.
14.  Enter the student's "Student Date of Birth" (Date of Birth).
15.  Enter the student's "Student Start Date" for lessons.
16.  Enter the student's "Student End Date" for lessons.
17.  Enter the teacher's "Teacher Name" field.
18.  Enter the teacher's "Teacher Phone" field.
19.  Enter the teacher's "Teacher Email" field.
20.  Enter the teacher's "Teacher Availability" for lessons.
21.  Add any additional "Student Notes" about the student.
22.  Add any additional "Teacher Notes" about the student.
23.  Enter the student's "Student Instrument" field.
24.  Enter any additional "Additional Notes" about the student.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Student Name** (`Student Name`, text, required): Enter the student's name.
*   **Parent Name** (`Parent Name`, text, required): Enter the parent/guardian's name.
*   **Student Grade Level** (`Grade Level`, text, required): Choose the student's grade level.
*   **Instrument** (`Instrument`, text, required): Choose the student's instrument.
*   **Music Style** (`Music Style`, select_one, required): Choose the student's music style (e.g., Jazz, Classical, Folk, Pop, Rock).
*   **Practice Time per Week** (`Practice Time per Week`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours the student will practice per week.
*   **Lessons per Week** (`Lessons per Week`, number, optional): Enter the number of lessons the student will have per week.
*   **Tuition** (`Tuition`, number, optional): Enter the tuition amount for the lessons.
*   **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select_multiple, required): Choose the method of payment (e.g., Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer).
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (`Emergency Contact Name`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's name.
*   **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`Emergency Contact Relationship`, text, optional): Enter the relationship to the student.
*   **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact's phone number.
*   **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, email, optional): Enter the contact's email address.
*   **Student Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, optional): Enter the student's Date of Birth.
*   **Student Start Date** (`Start Date`, date, optional): Enter the student's Start Date for lessons.
*   **Student End Date** (`End Date`, date, optional): Enter the student's End Date for lessons.
*   **Teacher Name** (`Teacher Name`, text, optional): Enter the teacher's name.
*   **Teacher Phone** (`Teacher Phone`, text, optional): Enter the teacher's phone number.
*   **Teacher Email** (`Teacher Email`, email, optional): Enter the teacher's email address.
*   **Teacher Availability** (`Teacher Availability`, time, optional): Enter the teacher's availability.
*   **Student Notes** (`Student Notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the student.
*   **Teacher Notes** (`Teacher Notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the student.
*   **Student Instrument** (`Student Instrument`, text, optional): Enter the student's instrument.
*   **Additional Notes** (`Additional Notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the student.
