# optional_support_class_selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from students about their preferences for support classes, which can help teachers and staff provide better support and accommodations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Interest** (`student_interest`, text, optional): This field allows students to describe their interests and needs.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, number, optional): This field asks students to select their current grade level in school.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, optional): This field asks students to select a time of day or a range of times for the support class.
* **Session Preferences** (`session_preferences`, text, optional): This field allows students to specify any information they want to share about their preferences for the support class.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): This field asks students to enter their school's name.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): This field asks students to enter their full legal name.
