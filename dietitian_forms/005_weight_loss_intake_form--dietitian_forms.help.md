# Weight Loss Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weight Loss Intake Form is a tool for patients to share information about their weight loss goals, medical history, and exercise habits. This form helps healthcare providers understand the patient's current situation and create a personalized treatment plan tailored to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your name in the **patient_name** field.
2. Provide your age in the **patient_age** field.
3. Share your medical history in the **medical_history** field.
4. Select the treatment area you are currently experiencing issues with (Cardiovascular, Diabetes, Obesity, or Other).
5. Specify your weight loss goals and motivations (Goals, Motivation, or Other).
6. If applicable, provide details about your current weight loss plan.
7. Indicate how often you exercise (1-2 times/week, 3-4 times/week, 5-6 times/week, 7 or more times/week, or None).
8. Specify the duration of your exercise (Low Intensity, High Intensity, Long Duration).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_name** (text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **patient_age** (number, required): Enter your age to help us understand your demographic details.
* **medical_history** (text, required): Share any medical conditions or allergies you may have that could impact your weight loss journey.
* **treatment_needs** (select one, required): Select the area of treatment you are currently experiencing issues with (Cardiovascular, Diabetes, Obesity, or Other).
* **weight_loss_goals** (select multiple, optional): Specify your weight loss goals and motivations (Goals, Motivation, or Other).
* **weight_loss_plan** (text, optional): If you have a weight loss plan in place, share details about it.
* **exercise_frequency** (select one, optional): Indicate how often you exercise (1-2 times/week, 3-4 times/week, 5-6 times/week, 7 or more times/week, or None).
* **exercise_duration** (select multiple, optional): Specify the duration of your exercise (Low Intensity, High Intensity, Long Duration).

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough when filling out this form to ensure your healthcare provider can create an effective treatment plan tailored to your needs.
* If you have any concerns or questions, ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
* This form should not be used as a substitute for professional medical advice or treatment.
