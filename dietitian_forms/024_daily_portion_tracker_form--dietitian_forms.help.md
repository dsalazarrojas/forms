# daily_portion_tracker_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The daily portion tracker form is designed to help individuals track their daily food portions, providing a comprehensive overview of their eating habits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the daily portion tracker form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Daily Portion" field with the total number of portions consumed in a day.
2. Select the type of food from the "Food" field, using the options provided.
3. For each food type, enter the corresponding portion size in the relevant unit (Grams, ML, Cups, etc.).
4. Choose the serving size from the "Serving Size" field.
5. Select the "Food Frequency" option that best describes the frequency of food consumption.
6. Select the "Food Group" that the food item belongs to.
7. Enter the tracking date in the "Tracking Date" field.
8. Optionally, enter the tracking time in the "Tracking Time" field.
9. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
10. If applicable, provide your email address and phone number in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Daily Portion** (`Daily Portion`, number, required): Enter the total number of portions consumed in a day.
* **Food** (`Food`, text, required): Select the type of food from the options provided.
* **Serving Size** (`Serving Size`, number, required): Enter the serving size of the food.
* **Grams** (`Grams`, number, required): Enter the portion size in grams.
* **ML** (`ML`, number, required): Enter the portion size in milliliters.
* **Cups** (`Cups`, number, required): Enter the portion size in cups.
* **Tablespoons** (`Tablespoons`, number, required): Enter the portion size in tablespoons.
* **Ounces** (`Ounces`, number, required): Enter the portion size in ounces.
* **Pounds** (`Pounds`, number, required): Enter the portion size in pounds.
* **Food Frequency** (`Food Frequency`, select_multiple, required): Select the frequency of food consumption from the provided options (Water, Beverage, Fruits, Vegetable, Fats, Sweets, Protein, Grains).
* **Food Group** (`Food Group`, select_one, required): Select the group that the food item belongs to (Fruits, Vegetable, Fats, Sweets, Protein, Grains).
* **Tracking Date** (`Tracking Date`, date, required): Enter the date on which the food tracking took place.
* **Tracking Time** (`Tracking Time`, time, optional): Enter the time on which the food tracking took place.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the food item.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
