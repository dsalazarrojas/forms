# eating_disorder_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help professionals in the mental health field understand the severity of a person's eating habits and symptoms, and provide guidance on how to proceed with treatment or further assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question honestly and thoroughly. You may skip questions that are not relevant to your situation. Please note that some questions may have multiple answers (e.g., "Other" in the Symptoms field).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (1)**: This section is an optional introductory note for you to provide any additional context or information about your situation.
* **Eating Habits (2)**: Describe your eating habits, such as frequency and type of food intake.
* **Symptoms (3)**: Select all that apply: 
  + Restrictive Anorexia: Feeling a strong fear of gaining weight, eating very little food.
  + Binge Eating: Consuming large amounts of food in a short period.
  + No Symptoms: You do not experience any of the above symptoms.
  + Other: Describe your symptoms in the "Other" field.
* **Eating Disorder Diagnosis (4)**: Select all that apply: 
  + Anorexia Nervosa: A psychological disorder characterized by a fear of gaining weight and a distorted body image.
  + Binge Eating Disorder: Consuming large amounts of food in a short period, often leading to feelings of guilt and shame.
  + Avoidant/Restrictive Food Intake Disorder: Avoiding food due to a fear of eating or feeling full.
  + Other: Describe your diagnosis in the "Other" field.
  + No Diagnosis: You do not have an eating disorder.
* **Eating Habits Frequency (5)**: How often do you engage in your eating habits?
* **Food Aversion (6)**: How often do you experience food aversion?
* **Food Frequency (7)**: Describe your typical food intake frequency.
* **Weight Loss (8)**: How much weight have you lost?
* **Weight Gain (9)**: How much weight have you gained?
* **Weight (10)**: Describe your current weight.
* **Height (11)**: Describe your current height.
* **Date Of Last Meal (12)**: Record the date of your last meal.
* **Time Of Last Meal (13)**: Record the time of your last meal.
* **Doctor Visits (14)**: How often do you visit the doctor?
* **Medication (15)**: Do you take medication for your eating habits or disorder? 
* **Hospital Visits (16)**: How often do you visit the hospital?
* **Other Doctor Visits (17)**: Describe any other doctor visits you've had.
* **Other Doctor Visits Frequency (18)**: Describe the frequency of those visits.
* **Emergency Room Visits (19)**: How often do you visit the emergency room?
* **Emergency Room Visits Frequency (20)**: Describe the frequency of those visits.
* **Family History (21)**: Select all that apply: 
  + Anorexia Nervosa: A family history of anorexia nervosa.
  + Binge Eating Disorder: A family history of binge eating disorder.
  + Avoidant/Restrictive Food Intake Disorder: A family history of avoidant/restrictive food intake disorder.
  + Other: Describe your family history in the "Other" field.
  + No Diagnosis: There is no eating disorder history in your family.
* **Family History Frequency (22)**: How often do you experience family eating habits or disorders?
* **Friends (23)**: Select all that apply: 
  + Close: You have close friends who support you.
  + Distant: You have distant friends who do not support you.
  + False: You do not have friends.
* **Friends Frequency (24)**: How often do you interact with friends?
* **Notes (25)**: Provide any additional information you think would be helpful for the professionals to understand your situation better. 

Please note that this is a general guide and that specific requirements may vary depending on individual circumstances. If you are unsure about any question or section, please ask a professional for clarification.
