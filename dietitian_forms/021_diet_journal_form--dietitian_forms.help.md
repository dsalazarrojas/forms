# diet_journal_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The diet journal form is for recording and maintaining a daily diet journal. It is used to keep track of daily intake, store and analyze data on daily consumption patterns, and provide personalized dietary advice or recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields as needed, starting from the "date" field.
2. Enter your daily intake for each meal and snack.
3. Enter your email and phone number.
4. If applicable, select the assigned tool and form type.
5. Review and update the form as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Daily Intake Form**:
	+ A text field to record your daily food intake. 
* **Breakfast**:
	+ Enter your breakfast details.
* **Morning Snack**:
	+ Enter your morning snack details.
* **Lunch**:
	+ Enter your lunch details.
* **Afternoon Snack**:
	+ Enter your afternoon snack details.
* **Dinner**:
	+ Enter your dinner details.
* **Bed-time Munchies**:
	+ Enter your bed-time snack details.
* **Date**:
	+ A required field to select the date of the entry.
* **Time**:
	+ A field to select the time of the entry.
* **Notes**:
	+ A field to record any additional notes or comments.
* **Email**:
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Phone**:
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Assigned Tool**:
	+ Select the tool assigned to you.
* **Form ID**:
	+ A field to select the form ID.
* **Form Type**:
	+ Select the type of form.
* **Form Version**:
	+ Enter the form version.
* **Form Status**:
	+ Select the form status.
* **Form Created At**:
	+ A field to select the date the form was created.
* **Form Updated At**:
	+ A field to select the date the form was updated.
* **Form Creator**:
	+ Enter your name. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, including the "date" field.
* Review and update the form as needed.
