# Personalized Weight Loss Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your health, lifestyle, and medical history to help create a personalized weight loss plan tailored to your needs. Please provide as much detail as possible to ensure accurate assessment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer all the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Provide as much detail as possible in the optional fields.
3. Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
4. If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`1`, `text`, required): This field is used to collect your basic information such as name, age, and contact details.
* **Health Information** (`2`, `text`, required): Please provide your health information such as current weight, height, and any medical conditions you may have.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`3`, `text`, required): List any food restrictions or dietary requirements you may have, such as vegetarian or gluten-free.
* **Exercise Habits** (`4`, `text`, required): Describe your current exercise habits, including frequency and type of physical activity.
* **Weight Loss Goals** (`5`, `text`, required): Specify your weight loss goals, including the amount of weight you want to lose and any specific goals you have.
* **Current Diet** (`6`, `text`, required): Describe your current eating habits, including your typical diet and any specific food restrictions.
* **Medical Conditions** (`7`, `text`, optional): If you have any medical conditions, please list them here.
* **Medications** (`8`, `text`, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Previous Weight Loss Programs** (`9`, `text`, optional): If you have participated in any previous weight loss programs, describe them here.
* **Family History** (`10`, `text`, optional): Provide any relevant medical history from your family members.
* **Social Media** (`11`, `text`, optional): Please list any social media usage that may be relevant to your weight loss journey.
* **Medical Test Results** (`12`, `text`, optional): Upload any relevant medical test results or reports.
* **Medical History** (`13`, `text`, required): Please provide any relevant medical history, including surgeries, hospitalizations, or other health events.
* **Emergency Contact** (`14`, `text`, required): Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`15`, `text`, required): Provide any relevant emergency contact information such as phone numbers and addresses.
* **Weight Loss Coaches** (`16`, `text`, optional): If you have worked with a weight loss coach, describe their role and any relevant information they provided.
* **Medical Insurance** (`17`, `text`, optional): If you have medical insurance, please provide the name and policy number.
* **Billing** (`18`, `text`, required): Provide any billing information for your medical insurance or any out-of-pocket payments.
* **Consent** (`19`, `text`, required): Please confirm that you consent to the collection and use of your personal data for weight loss purposes.
* **Notes** (`20`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about your weight loss journey.
* **Medical Professionals** (`21`, `text`, optional): Please list any medical professionals you have consulted or are currently working with.
* **Medical Test Results 1** (`22`, `text`, optional): Upload any relevant medical test results or reports.
* **Medical Test Results 2** (`23`, `text`, optional): Upload any additional relevant medical test results or reports.
* **Medical Test Results 3** (`24`, `text`, optional): Upload any other relevant medical test results or reports.
* **Medical Test Results 4** (`25`, `text`, optional): Upload any other relevant medical test results or reports.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
* Please provide as much detail as possible in the optional fields.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us.
