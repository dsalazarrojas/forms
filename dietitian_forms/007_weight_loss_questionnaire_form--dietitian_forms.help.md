# Weight Loss Questionnaire Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help healthcare professionals gather information about a patient's weight loss goals, medical history, and habits. The goal of this form is to provide a comprehensive picture of the patient's weight loss journey, allowing healthcare professionals to offer personalized guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and select the relevant answers for each question. Make sure to answer all the questions honestly and accurately, as this information will help your healthcare professional create a tailored plan for your weight loss goals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Weight Loss Goals
* **Weight Loss Goals** (`weight_loss_goals`, `text`, required: false): This field allows you to describe your weight loss goals. Be as specific and detailed as possible about what you hope to achieve.

### 2. Age
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, required: true): Please enter your age in years. This information is essential for us to understand your current health status and potential risks associated with weight loss.

### 3. Height
* **Height** (`height`, `text`, required: false): Enter your height in centimeters or feet and inches. This information will help us understand your body mass index (BMI) and potential risks associated with weight loss.

### 4. Weight
* **Weight** (`weight`, `number`, required: true): Enter your current weight in kilograms or pounds. This information is crucial for us to understand your current weight status and create a suitable weight loss plan.

### 5. Target Weight
* **Target Weight** (`target_weight`, `number`, required: false): If you have a target weight in mind, please enter it here. This will help us understand your desired weight loss goal.

### 6. Motivation
* **Motivation** (`motivation`, `text`, required: false): Describe your motivation for weight loss. This can be a personal goal or a medical reason, such as a health condition. Be as specific and honest as possible.

### 7. Medical Condition
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `select_one`, required: false): Do you have any existing medical conditions that may affect your weight loss journey? Select 'Yes' if you have any medical conditions, and we will take this into consideration when creating your weight loss plan.

### 8. Medical Condition Options
* **Medical Condition Options** (`medical_condition_options`, `select_multiple`, required: false): If you selected 'Yes' for the previous question, please specify any medical conditions you have. This will help us understand your unique needs and risks.

### 9. Medications
* **Medications** (`medications`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Are you currently taking any medications that may affect your weight loss journey? Select 'Yes' if you are taking any medications, and we will take this into consideration when creating your weight loss plan.

### 10. Supplements
* **Supplements** (`supplements`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Are you currently taking any supplements that may affect your weight loss journey? Select 'Yes' if you are taking any supplements, and we will take this into consideration when creating your weight loss plan.

### 11. Allergies
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Do you have any allergies or sensitivities that may affect your weight loss journey? Select 'Yes' if you have any allergies or sensitivities, and we will take this into consideration when creating your weight loss plan.

### 12. Medical History
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Please describe any relevant medical history or past experiences that may impact your weight loss journey. This can include previous weight loss attempts or medical conditions that may affect your weight loss.

### 13. Current Diet
* **Current Diet** (`current_diet`, `text`, required: false): Describe your current diet and eating habits. This will help us understand your nutritional needs and preferences.

### 14. Physical Activities
* **Physical Activities** (`physical_activities`, `text`, required: false): Describe any physical activities you enjoy or plan to incorporate into your weight loss journey. This can include exercise, sports, or other physical activities that may impact your weight loss.

### 15. Diet Plan
* **Diet Plan** (`diet_plan`, `text`, required: false): Describe your current diet plan or any meal plans you follow. This will help us understand your nutritional goals and preferences.

### 16. Exercise Plan
* **Exercise Plan** (`exercise_plan`, `text`, required: false): Describe your current exercise plan or any exercise goals you have. This will help us understand your physical activity needs and preferences.

### 17. Goal Weight Loss Percentage
* **Goal Weight Loss Percentage** (`goal_weight_loss_percentage`, `number`, required: false): If you have a specific goal weight loss percentage in mind, please enter it here.

### 18. Weight Loss Start Date
* **Weight Loss Start Date** (`weight_loss_start_date`, `date`, required: false): If you have a specific start date for your weight loss journey, please enter it here.

### 19. Weight Loss End Date
* **Weight Loss End Date** (`weight_loss_end_date`, `date`, required: false): If you have a specific end date for your weight loss journey, please enter it here.

### 20. Weight Change
* **Weight Change** (`weight_change`, `text`, required: false): Describe any weight changes you've experienced in the past. This can include weight loss or gain, and will help us understand your past experiences with weight loss.

### 21. Weight Loss Method
* **Weight Loss Method** (`weight_loss_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Are you interested in using any specific weight loss methods, such as surgery or medication? Select 'Yes' if you are interested in any of these methods, and we will take this into consideration when creating your weight loss plan.

### 22. Weight Loss Goal Duration
* **Weight Loss Goal Duration** (`weight_loss_goal_duration`, `number`, required: false): If you have a specific time frame for your weight loss journey, please enter it here.

### 23. Weight Loss Goal Frequency
* **Weight Loss Goal Frequency** (`weight_loss_goal_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): How often do you plan to work on your weight loss goals? Select 'Yes' if you plan to work on your goals daily, weekly, or monthly, and we will take this into consideration when creating your weight loss plan.

### 24. Weight Loss Goal Intensity
* **Weight Loss Goal Intensity** (`weight_loss_goal_intensity`, `select_one`, required: false): How intense do you plan to work on your weight loss goals? Select 'Yes' if you plan to work hard or lightly, and we will take this into consideration when creating your weight loss plan.

### 25. Weight Loss Goal Frequency Other
* **Weight Loss Goal Frequency Other** (`weight_loss_goal_frequency_other`, `text`, required: false): If you selected 'Yes' for the previous question, please describe how often you plan to work on your weight loss goals.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when answering these questions.
- Take your time to think about your weight loss goals and motivations.
- If you're unsure about any of the questions, please ask for clarification.
- This form is designed to help your healthcare professional understand your needs and create a personalized weight loss plan.
- Please follow the advice and guidance provided by your healthcare professional.
- Remember, a healthy weight loss journey is a long-term process; be patient and consistent.
