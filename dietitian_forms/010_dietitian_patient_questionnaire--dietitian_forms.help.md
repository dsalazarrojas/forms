# Dietitian Patient Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dietitian Patient Questionnaire is designed to collect vital information about new patients to assist dietitians in providing personalized nutrition advice and support. Please take a few minutes to complete this form accurately and honestly, and we'll do our best to tailor our services to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start on page 1, "New Patient Enrollment." This page serves as an introduction to the form and is not a required field.
2.  Proceed to page 2, "Personal Info." Here, you'll be asked to provide basic information about yourself, such as your contact details.
3.  On page 3, "Medical History," you'll be asked to describe your medical history. This section is not mandatory, but it helps us understand your health background for better care.
4.  Move on to page 4, "Contact Info." This section allows you to provide additional contact information for follow-up consultations and support.
5.  Finally, on page 5, "Nutrition Habits," you'll be asked about your eating habits and dietary preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **New Patient Enrollment (Page 1)** (`new_patient_enrollment`, `note`, not required): This page is an introduction to the form and is not a required field. You can skip it if you wish.
*   **Personal Info (Page 2)** (`personal_info`, `text`, not required): This section asks for your basic information, including your contact details. Please provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure we can contact you effectively.
*   **Medical History (Page 3)** (`medical_history`, `note`, not required): In this section, please describe your medical history. This helps us understand your health background and provide better guidance.
*   **Contact Info (Page 4)** (`contact_info`, `text`, not required): You can provide additional contact information for follow-up consultations and support. This ensures we can reach you promptly.
*   **Nutrition Habits (Page 5)** (`nutrition_habits`, `text`, not required): This section inquires about your eating habits and dietary preferences. Please answer honestly to help us tailor our support to your needs.

## Tips

*   Be accurate and honest when filling out the form to ensure the best possible care and support.
*   If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask your dietitian for clarification.
*   Completing this form will help us better understand your needs and provide personalized guidance.
