# Dietary Preferences Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool for healthcare professionals or nutritionists to gather detailed information about a client's dietary habits, restrictions, and health conditions. It helps in creating a comprehensive profile of the client's diet and nutritional needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the correct options for each field as relevant to your dietary habits and conditions.
* Answer all fields, but note that some fields are optional.
* Be honest and thorough in your responses, as this information will be used to provide personalized guidance.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Food Restrictions
* **Food Restrictions** (`food_restrictions`, `text`, required: false): This field asks you to list any food restrictions you have, e.g., vegetarian, gluten-free, or lactose intolerance.

### Eating Habits
* **Eating Habits** (`eating_habits`, `text`, required: false): Describe your regular eating habits, including the frequency of meals and snacking.

### Meal Frequency
* **Meal Frequency** (`meal_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Option 1: Daily
	+ Option 2: 5-6 times a week
	+ Option 3: 3-4 times a week
	+ Option 4: Rarely

Select the frequency that best describes your usual meal schedule.

### Favorite Fruits and Veggies
* **Favorite Fruits and Veggies** (`favorite_foods`, `text`, required: false): List your favorite fruits and vegetables. This can be as many or as few as you like.

### Food Allergies
* **Food Allergies** (`food_allergies`, `text`, required: false): If you have any food allergies, please list them here.

### Dietary Restrictions
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Option 1: Vegetarian
	+ Option 2: Vegan
	+ Option 3: Low-carb
	+ Option 4: Other (please specify)

Select the dietary restrictions that apply to you.

### Health Conditions
* **Health Conditions** (`health_conditions`, `text`, required: false): If you have any health conditions, such as diabetes or celiac disease, please list them here.

### Medications
* **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, required: false): If you take any medications, please list them here.

### Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional information that you think is relevant to your diet and health.

### Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your contact information so that the healthcare professional or nutritionist can follow up with you.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your responses.
- Use the space provided to elaborate on your answers if needed.
- If unsure about any question, feel free to ask for guidance.
- This form is designed to be completed with the help of a healthcare professional or nutritionist.
- If you have completed this form and have further questions, contact the professional who provided it to you.
