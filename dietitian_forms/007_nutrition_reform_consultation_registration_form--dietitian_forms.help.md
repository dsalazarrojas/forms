# Nutrition Reform Consultation Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals register for a nutrition reform consultation. It collects relevant information to facilitate a smooth and effective consultation process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field (format: <username>@<domain>).
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
6. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
7. Enter your time of birth in the "Time of Birth" field (format: HH:MM).
8. Enter the date of the consultation you wish to schedule in the "Date of Consultation" field (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
9. Enter the time of the consultation you wish to schedule in the "Time of Consultation" field (format: HH:MM).
10. Finally, enter any additional consultation details in the "Consultation Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., passport, driver's license).
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., passport, driver's license).
* **Email** (email): Enter your valid email address. This will be used for communication and scheduling purposes.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for scheduling and consultation purposes.
* **Address** (text): Enter your address where you can be reached for consultation purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (date): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 1999-01-01).
* **Time of Birth** (time): Enter your time of birth in the format HH:MM (e.g., 08:00).
* **Date of Consultation** (date): Enter the date you wish to schedule the consultation in the format YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 2024-03-01).
* **Time of Consultation** (time): Enter the time you wish to schedule the consultation in the format HH:MM (e.g., 14:00).
* **Consultation Details** (text): Enter any additional details or information about your consultation requirements.
