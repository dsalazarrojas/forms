# Nutritional Counseling Liability Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nutritional Counseling Liability Release Form is designed to gather essential information from both clients and counselors to establish clear communication and expectations before proceeding with nutritional counseling services. This form helps to ensure that both parties understand their responsibilities and limitations, thereby preventing potential misunderstandings and liability issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Please fill out the form thoroughly and accurately**. All fields are required, as indicated by the asterisk (\*) symbol.
2. Click "Submit" to send the form once you have completed it.
3. Review and sign the form to confirm your understanding and agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (Page 1)**:
  * **Client Information (<name>):** Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
  * **Client Information (<type>):** This field is for your demographic information, such as address and contact details.
* **Counselor Information (Page 2)**:
  * **Counselor Information (<name>):** Enter the name of your counselor or dietitian.
  * **Counselor Information (<type>):** This field is for your counselor's or dietitian's information, such as title or contact details.
* **Limitations Acknowledgment (Page 3)**:
  * **Limitations Acknowledgment (<name>):** Read and acknowledge the limitations of the services provided.
  * **Limitations Description (<type>):** This field is for the detailed description of the limitations.
* **Client Acknowledgment (Page 4)**:
  * **Client Acknowledgment (<name>):** Read and sign to acknowledge your understanding of the services provided.
  * **Client Acknowledgment (<type>):** This field is for your acknowledgment of the services limitations.
* **Counselor Agreement (Page 5)**:
  * **Counselor Agreement (<name>):** Read and sign to confirm your agreement with the terms of service.
  * **Counselor Agreement (<type>):** This field is for your acknowledgment of the services provided.
* **Limitations Description (Page 6)** (Optional):
  * **Limitations Description (<name>):** A detailed description of the limitations of the services provided.
  * **Limitations Description (<type>):** This field is for the counselor to provide additional information about the limitations.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions thoroughly and accurately to ensure clear communication and understanding.
* If you have any concerns or questions, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
* Review the form carefully before signing to ensure you understand the terms and limitations.
