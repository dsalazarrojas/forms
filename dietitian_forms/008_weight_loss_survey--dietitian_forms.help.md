# Weight Loss Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their weight loss goals, habits, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and honest information about your weight loss goals and habits.
2. Answer each question carefully, and make sure to select the options that best match your preferences.
3. If you're unsure about any of the questions, don't hesitate to ask for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Current Weight** (`current_weight`, number, optional): Enter your current weight in kilograms or pounds.
* **Height** (`height`, number, optional): Enter your height in centimeters or inches.
* **Weight Loss Goal** (`weight_loss_goal`, select_one, optional): Select the weight loss goal that best fits your needs, ranging from 5-50 pounds/10-20 kg.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): Enter your age in years.
* **Diet Background** (`diet_background`, text, optional): Describe your current diet habits, including any dietary restrictions or preferences.
* **Physical Activity** (`physical_activity`, select_multiple, optional): Select the physical activities you regularly engage in, such as walking, running, cycling, or swimming.
* **Sleep Duration** (`sleep_duration`, time, optional): Enter your average sleep duration in hours and minutes.
* **Food Intake** (`food_intake`, note, optional): Describe your food intake habits, including any eating patterns or restrictions.
* **Exercise Frequency** (`exercise_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select the frequency of your regular exercise, such as 1-2 times a day or 5-6 times a week.
* **Weight Loss Method** (`weight_loss_method`, select_one, optional): Choose the weight loss method you prefer, including diet, exercise, both, or other.
