# Healthy Eating Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Healthy Eating Survey is a form designed to collect information about your eating habits, exercise, and lifestyle. It's essential to fill out the form accurately to ensure the collected data is reliable and useful for further analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Answer the questions honestly and accurately to provide useful information.
* Select the correct frequency or quantity for each question.
* If a question doesn't apply to you, select "null" or "not applicable" to indicate that.
* You can also add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What frequency is this survey about? (`survey_frequency`)**: This question asks about the frequency of your eating habits. Please select the correct frequency from the available options: daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly.
* **What is your typical food consumption? (`food_consumption`)**: This question asks about your typical food consumption. Select all the food groups that apply to you, such as fruits, vegetables, grains, dairy, or meat.
* **How much water do you drink each day? (`water_intake`)**: Please enter the number of water you drink each day. Note that this question is not limited to a specific unit of measurement (e.g., cups, liters).
* **What frequency do you exercise? (`exercise_frequency`)**: This question asks about the frequency of your physical activity. Select the correct frequency from the available options: rarely, sometimes, often, or daily.
* **How many hours do you sleep each night? (`sleep_duration`)**: Please enter the number of hours you sleep each night.
* **How often do you eat meals? (`meal_frequency`)**: This question asks about the frequency of your meals. Select the correct frequency from the available options: less than 3, 3-5, 6-9, or more than 9 times a day.
* **Do you have any physical activity? (`physical_activity`)**: This question asks about your physical activity level. Select all the activities that apply to you, such as yes or no.
* **Would you like a dietitian's recommendation? (`dietician_recommendation`)**: This question asks if you would like to receive dietary advice or recommendations. Select yes or no.
* **Notes (`notes`)**: This is an optional field where you can add any additional comments or notes about your eating habits or lifestyle.
* **Email address (`email`)**: Please enter your email address.
* **Phone number (`phone`)**: Please enter your phone number.

Note: This guide is intended to help users understand the form and its purpose. The form should not be used as a medical diagnosis tool, and any results or recommendations should only be interpreted by healthcare professionals.
