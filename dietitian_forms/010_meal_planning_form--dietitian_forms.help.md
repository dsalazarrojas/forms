# Meal Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meal Planning Form is designed to collect information about your dietary preferences, habits, and goals to help create personalized meal plans tailored to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select one option for "What type of meals do you typically eat?" on page 1.
2. On page 2, select all relevant options for "What are your dietary preferences?" and "Do you have any food allergies or intolerances?"
3. On page 3:
	* Select one option for "What are your meal planning goals?"
	* Select all relevant options for "What are your favorite foods or cuisines?" and "Do you have any specific dietary restrictions?"
	* Select one option for "How often do you eat out or order takeout?"
4. On page 4:
	* Select one option for "What is your preferred method of meal planning?"
	* Select all relevant options for "Do you have any specific dietary needs or concerns?"
5. On pages 5-6:
	* Select all relevant options for "Do you have any specific meal planning schedules?" and "Do you have a preferred cooking method?"
6. On page 7:
	* Select the number of meals you typically eat per day.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What type of meals do you typically eat?** (page 1)
	+ Choose one option that best describes your typical meal types.
* **What are your dietary preferences?** (page 2)
	+ Select all options that apply to your dietary preferences (Vegan, Gluten-free, Dairy-free, Low-carb).
* **Do you have any food allergies or intolerances?** (page 2)
	+ Select all applicable options for food allergies or intolerances.
* **What are your meal planning goals?** (page 3)
	+ Choose one option that best describes your meal planning goals (Weight Loss, Weight Gain, Health Maintenance).
* **What are your favorite foods or cuisines?** (page 3)
	+ Select all options that apply to your favorite foods or cuisines.
* **Do you have any specific dietary restrictions?** (page 3)
	+ Select all options that apply to your dietary restrictions.
* **How often do you eat out or order takeout?** (page 3)
	+ Choose one option that best describes your eating out frequency.
* **What is your preferred method of meal planning?** (page 4)
	+ Choose one option that best describes your meal planning method (App, Cookbook, Online Recipes).
* **Do you have any specific dietary needs or concerns?** (page 4)
	+ Select all options that apply to your dietary needs or concerns.
* **Do you have any specific meal planning schedules?** (page 5)
	+ Select all options that apply to your meal planning schedules (Meal Prep, Batch Cooking, Other).
* **Do you have a preferred cooking method?** (page 5)
	+ Choose one option that best describes your preferred cooking method.
* **How many meals do you typically eat per day?** (page 7)
	+ Enter the number of meals you typically eat per day.

## Tips
* Take your time to review each page carefully before submitting your answers.
* Make sure to select all relevant options for each field to provide a comprehensive understanding of your preferences.
* Don't hesitate to select "Other" or "Batch Cooking" for meal planning schedules if they don't fit the provided options.
