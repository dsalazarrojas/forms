# Ideal Weight Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an individual's ideal weight, including their gender, height, and body mass index (BMI). The form is used to help individuals understand their ideal weight range and provide guidance on how to achieve it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your gender from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter your ideal weight in the corresponding unit (pounds, kilograms, or stones).
3. Enter your height in centimeters or feet and inches.
4. If you have any special instructions or additional information, enter it in the "Special Instructions" field.
5. Select your ideal BMI range from the dropdown menu.
6. Choose your gender identity (Female, Male, Non-Binary, or Other).
7. Enter your date of birth.
8. If you have any additional special instructions, enter it in the "Special Instructions 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gender**: Select your gender (Female, Male, Other).
	+ This field is used to determine your gender.
* **Ideal Weight**: Enter your ideal weight.
	+ Enter your ideal weight in the corresponding unit (pounds, kilograms, or stones).
	+ Example: 150 pounds or 67.5 kilograms.
* **Height**: Enter your height.
	+ Enter your height in centimeters or feet and inches.
	+ Example: 170 cm or 5'8" (180 cm).
* **Special Instructions**: Enter any additional information about your ideal weight.
	+ If you have any specific health conditions or concerns, you can enter them here.
* **Weight Unit**: Select the unit of your ideal weight (pounds, kilograms, or stones).
	+ This field is used to convert your ideal weight to the corresponding unit.
* **Body Mass Index Range**: Select your ideal BMI range.
	+ This field is used to determine your ideal BMI.
* **Gender Identity**: Select your gender identity (Female, Male, Non-Binary, or Other).
	+ This field is used to determine your gender identity.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
	+ This field is used to determine your age.
* **Special Instructions 2**: Enter any additional information or special instructions.
	+ If you have any additional concerns or comments, you can enter them here.
