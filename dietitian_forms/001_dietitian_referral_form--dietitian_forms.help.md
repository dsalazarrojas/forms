# Dietitian Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dietitian Referral Form is designed to collect information about patient referrals made to the dietitian's practice. This form helps track and record the source, reason, and details of each referral. Completing this form accurately provides valuable insights for the dietitian to assess and follow up on referrals.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Start by selecting the Referral Source**: Choose the source of the referral, such as "Client", "Colleague", or "Online Search".
2.  **Enter the Referral Date**: Select the date when the referral was made.
3.  **Provide the Referral Name and Contact Information**: Enter the name and contact details of the person or organization making the referral.
4.  **Explain the Referral Reason**: Describe the reason for the referral.
5.  **Specify the Referred To**: Choose to whom the patient was referred (Dietitian or Other).
6.  **Add a Referral Comment**: Enter any additional comments or details about the referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Referral Source**: Select the source of the referral, such as "Client", "Colleague", or "Online Search". This helps the dietitian understand how the referral was made.
*   **Referral Date**: Enter the date when the referral was made. This ensures accurate tracking and follow-up.
*   **Referral Name**: Enter the name of the person or organization making the referral. This helps the dietitian verify the referral's authenticity.
*   **Referral Contact Info**: Provide the contact details of the person or organization making the referral. This facilitates communication and follow-up.
*   **Referral Reason**: Explain the reason for the referral. This helps the dietitian understand the patient's needs and provide tailored care.
*   **Referred To**: Choose to whom the patient was referred (Dietitian or Other). This ensures accurate distribution of referral information.
*   **Referral Comment**: Enter any additional comments or details about the referral. This provides context and insight for the dietitian to assess and follow up on the referral.

## Tips

*   Ensure accuracy when selecting the Referral Source to ensure accurate referral tracking.
*   Enter the Referral Date as accurately as possible to avoid confusion.
*   Provide clear and concise Referral Reason to help the dietitian understand the patient's needs.
*   Double-check the Referral Contact Info to ensure correct contact details are recorded.
*   Be detailed in the Referral Comment to provide context and insight for follow-up.
