# nutrition consultation form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a comprehensive platform for patients to share their health details and goals with dietitians and healthcare professionals, enabling personalized consultations and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and date of birth to identify yourself as a patient.
2. Provide your contact number and email (if applicable) for communication purposes.
3. Indicate your medical conditions, allergies, and medications to ensure a safe and effective consultation.
4. Specify the reason for your consultation, which will help the dietitian understand your needs.
5. Choose the consultation date and time that suits you best.
6. Set the expected duration of the consultation.
7. Share your dietary preferences.
8. Describe your goals and expectations from the consultation.
9. Select the date for achieving your goals.
10. Submit the form to initiate the consultation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Patient Date of Birth** (`patient_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format dd/mm/yyyy or dd/mm/yy.
* **Patient Contact Number** (`patient_contact_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for secure communication (not required but recommended).
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_multiple, required): Indicate any medical conditions you have, such as diabetes, hypertension, or allergies. Select 'Yes' if you have any of these conditions.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, required): List any allergies or substances you are allergic to, such as nuts, peanuts, or shellfish. Select 'Yes' for each allergy you have.
* **Medications** (`medications`, select_multiple, required): List any medications you are currently taking, such as prescription or over-the-counter medications. Select 'Yes' for each medication you are taking.
* **Reason for Consultation** (`reason_for_consultation`, text, required): Describe the reason why you are seeking nutritional consultation, e.g., weight loss, nutrition planning, or dietary advice.
* **Consultation Date** (`consultation_date`, date, required): Choose a date for the consultation.
* **Consultation Time** (`consultation_time`, time, required): Select a time for the consultation.
* **Consultation Duration** (`consultation_duration`, number, required): Set the expected duration of the consultation.
* **Dietary Preferences** (`dietary_preferences`, select_multiple, required): Indicate your dietary preferences, such as vegetarian, vegan, or gluten-free.
* **Goals Description** (`goals_description`, text, required): Describe your goals and expectations from the consultation, such as weight loss or improved overall health.
* **Goals Date** (`goals_date`, date, required): Choose a date by which you aim to achieve your goals.
