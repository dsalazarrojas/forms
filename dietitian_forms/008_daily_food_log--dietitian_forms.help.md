# Daily Food Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Daily Food Log" form is designed to track your daily food intake, including the amount consumed and the nutrients present in each food item. This form helps you monitor your dietary habits and makes it easier to track your progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the date fields for each day you want to log. You can do this by typing the date in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
2. For each food item, enter the name of the food.
3. Enter the quantity consumed for each food item in the corresponding "Quantity" field. You can enter values in units of g, oz, or ml.
4. Select the nutrient you want to log for each food item by choosing "Yes" or "No" from the drop-down menu.
5. Finally, enter your weight and height in the respective fields.
