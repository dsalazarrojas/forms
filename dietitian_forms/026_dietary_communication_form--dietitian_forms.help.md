# Dietary Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect dietary information from users for healthcare or nutrition-related purposes, such as assessing dietary restrictions or planning meal plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above and answer each question accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Information (1)** (`user_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and contact details to enable us to communicate with you effectively.
*   **Food Preferences (2)** (`food_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the types of food you prefer or dislike.
*   **Medical Restrictions (3)** (`medical_restrictions`, select_one, required): Select any medical restrictions you have, such as allergies or conditions that affect your diet.
*   **Emergency Contact Information (4)** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
*   **Allergies (5)** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies you have.
*   **Medical Conditions (6)** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Mention any significant medical conditions you are experiencing.
*   **Medications (7)** (`medications`, text, optional): Indicate any medications you are currently taking.
*   **Notes (8)** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes that you think are relevant.
*   **Contact Preferences (9)** (`contact_preferences`, select_one, required): Select how you prefer to be contacted.
*   **Language (10)** (`language`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred language.
*   **Dietary Restrictions (11)** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, optional): Specify any dietary restrictions you have.
*   **Date of Birth (12)** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
*   **Date of Last Medical Visit (13)** (`date_of_last_medical_visit`, date, optional): Record the date and details of your last medical visit, if applicable.
*   **Last Medical Visit Diagnosis (14)** (`last_medical_visit_diagnosis`, text, optional): Provide the diagnosis from your last medical visit, if applicable.
*   **Medical History (15)** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Provide your medical history.

Please take the time to answer each question carefully, as the information will be used for the purpose of assessing your dietary needs and planning. If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to ask.
