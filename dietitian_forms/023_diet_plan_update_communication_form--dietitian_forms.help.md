# Diet Plan Update Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather and communicate updates for the next meeting between the dietitian and the client. It ensures a smooth and effective communication process, preventing any potential misunderstandings or miscommunications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the required information. You can find more detailed explanations of each field below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Updated Diet Plan** (`updated_diet_plan`, `text`, optional): This field is used to enter the updated diet plan details. Please provide a concise and clear description of the updated plan.
* **Client Comments** (`client_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments or feedback from the client regarding the updated diet plan.
* **Next Meeting Date** (`next_meeting_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the next meeting between you and the dietitian.
* **Next Meeting Time** (`next_meeting_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the next meeting between you and the dietitian.
* **Updated Dietician Comments** (`updated_dietician_comments`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all applicable comments from the provided options.
* **Updated Dietician Suggestions** (`updated_dietician_suggestions`, `text`, optional): Enter any suggestions or ideas from the dietitian regarding the updated diet plan.
* **Next Meeting Place** (`next_meeting_place`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of the next meeting between you and the dietitian.
* **Next Meeting Agenda** (`next_meeting_agenda`, `text`, optional): Enter the agenda for the next meeting between you and the dietitian.
* **Updated Diet Plan Notes** (`updated_diet_plan_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes regarding the updated diet plan.
* **Updated Diet Plan Comments** (`updated_diet_plan_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments regarding the updated diet plan.
* **Next Meeting Notes** (`next_meeting_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any notes for the next meeting.
* **Updated Dician Phone** (`updated_dietician_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the updated phone number of the dietitian.
* **Updated Dician Email** (`updated_dietician_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the updated email of the dietitian.
* **Updated Dician Address** (`updated_dietician_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the updated address of the dietitian.
* **Next Meeting Location** (`next_meeting_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of the next meeting.
* **Updated Diet Plan Status** (`updated_diet_plan_status`, `text`, optional): Enter the status of the updated diet plan.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `note`, optional): This field is reserved for the client's signature.
* **Updated Dician Name** (`updated_dietician_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the updated name of the dietitian.
* **Updated Dician Phone 2** (`updated_dietician_phone_2`, `text`, optional): Enter the updated phone number of the dietitian.
* **Updated Dician Email 2** (`updated_dietician_email_2`, `email`, optional): Enter the updated email of the dietitian.
* **Updated Dician Address 2** (`updated_dietician_address_2`, `text`, optional): Enter the updated address of the dietitian.
* **Next Meeting Time 2** (`next_meeting_time_2`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the next meeting.
* **Next Meeting Date 2** (`next_meeting_date_2`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the next meeting.
* **Next Meeting Place 2** (`next_meeting_place_2`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of the next meeting.

## Tips
Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure a smooth communication process. If you have any questions or doubts, please don't hesitate to reach out to your dietitian for assistance.
