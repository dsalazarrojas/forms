# Sugar Intake Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your sugar intake habits. Please take a few minutes to complete this survey to help us better understand your habits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that apply to you for each question. You can select multiple answers for some questions, depending on the type of question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sugar Intake**: (`sugar_intake`) (`select_multiple`, required/optional) 
Please select all the times you consume sugar from the options provided.
* **Frequency of Sugar Consumption**: (`sugar_frequency`) (`select_one`, required/optional) 
Please select how often you consume sugar.
* **Added Sugars**: (`added_sugars`) (`number`, required/optional) 
Please enter the amount of added sugars you consume in the space provided.
* **Daily Sugar Intake**: (`daily_sugar_intake`) (`number`, required/optional) 
Please enter the amount of sugar you consume in a typical day.
* **Time of Day**: (`time_of_day`) (`time`, required/optional) 
Please select a typical time of day when you consume sugar.
* **Additional Notes**: (`note`, required/optional) 
Please add any additional comments or questions you have about your sugar intake habits.
* **Email**: (`email`, required/optional) 
Please enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted with more information about your sugar intake habits.
* **Phone**: (`phone`, required/optional) 
Please enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted with more information about your sugar intake habits.
* **Name**: (`name`, required/optional) 
Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed when contacted.
