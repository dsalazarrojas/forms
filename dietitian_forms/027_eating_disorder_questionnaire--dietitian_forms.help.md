# Eating Disorder Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This questionnaire is designed to collect information about your eating habits, patterns, and experiences related to eating disorders. The information collected will help our dietitian understand your behavior and provide personalized guidance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions honestly and as accurately as possible.
2. For questions with multiple options, choose the one that best describes your experience.
3. Describe your answers in as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your typical daily food intake like?** (`eating_disorder_questionnaire_1`, text, required)
	+ Describe your typical daily food intake, including breakfast, lunch, and dinner.
* **How often do you experience hunger or fullness?** (`hunger_frequencies`, number, required)
	+ Use a scale from 1 to 10 to rate your hunger or fullness levels.
* **Can you describe your eating patterns over the past week?** (`eating_patterns`, text, required)
	+ Describe your eating patterns, including eating times, food types, etc.
* **Have you noticed any changes in your weight recently?** (`weight_changes`, select_one, required)
	+ Choose 'True' if you have noticed weight changes, 'False' otherwise.
* **How do you feel emotionally when you're eating or thinking about food?** (`emotional_states`, text, required)
	+ Describe your emotional states, e.g., anxious, satisfied, or stressed.
* **Can you list any symptoms you experience related to eating disorders?** (`eating_disorder_symptoms`, checkbox, required)
	+ List any symptoms you experience, e.g., binge eating, purging, etc.
* **Are there any social pressures that affect your eating habits?** (`social_pressures`, select_one, required)
	+ Choose 'True' if you experience social pressures, 'False' otherwise.
* **Have you experienced any emotional states while eating or thinking about food?** (`emotional_states_2`, text, required)
	+ Describe your emotional states.
* **Are there any specific food intakes that trigger or alleviate symptoms?** (`food_intake_2`, checkbox, required)
	+ List specific food intakes.
* **Weight Changes 2** (`weight_changes_2`, select_one, required)
	+ Choose 'True' if you have noticed weight changes, 'False' otherwise.
* **Can you describe your current eating habits?** (`eating_habits_2`, text, required)
	+ Describe your current eating habits.
* **Can you list any additional eating patterns that contribute to your symptoms?** (`eating_patterns_2`, checkbox, required)
	+ List any additional eating patterns.
* **Are there any additional symptoms related to eating disorders?** (`eating_disorder_symptoms_2`, checkbox, required)
	+ List any additional symptoms.
* **Are there any social pressures that affect your food intake?** (`social_pressures_2`, select_one, required)
	+ Choose 'True' if you experience social pressures, 'False' otherwise.
* **Eating Habits 3** (`eating_habits_3`, text, required)
	+ Describe your eating habits.
* **Eating Patterns 3** (`eating_patterns_3`, checkbox, required)
	+ List any additional eating patterns.
* **Eating Disorder Symptoms 3** (`eating_disorder_symptoms_3`, checkbox, required)
	+ List any additional symptoms.
* **Social Pressures 3** (`social_pressures_3`, select_one, required)
	+ Choose 'True' if you experience social pressures, 'False' otherwise.
* **Food Intake 3** (`food_intake_3`, checkbox, required)
