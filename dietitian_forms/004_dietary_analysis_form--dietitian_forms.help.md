# Dietary Analysis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dietary Analysis Form is designed to gather information from patients or their representatives to inform dietary recommendations and plans. The form covers various aspects of the patient's health and dietary needs, including medical conditions, food preferences, restrictions, and medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by completing each page of the form thoroughly and accurately.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Ensure you have permission from the patient to share their information.
4. Double-check your answers for completeness and accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, required: false): This field is intended to capture the patient's basic information, such as their name and contact details.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, required: false): Describe any medical conditions or health issues the patient is experiencing.
* **Food Preferences** (`food_preferences`, select_one, required: false): Indicate the patient's food preferences, including any likes or dislikes.
* **Food Restrictions** (`food_restrictions`, text, required: false): List any food restrictions or allergies the patient has.
* **Dietary Goals** (`dietary_goals`, text, required: false): Describe the patient's dietary goals, such as weight loss or improved overall health.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, required: false): List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Supplements** (`supplements`, text, required: false): List any supplements the patient is currently taking.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Describe the patient's medical history, including any significant events or conditions.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, text, required: false): This field is not clearly defined; it may be intended to capture the patient's social media presence or online activity.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Provide the patient's contact information, such as phone number and email.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required: false): Describe any allergies or intolerances the patient has.

* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter the patient's date of birth.
