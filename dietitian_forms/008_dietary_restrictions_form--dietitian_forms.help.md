# Dietary Restrictions Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect dietary restrictions and preferences from individuals, which are essential for providing personalized nutrition advice and ensuring safe meal planning.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the medical condition questions (fields 2-3) to inform us about any medical conditions that may affect your diet.
2. Select your diet type (field 5) from the available options to indicate your dietary preferences.
3. List any other dietary restrictions or preferences in field 6.
4. Select any food allergies you have (field 7) from the available options.
5. Choose your meal preferences (field 8) from the available options.
6. List any additional dietary restrictions or preferences in field 10.
7. Provide a note about your medical conditions or allergies (field 11) if needed.
8. Enter your contact email (field 12) and phone number (field 13) so we can contact you for further information or clarification.
9. If you have an emergency contact, provide their name (field 14), phone number (field 15), and relationship (field 16) to ensure we can reach them in case of an emergency.
10. Provide the name and phone number of your healthcare provider (fields 18 and 19) if applicable.
11. Finally, review and submit your form (field 25) to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical condition** (field 2): Please list any medical conditions that may affect your diet, such as diabetes or celiac disease.
* **Medical device 1** (field 3): Please list any medical devices or equipment you use, such as an insulin pump.
* **Diet type** (field 5): Select from the following options: Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free, Dairy-free, Low-carb.
* **Other restrictions** (field 6): List any other dietary restrictions or preferences not listed above.
* **Food allergies** (field 7): Select from the following options: Peanut, Tree nut, Shellfish, Fish, Soy, Egg, Dairy, Wheat, Gluten, Sesame.
* **Meal preferences** (field 8): Select from the following options: Brunch, Breakfast, Dinner.
* **Additional restrictions** (field 10): List any additional dietary restrictions or preferences.
* **Contact email** (field 12): Enter your contact email for us to reach out to you.
* **Contact phone** (field 13): Enter your contact phone number for us to reach out to you.
* **Emergency contact name** (field 14): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency contact phone** (field 15): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency contact relation** (field 16): Select from the following options: Mother, Father, Brother, Sister, Friend, Other.
* **Emergency contact note** (field 17): Provide any additional information about your emergency contact.
* **Healthcare provider** (field 18): Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
* **Healthcare provider phone** (field 19): Enter the phone number of your healthcare provider.
* **Healthcare provider note** (field 20): Provide any additional information about your healthcare provider.
* **Emergency contact relation** (field 21): Provide the relationship of your emergency contact.
* **Healthcare provider relationship** (field 22): Provide the relationship of your healthcare provider.
* **Healthcare provider name** (field 23): Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
* **Healthcare provider license** (field 24): Enter the license of your healthcare provider.
* **Submit** (field 25): Select to submit your form.
