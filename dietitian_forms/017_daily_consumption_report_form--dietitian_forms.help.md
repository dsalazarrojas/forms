# Daily Consumption Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about a user's daily consumption of food and drinks. It is meant to be completed by individuals who need to report their daily intake for health or nutritional monitoring purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of food and drink that you consumed today.
2. Choose the subcategories (if applicable) for the selected category.
3. Enter the date of consumption.
4. Enter the time of consumption.
5. Indicate the amount of consumption (number of units or amount).
6. Add any additional notes about your consumption.
7. Enter your phone number (if you have one).
8. Enter your email address (if you have one).
9. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What did you eat today?** (`page_1`, text, not required): This field is where you should describe the food and drinks you consumed today. Write a brief account of your intake.
* **Date** (`page_2`, date, required): Enter the date of consumption in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Time** (`page_3`, time, required): Enter the time of consumption in HH:MM AM/PM format.
* **Consumption Amount** (`page_4`, number, required): Indicate the number of units or amount consumed.
* **Notes** (`page_5`, note, not required): Write any additional notes or comments about your consumption.
* **Phone** (`page_6`, text, not required): If you want to include your phone number or extension, enter it here.
* **Email** (`page_7`, email, not required): If you want to include your email address, enter it here.
* **Category** (`page_8`, select_one, required): Select the category of food and drink you consumed (e.g., Fresh Fruits, Cooked Food, Snacks, etc.).
* **Subcategory** (`page_9`, select_multiple, not required): If applicable, choose the subcategories for the selected category.
* **Submit** (`page_10`, text, not required): Once you have completed the form, click this button to submit it.
* **Cancel** (`page_11`, text, not required): If you are not ready to submit the form, click this button to cancel and return to the previous page.
* **Back** (`page_12`, text, not required): To go back to the previous page and make changes or corrections.
* **Save** (`page_13`, text, not required): To save the form without submitting it.
* **Reset** (`page_14`, text, not required): To reset the form and start from scratch.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure that your report is helpful for monitoring purposes.
* If you have any trouble completing this form, please contact your healthcare provider for assistance.
* You can save and review this form later if needed.
* You can also submit this form multiple times a day or on a regular schedule as instructed.
