# Nutrition Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients about their dietary habits, health goals, and other relevant details to help dietitians or healthcare professionals provide personalized advice.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully and fill out the required fields.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Do not skip any sections as they are all relevant to the consultation.
4. If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Client Intake
* **Client Intake (1)** (`text`, required: false)
	+ Please provide a brief description of your current diet, including any allergies, intolerances, or food preferences.

### 2. Dietary Habits
* **Dietary Habits (2)** (`select_multiple`, required: true)
	+ Select all the following that apply:
		+ True
		+ False
		+ Undisclosed
	+ This question helps us understand your current eating habits and whether you have any specific dietary restrictions.

### 3. Health Goals
* **Health Goals (3)** (`select_one`, required: false)
	+ Please select the health goal you are currently working towards:
		+ Lose weight
		+ Manage blood sugar
		+ Improve gut health
		+ Other (please specify)
	+ This question helps us understand what you are trying to achieve and what we can help you with.

### 4. Food Intake
* **Food Intake (4)** (`select_one`, required: false)
	+ Please select the type of diet you are currently on:
		+ Omnivore
		+ Vegetarian
		+ Vegan
		+ Raw food
	+ This question helps us understand your dietary preferences and restrictions.

### 5. Supplements
* **Supplements (5)** (`number`, required: false)
	+ Please list the number of supplements you are currently taking.
	+ This question helps us understand your supplement intake and potential interactions.

### 6. Medications
* **Medications (6)** (`text`, required: false)
	+ Please list any medications you are currently taking.
	+ This question helps us understand your medical history and potential interactions.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough when answering the questions.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
* Please do not skip any sections as they are all relevant to the consultation.
* This form is designed to gather information for a health assessment, not for a diagnosis. If you need a diagnosis, please consult a healthcare professional in person.
