# Special Diet Counseling Discharge Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Special Diet Counseling Discharge Form is designed to document the progress and discharge of a patient undergoing diet counseling. Completing this form will help healthcare professionals to:

* Capture essential information about the patient's medical condition
* Document the patient's diet plan and any food restrictions
* Record any medications the patient may be allergic to
* Set a discharge date and time

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the patient's name in the **patient_name** field.
2. Select the type of medical condition the patient has from the **medical_condition** dropdown menu.
3. List any medications the patient is allergic to in the **medication_allergies** field.
4. Enter the patient's diet plan in the **diet_plan** field.
5. Select any food restrictions the patient has from the **food_restrictions** field.
6. Set the discharge date and time in the **discharge_date** and **discharge_time** fields.
7. Enter the name of the dietitian who conducted the counseling in the **dietitian_name** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **special_diet_counseling_discharge_form**: This is the title of the form.
* **patient_name**: Enter the name of the patient.
* **medical_condition**: Select the type of medical condition the patient has:
	+ Idiopathic: A type of medical condition that is not known or unknown to be caused by a specific factor or cause.
	+ Congenital: A medical condition that is present at birth.
	+ Acquired: A medical condition that develops after birth.
* **medication_allergies**: List any medications the patient is allergic to.
* **diet_plan**: Enter the patient's diet plan, including any recommendations or restrictions.
* **food_restrictions**: Select any food restrictions the patient has:
	+ Dairy: Milk, cheese, and other dairy products.
	+ Gluten: A protein found in wheat, barley, and rye.
	+ Nuts: Peanuts, walnuts, and other nuts.
* **discharge_date**: Set the date of discharge.
* **discharge_time**: Set the time of discharge.
* **dietitian_name**: Enter the name of the dietitian who conducted the counseling.

## Tips

* Please fill in all required fields to ensure accurate documentation.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with a healthcare professional.
* Remember to review and update the form regularly to ensure it remains relevant and accurate.
