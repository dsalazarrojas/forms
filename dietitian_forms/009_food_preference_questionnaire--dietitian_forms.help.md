# Food Preference Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Food Preference Questionnaire is designed to collect information about your dietary preferences and needs. This form helps us understand what kind of food you like, dislike, or are allergic to, so we can provide you with accurate and personalized recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please go through each page and answer the questions accordingly. You can type your responses in the text fields, select from the options for the select fields, and choose multiple options for the select multiple fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What are your dietary preferences** (Text, Optional): This is a free-text field where you can describe your dietary preferences and needs. You can type anything you like, from "I prefer vegetarian" to "I have a severe gluten intolerance".
* **Category** (Select One, Optional): Please select one category that best describes your dietary preferences:
	+ Dairy
	+ Gluten-free
	+ Vegan
	+ Vegetarian
* **Do you have any special diets** (Select Multiple, Optional): If you have any special diets, please select the ones that apply to you:
	+ I have diabetes
	+ I have a food allergy
	+ I am a vegetarian
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Date of birth** (Date, Optional): If you'd like to share your date of birth, please type it in this field.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): If you'd like to provide your email address, please type it in this field.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): If you'd like to share your phone number, please type it in this field.
* **Notes** (Note, Optional): This is a free-text field where you can add any additional comments or questions you have.

Note: Please fill out as many or as few fields as you need, depending on your specific needs.
