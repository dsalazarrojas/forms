# Calories Diet Plan Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Calories Diet Plan Form" is designed to gather information about your nutritional needs and preferences. This form will help your dietitian create a personalized diet plan tailored to your requirements. Please answer the questions truthfully and accurately to ensure that your diet plan is effective.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field on the form carefully and completely.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields (marked as required).
3. If you have any dietary restrictions or preferences that are not listed, please mention them in the "Additional Notes" field.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted for meal plans and follow-up information.
* **Target Weight in kg** (`target_weight`, number, optional): If you have a specific weight goal, enter it here. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Daily Calorie Goal** (`daily_calorie_goal`, number, required): Enter your recommended daily calorie intake based on your activity level and other factors.
* **Dietary Preferences** (`dietary_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable dietary preferences that apply to you, such as vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, or dairy-free.
* **Food Allergies or Intolerances** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any food allergies or intolerances you have.
* **Typical Activity Level** (`activity_level`, select_one, required): Choose your typical activity level to help determine your calorie needs.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, text, optional): If you have any medications that may affect your diet, list them here.
* **Meal Preferences or Restrictions** (`meal_preferences`, text, optional): If you have any meal preferences or restrictions, list them here.
* **Additional Notes for Dietitian** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional information that may be relevant to your diet plan.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your dietitian for clarification.
* Make sure to save your progress as you fill out the form, in case you need to return to a previous page.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your dietitian for assistance.
