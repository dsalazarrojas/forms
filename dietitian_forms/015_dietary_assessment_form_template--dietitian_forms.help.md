# Dietary Assessment Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dietary Assessment Form Template is designed to gather information about an individual's overall diet, food intake, and health status. This form helps healthcare professionals assess the dietary habits and nutritional needs of their patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions as accurately as possible. If you're unsure about any of the questions, feel free to ask for clarification.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*) in the field label.
3. If a field is optional, you can leave it blank, but be aware that providing more information can help healthcare professionals make a more comprehensive assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Overall Diet** (`Overall Diet`, required: false)
This field is for you to describe your overall diet, including any dietary preferences or restrictions you may have.

### **Food Intake** (`Food Intake`, required: false)
In this field, please list the foods you eat regularly. You can be as specific as possible, including brand names or types of food.

### **Food Frequency** (`Food Frequency`, required: false)
Here, you can choose how often you eat each food:
* Never
* Rarely
* Occasionally
* Daily

### **Overall Health** (`Overall Health`, required: false)
Please describe your overall health status, including any health conditions or concerns you may have.

### **Medications** (`Medications`, required: false)
List any medications you are currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.

### **Medical Conditions** (`Medical Conditions`, required: false)
Describe any medical conditions you may have, including allergies, sensitivities, or health concerns.

### **Allergies** (`Allergies`, required: false)
List any allergies or sensitivities you may have.

### **Food Allergies** (`Food Allergies`, required: false)
Specify any food allergies or sensitivities you may have.

### **Special Dietary Needs** (`Special Dietary Needs`, required: false)
Describe any special dietary needs or requirements you may have, such as vegan, gluten-free, or lactose intolerance.

### **Dietary Restrictions** (`Dietary Restrictions`, required: false)
List any dietary restrictions you may have, such as food allergies or intolerances.

### **Height** (`Height`, required: false)
Enter your height in centimeters (cm).

### **Weight** (`Weight`, required: false)
Enter your weight in kilograms (kg).

### **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, required: false)
Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

### **Date of Last Contact** (`Date of Last Contact`, required: false)
Enter the date of your last contact with a healthcare professional.

### **Last Visit** (`Last Visit`, required: false)
Enter the date and time of your last visit to a healthcare professional.

### **Email** (`Email`, required: false)
Enter your email address.

### **Phone** (`Phone`, required: false)
Enter your phone number.

### **Address** (`Address`, required: false)
Enter your address, including street number, city, and zip code.
