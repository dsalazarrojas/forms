# Nutrition Care Plan Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nutrition Care Plan Form is designed to help healthcare professionals document a patient's dietary needs, goals, and restrictions to develop a comprehensive care plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's `Patient ID` field with their unique identifier.
2. Enter the patient's `Patient Name` in the corresponding field.
3. In the `Dietary Needs` field, describe the patient's nutritional requirements, such as food allergies or sensitivities.
4. In the `Dietary Goals` field, outline the patient's short-term and long-term dietary goals.
5. List any `Interventions` that the patient is currently undergoing, such as medications or supplements.
6. In the `Dietary Restrictions` field, note any food restrictions or limitations the patient has.
7. Add any relevant `Nutritionist Note` to provide additional information.
8. Enter the `Care Provider` information, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient ID** (`Patient ID`, text, required): Enter the patient's unique identifier number.
* **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Dietary Needs** (`Dietary Needs`, text, optional): Describe the patient's nutritional requirements, such as food allergies or sensitivities.
* **Dietary Goals** (`Dietary Goals`, text, optional): Outline the patient's short-term and long-term dietary goals.
* **Interventions** (`Interventions`, text, optional): List any medications or supplements the patient is currently taking.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`Dietary Restrictions`, text, optional): Note any food restrictions or limitations the patient has.
* **Nutritionist Note** (`Nutritionist Note`, text, optional): Add any relevant information from the nutritionist's note.
* **Care Provider** (`Care Provider`, text, optional): Enter the care provider's name and title, if applicable.
