# Dietetics And Nutrition Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Dietetics And Nutrition Discharge Form is used by healthcare providers to document the discharge process for patients receiving dietetic and nutrition services. This form helps healthcare providers gather essential information about the patient's care and outcome.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's personal details, such as their name, date of birth, weight, height, and contact information.
2. Indicate the patient's allergies, medications, and dietary restrictions.
3. Record the patient's discharge date and follow-up appointment date.
4. Provide the patient's healthcare provider information, including their name, phone number, and email.
5. Document the patient's dietician note, diagnosis, diet plan, nutrition counsel, education, outcome, and family history.
6. Add any discharge comments or additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical record.
* **Weight**: Record the patient's weight in kilograms or pounds.
* **Height**: Record the patient's height in centimeters or inches.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Allergies**: Check if the patient has any allergies.
* **Medications**: Check if the patient is taking any medications.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Check if the patient has any dietary restrictions.
* **Discharge Date**: Enter the date the patient was discharged.
* **Follow-Up Appointment**: Enter the date of the patient's follow-up appointment.
* **Patient Contact**: Enter the patient's contact information.
* **Healthcare Provider**: Check if the patient has a healthcare provider.
* **Dician Note**: Enter any notes from the dietitian.
* **Patient Diagnosis**: Enter the patient's diagnosis.
* **Patient Diet Plan**: Enter the patient's diet plan.
* **Patient Nutrition Counsel**: Enter any nutrition counsel provided to the patient.
* **Patient Education**: Enter any education provided to the patient.
* **Patient Outcome**: Enter the patient's outcome.
* **Patient Follow Up**: Enter the patient's follow-up information.
* **Discharge Comments**: Enter any discharge comments or additional information.
* **Patient Diets**: Enter any additional diets or recommendations.
* **Patient Family History**: Enter the patient's family medical history.
* **Patient Dietary Lifestyle**: Enter any information about the patient's dietary lifestyle.
* **Healthcare Provider Name**: Enter the name of the patient's healthcare provider.
* **Healthcare Provider Phone**: Enter the patient's healthcare provider phone number.
* **Healthcare Provider Email**: Enter the patient's healthcare provider email.

Please note that all fields are optional unless marked as required.
