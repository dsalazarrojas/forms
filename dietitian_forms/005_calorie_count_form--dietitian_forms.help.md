# Calorie Count Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's daily calorie intake for nutritional assessment and guidance purposes. It is intended for healthcare professionals to use in clinical settings to track and analyze a patient's eating habits.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please answer each field in as much detail as possible.
- Use the "Patient Name" field to enter the patient's full name.
- Enter the patient's "Age" in whole numbers (e.g. 30) in the "Age" field.
- Enter the patient's "Current Weight" in pounds in the "Current Weight" field.
- Enter the patient's height in inches in the "Height" field.
- Use the "Date of Calorie Count" field to enter the date of the calorie count.
- For each meal and snack section, please enter the food items and quantities consumed, using the "Description" field.
- Enter the calorie count for each meal and snack in the corresponding "Calories" field.
- For the "Beverages" section, enter the types and quantities of beverages consumed.
- Enter the total daily calories consumed in the "Total Daily Calories" field.
- Set the daily calorie goal for the patient in the "Daily Calorie Goal" field.
- If there are any dietary concerns or restrictions, please enter them in the "Dietary Concerns or Restrictions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name.
* **Age**: Enter the patient's age in whole numbers (e.g. 30).
* **Current Weight (lbs)**: Enter the patient's current weight in pounds.
* **Height (inches)**: Enter the patient's height in inches.
* **Date of Calorie Count**: Enter the date of the calorie count.
* **Breakfast - Food Items and Quantities**: Enter the food items and quantities consumed for breakfast.
* **Breakfast Calories**: Enter the calorie count for breakfast.
* **Morning Snack - Food Items and Quantities**: Enter the food items and quantities consumed for morning snack.
* **Morning Snack Calories**: Enter the calorie count for morning snack.
* **Lunch - Food Items and Quantities**: Enter the food items and quantities consumed for lunch.
* **Lunch Calories**: Enter the calorie count for lunch.
* **Afternoon Snack - Food Items and Quantities**: Enter the food items and quantities consumed for afternoon snack.
* **Afternoon Snack Calories**: Enter the calorie count for afternoon snack.
* **Dinner - Food Items and Quantities**: Enter the food items and quantities consumed for dinner.
* **Dinner Calories**: Enter the calorie count for dinner.
* **Evening Snack - Food Items and Quantities**: Enter the food items and quantities consumed for evening snack.
* **Evening Snack Calories**: Enter the calorie count for evening snack.
* **Beverages - Types and Quantities**: Enter the types and quantities of beverages consumed.
* **Beverage Calories**: Enter the calorie count for beverages.
* **Total Daily Calories**: Enter the total daily calorie count.
* **Daily Calorie Goal**: Enter the daily calorie goal for the patient.
* **Dietary Concerns or Restrictions**: Enter any dietary concerns or restrictions for the patient.
