# Histamine Intolerance Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Histamine Intolerance Quiz is a tool designed to help healthcare professionals diagnose and provide recommendations for managing histamine intolerance. This quiz will guide you through a series of questions to help identify your potential histamine intolerance status and provide personalized advice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions truthfully and accurately.
2. Select the correct options for each question.
3. Submit your answers to get personalized recommendations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What is your age
 * **What is your age**: This question is asking for your age to ensure that the recommendations provided are suitable for your age group.

 * **Histamine levels**: Select one of the four options to indicate your histamine levels.

   - **Very High**: You experience very high histamine levels.

### 7. Histamine symptoms
 * **Histamine symptoms**: Please describe any symptoms you are experiencing that could be related to histamine intolerance.

### 8. Histamine triggers

### 9. Histamine treatment
 * **Histamine treatment**: Select all the treatments that you have tried or are currently using.
