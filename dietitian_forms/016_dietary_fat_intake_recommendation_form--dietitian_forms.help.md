# Dietary Fat Intake Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dietary Fat Intake Recommendation Form is designed to collect information about your dietary fat intake habits. This information will help us provide you with personalized guidance on achieving your dietary goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the instructions and questions carefully.
2. Fill out the required and optional fields as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Intake Details** (`intake_details`, text, optional):
Enter a short description of your current dietary habits.
- **Daily Fat Goals** (`fat_goals`, number, optional):
Enter your current goal for daily fat intake. This will help us understand your short-term goals.
- **Daily Fat Intake** (`daily_intake`, number, optional):
Enter your current daily fat intake. This will help us understand your current consumption habits.
- **Daily Fat Intake Goal** (`daily_intake_goal`, number, optional):
Enter your target daily fat intake. This will help us understand your goals and preferences.
- **Weekly Fat Intake** (`weekly_intake`, number, optional):
Enter your current weekly fat intake. This will help us understand your long-term habits.
- **Weekly Fat Intake Goal** (`daily_intake_goal_weekly`, number, optional):
Enter your target weekly fat intake. This will help us understand your goals and preferences.
- **Monthly Fat Intake** (`monthly_intake`, number, optional):
Enter your current monthly fat intake. This will help us understand your long-term habits.
- **Monthly Fat Intake Goal** (`monthly_intake_goal`, number, optional):
Enter your target monthly fat intake. This will help us understand your goals and preferences.
- **Intake Goals** (`intake_goals`, select_one, optional):
Indicate if you have any specific goals for your fat intake (Yes/No). This will help us understand your motivation for tracking your fat intake.

# Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for further assistance.
* This form is designed to be completed on a computer or mobile device with a browser.
* Please make sure to save or print the form for your reference after completion.
