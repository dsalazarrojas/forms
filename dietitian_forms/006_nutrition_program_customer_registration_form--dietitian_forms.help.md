# Nutrition Program Customer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help new customers register for your nutrition program. It will collect the necessary information to contact and assist them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your **First Name** in the field labeled "First Name". This is where you put your personal first name.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the field labeled "Last Name". This is your family name, or the name given to you after marriage.
3. Enter your **Email** in the field labeled "Email". This is the email address we will use to contact you about your program participation.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the field labeled "Phone". This is your contact number, where we can call or text you.
5. Select your **Customer Type** from the drop-down menu. Please choose if you are a customer or not.
6. Check your preferred **Contact Preference**. You can be contacted via Phone Call, Email, In-person Visit, Online Chat, or Other.
7. Indicate your **Dietary Lifestyle Restrictions**. If you are vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, or have no restrictions, please select accordingly.
8. Describe your **Goals** in the field provided. This is where you can tell us about your expectations and aspirations from the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: A text field where you can enter your personal first name.
* **Last Name**: A text field where you can enter your family name or the name given to you after marriage.
* **Email**: An email field where you can enter your contact email address.
* **Phone**: A text field where you can enter your contact phone number.
* **Customer Type**: A drop-down menu with options for Customer and Non-Customer.
* **Contact Preference**: A multiple selection field where you can choose how you want to be contacted (Phone Call, Email, In-person Visit, Online Chat, or Other).
* **Dietary Lifestyle Restrictions**: A drop-down menu where you can select your dietary restrictions (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free, or None).
* **Goals**: A text field where you can describe your expectations and aspirations from the program.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are not already enrolled in the program.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* We will review and follow up on your registration.
