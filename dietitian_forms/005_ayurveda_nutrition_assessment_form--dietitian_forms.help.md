# Ayurveda Nutrition Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help us better understand your needs and preferences for optimal nutrition. Please answer the questions honestly, and provide as much detail as possible. Your responses will help us create a personalized plan to support your health and wellness goals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **Client Full Name** (required). This is the name by which we will refer to you throughout the assessment process.
2. Enter your **Age** (required). This will help us understand your life stage and potential nutritional needs.
3. Provide your **Email Address** (required). This is how we will contact you with questions, updates, and results.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** (optional). This is useful in case we need to reach out to you directly.
5. Choose your **Ayurvedic Constitution (Dosha)** (optional). This is a concept in Ayurveda that describes your unique biological and psychological makeup.
6. Describe your **Primary Health Concerns** (required). Share your main health issues or concerns that you are currently facing.
7. List your **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (optional). Include any food allergies, intolerances, or preferences you have.
8. Indicate how many **Meals Per Day** you typically eat (required).
9. Specify the approximate **Meal Times** (optional). This will help us understand your eating habits and schedule.
10. Enter your **Daily Water Intake** (optional). This will help us assess your hydration needs.
11. Describe your **Digestion Issues** (optional). If you experience any digestive issues, such as bloating or constipation, please list them.
12. Choose your **Energy Levels Throughout the Day** (optional). Select how you feel at different times.
13. Evaluate your **Sleep Quality** (optional). Describe how well you are sleeping.
14. Assess your **Stress Level** (optional). Choose how stressed you are feeling.
15. Indicate your **Exercise Frequency** (optional). How often do you engage in physical activity.
16. Describe your **Current Diet** (optional). What types of foods do you usually eat?
17. Set your **Nutritional Goals** (optional). What you would like to achieve through this assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name (required)**: Your full name for reference and communication purposes.
* **Age (required)**: Your current age in years.
* **Email Address (required)**: Your contact email for updates and questions.
* **Phone Number (optional)**: Your phone number for direct contact.
* **Ayurvedic Constitution (Dosha) (optional)**: A concept in Ayurveda that describes your unique biological and psychological makeup.
* **Primary Health Concerns (required)**: Your main health issues or concerns.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies (optional)**: Include any food allergies, intolerances, or preferences.
* **Meals Per Day (required)**: How many meals you typically eat per day.
* **Meal Times (optional)**: The approximate time of your meals.
* **Daily Water Intake (optional)**: How much water you drink per day (in ounces).
* **Digestion Issues (optional)**: Any digestive issues you experience, such as bloating or constipation.
* **Energy Levels Throughout the Day (optional)**: How you feel at different times of the day.
* **Sleep Quality (optional)**: How well you sleep at night.
* **Stress Level (optional)**: How stressed you feel at the moment.
* **Exercise Frequency (optional)**: How often you engage in physical activity.
* **Current Diet (optional)**: The types of foods you usually consume.
* **Nutritional Goals (optional)**: What you would like to achieve through this assessment.
