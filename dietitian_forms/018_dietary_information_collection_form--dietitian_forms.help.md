# Dietary Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect dietary information from individuals to aid healthcare professionals in understanding their needs and restrictions. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by choosing your preferred diet type (vegetarian, non-vegetarian, vegan, or other) on **1. Food Preferences**.
2. Answer each **Foods Intake** question (Breakfast, Snacks, Dinner, Dessert) with the relevant information, as each question is required.
3. List any allergies or dietary restrictions in the **Dietary Restrictions** section.
4. Enter any additional comments or notes in the **Note** field.
5. Provide your contact information (email and phone number) in the **Email** and **Phone** fields, respectively.
6. Add any additional comments in the **Additional Comments** field.
7. Specify your dietary preferences in the **Dietary Preferences** field.
8. Record your usual meal intake habits by filling out the **Dietary Intake** questions (Breakfast Intake, Dinner Intake, Meal Timing, Meal Frequency, Total Daily Intake).
9. Complete the form in its entirety and review carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Food Preferences**: Select the type of diet you prefer (vegetarian, non-vegetarian, vegan, or other).
* **Foods Intake (Breakfast)**: Record your typical breakfast intake.
* **Foods Intake (Snacks)**: List any snacks you usually consume.
* **Foods Intake (Dinner)**: Note your usual dinner intake.
* **Foods Intake (Dessert)**: Record any dessert intake.
* **Beverages**: List any beverages you consume.
* **Allergies**: List any known allergies or dietary restrictions.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Select any relevant restrictions (lactose intolerance, gluten-free, low-fat, or others).
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes for healthcare professionals.
* **Email**: Provide your email address for communication.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or discussion points.
* **Dietary Preferences**: Specify your dietary preferences (e.g., lactose-free, gluten-free).
* **Dietary Intake (Breakfast Intake)**: Record your usual breakfast intake time.
* **Dietary Intake (Dinner Intake)**: Note your usual dinner intake time.
* **Dietary Intake (Meal Timing)**: Record your typical meal timing (e.g., 3 meals a day).
* **Dietary Intake (Meal Frequency)**: Specify your usual meal frequency (e.g., 3 meals a day).
* **Total Daily Intake**: Record your daily meal intake total.

By following this guide and completing the form accurately, you will help healthcare professionals understand your dietary needs and provide the best possible care for you.
