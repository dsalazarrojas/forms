## Food Allergy Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Allergy Form is designed to gather information about a patient's food allergies, which may help healthcare professionals to provide personalized care and take necessary precautions during treatments.
## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by filling out the "patient_name" field with your name.
2.  Select the type of food you are allergic to from the "food_allergy" field.
3.  If applicable, describe your allergy in the "allergy_description" field.
4.  If you have any medical conditions related to your allergy, enter them in the "food_allergy_medical_condition" field.
5.  Indicate the severity of your allergy by selecting all that apply from the "allergy_severity" field.
6.  If you have any medication allergies, select all that apply from the "medication_allergy" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **food_allergy_form** (Text, Required: No): This field is a placeholder for the form name. You can ignore it.
* **patient_name** (Text, Required: No): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **food_allergy** (Select One, Required: No): Choose one of the food options to declare your allergy:
  • Dairy
  • Egg
  • Fish
  • Poultry
  • Shellfish
  • Tree Nut
  • Wheat
  • Soy
  • Other
* **allergy_description** (Text, Required: No): If required, describe your allergy in detail here.
* **food_allergy_medical_condition** (Text, Required: No): If applicable, enter any medical conditions related to your allergy.
* **allergy_severity** (Select Multiple, Required: No): Indicate the severity of your allergy by selecting all that apply:
  • Milder
  • Moderate
  • Severe
* **medication_allergy** (Select Multiple, Required: No): If you have any medication allergies, select all that apply:
  • Penicillin
  • Epi-pen
  • Other medication allergy
