# Weight Loss Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Weight Loss Consultation Form is designed to collect information about your health and weight loss goals. This form is intended for individuals seeking guidance on achieving their ideal weight and improving their overall health. Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure the best possible consultation with our dietitians.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (text)
* The field for your First Name is a text input field where you should enter your first name.

### Last Name (text)
* The field for your Last Name is a text input field where you should enter your last name.

### Birth Date (date)
* The Birth Date field is used to collect your birthdate. Please use the format mm/dd/yyyy.

### Age (number)
* The Age field is used to collect your age.

### Height (number)
* The Height field is used to collect your height. Please enter your height in inches.

### Weight (number)
* The Weight field is used to collect your current weight.

### Phone Number (text)
* The Phone Number field is used to collect your contact phone number.

### Medical Conditions (text)
* The Medical Conditions field is used to collect any pre-existing medical conditions you may have.

### Medications (text)
* The Medications field is used to collect any medications you are currently taking.

### Supplements (text)
* The Supplements field is used to collect any supplements you are currently taking.

### Goal Weight (number)
* The Goal Weight field is used to collect your desired weight.

### Ideal Weight (number)
* The Ideal Weight field is used to collect your ideal weight.

### Target Weight (number)
* The Target Weight field is used to collect your target weight.

### Height Feet (number)
* The Height Feet field is used to collect your height in feet.

### Height Inches (number)
* The Height Inches field is used to collect your height in inches.

### Body Fat Percentage (number)
* The Body Fat Percentage field is used to collect your body fat percentage.

### Waist Circumference (number)
* The Waist Circumference field is used to collect your waist measurement in inches.

### Hip Circumference (number)
* The Hip Circumference field is used to collect your hip measurement in inches.

### Hip Width (number)
* The Hip Width field is used to collect your hip width measurement in inches.

### Blood Pressure (text)
* The Blood Pressure field is used to collect your current blood pressure reading.

### Blood Pressure Unit (select_one)
* The Blood Pressure Unit field is used to select the unit of your blood pressure measurement. Please choose between mmHg or mmBpsys.

### Selected Blood Pressure (text)
* The Selected Blood Pressure field is used to collect your blood pressure reading in the selected unit. Please enter your blood pressure reading using the unit you selected in the Blood Pressure Unit field.
