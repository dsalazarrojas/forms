# Eating Habits Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This Eating Habits Questionnaire is designed to gather information about your eating habits, including your meal times, food preferences, and any professional help you may have sought for your eating habits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each page carefully and answer the questions accordingly. You can select one or multiple options from the dropdown menus, and fill in the text boxes as instructed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, required: false): This is a brief introduction page that explains the purpose of the form and provides essential details about what you can expect from it. You can read this page carefully before proceeding.
* **How Often Do You Eat** (`How Often Do You Eat`, `text`, required: false): Please enter how often you eat in a day. This will help in understanding your eating frequency.
* **Food Preferred** (`Food Preferred`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks about your preferred food type. Select one of the options provided: None, Vegetarian, Vegan, or Omnivore.
* **What Food Groups Do You Eat Most Days A Week** (`What Food Groups Do You Eat Most Days A Week`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the food groups that you eat most frequently. Select one or more from the options: None, Fruits, Vegetables, Protein, Grains.
* **What Time Do You Usualy Start Your Meal** (`What Time Do You Usualy Start Your Meal`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time when you usually start your meal. This will help in understanding your meal timing.
* **Do You Usualy Finish Your Meal** (`Do You Usualy Finish Your Meal`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time when you usually finish your meal. This will help in understanding your meal duration.
* **Have You Ever Sought Professional Help For Your Eating Habits** (`Have You Ever Sought Professional Help For Your Eating Habits`, `select_one`, required: false): This question is about whether you have ever sought professional help for your eating habits. Select from the options: True, False, Maybe.
* **How Do You Feel When You Cannot Eat Your Preferred Food** (`How Do You Feel When You Cannot Eat Your Preferred Food`, `note`, required: false): This is a text box where you can describe how you feel when you are unable to eat your preferred food. Be honest and provide as much detail as possible.
