# Home Dietetics Support Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information, medical history, health status, medications, diet and nutrition details, allergies and contraindications, medical conditions, and lab test results from individuals seeking home dietetics support. The form is intended to be a preliminary tool to gather relevant information for further consultation with healthcare professionals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Provide your contact information**: Please fill in your name, phone number, and email address.
2. **Share your medical history**: Describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses or surgeries.
3. **Report your current health status**: Describe your current health status, including any ongoing health issues.
4. **List your medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
5. **Describe your diet and nutrition**: Share details about your typical diet and nutrition habits.
6. **List your allergies and contraindications**: Describe any allergies or conditions that may impact your dietary needs.
7. **Mention any medical conditions**: Share any ongoing medical conditions or concerns.
8. **Attach lab test results**: If available, attach any relevant lab test results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Information
* **Contact Information (name, phone number, email)**: Your name, phone number, and email address will be used to communicate with you about your dietetics support needs.

### Medical History
* **Medical History**: Describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses or surgeries, to help us understand your overall health context.

### Health Status
* **Health Status**: Report your current health status, including any ongoing health issues, to help us understand your current needs.

### Medications
* **Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking, including dosages and frequency.

### Diet and Nutrition
* **Diet and Nutrition**: Describe your typical diet and nutrition habits, including any specific dietary needs or restrictions.

### Allergies and Contraindication
* **Allergies and Contraindication**: List any allergies or conditions that may impact your dietary needs.

### Medical Conditions
* **Medical Conditions**: Share any ongoing medical conditions or concerns that may affect your dietary needs.

### Lab Test Results
* **Lab Test Results**: Attach any relevant lab test results, including blood glucose, blood pressure, or other relevant data.
