# Nutrition And Wellness Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nutrition And Wellness Intake Form is designed to gather information from users to help healthcare professionals or dietitians understand their dietary needs and medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Report your medical history, including any previous medical conditions you've had.
3. Indicate if you are taking any medications or have allergies.
4. Answer questions about your nutrition goals.
5. Report your lifestyle habits.
6. Provide information about any medications that cause allergies or side effects.
