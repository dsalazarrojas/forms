# Food Product Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and evaluation of food products from customers. It is intended for product developers, manufacturers, or quality assurance teams to assess customer satisfaction, preferences, and purchasing habits.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and truthful information about your experience with the product.
2. Choose the correct options for each field based on your actual experience.
3. Provide additional comments if you have any further thoughts or suggestions.
4. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Information** (Text): Enter the name of the food product you are evaluating.
* **Taste** (Number): Choose the number that best describes your taste experience (e.g., 1-10).
* **Color** (Select Multiple): Choose all the colors you associate with the product's appearance.
* **Smell** (Select One): Choose the option that best describes your sense of smell experience (e.g., Pleasant, Strong, Faint, None).
* **Texture** (Text): Enter any notable characteristics you associate with the product's texture.
* **Packaging** (Time): Enter the time it took to open, use, or consume the product (e.g., seconds, minutes, hours).
* **Price** (Number): Enter the actual price you paid for the product.
* **Nutrition** (Select One): Choose the option that best describes the product's nutritional content (e.g., Low, High, Balanced).
* **Overall Satisfaction** (Number): Choose the number that best represents your overall satisfaction with the product (e.g., 1-10).
* **Product Recommendation** (Select Multiple): Choose all the options that apply to whether you would recommend the product (e.g., True, False, Maybe).
* **Rating** (Number): Enter a number that represents your rating of the product (e.g., 1-10).
* **Additional Comments** (Note): Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have for the product.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address for follow-up or further communication.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number for follow-up or further communication.
* **Name** (Text): Enter your name (optional but recommended for feedback analysis).
* **Age** (Number): Enter your age for demographic analysis (optional but recommended for research purposes).
* **Gender** (Select One): Choose the option that best describes your gender.
* **Location** (Text): Enter your location for geographic analysis (optional but recommended for research purposes).
* **Product Purchased** (Select Multiple): Choose all the options that apply to whether you purchased the product (e.g., True, False).
* **Product Wanted** (Select One): Choose the option that best describes why you purchased the product (e.g., True, False).
* **Purchased From** (Select One): Choose the option that best describes where you purchased the product (e.g., Online, Store, Other).
* **Purchase Date** (Date): Enter the date you purchased the product.
* **Purchase Time** (Time): Enter the time you purchased the product.
* **Price Paid** (Number): Enter the price you paid for the product.
* **Total Cost** (Number): Enter the total cost you paid for the product.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by customers to provide feedback and evaluation of food products. It is not intended for other purposes such as order placement or customer support requests.
