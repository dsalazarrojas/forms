# Medical Records Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to grant healthcare providers access to a patient's medical records for a specified period. It ensures that patients and healthcare providers can easily and securely share medical information necessary for care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the patient's name in the **patient_name** field, which should match their official name as listed on their medical records.
2.  Enter the healthcare provider's name in the **healthcare_provider** field, who will be accessing the patient's records.
3.  If necessary, enter the healthcare provider's full name (if different from their common name) in the **healthcare_provider_name** field.
4.  Enter the date when the healthcare provider's access to the records is authorized to begin in the **authorization_date** field.
5.  Enter the date when the healthcare provider's access to the records is set to expire in the **authorized_until** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **patient_name** (`text`, required: false): Enter the patient's official name as listed on their medical records.
*   **healthcare_provider** (`text`, required: false): Enter the healthcare provider's name.
*   **healthcare_provider_name** (`text`, required: false): Enter the full name of the healthcare provider if different from the provider's common name.
*   **authorization_date** (`date`, required: false): Select the date when the healthcare provider's access to the records begins.
*   **authorized_until** (`date`, required: false): Select the date when the healthcare provider's access to the records expires.

## Tips

*   Ensure you have the necessary permissions to access and share medical records.
*   Double-check the dates entered in **authorization_date** and **authorized_until** for accuracy.
*   This form is designed to be used securely and for legitimate purposes. Misuse or unauthorized access to medical records is strictly prohibited.
