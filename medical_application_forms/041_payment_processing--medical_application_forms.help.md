# Payment Processing - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payment Processing form is used to collect payment information from users as part of a medical application process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1.  Select your payment method from the options provided:
   - Visa
   - Mastercard
   - American Express

    This is the first step in processing your payment.
2.  If required, select the Medical Group that corresponds to your application.
3.  Enter your name, if required.
4.  Enter your email address, if required.
5.  Enter your phone number, if required.
6.  Enter the date and time of the appointment or procedure, if required.
7.  Enter any additional notes or comments about your payment.
8.  Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, required/optional): Select your payment method from the options provided. Please choose one of the following options:
    *   Visa
    *   Mastercard
    *   American Express
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Medical Group** (`medical_group`, required): Select the Medical Group that corresponds to your application.
* **Name** (`name`, optional): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of your appointment or procedure in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time** (`time`, optional): Enter the time of your appointment or procedure in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Note** (`note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your payment.
* **Submit** (`submit`, required): Click this button to complete and submit your form.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have selected a valid Medical Group and payment method.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid delays or complications with your application.
* If you are unsure about any field or option, please contact support for clarification.
