# Telemedicine Encounter Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telemedicine Encounter Form is designed to collect relevant information about a patient's telemedicine encounter. This includes their contact information, medical condition, and treatment plans to ensure accurate and efficient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the patient information section, ensuring all required fields are completed and accurate.
2. Select the correct encounter type (e.g., Telemedicine, Office, or Emergency Room).
3. Enter the date and time of the encounter, ensuring the correct date and time are entered.
4. Enter the patient's name and any relevant medical conditions.
5. Add any treatment plans or notes, if applicable.
6. Enter the patient's email and phone number for easy communication.
7. Complete the patient's address and city information for billing and contact purposes.
8. Review and submit the form for final review and validation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Patient Information** (`Patient Name`): 
This field is used to enter the patient's name. Please enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification.

### **Encounter Type** (`Encounter Type`): 
Select the correct type of encounter (e.g., Telemedicine, Office, or Emergency Room). This will help determine the appropriate course of action for the patient's care.

### **Date Of Encounter** (`Date Of Encounter`): 
Enter the date of the encounter, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### **Time Of Encounter** (`Time Of Encounter`): 
Enter the time of the encounter, in the format HH:MM AM/PM.

### **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`): 
Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification.

### **Medical Condition** (`Medical Condition`): 
Describe the patient's medical condition or reason for visit.

### **Treatment Planned** (`Treatment Planned`): 
Enter any treatment plans or instructions for the patient's care.

### **Notes** (`Notes`): 
Add any additional notes or comments about the patient's care.

### **Email** (`Email`): 
Enter the patient's email address for easy communication.

### **Phone** (`Phone`): 
Enter the patient's phone number for communication.

### **Patient Address** (`Patient Address`): 
Enter the patient's address for billing and contact purposes.

### **Patient City** (`Patient City`): 
Enter the patient's city of residence.

### **Patient State** (`Patient State`): 
Enter the patient's state of residence.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check the patient's information for accuracy.
* Use the correct format for date and time entries.
* Enter any relevant medical conditions or treatment plans for the patient's care.
