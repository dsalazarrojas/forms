# Clinical Ethics Research Collaboration Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from applicants who wish to collaborate on a research project that involves ethical considerations. The form is designed to gather details about the project, including institution, research focus, scope, timeline, and more.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name and contact information (Section 1: Applicant Information)
2. Enter your email address and phone number (Section 2: Applicant Information)
3. Select your institution and department (Section 3: Applicant Information)
4. Enter your position/title (Section 3: Applicant Information)
5. Provide a brief overview of your research project (Section 4: Research Project Information)
6. Choose the primary ethics focus areas of your research (Section 4: Research Project Information)
7. Select the research scope (Section 4: Research Project Information)
8. Enter the anticipated start and end dates of your project (Section 5: Project Timeline)
9. Provide information about the study participants, including the estimated number and whether they are vulnerable populations (Section 6: Study Participants)
10. Describe your research methods and approach (Section 7: Research Methods)
11. Assess the risk level to participants (Section 7: Research Methods)
12. Outline the expected benefits to participants and society (Section 8: Benefits and Outcomes)
13. Identify your goals for collaboration (Section 9: Collaboration Interests)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Institution/Organization** (`institution`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your institution or organization.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your department.
* **Position/Title** (`position_title`, `text`, required): Enter your position or title.
* **Project Title** (`project_title`, `text`, required): Enter a brief title for your research project.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, `text`, required): Provide a brief overview of your research project.
* **Primary Ethics Focus Areas** (`ethics_focus`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the primary ethics focus areas that apply to your research project.
* **Research Scope** (`research_scope`, `select_one`, required): Select the scope of your research project (Single institution, Multi-institutional, or International).
* **Anticipated Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the anticipated start date of your project.
* **Anticipated End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the anticipated end date of your project.
* **Study Population** (`participant_population`, `text`, required): Enter the type of population that will be studied.
* **Estimated Number of Participants** (`estimated_participants`, `number`, required): Enter the estimated number of participants in your study.
* **Will study involve vulnerable populations?** (`vulnerable_populations`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether your study will involve vulnerable populations.
* **Research Methodology** (`research_methods`, `text`, required): Describe your research methodology and approach.
* **Risk Level to Participants** (`risk_level`, `select_one`, required): Assess the risk level to participants (Minimal, Low, Moderate, or High).
* **Expected Benefits to Participants and Society** (`expected_benefits`, `text`, required): Outline the expected benefits to participants and society.
* **Goals for Collaboration** (`collaboration_goals`, `text`, required): Identify your goals for collaboration.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, please reach out to the relevant authorities for clarification.
