# Free Medication Support Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help patients submit a request for free medication support. It gathers relevant information from patients, which will be reviewed by our medical team to assess eligibility for support. This form is intended for patients who cannot afford their medications or need assistance with their medication regimen. 

Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that we can provide the best possible support for your needs. 

## How To Complete This Form
Complete each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **medication_form_data** (Text, Optional): Use this field to provide any additional information about your medication needs. This could be details about your medical condition, dosage, or current treatment plan.
* **Patient Name** (Text, Optional): Please fill out your complete name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Contact Number** (Text, Optional): Provide your contact number so we can get in touch with you regarding your application.
* **Email Address** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address so we can send you important updates about your application.
* **Medication Needed** (Select Multiple, Optional): Check "Yes" if you are currently in need of free medication or "No" if you are not.
* **Medication Name** (Select One, Optional): Check "Yes" if you have a medication currently prescribed by your healthcare provider or "No" if you do not.
* **Reason for Request** (Text, Optional): Briefly explain why you are requesting free medication support.
* **Healthcare Provider** (Text, Optional): If you have a healthcare provider, please provide their name and contact information for verification purposes.
* **Doctor Name** (Text, Optional): If you have a doctor who prescribed your medication, please provide their name for verification purposes.

## Tips
* Please be honest about your medication needs to ensure that we can provide the right level of support.
* Double-check your contact information to ensure that we can get in touch with you efficiently.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us and we will guide you through the process.
