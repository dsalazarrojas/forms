# Telehealth Psychotherapy Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect your contact information and consent for telehealth services. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth and efficient experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form with your basic contact information, including:
    * First Name
    * Last Name
    * Date of Birth
    * Contact Email
    * Contact Phone Number
    * Address
    * City
    * State
    * ZIP
2.  If you have an emergency contact, please provide their name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your full first name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Last Name**: Enter your full last name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Contact Email**: Enter your active email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Contact Phone Number**: Enter your active phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Address**: Enter your mailing address where you can be contacted.
* **City**: Enter your city or town where you live.
* **State**: Enter your state where you live.
* **ZIP**: Enter your mailing zip code.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person we can contact in case of an emergency.

## Tips
* Ensure you enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
* If you need help filling out the form, please contact us.
