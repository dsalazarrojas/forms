# CT Scan Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CT Scan Referral Form is used to request a CT scan for a patient. It collects information about the patient, the clinical history and indications for the scan, and the referring practitioner's details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the patient's full name in the **Patient Full Name** field.
* Enter the patient's date of birth in the **Date of Birth** field.
* Enter the patient's phone number in the **Patient Phone Number** field.
* Enter the patient's provisional diagnosis and clinical indication for the scan in the **Provisional Diagnosis and Clinical Indication** field.
* Select the body area to be scanned in the **Body Area to be Scanned** field.
* Select the priority of the referral in the **Priority** field.
* Indicate if contrast is required in the **Is Contrast Requested** field.
* If applicable, specify any known allergies and provide recent creatinine or GFR results in the **Known Allergies** field and **Recent Creatinine or GFR Results** field respectively.
* If the patient is pregnant or possibly pregnant, select **True** in the **Is the patient pregnant or possibly pregnant** field.
* Enter the referring physician's name and phone number in the **Referring Physician Name** and **Physician Contact Phone** fields respectively.
* Enter the clinic or hospital name in the **Clinic or Hospital Name** field.
* Enter the date of referral in the **Date of Referral** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name**: Enter the patient's full name, as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format **MM/DD/YYYY**.
* **Patient Phone Number**: Enter the patient's phone number, if known.
* **Provisional Diagnosis and Clinical Indication**: Specify the provisional diagnosis and clinical indication for the scan. This should include the patient's reason for requesting a CT scan.
* **Body Area to be Scanned**: Select the area of the body to be scanned from the options provided.
* **Priority**: Select the priority of the referral from the options provided (Routine, Urgent - within 48 hours, Critical/Emergency).
* **Is Contrast Requested**: Indicate if contrast is required for the scan.
* **Known Allergies**: Specify any known allergies, if applicable.
* **Recent Creatinine or GFR Results**: If applicable, provide recent creatinine or GFR results.
* **Is the patient pregnant or possibly pregnant**: Select **True** if the patient is pregnant or possibly pregnant.
* **Referring Physician Name**: Enter the referring physician's name.
* **Physician Contact Phone**: Enter the referring physician's phone number.
* **Clinic or Hospital Name**: Enter the name of the clinic or hospital where the patient is being referred from.
* **Date of Referral**: Enter the date of referral in the format **MM/DD/YYYY**.
