# mri_screening_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a patient's medical condition and history for MRI screening purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's basic information in the "Patient Information" section.
2. Fill in the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information in the subsequent sections.
3. Provide details about the patient's medical condition, allergies, medications, medical devices, and medical procedures in the respective sections.
4. Specify the type and location of the MRI examination in the "MRI Information" section.
5. Enter the date and time of the MRI scan in the "Date of MRI" section.
6. Provide the patient's signature and medical history, emergency contact information, and medical team contact details in the remaining sections.
7. Review and submit the form once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's basic information.
* **Patients Name**: Enter the patient's name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Contact Information**: Enter the patient's contact information.
* **Patients Contact**: Enter the patient's contact information.
* **Medical Condition**: Describe the patient's medical condition.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies the patient may have.
* **Medications**: List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Medical Devices**: List any medical devices the patient uses.
* **Medical Procedures**: List any medical procedures the patient has undergone.
* **MRI Information**: Specify the type and location of the MRI examination.
* **MRI Type**: Specify if the MRI examination is a Yes or No.
* **MRI Location**: Specify if the MRI examination is located at Yes or No.
* **Date of MRI**: Enter the date and time of the MRI scan.
* **MRI Scan Time**: Enter the time of the MRI scan.
* **Signature**: Enter the patient's signature.
* **Medical History**: Describe the patient's medical history.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the emergency contact's name and contact information.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Specify the relationship between the patient and the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the emergency contact's email address.
* **Patient Notes**: Enter any additional information about the patient.
* **Medical Team**: Enter the medical team's name and contact information.
* **Medical Team Phone**: Enter the medical team's phone number.
* **Medical Team Email**: Enter the medical team's email address.

Note: Some fields are optional and can be left blank. However, ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and truthfully to avoid any issues during the MRI screening.
