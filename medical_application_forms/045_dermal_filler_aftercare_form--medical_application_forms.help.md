# Dermal Filler Aftercare Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the dermal filler aftercare of patients who have received dermal filler injections. It's used by healthcare professionals to track and record this information for future reference and to monitor the patient's recovery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the dermal filler injection date in the date field.
2. Select the injection site(s) from the dropdown list.
3. Enter the time of the injection.
4. Specify the duration of the injection.
5. Choose the aftercare products used from the dropdown list.
6. Write a brief description of your aftercare instructions.
7. Select any adverse effects experienced from the dropdown list.
8. Provide contact information for follow-up purposes.
9. Select the follow-up method (phone, email, or in-office).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dermal Filler Injection Date** (Date, required): Enter the date of the dermal filler injection.
* **Dermal Filler Injection Site** (Select Multiple, required): Select all relevant areas where dermal filler injections were administered, such as Face, Lips, Hands, or Feet.
* **Dermal Filler Injection Time** (Time, optional): Enter the time of the injection.
* **Dermal Filler Injection Duration** (Number, optional): Enter the duration of the injection.
* **Dermal Filler Aftercare Products Used** (Select One, required): Choose the aftercare products used, such as Moisturizer, Sunscreen, or Antioxidant.
* **Dermal Filler Aftercare Instructions** (Text, required): Write a brief description of your aftercare instructions.
* **Dermal Filler Aftercare Adverse Effects** (Select Multiple, optional): Select any adverse effects experienced, such as Redness, Swelling, Blisters, or Pain.
* **Contact Information** (Text, optional): Provide any contact information for follow-up purposes.
* **Follow Up** (Select Multiple, required): Select a follow-up method, such as Phone, Email, or In Office.
