# Dengue Medical Certificate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dengue Medical Certificate Form is used to document and verify the medical status of an individual who has been diagnosed with dengue fever. This form is typically completed by medical professionals to provide essential information about the patient's medical history, symptoms, and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field (id: 1).
3. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Number" and "Contact Email" fields (ids: 2 and 3), if applicable.
4. Describe your medical history in the "Medical History" field (id: 4).
5. List your symptoms in the "Symptoms" field (id: 5).
6. Select the type of medical certificate from the options provided in the "Medical Certificate Type" field (id: 6).
7. Enter the start date of your medical certification in the "Start Date" field (id: 7), if applicable.
8. Enter the end date of your medical certification in the "End Date" field (id: 8).
9. Enter the medical certificate number issued to you in the "Medical Certificate Number" field (id: 9).
10. Enter the date of the medical certificate in the "Medical Certificate Date" field (id: 10).
11. Select the facility where the medical certificate was issued from the options provided in the "Issuing Facility" field (id: 11).
12. Provide a note for your signature in the "Signature" field (id: 12), if applicable.
13. Sign your name in the "Signature" field (id: 12), if required.
14. Enter the date of your signature in the "Signature Date" field (id: 13).
15. Select the facility where you signed the form from the options provided in the "Signature Facility" field (id: 14).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (id: 1, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Number** (id: 2, number, optional): Provide your contact information, including phone numbers or other relevant contact details.
* **Contact Email** (id: 3, email, optional): Enter your email address, if applicable.
* **Medical History** (id: 4, text, required): Describe your medical history in this field. Be as detailed as possible, including any previous medical conditions or allergies.
* **Symptoms** (id: 5, text, required): List your current symptoms, including any pain or discomfort experienced.
* **Medical Certificate Type** (id: 6, select_one, required): Select the type of medical certificate you are applying for. This will typically be one of the following options:
	+ dengue fever
	+ sick leave
	+ school absence
	+ insurance documentation
* **Start Date** (id: 7, date, optional): Enter the start date of your medical certification, if applicable.
* **End Date** (id: 8, date, required): Enter the end date of your medical certification.
* **Medical Certificate Number** (id: 9, number, required): Enter the number issued to you by the issuing facility.
* **Medical Certificate Date** (id: 10, date, required): Enter the date of your medical certification.
* **Issuing Facility** (id: 11, select_one, required): Select the facility where you received your medical certificate from the options provided.
* **Signature** (id: 12, note, optional): Provide a note for your signature, if required.
* **Signature Date** (id: 13, date, required): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Signature Facility** (id: 14, select_one, required): Select the facility where you signed the form from the options provided.
