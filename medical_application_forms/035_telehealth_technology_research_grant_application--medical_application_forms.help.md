# telehealth-technology-research-grant-application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for medical institutions or organizations to apply for telehealth technology research grants. It gathers information about applicants, projects, funding, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form completely, including all required fields (if any).
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* Ensure that all information is accurate and up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant**: Enter your name as the representative of the institution or organization.
* **Project**: Describe your telehealth technology research project in a few sentences.
* **Funding**: Provide details about the funding you are seeking.
* **Organization**: Enter the name of your institution or organization.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Address**: Enter your institution's or organization's address.
* **Funding Type**: Select the type of funding you are applying for (University, Hospital, Nonprofit).
* **Funding Duration**: Enter the duration of the funding period.
* **Funding Amount**: Enter the amount of funding you are seeking.
* **Output Format**: Select the format in which you would like to receive the grant outcome (Excel, CSV, PDF).
* **Submission Status**: Select the status of your submission (Draft, Submit).
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the application.
* **Submitted On**: Enter the date of submission.
* **Submitted Time**: Enter the time of submission.
* **Review Status**: Select the status of your review (Draft, Reviewing, Approved).
* **Review By**: Enter the name of the reviewer.
 
Note: The form fields marked as required will be indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the field labels.
