# Medication Record Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Medication Record Form is designed to document and track medication records for healthcare professionals and patients. This form helps to record accurate and reliable information about medications, ensuring that medical staff and practitioners can make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the medication name in the designated field.
2. Indicate how often you took the medication (e.g., once a day, twice a day, etc.).
3. Provide the start date when you first started taking the medication.
4. If applicable, enter the end date when you stopped taking the medication.
5. Enter the dosage instructions for the medication.
6. Describe the medication schedule, including how often it was taken and at what times.
7. Briefly explain the reason you were prescribed the medication.
8. Add any additional notes or comments about the medication.
9. Enter the name of the prescribing physician.
10. Optionally, enter the physician's title or position.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medication Name** (`medication_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the medication you are taking.
* **Frequency Filled** (`frequency_filled`, text, optional): Indicate how often you took the medication (e.g., once a day, twice a day, etc.).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when you first started taking the medication.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the date when you stopped taking the medication.
* **Dose** (`dose`, text, optional): Enter the dosage instructions for the medication (e.g., 1 tablet twice a day).
* **Medication Schedule** (`medication_schedule`, text, optional): Describe the medication schedule, including how often it was taken and at what times.
* **Reason Given** (`reason_given`, text, optional): Briefly explain the reason you were prescribed the medication.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the medication.
* **Physician's Name** (`physician_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the prescribing physician.
* **Physician's Title** (`physician_title`, text, optional): Optionally enter the physician's title or position.</thinking>
