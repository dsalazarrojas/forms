# Endodontist Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Endodontist Referral Form is used to gather information from the referring dentist to facilitate communication between the endodontist and the patient's treating dentist. The form is designed to provide the endodontist with the necessary information to assess the patient's condition and develop an appropriate treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information in the **Patient Information** section.
2. Provide details about the patient's condition in the **Patient Condition** section.
3. Specify the treatment needed for the patient in the **Treatment Needed** section.
4. Describe the recommended treatment plan for the patient in the **Treatment Plan** section.
5. Select the preferred dentist for the patient in the **Dentist Information** section.
6. Enter the date and time needed for the treatment in the **Date Needed** and **Time Needed** sections.
7. Enter the patient's email and phone number in the **Email** and **Phone** sections, respectively.
8. Add any additional comments or information about the patient in the **Additional Comments** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant information about the patient.
* **Referring Dentist Information**: Enter the name and contact information of the referring dentist.
* **Patient Condition**: Select the patient's condition from the available options (Cavitation, Abscess, Fracture, or Other).
* **Treatment Needed**: Select all relevant treatments needed for the patient (root canal, crown, filling, or Other).
* **Treatment Plan**: Describe the recommended treatment plan for the patient, including any specific procedures or medications that may be required.
* **Dentist Information**: Select the preferred dentist for the patient from the available options (Dr. Smith, Dr. Johnson, or Dr. Lee).
* **Date Needed**: Enter the date when the treatment is needed.
* **Time Needed**: Enter the time when the treatment is needed.
* **Email**: Enter the patient's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or information about the patient that may be helpful for the endodontist.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter all required information to ensure the endodontist has enough information to make an informed decision about the patient's treatment.
* Double-check the patient's contact information to ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* Be as specific as possible when describing the patient's condition and treatment plan to help the endodontist provide the best possible care.
