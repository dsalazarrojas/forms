# Healthcare Workforce Resilience Grant Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Healthcare Workforce Resilience Grant Application form is designed to gather information from healthcare organizations to support their recovery and growth. This form helps applicants to present their project details, leadership, and funding requests in a structured and clear manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Fill out the "Project Details" field with a brief description of your project and its objectives.
2.  Enter the project lead's contact information, including their name, phone number, and email address.
3.  Confirm the project lead's job title and name of the organization.
4.  Enter the name of the organization or institution where the project is located.
5.  Describe the funding request and specify the amount of funding required.
6.  Enter the amount of funding requested in the "Funding Amount" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details** (Text): This field is used to provide a brief description of your project and its objectives. Please enter a concise and clear description of your project.
* **projectLead** (Text): This field is used to provide the contact information of the project lead, including their name. Please enter their name in this field.
* **leadPhone** (Text): This field is used to enter the phone number of the project lead. Please enter their phone number in this format: XXX-XXX-XXXX.
* **leadEmail** (Email): This field is used to enter the email address of the project lead. Please enter their email address in the correct format: name@domain.com.
* **leadJobTitle** (Text): This field is used to confirm the project lead's job title. Please enter their job title in this field.
* **leadOrganization** (Text): This field is used to enter the name of the organization or institution where the project is located. Please enter the name of the organization in this field.
* **fundingRequest** (Text): This field is used to describe the funding request. Please enter a brief description of the funding request in this field.
* **fundingAmount** (Number): This field is used to enter the amount of funding requested. Please enter the amount of funding requested in this field.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely to avoid any delays or rejections.
* Please enter the correct format for the phone number and email address.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for support.
