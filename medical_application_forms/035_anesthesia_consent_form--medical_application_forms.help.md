# Anesthesia Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to obtain informed consent from patients (or their owners in the case of veterinary procedures) for anesthesia administration. It ensures that patients (or their owners) understand the risks and complications associated with anesthesia and have authorized emergency treatment in case of any complications.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the full name of the patient (or owner if the procedure is for an animal).
2. Select the type of patient (Human or Veterinary).
3. Enter the description of the procedure to be performed.
4. Select all potential risks associated with the procedure that you understand.
5. Choose whether you authorize all necessary measures in case of emergencies or have specific limitations.
6. If you have any limitations, enter a brief description.
7. Confirm that all questions regarding anesthesia have been answered.
8. Select whether you agree to the terms of the consent.
9. Date and sign the form to acknowledge your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name of Patient (or Owner if Applicable)** (`patient_or_owner_name`, Text, Required): Enter the full name of the patient or owner of the animal.
* **Patient Type** (`patient_type`, Select One, Required): Select whether the patient is human or an animal.
* **Scheduled Surgical or Diagnostic Procedure** (`scheduled_procedure`, Text, Required): Enter a brief description of the procedure to be performed.
* **I understand that the administration of anesthesia involves certain risks and possible complications.** (`risk_acknowledgement`, Note, Required): This is a statement explaining the risks associated with anesthesia administration.
* **Acknowledged Potential Risks** (`acknowledged_risks`, Select Multiple, Required): Select all potential risks that you understand.
* **Authorization for Emergency Treatment** (`emergency_treatment_authorization`, Select One, Required): Choose whether you authorize all necessary measures in case of emergencies or have specific limitations.
* **Specific Emergency Limitations (If Any)** (`emergency_limitations_if_any`, Text, Required): If you have limitations, enter a brief description.
* **Have all your questions regarding anesthesia been answered?** (`questions_answered`, Select One, Required): Confirm that all questions regarding anesthesia have been answered.
* **Consent Acknowledgment** (`signature_of_consent`, Select One, Required): Choose whether you agree to the terms of the consent.
* **Date of Signature** (`date_of_signature`, Date, Required): Date and sign the form to acknowledge your agreement.
* **Witness Name (If Required)** (`witness_name_if_required`, Text, Required): If required, enter the name of the witness.
