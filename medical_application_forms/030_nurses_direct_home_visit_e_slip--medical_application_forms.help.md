# Nurses Direct Home Visit E Slip - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form, "Nurses Direct Home Visit E Slip", is used to collect information from patients who require direct visits from nurses. The form is designed to be filled out by patients or their representatives to provide essential details about the patient's visit, including contact information, medical condition, and scheduling information. This form helps healthcare providers to prepare for the visit and ensure a smooth experience for both the patient and the nursing team.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's first and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Fill in the date of birth of the patient.
3. Provide the patient's address and post code.
4. Enter the patient's email address.
5. Enter the patient's phone number.
6. Describe the patient's medical condition.
7. Enter the reason for the visit.
8. Indicate the number of visits required.
9. Specify the duration of each visit.
10. Enter the start and end dates for the visits.
11. Enter the start and end times for each visit.
12. Indicate the number of visits desired.
13. Select the medical team involved in the visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the patient's date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YY).
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the patient's address.
* **Post Code** (`post_code`, text, optional): Enter the patient's post code.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): Describe the patient's medical condition.
* **Reason for Visit** (`reason_for_visit_prophets`, text, optional): Enter the reason for the patient's visit.
* **Number of Visits** (`number_ofistency_visits`, number, optional): Indicate the number of visits required.
* **Duration of Visit** (`duration_of_visit`, number, optional): Specify the duration of each visit.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date for the visit.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date for the visit.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time for the visit.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time for the visit.
* **Number of Visits 2** (`number_of_visits_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select the number of visits required (1, 2, or 3).
* **Medical Team** (`medical_team`, select_multiple, optional): Select the medical team involved in the visit (1, 2, or 3).

## Tips

* Please ensure that the patient's information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with the patient or their representative.
* This form is designed to facilitate communication between the patient and the nursing team. Please ensure that you fill it out carefully and completely.
