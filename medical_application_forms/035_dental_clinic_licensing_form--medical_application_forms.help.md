# Dental Clinic Licensing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for dental clinics to obtain a license to practice in a particular region.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your clinic's address in the "Clinic Address" field.
2. Enter your clinic's phone number in the "Clinic Phone" field.
3. Enter your clinic's email address in the "Clinic Email" field.
4. Select whether your clinic has a license ("Yes" or "No") in the "Clinic License Type" field.
5. If your clinic has a license, enter its license number in the "Clinic License Number" field.
6. Enter the expiration date of your clinic's license in the "Clinic License Expiration Date" field.
7. Enter the name of the supervising dentist in the "Dentist Name" field.
8. Enter the license number of the supervising dentist in the "Dentist License Number" field.
9. Enter the expiration date of the supervising dentist's license in the "Dentist License Expiration Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Clinic Address** (text): Enter your clinic's address.
* **Clinic Phone** (text): Enter your clinic's phone number.
* **Clinic Email** (email): Enter your clinic's email address.
* **Clinic License Type** (select one, "Yes" or "No"): Select whether your clinic has a license.
* **Clinic License Number** (number): Enter your clinic's license number if you have a license.
* **Clinic License Expiration Date** (date): Enter the expiration date of your clinic's license.
* **Dentist Name** (text): Enter the name of the supervising dentist.
* **Dentist License Number** (number): Enter the license number of the supervising dentist.
* **Dentist License Expiration Date** (date): Enter the expiration date of the supervising dentist's license.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for your clinic and its staff.
* Double-check your clinic's license status and expiration date to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact our support team for assistance.
