# Medical Research Collaboration Proposal Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Medical Research Collaboration Proposal Application form is designed to guide users in creating a well-structured and comprehensive proposal for medical research collaborations. The form is intended to collect essential information about the user, proposal, research team, research institution, and any supporting documents.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Step 1: Gather Information**
	* Collect all the necessary information required for the form, including personal details, proposal overview, research team, research institution, and any supporting documents.
2. **Step 2: Fill Out the Form**
	* Start by filling out the "User Information" section, providing a brief description of yourself.
	* Next, move on to the "Proposal Information" section and enter a concise overview of your proposal and its objectives.
	* Continue by filling out the "Research Team" section, providing details about the team members involved in the proposal.
	* Proceed to the "Research Institution" section and enter the institution where the research will take place.
	* Finally, upload any additional supporting documents in the "Supporting Document" section.
3. **Step 3: Review and Submit**
	* Take a moment to review your filled-out form for completeness and accuracy.
	* Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
	* Make any necessary adjustments before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **User Information**
* **Label:** User Information
* **Type:** Text
* **Required:** False
* **Explanation:** A brief description of the user submitting the proposal. Enter your name, institution, and contact information.

### 2. **Proposal Information**
* **Label:** Proposal Information
* **Type:** Text
* **Required:** False
* **Explanation:** A concise overview of the proposal and its objectives. Describe the research goals, objectives, and outcomes.

### 3. **Research Team**
* **Label:** Research Team
* **Type:** Text
* **Required:** False
* **Explanation:** Details about the research team involved in the proposal. Include team members' names, affiliations, and contact information.

### 4. **Research Institution**
* **Label:** Research Institution
* **Type:** Text
* **Required:** False
* **Explanation:** Information about the institution where the research will take place. Provide the institution's name, address, and contact information.

### 5. **Supporting Document**
* **Label:** Supporting Document
* **Type:** Text
* **Required:** False
* **Explanation:** Any additional document that supports the proposal, such as a CV, publication, or letter of support.

### 6. **Review**
* **Label:** Review
* **Type:** Text
* **Required:** False
* **Explanation:** A field for any additional comments or feedback from the reviewer.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are filled out to avoid any issues with submission.
* Use clear and concise language throughout the form.
* Proofread your filled-out form for errors and completeness.
* Contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) if you have any questions or concerns about the form.
