# Medical Staff Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Medical Staff Application Form is used to collect information from medical staff applicants. This form helps us to assess their qualifications, experience, and availability for shifts. Please complete the form carefully, ensuring that all required fields are filled out accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Submit" button at the end of the form to save your progress.
2. Fill out each field according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Staff Application** (`1`, `note`, required/optional): This is a note field for the application form, but it doesn't have a specific field label or hint to indicate its purpose. It might be for a description of the applicant's qualifications, experience, or any other relevant information. Please write a brief introduction or description of your qualifications and experience as a medical staff applicant.
* **Name** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`5`, `date`, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Medical License** (`6`, `select_one`, required/optional): Check if you have a medical license (True) or not (False).
* **Medical Board Certification** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): List any medical board certifications you hold (True, False).
* **Medical Degree** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select your medical degree (Doctor, Pharmacist, Other).
* **Medical Specialty** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your medical specialty or specialty area of practice.
* **Years of Experience** (`10`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of years you have worked in the medical field.
* **Available to Work** (`11`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date when you are available to work.
* **Medical Languages** (`12`, `text`, required/optional): List the languages you are proficient in, particularly languages related to medical terminology.
* **Medical Certifications** (`13`, `text`, required/optional): List any medical certifications or licenses you hold.
* **Medical Equipment** (`14`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check if you have experience with medical equipment (True, False).
* **Medical Certification Issuer** (`15`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the issuer of your medical certification or license.
* **Medical Certification Date** (`16`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of your medical certification or license.
* **Medical Certification Number** (`17`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the number of your medical certification or license.
* **Medical License Number** (`18`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your medical license number.
* **Available Shifts** (`19`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the shifts you are available to work (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Available Holidays** (`20`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check if you are available to work on holidays (True, False).
* **Available Weekends** (`21`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check if you are available to work on weekends (True, False).
* **Emergency Contact** (`22`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the contact information for an emergency contact person.
* **Contact Person** (`23`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Number** (`24`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the contact phone number of the contact person.
* **Additional Comments** (`25`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your application.
