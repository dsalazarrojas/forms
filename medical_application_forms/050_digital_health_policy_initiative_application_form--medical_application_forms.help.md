# digital_health_policy_initiative_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for individuals to apply for the Digital Health Policy Initiative, a program aimed at promoting digital health and well-being. The form allows applicants to provide their personal information, policy proposals, and other relevant details required for the application process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the application form with your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select your policy proposals from the list of options provided.
3. Provide your date of birth and time of application.
4. Indicate whether you have medical certifications or not.
5. Add any additional comments about your application.
6. Upload any required files (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form** (Application Form, text, optional): This is the main form where you can provide your personal details.
* **Policy Proposals** (Policy Proposals, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more policy proposals that align with your application.
	+ Select all proposals that apply to you.
* **Name** (name, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time of Application** (Time of Application, time, optional): Enter the time you submitted this application.
* **Medical Certifications** (Medical Certifications, select_one, optional): Indicate if you have medical certifications or not.
* **Not Certified**: Select this option if you do not have medical certifications.
* **Not Provided**: Select this option if you do not provide medical certifications information.
	+ Select one option that best describes your situation.
* **Comments** (Comments, note, optional): Provide any additional comments about your application.
* **File Upload** (File Upload, text, optional): Upload any required files for your application.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately to ensure a complete and successful application.
* If you have any medical certifications, please provide proof of them as required.
* If you have any additional documents to support your application, upload them as a file.
* Review your application carefully before submitting it for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, contact the initiative administrators for support.
