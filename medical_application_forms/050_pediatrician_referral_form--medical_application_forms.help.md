# Pediatrician Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect relevant information from a patient's pediatrician regarding their medical history, current health status, and any necessary medical procedures or referrals. The form helps to ensure that the pediatrician has the necessary information to provide accurate and effective care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you understand each section.
2. Complete all required fields (indicated by the * symbol).
3. Enter the patient's personal information in the **Patient Information** section, including their name, contact details, and medical history.
4. Select the relevant medical history and conditions that apply to the patient.
5. List the patient's current medications, allergies, and medical referrals as necessary.
6. Enter the patient's address, contact information, and any relevant doctor's contact information.
7. Provide any necessary doctor's notes or comments.
8. Complete the date and time of the consultation.
9. Enter any relevant medical condition or diagnosis.
10. Sign and date the form with your doctor's signature and ID number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`): Describe the patient's past medical history, including any previous illnesses, surgeries, or treatments.
* **Medications** (`medications`): Select all medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`): Select any allergies the patient has.
* **Medical Referrals** (`medical_referrals`): Select any medical referrals the patient requires.
* **Patient Contact** (`patient_contact`): Enter the patient's email address and/or phone number.
* **Doctor Information** (`doctor_info`): Enter the doctor's contact information and any relevant notes or comments.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`): Enter the patient's address.
* **Doctor Phone** (`doctor_phone`): Enter the doctor's phone number.
* **Doctor Email** (`doctor_email`): Enter the doctor's email address.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`): This field is duplicated, please enter the patient's address here.
* **Doctor Address** (`doctor_address`): Enter the doctor's address.
* **Doctor Notes** (`doctor_notes`): Enter any relevant notes or comments from the doctor.
* **Patient Notes** (`patient_notes`): Enter any notes or comments from the patient.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date of the consultation.
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time of the consultation.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`): Describe the patient's current medical condition or diagnosis.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`): Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses or treatments.
* **Medical Procedure** (`medical_procedure`): Describe any medical procedures or treatments the patient has undergone.
* **Medical Specialties** (`medical_specialties`): List any relevant medical specialties or areas of expertise.
* **Medical Diagnosis** (`medical_diagnosis`): Enter the patient's current medical diagnosis.
* **Doctor Signature** (`doctor_signature`): Sign the form with your doctor's signature.
* **Doctor License** (`doctor_license`): Enter your doctor's license number.
* **Doctor ID** (`doctor_id`): Enter your doctor's ID number.

Note: Please review each field carefully and ensure the correct information is entered. If a field is marked as required, it is essential to complete it to ensure accurate and effective care.
