# medical_age_certificate_submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The medical age certificate submission form is designed to collect demographic information and other necessary details for the applicant's medical age certification process.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To complete this form, the applicant's healthcare provider should ensure they are at least 18 years old.
2.  The healthcare provider should fill in the required fields accurately and completely.
3.  Double-check the provided information for any errors or inconsistencies.
4.  Review and agree on the submission type (Age Verification or Other) before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Applicant** (`1`, `text`, required: `false`): The name of the applicant.
* **Date Of Birth** (`2`, `date`, required: `false`): The date of birth of the applicant.
* **Medical Certification** (`3`, `text`, required: `false`): A medical certification number or any other identifier related to the applicant's medical certification.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`4`, `text`, required: `false`): The name and contact information of the healthcare provider submitting the form.
* **Signature** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): A confirmation that the healthcare provider has verified the information provided.
* **Medical Certificate Number** (`6`, `text`, required: `false`): The number of the medical certificate issued to the applicant.
* **Submission Type** (`7`, `select_one`, required: `false`): The type of submission (Age Verification or Other).
* **Notes** (`8`, `note`, required: `false`): Any additional comments or explanations provided by the healthcare provider.
* **Medical Age Certificate Submission Date** (`9`, `time`, required: `false`): The date when the medical age certificate was submitted.
* **Medical Age** (`10`, `number`, required: `false`): The age of the applicant.
* **Medical Gender** (`11`, `select_one`, required: `false`): The gender of the applicant (Male, Female, or Other).
* **Medical Ethnicity** (`12`, `select_one`, required: `false`): The ethnicity of the applicant (Asian, Black, White, or Other).
* **Medical Race** (`13`, `select_one`, required: `false`): The race of the applicant (American Indian/Alaska Native, Asian, Black, or White).
* **Medical Country of Birth** (`14`, `select_one`, required: `false`): The country where the applicant was born (US or Other).
* **Medical Age Group** (`15`, `select_one`, required: `false`): The age group of the applicant (0-1, 2-4, 5-9, 10-14, 15-19, 20-24, 25-29, 30-34, 35-39, 40-44, 45-49, 50-54, 55-59, 60-64, 65 or Older).
* **Medical Language** (`16`, `select_one`, required: `false`): The language spoken by the applicant (English, Spanish, or Other).
* **Medical Contact** (`17`, `text`, required: `false`): The contact information of the healthcare provider.
* **Medical Email** (`18`, `email`, required: `false`): The email address of the healthcare provider.
* **Medical Phone** (`19`, `text`, required: `false`): The phone number of the healthcare provider.
* **Medical Fax** (`20`, `text`, required: `false`): The fax number of the healthcare provider.
* **Medical Address** (`21`, `text`, required: `false`): The address of the healthcare provider.
* **Medical City** (`22`, `text`, required: `false`): The city of the healthcare provider.
* **Medical State** (`23`, `select_one`, required: `false`): The state of the healthcare provider's location (Alabama, Alaska, Arizona, Arkansas, California, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Florida, Georgia, Hawaii, Idaho, Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Kentucky, Louisiana, Maine, Maryland, Massachusetts, Michigan, Minnesota, Mississippi, Missouri, Montana, Nebraska, Nevada, New Hampshire, New Jersey, New Mexico, New York, North Carolina, North Dakota, Ohio, Oklahoma, Oregon, Pennsylvania, Rhode Island, South Carolina, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, Utah, Vermont, Virginia, Washington, West Virginia, Wisconsin, Wyoming).
* **Medical Postal Code** (`24`, `text`, required: `false`): The postal code of the healthcare provider.
* **Medical Zip** (`25`, `text`, required: `false`): The zip code of the healthcare provider.

## Tips
* Review the provided information carefully before submission.
* Ensure accurate and complete information.
* Submit the form only if the applicant is at least 18 years old.
* Use the correct submission type (Age Verification or Other).
