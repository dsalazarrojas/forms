# Clinical Trial Participant Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals who wish to participate in a clinical trial. It helps researchers collect demographic data, medical history, and eligibility criteria for potential participants. Please ensure that you have carefully reviewed the provided instructions and that your answers are honest and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form completely, ensuring you answer every question.
2.  Be honest and accurate with your information.
3.  If you are unsure about any question, please contact us for clarification.
4.  Review the form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`Full Name`, text, required):
    Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.

*   **Email Address** (`Email Address`, email, required):
    Provide your email address for communication purposes.

*   **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required):
    Enter your phone number for easy contact.

*   **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required):
    Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

*   **-- Trial Interest --** (`-- Trial Interest --`, note, required):
    This section is for your comments or explanations about why you are interested in participating in this trial.

*   **Which clinical trial are you applying for?** (`Which clinical trial are you applying for?`, text, required):
    Specify which clinical trial you are applying for from the available options or describe which one you are interested in.

*   **Why are you interested in participating in this clinical trial?** (`Why are you interested in participating in this clinical trial?`, text, required):
    Provide a detailed explanation of your interest in participating in this trial.

*   **-- Background Screening --** (`-- Background Screening --`, note, required):
    This section is for your comments or explanations about your medical history.

*   **Do you have any known medical conditions?** (`Do you have any known medical conditions?`, text, required):
    List or type 'None' if you have no known medical conditions.

*   **Are you currently taking any prescription medications?** (`Are you currently taking any prescription medications?`, text, required):
    List or type 'None' if you are not taking any prescription medications.

*   **Have you ever participated in a clinical trial before?** (`Have you ever participated in a clinical trial before?`, select_one, required):
    Select 'True' if you have participated in a clinical trial before, 'False' otherwise.

*   **-- Eligibility and Logistics --** (`-- Eligibility and Logistics --`, note, required):
    This section is for your comments or explanations about your availability and transportation.

*   **Are you available for monthly clinic visits for the next 12 months?** (`Are you available for monthly clinic visits for the next 12 months?`, select_one, required):
    Select 'True' if you are available for visits, 'False' otherwise.

*   **Do you have reliable transportation to the research site?** (`Do you have reliable transportation to the research site?`, select_one, required):
    Select 'True' if you have reliable transportation, 'False' otherwise.

*   **I understand that this is an application and does not guarantee enrollment** (`I understand that this is an application and does not guarantee enrollment`, select_one, required):
    Select 'I understand' to acknowledge that this is an application and not an enrollment confirmation.

*   **Applicant Digital Signature** (`Applicant Digital Signature`, text, required):
    Sign your name digitally to confirm that your information is accurate.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
*   If you are unsure about any question, contact us for clarification.
*   Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
*   This form may be used for research purposes only.
*   Your information will be handled in accordance with our research site's policies.
