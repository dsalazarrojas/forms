# Veterinary Prescription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Prescription Form is designed to collect essential information from pet owners for veterinary care and treatment. It is a general-purpose form, not intended for specific medical conditions that require specialized forms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the owner's name and phone number in the respective fields.
2. Enter the veterinarian's name in the `veterinarian` field.
3. Enter the pet's name in the `pet_name` field.
4. Select the medical condition affecting the pet from the `medical_condition` drop-down list.
5. Describe the treatment or medication prescribed by the veterinarian in the `prescription` field.
6. Schedule any follow-up appointments or treatment sessions in the `follow_up` field.
7. Sign and date the form by entering your name in the `signature` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <owner_name>
* **Owner's Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the pet owner.
### <owner_phone>
* **Owner's Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter the pet owner's phone number.
### <veterinarian>
* **Veterinarian** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the veterinarian providing the prescription.
### <pet_name>
* **Pet's Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the pet receiving treatment.
### <prescription>
* **Prescription** (text, required/optional): Describe the treatment or medication prescribed by the veterinarian.
### <medical_condition>
* **Medical Condition** (select one, required/optional): Select the medical condition affecting the pet from the drop-down list. Options are:
	+ Arthritis
	+ Cancer
	+ Diabetes
	+ Other
### <treatment>
* **Treatment** (text, required/optional): Describe any additional treatment or instructions provided by the veterinarian.
### <follow_up>
* **Follow-up** (time, required/optional): Schedule any follow-up appointments or treatment sessions.
### <signature>
* **Signature** (text, required/optional): Sign and date the form by entering your name.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields for accurate and efficient processing.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* This form is not intended for medical billing or insurance purposes.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your veterinarian for assistance.
