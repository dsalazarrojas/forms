# new_patient_medical_history_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about a new patient's medical history. It includes fields for their first name, last name, date of birth, address, and medical history. This form is intended to be used in a medical setting to gather relevant information about the patient's medical background.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field (in the format MM/DD/YYYY).
4. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
5. Describe your medical history in the "Medical History" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Required: false): Enter your first name as it is commonly known.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Required: false): Enter your last name as it is commonly known.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Required: false): Provide your birth date in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 02/12/1990).
* **Address** (`address`, Text, Required: false): Enter your current address.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, Text, Required: false): Describe any relevant medical conditions, allergies, or previous surgeries.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are providing accurate and truthful information about your medical history.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
* This form is intended for the collection of basic medical history information and should not be used for other purposes.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your healthcare provider for assistance.
