# First Responder Discharge Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The First Responder Discharge Form is used to document the details of a patient's discharge from the hospital or medical facility. This form is essential for maintaining accurate and up-to-date records of the patient's condition, treatment, and care instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the form, which is the name of the discharge form.
2. Provide the patient's name.
3. Enter the medical record number, if applicable.
4. Enter the name of the first responder (e.g., doctor, nurse, or medical assistant).
5. Enter the date of discharge.
6. Select the discharge disposition from the available options.
7. Enter the discharge date and time.
8. Provide the reason for discharge.
9. Enter the follow-up care instructions for the patient.
10. Enter any medication administered to the patient.
11. Identify the follow-up provider.
12. Enter the follow-up date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Responder Discharge Form** (<name>, text, not required): Enter the title of the form, which is a required field for administrative purposes.
* **Patient Name** (<name>, text, not required): Enter the name of the patient being discharged.
* **Medical Record Number** (<name>, text, not required): Enter the patient's medical record number, if applicable.
* **First Responder Name** (<name>, text, not required): Enter the name of the first responder who is discharging the patient (e.g., doctor, nurse, or medical assistant).
* **Date Of Discharge** (<name>, date, not required): Enter the date of discharge in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Discharge Disposition** (<name>, select one, not required): Select the reason for discharge from the available options.
* **Discharge Date** (<name>, date, not required): Enter the date of discharge in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Discharge Time** (<name>, time, not required): Enter the time of discharge in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00).
* **Reason For Discharge** (<name>, text, not required): Provide a brief explanation for the reason of discharge.
* **Follow-up Care Instructions** (<name>, text, not required): Enter any follow-up care instructions for the patient.
* **Medication Administered** (<name>, text, not required): Enter any medication administered to the patient.
* **Follow-up Provider** (<name>, text, not required): Identify the provider who will be following up with the patient.
* **Follow-up Date** (<name>, date, not required): Enter the date for the follow-up appointment in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Follow-up Time** (<name>, time, not required): Enter the time for the follow-up appointment in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00).

**Tips**

* Please ensure that the date and time formats are in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY for dates and 24-hour format for times).
* Double-check that all necessary fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If the follow-up provider is different from the first responder, enter the correct information.
