# Pre Appointment Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a pre-appointment declaration form designed to collect essential information from patients or individuals before their appointment with a healthcare provider. It helps the healthcare provider understand the patient's medical history, social contacts, and current symptoms to provide personalized care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Enter your full name and contact information in the "Contact Info" section.
3. Provide your medical history in the "Medical History" section, including any previous illnesses, allergies, or medical conditions.
4. List your social contacts, including family members or caregivers, in the "Social Contacts" section.
5. Describe your current symptoms in the "Symptoms" section.
6. Answer any additional questions on subsequent pages as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info (page 6)**: Enter your name and contact details, including phone numbers and email addresses.
* **Medical History (page 3)**: Describe any previous illnesses, allergies, or medical conditions that may impact your appointment or treatment. This includes previous surgeries, hospitalizations, or medical procedures.
* **Social Contacts (page 4)**: List the names and contact information of family members or caregivers who are relevant to your appointment or treatment.
* **Symptoms (page 5)**: Describe your current symptoms, including any new or worsening symptoms that have led you to schedule an appointment.
* **Page 1-9**: These pages are reserved for additional questions or notes from the healthcare provider. Please review them carefully and provide any necessary information.

## Tips

* Please provide accurate and detailed information to ensure your healthcare provider has a complete understanding of your medical history and current situation.
* If you are unsure about any question or section, ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* Complete this form only once for your upcoming appointment, to avoid unnecessary duplication of effort and potential data redundancy.
