# Oncology Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Oncology Discharge Form is designed to collect essential information about a patient's cancer treatment, medication, and follow-up appointments after discharge.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the **patient_name** field.
2. Provide the patient's medical record number.
3. Enter the patient's date of birth.
4. Enter the patient's phone number.
5. Select the type of cancer treatment the patient received (**cancer_treatment** field).
6. Enter the date of the next follow-up appointment (**followup_date**).
7. Enter the time of the next follow-up appointment (**followup_time**).
8. Enter the name of any medication the patient is currently taking (**medication_name**).
9. Enter the dosage frequency of the medication (**dosage_frequency**).
10. Describe any side effects of the medication (**medication_side_effects**).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their medical record.
* **medical_record_number**: Enter the patient's medical record number for easy identification.
* **patient_date_of_birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **patient_phone**: Enter the patient's phone number where they can be reached.
* **cancer_treatment**: Select the type of cancer treatment the patient received from the provided options.
* **followup_date**: Enter the date of the next follow-up appointment in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **followup_time**: Enter the time of the next follow-up appointment in the format HH:MM.
* **medication_name**: Enter the name of any medication the patient is currently taking.
* **dosage_frequency**: Enter the frequency at which the patient takes their medication (e.g., once daily, twice a day, etc.).
* **medication_side_effects**: Describe any side effects of the medication experienced by the patient.
