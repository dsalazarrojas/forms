# pet_medical_financial_assistance_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pet medical financial assistance application form is designed to collect necessary information for pet owners to seek financial assistance for their pet's medical needs. This form aims to gather vital details about the pet, owner, and medical information to process the application efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Enter the required information for your pet, including its ID, medical history, and treatment details.
2. Provide your contact details, such as phone number and email, so we can reach out to you regarding any updates or further assistance.
3. Review your submission carefully before submitting the form to ensure all information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **pet_info** (Text, Required): Enter your pet's information, including its ID, name, age, breed, and medical conditions.
* **owner_info** (Text, Optional): Enter any relevant information about yourself as the pet owner, including your relationship with the pet and any relevant medical history.
* **medical_info** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional medical information about your pet, including diagnosis, treatment, or medication.
* **treatment_info** (Text, Optional): Describe the treatment or therapy your pet is undergoing, including frequency and duration.
* **funding_info** (Text, Optional): Explain your financial situation and any financial constraints you're facing for your pet's medical care.
* **other_info** (Text, Optional): Share any other relevant information that might assist our team in processing your application.
* **notes** (Note, Optional): Add any additional comments or concerns you'd like to share with our team.
* **owner_signature** (Number, Required): A unique number representing your owner's signature for authentication purposes.
* **owner_phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your contact phone number for us to reach out with updates.
* **owner_email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address for us to communicate about your application.
* **vet_info** (Text, Optional): Provide any information about your pet's current vet or veterinarian, including their contact details. 

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and truthfully.
- If your pet has a medical condition that is not listed in the medical_info field, please provide additional details in the other_info field.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
