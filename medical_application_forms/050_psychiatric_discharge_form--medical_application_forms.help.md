# psychiatric_discharge_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Psychiatric Discharge Form is a document used to collect essential information from patients and their contacts during the discharge process. This form helps ensure a smooth transition for the patient, facilitates follow-up care, and provides necessary details for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your name and contact information in the **Patient Information** section.
2.  If you have a contact person (e.g., family member or caregiver) who will be responsible for your care after discharge, fill in their contact information in the **Contact Information** section.
3.  In the **Treatment Plan** section, select the treatments you have received during your stay (e.g., Medication, Therapy, Support Group, or Other). You can select multiple options if applicable.
4.  In the **Follow-Up Information** section, provide any relevant details about your scheduled follow-up appointments or further care instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Information** (text): Enter your name, date of birth, and any other relevant information about yourself.
*   **Contact Information** (text): If applicable, enter the name, relationship, and contact details of the person responsible for your care after discharge.
*   **Treatment Plan** (select multiple options): Choose the treatments you have received during your stay, such as Medication, Therapy, Support Group, or Other. Select multiple options if you have received more than one type of treatment.
*   **Follow-Up Information** (text): Provide any relevant details about your scheduled follow-up appointments or further care instructions, such as medication refills or follow-up dates.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that your care team has the necessary information for your transition and follow-up care.
*   If you are unsure about any information or need help with completing the form, ask your healthcare provider for assistance.
*   Be as detailed as possible when filling in the **Follow-Up Information** section to help your care team coordinate your follow-up care effectively.
