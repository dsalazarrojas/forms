# Radiology Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The radiology order form is a medical form used to gather information for radiology tests and exams. The form is essential for healthcare providers to collect accurate and complete information about patients before proceeding with imaging procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you have all the necessary information to complete it accurately.
2. Select the "Patient Info" page and provide the patient's basic information such as name, date of birth, and contact details.
3. Move to the "Imaging Info" page and enter the required details about the imaging procedure.
4. On the "Medical History" page, provide any relevant medical history that may affect the imaging procedure.
5. In the "Allergy List" section, select the allergies the patient has (if any).
6. In the "Medications" section, list any medications the patient is currently taking.
7. In the "Order Reason" section, enter the reason for the order (if not already stated).
8. Select "Exam Type" to choose the type of exam the patient needs.
9. In the "Exam Date" section, enter the date of the exam.
10. In the "Exam Time" section, enter the time of the exam.
11. On the "Imaging Requested" page, select whether imaging is requested.
12. In the "Imaging Procedure" section, enter the imaging procedure details (if any).
13. Finally, in the "Imaging Location" section, enter the location of the exam.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info**: Enter the patient's basic information such as name, date of birth, and contact details.
* **Imaging Info**: Enter the required details about the imaging procedure, such as the type of imaging and any relevant medical history.
* **Medical History**: Provide any relevant medical history that may affect the imaging procedure.
* **Allergy List**: Select the allergies the patient has (if any).
* **Medications**: List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Order Reason**: Enter the reason for the order (if not already stated).
* **Exam Type**: Choose the type of exam the patient needs.
* **Exam Date**: Enter the date of the exam.
* **Exam Time**: Enter the time of the exam.
* **Imaging Requested**: Select whether imaging is requested.
* **Imaging Procedure**: Enter the imaging procedure details (if any).
* **Imaging Location**: Enter the location of the exam.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the form for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask for assistance.
* This form is not a substitute for a comprehensive medical evaluation. If you have any concerns, please contact the patient's healthcare provider.
