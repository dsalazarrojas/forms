# Medical Sustainability Innovation Challenge Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Sustainability Innovation Challenge Application Form is for applicants to submit their innovative ideas that promote medical sustainability. This form will guide you through the application process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by reviewing each page**:
	* Page 1: Medical Information
	* Page 2: Innovation Details
	* Page 3: Application
	* Page 4: Submission
	* Page 5: Medical Sustainability
2. **Answer each question in the provided pages**:
	* Be clear and concise in your responses
	* Use relevant attachments or supporting documents if applicable
3. **Submit your application**:
	* Review your responses before submission
	* Ensure all required fields are filled out

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical Information (page_1)**:
	+ This page is for you to provide essential medical information.
	+ You can type your response in this text field.
* **Innovation Details (page_2)**:
	+ This page is for you to describe your innovative idea related to medical sustainability.
	+ Be specific about the innovation and its benefits.
* **Application (page_3)**:
	+ This page is for you to describe your application process.
	+ Explain how your innovation will promote medical sustainability.
* **Submission (page_4)**:
	+ This page is for you to submit your application.
	+ Attach any supporting documents or files if required.
* **Medical Sustainability (page_5)**:
	+ This page is for you to provide additional medical sustainability details.
	+ Explain how your innovation addresses medical sustainability.

## Tips
* Double-check your responses for accuracy and completeness.
* Be creative and concise in your answers.
* Attach necessary documents or files to support your application.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out before submission.
