# New Patient Registration Form (Over 18 Only) - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and medical information from patients over 18 years of age for medical registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are over 18 years old.
2. Fill in your first name and surname in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
3. Enter your date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field.
4. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Number" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. If you have an emergency contact, provide their name and contact number in the "Emergency Contact Name" and "Emergency Contact Number" fields.
7. If you have any medical history, provide it in the "Medical History" field.
8. List any allergies you may have in the "Allergies" field.
9. Mention any medical conditions you may have in the "Medical Conditions" field.
10. Indicate your medication status in the "Medications" field.
11. Confirm your emergency contact agreement and terms and conditions in the "Emergency Contact Agreement" and "Terms And Conditions" fields.
12. Sign and date the form by selecting "Yes" in the "Signature" and "Signature Date" fields.
13. Confirm that you are the patient and have read the terms and conditions by selecting "Yes" in the "Patient Signature" field.
14. Sign and date the form again by selecting "Yes" in the "Date Of Signature" field.
15. Provide the date and time of signing in the "Date Signed" field.
16. Enter the name of the doctor who signed the form in the "Signed By" field (optional).
17. Enter a unique ID for your signature in the "Signature ID" field.
18. Confirm that the doctor has reviewed the form by selecting "Yes" in the "Doctor Signature" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Date Of Birth** (Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Address** (Text, Optional): Provide your home address.
* **Contact Number** (Number, Required): Enter your contact number.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (Number, Optional): Enter your emergency contact's number.
* **Date Of Admission** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of admission.
* **Medical History** (Text, Optional): Provide any medical history you may have.
* **Allergies** (Text, Optional): List any allergies you may have.
* **Medical Conditions** (Text, Optional): Mention any medical conditions you may have.
* **Medications** (Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate if you are currently taking any medications.
* **Emergency Contact Agreement** (Select One, Required): Confirm that you have made an emergency contact arrangement.
* **Terms And Conditions** (Select One, Required): Confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Signature Date** (Date, Required): Sign and date the form.
* **Signature** (Select One, Required): Confirm that you are the patient and have read the terms and conditions.
* **Patient Signature** (Select One, Required): Confirm that you are the patient.
* **Date Of Signature** (Date, Required): Sign and date the form again.
* **Date Signed** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of signing.
* **Time Signed** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of signing.
* **Signed By** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the doctor who signed the form.
* **Signature ID** (Text, Required): Enter a unique ID for your signature.
* **Doctor Signature** (Select One, Optional): Confirm that the doctor has reviewed the form.
* **Submitted By** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
