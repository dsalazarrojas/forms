# Specialist Doctor Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and professional information from specialist doctors, which will be used for internal purposes such as database management and notifications.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your information is up-to-date and correct.
2. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Select your medical specialty from the "Medical Specialty" dropdown menu.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Enter your date of birth in the format "mm/dd/yyyy" for the "Date Of Birth" fields.
8. Select the correct date, month, and day of your birth from the "Date Of Birth" dropdown menus.
9. Select the correct time of day for your birth (AM/PM) from the "Date Of Birth Am Pm" dropdown menu.
10. Enter the name of your medical institution in the "Medical Institution" field.
11. Enter your medical certification in the "Medical Certification" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your given name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format "mm/dd/yyyy".
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you use for professional purposes.
* **Medical Specialty**: Select the specific area of medicine you specialize in (e.g. cardiology, oncology, etc.).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date Of Birth Date**: Enter the date of your birth in the format "dd/mm/yyyy".
* **Date Of Birth Year**: Enter the year you were born.
* **Date Of Birth Month**: Select the month of your birth from the dropdown menu.
* **Date Of Birth Day**: Select the day of your birth from the dropdown menu.
* **Date Of Birth Am Pm**: Select the time of day you were born (AM or PM).
* **Medical Institution**: Enter the name of your medical institution or workplace.
* **Medical Certification**: Enter your medical certification or license number.
