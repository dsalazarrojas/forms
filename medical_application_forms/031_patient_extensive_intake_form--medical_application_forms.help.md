# Patient Extensive Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect comprehensive information about a patient's health history and medical conditions.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Gather All Required Information

Please answer each question as accurately and thoroughly as possible. If a question does not apply to you, please indicate "N/A" (Not Applicable) or "Unknown" where applicable.

### Step 2: Review the Form Sections

1.  **Patient Information**: This section collects basic information about the patient, including their name, date of birth, and contact details.
    - **Patient Information (Patient Information)**: Enter the patient's name as they would like to be known.
2.  **Chief Complaint**: Describe your primary reason for the visit or the main issue you are experiencing.
3.  **Patient History**: Provide as much detail as possible about your medical history, including any previous illnesses, surgeries, or health conditions.
4.  **Allergies (Allergies)**: Indicate if you have any allergies or sensitivities to food, medications, or environmental factors.
5.  **Medications (Medications)**: List any medications you are currently taking or have taken in the past.
6.  **Medical History**: Describe any significant medical conditions, illnesses, or procedures you have had.
7.  **Vital Signs**: Record your current vital signs, including your blood pressure, pulse, respiratory rate, and other relevant metrics.
8.  **Lab Results**: Share any lab test results that may be relevant to your medical condition.
9.  **Medical Imaging**: Describe any recent medical imaging tests, such as X-rays, MRIs, or CT scans.
10. **Medical History 2 (Medical History 2)**: Indicate if you have any additional medical conditions or history that may be relevant to your current issue.
11. **Allergies 2 (Allergies 2)**: Identify any additional allergies or sensitivities that may be important for your treatment.
12. **Medications 2 (Medications 2)**: List any additional medications you are taking or have taken in the past.

## Tips

*   Be as thorough and accurate as possible when completing this form.
*   If you are unsure about any question or need clarification, please ask for assistance.
*   Remember to review and verify any entered information before submission.
*   If you have any concerns or questions about your treatment, please raise them with your healthcare provider.
