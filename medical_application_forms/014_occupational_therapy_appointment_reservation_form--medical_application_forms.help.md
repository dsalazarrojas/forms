# Occupational Therapy Appointment Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule an appointment with an occupational therapist for personal or professional needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each field carefully and ensure you have all necessary information.
2.  Enter your details accurately and thoroughly.
3.  Save and submit the form when ready.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **1.  Patient Form (prophets)** (`text`, `required: false`): Please provide a brief description of your purpose for visiting the occupational therapist. This will help us understand your needs better.
*   **2.  Appointment Date** (`date`, `required: false`): Enter the date you would like to schedule your appointment for.
*   **3.  Appointment Time** (`time`, `required: false`): Provide the specific time you would like to schedule your appointment.
*   **4.  Appointment Duration** (`number`, `required: false`): If known, please provide the expected duration of your appointment.
*   **5.  Therapy Session** (`text`, `required: false`): Briefly describe the type of therapy session you are interested in.
*   **6.  Therapist** (`select_one`, `required: false`):
    *   Choose 'Yes' if you have seen this therapist before and prefer working with them.
    *   Choose 'No' otherwise.
*   **7.  Available Slots** (`select_multiple`, `required: false`):
    *   Select 'Yes' for the slots you are available to attend.
*   **8.  Notes** (`note`, `required: false`): If you have any specific requests or comments, please type them here.
*   **9.  Email** (`email`, `required: false`): Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates or further information.
*   **10.  Phone** (`text`, `required: false`): Provide your contact phone number for any follow-up communication.
*   **11.  Save** (`select_one`, `required: false`):
    *   Choose 'Yes' to save your progress without submitting.
    *   Choose 'No' to discard any unsaved changes.
*   **12.  Submit** (`select_one`, `required: false`):
    *   Choose 'Yes' to finalize your appointment request.
    *   Choose 'No' to continue editing without submitting.
*   **13.  Cancel** (`select_one`, `required: false`):
    *   Choose 'Yes' to cancel your appointment request.
    *   Choose 'No' to continue as planned.
*   **14.  Confirm** (`select_one`, `required: false`):
    *   Choose 'Yes' to confirm your appointment request.
    *   Choose 'No' to continue with further review.
*   **15.  Confirm Email** (`select_one`, `required: false`):
    *   Choose 'Yes' to confirm you will receive emails about your appointment.
    *   Choose 'No' otherwise.

Please review each field carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before submitting your form. If you have any questions or need assistance, don't hesitate to reach out to the relevant personnel.
