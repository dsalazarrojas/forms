# Clinical Innovation Accelerator Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Clinical Innovation Accelerator Application Form is a tool for applicants to submit their project proposals for review. It helps the program team evaluate the merits of each project and make informed decisions about funding and support.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your contact information: Enter your primary name, email address, and the name of your organization or clinical site.
2.  Provide project details: Describe your innovation project, including the title, problem statement, and proposed solution.
3.  Outline expected impact: Share your expected clinical impact and how you plan to measure it.
4.  Identify required resources: Select all the resources you need from the accelerator, such as funding, mentorship, lab space, and clinical data access.
5.  Estimate implementation timeline: Choose a realistic timeline for implementing your project.
6.  Introduce your team: List the key team members and their roles.
7.  Share prior funding: Indicate if you have received prior funding for this project.
8.  Add supporting documentation: Upload any relevant documentation, such as whitepapers or pitch decks.
9.  Sign and submit: Sign the form digitally to confirm your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Primary Applicant Name**: Enter your name as the primary contact person for this application.
*   **Email Address**: Enter your email address for communication and follow-up.
*   **Organization or Clinical Site Name**: Enter the name of your organization or clinical site.
*   **Innovation Project Details**: This is a note section for providing an overview of your project.
*   **Title of Innovation Project**: Enter a brief title of your innovation project.
*   **Problem Statement - What clinical gap are you addressing?**: Describe the clinical gap you're trying to address with your project.
*   **Proposed Solution**: Describe your proposed solution to address the clinical gap.
*   **Current Stage of Innovation**: Select the current stage of your innovation project (concept, prototype, pilot, or scaling).
*   **Expected Clinical Impact**: Describe your expected clinical impact and how you plan to measure it.
*   **Required Resources from Accelerator**: Select all the resources you need from the accelerator to support your project.
*   **Estimated Timeline for Implementation**: Choose a realistic timeline for implementing your project.
*   **Key Team Members and Roles**: List the key team members and their roles in your project.
*   **Have you received prior funding for this project?**: Indicate if you have received prior funding for this project.
*   **Link for Supporting Documentation - e.g. whitepapers, pitch decks**: Upload any relevant documentation to support your project.
*   **Applicant Digital Signature**: Sign the form digitally to confirm your application.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
*   Be specific and detailed in your project description and expected impact.
*   Proofread your application for errors before submitting.
*   If you have any questions or need guidance, don't hesitate to reach out to the program team.
