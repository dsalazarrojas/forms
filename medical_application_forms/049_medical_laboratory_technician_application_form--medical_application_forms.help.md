**Medical Laboratory Technician Application Form - Help Guide**

**Purpose**
The Medical Laboratory Technician Application Form is a document used to collect information from applicants interested in working as a Medical Laboratory Technician. The purpose of this form is to gather relevant information about an applicant's education, work experience, and qualifications, as well as their language proficiency and professional references.

**How To Complete This Form**

To fill out this form, simply follow these steps:

* Enter your name and other contact information on page 2 (contact_name)
* Answer questions about your education, such as whether you attended medical school (page 3: attended_medical_school)
* Enter your address and phone number (page 4: address and phone_number)
* Describe your work experience, if applicable (page 5: work_experience)
* Enter your email address (page 6: email)
* If applicable, enter your medical lab technician license information, certifications, and training (pages 7-11: medical_lab_technician_license, medical_lab_technician_certifications, medical_lab_technician_training, medical_lab_technician_certifications_other, medical_lab_technician_certifications_issued_date, medical_lab_technician_exp_date, medical_lab_technician_certifications_ending_date)
* Answer questions about your language proficiency and professional organizations (pages 12-15: medical_lab_technician_english_language_proficiency, medical_lab_technician_other_language, medical_lab_technician_language_certificate, medical_lab_technician_professional_organizations)

**Field-by-Field Explanation**

* **1: Application Form Data**: This field is optional and should not be filled out unless you have any additional information you want to add about your application.
* **2: Contact Name**: Please enter your full name as you want it to be listed in our records.
* **3: Attended Medical School**: Answer "Yes" if you have attended medical school, otherwise answer "No".
* **4: Address**: Enter your home or work address.
* **5: Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **6: Work Experience**: If you have worked as a medical laboratory technician, describe your experience in this field.
* **7: Email**: Enter your email address.
* **8: Medical Laboratory Technician License**: If you have a medical laboratory technician license, enter the details about it.
* **9: Medical Laboratory Technician Certifications**: Check "Yes" if you have any medical laboratory technician certifications. If you have certifications, enter the details about them.
* **10: Medical Laboratory Technician Certifications Issued Date**: If you have certifications, enter the date they were issued.
* **11: Medical Laboratory Technician Certifications Ending Date**: If you have certifications, enter the date they are valid until.
* **12: Medical Laboratory Technician Certifications Issued**: Check "Yes" if you have any medical laboratory technician certifications issued.
* **13: Medical Laboratory Technician Certifications Ending**: Check "Yes" if you have any medical laboratory technician certifications ending.
* **14: Medical Laboratory Technician Work Experience**: Check "Yes" if you have worked as a medical laboratory technician.
* **15: Medical Laboratory Technician English Language Proficiency**: Check "Yes" if you are proficient in English.
* **16: Medical Laboratory Technician Other Language**: Enter any other languages you are proficient in.
* **17: Medical Laboratory Technician Language Certificate**: Enter any language certificates you have.
* **18: Medical Laboratory Technician Professional Organizations**: Enter any professional organizations you belong to.
* **19: Medical Laboratory Technician References**: Enter any professional references you have.

**Tips**

* Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
* If you have any supporting documents, please attach them to your application.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us for assistance.
