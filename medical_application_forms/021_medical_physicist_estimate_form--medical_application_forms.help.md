# Medical Physicist Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help medical physicists estimate costs and time for various tasks and projects. It collects information on the type of estimate, cost types, total labor, material, and other costs, estimated time, comments, and assigned personnel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of estimate you are making (e.g. Equipment Upgrades, Recalibration, Educational Programs, or Research Projects).
2. Choose the types of costs you are estimating (e.g. Labor, Material, or Other).
3. Enter the total labor cost, total material cost, and total other costs.
4. Estimate the time required for the task.
5. Add any additional comments about the project or task.
6. Assign the tool or task to a specific user.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Estimate Type** (`estimate_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of estimate you are making (e.g. Equipment Upgrades, Recalibration, Educational Programs, or Research Projects).
* **Cost Type** (`cost_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the types of costs you are estimating (e.g. Labor, Material, or Other).
* **Total Labor Cost** (`total_labor_cost`, number, required: false): Enter the total labor cost for the task or project.
* **Total Material Cost** (`total_material_cost`, number, required: false): Enter the total material cost for the task or project.
* **Total Other Costs** (`total_other_costs`, number, required: false): Enter the total other costs for the task or project.
* **Estimated Time** (`estimated_time`, number, required: false): Estimate the time required for the task or project.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required: false): Assign the task or project to a specific tool.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, required: false): Assign the task or project to a specific user.

## Tips
* Make sure to choose the correct estimate type and cost types for your project or task.
* Be accurate when estimating the total labor, material, and other costs.
* Keep the estimated time reasonable and realistic.
* Add any additional comments or information about the project or task.
