# Medical Simulation Training Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from applicants for the Medical Simulation Training Fellowship program. It includes details about the applicant's background, experience, and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form

### 1. **Applicant Information**
To begin, fill in the **Applicant Information** section with your personal details.

### 2. **Contact Information**
Provide your email address under **Contact Information** so we can contact you with further instructions or updates.

### 3. **Education Background**
In the **Education Background** section, describe your academic background and any relevant education or training.

### 4. **Personal References**
Check the box for each person who can vouch for your professional character. Please only select individuals who are familiar with your work and can attest to your skills and qualifications.

### 5. **Medical Certification**
Indicate whether you hold a medical certification. If you have other certifications not listed, select **Other** and provide more information.

### 6. **Work Experience**
Describe your relevant work experience, including any relevant training or simulation experience.

### 7. **Personal Statement**
Write a personal statement that explains why you are a good fit for the Medical Simulation Training Fellowship program.

### 8. **Emergency Contact**
Provide the contact information for an emergency contact, in case we need to reach out to them.

### 9. **Medical License**
If you hold a medical license, provide more information about it.

## Tips

* Ensure all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Use clear and concise language when filling in the fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
