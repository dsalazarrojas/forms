# Reiki Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reiki Intake Form is a document used to gather information about a client's Reiki experience, preferences, and contact details. This form is designed to be completed by the client or their representative in preparation for a Reiki session or consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose your response to "Have you previously practiced Reiki?" by selecting "Yes" or "No". This will help the practitioner understand your experience level with Reiki.
2. Enter your name in the "Client Name" field. This is the name that will be used to address you during the session.
3. Indicate your preferred contact method in the "Contact Method" field. This can be "Yes" (phone, email, or in-person) or "No" (we will not contact you).
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, if you wish to be contacted via email.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, if you wish to be contacted via phone.
6. Enter any relevant details about your address in the "Prophets" field, if required.
7. Sign your agreement to participate in the session by typing your electronic signature in the "Electronic Signature" field.
8. Enter the date you signed the agreement in the "Electronic Signature Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Have you previously practiced Reiki?** (`reiki_intake_form`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ This field helps the practitioner understand your experience level with Reiki.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your name as it should be addressed during the session.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Indicate whether you prefer to be contacted via phone, email, or not at all.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false)
	+ Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted via email.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted via phone.
* **Prophets** (`prophets`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter any relevant details about your address.
* **Electronic Signature** (`e_signature`, note, required: false)
	+ Type your electronic signature to agree to participate in the session.
* **Electronic Signature Date** (`e_signature_date`, date, required: false)
	+ Enter the date you signed the agreement.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your practitioner for clarification.
* This form is used to ensure a smooth and effective Reiki session, please be as detailed and honest as possible.
