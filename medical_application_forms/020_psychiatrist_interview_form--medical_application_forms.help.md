# Psychiatrist Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a patient's interaction with a psychiatrist, including medical history, symptoms, and treatment plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the patient information section with the patient's details.
2. Select the relevant medical history options that apply to the patient.
3. Describe the patient's symptoms in the symptom section.
4. List any medication allergies in the medication allergies section.
5. Choose the patient's status and mental status from the provided options.
6. Select the assessment outcome and choose the patient's relationship with the psychiatrist.
7. Enter any additional comments or notes about the patient in the patient notes section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Medical History**: Select any previous medical conditions or surgeries that the patient has had.
* **Assessment Date**: Enter the date and time of the psychiatrist's assessment.
* **Symptoms**: Describe the patient's symptoms, including any physical or mental health issues.
* **Medication Allergies**: List any medications that the patient is allergic to.
* **Patient Status**: Choose the patient's status from the provided options, such as "active" or "inactive".
* **Mental Status**: Select the patient's mental status from the provided options, such as "stable" or "unstable".
* **Assessment Outcome**: Select the outcome of the psychiatrist's assessment, such as "positive" or "negative".
* **Treatment Plan**: Enter any treatment plans or recommendations made by the psychiatrist.
* **Follow Up**: Enter the date and time of the next scheduled follow up appointment.
* **Psychiatrist Name**: Enter the name of the psychiatrist who filled out the form.
* **Psychiatrist License**: Enter the license number of the psychiatrist who filled out the form.
* **Patient Relationship**: Choose the patient's relationship with the psychiatrist, such as "new" or "established".
* **Patient Contact Info**: Enter the patient's contact information.
* **Patient Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the patient.
* **Medical History 2**: Select any additional medical history that is relevant to the patient's current condition.
* **Patient Family History**: Enter any relevant family medical history.
* **Medical Procedure**: Enter any medical procedures that have been performed on the patient.
* **Medication Allergies 2**: List any additional medication allergies.
* **Medical Procedure 2**: Enter any additional medical procedures that have been performed on the patient.
* **Patient Status 2**: Choose the patient's status from the provided options.
* **Mental Status 2**: Select the patient's mental status from the provided options.
* **Assessment Outcome 2**: Select the outcome of the psychiatrist's assessment.
* **Psychiatrist Name 2**: Enter the name of the psychiatrist who filled out the form.
* **Patient Relationship 2**: Choose the patient's relationship with the psychiatrist.

Note: The fields marked as required are mandatory and must be filled out.
