# Orthopedic Surgery Patient Summary - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Orthopedic Surgery Patient Summary form is a collection of fields that gather essential information about a patient's details, medical history, and surgical procedure specifics. It is intended to provide a comprehensive overview of the patient's condition and requirements for orthopedic surgery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Start with the patient's personal details such as their First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, Contact Number, and Email.
2. Provide the Patient Address and Medical Provider Name if applicable.
3. Indicate the Procedure Type and whether or not the patient has a Medical History.
4. Specify the Surgical Team Name and Surgeon Specialty if required.
5. Enter the Operating Room Number, Patient Height, and Procedure Length if necessary.
6. Enter the Patient Weight and Patient Dose if required.
7. Specify whether the surgical procedure was a Surgical Procedure and whether there is a Medical Supervising Provider.
8. Enter the Procedure Date and Operating Room Time if necessary.
9. Specify the Patient Condition, Anesthesia, Surgical Approach, Postoperative Care, and whether the procedure was a Yes or No option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter the patient's first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter the patient's last name.
* **Date Of Birth** (Date, Required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Number** (Text, Optional): Enter the patient's contact number.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Patient Address** (Text, Optional): Enter the patient's address.
* **Procedure Type** (Yes/No, Optional): Indicate whether the procedure is a surgical procedure.
* **Medical History** (Text, Optional): Provide any relevant medical history.
* **Surgical Team Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the surgical team.
* **Surgeon Specialty** (Yes/No, Optional): Specify the surgeon's specialty.
* **Operating Room Number** (Number, Optional): Enter the operating room number.
* **Patient Height** (Number, Optional): Enter the patient's height.
* **Procedure Length** (Yes/No, Optional): Indicate the length of the procedure.
* **Patient Weight** (Number, Optional): Enter the patient's weight.
* **Patient Dose** (Number, Optional): Enter the patient's dose.
* **Medical Provider Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the medical provider.
* **Surgical Procedure** (Yes/No, Optional): Specify whether the procedure was a surgical procedure.
* **Medical Supervising Provider** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the medical supervising provider.
* **Procedure Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the procedure date.
* **Operating Room Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the operating room time.
* **Patient Condition** (Yes/No, Optional): Indicate the patient's condition.
* **Anesthesia** (Yes/No, Optional): Indicate whether the procedure involved anesthesia.
* **Surgical Approach** (Yes/No, Optional): Specify the surgical approach.
* **Postoperative Care** (Yes/No, Optional): Specify the postoperative care.

## Tips
* Always ensure that the form is completed accurately and consistently to avoid errors.
* If a field is not applicable, mark it as "N/A" or leave it blank.
* If you are unsure about a field or section, refer to the surgical team or medical provider for guidance.
