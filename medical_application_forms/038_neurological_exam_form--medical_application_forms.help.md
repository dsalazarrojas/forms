# Neurological Exam Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Neurological Exam Form is a tool used to assess a patient's neurological status. It is designed to gather information about various aspects of a patient's neurological system, including examination findings, cranial calcification, cranial meninges, and other related parameters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the patient's medical history and conduct a thorough physical examination.
2. Answer the questions in this form based on your observations and assessments.
3. Select the relevant options for each question from the dropdown menus.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Examination Findings**: Describe any notable or unusual findings during the examination.
    * (`title`, `text`, required/optional): A short description of any notable or unusual findings during the examination.
- **Cranial Calcification**: Select the type of cranial calcification present:
    * (`label`, `select_one`, required/optional): Cushing's Disease, Hydrocephalus, or Metastasis
- **Cranial Meningitis**: Select all types of cranial meninges present:
    * (`label`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Meningocele, Meningitis
- **Cranial Muscle**: Select all types of cranial muscle present:
    * (`label`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Normal, Abnormal, Weak
- **Cranial Nerve**: Select the state of the cranial nerve:

- **Mental Status**: Select all types of mental status present:

## Tips
* Please review the patient's medical history and conduct a thorough examination before filling out this form.
