# healthcare_process_optimization_fellowship_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants for the healthcare process optimization fellowship program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary documents ready, including a CV and Resume.
2. Complete the Application Form page with your basic contact information.
3. On the CV page, attach your CV as a supporting document.
4. On the Motivations page, provide a brief description of why you are interested in the fellowship program.
5. On the Resume page, attach your Resume as a supporting document.
6. On the Additional Information page, provide any additional details you'd like to share with the program administrators.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Form** (`Application Form`, `text`, required: false): This page is where you will enter your basic contact information, including your name, email, and other contact details.
* **CV** (`cv`, `text`, required: false): Attach your current CV as a supporting document to your application.
* **Motivations** (`motivations`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of why you are applying for the fellowship program. Be concise and clear in your answer.
* **Resume** (`resume`, `text`, required: false): Attach your current Resume as a supporting document to your application.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, `text`, required: false): Use this section to provide any additional details that you think would be helpful to the program administrators.

## Tips
* Make sure to attach both your CV and Resume as separate supporting documents.
* Keep your answers to the Motivations section concise and clear.
* Ensure your contact information on the Application Form is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review and edit your application carefully before submitting it.
