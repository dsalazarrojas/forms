# physical_therapist_recommendation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The physical therapist recommendation form is designed to collect relevant information about a patient's medical condition, treatment history, and other details to provide an informed recommendation. This form is intended for use by physical therapists in their practice.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's contact email in the "Contact Email" field.
3. Enter the patient's contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
4. Enter the patient's address in the "Patient Address" field.
5. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Patient Date of Birth" field. Use the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
6. Describe the patient's medical condition in the "Medical Condition" field.
7. Provide a brief treatment history in the "Treatment History" field.
8. Specify the reason for the referral in the "Referral Reason" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's full name, including first name and last name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the patient's email address where they can be contacted.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number where they can be contacted.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, optional): Enter the patient's home or physical address.
* **Patient Date of Birth** (`patient_date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): Describe the patient's current medical condition, including any relevant symptoms or diagnoses.
* **Treatment History** (`treatment_history`, text, optional): Provide a brief history of any previous treatments or therapies the patient has received.
* **Referral Reason** (`referral_reason`, text, optional): Specify the reason for the physical therapist's recommendation, such as a new patient evaluation or a follow-up appointment.
