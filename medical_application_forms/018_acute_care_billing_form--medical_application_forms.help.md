# Acute Care Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information necessary for accurate and efficient billing and payment processing after an acute care visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure all information is correct and up-to-date.
3. If you have any discrepancies or questions, do not hesitate to contact the billing department for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient Full Legal Name**
 Patient Full Legal Name: This field is required and asks for the patient's full legal name.

*   **Patient Account / MRN Number** (ID: 3)
    This field is required and asks for the patient's unique account number.

*   **Date of Service**
    This field is required and asks for the date of service.

*   **Acute Care Facility Name**
    This field is required and asks for the name of the acute care facility that provided the service.

*   **Primary Insurance Provider**
    This field is required and asks for the name of the primary insurance provider.

*   **Policy Holder Name**
    This field is required and asks for the name of the policy holder.

*   **Policy / Member ID Number**
    This field is required and asks for the policy or member ID number.

*   **Do you have secondary insurance?**
    This field is required and asks if you have secondary insurance. Choose "True" if you have secondary insurance, and "False" otherwise.

*   **Primary Service Category**
    This field is required and asks for the primary service category.

*   **Estimated Total Charges**
    This field is not required and asks for the estimated total charges.

*   **Financial Responsibility Agreement**
    This field is required and asks you to confirm your financial responsibility.

*   **Preferred Payment Method for Co-pay**
    This field is required and asks for your preferred payment method.

*   **Billing Contact Phone**
    This field is required and asks for the contact phone number of the billing department.

*   **Billing Contact Email**
    This field is required and asks for the email address of the billing department.

## Tips
*   Always double-check the information you provide for accuracy.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact the billing department for assistance.
*   Ensure all required fields are filled out completely and accurately.
