# Medical Simulation Design Research Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a medical simulation design research application. It is used to gather details about the research proposal, including the simulation center name, researcher information, and reviewing units' contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the research proposal section with a clear and concise description of your research.
2. Select the simulation center where your research will be conducted.
3. Provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
4. Identify the reviewing units that will be involved in the review process.
5. Enter the reviewing units' contact information, including their email, phone number, and address.
6. Choose the status of your form from the options provided.
7. Add any reviewer's name, email, and phone number if applicable.
8. Optionally, add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Research Proposal** (Research Proposal, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your research proposal.
* **Simulation Center Name** (Simulation Center Name, select_one, optional): Select the simulation center where your research will be conducted.
* **Simulation Center Email** (Simulation Center Email, email, optional): Enter the email address of the simulation center.
* **Researcher Name** (Researcher Name, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Researcher Email** (Researcher Email, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Researcher Phone** (Researcher Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Institutional Review** (Institutional Review, select_multiple, optional): Select whether you have or have not been through an institutional review.
* **Reviewing Units** (Reviewing Units, select_one, optional): Select the reviewing units that will be involved in the review process.
* **Reviewing Units Email** (Reviewing Units Email, email, optional): Enter the email address of the reviewing units.
* **Reviewing Units Phone** (Reviewing Units Phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the reviewing units.
* **Reviewing Units Phone Number** (Reviewing Units Phone Number, text, optional): Enter the phone extension of the reviewing units.
* **Reviewing Units Email Address** (Reviewing Units Email Address, text, optional): Enter the email address of the reviewing units.
* **Reviewing Units Department** (Reviewing Units Department, text, optional): Enter the department of the reviewing units.
* **Reviewing Units Fax Number** (Reviewing Units Fax Number, number, optional): Enter the fax number of the reviewing units.
* **Reviewing Units Address** (Reviewing Units Address, text, optional): Enter the address of the reviewing units.
* **Reviewing Units City** (Reviewing Units City, text, optional): Enter the city of the reviewing units.
* **Reviewing Units State** (Reviewing Units State, text, optional): Enter the state of the reviewing units.
* **Reviewing Units Postal Code** (Reviewing Units Postal Code, text, optional): Enter the postal code of the reviewing units.
* **Reviewing Units Country** (Reviewing Units Country, text, optional): Enter the country of the reviewing units.
* **Reviewing Units Comments** (Reviewing Units Comments, note, optional): Enter any additional comments about the reviewing units.
* **Form Review Status** (Form Review Status, select_one, optional): Select the status of your form from the options provided.
* **Reviewer Name** (Reviewer Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Email** (Reviewer Email, email, optional): Enter the email address of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Phone** (Reviewer Phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Notes** (Reviewer Notes, text, optional): Enter any additional notes from the reviewer.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* Enter the correct simulation center and reviewing units' information.
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
