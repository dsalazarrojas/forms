# Dental Procedure Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dental Procedure Discharge Form is a form designed to collect information about a patient's recovery and contact details for follow-up care after a completed dental procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your name and contact information.
2. Provide a brief description of your recovery status.
3. Enter your contact details, including your phone number and email or other preferred contact method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Recovery Status** (recovery_info, text, required): 
Provide a brief description of your current recovery status, including any relevant details or concerns about your condition.

### 2. **Contact Information** (contact_info, text, required): 
Enter your name and preferred contact method (phone number, email, or other). This will help our team stay in touch and answer any questions or concerns you may have after your procedure.

### 3. **Post-Procedure Care** (post_op_care, text, optional): 
If you have any specific questions or concerns about post-procedure care, or if you would like to schedule a follow-up appointment, enter your thoughts here. (Optional, but recommended if you have any questions or need further guidance.)

### 4. **Patient Information** (patient_info, text, optional):
This field is not required, but feel free to enter any relevant information about yourself if you'd like to share it with us.
