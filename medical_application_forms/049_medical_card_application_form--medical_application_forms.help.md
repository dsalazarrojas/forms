# Medical Card Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Card Application Form is a crucial document used to gather essential information about a patient's medical history, medical card, and other relevant details. This form is designed to provide a comprehensive overview of the patient's medical situation, enabling healthcare professionals to make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
3. Enter the name of the healthcare professional who will be treating the patient in the "Doctor Name" field.
4. Provide the patient's medical card number in the "Medical Card Number" field.
5. Select the relevant medical card type from the list provided in the "Medical Card Type" field.
6. Enter the date and time of the application in the "Application Date" and "Application Time" fields, respectively.
7. If applicable, enter any relevant patient ID in the "Patient ID" field.
8. If applicable, enter any relevant doctor ID in the "Doctor ID" field.
9. Provide a brief reason for the application in the "Application Reason" field.
10. Enter the patient's address in the "Patient Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Name (Text Field)
* The "Patient Name" field is where you enter the patient's full name.

### Contact Number (Text Field)
* The "Contact Number" field is where you enter the patient's phone number or contact information.

### Doctor Name (Text Field)
* The "Doctor Name" field is where you enter the name of the healthcare professional who will be treating the patient.

### Medical Card Number (Text Field)
* The "Medical Card Number" field is where you enter the patient's medical card number, if applicable.

### Medical Card Type (Select Multiple Field)
* The "Medical Card Type" field allows you to select one or more types of medical cards that apply to the patient.

### Application Date (Date Field)
* The "Application Date" field is where you enter the date of the application.

### Application Time (Time Field)
* The "Application Time" field is where you enter the time of the application.

### Patient ID (Text Field)
* The "Patient ID" field is where you enter any relevant patient ID number, if applicable.

### Doctor ID (Text Field)
* The "Doctor ID" field is where you enter any relevant doctor ID number, if applicable.

### Application Reason (Text Field)
* The "Application Reason" field is where you provide a brief reason for the application.

### Patient Address (Text Field)
* The "Patient Address" field is where you enter the patient's address.

### Contact Person (Text Field)
* The "Contact Person" field is where you enter any additional contact information for the patient.
