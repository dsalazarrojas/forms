# Medical Technology Acceleration Program Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to support applications to the Medical Technology Acceleration Program. It helps us understand your needs and requirements for medical technology acceleration. Please ensure you provide accurate information to increase the chances of a successful application.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form thoroughly, ensuring all required fields are completed.
- If you're unsure about a field, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section for more details.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, contact your designated contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Application Form** (1): A brief overview of your company and your medical technology or product.
- **Company Name** (2): The name of your company as it appears on your business cards or website.
- **Company Address** (3): Your company's physical address.
- **Company City** (4): The city where your company is located.
- **Company State** (5): The state where your company is located.
- **Company Zip** (6): The zip code for your company's address.
- **Medical Device or Product** (7): The name of the specific medical device or product you're applying for.
- **Description of Device or Product** (8): A detailed description of the device or product, including any relevant features or benefits.
- **Medical Field** (9): The medical field related to your application, such as Cardiology, Dermatology, etc.
- **Medical Condition or Disease** (10): The medical condition or disease your technology or product is intended to address. You can select multiple options.
- **Target Population** (11): The group of people your technology or product is designed for, such as Pediatric, Adult, etc. You can select multiple options.
- **Contact Person** (12): The person responsible for this application, including their name and title.
- **Contact Phone** (13): The phone number of your contact person.
- **Contact Email** (14): The email address of your contact person.
- **Medical Technology Acceleration Program** (15): The specific program you're applying for, such as Medtech Program, Research Program, etc.
- **How Long** (16): The time frame for which you require funding or support.
- **Funding Required** (17): The amount of funding required for your project.
- **Location** (18): The location where you'll be working on your project.
- **Date of Submission** (19): The date you're submitting your application.
- **Time of Submission** (20): The time at which you'll submit your application.

## Tips
- Please ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
- If you have any questions, contact your designated contact person.
- Applications will be reviewed and evaluated based on the information provided in this form.
