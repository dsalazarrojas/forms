# Medical App Development Beta Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical App Development Beta Participation Consent Form is designed to obtain informed consent from participants before they start the beta testing process of the medical app. This form is essential to ensure that participants understand the terms and conditions of the beta testing process and agree to participate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the participant information section, which includes your name, contact number, and contact email.
2.  Select the medical app version you are testing.
3.  Choose your operating system.
4.  Provide your device model.
5.  Select your device type.
6.  Indicate whether you are a beta tester.
7.  Optionally, provide feedback, comments, and signature sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information**: Please fill out your name in this field.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your contact number and email address.
* **Informed Consent**: Please provide informed consent to participate in the beta testing process by checking the box. This indicates that you understand the terms and conditions and are willing to follow them.
* **Medical App Version**: Choose the version of the medical app you are testing from the dropdown menu.
* **Operating System**: Select the operating system you are using.
* **Device Model**: Enter your device model.
* **Device Type**: Choose the type of device you are using.
* **Beta Tester**: Indicate whether you are a beta tester or not.
* **Feedback**: If you have any feedback or suggestions, please enter them here.
* **Comments**: If you have any comments, please enter them here.
* **Signature**: Provide your signature to indicate that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Participant Signature**: Enter your signature to confirm your participation.
* **Beta Participant Agreement**: This is an agreement that you must read and sign to indicate that you understand the terms and conditions.
* **Beta Agreement Signature**: Sign this section to confirm your agreement.
* **Tester Agreement**: This is an agreement that the beta tester must read and sign.
* **Tester Agreement Signature**: Sign this section to confirm your agreement as a tester.
* **Tester Comments**: If you have any comments, please enter them here.

Note: All fields marked as required are mandatory. Please ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately and truthfully.
