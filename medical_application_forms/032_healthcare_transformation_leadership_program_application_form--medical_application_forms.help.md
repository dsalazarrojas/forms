# Healthcare Transformation Leadership Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from applicants who are interested in participating in the Healthcare Transformation Leadership Program. The program aims to support healthcare professionals in their leadership development and transformation journey.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
2. Gather all necessary information and documents required for each field.
3. Answer each question accurately and completely.
4. Ensure that you have attached any required documentation or attachments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information**: This is the first page of the form where you will provide your basic details such as name, contact information, and other relevant details.
* **Personal Details**: Here, you will provide more specific details about your personal background, education, and professional experience.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your work and personal contact information for communication purposes.
* **Medical License**: Select the type of medical license you hold or are licensed to practice. Please note that this field is optional.
* **Work History**: Briefly describe your work experience, focusing on your role, job title, and years of service.
* **Education Background**: Describe your educational background, including degrees, universities attended, and graduation years.
* **Leadership Experience**: Share your leadership experience and position(s) held. Please note that this field is optional.
* **Professional Certifications**: Select any professional certifications you hold that are relevant to your role or experience.
* **Medical School**: Choose the medical school you attended.
* **Medical Degree**: Select the type of medical degree you hold.
* **Graduation Year**: Enter the year you graduated from medical school.
* **Practice Setting**: Select the type of practice setting you work or have worked in.
* **Current Position**: Select your current job position.
* **Work Type**: Choose the type of work you do.
* **Job Title**: Select your job title.
* **Years of Service**: Enter the number of years you have worked in the healthcare industry.
* **Clinical Experience**: Enter the number of years you have clinical experience.
* **Leadership Experience**: Enter the number of years you have leadership experience.
* **Leadership Position**: Enter the number of leadership positions you have held.
* **Leadership Title**: Briefly describe your leadership role(s).
* **Work Address**: Enter your work address for communication purposes.
* **Work Email**: Enter your work email address.
* **Work Phone**: Enter your work phone number.
* **Personal Email**: Enter your personal email address.
* **Personal Phone**: Enter your personal phone number.

Note: Some fields may require additional documentation or attachments. Please ensure that you have attached the necessary supporting documents before submitting the form. If you are unsure about any part of the form, please reach out to the program administrators for clarification.
