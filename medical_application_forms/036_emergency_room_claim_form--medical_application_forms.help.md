# emergency_room_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to walk you through the process of filling out the emergency room claim form. This form is used to collect information from claimants for insurance claims or billing purposes in the medical industry.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before starting to fill out the fields, as each section is important for claim processing.
2. Fill out the form completely, as incomplete or inaccurate information can delay or complicate claims processing.
3. Enter accurate and truthful information in all fields, as this information is used to process claims efficiently and effectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claimant Information** (`claimant_info`, text, optional): Please enter the claimant's name and any other identifying information.
* **Claimant Relationship** (`claimant_relationship`, select_multiple, optional): Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the claimant's relationship to the patient or individual receiving treatment.
* **Claimant Contact Information** (`claimant_contact_info`, text, optional): Enter the claimant's contact information, such as phone number and address.
* **Claimant Medicare Information** (`claimant_medicare_info`, text, optional): Enter the claimant's Medicare information, including any relevant Medicare ID numbers.
* **Claimant Employer** (`claimant_employer`, text, optional): Enter the claimant's employer information, if applicable.
* **Claimant Insurance Information** (`claimant_insurance_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant insurance information, such as policy numbers and provider names.
* **Claimant Emergency Information** (`claimant_emergency_info`, text, optional): Enter any information related to the emergency room visit, such as the date and time of treatment.
* **Provider Information** (`provider_info`, text, optional): Enter the provider's information, including their name and contact details.
* **Claimant Injury Information** (`claimant_injury_info`, text, optional): Enter any information related to the injury or condition being claimed.
* **Claimant Claim Information** (`claimant_claim_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant claim information, such as dates and details of treatment.
* **Claimant Other Information** (`claimant_other_info`, text, optional): Enter any other relevant information not covered in other fields.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): This field is for signing and dating the form. Please sign and date as instructed.
* **Claimant Signature** (`claimant_signature`, note, optional): This field is also for signing and dating by the claimant. Please sign and date as instructed.

Note: Some fields are optional, but please ensure accuracy and completeness whenever possible.
