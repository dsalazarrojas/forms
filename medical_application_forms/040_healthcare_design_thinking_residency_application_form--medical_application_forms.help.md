# Healthcare Design Thinking Residency Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Healthcare Design Thinking Residency Application Form is designed to collect relevant information from applicants for the residency program. The form is a self-contained, one-page application that aims to gather all necessary information to evaluate the applicants' qualifications and fit for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the fields with your personal and professional information as requested.
2. Provide as much detail as possible in each field to give the best representation of your qualifications.
3. Make sure to attach any relevant documents or files as requested in the "Attachments" field.
4. Once you have filled in all the necessary information, review the form carefully to ensure everything is complete and accurate.
5. Click the "Submit" button to send the application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <page_1>
* **Page 1**: This is the first and only page of the application form.

### <contact_info>
* **Contact Info**: Please provide your current contact information, including your name, email, and phone number. This is the best way for us to communicate with you throughout the application process.

### <personal_info>
* **Personal Info**: In this field, please provide a brief description of your personal background, experience, and qualifications.

### <contact_relationship>
* **Contact Relationship**: If you have any personal or professional connections with someone at the residency program, please describe your relationship here.

### <education_work_experience>
* **Education Work Experience**: Here, please describe your educational background and any relevant work experience. Try to be as detailed as possible.

### <education>
* **Education**: If you have any additional education or special training that is not included in your previous answer, please describe it here.

### <work_experience>
* **Work Experience**: Describe your work experience, including the name of the company, job title, and a brief description of your job duties.

### <medical_schools>
* **Medical Schools**: If you have attended any medical school, please list the name of the school and the year you graduated.

### <references>
* **References**: Please provide the names and contact information of two professional references that can vouch for your skills and qualifications.

### <attachments>
* **Attachments**: If you have any supporting documents, such as resumes, diplomas, or certifications, please attach them here.

### <medical_degree>
* **Medical Degree**: If you have earned a medical degree, please specify the degree and the institution where you earned it.

### <other_certifications>
* **Other Certifications**: If you have any other certifications, please list them here.

### <residency_program>
* **Residency Program**: Please describe the residency program you are applying for.

### <submit>
* **Submit**: Once you have filled in all the necessary information, click this button to send your application.
