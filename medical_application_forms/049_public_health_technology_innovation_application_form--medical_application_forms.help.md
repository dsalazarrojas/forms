# public_health_technology_innovation_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about public health technology innovation applications. It is intended for individuals, startups, or organizations to submit proposals for projects that aim to improve public health outcomes. The form helps to capture essential details about the project, its potential impact on public health, and any collaborating partners involved.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **User Information**: Please fill in your name, email, and any other relevant contact information.
2. **Project Information**: Provide a brief description of your project, highlighting its key features and functionalities.
3. **Public Health Impact**: Describe the potential public health impact of your proposal, including any benefits or outcomes you expect to achieve.
4. **Project Details**: Elaborate on the specifics of your project, such as its goals, objectives, and any relevant data or research supporting it.
5. **Collaboration**: If you are working in collaboration with others, list the names of your partners, specifying their role as Individuals, Startups, or Organizations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_information`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to collect your contact information, including your name and email. Please ensure that the information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Project Information** (`project_information`, `text`, required: false): This field is for describing your project in a few sentences or bullet points. Focus on its key features and functionalities that make it innovative and beneficial for public health.
* **Public Health Impact** (`public_health_impact`, `text`, required: false): In this field, describe the potential benefits or outcomes you expect from your project. Be specific about how your innovation could contribute to improving public health.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, `text`, required: false): Here, provide more detailed information about your project, such as its goals, objectives, and any supporting data or research.
* **Collaboration** (`collaboration`, `select_multiple`, required: false): If you are collaborating with others, select the types of partners involved (Individuals, Startups, or Organizations) and list their names.

## Tips

* Be concise and clear in your answers to each field, ensuring that your proposal is well-understood by our team.
* If you are unsure about any field or require clarification, don't hesitate to contact us at `form@gic.mx`.
* Please fill in as much information as necessary, but avoid providing excessive or redundant details.
* This form is designed to be a starting point for our review and discussion, so feel free to elaborate on your ideas and concepts as you see fit.
