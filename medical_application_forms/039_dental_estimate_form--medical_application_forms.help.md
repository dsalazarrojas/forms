# Dental Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dental Estimate Form is designed to collect patient information, procedure details, and billing details for dental procedures. This form is intended to aid in providing estimates for patients and ensuring accurate billing information is collected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Select the relevant procedure from the options provided.
3. Enter the patient's cost estimate, if applicable.
4. Choose the insurance provider, if applicable.
5. Enter the patient's contact information, including phone number and address.
6. Provide the doctor's information, including date of appointment.
7. Specify the payment terms.
8. Enter any additional billing information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (<patient_info>**, Text, Required/Optional: Optional): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant details.
* **Procedure (<procedure>**, Select One, Required/Optional: Optional): Select the type of dental procedure being performed.
* **Cost Estimate (<cost_estimate>** Text, Required/Optional: Optional): Enter an estimate of the cost of the procedure.
* **Insurance Provider (<insurance_provider>**, Select One, Required/Optional: Optional): Choose the insurance provider for the patient.
* **Patient Phone (<patient_phone>**, Text, Required/Optional: Optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Patient Address (<patient_address>** Text, Required/Optional: Optional): Enter the patient's address.
* **Doctor Info (<doctor_info>** Text, Required/Optional: Optional): Enter the doctor's name and any other relevant information.
* **Appointment Date (<appointment_date>**, Date, Required/Optional: Optional): Enter the date of the appointment.
* **Payment Terms (<payment_terms>** Text, Required/Optional: Optional): Specify any payment terms or conditions.
* **Billing Information (<billing_information>** Text, Required/Optional: Optional): Enter any additional billing information.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
* If an insurance provider is selected, ensure that the billing information is up-to-date.
* Double-check the appointment date to ensure it aligns with the doctor's schedule.
* Review the payment terms carefully to ensure they align with your agreement.
* Keep the billing information up-to-date and accurate.
