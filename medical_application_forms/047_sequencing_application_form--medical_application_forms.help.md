Sequencing Application Form Help Guide
=====================================

### Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their sequencing needs and preferences. It helps us understand your requirements for sequencing, which will aid us in providing tailored services to meet your needs.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in the form fields as prompted.
2. Select the options that apply to your sequencing needs from the select_one, select_multiple, and select_one fields.
3. Make sure to enter a valid email address in the form_email field.
4. Complete all the fields as thoroughly as possible to ensure we have a clear understanding of your needs.

### Field-by-Field Explanation
Each field in the form has a specific purpose, as explained below:

* **form_title** (Text, Optional): Enter the title of your project or application.
* **form_name** (Text, Optional): Enter your name as you would like to be referred to.
* **form_last_name** (Text, Optional): Enter your last name as you would like to be referred to.
* **form_email** (Email, Optional): Enter a valid email address associated with your project or application.
* **form_phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **form_address** (Text, Optional): Enter your address for contact purposes.
* **form_city** (Text, Optional): Enter the city associated with your project or application.
* **form_state** (Select One, Optional): Select the option that best describes your project or application.
* **form_zip** (Text, Optional): Enter your postal code.
* **form_sequence_research** (Text, Optional): Provide details about your research or application.
* **form_sequence_terms** (Text, Optional): Enter any specific terms or conditions related to your project.
* **form_sequence_area** (Text, Optional): Enter the specific area of interest.
* **form_sequence_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of interest.
* **form_sequence_time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of interest.
* **form_sequence_duration** (Number, Optional): Enter the duration of your project.
* **form_sequence_research_details** (Text, Optional): Provide additional information about your research or application.
* **form_agreed** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the options that apply to your project or application.
* **form_sequence_agreement** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional agreements or terms related to your project.
* **form_sequence_terms** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional terms or conditions related to your project.
* **form_sequence_sign** (Text, Optional): Enter your signature.
* **form_sequence_verified** (Select One, Optional): Select the option that best describes the status of your project.
* **form_sequence_date_signed** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of signing.
* **form_sequence_signed_by** (Text, Optional): Enter the person signing.
* **form_sequence_signature** (Text, Optional): Enter your signature again.
* **form_sequence_submit** (Text, Optional): Enter your submission.

Please note that all fields are optional and are designed to help us understand your specific needs and preferences. Please fill in as much information as you can to enable us to provide better service.
