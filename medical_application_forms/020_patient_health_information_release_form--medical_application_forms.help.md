# patient-health-information-release-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a medical records release form, used to grant access to patient health information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of medical provider you want to grant access to your health information.
2. Choose the date you want to authorize the release of your medical records.
3. Select the type of access you want to grant (full or partial).
4. Choose the parties you want to grant access to your health information (healthcare providers, specialists, or others).
5. Specify the duration of the record you want to release (1, 5, or 10 years).
6. Enter the date you want to release your medical records.
7. Specify the date you want to expire the release.
8. Choose when you want the medical records to be released (immediately, upon release, or pending).
9. Select the methods to release your medical records (email, mail, or fax).
10. Enter any notes about the release of your medical records.
11. Specify the parties you want to release your medical records to (healthcare providers, specialists, or others).
12. Choose whether you want to keep your medical records (True or False).
13. Specify the storage method for your medical records (local, remote, or other).
14. Choose the authorized signer(s) for your medical records (patient, healthcare provider, or other).
15. Specify whether you want to sign the release (True or False).
16. Enter the date you sign the release.
17. Enter any notes for this form.
18. Specify the parties you want to release your medical records to (healthcare providers, specialists, or others).
19. Choose whether you want to store your medical records locally or remotely (True or False).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Healthcare Provider** (`form_1`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of healthcare provider you want to grant access to your health information (healthcare providers, specialists, or other).
* **Authorization Date** (`form_2`, `date`, required): Enter the date you want to authorize the release of your medical records.
* **Release Type** (`form_3`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of access you want to grant (full or partial).
* **Authorized Parties** (`form_4`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the parties you want to grant access to your health information (healthcare providers, specialists, or other).
* **Medical Records Notes** (`form_5`, `note`, required): Enter any notes about the release of your medical records.
* **Providers** (`form_6`, `select_multiple`, required): Specify the parties you want to release your medical records to (healthcare providers, specialists, or others).
* **Record Duration** (`form_7`, `number`, required): Specify the duration of the record you want to release (1, 5, or 10 years).
* **Release Limit** (`form_8`, `select_one`, required): Specify the release limit (1 year, 5 years, or 10 years).
* **Release Date** (`form_9`, `date`, required): Enter the date you want to release your medical records.
* **Expiration Date** (`form_10`, `date`, required): Enter the date you want to expire the release.
* **Medical Records Release** (`form_11`, `select_one`, required): Choose when you want the medical records to be released (immediately, upon release, or pending).
* **Release Method** (`form_12`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the methods to release your medical records (email, mail, or fax).
* **Medical Records Release Notes** (`form_13`, `note`, required): Enter any notes about the release of your medical records.
* **Release Recipient** (`form_14`, `select_multiple`, required): Specify the parties you want to release your medical records to (healthcare providers, specialists, or others).
* **Record Archival** (`form_15`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you want to keep your medical records (True or False).
* **Record Storage** (`form_16`, `select_multiple`, required): Specify the storage method for your medical records (local, remote, or other).
* **Authorized Signer** (`form_17`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the authorized signer(s) for your medical records (patient, healthcare provider, or other).
* **Signature** (`form_18`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you want to sign the release (True or False).
* **Signature Date** (`form_19`, `date`, required): Enter the date you sign the release.
* **Form 20** (`form_20`, `text`, required): Enter any additional information.
* **Notes** (`form_21`, `note`, required): Enter any notes about this form.
* **Medical Records Release Notes** (`form_22`, `note`, required): Enter any notes about the release of your medical records.
* **Form 23** (`form_23`, `select_multiple`, required): Specify the parties you want to release your medical records to (healthcare providers, specialists, or others).
* **Form 24** (`form_24`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you want to store your medical records locally or remotely (True or False).
* **Form 25** (`form_25`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is not required and does not have an explanation.
