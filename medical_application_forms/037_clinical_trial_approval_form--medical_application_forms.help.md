# Clinical Trial Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information for clinical trials, providing a structured and organized approach to ensure that all required details are collected and reviewed. It helps to streamline the approval process, reducing errors and ensuring that necessary documentation is in place before proceeding with the trial.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Principal Investigator Name field with your name as the principal investigator of the clinical trial.
2. Enter the Trial ID or Protocol Number, which is a unique identifier for the trial.
3. Choose the current approval stage of the trial from the dropdown menu, indicating the current status of the approval process.
4. Provide the Full Clinical Trial Title, which should include the title and a brief description of the trial.
5. Select the Phase of the Clinical Trial, which indicates the stage of the trial (Phase I, II, III, or IV).
6. Enter the Target Participant Enrollment Number, which is the expected number of participants in the trial.
7. Estimate the Total Trial Duration, choosing the duration from the provided options.
8. If applicable, choose whether an Adverse Event Reporting Plan has been attached.
9. Select whether the Informed Consent Template has been approved.
10. Indicate if a Conflict of Interest has been declared.
11. Enter the Date of Institutional Approval, if applicable.
12. Sign with your Authorized Name and Digital Signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Principal Investigator Name**: Enter your name as the principal investigator of the clinical trial. This is a required field.
* **Trial ID or Protocol Number**: Enter a unique identifier for the trial, which is used to track and identify the trial. This is a required field.
* **Current Approval Stage**: Choose the current status of the approval process from the dropdown menu:
	+ **Internal Review**: The trial is currently undergoing internal review.
	+ **Ethics Committee**: The trial has been reviewed by the ethics committee.
	+ **Regulatory Body**: The trial has received approval from a regulatory body.
	+ **Final Approval**: The trial has received final approval.
* **Full Clinical Trial Title**: Enter the title of the clinical trial, including a brief description.
* **Phase of Clinical Trial**: Select the stage of the trial:
	+ **Phase I**: The trial is in its first phase.
	+ **Phase II**: The trial is in its second phase.
	+ **Phase III**: The trial is in its third phase.
	+ **Phase IV**: The trial is in its fourth phase.
* **Target Participant Enrollment Number**: Enter the expected number of participants in the trial.
* **Estimated Total Trial Duration**: Choose the estimated duration of the trial:
	+ **6 months**: The trial is expected to last 6 months.
	+ **1 year**: The trial is expected to last 1 year.
	+ **2 years**: The trial is expected to last 2 years.
	+ **3 or more years**: The trial is expected to last 3 or more years.
* **Adverse Event Reporting Plan**: Indicate whether an adverse event reporting plan has been attached.
* **Informed Consent Template**: Select whether the informed consent template has been approved.
* **Conflict of Interest**: Indicate if a conflict of interest has been declared.
* **Date of Institutional Approval**: Enter the date of institutional approval, if applicable.
* **Authorized Signatory Name**: Enter your name as the authorized signatory.
* **Authorized Digital Signature**: Enter your digital signature, confirming your approval and commitment to the trial.
