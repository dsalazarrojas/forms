# Animal Nutrition Research Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from lead researchers about their proposed research projects in the field of animal nutrition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and title as the lead researcher on the first page.
2. Enter your academic institution or research center on the second page.
3. Provide a title for your research project on the third page.
4. Write a 500-word abstract of your proposed research on the fourth page.
5. Specify the nutrients or diets under study on the fifth page.
6. Enter the estimated study duration in months on the sixth page.
7. Enter the total budget requested for your project in USD on the seventh page.
8. Provide a breakdown of your budget on the eighth page.
9. Indicate the status of IACUC or ethical approval for your project on the ninth page.
10. List your top 3 recent publications in animal nutrition on the 10th page (if applicable).
11. Enter your contact email address on the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Lead Researcher Name** (text, required): Enter your name as the lead researcher on the project.
- **Academic Institution or Research Center** (text, required): Type your institution or research center's name.
- **Research Project Title** (text, required): Provide a concise title for your research project.
- **Abstract of Proposed Research** (text, required): Write a 500-word abstract of your proposed research. Keep in mind that this is a brief summary of your project.
- **Specific Nutrients or Diets under Study** (text, required): Specify the nutrients or diets that you will be studying in your project.
- **Estimated Study Duration - Months** (number, required): Enter the estimated duration of your study in months.
- **Total Budget Requested** (number, required): Enter the total budget requested for your project in USD.
- **Use of Funds Breakdown** (text, required): Briefly explain how you plan to use the funds requested.
- **IACUC or Ethical Approval Status** (select_one, required): Indicate the status of IACUC or ethical approval for your project (Approved, Pending, Not yet submitted, Not applicable).
- **Recent Publications in Animal Nutrition** (text, optional): If you have published recently in animal nutrition, list your top 3 publications.
- **Contact Email** (email, required): Enter your email address for further communication.

## Tips
- Review each field carefully before submitting your form.
- Ensure that you have all necessary information ready before starting to fill in the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the explanations above.
- Double-check your contact email address for accuracy.
- Make sure to proofread your abstract and other text fields for grammar and spelling errors.
