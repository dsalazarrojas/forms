# patient_bio_fact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The patient bio fact form is used to collect basic demographic information from patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name**.
2. Enter your **Last Name**.
3. Enter your **Email** in the format "username@domain.com".
4. Enter your **Phone Number** (with your country code).
5. Enter your **Medical ID** number.
6. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the format "YYYY-MM-DD" (e.g. 1999-01-01).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your given name (e.g. John).
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname (e.g. Doe).
* **Email**: Enter your email address that you use for communication.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number, including the country code (e.g. +1-123-456-7890).
* **Medical ID**: Enter your unique patient identification number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format "YYYY-MM-DD" (e.g. 1999-01-01).

## Tips
* Be accurate and truthful when filling in your personal details.
* Ensure that your email and phone number are up-to-date and correct.
* If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please indicate this in the medical record or consult with your medical staff member.
* If you are unsure about any information, please ask your medical staff member for guidance.
