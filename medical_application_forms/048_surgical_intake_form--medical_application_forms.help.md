# Surgical Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Surgical Intake Form is a medical form designed to collect relevant patient information and medical history prior to surgical procedures. It helps medical professionals make informed decisions and provide optimal care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Answer each question honestly and accurately to the best of your ability**. If you are unsure or need clarification about any question, please ask your medical professional for assistance.
2. **Fill out all required fields**. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*) in their labels.
3. **Use clear and concise writing** when answering questions, especially those with text inputs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Patient Information**
* **First Name and Last Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `text`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required): Enter your contact information, including your address, phone number, and email (if applicable).
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, `text`, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., phone, email, or in-person).
* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, `text`, required): Enter your patient ID, if you have one.

### **Contact Information**
* **Relationship to Patient** (`relationship`, `text`, required): Describe your relationship to the patient (e.g., parent, guardian, or self).
* **Contact Name and Number** (`contact_name_and_number`, `text`, required): Enter the name and phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `text`, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.

### **Medical History**
* **Past Medical Conditions** (`past_medical_conditions`, `text`, required): Describe any past medical conditions or illnesses you have experienced.
* **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, required): List all medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, required): Describe any allergies or sensitivities you have.

### **Allergies**
* **List Allergens** (`allergens`, `text`, required): List all substances or materials you are allergic or sensitive to.

### **Medical Conditions**
* **Current Medical Conditions** (`current_medical_conditions`, `text`, required): Describe your current medical conditions or illnesses.

### **Surgical History**
* **Past Surgeries** (`past_surgeries`, `text`, required): Describe any past surgical procedures you have undergone.

### **Consent**
* **Consent to Treatment** (`consent_to_treatment`, `text`, required): Confirm that you have read, understand, and agree to the terms of treatment.

### **Medication History**
* **Medication History** (`medication_history`, `text`, required): Describe your current and past medication history.

### **Tips**
* **Please be honest and accurate**. Inaccurate or incomplete information may lead to misdiagnosis or inadequate treatment.
* **If you need help or clarification** on any question, please ask your medical professional for assistance.
