# Chiropractic Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chiropractic Intake Form is a medical form designed to collect necessary information from patients for initial consultation or first visit. This form helps the chiropractor assess and create a personalized treatment plan tailored to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your **Patient Full Name**, including any known aliases or nicknames.
2.  Enter your **Contact Information**, including phone number and email for appointment scheduling.
3.  Enter your **Date of Birth** for medical records purposes.
4.  List your **Current Medications**, including dosage and frequency, if applicable.
5.  Describe your **Medical History**, including past medical conditions, surgeries, or allergies.
6.  Provide a detailed **Description of Symptoms**, including where and how you experience them.
7.  Mention any **Prior Treatments Tried**, including previous therapies or surgeries.
8.  Provide your **Insurance Information**, including provider and policy number.
9.  Enter the **Emergency Contact** information, including name and phone number.
10. Select your **Consent to Treatment**, agreeing to the chiropractic treatment plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your phone number and email for scheduling appointments with your healthcare provider.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth for medical records purposes.
* **Current Medications**: List any medications, including dosage and frequency.
* **Medical History**: Describe past medical conditions, allergies, or surgeries that may affect your treatment.
* **Description of Symptoms**: Where and how you experience symptoms.
* **Prior Treatments Tried**: Any previous therapies or surgeries.
* **Insurance Information**: Enter your insurance provider and policy number.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact.
* **Consent to Treatment**: Select "I consent" to agree to the chiropractic treatment plan.
