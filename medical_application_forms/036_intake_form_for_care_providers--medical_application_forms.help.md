# Intake Form For Care Providers - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Intake Form For Care Providers is a comprehensive form designed to gather essential information from care providers about their patients. This form helps ensure that all necessary details are collected and documented accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to understand the purpose and requirements.
2. Complete each question according to your knowledge or documentation.
3. Ensure that you answer all required questions accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **patient_info (Text, 1, False)** 
Enter the patient's demographic information, including their name, address, and contact details.

### 2. **medical_history (Text, 2, False)** 
Describe the patient's medical history, including any relevant medical conditions, allergies, or previous treatments.

### 3. **agreement_form (Text, 3, False)** 
Confirm that you have obtained the patient's agreement to participate in care.

### 4. **care_team (Select Multiple, 4, False)** 
Indicate whether the patient has a care team or support system in place.

### 5. **facility_questions (Text, 5, False)** 
Answer any facility-related questions, such as emergency contact information or insurance details.

### 6. **patient_statement (Select One, 6, False)** 
Indicate whether the patient has a statement or declaration form on file.

### 7. **medical_record (Select Multiple, 7, False)** 
Confirm whether the patient has a medical record or history.

### 8. **emergency_contacts (Select Multiple, 8, False)** 
List the patient's emergency contacts, including names and contact details.

### 9. **insurance (Select One, 9, False)** 
Indicate whether the patient has insurance coverage.

### 10. **billing_information (Number, 10, False)** 
Provide the patient's billing information, including account numbers or billing addresses.

### 11. **payment_method (Select One, 11, False)** 
Indicate the payment method used for billing.

### 12. **total_servic_costs (Number, 12, False)** 
Estimate the total service costs for the patient.

### 13. **billing_agreement (Select One, 13, False)** 
Confirm that you have obtained the patient's billing agreement.

### 14. **patient_agreement (Select One, 14, False)** 
Confirm that you have obtained the patient's agreement to participate.

### 15. **patient_signature (Text, 15, False)** 
Obtain the patient's signature on the agreement form.

### 16. **care_provider_info (Select Multiple, 16, False)** 
Provide information about the care provider's qualifications and experience.

### 17. **facility_signature (Text, 17, False)** 
Obtain the facility's signature on the agreement form.

### 18. **patient_id (Number, 18, False)** 
Enter the patient's ID number.

### 19. **patient_dob (Date, 19, False)** 
Enter the patient's date of birth.

### 20. **patient_phone_number (Text, 20, False)** 
Enter the patient's phone number.

### 21. **patient_email (Email, 21, False)** 
Enter the patient's email address.

### 22. **patient_primary_contact_name (Text, 22, False)** 
Enter the name of the patient's primary contact.

### 23. **patient_primary_contact_number (Text, 23, False)** 
Enter the phone number of the patient's primary contact.

### 24. **patient_primary_contact_email (Email, 24, False)** 
Enter the email address of the patient's primary contact.

### 25. **patient_primary_contact_address (Text, 25, False)** 
Enter the address of the patient's primary contact.
