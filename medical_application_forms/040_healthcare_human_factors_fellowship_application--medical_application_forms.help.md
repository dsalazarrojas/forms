# Healthcare Human Factors Fellowship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Healthcare Human Factors Fellowship Application form is designed to collect information from applicants for the Healthcare Human Factors Fellowship program. The form is used to gather details about the applicant's background, experience, education, and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the fellowship program requirements and guidelines before starting the application.
2.  Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
3.  Ensure that all required fields are completed.
4.  Upload any supporting documents as required.
5.  Proofread the form for errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Applicant Information (1)**: This section is used to capture basic information about the applicant, including their name and contact details. Please enter your name and email address accurately.
*   **Application Form 1 (2)**: This section is used to capture additional information about the applicant. Please enter relevant details, such as research experience or academic background. Enter text in this field to provide further details.
*   **Application Form 2 (3)**: This section is another opportunity to capture further details about the applicant. Please enter any relevant experiences, skills, or achievements.
*   **Resume (4)**: Attach a copy of your resume to support your application. Please ensure it is up-to-date and relevant to the fellowship program.
*   **Personal Statement (5)**: Please provide a personal statement (about 1-2 pages) describing your interests, goals, and motivations for participating in the fellowship program.
*   **Education (6)**: List your educational background, including degrees earned and institutions attended.
*   **Experience (7)**: Describe your relevant work experience, including job roles and dates of employment.
*   **Documents (8)**: Attach any supporting documents, such as letters of recommendation or certificates of completion, to support your application.
