# Home Health Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for healthcare providers or home health agencies to gather information for patient referrals, assessments, or other home health-related services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the patient's name in the **Patient Name** field.
2. Enter the patient's address in the **Patient Address** field.
3. Provide the patient's phone number in the **Patient Phone** field.
4. Enter the referral date in the **Referral Date** field.
5. Select the referring doctor in the **Referring Doctor** field.
6. Enter the patient's medical condition in the **Patient Condition** field.
7. Provide a brief medical history in the **Medical History** field.
8. Enter the home health agency's name in the **Home Health Agency** field.
9. Enter the contact number of the home health agency in the **Home Health Agency Contact Number** field.
10. Select the healthcare provider in the **Healthcare Provider** field.
11. Enter the patient's age in the **Patient Age** field.
12. Enter the referral reason in the **Referral Reason** field.
13. Enter the patient's date of birth in the **Patient DOB** field.
14. Select the home health service in the **Home Health Service** field.
15. Enter the home health agency's referral form ID in the **Home Health Referral Form ID** field.
16. (Optional) Enter an additional contact number in the **Contact Number 2** field.
17. (Optional) Select an additional healthcare provider in the **Healthcare Provider 2** field.
18. (Optional) Enter the patient's medical condition again in the **Patient Medical Condition** field.
19. (Optional) Enter the patient's medical record number in the **Medical Record Number** field.
20. (Optional) Enter the patient's medical record number copy in the **Medical Record Number (Copy)** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name.
* **Patient Address**: Enter the patient's address for home health visits.
* **Patient Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number for communication.
* **Referral Date**: Enter the date the referral was made.
* **Referring Doctor**: Select the doctor who referred the patient.
* **Patient Condition**: Enter the patient's current medical condition.
* **Medical History**: Provide a brief summary of the patient's medical history.
* **Home Health Agency**: Enter the name of the home health agency.
* **Home Health Agency Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the home health agency.
* **Healthcare Provider**: Select the healthcare provider who will be providing service.
* **Patient Age**: Enter the patient's age.
* **Referral Reason**: Enter the reason for the referral.
* **Patient DOB**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Contact Number 2**: Enter an additional contact number for the patient.
* **Home Health Service**: Select the type of home health service required.
* **Home Health Referral Form ID**: Enter the unique ID of the home health referral form.
* **Patient Medical Condition**: Enter the patient's medical condition again (if necessary).
* **Medical Record Number**: Enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Medical Record Number (Copy)**: Enter a copy of the patient's medical record number.
* **Healthcare Provider 2**: Select an additional healthcare provider.
* **Home Health Agency Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the home health agency again.
* **Referral Date 2**: Enter the date of the referral again (if necessary).
* **Patient Phone 2**: Enter the patient's phone number again (if necessary).
* **Home Health Referral Form ID 2**: Enter the unique ID of the home health referral form again (if necessary).

## Tips

* Ensure to fill out each field accurately and completely.
* Use the **Patient DOB** and **Patient Age** fields to help verify the patient's identity.
* The **Medical Record Number** and **Medical Record Number (Copy)** fields are for your reference and internal use.
* Double-check the form before submitting it to ensure it's complete and accurate.
