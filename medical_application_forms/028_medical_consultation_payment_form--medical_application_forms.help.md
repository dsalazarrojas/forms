# Medical Consultation Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Consultation Payment Form is designed for patients to provide payment information for their medical consultations. It helps healthcare providers to track and manage payments efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Medical Consultation Date**: Enter the date of your medical consultation.
2. **Patient Information**:
	* Enter your details to ensure that you are correctly billed for the consultation.
3. **Payment Method**: Select one of the following payment methods:
	* **Cash**: Payment made in cash.
	* **Card**: Payment made through a credit or debit card.
	* **Bank Transfer**: Payment made through a bank transfer.
4. **Payment Amount**: Enter the total amount paid for the consultation.
5. **Billing Provider Name**: Enter the name of your billing provider.
6. **Patient Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your medical records.
7. **Patient Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
8. **Patient E-mail**: Enter your email address.
9. **Medical Service Rendered**: Enter a brief description of the medical service rendered.
10. **Payment Status**: Select one of the following options:
	* **Paid**: Payment has been made in full.
	* **Unpaid**: Payment has not been made.
	* **Partial**: Partial payment has been made.
11. **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the payment.
