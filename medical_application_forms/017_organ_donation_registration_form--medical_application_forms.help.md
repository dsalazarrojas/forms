# Organ Donation Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organ Donation Registration Form is a medical application form for individuals who wish to register themselves as organ donors. This form collects personal and medical information to facilitate the organ donation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name, email address, and contact phone number in the "Registrant Name," "Email," and "Contact Phone" fields.
2. Select your blood type from the "Blood Type" dropdown menu.
3. If you have any medical conditions, select them from the "Donor Disease" dropdown menu.
4. Enter your medical provider's information in the "Medical Provider" field.
5. Enter your address, city, state, and zip code in the "Address," "City," "State," and "Zip" fields.
6. Select your emergency contact from the "Emergency Contact" dropdown menu.
7. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
8. If you have multiple contact phone numbers, enter them in the "Contact Phone 1" and "Contact Phone 2" fields.
9. If you have multiple contact numbers, enter them in the "Contact Number 1" and "Contact Number 2" fields.
10. Enter any relevant medical history in the "Medical History" field.
11. Enter the date of your medical condition in the "Medical Condition" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registrant Name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your active email address for communication purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your primary contact phone number.
* **Blood Type** (Select One, Required): Select your blood type from the dropdown menu.
* **Donor Disease** (Select Multiple, Optional): If you have any medical conditions, select them from the dropdown menu.
* **Medical Condition** (Date, Required): Enter the date of your medical condition.
* **Medical History** (Note, Required): Enter any relevant medical history.
* **Emergency Contact** (Select One, Required): Select your emergency contact from the dropdown menu.
* **Contact Email** (Email, Required): Enter your active email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone 1/2** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional contact phone numbers.
* **Contact Number 1/2** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional contact numbers.
* **Medical Provider** (Text, Required): Enter your medical provider's information.
* **Address** (Text, Optional): Enter your full address.
* **City** (Text, Required): Enter your city.
* **State** (Select One, Required): Select your state from the dropdown menu.
* **Zip** (Number, Required): Enter your zip code.
