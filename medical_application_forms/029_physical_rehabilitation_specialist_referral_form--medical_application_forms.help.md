# Physical Rehabilitation Specialist Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate referrals to physical rehabilitation specialists, ensuring that patients receive the necessary care and support for their recovery and rehabilitation needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Patient info**: Enter the patient's basic details, including their name, date of birth, and contact information.
2. **Contact info**: Provide the patient's contact information, including their address, phone number, and email.
3. **Provider info**: Enter your own contact information as the healthcare provider referring the patient.
4. **Referral info**: Describe the reason for referral, including the patient's medical condition and the expected treatment plan.
5. **Medical info**: Enter any relevant medical information about the patient, such as their medical history or current conditions.
6. **Rehab info**: Provide details about the patient's rehabilitation needs and goals.
7. **Referral notes**: Add any additional comments or instructions for the rehabilitation specialist.
8. **Patient signature**: Have the patient sign and date this section, indicating their agreement to be referred.
9. **Provider signature**: Sign and date this section as the healthcare provider referring the patient.
