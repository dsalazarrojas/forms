# medical_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The medical_intake_form is a form designed to collect basic personal and medical information from patients. It is intended to aid in the diagnosis and treatment of medical conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field using the MM/DD/YYYY format.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. If you have any previous medical history, enter it in the "Medical History" field.
6. If you have undergone any past surgeries, enter them in the "Past Surgeries" field.
7. If you have any genetic information you would like to share, enter it in the "Genetics" field.
8. If you are experiencing any symptoms, enter a description of them in the "Symptoms" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name** (<name>, <text>): Enter your full name.
*   **Date of Birth** (<date>, required/optional): Enter your date of birth using the MM/DD/YYYY format. (MM = month, DD = day, YYYY = year)
*   **Phone Number** (<phone_number, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
*   **Email** (<email, required/optional>): Enter your email address.
*   **Medical History** (<text>, required/optional): If you have any previous medical history, enter it here.
*   **Past Surgeries** (<text, required/optional>): If you have undergone any past surgeries, enter them here.
*   **Genetics** (<text, required/optional>): If you have any genetic information you would like to share, enter it here.
*   **Symptoms** (<text, required/optional>): If you are experiencing any symptoms, enter a description of them here.

## Tips
*   Double-check that all required fields are filled in accurately before submitting the form.
*   Ensure all entered information is accurate and up-to-date.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, ask your healthcare provider for assistance.
