# Patient Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Patient Intake Form is a medical application form designed to collect essential information from patients for medical records and intake processes. It is intended to provide a comprehensive overview of the patient's history, health status, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the **Patient Name** field.
2.  Provide your date of birth in the **Date of Birth** field.
3.  Enter your medical record number if you are an existing patient.
4.  Select your **Medical Record Type** from the provided options.
5.  Choose your **Medical Specialty** that best describes your condition or visit reason.
6.  Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field for contact purposes.
7.  Provide your email address in the **Email** field for communication.
8.  Select your **Insurance Provider** from the provided options.
9.  Enter any relevant **Medical Record Notes** in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Name**: Enter your full name as it is written on your identification documents.
*   **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
*   **Medical Record Number**: Enter your existing medical record number, if applicable.
*   **Medical Record Type**: Choose from the options:
    *   **New Patient**: You are visiting us for the first time.
    *   **Existing Patient**: You already have a medical record with us.
    *   **Unknown Patient**: You are unsure or cannot remember your medical record status.
*   **Medical Specialty**: Select the medical specialty that best describes your condition or visit reason:
    *   **Cardiology**: Heart-related conditions.
    *   **Dermatology**: Skin-related conditions.
    *   **Endocrinology**: Hormone-related conditions.
    *   **Gastroenterology**: Digestive system conditions.
    *   **General Practice**: General medical concerns.
    *   **Nephrology**: Kidney-related conditions.
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address for communication and correspondence.
*   **Insurance Provider**: Choose from the options:
    *   **Private**: Private insurance provider.
    *   **Medicare**: Medicare insurance provider.
    *   **Medicaid**: Medicaid insurance provider.
    *   **Tricare**: Tricare insurance provider.
    *   **Other**: Other insurance provider.
*   **Medical Record Notes**: Enter any relevant medical record notes or history.
