# Mental Health Service Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Service Claim Form is a document used to gather information about mental health services received by patients. This form helps to ensure that the necessary details are documented accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Patient Information (page 1)**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant identifying information.
2. **Claim Type (page 2)**: Select the type of mental health service being claimed (e.g. therapy session, medication, etc.).
3. **Service Date (page 3)**: Enter the date(s) on which the mental health service was provided.
4. **Provider Information (page 4)**: Enter the name and contact information of the healthcare provider who provided the service.
5. **Claim Information (page 5)**: Enter a brief description of the service provided and any other relevant details.
6. **Claim Amount (page 6)**: Enter the total amount claimed for the service.
7. **Supporting Documents (page 7)**: Attach any supporting documents, such as receipts or invoices, that validate the claim.
8. **Notes (page 8)**: Add any additional comments or explanations about the claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information (page 1)**:
	+ **Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification document (e.g. ID, passport, etc.).
	+ **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Claim Type (page 2)**:
	+ **Mental Health Service**: Select this option if the claim is for mental health services (e.g. therapy, counseling, etc.).
	+ **Other Service**: Select this option if the claim is for a different type of service not covered under mental health (e.g. medical services, etc.).
* **Service Date (page 3)**:
	+ Enter the date(s) on which the mental health service was provided. You can enter multiple dates if the service was provided on multiple dates.
* **Provider Information (page 4)**:
	+ **Provider Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider who provided the service.
	+ **Provider Contact Information**: Enter the provider's contact information, including phone number and email address.
* **Claim Information (page 5)**:
	+ Enter a brief description of the service provided and any other relevant details.
* **Claim Amount (page 6)**:
	+ Enter the total amount claimed for the service.
* **Supporting Documents (page 7)**:
	+ Attach any supporting documents, such as receipts or invoices, that validate the claim.
* **Notes (page 8)**:
	+ Add any additional comments or explanations about the claim.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, as this information will be used to process the claim.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the patient's medical record or contact the healthcare provider directly.
* Ensure that you have the patient's consent to fill out this form on their behalf.
* Attach any supporting documents that validate the claim.
