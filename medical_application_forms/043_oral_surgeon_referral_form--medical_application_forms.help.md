# Oral Surgeon Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Oral Surgeon Referral Form is designed for referring healthcare professionals to provide medical information about their patients to the Oral Surgery department at the hospital. This form is used to facilitate the evaluation and treatment of patients by the oral surgery team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields (First Name, Last Name, Contact Number, and Email) with the relevant information for the patient you are referring.
* Enter the patient's First Name in the "Patient First Name" field. This will be the name of the patient.
* Enter the patient's Last Name in the "Patient Last Name" field. This will be the last name of the patient.
* Enter the patient's Contact Number in the "Contact Number" field. This can be a phone number or any other contact method (e.g., pager, etc.).
* Enter the patient's Email in the "Email" field. This will be the patient's email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`patient_first_name`, text, required): The patient's first name.
* **Last Name** (`patient_last_name`, text, required): The patient's last name.
* **Referring Doctor** (`doctor_referring_doctor`, text, optional): The name of the doctor who is referring the patient.
* **Contact Number** (`patient_contact_phone`, text, required): The patient's contact information (phone number, pager, etc.).
* **Email** (`patient_email`, email, required): The patient's email address.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information about the patient.
* If the patient does not have an email address, leave the "Email" field blank.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask a supervisor or the Oral Surgery department for guidance.
