# Medical Biller Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and address information required for a medical application. It is intended for individuals who are applying for medical services or billing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete the form, start by entering your First and Last Name in the designated field.
2. Next, enter your address in the provided field.
3. Then, provide your contact number, which should be a valid phone number or email address.
4. Finally, enter your Date of Birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First and Last Name** (text, required): Enter your full name, including first name and last name, to enable us to accurately identify you in our records.
* **Address** (text, optional): Enter your current address to help us contact you or send you information related to your medical application.
* **Contact Number** (text, optional): Enter a valid phone number or email address where we can contact you for further information or to update you on your medical application status.
* **Date of Birth** (date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY, as this will help us verify your age and ensure you meet the requirements for medical services or billing purposes.
