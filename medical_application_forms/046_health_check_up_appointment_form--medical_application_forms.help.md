# health_check_up_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The health_check_up_appointment_form is a critical form used to schedule and manage patient appointments. It collects essential information about the patient, their appointment preferences, and medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the field labeled "Name."
2. Enter your email address in the field labeled "Email" (optional but recommended).
3. Enter your phone number in the field labeled "Phone Number" (required).
4. Select the appointment type from the list provided in the field labeled "Appointment Type" (optional).
5. Enter the date of your appointment in the format YYYY-MM-DD in the field labeled "Appointment Date" (required).
6. Enter the time of your appointment in the format HH:MM in the field labeled "Appointment Time" (required).
7. Provide a brief note about your appointment in the field labeled "Note" (optional).
8. Enter any additional patient information in the field labeled "Patient Information" (required).
9. If applicable, describe your relationship with the patient in the field labeled "Patient Relationship" (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (required): Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (optional): Enter your email address for easy communication with our team.
* **Phone Number** (required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Appointment Type** (optional): Select the type of appointment you're scheduling.
	+ 'Yes': Regular check-up
	+ 'No': Other (please specify in the "Note" field)
* **Appointment Date** (required): Enter the date of your appointment in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Appointment Time** (required): Enter the time of your appointment in the format HH:MM.
* **Note** (optional): Provide any additional information about your appointment.
* **Patient Information** (required): Enter any relevant medical history or details about your condition.
* **Patient Relationship** (optional): If you have a relationship with the patient (e.g., family member or caregiver), describe it here.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* For any issues with your appointment, kindly reach out to us as soon as possible to avoid delays.
