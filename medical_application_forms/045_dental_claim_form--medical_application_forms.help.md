# Dental Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dental claim form is a tool used to submit claims for reimbursement of dental services provided to patients. It is an essential part of the medical application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Gather all the necessary information required for the claim, including patient details, medical information, billing details, and claim status.
2. Ensure that you are submitting a claim that has not already been submitted or processed.
3. Select the claim status and type from the respective dropdown menus.
4. Enter the patient's date of birth, name, and phone number.
5. Enter the billing provider's name and address.
6. Enter the billing date and time.
7. Enter the billing amount.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_info**: Enter the patient's general information such as address and contact number.
* **medical_info**: Provide any relevant medical information related to the claim.
* **billing_info**: Enter the billing details, including the provider's name and address.
* **medical_history**: Enter any medical history relevant to the claim.
* **claim_status**: Select the current status of the claim from the dropdown menu (Active, Pending, Closed).
* **claim_type**: Select the type of claim from the dropdown menu (Claim, Appeal, Amendment).
* **patient_date_of_birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **patient_name**: Enter the patient's name.
* **patient_address**: Enter the patient's address.
* **billing_provider_name**: Enter the name of the billing provider.
* **billing_provider_address**: Enter the address of the billing provider.
* **patient_phone**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **billing_date**: Enter the date of the billing.
* **billing_time**: Enter the time of the billing.
* **billing_amount**: Enter the amount due for the claim.

## Tips
- Double-check all information before submitting the claim to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, please refer to our documentation or contact our support team.
