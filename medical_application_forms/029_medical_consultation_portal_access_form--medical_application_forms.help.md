# Medical Consultation Portal Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Consultation Portal Access Form is a gateway for patients to access the Medical Consultation Portal. This form will help to filter and categorize the patients based on their medical needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form accurately and completely.
2. Select the correct options for the "medical_provider" field, which should be the doctor you are interested in consulting.
3. Select the correct medical specialty from the "medical_specialty" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
6. Choose your preferred language from the "preferred_language" field.
7. Select the "remember me" option to save your login information on the portal.
8. Check the "Medical Consultation Portal Terms" field to ensure you understand the terms of use.
9. Select the "I agree to the terms of use" option to indicate your agreement.
10. Fill in any additional comments in the "Medical Consultation Portal Additional Comments" fields.
