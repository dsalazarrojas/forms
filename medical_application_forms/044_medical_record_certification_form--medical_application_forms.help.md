# Medical Record Certification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect accurate and detailed information from patients for certification purposes in medical settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

* Please ensure that you have filled out all the fields marked as "required".
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with a healthcare professional.
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact a healthcare professional for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Field 1** (`field_1`, `text`, required): Enter your name, as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Field 2** (`second_field`, `text`, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Field 3** (`third_field`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information about your medical history or condition.
* **Field 4** (`field_4`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your primary care physician or healthcare provider.
* **Field 5** (`fifth_field`, `select_one`, required): Answer "Yes" if you have any current medical conditions that need to be disclosed, or "No" otherwise.
* **Field 6** (`sixth_field`, `select_multiple`, required): List any current medications you are taking.
