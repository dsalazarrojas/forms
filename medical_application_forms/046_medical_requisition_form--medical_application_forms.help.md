# Medical Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Requisition Form is a tool used by medical professionals to request medical supplies, equipment, or medications for patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of equipment or supplies you are requesting.
2. Enter the patient's ID number.
3. Choose the medical professional who will be requisitioning the supplies or equipment.
4. Select the date and time of the requisition.
5. Add any additional comments or information about the request.
6. Enter your name for the person who submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment Requested** (`Equipment Requested`, select_multiple): Choose the type of equipment or supplies you are requesting. Select all that apply.
* **Patient ID** (`Patient ID`, text): Enter the patient's ID number.
* **Medical Requisitioner** (`Medical Requisitioner`, select_one): Choose the medical professional who will be requisitioning the supplies or equipment.
* **Requisition Date** (`Requisition Date`, date): Enter the date you are requesting the supplies or equipment.
* **Requisition Time** (`Requisition Time`, time): Enter the time you are requesting the supplies or equipment.
* **Requisition Time HH-MM** (`Requisition Time HH-MM`, time): Enter the time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text): Add any additional comments or information about the request.
* **Requisitioned By** (`Requisitioned By`, text): Enter your name.
* **Date Submitted** (`Date Submitted`, date): Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **Time Submitted** (`Time Submitted`, time): Enter the time you submitted the form.
* **Form ID** (`Form ID`, text): Not used in this form.

## Tips

* Only authorized medical professionals should complete this form.
* Make sure to select all applicable equipment or supply types.
* Double-check the patient's ID number for accuracy.
* Enter the correct date and time of the requisition.
* Use clear and concise language in the comments field.
* Enter your name correctly in the requisitioned by field.
* Make sure to submit the form after completing all required fields.
