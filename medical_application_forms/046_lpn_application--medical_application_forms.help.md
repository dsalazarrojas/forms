# LPN Application - Help Guide
## Purpose

This LPN Application form is designed to collect essential information from LPNs for medical professional licensure, certification, or registration purposes. This form helps to ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information to the relevant medical board or regulatory agency.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer all questions honestly and accurately.
2. Ensure you have all necessary information and documents before starting.
3. Enter your answers in the required format, such as dates in MM/DD/YYYY format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Application Form** (`medical_application_form`):
	+ A brief description of your medical practice or interest in practicing as an LPN.
	+ This is a free-text field for you to provide an overview of your medical background and experience.
* **Personal Details** (`personal_details`):
	+ Provide your full name, address, and contact information.
	+ This information will be used to contact you and verify your identity.
* **Education Background** (`education_background`):
	+ List your education level (e.g., Diploma, Associate's Degree, Bachelor's Degree).
	+ Provide the highest level of education completed and relevant courses or training received.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`):
	+ Describe your work experience as an LPN, including job title, employer, and dates of employment.
	+ List any relevant certifications, licenses, or special training received.
* **Licensure** (`licensure`):
	+ If applicable, provide your current medical licensure status (e.g., licensed, registered, certified).
	+ List any lapsed or inactive licenses, and the reason for their status.
* **References** (`references`):
	+ List two professional references, including their name, title, and contact information.
	+ These references should be able to vouch for your medical practice and character.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`):
	+ Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
	+ This person should be able to reach you in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Licensure** (`medical_licensing`):
	+ If applicable, provide your current medical licensure status (e.g., licensed, registered, certified).
	+ List any lapsed or inactive licenses, and the reason for their status.
* **Professional Memberships** (`professional_memberships`):
	+ List any professional memberships or associations related to the LPN profession.
	+ These memberships can help demonstrate your commitment to ongoing education and professional development.
* **Professional Liability Insurance** (`professional_liability_insurance`):
	+ If applicable, provide your current professional liability insurance status (e.g., policy number, expiration date).
	+ This information is essential for your medical practice and liability coverage.
* **Medical Board Certifications** (`medical_board_certifications`):
	+ If applicable, list any medical board certifications or special training received.
	+ These certifications can enhance your medical practice and demonstrate expertise.
* **Medical Board Certifications Details** (`medical_board_certifications_details`):
	+ Provide a brief description of your medical board certifications or special training.
	+ This information can help demonstrate your commitment to ongoing education and professional development.
* **Certification** (`certification`):
	+ If applicable, provide your current certification status (e.g., certified, registered).
	+ List any lapsed or inactive certifications, and the reason for their status.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`):
	+ List your education level (e.g., Diploma, Associate's Degree, Bachelor's Degree).
	+ This information is essential for your medical practice and licensure.
* **Highest Level of Education Completed** (`highest_level_of_education_completed`):
	+ Provide the highest level of education completed (e.g., diploma, associate's degree, bachelor's degree).
	+ This information can help demonstrate your commitment to ongoing education and professional development.
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`):
	+ If applicable, provide the graduation date of your highest level of education completed.
	+ This information can help verify your education level and completion.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`):
	+ Provide your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
	+ This information is essential for your medical practice and licensure.
* **Date of Graduation** (`date_of_graduation`):
	+ If applicable, provide the date of graduation for your highest level of education completed.
	+ This information can help verify your education level and completion.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`):
	+ If applicable, provide the start date of your medical practice or employment.
	+ This information can help verify your work experience and licensure.
* **End Date** (`end_date`):
	+ If applicable, provide the end date of your medical practice or employment.
	+ This information can help demonstrate your work experience and licensure.
* **Date** (`date`):
	+ If applicable, provide any relevant dates (e.g., certification date, graduation date).
	+ This information can help verify your education, licensure, or certifications.
