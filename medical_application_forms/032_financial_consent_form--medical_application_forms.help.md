# Financial Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Consent Form is a document used to obtain financial consent from patients for medical services. It serves as a critical component in the billing and payment process of medical practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information, including their name and address.
2. Enter the practitioner's information, including their name and address.
3. Enter the fee amount for the services provided.
4. Select the payment terms and fee terms.
5. Confirm the signatures of both the patient and practitioner.
