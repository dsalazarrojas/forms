# physical-therapy-appointment-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Physical Therapy Appointment Form. This form is used to collect necessary information from patients before scheduling a physical therapy appointment. The information will be reviewed and confirmed by the physician before the appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the patient's information on the "Patient Information" page.
2. Select an appointment date on the "Select an appointment date" field.
3. Choose a physician preference on the "Physician preference" field.
4. Enter the patient's phone number and email address in their respective fields.
5. Select the time slots on the "Select time slots" field.
6. If applicable, select an appointment time on the "Select an appointment time" field.
7. Add any comments about the appointment on the "Comments" field.
8. Enter any notes for the physician on the "Patient Notes" field.
9. Finally, enter any additional notes for the physician on the "Physician Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (Patient Information, Text, Required): Enter the patient's name, age, and any other relevant medical information.
* **Select an appointment date** (appointment_date, Date, Required): Select a date for the appointment to be scheduled.
* **Physician preference** (physician_preference, Select One, Required): Choose the preference of the physician who will be seeing the patient. Options include: Cardiovascular, Neurology, and Orthopedic.
* **Phone number** (phone, Text, Required): Enter the patient's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (email, Email, Required): Enter the patient's email address for contact purposes.
* **Select time slots** (time_slots, Select Multiple, Required): Choose the time slots available for the appointment.
* **Select an appointment time** (appointment_time, Time, Required): If an appointment time is available, select a time for the appointment.
* **Comments** (comments, Note, Optional): Add any comments about the appointment.
* **Patient Notes** (patient_notes, Note, Optional): Enter any notes for the patient.
* **Physician Notes** (physician_notes, Note, Optional): Enter any notes for the physician.

## Tips

* Be sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the appointment proceeds smoothly.
* Make sure to select the correct physician preference and time slots for the appointment.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact the physician or staff.
