# Breast Surgery Training Program Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your background information, professional experience, and motivation for participating in the breast surgery training program. It is crucial to answer all questions accurately and truthfully to ensure a successful application process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Full Name**: Please enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents. Ensure that your name is spelled correctly and matches the format required.
2.  **Email Address**: Enter your official email address associated with your professional work or education. This will be used for communication and program updates.
3.  **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number where we can reach you for further discussions or program information.
4.  **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth to verify your age and eligibility for the program. Please ensure that your date of birth is accurate.
5.  **Medical Degree**: Select the type of medical degree you hold. This will help us assess your qualifications for the program.
6.  **Medical School Attended**: Enter the name of your medical school where you earned your degree.
7.  **Year of Medical Degree Completion**: Enter the year you completed your medical degree.
8.  **Current Professional Position**: Describe your current professional position or role in the medical field.
9.  **Current Institution**: Enter the name of your current institution or hospital where you work.
10.  **Years of Surgical Experience**: Enter the total years of experience you have in the surgical field.
11.  **Years of Breast Surgery Experience**: Enter the years of experience you have specifically in breast surgery.
12.  **Primary Surgical Specialization**: Select your primary surgical specialization from the options provided.
13.  **Board Certification Status**: Indicate your board certification status. If applicable, select "Board certified in specialty" and enter your board specialty.
14.  **Board Certification Specialty**: If you have selected "Board certified in specialty", enter your board specialty.
15.  **Estimated Annual Surgeries**: Enter the estimated number of procedures you perform annually.
16.  **Desired Training Focus**: Select all areas you are interested in focusing on during the program.
17.  **Motivation Statement**: Write a brief statement explaining why you are interested in this program.
18.  **Preferred Program Duration**: Select the preferred duration of the program that suits your schedule.
19.  **Preferred Start Date**: Enter the date when you would like to start the program.
20.  **Professional References**: Enter the names and contact information of your professional references.
21.  **Curriculum Vitae (CV) - File Upload**: Upload a copy of your CV or resume, highlighting your professional experience and qualifications.
22.  **Additional Documents**: Upload any other supporting documents relevant to your application.

Please ensure that you have all necessary information and documentation before submitting your application. It is recommended to proofread your answers carefully to avoid any errors or inaccuracies.
