# public_health_initiative_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from participants in a public health initiative study.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information about the participant, study, and investigator.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Details (1)**: Enter the participant's name and relevant contact information.
  This field is required to ensure that we can communicate with the participant throughout the study.
- **Study Details (2)**: Enter a brief description of the study, including its purpose and objectives.
  This field is required to ensure that the participant understands the study's scope and implications.
- **Informed Consent (3)**: A detailed explanation of the study, its purpose, and how data will be used.
  This field is optional but highly recommended for transparency and participant understanding.
- **Contact Info (4)**: Additional contact information for the participant, if applicable.
  This field is optional and only needed if the participant has specific contact requirements.
- **Study Location (5)**: Where will the study be conducted?
  This field is required to ensure that the participant is aware of the study's location and logistics.
- **Participant Signature (6)**: The participant must sign to confirm they have read and understood the information above.
  This field is required and has two options: "Participant has read and understood the above information" and "Participant has signed this consent form".
- **Investigator Signature (7)**: The investigator must sign to confirm they have reviewed the consent form and that the participant has consented.
  This field is required and has two options: "Institutional Review" and "Participant has consented to participate in the study".
- **Date Signed (8)**: The date the participant signed the form, in YYYY-MM-DD format.
  This field is required and must be filled out in date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
- **Study ID (9)**: A unique identifier for the study.
  This field is required and must be a number.
- **Participant Institution (10)**: The participant's institution or affiliation.
  This field is optional and only needed for participants with a professional affiliation.
- **Researcher Institution (11)**: The researcher's institution or affiliation.
  This field is optional and only needed for investigators with a professional affiliation.
- **Investigator Info (12)**: Additional information about the investigator.
  This field is optional and only needed if the investigator has additional information to provide.
- **Participant Consent Version (13)**: The version of the consent form used.
  This field is required and must be filled out by the participant.
- **Investigator Consent Version (14)**: The version of the consent form used by the investigator.
  This field is optional and only needed if the investigator has a specific version of the consent form.
- **Participant Consent Date (15)**: The date the participant signed the consent form.
  This field is required and must be filled out in date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
- **Investigator Consent Date (16)**: The date the investigator signed the consent form.
  This field is optional and only needed if the investigator has a specific date of consent.
- **Participant Signature Date (17)**: The date the participant signed the form.
  This field is optional and only needed if the participant has a specific signature date.
- **Study Status (18)**: The current status of the study (Active or Inactive).
  This field is required and must be one of the two options provided.
- **Participant Status (19)**: The current status of the participant (Active or Inactive).
  This field is required and must be one of the two options provided.
- **Participant Status Date (20)**: The date the participant's status was last updated.
  This field is required and must be filled out in date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
- **Study Start Date (21)**: The date the study began.
  This field is required and must be filled out in date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
- **Study End Date (22)**: The date the study ended.
  This field is required and must be filled out in date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
- **Study Duration (23)**: The duration of the study in days.
  This field is required and must be a number.
- **Participant Duration (24)**: The duration of the participant's involvement in the study.
  This field is optional and only needed if the participant has been involved for a specific duration.
- **Participant Notes (25)**: Additional notes about the participant.
  This field is optional and only needed if the participant has specific requirements or concerns.
- **Investigator Notes (26)**: Additional notes about the investigator.
  This field is optional and only needed if the investigator has specific requirements or concerns.
- **Participant Institutional Review (27)**: Whether the participant has reviewed the institutional review.
  This field is required and must be one of the two options provided.
- **Investigator Institutional Review (28)**: Whether the investigator has reviewed the institutional review.
  This field is optional and only needed if the investigator has reviewed the institutional review.
- **Study Institutional Review (29)**: Whether the study has reviewed the institutional review.
  This field is optional and only needed if the study has reviewed the institutional review.
