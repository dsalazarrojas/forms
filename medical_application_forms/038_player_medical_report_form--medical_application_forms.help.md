# player_medical_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Player Medical Report Form is used to collect player medical history, allergies, and contact information. This information is essential for ensuring player safety and well-being, particularly during medical emergencies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, start by providing your player's name in the "Player Name" field. This is a required field, so please enter the player's full name as it appears on their government-issued ID.
2. Next, enter the player's date of birth in the "Player Date of Birth" field. This will help us better understand the player's age and medical history.
3. In the "Medical Condition" field, describe any pre-existing medical conditions that the player has. This could include chronic illnesses, disabilities, or injuries that may affect their ability to play.
4. List any allergies or sensitivities the player has in the "Allergies" field. This is crucial for ensuring player safety, especially if they are taking medications or have specific dietary restrictions.
5. Provide a brief description of the player's medical history in the "Medical History" field. This should include any previous injuries, illnesses, or medical procedures they have undergone.
6. In the "Medications" field, select any medications the player is currently taking. This will help us understand potential interactions or sensitivities.
7. In the "Medical Contact" field, provide the name and contact information of the player's primary medical contact. This could be a doctor, nurse, or other medical professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Name** (player_medical_report_form_player_name, text, required): Enter the player's full name as it appears on their government-issued ID.
* **Player Date of Birth** (player_medical_report_form_player_date_of_birth, date, required): Enter the player's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Medical Condition** (player_medical_report_form_medical_condition, text, required): Describe any pre-existing medical conditions, such as chronic illnesses, disabilities, or injuries.
* **Allergies** (player_medical_report_form_allergies, text, required): List any allergies or sensitivities, including medications or food sensitivities.
* **Medical History** (player_medical_report_form_medical_history, note, required): Provide a brief description of the player's medical history, including any previous injuries, illnesses, or medical procedures.
* **Medications** (player_medical_report_form_medications, select_multiple, optional): Select any medications the player is currently taking.
* **Medical Contact** (player_medical_report_form_medical_contact, text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of the player's primary medical contact.
