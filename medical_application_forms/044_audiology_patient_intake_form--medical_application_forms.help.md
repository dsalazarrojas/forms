# Audiology Patient Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Audiology Patient Intake Form is a comprehensive document used to collect information from patients to aid in the diagnosis and treatment of hearing-related issues.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you answer all required fields (marked as required).
3. If a field is not applicable, please select the "Not applicable" or "None of the above" option, if available.
4. If you need assistance with any questions, contact the healthcare professional administering the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

**Full Legal Name (Patient Name)**
• (id: 2) This field collects the patient's full legal name.

**Home Address (Patient Address)**
• (id: 3) This field collects the patient's home address, including street, city, state, and ZIP code.

**Gender**
• (id: 4) This field asks the patient's gender. Please select from the available options: Male, Female, Non-binary, or Prefer not to say.

**Primary Phone Number**
• (id: 5) This field collects the patient's primary phone number.

**Email Address**
• (id: 6) This field collects the patient's email address.

**Insurance Information - Insurance Carrier Name**
• (id: 8) This field collects the name of the patient's insurance carrier.

**Insurance Information - Policy ID Number**
• (id: 9) This field collects the patient's policy ID number.

**Hearing and Health History - Hearing Loss Duration**
• (id: 11) This field asks how long the patient has noticed hearing difficulty. Please select from the available options: Less than 6 months, 6 months to 2 years, 2 to 5 years, Over 5 years, or Not Applicable.

**Hearing and Health History - Family History of Early-Onset Hearing Loss**
• (id: 12) This field asks if there is a family history of early-onset hearing loss. Please select 'True' or 'False'.

**Hearing and Health History - Past or Present Exposure to Loud Noise**
• (id: 13) This field asks about past or present exposure to loud noise. Please select from the available options: Frequent Exposure, Occasional Exposure, or Minimal/No Exposure.

**Hearing and Health History - Previous Ear Surgeries or Treatments**
• (id: 14) This field collects information about previous ear surgeries or treatments.

**Hearing and Health History - Current Medications**
• (id: 15) This field collects information about the patient's current medications, especially those for heart, blood pressure, or dizziness.

**Consent for Evaluation**
• (id: 16) This field indicates that the patient consents to the diagnostic hearing evaluation.

**Consent for Evaluation - Consent Statement**
• (id: 17) This field asks the patient to confirm their consent for the evaluation. Please select I Consent or I Do Not Consent.

**Date of Intake**
• (id: 18) This field collects the date of the patient's intake.

**Patient Digital Signature (Type Name)**
• (id: 19) This field requires the patient to provide their digital signature, which should be typed out.

Please note that this form is intended for new patients and should only be filled out once. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the healthcare professional administering the form.
