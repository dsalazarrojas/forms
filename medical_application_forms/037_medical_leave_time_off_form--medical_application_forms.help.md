# Medical Leave Time Off Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Medical Leave Time Off Form is a tool used to record and process internal HR-related requests for medical leave.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name and job title.
2. Select the reason for your leave and choose the relevant medical leave type.
3. Provide your doctor's name, doctor's office, and medical record number (if applicable).
4. Specify the duration of your leave and start and end times.
5. Sign the form with your signature and date.
6. Your manager will review and sign the form.
7. Submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your official job title as per the company's HR records.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you plan to start your medical leave.
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, required): Briefly explain the reason for your medical leave (e.g., illness, family member's illness, injury, or other).
* **Doctor's Certificate** (`doctor_certificate`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more of the medical certificates you have obtained from your doctor (Certificate of Illness, Doctor's Note, or Disability Certificate).
* **Doctor's Name** (`doctor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your doctor.
* **Doctor's Office** (`doctor_office`, text, required): Enter the name of your doctor's office or clinic.
* **Medical Leave Type** (`medical_leave_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of medical leave you are requesting (e.g., Medical Leave - Illness, Medical Leave - Disability, Medical Leave - Family Member Illness, etc.).
* **Duration of Leave** (`duration_of_leave`, number, required): Specify the number of days or hours you need for your medical leave.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of your medical leave.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of your medical leave.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, note, required): Sign the form with your official signature.
* **Manager's Comment** (`manager_comment`, text, required): Your manager will add any necessary comments or approval information.
* **Manager's Signature** (`manager_signature`, note, required): Your manager will sign the form to approve your medical leave.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, required): Enter the date when you submit the form for processing.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter your employee ID or number as per the company's HR records.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, text, required): Enter your medical record number if you have one.
* **Medical Record Date** (`medical_record_date`, date, required): Enter the date when your medical record was created or last updated.
* **Medical Provider Name** (`medical_provider_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your medical provider (e.g., doctor or hospital).
* **Medical Provider Contact** (`medical_provider_contact`, text, required): Enter the contact information of your medical provider.
* **Medical Provider Phone** (`medical_provider_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your medical provider.
* **Medical Provider Email** (`medical_provider_email`, email, required): Enter the email of your medical provider.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company where you are employed.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the name of your department.
* **Manager's Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your manager.
* **Doctor's Specialty** (`doctor_specialty`, text, required): Enter your doctor's specialty or area of expertise.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are accurately and completely filled out.
* If you have any doubts or questions about the form, consult with your HR representative or manager.
* Make sure to submit the form before your medical leave start date to avoid any potential issues.
