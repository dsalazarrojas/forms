# ObstetricFellowshipApplication - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the obstetric fellowship application form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the **Personal details** page with your name, address, and other relevant personal information.
2. On the **Contact details** page, provide your email address, phone number, and any other contact information.
3. In the **Education** page, describe your academic background, including any relevant certificates or degrees.
4. For **Work experience**, list your previous work experience related to obstetrics.
5. In the **Skills** page, specify any relevant skills you possess, such as programming languages, tools, etc.
6. The **References** page is for listing the names and contact information of individuals who can vouch for your qualifications.
7. On the **Attachments** page, upload any supporting documents required for the application.
8. In the **Application form** page, fill out the application form, which will ask for specific details about your qualifications and experience.
9. If there are **Additional pages**, review and complete them according to the instructions provided.
10. Finally, click the **Submit** button to complete your application.
