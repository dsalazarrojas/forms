# Travel Medicine Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect payment details for travel medicine services. Please fill out this form to provide the necessary information for our billing department.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form accurately and completely.
- Review each section carefully before submission.
- Make sure to enter all required fields, if any.
- Double-check that all entered information is correct before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Patient Name
• **patient name** (text, required): This is the patient's full name. Please enter your name as it appears on your ID or travel documents.

### 2. Payment Amount
• **payment amount** (number, optional): Enter the total amount you would like to pay for the medical services rendered while traveling.

### 3. Payment Method
• **payment method** (select one): Please select how you would like to pay for the services:
	+ Cash
	+ Credit/Debit Card
	+ Check
	+ Insurance

### 4. Patient Email
• **patient email** (email, optional): Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.

### 5. Patient Phone
• **patient phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact and communication purposes.

### 6. Payment Date
• **payment date** (date, optional): This is the date on which the payment is being submitted.

### 7. Payment Time
• **payment time** (time, optional): This is the time on which the payment is being submitted.

### 8. Billing Information
• **billing info** (note, optional): Enter any additional information related to billing, such as account numbers or invoice details.

### 9. Notes
• **notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional comments or information related to your payment.

Note that some fields are optional, please fill out only what is necessary and relevant to the payment process.
