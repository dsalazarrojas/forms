# Medical Necessity Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Necessity Form is designed to collect relevant information from patients about their medical conditions, treatments, and other healthcare-related details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and fields to understand what information is being asked.
2. Ensure you provide accurate and complete information about your medical condition, treatment, and other relevant details.
3. Use the provided fields to capture specific information about your medical condition, such as the type and severity.
4. If you have any additional information that might be relevant to your care, use the "Additional Information" field to provide more context.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Information
* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you should provide general information about yourself, such as your name, contact details, and any other relevant personal details.

### Medical Condition
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, required: false): Please describe your current medical condition, including the type and severity.

### Medication Necessity
* **Medication Necessity** (`medication_necessity`, `text`, required: false): Describe the medication you are taking or need, including the dosage and frequency.

### Medical Treatment
* **Medical Treatment** (`medical_treatment`, `text`, required: false): Describe any medical treatment you are currently undergoing, including the type and frequency.

### Insurance Company
* **Insurance Company** (`insurance_company`, `text`, required: false): If you have an insurance company, please provide the name and contact details.

### Healthcare Service
* **Healthcare Service** (`healthcare_service`, `text`, required: false): Describe the healthcare service you are currently receiving or need, including the type and frequency.

### Medical Service
* **Medical Service** (`medical_service`, `text`, required: false): Describe any additional medical services you are receiving or need, including the type and frequency.

### Patient Relationship
* **Patient Relationship** (`patient_relationship`, `text`, required: false): Describe your relationship with your healthcare provider, including how often you see them and how long you have been seeing them.

### Additional Information
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, `text`, required: false): If you have any additional information that might be relevant to your care, please provide it in this field.

## Tips
* Please provide as much detail as possible for each field to ensure accurate and comprehensive information.
* If you are unsure about any field or information, ask your healthcare provider for clarification or guidance.
* Remember to review and revise your answers carefully before submitting the form.
