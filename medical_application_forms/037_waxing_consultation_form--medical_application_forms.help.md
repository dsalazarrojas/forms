# Waxing Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your medical history and medical conditions that may affect your waxing experience. Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a safe and successful treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter your age.
3. Select your desired hair length.
4. Select any medical conditions you have from the list.
5. Enter any relevant medical notes or history.
6. Select any hair type (curly, straight, or wavy).
7. Identify any medical contraindications or medical histories.
8. Select any hair contraindications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
  - This information is required for our records and to ensure accurate billing.
- **Age** (`age`, number, required/optional): Enter your age in years.
  - This will help us assess your risk level for waxing.
- **Hair Length** (`hair_length`, text, required/optional): Choose the length of your hair.
  - This will help us determine the duration and type of waxing treatment you need.
- **Desired Length** (`desired_length`, number, required/optional): Select the length of hair you desire.
  - This will help us understand your expectations and goals.
- **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_one, required/optional): Select any medical conditions you have from the list.
  - This is a multiple-choice question that helps us identify potential risks and contraindications.
- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, note, required/optional): Enter any relevant medical notes or history.
  - This is a text field for you to provide any additional information about your medical conditions or previous experiences.
- **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional medical notes or information.
  - This is a text field for you to provide any extra information about your medical conditions or previous experiences.
- **Hair Type** (`hair_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your hair type (curly, straight, or wavy).
  - This will help us determine the best waxing technique for your hair.
- **Medical Contraindications** (`medical_contraindications`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any medical contraindications from the list.
  - This is a multiple-choice question that helps us identify potential risks and contraindications.
- **Medical Contrainicated** (`medical_contrainicated`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any medical contraindications from the list.
  - This is a multiple-choice question that helps us identify potential risks and contraindications.
- **Contraindicated Medical History** (`contraindiated_medical_history`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any medical history from the list.
  - This is a multiple-choice question that helps us identify potential risks and contraindications.
- **Contraindications** (`contraindications`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any hair contraindications from the list.
  - This is a multiple-choice question that helps us identify potential risks and contraindications.
- **Hair Contraindications** (`hair_contraindications`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any hair contraindications from the list.
  - This is a multiple-choice question that helps us identify potential risks and contraindications.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions accurately and thoroughly.
- If you are unsure about any information, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
- Please review and sign the consent form to confirm your understanding and agreement.
