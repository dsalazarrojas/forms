# Medical History And Injury Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about your medical history and current injury. Completing this form will help healthcare providers understand your medical background and make informed decisions about your care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2. If you are unsure about any question, discuss it with your healthcare provider.
3. Use the space provided for each section to provide detailed and clear information.
4. If you have any additional medical history or information, use the "Additional Medical History" fields to provide more details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Medical History**: This section is where you can provide a brief overview of your general medical history. Please answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
	+ **Medical Conditions**: Select all the medical conditions you have or have had in the past, such as Hypertension, Diabetes, Heart Conditions, Cancer, or Other (if applicable).
	+ **Injury Description**: Provide a detailed description of your injury, including when and how it occurred.
	+ **Treatment History**: If you have any medical treatments or procedures, provide a detailed description of them.
* **Allergies**: If you have any allergies, select all the relevant options, such as Penicillin, Aspirin, or Other (if applicable).
* **Medications**: If you are currently taking any medications, select all the relevant options, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Medical Team**: List all the healthcare providers you are currently seeing, such as doctors, nurses, or other medical professionals.
* **Injury Date**: Provide the date when your injury occurred.
* **Injury Time**: Provide the time when your injury occurred.
* **Injury Pain Level**: Select the level of pain you are currently experiencing, from Low to Very High.
* **Injury Type**: Select all the options that apply to your injury, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Medical History**: This is a space for you to provide any additional medical history you think is relevant to your care.
* **Additional Medical History 1 and 2**: If you have any additional medical history, use these spaces to provide more details.
* **Injury Type**: This field is meant to describe the type of injury you have. However, it seems that the options are not properly defined. Please provide more context or clear instructions to ensure accurate completion.
