# Medical Insurance Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Insurance Application form is a tool used to collect information from individuals seeking medical insurance. This form is designed to gather essential details about the applicant, their medical history, and their insurance preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your birthdate in the format "mm/dd/yyyy" in the "Birthdate" field.
4. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
5. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
6. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have a medical group affiliation in the "Medical Group" field.
7. Choose the type of medical plan you are interested in by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Medical Plan" field.
8. Select the type of insurance you are seeking by choosing "Yes" or "No" in the "Type of Insurance" field.
9. Enter the start date of your desired insurance coverage in the format "HH:MM:SS" in the "Start Date" field.
10. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
11. Provide any relevant medical history in the "Medical History" field.
12. Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
13. Enter the phone number of the emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
14. Enter your insurance ID in the "Insurance ID" field.
15. Enter your medical provider's name in the "Medical Provider" field.
16. Enter your medical provider's phone number in the "Medical Provider Phone" field.
17. Enter the address of your medical provider in the "Medical Provider Address" field.
18. Enter your medical provider's license number in the "Medical Provider License Number" field.
19. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if your medical provider has a specialty in the "Medical Provider Type" field.
20. Enter your medical provider's specialty in the "Medical Provider Specialty" field.
21. Enter any additional notes about your medical provider in the "Medical Provider Notes" field.
22. Enter a description of your medical group in the "Medical Group Description" field.
23. Enter the address of your medical group in the "Medical Group Address" field.
