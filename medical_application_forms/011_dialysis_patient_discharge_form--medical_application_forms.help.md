# dialysis_patient_discharge_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information about patients undergoing dialysis treatment for discharge purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name as it is recorded in your medical records.
2.  Enter your Date of Birth.
3.  Provide your contact number where you can be reached for follow-up.
4.  Describe your medical history, including any relevant medical conditions, surgeries, or allergies.
5.  Enter your current blood pressure reading.
6.  Enter your current weight.
7.  Record your current body temperature.
8.  List any medications you are currently taking.
9.  List any known allergies or sensitivities you have.
10. Enter the details of your next of contact person or family member responsible for your care.
11.  Enter the date and time you are being discharged.
12.  Enter the scheduled follow-up visit details.
13.  Write down any specific discharge instructions for your healthcare provider.
14.  Share any additional comments or concerns you have with your healthcare provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Name (1)** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it is recorded in your medical records.
*   **Date Of Birth (2)** (`dob`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
*   **Contact Number (3)** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter a number where you can be reached for follow-up.
*   **Medical History (4)** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Describe any medical conditions, surgeries, or allergies that may impact your treatment.
*   **Blood Pressure (5)** (`blood_pressure`, text, optional): Enter your current blood pressure reading.
*   **Weight (6)** (`weight`, number, optional): Enter your current weight.
*   **Temperature (7)** (`temperature`, number, optional): Enter your current body temperature.
*   **Medications (8)** (`medications`, text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
*   **Allergies (9)** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any known allergies or sensitivities you have.
*   **Next Of Contact (10)** (`next_of_contact`, text, optional): Enter the details of your next of contact person or family member responsible for your care.
*   **Discharge Date (11)** (`discharge_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of discharge.
*   **Discharge Time (12)** (`discharge_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of discharge.
*   **Follow Up Visit (13)** (`follow_up_visit`, text, optional): Enter the details of your follow-up visit.
*   **Discharge Instructions (14)** (`discharge_instructions`, text, optional): Write down any specific instructions or guidelines for your healthcare provider.
*   **Patient Comments (15)** (`patient_comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or concerns you have with your healthcare provider.
