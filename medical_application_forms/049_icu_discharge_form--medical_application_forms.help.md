# ICU Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ICU Discharge Form is a critical document that captures essential information about a patient's status during their ICU stay. This information helps healthcare professionals make informed decisions and provide effective care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

*   Gather all necessary information about the patient's current status.
*   Ensure all required fields are completed.
*   Fill in all relevant details, even if the patient has no medical conditions or allergies.
*   Select the correct options for the Medical Team and ICU Discharge Status fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Information**: This field is for capturing basic patient details, including their name, address, phone number, and email.
    *   **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
    *   **Patient Address**: Enter the patient's address, which is essential for communication and follow-up care.
    *   **Patient Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number for contact purposes.
    *   **Patient Email**: Enter the patient's email address, if available.
*   **Date of Discharge**: Enter the date the patient is being discharged from the ICU.
*   **Time of Discharge**: Enter the time the patient is being discharged from the ICU.
*   **Vital Signs**: Enter the patient's current vital signs, such as temperature, pulse, and blood pressure.
*   **Temperature**: Enter the patient's current body temperature.
*   **Pulse**: Enter the patient's current pulse rate.
*   **Blood Pressure**: Enter the patient's current blood pressure readings.
*   **Oxygen Saturation**: Enter the patient's current oxygen saturation levels.
*   **Medication**: List any medications the patient is currently taking.
*   **Discharge Diagnosis**: Enter the reason for the patient's discharge.
*   **Instructions**: Enter any special instructions for the patient or their caregivers.
*   **Medical Team**: Select the medical team members who have been involved in the patient's care.
    *   **Option 1**: Select one of the available options for the medical team.
    *   **Option 2**: Select one of the available options for the medical team.
    *   **Option 3**: Select one of the available options for the medical team.
*   **Medical Notes**: Select multiple medical notes that apply to the patient's condition.
    *   **Option 1**: Select one of the available options for medical notes.
    *   **Option 2**: Select one of the available options for medical notes.
    *   **Option 3**: Select one of the available options for medical notes.
    *   **Option 4**: Select one of the available options for medical notes.
    *   **Option 5**: Select one of the available options for medical notes.
*   **Next of Contact**: Enter the name and contact information for the person to contact in case of an emergency.
*   **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
*   **Date of Admission**: Enter the date the patient was admitted to the ICU.
*   **Attending Physician**: Enter the name of the attending physician.
*   **ICU Discharge Status**: Select the status of the patient's discharge.
    *   **Option 1**: Select one of the available options for discharge status.
    *   **Option 2**: Select one of the available options for discharge status.
    *   **Option 3**: Select one of the available options for discharge status.
*   **ICU Discharge Date**: Enter the date the patient was discharged from the ICU.
*   **Discharge Summary**: Enter a brief summary of the patient's discharge.

## Tips

*   Always check for updates on patient conditions and medical team assignments before submitting the form.
*   Ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
*   Verify the patient's information for accuracy before submission.
