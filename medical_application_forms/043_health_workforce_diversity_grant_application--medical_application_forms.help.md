# Health Workforce Diversity Grant Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Workforce Diversity Grant Application is designed to support initiatives that promote diversity in the health workforce. This guide will walk you through the process of completing the application form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields accurately and thoroughly.
2. Make sure to answer all the questions truthfully and in a clear manner.
3. Review your submission before submitting it for evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Info** (Page 1, Required: False): This section is where you will enter your personal details as an applicant.
	+ Please enter your name and any other identifying information.
* **Project Details** (Page 2, Required: False): Describe your project in a clear and concise manner.
	+ Explain how your project aims to promote diversity in the health workforce.
* **Funding Request** (Page 3, Required: False): Specify the amount of funding you are requesting for your project.
	+ Be as specific as possible when requesting funds.
* **Diversity Impact** (Page 4, Required: False): Describe how your project will positively impact diversity in the health workforce.
	+ Quantify your impact wherever possible.
* **Project Budget** (Page 5, Required: False): Provide a detailed breakdown of your project budget.
	+ Include all projected expenses and revenues.
* **Start Date** (Page 6, Required: False): Enter the start date of your project.
	+ Make sure this date aligns with your project timeline.
* **End Date** (Page 7, Required: False): Enter the end date of your project.
	+ Ensure that the end date is realistic and consistent with your start date.
* **Project Location** (Page 8, Required: False): Specify the location of your project.
	+ This could be a physical location or a virtual project environment.
* **Contact Info** (Page 9, Required: False): Provide your contact details so that we can reach out to you with questions or updates.
	+ Include a valid email and phone number.
* **Email** (Page 10, Required: False): Enter your email address.
	+ This will be used for correspondence and communication.
* **Phone** (Page 11, Required: False): Enter your phone number.
	+ This will be used for phone calls and updates.

## Tips
- Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
- Be honest and thorough in your submissions.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
