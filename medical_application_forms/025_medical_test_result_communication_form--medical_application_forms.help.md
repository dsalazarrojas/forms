# medical_test_result_communication_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to record and communicate medical test results with patients. It allows healthcare providers to enter patient information, test results, and any additional follow-up instructions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name and email address.
2. Record the medical test result, including any notes or explanations.
3. Add any attachments related to the lab results, if applicable.
4. Enter the date of the test.
5. Add any additional follow-up instructions for the patient.
6. Enter the name of the medical provider, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical records.
* **Patient Email**: Enter the patient's email address to ensure they can receive communication about their test results.
* **Medical Test Result**: Enter the actual test result, including any relevant information such as test name, value, and comments.
* **Test Date**: Enter the date of the test, if known.
* **Follow-up Instructions**: Add any additional instructions or advice for the patient to follow up on their test results.
* **Lab Results Attachment**: If you have a document or file attachment related to the lab results, upload it here.
* **Lab Results Attachment URL**: If the lab results are available online, provide the URL where the patient can access them.
* **Lab Results Attachment Description**: Briefly describe the attachment or the lab results.
* **Test Result Explanation**: Add any additional explanation or context about the test result, if needed.
* **Medical Provider**: Enter your name and contact information, if you are providing this form to a patient.

# Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Double-check that the test result is accurate and up-to-date.
* Consider uploading any relevant attachments or documents to support the test result.
* If the patient's email is not entered, they will not receive any form submissions or updates.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact the patient or medical team directly.
