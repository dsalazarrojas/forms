# Advanced Diabetes Supply Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Advanced Diabetes Supply Order Form is a tool for medical organizations to order supplies related to diabetes management. It helps in providing essential information for the delivery of these supplies, ensuring that the right supplies reach the right place at the right time.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **Facility or clinic name** where the supplies should be delivered.
2.  Provide the **Contact person name** responsible for delivery coordination.
3.  Enter the **Contact phone number**, including your mobile number if possible.
4.  List the **Requested supplies and quantities** for delivery.
5.  If required, select your **Preferred supplier** from the provided options.
6.  Choose the **Delivery priority** as either **Normal** or **Urgent**.
7.  Enter the **Billing account or code** for invoicing purposes.
8.  Select a **Requested delivery date** as the target date for delivery.
9.  Provide **Additional notes** if any special handling instructions are required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility or clinic name**: Please enter the name of the facility or clinic where you would like to receive the supplies.
* **Contact person name**: This is the name of the person responsible for delivery coordination.
* **Contact phone**: Enter your phone number for easy coordination with the delivery team.
* **Requested supplies and quantities**: List each item of the supply you require and the corresponding quantity.
* **Preferred supplier** (optional): If you have a preferred supplier, select them from the options provided.
* **Delivery priority**: Choose between Normal and Urgent priority depending on the urgency of your needs.
* **Billing account or code**: Enter the billing account or code for accurate invoicing.
* **Requested delivery date**: Select a target date for delivery of the supplies.
* **Additional notes**: Provide any special instructions for handling the supplies if required.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately to avoid delays in processing your order.
* Double-check your requested supplies and quantities for accuracy.
* If you have any special requests or instructions, please provide them in the **Additional notes** field.
* For urgent deliveries, select **Urgent** priority to expedite the process.
