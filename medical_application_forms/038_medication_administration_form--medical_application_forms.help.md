# medication_administration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record information about medication administration, including patient details, medication details, and administration details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information on the "patient_information" page.
2. Enter the medication information on the "medication_info" page.
3. Enter the dosage information on the "dosage_info" page.
4. Select the administration route on the "administration_route" page.
5. Enter the start and end dates of administration on the "start_date" and "end_date" pages.
6. Enter the administration frequency on the "frequency" page.
7. Add any notes or comments on the "notes" page.
8. Indicate whether the medication was administered by a healthcare professional on the "Medication_administered_by" page.
9. Add the title and name of the healthcare professional on the "Medication_administered_by_title" and "medication_administered_by" pages.
10. Enter the medical record number on the "Medical_record_number" page.
11. Enter the date and time of administration on the "Date_of_administration" and "Time_of_administration" pages.
12. Sign the form with the patient's signature on the "Patient_signature" page.
13. Sign the form with the prescriber's signature on the "Prescriber_signature" page.
14. Indicate whether the medication was administered by a healthcare professional on the "Medication_administered_by_abbreviation" page.
15. Enter the job title of the healthcare professional on the "Medication_administered_by_job_title" page.
16. Enter the medical license number on the "Medical_license_number" page.
17. Enter the medical license expiration date on the "Medical_license_exp" page.
18. Enter the medical department on the "Medical_department" page.
19. Enter the medical license number expiration date on the "Medical_license_number_exp" page.
20. Enter the medical license type on the "Medical_license_type" page.
21. Enter the medical license status on the "Medical_license_status" page.
22. Enter the medical license state on the "Medical_license_state" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_information (text, required/optional):** Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and other identifying information.
* **medication_info (text, required/optional):** Enter the name of the medication, dosage, and any other relevant details.
* **Dosage (number, required/optional):** Enter the dose of the medication.
* **administration_route (text, required/optional):** Select the route of administration (e.g., oral, IV, etc.).
* **start_date (date, required/optional):** Enter the start date of medication administration.
* **end_date (date, required/optional):** Enter the end date of medication administration.
* **frequency (number, required/optional):** Enter the frequency of medication administration (e.g., daily, weekly, etc.).
* **notes (note, required/optional):** Add any notes or comments about the medication administration.
* **Medication_administered_by (select_one, required/optional):** Indicate whether the medication was administered by a healthcare professional.
* **Medication_administered_by_title (text, required/optional):** Enter the title of the healthcare professional who administered the medication.
* **Medical_record_number (text, required/optional):** Enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Date_of_administration (date, required/optional):** Enter the date of medication administration.
* **Time_of_administration (time, required/optional):** Enter the time of medication administration.
* **Patient_signature (note, required/optional):** Sign the form with the patient's signature.
* **Prescriber_signature (note, required/optional):** Sign the form with the prescriber's signature.
* **Medication_administered_by_abbreviation (select_one, required/optional):** Indicate whether the medication was administered by a healthcare professional.
* **Medication_administered_by_job_title (text, required/optional):** Enter the job title of the healthcare professional who administered the medication.
* **Medical_license (text, required/optional):** Enter the healthcare professional's medical license.
* **Medical_license_number (text, required/optional):** Enter the healthcare professional's medical license number.
* **Medical_license_exp (text, required/optional):** Enter the date of expiration of the healthcare professional's medical license.
* **Medical_department (text, required/optional):** Enter the department of the healthcare professional who administered the medication.
* **Medical_license_number_exp (text, required/optional):** Enter the date of expiration of the healthcare professional's medical license number.
* **Medical_license_type (text, required/optional):** Enter the type of medical license of the healthcare professional.
* **Medical_license_status (text, required/optional):** Enter the status of the healthcare professional's medical license.
* **Medical_license_state (text, required/optional):** Enter the state where the healthcare professional is licensed.
