# Patient Waiting List - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Waiting List form is used to track patients' information and appointments. This form helps medical professionals to keep track of patients' names, contact numbers, appointment dates, examination results, notes, and email addresses.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the text field labeled "patient name".
2. Enter the patient's contact number in the text field labeled "contact number".
3. Select the appointment date from the calendar picker for "appointment date".
4. Enter the examination results in the text field labeled "examination".
5. Add any notes or comments about the patient in the text field labeled "notes".
6. Enter the patient's email address in the text field labeled "patient email".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's name to identify them in the waiting list.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the patient's contact number, if available.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the patient's appointment.
* **Examination** (`examination`, text, optional): Enter the examination results or any notes about the patient's examination.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the patient's visit.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, optional): Enter the patient's email address, if available.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the patient's data is correct.
* If the patient has multiple appointments, select the date of each appointment separately.
* If the patient has no examination results, enter "No examination results available" to indicate this.
* If the patient has no contact number, enter "No contact number available" to indicate this.
* If the patient has no email address, enter "No email address available" to indicate this.
