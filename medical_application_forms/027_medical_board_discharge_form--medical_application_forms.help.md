# Medical Board Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Board Discharge Form is used to document the discharge process for patients after their hospitalization or treatment. This form is essential for capturing relevant information about the patient's condition, medical treatment, and follow-up instructions for continued care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling in the patient's personal information on page 1, including their name, date of birth, and contact details.
2.  On page 2, provide medical information about the patient, such as their diagnosis, medications, and medical conditions.
3.  On page 3, enter follow-up instructions for the patient, including any ongoing treatment or care plans.
4.  On page 4, summarize the discharge information, including any final diagnoses, medications, and discharge instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (Page 1, **patient_info**):
    *   **Name** (`<name>`, text, required): The patient's name as it appears on their identification.
    *   **Date of Birth** (`<date_of_birth>`, text, required): The patient's date of birth as recorded in their medical records.
    *   **Contact Details** (`<contact_details>`, text, required): The patient's contact information, including phone number and address.
*   **Medical Information** (Page 2, **medical_info**):
    *   **Diagnosis** (`<diagnosis>`, text, required): A description of the patient's current medical condition.
    *   **Medications** (`<medications>`, text, required): A list of the patient's current medications and dosages.
    *   **Medical Conditions** (`<medical_conditions>`, text, required): A list of the patient's pre-existing or current medical conditions.
*   **Follow Up** (Page 3, **follow_up**):
    *   **Follow Up Instructions** (`<follow_up_instructions>`, text, required): Any additional instructions or guidance for the patient's follow-up care.
*   **Discharge Information** (Page 4, **discharge_info**):
    *   **Final Diagnosis** (`<final_diagnosis>`, text, required): A summary of the patient's final diagnosis.
    *   **Discharge Instructions** (`<discharge_instructions>`, text, required): Instructions for the patient's continued care after discharge.

## Tips
This form is designed to be completed in conjunction with the patient's healthcare provider. Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely for optimal patient care. If you are unsure about any field or have questions, please consult with your healthcare provider before submitting the form.
