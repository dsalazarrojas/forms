# Radiology Patient Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Radiology Patient Discharge Form is a document used to record a patient's status after a radiology procedure. It ensures accurate and up-to-date information for patient care and facilitates communication between medical staff.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each field carefully before proceeding.
2. Complete the form in collaboration with the patient and radiologist involved.
3. Ensure accurate and legible handwriting for all fields.
4. Obtain patient and radiologist signatures upon completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Patient Status (1)
* **Patient Status (select_one)** (Active, Inactive): Select one of the two options to indicate the patient's status.

### Current Medication (2)
* **Current Medication (text):** Enter the patient's current medication regimen. This information is essential for understanding the patient's treatment plan.

### Allergies (3)
* **Allergies (text):** Record any allergies or sensitivities the patient may have. This is critical for future medical procedures.

### Medical History (4)
* **Medical History (text):** Write a brief summary of the patient's medical history to ensure continuity of care.

### Follow-up Instructions (5)
* **Follow-up Instructions (text):** Provide any necessary follow-up instructions for the patient, such as appointment scheduling or medication dosage.

### Radiologist Comments (6)
* **Radiologist Comments (text):** Include any comments or notes from the radiologist about the patient's condition or procedure.

### Next Steps (7)
* **Next Steps (text):** Specify any next steps for the patient's care, such as follow-up appointments or further testing.

### Patient Signature (8)
* **Patient Signature (note):** Obtain the patient's signature to acknowledge their understanding of the form's content.

### Doctor Signature (9)
* **Doctor Signature (note):** Obtain the radiologist's or doctor's signature to confirm completion and review of the form's content.

### Tips

- Ensure accurate and complete information is recorded for the patient's care.
- Double-check patient and radiologist signatures for authenticity.
- Store the completed form in the patient's medical record for easy access.
- Use this form as a reference for future appointments and consultations.
