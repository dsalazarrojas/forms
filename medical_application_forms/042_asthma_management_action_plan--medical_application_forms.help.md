# Asthma Management Action Plan - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Asthma Management Action Plan is a tool designed to help healthcare providers and patients collaborate on managing asthma symptoms. This plan outlines steps to follow in case of asthma triggers, medication usage, and emergency situations. Fill out this form to create a personalized plan with your healthcare provider.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter patient information:
	* **Patient First Name**: Enter the patient's first name.
	* **Patient Last Name**: Enter the patient's last name.
	* **Patient ID Number**: Enter the patient's medical record number.
	* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
2. Select the severity of asthma:
	* **Asthma Severity Classification**: Choose the severity level of asthma from the following options:
		+ Mild Intermittent
		+ Mild Persistent
		+ Moderate Persistent
		+ Severe Persistent
3. Identify asthma triggers:
	* **Identified Asthma Triggers**: Describe what triggers your asthma.
	* **Specific Triggers**: Check all that apply:
		+ Allergens (pollen, dust, pets)
		+ Exercise
		+ Respiratory infections
		+ Cold air
		+ Air pollution
		+ Strong odors or chemicals
		+ Stress or emotional changes
		+ Gastric reflux
		+ Food allergies
		+ Other
4. Plan to avoid triggers:
	* **Plan to Avoid Triggers**: Describe strategies to minimize exposure to triggers.
5. Medication information:
	* **Quick-Relief (Rescue) Medication**: Name of rescue inhaler.
	* **Rescue Medication Dosage and Instructions**: Describe how to use rescue medication.
	* **Daily Controller Medication**: Name and type of controller drug.
	* **Controller Medication Dosage and Instructions**: Describe how to take controller medication.
	* **Other Medications or Treatments**: Describe additional medications prescribed.
6. Symptom monitoring and emergency plans:
	* **Green Zone Symptoms (Doing Well)**: Describe symptoms when doing well.
	* **Yellow Zone Symptoms (Caution)**: Describe early warning signs.
	* **Yellow Zone Action Plan**: Describe actions to take when experiencing warning signs.
	* **Red Zone Symptoms (Emergency)**: Describe severe symptoms requiring immediate attention.
	* **Red Zone Action Plan**: Describe steps to take in case of emergency.
7. Follow-up and monitoring:
	* **Peak Flow Target Range**: Describe expected personal best measurement.
	* **Monitoring Frequency**: Choose how often to check peak flow.
	* **Follow-Up Appointment Schedule**: Describe when to see healthcare provider.
8. Emergency contact:
	* **Emergency Contact Information**: Provide name and phone number of emergency contact.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and update your plan regularly with your healthcare provider.
* Keep a copy of this plan for reference and share it with others who need to know.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to your healthcare provider.
