# Medical Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Application Form is designed for collecting contact and medical information from individuals in a medical application setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required contact numbers (Contact Number and phone number).
2. If desired, provide medical history and medical information in the relevant fields.
3. Review the form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required):
	* This field is for you to enter your contact number.
	* Please provide your active phone number that can be used to contact you.
- **phone number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required):
	* This field is for you to enter your phone number.
	* Please provide your active phone number that can be used to contact you.
- **email address** (`email_address`, `email`, required):
	* This field is for you to enter your email address.
	* Please provide your active email address that can be used to contact you.
- **medical history** (`medical_history`, `text`, optional):
	* This field is for you to provide any relevant medical history.
	* If you have any previous medical conditions or allergies, please describe them here.
- **medical info** (`medical_info`, `text`, optional):
	* This field is for you to provide any additional medical information.
	* If you have any medical procedures, medications, or concerns, please describe them here.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date contact information.
- If you have any medical concerns or previous medical conditions, please describe them in the medical history and medical info fields.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
