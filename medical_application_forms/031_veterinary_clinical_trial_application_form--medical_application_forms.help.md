# Veterinary Clinical Trial Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about an animal's medical history, contact information, and other relevant details in order to better understand its needs and circumstances.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Animal Details section with the animal's information (optional).
2. Provide the animal's Medical History information (optional).
3. Fill out the Contact Information section with your contact details (optional).
4. Check the Consent checkbox to indicate if you agree to the terms (required).
5. Select the Animal Type (e.g., Dog, Cat, Other) (optional).
6. Choose the Animal's Medical Status (optional).
7. Choose your preferred Contact Method (e.g., Phone, Email) (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Animal Details
* **Animal Details** (`animal_details`, text, optional): Enter the animal's name, breed, and other relevant details.
### Medical History
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Provide any relevant medical history, including previous illnesses, injuries, or treatments.
### Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, phone number, and email address so we can reach out to you with any questions or updates.
### Consent
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Check the box to indicate that you consent to the terms and conditions of the clinical trial.
### Owner
* **Owner** (`owner`, text, optional): Enter your name and other relevant details as the animal's owner.
### Animal Type
* **Animal Type** (`animal_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of animal you are applying for (e.g., Dog, Cat, Other).
### Medical Status
* **Medical Status** (`medical_status`, number, optional): Choose the animal's current medical status (e.g., Healthy, Sick, Unknown).
### Contact Method
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., Phone, Email).

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide accurate and clear information to ensure that we can better understand your animal's needs.
* If you are unsure about any section, please contact us for clarification.
* Make sure to review each section carefully before submitting the form.
