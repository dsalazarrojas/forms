# Physician Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Physician Application Form is designed to collect information from physicians applying for a position or membership in a medical organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your physician license number in the "Physician License Number" field.
5. Select your medical specialty from the "Specialty" field.
6. Describe your work experience in the "Work Experience" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (First Name, text, required: false):** Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (Last Name, text, required: false):** Enter your last name.
* **Email (Email, email, required: false):** Enter your email address.
* **Physician License Number (Physician License Number, text, required: false):** Enter your physician license number.
* **Specialty (Specialty, select_one, required: false):** Select your medical specialty from the list.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Work Experience (Work Experience, text, required: false):** Describe your relevant work experience.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your email address for any typos or errors.
* Make sure to select the correct specialty that matches your qualifications.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
