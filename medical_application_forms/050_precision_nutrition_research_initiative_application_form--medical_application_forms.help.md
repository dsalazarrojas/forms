# Precision Nutrition Research Initiative Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Precision Nutrition Research Initiative Application Form is a tool used to gather information about researchers and their projects. It will help the research team to review and consider your application for further consideration in the Precision Nutrition Research Initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your research project's name in the **Research Name** field.
2. Provide a brief description of your research project in the **Research Description** field.
3. Enter your name and institutional affiliation in the **Researcher Information** field.
4. Enter your contact information, including your name and email address (if applicable) in the **Contact Information** field.
5. Indicate the funding source for your research project (yes or no) in the **Funding Source** field.
6. Specify whether your research project is currently active or inactive in the **Research Status** field.
7. Choose whether your research project is related to the Precision Nutrition Research Initiative (yes or no) in the **Research Type** field.
8. Provide a project duration in the **Project Duration** field.
9. Enter your email address and phone number in the **Email** and **Phone** fields, respectively.
10. Enter your date of birth in the **Date of Birth** field.
11. Enter your date of death (if applicable) in the **Date of Death** field.
12. Provide your medical history in the **Medical History** field.
13. Specify any medical conditions you have in the **Medical Condition** field.
14. List any medications you are currently taking in the **Medications** field.
15. List any allergies you have in the **Allergies** field.
16. Enter the name of the institution where your research project will be conducted in the **Research Institution** field.
17. Enter the name of the research group conducting the project in the **Research Group** field.
18. Enter the location of the research project in the **Research Location** field.
19. Enter the ID of the grant funding your research project in the **Grant ID** field.
20. Enter any submission comments or notes in the **Submission Comments** field.
21. Specify whether your submission is active or inactive in the **Submission Status** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Research Name** (1, text, required): Enter your research project's name.
* **Research Description** (2, text, required): Provide a brief description of your research project.
* **Researcher Information** (3, text, required): Enter your name and institutional affiliation.
* **Contact Information** (4, text, required): Enter your contact information, including your name and email address (if applicable).
* **Funding Source** (5, select_one, required): Indicate whether your research project is funded (yes or no).
* **Research Status** (6, select_one, required): Specify whether your research project is currently active or inactive.
* **Research Type** (7, select_one, required): Choose whether your research project is related to the Precision Nutrition Research Initiative (yes or no).
* **Project Duration** (9, time, required): Enter the duration of your research project.
* **Email** (10, email, optional): Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Phone** (11, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (12, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Date of Death** (13, date, optional): Enter your date of death (if applicable).
* **Medical History** (14, note, required): Provide your medical history.
* **Medical Condition** (15, text, required): Specify any medical conditions you have.
* **Medications** (16, text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies** (17, text, optional): List any allergies you have.
* **Research Institution** (18, text, required): Enter the name of the institution where your research project will be conducted.
* **Research Group** (19, text, required): Enter the name of the research group conducting the project.
* **Research Location** (20, text, required): Enter the location of the research project.
* **Grant ID** (21, text, required): Enter the ID of the grant funding your research project.
* **Submission Comments** (23, text, optional): Enter any submission comments or notes.
* **Submission Status** (24, select_one, required): Specify whether your submission is active or inactive.
