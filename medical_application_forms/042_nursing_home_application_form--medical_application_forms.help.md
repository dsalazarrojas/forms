# Nursing Home Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The nursing home application form is a tool used to collect information from individuals who wish to apply for a nursing home or long-term care facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the applicant details page (page 1) with your personal information, including any relevant details that may be required for your application.
2. Proceed to the contact details page (page 2) and provide your contact information.
3. On the medical details page (page 3), provide any necessary medical information that may be required for your care.
4. Finally, on the medical history page (page 4), provide any relevant medical history that may be necessary for your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Details** (`applicant_details`, text, required): This field is for you to provide your personal details, such as your name, address, and any other relevant information that may be required.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required): This field is for you to provide your contact information, including your phone number and email.
* **Medical Details** (`medical_details`, text, required): This field is for you to provide any necessary medical information that may be required for your care, such as your medication list or medical conditions.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): This field is for you to provide any relevant medical history that may be necessary for your application, such as previous illnesses or surgeries.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and truthful information in each field.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the nursing home or long-term care facility for clarification.
* Please note that this form is for information purposes only and does not guarantee placement in a nursing home or long-term care facility.
