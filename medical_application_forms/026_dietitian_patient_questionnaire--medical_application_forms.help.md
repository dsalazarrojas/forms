# Dietitian Patient Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of the Dietitian Patient Questionnaire is to gather information from patients to help dietitians better understand their health and nutritional needs. This information will be used to create personalized diet plans and provide patients with relevant advice on maintaining a healthy lifestyle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your contact number in the "contact_number" field.
5. Describe your medical history in the "medical_history" field.
6. Share your current nutrition habits in the "nutrition_habits" field.
7. Enter your address in the "address" field.
8. List any medical conditions you may have in the "medical_conditions" field.
9. Describe any medical procedures you may have undergone in the "medical_procedures" field.
10. List any allergies you have in the "allergies" field.
11. Select all emergency contacts from the list of options in the "emergency_contacts" field. This can include a Mother, Father, Spouse, Sister, Brother, or Friend.
12. If necessary, provide additional medical history in the "medical_history_2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name**: Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **last_name**: Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **email**: Provide your email address for us to contact you with any questions or updates.
* **contact_number**: Enter your contact number so we can reach you if needed.
* **medical_history**: Describe any medical conditions, surgeries, or allergies you have. This will help us better understand your nutritional needs.
* **nutrition_habits**: Share your current eating habits, including your typical diet and any food preferences or dislikes.
* **address**: Enter your home address for our records.
* **medical_conditions**: List any medical conditions you may have, such as diabetes, high blood pressure, etc.
* **medical_procedures**: Describe any medical procedures you may have undergone, such as surgeries or implants.
* **allergies**: List any allergies you may have, such as food allergies or medication allergies.
* **emergency_contacts**: Select all emergency contacts from the list, such as a mother, father, spouse, sister, brother, or friend. This will help us know who to contact in case of an emergency.
* **medical_history_2**: If you have any additional medical history that didn't fit in the previous field, please provide it here.
* **emergency_contacts**: Select all emergency contacts from the list of options.
