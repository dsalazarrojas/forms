# Infection Control Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Infection Control Discharge Form is used to document the status of infection control measures taken for a patient upon discharge from a healthcare facility. This form helps ensure that the patient's safety is maintained and potential complications are prevented.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer all questions accurately.
2. Enter relevant information for each field as instructed.
3. Sign the form as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **General Information** (`general_information`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional information related to the patient's general health status.
- **Patient Identification** (`patient_identification`, Text, Optional): Enter the patient's identification number or any other relevant details.
- **Infection Control Measures** (`infection_control_measures`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select all applicable infection control measures taken for the patient, such as Antimicrobial, Antibacterial, Antifungal, or Antiviral.
- **Antibiotic Allergy** (`antibiotic_allergy`, Select One, Optional): If the patient has an allergy to any antibiotics, select the relevant option (e.g., Penicillin, Cephalosporin, Vancomycin, Ciprofloxacin, Azilsam).
- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, Note, Optional): Enter any relevant medical history or information that may impact the patient's health.
- **Medication Allergies** (`medication_allergies`, Select Multiple, Optional): If the patient has allergies to any medications, select the relevant options (e.g., Aspirin, Penicillin, Sulfa, Amphotrcin, Cephalosporin).
- **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of the patient's discharge.
- **Physician Signature** (`physician_signature`, Note, Optional): The doctor's signature confirms that the form has been filled out accurately.
- **Medical Staff Signature** (`medical_staff_signature`, Note, Optional): The healthcare staff member's signature confirms that the form has been filled out accurately.
- **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, Note, Optional): The patient's signature confirms that they have been informed and agree to the information on the form.
- **Infection Control Status** (`infection_control_status`, Select One, Optional): Select the status of infection control for the patient, (e.g., Not at risk, At risk, High risk).
- **Follow Up Instructions** (`follow_up_instructions`, Text, Optional): Enter any follow-up instructions for the patient.
- **Follow Up Date** (`follow_up_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date for the follow-up appointment.
- **Follow Up Time** (`follow_up_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time for the follow-up appointment.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, Text, Optional): Enter any relevant contact information for the patient.
- **Physician Comments** (`physician_comments`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select if the doctor has any comments or recommendations for the patient.
- **Patient Comments** (`patient_comments`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select if the patient has any comments or recommendations for the doctor.

**Tips**

* Always review each field carefully to ensure accurate and complete information.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed to prevent potential complications.
* If unsure about any information, consult with a healthcare professional.
* Store the completed form in the patient's medical record for future reference.
