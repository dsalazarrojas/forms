# Veterinary Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Records Release Form is designed to facilitate the transfer of veterinary records between clinics. This form helps ensure that all relevant information is collected and documented during the transfer process, ensuring a smooth transition for the pet owner and the new clinic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Pet Owner's Name**, **Owner's Address**, and **Pet's Name** sections with the relevant information.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for **Veterinary Clinic** regarding whether the pet is being transferred to a new clinic.
3. Enter the **Date of Transfer** when the pet is being transferred.
4. Provide a brief reason for the **Reason for Transfer**.
5. If the pet is being transferred to a new clinic, fill in the **Name of New Veterinary Clinic**, **Contact Person at New Clinic**, and **Contact Person's Phone** and **Contact Person's Email** if available.
6. Sign and date the **Date** section as the **Veterinarian's Signature** and the **Clinic Signature Date**.
7. Confirm that the pet is being **Authorized** to be transferred by entering "Yes" in the **Authorization** section.
8. If the pet is being transferred, indicate the **Type of Transfer** as "Yes" or "No".
9. Enter the **Owner Acknowledgement** and **Clinic Acknowledgement**.
10. Fill in the **Clinic Contact**, **Owner Contact**, **Transfer Date**, **Veterinary License**, and **Clinic License**.
11. Add any additional **Notes** about the transfer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Owner's Name**: The name of the pet owner.
* **Owner's Address**: The address of the pet owner.
* **Pet's Name**: The name of the pet being transferred.
* **Veterinary Clinic**: Indicate if the pet is being transferred to a new clinic ("Yes" or "No").
* **Date of Transfer**: The date the pet is being transferred.
* **Reason for Transfer**: A brief explanation for the transfer.
* **Name of New Veterinary Clinic**: The name of the new clinic the pet is being transferred to (if applicable).
* **Contact Person at New Clinic**: The name of the contact person at the new clinic (if applicable).
* **Contact Person's Phone**: The phone number of the contact person at the new clinic (if applicable).
* **Contact Person's Email**: The email of the contact person at the new clinic (if applicable).
* **Date**: Sign and date this section as the veterinarian's signature.
* **Veterinarian's Signature**: The veterinarian's signature.
* **Clinic Signature Date**: Sign and date this section as the clinic signature.
* **Authorization**: Confirm that the pet is being authorized to be transferred by entering "Yes".
* **Type of Transfer**: Indicate if the pet is being transferred ("Yes" or "No").
* **Owner Acknowledgement**: Confirm that the owner understands the transfer.
* **Clinic Acknowledgement**: Confirm that the clinic understands the transfer.
* **Clinic Contact**: The contact information for the new clinic.
* **Owner Contact**: The contact information for the owner.
* **Transfer Date**: The date of the transfer.
* **Veterinary License**: The license number of the veterinarian.
* **Clinic License**: The license number of the new clinic.
* **Notes**: Any additional comments or notes about the transfer.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Verify that the **Pet Owner's Name** and **Owner's Address** match the patient's records.
* Review the **Reason for Transfer** for clarity and completeness.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out before signing the form.
