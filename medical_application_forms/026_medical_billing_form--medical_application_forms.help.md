# Medical Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Billing Form is used to gather information from patients or medical practitioners for billing and administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information in the **Patient Information** section.
2. Enter your insurance details in the **Insurance Information** section.
3. Describe the procedure or services you are seeking or have received in the **Procedure Information** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `text`, optional): This section is where you can enter your personal details, such as your name, address, and contact information.
* **Insurance Information** (`insurance_info`, `text`, optional): Enter your insurance provider's name, policy number, and any other relevant details to facilitate medical billing.
* **Procedure Information** (`procedure_info`, `text`, optional): Describe the medical procedure or services you are seeking or have received, including dates and details of the treatment.
