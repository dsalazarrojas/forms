# Medical Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help patients and healthcare professionals complete the Medical Consultation Form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  Ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
3.  Review the form for any errors or discrepancies before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Patient Information

*   **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, **required**): Please enter the patient's full name.
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, **required**): Describe the patient's past medical experiences and conditions.
*   **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, **required**): List any allergies or sensitivities the patient has.
*   **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, **required**): Provide a detailed description of the patient's current medical conditions.
*   **Family History** (`family_history`, `text`, **required**): Share the patient's family medical history.
*   **Social History** (`social_history`, `text`, **required**): Describe the patient's social history and any relevant details.
*   **Patient Goals** (`patient_goals`, `text`, **required**): Outline the patient's goals and expectations.

### Page 2: Medical Consultation Information

*   **Follow-up Appointments** (`follow_up_appointments`, `date`, **required**): Schedule follow-up appointments for consistent care and monitoring.
*   **Next Visit Date** (`next_visit_date`, `date`, **required**): Plan for future visits and check-ups.
*   **Medical Professional** (`medical_professional`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the medical professional who will provide care.
*   **Patient Contact** (`patient_contact`, `text`, **required**): Ensure effective communication and coordination with the patient.
*   **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, **required**): Identify an emergency contact for unexpected situations.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, **required**): Ensure the emergency contact is properly notified in case of an emergency.
*   **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, **required**): Understand the relationship between the emergency contact and the patient.
*   **Address** (`address`, `text`, **required**): Provide the patient's address for medical record-keeping and billing.
*   **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, `text`, **optional**): Share the patient's insurance provider for billing and insurance claims.
*   **Insurance Policy Number** (`insurance_policy_number`, `text`, **optional**): Provide the patient's insurance policy number for verification.
*   **Medical Group** (`medical_group`, `text`, **required**): Choose the medical group that will provide care.
*   **Medical Group Location** (`medical_group_location`, `text`, **required**): Identify the location of the medical group for scheduling appointments.
*   **Medical Group Phone** (`medical_group_phone`, `text`, **required**): Ensure communication with the medical group.
*   **Medical Group Email** (`medical_group_email`, `text`, **optional**): Share the medical group's email address for communication.

### Additional Information

*   **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, `text`, **optional**): Share the patient's current medications for medication management.
*   **Medication Adherence** (`medication_adherence`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Indicate the patient's medication adherence.
*   **Doctor Contact** (`doctor_contact`, `text`, **optional**): Share the doctor's contact information for communication.
*   **Next Visit Date** (`next_visit_date`, `date`, **optional**): Plan for future visits and check-ups.

## Tips

*   Please review the form for accuracy before submitting.
*   Ensure all required fields are filled in before proceeding.
*   If you need help or clarification on any field, contact the medical team.
*   The medical consultation form is designed to aid in providing effective care; please complete it carefully and accurately.
