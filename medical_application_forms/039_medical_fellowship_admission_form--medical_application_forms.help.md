## ## Medical Fellowship Admission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants to the Medical Fellowship program. It is not intended for patients or medical professionals seeking medical treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Application Form** section with your basic contact information, such as name, address, and phone number.
2. In the **Applicant Information** section, provide more detailed information about yourself, such as your education and employment background.
3. In the **Medical History** section, disclose any relevant medical conditions that may affect your participation in the fellowship program.
4. In the **Medical Fellowship Information** section, provide specific details about your medical fellowship goals and objectives.
5. In the **Application Documents** section, attach any required documents, such as your curriculum vitae or proof of medical licensure.
6. Finally, click **Submit Application** to send your completed application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form** (<type:text>, <required: false>): This field is for your basic contact information. Fill in your name, address, and phone number.
* **Applicant Information** (<type:text>, <required: false>): In this section, provide more detailed information about yourself, including your education and employment history.
* **Medical History** (<type:text>, <required: false>): Disclose any relevant medical conditions that may affect your participation in the fellowship program.
* **Medical Fellowship Information** (<type:text>, <required: false>): Provide specific details about your medical fellowship goals and objectives.
* **Application Documents** (<type:text>, <required: false>): Attach any required documents, such as your curriculum vitae or proof of medical licensure.
* **Submit Application** (<type:text>, <required: false>): Click this button to send your completed application.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required sections and attach all necessary documents.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting your application.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
