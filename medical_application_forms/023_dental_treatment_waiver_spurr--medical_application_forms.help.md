# Dental Treatment Waiver Spurr - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect patient information and obtain consent for dental treatment from patients. It is essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that the patient's rights and medical practice's information are properly documented.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate medical practice for the treatment.
2. Enter the reason for the treatment.
3. Select the patient's information (gender, race, ethnicity).
4. Enter the patient's medical condition.
5. Select the medical treatment.
6. Enter the cost of the treatment.
7. Enter the medical practice's name, address, phone number, email, and fax number.
8. Enter your doctor's title and name.
9. Enter your date of birth.
10. Select your gender, race, and ethnicity.
11. Enter any medical conditions you are currently experiencing.
12. Select the medical treatment(s) you will be undergoing.
13. Enter the cost of the treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Practices** (`medical_practices`): Select the medical practice you are visiting.
* **Treatement Reason** (`treatment_reason`): Enter the reason for the treatment.
* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`): Select your gender, race, and ethnicity.
* **Patient Medical Condition** (`patient_medical_condition`): Enter any medical conditions you are currently experiencing.
* **Medical Treatment** (`medical_treatment`): Select the medical treatment(s) you will be undergoing.
* **Treatment Cost** (`treatment_cost`): Enter the cost of the treatment.
* **Medical Practice Name** (`medical_practice_name`): Enter the name of the medical practice.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`): Enter your doctor's name.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date of the treatment.
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time of the treatment.
* **Medical Practice Abbreviation** (`medical_practice_abbrev`): Enter the abbreviation of the medical practice.
* **Medical Practice Address** (`medical_practice_address`): Enter the address of the medical practice.
* **Medical Practice Phone** (`medical_practice_phone`): Enter the phone number of the medical practice.
* **Medical Practice Email** (`medical_practice_email`): Enter the email of the medical practice.
* **Medical Practice Fax** (`medical_practice_fax`): Enter the fax number of the medical practice.
* **Doctor Title** (`doctor_title`): Enter your doctor's title.
* **Patient DOB** (`patient_dob`): Enter your date of birth.
* **Patient Gender** (`patient_gender`): Select your gender.
* **Patient Race** (`patient_race`): Select your race.
* **Patient Ethnicity** (`patient_ethnicity`): Select your ethnicity.
* **Patient Medical Condition** (`patient_medical_condition`): Enter any medical conditions you are currently experiencing.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`): Sign the form to indicate your consent.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* Ensure that you understand each field's purpose before filling it out.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask your doctor or medical practice staff for clarification.
