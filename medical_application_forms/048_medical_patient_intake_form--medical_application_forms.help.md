# Medical Patient Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Patient Intake Form is a comprehensive form designed to collect detailed medical information from patients, including their medical history, allergies, medications, and emergency contacts. This information will be used for future medical reference and diagnosis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, surgeries, or medical conditions.
2. List any allergies or medical conditions you have, such as Peanut Allergies or Dairy Allergies.
3. List any medications you are currently taking, such as Antidepressants or Anti-inflammatory medications.
4. Provide contact information for emergency contacts, such as a Primary Care Physician or Emergency Room.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm you have read and understood the terms of use.
6. Optionally, add any additional medical notes or test results.
