# Medical Withdrawal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document a user's medical withdrawal status, including their medical condition, reason for withdrawal, and associated documentation.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please provide the following user information:
    *   **User Information** (`User Information`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant details.
    *   **Medical Condition** (`Medical Condition`, text, optional): Describe your current medical condition.
    *   **Medical Certificate** (`Medical Certificate`, text, optional): Attach a medical certificate related to your condition, if applicable.
    *   **Medical Documentation** (`Medical Documentation`, text, optional): Upload any relevant medical documentation, such as test results or doctor's notes.
    *   **Date of Last Attendance** (`Date of Last Attendance`, date, optional): Record the date of your last attendance.
    *   **Expected Return Date** (`Expected Return Date`, date, optional): Specify the date you expect to return.
    *   **Medical Withdrawal Reason** (`Medical Withdrawal Reason`, text, optional): Provide the reason for your medical withdrawal.
    *   **Medical Certificate Upload** (`Medical Certificate Upload`, text, optional): Upload a medical certificate.
    *   **Medical Documentation Upload** (`Medical Documentation Upload`, text, optional): Upload any further documentation related to your condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Information**: The space to enter your name and any other relevant details. If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please provide this information.
*   **Medical Condition**: A text box to describe your current medical condition. This will help us understand your current health status.
*   **Medical Certificate**: Attach a medical certificate related to your condition, if applicable. This will help verify your information.
*   **Medical Documentation**: Upload any relevant medical documentation, such as test results or doctor's notes. Please ensure these documents are clear and easily readable.
*   **Date of Last Attendance**: Record the date of your last attendance to help us track your progress.
*   **Expected Return Date**: Specify the date you expect to return. This will help with our planning and coordination.
*   **Medical Withdrawal Reason**: Provide the reason for your medical withdrawal. This will help us understand your current circumstances.
*   **Medical Certificate Upload**: Upload a medical certificate, if you have one. This will help verify your information.
*   **Medical Documentation Upload**: Upload any further documentation related to your condition. Please ensure these documents are clear and easily readable.

## Tips
*   Please ensure all required fields are completed.
*   If you have any supporting documentation, please upload it for our review.
*   Please be honest and provide accurate information.
*   If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us.
