# Pediatric Care Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pediatric Care Billing Form is designed to collect billing information for pediatric care services provided to patients under the age of 18. This form should be used to document and process billing information for non-emergency situations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Patient Information** section with the patient's name, date of birth, and age.
2. In the **Service Information** section, describe the services provided to the patient.
3. In the **Payment Information** section, provide payment details, such as insurance information or payment method.
4. In the **Insurance Information** section, provide any relevant insurance details, such as policy number and insurance provider.
5. In the **Provider Information** section, fill out the healthcare provider's name and contact information.
6. In the **Note** section, add any additional comments or notes about the patient's visit.
7. In the **Email** section, provide the patient's email address (if applicable).
8. In the **Phone** section, provide the patient's phone number (if applicable).
9. In the **Patient Age** section, enter the patient's age.
10. In the **Date of Birth** section, enter the patient's date of birth.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pediatric Care Billing** (`billing_info`, text, false): Enter the billing information for the patient.
* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, false): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and age.
* **Service Information** (`service_info`, text, false): Describe the services provided to the patient.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, false): Provide payment details, such as insurance information or payment method.
* **Insurance Information** (`insurance_info`, text, false): Provide any relevant insurance details, such as policy number and insurance provider.
* **Provider Information** (`provider_info`, text, false): Fill out the healthcare provider's name and contact information.
* **Note** (`note`, note, false): Add any additional comments or notes about the patient's visit.
* **Email** (`email`, email, false): Enter the patient's email address (if applicable).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, false): Enter the patient's phone number (if applicable).
* **Patient Age** (`patient_age`, number, false): Enter the patient's age.
* **Date of Birth** (`patient_dob`, date, false): Enter the patient's date of birth.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Review the form for errors before submission.
* Use this form for non-emergency billing situations only.
* Contact a healthcare provider or billing staff if you have any questions or concerns about the form.
