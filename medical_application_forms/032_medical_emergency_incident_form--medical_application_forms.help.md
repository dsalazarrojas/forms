# medical_emergency_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The medical emergency incident form is used to report and document incidents that have occurred during medical procedures or treatments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1.  Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the purpose and fields required.
2.  Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
3.  Ensure the form is signed and dated by the person filling it out.
4.  Submit the form to the relevant personnel for review and incident response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Incident details**: Enter a brief description of the incident, including the circumstances and any relevant details.
*   **Patient info**: Enter the patient's information, including their name, date of birth, and any other relevant information.
*   **Medical Emergency Type**: Select the type of medical emergency that occurred (e.g., 'Yes' for a severe medical emergency or 'No' for a minor incident).
*   **Medical treatments**: Enter any medical treatments or interventions administered during the incident.
*   **Incident location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred, including the room or area number.
*   **Incident date**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
*   **Incident time**: Enter the time the incident occurred.
*   **Incident description**: Enter a detailed description of the incident, including the events leading up to and following the incident.

## Tips
*   Always review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accurate and complete information.
*   Be thorough and provide as much detail as possible when completing the form.
*   If you're unsure about any part of the form, consult with your supervisor or relevant personnel for guidance.
*   The form is only for authorized personnel, so please ensure you're filling it out within your scope of work and responsibilities.
