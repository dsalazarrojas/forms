# Health Monitoring Alarm Setup - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Health Monitoring Alarm Setup" form is a crucial tool for healthcare professionals to configure alarm settings for health monitoring systems. This form helps you set up alarm frequencies, types, and notification settings to ensure timely and effective patient care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Setup" page to start the configuration process.
2. Fill out the form fields as prompted, ensuring you have selected the correct alarm frequency and type.
3. Specify the alarm threshold value, which determines when an alarm will be triggered.
4. Choose the alarm duration, indicating how long the alarm will sound.
5. Select the medication reminders you want to receive, such as Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
6. Choose your preferred alarm sound, volume, and message.
7. Enter your contact information, including your contact person, phone number, and email.
8. Review and save your setup.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Setup**: This page is where you'll begin configuring your alarm settings. Select it to start the process.
* **Alarm frequency**: Choose how often you want to receive alarms, using a numeric value.
* **Alarm type**: Select from three options: High, Low, or Medium, which will determine the urgency of the alarm.
* **Alarm threshold**: Enter a numeric value to determine when an alarm will be triggered.
* **Alarm duration**: Specify the length of time the alarm will sound, in numeric value.
* **Medication reminders**: Select one or more times when you want to receive medication reminders, such as Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
* **Alarm sound**: Choose from three options: Slight, Loud, or Silent.
* **Alarm volume**: Select from three options: Low, Medium, or High.
* **Alarm message**: Enter a custom message to be displayed during the alarm.
* **Contact person**: Enter your name and contact information.
* **Contact phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Contact email**: Enter your email address.
* **Emergency contact**: Enter the contact information for an emergency contact person.
* **Setup completed**: This field is a note for you to acknowledge that the setup is complete.
* **Setup instructions**: This field is for any additional comments or notes you'd like to include with your setup.
* **Setup comments**: This field is for any general comments or feedback you'd like to provide.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or feedback about your setup.

Tips:
* Double-check your setup before saving to avoid any issues.
* Make sure to review and edit your setup as needed.
* Keep your contact information up-to-date to ensure timely communication.
