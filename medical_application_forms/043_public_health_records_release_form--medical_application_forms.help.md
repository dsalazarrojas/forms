# Public Health Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a patient's medical records, including access to their records and the duration of such access. The form helps ensure that the patient's personal and medical information is handled according to their preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether the provider is the patient's primary care provider.
2. Enter the patient's name.
3. Enter the patient's date of birth.
4. Select whether the provider has contact information.
5. Select a date and time for record access.
6. Select a duration for record access.
7. Select whether the authorization is active or inactive.
8. Enter the authorization reason (optional).
9. Sign and date the form as the patient or their representative.
10. Sign and date the form as the provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select provider**: This field allows you to select whether the provider is the patient's primary care provider. You can choose "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
* **patient name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical record.
* **date of birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **provider contact information**: This field allows you to select whether the provider has contact information. You can choose "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
* **date of record access**: Select a date for when the record will be accessed.
* **record access time**: Select a time for when the record will be accessed.
* **record access duration**: Enter the duration of record access as a number (e.g., 1 hour, 2 days, etc.).
* **authorization type**: Select whether the authorization is active or inactive.
* **authorization reason**: If authorized, enter the reason for the authorization (e.g., "For medical research").
* **patient signature**: Sign and date the form as the patient or their representative, indicating that they have given consent.
* **provider signature**: Sign and date the form as the provider, indicating that they have agreed to access the patient's records.
* **medical record**: Select whether a medical record is associated with the patient. You can choose "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
* **medical record number**: Enter the patient's medical record number (if applicable).
* **record access location**: Select where the record will be accessed (e.g., in-person, remotely, etc.).
* **record access status**: Select whether the record is active or inactive.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by the patient or their representative, and should be reviewed and signed by the provider to ensure that the patient's medical information is handled according to their preferences.
