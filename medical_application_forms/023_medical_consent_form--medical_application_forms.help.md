# Medical Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain medical consent from patients for medical procedures. It collects essential information about the patient's medical details, treatment, and consent status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Choose the correct medical procedure from the options provided.
3. Enter the consent date and medical provider's information.
4. Add any relevant medical notes if necessary.
5. Enter the patient's email and phone number for follow-up purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Consent Form** (Text, Required: No): This field is a general description of the form and is not editable. Please do not fill in this field.
* **Patient Details** (Text, Required: No): Here, you can enter any relevant information about the patient, such as name, date of birth, and medical history.
* **Medical Procedure** (Select One, Required: No): Please choose the medical procedure that the patient is undergoing or has undergone. This will help the healthcare provider understand the nature of the treatment.
* **Consent Date** (Date, Required: No): Enter the date of when the patient gave their consent for the medical procedure.
* **Medical Provider** (Text, Required: No): Enter the name and contact information of the medical provider who is treating the patient.
* **Medical Treatment** (Text, Required: No): Describe the medical treatment the patient is receiving or has received.
* **Medical Condition** (Text, Required: No): Enter any relevant medical conditions that the patient has or is being treated for.
* **Medical Status** (Select One, Required: No): Please choose the current medical status of the patient, such as "Active Treatment" or "Discharged".
* **Medical Notes** (Note, Required: No): Add any additional medical notes or comments that are relevant to the patient's treatment.
* **Medical Email** (Email, Required: No): Enter the patient's email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Medical Phone** (Text, Required: No): Enter the patient's phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **Medical Form Submitted By** (Text, Required: No): This field is for recording the name of the person who submitted the form, not for the patient.
