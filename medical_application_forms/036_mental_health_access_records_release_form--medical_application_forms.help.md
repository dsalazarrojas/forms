# Mental Health Access Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Access Records Release Form is designed to collect patient-specific information for mental health treatment and care. It helps medical professionals understand a patient's medical history, treatment preferences, and consent status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct answer to "Patient Identifier" regarding your status as a patient.
2. If you are filling out this form as an authorized representative, provide the name and contact information of the patient they are representing.
3. Answer the question regarding whether a mental health provider is involved or not.
4. Provide the date of the treatment or release, if applicable.
5. Write down the reason for the treatment release, if any.
6. Indicate your consent status.
7. Choose a preferred contact method for follow-up.
8. Indicate your consent status for the release of your medical records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Identifier (Page 1)**: This field determines whether you are a patient or not.
  - If you answer "Yes", you will be considered a patient and the form will proceed accordingly.
  - If you answer "No", the form will not be completed.
* **Authorized Representative (Page 2)**: This field is for patients who are not able to complete the form independently.
  - Please provide the name and contact information of the representative.
* **Mental Health Provider (Page 3)**: This field asks whether a mental health provider is involved in your treatment.
  - Answer "Yes" if you are currently receiving mental health treatment.
  - Answer "No" if you are not receiving mental health treatment.
* **Date (Page 4)**: This field is for recording the date of treatment or release.
  - Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2020).
* **Time (Page 5)**: This field is for recording the time of treatment or release.
  - Please enter the time in 12-hour format (e.g., 10:00 AM).
* **Reason for Release (Page 6)**: This field is optional and allows you to provide a reason for the treatment release.
  - Please provide a clear and concise explanation for the treatment release.
* **Consent (Page 7)**: This field asks whether you grant or deny consent for the release of your medical records.
  - Answer "Yes" if you grant consent.
  - Answer "No" if you deny consent.
* **Release Date (Page 8)**: This field is for recording the date when your medical records are released.
  - Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Signature (Page 9)**: This field is for the authorized representative to sign and date.
  - Please print your name and date.
* **Contact Preference (Page 10)**: This field asks whether you prefer a contact method (e.g., phone, email, or mail).
  - Choose your preferred contact method from the options.
