# Medical Consent Withdrawal Amendment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Medical Consent Withdrawal Amendment Form is used to withdraw or amend a medical consent that has been previously granted. This form is typically used when a patient or their representative wishes to rescind or modify their consent for a particular treatment or procedure.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" from the drop-down menu to indicate whether you wish to withdraw your medical consent.
2. If you select "Yes", describe the reason for withdrawal in the text field provided.
3. In the "Medical Provider Comments" field, the healthcare provider can add any additional comments about the situation.
4. In the "Patient Comments" field, the patient or their representative can add any additional comments about the situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **<label> Withdraw Medical Consent** (<type> `select_one`, **required** no): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you wish to withdraw your medical consent. This is the main decision point for withdrawing consent.
* **Reason for Withdrawal** (<type> `text`, **required** no): If you selected "Yes" in the previous field, describe the reason for withdrawing your consent in this text field.
* **Medical Provider Comments** (<type> `text`, **required** no): If the healthcare provider has any additional information to add about the situation, they can enter their comments here.
* **Patient Comments** (<type> `text`, **required** no): The patient or their representative can add any additional comments or questions they have about the form or the situation here.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you understand the implications of withdrawing or amending your medical consent before completing this form.
* If you are having trouble understanding any of the fields, ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* Complete this form carefully and accurately to ensure that your wishes are respected and recorded correctly.
