# Health Screening Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help patients provide medical information to the healthcare provider for routine health screenings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your patient information in the "patient_information" field.
2. Provide your medical history in the "medical_history" field.
3. Select any relevant health screenings from the "health_screenings" field.
4. List any medical conditions you have in the "medical_conditions" field.
5. Specify any allergies you have in the "allergies" field.
6. List your current medications in the "medications" field.
7. Provide your contact information in the "contact_information" field.
8. Specify the medical facility you're visiting in the "medical_facility" field.
9. Choose your healthcare provider from the "healthcare_provider" field.
10. Enter the date of your visit in the "date_of_visit" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`): Enter your personal details, such as your name, address, and contact number.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`): Write down any relevant medical history, such as previous surgeries or illnesses.
* **Health Screenings** (`health_screenings`): Select any health screenings you've undergone or plan to undergo.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`): List any chronic conditions you have, such as diabetes or high blood pressure.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`): Specify any allergies you have, such as latex or peanuts.
* **Medications** (`medications`): List any medications you're currently taking.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`): Provide your contact information, such as your phone number and address.
* **Medical Facility** (`medical_facility`): Specify the medical facility you're visiting.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`): Choose your healthcare provider from the dropdown menu.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`): Enter the date of your scheduled visit.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* Make sure to include any relevant medical history or allergies.
* If you're unsure about any field, don't hesitate to ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
