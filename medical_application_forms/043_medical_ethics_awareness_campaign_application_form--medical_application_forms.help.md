# Medical Ethics Awareness Campaign Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Ethics Awareness Campaign Application Form is designed to gather information from participants on their motivations, consent, and details for the campaign. This form will help us understand participants' attitudes and behaviors regarding medical ethics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the **Participant Details** section, providing any relevant information about yourself.
2. In the **Motivations** section, explain why you're interested in participating in the campaign.
3. For **Consent**, select either "Yes" or "No" to indicate your willingness to participate.
4. In the **Consent 2** field, repeat the same consent statement as in **Consent**.
5. The **Additional Participant Details** sections provide extra information about your background and interests. You can select one or multiple options depending on the field type.
6. In the **Email** field, enter your email address if you want to be contacted for further information.
7. For **Phone**, provide your phone number if you want to be contacted via phone.
8. In the **Participant Details 4** and **Participant Details 8** fields, provide any additional details you think are relevant to your participation.
9. In the **Additional Participant Details 2** field, select one option that best describes your situation.
10. In the **Participant Details 3** field, provide any additional details you think are relevant.
11. In the **Additional Participant Details 3** field, select one or multiple options that best describe your situation.
12. In the **Participant Details 7** field, provide any additional details you think are relevant.
13. In the **Additional Participant Details 4** field, enter a number that describes your level of interest.
14. In the **Participant Details 9** field, provide any additional details you think are relevant.
15. In the **Additional Participant Details 5** field, provide any additional details you think are relevant.
