# Telehealth Training Certification Program Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telehealth Training Certification Program Application form is used to apply for a telehealth training certification program. This form is intended for individuals who are interested in becoming certified in telehealth and want to demonstrate their knowledge, skills, and experience in this field.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Enter your **Program Name**, which is the title of the telehealth program you are applying for.
2. Provide your **Email**, which will be used as your contact information.
3. Enter your **Phone** number, which will be used for communication purposes.
4. Enter the **Training Provider Name**, which is the organization or individual providing the telehealth training.
5. Select your **Training Type**, which can be Telehealth, In-person, or Online.
6. Enter the **Start Date** and **End Date** of the telehealth training program.
7. Enter your **Address**, which includes the physical location of the training program.
8. Enter the **City** where the training program is located.
9. Enter the **State** where the training program is located.
10. Enter the **Zip** code of the training program location.
11. Enter your **Contact Name**, which is the name of the individual or organization contacting you.
12. Select your **Role**, which can be Director, Coordinator, Trainer, or Administrator.
13. Enter **Program Details**, which is a brief description of the telehealth training program.
14. Enter the **Hours Completed**, which is the number of hours you have completed in the program.
15. Enter the **Hours Remaining**, which is the number of hours you have remaining in the program.
16. Select the **Certificate Status**, which can be Not Issued, Pending, Issued, or Expired.
17. Enter the **Certification Date**, which is the date you received your telehealth certification.
18. Enter the **Last Update**, which is the date of your last update to the certification program.
