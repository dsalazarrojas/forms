# Hospital Leadership Exchange Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Leadership Exchange Program Application Form is designed to collect information from hospital employees who wish to participate in a leadership exchange program. This program allows employees to gain leadership experience and skills by working in different hospital settings, broadening their perspectives and expertise. To ensure the integrity and effectiveness of the program, this form serves as a tool for evaluating applicants' suitability, work experience, and motivation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out your personal details: Start by providing your applicant name.
* Answer questions truthfully: You will be asked about your work experience, motivation for participating in the program, contact information, and if you hold any medical licensure. Answer these questions based on your actual experiences and motivations.
* Select your relevant details: For the medical licensure question, you will need to select 'True' or 'False' depending on your actual licensure status.
* Verify your contact details: Select the correct contact method(s) you prefer to be contacted through (e.g., phone or email).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name (1)**: This is the first page of the form, where you will be asked to enter your name.
* **Work Experience (2)**: Indicate if you have work experience. If yes, you can select the options that best describe your experience.
* **Motivation (3)**: Share your motivation for participating in the leadership exchange program.
* **Contact Details (4)**: Choose your preferred contact method(s) for the program organizers to reach you.
* **Medical Licensure (5)**: Indicate if you hold any medical licensure or not.

## Tips
This form is designed to be straightforward, but if you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
