# Medical Insurance Verification Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by medical professionals to collect and verify patient insurance information, ensuring accurate and complete data for efficient claims processing and patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the patient's name in the **patient_name** field.
2.  Provide the patient's date of birth in **patient_dob** field in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
3.  Enter the patient's address in **patient_address** field.
4.  Enter the patient's phone number in **patient_phone** field.
5.  Enter any relevant insurance information (e.g., provider name, group number, etc.) in **insurance_info** field.
6.  Enter any information about the doctor or medical service provider in **doctor_info** field.
7.  Enter any claims-related information in **claim_info** field.
8.  Enter billing information for the patient's care in **billing_info** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **patient_name (patient_name)**: Enter the patient's full name.
*  **patient_dob (patient_dob)**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*  **patient_address (patient_address)**: Enter the patient's current address.
*  **patient_phone (patient_phone)**: Enter the patient's phone number.
*  **insurance_info (insurance_info)**: Enter any relevant insurance details (e.g., provider name, group number, etc.).
*  **doctor_info (doctor_info)**: Enter the name and contact information of the doctor or medical service provider.
*  **claim_info (claim_info)**: Enter any claims-related information (e.g., claim number, claim status, etc.).
*  **billing_info (billing_info)**: Enter billing information for the patient's care (e.g., insurance company, payment methods, etc.).
