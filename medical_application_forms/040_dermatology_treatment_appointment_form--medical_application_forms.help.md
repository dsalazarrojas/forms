# Dermatology Treatment Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient information and schedule appointments for various dermatological treatments.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Patient Name**: Please enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification documents.
2. **Contact Number**: Enter the patient's contact phone number.
3. **Email**: Enter the patient's email address.
4. **Preferred Contact Method**: Select your preferred contact method (Phone, Email, or Mail).
5. **Appointment Date**: Enter the date of the appointment.
6. **Appointment Time**: Enter the time of the appointment.
7. **Treatment Type**: Select the type of treatment you are booking an appointment for (Consultation, Skin Treatment, or Surgery).
8. **Appointment Length**: Enter the length of the appointment (in minutes).
9. **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments for the appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: The patient's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Contact Number**: The patient's contact phone number.
* **Email**: The patient's email address.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: The method you prefer to be contacted (Phone, Email, or Mail).
* **Appointment Date**: The date of the scheduled appointment.
* **Appointment Time**: The time of the scheduled appointment.
* **Treatment Type**: The type of dermatological treatment being booked.
* **Appointment Length**: The length of the appointment in minutes.
* **Additional Notes**: Any additional comments or notes for the appointment.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in completely and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact the clinic staff.
* If you need to make any changes to the appointment, please contact the clinic staff to arrange an alternative time.
