# Against Medical Advice Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Against Medical Advice Form" is a critical document used to protect patients and healthcare providers when a patient decides to leave the hospital against the advice of their physician. It ensures that patients understand the risks involved and acknowledges their decision to take responsibility for their health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information and documentation ready before starting the form.
2. Fill in the patient's full name, date of birth, and medical record number (MRN) in the respective fields.
3. Enter the attending physician's name, as this is a critical medical professional involved in the patient's care.
4. Review and acknowledge the risks associated with leaving the hospital, selecting all applicable risks from the list provided.
5. Provide the patient's stated reason for leaving the hospital in the "Patient's stated reason for leaving" field.
6. Sign the liability release, acknowledging that you release the hospital and physicians from any liability resulting from your decision to leave.
7. Enter the witness's name, who can attest to the patient's understanding of the risks and their decision to leave.
8. Finally, enter the date and time of the patient's departure.
