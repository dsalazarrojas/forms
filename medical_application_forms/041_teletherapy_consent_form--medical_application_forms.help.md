# teletherapy_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient information and consent for teletherapy sessions. It ensures that patients are aware of their personal data and provide their consent for the collection and use of such data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information, including their name, date of birth, and contact details.
2. Review and agree to the consent statement, ensuring you understand the terms and conditions.
3. Sign the E-Signature field, confirming that you have read and understood the consent statement.
4. Provide the current date and time of the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (patient_info)**: This field is meant to collect the patient's basic information, including their name and date of birth.
* **Contact Information (contact_info)**: Enter the patient's contact details, which will be used to communicate with them about their therapy sessions.
* **Consent Statement (consent_statement)**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the teletherapy sessions, which includes the use of their personal data.
* **Date of Birth (date_of_birth)**: Enter the patient's date of birth, which will be used to verify their age eligibility for the therapy sessions.
* **E-Signature (e_signature)**: Sign this field to confirm that you have read and understood the consent statement.
* **Patient Name (patient_name)**: Enter the patient's name, which will be associated with their therapy sessions.
* **Date (patient_date)**: Enter the current date of the therapy session.
* **Time (patient_time)**: Enter the current time of the therapy session.

## Tips

* Make sure to read and understand the consent statement before signing the E-Signature field.
* Use this form only for legitimate purposes and not for spamming or data collection.
* Refer to your organization's guidelines and regulations for further guidance on data protection.
