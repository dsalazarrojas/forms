# Clinical Trial Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect clinical trial information from participants for research or administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as a participant.
2. Enter the clinical trial ID number.
3. Provide your date of joining the clinical trial.
4. Indicate your current study visit number.
5. Describe any new health conditions you have experienced since joining the trial.
6. List your current medications and their dosage.
7. Rate your satisfaction with the study communication (1-5).
8. Answer whether you find the study visit locations convenient for you.
9. Provide any suggestions for improving participant engagement.
10. Choose your preferred method for receiving study updates (Email, SMS, or Phone Call).
11. Sign with your digital signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Full Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as a participant.
* **Clinical Trial ID Number** (`trial_id`, text, required): Enter the clinical trial ID number.
* **Date Joined Clinical Trial** (`join_date`, date, required): Specify the date you joined the clinical trial.
* **Current Study Visit Number** (`visit_number`, number, required): Enter your current study visit number.
* **Have you experienced any new health conditions since joining?** (`new_health_conditions`, select_one, required): Select "True" if you have experienced any new health conditions since joining the clinical trial, or "False" if not.
* **Current Medications - Please include name and dosage** (`current_medications_dosage`, text, required): List your current medications and their dosage.
* **How satisfied are you with the study communication?** (`communication_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with the study communication on a 1-5 scale.
* **Are the study visit locations convenient for you?** (`visit_location_convenience`, select_one, required): Choose whether the study visit locations are convenient for you ("True" or "False").
* **Do you have any suggestions for improving participant engagement?** (`engagement_suggestions`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions for improving participant engagement.
* **Preferred method for receiving study updates** (`update_method_pref`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred method for receiving study updates (Email, SMS, or Phone Call).
* **Participant Digital Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, required): Sign with your digital signature.
