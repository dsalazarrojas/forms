## pre_visit_planning_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Pre-Visit Planning Form** is designed to collect essential information from patients before their scheduled visit. This form helps healthcare professionals gather necessary details to ensure a smooth and efficient visit experience. By completing this form, patients can provide their medical history, contact information, and other relevant details that will aid the healthcare team in preparing for their visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Pre-Visit Planning Form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your **Patient Name**, ensuring it matches the one on your appointment schedule.
2. In the **Medical History** field, share any previous medical conditions, allergies, or medications that may be relevant to your visit.
3. In the **Contact Information** field, list your emergency contact's name and any other important contact numbers.
4. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the **Reason For Visit** question, which helps us understand the purpose of your appointment.
5. If necessary, provide your **Contact Number** so we can reach you quickly in case of an emergency.
6. If applicable, list the name and contact information of your **Emergency Contact** who we can contact in case of an emergency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your appointment schedule.
- **Medical History**: This field is for any previous medical conditions, allergies, or medications that you are currently taking or have taken in the past.
  * Please be as detailed as possible to help us better understand your health history.
- **Contact Information**: In this field, list your emergency contact's name and any other contact numbers where we can reach you.
  * Ensure that you have their approval to share this information with us.
- **Reason For Visit**: Please answer "Yes" if your visit is for a non-routine checkup or if you are unsure. Answer "No" if you are here for a scheduled surgery, lab tests, or other specific appointments.
  * This helps us understand the purpose of your visit, which is crucial for our preparation and attention.
- **Contact Number**: If you wish to be contacted in case of an emergency, please provide your contact number. If not necessary, you may leave it blank.
- **Emergency Contact Name**: If you have an emergency contact person, please list their name here, along with their contact information.
  * Ensure they are aware of your visit and can be reached in case of an emergency.

Note: This form is not intended for patients to fill out on their own. It should be completed by healthcare professionals before the patient's visit.
