# Circulatory Health Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Circulatory Health Assessment Form is designed to collect information about a patient's circulatory system health.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the patient's demographic information, including their name, date of birth, and patient ID or medical record number.
2. Record the assessment date to track when the evaluation was performed.
3. Record the patient's vital signs, including blood pressure, heart rate, height, and weight.
4. Check the patient's current symptoms, such as chest pain, shortness of breath, and leg or ankle swelling.
5. Record the patient's medical and family history, including history of high blood pressure, high cholesterol, diabetes, and family history of heart disease.
6. Record the patient's lifestyle factors, including smoking status, alcohol use, and physical activity level.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Please enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Patient ID or Medical Record Number**: Enter the patient's ID or medical record number if known.
* **Assessment Date**: Record the date the evaluation was performed.
* **Systolic Blood Pressure (mmHg)**: Record the patient's systolic blood pressure in millimeters of mercury.
* **Diastolic Blood Pressure (mmHg)**: Record the patient's diastolic blood pressure in millimeters of mercury.
* **Heart Rate (beats per minute)**: Record the patient's heart rate in beats per minute.
* **Height (cm or inches)**: Record the patient's height in centimeters or inches.
* **Weight (kg or lbs)**: Record the patient's weight in kilograms or pounds.
* **Experiencing Chest Pain or Discomfort**: Check "Yes" if the patient is experiencing chest pain or discomfort.
* **Description of Chest Pain**: Provide a brief description of the chest pain or discomfort, including location and severity.
* **Shortness of Breath**: Check "Yes" if the patient is experiencing shortness of breath.
* **Dizziness or Fainting Episodes**: Check "Yes" if the patient is experiencing dizziness or fainting episodes.
* **Leg or Ankle Swelling**: Check "Yes" if the patient is experiencing leg or ankle swelling.
* **Heart Palpitations or Irregular Heartbeat**: Check "Yes" if the patient is experiencing heart palpitations or irregular heartbeat.
* **History of High Blood Pressure**: Check "Yes" if the patient has a history of high blood pressure.
* **High Cholesterol**: Check "Yes" if the patient has high cholesterol.
* **Diabetes**: Check "Yes" if the patient has diabetes.
* **Family History of Heart Disease**: Check "Yes" if the patient has a family history of heart disease.
* **Smoking Status**: Check "Never smoked," "Former smoker," or "Current smoker."
* **Alcohol Use**: Check "None," "Occasional," or "Heavy."
* **Physical Activity Level**: Check "Sedentary," "Light activity," "Moderate activity," or "Vigorous activity."
* **Details of Previous Cardiac Events**: Provide a brief description of any previous cardiac events, including date, type, and treatment.
* **Exercise Tolerance**: Provide a brief description of how much physical activity the patient can perform before symptoms appear.
