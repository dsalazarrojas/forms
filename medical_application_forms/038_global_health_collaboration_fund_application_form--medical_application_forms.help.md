# Global Health Collaboration Fund Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Global Health Collaboration Fund Application Form is designed to gather information from applicants for the Global Health Collaboration Fund. This form is used to collect details about the applicant, the project, and the submitter, as well as their agreements and acknowledgements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Start Application" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Fill out each section of the form carefully, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
3. Review the form for accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Applicant Details
* **Applicant Details** (`applicant_details`, text, required): Please enter your name and contact information as the applicant.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required): Enter your name and contact information as the contact person for the project.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, required): Describe your project in a brief paragraph.
* **Expected Start Date** (`expected_start_date`, date, required): Enter the expected start date of your project.
* **Expected End Date** (`expected_end_date`, date, required): Enter the expected end date of your project.
* **Budget Details** (`budget_details`, number, required): Enter the budget details for your project.
* **Submitter Details** (`submitter_details`, text, required): Enter your name and contact information as the submitter.
* **Submitter Info** (`submitter_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about yourself as the submitter.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the contact person for the project.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the contact person for the project.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person for the project.
* **Submitter Signature** (`submitter_signature`, note, required): Sign the form to confirm that you are the submitter.
* **Submitter Comments** (`submitter_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your project.
* **Submitter Agreement** (`submitter_agreement`, select_one, required): Select one of the options to indicate your agreement with the terms and conditions of the Global Health Collaboration Fund.
* **Submitter Acknowledgement** (`submitter_acknowledgement`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more of the options to acknowledge your understanding of the terms and conditions of the Global Health Collaboration Fund.
* **Applicant Signature** (`applicant_signature`, note, required): Sign the form to confirm that you are the applicant.
* **Applicant Comments** (`applicant_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your project.
* **Applicant Agreement** (`applicant_agreement`, select_one, required): Select one of the options to indicate your agreement with the terms and conditions of the Global Health Collaboration Fund.
* **Applicant Acknowledgement** (`applicant_acknowledgement`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more of the options to acknowledge your understanding of the terms and conditions of the Global Health Collaboration Fund.
* **Applicant Agreement 2** (`applicant_agreement2`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options to indicate your agreement with the terms and conditions of the Global Health Collaboration Fund (if required by the fund).
* **Submitter Info 2** (`submitter_info2`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about yourself as the submitter.
* **Submitter Comments 2** (`submitter_comments2`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your project.
* **Submitter Agreement 2** (`submitter_agreement2`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options to indicate your agreement with the terms and conditions of the Global Health Collaboration Fund (if required by the fund).
* **Submitter Acknowledgement 2** (`submitter_acknowledgement2`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the options to acknowledge your understanding of the terms and conditions of the Global Health Collaboration Fund (if required by the fund).
* **Applicant Info 2** (`applicant_info2`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about yourself as the applicant.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you are reapplying, please ensure that you are not duplicating information or submitting a revised application for the same project.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all information is accurate and complete.
