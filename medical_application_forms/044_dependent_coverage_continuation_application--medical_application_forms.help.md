# Dependent Coverage Continuation Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dependent Coverage Continuation Application form is designed for individuals to apply for dependent coverage continuation, allowing them to maintain their health insurance coverage for their dependents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide information about your dependent(s) in the "Dependent Info" field.
2. Select the type of dependent you are applying for (Child, Spouse, or Domestic Partner) in the "Dependent Type" field.
3. Specify the type of coverage your dependent requires (Full-Time, Part-Time, or Variable) in the "Dependent Coverage Type" field.
4. Provide any additional dependent information in the "Dependent Info 2" field.
5. Enter your information as the applicant in the "Applicant Info" field.
6. Select the reason for continuation (Medical, Non-Medical, or Other) in the "Reason for Continuation" field.
7. Provide a detailed reason for continuation in the "Reason for Continuation Detail" field, if applicable.
8. If you selected "Other" as the reason for continuation, provide a brief explanation in the "Reason for Continuation Other" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dependent Info**: Enter a brief description of your dependent(s), including their name, date of birth, and any other relevant details.
* **Dependent Type**: Select the type of dependent you are applying for. This field is used to determine the eligibility of your dependent for coverage.
	+ Child: Select this option if you are a parent or guardian applying for coverage for your child(ren).
	+ Spouse: Select this option if you are applying for coverage for your spouse.
	+ Domestic Partner: Select this option if you are in a domestic partnership and applying for coverage for your partner.
* **Dependent Coverage Type**: Specify the type of coverage your dependent requires. This field helps us understand their coverage needs.
	+ Full-Time: Select this option if your dependent requires full-time coverage.
	+ Part-Time: Select this option if your dependent requires part-time coverage.
	+ Variable: Select this option if your dependent requires variable coverage.
* **Dependent Info 2**: Provide any additional information about your dependent, such as their address or contact information.
* **Applicant Info**: Enter your information as the applicant, including your name, date of birth, and any other relevant details.
* **Reason for Continuation**: Select the reason for continuation. This field helps us understand why you are applying for dependent coverage continuation.
	+ Medical: Select this option if you are applying for medical coverage for your dependent.
	+ Non-Medical: Select this option if you are applying for non-medical coverage for your dependent.
	+ Other: Select this option if you are applying for other reasons.
* **Reason for Continuation Detail**: Provide a detailed reason for continuation, if applicable. This field helps us understand your dependent's specific needs.
* **Reason for Continuation Other**: If you selected "Other" as the reason for continuation, provide a brief explanation of your dependent's situation.
