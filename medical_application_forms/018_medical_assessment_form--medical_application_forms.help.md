# Medical Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect medical information about a patient's history, conditions, and assessment results. It is intended to be used by healthcare professionals to gather relevant data for medical assessment and treatment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling out the **Medical History** field, which is a text field where you can provide a brief description of the patient's medical history.
2. Select the relevant medical conditions that apply to the patient from the **Contact Details** field. You can choose multiple options from the dropdown menu.
3. In the **Medical Conditions** field, select the specific medical conditions that the patient has. You can choose one option from the dropdown menu.
4. For the **Medical History Disease** field, select the diseases that the patient has had in their medical history. You can choose multiple options from the dropdown menu.
5. In the **Medical History Medication** field, select the medications that the patient is currently taking. You can choose multiple options from the dropdown menu.
6. For the **Medical History Allergies** field, select the allergies that the patient has. You can choose multiple options from the dropdown menu.
7. In the **Medical History Operations** field, select the surgeries or operations that the patient has undergone. You can choose multiple options from the dropdown menu.
8. For the **Medical History Injuries** field, select the injuries that the patient has experienced. You can choose multiple options from the dropdown menu.
9. In the **Medical History Families** field, select the family medical history that applies. You can choose multiple options from the dropdown menu.
10. For the **Medical History Social** field, select the social history that is relevant to the patient's medical condition. You can choose multiple options from the dropdown menu.
11. In the **Medical History Education** field, select the patient's educational background. You can choose multiple options from the dropdown menu.
12. For the **Medical History Employment** field, select the patient's employment status. You can choose multiple options from the dropdown menu.
13. In the **Medical History Residences** field, select the patient's residence status. You can choose multiple options from the dropdown menu.
14. For the **Medical History Travel** field, select the places the patient has traveled to. You can choose multiple options from the dropdown menu.
15. In the **Medical History Vaccinations** field, select the vaccinations the patient has received. You can choose multiple options from the dropdown menu.
16. For the **Medical History Immunizations** field, select the immunizations the patient has received. You can choose multiple options from the dropdown menu.
17. In the **Medical History Medication** field, select the medications the patient is currently taking. You can choose multiple options from the dropdown menu.
18. For the **Medical History Other** field, provide any other relevant medical history information that is not included in the other fields. This is a free text field.
19. In the **Medical Assessment Result** field, select the outcome of the medical assessment. You can choose one option from the dropdown menu.
20. For the **Medical Test Request** field, select the medical tests or examinations that are required. You can choose multiple options from the dropdown menu.
21. In the **Medical Test Result** field, select the result of the medical tests or examinations. You can choose multiple options from the dropdown menu.
22. For the **Medical Assessment Form Status** field, select the status of the medical assessment form. You can choose one option from the dropdown menu.
23. In the **Medical Assessment Form Version** field, select the version of the medical assessment form. You can choose one option from the dropdown menu.
24. For the **Medical Assessment Form Date** field, enter the date of the medical assessment form completion.
