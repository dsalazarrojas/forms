# Patient Encounter Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's medical encounter. It's meant to be used for medical applications, such as patient visits, procedures, or billing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Form Title** field with a brief description of the patient's encounter.
2. Enter the **Date** of the patient's encounter in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Enter the **Time** of the patient's encounter.
4. Select the **Provider** who attended to the patient's encounter.
5. Describe the **Purpose** of the patient's encounter in the text field.
6. Enter the **Charges** associated with the patient's encounter.
7. Select the **Insurance** type from the options provided (Private, Medicaid, Medicare).
8. Enter the **Address** of the patient's encounter.
9. Enter the **City** of the patient's encounter.
10. Enter the **State** of the patient's encounter.
11. Enter the **Zip** code of the patient's encounter.
12. Enter the **Country** of the patient's encounter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (text): Enter a brief description of the patient's encounter.
* **Date** (date): Enter the date of the patient's encounter in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time** (time): Enter the time of the patient's encounter.
* **Provider** (text): Select the healthcare provider who attended to the patient's encounter.
* **Purpose** (text): Describe the purpose of the patient's encounter in the text field.
* **Charges** (number): Enter the charges associated with the patient's encounter.
* **Insurance** (select_one): Select the insurance type from the options provided (Private, Medicaid, Medicare).
* **Address** (text): Enter the address of the patient's encounter.
* **City** (text): Enter the city of the patient's encounter.
* **State** (text): Enter the state of the patient's encounter.
* **Zip** (text): Enter the zip code of the patient's encounter.
* **Country** (text): Enter the country of the patient's encounter.
