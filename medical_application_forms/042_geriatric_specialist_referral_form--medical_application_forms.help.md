# Geriatric Specialist Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Geriatric Specialist Referral Form is used to collect information for referral to a geriatric specialist. This form is designed to be completed by healthcare providers and will be reviewed by a medical team before being sent to the specialist.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Patient Information" page with the patient's details, including any relevant medical history.
2. Select the patient's geriatric status: Geriatric, Non Geriatric, or Not Applicable.
3. Enter any Referral Information, such as the reason for referral.
4. Choose a date for referral.
5. Enter any relevant medical history.
6. Provide any additional Specialist Information, such as specific treatments or medications.
7. Enter any Referral Details, such as the specialist's name and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (Page 1)** (`patient_info`, text, required): Please enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant details.
* **Geriatric Status (Page 2)** (`geriatric_status`, select_multiple, required): Select the patient's geriatric status: Geriatric, Non Geriatric, or Not Applicable.
* **Referral Information (Page 3)** (`referral_info`, text, required): Enter the reason for referral, such as a specific medical condition or treatment.
* **Referral Date (Page 4)** (`referral_date`, date, required): Enter the date for referral.
* **Medical History (Page 5)** (`medical_history`, text, required): Enter any relevant medical history, such as previous diagnoses or treatments.
* **Specialist Information (Page 6)** (`specialist_info`, text, required): Enter the specialist's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Referral Details (Page 7)** (`referral_details`, text, required): Enter any additional information about the referral, such as the specialist's contact information or any relevant medical records.

Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely before submitting the form.
