# Medical Application Signature Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Application Signature Request form is designed to collect medical information for applications, including patient signatures, date of birth, and medical histories.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand all the fields and their purposes.
2. For each field, provide the required information or select a relevant option.
3. Complete all the fields in a clear and legible handwriting.
4. Sign the form with your signature and date it.
5. Return the completed form to the designated authority.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical Application Form**: This field is a free-text area where you can provide any necessary medical information.
* **Patient Signature**: Select 'Yes' if you are the patient, or 'No' if you are not. This will confirm that you are the patient or not.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Medical History**: This field is for you to describe any pre-existing medical conditions or allergies you may have.
* **Medical History 1/2**: These fields are for you to describe any additional medical histories or conditions you may have. You can use as many or as few as you need.
* **Medical Procedure**: Describe any medical procedures or treatments you have undergone or are recommended to undergo.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date and time you signed this form.
* **Healthcare Provider Name**: Enter the name of your healthcare provider who referred you for this medical application.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- Ensure all information is complete and legible.
- Return the form to the designated authority after completion.
- Do not use this form for other purposes than medical applications.
