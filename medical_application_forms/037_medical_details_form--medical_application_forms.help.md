# Medical Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Details Form is designed to collect essential medical information from users, including their medical history and allergies. This form will help healthcare professionals gather accurate and complete data to provide optimal care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you understand what information is requested.
2. Fill out the Allergies and Medications page with any allergies and medications you are currently taking.
3. Provide a brief description of your allergies and medications on the Allergies and Medications Description page.
4. Select the correct Patient Info option from the dropdown list.
5. Complete the Medical History page with any relevant medical history you have.
6. Enter a brief description of your medical history on the Medical History Description page.
7. Enter the start date and end date of your medical history.
8. Enter the start and end times of your medical history.
9. If applicable, enter the second start date of your medical history.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Allergies and Medications** (text): Enter any allergies or medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies and Medications Description** (text): Provide a brief description of your allergies and medications.
* **Patient Info** (select_one): Select the correct option from the dropdown list.
* **Medical History** (text): Enter any relevant medical history.
* **Medical History Description** (text): Enter a brief description of your medical history.
* **Medical History Start Date** (date): Enter the start date of your medical history.
* **Medical History End Date** (date): Enter the end date of your medical history.
* **Medical History Start Time** (time): Enter the start time of your medical history.
* **Medical History End Time** (time): Enter the end time of your medical history.
* **Medical History Start Date 2** (date): If applicable, enter the second start date of your medical history.
