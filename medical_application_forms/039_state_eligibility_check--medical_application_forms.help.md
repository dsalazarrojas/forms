# State Eligibility Check - Help Guide

## Purpose
The State Eligibility Check form is used to verify whether a patient is eligible for medical services within a specific state. This form helps healthcare professionals to assess and determine the eligibility of patients for services such as medication, treatment, or procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the correct state from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the patient's zip code.
3. Enter the patient's city name.
4. Provide any additional comments or information relevant to the eligibility check.
5. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **State Eligibility Check** (<state_eligibility_check_form>, select_one): Select 'Yes' if the patient is eligible for medical services in the state, or 'No' if they are not.
* **State Name** (<state_name>, text): Enter the name of the state where the patient is seeking medical services.
* **Zip Code** (<zip_code, number): Enter the patient's zip code to help narrow down their location within the state.
* **City Name** (<city_name, text): Enter the name of the city where the patient is receiving medical services.
* **State Eligibility Check Comments** (<state_eligibility_check_comments, note): Provide any additional comments or information regarding the patient's eligibility status.

## Tips
- Make sure to select the correct state from the dropdown menu to ensure accurate eligibility verification.
- Double-check the patient's zip code and city name to ensure accuracy.
- Be thorough in providing comments or additional information, as it may be relevant to the patient's eligibility status.
