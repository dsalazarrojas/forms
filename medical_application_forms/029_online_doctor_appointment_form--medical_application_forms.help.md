# Online Doctor Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online doctor appointment form is designed to collect information from patients to schedule appointments with healthcare providers. This form helps healthcare providers manage their schedules and patient interactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the patient information section with your name, phone number, and any other relevant details.
2. Choose the schedule option (Yes or No) to indicate whether you are available to schedule an appointment.
3. Select a date and time for your appointment, if applicable.
4. Enter your doctor's contact information (Doctor ID) if you wish to receive updates or confirmations.
5. Provide additional information about your appointment, such as duration and type.
6. Add any notes or comments about your appointment.
7. Confirm your appointment details and select the confirmation option (Option 1, 2, or 3) to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text): This section captures your basic patient information, including your name and phone number.
- **Schedule Appointment** (`schedule_appointment`, select_one): This field asks whether you are available to schedule an appointment. Choose "Yes" if you are available, or "No" if you are not.
- **Selected Date** (`selected_date`, date): Enter the date of your appointment.
- **Selected Time** (`selected_time`, time): Enter the time of your appointment.
- **Doctor ID** (`doctor_id`, number): Enter your doctor's contact information (ID) if you wish to receive updates or confirmations.
- **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text): Enter your name as the patient.
- **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text): Enter your phone number.
- **Doctor Email** (`doctor_email`, email): Enter your doctor's email address (if applicable).
- **Appointment Duration** (`appointment_duration`, number): Enter the length of your appointment.
- **Appointment Type** (`appointment_type`, select_multiple): Select the type of appointment you would like to schedule.
- **Notes** (`notes`, text): Add any additional comments or notes about your appointment.
- **Confirm Appointment** (`confirm_appointment`, select_one): Confirm your appointment details by selecting one of the options (Option 1, 2, or 3).
- **Confirm Time** (`confirm_time`, time): Confirm the time of your appointment.
- **Confirm Date** (`confirm_date`, date): Confirm the date of your appointment.
- **Doctor ID** (`doctor_id`, number): Confirm your doctor's contact information (ID).
- **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, number): Confirm your patient ID.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and confirm your appointment details before submitting the form.
* Enter accurate and complete information to ensure smooth communication and coordination with your healthcare provider.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact your healthcare provider.
