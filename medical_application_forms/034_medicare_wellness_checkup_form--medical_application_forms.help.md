# medicare_wellness_checkup_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medicare Wellness Checkup Form is a comprehensive form designed to gather information about a patient's health and medical history. This form is used to identify potential health risks and provide personalized guidance for patients to improve their overall health and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have a stable medical history and are not experiencing a medical emergency.
2. Enter your patient's name and contact information (email, phone number, and address).
3. Provide your medical group or provider information.
4. List your current medical conditions and any allergies or medical concerns.
5. Report any recent medical history, including any medications or treatments you have received.
6. Review and confirm your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your medical record.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you with any questions or updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
* **Medical Group**: Select your primary medical group or provider.
* **Medical Condition**: List your current medical condition(s) (e.g., high blood pressure, diabetes, etc.).
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address**: Enter your current address.
* **City**: Enter your city of residence.
* **State**: Enter your state of residence.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Medical Provider**: List your current medical provider(s).
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Medical History**: Provide a detailed description of your medical history, including any recent diagnoses, treatments, or procedures.
* **Medication List**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medication Allergies**: List any allergies you have to medications.
* **Medical Providers**: List any medical providers you have seen recently.
* **Medical Conditions List**: List any medical conditions you have been diagnosed with.
* **Medical Providers List**: Indicate if you have a medical provider for each condition.
* **Medical Conditions List 2**: List any other medical conditions you have been diagnosed with.
* **Patient Address**: Enter your current address.
* **Patient City**: Enter your city of residence.
* **Patient State**: Enter your state of residence.
* **Patient Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional comments or concerns you have about your medical history.

## Tips
Please ensure that you provide accurate and detailed information to help us better understand your medical history. If you are unsure about any field, please contact a medical professional or health care provider for assistance.
