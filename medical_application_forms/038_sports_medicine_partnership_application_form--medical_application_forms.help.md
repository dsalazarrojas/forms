# Sports Medicine Partnership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for sports medicine organizations and medical professionals to partner with our organization. It collects information about your organization, medical specialties, and payment methods, allowing us to review and consider your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your user information (page 1)
2. Select your preferred contact method (page 2)
3. Provide information about your organization and medical specialty (page 3)
4. Enter your practice hours and location (page 4)
5. Describe your partner experience (page 5)
6. Add emergency contact information (page 6)
7. Enter your medical licensing and certification information (page 7)
8. List your medical certifications (page 8)
9. Provide insurance information (page 9)
10. Add practice name and website (page 10)
11. Add social media links (page 11)
12. Enter any other relevant information (page 12)
13. Review and sign the partner agreement (page 13)
14. Enter your medical license number (page 14)
15. Specify your partner fee (page 15)
16. Choose your payment method (page 16)
17. Review the terms and conditions of partnership (page 17)
18. Select submission format (page 18)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Info (page 1)
* **User Info**: This is your contact information. Please fill out your name, email address, and any other relevant details.

### Contact Method (page 2)
* **Contact Method**: This selects your preferred contact method. You can choose to contact us via Phone or Email.

### Partner Info (page 3)
* **Partner Info**: This is a text field for you to provide information about your organization.

### Medical Specialty (page 4)
* **Medical Specialty**: This is where you select your medical specialty. Please choose from Sports Medicine, Physiotherapy, or Other.

### Work Location (page 5)
* **Work Location**: This is where you enter your practice location.

### Partner Experience (page 6)
* **Partner Experience**: This is a text field for you to describe your experience as a partner.

### Emergency Contact Info (page 7)
* **Emergency Contact Info**: This is where you enter emergency contact information in case we need to reach out to you.

### Medical Licensing Info (page 8)
* **Medical Licensing Info**: This is a text field for you to provide information about your medical licensing.

### Medical Certifications (page 9)
* **Medical Certifications**: This is a text field for you to list your medical certifications.

### Insurance Info (page 10)
* **Insurance Info**: This is a text field for you to provide insurance information.

### Practice Name (page 11)
* **Practice Name**: This is where you enter your practice name.

### Website (page 12)
* **Website**: This is where you enter your website link.

### Social Media (page 13)
* **Social Media**: This is where you enter your social media links.

### Other Info (page 14)
* **Other Info**: This is a text field for you to enter any other relevant information.

### Partner Agreement (page 15)
* **Partner Agreement**: This is a note field where you review and sign the partner agreement.

### Medical License (page 16)
* **Medical License**: This is where you enter your medical license number.

### Partner Fee (page 17)
* **Partner Fee**: This is where you specify your partner fee.

### Payment Method (page 18)
* **Payment Method**: This is where you choose your payment method. You can choose to pay via Bank Transfer, Check, or Other.

### Terms and Conditions (page 19)
* **Terms and Conditions**: This is a text field for you to review the terms and conditions of partnership.

### Submission Form (page 20)
* **Submission Form**: This is where you select whether you are willing to submit your application in the selected format.

### Submission Format (page 21)
* **Submission Format**: This is where you select the format you prefer to submit your application in. You can choose PDF, Docx, or Excel.
