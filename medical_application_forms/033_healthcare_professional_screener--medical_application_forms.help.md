# Healthcare Professional Screener - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Healthcare Professional Screener form is designed to collect information about healthcare professionals, including their role, specialty, experience, work location, credentials, and work status. This information is used to facilitate efficient and accurate screening processes within our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your role: Choose your job title, such as Nurse, Doctor, Dentist, etc.
2. Choose your specialty: Select a specific area of medical expertise, such as Anesthesia, Cardiology, Dermatology, etc.
3. Specify your work experience: Indicate the type of experience you have in the medical field, such as Residency, Fellowship, Clinical Experience, etc.
4. Select your work location: Choose where you work, such as Hospital, Clinic, Private Practice, etc.
5. Provide your credentials: Enter your professional certifications, e.g., MD, DO, RN, etc.
6. Describe your work status: Briefly describe your current work status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Medical Staff
* **Medical Staff** (`title`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your job title as a healthcare professional.
### Role
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select your job title from the available options (e.g., Nurse, Doctor, etc.).
### Specialty
* **Specialty** (`specialty`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select a specific area of medical expertise from the available options (e.g., Anesthesia, Cardiology, etc.).
### Specialty (continued)
* **Specialty** (`specialty`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select another specific area of medical expertise from the available options.
### Experience
* **Experience** (`experience`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Indicate the type of experience you have in the medical field (e.g., Residency, Fellowship, Clinical Experience, etc.).
### Years
* **Years** (`experience_years`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the number of years you have gained experience in the medical field.
### Work Location
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose where you work (e.g., Hospital, Clinic, Private Practice, etc.).
### Credentials
* **Credentials** (`credentials`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your professional certifications, e.g., MD, DO, RN, etc.
### Work Status
* **Work Status** (`work_status`, text, optional):
	+ Briefly describe your current work status.
