# Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about applicants for the Health Equity In Data Science application. The purpose of this form is to gather necessary details for review and consideration of the application.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the application form by providing your contact information, program details, and any other required fields.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out, as indicated by the asterisk (*) symbol.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form** (`application_form`, `text`, not required): This is the main application form where you can provide a brief description of your application.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the application.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, not required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_person_phone`, `text`, not required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Program** (`program`, `text`, not required): Specify the program you are applying for.
* **Program Starting Date** (`program_starting_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date when the program will start.
* **Application Status** (`application_status`, `text`, not required): Indicate the current status of your application.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, `text`, not required): Describe the current review status of your application.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, not required): Add any additional comments or feedback related to your application.

## Tips

* Double-check that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Ensure that all contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
