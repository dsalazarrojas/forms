# Surgical Error Incident Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Surgical Error Incident Form is used to document and report incidents related to surgical errors that occurred during procedures. This form helps healthcare professionals identify the root causes of errors, take corrective actions, and prevent similar incidents in the future. Please ensure you are filling out this form for a legitimate reason, and that the incident is not already reported or reviewed.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the label.
2. Select relevant options from the dropdown menus for fields such as Error Type, Error Cause, and Incident Review.
3. Enter a brief description of the error in the Error Description field.
4. Select the correct reviewer for the Supervisor Review and Incident Review fields.
5. Enter the Review Date and Reviewed By fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date**: Enter the date the incident occurred. This should be in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time the incident occurred. This should be in the format 12:00 AM.
* **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient involved in the incident.
* **Surgeon Name**: Enter the name of the surgeon involved in the incident.
* **Procedure Name**: Enter the name of the procedure during which the incident occurred.
* **Error Type**: Select the type of error that occurred (Surgical Error, Medical Error, or Other).
* **Error Description**: Briefly describe the error that occurred.
* **Error Location**: Enter the location where the error occurred.
* **Error Cause**: Select the cause of the error (Surgeon, Anesthesia, Equipment, or Other).
* **Corrective Action**: Describe the action taken to correct the error.
* **Incident Reporter**: Select the person who reported the incident (Doctor, Nurse, or Other).
* **Incident Reporter Name**: Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
* **Incident Reporter Email**: Enter the email address of the person who reported the incident.
* **Incident Reporter Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who reported the incident.
* **Supervisor Review**: Select the person who reviewed the incident (Doctor, Supervisor).
* **Review Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback from the reviewer.
* **Incident Review**: Select the status of the incident review (Completed, In Progress).
* **Review Date**: Enter the date the incident was reviewed.
* **Reviewed By**: Enter the name of the person who reviewed the incident.
* **Supervisor Review**: Select the person who reviewed the incident.

Note: Please ensure you have filled out all required fields and reviewed the form carefully before submission.
