# Mental Capacity Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Capacity Assessment Form is used to evaluate a patient's mental capacity, which is their ability to understand, make decisions, and manage their medical condition.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form in a quiet and comfortable environment, free from distractions.
2. Answer all questions thoroughly and honestly, based on your current state.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please consult with your healthcare provider.
4. Once you have completed the form, review it carefully to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (patient_name)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Healthcare Provider Name (healthcare_provider_name)**: If you have a healthcare provider, please enter their name.
* **Date of Last Assessment (date_of_last_assessment)**: The date of your last mental capacity assessment.
* **Patient Address (patient_address)**: Enter your current address.
* **Patient Phone Number (patient_phone_number)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Patient Email (patient_email)**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Reason for Referral (reason_for_referral)**: Describe the reason for your referral to a mental capacity assessment.
* **Mental State at Last Assessment (mental_state_at_last_assessment)**: Describe your mental state at the time of your last assessment.
* **Medical Diagnosis (medical_diagnosis)**: Enter your current medical diagnosis.
* **Medical Treatment Plan (medical_treatment_plan)**: Describe your current treatment plan.
* **Mental Capacity Level (mental_capacity_level)**: Select the level of your mental capacity from the options provided.
  - Level 1: The patient has difficulty communicating and interacting with healthcare providers but can understand and process information.
  - Level 2: The patient has difficulty understanding and processing information but can make some decisions.
  - Level 3: The patient has difficulty making decisions but can understand and process information.
  - Level 4: The patient has difficulty understanding and processing information but can make some decisions.
  - Level 5: The patient has no difficulty understanding and processing information and can make decisions.
* **Capacity to Make Healthcare Decisions (capacity_to_make_healthcare_decisions)**: Select all that apply:
  - True: You have the ability to make healthcare decisions.
  - False: You do not have the ability to make healthcare decisions.
* **Capacity to Manage Daily Living (capacity_to_manage_daily_living)**: Select all that apply:
  - True: You have the ability to manage daily living activities.
  - False: You do not have the ability to manage daily living activities.
* **Capacity to Understand Medical Information (capacity_to_understand_medical_information)**: Select all that apply:
  - True: You have the ability to understand medical information.
  - False: You do not have the ability to understand medical information.
* **Capacity to Understand Medical Instructions (capacity_to_understand_medical_instructions)**: Select all that apply:
  - True: You have the ability to understand medical instructions.
  - False: You do not have the ability to understand medical instructions.
* **Capacity to Understand Own Condition (capacity_to_understand_own_condition)**: Select all that apply:
  - True: You have the ability to understand your own medical condition.
  - False: You do not have the ability to understand your own medical condition.
* **Capacity to Understand Effects of Medications (capacity_to_understand_the_effects_of_medications)**: Select all that apply:
  - True: You have the ability to understand the effects of your medications.
  - False: You do not have the ability to understand the effects of your medications.
* **Capacity to Understand Medical Treatment Options (capacity_to_understand_medical_treatment_options)**: Select all that apply:
  - True: You have the ability to understand medical treatment options.
  - False: You do not have the ability to understand medical treatment options.
* **Capacity to Understand Medical Treatment Alternatives (capacity_to_understand_medical_treatment_alternatives)**: Select all that apply:
  - True: You have the ability to understand medical treatment alternatives.
  - False: You do not have the ability to understand medical treatment alternatives.
* **Capacity to Understand Medical Risks and Benefits (capacity_to_understand_medical_risks_and_benefits)**: Select all that apply:
  - True: You have the ability to understand medical risks and benefits.
  - False: You do not have the ability to understand medical risks and benefits.
* **Capacity to Understand Medical Consequences (capacity_to_understand_medical_consequences)**: Select all that apply:
  - True: You have the ability to understand medical consequences.
  - False: You do not have the ability to understand medical consequences.
* **Capacity to Make Healthcare Decisions with Support (capacity_to_make_healthcare_decisions_with_support)**: Select all that apply:
  - True: You can make healthcare decisions with support.
  - False: You cannot make healthcare decisions with support.
* **Capacity to Manage Money (capacity_to_manage_money)**: Select all that apply:
  - True: You have the ability to manage your money.
  - False: You do not have the ability to manage your money.
* **Capacity to Manage Medical Condition (capacity_to_manage_medical_condition)**: Select all that apply:
  - True: You have the ability to manage your medical condition.
  - False: You do not have the ability to manage your medical condition.
* **Capacity to Understand Disease Management (capacity_to_understand_disease_management)**: Select all that apply:
  - True: You have the ability to understand disease management.
  - False: You do not have the ability to understand disease management.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, please consult with your healthcare provider.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your healthcare provider.
* The results of this form will be used to understand your mental capacity and provide appropriate care and support.
