# Behavioral Science In Medicine Research Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Behavioral Science In Medicine Research Application Form is a comprehensive form designed to gather information from applicants for research projects related to behavioral science in medicine. This form helps us understand your research objectives, methodologies, and qualifications, ultimately aiding us in evaluating your application for funding and research collaboration opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details such as name, email, phone number, and institutional affiliation.
2. Describe your current position and title in the relevant field.
3. Provide a concise title and summary of your proposed research project.
4. Select the primary behavioral science focus area and medical application area relevant to your research.
5. Indicate the duration of your research project and whether funding is required.
6. Specify whether you have obtained or applied for ethics committee approval.
7. Describe your previous research experience and any relevant publications or presentations.
8. If applicable, provide your supervisor's name and email.
9. Indicate your interest in collaborating with other researchers.
10. Include any additional comments or questions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name** (1) (required): Enter your first and last name to be contacted regarding your application.
* **Email Address** (2) (required): Provide a valid email for correspondence related to your research project.
* **Phone Number** (3) (required): Include your country code if applicable.
* **Institution or Organization Affiliation** (4) (required): Name of your university, hospital, or research center.
* **Department or Division** (5) (not required): Specify your department within the institution.
* **Current Position or Title** (6) (required): e.g., Research Assistant, Professor, Medical Student.
* **Research Project Title** (7) (required): Provide a concise title for your proposed research.
* **Research Summary** (8) (required): Briefly describe your research objectives and methodology.
* **Behavioral Science Focus Area** (9) (required): Select the primary behavioral science domain relevant to your research.
* **Medical Application Area** (10) (required): Which medical field does your research apply to.
* **Expected Research Duration** (11) (required): Choose the duration of your research project.
* **Is Funding Required** (12) (required): Indicate whether you need financial support for this research.
* **Estimated Funding Amount Needed** (13) (not required): Specify the funding amount in USD if required.
* **Ethics Approval Status** (14) (required): Indicate whether you have obtained or applied for ethics committee approval.
* **Previous Research Experience** (15) (required): Describe your relevant research background.
* **Relevant Publications** (16) (not required): List any related publications or presentations.
* **Research Supervisor Name** (17) (not required): If applicable, provide your supervisor's full name.
* **Supervisor Contact Email** (18) (not required): Provide supervisor email for verification.
* **Interest in Collaboration** (19) (required): Indicate your interest in collaborating with other researchers.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (20) (not required): Include any other information you would like to share.
