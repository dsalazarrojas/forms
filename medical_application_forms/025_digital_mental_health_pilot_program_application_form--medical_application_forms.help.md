# Digital Mental Health Pilot Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from participants for the Digital Mental Health Pilot Program. It is used to gather demographic and health history information, as well as contact and medical information, from individuals who are interested in enrolling in the program. The form is intended to be completed by participants who wish to join the program, and it will be used to determine eligibility and ensure a smooth onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  Please ensure that you fill out all required fields.
3.  If a field is not required, it is still recommended that you fill it out for the sake of completeness and thoroughness.
4.  Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Information**: Enter your name, address, and contact information.
    (participant_info, text, required: false)
*   **Mental Health History**: Describe your mental health conditions, treatment history, and any current or past diagnoses.
    (mental_health_history, text, required: false)
*   **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name and contact information of a person that will be notified in case of an emergency.
    (emergency_contact, text, required: false)
*   **Contact Information**: Enter the contact information of the participant, including their phone number and email address.
    (contact_info, text, required: false)
*   **Medical History**: Provide a brief description of any medical conditions that may impact your mental health.
    (medical_history, text, required: false)
*   **Mental Health Services**: Describe the mental health services you are seeking or have received.
    (mental_health_services, text, required: false)
*   **Emergency Medical Services**: Provide any information on emergency medical services you have received in the past.
    (emergency_medical_services, text, required: false)
*   **Participant Signature**: A digital signature of the participant.
    (participant_signature, text, required: false)
*   **Signature Date**: Enter the date when signing the form.
    (signature_date, date, required: false)
*   **Therapist Information**: Enter the name and information of any therapist you are currently seeing.
    (therapist_info, text, required: false)

## Tips

*   Be honest and detailed when providing information.
*   Double-check the accuracy of the information you enter.
*   Ensure that you have the necessary permissions to submit the form on behalf of someone else.
*   Review the completed form before submission.
