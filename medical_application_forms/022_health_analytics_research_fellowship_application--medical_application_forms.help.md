# Health Analytics Research Fellowship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact and research-related information from candidates applying for a Health Analytics Research Fellowship. It is essential to fill this form accurately and comprehensively to ensure a thorough review of your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact details, including your name and email address.
2. Provide a brief description of your research interests and areas of focus.
3. Specify whether you are uploading CVs or providing additional information.
4. If applicable, select the research focus areas that best fit your expertise.
5. Review and accept the terms and conditions of the fellowship.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details**: This section is where you'll provide your name and contact email address, which will be used to communicate with you throughout the application process.
* **Candidate Details**: Please describe your background, qualifications, and relevant experience.
* **Research Interests**: Outline your primary research areas of focus, including any areas you're particularly passionate about.
* **CV Attachment**: You can upload one or more CVs to support your application.
* **University Name**: If relevant, enter the name of your current or affiliated university.
* **University Address**: If applicable, provide the address of your university.
* **Research Institution**: If relevant, enter the name of your current or affiliated research institution.
* **Contact Email**: This is your email address that will be used for communication.
* **Contact Phone**: Your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Research Focus Areas**: Select all the research focus areas that you have expertise in.
* **CV Uploads**: Specify whether you are uploading CVs or providing additional information.
* **Additional Info**: Enter any additional information that may be helpful in reviewing your application.
* **CV Attachments 1**: You can upload one or more CVs to support your application.
* **CV Attachments 2**: Another opportunity to upload CVs or supporting documents.
* **Additional Info 1**: Enter any additional information that may be helpful in reviewing your application.
* **Additional Info 2**: Enter any final thoughts or comments you'd like to include with your application.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Please review and accept the terms and conditions of the fellowship.
* **Prophets and Mission**: This field is not clearly defined; however, it seems to be a redundant field with `Research Focus Areas`. It may be a field for future use or a placeholder.
* **Research Focus Areas 2**: Select all the research focus areas that you have expertise in.
* **CV Attachments 1 and 2**: These fields are intended for uploading CVs or supporting documents.
* **Additional Info 1 and 2**: Enter any additional information that may be helpful in reviewing your application.

Please ensure that you fill out all relevant fields to provide a complete and accurate application.
