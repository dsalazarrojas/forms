# Medical Testing Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from patients before and after medical tests, enabling healthcare professionals to monitor patient conditions and test results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form's general information section, which includes:
    *   Medical Test 1
    *   Medical Test 2

2.  After completing the general information section, you can proceed with the following steps:

    *   If you are taking a medical test, fill out the corresponding test result fields:
        +   Medical Test 1 Result
        +   Medical Test 2 Result

    *   If you don't have test results, please enter "Not available" or "No result."

3.  You can also provide any additional medical test results in the field "Medical Test Results"

4.  If you have any further comments or notes regarding your medical tests, please fill in the "Patient Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **General Information** (`general_info`, `text`, required: false)
This field is intended to collect the patient's general information, such as their ID number or name.

### 2. **Medical Test 1** (`medical_test_1`, `text`, required: false)
Please provide the details of the first medical test you are taking.

### 3. **Medical Test 2** (`medical_test_2`, `text`, required: false)
Please provide the details of the second medical test you are taking.

### 4. **Medical Test 1 Result** (`medical_test_1_result`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the result of the first medical test.

### 5. **Medical Test 2 Result** (`medical_test_2_result`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the result of the second medical test.

### 6. **Medical Test Result 3** (`medical_test_result_3`, `text`, required: false)
Please enter the result of any additional medical test.

### 7. **Medical Test Results** (`medical_test_results`, `text`, required: false)
Enter any other medical test results here.

### 8. **Patient Notes** (`patient_notes`, `text`, required: false)
If you have any further comments or notes regarding your medical tests, please enter them in this field.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you fill out all required fields, even if they are not mandatory.
*   If a field is not relevant to your situation, please mark it as "Not available" or "No result" to maintain the form's integrity.
