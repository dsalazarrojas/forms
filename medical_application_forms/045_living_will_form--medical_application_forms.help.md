# Living Will Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Living Will Form is a document that outlines an individual's wishes for their end-of-life medical treatment, property distribution, and asset dispersal. It ensures that their medical, financial, and personal wishes are respected and implemented in the event of incapacitation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the form on the first page, "Living Will Form."
2. Choose one of the Living Will Properties that best describes your wishes.
3. Enter the value of your assets on the "Living Will Assets" field.
4. Select how you want your assets to be distributed on the "Living Will Asset Distribution" page.
5. Choose the person or organization you want to receive your assets on the "Living Will Distribute To" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Living Will Form** (1, text, required): This is the main page where you'll write a brief description of your medical, financial, and personal wishes.
* **Living Will Property** (2, select_one, required): Select one of the Living Will Properties that best describes your wishes, such as "Living Will Property 1," "Living Will Property 2," or "Living Will Property 3."
* **Living Will Assets** (3, number, required): Enter the total value of your assets, such as your bank account, property, or other valuables.
* **Living Will Asset Distribution** (4, select_multiple, required): Select how you want your assets to be distributed among the options provided, such as "Living Will Distribution 1," "Living Will Distribution 2," or "Living Will Distribution 3."
* **Living Will Distribute To** (5, select_one, required): Choose the person or organization you want to receive your assets, such as "Living Will Recipient 1," "Living Will Recipient 2," or "Living Will Recipient 3."

## Tips
- Be specific and clear when describing your wishes in the "Living Will Form" field.
- Choose the Living Will Property that best aligns with your wishes to ensure accurate representation.
- Enter the correct value of your assets to ensure accurate asset distribution.
- Select the distribution options that align with your wishes for your assets.
- Choose the correct recipient for your assets to ensure they are handled according to your wishes.
