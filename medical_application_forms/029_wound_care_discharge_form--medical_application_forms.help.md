# Wound Care Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wound Care Discharge Form is used to document the details of a patient's discharge from wound care services, ensuring accurate communication between healthcare providers and patients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Patient Identification**: Enter the patient's unique identifier, usually their name, date of birth, or medical record number.
2. **Date Of Discharge**: Enter the date the patient was discharged from the wound care service.
3. **Wound Status**: Select the current status of the wound (Healing, Not Healing, Unhealed).
4. **Wound Size**: Enter the size of the wound.
5. **Wound Location**: Enter the location of the wound on the patient's body.
6. **Post Treatment Instructions**: Provide any instructions for post-treatment care.
7. **Discharge Diagnosis**: Select the final diagnosis for the discharge.
8. **Follow-up Care Plan**: Outline any necessary follow-up care for the patient.
9. **Next Appointment**: Schedule any follow-up appointments as needed.
10. **Wound Care Instructions**: Provide any instructions for ongoing wound care.
11. **Discharge Status**: Select the discharge status (Discharged, Not Discharged).
12. **Reason For Discharge**: Explain the reason for discharge.
13. **Post Discharge Instructions**: Provide instructions for post-discharge care.
14. **Next Provider**: Identify the next healthcare provider for follow-up care.
15. **Date Discharged**: Enter the date of discharge.
16. **Reason For Discharge Code**: Enter any relevant discharge code.
17. **Discharge Date**: Enter the date of discharge.
18. **Discharge Time**: Enter the time of discharge.
19. **Discharge Location**: Enter the location of discharge.
20. **Discharge Diagnosis Code**: Enter any relevant discharge diagnosis code.
21. **Follow-up Care Provider**: Identify the provider for follow-up care.
22. **Patient Signature**: Obtain the patient's signature, if applicable.
23. **Next Visit Date**: Schedule the next visit date.
24. **Next Visit Time**: Schedule the next visit time.
25. **Next Visit Location**: Identify the location of the next visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Identifier**: This field is used to identify the patient, ensuring accurate tracking and continuity of care.
	+ Example: John Doe, born 01-01-1990
* **Date Of Discharge**: This field is used to record the date the patient was discharged from wound care services.
	+ Format: MM/DD/YYYY
* **Wound Status**: Select the current status of the wound:
	+ Healing: The wound is recovering.
	+ Not Healing: The wound is not improving.
	+ Unhealed: The wound remains open and unhealed.
* **Wound Size**: Enter the size of the wound.
	+ Example: 2cm x 5cm
* **Wound Location**: Enter the location of the wound on the patient's body.
	+ Example: Left leg, mid-calf area
* **Post Treatment Instructions**: Provide instructions for post-treatment care.
	+ Example: "Take 500mg of antibiotics for 3 days."
* **Discharge Diagnosis**: Select the final diagnosis for the discharge.
	+ Satisfactory: The patient has made satisfactory progress.
	+ Unsatisfactory: The patient has not made satisfactory progress.
	+ Not Applicable: The patient was not applicable for discharge.
* **Follow-up Care Plan**: Outline any necessary follow-up care for the patient.
	+ Example: "Schedule a follow-up appointment in 2 weeks."
* **Next Appointment**: Schedule any follow-up appointments as needed.
	+ Example: 02-15-2024, 10:00 AM
* **Wound Care Instructions**: Provide instructions for ongoing wound care.
	+ Example: "Keep the wound clean and dry, apply dressing daily."
* **Discharge Status**: Select the discharge status.
	+ Discharged: The patient has been discharged.
	+ Not Discharged: The patient remains under care.
* **Reason For Discharge**: Explain the reason for discharge.
	+ Example: "The wound has healed, patient is now asymptomatic."
* **Post Discharge Instructions**: Provide instructions for post-discharge care.
	+ Example: "Attend a follow-up appointment in 2 weeks."
* **Next Provider**: Identify the next healthcare provider for follow-up care.
	+ Example: Dr. Smith, MD
* **Date Discharged**: Enter the date of discharge.
	+ Format: MM/DD/YYYY
* **Reason For Discharge Code**: Enter any relevant discharge code.
	+ Example: "V30.0"
* **Discharge Date**: Enter the date of discharge.
	+ Format: MM/DD/YYYY
* **Discharge Time**: Enter the time of discharge.
	+ Format: HH:MM
* **Discharge Location**: Enter the location of discharge.
	+ Example: Outpatient clinic
* **Discharge Diagnosis Code**: Enter any relevant discharge diagnosis code.
	+ Example: "A30.1"
* **Follow-up Care Provider**: Identify the provider for follow-up care.
	+ Example: Dr. Smith, MD
* **Patient Signature**: Obtain the patient's signature, if applicable.
	+ Example: John Doe's signature
* **Next Visit Date**: Schedule the next visit date.
	+ Format: MM/DD/YYYY
* **Next Visit Time**: Schedule the next visit time.
	+ Format: HH:MM
* **Next Visit Location**: Identify the location of the next visit.
	+ Example: Outpatient clinic
