# Diagnostic Test Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit claims for diagnostic tests, procedures, or services to insurance companies or other payers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" for the test claims section to indicate whether you are claiming for a test or procedure.
2. Enter the patient's information, including their name, address, and phone number.
3. Select the test results and claim status from the provided options.
4. Enter any relevant medical notes, payment information, and claim notes.
5. Enter the test date and time, and claim date and time.
6. Select the payment method and claim type.
7. Enter any additional claim status comments, payment terms, and payment terms notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Test Claims**: Select "Yes" to indicate that you are claiming for a test or procedure.
* **Patient Info**: Enter the patient's name, address, and phone number.
* **Test Results**: Select the test results from the options provided.
* **Provider Info**: Enter any relevant information about the healthcare provider who performed the test or procedure.
* **Address**: Enter the patient's address.
* **Claim Status**: Select the current claim status from the options provided.
* **Payment Info**: Enter any relevant payment information.
* **Test Date**: Enter the date and time of the test or procedure.
* **Test Time**: Enter the time of day the test or procedure was performed.
* **Claim Amount**: Enter the amount being claimed for the test or procedure.
* **Test Results Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the test results.
* **Claim Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the claim.
* **Patient Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Claim Date**: Enter the date the claim was submitted.
* **Claim Time**: Enter the time the claim was submitted.
* **Payment Method**: Select the method of payment (if applicable).
* **Claim Type**: Select the type of claim (if applicable).
* **Claim Status Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the claim status.
* **Medical Notes 2**: Enter any additional medical notes or information.
* **Payment Terms**: Enter any additional payment terms or conditions.
* **Payment Terms Notes**: Enter any additional notes about payment terms.
* **Claim Status Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the claim status.
