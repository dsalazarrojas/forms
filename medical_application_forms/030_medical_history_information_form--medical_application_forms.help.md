# Medical History Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed medical history information from patients to aid healthcare providers in making informed decisions and offering proper care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's sections carefully and answer each question accurately.
2. Ensure you complete all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) in the field label.
3. Enter your name and date of birth in the corresponding fields.
4. Select all applicable medical conditions, tests, and allergies from the provided lists.
5. Provide detailed explanations for your medical history and family history.
6. Specify any medical procedures you've undergone.
7. Review your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Medical Conditions
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please list any medical conditions you have, such as diabetes, hypertension, or asthma.

### Patient Name
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full name.

### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Medical Allergies
* **Medical Allergies** (`medical_allergies`, `select_one`, required: false): Check any allergies you have, such as penicillin, latex, or shellfish.

### Medical History
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Provide a detailed explanation of your medical history, including any significant events or conditions.

### Family History
* **Family History** (`family_history`, `select_multiple`, required: false): List any family medical history, such as diabetes, heart disease, or cancer.

### Medical Procedures
* **Medical Procedures** (`medical_medical_procedure`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Specify any medical procedures you've undergone, like blood transfusion or surgery.

### Medical History Other
* **Medical History Other** (`medical_medical_history_other`, `text`, required: false): If there's anything else you'd like to share about your medical history, enter it here.

### Medical Tests
* **Medical Tests** (`medical_tests`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose any medical tests you've undergone, such as EKG, blood test, or CT scan.

## Tips
- Be accurate and thorough when completing this form.
- If unsure about any field, ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
- Complete the form carefully to ensure your medical history is accurately recorded.
