# public-relations-volunteer-application-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a volunteer application for the public relations team. It requires you to provide information about yourself, your skills and qualifications, and your availability for volunteer work. The goal is to match you with volunteer opportunities that best fit your skills and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, you will need to provide the following information:
- Please fill in your contact information (page 1)
- Select your preferred contact method (page 2)
- Enter your job title and any relevant skills or qualifications you may have (page 3)
- Briefly describe your volunteer experiences and confirm whether you have any (page 4)
- Choose your available days for volunteering (page 5)
- Confirm your availability for volunteer work (page 6)
- Provide the name and contact information for at least two emergency contacts (pages 7-8)
- Enter the name and contact information for at least two references (pages 9-12)
- Confirm your volunteer availability and notice (pages 13-14)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (contact_information, text, required: false): This section is for your basic contact information, including name, address, email, and phone number.
* **Contact Preference** (contact_preference, select_one, required: false): Choose how you would like to be contacted: by Email, Phone, SMS, or In Person.
* **Job Title** (job_title, text, required: false): If you have a job title, please enter it here.
* **Skills and Qualifications** (skills_and_qualifications, note, required: false): List any relevant skills or qualifications you may have.
* **Volunteer Experiences** (volunteer_experiences, select_multiple, required: false): Briefly describe your past volunteer experiences.
* **Available Days** (available_days, select_one, required: false): Choose the days you are available to volunteer.
* **Availability Confirmation** (availability_confirmation, select_one, required: false): Confirm whether you are available or not.
* **Emergency Contact** (emergency_contact, text, required: false): Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (emergency_contact_phone, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Volunteer Availability Notice** (volunteer_availability_notice, date, required: false): Enter the date you are available to start volunteering.
* **Volunteer Reference** (volunteer_reference, text, required: false): Enter the name and contact information for at least two references.
* **Reference Name 1** (volunteer_reference_name_1, text, required: false): Enter the name of your first reference.
* **Reference Phone 1** (volunteer_reference_phone_1, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of your first reference.
* **Reference Email 1** (volunteer_reference_email_1, text, required: false): Enter the email of your first reference.
* **Reference Name 2** (volunteer_reference_name_2, text, required: false): Enter the name of your second reference.
* **Reference Phone 2** (volunteer_reference_phone_2, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of your second reference.
* **Reference Email 2** (volunteer_reference_email_2, text, required: false): Enter the email of your second reference.
