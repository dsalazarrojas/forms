# National Trust Volunteer Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to register as volunteers for various activities within the National Trust, such as conservation, community events, and fundraising. By completing this form, you will be able to express your interest and availability for these activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out.
3. Review your information carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Details** (`volunteer_details`, text, not required): This field is where you can provide any additional information about yourself that you feel is relevant to your volunteer work with the National Trust.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, not required): Please provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you about volunteer opportunities.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, number, required): Enter your primary contact number where we can reach you.
* **Volunteer Interest** (`volunteer_interest`, select_multiple, required): Select all the areas where you are interested in volunteering, such as National Trust, Conservation, Community, Events, or Fundraising.
* **Volunteer Skills** (`volunteer_skills`, select_multiple, required): List any relevant skills you have that align with your volunteer interest, such as Gardening, Photography, Conservation, Events, or Fundraising.
* **Availability** (`availability`, date, required): Please select a date to indicate your availability to volunteer.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where we can send you communication about volunteer opportunities.
* **Email Confirmed** (`email_confirmed`, text, not required): Check this box if you are willing to be contacted for future opportunities.
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, required): Check this box to indicate that you are willing to be contacted regarding volunteer opportunities.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, not required): This field is not necessary, but it will automatically be filled out with the current date when you submit the form.

## Tips
- Please make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
- We appreciate your interest in volunteering with the National Trust!
