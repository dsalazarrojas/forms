# International Student Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for international students to apply for a volunteer opportunity. It is intended to gather essential information about the applicant's personal details, volunteer preferences, and relevant skills.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed, even if you are unsure about a particular field, you can skip it for now and come back to it later.
3. If you encounter any issues while filling out the form, contact the support team for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **first_name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
- **last_name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
- **email** (email, required): Enter your valid email address that you check regularly.
- **phone_number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **address** (text, optional): Enter your home address or contact address.
- **date_of_birth** (date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 1990-01-01).
- **student_status** (select one, optional): Select 'Student' if you are a student, 'Non-Student' otherwise.
- **volunteer_start_date** (date, optional): Select the start date you are available to volunteer.
- **volunteer_end_date** (date, optional): Select the end date you are available to volunteer.
- **reason_for_volunteering** (text, optional): Briefly describe why you want to volunteer.
- **skills** (text, optional): List your relevant skills or qualifications.
 
 ## Tips
 
 * Ensure that you fill out the form accurately and completely.
 * You can save your progress and come back to the form later.
 * If you need help, contact the support team.
