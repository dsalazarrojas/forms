# free-local-assistance-request-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals or organizations seeking assistance, support, or resources from local organizations or community groups.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request for Assistance**: This field is a text field where you can describe the type of assistance you are seeking. Please be as detailed as possible so that we can better understand your needs.
* **Assistance Needed**: This field is a select-one field with options to choose from (e.g., financial assistance, mentorship, etc.). Select the option that best describes the type of assistance you are seeking.
* **Payment Method**: This field is a multiple-select field where you can select all applicable payment methods (e.g., cash, credit card, etc.). Please note that some methods may not be applicable in all situations.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you for further assistance or follow-up.
* **Phone Number**: Enter a valid phone number that we can use to contact you for further assistance or follow-up.
* **Date of Request**: Enter the date you are requesting assistance.
* **Time of Request**: Enter the time you are requesting assistance.
* **Additional Information**: This field is for any additional details that may be helpful for us to understand your need for assistance.
* **Confirm Email** and **Confirm Phone**: These fields are to confirm the information you entered in the previous steps. Please re-enter your email and phone number to ensure accuracy.
