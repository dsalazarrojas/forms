# trampoline_gymnastic_state_championships_volunteer_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The trampoline_gymnastic_state_championships_volunteer_registration_form is used to register volunteers for the Trampoline Gymnastic State Championships. This event is a platform for athletes to showcase their trampoline skills, and volunteers are crucial to its success.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Fill out the fields as instructed.
2.  Ensure you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Personal Info
*   **Personal Info** (`personal_info`, `text`, optional): This field is for entering your personal details. You can enter your name and any other relevant personal information you feel is necessary for us to know.

### 2. Contact Info
*   **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact information, such as your email or phone number, to help us get in touch with you about the event.

### 3. Email
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about the event.

### 4. Phone
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about the event.

### 5. Volunteer Role
*   **Volunteer Role** (`volunteer_role`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you are willing to volunteer for the event. This will help us determine how to allocate your duties.

### 6. Availability
*   **Availability** (`availability`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the times when you are available to volunteer. This will help us schedule volunteers and minimize conflicts.

### 7. Notes
*   **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional information you think is relevant to your volunteer application.
