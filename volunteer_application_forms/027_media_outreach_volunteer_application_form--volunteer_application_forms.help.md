# media_outreach_volunteer_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The media outreach volunteer application form is designed to collect information from potential volunteers for media outreach initiatives. This form helps the organization to identify suitable volunteers, ensure accurate communication, and build a comprehensive database for future collaborations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your personal and professional details.
2. Select your relevant skills and experience for media outreach initiatives.
3. Choose your availability for volunteering.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Name** (`Volunteer Name`, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (`Email`, required): Enter your work email address or personal email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, required): Enter your work phone number or personal phone number.
* **Skills** (`Skills`, required): List your skills and experience relevant to media outreach initiatives.
* **Availability** (`Availability`, required): Enter your availability for volunteering, in date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Media Outreach Initiatives** (`Media Outreach Initiatives`, required): Select all the media outreach initiatives you are interested in.
* **Volunteer Type** (`Volunteer Type`, required): Select the type of volunteer you are interested in.
* **Organization** (`Organization`, required): Enter the name of the organization you are volunteering for.
* **Department** (`Department`, required): Enter the name of the department you are volunteering for.
* **Volunteer Manager** (`Volunteer Manager`, required): Enter the name of your volunteer manager.
* **Manager Email** (`Manager Email`, required): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`Manager Phone`, required): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Relationship** (`Relationship`, required): Select your relationship with your manager.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, optional): Use this field to provide any additional information about yourself as a volunteer.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Review your application before submitting it to ensure it is correct.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* Make sure to select the correct volunteer type and media outreach initiatives.
