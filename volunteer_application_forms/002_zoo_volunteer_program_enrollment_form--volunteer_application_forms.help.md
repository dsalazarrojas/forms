# Zoo Volunteer Program Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Zoo Volunteer Program Enrollment Form is a necessary step for potential volunteers to apply for the program. It helps the zoo's staff to understand your interests, availability, and other important details to place you in the right role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact details in the "Contact Details" field.
2. Indicate your volunteer status by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Volunteer Role" field.
3. If you are available to volunteer, select your preferred availability in the "Availability" field. Please note that this field is a date picker.
4. In the "Shift Preference" field, select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your willingness to work on a shift.
5. If you have an emergency contact, fill in their name and relationship with you in the "Emergency Contact" and "Emergency Contact Relationship" fields, respectively.
6. In the "Volunteer Interest" field, write down your interests in volunteering for the zoo.
7. Review and agree to the "Volunteer Agreement" to confirm your understanding of the zoo's policies.
8. Finally, submit your application by filling in the "Submission" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Application Form** (`application_form`, text, required: false): This field is for general application information. Please provide a brief statement about why you want to volunteer at the zoo.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): Fill in your name, email address, and phone number to help us contact you.
- **Volunteer Role** (`volunteer_role`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate your level of involvement in the program. Please select "Yes" if you are interested in volunteering.
- **Availability** (`availability`, date, required: false): Choose a date when you are available to volunteer. This is a date picker field.
- **Shift Preference** (`shift_preference`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" if you are available to work on a shift.
- **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false): Provide the name and email address (or phone number) of your emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, required: false): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
- **Volunteer Interest** (`volunteer_interest`, note, required: false): Write down your interests in volunteering for the zoo.
- **Volunteer Agreement** (`volunteer_agreement`, text, required: false): Review and agree to the zoo's policies before submitting your application.
- **Submission** (`submission`, text, required: false): Fill in any additional comments or information that you would like to share with us.

## Tips
- Make sure to review all fields carefully before submitting your application.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
- We appreciate your willingness to volunteer and will get back to you soon with further instructions.
