# Domestic Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Domestic Volunteer Application Form is designed to gather information from potential volunteers to assess their qualifications and preferences for domestic volunteer opportunities. This form helps our team to determine the best fit for our domestic volunteers and ensures that we have accurate and up-to-date information about each applicant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **user information** (page 1) with your name, email, and any other relevant details.
2. Provide your **contact information** (page 2) including your phone number and address.
3. Share your **volunteer preferences** (page 3) with us, so we can get to know you better.
4. Enter your **emergency contact information** (page 4) for us to reach out to in case of any issues.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info (Page 1)**:
	+ **Title:** `name` : Your name should match the one on your government ID or driver's license.
	+ **Email:** `email` : Please use a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Contact Info (Page 2)**:
	+ **Phone Number:** `phone` : This is where we'll reach out to you for any updates or information.
	+ **Address:** `address` : This is where we can reach you in person or send you any necessary documents.
* **Volunteer Preferences (Page 3)**:
	+ **Volunteer Preferences:** `volunteer_preferences` : Share with us what type of volunteer work you're interested in, such as animal shelter, park cleanup, or disaster relief.
* **Emergency Contact (Page 4)**:
	+ **Emergency Contact Name:** `emergency_contact_name` : The person we should contact in case of an emergency.
	+ **Relationship:** `relationship` : Your relationship with the emergency contact, such as family member, friend, or neighbor.
	+ **Phone Number:** `emergency_contact_phone` : The emergency contact's phone number.
	+ **Address:** `emergency_contact_address` : The emergency contact's address.
* **Tips**

Please note that this form is for general information gathering purposes only and does not guarantee placement or acceptance as a volunteer. We'll review your application carefully and contact you if we need more information or if we're interested in moving forward with your application.
