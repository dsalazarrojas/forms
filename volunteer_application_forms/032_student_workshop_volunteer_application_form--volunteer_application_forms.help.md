# Student Workshop Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from students who wish to participate in a workshop hosted by our institution. The purpose of this form is to collect contact and workshop-related information from students who are interested in attending the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required and optional fields as prompted.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Info
* **User Info** (`text`, optional): This field is for you to provide a brief description of yourself, including your name and any other relevant information about your identity as a student.

### Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`text`, optional): This field is for you to provide your contact information, such as your email address and phone number. This will help us get in touch with you to confirm your participation in the workshop.

### Workshop Info
* **Workshop Info** (`text`, optional): This field is for you to provide any relevant information about the workshop you are interested in, such as the workshop title, date, or location.

### Availability
* **Availability** (`date`, optional): This field is for you to select a date when you are available to attend the workshop. Please make sure to choose a date that works best for you.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form.
* Use the same format as the examples provided (e.g., mm/dd/yyyy for dates).
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* The submitted form will be reviewed and processed internally. You will receive a confirmation email once we receive your submission.
