# Volunteer Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Interest Form is a crucial tool for individuals expressing interest in volunteering with our organization. It helps us understand their background, availability, and interests to find the best fit for volunteer opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each field to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as possible.
2. If a field is marked as required, please ensure you complete it accurately.
3. Review your information carefully before submitting the form to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, required: false): This field is an optional section where you can provide any additional information about yourself.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Please enter your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Volunteer Interest** (`volunteer_interest`, text, required: false): This field allows you to express your interest in volunteering with our organization. Be specific about the areas you are interested in, such as event planning, animal care, or event management.
* **Volunteer Experience** (`volunteer_experience`, text, required: false): If you have previous volunteer experience, please describe it briefly, including the organization and the tasks you performed.
* **Why Volunteer** (`why_volunteer`, text, required: false): Share your reasons for wanting to volunteer with our organization. This helps us understand your motivations and find the best fit for you.
* **Skills and Interests** (`skills_and_interests`, text, required: false): List your relevant skills and interests, such as event planning, photography, or social media management.
* **Availability** (`availability`, date, required: false): Select the dates you are available to volunteer, including the start and end dates of your availability period.
* **Time Commitment** (`time_commitment`, text, required: false): Indicate the amount of time you can commit to volunteering, such as part-time or full-time.
* **Volunteer Organisations** (`volunteer_organisations`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose from the options provided to indicate which volunteer organizations you are interested in working with.
* **Volunteer Frequency** (`volunteer_frequency`, select_one, required: false): Select how often you would like to volunteer, such as once a week, twice a month, or as needed.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or questions you may have about volunteering with our organization.
