> # green_energy_volunteer_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for individuals who wish to volunteer for the Green Energy project. The data collected will be used to assign volunteers to suitable volunteer opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you have provided accurate and complete information.
2. Fill in all required fields (indicated by <Required>).
3. For the "volunteer_interests" field, select all interested activities from the dropdown menu.
4. For the "available_dates" field, select a date in the format `MM/DD/YY`.
5. For the "available_hours" field, select a time in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
6. For the "contact_phone" and "contact_email" fields, enter your phone number and email address in the format `XXX-XXX-XXXX` and `username@example.com`, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **volunteer-application-form** (`text`, optional): Enter a brief introduction about yourself and your motivation for volunteering with the Green Energy project.
* **contact-information** (`text`, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information, such as your address or other contact methods.
* **volunteer-interests** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select all the volunteer activities you are interested in:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **energy-solutions** (`text`, optional): Enter any additional information about yourself or your skills that may be relevant to the volunteer opportunities you are interested in.
* **available-dates** (`date`, optional): Select a date when you are available to volunteer.
* **available-hours** (`time`, optional): Select a time when you are available to volunteer.
* **contact-phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number in the format `XXX-XXX-XXXX`.
* **contact-email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address in the format `username@example.com`.

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.
* Ensure that you are providing genuine information and not misrepresenting your availability or interests.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact the Green Energy project team.
