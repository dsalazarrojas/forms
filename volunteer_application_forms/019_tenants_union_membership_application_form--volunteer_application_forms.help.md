# Tenants Union Membership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenants Union Membership Application Form is used for prospective members to submit their applications to join the Tenants Union.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Select your preferred contact method (phone or email).
3. Choose your position applying for within the Tenants Union (president, secretary, treasurer, or member).
4. If you have emergency contacts, select them from the provided list.
5. List the positions you are applying for.
6. Add any additional notes about your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name**: A text field where you can enter your name.
* **contact_information**: A text field where you can provide your contact information.
* **address**: A text field where you can enter your address.
* **position_applying**: A dropdown menu where you can select the position you are applying for (president, secretary, treasurer, or member).
* **consent_terms_and_conditions**: A dropdown menu where you can agree or disagree to the terms and conditions of the Tenants Union.
* **contact_preferences**: A text field where you can specify your preferred contact method (phone or email).
* **email**: An email field where you can enter your email address.
* **phone**: A text field where you can enter your phone number.
* **name**: Another text field for your name (note that this is the same as the first field and seems to be a duplicate).
* **contact**: A text field where you can enter contact details.
* **relation**: A text field where you can specify your relation (not clear what this field is for, but it seems to be related to emergency contacts).
* **emergency_contacts_list**: A dropdown menu where you can select emergency contacts from the provided list.
* **position_applying_list**: A dropdown menu where you can select the positions you are applying for.
* **notes**: A text field where you can add any additional notes about your application.
* **consent_terms_and_conditions**: This field is actually "position_applying" and should be replaced with a real field named "consent_terms_and_conditions" to avoid confusion.
* **emergency_contacts_list**: This field seems to be a duplicate of "contact_preferences" and should be replaced or renamed.
* **phone**: This field should be replaced with a real phone number field or a more descriptive label. 

Note that I've kept the original field names and labels as they appear in the YAML file, even though they seem to be duplicated or unclear. I've only provided explanations and suggestions for improvement.
