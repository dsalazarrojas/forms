# Wcob Volunteer Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wcob Volunteer Application form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in volunteering for Wcob. This form helps us understand your skills, availability, and interests, which we use to match you with suitable volunteer roles within our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Phone number for contact purposes.
3. If you're interested in volunteering, choose a date from the calendar when you first noticed symptoms or reported any issues. This will help us understand your availability.
4. Select your available volunteer time in the Time field.
5. Once you've filled out the form, review your information for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (<name>:**First Name)**: Enter your given name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name (<name>:**Last Name)**: Enter your surname as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email (<name>:**Email)**: Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone (<name>:**Phone)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Reported Date (<name>:**Reported Date)**: If you're applying for a volunteer role related to health issues, please enter the date when you first noticed symptoms or reported any issues. 
* **Time (<name>:**Time)**: Select your available volunteer time from the options provided.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to the instructions above.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.

Note: This guide assumes that the form fields and labels are self-explanatory or have clear hints. If a field requires further clarification, please let me know. 

The guide has been written to be user-friendly and follows the instructions you provided. If you need any adjustments, I'll be happy to make them.
