# Diversity Council Membership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals interested in joining the Diversity Council, a group that promotes diversity and inclusion within our organization. The information collected will be used to evaluate your suitability for membership and to ensure we have accurate and complete information about each applicant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Begin by filling out the **Member Information** field, which requires your first and last name.
2. Next, select whether you hold a **Position** within the organization (True or False).
3. If you possess certain skills, select them from the options provided.
4. Choose a date when you are available for a meeting or event (optional).
5. Select a preferred time for a meeting or event (optional).
6. Enter your **Email Address** and **Phone Number** accurately and completely.
7. Decide if you would like to join a committee (True or False).
8. Select multiple interests you have from the options provided (optional).
9. Add any additional comments or notes in the **Note** field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Information** (text, required): Enter your first and last name to identify yourself as an applicant.
* **Position** (select_one, required): Check if you hold a position within the organization. This will help us understand your role and responsibilities.
* **Skills** (select_multiple, optional): Select the skills you possess that align with the Diversity Council's mission.
* **Available Date** (date, optional): When are you available for a meeting or event?
* **Preferred Time** (time, optional): What time would you prefer for a meeting or event?
* **Email Address** (email, required): Enter your email address accurately and completely.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your phone number accurately and completely.
* **Committee** (select_one, optional): Would you like to join a committee? This will help us understand your interest in leadership roles.
* **Interests** (select_multiple, optional): Select multiple interests you have that align with the Diversity Council's mission.
* **Note** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes that will help us understand your qualifications and preferences.

## Tips
