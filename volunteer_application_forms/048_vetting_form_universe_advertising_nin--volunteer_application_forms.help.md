# Vetting Form Universe Advertising NIN - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential volunteers for the "Universe Advertising NIN" project. Please fill out the form to apply for the volunteer position and provide your contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Volunteer Application" page with any relevant information about your application.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field. This is optional, but we recommend using your work or personal email address.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, if you prefer to be contacted by phone.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Volunteer Application
* **Volunteer Application** (Text, Optional): This field is for you to briefly describe your application and why you are interested in joining the project.

### Name
* **Name** (Text, Optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Email
* **Email** (Email, Optional): If you have a work or personal email address, please enter it here. This will help us contact you about your application and project updates.

### Phone
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): If you prefer to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number here.
