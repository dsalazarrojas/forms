# sports_team_volunteer_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from potential volunteers for sports teams, allowing them to express their interest in volunteering with the team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First name.
2. Fill in your Last name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Fill in your Phone number.
5. Fill in your Date of Birth.
6. Select your preferred volunteer shift.
7. Select the sports organization you are interested in (Sports Team A, B, or C).
8. Fill in your volunteer role.
9. Select your availability (Monday to Sunday).
10. Add any additional notes.
11. Indicate your volunteer consistency (Highly, Moderately, or Not at all).
12. Specify how long you plan to volunteer for (Less than 1 month, 1-3 months, 3-6 months, or More than 6 months).
13. Fill in the name of your emergency contact.
14. Specify your relationship with the emergency contact.
15. Fill in the emergency contact's phone number.
16. Enter the emergency contact's email address.
17. Fill in your volunteer experience, if any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`First name`, text, required): Fill in your First name.
* **Last name** (`Last name`, text, required): Fill in your Last name.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your Email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Fill in your Phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required): Fill in your Date of Birth.
* **Preferred Shift** (`Preferred Shift`, select_multiple, required): Select your preferred volunteer shift.
* **Sports Organization** (`Sports Organization`, select_one, required): Select the sports organization you are interested in (Sports Team A, B, or C).
* **Volunteer Role** (`Volunteer Role`, text, required): Fill in your volunteer role.
* **Availability** (`Availability`, select_multiple, required): Select your availability (Monday to Sunday).
* **Note** (`Note`, note, required): Add any additional notes.
* **Volunteer Consistency** (`Volunteer Consistency`, select_one, required): Indicate your volunteer consistency (Highly, Moderately, or Not at all).
* **How long** (`How long`, select_multiple, required): Specify how long you plan to volunteer for (Less than 1 month, 1-3 months, 3-6 months, or More than 6 months).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`Emergency Contact Name`, text, required): Fill in the name of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`Relationship`, text, required): Specify your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`Emergency Contact Phone`, text, required): Fill in the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`Emergency Contact Email`, email, required): Enter the emergency contact's email address.
* **Volunteer Experience** (`Volunteer Experience`, text, required): Fill in your volunteer experience, if any.
