# event_planning_volunteer_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in volunteering for an event. The form will be used by event hosts and volunteers to manage volunteer roles and coordinate event planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Provide accurate and up-to-date information about yourself.
3. Select the correct options for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (text, optional): Please enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on event materials.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for event-related communications.
* **Role** (select_multiple, optional):
	+ Event Host: The person or group that is organizing the event.
	+ Event Assistant: An individual who helps with event planning and execution.
	+ Event Volunteer: An individual who assists with event tasks and activities.
* **Availability** (time, optional): Select the times you are available to volunteer for the event. Please use the date picker to choose a specific time slot.
* **Skills** (select_multiple, optional):
	+ First Aid: Individuals with first aid training and certification.
	+ Event Planning: Experience with event planning and coordination.
	+ Marketing: Individuals with marketing and promotion skills.
* **Experience** (number, optional): Enter your number of years of experience with events or relevant volunteering.
* **Hours** (number, optional): Enter the number of hours you are available to volunteer for the event.
* **Contact** (note, optional): Provide any additional information about your contact preferences or availability.
* **Confirmation** (select_one, optional):
	+ 'True': Confirm that you are interested in volunteering.
	+ 'False': Decline volunteering.
* **Notes** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions about your application.
* **Event Host** (select_multiple, optional):
	+ Event Host: The person or group that is organizing the event.
	+ Event Organizer: An individual who helps plan and coordinate the event.
	+ Volunteer: An individual who assists with event tasks and activities.
* **Start** (date, optional): Enter the date the event starts.
* **End** (date, optional): Enter the date the event ends.
* **Volunteer** (select_one, optional):
	+ 'True': Confirm that you are a volunteer.
	+ 'False': Decline volunteering.
* **Assigned Tool** (select_multiple, optional):
	+ chatjimmy: A tool or resource assigned to you for event tasks.
	+ other: Any other tool or resource assigned to you.
