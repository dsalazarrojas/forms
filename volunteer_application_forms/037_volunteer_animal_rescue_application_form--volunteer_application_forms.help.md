# Volunteer Animal Rescue Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Animal Rescue Application Form is used to gather information from individuals interested in volunteering with the organization. This form is designed to collect contact details, skills, and availability for volunteer positions within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your address in the "address" field (optional).
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your primary phone number in the "phone" field (optional).
5. Enter your contact phone number in the "contact_phone" field (optional).
6. Describe your relevant experience in the "experience" field (optional).
7. List your skills in the "skills" field (optional).
8. Select your availability to volunteer, specifying start and end times in the "Availability" field.
9. (Optional) Specify how long you are available to volunteer, in the "volunteer_hours" field.
10. (Optional) Select how often you would like to volunteer, in the "volunteer_frequency" field.
11. Provide information about your animal experience in the "animal_experience" field (optional).
12. Choose your volunteer type (Volunteer, Foster, or Transport).
13. Select the type of animals you are willing to volunteer with (Cat, Dog, Bird, Fish, or Other).
14. Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
15. Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
16. Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
17. Enter your email address of your emergency contact (optional).
18. If applicable, list any medical conditions you have (optional).
19. If applicable, list any allergies you have (optional).
20. If you drive, enter your vehicle's make and model in the "vehicle" field (optional).
21. (Optional) If applicable, enter your vehicle's type (e.g. car, van, truck).
22. (Optional) If applicable, enter your vehicle's license plate number.
23. (Optional) If applicable, enter your vehicle's insurance provider and policy number.
24. Finally, select "Submit" to submit your application or "Save as Draft" to save your application for later.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **address**: Optional field for your home or work address.
* **email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **phone**: Enter your primary phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **contact_phone**: Enter an alternate phone number for emergency contact purposes (if different from primary phone number).
* **experience**: Describe any relevant experience you have in animal care or handling (if applicable).
* **skills**: List any relevant skills you have that would be beneficial for a volunteer role (e.g. animal handling, communication, etc.).
* **Availability**: Select your start and end times for volunteering in 24-hour format.
* **Availability Ending**: Select your end time in 24-hour format (if applicable).
* **animal_experience**: If applicable, describe any experience you have with animals.
* **volunteer_type**: Choose the type of volunteer role you are interested in (Volunteer, Foster, or Transport).
* **Animal Type**: Select the type of animals you are willing to work with (Cat, Dog, Bird, Fish, or Other).
* **volunteer_hours**: Enter the number of hours per week you are available to volunteer (if applicable).
* **volunteer_frequency**: Enter how often you would like to volunteer per week (if applicable).
* **emergency_contact**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **emergency_relationship**: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of your emergency contact (if applicable).
* **medical_condition**: If applicable, list any medical conditions you have that may affect your ability to volunteer.
* **Allergies**: If applicable, list any allergies you have.
* **Vehicle**: If you drive, enter your vehicle's make and model.
* **Vehicle Type**: If you drive, enter your vehicle's type (e.g. car, van, truck).
* **License**: If you drive, enter your vehicle's license plate number.
* **Insurance**: If you drive, enter your vehicle's insurance provider and policy number (if applicable).
* **Submit**: Select "Submit" to finalize your application or "Save as Draft" to save your application for later.
