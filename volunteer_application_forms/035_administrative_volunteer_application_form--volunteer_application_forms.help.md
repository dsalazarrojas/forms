# Administrative Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Administrative Volunteer Application Form is designed to gather information from applicants interested in becoming administrative volunteers within our organization. This form helps us understand your background, skills, and availability to determine the best fit for volunteer positions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. Provide a valid email address for communication purposes.
3. Enter your phone number for easy contact.
4. Fill in your current residential address.
5. Enter your city of residence.
6. Enter your state or province of residence.
7. Enter the postal code of your residence.
8. Provide your date of birth.
9. Choose the administrative position you are applying for.
10. Select the department you are interested in volunteering for.
11. Specify your availability schedule.
12. Enter the earliest date you can start volunteering.
13. Choose your expected commitment duration.
14. Enter the number of hours you can dedicate per week.
15. Choose your highest level of education.
16. If applicable, enter the details of your education background.
17. If applicable, describe any relevant administrative experience you have.
18. List any computer software skills you possess.
19. List all languages you speak fluently.
20. If applicable, describe any special skills you possess.
21. Explain why you want to volunteer with our organization.
22. Indicate whether references are available upon request.
23. Consent to a background check if required.
24. Date and sign the application form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`applicant_full_name`, text, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`applicant_email`, email, required): Provide a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address** (`applicant_address`, text, required): Enter your current residential address.
* **City** (`applicant_city`, text, required): Enter the city of your residence.
* **State/Province** (`applicant_state`, text, required): Enter the state or province of your residence.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (`applicant_zip`, text, required): Enter the postal code of your residence.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Position Applied For** (`position_applied_for`, text, required): Choose the administrative position you are applying for.
* **Department Applied For** (`department_applied_for`, select_one, required): Select the department you are interested in volunteering for.
* **Availability Schedule** (`availability_schedule`, text, required): Specify your availability schedule.
* **Earliest Start Date** (`earliest_start_date`, date, required): Enter the earliest date you can start volunteering.
* **Expected Commitment Duration** (`commitment_duration`, select_one, required): Choose your expected commitment duration.
* **Hours Available Per Week** (`hours_per_week`, number, required): Enter the number of hours you can dedicate per week.
* **Highest Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, required): Choose your highest level of education.
* **Education Details** (`education_details`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the details of your education background.
* **Administrative Experience** (`administrative_experience`, text, optional): If applicable, describe any relevant administrative experience you have.
* **Computer Skills** (`computer_skills`, text, required): List any computer software skills you possess.
* **Languages Spoken** (`languages_spoken`, text, optional): List all languages you speak fluently.
* **Special Skills** (`special_skills`, text, optional): If applicable, describe any special skills you possess.
* **Motivation for Volunteering** (`motivation_for_volunteering`, text, required): Explain why you want to volunteer with our organization.
* **References Available** (`references_available`, select_one, required): Indicate whether references are available upon request.
* **Background Check Consent** (`background_check_consent`, select_one, required): Consent to a background check if required.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, required): Date of submitting this application.
* **Applicant Signature** (`applicant_signature`, text, required): Electronic signature to confirm application.

Note to applicants: Please ensure that all fields are completed as accurately and thoroughly as possible. Incomplete or unclear submissions may affect the evaluation process.
