# professional_development_volunteer_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from volunteers for Professional Development opportunities, such as skills, experience, and motivation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the organization you are applying to volunteer with.
2. Choose the role you wish to take on.
3. Provide the contact person's information.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Choose your relevant skills (e.g. Leadership, Mentorship, Project Management).
6. If applicable, select a specific date and time frame for your availability.
7. Briefly describe your experience.
8. Explain why you are motivated to volunteer (motivation).
9. If you provide, please confirm that you are giving consent to proceed with your application.
10. Add any additional relevant information in the space provided.
11. If applicable, upload a file to support your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Organization
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the organization you are applying to volunteer with.

### 2. Role
* **Role** (`role`, `text`, required/optional): Choose a role that aligns with your interests and qualifications for the Professional Development opportunity.

### 3. Contact Person
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for this application.

### 4. Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you about your application.

### 5. Skills
* **Skills** (`skills`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all the relevant skills you have, from the options provided (e.g. Leadership, Mentorship, Project Management).

### 6. Availability
* **Availability** (`availability`, `date`, required/optional): If applicable, select a specific date and time frame for your availability.

### 7. Experience
* **Experience** (`experience`, `text`, required/optional): Describe any relevant experience you have that aligns with the Professional Development opportunity.

### 8. Motivation
* **Motivation** (`motivation`, `text`, required/optional): Explain why you are motivated to volunteer for this opportunity.

### 9. Consent
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you are giving consent to proceed with your application.

### 10. Additional Information
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional information that you think would be helpful for us to evaluate your application.

### 11. File
* **File** (`file`, `text`, required/optional): If applicable, upload a file to support your application.

### 11. Consent
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you are giving consent to proceed with your application.
