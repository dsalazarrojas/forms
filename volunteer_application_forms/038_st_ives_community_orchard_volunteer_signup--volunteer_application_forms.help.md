# st_ives_community_orchard_volunteer_signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
The St Ives Community Orchard Volunteer Sign-up Form is used to collect information from potential volunteers for the community orchard project. This form helps the project team to understand your interests, availability, and preferences, and to assign you to suitable volunteer roles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and accurately.
2. Provide all required information for each field.
3. Review and submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What they're interested in doing** (`volunteer_interests`): Please describe what type of activities or tasks you are interested in doing as a volunteer. This could include tasks such as tree planting, gardening, or helping with events.
* **Driving Licence** (`driving_licence`): If you have a valid driving license, please provide your license number and type (e.g., car, truck, or motorcycle).
* **Available to volunteer** (`available_to_volunteer`): If you are available to volunteer, select a date or range of dates when you are able to volunteer. This could be a specific date, a weekly time slot, or a month-long commitment.
* **What they hope to gain from volunteering** (`gain_from_volunteering`): If applicable, please explain what you hope to gain from volunteering with the project. This could include skills gained, experience, or social benefits.
* **Emergency contact person** (`emergency_contact`): Please provide the name and contact information of someone who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Any disabilities** (`disabilities`): If you have any disabilities, please describe them briefly so we can provide necessary support and accommodations.
* **Data protection** (`data_protection`): Indicate your level of consent for our data protection policies. Please note that this is a required field, but we value your confidentiality.
* **COVID-19 statement** (`covid19_statement`): Please confirm that you have read and understand our COVID-19 protocols and procedures.
* **Photographic permission** (`photographic_permission`): Please select the option that best suits your preferences regarding photographs taken during events or activities.
