# crisis_response_team_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from potential applicants who are interested in joining the crisis response team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the first two fields.
2. Enter your Email address to ensure we can contact you.
3. Enter your Phone number so we can reach you.
4. Select the Role you are applying for (Crisis Response Team or Support Services).
5. Choose any skills you possess that are relevant to the crisis response team (e.g. Crisis Response, Support).
6. Fill in your availability date and time if you are able to volunteer.
7. Provide any experience you have in volunteering or crisis response.
8. If you have volunteered before, provide the name of the organization, the number of hours you volunteered, and the language(s) you speak.
9. If you have any other languages you speak, please provide that information as well.
10. If you have any other education or certifications, please provide that information.
11. Provide the name, organization, and contact information for at least one professional reference.
12. Finally, click the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Your last name.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Your address, if you wish to provide it.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Your phone number so we can reach you.
* **Role Applying For** (`role_applying_for`, select_one, optional): The role you are applying for (Crisis Response Team or Support Services).
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, optional): Any skills you possess that are relevant to the crisis response team (e.g. Crisis Response, Support).
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): The contact information for a person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Availability** (`availability`, date, optional): The date you are able to volunteer.
* **Availability Time** (`availability_time`, time, optional): The time you are able to volunteer.
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, optional): Any experience you have in volunteering or crisis response.
* **Experience Years** (`experience_years`, number, optional): The number of years you have volunteered or have experience in crisis response.
* **Volunteer Organizations** (`volunteer_organizations`, select_one, optional): If you have volunteered before, please select the organization(s) where you have volunteered.
* **Volunteer Hours** (`volunteer_hours`, number, optional): The number of hours you have volunteered or plan to volunteer.
* **Languages** (`languages`, select_multiple, optional): Any languages you speak.
* **Other Languages** (`languages_other`, text, optional): If you speak any other languages not listed.
* **Education** (`education`, text, optional): Any other education or certifications you have.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, optional): The level of education you have (High School, College, Other).
* **Other Education** (`education_other`, text, optional): If you have any other education or certifications not listed.
* **References** (`references`, text, optional): The name of a professional reference.
* **Reference Name** (`references_name`, text, optional): The name of the reference.
* **Reference Organization** (`references_organization`, text, optional): The organization of the reference.
* **Reference Contact** (`references_contact`, text, optional): The contact information for the reference.
* **Submit** (`submit`, note, optional): Click to submit your application.
