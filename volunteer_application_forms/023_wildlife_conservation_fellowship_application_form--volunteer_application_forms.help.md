# Wildlife Conservation Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wildlife Conservation Fellowship Application Form is designed to collect information about your contact information, personal details, conservation experience, motivation, and other relevant facts for the Wildlife Conservation Fellowship program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
2. Provide your personal details in the "Personal Details" section.
3. Describe your conservation experience in the "Conservation Experience" section.
4. State your motivation for applying to the program in the "Motivation" section.
5. Select your availability in the "Availability" section.
6. Enter your email address and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone" sections.
7. Provide your date of birth and nationality in the "Date of Birth" and "Nationality" sections.
8. Select your language proficiency and education level in the "Language" and "Education Level" sections.
9. Specify your area of interest in the "Area of Interest" section.
10. Provide emergency contact information and medical history in the "Emergency Contact" and "Medical History" sections.
11. Indicate your medical condition and disability status in the "Medical Condition" and "Disability" sections.
12. Attach a medical certificate and signed consent form as proof in the "Medical Certificate" and "Consent Form" sections.
13. Sign and date the form in the "Signature" and "Date" sections.
14. Confirm your submission in the "Submission Confirmation" section.
15. Click the "Submit" button to complete the application.
16. Click the "Cancel" button to cancel the application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information**: Enter your current contact information, including your full name, address, and any other relevant details. This will help us contact you regarding your application. (Required)

### Personal Details
* **Personal Details**: Enter your personal details, including your date of birth, nationality, and any other relevant facts. This will help us understand your background and eligibility for the program. (Required)

### Conservation Experience
* **Conservation Experience**: Describe your experience with wildlife conservation efforts, including specific projects or initiatives you have been involved in. This will help us understand your qualifications for the program. (Required)

### Motivation
* **Motivation**: State your motivation for applying to the program. This will help us understand your goals and expectations. (Required)

### Availability
* **Availability**: Select your availability for the program. This will help us schedule your fellowship. (Required)

### Email
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This will be used for communication regarding your application. (Required)

### Phone
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number. This will be used for emergency contact. (Required)

### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth. This will help us understand your age and eligibility for the program. (Required)

### Nationality
* **Nationality**: Select your nationality. This will help us understand your citizenship status. (Required)

### Language
* **Language**: Select your language proficiency. This will help us understand your communication skills. (Optional)

### Education Level
* **Education Level**: Select your education level. This will help us understand your background and qualifications. (Optional)

### Area of Interest
* **Area of Interest**: Specify your area of interest. This will help us understand your goals and expectations. (Optional)

### Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter your emergency contact information. This will help us contact your emergency contact in case of an emergency. (Required)

### Medical History
* **Medical History**: Provide your medical history, including any medical conditions or allergies you may have. This will help us understand your medical status. (Optional)

### Medical Condition
* **Medical Condition**: Indicate if you have any medical conditions. This will help us understand your medical status. (Optional)

### Disability
* **Disability**: Indicate if you have any disabilities. This will help us understand your needs and requirements. (Required)

### Medical Certificate
* **Medical Certificate**: Attach a medical certificate as proof. This will help us verify your medical status. (Required)

### Consent Form
* **Consent Form**: Attach a signed consent form as proof. This will help us verify your consent. (Required)

### Signature
* **Signature**: Sign the form to confirm your identity. (Required)

### Date
* **Date**: Date the form to confirm the submission date. (Required)

### Submission Confirmation
* **Submission Confirmation**: Confirm your submission. This will help us understand your intention to submit the application. (Required)

### Submit
* **Submit**: Click the "Submit" button to complete the application. (Required)

### Cancel
* **Cancel**: Click the "Cancel" button to cancel the application. (Optional)
