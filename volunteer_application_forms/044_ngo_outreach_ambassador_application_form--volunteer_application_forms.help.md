# NGO Outreach Ambassador Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The NGO Outreach Ambassador Application Form is designed to collect information from potential ambassadors who want to represent the NGO in various outreach activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the "Volunteer Application Form 1" section, which includes a brief description of your interest in becoming an ambassador.
2.  Provide your contact details, including your name, email, and phone number.
3.  Specify your availability to participate in outreach activities and volunteer hours you are willing to commit.
4.  Select your availability date if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Application Form 1** (page 1): This section is an open-ended text field where you can describe your motivation for applying to be an ambassador and any relevant experience you have.
* **contact_details** (page 2): This field is for providing your full name, email, and phone number. Please ensure that the email address you provide is valid and you can receive emails from us.
* **name** (page 3): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on all promotional materials related to your ambassadorship.
* **email** (page 4): Enter your email address. This will be the primary means of communication between us and you.
* **phone** (page 5): Provide your phone number so we can contact you in case of emergencies or further discussions.
* **address** (page 6): Enter your physical address for our records.
* **availability** (page 7): Select "Yes" if you are available to participate in outreach activities and "No" if you are not. This will help us plan and schedule activities accordingly.
* **volunteer_hours** (page 8): Select how many hours you are willing to volunteer per week. This will help us plan the scope of the activities you can participate in.
* **availability_date** (page 9): If you are available at a specific date, provide it in this field to help us schedule activities.
