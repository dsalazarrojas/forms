# Generic Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect basic information from individuals interested in volunteering for a particular project or event. The information gathered will be used to contact and confirm volunteer participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to provide a valid email address for contact and communication purposes.
3. Select your availability (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening) and preferred contact method (e.g., Email, Phone, In Person) in the respective fields.
4. Click the submit button to send your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name (1)
• **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents. Please keep it concise and accurate.

### Last Name (2)
• **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents. Please keep it concise and accurate.

### Email (3)
• **Email**: Enter a valid email address where we can contact you for further information and updates. Please use a professional email address.

### Phone (4)
• **Phone**: Enter your phone number where we can reach you if needed. This can be a work, home, or personal number.

### Availability (5)
• **Availability**: Select one or more times of day when you are available to volunteer. Please be specific and realistic with your availability.

### Preferred Contact Method (6)
• **Preferred Contact Method**: Choose how you would like to be contacted. If you prefer Email, we will contact you via email. If you prefer Phone, we will call you, and if you prefer In Person, we will try to meet with you in person.

## Tips
- Be accurate and honest when filling out this form.
- Make sure to check your email and phone regularly for updates and information from us.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
