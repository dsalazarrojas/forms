# easter_holiday_club_volunteer_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Easter Holiday Club is seeking volunteers for its upcoming events. This form helps applicants provide necessary information to be considered for volunteer positions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you provide accurate and relevant information.
2. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields (though none are marked as required in this form, please make sure to fill out all relevant fields for a successful application).
3. Double-check your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **What Areas Can You Help With** (`volunteer_areas`, select_multiple, optional)
Select all the areas where you can assist, such as catering, watching kids, or welcoming parents.

### 2. **Easter Event Date** (`easter_event_date`, date, optional)
Choose the date of the Easter event you'd like to volunteer for. Please use the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### 3. **Easter Event Time** (`easter_event_time`, time, optional)
Select the time of the Easter event you'd like to volunteer for.

### 4. **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional)
Provide a valid email address for the club to contact you.

### 5. **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional)
Enter your phone number for the club to reach you.

### 6. **Additional Notes** (`note`, note, optional)
If you have any additional information or specific requests, provide it here.

### 7. **I confirm I have read and agree with the terms and conditions** (`volunteer_application_confirmation`, text, optional)
Check this box to indicate that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions of the club.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the form carefully before submission.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact the Easter Holiday Club directly.
- Only apply once; do not duplicate your application.
