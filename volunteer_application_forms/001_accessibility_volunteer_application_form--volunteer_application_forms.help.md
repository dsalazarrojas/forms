# Accessibility Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Accessibility Volunteer Application Form is designed for individuals interested in participating in accessibility initiatives and events. By filling out this form, you will be providing us with the necessary information to determine your suitability for volunteer roles. Your application will help us understand your availability, skills, and interests, allowing us to place you in positions that align with your strengths and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

*   Review each section carefully and ensure you have the necessary information ready.
*   Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, ensuring all required fields are completed.
*   Select the options that best fit your availability and interests.
*   Provide clear and detailed information where relevant.
*   Review your application carefully before submitting to ensure it is complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name**: Your full name, including first and last name.
*   **Email Address**: The email address we will use for volunteer communications. Make sure to use a valid email address that you regularly check.
*   **Phone Number**: Your best phone number where we can contact you in case of an emergency or need to confirm details.
*   **Organization Affiliation**: If you are representing an organization, please provide the name of the organization. If not, leave this field blank.
*   **Volunteer Roles Interested**: Select all roles you are interested and willing to perform, such as Event Support, Accessibility Auditing, Outreach and Advocacy, Documentation and Captioning, and Technical Assistance.
*   **Relevant Skills or Certifications**: If you have any relevant skills or certifications, such as sign language or web accessibility, please list them here.
*   **Availability Days**: Choose the regular days you are available to volunteer.
*   **Availability Time Frame**: If you have a specific time frame when you are available, please provide it here.
*   **Past Experience with Accessibility Initiatives**: If you have any experience with accessibility initiatives, please briefly describe it here.
*   **Accommodation Needs**: If you require any accommodations to participate, please let us know so we can make necessary arrangements.
*   **Emergency Contact Name**: Please provide the name of someone we can contact in case of an emergency.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone**: Provide the phone number of your emergency contact.
*   **Consent and Agreement**: By submitting this form, you agree to be contacted and to follow guidelines for volunteer work.
*   **Photo and Media Release**: If you agree to allow us to use your photo or media for outreach purposes, select "Yes, allow use." If not, choose "Do not allow use."
*   **Additional Notes or Questions**: If you have any additional information or questions, please provide them here.
