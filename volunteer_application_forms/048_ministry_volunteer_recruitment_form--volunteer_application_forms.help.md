# Ministry Volunteer Recruitment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in becoming volunteers for our ministry. The data collected will be used to understand your interests, availability, and preferences to match you with volunteer roles that best fit your skills and goals.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the form completely**: Make sure to provide all requested information for a more accurate matching process.
2. **Enter accurate information**: Double-check dates and contact details for correctness.
3. **Be honest and thorough**: Answer questions truthfully and with as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as you would like it to be referred to in our community.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as you would like it to be referred to in our community.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for future communication.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Ministry Interest** (`ministry_interest`, select_one, required: false):
  - **Biblical Studies**: You're interested in exploring and sharing biblical teachings.
  - **Children's Ministry**: You're passionate about serving and engaging with children and families.
  - **Counseling**: You're skilled at listening and offering guidance and support.
  - **Worship**: You enjoy leading or participating in worship and music.
  - **Youth Ministry**: You're interested in serving and mentoring young people.
- **Volunteer Role** (`volunteer_role`, select_multiple, required: false):
  - **Leader**: You're comfortable leading and directing others.
  - **Member**: You're comfortable participating and being part of a team.
  - **Supporter**: You're happy to help behind the scenes.
- **Availability** (`availability`, date, required: false): Format: MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2024). Indicate when you're available to volunteer.
- **Available Time** (`available_time`, time, required: false): Indicate your available time slots for volunteering.
- **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Share any additional comments or information about your volunteer experience.
- **How Did You Find Us** (`how_did_you_find_us`, select_one, required: false):
  - **Google Search**: You found us through an online search.
  - **Social Media**: You found us through social media or online platforms.
  - **Referral**: You were referred by a friend or family member.
  - **Other**: Other (please specify).
- **Volunteer Referral** (`volunteer_referral`, text, required: false): If someone referred you, please mention their name or contact information.
- **Volunteer Since** (`volunteer_since`, date, required: false): Format: MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2024). Indicate when you first started volunteering.
- **Last Volunteered** (`last_volunteered`, date, required: false): Format: MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2024). Indicate when you last volunteered.
- **Volunteer Frequency** (`volunteer_frequency`, select_multiple, required: false):
  - **Occasional**: You volunteer sporadically.
  - **Regular**: You regularly volunteer with us.
  - **As Available**: You volunteer as often as you're available.
- **Volunteer Hours** (`volunteer_hours`, number, required: false): Indicate how many hours you typically volunteer per month.
- **Volunteer Comments** (`volunteer_comments`, text, required: false): Share any additional comments or insights about your volunteer experience.
