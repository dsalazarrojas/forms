# Animal Shelter Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Animal Shelter Volunteer Application Form is designed to collect information about you, the applicant, to determine your suitability for volunteering at our shelter. The form will be reviewed by our team to assess your qualifications, interests, and commitment level.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your Full Name in the **Full Name** field.
2. Provide your **Email Address** and **Phone Number**.
3. Fill out the **Date of Birth** field with your birthdate.
4. Enter your current **Street Address**, **City**, and **State/Province**, and **ZIP or Postal Code**.
5. Provide the name and phone number of your **Emergency Contact**.
6. Select all the days you are available to volunteer from the **Available Days** field.
7. Choose all the times you are available to volunteer from the **Available Times** field.
8. Select your preferred **Hours Available Per Week**.
9. Choose all the areas of interest you have from the **Areas of Interest** field.
10. Select your **Preferred Volunteer Role**.
11. If you have previous volunteer experience, answer **Yes** in the **Do You Have Previous Volunteer Experience** field and provide details in the **Describe Your Experience** field.
12. If you have experience with animals, describe your background in the **Experience with Animals** field.
13. Select any **Special Skills or Talents** you have.
14. Indicate whether you can perform physical tasks in the **Can You Perform Physical Tasks** field.
15. If you have any animal allergies, select the level of allergy in the **Do You Have Any Animal Allergies** field and provide details in the **Allergy Details** field.
16. Answer whether you are willing to complete a background check in the **Are You Willing to Complete a Background Check** field.
17. Provide the names and contact information of two **References**.
18. Select how you heard about us in the **How Did You Hear About Us** field.
19. Share your motivation for volunteering in the **Why Do You Want to Volunteer With Us** field.
20. Confirm your understanding of the minimum commitment period in the **Minimum Commitment Understanding** field.
21. Agree to follow all shelter policies and procedures in the **I Agree to Follow All Shelter Policies and Procedures** field.
22. Sign your **Electronic Signature** by typing your full name.
23. Date of application in the **Application Date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Full Name
Full name as written on your identification documents.

### 2. Email Address
Contact email address where we can reach you.

### 3. Phone Number
Phone number we can contact you at.

### 4. Date of Birth
Your birthdate, must be 18 or older.

### 5. Street Address
Current street address where you reside.

### 6. City
City where you live.

### 7. State or Province
State or province you reside in.

### 8. ZIP or Postal Code
Postal code of your address.

### 9. Emergency Contact Name
Name of person or family member who can contact us in case of emergency.

### 10. Emergency Contact Phone
Phone number of your emergency contact.

### 11. Available Days
Select all the days you are available to volunteer (e.g. Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).

### 12. Available Times
Select all the times you are available to volunteer (e.g. Morning - 6 AM to 12 PM, Afternoon - 12 PM to 5 PM, Evening - 5 PM to 9 PM).

### 13. Hours Available Per Week
Select how many hours you can dedicate to volunteering per week.

### 14. Areas of Interest
Select all the areas where you are interested in volunteering (e.g. Dog Walking, Cat Socialization, etc.).

### 15. Preferred Volunteer Role
Describe the role you prefer to volunteer in.

### 16. Do You Have Previous Volunteer Experience
Indicate if you have previous experience in volunteering.

### 17. Describe Your Experience
If yes, describe your experience in volunteering.

### 18. Experience with Animals
Describe your background with animals.

### 19. Special Skills or Talents
Select all the special skills or talents you have.

### 20. Can You Perform Physical Tasks
Indicate if you can perform physical tasks such as lifting, bending, etc.

### 21. Do You Have Any Animal Allergies
Select the level of your animal allergy (e.g. No Allergies, Mild Allergies, Severe Allergies).

### 22. Allergy Details
If yes, provide details about your allergy.

### 23. Are You Willing to Complete a Background Check
Indicate if you are willing to complete a background check.

### 24. Provide Two References
Provide the name and contact information of two references.

### 25. How Did You Hear About Us
Select where you heard about us (e.g. Website, Social Media, etc.).

### 26. Why Do You Want to Volunteer With Us
Share your motivation for volunteering with us.

### 27. Minimum Commitment Understanding
Indicate if you understand and agree to the minimum commitment period.

### 28. I Agree to Follow All Shelter Policies and Procedures
Agree to follow all shelter policies and procedures.

### 29. Electronic Signature
Type your full name as an electronic signature.

### 30. Application Date
Date of application.

### 31. Application Date
Date of application.

Please note that this form is not a substitute for a formal application or interview process, and that our team will review your application thoroughly before making any decisions.
