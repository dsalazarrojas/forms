# public_health_volunteer_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The public health volunteer application form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in volunteering for public health efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Volunteer Information section with your personal details.
2. Provide your contact information in the Contact Information section.
3. Select your availability in the Availability section.
4. Describe your volunteer experience in the Volunteer Experience section.
5. List your skills in the Skills section.
6. Provide your medical information in the Medical Information section.
7. Enter emergency contact information in the Emergency Contacts section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Information**: This section collects your personal details, such as your name, date of birth, and address.
* **Contact Information**: This section captures your email, phone number, and other contact information.
* **Availability**: Please select when you are available to volunteer, such as weekdays or weekends.
* **Volunteer Experience**: Describe your previous volunteer experience, including the type of volunteering you have done and for how long.
* **Skills**: Choose the skills you have that are relevant to public health volunteering, such as medical, administrative, or communication skills.
* **Medical Information**: Provide any medical conditions or allergies you have that need to be disclosed.
* **Emergency Contacts**: Enter the name, relationship, phone number, and address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Information**: Enter any additional emergency contact information.
* **Medical History**: Describe any medical history you have, including previous injuries or illnesses.
* **Public Health Training**: Indicate if you have received public health training or certification.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **State**: Enter your state or province.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Enter your emergency contact's address.
* **Emergency Contact City**: Enter your emergency contact's city.
* **Emergency Contact State**: Enter your emergency contact's state or province.
* **Emergency Contact Zip**: Enter your emergency contact's zip code.

Note: All fields are optional, but please provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth assessment process.
