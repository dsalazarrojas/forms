# Student Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Volunteer Application Form is a tool designed to gather information from students interested in volunteering with [Organization Name]. This form helps us understand your availability, interests, and preferences for volunteering. Please take a few minutes to fill out the form, and we'll review it to determine the best volunteer opportunities for you.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). These are indicated as "required" in the field description.
2. Answer all fields accurately and truthfully, including your email and phone number.
3. If you're unsure about a field, please refer to the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section for additional guidance.
4. Review your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
5. Click "Submit" to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information** (`student_info`, text, required: false):
  This field is used to collect your name and basic contact information. Please fill it out with your full name, and we'll use it to address you personally.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false):
  Please fill out your current contact information, including your email and phone number, so we can reach you for any updates or further instructions.
* **Volunteer Information** (`volunteer_info`, text, required: false):
  This field allows you to briefly share your interests and motivations for volunteering. This information helps us match you with suitable volunteer opportunities.
* **Application Reason** (`application_reason`, text, required: false):
  Here, you can explain why you're interested in volunteering with us. Share any relevant experiences, skills, or goals that would support your application.
* **Available Hours** (`available_hours`, number, required: false):
  Please provide an estimate of your available hours per week. This helps us schedule volunteer shifts that fit your schedule.
* **Shifts** (`shifts`, select multiple, required: false):
  If you have availability for specific shifts, please select the ones that apply to you. For example, if you have availability on Monday and Wednesday, select "Yes" for those days.
* **Available Dates** (`available_dates`, date, required: false):
  Share any specific dates when you're unavailable. For example, if you have exams or a family event, let us know so we can plan accordingly.
* **Available Time** (`available_time`, time, required: false):
  If there's a particular time frame when you're available for volunteering, please let us know. This helps us schedule volunteer activities that fit your schedule.
* **Application Comments** (`application_comments`, text, required: false):
  If you have any further information or thoughts about your application, please share them here. This helps us better understand your needs and preferences.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false):
  Please ensure you use a valid email address that you check regularly. This is how we'll communicate with you for further instructions or updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false):
  Enter your phone number so we can contact you for any urgent matters or to provide updates.
* **Agreed Terms** (`agreed_terms`, select one, required: false):
  Please confirm that you've read and agree to our terms of use. This ensures you understand your responsibilities as a volunteer.
* **Agreed Privacy Policy** (`agreed_privacy_policy`, select one, required: false):
  Confirm that you've read and agree to our privacy policy. This ensures you're aware of how we handle your data.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select one, required: false):
  Once you've completed the form, select "Yes" to submit your application. If you're not ready to submit, select "No" and we'll review your progress.
* **Back** (`back`, select one, required: false):
  If you've filled out some fields and want to save your progress, select "Yes" to return to the previous page. Select "No" to continue with the form.

Please review the fields above and proceed accordingly. If you have any issues or need further clarification, don't hesitate to ask.
