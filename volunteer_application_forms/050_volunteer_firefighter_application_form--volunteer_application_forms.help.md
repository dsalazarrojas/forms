# Volunteer Firefighter Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from potential volunteer firefighters to assess their suitability for the role. It includes personal, contact, employment, and medical details to ensure a comprehensive evaluation process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your personal details, including your name, address, and contact information.
2. Provide your employment information, including your job title.
3. Indicate your availability for volunteer work.
4. Enter any relevant medical information.
5. Identify your emergency contact and any necessary emergency information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_info`): Please enter your personal details, such as your name and any other identifying information.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter your home address, including street number and name, city, state, and zip code.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): Provide your contact details, including phone number and email address.
* **Employment** (`employment`): Enter your job title and any relevant work experience.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Enter your current or previous job title.
* **Firefighter Details** (`firefighter_details`): Indicate whether you are currently an active or non-active firefighter.
* **Availability** (`availability`): Please specify when you are available to volunteer, e.g., days and hours of the week.
* **Medical Information** (`medical_info`): If you have any medical concerns or conditions, please enter them here.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): Identify someone who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Info** (`emergency_info`): Provide any additional emergency information, such as medical alert or allergies.
