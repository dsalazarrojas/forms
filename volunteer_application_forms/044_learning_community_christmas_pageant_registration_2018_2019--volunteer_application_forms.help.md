# Learning Community Christmas Pageant Registration 2018 2019 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Learning Community Christmas Pageant Registration form is designed to collect contact information and demographic data from participants, allowing us to efficiently manage event registrations and prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have not already registered for this event.
2. Enter your first name in the "first name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
4. Provide your email address in the "email" field (this will be used for event communications).
5. Enter your phone number in the "phone number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name in this field.
* **last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name in this field.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address in this field. Please ensure this is a valid email address that we can use for event communications.
* **phone number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number in this field. This will be used for any necessary contact or communication.
