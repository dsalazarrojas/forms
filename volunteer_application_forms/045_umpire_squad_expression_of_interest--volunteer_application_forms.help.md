# Umpire Squad Expression Of Interest - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Umpire Squad Expression Of Interest" form is designed to gather information from interested individuals who wish to join the Umpire Squad. This form is intended for those who want to express their interest in becoming part of the Umpire Squad and potentially be contacted for further evaluation and consideration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with accurate information about yourself. Start by providing your name, followed by your email address and phone number. Then, select a specific date and time when you are available to be contacted. Finally, provide any relevant details about your umpiring experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What Is Your Name** (1, text, required):
	+ This field is intended to collect your full name, as you would like to be known.
* **Email** (2, email, required):
	+ This field is for your contact email address, where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Phone Number** (3, text, required):
	+ This field is for your phone number, where you can be contacted for interviews or other relevant discussions.
* **Availability** (4, date, required):
	+ Select a specific date and time when you are available to be contacted for an interview or other evaluation purposes.
* **Experience Details** (5, text, required):
	+ Provide any relevant information about your umpiring experience, including any notable tournaments, leagues, or other experiences that make you a strong candidate for the Umpire Squad.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We appreciate your interest in joining the Umpire Squad and look forward to reviewing your application.
