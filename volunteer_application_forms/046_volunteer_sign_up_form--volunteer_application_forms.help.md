# Volunteer Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals interested in volunteering with the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Select your preferred volunteer type and shift.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback.
4. Confirm your submission by filling out the confirmation field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Name** (`volunteer_name`, Text, Required): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on our records.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter a valid email address for communication and updates.
* **Date** (`date`, Date, Required): Enter the date you are available to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Type** (`volunteer_type`, Select One, Required): Choose whether you are a Volunteer or Non-Volunteer.
* **Hours** (`hours`, Number, Required): Enter the number of hours you are available to volunteer per week.
* **Shift** (`shift`, Select One, Required): Select your preferred shift (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Other).
* **Comment** (`comment`, Text, Required): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your volunteer experience.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, Text, Required): Confirm your submission by entering your name and email address.
