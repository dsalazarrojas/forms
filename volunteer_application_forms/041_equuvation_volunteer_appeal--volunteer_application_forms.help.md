# equuvation_volunteer_appeal - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be used by individuals who wish to volunteer with EquuVation, a non-profit organization providing aid to marginalized communities. The form gathers contact information and volunteer details to facilitate communication and volunteer work assignment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **MainPage**
   On this page, you can add any notes or comments that you'd like to share with us.
2. Enter the contact information for the person filling out this form. This includes:
   * **contact_info**: Enter your name and contact details, such as phone number and email.
3. Specify your volunteer preferences:
   * **contact_preferences**: Let us know your availability, interests, and any specific causes you'd like to help with.
4. If you have any additional information or details you'd like to share about your volunteer work history or qualifications:
   * **volunteer_details**: Enter this information here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **contact_info** (contact_info, text, required):
	+ This field is where you will enter your name and contact details, such as your phone number and email. Please use a format like "Name: John Doe, Phone: 123-456-7890, Email: [john.doe@example.com](mailto:john.doe@example.com).
* **contact_preferences** (contact_preferences, text, optional):
	+ Share with us about your volunteer preferences, such as the causes you're interested in helping with, the number of hours you can volunteer, and any other relevant information.
* **volunteer_details** (volunteer_details, text, optional):
	+ If you have any additional information about your volunteer work history or qualifications, enter it here.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide clear and accurate contact information so we can contact you efficiently.
* Be specific about your volunteer preferences to increase the chances of being matched with opportunities that align with your interests.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us through the information provided in the contact_info field.
