# Farm Volunteer Worker Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in volunteering on the farm. It's essential to complete this form accurately to ensure we have the right information to contact and schedule volunteers effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Provide your basic contact information in the "Name," "Email," and "Phone Number" fields.
* Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
* If applicable, fill in the "Farm Name" and "Farm Location" fields with the specific farm you're associated with.
* Choose your available volunteer start and end dates using the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields.
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your first and last name as it appears on your official documents. This will help us contact you effectively.
* **Email**: Provide a valid email address we can use to communicate with you about volunteer opportunities and schedule changes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary phone number where we can reach you. This can be a mobile or landline number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY" (e.g., 05/25/1995).
* **Farm Name** (Optional): If you're associated with a specific farm, enter the name of that farm. This helps us track your volunteer work across multiple farms.
* **Farm Location** (Optional): If applicable, provide the location of the farm you're associated with. This can be a zip code, city, or farm address.
* **Start Date**: Choose your earliest available volunteer start date. This is the date you're ready to begin volunteering.
* **End Date**: Choose your last available volunteer end date. This is the date you'll be ending your volunteer work.

## Tips

* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact us at [Your Contact Info].
* We appreciate your interest in volunteering and look forward to having you on the farm!
