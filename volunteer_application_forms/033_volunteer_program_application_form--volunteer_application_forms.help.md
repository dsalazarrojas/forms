# Volunteer Program Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Volunteer Program Application Form is designed to collect information from interested individuals who want to join the volunteer program. It helps us understand their availability, skills, and preferences to match them with suitable volunteer opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information: This includes your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your volunteer hours: Indicate how many hours you can commit to volunteering per week.
3. Choose your application date: Select the date you would like to apply to the volunteer program.
4. Select your volunteer role(s): Pick one or multiple roles that interest you from the volunteer roles listed.
5. Choose your availability: Select the days you are available to volunteer.
6. Share your volunteer experience: Briefly describe your relevant volunteer experience.
7. Explain why you want to volunteer: Write a short paragraph on why you want to join the volunteer program.
8. List your skills: Mention any skills you have that would be beneficial for the volunteer program.
9. Provide availability details: Select specific details about your availability.
10. Add any application comments: If you have any additional information to provide, use this section.
11. Give a volunteer reference: Provide information about a person who can vouch for your volunteering skills and character.
12. Choose your application status: Select the status of your application.
13. Add volunteer application notes: If you have any additional comments or questions, use this section.
14. Add any additional notes: Provide any other information that you think is relevant to your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (id: 2)**: A text field to input your full name.
* **Email (id: 3)**: A text field to input your email address.
* **Phone (id: 4)**: A text field to input your phone number.
* **Volunteer Hours (id: 5)**: A field to input how many hours you can volunteer per week.
* **Application Date (id: 6)**: A date field to select the date you would like to apply to the volunteer program.
* **Volunteer Role (id: 7)**: A select multiple field to choose one or multiple roles that interest you from the listed options.
* **Availability (id: 8)**: A time field to select specific days you are available to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Experience (id: 9)**: A text field to share your relevant volunteer experience.
* **Why Volunteer (id: 10)**: A text field to explain why you want to join the volunteer program.
* **Skills (id: 11)**: A text field to list your skills.
* **Availability Details (id: 12)**: A select multiple field to select specific details about your availability.
* **Application Comments (id: 13)**: A text field to add any additional comments.
* **Volunteer Reference (id: 14)**: A text field to provide information about a person who can vouch for your volunteering skills and character.
* **Volunteer Application Status (id: 15)**: A select one field to select the status of your application.
* **Volunteer Application Notes (id: 16)**: A text field to add any additional comments or questions.
* **Additional Notes (id: 17)**: A note field to provide any other information that you think is relevant to your application.
* **Submit (id: 18)**: A text field to submit your application.
