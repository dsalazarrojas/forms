# Faith Based Health Worker Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals who wish to apply to become Faith Based Health Workers. The purpose of this form is to collect information about the applicant's details, experience, availability, value agreements, and references. The data collected will be used to assess the applicant's suitability for the role and to facilitate a smooth and efficient application process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Provide your personal and contact details in the "Applicant Details" page.
2. Describe your relevant experience in the "Experience" page.
3. Specify your availability in the "Availability" page.
4. List your value agreements in the "Value Agreements" page.
5. Select your references from the list of options in the "References" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Details (Page 1)**
	+ **Title:** Faith Based Health Worker Application Form
	+ **Description:** This is the first page of the form where you will be asked to provide your personal and contact details.
* **Experience (Page 2)**
	+ **Label:** Experience
	+ **Description:** Please describe your relevant experience in this field.
* **Availability (Page 3)**
	+ **Label:** Availability
	+ **Description:** Specify your availability for the role, including your work schedule and any conflicts or constraints.
* **Value Agreements (Page 4)**
	+ **Label:** Value Agreements
	+ **Description:** List your value agreements, such as codes of conduct or confidentiality agreements.
* **References (Page 5)**
	+ **Label:** References
	+ **Description:** Select any previous employers or supervisors who can vouch for your skills and experience.
