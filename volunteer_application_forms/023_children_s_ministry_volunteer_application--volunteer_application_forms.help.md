# Children's Ministry Volunteer Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Children's Ministry Volunteer Application form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in volunteering with children's ministry programs. This form helps us understand your background, interests, and availability to serve. Please fill out this form to express your interest in joining our team and helping us serve the children in our community.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and fill out your answers accurately.
2. Provide your true and genuine answers to all required fields.
3. If a question allows multiple selections, select all that apply.
4. Ensure that you have completed all required fields before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name** (1): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
*Required*

- **Last Name** (2): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
*Required*

- **Date of Birth** (4): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
*Required*

- **Email Address** (5): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
*Required*

- **Phone Number** (6): Enter a contact phone number where we can reach you.
*Required*

- **Home Address** (7): Enter your home address where we can mail you important documents.
*Required*

- **City** (8): Enter the city where you reside.
*Required*

- **State** (9): Enter the state where you reside.
*Required*

- **Zip Code** (10): Enter your zip code.
*Required*

- **Years of experience working with children** (12): If you have experience working with children, enter the number of years you have served.
*Required*

- **Have you volunteered with children before?** (13): Select 'Yes' if you have volunteered with children previously, 'No' otherwise.
*Required*

- **Describe your volunteer experience with children** (14): Share a brief description of your volunteer experience with children. This will help us understand your qualifications.
*Optional*

- **Why are you interested in volunteering with children's ministry?** (15): Share why you want to join our team and serve children.
*Required*

- **Special skills or talents** (17): List any special skills or talents you have that could benefit children, such as arts, music, sports, or tutoring.
*Optional*

- **Preferred age group to work with** (18): Select the age group you are most interested in serving, such as infants, toddlers, preschoolers, etc.
*Required*

- **Areas of ministry you are interested in** (19): Select all areas you are interested in serving, such as classroom teaching, mentoring, activity leader, etc.
*Required*

- **When are you available to volunteer?** (21): Select all days and times you are available to volunteer, such as Sundays, weekdays, etc.
*Required*

- **Approximate hours available per month** (22): Estimate the number of hours you are available to volunteer per month.
*Required*

- **Do you consent to a background check?** (24): Select 'Yes' if you agree to undergo a background check, 'No' otherwise.
*Required*

- **I agree to follow our child safety policies** (25): Confirm that you will abide by our child safety policies.
*Required*

Please note that all required fields are marked with *Required*. Ensure that you complete all required fields before submitting the form.
