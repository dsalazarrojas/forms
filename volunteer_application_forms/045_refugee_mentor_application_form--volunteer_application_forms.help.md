# Refugee Mentor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Refugee Mentor Application Form is designed to collect information from potential mentors to facilitate the mentorship program for refugees in your area. This form helps us understand your availability, interests, and skills to match you with a refugee mentee who would benefit from your expertise.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Provide your Email address. Please ensure it is a valid email address that you regularly check.
4. Enter your Phone number. This will be used to contact you about your application and any further opportunities.
5. Provide your Date of Birth. This will help us ensure you are eligible for the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**:
	+ A text field to collect your first name.
* **Last Name**:
	+ A text field to collect your last name.
* **Email**:
	+ A field to collect your email address. Please use a valid email address that you regularly check.
* **Phone**:
	+ A text field to collect your phone number.
* **Date of Birth**:
	+ A date field to collect your date of birth.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are accurately filled out to help us match you with a suitable mentee. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us and we will be happy to assist you.
