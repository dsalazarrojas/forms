# Community Resource Coordinator Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Resource Coordinator Application Form is for dedicated individuals to apply for a volunteer coordinator position within our community. This guide will walk you through each step of the form to help you understand what each field is for and how to complete it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please review the form carefully and ensure all required fields are filled in accurately.
2. Provide clear and honest information in all fields.
3. Select the most suitable options for your availability, commitment level, skills, and other sections.
4. Sign and date the application to complete it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name** (`applicant_full_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Applicant Email Address** (`applicant_email_address`, email, required): Enter your valid email address to ensure we can contact you.
* **Applicant Phone Number** (`applicant_phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Are you a current resident of the community?** (`residency_status_check`, select_one, required): Select 'True' if you reside within our community; 'False' otherwise.
* **Weekly Volunteer Availability** (`volunteer_availability_selection`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply, indicating your available days and times for volunteer work.
* **Desired Commitment Level** (`volunteer_commitment_level`, select_one, required): Choose your expected volunteer commitment hours per week.
* **Coordination Skills and Experience** (`coordinator_skills_selection`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply, indicating your relevant coordination skills and experience.
* **Tell us about your coordination experience** (`coordinator_experience_desc`, text, required): Describe your past roles, projects, or experiences relevant to this position.
* **Why do you want to be a Community Resource Coordinator?** (`motivation_for_applying`, text, required): Explain your motivation and interest in this role.
* **What do you see as the greatest need in our community?** (`community_needs_perception`, text, required): Share your insight on the community's needs.
* **Professional or Personal References** (`professional_references_list`, text, required): List 2-3 professional or personal references with their name and contact information.
* **Consent to a criminal background check** (`criminal_background_check_consent`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes - I Consent' or 'No - I Do Not Consent' for the background check.
* **Applicant Signature** (`volunteer_signature`, text, required): Type your full name to sign the application.
* **Today's Date** (`application_date`, date, required): Date and time of application submission.
