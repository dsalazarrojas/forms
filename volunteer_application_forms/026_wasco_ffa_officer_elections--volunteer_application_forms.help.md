# Wasco_FFA_Officer_Elections - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wasco_FFA_Officer_Elections form is used to organize and conduct an election for the Farmers' Federation Association chapter within Wasco. This form captures essential details about the election, such as the title of the election, candidate information, and election schedule and location.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form with the required information, following the guidelines above.
2.  Ensure that the form is complete and accurate before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Election Title** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the title of the election, which will be displayed on all promotional materials and announcements.
*   **Candidate Name** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the candidate running for the position.
*   **Candidate Email** (`3`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the candidate, which will be used for communication and contact purposes.
*   **Contact Phone** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the 10-digit contact phone number of the candidate.
*   **Election Date** (`5`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the election, in the format "YYYY-MM-DD", e.g., "2024-05-10".
*   **Election Time** (`6`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the election, in the format "HH:MM" (e.g., 08:00).
*   **Election Location** (`7`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location where the election will take place.
*   **Election Note** (`8`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional information or notes about the election.
