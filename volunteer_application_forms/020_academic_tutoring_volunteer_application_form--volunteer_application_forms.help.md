# Academic Tutoring Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Academic Tutoring Volunteer Application Form is a necessary step for individuals interested in volunteering as tutors, particularly for students, graduates, faculty, or community members who can provide academic support to peers. This form is used to collect essential details about the applicants, ensuring that they meet the required qualifications to effectively contribute to the tutoring program.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out the Academic Tutoring Volunteer Application Form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by providing your full name and contact email, as these are essential details for communication and verification purposes.
2.  Fill in your phone number, which we prefer to be your mobile number for ease of contact.
3.  Next, indicate your current status, whether you are a student, graduate, faculty member, or community member.
4.  List the subject or area in which you have the greatest expertise.
5.  If applicable, enter your current GPA, which we use to assess your academic performance.
6.  Select all the subjects you are qualified to tutor, as we have a list of options provided.
7.  Choose the student level you prefer to tutor (elementary, middle school, high school, or college).
8.  Indicate the days you are available to tutor, as we operate on a schedule.
9.  Briefly describe any prior tutoring or teaching experience you have had.
10.  Consent to a standard background check, which is required for working with minors.
11.  Provide the name, contact information, and relationship of one academic or professional reference.
12.  Finally, confirm your commitment to at least 2 hours of tutoring per week for one semester.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (text): Provide your full name as it appears on official documents.
*   **Email Address** (email): Enter your primary contact email for efficient communication.
*   **Phone Number** (text): Enter your mobile number for ease of contact.
*   **Current Status** (select_one): Choose your current status as a student, graduate, faculty member, or community member.
*   **Major Subject or Area of Expertise** (text): List the subject or area you are most knowledgeable in.
*   **Current or Final GPA** (number): Enter your current or final GPA, if applicable.
*   **Subjects You are Qualified to Tutor** (select_multiple): Select all the subjects you are qualified to tutor from the provided options.
*   **Preferred Student Level** (select_one): Choose the student level you prefer to tutor (elementary, middle school, high school, or college).
*   **Days Available to Tutor** (select_multiple): Choose the days you are available to tutor.
*   **Describe any Prior Tutoring or Teaching Experience** (text): Briefly describe any prior tutoring or teaching experience you have had.
*   **Background Check Consent** (select_one): Confirm your consent to a standard background check.
*   **Name and Contact of One Academic or Professional Reference** (text): Provide the name, contact information, and relationship of one academic or professional reference.
*   **Commitment** (select_one): Confirm your commitment to at least 2 hours of tutoring per week for one semester.
