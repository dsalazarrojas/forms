# Movement Partner Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Movement Partner Application Form is a tool used to gather information from potential partner organizations that wish to collaborate with us. This form helps us understand their interests, goals, and qualifications to determine whether they are a good fit for our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your partner's name in the "Enter Partner Name" field.
2. Provide your partner's address in the "Partner Address" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please use the format: username@domain.com
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Choose your areas of interest by selecting from the list provided in the "Areas of Interest" field. You can choose multiple areas.
6. Select your preferred type of collaboration by choosing from the options in the "Collaboration Request" field. You can choose multiple options.
7. In the "Motivation" field, explain in a few sentences why you are interested in partnering with us.
8. If you have a website for your organization, provide the URL in the "Partner Website" field. This will help us learn more about your organization and its capabilities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter Partner Name (Text)**: The name of your partner organization.
* **Partner Address (Text)**: The address of your partner organization.
* **Email (Email)**: Your email address, where we can reach out to you about your application and potential collaboration.
* **Phone (Text)**: Your phone number, where we can contact you to discuss your application.
* **Areas of Interest (Select Multiple)**: Choose the areas you are interested in, such as Partnership, Collaboration, etc.
* **Collaboration Request (Select Multiple)**: Choose the type of collaboration you are looking for, such as Partnership, Collaboration, etc.
* **Motivation (Text)**: Explain in a few sentences why you are interested in partnering with us.
* **Partner Website (Text)**: If you have a website for your organization, provide the URL here.
