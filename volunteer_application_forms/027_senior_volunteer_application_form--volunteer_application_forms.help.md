# Senior Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Senior Volunteer Application Form is designed to collect information from senior volunteers who wish to participate in various roles within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure that you have reviewed the form carefully and that you understand the purpose and requirements.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately, paying attention to the required and optional fields.
3. Ensure that you have permission from your emergency contact before providing their contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (<name>.<type> required/optional)
• **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): This field is used to collect your first name.
### Last Name (<name>.<type> required/optional)
• **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): This field is used to collect your last name.
### Email (<name>.<type> required/optional)
• **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): If you have an email address, please provide it here. This will help us contact you with further information.
### Phone Number (<name>.<type> required/optional)
• **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): This field is used to collect your phone number.
### Date of Birth (<name>.<type> required/optional)
• **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): This field is used to collect your date of birth.
### Address (<name>.<type> required/optional)
• **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): This field is used to collect your address.
### Volunteer Experience (<name>.<type> required/optional)
• **Volunteer Experience** (`volunteer_experience`, `text`, required): This field is used to collect your past volunteer experience.
### Availability (<name>.<type> required/optional)
• **Availability** (`availability`, `text`, required): This field is used to collect your availability to volunteer.
### Availability Details (<name>.<type> required/optional)
• **Availability Details** (`availability_details`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional information about your availability, please provide it here.
### Volunteer Role (<name>.<type> required/optional)
• **Volunteer Role** (`volunteer_role`, `select_one`, required): Please select one of the available volunteer roles that you are interested in.
### Emergency Contact Name (<name>.<type> required/optional)
• **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): This field is used to collect the name of your emergency contact.
### Relationship (<name>.<type> required/optional)
• **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `select_one`, optional): If you have provided an emergency contact, please select their relationship to you.
### Emergency Contact Phone (<name>.<type> required/optional)
• **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): This field is used to collect the phone number of your emergency contact.
### Emergency Contact Address (<name>.<type> required/optional)
• **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, `text`, optional): If you have provided an emergency contact phone number, please provide their address as well.
### Emergency Contact Email (<name>.<type> required/optional)
• **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, `email`, optional): If you have provided an emergency contact phone number, you can provide their email address here.
### References (<name>.<type> required/optional)
• **References** (`references`, `text`, required): This field is used to collect information from your references.
### References Details (<name>.<type> required/optional)
• **References Details** (`references_details`, `text`, optional): If you have provided references, please provide additional information about them.
### Medical Certification (<name>.<type> required/optional)
• **Medical Certification** (`medical_certification`, `text`, optional): If you have any medical certification, please provide it here.
### Medical Certification Details (<name>.<type> required/optional)
• **Medical Certification Details** (`medical_certification_details`, `text`, optional): If you have provided a medical certification, please provide details about it.
### Background Check (<name>.<type> required/optional)
• **Background Check** (`background_check`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether you have had a background check.
### Background Check Result (<name>.<type> required/optional)
• **Background Check Result** (`background_check_result`, `text`, optional): If you have selected "Yes" for the background check, you can provide further information about it here.
### Background Check Details (<name>.<type> required/optional)
• **Background Check Details** (`background_check_details`, `text`, optional): If you have provided a background check result, you can provide additional information about it.
### Language Preference (<name>.<type> required/optional)
• **Language Preference** (`language_preference`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you would like to be assigned to a role with a specific language requirement, please select the languages you are proficient in.
### Additional Comments (<name>.<type> required/optional)
• **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or information, please provide it here.
### Volunteer Application Form Agreement (<name>.<type> required/optional)
• **Volunteer Application Form Agreement** (`volunteer_application_form_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether you understand and agree to the terms and conditions of this application.
### Tips

- Please ensure that you have filled out the form completely and accurately.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
- We appreciate your time and effort in completing this form.
