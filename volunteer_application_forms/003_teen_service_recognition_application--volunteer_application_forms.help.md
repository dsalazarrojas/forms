# Teen Service Recognition Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Teen Service Recognition Application form is designed to help identify and recognize the dedication and service of teenagers in their communities. This form is typically used to nominate outstanding teen volunteers, athletes, and other individuals who have made significant contributions to their school or community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the name of the teen being nominated.
2. Indicate the school or organization that the teen is associated with.
3. Select the type of service the teen has provided (e.g., Volunteer, Award, or Other).
4. Enter the date the nomination was made.
5. Provide a brief description of the hours donated by the teen.
6. Attach any supporting materials (e.g., letters of recommendation, certificates of participation) that demonstrate the teen's service.
7. Confirm that the submission is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the teen being nominated.
* **school_or_organization** (text, required): Enter the name of the school or organization associated with the teen.
* **service_type** (select_one, required): Choose the type of service the teen has provided (e.g., Volunteer, Award, Other).
* **nomination_date** (date, required): Enter the date the nomination was made.
* **hours_donated** (text, required): Provide a brief description of the hours donated by the teen.
* **supporting_materials** (text, required): Attach any supporting materials that demonstrate the teen's service.
* **submission_confirmation** (text, required): Confirm that the submission is accurate and complete.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information about the teen's service.
* Attach supporting materials to demonstrate the teen's service and dedication.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled in.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
