# Rescue Mission Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in joining a rescue mission. It aims to assess their level of readiness and preparedness for the mission, as well as collect their personal details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Please provide your honest answers to the questions.
3. Ensure that you sign the form to indicate your approval and understanding of the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Details** (`applicant_details`, `text`, required):
	+ This field is used to collect your personal contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Rescue Mission Readiness** (`rescue_mission_readiness`, `text`, required):
	+ This field assesses your level of readiness for the rescue mission. Please describe your previous experience, skills, and qualifications that relate to the mission.
* **Rescue Mission Preparedness** (`rescue_mission_preparedness`, `text`, required):
	+ This field evaluates your preparedness for the mission, including any specific training or equipment you have. Please be specific about your level of experience and any specialized skills you possess.
* **Review and Sign** (`review_and_signature`, `text`, required):
	+ This field is a certification that you have read and understood the terms of the mission. Please sign below to confirm that you have carefully reviewed the information provided.

## Tips
- Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
- Double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us at form@gic.mx for assistance.
