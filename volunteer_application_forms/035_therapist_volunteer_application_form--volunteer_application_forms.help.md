# Therapist Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to volunteer as therapists in a therapeutic setting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields marked with \*.
2. Answer each question carefully, as they are all necessary for the organization to review your application.
3. Be honest and accurate in your responses.
4. Do not include any confidential or sensitive information in the comments sections.
5. When selecting options, select only one option per selection field.
6. If you have any additional comments or information, include them in the additional comments section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, \*: required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, \*: required): Enter a valid email address for the organization to contact you at.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, \*: required): Enter a valid phone number for the organization to contact you at.
* **Therapist Name** (`therapist_name`, `text`, not required): If you are not a licensed therapist, please do not fill out this field.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `select_one`, not required): Select the organization with which you are currently or have previously worked.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, \*: required): Enter the date you are available to start volunteering.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, \*: required): Enter the date you are available to end volunteering.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `select_multiple`, \*: required): Select the days of the week you are available to volunteer.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, \*: required): Please provide any additional comments or information that are relevant to your application.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, \*: required): Please provide any additional comments or information that are relevant to your application.
* **Volunteer Type** (`volunteer_type`, `select_one`, \*: required): Select the type of volunteer you wish to be (Clinical, Non-Clinical, or Both).
* **Clinical Experience** (`clinical_experience`, `text`, not required): If you have clinical experience, please describe it briefly.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, `select_multiple`, \*: required): Select the highest level of education you have completed.
* **Language** (`language`, `text`, not required): If you are fluent in any languages other than English, please list them.
* **Hours Per Week** (`hours_per_week`, `number`, \*: required): Enter the number of hours you are available to volunteer per week.
* **Availability Period** (`availability_period`, `select_multiple`, \*: required): Select the length of time you are available to volunteer.
* **Availability Days** (`availability_days`, `select_multiple`, \*: required): Select the days of the week you are available to volunteer.
* **Availability Start Time** (`availability_start_time`, `time`, \*: required): Enter the start time you are available to volunteer.
* **Availability End Time** (`availability_end_time`, `time`, \*: required): Enter the end time you are available to volunteer.
* **Skills** (`skills`, `text`, not required): If you have any relevant skills, please list them.
* **Experience** (`experience`, `text`, not required): If you have any relevant experience, please describe it briefly.
* **References** (`references`, `text`, \*: required): Please provide the names and contact information of two professional references.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, \*: required): Select whether you consent to being contacted by the organization.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, \*: required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.

Please note that this form is for informational purposes only and should not be used for actual application submission. Please review and follow the organization's instructions for actual submission and evaluation.
