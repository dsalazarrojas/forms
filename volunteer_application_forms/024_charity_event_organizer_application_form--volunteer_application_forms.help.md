# Charity Event Organizer Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide is for individuals or organizations applying to host a charity event through our platform. We review applications thoroughly to ensure that each event meets our guidelines and standards. Please ensure you have reviewed and understand our [Terms of Service](link to terms of service).

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details (pages 1-3).
2. Provide information about your organization (pages 4-5).
3. Describe your event proposal (pages 6-14).
4. Specify funding sources and team members (pages 15-7).
5. Answer additional questions (pages 18-19).
6. Upload supporting documents or professional references (pages 20-21).
7. Confirm agreement to our guidelines (page 22).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Full Name** (Page 1, Required)
* Legal name: Enter your full name as per your identification documents.

### **Email Address** (Page 2, Required)
* Contact email: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.

### **Phone Number** (Page 3, Required)
* Contact number: Enter a phone number where we can reach you.

### **Mailing Address** (Page 4, Required)
* Full address: Enter your full mailing address for communication purposes.

### **Organization Name** (Page 5, Required)
* Charity or group: Enter the name of your organization or group.

### **Organization Type** (Page 6, Required)
* Legal structure: Select the type of your organization: 501c3 nonprofit, Nonprofit pending, Community group, Religious organization, School or educational, or Other.

### **EIN or Tax ID** (Page 7, Not Required)
* If applicable: Enter your EIN or Tax ID number if your organization has one.

### **Event Proposal Title** (Page 8, Required)
* Event name: Enter a title for your charity event.

### **Event Description** (Page 9, Required)
* What is the event: Describe the event you are planning to host.

### **Event Purpose** (Page 10, Required)
* Goals and objectives: Describe the purpose and objectives of your event.

### **Target Audience** (Page 11, Required)
* Who will attend: Identify the target audience for your event.

### **Expected Attendance** (Page 12, Required)
* Number of attendees: Enter the estimated number of attendees for your event.

### **Proposed Event Date** (Page 13, Required)
* Preferred date: Enter the preferred date for your event.

### **Proposed Location** (Page 14, Required)
* Venue or area: Enter the location where you plan to host your event.

### **Estimated Budget** (Page 15, Required)
* Total budget: Enter the estimated total budget for your event.

### **Funding Sources** (Page 16, Required)
* How will you fund: Select the sources of funding for your event: Organization funds, Sponsorships, Ticket sales, Donations, Grants, or Other.

### **Core Team Members** (Page 17, Required)
* Key organizers: Enter the names of key organizers for your event.

### **Prior Event Experience** (Page 18, Required)
* Describe experience: Describe any prior experience of hosting events.

### **Risk Management Plan** (Page 19, Required)
* Safety measures: Describe your plan for managing risks and ensuring attendee safety.

### **Supporting Documents** (Page 20, Not Required)
* Upload if available: Upload any supporting documents for your application.

### **Professional References** (Page 21, Not Required)
* Names and contacts: Enter names and contacts of professional references.

### **Agreement** (Page 22, Required)
* Terms acceptance: Select "Yes I agree" if you understand and accept our guidelines.
