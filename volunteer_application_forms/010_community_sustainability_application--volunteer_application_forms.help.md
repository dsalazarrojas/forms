# Community Sustainability Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Sustainability Application form is used by community members to apply for funding and support for their sustainability projects. The form helps assess a project's potential impact, feasibility, and fit with the community's needs and priorities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Full Name**: Enter your primary contact name.
2. **Email Address**: Enter your primary contact email address.
3. **Phone Number**: Enter your best contact phone number.
4. **Organization Name** (optional): If you are representing an organization, enter its name.
5. **Organization Type**: Select the type of organization you are representing (individual, non-profit, community group, school/university, business, government agency, religious organization, or other).
6. **Project Title**: Enter a brief title for your sustainability project.
7. **Project Category**: Select the primary focus area of your project (renewable energy, waste reduction, water conservation, sustainable agriculture, green transportation, urban gardening, environmental education, climate action, biodiversity conservation, circular economy, or other).
8. **Additional Focus Areas**: Select any additional focus areas relevant to your project (community engagement, youth involvement, social equity, economic development, health and wellness, technology innovation, policy advocacy, research and data).
9. **Project Description**: Provide a detailed description of your project.
10. **Project Goals**: State the specific, measurable objectives of your project.
11. **Target Population**: Identify the community or population that will benefit from your project.
12. **Project Location**: Enter the geographic location where your project will take place.
13. **Project Start Date**: Enter the date when you plan to begin your project.
14. **Project End Date**: Enter the expected completion date of your project.
15. **Project Phase**: Select the current stage of your project (planning, pilot, implementation, expansion, established).
16. **Team Size**: Enter the number of people on your team.
17. **Volunteers Involved**: Enter the number of volunteers involved in your project (optional).
18. **Budget Requested**: Enter the amount of funding you are requesting.
19. **Total Project Budget**: Enter the total budget for your project, including other funding sources.
20. **Other Funding Sources**: Indicate if you have other funding sources (yes, no, or partially funded).
21. **Other Funding Details**: If you have other funding sources, describe them in detail.
22. **Environmental Impact**: Describe the expected environmental outcomes of your project.
23. **Community Impact**: Describe the expected social benefits of your project.
24. **Sustainability Plan**: Explain how your project will be sustained long-term after initial funding.
25. **Partnerships**: Indicate if you have community partners (yes, no, or number of partners).
26. **Partnership Details**: If you have partners, list their names.
27. **Previous Projects**: Indicate if you have completed similar projects before (no, yes, number of projects).
28. **Previous Project Details**: If you have completed similar projects, describe them briefly.
29. **Challenges Anticipated**: Identify any potential obstacles you expect to face.
30. **Support Needed**: Select the type of support you need most (funding, technical expertise, volunteer support, equipment/materials, space/facilities, marketing/outreach, partnerships, training).
31. **Success Metrics**: Describe how you will measure the success of your project.
32. **Reporting Commitment**: Indicate if you are willing to provide regular project updates (yes, quarterly, bi-annual, annual, upon request).
33. **Application Date**: Enter the date you are submitting your application.
