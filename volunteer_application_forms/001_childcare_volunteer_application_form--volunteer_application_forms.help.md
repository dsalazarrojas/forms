# Childcare Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Childcare Volunteer Application Form is a tool used to collect information from individuals interested in volunteering at a childcare facility. This form is used to assess a volunteer's experience, availability, and other relevant details.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Please enter your personal information in the required fields, starting with your Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number, Date of Birth, and Residential Address.
2. Describe your previous experience working with children, including volunteer and professional roles.
3. Select all the relevant certifications you possess, such as First Aid and CPR certification.
4. Choose your preferred age groups to work with.
5. Select the days you are available to volunteer.
6. Enter the earliest possible start date you can begin volunteering.
7. Provide at least one professional reference with their name and contact information.
8. Consent to a criminal background check, which is required for all childcare positions.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your legal name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): We will use this for primary communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Please enter your contact number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Volunteers must be 18 or older.
* **Residential Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Describe your previous experience working with children** (`childcare_experience`, `text`, required): Include volunteer and professional roles.
* **Relevant Certifications** (`certifications`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply, such as First Aid and CPR certification.
* **Preferred Age Groups to Work With** (`preferred_age_groups`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all the age groups you are interested in working with.
* **Available Days** (`available_days`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the days you are available to volunteer.
* **Earliest Possible Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you can begin volunteering.
* **Reference Name and Contact Info** (`reference_name`, `text`, required): Please provide at least one professional reference.
* **Do you consent to a criminal background check?** (`background_consent`, `select_one`, required): Required for all childcare positions.
