# Community Center Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from individuals interested in volunteering at the community center.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible. For each field, make sure to provide the most up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name of Applicant** (`volunteer_full_name_app`, text, required): Please fill out your full name, including your first name and last name.
* **Email Address** (`volunteer_email_app`, email, required): Enter your email address for us to contact you regarding your application status and results.
* **Primary Contact Phone Number** (`volunteer_phone_app`, text, required): Provide your primary phone number for us to contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (`volunteer_dob_app`, date, required): Enter your date of birth for age verification purposes.
* **Residential Address** (`volunteer_address_app`, text, required): Fill out your full residential address, including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Describe any previous volunteer experience** (`previous_volunteer_experience_app`, text, required): Share your previous volunteer experience to demonstrate your qualifications.
* **Key skills or expertise** (`key_skills_expertise_app`, text, required): Specify your key skills or expertise that you can bring to the community center.
* **Highest Level of Education Completed** (`educational_background_app`, select_one, required): Choose your highest level of education completed from the list provided.
* **Current Occupation (If applicable)** (`current_occupation_app`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your current occupation.
* **Program Area of Interest** (`primary_volunteer_interest_app`, select_one, required): Select the program area that you are most interested in volunteering for.
* **Weekly Commitment Hours** (`weekly_availability_hours_app`, number, required): Indicate how many hours you can commit to volunteering per week.
* **Preferred Volunteer Times** (`preferred_volunteer_times_app`, select_multiple, required): Choose your preferred volunteer times from the list provided.
* **Reason for volunteering with us** (`reason_for_volunteering_app`, text, required): Share your motivations and reasons for wanting to volunteer with the community center.
* **Personal Reference Name** (`personal_reference_name_app`, text, required): Provide the name of a personal reference not related to you.
* **Personal Reference Contact Info** (`personal_reference_contact_app`, text, required): Enter your personal reference's contact information (phone or email).
* **Digital Signature** (`volunteer_signature_digital_app`, text, required): Sign with your full name, certifying that the information provided is correct.
* **Date of Submission** (`submission_date_final_app`, date, required): Enter the date you submit this application.
* **Background Check Consent** (`background_check_consent_app`, select_one, required): Choose whether you consent to a background check.
