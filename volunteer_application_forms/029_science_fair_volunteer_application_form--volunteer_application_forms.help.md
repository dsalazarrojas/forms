# Science Fair Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from volunteers for the Science Fair event. It helps the organizers know who to contact, what role and availability each volunteer is willing to take on, and collects other essential contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your name, role, email, phone number, and address.
2. Choose your availability (weekends, weekdays, or both).
3. Select your education level and emergency contact information (name, phone number, relation, and address).
4. Choose your language proficiency.
5. Agree to the terms of the Science Fair Volunteer Application Form.
6. Leave any additional comments if necessary.
7. Sign your name to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Name**: This is your name and will be used to contact you.
* **Volunteer Role** (Select One): Choose one option:
	+ Volunteer
	+ Staff
* **Volunteer Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Volunteer Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Volunteer Address**: Enter your home address.
* **Date Available**: Enter your available date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time Available**: Enter your available time in HH:MM AM/PM format.
* **Role Duration**: Leave this field empty for now.
* **Volunteer Experience**: Briefly describe your relevant volunteer experience.
* **Language**: Leave this field empty if you don't know your language proficiency.
* **Education Level** (Select One):
	+ High School Diploma
	+ Bachelor's Degree
	+ Master's Degree
	+ Ph.D.
* **Availability** (Select Multiple):
	+ Weekends
	+ Weekdays
	+ Both
	+ Flexible
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of a family member, friend, or neighbor.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relation** (Select One):
	+ Family Member
	+ Friend
	+ Neighbor
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Leave this field empty if your emergency contact doesn't have an email.
* **Volunteer Notes**: Leave this field empty for now.
* **Volunteer Signature**: Sign your name to confirm your agreement.
* **Volunteer Agreement** (Select One):
	+ I have read and agree to the terms of the Science Fair Volunteer Application Form.
	+ I have not read and do not agree to the terms of the Science Fair Volunteer Application Form.
* **Volunteer Agreement Notes**: Leave this field empty for now.
* **Additional Comments**: Leave this field empty for now.
* **Science Fair Location**: Leave this field empty for now.
* **Science Fair Date**: Leave this field empty for now.
* **Science Fair Time**: Leave this field empty for now.

Note: Please make sure to agree to the terms of the Science Fair Volunteer Application Form before submitting. If you have any questions or concerns, contact [contact email].
