International Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
=============================================

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in volunteering with our organization. By filling out this form, you will be able to specify your volunteer type, availability, and other relevant details to help us better understand your qualifications and fit for volunteer opportunities. Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date, as it will be used for volunteer matching and scheduling purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email and Phone Number to enable us to contact you.
3. Select your volunteer type by choosing one or more options from the options list.
4. Choose your Start Date and End Date for your volunteering period.
5. Specify your Location for volunteering.
6. Enter your Organization Name and your Role in the organization.
7. Choose your preferred Shift for volunteering.
8. Select your volunteer Frequency (e.g., Daily, Weekly, etc.).
9. Specify your commitment to volunteering.
10. Provide your availability for volunteering (choose a date).
11. Enter the name, relationship, and contact information of an emergency contact person.
12. List any medical conditions or allergies you may have.
13. Specify any special needs you may have.
14. List the languages you are proficient in.
15. Provide references (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary phone number where we can reach you.
* **Volunteer Type**: Choose one or more options that best describe your volunteer role (e.g., Volunteer Leader, Volunteer Team Member, etc.).
* **Start Date**: Specify the date you would like to start volunteering.
* **End Date**: Specify the date you would like to end volunteering.
* **Location**: Enter the location where you would like to volunteer.
* **Organization Name**: Enter the name of the organization you are volunteering with (if applicable).
* **Role in Organization**: Enter your role in the organization (if applicable).
* **Shift**: Choose your preferred shift for volunteering (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, etc.).
* **Volunteer Frequency**: Choose how often you would like to volunteer (e.g., Daily, Weekly, etc.).
* **Commitment**: Enter your commitment to volunteering (e.g., regular shifts, specific dates, etc.).
* **Availability**: Choose a date for your availability to volunteer.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of an emergency contact person.
* **Relationship**: Enter your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions you may have.
* **Medical Allergies**: List any medical allergies you may have.
* **Special Needs**: Specify any special needs you may have.
* **Language**: Enter any languages you are proficient in.
* **Known Languages**: Choose languages you are proficient in (e.g., English, Spanish, etc.).
* **References**: Provide any relevant references (if applicable).
