### Disaster Relief Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Disaster Relief Volunteer Application Form is designed to gather information from potential volunteers who wish to assist with disaster relief efforts. This form will help us understand your availability, skills, and experience, which will be used to assign you a suitable volunteer role.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  Ensure that all required fields are filled out, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the label.
3.  Use the options provided in select fields to make your selection.
4.  Proofread your form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Application Form (`application_form`, text, not required):** Please provide a brief description of your background, experience, and motivation for volunteering with disaster relief efforts.
*   **Volunteers Name (`volunteers_name`, text, not required):** Enter your name.
*   **Volunteers Email (`contact_email`, email, not required):** Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
*   **Volunteers Phone (`contact_phone`, text, not required):** Enter your phone number for emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Name (`emergency_contact_name`, text, not required):** Enter the name of an emergency contact person (e.g., family member or friend).
*   **Emergency Contact Phone (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, not required):** Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
*   **Volunteers Address (`address`, text, not required):** Enter your home address.
*   **Available To Work (`available_to_work`, select multiple, not required):** Select the days of the week you are available to work:
    •   Option 1: Monday
    •   Option 2: Tuesday
    •   Option 3: Wednesday
    •   Option 4: Thursday
    •   Option 5: Friday
    •   Option 6: Saturday
    •   Option 7: Sunday
*   **Preferred Role (`preferred_role`, select one, not required):** Select the type of role you are interested in:
    •   Option 1
    •   Option 2
    •   Option 3
*   **Skills And Experience (`skills_and_experience`, text, not required):** Please describe any skills or experience you have that would be beneficial for the disaster relief effort.
*   **Volunteers Availability (`availability`, date, not required):** Select a specific date when you are available to volunteer.
*   **Volunteers End Date (`end_date`, date, not required):** Select a specific date when you would like to end your volunteering period.
*   **Volunteers Group (`volunteer_group`, select one, not required):** Select the type of group you would like to volunteer with:
    •   Option 1
    •   Option 2
    •   Option 3
*   **Volunteers Message (`volunteers_message`, note, not required):** If you have any additional comments or requests, please write them here.

### Tips

*   Be accurate and thorough in your responses to ensure proper volunteer assignment.
*   If you need help, don't hesitate to ask.
*   We appreciate your willingness to volunteer and contribute to disaster relief efforts.

This help guide is designed to support you in completing the form efficiently and effectively. Thank you for your interest in volunteering with us!
