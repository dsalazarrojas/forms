# Social Impact Mentor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Impact Mentor Application Form is used to gather information from potential mentors and mentees who are interested in participating in a mentorship program. The form helps to assess their suitability for the program and connect people with similar interests and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the category that best describes you (Mentor, Student, or Other).
2. Provide your contact information (Phone, Email, or Other).
3. If you have relevant work experience, please provide it in the format of a date.
4. Indicate whether you are a member of a mentorship committee.
5. Select the skills that you possess (Leadership, Project Management, Communication, etc.).
6. Provide a brief bio.
7. Explain your motivation for participating in the program.
8. Enter any availability constraints.
9. Enter your submission title.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start** (`id: 1`): Select the category that best describes you (Mentor, Student, or Other).
* **personal info** (`id: 2`): Provide your contact information (Phone, Email, or Other).
* **contact info** (`id: 12`): Provide your contact information (Phone, Email, or Other). 
* **personal info 2** (`id: 20`): Select the category that best describes you (Mentor, Student, or Other).
* **work experience 2** (`id: 13`): If you have relevant work experience, provide it in the format of a date.
* **mentorship committee 2** (`id: 14`): Indicate whether you are a member of a mentorship committee.
* **skills 2** (`id: 15`): Select the skills that you possess (Leadership, Project Management, Communication, etc.).
* **bio 2** (`id: 16`): Provide a brief bio.
* **motivation 2** (`id: 17`): Explain your motivation for participating in the program.
* **availability 2** (`id: 18`): Enter any availability constraints.
* **submission 2** (`id: 19`): Enter your submission title. 

Note: The fields with duplicate labels are used for redundancy to ensure that users can easily access their corresponding information without having to navigate through different pages to find it.
