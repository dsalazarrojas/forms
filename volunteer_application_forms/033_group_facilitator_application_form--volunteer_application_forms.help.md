# Group Facilitator Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Group Facilitator Application Form is a tool used to collect information from individuals interested in becoming group facilitators for upcoming workshops or events. This form will help us determine the best fit for the position and ensure that the right individuals are selected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **First page:** Enter your first name in the "First Name" field. This is the name you'd like to be known by as a facilitator.
2.  **Second page:** Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is the email address we'll use to contact you about your application and any further instructions.
3.  **Third page:** Choose a date for the "Application Date" field. This is the date you're submitting your application.
4.  **Fourth page:** Enter the workshop or event title you're interested in facilitating in the "Workshop Title" field. This helps us know which specific event you're applying for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`group_facilitator_application_form_1`, Text, Optional): This is the name you'd like to be known by as a facilitator.
* **Email** (`group_facilitator_application_form_2`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Application Date** (`group_facilitator_application_form_3`, Date, Optional): This is the date you're submitting your application.
* **Workshop Title** (`group_facilitator_application_form_4`, Text, Optional): This is the title of the workshop or event you're interested in facilitating.
