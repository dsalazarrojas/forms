# Volunteer Recruitment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Recruitment Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in volunteering for a specific cause or organization. This form aims to gather essential details to streamline the volunteer matching process and ensure a smooth experience for both volunteers and the organization. Please fill out the form carefully, and make sure to answer the fields honestly and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields according to the instructions provided below.
2. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Info** (`personal_info`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to capture your personal details, such as your name, email, or any other identifying information.
	+ Please provide a brief introduction or a short bio about yourself.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional):
	+ Use this field to provide your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
	+ Make sure to include any relevant contact method you prefer (e.g., email, phone, or messaging app).
* **Days And Time Preferences** (`days_and_time_preferences`, text, optional):
	+ Describe the days and time slots you are available to volunteer, including specific dates or time ranges.
	+ Be as specific as possible to increase the chances of being matched with suitable volunteer opportunities.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer the questions accurately and honestly to ensure a smooth volunteer matching process.
* If you have any specific skills or experience that would benefit your volunteer work, please mention them in the corresponding field (e.g., Personal Info).
* If you need assistance or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out to the organization or contact person mentioned in the Contact Info field.
