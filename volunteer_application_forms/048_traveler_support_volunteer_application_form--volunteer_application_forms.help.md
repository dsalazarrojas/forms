# Traveler Support Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Traveler Support Volunteer Application Form is designed to collect information from travelers who require support during their journey. This form helps us understand your needs and provide the necessary assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the following sections:

1. **Volunteer Application**: Provide a brief description of why you are applying for support.
2. **Contact Details**: Enter your contact information, including your name and contact method.
3. **Travel History**: Describe your travel history, including any relevant experiences or events.
4. **Travel Plans**: Outline your travel plans, including destinations and dates.
5. **Skills**: Select all the skills you have that may be useful for volunteer support, such as Volunteer Coordinator, Travel Guide, or Language Interpreter.
6. **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name and phone number of a contact person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
7. **Emergency Name**, **Emergency Phone**, **Emergency Relationship**, and **Emergency Address**: Enter the details of your emergency contact, including their name, phone number, and relationship to you.
8. **Emergency Email** and **Emergency Other**: Enter any additional information about your emergency contact.
9. **Medical History**, **Medical Information**, **Allergies**, **Medication**, **Procedures**, and **Medical Conditions**: Describe your medical history, including any allergies, medical conditions, or procedures.
10. **Travel Documents**: Scan and attach a copy of your travel documents, such as passports or visas.
11. **Travel Agency**, **Travel Agency Address**, **Agency Phone**, **Agency Email**, and **Agency Notes**: Provide information about the travel agency that arranged your trip.
12. **Submit**: Click the submit button to complete and submit your application.
