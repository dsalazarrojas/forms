# Soup Kitchen Volunteer Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the soup kitchen volunteer application form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
6. Select your preferred volunteer shift from the "Volunteer Shift" dropdown menu.
7. Select your volunteer frequency from the "Volunteer Frequency" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (<name>, <type>, required)
* **First Name** (`text`, Required): Enter your first name here.

### Last Name (<name>, <type>, required)
* **Last Name** (`text`, Required): Enter your last name here.

### Email (<name>, <type>, required)
* **Email** (`email`, Required): Enter your email address here. Please use a valid email format.

### Phone Number (<name>, <type>, required)
* **Phone Number** (`text`, Required): Enter your phone number here.

### Address (<name>, <type>, optional)
* **Address** (`text`, Optional): Enter your address here. (Optional)

### City (<name>, <type>, optional)
* **City** (`text`, Optional): Enter your city here. (Optional)

### State (<name>, <type>, optional)
* **State** (`text`, Optional): Enter your state here. (Optional)

### Zip Code (<name>, <type>, optional)
* **Zip Code** (`text`, Optional): Enter your zip code here. (Optional)

### Volunteer Shift (<name>, <type>, required)
* **Volunteer Shift** (`select_one`, Required): Select your preferred volunteer shift.

### Volunteer Frequency (<name>, <type>, required)
* **Volunteer Frequency** (`select_multiple`, Required): Select your volunteer frequency.

### Volunteer Day (<name>, <type>, optional)
* **Volunteer Day** (`text`, Optional): Enter any additional information about your volunteer day here. (Optional)
