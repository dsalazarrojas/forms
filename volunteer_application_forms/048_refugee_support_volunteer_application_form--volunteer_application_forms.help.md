# Refugee Support Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in volunteering to support refugees. It will be used to assess their suitability for volunteer work and to verify their application details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: **Volunteer Name**, **Phone Number**, **Email**, **Refugee Status**, **Volunteer Experience**, **Volunteer Interests**, **Volunteer Agreement**, **Application Confirmation**, **Volunteer Confirmation**, and **Background Check Consent**.
2. If applicable, select your **Emergency Contact** from the list of options.
3. Provide details for **Volunteer Agreement Details** and **Volunteer Reference Details** if required.
4. Select your **Volunteer Availability** and provide details in the **Volunteer Availability Details** field.
5. Select **True** for **Volunteer Agreement**, **Application Confirmation**, **Volunteer Confirmation**, and **Background Check Consent** to confirm your application.
6. Fill in the date and time for **Volunteer Agreement Date** and **Volunteer Agreement Time**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your personal email address.
* **Refugee Status**: Please select "Refugee" if you are currently a refugee or "Asylum Seeker" if you are seeking asylum.
* **Volunteer Experience**: Please select how long you have been volunteering, either "None", "Less than 6 months", or "More than 6 months".
* **Volunteer Skills**: Enter any relevant skills you have, such as language proficiency, experience with refugees, or specific technical skills.
* **Availability**: Select how you can be available to volunteer, such as "Full-time", "Part-time", "Flexible", or "Variable". Enter details about your availability in **Volunteer Availability Details**.
* **Emergency Contact**: Select your emergency contact from the list of options, such as "None", "Family Member", "Friend", or "Neighbor".
* **Volunteer Interests**: Select all the interests you are interested in volunteering for, such as "Community Service", "Disaster Relief", "Education", and so on.
* **Volunteer Agreement**: Please confirm your agreement to volunteer by selecting **True**.
* **Volunteer Agreement Date** and **Volunteer Agreement Time**: Enter the date and time you agree to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Confirmation**: Please confirm your application by selecting **True**.
* **Background Check Consent**: Please confirm your consent for background checks by selecting **True**.
* **Criminal History**: Please select **False** if you have no criminal history.
* **Volunteer Reference Details**: Enter details about your volunteer references, including their name, contact information, and experience working with them.
* **Volunteer Skills Details**: Enter any additional details about your skills, such as languages spoken or certifications held.
* **Volunteer Availability**: Select how you can be available to volunteer and enter details about your availability in **Volunteer Availability Details**.
* **Volunteer Orientation**: Please select **True** if you have completed an orientation session.
* **Volunteer Orientation Details**: Enter any additional details about your orientation, such as date and time completed.
* **Background Check Consent**: Please confirm your consent for background checks by selecting **True**.
* **Application Confirmation**: Please confirm your application by selecting **True**.
