# Youth Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Volunteer Application Form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to volunteer with the organization. This form helps us understand your interests, availability, and contact details to place you in the best-suited role within our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your information.
2. Ensure you have filled in all required fields.
3. Review your application for any errors before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Volunteer Application Form
* **Volunteer Application Form** (`volunteer_application_form`, text, optional): This is the main form that captures your application.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please provide your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please provide a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please provide your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Please enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.

### Page 2
* **Volunteer Shifts** (`volunteer_shifts`, select_multiple, true): Select the volunteer shifts you are available to work. You can choose one or more of the following:
	+ Morning
	+ Afternoon
	+ Evening
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_one, true): Please select your availability:
	+ Full-time
	+ Part-time
* **Volunteer Experience** (`volunteer_experience`, text, optional): If you have any volunteer experience, please describe it briefly.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Please provide the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, optional): Please provide the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`, text, optional): Please provide the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Address** (`emergency_address`, text, optional): Please provide the address of your emergency contact.

### Page 3
* **Volunteer Type** (`volunteer_type`, select_one, true): Please select your volunteer type:
	+ Volunteer
	+ Volunteer Leader
	+ Volunteer Team Leader
* **Volunteer Group** (`volunteer_group`, text, optional): If you are applying to a specific volunteer group, please specify it.

Tips:

* Please ensure you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We appreciate your interest in volunteering with our organization and look forward to reviewing your application!
