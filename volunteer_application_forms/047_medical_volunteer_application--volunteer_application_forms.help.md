# Medical Volunteer Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects necessary information from medical volunteers to help our organization assess their qualifications and availability for medical volunteer work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:
1. Fill in your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your email address and phone number in the respective fields.
3. If you have a medical license, provide your license number in the Medical License Number field.
4. Select the name of the hospital where you are affiliated with or where you have worked previously.
5. Choose the shifts you are available to work, if applicable.
6. Select the medical school you attended for your qualifications.
7. Provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
8. Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact (e.g., Mother, Father, Brother, etc.).
9. Choose the medical volunteer role(s) you are interested in or have experience with.
10. Select the date when you became available to volunteer.
11. Select the date when you expect to stop volunteering.
12. Enter the total hours you have worked in the past, if applicable.
13. Enter the number of hours you are available to work in a typical week, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Medical License Number** (`medical_license_number`, text, optional): If you have a medical license, provide your license number.
* **Hospital** (`hospital`, text, optional): If you are affiliated with or have worked at a hospital, select the name of the hospital.
* **Shift** (`shift`, text, optional): If you are available to work specific shifts, select the shift(s) you prefer.
* **Medical School** (`medical_school`, text, optional): Enter the name of the medical school you attended.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Provide the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Relation** (`emergency_contact_relation`, select_one, optional): Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact.
* **Medical Volunteer Role** (`medical_volunteer_role`, select_multiple, optional): Select the medical volunteer role(s) you are interested in or have experience with.
* **Medical Volunteer Shift** (`medical_volunteer_shift`, select_one, optional): Select the shift you are available to work.
* **Medical Volunteer Available Date** (`medical_volunteer_available`, date, optional): Enter the date when you became available to volunteer.
* **Medical Volunteer Ending Date** (`medical_volunteer_ending_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when you expect to stop volunteering.
* **Medical Volunteer Hours Worked** (`medical_volunteer_hours_worked`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the total hours you have worked previously.
* **Medical Volunteer Total Hours Worked** (`medical_volunteer_total_hours`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the total hours you are available to work in a typical week.
