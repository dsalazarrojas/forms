# Hospital Volunteeer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for individuals to express interest in volunteering at the hospital. It collects contact information and relevant medical certifications for review and potential follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field. This will be the primary contact method for the hospital.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field. This will help hospital staff contact you for further information.
5. If you have any relevant medical certifications or training, list them in the "Medical Certifications" field. This will help the hospital evaluate your qualifications for volunteering.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (Text, Required: False)
• **First Name**: Enter your given name or the name by which you are known. This is the first piece of information the hospital will use to identify you.

### Last Name (Text, Required: False)
• **Last Name**: Enter your surname or the name your parents or guardians gave you at birth. This is the second piece of information the hospital will use to identify you.

### Email (Email, Required: False)
• **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you regularly check. This will be the primary contact method for the hospital to reach you.

### Phone (Text, Required: False)
• **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached during business hours.

### Medical Certifications (Text, Required: False)
• **Medical Certifications**: List any relevant medical certifications or training you have received. This will help the hospital assess your qualifications for volunteering. Examples might include CPR, first aid, or other non-clinical certifications.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for your email and phone number.
- If you are uncertain about what medical certifications are relevant, contact hospital staff for guidance. 
- This form is for initial interest and application. Additional questions and assessments will occur during the volunteer process.
