# Financial Literacy Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Literacy Volunteer Application Form is designed for individuals who want to volunteer their time to help others in need of financial literacy education. This form is used to collect information about the applicant, which will be reviewed by the organization's selection committee to determine the best fit for the volunteer position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Basic Info section with your name, email, and phone number.
2. Enter your address in the Address section.
3. Choose your volunteer experience in the Experience section.
4. Enter your volunteer information in the Volunteer Info section.
5. If applicable, enter the number of volunteer hours you are able to commit to in the Volunteer Hours section.
6. Select your availability in the Availability section.
7. Click the Submit button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Basic Info (Required)**: This section is for you to enter your name, email, and phone number. This information will be used to contact you and keep you updated on the volunteer position.
* **Address (Required)**: This is where you live, and we'd like to know so that we can send you updates and instructions if needed.
* **Experience (Optional)**: This section is for you to choose from multiple options of your experience in financial literacy. This will help us understand your background and what you can bring to the volunteer position.
* **Volunteer Info (Optional)**: This section is for you to write a little about yourself and why you are interested in volunteering for financial literacy. Please be brief and concise.
* **Volunteer Hours (Optional)**: If you are able to commit to a specific number of hours per week, please let us know in this section. This will help us to plan the volunteer schedule.
* **Availability (Optional)**: This section is for you to select when you are available to volunteer. Please choose all the days of the week that you are available to volunteer.
* **Submit (Required)**: This is the button to submit your application after you have completed all the required fields. Please review all the sections carefully before submitting your application.
