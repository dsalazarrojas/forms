# Education Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to volunteer with our organization in an educational capacity. The form will be used to determine the best fit for each volunteer and to ensure that our organization is in compliance with any relevant laws and regulations. Please only submit one application per person.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out all required fields (marked as required) and provide any necessary supporting information. You may submit the form if you are 18 years or older and have a valid email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First name
* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you use regularly. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Contact preference**: Select your preferred method of contact. Options are:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ In-person
* **Availability**: Select your availability for volunteering. Options are:
	+ Monday to Friday
	+ Saturday
	+ Sunday
	+ Other (please specify in the field below)
* **Start date**: Enter the date you would like to start volunteering. Use the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **End date**: Enter the date you would like to finish volunteering. Use the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Volunteer role**: Enter a brief description of the role you are applying for.
* **Skills**: Enter any relevant skills you have that would be beneficial for the volunteer position.
* **Interests**: Enter any interests you have that align with our organization's goals.
* **Availability 1**: Select your availability for volunteering. Options are:
	+ Monday
	+ Tuesday
	+ Wednesday
	+ Thursday
	+ Friday
* **Availability 2**: Select your availability for volunteering. Options are:
	+ Monday to Friday
	+ Weekend
* **Volunteer type**: Enter a brief description of the type of volunteer you would like to be.
* **Volunteer location**: Enter the location where you would like to volunteer.
* **Volunteer hours**: Enter the number of hours you are able to volunteer per week.
* **Emergency contact name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact relationship**: Enter the relationship between you and the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact email**: Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact phone 2**: Enter the phone number of the secondary emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact email 2**: Enter the email address of the secondary emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact relationship 2**: Enter the relationship between you and the secondary emergency contact person.

## Tips
* Please make sure to review your application carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* Thank you for your interest in volunteering with our organization!
