# Student Ministry Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students interested in joining our ministry team. We use this information to understand your availability, skills, and preferences, which helps us to place you in suitable volunteer roles.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
3. Select your contact details (Student, Staff, Faculty, or Other).
4. Share any relevant emergency information.
5. Describe your volunteer interests and strengths.
6. Choose your preferred volunteer type (Local Outreach, Community Outreach, Missionaries, or Other).
7. Indicate your available days for volunteering.
8. Select your time commitment for volunteering (Less than 1 hour, 1-2 hours, or More than 2 hours).
9. Enter your available volunteer time.
10. Specify your preferred volunteer location(s).
11. Describe your volunteer shifts (e.g., regular schedule, specific dates).
12. Indicate your expected start and end dates for volunteering.
13. Confirm your student status (Student, Staff, Faculty, or Other).
14. If applicable, provide any relevant student information (e.g., student ID number, course).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info**: Enter your name, email, and phone number.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name and contact information of someone we can contact in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Details**: Select how we'll contact you (Student, Staff, Faculty, or Other).
* **Emergency Information**: Share any emergency contact information or special needs.
* **Volunteer Info**: Describe your strengths, skills, or previous volunteer experience.
* **Volunteer Type**: Select the type of volunteer work you'd like to be involved in (Local Outreach, Community Outreach, Missionaries, or Other).
* **Available Days**: Indicate the days you're available to volunteer.
* **Time Commitment**: Choose how many hours you can dedicate per volunteer shift.
* **Available Time**: Enter the specific times you're available for volunteering.
* **Preferred Location**: Specify where you'd like to volunteer (e.g., on campus, off campus, or specific locations).
* **Volunteer Shifts**: Describe your preferred volunteer schedule (e.g., regular shifts, specific dates).
* **Volunteer Start Date**: Enter your earliest expected start date for volunteering.
* **Volunteer End Date**: Enter your latest expected end date for volunteering.
* **Student Status**: Confirm your student status (Student, Staff, Faculty, or Other).
* **Student Info**: If applicable, provide any relevant student information (e.g., student ID number, course).

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when providing your contact information and emergency contact.
* Choose your volunteer type carefully, as this may affect your volunteer role.
* Be honest about your available time and days to ensure we can match you with suitable volunteer opportunities.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We'll review your application and get back to you with further instructions.
