# CSBG Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CSBG Application Form is designed to collect information about organizations that are applying for funding through the Community Development Block Grant (CSBG). This form is used to gather data about the organization's identity, services, and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your organization's information, including the Organization Name, Organization Type, EIN - Employer Identification Number, and Street Address.
2. Provide your Contact Person's information, including their Name, Title, Email Address, and Phone Number.
3. Answer questions about your organization's services, including the Service Area or Geographic Area, Populations Served, and Services Provided.
4. Describe how you will use the funding and what measurable outcomes and goals you expect to achieve.
5. Sign and date the form as the authorized representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name**: Enter the name of your organization.
* **Organization Type**: Select the type of organization you are applying for, such as Nonprofit, Community Action Agency, Local Government, or Faith-Based Organization.
* **EIN - Employer Identification Number**: Enter your organization's Federal Tax Identification Number.
* **Street Address**: Enter the street address of your organization's principal office.
* **City, State, ZIP Code**: Enter your organization's city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your organization's phone number.
* **Website**: Enter your organization's website URL (optional).
* **Contact Person Name**: Enter the name of the contact person for your organization.
* **Contact Person Title**: Enter the title or position of the contact person.
* **Contact Email Address**: Enter the contact email address of the contact person.
* **Executive Director or Executive Name**: Enter the name of the executive director or executive responsible for the application.
* **Year Organization Established**: Enter the year your organization was established.
* **Service Area or Geographic Area**: Enter the area where your organization operates.
* **Populations Served**: Select the populations your organization serves, such as Low-Income individuals, Families, Seniors, etc.
* **Services Provided**: Describe the primary services your organization offers.
* **Annual Operating Budget**: Enter your organization's approximate annual operating budget (optional).
* **CSBG Funding Amount Requested**: Enter the amount of funding you are requesting.
* **How Funding Will Be Used**: Describe how you plan to use the funding received.
* **Measurable Outcomes and Goals**: Describe the measurable outcomes and goals you expect to achieve.
* **Authorized Representative Signature**: Sign and date the form as the authorized representative of your organization.

Note: Please ensure that all fields marked as "required" are completed accurately to avoid any issues during the application process.
