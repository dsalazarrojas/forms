# Covid-19 Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Covid-19 Volunteer Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals who want to volunteer to help with the response to the Covid-19 pandemic. The form is used to gather essential details such as contact information, availability, and volunteer type to help the organization manage the volunteer program efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, Address, City, and Zip code in the respective fields.
2. If you have a Birth Date, please provide it. Note that this is not a required field.
3. Select your Volunteer Type from the available options.
4. Choose the Medical Practice you will be working with from the list of available options.
5. Select your available Shifts (if any) and Availabilty (if any) from the respective lists.
6. If you have an Emergency Contact, provide their First Name, Last Name, and Relationship.
7. Double-check all fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address that the organization will use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number that the organization will use to contact you.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address as it appears on your official documents.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter your city as it appears on your official documents.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter your state as it appears on your official documents.
* **Zip** (`zip`, number, required): Enter your zip code as it appears on your official documents.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, optional): If you want to provide your birth date, please enter it in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Volunteer Type** (`volunteer_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of volunteer you wish to be (e.g. Emergency Medical Technician, Doctor, Nurse, etc.).
* **Medical Practice** (`medical_practice`, select_multiple, required): Choose the medical practice you will be working with from the list of available options.
* **Shift** (`shift`, select_multiple, optional): If you are available to volunteer during a specific shift, please select it from the list of available options.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, optional): If you are available to volunteer during a specific time, please select it from the list of available options.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (`emergency_contact_first_name`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact, enter their first name.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (`emergency_contact_last_name`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact, enter their last name.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact, describe your relationship with them.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the organization's support team for clarification.
* The organization will review your application and get back to you soon.
