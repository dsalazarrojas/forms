# facilitator_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The facilitator questionnaire form is designed to gather essential information from facilitators who will be working with our organization. This form helps us to verify contact details, understand setup requirements, and keep facilitators informed about upcoming events and opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the facilitator questionnaire form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are filling out the correct information.
2. Enter your facilitator details on page 1, including any relevant contact information.
3. On page 2, provide your organization's details, such as your role and setup requirements.
4. Fill in your phone number and email address on page 3.
5. If you have any specific setup requirements, such as a preferred date and time, please enter this information on page 4.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Facilitator Details** (`facilitator_details`, text, required: false): Please enter your name, any relevant business or organization name, and any other details you feel are important for us to know.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): This is a space for you to enter your contact information, including your phone number and email address.
- **Organization Details** (`organization_details`, text, required: false): Enter your organization's name, website, and any other relevant details.
- **Setup Requests** (`setup_requests`, text, required: false): If you have any specific setup requirements, such as equipment or room preferences, please enter this information here.
- **Facilitator Phone** (`facilitator_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
- **Facilitator Email** (`facilitator_email`, text, required: false): Enter your email address so we can keep you updated.
- **Organization** (`organization`, text, required: false): Enter your organization's name.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter your job title within the organization.
- **Setup Date** (`setup_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you would like to have your setup confirmed.
- **Setup Time** (`setup_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you would like to have your setup confirmed.
