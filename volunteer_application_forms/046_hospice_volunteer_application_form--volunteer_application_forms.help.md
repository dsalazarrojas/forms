# Hospice Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospice Volunteer Application Form is a collection of questions that help us understand your background, availability, and commitment to volunteering at our hospice. Completing this form will help us assess your suitability for our volunteer program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully and answer all questions honestly and accurately.
2. Provide as much detail as possible in your responses.
3. Make sure to review and agree to all the terms and conditions of volunteering at our hospice.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (2, text, Required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents (e.g. passport, driver's license).
* **Address** (3, text, Required): Please enter your current address (e.g. street, city, state, zip).
* **Phone** (4, text, Required): Please enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Email** (5, email, Required): Please enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Hospice Location** (6, text, Required): Please select the location where you would like to volunteer.
* **Availability** (8, select_one, Required): Please indicate if you are available to volunteer (Yes/No).
* **Shifts** (9, select_one, Required): Please select the shifts you are available to volunteer (e.g. morning, afternoon, evening).
* **Days Available** (10, text, Required): Please enter the days of the week you are available to volunteer (e.g. Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday).
* **Days Commitment** (11, text, Required): Please enter the number of days per week you are committed to volunteering.
* **Months Commitment** (12, text, Required): Please enter the number of months per year you are committed to volunteering.
* **Years Commitment** (13, text, Required): Please enter the number of years you are committed to volunteering.
* **Reason for Volunteering** (14, text, Required): Please explain why you are interested in volunteering at our hospice.
* **Hospice Volunteer Training** (15, select_one, Required): Please indicate if you have completed or will complete our volunteer training (Yes/No).
* **Hospice Volunteer Experience** (16, text, Optional): Please provide any relevant volunteer experience you have had in the past.
* **Hospice Volunteer Vehicle** (17, text, Optional): Please enter any information about your volunteer vehicle (e.g. make, model, license plate).
* **Hospice Volunteer Reference** (18, text, Required): Please provide a contact information for a personal reference (e.g. name, phone number, relationship).
* **Hospice Volunteer Signature** (19, text, Required): Please sign your name to confirm your application.
* **Hospice Volunteer Agreement** (20, select_multiple, Required): Please agree to all terms and conditions of volunteering at our hospice.
* **Hospice Volunteer Agreement Terms** (21, select_multiple, Required): Please agree to all terms and conditions of volunteering at our hospice.

* **Hospice Volunteer Agreement 3** (23, select_multiple, Required): Please agree to all terms and conditions of volunteering at our hospice.
* **Hospice Volunteer Agreement 4** (24, select_multiple, Required): Please agree to all terms and conditions of volunteering at our hospice.
* **Hospice Volunteer Notes** (25, note, Optional): Please include any additional information or comments you would like to share with us.
