# library_volunteer_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals interested in volunteering at the library. The library needs this information to determine the best fit for volunteer roles and schedule volunteers effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, address, and email.
2. Select your preferred volunteer position(s) from the list provided.
3. Choose the days you are available to volunteer (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, etc.).
4. If you have any additional notes or comments, feel free to include them in the "notes" field.
5. Select how you would like to submit your application (e.g., submitted by library staff or self-submitted).
6. Finally, confirm your application by filling in the "phone" field with your contact phone number (optional but recommended for library staff follow-up).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info (contact_info, text, required):** Please fill in your contact information, including your name and address.
* **Phone (phone, text, optional):** If you'd like to provide a contact phone number, fill it in here. This helps the library staff get in touch with you about your volunteer role.
* **Email (email, email, optional):** Enter your email address so the library can contact you about volunteer opportunities and updates.
* **Volunteer Position (volunteer_position, select_one, optional):** Choose the volunteer role you're interested in:
	+ Volunteer: General volunteer duties.
	+ Library Assistant: Assisting library staff with tasks and projects.
	+ Library Ambassador: Representing the library in events and outreach.
* **Availability (availability, select_multiple, optional):** Check the days you are available to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Shifts (volunteer_shifts, number, optional):** If applicable, indicate how many shifts per week you can commit to.
* **Volunteer Purpose (volunteer_purpose, text, optional):** If you'd like to share why you're interested in volunteering or any specific goals for your volunteer experience, feel free to add them here.
* **Notes (notes, note, optional):** Any additional information or comments you'd like to include about your application or volunteer experience.
* **Submitted By (submitted_by, select_one, optional):** How you would like to submit your application:
	+ Submitted by library staff: The library staff will process your application and follow up with you.
	+ Self-submitted: You'll be responsible for submitting your application directly to the library.

## Tips

* Please fill in your application as thoroughly and accurately as possible.
* The more detailed information you provide, the better the library can place you in a suitable volunteer role.
* If you have any questions or concerns about your application or volunteer experience, feel free to contact the library staff.
* The library will be in touch with you regarding your application status and further volunteer opportunities.
