# Jeanne De Arc Medical Investigations Institute - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Jeanne De Arc Medical Investigations Institute is a platform for individuals to submit their applications to join our medical investigations team. This form is designed to gather necessary information from applicants, including their name, email, and phone number, to facilitate the application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the first field.
2. Enter your email address in the second field. Please ensure that your email is a valid email format (e.g., `john.doe@example.com`).
3. Enter your phone number in the third field. This field is not required, so you can leave it blank if you don't want to provide your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): This field is where you enter your full name. You can enter any name you would like to be known as while interacting with our team.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This is where you enter your email address. Please ensure it's a valid email format.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is for your phone number. If you have a phone number, please enter it. If not, leave it blank.
