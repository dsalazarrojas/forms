# healthcare_volunteer_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The healthcare volunteer application form is designed to gather information from individuals who wish to volunteer their time and services to healthcare organizations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name, email address, and phone number accurately.
2. Provide information about your medical history and any medical conditions you may have.
3. Check yes or no to indicate whether you have a medical provider and whether you are available to volunteer.
4. Enter your skills and experience in the text box.
5. Enter the number of hours you are able to volunteer per week.
6. Provide information for emergency contacts, including their name, relationship, and contact details.
7. Enter any references you have.
8. Check yes or no to indicate whether you are willing to undergo a background check.
9. Enter the date you submitted the application.
10. Check yes or no to indicate whether you submitted the application yourself.
11. Enter your email address.
12. Enter your phone number.
13. Enter your address.
14. Enter the date and time you submitted the application.
15. Enter any additional notes about your application.
16. Enter your signature.
17. Enter your position, department, and job title.
18. Enter your job type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Medical History**: Enter any medical conditions or concerns you may have.
* **Medical Condition**: Check yes or no to indicate whether you have any medical conditions.
* **Medical Provider**: Check yes or no to indicate whether you have a medical provider.
* **Availability**: Enter the number of hours you are able to volunteer per week.
* **Skills**: Enter any skills or experience you have that would be beneficial for volunteering.
* **Volunteer Experience**: Enter the number of hours you have volunteered in the past.
* **Volunteer Hours**: Enter the number of hours you are able to volunteer per week.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name, relationship, and contact details of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Check yes or no to indicate the relationship of your emergency contact.
* **References**: Enter any references you have.
* **Background Check**: Check yes or no to indicate whether you are willing to undergo a background check.
* **Application Date**: Enter the date you submitted the application.
* **Submitted By**: Check yes or no to indicate whether you submitted the application yourself.
* **Submitted By Email**: Enter the email address of who submitted the application.
* **Submitted By Phone**: Enter the phone number of who submitted the application.
* **Submitted By Address**: Enter the address of who submitted the application.
* **Submitted By Date**: Enter the date the application was submitted.
* **Submitted By Time**: Enter the time the application was submitted.
* **Submitted By Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the application.
* **Submitted By Signature**: Enter your signature as proof of submission.
* **Submitted By Position**: Enter your position, department, and job title.
* **Submitted By Department**: Enter the department who submitted the application.
* **Submitted By Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Submitted By Job Type**: Enter the type of job you hold.
