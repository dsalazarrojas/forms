# Job Fair Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Fair Volunteer Application Form is a form designed for volunteers to apply for job fair volunteer opportunities. To ensure a smooth and effective application process, please carefully read and complete this guide.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Fill out the **Job Fair Details** page with your basic information.
2. Provide your **Contact Information**, including your name, phone number, and email address.
3. Share your **Skills and Experience** relevant to the job fair volunteer role.
4. List your **Work History**, including any relevant work experience.
5. Select your preferred **Volunteer Shifts** from the available options.
6. Provide emergency contact information, including the **Emergency Contact** and their **Relationship**.
7. Enter your **Volunteer Shift Start** and **End** times.
8. If applicable, add any additional information about the **Job Fair Attendee** you will be interacting with.
9. List any relevant **Job Fair Organizations** you have worked with or would like to work with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Fair Details**: 
  • **Job Fair Details** is the first page of the form where you will provide your basic information. Please fill out your name, email, and any other relevant details for the job fair.
* **Contact Information**: 
  • **Contact Information** is used to provide your personal contact details. This includes your name, phone number, and email address.
  • This information will be used to communicate with you about your volunteer shifts and any other relevant job fair information.
* **Skills and Experience**: 
  • **Skills and Experience** is used to highlight any relevant skills or experience you have that can be beneficial for the job fair. Please be specific and provide examples.
* **Work History**: 
  • **Work History** is used to list any previous work experience that may be relevant to the job fair.
* **Volunteer Shifts**: 
  • **Volunteer Shifts** is used to select the shifts you are available to work. Please choose the shifts that best fit your schedule.
* **Emergency Contact**: 
  • **Emergency Contact** is used to provide information about an emergency contact person. This person will be contacted in case of an emergency.
  • Please provide their name, phone number, and email address.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: 
  • **Emergency Contact Relationship** is used to describe your relationship to the emergency contact. (e.g. Spouse, parent, sibling)
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Title**: 
  • **Emergency Contact Relationship Title** is used to specify the title or role of the emergency contact person in your life. (e.g. Spouse, parent, sibling)
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Name**: 
  • **Emergency Contact Relationship Name** is used to provide the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Phone**: 
  • **Emergency Contact Relationship Phone** is used to provide the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Email**: 
  • **Emergency Contact Relationship Email** is used to provide the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Volunteer Shift**: 
  • **Volunteer Shift** is used to select your preferred volunteer shift. Please choose the shift that best fits your schedule.
* **Volunteer Shift Start**: 
  • **Volunteer Shift Start** is used to specify the start time of your volunteer shift.
* **Volunteer Shift End**: 
  • **Volunteer Shift End** is used to specify the end time of your volunteer shift.
* **Job Fair Attendee**: 
  • **Job Fair Attendee** is used to provide any additional information about the job fair attendee you will be interacting with.
  • Please provide any relevant details about the attendee, such as their name, phone number, and email address.
* **Job Fair Attendee Phone**: 
  • **Job Fair Attendee Phone** is used to provide the phone number of the job fair attendee.
* **Job Fair Attendee Email**: 
  • **Job Fair Attendee Email** is used to provide the email address of the job fair attendee.
* **Job Fair Attendee Address**: 
  • **Job Fair Attendee Address** is used to provide the address of the job fair attendee.
* **Job Fair Attendee City**: 
  • **Job Fair Attendee City** is used to provide the city of the job fair attendee.
* **Job Fair Attendee State**: 
  • **Job Fair Attendee State** is used to provide the state of the job fair attendee.
* **Job Fair Attendee Zipcode**: 
  • **Job Fair Attendee Zipcode** is used to provide the zip code of the job fair attendee.
* **Job Fair Organizations**: 
  • **Job Fair Organizations** is used to list any relevant job fair organizations you have worked with or would like to work with.
  • Please provide any relevant information about your experience with the organizations.
* **Job Fair Organization 1**: 
  • **Job Fair Organization 1** and **Job Fair Organization 2** are used to list any additional job fair organizations you have worked with or would like to work with. Please provide any relevant information about your experience with the organizations.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
- We will review your application and contact you with further instructions.
- Thank you for your time and consideration in volunteering for the job fair.
