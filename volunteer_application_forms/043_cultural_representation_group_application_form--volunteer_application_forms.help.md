# cultural_representation_group_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "cultural_representation_group_application_form" is a form designed for individuals to submit their information for consideration to join a cultural representation group. This form aims to collect relevant details about the applicant, including their name, contact information, and demographic data. Please ensure you are applying to the correct cultural representation group and that you are not submitting duplicate applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required information in each field as prompted.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.
3. Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, required/optional): This field is for any additional comments or information you want to provide about yourself.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Provide your current address for contact and logistical purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, text, required/optional): Select your time zone to help us better understand your schedule.
* **Time Zone Offset** (`time_zone_offset`, time, required/optional): Enter your time zone offset in hours and minutes from UTC.
* **Submit** (`submit`, note, required/optional): This field is for you to confirm that you have read and understood the application instructions.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you are submitting this application.

Note: The `submit` field is not a field for you to submit the form, but rather a field for you to confirm that you have read and understood the application instructions.
