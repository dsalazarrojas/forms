# Community Fridge Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Fridge Volunteer Application Form is a necessary step to become a volunteer at our Community Fridge organization. This form helps us understand your background, availability, and skills to best match you with volunteer opportunities that fit your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly. If you have any doubts or need clarification on a question, please reach out to us. The form is divided into several sections, each with its own set of questions. Please follow the instructions and guidelines for each section to ensure accurate submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your legal first name. Please use your real name.
* **Last Name**: Your legal last name. Please use your real name.
* **Email Address**: Your primary contact email. We will use this to communicate with you about volunteer opportunities and other updates.
* **Phone Number**: Your primary contact phone number. This helps us reach you in case of emergencies or last-minute volunteer opportunities.
* **Date of Birth**: MM/DD/YYYY (Month/Day/Year) to ensure we understand your age and eligibility for volunteer work.
* **Street Address**: Your home address. This helps us verify your identity and location.
* **City, State, ZIP**: Please enter your city, state, and ZIP code for the same reasons as above.
* **Availability**: Select all the times when you are available to volunteer. This helps us match your schedule with volunteer opportunities.
* **Preferred Volunteer Role**: Choose one or more roles that interest you, such as Food Sorting, Fridge Restocking, or Community Outreach. We will match your skills and interests with volunteer tasks.
* **Previous Volunteer Experience**: Describe any previous experience you have that can help us assess your suitability for volunteer work. This can be school projects, work experience, or other volunteer work.
* **Motivation**: Explain why you want to volunteer with us. This helps us understand your goals and expectations.
* **Skills You Can Offer**: List any skills you can contribute, such as communication, teamwork, or leadership skills.
* **Reliable Transportation**: Please select True if you can physically get to the fridge location.
* **Can You Lift Up to 25 Pounds**: Select True if you are capable of lifting up to 25 pounds. This is a physical requirement for some tasks.
* **Food Handling Certification**: Select your current status regarding food handling certification.
* **Willing to Complete Background Check**: Select True if you are willing to complete a background check. This is a requirement for all volunteers.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: Select how you learned about the Community Fridge. This helps us understand our outreach efforts.
* **Volunteer Agreement**: Select "Yes, I Agree" if you agree to follow our volunteer guidelines.
