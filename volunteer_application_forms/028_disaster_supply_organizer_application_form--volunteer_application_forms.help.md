# Disaster Supply Organizer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disaster Supply Organizer Application Form is designed to collect information about individuals interested in volunteering for disaster supply organization efforts. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the following information:

1. **First name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
2. **Last name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
3. **Address**: Provide your mailing address where you can be reached for contact purposes.
4. **Email**: Enter your active email address for communication.
5. **Phone**: Enter your primary phone number where you can be contacted.
6. **Emergency Contact Name**: If you want someone to be notified in case of an emergency, please provide their name.
7. **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
8. **Relationship to Emergency Contact**: Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact.
9. **Type of Volunteer**: Choose the type of volunteer role you're interested in (General, Emergency Response Team, Supply Coordinator, or Logistics Coordinator).
10. **Volunteer Hours**: Enter the number of hours you are willing to volunteer per week.
11. **Volunteer Frequency**: Select how often you are available to volunteer (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly).
12. **Skills**: If you have any relevant skills, please provide them in this field.
13. **Volunteer Interest**: Select the areas where you are interested in volunteering (Disaster supply distribution, Disaster relief, First aid, Fundraising, Communications, or Logistics).
14. **Volunteer Experience**: If you have any volunteer experience, please provide a brief description.
15. **Services Needed**: If there are any services you need, please provide them in this field.
16. **Equipment Donation**: If you have any equipment you're willing to donate, please provide details.
17. **Vehicle Donation**: If you have a vehicle you're willing to donate, please provide details.
18. **Insurance**: If you have insurance coverage that's relevant to disaster supply organization, please provide details.
19. **Medical Clearance**: If you have any medical clearance, please provide details.
20. **Volunteer Agreement**: If you have any concerns about volunteer agreement, please provide them in this field.
21. **Volunteer Orientation**: Select the date for the volunteer orientation.
22. **First Aid and CPR Certification Date**: If you have First Aid and CPR certification, provide the date obtained.
23. **First Aid and CPR Certification Provider**: If applicable, provide the name of the provider of your First Aid and CPR certification.
24. **Other Certifications**: If you have any other relevant certifications, please provide them in this field.
25. **Other Certification Details**: If you have any other relevant certifications, please provide details.

Note: All fields marked as "required" are necessary for processing your application. Please ensure you fill out all required fields to complete the form correctly.
