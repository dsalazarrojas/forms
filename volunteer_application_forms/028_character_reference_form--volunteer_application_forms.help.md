# Character Reference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather information about an applicant's character and qualifications from a person who has had a significant relationship with them.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, providing accurate and honest information about your relationship with the applicant.
2. Make sure to select the correct frequency of contact and duration of relationship.
3. Rate the applicant's character and skills using the scale provided (1-5).
4. Provide a brief summary of the applicant's character and any additional comments or context.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reference Full Name**: Enter your name as it appears on official records or identification.
* **Contact Email for Reference**: Enter your email address for follow-up and verification purposes.
* **Reference Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Current Occupation**: Enter your current profession or position.
* **Applicant Full Name**: Enter the name of the person you are providing a reference for.
* **Relationship to Applicant**: Select the most accurate description of your relationship with the applicant.
	+ Personal Friend
	+ Family Member
	+ Professional Colleague
	+ Mentor or Teacher
	+ Other
* **Duration of Relationship**: Rate how long you have known the applicant (e.g., 2 years, 10 years, Since childhood).
* **Frequency of Contact**: Select how often you are in touch with the applicant.
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Annually
	+ Infrequently
* **Integrity and Honesty Rating**: Rate the applicant's integrity and honesty from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Reliability and Responsibility Rating**: Rate the applicant's reliability and responsibility from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Teamwork and Collaboration Rating**: Rate the applicant's teamwork and collaboration from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Empathy and Compassion Rating**: Rate the applicant's empathy and compassion from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Major Strengths of Applicant**: List 2-3 significant positive traits of the applicant.
* **Areas for Growth or Improvement**: Identify areas where the applicant may need to improve or grow.
* **General Character Comment Summary**: Provide a brief summary of the applicant's character.
* **Final Recommendation**: Select the most accurate description of your final recommendation for the applicant.
	+ Highly Recommend
	+ Recommend
	+ Recommend with Reservations
	+ Do Not Recommend
* **Confirmation of Reference Details**: Confirm that you are providing honest and fair feedback.
* **Date of Submission**: Enter the current date.
* **Additional Comments or Information**: Provide any other relevant information or context about the applicant.
