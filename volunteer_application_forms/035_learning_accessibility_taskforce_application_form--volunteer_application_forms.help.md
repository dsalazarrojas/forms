# learning_accessibility_taskforce_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for individuals who want to apply for the Learning Accessibility Taskforce. It is designed to gather essential information about yourself and your experience and commitment to accessibility-related work.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and fill in all required fields.
2. Ensure that your answers are accurate and reflect your current skills and experiences.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please contact the taskforce administrator for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Application Form**: This field allows you to provide a brief introduction and statement of intent.
* **Contact Information**: This field helps us get in touch with you for any further questions or discussions. Please provide your email address or phone number.
* **Personal Details**: This field is where you can provide any additional personal details that might be helpful for our review process.
* **Accessibility Experience**: If you have previous experience with accessibility work, please describe your experiences and skills in this field.
* **Taskforce Background**: This field allows you to explain your background and experience with the taskforce and its work.
* **Availability**: If you have any specific availability constraints (e.g., time or date), please specify them here.
* **Volunteer Commitment**: This field allows you to describe your commitment to the taskforce, including any specific goals or objectives.
* **Availability Period**: This is where you can specify your preferred duration of availability.
* **Availability Frequency**: Describe how often you are available to participate in taskforce activities.
* **Accessibility Skills**: If you have any specific skills related to accessibility, please list them here.
* **Availability Time**: If there are any specific times when you are unavailable, please specify them here.
* **Contact Number**: Please provide a contact number where we can reach you.
* **Email Address**: This is your email address where we can send you any further instructions or information.
* **Preferred Time**: If you have a preferred time for meetings or discussions, please specify it here.
* **Accessibility Commitment**: This field allows you to describe your commitment to accessibility work.
* **Availability Start Date**: If you have a preferred start date for your availability, please specify it here.
* **Accessibility Frequency**: Describe how often you are available to participate in accessibility activities.
* **Accessibility Time**: If there are any specific times when you are unavailable, please specify them here.
* **Accessibility Period**: This is where you can specify your preferred duration of accessibility work.
* **Volunteer English Language**: This is where you can select your proficiency level in English, which is the primary language used in our taskforce.
* **Volunteer Language Other**: If you are fluent in another language, please specify it here.
* **Volunteer English Reading Level**: This is where you can select your reading level in English.
* **Volunteer English Writing Level**: This is where you can select your writing level in English.
* **Volunteer Previous Taskforces**: Have you participated in a taskforce before? If yes, please describe your experience.
* **Volunteer Other Commitments**: If you have any other commitments that might affect your availability, please list them here.
