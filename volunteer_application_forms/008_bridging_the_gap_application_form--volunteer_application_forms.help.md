# Bridging The Gap Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bridging The Gap Application Form is designed to collect information about individuals interested in volunteering with us. By filling out this form, you will be able to express your interest in volunteering with us, provide your personal and professional information, and specify your availability and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each section of the form to understand the required fields.
2. Fill out all required fields with accurate information.
3. Use the provided hints to guide your input for each field.
4. Submit your application when all required fields are filled.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Personal Information
* **Full Name** (1): Enter your full legal name.
* **Email Address** (2): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (3): Enter your primary contact number.
* **Date of Birth** (4): Select your date of birth.
* **Street Address** (5): Enter your street address.
* **City** (6): Enter your city.
* **State or Province** (7): Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (8): Enter your ZIP or postal code.

### Background and Experience
* **Current Occupation** (9): Enter your current occupation or status.
* **Highest Level of Education** (10): Select your highest level of education (e.g., High School, Some College, etc.).
* **Relevant Volunteer or Work Experience** (11): Describe any relevant volunteer or work experience.
* **Skills and Qualifications** (12): List your relevant skills and qualifications.

### Volunteer Interest
* **Preferred Area of Volunteering** (13): Select the area where you are interested in volunteering (e.g., Mentoring, Tutoring, etc.).
* **Availability** (14): Select your availability (e.g., Weekdays Only, Weekends Only, etc.).
* **Hours Available Per Week** (15): Enter the number of hours you can volunteer weekly.
* **Earliest Start Date** (16): Select the earliest date you can start.
* **Duration Commitment** (17): Select how long you can commit (e.g., 1-3 months, 3-6 months, etc.).

### Motivation and References
* **Why do you want to volunteer with us** (18): Describe your motivation for volunteering with us.
* **Reference Name** (19): Enter the name of a professional or personal reference.
* **Reference Contact Information** (20): Enter email or phone for your reference.
* **Additional Comments** (21): Enter any additional information.

Note that some sections are optional, but all required fields should be completed to ensure a successful application.
