# Humanitarian Logistics Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Humanitarian Logistics Application Form is designed for humanitarian efforts, such as aid distribution or disaster relief. This form helps ensure that aid is delivered accurately and efficiently to those in need.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the General Information page with your name and any other relevant details as required.
2. Enter your contact information on the Contact Information page.
3. Provide personal details, including emergency contact information on the Personal Details page.
4. Select the aid type and any other required fields as needed.
5. Review your form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`1`, text, required): Enter your name and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Information** (`2`, text, required): Enter your contact information, including phone number and email.
* **Personal Details** (`3`, text, required): Enter your personal details, including emergency contact information.
* **Emergency Contact** (`4`, text, required): Enter the contact information for your emergency contact.
* **Aid Details** (`5`, text, required): Enter the details of the aid you need.
* **Select One** (`7`, select multiple, optional): Select the option that best applies to your situation.
* **Select One Hidden** (`8`, select multiple, optional): Select one of the options that best applies to your situation (not visible on form).
* **Select One Dictionary** (`9`, select multiple, optional): Select the option that best applies to your situation.
* **Note** (`10`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes as needed.
* **Email** (`11`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`12`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`13`, date, required): Enter the date you need aid.
* **Time** (`14`, time, required): Enter the time you need aid.
* **Decimal** (`15`, number, optional): Enter a decimal number if applicable.
* **Integer** (`16`, number, optional): Enter an integer number if applicable.
* **Text** (`17`, text, optional): Enter any additional text if applicable.
* **Textarea** (`18`, text, optional): Enter any additional text if applicable.
* **Aid Type** (`19`, select one, optional): Select the type of aid you need.
* **Aid Amount** (`20`, number, optional): Enter the amount of aid you need.
* **Aid Currency** (`21`, select one, optional): Select the currency of aid you need.
* **Aid Date** (`22`, date, optional): Enter the date you need aid.
* **Aid Time** (`23`, time, optional): Enter the time you need aid.
* **Aid Note** (`24`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes as needed.
* **Submit** (`25`, text, required): Review your form carefully before submitting it.
