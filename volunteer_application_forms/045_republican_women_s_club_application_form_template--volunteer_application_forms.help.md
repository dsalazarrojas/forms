# republican_womens_club_application_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from applicants who are interested in becoming a volunteer or leader in the Republican Women's Club. It is essential to provide accurate and truthful information to ensure a successful application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First and Last Name in the "First and Last Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your address in the "Address" field (optional).
5. Enter your zip code in the "Zip Code" field.
6. Explain why you want to join the club in the "Why do you want to join" field.
7. Select your party affiliation in the "Party Affiliation" field.
8. Choose your volunteer interests in the "Volunteer Interest" field.
9. Select your availability in the "Availability" field.
10. Provide any relevant volunteer experience in the "Volunteer Experience" field (optional).
11. List any references in the "References" field (optional).
12. Indicate whether you are willing to undergo a background check in the "Background Check" field.
13. Confirm your consent to participate in the club in the "Consent" field.
14. Choose your committee interest in the "Committee Interest" field.
15. Select your volunteer role in the "Volunteer Role" field.
16. Enter the number of hours you can commit to volunteering in the "Volunteer Hours" field.
17. Provide the start and end dates of your volunteer term in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields.
18. Select your volunteer status in the "Status" field.
19. Enter the position you are interested in applying for in the "Position" field.
20. Enter the number of hours you can commit to volunteering per week and per month in the "Hours Per Week" and "Hours Per Month" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First and Last Name (Contact Info)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Address (Contact Info)**: Enter your address if you want to provide it (optional).
* **Zip Code (Contact Info)**: Enter your zip code.
* **Why do you want to join**: Explain why you are interested in joining the Republican Women's Club.
* **Party Affiliation**: Select your party affiliation (Republican, Democrat, Independent, or Other).
* **Volunteer Interest**: Choose the areas you are interested in volunteering for (Volunteer, Donate, Member, or Leader).
* **Availability**: Select your availability for volunteering.
* **Volunteer Experience (Optional)**: Provide any relevant volunteer experience you have had.
* **References (Optional)**: List any references that can vouch for your character and qualifications.
* **Background Check**: Indicate whether you are willing to undergo a background check.
* **Consent**: Confirm your consent to participate in the club.
* **Committee Interest**: Choose the committee that interests you (Fundraising, Leadership, or Event Planning).
* **Volunteer Role**: Select the role you are interested in applying for (Volunteer or Leader).
* **Volunteer Hours**: Enter the number of hours you can commit to volunteering.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of your volunteer term.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of your volunteer term.
* **Status**: Select your volunteer status (Active or Inactive).
* **Notes (Optional)**: Enter any additional comments or information.
* **Position**: Choose the position you are interested in applying for (Volunteer or Leader).
* **Hours Per Week**: Enter the number of hours you can commit to volunteering per week.
* **Hours Per Month**: Enter the number of hours you can commit to volunteering per month.
