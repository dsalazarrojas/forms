# zero_waste_planning_team_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help volunteers apply for the Zero Waste Planning Team. The purpose of this form is to gather essential information about each applicant, including their contact details, availability, skills, experience, education, references, and volunteer information. This information will be used to evaluate each applicant and ensure that the team's needs are met.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the required information. Each field is optional, but please provide as much detail as possible. You can use the field hints to guide you through the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Application Form** (Page 1): This is the first page of the form. It serves as the introduction and header for the entire application.
  • **Application Form** (`application_form`, text, false): This field is the main content area where you can provide a brief introduction about yourself.
  • **Submission Confirmation** (`submission_confirmation`, text, false): In this field, please confirm that you have read and understood the form's purpose and instructions.
- **Contact Details** (Page 2):
  • **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, false): Please provide your full name, email address, and any other contact information you wish to share with the team.
- **Availability** (Page 3):
  • **Availability** (`availability`, text, false): Describe your availability to volunteer, including your work hours, days off, and any other relevant schedule information.
- **Skills** (Page 4):
  • **Skills** (`skills`, text, false): List your relevant skills and qualifications that align with the team's requirements.
- **Experience** (Page 5):

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough when filling out this form.
