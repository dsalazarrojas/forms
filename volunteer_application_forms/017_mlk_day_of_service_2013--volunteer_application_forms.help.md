# mlk_day_of_service_2013 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for volunteers to apply for the MLK Day of Service 2013 event. It asks for the type of application form needed and other relevant details to help us process your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select all the volunteer application forms you need from the list.
2. Provide a brief description of your application.
3. Optionally, indicate if this is for the ICC of Minnesota Day of Service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Application Forms** (`select_multiple`, required): Select all the forms you need to apply for.
* **Description** (`text`, required): Give a brief description of your application. This is your chance to tell us about your interests and qualifications.
* **This is for the ICC of Minnesota Day of Service** (`text`, optional): If this is for the ICC of Minnesota Day of Service, select this field and provide any relevant details.
