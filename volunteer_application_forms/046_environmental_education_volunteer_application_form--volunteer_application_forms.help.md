# environmental_education_volunteer_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential volunteers who are interested in supporting our environmental education initiatives. By completing this form, you will be able to express your interests, availability, and experience, and we will use this information to match you with suitable volunteer roles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your first and last name in the fields labeled "first_name" and "last_name" respectively.
2. Enter your contact information in the field labeled "contact_number".
3. Provide your email address (if applicable) in the field labeled "email".
4. Select the volunteer opportunities that interest you in the field labeled "volunteer_interests".
5. If you have relevant experience, enter the number of years you have been volunteering in the field labeled "years_experience".
6. Select the dates you are available to volunteer in the field labeled "available_dates".
7. Enter your preferred location for volunteering in the field labeled "location".
8. Provide any additional notes or information about your volunteer experience and interests in the field labeled "notes".
9. Finally, select how you would like to proceed with your application in the field labeled "submit".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number or other contact information where we can reach you.
* **Email (optional)**: If you have a valid email address, enter it so we can contact you more efficiently.
* **Volunteer Interests**: Select all the volunteer opportunities that interest you. This could be anything from conservation work to educational programs.
* **Years Experience**: If you have experience volunteering, enter the number of years you have been volunteering. This helps us assess your level of experience and match you with suitable opportunities.
* **Available Dates**: Select the dates when you are available to volunteer. Be as specific as possible so we can match you with suitable opportunities.
* **Location**: Enter your preferred location for volunteering. This could be a specific park, nature reserve, or community center.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional information about your volunteer experience, skills, or interests that would be helpful for us to know.
* **Submit**: Select how you would like to proceed with your application. You can choose to submit your application, or request more information before proceeding.
