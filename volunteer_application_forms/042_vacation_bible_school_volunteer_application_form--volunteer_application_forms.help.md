# Vacation Bible School Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from interested individuals who want to volunteer for the Vacation Bible School program. By filling out this form, applicants can indicate their interest in specific roles, dates, and activities, which will help the program coordinators schedule volunteers effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select your answer to "Are you interested in volunteering?" (Yes or No)
* Fill in your name, email address, and phone number
* Choose your preferred role (Teacher, Helper, or Helper/Teacher)
* Specify your available hours, volunteer hours, and volunteer dates
* Select your preferred volunteer activity (Bible Study, Music, Teaching, Games, Snack Service, Arts & Crafts, or Other)
* Write a brief bio to help us get to know you better

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Information**
	+ This field allows you to indicate your interest in volunteering for the Vacation Bible School program. Answer "Yes" if you are interested in volunteering, or "No" if you are not.
* **Name**
	+ Please fill in your name as it appears on your identification or driver's license.
* **Email**
	+ Enter a valid email address where we can contact you regarding your volunteer application.
* **Phone**
	+ Enter a valid phone number where we can contact you regarding your volunteer application.
* **Role**
	+ Choose the role that best fits your skills and interests:
		- Teacher: Leads Bible studies, teaches classes, and provides guidance.
		- Helper: Assists with tasks, events, and other activities.
		- Helper/Teacher: Combination of the above roles.
* **Available Hours**
	+ Specify the number of hours you have available to volunteer per week.
* **Volunteer Hours**
	+ Specify the number of hours per week you are willing to volunteer.
* **Available Date**
	+ Specify a date when you are available to start volunteering.
* **Start Date**
	+ Specify a date when you would like to start volunteering.
* **End Date**
	+ Specify a date when you would like to end volunteering.
* **Volunteer Start Date**
	+ Specify a date when you are available to start volunteering as a volunteer.
* **Volunteer End Date**
	+ Specify a date when you would like to end volunteering as a volunteer.
* **Volunteer Status**
	+ Select your current volunteer status:
		- Active: Currently active as a volunteer.
		- Inactive: Currently inactive as a volunteer.
* **Volunteer Activity**
	+ Choose the activity that best matches your skills and interests:
		- Bible Study
		- Music
		- Teaching
		- Games
		- Snack Service
		- Arts & Crafts
		- Other (please describe)
* **Volunteer Bio**
	+ Provide a brief bio about yourself, including any relevant experience or skills you have.

## Tips

* Please be honest when filling out this form, as it will help us match you with the right roles and activities.
* If you have any special skills or experience that are not listed, please let us know in your bio.
* We will contact you after receiving your application to discuss further details.
