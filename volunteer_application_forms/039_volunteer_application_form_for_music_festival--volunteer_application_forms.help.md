# volunteer_application_form_for_music_festival - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from volunteers for music festival events. It will help the event organizers assign tasks and schedule accordingly, ensuring a smooth and enjoyable experience for all volunteers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **contact details** on page 1 (page id: 1).
2. Provide your **emergency contact details** on the same page.
3. Select the specific **task/job details** you are available for on page 2 (page id: 2).
4. Specify your **shifts and availability** for the festival on page 3 (page id: 3).
5. Finally, read and agree to the **terms and conditions** on page 4 (page id: 4).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Details
**Contact Details (Page 1)** 
* **Name (text)**: Fill in your full name as you want to be addressed.
* **Phone Number (text)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email (text)**: Provide your email address for official communication.

### Emergency Contact Details
**Emergency Contact Details (Page 1)** 
* **Name (text)**: Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Relationship (text)**: Specify your relationship with the emergency contact person (e.g., parent, spouse, etc.).
* **Phone Number (text)**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Email (text)**: Provide the email address of your emergency contact person.

### Task Job Details
**Task Job Details (Page 2)** 
* **Task Job Type (select_one)**: Choose the tasks you are available to do during the festival:
	+ Volunteer for event set-up
	+ Volunteer for event takedown
	+ Volunteer for event security
	+ Volunteer for event registration

### Shifts and Availability
**Shifts and Availability (Page 3)** 
* **Shift Details (text)**: Specify your shift preferences (e.g., morning, afternoon, whole day).
* **Availability (text)**: Provide your availability for shifts (e.g., specific dates, hours).

### Terms and Conditions
**Terms and Conditions (Page 4)** 
* **Agree to Terms and Conditions (text)**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions of volunteering for the music festival.
