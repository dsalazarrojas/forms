# Infant Daily Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Infant Daily Report Form is a document used to track the daily activities, medical conditions, and observations of infants in care facilities. It is designed to provide essential information about the infant's feeding schedule, sleep patterns, and any medical conditions that may require attention. By completing this form, caregivers and facility staff can ensure accurate and consistent reporting of vital information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Ensure you have all necessary information about the infant, including their unique identifier (baby_id), feeding status, and any medical conditions.
*   Select the correct feeding status for the day (feeding).
*   Enter the amount of feeding done (feeding_amount).
*   Indicate the current status of the infant's diaper (diaper_status).
*   Report the duration of the infant's sleep (sleep_duration).
*   Confirm if the infant slept at their regular schedule (sleep_schedule).
*   Indicate if any feeding was done at a scheduled time (feeding_schedule).
*   Report any medical conditions the infant may have (medical_condition).
*   Indicate if any medication was administered (medication).
*   Enter any observations or notes about the infant's behavior (observation).
*   If applicable, enter any notes about the facility (facility_notes).
*   Enter the name of the caregiver submitting the report (caregiver_name).
*   Confirm the title and role of the caregiver (caregiver_title).
*   Enter the name of the facility where the infant is cared for (facility_name).
*   Enter the address of the facility (facility_address).
*   Enter the phone number of the facility (facility_phone).
*   Enter the email of the facility (facility_email).
*   Enter the time the report was submitted (submission_time).
*   Enter the date the report was submitted (submission_date).
*   Confirm if the report was submitted by the correct user (submitted_by).
*   Enter the location where the report was submitted (submission_location).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **baby_id** (number): Enter the unique identifier of the infant.
*   **feeding** (select multiple): Select all the feeding times that occurred, if any.
*   **feeding_amount** (number): Enter the amount of feeding done for the day.
*   **diaper_status** (select one): Indicate the current status of the infant's diaper (Active or Inactive).
*   **sleep_duration** (number): Enter the duration of the infant's sleep.
*   **sleep_schedule** (select one): Confirm if the infant slept at their regular schedule (Yes or No).
*   **feeding_schedule** (select multiple): Indicate if feeding occurred at scheduled times (Yes or No).
*   **medical_condition** (select one): Report any medical conditions the infant may have.
*   **medication** (select multiple): Indicate if any medication was administered.
*   **observation** (text): Enter any observations or notes about the infant's behavior.
*   **note** (note): This field is optional and can be used for additional notes.
*   **submitted_by** (select one): Confirm that the report was submitted by the correct user (Yes or No).
*   **submission_date** (date): Enter the date the report was submitted.
*   **submission_time** (time): Enter the time the report was submitted.
*   **submitted_at** (date): This field is optional and not used for reporting purposes.
*   **submitted_by_user** (text): Enter the name of the user submitting the report.
*   **submission_location** (select one): Enter the location where the report was submitted (Yes or No).
*   **caregiver_name** (text): Enter the name of the caregiver submitting the report.
*   **caregiver_title** (select one): Confirm the title and role of the caregiver (Yes or No).
*   **facility_name** (text): Enter the name of the facility where the infant is cared for.
*   **facility_address** (text): Enter the address of the facility.
*   **facility_phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the facility.
*   **facility_email** (email): Enter the email of the facility.
*   **facility_fax** (text): This field is optional.
*   **facility_notes** (note): This field is optional for notes about the facility.
*   **caregiver_title** (select one): This field is redundant and not used.
