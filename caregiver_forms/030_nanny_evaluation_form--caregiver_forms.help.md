# Nanny Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help families or childcare institutions evaluate the performance of a caregiver. It assesses their overall work, reliability, responsibility, quality, teamwork, and punctuality, and allows for additional comments and recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information, which includes:
	* Caregiver Name: Enter the name of the caregiver being evaluated.
	* Evaluation Date: Enter the date of the evaluation.
	* Overall Rating: Choose an overall rating of the caregiver's performance.
2. Answer the questions about the caregiver's performance, including:
	* Communication
	* Reliability
	* Responsibility
	* Quality
	* Teamwork
	* Punctuality
	* Adaptability
	* Flexibility
	* Initiative
	* Reliability Awareness
	* Promptness Awareness
	* Punctuality Awareness
	* Adaptability Awareness
	* Flexibility Awareness
	* Initiative Awareness
	* Overall Awareness
3. Add any Additional Comments or Recommendations you have for the caregiver.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Caregiver Name** (`nanny_evaluation_form`, text, required): Enter the name of the caregiver being evaluated.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, required): Choose an overall rating of the caregiver's performance (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Communication** (`communication`, text, optional): Describe the caregiver's communication skills.
* **Reliability** (`reliability`, text, optional): Comment on the caregiver's reliability.
* **Responsibility** (`responsibility`, text, optional): Describe the caregiver's responsibility.
* **Quality** (`quality`, text, optional): Evaluate the caregiver's quality of service.
* **Teamwork** (`teamwork`, text, optional): Rate the caregiver's teamwork.
* **Punctuality** (`punctuality`, text, optional): Comment on the caregiver's punctuality.
* **Adaptability** (`adaptability`, text, optional): Describe the caregiver's adaptability.
* **Flexibility** (`flexibility`, text, optional): Comment on the caregiver's flexibility.
* **Initiative** (`initiative`, text, optional): Evaluate the caregiver's initiative.
* **Reliability Awareness** (`reliability_awareness`, text, optional): Describe the caregiver's reliability awareness.
* **Promptness Awareness** (`promptness_awareness`, text, optional): Evaluate the caregiver's promptness awareness.
* **Punctuality Awareness** (`punctuality_awareness`, text, optional): Comment on the caregiver's punctuality awareness.
* **Adaptability Awareness** (`adaptability_awareness`, text, optional): Describe the caregiver's adaptability awareness.
* **Flexibility Awareness** (`flexibility_awareness`, text, optional): Evaluate the caregiver's flexibility awareness.
* **Initiative Awareness** (`initiative_awareness`, text, optional): Comment on the caregiver's initiative awareness.
* **Overall Awareness** (`overall_awareness`, text, optional): Rate the caregiver's overall awareness.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or recommendations you have for the caregiver.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, optional): Provide any recommendations for improvement.
* **Evaluation Form Completed** (`evaluation_form_completed`, time, optional): Record the time the form was completed.

## Tips
* Be honest and specific when providing feedback on the caregiver's performance.
* Use the overall rating field to give a general assessment of the caregiver's work.
* Provide detailed comments on the specific attributes and areas where the caregiver excels or needs improvement.
