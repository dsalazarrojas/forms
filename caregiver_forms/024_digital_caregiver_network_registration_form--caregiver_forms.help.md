# Digital Caregiver Network Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Caregiver Network Registration Form is used to collect demographic information from caregivers to determine their qualifications and network compatibility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your demographic information.
2. Choose your caregiver type and network affiliation.
3. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Required): Enter your first name in this text field.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Required): Enter your last name in this text field.
- **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication with the network. Please use a valid email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Required): Enter your phone number, which will be used for communication with the network.
- **Goals** (`goals`, Note, Required): Describe your goals for joining the digital caregiver network in this free-form text field.
- **Address** (`address`, Text, Required): Enter your home address, which will be used to determine network compatibility.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Required): Enter your date of birth to determine your network compatibility.
- **Time** (`time`, Time, Required): Select a 24-hour time format to represent your availability.
- **Caregiver** (`caregiver`, Select Multiple, Required): Select all the caregivers you are affiliated with in the dropdown menu.
- **Network Type** (`network_type`, Select One, Required): Choose the type of network you want to join from the options provided.
- **Network** (`network`, Select Multiple, Required): Select all the networks you want to be affiliated with from the options provided.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you only fill out the form once.
- Use a valid email address for communication with the network.
- Enter your date of birth accurately to ensure proper network compatibility.
- Choose your network type and affiliations carefully to find the right matches.
