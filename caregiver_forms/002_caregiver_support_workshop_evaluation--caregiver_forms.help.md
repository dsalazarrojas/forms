# Caregiver Support Workshop Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from participants to help improve and refine the caregiver support workshop. This feedback will be used to make future workshops more effective and valuable for caregivers.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Read through each section carefully** and make sure to answer every question that applies to you.
2. **Enter your name and organization or group** (if applicable) to help us identify your role and connection to the workshop.
3. **Rate the overall quality of the workshop** by selecting one of the provided options.
4. **Choose the topics that were most useful to you** from the list of options.
5. **Assess the facilitator's effectiveness** by selecting one of the provided options.
6. **Describe the most valuable takeaway** from the workshop (optional).
7. **Share any suggestions for improvement** (optional).
8. **Indicate whether you would recommend this workshop** to other caregivers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as a participant in the workshop.
* **Organization or group** (`organization`, text, optional): If you are part of an organization or group, enter the name here.
* **Workshop date** (`workshop_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the workshop you attended.
* **Overall rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the workshop's overall quality using the provided options (Very poor, Poor, Fair, Good, Very good).
* **Topics most useful** (`topics_most_useful`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the topics that were most useful to you from the list (Care strategies, Self care, Communication, Community resources, Legal guidance).
* **Facilitator effectiveness** (`facilitator_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Assess the facilitator's effectiveness using the provided options (Not effective, Somewhat effective, Effective, Very effective).
* **Most valuable takeaway** (`most_valuable_takeaway`, text, optional): Briefly describe one key point you will use from the workshop.
* **Suggestions for improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, optional): Share any ideas for future workshops or logistics.
* **Would recommend** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you would recommend this workshop to other caregivers (True or False).
