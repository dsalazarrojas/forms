# Babysitter Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Babysitter Information Form is a tool used by parents or guardians to gather essential information about potential caregivers for their children. The form helps to ensure that the caregiver has the necessary qualifications, experience, and references to provide quality care for the children.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the first section, including your full name, date of birth, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your home address and years of babysitting experience.
3. Select all the age groups you are able to care for.
4. If applicable, select any certifications or special skills you have.
5. Provide information for two references, including their names and phone numbers.
6. Indicate whether you have completed a background check.
7. Specify your available days and times.
8. Enter your hourly rate.
9. Confirm whether you can provide your own transportation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `phone`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Home Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your full street address.
* **Years of Babysitting Experience** (`years_experience`, `integer`, required): Enter the number of years you have been babysitting.
* **Age Groups You Can Care For** (`age_groups`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the age groups you are able to care for (Infants (0-12 months), Toddlers (1-3 years), Preschool (3-5 years), School Age (6-12 years), Teenagers (13+ years)).
* **Certifications** (`certifications`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you have any certifications (CPR, First Aid, etc.), select them here.
* **Special Skills or Abilities** (`special_skills`, `text`, optional): Enter any special skills or abilities you have (languages spoken, activities expertise, etc.).
* **First Reference Name** (`reference_one_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your first reference (previous parent or employer).
* **First Reference Phone Number** (`reference_one_phone`, `phone`, required): Enter the phone number of your first reference.
* **Second Reference Name** (`reference_two_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your second reference (previous parent or employer).
* **Second Reference Phone Number** (`reference_two_phone`, `phone`, optional): Enter the phone number of your second reference.
* **Background Check Completed** (`background_check`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you have completed a background check ("Yes", "No", "In Progress").
* **Available Days/Times** (`availability`, `text`, required): Specify your available days and times.
* **Hourly Rate** (`rate_per_hour`, `decimal`, required): Enter your hourly rate in dollars.
* **Can You Provide Your Own Transportation?** (`transportation`, `select_one`, required): Confirm whether you can provide your own transportation ("Yes", "No").
