# Babysitter And Nanny Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential babysitters and nannies for employment consideration. It helps families or caregivers to assess your qualifications, experience, and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full legal name as the applicant.
2. Enter your email address for the family or caregiver to contact you for updates.
3. Provide your phone number for best contact.
4. (Optional) Enter your address, city, and postal code for location purposes.
5. Enter the number of years you have experience in childcare.
6. Select any relevant certifications or training you have (e.g., CPR, first aid, early childhood education).
7. Choose your availability for work (e.g., weekdays, evenings, weekends, overnight).
8. Provide the names, phone numbers, and relationship of your professional references.
9. (Optional) Upload a clear photo and video of yourself as described in the instructions below.
10. Select whether you consent to a background check for employment consideration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant name** (1, text, required): Enter your full legal name as the applicant.
* **Email address** (2, email, required): Enter your email address for the family or caregiver to contact you for updates.
* **Phone number** (3, text, required): Enter your phone number for best contact.
* **Address** (4, text, optional): Enter your address, city, and postal code for location purposes.
* **Years of childcare experience** (5, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have experience in childcare.
* **Certifications and training** (6, select_multiple, optional): Select any relevant certifications or training you have (e.g., CPR, first aid, early childhood education).
* **Availability** (7, select_multiple, required): Choose your availability for work (e.g., weekdays, evenings, weekends, overnight).
* **Professional references** (8, text, required): Provide the names, phone numbers, and relationship of your professional references.
* **Photo and video upload instructions** (9, note, optional): Provide a clear photo and video as described in the instructions below. Please note that this is an optional field.
* **Consent for background check** (10, select_one, required): Select whether you consent to a background check for employment consideration.

**Additional Note for Photo and Video Upload**

Please upload a clear and recent photo and video of yourself. The photo should be a headshot with a neutral background. The video should be a brief introduction of yourself, stating your name, experience, and availability. 

This will help the family or caregiver to get a better sense of who you are and what you can bring to their family.
