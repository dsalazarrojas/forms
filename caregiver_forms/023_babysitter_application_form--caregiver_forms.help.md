# Babysitter Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Babysitter Application Form is a comprehensive document that helps us evaluate your suitability for a babysitting position. Please provide accurate and truthful information in each field to ensure a smooth and efficient application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Full name, Email address, and Phone number. These are essential contact details that will be used for communication and notifications.
2. Provide your Emergency contact name and phone number (if applicable). This is a good opportunity for us to reach you or your family in case of an emergency.
3. Write a brief Summary of your babysitting experience, including past families you've worked with and duration of service.
4. If you can start immediately, select the date you're available to begin work. If not, indicate the date you can start.
5. Enter your Expected hourly rate in local currency (e.g., $10).
6. Select Yes or No to Can provide references if you have them.
7. Add any Additional certifications you've obtained, such as CPR or first aid training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (Full name, text, required):
	+ This is your first and last name as you would like to be referred to.
* **Email address** (Email address, email, required):
	+ A valid email address to receive notifications and communications.
* **Phone number** (Phone number, text, required):
	+ A phone number where we can contact you or reach you in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency contact name and phone** (Emergency contact name and phone, text, optional):
	+ The name and phone number of a family member or contact person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Summary of babysitting experience** (Summary of babysitting experience, text, required):
	+ A brief description of your past babysitting experiences, including the names of families you've worked with and the duration of service.
* **Available start date** (Available start date, date, optional):
	+ The date you can begin work. If you are available to start immediately, select the current date.
* **Expected hourly rate** (Expected hourly rate, number, optional):
	+ The rate you expect to be paid per hour in local currency.
* **Can provide references** (Can provide references, select one, required):
	+ Select Yes if you have references available, No otherwise.
* **Additional certifications** (Additional certifications, text, optional):
	+ A list of any additional certifications you've obtained, such as CPR or first aid training.

## Tips

* Ensure accuracy and completeness in all fields to facilitate a smooth application process.
* Double-check your submission date to ensure it's the correct date of application.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for assistance.
