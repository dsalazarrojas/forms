# Caregiver Support Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain authorization from the caregiver to release the patient's medical records to a healthcare provider/entity.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the patient's information section with the patient's name, date of birth, and medical record number (if known).
2.  Fill out the caregiver's information section with your full name, phone number, and email address.
3.  Select the healthcare provider/entity that will be receiving the patient's records.
4.  Choose the types of records that you are requesting to be released.
5.  Select the date range for the records.
6.  If you are requesting a specific date range, enter the dates in the format `mm/dd/yyyy - mm/dd/yyyy`.
7.  Select the purpose of the release.
8.  Enter your name as a digital signature to confirm that you have the legal authority to sign.
9.  Enter the date as today's date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Full Name** (`Patient Full Name`, required, text)
    \[Please enter the patient's full name as it appears on their medical record.\]
*   **Patient Date of Birth** (`Patient Date of Birth`, required, date)
    \[Enter the patient's date of birth.\]
*   **Medical Record Number (if known)** (`Medical Record Number (if known)`, optional, text)
    \[If the patient has a medical record number, please enter it.\]
*   **Caregiver Full Name** (`Caregiver Full Name`, required, text)
    \[Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.\]
*   **Relationship to Patient** (`Relationship to Patient`, required, select_one)
    \[Select the relationship you have to the patient. Options are: Spouse, Child, Parent, Legal Guardian, Professional Caregiver, or Other.\]
*   **Caregiver Phone Number** (`Caregiver Phone Number`, required, text)
    \[Please enter your phone number.\]
*   **Caregiver Email Address** (`Caregiver Email Address`, optional, email)
    \[Please enter your email address.\]
*   **Healthcare Provider/Entity Receiving Records** (`Healthcare Provider/Entity Receiving Records`, required, text)
    \[Enter the name of the healthcare provider/entity that will be receiving the records.\]
*   **Types of Records to be Released** (`Types of Records to be Released`, required, select_multiple)
    \[Select the types of records that you are requesting to be released. Options are: Clinical Notes, Lab Results, Immunizations, Imaging Reports, Billing Records, and Full Medical History.\]
*   **Date Range for Records** (`Date Range for Records`, required, select_one)
    \[Select the date range for the records. Options are: Past 12 months, Past 3 years, Entire medical history, or Specific date range.\]
*   **If Specific Range, Please Specify** (`If Specific Range, Please Specify`, optional, text)
    \[If you selected 'Specific date range', enter the dates in the format `mm/dd/yyyy - mm/dd/yyyy`.\]
*   **Purpose of Information Release** (`Purpose of Information Release`, required, select_one)
    \[Select the reason for the request. Options are: Care Coordination, Insurance, Personal Use, Legal, or Continued Care.\]
*   **Authorization Expiration Date** (`Authorization Expiration Date`, optional, date)
    \[Enter the date when this authorization expires (default 1 year if empty).\]
*   **I certify that I have the legal authority to sign** (`I certify that I have the legal authority to sign`, required, select_one)
    \[Select 'True' to confirm that you have the legal authority to sign.\]
*   **Confirmation of Consent** (`Confirmation of Consent`, required, text)
    \[Please type your full name as a digital signature.\]
*   **Today's Date** (`Today's Date`, required, date)
    \[Enter the current date.\]

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you have the legal authority to sign this form before submitting it.
*   Please select the correct date range for the records that you are requesting.
*   Please ensure that the healthcare provider/entity receiving the records is correct.
*   Please select the correct types of records to be released.
*   Please type your full name as a digital signature.
*   Please enter the correct date as today's date.
