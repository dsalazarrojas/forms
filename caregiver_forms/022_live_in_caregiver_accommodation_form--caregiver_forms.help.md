# live_in_caregiver_accommodation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "live_in_caregiver_accommodation_form" is a comprehensive questionnaire designed to gather information about a family's accommodation needs, ensuring that live-in caregivers have a clear understanding of the family's requirements and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Family Details" page, providing information about the family you will be caring for.
2. Move on to the "Caregiver Details" page, providing your personal information.
3. Continue to the "Contact Information" page, sharing your contact details.
4. Select your availability on the "Availability" page.
5. Specify your accommodation requests on the "Accommodation Request" page.
6. Choose your required caregiver requirements on the "Caregiver Requirements" page.
7. Select your preferred contact method on the "Contact Method" page.
8. Choose your neighborhood location on the "Neighborhood" page.
9. Select your shift preferences on the "Caregiver Shift" page.
10. Indicate your family's language on the "Family Language" page.
11. Specify the family size on the "Family Size" page.
12. Choose the type of accommodation on the "Accommodation Type" page.
13. Select the duration of your accommodation on the "Accommodation Duration" page.
14. Choose the frequency of your accommodation on the "Accommodation Frequency" page.
15. Select the neighborhood for your accommodation on the "Accommodation Neighborhood" page.
16. Specify the start and end dates of your accommodation on the "Accommodation Start Date" and "Accommodation End Date" pages.
17. Select the start and end times of your accommodation on the "Accommodation Start Time" and "Accommodation End Time" pages.
18. Choose the start and end days of your accommodation on the "Accommodation Start Day" and "Accommodation End Day" pages.
19. Specify the start and end times of day on the "Accommodation Start Time of Day" and "Accommodation End Time of Day" pages.
20. Provide any additional information on the "Additional Information" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Family Details (1)**: This page asks for information about the family you will be caring for, such as their name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Caregiver Details (2)**: This page is for you to provide your personal information, including your name, contact number, and other relevant details.
* **Contact Information (3)**: Share your contact information so we can reach out to you for any questions or concerns.
* **Availability (4)**: Select your available days and times for the accommodation.
* **Accommodation Request (5)**: Specify what type of accommodation you are requesting (e.g., shared bathroom, private room).
* **Caregiver Requirements (6)**: Choose what you need from the caregiver, such as experience or certification.
* **Contact Method (7)**: Select how you would prefer to be contacted (e.g., email, phone).
* **Neighborhood (8)**: Choose the neighborhood for your accommodation.
* **Caregiver Shift (9)**: Select your preferred shift (e.g., morning, afternoon).
* **Family Language (10)**: Indicate the languages spoken by the family.
* **Family Size (11)**: Specify the number of family members you will be caring for.
* **Accommodation Type (12)**: Choose the type of accommodation you need (e.g., short-term, long-term).
* **Accommodation Duration (13)**: Select the duration of your accommodation.
* **Accommodation Frequency (14)**: Choose the frequency of your accommodation.
* **Accommodation Neighborhood (15)**: Select the neighborhood for your accommodation.
* **Accommodation Start Date (16)**: Specify the start date of your accommodation.
* **Accommodation End Date (17)**: Specify the end date of your accommodation.
* **Accommodation Start Time (18)**: Select the start time of your accommodation.
* **Accommodation End Time (19)**: Select the end time of your accommodation.
* **Accommodation Start Day (20)**: Choose the start day of your accommodation.
* **Accommodation End Day (21)**: Choose the end day of your accommodation.
* **Accommodation Start Time of Day (22)**: Select the start time of day for your accommodation.
* **Accommodation End Time of Day (23)**: Select the end time of day for your accommodation.
* **Accommodation Neighborhood (24)**: Provide any additional neighborhood information.
* **Additional Information (25)**: Share any additional information about your accommodation needs or requests.
