# Caregiver Training Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to assess the knowledge and best practices of caregivers in various aspects of their job. It is intended for training and evaluation purposes, to help caregivers improve their skills and identify areas that require additional education. The form consists of 7 questions that cover key areas such as infection control, medication storage, effective communication, emergency response, and self-care. By completing this form, caregivers can gain a better understanding of their strengths and weaknesses and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and choose the best answer based on your experience and knowledge.
2. Enter your name and email address (if applicable) in the respective fields.
3. Select the best answer for each of the 7 questions.
4. Provide any additional feedback you may have on the training or quiz.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address, if you would like to receive the results of this quiz.
* **Job role**: Describe your job role or position as a caregiver.
* **Q1: Infection control best practice**: Choose the best answer from the list:
	+ Hand hygiene before contact
	+ Wear gloves at all times
	+ Disinfect only when visible dirt
	+ Use mouthwash before contact
* **Q2: Correct medication storage**: Select one:
	+ Store at room temperature in original packaging
	+ Mix medications together
	+ Store in bathroom cabinet
	+ Keep in unlocked drawer
* **Q3: Effective communication technique**: Choose one:
	+ Active listening
	+ Interrupting to provide advice
	+ Speaking loudly only
	+ Ignoring questions
* **Q4: Immediate step in emergency**: Select one:
	+ Assess safety and call for help
	+ Wait for next scheduled check
	+ Move the person without assessment
	+ Give medication immediately
* **Q5: Recommended self-care practice**: Choose one:
	+ Regular breaks
	+ Skip meals to finish tasks
	+ Avoid asking for help
	+ Work longer shifts
* **Q5: Additional feedback on training**: Provide any comments or suggestions you may have on the training or quiz. 

## Tips

* Please answer each question based on your actual experience and knowledge.
* If you are unsure or have no experience in a particular area, choose the option that best reflects your best guess or what you would do if you were in that situation.
* Take your time to review each question carefully and choose the best answer.
* Remember that this quiz is for educational purposes only, and the goal is to help you improve your skills as a caregiver.
