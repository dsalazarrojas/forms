# Caregiver Wellbeing Index Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the wellbeing of caregivers, providing a comprehensive evaluation of their physical and emotional health, stress levels, and support systems. The survey aims to help caregivers identify areas of strength and weakness, and provide insights for support and self-care recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

*   Answer the questions as honestly as possible, based on your experiences and feelings.
*   Select the options that best describe your situation.
*   Take your time, and feel free to refer back to the questions if you need to.
*   You can skip any questions that do not apply to you or your situation.
*   You can save your progress and come back later if needed.
*   Once you've completed the form, submit your answers and review your results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Caregiver Name (`caregiver_name`, text, optional):**
    *   This question is for personal tracking and identification purposes.
* **Survey Date (`survey_date`, date, required):**
    *   This field helps us record the date of your survey response.
* **How long have you been a Caregiver (`caregiving_duration`, select_one, required):**
    *   Please select the duration of time you've been providing care.
* **Who do you provide care for (`care_recipient`, select_multiple, required):**
    *   Select all individuals you provide care for, such as family members or clients.
*   This field will help us tailor our support suggestions accordingly.
* **Average Hours of Care Per Week (`weekly_hours`, select_one, required):**
    *   Please select the number of hours you spend caring for others each week.
*   This field helps us understand your workload and stress levels.
* **Current Stress Level (`stress_level`, select_one, required):**
    *   Rate your stress levels over the past week, using the options provided.
*   This field helps us assess the impact of caregiving on your emotional wellbeing.
* **How would you rate your physical health (`physical_health`, select_one, required):**
    *   Rate your overall physical wellbeing using the provided options.
*   This field helps us understand the relationship between your physical and emotional health.
* **How would you rate your emotional health (`emotional_health`, select_one, required):**
    *   Rate your mental and emotional wellbeing using the options provided.
*   This field helps us understand your overall wellbeing levels.
* **Sleep Quality Assessment (`sleep_quality`, select_one, required):**
    *   Evaluate your sleep patterns over the past week, considering factors like duration and quality.
*   This field helps us understand how your sleep affects your overall wellbeing.
* **Do you have adequate support from Family or Friends (`support_system`, select_one, required):**
    *   Select the level of support you receive from family or friends.
*   This field helps us assess your social support system.
*   Please select the option that best fits your current situation.
* **Do you receive professional support (`professional_support`, select_one, required):**
    *   Select your current level of support from professional services.
*   This field helps us understand your access to specialized support.
*   Select the option that best describes your current support system.
* **How often do you practice self-care (`self_care_frequency`, select_one, required):**
    *   Select how frequently you engage in activities for your wellbeing, like exercise, hobbies, or relaxation.
*   This field helps us understand your self-care habits.
*   Select the option that best fits your current practice.
* **Have you experienced Burnout Symptoms (`burnout_symptoms`, select_multiple, required):**
    *   Select all burnout symptoms you have experienced.
*   This field helps us understand potential burnout issues.
*   Please select all symptoms that apply to you.
*   Note: Burnout is a condition of emotional and physical exhaustion, and can affect wellbeing.
* **How satisfied are you with work-life balance (`work_life_balance`, select_one, required):**
    *   Select your satisfaction level with the balance between caregiving and personal life.
*   This field helps us understand the impact of caregiving on your personal life.
*   Please select the option that best fits your current feelings.
*   A balanced work-life is crucial for your overall wellbeing.
* **Do you experience Financial Strain (`financial_strain`, select_one, required):**
    *   Select the level of financial impact caregiving has on you.
*   This field helps us assess the financial stress levels.
*   Please select the option that best describes your situation.
*   Financial strain can affect overall wellbeing.
* **Do you use Respite Care Services (`respite_care`, select_one, required):**
    *   Select your current level of use for respite care services.
*   This field helps us understand your current support system.
*   Please select the option that best fits your current situation.
*   Respite care can help reduce burnout and improve wellbeing.
* **Overall Wellbeing Rating (`overall_wellbeing`, select_one, required):**
    *   Evaluate your general sense of wellbeing using the provided options.
*   This field helps us assess your overall health and happiness levels.
*   Please select the option that best fits your current feelings.
*   A good wellbeing is crucial for caregiving effectiveness.
* **What additional support would you like (`additional_support_needed`, select_multiple, optional):**
    *   Select all the support options that you feel would help you most.
*   This field helps us understand what support you need.
*   Please select all options that apply to you.
*   You can skip this section if you feel you don't need additional support.
*   Note: You can also add any comments or concerns you'd like to share.
*   This field is for personal feedback and suggestions.
*   We value your input and appreciate your honesty.

## Tips

*   Be honest and accurate with your answers, as they will help us provide tailored support.
*   Take breaks if you need to, and come back when you feel refreshed.
*   Remember, this survey is designed to help you identify areas for improvement and provide support for your specific situation.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.

This form is for your wellbeing, and your feedback is invaluable. Thank you for taking the time to complete it!
