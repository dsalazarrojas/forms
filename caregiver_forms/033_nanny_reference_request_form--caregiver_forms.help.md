# Nanny Reference Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a nanny's employment history and experience. Please provide detailed and accurate information about the nanny's job title, pay frequency, job duration, and any other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Employer Info**: Please fill out this field with your name and contact information so that the potential nanny can contact you with any questions or concerns.
2. **Request Date**: Enter the date you are requesting references for the nanny.
3. **Request Time**: Enter the time you are requesting references for the nanny.
4. **Nanny Name**: Enter the name of the nanny you are requesting references for.
5. **Contact Info**: Enter the nanny's contact information so that the potential nanny can contact them.
6. **Nanny Phone**: Enter the nanny's phone number.
7. **Employer Phone**: Enter your phone number so that the potential nanny can contact you.
8. **Reference Info**: Select the relevant information about your reference experience with the nanny (e.g. "None", "Other").
9. **Job Type**: Select the type of job the nanny was working (e.g. Full Time, Part Time, Temporary).
10. **Number of Children**: Enter the number of children in the household.
11. **Age Range**: Select the age range of the children the nanny cared for.
12. **Job Start Date**: Enter the start date of the nanny's employment.
13. **Job Duration**: Enter the length of time the nanny worked for you.
14. **Job End Date**: Enter the end date of the nanny's employment, if applicable.
15. **Pay Info**: Enter the nanny's pay rate.
16. **Pay Frequency**: Select the pay frequency (e.g. Hourly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, Weekly).
17. **Job Status**: Select the nanny's job status (e.g. Available, Not Available).
18. **Reference Status**: Select the status of the reference (e.g. Pending, Completed).
19. **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the nanny's work experience.
20. **Reference Date**: Enter the date of the reference.
21. **Reference Time**: Enter the time of the reference.
22. **Reference Location**: Enter the location where the reference was conducted.
23. **Reference Type**: Select the type of reference (e.g. In-person, Phone, Video).
24. **Job Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the nanny's work experience.
25. **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the nanny.
26. **Employer Signature**: Sign and date this form to confirm the information provided.
27. **Nanny Signature**: Sign this form to confirm the information provided.
28. **Date**: Enter the current date.
29. **Time**: Enter the time the form is being completed.
30. **Reference Signature**: Sign this form to confirm the reference.
