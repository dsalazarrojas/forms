# senior_mobility_aid_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information for a senior mobility aid referral, which is a process for referring patients to services that provide assistance with mobility-related tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary information and permissions to proceed with the referral.
2. Fill out each section of the form with the relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Senior Mobility Aid Request** (`senior_mobility_aid_request`): Enter a brief description of the patient's senior mobility aid request.
* **Caregiver Name** (`caregiver_name`): Enter the name of the caregiver or healthcare professional referring the patient.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`): Enter the name of the patient.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`): Enter the contact information for the caregiver or healthcare professional (e.g., phone number, email).
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`): Enter the patient's address.
* **Referral Urgency** (`referral_urgency`): Select the level of urgency for the referral (High, Medium, Low).
* **Priority Date** (`priority_date`): Enter the date by which the referral should be prioritized.
* **Follow-up Required** (`follow_up_required`): Check the box if follow-up is required.
* **Follow-up Details** (`follow_up_details`): Enter the details of the follow-up schedule.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`): Enter the next steps for the referral.
* **Follow-up Schedule** (`follow_up_schedule`): Enter the schedule for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`): Enter the time for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`): Enter the date for follow-up.

## Tips

* Ensure you have the necessary information and permissions to complete this form.
* Enter dates and times in the correct format (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD for dates, HH:MM for times).
* Use the correct format for phone numbers and email addresses.
* Be as detailed as possible in the Follow-up Details and Next Steps sections to facilitate efficient communication and next steps.
