# Child Care Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a child for care services. It collects information about the child, parent or guardian, and the care requirements to provide accurate and effective care.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form carefully, one field at a time.
- Make sure to provide accurate and consistent information for each field.
- Use the hints and explanations provided for each field to ensure understanding.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to this help guide or ask for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Registration Date (registration_date)**: Date of registration submission (required)
  - Please enter the date on which this registration is submitted.
  - Format is YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Child First Name (child_first_name)**: Legal first name of the child (required)
  - Enter the child's first name (e.g., John).
  - Use the first name as it appears on official documents.
- **Child Last Name (child_last_name)**: Legal last name of the child (required)
  - Enter the child's last name (e.g., Smith).
  - Use the last name as it appears on official documents.
- **Child Date of Birth (child_date_of_birth)**: Please provide the child's birth date (required)
  - Format is YYYY-MM-DD.
  - Enter the birth date as it appears on official documents.
- **Child Gender (child_gender)**: Select the child's gender (required)
  - Choose from the options: Male, Female, Non-binary, Prefer not to say, Other.
- **Parent or Guardian First Name (parent_guardian_first_name)**: Legal first name of parent or guardian (required)
  - Enter the parent or guardian's first name (e.g., John).
  - Use the first name as it appears on official documents.
- **Parent or Guardian Last Name (parent_guardian_last_name)**: Legal last name of parent or guardian (required)
  - Enter the parent or guardian's last name (e.g., Smith).
  - Use the last name as it appears on official documents.
- **Parent or Guardian Email (parent_guardian_email)**: Primary email address for contact (required)
  - Enter a valid email address.
  - This will be the primary contact email for communication.
- **Parent or Guardian Phone (parent_guardian_phone)**: Primary phone number for contact (required)
  - Enter a valid phone number.
  - This will be the primary contact number for communication.
- **Home Address (home_address)**: Full street address (required)
  - Enter the full address where the child resides.
  - Use the address format: Street Number, Street Name, City, State, Zip Code.
- **City (city)**: City of residence (required)
  - Enter the city where the child resides.
  - Use the full name of the city (e.g., New York).
- **State (state)**: State or province (required)
  - Enter the state or province where the child resides.
  - Use the full name of the state or province (e.g., California).
- **Zip Code (zip_code)**: Postal or zip code (required)
  - Enter the zip code of the child's residence.
  - Format is 5-digit (e.g., 10021).
- **Emergency Contact Name (emergency_contact_name)**: Full name of emergency contact person (required)
  - Enter the full name of the emergency contact person.
  - Use the name as it appears on official documents.
- **Emergency Contact Phone (emergency_contact_phone)**: Phone number for emergency contact (required)
  - Enter a valid phone number.
  - This will be the emergency contact number.
- **Preferred Care Schedule (preferred_care_schedule)**: Select your preferred care schedule (required)
  - Choose from the options: Full-time (Monday to Friday), Part-time (3 days per week), Part-time (2 days per week), Drop-in as needed, Other.
- **Medical Conditions or Allergies (medical_conditions)**: List any medical conditions or allergies the child has (optional)
  - If the child has any medical conditions or allergies, enter them here.
  - Provide a clear and concise description.
- **Special Needs or Accommodations (special_needs)**: Describe any special needs or required accommodations (optional)
  - If the child has any special needs or required accommodations, describe them here.
  - Provide a clear and concise description.
- **Authorized Pickup Persons (authorized_pickup_persons)**: List names of people authorized to pick up the child (required)
  - Enter the names of people authorized to pick up the child.
  - Separate names with commas (e.g., John Smith, Jane Doe).
- **How Did You Hear About Us (how_did_you_hear_about_us)**: How did you learn about our child care facility (optional)
  - Choose from the options: Friend or family referral, Online search, Social media, Community event, Previous client, Other.
  - Select the most accurate option.
