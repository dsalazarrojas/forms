# Before And After School Care Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a child for before and after school care services with the school. It captures information about the child's details, the child's care needs, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the date you would like to start the child's care.
2.  Enter the child's full name and date of birth.
3.  Choose the child's grade level in school.
4.  Enter the name of the child's current school.
5.  Provide information about the child's primary caregiver, including their name and relationship to the child.
6.  Enter the primary phone number and email address for the caregiver.
7.  Enter the home address for the child.
8.  If the child has an emergency contact, provide their name, phone number, and relationship to the child.
9.  Indicate if you are enrolling the child in before or after school care.
10.  Choose the number of days you would like to enroll the child in care per week.
11.  Enter the start date of the care program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Registration Date**: The date you would like to start the child's care. (Date, Required)
*   **Child Information --**: This section captures information about the child. (Note, Required)
*   **Child Full Name**: The full name of the child. (Text, Required)
*   **Date of Birth**: The date of birth of the child. (Date, Required)
*   **Grade Level**: The grade level of the child in school. (Select One, Required)
*   **Current School Name**: The name of the child's current school. (Text, Required)
*   **Primary Caregiver Information --**: This section is for the primary caregiver. (Note, Required)
*   **Caregiver Full Name**: The full name of the child's primary caregiver. (Text, Required)
*   **Relationship to Child**: The relationship between the primary caregiver and the child. (Select One, Required)
*   **Primary Phone Number**: The primary phone number for the caregiver. (Text, Required)
*   **Email Address**: The email address of the primary caregiver. (Email, Required)
*   **Home Address**: The home address of the child. (Text, Required)
*   **Emergency Contact Information --**: This section is for emergency contact information. (Note, Required)
*   **Emergency Contact Name**: The name of the emergency contact person. (Text, Required)
*   **Emergency Contact Phone**: The phone number of the emergency contact person. (Text, Required)
*   **Relationship to Child**: The relationship between the emergency contact and the child. (Text, Required)
*   **Program Enrollment --**: This section is for enrolling in the care program. (Note, Required)
*   **Enrolling in Before School Care**: Check if you are enrolling the child in before school care. (Select One, Required)
*   **Enrolling in After School Care**: Check if you are enrolling the child in after school care. (Select One, Required)
*   **Program Start Date**: The date to start the child's care. (Date, Required)
*   **Days Per Week Needed**: The number of days per week you would like to enroll the child in care. (Select One, Required)
