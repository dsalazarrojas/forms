# Doula Service Timesheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Doula Service Timesheet form is a comprehensive record-keeping tool for tracking and documenting service visits between a doula and a client. It ensures accurate and detailed documentation of service hours, visit details, and client contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To access the form, log in and navigate to the "Service Timesheet" page or use the provided link to access the form directly. 
2.  Click on the "New" button to start a new form entry.
3.  Fill out the form fields as instructed below, making sure to enter accurate and relevant information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Service Hours** (`id: 1`, `type: number`): Enter the total number of hours spent on the client visit, up to two decimal places. This is a required field for billing and tracking purposes.
*   **Visit Details** (`id: 2`, `type: text`): Enter a brief description or notes about the visit, including any important details about the client's needs or concerns. This field is not required but recommended for documentation.
*   **Client Sessions** (`id: 3`, `type: select_multiple`): Select whether this visit was a client session or not, which is a required field for billing and tracking.
*   **Client Email** (`id: 5`, `type: email`): Enter the client's email address. This is a required field for contact and follow-up purposes.
*   **Agency Name** (`id: 7`, `type: text`): Enter the name of the agency or organization providing doula services. This is a required field for billing and tracking.
*   **Doula Name** (`id: 8`, `type: text`): Enter the name of the doula providing services. This is a required field for identification and billing purposes.
*   **Date** (`id: 9`, `type: date`): Enter the date of the client visit. This is a required field for record-keeping and billing.
*   **Time** (`id: 10`, `type: time`): Enter the time of the client visit. This is a required field for record-keeping and billing.
*   **Agency Phone** (`id: 11`, `type: text`): Enter the phone number of the agency or organization providing doula services. This field is optional for contact purposes.
*   **Address** (`id: 12`, `type: text`): Enter the agency's or organization's address. This field is optional for contact purposes.
*   **Agency Email** (`id: 13`, `type: email`): Enter the email address of the agency or organization providing doula services. This field is optional for contact purposes.
*   **Agency Address** (`id: 14`, `type: text`): Enter the address of the agency or organization providing doula services. This field is optional for contact purposes.
*   **Visit Duration** (`id: 15`, `type: number`): Enter the duration of the client visit in minutes. This field is optional but recommended for billing and tracking.
*   **Visit Frequency** (`id: 16`, `type: number`): Enter the frequency of client visits. This field is optional but recommended for billing and tracking.
*   **Doula Notes** (`id: 17`, `type: note`): Enter any important notes or comments from the doula about the visit. This field is optional but recommended for future reference.
*   **Agency Notes** (`id: 18`, `type: note`): Enter any notes or comments from the agency about the visit. This field is optional but recommended for future reference.
*   **Follow-up Date** (`id: 19`, `type: date`): Enter the date for the next follow-up visit. This field is optional but recommended for scheduling purposes.
*   **Follow-up Time** (`id: 20`, `type: time`): Enter the time for the next follow-up visit. This field is optional but recommended for scheduling purposes.
*   **Agency Hours** (`id: 21`, `type: number`): Enter the total hours spent by the agency on the client visit. This field is optional but recommended for billing and tracking.
*   **Doula Hours** (`id: 22`, `type: number`): Enter the total hours spent by the doula on the client visit. This field is optional but recommended for billing and tracking.
*   **Total Hours** (`id: 23`, `type: number`): Enter the total hours spent on the client visit, calculated from Doula Hours and Agency Hours. This field is automatically populated and not editable.
*   **Total Visits** (`id: 24`, `type: number`): Enter the total number of client visits. This field is automatically populated and not editable.
*   **Submitted By** (`id: 25`, `type: text`): Enter the name of the person submitting the form. This field is optional but recommended for record-keeping purposes.

## Tips

*   Make sure to enter accurate and detailed information for each field to ensure proper billing and tracking.
*   Use the "Submit" button to save your form entry once you have completed all fields.
*   Review and edit your form entry as needed before submitting it for finalization.
*   Use the form to track and document client visits, ensuring accurate and detailed records for future reference.
