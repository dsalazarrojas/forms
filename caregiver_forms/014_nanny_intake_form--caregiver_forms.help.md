# Nanny Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals who are applying to be nannies or caregivers for children.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your First Name and Last Name in the first two fields.
2. Enter your Email address for contact purposes.
3. Provide your Phone number so we can reach you for any further information.
4. Enter your Address for location purposes.
5. Enter your Date of Birth for age verification.
6. Select your Social Security Number from the dropdown menu.
7. Enter the number of years of experience you have as a nanny or caregiver.
8. Choose your Availability (Full-time, Part-time, or Freelance) from the dropdown menu.
9. Select your Experience Level (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced) from the dropdown menu.
10. Choose your Education Level (High School, College, or University) from the dropdown menu.
11. Select the languages you speak from the dropdown menu.
12. If you have any Certifications, please select Yes or No from the dropdown menu.
13. If you want to disclose your Salary Range, enter the number.
14. Select the Availability Date from the calendar.
15. If you have been previously employed through an agency, select Yes or No from the dropdown menu.
16. If you have an Emergency Contact, please enter their name and relationship.
17. Enter the Emergency Contact's Phone number.
18. Enter the Emergency Contact's Email address.
19. Enter the Emergency Contact's Address.
20. If you want to add any notes about your Emergency Contact, please use the note field.
21. If you have been previously employed through an agency, select Yes or No for Agency 1.
22. If you have multiple agencies you have been employed through, select Yes or No for Agency 2.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you for any further information.
* **Address**: Enter your address for location purposes.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth for age verification.
* **Social Security Number**: Select your Social Security Number from the dropdown menu.
* **Years of Experience**: Enter the number of years of experience you have as a nanny or caregiver.
* **Availability**: Choose your availability (Full-time, Part-time, or Freelance) from the dropdown menu.
* **Experience Level**: Select your experience level (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced) from the dropdown menu.
* **Education Level**: Choose your education level (High School, College, or University) from the dropdown menu.
* **Languages Spoken**: Select the languages you speak from the dropdown menu.
* **Certifications**: If you have any certifications, select Yes or No from the dropdown menu.
* **Salary Range**: If you want to disclose your salary range, enter the number. (Optional)
* **Availability Date**: Select the date you are available to start work from the calendar.
* **Employment Agency**: If you have been previously employed through an agency, select Yes or No from the dropdown menu.
* **References**: If you have any references, please enter their name and relationship.
* **Emergency Contact**: If you have an emergency contact, enter their name and relationship.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the emergency contact's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Enter the emergency contact's address.
* **Emergency Contact Notes**: If you want to add any notes about your emergency contact, please use the note field.
* **Employment Agency 1**: If you have been previously employed through an agency, select Yes or No from the dropdown menu.
* **Employment Agency 2**: If you have multiple agencies you have been employed through, select Yes or No from the dropdown menu.
