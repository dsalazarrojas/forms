# Nanny Emergency Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nanny Emergency Contact Form is used to collect critical contact information from family members or caregivers for an individual being cared for. This information will be used to ensure that the care team is able to respond quickly and effectively in the event of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information for the individual being cared for.
2. Make sure to accurately enter the family member's phone number, home and work address, and email.
3. List any medical conditions or treatments that the individual is currently receiving.
4. Specify the relationship between the individual and the emergency contact.
5. Confirm if an authorized emergency contact exists, and if so, provide additional details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Family Member**: Enter the name of the family member or caregiver filling out the form.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the family member or caregiver. This number will be used for emergency contact.
* **Relationship**: Describe the relationship between the individual being cared for and the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship (Emergency Contact)**: Describe the relationship between the individual being cared for and the emergency contact.
* **Home Address**: Enter the home address of the individual being cared for.
* **Work Address**: Enter the work address of the individual being cared for.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the individual being cared for.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions or health concerns that the individual has.
* **Medical Treatment**: List any medical treatments or therapies that the individual is currently receiving.
* **Authorized Emergency Contact**: Specify if the emergency contact is authorized to make decisions on the individual's behalf. Options:
	+ Authorized: Confirms that the emergency contact is authorized.
	+ Not Authorized: Confirms that the emergency contact is not authorized.
	+ Only in case of Emergency: Confirms that the emergency contact is not authorized, but can be contacted in emergency situations.
	+ Other (explain in the comments): Select this option if the emergency contact relationship is different from the above options.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information that are relevant to the individual's care.

Note: Please make sure to review and confirm the accuracy of all information before submitting the form.
