This form is designed to collect information from caregivers about their availability to work as nannies. The form includes fields for essential details such as first and last name, email, start and end dates, time slots, and specific availability. Caregivers need to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth and efficient matching process.

The form consists of 15 fields, each requiring different types of information. Please follow the instructions below for each field to complete the form correctly.

### Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name**: Enter your first name.
* **last_name**: Enter your last name.
* **email**: Enter your email address.
* **start_date**: Select the date you are available to start work.
* **end_date**: Select the date you are available to work until.
* **days_available**: Specify the days you are available to work. (e.g., Monday to Friday)
* **start_time**: Enter the time you are available to start work.
* **end_time**: Enter the time you are available to end work.
* **availability**: Note any specific availability you have.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **notes**: Provide any additional comments or information.
* **availability_2** to **availability_15**: Repeat the process for each subsequent availability field.

### Tips
* Ensure you fill out all fields accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth and efficient matching process.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the instructions above or contact us for assistance.
