# Child Care Withdrawal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Child Care Withdrawal Form is used to notify us of a child's withdrawal from our care service. This form should be completed by the caregiver to provide us with necessary information about the child's last day of attendance and reason for withdrawal.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on the form link provided to access the form.
2.  Scroll down to the first section, "Student Information".
3.  Enter the child's full name as it was registered with us.
4.  Continue to the second section, "Withdrawal Details".
5.  Enter the last day the child attended our care service.
6.  Select the primary reason for the child's withdrawal from the provided options (Moving out of area, Financial reasons, Dissatisfaction with service, Transition to school, or Other).
7.  In the "Feedback" section, provide any comments or suggestions about our service.
8.  In the "Would you recommend us?" section, select "True" if you would recommend our service to others, or "False" otherwise.
9.  Finally, confirm the current date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Child Full Name (child_name, text, required)**: Enter the child's full name as it was registered with us.
*  **Last Day of Attendance (last_day, date, required)**: Enter the last day the child attended our care service.
*  **Primary Reason for Withdrawal (reason_for_withdrawal, select_one, required)**: Select the primary reason for the child's withdrawal from the provided options.
*  **Feedback (improvement_feedback, text, optional)**: Provide any comments or suggestions about our service.
*  **Would you recommend us? (would_recommend, select_one, required)**: Select "True" if you would recommend our service to others, or "False" otherwise.
*  **Today Date (submission_date, date, required)**: Confirm the current date.

## Tips

*  Make sure to submit the form with accurate information about the child's withdrawal.
*  If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
*  Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your input.
