# Home Care Caregiver Availability Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Care Caregiver Availability Form is designed to gather information about a caregiver's schedule, qualifications, and other relevant details to match them with clients who need home care services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the category that best describes the type of care the caregiver provides. This will help match them with clients who need specific types of care.
2. Enter the number of hours you are available to work per week.
3. Fill in the start date and end date of your availability period.
4. Provide the end time of your availability period.
5. Enter any additional availability notes or details.
6. Provide your address.
7. Enter your phone number.
8. Enter your email address.
9. Enter any additional availability notes or details.
10. If you have an assigned tool or equipment, provide its name and ID.
11. If you have an assigned user ID and name, provide them.
12. Enter the category (not to be confused with the category in step 1).
13. Enter a brief description of your qualifications and experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`care_type`, select_one): Select the type of care you provide. Choose from the options: Elderly, Bedridden, Unable to care for themselves.
* **Hours Available** (`hours_available`, text): Enter the number of hours you are available to work per week.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date): Fill in the start date of your availability period.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date): Fill in the end date of your availability period.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time): Provide the end time of your availability period.
* **Availability** (`availability`, text): Enter any additional availability notes or details.
* **Address** (`address`, text): Enter your home address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address.
* **Availability Note** (`availability_note`, text): Enter any additional availability notes or details.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text): If you have an assigned tool or equipment, provide its name and ID.
* **Assigned Tool Name** (`assigned_tool_name`, text): Enter the name of the assigned tool or equipment.
* **Form IDs** (`form_ids`, number): Enter any form IDs or numbers associated with your assigned tool or equipment.
* **Assigned Tool User ID** (`assigned_tool_user_id`, number): Enter the ID of the assigned user.
* **Assigned Tool User** (`assigned_tool_user`, text): Enter the name of the assigned user.
* **Category** (`category`, text): Enter the category or type of care you provide (not to be confused with the category in step 1).
* **Description** (`description`, text): Enter a brief description of your qualifications and experience.
