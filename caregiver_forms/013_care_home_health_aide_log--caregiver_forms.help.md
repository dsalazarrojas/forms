# Care Home Health Aide Log - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to capture information on the activities and events that occurred during a caregiver's shift in a care home setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Complete each section of the form, including:
	*   **Health Aide Name**: Enter the name of the health aide who completed the form.
	*   **Aide ID Number**: Enter the ID number of the health aide.
	*   **Shift Date**: Enter the date of the shift.
	*   **Shift Start Time**: Enter the time the shift started.
	*   **Shift End Time**: Enter the time the shift ended.
	*   **Resident Name**: Enter the name of the resident.
	*   **Resident Room Number**: Enter the room number of the resident.
2.  Complete the vital sign fields:
	*   **Temperature**: Enter the resident's temperature in Fahrenheit.
	*   **Pulse**: Enter the resident's pulse rate.
	*   **Respirations**: Enter the resident's respirations rate.
	*   **Blood Pressure**: Enter the resident's blood pressure in the format systolic over diastolic (e.g., 120/80).
	*   **Oxygen Saturation**: Enter the resident's oxygen saturation percentage.
	*   **Weight**: Enter the resident's weight (if measured).
3.  Complete the activities of daily living fields:
	*   **Activities of Daily Living Assistance**: Select all activities in which the resident needed assistance (e.g., bathing, dressing, grooming).
	*   **Mobility Status**: Select the resident's mobility status during the shift (e.g., independent, supervision needed, minimal assistance).
4.  Complete the food and fluid intake fields:
	*   **Meals Consumed**: Select all meals the resident consumed (e.g., breakfast, full portion; breakfast, partial portion; etc.).
	*   **Fluid Intake**: Enter the approximate amount of fluid intake in ounces.
5.  Complete the bathroom fields:
	*   **Bowel Movements**: Select the number of bowel movements the resident had (e.g., one, two, three or more).
	*   **Urinary Output**: Select the type of urinary output the resident had (e.g., normal, decreased, increased, etc.).
6.  Complete the skin condition field:
	*   **Skin Condition Observed**: Select all skin conditions observed (e.g., normal, redness, bruising, dry skin, open areas, etc.).
7.  Complete the medication fields:
	*   **Medications Administered**: List any medications administered during the shift.
8.  Complete the treatments fields:
	*   **Treatments Provided**: Select all treatments provided (e.g., wound care, range of motion exercises, etc.).
9.  Complete the behavior observations fields:
	*   **Behavior Observations**: Select all behavior observations (e.g., normal mood, anxious, depressed, etc.).
10. Complete the social interactions field:
	*   **Social Interactions**: Select the level of social interaction (e.g., very social and engaged, moderately social, minimal interaction, etc.).
11. Complete the incidents or notable changes fields:
	*   **Incidents or Notable Changes**: Describe any incidents or notable changes during the shift.
12. Complete the family or visitor contact field:
	*   **Family or Visitor Contact**: Select the level of family or visitor contact (e.g., yes - family visited, yes - phone call, etc.).
13. Complete the activities participated in fields:
	*   **Activities Participated In**: Select all activities the resident participated in (e.g., group activities, individual activities, etc.).
14. Complete the sleep patterns field:
	*   **Sleep Patterns**: Describe the resident's sleep patterns observed during the shift.
15. Complete the supplies needed field:
	*   **Supplies Needed for Next Shift**: List any supplies needed for the next shift.
16. Complete the handoff notes field:
	*   **Handoff Notes for Next Shift**: Record any important information for the oncoming staff.
17. Complete the supervisor notification field:
	*   **Supervisor Notified of Any Concerns**: Select yes if the supervisor was notified of any concerns.
18. Complete the log completion fields:
	*   **Log Completed By**: Enter your name or signature.
	*   **Log Completion Time**: Enter the time you completed the log.
