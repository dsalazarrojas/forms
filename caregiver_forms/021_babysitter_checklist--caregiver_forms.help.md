# Babysitter Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather essential information from parents or guardians to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for their children while they are in the care of a babysitter. The form covers important details such as contact information, care schedule, children's details, and specific instructions for the babysitter to follow. This information will help the babysitter provide top-notch care and attention to the children while they are in their charge.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and carefully, providing accurate information for each field.
2. Make sure to include all necessary contact information, such as phone numbers and names.
3. Be specific about the care schedule, including start and end times, and any special instructions for the babysitter to follow.
4. List all children being cared for, including their names and ages.
5. Provide clear instructions for bedtime routines, meals, and any special requirements.
6. Share any relevant medical information, such as allergies or dietary restrictions.
7. Discuss any medications that may need to be administered.
8. Specify rules for TV and screen time, homework or tasks, and house rules.
9. Identify any off-limit areas or rooms that the children should not access.
10. If applicable, provide information on pet care and security system instructions.
11. Include the pediatrician's contact information and insurance details.
12. Set expectations for behavior and discipline.
13. Add any additional notes or information that will help the babysitter understand the needs and preferences of the children.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the parent or guardian leaving instructions for the babysitter.
* **Parent Phone Number** (`parent_phone`, `text`, required): Provide the primary contact number for emergency situations.
* **Alternate Emergency Contact** (`alternate_contact`, `text`, required): Enter the name and phone number of a backup contact in case the parent or guardian is unavailable.
* **Babysitter Name** (`babysitter_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the caregiver or babysitter.
* **Date of Care** (`care_date`, `date`, required): Specify the date when care is scheduled to begin.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, required): Enter the expected start time for care.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, required): Enter the expected end time for care.
* **Children's Names and Ages** (`children_names`, `text`, required): List all children being cared for, including their names and ages.
* **Bedtime Routine** (`bedtime_routine`, `text`, required): Describe the bedtime procedures, including any specific tasks or routines to follow.
* **Meal and Snack Instructions** (`meal_instructions`, `text`, required): Specify what and when to feed the children.
* **Allergies or Dietary Restrictions** (`allergies_restrictions`, `text`, required): Share any allergies or dietary restrictions that the children may have.
* **Medication Instructions** (`medication_instructions`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide instructions for any medications that need to be administered.
* **TV and Screen Time Rules** (`tv_screen_rules`, `text`, required): Specify the rules for TV and screen time, including any limitations or restrictions.
* **Homework or Chore Tasks** (`homework_tasks`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide instructions or tasks for the children to complete.
* **Important House Rules** (`house_rules`, `text`, required): List any house rules that the children must follow.
* **Off-Limit Areas** (`off_limit_areas`, `text`, optional): Identify any rooms or areas that the children should not access.
* **Pet Care Instructions** (`pet_instructions`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide instructions for pet care.
* **Security System Instructions** (`security_system`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide instructions on how to arm or disarm the security system.
* **Doctor and Insurance Information** (`doctor_info`, `text`, required): Provide the pediatrician's contact information and insurance details.
* **Expected Behavior Guidelines** (`expected_behavior`, `text`, required): Specify how to handle discipline issues or behavioral expectations.
* **Morning Routine if Applicable** (`morning_routine`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide instructions for morning care.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information that will help the babysitter understand the children's needs and preferences.

This guide is designed to ensure that the babysitter has all the necessary information to provide top-notch care for the children while their parents or guardians are away. By following these steps and providing clear and accurate information, both the parents and the babysitter can have a stress-free and enjoyable experience.
