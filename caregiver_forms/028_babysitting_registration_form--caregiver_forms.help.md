# Babysitting Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Babysitting Registration Form is designed to collect information from families who are requesting babysitting services from our organization. This form helps us understand the family's needs, provide a safe and comfortable environment for the children, and ensure that we can match the family with a suitable sitter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "Required") with the requested information.
2. Provide as much detail as possible in the optional fields, so we can better understand your needs.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Family name** (1, Family name, Required): Enter the surname of the family, e.g., Smith.
* **Primary contact name** (2, Contact name, Required): Enter the name of the primary contact person, e.g., John Smith.
* **Contact phone** (3, Contact phone, Required): Enter the best phone number to reach the primary contact person.
* **Children details** (4, Children details, Required): Describe the names, ages, and any special notes about each child, e.g., "John aged 5, Jane aged 3."
* **Babysitting schedule needed** (5, Babysitting schedule needed, Required): Specify the days and times when you need babysitting services.
* **Special needs or instructions** (6, Special needs or instructions, Optional): If the children have any special needs or allergies, please provide information about them.
* **Is this a recurring service request?** (7, Is this a recurring service request, Required): Indicate if this is a one-time or ongoing service request.
* **Preferred sitter gender** (8, Preferred sitter gender, Optional): Choose your preference for the sitter gender, if any.
* **Payment details** (9, Payment details, Optional): Provide information on how you would like to pay for the services and any specific billing instructions.
* **Registration date** (10, Registration date, Required): The date you submitted this form.
