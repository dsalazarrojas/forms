# Emotional Resilience In Caregiving Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form aims to understand the emotional resilience of caregivers, helping them identify areas where they may need support or resources to maintain their emotional well-being.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required information:
	* Caregiver Name: Enter your name as the caregiver.
	* Program Name: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are participating in a program.
	* Phone: Enter your contact phone number.
	* Caregiver Notes (optional): Add any additional comments about your caregiving situation.
2. Rate your emotional resilience:
	* Emotional Resilience Scale 1-5: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your level of emotional resilience for each question.
3. Submit your form:
	* Form Comments: Add any comments or questions you have about the form or your experience as a caregiver.
	* Submission Comments: Describe your experience with the program or the form.
	* Program Notes (optional): Add any additional comments or questions about the program.
