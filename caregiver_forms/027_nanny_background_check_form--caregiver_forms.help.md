# Nanny Background Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nanny Background Check Form is a crucial document used by childcare agencies and families to collect and verify essential information about potential nannies or caregivers. This form is essential in ensuring that nannies or caregivers are thoroughly screened and vetted before being considered for positions.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure accurate and complete data collection, please follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your **First name** (ID 1) and **Last name** (ID 2) in the respective fields.
2. Enter your **Email** (ID 3) in the format of `user@example.com` (e.g., `johndoe@example.com`).
3. Provide your **Address** (ID 4) in the format of `Street Address, City, State, ZIP`.
4. Enter the **City** (ID 5) and **State** (ID 6) of your address.
5. Fill in your **Phone** (ID 7) number.
6. Enter your **Date of Birth** (ID 8) in the format `MM/DD/YY`.
7. If applicable, select your **Previous Employment** (ID 10) in the **Company** or **Job** field.
8. For **Previous Employment** (ID 10), you may also provide **Dates of employment**.
9. Select the highest level of **Previous Education** (ID 11) you have achieved, from **Bachelor**, **Master**, or **Doctorate**.
10. For **Previous Experience** (ID 12), select any relevant experience from **Childcare provider**, **Nanny**, **Babysitter**, **Teacher**, or **Other**.
11. Enter the **Reason for Applying** (ID 13) in the text field.
12. In the **Emergency Contact** (ID 14) field, provide the name of the emergency contact.
13. Specify the **Relationship** (ID 15) of the emergency contact.
14. Fill in the **Emergency Contact Phone** (ID 16) number.
15. Enter the **Emergency Contact Email** (ID 17) in the format `user@example.com` (e.g., `johndoe@example.com`).
16. Provide the **Emergency Contact Address** (ID 18) in the format of `Street Address, City, State, ZIP`.
17. Select **Previous criminal history** (ID 19) as **True** or **False**.
18. Select **Previous felonies** (ID 20) as **True** or **False**.
19. Select **Have you been convicted of a misdemeanor?** (ID 21) as **True** or **False**.
20. Select **Have you been convicted of a felony?** (ID 22) as **True** or **False**.
21. Select **Have you been terminated from employment?** (ID 23) as **True** or **False**.
22. Select **Have you been disciplined by an employment agency?** (ID 24) as **True** or **False**.
23. Finally, fill in your **Certification** (ID 25) in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name (ID 1)**: The field to input your first name.
* **Last name (ID 2)**: The field to input your last name.
* **Email (ID 3)**: A field to input your email address, in the format `user@example.com` (e.g., `johndoe@example.com`).
* **Address (ID 4)**: A field to input your full address, in the format of `Street Address, City, State, ZIP`.
* **City (ID 5)**: The field to input the city of your address.
* **State (ID 6)**: The field to input the state of your address.
* **Phone (ID 7)**: A field to input your phone number.
* **Date of Birth (ID 8)**: A field to input your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YY`.
* **Social Security Number (ID 9)**: A field to input your Social Security Number.
* **Previous Employment (ID 10)**: A field to select your previous employment in **Company** or **Job**.
* **Dates of employment (ID 10)**: A field to input dates of employment if applicable.
* **Previous Education (ID 11)**: A field to select your highest level of education from **Bachelor**, **Master**, or **Doctorate**.
* **Previous Experience (ID 12)**: A field to select your relevant experience from **Childcare provider**, **Nanny**, **Babysitter**, **Teacher**, or **Other**.
* **Reason for Applying (ID 13)**: A field to input the reason you are applying.
* **Emergency Contact (ID 14)**: A field to input the name of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship (ID 15)**: The field to input the relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (ID 16)**: A field to input the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email (ID 17)**: A field to input the email address of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address (ID 18)**: A field to input the address of the emergency contact.
* **Previous criminal history (ID 19)**: A field to select whether you have a previous criminal history as **True** or **False**.
* **Previous felonies (ID 20)**: A field to select whether you have previous felonies as **True** or **False**.
* **Have you been convicted of a misdemeanor? (ID 21)**: A field to select whether you have been convicted of a misdemeanor as **True** or **False**.
* **Have you been convicted of a felony? (ID 22)**: A field to select whether you have been convicted of a felony as **True** or **False**.
* **Have you been terminated from employment? (ID 23)**: A field to select whether you have been terminated from employment as **True** or **False**.
* **Have you been disciplined by an employment agency? (ID 24)**: A field to select whether you have been disciplined by an employment agency as **True** or **False**.
* **Certification (ID 25)**: A field to input your certification.
