# Strategic Planning Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Strategic Planning Form is a comprehensive tool for teams and consultants to gather critical information about a company's strategic plans. It's designed to capture essential details to aid collaboration and planning.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your company's name in the **Company Name** field.
2. Describe your company's goals in the **Goals** field.
3. List your company's strategies to achieve those goals in the **Strategies** field.
4. Select a date for the start of your project's timeline from the calendar picker in the **Timeline** field.
5. Indicate if you have a team for the project by selecting **Yes** or **No** in the **Team Members** field.
6. If applicable, select the dates for your project meetings in the **Meetings** field.
7. If you want to allocate a budget for your project, enter a numerical value in the **Budget** field (this field is optional).
8. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
9. Provide your phone number in the **Phone** field.
10. (Optional) Add any additional notes about your project in the **Note** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`1`, `text`, `required`): Enter your company's name to help us identify and address your needs.
* **Goals** (`2`, `text`, `required`): Describe your company's objectives, such as increasing revenue, improving customer satisfaction, or expanding product offerings.
* **Strategies** (`3`, `text`, `required`): Outline your company's approaches to achieve its goals, like market research, product development, or marketing campaigns.
* **Timeline** (`4`, `date`, `required`): Select a date for the start of your project, which will help us plan and schedule our work.
* **Team Members** (`5`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Indicate if you have a team working on this project. Selecting **Yes** means you'll have dedicated team members; **No** means you'll be working independently.
* **Meetings** (`6`, `date`, `required`): If you have project meetings, choose the dates for them using the calendar picker.
* **Budget** (`7`, `number`, `optional`): If you want to allocate a budget for your project, enter a numerical value (e.g., $100, 50, 2000).
* **Email** (`8`, `email`, `required`): Enter your email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone** (`9`, `text`, `required`): Provide your phone number for quick calls or discussions.
* **Note** (`10`, `note`, `optional`): Add any additional notes or comments about your project.
