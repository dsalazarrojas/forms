# Leadership Readiness Consulting Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to request leadership readiness consulting services. It helps identify the specific needs and goals of your organization's leadership team, allowing them to receive personalized guidance and support. Before proceeding, ensure that you have not submitted a similar request in the past and that you are authorized to represent your organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide a clear and concise title for your request.
2. Enter your name and any other relevant contact information (company, department, and position).
3. Enter your email address and phone number for communication.
4. Indicate whether a leadership consultant will be working with you directly or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title (`form-title`, text, required: false)**: Please enter a brief description of your request, ideally one sentence that summarizes your leadership consulting needs.
* **Name (`name`, text, required: false)**: Please enter your full name as the authorized representative of your organization.
* **Company (`company`, text, required: false)**: Enter the name of your organization.
* **Department (`department`, text, required: false)**: Enter the department or team you belong to within your organization.
* **Position (`position`, text, required: false)**: Enter your position or title within your organization.
* **Email (`email`, email, required: false)**: Enter your work email address, which will be used for communication and follow-up.
* **Phone (`phone`, text, required: false)**: Enter your work phone number, which will be used for scheduling and follow-up calls.
* **Consultant (`consultant`, select_multiple, required: false)**: Choose whether you would like to work with a leadership consultant directly or not.
