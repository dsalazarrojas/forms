# Business Idea Consultation Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your business idea and provide you with tailored consultation services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question as accurately and thoroughly as possible. If you have any questions or need help, do not hesitate to ask. Here's a step-by-step guide:

1.  **Requestor Information**: Provide your first name, last name, and email address. This information is required.
2.  **Background**: Describe your current employment status and any relevant industry experience you have.
3.  **Business Idea**: Describe your business idea, including the problem it addresses, target market, and competitive advantage.
4.  **Idea Stage**: Choose the current stage of your idea, such as early concept, detailed plan, prototype, or testing phase.
5.  **Market Research**: Indicate the level of market research you have conducted.
6.  **Financial Projections**: If applicable, provide your financial projections, including basic estimates, detailed projections, or professional forecasts.
7.  **Consultation Focus Areas**: Select all areas you would like to receive consultation on, such as business viability assessment, market analysis, business planning, and financial projections.
8.  **Specific Questions**: Describe any specific questions or concerns you have, and we will address them accordingly.
9.  **Startup Funding**: Indicate the level of startup funding you have available.
10. **Launch Timeline**: Choose when you expect to launch your business, within 3 months, 3-6 months, 6-12 months, or more than 1 year.
11. **Commitment Level**: Indicate how serious you are about your business idea, ranging from exploring options to fully committed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for follow-up calls or further questions.
* **Current Employment Status** (`current_employment`, `select_one`, required): Choose your current employment status: employed full-time, employed part-time, self-employed, unemployed, or student.
* **Industry Experience** (`industry_experience`, `text`, optional): Describe any industry experience you have.
* **Previous Entrepreneurial Experience** (`entrepreneurial_experience`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you have previous entrepreneurial experience: no, some attempts, one successful venture, or multiple ventures.
* **Business Concept** (`business_concept`, `text`, required): Describe your business idea.
* **Problem You Are Solving** (`problem_statement`, `text`, required): Explain the problem your business idea addresses.
* **Target Market** (`target_market`, `text`, required): Describe your target market.
* **Competitive Advantage** (`competitive_advantage`, `text`, required): Explain what makes your business idea unique.
* **Idea Stage** (`idea_stage`, `select_one`, required): Choose the current stage of your business idea: early concept, detailed plan, prototype, or testing phase.
* **Market Research Conducted** (`market_research`, `select_one`, required): Indicate the level of market research you have conducted: no, some informal research, detailed research, or comprehensive research.
* **Financial Projections** (`financial_projections`, `select_one`, optional): Provide your financial projections, including basic estimates, detailed projections, or professional forecasts.
* **Consultation Focus Areas** (`consultation_focus`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all areas you would like to receive consultation on.
* **Specific Questions** (`specific_questions`, `text`, required): Describe any specific questions or concerns you have.
* **Startup Funding** (`startup_funding`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate the level of startup funding you have available: no funds yet, limited funds, adequate funds, or strong funds.
* **Launch Timeline** (`launch_timeline`, `select_one`, required): Choose when you expect to launch your business.
* **Commitment Level** (`commitment_level`, `select_one`, required): Indicate how serious you are about your business idea: exploring options, seriously considering, very committed, or fully committed.
