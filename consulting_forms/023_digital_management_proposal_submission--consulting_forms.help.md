# digital_management_proposal_submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The digital management proposal submission form is used to submit a new digital management proposal to internal stakeholders for review.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the required fields.
2. If you have a phone number, you can optionally provide it.
3. Upload a file related to your proposal.
4. Enter a title and date for your proposal.
5. Provide a detailed description of your proposal in the proposal details field.
6. Indicate if you have any proposal attachments.
7. Select a reviewer for your proposal.
8. Indicate if your proposal is approved or not with a review score.
9. Provide any review comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name** (required): Enter your name as it will appear on the proposal.
* **Email** (required): Enter your email address where you will be contacted for review or further information.
* **Phone** (optional): Enter your phone number (if available).
* **File Upload** (required): Upload a relevant file related to your proposal.
* **Proposal Title** (required): Enter a short title for your proposal.
* **Proposal Date** (required): Enter the date of your proposal.
* **Proposal Details** (optional): Provide a detailed description of your proposal.
* **Proposal Attachments** (optional): Indicate if you have any attachments related to your proposal.
* **Reviewer** (required): Select a person to review your proposal.
* **Review Score** (required): Indicate if your proposal is approved (Yes) or not (No).
* **Review Comments** (optional): Provide any comments for the reviewer.
