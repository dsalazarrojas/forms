# Strategic Advisory Services Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Strategic Advisory Services Contact Form is a tool used to collect information from customers or potential customers who are interested in our services. It helps us understand their needs and challenges, providing an opportunity for our team to offer personalized assistance and guidance. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Enter your First Name in the first field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the second field.
3. Enter your Company Name in the third field, if you have one.
4. Enter your valid Email Address in the fourth field.
5. Provide your Phone Number in the fifth field, if applicable.
6. Write a brief Message describing your challenge or question in the sixth field.
7. Optionally, select from the options in the "How did you hear about us?" field to indicate how you came to know about our services.
8. Evaluate the qualification of your lead by selecting one of the options in the "How qualified is this lead?" field.
9. Optionally, provide a Follow-up Date in the "Follow-up" field, if you expect to hear back from the customer at a later time.
10. If assigned, enter the tool name or any specific tool related information in the "Assigned Tool" field.
11. Enter any additional Notes about the customer or conversation in the "Notes" field.
12. Finally, select the current Lead Stage of the customer from the options in the "Lead Stage" field, this will be used to determine the next steps in the sales process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required: false): If you are representing a company, please enter the company name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address that we can use to get in touch with you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number, if you would like to be contacted by phone.
* **Message** (`message`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the challenge or question you are facing.
* **Challenge** (`challenge`, text, required: false): Describe the challenge you are facing.
* **How did you hear about us?** (`challenge_select`, select_multiple, required: false): Select from the options how you heard about us.
* **How qualified is this lead?** (`lead_qualify`, select_one, required: false): Evaluate the qualification of this lead.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, date, required: false): If you would like to schedule a follow-up, enter the date.
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, required: false): If you would like to schedule a follow-up, enter the time of day.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required: false): If assigned, enter the tool name or any specific tool related information.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the customer or conversation.
* **Lead Stage** (`lead_stage`, select_one, required: true): Select the current Lead Stage of the customer. 

## Tips
* Please provide valid and accurate information to ensure that we can get back to you in a timely manner.
* The more detailed the information provided, the better we can assist you.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
