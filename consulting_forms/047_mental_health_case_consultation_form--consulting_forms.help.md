# mental_health_case_consultation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from patients about their mental health case, including their medical history, chief complaints, and mental status. It is intended to be used by healthcare professionals to assess and manage mental health cases effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Patient Info" page and enter your name, age, and contact information.
2. On the "Mental Status" page, select the current mental status of the patient.
3. On the "Chief Complaint" page, list the main reasons why the patient is seeking consultation.
4. On the subsequent "Chief Complaint" pages (Chief Complaint 2, Chief Complaint 3, etc.), list any additional concerns or issues that need to be addressed.
5. On the "Medical History" page, provide any relevant medical history information.
6. On the "Social History" page, provide any relevant social history information.
7. On the "Family History" page, provide any relevant family history information.
8. On the "Medications" page, list any medications the patient is currently taking.
9. Review all pages and submit the form once you have completed all required fields.
