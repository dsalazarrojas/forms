# Smart Contract Service Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about your interest in smart contract services. By providing the requested details, you'll help us understand your specific requirements, allowing us to offer personalized support and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information:
	* First Name: Enter your first name.
	* Last Name: Enter your last name.
	* Company Name (optional): If you're representing a company, enter the name of the company.
	* Email: Enter your email address.
	* Phone: Enter your phone number.
	* Contract Date: Enter the date of your contract (if applicable).
	* Service Type: Select the type of service you're interested in.
	* Contract Duration: Enter the duration of your contract (if applicable).
	* Contract Details: Provide any additional details about your contract.
	* Contract Status: Select the current status of your contract.
	* Inquiry Type: Select the type of inquiry you have (e.g., General, Technical, Business, Legal, Financial).
	* Inquiry Details: Enter any additional details about your inquiry.
	* Business Type: Enter the type of business you represent (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Your last name.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, optional): The name of the company you represent (if applicable).
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Your phone number.
* **Contract Date** (`contract_date`, `date`, optional): The date of your contract (if applicable).
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, `text`, optional): The type of service you're interested in.
* **Contract Duration** (`contract_duration`, `text`, optional): The duration of your contract (if applicable).
* **Contract Details** (`contract_details`, `text`, optional): Additional details about your contract.
* **Contract Status** (`contract_status`, `select_one`, optional): The current status of your contract.
	+ Select "Active" if the contract is active.
	+ Select "Inactive" if the contract is inactive.
* **Inquiry Type** (`inquiry_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): The type of inquiry you have.
	+ Select "General" for general inquiries.
	+ Select "Technical" for technical inquiries.
	+ Select "Business" for business inquiries.
	+ Select "Legal" for legal inquiries.
	+ Select "Financial" for financial inquiries.
* **Inquiry Details** (`inquiry_details`, `text`, optional): Additional details about your inquiry.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, `text`, optional): The type of business you represent (if applicable).

## Tips

* Please provide as much detail as possible to help us better understand your needs.
