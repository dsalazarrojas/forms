# cultural_integration_advisory_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cultural integration advisory request form is designed to gather information for requesting cultural integration advisory services. This form helps you submit your request for support with cultural orientation, language assistance, or other related services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
2. Provide your full name in the "Name" field.
3. Select your date of arrival in the "Date of Arrival" field.
4. If you have any support needs, describe them in the "Support Needs" field.
5. Choose how frequently you want the support to follow-up with you in the "Follow-up Frequency" field.
6. Select the preferred communication method(s) in the "Communication Method" field.
7. Enter any additional notes in the "Notes" field (if necessary).
8. Describe the specific support service you are requesting in the "Support Service" field.
9. Provide details about the support service you are requesting in the "Support Service Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email (Email, text, required)**: Please enter your valid email address where you can be reached for communication.
* **Name (Name, text, required)**: Provide your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Date of Arrival (Date of Arrival, date, required)**: Indicate the date you arrived at the location where you need cultural integration advisory services.
* **Support Needs (Support Needs, text, optional)**: If you have any specific support needs, describe them here.
* **Follow-up Frequency (Follow-up Frequency, select multiple, required)**: Choose how frequently you would like to have follow-up communications with us. You can select Weekly, Bi-Monthly, Monthly, or Quarterly.
* **Communication Method (Communication Method, select multiple, required)**: Select the method(s) you prefer to communicate with us, such as Email, Phone, or Mail.
* **Notes (Notes, text, optional)**: Enter any additional notes or information if needed.
* **Support Service (Support Service, text, required)**: Describe the specific support service you are requesting.
* **Support Service Details (Support Service Details, text, required)**: Provide detailed information about the support service you are requesting.
