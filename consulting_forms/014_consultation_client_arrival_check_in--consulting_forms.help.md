# Consultation Client Arrival Check In - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help the consultation team prepare for your visit by gathering important information about you and your appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the required fields and provide as much detail as possible for the optional fields. Ensure that you select the correct options for each field as they are presented.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`client_first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`client_last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, required): Your email address is required for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, text, not required): Please provide your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, not required): This is your organization if you're visiting us in a professional capacity.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your scheduled appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required): Select your appointment time slot.
* **Consultant Name** (`consultant_name`, select_one, required): Choose the consultant assigned to you or select "Walk-in" for self-service.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of consultation you have booked (e.g., initial, follow-up, strategy session, review meeting).
* **Appointment Format** (`appointment_format`, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like the consultation to be conducted (in person, video call, phone call).
* **Number in Your Party** (`number_in_party`, number, required): Provide the number of people attending the consultation.
* **Attendee Names** (`attendee_names`, text, not required): List the names of all attendees if applicable.
* **Is This Your First Visit** (`first_time_visitor`, select_one, required): Select "True" if this is your first consultation visit.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`referral_source`, select_one, not required): Select the source of your referral.
* **Referred By** (`referred_by`, text, not required): If referred, please provide the name of who referred you.
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time, required): Enter your scheduled arrival time.
* **Waiting Preference** (`waiting_preference`, select_one, not required): Choose your preferred waiting arrangement (waiting room, outside and texting me, walk around).
* **Special Needs or Accommodations** (`special_needs`, text, not required): If you have any special requests or requirements, please specify.
* **Main Topic of Consultation** (`consultation_topic`, text, required): Describe the main topic you'd like to discuss during your consultation.
* **Did You Bring Required Documents** (`documents_brought`, select_one, not required): Indicate if you have all required documents.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, not required): Add any other information that will help your consultant prepare for your meeting.

By filling out this form, you'll ensure that your consultant is prepared and ready for your consultation. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
