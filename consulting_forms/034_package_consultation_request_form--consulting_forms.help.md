# Package Consultation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users who are interested in consulting packages. Please fill out the form to inquire about available packages and schedule a consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields and select any additional information as needed. You can submit the form to initiate the consultation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name for your consultation request.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name for your consultation request.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you for your consultation.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address for your consultation request.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, optional): Please select a preferred date for your consultation.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, optional): Please select a preferred time for your consultation.
* **Services Requested** (`services_requested`, select_multiple, optional): Please select any services you are interested in consulting on.
* **Services Requested**: This field will display a list of services available. You can select one or multiple services.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Please enter any additional information you have about your consultation request.
* **Package Selected** (`package_selected`, select_one, optional): Please select the package you are interested in consulting on.
* **Package Selected**: This field will display a list of available packages. You can select one package.
* **Package Details** (`package_details`, text, optional): Please enter any additional information about the package you are interested in consulting on.
* **Package Price** (`package_price`, number, optional): Please enter the price you are willing to pay for the consultation.
