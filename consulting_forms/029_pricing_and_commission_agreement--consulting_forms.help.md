# Pricing And Commission Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a pricing and commission agreement between a business and a client. It outlines the terms and conditions of payment, commission structure, payment frequency, payment amount, payment method, business address, contact name, signature date, and signature.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the business name where it is located.
2. Enter the contract date when the agreement was made or will be made.
3. Choose whether there will be payment terms (yes/no).
4. Determine the commission structure (yes/no).
5. Select how often payment will be made (yes/no).
6. Enter the payment amount.
7. Choose the payment method (yes/no).
8. Enter the business address.
9. Enter the contact name.
10. Sign and date the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (`Business Name`, text, required): This is the name of the business.
* **Contract Date** (`Contract Date`, date, required): This is the date when the agreement was made or will be made.
* **Payment Terms** (`Payment Terms`, text, required/optional): This is where you can enter any terms or conditions that need to be met for payment.
* **Commission Structure** (`Commission Structure`, select_multiple, optional): This is where you choose whether a commission structure will be used (yes/no).
* **Payment Frequency** (`Payment Frequency`, select_one, required/optional): This is where you select how often payment will be made (yes/no).
* **Payment Amount** (`Payment Amount`, number, optional): This is the amount to be paid.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select_one, required/optional): This is where you choose the payment method (yes/no).
* **Business Address** (`Business Address`, text, optional): This is the address of the business.
* **Contact Name** (`Contact Name`, text, optional): This is the name of the contact.
* **Signature Date** (`Signature Date`, date, required): This is the date of the signature.
* **Signature** (`Signature`, text, required): This is where you sign the agreement.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`Terms and Conditions`, text, optional): This is where you can enter any additional terms and conditions that need to be met.
