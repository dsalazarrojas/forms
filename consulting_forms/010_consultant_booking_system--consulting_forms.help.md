# Consultant Booking System - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Consultant Booking System is designed to streamline the process of booking consulting sessions with our experts. This system ensures that we can tailor each session to your specific needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name.
2. Provide your email address for booking confirmation and correspondence.
3. Enter your phone number for easy contact.
4. If applicable, list your company name.
5. Choose your preferred consultant from the provided list.
6. Select the type of consultation needed.
7. Indicate the main topic or focus area for the consultation.
8. Select your preferred date and time for the session.
9. Choose how you would like to meet (in-person, video call, or phone call).
10. Specify the duration of the session required.
11. If applicable, list the number of participants attending.
12. Enter the names of all participants.
13. Provide any special requirements or accommodations needed.
14. Give some background information on the context of the consultation.
15. Specify the goals for this session.
16. If necessary, list any documents for the consultant to review beforehand.
17. If you have worked with us before, mark accordingly.
18. Indicate the source of your referral (e.g., website, social media, etc.).
19. Choose the payment method for this session.
20. Acknowledge the cancellation policy.
21. Finally, select the date of this booking request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)**: Enter your first name for our records.
* **Last Name (2)**: Enter your last name for our records.
* **Email Address (3)**: Enter a valid email for booking confirmation and correspondence.
* **Phone Number (4)**: Enter your contact phone number for easy communication.
* **Company Name (5)**: If you're representing a company, provide the name for our records.
* **Select Consultant (6)**: Choose your preferred consultant from the provided list.
* **Service Type (7)**: Select the type of consultation needed (e.g., initial consultation, follow-up session, etc.).
* **Consultation Topic (8)**: Specify the main topic or focus area for the consultation.
* **Preferred Date (9)**: Select your preferred appointment date.
* **Preferred Time (10)**: Choose your preferred time slot for the session.
* **Consultation Format (11)**: Select how you would like to meet (in-person, video call, etc.).
* **Session Duration (12)**: Indicate the length of the session required.
* **Number of Participants (13)**: If multiple people are attending, enter the number.
* **Participant Names (14)**: If multiple people are attending, list their names.
* **Special Requirements (15)**: If any special needs or requirements, please list them here.
* **Background Information (16)**: Provide some background information on the context of the consultation.
* **Goals for This Session (17)**: Specify what you hope to accomplish during the session.
* **Documents to Review (18)**: If necessary, list any documents for the consultant to review beforehand.
* **Are You a Previous Client (19)**: If you have worked with us before, mark true.
* **How Did You Hear About Us (20)**: Indicate the source of your referral.
* **Payment Method (21)**: Choose the payment method for this session.
* **Cancellation Policy Acknowledgment (22)**: Acknowledge our cancellation policy to avoid any confusion.
* **Booking Date (23)**: Enter the date of this booking request.

Tips:
- Be accurate and clear when filling out the form to ensure the consultant can tailor the session to your needs.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
- We appreciate your attention to detail in filling out this form to make the consultation process smooth and efficient.
