# Sugaring Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers who have expressed interest in the sugaring treatment. It's meant for customers who have already been contacted by the consulting team and are ready to proceed with the next steps.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address (if you have one).
3. Provide your Phone number for contact purposes.
4. Select the area of treatment you are interested in.
5. Choose the duration of the treatment (if applicable).
6. Add any additional Notes about your condition or concerns.
7. If you have a preferred date and time for the first conversation, you can enter it in the respective fields.
8. If you have a preferred location for the first conversation, select one from the options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your personal email address if you have one. This will help us get in touch with you more easily.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for us to reach out to you with any important updates or reminders.
* **Treatment Area**: Select the area of treatment you are interested in from the dropdown menu. This will help us better understand your needs.
* **Treatment Duration**: Choose the duration of the treatment that suits you best (if applicable).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information about your condition or concerns. This will help us tailor the treatment plan to your needs.
* **First Conversation Date**: If you have a specific date in mind for the first conversation, enter it here. This can be any date in the past or future.
* **First Conversation Time**: Enter the time of day you would prefer for the first conversation. This is not a required field, and we'll do our best to accommodate your request.
* **First Conversation Location**: If you have a preferred location for the first conversation, select one from the options. This can be any location where you feel comfortable.
* **Next Conversation**: Enter any important details or reminders you have for the next conversation.
