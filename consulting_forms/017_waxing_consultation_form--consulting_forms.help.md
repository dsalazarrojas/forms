# Waxing Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Waxing Consultation Form is a tool designed to collect information from customers about their skin condition, hair length, medical conditions, and other relevant details to provide personalized consultation services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it appears on your official identification documents.
2. Describe your current skin condition.
3. Choose the length of your hair.
4. List any medical conditions you are aware of.
5. Select the type of medical treatment you are currently undergoing.
6. Rate your current pain level.
7. Specify where you experience pain.
8. List your previous treatments for hair removal.
9. Describe your hair growth.
10. Provide any relevant medical history.
11. Specify your hair color.
12. Write a brief report about your consultation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name (1)**: Please enter your name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Skin Condition (2)**: Describe your current skin condition, including any skin irritations or allergic reactions you may have experienced.
* **Hair Length (3)**: Choose the length of your hair, which will help the consultant determine the best waxing method for you.
* **Medical Conditions (4)**: List any medical conditions you are aware of, such as diabetes, epilepsy, or high blood pressure.
* **Medical Treatment (5)**: Select the type of medical treatment you are currently undergoing, if any.
* **Pain Level (6)**: Rate your current pain level, where 1 is minimal and 5 is severe.
* **Pain Location (7)**: Specify where you experience pain, such as your bikini line or eyebrows.
* **Previous Treatment (8)**: List any previous treatments you have tried for hair removal, such as waxing, shaving, or sugaring.
* **Hair Growth (9)**: Describe your hair growth, including any areas where your hair grows thicker or faster.
* **Medical History (10)**: Provide any relevant medical history, such as previous allergies or reactions to certain treatments.
* **Hair Color (11)**: Specify your hair color.
* **Report (12)**: Write a brief report about your consultation, including any questions or concerns you may have.
