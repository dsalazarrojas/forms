# ADHD Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ADHD Consultation Form is used to collect detailed information about clients seeking attention and evaluation services for ADHD. The form is divided into several sections, each focusing on different aspects of the client's experience and history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide Basic Information**: Enter the client's full name and date of birth.
2. Select the correct response to each question, using the scale provided.
3. For free-text fields, type in the requested information.
4. Select your preferred contact method for follow-up communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name**: Enter the client's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the client's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Occupation/Grade in School**: Enter the client's current occupation or grade in school.
* **Reason for Seeking Consultation**: Explain why the client is seeking evaluation services for ADHD.
* **Primary Symptom Assessment**:
	+ **Difficulty Sustaining Attention**: Select how often the client experiences difficulty sustaining attention in tasks or play activities.
	+ **Difficulty Organizing Tasks**: Select how often the client experiences difficulty organizing tasks and activities.
	+ **Hyperactivity**: Select how often the client exhibits fidgeting or other hyperactive behaviors.
* **Impact on Daily Life**:
	+ **Impact on School or Work**: Select how much the client's ADHD symptoms interfere with school or work performance.
	+ **Impact on Social Life**: Select how much the client's ADHD symptoms interfere with social life or relationships.
* **Clinical and Developmental History**:
	+ **Previous ADHD Diagnosis**: Select if the client has ever been diagnosed with ADHD.
	+ **Previous Diagnosis Details**: Provide any relevant information about previous diagnosis, including when and by whom.
* **Current Medications**: List any medications currently being taken for ADHD or other conditions.
* **Co-occurring Conditions**: List any other diagnosed conditions the client may have, such as anxiety or depression.
* **Family History of ADHD**: Select if there is a family history of ADHD or other neurodevelopmental disorders.
* **Primary Goals for Consultation**: Explain the client's primary goals for the consultation, such as diagnosis or behavioral strategies.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select the preferred method of contact for follow-up communication.
* **Assigned Clinician (Internal Use Only)**: This field is for internal use only and should not be filled out by clients.
