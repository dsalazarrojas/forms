# Education Sector Consulting Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather contact and inquiry information from users in the education sector. It is used to facilitate communication with the consulting team and to provide a clear understanding of the user's requirements and goals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you enter your name, school name, email, and phone number correctly.
3. Choose a suitable assigned to and assigned tool based on your requirements.
4. Specify the follow-up date and time if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **School Name**: Enter your school's name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address in the format `[name}@schoolname.com` (e.g., `johndoe@school.edu`).
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in the format `XXX-XXX-XXXX` (e.g., `123-456-7890`).
* **Contact Details**: Enter any additional contact information you'd like to provide, such as your position or department.
* **Inquiry Details**: Provide a brief description of your inquiry or question.
* **Assigned To**: Select one or more individuals from the list to be assigned to your case.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select a tool that best suits your requirements from the available options.
* **Follow Up**: Check this box if you'd like to schedule a follow-up action.
* **Follow Up Date**: Select a date for the follow-up action if you've checked the "Follow Up" box.
* **Lead ID**: Enter a unique ID for your lead, if applicable.
