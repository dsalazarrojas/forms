# Insurance Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insurance Consultation Form is a tool used by insurance providers to gather information from users who require consultations on insurance policies or related services. This form helps users communicate their needs and preferences, allowing insurance professionals to offer informed guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your contact information, including your name and email address or phone number.
2. Enter your date of birth.
3. Select your insurance company from the available options (if you don't see your company listed, you can select "Other" and provide more details).
4. Specify the policy holder's name (usually yourself, but in some cases, you may need to provide information on behalf of someone else).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information (Contact Information, text, required: false)**: This is the section where you enter your contact details, including your name and preferred email address or phone number. Please ensure the information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Date of Birth (date, required: false)**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY). This information helps insurance providers understand your eligibility for certain services or policies.
* **Insurance Company (insurance_company, text, required: false)**: Select your insurance company from the list provided. If you don't see your company listed, select "Other" and provide a brief description of the company you are affiliated with.
* **Policy Holder (policy_holder, select_one, required: false)**: Choose the name of the policy holder (usually yourself, but could be someone else).
