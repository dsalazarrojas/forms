# Consultant Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The form is intended to gather information from potential consultants about their profile, skills, and preferences to match them with suitable opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Fill in your **Full Legal Name**, **Primary Email Address**, **Primary Phone Number**, and **Current Address (Street, City, State, ZIP)** in the corresponding sections.
2.  For **Highest Level of Education Attained**, select one of the given options (e.g., Bachelor's Degree, Master's Degree (MA/MS), MBA, etc.).
3.  Enter your **Major or Field of Study** and list any **Relevant Professional Certifications** you hold (e.g., PMP, CFA, etc.).
4.  Choose your **Primary Consulting Domain/Expertise** from the given options (e.g., Management/Strategy Consulting, Technology/IT Transformation, etc.).
5.  Select the **Target Industries Served** that your consultancy caters to (e.g., Financial Services, Technology/SaaS, etc.).
6.  Indicate your **Data Analysis and Visualization Proficiency** (Basic, Intermediate, Advanced, Expert).
7.  Rate your **Executive Presentation and Communication** skills (Basic, Intermediate, Advanced, Expert).
8.  Choose your preferred **Project Management Methodology** (Agile/Scrum, Waterfall, Lean/Six Sigma, etc.).
9.  Enter your **Willingness to Travel to Client Sites** as a percentage (0-25%, 25-50%, 50-75%, 75-100%).
10.  Select your **Earliest Possible Start Date** for the consultancy engagement.
11.  Enter your desired **Annual Base Salary** (including any bonuses).
12.  Write a brief description of **Why are you interested in joining our consultancy?** (max 1000 characters).
13.  Finally, select the **Date of Application**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Legal Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
*   **Primary Email Address**: Enter your main email address for communication purposes.
*   **Primary Phone Number**: Enter your main phone number for communication and outreach.
*   **Current Address (Street, City, State, ZIP)**: Enter your current address for logistical purposes.
*   **Current or Most Recent Job Title**: Enter your current or most recent job title.
*   **Total Years of Professional Experience**: Enter your total years of professional experience.
*   **Primary Consulting Domain/Expertise**: Select your primary area of expertise.
*   **Target Industries Served**: Select all industries you serve or have experience with.
*   **Highest Level of Education Attained**: Select your highest level of education.
*   **Major or Field of Study**: Enter your major or field of study.
*   **Relevant Professional Certifications**: List any professional certifications you hold.
*   **Data Analysis and Visualization Proficiency**: Rate your proficiency level (1-5).
*   **Executive Presentation and Communication**: Rate your proficiency level (1-5).
*   **Preferred Project Management Methodology**: Select your preferred methodology.
*   **Willingness to Travel to Client Sites**: Rate your willingness to travel as a percentage.
*   **Earliest Possible Start Date**: Select your earliest possible start date for the consultancy engagement.
*   **Desired Annual Base Salary**: Enter your desired annual base salary.
*   **Why are you interested in joining our consultancy?**: Enter a brief description of why you're interested in joining our consultancy.
