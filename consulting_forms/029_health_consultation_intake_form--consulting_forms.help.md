# Health Consultation Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from patients before a consultation with a healthcare professional. It aims to gather general details about the patient and their health status to provide effective care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your personal details in the 'patient info' section.
2. Describe your reason for the consultation in the 'consultation details' section.
3. List any allergies you have in the 'allergies' section.
4. If you take any medications, list them in the 'medication list' section (optional).
5. Provide contact information for an emergency contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient info** (`patient info`, `text`, **required**: False): This section is for providing your personal details.
* **consultation details** (`consultation details`, `text`, **required**: True): Describe your reason for visiting the healthcare professional.
* **contact info** (`contact info`, `text`, **required**: False): This is for providing contact information for a family member or friend who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **medical history** (`medical history`, `text`, **required**: True): Share any relevant medical history that may be useful for your treatment.
* **allergies** (`allergies`, `select_multiple`, **required**: True): Check any allergies you have, selecting from the options provided.
* **medication list** (`medication list`, `text`, **required**: False): List any medications you are currently taking (if none, you can leave this blank).
* **emergency contact** (`emergency contact`, `text`, **required**: False): Provide the name and contact information of a person to contact in case of an emergency.

## Tips

* Please be honest and thorough in your responses.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to ask your healthcare professional.
* This form is meant to be completed before your consultation, but if you need to make any changes, please inform your healthcare professional.
