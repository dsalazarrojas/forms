# Economic Impact Study Consulting Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from potential clients to initiate an economic impact study consultation with our team. It helps us understand your project's requirements and ensures a productive discussion during our consultation.
## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form to initiate a consultation about an economic impact study.
- Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date contact information (name, email, company, phone number).
- Describe your project briefly to give our team a clear understanding of what you are looking to achieve.
- Provide a budget for your project if you have one.
- Specify your desired consultation timeline.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`name`, text, required): Please provide your name as it appears on your business card or ID.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
- **Company** (`company`, text, required): Type the name of your company or organization.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached during office hours.
- **Project Brief** (`project_brief`, text, required): Describe your project briefly, including goals, objectives, and any specific requirements.
- **Budget** (`budget_amount`, number, optional): If you have a budget set for your project, please provide it.
- **Timeline** (`timeline`, date, optional): Specify your desired consultation timeline, including start and end dates.
**Tips**
- Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to ensure our team understands your project requirements.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us after submitting this form.
