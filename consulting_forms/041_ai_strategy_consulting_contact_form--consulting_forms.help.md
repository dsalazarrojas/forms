# AI Strategy Consulting Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential clients interested in AI strategy consulting services. It helps us understand their project needs, goals, and expectations, allowing us to provide tailored consulting support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief overview of your project and goals.
2. Fill in your contact information, including your name and email address.
3. Specify your estimated budget range and desired timeline.
4. Select your top priorities for the consulting engagement.
5. Choose a preferred contact time for us to reach out to you.
6. If you have any additional information, please provide it in the additional information field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact name** (`contact_name`, **text**, required): Enter your first and last name to help us address you properly.
* **Company or organization** (`company`, **text**, optional): If you represent a company or organization, please enter its name.
* **Email address** (`email`, **email**, required): A valid email address is required for us to follow up on your request.
* **Project summary or challenge** (`project_summary`, **text, required): Briefly describe your project goals and challenges.
* **Estimated budget range** (`budget_range`, **select_one, optional): Select one of the budget ranges to give us an estimate of your project budget.
* **Desired timeline** (`timeline`, **text, optional): Specify your start and end expectations for the project.
* **Top priorities for consulting engagement** (`priorities`, **select_multiple, required): Select up to three priorities for our consulting services.
* **Preferred contact time** (`preferred_contact_time`, **select_one, optional): Choose a preferred time for us to contact you.
* **Additional information** (`additional_information`, **text, optional): If you have any additional information, provide it here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure we can get back to you.
* Select only one budget range and one preferred contact time.
* Be as detailed as possible in the project summary and desired timeline fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
