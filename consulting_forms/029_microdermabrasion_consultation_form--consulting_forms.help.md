# Microdermabrasion Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for patients to provide their general information, medical history, allergies, and medications before undergoing a microdermabrasion treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please fill out the General Information section with your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* In the Medical History section, answer all questions honestly about your previous medical conditions, surgeries, or health issues that may impact the treatment.
* In the Allergies section, check all applicable boxes if you have any allergies.
* In the Medications section, list all your current medications.
