# practice_launch_pre_consult_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information about the client, including their background, experience, and consulting preferences. This information will help us better prepare for our consultation meeting and provide a personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Answer each question as thoroughly and accurately as possible.
2. Use clear and concise language in your responses.
3. If you have any additional comments or questions, please use the space at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is your background and experience?** (Page 1): Please describe your current position, industry, or field of expertise.
* 
- **What is your current role or job function?** (Page 2): Describe your current job function or role.
* 
- **How many years of experience do you have in your field?** (Page 3): Enter a number representing your years of experience.
* 
- **What is your current work location or industry?** (Page 4): Describe your current work location or industry.
* 
- **Do you have any existing relationships with clients?** (Page 5): Select 'True' if you have existing relationships with clients, 'False' otherwise.
* 
- **What is the client's preferred consulting style?** (Page 6): Select the client's preferred consulting style (Low-key, Medium-key, High-key).
* 
- **What are the client's top goals for our consultation?** (Page 7): Describe the client's top goals for our consultation.
* 
- **Additional notes or any other information?** (Page 8): Add any additional information you think would be helpful for our consultation.
* 
- **Do you have any additional comments or questions?** (Page 9): Share any additional comments or questions you may have.
* 
- **Do you have any other information that would be helpful for our consultation?** (Page 10): Add any additional information you think would be helpful for our consultation.
* 
- **(Pages 11-12 are blank pages.** They are likely placeholders and are not meant for completion.)
