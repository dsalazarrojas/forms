# Parent Consultation Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for parents to schedule consultations with teachers. It allows parents to provide necessary information and details for the appointment, including their contact information, preferred appointment type, date, time, location, and a brief message for the teacher.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your child's information in the `Parent Information` field.
2. Select the type of consultation you prefer from the `Appointment Type` dropdown list.
3. Choose a date and time for the consultation from the `Date` and `Time` fields.
4. Select the location where the consultation will take place from the `Location` field.
5. Add any additional meeting details or information in the `Meeting Details` field.
6. Enter a brief message for the parent in the `Parent Message` field.
7. Enter a brief message for the teacher in the `Teacher Message` field.
8. Confirm the appointment by selecting `True` in the `Confirm Appointment` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Information** (`student_info`, text, required): Enter your child's name and other relevant contact information (e.g., email and phone number).
* **Appointment Type** (`appointment_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of consultation you prefer (Meeting, Call, or Email).
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select a date for the consultation.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Choose a time for the consultation.
* **Location** (`location`, select_multiple, optional): Select the location where the consultation will take place (Virtual, School, or Home).
* **Meeting Details** (`meeting_details`, text, optional): Add any additional meeting details or information.
* **Parent Message** (`parent_message`, text, optional): Enter a brief message for the parent.
* **Teacher Message** (`teacher_message`, text, optional): Enter a brief message for the teacher.
* **Confirm Appointment** (`confirm_appointment`, select_one, optional): Confirm the appointment by selecting `True`.
* **Follow-up Message** (`follow_up_message`, text, optional): Enter a follow-up message after the consultation.

Note: The `Follow-up Message` field is optional and intended for any additional communication between parent and teacher after the consultation.
