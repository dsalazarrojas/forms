# Estate Planning Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about your estate planning goals and situation. It is designed to help you make informed decisions about your financial future and ensure that your wishes are respected and carried out after your passing.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
3. If you are unsure about any field, ask for clarification or guidance from the form administrator or estate planning professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information, including your address, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Current Address**: Enter your current address, including your street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Assets Owned**: If you own any assets, such as real estate, investments, or businesses, select 'Yes' to indicate that you do. If you do not own any assets, select 'No'.
* **Spouse Info**: If you have a spouse, select 'Yes' to indicate that you have a spouse who you would like to include in your estate planning. If you do not have a spouse, select 'No'.
* **Children Info**: If you have children, select 'Yes' to indicate that you would like to include them in your estate planning. If you do not have children, select 'No'.
* **Assets Wanted to Leave**: If you have assets that you would like to leave to specific beneficiaries, select 'Yes' to indicate that you do. If you do not have any assets to leave, select 'No'.
* **Beneficiaries Info**: If you have specific beneficiaries who you would like to include in your estate planning, select their names from the provided options. If you do not have any beneficiaries, select 'No'.

## Tips

* Please answer each field honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask for clarification or guidance from the form administrator or estate planning professional.
* This form is confidential and is used to help you make informed decisions about your financial future.
