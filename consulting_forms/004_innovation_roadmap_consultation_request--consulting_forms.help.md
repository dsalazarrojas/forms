# innovation-roadmap-consultation-request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for an innovation roadmap consultation request. It's meant to gather essential details from users to initiate a consultation process that suits their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your **User Name**, which is your name or the name of the person requesting the consultation.
2. Enter your **Organization**, if applicable, which is the name of the company or institution you are representing.
3. Specify your **Innovation Goals**, which are the specific objectives or outcomes you aim to achieve through the consultation.
4. Outline the **Challenges** you're facing, or the problems you'd like to solve.
5. Select your preferred **Meeting Times**, when you'd like to meet or discuss further.
6. Mention the **Expert Name**, if you'd like to work with a specific consultant or expert.
7. Choose your preferred **Meeting Preference**, which will help us understand how you'd like to proceed with the consultation (e.g., phone call, email, or in-person meeting).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: Enter your name or the name of the person requesting the consultation.
* **Organization**: If you are representing a company or institution, enter its name. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Innovation Goals**: Describe the specific objectives or outcomes you aim to achieve through the consultation.
* **Challenges**: Outline the problems or difficulties you'd like to address.
* **Preferred Meeting Times**: Select your preferred times for a meeting or discussion.
* **Expert Name**: If you have a specific consultant or expert in mind, mention their name.
* **Meeting Preference**: Choose your preferred way to proceed with the consultation (e.g., phone call, email, or in-person meeting).
