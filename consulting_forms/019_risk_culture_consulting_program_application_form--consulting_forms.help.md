# Risk Culture Consulting Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form
## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Organization Details
* **Organization Details** (`organization_details`, text, optional): Fill in your company's name, address, and contact information to help us get in touch with you.

### Risk Culture
* **Risk Culture** (`risk_culture`, select_one, optional): Select the level of risk culture that best describes your organization. The options are:
	+ Low
	+ Medium
	+ High

### Current Risk Level
* **Current Risk Level** (`current_risk_level`, text, optional): Provide any comments or information about your current risk level.

### Goals
* **Goals** (`goals`, note, optional): Describe what you hope to achieve through the Risk Culture Consulting Program. Be as specific as possible.

### Organization Size
* **Organization Size** (`organization_size`, select_one, optional): Choose the size of your organization from the options:
	+ Small
	+ Medium
	+ Large

### Employees
* **Employees** (`employees`, number, optional): Enter the number of employees in your organization.

### Risk Level Goals
* **Risk Level Goals** (`risk_level_goals`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the risk level goals that you want to achieve through the program. The options are:
	+ Improve Risk Culture
	+ Improve Efficiency
	+ Improve Compliance

### Consulting Goals
* **Consulting Goals** (`consulting_goals`, select_one, optional): Choose the consulting goals that align with your priorities. The options are:
	+ Improve Risk Culture
	+ Improve Efficiency
	+ Improve Compliance

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your application.
