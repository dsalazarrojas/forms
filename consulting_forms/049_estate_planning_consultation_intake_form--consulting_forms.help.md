# Estate Planning Consultation Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Estate Planning Consultation Intake Form is designed to gather information from you about your estate planning needs and preferences. This information will help us provide you with personalized guidance and recommendations to help you achieve your estate planning goals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and answer all questions accurately and truthfully.
2. Enter your information in the provided fields.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Estate Planning Consultation Form** (page 1, field 1):
	+ A brief description of your estate planning needs and goals.
* **Client Information** (page 1, field 2):
	+ Enter your name, date of birth, and any other relevant information.
* **Name** (page 1, field 3):
	+ Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on your estate documents.
* **Date of Birth** (page 1, field 4):
	+ Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Address** (page 1, field 5):
	+ Enter your current address or business address, as applicable.
* **Phone Number** (page 1, field 6):
	+ Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email Address** (page 1, field 7):
	+ Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Assets and Income** (page 1, field 8):
	+ Describe your current assets and income sources.
* **Dependents** (page 1, field 9):
	+ List your dependents, if any.
* **Next of Kin** (page 1, field 10):
	+ List your next of kin, if any.
* **Estate Planning Services** (page 2, field 11):
	+ Select all applicable estate planning services you are interested in (Estate Planning Consultation, Will and Trust Planning, Advance Planning, Trust Administration, Asset Protection Planning, Estate Taxes).
* **Estate Type** (page 2, field 12):
	+ Select the type of estate you have (Individual, Family, Business, Joint, Other).
* **Assets** (page 2, field 13):
	+ Enter the value of your total assets.
* **Liabilities** (page 2, field 14):
	+ Enter the value of your total liabilities.
* **Net Worth** (page 2, field 15):
	+ Enter your net worth.
* **Social Security Number** (page 2, field 16):
	+ Enter your social security number.
* **Driver License Number** (page 2, field 17):
	+ Enter your driver's license number.
* **Other Assets** (page 3, field 18):
	+ List any other assets you may have.
* **Business Name** (page 3, field 19):
	+ Enter your business name, if applicable.
* **Business Address** (page 3, field 20):
	+ Enter your business address, if applicable.
* **Tax Identification Number** (page 3, field 21):
	+ Enter your tax identification number.
* **Other Liabilities** (page 3, field 22):
	+ List any other liabilities you may have.
* **Estate Plan Instruments** (page 4, field 23):
	+ List any estate plan instruments you may have.
* **Financial Institutions** (page 4, field 24):
	+ List any financial institutions you may have.

Note: Please ensure that you have filled out all the required fields and that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
