# Consulting Value Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from consultants on their experiences with the service, providing valuable insights for improvement and future development.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Client Name**: Enter your full name or company name.
2. **Consultation Service Received**: Select the primary service provided by the consultant.
3. **Project Duration (Months)**: Enter the length of the engagement in months (if applicable).
4. **Service Value Assessment** (Note): This is a note field for you to provide a brief description of the service provided and its value.
5. **Strategic Insight and Advice**: Select the value of the strategic direction provided (1-5).
6. **Effectiveness in Problem Resolution**: Select how well your issues were addressed (1-5).
7. **Return on Investment (ROI)**: Select the perceived value relative to cost (1-5).
8. **Overall Experience with Consultant**: Select your general satisfaction with the engagement (1-5).
9. **Likelihood to Recommend**: Select how likely you are to recommend us to a colleague (0-10).
10. **Most Valuable Aspect of the Service**: Describe what you found most helpful about the service.
11. **Suggestions for Improvement**: Suggest how we can improve our services.
12. **Interest in Future Services**: Select if you would consider us for future projects (True/False/Maybe).
13. **Date of Completion**: Select today's date to ensure accurate tracking.

* **Client Name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name or company name.
