# Diet Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide information about your medical history, health status, and diet preferences. The data collected will help medical professionals offer personalized diet consultation services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your height in centimeters, feet, inches, or meters in the "height" field.
2. Enter your weight in kilograms or pounds in the "weight" field.
3. Select your height unit from the options "ft", "in", "cm", or "m" in the "height_units" field.
4. Enter your blood pressure reading in millimeters of mercury in the "blood_pressure" field.
5. Enter your pulse rate in beats per minute in the "pulse" field.
6. Describe any existing medical conditions you have, such as diabetes, high blood pressure, etc. in the "medical_conditions" field.
7. If you have any other medical conditions not listed, describe them in the "medical_conditions_other" field.
8. Describe your family medical history, including any inherited conditions or family members with medical issues, in the "family_history" field.
9. List any medication you are currently taking in the "medication" field.
10. List any allergies you have, such as food or environmental allergies, in the "allergies" field.
11. Select any medical tests you have had or are going to have, such as MRI, CT scan, etc. from the list of options in the "medical_tests" field.
12. If you have had any other medical tests not listed, select them from the "other_medical_tests" field.
13. Choose your diet preference from the options "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3" in the "diet_preferences" field.
14. Describe any diet restrictions you have, such as vegetarian or gluten-free, in the "diet_restrictions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **height** (Number): Enter your height in centimeters, feet, inches, or meters.
* **weight** (Number): Enter your weight in kilograms or pounds.
* **height_units** (Select One): Select your height unit from "ft", "in", "cm", or "m".
* **blood_pressure** (Number): Enter your blood pressure reading in millimeters of mercury.
* **pulse** (Number): Enter your pulse rate in beats per minute.
* **medical_conditions** (Text): Describe any existing medical conditions you have, such as diabetes, high blood pressure, etc.
* **medical_conditions_other** (Text): If you have any other medical conditions not listed, describe them here.
* **family_history** (Text): Describe your family medical history, including any inherited conditions or family members with medical issues.
* **medication** (Text): List any medication you are currently taking.
* **allergies** (Text): List any allergies you have, such as food or environmental allergies.
* **medical_tests** (Select Multiple): Select any medical tests you have had or are going to have, such as MRI, CT scan, etc.
* **other_medical_tests** (Select Multiple): If you have had any other medical tests not listed, select them here.
* **diet_preferences** (Select One): Choose your diet preference from the options "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **diet_restrictions** (Text): Describe any diet restrictions you have, such as vegetarian or gluten-free.
