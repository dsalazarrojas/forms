### Warehouse Consultant Quote Form - Help Guide

### Purpose
The Warehouse Consultant Quote Form is a tool used by consultants to collect information about a potential project or quote request from a client. It helps consultants gather details about the project, the services required, and the client's contact information. The form is intended to be completed by the client and returned to the consultant for further evaluation and quote preparation.

### How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, ensure you have all the required information about your project or quote request.
2.  Begin by filling in the **quote_request_details** field with a brief description of your project or request.
3.  Enter your **consultant_name** in the **consultant_name** field.
4.  In the **project_description** field, provide more detailed information about your project or request.
5.  Select the relevant **service_type** for your project or request from the provided options.
6.  If applicable, provide the expected **project_duration** and **project_cost** for your project or request.
7.  In the **project_terms** field, mention any specific terms or conditions related to your project or request.
8.  Enter your **consultant_terms** if you have any specific terms or conditions.
9.  Select the **project_status** as Active or Inactive, depending on your project's status.
10. In the **project_notes** field, add any additional notes about your project or request.
11. Enter your **client_email** and **client_phone** to ensure the consultant can contact you for further discussion.
12. Enter your **consultant_email** and **consultant_phone** so that the consultant can contact you for any additional information or clarification.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **quote_request_details** (Text Field): Enter a brief description of your project or quote request.
* **consultant_name** (Text Field): Enter your full name as the consultant or representative of the consulting firm.
* **project_description** (Note Field): Provide a detailed description of your project or request, including any specific requirements or needs.
* **service_type** (Select One Field): Choose the type of service you require for your project or request (Consulting, Engineering, Maintenance).
* **project_duration** (Number Field): Enter the expected duration of your project or request.
* **project_cost** (Number Field): Provide the expected cost of your project or request.
* **project_terms** (Note Field): Mention any specific terms or conditions related to your project or request.
* **consultant_terms** (Note Field): Enter any specific terms or conditions you have for your project or request.
* **project_status** (Select Multiple Field): Choose the status of your project as Active or Inactive.
* **project_notes** (Note Field): Add any additional notes about your project or request.
* **client_email** (Email Field): Enter your email address so the consultant can contact you for further discussion.
* **client_phone** (Text Field): Enter your phone number so the consultant can contact you for further discussion.
* **consultant_email** (Email Field): Enter the email address of the consultant or representative of the consulting firm.
* **consultant_phone** (Text Field): Enter the phone number of the consultant or representative of the consulting firm.

### Tips

*   Ensure all required fields are filled out to ensure the consultant can provide an accurate quote.
*   Double-check your contact information to ensure the consultant can reach you for further discussion.
*   Review the form carefully to ensure all necessary details are included.
*   If you have any questions or need help filling out the form, contact the consultant for support.
