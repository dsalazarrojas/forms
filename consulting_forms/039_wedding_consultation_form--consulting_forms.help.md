# Wedding Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Consultation Form is designed to gather information from couples about their wedding preferences, budget, and expectations. This form helps our wedding planning services to create a personalized plan that meets their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully to understand what is required.
2. Fill in the information accurately and honestly.
3. Save your progress and come back to complete the form at a later time if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Preferences**: Please describe your overall wedding style, location, and any specific details that are essential to you. (Text, optional)
* **Wedding Dates**: Enter the date(s) of your wedding, including the month and year. (Text, optional)
* **Wedding Location**: Specify the venue or location where you would like your wedding to take place. (Text, optional)
* **Wedding Guests**: Estimate the number of guests you plan to invite. (Number, optional)
* **Wedding Budget**: Enter your estimated budget for the wedding event. (Number, optional)
* **Wedding Style**: Select from the options provided: Formal, Semi-Formal, or Casual. (Select One, optional)
* **Wedding Colors**: Choose up to two colors that you would like to incorporate into your wedding theme. (Select Multiple, optional)
* **Wedding Decor**: Select up to two decor options that fit your wedding style: Floral, Modern, or Rustic. (Select Multiple, optional)
* **Wedding Food and Drink**: Choose up to two options for your wedding food and drink arrangements: Buffet, Family-Style, or Hors d'oeuvres. (Select Multiple, optional)
* **Wedding Entertainment**: Select up to two options for wedding entertainment: Dancing, Music, or Photo Booth. (Select Multiple, optional)

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when filling out each field.
* If you are unsure about a specific field, please ask our wedding planning services for clarification.
* Review your form carefully before submitting to ensure that all information is accurate.
