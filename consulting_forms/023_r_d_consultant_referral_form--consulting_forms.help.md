# R_and_D Consultant Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The R_and_D Consultant Referral Form is used to collect information about a consultant or company for referral purposes. This form helps to gather essential details such as contact information, project specifics, and referral information to facilitate the consulting process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Company Information" section with the details of the company you are referring.
2. Enter the "Contact Information" section with the contact details of the person you are referring.
3. Select the "Referral Type" from the dropdown menu in the "Referral Information" section to indicate whether the referral is internal or external.
4. Provide any additional project information in the "Project Information" section.
5. Add any additional notes about the referral in the "Consultant Notes" and "Company Notes" sections.
6. Review and submit the form once you have filled in all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Information**: This section is for entering the details of the company you are referring, such as the company name and URL.
	+ **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company you are referring.
	+ **Company URL**: Enter the URL of the company you are referring (optional).
* **Contact Information**: This section is for entering the contact details of the person you are referring, such as their name, email, and phone number.
	+ **Consultant Name**: Enter the name of the consultant you are referring.
	+ **Consultant Email**: Enter the email address of the consultant you are referring (note: this field is not checked for validity, so please ensure it is correct).
	+ **Consultant Phone**: Enter the phone number of the consultant you are referring (optional).
* **Referral Information**: This section is for selecting the type of referral, either internal or external.
	+ **Referral Type**: Select whether the referral is internal or external.
* **Project Information**: This section is for providing any additional project information.
	+ **Project Information**: Enter any additional details about the project (optional).
* **Additional Notes**: This section is for adding any additional notes about the referral.
	+ **Consultant Notes**: Enter any notes about the consultant you are referring.
	+ **Company Notes**: Enter any notes about the company you are referring.
* **Referral Date and Time**: This section is for entering the date and time of the referral.
	+ **Referral Date**: Enter the date of the referral (optional).
	+ **Referral Time**: Enter the time of the referral (optional).
* **Consultant Information**: This section is for entering the details of the consultant, such as their company and phone number.
	+ **Consultant Company**: Enter the company of the consultant you are referring (optional).
	+ **Consultant Phone**: Enter the phone number of the consultant you are referring (optional).
* **Consultant Email**: This field is already covered under "Contact Information" and is not necessary to be filled here.
