# Financial Consulting Efficiency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information from consultants and clients on the efficiency and effectiveness of financial consulting projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your consultant's name in the **Consultant Name** field.
2. Enter the consultant's title in the **Consultant Title** field (if applicable).
3. Enter the client's name in the **Client Name** field.
4. Enter the project duration in the **Project Duration** field (e.g., number of days).
5. Enter any feedback or comments from the client in the **Client Feedback** field.
6. Select one or more outcomes from the **Project Outcome** dropdown menu (e.g., "Outcome 1", "Outcome 2", "Outcome 3", "Outcome 4").
7. Select the current project status from the **Project Status** dropdown menu (e.g., "Active", "Inactive", "Archived").
8. Enter a number to indicate the client's satisfaction level on a scale of 1-5 in the **Client Satisfaction** field.
9. Enter a number to indicate your satisfaction level as a consultant on a scale of 1-5 in the **Consultant Satisfaction** field.
10. Enter the date the project was completed in the **Date Completed** field (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
11. Enter the time the project was completed in the **Time Completed** field (HH:MM format).
12. Enter the consultant's email address in the **Email** field.
13. Enter the consultant's phone number in the **Phone** field (optional).
14. Enter any additional notes or comments in the **Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consultant Name**: Enter your name as a consultant.
* **Consultant Title**: Enter your title or position as a consultant, if applicable.
* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client who received the consulting service.
* **Project Duration**: Enter the number of days, weeks, or months the project took to complete.
* **Client Feedback**: Enter any comments or feedback from the client on the project.
* **Project Outcome**: Select one or more outcomes that best describe the project's result (e.g., "Outcome 1", "Outcome 2", "Outcome 3", "Outcome 4").
* **Project Status**: Select the current status of the project (e.g., "Active", "Inactive", "Archived").
* **Client Satisfaction**: Enter a number between 1 and 5 to indicate the client's satisfaction level.
* **Consultant Satisfaction**: Enter a number between 1 and 5 to indicate your satisfaction level as a consultant.
* **Date Completed**: Enter the date the project was completed in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time Completed**: Enter the time the project was completed in HH:MM format.
* **Email**: Enter your email address as a consultant.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes on the project.
