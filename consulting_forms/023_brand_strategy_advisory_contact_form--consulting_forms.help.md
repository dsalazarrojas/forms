## # Brand Strategy Advisory Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals or organizations seeking brand strategy advisory services. The goal is to gather essential details that will help us understand their needs and provide tailored advice. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure we can effectively assist you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form completely, but do not worry about errors, as you can always correct them later.
2. Make sure to use your real name and email for the "Contact name" and "Contact email" fields, respectively.
3. If you want to receive our advisory services, select the "Brand positioning", "Messaging", "Visual identity", and/or "Go to market" options from the "Advisory focus" field.
4. You can leave the "Budget estimate" field blank if you're unsure about the budget.
5. Choose your preferred contact method (email, phone, or video call) from the "Preferred contact method" field.
6. If you have any specific dates in mind for the project, enter them in the "Project timeline" field.
7. Finally, use the "Additional notes" field to share any other relevant information that may aid in providing the best advice.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact name** (Required): Enter your real name, which will be used to address our communication.
* **Contact email** (Required): Enter your valid email address, which we will use for scheduling and communication.
* **Company name** (Optional): If you're representing an organization, provide its name for reference.
* **Advisory focus** (Required): Select all areas where you need advisory services. You can choose one or more options.
	+ **Brand positioning**: We will help you develop a unique positioning for your brand.
	+ **Messaging**: We will assist you in crafting compelling messages for your brand.
	+ **Visual identity**: We will help you create a visual identity that reflects your brand's personality.
	+ **Go to market**: We will aid you in developing a strategy to effectively enter the market.
* **Budget estimate** (Optional): If you have an estimated budget in mind, enter it to help us provide more accurate advice.
* **Preferred contact method** (Optional): Choose how you'd like to be contacted (email, phone, or video call).
* **Project timeline** (Optional): Enter your preferred project start and end dates.
* **Additional notes** (Optional): Share any other information that may be helpful for us to provide the best advice.
## Tips
* Please ensure the information provided is accurate to help us understand your needs correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
