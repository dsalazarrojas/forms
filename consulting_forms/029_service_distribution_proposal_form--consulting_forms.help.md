# Service Distribution Proposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Distribution Proposal Form is a tool used to collect information from businesses that are requesting a service distribution proposal. This form is used to gather details about the business to help evaluate their needs and preferences for services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Service Distribution Proposal Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the `business_name` field with your company name.
2. Provide a brief description of your business in the `description` field.
3. Select your services preferences in the `services` field. You can choose multiple services if needed.
4. Indicate your contact details in the `contact_details` field, which will help us get in touch with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name (business_name)**: Enter your company name as it appears on your business cards or website.
* **Description (description)**: Briefly describe your business, including your industry, products or services, and unique selling points.
* **Services (services)**: Select one or more services that you are interested in learning more about. Options include:
	+ Yes (you are interested in this service)
	+ No (you are not interested in this service)
* **Contact Details (contact_details)**: Indicate if you would like to be contacted by us for further discussion. Options include:
	+ Yes (you would like to be contacted)
	+ No (you do not want to be contacted)

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately, as this will help us understand your business needs better.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask and we will do our best to assist you.
* You may submit one proposal per business; if you need to make changes or update your proposal, please contact us directly.
