# Practice Growth Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from leads about their practice growth goals and other relevant details for the consultation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Lead Details** section with the lead's name, email, and phone number.
2. In the **Practice Goals** section, describe the lead's current practice growth goals.
3. Choose a suitable **Consultation Schedule** date and time for the lead's consultation.
4. Select the relevant **Consultant(s)** who will be involved in the consultation from the options provided.
5. Choose the lead's **Practice Info** from the available options.
6. If necessary, add a **Follow Up** time and date for further consultations.
7. Add any **Notes** about the lead's concerns or requirements.
8. Enter the **Lead Owner's** email address for record-keeping purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lead Details** (text, required): This is the section for filling in the lead's name, email, and phone number.
* **Practice Goals** (text, required): Describe the lead's current practice growth goals in brief.
* **Consultation Schedule** (date, required): Choose a suitable date and time for the lead's consultation.
* **Consultant Info** (select_multiple, required): Select the relevant **Consultant(s)** who will be involved in the consultation from the options provided.
* **Practice Info** (select_one, required): Choose the lead's **Practice Info** from the available options.
* **Follow Up** (time, optional): If necessary, add a **Follow Up** time and date for further consultations.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Add any **Notes** about the lead's concerns or requirements.
* **Lead Owner** (email, optional): Enter the **Lead Owner's** email address for record-keeping purposes.
* **Lead Phone** (text, optional): Fill in the lead's phone number for future contacts.
