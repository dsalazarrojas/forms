# Content Strategy Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Content Strategy Agreement form is designed to capture the key elements of a content strategy project, ensuring both the client and the content strategist or agency have a clear understanding of the project's scope, objectives, and timeline.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by reviewing the project's purpose and objectives, ensuring they are accurately captured in the "Agreement Overview" section (Page 1).
2.  Fill in the client's company name in the "Client Company Name" field (Page 2).
3.  Enter the name of the lead content strategist or agency in the "Lead Content Strategist/Agency Name" field (Page 3).
4.  Specify the effective date of the agreement in the "Agreement Effective Date" field (Page 4).
5.  Select the primary deliverables for the project in the "Core Strategy Deliverables" field (Page 6).
6.  Choose the target channels for the strategy in the "Target Channels for Strategy" field (Page 7).
7.  Set the total strategy fee in the "Total Strategy Fee (USD)" field (Page 9).
8.  Select the billing structure for the project in the "Billing Structure" field (Page 10).
9.  Enter the date for the final strategy delivery in the "Final Strategy Delivery Date" field (Page 13).
10.  Set the maximum number of days the client has to approve drafts in the "Client Approval Turnaround (Days)" field (Page 14).
11.  Sign the form digitally by entering your full name in the "Client Digital Signature" and "Strategist Digital Signature" fields (Pages 16 and 17).
12.  If necessary, add any special terms or conditions in the "Special Terms or Conditions" field (Page 18).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Agreement Overview --** (Page 1): This section provides a brief overview of the agreement, including the definition of parties and the project's primary objectives.
* **Client Company Name (Page 2)**: Enter your company's official name as it appears in public records.
* **Lead Content Strategist/Agency Name (Page 3)**: Enter the name of the individual or organization providing the content strategy services.
* **Agreement Effective Date (Page 4)**: Enter the date the agreement becomes effective.
* **-- Scope of Services --** (Page 5): This section provides a detailed description of the content strategy services to be delivered.
* **Core Strategy Deliverables (Page 6)**: Select the primary items to be delivered, such as content audits and competitor gap analyses.
* **Target Channels for Strategy (Page 7)**: Select the channels where the content strategy will be implemented.
* **-- Financial Terms --** (Page 8): This section outlines the payment terms and fees for the project.
* **Total Strategy Fee (USD) (Page 9)**: Enter the total fee for the project in USD.
* **Billing Structure (Page 10)**: Select the payment structure for the project, such as upfront payment or milestone-based payments.
* **-- Timeline and Milestones --** (Page 11): This section outlines key dates for project phases.
* **Phase 1 (Audit) Completion Date (Page 12)**: Enter the date for completing the project's audit phase.
* **Final Strategy Delivery Date (Page 13)**: Enter the date for delivering the final strategy.
* **Client Approval Turnaround (Days) (Page 14)**: Set the maximum number of days the client has to approve drafts.
* **-- Execution --** (Page 15): This section is for digital signing of the strategy agreement.
* **Client Digital Signature (Page 16)**: Enter your full name for digital signing.
* **Strategist Digital Signature (Page 17)**: Enter the name of the content strategist or agency providing the services for digital signing.
* **Special Terms or Conditions (Page 18)**: Add any special terms or conditions, if necessary.
