## Global Expansion Consulting Partner Contact Form - Help Guide

### Purpose
The Global Expansion Consulting Partner Contact Form is designed to collect contact information and project details from potential partners and clients for global expansion projects. This form helps to establish a connection between the consulting firm and the partner organization, ensuring a smooth collaboration process.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. Enter your **Email Address** in the "Email" field. Make sure it is a valid email address.
3. Select your **Job Title** from the drop-down menu in the "Job Title" field.
4. Enter your **Partner Organization** in the "Partner Organization" field.
5. Choose the **Inquiry Type** that best describes the reason for your contact, such as "Consulting", "Expansion", "Partnership", or "Other".
6. Select the **Project Location** where you are looking to expand or collaborate, such as "Americas", "EMEA", or "APAC".
7. Provide a brief **Description** of the project or your inquiry in the "Description" field.
8. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone" field, if applicable.
9. Select the **Date** you are looking to start or complete the project.
10. Choose the **Time** of day you are available for the project, if applicable.
11. Add any additional **Note** or comment about your inquiry.
12. Select the **Assigned Agent** who will handle your inquiry, such as "chatjimmy" or "Other".
13. Choose the **Lead Status** of your project, such as "Open", "Closed", or "In Progress".
14. Leave the **Created At** and **Updated At** fields blank, as these are automatically populated.
15. Enter the **Created By** field, if you are the one creating the form.
16. Enter the **Assigned To** field, if applicable.
17. Select the **Tags** that best describe your project, such as "Yes" or "No".
18. Finally, fill in the **Custom Fields 1-6** with any additional information you would like to provide.

### Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Select your job title from the drop-down menu.
* **Partner Organization** (`partner_organization`, text, required): Enter your partner organization.
* **Inquiry Type** (`inquiry_type`, select_one, required): Choose the reason for your contact, such as "Consulting", "Expansion", "Partnership", or "Other".
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, select_multiple, required): Select the region where you are looking to expand or collaborate, such as "Americas", "EMEA", or "APAC".
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the project or your inquiry.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date you are looking to start or complete the project.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Choose the time of day you are available for the project.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments about your inquiry.
* **Assigned Agent** (`assigned_agent`, select_one, required): Select the agent who will handle your inquiry.
* **Lead Status** (`lead_status`, select_multiple, required): Choose the status of your project, such as "Open", "Closed", or "In Progress".
* **Created At** (`created_at`, date, optional): This field is automatically populated.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, date, optional): This field is automatically populated.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who created the form.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person assigned to the project, if applicable.
* **Tags** (`tags`, select_multiple, optional): Select the tags that best describe your project, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Custom Field 1**, **2**, **3**, **4**, **5**, **6** (`custom_field_1`, `custom_field_2`, `custom_field_3`, `custom_field_4`, `custom_field_5`, `custom_field_6`, text, optional): Fill in any additional custom fields with relevant information.
