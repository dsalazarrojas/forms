# Telemedicine Consultant Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telemedicine Consultant Evaluation Form is used to collect feedback and ratings from patients who have participated in telemedicine consultations. This helps us understand the quality of service provided by our consultants and make improvements accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. Use the provided guidance for each field to ensure you complete the form correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Consultant Evaluation (id: 1)** (`text`, required: false): Describe your experience with the consultant, including their performance, communication, and overall service. Be as detailed as possible in your evaluation.
* **Consultant Name (id: 2)** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name of the consultant you interacted with.
* **Rating (id: 3)** (`select_one`, required: false): Rate the consultant's performance on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Feedback (id: 4)** (`text`, required: false): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience with the consultant.
* **Email (id: 5)** (`email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address to ensure we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone Number (id: 6)** (`text`, required: false): Enter your contact phone number if you'd like us to follow up with you.
* **Has Visited (id: 7)** (`select_one`, required: false): Check 'Yes' if you have visited the consultant before, 'No' otherwise.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate in your evaluation and feedback.
- Use a valid email address to ensure we can contact you if needed.
- If you have any concerns or issues, please provide detailed feedback.
