# Home Buyer Consultation Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from home buyers to gather a comprehensive understanding of their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your first name and last name in the corresponding text fields.
2. Enter your email address to stay in touch with our services.
3. Enter your phone number for future communication.
4. Select the type of home you are looking for (e.g., Home, Apartment, Condo, etc.).
5. Indicate your current home status (e.g., Owned, Renting, Buying, etc.).
6. Choose the reason you are selling or buying your home (e.g., Selling, Buying, Relocating, etc.).
7. Enter the date you expect to move into your new home.
8. Provide the location you prefer to live in.
9. Select the budget range for your new home.
10. Indicate the size of your family.
11. Choose the features you are looking for in your new home (e.g., Pool, Parking, Balcony, etc.).
12. Enter the address of your current or desired home.
13. Enter the city of your current or desired home.
14. Enter the province of your current or desired home.
15. Enter the postal code of your current or desired home.
16. Enter the number of the building where you live or plan to live.
17. Enter the floor of your current or desired home.
18. Enter the unit number of your current or desired home.
19. Select the type of building you are looking for (e.g., Single, Semi-detached, Townhouse, etc.).
20. Indicate the age of the building.
21. Select the neighborhood features you are looking for (e.g., Park, School, Grocery, etc.).
22. Choose your preferred commute time.
23. Select the type of commute you prefer (e.g., Car, Public Transit, Walk, etc.).
24. Indicate your preferred work hours.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Buyer Consultation** (`home_buyer_consultation`, text, Optional): Please provide a brief description of your home buying needs and preferences.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, Optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, Optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address to stay in touch with our services.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number for future communication.
* **Home Type** (`home_type`, select_one, Optional): Select the type of home you are looking for (e.g., Home, Apartment, Condo, etc.).
* **Home Status** (`home_status`, select_multiple, Optional): Indicate your current home status (e.g., Owned, Renting, Buying, etc.).
* **Reason for Selling/Buying** (`reason_for_selling`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose the reason you are selling or buying your home (e.g., Selling, Buying, Relocating, etc.).
* **Expected Move-in Date** (`expected_move_in_date`, date, Optional): Enter the date you expect to move into your new home.
* **Preferred Location** (`preferred_location`, text, Optional): Provide the location you prefer to live in.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_multiple, Optional): Select the budget range for your new home (e.g., Less than $0, $0-100k, etc.).
* **Family Size** (`family_size`, select_one, Optional): Indicate the size of your family (e.g., Single, Couple, Family, etc.).
* **Home Type Features** (`home_type_features`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose the features you are looking for in your new home (e.g., Pool, Parking, Balcony, etc.).
* **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, Optional): Enter the address of your current or desired home.
* **Home City** (`home_city`, text, Optional): Enter the city of your current or desired home.
* **Home Province** (`home_province`, text, Optional): Enter the province of your current or desired home.
* **Home Postal Code** (`home_postal_code`, text, Optional): Enter the postal code of your current or desired home.
* **Home Number** (`home_number`, text, Optional): Enter the number of the building where you live or plan to live.
* **Home Floor** (`home_floor`, number, Optional): Enter the floor of your current or desired home.
* **Home Unit** (`home_unit`, text, Optional): Enter the unit number of your current or desired home.
* **Home Building Type** (`home_building_type`, select_one, Optional): Select the type of building you are looking for (e.g., Single, Semi-detached, Townhouse, etc.).
* **Home Age of Building** (`home_age_of_building`, select_one, Optional): Indicate the age of the building (e.g., Less than 5, 5-10, etc.).
* **Home Neighborhood Features** (`home_neighborhood_features`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose the neighborhood features you are looking for (e.g., Park, School, Grocery, etc.).
* **Preferred Commute Time** (`preferred_commute_time`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose your preferred commute time (e.g., Less than 10, 10-20, etc.).
* **Preferred Commute Type** (`preferred_commute_type`, select_multiple, Optional): Select the type of commute you prefer (e.g., Car, Public Transit, Walk, etc.).
* **Preferred Work Hours** (`preferred_work_hours`, select_multiple, Optional): Indicate your preferred work hours (e.g., Less than 4, 4-6, etc.).
