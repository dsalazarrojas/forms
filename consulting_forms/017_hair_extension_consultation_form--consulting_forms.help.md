# Hair Extension Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers about their hair care needs, habits, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select the relevant option from the "Hair Care Needs" dropdown list.
2. Choose the frequency at which you care for your hair.
3. Select the type of procedure you are interested in.
4. Add any additional hair care notes or preferences.
5. Enter your email address (this will allow us to respond to your inquiry).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Hair Care Needs
* **Hair Loss** (`Hair Loss`, `select_one`, required: false): Select this option if you are experiencing hair loss or thinning.
* **Dandruff** (`Dandruff`, `select_one`, required: false): Select this option if you are experiencing dandruff or itchy skin on your scalp.
* **Itchy Scalp** (`Itchy Scalp`, `select_one`, required: false): Select this option if you are experiencing an itchy scalp.
* **Other** (`Other`, `select_one`, required: false): Select this option if you have any other hair care needs or concerns not listed.

### Hair Care Frequency
* **Daily** (`Daily`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select this option if you care for your hair daily.
* **Weekly** (`Weekly`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select this option if you care for your hair weekly.
* **Monthly** (`Monthly`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select this option if you care for your hair monthly.
* **Quarterly** (`Quarterly`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select this option if you care for your hair quarterly.

### Procedure Type
* **Hair Dyeing** (`Hair Dyeing`, `select_one`, required: false): Select this option if you are interested in hair dyeing or coloring.
* **Hair Cutting** (`Hair Cutting`, `select_one`, required: false): Select this option if you are interested in hair cutting or trimming.
* **Hair Styling** (`Hair Styling`, `select_one`, required: false): Select this option if you are interested in hair styling or grooming.

### Hair Care Notes
* **Hair Care Notes** (`Hair Care Notes`, `note`, required: false): This is a free text field where you can add any additional hair care notes or preferences.

### Customer Email
* **Customer Email** (`Customer Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address to allow us to respond to your inquiry.

## Tips
* Make sure to select all relevant options that apply to your hair care needs.
* Be as specific as possible when providing additional hair care notes.
* Enter a valid email address to ensure we can respond to your inquiry.
