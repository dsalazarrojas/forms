# Community Support Service Consultation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals seeking consultation services from our Community Support Service. The goal is to provide personalized support and guidance to address various needs and challenges that individuals and families may face.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through each page carefully to understand the questions and fields.
2. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly, as your responses will help us better understand your needs.
3. Select the correct options from the dropdown menus, and fill in text fields with accurate information.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Client Full Name, Text, Required): This is the first page of the form, where you will provide your full name. Please enter your first and last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (Client Email, Email, Required): Enter your primary contact email address. This is how we will communicate with you regarding your consultation.
* **Phone Number** (Client Phone, Text, Required): Provide your best contact phone number. This is how we will reach you if needed.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (Preferred Contact Method, Select One, Required): Choose how you would like to be contacted: Email, Phone Call, Text Message, or Video Call.
* **Household Size** (Household Size, Number, Required): Enter the number of people living in your household.
* **Do You Have Children Under 18?** (Has Children, Select One, Required): Answer "True" if you have children under the age of 18 living in your household. If not, select "False".
* **Number of Children Under 18** (Children Count, Number, Optional): If you answered "True" to the previous question, enter the number of children under 18 in your household.
* **Service Needed** (Service Needed, Select One, Required): Select the primary reason you are seeking consultation services.
* **Additional Services** (Additional Services, Select Multiple, Optional): If applicable, select all services that you are interested in.
* **Urgency Level** (Urgency Level, Select One, Required): Choose the level of urgency for your situation: Low, Medium, High, or Critical.
* **Current Challenges** (Current Challenges, Text, Required): Briefly describe the challenges that brought you to seek consultation.
* **Previous Assistance** (Previous Assistance, Select One, Required): Indicate if you have received similar assistance before: No, First Time, Yes - from this Organization, or Yes - from Another Organization.
* **Previous Assistance Details** (Previous Assistance Details, Text, Optional): If you answered "Yes" to the previous question, provide details about the assistance you received.
* **Approximate Household Income** (Income Level, Select One, Optional): Select an estimate of your household income for eligibility determination purposes.
* **Employment Status** (Employment Status, Select One, Required): Choose your current employment status.
* **Health Insurance Status** (Insurance Status, Select One, Optional): Indicate your current health insurance status.
* **Preferred Language** (Preferred Language, Select One, Required): Choose the language you would like to communicate in for your consultation.
* **Need Interpreter** (Needs Interpreter, Select One, Required): Answer "True" if you need language assistance.
* **Preferred Appointment Time** (Preferred Appointment Time, Select One, Required): Select the time of day that works best for your consultation.
* **Preferred Days** (Preferred Days, Select Multiple, Required): Choose all the days that you are available for your consultation.
* **Consultation Format** (Consultation Format, Select One, Required): Select how you would like to meet for your consultation.
* **Accessibility Needs** (Accessibility Needs, Text, Optional): If you have any accessibility needs, please describe them.
* **Referral Source** (Referral Source, Select One, Required): Indicate how you learned about our services.
* **Consent to Information Sharing** (Consent Information Sharing, Select One, Required): Choose if you consent to sharing information with partner agencies for coordinated care.
* **Request Date** (Request Date, Date, Required): Enter today's date.

This form will help us better understand your situation and provide you with tailored support and guidance. Thank you for taking the time to fill out this form. 

Please review your answers before submitting. If you need help with any questions or have concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
