# HR Advisory Consultation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for HR advisory consultation requests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the form below with your details and requests for HR advisory consultation.
2. Select your preferred contact method (HR, HR Manager, or Other) to ensure that someone from our HR department can reach out to you for further consultation.
3. Select any other relevant contacts (if applicable) to whom you would like to receive the follow-up.
4. Please indicate your preferred date and time for the consultation.
5. In the `key_details` field, provide a brief summary of the consultation topic and any other relevant information you think would be helpful for us to understand your request.
6. Finally, in the `appointment_request` field, please specify what specifically you are requesting from the HR consultation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user**:
	+ This is the field where you will provide your name and contact information.
* **preferred_time**:
	+ This field is for indicating your availability for the consultation. Please note that it is not mandatory.
* **appointment_request**:
	+ Use this field to specify what you are seeking from the HR consultation. This could be any HR-related matter, such as guidance on policy implementation, employee conflict resolution, or performance management.
* **key_details**:
	+ Provide a brief summary of the consultation topic and any other relevant information that you think would help us understand your request.
* **contact**:
	+ Choose how you would like to be contacted (HR, HR Manager, or Other) for follow-up purposes. Please select your preferred option to ensure we can reach out to you effectively.
* **follow_up**:
	+ Select any other contacts to whom you would like to receive follow-up emails about your consultation request.
* **preferred_date**:
	+ Indicate your preferred date for the consultation. This is also not mandatory.
* **contact**:
	+ Select your preferred contact for the follow-up. Please choose HR for direct communication or HR Manager for someone from the HR team.
* **follow_up**:
	+ Select other relevant contacts for follow-up communication.
