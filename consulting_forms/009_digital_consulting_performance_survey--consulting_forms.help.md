# digital_consulting_performance_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the performance of digital consulting services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intro** (`intro_label`, note, optional): This is a text box where you can provide any additional information or context about your experience with the digital consulting services.
* **Questions** (`questions_label`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the questions that apply to your experience. You can choose "Yes" for questions that were positively addressed, and "No" for questions that were not addressed or were not satisfactory.
* **Ratings** (`ratings_label`, select_one, optional): Please provide a rating for the services provided. You can choose "Good", "Fair", or "No" to indicate the quality of service.
* **Comments** (`comments_label`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback about your experience, please provide them in this text box.
* **Email** (`email_label`, email, optional): Please provide your email address if you would like to be contacted for follow-up or to receive any additional information about our services.
* **Phone** (`phone_label`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number.

## Tips
* Please be as specific and detailed as possible in your answers.
* Remember to provide your email and phone number if you would like to be contacted for follow-up.
* If you have any additional feedback or comments, please provide them in the comments field.
