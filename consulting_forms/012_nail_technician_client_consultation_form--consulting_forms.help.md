# Nail Technician Client Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from you about your nail care preferences and medical conditions to ensure that the nail technician can provide you with the best possible service during your visit.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form in its entirety to provide the technician with the necessary information.
2. Answer each question to the best of your abilities, and do not hesitate to ask if you are unsure about any of the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`nail_technician_client_consultation_form_page_1`, text, required):
This field is to collect your first name. Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`nail_technician_client_consultation_form_page_2`, text, required):
This field is to collect your last name. Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`nail_technician_client_consultation_form_page_3`, email, required):
This field is to collect your email address. Please enter your email address that the technician can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`nail_technician_client_consultation_form_page_4`, text, required):
This field is to collect your phone number. Please enter your phone number that the technician can use to contact you.
* **Nail Care Preferences** (`nail_technician_client_consultation_form_page_5`, select_one, required):
This field is to collect your preference for nail care. Please select either "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have any nail care preferences.
* **Brief Note about Your Nail Care Preferences** (`nail_technician_client_consultation_form_page_6`, note, required):
This field is to collect any additional information about your nail care preferences.
* **First Visit** (`nail_technician_client_consultation_form_page_7`, date, required):
This field is to collect the date of your first visit to the technician. Please use the date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Next Visit** (`nail_technician_client_consultation_form_page_8`, date):
This field is to collect the date of your next visit to the technician. Please use the date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **First Visit Time** (`nail_technician_client_consultation_form_page_9`, time):
This field is to collect the time of your first visit to the technician. Please use the 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
* **Next Visit Time** (`nail_technician_client_consultation_form_page_10`, time):
This field is to collect the time of your next visit to the technician. Please use the 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
* **Special Instructions** (`nail_technician_client_consultation_form_page_11`, checkbox, required):
This field is to collect any special instructions or requirements you have for your visit. Please select one or more options that apply to you.
* **Brief Note about Your Visit** (`nail_technician_client_consultation_form_page_12`, note, required):
This field is to collect any additional information about your visit.
* **First Visit Status** (`nail_technician_client_consultation_form_page_13`, select_one, required):
This field is to indicate whether you have a first visit scheduled. Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether this is your first visit.
* **Visit Frequency** (`nail_technician_client_consultation_form_page_14`, select_multiple, required):
This field is to collect how often you visit the technician. Please select one or more options that apply to you.
* **Brief Details about Your Visit History** (`nail_technician_client_consultation_form_page_15`, note, required):
This field is to collect any additional information about your visit history.
* **Nail Issues** (`nail_technician_client_consultation_form_page_16`, select_multiple, required):
This field is to collect any issues you have with your nails. Please select one or more options that apply to you.
* **Medical Conditions** (`nail_technician_client_consultation_form_page_17`, text):
This field is to collect any medical conditions you have. Please enter your medical conditions, if any.
* **Nail Allergies/Sensitivities** (`nail_technician_client_consultation_form_page_18`, checkbox):
This field is to collect any nail allergies or sensitivities you have. Please select one or more options that apply to you.

## Tips
* Please take your time to complete the form carefully and accurately.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please don't hesitate to ask your technician.
* This form is a one-time submission, and you can update it after your first visit if needed.
