# Brand Strategy Consulting Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information for brand strategy consulting quotations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill in your contact information**: Please provide your full name, email address, and phone number so we can reach you for further communication.
2. **Select your industry and company stage**: Choose the correct industry and company stage from the provided options.
3. **Select services needed**: Choose all the services you require for your project.
4. **Describe the project scope**: Explain the scope of work you need help with.
5. **Provide project details**: Describe your current brand challenges, project goals, target audience, and timeline expectations.
6. **Provide budget and decision-making information**: Choose your expected budget and the number of decision-makers involved.
7. **Optional sections**: If you have worked with brand consultants before, who are your main competitors, and how did you find us, please select the relevant options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Full Name (1)** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): This field is used to capture your full name so we can contact you for further communication.
* **Email Address (2)** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): This field is used to send the quote to you.
* **Phone Number (3)** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): This field is used to contact you for further discussions.
* **Company Name (4)** (`company_name`, `text`, required): This field is used to capture your company's legal name.
* **Company Website (5)** (`company_website`, `text`, optional): This field is optional and is used to capture your company's website URL.
* **Industry (6)** (`industry`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to select the industry your company operates in.
* **Company Stage (7)** (`company_stage`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to select the stage of your company.
* **Services Needed (8)** (`services_needed`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is used to select all the services you require for your project.
* **Project Scope (9)** (`project_scope`, `text`, required): This field is used to describe the scope of work required for your project.
* **Current Brand Challenges (10)** (`current_brand_challenges`, `text`, required): This field is used to describe the challenges your current brand is facing.
* **Project Goals (11)** (`project_goals`, `text`, required): This field is used to describe what you want to achieve with this project.
* **Target Audience (12)** (`target_audience`, `text`, required): This field is used to describe the target audience for your project.
* **Timeline Expectations (13)** (`timeline_expectations`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to select when you expect the project to be completed.
* **Budget Range (14)** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to select the expected budget for your project.
* **Number of Decision Makers (15)** (`decision_makers`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to select the number of decision-makers involved in your project.
* **Previous Consulting (16)** (`previous_consulting`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to select if you have worked with brand consultants before.
* **Main Competitors (17)** (`competitors`, `text`, optional): This field is optional and is used to capture your main competitors.
* **Preferred Start Date (18)** (`preferred_start_date`, `date`, optional): This field is optional and is used to select when you would like to begin the project.
* **Additional Requirements (19)** (`additional_requirements`, `text`, optional): This field is optional and is used to capture any special requirements or considerations.
* **How Did You Find Us (20)** (`referral_source`, `select_one`, optional): This field is optional and is used to select where you found our services.

## Tips
1. Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately.
2. If you have any special requirements or considerations, please mention them in the additional requirements field.
3. If you have worked with brand consultants before, please select the correct option.
4. If you have any questions or need further clarification, feel free to contact us.
