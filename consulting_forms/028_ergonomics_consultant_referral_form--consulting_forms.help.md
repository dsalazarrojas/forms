# ergonomics_consultant_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide information about a client or patient referred to our consulting services. It's essential to fill it out completely and accurately to enable us to best assist your client.


## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out all the required fields. 
2.  Select the appropriate option for the Referral Source from the available choices. 
3.  Enter all the requested contact details of the client and referring doctor. 
4.  Make sure to double-check your entries before submitting the form. 


## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details (1)**: Enter the contact information for the person filling out this form.
* **Medical History (2)**: Briefly describe the medical history of the client or patient. 
* **Referral Source (3)**: Select one of the options provided to indicate the source of the referral. 
    +   Client: The client or patient themselves has referred your company. 
    +   Healthcare Provider: A healthcare provider has referred your client or patient. 
    +   HR Department: The HR department has referred your client or patient.
* **Client Information (4)**: Enter any relevant client information, if applicable. 
* **Client Name (5)**: Enter the name of the client or patient being referred. 
* **Contact Person (6)**: Enter the name of the contact person at the client's location. 
* **Client Phone (7)**: Enter the phone number of the client or patient. 
* **Client Email (8)**: Enter the email address of the client or patient. 
* **Referring Doctor Info (9)**: Enter any relevant information about the referring doctor, if applicable. 
* **Doctor Name (10)**: Enter the name of the referring doctor. 
* **Doctor Title (11)**: Enter the title of the referring doctor. 
* **Doctor Phone (12)**: Enter the phone number of the referring doctor. 
* **Doctor Email (13)**: Enter the email address of the referring doctor. 

## Tips
* Please review the form carefully before submission to ensure all fields have been filled out correctly.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact us for clarification.
