# Business Growth Consulting Partnership Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for potential partners who are interested in collaborating on business growth consulting projects with us. Please complete this form to share your details and interests, so we can have an informed discussion about potential partnership opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the following questions to the best of your ability. Make sure to be as specific and accurate as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`partner_full_name`, text, **required**): Please enter your full name for us to address you correctly.
* **Professional Background / Expertise** (`partner_professional_background`, text, **required**): Briefly describe your professional background and areas of expertise. This should include relevant work experience, skills, or any other relevant information.
* **Partnership Interest Type** (`partnership_interest_category`, select_one, **required**): Select the primary nature of your interest (Referral Partner, Co-Consulting / Joint Venture, Strategic Alliance, Resource Sharing, White-Label Partnership).
* **Proposed Collaboration Area** (`collaboration_area_details`, text, **required**): Specify the area of collaboration you're interested in (e.g., marketing, finance, operations).
* **Relevant Experience in Similar Partnerships** (`relevant_partnership_experience`, text, **optional**): If you have experience in similar partnerships, please share it briefly.
* **Preferred Communication Channel** (`preferred_comms_channel`, select_one, **required**): Select how you'd like to be contacted initially (Email, Phone Call, LinkedIn Messaging, Video Meeting (Zoom/Teams)).
* **Partnership Contact Info** (`partner_contact_info`, text, **required**): Enter your contact email or phone number for easy communication.
* **Availability for Initial Meeting** (`initial_meeting_availability`, text, **required**): Indicate when you're generally available for a 30-minute sync.
* **Brief Partnership Proposal or Vision** (`shared_values_alignment_statement`, text, **required**): Share a brief statement on why you think this partnership would be mutually beneficial.
* **Date of Inquiry** (`partnership_inquiry_date`, date, **required**): Enter the date you're inquiring about this partnership opportunity.
* **Agreement to Non-Disclosure** (`agreement_to_nda_preliminary`, select_one, **required**): Confirm that you agree to treat all shared information as confidential.
