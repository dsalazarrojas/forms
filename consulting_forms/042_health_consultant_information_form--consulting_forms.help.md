# Health Consultant Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from clients or patients about their health consultant preferences, medical history, and contact information. It is designed to help us understand their needs and provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **User Information**: Please provide us with your basic contact information, including your name, address, and phone number.
2. **Medical History**: Please describe your medical history, including any allergies, medical conditions, or previous surgeries.
3. **Health Insurance**: Indicate if you have any health insurance coverage.
4. **Medical Specialties**: Select the medical specialties you are interested in or have experience with.
5. **Client Relationship**: Indicate if you have a pre-existing relationship with a healthcare professional or organization.
6. **Contact Information**: Let us know if you would like to be contacted by phone, email, or other means.
7. **Medical Certifications**: If applicable, list any relevant medical certifications or licenses you hold.
8. **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional information or comments, please include them here.
9. **Medical History 2-5**: Describe any other medical history or concerns you have.
