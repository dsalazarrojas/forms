# Automotive Consultant Estimate Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Automotive Consultant Estimate Form is a comprehensive guide for automotive service providers to estimate and provide quotes for various services related to vehicles. It helps consultants to gather essential information about the customer, their vehicle, and the desired services, ensuring that the estimates are accurate and complete.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Introduction**: Start by reading the introductory page, which provides a brief overview of the form and its purpose.
2. **Customer Information**: Fill in the customer's details, including their first name, last name, email address, phone number, and address (if applicable).
3. **Vehicle Information**: Enter the vehicle's details, such as the year, make, model, and mileage.
4. **Service Information**: Select the type of service required (e.g., diagnostic, repair, maintenance, inspection, consultation, or second opinion).
5. **Problem Description**: Describe the issue with the vehicle.
6. **Symptoms**: List any symptoms or conditions you've noticed.
7. **Service Type**: Choose the preferred method of communication for the estimate (phone, written, in-person inspection, or video consultation).
8. **Urgency**: Indicate how urgent the service is.
9. **Preferred Date and Time**: Enter your preferred date and time for the service.
10. **Budget Range**: Select the estimated budget range for the service.
11. **Parts Preference**: Specify your preference for original equipment manufacturer (OEM) parts or aftermarket parts.
12. **Consultant Information**: Leave this field for internal use by the consultant.
13. **Assigned Consultant**: Leave this field for internal use by the consultant.
14. **Consultation Fee**: Enter any applicable consultation fee.
15. **Diagnostic Fee**: Enter any applicable diagnostic fee.
16. **Estimate Validity**: Select the number of days the estimate is valid for.
17. **Labor Rate**: Enter the labor rate per hour.
18. **Estimated Hours**: Enter the estimated hours required for the service.
19. **Parts Estimate**: Enter the estimated cost of parts.
20. **Total Estimate**: Calculate the total cost of the service.
21. **Estimate Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments.
22. **Recommendations**: Suggest any necessary actions or services.
23. **Priority Level**: Indicate the level of urgency for the service.
24. **Follow-Up Needed**: Indicate if follow-up is required.
25. **Follow-Up Date**: Enter the date for follow-up if necessary.
26. **Customer Approval**: Approve or reject the estimate.
27. **Additional Comments**: Add any other notes or comments.
28. **Consultant Signature**: Leave this field for internal use by the consultant.
29. **Estimate Date**: Enter the date of the estimate.
