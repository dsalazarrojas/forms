# Consultant Onboarding Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on a consultant's onboarding experience, allowing for evaluation and improvement of the onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. Enter the date you started working with the client or organization.
3. Rate how clear your role and expectations were during onboarding.
4. Rate the level of support provided during onboarding.
5. If necessary, provide any additional training needs or requests.
6. If you have any feedback on the onboarding process, please include it in the text field provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consultant name** (`consultant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Start date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you started working with the client or organization.
* **Clarity of role** (`clarity_of_role`, `select_one`, required): Rate how clear your role and expectations were during onboarding, with options:
	+ Very clear
	+ Somewhat clear
	+ Unclear
* **Onboarding support** (`onboarding_support`, `select_one`, required): Rate the level of support provided during onboarding, with options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Training needs** (`training_needs`, `text`, required): If necessary, provide any additional training needs or requests.
* **Feedback on onboarding process** (`feedback_on_process`, `text`, required): If you have any feedback on the onboarding process, please include it in this text field.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer these questions honestly and thoroughly, as your feedback is invaluable in helping us improve the onboarding process.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas on how to improve the onboarding process, please include them in the "Feedback on onboarding process" field.
