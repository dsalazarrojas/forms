# equal-pay-calculator-for-collaborations - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equal Pay Calculator for Collaborations form is used to gather information about a collaboration project, including project details, roles and amounts, payment methods, and submission details. This form helps ensure equal pay among collaborators by providing a standardized and organized way to collect and record collaboration data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Project Name** in the first field. This is the name of the project you are submitting.
2. Select the roles involved in the project and whether they are Yes or No.
3. Enter the amount of hours worked or time allocated for each role.
4. Determine the **Total Amount** of pay for the collaboration.
5. Choose the payment method for the collaboration.
6. Enter the email address and phone number of the **Preferred Contact** for the collaboration.
7. Enter a brief description of any additional notes about the collaboration in the **Notes** field (optional).
8. Enter the email address and phone number of the person responsible for **Submission** (optional).
9. Specify the date and time when the collaboration will be submitted.
10. Choose the submission method for the collaboration.
11. Enter any additional comments or notes for the submission in the **Submission Notes** field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name** (project_name): Enter the name of the project you are submitting. This will help identify the project and its associated collaboration.
* **Project Roles** (roles): Select the roles involved in the project. This helps to determine the number of collaborators and their respective amounts.
* **Roles Amounts** (roles_amounts): Enter the amount of hours worked or time allocated for each role. This ensures equal pay among collaborators.
* **Total Amount** (total_amount): Enter the total amount of pay for the collaboration. This field is automatically calculated based on the roles and amounts entered.
* **Pay Method** (pay_method): Select the payment method for the collaboration.
* **Collaborators** (collaborators): Enter the email address and phone number of each collaborator involved in the project. This helps keep all collaborators informed.
* **Notes** (notes): Enter any additional comments or notes about the collaboration.
* **Preferred Contact Email** (preferred_email): Enter the email address of the preferred contact person for the collaboration.
* **Preferred Contact Phone** (preferred_phone): Enter the phone number of the preferred contact person for the collaboration.
* **Submission Email** (submission_email): Enter the email address of the person responsible for submission (optional).
* **Submission Phone** (submission_phone): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for submission (optional).
* **Submission Date** (submission_date): Enter the date when the collaboration will be submitted.
* **Submission Time** (submission_time): Enter the time when the collaboration will be submitted.
* **Submission Method** (submission_method): Select the submission method for the collaboration.
* **Submission Notes** (submission_note): Enter any additional comments or notes for the submission.
