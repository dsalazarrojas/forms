# Operational Resilience Consulting Project Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Operational Resilience Consulting Project Application Form is a tool for our consultants and clients to submit and apply for new project opportunities. It helps us understand their project details, needs, and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the project details and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Select the project type that best describes the nature of your project.
3. Choose the type of clients you are representing (individual, business, or organization).
4. Fill in your name and email address (if you would like to be contacted).
5. Provide your contact phone number and date/time of availability.
6. Enter your project address (optional).
7. Select your city and state (if applicable).
8. Enter your zip code.
9. Write a brief message to provide more context about your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details**: This field is meant for you to describe your project in a few words. Please be concise and provide any relevant details about the project.
* **Project Type**: Select the type of project you are applying for. Please choose from the options provided to best describe the nature of your project.
* **Client Type**: Indicate the type of client you are representing (individual, business, or organization).
* **Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to appear in our system. This is optional.
* **Email**: If you would like to be contacted, please provide your email address.
* **Phone**: Provide your contact phone number. This will help us reach out to you for further discussion.
* **Date**: Indicate your availability date for further discussion or project start date.
* **Time**: If applicable, enter the time you are available for further discussion or project start time.
* **Address**: Enter your project address (if applicable).
* **City**: If your project address is in a city, please select your city.
* **State**: If your project address is in a state, please select your state.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Message**: Provide a brief message with more details about your project, if needed.

Note: This form is for information gathering purposes only. Please fill in the required fields, and if any field is required, an asterisk (\*) will be displayed next to the label.
