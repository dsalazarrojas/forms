# AI Strategy Development Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from organizations about their AI strategy development needs. It is meant to provide a structured approach to identifying and defining the requirements for a successful AI strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the organization context section, providing information about your organization and its current objectives.
2. Move on to the next section, where you will provide the name of the contact person leading the strategy development process.
3. In the current objectives section, list the top three business objectives you are currently working to achieve.
4. Next, select the primary pain points that the AI strategy should address.
5. In the success criteria section, describe how you will measure the success of the AI strategy.
6. If applicable, provide any constraints that need to be considered when developing the AI strategy.
7. Choose the timeline priority for the delivery of the AI strategy.
8. If necessary, select the governance and oversight needs for the AI strategy.
9. Finally, provide any additional notes or comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Context -- (Organization Context)**: This field is meant to capture a brief description of your organization and its current objectives.
* **Organization Name** (Organization name): Provide the name of your organization.
* **Contact Person** (Contact person): Enter the name of the person leading the strategy development process.
* **Current Objectives** (Current business objectives): List the top three business objectives you are currently working to achieve.
* **Primary Pain Points AI Should Address** (Primary pain points AI should address): Select all the pain points that the AI strategy should address.
	+ Operational efficiency
	+ Customer experience
	+ Revenue growth
	+ Risk management
* **Success Criteria for AI Strategy** (Success criteria for AI strategy): Describe how you will measure the success of the AI strategy.
* **Constraints to Consider** (Constraints to consider): If applicable, provide any constraints that need to be considered when developing the AI strategy.
* **Timeline Priority for Delivery** (Timeline priority for delivery): Choose the timeline priority for the delivery of the AI strategy.
	+ Immediate (1-3 months)
	+ Short term (3-6 months)
	+ Medium term (6-12 months)
	+ Long term (12+ months)
* **Governance and Oversight Needs** (Governance and oversight needs): If necessary, select the governance and oversight needs for the AI strategy.
	+ Compliance reviews
	+ Ethical guidelines
	+ Technical audit
	+ Stakeholder steering committee
* **Additional Notes** (Additional notes): Provide any additional comments or notes you may have.
