# Makeup Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the customer to provide personalized makeup consultation services. It helps our team understand the customer's preferences, skin type, and other details to offer tailored advice on makeup products and techniques.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Select your preferred consultation type from the "Consultation Type" dropdown menu. This will help us understand your specific needs and offer targeted advice.
3. In the "Product Description" field, briefly describe the product you are interested in or need help with.
4. Add any additional consultation notes in the "Consultation Notes" field if you have any specific concerns or preferences.
5. Provide a URL to a photo of yourself, if applicable, in the "Customer Photo URL" field.
6. Enter your product ID (if applicable) in the "Product ID" field.
7. Select your makeup type from the "Customer Makeup Type" dropdown menu. This will help us understand your makeup preferences.
8. Choose your eye color from the "Customer Eye Color" dropdown menu. This will help us understand your eye color.
9. Select your skin type from the "Customer Skin Type" dropdown menu. This will help us understand your skin type.
10. Choose your hair color from the "Customer Hair Color" dropdown menu. This will help us understand your hair color.
11. Select your age group from the "Customer Age" field. This will help us understand your age group.
12. Enter your height in the "Customer Height" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Consultation Type** (`consultation_type`, select_one, required: true): Choose the type of consultation you need. This will help us understand your specific needs and offer targeted advice.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the product you are interested in or need help with.
* **Consultation Notes** (`consultation_notes`, text, required: false): Add any additional consultation notes if you have any specific concerns or preferences.
* **Customer Photo URL** (`customer_photo_url`, text, required: false): Provide a URL to a photo of yourself, if applicable.
* **Product ID** (`product_id`, number, required: false): Enter your product ID, if applicable.
* **Customer Makeup Type** (`customer_makeup_type`, select_multiple, required: true): Choose your makeup type. This will help us understand your makeup preferences.
* **Customer Eye Color** (`customer_eye_color`, select_multiple, required: true): Choose your eye color. This will help us understand your eye color.
* **Customer Skin Type** (`customer_skin_type`, select_multiple, required: true): Choose your skin type. This will help us understand your skin type.
* **Customer Hair Color** (`customer_hair_color`, select_multiple, required: true): Choose your hair color. This will help us understand your hair color.
* **Customer Age** (`customer_age`, number, required: false): Enter your age group.
* **Customer Height** (`customer_height`, text, required: false): Enter your height.
