# Workforce Performance Consulting Program Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workforce Performance Consulting Program Application form is designed to help organizations assess their performance and identify areas for improvement. This form will help you provide detailed information about your organization and its performance challenges, allowing our consulting team to better understand your needs and provide tailored guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Go to each page of the form and fill out the required fields.
2. For each field, enter the requested information as instructed.
3. Review the form for accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Details (Organization Details)**: Enter a brief description of your organization, including its name, size, industry, or other relevant details.
* **Performance Challenges (Performance Challenges)**: Describe the specific performance issues your organization is facing. This could include areas such as productivity, communication, or team engagement.
* **Priority Focus Areas (Priority Focus Areas)**: Identify the key areas where you would like to see improvement. This could be related to productivity, quality, or customer satisfaction.
* **Contact Information (Contact Information)**: Provide your contact information, including your name, title, email, and phone number. This will ensure that our consulting team can reach out to you for further discussion.

## Tips
* Please be specific and concise when filling out the form to ensure accurate data analysis.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for assistance.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
* If you have any questions or need further guidance, please don't hesitate to reach out to our consulting team.
