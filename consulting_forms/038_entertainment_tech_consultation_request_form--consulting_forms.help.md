# Entertainment Tech Consultation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Entertainment Tech Consultation Request Form is designed to collect information from clients about their project details, contact information, and technology needs to provide consultation services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the project details, including any relevant project information.
2. Provide your contact details, including your name, email address, and phone number.
3. Select the technologies you require for your project.
4. Add any additional comments or information about your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Details** (project_details):
	+ A brief description of your project, including the type of event, venue, and any specific requirements.
* **Contact Details** (contact_details):
	+ Your name, email address, and phone number, so we can get in touch with you.
* **Technology Needs** (technology_needs):
	+ Select from the options:
		- None
		- Audio Equipment
		- Visual Effects
		- Video Equipment
	+ Indicate the technologies you need for your project.
* **Additional Comments** (additional_comments):
	+ Any additional information or questions you have about your project.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when filling out the project details.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* For urgent requests, please provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you promptly.
