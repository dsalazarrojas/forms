# Store Design Consultation Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients about their store design needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the form**: Carefully fill out each field according to your needs and preferences.
2. **Review and submit**: Double-check your answers and submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Form** (`contact_form`, text, required/optional): This is where you can enter your name, email, or any other contact information.
- **Store Design Requirements** (`store_design_requirements`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one or more options from the dropdown menu to indicate your store's design requirements, such as Client Requirements, Client Preferences, or Store Requirements.
- **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, number, required/optional): If you have a specific budget in mind for your project, enter it here.
- **Expected Delivery Date** (`expected_delivery_date`, date, required/optional): If you have a specific deadline for the project, enter the expected delivery date.
- **Designer Details** (`designer_details`, select_one, required/optional): Select the preferred designer for your project from the dropdown menu.
- **Client Preferences** (`client_preferences`, text, required/optional): Enter any specific preferences you have for your store's design.
- **Store Details** (`store_details`, text, required/optional): Enter any specific details about your store that may be helpful for the designer.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact information, such as phone number or address.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about your project.
- **Store Design Project Name** (`store_design_project_name`, text, required/optional): Enter a name for your project that will help us identify it.
- **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of your project.
- **Preferred Design Style** (`preferred_design_style`, select_one, required/optional): Select the preferred design style for your store from the dropdown menu.
- **Preferred Color Scheme** (`preferred_color_scheme`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one or more color schemes for your store from the dropdown menu.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure we can create a tailored design concept that meets your needs.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
- We will use the information collected from this form to create a design concept for your store.
