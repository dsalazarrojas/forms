# yoga_consultation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect contact and health information from individuals seeking yoga consultations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Fill in the contact information fields with your name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide information about your health status, including any allergies or medical conditions.
3. Share your yoga practice experience, including your favorite styles and preferences.
4. Identify your preferred instructor or instructor style.
5. Provide studio information, if applicable.
6. List any emergency contacts.
7. Confirm any additional emergency contacts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (Required)**: Fill in your name, email, and phone number to ensure we can contact you regarding your consultation.
	+ Name: Your full name for our records.
	+ Email: Your email address for communication and confirmation.
	+ Phone: Your phone number for last-minute inquiries.
* **Health Information (Optional)**: Provide any relevant health information, such as allergies, medical conditions, or previous injuries.
* **Practice Information (Required)**: Share your yoga practice experience, including your favorite styles (e.g., Hatha, Vinyasa, Restorative) and preferences (e.g., pace, intensity).
* **Instructor Information (Required)**: Identify your preferred instructor or style of teaching.
* **Studio Information (Optional)**: If you have a preferred studio or location, please let us know.
* **Emergency Contact (Required)**: Provide contact information for an emergency contact, including their relationship to you.
* **Allergies and Medical History (Optional)**: List any allergies or medical conditions that may impact your practice.
* **Additional Emergency Contact (Optional)**: Provide an additional emergency contact, if applicable.

## Tips
- Please ensure the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
- This form is for internal use only, and all data will be treated with confidentiality and respect.
