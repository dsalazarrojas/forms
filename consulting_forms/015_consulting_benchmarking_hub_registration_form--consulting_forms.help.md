# Consulting Benchmarking Hub Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register your organization for the Consulting Benchmarking Hub. By submitting this form, you will be able to access benchmarking data, participate in peer groups, and receive regular reports on your performance compared to industry peers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your organization's name, type, industry, and size.
2. Enter your contact information, including first and last name, job title, email, phone number, and address.
3. Select your areas of interest for benchmarking.
4. Confirm your current use of benchmarking and your goals.
5. Provide consent for data sharing and select your participation level.
6. Choose your preferred peer group for comparison.
7. Select your reporting frequency.
8. Add any additional requirements or requests.
9. Finally, submit the form to register your organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the legal name of your organization.
* **Organization Type** (`organization_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of your organization (Corporation, Small Business, Nonprofit, Government Agency, Educational Institution, Healthcare Organization, Other).
* **Industry Sector** (`industry`, select_one, required): Select your primary industry (Technology, Finance, Healthcare, Manufacturing, Retail, Energy, Education, Government, Nonprofit, Other).
* **Organization Size** (`organization_size`, select_one, required): Select the number of employees in your organization (1-50, 51-200, 201-500, 501-1000, 1001-5000, 5000+).
* **Annual Revenue Range** (`annual_revenue`, select_one, optional): Select your annual revenue range (Under $1M, $1M - $10M, $10M - $50M, $50M - $100M, $100M - $500M, Over $500M).
* **Contact First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, required): Enter the first name of your primary contact.
* **Contact Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, required): Enter the last name of your primary contact.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, text, required): Enter the job title of your primary contact.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of your primary contact.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your primary contact.
* **Organization Address** (`organization_address`, text, required): Enter your organization's street address.
* **City** (`organization_city`, text, required): Enter the city location of your organization.
* **State or Province** (`organization_state`, text, required): Enter the state or province where your organization is located.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`organization_zip`, text, required): Enter the postal code of your organization.
* **Country** (`organization_country`, text, required): Enter the country of your organization's operation.
* **Areas of Interest for Benchmarking** (`benchmarking_areas`, select_multiple, required): Select the areas you would like to benchmark (Operational Efficiency, Financial Performance, Customer Satisfaction, Employee Engagement, Technology Adoption, Innovation Metrics, Quality Management, Supply Chain, Sales Performance, Marketing ROI).
* **Currently Using Benchmarking** (`current_benchmarking`, select_one, required): Confirm if you currently use benchmarking (Yes, Formally, Yes, Informally, 'False').
* **Benchmarking Goals** (`benchmarking_goals`, text, required): Enter your goals for benchmarking.
* **Consent to Data Sharing** (`data_sharing_consent`, select_one, required): Agree to share anonymized data (Yes, I Consent, No, I Do Not Consent).
* **Participation Level** (`participation_level`, select_one, required): Select your desired level of participation (Full Participant, Data Contributor Only, Observer).
* **Peer Group Preference** (`peer_group_preference`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred peer group for comparison (Same Industry, Same Size, Same Region, Best in Class, No Preference).
* **Preferred Reporting Frequency** (`reporting_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often you want reports (Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annually, Annually).
* **Additional Requirements** (`additional_requirements`, text, optional): Enter any special needs or requests.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): Enter the date of submission.
