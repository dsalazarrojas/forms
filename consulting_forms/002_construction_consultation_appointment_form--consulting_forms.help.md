# Construction Consultation Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Construction Consultation Appointment Form is used to gather information about clients and their projects, enabling consultants to prepare effectively for the meeting and providing a productive experience for all parties involved.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your information.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submission.
3. Review your form for any typos or errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`Client name`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter the full name of the client or company name for whom you are requesting a consultation.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact email`, `email`, required):
	+ Enter the email address you want the consultant to use for appointment confirmation.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact phone`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter the best phone number for the consultant to contact you.
* **Project Address** (`Project address`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter the address or site location of the project.
* **Preferred Consultation Date** (`Preferred consultation date`, `date`, required):
	+ Choose a specific date for the consultation.
* **Preferred Consultation Time** (`Preferred consultation time`, `time`, required):
	+ Provide a specific time window for the consultation.
* **Consultation Type** (`Consultation type`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Select the most relevant service type for your consultation (e.g., Site visit, Remote meeting, Phone call, Project review).
* **Brief Project Description** (`Brief project description`, `text`, optional):
	+ Provide a short description of the project to help the consultant prepare for the meeting.
* **Attachments Needed** (`Attachments needed`, `text`, optional):
	+ List any documents or files you need to bring or upload for the consultation.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when providing your contact information and project details.
* Double-check your form for accuracy before submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
