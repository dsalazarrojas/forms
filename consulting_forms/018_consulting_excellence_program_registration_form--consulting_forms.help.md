# Consulting Excellence Program Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
<thinking>
The purpose of this form is to allow you to register for the Consulting Excellence Program. The form is designed to collect relevant information about you and your company to ensure that you are eligible for the program and to understand your needs and expectations. Please answer the questions carefully to ensure that your registration is accurate and complete.
</thinking>

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal information, including your name, email address, phone number, job title, company name, and industry.
2. Select the program you want to register for and choose how you will participate.
3. Choose your preferred start date and the number of employees in your company.
4. Select your career level and specify your years of professional experience and years of consulting experience.
5. Choose the skill areas you want to develop.
6. List any previous related training you have completed.
7. If your company is sponsoring your registration, select "Yes, Company Sponsored".
8. Choose your payment method.
9. If you have any dietary requirements or accessibility needs, please specify them.
10. Select how you heard about this program.
11. Provide any additional comments or information.
12. Finally, agree to the terms and conditions of the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. First Name
**participant_first_name (First Name)**: Your first name
* Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 2. Last Name
**participant_last_name (Last Name)**: Your last name
* Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 3. Email Address
**participant_email (Email Address)**: Primary email address
* Please enter your primary email address that we will use to communicate with you.

### 4. Phone Number
**participant_phone (Phone Number)**: Contact phone number
* Please enter your contact phone number where we can reach you.

### 5. Job Title
**job_title (Job Title)**: Your current position
* Please enter your current job title as it appears on your business card or resume.

### 6. Company Name
**company_name (Company Name)**: Name of your organization
* Please enter the name of your company as it appears on your business card or letterhead.

### 7. Company Industry
**company_industry (Company Industry)**: Industry sector
* Select the industry sector that best describes your company.

### 8. Company Size
**company_size (Company Size)**: Number of employees
* Select the number of employees in your company.

### 9. Program Selection
**program_selection (Select Program)**: Which excellence program are you registering for

### 10. Program Format

### 11. Preferred Start Date
