# economic_policy_consulting_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your economic policy consulting needs so that we can properly assign a person or team to assist you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter a brief title for your inquiry in the **Consulting Form** field.
2.  Choose the category that best describes your request from the **Category** dropdown menu.
3.  Describe the specific issue or question you need help with in the **What can we help you with?** field.
4.  Provide your email address in the **Email** field so that we can respond to your inquiry.
5.  Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field if you would like to be contacted by phone.
6.  In the **Submission** field, you can include any additional notes or comments about your inquiry.
7.  If applicable, select the **Assigned Tool** that you would like to use for this project.
8.  Finally, enter a file name for the **Output File** if you would like to receive a report or document with your response.
9.  You can also enter any other information you feel is relevant in the **Form ID** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consulting Form**: A brief title for your inquiry.
* **Category**: The category that best describes your request (Consulting Forms, Other, or Services).
* **What can we help you with?**: A description of the issue or question you need help with.
* **Email**: Your email address so that we can respond to your inquiry.
* **Phone**: Your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
* **Submission**: Any additional notes or comments about your inquiry.
* **Assigned Tool**: The tool you would like to use for this project (chatjim, chatjimmy, or chatjimmy 2).
* **Output File**: A file name for the report or document you would like to receive.
* **Form ID**: Any other information you feel is relevant to your inquiry.

## Tips

* Be sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a proper response.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We will review your form and contact you shortly after submission.
