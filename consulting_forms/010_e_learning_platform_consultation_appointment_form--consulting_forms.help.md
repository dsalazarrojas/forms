# E-Learning Platform Consultation Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for an E-Learning Platform consultation appointment. It aims to capture essential details from clients about their E-Learning Platform consultation needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as the client in the "Client Name" field.
2. Select the date for the consultation from the calendar provided in the "Date" field.
3. Choose the time slot for the consultation from the options provided in the "Time" field.
4. Indicate whether you are using an E-Learning Platform by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "E-Learning Platform" field.
5. Enter the name of the consultant you would like to work with in the "Consultant Name" field.
6. Provide your email address in the "Email" field (this is optional but recommended for communication purposes).
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (this is optional but recommended for communication purposes).
8. Type any additional comments or questions you have in the "Message" field.
9. Choose the duration of the consultation from the options provided in the "E-Learning Platform Consultation" field.
10. Select the assigned tool for the consultation from the options provided in the "Assigned Tool" field.
11. Enter any output or results from the consultation in the "Output File" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, required): Enter your name as the client.
* **Date** (`date`, required): Select the date for the consultation from the calendar.
* **Time** (`time`, required): Choose the time slot for the consultation.
* **E-Learning Platform** (`e_learning_platform`, required): Indicate whether you are using an E-Learning Platform.
* **Consultant Name** (`consultant_name`, required): Enter the name of the consultant you would like to work with.
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Provide your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Message** (`message`, required): Type any additional comments or questions.
* **E-Learning Platform Consultation** (`e_learning_platform_consultation`, required): Choose the duration of the consultation.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, required): Select the assigned tool for the consultation.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, required): Enter any output or results from the consultation.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately to ensure a smooth consultation process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to type them in the "Message" field for the consultant's attention.
* Ensure that you are using a compatible tool for the consultation, as selected in the "Assigned Tool" field.
