# Consultancy Service Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your consultancy service needs. Please fill it out accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth and effective service delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields. Fields marked as "required" are crucial for us to provide the best service, so make sure to provide accurate information.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all necessary fields are filled out.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Full Name** (`client_full_name_ident`, text, required): This field is for your full name.
* **Organization/Company Name** (`organization_legal_name`, text, required): This field is for your organization's name.
* **Primary Contact Email** (`primary_contact_email_ident`, email, required): This field is for your primary contact email address.
* **Primary Contact Phone** (`primary_contact_phone_ident`, text, required): This field is for your primary contact phone number. Please format it as (555) 555-5555.
* **Organization Location (City, State, Country)** (`organization_location_address`, text, required): This field is for your organization's location.
* **Primary Service Area of Interest** (`primary_service_needed_ident`, select_one, required): This field is for your primary service area of interest.
* **Estimated Project Duration** (`estimated_project_duration_ident`, select_one, required): This field is for the estimated project duration. Please choose one of the provided options.
* **Estimated Project Budget Range** (`estimated_project_budget_range`, select_one, required): This field is for the estimated project budget range. Please choose one of the provided options.
* **How soon do you plan to start?** (`project_urgency_ident`, select_one, required): This field is for the project start date. Please choose one of the provided options.
* **Preferred Engagement Model** (`preferred_engagement_model_ident`, select_one, required): This field is for your preferred engagement model. Please choose one of the provided options.
* **How did you hear about our consultancy?** (`referral_source_ident`, select_one, optional): This field is for the source of your referral.
* **Any additional details you'd like to share** (`additional_comments_desc`, text, optional): This field is for any additional information you'd like to share with us.
* **Date of Service Information Collection** (`registration_date`, date, required): This field is for the date of service information collection.
* **Would you like to receive our quarterly industry insights?** (`confirmation_email_opt_in`, select_one, required): This field is for your consent to receive our quarterly industry insights.
* **Authorized Representative Name** (`authorized_representative_name_label`, text, required): This field is for your authorized representative's name.
* **Authorized Representative Title** (`authorized_representative_title_label`, text, required): This field is for your authorized representative's title.
