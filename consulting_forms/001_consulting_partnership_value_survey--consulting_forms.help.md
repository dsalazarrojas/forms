> # Consulting Partnership Value Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the consulting partnership experience. It aims to gauge the client's perception of the services provided, their satisfaction with communication, and overall value delivered.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the full name of the primary client contact.
2. Enter the name of the client organization (company or division).
3. Provide a business email address for follow-up communication.
4. Select the current phase of the engagement (Discovery, Implementation, Ongoing support, or Completed).
5. Rate the overall value delivered on a scale of 1 to 5.
6. Select your level of satisfaction with the communication from the partner.
7. Describe the top outcome achieved from the partnership (optional).
8. Share any suggestions for improvement with the consulting team.
9. Indicate whether you would recommend the consulting partner to others.
10. Provide any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client name** (Client name): Enter the full name of the primary client contact.
* **Client organization** (Client organization): Enter the name of the client's company or division.
* **Contact email** (Contact email): Provide a business email address for follow-up communication.
* **Engagement phase** (Engagement phase): Select the current phase of the engagement (Discovery, Implementation, Ongoing support, or Completed).
* **Perceived value score** (Perceived value overall): Rate the overall value delivered on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Satisfaction with communication** (Satisfaction with communication): Select your level of satisfaction with the partner's communication.
* **Most valuable outcome** (Most valuable outcome achieved): Describe the top outcome from the partnership (optional).
* **Improvement suggestions** (Improvement suggestions): Share any suggestions for improvement with the consulting team.
* **Recommend to peer** (Recommend consulting partner to peer): Indicate whether you would recommend the consulting partner to others.
* **Additional comments** (Additional comments): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
