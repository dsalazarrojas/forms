# Environmental Performance Consulting Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients seeking consulting services related to environmental performance. It will be used to provide the necessary support and expertise for the consulting services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) in the "Required" column.
2. Provide clear and concise answers to each question.
3. Make sure to enter your email address correctly, as it will be used for communication.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Contact Person** (2, text, required): Enter the name of the person to contact.
* **Phone Number** (3, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (4, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Company Description** (5, text, required): Briefly describe your organization.
* **Contact Phone** (6, text, required): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Email** (7, email, required): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Service Request** (8, text, required): Describe the service you are seeking.
* **Consultant Name** (9, text, required): Enter the consultant's name.
* **Consultant Email** (10, email, required): Enter the consultant's email address.
* **Consultant Phone** (11, text, required): Enter the consultant's phone number.
* **Service Type** (12, select_one, required): Select one of the service types: Energy Consulting, Water Consulting, or Greenhouse Gas.
* **Service Location** (13, text, required): Enter the location where the service will be provided.
* **Date of Visit** (14, date, required): Enter the date of the service (e.g., 2023-02-15).
* **Start Time** (15, time, required): Enter the start time of the service (e.g., 14:00).
* **End Time** (16, time, required): Enter the end time of the service (e.g., 16:00).
* **Additional Info** (17, text, required): Provide any additional information about the service request.
* **Service Date** (18, date, required): Enter the date of the service (e.g., 2023-02-15).
* **Service Time** (19, time, required): Enter the time of the service (e.g., 14:00).
* **Follow-up Info** (20, text, required): Provide any follow-up information about the service request.
* **Service Request Note** (21, text, required): Enter any notes about the service request.
* **Payment Terms** (22, text, required): Enter the payment terms.
* **Signature** (23, text, required): Provide a signature.
* **Agreement** (24, text, required): Enter any agreement terms.
* **Submit** (25, text, required): Review and submit the form.

Note: This form is for internal use only and not intended for public consumption.
