# Energy Efficiency Consultation Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register new clients for energy efficiency consultations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your contact details in the "Contact Details" section.
2.  Provide your property information in the "Property Info" section.
3.  Choose your preferred date and time for the consultation in the "Preferred Date Time" section.
4.  Select your consent to be contacted in the "Consent" section.
5.  Provide your contact name, email, and phone number in the "Contact Name", "Contact Email", and "Contact Phone" sections respectively.
6.  Enter your property address in the "Property Address" section.
7.  Select your property type from the options provided in the "Property Type" section.
8.  Enter your preferred date for the consultation in the "Preferred Date" section.
9.  Enter your preferred time for the consultation in the "Preferred Time" section.
10. Enter any additional message or comments in the "Message" section.
11. Select the person assigned to handle your case in the "Assigned To" section.
12. Enter a form ID in the "Form ID" section.
13. Enter your category in the "Category" section.
14. Enter a description of your case in the "Description" section.
15. Enter the title of your form in the "Form Title" section.
16. Enter a description of your form in the "Form Description" section.
17. Enter your form category in the "Form Category" section.
