# Bankruptcy Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bankruptcy Consultation Form is a tool to gather essential information from clients seeking consultation on bankruptcy. It is used to understand their financial situation, identify areas of concern, and schedule a consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
Before filling out the form, please read through each field carefully. If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance. Ensure that you fill out the form accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name** (1, text, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (2, email, required): Provide your contact email address where we can reach you.
* **Phone Number** (3, text, required): Enter your contact phone number for further communication.
* **Mailing Address** (4, text, required): Enter your street address, city, state, and ZIP code for contact and communication purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (5, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Marital Status** (6, select_one, required): Choose your current marital status.
* **Spouse Name** (7, text, optional): If applicable, enter your spouse's name for consultation purposes.
* **Number of Dependents** (8, number, required): Enter the number of dependents (children, spouse, etc.).
* **Employment Status** (9, select_one, required): Choose your current employment status.
* **Employer Name** (10, text, optional): Enter your current employer's name for consultation purposes.
* **Monthly Gross Income** (11, number, required): Enter your monthly gross income before taxes.
* **Total Debt Amount** (12, number, required): Enter your approximate total debt amount.
* **Types of Debt** (13, select_multiple, required): Select all debt types that apply to your situation.
* **Real Estate Assets** (14, text, optional): List any real estate assets you own.
* **Vehicle Assets** (15, text, optional): List any vehicle assets you own.
* **Bank Account Balances** (16, number, optional): Enter the total balance of your bank accounts.
* **Other Assets** (17, text, optional): List any other assets (investments, valuables, etc.).
* **Monthly Expenses** (18, number, required): Enter your total monthly living expenses.
* **Foreclosure Status** (19, select_one, optional): Check if you have any foreclosure actions or threats.
* **Repossession Status** (20, select_one, optional): Check if you have any repossession actions or threats.
* **Wage Garnishment** (21, select_one, required): Choose your current wage garnishment status.
* **Previous Bankruptcy Filing** (22, select_one, required): Choose your previous bankruptcy filing status.
* **Consultation Type** (23, select_one, required): Choose the type of consultation you require (in-person, phone call, video conference, or email).
* **Preferred Consultation Date** (24, date, required): Enter the date and time you prefer for the consultation.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (25, select_one, optional): Choose how you heard about us (online search, attorney referral, friend or family, advertisement, or other).
* **Additional Comments** (26, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information that may help us provide better consultation.
