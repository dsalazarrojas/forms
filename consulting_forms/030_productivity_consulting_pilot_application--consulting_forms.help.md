# Productivity Consulting Pilot Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Productivity Consulting Pilot Application form is designed to collect information from potential customers interested in participating in a productivity consulting pilot program. This guide will walk you through each field of the form and explain its purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Organization Name in the Organization Name field.
4. Enter your Contact Email in the Contact Email field.
5. Enter your Contact Phone number in the Contact Phone field.
6. Describe your key challenges in the Key Challenges field.
7. Explain your goals and objectives in the Goals and Objectives field.
8. Select all that apply from the Pilot Commitment field.
9. Choose whether you consent to collect data in the Consent to Collect Data field.
9. Add any additional comments in the Additional Comments field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name in this field.
### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name in this field.
### Organization Name
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your organization in this field.
### Contact Email
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address to ensure we can contact you.
### Contact Phone
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
### Key Challenges
* **Key Challenges** (`key_challenges`, `text`, required): Describe the key challenges you are facing in this field.
### Goals and Objectives
* **Goals and Objectives** (`goals_and_objectives`, `text`, required): Explain your goals and objectives in this field.
### Pilot Commitment
* **Pilot Commitment** (`pilot_commitment`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply from the options provided.
### Consent to Collect Data
* **Consent to Collect Data** (`consent_to_collect_data`, `select_one`, required): Choose 'True' if you consent to collect data.
### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments about your participation or interest in the pilot program.
