# Consultant Contract Completion Offboarding Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to guide consultants through the offboarding process, ensuring all necessary steps are taken to finalize their engagement. It verifies completion of tasks, transfers documentation, and confirms payment submission.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the consultant's details:
	*   **Consultant Full Name**: Enter the consultant's full name.
	*   **Consultant's Company/Firm**: Enter the consultant's company or firm name.
	*   **Contract ID or Project Code**: Enter the unique contract ID or project code.
2. Verify completion:
	*   **Final Deliverables Received and Verified**: Confirm that all final deliverables have been received and verified.
	*   **All Related Documentation Transferred to Internal Shared Drives**: Confirm that all documentation has been transferred.
	*   **Account Credentials and Administrative Access Revoked/Returned**: Confirm that account credentials and access have been revoked or returned.
	*   **Company Hardware Returned (If Applicable)**: If applicable, confirm that company hardware has been returned.
3. Confirm financials:
	*   **Final Project Invoice Submitted for Payment**: Confirm that the final project invoice has been submitted for payment.
	*   **All Reimbursable Expenses Submitted and Approved**: Confirm that all reimbursable expenses have been submitted and approved.
4. Provide feedback:
	*   **Overall Consultant Performance Rating (1-5)**: Rate the consultant's performance.
	*   **Eligible for Future Rehire or New Contracts?**: Indicate if the consultant is eligible for future rehire or new contracts.
	*   **What was the primary success factor for this engagement?**: Provide feedback on the primary success factor.
	*   **Key Lessons Learned for Future Engagements**: Provide key lessons learned.
5. Finalize submission:
	*   **Date of Offboarding Completion**: Enter the date of offboarding completion.
	*   **Authorized By (Name and Title)**: Enter the name and title of the authorized person.
