# Data Modernization Consulting Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Data Modernization Consulting Contact Form" is designed to capture leads for a consulting service that helps businesses modernize their data and processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and determine which page best describes your current status.
	* If you have not yet decided on your data modernization goals or are not ready to schedule a discovery call, complete the lead capture page.
	* If you have a clear idea of your data modernization goals, complete the modernization goals page.
	* If you are ready to schedule a call with the consultant, complete the schedule a discovery call page.
2. Fill out the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) on the form.
3. Enter any additional information you feel is relevant to your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (Company Name, text, optional): Enter your company name to help us understand your business context.
* **What data modernization goals do you have in mind?** (What data modernization goals, text, optional): Describe your data modernization goals and objectives.
* **Schedule a discovery call** (Schedule a discovery call, text, optional): Enter a date and time for a discovery call with our consultant. Please note that this field is required if you are interested in proceeding with our services.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address to help us contact you about your inquiry.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number to help us contact you about your inquiry.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
* If you have any additional comments or questions, please include them in the "What data modernization goals do you have in mind?" field.
* A member of our team will review your submission and follow up with you within 2-3 business days.
* Please be aware that this form is for capturing leads, not for placing an order or requesting quotes. If you would like to request a quote or place an order, please contact us directly.
