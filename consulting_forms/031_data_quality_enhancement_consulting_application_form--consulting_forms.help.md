# data_quality_enhancement_consulting_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for the purpose of data quality enhancement consulting applications. The form is intended to gather essential details about the user, including their name, contact information, job title, and other relevant work-related details. This information will be used to provide personalized consulting services to improve data quality and meet business needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Provide your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone number.
5. Type in your Company name.
6. Specify your Job Title.
7. Select your Department from the available options.
8. Select two Job Titles from the available options.
9. Choose your Job Level.
10. Enter the number of Years of Service.
11. Enter your Date of Birth.
12. Enter your Time of Birth (optional).
13. Enter any additional notes or comments in the Note field.
14. Select your Job Title 3 from the available options.
15. Enter the Date of Submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (First Name)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (Last Name)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address in the format `username@example.com`.
* **Phone (Phone)**: Enter your phone number in the format `123-456-7890`.
* **Company (Company)**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Job Title (Job Title)**: Enter your job title.
* **Department (Department)**: Select your department from the available options.
* **Job Title 2 (Job Title 2)**: Select two job titles from the available options.
* **Job Level (Job Level)**: Choose your job level.
* **Years of Service (Years of Service)**: Enter the number of years you have been with your company.
* **Date of Birth (Date of Birth)**: Enter your date of birth in the `DD/MM/YYYY` format.
* **Time of Birth (Time of Birth)**: Enter your time of birth (optional).
* **Note (Note)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your submission.
* **Job Title 3 (Job Title 3)**: Select two job titles from the available options.
* **Date of Submission (Date of Submission)**: Enter the date you are submitting this form.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Use the format `DD/MM/YYYY` for dates.
* Select only applicable options for job titles.
* Enter your phone number in the format `123-456-7890`.
* Double-check your email address for correct formatting.
* Be accurate when selecting job levels and job titles.
