# Agricultural Consultant Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agricultural Consultant Estimate Form is designed for agricultural consultants to provide estimates for their services. It helps consultants gather necessary information to create a detailed estimate for the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Client Name**, **Farm or Business Name**, and **Consultant Name**.
2. Choose the **Consultation Type** that best describes the area of service needed.
3. Enter the **Date of Estimate**, which is the date the form was completed.
4. Provide a **Service Description** of the services requested.
5. Enter the **Estimated Hours** you expect to spend on the task and the **Hourly Rate**.
6. If applicable, add **Material Costs** and **Travel Expenses**.
7. Calculate the **Total Estimated Cost** based on the hourly rate and estimated hours.
8. Select the **Payment Schedule** that suits your requirements, which may be **Upfront**, **50% Deposit**, **Upon Completion**, or **Net 30**.
9. Optionally, add **Notes** for any additional terms or conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client.
* **Farm or Business Name**: Enter the name of the farm or business.
* **Consultant Name**: Enter the name of the person preparing the estimate.
* **Consultation Type**:
	+ Choose the primary area of consultation: **Crop Management**, **Soil Analysis**, **Pest Control**, **Livestock Health**, **Business Strategy**, or **Regulatory Compliance**.
* **Date of Estimate**: Enter the date the estimate was completed.
* **Service Description**: Provide a detailed description of the services requested.
* **Estimated Hours**: Enter the number of hours expected to complete the services.
* **Hourly Rate**: Enter the cost per hour of service.
* **Material Costs**: Enter the estimated cost of materials needed for the service (if any).
* **Travel Expenses**: Enter the estimated travel costs (if any).
* **Total Estimated Cost**: This field is calculated based on the hourly rate and estimated hours.
* **Valid Until**: Enter the date this estimate expires.
* **Payment Schedule**:
	+ Choose the payment terms: **Upfront**, **50% Deposit**, **Upon Completion**, or **Net 30**.
* **Notes**: Add any additional terms or conditions (if necessary).

## Tips
- Ensure accurate details are provided to create a reliable estimate.
- Review and update the form regularly to ensure it accurately represents the services offered.
- Communicate clearly with clients to confirm their needs and expectations.
