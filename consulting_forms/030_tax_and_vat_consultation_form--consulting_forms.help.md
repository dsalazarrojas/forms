# Tax And VAT Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax And VAT Consultation Form is designed to gather information from businesses seeking tax and VAT consultation services. This form helps us understand your business details, tax-related information, and reasons for consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill in your business name, owner's name, and other required details on page 1.
*   Select the relevant tax type and business type that applies to your business on page 2.
*   Provide your business address, phone number, and email address on page 3.
*   Specify the reason for consultation, amount to be refunded, and currency on page 4.
*   Enter any additional details or notes on page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Business Name** (`business_name`, Text, Required): Enter your business name as it appears on your business registration document.
*   **Business Owner** (`business_owner`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the owner of the business.
*   **Tax Year** (`tax_year`, Number, Optional): If you have a specific tax year in mind, enter it here.
*   **VAT Number** (`vat_number`, Text, Optional): If you have a VAT number, enter it here.
*   **Business Address** (`business_address`, Text, Optional): Enter your business address as it appears on your business registration document.
*   **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your business phone number.
*   **Business Email** (`business_email`, Email, Optional): Enter your business email address.
*   **Business Website** (`business_website`, Text, Optional): If you have a business website, enter it here.
*   **Reason for Contact** (`reason_for_contact`, Text, Optional): Briefly describe the reason for your tax and VAT consultation.
*   **Tax Type** (`tax_type`, Select One, Optional): Select the type of tax you are inquiring about (VAT, Tax, or other).
*   **Business Type** (`business_type`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the type of business you own (Business, Individual, Partnership, or Company).
*   **Tax Regulation** (`tax_regulation`, Select One, Optional): If applicable, select the relevant tax regulation that applies to your business.
*   **Date of Last Tax Declaration** (`date_of_last_tax_declaration`, Date, Optional): If you have a specific date in mind, enter it here.
*   **Time of Last Tax Declaration** (`time_of_last_tax_declaration`, Time, Optional): If you have a specific time in mind, enter it here.
*   **Reason for Refund** (`reason_for_refund`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the reason for your refund (Reason 1, Reason 2, or Reason 3).
*   **Amount to Refund** (`amount_to_refund`, Number, Optional): If you have a specific amount in mind, enter it here.
*   **Currency** (`currency`, Select One, Optional): Select the currency you would like to receive the refund in (Euro, USD, or GBP).
*   **VAT Rate** (`vat_rate`, Number, Optional): If applicable, enter the VAT rate you are inquiring about.
*   **Tax Rate** (`tax_rate`, Number, Optional): If applicable, enter the tax rate you are inquiring about.
*   **Other Details** (`other_details`, Text, Optional): If you have any additional information, enter it here.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, Note, Optional): Enter any relevant notes or comments about your tax and VAT consultation.
*   **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address to receive updates or responses.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number to receive updates or responses.
*   **Website** (`website`, Text, Optional): If you have a website, enter it here.
*   **Address** (`address`, Text, Optional): Enter your business address as it appears on your business registration document.

By following these steps and explanations, you will be able to complete the Tax And VAT Consultation Form effectively and accurately.
