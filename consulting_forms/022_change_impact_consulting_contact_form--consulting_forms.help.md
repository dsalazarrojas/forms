# Change Impact Consulting Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Change Impact Consulting Contact Form is designed to collect information from clients or potential clients about their needs, preferences, and expectations for potential consulting projects.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Fill out each field according to the instructions provided.
3. Make sure to provide as much information as possible for each field, as this will help us better understand your needs.
4. Once you have completed all fields, click "Submit" to send your inquiry to our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Date of Inquiry**: <input type="date" id="inquiry_date"> When contacting us.
* **Company Name**: <input type="text" id="company_name"> Your organization.
* **Industry or Sector**: <input type="text" id="industry"> What industry are you in.
* **Company Size**: <input type="select_one" id="company_size" options="1-50, 51-200, 201-500, 501-1000, 1000+"> Number of employees.
* **Contact Person Name**: <input type="text" id="contact_name"> Your name.
* **Contact Person Title**: <input type="text" id="contact_title"> Your position.
* **Email Address**: <input type="email" id="email_address"> Your contact email.
* **Phone Number**: <input type="text" id="phone_number"> Your contact phone.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: <input type="select_one" id="preferred_contact_method" options="Email, Phone, Either"> How would you like us to contact you.
* **Type of Inquiry**: <input type="select_one" id="inquiry_type" options="Change Management Consulting, Organizational Transformation, Merger or Acquisition Support, Digital Transformation, Other"> What are you interested in.
* **Current Organizational Challenge**: <input type="text" id="current_challenge"> What is your main challenge.
* **Scope of Change Needed**: <input type="text" id="change_scope"> Describe the change needed.
* **Desired Project Timeline**: <input type="select_one" id="project_timeline" options="Immediate - Next 30 Days, Soon - 1-3 Months, Medium Term - 3-6 Months, Long Term - 6+ Months, Flexible"> When do you need to start.
* **Approximate Budget Range**: <input type="select_one" id="budget_range" options="Under $25,000, $25,000 - $50,000, $50,000 - $100,000, $100,000 - $250,000, $250,000+, To Be Determined"> Budget allocated for consulting.
* **Key Stakeholders Involved**: <input type="text" id="key_stakeholders"> Who are the decision makers.
* **Current Organizational Readiness**: <input type="select_one" id="current_readiness" options="Very Ready, Ready, Somewhat Ready, Not Ready, Unsure"> How prepared is your organization.
* **Previous Consulting Experience**: <input type="select_one" id="previous_consulting" options="Yes, No"> Have you used consultants before.
* **Specific Expertise Needed**: <input type="text" id="specific_expertise"> What kind of expertise are you looking for.
* **Key Success Metrics or Goals**: <input type="text" id="success_metrics"> How will you measure success.
* **Additional Information or Comments**: <input type="text" id="additional_information"> Anything else we should know.
