# National Park Consulting Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us gather information from potential clients interested in consulting services for their national park projects. By filling out this form, you will be able to request a quote for our consulting services and provide us with the necessary details to better understand your project needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, marked with an asterisk (\*). This includes:
	* Project name (project_name)
	* National park (national_park)
	* Start date (start_date)
2. Optionally, provide additional details about the project and your contact information:
	* Consultant name (consultant_name)
	* Contact email (contact_email)
	* Contact phone (contact_phone)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name (project_name)** (`text`, required): Enter the name of your national park project.
* **National Park (national_park)** (`select_one`, required): Select whether the national park project is "Yes" or "No". This field is used to determine the type of project.
* **Start Date (start_date)** (`date`, required): Enter the start date of the project in the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Consultant Name (consultant_name)** (`text`, optional): Provide your name and title or any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Contact Email (contact_email)** (`email`, optional): Enter a valid email address to contact you.
* **Contact Phone (contact_phone)** (`text`, optional): Enter a phone number to contact you.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information about your project to ensure we can better understand your needs and provide a suitable quote.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
- We will review and respond to your request within 48 hours.
