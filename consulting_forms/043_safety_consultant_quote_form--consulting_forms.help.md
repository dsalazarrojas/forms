# Safety Consultant Quote Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Safety Consultant Quote Form is a tool for consultants to provide quotes or estimates for safety-related services. It is designed to capture essential information from clients seeking these services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Contact Information** (page 1)
	* Enter your name, email, and phone number in the format: "Name (email:phone)".
	* Ensure that your contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
2. **Services** (page 1)
	* List the services you offer, such as safety audits, training, or consulting.
	* Be specific about the services you provide and any relevant certifications or qualifications.
3. **Service Details** (page 2)
	* Describe the services you are providing in detail, including the scope of work and any specific requirements.
	* Ensure that your description is clear and concise.
4. **Project Information** (page 2)
	* Provide detailed information about the project, including the location, duration, and any specific requirements.
	* Be as specific as possible to ensure an accurate quote.
5. **Quote Details** (page 3)
	* Provide a detailed quote, including the estimated cost and any additional services included.
	* Ensure that your quote is accurate and transparent.
6. **Submit** (page 4)
	* Review your completed form before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
	* Click the submit button to send the form to the client.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Contact Information** (page 1)
* **Name**: This is your name as the consultant.
* **Email**: This is your email address for communication with the client.
* **Phone**: This is your phone number for further contact.

### 2. **Services** (page 1)
* **Services**: List the safety services you offer, such as audits, training, or consulting.
* **Additional information**: Provide any additional details about the services you offer, such as certifications or qualifications.

### 3. **Service Details** (page 2)
* **Service Details**: Describe the services you provide, including the scope of work and any specific requirements.
* **Additional information**: Provide any additional details about the services you are providing, such as equipment or materials needed.

### 4. **Project Information** (page 2)
* **Project Location**: Provide the location of the project.
* **Project Duration**: Provide the estimated duration of the project.
* **Project Requirements**: List any specific requirements for the project.

### 5. **Quote Details** (page 3)
* **Estimated Cost**: Provide an estimated cost for the services.
* **Additional Services**: List any additional services included in the quote.
* **Additional Information**: Provide any additional details about the quote, such as payment terms or conditions.
