# property_climate_consultant_contact_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The property_climate_consultant_contact_form is designed to allow customers to contact a climate consultant for consulting services related to properties. This form is meant to be a straightforward and efficient way to initiate communication with the consultant and potentially schedule a consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone number" field.
5. Provide a brief description of your property in the "Property details" field.
6. Select one or more services you are interested in from the "Consulting services" dropdown menu.
7. Choose a date for the consultation in the "Schedule consultation" field.
8. Add any additional notes in the "Note" field.
9. If you have a preferred tool or software for the consultation, select it from the "Assigned tool" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name**: Enter your first name.
- **Last name**: Enter your last name.
- **Email**: Enter a valid email address.
- **Phone number**: Enter a valid phone number.
- **Property details**: Provide a brief description of the property you are interested in.
- **Consulting services**: Select one or more of the following services:
	+ Climate Consulting
	+ Energy Audits
	+ Sustainability Consulting
	+ Other (please specify)
- **Schedule consultation**: Choose a date and time for the consultation.
- **Note**: Enter any additional notes about your consultation.
- **Assigned tool**: If you have a preferred tool or software for the consultation, select it from the list. Options include:
	+ chatjimmy
	+ other
