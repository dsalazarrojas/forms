# real_time_control_consultant_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The real-time control consultant survey is a form designed to collect feedback from clients about their experience with the consultant. This form helps to identify areas of improvement and provide valuable insights for the consultant to enhance their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your information.
2. Review the feedback questions and answer them accordingly.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Client Name** (Client Name, text, required: false)
* Enter the name of the client you are providing feedback for.

### **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, required: false)
* Enter the email address of the client you are providing feedback for.

### **Project Name** (Project Name, text, required: false)
* Enter the name of the project you were working on with the client.

### **Feedback Question 1** (Feedback Question 1, select_one, required: false)
* Answer "Yes" if you were satisfied with the consultant's services, and "No" otherwise.

### **Feedback Question 2** (Feedback Question 2, select_multiple, required: false)
* Answer "Yes" if the consultant demonstrated good communication skills, "Yes" if they showed good time management, and so on. You can select multiple answers as needed.

### **Feedback Question 3** (Feedback Question 3, text, required: false)
* Provide any additional feedback you may have about your experience with the consultant.
