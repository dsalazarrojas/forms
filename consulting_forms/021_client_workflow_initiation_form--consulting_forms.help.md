# Client Workflow Initiation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Workflow Initiation Form is a crucial step in the consulting process, providing essential information about your project and requirements. By filling out this form, you will help us understand your needs and ensure that we can effectively support you in achieving your goals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Client Name," "Company Name," and "Email Address" fields and enter the required information.
2. Provide a brief "Project Name" and "Project Description" that outlines the purpose and objectives of your project.
3. Choose the "Type of Project" that best describes your project.
4. Enter the "Desired Project Start Date" and "Target Project End Date" according to your project schedule.
5. Select the "Timeline flexibility" and "Project urgency level" that best suit your project needs.
6. Choose the "Expected business impact" of your project.
7. Enter the "Project Budget" and "Currency" for your project expenses.
8. If applicable, select the "Internal team size" and "Key stakeholders" involved in your project.
9. If necessary, upload any "Supporting documents" related to your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (Required): Enter the name of the client or representative responsible for the project.
* **Company Name** (Required): Enter the name of the client's company.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter the email address of the client or representative.
* **Project Name** (Required): Give a brief title to your project.
* **Project Description** (Required): Describe the purpose and objectives of your project in a few sentences.
* **Type of Project** (Required): Choose the type of project that best describes your project (Strategy Development, Process Improvement, Systems Implementation, Organizational Change, Training and Development, or Other).
* **Desired Project Start Date** (Required): Enter the date when you expect the project to start.
* **Target Project End Date** (Required): Enter the date by which you expect the project to be completed.
* **Timeline flexibility** (Optional): Select the level of flexibility you have in your project timeline (Fixed deadline, Somewhat flexible, or Flexible).
* **Project urgency level** (Required): Choose the level of priority for your project (Low priority, Medium priority, High priority, or Critical/Urgent).
* **Expected business impact** (Required): Select the level of business impact you expect from your project (Minor, Moderate, Significant, or Transformational).
* **Project Budget** (Required): Enter the total budget allocated for your project.
* **Currency** (Optional): If your project expenses are in a different currency, enter that currency here.
* **Internal team size** (Optional): Enter the number of staff members working on your project.
* **Key stakeholders** (Optional): List the key stakeholders involved in your project.
* **Supporting documents** (Optional): Upload any relevant documents that support your project requirements and specifications.

By following these steps and explanations, you will effectively complete the Client Workflow Initiation Form and initiate the consulting process for your project.
