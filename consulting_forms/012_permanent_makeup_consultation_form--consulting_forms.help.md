# Permanent Makeup Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Permanent Makeup Consultation Form is designed to collect information from clients for permanent makeup procedures. It helps clients provide essential details about their personal preferences, contact information, and appointment scheduling. This form is crucial for our permanent makeup artists to understand the client's needs and preferences before the appointment, ensuring a personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **To start, fill in your personal details**, including your name, email, and phone number. These fields are essential for us to contact you regarding your appointment and any follow-up information.
2. **Choose a suitable appointment time** that works best for you. Select a date and time that suits your schedule, and make sure to check the options carefully for the best results.
3. **Provide your contact address** if you'd like us to send any additional information or reminders via mail.
4. **Select any additional options** that apply to your appointment, such as selecting multiple options or providing extra notes.
5. **Double-check all fields** before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
