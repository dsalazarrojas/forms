# Digital Talent Strategy Consulting Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in digital talent strategy consulting services. It aims to match their skills and expertise with suitable clients.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **User Info** (page 1). This section is optional and asks for your basic information.
2. Provide your **Contact Info** (page 2) to get in touch with you.
3. Share your **Company Info** (page 3) if you're representing a company or business.
4. Describe your **Work Experience** (page 4), highlighting your relevant experience in digital talent strategy consulting.
5. List your **Qualifications** (page 5) that are relevant to the consulting services.
6. Share your **Portfolio** (page 6) to showcase your work and skills.
7. Upload your **Resume** (page 7) to provide further information about your background and experience.
8. Provide **Certifications** (page 8) if you have any relevant certifications.
9. Add **Additional Work Experience** (page 9) if you have other relevant experience to share.
10. List your **Skills** (page 10) that are relevant to digital talent strategy consulting.
11. Share your **Education** (page 11) to demonstrate your academic background.
12. Provide **Company Info 2** (page 12) if you're representing a company or business.
13. Share your **Portfolio 2** (page 13) to showcase your work and skills.
14. Upload your **Resume 2** (page 14) to provide further information about your background and experience.
15. Provide **Certifications 2** (page 15) if you have any relevant certifications.
16. Add **More Work Experience** (page 16) if you have other relevant experience to share.
17. List your **Skills 2** (page 17) that are relevant to digital talent strategy consulting.
18. Share your **Education 2** (page 18) to demonstrate your academic background.
19. Select your **Work Location** (page 19) from the available options.
20. Choose your **Availability** (page 20) from the options provided.
21. Select your **Salary** (page 21) from the options provided.
22. Set your **Notice Period** (page 22) if applicable.
23. Choose your **Employment Status** (page 23) from the options provided.
24. Select your **Employment Type** (page 24) from the options provided.
25. Choose your **Employment Status 2** (page 25) from the options provided.
