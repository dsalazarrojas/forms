# Digital Architecture Strategy Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Architecture Strategy Contact Form is a tool to gather contact information and inquire about architectural strategies from potential clients or partners.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Double-check that you're not already in touch with us regarding the same topic.
3. Use the inquiry type field to provide more context about your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`): Enter your name and contact information.
	+ This field is optional, but providing your email address and phone number can help us contact you efficiently.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your name as you'd like to be referred to.
	+ This field is not required, but it helps us address you personally in our response.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address to receive responses and further discussion.
	+ This field is not required, but it's essential for efficient communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number for potential follow-up calls.
	+ This field is not required, but it can aid in timely responses.
* **Company** (`company`): If you're representing a company, enter its name.
	+ This field is not required, but it helps us understand your affiliation.
* **Description** (`description`): Provide a brief description of your inquiry or project.
	+ This field is not required, but it gives us context for your inquiry.
* **Inquiry Type** (`inquiry_type`): Select one of the provided options to describe your inquiry.
	+ Options include "architecture and it strategy" or "other".
* **Other (if not listed)** (`other_inquiry`): If you selected "other" in the above field, provide a brief description of your inquiry.
	+ This field is not required, but it helps us understand your specific needs.
* **Submit** (`submit`): Click this button to send your completed form to us.

## Tips

- Be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out the form.
- If you have any attachments or supporting documents, you can send them separately to us via email.
- We'll respond to your inquiry as soon as possible, and we're looking forward to collaborating with you.
