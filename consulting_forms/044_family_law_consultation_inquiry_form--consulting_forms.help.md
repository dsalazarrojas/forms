# Family Law Consultation Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to submit their inquiry for family law consultation services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go to the Main Page and enter your information in the required fields.
2. Select your Available Date for the consultation.
3. Choose your Available Time for the consultation.
4. Enter your Preferred Time for the consultation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Page** (no label): This is the first page of the form where you will enter your basic information.
	+ **Available Date** (date, required): Enter the date you are available for the consultation. This will help the consultant schedule the meeting at the earliest convenience.
	+ **Available Time** (time, required): Select a time slot that you are available for the consultation.
	+ **Preferred Time** (time, optional): Choose a time that you would prefer for the consultation.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information in the Available Date and Available Time fields to ensure a smooth consultation process.
* Enter your Preferred Time only if you have a specific time in mind. Otherwise, the consultant will choose a suitable time.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
