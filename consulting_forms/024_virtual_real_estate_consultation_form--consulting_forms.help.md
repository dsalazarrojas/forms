> # Virtual Real Estate Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your interests and preferences for a virtual real estate consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out all the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
2. For the "What do you want?" field, select only one option that best represents your desire.
3. For the "What do you like?" field, select all options that apply to you.
4. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Note" field, if necessary.
5. Fill out the "Date" and "Time" fields with your preferred consultation date and time.
6. Finally, submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Address** (text, optional): Enter your address for us to reach you, if desired.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address, if you want to be contacted.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number, if you want to be contacted.
* **Date** (date, optional): Select the date for your consultation.
* **Time** (time, optional): Select the time for your consultation.
* **Note** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **What do you want?** (select_one, optional): Select the option that best represents what you want from our consultation.
* **What do you like?** (select_multiple, optional): Select all options that apply to you.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to get the best consultation experience.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* You can fill out this form multiple times if you need to make multiple consultations.
