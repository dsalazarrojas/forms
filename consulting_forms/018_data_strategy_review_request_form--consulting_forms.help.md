# data_strategy_review_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a data strategy review from a team or individual. It captures relevant information to facilitate the review process, ensuring that all necessary details are collected and communicated to the reviewer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the company requesting the review.
2. Enter the name of the person or team requesting the review.
3. Enter your contact email address so we can reach you.
4. Describe the scope of the review (e.g., financials, operations, marketing, etc.).
5. Select how frequently the review should be performed (e.g., monthly, quarterly, semi-annually, annually).
6. Select the data sources that need to be reviewed (e.g., financials, operations, marketing, etc.).
7. Provide a brief explanation of the business impact of the review.
8. Enter the date by which the review should be completed.
9. Add any additional review notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the company requesting the review.
* **Review Requestor** (`review_requestor`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person or team requesting the review.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter your contact email address so we can reach you.
* **Review Scope** (`review_scope`, text, required: false): Describe the scope of the review (e.g., financials, operations, marketing, etc.).
* **Review Frequency** (`review_frequency`, select_one, required: false): Select how frequently the review should be performed (e.g., monthly, quarterly, semi-annually, annually).
* **Data Sources** (`data_sources`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the data sources that need to be reviewed (e.g., financials, operations, marketing, etc.).
* **Business Impact** (`business_impact`, text, required: false): Provide a brief explanation of the business impact of the review.
* **Review Due Date** (`review_due_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date by which the review should be completed.
* **Review Notes** (`review_notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional review notes or comments.
