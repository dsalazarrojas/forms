# Innovation Consulting Network Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register you to the Innovation Consulting Network, a platform for individuals to collaborate, share knowledge, and connect with others who share similar interests in innovation, consulting, technology, strategy, marketing, and finance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, including your First Name, Last Name, Email, and Date (if applicable).
2. Select your areas of expertise and interest from the provided options.
3. Add any additional notes or comments in the notes section.
4. Decide whether or not you would like to join our network.
5. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): If you are affiliated with a company, please enter it here.
* **Position** (`position`, text, optional): Please enter your current position or role.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number (optional).
* **Areas of Expertise** (`expertise`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all areas of expertise that apply to you (Innovation, Consulting, Technology, Strategy, Marketing, Finance).
* **Areas of Interest** (`interests`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all areas of interest that apply to you (Innovation, Technology, Strategy, Marketing, Finance).
* **Notes** (`events`, note, optional): Please add any additional comments or notes about yourself.
* **Collaborate with other members** (`collaboration`, note, optional): If you are interested in collaborating with other members, please add a note.
* **Participate in Knowledge Sharing** (`knowledge_sharing`, note, optional): If you are interested in participating in knowledge sharing, please add a note.
* **Join our Network** (`network`, select_one, optional): Please select whether or not you would like to join our network (True or False).
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): This field is for internal use only.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Please add any additional comments or notes.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Please select a date (optional).
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Please select a time (optional).
* **Submit/Cancel** (`submit`, select_one, optional): Please select Submit to complete the form, or Cancel to exit.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, select_one, optional): Not applicable, just here in case you need to exit the form. 

Please note that the form is designed to be user-friendly and easy to fill out. If you have any issues or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out.
