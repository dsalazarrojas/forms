# Criminal Defense Consultation Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Criminal Defense Consultation Intake Form is designed to collect essential information from clients seeking criminal defense consultation. This information will be reviewed by our team before being presented to a lawyer or legal advisor for consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out each field accurately and thoroughly.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for the required fields.
3. Enter any additional information that may be helpful for the consultation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Client Info (Page 1)
* **Client Info**: Enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.

### 2. Case Details (Page 1)
* **Case Details**: Describe the details of your case, including charges, dates, and any other relevant information.

### 3. Contact Methods (Page 1)
* **Contact Methods**: Select how you would like to be contacted for the consultation, either by email or phone.

### 4. Case Status (Page 1)
* **Case Status**: Indicate the current status of your case, whether it is in progress, pending, or closed.

### 5. Defense Attorney (Page 2)
* **Defense Attorney**: If you have a defense attorney, please indicate whether they are currently representing you.

### 6. Next Steps (Page 2)
* **Next Steps**: Briefly describe the next steps you would like to take in your case.

### 7. Other Documents (Page 2)
* **Other Documents**: If there are any additional documents related to your case that you would like to provide, please attach them here.

### 8. Case Notes (Page 3)
* **Case Notes**: Enter any additional information that may be helpful for the consultation process.

### 9. Contact Person (Page 3)
* **Contact Person**: If someone is responsible for handling your case, please enter their name and title.

### 10. Law Firm (Page 3)
* **Law Firm**: If you are working with a law firm, please enter their name.

### 11. Contact Method (Page 4)
* **Contact Method**: Select how you would like to be contacted for the consultation, either by phone, email, or other.

### 12. Client Signature (Page 4)
* **Client Signature**: The date of the client's signature, which can be left blank if not applicable.

### 13. Intake Form Date (Page 4)
* **Intake Form Date**: The date this consultation intake form was completed.

### 14. Submitted By (Page 4)
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form, typically you.

### 15. Submitted Date (Page 4)
* **Submitted Date**: The date this form was submitted.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- If you are unsure about any field, please consult with our team before proceeding.
