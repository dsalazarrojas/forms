# Invisible Bead Extension Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Invisible Bead Extension Consultation Form is designed to collect information from clients or customers about their hair extension needs. This form will help our team to understand your preferences and requirements for a successful hair extension experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Enter your client name in the "client_name" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phone_number" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "email" field (optional, but recommended).
5. Provide a brief description of your hair history in the "hair_history" field.
6. Select the type of hair extension you are interested in from the "extension_type" field (micro loop, nano loop, or nano silk).
7. Select the color of your hair from the "extension_color" field (blonde, brunette, redhead, or other).
8. Enter the desired length of your hair extension in inches in the "desired_length" field.
9. Select the type of hair you have from the "hair_type" field (curly, straight, wavy, or other).
10. Select the length of your hair from the "hair_length" field (short, medium, long).
11. Select your preferred length for the hair extension in inches from the "extension_preferred_length" field (4 inch, 6 inch, 8 inch, 10 inch).
12. Enter any additional notes or comments about your hair or preferences in the "notes" fields.
13. Enter your appointment date in the "date" field.
14. Enter your appointment time in the  "time" field.
15. Finally, review all fields for accuracy before submitting.
