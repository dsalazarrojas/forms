# entrepreneurial_support_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from entrepreneurs about their business advisors, appointment dates and times, email and phone numbers, appointment duration, and additional info. It will help our consultants to schedule effective appointments with business advisors and provide better support to our clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you need a business advisor.
2. If you selected "Yes", select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you want to select multiple business advisors.
3. Enter the date of your desired appointment.
4. Enter the time of your desired appointment.
5. Enter the email of your business advisor.
6. Enter the phone number of your business advisor.
7. Enter the duration of your appointment in text format (e.g., "30 minutes", "1 hour", etc.).
8. Enter any additional information about your appointment.
9. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you want to select additional info.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Advisor** (text): Enter the name of your business advisor.
* **Date** (date): Enter the date of your desired appointment.
* **Time** (time): Enter the time of your desired appointment.
* **Business Advisor Email** (email): Enter the email of your business advisor.
* **Business Advisor Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of your business advisor.
* **Appointment Duration** (text): Enter the duration of your appointment in text format (e.g., "30 minutes", "1 hour", etc.).
* **Additional Info** (text): Enter any additional information about your appointment.
* **Select Business Advisor** (select_one): Select "Yes" to indicate if you need a business advisor.
* **Select Business Advisor 2** (select_multiple): Select "Yes" to indicate if you want to select multiple business advisors.
* **Appointment Time Zone** (time): Enter the time zone of your desired appointment.
* **Select Additional Info** (select_multiple): Select "Yes" to indicate if you want to select additional info.
* **Select Business Advisor** and **Select Business Advisor 2** are duplicate fields that can be merged into a single field. The intended purpose of these fields was to collect information about the business advisor, but they can be replaced with a single field that allows users to select multiple business advisors.
*   Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you want to select additional info.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth appointment process.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact our support team for assistance.
