# Sleep Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users gather information about their sleep patterns and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please select all applicable forms from the "Sleep Consultation Form" section.
* Answer the "Sleep Quality" question by selecting a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is the worst and 10 is the best sleep quality.
* Answer the "Duration" question by selecting the number of hours you sleep each night.
* Answer the "Frequency" question by selecting how often you sleep during the day.
* Select your preferred "Sleep Schedule" from the available options (Morning, Afternoon, Night).
* Choose your wake-up time from the available options.
* Answer the "Sleep Quality Rating" question by selecting a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is the worst and 10 is the best sleep quality.
* Select all applicable symptoms from the "Symptoms" list.
* Enter any recommendations or observations in the "Recommendations" field.
* Select all applicable forms from the "Sleep Consultation Form 1-10" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sleep Consultation Form**: This section allows you to select up to 10 different forms to help us better understand your sleep needs and experiences.
	+ **Sleep Consultation Form 1**: Select this form if you need help with sleep quality.
	+ **Sleep Consultation Form 2**: Select this form if you need help with sleep scheduling.
	+ **Sleep Consultation Form 3**: Select this form if you need help with sleep disorders.
	+ **Sleep Consultation Form 4**: Select this form if you need help with sleep medications.
	+ **Sleep Consultation Form 5**: Select this form if you need help with sleep environment.
	+ **Sleep Consultation Form 6**: Select this form if you need help with sleep habits.
	+ **Sleep Consultation Form 7**: Select this form if you need help with sleep routines.
	+ **Sleep Consultation Form 8**: Select this form if you need help with sleep goals.
	+ **Sleep Consultation Form 9**: Select this form if you need help with sleep challenges.
	+ **Sleep Consultation Form 10**: Select this form if you need help with sleep resources.
* **Sleep Quality**: This question asks about your sleep quality, with 1 being the worst and 10 being the best.
* **Duration**: This question asks about the number of hours you sleep each night.
* **Frequency**: This question asks about how often you sleep during the day.
* **Sleep Schedule**: This question asks about your preferred sleep schedule (Morning, Afternoon, Night).
* **Wake-up Time**: This question asks about your wake-up time.
* **Sleep Quality Rating**: This question asks about your sleep quality rating, with 1 being the worst and 10 being the best.
* **Symptoms**: This list of symptoms includes:
	+ **Insomnia**: Difficulty falling asleep or staying asleep.
	+ **Snoring**: Loud snoring that disrupts sleep.
	+ **Sleepiness**: Feeling tired or drowsy during the day.
	+ **Daytime Fatigue**: Feeling fatigued or exhausted during the day.
	+ **Mood Swings**: Mood changes due to sleep deprivation.
	+ **Concentration Problems**: Difficulty concentrating due to sleep deprivation.
	+ **Memory Loss**: Difficulty remembering things due to sleep deprivation.
	+ **Sleep Apnea**: Abnormal breathing during sleep.
	+ **Restless Leg Syndrome**: Uncomfortable sensations in the legs during sleep.
	+ **Sleep Talking**: Talking in your sleep.
	+ **Sleep Paralysis**: Feeling like you're paralyzed during sleep.
* **Recommendations**: Enter any recommendations or observations you have about your sleep.
* **Sleep Consultation Form 1-10**: Select up to 10 forms to help us better understand your sleep needs and experiences.
