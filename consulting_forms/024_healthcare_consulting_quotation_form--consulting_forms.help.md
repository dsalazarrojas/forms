# Healthcare Consulting Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Healthcare Consulting Quotation Form is designed to gather essential information from patients for the purpose of creating quotes or proposals for healthcare-related consulting services. This form ensures that all necessary details are collected in a structured and organized manner, facilitating a more efficient and accurate consultation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Patient Name**, which should match the name of the individual or organization seeking the consulting services.
2. Provide your **Contact Number** to enable our consultants to reach out to you directly.
3. Enter your **Email** address so that we can send you quotes or proposals.
4. Choose the **Services** you require from the provided options, which may include various healthcare consulting services.
5. Select the **Location** where you would like to receive the consulting services, if not already specified in the services section.
6. Select a date and time for the consultation, taking into account the availability of our consultants.
7. If you have any specific **Note** or special requirements, please include them in the corresponding field.
8. If your required services are not listed, please specify them in the **Services Other** field.
9. If a specific location is not listed, please enter it in the **Location Other** field.
10. Finally, provide your **Consultant Name** and **Company** information so that we can reach you and your company for further communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the individual or organization seeking healthcare consulting services.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact number to enable our consultants to reach out to you directly.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for us to send quotes or proposals.
- **Services** (`services`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the healthcare consulting services you require from the options provided.
- **Location** (`location`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the location where you would like to receive the consulting services.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Select a date for the consultation, taking into account the availability of our consultants.
- **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Choose a time for the consultation that suits you best.
- **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): Provide any specific note or special requirements for the consulting services.
- **Services Other** (`services_other`, text, required/optional): Enter any other services you require if not listed above.
- **Location Other** (`location_other`, text, required/optional): Enter a location if not listed above.
- **Consultant Name** (`consultant_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the consultant or professional providing the services.
- **Company** (`consultant_company`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the company providing the consulting services.
- **Email** (`consultant_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the consultant or company.

## Tips
- Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
- Review your services and location carefully to ensure you've selected the correct options.
- If you have any doubts or questions, please contact us for assistance.
