# sprint_service_agreement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sprint_service_agreement_form is a tool to help project managers or team leads determine if the team is adequately prepared for a sprint. It collects information about the team's ability to perform specific services during the sprint, as well as their scope and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's pages and ensure you've selected the correct information.
2. For each field, provide the relevant information as requested.
3. Ensure you've provided sufficient information to accurately represent the team's capabilities and objectives.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **sprint_details** (select_one): Choose 'Yes' if the team is prepared to perform the services required for the sprint, or 'No' if they are not.
* **team_details** (select_multiple): Select 'Yes' for each service that the team is capable of performing, or 'No' for those they are not.
* **scope** (text): Enter a brief description of the scope of the work to be performed during the sprint.
* **goals_and_objectives** (text): Enter the team's objectives and goals for the sprint.

## Tips
* Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required information is provided.
