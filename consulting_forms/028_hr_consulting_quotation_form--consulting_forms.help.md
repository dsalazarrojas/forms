# HR Consulting Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients for the purpose of creating quotations for HR consulting services. It's crucial to gather the right information to provide an accurate and detailed quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide the required information for the **Form Data** (page 1).
2. Choose the relevant **Client Info** option (page 2).
3. Select the services offered from the list (page 3).
4. Enter the **Quotation Details** for the services provided (page 4).
5. Choose the **Communication Channels** to be used for the quotation (page 5).
6. Select the client's **Client Preferences** for the quotation (page 6).
7. Enter the **Quotation Amount** for the services provided (page 7).
8. Sign and date the form to confirm completion (page 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data**: A free-text field where you can enter any additional information about the consultation or services provided.
* **Client Info**: Please select "Yes" if the client is a new or existing customer.
* **Services Offered**: Choose all the services you will provide for the client (e.g., "Yes" for each service).
* **Quotation Details**: Enter a detailed description of the services and their corresponding costs.
* **Communication Channels**: Select all the channels you will use to communicate with the client (e.g., "Yes" for email, phone, etc.).
* **Client Preferences**: Select all the client's preferences for the quotation (e.g., "Yes" for language, format, etc.).
* **Quotation Amount**: Enter the total amount for the services provided.
* **Form Signature**: Sign and date this form to confirm completion and agreement with the quotation terms.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and consistently.
* Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the HR consulting team for assistance.
