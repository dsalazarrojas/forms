# Business Impact Measurement Consulting Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for companies to submit applications for consulting services related to business impact measurement. Our team will use this information to understand your company's needs and provide customized consulting services to help you achieve your measurement goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out your company's profile information, including your name and project type.
2. Describe your current measurement tools usage and challenges in tracking impact.
3. Share your primary measurement goals and the biggest challenges you are facing in this area.
4. Inform us about the availability of your existing data and any potential obstacles to its analysis.
5. Provide us with your preferred start date for the consulting project and your primary contact details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (1): Please enter your company's name for identification purposes.
* **Project Type** (3): Select the type of impact you are looking to measure, such as Social Impact Assessment, Environmental / ESG Reporting, or Economic Impact Analysis.
* **Company Profile** (4): This is a note section to provide additional information about your company.
* **Current Measurement Tools in Use** (5): Choose all the tools you currently use for measurement, such as internal spreadsheets, specialized software, or third-party frameworks.
* **Measurement Goals** (6): Describe what you aim to achieve with this data and why it's crucial for your business.
* **Biggest Challenge** (7): Identify the main obstacle you face in tracking impact, such as data collection, lack of expertise, or stakeholder alignment.
* **Existing Data Availability Status** (8): Evaluate the readiness of your data for analysis, considering its structure and quality.
* **Expected Duration of Consulting** (10): Select the target timeline for the consulting project, whether it's 3 months, 6 months, 12 months, or ongoing.
* **Budget Allocation for Project (USD)** (11): Although not required, providing a budget range helps us tailor our proposal to your needs.
* **Primary Contact Person** (12): Enter your primary contact person's name and title for our team to communicate with.
* **Contact Email Address** (13): Provide your contact email address for future communications.
* **Preferred Start Date** (14): Select the preferred start date for the consulting project.
* **Application Date** (15): This field is automatically filled with the current date.

By following this guide, you should be able to complete the form accurately and efficiently, ensuring that our team has the necessary information to provide you with the best consulting services for your business impact measurement needs.
