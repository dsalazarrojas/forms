# Process Optimization Research Collaboration Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The form is used to gather information from participants in a Process Optimization Research Collaboration project. This information will be used to understand your interests, preferences, and needs, ensuring a collaborative and productive research environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your Participant Details.
2. Select your Research Interests.
3. Choose your Collaboration Preferences.
4. Choose your preferred No Code Form Builder (if applicable).
5. Select your Drag and Drop Interface preference (if applicable).
6. Add any Participant Comments or notes you'd like to share with the team.
7. Enter your Email address.
8. Enter your Phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, optional): Fill in your name and any other details you'd like to share with the team.
* **Research Interests** (`research_interests`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the topics or areas that interest you the most in the project. This will help us understand your focus and expectations.
* **Collaboration Preferences** (`collaboration_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Select the collaboration methods you prefer, such as in-person, remote, or a combination of both.
* **No Code Form Builder** (`no_code_form_builder`, select_one, optional): If you're comfortable with no-code form builders, choose the one you'd like to use for the project.
* **Drag and Drop Interface** (`drag_and_drop_interface`, select_one, optional): If you have experience with drag-and-drop interfaces, choose the one you'd like to use for the project.
* **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to share with the team.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your Email address so we can contact you with updates and information about the project.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your Phone number so we can contact you if needed.

Remember to submit your registration once you've completed all the fields. Your input is valuable, and we appreciate your contribution to the project!
