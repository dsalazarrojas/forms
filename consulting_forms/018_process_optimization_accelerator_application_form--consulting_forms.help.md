# Process Optimization Accelerator Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from companies interested in optimizing their business processes. It will help us understand their current processes, goals, and project details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each field in the form, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please let us know in the "Do you have any other comments or questions?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your current process?** (`process_optimization_accelerator_application_form_page_1`, text, Required): This field is where you should describe your company's current process in detail.
* **What is your process goal?** (`process_optimization_accelerator_application_form_page_2`, text, Required): In this field, describe what you aim to achieve through process optimization.
* **What is the name of your business?** (`process_optimization_accelerator_application_form_page_3`, text, Required): Provide the name of your company.
* **What is your current role in the business?** (`process_optimization_accelerator_application_form_page_4`, text, Required): Describe your role within the company.
* **What is the current pain point?** (`process_optimization_accelerator_application_form_page_5`, text, Required): Describe the specific problem or issue you are trying to address through process optimization.
* **What is your desired outcome?** (`process_optimization_accelerator_application_form_page_6`, text, Required): Specify what you hope to achieve through the project.
* **What is your budget for this project?** (`process_optimization_accelerator_application_form_page_7`, number, Required): Provide the budget allocated for the project.
* **How did you hear about us?** (`process_optimization_accelerator_application_form_page_8`, text, Required): Tell us how you found out about us.
* **How many employees does your company have?** (`process_optimization_accelerator_application_form_page_9`, number, Required): Indicate the number of employees in your company.
* **How many locations does your company have?** (`process_optimization_accelerator_application_form_page_10`, number, Required): Provide the number of locations your company has.
* **What is your company's annual revenue?** (`process_optimization_accelerator_application_form_page_11`, number, Required): Enter your company's annual revenue.
* **What is the current date of your project start?** (`process_optimization_accelerator_application_form_page_12`, date, Required): Enter the start date of your project.
* **What is the current date of your project end?** (`process_optimization_accelerator_application_form_page_13`, date, Required): Enter the end date of your project.
* **What is the time of your project start?** (`process_optimization_accelerator_application_form_page_14`, time, Required): Enter the start time of your project.
* **What is the time of your project end?** (`process_optimization_accelerator_application_form_page_15`, time, Required): Enter the end time of your project.
* **What is your email address?** (`process_optimization_accelerator_application_form_page_16`, email, Required): Provide your company's email address.
* **What is your phone number?** (`process_optimization_accelerator_application_form_page_17`, text, Required): Enter your company's phone number.
* **What is your project manager?** (`process_optimization_accelerator_application_form_page_18`, text, Required): Describe your project manager's role and responsibilities.
* **Do you have a project manager?** (`process_optimization_accelerator_application_form_page_19`, select_one, Required): Select "True" if you have a project manager.
* **What is your project status?** (`process_optimization_accelerator_application_form_page_20`, select_multiple, Required): Select all relevant project statuses (In Progress, Completed, Cancelled).
* **Do you have a project timeline?** (`process_optimization_accelerator_application_form_page_21`, select_multiple, Required): Select all relevant project timelines (Short term, Medium term, Long term).
* **What is your budget for this project?** (`process_optimization_accelerator_application_form_page_22`, number, Required): Enter the budget allocated for the project.
* **What is your budget for this project (optional)?** (`process_optimization_accelerator_application_form_page_23`, number, Required): Enter any additional, optional budget information.
* **Do you have any other comments or questions?** (`process_optimization_accelerator_application_form_page_24`, text, Optional): Use this field for any additional comments or questions you may have.
* **Do you have any other comments or questions (optional)?** (`process_optimization_accelerator_application_form_page_25`, text, Optional): Use this field for any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Tips
- Please ensure all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- If you have any questions or need further clarification, please use the "Do you have any other comments or questions?" field.
- This form is for process optimization and will help us understand your project details.
- Once completed, the form will be reviewed, and you will be contacted for further information or next steps.
