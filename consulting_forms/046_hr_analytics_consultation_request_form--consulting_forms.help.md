# hr_analytics_consultation_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request an analytics consultation with our team. This will help us understand your specific needs and schedule a consultation to discuss further.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the requested information, and we'll be in touch with you to confirm your consultation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
 * **First Name** (`First Name`, text, optional): Please enter your full first name.

### Last Name
 * **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, optional): Please enter your full last name.

### Contact Number
 * **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, text, optional): Please enter your contact number to ensure we can reach you to schedule the consultation.

### Email
 * **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address so we can reach you for further instructions or to provide updates.

### Analytics Needs
 * **Analytics Needs** (`Analytics Needs`, text, optional): Describe your specific analytics needs or questions, and we'll tailor the consultation to address them.

### Preferred Date
 * **Preferred Date** (`Preferred Date`, date, optional): If you have a specific date in mind for the consultation, please enter it here. If not, we'll contact you to schedule a date that suits you.

### Preferred Time
 * **Preferred Time** (`Preferred Time`, time, optional): If you have a specific time in mind for the consultation, please enter it here. If not, we'll contact you to schedule a time that suits you.

### Additional Information
 * **Additional Information** (`Additional Information`, text, optional): If there's anything else you'd like to share about your consultation, please let us know in the "Additional Information" field.

## Tips
* Be sure to check your email and contact number for updates and confirmations from our team.
* If you have any issues or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* Please note that we'll contact you to confirm your consultation details once we receive your request.
