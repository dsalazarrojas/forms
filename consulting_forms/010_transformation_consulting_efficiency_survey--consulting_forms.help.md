# Transformation Consulting Efficiency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transformation Consulting Efficiency Survey is designed to gather feedback on the consulting process from clients. It assesses the quality of services provided by consultants, including communication, availability, project management, and overall satisfaction. This survey helps consultants to identify areas of improvement and provides valuable insights for future projects.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project you are providing feedback on.
2. **Project Description**: Briefly describe the project in a few sentences.
3. **Consultant Name**: Enter the name of your consultant.
4. **Consultant Email**: Enter the email address of your consultant.
5. **Client Feedback**: Share your experience with the project, highlighting any positive or negative aspects.
6. **Communication Effectiveness**: Rate how effective your consultant's communication was during the project (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
7. **Communication Time Effectiveness**: Rate how efficient your consultant's communication time management was (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
8. **Consultant Availability**: Rate your consultant's availability to respond to your needs (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
9. **Project Impact**: Rate the impact of this project on your business (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
10. **Value for Money**: Rate the value for money of this project (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
11. **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the project (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
12. **Consultant Quality**: Rate the quality of your consultant's services (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
13. **Communication Quality**: Rate the quality of your consultant's communication (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
14. **Communication Time**: Rate the efficiency of your consultant's communication time management (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
15. **Consultant Availability 2**: Rate your consultant's availability to respond to your needs (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
16. **Project Scope**: Rate the project scope and its alignment with your expectations (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
17. **Project Progress**: Rate the project progress and its alignment with your expectations (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
18. **Client Relationship**: Rate the client relationship with your consultant (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
19. **Communication Clarity**: Rate the clarity of your consultant's communication (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
20. **Value for Money 2**: Rate the value for money of this project (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
21. **Project Goals**: Rate how well your consultant achieved your project goals (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
22. **Consultant Skills**: Rate your consultant's skills and expertise (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
23. **Communication Skills**: Rate your consultant's communication skills (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
24. **Project Timeline**: Rate how well your consultant managed the project timeline (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
25. **Project Budget**: Rate how well your consultant managed the project budget (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).

* Be honest and objective when providing feedback.
* Rate each field based on your actual experience with the project.
* Take care to answer each question carefully and accurately.
* This feedback will help improve the services provided by consultants.
