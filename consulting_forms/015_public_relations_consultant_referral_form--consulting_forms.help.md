# public_relations_consultant_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The public_relations_consultant_referral_form is a tool used to refer public relations consultants to potential clients. It helps in collecting information from the referrer, which is then used to match the best-suited consultant with the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. For each field, enter the requested information and ensure it is accurate.
3. Once you have completed all fields, click the submit button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company Name (\*):** Enter the name of the company you are referring.
- **Contact Person (\*):** Enter the first and last name of the person you are referring.
- **Phone Number (\*):** Enter the phone number of the contact person.
- **Email Address (\*):** Enter the email address of the contact person.
- **Company Address (\*):** Enter the address of the company.
- **Job Title (\*):** Enter the job title of the contact person.
- **Company (\*):** Enter the name of the company again.
- **Industry (\*):** Select the industry the company belongs to.
  • Software
  • Financial
  • Technology
- **Services (\*):** Select the services offered by the company.
  • Media relations
  • Crisis Communications
  • Public relations
- **Message (\*):** Enter a brief message about the referral.
- **Assigned to (\*):** Enter the name of the consultant assigned to this referral.
- **Output File (\*):** Enter the file name or ID of the output file.
- **Category (\*):** Select the category for this referral.
  • Consulting Forms
  • Marketing Materials
  • Other
- **Created by:** Enter the name of the person who created this referral (optional).
- **Form ID:** Enter the ID of the form (optional).
- **Description:** Enter a brief description of the referral (optional).

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
- Double-check the accuracy of the information entered in the form.
- This form is designed to facilitate quick and efficient referrals, so please be concise in filling out the form.
- If you need help, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
