# Microneedling Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Microneedling Consultation Form is designed to collect information from clients for a microneedling consultation. This form is used to gather client information, medical history, and other relevant details to provide personalized advice or treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure that all required fields are filled out.
2. Enter your name, email, phone number, and address in the corresponding fields.
3. Provide your date of birth and time of visit.
4. Select your medical specialty and years of experience.
5. List any medical contraindications, current medications, skin conditions, and medical conditions that may impact your treatment.
6. List any allergies you have.
7. Provide information about any previous procedures you have had.
8. Add any notes or comments about your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (Page 1): Enter your name, email, phone number, and address to provide a clear way for us to contact you.
* **Skin Details** (Page 2): Describe your skin details and conditions, if any.
* **Medical History** (Page 3): Provide information about your medical history, including any conditions or treatments you have had.
* **Medical Contraindications** (Page 4): Select any medical conditions or allergies that may impact your treatment, such as allergic reactions, bleeding disorders, or open wounds.
* **Current Medications** (Page 5): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Skin Conditions** (Page 6): Describe any skin conditions you have.
* **Medical Conditions** (Page 7): Provide information about any medical conditions you have.
* **Allergies** (Page 8): List any allergies you have.
* **Previous Procedures** (Page 9): Describe any previous procedures you have had.
* **Notes** (Page 10): Add any notes or comments about your visit.
* **Email** (Page 11): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (Page 12): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Address** (Page 13): Enter your address so we can contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (Page 14): Enter your date of birth.
* **Time of Visit** (Page 15): Enter the time of your visit.
* **Medical Specialty** (Page 16): Select your medical specialty, such as dermatologist or plastic surgeon.
* **Years of Experience** (Page 17): Enter the number of years you have been practicing your medical specialty.

Note: The form is designed to be completed by clients who are seeking microneedling services. If you are not seeking microneedling services, this form may not be relevant to you.
