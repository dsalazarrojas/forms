# New Guest Digital Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for guests to request a digital consultation with the service provider. It helps the provider understand the guest's requirements and preferences, ensuring a personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Fill out all required fields, denoted by an asterisk (*) next to the label.
3. If you're unsure about any field, you can always ask the service provider for clarification.
4. Review your submission before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, **Text**, Required): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, **Text, Required)**: Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, **Email**, Optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted about your consultation.
* **Phone** (`phone`, **Text, Optional)**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted about your consultation.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, **Date, Optional)**: Enter your date of birth if you want to be identified as a minor or have specific service requirements.
* **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, **Date, Optional)**: Enter the date you are requesting service, if known.
* **Time of Service** (`time_of_service`, **Time, Optional)**: Enter the time you are requesting service, if known.
* **Duration of Service** (`duration_of_service`, **Select One, Optional)**: Choose the estimated duration of your service request (15-30, 30-60, or 60-90 minutes).
* **Description** (`description`, **Note, Optional)**: Provide any additional information about your service request.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, **Select One, Optional)**: Choose the tool you'd like to be assigned for your consultation (Chatjimmy or Other).
* **First Contact** (`first_contact`, **Select Multiple, Optional)**: Choose how you'd like to be contacted first about your consultation (Email, Phone, Chat, or Text).
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, **Select One, Optional)**: Choose your preferred language for the consultation (English, Spanish, or French).
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, **Text, Optional)**: Provide any additional information about your consultation.
* **Signature** (`signature`, **Text, Required)**: Enter your digital signature as proof of your submission.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, **Note, Optional)**: Review and acknowledge the terms and conditions of the service.
* **Confirm Signature** (`confirm_signature`, **Select One, Optional)**: Confirm that you've signed the form.
* **Confirm Terms and Conditions** (`confirm_terms_and_conditions`, **Select One, Optional)**: Confirm that you've reviewed and acknowledged the terms and conditions.

Note: This form requires a digital signature as proof of submission. Please ensure you have the necessary permissions to sign digitally before submitting.
