# Aromatherapy Client Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about the client, their medical history, and aromatherapy preferences. It will help the aromatherapist provide personalized and effective treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the required fields, marked as such on the form.
2. Be as detailed as possible when answering questions, especially those marked as "text" fields.
3. If you're unsure about any question, please ask the aromatherapist for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (2, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Phone Number** (3, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email Address** (4, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Date of Birth** (5, date, optional): If you'd like to share your date of birth for better understanding of your medical history.
* **Date of Consultation** (6, date, required): Enter the date of the consultation.
* **Primary Concern or Reason for Visit** (8, text, required): Describe the reason for your consultation in as much detail as possible.
* **How long has this been a concern?** (9, select_one, required): Choose how long you've been experiencing this issue.
* **Previous aromatherapy or similar treatments** (10, select_one, required): Indicate if you've had aromatherapy or similar treatments before.
* **Current Medical Conditions** (12, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions, list them here.
* **Known Allergies** (13, text, optional): List any known allergies, including essential oil sensitivities.
* **Current Medications** (14, text, optional): List any current medications.
* **Are you pregnant or nursing?** (15, select_one, optional): Choose your pregnancy status.
* **Current Stress Level** (16, select_one, required): Indicate your current stress level.
* **Sleep Quality** (17, select_one, optional): Describe your sleep quality.
* **Essential Oil Preferences or Favorite Scents** (19, text, optional): Share any essential oil preferences or favorite scents.
* **Essential Oils to Avoid** (20, text, optional): List any essential oils to avoid due to sensitivities or dislikes.
* **Preferred Treatment Method** (21, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferred treatment method.
* **What are your goals for this consultation?** (23, text, required): Describe your goals for this consultation.
* **Expected Outcome or Result** (24, text, optional): Share what you expect to achieve from this consultation.

## Tips

* Please fill in the form carefully and accurately.
* If you're unsure about any question, please ask the aromatherapist for clarification.
* Be as detailed as possible when answering questions.
* If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please list them accurately.
