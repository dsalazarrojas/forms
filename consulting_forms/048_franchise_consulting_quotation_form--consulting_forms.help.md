# Franchise Consulting Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for clients to provide details about themselves and their project to facilitate consultation services.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2.  Provide the client's phone number in the "Client Phone" field.
3.  Enter the client's email address in the "Client Email" field.
4.  Enter the client's address in the "Client Address" field.
5.  Provide the project's name in the "Project Name" field.
6.  Enter the project start date in the "Project Start Date" field.
7.  Enter the project budget in the "Project Budget" field.
8.  If necessary, provide information about the consultation in the "Consultation" field.
9.  Enter the desired quotation in the "Quotation" field.
10.  If necessary, provide the quotation date in the "Quotation Date" field.
11.  Enter the next steps for the project in the "Next Steps" field.
12.  Enter the consultant's name in the "Consultant Name" field.
13.  Provide the consultant's phone number in the "Consultant Phone" field.
14.  Enter the consultant's email address in the "Consultant Email" field.
15.  Finally, enter your signature in the "Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the client's name.
- **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, required): Enter the client's phone number.
- **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter the client's email address.
- **Client Address** (`client_address`, text, required): Enter the client's address.
- **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the project.
- **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, date, required): Enter the project start date.
- **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, number, required): Enter the project budget.
- **Consultation** (`consultation`, text, optional): Provide information about the consultation.
- **Quotation** (`quotation`, text, optional): Enter the desired quotation.
- **Quotation Date** (`quotation_date`, date, optional): Enter the quotation date.
- **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional): Enter the next steps for the project.
- **Consultant Name** (`consultant_name`, text, required): Enter the consultant's name.
- **Consultant Phone** (`consultant_phone`, text, required): Enter the consultant's phone number.
- **Consultant Email** (`consultant_email`, email, required): Enter the consultant's email address.
- **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Enter your signature.
