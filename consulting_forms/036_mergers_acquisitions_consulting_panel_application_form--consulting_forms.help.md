# Mergers & Acquisitions Consulting Panel Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mergers & Acquisitions Consulting Panel Application Form is designed to collect information about potential consultants who wish to join the panel. The form will be used to evaluate their relevant experience, skills, and agreement to terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields as instructed. Please fill out all required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your company's name as it appears in public records.
* **Company URL** (`company_url`, text, required/optional): Provide your company's website URL if applicable.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Input your contact phone number for easy communication.
* **Consultant Name** (`consultant_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your personal name or title as it appears in public records.
* **Industry Experience** (`industry_experience`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check 'Yes' if you have experience in the mergers and acquisitions industry or related field.
* **Relevant Work Experience** (`relevant_work_experience`, text, required/optional): Describe your relevant work experience related to mergers and acquisitions or related fields.
* **Relevant Courses** (`relevant_courses`, text, required/optional): List any relevant courses or training programs you have completed.
* **Certifications** (`certifications`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check 'Yes' if you hold any relevant industry certifications.
* **Agree** (`agree`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' to acknowledge you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Agree to Terms** (`agree_terms`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' to confirm you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.

## Tips
Please fill out the form honestly and accurately to ensure your application is considered for the panel. Ensure you check 'Yes' for all 'Agree' fields to confirm your agreement to terms and conditions.
