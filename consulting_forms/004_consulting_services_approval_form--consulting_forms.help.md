# Consulting Services Approval Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and process requests for consulting services from internal teams. The form is used to gather essential information about the project, the requester, and the approval status, ensuring that each request is properly assessed and authorized before commencing services. This guide will walk you through each section of the form, helping you complete it accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you are submitting a valid and well-structured request to avoid duplication or rejection.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
3. Be prepared to provide additional details in the 'Business justification' field to help approvers understand the need for consulting services.
4. Select the correct approval status after reviewing the request.
5. Provide approver comments if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project name** (`project_name`, text, required): Enter a short descriptive title for the project.
* **Requester name** (`requester_name`, text, required): Provide the name of the person requesting consulting services.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Identify the internal unit or department submitting the request.
* **Requested start date** (`requested_start_date`, date, required): Enter the preferred commencement date for the consulting engagement.
* **Estimated duration weeks** (`estimated_duration_weeks`, number, required): Specify the expected number of weeks for the consulting engagement.
* **Estimated budget** (`estimated_budget`, number, required): Enter the estimated total budget for the engagement in USD.
* **Business justification** (`business_justification`, text, required): Clearly explain the reason for the consulting support and desired outcomes.
* **Approval status** (`approval_status`, select_one, required): Select one of the three options: 'Pending', 'Approved', or 'Rejected'.
* **Approver name** (`approver_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person approving the request.
* **Approver comments** (`approver_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback from the approver.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled accurately to avoid delays in processing your request.
* If you are unsure about any field, reach out to your internal support team for clarification.
* Review your request carefully before submission to minimize the need for corrections or revisions.
