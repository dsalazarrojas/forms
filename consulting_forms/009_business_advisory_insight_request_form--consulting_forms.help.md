# Business Advisory Insight Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients requesting business advisory services. Please fill out this form to provide detailed insights on your organization's needs and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your organization's details, including the company name, industry sector, size, and annual revenue range.
2. Enter your contact information, including your name, job title, email address, and phone number.
3. Describe the primary topic you're seeking advisory on and provide specific challenges or issues you're facing.
4. Outline your business goals and the efforts you've taken so far.
5. Specify your expected outcomes and the timeline for achieving these outcomes.
6. If applicable, indicate if internal approval is required for recommendations.
7. Add any additional comments or context to help us better understand your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company Name** (`company_name`, required, text): Enter your company's legal name.
- **Industry Sector** (`industry_sector`, required, text): Describe your company's primary industry or market.
- **Company Size** (`company_size`, required, select_one): Choose the approximate number of employees.
- **Annual Revenue Range** (`annual_revenue`, required, select_one): Select your company's approximate annual revenue range.
- **Requester Name** (`requester_name`, required, text): Enter your name as the person making the request.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, required, text): Enter your job title or position.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, required, email): Enter your business email address.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, required, text): Enter your direct contact phone number.
- **Primary Topic of Request** (`primary_topic`, required, select_one): Choose the main area requiring advisory.
- **Specific Challenges or Issues** (`specific_challenges`, required, text): Provide a detailed description of the challenges or issues.
- **Business Goals** (`business_goals`, required, text): Outline your company's goals and objectives.
- **Current Efforts or Attempts** (`current_efforts`, optional, text): Describe any steps taken so far to address the challenges or issues.
- **Timeline for Solution** (`timeline`, required, select_one): Choose the expected timeline for achieving results.
- **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, optional, select_one): Select your estimated budget for consulting services.
- **Expected Outcomes** (`expected_outcomes`, required, text): Define what success looks like in terms of outcomes.
- **Key Decision Makers** (`decision_makers`, optional, text): Identify key decision-makers who need to approve recommendations.
- **Board or Executive Approval Needed** (`approval_needed`, optional, select_one): Indicate if internal approval is required for recommendations.
- **Additional Comments or Context** (`additional_comments`, optional, text): Provide any other relevant information or context.
