# ESG Framework Development Program Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The ESG Framework Development Program is designed to support organizations in developing and integrating Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) frameworks to enhance their sustainability practices and reporting. The program aims to foster cooperation, knowledge sharing, and capacity-building among participating organizations to promote a culture of sustainable development.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section thoroughly before answering the questions.
2. Be concise and clear in your answers, ensuring that you have addressed each question accurately.
3. If a question does not apply to your organization, please mark the option that best represents "Not Applicable" or describe your organization's unique circumstances in the provided text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **applicant** (Select one: ESG Framework Developer, Sustainability Consultant, ESG Framework Project Manager): Select the category that best describes your role within the organization.
* **organization_name** (Text): Enter your organization's name.
* **contact_email** (Email): Enter your contact email address.
* **contact_phone** (Text): Enter your contact phone number (optional).
* **organization_address** (Text): Enter your organization's address.
* **esg_readiness** (Select one: High, Medium, Low): Evaluate your organization's ESG readiness level. Please note that this is a self-assessment and not an expert evaluation.
* **goals_participation** (Text): Describe the goals you are seeking participation in ESG development programs for your organization.
* **esg_development_programs** (Select multiple): Select the ESG development programs your organization is interested in (optional).
* **esg_commitment_statement** (Text): Provide a brief statement of your organization's commitment to ESG.
* **esg_framework_development** (Text): Explain how your organization will implement ESG in your operations and practices.
* **esg_framework_readiness** (Text): Describe your organization's ESG readiness level and the steps planned to improve it.
