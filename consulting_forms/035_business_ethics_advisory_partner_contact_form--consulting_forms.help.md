# Business Ethics Advisory Partner Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Ethics Advisory Partner Contact Form is designed to gather information from individuals or organizations seeking expert guidance on business ethics concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide your contact information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the type of inquiry you are making (e.g., Ethics Consultation, Compliance Review, Policy Advisory, Training Request, etc.).
3. Describe the ethics concern you are experiencing (e.g., conflict of interest, harassment, labor practices, etc.).
4. Choose the relevant services you are interested in (e.g., consultation, investigation, remediation plan, training, etc.).
5. Indicate the urgency level of your inquiry (e.g., Routine, Standard, Urgent, Critical, etc.).
6. Specify when you would like to see action on your inquiry (e.g., Immediate, Within 1 week, Within 2 weeks, etc.).
7. Provide any additional information that may be helpful for the advisory team to understand your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Required): Your complete name for communication purposes.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter your business email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your contact phone number for quick communication.
* **Organization Name** (Required): Enter your company or organization name.
* **Job Title** (Required): Enter your job title for context.
* **Department** (Optional): If applicable, enter your department name for context.
* **Type of Inquiry** (Required): Select the type of inquiry you are making (e.g., Ethics Consultation, Compliance Review, Policy Advisory, etc.).
* **Issue Description** (Required): Describe the ethics concern you are experiencing.
* **Issue Category** (Required): Select all relevant categories related to your issue (e.g., Conflict of Interest, Fraud or Misconduct, Harassment, etc.).
* **Issue Timeline** (Required): Select when the issue occurred or when it is expected to occur.
* **People Involved** (Optional): Enter the number of people involved in the issue.
* **Documentation Available** (Optional): Indicate if you have supporting documentation available.
* **Services Requested** (Required): Select all relevant services you are interested in (e.g., consultation, investigation, remediation plan, training, etc.).
* **Urgency Level** (Required): Select the level of urgency for your inquiry (e.g., Routine, Standard, Urgent, Critical, etc.).
* **Timeline for Resolution** (Required): Specify when you would like to see action on your inquiry.
* **Budget Range** (Optional): Enter your estimated budget range for the services.
* **Additional Information** (Optional): Provide any other relevant details that may be helpful for the advisory team.
