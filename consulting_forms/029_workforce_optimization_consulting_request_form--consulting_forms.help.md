# Workforce Optimization Consulting Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from end users about their workforce challenges and optimization needs. It helps internal teams understand project inquires, workforce challenges, and consultation requests efficiently, enabling effective consultation and solution implementation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief project inquiry or description in the "Project Inquiry" field.
2. Describe your current workforce challenge in the "Workforce Challenge" field.
3. If you would like to request consultation or support, type your request in the "Consultation Request" field.
4. Provide the start and end dates for your project in "Project Start Date" and "Project End Date" fields, respectively.
5. Specify your preferred consultation scheduling in the "Consultation Scheduling" field.
6. Indicate the consulting firm(s) you would like to use for your project in the "Consulting Firm" field.
7. Mention any specific HR teams you would like to involve in the project in the "HR Team" field.
8. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
9. Provide your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
10. Give a detailed description of your project in the "Project Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Inquiry** (Project Inquiry, text, optional): A brief description of your project or challenge.
* **Workforce Challenge** (Workforce Challenge, text, optional): A detailed description of your current workforce challenges.
* **Consultation Request** (Consultation Request, text, optional): A specific request for consultation or support.
* **Project Start Date** (Project Start Date, date, optional): The start date of your project.
* **Project End Date** (Project End Date, date, optional): The end date of your project.
* **Consultation Scheduling** (Consultation Scheduling, text, optional): Your preferred consultation scheduling details.
* **Consulting Firm** (Consulting Firm, text, optional): The consulting firm(s) you would like to use for your project.
* **HR Team** (HR Team, text, optional): The HR team(s) you would like to involve in your project.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, optional): Your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, optional): Your contact phone number.
* **Project Description** (Project Description, text, optional): A detailed description of your project. 

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submission.
* Review your form carefully to avoid duplication of efforts.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to our support team.
