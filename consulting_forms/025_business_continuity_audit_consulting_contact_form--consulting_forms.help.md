# Business Continuity Audit Consulting Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help our business continuity consulting services understand your company's needs and provide you with suitable advice on how to improve your business continuity program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required information about your company, including your name, industry, location, and number of employees.
2.  Provide contact information, including your name, title, department, email, and phone number.
3.  Specify the type of audit you need and the areas you want to focus on.
4.  Mention any specific concerns or issues you have about your business continuity program.
5.  If you've had previous audits, indicate that in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name**: Your company's legal name.
* **Industry Sector**: Your company's primary industry.
* **Company Headquarters Location**: The city and state where your company is located.
* **Number of Operating Locations**: How many facilities your company operates.
* **Total Employees**: Overall staff count.
* **Your Name**: Your full name.
* **Your Title**: Your position or role in the company.
* **Your Department**: Your department or function in the company.
* **Email Address**: Your business email address.
* **Phone Number**: Your direct contact phone number.
* **Type of Audit Needed**: The type of business continuity audit you need (e.g., Business Continuity Plan Audit, Disaster Recovery Audit, etc.).
* **Audit Scope**: The areas to focus on during the audit (e.g., People and Staffing, Technology Systems, etc.).
* **Most Critical Business Functions**: The most critical operations of your business.
* **Current BC Program Status**: The current status of your business continuity program (e.g., no formal program, plan exists but untested, etc.).
* **Audit Goals**: What you want to achieve through the audit.
* **Specific Concerns or Issues**: Any known gaps or problems in your business continuity program.
* **Previous BC Audits**: Have you had audits before? If so, indicate that here.
* **Preferred Audit Timeline**: When you would like the audit to occur.
* **Estimated Budget**: The estimated budget range for the audit.
* **Additional Notes**: Any other information you want to share with us.

Please note that all fields are required unless otherwise stated. If you have any questions or concerns about any field, please don't hesitate to contact us.
