# Consulting Company Storage Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your company's storage needs, ensuring we provide you with the best possible services. Please answer the questions accurately, and if you have any doubts, we'll be happy to help.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate information about your company's storage needs.
2. Review each field carefully and ensure you've entered the correct information.
3. When you're finished, submit the form, and our team will review it to create a tailored plan for your storage requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name**: `Company Name`
	+ (1) A text field to input your company's name.
	+ This is the name of your organization.
* **Company Address**: `Company Address`
	+ (2) A text field to input your company's street address.
	+ This is the address where your company is located.
* **City**: `City`
	+ (3) A text field to input the city where your company is located.
	+ This is the city location of your company.
* **State or Province**: `State or Province`
	+ (4) A text field to input the state or province where your company is located.
	+ This is the state or province where your company is located.
* **ZIP or Postal Code**: `ZIP or Postal Code`
	+ (5) A text field to input your company's postal code.
	+ This is the postal code of your company.
* **Contact Name**: `Contact Name`
	+ (6) A text field to input the name of your primary contact person.
	+ This is the primary contact person for your company.
* **Contact Email**: `Contact Email`
	+ (7) A text field to input your company's email address.
	+ This is the email address of your company.
* **Contact Phone**: `Contact Phone`
	+ (8) A text field to input your company's phone number.
	+ This is the phone number of your company.
* **Storage Type Needed**: `Storage Type Needed`
	+ (9) A select-one field with options for your storage type needs.
	+ Select the type of storage you require (e.g., Document Storage, Digital Data Storage, etc.).

	+ (13) A select-multiple field with options for security features needed.

	+ (14) A select-one field with options for how often you'll need access.

* **Preferred Access Hours**: `Preferred Access Hours`
	+ (15) A select-one field with options for preferred access hours.
	+ Select when you need access (e.g., 24/7 Access, Business Hours Only, Extended Hours, By Appointment, etc.).
* **Items to Store**: `Items to Store`
	+ (16) A text field to describe what you'll be storing.
	+ Please provide a detailed description of the items you'll be storing.
* **Estimated Volume**: `Estimated Volume`
	+ (17) A text field to approximate the quantity or volume of items.
	+ Please provide an estimated quantity or volume of items.
* **Special Handling Requirements**: `Special Handling Requirements`
	+ (18) A text field to describe any special care or handling needs.
	+ Please list any special care or handling requirements for the items you'll be storing.

	+ (19) A select-one field with options for storing hazardous materials.
	+ Select if you'll be storing hazardous materials (e.g., True, False, etc.).

	+ Please select your preferred start date for storage.
