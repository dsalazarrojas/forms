# Creative Design Risk Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Creative Design Risk Assessment form is used to identify and document potential risks that may impact a project, along with plans to mitigate those risks. This form helps project managers and team members to provide a structured and thorough risk assessment for each project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the project from the "Design Project Id" field. This will automatically populate the "Project Description" field with the corresponding project description.
2. Describe the project in the "Project Description" field.
3. Select the project category from the "Project Category" field. This will help in categorizing the project for further reference.
4. List the risk factors that could impact the project in the "Risk Factors" field.
5. Select the risk level and risk impact from the "Risk Level" and "Risk Impact" fields, respectively.
6. Enter the risk probability in the "Risk Probability" field.
7. Provide a brief summary of the identified risks in the "Risk Summary" field.
8. List the plans to mitigate the risks in the "Mitigation Plans" field.
9. Enter the risk level impact in the "Risk Level Impact" field.
10. Enter the mitigation impact in the "Mitigation Impact" field.
11. Enter the risk risk impact in the "Risk Risk Impact" field.
12. Select the project status from the "Project Status" field.
13. Enter any additional project notes in the "Project Notes" field.
14. Enter the project deadline in the "Project Deadline" field.
15. Enter the project completion date in the "Project Completion Date" field.
16. Enter the assigned user's name in the "Assigned User" field.
17. Enter the assigned user's ID in the "Assigned User ID" field.
18. Enter the assigned group's name in the "Assigned Group" field.
19. Enter the assigned group's ID in the "Assigned Group ID" field.
20. Enter the submitted by user's name in the "Submitted By" field.
21. Enter the submitted date in the "Submitted Date" field.
22. Enter the updated by user's name in the "Updated By" field.
23. Enter the updated date in the "Updated Date" field.
24. Enter any comments in the "Comments" field.
