# ESG Transition Consulting Network Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ESG Transition Consulting Network Application form is designed to collect information from individuals or organizations interested in joining the ESG Transition Consulting Network. This form will help us understand the applicant's qualifications, experience, and contact information to assess their suitability for the network.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the application form in English.
2. Select all relevant experience areas that you have expertise in.
3. Enter a brief description of your experience.
4. Attach or link your resume.
5. Select your name and enter your contact details.
6. Confirm your submission by clicking the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Application** (1):
	* A text field for you to enter any additional comments or information about your application.
 
- **Experience** (2):
	* Please select all relevant experience areas that you have:
	+ 5+ years of experience in ESG
	+ Other

- **Expertise Areas** (3):
	* Please select one area of expertise:
	+ Financial
	+ Technical
	+ Strategic
	+ Operational

- **Resume** (4 and 11):
	* Either attach a resume document or enter a link to your resume online.

- **Contact** (15):
	* Please enter your name.
 
- **Email** (14):
	* Please enter a valid email address.
 
- **Phone** (15):
	* Enter your phone number.

- **Address** (16):
	* Enter your address.

- **Website** (17):
	* Enter the URL of your website.

- **Confirm Submit** (18):
	* Confirm that you have completed the form accurately.
 
- **Submission Comments** (19):
	* Enter any additional comments about your submission.

- **Submission Date** (20):
	* Enter the date of your submission.

- **Submission Time** (21):
	* Enter the time of your submission.

- **Submission Hour** (22):
	* Enter the hour of your submission.

- **Submission Minute** (23):
	* Enter the minute of your submission.

- **Submission Second** (24):
	* Enter the second of your submission.

- **Submit Button** (25):
	* Click this button to submit your application.
 
- **Experience Years** (8):
	* Enter the number of years you have experience in ESG.
 
- **Experience Description** (9):
	* Enter a brief description of your experience.
