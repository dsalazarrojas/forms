# plasma_pen_consultation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information from individuals who are consulting with a healthcare professional, including consultation details, patient names, and medical histories.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on "Consultation Details" page.
2. Enter any additional details about the consultation (optional).
3. Click on "Patient Name" page.
4. Enter the patient's name.
5. Click on "Medical History" page.
6. Enter any relevant medical history information about the patient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consultation Details** (`consultation_details`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for entering any additional details about the consultation.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required):
	+ This field is for entering the patient's name.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required):
	+ This field is for entering any relevant medical history information about the patient.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and detailed information in the "Patient Name" and "Medical History" fields.
- The "Consultation Details" field is optional, so feel free to skip it if not necessary.
- Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
