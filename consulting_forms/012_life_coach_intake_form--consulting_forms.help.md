# Life Coach Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Life Coach Intake Form is used to collect information from clients about their coaching preferences, goals, and expectations. This information helps us to match clients with the best coach for their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read through the form carefully and only fill out the fields that apply to you.
- Answer each question truthfully and as accurately as possible.
- Please fill out all required fields (indicated by \*).
- You can skip or leave blank fields that are not applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. First Name (\*): 
* The First Name field is used to collect the client's first name.

### 2. Last Name (\*): 
* The Last Name field is used to collect the client's last name.

### 3. Email: 
* This field is used to collect the client's email address.
* Please enter a valid email address for correspondence and updates.

### 4. Phone Number: 
* This field is used to collect the client's phone number.
* Please enter a valid phone number for communication.

### 5. Date of Birth: 
* This field is used to collect the client's date of birth.
* If you don't know or are unsure, please leave this field blank.

### 6. Coach Level: 
* This field is used to choose your current coaching level:
	+ Beginner: Just starting out with coaching
	+ Intermediate: Have some experience with coaching
	+ Advanced: Highly experienced with coaching

### 7. Coaching Goals: 
* This field is used to describe what you want to achieve with coaching:
* Please write a brief description of your coaching goals.

### 8. Coaching Frequency: 
* This field is used to choose your preferred coaching frequency:
	+ Weekly: Coaching on a weekly basis
	+ Biweekly: Coaching every other week
	+ Monthly: Coaching once a month

### 9. Notes: 
* This field is used for any additional information or comments you'd like to add:
* Please keep this field brief and clear for the coach's understanding.

## Tips
- Make sure to review this form carefully before submitting.
- If you're unsure about any field, leave a question mark (\?) or ask your coach.
- Remember to be honest and accurate in your answers to help us provide the best service.
