# Low Code Development Consultation Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers about their projects and consultation requests. This information will be used for internal project management and consultation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Provide a brief description of your project in the **Project Description** field.
* Select the **Project Type** that best describes your project.
* Enter your **Lead Contact** information (email and phone number).
* Choose a **Consultation Scheduling** time slot that suits you.
* If your project has a deadline, enter it in the **Project Deadline** field.
* Provide your project's **Project Budget**.
* Select the **Lead Status** and **Lead Source** of your project lead.
* Enter any additional **Lead Qualifications** or **Lead Notes** about your project.
* Finally, add any additional comments or questions in the **Lead Comment** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details** (`project_details`): A brief description of your project.
* **Lead** (`project_lead`): Your name or company name.
* **Lead Contact** (`lead_contact`): Enter your email address and/or phone number.
* **Consultation Scheduling** (`consultation_scheduling`): Choose a date and time for the consultation.
* **Project Deadline** (`project_deadline`): Enter the deadline for your project, if applicable.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`): A more detailed description of your project.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`): Enter your project budget.
* **Project Type** (`project_type`): Select the type of project you are requesting consultation for. This will help us understand your project's specific needs.
* **Lead Email** (`lead_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Lead Phone** (`lead_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Consultation Time** (`consultation_time`): Choose the time slot for the consultation.
* **Lead Status** (`project_lead_status`): Select the current status of your project lead.
* **Lead Source** (`project_lead_source`): Select where you obtained your project lead from.
* **Lead Qualifications** (`lead_qualifications`): Add any additional qualifications or skills you have for your project.
* **Lead Notes** (`lead_notes`): Add any additional comments or questions about your project.
* **Lead Comment** (`project_lead_comment`): Enter any additional comments or questions you have about your project.
