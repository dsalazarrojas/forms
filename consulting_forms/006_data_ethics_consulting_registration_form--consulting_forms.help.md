# Data Ethics Consulting Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from end users about their needs and preferences related to data ethics consulting services. Please fill out this form to help us better understand your requirements and match you with suitable consultants or services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete each field to the best of your ability.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out (if any).
3. If a field is not relevant to you, please leave it blank.
4. If you need help or have questions, feel free to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, text, not required): This field is a free-form text area where you can provide any additional information about your registration.
* **Client Details** (`client_details`, text, not required): Please provide your company or organization name, contact person, and any other relevant details about your entity.
* **Interests** (`interests`, select multiple, not required): Select all the interests that apply to your data ethics consulting needs. You can choose multiple options from the list. (e.g., Compliance, Data Protection, Security)
* **Preferred Consultation Time** (`preferred_consultation_time`, select multiple, not required): Select your preferred time for consultation from the available options.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, not required): Provide your contact information, including name, title, email, and phone number.
* **Consultant Info** (`consultant_info`, text, not required): If you have a preferred consultant or have any specific requirements for the consultant, please provide their information.
* **Firm Info** (`firm_info`, text, not required): Provide information about your company or organization, including name, address, and any other relevant details.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when filling out the form to help us better understand your needs.
* If you have any questions or need help, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your time and effort in providing us with accurate and complete information.
