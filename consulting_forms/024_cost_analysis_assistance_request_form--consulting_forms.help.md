# Cost Analysis Assistance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cost Analysis Assistance Request Form is a tool used to collect and organize project information for cost analysis purposes. This form will help our team understand your project's specifics and gather the necessary details to provide accurate cost analysis and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your project details, including any relevant files or attachments.
2. Ask any questions related to your project.
3. Provide your contact information and email for follow-up questions or clarification.
4. Specify a due date for the project's cost analysis.
5. Choose who from our team to assign the cost analysis task to.
6. Optionally, indicate the priority level of the project for our team's awareness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Project Details
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, required): Please provide a brief description of your project, including its scope and objectives.
### Project Files
* **Project Files** (`project_files`, select_multiple, optional): If you have any relevant project files or attachments, please upload them here. You can select multiple files.
*   Options: You can choose between Option 1 and Option 2.
### Questions
* **Questions** (`questions`, note, optional): Ask any questions related to your project or cost analysis.
### Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide your name, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for our team to reach out to you with questions or updates.
### Due Date
* **Due Date** (`due_date`, time, optional): Specify the date by which you need the cost analysis completed.
*   Please note that due dates are not guaranteed and may vary based on the complexity of the project.
### Assigned To
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional): Choose who from our team to assign the cost analysis task to. Please select between Option 1 and Option 2.
### Priority
* **Priority** (`priority`, number, optional): Indicate the priority level of your project for our team's awareness.
