# Extension Pre Consultation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients and users about their needs and preferences for an extension project. The data collected will be used to tailor the consulting service to meet their specific requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions in the space provided.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select one or more options that apply to you.
3. If a question is not applicable, feel free to add any additional information to help the consultant understand your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Project Goals** (`extension_pre_consultation_survey`, text, optional): Briefly describe your project goals and objectives. This will help the consultant understand your overall project vision.
- **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, number, optional): Provide an estimate of your project budget. This will help the consultant understand your financial constraints and plan accordingly.
- **Extension Preferences** (`extension_preferences`, select multiple, optional): Select all preferences that apply to you from the options provided:
	+ *budget*: You have a specific budget in mind and would like to stay within it.
	+ *time*: You have a specific deadline or time frame in mind for the project.
	+ *location*: You have a specific location in mind where you would like the project to be executed.
- **Schedule Preferences** (`schedule_preferences`, select one, optional): Select one of the following options that best fits your project schedule:
	+ *urgent*: The project needs to be completed as soon as possible.
	+ *normal*: The project has a standard timeline and does not require urgent completion.
	+ *relaxed*: The project has a flexible timeline and can be completed at a leisurely pace.
- **Budget** (`budget`, number, optional): Provide an estimate of your project budget. This will help the consultant understand your financial constraints and plan accordingly.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately to ensure that the consultant has a clear understanding of your needs.
- If you have any additional comments or information, feel free to add them to the space provided.
