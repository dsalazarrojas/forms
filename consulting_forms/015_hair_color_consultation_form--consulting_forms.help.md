# Hair Color Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hair Color Consultation Form is designed to gather information from clients about their hair color, condition, length, type, porosity, diameter, and other relevant details. This information will be used by hair stylists or consultants to provide personalized services and follow up with clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select your hair color from the list of options.
2. Indicate your hair condition (e.g. dry, damaged, curly, etc.).
3. Choose your hair length (e.g. short, medium, long).
4. Select your hair type (e.g. curly, straight, wavy, etc.).
5. Choose your hair porosity (e.g. fine, coarse, medium).
6. Select your hair diameter (e.g. small, medium, large).
7. Add any notes or comments about your hair in the Client Note section.
8. If desired, choose follow-up methods for both clients and consultants.
9. Enter your email address and phone number for communication.
10. Enter your address, city, state, and zip code for location information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hair Color** (`hair_color_consultation_form`, select_one, required/optional): Select your preferred hair color from the list of options.
* **Hair Condition** (`hair_condition`, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate your hair's current condition (e.g. dry, damaged, curly, etc.).
* **Hair Length** (`hair_length`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your hair length (e.g. short, medium, long).
* **Hair Type** (`hair_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select your hair type (e.g. curly, straight, wavy, etc.).
* **Hair Porosity** (`hair_porosity`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose your hair's porosity (e.g. fine, coarse, medium).
* **Hair Diameter** (`hair_diameter`, select_one, required/optional): Select your hair diameter (e.g. small, medium, large).
* **Client Note** (`client_note`, note, required/optional): Add any notes or comments about your hair or consultation.
* **Client Follow-up** (`client_followup`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose follow-up methods for you (e.g. phone, email, text).
* **Consultant Follow-up** (`consultant_followup`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose follow-up methods for the consultant (e.g. phone, email, text).
* **Consultant Info** (`consultant_info`, text, required/optional): Enter information about the consultant.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required/optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`, text, required/optional): Enter your state.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required/optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the appointment date.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the appointment time.
* **Hair Length 2** (`hair_length2`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your hair length 2 (e.g. short, medium, long).
* **Hair Type 2** (`hair_type2`, select_one, required/optional): Select your hair type 2 (e.g. curly, straight, wavy, etc.).
* **Hair Porosity 2** (`hair_porosity2`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose your hair porosity 2 (e.g. fine, coarse, medium).
* **Hair Diameter 2** (`hair_diameter2`, select_one, required/optional): Select your hair diameter 2 (e.g. small, medium, large).
* **Client Follow-up 2** (`client_followup`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose follow-up methods 2 for you (e.g. phone, email, text).
* **Consultant Follow-up 2** (`consultant_followup`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose follow-up methods 2 for the consultant (e.g. phone, email, text).
* **Appointment Status** (`appointment_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the appointment status (e.g. scheduled, not scheduled, follow-up required).

## Tips
* Please select all relevant options for your hair color, condition, length, type, porosity, and diameter.
* If you have multiple follow-up methods, select all that apply.
* Enter your email address and phone number for easy communication.
* Enter your address, city, state, and zip code for location information.
* Use the Client Note section to add any notes or comments about your hair or consultation.
* Use the Consultant Info section to enter information about the consultant.
* Make sure to select the correct appointment status to indicate if the appointment is scheduled or not.
