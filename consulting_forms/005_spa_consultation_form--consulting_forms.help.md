# Spa Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spa Consultation Form is designed to collect information from potential customers about their spa preferences and needs. This information will be used to tailor their spa experience and ensure that they receive the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to provide clear information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out, as indicated by the red asterisk (*).
3. For the "How do you prefer to be contacted" field, select one of the options provided.
4. For the "Which service are you interested in" field, select all services that apply.
5. For the "Medical conditions" and "Allergies" fields, provide as much detail as possible.
6. For the "Are you taking any medications" field, provide a detailed list of all medications you are currently taking.
7. For the "Medical history" field, provide a detailed account of your medical history.
8. Once you have completed all fields, click the submit button to send your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name (Text)** (`name`, `text`, *required*): This field is used to collect your name. Please enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, `email`, *required*): Enter your valid email address. This will be used to contact you with your consultation results and appointment scheduling.
* **Contact Number (Text)** (`contact_number`, `text`, *required*): Enter your contact number. This can be a phone number or a text number.
* **How do you prefer to be contacted (Select One)** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_one`, *required*): Select one of the options provided to indicate how you would like to be contacted.
* **Consultation time (Time)** (`consultation_time`, `time`, *required*): Select a time for your consultation appointment.
* **Which service are you interested in (Select Multiple)** (`preferred_service`, `select_multiple`, *required*): Select all services that you are interested in, such as Massage, Haircut, Nail, or Makeup.
* **Do you have any medical conditions (Text)** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, *required*): If you have any medical conditions, please provide details about them.
* **Do you have any allergies (Text)** (`allergies`, `text`, *required*): If you have any allergies, please provide details about them.
* **Are you taking any medications (Text)** (`medication`, `text`, *required*): Please list all medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical history (Text)** (`medical_history`, `text`, *required*): Please provide a detailed account of your medical history.
