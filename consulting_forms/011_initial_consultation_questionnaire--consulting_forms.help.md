# Initial Consultation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Initial Consultation Questionnaire is a form designed to gather information for the provider's initial consultation with a client. It helps providers understand the client's needs, preferences, and goals, ensuring a smooth and effective consultation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section carefully, making sure to provide accurate and clear information.
2. Be honest and thorough in your responses to ensure the provider has a complete understanding of your needs.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact your provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Meeting Details (1)
* **First Meeting Details**: Enter information about your first meeting with the provider, including details about the meeting's purpose and any discussions or outcomes.

### Client Contact Info (2)
* **Client Contact Info**: Indicate whether you have provided your contact information to the provider or not.

### Meeting Outcome (3)
* **Meeting Outcome**: Select the outcome of your meeting with the provider, such as "Successful Onboarding" or "Reschedule Meeting".

### Client Goals (4)
* **Client Goals**: Enter your goals and objectives for the consultation process.

### Provider Info (5)
* **Provider Info**: Indicate whether the provider has informed you about their specialties or not.

### Communication Preferences (6)
* **Communication Preferences**: Select your preferred communication methods, such as "Yes" or "No".

### First Language (7)
* **First Language**: Select your first language, which is English, Spanish, or Other.
