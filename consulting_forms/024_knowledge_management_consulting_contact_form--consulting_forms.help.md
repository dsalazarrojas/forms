# knowledge_management_consulting_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The knowledge management consulting contact form is designed to gather information about your knowledge management consulting needs. It helps us understand your requirements, preferences, and constraints to provide you with tailored advice and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To get started, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required and optional fields based on your needs.
2. Be specific about your consulting needs and preferences.
3. Double-check your details before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name (<user_name>, text, optional)**: Please provide your name for internal reference and follow-up purposes.
* **User Email (<user_email>, email, optional)**: Enter your email address to allow us to get in touch with you for further clarification or follow-up.
* **User Phone (<user_phone, text, optional)**: Provide your phone number if you'd like to be contacted via phone.
* **Inquiry (<inquiry, text, optional)**: Describe your consulting needs, questions, or concerns in detail.
* **Company (<company, text, optional)**: If applicable, mention your company or organization name for context.
* **Location (<location, text, optional)**: If relevant, specify your location or time zone for scheduling purposes.
* **Contact Preferences (<contact_preferences, select_multiple, optional)**: Select the types of services you are interested in (e.g., Consulting Services, Strategy & Advisory Services, or Other).
* **Services Needed (<services_needed, select_one, optional)**: Choose the services you require (e.g., Consulting Services, Tools & Platforms, or Other).
* **Project Details (<project_details, date, optional)**: If applicable, provide the specific details of your project, including dates.
* **Submission Date (<submission_date, date, optional)**: If known, enter the date of your submission to help us track progress.
* **Follow-up Information (<follow_up_info, note, optional)**: Add any additional information that might help us understand your needs or preferences.
* **Assigned User (<assigned_user, note, optional)**: This field is used for internal purposes to assign tasks to relevant users.
* **Status (<status, select_one, optional)**: Select the current status of your inquiry (e.g., Draft, In Progress, or Completed).
* **Assigned User ID (<assigned_user_id, text, optional)**: This field is not applicable for users; it's for internal reference.
* **Status (<status, select_one, optional)**: Please select the status of your inquiry to help us track progress.
