# On Call Consultant Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The On Call Consultant Request Form is designed to be a quick and efficient way to request consulting services from internal experts in response to critical issues within the organization. It's essential to accurately and thoroughly describe the issue, including any relevant background or context that may aid in the consultant's understanding and resolution. 

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have the authority to submit a request for the issue.
2.  On the first page of the form, enter the issue's brief description, focusing on the key details and any relevant background information.
3.  On the second page, provide the name and contact information of the internal consultant you're working with.
4.  In the third page, enter the consultant's phone number for easy contact.
5.  On the fourth page, select the issue's priority level and confirm whether it's related to an issue type.
6.  On the fifth page, select the schedule date for the consultation.
7.  On the sixth page, select the schedule time for the consultation.
8.  Finally, add any additional notes about the consultation or schedule.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Issue Description** (`issue_description`): Briefly describe the issue that the consultant needs to address.
*   **Consultant Name** (`consultant_name`): Enter the name of the internal consultant you're working with.
*   **Consultant Email** (`consultant_email`): Provide the email address of the internal consultant you're working with.
*   **Consultant Phone** (`consultant_phone`): Enter the phone number of the internal consultant for easy contact.
*   **Issue Priority** (`issue_priority`): Choose a priority level (e.g., high, low) to help allocate resources.
*   **Issue Type** (`issue_type`): Confirm whether the issue is related to a specific type or category.
*   **Schedule Date** (`schedule_date`): Select the date for the consultation or appointment.
*   **Schedule Time** (`schedule_time`): Choose the specific time for the consultation.
*   **Schedule Note** (`schedule_note`): Add any additional comments or notes about the consultation or schedule.

**Tips:**

*   Be as accurate and detailed as possible when describing the issue for the best possible outcome.
*   Ensure that you and the consultant are available and prepared on the chosen schedule date and time.
*   Review and verify the form contents before submitting it to avoid mistakes and ensure a smooth consultation process.
