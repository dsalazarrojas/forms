# Client Strategic Plan Upload Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to allow clients to upload their strategic plans for review and consideration. It captures essential information about the client, their strategic plan, and associated documents and timelines.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the client's name, company name, contact person, and email address.
2. Select the type of strategic plan being uploaded.
3. Indicate the primary focus area of the plan.
4. Upload the strategic plan document (as a PDF, Word, or Excel file).
5. Upload any supporting documents (if applicable).
6. Set the start and end dates of the plan.
7. Choose the current implementation status of the plan.
8. Fill in the total budget allocated and its currency (if applicable).
9. Add any additional notes or context about the plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client, which is a required field.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company, which is a required field.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the contact person's name, who will be handling the strategic plan, which is a required field.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the contact person, which is a required field to enable communication.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the contact person's phone number, which is a required field for easy communication.
* **Type of strategic plan**: Select the type of strategic plan being uploaded from the provided options (e.g., 3-Year Plan, 5-Year Plan, 10-Year Plan, etc.).
* **Primary focus area**: Select all applicable focus areas for the strategic plan (e.g., Growth and Expansion, Cost Reduction, Market Position, etc.).
* **Upload Strategic Plan Document**: Upload the strategic plan document as a PDF, Word, or Excel file.
* **Supporting documents (optional)**: Upload any supporting documents, such as budgets, timelines, or market research, that are relevant to the plan.
* **Plan start date**: Enter the start date of the plan.
* **Plan end date**: Enter the end date of the plan.
* **Current implementation status**: Choose the current implementation status of the plan from the provided options (e.g., Not started, In planning phase, Initial implementation, etc.).
* **Total budget allocated**: Enter the total budget allocated for the plan, which is an optional field.
* **Currency**: Enter the currency of the budget (if applicable).
* **Additional notes or context**: Enter any additional information about the plan or its context, which is an optional field.
