# IT Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IT Consultation Form is designed to collect information from clients for the purpose of facilitating consultations. This form should only be used for clients who require guidance on technical matters related to IT services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form with the required information. Fields marked as required must be filled out.
2.  Double-check your inputs for accuracy and completeness.
3.  Review the form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **main_page** (Page 1, text, optional): This is the main page of the form where you can enter your name and other details as required.
* **client_name** (Client Name, text, optional): Enter your name as the person requesting the consultation.
* **client_email** (Client Email, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **client_phone** (Client Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you directly.
* **client_question** (Client Question, text, optional): Describe the issue or matter you'd like to discuss.
* **client_description** (Description, text, optional): Provide more context or information about the issue.
* **consultant_name** (Consultant Name, text, optional): This field is meant for the name of the consultant who will be assisting you.
* **consultant_phone** (Consultant Phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the consultant if you'd like to be contacted directly.
* **consultant_email** (Consultant Email, email, optional): Enter the email address of the consultant if you'd like to be contacted via email.
* **consultation_details** (Consultation Details, text, optional): Add any relevant details about the consultation.
* **next_steps** (Next Steps, text, optional): Outline the next steps you'd like to take or the expected outcomes.
* **other_notes** (Other Notes, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the consultation.

Note: Fields with a red asterisk (\*) are required. Make sure to fill them out for a successful form submission.
