# Consultancy Employment Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential candidates for consultancy positions within our organization. Please fill out this form carefully and thoroughly, ensuring that you provide accurate and complete information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the position you are applying for from the "Position Applied For" field.
2. Choose your availability to start from the "Date Available to Start" field.
3. Enter your legal first name in the "First Name" field.
4. Enter your legal last name in the "Last Name" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
7. Enter your street address in the "Street Address" field.
8. Enter your city of residence in the "City" field.
9. Enter your state or province in the "State or Province" field.
10. Enter your zip or postal code in the "ZIP or Postal Code" field.
11. Select your highest education level from the "Highest Education Level" dropdown.
12. Enter the name of the school where you obtained your degree in the "University or Institution" field.
13. Enter your major or area of concentration in the "Field of Study" field.
14. Enter the year you completed your degree in the "Year of Graduation" field.
15. Enter the number of years you have spent in the consulting or related field in the "Years of Consulting Experience" field.
16. List your relevant skills and expertise in the "Relevant Skills and Expertise" field.
17. Select the industries you have worked in from the "Industry Experience" dropdown.
18. Enter the name of your most recent employer and dates of employment in the "Most Recent Employer" field.
19. Enter your most recent job title and key responsibilities in the "Most Recent Position" field.
20. If applicable, enter your reason for leaving in the "Reason for Leaving" field.
21. List any professional certifications you hold in the "Professional Certifications" field.
22. List languages you are proficient in and their proficiency level in the "Languages Spoken" field.
23. Select your willingness to travel from the "Willingness to Travel" dropdown.
24. Select whether you are willing to relocate from the "Willingness to Relocate" dropdown.
25. Enter your expected salary or hourly rate in the "Salary Expectations" field.
26. Select whether you have references available from the "Are References Available" dropdown.
27. Select the source of the job posting from the "How Did You Hear About This Position" dropdown.
28. If applicable, enter the name of the person who referred you to this position in the "Referral Name" field.
29. Attach a brief statement about why you are a good fit for the position in the "Cover Letter" field.
30. Attach any additional information you would like to share in the "Additional Information" field.
31. Attach your current resume or CV in the "Upload Resume" field.
32. Finally, click the submit button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Position Applied For**: Select the job title or role you are applying for.
* **Date Available to Start**: Choose when you can start the position.
* **First Name**: Enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your legal last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary contact number.
* **Street Address**: Enter your current residential address.
* **City**: Enter the city of your residence.
* **State or Province**: Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP or Postal Code**: Enter your zip or postal code.
* **LinkedIn Profile URL**: Enter the URL of your LinkedIn profile.
* **Portfolio or Website URL**: Enter the URL of your professional portfolio or website.
* **Highest Education Level**: Select your highest degree obtained.
* **University or Institution**: Enter the name of the school where you obtained your degree.
* **Field of Study**: Enter your major or area of concentration.
* **Year of Graduation**: Enter the year you completed your degree.
* **Years of Consulting Experience**: Enter the total years you have spent in the consulting or related field.
* **Relevant Skills and Expertise**: List your key consulting skills.
* **Industry Experience**: Select the industries you have worked in.
* **Most Recent Employer**: Enter the name of your most recent employer and dates of employment.
* **Most Recent Position**: Enter your most recent job title and key responsibilities.
* **Reason for Leaving**: If applicable, enter your reason for leaving your previous position.
* **Professional Certifications**: List any professional certifications you hold.
* **Languages Spoken**: List languages you are proficient in and their proficiency level.
* **Willingness to Travel**: Select your willingness to travel for work.
* **Willingness to Relocate**: Select whether you are willing to relocate for the position.
* **Salary Expectations**: Enter your expected salary or hourly rate.
* **Are References Available**: Select whether you have references available.
* **How Did You Hear About This Position**: Select the source of the job posting.
* **Referral Name**: If applicable, enter the name of the person who referred you to this position.
* **Cover Letter**: Attach a brief statement about why you are a good fit for the position.
* **Additional Information**: Attach any additional information you would like to share.
* **Resume Upload**: Attach your current resume or CV.
* **Application Date**: The date of submission.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* Attach a professional-looking resume or CV.
* If applicable, provide a brief statement about why you are a good fit for the position.
* If applicable, list any professional certifications you hold.
* If you have references available, select that option.
* If you are willing to travel or relocate, select that option.
* Enter your expected salary or hourly rate honestly.
* Attach any additional information you would like to share.
