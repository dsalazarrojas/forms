# hairdressing_consultation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for a hairdressing consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter your personal details in the Personal Info section.
2. Provide your appointment information in the Appointment section.
3. Select your preferred hair service from the Hair Service dropdown list.
4. Enter your preferences in the Preferences field.
5. Describe your hair condition in the Hair Condition field.
6. Share your hair history in the Hair History field.
7. Choose a date and time for your consultation in the Date and Time fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Personal Info** (`personal_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details for the consultation.
  - **Appointment** (`appointment`, `text`, required: false): Provide any necessary appointment information, such as date, time, and location.
  - **Hair Service** (`hair_service`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether you're interested in a hair service (Yes/No).
  - **Preferences** (`preferences`, `text`, required: false): Share any specific preferences you have for your hairdressing consultation.
  - **Hair Condition** (`hair_condition`, `text`, required: false): Describe your current hair condition, including any issues or concerns.
  - **Hair History** (`history`, `text`, required: false): Share a brief history of your hair, including any relevant information about your hair care routine.
  - **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Choose a date for your consultation.
  - **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Choose a time for your consultation.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when providing information in each field.
- Make sure to select the correct hair service you're interested in.
- If you're unsure about any field, don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.
