# Bra Fit and Comfort Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help consultants provide personalized recommendations to clients about their bra-fitting needs. It collects information about the client's preferences, medical history, and comfort priorities to ensure a comfortable and supportive fit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name.
2. Provide your last name.
3. Enter your email address for communication purposes.
4. Enter your primary contact number.
5. Select the date of your consultation.
6. Choose the type of consultation you are seeking.
7. If known, enter your current bra size.
8. Select any issues you are experiencing with your current bra fit.
9. Identify your top comfort priorities.
10. Select your typical activities and lifestyle.
11. Choose your preferred bra styles.
12. Select your preferred fabrics.
13. Select your preferred colors.
14. Set a budget range for the recommended bra.
15. Check if you have any sensitivity issues.
16. Disclose any medical considerations.
17. Indicate if you have had breast surgery.
18. Indicate if you are pregnant or nursing.
19. Confirm if you have experienced recent size changes.
20. Describe any shopping challenges you face.
21. (Optional) Enter your favorite or disliked brands.
22. Indicate your comfort level with fittings.
23. (Optional) Note any additional information for the consultant.
24. (Optional) Receive any recommendations or suggestions from the consultant.
25. Confirm if follow-up is needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: This will be the primary way the consultant contacts you for further discussions and follow-ups.
* **Phone Number**: This is your primary contact number for communication.
* **Consultation Date**: Enter the date you would like to schedule the consultation.
* **Consultation Type**: Select the type of consultation you are seeking. This will guide the consultant in providing tailored advice.
* **Current Bra Size (If Known)**: If you know your current bra size, please enter it.
* **Current Fit Issues**: Select any issues you are experiencing with your current bra fit.
* **Comfort Priorities**: Select up to three of your top comfort priorities. This will help the consultant understand your needs.
* **Lifestyle and Activities**: Select your typical activities and lifestyle to help the consultant understand your needs.
* **Bra Style Preference**: Select your preferred bra styles.
* **Fabric Preference**: Select your preferred fabrics. If you have no preference, select 'No preference'.
* **Color Preference**: Select your preferred colors.
* **Budget Range**: Set a budget range for the recommended bra.
* **Sensitivity Issues**: Check if you have any sensitivity issues.
* **Medical Considerations**: If you have any medical conditions that may affect your bra fit, please describe them here.
* **Post-Surgery Status**: Indicate if you have had breast surgery.
* **Pregnancy or Nursing**: Indicate if you are pregnant or nursing.
* **Recent Size Changes**: Confirm if you have experienced recent size changes.
* **Shopping Challenges**: Describe any challenges you face while shopping for bras.
* **Brand Preferences**: (Optional) Enter your favorite or disliked brands.
* **Fitting Room Comfort Level**: Indicate your comfort level with fittings.
* **Consultant Notes**: (Optional) Enter any additional information for the consultant.
* **Recommendations**: (Optional) Receive any recommendations or suggestions from the consultant.
* **Follow-up Needed**: Confirm if follow-up is recommended.

**Tips**

* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form to ensure the consultant provides the best possible recommendations.
* If you have any medical conditions that may affect your bra fit, please describe them in the Medical Considerations field.
* If you have had breast surgery, please indicate the type of surgery you had.
* If you are pregnant or nursing, please be aware that certain bras may not be suitable for your condition.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask the consultant for clarification.
* This form is designed to help you achieve the best possible bra fit, so please be patient and thorough when completing it.
