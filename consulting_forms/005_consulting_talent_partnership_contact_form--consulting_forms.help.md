# Consulting Talent Partnership Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects information from potential partners to better understand their needs and interests for consulting talent partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your full name.
2. Enter your organization's name.
3. Provide your email address for contact.
4. If you have a phone number, please enter it for scheduling calls.
5. Specify your role in the organization.
6. Select the primary area of interest for partnership.
7. Describe the approximate project timeline.
8. Provide a brief description of the partnership goals.
9. Choose how you would like to be followed up (via Email, Phone, or Video call).
10. Add any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name**: Enter your primary contact name.
- **Organization**: Enter your company or team name.
- **Email address**: Provide your best email for contact.
- **Phone number**: Enter your phone number for scheduling calls (optional).
- **Role of contact**: Describe your job title or function.
- **Area of partnership interest**: Select one option that best describes your partnership interest.
  -   Talent sourcing: We help find and connect top talents to your organization.
  -   Training and Development: We provide training and development solutions for your organization.
  -   Strategic advisory: We offer expert advice on business strategy and operations.
  -   Contract staffing: We assist with contract staffing needs.
  -   Other: Describe your specific interest area.
- **Desired project timeline**: Enter approximate start and end dates or duration.
- **Summary of need**: Briefly describe the goals of the partnership.
- **Follow-up preference**: Choose how you would like to be followed up (via Email, Phone, or Video call).
- **Additional notes**: Add any other relevant details.
