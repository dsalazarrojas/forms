# Organizational Learning Strategy Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your contact information and learning goals so we can better understand your inquiry and provide you with the most suitable learning resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email**. These are required fields.
2. Enter your **Job Title**, **Department**, and **Phone** numbers if available.
3. Describe your **Goals** and **Learning Objectives**. This will help us understand what you're looking for.
4. Select the type of **Inquiry Type** that best describes your question or concern.
5. If you have any additional resources or context that would be helpful, enter them in the **Additional Resources** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name. This field is required.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name. This field is required.
* **Email**: Enter your work email. This field is required.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or role within the organization. 
* **Department**: Enter the department or team you belong to.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number. This is an optional field.
* **Organization**: Enter your current organization name or affiliation.
* **Goals**: Describe your learning goals or what you hope to achieve.
* **Learning Objectives**: Describe the specific objectives you want to attain.
* **Inquiry Type**: Select the type of inquiry that best describes your question or concern.
* **Additional Resources**: Enter any additional resources or context that would be helpful for us to provide a better response.

## Tips
* Please ensure you enter all required fields (First Name, Last Name, Email, and Job Title).
* Be as specific as possible when describing your goals and learning objectives.
* Select the most accurate Inquiry Type to help us understand your concern.
* If you have any additional resources or context, please provide them for a more efficient response.
