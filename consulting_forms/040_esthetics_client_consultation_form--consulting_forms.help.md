# Esthetics Client Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from clients about their esthetic needs and preferences, enabling the esthetician to provide personalized recommendations and tailor their services accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all requested information.
2. Be honest about your medical history, allergies, and concerns.
3. Select the options that best describe your skin and hair concerns.
4. Provide information about your esthetic preferences and medical procedures.
5. Review and verify the accuracy of the information you provide.
