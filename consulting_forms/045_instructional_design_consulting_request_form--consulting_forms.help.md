# Instructional Design Consulting Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your project's requirements for the Instructional Design consulting service. This will help our team provide you with effective instructional design services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your organisation's name and email address in the Organisation field.
2. Enter your phone number in the Phone field (optional).
3. Choose whether your department is affected by the project by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the Department field.
4. Provide a name for your project in the Project Name field.
5. Enter a brief description of your project in the Project Description field.
6. Choose the start and end dates for your project in the Project Start Date and Project End Date fields.
7. Enter your project's budget in the Project Budget field.
8. Finally, click the Submit Button to submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organisation** (`organisation`, `text`, required): Enter your organisation's name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you would like to receive updates and information about your project.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether your department is affected by the project. Select "Yes" if it is, and "No" if it is not.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, `text`, required): Enter a name for your project.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of your project.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, `date`, required): Choose the start date for your project.
* **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`, `date`, optional): Choose the end date for your project.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, `number`, required): Enter your project's budget.
* **Submit Button** (`submit_button`, `text`, optional): Click this button to submit your request.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have reviewed and understood the terms and conditions of our services before submitting this form.
* If you have any questions or need help, please do not hesitate to contact us.
