# Financial Planning Consultation Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about a financial planning consultation. Please fill out this form to help us understand your financial needs and goals, so we can provide you with personalized recommendations and support.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and provide accurate information about yourself.
2. Fill out each section completely, as required.
3. If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data**: This is the main content section of the form, where you can provide any additional information or comments.
* **Client Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
* **Risk Score**: This is a subjective measure of your risk tolerance, with higher scores indicating greater risk tolerance.
* **Investment Goal**: Describe your short-term and long-term investment goals.
* **Financial Goal**: Describe your financial goals, such as saving for a down payment on a house or retirement.
* **Financial History**: Provide a brief summary of your financial history, including any significant events or transactions.
* **Income**: Enter your monthly income.
* **Assets**: List your assets, such as investments, properties, or other valuable items.
* **Liabilities**: List your liabilities, such as debts or outstanding loans.
* **Net Worth**: Enter your current net worth.
* **Investment Experience**: Rate your level of investment experience, with higher scores indicating more experience.
* **Education**: Select the level of education you have completed.
* **Marital Status**: Select your current marital status.
* **Dependents**: Enter the number of dependents you have.
* **Employment Status**: Select your current employment status.
* **Income Frequency**: Select how often you receive income.
* **Investment Frequency**: Select how often you invest.
* **Financial Planning Service**: Select the financial planning services you need or are interested in.
* **Financial Goal Frequency**: Select how often you review your financial goals.
* **Follow-up Frequency**: Select how often you would like to be contacted for follow-up.

Please note that some fields are optional, but providing accurate and complete information will help us better understand your needs and provide more effective support.
