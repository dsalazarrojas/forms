# Relocation Consultant Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Relocation Consultant Referral Form is used to collect information from relocation consultants who are being referred to new clients. This form helps to keep track of the consultants' contact information and the details of the referrals they receive.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information in the "Consultant Name", "Consultant Phone", and "Consultant Email" fields.
2. Select the source of the referral from the "Referral Source" field. You can choose from "Client", "Social Media", "Networking", "Word of Mouth", or "Other".
3. Enter the date of the referral in the "Referral Date" field.
4. Enter the client's name and contact information in the "Client Name", "Client Email", and "Client Phone" fields.
5. Select the reason for the referral from the "Referral Reason" field. You can choose from "New Business", "New Partnership", "New Location", or "Other".
6. Select the locations you are referring from the "Consultant Location" and "Client Location" fields. You can choose from "Location 1", "Location 2", and "Location 3".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Consultant Name**: Enter your name and title as a relocation consultant.
* **Consultant Phone**: Enter your phone number so the team can contact you regarding the referral.
* **Consultant Email**: Enter your email address so the team can contact you regarding the referral.
* **Referral Source**: Select the source of the referral (e.g., client, social media, networking, word of mouth, or other).
* **Referral Date**: Enter the date of the referral.
* **Client Name**: Enter the client's name.
* **Client Email**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Client Phone**: Enter the client's phone number.
* **Client Company**: Enter the client's company name.
* **Referral Reason**: Select the reason for the referral (e.g., new business, new partnership, new location, or other).
* **Consultant Location**: Select the locations you are referring from.
* **Client Location**: Select the locations the client is interested in.
* **Form Footer**: This is a note field for any additional information.

Note: All fields are optional except for the fields marked as "required".
