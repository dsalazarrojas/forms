# Eyelash Extension Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your desired eyelash extension services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **Customer Name** and **Email**.
2. Select your **Desired Look** from the options provided.
3. Choose the **Lash Materials** you are interested in.
4. Select the **Lash Techniques** you would like to receive.
5. Enter your **Desired Length** of lash extensions.
6. Choose how you would like to be **Contacted** (Phone, Email, or In-person).
7. Provide your **Available Date** and **Available Time** for a consultation.
8. Select your preferred **Available Time Slot** from the options provided.
9. Enter the name of the **Lash Technician** you would like to schedule with.
10. Fill in your **Contact Name** and **Contact Phone Number**.
11. Enter your **Lash Length** preference.
12. Select your preferred **Lash Price Range**.
13. Enter your **Contact Address**.
14. Enter the name of the **Lash Artist** you would like to schedule with.
15. Provide any additional **Comments** you would like to share.
16. For a second set of contact information, fill in **Contact Name 2**, **Contact Phone 2**, and choose your preferred **Contact Method 2**.
17. Finally, enter the name of the **Lash Technician 2** you would like to schedule with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Desired Look** (`desired_look`, text, optional): Describe the look you are interested in for your eyelash extensions.
* **Lash Materials** (`lash_materials`, select multiple, optional): Choose from the available lash materials you are interested in.
* **Lash Techniques** (`lash_techniques`, select one, optional): Select the lash technique you would like to receive (Fanning, Volume, or Lash Lift).
* **Desired Length** (`desired_length`, number, optional): Enter the length of lash extensions you are interested in.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select one, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or In-person).
* **Available Date** (`available_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are available for a consultation.
* **Available Time** (`available_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you are available for a consultation.
* **Available Time Slot** (`available_time_slot`, select one, optional): Select the time slot you are available during the day.
* **Available Time Slot 2** (`available_time_slot_2`, select one, optional): Select the time slot you are available during the day.
* **Lash Technician** (`lash_technician`, text, optional): Enter the name of the lash technician you would like to schedule with.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter your name for contact purposes.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Lash Length** (`lash_length`, number, optional): Enter your preferred lash length.
* **Lash Price Range** (`lash_price_range`, select one, optional): Select your preferred price range for lash extensions.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, optional): Enter your address for contact purposes.
* **Lash Artist** (`lash_artist`, text, optional): Enter the name of the lash artist you would like to schedule with.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about your preferences.
* **Contact Name 2** (`contact_name_2`, text, optional): Enter a second contact name for your records.
* **Contact Phone 2** (`contact_phone_2`, text, optional): Enter a second contact phone number for your records.
* **Contact Method 2** (`contact_method_2`, select one, optional): Choose the second contact method you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or In-person).
* **Lash Technician 2** (`lash_technician_2`, text, optional): Enter the name of the second lash technician you would like to schedule with.
