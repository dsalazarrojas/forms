# hair_consultation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hair_consultation_form is a tool for a professional stylist or barber to gather information about a client's hair preferences and characteristics. This information will help the stylist make informed decisions about styling the client's hair.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. The form is organized into 1 page with 10 fields. Please fill out each field as accurately as possible.
2. For each field, enter the information requested in the corresponding field.
3. The form is optional, so you can skip any field you do not feel comfortable providing information on.
