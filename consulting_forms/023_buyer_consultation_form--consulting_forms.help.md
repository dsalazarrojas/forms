# Buyer Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Buyer Consultation Form is a tool to help our consulting team understand your requirements, challenges, and goals, ensuring a tailored and effective consultation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please answer the questions according to your needs and preferences. Fill in all required fields (* indicates a required field). If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Client Name
* **Client Name**: Your full name.
### 2. Client Company
* **Company Name**: Your organization or business name (optional).
### 3. Email Address
* **Email Address**: Your work email address.
### 4. Phone Number
* **Phone Number**: Your contact number.
### 5. Consultation Type
* **Consultation Type**: Choose the area you need help with: Product Purchase Decision, Vendor Selection, Contract Review, Procurement Strategy, Supplier Evaluation, or Other.
### 6. Describe Your Current Situation
* **Describe Your Current Situation**: Briefly explain what you are trying to accomplish.
### 7. Specific Goals and Objectives
* **Specific Goals and Objectives**: Describe the outcomes you want to achieve.
### 8. Budget Range
* **Budget Range**: Choose an estimated spending range: Under 1000, 1000 - 5000, 5000 - 10000, 10000 - 50000, Over 50000, or Not yet determined.
### 9. Timeline for Decision
* **Timeline for Decision**: Select when you need to decide: Urgent - within 1 week, Soon - within 2-4 weeks, Medium - within 1-3 months, Flexible - over 3 months.
### 10. Key Requirements
* **Key Requirements**: List any must-have features or criteria for your project.
### 11. Potential Challenges or Concerns
* **Potential Challenges or Concerns**: List any obstacles or issues you anticipate (optional).
### 12. Number of Decision Makers
* **Number of Decision Makers**: Specify the number of people involved in the decision (optional).
### 13. Preferred Consultation Approach
* **Preferred Consultation Approach**: Choose how you would like to be advised: Phone consultation, Video meeting, In-person meeting, Email communication, Mixed approach.
### 14. Additional Information
* **Additional Information**: Provide any other relevant details we should know about your project.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when answering the questions to help us provide accurate and helpful advice.
* If you have any questions or need clarification about a field, please ask.
* This form is confidential, and we will use the information provided to tailor our consultation to your needs.
