# Transformation Strategy Implementation Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information from individuals interested in implementing a transformation strategy within their organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Provide your phone number, email address, and company name.
3. Select the relevant project type (e.g., IT, Digital, Data, or Operations).
4. Select the project stage (e.g., In Progress, On Hold, Cancelled, or Completed).
5. Indicate the lead status (e.g., Qualified, Unqualified, or No Lead).
6. Choose your company size (e.g., Small, Medium, Large, or Enterprise).
7. Indicate whether you have a contact request (True or False) and whether you would like follow-up (True or False).
8. Add any additional notes about your project.
9. If applicable, provide the name of the project lead and the lead owner.
10. Confirm whether you have sent an email or a phone number to the lead.
11. Confirm whether you have sent a follow-up and using which method (e.g., Email, Phone, Meeting, or None).
12. Provide a date for the follow-up and the follow-up time.
13. Add any follow-up notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company name.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, `text`, required: false): Describe your job function.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required: false): Describe your department.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your job title.
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more project types (e.g., IT, Digital, Data, Operations).
* **Project Stage** (`project_stage`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the project stage (e.g., In Progress, On Hold, Cancelled, Completed).
* **Lead Status** (`lead_status`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the lead status (e.g., Qualified, Unqualified, No Lead).
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select your company size (e.g., Small, Medium, Large, Enterprise).
* **Contact Request** (`contact_request`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate whether you have a contact request (True or False).
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate whether you would like follow-up (True or False).
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes about your project.
* **Project Lead** (`project_lead`, `text`, required: false): If applicable, enter the name of the project lead.
* **Lead Owner** (`lead_owner`, `text`, required: false): If applicable, enter the name of the lead owner.
* **Email Sent** (`email_sent`, `select_one`, required: false): Confirm whether you have sent an email to the lead.
* **Phone Sent** (`phone_sent`, `select_one`, required: false): Confirm whether you have sent a phone number to the lead.
* **Follow-up Sent** (`follow_up_sent`, `select_one`, required: false): Confirm whether you have sent a follow-up.
* **Follow-up Method** (`follow_up_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the follow-up method (e.g., Email, Phone, Meeting, None).
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date for the follow-up.
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the follow-up time.
* **Follow-up Notes** (`follow_up_notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any follow-up notes.
