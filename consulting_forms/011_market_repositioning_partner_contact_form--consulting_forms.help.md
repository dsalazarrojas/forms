# Market Repositioning Partner Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Market Repositioning Partner Contact Form is designed to help us assess your business needs and identify potential partners for market repositioning. This form is intended for businesses interested in partnering with us, not for general feedback or sales inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Strategic Fit" option that best describes your business.
2. Provide a detailed description of your business needs in the "Submission Details" field.
3. Enter your name as the contact person in the "Contact Name" field.
4. Enter your company's email address in the "Contact Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
6. Type your message to our team in the "Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Partnership Inquiry** (`participan_inquiry`, text, optional): A brief description of your interest in partnering with us.
* **Strategic Fit** (`strategic_fit`, select_one, optional): Select the option that best describes your business:
	+ Option 1: We are a new business looking to enter the market.
	+ Option 2: We are an established business looking to reposition our product/service in the market.
	+ Option 3: We are a business looking to exit the market.
* **Submission Details** (`submission_details`, text, optional): A detailed description of your business needs and goals.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person filling out this form.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your company's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your company's phone number.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): A message to our team, including any additional information about your business.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible in your inquiry and responses.
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and authority to submit this form on behalf of your organization.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, please reach out to your internal stakeholders.
