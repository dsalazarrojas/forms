# Consulting Technology Partnership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your organization's interests and goals for partnering with our company. By filling out this form, you'll be able to:
* Provide your organization's information and contact details
* Showcase your technology expertise and partnership goals
* Share examples of your recent projects and budget expectations
* Indicate your preferred timeline for partnership activities

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Applicant Organization Information section with your organization's name, website, and contact information.
2. Select your organization's technology expertise areas from the dropdown menu.
3. Briefly describe your partnership goals and what you hope to achieve from this partnership.
4. Enter the typical project size in months and select the approximate budget range for collaborative projects.
5. List up to three representative projects and provide links to learn more.
6. Provide your preferred start date for partnership activities.
7. Add any additional notes that you think would be helpful for our review team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization name**: Full legal name of the organization. (Required)
* **Organization website**: Main website or portfolio URL. (Optional)
* **Primary contact name**: Person responsible for partnership communications. (Required)
* **Primary contact email**: Business email for partnership correspondence. (Required)
* **Technology expertise areas**: Select the primary technology domains you work in. (Required)
	+ Example: "Cloud infrastructure, AI and machine learning, Data engineering"
* **Partnership goals**: Briefly describe what you hope to achieve from this partnership. (Required)
* **Typical project size**: Enter whole number of months. (Optional)
* **Budget range**: Select the approximate budget range for collaborative projects. (Required)
	+ Examples: "Under 25k", "25k to 100k", "100k to 500k", "Over 500k"
* **Sample projects**: List up to three representative projects and provide links to learn more. (Optional)
* **Timeline availability**: Preferred start date for partnership activities. (Optional)
* **Additional notes**: Any other information the review team should know. (Optional)

Please fill out this form carefully and thoroughly, as it will help us understand your organization's interests and goals better.
