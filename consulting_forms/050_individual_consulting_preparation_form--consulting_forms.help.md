# Individual Consulting Preparation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals before consulting with them. 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form thoroughly and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields (marked with an asterisk \* ) are filled in correctly.
3. Review and verify the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. <a href="https://example.com/links/field1">Client Information</a> (Client Information)
* <label>Client Information</label> (<name>client_info</name>, <type>text</type>, required/optional: false)
* This field is for entering any relevant details about the client, such as their name, contact information, or any specific requirements.

### 2. <a href="https://example.com/links/field2">Email</a> (<name>email</name>, <type>email</type>, required/optional: true)
* <label>Email</label>
* This field is used to collect the client's email address. Enter the client's email in the format of `example@example.com`.

### 3. <a href="https://example.com/links/field3">Phone</a> (<name>phone</name>, <type>text</type>, required/optional: false)
* <label>Phone</label>
* Enter the client's phone number in the format of `123-456-7890`.

### 4. <a href="https://example.com/links/field4">Address</a> (<name>address</name>, <type>text</type>, required/optional: false)
* <label>Address</label>
* Enter the client's address in the format of `123 Main St, Anytown, USA 12345`.

### 5. <a href="https://example.com/links/field5">Consulting Information</a> (<name>consulting_info</name>, <type>text</type>, required/optional: false)
* <label>Consulting Information</label>
* Enter any relevant details about the consulting session, such as the purpose of the meeting or any specific topics to be discussed.

### 6. <a href="https://example.com/links/field6">Service Type</a> (<name>type</name>, <type>select_one</type>, required/optional: true)
* <label>Service Type</label>
* Select the type of service the client is seeking (Option 1 or Option 2).

### 7. <a href="https://example.com/links/field7">Date</a> (<name>date</name>, <type>date</type>, required/optional: true)
* <label>Date</label>
* Enter the date for the consulting session in the format of `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### 8. <a href="https://example.com/links/field8">Time</a> (<name>time</name>, <type>time</type>, required/optional: true)
* <label>Time</label>
* Enter the time for the consulting session in the format of `HH:MM`.

### 9. <a href="https://example.com/links/field9">Duration</a> (<name>duration</name>, <type>text</type>, required/optional: true)
* <label>Duration</label>
* Enter the expected duration of the consulting session.

### 10. <a href="https://example.com/links/field10">Notes</a> (<name>notes</name>, <type>note</type>, required/optional: false)
* <label>Notes</label>
* Enter any additional notes or comments about the client.

### 11. <a href="https://example.com/links/field11">Client Comments</a> (<name>client_comments</name>, <type>text</type>, required/optional: false)
* <label>Client Comments</label>

### 12. <a href="https://example.com/links/field12">Consultant Comments</a> (<name>consultant_comments</name>, <type>text</type>, required/optional: false)
* <label>Consultant Comments</label>

### 13. <a href="https://example.com/links/field13">Follow-up</a> (<name>follow_up</name>, <type>select_multiple</type>, required/optional: true)
* <label>Follow-up</label>

### 14. <a href="https://example.com/links/field14">Attachments</a> (<name>attachments</name>, <type>text</type>, required/optional: false)
* <label>Attachments</label>
