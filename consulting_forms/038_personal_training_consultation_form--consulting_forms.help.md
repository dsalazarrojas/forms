# Personal Training Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients for personal training consultations. It gathers details such as client contact information, training preferences, and consultation session details. The form helps the assigned user understand the client's needs and goals, enabling them to provide effective personal training.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Select your preferred session time and date for the consultation.
3. Specify your training goals and provide a brief description of your goals.
4. Choose one or multiple training goals that apply to you.
5. Indicate your availability for the consultation.
6. If necessary, select additional training options or list multiple goals.
7. Provide any relevant notes about your goals and consultation.
8. Assign a user to handle your consultation.
9. Add any notes for the assigned user.
10. Add any comments or feedback for the consultation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (Client Information, text, required: false)
    * Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Session Time** (Session Time, date, required: false)
    * Select your preferred time for the consultation session.
* **Session Date** (Session Date, date, required: false)
    * Select the date for the consultation session.
* **Preferred Time** (Preferred Time, time, required: false)
    * Specify your preferred time for the consultation.
* **Training Goals** (Training Goals, text, required: false)
    * Enter your training goals and objectives.
* **Goals Description** (Goals Description, text, required: false)
    * Provide a brief description of your training goals.
* **Availability** (Availability, text, required: false)
    * Indicate your availability for the consultation.
* **Goal** (Goal, select_one, required: false)
    * Choose one training goal that applies to you.
* **Goal List** (Goal List, select_multiple, required: false)
    * Select multiple training goals that apply to you.
* **Select Multiple Goal List** (Select Multiple Goal List, select_multiple, required: false)
    * Select multiple training goals that apply to you.
* **Email** (Email, email, required: false)
    * Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required: false)
    * Enter your phone number.
* **Training Plan** (Training Plan, text, required: false)
    * Provide any additional information about your training plan.
* **Train Type** (Train Type, select_one, required: false)
    * Indicate your preference for personal training.
* **Train List** (Train List, select_multiple, required: false)
    * Select multiple training options.
* **Select Multiple Train List** (Select Multiple Train List, select_multiple, required: false)
    * Select multiple training options.
* **Notes** (Notes, text, required: false)
    * Enter any additional notes about your goals or consultation.
* **Assigned User** (Assigned User, text, required: false)
    * Assign a user to handle your consultation.
* **Assigned User Email** (Assigned User Email, email, required: false)
    * Enter the assigned user's email address.
* **Assigned User Phone** (Assigned User Phone, text, required: false)

* **Assigned User Notes** (Assigned User Notes, text, required: false)
    * Add any additional notes for the assigned user.
* **Comments** (Comments, text, required: false)
