# PPC Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PPC Agreement Form is a document used to collect information and data related to PPC campaigns. Its purpose is to ensure all relevant details are captured to facilitate efficient and accurate campaign management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the PPC Agreement Form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form fields below and fill them out according to the provided instructions for each field.
2.  Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Campaign Scope
* **Campaign Scope** (`campaign_scope`, text, optional): Describe the scope of the campaign, including the target audience, goals, and objectives.

### Page 1: Timelines
* **Timelines** (`timelines`, text, optional): Specify the start and end dates for the campaign, as well as any key milestones or deadlines.

### Page 1: Reporting Expectations
* **Reporting Expectations** (`reporting_expectations`, text, optional): Outline the expected reporting frequency and format, including any specific metrics or KPIs to be tracked.

### Page 1: Approval
* **Approval** (`approval`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the approval status for the campaign, allowing multiple selections (e.g., "Yes" or "No").

### Page 1: Approval List
* **Approval List** (`approval_list`, select_one, optional): Select a single approval option (e.g., "Yes" or "No").

### Page 1: Data Collection
* **Data Collection** (`data_collection`, text, optional): Describe the data collection process, including any specific requirements or constraints.

### Page 1: Data Frequency
* **Data Frequency** (`data_frequency`, text, optional): Specify the data collection frequency, including how often data will be collected or updated.

### Page 1: PPC Terms & Conditions
* **PPC Terms & Conditions** (`ppc_terms_conditions`, text, optional): Review and agree to the PPC terms and conditions, ensuring understanding of the campaign's rules and regulations.

### Page 1: Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes related to the campaign.

### Page 1: Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the contact email address for the campaign owner or manager.

### Page 1: Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number for the campaign owner or manager.

### Page 1: Agreement
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, text, optional): Confirm your agreement to the campaign terms and conditions.

### Page 1: Signature Date
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, optional): Record the date of your signature, indicating your agreement to the terms and conditions.

### Page 1: Signature Time
* **Signature Time** (`signature_time`, time, optional): Record the time of your signature, ensuring accurate timestamping.

### Page 1: Signature
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Provide your electronic or physical signature, confirming your agreement to the campaign terms and conditions.

### Page 1: Approval Date
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, optional): Record the date of approval, indicating when the campaign was approved.

### Page 1: Approved By
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, text, optional): Identify the person or entity granting approval for the campaign.

### Page 1: Approved At
* **Approved At** (`approved_at`, time, optional): Record the time of approval, ensuring accurate timestamping.

### Page 1: Created At
* **Created At** (`created_at`, time, optional): Displays the date and time the campaign was created.

### Page 1: Updated At
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, time, optional): Displays the date and time the campaign was last updated.

## Tips
- Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are completed before submitting.
- Use the provided instructions for each field to ensure accurate and correct completion.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, consult the campaign owner or manager.
