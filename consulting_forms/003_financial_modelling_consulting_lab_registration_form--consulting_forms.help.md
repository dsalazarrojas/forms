# Financial Modelling Consulting Lab Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering new users in the Financial Modelling Consulting Lab. It is essential to provide accurate and honest information to ensure a smooth and successful experience in the lab.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your details, interests, and role in the lab.
2. Make sure to review your information carefully before submission.
3. Submit the form to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Gender** (Select One, Optional): Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Role in Lab** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose one or more roles you would like to play in the lab from the options provided.
* **Workshop Interests** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the workshops you are interested in attending from the options provided.
* **Additional Comments** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional comments or information you would like to provide.
