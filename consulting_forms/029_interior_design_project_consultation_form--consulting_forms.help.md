# interior_design_project_consultation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect project details and contact information from clients and customers for interior design projects. The form helps to identify project requirements, contact details, and other relevant information that will be used to provide accurate and effective interior design services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Project Details" section, which includes project information such as location, scope, and description.
2. Provide contact information in the "Contact Info" section, including name, email, and phone number.
3. Choose a suitable project location from the drop-down menu in the "Project Location" section.
4. In the "Project Description" section, enter a detailed description of your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Project Details

* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, required: false): Enter a brief overview of your project, including any relevant details such as location, scope, and objectives.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, select_one, required: false): Select the location of your project. Options include "Home", "Office", or "Other".
* **Project Scope** (`project_scope`, text, required: false): Briefly describe your project scope.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required: false): Describe your project in detail, including any specific requirements or objectives.

### Contact Info

* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information, including any relevant details.
* **Contact Info 2** (`contact_info_2`, text, required: false): Enter any additional contact information.
* **First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to help us better understand your project needs.
* If you have any additional information or attachments, please attach them to your form submission.
* Our team will review your submission and contact you with any questions or concerns.
* We appreciate your attention to detail and prompt response.
