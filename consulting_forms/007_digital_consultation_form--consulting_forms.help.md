# digital_consultation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients or customers for a specific service or consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" field (Page 1).
2. Select one or multiple services you are interested in from the "Service Prophets" dropdown list (Page 1).
3. Choose the type of service you are interested in from the "What is your desired service?" dropdown list (Page 1).
4. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "notes" field(s) (Pages 2-3).
5. Select a date and time for your appointment from the "Date" and "Time" fields (Page 2).
6. Choose the duration of your appointment from the "Appointment" dropdown list (Page 2).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info** (Page 1):
	+ A text field to enter your contact information, such as name and email address.
* **Service Prophets** (Page 1):
	+ A dropdown list to select one or multiple services you are interested in (e.g., Hair, Barber).
* **What is your desired service?** (Page 1):
	+ A dropdown list to choose the type of service you are interested in (e.g., Haircut, Shaving, Trim).
* **notes** (Pages 2-3):
	+ A free-text field for entering additional notes or comments.
* **Date** (Page 2):
	+ A date field to select the date of your appointment.
* **Time** (Page 2):
	+ A time field to select the time of your appointment.
* **Appointment** (Page 2):
	+ A dropdown list to choose the duration of your appointment (e.g., 15, 30, 60 minutes).
* **notes2-19** (Pages 2-3):
