# Client Value Measurement Network Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your interests, goals, and expectations for joining the Client Value Measurement Network. By providing your personal and professional information, you will help us tailor the network's activities and content to meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Introduction**: Start by reading the introduction and understanding the purpose of the form.
2. Fill out the form in sections, working from top to bottom.
3. Make sure to answer all questions that are marked as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your work or personal phone number.
* **Company or Organization Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Job Title or Position** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title or position.
* **Industry** (`industry`, `text`, required): Select the industry you work in.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, `select_one`, required): Select the number of employees in your company.
* **Years of Experience in Industry** (`years_in_industry`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years you have worked in your industry.
* **Years in Current Role** (`years_in_role`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years you have been in your current role.
* **Areas of Expertise** (`expertise_areas`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the areas of expertise that apply to you.
* **Participation Goals** (`participation_goals`, `text`, required): Enter your goals for joining the network.
* **Value Focus** (`value_focus`, `select_multiple`, required): Select up to 3 aspects of value measurement that interest you most.
* **Desired Level of Participation** (`participation_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your desired level of participation.
* **Available Time per Month for Network Activities** (`time_commitment`, `select_one`, required): Select the amount of time you can dedicate to the network each month.
* **How Can You Contribute to the Network?** (`expertise_contribution`, `text`, optional): Enter how you can contribute to the network (e.g., speaking, mentoring, sharing case studies).
* **What Do You Expect to Gain from Network Participation?** (`expected_benefits`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the benefits and expectations you have for joining the network.
* **Current Business Challenges** (`current_challenges`, `text`, optional): Enter any current business challenges you face.
* **Topics or Skills You Want to Develop** (`learning_interests`, `text`, optional): Enter topics or skills you want to develop.
* **Relevant Certifications or Credentials** (`certifications`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant certifications or credentials you have.
* **Published Articles or Thought Leadership** (`publications`, `text`, optional): Enter any published articles or thought leadership work you have done.
* **Professional Networks or Associations** (`professional_network`, `text`, optional): Enter any professional networks or associations you belong to.
* **How Did You Hear About This Network?** (`referral`, `select_one`, optional): Select how you heard about the network.
* **Additional Information or Comments** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information or comments.
* **I Agree to Abide by the Network Code of Conduct** (`code_of_conduct`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to agree to abide by the network code of conduct.
* **I Consent to My Profile Being Shared with Network Members** (`data_sharing`, `select_one`, optional): Select "Yes" to agree to share your profile with network members.

## Tips

* Make sure to be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Answer all required questions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [contact information].
