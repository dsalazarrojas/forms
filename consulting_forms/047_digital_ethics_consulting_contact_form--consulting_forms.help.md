# digital-ethics-consulting-contact-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who are interested in consulting on digital ethics issues. The form is intended to help the consulting team better understand the user's inquiry and provide relevant guidance and support. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of digital ethics inquiry you are interested in (Artificial Intelligence, Data and Privacy, Digital Identity, or Other).
2. Provide a brief description of your inquiry, including any relevant details or questions you may have.
3. Choose your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, or Other).
4. Enter your contact information (Name, Phone Number, Email Address, and Address).
5. Specify your availability (Date and Time) if applicable.
6. Provide any additional comments or questions you may have.
7. If applicable, upload a file related to your inquiry.
8. Specify if you would like a review of your submission.
9. Choose who would like to review your submission (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inquiry Type** (`inquiry_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of digital ethics inquiry you are interested in. This will help the consulting team provide specific guidance and support.
* **Description** (`description`, `note`, required: false): A brief description of your inquiry, including any relevant details or questions you may have. This will help the consulting team understand your needs better.
* **Contact Preference** (`contact_preference`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your preferred contact method. This could be Email, Phone, or Other.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address if you have not chosen Email as your contact preference.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name if you are contacting us through this form.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number if you have not chosen Phone as your contact preference.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address if you are contacting us through this form.
* **Address Line 1** (`contact_addr_1`, `text`, required: false): Enter the first line of your address if you are contacting us through this form.
* **Address Line 2** (`contact_addr_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the second line of your address if you are contacting us through this form.
* **Contact City** (`contact_city`, `text`, required: false): Enter the city of your address if you are contacting us through this form.
* **Contact State** (`contact_state`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the state of your address from the options (California, New York, Other).
* **Zip** (`contact_zip`, `number`, required: false): Enter the zip code of your address if you are contacting us through this form.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you are available to meet if applicable.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you are available to meet if applicable.
* **Inquiry Questions** (`inquiry_questions`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional questions you may have related to your inquiry.
* **Inquiry Comments** (`inquiry_comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or context related to your inquiry.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or information you may have.
* **Inquiry File Upload** (`inquiry_file_upload`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Specify if you would like to upload a file related to your inquiry.
* **Inquiry File URL** (`inquiry_file_url`, `text`, required: false): If you choose to upload a file, enter the URL where it is located.
* **Inquiry File Name** (`inquiry_file_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter a name for the file you are uploading if applicable.
* **Inquiry File Size** (`inquiry_file_size`, `number`, required: false): Enter the size of the file you are uploading if applicable.
* **Inquiry File Extension** (`inquiry_file_extension`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the file extension of the file you are uploading (doc, xls, pdf, jpg).
* **Submission Review Status** (`submission_review_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Specify if you would like a review of your submission (In Progress, Completed, Held).
* **Submission Review By** (`submission_review_by`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose who you would like to review your submission (Yes, No).

## Tips
Please ensure that your submission is accurate and complete, as this will help the consulting team better assist you with your inquiry. If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us through this form or through other means.
