# Government Contract Consulting Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for government agencies to contact us about consulting services. It allows us to gather necessary information to provide personalized support and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out all required fields (marked as "required").
2. Provide clear and concise information in each field.
3. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Details (Required)
* **User Details** (`user_details`, `text`, required): This field is for providing your name and any other relevant contact information. Please ensure that your name and contact details are accurate and complete.

### Message (Required)
* **Message** (`message`, `note`, required): In this field, please provide a clear and concise description of your needs and requirements. This will help us to better understand your needs and provide the best possible support.

### Contract Type
* **Contract Type** (`contract_type`, `select_one`, optional): If you have a contract with us, please select "Yes". If not, select "No". This field will help us understand your current contract status.

### Government Agency (Required)
* **Government Agency** (`government_agency`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select all the government agencies that you represent. This will help us to tailor our services to your specific needs.

### Project Start Date (Required)
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, `date`, required): Please enter the start date of your project. This will help us to better understand your project timeline.

Note: The fields marked with `**Required** indicate fields that must be completed for the form to be submitted successfully.
