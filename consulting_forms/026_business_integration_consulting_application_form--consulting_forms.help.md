# Business Integration Consulting Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a application for our consulting services to help your organization integrate different systems, services, or processes. We will use the information you provide to evaluate your project and potentially offer our consulting solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields (marked as "true" in the form) with accurate and up-to-date information about your organization and project.
* Use the "Company name" field to provide your organization's name.
* Enter the name of the primary contact person for the project in the "Contact name" field.
* Enter the contact email address of the primary contact person in the "Contact email" field.
* In the "Project scope" field, describe the integration needs and objectives of your project.
* If applicable, provide the expected start and end dates or duration for the project in the "Timeline expectation" field.
* If you have an estimated budget for the consulting engagement, enter it in the "Estimated budget" field.
* List the key stakeholders to be involved in the project in the "Key stakeholders" field.
* If applicable, describe the main systems to be integrated in the "Current systems" field.
* Finally, confirm your agreement to our consulting terms by selecting "True" in the "Agree to terms" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Consulting Application --**: A header field indicating the start of the application form.
* **Company name** (`company_name`, **text**, required): Enter your organization's name accurately.
* **Contact name** (`contact_name`, **text**, required): Enter the primary contact person's name for the project.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, **email**, required): Enter the contact email address of the primary contact person.
* **Project scope** (`project_scope`, **text**, required): Describe the integration needs and objectives of your project.
* **Timeline expectation** (`timeline_expectation`, **text**, optional): Provide the expected start and end dates or duration for the project, if applicable.
* **Estimated budget** (`estimated_budget`, **number**, optional): Enter the budget range for the consulting engagement, if applicable.
* **Key stakeholders** (`key_stakeholders`, **text**, optional): List the key stakeholders to be involved in the project.
* **Current systems** (`current_systems`, **text**, optional): Describe the main systems to be integrated in the project, if applicable.
* **Agree to terms** (`agree_to_terms`, **select_one**, required): Confirm your agreement to our consulting terms by selecting "True".

Note: All required fields are marked as "true" in the YAML schema.
