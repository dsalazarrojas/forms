# Consulting Innovation Roundtable Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registration for the Consulting Innovation Roundtable, a platform for senior professionals to share experiences, ideas, and best practices on innovation topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal details in the required fields (First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Job Title, Company Name, Company Industry, Seniority Level, Event Date, Attendance Format, Innovation Topics of Interest, What Do You Hope to Gain, Photo and Recording Consent, Terms and Conditions).
2. Fill out any optional fields that you want to provide additional information about yourself and your organization.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **First Name** (`attendee_first_name`, text, required)
Your first name.

### 2. **Last Name** (`attendee_last_name`, text, required)
Your last name.

### 3. **Email Address** (`attendee_email`, email, required)
Email address for event communications.

### 4. **Phone Number** (`attendee_phone`, text, required)
Contact phone number.

### 5. **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required)
Your current position in the organization.

### 6. **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required)
Name of your organization.

### 7. **Company Industry** (`company_industry`, select_one, required)
Choose the industry sector of your organization:

* Technology
* Finance
* Healthcare
* Manufacturing
* Retail
* Consulting
* Energy
* Government
* Education
* Other

### 8. **Seniority Level** (`seniority_level`, select_one, required)
Choose your level:

* C-Level Executive
* VP / SVP
* Director
* Senior Manager
* Manager
* Other

### 9. **Event Date** (`roundtable_date`, date, required)
Select the date you will attend.

### 10. **Attendance Format** (`attendance_format`, select_one, required)
Choose how you will attend:

* In Person
* Virtual

### 11. **Innovation Topics of Interest** (`innovation_topics`, select_multiple, required)
Select topics you want to discuss:

* Digital Transformation
* AI and Machine Learning
* Business Model Innovation
* Product Innovation
* Process Innovation
* Customer Experience
* Sustainability
* Disruptive Technologies
* Change Management
* Innovation Culture

### 12. **What Do You Hope to Gain** (`discussion_goals`, text, required)
State your goals for attending.

### 13. **Current Innovation Challenges** (`innovation_challenges`, text, optional)
Describe any innovation challenges your organization is facing.

### 14. **Will You Share Experiences?** (`willing_to_share`, select_one, required)
Do you agree to share your experiences?

* Yes, Gladly
* Yes, If Asked
* Prefer to Listen Only

### 15. **Networking Interest** (`networking_interest`, select_one, optional)
How interested are you in networking:

* Very Interested
* Somewhat Interested
* Not a Priority

### 16. **Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, text, optional)
Do you have any dietary restrictions?

### 17. **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, text, optional)
Do you have any accessibility needs?

### 18. **How Did You Hear About This Event?** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, optional)
Choose the source of information:

* Email Invitation
* Website
* Social Media
* Colleague Referral
* Previous Event
* Other

### 19. **Referred By** (`referral_name`, text, optional)
Provide the name of the person who referred you.

### 20. **Marketing Communications** (`marketing_consent`, select_one, optional)
Do you want to receive updates about future events?

* Yes, Please
* No, Thank You

### 21. **Photo and Recording Consent** (`photo_consent`, select_one, required)
Do you agree to be photographed or recorded?

* I Consent
* I Do Not Consent

### 22. **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_acceptance`, select_one, required)
Do you agree to the event terms?

* I Agree
* Other

### 23. **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required)
The date and time of submission.
