# Virtual Financial Consultation Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Financial Consultation Booking Form is designed to collect information from clients to schedule a virtual financial consultation. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your details.
2. Select a preferred date and time for your consultation.
3. Choose a consultation type (Meeting, Phone Call, or Video Call).
4. Enter any additional message or comments.
5. Submit the form to schedule your consultation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name for identification purposes.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### Preferred Date
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, optional): Select your preferred date for the consultation.

### Selected Date
* **Selected Date** (`selected_date`, date, optional): If available, select a date for the consultation. This field will be populated automatically if the preferred date is not available.

### Available Time
* **Available Time** (`available_time`, time, optional): Select a time for the consultation.

### Consultation Type
* **Consultation Type** (`consultation_type`, select_one, required): Choose a consultation type (Meeting, Phone Call, or Video Call).

### Consultation Topic
* **Consultation Topic** (`consultation_topic`, text, optional): Enter any specific topic for discussion during the consultation.

### Message
* **Message** (`message`, note, optional): Enter any additional message or comments.

### Selected Time
* **Selected Time** (`selected_time`, time, optional): This field is auto-populated if you select a date and available time.
