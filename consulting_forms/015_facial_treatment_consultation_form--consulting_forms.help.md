# Facial Treatment Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from clients about their facial treatment preferences and schedule. This form will help you understand their concerns, needs, and expectations from your consulting services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your client's details in the "Client Details" field.
2. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
3. Describe the facial treatment you are interested in, including the details of the treatment in the "Facial Treatment Details" field.
4. Specify the schedule you would like to take the treatment on in the "Treatment Schedule" field.
5. Provide any payment details, if applicable, in the "Payment Details" field.
6. If you have any special instructions or requests, please mention them in the "Special Instructions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details**: Enter your name, address, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Contact Information**: Provide your phone number and email address so we can contact you to confirm your treatment schedule and any further instructions.
* **Facial Treatment Details**: Describe the type of facial treatment you are interested in, including any specific concerns or issues you would like to address (e.g. acne, aging skin, etc.).
* **Treatment Schedule**: Choose a date and time for your treatment appointment.
* **Payment Details**: If you have already made an appointment, please let us know how you intend to pay for the treatment (e.g. credit card, cash, etc.).
* **Special Instructions**: If you have any specific requests or requirements, such as language assistance or accessibility needs, please mention them here.

## Tips
* Please be as clear and specific as possible when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask us and we will be happy to help.
* We will review your form submission and get back to you as soon as possible to confirm your appointment and payment details.
