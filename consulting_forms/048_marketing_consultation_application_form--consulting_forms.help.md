# Marketing Consultation Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential clients or companies seeking marketing consultation services. It is intended for internal use by marketing and business development staff to determine the feasibility of a potential collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Company information accurately and completely.
2. Select the Marketing Channel(s) through which you wish to conduct your marketing efforts.
3. Choose your preferred Marketing Budget and any additional channels you would like to target.
4. Select the type of Consultation you require (One-on-one or Group consultation).
5. Indicate whether you would like for the consultation to be followed up on after the consultation.
6. Enter any additional comments or goals you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name as it appears on official documents. 
* **Last Name**: Your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Company**: The name of the company you represent or the company for which you are applying.
* **Position**: Your title or position within the company.
* **Email**: A valid email address associated with your company or personal email.
* **Phone**: A valid phone number associated with your company or personal phone.
* **Goals**: A brief description of your marketing goals.
* **Budget**: The amount you are willing or able to invest in marketing efforts. Please select a number that best reflects your budget.
* **Marketing Channel**: Select the channel(s) through which you would like to conduct your marketing efforts. (Digital, Social Media, Print, Email, Online)
* **Marketing Budget**: Select one or more of the provided budget options that best reflect your available marketing budget. 
* **Consultation Type**: Select the type of consultation you require (One-on-one or Group consultation).
* **Follow-up**: Indicate whether you would like for the consultation to be followed up on after the consultation. 
* **Additional Comments**: Any additional information or comments you would like to share with our team.

Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, and we will review your application and respond to you shortly. 

Tips:

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out completely before submitting the form. Incomplete forms may lead to delays in processing your application. 
* If you have any issues or need further clarification, please contact us at [your contact email/phone number].
