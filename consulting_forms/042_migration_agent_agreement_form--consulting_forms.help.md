# Migration Agent Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Migration Agent Agreement Form is designed to collect information from clients for the service migration process. This form helps us gather essential details to ensure a smooth and successful migration experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and understand the fields required.
2. Enter the client's details in the corresponding fields (Client Details, Client Name, Email, Phone, etc.).
3. Confirm that you have read and understand the service terms.
4. Provide onboarding details (Onboarding Details, Onboarding Details 2, etc.).
5. Enter any additional information required (Client Info Form, Service Agency Info, etc.).
6. Sign and date the form (Agreement Form, Date, Time).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details** (`client_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter the client's name, email, and phone number in this field.
* **Service Terms** (`service_terms`, `note`, required: false): Review and confirm that you have read and understand the service terms.
* **Client Consent** (`client_consent`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select your level of understanding of the service terms (I have read and understand, I have not read the terms, Not sure).
* **Onboarding Details** (`onboarding_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional onboarding information.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the client's name (this field is duplicated, please use the first field for the main client name).
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the client's email address (this field is duplicated, please use the first field for the main email).
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the client's phone number (this field is duplicated, please use the first field for the main phone).
* **Service Agency** (`service_agency`, `text`, required: false): Enter the service agency details (this field is duplicated, please use the first field for the main agency).
* **Agency Address** (`agency_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the agency address (this field is not duplicated, use this for the agency address).
* **Client Consent 2** (`client_consent_1`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select your level of understanding of the service terms (this field is duplicated, please use the first field for the main consent).
