# process_automation_consulting_partner_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather contact information and inquiry details from potential consulting partners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the `First Name` field.
2. Enter your last name in the `Last Name` field.
3. Enter your email address in the `Email` field. Please make sure to use a valid email address.
4. Enter your company name in the `Company` field.
5. Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field (if you wish to be contacted).
6. Select your company type from the list of options in the `Company Type` field.
7. Select all the types of inquiries you have regarding our services in the `Inquiry Type` field (you can select multiple options).
8. Enter your message in the `Message` field, explaining the details of your inquiry.
9. If you have a preferred method of communication, select it in the `Assigned Tool` field.
10. If you have a company website, enter the URL in the `Company Website` field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter a valid email address in this field.
* **Company** (Company, text, required): Enter your company name in this field.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number in this field.
* **Company Type** (Company Type, select_one, required): Select the type of company you represent from the list of options.
* **Inquiry Type** (Inquiry Type, select_multiple, optional): Select all the types of inquiries you have regarding our services.
* **Message** (Message, note, required): Enter a detailed message about your inquiry.
* **Assigned Tool** (Assigned Tool, select_one, optional): Select how you would like to be contacted.
* **Company Website** (Company Website, text, optional): Enter your company website URL (if applicable).
