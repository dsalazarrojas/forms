# Educational Growth Plan Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather relevant information for creating an educational growth plan that caters to students' and educators' needs. By filling out this form, users will help us better understand their goals, challenges, and insights, which will aid in designing a more effective growth plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category that best fits your consultation type.
2. Provide a brief description of your educational growth plan consultation.
3. Specify your academic goals as a student.
4. Describe your current academic challenges.
5. Share your insights as an educator or expert.
6. Suggest potential strategies for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category (Select One)** (`category`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Please choose the category that best describes your educational growth plan consultation (e.g., Consulting Forms, Other Forms).
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter a brief description of your educational growth plan consultation.
* **Student Academic Goals** (`student_academic_goals`, `text`, optional):
	+ Share your academic goals as a student, such as improving grades, increasing motivation, or better understanding a subject.
* **Student Challenges** (`student_challenges`, `text`, optional):
	+ Describe the challenges you're currently facing in your academic journey, like time management, learning difficulties, or motivation issues.
* **Educator Insights** (`educator_insights`, `text`, optional):
	+ If you're an educator, share your professional insights on how to improve educational growth plans.
* **Strategies for Improvement** (`strategies_for_improvement`, `text`, optional):
	+ Propose potential strategies that could aid in creating a more effective educational growth plan.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing more information will help us tailor the growth plan to your specific needs.
