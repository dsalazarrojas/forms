# Sustainability Consultant Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to refer a sustainability consultant to a potential client. It provides the necessary information about the consultant and their expertise to help facilitate a potential collaboration or partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling in the company and consultant information sections.
- Ensure that the consultant's name, email, and phone number are accurate.
- Select a suitable referral date for the potential collaboration or partnership.
- Add any additional notes that you think would be helpful for the potential client to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name**: The name of the company being referred.
* **First Name**: The first name of the consultant being referred.
* **Last Name**: The last name of the consultant being referred.
* **Email**: The email address of the consultant. This should be the email address they can be contacted at.
* **Company Address**: The address of the company being referred.
* **Company Phone**: The phone number of the company being referred.
* **Company Website**: The website of the company being referred (optional).
* **Company Industry**: The industry of the company being referred (optional).
* **Company Sustainability Focus**: A brief description of the company's sustainability focus (optional).
* **Consultant Name**: The name of the consultant being referred (optional).
* **Consultant Email**: The email address of the consultant (optional).
* **Consultant Phone**: The phone number of the consultant (optional).
* **Consultant Website**: The website of the consultant (optional).
* **Consultant Industry**: The industry of the consultant (optional).
* **Consultant Sustainability Focus**: A brief description of the consultant's sustainability focus (optional).
* **Referral Date**: The date of the referral. This is a required field and should be a specific date.
* **Note**: Any additional notes that would be helpful for the potential client to know.

Note: Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly to ensure a successful referral.
