# secure_your_strategy_consultation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a lead's details and preferences for a secure your strategy consultation. The form helps to gather essential data to provide a personalized experience for the lead.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
* Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
* Enter your name and email address to ensure that we can contact you effectively.
* Provide your phone number to allow us to communicate with you.
* Enter the details of your appointment, including the date and time.
* Add any additional notes or comments you'd like to share with us.
* Select your options for our consultation services.
* Enter your lead details, including address, company, position, job title, department, job role, and job level.
