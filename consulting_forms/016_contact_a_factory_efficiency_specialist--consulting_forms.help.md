# Contact A Factory Efficiency Specialist - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from manufacturing facilities about their efficiency challenges and goals. By filling out this form, you will be providing our factory efficiency specialists with the necessary information to assess your facility's efficiency needs and provide guidance on improving your operations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the Company Information section with your facility's name and contact information.
2. In the Efficiency Challenges section, select the primary efficiency bottleneck you are facing and provide more detail about the challenges you are experiencing.
3. In the Efficiency Goals section, select the primary efficiency objective you hope to achieve and the target improvement percentage.
4. Select a project timeline for when you would like to begin the assessment.
5. Add any additional comments or information that our specialist should know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your manufacturing facility.
* **Contact Person Full Name** (`contact_person_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as the contact person for this facility.
* **Job Title** (`contact_person_job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title or position in the facility.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your primary business email address.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the best number to reach you.
* **Facility Location** (`facility_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the city and state or province where your facility is located.
* **Primary Efficiency Bottleneck** (`primary_efficiency_bottlenecks`, `select_one`, required): Select the single biggest issue you are facing, from the options: High Labor Costs, Machine Downtime, Low Throughput, Quality Control Issues, Supply Chain Inefficiency, Energy/Utility Costs, or Outdated Technology/Software.
* **Description of Current Challenges** (`description_of_current_challenges`, `text`, required): Provide more detail about the challenges you are experiencing.
* **Estimated Monthly Loss** (`estimated_monthly_loss`, `select_one`, optional): Select a range for the estimated monthly loss due to inefficiency, from the options: Under $10,000, $10,000-$50,000, $50,001-$100,000, or $100,001+.
* **Primary Efficiency Objective** (`primary_efficiency_objective`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the primary efficiency objective you hope to achieve, from the options: Increase Throughput/Capacity, Reduce Labor Costs, Minimize Machine Downtime, Improve Quality/Reduce Waste, Lower Energy Consumption, or Automate Manual Processes.
* **Target Improvement Percentage** (`target_improvement_percentage`, `number`, optional): Enter the target improvement percentage, e.g., 20% increase in throughput.
* **Project Timeline** (`project_timeline`, `select_one`, required): Select the project timeline for when you would like to begin the assessment, from the options: ASAP, Within 1 Month, 1-3 Months, or 3-6 Months.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information that our specialist should know.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the current date.
