# Wedding Cake Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from couples who are interested in booking a wedding cake consultation. The consultant will use this information to understand their needs and preferences for their wedding cake.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, fill out the required information, which includes:
	* Couple Name
	* Wedding Date
	* Contact Email
2. Provide additional details about your event:
	* Event Location
	* Event Type (e.g., Wedding, Birthday, Graduation)
	* Number of Guests
3. Optionally, you can provide more specific information about your event and preferences:
	* Time of Event
	* Message (any other information you'd like to share with the consultant)
4. Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Couple Name** (`couple_name`, text): Enter the names of the couple interested in booking the wedding cake consultation.
* **Wedding Date** (`wedding_date`, date): Enter the date of the wedding or special event.
* **Time of Event** (`time_of_event`, time): If applicable, enter the time of the event. (This field is optional.)
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one): Select the type of event (e.g., Wedding, Birthday, Graduation, or Other).
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number): Enter the estimated number of guests attending the event.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email): Enter the email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text): Enter your contact phone number for additional communication.
* **Message** (`message`, note): Share any additional information about your event or preferences with the wedding cake consultant.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your contact email and phone number for accuracy.
* Be as specific as possible about your event details to help the consultant better understand your needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
