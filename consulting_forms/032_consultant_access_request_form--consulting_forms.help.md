# Consultant Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Consultant Access Request Form is a tool used to request access for consultants to access specific areas of the system. This form allows consultants to provide their information, specify the organization they represent, the purpose of access, and the duration of access.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked as "required" (indicated by `required: true` in the schema).
2. Provide your full name as the consultant needing access.
3. Specify the organization you represent (if applicable).
4. Describe the reason for access or project reference.
5. Choose the access level required (select from "Site access only," "Document access," or "System access").
6. Identify the project or security approver for your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Consultant name
• **Consultant name**: Enter your full name as the consultant needing access.
### Organization
• **Organization**: This is your consultant firm or independent organization.
### Reason for Access
• **Access required for**: Provide the reason for access or project reference. This will help the approver understand the purpose of your request.
### Access start date
• **Access start date**: Enter the date when you would like access to begin.
### Access end date
• **Access end date**: Enter the date when access should end.
### Access level needed
• **Access level needed**: Select the level of access required:
	+ **Site access only**: Access to the site only, restricted to viewing information.
	+ **Document access**: Access to documents and related materials.
	+ **System access**: Full access to the system, including all its features and functions.
### Approver name
• **Approver name**: Identify the project or security approver who will review your request.

## Tips
* Double-check that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Ensure you have obtained the approver's approval before accessing any areas of the system.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for assistance.
