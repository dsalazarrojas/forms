# Sales Consultation Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule sales consultations with clients. It helps to collect necessary information about the client and the consultation details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the general information about the client, including their name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the desired location for the consultation.
3. Choose a date and time for the consultation from the available options.
4. Select the sales representatives assigned to the consultation.
5. Briefly describe the topic of discussion for the consultation.
6. Add any additional notes about the consultation.
7. Finally, review and submit the form to schedule the consultation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_info`, `text`, optional): This field is a text field where you can fill in any general information about the client that is relevant to the consultation.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, optional): This field is for the client's name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Please enter the client's email address here. This will help the sales representatives contact the client.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the client's phone number here.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, optional): Fill in the name of the client's company.
* **Location** (`location`, `select_one`, optional): Select the location for the consultation from the available options.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Choose the date for the consultation from the available options.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Select the time for the consultation from the available options.
* **Schedule** (`schedule`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the available time slots for the consultation.
* **Consultation Topic** (`what_is_your_topic`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe the topic of discussion for the consultation.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about the consultation here.
* **Assigned Representative** (`assigned_rep`, `select_one`, optional): Select the sales representative assigned to the consultation from the available options.
