# Botulinum Toxin Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your personal details, medical history, and expectations for botulinum toxin treatment. It will help our consultants understand your primary concern, medical conditions, and lifestyle factors to provide you with the best possible treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your first and last name, date of birth, phone number, email address, and address.
2. Select your gender from the options provided.
3. Choose your preferred consultation date.
4. Select how you heard about our clinic.
5. Indicate your primary concern and treatment goals.
6. Answer the questions about your previous botulinum toxin treatments, medical conditions, current medications, allergies, and other cosmetic procedures.
7. Consider and select relevant lifestyle factors and skin type.
8. Answer questions about your sun exposure habits and expectations from the treatment.
9. Confirm your budget consideration and readiness to proceed with the treatment.
10. List any questions or concerns you may have.
11. Finally, confirm your readiness to schedule the treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1): Your legal first name.
* **Last Name** (2): Your legal last name.
* **Date of Birth** (3): Your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender** (4): Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Phone Number** (5): Your primary contact number for appointment communications.
* **Email Address** (6): Your email address for appointment communications.
* **Address** (7): Your complete home address.
* **Consultation Date** (8): The date of your consultation.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (9): How you found out about our clinic.
* **Primary Concern** (10): Your main reason for consulting with us.
* **Treatment Goals** (11): What you are hoping to achieve with the treatment.
* **Previous Botulinum Toxin Treatments** (12): If you have had botulinum toxin treatments before, select the answer that best describes your experience.
* **Last Treatment Date** (13): If you have had botulinum toxin treatments before, enter the date of your most recent treatment.
* **Medical Conditions** (14): Select any medical conditions that apply to you.
* **Current Medications** (15): List all your current medications and supplements.
* **Allergies** (16): List any allergies you have.
* **Other Cosmetic Procedures** (17): If you have had other cosmetic procedures, select the relevant ones.
* **Lifestyle Factors** (18): Select relevant lifestyle factors that may affect the treatment.
* **Skin Type** (19): How would you describe your skin type?
* **Sun Exposure** (20): How often do you expose yourself to the sun?
* **Expectations** (21): Are your expectations realistic for the treatment?
* **Budget Consideration** (22): Have you considered the cost of the treatment?
* **Questions** (23): If you have any questions or concerns, list them here.
* **Ready to Proceed** (24): Confirm your readiness to schedule the treatment.
* **Consultant Notes** (25): Notes for our internal use only, not visible to you.
