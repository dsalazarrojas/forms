# sustainability_planning_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for sustainability planning appointments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: 
  * Title: This is the title of the form, which is automatically populated.
  * Organization: Enter the name of the organization.
  * Contact Name: Enter the name of the contact person.
  * Email Address: Enter the email address of the contact person.
  * Preferred Contact Method: Select the preferred contact method of the contact person (Phone, Email, or Mail).
  * Assigned To: Select the person the appointment is assigned to (John, Jane, or Jim).
2. Fill in the optional fields: 
  * Phone Number: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
  * Appointment Date: Enter the date of the appointment.
  * Appointment Time: Enter the time of the appointment.
  * Consultation Topic: Enter the topic to be discussed during the appointment.
  * Notes: Enter any additional notes or topics to be discussed during the appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter Organization** (`organization`, text, required): Enter the name of the organization.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, required): Select the preferred contact method of the contact person.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the appointment.
* **Consultation Topic** (`consultation_topic`, text, optional): Enter the topic to be discussed during the appointment.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or topics to be discussed during the appointment.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required): Select the person the appointment is assigned to.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have permission from the contact person before filling out this form.
* Complete the form only once for each appointment and each contact.
* Be accurate when entering contact information to ensure timely and effective communication.
* If the contact method is not selected, the form will not be complete.
* The form is designed to be completed by either internal staff members or external contacts.
