# Consulting Business Consultant Job Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential candidates for consulting business consultant positions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your legal full name, contact email, and years of experience.
2. Provide your mobile phone number for scheduling interviews.
3. Describe your key consulting skills and tools.
4. Share your resume link or a brief summary, and
5. Optionally, mention your earliest availability to start or notice period.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (Full name, text, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your contact email for application updates and communication from the consulting firm.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Provide your mobile phone number for scheduling interviews or further communication.
* **Years of experience** (Years of experience, number, required): Enter the total years of experience you have in consulting or related fields.
* **Core skills** (Core skills, text, required): Describe your key consulting skills and tools, such as project management, data analysis, or software proficiency.
* **Resume link or summary** (Resume link or summary, text, optional): Share a URL to your resume or a brief summary of your work history and experience.
* **Availability to start** (Availability to start, text, optional): If applicable, mention your earliest availability to start or notice period. This will help the consulting firm plan the hiring process and schedule interviews. 

## Tips
- Make sure to proofread your submission for spelling and grammar errors.
- Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the consulting firm for assistance.
