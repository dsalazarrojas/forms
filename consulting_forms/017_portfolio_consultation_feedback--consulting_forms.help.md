# portfolio_consultation_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients and stakeholders on their experience with the portfolio consultation. It helps to identify areas of improvement and to tailor future services to better meet the needs of clients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your answer for "Portfolio Consultation Feedback" from the options provided.
2. Rate your experience with our services by selecting the corresponding number from 1-5.
3. If you have any suggestions or comments, type them in the text box provided.
4. Provide any comments you may have in the "Comments" section.
5. Suggest what you would like to see changed or added with regards to our services.
6. Provide your suggestions on how we can improve.
7. Choose your next steps, or what action you would like us to take next.
8. Provide your contact information so we can follow up with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Portfolio Consultation Feedback** (Required, Select Multiple):
	+ A multiple-choice question to gauge your overall satisfaction with our portfolio consultation services.
* **How would you rate our services** (Required, Select One):
	+ Rate your experience with our services from 1 (Unsatisfied) to 5 (Extremely Satisfied).
* **What would you like to see changed or added** (Optional, Text):
	+ Provide any suggestions or comments you have on what we can do to improve our services.
* **Any comments** (Optional, Note):
	+ Provide any comments you may have, or if you have no comments, type "None" and we will consider it as feedback.
* **How do you think we could improve** (Optional, Text):
	+ Share any ideas or suggestions you have on how we can improve our services.
* **Next steps** (Optional, Select One):
	+ Choose what action you would like us to take next.
* **How may we contact you** (Optional, Text):
	+ Provide your contact information so we can follow up with you.
* **Email** (Optional, Email):
	+ Enter your email address so we can contact you.
 
## Tips
Please be honest in your feedback, it will help us improve our services.
