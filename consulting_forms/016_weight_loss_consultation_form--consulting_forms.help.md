# Weight Loss Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from individuals who are seeking personalized guidance on weight loss. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide the necessary details to our weight loss consultants, allowing them to tailor their advice to your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each field carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
3. Provide your Contact Information, including any necessary details.
4. Enter your Age and Weight accurately.
5. Record your Height accurately.
6. Enter your Email address.
7. Enter your Phone number.
8. If applicable, select any Medical Conditions you have.
9. List any Medications you are currently taking.
10. Select any relevant Medical History.
11. Choose your Goal Weight Loss option.
12. Choose your desired Weight Loss Goal Duration.
13. Select your Exercise Frequency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed in our communication.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed in our communication.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, note, optional): Provide any additional contact information or details you feel are necessary for us to reach you.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): Enter your age accurately.
* **Weight** (`weight`, number, optional): Enter your current weight accurately.
* **Height** (`height`, number, optional): Enter your height accurately.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for us to communicate with you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_one, optional): Select any medical conditions you have, or choose "Other" if none apply.
* **Medications** (`medications`, select_multiple, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, select_multiple, optional): Select any relevant medical history.
* **Goal Weight Loss** (`goal_weight_loss`, select_one, optional): Choose your desired weight loss goal.
* **Weight Loss Goal Duration** (`weight_loss_goal_duration`, select_multiple, optional): Select your desired weight loss goal duration.
* **Exercise Frequency** (`exercise_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose your exercise frequency.

## Tips

* Please ensure the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* A weight loss consultant will review your submission and be in touch with you soon.
