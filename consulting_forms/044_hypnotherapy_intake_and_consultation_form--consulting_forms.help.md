# Hypnotherapy Intake And Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about your medical history and conditions before your hypnotherapy session. Please provide detailed answers to ensure accurate assessment and effective treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in your First Name.
2. Enter your Email address, if you have one.
3. Provide your Phone number for contact and follow-up purposes.
4. Enter your address or physical location.
5. Share your Occupation or professional position.
6. Describe any medical history you or your family members have experienced.
7. Provide Additional Medical History.
8. Share Previous Medical History.
9. Describe Family Medical History.
10. Share Current Medical History.
11-15: List any Medical Conditions you or your family members experience, such as diabetes, allergies, etc.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name, which will be used to address you during the consultation.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): If you have a valid email address, provide it for communication and follow-up purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for the practitioner to reach you.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your physical address or location where you can be contacted or attended to.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, text, required/optional): Describe your occupation or professional position.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required/optional): Provide detailed information about your medical history.
* **Additional Medical History** (`medical_history_2`, text, required/optional): Add any further information that may be relevant to your medical history.
* **Previous Medical History** (`medical_history_3`, text, required/optional): Enter any history of medical issues or conditions you or your family have experienced.
* **Family Medical History** (`medical_history_4`, text, required/optional): Share any medical conditions or issues in your family.
* **Current Medical History** (`medical_history_5`, text, required/optional): Describe any ongoing medical conditions or health issues you're currently experiencing.
* **Medical Condition 1** (`medical_condition_1`, text, required/optional): List any medical conditions you or your family experience, starting with the first condition.
* **Medical Condition 2-15** (`medical_condition_2`, `medical_condition_3`, ..., `medical_condition_15`, text, required/optional): Continue listing any medical conditions you or your family experience.

Note: Some fields are marked as optional, but please provide accurate and complete information to ensure the best possible hypnotherapy experience.
