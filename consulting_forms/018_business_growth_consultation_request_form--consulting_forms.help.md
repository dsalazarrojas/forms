# Business Growth Consultation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential clients about their business growth goals and challenges, allowing us to provide tailored consultation services. Please fill out this form to help us understand your business needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your full name, professional email address, phone number, and company website link.
2. Provide your current monthly revenue range and select your top business goal for the next 6 months.
3. Describe your biggest growth obstacle, which is currently holding you back.
4. Choose a preferred time for the consultation call and select how soon you want to start implementing changes.
5. Estimate your monthly marketing budget.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Your actual name as it appears on your business cards or correspondence.
* **Professional Email Address**: Your business email address, not your personal one. This is where we will contact you for follow-up information.
* **Contact Phone Number**: Include your country or area code for international calls.
* **Company Website Link**: A link to your business website, so we can better understand your business before our call.
* **Current Monthly Revenue Range**: Choose from the options below to indicate your current revenue range. This will help us understand your business size.
	+ Under 5,000 USD
	+ 5,001 - 25,000 USD
	+ 25,001 - 100,000 USD
	+ Over 100,000 USD
* **Top Business Goal for Next 6 Months**: Choose one of the options below to indicate your main goal.
	+ Increase Sales Revenue
	+ Improve Operational Efficiency
	+ Expand to New Markets
	+ Launch New Product/Service
	+ Build / Scale Team
* **Biggest Growth Obstacle**: Describe the main challenge that's holding you back from achieving your goals.
* **Preferred Time for Consultation Call**: Choose one of the time slots below to schedule your call.
	+ Morning (9AM - 12PM)
	+ Afternoon (12PM - 4PM)
	+ Late Afternoon / Evening (4PM - 7PM)
* **How Soon do You Want to Start Implementing Changes**: Choose from the options below to indicate when you'd like to start making changes.
	+ Immediately
	+ Within 1 month
	+ Within 3 months
	+ Just exploring for now
* **Estimated Monthly Marketing Budget**: Choose from the options below to indicate your monthly marketing budget.
	+ Under 1,000 USD
	+ 1,000 - 5,000 USD
	+ 5,001 - 15,000 USD
	+ 15,000+ USD
* **Request Date**: Choose a date for your consultation request.
