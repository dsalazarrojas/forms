# Massage Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Massage Consultation Form is designed for patients to provide information about their massage consultation needs. To complete this form, please follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the required fields. Start by providing your name, contact number, and email address. Then, select the date of your consultation and choose the type of massage service you prefer (Massage, Aromatherapy, Swedish, or Deep Tissue). Next, describe the benefits of massage and your medical history. Finally, enter the frequency of your massage consultations and provide any relevant information about your pain level and type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed for the massage therapy service.
* **2. Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number so we can reach out to you with any updates or questions.
* **3. Email Address** (`email_address`, text, required): Provide your email address so we can communicate with you about your massage consultation.
* **4. Consultation Date** (`consultation_date`, date, required): Select the date of your scheduled consultation.
* **5. Massage Services** (`massage_services`, select_one, required): Choose the type of massage service you prefer from the options provided: Massage, Aromatherapy, Swedish, or Deep Tissue.
* **6. Benefits of Massage** (`benefits_of_massage`, text, required): Describe the benefits you expect from your massage consultation.
* **7. Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Share any relevant medical history or health concerns that may affect your massage consultation.
* **8. Massage Therapist** (`massage_therapist`, text, required): Enter the name of your preferred massage therapist.
* **9. Massage Frequency** (`massage_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how often you would like to have massage consultations from the options provided: 1 time, 2 times a week, 3 times a week, 4 times a week, or daily.
* **10. Massage Therapist 2** (`massage_therapist_2`, text, required): Enter any additional information about your massage therapist (if applicable).
* **11. Pain Level** (`pain_level`, text, optional): If you are experiencing pain, describe the level of pain you are experiencing.
* **12. Pain Type** (`pain_type`, text, optional): If you are experiencing pain, describe the type of pain you are experiencing.

Note: Please fill in all required fields. If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
