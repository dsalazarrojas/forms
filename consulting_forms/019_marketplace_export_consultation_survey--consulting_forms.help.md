# marketplace_export_consultation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketplace Export Consultation Survey is designed to help us understand your export consultation needs and goals. This information will help us tailor our export services to meet your specific requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully to ensure you understand each section and its purpose.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing clear and concise information.
3. Submit the form when you are ready.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction** (id: 1, text, required: false)
	+ This is a short introduction section where you can briefly explain your export needs and goals.

### Market Insights
* **Market Insights** (id: 2, text, required: false)
	+ Describe your current market insights, including any relevant information about your target markets and competitors.

### Export Strategy
* **Export Strategy** (id: 3, text, required: false)
	+ Share your current export strategy, including any existing or planned export channels or methods.

### Export Readiness
* **Export Readiness** (id: 4, select_one, required: true)
	+ Choose whether you are ready to export, with options 'True' or 'False'.

### Target Markets
* **Target Markets** (id: 5, select_one, required: true)
	+ Select your target markets, with options 'USA', 'EU', or 'Asia'.

### Export Channels
* **Export Channels** (id: 6, select_multiple, required: true)
	+ Choose the export channels you plan to use, with options 'True' or 'False'.

### Export Planning
* **Export Planning** (id: 7, time, required: false)
	+ This section is not applicable for this form, as it only allows for a time input, but is not required. You can disregard this field.

### Export Goals
* **Export Goals** (id: 8, number, required: true)
	+ Enter a number representing your export goals, such as the number of new export opportunities you aim to achieve within a certain timeframe.

### Feedback
* **Feedback** (id: 9, email, required: true)
	+ Provide your email address for feedback and follow-up on your export consultation.

## Tips
* Ensure you answer all required fields to ensure your consultation request is properly evaluated.
* Be as detailed as possible in your answers to provide us with a clear understanding of your needs and goals.
* If you have any additional information or questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.
