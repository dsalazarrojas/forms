# Digital Business Optimization Consulting Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients about their business needs and preferences. It is used to provide a clear understanding of the client's requirements and to guide the consulting process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your client information, including name, contact details, and company information.
2. Select the services you are interested in.
3. Provide clear and concise information about your goals and objectives.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information** (`client_info`): Enter your company name and any other relevant information about your business.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): Enter your name, email, and phone number to ensure we can contact you.
* **Company Information** (`company_info`): Provide any additional information about your company.
* **Services** (`services`): Select the services you are interested in, or choose "Other" if none of the options are correct.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date for the desired consulting session.
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time for the desired consulting session.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you.
* **Note** (`note`): Enter any additional information or notes about your consultation.
* **Service B** (`service_b`): Select whether you are interested in Service B.
* **Service C** (`service_c`): Select whether you are interested in Service C.
* **Service A** (`service_a`): Select whether you are interested in Service A.
* **Goal** (`goal`): Enter your main goal or objective.
* **Goals** (`goal_2`, `goal_3`, `goals_1`, etc.): Enter any additional goals or objectives.

Please note that all fields are optional, and you can fill in as much or as little information as you need.
