# Free Client Consultation - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients for a free consultation session. The purpose of this form is to capture basic contact information, appointment details, and client preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone number.
2. Choose your Appointment Date and Time.
3. Select your Client Type (Business, Personal, or Other).
4. Choose your Schedule Type (In-person, Over-the-phone, Video call, or Online).
5. If you have any specific preferences or questions, type them in the Message field.
6. Click the "Submit" button to send your consultation request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name, please. (Text)
* **Last Name**: Your last name, please. (Text)
* **Email**: A valid email address where you can be reached. (Email)
* **Phone**: Your phone number. (Text)
* **Appointment Date**: The date of your desired consultation. (Date)
* **Appointment Time**: The time of your desired consultation. (Time)
* **Consultant Name**: The name of the consultant you'd like to meet with. (Text, not required)
* **Client Type**: Please select the type of consultation you're requesting (Business, Personal, or Other). (Select One)
* **Schedule Type**: Please choose a schedule type that suits you best (In-person, Over-the-phone, Video call, or Online). (Select Multiple)
* **Message**: Any specific questions or preferences you have for the consultation. (Note)
