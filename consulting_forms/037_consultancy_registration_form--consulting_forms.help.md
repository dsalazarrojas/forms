# Consultancy Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form gathers information about your consultancy needs to provide a personalized service. Please fill out the form to get started with our consultancy process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields marked as **required**.
2. Select the options carefully, as they may affect your consultancy experience.
3. Review your information before submission to ensure it's accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company/Organization Legal Name** (`company_legal_name`, text, **required**): Enter the official name of your company or organization.
* **Primary Contact Person** (`primary_contact_person`, text, **required**): Enter the name of your primary contact person.
* **Primary Email Address** (`primary_contact_email`, email, **required**): Enter the email address of your primary contact person, where follow-up communication will be sent.
* **Primary Phone Number** (`primary_contact_phone`, text, **required**, Format: (555) 555-5555): Enter the phone number of your primary contact person.
* **Company Location (City, State, Country)** (`company_location_address`, text, **required**): Enter the location of your company.
* **Primary Consultancy Service Needed** (`consultancy_service_needed`, select_one, **required**): Choose the most relevant consultancy service category (e.g. Management/Strategy Consulting, Technology/IT Transformation).
* **What is your primary project objective?** (`primary_project_objective`, text, **required**, Max 1000 characters): Enter a brief description of your project's goal.
* **Estimated Project Duration** (`estimated_project_duration`, select_one, **required**): Select the estimated duration of your project.
* **Estimated Project Budget Range ($)** (`estimated_project_budget`, select_one, **required**): Choose the budget range for your project.
* **How soon do you plan to start?** (`project_urgency_ident`, select_one, **required**): Select the timeframe for your project's start.
* **Preferred Engagement Model** (`preferred_engagement_model`, select_one, **required**): Choose the preferred engagement model for your project (e.g. Time and Materials (T&M), Fixed Price/Scope).
* **How did you hear about our consultancy?** (`referral_source_ident`, select_one, **optional**): Choose how you heard about our consultancy (e.g. Website/Search Engine, Professional Referral).
* **Any additional details you'd like to share?** (`additional_comments_desc`, text, **optional**): Add any additional comments or details about your project.
* **Date of Registration** (`registration_date`, date, **required**): Select the date of your registration.
* **Would you like to receive our quarterly industry insights?** (`confirmation_email_opt_in`, select_one, **required**): Select whether you'd like to receive our quarterly industry insights.

Note: The fields marked as **optional** can be filled out if you feel it's necessary or relevant to your project.
