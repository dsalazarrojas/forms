# Fashion Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fashion Consultation Form is designed to gather information from customers about their fashion preferences and needs. This form will be used to provide personalized fashion advice and consultation services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), which are:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Email
	* Consultation Type (Wardrobe, Styling, Personalized Fashion Advice, or Other)
2. Choose your preferred contact method (Phone, Email, Mail, or Other)
3. Provide any additional comments or details in the "Description" field, if necessary.
4. Choose your style preferences (Fashionable, Elegant, Trendy, Classic, Quirky, or Other)
5. Select your color preferences (Black, White, Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, or Other)
6. Choose your clothing size (2-4, 6-8, 10-12, 14-16, or Other)
7. Select your available days (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, or Sunday)
8. Specify the duration of your consultation time (number of minutes)
9. If necessary, provide any additional comments or details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`firstName`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Last Name** (`lastName`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Date of Visit** (`dateOfVisit`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your preferred visit (if applicable).
* **Time of Visit** (`timeOfVisit`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of your preferred visit (if applicable).
* **Consultation Type** (`consultationType`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of consultation you are seeking (Wardrobe, Styling, Personalized Fashion Advice, or Other).
* **Description** (`description`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or details about your consultation needs.
* **Style Preferences** (`stylePreferences`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the style preferences that best suit your fashion needs (Fashionable, Elegant, Trendy, Classic, Quirky, or Other).
* **Color Preferences** (`colorPreferences`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the colors you prefer for fashion advice (Black, White, Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, or Other).
* **Budget** (`budget`, `number`, optional): Enter your budget for the consultation (if applicable).
* **Clothing Size** (`clothingSize`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your clothing size (2-4, 6-8, 10-12, 14-16, or Other).
* **Available Days** (`availableDays`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the days you are available for the consultation (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, or Sunday).
* **Time of Visit Duration** (`timeOfVisitDuration`, `number`, optional): Enter the duration of your consultation time (in minutes).
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferredContactMethod`, `select_one`, optional): Select your preferred contact method (Phone, Email, Mail, or Other).

Note: The fields marked as optional are not required for the form submission, but filling them out can provide additional information for the consultant.
