# Consulting Services Agreement Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a template for a consulting services agreement between a consultant and a client. It outlines the terms of the engagement, including the scope of work, payment terms, and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form

### Page 1: Consultant Details
This page requires information about the consultant, including their full name, business address, and signature (if applicable).

* **Consultant Full Name**: Enter the consultant's full name as it appears on their business card or license.
* **Consultant Business Address**: Enter the primary business address of the consultant.
* **Agreement Efficie Date**: Enter the date when the agreement starts. This is usually the same date as today's date.

### Page 2: Client Details
This page requires information about the client, including their company name, contact name, contact email, and signature.

* **Client Company Name**: Enter the name of the company hiring the consultant.
* **Client Contact Name**: Enter the full name of the primary contact person.
* **Client Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the primary contact person.
* **Agreement Signature Date**: Enter today's date.

### Page 3: Scope of Work
This page outlines the details of the project, including the project name, description, and estimated duration.

* **Project Name**: Give the project a name that is clear and descriptive.
* **Scope Description**: Enter a detailed description of the work to be performed.
* **Estimated Project Duration**: Estimate how long the project is expected to take.
* **Key Deliverables**: List the primary outputs of the project.

### Page 4: Financial Terms
This page outlines the financial terms of the engagement, including the fee structure, amount, and payment terms.

* **Fee Structure**: Choose the payment structure from the available options (Hourly Rate, Fixed Project Fee, Monthly Retainer, Performance Based).
* **Fee Amount**: Enter the dollar amount of the fee.
* **Payment Terms (Net Days)**: Enter the number of days after the invoice to pay.

### Page 5: Legal Agreement
This page confirms that both parties agree on the terms of the engagement.

* **Confidentiality Clause**: Confirm that the consultant agrees to keep client data private.
* **Agreement Efficie Date**: Confirm the date when the agreement starts.
* **Agreement Signature Date**: Confirm the date when the agreement is signed.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form carefully and accurately to avoid any miscommunication or misinterpretation.
* Review the agreement carefully before signing to ensure you understand the terms and responsibilities.
* Keep the agreement on file for future reference and to ensure compliance with the terms. 

Note: This guide is based on the provided form structure and may not cover all possible use cases. Please adjust as needed.
