# Expert Consultation Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to request expert consultation for users seeking guidance on a specific topic or issue. It is intended for users who require expert advice or opinion on a particular matter. The form is meant to be completed by the user seeking consultation, providing relevant information about their request and the expert they wish to consult with.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form header to understand the purpose and scope of your consultation request.
2. Provide the necessary information on "Information Needed" in the text box.
3. Answer "Preferred Dates" by selecting one or more preferred dates for the consultation.
4. Answer "Consultation Details" by selecting the nature of the consultation (yes/no).
5. Provide details about the expert you would like to consult with in "Consultant Info".
6. Specify the type of consultation (Consulting/Meeting/Other).
7. Enter the consultant's name in "Consultant Name".
8. Enter the consultant's email address in "Consultant Email".
9. Enter the consultant's phone number in "Consultant Phone".
10. Provide additional client information in "Client Info".
11. Enter any meeting details in "Meeting Details".
12. Finally, select the preferred method of communication (Phone/Email/In-person) and preferred location (Yes/No) for the consultation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header** (1): A note that briefly explains the form purpose and instructions.
* **Information Needed** (2): A text field where you can provide any relevant information about your consultation request.
* **Preferred Dates** (3): A select multiple field to indicate your preferred dates for the consultation.
* **Consultation Details** (4): A select one field to indicate if you have any details about the consultation (yes/no).
* **Consultant Info** (5): A text field to provide any relevant information about the expert you would like to consult with.
* **Consultation Type** (6): A select one field to specify the type of consultation (Consulting/Meeting/Other).
* **Consultant Name** (7): A text field to enter the expert's name.
* **Consultant Email** (8): An email field to enter the expert's email address.
* **Consultant Phone** (9): A text field to enter the expert's phone number.
* **Client Info** (10): A text field to provide any additional client information.
* **Meeting Details** (11): A text field to enter any meeting details.
* **Preferred Method** (12): A select one field to select your preferred method of communication (Phone/Email/In-person).
* **Preferred Location** (13): A select one field to indicate if you prefer to have the consultation at a specific location (Yes/No).
