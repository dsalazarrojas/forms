# Reconstructive Keratin Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture information about a client's keratin treatment goals, hair history, and personal details to provide tailored advice and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Provide clear and concise answers to each question.
3. Ensure that each submission is unique and reflects the current client's requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Info (`general_info`, text, optional)**: This field is for providing general information about the client, such as name, address, or any other relevant details.
* **Hair History (`hair_history`, text, optional)**: Describe the client's hair history, including any relevant health issues, previous treatments, or changes in hair behavior.
* **Keratin Goals (`keratin_goals`, text, optional)**: Explain the client's specific keratin treatment goals, such as improving hair texture, reducing frizz, or eliminating split ends.
* **Treatment Goals (`treatment_goals`, text, optional)**: Describe the client's expectations for the treatment, including any specific outcomes or results they hope to achieve.
* **Notes (`notes`, note, optional)**: Provide any additional comments or questions from the client that may be relevant to the treatment.
* **Client Email (`client_email`, email, optional)**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Client Phone (`client_phone`, text, optional)**: Enter the client's phone number.
* **Hair Problems (`hair_problems`, text, optional)**: List any hair problems or issues that the client is experiencing.
