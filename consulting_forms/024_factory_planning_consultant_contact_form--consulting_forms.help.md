# Factory Planning Consultant Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather contact information from factory planning consultants who are interested in collaborating with the factory planning team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the text field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the text field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the format of `username@example.com`.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the format of `XXX-XXX-XXXX`.
5. Enter your **Company** name.
6. Select your **Role** from the dropdown menu:
	* Consultant
	* Advisor
	* Manager
7. Enter your **Consultant Experience** in a numerical value (e.g. 5 years).
8. Select your **Company Size** from the dropdown menu:
	* Small
	* Medium
	* Large
9. Indicate if you are a lead source:
	* Yes
	* No
10. (Optional) If you have selected "Yes" above, please specify the source of your lead in the text field below.
11. Enter any additional **Message** you would like to include in your contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address in the format of `username@example.com`.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number in the format of `XXX-XXX-XXXX`.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter your company name.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Select your role in the factory planning consultant team.
* **Consultant Experience** (`consultant_experience`, number, required): Enter your years of experience as a consultant.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, required): Select the size of your company.
* **Lead Source** (`lead_source`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if you are a lead source.
* **Message** (`message`, text, required): Enter any additional information you would like to include in your contact information.

## Tips
* Please be concise in your responses.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for formatting accuracy.
* If you are a lead source, please specify the source of your lead to help us better understand your interest in collaborating with our team.
