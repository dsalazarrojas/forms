# corporate_social_responsibility_quotation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Corporate Social Responsibility Quotation Form is designed to capture essential information about Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) initiatives undertaken by a vendor for a customer. This form will help vendors to create a quote for their CSR services, including details about the initiative, customer information, and quote price.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter a brief description of the Corporate Social Responsibility initiative in the "Corporate Social Responsibility Initiative" field.
2. Provide the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
3. Input the start and end dates of the initiative in the "Initiative Start Date" and "Initiative End Date" fields, respectively.
4. Enter the vendor's name in the "Vendor Name" field.
5. Enter the vendor's address in the "Vendor Address" field.
6. (Optional) Enter the vendor's email in the "Vendor Email" field.
7. (Optional) Enter the vendor's phone number in the "Vendor Phone Number" field.
8. Enter the quote price in the "Quote Price" field.
9. (Optional) Enter any additional services in the "Additional Services" field. This field offers multiple options, such as:
	* Option 1
	* Option 2
	* Option 3
10. Finally, add any additional comments or notes in the "Quotation Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Corporate Social Responsibility Initiative**: Enter a brief description of the CSR initiative. This field is required.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer for whom the CSR initiative is being done.
* **Initiative Start Date**: Input the start date of the CSR initiative. This field is required.
* **Initiative End Date**: Input the end date of the CSR initiative. This field is required.
* **Vendor Name**: Enter the name of the vendor performing the CSR initiative.
* **Vendor Address**: Enter the address of the vendor performing the CSR initiative.
* **Vendor Email**: (Optional) Enter the email address of the vendor performing the CSR initiative.
* **Vendor Phone Number**: (Optional) Enter the phone number of the vendor performing the CSR initiative.
* **Quote Price**: Enter the price quote for the CSR initiative. This field is required.
* **Quotation Notes**: (Optional) Enter any additional comments or notes about the CSR initiative.
* **Additional Services**: (Optional) Select any additional services offered by the vendor as part of the CSR initiative. This field allows multiple selections.
