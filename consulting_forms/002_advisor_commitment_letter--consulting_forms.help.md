# Advisor Commitment Letter - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Advisor Commitment Letter is a form that collects information from advisors to ensure a clear understanding of their commitment to the organization. It's a crucial document that outlines the terms of collaboration, including areas of expertise, availability, and compensation expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Full Name**, which should be your legal name.
2. Fill in your **Title or Position**, which represents your professional title in your current organization.
3. Provide your **Company or Organization**, which is where you currently work or are affiliated with.
4. Enter your **Professional Email Address** for official correspondence.
5. Fill in your **Phone Number** for business purposes.
6. List your **Areas of Expertise** in the relevant field.
7. Choose the **Expected Commitment Duration**, which is how long you're willing to serve as an advisor.
8. Specify your **Estimated Availability Hours Per Month** you can dedicate to advisory activities.
9. Select your **Preferred Meeting Format**, which is how you'd like to conduct advisory meetings.
10. Choose your **Compensation Expectations**, which determines how you'll be compensated for your services.
11. Fill in the **Conflict of Interest Disclosure** if you have any potential conflicts.
12. Read and acknowledge the **Agreement Terms**, which outline the terms of your collaboration.
13. Confirm that you've read and understood the **Agreement Terms**.
14. Sign the **Confidentiality Agreement** if you agree to maintain confidentiality.
15. Understand that any shared ideas during advisory sessions may be owned by the organization (check **Intellectual Property Acknowledgment**).
16. Sign the **Confidentiality Agreement** with your full name.
17. Date your **Signature**, which is when you sign this commitment letter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Advisor Information**: This section is an introduction to your profile, which helps the organization understand your background.
* **Full Name**: Your legal name, used to identify you in official records.
* **Title or Position**: Your current professional title in your organization.
* **Company or Organization**: Your current employer or affiliated organization.
* **Professional Email Address**: Your email address used for business correspondence.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number for business purposes.
* **Areas of Expertise**: A list of your primary areas of expertise, which helps the organization understand your areas of focus.
* **Expected Commitment Duration**: The length of time you're willing to serve as an advisor to the organization.
* **Estimated Availability Hours Per Month**: The number of hours you can dedicate to advisory activities.
* **Preferred Meeting Format**: How you'd like to conduct meetings (in-person, video conference, phone call, or hybrid).
* **Compensation Expectations**: How you'd like to be compensated for your services.
* **Conflict of Interest Disclosure**: If you have any potential conflicts of interest, please disclose them.
* **Agreement Terms**: A note section that outlines the terms of your collaboration.
* **Terms Acknowledged**: Confirm that you've read and understood the agreement terms.
* **Confidentiality Agreement**: Agree to maintain confidentiality of proprietary information.
* **Intellectual Property Acknowledgment**: Understand that any shared ideas may be owned by the organization.
* **Electronic Signature**: Sign this commitment letter with your full name.
* **Date of Signature**: The date when this commitment letter is signed.
