# Virtual Assistant / Online Business Manager Service Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Assistant / Online Business Manager Service Request form is designed to collect information from potential clients who are interested in our virtual assistant or online business manager services. This form will be used to understand their specific needs and schedule a follow-up meeting or onboarding session.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your business name in the "Business Name" field. This is the name of your business or organization.
2.  Enter your contact email in the "Contact Email" field. This is the email address that we will use to contact you.
3.  Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field. This is the number we will use to call you.
4.  In the "Service Inquiry" field, describe the specific services you are interested in and how we can assist you.
5.  Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Business Type" field to indicate if your business is a personal or a corporate entity.
6.  Enter the date you are available for the service request in the "Service Request Date" field.
7.  Enter the time you are available for the service request in the "Service Request Time" field.
8.  Enter the location for the service request in the "Service Request Location" field.
9.  In the "Additional Info" field, provide any additional information that will help us understand your specific needs.
10.  The "Assigned Agent" field is optional and will be used by our team to assign a specific agent to work with you.
11.  The "Created", "Updated", "Submitted", "Follow Up Date", "Follow Up Time", "Onboarding Date", and "Onboarding Time" fields are automatically generated and do not need to be filled out.
12.  Select the status of your request in the "Status" field. This will be used to track the progress of your request.
13.  In the "Notes" field, provide any additional comments or information about your request.
14.  In the "Tags" field, enter any relevant tags or keywords related to your request.
15.  Enter the person who submitted the request in the "Submitted by" field. This will be used to track who submitted the request.
16.  Enter the date the request was submitted in the "Submitted date" field.
17.  Enter the time the request was submitted in the "Submitted time" field.
