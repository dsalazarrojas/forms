# Innovation Strategy Advisory Program Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Innovation Strategy Advisory Program Application Form is designed to help individuals or teams seeking guidance on innovation strategies. By completing this form, you will help us understand your background, company, and project focus, and receive tailored advice on how to develop a successful innovation strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please ensure you have all the necessary information about yourself and your project before starting the form.
2. Read each field carefully and enter the required information.
3. Review each field carefully before submitting your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form (`application_form`)**: A brief description of your innovation project. Please provide a clear and concise overview of your project, including your goals and objectives.
* **Applicant Name (`applicant_name`)**: Your name as it should be displayed on any correspondence and reports related to your project.
* **Company Name (`company_name`)**: The name of your company or organization.
* **Contact Email (`contact_email`)**: Your email address that we will use to contact you regarding your application and any further support.
* **Contact Phone (`contact_phone`)**: Your phone number that we will use to contact you regarding your application and any further support.
* **Address (`address`)**: Your company's or organization's physical address.
* **Date of Birth (`date_of_birth`)**: The date of your birth (not required, but it can be useful for us to understand your experience level).
* **Innovation Focus (`innovation_focus`)**: Select all areas where you would like to focus your innovation strategy, such as Innovation Strategy, Innovation Culture, or Digital Transformation.
* **Innovation Description (`innovation_description`)**: A brief description of your project's innovation focus area.
* **Innovation Status (`innovation_status`)**: Select whether your innovation project is currently Active or Inactive.
* **Team Size (`team_size`)**: Select the size of your team, which can be 1-5 members, 6-10 members, 11-50 members, or 51+ members.
* **Industry (`industry`)**: Select the industry related to your project.
* **Job Title (`job_title`)**: A brief description of your job title.
* **Work Experience (`work_experience`)**: Select the length of your work experience, which can be 1-5 years, 5-10 years, 10-20 years, or 21+ years.
* **Innovation Location (`innovation_location`)**: Select where you plan to work on your innovation project, which can be Remote, On-site, Hybrid, or Other.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in completely and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* Please review your application carefully before submitting it to ensure it is complete and accurate.
