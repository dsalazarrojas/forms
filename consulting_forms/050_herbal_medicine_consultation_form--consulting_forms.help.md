# Herbal Medicine Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Herbal Medicine Consultation Form is a tool used by clients to provide their personal and medical details to herbal medicine practitioners. This information is used to guide the consultation process and create a treatment plan tailored to the client's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out all the required fields as accurately as possible.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact the practitioner for clarification.
- Review the information carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details (page 1)**: Provide your name, email address, and phone number for contact purposes.
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **email**: Enter your email address as it appears on your identification.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number as it appears on your identification.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions you have.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies or sensitivities you have.
* **Medical History**: Describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses or surgeries.
* **Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical Specialties**: Mention any medical specialties you have, such as diabetes or high blood pressure.
* **Consultation Type**: Select the type of consultation you need, such as initial assessment or follow-up consultation.
* **Appointment Time**: Choose your preferred appointment time.
* **Appointment Date**: Choose your preferred appointment date.
* **Herbal Medicine Practitioner Info**: Provide any relevant information about your current medical practitioner or previous consultations.
* **Treatment Plan**: Enter any specific treatment plans you have already received.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or concerns you have about your health.
* **Follow-up Instructions**: If applicable, provide any instructions for follow-up appointments.
* **Herbal Recommendations**: Provide any herbal recommendations you have received from previous practitioners.
* **Follow-up Appointments**: List any follow-up appointments you have scheduled.
* **Client Signature**: Sign and date the form, indicating that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms.
* **Practitioner Signature**: Sign and date the form, indicating that you have accepted the form.
* **Follow-up Date**: Provide the date of your next follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-up Time**: Provide the time of your next follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-up Duration**: Describe the length of your next follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-up Details**: Provide any details about your next follow-up appointment.
