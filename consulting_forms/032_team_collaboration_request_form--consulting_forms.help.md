# Team Collaboration Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Collaboration Request Form is a tool used to request support and resources from team members when working on a project or task that requires collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the team member(s) who will be collaborating on the project.
2. Choose the type of support you need (Team Lead Support, Project Manager Support, or Project Team Support).
3. Provide details about the collaboration, including the date and start and end times.
4. Add any additional comments or notes about the collaboration.
5. Enter your email address and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Member** (`team_member_details`): Select the team member(s) who will be collaborating on the project. This will help the team lead/project manager understand who is involved and ensure that everyone is informed.
* **Requested Resource** (`requested_resource`): Choose the type of support you need for the collaboration. Options include Team Lead Support, Project Manager Support, and Project Team Support.
* **Project Collaboration Details** (`project_collaboration_details`): Provide a brief description of the collaboration, including any relevant project details.
* **Collaboration Date** (`collaboration_date`): Enter the date on which the collaboration will take place.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`): Enter the start time of the collaboration.
* **End Time** (`end_time`): Enter the end time of the collaboration.
* **Collaboration Note** (`collaboration_note`): Add any additional comments or notes about the collaboration.
* **E-Mail** (`email`): Enter your email address so that the team lead/project manager can contact you with any updates or questions.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number so that the team lead/project manager can contact you with any updates or questions.
* **E-Mail and Phone** fields are required to be able to contact you.
Note: Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your collaboration request is processed efficiently. If you have any questions or need further assistance, do not hesitate to contact your team lead/project manager.
