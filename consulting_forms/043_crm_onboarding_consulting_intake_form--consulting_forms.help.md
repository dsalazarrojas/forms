# CRM Onboarding Consulting Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information about your company and its needs for CRM onboarding consulting services. We will use this information to tailor our consulting approach to your specific requirements and provide you with a tailored solution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as instructed, providing as much detail as possible for each question. Make sure to answer all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*). If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Please enter your company's name as it appears on your website or business cards.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, optional): If you have a website, please enter the URL.
* **Industry or Business Type** (`industry`, text, required): Briefly describe your company's industry or business type.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, required): Select the number of employees in your company:
	+ Startup (1-10)
	+ Small (11-50)
	+ Medium (51-200)
	+ Large (201-1000)
	+ Enterprise (1000+)
* **Primary Contact Name** (`primary_contact`, text, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person for your company.
* **Contact Title or Position** (`contact_title`, text, required): Enter the title or position of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the primary contact person.
* **CRM Experience Level** (`crm_experience`, select_one, required): Select your team's level of CRM expertise:
	+ No experience
	+ Some experience
	+ Moderate experience
	+ Extensive experience
* **Currently Using CRM System** (`current_crm`, select_one, required): Select if you are currently using a CRM system:
	+ Salesforce
	+ HubSpot
	+ Microsoft Dynamics
	+ Pipedrive
	+ Zoho
	+ Other
	+ Not using CRM
* **Current CRM System Name** (`current_crm_name`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" above, enter the name of your current CRM system.
* **Main Onboarding Goals** (`onboarding_goals`, text, required): Describe the main goals you want to achieve through the onboarding process.
* **Current Business Challenges** (`key_challenges`, text, required): Describe the current business challenges you are facing that a CRM can help solve.
* **Required CRM Features** (`required_features`, select_multiple, required): Check all the features you require from your CRM:
	+ Sales pipeline management
	+ Contact management
	+ Lead scoring
	+ Workflow automation
	+ Reporting and analytics
	+ Email integration
	+ Mobile app
	+ API access
	+ Integration with other systems
* **Data Migration Needed** (`data_migration`, select_one, required): Select if you need to import existing data:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Unsure
* **Data Source for Migration** (`data_source`, text, optional): If you selected "True" above, enter the current system or format of your data.
* **CRM User Team Size** (`team_size`, number, required): Enter the number of team members who will use the CRM.
* **Training and Support Needs** (`training_needs`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the training and support needs you require:
	+ User training
	+ Administrator training
	+ Documentation
	+ Ongoing support
	+ Custom development
* **Preferred Timeline** (`timeline`, select_one, required): Select your preferred onboarding timeline:
	+ 2-4 weeks
	+ 1 month
	+ 2-3 months
	+ 3-6 months
	+ 6+ months
	+ Flexible
* **Budget Estimate** (`budget_estimate`, select_one, required): Select your approximate budget for the project:
	+ Under 5000
	+ 5000-15000
	+ 15000-25000
	+ 25000-50000
	+ Over 50000
	+ To be determined
