# Business Process Audit Consulting Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from clients seeking business process audit consulting services. It helps the consultant understand the client's company details, audit objectives, and scope, as well as their expectations for the audit process and timeline.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the client information section to provide the consultant with your company's details.
2. Choose the primary audit objectives and processes to be audited.
3. Describe the current challenges you are experiencing and the expected outcomes from the audit.
4. Provide the desired audit start date and your timeline urgency.
5. Indicate your budget range for the audit.
6. Confirm your data availability and personnel availability for interviews.
7. Add any additional information that might be relevant to the audit process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Company Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Enter your company's name.
* **Client Type** (`client_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the industry or sector your company belongs to (e.g., Manufacturing, Retail, Technology, etc.).
* **Company Size** (`client_size`, `select_one`, required): Choose the approximate number of employees in your company.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for the audit project.
* **Title or Position** (`contact_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title or position of the contact person.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the business email of the contact person.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the direct contact number of the contact person.
* **Primary Audit Objectives** (`audit_objectives`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the primary objectives for the audit (e.g., Process Efficiency, Cost Optimization, etc.).
* **Processes to Be Audited** (`processes_to_audit`, `text`, required): Describe the business processes to be audited.
* **Current Challenges** (`current_challenges`, `text`, required): Describe the current challenges you are experiencing.
* **Expected Outcomes** (`expected_outcomes`, `text`, required): Describe the expected improvements from the audit.
* **Desired Audit Start Date** (`audit_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the desired start date for the audit.
* **Timeline Urgency** (`timeline_urgency`, `select_one`, required): Indicate the level of urgency for the audit (e.g., Not urgent, Moderate, High, etc.).
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, required): Enter the approximate consulting budget for the audit.
* **Data Availability** (`data_availability`, `select_one`, required): Confirm the availability of necessary documentation for the audit.
* **Personnel Availability** (`personnel_availability`, `select_one`, required): Confirm the availability of key staff for interviews.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, `text`, required): Enter any other relevant details about the audit project.
