# Risk Intelligence Consulting Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about the prospect's risk concerns and objectives, providing valuable insights for risk intelligence consulting services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your name and contact information in the "Prospect Info" field.
2. Select all the risk concerns that apply to you in the "Risk Concerns" field. You can choose one or multiple options.
3. Indicate your current risk maturity level in the "Risk Maturity" field.
4. Choose the primary objective of your organization in the "Objective" field.
5. Provide a brief description of the risks you are concerned about in the "Risk Description (optional)" field.
6. Outline the next steps you would like the consulting service to take in the "Next Steps" field.
7. Add any additional comments or information in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Prospect Info**: This field is for you to enter your name and contact information.
* **Risk Concerns**: This field allows you to select all the risk concerns that apply to you. Please choose one or multiple options from the list:
	+ Regulatory Compliance
	+ Cyber Security
	+ Operational Risk
	+ Data Privacy
	+ Financial Risk
	+ Other
* **Risk Maturity**: This field is for you to indicate your current risk maturity level. Please select one of the following options:
	+ Low
	+ Medium
	+ High
* **Objective**: This field is for you to choose the primary objective of your organization. Please select one of the following options:
	+ Identify and mitigate risks
	+ Manage and monitor risks
	+ Assess and evaluate risks
* **Risk Description (optional)**: This field is for you to provide a brief description of the risks you are concerned about.
* **Next Steps**: This field is for you to outline the next steps you would like the consulting service to take.
* **Additional Comments**: This field is for you to add any additional comments or information.
