# Consulting Service Expansion Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about your consulting service expansion request. Please fill out this form thoroughly and accurately to ensure your request is properly reviewed and processed.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your company's information in the **Client Company Name** field.
2. Provide your full name and email address in the **Contact Person** and **Contact Email** fields, respectively.
3. If applicable, enter your current project ID in the **Current Project ID** field.
4. Select one or more types of expansion requested (e.g., Additional Consulting Hours, New Project Phase, etc.) in the **Type of Expansion Requested** field.
5. Enter the proposed start date for the expansion in the **Proposed Expansion Start Date** field.
6. Estimate the additional hours needed per month in the **Estimated Additional Hours Needed** field (if applicable).
7. Provide a brief explanation for your expansion request in the **Primary Reason for Expansion** field.
8. Describe the expected outcomes you hope to achieve with this expansion in the **Expected Outcomes** field.
9. Update the **Budget Approval Status** field to reflect the current status of your budget approval (e.g., Approved, Pending Approval, Not Yet Submitted).
10. Add any additional comments or information in the **Additional Comments** field (if necessary).
11. Finally, confirm the submission date by filling out the **Submission Date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Company Name**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Contact Person**: Provide your full name as the person filling out this form.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Current Project ID**: If you are currently working on a project, enter the ID for that project.
* **Type of Expansion Requested**: Select one or more types of expansion requested, such as Additional Consulting Hours, New Project Phase, etc.
* **Proposed Expansion Start Date**: Enter the date when you would like to start the expansion.
* **Estimated Additional Hours Needed**: If applicable, estimate the number of hours needed for the expansion.
* **Primary Reason for Expansion**: Provide a brief explanation for why this expansion is necessary.
* **Expected Outcomes**: Describe what you hope to achieve with this expansion.
* **Budget Approval Status**: Update the status of your budget approval.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or information about your expansion request.
* **Submission Date**: Confirm the date you submit this form.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* Be as specific as possible when answering the **Primary Reason for Expansion** and **Expected Outcomes** fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please reach out to us.
* Please review this form carefully before submission to avoid any potential issues.
