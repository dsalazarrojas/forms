# Strategy Consulting Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you apply for a strategy consulting fellowship. Please fill out this form to provide us with your application and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and understand each section.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Make sure to provide all required information, as indicated by the "required" status in each field.
4. Review your form before submitting to ensure it is complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Form (id: 1)**: This is the main form where you can provide a brief introduction and overview of your application.
* **Candidate Information (id: 2)**: Please provide your full name, title, and any other relevant details that would help us understand your background and qualifications.
* **Contact Information (id: 3)**: Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number, so we can get in touch with you.
* **Project Information (id: 4)**: If you have any relevant project experience, please describe it briefly.
* **Education (id: 5)**: List your educational background, including degrees earned and institutions attended.
* **Work Experience (id: 6)**: Describe your work experience relevant to the fellowship.
* **Portfolio (id: 7)**: If you have a portfolio of work, please provide a link or attach a relevant document.
* **Additional Information (id: 8)**: If there's anything else you think is important for us to know about you, please include it here.

## Tips
* Make sure to proofread your form carefully before submitting.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out each section.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
