# Consultant Interview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Consultant Interview Form is a crucial tool for the hiring team to assess a candidate's fit and potential for the position. It helps to gather detailed feedback and data on the candidate's performance during the interview process.

## How To Complete This Form

### Section 1: Candidate Identification
1. **Candidate Full Name** (Required): Enter the candidate's full name as they introduced themselves during the interview.
2. **Candidate Email Address** (Required): Enter the candidate's email address as provided by the candidate.
3. **Candidate Phone Number** (Required): Enter the candidate's phone number as provided by the candidate.
4. **Date of Interview** (Required): Enter the date of the interview in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

### Section 2: Interviewer Details
1. **Interviewer's Name and Title** (Required): Enter your name and title as the interviewer.
2. **Interviewing Department** (Required): Select the department that interviewed the candidate.

### Section 3: Skills and Competency Assessment
1. **Analytical and Problem-Solving Skills** (Required): Rate the candidate's analytical and problem-solving skills from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Exceptional).
2. **Written and Verbal Communication Skills** (Required): Rate the candidate's written and verbal communication skills from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Exceptional).
3. **Domain-Specific Subject Matter Expertise** (Required): Rate the candidate's domain-specific subject matter expertise from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Exceptional).
4. **Time Management and Prioritization Skills** (Required): Rate the candidate's time management and prioritization skills from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Exceptional).
5. **Adaptability and Resilience in Fast-Paced Environments** (Required): Rate the candidate's adaptability and resilience in fast-paced environments from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Exceptional).

### Section 4: Qualitative Feedback
1. **What is this candidate's single greatest professional strength?** (Required): Describe the candidate's single greatest professional strength.
2. **What is the primary area for concern/improvement?** (Required): Describe the candidate's primary area for concern or improvement.
3. **Perceived Cultural Fit with the Consultancy** (Required): Rate the candidate's perceived cultural fit with the consultancy from 1 (Poor Fit) to 5 (Excellent Fit).

### Section 5: Hiring Recommendation
1. **Overall Hiring Recommendation** (Required): Select one of the following options:
	* Proceed (High Priority)
	* Proceed (Moderate Priority)
	* Consider (Backup Candidate)
	* Reject (Not a fit)
2. **Primary Reason for Recommendation** (Required): Provide a brief description of the primary reason for your hiring recommendation (max 1000 characters).
3. **Any additional interview comments** (Optional): Provide any additional comments or observations about the candidate.

### Section 6: Date of Interview Feedback Completion
1. **Date of Interview Feedback Completion** (Required): Enter the date you completed the feedback.

Note: All fields marked as "Required" must be completed. The interviewer should answer each question as accurately and thoroughly as possible to ensure the candidate's performance and fit are properly evaluated.
