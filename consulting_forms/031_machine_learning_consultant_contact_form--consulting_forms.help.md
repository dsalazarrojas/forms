# Machine Learning Consultant Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for new project inquiries, allowing potential clients to provide information about their project needs and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all necessary information.
2. Ensure that you are contacting us about a new project, as this form is not intended for follow-up on existing projects.
