# Website Improvement Proposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Website Improvement Proposal Form is used for users to submit proposals for improving the website.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the title of the proposed improvement in the "Website Improvement Proposal" field.
2. Describe the project requirements, including any necessary resources, in the "Project Requirements" field.
3. Specify the project timeline, including start and end dates, in the "Project Timeline" field.
4. Enter the proposed project budget in the "Project Budget" field.
5. List the project resources required for the improvement in the "Project Resources" field.
6. Specify the proposal timeline, including submission and review dates, in the "Proposal Timeline" field.
7. Select the current project status from the "Project Status" dropdown menu.
8. Provide a detailed description of the proposed improvement in the "Project Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Website Improvement Proposal**: Enter a clear and concise title for your proposal, describing the improvement you're suggesting.
* **Project Requirements**: Describe the resources, equipment, or personnel needed for the improvement, if any.
* **Project Timeline**: Enter the start and end dates for the project, including any deadlines or milestones.
* **Project Budget**: Enter the estimated cost for the improvement.
* **Project Resources**: List any necessary equipment, software, or personnel required for the improvement.
* **Proposal Timeline**: Enter the dates for submitting and reviewing the proposal.
* **Project Status**: Select the current status of the improvement proposal from the menu:
	+ **Active**: The proposal is currently being reviewed or implemented.
	+ **Completed**: The proposal has been fully implemented.
	+ **Pending**: The proposal is still in the review and implementation process.
* **Project Description**: Provide a detailed description of the proposed improvement, including any justification or supporting information.

## Tips
* Ensure the proposal is well-structured and clearly written.
* Proofread your proposal for clarity and grammar.
* Check for duplicate proposals before submitting.
* Attach relevant files or images to support your proposal if necessary.

This guide will help you complete the Website Improvement Proposal Form correctly and ensure that your proposals are considered for website improvement.
