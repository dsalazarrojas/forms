# Web Development Consultation Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to book a consultation with a web development team. Please fill out the form to provide the necessary information for your appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To book a consultation, please fill out the form completely. If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Consultation Date** (`consultation_date`, date, required): Enter the date you would like to book the consultation.
* **Consultation Time** (`consultation_time`, time, required): Enter the time you would like to book the consultation.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number where the team can contact you.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): Enter your business name or company name (if applicable).
* **Services** (`services`, select_multiple, optional): Select the services you would like to book (multiple selections allowed).
* **Appointment Type** (`appointment_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of appointment you prefer (In-person or Online).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your consultation.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter your name (optional, used for internal tracking).
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, date, required): This will be automatically filled out by the system.
* **Best Time** (`best_time`, date, optional): This field is not currently used and may be removed.
