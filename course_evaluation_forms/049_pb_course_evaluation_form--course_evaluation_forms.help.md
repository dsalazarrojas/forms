# PB Course Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The PB Course Evaluation Form is a tool used to gather feedback from students on their course experience. This form is used to help instructors and administrators understand the strengths and weaknesses of the course, and to make improvements for future students.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the course you are evaluating in the "Course Name" field.
3. Select the date you completed the course from the calendar in the "Evaluation Date" field.
4. Provide your feedback on the course in the "Feedback" field.
5. Choose the type of evaluation you are submitting (Formative or Summative).
6. Rate your overall experience with the course using the numeric rating scale (1-5).
7. Leave any additional comments you would like to provide about your experience in the "Comment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Course Name (required, text)

* The "Course Name" field is where you enter the name of the course you are evaluating. This helps us understand which course you are providing feedback on.

### Student Name (required, text)

* The "Student Name" field is where you enter your own name, so we can associate your feedback with your identity.

### Evaluation Date (required, date)

* The "Evaluation Date" field is where you select the date you completed the course. This helps us understand when you took the course.

### Feedback (required, text)

* The "Feedback" field is where you provide your thoughts and suggestions on how the course went. This is an open-ended space for you to be as specific as you would like.

### Evaluation Type (required, select_one)

* The "Evaluation Type" field gives us a sense of what type of evaluation you are submitting. This helps us tailor our feedback and improvement efforts to your specific needs.

### Rating (required, number)

* The "Rating" field uses a 1-5 scale to help us measure your satisfaction with the course. A higher number indicates a more positive experience.

### Comment (optional, note)

* The "Comment" field is an optional, free-form text field where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about your experience with the course.

## Tips

* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback. Your input helps us improve the course for future students.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, please include them in the "Comment" field.
* If you have already completed a similar evaluation in the past, please do not submit this form again.
