# digital_marketing_instructor_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from instructors on the effectiveness of a digital marketing course. Instructors will be asked to evaluate the course's quality and provide their opinions on its usefulness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Read the form carefully and answer each question truthfully.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Course Evaluation Form" question.
3. Enter your name in the "instructor_name" field.
4. Enter the name of the course you are evaluating in the "course_name" field.
5. Provide feedback on the course in the "feedback" field.
6. Enter a rating for the course on a scale from 1-10 in the "rating" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
9. Provide any additional comments you have about the course in the "comments" field.
10. Check the "submitted_by" field to indicate who completed the form and check the "submitted_to" field to indicate who the form is being submitted to.
10. Enter the date the form was submitted in the "submitted_date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Evaluation Form (Yes/No)**: This question asks if you, as an instructor, believe the course has been effective. Please select "Yes" if you think the course was effective and "No" otherwise.
* **instructor_name**: This field allows you to enter your name as the instructor who evaluated the course.
* **course_name**: This field is where you enter the name of the course that you are providing feedback on.
* **feedback**: This field allows you to provide any comments or thoughts you have about the course.
* **rating**: This field asks you to rate the course on a scale from 1-10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest rating.
* **email**: This field is where you enter your email address so that we can contact you with any further questions or concerns.
* **phone**: This field is where you enter your phone number so that we can contact you with any further questions or concerns.
* **comments**: This field allows you to provide any additional comments you have about the course.
* **additional_comments**: This field allows you to provide any additional comments you have about the course.
* **submitted_by**: This field allows you to indicate who completed the form, you or someone else.
* **submitted_to**: This field allows you to indicate who the form is being submitted to.
* **submitted_date**: This field is where you enter the date the form was submitted.

## Tips
* Please be honest and truthful in your answers.
* Please provide as much information as you can about the course.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification before submitting the form.
