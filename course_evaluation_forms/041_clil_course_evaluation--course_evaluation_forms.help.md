# Clil Course Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Clil Course Evaluation form is designed to gather feedback on the course's effectiveness and quality. Your responses will help Clil improve its courses and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide your personal details in the **Participant Information** section.
2. Answer the satisfaction and rating questions in the **Satisfaction and Rating** section.
3. Evaluate the course's learning and content in the **Learning and Content** section.
4. Assess the instructor's quality and teaching methods in the **Instruction Quality** section.
5. Rate the course's organization and pacing in the **Course Pacing and Organization** section.
6. Evaluate the materials and resources provided in the **Materials and Resources** section.
7. Consider your engagement and interaction with the course in the **Engagement and Interactivity** section.
8. Add any additional feedback or suggestions in the **Feedback and Improvements** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Information
• **Full Name** (Required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
• **Email Address** (Required): Enter your email address for verification purposes.
• **Organization or School** (Optional): If you're from an educational institution, please provide it.

### Satisfaction and Rating
• **Overall Satisfaction with Course** (Required): Select how satisfied you are with the course. Options are: Very Dissatisfied, Dissatisfied, Neutral, Satisfied, Very Satisfied.
• **Rate the Course - 1 to 10** (Required): Rate the course's quality on a scale of 1 to 10, where 1 is Poor and 10 is Excellent.
• **I Would Recommend This Course to Others** (Required): Select whether you would recommend this course to others. Options are: Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree.

### Learning and Content
• **Course Learning Objectives Were Clear** (Required): Assess how clear the course's learning objectives were. Options are: Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree.
• **Quality of Course Content** (Required): Assess the quality of the course content. Options are: Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, Excellent.
• **Content Was Relevant to Your Needs** (Required): Evaluate how relevant the course content was to your needs. Options are: Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree.
• **You Gained New Knowledge or Skills** (Required): Evaluate whether you gained new knowledge or skills from the course. Options are: Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree.

### Instruction Quality
• **Instructor Quality** (Required): Evaluate the instructor's quality. Options are: Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, Excellent.
• **Instructor Demonstrated Subject Expertise** (Required): Assess whether the instructor demonstrated subject expertise. Options are: Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree.
• **Teaching Methods Were Effective** (Required): Evaluate the teaching methods used. Options are: Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree.
• **Instructor Communicated Effectively** (Required): Assess whether the instructor communicated effectively. Options are: Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree.

### Course Pacing and Organization
• **Course Was Well-Organized** (Required): Evaluate the course's organization. Options are: Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree.
• **Course Pacing Was Appropriate** (Required): Assess whether the course pacing was appropriate. Options are: Too Slow, Slightly Slow, Just Right, Slightly Fast, Too Fast.

### Materials and Resources
• **Quality of Provided Materials** (Required): Evaluate the quality of provided materials. Options are: Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, Excellent.
• **Materials Will Be Useful After Course** (Required): Assess whether the materials will be useful after the course. Options are: Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree.

### Engagement and Interactivity
• **Course Engagement Level** (Required): Evaluate the course's engagement level. Options are: Very Low, Low, Moderate, High, Very High.
• **Opportunities for Participant Interaction** (Required): Assess whether there were opportunities for participant interaction. Options are: Very Limited, Limited, Moderate, Good, Excellent.

### Feedback and Improvements
• **What Was the Best Aspect of This Course?** (Optional): Provide any feedback on the best aspect of the course.
• **What Could Be Improved?** (Optional): Suggest any improvements for future courses.

### Additional Comments
• **Additional Comments or Suggestions** (Optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions.

By completing this form, you're helping Clil improve its courses and services. Your feedback is greatly appreciated!
