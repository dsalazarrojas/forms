# Trial Class Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trial Class Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback from course participants after attending a trial class. It helps us understand their overall experience and provides valuable insights to improve our training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*):
	* Overall Experience (page 1)
	* Instructor Knowledge (page 1)
	* Organization (page 1)
	* Trial Class Structure (page 2)
	* Overall Rating (page 3)
2. If you want to provide additional feedback, you can use the text fields:
	* Feedback (page 4)
	* Suggestions (page 4)
	* Comments (page 5)
3. You can skip the non-required fields if you don't have any additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Experience**: Rate your overall experience with the trial class from 1 to 5.
* **Instructor Knowledge**: Rate the instructor's knowledge and expertise from 1 to 5.
* **Organization**: Select the type of organization that hosted the trial class:
	+ Educational Institution
	+ Training Center
	+ Fitness Studio
* **Trial Class Structure**: Rate the trial class structure and organization from 1 to 5.
* **Overall Rating**: Rate your overall experience with the trial class from 1 to 5.
* **Feedback**: Provide any additional feedback about your experience. Please keep it concise and clear.
* **Suggestions**: Suggest any improvements or changes for future training programs. Please be specific and concrete.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or questions you have. This is an optional field, but your thoughts are valuable to us!
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or information that you think will help us improve our training programs. This is an optional field, but your feedback is crucial.
