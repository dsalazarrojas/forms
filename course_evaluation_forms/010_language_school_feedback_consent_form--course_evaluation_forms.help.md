# Language School Feedback & Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather feedback and consent from students who have attended a language school. The purpose of this form is to collect information about their experiences, satisfaction levels, and consent to use their feedback. This information will help us improve our language school programs and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Fill in your Last Name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Choose whether or not you consent to use your feedback.
5. Share your feedback about your experience with the language school.
6. Rate your overall satisfaction with the school.
7. Add any additional comments you have about your experience.
8. Fill in the name of the language school you attended.
9. Enter the school's email address (if available).
10. Enter the name of your instructor.
11. Enter the name of the course you attended.
12. Enter the start date of the course.
13. Enter the end date of the course.
14. If applicable, fill in the time you spent on the course.
15. If applicable, fill in the day you spent on the course.
16. Select the language you studied.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Consent to Use Feedback** (Consent to Use Feedback, select multiple, required): Choose whether or not you consent to use your feedback. Please select one or both of the options: 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Feedback** (Feedback, text, required): Share your feedback about your experience with the language school.
* **Rating** (Rating, select one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the school. Please select one of the following options: '1', '2', '3', '4', or '5'.
* **Comments** (Comments, text, optional): Add any additional comments about your experience.
* **Language School Name** (Language School Name, text, required): Fill in the name of the language school you attended.
* **School Email** (School Email, email, optional): If available, enter the school's email address.
* **Instructor Name** (Instructor Name, text, required): Enter the name of your instructor.
* **Course Name** (Course Name, text, required): Enter the name of the course you attended.
* **Course Start Date** (Course Start Date, date, required): Enter the start date of the course.
* **Course End Date** (Course End Date, date, required): Enter the end date of the course.
* **Time** (Time, time, optional): If applicable, fill in the time you spent on the course.
* **Day** (Day, date, optional): If applicable, fill in the day you spent on the course.
* **Language** (Language, select multiple, required): Select the language you studied. Choose one or more of the following options: English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, or Mandarin.
