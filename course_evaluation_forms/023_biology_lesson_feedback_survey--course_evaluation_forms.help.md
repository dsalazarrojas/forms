Biology Lesson Feedback Survey - Help Guide
======================================

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students about their experience with a biology lesson. The goal is to gather information on various aspects of the lesson, including overall satisfaction, clarity, pacing, engagement, and understanding of material. The feedback will help instructors evaluate the effectiveness of the lesson and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your student information (name, grade level, etc.) if you feel comfortable sharing it.
2. Answer the questions regarding the lesson you just completed, rating your satisfaction, clarity, and engagement, as well as your understanding of the material.
3. Select the topics you found most helpful for your learning.
4. Indicate what hindered or helped your learning during the lesson.
5. Optionally, provide any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student Information
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required: false): This section is for you to provide your name, if you feel comfortable sharing it.

### Grade Level
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your current grade level (9th, 10th, 11th, 12th, College/University, or Other).

### Lesson Information
* **Lesson Information** (`lesson_information`, `note`, required: false): This section provides a space for you to take a note about the lesson.

### Lesson Topic
* **Lesson Topic** (`lesson_topic`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose the topic that was covered during the lesson (Cell Biology, Genetics, Evolution, Ecology, Human Anatomy, etc.).

### Lesson Date
* **Lesson Date** (`lesson_date`, `date`, required: true): This is the date of the lesson you just completed.

### Overall Satisfaction
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate your overall satisfaction with the lesson (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).

### Lesson Clarity
* **Lesson Clarity** (`lesson_clarity`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate how clear the lesson content was (Very Clear, Clear, Somewhat Clear, Unclear, Very Unclear).

### Lesson Pacing
* **Lesson Pacing** (`pacing`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate the pace of the lesson (Too Slow, Just Right, Too Fast, Very Too Fast).

### Engagement Level
* **Engagement Level** (`engagement_level`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate how engaged you felt during the lesson (Very Engaged, Engaged, Somewhat Engaged, Not Very Engaged, Not Engaged At All).

### Material Understanding
* **Material Understanding** (`material_understanding`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate how well you understand the material (Excellent Understanding, Good Understanding, Fair Understanding, Poor Understanding, No Understanding).

### Teaching Effectiveness
* **Teaching Effectiveness** (`teaching_effectiveness`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate the effectiveness of the teaching methods (Very Effective, Effective, Somewhat Effective, Not Very Effective, Ineffective).

### Visual Aids and Materials
* **Visual Aids and Materials** (`visual_aids`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate how helpful visual aids were (Very Helpful, Helpful, Somewhat Helpful, Not Helpful, No Visual Aids Used).

### Difficulty Level
* **Difficulty Level** (`difficulty_level`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate the difficulty level of the lesson (Too Easy, Easy, Just Right, Difficult, Too Difficult).

### Opportunity for Participation
* **Opportunity for Participation** (`participation_opportunity`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate if you had enough opportunity to participate (Yes - More Than Enough, Yes - Adequate, Neutral, No - Not Enough, No - None At All).

### What Helped Your Learning Most
* **What Helped Your Learning Most** (`what_helped_learning`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select all the factors that helped your learning (Lecture Explanation, Visual Diagrams, Hands-on Activities, Group Discussions, Practice Problems, Videos or Animations, Textbook Readings, Nothing Helped).

### What Hindered Your Learning
* **What Hindered Your Learning** (`what_hindered_learning`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select all the factors that hindered your learning (Nothing - Everything Was Good, Too Much Information, Too Fast Paced, Too Slow Paced, Unclear Explanations, Distractions, Lack of Examples, Technical Issues).

### Topics Needing More Review
* **Topics Needing More Review** (`topics_needing_review`, `text`, required: false): If there are any topics that need more explanation, provide them here.

### Suggestions for Improvement
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, `text`, required: false): Any other feedback or suggestions for improvement.

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): Any other comments or feedback.

### Would You Recommend This Lesson Format
* **Would You Recommend This Lesson Format** (`would_recommend`, `select_one`, required: true): Would you recommend this lesson format to other students (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Not Sure, Probably No, Definitely No).

### Interest in Biology After This Lesson
* **Interest in Biology After This Lesson** (`interest_in_subject`, `select_one`, required: true): Has your interest in biology increased, decreased, or remained the same after this lesson (Increased Significantly, Increased Slightly, No Change, Decreased Slightly, Decreased Significantly).
