# College Course Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from students about their college course experiences. Your input will help instructors and administrators improve the course and provide a better learning experience for students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name, student ID (if applicable), and course details.
2. Answer the questions about the course content, organization, and instructor effectiveness.
3. Choose the ratings for each question, such as Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
4. Select multiple skills you gained from the course in the last question.
5. Provide any additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your name, or simply leave it blank if you don't want to reveal your identity.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, optional): If you have an ID, please enter it. Otherwise, you can leave it blank.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the course you are providing feedback on.
* **Course Code** (`course_code`, text, required): Enter the course code or number.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your instructor.
* **Academic Term** (`term`, select_one, required): Choose the academic term when you took the course (Fall 2025, Spring 2026, etc.).
* **Feedback Date** (`feedback_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting this feedback.
* **Course Content Quality** (`content_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the course content (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Content Organization** (`content_organization`, select_one, required): Rate how well the course content is organized (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Content Relevance** (`content_relevance`, select_one, required): Rate how relevant the course content is to your field (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Instructor Effectiveness** (`instructor_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Rate your instructor's effectiveness (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Instructor Clarity** (`instructor_clarity`, select_one, required): Rate how clear your instructor was (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Instructor Engagement** (`instructor_engagement`, select_one, required): Rate how engaging your instructor was (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Instructor Feedback Quality** (`instructor_feedback`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of your instructor's feedback (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Assignment Quality** (`assignments_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the assignments (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Assignment Difficulty** (`assignments_difficulty`, select_one, required): Rate how difficult the assignments were (Too Easy, Appropriate, Too Difficult).
* **Course Workload** (`workload`, select_one, required): Rate the amount of work required (Too Light, Appropriate, Too Heavy).
* **Engagement Level** (`engagement_level`, select_one, required): Rate how engaged you were in the course (Very Engaged, Engaged, Somewhat Engaged, Not Very Engaged, Not Engaged).
* **Learning Outcomes Achieved** (`learning_outcomes`, select_one, required): Rate whether you met your learning goals (Exceeded Expectations, Met Expectations, Partially Met Expectations, Did Not Meet Expectations).
* **Skills Gained** (`skills_gained`, select_multiple, required): Select multiple skills you gained from the course (Critical Thinking, Problem Solving, Communication, Research Skills, Technical Skills, Teamwork, Time Management, Subject Knowledge, Other).
* **Course Pace** (`course_pace`, select_one, required): Rate the speed of instruction (Too Slow, Appropriate, Too Fast).
* **Materials Quality** (`materials_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of course materials (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Assessment Fairness** (`assessment_fairness`, select_one, required): Rate the fairness of assessments (Very Fair, Fair, Somewhat Fair, Not Fair).
* **Would Recommend Course** (`would_recommend_course`, select_one, required): Rate whether you would recommend the course to other students (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Not Sure, Probably No, Definitely No).
* **Best Aspect of the Course** (`best_aspect`, text, required): Describe the best aspect of the course.
* **Worst Aspect of the Course** (`worst_aspect`, text, required): Describe the worst aspect of the course.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions for improvement.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any other feedback.

## Tips
Please answer each question honestly, and provide as much detail as possible. Your feedback is anonymous, so don't worry about sharing your thoughts freely.
