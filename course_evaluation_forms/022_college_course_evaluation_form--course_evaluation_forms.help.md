# College Course Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The College Course Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from students on their experiences in a college course. The form covers various aspects of the course, including organization, instruction, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Course Code or Number** in the corresponding field.
2. Enter the **Course Title** in the field provided.
3. Select the **Semester or Term** in which the course was taken (e.g., Spring, Fall, Summer, etc.).
4. Enter the **Academic Year** in which the course was taken.
5. Enter the **Instructor Name** in the field provided.
6. Enter your **Student ID** (optional).
7. Select your **Expected Grade in This Course** from the provided options.
8. Select the **Overall Course Difficulty** from the provided options.
9. Select the **Workload Assessment** from the provided options.
10. Select the **Course Organization and Structure** from the provided options.
11. Select the **Clarity of Learning Objectives** from the provided options.
12. Select the **Relevance of Course Material** from the provided options.
13. Select the **Instructor Teaching Effectiveness** from the provided options.
14. Select the **Instructor Accessibility and Availability** from the provided options.
15. Select the **Quality of Feedback on Assignments** from the provided options.
16. Select the **Quality and Relevance of Assignments** from the provided options.
17. Select the **Fairness of Exams and Assessments** from the provided options.
18. Select whether you would **Would you recommend this course** from the provided options.
19. Select whether you would **Would you take another course with this instructor** from the provided options.
20. Describe the **Strengths of the Course** in the space provided.
21. Describe the **Areas for Course Improvement** in the space provided.
22. Enter any **Additional Comments** you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Code or Number**: Enter the official code or number assigned to the course.
* **Course Title**: Enter the title of the course as it appears on your transcript or syllabus.
* **Semester or Term**: Select the semester or term in which the course was taken (e.g., Spring, Fall, Summer, etc.).
* **Academic Year**: Enter the academic year in which the course was taken.
* **Instructor Name**: Enter the name of the instructor who taught the course.
* **Student ID**: Enter your student ID number, if you wish to provide feedback without your name. If left blank, your feedback will be attributed to "Anonymous."
* **Expected Grade in This Course**: Select the grade you expect to receive in the course from the provided options.
* **Overall Course Difficulty**: Select the overall difficulty of the course from the provided options.
* **Workload Assessment**: Select the workload of the course from the provided options.
* **Course Organization and Structure**: Select how well-organized the course was from the provided options.
* **Clarity of Learning Objectives**: Select how clear the course objectives were from the provided options.
* **Relevance of Course Material**: Select how relevant the course material was to your learning from the provided options.
* **Instructor Teaching Effectiveness**: Select the effectiveness of the instructor in delivering the course from the provided options.
* **Instructor Accessibility and Availability**: Select how accessible and available the instructor was from the provided options.
* **Quality of Feedback on Assignments**: Select the quality of feedback on assignments from the provided options.
* **Quality and Relevance of Assignments**: Select the quality and relevance of assignments from the provided options.
* **Fairness of Exams and Assessments**: Select the fairness of exams and assessments from the provided options.
* **Would you recommend this course**: Select whether you would recommend this course to others from the provided options.
* **Would you take another course with this instructor**: Select whether you would take another course with this instructor from the provided options.
* **Strengths of the Course**: Describe the strengths of the course in the space provided.
* **Areas for Course Improvement**: Describe the areas for improvement in the course in the space provided.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback you may have about the course.
