# Financial Literacy Course Discharge Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your overall satisfaction with the financial literacy course you recently completed. Your feedback will help us improve our future courses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select your answer for each question, based on your experience with the course.
2. If you have comments or suggestions for us, please provide them in the "Comments and Suggestions" field.
3. Click the "Submit" button to send us your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Course Satisfaction
**Course Satisfaction** (`Very Satisfied`, `Somewhat Satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Somewhat Dissatisfied`, `Very Dissatisfied`):
Indicate your overall satisfaction with the course, based on what you experienced.

### Instructor Performance
**Instructor Performance** (`Excellent`, `Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`):
Rate the performance of the instructor, considering their teaching style, communication, and ability to answer your questions.

### Instruction Quality
**Instruction Quality** (`Very Good`, `Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`, `Very Poor`):
Evaluate the quality of the instruction provided, considering the relevance and effectiveness of the course materials.

### Course Materials
**Course Materials** (`Very Good`, `Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`):
Rate the quality and usefulness of the course materials, including any resources provided.

### Course Duration
**Course Duration** (date):
Enter the date you completed the course, as this will help us keep track of course completion.

### Course Facilitator
**Course Facilitator** (`Very Good`, `Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`):
Rate the facilitator's performance, considering their communication style and ability to manage the course.

### Comments and Suggestions
**Comments and Suggestions**:
Leave any comments or suggestions you may have about the course, including any areas for improvement.

### Financial Literacy Outcome
**Financial Literacy Outcome** (`Improved`, `No Change`, `Declined`):
Indicate if you experienced any financial literacy as a result of the course, or if you did not experience any change.
