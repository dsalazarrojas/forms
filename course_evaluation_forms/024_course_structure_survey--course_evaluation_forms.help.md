# Course Structure Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Structure Survey is designed to gather information from instructors about the structure of a course. This form helps us understand how the course is organized, its content, and how it can be improved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Be as detailed and accurate as possible when providing information.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "additional_comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **what_is_your_role**: \* This field is for you to describe your role in the course (e.g., instructor, TA, course designer).
* **instructor_feedback**: \* This field is for you to provide feedback about the course's structure, organization, and content.
* **course_administration**: \* This field is for you to describe how the course is administered (e.g., online, in-person, hybrid).
* **section_organizers**: \* This field is for you to list or describe the section organizers (e.g., team leads, subject matter experts).
* **course_content**: \* This field is for you to describe the course content, including any specific topics or subjects covered.
* **assessment_and_evaluation**: \* This field is for you to describe how the course content is assessed and evaluated.
* **course_duration**: \* This field is for you to describe the course duration (e.g., semester, quarter, year).
* **course_format**: \* This field is for you to describe the course format (e.g., online, in-person, hybrid).
* **course_structure**: \* This field is for you to describe the course structure, including any specific sections or modules.
* **course_outcomes**: \* This field is for you to describe the course outcomes (e.g., skills, knowledge, attitudes gained).
* **additional_comments**: \* This field is for you to provide any additional comments or feedback about the course.
* **course_evaluation_form_submission**: \* This field is for you to indicate the submission of the course evaluation form.
* **email**: \* This field is for you to provide your email address.
* **phone**: \* This field is for you to provide your phone number.
* **submission_date**: \* This field is for you to provide the date of submission.
* **submission_time**: \* This field is for you to provide the time of submission.
* **note**: \* This field is for you to provide any additional notes.
* **select_one**: \* This field is for you to select one option from the list.
* **select_multiple**: \* This field is for you to select multiple options from the list.
* **date**: \* This field is for you to provide a date.
* **time**: \* This field is for you to provide a time.
* **email2**: \* This field is for you to provide an email address (second instance).
* **phone2**: \* This field is for you to provide a phone number (second instance).
* **note2**: \* This field is for you to provide any additional notes (second instance).
* **select_one2**: \* This field is for you to select one option from the second list.
* **email**: \* This field is for you to provide your email address.
* **phone**: \* This field is for you to provide your phone number.
* **submission_date**: \* This field is for you to provide the date of submission.
* **submission_time**: \* This field is for you to provide the time of submission.
* **note**: \* This field is for you to provide any additional notes.

## Tips
- Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out the form.
- If you're unsure about any field or its purpose, please refer to the field-by-field explanation above.
- If you have any additional questions or concerns, please contact us.
