# Intermediate Course Delegate Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Intermediate Course Delegate Feedback Form is a tool for delegates to provide their opinions on the intermediate course they have attended. Your feedback will help course providers improve the course content and presentation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and honestly to provide the most valuable feedback to course providers.
2. Please ensure that you have attended the intermediate course before submitting feedback.
3. If you have already submitted feedback, please do not resubmit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Course Provider** (Optional)
This field is a text box where you can provide any additional information about your course provider that you think is relevant to your feedback.

### 2. **Feedback on Materials** (Optional)
This field is a multiple-choice question with the following options:
* Option 1
* Option 2
* Option 3

Please select all that apply to provide feedback on the course materials.

### 3. **Course Structure** (Optional)
This field is a date field where you can provide the date of the course you attended.

### 4. **Course Presentation** (Optional)
This field is a time field where you can provide the time of day you attended the course.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* If you are unsure about the format or any other aspect of the form, please contact the course provider for clarification.
* Your feedback is valuable and will help course providers improve the course. Please take your time to provide thorough and honest feedback.
