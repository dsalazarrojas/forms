# Language Course Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and evaluation data on language courses provided to students. Your input is crucial in helping us refine and improve our language courses.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your answer for each field carefully and honestly.
2. If a field is not applicable or not relevant, select "No" or leave it blank.
3. Provide clear and concise answers for text fields (e.g., Teacher, Materials, Outcomes, Comments).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Language** (`Language`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the language of the course you attended from the provided options (Yes, No). This field is not required, so you can leave it blank if the course was not in the language you're interested in.
- **Teacher** (`Teacher`, `text`, required: false): Describe your language teacher's performance, strengths, and weaknesses.
- **Level** (`Level`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the level of the course (High, Low) based on your experience and satisfaction.
- **Start Date** (`Start Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date of the course in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
- **End Date** (`End Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date of the course in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
- **Duration** (`Duration`, `number`, required: false): Enter the duration of the course in days or weeks, e.g., 10 weeks.
- **Language Instructors** (`Language Instructors`, `text`, required: false): List any other language instructors that taught the course with you.
- **Number of Students** (`Number of Students`, `text`, required: false): Provide the number of students that attended the course with you.
- **Location** (`Location`, `text`, required: false): Describe the location of the course, e.g., online, classroom, etc.
- **Materials** (`Materials`, `text`, required: false): List any materials provided during the course, e.g., textbooks, online resources, etc.
- **Outcomes** (`Outcomes`, `text`, required: false): Describe the outcomes or results you achieved through the course, e.g., improved language skills, new knowledge, etc.
- **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or suggestions about the course.
- **Form Completed** (`Form Completed`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate whether you completed the form (Yes, No).
- **Completed** (`Completed`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Confirm whether the course was completed (Yes, No).
