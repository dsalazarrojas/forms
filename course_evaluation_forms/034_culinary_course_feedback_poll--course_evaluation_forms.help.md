# Culinary Course Feedback Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Culinary Course Feedback Poll is a form designed to collect feedback from students who have completed a culinary course. It helps us understand the course's quality and effectiveness, providing valuable insights to improve the course and its delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the course title as you remember it from the course.
2. Enter the date you completed the course.
3. Rate the course content, instructor, and facilities as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
4. Provide any suggestions for improvement in the text box.
5. Add any comments about your experience with the course.
6. Rate your overall satisfaction with the course on a scale of 1-5.
7. Enter your email address and phone number so we can contact you for further feedback.
8. Provide your name for reference.
9. Select the type of course you attended (In-class, Online, or Hybrid).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Title** (`course_title`, text, optional): Please enter the title of the course as you remember it.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, optional): Enter the date you completed the course.
* **Feedback Score** (`feedback_score`, number, optional): This score is an optional way to rate your overall satisfaction with the course. If you choose not to use it, you can simply skip it.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Please provide any suggestions you have for improving the course.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Share any comments or thoughts you have about your experience with the course.
* **Rating of Course Content** (`rating_course_content`, select_one, optional): Rate the course content as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Rating of Instructor** (`rating_instructor`, select_one, optional): Rate the instructor as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Rating of Facilities** (`rating_facilities`, select_multiple, optional): Rate the facilities as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor (you can select multiple options).
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the course on a scale of 1-5.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further feedback.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Provide your name for reference.
* **Feedback Type** (`feedback_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of course you attended (In-class, Online, or Hybrid).
