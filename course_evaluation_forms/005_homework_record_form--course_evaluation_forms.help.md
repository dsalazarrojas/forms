# homework_record_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record student homework submissions, enabling educators to track student progress and assignments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these general steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary information about the student and assignment.
2. Select the relevant pages corresponding to the student and assignment you're tracking.
3. Fill in the required and optional fields as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID** (1): Enter the student's identification number.
* **Student Name** (2): Enter the student's name.
* **Assignment ID** (3): Enter the assignment number or identifier.
* **Completed Tasks** (4): Indicate whether the student has completed the tasks assigned for this homework.
  - Select 'Yes' if the student has finished all tasks.
  - Select 'No' otherwise.
* **Due Date** (5): Enter the due date for this assignment submission.
* **Submission Status** (6): Indicate the current submission status.
  - 'Active' if the submission is active and being reviewed.
  - 'Inactive' if the submission is inactive or incomplete.
* **Comments** (7): Enter any comments or feedback you have for the student.
* **Grade** (8): Enter the grade for this assignment submission.
* **Teacher Comments** (9): Enter any comments or feedback from the teacher to the student.
* **Submission Date** (10): Enter the date the submission was received from the student.

## Tips
- Double-check the entered information for accuracy before submitting.
- Ensure you've filled in all required fields to avoid errors.
- If you're unsure about any field, consult your teacher or instructor for guidance.
