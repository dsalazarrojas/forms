# DIY Lease Course Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students who have taken a DIY lease course. Your responses will help us understand the course's effectiveness, structure, and overall satisfaction, which will aid us in making improvements for future course offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Enter your overall satisfaction with the course in the "overall satisfaction" field.
2.  Select your answer to "course structure" by choosing one or more options from the list.
3.  Rate your experience with the course by selecting one of the rating options.
4.  Provide any comments or feedback you may have about the course in the "comments" field.
5.  Enter the name of the course and the instructor's name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### course name
*   **course name**: Enter the name of the course you are providing feedback on.

### rating
*   **rating**: Rate your experience with the course using one of the following options:
    *   Very Dissatisfied
    *   Somewhat Dissatisfied
    *   Neutral
    *   Somewhat Satisfied
    *   Very Satisfied

### overall satisfaction
*   **overall satisfaction**: Enter your overall satisfaction with the course.

### comments
*   **comments**: Provide any comments or feedback you may have about the course.

### course structure
*   **course structure**: Select one or more of the following options to describe the course structure:
    *   Yes
    *   No

### instructor name
*   **instructor name**: Enter the name of the instructor who taught the course.

### feedback
*   **feedback**: Provide any additional feedback or suggestions you may have about the course.
