# Course Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Evaluation form is used to collect feedback from students or learners about their experiences with a course. This feedback is essential for the course creators to understand what works and what doesn't, helping them improve the course content and quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the course you are evaluating.
2. Select the date you took the course or when you are doing the evaluation.
3. Provide a score out of a certain number (we recommend using the scale provided below).
4. Choose a rating based on your experience (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
5. Add any comments or observations you have about the course.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the course you are evaluating.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required): Select the date you took the course or when you are doing the evaluation. This date will help us track the feedback over time.
* **Score** (`score`, number, required): Provide a score out of a certain number (please use the provided scale below) to rate your overall experience with the course.
	+ 1: Very Poor
	+ 2: Poor
	+ 3: Fair
	+ 4: Good
	+ 5: Excellent
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, required): Choose a rating based on your experience with the course (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any comments or observations you have about the course. This is your chance to share any feedback or suggestions that you think will be helpful for the course creators.
