# Master Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Master Program Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from students on the overall quality and effectiveness of the academic programs they're enrolled in. This form helps program administrators and staff to identify areas of improvement and make necessary adjustments to enhance the learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you're answering questions relevant to your experience with the program.
2. Complete each field as accurately and honestly as possible.
3. Take care to answer each question with the correct type of response (e.g., select 'Yes' or 'No' for binary questions, choose a number for satisfaction level questions, and write a text for comments).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Course Title** (`course_title`, text, optional)
* This field is where you enter the title of the course or program you're evaluating.
* Enter the exact title of the course, as it appears on your transcript or course materials.

*   **Overall Faculty Performance** (`overall_faculty_performance`, select_multiple, optional)
    * This field asks you to evaluate the performance of your instructors, which may include teaching, engagement, and availability.
    * Select all the options that apply to your experience.

*   **Accessing Information** (`accessing_information`, select_one, optional)
    * This question assesses how easy or difficult it was for you to access information related to the course.
    * Choose the option that best describes your experience.

*   **Satisfaction Level Accessing Information** (`satisfaction_level_accessing_info`, number, optional)
    * This field is meant for you to provide a numerical value (1-5) representing your level of satisfaction with accessing information for the course.
    * A higher number indicates greater satisfaction.

*   **Overall Clarity Policies** (`overall_clarity_policies`, select_multiple, optional)
    * This field asks you to evaluate how clear and transparent the program's policies were to you.
    * Select all options that apply.

*   **Satisfaction Level Clarity Policies** (`satisfaction_level_clarity_policies`, number, optional)
    * This question gauges your satisfaction with the clarity of policies.
    * Use a number between 1-5 to answer this question.

*   **Overall Faculty Issues** (`overall_faculty_issues`, select_multiple, optional)
    * This question inquires about any difficulties or issues you've encountered with the faculty.
    * Select all that apply.

*   **Satisfaction Level Faculty Issues** (`satisfaction_level_faculty_issues`, number, optional)
    * This field is meant to measure your satisfaction with faculty performance.
    * Choose a number between 1-5 to answer this question.

*   **Overall Academic Support** (`overall_academic_support`, select_multiple, optional)
    * This field inquires about your experience with the support services available within the program.
    * Select all options that apply.

*   **Satisfaction Level Academic Support** (`satisfaction_level_academic_support`, number, optional)
    * This question is meant for you to evaluate your satisfaction with the level of academic support.
    * Choose a number between 1-5.

*   **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional)
    * This field is where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about the program.
    * Please keep your comments concise and relevant to your experience.
