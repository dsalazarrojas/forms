# University Distance Learning Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Distance Learning Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback from students on their online learning experience. This feedback will help the university improve the quality and effectiveness of its online courses, support services, and overall learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the title of the survey.
2. Answer the questions about your online learning experience, choosing the best option that describes your experience.
3. Rate the quality of online support services and courses.
4. Finally, add any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title (Survey Title)** (`survey_title`, text, required): Please enter a title to help us understand which part of the online courses you are providing feedback on.
* **How often do you log in to the online learning platform?** (`survey_questions`, number): Choose how often you log in to the online platform.
* **How often do you feel supported by instructors and staff?** (`communication_support`, select_one): Choose how often you feel supported by instructors and staff.
* **Technical issues while accessing online course materials** (`technical_issues`, select_multiple): Choose any technical issues you have encountered while accessing online course materials.
* **How often do you use online support services?** (`support_services`, select_one): Choose how often you use online support services (e.g., email, phone, chat).
* **How often do you give feedback on online courses?** (`feedback_frequency`, select_one): Choose how often you give feedback on online courses.
* **How would you rate the quality of your online courses?** (`course_quality`, select_one): Choose how you rate the quality of your online courses.
* **How often do you feel you have received adequate help from instructors and staff?** (`learning_support`, select_multiple): Choose how often you feel you have received adequate help from instructors and staff.
* **How often do you rate the quality of online support services?** (`support_services_quality`, select_one): Choose how often you rate the quality of online support services.
* **What is your overall experience with the online courses?** (`overall_experience`, select_one): Choose your overall experience with online courses.
* **Do you have any additional comments regarding your online course experience?** (`additional_comments`, note): Share any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Choose the best option that describes your experience for each question.
* If you have any additional feedback or comments, please share them in the box at the end of the form.
