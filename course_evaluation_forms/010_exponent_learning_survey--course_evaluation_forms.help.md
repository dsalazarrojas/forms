# Exponent Learning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exponent Learning Survey is a tool used to gather feedback and opinions from participants in a course or program offered by Exponent Learning. The goal is to evaluate the participant's experience and suggest areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Participant Name** in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Enter your **Email** address if you're willing to be contacted by Exponent Learning.
3. Rate your **Overall Satisfaction** from 1 to 5, where 1 is Very Dissatisfied and 5 is Extremely Satisfied.
4. Rate the **Learning Experience** from 1 to 5, where 1 is Not Satisfied and 5 is Extremely Satisfied.
5. Rate the **Program Content** from 1 to 5, where 1 is Not Satisfied and 5 is Extremely Satisfied.
6. Rate the **Materials/Resources** from 1 to 5, where 1 is Not Satisfied and 5 is Extremely Satisfied.
7. Rate the **Facilitator/Trainer** from 1 to 5, where 1 is Not Satisfied and 5 is Extremely Satisfied.
8. Provide any additional **Comments** about your experience.
9. Offer **Improvement Suggestions** for future programs.
10. Provide your **Contact Information**, including a phone number or email address, so that Exponent Learning can follow up with you.
11. Select whether you **Agree** to be contacted by Exponent Learning.
12. If you agree to be contacted via email, enter your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (1, text, Required): Enter your name as a participant in the program.
* **Email** (2, email, Optional): Enter your email address so that Exponent Learning can contact you.
* **Course Name** (3, text, Optional): Enter the name of the course you participated in.
* **Program Rating** (4, number, Optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the program, from 1 to 5.
* **Feedback and Suggestions** (5, text, Optional): Provide any additional feedback or suggestions you have about the program.
* **Ratings 1 - 5** (6-10, select_one, Optional): Rate your satisfaction with different aspects of the program, from 1 to 5.
* **Comments** (11, text, Optional): Provide any additional comments about your experience.
* **Improvement Suggestions** (12, text, Optional): Suggest areas for improvement in future programs.
* **Contact Info** (13, text, Optional): Enter a phone number or email address so that Exponent Learning can follow up with you.
* **Submission Date** (14, date, Optional): The date you're submitting this form.
* **Submission Time** (15, time, Optional): The time you're submitting this form.
* **Submission Location** (16, text, Optional): Enter the location where you're submitting this form.
* **Participant Agreement** (17, select_one, Optional): Select whether you agree to be contacted by Exponent Learning.
* **Email Agreement** (18, select_one, Optional): Select whether you agree to be contacted via email.
* **Email** (19, email, Optional): Enter your email address if you agree to be contacted via email.
* **Participant Email** (20, email, Optional): Enter your email address for Exponent Learning to contact you.
