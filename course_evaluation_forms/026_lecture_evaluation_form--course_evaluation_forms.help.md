# lecture_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lecture Evaluation Form is a tool designed to gather feedback on the effectiveness of a lecture. It helps identify areas of improvement and provides insights for future lectures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Course Name** in the text field provided.
2. Enter the **Instructor Name** in the text field provided.
3. Select the **Date of Lecture** using the date picker.
4. Enter the **Start Time** in the format HH:MM:SS.
5. Enter the **Lecture Length** in the format of the total duration of the lecture.
6. Enter a brief **Course Rating**, describing your thoughts on the overall quality of the course content.
7. Enter a brief **Instructor Rating**, describing your thoughts on the overall quality of the instructor.
8. Enter a brief **Content Organization**, describing your thoughts on the organization of the course materials.
9. Enter a brief **Engagement Level**, describing your level of engagement during the lecture.
10. Enter a brief **Overall Experience**, describing your overall experience with the course.
11. Provide any additional **Comments** you have about the lecture.
12. Select **Relevant Courses** that you found the lecture to be relevant for.
13. Select **Most Relevant Course** from the options.
14. Select **Relevant Topics** that you found the lecture to be relevant for.
15. Select **Most Relevant Topic** from the options.
16. Select **Relevant Skills** that you found the lecture to be relevant for.
17. Select **Most Relevant Skill** from the options.
18. Select **Relevant Resources** that you found the lecture to be relevant for.
19. Select **Most Relevant Resource** from the options.
20. Select **Relevant Challenges** that you found the lecture to be relevant for.
21. Select **Most Relevant Challenge** from the options.
22. Select **Relevant Solutions** that you found the lecture to be relevant for.
23. Select **Most Relevant Solution** from the options.
24. Select **Relevant Next Steps** that you found the lecture to be relevant for.
25. Select **Most Relevant Next Step** from the options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the course.
* **Instructor Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the instructor.
* **Date of Lecture (Date)**: Select the date of the lecture using the date picker.
* **Start Time (Time)**: Enter the start time of the lecture in the format HH:MM:SS.
* **Lecture Length (Number)**: Enter the total duration of the lecture.
* **Course Rating (Text)**: Describe your overall impression of the course.
* **Instructor Rating (Text)**: Describe your overall impression of the instructor.
* **Content Organization (Text)**: Describe your thoughts on the organization of the course materials.
* **Engagement Level (Text)**: Describe your level of engagement during the lecture.
* **Overall Experience (Text)**: Describe your overall experience with the course.
* **Comments (Note)**: Enter any additional comments about the lecture.
* **Relevant Courses (Select Multiple)**: Select all relevant courses related to this lecture.
* **Most Relevant Course (Select One)**: Select the course that you think is most relevant for this lecture.
* **Relevant Topics (Select Multiple)**: Select all relevant topics related to this lecture.
* **Most Relevant Topic (Select One)**: Select the topic that you think is most relevant for this lecture.
* **Relevant Skills (Select Multiple)**: Select all relevant skills learned from this lecture.
* **Most Relevant Skill (Select One)**: Select the skill that you think is most relevant for this lecture.
* **Relevant Resources (Select Multiple)**: Select all relevant resources you used or would use for this lecture.
* **Most Relevant Resource (Select One)**: Select the resource that you think is most relevant for this lecture.
* **Relevant Challenges (Select Multiple)**: Select all relevant challenges you faced during the lecture.
* **Most Relevant Challenge (Select One)**: Select the challenge that you think is most relevant for this lecture.
* **Relevant Solutions (Select Multiple)**: Select all relevant solutions you found or would find during this lecture.
* **Most Relevant Solution (Select One)**: Select the solution that you think is most relevant for this lecture.
* **Relevant Next Steps (Select Multiple)**: Select all relevant next steps you would take based on this lecture.
* **Most Relevant Next Step (Select One)**: Select the next step that you think is most relevant for this lecture.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete the form as soon as possible after the lecture.
* Take your time to think about each question before answering.
* Use specific examples or anecdotes to support your answers.
* Keep your answers brief but descriptive.
* Review your answers before submitting the form.
* Do not repeat answers that you have already submitted for the same lecture.
