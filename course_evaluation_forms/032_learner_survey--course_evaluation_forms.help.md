# Learner Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Learner Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from learners about their experience with a course. The goal is to gather insights on how to improve the course content, instruction, and overall learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page carefully and honestly.
2. Select the options that best match your experience and opinions.
3. Make sure to fill out all the required fields (indicated by an asterisk *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How do you rate your overall satisfaction with this course** (`select_one`)
	+ This question asks for your overall rating of the course, from Excellent to Poor.
* **What were the primary learning objectives of the course** (`select_multiple`)
	+ Choose the options that best describe the primary learning objectives of the course.
* **How would you rate the instructor** (`select_one`)
	+ Rate the instructor from Excellent to Poor based on your experience.
* **What do you think of the course content** (`select_multiple`)
	+ Choose the options that best describe your opinion on the course content.
* **How would you rate the instructor** (`select_one`)
	+ Rate the instructor from Excellent to Poor based on your experience.
* **How would you rate the course overall** (`select_one`)
	+ Rate the course from Excellent to Poor based on your overall experience.
* **What is your contact information** (`email`)
	+ Enter your email address to allow us to follow up with you about your feedback.
* **Do you have any suggestions** (`text`)
	+ Share any suggestions or ideas you have for improving the course.
* **Do you want to sign up for more training** (`select_one`)
	+ Indicate if you are interested in signing up for more training opportunities.
