# Heavy Vehicle Rescue Course Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students, instructors, and course administrators on the Heavy Vehicle Rescue Course. Your input will help us improve the course content, teaching methods, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Rate the Course
1. Select one or more of the following options to rate the course:
	* Good
	* Fair
* Step 2: Express Overall Satisfaction
1. Choose one of the following options to express your overall satisfaction with the course:
	* Satisfied
	* Dissatisfied
* Step 3: Provide Instructor Information
1. Enter the name of the instructor who led the course.
* Step 4: Rate the Instructor
1. Enter a numerical value between 1 and 5 to rate the instructor's performance.
* Step 5: Rate the Course
1. Enter a numerical value between 1 and 5 to rate the course itself.
* Step 6: Add Improvement Notes and Comments
1. If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, enter them in the text area.
2. Add any additional comments about your experience with the course.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Ratings** (required): 
	+ Choose one or more of the following options to rate the course: Good, Fair
* **Overall Satisfaction** (required): 
	+ Choose one of the following options to express your overall satisfaction with the course: Satisfied, Dissatisfied
* **Instructor Name** (required): 
	+ Enter the name of the instructor who led the course.
* **Instructor Rating** (required): 
	+ Enter a numerical value between 1 and 5 to rate the instructor's performance.
* **Course Rating** (required): 
	+ Enter a numerical value between 1 and 5 to rate the course itself.
* **Improvement Notes** (optional): 
	+ If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, enter them in the text area.
* **Comments** (optional): 
	+ Add any additional comments about your experience with the course.
