# Geology Course Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to collect feedback from students about a geology course. The feedback gathered through this form will help the instructor assess the course's effectiveness and make improvements for future students.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the required questions, marked with an asterisk (\*). The required fields are:
	* Instructor Rating
	* Overall Rating
2. Choose the relevant options for the non-required fields, such as Course Quality and Department Feedback.
3. Provide detailed answers for the text fields, such as Evaluation and Student Suggestion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Instructor Rating (<name>: Instructor Rating, <type>: number, required: true)**: Rate the instructor's performance, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Course Quality (<name>: Course Quality, <type>: select_one, required: false)**: Choose how satisfactory you found the course quality, with options for Highly Satisfactory, Satisfactory, and Poor.
* **Course Satisfaction (<name>: Course Satisfaction, <type>: number, required: false)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the course, with 1-5 as the scale.
* **Overall Rating (<name>: Overall Rating, <type>: select_multiple, required: true)**: Select the relevant ratings for your overall experience, with options 1-5.
* **Suggestion (<name>: Suggestion, <type>: note, required: false)**: Provide any suggestions or comments about the course.
* **Evaluation (<name>: Evaluation, <type>: text, required: false)**: Share your evaluation of the course, providing detailed feedback.
* **Rating Scale (<name>: rating_scale, <type>: select_one, required: false)**: Choose the rating scale you used for your Overall Rating, with options for 1-5, 4-5, 3-5, 2-5, and 1-4.
* **Department Feedback (<name>: Department Feedback, <type>: select_multiple, required: false)**: Select the department that the course belongs to, with options for geology and other.
* **Instructor Feedback (<name>: Instructor Feedback, <type>: select_one, required: false)**: Choose how you found the instructor, with options for Highly Recommended, Not Recommended, and Not Applicable.
* **Student Suggestion (<name>: Student Suggestion, <type>: text, required: false)**: Provide any suggestions or comments from a student's perspective.
* **Course Suggestion (<name>: Course Suggestion, <type>: text, required: false)**: Share any suggestions or comments about the course.
* **Additional Course Suggestions (<name>: Additional Course Suggestions, <type>: note, required: false)**: Provide any additional suggestions or comments about the course.

Note: Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly, as your feedback is crucial to help the instructor improve the course.
