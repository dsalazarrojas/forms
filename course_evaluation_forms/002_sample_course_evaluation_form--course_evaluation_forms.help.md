# Sample Course Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from students on their experience with our courses. This form will help us understand what we are doing well and what we can improve on to provide better services to our students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields.
3. Review each page before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Course Experience**: This question asks for your overall opinion of the course. Please choose the number that best represents your experience.
	+ 1: Very Poor
	+ 2: Average
	+ 3: Excellent
* **Course Format**: This question asks about the format of the course. Please select all that apply.
	+ Online
	+ Face-to-face
	+ Hybrid
* **Course Content**: Please provide your thoughts on the course content.
* **Course Presentation**: Please provide your thoughts on the course presentation.
* **Course Faculty**: Please provide your thoughts on the course faculty.
* **Course Support Materials**: Please provide your thoughts on the course support materials.
* **Student Support**: Please provide your thoughts on the student support.
* **Date**: This question asks for the date you completed the course.
* **Time**: This question asks for the time you completed the course.
* **Note**: Please provide any additional comments or notes about your experience.
* **Email**: This question asks for your email address (if you'd like to be contacted).
* **Phone**: This question asks for your phone number (if you'd like to be contacted).
* **Evaluation Date**: This question asks for the date you are evaluating the course.

## Tips
* Please be as thorough and honest as possible in your answers.
* If you have any questions or need help, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your feedback and will use it to improve our services.
