# Sociology Student Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from sociology students about their experience in the course, which will help the instructors and the department to evaluate the course's performance and make improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the correct answer for each question by choosing the option that best reflects your experience.
2. Fill in any required fields, such as your student name and email.
3. Provide as much information as possible in each section to help the department get a clear picture of your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sociology Student Feedback Survey** (`course_title`, text, optional): This is the title of the form, which is used to identify the course.
* **Sociology Course ID** (`course_id`, number, optional): Enter the ID of the course you are taking.
* **Satisfaction** (`feedback_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Choose how satisfied you are with the course overall.
	+ Very Satisfied: I am extremely happy with the course.
	+ Somewhat Satisfied: I am somewhat happy with the course.
	+ Neither Satisfied Nor Dissatisfied: I am neutral about the course.
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied: I am somewhat unhappy with the course.
	+ Very Dissatisfied: I am extremely unhappy with the course.
* **Teaching Effectiveness** (`teaching_effectiveness`, select_multiple, optional): Rate the effectiveness of the course's teaching materials.
	+ Excellent: The course materials were excellent.
	+ Good: The course materials were good.
	+ Fair: The course materials were fair.
	+ Poor: The course materials were poor.
* **Improvement Opportunities** (`improvement_opportunities`, note, optional): Provide any suggestions or areas for improvement.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as a student.
* **Student Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Course Department** (`course_department`, select_one, optional): Choose the department of the course.
	+ Sociology: The course is in the sociology department.
	+ Psychology: The course is in the psychology department.
	+ Other: The course is in another department.
* **Course Instructor Name** (`course_instructor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the instructor.
* **Course Location** (`course_location`, select_one, optional): Choose the location of the course.
	+ Online: The course is online.
	+ In-person: The course is in-person.
	+ Both: The course is both online and in-person.
* **Course Instructor Evaluation** (`course_instructor_evaluation`, select_multiple, optional): Rate the effectiveness of the instructor.
	+ Excellent: The instructor was excellent.
	+ Good: The instructor was good.
	+ Fair: The instructor was fair.
	+ Poor: The instructor was poor.
* **Course Materials** (`course_materials`, select_multiple, optional): Rate the quality of the course materials.
	+ Excellent: The course materials were excellent.
	+ Good: The course materials were good.
	+ Fair: The course materials were fair.
	+ Poor: The course materials were poor.
	+ No materials: There were no materials provided.
* **Course Scheduling** (`course_scheduling`, select_multiple, optional): Rate the effectiveness of the course's scheduling.
	+ Excellent: The course scheduling was excellent.
	+ Good: The course scheduling was good.
	+ Fair: The course scheduling was fair.
	+ Poor: The course scheduling was poor.
* **Instructor Support** (`instructor_support`, select_multiple, optional): Rate the support provided by the instructor.
	+ Excellent: The instructor provided excellent support.
	+ Good: The instructor provided good support.
	+ Fair: The instructor provided fair support.
	+ Poor: The instructor provided poor support.
