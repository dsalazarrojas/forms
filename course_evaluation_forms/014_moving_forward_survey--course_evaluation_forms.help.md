# Moving Forward Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Moving Forward Survey is a self-assessment and feedback tool designed to help you reflect on your progress, set goals, and provide feedback to your manager and colleagues. This guide will walk you through the form's structure and explain each field in detail.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Moving Forward Survey form, follow these steps:

1. Start by writing your responses to the open-ended questions (pages 1 and 5) and providing a brief reflection of the past year (page 2).
2. Select the goals you aim to achieve in the future (page 3).
3. Prioritize your goals using the number field (page 4).
4. Write any additional feedback or comments to your manager (page 6) and colleagues (page 7).
5. Provide any general feedback or comments (page 8).
6. Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Moving Forward Survey** (Page 1):
	+ A place to write your initial thoughts and introduction to the survey.
* **Year Reflection** (Page 2):
	+ Briefly reflect on your progress and experiences over the past year.
* **Goals Future** (Page 3):
	+ Select up to three goals you aim to achieve in the future.
* **Goals Priority** (Page 4):
	+ Use the number field to prioritize your selected goals. The higher the number, the more important the goal.
* **Self Evaluation** (Page 5):
	+ Write a brief self-evaluation of your performance and progress.
* **Feedback to Manager** (Page 6):
	+ Provide any feedback or comments to your manager.
* **Feedback to Colleague** (Page 7):
	+ Provide any feedback or comments to your colleagues.
* **Feedback to Other** (Page 8):
	+ Provide any general feedback or comments.
* **Final Comments** (Page 9):
	+ Leave any additional comments or feedback.
* **Date Submitted** (Page 10):
	+ The date and time the form was submitted.

## Tips
- Be honest and authentic in your responses.
- Review and edit your answers before submitting.
- Use clear and concise language.
- Make sure to prioritize your goals using the number field.
- Provide specific and actionable feedback to your manager and colleagues.
