# Birth Arts International Parental Birth Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from parents who have used the services of a doula (a trained birth support person). The form helps evaluate the quality of services provided by the doula, identify areas for improvement, and gather feedback for future development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your personal details (first name, last name, and email address)
2.  Rate your experience with the doula's services
3.  Answer questions about the quality of support you received (physical comfort, emotional support, and communication with medical providers)
4.  Share your overall satisfaction with the birth experience
5.  Provide feedback on areas for improvement and suggestions for better services

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Parent First Name** (`parent_first_name`, `text`, `required`): Your first name that you prefer to be called during the birth experience.
    \*\*Provide your first name in this field.\*
*   **Parent Last Name** (`parent_last_name`, `text`, `required`): Your last name that you prefer to be called during the birth experience.
    \*\*Provide your last name in this field.\*
*   **Email Address** (`parent_email`, `email`, `required`): Your contact email that the doula's team can use to follow up with you.
    \*\*Provide your email address that you'd like the doula's team to use for follow-up.\*
*   **Doula Name** (`doul_name`, `text`, `required`): The name of the doula who provided support during your birth experience.
    \*\*Enter the name of the doula who supported you during your birth.\*
*   **Birth Date** (`evaluation_date`, `date`, `required`): The date of your birth.
    \*\*Provide the date of your birth.\*
*   **Overall Birth Experience Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `number`, `required`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the birth experience from 1 to 10.
    \*\*Rate your satisfaction with the entire birth experience.\*
*   **Quality of Doula Support** (`doula_support_quality`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate the support you received from the doula (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
    \*\*Rate the support you received from the doula, from Excellent to Poor.\*
*   **Doula Communication and Presence** (`doula_communication`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate the communication you received from the doula (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
    \*\*Rate the communication and presence of the doula.\*
*   **Emotional Support Provided** (`emotional_support`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate the emotional support you received from the doula (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
    \*\*Rate the emotional support you received from the doula.\*
*   **Physical Comfort Measures** (`physical_comfort`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate the help you received with physical comfort (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
    \*\*Rate the support you received with physical comfort.\*
*   **Advocacy** (`advocacy`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate the advocacy you received from the doula (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
    \*\*Rate the advocacy you received from the doula.\*
*   **Impact on Birth Experience** (`doula_impact`, `text`, `required`): Describe the impact the doula had on your birth experience.
    \*\*Describe the positive or negative impact the doula had on your birth experience.\*
*   **What Was Most Helpful About Doula Support** (`positive_aspects`, `text`, `required`): Share the most helpful aspects of the doula's support.
    \*\*Share the most helpful aspects of the doula's support.\*
*   **Pain Management** (`pain_management`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate the pain management support you received from the doula (Very Helpful, Helpful, Somewhat Helpful, Not Helpful).
    \*\*Rate the pain management support you received.\*
*   **Confidence Support** (`confidence_support`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate the support that helped build your confidence (Very Much, Somewhat, A Little, Not at All).
    \*\*Rate the support you received with building your confidence.\*
*   **Satisfaction with Birth Outcome** (`birth_outcome_satisfaction`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate your satisfaction with the birth outcome (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
    \*\*Rate your satisfaction with the birth outcome.\*
*   **Collaboration with Medical Team** (`medical_team_collaboration`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate the collaboration between the doula and medical team (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
    \*\*Rate the collaboration between the doula and medical team.\*
*   **Felt Heard by Medical Providers** (`communication_with_doctors`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate whether you felt heard by medical providers (Completely, Mostly, Somewhat, Not Really, Not at All).
    \*\*Rate whether you felt heard by the medical providers.\*
*   **Would You Recommend This Doula** (`would_recommend_doula`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate whether you would recommend the doula to others (Strongly Recommend, Recommend, Neutral, Not Recommend, Strongly Advise Against).
    \*\*Rate whether you would recommend the doula to others.\*
*   **Would You Use Doula Support for Future Birth** (`would_use_doula_again`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate whether you would use the doula's support again in future (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Maybe, Probably No, Definitely No).
    \*\*Rate whether you would use the doula's support again in future.\*
*   **Suggestions for Improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, `required`): Share your suggestions for improvement for the doula's services.
    \*\*Share your suggestions for improvement for the doula's services.\*
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_feedback`, `text`, `required`): Share any other feedback you have for the doula's services.
    \*\*Share any additional feedback you have.\*
*   **Postpartum Contact or Follow-up** (`postpartum_contact`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate whether you received postpartum contact or follow-up from the doula (Yes, Excellent Follow-up, Yes, Some Follow-up, Minimal Follow-up, No Follow-up).
    \*\*Rate whether you received postpartum contact or follow-up from the doula.\*
*   **Resource Information** (`resource_information`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate whether you received resource information from the doula (Yes, Comprehensive Resources, Yes, Some Resources, Minimal Resources, No Resources).
    \*\*Rate whether you received resource information from the doula.\*
*   **May We Use Your Comments as Testimonial** (`permission_testimonial`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate whether you give permission for the doula's team to use your comments as a testimonial (Yes, Use My Feedback, No, Keep Private).
    \*\*Rate whether you give permission for the doula's team to use your comments as a testimonial.\*
