# Women's Self Defense Course Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on the Women's Self Defense Course, specifically on the session attended by the user. This feedback will help the instructors and course administrators improve the course and enhance the overall learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form

Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Fill out the "Course Name" field with the name of the course you attended.
2. Enter the "Session Date" of the course you attended.
3. Provide your "Instructor Name".
4. Enter your "Feedback Comments" (if any).
5. Rate the "Overall Rating" of the course on a scale of 0-5.
6. Evaluate the "Instructor Performance" as "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
7. Choose whether the "Class Materials" were sufficient, not sufficient, or average.
8. Rate the "Course Organization" as "Well-organized", "Not Well-organized", or "Average".
9. Evaluate the "Instructor Presentation" as "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
10. Provide feedback to the "Instructor" if you have any.
11. Answer "Will Attend Again" with "True" or "False".
12. Answer "Recommend Course" with "True" or "False".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Name** (`Course Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the course you attended.
* **Session Date** (`Session Date`, `date`, required/true): Enter the date of the session you attended.
* **Instructor Name** (`Instructor Name`, `text`, required/true): Enter the name of the instructor who led the session.
* **Feedback Comments** (`Feedback Comments`, `text`, required/false): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the course.
* **Overall Rating** (`Overall Rating`, `number`, required/true): Rate the overall quality of the course on a scale of 0-5.
* **Instructor Performance** (`Instructor Performance`, `select_one`, required/true): Evaluate the performance of the instructor as "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Class Materials** (`Class Materials`, `select_multiple`, required/false): Choose whether the class materials were sufficient, not sufficient, or average.
* **Course Organization** (`Course Organization`, `select_multiple`, required/false): Rate the course organization as "Well-organized", "Not Well-organized", or "Average".
* **Instructor Presentation** (`Instructor Presentation`, `select_one`, required/true): Evaluate the presentation skills of the instructor as "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Feedback to Instructor** (`Feedback to Instructor`, `text`, required/false): Provide any feedback you have for the instructor.
* **Will Attend Again** (`Will Attend Again`, `select_one`, required/true): Answer with "True" if you would attend the course again, "False" otherwise.
* **Recommend Course** (`Recommend Course`, `select_one`, required/true): Answer with "True" if you would recommend the course to others, "False" otherwise.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, required/false): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
