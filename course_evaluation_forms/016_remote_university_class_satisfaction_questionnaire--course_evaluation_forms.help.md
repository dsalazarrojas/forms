# Remote University Class Satisfaction Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remote University Class Satisfaction Questionnaire is a tool used to collect feedback from students about their experience in a remote university class. It's a way for instructors to gauge their performance, understand student needs, and improve the course content and delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please answer all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), as truthfully as possible.
* If you're unsure about a question or feel it's not applicable, feel free to skip it or write "N/A" (Not Applicable).
* Use clear and concise language when providing feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Remote Class Satisfaction** (`remote_university_class_satisfaction`, Number, Optional)
	+ This field asks for your overall satisfaction with the remote class. Please rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1-10, where 10 is excellent and 1 is not satisfactory.
* **Class Name** (`class_name`, Text, Optional)
	+ Please provide the name of the class you're providing feedback about.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, Text, Optional)
	+ Provide the name of the instructor who led the class.
* **Class Rating** (`class_rating`, Number, Optional)
	+ Rate your experience with the class content on a scale of 1-10, where 10 is excellent and 1 is not satisfactory.
* **Instructor Rating** (`instructor_rating`, Number, Optional)
	+ Rate your experience with the instructor on a scale of 1-10, where 10 is excellent and 1 is not satisfactory.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, Number, Optional)
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction with the class on a scale of 1-10, where 10 is excellent and 1 is not satisfactory.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Note, Optional)
	+ This is an optional field for providing any additional comments or suggestions you might have about the class.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional)
	+ If you'd like to be contacted about any follow-up questions or concerns, please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional)
	+ If you'd like to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number.
Please note that some fields are optional, so you can choose to fill them out or not. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the relevant support team.
