# Film Course Participant Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Film Course Participant Evaluation form is a crucial tool for instructors to gauge their teaching effectiveness and make informed decisions to improve future courses. Your feedback will help shape the course's development and ensure that future participants receive a more valuable learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer based on your genuine experience.
2. Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
3. Write your thoughts and suggestions in the "Additional Comments" field.
4. Submit your feedback, and thank you for your contribution!

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, required: false): A brief description of the course.
* **Feedback on Lessons** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ What did you learn about film craft?
	+ What would you like to know more about?
* **Feedback on Exercises** (`3`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ How would you rate the exercises and activities?
	+ (Note: Select "Yes" if you found them helpful, and "No" if you didn't.)
* **Instructor Performance** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ How did the instructor perform during the course?
	+ (Note: Select "Yes" for positive aspects and "No" for areas that need improvement.)
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`5`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ How do you feel about your overall experience?
	+ (Note: Select "Satisfied" for a positive experience, and "Dissatisfied" for a negative one.)
* **Additional Comments** (`6`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Do you have any additional comments or suggestions about the course?
