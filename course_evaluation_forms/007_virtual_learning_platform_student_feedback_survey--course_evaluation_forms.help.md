# virtual_learning_platform_student_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students who have used the virtual learning platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the course name field with the name of the course you are providing feedback on.
2. If you have a email address associated with your account, fill out the email field. If not, you can leave it blank.
3. If you have a phone number, fill out the phone field. If not, you can leave it blank.
4. Fill out the date of birth field with your actual date of birth.
5. For the "How likely are you to recommend this course?" field, select the option that best describes your sentiment.
6. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, fill out the "Any additional comments" field.
7. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure they are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is the name of the course**: This field is used to gather information on the course you are providing feedback on. Please fill out the name of the course accurately.
* **Email**: This field is used to gather your email address. If you have an email associated with your account, please fill it out. If not, you can leave it blank.
* **Phone**: This field is used to gather your phone number. If you have a phone number, please fill it out. If not, you can leave it blank.
* **Date of Birth**: This field is used to gather your date of birth.
* **How likely are you to recommend this course?**: This field is used to gauge your sentiment towards the course. Please select the option that best describes your feelings.
* **Select all that apply**: This field allows you to select multiple options from the list.
* **Time**: This field is used to gather information on the time you spent on the course. Please fill out the time accurately.
* **Any additional comments**: This field is used to gather any additional comments or suggestions you have.

Note: The fields are marked as optional unless they are marked as required. Please be aware that your feedback is valuable to us and your input will help us improve the platform.
