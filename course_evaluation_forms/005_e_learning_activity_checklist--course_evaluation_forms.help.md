# E Learning Activity Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate and assess the progress of E-Learning activities by collecting data on various tasks and activities completed by participants. It ensures that the activities meet the required standards and standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

* Start by reviewing the E-Learning activity details, such as tasks, progress, and completion status.
* For each task, select the relevant options or enter the required information.
* Ensure that all fields marked as required are completed, as they are crucial for a comprehensive assessment.
