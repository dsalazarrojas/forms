# Academic Performance Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Academic Performance Questionnaire is a tool used to assess students' performance and experiences in a specific course. It helps instructors and educators evaluate students' understanding, engagement, and challenges faced during the course, providing valuable insights for improvement and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Ensure you have all the necessary information and answers ready before starting.
2.  Complete each section, providing accurate and honest responses.
3.  For select_one and select_multiple questions, choose the best option that matches your experience.
4.  For text fields, provide a brief but detailed answer.
5.  Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official records.
* **Student Identification Number** (`student_id`, text, required): Enter your unique university ID.
* **Course Title** (`course_title`, text, required): Enter the full name of the course being evaluated.
* **Current Semester** (`semester`, select_one, required): Select the term being evaluated (Fall 2025, Spring 2026, or Summer 2026).
* **How often did you attend classes?** (`attendance_frequency`, select_one, required): Indicate how frequently you attended classes, considering lectures and seminars.
	+ Always: I attended all classes regularly.
	+ Frequently: I attended most classes but missed some.
	+ Occasionally: I attended some classes but missed others.
	+ Rarely: I barely attended classes.
	+ Never: I did not attend classes.
* **Average hours spent studying for this course per week** (`weekly_study_hours`, number, required): Enter the average hours spent studying for this course, excluding scheduled class time.
* **How would you rate your understanding of the course content?** (`understanding_of_material`, select_one, required): Honestly assess your comprehension of the course material.
	+ Complete Mastery: I have a complete understanding of the material.
	+ Strong Understanding: I have a good grasp of the material.
	+ Basic Understanding: I have some understanding but need more.
	+ Struggling: I am having difficulty understanding the material.
* **Did you submit all assignments on time?** (`assignment_completion`, select_one, required): Indicate the status of your assignment submissions.
	+ Yes - All: I submitted all assignments on time.
	+ Most of them: I submitted most assignments on time.
	+ Some of them: I submitted some assignments late.
	+ Rarely: I rarely submitted assignments on time.
* **Rate your level of active participation in class discussions** (`participation_level`, select_one, required): Indicate your level of engagement during lessons.
	+ Very High: I was very engaged and active.
	+ High: I was fairly engaged and active.
	+ Moderate: I was moderately engaged.
	+ Low: I was least engaged.
	+ None: I did not participate at all.
* **Identify any difficulties faced during the course** (`difficulties_encountered`, select_multiple, optional): Select all challenges you faced during the course (e.g., Complex Concepts, Workload, Language Barriers, Technical Issues, Personal Reasons).
* **What additional support would have improved your performance?** (`support_needed`, text, optional): Provide specific suggestions on how you would have benefited from additional support.
* **What grade do you expect to receive in this course?** (`grade_expectation`, text, optional): Anticipate your expected final result.

## Tips

Please answer the questions honestly and provide clear, concise responses. If you have any doubts or questions, refer to the course instructor or the institution's support resources.
