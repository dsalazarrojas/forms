# relax_kids_term_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "relax_kids_term_feedback_form" is designed to gather feedback from users on various aspects of their experience at "relax" kids term, allowing us to continually improve and enhance the quality of our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Provide your Feedback: On the first page, please give us a brief testimonial about your overall experience at "relax" kids term.
2. Rate Your Experience: On the subsequent pages, rate your experience in different areas, such as staff, content, venue, facilities, and food. You can select from the given options or provide a numerical rating.
3. Submit Your Feedback: Once you have completed all the pages, click the submit button to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback** (`testimonial`, `text`, required/optional): A brief testimonial about your overall experience at "relax" kids term.
* **How would you rate your overall experience?** (`testimonial_2`, `number`, required/optional): Rate your experience on a scale of 1-10.
* **How would you rate the staff?** (`testimonial_3`, `select_one`, required/optional): Rate the staff as Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **How would you rate the content?** (`testimonial_4`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or more of the following options: Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent.
* **How would you rate our venue?** (`testimonial_5`, `number`, required/optional): Rate the venue on a scale of 1-10.
* **How would you rate our facilities?** (`testimonial_6`, `date`, required/optional): This field is currently not supported, as no date options are provided.
* **How would you rate our food?** (`testimonial_7`, `time`, required/optional): This field is currently not supported, as time options are not relevant for rating purposes.
* **How would you rate our overall experience?** (`testimonial_8`, `text`, required/optional): A brief testimonial to rate your overall experience at "relax" kids term.
