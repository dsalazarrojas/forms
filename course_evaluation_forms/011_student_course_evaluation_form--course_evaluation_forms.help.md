# Student Course Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from students about their experience with a course. It helps instructors understand what worked well and what didn't in the course, allowing them to make improvements for future classes. The form assesses the student's perception of the course's overall quality, organization, and supportiveness.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Choose the options that best describe your experience with the course.
3. You may choose one or multiple options for each question.
4. If you have additional comments or suggestions, use the "additional_comments" field to provide more detail.
5. Submit the form when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **course_rating** (`number`, optional): Rate the course overall on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
- **course_materials** (`text`, optional): Describe the materials provided in the course, such as textbooks, handouts, or online resources.
- **teacher_support** (`select_one`, optional): Choose how you would describe the teacher's support in the course, e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
- **teacher_clarity** (`select_one`, optional): Choose how clear the teacher was in explaining course concepts, e.g., Clear, Somewhat clear, Not clear, Very unclear.
- **course_interest** (`select_one`, optional): Choose how interesting you found the course material, e.g., Very interesting, Somewhat interesting, Not very interesting, Not at all interesting.
- **teacher_interest** (`select_one`, optional): Choose how interesting you found the teacher's approach, e.g., Very interesting, Somewhat interesting, Not very interesting, Not at all interesting.
- **course_difficulty** (`select_one`, optional): Choose how difficult you found the course, e.g., Easy, Somewhat easy, Difficult, Very difficult.
- **teacher_difficulty** (`select_one`, optional): Choose how difficult you found the teacher, e.g., Easy, Somewhat easy, Difficult, Very difficult.
- **course_relevance** (`select_one`, optional): Choose how relevant the course material was to your interests, e.g., Very relevant, Somewhat relevant, Not very relevant, Not at all relevant.
- **teacher_relevance** (`select_one`, optional): Choose how relevant you found the teacher's approach, e.g., Very relevant, Somewhat relevant, Not very relevant, Not at all relevant.
- **course_organization** (`select_one`, optional): Choose how well the course was organized, e.g., Well-organized, Somewhat organized, Somewhat disorganized, Very disorganized.
- **teacher_organization** (`select_one`, optional): Choose how well the teacher was organized, e.g., Well-organized, Somewhat organized, Disorganized, Very disorganized.
- **course_helpfulness** (`select_one`, optional): Choose how helpful you found the course material, e.g., Very helpful, Somewhat helpful, Not very helpful, Not at all helpful.
- **teacher_helpfulness** (`select_one`, optional): Choose how helpful you found the teacher's support, e.g., Very helpful, Somewhat helpful, Not very helpful, Not at all helpful.
- **course_fairness** (`select_one`, optional): Choose how fair the course was, e.g., Very fair, Somewhat fair, Not very fair, Not at all fair.
- **teacher_fairness** (`select_one`, optional): Choose how fair you found the teacher's approach, e.g., Very fair, Somewhat fair, Not very fair, Not at all fair.
- **course_value** (`select_one`, optional): Choose how valuable you found the course, e.g., Very valuable, Somewhat valuable, Not very valuable, Not at all valuable.
- **teacher_value** (`select_one`, optional): Choose how valuable you found the teacher's approach, e.g., Very valuable, Somewhat valuable, Not very valuable, Not at all valuable.
- **teacher_friendly** (`select_one`, optional): Choose how friendly the teacher was, e.g., Very friendly, Somewhat friendly, Somewhat unfriendly, Not at all friendly.
- **course_friendly** (`select_one`, optional): Choose how friendly you found the course, e.g., Very friendly, Somewhat friendly, Somewhat unfriendly, Not at all friendly.
- **teacher_supportiveness** (`select_one`, optional): Choose how supportive the teacher was, e.g., Very supportive, Somewhat supportive, Not very supportive, Not at all supportive.
- **course_supportiveness** (`select_one`, optional): Choose how supportive the course was, e.g., Very supportive, Somewhat supportive, Not very supportive, Not at all supportive.
- **course_satisfaction** (`select_one`, optional): Choose how satisfied you are with the course, e.g., Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Not very satisfied, Not at all satisfied.
- **teacher_satisfaction** (`select_one`, optional): Choose how satisfied you are with the teacher's approach, e.g., Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Not very satisfied, Not at all satisfied.
- **overall_satisfaction** (`select_one`, optional): Choose how satisfied you are with your overall experience, e.g., Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Not very satisfied, Not at all satisfied.
- **additional_comments** (`note`, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
- **course_recommended** (`select_multiple`, optional): Choose whether you would recommend the course to others, e.g., True, False.
- **teacher_recommended** (`select_multiple`, optional): Choose whether you would recommend the teacher to others, e.g., True, False.
- **would_take** (`select_multiple`, optional): Choose whether you would take another course with this teacher, e.g., True, False.
- **course_take** (`select_multiple`, optional): Choose whether you would take another course with a similar topic, e.g., True, False.
- **teacher_take** (`select_multiple`, optional): Choose whether you would take another course with this teacher, e.g., True, False.
