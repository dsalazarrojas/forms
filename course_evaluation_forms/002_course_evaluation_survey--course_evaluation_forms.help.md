> # Course Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback on the quality and effectiveness of a course. It helps instructors and administrators understand what works well and what needs improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each page of the form carefully and answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the option that best fits your opinion or experience.
3. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as possible.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
5. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Note): This is a brief introduction to the course evaluation survey. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Course Content** (Select Multiple): Choose all the options that apply to your experience with the course content. 
* **Teaching Quality** (Select One): Choose the option that best reflects your opinion on the quality of the teaching.
	+ Option 1: The teaching was excellent
	+ Option 2: The teaching was satisfactory
	+ Option 3: The teaching needs improvement
* **Learning Experience** (Select Multiple): Choose all the options that apply to your experience with the learning experience. 
* **Course Materials** (Number): Rate the quality of the course materials from 1-10.
* **Student Involvement** (Select Multiple): Choose all the options that apply to your experience with student involvement. 
* **Communication** (Select One): Choose the option that best reflects your opinion on the communication of the instructor.
	+ Option 1: Excellent
	+ Option 2: Satisfactory
	+ Option 3: Needs Improvement
* **Assessment** (Select Multiple): Choose all the options that apply to your experience with the assessment. 
* **Course Relevance** (Select One): Choose the option that best reflects your opinion on the relevance of the course.
	+ Option 1: Very Relevant
	+ Option 2: Somewhat Relevant
	+ Option 3: Not Relevant
* **Instructor Effectiveness** (Select Multiple): Choose all the options that apply to your experience with the instructor. 
* **Feedback** (Note): This is an open-ended field for any comments or feedback on the course.
* **Student Support** (Select One): Choose the option that best reflects your experience with student support. 
	+ Option 1: Very Supportive
	+ Option 2: Somewhat Supportive
	+ Option 3: Not Supportive
* **Course Structure** (Number): Rate the quality of the course structure from 1-10.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (Select One): Choose the option that best reflects your overall satisfaction with the course. 
	+ Option 1: Very Satisfying
	+ Option 2: Somewhat Satisfying
	+ Option 3: Not Satisfying
* **Recommendation** (Select One): Choose the option that best reflects your recommendation of the course. 
	+ Option 1: Definitely Recommend
	+ Option 2: Possibly Recommend
	+ Option 3: Not Recommend
* **Instructor Feedback** (Text): This is an open-ended field for any feedback on the instructor.
* **Student Feedback** (Email): This is an open-ended field for any feedback on the course or instructor to be sent to the student's email.
* **Comments** (Text): This is an open-ended field for any additional comments on the course.
* **Suggestions** (Text): This is an open-ended field for any suggestions on how to improve the course.
* **Rating** (Number): Rate the overall quality of the course from 1-10.
* **Student Attendance** (Number): Rate the student attendance from 1-10.
* **Student Engagement** (Number): Rate the student engagement from 1-10.
* **Teacher Attendance** (Number): Rate the teacher attendance from 1-10.
* **Class Size** (Number): Rate the class size from 1-10.
* **Average Rating** (Number): This is an automatically calculated field to rate the average quality of the course based on your feedback.
