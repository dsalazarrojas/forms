# AI Prompt Engineering Course Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from course participants to help course administrators improve the AI Prompt Engineering Course.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions in the Field-by-Field Explanation section above.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Information**: A field for adding any notes about the course session.
* **Participant name**: A field for entering your name as a participant of the course.
* **Course date**: A field for selecting the date of the course session you attended.
* **Instructor effectiveness**: A field for rating the instructor's effectiveness from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
* **Course content quality**: A field for rating the course content quality from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
* **Usefulness of practical exercises**: A field for rating the usefulness of the practical exercises from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
* **Did the course meet your learning outcomes**: A field for selecting whether the course met your learning outcomes.
* **Suggestions for improvement**: A field for typing any suggestions you have for improving the course.
* **Would you recommend this course**: A field for selecting whether you would recommend this course.
* **Additional comments**: A field for typing any additional comments you have about the course.
