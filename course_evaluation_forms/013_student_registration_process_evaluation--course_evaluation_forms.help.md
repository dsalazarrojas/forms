# Student Registration Process Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Registration Process Evaluation form is designed to collect feedback on the student's registration experience at an educational institution.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before starting.
2. Ensure you provide accurate and honest answers.
3. Complete each section according to the explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Details** (`registration_details`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your registration experience.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number.
* **Date of Registration** (`date_of_registration`, date, optional): Enter the date of your registration.
* **Time of Registration** (`time_of_registration`, time, optional): Enter the time of your registration.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any comments about your registration experience.
* **Support** (`support`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Option 1: Satisfactory
	+ Option 2: Needs improvement
	+ Option 3: Unavailable
* **Communication** (`communication`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Option 1: Satisfactory
	+ Option 2: Needs improvement
	+ Option 3: Unavailable
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Option 1: Satisfactory
	+ Option 2: Needs improvement
	+ Option 3: Unavailable
* **Support Rating** (`support_rating`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Option 1: Excellent
	+ Option 2: Good
	+ Option 3: Poor
* **Communication Rating** (`communication_rating`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Option 1: Excellent
	+ Option 2: Good
	+ Option 3: Poor
* **Overall Satisfaction Rating** (`overall_satisfaction_rating`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Option 1: Excellent
	+ Option 2: Good
	+ Option 3: Poor
* **Comments about Support** (`comments_about_support`, note, optional): Add any comments about support provided during your registration.
* **Comments about Communication** (`comments_about_communication`, note, optional): Add any comments about communication during your registration.
* **Comments about Registration Process** (`comments_about_registration_process`, note, optional): Add any comments about the registration process.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, note, optional): Suggest any improvements or changes for future registrations.
* **Contact Us** (`contact_us`, email, optional): Enter your contact information for follow-up or further discussion.
* **Contact Number 2** (`contact_number_2`, text, optional): Enter a secondary contact number.
* **Date of Registration 2** (`date_of_registration_2`, date, optional): Enter the date of your second registration (if applicable).
* **Time of Registration 2** (`time_of_registration_2`, time, optional): Enter the time of your second registration (if applicable).
* **Comments 2** (`comments_2`, note, optional): Add any comments about your second registration (if applicable).
* **Support 2** (`support_2`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable options for support provided during your second registration.
* **Communication 2** (`communication_2`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable options for communication during your second registration.
* **Overall Satisfaction 2** (`overall_satisfaction_2`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable options for overall satisfaction during your second registration.
