# Course Curriculum Overview - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Curriculum Overview form is designed to collect and document a course's curriculum structure, content, and learning objectives. This information is crucial for teachers, instructors, or administrators to review, evaluate, and plan courses effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, begin by entering a brief description of the course. This should be a concise summary of the course's content and objectives.
2.  Next, enter the course name in the provided text field.
3.  If relevant, select the course level from the dropdown menu to indicate the course's grade level or academic standing.
4.  Enter the course department, which refers to the academic division or department in a school or university responsible for the course.
5.  Specify the course subject, which is the subject matter or discipline the course covers.
6.  Briefly describe the course outcomes, which are the skills, knowledge, or competencies students will attain upon completing the course.
7.  If applicable, select one or more curriculum maps to associate with the course.
8.  Finally, review your entries to ensure they accurately reflect the course's curriculum overview.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the course, such as "Introduction to Computer Science" or "Mathematics for Engineers".
- **Course Description** (`course_description`, text, required): Provide a brief and concise description of the course, including its objectives, topics, and expected learning outcomes.
- **Course Objectives** (`course_objectives`, text, required): Describe the course's learning objectives, such as skills or knowledge students will attain upon completion.
- **Course Level** (`course_level`, select_one, optional): Choose from the dropdown menu to indicate the course's grade level or academic standing, such as High School, College, or Middle School.
- **Course Department** (`course_department`, text, optional): Specify the academic division or department responsible for the course, e.g., Mathematics, Science, or Humanities.
- **Course Subject** (`course_subject`, text, optional): Enter the subject matter or discipline the course covers, e.g., Computer Science, Mathematics, or Literature.
- **Course Outcomes** (`course_outcomes`, text, optional): Briefly describe the course outcomes, such as skills, knowledge, or competencies students will attain upon completion.
- **Curriculum Maps** (`course_curriculum_map`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more curriculum maps relevant to the course, which may include descriptions or resources related to the course's content and structure.

## Tips

- Be as concise as possible when filling in the form, using clear and simple language.
- Ensure that the entries accurately reflect the course's curriculum overview.
- Review and update the form regularly to reflect changes in the course's content or structure.
- If you are unsure about any field, review the form's help guide or consult with your organization's experts or stakeholders.
