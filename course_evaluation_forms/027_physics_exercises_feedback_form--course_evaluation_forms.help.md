# Physics Exercises Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physics Exercises Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback on physics exercises completed by students. This form will help instructors and course teams evaluate student performance, provide constructive feedback, and identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are filled.
2.  Choose the feedback type relevant to the exercise.
3.  Describe the problem statement or assignment in the "Problem Statement" field.
4.  Provide feedback on the student's answer in the "Teacher Feedback" field.
5.  Offer peer feedback in the "Peer Feedback" field.
6.  Include any comments from the course team in the "Course Team Feedback" field.
7.  Record the date and time of submission in the "Submission Date" and "Submitted Time" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Course Code** (text, not required): This field is for entering the course code or ID number associated with the exercise.
*   **Student Id** (number, not required): Enter the student's ID number, if applicable.
*   **Question Number** (number, not required): Enter the question or exercise number.
*   **Feedback Type** (select_one, not required): Choose from the following options:
    *   Not Applicable
    *   General Comments
    *   Problem Solving
    *   Physics Knowledge
    *   Other
*   **Problem Statement** (text, not required): Describe the problem statement or assignment here.
*   **Student Answer** (text, not required): Provide feedback on the student's answer.
*   **Teacher Feedback** (text, not required): Describe how you will help or improve this problem or assignment.
*   **Peer Feedback** (text, not required): Offer feedback to your peers.
*   **Course Team Feedback** (text, not required): Include any comments from the course team.
*   **Submission Date** (date, not required): Record the date of submission.
*   **Submission Time** (time, not required): Record the time of submission.
*   **Submission Date** (date, not required): This field is a duplicate of "Submission Date" and may be ignored.
*   **Submitted Time** (time, not required): This field is a duplicate of "Submission Time" and may be ignored.

## Tips

*   Be as specific as possible when filling out each field.
*   Use clear and concise language in the feedback fields.
*   Ensure all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
*   If a field is not applicable, leave it blank or select the corresponding option.
