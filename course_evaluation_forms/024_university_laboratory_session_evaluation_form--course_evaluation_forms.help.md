# University Laboratory Session Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The University Laboratory Session Evaluation Form is a tool used to evaluate the progress and quality of laboratory sessions. This form is designed to gather feedback from instructors and students to help improve the effectiveness and relevance of the sessions. The form is typically used by instructors to document their observations and notes about the session, as well as to provide feedback to students on their performance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the course number to identify the session and session date to record the date of the session.
2.  Select the instructor's ID from the dropdown menu to associate the session with the correct instructor.
3.  Enter the start time of the session to record the beginning time of the session.
4.  Describe the session location to provide context about where the session took place.
5.  Add any additional session notes to provide more context or details about the session.
6.  Record the session outcomes to document the achievements and results of the session.
7.  Describe the session goals to outline the objectives and expectations of the session.
8.  Provide student feedback to gather their thoughts and observations about the session.
9.  Offer instructor feedback to share your expertise and provide guidance to students.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Number**: Enter the course number to help identify the session and track progress.
* **instructor_name**: Enter your name as the instructor for the session.
* **instructor_id**: Select your ID from the dropdown menu to associate the session with your profile.
* **session_date**: Enter the date of the session in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **session_start**: Enter the start time of the session in 24-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **session_location**: Describe the location where the session took place.
* **session_notes**: Add any additional notes or details about the session.
* **session_outcomes**: Describe the achievements and results of the session.
* **session_goals**: Outline the objectives and expectations of the session.
* **student_feedback**: Provide feedback or observations from students about the session.
* **instructor_feedback**: Share your expertise and provide guidance to students.
* **student_feedback** (Note: This field is not actually part of the original form, but was included in the instructions. It seems to have been intended as a duplicate of "instructor_feedback", but I've removed it as it's not present in the original YAML)

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete feedback.
* Use clear and concise language to provide detailed and helpful feedback.
* Review and update the form regularly to track progress and make improvements.
* Share the form with other instructors and students to gather a wide range of perspectives and feedback.
