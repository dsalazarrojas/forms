# Online Class Format Opinion Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect opinions on the online class format from course creators. It aims to gather feedback on various aspects of the class format, including the type of format, the level of instructor support, and the availability of support teams.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your opinion**:
	* For each question, choose the answer that best reflects your opinion.
	* If you have any additional comments or concerns, please type them in the **comments** or **additional_comments** fields.
2. **Submit your feedback**:
	* Once you have completed all the questions, click the **submit** button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Online Class Format Opinion Survey**
	* This is the title of the form.

### 2. **Format**
	* **Format**:
		+ Select the type of format that best describes your online class.
		+ Options: In-person, Hybrid (online with in-person), Online

### 3. **Format Justification**
	* **Format Justification**:
		+ Select the reason why you prefer this format.
		+ Options: More face-to-face interaction, Less face-to-face interaction, No difference

### 4. **Time Format**
	* **Time Format**:
		+ Select the day(s) you prefer for the class.
		+ Options: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday

### 5. **Duration (minutes)**
	* **Duration (minutes)**:
		+ Enter the duration of the class in minutes.

### 6. **Duration (hours)**
	* **Duration (hours)**:
		+ Enter the duration of the class in hours.

### 7. **Schedule**
	* **Schedule**:
		+ Enter the schedule of the class.

### 8. **Instructor Support**
	* **Instructor Support**:
		+ Select the level of support you prefer from the instructor.
		+ Options: True, False

### 9. **Instructor Availability**
	* **Instructor Availability**:
		+ Select the availability of the instructor.
		+ Options: Always available, Mostly available, Occasionally available, Rarely available

### 10. **Student Support**
	* **Student Support**:
		+ Select the availability of support for students.
		+ Options: Always available, Mostly available, Occasionally available, Rarely available

### 11. **Support Team Availability**
	* **Support Team Availability**:
		+ Select the availability of the support team.
		+ Options: Always available, Mostly available, Occasionally available, Rarely available

### 12. **Comments**
	* **Comments**:
		+ Enter any additional comments about the class format.

### 13. **Additional Comments**
	* **Additional Comments**:
		+ Enter any additional comments about the class format.

### 14. **Email**
	* **Email**:
		+ Enter your email address.

### 15. **Phone**
	* **Phone**:
		+ Enter your phone number.

### 16. **Submit**
	* **Submit**:
		+ Click this button to submit your feedback.

Note: This form is not a comprehensive assessment of the class, but rather a snapshot of opinions at a specific point in time.
