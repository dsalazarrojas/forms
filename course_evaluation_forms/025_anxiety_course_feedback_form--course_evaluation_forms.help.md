# Anxiety Course Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants about their experience with our anxiety course. Your input will help us improve the course and provide better resources for future participants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the **Participant Name** field with your name as it appears on your course certificate or as you would like it to be recorded.
2. Select the title of the course you attended from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter the date you completed the course.
4. Rate the ease of understanding of the course material from 1 to 5.
5. Choose the usefulness of the coping strategies taught during the course from Highly Useful, Useful, Neutral, or Not Useful.
6. Rate the instructor's empathy and approachability from 1 to 5 (though the scale is not visible in the form, please refer to the hint: 1 to 5 scale).
7. Select the instructor's knowledge of the subject from Expert, Proficient, Average, or Lacking.
8. Choose the quality of the course resources (PDFs, videos) from Excellent, Good, or Poor.
9. If you wish, share your personal progress since starting the course.
10. Write down the most valuable takeaway from the course.
11. Rate your likelihood to recommend this course from 0 to 10.
12. Finally, suggest any topics you would like to see in future courses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (participant_name): Enter your name as it appears on your course certificate or as you would like it to be recorded.
* **Course Name** (course_title): Select the title of the course you attended from the dropdown menu.
* **Course Completion Date** (course_date): Enter the date you completed the course.
* **Ease of Understanding Course Material** (content_clarity): Rate the ease of understanding of the course material from 1 to 5.
* **Usefulness of Coping Strategies Taught** (strategy_usefulness): Choose the usefulness of the coping strategies taught during the course from Highly Useful, Useful, Neutral, or Not Useful.
* **Instructor's Empathy and Approachability** (instructor_empathy): Rate the instructor's empathy and approachability from 1 to 5 (though the scale is not visible in the form, please refer to the hint: 1 to 5 scale).
* **Instructor's Knowledge of the Subject** (instructor_knowledge): Select the instructor's knowledge of the subject from Expert, Proficient, Average, or Lacking.
* **Quality of Provided Course Resources** (resource_quality): Choose the quality of the course resources (PDFs, videos) from Excellent, Good, or Poor.
* **Personal Progress** (personal_progress): If you wish, share your personal progress since starting the course.
* **Most Valuable Takeaway** (most_valuable_takeaway): Write down the most valuable takeaway from the course.
* **Likelihood to Recommend this Course** (course_recommendation): Rate your likelihood to recommend this course from 0 to 10.
* **Topics You Would Like to See in Future Courses** (future_topics): Suggest any topics you would like to see in future courses.
