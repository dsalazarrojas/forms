# E Learning Course Effectiveness Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E Learning Course Effectiveness Assessment Form is designed to gather feedback from end-users about their experience with an e-learning course. This form helps to identify areas of improvement and provide valuable insights to course developers and instructors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Course Title field with the name of the course you are assessing.
2. Select the rating that best describes your overall experience with the course (E-learning is very effective, E-learning is somewhat effective, E-learning is not effective, or E-learning is somewhat ineffective).
3. Choose the options that best describe your experience with the course content (Engaging and interactive, Relevant and up-to-date, Well-organized, Not engaging at all, or Not well-organized).
4. Enter your comments and feedback about the instructor and course in the Feedback field.
5. Rate your overall experience with the course (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
6. Enter your email address (if you would like to receive updates about course improvements).
7. Enter your phone number (if you would like to be contacted for further discussion).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Title (1)**: Enter the name of the course you are assessing.
* **Rating (2)**: Select the option that best describes your overall experience with the course:
	+ E-learning is very effective
	+ E-learning is somewhat effective
	+ E-learning is not effective
	+ E-learning is somewhat ineffective
* **Course Content (3)**: Select the options that best describe your experience with the course content:
	+ Engaging and interactive
	+ Relevant and up-to-date
	+ Well-organized
	+ Not engaging at all
	+ Not well-organized
* **Instructor (4)**: Enter any comments or feedback you have about the instructor.
* **Feedback (5)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about the course.
* **Overall Experience (6)**: Select the option that best describes your overall experience with the course:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Email (7)**: Enter your email address (if you would like to receive updates about course improvements).
* **Phone (8)**: Enter your phone number (if you would like to be contacted for further discussion).

## Tips
* Be as specific and honest as possible when filling out this form.
* Please do not repeat comments or feedback you have already provided.
* If you would like to suggest improvements or changes, please provide concrete suggestions.
* Your feedback is valuable and will help improve the course for future users.
