# Advanced Endobariatric Course Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide feedback on the Advanced Endobariatric Course. Your input is essential for us to improve the course content, delivery, and overall quality.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form carefully and fill it out completely.
- Answer all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
- For select_one fields, choose only one option from the provided list.
- For required fields, please provide accurate and honest feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **-- Course Feedback --**: This is a header note providing information about the form's purpose.
- **Attendee name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification or the name you used to register for the course.
- **Session date attended**: Enter the date you attended the course in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Content relevance to practice**: Rate how relevant the course content was to your current practice or work. Choose from:
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Excellent
- **Hands on training quality**: Rate the practical sessions' quality. Choose from:
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Excellent
- **Faculty clarity and expertise**: Rate the instructor's performance and their level of expertise. Choose from:
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Excellent
- **Suggestions for future courses**: Provide any suggestions or recommendations you may have for future course topics or formats.
- **Would you recommend this course?**: Select yes or no.
- **Overall rating**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the course on a scale from 1 to 5.
	+ 1: Poor
	+ 2: Fair
	+ 3: Good
	+ 4: Excellent
	+ 5: Outstanding

## Tips
- Please be honest and provide accurate feedback.
- If you have any concerns or issues with the course, please describe them in the "Suggestions for future courses" field.
- If you would like to provide any additional comments or feedback not captured in this form, please include them in the "Suggestions for future courses" field.
