# Exercise Physiology Course Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from students who have completed the Exercise Physiology course. This feedback will be used to improve the course and provide valuable insights to the instructors and course administrators.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely, including all required and optional fields.
2. Be honest and provide detailed answers to the questions.
3. If you have any suggestions or comments, please make sure to include them in the "Additional Comments" field.
4. Once you have filled out the form, click the "Submit" button to save your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required: false): This field is used to record the date of the session you attended. If you don't have a specific date, you can leave this field blank.
* **Session Title** (`session_title`, text, required: false): This field is used to record the title of the session you attended. If you don't have a specific title, you can leave this field blank.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, required: false): This field is used to rate your overall experience with the course. Please use the following scale: 1 (poor), 2 (fair), 3 (good), 4 (very good), 5 (excellent).
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, required: false): This field is used to record the name of the instructor who led the session.
* **Course Rating** (`course_rating`, number, required: false): This field is used to rate your overall experience with the course. Please use the same scale as the "Overall Rating" field.
* **What did you think of the course content?** (`feedback_question_1`, text, required: false): Please provide your thoughts and feedback about the course content.
* **What did you think of the instructor?** (`feedback_question_2`, text, required: false): Please provide your thoughts and feedback about the instructor.
* **Any course suggestions?** (`course_suggestions`, text, required: false): Please provide any suggestions you have for improving the course.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): If you have any additional comments or feedback, please include them here.

## Tips

* Please be honest and provide detailed answers to the questions.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, please make sure to include them in the "Additional Comments" field.
* If you are unable to attend a session and still want to provide feedback, please fill out as much of the form as you can.
* This form should only be filled out by students who have completed the course.
