# Parent Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Parent Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from parents to help us understand their experience with our institution. Your input is invaluable in helping us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please ensure that you have all the necessary information before filling out this form.
2.  Select the answer that best describes your experience with the overall satisfaction.
3.  Share any suggestions you may have to help us improve our services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required: false): This question asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with our institution. Choose the option that best reflects your feelings.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, required: false): This is an open-ended question where you can share any suggestions or ideas you may have on how we can improve our services.

## Tips

* Please answer each question honestly to provide the most valuable feedback.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
