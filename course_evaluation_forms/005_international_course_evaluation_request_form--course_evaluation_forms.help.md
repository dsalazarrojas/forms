# international_course_evaluation_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and evaluation from participants after a course or session. It will help us understand their satisfaction, opinions, and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email for identification purposes.
2. Select the date of the course or session you attended.
3. Enter the name of the course or session.
4. Choose your participant's satisfaction level for each section (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, etc.).
5. Rate the instructor's performance, course satisfaction, and other aspects of the session using numbers (e.g., 1-5).
6. Provide additional comments or feedback if you have any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for us to contact you (if needed).
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the course or session.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the course or session.
* **Session Number** (`session_number`, number, required): Select the number of the session.
* **Participant Satisfaction** (`participant_satisfaction`, select_multiple, required): Choose your level of satisfaction for the session (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, etc.).
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the instructor.
* **Instructor Performance** (`instructor_performance`, select_one, required): Rate the instructor's performance (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, etc.).
* **Session Satisfaction** (`session_satisfaction`, select_multiple, required): Choose your level of satisfaction for the session (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, etc.).
* **Course Satisfaction** (`course_satisfaction`, select_multiple, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the course (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, etc.).
* **Session Improvement** (`session_improvement`, select_one, required): Suggest an improvement for the session.
* **Course Improvement** (`course_improvement`, select_one, required): Suggest an improvement for the course.
* **Participant Feedback** (`participant_feedback`, select_multiple, required): Provide any feedback or suggestions for future sessions.
* **Course Satisfaction Rating** (`course_satisfaction_rating`, number, required): Rate your satisfaction with the course (1-5).
* **Participant Feedback Rating** (`participant_rating`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the session (1-5).
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction (1-5).
* **Participant Satisfaction Rating** (`participant_satisfaction_rating`, number, required): Rate your satisfaction with the course materials or content.
* **Course Evaluation Form Rating** (`course_evaluation_form_rating`, number, required): Rate the effectiveness of this evaluation form (1-5).
* **Instructor Evaluated** (`instructor_evaluated`, number, required): Rate how well the instructor was evaluated (1-5).
* **Session Evaluated** (`session_evaluated`, number, required): Rate how well the session was evaluated (1-5).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
