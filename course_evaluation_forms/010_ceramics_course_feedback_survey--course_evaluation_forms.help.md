# Ceramics Course Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students who have taken a ceramics course. It's essential to fill out this form to help us improve our courses and provide a better learning experience for future students.

## How To Complete This Form
- Take a few minutes to read through each question carefully.
- Fill in your details honestly, as your feedback is crucial for us to grow.
- If you're unsure about any question, try to answer it to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Survey Completion Date** (`survey_date`, `date`, **Required**): This is the date you're submitting your feedback. Please enter the date in the format `DD/MM/YY`, e.g., 12/05/2023.
  - **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, **Optional**): This is your name. Please enter your full name as it appears on your certificate or ID card.
  - **Email Address** (`student_email`, `email`, **Optional**): If you'd like us to contact you for any follow-up questions or to provide additional information, please enter your email address here.
  - **Course Name** (`course_name`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select the name of the course you took:
    + Introduction to Ceramics
    + Wheel Throwing Level 1
    + Wheel Throwing Level 2
    + Hand Building Techniques
    + Advanced Ceramics
    + Glazing and Surface Design
    + Sculptural Ceramics
    + Private Lessons
  - **Course Dates** (`course_dates`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the dates you attended the course. Please be as specific as possible, including the start and end dates.
  - **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the name of your course instructor.
  - **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, **Required**): Rate your overall satisfaction with the course:
    + Very satisfied
    + Satisfied
    + Neutral
    + Dissatisfied
    + Very dissatisfied
  - **Instructor Rating** (`instructor_rating`, `select_one`, **Required**): Rate your instructor:
    + Excellent
    + Very good
    + Good
    + Fair
    + Poor
  - **Instructor Knowledge** (`instructor_knowledge`, `select_one`, **Required**): Rate your instructor's technical knowledge:
    + Strongly agree
    + Agree
    + Neutral
    + Disagree
    + Strongly disagree
  - **Instructor Support** (`instructor_support`, `select_one`, **Required**): Rate the support provided by your instructor:
    + Strongly agree
    + Agree
    + Neutral
    + Disagree
    + Strongly disagree
  - **Course Materials Quality** (`course_materials`, `select_one`, **Required**): Rate the quality of the materials provided:
    + Excellent
    + Very good
    + Good
    + Fair
    + Poor
  - **Studio Facility Rating** (`facility_rating`, `select_one`, **Required**): Rate the studio facilities and equipment:
    + Excellent
    + Very good
    + Good
    + Fair
    + Poor
  - **Skill Improvement** (`skill_improvement`, `select_one`, **Required**): Rate the improvement in your ceramics skills:
    + Significantly improved
    + Moderately improved
    + Slightly improved
    + No improvement
    + Skills decreased
  - **Course Pace** (`course_pace`, `select_one`, **Required**): Rate the pace of the course:
    + Too slow
    + Just right
    + Too fast
  - **Course Difficulty** (`course_difficulty`, `select_one`, **Required**): Rate the difficulty level of the course:
    + Too easy
    + Appropriate
    + Too difficult
  - **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, `select_one`, **Required**): Rate the value of the course in relation to the cost:
    + Excellent value
    + Good value
    + Fair value
    + Poor value
    + Very poor value
  - **Recommend to Others** (`recommend_to_others`, `select_one`, **Required**): Would you recommend this course to others?
    + Definitely yes
    + Probably yes
    + Not sure
    + Probably not
    + Definitely not
  - **Enroll in Another Course** (`enroll_again`, `select_one`, **Required**): Would you take another course with us?
    + Definitely yes
    + Probably yes
    + Not sure
    + Probably not
    + Definitely not
  - **Best Aspect** (`best_aspect`, `text`, **Optional**): What did you enjoy most about the course?
  - **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, `text`, **Optional**): What could be improved in the course?
  - **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, **Optional**): Any other feedback you'd like to share?

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your answers.
- If you're unsure about any question, try to answer it to the best of your ability.
- If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
