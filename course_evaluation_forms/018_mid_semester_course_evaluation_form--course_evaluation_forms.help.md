# Mid Semester Course Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather student feedback on various aspects of the course and provide instructors with actionable information for mid-semester course adjustments.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each field carefully and provide honest feedback.
2. Answer each question thoroughly and provide specific details.
3. Save your progress and come back to complete the form at your convenience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Feedback (id: 1)**: Provide general feedback about your experience in the course so far. Be as specific as possible.
* **Learning Experience (id: 2)**: Rate your overall learning experience in this course, using the provided ratings system (1-Star to 5-Star).
* **Timely Course Improvement (id: 3)**: Indicate whether you feel that the course is improving or not. Choose 'True' if you feel the course is improving and 'False' if not.
* **Midterm Assessment (id: 4)**: Provide your grade in the course so far, if applicable.
* **Email Notification (id: 5)**: If you would like to receive email updates about course announcements, events, or important information, select this option.
* **How can we improve the course (id: 6)**: Provide specific ideas on how the course can be improved. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Instructor Support (id: 7)**: Rate the support provided by the instructor. Choose from the options: Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Course Format (id: 8)**: Choose the format in which you are currently taking the course: Online, In-person, or Hybrid.
* **Overall Experience (id: 9)**: Rate your overall experience in the course, using the provided rating system (1-Star to 5-Star).
* **Course Materials (id: 10)**: Rate the course materials provided, choosing from Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Instructor Presence (id: 11)**: Rate the instructor's presence in the classroom, choosing from Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Peer Presence (id: 12)**: Rate the presence and engagement of your peers in the classroom, choosing from Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Time Management (id: 13)**: Rate how well you feel the course is managed in terms of time, choosing from Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Course Structure (id: 14)**: Rate the course structure and organization, choosing from Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Overall Learning Outcome (id: 15)**: Rate your learning outcome in the course, choosing from Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Future Course Planning (id: 16)**: Rate your confidence in future course planning, choosing from Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Additional Comments (id: 17)**: Leave any additional comments or feedback about the course.

Note: The completion of this form is optional, but it will provide valuable insights to instructors to make informed decisions about the course.
