# E Learning Session Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback about the E Learning session, including ratings and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit Feedback" button on the next page to open the form.
2. Review each page of the form, which will guide you through the different sections.
3. Answer the questions as honestly and thoroughly as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Session Feedback
* **Session Feedback**: This page allows you to provide any comments or notes about the session. It is not mandatory, but any feedback you provide will be helpful for us to improve the session.

### Session Rating
* **Session Rating**: This is a  scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is "Very Poor" and 5 is "Excellent". Please rate the session based on your experience.

### Session Satisfaction
* **Session Satisfaction**: This is a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is "Very Dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied". Please rate how satisfied you are with the session.

### Session Suggestions
* **Session Suggestions**: Here, you can provide any suggestions or ideas for improvement for the session. This could be anything from changing the format or content to new features or tools.

### Course Evaluation
* **Course Evaluation**: This is a dropdown menu with four options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, and Option 4. Please select the option that best describes your evaluation of the course.

### Overall Satisfaction
* **Overall Satisfaction**: This is a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is "Very Dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied". Please rate your overall satisfaction with the session.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
* You can skip any section if you don't have anything to add.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us.
