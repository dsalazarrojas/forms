# Agility Foundations Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from course participants about their experience with the Agility Foundations course. The collected feedback will help to improve future iterations of the course and provide valuable insights to course instructors and administrators.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with your honest feedback.
2. Be as detailed as possible in your responses.
3. Make sure to answer all required questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the course you attended.
* **Course Date** (`course_date`, text, required): Enter the date(s) when you attended the course.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the course instructor.
* **Overall Course Satisfaction** (`overall_course_satisfaction`, select_one, required): How satisfied are you with the course overall? Choose from: Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied.
* **Course Content Relevance** (`course_content_relevance`, select_one, required): How relevant was the course content to your needs? Choose from: Very Relevant, Relevant, Neutral, Irrelevant, Very Irrelevant.
* **Content Depth Appropriateness** (`content_depth_appropriateness`, select_one, required): Was the depth of content appropriate for your experience level? Choose from: Too Shallow, Appropriate, Too Deep.
* **Instructor Effectiveness** (`instructor_effectiveness`, select_one, required): How effective was the instructor in delivering the content? Choose from: Very Effective, Effective, Neutral, Ineffective, Very Ineffective.
* **Knowledge Gained** (`knowledge_gained`, select_one, required): How much new knowledge did you gain from the course? Choose from: A lot, Some, Little, None.
* **Practical Applicability** (`practical_applicability`, select_one, required): How applicable is the course content to your work situation? Choose from: Very Applicable, Applicable, Somewhat Applicable, Not Very Applicable, Not Applicable.
* **Confidence Increase** (`confidence_increase`, select_one, required): Did the course increase your confidence in applying agility concepts? Choose from: Significantly Increased, Increased, No Change, Decreased, Significantly Decreased.
* **Course Materials Quality** (`course_materials_quality`, select_one, required): How would you rate the quality of the course materials? Choose from: Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, Poor.
* **Course Structure and Organization** (`course_structure_organization`, select_one, required): How well was the course structured and organized? Choose from: Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, Poor.
* **Recommendation Likelihood** (`recommendation_likelihood`, select_one, required): How likely are you to recommend this course to colleagues? Choose from: Very Likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, Very Unlikely.
* **Future Course Interest** (`future_course_interest`, select_one, required): Would you be interested in taking advanced agility courses? Choose from: Yes, No, Maybe.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required): Any other comments or feedback about the course.
* **Positive Aspects** (`positive_aspects`, text, required): What did you like most about the course.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, required): What aspects of the course could be improved.
* **Additional Topics Desired** (`additional_topics_desired`, text, required): What additional topics would you like to see covered.
* **Recommendation** (Note: This field does not exist in the form schema but was added to match the form structure) 

## Tips

* Please be honest and detailed in your responses.
* If you have any comments or suggestions, please feel free to add them.
* If you have any questions or need help, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
