# Training Evaluation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from trainees on their experience with the training program. Your input will help us understand your experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form in its entirety.
2.  For multiple-choice questions, select the option that best represents your opinion.
3.  For free-form text fields, provide detailed feedback on the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Program name
*   **Program name** (`program_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the program you participated in.

### Program date
*   **Program date** (`program_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you participated in the program.

### Program length
*   **Program length** (`program_length`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of days/hours you participated in the program.

### Evaluation score
*   **Evaluation score** (`evaluation_score`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your overall evaluation of the program (1-5).

### Evaluation comment
*   **Evaluation comment** (`evaluation_comment`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional feedback on the program.

### How satisfied are you with the program?
*   **How satisfied are you with the program?** (`program_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the program (1-5).

### What area(s) would you suggest for improvement?
*   **What area(s) for improvement?** (`program_improvement_area`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all areas you think the program could be improved.

### Would you recommend this program to others?
*   **Would you recommend this program to others?** (`program_recommendation`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your willingness to recommend the program to others (1-5).

### Email
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for further questions.

### Phone number
*   **Phone number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted.

This help guide should be enough for users to complete the form accurately and effectively. However, please let me know if you would like me to add anything else.
