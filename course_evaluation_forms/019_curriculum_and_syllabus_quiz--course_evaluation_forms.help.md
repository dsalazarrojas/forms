# Curriculum And Syllabus Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Curriculum and Syllabus Quiz is designed to gather feedback and input on the structure, content, and effectiveness of a course's curriculum. The purpose of this form is to help instructors and staff evaluate and improve the curriculum.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review the course's curriculum and syllabus before filling out the form.
2.  Answer each section and question based on your knowledge and experience with the course.
3.  Be as detailed and specific as possible in your answers.
4.  Submit your completed form for review and evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **introduction** (1, text): Please provide a brief introduction to your feedback. Introduce yourself and provide some background on your experience with the course.
* **section_1** (2, select_multiple): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the section 1 meets your expectations. You can select multiple options.
* **question_1** (3, text): Answer this question based on your experience with the section 1. Be as specific as possible.
* **section_2** (5, select_multiple): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the section 2 meets your expectations. You can select multiple options.
* **question_3** (6, text): Answer this question based on your experience with the section 2. Be as specific as possible.
* **question_4** (7, text): Answer this question based on your experience with the section 2. Be as specific as possible.
* **conclusion** (8, text): Provide a brief conclusion on your overall experience with the course's curriculum and syllabus.
* **evaluation** (9, time): Indicate how long you spent on the evaluation.
* **submission** (10, text): Enter your name or contact information for submission.
* **feedback** (11, note): Provide any additional feedback or comments.
* **email** (12, email): Provide your email address for further communication.

This guide should help you complete the form accurately and provide valuable feedback and input on the course's curriculum and syllabus.
