# well_inspection_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a well inspection report, which is essential for facility managers and maintenance personnel to assess the condition and maintenance status of a well.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the well name in the "well_name" field.
2. Enter the date of the inspection in the "date_visited" field.
3. Measure and record the water level in the "water_level" field.
4. Record the flow measurement in the "flow_measurement" field.
5. Describe the inspection condition of the well in the "inspection_condition" field.
6. Add any recommended work or repairs in the "recommended_work" field.
7. Attach any photos or documents related to the well in the "photo_attachment_1" and "photo_attachment_2" fields.
8. Enter the name and contact information of the facility manager in the "facility_manager" field.
9. Sign and date the form in the "inspector_signature" field with your name in "inspector_name", "inspector_company", and "inspector_phone".
10. Add any notes or comments about the inspection in the "notes", "comments", and "recommended_reading" fields.
11. Describe the location of the well in the "well_location" field.
12. Record the depth of the well in the "well_depth" field.
13. Assess the casing condition of the well in the "casing_condition" field.
14. Describe the type of screen installed in the "screen_type" field.
15. Record the size of the screen in the "screen_size" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **well_name**: Enter the name of the well being inspected.
* **date_visited**: Enter the date of the inspection in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **water_level**: Measure and record the current water level in the well.
* **flow_measurement**: Record the flow measurement of the well.
* **inspection_condition**: Describe the condition of the well, using Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **recommended_work**: Suggest any recommended work or repairs for the well.
* **photo_attachment_1**: Upload a photo or document related to the well.
* **photo_attachment_2**: Upload another photo or document related to the well.
* **facility_manager**: Enter the name and contact information of the facility manager.
* **inspector_signature**: Sign and date the form with your name, company, and phone number.
* **inspector_name**: Enter your name as the inspector.
* **inspector_company**: Enter your company name as the inspector.
* **inspector_phone**: Enter your phone number as the inspector.
* **notes**: Add any notes or comments about the inspection.
* **comments**: Add any comments about the inspection.
* **recommended_reading**: Record the recommended reading date.
* **notes_attachment_1**: Attach another document or photo related to the well.
* **notes_attachment_2**: Attach another document or photo related to the well.
* **inspection_date**: Record the date of the inspection.
* **well_location**: Describe the location of the well.
* **well_depth**: Record the depth of the well.
* **casing_condition**: Assess the casing condition of the well, using Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **well_screen**: Describe the type of screen installed in the well.
* **screen_type**: Describe the type of screen installed in the well.
* **screen_size**: Record the size of the screen.

## Tips

* Make sure to attach relevant photos or documents to support your inspection findings.
* Clearly describe the condition of the well and suggest recommended work or repairs.
* Ensure that the facility manager's information is accurate and up-to-date. 
* Sign and date the form to authenticate the inspection. 

Note: The fields marked as "number" are expected to be numeric values, while fields marked as "date" are expected to be in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
