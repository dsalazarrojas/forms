# IT Room Weekly Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IT Room Weekly Inspection Checklist is a form designed to help IT staff inspect and report on the condition of the IT room on a weekly basis. This form is used to ensure that the IT room is in good condition, safe, and secure. The form helps identify any issues or concerns that need to be addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the inspector's name from the dropdown menu to identify who performed the inspection.
2.  Enter the date of the inspection.
3.  Evaluate the condition of the equipment and select whether it is Good, Fair, or Poor.
4.  Evaluate the cleanliness of the room and select whether it is Good, Fair, or Poor.
5.  Enter the current temperature of the room (if available).
6.  Check for any security issues and describe them in the text field.
7.  Provide details about the condition of the equipment (if any issues were found).
8.  Provide details about the cleanliness of the room (if any issues were found).
9.  Provide details about any security issues that were found.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Inspector Details**
*   `Inspector Details` (select_one): Select your name from the dropdown menu. If you are not on the list, please choose `Other` and provide your name in the text field below.
*   `Inspector Name` (text): Enter your name if you are not on the list.

### 2. **Date of Inspection**
*   `Date of Inspection` (date): Enter the date on which you performed the inspection.

### 3. **Equipment Condition**
*   `Equipment Condition` (select_multiple): Evaluate the condition of all equipment and select Good, Fair, or Poor.

### 4. **Cleanliness**
*   `Cleanliness` (select_multiple): Evaluate the cleanliness of the room and select Good, Fair, or Poor.

### 5. **Temperature**
*   `Temperature` (number): Enter the current temperature of the room (if available).

### 6. **Security Issues**
*   `Security Issues` (note): Check for any security issues and describe them in this field.

### 7. **Equipment Condition Details**
*   `Equipment Condition Details` (text): Provide details about the condition of any equipment that needs attention.

### 8. **Cleanliness Details**
*   `Cleanliness Details` (text): Provide details about any cleanliness issues that need attention.

### 9. **Temperature Details**
*   `Temperature Details` (text): Provide details about any temperature-related issues or concerns.

### 10. **Security Issues Details**
*   `Security Issues Details` (text): Provide details about any security issues that need attention.
