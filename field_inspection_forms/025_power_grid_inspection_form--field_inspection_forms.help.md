# Power Grid Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Power Grid Inspection Form is used to collect data about the condition of a power grid. This information helps maintenance personnel identify potential issues and plan maintenance activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you are familiar with the grid's layout and components before filling out this form.
2. Fill out the form for each asset as you inspect it.
3. Use the "findings" field to document any issues you encounter.
4. Take photos of any notable or problematic areas, and upload them using the "photos" field.
5. If follow-up tasks are required for any assets, select "Yes" in the "follow_up_tasks" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **grid_asset** (text): Enter the name or identifier of the asset you are inspecting.
* **findings** (text): Document any issues or problems you encounter. Be as specific as possible, including details about the asset and its condition.
* **photos** (select multiple): Upload photos of any notable or problematic areas. This can be useful for further inspection or reference.
* **follow_up_tasks** (select multiple): Select "Yes" if follow-up tasks are required for this asset. This will help maintenance personnel plan future activities.
* **maintenance_schedule** (select multiple): Select "Yes" if maintenance is required or scheduled for this asset. This will help maintenance personnel plan future activities.

## Tips
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required information is included.
* If you are unsure about any field or its purpose, please contact the maintenance team for clarification.
* This form is intended to be used as a quick reference guide, please do not hesitate to add additional comments or notes if needed.
