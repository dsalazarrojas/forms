# Snow Conditions Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Snow Conditions Report form is designed to gather information about the current snow conditions on a specific location. This form helps you to report and record the depth and coverage of snow on the ground, as well as any other relevant comments or observations. This information is essential for maintaining accurate records and making informed decisions regarding snow removal, maintenance, or other winter-related operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Snow Conditions Report form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the report (if required).
2. Report the depth of snow on the ground by entering a numerical value in the `snow_depth` field.
3. Describe the coverage of snow on the ground in the `snow_coverage` field.
4. Provide details about the conditions of the ground surface in the `ground_surface_conditions` field.
5. Describe any conditions on the road surface in the `road_surface_conditions` field.
6. Add any additional comments or observations about the snow conditions in the `comments` field.
7. Report any snowfall information you have in the `snowfall` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Snow Depth (snow_depth)**: Enter a numerical value indicating the depth of snow on the ground.
* **Snow Coverage (snow_coverage)**: Describe the coverage of snow on the ground, e.g., light dusting, moderate, heavy, etc.
* **Ground Surface Conditions (ground_surface_conditions)**: Provide details about the conditions of the ground surface, e.g., icy, slushy, bare, etc.
* **Road Surface Conditions (road_surface_conditions)**: Describe any conditions on the road surface, e.g., slippery, hazardous, etc.
* **Comments (comments)**: Add any additional comments or observations about the snow conditions.
* **Snowfall (snowfall)**: Report any snowfall information you have, e.g., amount of snowfall, duration, etc.
* **Snow Conditions Report Title (title)**: Enter a brief title of the report (if required).
