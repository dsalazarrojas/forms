# Valve Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to guide inspectors through the process of evaluating and documenting valve conditions during plant inspections. By filling out this form accurately, you can help maintain a high level of plant safety and performance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by selecting the valve's condition from the options provided.
2. Enter the date of the inspection, in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
3. Provide the name of the inspector performing the inspection.
4. Enter any relevant notes or remarks about the valve in the 'Notes' field.
5. In the 'Photos' field, select 'Yes' if you took photos of the valve during the inspection, and 'No' otherwise.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Valve Condition** (`valve_condition`, text, required/optional): Describe the condition of the valve, e.g., good, bad, needs repair, etc.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, required/optional): Record the date of the inspection, in 'YYYY-MM-DD' format.
* **Inspector's Name** (`inspector_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person conducting the inspection.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any notable remarks or observations about the valve.
* **Photos** (`photos`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select 'Yes' to indicate that photos were taken during the inspection, or 'No' if not.
