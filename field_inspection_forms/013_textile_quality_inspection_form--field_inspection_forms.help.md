# Textile Quality Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to conduct a textile quality inspection to assess the quality of textiles provided by suppliers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the supplier's name and contact information in the "Supplier Name" and "Supplier Contact" fields, respectively.
2. Provide a brief description of the textile material in the "Material Description" field.
3. Describe the textile's characteristics, such as its texture, weight, and color, in the "Textile Characteristics" field.
4. Evaluate the quality of the textile by selecting one of the three options in the "Quality Outcomes" field.
5. Choose all applicable quality standards met by the textile in the "Standard Reviews" field.
6. Provide any additional comments about the inspection in the "Comments" field.
7. Enter the date and time of the inspection using the "Date of Inspection" and "Time of Inspection" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the supplier providing the textile.
* **Supplier Contact** (`supplier_contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact information for the supplier, including phone number and email.
* **Material Description** (`material_description`, text, optional): Describe the type of material used in the textile.
* **Textile Characteristics** (`textile_characteristics`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the textile's features, such as texture, weight, and color.
* **Quality Outcomes** (`quality_outcomes`, select_one, optional): Evaluate the quality of the textile as:
	+ Excellent: The textile meets the highest quality standards.
	+ Acceptable: The textile meets the minimum quality standards.
	+ Unacceptable: The textile does not meet the required quality standards.
* **Standard Reviews** (`standard_reviews`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all applicable quality standards met by the textile.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or observations about the inspection.
* **Date of Inspection** (`date_of_inspection`, date, optional): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Time of Inspection** (`time_of_inspection`, time, optional): Enter the time of the inspection.

Note: Fields marked as "optional" are not required, but it's recommended to fill them in for a more comprehensive inspection report.
