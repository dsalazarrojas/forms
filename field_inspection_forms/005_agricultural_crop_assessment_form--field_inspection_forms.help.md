# Agricultural Crop Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from a field inspector about their assessment of an agricultural crop. The collected data will be used to inform decisions on crop management and potential interventions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your name as the assessor performing the assessment.
2. Choose a date for the assessment.
3. Enter the farm location or provide GPS coordinates.
4. Specify the primary crop type observed during the assessment.
5. Determine the growth stage of the crop.
6. Provide any observations about pest issues, if any.
7. Describe any disease symptoms observed, if any.
8. Estimate the canopy coverage percentage of the crop.
9. Calculate the plant density per square meter of the crop.
10. Suggest any recommended actions for the crop's management.
11. Provide a date for the next recommended assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessor Name**: Enter your name as the person performing the assessment.
* **Assessment Date**: Enter the date on which the assessment was performed.
* **Farm Location**: Enter the farm's name or provide the GPS coordinates.
* **Crop Type**: Specify the primary crop type observed during the assessment.
* **Growth Stage**: Choose the growth stage of the crop from the following options: Seedling, Vegetative, Flowering, Fruiting, or Maturity.
* **Pest Observations**: If you observed any pest issues, describe them briefly.
* **Disease Symptoms**: If you observed any disease symptoms, describe them briefly.
* **Canopy Coverage Percentage**: Estimate the canopy coverage percentage of the crop.
* **Plant Density**: Enter the approximate plant density per square meter of the crop.
* **Recommended Action**: Suggest any recommended actions for the crop's management, such as pesticides or fertilizers.
* **Follow-up Date**: Enter a date for the next recommended assessment.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field or have questions, refer to your organization's guidelines or consult with your supervisor.
* This form should only be filled out by individuals who have performed a physical assessment of the crop in the field.
