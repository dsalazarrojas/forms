# Flag Pole Installation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the installation of a flag pole, ensuring that all necessary information is collected and recorded accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the flag pole installation in the "Installation Date" field.
2. Enter the location of the installation in the "installation_location" field.
3. Provide details about the flag pole material in the "Pole Material" field.
4. Enter the height of the flag pole in the "Flag Pole Height" field.
5. Describe the type of ground on which the flag pole is installed in the "Ground Type" field.
6. Record the condition of the flag pole after installation in the "Pole Condition" field.
7. Check the "Safety Steps" field and select all relevant safety measures taken during the installation.
8. Enter your signature in the "Inspector Signature" field.
9. Enter your name in the "Inspector Name" field.
10. Enter your role in the relation to the installation in the "Inspector Role" field.
11. Record the date and time of completion in the "Date Signed" and "Time Signed" fields.
12. Enter the job number assigned to the installation in the "Job Number" field.
13. Enter the name of the customer associated with the installation in the "Customer Name" field.
14. Enter the contact information of the customer in the "Customer Contact" field.
15. Record the location of the job site in the "Job Site" field.
16. Enter the type of flag pole installed in the "Flag Pole Type" field.
17. Enter the type of anchor used in the "Anchor Type" field.
18. Enter the size of the anchor in the "Anchor Size" field.
19. Provide any additional details about the installation in the "Installation Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Installation Date** (`installation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the flag pole was installed.
* **Installation Location** (`installation_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the flag pole was installed.
* **Pole Material** (`pole_material`, text, optional): Provide details about the material used for the flag pole.
* **Flag Pole Height** (`flag_pole_height`, number, optional): Enter the height of the flag pole.
* **Ground Type** (`ground_type`, text, optional): Describe the type of ground on which the flag pole is installed.
* **Pole Condition** (`pole_condition`, text, optional): Record the condition of the flag pole after installation.
* **Safety Steps** (`safety_steps`, select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant safety measures taken during the installation.
* **Inspector Signature** (`inspector_signature`, text, optional): Enter your signature as the inspector.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the inspector.
* **Inspector Role** (`inspector_role`, text, optional): Enter your role in relation to the installation.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, optional): Record the date on which you signed the form.
* **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, time, optional): Record the time on which you signed the form.
* **Job Number** (`job_number`, text, optional): Enter the job number assigned to the installation.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the customer associated with the installation.
* **Customer Contact** (`customer_contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact information of the customer.
* **Job Site** (`job_site`, text, optional): Record the location of the job site.
* **Flag Pole Type** (`flag_pole_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of flag pole installed.
* **Anchor Type** (`anchor_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of anchor used.
* **Anchor Size** (`anchor_size`, text, optional): Enter the size of the anchor.
* **Installation Details** (`installation_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional details about the installation.
