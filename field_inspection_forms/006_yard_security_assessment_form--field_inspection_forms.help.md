# Yard Security Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Yard Security Assessment Form is a standardized tool used to evaluate the security level of a yard in a commercial or industrial setting. This form helps identify areas of improvement and ensures consistency in security assessments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the yard's security risk level and select the relevant option from the "Security Risk Level" dropdown menu.
2. Describe the risk level in detail in the "Risk Level Description" field.
3. Identify any risk factors that contribute to the current security level.
4. Select any control measures that are currently in place to mitigate risks.
5. Record the frequency of risk level in the "Risk Level Frequency" field (if applicable).
6. Rate the security risk level using the "Security Risk Rating" field (if applicable).
7. Provide any recommended actions for improvement in the "Recommended Action" field.
8. Add any additional comments or observations in the "Additional Comments" field.
9. Provide your name, company, position, department, and contact information as the reviewer.
10. Date and sign the review, and provide any comments or observations as the reviewer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Security Risk Level** (`Security Risk Level`, select_one): Select the current security risk level of the yard from the provided options.
- **Risk Level Description** (`Risk Level Description`, text): Describe the reasons behind the selected risk level in detail.
- **Risk Factors** (`Risk Factors`, select_multiple): Identify any risk factors that contribute to the current security level (Yes/No).
- **Control Measures** (`Control Measures`, select_multiple): List any control measures that are currently in place to mitigate risks (Yes/No).
- **Risk Level Frequency** (`Risk Level Frequency`, date): Record the frequency of risk level (if applicable).
- **Security Risk Rating** (`Security Risk Rating`, number): Rate the security risk level (if applicable).
- **Recommended Action** (`Recommended Action`, note): Suggest any recommended actions for improvement.
- **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, note): Add any additional comments or observations.
- **Reviewer** (`Reviewer`, text): Enter your name as the reviewer.
- **Reviewer Company** (`Reviewer Company`, text): Enter your company name.
- **Reviewer Position** (`Reviewer Position`, text): Enter your position or title.
- **Reviewer Department** (`Reviewer Department`, text): Enter your department or division.
- **Reviewer Date** (`Reviewer Date`, date): Date of review.
- **Reviewer Signature** (`Reviewer Signature`, text): Sign your name as the reviewer.
- **Reviewer Email** (`Reviewer Email`, email): Enter your email address.
- **Reviewer Phone** (`Reviewer Phone`, text): Enter your phone number.
- **Reviewer Title** (`Reviewer Title`, text): Enter your title or position.
- **Reviewer ID** (`Reviewer ID`, text): Enter your ID number or badge number.
- **Reviewer Name** (`Reviewer Name`, text): Enter your name as the reviewer.
- **Reviewer Company Name** (`Reviewer Company Name`, text): Enter your company name.
- **Reviewer Comments** (`Reviewer Comments`, text): Add any additional comments or observations as the reviewer.
- **Reviewer Comments 2** (`Reviewer Comments 2`, text): Add any additional comments or observations as the reviewer.
- **Reviewer Comments 3** (`Reviewer Comments 3`, text): Add any additional comments or observations as the reviewer.
- **Reviewer Comments 4** (`Reviewer Comments 4`, text): Add any additional comments or observations as the reviewer.

## Tips
Remember to fill out the form accurately and honestly, considering all relevant factors. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed. If you're unsure about any field, please refer to the form's instructions or consult the relevant department or expert.
