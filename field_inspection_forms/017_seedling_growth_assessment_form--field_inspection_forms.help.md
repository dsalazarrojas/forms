## How To Complete This Form
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* *Seedling Growth Assessment Form 1* (id: 1, name: seedling_growth_assessment_form_1, type: text, required: false)
	+ A text field for any additional comments or information about the seedling.
* *Seedling Growth Data Date* (id: 2, name: seedling_growth_form_data_date, type: date, required: false)
	+ Enter the date when the seedling's growth data was collected.
* *Seedling Growth Data Time* (id: 3, name: seedling_growth_form_data_time, type: time, required: false)
	+ Record the time when the seedling's growth data was collected.
* *Seedling Growth Nutrient Form* (id: 4, name: seedling_growth_nutrient_form, type: select_one, required: false)
	+ Choose the relevant nutrient level for the seedling.
* *Seedling Growth pH Form* (id: 5, name: seedling_growth_ph_form, type: number, required: false)
	+ Enter the seedling's pH level.
* *Seedling Growth Moisture Form* (id: 6, name: seedling_growth_moisture_form, type: number, required: false)
	+ Indicate the seedling's moisture level.
* *Seedling Growth Temperature Form* (id: 7, name: seedling_growth_temperature_form, type: select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Choose the relevant temperature condition for the seedling.
* *Seedling Growth Soil Moisture Form* (id: 8, name: seedling_growth_soil_moisture_form, type: select_one, required: false)

* *Seedling Growth Notes* (id: 9, name: seedling_growth_notes, type: note, required: false)
