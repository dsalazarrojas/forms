# Daily Concrete Pour Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Concrete Pour Log is a form used to document and track concrete pours. It is intended to be filled out by inspectors during or after a concrete pour, and will be used to record and report on the pour's completion, status, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the concrete pour details, including the date and time of the pour, project location, and inspector's name.
2. Select the appropriate status for the pour, such as Complete, Incomplete, or Pending.
3. Enter any comments or notes about the pour, if applicable.
4. Select the concrete type and curing method, if applicable.
5. Enter the cure time and temperature, if applicable.
6. Select the project status and status history, if applicable.
7. Enter the date and time the log was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Concrete Pour Details**: Enter the date and time of the concrete pour.
* **Concrete Curing**: Enter any information about the concrete curing process, including whether it was completed or not.
* **Project Location**: Select the location where the concrete was poured.
* **Date Poured**: Enter the date the concrete was poured.
* **Time Poured**: Enter the time the concrete was poured.
* **Concrete Pour Comments**: Enter any comments or notes about the pour.
* **Inspector Name**: Enter your name as the inspector who filled out the form.
* **Inspector Signature**: Enter your signature as the inspector who filled out the form.
* **Concrete Pour Log Status**: Select the status of the pour, such as Complete, Incomplete, or Pending.
* **Project ID**: Enter the ID number for the project.
* **User ID**: Enter your user ID number.
* **Concrete Pour Log Comments**: Enter any comments or notes about the pour log.
* **Concrete Type**: Select whether the pour was for a Yes or No concrete type.
* **Concrete Cure Time**: Enter the time the concrete was cured.
* **Concrete Cure Temperature**: Enter the temperature at which the concrete was cured.
* **Concrete Cure Method**: Select whether the cure was done using the Yes or No method.
* **Concrete Cure Age**: Enter the age of the cure.
* **Inspector Notes**: Enter any notes or comments from the inspector.
* **Concrete Pour Log Attachments**: Enter any attachments or files related to the pour log.
* **Project Status**: Select the status of the project, such as Active or Inactive.
* **Project Status History**: Select the status history of the project, such as Active or Inactive.
* **User Status**: Select the status of the user, such as Active or Inactive.
* **Concrete Pour Log Date Submitted**: Enter the date and time the log was submitted.
* **Concrete Pour Log Time Submitted**: Enter the time the log was submitted.

Note: Some fields may not be applicable to every concrete pour, and some fields may have additional options or requirements not listed here. Always follow your organization's specific procedures and guidelines for completing this form.
