# Underground Insulation Resistance Testing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record data related to underground insulation resistance testing. It captures key information about the test location, test conditions, and results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Test Location** where the underground insulation resistance testing was conducted. This field is not required.
2. Select the **Test Date** when the testing was performed. This field is not required.
3. Record the **Test Time** when the testing was conducted. This field is not required.
4. Choose the **Insulation Type** being tested. This field is not required.
5. Enter the **Insulation Thickness** of the material being tested. This field is not required.
6. Select the **Test Temperature** and **Test Voltage** for the testing. These fields are not required.
7. Record the **Insulation Resistance** value for the testing. This field is not required.
8. Add any additional **Notes** about the testing process. This field is not required.
9. Sign and date your **Technician Signature** to confirm that the testing was conducted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Test Location**: Enter the location where the testing was conducted.
* **Test Date**: Enter the date when the testing was performed.
* **Test Time**: Enter the time when the testing was conducted.
* **Insulation Type**: Choose the type of insulation being tested.
* **Insulation Thickness**: Enter the thickness of the insulation material.
* **Test Temperature**: Select the temperature at which the testing was performed.
* **Test Voltage**: Select the voltage at which the testing was performed.
* **Insulation Resistance**: Record the resistance value of the insulation material.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or details about the testing process.
* **Technician Signature**: Sign and date your signature to confirm that the testing was conducted.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields with a "required: false" designation are only filled out if necessary.
- Review the form for accuracy before submitting it.
- Ensure that the data entered is consistent and accurate.
