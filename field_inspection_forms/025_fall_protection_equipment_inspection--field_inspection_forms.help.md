# Field Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for field inspections to gather information about a specific field or area of interest.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **title** (title, required): Enter the title of the field being inspected.
* **text without colons** (text without colons, optional, on 'snake_case_string' page): Enter a brief description or comment about the field.
* **text without colons** (text without colons, optional, on 'snake_case_string' page): Provide any additional information or comments about the field.
## Tips
