# Nut Tree Compliance Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Nut Tree Compliance Checklist is designed to help orchard inspectors collect and store inspection data, manage ongoing orchard operations, and maintain compliance with relevant regulations and standards.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the orchard inspection procedures and guidelines before filling out the form.
2.  Complete each page of the form, answering questions and filling in relevant information according to the page's specific requirements.
3.  Make sure to fill in all required fields and provide clear, concise responses.
