# Fishery Pond Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fishery Pond Inspection Form is a tool to help fishery professionals gather information about the condition of a fishery pond. The form can be used to record and report on water conditions, temperature, life forms, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your general information, such as your name and any other relevant details.
2. Describe the current conditions of the pond, including any notable features or issues.
3. Select the correct water quality option from the dropdown list.
4. Enter the current water temperature in °C.
5. Estimate the number of fish in the pond.
6. Select the current water level (High or Low).
7. Choose any life forms that you have observed in the pond.
8. Indicate whether you have observed any fish disease.
9. Add any additional notes about your observations.
10. Enter your email address if you would like to receive notifications about new updates or inspections.
11. Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted directly.
12. Fill in the date of the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`General Information`, text, required/optional): Fill in your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Pond Conditions** (`Pond Conditions`, text, required/optional): Enter a detailed description of the pond conditions.
* **Water Quality** (`Water Quality`, select_one, required/optional): Select one of the options to indicate the current water quality.
* **Water Temperature (°C)** (`Water Temperature (°C)`, number, required/optional): Enter the current water temperature in °C.
* **Fish Count** (`Fish Count`, number, required/optional): Estimate the number of fish in the pond.
* **Water Level** (`Water Level`, select_one, required/optional): Select High or Low to indicate the current water level.
* **Pond Life** (`Pond Life`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose any life forms you have observed in the pond.
* **Fish Disease** (`Fish Disease`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether you have observed any fish disease.
* **Note** (`Note`, text, required/optional): Add any additional notes about your observations.
* **Email Notify** (`Email Notify`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address if you would like to receive notifications about new updates or inspections.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted directly.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required/optional): Fill in the date of the inspection.
