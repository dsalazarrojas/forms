# Retail Visit Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Visit Report form is designed to document and track information about visits to retail stores, ensuring consistency and accuracy in the evaluation process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form for each store visit, using the corresponding store ID and name.
2. Enter the visit date and time.
3. Record the store's rating (1-5) and provide any relevant notes.
4. Specify if follow-up actions are needed for the store manager or inspector.
5. Enter any additional notes for the store manager or inspector.
6. Indicate if follow-up actions are needed for the store's customer service or if any notes are available.
7. Enter the store's address, contact information, and social media handles.
8. Complete any other relevant fields as needed, depending on the specific visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store ID** (`store_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter the store's unique ID number.
* **Store Name** (`store_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the store's name.
* **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the visit.
* **Visit Time** (`visit_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the visit.
* **Store Rating** (`store_rating`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the store from 1 to 5.
* **Store Notes** (`store_notes`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the store.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Specify if follow-up actions are needed for the store manager or inspector.
* **Next Visit Date** (`next_visit_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the next visit.
* **Next Visit Time** (`next_visit_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the next visit.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the person assigned to handle the store visit.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date and time the form was submitted.
* **Store Manager Notes** (`store_manager_notes`, `note`, required: false): Enter any notes from the store manager.
* **Store Manager Follow Up** (`store_manager_follow_up`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Specify if follow-up actions are needed for the store manager.
* **Store Inspector Notes** (`store_inspector_notes`, `note`, required: false): Enter any notes from the store inspector.
* **Store Inspector Follow Up** (`store_inspector_follow_up`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Specify if follow-up actions are needed for the store inspector.
* **Customer Service Notes** (`customer_service_notes`, `note`, required: false): Enter any notes from the store's customer service.
* **Store Address** (`store_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the store's address.
* **Store Contact** (`store_contact`, `text`, required: false): Enter the store's contact information.
* **Store Phone** (`store_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the store's phone number.
* **Store Email** (`store_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the store's email address.
* **Store Website** (`store_website`, `text`, required: false): Enter the store's website.
* **Store Facebook** (`store_facebook`, `text`, required: false): Enter the store's Facebook handle.
* **Store Twitter** (`store_twitter`, `text`, required: false): Enter the store's Twitter handle.
* **Store Instagram** (`store_instagram`, `text`, required: false): Enter the store's Instagram handle.

## Tips
- Make sure to only fill out the form for actual store visits.
- Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Avoid duplicating data entry for previous visits.
- Use clear and concise language in all notes and comments.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Review and update form fields regularly to reflect any changes in store information or visit protocols.
