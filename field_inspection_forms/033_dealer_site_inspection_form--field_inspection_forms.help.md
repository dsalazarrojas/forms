# Dealer Site Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dealer Site Inspection Form is a tool used to document site inspections of dealers' locations. It helps gather information about the site, the visit, and the findings, ensuring that all necessary data is collected accurately and consistently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information and materials before starting the inspection.
2. Select the correct site visit status (Active or Inactive) to accurately reflect the current state of the dealer's site.
3. Enter the visit ID, date, and time of the inspection.
4. Record the dealer's ID and site inspector's ID.
5. Provide a detailed description of the site visit, including any notable observations or action items.
6. Select whether any photos were taken during the inspection (Yes or No).
7. If photos were taken, provide a brief description of each.
8. Answer the following questions:
	* Was the site in compliance with regulations? (Yes or No)
	* Are there any action items that need to be addressed? (Yes or No)
	* Is the site inspection completed? (Yes or No)
9. Enter any additional notes about the site visit.
10. Attach any relevant documents or images to the form.
11. Sign and date the form, ensuring your inspector's signature is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Visit ID**: Enter the unique ID assigned to this site visit.
* **Site Visit Date**: Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Site Visit Time**: Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Dealer ID**: Enter the unique ID of the dealer being inspected.
* **Site Inspector ID**: Enter your unique ID as the site inspector.
* **Site Visit Description**: Provide a detailed description of the site visit, including notable observations or action items.
* **Photos Taken**: Select Yes or No to indicate whether photos were taken during the inspection.
* **Photos Description**: If photos were taken, enter a brief description of each photo.
* **Action Items**: Select Yes or No to indicate if there are any action items that need to be addressed.
* **Site Inspection Completed**: Select Yes or No to indicate if the site inspection is completed.
* **Site Visit Status**: Select Active or Inactive to reflect the current state of the dealer's site.
* **Inspector Signature**: Sign and date the form, ensuring your accurate inspector's signature.
* **Inspector Name**: Enter your name as the site inspector.
* **Inspector Title**: Enter your title as the site inspector.
* **Inspector Company**: Enter the name of the company you represent.
* **Inspector Contact**: Enter your contact information as the site inspector.
* **Dealer Contact**: Enter the contact information of the dealer being inspected.

This guide is designed to help you complete the form accurately and effectively. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or support team.
