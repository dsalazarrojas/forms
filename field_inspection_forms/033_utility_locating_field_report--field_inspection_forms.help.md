# Utility Locating Field Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for reporting on utility locating field activities. It is meant to provide a record of the inspection process and any issues discovered or noted during the inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with as much information as you have available. Make sure to include as much detail as possible about the locations you inspected.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Utility Locating Field Report** (1): This is the title of the form.
* **Project Name** (2): Enter the name of the project or job you are working on.
* **Site Address** (3): Enter the address of the location being inspected.
* **Location 1** (4), **Location 2** (5), **Location 3** (6), **Location 4** (7), **Location 5** (8), **Location 6** (9), **Location 7** (10), **Location 8** (11), **Location 9** (12), **Location 10** (13), **Location 11** (14), **Location 12** (15), **Location 16** (17), **Location 17** (18), **Location 18** (19), **Location 20** (21), **Location 21** (22), **Location 22** (23): These fields are for noting any locations you inspected during the field activity.
* **Notes** (26): Use this field to add any additional notes or comments about the inspection.
* **Location of Damage** (27): If you discovered any damage, enter the location of the damaged area.
* **Location of Buried Duct** (28): If you encountered any buried ducts, enter the location of the duct.
* **Photo** (29): If you took a photo during the inspection, enter the file name or location of the photo.
* **Assigned to** (30): Enter the name of the person responsible for this field activity.
* **Date Inspected** (31): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Time Inspected** (32): Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Assigned Tool** (33): Enter the name of the tool used for the inspection.
* **Tool Number** (34): Enter the number of the tool used.
* **Comments** (35): Use this field to add any additional comments about the inspection.
* **Submit** (36): Once you have completed the form, click this button to submit your report.

## Tips
* Make sure to include as much detail as possible when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or need clarification about a field, please contact the relevant personnel.
* This form is for reporting on utility locating field activities. If you are not sure what this form is for, please contact the relevant personnel for guidance.
