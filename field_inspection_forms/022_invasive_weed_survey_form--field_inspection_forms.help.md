# Invasive Weed Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about sightings of invasive weeds. This data will help in monitoring and managing invasive weed populations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required information for each field as accurately as possible.
2. If you have any doubts or concerns about a specific field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Species Name**: Enter the scientific name of the invasive weed species that was observed.
* **Sighting Date**: Enter the date you observed the invasive weed species, in the format mm/dd/yy.
* **Sighting Time**: Enter the time you observed the invasive weed species.
* **Location**: Enter the location where you observed the invasive weed species.
* **Sighting Description**: Provide a brief description of the sighting, including details about the species and its location.
* **Follow-up Status**: Select the current status of follow-up actions regarding this sighting:
	+ Active: Follow-up actions are ongoing or planned.
	+ Inactive: No follow-up actions are planned or needed.
* **Follow-up Notes**: Enter any additional notes or information about follow-up actions or next steps.
* **Follow-up Date**: Enter the date of the follow-up action, if applicable.
* **Follow-up Time**: Enter the time of the follow-up action, if applicable.
* **Species ID**: This field is not currently used in the form.
* **Priority Level**: Select the level of priority for this sighting:
	+ High: High priority due to severity or impact.
	+ Low: Low priority due to minor presence or impact.
	+ Medium: Average priority for average presence or impact.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information or comments about the sighting.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for further contact or follow-up.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for further contact or follow-up.
* **Follow-up Status Other**: Select an alternative follow-up status not listed above.

## Tips

* Be as accurate as possible when filling in the fields, as this information will help in effective management and follow-up of invasive weed sightings.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation above.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us.
