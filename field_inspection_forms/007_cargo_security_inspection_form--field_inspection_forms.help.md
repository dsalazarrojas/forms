## Cargo Security Inspection Form - Help Guide

This form is used to document security inspections of cargo during transportation. The purpose of this form is to ensure that all cargo is properly secured and inspected according to security protocols. This form should only be completed by certified inspectors and is a mandatory requirement for all cargo inspections.

### How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the required information for the inspection, including the inspection date, time, inspector's name, and ID number, location, and cargo details.
2.  Evaluate the cargo and document any findings in the "Inspection Findings" section.
3.  Determine if any discrepancies were found during the inspection.
4.  Document any discrepancies in the "Discrepancies Found" section and provide a detailed explanation in the "Discrepancy Details" section.
5.  Record the overall inspection result and provide any corrective actions if necessary.
6.  Sign and date the form to confirm completion.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Cargo Security Inspection Report**: This section is a brief overview of the inspection.
*   **Inspection ID Number**: A unique identifier for the inspection.
*   **Inspection Date**: The date of the inspection.
*   **Inspection Time**: The time of the inspection.
*   **Inspector Name**: The name of the certified inspector conducting the inspection.
*   **Inspector ID Number**: A badge or employee number for the inspector.
*   **Inspection Location**: The facility or site where the inspection took place.
*   **Cargo Information**: Additional details about the shipment, including origin point and destination.
*   **Shipment or Container Number**: A tracking number for the shipment or container.
*   **Type of Cargo**: A classification of the cargo (e.g. general freight, hazardous materials, etc.).
*   **Origin Point**: The point of origin of the cargo.
*   **Final Destination**: The destination of the cargo.
*   **Carrier Information**: Details about the transport company.
*   **Vehicle Information**: Additional details about the transport vehicle.
*   **Vehicle Type**: A classification of the transport vehicle.
*   **Vehicle Identification Number**: A license plate or unit number for the vehicle.
*   **Driver Name**: The name of the driver operating the vehicle.
*   **Driver License Number**: A verification of the driver's license.
*   **Security Inspection Checklist**: A section for documenting any security-related items during the inspection.
*   **Seal Integrity Check**: A classification of the seal condition.
*   **Seal Number**: The recorded seal identifier.
*   **Container or Vehicle Condition**: An assessment of the physical state of the cargo or vehicle.
*   **Cargo Securement Check**: A classification of the cargo's load stability.
*   **Documentation Verification**: A classification of the accuracy and completeness of the paperwork and manifests.
*   **Hazardous Materials Present**: An indicator of whether hazardous materials are present.
*   **Temperature Verification**: A classification of the temperature of temperature-controlled cargo.
*   **Temperature Reading**: The current temperature of the cargo.
*   **Inspection Findings**: A summary of any observations made during the inspection.
*   **Were Any Discrepancies Found**: A classification of whether any discrepancies were identified.
*   **Discrepancy Details**: A detailed explanation of any discrepancies found.
*   **Were Photos Taken**: A classification of whether photos were taken during the inspection.
*   **Overall Inspection Result**: A classification of the overall inspection result (e.g. pass or fail).
*   **Required Corrective Actions**: Any steps needed to comply with security regulations.
*   **Is Reinspection Required**: A classification of whether a follow-up inspection is necessary.
*   **Inspector Signature Date**: The date of completion of the inspection.
*   **Supervisor Review Required**: A classification of whether management review is needed.

Note: This help guide is for reference and should be reviewed and updated as necessary to reflect changes in the form and security protocols.
