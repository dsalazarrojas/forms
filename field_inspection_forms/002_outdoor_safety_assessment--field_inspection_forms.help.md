# Outdoor Safety Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information for an outdoor safety assessment, which is used to evaluate the safety conditions of a site or location.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions and guidelines provided in the Field-by-Field Explanation section.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspector Info**: Enter your name and contact information as the inspector.
  * (inspector_info, text, required)
* **Date and Time**: Enter the date and time of the inspection.
  * (date_and_time, date, required)
* **Site Info**: Enter a brief description of the site or location being assessed.
  * (site_info, text, required)
* **Hazards**: Select all hazards observed during the inspection.
  * (hazard_type, select_multiple, required)
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes regarding the site or location.
  * (notes, text, optional)
* **Inspector's Signature**: This field is not required, as it is primarily used for internal purposes.
  * (inspector_sign, note, optional)
* **Inspector's Name**: Enter your name as the inspector.
  * (inspector_name, text, required)
* **Inspector's Title**: Enter your title or position.
  * (inspector_title, text, required)
* **Inspector's Organization**: Enter the organization or company you represent.
  * (inspector_org, text, required)
* **Inspector's Email**: Enter your email address.
  * (inspector_email, email, required)
* **Inspector's Phone**: Enter your phone number.
  * (inspector_phone, text, required)
* **Inspector's Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes.
  * (inspector_comments, text, optional)
* **Location**: Enter the location or site being assessed.
  * (site_location, text, required)
* **Date of Visit**: Enter the date of the inspection.
  * (date_of_visit, date, required)
* **Time of Visit**: Enter the time of the inspection.
  * (time_of_visit, time, required)
* **Weather Conditions**: Enter any comments regarding the weather conditions.
  * (weather_conditions, text, optional)
* **Lighting Conditions**: Enter any comments regarding the lighting conditions.
  * (lighting_conditions, text, optional)
* **Temperature Conditions**: Enter any comments regarding the temperature conditions.
  * (temperature_conditions, text, optional)
* **Wind Conditions**: Enter any comments regarding the wind conditions.
  * (wind_conditions, text, optional)
* **Visibility**: Enter any comments regarding the visibility.
  * (visibility, text, optional)
* **Weather Comments**: Enter any additional comments regarding the weather.
  * (weather_comments, text, optional)
* **Hazards Observed**: Select 'Yes' if hazards were observed during the inspection.
  * (hazards_observed, select_one, required)
* **Submit**: Click the 'Submit' button to save the information.
  * (submit, text, optional)
