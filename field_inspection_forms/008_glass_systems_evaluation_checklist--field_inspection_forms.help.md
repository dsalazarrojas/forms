# Glass Systems Evaluation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps you assess the condition and maintenance needs of a glass system, ensuring it meets safety and performance standards.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by reviewing each field carefully and answer all questions accurately to provide a comprehensive evaluation.
2. Complete all the fields, even if you're unsure about certain aspects of the glass system.
3. If you need to add more information or notes, use the "Safety Equipment Notes" field for that purpose.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check Glass Condition**: This field asks you to assess the overall condition of the glass system. Please select the option that best describes its state, such as "Good," "Fair," "Poor," or "Severely Damaged."
* **Assess Hardware Performance**: In this field, describe any issues with the glass system's hardware, such as scratches, cracks, or other noticeable defects.
* **Document Maintenance Needs**: Provide any maintenance needs or concerns you've identified for the glass system, such as replacing broken parts or inspecting for damage.
* **Safety Equipment Present**: Check the boxes for the safety equipment that is present in the glass system, such as fire extinguishers, fire alarms, or fire sprinkler systems.
* **Safety Equipment Notes**: If you need to add more information or notes about safety equipment, use this field to provide additional details.
* **Glass Type**: Select the type of glass used in the system, which could be Insulated, Non-Insulated, Laminated, or Coated.
* **Glass Condition**: Evaluate the condition of the glass in the system, selecting from the options "Good," "Fair," "Poor," "Severely Damaged," or "Not Tested."
* **Hardware Performance**: Describe any issues with the hardware of the glass system, such as performance problems or maintenance concerns.
* **Maintenance Actions**: Check the boxes for the actions you've taken or plan to take on the glass system, such as replacement, inspection, or repair.
* **Maintenance Frequency**: Select how often the glass system requires maintenance, including options like daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, or not specified.
* **Last Maintenance Date**: If available, provide the date of the last maintenance action for the glass system.
