# Greenhouse Crop Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Greenhouse Crop Inspection Form is used to document and record the status of crops in a greenhouse, helping staff monitor and assess their quality and condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Greenhouse Crop Inspection" page.
2. Fill out the form fields as described below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Greenhouse Crop Inspection** (1, text): This field is where you'll describe the condition or status of the crops you've inspected.
* **Notes** (2, text): Use this field to add any additional comments or observations about the crops.
* **Crop Status** (3, select_one): Use this dropdown menu to report the overall health of the crop, with options being "good", "bad", or "unsure". Note that this is not a formal diagnosis, and is intended to indicate the general quality of the crops.
* **Date** (4, date): Enter the date of the inspection in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time** (5, time): Enter the time of the inspection in the format HH:MM:SS AM/PM.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the "Greenhouse Crop Inspection" field.
* If you're unsure about the status of a crop, select "unsure" to indicate that you need further evaluation.
* This form is intended for internal use only, so do not share it with external parties without permission.
