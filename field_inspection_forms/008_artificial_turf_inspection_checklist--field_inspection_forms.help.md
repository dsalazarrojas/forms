# Artificial Turf Inspection Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Artificial Turf Inspection Checklist is used to assess the condition, maintenance, and other relevant details of artificial turf fields. This form helps inspectors document their findings and report them in a standardized manner, ensuring consistency and accuracy in their inspections.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by entering the **Inspection Date** field (1) with the date of the inspection.
2. Enter the **Field Name or Location** (2) to identify the specific field being inspected.
3. Record the **Field Address** (3) to help locate the field in the event of future inspections or maintenance.
4. Provide your **Inspector Name** (4) and **Inspector Role or Title** (5) to identify yourself and your position.
5. Assess the **Turf Installation Age - Years** (6) to determine how long the turf has been in use.
6. Evaluate the **Overall Field Condition** (7) by selecting from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Requires Replacement.
7. Check the **Surface Wear or Damage Assessment** (8) by selecting the applicable options: Minimal Wear, Moderate Wear, Heavy Wear, Bare Spots, Seam Separation, or Pile Loss.
8. Assess the **Drainage System Condition** (9) by choosing from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
9. Evaluate the **Infill Material Assessment** (10) by entering any relevant information.
10. Check the **Perimeter and Border Condition** (11) by selecting from the options: Good, Minor Issues, or Major Issues.
11. Record the **Lighting and Safety Features** (12) by entering any relevant details.
12. Determine the **Maintenance or Repairs Required** (13) by selecting from the options: None, Routine Maintenance, Minor Repairs, Major Repairs, or Full Replacement.
13. Enter **Maintenance Details and Priority** (14) to provide context for any necessary repairs.
14. If applicable, record the **Recommended Next Inspection Date** (15).
15. Indicate whether **Photos Attached** (16) are available.
16. Enter any **Additional Notes or Observations** (17) to provide further context or comments.

By following this guide, you'll be able to complete the Artificial Turf Inspection Checklist accurately and efficiently, ensuring that you collect all necessary information during your inspections.
