# Hydrocarbon Assessment Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Hydrocarbon Assessment Checklist" is a form used to evaluate and document hydrocarbon storage and spill response procedures. It helps ensure that the hydrocarbon storage is properly maintained, and spill response actions are taken promptly and effectively.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the hydrocarbon storage conditions and assess the overall condition of the storage facilities.
2.  Evaluate the availability and condition of spill response equipment and staff training records.
3.  Record any corrective actions taken or planned for hydrocarbon spills.
4.  Update the status of staff training and corrective actions.
5.  Review and update the form regularly to ensure compliance with relevant regulations and standards.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Hydrocarbon Storage**: <u>1. Hydrocarbon Storage</u> (<u>hydrocarbon_storage</u>): Describe the condition of the hydrocarbon storage facilities, including any issues or concerns.
*   **Spill Response**: <u>2. Spill Response</u> (<u>spill_response</u>): Describe the spill response procedures and protocols in place.
*   **Staff Training**: <u>3. Staff Training</u> (<u>staff_training</u>): Evaluate the level of staff training on hydrocarbon management and spill response procedures.
*   **Hydrocarbon Storage Condition**: <u>4. Hydrocarbon Storage Condition</u> (<u>hydrocarbon_storage_condition</u>): Select the condition of the hydrocarbon storage facilities (<u>Excellent</u>, <u>Fair</u>, or <u>Poor</u>).
*   **Spill Response Equipment**: <u>5. Spill Response Equipment</u> (<u>spill_response_equipment</u>): Describe the condition and availability of spill response equipment.
*   **Corrective Action**: <u>6. Corrective Action</u> (<u>corrective_action</u>): Record any corrective actions taken or planned for hydrocarbon spills.
*   **Hydrocarbon Storage Equipment**: <u>7. Hydrocarbon Storage Equipment</u> (<u>hydrocarbon_storage_equipment</u>): Describe the condition and availability of hydrocarbon storage equipment.
*   **Spill Response Training**: <u>8. Spill Response Training</u> (<u>spill_response_training</u>): Evaluate the level of spill response training provided to staff.
*   **Hydrocarbon Spill Frequency**: <u>9. Hydrocarbon Spill Frequency</u> (<u>hydrocarbon_spill_frequency</u>): Select the frequency of hydrocarbon spills (<u>High</u>, <u>Moderate</u>, or <u>Low</u>).
*   **Spill Response Time**: <u>10. Spill Response Time</u> (<u>spill_response_time</u>): Record the time it takes to respond to a hydrocarbon spill (<u>Minutes</u>, <u>Hours</u>, or <u>Days</u>).
*   **Spill Response Time Unit**: <u>11. Spill Response Time Unit</u> (<u>spill_response_time_unit</u>): Select the unit of time used to measure the response time (<u>Minutes</u>, <u>Hours</u>, or <u>Days</u>).
*   **Hydrocarbon Spill Size**: <u>12. Hydrocarbon Spill Size</u> (<u>hydrocarbon_spill_size</u>): Record the size of the hydrocarbon spill.
*   **Spill Location**: <u>13. Spill Location</u> (<u>spill_location</u>): Select the location where the hydrocarbon spill occurred (<u>Tank</u>, <u>Pipeline</u>, <u>Pipeline and tank</u>, or <u>Other</u>).
*   **Spill Date**: <u>14. Spill Date</u> (<u>spill_date</u>): Record the date the hydrocarbon spill occurred.
*   **Spill Time**: <u>15. Spill Time</u> (<u>spill_time</u>): Record the time the hydrocarbon spill occurred.
*   **Spill Location Latitude**: <u>16. Spill Location Latitude</u> (<u>spill_location_lat</u>): Record the latitude of the spill location.
*   **Spill Location Longitude**: <u>17. Spill Location Longitude</u> (<u>spill_location_long</u>): Record the longitude of the spill location.
*   **Hydrocarbon Storage Location**: <u>18. Hydrocarbon Storage Location</u> (<u>hydrocarbon_storage_location</u>): Describe the location where the hydrocarbon storage facilities are located.
*   **Hydrocarbon Storage Capacity**: <u>19. Hydrocarbon Storage Capacity</u> (<u>hydrocarbon_storage_capacity</u>): Describe the capacity of the hydrocarbon storage facilities.
*   **Hydrocarbon Spill Volumes**: <u>20. Hydrocarbon Spill Volumes</u> (<u>hydrocarbon_spill_volumes</u>): Record the volume of the hydrocarbon spill.
*   **Corrective Action Status**: <u>21. Corrective Action Status</u> (<u>corrective_action_status</u>): Select the status of the corrective actions taken or planned (<u>Completed</u>, <u>In Progress</u>, or <u>Not Started</u>).
*   **Corrective Action Date**: <u>22. Corrective Action Date</u> (<u>corrective_action_date</u>): Record the date the corrective action was taken or planned.
*   **Comments**: <u>23. Comments</u> (<u>comments</u>): Enter any additional comments or notes regarding hydrocarbon management and spill response.
*   **Staff Training Status**: <u>24. Staff Training Status</u> (<u>staff_training_status</u>): Select the status of staff training (<u>Completed</u>, <u>In Progress</u>, or <u>Not Started</u>).
*   **Staff Training Date**: <u>25. Staff Training Date</u> (<u>staff_training_date</u>): Record the date staff training was completed or started.
## Tips

*   Always follow the procedures outlined in this form to ensure accurate and consistent evaluation and documentation of hydrocarbon storage and spill response procedures.
*   Review and update this form regularly to ensure compliance with relevant regulations and standards.
*   Ensure that staff is trained on hydrocarbon management and spill response procedures before conducting any activities related to hydrocarbon storage and spill response.
*   Document all corrective actions taken or planned for hydrocarbon spills, including dates and status.
*   Keep the form up-to-date and accurate for future reference and compliance audits.
