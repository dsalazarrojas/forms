# Worker Equipment Inspection Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Worker Equipment Inspection Request Form is used to track and record information about equipment condition, inspection dates, and other relevant details. This form is intended for workers to report on the status of their equipment, which is essential for maintaining accurate records and ensuring compliance with safety regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the required fields and the options available for each field.
2. Answer the questions truthfully, providing accurate and detailed information about your equipment.
3. Select the correct options for each field from the provided list.
4. Upload any supporting photos of the equipment if necessary.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment Condition** (Yes/No): Check "Yes" if your equipment is in good condition and meets the required standards. Check "No" if your equipment is defective, damaged, or not functioning properly.
* **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the previous inspection or the date you would like to schedule for the next inspection.
* **Supporting Photos** (Yes/No): Check "Yes" if you have attached photos of the equipment to support your inspection report. Check "No" otherwise.
* **Inspection Frequency** (Daily, Weekly, Monthly): Select the frequency at which you would like to schedule the next inspection.
