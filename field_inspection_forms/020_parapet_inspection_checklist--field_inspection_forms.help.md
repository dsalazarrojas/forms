# parapet_inspection_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The parapet_inspection_checklist is designed to collect information about the condition of a parapet, including any visible signs of damage or wear.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by reviewing the parapet and its surroundings.
2. Fill out each field thoroughly and accurately, using the information you observe during the inspection.
3. For each field, provide as much detail as possible to ensure a comprehensive record of the parapet's condition.
