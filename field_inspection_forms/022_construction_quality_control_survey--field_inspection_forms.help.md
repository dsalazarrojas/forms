# Construction Quality Control Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Construction Quality Control Survey form is used to evaluate the quality of construction work on a project site, ensuring that the project meets the required standards and specifications.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. For each field, provide accurate and thorough information.
3. Use the provided options for select-one and select-multiple fields.
4. If a field is not applicable, select "None" or leave it blank.
5. Take photos of any defects or issues, if applicable.
6. Use the text fields to provide descriptions and comments.
7. Ensure that all fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Date** (Required): Date of the quality control survey.
* **Inspector Name** (Required): Name of the quality inspector conducting the survey.
* **Inspector Credentials** (Optional): Qualifications or certifications of the quality inspector.
* **Project Name** (Required): Name of the construction project.
* **Project Address** (Required): Site location of the project.
* **Area or Phase Inspected** (Required): Specific area or work phase inspected.
* **Construction Phase** (Required): Current phase of construction (e.g., Site Work, Foundation, etc.).
* **Workmanship Quality** (Required): Rate the overall workmanship quality (Excellent, Good, Acceptable, Below Standard, Unacceptable).
* **Materials Quality** (Required): Rate the quality of materials used (As Specified, Acceptable Quality, Below Specification, Unacceptable).
* **Compliance with Specifications** (Required): Is the work compliant with project specifications?
* **Compliance with Drawings** (Required): Is the work compliant with approved drawings?
* **Dimensional Accuracy** (Required): Are dimensions within tolerance?
* **Finish Quality** (Optional): Rate the quality of finished work (Excellent, Good, Acceptable, Needs Improvement, Poor).
* **Number of Defects Found** (Required): Count of quality defects.
* **Types of Defects** (Required): Select all types of defects found (Cracks, Misalignment, Poor Joints, Surface Damage, etc.).
* **Defect Description** (Optional): Describe the defects found.
* **Corrective Action Needed** (Required): What corrections are required to address defects.
* **Rework Required** (Required): Does work need to be redone?
* **Photos Taken** (Optional): Were photos taken for documentation?
* **Testing Required** (Optional): Is additional testing needed?
* **Type of Testing** (Optional): What tests are needed (Concrete Strength, Soil Compaction, etc.)?
* **Hold Point Required** (Required): Should work stop until approved?
* **Overall Quality Assessment** (Required): Final quality determination (Approved, Approved with Minor Items, Conditional Approval, Rejected).
* **Follow-Up Required** (Required): Is reinspection needed?
* **Follow-Up Date** (Optional): Scheduled reinspection date.
* **Inspector Comments** (Optional): Additional observations.
* **Inspector Signature** (Required): Type full name as signature.

Note: This help guide is based on the provided YAML fields and is not intended to be a comprehensive or exhaustive guide.
