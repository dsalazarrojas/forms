# Real Estate Drone Inspection Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Drone Inspection Application Form is designed to collect relevant information for real estate drone inspections. This form helps to gather general information, property details, inspection schedule, inspection reports, contact information, drone inspection data, and submission tracking data in a structured and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the fields as instructed in the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section below.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
3. Review the form for any errors or missing information before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (1): This field is for general information about the client or property owner. Please provide a brief description of the client or property owner.
* **Property Information** (2): Enter the address and details of the property to be inspected.
* **Inspection Schedule** (3): Enter the planned inspection schedule, including the date and time of the inspection.
* **Inspection Report** (4): This field is for any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Contact Info** (5): Enter your contact information, including your name and email address, so that we can get in touch with you regarding the inspection.
* **Drone Inspection** (6): Please describe the purpose and scope of the drone inspection.
* **Inspection Photos** (7): Upload any relevant photos taken during the inspection.
* **Submission Tracking** (8): Use this field to track the status of your submission, including any comments or feedback from our team.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* Please attach any relevant supporting documents or photos to the corresponding fields.
* Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors or missing information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [contact email].
