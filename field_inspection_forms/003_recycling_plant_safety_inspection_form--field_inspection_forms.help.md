# Recycling Plant Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to be used by facility managers or supervisors to conduct a safety inspection of a recycling plant.

## How to Complete This Form

1. Fill in the plant's address in the "Plant Address" field.
2. Enter the name of the plant in the "Plant Name" field.
3. Enter the facility's ID number in the "Facility ID" field.
4. Select the safety officer responsible for the plant's safety from the "Safety Officer" dropdown list.
5. Enter the email address of the safety officer in the "Safety Officer Email" field.
6. Enter the phone number of the safety officer in the "Safety Officer Phone" field.
7. Enter the ID number of the safety inspector in the "Safety Inspector ID" field.
8. Enter the phone number of the safety inspector in the "Safety Inspector Phone" field.
9. List the types of plant equipment that are in good condition and functioning properly in the "Plant Equipment" field.
10. Select the condition of the equipment from the "Equipment Condition" dropdown list.
11. Select the types of electrical equipment that are in good condition and functioning properly in the "Electrical Equipment" dropdown list.
12. Select the types of mechanical equipment that are in good condition and functioning properly in the "Mechanical Equipment" dropdown list.
13. Select the types of control systems that are in good condition and functioning properly in the "Control Systems" dropdown list.
14. Select the types of safety signage that are in good condition and visible in the "Safety Signage" dropdown list.
15. Select the types of safety equipment that are available and in good condition in the "Safety Equipment" dropdown list.
16. Select the types of spill containment measures that are in place in the "Spill Containment" dropdown list.
17. Select the types of waste containment measures that are in place in the "Waste Containment" dropdown list.
18. Select the types of training programs that have been conducted for employees in the "Training" dropdown list.
19. Select the types of reviews that have been conducted in the "Review" dropdown list.
20. Select the types of follow-up actions that have been taken to address safety concerns in the "Follow-up" dropdown list.
21. Enter any additional comments or observations in the "Comments" field.
22. Enter the date and time of the inspection in the "Date" and "Time" fields, respectively.
23. Enter the name and title of the person submitting the form in the "Submitter" field.
