# Framing Inspection Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Framing Inspection Checklist is a tool designed to help ensure the quality of framed structures by providing a systematic way to inspect and document the condition of the framing components. This form is intended to be used to identify potential issues and defects in the framing system, promoting a more reliable and safer construction process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the checklist carefully to understand the questions and options provided.
2. Answer each question based on your observation and examination of the framing components.
3. Select the corresponding options for each question, ensuring you provide a clear and accurate response.
4. Complete all sections of the form, as each one is relevant to the inspection of the framed structure.
