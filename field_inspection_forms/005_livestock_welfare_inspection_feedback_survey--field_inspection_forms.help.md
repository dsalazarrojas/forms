# Livestock Welfare Inspection Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the animal welfare conditions at your facility. Please provide accurate and detailed answers to help us improve the overall welfare of the animals and the farm's operations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide the required information about your farm, including the farm name, facility ID, facility name, and address.
2. Report the number of animals on the farm.
3. Provide the IDs of the animals that were inspected.
4. Describe the current animal welfare conditions at the facility, including any issues or concerns.
5. Rate the facility's overall rating (out of 10).
6. Provide any comments or feedback about the facility.
7. Enter the email address and phone number of the facility's contact person.
8. Enter the date and time of the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **farm_name**: Please enter the name of your farm.
* **farm_id**: Please enter the ID number of your farm, if applicable.
* **facility_name**: Please enter the name of your facility.
* **facility_address**: Please enter the address of your facility.
* **animal_count**: Please report the total number of animals on the farm.
* **animal_ids**: Please enter the IDs of the animals that were inspected.
* **animal_status**: Describe the current status of the animals at the facility.
* **animal_welfare_condition**: Describe any concerns or issues with the animal welfare conditions at the facility.
* **facility_rating**: Rate the facility's overall rating (out of 10).
* **facility_comments**: Provide any comments or feedback about the facility.
* **facility_email**: Please enter the email address of the facility's contact person.
* **facility_phone**: Please enter the phone number of the facility's contact person.
* **facility_date**: Please enter the date of the inspection.
* **facility_time**: Please enter the time of the inspection.
