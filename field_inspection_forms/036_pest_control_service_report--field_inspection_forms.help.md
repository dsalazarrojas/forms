# Pest Control Service Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pest Control Service Report form is used to record and report pest control services provided to a property. It captures essential information for future reference, including the date of visit, services performed, and contact details.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the visit in the `Date of Visit` field.
2. Select the pest control services provided from the list in the `Pest Control Services` field. Multiple services can be selected if necessary.
3. Enter any additional notes or information in the `Notes` field.
4. Enter the name of the person who submitted the report in the `Submitted by` field.
5. Enter the name of the assigned technician in the `Assigned Technician` field.
6. Enter the contact name in the `Contact Name` field.
7. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the `Phone Number` field.
8. Enter the email address of the contact person in the `Email` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the pest control service was performed.
* **Pest Control Services** (`pest_control_services`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the services provided to the property, such as Bed bug treatment, Cockroach treatment, etc.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required/optional): Add any additional information or comments about the visit.
* **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who submitted the report.
* **Assigned Technician** (`assigned_technician`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the technician who performed the service.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
