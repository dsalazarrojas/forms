# Field Inspection Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record the results of a field inspection of a site's physical condition and compliance with safety and regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Inspection Date** field with the date on which the inspection was conducted.
2. Enter the **Facility** name in the corresponding field.
3. In the **Inspector** field, enter your name or the name of the person conducting the inspection.
4. In the **Notes** field, enter any observations or comments you made during the inspection.
5. Complete any other required or optional fields as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date): Enter the date on which the inspection was conducted.
* **Inspector** (`inspector`, text): Enter your name or the name of the person conducting the inspection.
* **Facility** (`facility`, text): Enter the name of the facility being inspected.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text): Enter any observations or comments made during the inspection.
