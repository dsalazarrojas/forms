# Electrical Injection Testing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data for electrical injection testing, likely for quality control or assurance purposes in industrial or electrical engineering settings. The form may be used to document test results, track equipment performance, or monitor environmental conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant date for the electrical injection testing.
2. Enter the equipment ID number for the tested equipment.
3. Provide the location where the testing was performed.
4. Note down the parameters or conditions under which the testing was done.
5. Document any results or observations from the testing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Test Date** (`test_date`, `date`, required: false): The date on which the electrical injection testing was performed. Select a date from the calendar or enter it manually in the correct format (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Equipment ID** (`equipment_id`, `text`, required: false): A unique identifier for the equipment being tested. This could be a serial number, model number, or any other distinctive code assigned to the equipment.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required: false): The site or area where the testing was performed. This could be a specific room, department, or facility.
* **Parameters** (`parameters`, `text`, required: false): A list or description of the parameters or conditions under which the testing was done, such as voltage, current, or frequency.
* **Results** (`results`, `text`, required: false): Any observations, notes, or results from the testing, which could include measurements, readings, or comments.
