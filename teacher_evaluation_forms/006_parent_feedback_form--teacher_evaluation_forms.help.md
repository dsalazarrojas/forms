# Parent Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Parent Feedback Form is designed to collect your thoughts and opinions on your experience with the school. Your feedback is valuable and helps us improve our services and facilities to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please read each section carefully and complete each field according to the instructions.
2.  Use your honest and sincere feedback to provide us with an accurate picture of your experience.
3.  If you need help or have questions, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Parent Feedback** (`parent_feedback`, Text, Optional): Please provide your overall feedback on your experience with the school. This can include any comments or suggestions you have for improvement.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, Text, Optional): This is a text box where you can provide any additional comments or feedback you have on your experience with the school.
*   **Feedback on Climate** (`feedback_on_climate`, Select Multiple, Optional): Please select one or more of the following options that best describe your experience with the school's climate:
    + Climate is too hot
    + Climate is too cold
    + Climate is just right
*   **Feedback on Facilities** (`feedback_on_facilities`, Select One, Optional): Please select one of the following options that best describes your experience with the school's facilities:
    + Excellent
    + Good
    + Fair
    + Poor
*   **Feedback on Services** (`feedback_on_services`, Select Multiple, Optional): Please select one or more of the following options that best describe your experience with the school's services:
    + Excellent
    + Good
    + Fair
    + Poor
*   **Teacher Performance** (`teacher_performance`, Number, Optional): If applicable, please rate your teacher's performance on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Student Performance** (`student_performance`, Select One, Optional): Please select one of the following options that best describes your experience with the student's performance:
    + Excellent
    + Good
    + Fair
    + Poor
*   **Date** (`date`, Date, Optional): Please enter the date of your last visit or interaction with the school.
*   **Time** (`time`, Time, Optional): Please enter the time of your last visit or interaction with the school.
*   **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Please enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Please enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
*   **Feedback on Safety** (`feedback_on_safety`, Select Multiple, Optional): Please select one or more of the following options that best describe your experience with the school's safety:
    + Always Safe
    + Mostly Safe
    + Sometimes Safe
    + Never Safe
*   **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, Text, Optional): Please provide any suggestions you have for improvement. 

## Tips

*   Please be honest and sincere in your feedback.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us.
*   If you have filled out this form before, please review your previous feedback to ensure consistency and avoid duplication.
