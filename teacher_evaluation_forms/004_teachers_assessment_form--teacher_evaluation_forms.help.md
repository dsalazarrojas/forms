# Teachers Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teachers Assessment Form is designed to help administrators and evaluators collect feedback on teachers' performance, focusing on their teaching methods, tools, and effectiveness. This form provides a structured way to gather qualitative comments and ratings that can aid in improving teaching skills and providing valuable insights to school administrators.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
6. Choose a rating for the teacher's performance in the "Rating" field.
7. Write a comment about the teacher in the "Comment" field.
8. Select the tool assigned to the teacher in the "Assigned Tool" field.
9. Choose the category that the form belongs to in the "Category" field, selecting all relevant options.
10. Enter the category name in the "Description" field.
11. Enter a title for the form in the "Title" field.
12. Select the category that the form belongs to in the "Category 2" field.
13. Select the tool assigned to the teacher in the "Assigned Tool 2" field.
14. Select the date of birth of the teacher in the "Date Of Birth 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the first name of the teacher you are assessing.
* **Last Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the last name of the teacher you are assessing.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the teacher you are assessing.
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the teacher you are assessing.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required/optional): Select the date of birth of the teacher.
* **Rating** (select_one, required): Choose a rating for the teacher's performance, such as "Teacher is doing a great job", "Not very good", or "Needs improvement".
* **Comment** (note, required/optional): Write a comment about the teacher's performance.
* **Assigned Tool** (select_one, required): Select the tool assigned to the teacher, such as "chatjimmy", "google", or "other".
* **Form ID** (number, required/optional): Enter a unique identifier for the form.
* **Category** (select_multiple, required): Select all categories that the form belongs to, such as "Teacher Evaluation Forms", "Student Evaluation Forms", or "Other".
* **Description** (text, required/optional): Enter a description of the form.
* **Title** (text, required/optional): Enter a title for the form.
* **Category 2** (select_one, required): Select the category that the form belongs to.
* **Assigned Tool 2** (select_one, required): Select the tool assigned to the teacher.
* **Date Of Birth 2** (date, required/optional): Select the date of birth of the teacher.

Note: Some fields may appear redundant, but following the form's structure and requirements can help to minimize confusion.
