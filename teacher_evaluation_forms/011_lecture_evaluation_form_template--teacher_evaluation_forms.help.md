# Lecture Evaluation Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help lecturers gauge their performance by collecting feedback from students on the quality of their content, presentation, and engagement during a lecture. Your input will help the lecturer improve and provide a better learning experience for you and your peers.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the course you are evaluating.
3. Enter the lecturer's name who gave the lecture.
4. Fill in the date of the evaluation.
5. Rate the overall feeling about the lecture using the number scale (e.g., 1-5).
6. Rate the quality of the content, presentation, and engagement on a scale of 1-5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as a student in the lecture.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the course you are evaluating.
* **Lecturer Name** (`lecturer_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person who gave the lecture.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Overall Feeling** (`overall_feeling`, number, required: false): Rate your overall feeling about the lecture on a scale of 1-5.
* **Quality Of Content** (`quality_of_content`, number, required: false): Rate the quality of the content on a scale of 1-5.
* **Quality Of Presentation** (`quality_of_presentation`, number, required: false): Rate the quality of the presentation on a scale of 1-5.
* **Engagement** (`engagement`, number, required: false): Rate the level of engagement during the lecture on a scale of 1-5.

* Be honest and provide feedback that will help the lecturer improve.
