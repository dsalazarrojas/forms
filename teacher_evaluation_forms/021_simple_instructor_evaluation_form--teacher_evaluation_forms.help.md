# Simple Instructor Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Simple Instructor Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from students about their instructors and courses. This form is used to evaluate the effectiveness and quality of instruction, provide valuable insights for instructors, and help improve teaching methods and course materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student name in the first field.
2. Enter the instructor's name in the second field.
3. Enter the course number in the third field.
4. Select whether the evaluation period is 'Yes' or 'No' in the fourth field.
5. Optional: provide a rating from 1 to 100 for the fifth field.
6. Provide any comments about the student in the sixth field.
7. Provide any comments about the instructor in the seventh field.
8. Optional: enter a score from 1 to 100 for the eighth field.
9. Select whether the course was helpful, good, or poor in the ninth field.
10. Select whether the course was helpful, relevant, or not in the tenth field.
11. Select whether the course was difficult or not in the eleventh field.
12. Select whether the course was relevant or not in the twelfth field.
13. Select your interest level as High or Low in the thirteenth field.
14. Select the instructor's interest level as High or Low in the fourteenth field.
15. Select your overall satisfaction as Satisfied or Dissatisfied in the fifteenth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### student_name (required)
* A field to input your student name

### instructor_name (required)
* A field to input the instructor's name

### course_number (required)
* A field to input the course number

### evaluation_period (required)
* Select whether the evaluation period is 'Yes' or 'No'

### rating_0 (optional)
* Optional: provide a rating from 1 to 100

### student_comments (required)
* A field to input comments about the student

### instructor_comments (optional)
* A field to input comments about the instructor

### student_rating_score (optional)
* Optional: enter a score from 1 to 100

### course_quality (optional)
* Select whether the course was Good, Poor, or Not Applicable

### course_helpfulness (optional)
* Select whether the course was Helpful, Not Helpful, or Not Applicable

### course_difficulty (optional)
* Select whether the course was Difficult, Not Difficult, or Not Applicable

### course_relevance (optional)
* Select whether the course was Relevant, Not Relevant, or Not Applicable

### student_interest_level (optional)
* Select your interest level as High or Low

### instructor_interest_level (optional)
* Select the instructor's interest level as High or Low

### overall_satisfaction (optional)
* Select your overall satisfaction as Satisfied or Dissatisfied
