# eecs gta evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and evaluation information from teachers about their Graduate Teaching Assistant (GTA) experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the GTA's name in the field labeled "gta name".
2. Provide your feedback about the GTA's performance in the "feedback" field.
3. Enter the GTA's ID number in the "gta id" field.
4. Select the rehire decision for the GTA by choosing between "rehire" and "not rehire" in the "rehire decision" field.
5. Enter the date of the evaluation in the "date" field.
6. Enter the time of the evaluation in the "time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### GTA name
* **gta name** (text, required): Enter the name of the Graduate Teaching Assistant being evaluated.

### Feedback
* **feedback** (text, required): Share your thoughts and impressions about the GTA's performance and behavior.

### Performance
* **performance** (text, required): Rate the GTA's performance, providing specific examples and details.

### GTA ID
* **gta id** (text, required): Enter the GTA's ID number, which is usually assigned by the department or institution.

### Rehire Decision
* **rehire decision** (select_one, required): Choose between "rehire" and "not rehire" to indicate your decision regarding the GTA's future employment.

### Date
* **date** (date, required): Enter the date of the evaluation, following the format mm/dd/yyyy or any other format accepted by your institution.

### Time
* **time** (time, required): Enter the time of the evaluation, using the 24-hour format (HH:MM) or the AM/PM format (HH:MM AM/PM).

## Tips
* Please provide honest and constructive feedback to help the GTA improve.
* Be specific about the GTA's performance, providing examples and details.
* Enter the correct GTA ID number to avoid confusion.
* Choose the rehire decision based on the GTA's performance and behavior.
* Enter the date and time accurately to ensure the evaluation is recorded correctly.
