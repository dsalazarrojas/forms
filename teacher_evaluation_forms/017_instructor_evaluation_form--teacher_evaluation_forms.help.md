# instructor_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Instructor Evaluation Form is used to collect feedback on the performance of an instructor, providing valuable insights for improvement and recognition of outstanding teaching practices. This form helps to identify areas of strength and weakness in instruction, ensuring that teaching quality and effectiveness are maintained and improved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the title of the instructor being evaluated.
2. Rate the instructor's performance using the scale provided.
3. Provide detailed comments about the instructor's strengths and weaknesses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Instructor Evaluation Form** (`title`, text, required): This is the title of the form, which is not editable and only needs to be filled in for clarity.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): Rate the instructor's performance using the provided scale. Please provide a number between 1 and 5 to reflect your assessment of their teaching quality.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Share your thoughts and feedback about the instructor's teaching practices. Be specific about what they do well and what could be improved.

## Tips
* Please be constructive and specific when providing feedback.
* Focus on the instructor's performance and how it can be improved, rather than personal opinions.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, be sure to include them in the comments field.
