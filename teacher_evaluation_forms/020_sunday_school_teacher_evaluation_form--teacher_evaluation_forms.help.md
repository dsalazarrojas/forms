# Sunday School Teacher Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sunday School Teacher Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from students, parents, or staff members about the performance of Sunday school teachers. This form aims to evaluate the teacher's effectiveness, availability, teaching style, and other aspects of their work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information about the Sunday school teacher you are evaluating.
2. Select the relevant options from the dropdown menus to provide your feedback.
3. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the text field.
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sunday School Teacher** (`1`, `text`, **not required**): Please enter the name of the Sunday school teacher you are evaluating.
* **Lesson Effectiveness** (`2`, `select_one`, **not required**): Rate the effectiveness of the teacher's lessons. Choose from the options:
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Teacher Availability** (`3`, `number`, **not required**): Enter the number of Sundays the teacher was available to lead the class.
* **Class Size** (`4`, `select_one`, **not required**): Select the size of the class:
	+ Small
	+ Medium
	+ Large
* **Teaching Style** (`5`, `select_multiple`, **not required**): Choose the teaching styles the teacher employed:
	+ Conventional
	+ Innovative
	+ Interactive
* **Lesson Length** (`6`, `time`, **not required**): Enter the length of each lesson, in minutes.
* **Overall Impression** (`7`, `text`, **not required**): Enter any comments or feedback about your overall impression of the teacher.
* **Teacher Support** (`8`, `select_multiple`, **not required**): Rate the support the teacher provided:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Teacher Materials** (`9`, `select_multiple`, **not required**): Choose the quality of the teacher's materials:
	+ Adequate
	+ Good
	+ Excellent

## Tips

* Please be honest and objective when filling out this form.
* Select all the options that apply to your experience with the teacher.
* If you have any additional feedback or comments, please enter them in the text field.
* If you are unable to provide feedback for any section, leave it blank.
