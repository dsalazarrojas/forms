# Educator Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educator Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback and ratings from students to evaluate their educators' performance. This form is intended to help educators identify areas of strength and areas for improvement, allowing them to grow and develop as professionals. To get the most out of this form, please ensure that you are evaluating an educator you have had direct interaction with, and that your feedback is constructive and actionable.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First name, Last name, and Email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Choose a rating for the educator from 1.0 to 3.0 in the "Rating" field.
3. Provide a brief description of how the educator applied their knowledge in the "Knowledge Application" field.
4. Describe the educator's classroom management skills in the "Classroom Management" field.
5. If you have any additional comments or suggestions for the educator, please type them in the "Additional Comments" field.
6. If applicable, select the tool that was assigned to the educator from the "Assigned Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (1): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (2): Enter your last name.
* **Email address** (3): Enter your email address (e.g., example@example.com). This field is required to ensure we can contact you for follow-up questions or to send you any relevant information.
* **Rating** (5): Choose a rating between 1.0 and 3.0 based on your overall experience with the educator. A higher rating indicates a better experience.
* **Knowledge Application** (6): Describe how the educator applied their knowledge to help you in the course. Please be specific and provide examples if possible.
* **Classroom Management** (7): Rate the educator's ability to manage the classroom. Describe any strengths or weaknesses you observed in this area.
* **Additional Comments** (8): Type any additional comments or suggestions you have for the educator. If you have specific feedback or recommendations, feel free to include them here.
* **Assigned Tool** (9): If you were assigned to work with the educator using a specific tool (e.g., Chat Jimmy), please select it from the list of options. If you didn't work with the educator using a tool, simply select "Other".
