# Teacher Cadet Education Quote Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Cadet Education Quote Evaluation Form is used to gather feedback and evaluation from teachers regarding a teacher cadet's performance, specifically in terms of their educational activities, meetings, and interactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you understand its purpose and the fields it contains.
2. Answer each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
3. Ensure all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Weekly meeting notes (Page 1)
* **Weekly meeting notes** (text, required: false): Please provide a brief summary of what you discussed during the weekly meeting.

### Quote of the week (Page 1)
* **Quote of the week** (text, required: false): Please share a notable quote, phrase, or passage from the meeting that resonated with you.

### Weekly meeting evaluation (Page 1)
* **Weekly meeting evaluation** (select_one, required: false):
	+ Excellent: The meeting was exceptionally well-organized and productive.
	+ Fair: The meeting was average, with some areas for improvement.
	+ Poor: The meeting was poorly organized and unproductive.
Please select the level of evaluation that best describes your experience.

### Date (Page 2)
* **Date** (date, required: false): Please enter the date of the meeting.

### Time (Page 2)
* **Time** (time, required: false): Please enter the time of the meeting.

### Email (Page 3)
* **Email** (email, required: false): Please enter your email address for follow-up communication.

Note: If a field is marked as "required: false", it is not mandatory to fill out, but filling it out can provide additional context and information for the evaluation.
