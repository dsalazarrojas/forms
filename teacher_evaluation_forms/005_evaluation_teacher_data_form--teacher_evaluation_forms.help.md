# Evaluation Teacher Data Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Evaluation Teacher Data Form is a critical tool used to gather essential information about teachers, providing valuable insights for evaluation and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Teacher Name** in the "Teacher Name" field.
2. Enter your **school** name in the "school" field.
3. Provide your **email** address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your **department** or subject area in the "department" field.
5. Select your **subject area** in the "subject area" field.
6. Rate your **Performance Rating** on a scale (e.g., 1-5 or 1-10).
7. Provide any **Comments** regarding your performance or suggestions for improvement in the "Comments" field (optional).
8. Add any **Recommendations** for improvement or future development in the "Recommendations" field (optional).
9. Enter the date of the **evaluation** in the "evaluation_date" field.
10. Select the **evaluation_time** when the evaluation took place.
11. Specify who **submitted this evaluation** in the "submitted_by" field.
12. Enter the date you **submitted this evaluation** in the "submission_date" field.
13. Select the time you **submitted this evaluation** in the "submitted_at" field.
14. Choose the **evaluation method** used, which can be face-to-face, online, or video.
15. Select who this evaluation was **submitted to**, which can be school admin, department head, or principal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID badge or your preferred name to be used in this evaluation.
* **school** (`school`, text, required): Type your school name as it appears on your official documents.
* **email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your official email address for contact purposes.
* **department** (`department`, text, required): Specify your department or subject area of expertise.
* **subject area** (`subject area`, text, required): Enter your primary subject area of teaching.
* **Performance Rating** (`performance_rating`, number, required): Rate your performance on a scale (e.g., 1-5 or 1-10).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Share any comments or feedback you have about your performance or areas of improvement.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, optional): Suggest recommendations or areas of improvement for future development.
* **evaluation_date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of this evaluation.
* **evaluation_time** (`evaluation_time`, time, required): Select the time of this evaluation.
* **submitted_by** (`submitted_by`, text, required): Specify who completed this evaluation.
* **submission_date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date this evaluation was submitted.
* **submitted_at** (`submitted_at`, time, required): Select the time this evaluation was submitted.
* **evaluation_method** (`evaluation_method`, select_one, required): Choose the method used for this evaluation (face-to-face, online, or video).
* **submitted_to** (`submitted_to`, select_one, required): Select the recipient of this evaluation (school admin, department head, or principal).

**Tips**

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in for accurate data entry.
* If you have any issues or need clarification, contact your school admin or department head.
* Review your form submission carefully before submitting.
