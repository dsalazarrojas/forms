# teaching_methods_quality_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the teaching methods used in a particular class or course.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the teaching methods and materials used in the class or course.
2. Answer each question based on your honest feedback and learning experience.
3. Provide detailed comments and suggestions for improvement if possible.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, required: false): A brief introduction to the class or course, including any relevant details or context.
* **Teacher Name** (`2`, `text`, required: false): The name of the teacher who taught the class or course.
* **Teacher ID** (`3`, `number`, required: false): A unique identifier for the teacher, usually an employee ID or student ID.
* **Teaching Method** (`4`, `text`, required: false): A description of the teaching method used, including any specific techniques or strategies employed.
* **Teaching Method 1** (`5`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the following options: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **Teaching Method 2** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all that apply from the following options: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **Teaching Method 3** (`7`, `text`, required: false): A further description of the teaching method used, including any additional details or comments.
* **Date** (`8`, `date`, required: false): The date you completed this form, usually the date you received the teaching method.
* **Time** (`9`, `time`, required: false): The time you completed this form, usually the time of day or date and time.
* **Results** (`10`, `number`, required: false): A measure of how well you achieved the learning objectives or outcomes of the class or course.
* **Results Over Time** (`11`, `time`, required: false): A measure of how your results changed over time or across multiple sessions.
* **Better Learning Experience** (`12`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the following options: True or False.
* **Better Learning Experience 2** (`13`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all that apply from the following options: True or False.
* **Comments** (`14`, `text`, required: false): Any additional comments or suggestions you have on the teaching method.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`15`, `text`, required: false): A summary of your evaluation of the teaching method, including any strengths or weaknesses.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`16`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the following options: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, or Not Satisfied.
* **Overall Satisfaction 2** (`17`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the following options: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, or Not Satisfied.
* **Teacher Comments** (`18`, `text`, required: false): Any additional comments or feedback you have for the teacher.
* **Date Submitted** (`19`, `date`, required: false): The date and time you submitted this form.
* **Submitted By** (`20`, `email`, required: false): The email address of the person who submitted this form.
* **Email** (`21`, `email`, required: false): An email address associated with the person who submitted this form.
* **Phone** (`22`, `text`, required: false): A phone number associated with the person who submitted this form.
* **Comments** (`23`, `text`, required: false): Any additional comments or feedback you have on the form or its completion.
* **Submit** (`24`, `text`, required: false): A button to submit your evaluation and feedback.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have provided honest and accurate feedback.
* Be as specific as possible when providing comments and suggestions.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact the teacher or administrator.
* The teacher and administrator will review your feedback and use it to improve the teaching method and materials.
* This form will help to ensure that the teaching methods are effective and beneficial for all students.
