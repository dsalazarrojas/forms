# Tell Me About Your Child Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about your child, allowing us to better understand their needs and support their educational progress.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with as much detail as possible, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
2. Enter the student's name in the **child_name** field.
3. Select the student's grade level from the **grade_level** select-one field.
4. Enter any relevant information about the student's interests and needs in the **child_interests** field.
5. Enter the student's ID in the **student_id** field.
6. Enter your contact phone number in the **contact_phone** field.
7. Enter your email address in the **email** field.
8. Provide any additional comments or notes about the student in the **teacher_comments** and **parent_comments** fields.
9. Enter any further details about this submission in the **submitted_by** field.
10. Enter the date of submission in the **submission_date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **tell_me_about_your_child** (Text, Optional): This is a text field for the student's name.
* **grade_level** (Select One, Optional): This is a select-one field for the student's grade level. Please select the correct grade level for the student.
* **parent_name** (Text, Optional): This is a text field for the parent's name.
* **child_interests** (Text, Optional): This is a text field for the student's interests and needs.
* **student_id** (Text, Optional): This is a text field for the student's ID.
* **contact_phone** (Text, Optional): This is a text field for the parent's contact phone number.
* **email** (Email, Optional): This is an email field for the parent's email address.
* **additional_comments** (Note, Optional): This is a note field for any additional comments or notes about the student.
* **teacher_comments** (Note, Optional): This is a note field for any comments or notes from the teacher.
* **parent_comments** (Note, Optional): This is a note field for any comments or notes from the parent.
* **submitted_by** (Text, Optional): This is a text field for any additional details about this submission.
* **submission_date** (Date, Optional): This is a date field for the date of submission.

Note: The date field is displayed in the format 'YYYY-MM-dd'.
