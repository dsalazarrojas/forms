# social_work_referral_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to refer students to social work services due to concerning behavioral issues.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please enter the student's name in the field labeled "Student Name". This field is required for identification purposes.
2. Enter the teacher's name in the field labeled "Teacher Name". This field is required for verification purposes.
3. Enter the student's grade level in the number field labeled "Student Grade". This field is required for accurate referral.
4. Enter the date of the referral in the field labeled "Referral Date". This field is required for tracking purposes.
5. Select the type of referral by choosing from the options: "In-School Referral" or "Out-of-School Referral" from the field labeled "Referral Type". This field is required for categorizing referrals.
6. List any concerning behaviors exhibited by the student in the field labeled "Student Behaviors". Select multiple options as necessary.
7. Enter your assessment of the student's behavior and any suggestions for support in the field labeled "Teacher Assessment". This field is required for your evaluation.
8. Enter any comments or concerns you have about the student in the field labeled "Referral Comments". This field is required for further discussion.
9. Enter the parent's name in the field labeled "Parent Name". This field is required for verification purposes.
10. Enter the student's ID number in the field labeled "Student ID". This field is required for tracking purposes.
11. Select "Referral" from the options to indicate that you are referring the student to social work services from the field labeled "Teacher Referral". This field is required.
12. Enter any suggestions for the social worker from you, the teacher, in the field labeled "Teacher Suggestions". This field is required.
13. Sign and date your referral in the field labeled "Social Worker Signature". This field is required for official acknowledgement.
14. Enter your name as the social worker who will be providing services to the student in the field labeled "Social Worker Name". This field is required for verification purposes.
15. Enter your title as the social worker who will be providing services to the student in the field labeled "Social Worker Title". This field is required for verification purposes.
16. Enter the name of the organization providing the services in the field labeled "Organization Name". This field is required for tracking purposes.
17. Enter the contact phone number for the organization providing the services in the field labeled "Contact Number". This field is required for follow-up purposes.
18. Enter the email of the organization providing the services in the field labeled "E-mail". This field is required for communication purposes.
19. Enter the fax number for the organization providing the services in the field labeled "Fax". This field is required for additional communication.
20. Enter any comments for the social worker from you, the teacher, in the field labeled "Social Work Referral Form Comments". This field is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (text, required): Enter the student's name for identification purposes.
* **Teacher Name** (text, required): Enter your name as the referring teacher.
* **Student Grade** (number, required): Enter the student's grade level.
* **Referral Date** (date, required): Enter the date of the referral for tracking purposes.
* **Referral Type** (select_one, required): Select the type of referral: "In-School Referral" or "Out-of-School Referral".
* **Student Behaviors** (select_multiple, required): List concerning behaviors exhibited by the student.
* **Teacher Assessment** (text, required): Enter your assessment of the student's behavior and any suggestions for support.
* **Referral Comments** (text, required): Enter any comments or concerns about the student.
* **Parent Name** (text, required): Enter the parent's name for verification purposes.
* **Student ID** (text, required): Enter the student's ID number for tracking purposes.
* **Teacher Referral** (select_one, required): Select "Referral" to indicate that you are referring the student to social work services.
* **Teacher Suggestions** (text, required): Enter any suggestions for the social worker from you, the teacher.
* **Social Worker Signature** (text, required): Sign and date your referral for official acknowledgement.
* **Social Worker Name** (text, required): Enter your name as the social worker providing services to the student.
* **Social Worker Title** (text, required): Enter your title as the social worker providing services to the student.
* **Organization Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the organization providing services.
* **Contact Number** (text, required): Enter the contact phone number for the organization.
* **E-mail** (text, required): Enter the email of the organization.
* **Fax** (text, required): Enter the fax number for the organization.
* **Social Work Referral Form Comments** (text, required): Enter any comments or concerns for the social worker. 
* **Teacher Licensed** (text, required): Enter your license or certification level.
* **Teacher Certified** (text, required): Enter your certification level.
* **Teacher School** (text, required): Enter your school name.
* **Teacher Department** (text, required): Enter your department name.
* **Teacher Building** (text, required): Enter your building name.
