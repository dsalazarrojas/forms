# Substitute Teacher Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for teachers to report on the behavior and performance of students during a substitute teacher's lessons. It is designed to gather information on the student's engagement, behavior, and overall performance for the regular teacher or school administrators.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form as completely and accurately as possible.
2. Select the lesson planned for the period of the substitute teacher's visit.
3. Provide notes about the lesson and any relevant details that may be helpful for the regular teacher.
4. Enter any notes about the students, such as their behavior, performance, or any notable events.
5. Enter the names of the students, separated by commas.
6. Select the number of students present during the lesson.
7. Choose how the students were engaged with the lesson:
	* On task: Students were engaged with the lesson and completed the assigned tasks.
	* Off task: Students were not engaged with the lesson and did not complete the assigned tasks.
	* On task but not following directions: Students were engaged with the lesson but did not follow the instructions correctly.
	* Off task and following directions: Students were not engaged with the lesson but followed the instructions correctly.
8. Provide any comments or feedback for the students, such as suggestions for improvement or positive reinforcement.
9. Select the teacher's feedback on the lesson:
	* Satisfied: The teacher is satisfied with the lesson's progress and performance.
	* Neutral: The teacher is neutral or did not observe any significant issues with the lesson.
	* Dissatisfied: The teacher is dissatisfied with the lesson's progress and performance.

* **Substitute Teacher ID**: Enter your ID number, if required.

* **Number of Students**: Select the number of students present during the lesson.

	+ Off task: Students were not engaged with the lesson and did not complete the assigned tasks.
	+ On task but not following directions: Students were engaged with the lesson but did not follow the instructions correctly.
	+ Off task and following directions: Students were not engaged with the lesson but followed the instructions correctly.
* **Comments**: Provide any comments or feedback for the students, such as suggestions for improvement or positive reinforcement.
* **Teacher Feedback**: Select the teacher's feedback on the lesson:
	+ Satisfied: The teacher is satisfied with the lesson's progress and performance.
	+ Neutral: The teacher is neutral or did not observe any significant issues with the lesson.
