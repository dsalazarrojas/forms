# online_instructors_mini_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be used by students to evaluate their online instructor's performance in delivering their online course. The purpose of this form is to help instructors identify areas of improvement in their teaching methods and communication with students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant instructor ID to be evaluated.
2. Rate the instructor's performance from 1-5, where 1 is a low rating and 5 is a high rating.
3. Write any comments or feedback you may have about the instructor's performance.
4. Rate your overall satisfaction with the instructor's teaching style.
5. Write any additional comments or feedback you may have about the course content or instruction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **instructor_id**: This field is used to select the instructor that you wish to evaluate. Please enter the corresponding ID number for the instructor you wish to evaluate.
* **rating**: This field is a rating system from 1-5, where 1 is a low rating and 5 is a high rating. Use this scale to rate the instructor's performance based on their teaching methods and communication.
* **comments**: This field is for you to provide any comments or feedback you may have about the instructor's performance. This can include things such as what you liked or disliked about their teaching style.
* **overall_satisfaction**: This field is for you to rate your overall satisfaction with the instructor's teaching style. Use the same rating system as the "rating" field.
* **additional_comments**: This field is for you to provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about the course content or instruction. This can include things such as suggestions for improvement or things that worked well in the course.

## Tips
* Please make sure to select the correct instructor ID to ensure your evaluation is properly recorded.
* Be as detailed as possible in your comments and suggestions for improvement to help the instructor provide better instruction in the future.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, make sure to include them in the "additional_comments" field.
* Make sure to use the proper rating scale for all fields to ensure accurate and helpful feedback.
