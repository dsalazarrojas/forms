# Leadership Assessment For Early Education - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leadership Assessment For Early Education form is designed to gather feedback on a teacher's leadership qualities. This feedback is intended to help teachers grow and improve their leadership skills, potentially as part of a larger performance evaluation process.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Please fill out the form carefully and honestly, providing your assessment based on your observations and experiences with the teacher's leadership qualities.**
- **Make sure to review each question carefully and select the rating that best reflects your assessment.**
- **You can select only one rating per question.**

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **teachers_name** (Text, optional): Enter the name of the teacher being assessed. This is not necessarily a required field, but providing it will help with later reference and identification.
- **school_name** (Text, optional): Enter the name of the school where the teacher works. Like with the teacher's name, this is not required but will be helpful for reference and context.
- **teacher_position** (Text, optional): Enter the teacher's position or title within the school. This can be helpful to understand their role and responsibilities.
- **assessment_date** (Date, optional): Record the date of the assessment. This is not a required field, but it's recommended for tracking and record-keeping purposes.
- **rating_leadership** (Select Multiple, optional): Rate the teacher's leadership qualities as Good or Fair. This is a subjective assessment, so consider the teacher's overall leadership skills and approach.
	+ **Good:** The teacher demonstrates strong leadership skills, inspiring and motivating students and colleagues.
	+ **Fair:** The teacher has some leadership qualities but may lack in certain areas or require improvement.
- **rating_effectiveness** (Select Multiple, optional): Rate the teacher's effectiveness in leading the class and school community. This is also a subjective assessment, focusing on their ability to get things done.
	+ **Good:** The teacher is highly effective in leading the class and school community.
	+ **Fair:** The teacher has some effectiveness but may struggle with specific tasks or areas.
- **rating_communicate** (Select Multiple, optional): Rate the teacher's communication skills. This is a crucial aspect of their leadership, affecting how they interact with students and staff.
	+ **Good:** The teacher is excellent at communicating their ideas and expectations clearly and effectively.
	+ **Fair:** The teacher has some communication skills but may struggle with clarity or consistency.
- **rating_inspire** (Select Multiple, optional): Rate the teacher's ability to inspire and motivate students and colleagues. This is a key aspect of their leadership, driving engagement and growth.
	+ **Good:** The teacher is highly inspiring and motivating, encouraging students and staff to reach their full potential.
	+ **Fair:** The teacher may lack some inspiration or motivation, affecting engagement and performance.

Note: The provided form fields are designed to gather specific, subjective assessments of the teacher's leadership qualities. Be honest and thoughtful in your evaluations to provide accurate and helpful feedback.
