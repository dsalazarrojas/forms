# Mentor Observation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback and data from mentors on new teachers during their observation process. The form helps to evaluate the new teacher's performance, identify areas for improvement, and track progress towards meeting teaching standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select the new teacher's name from the dropdown menu under "New Teacher".
2. Enter the date of the first observation in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2023).
3. Enter the frequency of observations, if any, in the text field (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, etc.).
4. Enter the duration of each observation in hours and minutes (e.g., 1 hour, 30 minutes, etc.).
5. Provide any comments or feedback about the observation in the text area.
6. Select the areas where the new teacher needs improvement (communication, lesson plan, classroom management, etc.).
7. Rate the overall satisfaction with the new teacher's performance (very satisfied, somewhat satisfied, not satisfied).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Teacher** (`new_teacher`, select_one): Select the name of the new teacher being observed. Choose from the list provided in the dropdown menu.
* **Date of First Observation** (`date_of_first_observation`, date): Enter the date of the first observation in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Frequency of Observation** (`frequency_of_observation`, text): If applicable, enter the frequency of observations, such as weekly, bi-weekly, etc.
* **Duration of Observation** (`observation_duration`, time): Enter the duration of each observation in hours and minutes (e.g., 1 hour 30 minutes).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note): Provide any comments or feedback about the observation.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, select_multiple): Select the areas where the new teacher needs improvement (communication, lesson plan, classroom management, etc.).
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one): Rate the overall satisfaction with the new teacher's performance (very satisfied, somewhat satisfied, not satisfied).
