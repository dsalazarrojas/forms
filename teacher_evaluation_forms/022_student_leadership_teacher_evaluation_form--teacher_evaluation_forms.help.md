# Student Leadership Teacher Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Leadership Teacher Evaluation Form is a document used to gather feedback and evaluation from teachers on a student's leadership qualities. This form will help teachers to provide valuable insights into a student's strengths and areas of improvement in leadership roles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information before starting the form.
2. Start by filling out the required fields, which include Student Name and Teacher Evaluation Form ID.
3. Provide an introduction to the evaluation (optional).
4. Choose the correct Option 1 from the select-one options.
5. Select multiple options from the select-multiple options.
6. Enter the student's leadership qualities and any additional comments.
7. Enter your evaluation date.
8. Enter your email address (if applicable).
9. Enter your phone number (if applicable).
10. Review and submit the form for final review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, Text, Required): This field is used to identify the student being evaluated.
* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, Text, Optional): A brief introduction to the evaluation.
* **Student Leadership Qualities** (`Student Leadership Qualities`, Text, Optional): Describe the student's leadership qualities and strengths.
* **Evaluation Date** (`Evaluation Date`, Date, Required): The date when the evaluation was performed.
* **Teacher Comments** (`Teacher Comments`, Text, Optional): Additional comments from the teacher.
* **Evaluation Average** (`Evaluation Average`, Number, Optional): A numerical evaluation of the student's leadership qualities (e.g., 1-5 scale).
* **Option 1** (`Option 1`, Select One, Optional): Choose one option from the list.
* **Select Multiple Options** (`Select Multiple Options`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select multiple options from the list.
* **Email** (`Email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Teacher Evaluation Form ID** (`Teacher Evaluation Form ID`, Text, Required): A unique identifier for the form.

**Tips**

* Please be as specific and detailed as possible in your evaluation and comments.
* Ensure that you complete all required fields before submitting the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities.
