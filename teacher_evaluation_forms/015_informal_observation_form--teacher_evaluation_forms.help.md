# informal_observation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "informal observation form" is a tool used for teachers' performance evaluation. The form collects feedback from observers on various aspects of a teacher's practice to assess their teaching skills, subject matter knowledge, and overall impact.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, start by reading the instructions and questions carefully.
- Answer all questions based on your observations and experiences with the teacher.
- Choose the correct answer options for each question.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, refer to your training and guidance provided by your institution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **teaching_skills** (select_multiple): Answer 'Yes' if the teacher demonstrates teaching skills such as lesson planning, delivery, and assessment. Answer 'No' otherwise.
- **subject_matter** (select_multiple): Answer 'Yes' if the teacher demonstrates subject matter knowledge and understanding. Answer 'No' otherwise.
- **communication_skills** (select_multiple): Answer 'Yes' if the teacher has effective communication skills, such as clarity, tone, and body language. Answer 'No' otherwise.
- **class_environment** (select_multiple): Answer 'Yes' if the teacher maintains a positive class environment, such as respect, engagement, and safety. Answer 'No' otherwise.
- **teacher_classroom_management** (select_multiple): Answer 'Yes' if the teacher demonstrates effective classroom management, such as organization, time management, and discipline. Answer 'No' otherwise.
- **professionalism** (select_multiple): Answer 'Yes' if the teacher demonstrates professionalism, such as a positive attitude, respect, and punctuality. Answer 'No' otherwise.
- **overall_impact** (select_one): Rate the overall impact of the teacher's performance, based on your observation. The options are: Highly Positive, Positive, Neutral, Negative, Highly Negative.
- **teacher_comment** (note): Provide any additional comments about the teacher's performance, including strengths and areas for improvement.

## Tips
- Be honest and objective in your assessment.
- Focus on specific behaviors and actions you observed.
- Provide concrete examples to support your answers.
- Avoid biases and personal opinions.
- Keep your comments concise and clear.
