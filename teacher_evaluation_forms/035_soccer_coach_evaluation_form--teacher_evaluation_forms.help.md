# Soccer Coach Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on a soccer coach's performance, helping to identify areas of strength and weakness. The form is intended to be used by team administrators or parents to provide constructive feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter the team name** (`team_name`): Please enter the name of the team being evaluated.
2. **Enter the coach's name** (`coach_name`): Please enter the name of the coach being evaluated.
3. **Assess Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`): Choose the best option from:
	* Option 1: Excellent
	* Option 2: Good
	* Option 3: Fair
4. **Evaluate Leadership Skills** (`leadership_skills`): Enter a text-based feedback on the coach's leadership skills.
5. **Rate Team Morale** (`team_morale`): Enter a numerical rating from 1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
6. **Assess Sportsmanship** (`sportsmanship`): Enter a numerical rating from 1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
7. **Evaluate Coach Development** (`coach_development`): Enter a time-based feedback, e.g., "Last month", "Quarterly", etc.
8. **Rate Coach Coaching** (`coach_coaching`): Enter a date-based feedback, e.g., "Last season", "Upcoming season", etc.
9. **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`): Choose the best option from:
	* Excellent
	* Good
	* Neutral
	* Bad
	* Very Bad
10. **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Share any additional feedback or suggestions you have for the coach.
11. **Enter Email** (`email`): Please enter the coach's email address.
12. **Enter Phone Number** (`phone`): Please enter the coach's phone number.
13. **Select Category** (`category`): Choose from:
	* Category 1
	* Category 2
	* Category 3

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name**: A field for entering the team name.
* **Coach Name**: A field for entering the coach's name.
* **Communication Skills**: Assess the coach's communication skills with team members, parents, and other staff. Choose from excellent, good, or fair.
* **Leadership Skills**: Enter a text-based feedback on the coach's leadership skills.
* **Team Morale**: Evaluate the team's morale using a numerical rating from 1-10.
* **Sportsmanship**: Evaluate the coach's sportsmanship using a numerical rating from 1-10.
* **Coach Development**: Enter a time-based feedback on the coach's development, e.g., last month, quarterly, etc.
* **Coach Coaching**: Enter a date-based feedback on the coach's coaching, e.g., last season, upcoming season, etc.
* **Overall Rating**: Choose from excellent, good, neutral, bad, or very bad.
* **Additional Comments**: Share any additional feedback or suggestions for the coach.
* **Email**: Enter the coach's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the coach's phone number.
* **Category**: Choose from category 1, category 2, or category 3.
