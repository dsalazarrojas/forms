# PM1 Teacher Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PM1 Teacher Evaluation form is a tool for students or administrators to provide feedback on a teacher's performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the teacher's name in the "Teacher Name" field.
2.  Select the school year for the teacher's performance.
3.  Indicate if the teacher is in the subject area you are evaluating.
4.  Enter the date of the evaluation.
5.  Select your overall experience with the teacher's quality of service.
6.  Check the boxes for the teacher's lesson planning, teaching style, student engagement, student progress, and classroom environment.
7.  Optionally, enter any additional comments about the teacher's availability or performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Teacher Name (text)**: Enter the teacher's name as they are known to you.
*   **School Year (number)**: Select the academic year you are evaluating the teacher for.
*   **Subject Area (select_one)**: Choose 'Yes' if the teacher is in the subject area you are evaluating.
*   **Evaluation Date (date)**: Enter the date of the evaluation in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
*   **Overall Quality of Service (select_multiple)**: Choose from 'Good', 'Poor' to rate the teacher's quality of service.
*   **Lesson Planning (select_multiple)**: Check 'Yes' if the teacher's lesson planning was effective.
*   **Teaching Style (select_multiple)**: Check 'Yes' if the teacher's teaching style was effective.
*   **Student Engagement (select_multiple)**: Check 'Yes' if the student engagement was high.
*   **Student Progress (select_multiple)**: Check 'Yes' if the student made progress under the teacher's guidance.
*   **Classroom Environment (select_multiple)**: Check 'Yes' if the classroom environment was well-organised.
*   **Teacher Availability (note)**: If you have any comments about the teacher's availability, enter them here.
*   **Teacher Comments (note)**: Enter any additional comments about the teacher's performance.

## Tips

*   Be honest and fair in your evaluation.
*   Use specific examples to support your comments.
*   Keep your comments concise and constructive.
*   If unsure about a field, feel free to ask for clarification.
