# teacher_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for evaluating a teacher's performance. It collects information on their general information, teaching criteria, and other relevant details to assess their overall performance and provide feedback for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant information for the teacher you're evaluating, such as their general information, evaluation criteria, and teaching information.
2. Choose the evaluation scale that best represents the teacher's performance.
3. Provide a brief comment on your evaluation of the teacher.
4. Rate the teacher's performance using the evaluation criteria.
5. Enter the date of the evaluation and the submission date.
6. Enter the teacher's name and email (if applicable).
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Info (ID 1)
* **General Info** (`general_info`, text, optional): Please enter a brief description of the teacher you're evaluating.
### Evaluation Criteria (ID 2)
* **Evaluation Criteria** (`evaluation_criterion`, select_one, optional): Please select the evaluation criteria that applies to the teacher's performance. Options: 'Yes', 'No'.
### Teacher Info (ID 3)
* **Teacher Info** (`teacher_info`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the relevant information about the teacher. Options: Mr, Ms, Dr.
### Evaluation Scale (ID 4)
* **Evaluation Scale** (`evaluation_scale`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the evaluation scale that best represents the teacher's performance. Options: Scale 5, Scale 7, Scale 9, Scale 10.
### Evaluation Comment (ID 5)
* **Evaluation Comment** (`evaluation_comment`, text, optional): Please provide a brief comment on your evaluation of the teacher.
### Teacher Evaluation (ID 6)
* **Teacher Evaluation** (`teacher_evaluation`, select_multiple, optional): Please rate the teacher's performance using the evaluation criteria. Options: Excellent, Good, Average.
### Date of Evaluation (ID 7)
* **Date of Evaluation** (`date_of_evaluation`, date, optional): Please enter the date of the evaluation.
### Submission Date (ID 8)
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Please enter the submission date of the evaluation.
### Teacher Name (ID 9)
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the teacher being evaluated.
### Teacher Email (ID 10)
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email, optional): Please enter the teacher's email address (if applicable).

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and honest information about the teacher's performance.
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
