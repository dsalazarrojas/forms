# mentor_evaluation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mentor Evaluation Form is designed to help evaluate a mentor's performance and provide feedback on their ability to give clear instructions, communicate ideas, provide feedback, be approachable, and provide timely feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information:
	* **Title**: Enter the title of the mentorship program or project.
	* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the program or project.
	* **Category**: Choose the category of the program or project.
	* **Tags**: Add any relevant tags or keywords related to the program or project.
2. Evaluate your mentor's performance:
	* **How would you rate your current mentor's overall ability to give clear instructions?**: Choose one of the rating options (Below average, Average, Above average, Not evaluated) based on your experience with the mentor.
	* **How would you rate your current mentor's overall ability to communicate their ideas clearly?**: Choose one of the rating options (Below average, Average, Above average, Not evaluated) based on your experience with the mentor.
	* **How would you rate your current mentor's overall ability to provide feedback?**: Choose one of the rating options (Below average, Average, Above average, Not evaluated) based on your experience with the mentor.
	* **How would you rate your current mentor's overall ability to be approachable?**: Choose one of the rating options (Below average, Average, Above average, Not evaluated) based on your experience with the mentor.
	* **How would you rate your current mentor's overall ability to provide timely feedback?**: Choose one of the rating options (Below average, Average, Above average, Not evaluated) based on your experience with the mentor.
3. Add any additional comments:
	* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your mentor's performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, not required): Enter a unique identifier for the form.
* **Form Title 1** (`form_title`, text, not required): Enter the title of the form.
* **Form Description 1** (`form_description`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the form.
* **Form Category 1** (`form_category`, text, not required): Choose the category of the form.
* **Form Tags 1** (`form_tags`, text, not required): Add any relevant tags or keywords related to the form.
* **How would you rate your current mentor's overall ability to give clear instructions?** (`rating1`, select_one, not required): Rate the mentor's ability to give clear instructions using one of the options (Below average, Average, Above average, Not evaluated).
* **How would you rate your current mentor's overall ability to communicate their ideas clearly?** (`rating2`, select_one, not required): Rate the mentor's ability to communicate ideas clearly using one of the options (Below average, Average, Above average, Not evaluated).
* **How would you rate your current mentor's overall ability to provide feedback?** (`rating3`, select_one, not required): Rate the mentor's ability to provide feedback using one of the options (Below average, Average, Above average, Not evaluated).
* **How would you rate your current mentor's overall ability to be approachable?** (`rating4`, select_one, not required): Rate the mentor's ability to be approachable using one of the options (Below average, Average, Above average, Not evaluated).
* **How would you rate your current mentor's overall ability to provide timely feedback?** (`rating5`, select_one, not required): Rate the mentor's ability to provide feedback using one of the options (Below average, Average, Above average, Not evaluated).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required): Add any additional comments about the mentor's performance.
* **Form ID 2** (`form_id`, text, not required): Enter a unique identifier for the second form section.
* **Form Title 2** (`form_title_2`, text, not required): Enter the title of the second form section.
* **Form Description 2** (`form_description_2`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the second form section.
* **Form Category 2** (`form_category_2`, text, not required): Choose the category of the second form section.
* **Form Tags 2** (`form_tags_2`, text, not required): Add any relevant tags or keywords related to the second form section.

Note: This form is for evaluation purposes only and should be completed honestly and objectively.
