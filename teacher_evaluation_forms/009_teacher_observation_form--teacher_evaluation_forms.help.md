# Teacher Observation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide a comprehensive assessment of a teacher's performance during an observation. It's essential to complete it accurately to ensure a fair evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand what each field represents.
2. Ensure you've observed the teacher in their regular classroom setting.
3. Fill out the form accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
4. Be specific and concrete in your answers.
5. Review and submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (Page 1):
	+ This section is for capturing basic information about the observation, such as the date and time of the observation.
* **Teacher Name** (Page 1):
	+ This field is for the name of the teacher being observed.
* **School Name** (Page 1):
	+ This field is for the name of the school where the observation took place.
* **Class Name** (Page 1):
	+ This field is for the name of the class being observed.
* **Overall Rating** (Page 1):
	+ This field is for your overall assessment of the teacher's performance, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Teaching Effectiveness** (Page 1):
	+ This field is for your assessment of the teacher's effectiveness in delivering the lesson, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Student Involvement** (Page 1):
	+ This field is for your assessment of how involved the students were in the lesson, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments** (Page 1):
	+ This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you have about the teacher's performance.

## Tips
* Be honest and objective in your assessment.
* Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your ratings.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or a colleague for guidance.
