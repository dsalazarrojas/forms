# Butcher Competition Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Butcher Competition Registration form is used to register competitors for the Butcher Competition. This form collects essential information from each competitor, including their contact details, business information, and competition details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Enter your competitor's name**, email address, phone number, and business name in the respective fields.
2. **Select the correct experience level** that reflects your level of expertise in the butchery profession.
3. **Choose the correct competition category**, which determines the type of competition you will be participating in.
4. **Indicate if you will be competing as a team** and if so, list the names of your team members.
5. **Specify any special equipment or requirements** that you may need during the competition.
6. **Agree to the competition rules**, which are essential for participating in the competition.
7. **Acknowledge the liability waiver**, which is a necessary step before participating in the competition.
8. **Enter the registration fee amount**, which is the cost of participating in the competition.
9. **Choose your preferred payment method**, which determines how you will pay the registration fee.
10. **Review and submit your registration** to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Competitor Full Name**: This field is used to capture the complete name of the competitor.
* **Email Address**: This field is used to collect the contact email address of the competitor.
* **Phone Number**: This field is used to collect the contact phone number of the competitor.
* **Business or Shop Name**: This field is used to collect the name of the competitor's business or shop.
* **Business Address**: This field is used to collect the full address of the competitor's business.
* **Business Phone Number**: This field is used to collect the contact phone number of the business.
* **Years in Butchering Business**: This field is used to capture the number of years the competitor has been in the butchery business.
* **Experience Level**: This field is used to select the competitor's level of expertise in the butchery profession.
* **Competition Category**: This field is used to select the type of competition the competitor will be participating in.
* **Will You Compete as Team**: This field is used to indicate if the competitor will be competing as a team.
* **Team Members Names**: This field is used to list the names of other team members if applicable.
* **Special Equipment or Requirements**: This field is used to specify any special equipment or requirements the competitor may need during the competition.
* **Do You Agree to Competition Rules**: This field is used to indicate if the competitor agrees to the competition rules.
* **Do You Accept Liability Waiver**: This field is used to acknowledge the liability waiver, which is a necessary step before participating in the competition.
* **Registration Fee Amount**: This field is used to collect the amount of the registration fee.
* **Preferred Payment Method**: This field is used to select the preferred payment method for the registration fee.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Review the competition rules and liability waiver before submitting your registration.
* The registration fee must be paid in the selected payment method.
