# promotional_contest_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering for a promotional contest. The contest can be a single-entry or multiple-entry event, offering various prizes, including gift cards, gift card bundles, or free services. To participate, fill in the required fields and agree to the terms of service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the contest type: single entry, multiple entries, or both.
2. Choose the prize type: gift card, gift card bundle, or free services.
3. Enter your first name, last name, and email address.
4. Provide your birthday.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Fill in your company name (if applicable).
7. Enter your address, city, and state.
8. Provide your zip code.
9. Enter a message or comment if you have any.
10. Choose the promotion you're interested in: win a year of freebies, free biz services, free products, win a car, or win a trip.
11. Select the entry date for the contest.
12. Confirm your contact information.
13. Accept the terms of service and legal disclaimers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your ID or documentation.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your ID or documentation.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Birthday**: Enter your birthday in the format `MM/DD/YY`.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Company**: If you're representing a company, enter its name.
* **Address**: Enter your physical address.
* **City**: Enter the city where you live or work.
* **State**: Enter the state where you live or work.
* **Zip**: Enter your postal code.
* **Message**: Optional field for any comments or message.
* **Promotion**: Choose the promotion you're interested in.
* **Entry Date**: Enter the date you're entering the contest.
* **Contest Type**: Select the type of contest: single entry, multiple entries, or both.
* **Prize Type**: Choose the type of prize: gift card, gift card bundle, or free services.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of entries you want to make.
* **Entry Limit**: Enter the maximum number of entries you can make.
* **Winner Limit**: Enter the maximum number of winners the contest can have.
* **Draw Date**: Enter the date when the draw will be held.
* **End Date**: Enter the last date to register for the contest.
* **Prize Description**: Optional field for describing the prize.
* **Rules**: Optional field for any specific rules of the contest.
* **Contact Info**: Confirm your contact information.
* **Terms Of Service**: Accept the terms of service for the contest.
* **Legal Disclaimers**: Accept the legal disclaimers for the contest.
