# Talent Show Auditions Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Talent Show Auditions Sign Up Form is designed to help us get to know you better and understand your talents. Please fill out this form to register for the talent show and we'll be in touch with you soon.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to provide your email address in the correct format (e.g. name@domain.com).
3. Be realistic when selecting your availability for the auditions.
4. Choose the correct submitter from the list.
5. Double-check your bio to ensure it is a brief description of your talent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contestant Name** (`contestant_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as you would like to be referred to in the talent show.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address to receive important updates and notifications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Talent Type** (`talent_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of talent you will be showcasing in the talent show (e.g. singing, dancing, etc.).
* **Act Length** (`act_length`, number, required: false): Indicate the approximate length of time you will perform your act (in minutes).
* **Bio** (`bio`, note, required: false): Briefly describe your talent and what you can expect to see in your performance.
* **Availability** (`availability`, time, required: false): Select the date and time you are available to be auditioned, taking into account that we'll try to schedule auditions around your chosen time slots.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the person or organization submitting this form on your behalf (e.g. school, family member, etc.).

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* If you have any special requests or need help, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We'll be in touch with you once we receive your registration.
