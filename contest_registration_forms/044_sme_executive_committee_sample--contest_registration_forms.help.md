# SME Executive Committee Sample - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information from potential speakers for the SME Executive Committee. It is not a survey or a data collection form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these general steps:

* Please fill in your First name and Last name in the corresponding fields.
* If you are interested in presenting to the SME Executive Committee, fill in the Executive Committee Submission Form with a brief note about your topic or subject matter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Call for Speakers**: This field is a text field where you can write a brief note about your interest in presenting or speaking at the SME Executive Committee.
* **First name**: This field is for your first name, and you should fill it in accordingly.
* **Last name**: This field is for your last name, and you should fill it in accordingly.
* **Company**: If you are representing a company or have a company, you can fill in the name here.
* **Executive Committee Submission Form**: This field is a note field where you can submit your topic or subject matter for consideration by the SME Executive Committee.
* **Executive Committee Submission Form**: This field has no specific requirements and you can fill it in as you see fit.
 
## Tips
* Please be as brief and clear as possible in your submission.
* Do not provide any sensitive or confidential information.
* The information collected will be used to review and consider you for presentation at the SME Executive Committee.
