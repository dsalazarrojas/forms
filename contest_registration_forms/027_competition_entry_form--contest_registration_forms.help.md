# Competition Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Competition Entry Form is a registration form for individuals or groups to submit their entries for a competition. To complete this form, follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Entrant Full Name field with your or your group leader's full name.
2. Enter your Entrant Email Address in the corresponding field.
3. Select the relevant Competition Category from the dropdown list.
4. Add the Title of Your Entry and provide a Brief Description of Entry in the text fields.
5. Enter the Total Entry Fee Amount in the corresponding field.
6. Select your Preferred Payment Method from the dropdown list.
7. If you've paid the entry fee using a bank transfer or online payment, enter the Payment Reference Number.
8. Read and agree to the Terms and Conditions, including copyright and usage rights.
9. Finally, enter the Date of Entry using the calendar picker.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Entrant Full Name** (`entrant_full_name`, text, **required**): Enter your full name as the leader of the entry.
* **Entrant Email Address** (`entrant_email_address`, email, **required**): Enter your email address for official entry notifications.
* **Competition Category** (`competition_category_entry`, select_one, **required**): Select the category your entry belongs to (e.g., Arts and Design, Technology / Innovation, etc.).
* **Title of Your Entry** (`specific_entry_title`, text, **required**): Enter a title for your entry (e.g., Project Name, Song Title, etc.).
* **Brief Description of Entry** (`entry_description_summary`, text, **required**): Provide a 200-word summary of your entry.
* **Total Entry Fee Amount** (`entry_fee_amount`, number, **required**): Enter the total amount you are paying for the entry fee in USD.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method_selection`, select_one, **required**): Select how you will pay the entry fee (e.g., Credit / Debit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, etc.).
* **Payment Reference Number** (`payment_reference_number`, text, **optional**): If you've paid using a bank transfer or online payment, enter the transaction ID.
* **Date of Entry** (`entry_submission_date`, date, **required**): Enter the current date using the calendar picker.
* **I Agree to Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions_entry`, select_one, **required**): Select "I Agree" to confirm you have read and understood the terms and conditions.
