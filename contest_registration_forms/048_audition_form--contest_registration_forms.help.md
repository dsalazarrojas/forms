# Audition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from performing artists interested in participating in auditions. It is used by production teams to gather details about the performer's background, experience, and requirements for the audition.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name as it appears on your official identification.
2. Provide your stage name or professional name, if different from your full name.
3. Enter your contact email address and phone number.
4. Enter your mailing address.
5. Enter your date of birth for age verification.
6. Indicate your age verification status.
7. If you are a minor, provide the name and contact information of your parent or guardian.
8. Select the type of performance you are auditioning for (theater, film, etc.).
9. List your relevant skills and talents.
10. Indicate your experience level (professional, semi-professional, trained, etc.).
11. If you are a union member, select the relevant union.
12. Specify your availability for the audition.
13. Indicate if you have an agent or manager, and provide their contact information if applicable.
14. If you have submitted a headshot, select the relevant option.
15. If you have submitted a resume, select the relevant option.
16. Provide a link to your demo reel or portfolio, if applicable.
17. List any special requirements or accommodations you may need.
18. Finally, type your full name as an electronic signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performer Full Name (1)**: This is the legal name of the performer.
* **Stage Name (2)**: This is the professional or stage name of the performer, if different from their full name.
* **Email Address (3)**: This is the contact email address of the performer.
* **Phone Number (4)**: This is the contact phone number of the performer.
* **Mailing Address (5)**: This is the full mailing address of the performer.
* **Date of Birth (6)**: This is the date of birth of the performer, required for age verification.
* **Age Verification (7)**: This indicates if the performer is 18 years of age or older.
* **Parent or Guardian Name (8)**: If the performer is a minor, provide the name and contact information of their parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Contact (9)**: If the performer is a minor, provide the contact information of their parent or guardian.
* **Production Name (10)**: This is the name of the production the performer is auditioning for.
* **Role Auditioning For (11)**: This is the specific role the performer is auditioning for.
* **Audition Date (12)**: This is the date of the audition.
* **Audition Location (13)**: This is the location of the audition.
* **Performance Type (14)**: This is the type of performance the performer is auditioning for (theater, film, etc.).
* **Skills and Talents (15)**: This is a list of the performer's relevant skills and talents.
* **Experience Level (16)**: This indicates the performer's experience level (professional, semi-professional, trained, etc.).
* **Union Membership (17)**: This indicates if the performer is a union member or not.
* **Availability (18)**: This is the performer's availability for the audition.
* **Representation (19)**: This indicates if the performer has an agent or manager, and their contact information if applicable.
* **Representation Contact (20)**: This is the contact information of the performer's agent or manager.
* **Headshot Submitted (21)**: This indicates if the performer has submitted a headshot.
* **Resume Submitted (22)**: This indicates if the performer has submitted a resume.
* **Demo Reel Link (23)**: This is the URL of the performer's demo reel or portfolio.
* **Special Requirements (24)**: This is any special requirements or accommodations the performer may need.
* **Performer Signature (25)**: This is the electronic signature of the performer, typed in their full name.
