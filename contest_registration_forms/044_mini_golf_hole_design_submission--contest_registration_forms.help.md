# mini-golf-hole-design-submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for designers to submit their mini golf course creations and associated metadata.

## How To Complete This Form

To successfully submit a mini golf course design, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields.
2. Provide accurate and complete information for all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contest Name (Page 1)**: Enter the name of the contest you are submitting your design for.
* **Designer Name (Page 1)**: Enter your name as the designer of the submitted mini golf course.
* **Description (Page 1)**: Provide a brief description of your design, if desired.
* **Sketch Uploads (Page 1)**: Upload any relevant sketches or images of your design. 
* **Design File Uploads (Page 1)**: Upload any supporting files or documents related to your design.
* **Submit Date (Page 1)**: Enter the date you would like to submit your design.
* **Submit Time (Page 1)**: Choose the time of day you would like to submit your design.
* **Location (Page 2)**: Select either 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether your design includes a location feature.
* **Submit By Email (Page 2)**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number (Page 2)**: Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address (Page 2)**: Enter your address.
* **City (Page 2)**: Enter your city.
* **State (Page 2)**: Enter your state.
* **Zip (Page 2)**: Enter your zip code.
* **Country (Page 2)**: Enter your country.
* **Notes (Page 2)**: Provide any additional notes about your design.
* **Contest Category (Page 3)**: Select a category for your design.
* **Contest Tag (Page 3)**: Enter tags for your design.
* **Designer Website (Page 3)**: Enter your website, if applicable.
* **Designer Email (Page 3)**: Enter your email address.
* **Designer Sketches (Page 3)**: Select whether you have sketches or images of your design.
* **Designer Bio (Page 3)**: Enter a brief bio about yourself.
* **Designer Image (Page 3)**: Upload an image of yourself.
* **Contest Image (Page 3)**: Upload an image of your design.
* **Submission Date (Page 3)**: Enter the date you submitted your design.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when providing information.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in.
* Use valid and relevant data for all fields.
