# Poetry Contest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Poetry Contest Registration Form is designed to collect information from participants who wish to enter the contest. This form is used to gather details about the participant and their poem, ensuring a smooth and efficient registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of institution or organization you represent (School or Organization).
2. Enter your first name.
3. Enter your last name.
4. Provide your email address.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Choose the type of poetry you are submitting (Free Verse, Narrative, or Sonnet).
7. Select the entry form you are using (Entry Form 1, Entry Form 2, or Entry Form 3).
8. Enter the title of your poem.
9. Enter your poem in the provided text box.
10. Provide your address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School or Organization** (`school_or_organization`): This field allows you to select whether you are representing a school or an organization.
* **Participant First Name** (`participant_first_name`): This field is used to enter your first name.
* **Participant Last Name** (`participant_last_name`): This is used to enter your last name.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`): Enter your email address here.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Poetry Type** (`poetry_type`): Choose the type of poetry you are submitting (Free Verse, Narrative, or Sonnet).
* **Entry Form** (`entry_form`): Select the entry form you are using (Entry Form 1, Entry Form 2, or Entry Form 3).
* **Entry Title** (`entry_title`): Enter the title of your poem.
* **Entry Poem** (`entry_poem`): This is the field where you can enter your poem.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`): Provide your address.

## Tips

* Ensure that you enter the correct type of poetry and entry form to avoid disqualification.
* Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
* Enter your poem carefully and completely, as this is the primary content being evaluated in the contest.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the contest organizers for assistance.
