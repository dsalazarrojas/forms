# Smart Store Concept Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Store Concept Registration Form is used to register your new store concept with the company. It's a mandatory step to get on board with the company's smart store program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the Smart Store Concept field with a brief description of your store concept.
2. Provide your Business Details in the corresponding field.
3. Specify your Technology Interests for the smart store program.
4. Enter your Retailer Details in the specified field.
5. Enter your Store Name.
6. Enter your Store Address.
7. Select the type of store you are registering (Store Type).
8. Choose the size of your store (Store Size).
9. Enter the date your store is located (Store Location).
10. Enter the opening time of your store (Store Opening).
11. Enter your Business Phone number.
12. Enter your Email address.
13. Enter your Store Website URL (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Smart Store Concept**: Enter a brief description of your store concept.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Business Details**: Enter your company's business details, including company name, address, etc.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Technology Interests**: Specify your technology interests for the smart store program.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Retailer Details**: Enter your retailer details, including contact information.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Store Name**: Enter your store's name.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Store Address**: Enter your store's address.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Store Type**: Select the type of store you are registering (e.g. Option 1, Option 2).
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: no
* **Store Size**: Choose the size of your store (e.g. Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: no
* **Store Location**: Enter the date your store is located.
	+ Type: date
	+ Required: no
* **Store Opening**: Enter the opening time of your store.
	+ Type: time
	+ Required: no
* **Business Phone**: Enter your business phone number.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
	+ Type: email
	+ Required: no
* **Website**: Enter your store's website URL.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no

Note: The form fields are not required by default, but it's recommended to fill all the fields to ensure accurate registration.
