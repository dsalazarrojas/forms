# Energy Product Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect entries for an energy product contest. Please ensure that you understand the form's purpose and what kind of information is being collected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** (fields 1 and 2). Please note that your last name is optional.
2. Enter your **Email** (field 3) in the correct format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
3. Enter your **Company** (field 4) name, if applicable.
4. Provide the **Product Name** (field 5) of the energy product you are submitting.
5. Describe your energy product in detail in the **Description** (field 6) field.
6. If applicable, provide a **URL** (link) to a high-quality image of your product (field 7).
7. If applicable, provide a **Video URL** (link) to a video of your product (field 8).
8. If providing an image, add an **Image Alt Text** (field 9) to describe the image for accessibility purposes.
9. If applicable, upload an image of your product (field 10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (field 1, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (field 2, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (field 3, required): Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
* **Company** (field 4, optional): Enter the name of the company you work for or represent, if applicable.
* **Product Name** (field 5, required): Enter the name of the energy product you are submitting.
* **Description** (field 6, required): Describe your energy product in detail, including its features and benefits.
* **Image URL** (field 7, optional): Provide a URL (link) to a high-quality image of your product.
* **Video URL** (field 8, optional): Provide a URL (link) to a video of your product.
* **Image Alt Text** (field 9, optional): Add a text describing your image for accessibility purposes.
* **Product Image** (field 10, optional): Upload an image of your product.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Ensure that the image you upload is a high-quality image.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact form@gic.mx for clarification.
