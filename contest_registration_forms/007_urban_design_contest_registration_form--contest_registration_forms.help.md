# Urban Design Contest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants who wish to take part in the urban design contest. It is required to be filled out in order to be considered a participant in the contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* First name
	* Last name
	* Email
	* Phone number
	* Date of birth
2. Provide your address:
	* City
	* Country
3. Describe your urban design:
	* Design description
	* Design type (yes/no)
	* Design scale (yes/no)
4. Submitter information:
	* Whether you are the submitter (yes/no)
	* Type of submitter (yes/no)
	* Email of submitter
	* Phone number of submitter
	* Address of submitter
	* City of submitter
	* Country of submitter
5. Enter submission date

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city.
* **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Enter your country.
* **Design description** (`design_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your urban design.
* **Design type** (`design_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose whether your design is yes or no.
* **Design scale** (`design_scale`, select_multiple, optional): Choose whether your design is yes or no.
* **Submission date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of submission in the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you are the submitter or not.
* **Submitter type** (`submitter_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of submitter.
* **Submitter email** (`submitter_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the submitter.
* **Submitter phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the submitter.
* **Submitter address** (`submitter_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the submitter.
* **Submitter city** (`submitter_city`, text, optional): Enter the city of the submitter.
* **Submitter country** (`submitter_country`, text, optional): Enter the country of the submitter.
