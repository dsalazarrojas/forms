# Influencer Contest Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a brand's interest in participating in a contest. It is intended for authorized brand representatives to provide information about their brand and the contest they want to participate in.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the brand name and contest name in the designated fields.
2. Provide a valid email address.
3. Enter a phone number.
4. Choose a start and end date for the contest.
5. Optionally, specify the location.
6. Confirm your agreement with the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Brand Name (1)** (brand_name, text, required): Enter the name of the brand you represent.
* **Contest Name (2)** (contest_name, text, required): Enter the name of the contest you want to participate in.
* **Email** (3) (email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone (4)** (phone, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Start Date (5)** (start_date, date, required): Enter the start date of the contest.
* **End Date (6)** (end_date, date, required): Enter the end date of the contest.
* **Location (7)** (location, text, optional): Enter the location where the contest will take place, if applicable.
* **Agreement (8)** (agreement, select_one, required): Choose 'Agreement' to confirm your agreement with the terms and conditions.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter the correct brand name and contest name as they appear in the contest's official documentation.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* The start and end dates should be in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* The location field is optional, but if you do not enter a location, it will be assumed that the contest is open to all locations.
* Make sure to review and agree to the terms and conditions before submitting the form.
