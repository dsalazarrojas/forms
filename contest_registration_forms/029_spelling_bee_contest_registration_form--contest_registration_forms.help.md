# Spelling Bee Contest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects participant information for the Spelling Bee contest. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to register your school and participant(s) for the contest.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field carefully, as the information will be used for participant registration and communication.
2. Make sure to enter the correct school name if you are registering on behalf of a school.
3. If you're unsure about any field, please ask a contest staff member for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name (1)**: Enter the name of your school. If you're registering for the first time, please ensure that your school is participating in this year's contest.
* **Participant Name (2)**: Enter the name of the participant you are registering for the contest.
* **Participant Grade Level (3)**: Select "Yes" if the participant is eligible to compete. If "No", please provide a brief explanation in the space below.
* **Participant Shirt Size (4)**: Choose the size of shirt you would prefer for your participant. Please select "Small", "Medium", "Large", "X-Large", or "XX-Large".
* **Participant Shirt Color (5)**: Select the color of shirt you would prefer for your participant. Please select multiple colors as necessary (e.g., Red, Blue, Green).
* **Participant Parent Name (6)**: Enter the name of the participant's parent/guardian.
* **Participant Parent Email (7)**: Enter the email address of the participant's parent/guardian.
* **Participant Parent Phone (8)**: Enter the phone number of the participant's parent/guardian.
* **Participant Parent Relationship (9)**: Select "Parent", "Guardian", or "Other" to indicate your relationship with the participant.
* **Participant Parent School (10)**: Enter the name of the school your participant attends (if not the same as the school name above).

## Tips
* Please make sure to register each participant separately, even if you're registering multiple participants from the same school.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact contest staff for assistance.
* Ensure that you have permission from the participant's parent/guardian before registering them for the contest.
* Review your registration carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
