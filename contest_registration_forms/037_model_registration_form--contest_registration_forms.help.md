# Model Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from model participants, ensuring accurate and comprehensive data collection for modeling purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your First Name in the text field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your Last Name in the text field labeled "Last Name".
3. Enter your birth date in the format YYYY-MM-DD in the text field labeled "Birth Date".
4. Enter your Contact Email in the format of a valid email address in the text field labeled "Contact Email".
5. Enter your Contact Phone number in the text field labeled "Contact Phone".
6. Enter your Contact Address in the text field labeled "Contact Address".
7. Provide any relevant Health Info in the text field labeled "Health Info".
8. Enter your Measurements in the text field labeled "Measurements".
9. Enter your Height in the format of a whole number in the text field labeled "Height".
10. Enter your Weight in the format of a whole number in the text field labeled "Weight".
11. Enter your Measurements Helix in the text field labeled "Measurements Helix".
12. Enter your Measurements Bust in the text field labeled "Measurements Bust".
13. Enter your Waist measurement in the format of a whole number in the text field labeled "Waist".
14. Enter your Hip measurement in the format of a whole number in the text field labeled "Hip".
15. Enter your Shoulder measurement in the format of a whole number in the text field labeled "Shoulder".
16. Enter your Chest measurement in the format of a whole number in the text field labeled "Chest".
17. Enter your Head measurement in the text field labeled "Head".
18. Enter your Measurements Hemlines in the text field labeled "Measurements Hemlines".
19. Enter your Neck measurement in the text field labeled "Neck".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is displayed on any official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it is displayed on any official documents.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, required): Enter your birth date in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, optional): Enter your contact address.
* **Health Info** (`health_info`, text, required): Describe any relevant health information.
* **Measurements** (`measurements`, text, optional): Enter any relevant measurements.
* **Height** (`height`, number, required): Enter your height in centimeters.
* **Weight** (`weight`, number, required): Enter your weight in kilograms.
* **Measurements Helix** (`measurements_helix`, text, required): Enter any relevant measurements.
* **Measurements Bust** (`measurements_bust`, text, required): Enter any relevant measurements.
* **Waist** (`waist`, number, required): Enter your waist measurement in centimeters.
* **Hip** (`hip`, number, required): Enter your hip measurement in centimeters.
* **Shoulder** (`shoulder`, number, required): Enter your shoulder measurement in centimeters.
* **Chest** (`chest`, number, required): Enter your chest measurement in centimeters.
* **Head** (`head`, text, required): Describe your head measurement.
* **Measurements Hemlines** (`measurements_hemlines`, text, required): Enter any relevant measurements.
* **Neck** (`neck`, text, required): Describe your neck measurement.

## Tips
Please ensure that the provided information is accurate and up-to-date. This form is intended for single-use registration, so avoid submitting multiple entries for the same model. If you have any questions or concerns, contact the model administrator.
