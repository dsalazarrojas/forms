# Macaroni and Cheese Contest Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Macaroni and Cheese Contest Entry form is used to collect information from participants entering the contest. This form gathers data about the participant, their school, and their entry.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your recipe in the "Enter Your Recipe" field. This is the main entry point for your entry.
2. Upload a photo of your entry in the "Photo" field. This will be displayed alongside your entry.
3. Provide your name and contact information in the "First Name", "Last Name", "Email", "Participant Name", "Participant Email", and "Participant Phone" fields. This is necessary for us to contact you and display your entry.
4. Enter your school name and grade level in the "School Name" and "School Grade" fields. This helps us identify your school affiliation.
5. Enter your participant's school name and grade level in the "Participant School" and "Participant School Grade" fields.
6. Select the type of entry you are submitting (Festival, School, Nonprofit, Brand, or Other) in the "Entry Type" field.
7. Enter a description of your entry in the "Recipe Description" field.
8. If you have a preferred photo caption for your participant, enter it in the "Participant Photo Caption" field.
9. Enter the URL of your recipe in the "Recipe URL" field.
10. Enter the date and time of your entry in the "Entry Date" and "Entry Time" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Your Recipe** (text): Enter a description of your recipe.
* **Photo** (text): Upload a photo of your entry.
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Name** (text, required): Enter your participant's name.
* **Participant Email** (email, required): Enter your participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (text, required): Enter your participant's phone number.
* **School Name** (text, required): Enter your school name.
* **School Age** (number, required): Enter your school age.
* **School Grade** (text, required): Enter your school grade level.
* **Nonprofit Name** (text, required): Enter your nonprofit name.
* **Brand Name** (text, required): Enter your brand name.
* **Participant Age** (number, required): Enter your participant's age.
* **Entry Type** (select_one, required): Select the type of entry you are submitting (Festival, School, Nonprofit, Brand, or Other).
* **Recipe Description** (text, required): Enter a description of your recipe.
* **Participant Photo Caption** (text, required): Enter a preferred photo caption for your participant.
* **Recipe URL** (text, required): Enter the URL of your recipe.
* **Entry Date** (date, required): Enter the date of your entry.
* **Entry Time** (time, required): Enter the time of your entry.

Please note that all fields marked as "required" must be completed in order to submit a valid entry.
