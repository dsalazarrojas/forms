# Choral Festival Tournament Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for choirs or groups to register for the Choral Festival Tournament. It collects information about the ensemble, contact details, and performance specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
   - **Choir or group name**: Enter the official name of your choir or group.
   - **Director or contact name**: Enter the name and contact information for your primary contact person.
   - **Contact email**: Enter the email address to be used for event correspondence.
   - **Category entering**: Choose the competition category your choir is entering (Adult, Youth, Community, or School).
   - **Number of performers**: Enter the total number of performers in your choir or group.
   - **Repertoire list**: Provide a list of titles and composers for your performances.
   - **Emergency contact name and phone**: Enter the name and phone number of someone to contact on event day.
2. Optionally, fill in:
   - **Contact phone number**: Provide your primary contact's phone number for event day coordination.
   - **Technical requirements**: Mention any specific AV or accompanist needs for your performances.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Choir or group name** (`Choir_name`, `text`, required): Enter the official name of your choir or group.
* **Director or contact name** (`Director_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name and contact information for your primary contact person.
* **Contact email** (`Contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address to be used for event correspondence.
* **Contact phone number** (`Contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Provide your primary contact's phone number for event day coordination.
* **Category entering** (`Category_entering`, `select_one`, required): Choose the competition category your choir is entering from the options (Adult, Youth, Community, or School).
* **Number of performers** (`Number_of_performers`, `number`, required): Enter the total number of performers in your choir or group.
* **Repertoire list** (`Repertoire_list`, `text`, required): Provide a list of titles and composers for your performances.
* **Technical requirements** (`Technical_requirements`, `text`, optional): Mention any specific AV or accompanist needs for your performances.
* **Emergency contact name and phone** (`Emergency_contact`, `text`, required): Enter the name and phone number of someone to contact on event day.
*   **Contact phone number** (`Contact_phone`, `text`, optional): This field is redundant and will be removed in future updates.
*   **Technical requirements** (`Technical_requirements`, `text`, optional): This field is for specifying AV or accompanist needs, if any.
