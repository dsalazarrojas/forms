# Radio Jingle Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants who want to enter the Radio Jingle Tournament. Fill out this form to submit your entry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your information, including your name and email address.
2. Choose the type of entry you are submitting (e.g., Radio Jingle, Jingle, Song, or Remix).
3. If you have an audio file associated with your entry, provide the filename or description.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (1)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (2)**: Enter your last name.
* **Nickname (3)**: Enter a nickname you prefer to be known by.
* **Email (4)**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Radio Station (5)**: Enter the radio station you are associated with.
* **Entry Type (6)**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Radio Jingle: A jingle associated with a radio station.
	+ Jingle: A jingle.
	+ Song: A song.
	+ Remix: A remix.
* **Audio File (7)**: If you have a file associated with your entry, enter the filename.
* **Submit (8)**: Click this button to submit your entry.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to avoid delays in processing your entry.
* If you are unable to submit an audio file, do not worry, it's optional.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact "form@gic.mx".
