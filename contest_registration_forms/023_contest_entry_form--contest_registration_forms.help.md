# Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contest Entry Form is used to collect information from contestants entering a contest. This form is used to ensure that each entry is properly processed and that all required information is provided.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the entrant's personal information, including their full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the appropriate category for your entry.
3. Enter the title of your entry.
4. Provide a brief description of your entry.
5. Upload or reference your entry file.
6. Enter the date you submitted your entry.
7. Confirm that you are 18 years of age or older (if under 18, ensure you have obtained parental consent).
8. Certify that this is your original work.
9. Grant permission for your entry to be displayed online.
10. Indicate your entry fee payment status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   *Entrant Full Name*: Enter your full name to identify yourself.
*   *Email Address*: Enter your email address to facilitate communication.
*   *Phone Number*: Enter your phone number for further contact.
*   *Contest Category*: Choose the correct category for your entry from the list.
*   *Title of your Entry*: Enter a brief and descriptive title for your entry.
*   *Brief Description/Statement*: Provide a 150-word maximum description of your work.
*   *Uploaded File Name Reference*: Enter the name of the file you uploaded, e.g., "my_artwork_v1.jpg".
*   *Submission Date*: Enter the date you submitted your entry in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
*   *-- Contest Rules --*: This is a note section for you to review the contest rules.
*   *I am 18 years of age or older*: Confirm that you meet the age requirement for entry, or if under 18, indicate that you have obtained parental consent.
*   *I certify this is my own original work*: Confirm that this is your original work, and if not, choose "Other".
*   *I grant permission to display my work online*: Confirm that you grant permission for your entry to be displayed online for promotional and exhibition purposes.
*   *Entry Fee Payment Status*: Indicate the payment status of your entry fee.
*   *Additional Comments (Optional)*: Provide any additional information you'd like to share about your entry. This field is not required but can be useful for your work to be understood better.

Note: All required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*)
