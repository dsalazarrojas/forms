# Sneaker Design Contest Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect submissions for a sneaker design contest. It aims to gather essential information from interested participants, including their contact details, design descriptions, and terms of service agreements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your submitter information, including your name and contact details.
2. Upload your artwork files and design files, if applicable.
3. Describe your design in the text field "Design Description."
4. Select the type of design you are submitting (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
5. Choose the category of your design (e.g., Category 1, Category 2, or Category 3).
6. Check the boxes to agree or disagree with the terms of service, terms of conditions, privacy policy, and referral policy.
7. Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitter Information**: This is the section where you provide your personal contact information, including your name and contact details. This information is required for us to contact you if we need more information or have questions about your submission.
* **Artwork Files**: This field is for uploading the artwork related to your design submission. Please upload files that best represent your design concept.
* **Design Description**: This text field is for providing a detailed description of your design. Be as clear and concise as possible so we can understand your concept.
* **Upload Design File**: This field is for uploading your design file, if applicable.
* **Select Design Type**: Choose the type of design you are submitting from the options provided. This will help us categorize your design submission.
* **Select Design Category**: Select the category that best fits your design from the options provided.
* **Submitter Notes**: This is an optional text field where you can provide any additional comments or notes about your submission.
* **Submitter Terms of Service**: Check the box to indicate that you agree to the terms of service. This is a mandatory field.
* **Submitter Agreement**: Check the box to agree with the submission agreement terms. This is a mandatory field.
* **Submitter Terms of Conditions**: Check the box to agree with the terms of conditions. This is a mandatory field.
* **Submitter Privacy Policy**: Check the box to agree with the privacy policy. This is a mandatory field.
* **Submitter Referral**: Check the box to indicate that you have read and agree with the referral policy. This is a mandatory field.
* **Submitter Terms of Service 2**: Check the box to indicate that you have read and agree with the terms of service 2. This is a mandatory field.
