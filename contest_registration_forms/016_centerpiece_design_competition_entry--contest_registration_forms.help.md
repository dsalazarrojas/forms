# Centerpiece Design Competition Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the centerpiece design competition entry. It is used to gather data from designers participating in the competition, including their personal details, design attributes, and photo submissions. The form aims to ensure that all necessary information is captured accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Begin by filling in your personal details, including your full name, email address, and phone number. These are required fields to help us contact you regarding competition updates and notifications.
2.  Select the competition category that best fits your design from the provided options.
3.  Enter a unique title for your design and provide a brief description of its concept and inspiration.
4.  Choose the primary materials used in your design from the list of options.
5.  Enter the height and width of your design in inches.
6.  Confirm the status of your photo submission, whether it's already uploaded or not.
7.  Agree to the competition rules and consent for your work to be photographed.
8.  Finally, select the date of entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Designer Full Name**: This is your name as it appears on official documents.
*   **Designer Email Address**: Enter your email address for competition updates and notifications.
*   **Designer Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for additional contact purposes.
*   **Design Category**: Choose the category that best fits your design from the provided options.
*   **Title of Design**: Enter a unique name for your design.
*   **Description of Design**: Provide a brief explanation of the concept and inspiration behind your work.
*   **Primary Materials**: Select all the materials used in your design from the provided options.
*   **Height of Design (inches)**: Enter the height of your design in inches.
*   **Width of Design (inches)**: Enter the width of your design in inches.
*   **Design Photo Upload Status**: Confirm the status of your photo submission.
*   **Competition Rule and Photo Consent**: Agree to the competition rules and for your work to be photographed.
*   **Entry Date**: Enter today's date to finalize your submission.
