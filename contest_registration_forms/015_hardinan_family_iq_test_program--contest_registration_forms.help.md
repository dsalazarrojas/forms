# Hardinan Family IQ Test Program - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hardinan Family IQ Test Program is designed for individuals to assess their cognitive abilities through a series of questions and tests. This program aims to evaluate users' intelligence quotient (IQ) and provide insights into their cognitive strengths and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To register for the Hardinan Family IQ Test Program, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "user_first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "user_last_name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "user_email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "user_phone" field.
5. Select your birth date in the "birth_date" field.
6. Select your birth time in the "birth_time" field.
7. Enter your IQ score, if you have one, in the "iq_score" field.
8. Choose the IQ test you'd like to take from the "selected_iq_test" dropdown menu.
9. Select any additional options you'd like to participate in from the "selected_iq_option" and "selected_iq_option2" multiple-select menus.
10. Provide any additional comments or information about your participation in the "additional_comments" note field.
