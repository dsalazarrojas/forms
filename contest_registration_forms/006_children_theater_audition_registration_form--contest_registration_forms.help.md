# Children Theater Audition Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Children Theater Audition Registration Form is a required document for children to participate in theater auditions. It is used to collect essential information about the child, including their name, contact details, performance experience, and any special requirements they may need during the audition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form entirely, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Enter the child's and guardian's contact information accurately.
3. Choose the correct age group and experience level for the child.
4. Provide a clear description of the audition piece to be performed.
5. If necessary, indicate any special requirements or accommodations needed during the audition.
6. Confirm guardian consent to participate and follow the rules.
7. Enter the emergency contact phone number for the child.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guardian Name**: The full name of the parent or guardian registering the child.
* **Guardian Email**: The email used for audition communications with the child or guardian.
* **Child Name**: The full legal name of the child participating in the audition.
* **Child Date of Birth**: The date of birth of the child for age verification purposes.
* **Age Group**: Select the correct age bracket for the child (4 to 6, 7 to 9, 10 to 12, or 13 to 15).
* **Audition Piece**: Describe the song, monologue, or performance piece the child will perform during the audition.
* **Experience Level**: Choose the level of prior performance experience the child has (None, Some experience, Regular classes or performances).
* **Special Requirements**: Note any accommodations or special requirements the child may need during the audition (e.g., wheelchair accessibility).
* **Parent Consent**: Confirm guardian consent to participate and follow the audition rules.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian to be contacted in case of an emergency during the audition.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are completed accurately to avoid delays in the registration process.
* Double-check the child's age group and experience level to ensure they are placed in the correct age bracket.
* Provide clear descriptions of the audition piece and any special requirements to ensure a smooth and enjoyable audition process for everyone involved.
