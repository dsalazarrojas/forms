# Downhill Skiing Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from participants for the Downhill Skiing Tournament. It is intended to gather essential details about each participant, including general information, participant contact details, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
3. Review your information carefully before final submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (`General Info`, `text`, required/optional): This field collects general information about the participant.
* **Participant Info** (`Participant Info`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide details about the participant, such as name and contact information.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, `text`, required/optional): In the event of an emergency, provide contact information for someone who can be reached in case of an urgent situation.
* **Medical Info** (`Medical Info`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide any relevant medical information that may be essential for our event staff and medical team.
* **Ski Info** (`Ski Info`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for information about the participant's skiing skills and experience.
* **Emergency Release** (`Emergency Release`, `text`, required/optional): If the participant is under 18 years old, a parent or guardian must sign this field.
* **Registration Info** (`Registration Info`, `text`, required/optional): This field collects information about the participant's tournament registration details.
* **Participant Agreement** (`Participant Agreement`, `text`, required/optional): Read and sign the participant agreement form to confirm understanding of the tournament rules and regulations.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`Emergency Contact Info`, `text`, required/optional): Provide contact information for the emergency contact in the event of an urgent situation.
* **Medical Authorization** (`Medical Authorization`, `text`, required/optional): If the participant has any medical concerns or allergies, please provide relevant information.
* **Emergency Medical Info** (`Emergency Medical Info`, `text`, required/optional): If the participant has any medical conditions that may affect their participation, please provide further details.
* **Participant Details** (`Participant Details`, `text`, required/optional): This field collects participant details such as height, weight, and other relevant information.
* **Payment Info** (`Payment Info`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide payment information for registration and other associated costs.
* **Confirmation** (`Confirmation`, `text`, required/optional): Confirm that the form is completed correctly and accurately reflects participant information.
