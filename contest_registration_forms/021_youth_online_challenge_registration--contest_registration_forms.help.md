# Youth Online Challenge Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Online Challenge Registration form is used to collect information from parents/guardians and their child(ren) participating in the event. The form is divided into several sections, each covering different aspects of participant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and other participant details in the **Participant Details** section (Field 1).
2. Provide information about the parent/guardian in the **Parent / Guardian Information** section (Field 2).
3. Sign the **Parent / Guardian Consent** section (Field 3) to give consent for your child's participation.
4. Review the **Event Details** section (Field 4) for any additional information about the event.
5. Read and agree to the **Participant Agreement** (Field 5).
6. If you have any additional comments or questions, enter them in the **Additional Comments** section (Field 6).
7. Click on the **Submit** button (Field 7) to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Details (Field 1)
* **Participant Details**: Enter your child's name, age, and any other relevant participant details.

### Parent / Guardian Information (Field 2)
* **Parent / Guardian Information**: Enter your name and contact information.

### Parent / Guardian Consent (Field 3)
* **Parent / Guardian Consent**: Sign this section to give consent for your child's participation in the event.

### Event Details (Field 4)
* **Event Details**: Enter any additional information about the event, such as dates and times.

### Participant Agreement (Field 5)
* **Participant Agreement**: Read and agree to the terms of the participant agreement.

### Additional Comments (Field 6)
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or questions you may have.

### Save and Print (Field 8)
* **Save and Print**: This button allows you to save a copy of the form and print it for your records.

### Back (Field 9)
* **Back**: This button takes you back to the previous page if you need to make any changes to your answers.

Note: The **Submit** button is not necessary to be clicked, as it is only a button to submit the form after filling in all the required fields.
