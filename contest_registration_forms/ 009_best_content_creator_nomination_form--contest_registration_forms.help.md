# Best Content Creator Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Best Content Creator Nomination Form is used to nominate an individual or team for recognition as the best content creator. This form gathers information about the nominee, including personal details, contact information, and relevant details about their content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your full name and email address to ensure that we can contact you if needed.
2.  Enter your phone number (including country code) if you wish to be contacted about this nomination.
3.  Choose the relevant date for your nomination.
4.  Select the type of category that best applies to the content creator (Attendee, Exhibitor, or Speaker).
5.  Enter a whole number or rating for the content creator's work, if applicable.
6.  Provide any additional relevant details about the content creator.
7.  Confirm your agreement with the terms of the nomination.
8.  Enter the estimated value of the content creator's work, if applicable.
9.  Enter a preferred time for scheduling, if applicable.
10.  Add any final comments or notes about the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal full name.
*   **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email for contact purposes.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Include country code if outside local region.
*   **Date** (`reference_date`, `date`, required): Select the relevant date for your nomination.
*   **Primary Selection** (`primary_selection`, `select_one`, required): Choose the option that best applies to the content creator (Attendee, Exhibitor, or Speaker).
*   **Quantity or Rating** (`quantity_or_rating`, `number`, optional): Provide a whole number or rating for the content creator's work, if applicable.
*   **Additional Details** (`additional_details`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional relevant information about the content creator.
*   **Please Confirm Agreement** (`agreement`, `select_multiple`, required): Select any that apply.
*   **Estimated Value** (`estimated_value`, `number`, optional): Use decimal format when applicable.
*   **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, `time`, optional): Enter a time if scheduling is required.
*   **Final Comments or Notes** (`final_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any final comments or notes about the nomination.
