# Art Contest Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for artists to register their artwork for a contest. It collects personal and artwork information, including contact details, art experience, and submission specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your artist information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select your experience level and indicate if you have prior exhibition experience.
3. Describe your artwork, including its title, medium, dimensions, and year created.
4. Specify the price or value of your artwork.
5. Choose your contest category and indicate if you grant permission to display and photograph your artwork.
6. Provide the submission date and additional information about your artwork, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Full Name** (`artist_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your complete name as it is to be displayed on the certificate and other promotional materials.
* **Email Address** (`artist_email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your email address so that we can contact you about your submission.
* **Phone Number** (`artist_phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you about your submission.
* **Artist Website or Portfolio** (`artist_website`, `text`, **optional**): If you have a website or online portfolio, enter the URL so we can view your work.
* **Years of Art Experience** (`experience_level`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your experience level as:
	+ Beginner - less than 1 year
	+ Student - 1-3 years
	+ Amateur - 3-5 years
	+ Semi-professional
	+ Professional
* **Prior Exhibition Experience** (`prior_exhibitions`, `select_one`, **required**): Indicate if you have prior exhibition experience:
	+ False
	+ Yes - local
	+ Yes - regional
	+ Yes - national
	+ Yes - international
* **Artwork Title** (`artwork_title`, `text`, **required**): Enter the title of your artwork.
* **Medium or Technique** (`artwork_medium`, `text`, **required**): Enter the medium or technique used to create your artwork (e.g., oil, acrylic, digital, etc.).
* **Artwork Dimensions** (`artwork_dimensions`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the height, width, and depth of your artwork (if applicable).
* **Artwork Weight** (`artwork_weight`, `number`, **optional**): Enter the weight of your artwork (if applicable).
* **Year Created** (`artwork_year`, `number`, **required**): Enter the year you created your artwork.
* **Artwork Description** (`artwork_description`, `text`, **required**): Describe your artwork, including its inspiration, technique, and meaning.
* **Artwork Price or Value** (`artwork_price`, `number`, **optional**): Enter the price or value of your artwork.
* **Is the artwork for sale** (`artwork_for_sale`, `select_one`, **optional**): Select if your artwork is for sale:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Open to offers
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date you are submitting your artwork.
* **Contest Category** (`contest_category`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the category your artwork belongs to:
	+ Painting
	+ Sculpture
	+ Digital art
	+ Photography
	+ Mixed media
	+ Other
* **Permission to Display Artwork** (`permission_display`, `select_one`, **required**): Indicate if you grant permission to display your artwork:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Permission to Photograph for Promotion** (`permission_photograph`, `select_one`, **required**): Indicate if you grant permission to photograph your artwork for promotion:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Artwork Insurance Value** (`artwork_insurance`, `number`, **optional**): Enter the insurance value of your artwork (if applicable).
* **Special Handling or Display Requirements** (`special_handling`, `text`, **optional**): Enter any special handling or display requirements for your artwork.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, `text`, **optional**): Enter any additional information about your artwork or submission.
