# latte_art_competition_signup_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "latte_art_competition_signup_form" is a form designed to collect information from participants for a competition related to latte art, a type of latte drink design that requires artistic skill and creativity. The form is divided into several sections, each with a specific purpose to gather relevant details about the participant.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the entry_form field with your entry name.
2. Provide your participant details in the participant_details field.
3. Indicate whether or not you will be submitting an entry for the competition in the entry_submission field.
4. Upload your latte art design image in the latte_art_image field.
5. Enter your contact details in the contact_details field.
6. Enter the name of the event for which you are registering in the event_name field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **entry_form** (entry_form, text, optional): Enter your entry name for the competition. This will be used to identify your entry.
* **participant_details** (participant_details, text, optional): Enter your name and contact information so we can contact you with more information about the competition and its outcome.
* **entry_submission** (entry_submission, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether or not you will be submitting an entry for the competition. Options are "Yes" and "No".
* **latte_art_image** (latte_art_image, text, optional): Upload your latte art design image file. This should be a photo or digital image of your latte art design.
* **contact_details** (contact_details, text, optional): Enter your contact details, including your name, email, and phone number. This is used for communication purposes.
* **event_name** (event_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the event for which you are registering, such as "Latte Art Competition 2023". 

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your entry is complete and eligible for the competition.
* If you are submitting an entry, ensure that your image is of good quality and meets the competition's requirements.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
