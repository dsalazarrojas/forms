> # Water Conservation Challenge Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students and schools participating in the Water Conservation Challenge. The information collected will be used to facilitate the challenge and track participant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your first name in the text box labeled "First name".
2. Enter your last name in the text box labeled "Last name".
3. Enter your email address in the text box labeled "Email".
4. Enter your school's name in the text box labeled "School name".
5. Enter your school's address in the text box labeled "School address".
6. Enter your school's phone number in the text box labeled "Phone".
7. Enter your school's email address in the text box labeled "School email".
8. Enter the number of students participating from your school in the numerical field labeled "Number of students".
9. Enter the name of the participant (e.g., student or teacher) in the text box labeled "Participant name".
10. Enter the age of the participant in the numerical field labeled "Age".
11. Select the gender of the participant from the dropdown menu labeled "Gender".
12. Select whether the participant is a student or not from the dropdown menu labeled "Type of student".
13. Select whether the participant is active or inactive from the dropdown menu labeled "Participant status".
14. Enter the type of school (e.g., public, private) in the text box labeled "Type of school".
15. Enter the location of the school in the text box labeled "School location".
16. Enter the name of the teacher in charge of the participant in the text box labeled "Teacher name".
17. Select the type of participant (e.g., student, teacher) from the dropdown menu labeled "Participant type".
18. Enter the teacher's email address in the text box labeled "Teacher email".
19. Enter the name of the school in the text box labeled "Participant school".
20. Enter the year of the school in the text box labeled "School year".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address that you use for communication.
* **School name**: Enter the name of your school or institution.
* **School address**: Enter your school's address.
* **Phone**: Enter your school's phone number.
* **School email**: Enter your school's email address.
* **Number of students**: Enter the number of students participating from your school.
* **Participant name**: Enter the name of the participant (e.g., student or teacher).
* **Age**: Enter the age of the participant.
* **Gender**: Select the gender of the participant.
* **Type of student**: Select whether the participant is a student or not.
* **Gender**: Select whether the participant is male or female.
* **Type of school**: Select the type of school (e.g., public, private).
* **School location**: Enter the location of the school.
* **Teacher name**: Enter the name of the teacher in charge of the participant.
* **Participant type**: Select the type of participant (e.g., student, teacher).
* **Participant status**: Select whether the participant is active or inactive.
* **School year**: Enter the year of the school.
* **Teacher email**: Enter the teacher's email address.
* **Participant school**: Enter the name of the school.
* **School year**: Enter the year of the school.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have permission from your school or institution to participate in the Water Conservation Challenge.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information you provide to avoid errors.
* If you have any issues or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the organizers of the challenge for assistance.
