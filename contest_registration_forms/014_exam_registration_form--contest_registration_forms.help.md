# Exam Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students to register for an exam or test. It gathers details such as name, contact information, examination dates, times, locations, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter your valid email address in the "Email" field.
3. Select a date for the exam from the calendar in the "Exam Date" field.
4. Choose a time for the exam from the time picker in the "Exam Time" field.
5. Enter the location where the exam will take place in the "Exam Location" field.
6. Select the type of exam you're registering for from the "Exam Type" dropdown menu.
7. Choose the language in which the exam will be administered in the "Exam Language" field.
8. Select the duration of the exam from the "Exam Duration" dropdown menu.
9. Optionally, provide a brief description of the exam in the "Exam Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): This is the field where you enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address associated with your account.
* **Exam Date** (`exam_date`, date, required/optional): Select a date for the exam from the calendar.
* **Exam Time** (`exam_time`, time, required/optional): Choose a time for the exam using the time picker.
* **Exam Location** (`exam_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location where the exam will take place.
* **Exam Type** (`exam_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of exam you're registering for (Exam, Test, Both).
* **Exam Language** (`exam_language`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose one or more languages in which the exam will be administered (English, Spanish, French).
* **Exam Duration** (`exam_duration`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the duration of the exam (1 hour, 2 hours, 3 hours, 4 hours).
* **Exam Description** (`exam_description`, text, required/optional): Optionally, provide a brief description of the exam.
