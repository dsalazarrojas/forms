# Gaming Tournament Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gaming Tournament Registration Form is a tool used to collect information from participants who want to join a gaming tournament. This form is designed to gather necessary details such as team information, team members, and game preferences to create a smooth and organized experience for both the participants and tournament organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the **User Input** page with your personal details.
2. Provide information about your team on the **Team Information** page, including the team name and any other relevant details.
3. Select your team members from the available options on the **Team Members** page.
4. Choose your Discord settings on the **Discord** page, selecting your username, tag, channel, or server.
5. Enter your team name on the **Team Name** page.
6. Select the game you want to participate in on the **Game** page.
7. Choose the type of tournament you want to participate in on the **Tournament Type** page.
8. Select your division on the **Division** page.
9. Fill out the start and end dates of the tournament on the **Start Date** and **End Date** pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input**: This page is the first step in the registration process. Enter your personal details here, such as your name and any other relevant information that you want to share with the tournament organizers.
* **Team Information**: This page asks for information about your team, including your team name and any additional details you want to provide.
* **Team Members**: Select your team members from the available options, making sure to include all team members participating in the tournament.
* **Discord**: Choose your Discord settings, including your username, tag, channel, or server. This will allow us to contact you and share important updates.
* **Team Name**: Enter your team name here, this will be your identity in the tournament.
* **Game**: Select the game you want to participate in. Make sure to choose the correct game from the available options.
* **Tournament Type**: Choose the type of tournament you want to participate in.
* **Division**: Select your division for the tournament. This will determine your ranking and standing in the competition.
* **Start Date** and **End Date**: Fill out the start and end dates of the tournament. This will help us schedule the tournament and ensure everyone is aware of the timeline.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to avoid any delays or issues with your registration.
- Double-check your selections and entries to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
