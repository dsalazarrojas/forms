# National Online Forensics Competition Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help users understand the purpose and functionality of the National Online Forensics Competition Registration form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the participant's information, including name, email, phone number, address, and additional info.
2.  Fill in the coach's information, including name, email, phone number, address, and additional info.
3.  Enter the event details, including name, date, time, location, and status.
4.  Indicate the participant's registration status and coach's approval status.
5.  Collect any additional information about the participant and coach.
6.  Upload images of the participant and event.
7.  Enter the submission date and time and status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Form Information**: This section serves as a title or introduction for the form.
*   **Participant Name**: Enter the participant's name.
*   **Participant Email**: Enter the participant's email address.
*   **Participant Phone**: Enter the participant's phone number.
*   **Participant Address**: Enter the participant's mailing address.
*   **Coach Name**: Enter the coach's name.
*   **Coach Email**: Enter the coach's email address.
*   **Coach Phone**: Enter the coach's phone number.
*   **Coach Address**: Enter the coach's mailing address.
*   **Event Name**: Enter the event name.
*   **Event Date**: Enter the event date.
*   **Event Time**: Enter the event time.
*   **Event Location**: Enter the event location.
*   **Participant Registration**: Indicate whether the participant is registered or not.
*   **Coach Approval**: Indicate whether the coach has approved the participant's registration.
*   **Event Status**: Indicate whether the event is active or inactive.
*   **Participant Additional Info**: Provide any additional information about the participant.
*   **Coach Additional Info**: Provide any additional information about the coach.
*   **Participant Image**: Upload an image of the participant.
*   **Event Image**: Upload an image of the event.
*   **Submission Date**: Enter the submission date.
*   **Submission Time**: Enter the submission time.
*   **Submission Status**: Indicate the status of submission.
*   **Participant Comments**: Enter any comments from the participant.
*   **Coach Comments**: Enter any comments from the coach.
