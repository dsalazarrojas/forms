# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from teams for a dance competition or event. It will help the organizers to verify and communicate with teams, and also to evaluate their performance or other judging criteria.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: Team Leader Email, Team Leader Phone, Team Leader Name, and School Name.
2. Provide details about your team with the Team Description field.
3. Choose the categories that apply to your team using the Team Category field.
4. (Optional) Upload a team image or video to provide visual reference for the event organizers or judges.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Leader Email** (`team_leader_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of your team leader. This is the primary contact method for the team.
* **Team Leader Phone** (`team_leader_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your team leader. This is the secondary contact method for the team.
* **Team Leader Name** (`team_leader_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your team leader.
* **Team Details** (`team_details`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your team, including any notable achievements or qualifications.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required): Enter a unique name for your team that will be used for identification and communication.
* **Team Name Alias** (`team_name_alias`, text, optional): Provide an alias or variation of your team name if you have one. This can be used as an alternative name for your team.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school or institution associated with your team.
* **Team Address** (`team_address`, text, optional): Enter the address where your team can be contacted or found.
* **Team Description** (`team_description`, text, optional): Provide a more detailed description of your team, including any relevant history or experience.
* **Team Category** (`team_category`, select_multiple, required): Select all categories that apply to your team from the provided options.
* **Team Image** (`team_image`, text, optional): Upload an image of your team that will be used for reference or promotion.
* **Team Video** (`team_video`, text, optional): Upload a video that represents your team, such as a promotional video or performance reel.
* **Team Leader School Dance Background** (`team_leader_school_dance_background`, note, optional): Provide any relevant information about your team leader's dance background or experience.
* **Tips**:
	+ Make sure to double-check your contact information and team details for accuracy.
	+ Use clear and concise language in your team description and image/video uploads.
	+ Choose the correct categories for your team to ensure proper evaluation and communication.
