# Spin To Win Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users who want to participate in a contest, including their contact details, contest type, and terms and conditions acceptance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Name
2. Enter your email address
3. Provide your phone number
4. Enter your zip code
5. Provide your address
6. Select the type of contest you are participating in (Spin to Win or Instant Win)
7. If this contest is open to you, you will be allowed to enter, if not, choose "No"
8. Provide details about the prize you are interested in
9. Accept or decline the terms and conditions of the contest
10. Select your current participant status (Active or Inactive)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address
* **User Phone** (`user_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number
* **User Zip** (`user_zip`, number, required/optional): Enter your zip code
* **User Address** (`user_address`, note, required/optional): Enter your address
* **Contest Type** (`contest_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the type of contest you are participating in (Spin to Win or Instant Win)
* **Eligibility Start Date** (`eligibility_start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of the contest eligibility period
* **Entry Method** (`entry_method`, select_one, required/required): Check if you are allowed to enter (Yes or No)
* **Prize Details** (`prize_details`, text, required/optional): Provide details about the prize you are interested in
* **Terms & Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, select_multiple, required/required): Accept or decline the terms and conditions of the contest
* **Participant Status** (`participant_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select your current participant status (Active or Inactive)
