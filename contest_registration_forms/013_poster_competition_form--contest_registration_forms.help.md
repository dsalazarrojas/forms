# Poster Competition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Poster Competition Form is designed to collect essential details about your poster submission, ensuring you provide accurate and complete information to increase your chances of being selected as a finalist.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the required information ready.
2. Fill in each field accurately and completely, paying attention to the field labels and hints.
3. If you have any questions or doubts, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/required): Enter a valid email address associated with your poster submission.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required/required): Provide your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Poster Title** (`poster_title`, `text`, required/required): Enter a title for your poster that captures its essence and significance.
* **Poster File** (`poster_file`, `text`, required/required): Upload your poster file, ensuring it is in the correct format (e.g., PDF).
* **Submission Type** (`submission_type`, `select_one`, required/required): Choose the type of submission (e.g., Research, Art, or Design).
* **Additional Files** (`additional_files`, `text`, optional/optional): If applicable, provide additional files related to your poster.
* **Institution** (`institution`, `text`, required/required): Enter the name of the institution where you are affiliated with.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, `text`, required/required): Enter the name of your supervisor or mentor.
* **Supervisor Institution** (`supervisor_institution`, `text`, required/required): Enter the name of your supervisor's institution.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required/required): Provide the date of submission in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Poster Type** (`poster_type`, `select_multiple`, optional/optional): Select the type(s) of poster (e.g., Research, Art, Design).
* **Poster Format** (`poster_format`, `select_one`, required/required): Choose the format of your poster (e.g., PDF, Image).
* **Poster Size** (`poster_size`, `select_one`, required/required): Select the size of your poster (e.g., A0, A3).
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, optional/optional): Select the category of your poster (e.g., Science, Art).
* **Poster File Description** (`poster_file_description`, `text`, required/required): Describe your poster file in a few sentences.
* **Poster Image** (`poster_image`, `text`, required/required): Upload an image of your poster.
* **Poster Video** (`poster_video`, `text`, optional/optional): If applicable, provide a link to a video related to your poster.
* **Other Files** (`other_files`, `text`, optional/optional): If applicable, provide other files related to your poster.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required/required): Provide any relevant comments about your submission.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional/optional): If you have any additional comments, enter them here.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, `select_one`, required/required): Indicate the status of your submission (e.g., Completed, In Progress).
* **Submission Comments** (`submission_comments`, `text`, required/required): Enter any comments about your submission.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required/required): Sign your submission with your full name and institution.
