# Judging Application For Competition - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Judging Application For Competition" form is designed for judges to apply for a competition judging role. This form helps us to collect essential information about the judge's personal details, availability, and preferences for judging.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the "Competition Details" field.
2. Provide your judge details in the "Judge Details" field.
3. Share your experience in the "Experience" field.
4. Specify your availability in the number format in the "Availability" field.
5. Check the "Conflicts of Interest" field and answer "Yes" or "No" accordingly.
6. Select your preferences for conflicts in the "Conflict Preferences" field.
7. Add any additional details about your experience in the "Experience Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Competition Details** (`competition_details`, text, optional)
* This field is a text field where you can provide a brief description of the competition you are applying for.

### **Judge Details** (`judge_details`, text, optional)
* This field is a text field where you can provide your judge's name and contact information.

### **Experience** (`experience`, text, optional)
* This field is a text field where you can describe your past experience in judging similar competitions.

### **Availability** (`availability`, number, optional)
* This field is a number field where you can specify your availability for the competition, e.g., "Yes" for available, "No" for unavailable.

### **Conflicts of Interest** (`conflicts_of_interest`, text, optional)
* This field is a text field where you can declare any conflicts of interest you may have with the competition.

### **Judge Preferences** (`judge_preferences`, select_multiple, optional)
* This field is a multiple select field where you can choose whether you have any preferences for the competition, e.g., "Yes" for interested, "No" for not interested.

### **Conflict Preferences** (`conflict_preferences`, select_multiple, optional)
* This field is a multiple select field where you can choose how you would handle any potential conflicts of interest that may arise during the competition.

### **Experience Details** (`experience_details`, text, optional)
* This field is a text field where you can provide any additional details about your experience.
