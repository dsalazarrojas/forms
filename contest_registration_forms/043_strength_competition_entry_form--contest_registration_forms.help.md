# Strength Competition Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a strength competition. It is used to register participants in a strength competition, gathering personal details, competition preferences, and submission information. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email Address and Phone Number.
3. Select your Weight Class and Division from the given options.
4. If applicable, select the types of competitions you would like to participate in (Squat, Deadlift, Bench Press, or Total).
5. Enter any additional Notes if necessary.
6. Select who you are submitting this entry by and who you are submitting it to.
7. Optionally, select the Assigned User and Assigned To if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is spelled.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it is spelled.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Weight Class** (`weight_class`, select_one, required): Select your weight class from the given options (Heavyweight, Lightweight, Middleweight).
* **Division** (`division`, select_one, required): Select your division from the given options (Male, Female, Open).
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, required): Enter your birth date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required): Enter your age in years.
* **Competition Type** (`competition_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the types of competitions you would like to participate in (Squat, Deadlift, Bench Press, Total).
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or remarks if necessary.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required): Select who you are submitting this entry by (Self, Co-worker, Manager).
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, select_one, required): Select who you are submitting this entry to (Manager, HR).
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, required): Enter the date of submission in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Team Member** (`team_member`, select_one, required): Select whether you are a team member ('Yes', 'No').
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, time, required): Enter the time of submission in the format HH:MM:SS.
* **Submitted By Title** (`submitted_by_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the person submitting this entry.
* **Submitted By Name** (`submitted_by_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person submitting this entry.
* **Submitted By Department** (`submitted_by_department`, text, required): Enter the department of the person submitting this entry.
* **Submitted By Position** (`submitted_by_position`, text, required): Enter the position of the person submitting this entry.
* **Submitted To Title** (`submitted_to_title`, text, required): Enter the title of who this entry is being submitted to.
* **Submitted To Name** (`submitted_to_name`, text, required): Enter the name of who this entry is being submitted to.
* **Submitted To Department** (`submitted_to_department`, text, required): Enter the department of who this entry is being submitted to.
* **Submitted To Position** (`submitted_to_position`, text, required): Enter the position of who this entry is being submitted to.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, select_one, required): Select who this entry is assigned to from the given options (chatjimmy, Other).
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required): Select who this entry is assigned to from the given options (chatjimmy, Other).
