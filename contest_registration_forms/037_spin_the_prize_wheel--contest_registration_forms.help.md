# Spin The Prize Wheel - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Spin The Prize Wheel" form is used to register a customer's entry into the Spin The Prize Wheel contest. It is used to collect demographic information and entry details from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the **Event ID** of the contest you are registering for.
2. Enter your **Customer Name**.
3. Enter your **Email** address.
4. Select the **Prize ID** of the prize you would like to win.
5. Select the **Customer Prize Choice**.
6. Enter the date you would like to register for the contest.
7. Select the time you would like to enter the contest.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event ID** (`1`, `number`, required: false): Enter the ID of the contest you are registering for.
* **Customer Name** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Prize ID** (`4`, `number`, required: false): Select the ID of the prize you would like to win.
* **Customer Prize Choice** (`5`, `number`, required: false): Select the prize you would like to choose from the list of prizes.
* **Entry Date** (`6`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you would like to register for the contest.
* **Entry Time** (`7`, `time`, required: false): Select the time you would like to enter the contest.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct Event ID for the contest you are registering for.
* Ensure your email address is valid and up-to-date.
* Double-check your prize choice before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact our support team.
* This form should only be filled out once per customer per contest.
* The form is meant to be filled out for analysis and reward purposes only.
