# Camper Contest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants for a camping contest. The collected information includes personal details, address, parent or guardian information, contest details, skills and experience, and acceptance of contest rules and waivers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **First Section: Participant Information**
   - Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email Address**, **Phone Number**, and **Age**.
2. **Second Section: Address Information**
   - Enter your **Street Address**, **City**, **State or Province**, **Zip or Postal Code**, and **Country**.
3. **Third Section: Parent or Guardian Information**
   - If applicable, enter your **Parent or Guardian Name**, **Guardian Contact Number**, and **Guardian Email Address**.
4. **Fourth Section: Contest Details**
   - Select the **Contest Name** and choose the appropriate **Contest Category**.
   - Choose a preferred **Competition Date**.
5. **Fifth Section: Skills and Experience**
   - Select your **Experience Level with Camping** and specify any **Specific Skills to Demonstrate**.
6. **Final Section: Acceptance**
   - Select **Yes, I accept** to confirm that you have read and agreed to all contest rules and waivers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter a working phone number where you can be reached.
* **Age**: Enter your age as of the current date.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Street Address**: Enter your street address.
* **City**: Enter the name of your city.
* **State or Province**: Enter the name of your state or province.
* **Zip or Postal Code**: Enter your zip or postal code.
* **Country**: Enter the name of your country.
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: If applicable, enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Guardian Contact Number**: If applicable, enter a contact number for your guardian.
* **Guardian Email Address**: If applicable, enter the email address of your guardian.
* **Contest Name**: Select the name of the contest you are registering for.
* **Contest Category**: Choose the category for the contest.
* **Competition Date**: Choose a date when you are available to participate in the competition.
* **Experience Level with Camping**: Select your experience level with camping.
* **Specific Skills to Demonstrate**: If applicable, specify the specific skills you will demonstrate.
* **I accept all contest rules and waivers**: Select this option to confirm that you have read and agreed to all contest rules and waivers.
