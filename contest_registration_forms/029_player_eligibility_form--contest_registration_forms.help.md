# player_eligibility_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The player_eligibility_form is used to collect information about a player's personal details and medical history for potential participation in a contest or competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure that you have the consent of the player before filling in the form.
3. Review the terms and conditions carefully before selecting the relevant option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the player's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the player's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the player's email address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the player's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the player's current address (not required, but recommended for emergency contact purposes).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the player's phone number.
* **Sport History** (`sport_history`, text, optional): Enter any previous sports history or experiences.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): If the player has any medical conditions, please list them.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): If the player has an emergency contact, enter their name.
* **Emergency Contact Relation** (`emergency_contact_relation`, text, optional): If the player has an emergency contact, enter their relation to the player.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): If the player has an emergency contact, enter their phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Phone 2** (`emergency_contact_phone2`, text, optional): If the player has another emergency contact, enter their phone number.
* **Consent Terms** (`consent_terms`, select one, required): Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Sign with your name to confirm your participation.
