# Contest Winner Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contest Winner Update Form is designed to collect accurate and up-to-date information from contest winners to facilitate prize processing and public announcement. This form is only accessible to winners after the contest has closed to prevent duplicate entries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all required fields filled out accurately.
2. Enter your full name as it appears on your identification document.
3. Enter the name of the contest you won.
4. Select the correct date of the prize announcement.
5. Enter your current email address.
6. Enter your mobile phone number.
7. Enter your full shipping address, including city, state, and zip code.
8. Select your preferred prize delivery method from the options provided.
9. If applicable, enter your government ID type and reference number.
10. Select your consent for social media announcement.
11. Enter a link to a professional photo of yourself (optional).
12. Check the box to confirm the accuracy of the provided information.
13. Select the current date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (Legal)** (`winner_full_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification document. This is a required field.
* **Name of Contest Won** (`contest_won_name`): Enter the name of the contest you won. This is a required field.
* **Date of Prize Announcement** (`winning_date`): Select the date of the prize announcement. This is a required field.
* **Current Email Address** (`current_email`): Enter your current email address. This is a required field.
* **Mobile Phone Number** (`phone_number_mobile`): Enter your mobile phone number. This is a required field.
* **Full Shipping/Mailing Address** (`shipping_address_full`): Enter your full shipping address, including city, state, and zip code. This is a required field.
* **Preferred Prize Delivery Method** (`prize_delivery_preference`): Select your preferred prize delivery method from the options provided. This is a required field.
* **Government ID Type** (`government_id_reference`): If your prize is over $600, enter your government ID type and reference number. This is not a required field.
* **Consent to announce on Social Media** (`social_media_consent`): Select your consent for social media announcement. This is a required field.
* **Link to Winner Photo (Optional)** (`photo_for_announcement_link`): Enter a link to a professional photo of yourself (optional). This is not a required field.
* **I confirm the accuracy of this update** (`signature_acknowledgement`): Check the box to confirm the accuracy of the provided information. This is a required field.
* **Today's Date** (`submission_timestamp`): Select the current date. This is a required field.

## Tips
* Ensure accurate spelling and formatting of your name, email address, phone number, and shipping address.
* If applicable, ensure you provide your government ID type and reference number.
* If you select "Yes - Full Name and Photo" for social media announcement, ensure the provided photo meets the required standards.
* Review the form before submitting to ensure all required fields are accurate and complete.
