# statewide_singing_competition_entry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The state-wide singing competition entry form is designed to gather information from users who wish to participate in the competition. This form captures essential details such as name, contact information, performance experience, and submission data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely, paying attention to the required fields (marked as "required" in this guide).
2. Enter your name in the **Name** field, which is your full legal name.
3. Provide your address in the **Address** field.
4. Enter your email address in the **Email** field, ensuring it matches the format of `username@domain.com`.
5. If you have a phone number, enter it in the **Phone** field (optional).
6. Describe your performance experience in the **Performance Experience** field.
7. Select the category that best fits your singing style from the **Singing Category** dropdown menu.
8. If you have an audition date, you can enter it in the **Audition Date** field (optional).
9. Enter the time of your audition in the **Audition Time** field (optional).
10. Set the date for the submission of your performance (if you are selected to audition).
11. Enter a brief message in the **Form Submission** field, including any relevant details about your submission.
12. (Optional) Add any additional comments or notes in the **User Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (id: 2, required): Enter your full legal name.
* **Address** (id: 3, required): Enter your physical address.
* **Email** (id: 4, required): Enter a valid email address in the format of `username@domain.com`.
* **Phone** (id: 5, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Performance Experience** (id: 6, required): Describe your singing experience, including any training or performances you have done.
* **Singing Category** (id: 7, required): Select the category that best fits your singing style from the options provided.
* **Audition Date** (id: 8, optional): Enter the date of your scheduled audition (if applicable).
* **Audition Time** (id: 9, optional): Enter the time of your scheduled audition (if applicable).
* **Submission Date** (id: 10, required): Set the date you are submitting your performance (if you are selected to audition).
* **Form Submission** (id: 11, required): Enter a brief message describing your submission.
* **User Notes** (id: 12, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your submission.

Note: The form will be reviewed and processed upon completion and submission.
