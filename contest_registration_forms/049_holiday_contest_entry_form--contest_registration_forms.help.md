# Holiday Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit entries for a holiday contest. The form allows you to upload various types of media, including documents, photos, videos, audio files, and notes, to participate in the contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form completely, including all required and optional fields.
* Upload the requested media types using the corresponding fields.
* Review your submission carefully before clicking the "Submit" button.
* Click the "Submit" button to confirm your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Holiday Contest Entry Form (1)**: Enter your holiday contest entry in this field. This is a text field where you can type your entry.
* **Upload Documents (2)**: If you have any supporting documents for your entry, upload them here. This field is optional.
* **Upload Photos (3)**: If you have any photos relevant to your entry, upload them here. This field is optional.
* **Upload Videos (4)**: If you have any videos relevant to your entry, upload them here. This field is optional.
* **Upload Audio (5)**: If you have any audio files relevant to your entry, upload them here. This field is optional.
* **Upload Notes (6)**: If you have any notes or comments about your entry, upload them here. This field is optional.
* **Upload Files (7)**: If you have any additional files relevant to your entry, upload them here. This field is optional.
* **Submit (8)**: Click this button to review and confirm your entry. This field is required, but it's a button, not a field that needs to be filled out.

## Tips
* Make sure to review your submission carefully before clicking the "Submit" button to ensure everything is complete and accurate.
* Use the correct media type for each field, such as uploading a document for a written entry and a photo for a visual entry.
* If you have any issues with the form, contact us at form@gic.mx for assistance.
