# Short Movie Contest Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Short Movie Contest Application Form is a collection of fields designed to gather information from contestants interested in participating in a short movie contest. This form aims to capture relevant details about the contestant, their short movie, and other essential information required for the contest. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Contestant Information**: Enter your details in the first field, including your name and any other relevant information. This field is not required.
2.  **Short Movie Info**: Provide a brief description of your short movie, including its title and a short summary.
3.  **Contact Information**: Enter your contact details in this field, including your email address and any other contact information you wish to share.
4.  **Upload File**: Upload any supporting file(s) related to your short movie, such as posters or screenshots.
5.  **Movie Link**: Enter the link to your short movie on a relevant platform (e.g., YouTube, Vimeo).
6.  **Category**: Select the category your movie best fits into (Film, Short Film, Documentary, or Other).
7.  **Genre**: Select the genre of your movie from the available options (Action, Comedy, Drama, Horror, or Other).
8.  **Runtime**: Select the length of your movie in minutes (1, 5, 10, 15, 30, or 60).
9.  **Synopsis**: Provide a brief summary of your movie, including the plot and main characters.
10. **Director**: Enter the name of the director of your movie.
11. **Cinematographer**: Enter the name of the cinematographer of your movie.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contestant Information** (`Contestant Information`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant information about yourself as a contestant.
  * This field is not required, but it helps us understand who you are.
- **Short Movie Info** (`Short Movie Info`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of your short movie.
  * Keep it short and concise, around 100-150 characters.
- **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required: false): Enter your email address and any other contact information you wish to share.
  * We will use this to contact you if needed.
- **Upload File** (`Upload File`, `text`, required: false): Upload any supporting file(s) related to your short movie.
  * Keep it relevant and not too big.
- **Movie Link** (`Movie Link`, `text`, required: false): Enter the link to your movie on a relevant platform.
  * We will watch it to evaluate your entry.
- **Category** (`Category`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the category your movie best fits into (Film, Short Film, Documentary, or Other).
  * Choose the one that best describes your movie.
- **Genre** (`Genre`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the genre of your movie from the available options (Action, Comedy, Drama, Horror, or Other).
  * Choose all that apply.
- **Runtime** (`Runtime`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the length of your movie in minutes (1, 5, 10, 15, 30, or 60).
  * Choose the one that best fits your movie.
- **Synopsis** (`Synopsis`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief summary of your movie, including the plot and main characters.
  * Keep it concise and around 150-200 words.
- **Director** (`Director`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the director of your movie.
  * The person who directed your movie.
- **Cinematographer** (`Cinematographer`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the cinematographer of your movie.
  * The person who did the camera work for your movie.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information.
* Be concise and clear in your descriptions.
* If you need help, contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
