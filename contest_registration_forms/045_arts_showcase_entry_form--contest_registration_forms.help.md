# Arts Showcase Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Arts Showcase Entry Form is used for artists to submit their artwork to a showcase or exhibition. This form helps to gather essential information about the artist and their artwork for the showcase organizers and attendees.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Please enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **Email Address** field. Please ensure this is a valid email address you control.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
5. Enter your website or portfolio URL in the **Website or Portfolio URL** field.
6. Enter your social media handles in the **Social Media Handles** field. (Example: Instagram: @username, Facebook: @username)
7. Enter the name of the showcase event or exhibition in the **Showcase Event or Exhibition Name** field.
8. Select the category of your artwork from the **Entry Category** dropdown list.
9. Enter the title of your artwork in the **Artwork Title** field.
10. Enter a brief description of your artwork in the **Artwork Description** field.
11. Enter the dimensions of your artwork in the **Artwork Dimensions** field (Height x Width x Depth).
12. Enter the estimated value or sale price of your artwork in the **Estimated Artwork Value or Sale Price** field (if applicable).
13. Please upload a high-resolution photograph or digital file of your artwork in the **Artwork Image Upload** field.
14. Enter the year your artwork was created in the **Year Artwork Was Created** field.
15. Indicate if your artwork is available for sale or exhibition in the **Is Artwork Available for Sale or Exhibition** field.
16. Enter your years of professional art experience in the **Years of Professional Art Experience** field (if applicable).
17. Enter the entry fee in the **Entry Fee** field (if applicable).
18. Enter your artist statement in the **Artist Statement** field (100-150 words).
19. If this is your first time submitting to this showcase, select **Yes** in the **First Time Submitting to This Showcase** field.
20. Finally, agree to the showcase terms and rights agreement by selecting **Yes** in the **I Agree to Showcase Terms and Rights Agreement** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`artist_first_name`, text, required): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (`artist_last_name`, text, required): Enter your surname.
* **Email Address** (`artist_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address you control.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Website or Portfolio URL** (`artist_website`, text, optional): Enter your website or portfolio URL.
* **Social Media Handles** (`social_media`, text, optional): Enter your social media handles (e.g., Instagram: @username, Facebook: @username).
* **Showcase Event or Exhibition Name** (`showcase_event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the showcase event or exhibition.
* **Entry Category** (`entry_category`, select_one, required): Select the category of your artwork from the list.
* **Artwork Title** (`artwork_title`, text, required): Enter a title for your artwork.
* **Artwork Description** (`artwork_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your artwork.
* **Artwork Dimensions** (`artwork_dimensions`, text, optional): Enter the dimensions of your artwork (Height x Width x Depth).
* **Estimated Artwork Value or Sale Price** (`artwork_value`, number, optional): Enter the estimated value or sale price of your artwork.
* **Artwork Image Upload** (`artwork_image`, text, required): Upload a high-resolution photograph or digital file of your artwork.
* **Year Artwork Was Created** (`creation_year`, number, optional): Enter the year your artwork was created.
* **Is Artwork Available for Sale or Exhibition** (`artwork_availability`, select_one, optional): Indicate if your artwork is available for sale or exhibition.
* **Years of Professional Art Experience** (`artist_experience`, number, optional): Enter your years of professional art experience.
* **Entry Fee** (`entry_fee`, number, optional): Enter the entry fee for the showcase.
* **Artist Statement** (`artist_statement`, text, required): Enter a 100-150 word artist statement.
* **First Time Submitting to This Showcase** (`first_time_submitting`, select_one, optional): Indicate if this is your first time submitting to this showcase.
* **I Agree to Showcase Terms and Rights Agreement** (`rights_agreement`, select_one, required): Agree to the showcase terms and rights agreement.
