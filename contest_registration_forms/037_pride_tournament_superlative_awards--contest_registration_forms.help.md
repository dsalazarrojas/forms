> # Pride Tournament Superlative Awards - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pride Tournament Superlative Awards form is designed to collect information for nominations and submissions for the awards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **Nomination Story** of the nominee in the text field.
2.  Select the relevant **Nomination Categories** from the provided options.
3.  Enter the **Email** of the person submitting the nomination in the text field.
4.  Select the **Nomination Team** type from the provided options.
5.  Enter the **Nomination Name** of the nominee in the text field.
6.  Enter the **Nomination Category** of the nominee in the text field.
7.  Enter any additional **Nomination Details** about the nominee in the text field.
8.  Enter the **Nominee's Name** of the person being nominated in the text field.
9.  Enter the **Nominee's Email** of the person being nominated in the text field.
10. Enter the **Nominee's Phone** of the person being nominated in the text field.
11. Enter the **Nominee's Address** of the person being nominated in the text field.
12. Enter the **Nominee's City** of the person being nominated in the text field.
13. Enter the **Nominee's Region** of the person being nominated in the text field.
14. Enter the **Nominee's Country** of the person being nominated in the text field.
15. Enter the **Nominee's Zip** of the person being nominated in the text field.
16. Enter the **Nominee's Company** of the person being nominated in the text field.
17. Enter the **Nominee's Title** of the person being nominated in the text field.
18. Enter the **Nominee's Department** of the person being nominated in the text field.
19. Enter the **Nominee's Job Title** of the person being nominated in the text field. (Note: this field is optional if "Job Title" is selected)
20. Enter any other **Nominee's Job Title** if "Job Title" is not selected.
21. Enter any additional **Nominee's Job Title Note**.
22. Enter the **Nominee's Job Function** of the person being nominated in the text field. (Note: this field is optional if "Job Function" is not selected)
23. Enter any other **Nominee's Job Function** if "Job Function" is not selected.
24. Enter any additional **Nominee's Job Function Note**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Story** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the nominee and their achievements.
* **Nomination Categories** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the categories that the nominee belongs to.
* **Nomination Contact** (`3`, `text`, optional): Enter the email of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Email** (`4`, `email`, required): Enter the email of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nomination Team** (`5`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the nominee is part of a team or not.
* **Nomination Name** (`6`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the nominee.
* **Nomination Category** (`7`, `text`, optional): Enter the category that the nominee belongs to.
* **Nomination Details** (`8`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional details about the nominee.
* **Nominee's Name** (`9`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee's Email** (`10`, `email`, optional): Enter the email of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee's Phone** (`11`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee's Address** (`12`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee's City** (`13`, `text`, optional): Enter the city of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee's Region** (`14`, `text`, optional): Enter the region of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee's Country** (`15`, `text`, optional): Enter the country of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee's Zip** (`16`, `text`, optional): Enter the zip of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee's Company** (`17`, `text`, optional): Enter the company of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee's Title** (`18`, `text`, optional): Enter the title of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee's Department** (`19`, `text`, optional): Enter the department of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee's Job Title** (`20`, `text`, optional): Enter the job title of the person being nominated. (Note: this field is optional if "Job Title" is not selected)
* **Nominee's Job Title Other** (`21`, `text`, optional): Enter any other job title if "Job Title" is not selected.
* **Nominee's Job Title Note** (`22`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional job title note.
* **Nominee's Job Function** (`23`, `text`, optional): Enter the job function of the person being nominated. (Note: this field is optional if "Job Function" is not selected)
* **Nominee's Job Function Other** (`24`, `text`, optional): Enter any other job function if "Job Function" is not selected.
* **Nominee's Job Function Note** (`25`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional job function note.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy and completeness.
* Ensure that the nominee's details are up-to-date and correct.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification before submitting the form.
