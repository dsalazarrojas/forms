# Baby Photo Contest Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Baby Photo Contest Registration Form is a form designed to collect essential information from entrants who wish to participate in the contest. The form is used to register a single entry per submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the entrant's name in the "Entrant name" field.
2. Enter the child's name as it appears on their entry.
3. Enter the child's age at the time of submission.
4. Provide a valid email address for contact and notification purposes.
5. Enter your contact phone number, if desired (this is optional).
6. If applicable, add a brief title or caption to your photo.
7. Choose the category for your photo from the provided options.
8. Confirm your consent to use the photo for promotional purposes.
9. Enter any additional details or notes about your entry, if needed.
10. Select a date for submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Entrant name (Entrant name)
* **Entrant name** (text, required): Enter your name as the parent or guardian of the child.

### Child name (Child name)
* **Child name** (text, required): Enter the child's name as it appears on their entry.

### Age of child (Age of child)
* **Age of child** (text, required): Enter the child's age at the time of submission in months or years.

### Contact email (Contact email)
* **Contact email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address for contact and notification purposes.

### Contact phone (Contact phone)
* **Contact phone** (text, optional): If you wish to provide an optional contact phone number for further communication.

### Photo title (Photo title)
* **Photo title** (text, optional): A brief caption or title for your photo, if desired.

### Contest category (Contest category)
* **Contest category** (select_one, required): Choose one category for your photo: Best smile, Best candid, Most creative, or Cutest outfit.

### Consent and release (Consent and release)
* **Consent and release** (select_one, required): Confirm that you grant permission for the photo to be used for promotional purposes.

### Submission notes (Submission notes)
* **Submission notes** (text, optional): Enter any additional details or notes about your entry, if needed.

### Entry date (Entry date)
* **Entry date** (date, required): Select a date for submission of your entry.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely to ensure your entry's eligibility for the contest.
