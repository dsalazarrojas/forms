# Spot The Differences Contest Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spot The Differences Contest Registration form is a simple form designed to collect personal details from users who wish to participate in the "Spot The Differences Contest". This form is meant to be filled out once by each participant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your last name in the field labeled "Last Name".
3. Enter your email address in the field labeled "Email".
4. Enter your phone number in the field labeled "Phone".
5. Enter your date of birth in the format "mm/dd/yyyy" in the field labeled "Date of Birth".
6. Enter your age in the field labeled "Age".
7. Select your gender from the options provided in the field labeled "Gender".
8. Enter your address in the field labeled "Address".
9. Enter your city in the field labeled "City".
10. Enter your state in the field labeled "State".
11. Enter your country in the field labeled "Country".
12. Select "I agree" to agree to the terms of the contest in the field labeled "Agree to terms".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted about the contest.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format "mm/dd/yyyy".
* **Age**: Enter your age as of today.
* **Gender**: Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Address**: Enter your address where you can be delivered mail or other contest information.
* **City**: Enter the city where you live.
* **State**: Enter the state where you live.
* **Country**: Enter the country where you live.
* **Agree to terms**: Select "I agree" to indicate that you understand and accept the terms of the contest.
