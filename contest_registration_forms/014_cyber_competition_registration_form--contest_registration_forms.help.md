# Cyber Competition Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cyber Competition Registration Form is designed to collect necessary information from participants to register for a cyber competition. This form gathers details such as name, contact, demographic, and category selection to ensure accurate and efficient registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Participant Name** in the text field provided. This is a required field.
2. Enter your **Email** address. This is a required field.
3. Enter your **Phone Number** (optional). This field is not required.
4. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the format of MM/DD/YYYY. This is a required field.
5. Enter your **Address** (optional). This field is not required.
6. Enter your **City** (optional). This field is not required.
7. Enter your **Country** (optional). This field is not required.
8. Enter your **Zip Code**. This is a required field.
9. Select your **Participant Type**. Choose between **Cyber** or **Physical** from the dropdown menu. This is a required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your active email address to receive competition updates and communications.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes (optional).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your current address for contact purposes (optional).
* **City** (`city`, `text`, optional): Enter your city for contact purposes (optional).
* **Country** (`country`, `text`, optional): Enter your country for contact purposes (optional).
* **Zip Code** (`zip`, `number`, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Participant Type** (`participant_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose between **Cyber** or **Physical** participant type.

## Tips
* Ensure your contact information is accurate and up-to-date to receive competition updates and communications.
* Select the correct participant type to ensure you are registered for the correct category.
* Double-check your zip code for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact competition organizers for assistance.
