# International Dance Competition Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to register for an international dance competition. The form collects essential information for the organizers to process registrations and communicate with participants, including contact details, dance style, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name, age group, and dance style.
2. Enter your contact information and dance experience (if applicable).
3. Choose whether or not you have a dance partner participating.
4. If you have a dance partner, provide their name.
5. Enter your emergency contact information.
6. Confirm the accuracy of your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Information
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, text, required): Please enter your name as it should be spelled and formatted for communication purposes.
### Dance Style
* **Dance Style** (`dance_style`, select_one, required): Select the dance style you will be participating in (e.g. ballet, contemporary, hip-hop, jazz, modern, or tap).
### Age Group
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, required): Choose your age group (5-8, 9-12, 13-18, 19-24, 25-30, 31-40, 41-50, 51-60).
### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, email, and phone number as you would like to be contacted.
### Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name, phone number, and email of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
### Dance Partner
* **Dance Partner** (`dance_partner`, select_multiple, optional): Choose whether or not you have a dance partner participating.
### Dance Partner Name
* **Dance Partner Name** (`dance_partner_name`, text, optional): If you have a dance partner, enter their name.
### Emergency Contact Name
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
### Emergency Contact Phone
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
### Emergency Contact Email
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, text, optional): Enter the email of your emergency contact.
### Dance Experience
* **Dance Experience** (`dance_experience`, text, optional): If you have prior experience with dance, enter a brief description.

## Tips

* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Make sure your dance style matches the style of your performance.
* If you have a dance partner, ensure they are registered as well.
* In the emergency contact section, please provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you are under 18, please have a parent or guardian fill out this form on your behalf.
