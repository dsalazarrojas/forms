# Golden Ticket Contest Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Golden Ticket Contest Registration form is designed to collect information from contest participants. It is used to gather personal and entry details, as well as preferences and interests. Please take a few minutes to review the form carefully to ensure you provide accurate and complete information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out each page of the form, which are:
	* Name Page (Page 1)
	* Contact Information (Page 2)
	* Entry Information (Page 3)
	* More Information (Page 4)
	* Confirm (Page 5)
	* Name Page 2 (Page 6)
	* Contact Page 2 (Page 7)
	* Entry Page 2 (Page 8)
	* Info Page 2 (Page 9)
2. Provide accurate and complete information on each page.
3. Click the "Submit" button when you have completed all the pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (Page 1, **Name Page**, text, required): Please enter your name.
* **Contact Information** (Page 2, **Contact Page**, text, optional): Please enter your contact information, including your address, phone number, and email.
* **Entry Information** (Page 3, **Entry Page**, text, optional): Please enter your entry information, including your entry number and any additional details.
* **More Information** (Page 4, **More Page**, text, optional): Please enter more information about yourself.
* **Entry Number** (Page 5, **Entry Number**, number, required): Please enter your entry number.
* **Date of Birth** (Page 5, **Birthday Page, date, required): Please enter your date of birth.
* **Time of Entry** (Page 5, **Time Page, time, required): Please enter your time of entry.
* **Select a Favorite Color** (Page 5, **Fav Color, select_one, required): Choose a favorite color from the options: Red, Blue, Green.
* **Select Multiple Colors** (Page 5, **Multiple Colors, select_multiple, required): Select multiple favorite colors from the options: Red, Blue, Green.
* **Check the boxes** (Page 5, **Hobby, checkbox, optional): Check the boxes for hobbies you have a favorite, love playing video games, or enjoy reading books.
* **Radio Button** (Page 5, **Radio, radio, required): Choose an option from: Option 1, Option 2.
* **Info Page** (Page 6, **Info Page, text, optional): Please enter more information.
* **Less Information** (Page 7, **Less Info, text, optional): Please enter less information.
* **Confirmation** (Page 8, **Confirm, note, optional): This is a confirmation page, please review your information.
* **Name Page 2** (Page 9, **Name Page 2, text, optional): Please enter your name.
* **Contact Page 2** (Page 10, **Contact Page 2, text, optional): Please enter your contact information.
* **Entry Page 2** (Page 11, **Entry Page 2, text, optional): Please enter your entry information.
* **Info Page 2** (Page 12, **Info Page 2, text, optional): Please enter more information.

## Tips
* Please review each page carefully before proceeding to ensure accuracy and completeness of your information.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact our support team.
* This form is for contest participants only.
