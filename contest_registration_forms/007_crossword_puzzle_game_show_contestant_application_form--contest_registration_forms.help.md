# Crossword Puzzle Game Show Contestant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential contestants for a crossword puzzle game show. It will be used to assess your eligibility and suitability to participate in the game show.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contestant details, including first name, last name, and age.
2. Enter your email address, which will be used to contact you with further information about your application.
3. Select your occupation and type of role (e.g. Game Show Host, Game Show Host Assistant, etc.).
4. Choose the category that best describes your submission (e.g. Contest Registration Forms, Entertainment Forms, etc.).
5. Provide any additional notes or comments about your application.
6. Submit your form.
