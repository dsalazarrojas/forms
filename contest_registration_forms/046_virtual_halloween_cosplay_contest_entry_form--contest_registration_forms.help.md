# Virtual Halloween Cosplay Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to submit their entries for the Virtual Halloween Cosplay Contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your form ID, if you have one (not required).
2. Fill in your name (optional, but recommended for transparency).
3. Provide your email address (optional, but recommended for communication).
4. Enter your phone number (optional).
5. Enter the name of your cosplay (e.g., "The Pumpkin King").
6. Briefly describe your cosplay (e.g., "A spooky pumpkin king with a fiery personality").
7. Provide the URL of the image of your cosplay (e.g., a URL from a photo sharing platform).
8. Give a title to your image (e.g., "The Pumpkin King's Revenge").
9. Choose the category for your entry (e.g., Fantasy, Horror, Science Fiction, Comedy, Action).
10. Select the tags relevant to your entry (multiple selections allowed, e.g., Fantasy, Horror).
11. Fill in your name and email address for communication purposes (optional, but recommended for transparency).
12. Enter your phone number (optional).
13. Describe yourself, if you'd like to provide more context about your submission (optional).
14. Provide the URL of the image of yourself, if you'd like to include a personal photo (optional).
15. Give a title to the image of yourself, if you'd like to include a personal photo (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, optional): A unique ID for your entry, if you have one. If you don't have one, you can leave it blank.
* **Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your name, if you'd like to provide it for transparency.
* **Email** (`user_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, if you'd like to provide it for communication.
* **Phone** (`user_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if you'd like to provide it.
* **Cosplay Name** (`cosplay_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your cosplay (e.g., "The Pumpkin King").
* **Cosplay Description** (`cosplay_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe your cosplay (e.g., "A spooky pumpkin king with a fiery personality").
* **Image URL** (`image_url`, text, optional): Provide the URL of the image of your cosplay (e.g., a URL from a photo sharing platform).
* **Image Title** (`image_title`, text, optional): Give a title to your image (e.g., "The Pumpkin King's Revenge").
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Choose the category for your entry (e.g., Fantasy, Horror, Science Fiction, Comedy, Action).
* **Tags** (`tag_list`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple tags relevant to your entry (e.g., Fantasy, Horror, Science Fiction, Comedy, Action).
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, optional): Enter your name, if you'd like to provide it for transparency.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, if you'd like to provide it for communication.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if you'd like to provide it.
* **Submitter Description** (`submitter_description`, text, optional): Describe yourself, if you'd like to provide more context about your submission.
* **Submitter Image URL** (`submitter_image_url`, text, optional): Provide the URL of the image of yourself, if you'd like to include a personal photo.
* **Submitter Image Title** (`submitter_image_title`, text, optional): Give a title to the image of yourself, if you'd like to include a personal photo.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and clear information for your entry to ensure a smooth evaluation process.
* Make sure to keep your contact information up-to-date to facilitate communication with the contest organizers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
