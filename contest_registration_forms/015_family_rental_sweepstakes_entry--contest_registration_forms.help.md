# Family Rental Sweepstakes Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a family rental sweepstakes entry. It is a one-time form and is not intended for recurring submissions.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are entering for a single person, not multiple individuals.
2. Enter your contact information, including your email address, phone number, and a note (optional).
3. Select your entry type from the given options.
4. Enter your entry date and time.
5. Confirm your email address.
6. Confirm your phone number (if entered).
7. Confirm your note (if entered).
8. Indicate how you would like to be contacted if you are a winner.
9. Enter any additional details about your sweepstakes entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input**: Enter the name of the person entering the sweepstakes.
* **Contact Information**: Enter the contact information for the person entering the sweepstakes, including name, phone number, and an optional note.
* **Sweepstakes Details**: Enter any additional details about your sweepstakes entry.
* **Entry Form**: Enter any additional information about your entry.
* **Confirm**: Re-enter your email address.
* **Submission**: Re-enter your phone number and note (if entered).
* **Entry Type**: Select the type of entry you are submitting.
* **Date of Entry**: Enter the date you entered the sweepstakes.
* **Entry Time**: Enter the time you entered the sweepstakes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Note**: Enter any additional information about your entry (optional).
* **Confirm Email**: Re-enter your email address.
* **Confirm Phone**: Re-enter your phone number.
* **Confirm Note**: Re-enter your note (if entered).
* **Winner Announcement**: If you win, how would you like to be contacted?
* **Contact**: Enter any contact information for the winner announcement.
* **Winner Details**: Enter any additional details about the winner.
 

Note: Fields with "Confirm" in their name are for re-entering information to ensure accuracy.
