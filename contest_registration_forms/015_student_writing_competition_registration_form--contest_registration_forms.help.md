# Student Writing Competition Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Writing Competition Registration Form is for students to register for the writing competition by filling out the provided fields.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by entering your first name.
2.  Enter your last name.
3.  If you have a student id, enter it. It could be helpful for school record-keeping purposes.
4.  If applicable, enter your school name.
5.  Specify your grade level. This could be helpful for categorization purposes.
6.  Enter your email address for communication purposes.
7.  Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
8.  Select the type of entry or category you would like to be considered for.
9.  If required, specify the entry date of your piece of writing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First name**: Enter your first name.
    *   Type: Text
    *   Required: No
*   **Last name**: Enter your last name.
    *   Type: Text
    *   Required: No
*   **Student id**: Enter your student id number.
    *   Type: Number
    *   Required: No
*   **School name**: Enter your school name.
    *   Type: Text
    *   Required: No
*   **Grade level**: Select your grade level, e.g., primary, middle, high school.
    *   Type: Number
    *   Required: No
*   **Email**: Enter your email address.
    *   Type: Email
    *   Required: No
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
    *   Type: Text
    *   Required: No
*   **Entry type**: Select the type of entry or category you would like to be considered for.
    *   Type: Select one
    *   Required: Yes
*   **Entry date**: If required, specify the entry date of your piece of writing.
    *   Type: Date
    *   Required: No
