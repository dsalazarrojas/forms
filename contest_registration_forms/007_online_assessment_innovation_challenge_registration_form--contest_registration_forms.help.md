# Online Assessment Innovation Challenge Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Assessment Innovation Challenge Registration Form is designed to collect information from participants who want to join the "Online Assessment Innovation Challenge". It is a simple registration process that helps the organizers understand who is participating and what they want to achieve from the challenge.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Scroll down to the first page, labeled "Registration Form".
2. Fill in your Participant Info.
3. Provide your Contact Info.
4. Enter your Participant Address.
5. Share your Challenge Info.
6. Click the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Info**: Enter your name and a brief description of yourself.
* **Contact Info**: Provide your email, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Participant Address**: Enter your physical address, including your city, state, and zip code.
* **Challenge Info**: Share your thoughts, ideas, or goals related to the challenge. This field is optional but will help the organizers understand your motivation and goals.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your registration form. Make sure to review your information before clicking the button.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or need help, contact the form creators at form@gic.mx.
