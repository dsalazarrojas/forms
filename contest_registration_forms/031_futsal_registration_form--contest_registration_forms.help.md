# Futsal Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for players and team managers to register for a Futsal event. It gathers information about the player/team and their event preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your Player Name, Team Name, and Player Age in the respective fields.
2. Select the desired Event Date for your registration.
3. Choose the number of players in your team.
4. Enter your Contact Email and Team Phone numbers.
5. Choose the Player Position(s) and Event Type for your team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Name** (text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Team Name** (text, required: false): Enter your team's name, if applicable.
* **Player Age** (number, required: false): Enter your age at the time of the event.
* **Event Date** (date, required: false): Select the date you want to participate in the event.
* **Team Size** (number, required: false): Enter the number of players in your team.
* **Contact Email** (email, required: false): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Team Phone** (text, required: false): Enter your team's phone number.
* **Player Position** (select_one, required: false):
	+ Goalkeeper
	+ Defender
	+ Midfielder
	+ Forward
* **Event Type** (select_one, required: false):
	+ Tournament
	+ Friendly
	+ League
	+ Practice

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately to avoid any delays or issues with your registration. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.

Note: This form is for internal use and should not be shared publicly.
