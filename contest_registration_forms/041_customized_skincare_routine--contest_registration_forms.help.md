# Customized Skincare Routine - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a client's customized skincare routine.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client ID** (`client_id`, number, required): Enter your unique client ID number.
* **Skincare Plan** (`skincare_plan`, text, optional): Describe your current skincare plan, including any specific goals or concerns you have.
* **Skincare Routine** (`skincare_routine`, select one, optional): Indicate if you currently follow a skincare routine.
* **Skincare Products** (`skincare_products`, select multiple, optional): Select the skincare products you use, if any.
* **Skincare Frequency** (`skincare_frequency`, date, optional): Enter the frequency at which you perform your skincare routine.
* **Skincare Duration** (`skincare_duration`, time, optional): Enter the duration of each skincare session.
* **Skincare Notes** (`skincare_notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional notes about your skincare routine, including any challenges or successes you've experienced.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when completing the form to ensure accurate and effective recommendations.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your skincare professional for guidance.
- This information will be used to provide personalized skincare recommendations, so please answer honestly and accurately.
