# Fishing Competition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Fishing Competition Form" is designed to collect information about fishing competitions, including flight details, contestant information, event information, and prize details. This form helps organizers and participants to efficiently gather and manage the necessary data for a smooth and successful competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as instructed.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
3. Review your entries carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Flight Information
* **Flight Information** (`flight_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the details of the flight you will be participating in, including the name, date, location, and any other relevant information.

### Contestant Information
* **Contestant Information** (`contestant_info`, text, required/optional): Provide your name, contact number, and any other relevant information as a contestant. This will help the event organizers to get in touch with you.

### Event Information
* **Event Information** (`event_info`, text, required/optional): Describe the event you are participating in, including any specific details about the event, such as the rules, format, and any restrictions.

### Prize Information
* **Prize Information** (`prize_info`, text, required/optional): If there is a prize for the competition, provide details about it here, including its value and any conditions or restrictions that apply.

### Contestant List
* **Contestant List** (`contestant_list`, text, required/optional): If there is a list of contestants, provide it here. This will help the event organizers to keep track of all participants.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizers for assistance.
