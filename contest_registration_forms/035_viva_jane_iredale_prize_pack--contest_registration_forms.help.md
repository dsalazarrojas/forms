# Viva Jane Iredale Prize Pack - Help Guide

## Purpose
The form is a simple registration form for contestants to provide their information for the Viva Jane Iredale Prize Pack.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure everything is correct.
3. Submit the form to process your prize pack request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contestant's name** (`contestant_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email_address`, email, required: true): Enter your valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number, if applicable.
* **Address** (`home_address`, text, required: false): Enter your home address, if applicable.
* **Prize Pack** (`prize_pack`, select_one, required: false): Select the desired prize pack from the available options: "Pack 1" and "Pack 2".

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately.
* Double-check your email address for correctness.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact the contest organizers for assistance.
