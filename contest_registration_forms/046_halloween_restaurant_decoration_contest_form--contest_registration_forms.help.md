# Halloween Restaurant Decoration Contest Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for contest organizers to register their Halloween-themed restaurant decoration contest. The form helps us gather information about the contest, including the name, date, location, prizes, and rules. We will use this information to promote the contest and ensure a fair and exciting competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields marked as `true` in the fields below.
2. Make sure to provide accurate information about your contest.
3. Double-check your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the name of your contest?** (`contest_name`, text, required): This is the name of your contest. Please enter a brief and descriptive title that will make your contest stand out.
* **What date is your contest held on?** (`contest_date`, date, required): Enter the date and time of your contest.
* **What is the location of your contest?** (`contest_location`, text, required): Enter the address or location where the contest will take place.
* **What prizes are you offering?** (`prize_details`, text, required): Describe the prizes you are offering for the contest winners.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or team behind the contest.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **What are the rules?** (`rules`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the contest rules and guidelines.
* **What are the entry rules?** (`entry_rules`, text, required): Describe the rules for contest entries.
* **What is the rule about file uploads?** (`rules_uploads`, text, required): Explain the rules for file uploads during the contest.
* **Who is the contact person for this contest?** (`rules_contact`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person for the contest.
* **Who is the contest organizer?** (`contest_organizer`, text, required): Enter the name of the contest organizer.
* **Who is the organizer for the rules?** (`rules_organizer`, text, required): Enter the name of the person who created the contest rules.
* **Who are the contest judges?** (`rules_judges`, text, required): List the names of the contest judges.
* **More information on the judges?** (`judges_info`, text, required): Provide more details about the judges.
* **How are scores determined?** (`rules_scores`, text, required): Explain how the scores will be calculated.
* **On what criteria will scores be determined?** (`scores_criteria`, text, required): Describe the criteria for scoring.
* **What are the rules for winning?** (`rules_winners`, text, required): Set clear rules for the winners.
* **What are the details on the winner?** (`winner_details`, text, required): Provide details on the prizes and rewards for the winners.
* **Will the contest be published on social media?** (`rules_publication`, select_multiple, required): Choose whether the contest will be published on social media and select the platforms.
* **If yes, which platforms will it be published on?** (`publication_platforms`, select_multiple, required): Select the social media platforms to publish the contest.
* **More information on publication?** (`rules_publication_details`, text, required): Enter additional details about the publication.
* **Publication date of the contest results?** (`rules_publication_date`, date, required): Enter the date of publication.
* **Publication time of the contest results?** (`rules_publication_time`, time, required): Enter the time of publication.
* **Any additional rules or notes?** (`rules_additional`, text, required): Enter any additional rules or notes about the contest.
