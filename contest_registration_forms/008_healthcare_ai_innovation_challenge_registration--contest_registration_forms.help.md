# Healthcare AI Innovation Challenge Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Healthcare AI Innovation Challenge Registration form is designed to collect information from teams participating in the challenge. This form helps the challenge organizers to understand your team's project and evaluate your participation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the "Team Details" page and fill in the following fields:
	* Team Lead Name
	* Team Lead Email
	* Team Lead Phone
2. In the "Team Members" section, select the team members from the list of options provided. Make sure to select at least one team member.
3. In the "Project Summary" section, provide a brief description of your project.
4. In the "Project Name" section, enter a unique name for your project.
5. In the "Innovation Description" section, describe your innovation in detail.
6. In the "Innovation Category" section, select the category that best describes your innovation.
7. In the "Innovation Keywords" section, enter relevant keywords related to your innovation.
8. In the "Team Image URL" section, enter a URL of your team's image.
9. In the "Project Image URL" section, enter a URL of your project's image.
10. In the "Project Video URL" section, enter a URL of your project's video.
11. In the "Team Document URL" section, enter a URL of your team's document.
12. In the "Innovation Tag" section, enter a tag that best describes your innovation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Details** (Team Details, text, required): Enter your team's basic information.
* **Team Lead Name** (Team Lead Name, text, required): Enter the name of your team lead.
* **Team Lead Email** (Team Lead Email, email, optional): Enter your team lead's email address.
* **Team Lead Phone** (Team Lead Phone, text, optional): Enter your team lead's phone number.
* **Team Members** (Team Members, select_multiple, optional): Select the team members from the list of options provided.
* **Project Summary** (Project Summary, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your project.
* **Project Name** (Project Name, text, optional): Enter a unique name for your project.
* **Innovation Description** (Innovation Description, text, optional): Describe your innovation in detail.
* **Innovation Category** (Innovation Category, text, optional): Select the category that best describes your innovation.
* **Innovation Keywords** (Innovation Keywords, text, optional): Enter relevant keywords related to your innovation.
* **Team Image URL** (Team Image URL, text, optional): Enter a URL of your team's image.
* **Project Image URL** (Project Image URL, text, optional): Enter a URL of your project's image.
* **Project Video URL** (Project Video URL, text, optional): Enter a URL of your project's video.
* **Team Document URL** (Team Document URL, text, optional): Enter a URL of your team's document.
* **Innovation Tag** (Innovation Tag, text, optional): Enter a tag that best describes your innovation.
