# Team List Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Team List Submission Form is used to submit a list of players for a contest. This form is intended for authorized team representatives and should only be submitted once for each team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Make sure you are submitting on behalf of an authorized team representative.
2. Ensure you have not previously submitted a team list for this team.
3. Fill in the form with the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **team_players**: Enter a list of players who will be participating in the contest.
* **team_officials**: Enter a list of team officials, such as coaches, managers, or other support staff.
* **jersey_numbers**: Indicate whether or not the team has assigned jersey numbers for each player. Choose "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown list.
* **submit**: Confirm that the form information is accurate and complete.
* **players_names**: Enter the names of the players on your team.
Note: The "players_names" field is a required field, but the form will not validate it as it seems to be missing a validation rule in the original YAML.
