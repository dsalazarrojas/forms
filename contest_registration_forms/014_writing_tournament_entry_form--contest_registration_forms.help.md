# writing_tournament_entry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect entries for a writing tournament. It will be used to gather information about each participant, including their user name, contact information, entry title, entry category, story length, and a brief description of their story or manuscript.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **User Name** field with your name as you would like it to appear on the writing tournament entry list.
2. Enter your **Email** address to ensure we can contact you with updates and notifications.
3. Enter your **Phone Number** if you would like to be contacted by phone.
4. Enter a brief **Entry Title** that describes your story or manuscript.
5. Select your **Entry Category** from the options provided. This will help us categorize your entry.
6. Enter the approximate **Story Length** of your story or manuscript.
7. Provide a brief description of your **Manuscript** in the provided text field.
8. If you have a manuscript to upload, click on the **Upload Manuscript** button and select your file. Note that this field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: A field to input your name as you would like it to appear on the writing tournament entry list. This will be used to display your name on the entry list.
* **Email**: A field to input your email address, which we will use to contact you with updates and notifications.
* **Phone Number**: A field to input your phone number, which we will use to contact you if needed.
* **Entry Title**: A field to input a brief description of your story or manuscript.
* **Entry Category**: A field to select your entry's category from the provided options.
* **Story Length**: A field to input the approximate length of your story or manuscript.
* **Manuscript**: A field to input a brief description of your manuscript.
* **Upload Manuscript**: A field to upload your manuscript file, which is optional.
* **Story**: This field is currently not used and can be ignored.
* **phone_number**: This field is not used for phone number validation, it seems like a typo. Please ignore it.
* **entry_title**: This field has the same purpose as the **Entry Title** field, it seems like a duplicate. Please ignore it.
* **entry_category**: This field is currently not used and can be ignored.
