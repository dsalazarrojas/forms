# district_6710_speech_contest_club_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The district_6710_speech_contest_club_registration form is used for club registration in the District 6710 Speech Contest. Complete this form to provide essential details about your club's speech contest team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the club's details, including any relevant information about the club.
2. Enter your name and contact information as the coordinator.
3. Provide your email address in the proper format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
4. Enter your phone number and address.
5. Describe the district speech contest information.
 
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Club Speech Contest Club Details** (`club_speech_contest_club_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your club, including any relevant details about your team.
* **club_speech_contest_club_coordinator** (`club_speech_contest_club_coordinator`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for the club's speech contest team.
* **coordinator_contact_info** (`coordinator_contact_info`, text, optional): Enter any contact information for the coordinator, such as a phone number or address.
* **name** (`coordinator_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the coordinator.
* **phone** (`coordinator_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **email** (`coordinator_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address in the proper format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
* **address** (`coordinator_address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **district_speech_contest_info** (`district_speech_contest_info`, text, optional): Enter any information about the district speech contest, such as the rules or schedule.
