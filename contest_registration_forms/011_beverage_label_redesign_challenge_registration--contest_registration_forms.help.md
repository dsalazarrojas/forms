# Beverage Label Redesign Challenge Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beverage Label Redesign Challenge is a contest where contestants are asked to create innovative labels for different types of beverages. This form will help us understand your design concept and gather essential details about your background and submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure a smooth and successful submission, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details such as First Name, Last Name, and Email Address.
2. Provide your Professional Background information, including your Profession, Years of Design Experience, and if applicable, your Company or Organization.
3. Specify the type of beverage you are redesigning (Soft drink, Energy drink, Juice or smoothie, Water or sports drink, Coffee or tea, Alcoholic beverage, or Other).
4. Describe the target demographic for your label (Children, Teens, Young adults, Adults, Health-conscious consumers, Eco-conscious consumers, All ages).
5. Briefly describe your design concept and key features.
6. If you have a portfolio, provide a link or mention your portfolio website.
7. List the primary colors used in your design.
8. Specify if your design emphasizes sustainability (yes or no).
9. Choose the software you used to create your design and the format of your design file.
10. Confirm that your design is original, and grant the required rights for it to be used.
11. Accept the challenge terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your primary contact email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Your Profession or Main Role** (`profession`, select_one, required): Choose your profession from the provided options (Graphic designer, Artist, Student, Marketing professional, Package designer, Other creative professional, Hobbyist).
* **Years of Design Experience** (`experience_years`, select_one, required): Choose your years of professional design experience (Less than 1 year, 1-3 years, 3-5 years, 5-10 years, Over 10 years, No professional experience).
* **Company or Organization** (`company_affiliation`, text, optional): Provide your employer or agency name if applicable.
* **Portfolio Website or Link** (`portfolio_website`, text, optional): Provide a link to your portfolio website.
* **Specific Beverage Name or Brand** (`target_beverage_name`, text, required): Enter the name or brand of the beverage you are designing for.
* **Target Demographic** (`target_demographic`, select_multiple, required): Choose the target demographic for your label (Children, Teens, Young adults, Adults, Health-conscious consumers, Eco-conscious consumers, All ages).
* **Briefly Describe Your Design Concept** (`design_concept`, text, required): Write a brief description of your design concept.
* **Key Design Features** (`key_features`, text, required): List the key features of your design.
* **Primary Colors Used** (`color_palette`, text, optional): List the primary colors used in your design.
* **Sustainability Focus** (`sustainability_focus`, select_one, optional): Choose if your design emphasizes sustainability (Yes, eco-friendly materials, Yes, sustainable messaging, Somewhat, False).
* **Software Used** (`design_software`, text, required): Choose the software you used to create your design (Adobe, Canva, Other).
* **Design File Format** (`file_format`, select_one, required): Choose the format of your design file (PDF, AI or EPS, PSD, PNG, JPG, Other).
* **High-Resolution Design** (`high_resolution`, select_one, required): Choose if your design is high-resolution (300 DPI or higher, 150 DPI, Screen resolution, Not sure).
* **Original Work** (`original_work`, select_one, required): Confirm that your design is entirely original (Yes, entirely original, Yes, with some stock elements, No, based on existing design).
* **Rights Grant** (`rights_grant`, select_one, required): Grant the required rights for your design to be used (Yes, fully grant rights, Yes, for judging only, I need to negotiate rights).
* **Accept Challenge Terms and Conditions** (`terms_acceptance`, select_one, required): Accept the challenge terms and conditions (I accept, I do not accept).

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and truthfully.
* Be as descriptive and creative as possible when filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
