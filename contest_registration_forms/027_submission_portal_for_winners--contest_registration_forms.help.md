# Submission Portal For Winners - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register submissions from users, such as contest or giveaway winners. It collects information about the submitted content, including contact details and submission specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all required fields with accurate and correct information.
2. Review each field to ensure you are providing the correct information.
3. Once completed, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **title** (`Submission Portal For Winners`, not applicable): This is the form title.
- **contest_form** (Text Field, Optional): Enter a short description of your submission.
- **contact_information** (Text Field, Optional): Enter your name or the name of the person submitting the content.
- **submission_details** (Text Field, Optional): Enter any additional details about your submission.
- **submission_date** (Date Field, Optional): Enter the date of the submitted content.
- **winner_category** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose all that apply to your submission (e.g., Winner, Non-Winner).
- **submission_type** (Select One, Optional): Choose the type of submission (e.g., Contest, Giveaway, Other).
- **submission_url** (Text Field, Optional): Enter a URL for the submitted content.
- **contact_person** (Text Field, Optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for this submission.
- **contact_email** (Email Field, Optional): Enter the email address of the person responsible for this submission.
- **contact_phone** (Text Field, Optional): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for this submission.
- **contact_address** (Text Field, Optional): Enter the address of the person responsible for this submission.
- **submission_status** (Select One, Optional): Choose the current status of your submission (e.g., Published, Unpublished, Reviewing).
