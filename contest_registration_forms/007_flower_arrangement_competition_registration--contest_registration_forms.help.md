# flower_arrangement_competition_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The flower arrangement competition registration form is designed for individuals or teams to register for a flower arrangement competition. This form collects necessary information to ensure smooth event organization and participant management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill out your personal details, including first name, last name, organization (if applicable), and email address.
- Provide your phone number (optional).
- Share your address (optional, but recommended for event details and communication).
- In the event details section, provide as much information as you can about the event you wish to register for (event type, date, and time).
- Select the type of event you wish to participate in from the available options.
- If you are registering as part of a team, please ensure that all team members have their own registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required: false): If you are representing an organization, enter the name here.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address for communication and event details.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for additional contact.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your address for event details and communication.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, `note`, required: false): Provide as much information as you can about the event you wish to register for.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the type of event you wish to participate in from the options provided.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the event you wish to register for.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the event you wish to register for.

## Tips
- Ensure all team members have their own registration if you are part of a team.
- Use a valid email address for event updates and communication.
- Provide as much detail as possible in the event details section for accurate event management.
- If any of the required fields are not filled, your registration may not be accepted.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to contact us for assistance.
