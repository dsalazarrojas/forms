# Film Festival Judge Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in judging the upcoming film festival. Please answer the questions honestly and accurately to help us find the best match for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information on each page. You can always go back to the previous page to review and make changes before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (id: 1)
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
### Last Name (id: 2)
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
### Email (id: 3)
* **Email**: Enter your professional email address where you can be reached for any festival updates or communication.
### Phone (id: 4)
* **Phone**: Enter your work or mobile phone number where you can be reached for any festival matters.
### Rating (id: 5)
* **Rating**: Enter your rating as a judge on a scale of 1 to 10.
### Goals (id: 6)
* **Goals**: Describe your goals and interests as a judge, such as specific film genres or themes you'd like to focus on.
### Favorite Genre (id: 7)
* **Favorite Genre**: Select the types of films you enjoy judging the most, such as Action, Comedy, Drama, Horror, or Romance.
### Favorite Director (id: 8)
* **Favorite Director**: Choose the director(s) whose work you admire and enjoy watching.
### Favorite Actor (id: 9)
* **Favorite Actor**: Select the actor(s) whose performances you enjoy watching.
### Time (id: 10)
* **Time**: Note any time-specific commitments or preferences you have, such as daytime or evening shifts.
### Notes (id: 11)
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or information you'd like to share about yourself as a judge.
### Agree (id: 12)
* **Agree**: Select "True" if you agree to be a judge, "False" if you do not, or "Maybe" if you're unsure.
### Submit Button (id: 13)
* **Submit**: You will be asked to select a date and time to be available for the festival.
### Submit (id: 14)
* **Submit**: Confirm your availability for the selected date and time.
### Terms and Conditions (id: 15)
* **Terms and Conditions**: Agree to the festival's terms and conditions by checking the box or filling in the text area.

Please fill out the form accurately and completely, and we'll review your submission to find the best match for you as a judge. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
