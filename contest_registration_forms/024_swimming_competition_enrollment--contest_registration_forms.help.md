# swimming_competition_enrollment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register participants for the swimming competition. It collects information about the participant's personal details, swim events, and other relevant details to facilitate the enrollment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your basic participant information on page 1.
* On page 2, select the swim events you are participating in and choose the type of swimming (if applicable) and distance of the swim.
* Make sure to fill in all required fields, which are marked as such.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, text, required/optional): This field is for your basic participant information. Please fill in your details accurately.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your full name.
* **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, number, required/optional): Enter your age.
* **Participant School** (`participant_school`, text, required/optional): If you are participating through a school, enter your school name.
* **Participant Club** (`participant_club`, text, required/optional): If you are participating through a club, enter your club name.
* **Participant Coach** (`participant_coach`, text, required/optional): If you have a coach, enter their name.
* **Swimmer Info** (`swimmer_info`, text, required/optional): This field is for any additional information about you as a swimmer.
* **Swim Events** (`swim_events`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the swim events you are participating in. You can select only one event.
* **Swim Type** (`swim_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): If applicable, choose the type of swimming you will be participating in.
* **Swim Distance** (`swim_distance`, number, required/optional): Enter the distance of the swim.
* **Swim Time** (`swim_time`, time, required/optional): Enter your expected swim time.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address (if required by the competition).
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number (if required by the competition).
