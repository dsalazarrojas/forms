# Virtual Contest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for contestants to register for a virtual contest. To ensure a smooth registration process, please fill out the form carefully and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone number.
5. Enter your Address.
6. Select your City.
7. Select your State from the provided options.
8. Enter your Zip code.
9. Select the Contest you are registering for.
10. Select your Entry Type(s) (choose one or multiple options).
11. Enter any Message (optional).
12. Choose a Date and Time for the contest.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text): Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Last Name** (Text): Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number for future contact purposes.
* **Address** (Text): Enter your physical address for delivery purposes (if necessary).
* **City** (Text): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (Select One): Select your state from the provided options.
* **Zip** (Number): Enter your zip code for delivery purposes (if necessary).
* **Contest** (Select One): Select the contest you are registering for from the provided options.
* **Entry** (Select Multiple): Select the type(s) of entry you are submitting for the contest (choose one or multiple options).
* **Message** (Text): Enter any additional message or comments (optional).
* **Date** (Date): Choose a date for the contest (if applicable).
* **Time** (Time): Choose a time for the contest (if applicable).
* **Submit** (Text): Click this button to submit your registration form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to avoid any issues during the contest.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
