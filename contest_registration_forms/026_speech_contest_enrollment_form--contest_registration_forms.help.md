# Speech Contest Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants who want to enroll in a speech contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address.
2. Select your Participant Category (Participant A, Participant B, or Participant C).
3. If you are representing a school or club, enter your School/Club Name.
4. Optionally, specify the Speech Topic you want to talk about.
5. Choose the Speech Length you plan to take.
6. Enter the Start Time for your speech.
7. Enter the Date of your speech.
8. Optionally, provide the End Time of your speech.
9. Provide the Location where your speech will take place.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address. This will be used to communicate with you about your participation.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for any further communication.
* **Participant Category** (`participant_category`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose from the following options: Participant A, Participant B, or Participant C.
* **School/Club Name** (`school_club_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the school or club you are representing, if applicable.
* **Speech Topic** (`speech_topic`, text, required: false): Optionally, enter the topic you plan to talk about in your speech.
* **Speech Length** (`speech_length`, number, required: false): Choose the length of your speech from the available options.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required: false): Enter the start time of your speech.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your speech.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required: false): Optionally, enter the end time of your speech.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where your speech will take place.
