# cultural_folk_dance_tournament_entry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register entries for a cultural folk dance tournament. Fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure your team's entry is considered for participation in the tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out all required fields (indicated by a "required" tag in the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section).
2. Ensure you fill out all fields with accurate and complete information.
3. Review your entry before submitting to ensure it is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Event Name** (text): Enter the name of the event you are registering for.
* **Team Name** (text): Enter the name of your team, if applicable.
* **Team Type** (select multiple):
	+ Dance Team
	+ Cheerleading
	+ Drill Team
	+ School Group
	+ School Team
* **Category** (select one):
	+ Category 1
	+ Category 2
	+ Category 3
* **Entry Fee** (number): Enter the amount you are paying as an entry fee.
* **Entry Status** (select one):
	+ Approved
	+ Pending
	+ Denied
* **Contact Number** (text): Enter your contact number where you can be reached.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address where you can be reached.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
