# Property Photography Contest Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Photography Contest Registration form is designed to collect participant information and photo entries for a photography competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant details in the **participant_details** field.
2. Enter your photo entries in the **photo_entries** field.
3. Accept or decline the participant agreements in the **participant_agreements** field.
4. Provide your team details in the **team_details** field (if applicable).
5. Enter your contact information in the **contact_info** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_details**: This field is for your basic contact information, such as your name and email address.
* **photo_entries**: This field is for you to upload or describe your photo entries. Please be sure to include a title and a description for each photo.
* **participant_agreements**: By accepting this agreement, you confirm that you have read and understood the contest rules and guidelines. Please check the box to confirm your acceptance.
* **team_details**: If you are submitting as part of a team, please provide the names and contact information of your team members.
* **contact_info**: This field is for your main contact information, such as your name, email address, and phone number.
