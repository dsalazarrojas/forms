# Harvest Baskt Contest Entry - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Harvest Baskt Contest Entry form is designed to collect contest entry information from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and email address.
2. Enter the contest name.
3. Optionally, add your contact number, entry date, entry time, and entry photo.
4. Describe your entry details.
5. Select your entry type (if not required).
6. Click the "Submit" button to submit your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (Required): Enter your name as you would like to be known.
* **Email** (Required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Contact Number** (Optional): Enter your contact number.
* **Contest Name** (Required): Enter the name of the contest you are submitting for.
* **Entry Date** (Optional): Enter the date of your entry.
* **Entry Time** (Optional): Enter the time of your entry.
* **Entry Photo** (Optional): If applicable, attach a photo to support your entry.
* **Entry Details** (Optional): Describe your entry in detail.
* **Entry Type** (Optional): Select the type of entry you are submitting.
* **Submit** (Required): Click the button to submit your entry.

## Tips
Ensure that you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
