# Robotics Competition Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to register for a robotics competition. Please fill out the form carefully to provide accurate information.

## How To Complete This Form
To register for the robotics competition, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out each section carefully, ensuring you answer all required fields.
2.  Review each field's label for a clear understanding of what's being asked.
3.  If a field is marked as "required," make sure to provide a valid input.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Section 1**: This section is for general information.
	+ **Section 1 Label (Text)**: A text box for providing a brief introduction about yourself.
* **Section 2**: This section is for selecting your robotics experience.
	+ **Section 2 Label (Select One)**: Choose your relevant robotics experience level:
		- Option 1: Beginner
		- Option 2: Intermediate
		- Option 3: Advanced
* **Section 3**: This section is for your contact information.
	+ **Section 3 Label (Email)**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Section 4**: This section is for providing more details about your experience.
	+ **Section 4 Label (Text)**: A text box for explaining your robotics experience.
* **Section 5**: This section is for any additional comments.
	+ **Section 5 Label (Text)**: A text box for any additional comments or information.
* **Section 6**: This section is for uploading your robot design files.
	+ **Section 6 Label (Text)**: A text box for uploading your robot design files.
* **Section 7**: This section is for describing your robot's features.
	+ **Section 7 Label (Text)**: A text box for describing your robot's features.
* **Section 8**: This section is for providing your team information.
	+ **Section 8 Label (Text)**: A text box for providing your team information.
* **Section 9**: This section is for uploading your competition entry.
	+ **Section 9 Label (Text)**: A text box for uploading your competition entry.
* **Section 10**: This section is for submitting your registration.
	+ **Section 10 Label (Text)**: A text box for submitting your registration information.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are filled before submitting.
