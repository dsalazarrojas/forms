# Most Creative Branding Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect nominations for the "Most Creative Branding" contest. This form is meant to gather relevant information about each nomination, which will be reviewed and evaluated by the contest organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with all the required and optional fields as prompted.
2. Please ensure that all fields are filled out correctly and accurately.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required information is included.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Name** (`nomination_name`, text, required: false): 
  Enter the name of the nomination you are submitting. Please provide a brief and descriptive title for the nomination.

* **Nomination Category** (`nomination_category`, select_multiple, required: false): 
  Choose the category that best fits your nomination. Please select all applicable categories from the list.

* **Nominee Name** (`nominee_name`, text, required: false): 
  Enter the name of the person or entity nominated. Please include the full name of the individual or organization.

* **Nominee Email** (`nominee_email`, email, required: false): 
  Enter a valid email address for the nominee. This will help us contact them if needed.

* **Nominee Phone** (`nominee_phone`, text, required: false): 
  Enter a phone number for the nominee. This is optional but will help us contact them if needed.

* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required: false): 
  Enter the date the nomination was submitted. This will help us track the submission timeline.

* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required: false): 
  Enter the time the nomination was submitted. This will help us track the submission time.

* **Nomination Reason** (`nomination_reason`, text, required: false): 
  Provide a brief description of why you are nominating this person/organization. 

* **Nominee Title** (`nominee_title`, text, required: false): 
  Enter the title or position of the nominee.

* **Nominee Position** (`nominee_position`, text, required: false): 
  Enter the position or department of the nominee.

Note: All fields are optional, but please ensure to complete all required fields to ensure a complete submission.
