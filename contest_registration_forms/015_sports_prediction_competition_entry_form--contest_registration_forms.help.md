# sports_prediction_competition_entry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sports prediction competition entry form is designed to collect user entries for a sports prediction competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Enter your predicted score for the competition.
3. Select the event you are participating in from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter your payout for winning.
5. Enter your personal prediction.
6. Enter a brief comment.
7. Select the date you submitted your entry.
8. Enter your name.
9. Enter your email address.
10. Enter your phone number.
11. Select any additional information you would like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **entry_form_competition_report_score**
*   (`title`: sports_prediction_competition_entry_form, `type`: number, required: true)
    Enter your predicted score for the competition. A number between 0 and 100 will be accepted.

### 2. **entry_form_event**
*   (`title`: sports_prediction_competition_entry_form, `type`: select_one, required: true)
    Select the event you are participating in from the dropdown menu.

### 3. **entry_form_payout**
*   (`title`: sports_prediction_competition_entry_form, `type`: number, required: true)
    Enter your payout for winning. A number between 0 and 100 will be accepted.

### 4. **entry_form_user_prediction**
*   (`title`: sports_prediction_competition_entry_form, `type`: text, required: true)
    Enter your personal prediction. Keep it brief and concise.

### 5. **entry_form_user_comment**
*   (`title`: sports_prediction_competition_entry_form, `type`: note, required: true)
    Enter a brief comment about your entry.

### 6. **entry_form_date_submitted**
*   (`title`: sports_prediction_competition_entry_form, `type`: date, required: true)
    Select the date you submitted your entry.

### 7. **entry_form_user_name**
*   (`title`: sports_prediction_competition_entry_form, `type`: text, required: true)
    Enter your name.

### 8. **entry_form_email**
*   (`title`: sports_prediction_competition_entry_form, `type`: email, required: true)
    Enter your email address.

### 9. **entry_form_phone**
*   (`title`: sports_prediction_competition_entry_form, `type`: text, required: true)
    Enter your phone number.

### 10. **entry_form_additional_info**
*   (`title`: sports_prediction_competition_entry_form, `type`: select_multiple, required: false)
    Select any additional information you would like to provide.

### 11. **entry_form_submit**
*   (`title`: sports_prediction_competition_entry_form, `type`: text, required: true)
    Click "Submit" to submit your entry.
