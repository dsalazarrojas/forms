# ppmt_print_competition_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect entry details for the art competition. It's a crucial step in the submission process, and it's essential to fill it out accurately to avoid any issues with your entry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the entry details section, providing a brief description of your entry.
2. Choose the correct payment option (Yes/No) to indicate if you're paying online or offline.
3. Select the type of entry you're submitting (Photograph, Artist, Artisan, or Student).
4. Enter the name of the artist associated with the entry.
5. Provide the email address of the artist or submitter.
6. Enter the phone number of the artist or submitter.
7. Enter the name of the entry.
8. Enter the title of the entry.
9. Select the entry date and time.
10. Upload any relevant images or files.
11. Enter the price of the entry.
12. Select the currency for the entry price.
13. Select the status of the entry (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
14. Provide any additional notes about the entry.
15. Optionally, provide submitter information (name, email, phone number, etc.) to associate with the entry.

## Tips

* Double-check your entries for any errors before submitting.
