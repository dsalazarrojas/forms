# Free Throw Contest Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Free Throw Contest Registration form is a collection of information needed to register a participant in the Free Throw Contest. This form is used to gather details about the participant, their school, and their league affiliation. Completing this form accurately and thoroughly is essential for ensuring that the participant's information is correctly recorded and processed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by providing your participant's details:
	* **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant as it appears on their official documents.
	* **Participant Email**: Enter the email address associated with the participant.
	* **Participant Phone**: Enter the phone number of the participant.
	* **Participant Age**: Enter the age of the participant.
	* **Participant Date of Birth**: Enter the participant's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yy.
	* **Participant Gender**: Select the participant's gender (male or female).
	* **Participant Address**: Enter the participant's address.
	* **Participant City**: Enter the city where the participant resides.
	* **Participant State**: Enter the state where the participant resides.
	* **Participant Postal Code**: Enter the postal code of the participant's location.
	* **Participant Country**: Select the country where the participant resides (USA or Other).
2. If your league is affiliated with a school, please provide the school information:
	* **School Name**: Enter the name of the participant's school.
	* **School Contact**: Enter the contact person's name and phone number at the school.
	* **School Email**: Enter the school's email address.
3. Confirm your school's and league's participation:
	* **School Signature**: Select the name of your school from the list (not applicable if your school is not affiliated).
	* **League Signature**: Select the name of your league from the list (not applicable if your league is not affiliated).
	* **Community Event Signature**: Select the name of the community event your school participates in (not applicable if not participating).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details**: 
  * **Participant Name** (text): Enter the name of the participant as it appears on their official documents.
  * **Participant Email** (email): Enter the email address associated with the participant.
  * **Participant Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the participant.
  * **Participant Age** (number): Enter the age of the participant.
  * **Participant Date of Birth** (date): Enter the participant's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yy.
  * **Participant Gender** (select_one): Select the participant's gender (male or female).
  * **Participant Address** (text): Enter the participant's address.
  * **Participant City** (text): Enter the city where the participant resides.
  * **Participant State** (text): Enter the state where the participant resides.
  * **Participant Postal Code** (text): Enter the postal code of the participant's location.
  * **Participant Country** (select_one): Select the country where the participant resides (USA or Other).
* **School and League Details**:
  * **School Name** (text): Enter the name of the participant's school.
  * **School Contact** (text): Enter the contact person's name and phone number at the school.
  * **School Email** (email): Enter the school's email address.
  * **League Name** (text): Enter the name of the participant's league.
  * **League Contact** (text): Enter the contact person's name and phone number at the league.
  * **Community Event Signature** (select_multiple): Select the name of the community event your school participates in (not applicable if not participating).
  * **School Signature** (select_multiple): Select the name of your school from the list (not applicable if your school is not affiliated).
  * **League Signature** (select_multiple): Select the name of your league from the list (not applicable if your league is not affiliated).
  * **Waiver Acceptance** (select_multiple): Select whether you accept the waiver terms.
* **Signature Fields**:
  * **School Signature** (select_multiple): Select the name of your school from the list (not applicable if your school is not affiliated).
  * **League Signature** (select_multiple): Select the name of your league from the list (not applicable if your league is not affiliated).
  * **Community Event Signature** (select_multiple): Select the name of the community event your school participates in (not applicable if not participating).

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled accurately and thoroughly to avoid any processing errors.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the contest organizers for clarification.
* This form is designed to be completed by the participant or their authorized representative.
