# Music Festival Vendor Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Festival Vendor Extension Form is designed to collect information from music vendors to facilitate their extension to the festival.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the required fields to ensure your information is submitted correctly.
2. Enter your vendor name in the "Enter Vender Name" field.
3. Provide your contact email in the "Vender Contact Email" field.
4. Select the desired dates for your performance in the "Desired Dates" field.
5. Choose the desired time slots for your performance in the "Desired Time Slots" field.
6. Optionally, add any additional notes about your performance in the "Additional Notes" field.
7. Select your music genres in the "Music Genres" field.
8. Provide the URL of your vendor image in the "Vender Image URL" field.
9. Choose your contract type in the "Contract Type" field.
10. Select the extension date for your performance in the "Extension Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Vender Name**: This field is a text input where you can enter your vendor name.
* **Vender Contact Email**: This field is a email input where you can enter your contact email.
* **Desired Dates**: This field is a date input where you can select the dates for your performance.
* **Desired Time Slots**: This field is a time input where you can select the time slots for your performance.
* **Additional Notes**: This field is a note input where you can add any additional notes about your performance.
* **Music Genres**: This field is a multiple select input where you can select the music genres you perform.
* **Vender Image URL**: This field is a text input where you can enter the URL of your vendor image.
* **Contract Type**: This field is a select one input where you can choose your contract type.
* **Extension Date**: This field is a date input where you can select the extension date for your performance.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill in all required fields correctly.
* Use the correct format for date and time inputs.
