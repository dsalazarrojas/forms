# Pet Adoption Agreement Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Adoption Agreement Extension Form is a document used to formalize the adoption process for a pet. It ensures that both the adopter and the organization are clear on their responsibilities and commitments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as `required`) with your information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand its purpose and what information is needed.
3. Submit the completed form to the organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **First Page** (`first_page`, text, not required)
This is the first page of the form, where you can enter any additional comments or details about the pet adoption.

### **Name** (`name`, text, not required)
Please enter your name as the adopter.

### **Name of Pet** (`pet_name`, text, not required)
Enter the name of the pet being adopted.

### **Reason for Extension** (`reason_for_extension`, select_one, not required)
Select one of the following reasons for extension:
* Pet is showing aggression towards another pet.
* Pet is showing aggression towards people.
* Other

### **Am I Responsible and Willing?** (`am_i_responsible_and_willing`, select_one, not required)
Indicate whether you are responsible and willing to care for the pet:
* True
* False

### **I Agree to Ensure** (`i_agree_to_ensure`, select_multiple, not required)
Select all the following that you agree to ensure:
* To provide a suitable environment for the pet.
* To provide necessary care and attention.
* To keep the pet indoors.
* To keep the pet outdoors.

### **I Will Ensure** (`i_will_ensure`, select_multiple, not required)
Select all the following that you agree to do:
* To provide necessary vaccinations.
* To provide regular veterinary care.
* To provide a safe and healthy environment.
* To keep the pet from running away.

### **Adopter Signature** (`adopter_signature`, date, not required)
Please sign and date this section to confirm your agreement.

### **Adopter Notes** (`adopter_notes`, text, not required)
Add any additional comments or details about the pet adoption.

### **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, not required)
Enter the name of the organization.

### **Organization Contact** (`organization_contact`, text, not required)
Enter the contact information for the organization.

### **Organization Email** (`organization_email`, email, not required)
Enter the email address for the organization.

### **Organization Phone** (`organization_phone`, text, not required)
Enter the phone number for the organization.

### **Organization Signature** (`organization_signature`, date, not required)
Please sign and date this section to confirm the organization's agreement.

### **Organization Notes** (`organization_notes`, text, not required)
Add any additional comments or details from the organization.

### **Signature of Adoptor** (`signature_of_adoptor`, text, not required)
Please sign and date this section to confirm your agreement as the adopter.

### **Date** (`date`, date, not required)
Please enter the date of signing.

### **Time** (`time`, time, not required)
Please enter the time of signing.

### **Address** (`address`, text, not required)
Enter your address.

### **City** (`city`, text, not required)
Enter the city of your location.

### **State** (`state`, text, not required)
Enter the state of your location.

### **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, not required)
Enter the zip code of your location.

### **Country** (`country`, text, not required)
Enter the country of your location.

### **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, select_one, not required)
Select the type of pet:
* Cat
* Dog
* Other

### **Pet ID** (`pet_id`, text, not required)
Enter the ID number of the pet.
