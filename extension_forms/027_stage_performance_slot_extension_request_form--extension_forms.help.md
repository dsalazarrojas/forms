# Stage Performance Slot Extension Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for requesting an extension for a stage performance. It is used by organizers to submit their requests for an extension, which are then reviewed and approved by the performance management team. To avoid duplication, please ensure that you have not previously submitted a request for the same performance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and the name of the performer you are submitting the request on behalf of.
2. Provide the date and reason for your request.
3. Specify the duration of the extension you are requesting.
4. Add any additional notes or comments.
5. Fill in the contact information for the person requesting the extension.
6. Click the "Submit" button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **stage_performance_slot_extension_request_form**: This is the title of the form. Enter a brief title for your request.
* **request_date**: The date you are requesting the extension for. Use the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **request_duration**: The length of time you are requesting for the extension.
* **reason_for_request**: The reason you are requesting the extension. Please be specific and provide as much detail as possible.
* **performer_name**: The name of the performer you are submitting the request on behalf of.
* **performer**: Select the performer you are submitting the request for from the list. Please ensure you select the correct performer to avoid any confusion.
* **reason_1**: A second reason for your request. Please be specific and provide as much detail as possible.
* **reason_2**: A third reason for your request. Please be specific and provide as much detail as possible.
* **reason_3**: A fourth reason for your request. Please be specific and provide as much detail as possible.
* **additional_notes**: Any additional comments or notes you would like to add.
* **organizer_name**: The name of the person or organization requesting the extension.
* **organizer_email**: The email address of the person or organization requesting the extension.
* **organizer_phone**: The phone number of the person or organization requesting the extension.
* **organizer_notes**: Any additional notes or comments from the person or organization requesting the extension.
* **organizer_title**: The title of the person requesting the extension.
* **organizer_position**: The position of the person requesting the extension.
* **organizer_organization**: The organization of the person requesting the extension.
* **organizer_url**: The URL of the person or organization requesting the extension.
* **organizer_fax**: The fax number of the person or organization requesting the extension.
* **additional_organizer_notes**: Any additional notes or comments from the person or organization requesting the extension.
