# Stamped Extension Pre Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stamped Extension Pre Consultation Form is designed to gather information from clients about their specific needs and requirements before a consultation. This form helps to ensure that the consultant has the necessary information to provide effective assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your first name in the **first_name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **email** field.
3. Provide your phone number in the **phone_number** field.
4. Select the type of **extension_type** you need. You can choose from the available options or type in your own **other_extension_types**.
5. Specify your **availability** in hours, using the format HH:MM or 12:00 (24-hour clock).
6. Select the duration of your **time** requirement.
7. Add any additional **note** about your request.
8. Specify the **client_dose** amount.
9. Indicate whether you are 18 or older (**is_client_18**) or 21 or older (**is_client_21**) or 25 or older (**is_client_25**) or 26 or older (**is_client_26**) or 27 or older (**is_client_27**) or 28 or older (**is_client_28**) or 30 or older (**is_client_30**) or 32 or older (**is_client_32**) or 34 or older (**is_client_34**) or 35 or older (**is_client_35**) or 36 or older (**is_client_36**).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name** (text, required/optional): Your first name.
* **email** (email, required/optional): Your email address.
* **phone_number** (text, required/optional): Your phone number.
* **extension_type** (select_one, required/optional): The type of extension you need (e.g., Extension 1, Extension 2).
* **other_extension_types** (select_multiple, required/optional): If your extension type is not listed, you can type in your own option.
* **availability** (number, required/optional): Your availability in hours.
* **time** (time, required/optional): The duration of your time requirement.
* **note** (note, required/optional): Any additional note about your request.
* **client_dose** (text, required/optional): The client dose.
* **is_client_18** (select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether you are 18 or older (Yes/No).
* **is_client_21** (select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether you are 21 or older (Yes/No).
* **is_client_25** (select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether you are 25 or older (Yes/No).
* **is_client_26** (select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether you are 26 or older (Yes/No).
* **is_client_27** (select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether you are 27 or older (Yes/No).
* **is_client_28** (select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether you are 28 or older (Yes/No).
* **is_client_30** (select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether you are 30 or older (Yes/No).
* **is_client_32** (select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether you are 32 or older (Yes/No).
* **is_client_34** (select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether you are 34 or older (Yes/No).
* **is_client_35** (select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether you are 35 or older (Yes/No).
* **is_client_36** (select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether you are 36 or older (Yes/No).

## Tips
* Please fill out the form carefully to ensure your needs are accurately captured.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the support team.
* Double-check your selections before submitting the form.
