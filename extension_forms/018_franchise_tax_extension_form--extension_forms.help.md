# Franchise Tax Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide an extension of the franchise tax return filing deadline for an entity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out all required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
* Select the entity type from the drop-down menu that best describes your entity.
* Choose the filing period for which you are requesting an extension.
* Select the filing status that applies to your entity.
* Choose the filing method used by your entity.
* Enter the frequency of filing for your entity.
* Enter the reason for the extension, if necessary.
* Enter the date of the extension, if applicable.
* Select the extension status.
* Enter any comments about the extension.
* Select the agreed status.
* Enter the name of your entity.
* Enter the address of your entity.
* Enter the date on which the extension was agreed to.
* Select the agreed status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Franchise Tax Extension Form Input (\*):**
	+ Enter the details about your franchise tax extension request.
* **Entity Type:** (select\_one)
	+ Choose the entity type that best describes your entity.
* **Franchise Tax Return Due Date (\*):**
	+ Enter the date by which the franchise tax return is due.
* **Franchise Tax Filing Period (\*):**
	+ Choose the period for which you are requesting an extension.
* **Franchise Tax Filing Status (\*):**
	+ Select the status that applies to your entity.
* **Franchise Tax Filing Method (\*):**
	+ Choose the method used by your entity.
* **Franchise Tax Filing Frequency:**
	+ Enter the frequency of filing for your entity.
* **Franchise Tax Extension Reason:**
	+ Enter the reason for the extension, if necessary.
* **Franchise Tax Extension Date:**
	+ Enter the date of the extension.
* **Franchise Tax Extension Status:**
	+ Select the status of the extension.
* **Franchise Tax Extension Comment:**
	+ Enter any comments about the extension.
* **Franchise Tax Extension Agreed:**
	+ Select the agreed status.
* **Entity Name (\*):**
	+ Enter the name of your entity.
* **Entity Address (\*):**
	+ Enter the address of your entity.
* **Franchise Tax Extension Agreed Date:**
	+ Enter the date on which the extension was agreed to.
* **Franchise Tax Extension Agreed Status:**
	+ Select the status of the agreed extension.

Note: The fields with an asterisk (\*) are required fields.
