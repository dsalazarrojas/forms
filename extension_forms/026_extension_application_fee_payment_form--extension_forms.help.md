# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and manage applications and payments for extensions. It provides a structured format for users to input their information and payment details. The form is intended for specific types of extensions, so please review the form carefully before submitting it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Application Details** page with your name, address, and other required information.
2. Proceed to the **Payment Details** page and enter your payment method and amount received.
3. On the **Extension Request** page, select whether you are submitting a true or false extension request.
4. Next, on the **Payment Method** page, choose your preferred payment method from the options provided.
5. Enter the **Application Fee** and **Amount Received** on the next page.
6. On the following page, enter the date you received the amount.
7. On the **Payment Status** page, select the status of your payment.
8. Finally, on the last page, enter any additional notes and your email and phone number.

* **Application Details (Application Details)** (`application_details`, required, true): This field is for entering your name, address, and other relevant details for the application.
* **Payment Details (Payment Details)** (`payment_details`, required, true): This field is for entering payment method and amount received.
* **Extension Request (Extension Request)** (`extension_request`, optional, false): This field is for indicating whether you are submitting a true or false extension request.
* **Payment Method (Payment Method)** (`payment_method`, optional, false): This field is for selecting your preferred payment method.
* **Application Fee (Application Fee)** (`application_fee`, required, true): This field is for entering the application fee.
* **Amount Received (Amount Received)** (`amount_received`, required, true): This field is for entering the amount received.
* **Date Received (Date Received)** (`date_received`, required, true): This field is for entering the date you received the amount.
* **Payment Status (Payment Status)** (`payment_status`, optional, false): This field is for selecting the status of your payment.
* **Note (Note)** (`note`, optional, false): This field is for entering any additional notes.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, optional, false): This field is for entering your email address.
