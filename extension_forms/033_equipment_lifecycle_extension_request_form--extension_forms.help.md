# equipment_lifecycle_extension_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The equipment lifecycle extension request form is used to capture information for the equipment lifecycle extension request process. This form helps asset managers gather the necessary details to evaluate and extend the lifecycle of equipment assets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Is the asset aging**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the asset is currently in an aging process.
2. **Request Date**: Enter the date when the request was made.
3. **Equipment description**: Provide a brief description of the asset, including its make, model, and any other relevant information.
4. **Asset status**: Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate the asset's current status.
5. **Approval date**: Enter the date when the asset was approved for extension.
6. **Supervisor confirmation date**: Enter the date when the supervisor confirmed the asset's extension.
7. **Supervisor name**: Enter the name of the supervisor who confirmed the asset's extension.
8. **Supervisor email**: Enter the email address of the supervisor who confirmed the asset's extension.
9. **Category**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the asset falls under a specific category.
10. **Type**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the asset belongs to a specific type.
11. **Asset number**: Enter the asset's identification number.
12. **Requestor name**: Enter the name of the person who made the request.
13. **Requestor phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who made the request.
14. **Requestor email**: Enter the email address of the person who made the request.
15. **Department**: Enter the department that the asset belongs to.
16. **Location**: Enter the location where the asset is currently stored.
17. **Asset condition**: Provide a brief description of the asset's condition, including any wear and tear, or maintenance requirements.
