# Property Stay Extension Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Stay Extension Agreement Form is a document that captures the details of an agreement between a property manager and a landlord to extend a stay period. This form is used to formalize the terms of the extension, including the start and end dates of the stay, and the duration of the extended stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and title.
2. Enter your name and title.
3. Enter the start and end dates of the stay, in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
4. Enter the new duration of the stay.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Property Manager**: This field is where you enter your name as the property manager.
	* If you are the person in charge of managing the property, enter your name here.
- **Landlord**: This field is where you enter the name of the landlord.
	* If you are the owner of the property, enter your name here.
- **Stay Start Date**: This field is where you enter the start date of the stay.
	* Enter the date when the stay will begin, in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- **Stay End Date**: This field is where you enter the end date of the stay.
	* Enter the date when the stay will end, in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- **New Stay Duration**: This field is where you enter the new duration of the stay.
	* Enter a whole number representing the new duration of the stay in years or months.
- **Tips** 
* Be accurate when entering the dates to ensure that they are correct.
* Make sure to enter the new stay duration correctly.
* Review all the fields carefully before submitting the form.
