## Form 4868 Tax Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Form 4868 Tax Extension Form is used to request a tax deadline extension for a specific tax return. This form is used to apply for an automatic extension of time to file, which grants the taxpayer an additional 6 months to file their tax return.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information and documents ready before starting.
2. Fill in the "Tax Extension Form" field with your information in the designated area.
3. Select one option for "Apply for tax deadline extension" to choose the reason for your application.
4. Select multiple options for "Tax Return" to specify which return you are applying for an extension.
5. Optionally, you can enter any additional information in the "Fill out on any device" field.
6. Review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tax Extension Form** (`tax_extension_form`, text, required): This field is where you will enter your tax information and application details.
* **Apply for tax deadline extension** (`apply_for_tax_deadline_extension`, select_one, required): Select one option from the drop-down menu to specify the reason for your tax extension application. This will help the tax authority to process your request efficiently.
* **Tax Return** (`tax_return`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable tax returns that you are applying for an extension. This will ensure that your application covers all relevant returns.
* **Fill out on any device** (`fillable_form`, text, optional): If you need to add any additional information or explanation for your application, this field is for you to provide it.
* **Converts into original PDF layout upon submission** (`converts_into_original_pdf_layout`, text, optional): This field is not applicable for your current form submission.
