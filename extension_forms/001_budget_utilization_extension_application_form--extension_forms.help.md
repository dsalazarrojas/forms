# Budget Utilization Extension Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Budget Utilization Extension Application Form is a formal request to extend the time allocated for a specific budget line item. This form is used to submit a justification for why additional time is needed and to provide an expected completion date for the remaining activities.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Review the form carefully and ensure you have answered all required fields (indicated by a asterisk *).
*   Fill in your information accurately and concisely.
*   Be prepared to provide supporting documentation or evidence if required by the reviewer.
*   Review the form for completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Applicant Name** (Applicant Name, text, *): This is your full name as it appears on your official identification.
*   **Department or Unit** (Department or Unit, text, *): The department or unit responsible for the budget line item being extended.
*   **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, *): A valid email address where the reviewer can reach out for follow-up or additional information.
*   **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, ): Optional phone number for urgent contact.
*   **Project or Initiative Name** (Project or Initiative Name, text, *): The name of the project or initiative associated with the budget line item.
*   **Budget Line Item** (Budget Line Item, text, *): Identify the specific budget line item requiring the extension.
*   **Current Utilization Percent** (Current Utilization Percent, number, *): Enter the percentage of funds already used for the project (e.g., 75).
*   **Requested Extension Period** (Requested Extension Period, select\_one, *): Select the number of additional days requested for the extension (30, 60, 90, or 120 days).
*   **Justification for Extension** (Justification for Extension, text, *): Explain why additional time is needed and provide details on planned activities.
*   **Expected Completion Date** (Expected Completion Date, date, *): Anticipated date when remaining activities will be completed.
*   **Manager Comments** (Manager Comments, note, ): Optional comments for the reviewer.
*   **Approval Status** (Approval Status, select\_one, *): The reviewer's administrative decision on the extension request (Pending, Approved, Denied).
