# point_of_sale_agreement_extension_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The point of sale agreement extension form is used by customers to provide additional information or terms to an existing agreement. This form is designed to be completed by the customer in order to ensure that all necessary details are documented and agreed upon.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and requirements of the form.
2. Complete the form accurately and thoroughly, paying attention to all required and optional fields.
3. If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, please don't hesitate to seek clarification from the relevant parties.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Information** (1, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the agreement extension, including the reason for the extension and any relevant details.
* **Customer Info** (2, select_multiple, optional): Check the relevant boxes to indicate the type of customer info to include in the agreement extension:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Agreement Extension Details** (3, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the agreement extension, including any changes or amendments to the original terms.
* **Extension Effective Date** (4, date, optional): Enter the effective date of the agreement extension, if applicable.
* **Termination Date** (5, date, optional): Enter the termination date of the agreement, if applicable.
* **Payment Terms** (6, text, optional): Enter any payment terms or conditions that are specific to the agreement extension.
* **Other Terms** (7, text, optional): Enter any other terms or conditions that are specific to the agreement extension.
* **Review** (8, select_one, optional): Check 'Yes' if you have reviewed the agreement extension carefully and 'No' if you need to review it further.
* **Customer Notes** (9, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments from the customer.
* **Business Terms** (10, text, optional): Enter any business terms or conditions that are specific to the agreement extension.
* **Payment Terms 2** (11, text, optional): Enter any additional payment terms or conditions that are specific to the agreement extension.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when completing the form to avoid any future discrepancies or conflicts.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, please don't hesitate to seek clarification from the relevant parties.
* Ensure that you have a clear understanding of the terms and conditions of the agreement and any changes you wish to make before completing the form.
