# Relocation Budget Extension Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Relocation Budget Extension Request Form is used to request an extension to an employee's approved relocation budget for travel. This form must be completed by the employee and approved by both their manager and department manager before it can be processed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee name
2. Select your department
3. Enter your start date (if applicable)
4. Specify your travel destination
5. State the purpose of your travel
6. Provide the reason for your request
7. Get approval from both your manager and department manager
8. Enter the approval date and approval decision

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID badge or employee database.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Select your department from the list.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): If your travel dates have changed, please enter the new start date.
* **Travel Destination** (`travel_destination`, text, required): Enter the destination city or location of your travel.
* **Purpose of Travel** (`purpose_of_travel`, text, required): Briefly explain the reason for your travel.
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, text, required): Provide a justification for your request to extend your relocation budget.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, select_one, required): Have your manager approve your request by selecting 'Yes' in the dropdown menu.
* **Department Manager Signature** (`department_manager_signature`, select_one, required): Have your department manager approve your request by selecting 'Yes' in the dropdown menu.
* **Approval Comment** (`approval_comment`, text, optional): Add any comments or notes for the approval process.
* **Additional Comment** (`additional_comment`, text, optional): Add any additional information or context that may be helpful for the approval process.
* **Approval Decision** (`approval_decision`, select_one, required): The approval decision should be 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate approval or denial.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, required): Enter the date of approval.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter your manager's name.
* **Department Name** (`department_name`, text, required): Enter your department name.
* **Department Manager Name** (`department_manager_name`, text, required): Enter your department manager's name.

Note: Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly, as this form will be reviewed and processed accordingly.
