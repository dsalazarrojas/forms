# Volunteer Registration Extension Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an extension to an existing volunteer registration. If you are experiencing an extension of your volunteer registration, please fill out this form to submit your request. This form will help our team review and process your request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information in the "User Info" section.
2. Describe your request in the "Request Info" section.
3. Select your current volunteer status (Active, Inactive, or On Leave of Absence) in the "Volunteer Status" section.
4. Enter the start date and end date of the extension, if applicable, in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields.
5. Provide your Volunteer ID if you are an existing volunteer.
6. Specify the number of hours you are requesting to extend in the "Hours per Week" field.
7. Provide a reason for your extension request in the "Reason" section.
8. If applicable, specify who requested this extension (e.g., manager, team leader) in the "Extension Requested By" field.
9. Select the status of your extension request (Approved, Denied, or In Progress) in the "Manager Approval" field.
10. If your extension request is approved, enter the name of the person who approved it in the "Extension Approved By" field.
11. Enter the date your extension was approved in the "Extension Approved Date" field.
12. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" section.
13. Finally, submit the form by entering your name in the "Form Submitted By" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Info (Text, <required/optional>)**: This is where you should enter your personal information, such as your name and contact details.
- **Request Info (Text, <required/optional>)**: Please describe your extension request in this field.
- **Volunteer Status (Select One, <required/optional>)**: Select your current volunteer status (Active, Inactive, or On Leave of Absence).
- **Start Date (Date, <required/optional>)**: If you are requesting an extension, enter the start date of the extension.
- **End Date (Date, <required/optional>)**: If you are requesting an extension, enter the end date of the extension.
- **Volunteer ID (Text, <required/optional>)**: If you are an existing volunteer, please enter your Volunteer ID.
- **Hours per Week (Number, <required/optional>)**: Enter the number of hours you are requesting to extend.
- **Reason (Text, <required/optional>)**: Please provide a reason for your extension request.
- **Extension Requested By (Text, <required/optional>)**: If applicable, specify who requested this extension.
- **Manager Approval (Select Multiple, <required/optional>)**: Select the status of your extension request (Approved, Denied, or In Progress).
- **Extension Approved By (Text, <required/optional>)**: If your extension request is approved, enter the name of the person who approved it.
- **Extension Approved Date (Date, <required/optional>)**: Enter the date your extension was approved.
- **Notes (Note, <required/optional>)**: Add any additional notes or comments.
- **Form Submitted By (Text, <required/optional>)**: Please enter your name to submit the form.
