# Supplier Agreement Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Supplier Agreement Extension Form" is used for requesting changes to a supplier's agreement. This form helps you communicate the reason, type, and details of the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Choose the type of extension you need to request by selecting the correct option in the "Extension Type" dropdown menu.
2. Enter the date you want the extension to start and end in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields, respectively.
3. Provide a clear reason for the extension in the "Reason For Extension" field.
4. Enter the supplier's name and contact information, including their phone number and email address, in the "Supplier Name", "Contact Number", and "Email Address" fields.
5. Include any additional details about the extension in the "Extension Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Agreement Extension Form** (`supplier_agreement_extension_form`, text, optional):
    This is the main section for entering the reason and details of the extension.
* **Extension Type** (`extension_type`, select, optional):
    Select the correct extension type from the dropdown menu:
    * Supplier Agreement Contract: This is for extending the contract for a supplier agreement.
    * Contract Renewal: This is for renewing a contract for a supplier agreement.
    * Contract Revision: This is for revising a contract for a supplier agreement.
    * Contract Cancellation: This is for cancelling a contract for a supplier agreement.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional):
    Enter the date you want the extension to start.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional):
    Enter the date you want the extension to end.
* **Reason For Extension** (`reason_for_extension`, text, optional):
    Provide a clear reason for the extension, such as "To complete the project within the new deadline" or "Due to unforeseen circumstances".
* **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, text, optional):
    Enter the supplier's name.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional):
    Enter the supplier's contact number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, text, optional):
    Enter the supplier's email address.
* **Extension Details** (`extension_details`, text, optional):
    Include any additional details about the extension, such as the reason for the extension or the scope of the extended contract.
