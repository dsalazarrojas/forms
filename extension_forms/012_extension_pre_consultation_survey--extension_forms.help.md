# Extension Pre Consultation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your project goals, budget, scheduling preferences, and schedule. The data collected will help us to better understand your project needs and provide you with more effective assistance in the pre-consultation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each section according to the guidelines provided below.
2. Review the form thoroughly before submitting it to ensure accuracy.
3. Click the "Submit" button to send the form to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Project goals** (`project_goals`, text, optional): Briefly describe your project goals and objectives. This information will help us understand your project's scope and focus.
  * Please provide a concise and clear description of your project's main goals.
  * If you have multiple goals, list them separately.
- **Project budget** (`project_budget`, number, optional): If applicable, provide your estimated project budget. This information will help us better understand your project's financial requirements.
  * If you don't have a budget, simply leave this field blank.
- **Scheduling preferences** (`scheduling_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferred scheduling options for the project.
  * Select all that apply.
  * If none of the options meet your needs, please let us know and we'll work with you to find a suitable alternative.
- **Project schedule** (`project_schedule`, date, optional): If known, provide your estimated project start and end dates. This information will help us schedule the consultation meetings efficiently.
  * If you don't have a schedule in mind, simply leave this field blank.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have reviewed the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
* We will contact you to discuss your project further after receiving your submission.
