# LLC Extension Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The LLC Extension Request Form is used to request an extension for an LLC in [insert context, e.g., tax purposes]. To be eligible for an extension, please ensure that you have a valid reason for the extension and that all required documents are attached.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of your company in the "Company Name" field.
2. Provide the address of your company in the "Company Address" field.
3. Enter the phone number of your company in the "Company Phone" field.
4. Enter the email address of your company in the "Company Email" field.
5. Attach any relevant documents, such as [insert specific document types, e.g., articles of incorporation, tax ID number].
6. Select whether you are authorized or not authorized to access the extension in the "Authorization" field.
7. Choose a deadline for when you would like to submit the extension request in the "Deadline" field.
8. Optionally, select a follow-up date for any additional information or follow-up action in the "Follow Up" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (company_name, text, required): Enter the name of your company as it appears on your tax documents.
* **Company Address** (company_address, text, required): Enter the address where your company is registered or located.
* **Company Phone** (company_phone, text, required): Enter the phone number of your company.
* **Company Email** (company_email, email, required): Enter the email address associated with your company.
* **Document 1** (document_1, text, optional): Attach any relevant documents related to your company, such as articles of incorporation.
* **Document 2** (document_2, text, optional): Attach any additional relevant documents related to your company.
* **Authorization** (authorization, select_one, required): Select whether you are authorized or not authorized to access the extension.
* **Deadline** (deadline, date, optional): Choose a date by which you would like to submit the extension request.
* **Follow Up** (follow_up, date, optional): Select a date for any follow-up action or information related to your extension request.
