# IVD Machine Calibration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about IVD (In Vitro Diagnostic) machine calibration, including the date and frequency of calibration, as well as any comments or observations made during the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the "Calibration Form" page.
2. Fill in the "At" field with the date when the machine was last calibrated.
3. Enter the "Calibration Interval" field with the recommended frequency of calibration (e.g., every 1-6 months).
4. Fill in the "Calibrator" field with the name of the person who performed the calibration.
5. Select the "Calibration Date" field and enter the date of the last calibration.
6. Enter the "Next Calibration Interval" field with the recommended frequency of the next calibration (e.g., every 1-6 months).
7. Fill in the "Comments" field with any comments or observations made during the calibration process.
8. Fill in the "Reviewers Comments" field with any comments or feedback from the reviewer.
9. Fill in the "Form Submitted By" field with the name of the person who submitted the form.
10. Select the "Form Submitted On" field and enter the date the form was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Calibration Form** (`survey_form`, text, optional): This field is the main content area for entering information about the calibration process.
* **At** (`calibrate_at`, date, optional): Enter the date when the machine was last calibrated.
* **Calibration Interval** (`calibration_interval`, number, optional): Enter the recommended frequency of calibration (e.g., every 1-6 months).
* **Calibrator** (`calibrator`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who performed the calibration.
* **Calibration Date** (`calibration_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last calibration.
* **Next Calibration Interval** (`next_calibration_interval`, number, optional): Enter the recommended frequency of the next calibration (e.g., every 1-6 months).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or observations made during the calibration process.
* **Reviewers Comments** (`reviewer_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback from the reviewer.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Form Submitted On** (`form_submitted_on`, date, optional): Enter the date the form was submitted.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the relevant expert or authority.
* Please submit the form as soon as possible after the calibration process is completed.
