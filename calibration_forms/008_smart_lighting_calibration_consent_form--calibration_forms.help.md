# Smart Lighting Calibration Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to provide their personal details for smart lighting calibration services. It is intended to be completed by individuals requesting calibration services for their smart lighting systems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the `First Name` field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the `Last Name` field.
3. Provide your Email address in the `Email` field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the `Phone` field.
5. Fill in your Address in the `Address` field.
6. Enter the name of the City where you reside in the `City` field.
7. Select the Country where you are located from the dropdown list in the `Country` field.
8. Enter the date of your approval for the service in the `Approval Date` field.
9. Enter the time of your approval for the service in the `Approval Time` field.
10. Provide any additional comments or notes about your service request in the `Comments` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your personal first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your personal last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address, e.g., example@example.com.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Fill in your full address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the city where you reside.
* **Country** (`country`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the country where you are located from the dropdown list.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your approval for the service.
* **Approval Time** (`approval_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of your approval for the service.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or notes about your service request.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether you have an assigned tool for the service.
* **Service Provider** (`service_provider`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select whether you have any service provider involved in the service.
