# equipment_calibration_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The equipment_calibration_check_in_form is used to record the status of equipment that needs calibration. This form is used to ensure that all equipment is properly maintained and calibrated to prevent errors and ensure optimal performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the Equipment ID of the device you want to check in.
2. Select the technician who performed or will perform the calibration.
3. Enter the date of the calibration.
4. If necessary, provide any additional remarks about the calibration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equipment ID** (`Equipment ID`, text, required: false): Enter the unique identifier of the equipment that needs to be calibrated.
* **Calibration Date** (`calibration_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when the calibration was done or is scheduled to be done.
* **Technician** (`technician`, select_one, required: false): Select the technician who performed or will perform the calibration.
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, text, required: false): If there is any additional information about the calibration, enter it here.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and correctly.
- Use the correct format for the date (YYYY-MM-DD).
- Ensure that the Equipment ID is correct and matches the equipment's identifier.
- If the calibration is completed, make sure to mark the form as complete and submit it.
- Review the form before submitting it to ensure all information is correct.
