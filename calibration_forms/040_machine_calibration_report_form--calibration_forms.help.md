# Machine Calibration Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Machine Calibration Report Form is designed to collect information about machine calibration events. This form helps maintenance personnel or supervisors to record details about the machine, its condition, and the calibration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Machine Calibration Form** section with the specific details about the machine being calibrated.
2. Provide the **Equipment Id**, **Location**, and **Serial Number** of the machine.
3. Choose the **Calibration Type**, which can be **Calibration**, **Re-Calibration**, or **Maintenance**.
4. Assess the **Equipment Condition** by selecting **Good**, **Fair**, or **Bad**.
5. Enter the **Technician Name** who performed the calibration and provide any additional **Location Details**.
6. Add any **Notes** about the calibration process.
7. Select the **Date Of Calibration** and **Time Of Calibration**.
8. Check the **Form Submitted** checkbox to indicate that the form has been completed.
9. Finally, enter your **Submitted By** name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Machine Calibration Form** (text): This section is the main part of the form where you will provide specific details about the machine being calibrated.
* **Equipment Id** (text): Enter the unique identifier for the machine.
* **Location** (text): Specify the location where the machine is being calibrated.
* **Calibration Type** (select one): Choose the type of calibration event, which can be **Calibration**, **Re-Calibration**, or **Maintenance**.
* **Serial Number** (text): Enter the serial number of the machine.
* **Model Number** (text): Enter the model number of the machine.
* **Equipment Condition** (select one): Assess the condition of the machine as **Good**, **Fair**, or **Bad**.
* **Technician Name** (text): Enter the name of the technician who performed the calibration.
* **Location Details** (text): Provide any additional details about the location where the calibration took place.
* **Notes** (note): Add any notes or comments about the calibration process.
* **Date Of Calibration** (date): Enter the date of the calibration event.
* **Time Of Calibration** (time): Enter the time of the calibration event.
* **Form Submitted** (select one): Check this checkbox to indicate that the form has been completed.
* **Submitted By** (text): Enter your name to confirm that you submitted the form.

## Tips
Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that the calibration data is reliable and useful for future reference. If you're unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or maintenance personnel for guidance.
