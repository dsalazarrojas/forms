# Equipment Calibration Appointment Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equipment Calibration Appointment Request form is used to schedule a calibration appointment for your equipment. Please fill out this form with the necessary information to ensure a smooth and efficient calibration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide equipment information (Equipment ID, Equipment Name, Model, Serial Number, etc.)
2. Choose the preferred dates and times for the calibration
3. Select the location for the calibration (onsite or offsite)
4. Enter contact information (name, email, phone number)
5. Specify the service provider(s) required for the calibration
6. Add any additional notes or location details

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment Information** (`equipment_info`): Enter the equipment's ID, name, model, and serial number.
* **Preferred Dates** (`preferred_dates`): Choose the preferred dates for the calibration. Please select one or more dates from the list.
* **Locations** (`locations2`): Select the location where the calibration will take place. Choose between onsite or offsite.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): Enter the contact name, email, and phone number of the person responsible for the equipment.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): Verify the contact information entered earlier.
* **Equipment Details** (`equipment_details`): Enter any additional details about the equipment.
* **Preferred Dates2** (`preferred_dates2`): This field is intended for selecting the preferred dates for the calibration, but it's currently not functional (use the `preferred_dates` field instead).
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_times`): This field is intended for selecting the preferred time for the calibration, but it's not used in this form (use `preferred_dates` to select the date and time).
* **Locations2** (`locations2`): Select the location where the calibration will take place. This field is similar to the `locations` field.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter the contact phone number of the person responsible for the equipment.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter the contact email of the person responsible for the equipment.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter the name of the person responsible for the equipment.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): This field is intended to be verified contact information, but it seems to be redundant (use `contact_info` from the previous section).
* **Location Details** (`location_details`): Enter any additional location details that are relevant to the calibration.
* **Service Provider** (`service_provider`): Select the service provider required for the calibration.
* **Equipment ID** (`equipment_id`): Select the Equipment ID for the calibration.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional notes or comments about the calibration.
* **Date** (`date`): This field is not functional (use the `preferred_dates` field instead).
* **Time** (`time`): This field is not functional (use the `preferred_dates` field instead).
* **Location** (`location`): This field is intended for entering a location, but it's not used in this form (use `locations2` instead).
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`): Enter the type of equipment that needs to be calibrated.
* **Equipment Subtype** (`equipment_subtype`): Enter the subtype of the equipment.
* **Equipment Name** (`equipment_name`): Enter the name of the equipment.
* **Equipment Model** (`equipment_model`): Enter the model of the equipment.
* **Equipment Serial Number** (`equipment_serial_number`): Enter the serial number of the equipment.
