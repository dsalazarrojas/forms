# Calibration And Weight Check Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record and document the calibration of measuring devices, such as scales, balances, and other equipment. It helps ensure that the equipment is functioning correctly and meets the required standards.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter the date and time of the calibration.
3. Enter the name and ID of the technician performing the calibration.
4. Select the department or area where the calibration was performed.
5. Enter the name of the equipment being calibrated.
6. Enter the equipment ID or asset number.
7. Select the type of equipment being calibrated.
8. Enter the calibration standard used.
9. Enter the test weights used.
10. Record the results of the zero, span, and linearity tests.
11. Enter any adjustments made to the equipment.
12. Select the next calibration due date.
13. Select the calibration interval (e.g., monthly, quarterly, semi-annually, annually, as needed).
14. Evaluate the overall condition of the equipment (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Needs replacement).
15. Enter any recommendations for the equipment.
16. Sign and date the form with your technician's signature.
17. Enter any additional notes about the calibration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Technician Name** (`technician_name`): Enter the name of the person performing the calibration.
- **Technician ID** (`technician_id`): Enter the employee or technician identification number.
- **Calibration Date** (`calibration_date`): Enter the date of the calibration in MM/DD/YY format.
- **Calibration Time** (`calibration_time`): Enter the time of the calibration in 12-hour format (e.g., 08:00 AM).
- **Location** (`location`): Select the department or area where the calibration was performed.
- **Equipment Name** (`equipment_name`): Enter the name of the equipment being calibrated.
- **Equipment ID** (`equipment_id`): Enter the asset or serial number of the equipment.
- **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`): Select the type of equipment being calibrated (e.g., scale, balance, load cell).
- **Manufacturer** (`manufacturer`): Enter the equipment manufacturer.
- **Model Number** (`model_number`): Enter the equipment model number.
- **Serial Number** (`serial_number`): Enter the equipment serial number.
- **Capacity** (`capacity`): Enter the maximum capacity or range of the equipment.
- **Calibration Standard Used** (`calibration_standard_used`): Enter the reference standard used for calibration.
- **Standard Certificate Number** (`standard_certificate_number`): Enter the certificate number for the reference standard.
- **Standard Due Date** (`standard_due_date`): Enter the date when the standard is due for recalibration.
- **Test Weights Used** (`test_weights_used`): Enter the weights used for testing.
- **Zero Test** (`zero_test`): Select whether the equipment read zero correctly (Pass, Fail, Not applicable).
- **Span Test** (`span_test`): Select whether the equipment read span correctly (Pass, Fail, Not applicable).
- **Linearity Test** (`linearity_test`): Select whether the equipment passed linearity check (Pass, Fail, Not performed).
- **Readings Taken** (`readings_taken`): Document any test readings.
- **Tolerance Met** (`tolerance_met`): Select whether all readings were within tolerance (Yes, No, Marginal).
- **Calibration Result** (`calibration_result`): Select the overall result of the calibration (Pass - calibrated, Pass - as found, Fail - adjusted, Fail - out of tolerance, Fail - damaged).
- **Adjustments Made** (`adjustments_made`): Enter any adjustments made to the equipment.
- **Next Calibration Date** (`next_calibration_date`): Enter the next calibration due date.
- **Calibration Interval** (`calibration_interval`): Select the calibration interval (e.g., monthly, quarterly, semi-annually, annually, as needed).
- **Equipment Condition** (`equipment_condition`): Select the overall condition of the equipment (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Needs replacement).
- **Recommendations** (`recommendations`): Enter any recommendations for the equipment.
- **Technician Signature** (`technician_signature`): Sign and date with your technician's signature.
- **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`): Enter any other information about the calibration.
