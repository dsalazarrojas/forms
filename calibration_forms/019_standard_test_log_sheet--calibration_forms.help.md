# Standard Test Log Sheet - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for recording and tracking the results of standard tests. It captures key information about each test, including the test date, type, item, and results. This data is crucial for tracking progress and making informed decisions. Ensure you complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure effective test log management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Start by filling in the `Test Date` field, which should reflect the date of the test.
*   Next, select the `Test Type` from the available options.
*   Describe the `Test Item` being tested, providing relevant details.
*   For each test, select the corresponding `Test Result`(s) from the available options.
*   If an attachment is uploaded, provide the `Attachment Url`.
*   Enter the `Review Status`, selecting either `Option 1` or `Option 2`.
*   Enter the name and details of the person `Assigned To`.
*   Provide the date and details of the person `Review By`.
*   Enter any `Comments` regarding the test.
*   If an attachment is uploaded, enter its `Attachment Name`, `Attachment Type`, and provide the `Attachment Size` in bytes.
*   Finally, ensure you have entered the `Attachment Url`, `Attachment Comment`, and `Attachment Name`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   *Test Date* (`test_date`): The date of the test. Enter the date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
*   *Test Type* (`test_type`): The type of test being performed. Select from the available options.
*   *Test Item* (`test_item`): A description of the item being tested. Provide relevant details.
*   *Test Result* (`test_result`): The outcome of the test. Select the corresponding result from the available options.
*   *Test Result 2* (`test_result2`): Additional test results. Select multiple options if applicable.
*   *Notes* (`notes`): Any additional comments regarding the test.
*   *Email* (`email`): The email address of the person performing the test.
*   *Phone* (`phone`): The phone number of the person performing the test.
*   *Assigned To* (`assigned_to`): The name of the person or team assigned to the test.
*   *Review Status* (`review_status`): The status of the review. Select from the available options.
*   *Review Date* (`review_date`): The date of the review.
*   *Review By* (`review_by`): The name of the person performing the review.
*   *Comments* (`comments`): Any comments regarding the test.
*   *Attachment* (`attachment`): The attachment uploaded for the test.
*   *Attachment Name* (`attachment_name`): The name of the attachment.
*   *Attachment Type* (`attachment_type`): The type of attachment.
*   *Attachment Url* (`attachment_url`): The URL of the attachment.
*   *Attachment Size* (`attachment_size`): The size of the attachment in bytes.
*   *Attachment Comment* (`attachment_comment`): A comment regarding the attachment.
*   *Test Result 3* (`test_result_3`): The third test result.
*   *Test Result 4* (`test_result_4`): The fourth test result.
*   *Test Result 5* (`test_result_5`): A field for notes.
*   *Test Result 6* (`test_result_6`): A field for text input.
*   *Test Result 7* (`test_result_7`): A field for a number value.
*   *Test Result 8* (`test_result_8`): Another field for a number value.

This guide is designed to provide a clear understanding of each field and how to complete the form accurately. If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
