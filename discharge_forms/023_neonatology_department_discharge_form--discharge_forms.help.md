# Neonatology Department Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Neonatology Department Discharge Form is used to document patient information and provide necessary details for follow-up care after discharge from the department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all necessary patient information, including their contact details and care instructions.
2.  Fill out each field with the required information. Note that fields marked as "required" must have a valid answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the patient's name to identify them in the department's records.
*  **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date`, `date`, required/optional): Record the date the patient was discharged from the department.
*  **Care Instructions** (`care_instructions`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any necessary care instructions or advice for the patient's care provider.
*  **Follow Up Visit** (`follow_up_visit`, `date`, required/optional): Schedule a follow-up visit for the patient in the specified date and time.
*  **Next Provider Visit** (`next_provider_visit`, `date`, required/optional): Record the date and time of the patient's next visit with their healthcare provider.
*  **Follow Up Phone** (`follow_up_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the follow-up phone details, including date and time.
*  **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Record the patient's phone number.
*  **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the patient's email address.
*  **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, `text`, required/optional): Record the patient's address.

## Tips
*   Double-check all information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
*   Verify that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
*   If you need help or clarification on any field, contact your department's administration.
