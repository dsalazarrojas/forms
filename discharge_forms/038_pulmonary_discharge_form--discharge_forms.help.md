# Pulmonary Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the details of a patient's discharge from a healthcare facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "patient_info" field with the patient's information.
2. Enter the date of discharge in the "discharge_date" field.
3. Specify the planned discharge time in the "planned_discharge_time" field.
4. Select the discharge status from the "discharge_status" field.
5. Provide follow-up instructions in the "follow_up_instructions" field.
6. Enter the follow-up phone number in the "follow_up_phone" field.
7. Provide the patient's contact information in the "patient_contact_info" field.
8. Enter the hospital location in the "hospital_location" field.
9. Enter the follow-up doctor's information in the "follow_up_doctor_info" field.
10. Enter the follow-up clinic information in the "follow_up_clinic_info" field.
11. List any patient allergies in the "patient_allergies" field.
12. List the patient's medications in the "patient_medications" field.
13. Provide the patient's medical history in the "patient_medical_history" field.
14. Provide the patient's family history in the "patient_family_history" field.
15. Provide the patient's social history in the "patient_social_history" field.
16. Enter the patient's mental status in the "patient_mental_status" field.
17. Enter the patient's occupation in the "patient_occupation" field.
18. Enter the patient's educational level in the "patient_education" field.
19. Enter the patient's income in the "patient_income" field.
20. Enter the caregiver's name in the "patient_caregiver_name" field.
21. Specify the relationship between the patient and caregiver in the "patient_caregiver_relationship" field.
22. Enter the caregiver's phone number in the "patient_caregiver_phone" field.
23. Enter the hospital staff member's name in the "hospital_staff_name" field.
24. Enter the hospital staff member's phone number in the "hospital_staff_phone" field.
25. Enter the follow-up date in the "follow_up_date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_info** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter the patient's basic information such as name, date of birth, and address.
* **discharge_date** (`date`, `required: false`): Enter the date of discharge.
* **planned_discharge_time** (`time`, `required: false`): Specify the planned discharge time.
* **discharge_status** (`select_one`, `required: false`): Select the discharge status (e.g., "Hospital", "Clinic", "Both").
* **follow_up_instructions** (`text`, `required: false`): Provide follow-up instructions for the patient.
* **follow_up_phone** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter the follow-up phone number.
* **patient_contact_info** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter the patient's contact information.
* **hospital_location** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter the hospital location.
* **follow_up_doctor_info** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter the follow-up doctor's information.
* **follow_up_clinic_info** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter the follow-up clinic information.
* **patient_allergies** (`text`, `required: false`): List any patient allergies.
* **patient_medications** (`text`, `required: false`): List the patient's medications.
* **patient_medical_history** (`text`, `required: false`): Provide the patient's medical history.
* **patient_family_history** (`text`, `required: false`): Provide the patient's family history.
* **patient_social_history** (`text`, `required: false`): Provide the patient's social history.
* **patient_mental_status** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter the patient's mental status.
* **patient_occupation** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter the patient's occupation.
* **patient_education** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter the patient's educational level.
* **patient_income** (`number`, `required: false`): Enter the patient's income.
* **patient_caregiver_name** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter the caregiver's name.
* **patient_caregiver_relationship** (`text`, `required: false`): Specify the relationship between the patient and caregiver.
* **patient_caregiver_phone** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter the caregiver's phone number.
* **hospital_staff_name** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter the hospital staff member's name.
* **hospital_staff_phone** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter the hospital staff member's phone number.
* **follow_up_date** (`date`, `required: false`): Enter the follow-up date.
