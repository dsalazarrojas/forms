# HIV/AIDS Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HIV/AIDS Discharge Form is a document used to document the discharge process of patients with HIV/AIDS from a medical facility or hospital. This form collects essential information about the patient's condition, medical history, and care instructions to ensure a smooth transition between care settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the **Patient Name** field.
2. If applicable, provide a brief medical history in the **Medical History** field.
3. Enter the patient's contact number in the **Contact Number** field.
4. Select the patient's contact address from the provided options in the **Contact Address** field.
5. Enter the discharge date in the **Discharge Date** field.
6. Provide instructions for the patient's care in the **Care Instructions** field.
7. Enter the follow-up date in the **Follow-up Date** field.
8. List any medications the patient is currently taking in the **Medication List** field.
9. Describe the patient's current condition in the **Patient Condition** field.
10. Enter the date of the next contact with the patient in the **Next Contact** field.
11. Enter the medical facility where the patient was treated in the **Medical Facility** field.
12. Enter the name of the patient's doctor in the **Doctor Name** field.
13. Enter the doctor's phone number in the **Doctor Phone** field.
14. Enter the address of the doctor's medical facility in the **Doctor Address** field.
15. Select the patient's current status from the provided options in the **Patient Status** field.
16. Enter the name of the doctor's facility in the **Doctor Facility** field.
17. Select the contacts to follow up with the patient from the provided options in the **Follow-up Contact** field.
18. Enter the medication dosage for any prescribed medications in the **Medication Dosage** field.
19. Enter the patient's ID number in the **Patient ID** field.
20. Select the reason for the patient's discharge from the provided options in the **Discharge Reason** field.
21. Enter any additional medical notes in the **Medical Notes** field.
22. Enter the doctor's specialty in the **Doctor Specialty** field.
23. Enter the discharge date and time in the **Discharge Date/Time** field.
24. Enter any notes from the doctor in the **Doctor Notes** field.
25. Provide instructions for the patient's follow-up in the **Follow-up Instructions** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Provide a brief medical history of the patient, if applicable.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the patient's contact number.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, select_one, optional): Select the patient's contact address from the provided options.
* **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the patient's discharge.
* **Care Instructions** (`care_instructions`, text, required): Provide instructions for the patient's care.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the next follow-up with the patient.
* **Medication List** (`medication_list`, text, required): List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Patient Condition** (`patient_condition`, text, optional): Describe the patient's current condition.
* **Next Contact** (`next_contact`, date, optional): Enter the date of the next contact with the patient.
* **Medical Facility** (`medical_facility`, text, optional): Enter the name of the medical facility where the patient was treated.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the patient's doctor.
* **Doctor Phone** (`doctor_phone`, text, optional): Enter the doctor's phone number.
* **Doctor Address** (`doctor_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the doctor's medical facility.
* **Patient Status** (`patient_status`, select_one, optional): Select the patient's current status from the provided options.
* **Doctor Facility** (`doctor_facility`, text, optional): Enter the name of the doctor's facility.
* **Follow-up Contact** (`follow_up_contact`, select_multiple, optional): Select the contacts to follow up with the patient from the provided options.
* **Medication Dosage** (`medication_dosage`, number, optional): Enter the medication dosage for any prescribed medications.
* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, number, required): Enter the patient's ID number.
* **Discharge Reason** (`discharge_reason`, select_one, optional): Select the reason for the patient's discharge from the provided options.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, text, required): Enter any additional medical notes.
* **Doctor Specialty** (`doctor_specialty`, text, required): Enter the doctor's specialty.
* **Discharge Date/Time** (`discharge_date_time`, time, optional): Enter the discharge date and time.
* **Doctor Notes** (`doctor_notes`, text, required): Enter any notes from the doctor.
* **Follow-up Instructions** (`follow_up_instructions`, text, required): Provide instructions for the patient's follow-up.
