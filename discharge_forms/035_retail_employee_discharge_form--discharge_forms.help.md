# Retail Employee Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Retail Employee Discharge Form" is used to document the details of an employee's termination or discharge from the company. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure proper documentation and compliance with company policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and supporting documents before starting.
2. Fill in the employee's details (employee ID, employee name, etc.).
3. Choose the correct location for the discharge (if applicable).
4. Complete the date and time of discharge.
5. Select the reasons for discharge (as applicable).
6. Obtain the manager's signature, name, phone number, and email address.
7. Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and consistently.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `number`, **required**):
    An employee's ID number.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, **required**): The employee's full name.
* **Reason For Discharge** (`reason_for_discharge`, `text`, **optional**):
    A clear and concise description of the reason for the employee's discharge.
* **Date Of Discharge** (`date_of_discharge`, `date`, **optional**):
    The date on which the employee was discharged.
* **Time Of Discharge** (`time_of_discharge`, `time`, **optional**):
    The time at which the employee was discharged.
* **Location** (`location`, `select_multiple`, **optional**):
    A list of locations where the employee was discharged (e.g., store, warehouse, etc.).
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, `text`, **optional**):
    The manager's signature, confirming they reviewed and approved the form.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, **optional**):
    The name of the manager who reviewed and approved the form.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, `text`, **optional**):
    The phone number of the manager who reviewed and approved the form.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, `email`, **optional**):
    The email address of the manager who reviewed and approved the form.
* **Reason For Discharge 2** (`reason_for_discharge_2`, `text`, **optional**):
    A secondary reason for the employee's discharge (if applicable).
* **Date Of Discharge 2** (`date_of_discharge_2`, `date`, **optional**):
    The date on which the employee was discharged (if a second discharge is applicable).
* **Time Of Discharge 2** (`time_of_discharge_2`, `time`, **optional**):
    The time at which the employee was discharged (if a second discharge is applicable).
* **Location 2** (`location_2`, `select_multiple`, **optional**):
    A list of locations where the employee was discharged (if a second discharge is applicable).
* **Reason For Discharge 3** (`reason_for_discharge_3`, `text`, **optional**):
    A third reason for the employee's discharge (if applicable).

Note: The fields marked as "optional" are not required but are included for completeness and additional documentation.
