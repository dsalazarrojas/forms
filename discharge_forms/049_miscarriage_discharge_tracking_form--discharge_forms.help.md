# Miscarriage Discharge Tracking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to track the discharge status of a patient's miscarriage case. It helps healthcare providers to keep a record of the patient's discharge date, follow-up plan, and other essential details. This form is an essential tool for managing patient care and ensuring continuity of treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information in the "patient_info" field. This is a text field where you can type in the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant details.
2. Select the discharge date from the calendar picker or enter it manually in the "discharge_date" field. This is an optional field, but it's useful for tracking the date of discharge.
3. In the "follow_up_plan" field, describe the plan for the patient's follow-up care. This can include any instructions or recommendations for the patient's next steps.
4. In the "patient_contact" field, provide the patient's contact information. This can include their phone number, email, or any other way to reach them.
5. In the "doctor_contact" field, provide your contact information as the healthcare provider. This will help the patient contact you with any questions or concerns.
6. In the "discharge_status" field, select the status of the patient's case. This is a required field and should be marked as "Active" or "Inactive".
7. In the "follow_up_frequency" field, select whether the patient will require follow-up care. This is an optional field, but it helps in scheduling follow-up appointments.
8. If the patient will require follow-up care, enter the next follow-up date in the "next_follow_up_date" field. This is an optional field, but it's useful for planning follow-up appointments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_info** (`<text>` , **Required**): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant details.
* **discharge_date** (`<date>` , **Optional**): Enter the discharge date of the patient or select it from the calendar picker.
* **follow_up_plan** (`<text>` , **Optional**): Describe the plan for the patient's follow-up care.
* **patient_contact** (`<text>` , **Optional**): Enter the patient's contact information, such as their phone number or email.
* **doctor_contact** (`<text>` , **Optional**): Enter your contact information as the healthcare provider.
* **discharge_status** (`<select_one>` , **Required**): Select the status of the patient's case, either "Active" or "Inactive".
* **follow_up_frequency** (`<select_one>` , **Optional**): Select whether the patient will require follow-up care, either "Yes" or "No".
* **next_follow_up_date** (`<date>` , **Optional**): Enter the next follow-up date if the patient requires follow-up care.
