# Mortgage Discharge Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document the details of a mortgage being discharged. It captures information about the property owner, mortgage details, and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, paying special attention to the fields that are marked as required.
2. Select the correct options for each field based on the information you have about the mortgage being discharged.
3. If you're unsure about any of the fields, consult with the relevant parties before making any entries.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Owner** (`property_owner`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and contact details of the property owner or their representative.
* **property_address** (`property_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of the property being mortgaged.
* **Mortgage Details** (`mortgage_details`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select 'Yes' if the mortgage is being paid off or discharged. You may select multiple options.
* **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the mortgage is being discharged.
* **Mortgage Holder** (`mortgage_holder`, `select_one`, optional): Select the holder of the mortgage from the provided options.
* **Amount Paid** (`amount_paid`, `number`, optional): Enter the total amount paid to discharge the mortgage.
* **Balance** (`balance`, `number`, optional): Enter the balance of the mortgage being discharged (if applicable).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the method of payment used to discharge the mortgage. You may select multiple options.
* **Signed By** (`signed_by`, `select_one`, optional): Select the person signing the form from the provided options.
* **Discharge Reason** (`discharge_reason`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the reason for the mortgage being discharged. You may select multiple options.

## Tips

* Be sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that all necessary information is documented.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, consult with the relevant parties before making any entries.
* This form should only be used for mortgage discharges and not for other types of forms or documents.
