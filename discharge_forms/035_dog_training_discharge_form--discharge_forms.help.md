# Dog Training Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dog Training Discharge Form is used to document the discharge of a dog from its training program. This form captures essential information about the dog's status and condition, ensuring accuracy and consistency in recording.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Trainer's Name.
2. Enter the Owner's Name.
3. Enter the Trainer's License Number.
4. Optional: Enter the Dog ID, Dog Weight, and Training Duration.
5. Enter the Trainer's Notes and Owner's Notes (optional).
6. Enter the Discharge Status (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).
7. Select the Dog Condition (Healthy, Injured, or Diseased).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trainer Name** (Trainer Name, text, required): Enter the name of the trainer who is discharging the dog.
* **Owner Name** (Owner Name, text, required): Enter the name of the dog's owner or the person responsible for the dog's care.
* **Trainer License Number** (Trainer License Number, text, required): Enter the trainer's license number for identification purposes.
* **Dog ID** (Dog ID, number, optional): Enter the dog's ID number if available.
* **Dog Weight** (Dog Weight, number, optional): Enter the dog's weight in kilograms or pounds.
* **Trainer Notes** (Trainer Notes, text, optional): Enter any additional notes from the trainer about the dog's training progress or condition.
* **Owner Notes** (Owner Notes, text, optional): Enter any additional notes from the owner about the dog's condition or care.
* **Training Duration** (Training Duration, time, optional): Enter the duration of the dog's training program.
* **Date Of Discharge** (Date Of Discharge, date, optional): Enter the date of the dog's discharge from the training program.
* **Discharge Status** (Discharge Status, select_one, required): Select the status of the dog's discharge (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).
* **Dog Condition** (Dog Condition, select_multiple, required): Select one or more conditions of the dog's health status (Healthy, Injured, or Diseased).

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Enter the correct Dog ID if available.
* If the dog's condition is unknown, select all applicable conditions for accurate representation.
* Save and submit the form after completion to ensure that the dog's discharge is recorded accurately.
