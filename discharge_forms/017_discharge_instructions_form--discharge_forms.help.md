# Discharge Instructions Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form serves as a tool for healthcare providers to document and share essential information with patients about their care and follow-up instructions after discharge from the hospital.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the patient's personal details, such as their name and contact information.
2. Record the date of discharge and follow-up appointment, including the date and time.
3. Provide information about any medication the patient is taking, including the dosage and frequency.
4. Document any side effects of the medication and the number of refills available.
5. Specify the follow-up details, including the date, time, and location of the appointment.
6. Add any additional follow-up notes or comments.
7. Finally, obtain the patient's signature to confirm they have received and understand the discharge instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date of Discharge** (`date_of_discharge`, date, optional): Enter the date of discharge from the hospital.
* **Date of Follow Up** (`date_of_follow_up`, date, optional): Enter the date and time of the follow-up appointment.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, optional): List the medication(s) the patient is currently taking.
* **Medication Dosage** (`medication_dosage`, text, optional): Specify the dosage of the medication(s).
* **Medication Frequency** (`medication_frequency`, number, optional): Indicate how often the medication is taken (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
* **Medication Side Effects** (`medication_side_effects`, text, optional): Record any known side effects of the medication.
* **Medication Refills** (`medication_refills`, number, optional): Specify the number of refills available for the medication.
* **Follow Up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the follow-up appointment.
* **Follow Up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, optional): Specify the time of the follow-up appointment.
* **Follow Up Location** (`follow_up_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the follow-up appointment.
* **Follow Up Note** (`follow_up_note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the follow-up appointment.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, optional): Obtain the patient's signature to confirm they have received and understand the discharge instructions.
* **Patient Phone Number** (`patient_phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, optional): Enter the patient's address.
* **Patient City** (`patient_city`, text, optional): Enter the patient's city.
* **Patient State** (`patient_state`, text, optional): Enter the patient's state.
* **Patient Zip** (`patient_zip`, text, optional): Enter the patient's zip code.
* **Physician Name** (`physician_name`, text, optional): Enter the physician's name.
* **Physician Title** (`physician_title`, text, optional): Enter the physician's title.
* **Physician Phone Number** (`physician_phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the physician's phone number.
* **Physician Email** (`physician_email`, email, optional): Enter the physician's email address.
* **Hospital Name** (`hospital_name`, text, optional): Enter the hospital's name.
* **Hospital Contact** (`hospital_contact`, text, optional): Enter the hospital's contact information.

Note: The fields marked as "optional" can be left blank if not applicable.
