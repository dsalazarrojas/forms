# Neurosurgery Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Neurosurgery Discharge Form is used to gather essential information for the discharge process of patients undergoing neurosurgical procedures. This form helps ensure that all necessary details are documented and communicated effectively between medical staff and patients.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form carefully, providing accurate and complete information for each field.
2. Make sure to review the form before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
3. If you are unsure about any field or need clarification, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Details (page 1)** (`patient_details`, text, required): This section is used to gather basic patient information, such as name, date of birth, and contact details.
* **Neurosurgeon Comments (page 1)** (`neurosurgeon_comments`, text, required): Here, you can enter any comments or notes from the neurosurgeon regarding the patient's condition, treatment, or any concerns.
* **Patient Comments (page 1)** (`patient_comments`, text, required): Please enter any comments or concerns you have as the patient or their representative.
* **Hospital Staff (page 1)** (`hospital_staff`, select_one, required): Please select the hospital staff member who is responsible for the patient's care.
* **Date of Discharge (page 2)** (`date_of_discharge`, date, required): Enter the date of the patient's discharge.
* **Time of Discharge (page 2)** (`time_of_discharge`, time, required): Enter the time of the patient's discharge.
* **Medications (page 2)** (`medications`, text, required): List any medications the patient is taking or has been prescribed during their stay.
* **Instructions (page 2)** (`instructions`, text, required): Enter any specific instructions for the patient to follow during their recovery.
* **Follow-up Appointments (page 2)** (`follow_up_appointments`, number, required): Enter the number of follow-up appointments scheduled for the patient.
* **Follow-up Appointments Description (page 2)** (`follow_up_appointments_description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the follow-up appointments, including any necessary details or instructions.

## Tips

* Please double-check the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
* This form is an essential part of the discharge process, so please complete it carefully to ensure a smooth transition for the patient.
