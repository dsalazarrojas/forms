# wellness_center_client_discharge_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The wellness center client discharge form is a document used to record and verify important information during the discharge process of a client at a wellness center. This form helps ensure a thorough and organized discharge, reducing the risk of miscommunication and errors.

## How To Complete This Form

### General Information (Page 1)
Complete the General Information section by providing the client's basic details. This includes any relevant information about the client's stay at the wellness center.

### Discharge Details (Page 2)
In the Discharge Details section, provide a detailed description of the client's discharge process. This includes any important notes or instructions for the client's next steps.

### Discharge Plan (Page 3)
Here, describe the client's discharge plan in a clear and concise manner. This will serve as a guide for the client's post-discharge care.

### Follow Up Information (Page 4)
In the Follow Up Information section, note any necessary follow-up actions or appointments the client may need to attend.

### Medications (Page 5)
List any medications the client is currently taking or needs to take after discharge.

### Allergies (Page 6)
Record any allergies the client may have.

### Health History (Page 7)
Provide a brief overview of the client's health history, including any relevant medical conditions or procedures.

### Next Steps (Page 8)
Document the client's next steps after discharge.

### Signature (Page 9)
Include the client's signature to acknowledge their understanding of the discharge process and plan.

### Medical Contact (Page 10)
Record any necessary contact information for the client's medical contact person or representative.

## Tips

*   Ensure all fields are filled out carefully and accurately to avoid any miscommunication or errors.
*   Use this form for client discharge at a wellness center only.
*   Review the form thoroughly before using it to ensure you're aware of its purpose and fields.
