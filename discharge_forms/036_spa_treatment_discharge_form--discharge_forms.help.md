# Spa Treatment Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spa Treatment Discharge Form is used to ensure that clients receive all necessary information about their treatment and are formally discharged at the end of their visit. This form is typically used for clients who are receiving a specific treatment and need to be informed and acknowledged before leaving the spa.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully to ensure you understand what information is being requested.
2. Fill in your information in the relevant fields:
	* Client Name: Enter your full name.
	* Client Phone Number: Enter your phone number.
	* Treatment ID: Enter the ID number assigned to your treatment.
	* Treatment Start Date: Enter the date when your treatment started.
	* Treatment End Date: Enter the date when your treatment ended.
	* Notes: Add any additional comments or details about your experience.
	* Acknowledgement: Select one of the options to acknowledge that you have received all the necessary information.
	* Discharge Date: Enter the date when your treatment was completed.
	* Client Signature: Sign your name to confirm your acknowledgement.
	* Treatment Notes: Add any additional comments or details about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your full name to complete this field.
* **Client Phone Number** (`client_phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you after your treatment.
* **Treatment ID** (`treatment_id`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the ID number assigned to your treatment to identify it uniquely.
* **Treatment Start Date** (`treatment_start_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date when your treatment started.
* **Treatment End Date** (`treatment_end_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date when your treatment ended.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or details about your experience.
* **Acknowledgement** (`acknowledgement`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the options to acknowledge that you have received all the necessary information.
* **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the date when your treatment was completed.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `text`, required/optional): Sign your name to confirm your acknowledgement.
* **Treatment Notes** (`treatment_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or details about your experience.

## Tips
* Please make sure to review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask your treatment provider.
* This form is designed to ensure your safety and well-being after your treatment. Please take a few minutes to review it carefully.
