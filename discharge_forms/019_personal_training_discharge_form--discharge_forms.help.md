# Personal Training Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Training Discharge Form is used to document the client's disconnection from a personal training program. This form ensures all necessary details are captured and helps trainers close the client's program effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Gather the client's information, such as contact details and workout history.
2. Provide the program details, including the client's progress and any changes made.
3. Collect the client's workout log, which should contain a record of their workouts.
4. Note the next steps for the client, outlining their future progress and follow-up requirements.
5. Capture the discharge notes, which should include any important details about the discharge process.
6. Sign off with your trainer ID.
7. Obtain the client's signature, confirming they have reviewed and agree with the form details.
8. Record the current date for the discharge date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Info (1)**:
	+ Enter the client's name, phone number, and other relevant contact information.
* **Trainer Info (2)**:
	+ Provide your trainer ID and any other relevant contact details.
* **Program Details (3)**:
	+ Describe the client's progress and any changes made to their program.
* **Workout Log (4)**:
	+ Enter a detailed record of the client's workouts, including dates and exercises completed.
* **Next Steps (5)**:
	+ Outline the client's future progress and any follow-up requirements.
* **Discharge Notes (6)**:
	+ Include any important details about the discharge process, such as reasons for discharge or recommendations for future progress.
* **Trainer Signoff (7)**:
	+ Enter your trainer ID, confirming you have reviewed and approved the discharge details.
* **Client Signature (8)**:
	+ Obtain the client's signature, confirming they have reviewed and agree with the form details.
* **Trainer Signature (9)**:
	+ Enter your signature, confirming you have witnessed the client's signature and agree with the form details.
* **Date (10)**:
	+ Record the current date for the discharge date.

## Tips
- Always review the form carefully before submitting it to the client.
- Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- Store the completed form as a record of the client's discharge process.
