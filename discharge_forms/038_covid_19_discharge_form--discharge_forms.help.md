### COVID 19 Discharge Form - Help Guide

### Purpose
The COVID-19 Discharge Form is used to document the patient's discharge from hospital care, ensuring that all necessary information is collected to provide proper care and follow-up.

### How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure that the patient's discharge date is correctly entered in the **Discharge Date** field.
2. Enter the patient's **Patient Name**, which is their full legal name.
3. Provide the patient's **Date of Birth** in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter the patient's **Patient ID/Medical Record Number** for easy reference.
5. Specify the **Hospital/Facility Name** where the patient was treated.
6. Enter the **Admission Date** when the patient was initially admitted.
7. Select whether the patient was **Admitted to ICU?** ("Yes" or "No").
8. If the patient was admitted to ICU, enter the **Number of Days in ICU**.
9. Select whether **Mechanical Ventilation Required?** ("Yes" or "No").
10. If mechanical ventilation was required, enter the **Duration of Mechanical Ventilation**.
11. Choose the **Discharge Disposition**, indicating where the patient is being discharged to (e.g., Home, Skilled Nursing Facility, etc.).
12. Select whether the patient is **Cleared to Discontinue Isolation?** ("Yes", "No", or "With Precautions").
13. If necessary, describe any **Isolation Instructions** for the patient.
14. Confirm if **Discharge Instructions Provided?** ("Yes", "No", or "Verbal only").
15. List the **Medications Prescribed at Discharge** for the patient.
16. Specify the **Medical Equipment Needed at Home**, such as oxygen or a nebulizer.
17. Choose whether a **Follow-up Care Appointment Scheduled?** ("Yes", "No", or "Patient will schedule").
18. Enter the name of the **Follow-up Provider**.
19. If applicable, enter the **Recommended Follow-up Date**.
20. Select whether a **Home Health Referral was Provided?** ("Yes", "No", or "Patient declined").
21. If applicable, enter the name of the **Home Health Agency**.
22. Describe any **Warning Signs to Seek Emergency Care** for the patient.
23. Explain **Physical Activity Restrictions**, if any, for the patient.
24. Select whether **Caregiver Support is Arranged?** ("Yes", "No", or "In progress").
25. Choose whether **Mental Health or Counseling Referral?** ("Yes", "No", or "Patient declined").
26. Enter the name of the **Discharging Physician**.

### Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
* If a field is optional, include it if possible, but it is not mandatory.
* Review each field carefully to ensure that all necessary information is collected.

By following this guide, you will be able to complete the COVID-19 Discharge Form accurately and ensure that all necessary information is collected to provide proper care and follow-up for the patient.
