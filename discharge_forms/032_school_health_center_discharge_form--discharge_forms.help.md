# School Health Center Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Health Center Discharge Form is used to document the discharge process for students visiting the school health center, ensuring their safe and healthy return to school activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the student's name and student ID number.
2. Provide the date and time of the health center visit.
3. Select the reason for the visit from the provided options.
4. Enter any specific discharge instructions provided to the student.
5. Ensure that the student, parent, and healthcare provider have all acknowledged and understood the discharge instructions by selecting the corresponding options.
6. Sign and date the form where indicated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Name** (Student Name): Enter the student's full name as it appears on their school records.
- **Student ID** (Student ID): Enter the student's unique ID number assigned by the school.
- **School Name** (School Name): Enter the name of the school the student attends.
- **Health Center Visit Date** (Health Center Visit Date): Enter the date of the health center visit.
- **Health Center Visit Time** (Health Center Visit Time): Enter the time of the health center visit.
- **Visit Reason** (Visit Reason): Select one of the provided options to indicate the reason for the visit (None, General Check-Up, Illness, Vaccine, Medication).
- **Discharge Instructions** (Discharge Instructions): Enter any specific instructions given to the student during the visit.
- **Student Signature** (Student Signature): Ensure the student has acknowledged and understood the discharge instructions by selecting one of the provided options.
- **Parent Signature** (Parent Signature): Ensure the parent has acknowledged and understood the discharge instructions by selecting one of the provided options.
- **Healthcare Provider Signature** (Healthcare Provider Signature): Ensure the healthcare provider has reviewed and provided accurate information about the student's health and condition by selecting one of the provided options.
- **School Admin Signature** (School Admin Signature): Ensure the school administrator has reviewed and ensured the student's understanding of the discharge instructions by selecting one of the provided options.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are completed accurately and consistently.
- Verify that the student, parent, and healthcare provider have all acknowledged and understood the discharge instructions.
- Keep the form for future reference in case of any follow-up or concerns.
