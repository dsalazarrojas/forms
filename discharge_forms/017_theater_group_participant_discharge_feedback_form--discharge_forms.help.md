# Theater Group Participant Discharge Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from participants after they have been discharged from the theater group. This feedback will help us improve the program and provide better services to future participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please ensure you have all the necessary information and answers ready before starting.
2. Complete each field accurately and thoroughly.
3. Review your submissions carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant info** (`participant_info`, number, required): Please provide your participant information, including your name, ID, or any other relevant details.
* **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date`, date, required): Enter the date you were discharged from the theater group, in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Discharge Time** (`discharge_time`, time, optional): If applicable, enter the time you were discharged, in HH:MM AM/PM format.
* **Participant Rating** (`participant_rating`, number, required): Rate your overall experience as a participant in the program, using a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Reason for Departure** (`reason_for_departure`, text, required): Describe the reason why you departed from the program.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, text, required): Share your general comments about your experience in the program.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, required): Provide any suggestions on how we can improve the program.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Additional comments or feedback you would like to share.
* **Submit** (select_one, required): Choose whether to submit your feedback immediately or save it as a draft for later submission.

## Tips
Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, and don't hesitate to provide any additional comments or feedback.
