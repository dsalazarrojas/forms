# Inmate Release Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the details of an inmate's release from a facility. It helps maintain accurate and consistent records, ensuring timely and accurate tracking and reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the release date and time in the corresponding fields.
2. Sign and identify yourself as the officer releasing the inmate in the "Officer Signature" and "Officer Name" fields.
3. Enter your officer ID number to ensure proper identification.
4. Provide the name of the facility from which the inmate is being released.
5. Enter the name of the inmate being released.
6. Specify the reason for the inmate's release in the "Release Reason" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date on which the inmate is being released from the facility.
* **Release Time** (`release_time`, `time`, not required): Enter the time at which the inmate is being released from the facility.
* **Officer Signature** (`officer_signature`, `text`, not required): Sign your name as the officer releasing the inmate, confirming that the release occurred.
* **Officer Name** (`officer_name`, `text`, not required): Enter your official officer name.
* **Officer ID Number** (`officer_id_number`, `text`, not required): Enter your officer ID number for verification purposes.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the facility from which the inmate is being released.
* **Inmate Name** (`inmate_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the inmate being released.
* **Release Reason** (`release_reason`, `text`, not required): Specify the reason why the inmate is being released.
