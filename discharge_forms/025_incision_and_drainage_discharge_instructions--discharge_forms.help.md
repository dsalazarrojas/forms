# incision-and-drainage-discharge-instructions - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document patient discharges from incision and drainage procedures. It gathers necessary information for medical professionals, including patient contact details, discharge dates, and procedural specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Patient Information**: Fill in the patient's name, contact method, address, email, and phone number as relevant.
2. **Discharge Details**: Provide the date of discharge.
3. **Procedure Details**: Enter the name of the surgeon and the location of the incision site.
4. **Additional Documentation (if applicable)**: If required, upload photos of the incision site.
5. **Finalize with Questionnaire**: Select any relevant options from the provided list to complete the questionnaire.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to contact the patient (e.g., 'Yes' for direct contact, 'No' if other arrangements have been made).
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, optional): Enter the patient's contact address, if applicable.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the patient's email address, if applicable.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the patient’s phone number, if applicable.
* **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the discharge as per the medical records.
* **Surgeon** (`surgeon`, text, optional): Enter the name of the operating surgeon.
* **Incision Site** (`incision_site`, text, optional): Describe the site of the incision.
* **Incision Site Photos** (`incision_site_photos`, note, optional): If required, upload photos of the incision site as a note.
* **Questionnaire Sign** (`questionnaire_sign`, select_multiple, optional): Select any options that apply from the provided list for the questionnaire.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely to ensure proper patient care and communication.
- Upload any required photos according to the specific instructions given by the form's purpose.
