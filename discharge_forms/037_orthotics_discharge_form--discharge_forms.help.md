# Orthotics Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Orthotics Discharge Form is used to document the completion of the orthotics treatment for a patient. It helps in tracking the progress of the treatment and ensuring that it is finished successfully.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the **Discharge Date**, the date on which the patient's orthotics treatment was completed.
2. Enter the **Discharge Time**, the time when the treatment was completed.
3. Select the **Discharge Status** from the available options (Active, Complete, Discharged). This indicates the status of the patient's treatment.
4. If necessary, enter the **Reason for Discharge**, a brief description of why the patient was discharged.
5. If required, enter your **Patient Signature** to confirm that they understand and agree to the discharge.
6. If required, enter your **Healthcare Provider Signature** to confirm that the treatment was completed successfully.
7. Schedule a **Follow Up Date** for any necessary future appointments or check-ups.
8. Enter the **Follow Up Time** for any necessary follow-up appointments or check-ups.
9. Enter the **Follow Up Provider** name and contact information for future appointments or check-ups.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Discharge Date**: Enter the date on which the patient's orthotics treatment was completed.
* **Discharge Time**: Enter the time when the treatment was completed.
* **Discharge Status**: Select the status of the patient's treatment from the available options.
* **Reason for Discharge**: If necessary, enter a brief description of why the patient was discharged.
* **Patient Signature**: Enter your patient's signature to confirm that they understand and agree to the discharge.
* **Healthcare Provider Signature**: Enter your healthcare provider's signature to confirm that the treatment was completed successfully.
* **Follow Up Date**: Schedule a follow-up appointment or check-up for the patient.
* **Follow Up Time**: Enter the time for the follow-up appointment or check-up.
* **Follow Up Provider**: Enter the name and contact information of the healthcare provider for follow-up appointments or check-ups.

## Tips
* Ensure that the discharge status is set to "Discharged" when the patient's treatment is completed.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date information in the form to ensure correct tracking and follow-up.
* Use this form for the intended purpose to avoid confusion and ensure correct treatment progress tracking.
