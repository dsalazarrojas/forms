# treatment completion confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The treatment completion confirmation form is used to officially mark the completion of a treatment. This form should be completed by the healthcare provider after the treatment has been administered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate status of the treatment from the "treatment completion" dropdown menu.
2. Enter the date the treatment was completed.
3. Provide your signature as a confirmation of the treatment completion.
4. Enter the name of the provider who administered the treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **treatment completion** (select_one): Choose "treatment completion" if the treatment has been administered as intended. Choose "Other" if the treatment was not completed.
* **date completed** (date): Enter the date the treatment was completed using the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **signature** (note): Provide your official signature as a confirmation of the treatment completion. This should be a clear and legible signature.
* **provider name** (text): Enter the name of the healthcare provider who administered the treatment.
