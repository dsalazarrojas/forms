# Addiction Recovery Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Addiction Recovery Discharge Form is used to document the discharge process of patients from an addiction recovery program. It ensures a smooth transition and maintains clear records of treatment progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the patient's treatment progress and ensure you have all necessary information.
2. Enter the patient's full legal name in the "Patient name" field.
3. Enter the admission date of the patient in the "Admission date" field.
4. Enter the discharge date of the patient in the "Discharge date" field.
5. Select the reason for discharge from the options provided (Completed program, Transferred, Against advice, or Other).
6. Briefly summarize the patient's condition at discharge in the "Condition at discharge" field.
7. Outline the follow-up plan for the patient in the "Follow up plan" field.
8. List any support contacts and resources for the patient's aftercare in the "Support contacts and resources" field (if applicable).
9. Confirm whether the patient received written discharge instructions in the "Discharge instructions given" field.
10. Enter the name of the clinician completing the discharge process in the "Discharging clinician name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient name** (patient_name): Enter the patient's full legal name.
* **Admission date** (admission_date): Enter the date the patient entered the program.
* **Discharge date** (discharge_date): Enter the date the patient is discharging from the program.
* **Reason for discharge** (reason_for_discharge): Select the reason for discharge from the provided options (Completed program, Transferred, Against advice, or Other).
* **Condition at discharge** (condition_at_discharge): Briefly summarize the patient's clinical status at discharge.
* **Follow up plan** (follow_up_plan): Outline the plan for the patient's aftercare, including any appointments, medications, or referrals.
* **Support contacts and resources** (support_contacts): List any support contacts or resources for the patient's aftercare (if applicable).
* **Discharge instructions given** (discharge_instructions_given): Confirm whether the patient received written discharge instructions.
* **Discharging clinician name** (discharging_clinician): Enter the name of the clinician completing the discharge process.

## Tips

* Ensure you have all necessary information and approvals before completing the form.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered in each field.
* Review the form carefully to ensure it's complete and accurate before submission.
