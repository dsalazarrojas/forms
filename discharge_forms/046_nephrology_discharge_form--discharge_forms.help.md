# Nephrology Discharge Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from patients undergoing nephrology treatment. It is used to document the patient's condition, treatment, and follow-up details during discharge.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter your patient's address in the "Address" field.
3. Provide a brief medical history of your patient in the "Medical History" field.
4. Record your patient's vital signs in the "Vital Signs" field.
5. Check if your patient is currently on dialysis treatment in the "Dialysis Treatment" field.
6. Specify the date of discharge in the "Discharge Date" field.
7. Enter the date for the follow-up appointment in the "Follow Up Date" field.
8. Provide contact information for the patient's follow-up in the "Contact Info" field.
9. Specify the medical specialist attending to the patient in the "Medical Specialist" field.
10. Enter the reason for discharge in the "Discharge Reason" field.
11. Record any other conditions the patient may have in the "Patient Condition" field.
12. Enter the name of the nephrology specialist attending to the patient in the "Nephrology Specialist" field.
13. Record any other conditions the patient may have in the "Other Conditions" field.
14. Enter laboratory information for the patient in the "Laboratory Info" field.
15. Enter any notes related to the patient's treatment in the "Notes" field.
16. Specify whether the medical team has been contacted in the "Medical Team" field.
17. Enter the contact person's name in the "Contact Person" field.
18. Enter the contact person's phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
19. Enter the contact person's email in the "Contact Email" field.
20. Specify the follow-up status in the "Follow Up Status" field.
21. Enter the discharge location in the "Discharge Location" field.
22. Enter the next appointment date in the "Next Appointment" field.
23. Record any other appointments the patient may have in the "Other Appointments" field.
24. Enter the discharge time in the "Discharge Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your patient's full name.
* **Address** (`patient_address`, text, required): Enter your patient's address for follow-up contact.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Briefly describe the patient's medical history, including any previous treatments or surgeries.
* **Vital Signs** (`vital_signs`, text, required): Record the patient's current vital signs, such as blood pressure, pulse, and temperature.
* **Medications** (`medications`, select_multiple, required): Check if your patient is currently taking any medications.
* **Dialysis Treatment** (`dialysis_treatment`, select_one, required): Specify if your patient is currently undergoing dialysis treatment.
* **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date`, date, required): Enter the date of discharge for the patient.
* **Follow Up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, required): Enter the date for the next follow-up appointment.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter contact information for the patient, including name, phone number, and email.
* **Medical Specialist** (`medical_specialist`, text, required): Enter the name of the medical specialist attending to the patient.
* **Discharge Reason** (`discharge_reason`, text, required): Specify the reason for discharge from treatment.
* **Patient Condition** (`patient_condition`, text, required): Record the patient's current medical condition.
* **Nephrology Specialist** (`nephrology_specialist`, text, required): Enter the name of the nephrology specialist attending to the patient.
* **Other Conditions** (`other_conditions`, text, required): Record any other conditions the patient may have.
* **Laboratory Info** (`laboratory_info`, text, required): Enter laboratory information related to the patient's treatment.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Enter any additional notes related to the patient's treatment.
* **Medical Team** (`medical_team`, select_one, required): Specify if the medical team has been contacted.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, text, required): Enter the contact person's email.
* **Follow Up Status** (`follow_up_status`, select_one, required): Specify the follow-up status.
* **Discharge Location** (`discharge_location`, text, required): Enter the location of discharge.
* **Next Appointment** (`next_appointment`, date, required): Enter the date of the next appointment.
* **Other Appointments** (`other_appointments`, text, required): Record any other appointments the patient may have.
* **Discharge Time** (`discharge_time`, time, required): Enter the time of discharge.
