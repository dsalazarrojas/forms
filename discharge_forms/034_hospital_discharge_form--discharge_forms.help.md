# hospital_discharge_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about patient discharge from a hospital or healthcare facility. It is intended for healthcare professionals to document essential details about the discharge process, ensuring a smooth transition for the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the patient's file to ensure you have a valid reason for discharge.
2. Enter the discharge information, including any relevant medical details.
3. Record the patient's name and doctor's name.
4. Select the discharge date from the provided calendar or enter it manually.
5. Enter any diagnosis or medical condition related to the discharge.
6. Document the patient's medical history, if applicable.
7. Specify the reason for discharge, if not self-explanatory.
8. Record any prescribed medication, if applicable.
9. Sign the discharge certificate to confirm the patient's discharge.
10. Provide follow-up instructions for the patient, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Discharge Information** (`discharge_info`): Enter a brief description of the patient's discharge, including any important details.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`): Enter the patient's full name, as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`): Enter the doctor's full name who treated the patient during their hospitalization.
* **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date`): Select or enter the date of discharge using the provided calendar.
* **Diagnosis** (`diagnosis`): Record any diagnosis or medical condition that led to the patient's discharge.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`): Enter the patient's medical history, including any allergies, medical conditions, or previous treatments.
* **Discharge Reason** (`discharge_reason`): Select the reason for discharge, if not self-explanatory. This field is optional.
* **Medication** (`medication`): Record any medication prescribed to the patient during their hospitalization.
* **Discharge Certificate** (`discharge_certificate`): Sign this field to confirm the patient's discharge, indicating that all necessary procedures have been completed.
* **Follow-up Instructions** (`follow_up_instructions`): Provide any follow-up instructions for the patient, including post-discharge care recommendations.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the patient's file for any updates before completing the form.
* Ensure you have accurate information for each field to maintain patient records.
* Keep the form concise and clear, using proper medical terminology and abbreviations.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, consult with the medical team or patient advocate before proceeding.
