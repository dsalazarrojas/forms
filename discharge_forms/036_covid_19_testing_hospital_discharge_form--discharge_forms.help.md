# COVID 19 Testing Hospital Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to document the discharge of a patient who has been tested for COVID-19. It captures essential information about the patient's condition, treatment, and post-discharge instructions to ensure safe and successful recovery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's details such as their first name, last name, date of birth, medical record number, phone number, and email address.
2. Select the patient's gender.
3. Enter the date of admission and reason for admission.
4. Enter the date of COVID-19 testing and the test result.
5. Indicate the patient's COVID-19 vaccination status.
6. If vaccinated, enter the most recent vaccine date.
7. Select the patient's condition at discharge.
8. List the medications prescribed at discharge.
9. Schedule a follow-up appointment date and provide the follow-up provider's name and phone number.
10. Enter the emergency contact's name and phone number.
11. Indicate if the patient has support at home and how they will be getting home.
12. Review and acknowledge emergency warning signs.
13. Confirm that you understand all discharge instructions.
14. Sign and date the form as the patient and the discharging provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Discharge Date**: Date of discharge from the hospital.
* **Discharge Time**: Time of discharge from the hospital.
* **Patient First Name**: Legal first name of the patient.
* **Patient Last Name**: Legal last name of the patient.
* **Date of Birth**: Patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender**: Select the patient's gender.
* **Medical Record Number**: Hospital ID assigned to the patient.
* **Phone Number**: Contact number of the patient.
* **Email Address**: Contact email of the patient.
* **Home Address**: Street address where the patient will be living at home.
* **City**: City where the patient is living.
* **State**: State where the patient is living.
* **Zip Code**: Postal code of the patient's location.
* **Admission Date**: Date of admission to the hospital.
* **Admission Reason**: Reason for the patient's admission.
* **COVID-19 Test Date**: Date of COVID-19 testing.
* **COVID-19 Test Result**: Select the test outcome: Positive, Negative, or Presumptive Positive.
* **COVID-19 Vaccination Status**: Select the patient's vaccination status: Fully vaccinated with booster, Fully vaccinated, Partially vaccinated, Not vaccinated, or Unknown.
* **Most Recent Vaccine Date**: Date of the most recent vaccine dose if vaccinated.
* **Condition at Discharge**: Select the patient's health status at discharge: Stable, Improved, Recovered, or Requires ongoing care.
* **Discharge Instructions Given**: Select all instructions provided to the patient at discharge (e.g., medication instructions, isolation guidelines, etc.).
* **Isolation Instructions**: List any home isolation guidance provided to the patient.
* **Medications Prescribed**: List all medications prescribed at discharge.
* **Follow-up Appointment Date**: Date of the scheduled follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-up Provider Name**: Name of the provider for the follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-up Provider Phone**: Contact number of the follow-up provider.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Full name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Will you have support at home**: Select if the patient has support at home (yes or no).
* **How are you getting home**: Select the method of transportation to get home (e.g., private vehicle, medical transport, etc.).
* **Have emergency warning signs been reviewed**: Select if the patient understands emergency warning signs.
* **Do you understand all discharge instructions**: Select if the patient understands all discharge instructions.
* **Patient Signature Date**: Date the patient signed the form.
* **Discharging Provider Name**: Name of the discharging provider.
* **Provider Signature Date**: Date the discharging provider signed the form.
