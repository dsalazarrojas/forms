# Dock Departure Discharge Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dock Departure Discharge Form is a document used to record the departure of a vessel from a dock. It is used to collect information about the vessel's departure details and any additional relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have the correct information about the vessel you are completing this form for.
2. Fill out the form carefully, providing accurate information for each field.
3. If the vessel has already departed, please refer to our previous departure documentation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dock Departure Discharge Form** (`page_1`, text, required):
	+ This is the title of the form, and you can leave it as it is.
* **Vessel Number** (`vessel_number`, text, required):
	+ Enter the number of the vessel you are discharging.
* **Port of Discharge** (`port_of_discharge`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select 'Yes' if the vessel has discharged its cargo or passengers, 'No' if it has not.
* **Departure Date** (`departure_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date the vessel is departing from the dock.
* **Departure Time** (`departure_time`, time, optional):
	+ Enter the time the vessel is departing from the dock.
* **Port of Arrival** (`port_of_arrival`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all the ports of arrival for this discharge.
* **Discharge Outcomes** (`discharge_outcomes`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all the discharge outcomes for this discharge.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional):
	+ Add any additional comments or information related to this discharge.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required):
	+ Enter the contact email of the person or organization discharging the vessel.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the contact phone number of the person or organization discharging the vessel.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please refer to our previous departure documentation or contact the relevant authorities.
* This form is for dock staff to record the departure of vessels from the dock, and any inaccuracies or omissions may lead to incorrect information being recorded.
