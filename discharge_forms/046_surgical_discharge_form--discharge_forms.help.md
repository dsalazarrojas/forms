# Surgical Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document a patient's discharge from a surgical procedure, capturing essential details for accurate and efficient care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **discharge_date** field with the date of the patient's discharge from the hospital.
2. Enter the **discharge_time** field with the time of the patient's discharge from the hospital.
3. Record the patient's **vital_signs** in the format of temperature (e.g., 98.6°F), blood pressure (e.g., 120/80), heart rate (e.g., 70 bpm), and oxygen saturation (e.g., 98%).
4. Provide a numerical **pain_score** to indicate the patient's level of pain after discharge.
5. Select the **discharge_disposition** option that best describes the patient's status after discharge (e.g., home, rehabilitation, ICU).
6. List any **allergies** the patient has in the space provided.
7. List the patient's **medications** they are currently taking or will need to take post-discharge.
8. Enter any **discharge_instructions** provided to the patient, such as post-operative care or follow-up appointments.
9. Provide **follow_up** information, including any scheduled appointments or contact information for the patient's care team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **discharge_date**: Enter the date the patient was discharged from the hospital.
* **discharge_time**: Enter the time the patient was discharged from the hospital.
* **vital_signs**: Record the patient's vital signs, including temperature, blood pressure, heart rate, and oxygen saturation.
* **pain_score**: Enter a numerical value to indicate the patient's level of pain after discharge.
* **discharge_disposition**: Select the patient's status after discharge, such as home, rehabilitation, or ICU.
* **allergies**: List any allergies the patient has, such as medication allergies or food allergies.
* **medications**: List the patient's current or post-discharge medications.
* **discharge_instructions**: Enter any instructions provided to the patient, such as post-operative care or follow-up appointments.
* **follow_up**: Provide information on follow-up care, including scheduled appointments or contact information for the care team.

## Tips
* Ensure to double-check the patient's information for accuracy before submission.
* Use clear and concise language in the discharge instructions.
* Be thorough in listing the patient's allergies and medications.
* Update the follow-up information regularly to reflect any changes in the patient's care schedule.
