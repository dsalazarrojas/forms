# Maternity Ward Discharge Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients being discharged from the maternity ward. This feedback will help hospital staff evaluate the quality of care and services provided during their stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your feedback and comments about your experience in the maternity ward.
2. Enter the name of the nurse who provided care for you.
3. Select the time of your discharge from the maternity ward.
4. (Optional) Enter the date of your discharge from the maternity ward.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Feedback** (`maternity_ward_discharge_feedback`, text, optional): This is the space where you can provide any comments or suggestions about your experience in the maternity ward. Please be honest and provide as much detail as you can.
- **Ward Nurse** (`ward_nurse`, text, required): This field should be filled with the name of the nurse who took care of you during your stay. This helps us identify who provided you with excellent service.
- **Discharge Time** (`discharge_time`, date, optional): Please select the time you were discharged from the maternity ward. This helps us track the timing of discharges.
- **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date`, time, optional): This field should be filled with the date you were discharged from the maternity ward. If you don't know the date, you can leave this field blank.

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask staff for help.
- Your feedback is invaluable to us. Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
