# Pediatric Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pediatric Discharge Form is used to document a patient's discharge from the hospital, ensuring that all necessary information is recorded and communicated to the patient's caregivers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the general information section with details about the patient's visit, including date of birth and medical condition.
2. Select the relevant medical condition that the patient was treated for.
3. Provide a treatment plan for the patient, if necessary.
4. List any follow-up appointments that the patient needs to attend.
5. Include any discharge instructions or special care instructions.
6. List any medications that the patient is taking or should take in the future.
7. Document any allergies or medical history that the patient has.
8. Record any laboratory results or radiology reports from the patient's visit.
9. Add any other relevant information that is not covered in the other sections.
10. Finally, fill out the discharge date and time, as well as the names and titles of the pediatrician and nurse who signed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (discharge_form_general_info)
	+ A text field for entering general information about the patient's visit, including date of birth.
* **Parent Name** (parent_name)
	+ A text field for entering the patient's parent's name.
* **Date Of Birth** (date_of_birth)
	+ A date field for entering the patient's date of birth.
* **Medical Condition** (medical_condition)
	+ A dropdown menu with a list of possible medical conditions the patient was treated for, such as Acute Lymphatic Leukemia or Pneumonia.
* **Treatment Plan** (treatment_plan)
	+ A text field for entering any treatment or medication instructions.
* **Follow Up Appointments** (follow_up_appointments)
	+ A dropdown menu with a list of possible follow-up appointments, such as Primary Care Physician or Pediatrician.
* **Discharge Instructions** (discharge_instructions)
	+ A text field for entering any special care instructions or advice for the patient and their caregivers.
* **Medication List** (medication_list)
	+ A text field for listing any medications the patient is taking or should take in the future.
* **Allergies** (allergies)
	+ A text field for documenting any allergies the patient has.
* **Special Care Instructions** (special_care_instructions)
	+ A text field for entering any special care instructions or advice for the patient and their caregivers.
* **Medical History** (medical_history)
	+ A text field for entering any relevant medical history that affects the patient's care.
* **Medication Administration** (medication_administration)
	+ A text field for recording any medication administration or dosage instructions.
* **Laboratory Results** (laboratory_results)
	+ A text field for entering any laboratory results or reports from the patient's visit.
* **Radiology Report** (radiology_report)
	+ A text field for entering any radiology reports from the patient's visit.
* **Other Information** (other_info)
	+ A text field for entering any other relevant information that is not covered in the other sections.
* **Discharge Date** (discharge_date)
	+ A date field for entering the discharge date.
* **Discharge Time** (discharge_time)
	+ A time field for entering the discharge time.
* **Pediatrician Name** (pediatrician_name)
	+ A text field for entering the name of the pediatrician who signed the form.
* **Physician Name** (physician_name)
	+ A text field for entering the name of the physician who signed the form.
* **Nurse Name** (nurse_name)
	+ A text field for entering the name of the nurse who signed the form.
* **Date Signed** (date_signed)
	+ A date field for entering the date the form was signed.
* **Time Signed** (time_signed)
	+ A time field for entering the time the form was signed.
* **Facility** (facility)
	+ A text field for entering the name of the facility where the patient was treated.
* **Patient Info** (patient_info)
	+ A text field for entering any additional information about the patient.
