# ICU Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the details of a patient's stay in the Intensive Care Unit (ICU), ensuring a smooth and safe transition of care for the patient as they prepare for discharge.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's basic information on Page 1.
2. Provide an up-to-date medication list and any allergies on Page 2.
3. Document lab results and current diagnosis on Page 3.
4. Note down the patient's medical history, any ongoing care required, and ICU services used on Page 4.
5. Confirm the discharge status and create a discharge plan on Page 5.
6. Complete the patient's family involvement details on Page 6.
7. Confirm the date and time of the discharge form completion and sign off on Page 7.
8. Review and update the form as needed on Page 8.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (Page 1)**: This field is for recording the patient's basic details, including name, date of birth, and hospital ID.
* **Medication (Page 2)**: This field is for listing the medications the patient is currently taking, as well as any allergies they may have.
* **Discharge Information (Page 3)**: This field is for documenting the patient's current medical status and any lab results relevant to their care.
* **Follow Up (Page 4)**: This field is for noting any ongoing care required after discharge, such as follow-up appointments or medication monitoring.
* **Medication List (Page 4)**: This field is for listing any medications the patient is currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.
* **Medication Allergy (Page 4)**: This field is for listing any known allergies the patient has, including the substance and reaction.
* **Lab Results (Page 5)**: This field is for documenting any relevant lab results from the patient's stay in the ICU.
* **Discharge Diagnosis (Page 5)**: This field is for recording the patient's final diagnosis before discharge.
* **Medical History (Page 5)**: This field is for recording the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses or conditions.
* **Patient Care (Page 6)**: This field is for noting any ongoing care required after discharge, such as follow-up appointments or medication monitoring.
* **ICU Services (Page 6)**: This field is for documenting any services used during the patient's stay in the ICU.
* **Discharge Status (Page 7)**: This field is for confirming the patient's discharge status.
* **Discharge Plan (Page 7)**: This field is for creating a plan for the patient's post-discharge care.
* **Patient Family (Page 7)**: This field is for documenting any family involvement or support for the patient after discharge.
* **Patient Status (Page 8)**: This field is for recording the patient's current status after discharge.
* **ICU Staff (Page 8)**: This field is for noting the staff member who last updated the form.
* **Patient Family Involvement (Page 8)**: This field is for noting any family involvement or support after discharge.
* **ICU Discharge Form Signature (Page 8)**: This field is for signing off on the form to confirm completion.
* **ICU Discharge Form Date (Page 8)**: This field is for recording the date the form was completed.
* **ICU Discharge Form Time (Page 8)**: This field is for recording the time the form was completed.
* **ICU Discharge Form Signed By (Page 8)**: This field is for recording the name of the person who signed the form.
* **ICU Discharge Form Signed Date (Page 8)**: This field is for recording the date the form was signed.
* **ICU Discharge Form Signed Time (Page 8)**: This field is for recording the time the form was signed.
* **ICU Discharge Form Version (Page 8)**: This field is for recording the version of the form.
* **ICU Discharge Form Updated By (Page 8)**: This field is for recording the name of the person who last updated the form.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review the form carefully before signing off.
* Keep the form up-to-date as needed.
* Store the form securely and confidentially.
* Use it only for the purpose of discharging patients from the ICU.
