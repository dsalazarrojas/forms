# Stroke Recovery Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to document a patient's discharge information after undergoing stroke recovery. It includes details about the care plan, follow-up information, and other relevant discharge details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form sections and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting to fill it out.
2. Complete each section with accurate and relevant details.
3. If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Stroke Recovery Information** (`stroke_recovery_discharge_form_data`, text, required: false): Enter any relevant information about the patient's stroke recovery, such as their progress, challenges, or concerns.
* **Care Plan** (`stroke_recovery_care_plan`, text, required: false): Describe the care plan put in place for the patient, including any medication, therapy, or other interventions.
* **Follow Up Information** (`stroke_recovery_follow_up_info`, text, required: false): Record any follow-up information, such as appointments or scheduled check-ups.
