# Patient Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Discharge Form is a document used to collect essential information for a patient's discharge process. It gathers details that are important for healthcare professionals to ensure a smooth and safe transition for the patient after treatment or hospitalization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the patient's name as it is recorded in your medical records.
2.  Choose the date of discharge, which is typically the date the patient is being discharged from the hospital or treatment facility.
3.  Select the date of the visit when the patient received treatment or care.
4.  Enter the patient's ID, which is a unique identifier used for medical records.
5.  Provide a brief description of the patient's medical history, including any relevant details about the treatment or conditions.
6.  Specify the discharge diagnosis, which is the final diagnosis or reason for discharge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name as it is recorded in your medical records.
* **Date of Discharge** (`date_of_discharge`, date, required): Choose the date the patient is being discharged from the hospital or treatment facility.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, required): Select the date the patient received treatment or care.
* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, text, optional): Enter a unique identifier for the patient's medical record.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the patient's medical history, including any relevant details about the treatment or conditions.
* **Discharge Diagnosis** (`discharge_diagnosis`, text, optional): Specify the discharge diagnosis or reason for discharge.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate patient information is recorded.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with the healthcare provider before completing the form.
* This form is for patient discharge purposes only, please do not use for other medical purposes without proper medical consultation and validation.
