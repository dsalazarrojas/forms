# ENT Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document a patient's discharge from the ENT department. It ensures that all necessary information is accurately recorded for future reference and to maintain compliance with regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Select the date of discharge from the calendar or enter it manually in the "Date Discharged" field.
3. Enter the surgeon's name in the "Surgeon Name" field.
4. Enter the reason for discharge in the "Reason for Discharge" field.
5. Sign the form with your name and title in the "Surgeon Signature" field and the patient's signature in the "Patient Signature" field.
6. Enter the ward number, hospital name, and discharge time in their respective fields.
7. Enter any prescribed medicine and medical conditions of the patient in the respective fields.
8. Provide any doctor comments and surgeon comments in the "Doctor Comments" and "Surgeon Comments" fields respectively.
9. Finally, enter any comments or remarks from the patient in the "Patient Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the patient.
* **Date Discharged** (`date_discharged`, date, required): Enter the date the patient was discharged.
* **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date`, date, required): Enter the date the patient was scheduled to be discharged.
* **Surgeon Name** (`surgeon_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the surgeon who performed the surgery.
* **Surgeon Signature** (`surgeon_signature`, text, required): Sign your name and title as the surgeon.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, required): Sign your name as the patient.
* **Reason for Discharge** (`reason_for_discharge`, text, required): Enter the reason for discharge, if any.
* **Surgeon In Charge Name** (`surgeon_in_charge_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the surgeon in charge of the case.
* **Hospital Name** (`hospital_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the hospital.
* **Patient Name 2** (`patient_name_2`, text, required): Enter any alternate patient names if necessary.
* **Discharge Time** (`discharge_time`, time, required): Enter the time the patient was discharged.
* **Ward Name** (`ward_name`, text, required): Enter the ward name where the patient was treated.
* **Doctor Prescribed Medicine** (`doctor_prescribed_medicine`, text, required): Enter any prescribed medicine.
* **Patient Medical Conditions** (`patient_medical_conditions`, text, required): Enter any pre-existing medical conditions.
* **Doctor Comments** (`doctor_comments`, text, required): Enter any comments from the doctor.
* **Surgeon Comments** (`surgeon_comments`, text, required): Enter any comments from the surgeon.
* **Patient Comments** (`patient_comments`, text, required): Enter any comments or remarks from the patient.
* **Doctor Prescriptions** (`doctor_prescriptions`, text, required): Enter any prescribed medicine.
* **Comments and Remarks** (`doctor_prescribed_medicine`, text, required): Enter any comments or remarks.
