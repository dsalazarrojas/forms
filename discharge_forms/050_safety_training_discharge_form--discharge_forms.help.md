# Safety Training Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to discharge an employee's safety training, confirming that they have completed the required training and providing information about the training they received.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's name and date of training.
2. Enter the name of the trainer who delivered the training.
3. If applicable, enter the number of hours completed.
4. Describe the topics covered during the training session.
5. Add any additional notes about the training session.
6. Indicate whether the training was completed (Yes/No).
7. If necessary, add any comments about the training.
8. Sign the form to confirm that the employee has completed the required training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional)
Enter the name of the employee who received the training.

### 2. **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, optional)
Enter the date on which the training was conducted.

### 3. **Trainer Name** (`trainer_name`, text, optional)
Enter the name of the trainer who delivered the training.

### 4. **Training Hours** (`training_hours`, number, optional)
If applicable, enter the number of hours completed during the training session.

### 5. **Training Session Topics** (`training_session_topics`, text, optional)
Describe the topics covered during the training session.

### 6. **Training Session Notes** (`training_session_notes`, note, optional)
Add any additional notes about the training session.

### 7. **Training Completed** (`training_completed`, select_one, optional)
Indicate whether the training was completed by selecting "Yes" or "No".

### 8. **Training Comments** (`training_comments`, text, optional)
Add any comments about the training, if necessary.

### 9. **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional)
Sign the form to confirm that the employee has completed the required training.
(Note: This field may be required in certain contexts or situations.)
