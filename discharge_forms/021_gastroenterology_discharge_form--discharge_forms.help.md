# Gastroenterology Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gastroenterology Discharge Form is used to collect patient information and follow-up instructions after a hospital or clinical visit, ensuring accurate and complete documentation of the patient's care.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Verify that the patient's medical record is up-to-date and accurate before completing the form.
2.  Review the patient's chart to gather all necessary information.
3.  Complete the form according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (1, text, required): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their medical record.
* **Medical Record Number** (2, number, optional): Enter the patient's medical record number, if available.
* **Date Discharged** (3, date, optional): Enter the date the patient was discharged from the hospital or clinical visit.
* **Discharge Instructions** (4, text, optional): Record any specific instructions or advice given to the patient at discharge.
* **Follow-Up Planning** (5, text, optional): Describe any follow-up appointments, tests, or procedures scheduled for the patient.
* **Follow-Up Date** (6, date, optional): Enter the date of the next scheduled follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-Up Contact** (7, text, optional): Identify the healthcare provider who will be contacting the patient for follow-up, if applicable.
* **Follow-Up Provider** (8, text, optional): Identify the healthcare provider who will be providing follow-up care, if applicable.
* **Patient Acknowledgment** (9, select_one, optional): Confirm that the patient understands their follow-up instructions and any next steps.
	+ I understand my follow-up appointment.
	+ I will follow-up with my doctor.
	+ I will follow-up with my family doctor.
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Other Follow-Up Provider** (10, text, optional): If the patient selected "Other" in the previous field, enter the name of the healthcare provider who will be providing follow-up care.
* **Patient Signature** (11, note, optional): The patient's signature confirming they have read and understood the discharge instructions.
* **Provider Signature** (12, note, optional): The healthcare provider's signature confirming they have discussed and documented the discharge instructions and follow-up plans with the patient.
