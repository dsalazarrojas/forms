# Discharge To Home Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Discharge To Home Planning Form is used to collect essential information for planning a patient's discharge from a healthcare facility. This form helps identify the patient's needs and requirements to ensure a seamless transition back home.

## How To Complete This Form
- Review the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
- Ensure that you have all the necessary information available before completing the form.
- Complete all required fields, indicated by a red asterisk (*), but fill out as much of the form as you can to provide a complete picture of the patient's needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Discharge to home planning form**:
    - This is the main section for describing the plan for discharging the patient back home.
    - Please provide a detailed explanation of the patient's needs and requirements.
- **patient_identifier**:
    - Enter the patient's unique identifier (e.g., MRN, PID, or any other patient-identifying information).
    - This helps healthcare providers quickly identify the patient's record.
- **date_of_discharge**:
    - Enter the date of the planned discharge.
    - This is essential for scheduling and organizing discharges.
- **time_of_discharge**:
    - Enter the time of the planned discharge.
    - This helps healthcare providers coordinate discharges around the clock.
- **follow_up_schedule**:
    - Check 'Yes' if the patient will require follow-up care or services after discharge.
    - This helps healthcare providers plan follow-up visits or interventions.
- **follow_up_frequency**:
    - If 'Yes' is checked for follow-up care, select the frequency of follow-up (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
    - This helps healthcare providers plan follow-up care accordingly.
- **follow_up_date**:
    - If follow-up care is required, enter the date of the follow-up visit.
    - This helps healthcare providers schedule follow-up appointments.
- **follow_up_time**:
    - If follow-up care is required, enter the time of the follow-up visit.
    - This helps healthcare providers coordinate follow-up appointments around the clock.
- **follow_up_location**:
    - Enter the location for follow-up care (e.g., home, clinic, hospital).
    - This helps healthcare providers ensure the patient knows where to go for follow-up care.
- **follow_up_notes**:
    - Enter any additional notes about the patient's follow-up care needs.
    - This provides healthcare providers with essential information for follow-up care.
- **care_plan**:
    - Enter any specific care instructions or plans for the patient's discharge.
    - This ensures the patient receives the necessary care upon discharge.
- **follow_up_provider**:
    - Enter the name of the healthcare provider responsible for follow-up care.
    - This helps healthcare providers coordinate care between themselves.
- **follow_up_contact_phone**:
    - Enter the contact phone number for the follow-up care provider or service.
    - This helps patients contact the follow-up care provider.
- **follow_up_contact_email**:
    - Enter the email address for the follow-up care provider or service.
    - This helps patients contact the follow-up care provider.
- **signature**:
    - Provide your signature as the person completing this form.
    - This confirms that you have reviewed and understood the patient's needs and requirements.
