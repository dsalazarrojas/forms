# Hospice Discharge Summary Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospice Discharge Summary Form is used to document the discharge summary of a patient from a hospice care program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have reviewed the patient's medical record and that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
2. Fill out the form in its entirety, including all required fields.
3. Be as thorough and detailed as possible when completing the form, especially for fields like Medical History and Discharge Diagnosis.
4. If you are unsure about any information, please consult with the patient's primary care physician or the hospice care team.
