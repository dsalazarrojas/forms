# Clinical Trial Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the participant's status and details at the end of a clinical trial. It is essential to ensure that the participant's care is properly discharged, and their safety is guaranteed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Make sure you have the necessary permissions and authorizations to complete this form.
2. Fill in your Participant ID Number (1) and the Clinical Trial Title (2).
3. Enter the Date of Study Discharge (3).
4. Select the Participant Discharge Status (5) from the provided options:
	* Completed Study
	* Early Termination
	* Safety Withdrawal
	* Withdrawn Consent
5. In the Summary of Study Participation (6), provide a brief description of the participant's involvement in the trial.
6. In the Follow-up care plan with your regular physician (9), provide a plan for post-trial care.
7. In the Scheduled final follow-up visit with research team (10), indicate the date of the scheduled final visit.
8. If necessary, report any adverse events (12) experienced since the last visit, and provide details in the Adverse Event Description (13).
9. Sign the form with your digital signature (15) and the Principal Investigator's digital signature (16).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant ID Number (1)**: Enter your participant ID number as assigned by the study team.
* **Clinical Trial Title (2)**: Enter the title of the clinical trial you participated in.
* **Date of Study Discharge (3)**: Enter the date of the participant's discharge from the study.
* **Participant Discharge Status (5)**: Select the reason for the participant's discharge.
* **Summary of Study Participation (6)**: Provide a brief description of the participant's involvement in the trial.
* **Follow-up care plan with your regular physician (9)**: Describe the post-trial care plan with your regular physician.
* **Scheduled final follow-up visit with research team (10)**: Indicate the date of the scheduled final visit.
* **Did you experience any new adverse events since your last visit? (12)**: Answer yes or no.
* **Adverse Event Description (13)**: If yes, provide details of the adverse event.
* **Participant Digital Signature (15)**: Sign the form with your digital signature.
* **Principal Investigator Digital Signature (16)**: Sign the form with the Principal Investigator's digital signature.
