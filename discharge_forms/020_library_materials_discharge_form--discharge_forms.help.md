# Library Materials Discharge Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Library Materials Discharge Form is used to document the removal or discharging of library materials from the collection. This form is essential for tracking the status of library items and ensuring that the collection is up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the Item Number of the item being discharged.
2.  Select the type of item (book, DVD, or Other).
3.  Specify the current status of the item (Available, Checked Out, Damaged, Lost, or Restricted).
4.  Provide a brief description of why the item is being removed from the collection (Reason for Discharge).
5.  Enter the date of removal.
6.  Enter the name of the person or department responsible for the removal.
7.  Any additional remarks about the removal process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Item Number** (item_number, text, required): Enter the unique identifier for the item being discharged.
* **Item Type** (item_type, select_one, required): Choose the type of item being removed (book, DVD, or Other).
*   **Item Status** (item_status, select_multiple, required): Select all the relevant statuses that apply to the item (Available, Checked Out, Damaged, Lost, or Restricted).
*   **Reason for Discharge** (reason_for_discharge, text, required): Briefly describe the reason for discharging the item from the collection.
*   **Date of Removal** (date_of_removal, date, required): Enter the date the item was removed from the collection.
*   **Removed by** (removal_by, text, required): Enter the name of the person or department responsible for the removal.
*   **Remarks** (remarks, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or observations about the removal process.
