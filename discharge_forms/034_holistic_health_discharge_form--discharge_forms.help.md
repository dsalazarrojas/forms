# Holistic Health Discharge Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Holistic Health Discharge Form is used to collect information from clients about their discharge from a wellness clinic, including their discharge confirmations, aftercare acknowledgments, client feedback, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

### General Instructions

- Complete each section of the form as required.
- Fill in all necessary fields as accurately as possible.
- Submit the form when you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Discharge Confirmations** (`discharge_confirmations`, text, required/optional): Confirm in writing that you, the client, understand the instructions and guidelines given to you at the wellness clinic, and that you are aware of the potential risks and benefits associated with your treatment.
* **Aftercare Acknowledgments** (`aftercare_acknowledgments`, text, required/optional): Acknowledge in writing that you understand and will follow the aftercare instructions provided to you by the clinic.
* **Client Feedback** (`client_feedback`, text, required/optional): Share your thoughts, comments, and suggestions about your experience at the wellness clinic. 
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as per your identification document.
* **Wellness Clinic Info** (`wellness_clinic_info`, text, required): Provide information about the wellness clinic, such as its address, contact number, and other relevant details.
* **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which you were discharged from the wellness clinic.
* **Practitioner Feedback** (`practitioner_feedback`, text, required/optional): Share any feedback or comments you have for the practitioner who treated you.
* **Next Appointment** (`next_appointment`, date, required/optional): If you have another appointment scheduled, select a date for it. 

### Tips

* Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask clinic staff for help.

This form was created using an AI tool and is intended to provide a helpful guide for clients to complete their discharge from the wellness clinic. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please contact form@gic.mx.
