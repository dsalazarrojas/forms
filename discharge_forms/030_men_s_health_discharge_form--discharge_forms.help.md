# Men’s Health Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information related to a male patient's discharge from a health facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Review the form**: Before starting, please make sure you have all necessary information and documents ready.
2. **Fill out the Patient Information section**: Complete the fields with your personal and medical details.
3. **Provide Follow-up Plan and Instructions**: Describe the plan for future appointments, medication, or other necessary actions.
4. **Enter Follow-up Details**: Add any additional information for the follow-up.
5. **Submit the form**: Complete the form and submit it to the relevant authorities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (Page 1)** (`men_s_health_discharge_form_page1`, text, required: false)
	+ This section is for providing general information about the patient, such as their name, date of birth, and contact details.
* **Follow-up Plan (Page 2)** (`men_s_health_discharge_form_page2`, text, required: false)
	+ This section is for outlining the plan for future appointments, medications, or other necessary actions after discharge.
* **Instructions (Page 3)** (`men_s_health_discharge_form_page3`, text, required: false)
	+ This section is for providing instructions or advice to the patient regarding their care after discharge.
* **Follow-up Details (Page 4)** (`men_s_health_discharge_form_page4`, text, required: false)
	+ This section is for adding any additional information for follow-up.

## Tips
Please ensure all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly. Review the form before submission to avoid errors. If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification from the relevant authorities.
