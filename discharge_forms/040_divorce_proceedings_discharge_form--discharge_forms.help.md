# Divorce Proceedings Discharge Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for discharging divorce proceedings, which is a legal process that ends a divorce action. It is intended for use in cases where the divorce action is being discharged, and the parties are seeking to formally end the divorce process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the reason for discharging the divorce proceedings from the provided options: Divorce proceedings, Discharge agreement, or Other.
2. Fill in the names of both spouses.
3. Enter the addresses of both spouses.
4. Provide the date of birth for both spouses.
5. Specify the date and time of the discharge.
6. Enter the court document number related to the case.
7. Provide the case number for reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Divorce Proceedings Discharge Form Data** (`select_multiple`): Select the reason for discharging the divorce proceedings from the provided options:
	+ **Divorce proceedings**: The divorce action is being formally ended.
	+ **Discharge agreement**: Both parties have agreed to end the divorce process.
	+ **Other**: Select this option if the reason is not listed.
* **Spouse One Name** (`text`, `spouse1_name`): Enter the name of the first spouse.
* **Spouse One Address** (`text`, `spouse1_address`): Enter the address of the first spouse.
* **Spouse One Date of Birth** (`date`, `spouse1_date_of_birth`): Enter the date of birth of the first spouse.
* **Spouse Two Name** (`text`, `spouse2_name`): Enter the name of the second spouse.
* **Spouse Two Address** (`text`, `spouse2_address`): Enter the address of the second spouse.
* **Spouse Two Date of Birth** (`date`, `spouse2_date_of_birth`): Enter the date of birth of the second spouse.
* **Discharge Date** (`date`, `discharge_date`): Enter the date on which the divorce proceedings are being discharged.
* **Discharge Time** (`time`, `discharge_time`): Enter the time on which the divorce proceedings are being discharged.
* **Court Document Number** (`text`, `court_document_number`): Enter the number of the court document related to the case.
* **Case Number** (`text`, `case_number`): Enter the number of the case for reference.
