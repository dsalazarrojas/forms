# Chronic Illness Management Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chronic Illness Management Discharge Form is a tool used by healthcare providers to document a patient's discharge information, including medication instructions, follow-up appointments, and post-discharge care.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review the form and ensure all required fields are filled in accurately.
2.  Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
3.  Enter your date of birth.
4.  Enter your medical record number (MRN) if known.
5.  Enter the date of admission and discharge.
6.  Enter your primary diagnosis and any secondary diagnoses.
7.  Select all chronic conditions that apply.
8.  List all medications to be continued post-discharge.
9.  Note any medication changes or new prescriptions.
10. Enter any special instructions for medications.
11.  List all follow-up appointments.
12.  Confirm if your PCP has been notified.
13.  Select any specialist referrals made.
14.  Enter any activity limitations.
15.  Select any diet instructions.
16.  Enter specific diet instructions.
17.  List any medical equipment sent home with you.
18.  Select any home care services arranged.
19.  Confirm if you have a caregiver at home.
20.  List any warning signs or symptoms to watch for.
21.  Enter when to seek help.
22.  Enter emergency contact information.
23.  Select transportation method to get home.
24.  Select your discharge destination.
25.  Confirm if education was provided.
26.  Confirm if you understand the instructions.
27.  Confirm if you were able to demonstrate understanding.
28.  Select your preferred language.
29.  Confirm if an interpreter was used.
30.  Confirm your acknowledgment of the discharge instructions.
31.  Enter the name of the discharging physician.
32.  Confirm if the provider has acknowledged the discharge.
33.  Enter the time of discharge.
34.  Enter any additional instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (2, date, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Medical Record Number** (3, text, required): Enter your MRN if known.
* **Admission Date** (4, date, required): Enter the date you were admitted.
* **Discharge Date** (5, date, required): Enter the date of discharge.
* **Primary Diagnosis** (7, text, required): Enter the main reason for your admission.
* **Secondary Diagnoses** (8, text, optional): Enter any other conditions treated.
* **Chronic Conditions** (9, select_multiple, required): Select all chronic conditions you have.
* **Discharge Medications** (11, text, required): List all medications to be continued post-discharge.
* **Medication Changes** (12, text, required): Note any medication changes or new prescriptions.
* **Medication Instructions** (14, text, optional): Enter any special instructions for medications.
* **Follow-Up Appointments** (16, text, required): List all follow-up appointments.
* **PCP Notification** (17, select_one, required): Confirm if your PCP has been notified.
* **Specialist Referrals** (18, text, optional): List any specialist referrals made.
* **Activity Restrictions** (20, text, required): Enter any activity limitations.
* **Diet Instructions** (21, select_one, required): Select any diet instructions.
* **Diet Details** (22, text, optional): Enter specific diet instructions.
* **Medical Equipment** (25, select_multiple, required): Select any medical equipment sent home with you.
* **Home Care Services** (27, select_multiple, required): Select any home care services arranged.
* **Caregiver Support** (28, select_one, required): Confirm if you have a caregiver at home.
* **Warning Signs** (30, text, required): Enter any warning signs or symptoms to watch for.
* **When to Seek Help** (31, text, required): Enter when to seek help.
* **Emergency Contact** (32, text, required): Enter emergency contact information.
* **Transportation Home** (34, select_one, required): Select your transportation method to get home.
* **Discharge Destination** (35, select_one, required): Select your discharge destination.
* **Discharge Education Provided** (37, select_one, required): Confirm if education was provided.
* **Patient Understanding** (38, select_one, required): Confirm if you understand the instructions.
* **Teach-Back Completed** (39, select_one, required): Confirm if you were able to demonstrate understanding.
* **Language Preference** (41, select_one, required): Select your preferred language.
* **Interpreter Used** (42, select_one, required): Confirm if an interpreter was used.
* **Patient Acknowledgment** (44, select_one, required): Confirm your acknowledgment of the discharge instructions.
* **Discharging Provider** (45, text, required): Enter the name of the discharging physician.
* **Provider Acknowledgment** (46, select_one, required): Confirm if the provider has acknowledged the discharge.
* **Discharge Time** (47, time, required): Enter the time of discharge.
* **Additional Instructions** (48, text, optional): Enter any other important information.
* **Provider Signature** (46, select_one, required): Confirm if the provider has acknowledged the discharge.
* **Caregiver Support** (28, select_one, required): Confirm if you have a caregiver at home.
* **Home Care Services** (27, select_multiple, required): Select any home care services arranged.
* **Warning Signs** (30, text, required): Enter any warning signs or symptoms to watch for.
* **Follow-Up Appointments** (16, text, required): List all follow-up appointments.
* **Discharge Medications** (11, text, required): List all medications to be continued post-discharge.
* **Medication Changes** (12, text, required): Note any medication changes or new prescriptions.
* **New Prescriptions** (13, text, optional): List any new medications prescribed.
* **Medication Instructions** (14, text, optional): Enter any special instructions for medications.
* **Diet Details** (22, text, optional): Enter specific diet instructions.
