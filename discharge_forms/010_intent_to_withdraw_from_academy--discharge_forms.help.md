# Intent To Withdraw From Academy - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Intent To Withdraw From Academy" form is designed to provide an official declaration of withdrawal from a academic program or course. This form must be completed and submitted when a student intends to stop attending the academy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your username in the "user_name" field. This field is used to identify you as the student submitting the form.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field. This field is used to contact you for further instructions or follow-up information.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field. This field is used to contact you in case of any urgent matters.
4. Enter a brief explanation for your reason of withdrawal in the "reason" field. This field helps us to understand your decision.
5. Enter the academic year you are currently in, if applicable, in the "academic_year" field.
6. Enter your student ID, if available, in the "student_id" field. This field helps us to quickly identify your student record.
7. Enter the name of the course you wish to withdraw from, if applicable, in the "course_name" field.
8. Add any follow-up notes or comments in the "follow_up" field. This field is optional and for any additional information you would like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_name** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your username to identify yourself as the student submitting the form.
* **email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address to contact you for further instructions or follow-up information.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number to contact you in case of any urgent matters.
* **reason** (`reason`, text, required): Enter a brief explanation for your reason of withdrawal.
* **academic_year** (`academic_year`, number, optional): Enter the academic year you are currently in, if applicable.
* **student_id** (`student_id`, text, optional): Enter your student ID, if available, to quickly identify your student record.
* **course_name** (`course_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the course you wish to withdraw from, if applicable.
* **follow_up** (`follow_up`, note, optional): Add any follow-up notes or comments you would like to provide.
