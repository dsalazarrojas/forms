# Volunteer Service Discharge Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Service Discharge Feedback Form is used to collect feedback from volunteers after their service has been discharged. This helps staff members understand the volunteer's experience and improve their service delivery.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Discharge Date** of the volunteer service in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
2. Enter the **Service Name** in the box provided.
3. Provide **Feedback** about your experience with the volunteer service. This is an optional field, but it helps us understand your perspective.
4. Select all the **Reasons for Discharge** that apply to your experience. Select multiple reasons if necessary.
5. Enter **Comments** about your experience, if you have any.
6. If applicable, select **Yes** or **No** to indicate whether a staff member was assigned to handle your discharge.
7. Enter the **Contact Email** of the assigned staff member, if applicable.
8. Enter the **Contact Phone** number of the assigned staff member, if applicable.
9. Enter any **Additional Notes** about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Service Discharge Form** (1): This is the title of the form, and you do not need to complete it.
* **Discharge Date** (2): The date your service was discharged. Enter it in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Service Name** (3): The name of the service you were involved in.
* **Feedback** (4): An optional field to provide feedback about your experience with the service.
* **Reasons for Discharge** (5): Select all the reasons that apply to your experience. Select multiple reasons if necessary.
* **Comments** (6): Provide any additional comments about your experience.
* **Assigned to** (7): Select **Yes** or **No** to indicate whether a staff member was assigned to handle your discharge.
* **Contact Email** (8): The email of the staff member assigned to handle your discharge, if applicable.
* **Contact Phone** (9): The phone number of the staff member assigned to handle your discharge, if applicable.
* **Additional Notes** (10): Any additional notes about your experience.
