# Cancer Support Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Cancer Support Discharge Form is designed to capture essential information when a patient discharges from the program. This form ensures that all necessary details are recorded and communicated effectively between caregivers, ensuring continuity of care and accurate tracking of patient outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Gather all necessary information from the patient's medical record and other relevant sources to avoid duplication.
2.  Enter the patient's full name as registered in the support program.
3.  Record the patient's date of birth.
4.  Enter the patient's medical record number (MRN).
5.  Enter the date of discharge from the program.
6.  Select the reason for discharge from the provided options.
7.  Summarize the patient's journey and experiences in the program.
8.  Select all the primary support services provided to the patient.
9.  Briefly describe the patient's progress and outcomes.
10. Choose the recommended next steps for the patient's care.
11. Enter the date and clinician's name for the follow-up appointment.
12. If necessary, enter the name of the clinician for the follow-up appointment.
13. Record any prescribed home exercises or routines for the patient.
14. Confirm that the patient has received all necessary documentation.
15. Record the patient's feedback on the support program.
16. Provide an overall satisfaction rating of the program from 1 to 5 stars.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Full Name** (Patient Full Name, Text, Required): Enter the patient's full name as registered in the support program.
*   **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, Date, Required): Record the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Medical Record Number** (Medical Record Number, Text, Required): Enter the patient's medical record number to link to their medical history.
*   **Discharge Date** (Discharge Date, Date, Required): Enter the date of discharge from the program.
*   **Reason for Discharge** (Reason for Discharge, Select One, Required): Select the reason for discharge from the provided options:
    *   Program Completion
    *   Patient Choice
    *   Relocation
    *   Medical Condition
    *   Other
*   **Program Summary** (Program Summary, Note, Not Required): Summarize the patient's journey and experiences in the program.
*   **Primary Support Services Provided** (Primary Support Services Provided, Select Multiple, Required): Select all the primary support services provided to the patient:
    *   Counseling
    *   Nutritional Support
    *   Physical Therapy
    *   Support Group
    *   Financial Assistance
    *   Other
*   **Summary of Progress** (Summary of Progress, Text, Required): Briefly describe the patient's progress and outcomes.
*   **Recommended Next Steps** (Recommended Next Steps, Select Multiple, Required): Choose the recommended next steps for the patient's care:
    *   Community Support
    *   Private Therapy
    *   Oncology Follow-up
    *   Wellness Program
    *   None Required
*   **Follow-up Appointment Date** (Follow-up Appointment Date, Date, Not Required): Enter the date of the follow-up appointment if already scheduled.
*   **Clinician for Follow-up** (Clinician for Follow-up, Text, Not Required): Enter the name of the clinician for the follow-up appointment if necessary.
*   **Prescribed Home Exercises or Routine** (Prescribed Home Exercises or Routine, Text, Not Required): Record any prescribed home exercises or routines for the patient.
*   **Confirmation** (Confirmation, Note, Not Required): Confirm that the patient has received all necessary documentation.
*   **Discharging Clinician Name** (Discharging Clinician Name, Text, Required): Enter your name for the official record.
*   **Clinician Signature Date** (Clinician Signature Date, Date, Required): Record the date of your signature.
*   **Patient or Guardian Acknowledgment** (Patient or Guardian Acknowledgment, Select One, Required): Confirm that the patient has received all necessary documentation:
    *   I have received the discharge instructions
    *   I have questions before discharge
*   **Patient Feedback on Program** (Patient Feedback on Program, Text, Not Required): Record the patient's feedback on the support program.
*   **Overall Satisfaction Rating** (Overall Satisfaction Rating, Number, Required): Rate the patient's satisfaction with the program from 1 to 5 stars.
