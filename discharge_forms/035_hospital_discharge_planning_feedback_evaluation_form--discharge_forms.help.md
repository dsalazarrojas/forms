# Hospital Discharge Planning Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from patients on their experience during the discharge process. It helps us evaluate the quality of care provided and make improvements for future discharges.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the relevant options for each question, indicating your satisfaction or experience with the provided care.
2.  Fill in the required date and time fields for discharge details.
3.  Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the text fields.
4.  Sign and date the form, as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Satisfaction** (page 1, required): Select the level of satisfaction you experienced during your discharge.
    *   This field will ask you to rate your satisfaction with the care you received during your discharge.
*   **Discharge Summary** (page 1, required): Choose the correct description of your discharge summary.
    *   This field will ask you to select the correct summary of your discharge from the provided options.
*   **Communication with nurse** (page 1, required): Enter the date of your communication with the nurse.
    *   This field is a date field where you will enter the date of your communication with the nurse.
*   **Discharge date** (page 1, required): Enter the date of your discharge.
    *   This field is a date field where you will enter the date of your discharge.
*   **Discharge time** (page 1, required): Enter the time of your discharge.
    *   This field is a time field where you will enter the time of your discharge.
*   **Doctor's suggestions** (page 1, required): Enter any suggestions or comments from your doctor.
    *   This field is a text field where you can enter any suggestions or comments from your doctor.
*   **Nurse's suggestions** (page 1, required): Enter any suggestions or comments from your nurse.
    *   This field is a text field where you can enter any suggestions or comments from your nurse.
*   **Other comments** (page 1, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback.
    *   This field is a text field where you can enter any additional comments or feedback.
*   **Patient's signature** (page 1, optional): If you are a patient, please sign and date this field.
    *   This field is where you will sign and date the form if you are a patient.
*   **Doctor's signature** (page 1, optional): If you are a doctor, please sign and date this field.
    *   This field is where you will sign and date the form if you are a doctor.
*   **Nurse's signature** (page 1, optional): If you are a nurse, please sign and date this field.
    *   This field is where you will sign and date the form if you are a nurse.
*   **Discharge planning** (page 1, optional): Enter any comments on the discharge planning process.
    *   This field is a text field where you can enter any comments on the discharge planning process.
*   **Follow-up care** (page 1, optional): Enter any follow-up care instructions or comments.
    *   This field is a text field where you can enter any follow-up care instructions or comments.
*   **Patient contact** (page 1, optional): Enter any contact information for the patient.
    *   This field is a text field where you can enter any contact information for the patient.
*   **Doctor contact** (page 1, optional): Enter any contact information for the doctor.
    *   This field is a text field where you can enter any contact information for the doctor.
*   **Nurse contact** (page 1, optional): Enter any contact information for the nurse.
    *   This field is a text field where you can enter any contact information for the nurse.
*   **Discharge location** (page 1, optional): Enter the location of your discharge.
    *   This field is a text field where you can enter the location of your discharge.
*   **Emergency contact** (page 1, optional): Enter any emergency contact information.
    *   This field is a text field where you can enter any emergency contact information.
*   **Patient condition** (page 2, optional): Check if you are a patient in a condition that requires ongoing care.
    *   This field is a select-one field where you can select if you are a patient in a condition that requires ongoing care.
*   **Doctor condition** (page 2, optional): Check if your doctor is in a condition that requires ongoing care.
    *   This field is a select-one field where you can select if your doctor is in a condition that requires ongoing care.
*   **Nurse condition** (page 2, optional): Check if your nurse is in a condition that requires ongoing care.
    *   This field is a select-one field where you can select if your nurse is in a condition that requires ongoing care.
*   **Patient care** (page 2, optional): Select any care services you are in need of.
    *   This field is a select-multiple field where you can select any care services you are in need of.
*   **Doctor care** (page 2, optional): Select any care services you are in need of from your doctor.
    *   This field is a select-multiple field where you can select any care services you are in need of from your doctor.
*   **Nurse care** (page 2, optional): Select any care services you are in need of from your nurse.
