# Pharmacy Medication Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the details of a patient's discharge from a healthcare facility, ensuring that the patient's medication list, allergies, and other relevant medical information are accurately recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields, indicated by a asterisk (\*).
2. For each field, provide as much information as possible to ensure accurate documentation.
3. If you need to add additional information, use the "Follow-Up" field for notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (text, not required): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Discharge Date** (date, not required): Enter the date of the patient's discharge from the healthcare facility.
* **Pharmacist Name** (text, not required): Enter the name of the pharmacist who is responsible for the patient's care.
* **Patient Phone** (text, not required): Enter the patient's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Patient Address** (text, not required): Enter the patient's address for delivery or follow-up purposes.
* **Medication List** (text, not required): List all medications the patient is currently taking, including dosage and frequency.
* **Allergy Info** (text, not required): List any allergies or sensitivities the patient has, including severity and treatment.
* **Medication Dose** (number, not required): If applicable, enter the dosage of the medication the patient is taking.
* **Medication Frequency** (text, not required): If applicable, enter how often the medication is taken.
* **Medical Condition** (text, not required): If the patient has a medical condition that requires follow-up care, enter a brief description.
* **Pharmacy Info** (text, not required): If this is not the patient's usual pharmacy, provide the name and address of the pharmacy where they can pick up their medication.
* **Discharge Info** (text, not required): If there is any additional information that needs to be communicated to the patient, enter it here.
* **Follow-Up** (note, not required): If the patient requires follow-up care or has any questions, use this field to add notes.
* **Medication List 2** (text, not required): This field can be used to add any additional medications the patient is taking, if necessary.
