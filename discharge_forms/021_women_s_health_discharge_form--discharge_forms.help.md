# Women’s Health Discharge Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Women’s Health Discharge Form is designed to collect information from female patients about their health status during discharge from our healthcare facility. This form helps our team understand your medical history, treatment, and follow-up needs for future care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions according to your experience and knowledge about your health. You may need to fill out multiple pages, each containing a few questions or options. You can always ask our staff for assistance if you are unsure about any of the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, Text, **Required: No**)
• This is the first section of the form where you will provide your basic information such as name, date of birth, and contact details. Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.

### **Medical History** (`medical_history`, Text, **Required: No**)
• In this section, you will provide a brief description of your medical history, including any previous diagnoses, treatments, or surgeries. Be as detailed as possible to help our team understand your overall health status.

### **Discharge Information** (`discharge_info`, Text, **Required: No**)
• This section is where you will report any important details about your discharge from our facility, such as the reason for discharge, any medication changes, or follow-up instructions. Please make sure to include all relevant information.

### **Medications** (`medications`, Select Multiple, **Required: No**)
• In this section, you will select all the medications you are currently taking or have taken in the past 24 hours. Please choose from the options provided, and include any other medications not listed if necessary.

### **Follow-up** (`follow-up`, Select One, **Required: No**)
• Please select one of the options to indicate whether you will need follow-up care or contact from our team after discharge. This helps us plan for your ongoing care.

### **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, Text, **Required: No**)
• In this final section, you will provide contact information for your next of kin or emergency contact person. This is crucial for our team to reach you if needed.

## Tips
• Please answer all questions honestly and accurately to ensure we can provide you with the best possible care.
• If you need assistance with any of the questions, do not hesitate to ask our staff.
• Remember to review and confirm your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
