# Government Contract Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to formally end a government contract. It is used by government agencies to record the discharge of a contract, which involves the termination of a contract by the client or the agency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contract number
2. Enter the client's name and contact details
3. Enter the start and end dates of the contract (if applicable)
4. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for client signature status
5. Select the agency acknowledgement status
6. Select the contract status
7. Enter any additional remarks
8. Attach the relevant file or document
9. Enter the agency and contractor contact information
10. Enter the contract amount

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contract Number** (`contract_number`): Enter the contract number as per the contract agreement.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`): Enter the full name of the client.
* **Contract Start Date** (`contract_start_date`): Enter the date the contract started (if applicable).
* **Contract End Date** (`contract_end_date`): Enter the date the contract ended (if applicable).
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`): Select 'Yes' if the client has signed the contract, otherwise select 'No'.
* **Agency Acknowledgement** (`agency_acknowledgement`): Select 'Approved' if the agency acknowledges the contract's discharge.
* **Agency Comment** (`agency_comment`): Enter any additional comments from the agency.
* **Date Discharged** (`date_discharged`): Enter the date the contract was discharged.
* **Contract Status** (`contract_status`): Select 'Active', 'Inactive', or 'Discharged' as per the contract's status.
* **Remarks** (`remarks`): Enter any additional comments.
* **Attach File** (`file`): Attach the relevant file or document.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): Enter the agency's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter the agency's phone number.
* **Contract Amount** (`contract_amount`): Enter the contract amount.
* **Contract Type** (`contract_type`): Enter the type of contract.
* **Agency** (`agency`): Enter the name of the agency.
* **Agency Contact** (`agency_contact`): Enter the agency contact name and details.
* **Agency Address** (`agency_address`): Enter the agency's address.
* **Agency Phone Number** (`agency_phone`): Enter the agency's phone number.
* **Agency Email** (`agency_email`): Enter the agency's email address.
* **Contractor** (`contractor`): Enter the contractor's name.
* **Contractor Contact** (`contractor_contact`): Enter the contractor's contact name and details.
* **Contractor Phone Number** (`contractor_phone`): Enter the contractor's phone number.
* **Contractor Email** (`contractor_email`): Enter the contractor's email address.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled in accurately to maintain the contract's historical record.
- Attach the relevant file or document for future reference.
- Test the form before submitting it to ensure all fields have been filled in correctly.
