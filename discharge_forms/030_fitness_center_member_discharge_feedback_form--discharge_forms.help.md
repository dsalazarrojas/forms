# Fitness Center Member Discharge Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from members who are discharging their membership from the fitness center. The form is completed by staff members to collect information about the reason for cancellation, the member's experience, and any additional insights or comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the following steps:

1. Enter the Form Title at the top of the page.
2. Enter your Member Name.
3. Select the Reason for Cancellation and provide a brief explanation.
4. If necessary, provide an additional Reason for Cancellation in the "Other" field.
5. Choose the Cancellation Date.
6. Provide a more detailed explanation of the Cancellation Reason.
7. Share any Cancellation Insights or feedback about your experience.
8. Enter Gym Feedback about the facilities, equipment, or staff.
9. Enter Staff Feedback about the staff members you interacted with.
10. Enter any additional Comments or suggestions.
11. Sign as the Member by typing your name in the "Member Signature" field.
12. Sign as the Staff by typing their name in the "Staff Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form Title
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter the title of this feedback form.

### Member Name
* **Member Name** (`member_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a member of the fitness center.

### Reason for Cancellation
* **Reason for Cancellation** (`reason_for_cancellation`, text, required): Choose the primary reason why you are discharging your membership.

### Other Reason for Cancellation
* **Other** (`reason_for_cancellation_other`, text, optional): If the provided reason doesn't fit your situation, provide an additional reason.

### Cancellation Date
* **Cancellation Date** (`cancellation_date`, date, optional): Choose the date your membership will be cancelled.

### Cancellation Reason
* **Cancellation Reason** (`cancellation_reason`, text, required): Provide a more detailed explanation of the reason for cancellation.

### Cancellation Insights
* **Cancellation Insights** (`cancellation_insights`, text, required): Share any additional information or feedback about your experience.

### Gym Feedback
* **Gym Feedback** (`gym_feedback`, text, required): Enter comments about the fitness center's facilities, equipment, or staff.

### Staff Feedback
* **Staff Feedback** (`staff_feedback`, text, required): Enter comments about the staff members you interacted with.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or suggestions.

### Member Signature
* **Member Signature** (`member_signature`, text, required): Sign as the member by typing your name.

### Staff Signature
* **Staff Signature** (`staff_signature`, text, required): Sign as the staff member by typing their name.
