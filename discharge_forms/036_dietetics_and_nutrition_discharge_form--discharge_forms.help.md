# Dietetics And Nutrition Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document important details related to a patient's discharge from the hospital or healthcare facility. It captures information about the patient's name, discharge date, instructions for discharge, medication information, and follow-up details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the date of discharge in the "Date Of Discharge" field.
3. Provide the name of the dietitian who attended to the patient in the "Dietitian Name" field.
4. In the "Discharge Instructions" field, enter any important instructions or information that the patient should be aware of upon discharge.
5. If applicable, enter the name of any medication the patient is currently taking in the "Medication Name" field.
6. If applicable, enter the dose and frequency of the medication in the "Medication Dose" and "Medication Frequency" fields, respectively.
7. In the "Diet Plan" field, enter any dietary restrictions or recommendations for the patient.
8. If necessary, schedule a follow-up appointment with the patient and enter the date and contact information in the "Follow-up Appointment", "Follow-up Contact", "Contact Number", and "Contact Email" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date Of Discharge** (`date_of_discharge`, date, required: false): Enter the date the patient is being discharged.
* **Dietitian Name** (`dietitian_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the dietitian who attended to the patient.
* **Discharge Instructions** (`discharge_instructions`, text, required: false): Enter any important instructions or information that the patient should be aware of upon discharge.
* **Medication Name** (`medication_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of any medication the patient is currently taking.
* **Medication Dose** (`medication_dose`, number, required: false): Enter the dose of the medication the patient is taking.
* **Medication Frequency** (`medication_frequency`, text, required: false): Enter the frequency of the medication the patient is taking.
* **Diet Plan** (`diet_plan`, text, required: false): Enter any dietary restrictions or recommendations for the patient.
* **Follow-up Appointment** (`follow_up_appointment`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the follow-up appointment with the patient.
* **Follow-up Contact** (`follow_up_contact`, text, required: false): Enter the contact information for the follow-up appointment.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's contact number for follow-up.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter the patient's contact email for follow-up.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are accurate and complete before submitting the form.
* If unsure about any information, consult with the patient or medical staff.
* Review the form for any errors before submitting it.
