# Health Profile Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect comprehensive health-related information from patients, allowing our healthcare team to provide personalized and accurate care. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Please fill in your demographic information, including your contact details.
- Provide your medical history, including any allergies, medications, and medical conditions.
- Share your mental health information, if applicable.
- Report your physical activity levels, if applicable.
- List any medical procedures or surgeries you have undergone.
- Share any additional information about your health that you feel is relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Demographics** (Patient Demographics, text, Optional): Please fill in your basic contact information, such as your name, address, and phone number.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, Optional): Provide any additional contact details that you feel are relevant, such as emergency contact information.
* **Medical History** (Medical History, text, Optional): List any previous medical conditions, allergies, or other health-related information that may be relevant to your care.
* **Allergies** (Allergies, text, Optional): List any substances you are allergic to, such as medications or foods.
* **Medications** (Medications, text, Optional): List any current medications you are taking, including dosages and frequencies.
* **Medical Conditions** (Medical Conditions, text, Optional): Report any chronic or ongoing medical conditions, such as diabetes or hypertension.
* **Mental Health** (Mental Health, text, Optional): Share any mental health information, such as anxiety or depression.
* **Physical Activity** (Physical Activity, number, Optional): Report your average level of physical activity per day, using the following scale:
	+ 1 (little to no physical activity)
	+ 5 (moderate physical activity)
	+ 9 (very active)
* **Medical Procedures** (Medical Procedures, text, Optional): List any upcoming medical procedures or surgeries you have scheduled.
* **Surgical History** (Surgical History, text, Optional): Share any previous surgical experiences or medical procedures you have undergone.

## Tips
Please provide as much detail as possible in each field, and do not hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or concerns.
