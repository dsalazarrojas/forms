# Nutrition Care Plan Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nutrition Care Plan Form is designed to help healthcare professionals create a comprehensive care plan for patients with specific nutrition-related needs. This form gathers information about the patient's medical history, dietary requirements, and goals, allowing healthcare professionals to provide personalized guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "patient_name" field.
2. Enter the patient's medical record number (if applicable) in the "medical_record_number" field.
3. Enter the name of the healthcare provider in the "healthcare_provider_name" field.
4. Enter the patient's medical history in the "medical_history" field.
5. Select the patient's nutrition focus areas from the options provided in the "nutrition_focus" field.
6. Enter the patient's goals in the "patient_goals" field.
7. Enter any specific interventions or instructions for the patient in the "interventions" field.
8. Select the patient's food restrictions from the options provided in the "food_restrictions" field.
9. Select the patient's macronutrient goals (e.g., carbohydrates, protein, fat) from the options provided in the "macronutrient_goals" field.
10. Select the patient's micronutrient goals (e.g., vitamin A, vitamin C, calcium) from the options provided in the "micronutrient_goals" field.
11. Enter the date of the next follow-up appointment in the "next_appointment" field.
12. Enter any medications the patient is currently taking in the "medications" field.
13. Enter any additional instructions or comments for the patient's care plan in the "patient_instructions" field.
14. Enter the name and contact information of the patient's caregiver in the "care_giver_name" field.
15. Enter the patient's weight and height in the "patient_weight" and "patient_height" fields, respectively.
16. Enter the date and time of the next follow-up appointment in the "follow_up_date" and "time" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_name (text, required: false)**: Enter the patient's name.
* **medical_record_number (number, required: false)**: Enter the patient's medical record number (if applicable).
* **healthcare_provider_name (text, required: false)**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider.
* **medical_history (text, required: false)**: Enter the patient's medical history.
* **nutrition_focus (select_multiple, required: false)**: Select the patient's nutrition focus areas (e.g., allergies, diabetes, food sensitivities).
* **patient_goals (text, required: false)**: Enter the patient's goals.
* **interventions (text, required: false)**: Enter any specific interventions or instructions for the patient.
* **food_restrictions (select_multiple, required: false)**: Select the patient's food restrictions (e.g., no restrictions, soft diet, mechanical soft diet).
* **macronutrient_goals (select_multiple, required: false)**: Select the patient's macronutrient goals (e.g., carbohydrates, protein, fat).
* **micronutrient_goals (select_multiple, required: false)**: Select the patient's micronutrient goals (e.g., vitamin A, vitamin C, calcium).
* **next_appointment (date, required: false)**: Enter the date of the next follow-up appointment.
* **medications (text, required: false)**: Enter any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **patient_instructions (text, required: false)**: Enter any additional instructions or comments for the patient's care plan.
* **care_giver_name (text, required: false)**: Enter the name and contact information of the patient's caregiver.
* **date (date, required: false)**: Enter the date of the form completion.
* **time (time, required: false)**: Enter the time of form completion.
* **patient_weight (number, required: false)**: Enter the patient's weight.
* **patient_height (number, required: false)**: Enter the patient's height.
* **follow_up_date (date, required: false)**: Enter the date of the next follow-up appointment.
* **patient_contact_info (text, required: false)**: Enter the patient's contact information.
