# daily_subconscious_tracker_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals track their daily subconscious experiences, including thoughts, emotions, goals, fears, anxieties, sleep, exercise, hydration, social contacts, and goal achievements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Select the relevant page for each section (thoughts, emotions, goals, fears, anxieties, sleep, exercise, hydration, and social contacts).
2. Fill out each field as prompted, using the options provided for select fields.
3. Be honest and accurate in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Thoughts (Page 1)
* **Thoughts today** (`title`, `text`, required: false): This field allows you to record your thoughts today. Please enter a brief description of your thoughts.

### How did I feel today? (Page 2)
* **How did I feel today?** (`emotions`, `text`, required: false): Describe your emotions in detail, including any feelings of happiness, sadness, anger, or calmness.

### Top Goal (Page 3)
* **What is my top goal?** (`goals`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your top goal for today from the options provided.

### Biggest Fear (Page 4)
* **What is my biggest fear?** (`fears`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your biggest fear for today from the options provided.

### Biggest Anxiety (Page 5)
* **What is my biggest anxiety?** (`anxieties`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your biggest anxiety for today from the options provided.

### Sleep (Page 6)
* **Did I get enough sleep?** (`sleep`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how much sleep you got today from the options provided.

### Exercise (Page 7)
* **Did I exercise?** (`exercise`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose whether you exercised today from the options provided.

### Hydration (Page 8)
* **How much water did I drink?** (`hydration`, `text`, required: false): Report the amount of water you consumed today.

### Social Contacts (Page 9)
* **Who did I contact today?** (`social_contacts`, `text`, required: false): List anyone you contacted today.

### Achieved Goals (Page 10)
* **Did I achieve my goals today?** (`goals_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose how many of your goals you achieved today from the options provided.

### Faced Fears (Page 11)
* **Did I face any fears today?** (`fears_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose how many of your fears you faced today from the options provided.

### Faced Anxieties (Page 12)
* **Did I face any anxieties today?** (`anxieties_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose how many of your anxieties you faced today from the options provided.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* Use the options provided for select fields.
* Take your time to complete each field thoroughly.
* Review and revise your responses as needed.
* Use this form regularly to track your daily experiences and gain a better understanding of your subconscious.
