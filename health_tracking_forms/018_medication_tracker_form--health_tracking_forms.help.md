# Medication Tracker Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medication Tracker Form is used to track medications taken by patients. It helps healthcare providers to keep track of medication schedules, dosages, and notes in a structured format.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the medication you want to track in the "Medication Name" field.
2. Enter the number of dosages you have administered in the "Dosage Administered" field.
3. Enter the number of remaining dosages in the "Dosages Remaining" field.
4. Select the start date of the medication schedule in the "Start Date" field.
5. Select the end date of the medication schedule in the "End Date" field.
6. Enter any additional notes about the medication in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medication Name** (`medication_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the medication you want to track.
* **Dosage Administered** (`dosages_administered`, number, optional): Enter the number of dosages you have administered so far.
* **Dosages Remaining** (`dosages_remaining`, number, optional): Enter the number of dosages remaining to be taken.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Select the date when you started taking the medication.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Select the date when you finished taking the medication.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the medication, such as any side effects or concerns you have.
