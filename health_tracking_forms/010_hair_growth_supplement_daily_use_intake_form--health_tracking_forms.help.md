# Hair Growth Supplement Daily Use Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you track your daily use and experience with the hair growth supplement. It should be filled out once per day, and only once per day.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Please fill out this form once a day, and only once a day.
*   Answer each question honestly and accurately to the best of your abilities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Daily Use** (`1`, `number`, optional): This field is used to track whether you took the supplement today. Please rate your level of adherence:
	+ 1 - Not taken
	+ 2 - Partially taken
	+ 3 - Taken as directed
	+ 4 - More than directed
* **Adherence Level** (`2`, `select_one`, optional): This field is used to rate your adherence level over the past 24 hours. Please choose from the options:
	+ 1 - Not taken
	+ 2 - Partially taken
	+ 3 - Taken as directed
	+ 4 - More than directed
* **Side Effects** (`3`, `note`, optional): This field is used to describe any side effects you experienced while taking the supplement.
* **Overall Experience** (`4`, `note`, optional): This field is used to describe your overall experience with the supplement.
* **Hair Growth** (`5`, `date`, optional): This field is used to measure your hair growth over the past 24 hours. Please enter the date you started measuring your hair growth.
* **Comments** (`6`, `note`, optional): This field is used to provide additional comments or notes.
* **Follow-up Questions** (`7`, `note`, optional): This field is used to ask any follow-up questions or discuss anything related to your supplement use or overall experience.
