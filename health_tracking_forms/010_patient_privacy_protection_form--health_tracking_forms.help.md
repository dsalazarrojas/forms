# Patient Privacy Protection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Privacy Protection Form is designed to collect sensitive personal information from patients in a secure and compliant manner. This form helps to protect the patient's identity and prevent unauthorized access to their sensitive data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Patient Name** in the text field provided.
2. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the format **MM/DD/YYYY**.
3. Provide your **Medical Record Number** for reference and record-keeping purposes.
4. Enter your **Address** where you can be contacted or received medical services.
5. Enter your **Phone Number** for contact and communication purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: A text field to enter your name as per your government issued ID or medical records.
	+ Please type in your full name as it appears on your ID or medical records.
* **Date of Birth**: A date field to enter your birthdate in the format **MM/DD/YYYY**.
	+ Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Medical Record Number**: A text field for your medical record number.
	+ If you have an existing medical record number, please enter it here.
* **Address**: A text field for your contact address.
	+ Enter your complete address where you can be contacted or received medical services.
* **Phone Number**: A text field for your contact phone number.
	+ Enter your valid phone number for contact and communication purposes.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information before submission to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please consult with our staff or contact us directly.
