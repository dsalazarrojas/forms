# Interdisciplinary Team Meeting Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This template is designed to help interdisciplinary teams capture and document the necessary details for a meeting, ensuring everyone is on the same page and facilitating effective collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the meeting details and ensure you have a clear understanding of the meeting's scope and objectives.
2. Fill out the meeting details (title, date, time, location, and purpose).
3. List the attendees and their attendance status (yes/no).
4. Add any relevant notes or comments.
5. Describe the next steps and any follow-up activities.
6. If applicable, specify the assigned tool or resource.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Details** (`meeting_details`, text, required: false): Enter the title of the meeting, which should be clear and concise.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the meeting.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the meeting.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the meeting (e.g., room number, building, etc.).
* **Purpose** (`purpose`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the purpose or objective of the meeting.
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, select_multiple, required: false): List the names of all team members or stakeholders attending the meeting, with their attendance status (yes/no).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any relevant comments or notes about the meeting.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, required: false): Describe the next steps or actions items agreed upon during the meeting.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, date, required: false): If applicable, specify the date for follow-up or next meeting.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required: false): If the meeting involved the use of a specific tool or resource, specify it here.

Remember, this form is a tool to help you document and organize your meetings. Feel free to adapt it to your team's specific needs and use it as a starting point for your own meeting template.
