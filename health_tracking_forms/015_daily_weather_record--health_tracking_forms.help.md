# Daily Weather Record - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Weather Record form is designed to collect weather data for monitoring weather patterns over time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Click on the "Record" page to start filling out the form.
2. On the "Record" page, select the location from the drop-down menu.
3. Choose the temperature scale you prefer (Celsius or Fahrenheit).
4. Select the weather conditions (Sunny, Cloudy, or Rainy).
5. Optionally, select the wind and humidity conditions.
6. Enter any additional notes or comments about the weather.
7. Fill in your email and phone number (if applicable).
8. Submit the form to save your weather record.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Location**: This field is required to select the specific location where the weather observation is being made.
	+ Select one of the provided options (Location 1, Location 2, Location 3) for the location where the weather observation is being made.
* **Date**: This field is for recording the date of the weather observation.
	+ Enter the date in the format specified in the input format.
* **Temperature**: This field is for recording the temperature at the location.
	+ Select one of the provided options (Temp 1, Temp 2, Temp 3) for the current temperature.
* **Scale**: This field is for selecting the temperature scale (Celsius or Fahrenheit).
	+ Select one of the provided options (Celsius or Fahrenheit) to indicate the temperature scale used.
* **Weather**: This field is for recording the weather conditions at the location.
	+ Select all that apply from the provided options (Sunny, Cloudy, Rainy) to indicate the current weather conditions.
* **Wind**: This field is for selecting the wind conditions.
	+ Select one of the provided options (Calm, Gentle, Strong) for the wind conditions.
* **Humidity**: This field is for selecting the humidity conditions.
	+ Select one of the provided options (Low, Medium, High) for the humidity conditions.
* **Notes**: This field is for recording any additional notes or comments about the weather.
	+ Enter any additional comments or notes about the weather observation.
* **Assigned Tool**: This field is for recording the tool or equipment used for the weather observation.
	+ Enter the tool or equipment used to take the weather reading.
* **Email**: This field is for recording your email address.
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: This field is for recording your phone number.
	+ Enter your phone number.
