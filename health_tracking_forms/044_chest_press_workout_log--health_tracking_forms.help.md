# Chest Press Workout Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chest Press Workout Log is designed to track an athlete's chest press workout, including details such as exercise type, weight, and rep ranges. This form is meant for individual use by athletes to monitor their progress and improve their performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your athlete name and workout date.
2. Select the exercise type you performed during the workout.
3. Enter the weight and rep ranges for each set.
4. Provide details about your form quality, energy levels, and any pain or discomfort experienced during the workout.
5. Add any notes about the workout, such as your goals for the next session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Name** (Required): Enter your full name.
* **Workout Date** (Required): Enter the date of this workout.
* **Workout Time** (Optional): Enter the time of this workout.
* **Gym Location** (Optional): Enter where you trained.
* **Exercise Type** (Required): Select the type of exercise you performed:
	+ Barbell bench press
	+ Dumbbell bench press
	+ Incline bench press
	+ Decline bench press
	+ Machine chest press
	+ Smith machine press
	+ Push-ups
	+ Dips
* **Grip Width** (Optional): Select the width of grip used:
	+ Narrow
	+ Medium
	+ Wide
	+ Not applicable
* **Bar Weight** (Optional): Enter the weight of the bar alone.
* **Added Weight** (Optional): Enter the weight added to the bar per side.
* **Total Weight** (Required): Enter the total weight lifted.
* **Dumbbell Weight** (Optional): Enter the weight per dumbbell if applicable.
* **Set 1 Weight** (Required): Enter the weight used for the first set.
* **Set 1 Reps** (Required): Enter the number of repetitions completed in the first set.
* **Set 1 RPE** (Optional): Enter the rate of perceived exertion for the first set (1-10).
* **Set 2 Weight** (Required): Enter the weight used for the second set.
* **Set 2 Reps** (Required): Enter the number of repetitions completed in the second set.
* **Set 2 RPE** (Optional): Enter the rate of perceived exertion for the second set (1-10).
* **Set 3 Weight** (Optional): Enter the weight used for the third set.
* **Set 3 Reps** (Optional): Enter the number of repetitions completed in the third set.
* **Set 3 RPE** (Optional): Enter the rate of perceived exertion for the third set (1-10).
* **Set 4 Weight** (Optional): Enter the weight used for the fourth set.
* **Set 4 Reps** (Optional): Enter the number of repetitions completed in the fourth set.
* **Set 4 RPE** (Optional): Enter the rate of perceived exertion for the fourth set (1-10).
* **Set 5 Weight** (Optional): Enter the weight used for the fifth set.
* **Set 5 Reps** (Optional): Enter the number of repetitions completed in the fifth set.
* **Set 5 RPE** (Optional): Enter the rate of perceived exertion for the fifth set (1-10).
* **Total Volume** (Optional): Enter the total weight x reps for all sets.
* **Rest Between Sets** (Optional): Enter the average rest time in seconds.
* **Warm-up Sets** (Optional): Enter the number of warm-up sets completed.
* **Did You Use a Spotter?** (Required): Select whether a spotter was present:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Form Quality** (Required): Select your form quality:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Energy Level** (Required): Select your energy level:
	+ Very high
	+ High
	+ Average
	+ Low
	+ Very low
* **Pain or Discomfort** (Required): Select whether you experienced any pain:
	+ None
	+ Mild
	+ Moderate
	+ Severe
* **Pain Location** (Optional): Enter where you felt pain if any.
* **Did You Set a Personal Record?** (Required): Select whether you set a new personal record:
	+ Yes, new max
	+ Yes, new reps
	+ False
* **Workout Notes** (Optional): Enter any notes about the workout.
* **Next Workout Goals** (Optional): Enter your goals for the next workout.
* **Coach Name** (Optional): Enter the name of your coach if applicable.
