# Physiotherapy Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physiotherapy Consent Form is a document that captures essential information from patients, including their contact details, medical history, and consent. It's designed to streamline the physiotherapy process and ensure that patients and physiotherapists are on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the patient information section, including the patient's name, contact details, and any medical history.
2. Provide information about any allergies, medications, and medical conditions you're currently managing.
3. List any concerns or questions you have about your treatment.
4. Give your consent for the treatment and procedures outlined in the form.
5. Sign the form to confirm your agreement and understanding.
6. Include any additional comments or information you'd like to share with your physiotherapist.
7. Sign the form with your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (Page 1): Enter your name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Contact Details** (Page 2): Enter the contact person's name, phone number, and email.
* **Physiotherapy Info** (Page 3): Enter the physiotherapist's name, contact information, and any relevant medical history.
* **Patient Concerns** (Page 4): List any concerns or questions you have about your treatment.
* **Medical History** (Page 5): Enter any relevant medical history, including previous injuries or surgeries.
* **Allergies and Medications** (Page 6): List any allergies, medications, or treatments you're currently taking.
* **Medical Conditions** (Page 7): Enter any current medical conditions, such as diabetes or hypertension.
* **Insurance Info** (Page 8): Enter your insurance information, if applicable.
* **Emergency Contact** (Page 9): Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Consent** (Page 10): Give your consent for the treatment and procedures outlined in the form.
* **Patient Declaration** (Page 11): Make any declarations or statements about your treatment.
* **Additional Comments** (Page 12): Include any additional comments or information you'd like to share with your physiotherapist.
* **Signature** (Page 13): Sign the form to confirm your agreement and understanding.
* **Physiotherapist Signature** (Page 14): Sign the form with your physiotherapist's signature.
* **Date** (Page 15): Enter the date of signing.
* **Time** (Page 16): Enter the time of signing.
* **Physiotherapist Name** (Page 17): Enter the name of your physiotherapist.
* **Patient Name** (Page 18): Enter your name.
* **Physiotherapist Name 2** (Page 19): Enter the name of your physiotherapist (if different from the previous page).
* **Patient Name 2** (Page 20): Enter your name (if you have multiple physiotherapists).
* **Date 2** (Page 21): Enter the date of signing (if different from the previous page).
* **Time 2** (Page 22): Enter the time of signing (if different from the previous page).
* **Contact Name** (Page 23): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (Page 24): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (Page 25): Enter the email of the contact person.

Note: Some fields have been intentionally left blank, as they are not relevant to the form's purpose. If you're unsure about a field, please refer to the physiotherapist for clarification.
