# Doctor Note Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to aid healthcare professionals in documenting patient information for medical records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter a detailed medical note in the "Doctor's Note" field.
3. Record the date of the patient's sickness in the "Date of Sickness" field.
4. Report the estimated date of return to work in the "Date of Return to Work" field.
5. Enter the patient's employer in the "Employer" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's complete name, as it appears on their medical records.
* **Doctor's Note**: Write a detailed note about the patient's medical condition, including any relevant symptoms, treatments, or medications.
* **Date of Sickness**: Record the date the patient became sick or started experiencing symptoms.
* **Date of Return to Work**: Estimate the date the patient is expected to return to work.
* **Employer**: Enter the name of the patient's employer, if applicable.

## Tips
* Make sure to keep the "Doctor's Note" field concise and accurate, as it serves as an essential reference for patient care.
* The "Date of Sickness" and "Date of Return to Work" fields should be entered in YYYY-MM-DD format.
