# Heart Rate Tracking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this Heart Rate Tracking Form is to collect data on users' heart rates during exercise or physical activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the User Name field.
2.  Record your heart rate reading in the Heart Rate field.
3.  Select the start date of the exercise or activity in the Start Date field.
4.  Select the end date of the exercise or activity in the End Date field.
5.  Choose the type of exercise or activity in the Exercise Type field.
6.  Enter the duration of the exercise or activity in the Exercise Duration field.
7.  Provide comments on the exercise or activity in the Comments field, if desired.
8.  Enter your email address in the Email field.
9.  Enter your phone number in the Phone field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Heart Rate** (`heart_rate`, number, required): Record your heart rate reading during the exercise or activity. Enter a whole number value, e.g., 80.
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Select the start date of the exercise or activity.
*   **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Select the end date of the exercise or activity.
*   **Exercise Type** (`exercise_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of exercise or activity you performed (e.g., running, swimming, etc.).
*   **Exercise Duration** (`exercise_duration`, number, required): Record the duration of the exercise or activity in minutes.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments on the exercise or activity.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
*   **User Phone** (`user_phone`, text, optional): Enter your personal phone number for contact purposes.
