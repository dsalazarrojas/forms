# Health Coaching Client Plan Adherence Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Coaching Client Plan Adherence Check In form is used to track the client's adherence to their health coaching plan, helping health coaches monitor progress and provide timely support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Enter the client's name**: Type the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2. **Enter the client's email**: Type the client's email address in the "Email" field.
3. **Enter the client's phone number**: Type the client's phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. **Select a date**: Select the current date in the "Date" field.
5. **Select a time**: Select the current time in the "Time" field.
6. **Mark adherence level**: Select the client's adherence level from 1-10 in the "Adherence" field.
7. **Describe progress**: Type a brief description of the client's progress in the "Progress" field.
8. **Schedule a follow-up**: Select a date for the next follow-up in the "Follow-up" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter the client's full name.
* **Email**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the client's phone number.
* **Date**: Select the current date.
* **Time**: Select the current time.
* **Adherence**: Select the client's adherence level from 1-10, where 1 is "Not Adherent" and 10 is "Fully Adherent".
* **Progress**: Describe the client's progress, such as any successes, challenges, or areas of improvement.
* **Follow-up**: Schedule a date for the next follow-up meeting or check-in with the client.
