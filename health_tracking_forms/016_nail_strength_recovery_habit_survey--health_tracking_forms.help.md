# Nail Strength Recovery Habit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your nail strength recovery habits. It aims to help us understand your routines, habits, and preferences regarding nail health and recovery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction page and ensure you understand the purpose of the form.
2. Scroll through each page, and fill out the required information for each field.
3. Click "Submit" to submit your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intro** (`intro`, text, optional): A brief introduction to the form, which you can ignore if you're already familiar with the content.
* **Nail Health Routine** (`nail_health_routine`, text, optional): Describe your current nail health routine, including any treatments or practices you follow to maintain healthy nails.
* **Product Use** (`product_use`, select_one, optional): Select one option that best describes your current nail care product usage. Options:
	+ Option 1: I use nail products regularly
	+ Option 2: I occasionally use nail products
	+ Option 3: I rarely use nail products
* **Recovery Habits** (`recovery_habits`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply, multiple options that describe your recovery habits for nail growth and health. Options:
	+ Option 1: Regularly soaking my fingers or toes in warm water
	+ Option 2: Massaging my nails with a specific oil or cream
	+ Option 3: Practicing gentle nail stretching exercises
* **Nail Strength Routine** (`nail_strength_routine`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value representing the frequency of your nail strength exercises or routines.
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value representing the frequency of your nail strength exercises or routines.
* **Duration** (`duration`, time, optional): Enter a time value representing the duration of your nail strength exercises or routines.
## Tips
Remember to provide accurate and honest information about your nail strength recovery habits.
