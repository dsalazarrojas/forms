# nutrition_behavior_change_motivation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **nutrition_behavior_change_motivation_survey** form is used to track the motivation score of individuals participating in a program or intervention aimed at promoting healthy eating habits.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form header section with your name or ID (optional)
* Answer the questions in the **Motivation Score** section, selecting the corresponding options for each question
* Answer the questions in the **Motivation Score (2)** and **Motivation Score (3)** sections, selecting the corresponding options for each question
* In the **Form Footer** section, provide any additional comments or feedback you may have
