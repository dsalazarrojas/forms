# sober_progress_tracking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sober progress tracking form is used to monitor an individual's progress in achieving sobriety. It helps track important milestones, such as the start date of sobriety, and daily experiences like mood and challenges faced.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the start date of your sobriety journey.
2. Type in the number of days you have been sober.
3. Select your current mood from the options provided.
4. Enter any challenges you are facing in the comments section.
5. Add any additional comments or progress notes.
6. Select your goals for achieving sobriety (optional).
7. Enter a follow-up date for your next log entry (optional).
8. Set your sobriety goals (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **start_date**: Enter the date you started your sobriety journey. This is the date you last consumed alcohol or used substances. (Required)
- **days_sob**: Type the number of days you have been sober since your start date. (Required)
- **mood_today**: Select your current mood from the options provided: Happy, Stressed, Anxious, Calm, Relaxed. (Required)
- **challenges_today**: Describe any challenges you are facing in the comments section. (Optional)
- **comments**: Add any additional comments or progress notes about your sobriety journey. (Optional)
- **goals**: Select one or more of the following goals for achieving sobriety: Financial, Emotional, Relationship, Spiritual. (Optional)
- **follow_up_date**: Enter a follow-up date for your next log entry. (Optional)
- **sobriety_goals**: Set a sobriety goal, such as a specific number of days or months you plan to maintain sobriety. (Optional)

## Tips
- Complete the form daily to track your progress accurately.
- Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
- Use the form to identify patterns and areas where you may need additional support or guidance.
- Review and revise your goals periodically to ensure they remain relevant and achievable.
