# Hip Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect general and specific information from patients for medical referrals. It helps healthcare providers gather essential details to assess the patient's condition and create a tailored treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and gather all necessary information from the patient.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed.
3. Fill in the General Information, Patient Info, Referral Info, Medical History, and Hip Conditions sections with accurate and relevant details.
4. In the Treatment Plan section, outline the proposed course of treatment and any necessary steps for the patient to follow.
5. Complete the Patient Assessment section by asking the patient about their symptoms, pain levels, and any concerns they may have.
6. Finally, obtain signatures from both the medical provider and the patient to confirm agreement with the referral details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (id: 1)** (`text`, Required: no)
	+ This section is for gathering basic information about the patient, such as their name, contact details, and date of birth.
* **Patient Info (id: 2)** (`text`, Required: no)
	+ This field is for collecting more specific details about the patient, including their address, phone number, and medical history.
* **Referral Info (id: 3)** (`text`, Required: no)
	+ Use this section to document the reason for the referral and any relevant medical history.
* **Medical History (id: 4)** (`text`, Required: no)
	+ Record the patient's medical history, including any previous conditions or treatments they have received.
* **Hip Conditions (id: 5)** (`text`, Required: no)
	+ List the specific conditions affecting the patient's hip or relevant health issues.
* **Treatment Plan (id: 6)** (`text`, Required: no)
	+ Outline the proposed treatment plan for the patient's condition, including any necessary medication or interventions.
* **Patient Assessment (id: 7)** (`text`, Required: no)
	+ Assess the patient's symptoms, pain levels, and any concerns they may have about their condition.
* **Medical Provider Signature (id: 8)** (`text`, Required: no)
	+ Obtain a signature from the medical provider to confirm their agreement with the referral details.
* **Patient Signature (id: 9)** (`text`, Required: no)
	+ Request the patient's signature to confirm their agreement with the referral details and treatment plan.
* **Other Information (id: 10)** (`text`, Required: no)
	+ Include any additional information that may be relevant to the patient's referral or treatment plan.
