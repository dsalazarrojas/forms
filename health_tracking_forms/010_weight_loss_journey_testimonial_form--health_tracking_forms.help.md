# Weight Loss Journey Testimonial Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weight Loss Journey Testimonial Form is designed to collect success stories from satisfied clients who have completed a weight loss program. The form is used to gather testimonials, which can be used to promote and improve the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the required fields (if any).
2. Enter your success story title.
3. Provide your name.
4. Select an image that represents your weight loss journey.
5. Choose how long you were on the program.
6. Choose how your weight loss journey has affected you.
7. If you have a testimonial, type it in.
8. If you were on the program, select whether you would recommend it to others.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Success Story Title** (`weight_loss_journey_testimonial_form_1`, text, optional): A brief title of your success story.
* **Client Name** (`weight_loss_journey_testimonial_form_2`, text, optional): Your name or nickname (if you'd like to be anonymous, you can use that as well).
* **Weight Loss Journey** (`weight_loss_journey_testimonial_form_3`, text, optional): Describe your weight loss journey, including any challenges you faced and how you overcame them.
* **Photo** (`weight_loss_journey_testimonial_form_4`, select multiple, optional): Select one or more images that represent your success story.
* **How long were you on program?** (`weight_loss_journey_testimonial_form_5`, date, optional): Enter the dates you were on the program.
* **How was your weight loss journey?** (`weight_loss_journey_testimonial_form_6`, select one, optional): Choose whether your weight loss journey was successful (I lost weight), unsuccessful (I gained weight), or other.
* **Weight loss testimonial** (`weight_loss_journey_testimonial_form_7`, select one, optional): Choose whether you lost weight, gained weight, or if your weight loss journey was other.
* **I would recommend the program** (`weight_loss_journey_testimonial_form_8`, select multiple, optional): Choose whether you would recommend the program to others.
* **Additional weight loss testimonial** (`weight_loss_journey_testimonial_form_9`, select multiple, optional): Choose whether you have an additional weight loss testimonial.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions truthfully.
- Make sure to choose the correct image for your success story.
- If you have any additional testimonial, please provide as much detail as possible.
