# Voiding Diary Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Voiding Diary Form is a medical record-keeping tool used to track a patient's urination habits and symptoms. It helps healthcare providers gather and analyze data to understand the patient's condition better, ultimately aiding in diagnosis and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **User ID**: This is your unique identifier that helps our system recognize you as a patient. If you're unsure about your User ID, please ask your healthcare provider for assistance.
2. Select the **Visit Date**: Choose the date of your visit or urination event you're recording. This will help us track your urination history over time.
3. Record your **Urination Time**: Enter the time it takes for you to urinate. This will help us understand your urination habits and patterns.
4. Report your **Urination Volume**: Enter the amount of urine you urinate each time. This is an essential aspect of understanding your urination habits.
5. Indicate whether you have any **Symptoms**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to report if you're experiencing any symptoms during urination.
6. Rate your **Pain Rating**: Enter a number rating (1-10) to describe your level of pain or discomfort during urination.
7. Add any additional **Notes**: Enter any relevant comments or observations you have about your urination experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User ID** (`user_id`, Number, Required: false): Enter your unique patient ID.
* **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, Date, Required: false): Choose the date of your visit or urination event.
* **Urination Time** (`urination_time`, Time, Required: false): Enter the time it takes for you to urinate.
* **Urination Volume** (`urination_volume`, Number, Required: false): Enter the amount of urine you urinate each time.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, Select One, Required: false): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to report if you're experiencing any symptoms during urination.
* **Pain Rating** (`pain_rating`, Number, Required: false): Enter a number rating (1-10) to describe your level of pain or discomfort during urination.
* **Notes** (`notes`, Note, Required: false): Enter any relevant comments or observations about your urination experience.
* **Voiding Diary Date** (`voiding_diary_date`, Date, Required: false): Choose the date you're recording your urination experience.
