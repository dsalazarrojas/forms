# COVID 19 Patient Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about the patient's personal details, symptoms, medical conditions, and emergency contact information to help healthcare professionals assess the patient's risk level and provide necessary medical attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information, including your full name, contact number, and email address.
2. Report your current body temperature.
3. Describe any respiratory symptoms you are experiencing.
4. Indicate if you have been tested for COVID-19 in the last 14 days and the result of the test.
5. List any chronic medical conditions you have.
6. Mention any current medications you are taking.
7. Provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number where the healthcare provider can reach you.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Current Body Temperature (F)**: Report your current body temperature using a digital thermometer or a reliable method. Please ensure it's accurate.
* **Are you experiencing any respiratory symptoms such as cough or shortness of breath?**: Answer truthfully if you are experiencing any respiratory symptoms.
* **Have you been tested for COVID-19 in the last 14 days?**: Indicate if you have been tested for COVID-19 in the last 14 days.
* **If tested, what was the result?**: If you have been tested, please select the correct result (Positive, Negative, Pending, or Not Applicable).
* **Do you have any of the following chronic medical conditions?**: Select all conditions that apply to you (Diabetes, Hypertension, Heart Disease, Lung Disease, Immunocompromised, or None).
* **List any current medications you are taking**: Provide a list of medications you are currently taking.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Provide the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter your emergency contact's phone number.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Use a digital thermometer to ensure accurate temperature reading.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
* Please note that this form is for screening purposes only, and further evaluation and testing may be required for a final diagnosis.
