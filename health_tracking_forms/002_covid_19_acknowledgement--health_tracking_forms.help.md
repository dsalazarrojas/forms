# Covid 19 Acknowledgement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information about an individual's understanding and consent regarding the administration of the Covid-19 vaccine. It is designed to ensure that individuals have been provided with relevant information and have given their consent before receiving the vaccine.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the correct options for each question. You can choose from the provided options for each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **acknowledgement_form (Select One)**: Please select one of the provided options to acknowledge your understanding of the vaccine's potential risks and benefits. This question is not required, but it is essential to ensure you have been given adequate information.
* **consent (Select Multiple)**: Choose all that apply from the provided options regarding your consent to receive the vaccine. This question is not required, but it is crucial to confirm your understanding of the consent process.
* **vaccine_administration (Note)**: This field is not required, but it allows you to provide additional comments or information about the vaccine administration process. You can type as much or as little as you need to answer this question.
* **vaccine_side_effects (Select Multiple)**: Choose all that apply from the provided options regarding your understanding of potential side effects. This question is not required, but it helps to ensure you are aware of the possible side effects of the vaccine.

## Tips
* Please take your time to read and understand each question carefully before selecting your options.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* This form is for your personal reference and record-keeping purposes only.
