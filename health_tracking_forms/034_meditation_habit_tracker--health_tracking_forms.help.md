# Meditation Habit Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track an individual's meditation habits, allowing them to reflect on their daily, weekly, or monthly meditation practice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following fields:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Daily Sessions** (Required: false): Enter the number of meditation sessions practiced daily.
- **Session Duration** (Required: false): Enter the duration of each meditation session.
- **Mindfulness Focus** (Required: true): Select the focus of your meditation practice:
  - Focusing on breath
  - Not focusing on breath
  - Mindful movement
  - Other (please specify)
- **Date** (Required: false): Enter the date of the meditation session.
- **Time** (Required: false): Enter the time of the meditation session.
- **Location** (Required: false): Enter the location where you meditated.
- **Notes** (Required: false): Leave any additional notes or comments about your meditation practice.
- **Type of Meditation** (Required: true): Select the type of meditation practiced:
  - Mindfulness meditation
  - Loving-Kindness meditation
  - Movement meditation
  - Guided meditation
  - Other (please specify)
- **Session Goal** (Required: false): Select the goal of your meditation practice:
  - Reduce stress
  - Increase focus
  - Improve mood
  - Other (please specify)
- **Session Frequency** (Required: false): Select the frequency of your meditation practice:
  - Daily
  - Weekly
  - Monthly
  - Other (please specify)
- **Consistency** (Required: true): Rate your consistency with meditation practice:
  - Very consistent
  - Moderately consistent
  - Somewhat inconsistent
  - Not at all consistent
- **Progress** (Required: true): Rate your progress with meditation practice:
  - Made progress
  - Didn't make progress
  - Neutral
- **Obstacles** (Required: false): Select any obstacles that hinder your meditation practice:
  - Lack of time
  - Distractions
  - Other (please specify)
- **Benefits** (Required: false): Select any benefits you have experienced from meditation practice:
  - Reduced stress
  - Improved focus
  - Improved mood
  - Improved sleep
  - Other (please specify)
- **Comments** (Required: false): Leave any additional comments or notes about your meditation practice.
- **Submitted By** (Required: false): Enter your name or email address.
- **Submitted At** (Required: false): Enter the date and time you submitted this form.
- **Assigned To** (Required: false): Select who your meditation practice is assigned to:
  - Myself
  - A coach or therapist
  - Someone I know
  - Other (please specify)
- **Assigned By** (Required: false): Select who assigned you to practice meditation:
  - Myself
  - A coach or therapist
  - Someone I know
  - Other (please specify)
- **Follow-up** (Required: false): Enter the date for follow-up.
- **Follow-up by** (Required: false): Enter the email address for follow-up.
- **Follow-up Time** (Required: false): Enter the time for follow-up.
- **Follow-up At** (Required: false): Enter the date for follow-up.

Note: The form is designed to be filled out by the individual tracking their meditation practice.
