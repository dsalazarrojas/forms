# Cooking Time Log - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Cooking Time Log form is designed to help you track and record the time spent preparing and cooking a recipe.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information for the recipe you are cooking.
2. Record the total time spent on the recipe.
3. Record the prep time (time spent preparing ingredients).
4. Record the cook time (time spent cooking the dish).
5. Add any additional notes about the recipe or cooking process.
6. Enter the date of when the recipe was cooked.
7. (Optional) Enter the name of the recipe.
8. (Optional) If you are submitting the form from a phone, enter your phone number.
9. (Optional) If you are submitting the form from a computer, enter your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Cooking Time Log** (`title`, text, not required): This is the main title of the form that you can fill in with the name of the recipe or any other relevant title.
* **Prep Time** (`prep_time`, number, not required): This field is for recording the time spent preparing ingredients before cooking.
* **Cook Time** (`cook_time`, number, not required): This field is for recording the time spent cooking the dish.
* **Total Time** (`total_time`, number, not required): This field is for recording the total time spent on the recipe.
* **Recipe Name** (`recipe_name`, text, not required): This field is for recording the name of the recipe.
* **Date** (`date`, date, not required): This field is for recording the date when the recipe was cooked.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required): This field is for adding any additional notes about the recipe or cooking process.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, email, not required): This field is for recording the email address of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted By Phone** (`submitted_by_phone`, text, not required): This field is for recording the phone number of the person submitting the form from a phone.
