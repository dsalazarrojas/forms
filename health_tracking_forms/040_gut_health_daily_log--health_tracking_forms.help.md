# Gut Health Daily Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Gut Health Daily Log" form is a tool designed for individuals to track their gut health on a daily basis. It is intended to help users monitor and record their symptoms, routines, and other relevant information to improve their gut health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the "Gut Health Daily Log" form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form daily, at the same time each day, to maintain consistency in tracking your gut health.
2.  Select the relevant options for each field based on your current symptoms, routines, or other relevant information.
3.  Provide detailed information in the "note" field for additional comments or notes related to your gut health.
4.  Ensure that you have filled out all the required fields, if any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Daily Log** (`daily_log`, `date`, required: false)
    *   This field is used to select the date of the log entry.
*   **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `select_one`, required: false)
    *   Select the symptom(s) you are experiencing from the available options, such as "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3".
*   **Routines** (`routine`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
    *   Choose the routines that you have completed from the available options, such as "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3".
*   **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false)
    *   Add any additional comments or notes about your gut health.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false)
    *   Enter your email address.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false)
    *   Enter your phone number.
*   **Output File** (`output_file`, `text`, required: false)
    *   Enter the name of the output file.
*   **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `select_one`, required: false)
    *   Select who submitted the log entry, such as "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3".
