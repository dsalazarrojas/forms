# Migraine Diary Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Migraine Diary Form is a tool for tracking and monitoring migraine experiences. By completing this form, you can help identify patterns and potential triggers for your migraines.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the date of your headache in the <code>headache_date</code> field.
2. Indicate whether you experienced a headache on the <code>headache_severity</code> field (Yes or No).
3. List any medication you took for your headache in the <code>medication</code> field.
4. If you took medication, specify the start time in the <code>medication_start_time</code> field.
5. If you experienced an aura, specify the start time in the <code>aura_start_time</code> field.
6. Describe the location of your pain in the <code>pain_location</code> field.
7. Describe your pain in the <code>pain_description</code> field.
8. If you experienced a headache, specify its duration in the <code>duration</code> field.
9. If you experienced an aura, describe it in the <code>aura_description</code> field.
10. If you experienced an aura, specify its frequency in the <code>aura_frequency</code> field.
11. Indicate whether you experienced nausea on the <code>nausea</code> field (Yes or No).
12. Indicate whether you were sensitive to light on the <code>sensitivity_to_light</code> field (Yes or No).
13. Indicate whether you were sensitive to sound on the <code>sensitivity_to_sound</code> field (Yes or No).
14. Indicate whether you were sensitive to smell on the <code>sensitivity_to_smell</code> field (Yes or No).
15. Indicate whether you experienced photophobia on the <code>photophobia</code> field (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **headache_date** (Date): This field is for the date of your headache.
* **headache_severity** (Yes/No): Indicate whether you experienced a headache (Yes or No).
* **medication** (Text): List any medication you took for your headache.
* **medication_start_time** (Time): If you took medication, specify the start time.
* **aura_start_time** (Time): If you experienced an aura, specify the start time.
* **pain_location** (Text): Describe the location of your pain.
* **pain_description** (Text): Describe your pain.
* **duration** (Number): If you experienced a headache, specify its duration.
* **aura_description** (Text): If you experienced an aura, describe it.
* **aura_frequency** (Number): If you experienced an aura, specify its frequency.
* **nausea** (Yes/No): Indicate whether you experienced nausea.
* **sensitivity_to_light** (Yes/No): Indicate whether you were sensitive to light.
* **sensitivity_to_sound** (Yes/No): Indicate whether you were sensitive to sound.
* **sensitivity_to_smell** (Yes/No): Indicate whether you were sensitive to smell.
* **photophobia** (Yes/No): Indicate whether you experienced photophobia.
