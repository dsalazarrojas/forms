# Community Health Program Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about participants in the community health program. Completing this form will help us better understand your needs and provide personalized support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have all the required information readily available.
2. Enter your full name as it appears on your records.
3. Enter your date of birth for identification purposes.
4. If available, enter your current weight in kilograms and your current blood pressure reading (systolic over diastolic if known).
5. Describe your primary health concern or reason for visiting the program.
6. Select any symptoms you are experiencing from the list provided.
7. If applicable, list your current medications and their dosages.
8. Agree to receive services from the program by selecting "True" in the consent section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant name** (`participant_name`, text, required): This field is used to collect your full name as it appears on your records.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): This field is for identification purposes, please enter your birthdate in a standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): If you have a mobile or landline number, please enter it here. This will help us contact you in case of any updates or follow-ups.
* **Current weight in kilograms** (`current_weight_kg`, number, optional): If you know your current weight in kilograms, please enter it here.
* **Current blood pressure reading** (`current_blood_pressure`, text, optional): If you know your blood pressure reading, please enter it here, using the systolic over diastolic format if known.
* **Primary health concern today** (`primary_concern`, text, required): Describe your primary reason for visiting the program or your current health concern.
* **Symptoms present** (`symptoms_present`, select multiple, optional): Select any symptoms you are experiencing from the list provided.
* **Current medications** (`medication_list`, text, optional): If you are taking any medications, please list them here along with their dosages.
* **Consent to receive services** (`consent_to_care`, select one, required): Agree to receive services from the program by selecting "True".
