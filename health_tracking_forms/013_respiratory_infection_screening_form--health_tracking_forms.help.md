# Respiratory Infection Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to screen patients for respiratory infections and aid in early detection and diagnosis.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter patient information (Page 1: Patient Information)
	* Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and other relevant details.
2. Describe the patient's symptoms (Page 2: Symptoms)
	* Provide a detailed description of the patient's symptoms, such as cough, fever, or difficulty breathing.
3. Record the patient's respiratory history (Page 3: Respiratory History)
	* Describe any previous respiratory conditions or allergies the patient has.
4. Document the patient's travel history (Page 4: Travel History)
	* Record any recent travel or international travel within the past 14 days.
5. Report the patient's contact history (Page 5: Contact History)
	* Identify anyone the patient has had close contact with in the past 14 days.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, optional): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and other relevant details.
* **Symptoms** (`patient_symptoms`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the patient's symptoms, such as cough, fever, or difficulty breathing.
* **Respiratory History** (`respiratory_history`, text, optional): Describe any previous respiratory conditions or allergies the patient has.
* **Travel History** (`travel_history`, text, optional): Record any recent travel or international travel within the past 14 days.
* **Contact History** (`contact_history`, text, optional): Identify anyone the patient has had close contact with in the past 14 days.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* If you are unsure about any question, consult with a healthcare professional.
* This form is for informational purposes only and should not be used for diagnostic purposes.
