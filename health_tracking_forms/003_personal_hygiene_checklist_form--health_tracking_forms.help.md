# Personal Hygiene Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on an individual's personal hygiene habits and practices.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully and truthfully to provide accurate information about your personal hygiene habits.
2. Select the correct options for each field, taking into account your actual practices and habits.
3. Fill in the date fields as required to record specific dates for certain practices.
4. Enter numerical values for fields that require frequency information.
5. Select the correct options for fields that offer multiple choices.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Hygiene** (Required: false)
	+ This field is used to take general notes on your personal hygiene habits. You can add any relevant information or comments about your personal hygiene practices.
* **Hygiene Frequency** (Required: true)
	+ This field asks if you practice personal hygiene regularly, and offers options for the frequency of the practice (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, never).
* **Personal Grooming** (Required: true)
	+ This field is used to gather information on your personal grooming practices, such as showering, dressing, using deodorant, etc. Select all applicable options that you practice.
* **Showering** (Required: true)
	+ This field is used to record the frequency and duration of your showering habits. Please enter the last date you showered.
* **Haircare** (Required: false)
	+ This field is used to collect information on your haircare routine, including the frequency of hair washing, hair combing, etc. Enter the frequency of your haircare activities.
* **Skincare** (Required: true)
	+ This field is used to record the time spent on skincare activities, such as applying moisturizer, etc.
* **Toothbrush** (Required: true)
	+ This field is used to gather information on your toothbrushing habits, including the frequency and duration of toothbrushing. Select all applicable options that you practice.
* **Nail Care** (Required: true)
	+ This field asks if you practice nail care, and offers options for the frequency of the practice.
* **Hygiene Frequency Frequency** (Required: false)
	+ This field is used to record the frequency of your practicing personal hygiene.
* **Frequency** (Required: false)
	+ This field is used to collect information on the frequency of your practicing personal hygiene.
* **Hair** (Required: false)
	+ This field is used to record the time spent on hair-related activities.

## Tips

* Please answer each question carefully and truthfully to provide accurate information about your personal hygiene habits.
* If you are unsure about any question, you can take your time to think about your habits before selecting an option or filling in a value.
* If you need help or clarification about any field, feel free to ask for assistance.
