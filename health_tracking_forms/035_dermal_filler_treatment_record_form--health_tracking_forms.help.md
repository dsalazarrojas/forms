# Dermal Filler Treatment Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and track dermal filler treatments for patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the relevant information about the patient's treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Treatment** (`first_treatment`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the treatment, e.g., "First treatment for lip augmentation."
* **Date of Treatment** (`treatment_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the treatment.
* **Time Spent** (`time_spent_treatment`, `text`, required: false): Record the time spent on the treatment.
* **Injection Sites** (`injection_sites`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the areas treated with dermal fillers, such as Forehead, Cheek, Nose, or Lips.
* **Number of Injections** (`num_injections`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of injections used during the treatment.
* **Number of Dermal Fillers** (`num_dermal_fillers`, `number`, required: false): Record the number of dermal fillers used during the treatment.
* **Number of Treatment Sessions** (`num_treatment_sessions`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of treatment sessions.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required: false): Record any additional notes from the practitioner about the treatment.
* **Treatment Notes** (`treatment_notes`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the treatment.
