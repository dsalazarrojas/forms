# Covid 19 Symptom Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you track your symptoms during the COVID-19 pandemic. It is not a medical diagnosis tool and should not be used as such.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each symptom listed below and answer "yes" or "no" to confirm or deny experiencing the symptom.
2. Select "yes" if you are currently experiencing the symptom or have experienced it in the past 24 hours. Select "no" if you are not experiencing the symptom or have not experienced it in the past 24 hours.
3. Move on to the next symptom and repeat the process until you have answered all the symptoms.
