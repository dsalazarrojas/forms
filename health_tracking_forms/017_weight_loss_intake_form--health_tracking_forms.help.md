# Weight Loss Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weight Loss Intake Form is designed to collect essential information from patients or clients who are seeking weight loss services. This form helps health professionals to understand your medical history, weight loss goals, and other relevant details to provide personalized advice and care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" section, including your name, phone number, and email address.
2. In the "Medical History" section, provide a brief description of any medical conditions, allergies, or medical procedures you have undergone or are currently experiencing.
3. In the "Weight Loss Goals" section, describe your weight loss goals and objectives.
4. If you have any medical conditions or allergies, list them in the "Medical Conditions" section.
5. If you are taking any medications, list them in the "Medications" section.
6. If you have any emergency contacts, provide their name and relationship to you in the "Emergency Contacts" section.
7. If you have any medical providers, list their name and contact information in the "Medical Providers" section.
8. In the "Medical Procedure Type" section, select the type of medical procedure or treatment you are seeking.
9. If you are taking any medications, list them in the "Meds 2" section.
10. If you have any allergies, list them in the "Allergies 2" section.
11. Finally, provide your medical professional's name and contact information in the "Medical Professional 2" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info** (contact_info, text, required): Enter your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Medical History** (medical_history, text, required): Describe any medical conditions, allergies, or medical procedures you have had.
* **Weight Loss Goals** (weight_loss_goals, text, required): Describe your weight loss goals and objectives.
* **Medical Conditions** (medical_conditions, text, required): List any medical conditions you have.
* **Meds** (medications, text, required): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies** (allergies, text, required): List any allergies you have.
* **Medical Professional** (medical_professional, text, required): Provide the name and contact information of your medical professional.
* **Emergency Contacts** (emergency_contacts, text, required): List the name and relationship of your emergency contact.
* **Medical Providers** (medical_providers, text, required): List the name and contact information of your medical providers.
* **Medical Conditions 2** (medical_conditions_2, text, required): List any additional medical conditions.
* **Medical Procedure Type** (medical_procedure_type, select_one, required): Select the type of medical procedure or treatment you are seeking.
* **Meds 2** (medications_2, text, required): List any additional medications you are taking.
* **Allergies 2** (allergies_2, text, required): List any additional allergies.
* **Medical Professional 2** (medical_professional_2, text, required): Provide the name and contact information of your medical professional.

Note: The form sections are not necessarily ordered in the order you will complete them. Please complete the sections in the order that is most logical and comfortable for you.
