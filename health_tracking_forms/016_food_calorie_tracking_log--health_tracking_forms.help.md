# Food Calorie Tracking Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Calorie Tracking Log is a form designed to help users monitor and record their daily food consumption, providing a clear and concise way to track calorie intake over time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have all the necessary information ready to be inputted, such as meal names, calorie counts, and any additional notes.
2. Input the date for the meal being tracked.
3. Enter the name of the meal or food item being consumed.
4. Enter the total calories consumed from that meal or food item.
5. Add any relevant notes about the meal or food item, such as any ingredients or cooking methods used.
6. Input the time of day the meal was consumed.
7. Enter any additional user-defined information, such as specific details about the meal or food item.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Date**: The date the meal was consumed. Please input in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Meal Name**: The name of the meal or food item consumed. This can be a specific dish, a food item, or any other relevant detail.
* **Calories**: The total number of calories consumed from that meal or food item. Please input a numeric value.
* **Notes**: Additional information about the meal or food item, such as ingredients, cooking methods, or any other relevant details.
* **Time of Day**: The time of day the meal was consumed. Please input in the standard time format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **User Input 2 1**: Additional user-defined information 1.
* **User Input 2 2**: Additional user-defined information 2.
* **Date 2**: Please note that this field is currently not being used and can be ignored. 

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled out, and the form is not left incomplete.
- The form can be used to track both daily and irregular meals, making it a versatile tool for monitoring your calorie intake.
- Consider using the notes field for any additional information that may be relevant to your tracked meals.
