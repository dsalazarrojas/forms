# Breast Pump Prescription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Breast Pump Prescription Form is a document used by healthcare providers to prescribe breast pumps to patients. It helps healthcare providers to gather information about the patient's breastfeeding needs and preferences, ensuring that they can provide the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's name, date of birth, and medical record number.
2. Select the insurance provider and member ID number (if applicable).
3. Choose the date of the prescription and the date when the patient needs the pump.
4. Select the patient's breastfeeding status and the type of pump that is prescribed.
5. If a specific model of pump is prescribed, enter the model number.
6. Decide whether the pump is to be rented or purchased.
7. If renting, select the rental duration.
8. Choose the medical necessity reason for the prescription.
9. Select any additional accessories that are needed.
10. If known, specify the flange size.
11. Enter any preferred supplier.
12. Enter any special billing instructions.
13. Fill in the name and contact information of the prescribing physician.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Please enter the full name of the patient, as it appears on their medical record.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth to ensure that we have up-to-date information about the patient's medical history.
* **Insurance Provider**: Select the insurance company that the patient is registered with, if applicable.
* **Insurance Member ID**: Enter the patient's insurance member ID number if they have one.
* **Insurance Group Number**: Enter the patient's insurance group number if applicable.
* **Date of Prescription**: Please enter the date of the prescription, which is today's date.
* **Date Pump Needed By**: Choose the date when the patient needs the pump, which may be a future date.
* **Current Breastfeeding Status**: Select the patient's current breastfeeding status, which can be:
	+ Exclusively breastfeeding
	+ Combination breast and formula
	+ Exclusively pumping
	+ Recently started breastfeeding
	+ Returning to work
* **Type of Pump Prescribed**: Select the type of pump that is prescribed, which can be:
	+ Single electric pump
	+ Double electric pump
	+ Hospital-grade pump
	+ Portable pump
	+ Manual pump
	+ Wearable pump
* **Specific Pump Model**: If a specific model is prescribed, enter the model number here.
* **Rental or Purchase**: Decide whether the pump is to be rented or purchased.
* **Rental Duration**: If renting, select the rental duration in months:
	+ 1 month
	+ 2 months
	+ 3 months
	+ 6 months
	+ 12 months
* **Medical Necessity Reason**: Select the reason for the prescription, which can be:
	+ Infant unable to breastfeed
	+ Maternal separation from infant
	+ Insufficient milk supply management
	+ Maternal health condition
	+ Returning to work or school
	+ Exclusive pumping choice
	+ Other
* **Accessories to Include**: Select any additional accessories that are needed, which can be:
	+ Extra bottles
	+ Flanges - different sizes
	+ Storage bags
	+ Cooler bag
	+ Replacement tubing
	+ Cleaning kit
	+ Hands-free bra
	+ All standard accessories
* **Flange Size**: If known, enter the flange size that is prescribed.
* **Preferred Supplier**: Enter any preferred supplier.
* **Billing Information**: Enter any special billing instructions.
* **Prescribing Physician Name**: Fill in the name of the healthcare provider who is prescribing the pump.
* **Physician Contact Information**: Enter the contact information of the prescribing physician.
* **Clinic or Practice Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare facility where the patient is being treated.
* **Special Instructions**: Enter any additional notes or instructions that are necessary.
