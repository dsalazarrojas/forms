# Dietary Analysis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your dietary analysis and preferences. The information collected will be reviewed by a healthcare professional to provide tailored guidance and support for your dietary needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read the form carefully and provide accurate and detailed information.
2. Answer all the required fields.
3. If a field is not applicable to you, please leave it blank.
4. If you are unsure about a field, please ask for clarification before proceeding.
5. Review the entire form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the Dietary Analysis Form**: This is the introduction to the form.
* **Food Preferences**: This field is used to collect your food preferences. Please select one of the options that best describes your eating habits.
	+ Appetizing: You find food appealing and enjoyable to eat.
	+ Average: You don't have any specific preferences for food.
	+ Fussy: You have specific preferences for food and tend to be picky.
	+ Not particular: You don't have any specific preferences for food.
	+ Picky: You tend to be picky about food.
	+ Selective: You have specific preferences for food but tend to be more open to trying new things.
	+ Somewhat picky: You tend to be slightly picky about food.
	+ Very picky: You are extremely picky about food.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: This field is used to collect any dietary restrictions you may have. Please select all that apply.
	+ Gluten-free
	+ Lactose intolerant
	+ None
	+ Other
	+ Vegetarian
	+ Vegan
* **Daily Intake**: Please enter the average amount of food you consume each day.
* **Daily Calories**: Please enter the average amount of calories you consume each day.
* **Physical Activity**: This field is used to collect information about your physical activity level. Please enter a brief description of your daily physical activity.
* **Health Goals**: This field is used to collect information about your health goals. Please select one of the options that best describes your goals.
	+ Weight loss
	+ Weight gain
	+ Weight maintenance
	+ Health maintenance
	+ Other
* **Health Concerns**: This field is used to collect any health concerns you may have. Please enter a brief description.
* **Medical Conditions**: This field is used to collect information about any medical conditions you may have. Please enter a brief description.
* **Medication**: This field is used to collect information about any medication you may be taking. Please enter a brief description.
* **Notes**: This field is used to collect any additional information you would like to share with the healthcare professional. Please enter a brief description.
* **Next Appointment**: This field is used to collect the date and time of your next appointment with a healthcare professional. Please enter the date in the format mm/dd/yyyy and the time in 12-hour format.
* **Next Appointment Time**: This field is used to collect the time of your next appointment with a healthcare professional. Please enter the time in 12-hour format.
* **Contact Info**: This field is used to collect your contact information. Please enter your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Follow-up Info**: This field is used to collect information about how you would like to be contacted for follow-up appointments. Please enter a brief description.
* **Submitted By**: This field is used to collect the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted To**: This field is used to collect the name of the healthcare professional receiving the form.
* **Submission Date**: This field is used to collect the date the form was submitted. Please enter the date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Submission Time**: This field is used to collect the time the form was submitted. Please enter the time in 12-hour format.

## Tips
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask for clarification.
* If you are having trouble filling out the form, contact the healthcare professional for assistance.
* The form will be kept private and confidential.
* The information collected will only be reviewed by healthcare professionals.
