# Personalized Activity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personalized Activity Survey form is designed to gather your physical activity preferences and habits to create a personalized schedule that suits your needs and lifestyle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each page carefully, making sure to fill out the required fields first.
2. Start on the "Activity Preferences" page, where you will select your preferred activities. Choose one or more from the options available (Running, Hiking, Biking).
3. On the "Time" page, enter the time of day you prefer to perform each activity. You can choose a specific time or leave it blank if you don't have a preference.
4. On the "Frequency" page, select how often you would like to perform each activity. Choose from Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
5. On the "Availability" page, select which days of the week you are available to perform each activity. Choose multiple days if applicable.
6. On the "Duration" page, enter the duration of each activity in minutes.
7. On the final page, confirm whether you want to end your session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Activity Preferences** (`activity_page`, select_one, Required): Select one or more activities you enjoy performing.
* **Time** (`time_page`, time, Optional): Enter the time of day you prefer to perform each activity. If no preference, leave it blank.
* **Frequency** (`frequency_page`, select_multiple, Optional): Select how often you want to perform each activity. Choose from Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Availability** (`availability_page`, select_multiple, Optional): Select the days of the week you are available to perform each activity.
* **Duration** (`duration_page`, number, Optional): Enter the duration of each activity in minutes.
* **End** (`end_page`, select_one, Optional): Confirm whether you want to end your session.
* **Note** (`note_page`, note, Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes you have about your preferences.

## Tips
* Please fill out each field accurately and honestly to get the most out of your personalized schedule.
* Make sure to select all required fields before proceeding to the next page.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this guide for clarification.
* Don't hesitate to add any additional comments or notes to help us better understand your preferences.
