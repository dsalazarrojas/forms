# Headache Diary Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Headache Diary Form is a tool for patients to record and track their headache experiences. It helps healthcare professionals gather accurate information about headache symptoms, triggers, treatment, medication, frequency, and other relevant details to provide effective care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions truthfully and accurately as you experience your headache.
2. Be specific about your symptoms, such as their severity, duration, and frequency.
3. Provide details about triggers that may have caused your headache, including any environmental or lifestyle factors.
4. Describe any treatments you have received or are currently taking.
5. Record the dates and times of your headaches, including the frequency and severity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Symptoms** (`patient_symptoms`): Describe your current headache symptoms, including any pain, nausea, or sensitivity to light and sound. This will help healthcare professionals understand the severity and impact of your headache.
* **Triggers** (`trigger`): Check "Yes" if you think your headache was triggered by something specific, such as stress, lack of sleep, or certain foods. Check "No" if you're unsure or don't think your headache was triggered.
* **Treatment** (`treatment`): Describe any treatments or medications you have taken for your headache, including dosages and frequencies.
* **Medication** (`medication`): Record any medications you are currently taking or have taken for your headache.
* **Headache Frequency** (`headache_frequency`): Check "Yes" if you experience frequent or recurring headaches, and "No" if they are sporadic.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional comments or details about your headache experience, such as triggers, symptoms, or treatments that haven't been covered elsewhere on the form.
* **Email** (`email_address`): This is not a required field for your personal use, but if you provide it, healthcare professionals can contact you for further questions or updates.
* **Phone** (`phone_number`): Like "Email", this is not a required field for your personal use, but if you provide it, healthcare professionals can contact you for further questions or updates.
* **Date of Last Headache** (`date_of_last_headache`): Record the date and time of your last headache, including the day and year.
* **Time of Last Headache** (`time_of_last_headache`): Record the time of day your last headache occurred.
