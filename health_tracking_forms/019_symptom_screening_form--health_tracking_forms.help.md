# Symptom Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Symptom Screening Form is a comprehensive tool designed to collect relevant information from patients or individuals about their medical history, current symptoms, allergies, medical conditions, and family history. This form is intended to assist healthcare professionals in gathering accurate and complete data for diagnosis and treatment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Enter relevant information about your symptoms, medical history, allergies, medications, and family history.
3. Review your answers carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
4. Submit the form to the healthcare provider for review and further guidance.
