# pre_op_anesthetic_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect pre-operative information related to a patient's anesthesia assessment. It helps healthcare providers gather the necessary details to ensure a safe and successful surgery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all necessary patient information and medical records readily available.
* Review each field carefully and answer the questions as accurately as possible.
* If you are unsure about any information, do not hesitate to consult with the patient's healthcare provider or other experts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient History** (`patient_history`): This field is a text box where you will record the patient's general medical history, including previous illnesses, allergies, and other relevant information.
* **Allergy Information** (`allergy_information`): Choose the correct option that best describes the patient's allergy information. If the patient has multiple allergies, select all relevant options.
* **Medication History** (`medication_history`): Enter the patient's current and past medications, including any medications that may interact with anesthesia.
* **Anesthesia/Medication Allergies** (`anesthesia_medication_allergies`): List any known allergies to anesthesia or medications that may be involved in the procedure.
* **Surgical History** (`surgical_history`): Describe the patient's past surgical experiences, including any complications or concerns.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`): Select all medical conditions that may affect the patient's anesthesia, such as diabetes or hypertension.
* **Anesthesia History** (`anesthesia_history`): If the patient has a history of anesthesia, select the relevant options. This information is crucial for the anesthesiologist.
* **Anesthetic Agents** (`anesthetic_agents`): Enter any medications or substances that the patient is currently taking that may interact with anesthesia.

## Tips
* Always double-check your answers with the patient's healthcare provider if you are unsure about any information.
* Be thorough and accurate when filling out this form to ensure the best possible outcome for the patient.
