# Drug Test Employer Account Set Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from employers for setting up a drug test account. It will help us understand your company's needs and preferences for drug testing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review and verify the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employer Name**: Enter your company's name as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
* **Address**: Enter your company's address where you would like us to send invoices and any other communication.
* **Email**: Enter your company's email address where you would like to receive communication from us.
* **Phone**: Enter your company's phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the person responsible for managing your account.
* **Account Type**: Select the type of account that best suits your company's needs. Please note that this selection will determine the features and benefits you will have access to.
* **Payment Terms**: Enter any payment terms or conditions that you would like to include in your account setup.
* **Payment Frequency**: Select how often you would like to make payments to us.
* **Payment Terms Date**: Enter the date when you would like payments to start.
* **Payment Terms Time**: Enter the time when you would like payments to be made.
* **Drug Test Frequency**: Select how often you would like to schedule drug tests for your employees.
* **Payment Frequency Hint**: Add any additional information or notes about your payment frequency.
* **Test Frequency**: Select how often you would like to have your employees tested for drugs.
* **Test Frequency Hint**: Add any additional information or notes about your test frequency.
* **Payment Terms Description**: Enter any additional description of your payment terms.
* **Payment Terms Note**: Add any additional notes or information about your payment terms.
* **Payment Terms Address**: Enter the address where you would like to receive payment invoices.
* **Payment Terms Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for payment inquiries.
* **Payment Terms Email**: Enter your email address where you would like to receive payment notifications.
* **Payment Terms Fax**: Enter your fax number where you can receive payment notices.
* **Test Type**: Select the type of test that you would like to have your employees tested for.
* **Drug Test Type**: Select the type of drug test that you would like to have your employees tested for.
* **Payment Terms Terms**: Enter any additional terms or conditions related to payments.
* **Payment Terms Terms Hint**: Add any additional information or notes about your payment terms.

Please note that some fields are not required, but it's recommended to fill them out for the best account setup experience. If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us.
