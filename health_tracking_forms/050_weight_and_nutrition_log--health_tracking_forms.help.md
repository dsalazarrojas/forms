# Weight And Nutrition Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps users track their weight and nutrition progress, providing an organized way to record weight measurements, food intake, exercises, and target dates and times.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, navigate through the pages by clicking on the "Next" and "Previous" buttons.
2. Select the relevant page for the information you want to provide (Weight Log, Food Log, Exercise Log).
3. Fill in the required fields for that page, and add any additional notes or comments if desired.
4. Repeat the process until you have filled out all the required fields for each page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Weight Log** (`1`, `note`, required: false): This field is for recording a note about your weight, such as a weight measurement or a description of your weight changes.
* **Food Log** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check "Yes" to indicate that you ate food on the selected date, and "No" if you did not eat food on that date.
* **Exercise Log** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check "Yes" to indicate that you performed exercises on the selected date, and "No" if you did not perform exercises on that date.
* **Weight Log** (`4`, `number`, required: false): Enter your weight measurement for the selected date.
* **Goal Weight** (`5`, `number`, required: false): Enter your target weight goal.
* **Target Date** (`6`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you want to achieve your target weight.
* **Target Time** (`7`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you want to achieve your target weight.
* **Notes** (`8`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about your weight and nutrition progress.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when recording your weight and nutrition activities.
* Use the form to track your progress over time and adjust your habits accordingly.
* Consult with a healthcare professional for personalized advice on weight and nutrition goals.
