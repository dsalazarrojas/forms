# Daily Health Monitoring Sheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Health Monitoring Sheet is a form designed for patients to provide daily updates on their health status, medical conditions, and physiological measurements. This form helps healthcare professionals stay informed and provide timely interventions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your current health status from the options provided.
2. List any current medications you are taking.
3. Report any symptoms you are experiencing.
4. Enter your current physiological measurements, such as:
	* Temperature
	* Pulse
	* Blood pressure
	* Weight
	* Blood glucose
	* Blood cholesterol
	* Body mass index (BMI)
5. Make sure to fill out all required fields, which are marked as "required".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Daily Health Monitoring Sheet** (`daily_health_monitoring_sheet`, text, not required): This is the title of the form, which is displayed at the top of the page.
- **Health Status** (`health_status`, select_one, not required): Select your current health status from the options provided. This will help healthcare professionals understand your overall well-being.
	+ Options: Active, Inactive
- **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, not required): List any symptoms you are experiencing, such as pain, fatigue, or difficulty breathing.
- **Medication** (`medication`, text, not required): List any medications you are currently taking.
- **Temperature** (`temperature`, number, not required): Enter your current body temperature in degrees Celsius or Fahrenheit.
- **Pulse** (`pulse`, number, not required): Enter your current heart rate in beats per minute.
- **Blood Pressure** (`blood_pressure`, number, not required): Enter your current blood pressure in millimeters of mercury (mmHg).
- **Weight** (`weight`, number, not required): Enter your current weight in kilograms or pounds.
- **Blood Glucose** (`blood_glucose`, number, not required): Enter your current blood glucose level in millimoles per liter (mmol/L).
- **Blood Cholesterol** (`blood_cholesterol`, number, not required): Enter your current blood cholesterol level in milligrams per deciliter (mg/dL).
- **Body Mass Index** (`body_mass_index`, number, not required): Enter your current body mass index (BMI) value.
