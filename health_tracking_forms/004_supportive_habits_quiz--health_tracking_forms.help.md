# Supportive Habits Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supportive Habits Quiz is designed to help you evaluate your daily habits and gain insight into areas that may need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2.  For each question, provide a brief description or answer in the corresponding text field.
3.  Use the space provided to elaborate on your habits and experiences.
4.  Submit the form to review your results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (1, text, optional): Briefly introduce yourself and explain what you hope to achieve by taking the quiz.
* **Habit Score 1** (2, number, optional): Rate your daily habits in terms of frequency and consistency, from 0 (not at all) to 10 (every day).
* **Sleep** (3, text, optional): Describe your sleep patterns, including duration, quality, and consistency.
* **Exercise** (4, text, optional): Detail your physical activity levels, including frequency, type, and duration.
* **Stress** (5, text, optional): Discuss your stress management strategies and how you handle stressful situations.
* **Self-Care** (6, text, optional): Describe your self-care practices, such as meditation, hobbies, or relaxation techniques.
* **Goal Setting** (7, text, optional): Outline your short-term and long-term goals, including specific objectives and deadlines.
* **Mindfulness** (8, text, optional): Explain your approach to mindfulness, including meditation, journaling, or other practices.
* **Goal Achievement** (9, text, optional): Share your methods for achieving goals, such as planning, tracking progress, or accountability partners.
* **Self-Regulation** (10, text, optional): Describe your self-regulation strategies, including emotional awareness, self-compassion, or emotional regulation techniques.
* **Emotional Regulation** (11, text, optional): Discuss your approaches to managing emotions, such as mindfulness, self-reflection, or seeking support.
* **Physical Activity** (12, text, optional): Detail your physical activity levels, including duration, intensity, or frequency.
* **Social Support** (13, text, optional): Describe your social connections, including relationships, support networks, or community involvement.
* **Sleep Aid** (14, text, optional): Explain any sleep aids or strategies you use, such as sleep masks, white noise, or sleep apps.

Note: This guide is intended to serve as a general explanation of each field. Please refer to your specific form instructions or guidance for any field-specific guidance.
