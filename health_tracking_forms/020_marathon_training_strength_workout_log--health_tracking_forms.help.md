# Marathon Training Strength Workout Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marathon Training Strength Workout Log is designed to track your marathon training sessions, providing a systematic way to monitor and analyze your progress.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are in a quiet, comfortable environment with a stable internet connection.
2. Select the session page to begin filling out the form.
3. Fill out each field as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session (Session)**: This is the title of your workout session. Enter a brief description, such as "Morning Run" or "Strength Training Session." This field is optional.
* **Date (Date)**: Enter the date of your workout session in the format "YYYY-MM-DD."
* **Distance (Distance)**: Enter the total distance covered during your workout. This is usually measured in miles (mi) or kilometers (km).
* **Time (Time)**: Report the total duration of your workout, including rest intervals. Use the format "HH:MM:SS" for hours, minutes, and seconds.
* **Pace (Pace)**: Describe the intensity of your workout. Choose "High", "Medium", or "Low" from the provided options.
* **Intensity (Intensity)**: Select the intensity of your workout from "High", "Medium", or "Low." This field will only appear if you selected "High" for Pace.
* **Exercises (Exercises)**: List the specific exercises you performed during your workout. This can include weightlifting, cardio, or any other physical activity.
* **Sets (Sets)**: Report the number of sets you completed for each exercise.
* **Reps (Reps)**: Record the number of repetitions for each exercise.
* **Rest Time (Rest Time)**: Enter the duration of rest time taken between sets or exercises.
* **Notes (Notes)**: Add any additional comments or thoughts about your workout session.

## Tips
- Use this form regularly to track your progress and optimize your training.
- Experiment with different intensities and exercises to find what works best for you.
- Review your notes for insights into your performance and areas for improvement.
