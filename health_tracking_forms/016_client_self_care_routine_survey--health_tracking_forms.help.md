# Client Self Care Routine Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Client Self Care Routine Survey is a form designed to help you reflect on your current health and wellness status, set goals, and identify areas for improvement. This information will help your healthcare provider understand your needs and preferences, enabling them to offer personalized guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Read each question carefully** and answer truthfully to the best of your ability.
2. **Complete each section in order**:
   - Section 1: Client Information (fields 1-5)
   - Section 2: Wellness and Health Goals (fields 6-8)
   - Section 3: Current Self-Care Routine (fields 9-17)
3. **Choose the answers that best fit your situation** for each question.
4. **Don't hesitate to ask for help** if you need clarification on any question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Client Information (fields 1-5)

*   **Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Email Address**: Enter your current email address for communication with your healthcare provider.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for scheduling appointments and follow-ups.
*   **Age Range**: Choose the age range that best fits your current age.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for scheduling appointments and follow-ups.

### Wellness and Health Goals (fields 6-8)

*   **How would you rate your current overall health?**: Choose one of the provided options (Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, Excellent) that best reflects your current health status.
*   **What are your primary health and wellness goals?**: Select all the options that apply to you (Weight Management, Stress Reduction, Improve Sleep, Increase Energy, Build Strength, Improve Flexibility, Better Nutrition, Mental Health, Other).
*   **What are your current health or wellness challenges?**: Describe your current challenges in a few sentences.

### Current Self-Care Routine (fields 9-17)

*   **How often do you exercise?**: Choose one of the options (Not at all, Less than once a week, Once a week, 2-3 times per week, 4-5 times per week, 6-7 times per week) that best fits your exercise frequency.
*   **Types of exercise you do regularly**: Select all the options that apply to you (Walking, Running, Strength Training, Yoga, Pilates, Swimming, Cycling, Sports, Other).
*   **Average hours of sleep per night**: Enter the number of hours you usually sleep each night.
*   **Quality of your sleep**: Choose one of the options (Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, Excellent) that best reflects your current sleep quality.
*   **How often do you eat meals per day?**: Choose one of the options (1-2 times, 3 times, 4 times, 5+ times) that best fits your meal frequency.
*   **How many glasses of water do you drink daily?**: Enter the number of glasses you drink each day.
*   **How would you rate the quality of your diet?**: Choose one of the options (Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, Excellent) that best reflects your current diet quality.
*   **Do you have any dietary restrictions or preferences?**: Describe any dietary restrictions or preferences you have in a few sentences.
*   **Current stress level**: Choose one of the options (Very Low, Low, Moderate, High, Very High) that best reflects your current stress level.
*   **How often do you engage in stress management activities?**: Choose one of the options (Never, Occasionally, Weekly, Several times a week, Daily) that best fits your stress management frequency.
*   **Mindfulness or relaxation practices you use**: Select all the options that apply to you (Meditation, Deep Breathing, Yoga, Journaling, Reading, Music, Nature Time, Other).
*   **Do you have a consistent bedtime routine?**: Choose one of the options (Yes, No, Sometimes) that best fits your bedtime routine.
*   **How much screen time before sleep?**: Enter the number of hours you spend on screens before sleep.
*   **Self-care activities you enjoy**: Describe your favorite self-care activities in a few sentences.
*   **What barriers prevent you from maintaining self-care routines?**: Describe any barriers that prevent you from maintaining self-care routines in a few sentences.
*   **Areas where you would like to improve your self-care**: Describe areas where you'd like to improve your self-care in a few sentences.

## Tips

*   Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
*   Don't worry if you're unsure about any question; the goal is to reflect on your current status and identify areas for improvement.
*   Ask your healthcare provider if you have any questions or need clarification on any question.

This help guide will ensure end users complete the Client Self Care Routine Survey efficiently and effectively.
