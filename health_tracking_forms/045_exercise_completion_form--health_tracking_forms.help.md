# Exercise Completion Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exercise Completion Form is a user-friendly tool designed to collect information from users about their exercise completion. This form is meant to be a simple and efficient way to gather essential details about the user's workout experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **User Input** field with your name or any other relevant information you'd like to share.
2.  Provide details about your **Workout Information** in the respective field.
3.  If you have any additional questions or comments, you can add them in the **Additional Questions** field.
4.  Click the submit button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Input** (text, required): Enter your name or any other relevant information you'd like to share.
    This field is a text input that allows you to provide any necessary details about yourself.
*   **Workout Information** (text, required): Describe your workout experience, including any notable events or achievements.
    This field is designed for you to provide detailed information about your workout.
*   **Additional Questions** (text, optional): If you have any further questions or comments, add them here.
    This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional context or questions you might have.

## Tips
- Be as detailed and honest as possible when filling out the form.
- Ensure that you fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete information is collected.
- If you have any concerns or need further clarification, contact us at form@gic.mx.
