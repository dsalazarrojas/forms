# Pet Sodium Intake Tracker Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Sodium Intake Tracker Form is designed to help pet owners record their pet's sodium intake for a day. This form helps gather essential information on the pet's sodium consumption from different sources, pet's health status, and owner contact details.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill in the required fields with accurate information about your pet's sodium intake.
2.  Ensure that all fields with a star \* are mandatory.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **morning_sodium_intake** (number, required): This field is used to record the amount of sodium your pet consumed during the morning. Enter the quantity of sodium in a specific unit (e.g., milligrams).
*   **sodium_source** (select_one, required): Select from the available options where the sodium intake comes from: food, water, or treats.
*   **afternoon_sodium_intake** (number, required): This field is used to record the amount of sodium your pet consumed during the afternoon.
*   **sodium_notes** (note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes related to your pet's sodium intake.
*   **comments** (text, optional): Add any additional information about your pet's health or behavior.
*   **owner_name** (text, required): Enter your name as the pet owner.
*   **vet_clinics** (select_multiple, optional): Select the vet clinics or locations where your pet visits. 
*   **pet_name** (text, required): Enter your pet's name.
*   **date** (date, required): Enter the date of the sodium intake recording.
*   **time** (time, required): Enter the time of the sodium intake recording.
*   **email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
*   **phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
*   **pet_weight** (number, required): Record your pet's weight in a specific unit.
*   **pet_age** (number, required): Enter your pet's age.
*   **pet_breed** (text, optional): Select the pet's breed.
*   **pet_diet** (text, optional): Specify your pet's dietary restrictions or preferences.
*   **sodium_limit** (text, optional): Enter your pet's sodium limit if applicable.
*   **pet_gender** (select_multiple, required): Choose from the options female or male to specify your pet's gender.
*   **pet_health_status** (select_multiple, required): Select your pet's health status from normal, overweight, or obesity.
*   **pet_health_condition** (text, optional): Provide any health condition information about your pet.
*   **pet_health_problem** (select_multiple, optional): Select any health problems your pet might be facing, such as normal, heart condition, or kidney condition.
*   **pet_health_issue** (select_multiple, required): Choose from the options normal, joint issue, or kidney issue as your pet's health issue.
*   **comments** (text, optional): Add any additional comments about your pet's health.
