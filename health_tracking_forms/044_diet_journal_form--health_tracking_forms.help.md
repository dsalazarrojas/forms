# diet_journal_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The diet_journal_form is a health tracking tool designed to help users record and monitor their dietary habits.

## How To Complete This Form
To get started, simply follow these steps:
1. Begin by filling out the User Input section. This is where you can provide any additional context or information about your current health and wellness status.
2. In the Food Items section, select the food items that you have consumed. You can choose from the pre-defined options or add your own custom items.
3. Next, select the appropriate Portion Sizes for the food items you've chosen. This will help track your serving sizes.
4. Record the dates and times you've served the food items. Enter the Serving Dates and Serving Times for each item.
5. If applicable, select the Serving Frequency for the food items. This will help track your eating habits.
6. Add any additional Notes about your food intake, such as any health concerns or special considerations.
7. If you need to contact someone, enter your Email and Phone number.
8. Finally, specify the tool or equipment used for tracking (e.g., a food diary or mobile app) and the person who created the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Input** (user_input): This is a text field where you can provide any additional information about your current health and wellness status.
- **Food Items** (food_items): Select the food items you've consumed. You can choose from the pre-defined options or add your own custom items.
- **Portion Sizes** (portion_sizes): Choose the serving size for each food item from the pre-defined options.
- **Serving Dates** (serving_dates): Enter the date(s) you've served each food item.
- **Serving Times** (serving_times): Enter the time(s) you've served each food item.
- **Serving Frequency** (serving_frequency): Select the frequency at which you served each food item.
- **Notes** (notes): Add any additional information or special considerations about your food intake.
- **Email** (email): Enter your Email address for contact purposes.
- **Phone** (phone): Enter your Phone number for contact purposes.
- **Assigned Tool** (assigned_tool): Specify the tool or equipment used for tracking (e.g., a food diary or mobile app).
- **Created By** (created_by): Specify the person who created this form.
