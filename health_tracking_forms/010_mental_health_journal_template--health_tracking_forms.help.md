# Mental Health Journal Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to track your mental health progress and goals over time. It helps you identify your daily mood, emotions, and activities that impact your mental well-being. By regularly filling out this form, you can gain valuable insights into your mental health patterns and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Answer each question honestly, using the options provided.
3. Select all applicable answers for each question.
4. Fill out each page (or section) completely before moving on to the next one.
5. When finished, review your answers and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Daily Mood** (`daily_mood`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all days of the week that you experienced a positive or negative mood.
* **Emotions Today** (`emotions_today`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all emotions you experienced today, such as happiness, sadness, or frustration.
* **Feelings Today** (`feelings_today`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all feelings you experienced today, like calmness, anxiety, or irritability.
* **Sleep Duration** (`sleep_duration`, `number`, optional): Report the amount of time you slept last night.
* **Wake Time** (`wake_time`, `time`, optional): Record the time you woke up today.
* **Sleep Quality** (`sleep_quality`, `number`, optional): Rate your sleep quality on a scale of 1-10.
* **Energy Level** (`energy_level`, `number`, optional): Rate your energy level on a scale of 1-10.
* **Exercise Duration** (`exercise_duration`, `number`, optional): Report the amount of time you spent exercising today.
* **Mental Health Progress** (`mental_health_progress`, `select_one`, required): Report your current mental health progress, choosing 'Yes' if you're improving, 'No' if you're not.
