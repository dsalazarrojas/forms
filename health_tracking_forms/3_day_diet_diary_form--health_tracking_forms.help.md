# 3 Day Diet Diary Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 3 Day Diet Diary Form is a tool used to track your dietary habits over a three-day period. This form is designed to help you monitor your eating habits, including the types of food you consume, the approximate times of consumption, and the estimated total calories ingested. Additionally, it tracks your water intake and exercise minutes for each day. This information will be used by registered dietitians to assist with diet planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. For each day, start by selecting the date for that day.
2. Describe the meals and approximate times you consumed food and beverages, including snacks and portion sizes.
3. Estimate the total calories you consumed for each day.
4. Report your water intake in ounces, measured as glasses of 8oz each.
5. Record the amount of exercise minutes you completed for each day.
6. If you wish to share your data with a registered dietitian for guidance on diet planning, select 'True' under the 'Consent to use this diary for diet planning' field.
7. Include any overall notes, reactions, or patterns you observe across the three days.
