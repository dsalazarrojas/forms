# Metabolic Age Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Metabolic Age Assessment form is designed to help individuals assess their biological age based on their physiological characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
*   Enter your correct first name in the "First Name" field.
*   Enter your correct last name in the "Last Name" field.
*   Enter your valid email address in the "Email" field.
*   Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
*   Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
*   Enter your height in centimeters in the "Height" field.
*   Enter your weight in kilograms in the "Weight in kg" field.
*   Select your physical activity level from the options provided in the "Physical Activity Level" field.
*   Select your sleep pattern from the options provided in the "Sleep Pattern" field.
*   Select your diet plan from the options provided in the "Diet Plan" field.
*   Enter your water intake in milliliters per day in the "Water Intake" field.
*   Select your exercise frequency from the options provided in the "Exercise Frequency" field.
*   Select your stress level from the options provided in the "Stress Level" field.
*   Indicate whether you take medication in the "Medication" field.
*   Indicate whether you have a medical condition in the "Medical Condition" field.
*   Indicate whether you have smoking habits in the "Smoking Habits" field.
*   Indicate your alcohol habits in the "Alcohol Habits" field.
*   Select your gender from the options provided in the "Gender" field.
*   Select your ethnicity from the options provided in the "Ethnicity" field.
*   Select your country of birth from the options provided in the "Country of Birth" field.
*   Enter your blood pressure in millimeters of mercury (mmHg) in the "Blood Pressure" field.
*   Enter your body mass index in kilograms per square meter in the "Body Mass Index" field.
*   Enter your body surface area in square meters in the "Body Surface Area" field.
*   Click "Submit" to submit your assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name (1)** (text, required): Enter your correct first name.
*   **Last Name (2)** (text, required): Enter your correct last name.
*   **Email (3)** (email, required): Enter your valid email address.
*   **Phone Number (4)** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
*   **Date of Birth (5)** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
*   **Height (6)** (number, required): Enter your height in centimeters.
*   **Weight in kg (7)** (number, required): Enter your weight in kilograms.
*   **Metabolic Age (8)** (number, required): This field will display the result of your metabolic age assessment.
*   **Physical Activity Level (9)** (select_one, optional): Select how physically active you are:
    +   Sedentary: Little or no exercise.
    +   Lightly Active: Light exercise/sports (e.g., walking/riding a bike, golf, dancing, gardening).
    +   Moderately Active: Moderate exercise/sports (e.g., running, cycling, swimming, tennis).
    +   Highly Active: Hard exercise/sports (e.g., weight training, boxing, rugby).
*   **Sleep Pattern (10)** (select_multiple, optional): Select how well you sleep:
    +   Poor: Sleep less than 6 hours per night.
    +   Fair: Sleep 6-7 hours per night.
    +   Good: Sleep 7-8 hours per night.
*   **Diet Plan (11)** (select_multiple, required): Select your diet plan:
    +   Vegetarian: A diet that includes vegetables and fruits but no meat or fish.
    +   Vegan: A diet that excludes all animal products (meat, fish, dairy products).
    +   Omnivore: A diet that includes both animal and plant products.
*   **Water Intake (12)** (number, required): Enter your water intake in milliliters per day.
*   **Exercise Frequency (13)** (select_one, optional): Select how frequently you exercise:
    +   Low: Less than 1 time per week.
    +   Medium: 1-2 times per week.
    +   High: More than 2 times per week.
*   **Stress Level (14)** (select_one, optional): Select your stress level:
    +   High: Feeling stressed most of the time.
    +   Medium: Feeling moderately stressed.
    +   Low: Feeling less stressed.
*   **Medication (15)** (select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you take any medication:
    +   True: Yes.
    +   False: No.
*   **Medical Condition (16)** (select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you have any medical condition:
    +   True: Yes.
    +   False: No.
*   **Smoking Habits (17)** (select_multiple, optional): Indicate your smoking habits:
    +   True: Yes.
    +   False: No.
*   **Alcohol Habits (18)** (select_multiple, optional): Indicate your alcohol habits:
    +   True: Yes.
    +   False: No.
*   **Gender (19)** (select_one, required): Select your gender:
    +   Male: Male.
    +   Female: Female.
    +   Other: Other.
*   **Ethnicity (20)** (select_one, optional): Select your ethnicity:
    +   Asian: Asian.
    +   Black: Black.
    +   White: White.
    +   Hispanic: Hispanic.
*   **Country of Birth (21)** (select_one, optional): Select your country of birth:
    +   USA: USA.
    +   Canada: Canada.
    +   Mexico: Mexico.
    +   Other: Other.
*   **Blood Pressure (22)** (number, optional): Enter your blood pressure in millimeters of mercury.
*   **Body Mass Index (23)** (number, optional): Enter your body mass index.
*   **Body Surface Area (24)** (number, optional): Enter your body surface area in square meters.
*   **Metabolic Age Result (25)** (text, required): This field displays the result of your metabolic age assessment.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill in all required fields.
*   Be accurate when providing information about your physiological characteristics.
*   Take the assessment seriously and provide honest answers to get accurate results.
*   The result of this assessment will help you understand your biological age better.
*   Discuss the result with a health and wellness professional for further guidance.
