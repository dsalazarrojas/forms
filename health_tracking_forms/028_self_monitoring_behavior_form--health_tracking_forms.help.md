> **Form Purpose**
This form is designed to collect information about an individual's self-monitoring behavior, including overall health, physical activity, sleep duration, mental wellness, eating habits, social connections, stress management, and self-awareness.

> **How To Complete This Form**

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. Fill in the required fields (marked as "Required") with your answers.
3. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose one or multiple options that best describe your behavior.
4. For note fields, type in your answer or note in the space provided.
5. Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

> **Field-by-Field Explanation**

* **Overall Health** (`overall_health`, text, required: false)
	+ Answer this question with a brief description of your current overall health status.
* **Physical Activity** (`exercise_frequency`, text, required: true)
	+ Describe how frequently you engage in physical activity, such as sports, exercise, or other physical activities.
* **Sleep Duration** (`sleep_duration`, number, required: true)
	+ Enter the number of hours you sleep each night.
* **Mental Wellness** (`mental_health`, text, required: false)
	+ Describe your current mental wellness status, such as feelings of happiness, stress, or anxiety.
* **Eating Habits** (`eating_habits`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Choose the option that best describes your current eating habits:
		- Healthy
		- Unhealthy
* **Social Connections** (`social_connections`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Select all the options that apply to your current social connections:
		- Friends
		- Family
		- Colleagues
* **Stress Management** (`stress_management`, text, required: false)
	+ Describe how you manage your stress, such as meditation, exercise, or talking to a friend.
* **Self-Awareness** (`self_awareness`, note, required: false)
	+ Write a brief note about your current level of self-awareness, such as your ability to recognize your thoughts, emotions, and behaviors.

> **Tips**

* Answer honestly and accurately to ensure your data is useful for future reference.
* Review your answers regularly to track changes in your behavior over time.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your healthcare professional for guidance.
