# Hormonal Health Cycle Tracking Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hormonal Health Cycle Tracking Log is a form designed to help women track their menstrual health, including their cycle phase, symptoms, moods, and other relevant metrics. This form is intended for personal use and can help users monitor their bodily changes and identify potential issues.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Open the form and select the "First Day Of Cycle" page.
*   Enter the date of your first day of your menstrual cycle.
*   Proceed to the next page, "Cycle Phase," where you can select the current phase of your cycle: Fertile, Menstrual, or Postmenopausal.
*   Continue to the "Symptoms" page, where you can enter any symptoms you are experiencing, such as cramps, fatigue, or other issues.
*   On the "Moods" page, describe your current mood or emotions.
*   Move on to the "Water Intake," "Exercise Minutes," "Sleep Quality," and "Stress Level" pages, and enter your corresponding data.
*   Finally, on the "First Day Of Last Period" and "Last Period Date" pages, enter the dates of your last menstrual period, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Day Of Cycle** (`First Day Of Cycle`, Number, Required: No): The date of your first day of your menstrual cycle.
*   **Cycle Phase** (`Cycle Phase`, Select One, Required: No): Select the current phase of your cycle: Fertile, Menstrual, or Postmenopausal.
*   **Symptoms** (`Symptoms`, Text, Required: No): Enter any symptoms you are experiencing, such as cramps, fatigue, or other issues.
*   **Moods** (`Moods`, Text, Required: No): Describe your current mood or emotions.
*   **Water Intake** (`Water Intake`, Number, Required: No): Record your water intake in the amount consumed.
*   **Exercise Minutes** (`Exercise Minutes`, Number, Required: No): Enter the duration of your exercise in minutes.
*   **Sleep Quality** (`Sleep Quality`, Number, Required: No): Rate your sleep quality (1-5).
*   **Stress Level** (`Stress Level`, Number, Required: No): Record your stress level (1-5).
*   **First Day Of Last Period** (`First Day Of Last Period`, Date, Required: No): Enter the date of your first day of your last menstrual period, if applicable.
*   **Last Period Date** (`Last Period Date`, Date, Required: No): Enter the date of your last menstrual period, if applicable.

## Tips

*   Be honest and thorough when filling out this form, as accurate tracking can help you better understand your body's changes.
*   Try to maintain consistency in your entries to track patterns and trends in your data.
*   Use this form regularly to monitor your menstrual health and make adjustments as needed.
