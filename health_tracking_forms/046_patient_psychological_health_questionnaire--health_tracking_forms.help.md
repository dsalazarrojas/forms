# Patient Psychological Health Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Psychological Health Questionnaire is a tool designed to assess a patient's mental well-being and psychological health. This form aims to gather information about the patient's mental health status, their current psychological state, and their medication status, providing healthcare professionals with valuable insights to better serve their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select one of the mental health conditions listed on page 1 of the form.
	* Choose the option that best describes your current condition.
	* This will help healthcare professionals understand your current mental health status.
2. Rate your mental health status from "Good", "Fair", or "Poor" on page 2.
	* Choose the option that best describes your current mental health status.
3. Provide a brief description of your current mental state on page 2.
4. Indicate if you are currently taking any medications on page 3.
	* Choose 'True' if you are taking medication, 'False' otherwise.
5. If you are taking medication, enter the type of medication on page 3.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Patient Psychological Health Questionnaire
* **Patient Psychological Health Questionnaire** (`patient_psychological_health_questionnaire`, select_one, required): Select one of the mental health conditions listed to describe your current condition.
* **Mental Health Status** (`mental_health_status`, select_multiple, required): Rate your current mental health status as "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Mental State** (`mental_state`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your current mental state.
### Page 2: Mental Health Status
* **Mental Health Status** (`mental_health_status`, select_multiple, required): Rate your current mental health status as "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Mental State** (`mental_state`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your current mental state.
### Page 3: Medication Status
* **Medication Status** (`medication_status`, select_one, required): Indicate if you are currently taking medication.
* **Medication Type** (`medication_type`, text, optional): If you are taking medication, enter the type of medication you are currently taking.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any concerns or doubts, feel free to discuss with a healthcare professional.
* This form is for assessment purposes only and is not intended to be used as a diagnostic tool.
