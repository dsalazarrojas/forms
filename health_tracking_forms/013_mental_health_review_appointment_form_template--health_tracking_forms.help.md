# mental_health_review_appointment_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to aid in scheduling and documenting mental health reviews. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure proper record-keeping and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form on the designated page (main_page).
- Start with the **mental_health_review** field, where you provide a concise summary of the mental health review.
- Proceed to **appointment_scheduling** to select the appointment date, time, and location.
- Choose the appropriate **healthcare_provider** type (Primary, Secondary, Tertiary, or Quaternary).
- For **review_frequency**, select how often the review is scheduled (Weekly, Bi-weekly, or Monthly).
- If necessary, specify the **review_start_time** and **review_end_time** of the review.
- Finally, add any necessary **appointment_notes**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **mental_health_review** (`mental_health_review`, text, optional): Briefly describe or summarize the mental health review.
- **appointment_scheduling** (`appointment_scheduling`, select multiple, optional): Select the date, time, and location of the appointment.
- **healthcare_provider** (`healthcare_provider`, select one, optional): Choose the type of healthcare provider (Primary, Secondary, Tertiary, or Quaternary).
- **review_frequency** (`review_frequency`, select one, optional): Select how often the review is scheduled (Weekly, Bi-weekly, or Monthly).
- **review_start_time** (`review_start_time`, time, optional): Specify the start time of the review.
- **review_end_time** (`review_end_time`, time, optional): Specify the end time of the review.
- **appointment_notes** (`appointment_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional information about the appointment.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are completed accurately to avoid confusion or errors.
- If a field is not applicable or known, select "Not Applicable" or choose an option that reflects an unknown status.
- Save the form when complete.
