# Daily Exercise Reminder - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Exercise Reminder form is used to track daily exercise activities. It helps users to monitor and record their exercise sessions, providing valuable data for personal fitness tracking and monitoring progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **user name** in the "user name" field. This will help us identify you as the user of this form.
2. Select the date of your exercise session in the "exercise date" field. This will help us track the date of your exercise activities.
3. Choose the time of your exercise session in the "exercise time" field. This is useful for tracking the duration of your exercise.
4. Select the type of exercise you performed in the "exercise type" field. You can choose from multiple options, such as "Yes" and "No".
5. Enter the duration of your exercise in the "workout duration" field. This can be the number of minutes or hours spent on exercise.
6. Record the number of **sets** you completed in the "sets" field. A set is a group of repetitions or cycles of an exercise.
7. Enter the number of **reps** you completed in the "reps" field. A rep is one repetition of an exercise.
8. Record the weight you lifted in the "weight lifted" field. This is the amount of weight you lifted during your exercise session.
9. Add any **notes** about your exercise session in the "notes" field. This is a text area for describing your exercise activities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name here. This will help us identify you as the user of this form.
* **Exercise Date** (`exercise_date`, date, required: false): Select the date of your exercise session. This is the day you performed your exercise.
* **Exercise Time** (`exercise_time`, time, required: false): Choose the time of your exercise session. This is useful for tracking the duration of your exercise.
* **Exercise Type** (`exercise_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type of exercise you performed. You can choose from multiple options.
* **Workout Duration** (`workout_duration`, number, required: false): Enter the duration of your exercise in minutes or hours.
* **Sets** (`sets`, number, required: false): Record the number of sets you completed. A set is a group of repetitions or cycles of an exercise.
* **Reps** (`reps`, number, required: false): Enter the number of reps you completed. A rep is one repetition of an exercise.
* **Weight Lifter** (`weight_lifted`, number, required: false): Enter the weight you lifted during your exercise session.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any notes about your exercise session. This is a text area for describing your exercise activities.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required: false): Select who submitted this form, whether it's you as the user, an assistant, or both.
