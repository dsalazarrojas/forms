# Wrist Stretching Exercise Log - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help you track your wrist stretching exercises. It's a simple tool to log the details of each session, including the date, type, and duration of stretches, and any other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the form to start logging your stretching sessions.
2. Enter the date of each session in the "Stretching Session Date" field.
3. Select the types of stretches performed in the "Stretch Type" field.
4. Enter the duration of each stretch in minutes in the "Stretch Duration" field.
5. Enter the number of sets and reps completed in the "Sets Completed" and "Reps Completed" fields respectively.
6. Choose how often you stretch per week in the "Stretching Frequency" field.
7. Select the time of day for the stretching session in the "Stretch Time of Day" field.
8. Choose the areas of the body stretched during the session in the "Stretch Area" field.
9. Select the level of stretch (mild, medium, high) in the "Stretch Level" field.
10. Add any additional comments about the session in the "Stretch Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Stretching Session Date (1)** (`stretching_session_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the stretching session.
* **Stretch Type (3)** (`stretch_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the types of stretches performed during the session. Examples: Elbow Flexors, Wrist Extensors, Forearm.
* **Stretch Duration (4)** (`stretch_duration`, number, required: false): Enter the duration of each stretch in minutes.
* **Sets Completed (5)** (`sets_completed`, number, required: false): Enter the number of sets completed during the session.
* **Reps Completed (6)** (`reps_completed`, number, required: false): Enter the number of reps completed during the session.
* **Stretching Frequency (7)** (`stretching_frequency`, select_one, required: false): Choose how often you stretch per week. Examples: 1 time, 2 times, 3 times, 4 times, 5 times or more.
* **Stretch Time of Day (8)** (`stretch_time_of_day`, time, required: false): Enter the time of day for the stretching session.
* **Stretch Area (9)** (`stretch_area`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the areas of the body stretched during the session. Examples: Wrist, Elbow, Shoulder.
* **Stretch Level (10)** (`stretch_level`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the level of stretch (mild, medium, high).
* **Stretch Comments (11)** (`stretch_comments`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments about the stretch session.
* **User Comments (12)** (`user_comments`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments from the user.
* **Trainer Comments (13)** (`trainer_comments`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments from the trainer.
* **Follow Up (14)** (`follow_up`, date, required: false): Enter the follow-up date for the next session.
