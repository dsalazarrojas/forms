The purpose of this form is to allow healthcare professionals to track the progress and outcomes of mental health sessions with patients. This form is designed to collect essential information about the patient's progress, treatment plan, and session details. 

The form is intended for use in clinical settings, such as mental health clinics or counseling offices. 

To ensure accurate and comprehensive data collection, please only complete this form for patients who have been seen during a mental health session.

## mental_health_progress_notes_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health progress notes form is a crucial tool for healthcare professionals to document the progress and outcomes of mental health sessions. It helps to ensure that patients receive consistent and high-quality care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant page and click on the "Progress Notes" field. Enter any important notes or observations about the patient's progress during the session.
2. If applicable, select the "Treatment Plan" field to indicate whether the patient has a treatment plan in place. You can also add any notes or comments about the plan.
3. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field. This is an essential field required for accurate identification.
4. Enter the date of the last session in the "Date of Last Session" field using the MM/DD/YYYY format.
5. Finally, select the "Session Outcome" field to report the outcome of the last session. If no outcome is applicable, you can leave this field blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Progress Notes**: Enter any important notes or observations about the patient's progress during the session.
* **Treatment Plan**: Select "Yes" if the patient has a treatment plan in place, and "No" otherwise. You can add any notes or comments about the plan.
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name in this field. This is required for accurate identification and tracking.
* **Date of Last Session**: Enter the date of the last session using the MM/DD/YYYY format. This is an essential field for tracking progress over time.
* **Session Outcome**: Select the outcome of the last session. If the patient has shown improvement, select "Yes". Otherwise, select "No" if the session was inconclusive or not applicable.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and thoroughly for each session. Inconsistent or incomplete data can compromise the quality of care provided to the patient.
* If you have any concerns or questions about completing this form, please reach out to your supervisor or clinical team.
