# mar_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for recording medication administration data. It gathers information about patients, medications, and the administration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's name in the "patient name" field.
2. Enter the name of the medication administered.
3. Provide the dosage and start time of the medication.
4. Specify the route and frequency of administration.
5. Enter the date the medication was given.
6. Add any relevant notes.
7. Enter the patient's ID and the form submitter's name.
8. Enter the date the form was submitted.
9. Optionally, provide the facility and medical department names.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (patient_name)**: Enter the name of the patient who received the medication.
* **Medication Name (medication_name)**: Enter the name of the medication administered.
* **Dosage Administered (dosage_administered)**: Enter the dosage of the medication given to the patient.
* **Start Time (start_time)**: Enter the time the medication was first administered.
* **Route Administered (route_administered)**: Enter the route through which the medication was administered (e.g., orally, intravenously).
* **Frequency Administered (frequency_administered)**: Check if the medication was administered at the correct frequency.
* **Date Given (date_given)**: Enter the date the medication was given to the patient.
* **Note (note)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the medication administration.
* **Patient ID (patient_id)**: Enter the patient's ID number.
* **Form Submitter (form_submitter)**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submit Date (submit_date)**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Facility Name (facility_name)**: If applicable, enter the name of the facility where the medication was administered.
* **Medical Department (medical_department)**: If applicable, enter the name of the medical department responsible for the patient.
* **Medication ID (medication_id)**: Enter the ID number of the medication.
* **Dose Administered (dose_administered)**: Enter the dose of the medication administered.
* **Time Administered (time_administered)**: Enter the time the medication was administered.
* **Date Completed (date_completed)**: Enter the date the medication administration process was completed.

## Tips

* Ensure all fields are accurately filled in before submitting the form.
* Double-check the medication name, dosage, and frequency to ensure accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any information, please consult the relevant medical staff.
