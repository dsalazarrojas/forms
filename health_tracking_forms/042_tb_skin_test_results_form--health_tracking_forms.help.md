# tb_skin_test_results_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The tb_skin_test_results_form is a tool used for recording and storing test results for a patient's skin condition. It helps health professionals document and track patient data for quality control and follow-up purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the test results for the patient, providing a clear and concise description of the condition.
2. Enter the date and time of the test.
3. Record the name and contact information of the physician who performed the test.
4. Enter the name and address of the facility where the test was conducted.
5. Record any lab results or other relevant test data.
6. Provide an interpretation or summary of the test results.
7. Enter any follow-up instructions or next steps for the patient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Test Results** (text): Enter a clear and concise description of the patient's skin condition test results.
* **Test Date** (date): Enter the date when the test was conducted.
* **Test Time** (time): Enter the time when the test was conducted.
* **Physician** (text): Enter the name and contact information of the physician who performed the test.
* **Facility** (text): Enter the name and address of the facility where the test was conducted.
* **Lab Results** (text): Enter any lab results or other relevant test data.
* **Interpretation** (text): Provide a summary or interpretation of the test results.
* **Follow Up** (text): Enter any follow-up instructions or next steps for the patient.
