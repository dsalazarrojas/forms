# eye-prescription-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from patients about their eye health and prescription status for new patient consultations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the "Patient Info" section.
2. Answer the question about your prescription status in the "Prescription Medications" section.
3. Provide your prescription schedule in the "Prescription Schedule" section.
4. Enter any relevant information about your eye doctor in the "Eye Doctor Info" section.
5. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Prescription Notes" section.
6. Share any relevant comments or observations about your condition in the "Patient Comments" section.
7. Enter any relevant comments or observations from your doctor in the "Doctor Comments" section.
8. Provide your medical history in the "Medical History" section.
9. Enter your prescription number (if applicable) in the "Prescription Number" section.
10. Record the date of your refraction in the "Refraction Date" section.
11. Answer the question about your eye condition in the "Eye Condition" section.
12. Select the type of prescription you have (if applicable) in the "Prescription Type" section.
13. Enter your email address in the "Patient Email" section.
14. Enter your phone number in the "Patient Phone" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info**: Enter your name, date of birth, and other relevant contact details.
* **Prescription Medications**: Please answer whether you are taking any prescription medications.
* **Prescription Schedule**: Enter the schedule of your prescription, including dates and dosages.
* **Eye Doctor Info**: Enter the name and contact information of your eye doctor.
* **Prescription Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your prescription.
* **Patient Comments**: Share any additional comments or observations about your eye condition.
* **Doctor Comments**: Enter any comments or observations from your doctor.
* **Medical History**: Enter any relevant medical history that may impact your eye health.
* **Prescription Number**: Enter your prescription number, if applicable.
* **Refraction Date**: Record the date of your last refraction.
* **Eye Condition**: Answer whether you have any known eye conditions.
* **Prescription Type**: Select the type of prescription you have, if applicable.
* **Patient Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Patient Phone**: Enter your phone number.

## Tips
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with your eye doctor or healthcare provider.
- If you have any additional comments or concerns, please enter them in the "Patient Comments" or "Doctor Comments" section.
- Please ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information in all fields.
- If you need help with any of the fields, please contact your eye doctor or healthcare provider.
