# Treatment Plan Development Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Treatment Plan Development Form is designed to collect and document patient information for treatment planning purposes.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter patient information on page 1, including name, birthday, and medical history.
2. Select the relevant diagnosis from the provided options.
3. Enter any relevant symptoms the patient is experiencing.
4. Document the patient's medical history.
5. Schedule the next appointment for the patient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient, as it should appear on the treatment plan.
* **Patient Birthday**: Enter the patient's birthday in the date format (e.g., 12/31/1999).
* **Treatment Plan**: Enter a brief description of the proposed treatment plan.
* **Diagnosis**: Choose the most accurate diagnosis from the provided options.
* **Symptoms**: List any symptoms the patient is experiencing, in order to provide a comprehensive treatment plan.
* **Medical History**: Enter any relevant information about the patient's medical history.
* **Next Appointment**: Schedule the next appointment date and time for the patient.

# Tips
- Ensure accuracy when entering patient information for the sake of the treatment plan.
- Double-check the diagnosis options to select the most relevant one.
- Be concise when documenting the patient's medical history.
- Schedule the next appointment in accordance with the patient's needs and the treatment plan.
