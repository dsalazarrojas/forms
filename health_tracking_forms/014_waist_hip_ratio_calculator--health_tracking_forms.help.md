# Waist Hip Ratio Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Waist Hip Ratio Calculator is a tool designed to help users understand their risk level associated with their waist to hip ratio (WHR) and hip to waist ratio (HWB). This form is intended for general awareness of body measurements and is not a diagnostic tool.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your height in centimeters (cm) in the "height" field.
2. Enter your weight in kilograms (kg) in the "weight" field.
3. Enter your hip circumference in centimeters (cm) in the "hip" field.
4. Enter your waist circumference in centimeters (cm) in the "waist" field.
5. Select the calculation type you would like to perform:
	* Waist to Hip Ratio
	* Hip to Waist Ratio
	* Waist to Hip
6. Review your results in the "Results" field, which will display your risk level based on your input values.
7. Optionally, enter your health risk in the "Health Risk" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **height**: Enter your height in centimeters (cm).
* **weight**: Enter your weight in kilograms (kg).
* **hip**: Enter your hip circumference in centimeters (cm).
* **waist**: Enter your waist circumference in centimeters (cm).
* **Calculation**: Select the calculation type you would like to perform:
	* Waist to Hip Ratio: Calculate your WHR using the formula: WHR = (waist circumference / hip circumference) x 100.
	* Hip to Waist Ratio: Calculate your HWB using the formula: HWB = (hip circumference / waist circumference) x 100.
	* Waist to Hip: Calculate the difference between your waist and hip circumferences.
* **Results**: Review your risk level based on your input values.
	+ Low risk: Your WHR/HWB is within a healthy range.
	+ Moderate risk: Your WHR/HWB is slightly above or below the healthy range.
	+ High risk: Your WHR/HWB is significantly above or below the healthy range.
	+ Very high risk: Your WHR/HWB is severely above or below the healthy range.
* **Health Risk**: Enter your health risk level based on your results.
	+ Enter your risk level based on the calculation results.
