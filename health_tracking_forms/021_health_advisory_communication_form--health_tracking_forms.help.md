# Health Advisory Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect health-related information from patients, including medical history, current health status, and follow-up details. The information gathered will help healthcare providers communicate effectively with the patient and provide the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Patient Info**: Fill out the patient's basic contact and demographic information.
2. **Health Status**: Describe the patient's current health status, including any symptoms or concerns.
3. **Medical History**: Provide a brief overview of the patient's medical history, including any past illnesses or allergies.
4. **Medication List**: Select all medications currently being taken by the patient.
5. **Health Advisory**: Describe any health advisories or concerns for the patient.
6. **Follow-up Info**: Provide any necessary follow-up information, such as appointment dates or notes.
7. **Patient Contact**: Enter the patient's contact information, including email and phone number.
8. **Healthcare Provider**: Select the healthcare provider who will be treating the patient.
9. **Medical Team Note**: Enter any additional notes from the healthcare team.
10. **Patient Signature**: Date and sign the form to confirm completion.
11. **Healthcare Provider Signature**: Date and sign the form to confirm review and agreement.
12. **Follow-up Date**: Enter the date of the follow-up appointment.
13. **Follow-up Time**: Enter the time of the follow-up appointment.
14. **Follow-up Location**: Enter the location of the follow-up appointment.
15. **Follow-up Note**: Enter any additional follow-up notes.
16. **Patient Email**: Enter the patient's email address.
17. **Medical Record Number**: Enter the patient's medical record number.
18. **Medical Record ID**: Enter the patient's medical record ID number.
19. **Patient Address**: Enter the patient's address.
20. **Patient City**: Enter the patient's city.
21. **Patient State**: Enter the patient's state or province.
22. **Patient Zip**: Enter the patient's zip code.
23. **Patient Country**: Enter the patient's country.
24. **Patient Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number.
