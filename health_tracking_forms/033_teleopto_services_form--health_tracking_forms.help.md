# Teleopto Services Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track services performed on a teleopto device.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the patient's first name.
2.  Enter the patient's email address.
3.  Enter the patient's phone number.
4.  Enter the patient's name.
5.  Enter the doctor's name.
6.  Enter the doctor's email address.
7.  Enter the doctor's phone number.
8.  Enter the teleopto device ID.
9.  Enter the device model.
10. Enter the device serial number.
11. Enter the date of the service provided.
12. Enter the duration of the monitoring period.
13. Enter the monitoring start date.
14. Enter the monitoring end date.
15. Enter the reason for ending monitoring.
16. Select the current monitoring status.
17. Enter the patient's signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **first_name** (Text, Required): Enter the patient's first name.
*   **email** (Email, Required): Enter the patient's email address.
*   **phone** (Text, Required): Enter the patient's phone number.
*   **patient_name** (Text, Required): Enter the patient's name.
*   **doctor_name** (Text, Required): Enter the doctor's name.
*   **doctor_email** (Email, Required): Enter the doctor's email address.
*   **doctor_phone** (Text, Required): Enter the doctor's phone number.
*   **teleopto_device_id** (Text, Required): Enter the teleopto device ID.
*   **device_model** (Text, Required): Enter the device model.
*   **device_serial_number** (Text, Required): Enter the device serial number.
*   **service_date** (Date, Required): Enter the date of the service provided.
*   **monitor_period** (Text, Required): Enter the duration of the monitoring period.
*   **monitor_start** (Date, Required): Enter the monitoring start date.
*   **monitor_end** (Date, Required): Enter the monitoring end date.
*   **monitor_reason** (Text, Required): Enter the reason for ending monitoring.
*   **monitoring_status** (Select One, Required): Select the current monitoring status.
*   **patient_signature** (Text, Required): Enter the patient's signature.

**Tips**

*   Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
*   Review and update the form regularly to reflect any changes in the patient's condition or services provided. 

Note: The form fields are marked as required for most fields, indicating that they must be completed before the form can be submitted. However, some fields may still be optional and can be left blank if not applicable.
