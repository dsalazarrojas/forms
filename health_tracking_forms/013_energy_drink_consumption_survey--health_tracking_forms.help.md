# Energy Drink Consumption Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Energy Drink Consumption Survey is a form designed to gather information about your energy drink consumption habits. It will help us understand your usage patterns and preferences.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the frequency of your energy drink consumption by choosing one of the options in the "How Frequently Do You Consume Energy Drinks?" dropdown menu.
2. Indicate the amount of energy drinks you consume per week by selecting one or more options in the "How Much Energy Do You Consume Per Week?" dropdown menu.
3. Describe any side effects you experience after consuming energy drinks in the "Side Effects" field.
4. If you experience any other effects, provide a brief description in the "Other Effects" field.
5. Select your age group from the "Age Group" dropdown menu.
6. Choose your income group from the "Income Group" dropdown menu.
7. Enter your occupation in the "Occupation" field.
8. Type your current location in the "Location" field.
9. (Optional) If you are a student, enter your school name in the "School Name" field.
10. (Optional) Enter your current school year in the "School Year" field.
11. (Optional) Select your school level from the "School Level" dropdown menu.
12. Once you've completed all the necessary fields, submit the form to send your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Start**: This is a note field that marks the beginning of the survey. You can ignore it, as it's not used for data collection.
* **How Frequently Do You Consume Energy Drinks?**: Select one of the options to indicate how often you consume energy drinks.
* **How Much Energy Do You Consume Per Week?**: Choose one or more options to show the amount of energy drinks you consume per week.
* **Why Do You Drink Energy Drinks?** : Describe why you consume energy drinks in this field.
* **Side Effects**: Select any side effects you experience after consuming energy drinks.
* **Other Effects**: If you experience any other effects, describe them in this field.
* **Age Group**: Choose your age group from the provided options.
* **Income Group**: Select your income group from the provided options.
* **Occupation**: Enter your occupation.
* **Location**: Type your current location.
* **School Name**: (Optional) If you are a student, enter your school name.
* **School Year**: (Optional) Enter your current school year.
* **School Level**: (Optional) Select your school level from the provided options.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly to provide accurate data.
* If you are unsure about any question, select "No" or "Don't know" if applicable.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
