# family_tree_development - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about family members in a family tree structure. It allows you to capture details about a single family member's name, birth date and place, relationship to you, and other attributes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the first name of the family member in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the last name of the family member in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter the date of birth of the family member in the "Birth Date" field.
4. Type the place where the family member was born in the "Birth Place" field.
5. Select the relationship type of the family member in the "Relation Type" dropdown menu (e.g. Brother, Sister, Parent, Child).
6. Enter the name of the family member in the "Family Member Name" field.
7. Optionally, you can enter the age of the family member in the "Family Member Age" field, if known.
8. Select the gender of the family member in the "Family Member Gender" dropdown menu (e.g. Male, Female, Other).
9. You can choose how you prefer to contact the family member (e.g. Email, Phone, None) in the "Family Member Contact Preference" dropdown menu.
10. Enter any additional notes or comments about the family member in the "Family Member Notes" field, if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter the first name of the family member.
- **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter the last name of the family member.
- **Birth Date** (Birth Date, date, required): Enter the date of birth of the family member in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Birth Place** (Birth Place, text, optional): Type the place where the family member was born.
- **Relation Type** (Relation Type, select_one, required): Select the relationship type of the family member (e.g. Brother, Sister, Parent, Child).
- **Family Member Name** (Family Member Name, text, required): Enter the name of the family member.
- **Family Member Age** (Family Member Age, number, optional): Enter the age of the family member, if known.
- **Family Member Gender** (Family Member Gender, select_multiple, required): Select the gender of the family member (e.g. Male, Female, Other).
- **Family Member Marriage Status** (Family Member Marriage Status, select_one, optional): Select the marriage status of the family member (e.g. Married, Single, Divorced).
- **Family Member Contact Preference** (Family Member Contact Preference, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you prefer to contact the family member (e.g. Email, Phone, None).
- **Family Member Notes** (Family Member Notes, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the family member.
