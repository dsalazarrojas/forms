# Blood Pressure Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Blood Pressure Log Form is designed to collect and record blood pressure readings from patients at various times of the day. This information will be used to help healthcare providers monitor and analyze the patient's blood pressure trends, enabling early detection of potential health issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Patient Name**, which is your identification for the form.
2. Enter your **Patient ID**, if applicable (this is not required).
3. Record the date of your **log entry** (required).
4. For each day part (Morning, Afternoon, Evening), enter your blood pressure readings for:
	* **Systolic (mmHg)**
	* **Diastolic (mmHg)**
	* **Pulse (bpm)**
5. Indicate your **Medications Taken**, if any (optional).
6. Select your **Activity Level** (optional):
	* **Sedentary**
	* **Light**
	* **Moderate**
	* **Vigorous**
7. Rate your **Stress Level** (1-10), with 1 being low and 10 being high (optional).
8. Report your **Sleep Quality**, as:
	* **Poor**
	* **Fair**
	* **Good**
	* **Excellent**
9. Add any **Diet Notes** (optional), such as salt intake.
10. Mention any **Symptoms or Health Issues** (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be recorded in the system.
* **Patient ID**: Enter your patient ID, if provided by your healthcare provider.
* **Log Date**: Enter the date of your log entry.
* **Morning Systolic (mmHg)**, **Afternoon Systolic (mmHg)**, **Evening Systolic (mmHg)**: Enter your blood pressure readings for each day part.
* **Morning Diastolic (mmHg)**, **Afternoon Diastolic (mmHg)**, **Evening Diastolic (mmHg)**: Enter your blood pressure readings for each day part.
* **Morning Pulse (bpm)**, **Afternoon Pulse (bpm)**, **Evening Pulse (bpm)**: Enter your pulse readings for each day part.
* **Medications Taken**: List any medications you are currently taking, if any.
* **Activity Level**: Choose how active or sedentary you were during the day.
* **Stress Level (1-10)**: Rate your stress level, with 1 being low and 10 being high.
* **Sleep Quality**: Report how well you slept the night before.
* **Diet Notes (salt intake, etc.)**: Add any diet-related notes, such as your salt intake.
* **Symptoms or Health Issues**: Report any health issues or symptoms you have experienced.
* **Log Entry Date**: This field is automatically populated with the current date and time.
