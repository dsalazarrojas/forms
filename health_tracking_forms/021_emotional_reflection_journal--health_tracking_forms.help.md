# emotional_reflection_journal_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The emotional reflection journal form is a self-reporting tool used to capture a user's emotional experiences, mood, and reflective thoughts. It is designed to help users monitor and track their emotional states over time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly about your current mood and emotional state.
2. Record your reflections on your emotional experiences and thoughts.
3. Provide details on the frequency, duration, and timing of your reflections.
4. Select any emotions you experienced during the reflection.
5. Rate the intensity of your emotions (if applicable).
6. Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Intro**: Enter a brief introduction or description of your current emotional state.
* **Mood**: Record your current mood using the scale (e.g., 1-10).
* **Intensity**: Rate the intensity of your emotions (if applicable).
* **Reflection**: Write down your reflective thoughts and experiences.
* **Frequency**: Select the frequency of your reflections (e.g., frequently, occasionally, rarely, never).
* **Time of Day**: Record the time of day when you reflected.
* **Duration**: Record the duration of your reflection.
* **Date**: Record the date of your reflection.
* **Emotional State**: Select any emotions you experienced during the reflection.
* **Emotional Intensity**: Rate the intensity of your emotions (if applicable).
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (if provided).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (if provided).

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when completing the form.
* Answer the questions thoroughly and provide as much detail as possible.
* If you experience any difficulties or challenges, please contact us for assistance.
