# Early Pregnancy Self Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Early Pregnancy Self Referral Form is a medical form designed for patients to gather information about their pregnancy journey. This form is meant to collect personal and health-related data that will help healthcare professionals assess the patient's pregnancy status and provide necessary care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

* Ensure you have all necessary contact information (email or phone number) ready to be entered.
* Complete each field accurately and thoroughly, providing the most recent information.
* Review your completed form before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your full name, as it appears on your identification or medical records.
* **Date of Birth**: The date you were born, in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Email**: Your primary email address. Ensure it matches the one you use to access the platform.
* **Phone Number**: Your personal or contact phone number, including area code and phone number.
* **Medical History**: Briefly describe your medical history, including any pre-existing conditions, surgeries, or allergies.
* **Address**: Your current residence address, including street address and apartment number (if applicable).
* **Symptoms**: Describe any symptoms you are currently experiencing that could be related to your pregnancy, such as fatigue, nausea, or pain.
* **Obstetric History**: If applicable, describe any previous pregnancies or deliveries, including dates, number of children, and any relevant medical information related to previous pregnancies.

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate in providing information.
* If unsure about any field, leave it blank and discuss with a healthcare professional.
* If you have any concerns or questions, do not hesitate to contact us at [insert contact info].

By following this guide, you will be able to efficiently and effectively provide necessary information for your healthcare needs during pregnancy.
