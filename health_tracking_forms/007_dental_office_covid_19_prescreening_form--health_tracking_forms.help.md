# dental_office_covid_19_prescreening_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from patients about their COVID-19 status and medical history before visiting the dental office. It's crucial for the office to have accurate and up-to-date information to ensure patient safety and provide necessary care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your accurate and truthful information. Make sure to double-check your entries before submission. If you have any concerns or questions, please contact the dental office staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info** (1, text, required: false): Please provide a brief description of your current symptoms, medical conditions, or any other information you think might be relevant to your visit.
* **Patient Name** (2, text, required: false): Please enter your full name as it appears on your medical records.
* **Email** (3, email, required: false): Please enter your email address so we can contact you for any follow-up questions or concerns.
* **Contact Number** (4, text, required: false): Please provide your contact number so we can reach you if needed.
* **Medical Record Number** (5, number, required: false): If you have a medical record number, please provide it for our records.
* **Date of Birth** (6, date, required: false): Please enter your date of birth for our records.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
* If you're unsure about any information, please ask a staff member for help.
* If you have any medical records or documentation, please bring them along to your visit for our reference.
