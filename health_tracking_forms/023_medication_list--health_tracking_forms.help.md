# medication_list - Help Guide

## Purpose
The medication list form is designed to gather information about a patient's medications, including their names, dosages, start and end dates, and frequencies.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the "Medication List" page. This is the main page for the form.
2. In the "Medication Name" field, enter the name of the medication you are tracking. For example, "Acetaminophen" or "Lisinopril".
3. In the "Dosage" field, enter the prescribed dosage for the medication. For example, "10mg" or "1 tablet".
4. In the "Start Date" field, select the date when the patient started taking the medication. This field is for date format, so use mm/dd/yyyy or m/d/yyyy, or dd mmm yyyy, as you prefer.
5. In the "End Date" field, select the date when the patient stopped taking the medication. Like the "Start Date" field, use a date in one of the supported formats.
6. In the "Frequency" field, enter the frequency at which the medication is taken, e.g., "daily", "weekly", or "as needed".
7. In the "Frequency Unit" field, describe the unit of measurement for the frequency, e.g., "times a week" or "hours a day".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medication Name**: Enter the name of the medication you are tracking. This field is required.
* **Dosage**: Enter the prescribed dosage for the medication. This field is optional.
* **Start Date**: Select the date when the patient started taking the medication. This field is optional.
* **End Date**: Select the date when the patient stopped taking the medication. This field is optional.
* **Frequency**: Enter the frequency at which the medication is taken. This field is optional.
* **Frequency Unit**: Describe the unit of measurement for the frequency. This field is optional.
