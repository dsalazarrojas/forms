# Daily Anxiety Routine Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Anxiety Routine Checklist is a form used to monitor and track changes in anxiety levels over time. It is intended for personal use and should not be used as a diagnostic tool without consulting a mental health professional.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select your current anxiety levels for the day from the options provided.
2. Choose how you are feeling (mood) from the options provided.
3. Rate your sleep quality.
4. Describe any coping strategies you used to manage anxiety.
5. Identify any triggers that may have caused anxiety.
6. Describe your daily anxiety routine.
7. If applicable, provide the name and contact information of your mental health professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **anxiety_levels (High, Low)**: Select the level of anxiety you are experiencing today. This could be High if you are feeling overwhelmed or Low if you are feeling calm.
* **mood (Happy, Sad)**: Choose how you are feeling today. This could be Happy if you are feeling positive or Sad if you are feeling down.
* **sleep_quality (Excellent, Poor)**: Rate your sleep quality for the past night. This could be Excellent if you slept well or Poor if you had trouble sleeping.
* **coping_strategies**: Describe any strategies you used to manage anxiety today. This could include things like exercise, meditation, or talking to a friend.
* **triggers**: Identify any triggers that may have caused anxiety today. This could be a specific situation, person, or thing that contributed to your anxiety.
* **anxiety_routine (Daily Anxiety Routine)**: Describe your daily routine. This could include things like work, social events, or daily activities that may contribute to anxiety.
* **mental_health_professional**: If you have a mental health professional, such as a therapist or counselor, please provide their name and contact information. This is optional but can be helpful in case you need to follow up with them about your progress.
* **Submit**: Click submit to save your progress and continue tracking your anxiety levels over time.

## Tips
* Take this form seriously and try to be as honest as possible when answering the questions.
* Be consistent in your responses to track patterns and trends in your anxiety levels.
* Use this form regularly to monitor your anxiety levels and make adjustments to your daily routine as needed.
* If you notice significant changes in your anxiety levels, consider consulting a mental health professional for guidance.
