# Sleep Tracker Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect data on users' sleep patterns, including sleep start and end dates and times, sleep duration, quality, and frequency.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the title of your sleep tracker form in the **Sleep Tracker Form** field.
2. Select the start date of your sleep period in the **Sleep Start Date** field.
3. Select the start time of your sleep period in the **Sleep Start Time** field.
4. Select the end date of your sleep period in the **Sleep End Date** field.
5. Select the end time of your sleep period in the **Sleep End Time** field.
6. If you want to specify the duration of your sleep period, enter the number of hours slept in the **Sleep Duration** field.
7. If you want to rate the quality of your sleep, enter a number in the **Sleep Quality** field.
8. Add any additional comments or notes about your sleep in the **Notes** field.
9. If you want to specify the frequency of your sleep, select the frequency from the **Sleep Frequency** field.
10. If you want to add any additional comments about your sleep frequency, enter them in the **Sleep Frequency Hint** field.
11. If you want to specify the location where you started sleeping, select the location from the **Sleep Start Location** field.
12. If you want to add any additional comments about the location where you started sleeping, enter them in the **Sleep Start Location Hint** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sleep Tracker Form** (`sleep_tracker_form`): Enter the title of your sleep tracker form.
* **Sleep Start Date** (`start_date`): Enter the start date of your sleep period.
* **Sleep Start Time** (`start_time`): Enter the start time of your sleep period.
* **Sleep End Date** (`end_date`): Enter the end date of your sleep period.
* **Sleep End Time** (`end_time`): Enter the end time of your sleep period.
* **Sleep Duration** (`sleep_duration`): Enter the duration of your sleep period in hours.
* **Sleep Quality** (`sleep_quality`): Rate the quality of your sleep from 1 to 5.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional comments or notes about your sleep.
* **Sleep Frequency** (`sleep_frequency`): Select how often you sleep.
* **Sleep Frequency Hint** (`frequency_hint`): Add any additional comments about your sleep frequency.
* **Sleep Start Location** (`start_location`): Select the location where you started sleeping.
* **Sleep Start Location Hint** (`location_hint`): Add any additional comments about the location where you started sleeping.
