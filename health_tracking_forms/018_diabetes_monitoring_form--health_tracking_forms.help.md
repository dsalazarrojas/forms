# Diabetes Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you track and monitor your diabetes-related health metrics, including blood sugar levels, blood pressure, weight, medication usage, diet, and exercise. Please complete this form to provide detailed information about your current health status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select an answer for each field, based on your current health status.
2. Enter your response for the "Medication Notes" field, as this field is used to provide additional information about your medication usage.
3. Enter your response for the "Diet" field, as this field is used to provide information about your current diet.
4. Enter your response for the "Exercise" field, as this field is used to provide information about your exercise habits.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Blood Sugar Level**: Choose your current blood sugar level. Options are High and Low.
* **Blood Pressure**: Indicate your current blood pressure status. Options are Yes and No.
* **Weight**: Answer this field is optional but helps to monitor weight.
* **Medication Notes**: Enter any additional information about your medication usage, such as dosage, frequency, or any side effects.
* **Diet**: Enter your current diet information, including details about food intake and any dietary restrictions.
* **Exercise**: Enter your current exercise habits, including frequency, duration, and intensity.
