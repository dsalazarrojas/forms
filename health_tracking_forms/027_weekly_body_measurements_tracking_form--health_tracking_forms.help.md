# Weekly Body Measurements Tracking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Weekly Body Measurements Tracking Form is designed to collect data on your body measurements and health status on a weekly basis. This form is meant to help you track your progress over time and provide insights into your physical health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Week Start" field and select the start date of the week for which you are tracking measurements.
2. In the "Body Measurements" field, select the body part that you are tracking measurements for (e.g. Chest, Hips, Waist, etc.).
3. Enter your current measurements for the selected body part.
4. If you have any comments or observations about your measurements, you can type them in the "Comments" field.
5. You can also add any additional comments or notes in the "User Comments" field.
6. Enter your current weight and height if you wish to track these metrics.
7. Select your age and gender from the respective fields.
8. Finally, select the "Next Action" field to specify what you would like to do next with this data (e.g. "Send Report", "No Action").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Week Start (Date)**: This field indicates the start date of the week for which you are tracking measurements. Enter the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Body Measurements (Select One)**: This field allows you to select the body part for which you are tracking measurements. Select from the list of available options (Chest, Hips, Waist, etc.).
* **Measurements (Number)**: Enter your current measurement for the selected body part. For example, if you selected "Chest", enter the current measurement in inches or centimeters.
* **Comments (Note)**: If you have any comments or observations about your measurements, type them in this field.
* **User Comments (Note)**: This is a secondary field for additional comments or notes about your progress.
* **User Comments 2 (Note)**: This is another secondary field for additional comments or notes about your progress.
* **Weight (Number)**: If you want to track your weight, enter it in this field.
* **Height (Number)**: If you want to track your height, enter it in this field.
* **Age (Number)**: Select your current age from the available options.
* **Gender (Select One)**: Select your gender from the available options (Male, Female, Other).
* **Next Action (Select One)**: This field allows you to specify what you would like to do next with this data. Select from the list of available options (Send Report, No Action).
