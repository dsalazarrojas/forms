**MSQ Questionnaire - Help Guide**

**Purpose**
The MSQ Questionnaire is a tool designed to collect information about patients' migraines and their related symptoms, medical history, and treatment experiences. This form aims to gather comprehensive details to help healthcare providers understand and manage patient care effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the MSQ Questionnaire, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in all required fields**: Ensure that you complete all fields marked as "required" to ensure your responses are saved.
2. **Answer each question honestly**: Provide accurate and truthful answers based on your experiences and conditions.
3. **Select options from lists**: Choose the correct options from dropdown lists (e.g., frequency, severity, medical conditions) that best describe your experiences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Pain Frequency** (`pain_frequency`, number, optional): Choose how frequently you experience pain.
* **Migraine Frequency** (`migraine_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select how often you experience migraines.
* **Migraine Severity** (`migraine_severity`, select_one, optional): Choose how severe your migraines are.
* **Disability** (`disability`, number, optional): Enter any disability or impairment related to your migraines.
* **Work Interference** (`work_interference`, select_one, optional): Indicate if your migraines affect your work.
* **Social Activities** (`social_activities`, select_multiple, optional): Check if your migraines affect your social activities.
* **Emotional Impact** (`emotional_impact`, select_one, optional): Choose how much your migraines affect your emotions.
* **Medication Use** (`medication_use`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you use medication for your migraines.
* **Medication Side Effects** (`medication_side_effects`, select_one, optional): Check if you experience side effects from your medication.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_multiple, optional): List any other medical conditions you have.
* **Medical Conditions Details** (`medical_conditions_details`, text, optional): Provide additional information about your medical conditions.
* **Other Conditions** (`medical_conditions_other`, text, optional): Mention any other medical conditions not listed.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact information.
* **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, text, optional): Provide the name of your medical provider.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Describe your medical history.
* **Allergy** (`allergy`, text, optional): List any allergies you have.
* **Medical Test** (`medical_test`, text, optional): Mention any medical tests or exams you've had.
* **Test Result** (`test_result`, text, optional): Report the results of your medical tests.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): Specify any medical condition you have.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, optional): Mention any medication you take.
* **Dosage** (`dosage`, text, optional): Provide your medication dosage.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your migraines or symptoms.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your migraines or symptoms (if applicable).
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, time, optional): Specify the time of day your migraines occur.

By understanding these instructions and explanations, you'll be able to complete the MSQ Questionnaire accurately and effectively.
