# Medication Reconciliation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Medication Reconciliation Form is used to gather information about a patient's current medications for reconciliation and review purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the patient's ID number in the "Patient ID" field.
2. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
3. Enter the name of the medication being taken in the "Medication Name" field.
4. Enter the dosage of the medication in the "Medication Dose" field.
5. Enter the frequency of the medication in the "Medication Frequency" field.
6. Enter the route of the medication in the "Medication Route" field.
7. Enter the start date of the medication in the "Medication Start Date" field.
8. Review and submit the form once all fields are complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, Number, Required): Enter the patient's ID number for quick identification.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the patient's name for reference.
* **Medication Name** (`medication_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the medication being taken.
* **Medication Dose** (`medication_dose`, Number, Optional): Enter the dosage of the medication.
* **Medication Frequency** (`medication_frequency`, Text, Optional): Enter how often the medication is taken (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
* **Medication Route** (`medication_route`, Text, Optional): Enter the route of administration (e.g., orally, topically, intravenously).
* **Medication Start Date** (`medication_start_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date the medication was started.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submission.
* Ensure all medications listed on this form are up-to-date and complete.
* If a medication is not listed, please note this in the comments section and provide an updated list of current medications.
