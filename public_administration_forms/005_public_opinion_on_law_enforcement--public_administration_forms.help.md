# Public Opinion On Law Enforcement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect public opinions on law enforcement, gathering information on trust levels, safety concerns, and suggestions for improvement. Submitters are also required to provide contact information for follow-up or verification purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide a title for your submission.
2. Select your level of trust in law enforcement from the options provided.
3. Choose your perception of safety levels in law enforcement from the options provided.
4. Share your ideas for improvement, if any.
5. Enter your comments, if you have any.
6. Provide your email address for contact purposes.
7. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
8. Provide a note about your submission, if necessary.
9. Enter the date and time of your submission.
10. Enter your IP address (optional).
11. Enter your name.
12. Enter your email address again for verification purposes.
13. Enter your phone number again for verification purposes.
14. Enter your IP address again for verification purposes.
15. Enter any comments about the submission process or law enforcement.
16. Select your agreement or disagreement with the terms and conditions of the submission.
17. Enter any comments about your agreement or disagreement.
18. If you have any other comments, please enter them here.
19. If you have any notes about your agreement or disagreement, please enter them here.
20. If you agree or disagree with the terms and conditions, please enter your reason for your choice.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Public Opinion On Law Enforcement** (`survey_title`, text, required): Provide a title for your submission.
* **Trust Levels** (`trust_level`, select_multiple, required): Select your level of trust in law enforcement from the options provided.
	+ Trust
	+ Distrust
	+ Neutral
	+ Undecided
* **Safety Levels** (`safety_level`, select_multiple, required): Choose your perception of safety levels in law enforcement from the options provided.
	+ Very Safe
	+ Somewhat Safe
	+ Somewhat Not Safe
	+ Not Safe At All
* **Improvement Ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, text, required): Share your ideas for improvement, if any.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments you have.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Contact Other** (`contact_other`, text, optional): Provide any other contact information, if necessary.
* **Submission Review** (`submission_review`, note, optional): Enter any note about your submission, if necessary.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required): Enter the time of your submission.
* **Submission IP** (`submission_ip`, text, required): Enter your IP address for submission.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for verification purposes.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for verification purposes.
* **Submitter IP** (`submitter_ip`, text, required): Enter your IP address for verification purposes.
* **Submitter Comments** (`submitter_comments`, text, required): Enter any comments about your submission.
* **Submitter Agreement** (`submitter_agreement`, select_one, required): Select your agreement or disagreement with the terms and conditions.
* **Submission IP Location** (`submission_ip_location`, text, optional): Provide the location of your IP address, if necessary.
* **Submitter Agreement Comments** (`submitter_agreement_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments about your agreement or disagreement.
* **Submitter Agreement Other** (`submitter_agreement_other`, text, optional): Provide any other comments about your agreement or disagreement.
* **Submitter Agreement Notes** (`submitter_agreement_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes about your agreement or disagreement.
