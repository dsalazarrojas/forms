# HIV Policy Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HIV Policy Feedback Survey is a tool to gather information about the HIV policy's effectiveness and challenges faced by users in different regions. It is a feedback mechanism for public administration officials to inform policy decisions and improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each section carefully and provide your honest opinion.
2. Answer each question in a text box, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Rate the policy effectiveness and implementation challenges on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is "not effective" and 10 is "very effective".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **This is the first question** (Page 1, This is the first question, note, not required): This is a introductory note to explain the purpose of the form.
* **What is your opinion on policy effectiveness in your region?** (Page 2, policy_effectiveness, text, not required): Please provide your honest opinion on the policy's effectiveness in your region.
* **What are the barriers you face when implementing this policy?** (Page 3, implementation_barriers, text, not required): List the challenges you face when implementing this policy.
* **What areas should be improved in this policy?** (Page 4, improvement_priorities, text, not required): Suggest areas that need improvement in the policy.
* **What is your perspective on this policy?** (Page 5, community_perspective, text, not required): Share your personal perspective on the policy.
* **What benefit do you think of this policy's implementation?** (Page 6, policy_implementation_benefit, text, not required): Describe the benefits you think the policy has brought about.
* **What is a regional insight you have from this policy?** (Page 7, regional_insight, text, not required): Share any notable insights you have from this policy.
* **Rate the policy effectiveness** (Page 8, policy_implementation_benefit, number, not required): Rate the policy's effectiveness on a scale of 1-10.
* **Rate the policy implementation barriers** (Page 9, policy_implementation_barriers, number, not required): Rate the challenges faced when implementing the policy on a scale of 1-10.
* **Rate the improvement priorities** (Page 10, improvement_priorities, number, not required): Rate the areas that need improvement on a scale of 1-10.
