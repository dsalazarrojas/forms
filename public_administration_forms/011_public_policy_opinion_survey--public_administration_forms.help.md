# public-policy-opinion-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The public-policy-opinion-survey form aims to gather information on various aspects of public policy. It is not mandatory and is designed to collect feedback from the public in a voluntary manner.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply select the relevant answers for each question and submit. You can skip any questions you don't feel comfortable answering. Make sure to provide accurate and honest information to help us better understand public opinions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your current employment status** (`what-is-your-current-employment-status`, select_one): Please select your current employment status from the options provided.
* **What is your annual income** (`what-is-your-annual-income`, number): If you don't feel comfortable sharing your income, you can skip this question.
* **Which political party do you support?** (`which-political-party`, select_multiple): Select one or more political parties that you support.
* **How old are you?** (`how-old-are-you`, number): Enter your age.
* **Do you agree with the following statement?** (`do-you-agree-with-the-following-statement`, select_one): Select whether you agree or disagree with the provided statement.
* **Additional comments** (`additional-comments`, text): If you have any additional comments or thoughts, please share them here.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions.
* **What is your education level?** (`what-is-your-education-level`, select_one): Select your education level from the options provided.
* **Which form of employment do you have?** (`which-form-of-employment`, select_one): Select your form of employment.
* **How often do you use the internet?** (`how-often-do-you-use-the-internet`, select_one): Select how often you use the internet.
* **Do you own or rent?** (`do-you-own-or-rent`, select_one): Select whether you own or rent your current dwelling.
* **What is your annual housing cost?** (`what-is-your-annual-housing-cost`, number): Enter your annual housing cost.
* **How many hours do you work?** (`how-many-hours-do-you-work`, number): Enter the number of hours you work per week.
* **Do you have a family?** (`do-you-have-a-family`, select_one): Select whether you have a family.
* **How many members in your family?** (`how-many-members-in-your-family`, number): Enter the number of family members.
* **What is your household income?** (`what-is-your-household-income`, number): Enter your household income per year.
* **How many children under 18 in your household?** (`how-many-children-under-18-in-your-household`, number): Enter the number of children under 18 in your household.
* **How many people in your household?** (`how-many-people-in-your-household`, number): Enter the number of people in your household.
* **How much does your household income per year?** (`how-much-does-your-household-income-per-year`, number): Enter the amount of household income per year.
* **Do you agree with the following statement 2?** (`do-you-agree-with-the-following-statement-2`, select_one): Select whether you agree or disagree with the provided statement.
* **How often do you read local newspaper?** (`how-often-do-you-read-local-newspaper`, select_one): Select how often you read local newspapers.
* **What is your annual income?** (`what-is-your-annual-income`, number): Enter your annual income.
* **Do you agree with the following statement 3?** (`do-you-agree-with-the-following-statement-3`, select_one): Select whether you agree or disagree with the provided statement.
* **Additional comments 2** (`additional-comments-2`, text): If you have any additional comments or thoughts, please share them here.
