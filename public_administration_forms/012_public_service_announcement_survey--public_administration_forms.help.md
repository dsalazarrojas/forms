# Public Service Announcement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on Public Service Announcements (PSAs) and assess user engagement and response to these announcements. It will help administrators better understand the effectiveness of PSAs over different time scales.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question as accurately as possible based on your experiences with PSAs.
2. Be honest about your intentions and actions in response to the last PSA you saw.
3. Select all applicable actions you took in response to the last PSA you saw.
4. Fill out the form thoroughly, and make sure to submit it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, optional): Briefly introduce yourself and your experience with PSAs.
* **How many public service announcements do you recall in the past week?** (`recall_week`, number, required): Count the number of PSAs you recall in the past week.
* **Message quality of the most recent public service announcement you saw** (`message_quality`, text, required): Rate the quality of the last PSA you saw. For instance, "informative," "entertaining," or "confusing."
* **What was your intended action in response to the last public service announcement you saw?** (`intended_actions_week`, select_one, required): Choose how you intended to act on the last PSA you saw. Choose "Read more" if you wanted to learn more, "Contact" if you wanted to get in touch, "Share" if you wanted to pass on the information, "Ignore" if you didn't take any action, or "Other (please specify)" if you have a different intention.
* **Select all actions you took in response to the last public service announcement you saw** (`multiple_intended_actions_week`, select_multiple, required): Choose all actions you took in response to the last PSA you saw. You may select multiple answers.
* **How many public service announcements do you recall in the past month?** (`recall_month`, number, required): Count the number of PSAs you recall in the past month.
* **Message Quality Month** (`message_quality_month`, text, optional): Rate the quality of PSAs you recall in the past month. 
* **Intended Actions Month** (`intended_actions_month`, select_one, optional): Choose how you intended to act on the PSAs you recall in the past month. Choose "Read more" if you wanted to learn more, "Contact" if you wanted to get in touch, "Share" if you wanted to pass on the information, "Ignore" if you didn't take any action, or "Other (please specify)" if you have a different intention.
* **Multiple Intended Actions Month** (`multiple_intended_actions_month`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all actions you took in response to the PSAs you recall in the past month. You may select multiple answers.
* **How many public service announcements do you recall in the past year?** (`recall_year`, number, required): Count the number of PSAs you recall in the past year.
* **Message Quality Year** (`message_quality_year`, text, optional): Rate the quality of PSAs you recall in the past year.
* **Intended Actions Year** (`intended_actions_year`, select_one, optional): Choose how you intended to act on the PSAs you recall in the past year. Choose "Read more" if you wanted to learn more, "Contact" if you wanted to get in touch, "Share" if you wanted to pass on the information, "Ignore" if you didn't take any action, or "Other (please specify)" if you have a different intention.
* **Multiple Intended Actions Year** (`multiple_intended_actions_year`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all actions you took in response to the PSAs you recall in the past year. You may select multiple answers.
