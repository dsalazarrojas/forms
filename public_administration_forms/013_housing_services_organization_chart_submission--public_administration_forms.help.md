# Housing Services Organization Chart Submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather and display the information about organizations offering housing services in the organization chart. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

- **Step 1:** Select one of the provided options for the "Submission" category:
  - Authority: Select this if you represent the highest authority for the housing service.
  - Manager: Choose this if you manage or oversee housing services.
  - Non-profit: This is for non-profit organizations offering housing services.

- **Step 2:** Provide the name of the authority providing the housing service.

- **Step 3:** Enter the department's name within the authority.

- **Step 4:** Add any additional details about the department.

- **Step 5:** Select the type of service your organization provides, using the provided options:
  - Housing Authority
  - Property Manager
  - Non-profit

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Submission** (`Submission`, select_multiple, required/optional): 
  Select the category that best represents your organization or the service you're submitting. This will help in organizing the chart correctly.
- **Authority** (`authority`, text, required/optional): 
  Enter the name of the authority providing the housing service.
- **Department** (`department`, text, required/optional): 
  Enter the name of the department within the authority offering the housing service.
- **Department details** (`department_details`, text, required/optional): 
  Add any additional details about the department, such as their role or function.
- **Staffing** (`staffing`, select_multiple, required/optional): 
  Select the type of service your organization provides:
  - Housing Authority: If your organization focuses on providing housing services directly.
  - Property Manager: If your organization oversees the management of properties related to housing.
  - Non-profit: If your organization is a non-profit entity providing housing services.

## Tips
* Make sure you are submitting accurate information.
* Double-check your selection before submitting the form to ensure your organization's information is correctly represented.
