# Police Scam Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information from victims of police scams, which can help aid in investigations and potentially prevent future scams.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Describe the incident as detailed as possible, including dates and locations, in the "Incident Details" field.
2. Provide your contact information, including your name, address, and any other relevant contact methods, in the "Contact Info" field.
3. Select one or more options from the "Additional Info" field that apply to the scam you experienced.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, required: false): Describe the incident as detailed as possible, including dates and locations. Please provide as much information as you can about what happened, including any relevant dates, times, and locations.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Provide your contact information so we can reach you. Please include your name, address, and any other relevant contact methods, such as email or phone numbers.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, required: false): Select one or more options from the list that apply to the scam you experienced. This will help us better understand the nature of the scam and aid in further investigation.
