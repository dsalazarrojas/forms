# Agricultural Advisory Council Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agricultural Advisory Council Petition Form is a tool for individuals and organizations to submit petitions and feedback to the council. This form is designed to collect relevant information to help the council understand the concerns and requests of the public.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address or phone number (if applicable) in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide a short title describing the petition.
3. Write a brief summary of the request or concern.
4. Describe the detailed information, facts, and requested actions in the petition details field.
5. List the affected areas or communities related to the petition.
6. Indicate if you have attached supporting documents and describe them in the supporting documents field.
7. Acknowledge that the submitted information is accurate by signing and consenting in the signature and consent field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Petitioner name** (<name>, <text>): The full name of the person submitting the petition.
* **Contact email** (<contact_email, <email>): The email address for follow-up communication.
* **Contact phone number** (<contact_phone, <text>): An optional phone number including country code.
* **Organization or affiliation** (<organization, <text>): The organization name if applicable.
* **Petition title** (<petition_title, <text>): A short descriptive title of the petition.
* **Petition summary** (<petition_summary, <text>): A brief summary of the request or concern.
* **Petition details** (<petition_details, <text>): Detailed information, facts, and requested actions in the petition.
* **Affected areas or communities** (<affected_areas, <text>): A list of locations affected by the issue.
* **Supporting documents available** (<supporting_documents, <text>): Indicate if attachments are available and describe them briefly.
* **Signature and consent acknowledgement** (<signature_consent, <note>): Acknowledge that the submitted information is accurate and up-to-date.
