# Maladministration Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maladministration Reporting Form is a tool used to report incidents or issues that affect the public or students in an educational institution. It is designed to support public administration and help identify areas that require attention from authorities or administrators.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide detailed information about the incident or issue in the Incident Details section.
2. Enter your contact information in the Contact Information section, so that you can be reached for follow-up questions or further investigation.
3. Select the appropriate reporting category from the Reporting Form section, such as 'Anonymous', 'Staff', 'Management', 'Student', or 'Public'.
4. Indicate whether you would like to be followed up on your report by answering 'True' or 'False' in the Follow-up section.
5. Submit the form to record the date and time of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, optional): In this section, describe the incident or issue that you are reporting in as much detail as possible.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information so that you can be reached for further questions or investigation.
* **Reporting Details** (`reporting_details`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information about the incident or issue.
* **Reporting Form** (`reporting_form`, select multiple, optional): Select one or more of the options to categorize the type of reporting:
	+ Anonymous: Report anonymously, without revealing your identity.
	+ Staff: Report on behalf of staff or employees.
	+ Management: Report on behalf of management or administrators.
	+ Student: Report on behalf of students or student organizations.
	+ Public: Report on behalf of the general public.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, select one, optional): Choose whether you would like to be followed up on your report:
	+ True: Yes, I would like to be contacted for further questions or investigation.
	+ False: No, I do not want to be contacted.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Enter your name or a reference number to identify the report.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, optional): This field is automatically populated with the current date and time when the form is submitted.
