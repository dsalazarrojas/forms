# Civic Project Idea Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect project ideas from the public for civic projects that can benefit the community. The project idea upload form ensures that project proposals are organized and presented in a structured manner, making it easier for project administrators to review and evaluate them.

### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your proposer name and organization (if applicable) in the respective fields.
2. Provide a short and descriptive title for your project.
3. Write a brief summary of your project, including its goals and expected outcomes.
4. Estimate the budget required for the project (if applicable).
5. Describe the expected impact on the community.
6. Specify the key milestones and timeline for the project implementation.
7. Identify any partners or organizations that will be involved in the project.
8. Attach any supporting documents or files relevant to your project (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Proposer name (1)**: Enter your full name as the project proposer.
* **Organization (2)**: If your project proposal is from an organization, enter the name of the organization (optional).
* **Project title (3)**: Provide a short and descriptive title for your project. This title will be used as the primary identifier for your project.
* **Project summary (4)**: Write a brief description of your project, including its goals and expected outcomes.
* **Expected impact (5)**: Describe the expected community benefits and outcomes of your project.
* **Estimated budget (6)**: If applicable, provide a rough estimate of the budget required for the project.
* **Implementation timeline (7)**: Specify the key milestones and timeline for the project implementation.
* **Partners needed (8)**: Identify any organizations or stakeholders that will be involved in the project.
* **File upload instructions (9)**: Use the upload feature to attach any supporting documents or files to your project proposal.
* **Submission date (10)**: Use the current date as the submission date for your project proposal.

### Tips

* Use the project title and summary to effectively communicate your project idea.
* Be realistic when estimating the budget and implementation timeline.
* Ensure that your project proposal is complete and well-structured to increase its chances of being reviewed and considered for approval.
* Use supporting documents and files to provide more context and details about your project.
* Double-check your submission date to ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
