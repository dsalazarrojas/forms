# Securities Regulator Online Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Securities Regulator Online Complaint Form is designed to enable users to submit online complaints related to investments, allowing them to report any concerns or issues they have encountered. This form should only be used for legitimate purposes and to report original complaints, not duplicates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please provide your name in the "name" field, which is a required field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field, which is also a required field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field, which is a required field.
4. Provide details about your investment in the "investment_details" field.
5. Select the type of complaint you are reporting in the "complaint_type" field by choosing from the available options.
6. Enter your complaint in the "submit" field, which is a required field.
7. Optionally, you can add any additional notes or comments in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **investment_related_complaints** (text, required/optional): Enter a description of your investment-related complaint.
* **name** (text, required): Enter your name.
* **email** (text, required): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **investment_details** (text, required/optional): Provide details about your investment.
* **complaint_type** (select_multiple, required/optional): Select the type of complaint you are reporting.
* **submit** (text, required): Enter a detailed description of your complaint.
* **notes** (note, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your complaint is accurate and true.
* Please do not file duplicate complaints.
* Please provide all required fields to ensure that your complaint can be processed efficiently.
