# Community Development Initiative Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from community members about their preferences, needs, and behaviors in relation to community development initiatives. The data collected will help the community administration understand what matters most to you and tailor their efforts to address the community's needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your name (if you wish to be identified).
2. Enter your ZIP code to help us understand your location within the community.
3. Choose your residency status, indicating your relationship with the community.
4. Select how long you have been living in the community.
5. Choose your top three priority initiatives for the community.
6. Indicate your support for tax funding for community development projects.
7. Tell us about your willingness to volunteer time for community initiatives.
8. Let us know how often you attend community meetings.
9. Select your preferred method of communication.
10. Finally, provide any additional input or ideas you may have.
