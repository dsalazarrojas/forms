# Community Engagement Audit - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Engagement Audit form is designed to collect information on community engagement activities and assess their effectiveness. It helps departments and programs track progress and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select a date of engagement audit.
2. Specify the name of the department or program being audited.
3. List the methods used for engagement, such as surveys, meetings, or outreach.
4. Assess the reach and participation level of the engagement activities.
5. Note any barriers to engagement and identify potential solutions.
6. Provide actionable recommendations for improvement.
7. Enter the name of the person completing the audit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Audit date** (`audit_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the engagement audit.
* **Department audited** (`department`, text, required): Enter the name of the department or program being audited.
* **Engagement methods** (`engagement_methods`, text, required): List the methods used for engagement, such as surveys, meetings, or outreach.
* **Reach and participation level** (`reach_and_participation`, text, required): Assess the reach and participation level of the engagement activities. This could include metrics like attendance, demographic reach, or other relevant data.
* **Barriers to engagement identified** (`barriers_identified`, text, optional): Note any obstacles that limit participation or engagement. This could include language barriers, limited access, or other challenges.
* **Recommendations to improve engagement** (`recommendations`, text, required): Provide actionable steps and responsible teams for improving engagement activities.
* **Auditor name** (`auditor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person completing the audit.

## Tips
* Ensure each audit date is unique to avoid duplication of effort.
* Use specific and measurable metrics to assess reach and participation level.
* Provide actionable recommendations that are realistic and feasible.
* Clearly identify potential barriers and suggest solutions.
* Use clear and concise language in the auditor name field.
