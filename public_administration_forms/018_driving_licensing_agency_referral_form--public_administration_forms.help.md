# driving_licensing_agency_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The driving licensing agency referral form is used to capture information about a driving licensing agency's referral to another organization. This includes details about the agency's contact information, number of employees, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the "Form Data" page and fill out the "Agency Name," "Agency Address," "Agency Phone," "Agency Email," and other details about the referring agency.
2. Provide information on the agency's employees, including the number of employees and those who have been served.
3. Fill out the "Referral Info" section with any additional information that might be helpful in the referral process.
4. In the "License Info" section, enter the license number, type, status, issue date, issue time, expiration date, expiration time, and renewal date for the referred driver.
5. Finally, review all the information you've entered to ensure it's accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Data** (`form_data`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the form itself or the referral process.
* **Applicant Info** (`applicant_info`, text, optional): Enter any information about the applicant being referred.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter contact information for the referring agency.
* **Referral Info** (`referral_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information that might be helpful in the referral process.
* **Agency Info** (`agency_info`, text, optional): Enter information about the referring agency.
* **License Number** (`license_number`, text, optional): Enter the license number of the referred driver.
* **License Type** (`license_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of license issued to the driver.
* **License Class** (`license_class`, select_multiple, optional): Select the class of license issued to the driver.
* **License Status** (`license_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the driver's license.
* **Driving License Number** (`driving_license_number`, number, optional): Enter the driving license number of the referred driver.
* **License Expiration Date** (`license_expiration_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the driver's license expires.
* **License Expiration Time** (`license_expiration_time`, time, optional): Enter the time when the driver's license expires.
* **License Issue Date** (`license_issue_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the driver's license was issued.
* **License Issue Time** (`license_issue_time`, time, optional): Enter the time when the driver's license was issued.
* **License Renewal Date** (`license_renewal_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the driver's license was renewed.
* **License Renewal Time** (`license_renewal_time`, time, optional): Enter the time when the driver's license was renewed.
* **License Reissue Date** (`license_reissue_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the driver's license was reissued.
* **License Reissue Time** (`license_reissue_time`, time, optional): Enter the time when the driver's license was reissued.
* **Agency Name** (`agency_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the referring agency.
* **Agency Address** (`agency_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the referring agency.
* **Agency Phone** (`agency_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the referring agency.
* **Agency Email** (`agency_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the referring agency.
* **Agency Employees** (`agency_employees`, number, optional): Enter the number of employees in the referring agency.
* **Agency Employees Hired** (`agency_employees_hired`, number, optional): Enter the number of employees hired by the referring agency.
* **Agency Employees Served** (`agency_employees_served`, number, optional): Enter the number of employees served by the referring agency.

By following this guide, you'll be able to fill out the form correctly and accurately, ensuring that the necessary information is captured to proceed with the referral process.
