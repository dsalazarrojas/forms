# State Seal Design Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the proposed state seal design. It will help the designers and decision-makers understand the opinions and expectations of various stakeholders, including the reviewer, review committee, resident, and stakeholder.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the proposed seal concept in the text field provided.
2. Rate the seal design using the scale provided (1 - Very Good, 2 - Average, 3 - Not Good).
3. Provide any additional feedback or comments in the text fields.
4. Fill out the rating for the review committee and resident (1 - Very Good, 2 - Average, 3 - Not Good).
5. Fill out the rating for the stakeholder and resident (1 - Very Good, 2 - Average, 3 - Not Good).
6. Fill out the rating for the review committee (1 - Very Good, 2 - Average, 3 - Not Good).
7. Provide any comments for the review committee, resident, and stakeholder in the respective text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Proposed Seal Concept**: This field is where you can submit your initial ideas and concepts for the state seal design. You can describe the color scheme, symbol, and design elements you envision.
* **Seal Rating** (`Seal Rating`, number, required): Please rate the seal design using the provided scale. Rate the design based on its overall effectiveness and representation of the state's values and identity.
* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, text, required): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have for the seal design. This could include ideas for improvements, concerns, or questions.
* **Reviewer** (`Reviewer`, text, required): Please enter your name and title or position.
* **Committee** (`Committee`, text, required): Enter the name and title or position of the review committee member.
* **Resident** (`Resident`, text, required): Enter the name of the resident who provided feedback or reviewed the design.
* **Stakeholder** (`Stakeholder`, text, required): Enter the name and title or position of the stakeholder who provided feedback or reviewed the design.
* **Review Committee** (`Review Committee`, text, required): Enter the name and title or position of the review committee member.
* **Review Committee Rating** (`Review Committee Rating`, number, required): Rate the review committee's rating of the seal design using the provided scale.
* **Review Committee Comments** (`Review Committee Comments`, text, required): Provide any comments or suggestions from the review committee.
* **Comments Resident** (`Comments Resident`, text, optional): Enter any comments or suggestions from the resident who reviewed the design.
* **Comments Stakeholder** (`Comments Stakeholder`, text, optional): Enter any comments or suggestions from the stakeholder who reviewed the design.
* **Rating Resident** (`Rating Resident`, number, required): Rate the resident's rating of the seal design using the provided scale.
* **Rating Stakeholder** (`Rating Stakeholder`, number, required): Rate the stakeholder's rating of the seal design using the provided scale.
* **Rating Committee** (`Rating Committee`, number, required): Rate the review committee's rating of the seal design using the provided scale.
