# Government Vendor Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register vendors who want to provide services or goods to the government.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the supplier details on the first page.
2. Choose the vendor qualifications on the second page.
3. Enter the vendor contact information on the third page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Details** (`supplier_details`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more of the following: text, select one, decimal, time, date, note, email, or phone.
* **Vendor Qualifications** (`government_vendor_registration_form_page_2`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more of the following: 5, 10, or 15.
* **Vendor Contact** (`vendor_contact`, text, required): Enter the vendor's contact information.

## Tips
* Ensure you have filled out the form accurately and truthfully.
* Do not register the same vendor multiple times.
