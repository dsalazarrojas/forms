# Municipal Tree Service Contact Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect contact requests from the public for municipal tree service, including tree trimming, removal, and maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Select the type of tree service you are requesting (e.g. "Tree Trimming", "Tree Removal", or "Tree Maintenance") in the "Request Type" field.
5. Enter the location of the tree service request.
6. Provide any additional details about your request in the "Details" field.
7. If applicable, select the person or team you would like to be assigned to your request (e.g. "chatjimmy" or "other") in the "Assigned To" field.
8. Enter the date you would like to submit your request in the "Submitted On" field. This field is automatically populated once you submit the form.
9. Add any notes or comments about your request in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request**: A field to enter your contact request.
* **Name**: Enter your name so that the municipal tree service team can contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so that the municipal tree service team can contact you.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so that the municipal tree service team can contact you.
* **Request Type**: Select the type of tree service you are requesting, such as "Tree Trimming", "Tree Removal", or "Tree Maintenance".
* **Location**: Enter the location of the tree service request.
* **Details**: Provide any additional details about your request.
* **Assigned To**: If applicable, select the person or team you would like to be assigned to your request.
* **Submitted By**: This field is automatically populated once you submit the form and is not editable.
* **Submitted On**: This field is automatically populated once you submit the form and is not editable.
