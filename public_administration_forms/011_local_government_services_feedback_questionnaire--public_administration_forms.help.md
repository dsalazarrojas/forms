> This is the "Local Government Services Feedback Questionnaire" form. The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from citizens about their experiences with local government services. This form is intended to help the administration understand what services need improvement and what areas of improvement are most important to citizens. It is used by public administrators to gather data that will inform future service development and delivery.

# Local Government Services Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The local government services feedback questionnaire is a tool used to collect information about citizens' experiences and perceptions of local government services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Rate the quality of our services by selecting a number between 1 and 5 in the "How would you rate the quality of our services?" field.
2. Indicate how often you visit our services by selecting a number that reflects your usage frequency.
3. Identify any issues you have experienced with our services from the list of options.
4. Choose your top three priorities for improvement in our services from the list of options.
5. Select the neighborhood you reside in from the list of options.
6. Provide any additional comments or feedback about our services in the "Any additional comments or feedback?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How would you rate the quality of our services?** (`service_quality`, number, required): This field allows you to rate the quality of our services on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **How often do you visit our services?** (`service_availability`, number, required): This field asks you to indicate how often you visit our services, with higher numbers indicating more frequent usage.
* **Which of the following issues have you experienced with our services?** (`issues`, select_multiple, required): Select all the issues that you have experienced with our services from the list of options. This will help us understand areas where we need improvement.
* **What are your top three priorities for improvement in our services?** (`improvement_priorities`, select_multiple, required): Identify your top three priorities for improvement in our services from the list of options. This will help us understand what matters most to you.
* **Which neighborhood do you reside in?** (`neighborhood`, select_one, required): Select the neighborhood you currently reside in from the list of options. This will help us understand which areas we need to focus on.
* **Any additional comments or feedback?** (`feedback`, note, required): Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about our services. This will help us understand your thoughts and help us improve.
