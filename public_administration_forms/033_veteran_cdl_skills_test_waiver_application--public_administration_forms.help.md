# Veteran CDL Skills Test Waiver Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide provides instructions on how to complete the Veteran CDL Skills Test Waiver Application form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review and understand the purpose of the form and the questions being asked.
2.  Fill in your personal details such as name, date of birth, and veteran status.
3.  Provide details about your military service, if applicable.
4.  Indicate whether you are requesting a waiver.
5.  Enter your veteran CDL license number, state license number, and state license expiration date, if applicable.
6.  Indicate whether you have completed CDL skill tests, if any.
7.  Provide reason for requesting a waiver, if applicable.
8.  Fill in your comments, if you have any.
9.  Finally, enter the details of the state administrator who is reviewing your waiver request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Section 1 - Veteran CDL Skills Test Waiver Application**: This section is the introduction to the form, which explains its purpose and the questions that follow.
*   **Waiver Request**: Select 'Yes' if you are requesting a waiver, and 'No' if you are not. This will determine the flow of the rest of the form.
*   **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your license.
*   **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format 'DD MMM YYYY' (e.g., 01 Jan 1990).
*   **Military Service**: If you have served in the military, enter the dates of your active service in the format 'DD MMM YYYY - DD MMM YYYY'. If you have never served, enter 'Never served in the military'.
*   **Veteran Status**: Select 'Active Duty', 'Discharged', 'Retired', or 'Never served in the military' to indicate your status.
*   **Veteran CDL License Number**: Enter your CDL license number, if applicable.
*   **State CDL License Number**: Enter your state CDL license number, if applicable.
*   **State CDL License Expires**: Enter the date your state CDL license expires.
*   **Driver License Number**: Enter your driver's license number, if applicable.
*   **Driver License Expires**: Enter the date your driver's license expires.
*   **CDL Skill Tests Completed**: Select all the CDL skill tests you have completed.
*   **Veteran CDL Waiver Approved**: Select 'Yes' if your waiver is approved, and 'No' if it is not.
*   **Veteran CDL Waiver Reason**: Enter the reason for your waiver request, if applicable.
*   **Veteran Comments**: Enter any comments you have about your waiver request.
*   **State Administrator's Name**: Enter the name of the state administrator reviewing your waiver request.
*   **State Administrator's Signature**: Enter the date of the administrator's signature.
*   **State Administrator's Title**: Enter the title of the state administrator reviewing your waiver request.
*   **State Administrator's Date**: Enter the date of the administrator's signature.
