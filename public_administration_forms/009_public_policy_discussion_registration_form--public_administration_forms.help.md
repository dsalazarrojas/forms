# public_policy_discussion_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The public policy discussion registration form is used to register participants for a public policy discussion event. This form captures necessary information for identification and categorization of participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
* Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
* Enter your Email in the Email field (ensure it is a valid email address).
* Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
* Select a Date for the event (if applicable).
* Select a Time for the event (if applicable).
* Choose your Participant Type (Participant, Observer, or Exhibitor) from the dropdown menu.
* Provide any additional Comments about your participation or the event.
* If assigned a tool, enter the name of the tool assigned to you.
* Finally, select a Category for your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your full name as it should be displayed to other participants.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it should be displayed to other participants.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for event coordination.
* **Date**: Select the date you plan to participate in the event (if applicable).
* **Time**: Select the time you plan to participate in the event (if applicable).
* **Participant Type**: Choose whether you are a Participant, Observer, or Exhibitor.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional information about your participation or the event.
* **Assigned Tool**: If assigned a tool, enter the name of the tool assigned to you.
* **Category**: Select a category for your participation (if applicable).
