# Pecuniary Interests Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pecuniary Interests Declaration Form is used to declare your financial interests and relationships with the organization or entities related to the organization. This form is essential for maintaining transparency and avoiding potential conflicts of interest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the reporting period for which you are declaring your pecuniary interests.
2. Enter your declaration date.
3. Select all direct interests you have with the organization.
4. Describe any indirect financial relationships with the organization or entities related to the organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Information** (`title`, text, required): Enter your name and any other relevant general information.
	* This field is used to gather your basic information for reference purposes.
* Please ensure that your name is spelled correctly and includes any other relevant details.
- **Reporting Period** (`reporting_period`, select_one, required): Select the year of the reporting period for which you are declaring your pecuniary interests.
	* This field is used to determine which period your declarations are for.
	* Please select the correct reporting period to ensure that your declarations are accurate and relevant.
- **Declaration Date** (`declaration_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which you are making this declaration.
	* This field is used to record the date of your declaration.
	* Please enter the correct date to ensure that your declaration is timestamped accurately.
- **Direct Interests** (`direct_interests`, select_multiple, required): Select all direct interests you have with the organization.
	* This field is used to declare any direct financial interests you have with the organization.
	* Please select all applicable interests to ensure that your declarations are comprehensive.
- **Indirect Interests 1-22** (`indirect_interests_1`, text, required): Describe any indirect financial relationships with the organization or entities related to the organization.
	* These fields are used to declare any indirect financial relationships you have with the organization or entities related to the organization.
	* Please describe each relationship accurately and thoroughly.
- **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which you are submitting this form.
	* This field is used to record the date of your submission.
	* Please enter the correct date to ensure that your submission is timestamped accurately.
