# Government Contract Discharge Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Government Contract Discharge Form is a standard document used by government agencies to report the discharge of a government contract. This form helps to ensure that all contracts are accurately tracked and documented, and that any necessary updates or changes are made as required.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Contract Number, which is the unique identifier assigned to the contract.
2. Enter the contract date, which is the date the contract was originally signed.
3. Enter the contract end date, which is the date the contract was scheduled to end.
4. Select the type of contract (Government, Non-Government, or Other).
5. Enter the reason for discharge (required for discharge status).
6. Check the Agency Approval status (Approve, Disapprove, or Pending).
7. Enter the Contractor Name.
8. Choose the discharge status (Discharge Complete, Discharge Partial, or Discharge Not Complete).
9. Enter the discharge date.
10. Add any additional comments about the discharge process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contract Number** (Required): Enter the unique identifier assigned to the contract.
* **Contract Date** (Optional): Enter the date the contract was originally signed.
* **Contract End Date** (Optional): Enter the date the contract was scheduled to end.
* **Contract Type** (Optional): Select the type of contract (Government, Non-Government, or Other).
* **Discharge Reason** (Required): Enter the reason for discharging the contract.
* **Agency Approval** (Optional): Check the status of agency approval (Approve, Disapprove, or Pending).
* **Contractor Name** (Required): Enter the name of the contractor.
* **Discharge Status** (Required): Choose the discharge status (Discharge Complete, Discharge Partial, or Discharge Not Complete).
* **Discharge Date** (Required): Enter the date the contract was discharged.
* **Comments** (Optional): Add any additional comments about the discharge process.

## Tips

* Ensure that you enter accurate information for all fields.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you are unsure about any field or have questions, please do not hesitate to reach out to your agency's contract administration team for assistance.
