# Parking Ticket Hearing Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a hearing for a parking ticket. It allows users to provide details about the ticket and their preferred date and time for the hearing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Parking Ticket Number** field with the actual number of the parking ticket you are requesting a hearing for.
2. Provide a detailed description of the **Parking Ticket Details** in the text field.
3. Select a **Preferred Hearing Date** and **Preferred Hearing Time** from the calendar and time picker fields, respectively.
4. Upload any **Supporting Documentation** (e.g. a photo of the parking ticket) to support your hearing request.
5. Choose your **Hearing Preferences** from the list of options.
6. Select **Submit** to send the form, or **Cancel** to discard your changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Parking Ticket Number**: The actual number of the parking ticket you are requesting a hearing for. Please enter the number as it appears on the ticket.
- **Parking Ticket Details**: A brief description of the parking ticket, including any relevant details about the circumstances surrounding the ticket.
- **Preferred Hearing Date**: The date you would like to schedule the hearing for. Please select a date that works for you.
- **Preferred Hearing Time**: The time you would like to schedule the hearing for. Please select a time that works for you.
- **Supporting Documentation**: Upload any supporting documentation, such as a photo of the parking ticket, to help support your hearing request.
- **Hearing Preferences**: Choose from a list of options to indicate your hearing preferences. This will help us understand your needs better.
- **Submit**: Send the form to schedule the hearing.
- **Cancel**: Discard your changes and start again if needed.
