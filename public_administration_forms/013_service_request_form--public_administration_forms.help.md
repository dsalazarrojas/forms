# Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Request Form is a tool used to record and manage service requests from citizens, customers, or employees. This form gathers contact information, service type, and other relevant details to facilitate prompt and efficient service delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including name, phone number, and email address (if applicable).
2. Select the preferred contact method (Call, Email, or Text).
3. Provide a brief description of the service request.
4. Specify the service type (Service A, Service B, or Service C).
5. Choose the service date and time (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (contact_information): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Contact Method** (contact_method): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Call, Email, or Text). This will help our team contact you in the most convenient way.
* **Service Request** (service_request): Describe the service you need in a few words or sentences.
* **Service Details** (service_details): Add any additional information about the service you requested, such as preferred schedule or special requirements.
* **Contact Name** (contact_name): Enter the name of the person making the service request.
* **Contact Phone** (contact_phone): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Contact Email** (contact_email): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Service Type** (service_type): Select the type of service you are requesting (Service A, Service B, or Service C).
* **Service Date** (service_date): Choose the date you would like to receive the service.
* **Service Time** (service_time): Select the time you would like to receive the service.

## Tips
- Please be as detailed as possible when filling in the service description and service details to ensure our team can provide you with the best possible service.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, please let us know so we can accommodate them.
- Our team will review and respond to your service request promptly.
- We appreciate your patience and understanding as we work to fulfill your request.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
 
Please take a moment to read through this help guide and ensure you have all the information needed to complete this form accurately. If you have any questions or issues, our team is here to help!
