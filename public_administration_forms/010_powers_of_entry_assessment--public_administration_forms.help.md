# Powers Of Entry Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from site inspectors about the power of entry of a site. This information is used to assess the level of access and control that an individual or group has over a particular site.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the site and choose the correct answer to "Site Entry Decision" to indicate whether the site has been entered or not.
2. Enter a brief justification for the entry decision in the "Justification" field.
3. If the site has been entered, enter the ID of the inspector who made the entry in the "Inspector" field.
4. If the site has been entered, enter the ID of the team lead who approved the entry in the "Team Lead" field.
5. Enter the date of the entry in the "Date of Entry" field.
6. Enter the time of the entry in the "Time of Entry" field.
7. Enter any comments from the site inspector in the "Site Inspector Comments" field.
8. Enter any comments from the team in the "Team Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Site Details** (site_details): Enter a brief description of the site.
* **Site Entry Decision** (site_entry_decision): Select 'Yes' to indicate that the site has been entered, or 'No' to indicate that it has not.
* **Justification** (justification): Enter a brief explanation for the site entry decision.
* **Inspector** (inspector_id): Enter the ID of the inspector who made the entry (if applicable).
* **Team Lead** (team_lead_id): Enter the ID of the team lead who approved the entry (if applicable).
* **Date of Entry** (date_of_entry): Enter the date of the entry in [YYYY-MM-DD format](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_8601).
* **Time of Entry** (time_of_entry): Enter the time of the entry in [HH:MM format](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/24-hour_clock).
* **Site Inspector Comments** (site_inspector_comments): Enter any comments from the site inspector (if applicable).
* **Team Comments** (team_comments): Enter any comments from the team (if applicable).
