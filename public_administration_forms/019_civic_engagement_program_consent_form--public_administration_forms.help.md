# Civic Engagement Program Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Civic Engagement Program Consent Form is used to gather information and consent from participants in civic engagement activities. This includes personal contact and emergency contact information, photo release, and liability waiver.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by carefully reading the form and its sections.
2.  Ensure you are aware of the purpose of each field before completing it.
3.  Complete all required fields.
4.  Answer each question truthfully and accurately.
5.  Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
6.  Sign and date the form as instructed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name of Civic Program/Project** (`program_name`, text, **Required**): Enter the name of the civic program or project you are participating in.
*   **Participant Full Name** (`participant_full_name`, text, **Required**): Enter the full name of the person participating in the civic activity.
*   **Date of Birth** (`participant_dob`, date, **Required**): Enter the participant's date of birth.
*   **Parent or Guardian Name (if under 18)** (`parent_guardian_name`, text, **Optional**): If the participant is under 18, enter the name of the parent or guardian.
*   **Primary Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, **Required**): Enter the primary contact phone number.
*   **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact_header`, text, **Required**): This is a placeholder for an emergency contact person. Please fill out the following fields with the emergency contact information.
    *   **Emergency Contact Person** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, **Required**): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
    *   **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, **Required**): Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
*   **Please list any allergies or medical conditions we should be aware of** (`medical_info_note`, text, **Optional**): If applicable, list any allergies or medical conditions the participant may have.
*   **I release the organization from liability for injury during the program** (`liability_waiver_agreement`, select one, **Required**): Select "I Agree".
*   **I grant permission for photos of me/my child to be used for program promotion** (`photo_release_opt`, select one, **Required**): Select "Yes, I agree".
*   **Date of Signature** (`digital_signature_date`, date, **Required**): Enter the date you sign this form.

## Tips
- Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
- If you are under 18, provide the name and contact information of your parent or guardian.
- Review and understand the implications of the liability waiver and photo release before signing.
