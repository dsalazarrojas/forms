# Community Tree Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Tree Preference Survey is designed to gather information about your preferences regarding tree planting in your area. It will help us understand your priorities and concerns about tree planting, which will aid us in making informed decisions about community tree planting initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the field labeled "Respondent name" if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up.
2. Select your neighborhood or ward from the list provided.
3. Rank the importance of tree planting in your area by selecting one of the four options: "Very important", "Important", "Neutral", or "Not important".
4. Select up to three preferred tree species from the list.
5. Choose up to three preferred locations for planting from the list.
6. If you have any concerns about tree planting, please enter them in the text field.
7. Indicate if you are interested in volunteering for tree planting by selecting "True" or "False".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Respondent name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): Your name will be used for follow-up, if desired.
* **Neighborhood or ward** (`neighborhood`, text, required): This helps us map your preferences by area.
* **Tree planting priority** (`tree_support_priority`, select_one, required): Rank the importance of tree planting in your area. Choose from: "Very important", "Important", "Neutral", or "Not important".
* **Preferred tree species** (`preferred_species`, select_multiple, optional): Select up to three tree species you prefer. Options: "Shade trees", "Flowering trees", "Evergreen", or "Native species".
* **Preferred locations for planting** (`preferred_location`, select_multiple, optional): Choose up to three locations for planting. Options: "Street verge", "Park", "School grounds", or "Private yard".
* **Concerns about tree planting** (`concerns_about_trees`, text, optional): If you have any concerns about tree planting, please enter them here.
* **Interest in volunteering** (`volunteer_interest`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you are interested in volunteering for tree planting. Choose "True" or "False".
