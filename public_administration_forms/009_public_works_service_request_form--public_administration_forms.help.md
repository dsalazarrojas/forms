# Public Works Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Public Works Service Request Form is designed to collect information from users for public works service requests. This form helps to streamline the process of submitting service requests, ensuring that all relevant details are captured accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and any other relevant contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
2. Select the type of service you are requesting in the "Service Type" dropdown menu.
3. Provide detailed information about your service request in the "Service Request Details" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. If the requested service is to be performed on a specific date, enter the date in the "Date" field.
7. If the requested service is to be performed at a specific time, enter the time in the "Time" field.
8. Provide any additional information or notes about your request in the "Notes" field.
9. Repeat the above steps for subsequent service requests if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (Required/Optional)
	+ Enter your name, email, and phone number for contact purposes.
* **Service Type** (Required/Optional)
	+ Select the type of service you are requesting.
* **Service Request Details** (Required/Optional)
	+ Provide detailed information about your service request.
* **Email** (Required/Optional)
	+ Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (Required/Optional)
	+ Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date** (Required/Optional)
	+ Enter the date on which the service is to be performed.
* **Time** (Required/Optional)
	+ Enter the time at which the service is to be performed.
* **Notes** (Required/Optional)
	+ Provide any additional information or notes about your request.
* **Submit** (Required/Optional)
	+ Click "Submit" to submit your service request.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any follow-up questions or concerns, please contact the public works department.
* If you need to cancel or update your service request, please notify the public works department in a timely manner.
