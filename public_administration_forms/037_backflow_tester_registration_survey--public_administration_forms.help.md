# Backflow Tester Registration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from registered backflow testers for administrative and regulatory purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name (Required)**:
	+ Enter your legal name.
* **Company Name (Optional)**:
	+ Enter your business name if applicable.
* **Email Address (Required)**:
	+ Enter your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number (Required)**:
	+ Enter your contact phone number.
* **Business Address (Required)**:
	+ Enter your full street address.
* **City (Required)**:
	+ Enter your city location.
* **State (Required)**:
	+ Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP Code (Required)**:
	+ Enter your postal code.
* **Certification Type (Required)**:
	+ Select your type of certification.
* **Certification Number (Required)**:
	+ Enter your certification ID.
* **Certifying Organization (Required)**:
	+ Select the organization that issued your certification.
* **Certification Date (Required)**:
	+ Enter the date you were certified.
* **Certification Expiry Date (Required)**:
	+ Enter the date your certification expires.
* **Years of Experience (Required)**:
	+ Select the number of years you have tested backflow.
* **Tests Performed Annually (Required)**:
	+ Select the approximate number of tests performed per year.
* **Service Areas (Required)**:
	+ Select the areas you serve.
* **Liability Insurance Coverage (Required)**:
	+ Select your insurance coverage status.
* **Insurance Provider (Optional)**:
	+ Enter the name of your insurance provider.
* **Testing Equipment Owned (Required)**:
	+ Select the testing equipment you own.
* **Equipment Calibration Current (Required)**:
	+ Select the status of your equipment calibration.
* **Professional References (Optional)**:
	+ List names and contact information.
* **Background Check Consent (Required)**:
	+ Select your consent status.
* **Agreement to Terms (Required)**:
	+ Select your agreement status.
* **Registration Date (Required)**:
	+ Date of this registration.

Note: The form is designed to collect information for administrative and regulatory purposes. It is essential to fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid any discrepancies. If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
