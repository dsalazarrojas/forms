# Urban Planning Community Needs Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information on the public's perception and needs regarding urban planning and community development in their neighborhoods. The collected data will help inform urban planning decisions and identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read through each page carefully.
2. Answer all questions truthfully.
3. Fill in all relevant information.
4. Submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`intro`, note, not required): This page provides a brief overview of the survey and its purpose.
* **Contact Information** (`contact`, email, not required): Please provide your contact information so we can follow up with you after the survey. If you don't have a preferred email address, you can leave this field blank.
* **How satisfied are you with your neighborhood** (`satisfaction`, number, not required): Rate your level of satisfaction with your neighborhood on a scale of 1-5.
* **What do you think is the primary issue with your neighborhood?** (`satisfaction_score`, select_one, not required): Select one option from the list to indicate what you think is the primary issue with your neighborhood.
	+ Housing
	+ Transportation
	+ Public Safety
	+ Public Spaces
	+ Other (please specify)
* **How satisfied are you with public services in your neighborhood?** (`satisfaction_score_2`, number, not required): Rate your level of satisfaction with public services in your neighborhood on a scale of 1-5.
* **What type of public services are most important to you?** (`public_services`, select_multiple, not required): Select all that apply from the list.
	+ Public Parks
	+ Public Schools
	+ Public Libraries
	+ Public Restrooms
	+ Public Sports Facilities
* **What are the top priorities in your neighborhood?** (`neighborhood_priorities`, select_multiple, not required): Select all that apply from the list.
	+ Affordable Housing
	+ Community Engagement
	+ Local Businesses
	+ Public Transportation
	+ Safety and Security
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Overall, how satisfied are you with your neighborhood?** (`neighborhood_satisfaction`, number, not required): Rate your overall level of satisfaction with your neighborhood on a scale of 1-5.
* **Any other comments or suggestions?** (`feedback`, text, not required): Feel free to share any other comments or suggestions you may have regarding your neighborhood or urban planning.
