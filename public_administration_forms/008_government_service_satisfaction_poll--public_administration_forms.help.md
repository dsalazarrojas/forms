# government_service_satisfaction_poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback on the quality of service provided by the office. It helps us understand how we can improve and better serve our customers.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the questions carefully and answer them as honestly as possible.
2. Fill in the form and submit it when you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a note field where you can write any comments or questions you may have about the form or the service.
* **How would you rate the service quality of our office?**: Please rate the service quality of our office on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "very dissatisfied" and 5 is "very satisfied".
* **What was the purpose of your visit?**: Please select the purpose of your visit from the options provided. This will help us understand how we can better serve you.
* **How did you feel about your overall experience at our office?**: Please select your level of satisfaction from the options provided.
* **What would make it more convenient for you to visit our office in the future?**: Write your thoughts on how we can improve our services to make it more convenient for you.
* **How likely are you to recommend this office to a friend or family member?**: Please select how likely you are to recommend our office.
* **How long did you spend at our office?**: Enter the time you spent at the office.
* **What day of the week did you visit our office?**: Please select the day of the week you visited our office.
* **What time of day did you visit our office?**: Enter the time of day you visited our office.
* **What time of day did you visit our office?** (This question is a duplicate of the previous one and is not necessary. You can skip this one.)
* **How do you usually contact our office?**: Please select how you usually contact our office. This will help us know how to better communicate with you.
* **How often do you visit our office?**: Please select how often you visit our office.
* **How do you usually find out about our office hours?**: Please select how you usually find out about our office hours.
* **What is your age?**: Enter your age.
* **How long have you lived in our city/area?**: Please select how long you have lived in our city/area.
* **How long have you known our office?**: Please select how long you have known our office.
* **Have you ever visited our office before?**: Please select if you have ever visited our office before.
* **How likely are you to recommend this office to a friend or family member?**: Please select how likely you are to recommend our office.
* **What is your income?**: Enter your income.
* **How many members are in your household?**: Enter the number of members in your household.
* **What is your level of education?**: Please select your level of education.
* **Which of the following best describes you?**: Please select how you best describe yourself.
* **What is your marital status?**: Please select your marital status.
* **What is your occupation?**: Write a brief description of your occupation.
* **Do you have any comments or concerns about our service?**: Please write any comments or concerns you may have about our service.

Note: Some fields have been left out of the guide as they were not provided with a label that is clear or relevant. If you need help with a specific field, please let me know and I will do my best to provide a clear explanation.
