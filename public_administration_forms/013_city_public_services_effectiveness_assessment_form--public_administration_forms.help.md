# City Public Services Effectiveness Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from the public on the effectiveness of city services such as sanitation, parks, and transportation. It helps the city administration to gauge the quality, timeliness, and overall satisfaction of these services, making informed decisions to improve them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name (optional).
2. Select the specific service area you are assessing (e.g. sanitation, parks, transportation).
3. Rate your overall satisfaction (1-5) with the service.
4. Rate the timeliness of service delivery (1-5).
5. Rate the quality of service provided (1-5).
6. Describe any major issues you have identified with the service.
7. Provide any actionable recommendations for improvement.
8. Indicate your level of trust in the service provider (1-5).
9. Answer whether you would recommend the service to others (Yes or No).
10. Fill in the submission date using the current date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent name** (respondent_name, text, Optional): This is your name, which will help us understand who is providing feedback.
* **Service area being assessed** (service_area, text, Required): Please select the specific service area you are evaluating (e.g. sanitation, parks, transportation).
* **Overall satisfaction rating** (satisfaction_rating, number, Required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service, from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Timeliness of service delivery** (timeliness_rating, number, Optional): Rate the promptness of service delivery, from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Quality of service provided** (quality_rating, number, Optional): Rate the quality of service provided, from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Major issues identified** (major_issues_identified, text, Optional): Describe any problems you have encountered with the service.
* **Suggestions for improvement** (suggestions_for_improvement, text, Optional): Provide any actionable recommendations for improving the service.
* **Trust in service provider** (trust_in_service_provider, number, Optional): Rate your level of trust in the service provider, from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
* **Would recommend service to others** (would_recommend, select_one, Optional): Answer whether you would recommend the service to others (Yes or No).
* **Submission date** (submission_date, date, Required): Please enter the current date to ensure accurate tracking of feedback.
