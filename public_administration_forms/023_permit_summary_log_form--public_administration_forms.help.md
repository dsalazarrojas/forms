# Permit Summary Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Permit Summary Log Form is a tool for logging permit related information for public administration. It allows authorized personnel to record and track permit details, status, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the provided fields and ensure you understand what information is being requested.
2. Enter the necessary details for the permit, such as the permit number, type, and status.
3. Select the correct status and type from the available options.
4. Enter the date the permit was issued, due, and last updated.
5. Add any additional comments or notes regarding the permit.
6. Verify all entered information is accurate and complete before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Permit Details** (`permit_details`, text, required/optional): A free text field for entering a brief description of the permit.
* **Status** (`permit_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the current status of the permit from the available options:
	+ Permit Approved
	+ Permit Denied
	+ Permit In Progress
	+ Permit Pending
	+ Permit Revoked
* **Issued Date** (`issued_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the permit was issued.
* **Due Date** (`due_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the permit is due.
* **Renewal Date** (`renewal_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the permit is set to renew.
* **Last Updated** (`last_updated`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the permit was last updated.
* **Permit Number** (`permit_number`, text, required/optional): A text field for entering the permit number.
* **Permit Type** (`permit_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of permit from the available options:
	+ Construction
	+ Facilities
	+ Property Management
	+ Other
* **Permit Status** (`permit_status`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the status of the permit from the available options:
	+ Approved
	+ Denied
	+ In Progress
	+ Pending
	+ Revoked
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): A text area for adding additional comments or notes regarding the permit.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address associated with the permit.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): A text field for entering the phone number associated with the permit.

## Tips

* Ensure all entered information is accurate and complete before submitting the form.
* Review the form regularly to update the permit status and other fields as necessary.
* Use the form to track permit details and status over time.
