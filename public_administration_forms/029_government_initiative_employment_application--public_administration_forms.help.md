# Government Initiative Employment Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Government Initiative Employment Application" form is designed to collect contact and application information from individuals interested in employment opportunities within the government initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact details section (page 1) with your name, address, and email.
2. In the "Application Form" section (page 1), write a brief description of yourself and how you think you can contribute to the government initiative.
3. Choose your job title from the dropdown menu in the "Job Title" section (page 2).
4. In the "Experience" section (page 3), describe your relevant work experience.
5. In the "Education" section (page 3), list your educational qualifications.
6. Enter your email address and phone number in the respective sections.
7. Upload your resume in PDF format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, required: false): This section collects your name, address, and email address. Please ensure that your email address is accurate and active.
* **Application Form** (`application_form`, required: false): This is a text field where you can write a brief description of yourself and how you think you can contribute to the government initiative. Be concise and clear in your response.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, required: false): Select one of the three job titles provided: Software Engineer, Data Scientist, or Project Manager. Choose the title that best represents your role or the position you are applying for.
* **Experience** (`experience`, required: false): Describe your relevant work experience, including your job title, company, and dates of employment. Focus on experiences that are most relevant to the government initiative.
* **Education** (`education`, required: false): List your educational qualifications, including degree and institution. This is an opportunity to showcase your academic background.
* **Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address. This will be used as contact information for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, required: false): Enter your phone number. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Resume (PDF)** (`resume`, required: false): Upload your resume in PDF format. This should include a detailed description of your work experience, skills, and qualifications.
