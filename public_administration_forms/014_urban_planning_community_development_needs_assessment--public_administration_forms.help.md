# urban_planning_community_development_needs_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from the public on areas of urban planning and community development that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the "What is this form about" question to give context to your feedback.
2. Provide your name and email address so we can get in touch with you if needed.
3. Share your phone number (optional) for easier contact.
4. Offer a suggestion or improvement for the community.
5. Specify the area you would like to suggest an improvement for.
6. Choose the area that is most in need of improvement from the provided options.
7. Provide specific details on how to improve the chosen area.
8. Choose the method through which you submitted your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is this form about** (page 1): This is a text field where you can briefly describe the purpose or context of your feedback.
* **Name** (page 1): This is a text field where you can enter your name or pseudonym.
* **Email** (page 1): This is a field where you can enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (page 1): This is an optional field where you can enter your phone number for easier contact.
* **Suggest an Improvement** (page 1): This is a text field where you can provide a brief description of your suggestion.
* **What area would you like to suggest an improvement for?** (page 1): This is a text field where you can specify the area you would like to suggest an improvement for.
* **What area is most in need of improvement?** (page 2): Choose from the provided options the area that is most in need of improvement.
* **Provide specific suggestions** (page 2): This is a text field where you can provide specific details on how to improve the chosen area.
* **How did you submit your feedback** (page 2): Choose how you submitted your feedback from the provided options.

## Tips
* Please provide clear and concise feedback.
* Be as specific as possible when suggesting improvements.
* Choose the correct area that is most in need of improvement.
* If you have any follow-up questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out.
