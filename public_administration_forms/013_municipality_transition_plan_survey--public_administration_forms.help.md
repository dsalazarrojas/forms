# Municipality Transition Plan Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information from municipalities on their transition plan, readiness, and any concerns they may have. The goal is to understand the current state of the municipalities' systems and processes and identify areas that need improvement or support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read the introduction section carefully to understand the context of the survey.
* Answer the readiness question by selecting a number between 1 and 5, indicating how ready you are to transition to a new system.
* Answer the concerns question by selecting one of the three options: Yes, No, or Maybe.
* Select all the improvement ideas that apply to your current situation.
* Fill in your contact information, including email and phone number.
* Provide any additional comments or feedback in the comments field.
* If you are aware of any stakeholders involved in the transition process, select them from the list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, optional): This is a brief introduction to the survey, explaining the purpose and context. It is not a required field, so you can skip it if you wish.
* **Readiness** (`readiness`, `number`, optional): Please answer on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 means not ready and 5 means fully ready for the transition.
* **Concerns** (`concerns`, `select_one`, optional): Please select one of the three options: Yes, No, or Maybe. This section is to identify any concerns you may have about the transition process.
* **Improvement Ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the ideas that apply to your current situation. These ideas will be reviewed and taken into consideration during the transition process.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `email`, optional): Fill in your email address so we can reach out to you for further communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Provide your phone number so we can contact you for further discussion or questions.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): This is an open-ended field for any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **Stakeholders** (`stakeholder`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you are aware of any stakeholders involved in the transition process, select them from the list.
