# Government Workshop Participant Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Government Workshop Participant Engagement Survey is designed to gather feedback from participants on their experience and suggestions for improvement. It helps organizers assess the overall engagement and satisfaction of the workshop, identifying areas of improvement and ensuring future events meet participant expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the survey options that best describe your experience.
2. Provide your name, email, and phone number (if applicable).
3. Rate your overall satisfaction with the workshop.
4. Share your comments and suggestions for improvement.
5. Indicate areas of the workshop that need improvement.
6. Provide any additional suggestions or comments.
7. Rate your overall experience (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Start** (`id: 1`, `type: select_one`): Choose whether you started the workshop (`Yes`) or not (`No`).
* **Participant Name** (`id: 2`, `type: text`): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the survey results.
* **Participant Email** (`id: 3`, `type: email`): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you for further discussion (if needed).
* **Participant Phone** (`id: 4`, `type: text`): Enter your phone number for us to contact you (if applicable).
* **Workshop Satisfaction** (`id: 5`, `type: select_multiple`): Select how satisfied you were with the workshop, using the following options:
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
* **Learning Experience** (`id: 6`, `type: number`): Rate your learning experience at the workshop, on a numerical scale (e.g., 1-10).
* **Participant Engagement** (`id: 7`, `type: date`): Choose a specific date to indicate your engagement with the workshop.
* **Participant Comments** (`id: 8`, `type: note`): Share any comments or feedback you have about the workshop.
* **Workshop Improvement Areas** (`id: 9`, `type: select_multiple`): Indicate areas of the workshop that need improvement, using the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Participant Suggestions** (`id: 10`, `type: text`): Provide any suggestions for improving future workshops.
* **Participant Rating** (`id: 11`, `type: number`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the workshop, on a numerical scale (e.g., 1-10).
* **Participant Recommend** (`id: 12`, `type: select_one`): Let us know if you would recommend the workshop to others, using the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate when providing feedback.
* Your comments and suggestions will help us improve the workshop.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
