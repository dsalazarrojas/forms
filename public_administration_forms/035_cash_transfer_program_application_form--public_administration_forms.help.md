# Cash Transfer Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from applicants who are applying for the Cash Transfer Program. This form is designed to assess the eligibility of applicants and determine the suitability of their requests for cash transfers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Application Information** section with your application date and the name of the Cash Transfer Program you are applying for.
2. In the **Applicant Information** section, provide your full name, date of birth, national ID number or passport, phone number, and email address.
3. In the **Residential Address** section, enter your street address, city, state or province, and postal code.
4. In the **Household Information** section, indicate the size of your household and how many dependents you have.
5. In the **Eligibility Information** section, select whether your household income meets the program criteria and indicate your residency status.
6. In the **Assistance Needed** section, indicate whether you need assistance and explain the amount of cash transfer you are requesting.
7. In the **Intended Use** section, describe how you plan to use the cash transfer funds.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Date**: Enter the date of your application (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Program Name**: Select the name of the Cash Transfer Program you are applying for.
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **National ID Number or Passport**: Enter your national ID number or passport number.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
* **Street Address**: Enter your street address.
* **City**: Enter the city of your residential address.
* **State or Province**: Enter the state or province of your residence.
* **Postal Code**: Enter the postal code of your residential address.
* **Household Size**: Indicate the number of people in your household.
* **Dependents**: Indicate the number of dependents you have.
* **Monthly Household Income**: Enter your monthly household income.
* **Sources of Household Income**: Select all sources of income for your household (e.g., employment, self-employment, social benefits, pension, informal work, family support, or other).
* **Income Meets Criteria**: Select whether your household income meets the program criteria.
* **Residency Status**: Select your residency status (permanent resident, temporary resident, citizen, or other).
* **Assistance Needed**: Indicate whether you need assistance and explain the amount of cash transfer you are requesting.
* **Cash Transfer Amount Requested**: Enter the dollar amount you are requesting for the cash transfer.
* **Intended Use**: Describe how you plan to use the cash transfer funds.
