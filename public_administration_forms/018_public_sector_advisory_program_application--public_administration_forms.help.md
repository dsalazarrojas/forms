# public_sector_advisory_program_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect application details from candidates for public sector advisory programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information before submitting.
2. Fill out the form accurately and truthfully, as any false or misleading information may disqualify your application.
3. Attach any required supporting documents, which will be specified in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Details** (`candidate_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name and other personal details as requested.
* **Experience** (`experience`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate your relevant experience, selecting either "Yes" or "No" depending on your situation.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the option that best suits your availability for the advisory program.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, `text`, required: false): Upload any supporting documents that may enhance your application, as specified in the form.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and truthfully.
* Attach all required supporting documents as specified in the form.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure completeness and accuracy.
