# Public Service Performance Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Service Performance Report Form is designed to collect data on the performance of a public service. This form helps users evaluate their experience and provide feedback to improve the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the public service you are reporting on in the **public_service_name** field.
2. Provide your rating of the service in the **rating** field.
3. Choose your rating level from the following options: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5.
4. In the **feedback** field, provide any comments or concerns you have about the service.
5. In the **suggestion** field, offer any suggestions or ideas for improving the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **public_service_name**: 
  This field is where you enter the name of the public service being reported on.
  Type: text
  Required: false
* **rating**: 
  This field is where you select a rating for the service, with 1 being lowest and 5 being highest.
  Type: number
  Required: false
* **service_level**: 
  This field is where you choose a service level from the options provided, indicating how well you think the service performs.
  Type: select_one
  Required: false
  Options: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5
* **feedback**: 
  This field is where you provide any comments or concerns you have about the service.
  Type: text
  Required: false
* **suggestion**: 
  This field is where you offer any suggestions or ideas for improving the service.
  Type: text
  Required: false
