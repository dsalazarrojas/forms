# Campus Vehicle Policy Petition - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Campus Vehicle Policy Petition form is used to propose changes to the campus vehicle policy, allowing students, faculty, and staff to suggest improvements or modifications to various aspects of vehicle-related policies on campus.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Provide Your Contact Information**: 
   - Fill in your full name in the "Petitioner Full Name" field.
   - Enter your email address in the "Petitioner Email Address" field for future contact.
   - If applicable, enter your phone number in the "Petitioner Phone Number" field.

2. **Identify the Petitioner**: 
   - Enter your university student identification number in the "Student ID Number" field if you are a student.
   - If you are not a student, leave this field blank.

3. **Describe the Petition**: 
   - In the "Petition Title" field, provide a brief title summarizing the purpose of your proposal.
   - In the "Petition Description" field, provide a detailed description of the proposed policy change.
   - Explain the "Current Issue or Problem" this petition aims to solve, and suggest your "Proposed Solution" in the respective fields.
   - Specify the "Expected Benefits" of the proposed change to the campus community.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Petitioner Full Name** (`petitioner_name`, text, required):
  - Name of the person leading this petition.

* **Petitioner Email Address** (`petitioner_email`, email, required):
  - Contact email for petition updates.

* **Petitioner Phone Number** (`petitioner_phone`, text, optional):
  - Phone number for follow-up questions.

* **Student ID Number** (`student_id`, text, required for students):
  - University student identification number.

* **Organization or Group Name** (`organization_name`, text, optional):
  - Name of student organization or group submitting the petition.

* **Petition Title** (`petition_title`, text, required):
  - Brief title for your vehicle policy petition.

* **Policy Area Affected** (`policy_area`, select_one, required):
  - Which area of vehicle policy does this address (e.g., parking allocation, electric vehicle charging, bicycle infrastructure).

* **Petition Description** (`petition_description`, text, required):
  - Detailed description of the proposed policy change.

* **Current Issue or Problem** (`current_issue`, text, required):
  - Explain the problem this petition aims to solve.

* **Proposed Solution** (`proposed_solution`, text, required):
  - Describe your recommended policy change.

* **Expected Benefits** (`expected_benefits`, text, required):
  - How will this change benefit the campus community.

* **Groups Affected by This Change** (`affected_groups`, select_multiple, required):
  - Select all groups that would be impacted by this policy change.

* **Suggested Implementation Timeline** (`implementation_timeline`, select_one, optional):
  - When should this policy take effect (e.g., immediate, next semester, next academic year, phased rollout, to be determined)?

* **Estimated Budget Impact** (`budget_impact`, select_one, required):
  - Will this policy require funding, and if so, is it under/over $1000/10000, or unknown at this time.

* **Supporting Documents Available** (`supporting_documents`, select_one, optional):
  - Do you have documents to support this petition (yes, no, or requesting assistance)?

* **Faculty Sponsor Name** (`faculty_sponsor`, text, optional):
  - Optional faculty advisor or sponsor.

* **Petition Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required):
  - Date of petition submission.

* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional):
  - Any other information to support your petition.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
- Be as specific as possible when describing the problem and proposed solution.
- If you have supporting documents, consider attaching them with your submission.
- If you need assistance, don't hesitate to contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
