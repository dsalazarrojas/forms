# City Public Safety Campaign Response Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a crucial tool to gather public feedback on the City's Public Safety Campaign. It aims to gauge the effectiveness of the campaign's messages, the public's level of awareness, and their willingness to participate in public safety initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name (if you wish to be contacted for follow-up).
2. Indicate the neighborhood or area you live or work in.
3. Rate your awareness of the campaign, with 1 indicating not aware and 5 indicating very aware.
4. Rate the clarity of the campaign's messages, with 1 indicating unclear and 5 indicating clear.
5. Select how likely you are to change your behavior due to the campaign.
6. Choose all the communication channels you prefer to receive information from the campaign.
7. Provide any suggestions you have for improving the campaign.
8. (Optional) Indicate your level of trust in public safety authorities.
9. (Optional) Consent to be contacted for follow-up purposes.
10. Enter the current date for the submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent name** (`respondent_name`, text, Optional): Enter your name if you wish to be contacted for follow-up purposes.
* **Neighborhood or area** (`neighborhood`, text, Required): Select the neighborhood or area where you live or work.
* **Awareness of campaign** (`awareness_level`, number, Required): Rate your awareness of the campaign, with 1 indicating not aware and 5 indicating very aware.
* **Clarity of campaign messages** (`message_clarity`, number, Required): Rate the clarity of the campaign's messages, with 1 indicating unclear and 5 indicating clear.
* **Likelihood of behavior change** (`behavior_change_intent`, select_one, Required): Select how likely you are to change your behavior due to the campaign.
	+ Very likely
	+ Somewhat likely
	+ Unlikely
* **Preferred communication channels** (`preferred_channels`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose all the communication channels you prefer to receive information from the campaign.
	+ Social media
	+ Local radio
	+ Flyers
	+ Community meetings
* **Suggestions to improve campaign** (`campaign_improvement_suggestions`, text, Optional): Provide any suggestions you have for improving the campaign.
* **Trust in public safety authorities** (`trust_in_authorities`, number, Optional): Rate your trust in public safety authorities, with 1 indicating low and 5 indicating high.
* **Consent for follow-up** (`follow_up_consent`, select_one, Optional): Indicate if you consent to be contacted for follow-up purposes.
	+ True
	+ False
* **Submission date** (`submission_date`, date, Required): Enter the current date for the submission.
