# Parks Investment Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Parks Investment Assessment form is used to gather information about parks in a public administration context. The form helps park administrators and consultants assess a park's investment needs and potential for improvement. It collects data on various aspects of a park, including its characteristics, conditions, and potential improvement priorities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please follow these steps:
   - Fill in the site's name, type, location, size, and condition rating.
   - Choose the improvement priority for the site.
   - Provide a brief description of the site.
   - Enter the site's address.
   - Describe the site's status.
   - Enter any improvement recommendations.
   - Suggest funding options.
   - Enter any consultant recommendations.
   - Enter the consultant's name, email, and phone number.
   - Enter the municipality's name and email.
2. Answer each question accurately and completely, taking care to note any missing or unclear information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site Name**: A text field for the park's name.
* **Site Type**: A text field for the type of park (e.g., playground, sports field, etc.).
* **Site Location**: A text field for the park's location (e.g., address, coordinates, etc.).
* **Site Size**: A numerical field representing the park's size.
* **Condition Rating**: A numerical field representing the park's condition rating (likely a scale from 1-5 or 1-10).
* **Improvement Priority**: A select-one field with options (likely High, Medium, Low).
* **Site Description**: A text field for a brief description of the park.
* **Site Address**: A text field for the park's address.
* **Site Status**: A text field for the park's status.
* **Improvement Recommendations**: A text field for any suggested improvements.
* **Funding Suggestion**: A text field for any funding suggestions.
* **Consultant Recommendation**: A text field for any consultant recommendations.
* **Consultant Name**: A text field for the consultant's name.
* **Consultant Email**: An email field for the consultant's email address.
* **Consultant Phone**: A text field for the consultant's phone number.
* **Municipality Name**: A text field for the municipality's name.
* **Municipality Email**: An email field for the municipality's email address.
