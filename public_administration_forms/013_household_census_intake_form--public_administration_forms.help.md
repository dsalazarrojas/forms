# Household Census Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a household for public administration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), which are indicated as "required" in the form.
2. Fill out all other fields according to your knowledge and availability of the information.
3. Make sure to review your input carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Head of Household**: Enter the name of the person in charge of the household.
* *household_head_of_household* (ID 2)
* *household_number_of_household_members* (ID 3): Enter the number of people living in the household.
* *household_gender* (ID 4): Select the gender of the household head from the options provided.
* *household_income* (ID 5): Enter the annual income of the household.
* *household_language* (ID 6): Select all languages spoken by members of the household.
* *household_ethnicity* (ID 7): Select the ethnicity of the household head.
* *household_race* (ID 8): Select the race of the household head.
* *household_age* (ID 9): Enter the age of the household head.
* *household_address* (ID 10): Enter the address of the household.
* *household_email* (ID 11): Enter a valid email address of the household.
* *household_phone* (ID 12): Enter a valid phone number of the household.
* *household_comments* (ID 13): Provide any additional comments or notes about the household.
* *household_additional_information* (ID 14): Enter any additional information about the household.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are filling out this form for the first time and not revisiting previous submissions.
* Double-check your input before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you need help or have questions, contact us!
