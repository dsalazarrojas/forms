# Fishing License Replacement Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fishing License Replacement Application form is used to replace or renew an existing fishing license. This form is intended for administrative or official purposes within the fishing department or authority.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and documents required for the replacement or renewal application.
2. Fill in the "Name" field with your full name as it appears on your fishing license.
3. In the "User Info" field, select your title (Mr, Mrs, or Ms).
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Type your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
6. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
7. In the "Select One" field, choose the relevant option for your application.
8. If applicable, select multiple options in the "Select Multiple" field.
9. Enter your date of birth in the "Date" field (if required).
10. Choose the correct time in the "Time" field (if required).
11. Upload the required document in the "Upload Document" field.
12. Review your answers and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title (User Info)** (`user_info`, select, required: false): Select your title (Mr, Mrs, or Ms) to identify yourself.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: true): Enter your full name as it appears on your fishing license.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Type in your phone number where you can be reached (optional).
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: true): Enter your address as it is registered on your fishing license.
* **Document** (`document`, text, required: false): Briefly describe or note any relevant details about your fishing license.
* **Upload Document** (`upload_document`, text, required: false): Upload any supporting documents required for the replacement or renewal process.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): Review and submit your application.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth if required for the application.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Choose the correct time for your application.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, required: true): Choose the relevant option for your application from the provided choices.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required: false): Select multiple options that apply to your application.
* **Decimal** (`decimal`, number, required: true): Enter a decimal value as required for the application.
* **Integer** (`integer`, number, required: true): Enter an integer value as required for the application.
* **Time 2** (`time_2`, time, required: true): Choose the correct time for your application.
* **Select One 2** (`select_one_2`, select_one, required: false): Choose an option from the provided choices.
* **Select Multiple 2** (`select_multiple_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Select multiple options as required for the application.
* **Date 2** (`date_2`, date, required: true): Enter your date as required for the application.
* **Submit 2** (`submit_2`, text, required: false): Review and submit your application.

## Tips
- Please ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
- Make sure to upload any required supporting documents.
- Review your application carefully before submission.
- If you have any issues or concerns, contact the relevant authority for assistance.
