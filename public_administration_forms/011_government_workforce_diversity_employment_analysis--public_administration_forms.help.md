# Government Workforce Diversity Employment Analysis - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on the diversity and employment aspects of government employees. It will help to analyze and improve the overall diversity and inclusivity within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and ensure you're filling out the correct information.
2. Select the correct options for each field based on your experience and knowledge.
3. Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Diversity** (1): Select 'Yes' if you identify as diverse, and 'No' if you don't.
* **Job Category** (2): Choose the category that best describes your job function.
* **Job Title** (3): Enter your job title as it appears on your official government job posting.
* **Job Grade** (4): Select the grade of your job, which should match your official government job posting.
* **Department** (5): Enter your department name as it appears on your official government job posting.
* **Supervisor Name** (6): Enter your supervisor's name as it appears on your official government job posting.
* **Supervisor Email** (7): Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Job Start Date** (8): Enter the date you started your job in the format YYYY-MM-YYYY.
* **Job End Date** (9): Enter the date you ended your job in the format YYYY-MM-YYYY. If you're still employed, leave this field blank.
* **Ethnicity** (10): Select 'Yes' if you identify as diverse, and 'No' if you don't.
* **Gender** (11): Select your gender, which should match your official government documents.
* **Age** (12): Enter your age in years.
* **Education** (13): Enter your highest level of education completed.
* **Job Performance** (14): Enter any relevant job performance notes or achievements.
* **Diversity Awareness** (15): Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Work-Life Balance** (16): Select one option that best describes your work-life balance.
* **Work Satisfaction** (17): Select one option that best describes your work satisfaction.
* **Job Satisfaction** (18): Select one option that best describes your job satisfaction.
* **Employee Satisfaction** (19): Select one option that best describes your job satisfaction.
* **Employee Awareness** (20): Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Job Awareness** (21): Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Job Diversity Commitment** (22): Select one option that best describes your job diversity commitment.
* **Department Awareness** (23): Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Job Security** (24): Select one option that best describes your job security.
* **Diversity Inclusion** (25): Enter any diversity and inclusion initiatives or programs you're involved in or support.
