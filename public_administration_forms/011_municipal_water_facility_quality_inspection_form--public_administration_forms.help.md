# Municipal Water Facility Quality Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the quality inspection of a municipal water facility. It is intended to be completed by facility inspectors to document the results of their inspections, including the facility's condition, score, and rating.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the municipal water facility being inspected.
2. **Inspector Name**: Enter your name and contact information.
3. **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the inspection.
4. **Inspection Time**: Enter the time of the inspection.
5. **Inspection Location**: Enter the location of the inspection.
6. **Facility Type**: Enter the type of facility being inspected (e.g. water treatment plant, pumping station, etc.).
7. **Facility ID**: Enter the ID number of the facility being inspected.
8. **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
9. **Email**: Enter the email address of the facility's contact person.
10. **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the facility's contact person.
11. **Address**: Enter the address of the facility.
12. **City**: Enter the city of the facility.
13. **State**: Enter the state of the facility.
14. **Zip Code**: Enter the zip code of the facility.
15. **Facility Status**: Select the status of the facility (Active, Inactive, Closed).
16. **Facility Condition**: Select the condition of the facility (Good, Fair, Poor).
17. **Facility Score**: Enter the score of the facility (a numeric value).
18. **Facility Rating**: Enter the rating of the facility (a numeric value).
19. **Facility Inspection Frequency**: Enter the frequency of inspections (e.g. monthly, quarterly, annually).
20. **Facility Last Inspection**: Enter the date of the last inspection.
21. **Facility Next Inspection**: Enter the date of the next scheduled inspection.
22. **Facility Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the facility.
23. **Facility Email**: Enter the email address of the facility's contact person.
24. **Facility Phone**: Enter the phone number of the facility's contact person.
25. **Facility Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the facility.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the municipal water facility being inspected.
* **Inspector Name**: Enter your name and contact information, so we can reach out to you with any questions or concerns.
* **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the inspection, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Inspection Time**: Enter the time of the inspection, in the format 12:00 PM or 1:00 PM etc.
* **Inspection Location**: Enter the location of the inspection, so we can keep track of which facility you visited.
* **Facility Type**: Enter the type of facility being inspected (e.g. water treatment plant, pumping station, etc.).
* **Facility ID**: Enter the ID number of the facility being inspected.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection, so we can better understand the findings.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the facility's contact person, in case we need to follow up with them.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the facility's contact person, in case we need to contact them.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the facility, so we can map it and keep track of its location.
* **City**: Enter the city of the facility, so we can keep track of its location.
* **State**: Enter the state of the facility, so we can keep track of its location.
* **Zip Code**: Enter the zip code of the facility, so we can keep track of its location.
* **Facility Status**: Select the status of the facility, which indicates whether it is active, inactive, or closed.
* **Facility Condition**: Select the condition of the facility, which indicates whether it is good, fair, or poor.
* **Facility Score**: Enter a numeric value that represents the facility's score, out of a total possible score.
* **Facility Rating**: Enter a numeric value that represents the facility's rating, out of a total possible rating.
* **Facility Inspection Frequency**: Enter the frequency of inspections for the facility, e.g. monthly, quarterly, or annually.
* **Facility Last Inspection**: Enter the date of the last inspection, so we can keep track of when the facility was last inspected.
* **Facility Next Inspection**: Enter the date of the next scheduled inspection, so we can keep track of when the facility is due for its next inspection.
* **Facility Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the facility.
* **Facility Email**: Enter the email address of the facility's contact person.
* **Facility Phone**: Enter the phone number of the facility's contact person.
* **Facility Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the facility.

Tips:
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out this form, so we can get a comprehensive understanding of the facility's condition.
* If you need to add any additional fields or categories, please contact us and we will consider your request.
