# Community Center Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Center Admission Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in becoming members at the center. This form helps us understand your personal details, contact information, and preferences to provide you with a smooth membership experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal details in the form fields below.
2. Select the correct membership type and programs you are interested in.
3. List any medical conditions or allergies we should be aware of.
4. Indicate if you require any accessibility accommodations.
5. Let us know how you heard about the community center.
6. Check the box agreeing to our terms and conditions.
7. Finally, confirm your date of signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, Text, Required): Please enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name** (2, Text, Required): Please enter your legal last name.
* **Date of Birth** (3, Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Gender** (4, Select One, Required): Select your gender identity from the options provided.
* **Street Address** (5, Text, Required): Enter your current residential street address.
* **City** (6, Text, Required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (7, Text, Required): Enter the state or province where you reside.
* **ZIP Code** (8, Text, Required): Enter your postal or zip code.
* **Phone Number** (9, Text, Required): Enter your primary contact phone number.
* **Email Address** (10, Email, Required): Enter your primary email address for communication.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (11, Text, Required): Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (12, Text, Required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (13, Text, Required): Describe your relationship to your emergency contact.
* **Membership Type** (14, Select One, Required): Select the type of membership you are applying for.
* **Programs Interested In** (15, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the programs you would like to participate in.
* **Medical Conditions or Allergies** (16, Text, Optional): List any medical conditions or allergies we should be aware of.
* **Accessibility Needs** (17, Text, Optional): Indicate if you require any accessibility accommodations.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (18, Select One, Optional): Let us know how you heard about the community center.
* **I Agree to Terms and Conditions** (19, Select One, Required): Check this box to agree to our center policies.
* **Date of Signature** (20, Date, Required): Confirm your date of signature as today's date.

## Tips
Please ensure all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly. If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for assistance.
