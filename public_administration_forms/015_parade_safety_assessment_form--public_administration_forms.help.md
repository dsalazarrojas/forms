# Parade Safety Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parade Safety Assessment Form is designed to help parade organizers assess and mitigate potential safety risks associated with their events. This form gathers critical information to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for participants and spectators.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of event you are organizing: Parade Float, Parade March, or Parade Float & March Combination.
2.  Provide emergency contact information, including a name and any relevant contact details.
3.  Enter the contact name and contact information.
4.  Enter your email address (if you wish to be contacted for further information).
5.  Describe the event information in as much detail as possible.
6.  Specify the event route.
7.  Assess and document the potential safety risks associated with your event.
8.  Outline your safety plan to address these risks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **Parade Organiser Info** (`parade_organizer_info`, `note`, required): This field is for you to provide your details and any relevant contact information for emergency response.
*  **Event Details** (`event_details`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of event you are organizing: Parade Float, Parade March, or Parade Float & March Combination.
*  **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information about emergency contacts, such as phone numbers or email addresses.
*  **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the event contact.
*  **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required): Enter your contact information, such as your organization's address or phone number.
*  **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for further information.
*  **Event Info** (`event_info`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional details about your event.
*  **Event Route** (`event_route`, `text`, required): Enter the specific route or path your event will follow.
*  **Safety Risks** (`safety_risk`, `text`, optional): Assess and document any potential safety risks associated with your event.
*  **Safety Plan** (`safety_plan`, `text`, required): Outline your safety plan to address the identified risks.
*  **Review** (`review`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or review about your event.

This form is designed to ensure a safe and enjoyable parade experience. Please take the time to fill out each field accurately and thoroughly, as the information collected will help us assess and improve your event's safety and organization.
