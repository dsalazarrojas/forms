# Police Report Amendment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Police Report Amendment Request Form is used for requesting changes or modifications to previously submitted police reports.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your personal details as requested, including first name, last name, and date of birth.
2.  Enter the name of the police officer who is submitting the request.
3.  Enter the date the request is being made.
4.  Provide a detailed description of the reason for the request.
5.  If necessary, provide additional information about the department, unit, and division involved.
6.  Enter contact information for the officer and their department.
7.  Provide contact information for the officer's supervisor.
8.  Review and submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, **required**): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
*   **Police Officer Name** (`police_officer_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your name as a police officer.
*   **Request Date** (`request_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date on which you are submitting this request.
*   **Description** (`description`, `text`, **required**): Provide a detailed explanation of the reason for the request.
*   **Reason** (`reason`, `text`, **optional**): If additional context is required, provide a brief explanation for the amendment request.
*   **Officer Signature** (`officer_signature`, `text`, **required**): Your signature as a police officer.
*   **Officer Title** (`officer_title`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your title or rank within the department.
*   **Department** (`department`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the department you are associated with.
*   **Division** (`division`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the division within your department.
*   **Unit** (`unit`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the unit within your division.
*   **Officer Phone** (`officer_phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter your department-issued phone number.
*   **Officer Email** (`officer_email`, `text`, **required**): Enter your department-issued email address.
*   **Department Phone** (`department_phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter your department's phone number.
*   **Department Email** (`department_email`, `text`, **required**): Enter your department's email address.
*   **Supervisor Name** (`officer_supervisor_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter the name of your direct supervisor.
*   **Supervisor Department** (`officer_supervisor_department`, `text`, **required**): Enter your supervisor's department.
*   **Supervisor Title** (`officer_supervisor_title`, `text`, **required**): Enter your supervisor's title or rank.
*   **Supervisor Phone** (`officer_supervisor_phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter your supervisor's department-issued phone number.
*   **Supervisor Email** (`officer_supervisor_email`, `text`, **required**): Enter your supervisor's department-issued email address.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete.
*   If you are unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or department administrator before submitting the form.
*   The submitted information will be reviewed and verified before any changes are made to the original report.
*   Delays in the amendment process may occur due to errors or discrepancies in the submitted information.
