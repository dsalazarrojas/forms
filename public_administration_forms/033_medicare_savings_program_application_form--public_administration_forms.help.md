# Medicare Savings Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medicare Savings Program Application Form is designed to collect information from individuals who are applying for the Medicare Savings Program. The form is used to verify applicant details, contact information, and eligibility status, ensuring accurate and efficient processing of the application.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the applicant's details, including their name and any other relevant information.
2. Provide contact information, such as email and phone number.
3. Indicate the applicant's Medicare Savings Program eligibility status (Eligible, Partially Eligible, or Not Eligible).
4. Enter the applicant's annual income.
5. Enter the applicant's date of birth.
6. Enter the applicant's Social Security number.
7. Enter the applicant's email address.
8. Enter the applicant's phone number.
9. Provide any additional comments about the application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Applicant Details
* **Applicant Details**: (`applicant_details`, text, optional): Enter the applicant's name and any other relevant information.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information**: (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter the applicant's email address and phone number.

### Medicare Savings Program Eligibility
* **Medicare Savings Program Eligibility**: (`medicare_savings_program_eligibility`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Eligible: If you are eligible for the Medicare Savings Program.
	+ Partially Eligible: If you are partially eligible for the Medicare Savings Program.
	+ Not Eligible: If you are not eligible for the Medicare Savings Program.

### Income Information
* **Income Information**: (`income_info`, number, optional): Enter the applicant's annual income.

### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth**: (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the applicant's date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

### Social Security Number
* **Social Security Number**: (`social_security_number`, text, optional): Enter the applicant's Social Security number.

### Email
* **Email**: (`email`, email, optional): Enter the applicant's email address.

### Phone
* **Phone**: (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the applicant's phone number.

### Medicare Savings Program Comments
* **Medicare Savings Program Comments**: (`medicare_savings_program_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the application.
