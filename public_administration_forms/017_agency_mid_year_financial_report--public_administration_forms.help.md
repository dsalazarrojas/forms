# Agency Mid Year Financial Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Agency Mid Year Financial Report is a form used to report on the financial status of an agency as of the mid-year point. This report is typically submitted to provide a snapshot of the agency's revenue and expenses for the first half of the fiscal year.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the agency name, reporting period, and the name and title of the person preparing the report.
2. Enter the total budgeted and actual revenue for the year to date.
3. Enter the variance between budgeted and actual revenue as a dollar amount and as a percentage.
4. Fill in the total budgeted and actual expenses for the year to date.
5. Enter the variance between budgeted and actual expenses as a dollar amount and as a percentage.
6. Indicate whether any budget adjustments have been made during the year, and provide details if applicable.
7. Provide a brief outlook for the remainder of the fiscal year.
8. Provide any recommendations for improving financial performance.
9. Add a digital signature of the authorized official.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agency Name** (`agency_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the agency.
* **Reporting Period** (`reporting_period`, text, required): Enter the fiscal year and period being reported.
* **Prepared By Name** (`prepared_by_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person preparing this report.
* **Prepared By Title** (`prepared_by_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the person preparing this report.
* **Report Date** (`report_date`, date, required): Enter the date this report is being submitted.
* **Revenue Section Header** (`revenue_section_header`, note, not required): This is a header for the revenue section.
* **Budgeted Revenue (Year to Date)** (`budgeted_revenue`, number, required): Enter the total budgeted revenue for the year to date.
* **Actual Revenue (Year to Date)** (`actual_revenue`, number, required): Enter the actual revenue received for the year to date.
* **Revenue Variance** (`revenue_variance`, number, required): Enter the variance between budgeted and actual revenue as a dollar amount.
* **Revenue Variance Percentage** (`revenue_variance_percentage`, number, required): Enter the variance as a percentage of budgeted revenue.
* **Expense Section Header** (`expense_section_header`, note, not required): This is a header for the expense section.
* **Budgeted Expenses (Year to Date)** (`budgeted_expenses`, number, required): Enter the total budgeted expenses for the year to date.
* **Actual Expenses (Year to Date)** (`actual_expenses`, number, required): Enter the actual expenses incurred for the year to date.
* **Expense Variance** (`expense_variance`, number, required): Enter the variance between budgeted and actual expenses as a dollar amount.
* **Expense Variance Percentage** (`expense_variance_percentage`, number, required): Enter the variance as a percentage of budgeted expenses.
* **Budget Adjustments Made** (`budget_adjustments`, select_one, required): Indicate whether any budget adjustments have been made during the year. Select 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Budget Adjustment Details** (`budget_adjustment_details`, text, not required): If budget adjustments were made, provide details about the adjustments.
* **Financial Outlook** (`financial_outlook`, text, not required): Provide a brief outlook for the remainder of the fiscal year.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, not required): Provide any recommendations for improving financial performance.
* **Authorized Signature** (`authorized_signature`, text, required): Digital signature of the authorized official.

## Tips
- Double-check your calculations for accuracy.
- If budget adjustments were made, ensure they are properly documented.
- Be thorough in providing details for any adjustments or recommendations.
- Make sure to submit the report on the designated report date.
