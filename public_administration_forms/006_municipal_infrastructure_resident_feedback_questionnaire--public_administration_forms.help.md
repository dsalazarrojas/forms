# municipal_infrastructure_resident_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from residents about the quality and availability of local services in their neighborhood.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the options that best describe your experience with local services in your neighborhood.
2. Rate the quality of local services and the condition of your neighborhood.
3. Indicate your level of satisfaction with your community.
4. Enter your contact information if you would like to be contacted for further discussion.
5. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What are you currently experiencing in your neighborhood?** (service_availability): Please select all that apply from the available options.
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Excellent
* **How would you rate the quality of local services?** (service_quality): Rate the quality of local services from 1 (lowest) to 10 (highest).
* **How would you rate the condition of your neighborhood?** (neighborhood_conditions): Select one option that best describes your opinion.
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Excellent
* **How satisfied are you with your community?** (community_satisfaction): Select one option that best describes your level of satisfaction.
	+ Very dissatisfied
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat satisfied
	+ Very satisfied
* **What is your contact information?** (contact_info): Enter your email address or phone number if you would like to be contacted for further discussion.
* **Any other comments or suggestions?** (comments): Provide any additional feedback or suggestions you have about local services.
* **How frequently do you use local services?** (service_frequency): Rate how frequently you use local services from 1 (lowest) to 10 (highest).
* **How important are local services?** (services_importance): Select all that apply from the available options.
	+ Very important
	+ Somewhat important
	+ Not very important
	+ Not at all important
