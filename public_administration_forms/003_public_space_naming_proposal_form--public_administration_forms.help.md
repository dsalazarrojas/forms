# Public Space Naming Proposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Space Naming Proposal Form is used to propose a new public space in our community. A public space is a designated area within our community that is open to the public. This form is designed to gather essential information from you, the proposer, to facilitate the evaluation and approval of your proposal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by giving a title to your proposal.
2. Provide the name of the park you are proposing.
3. Describe the location of the park.
4. Write a brief description of the proposed public space.
5. Enter your name and contact information so we can get in touch with you to discuss your proposal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Public Space Naming Proposal Form** (1): 
This is the title of your proposal, which will help us identify your submission.
- **Park Name** (2): 
Enter the name of the park you are proposing, which can be any name you think fits.
- **Name of Park** (3): 
This is where you describe the name of the park in more detail.
- **Location** (4): 
Please provide the address or coordinates of the park.
- **Description** (5): 
Write a brief description of the park you are proposing, including its features and amenities.
- **Name** (6): 
Enter your name, so we can get in touch with you to discuss your proposal.
- **Email** (7): 
Enter your email address so we can contact you about your proposal.
- **Phone** (8): 
Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you about your proposal.
- **Submit** (9): 
This is where you submit your proposal. Fill out all the fields and click submit to send it to us.
