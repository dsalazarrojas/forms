# Family Benefits Application Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers on their experience with the benefits application process. It helps us understand their satisfaction level, areas for improvement, and how we can better assist them in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your feedback on your recent benefits application experience.
2. Rate your overall satisfaction with the application process.
3. Provide suggestions on how we can improve the application form, website, customer service, and communication.
4. Choose the contact method you used to contact us (phone, email, walk-in, or online chat).
5. Select the contact person you spoke with (Jim, John, Jane, or another person).
6. Enter your contact number and email address.
7. Add any additional comments about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date): This is the date you submitted your benefits application.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note): Share your thoughts on your experience with the application process. Please be specific about what you liked or disliked.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`rating`, select one): Rate your overall satisfaction with the application process. Choose from Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestion`, select multiple): If you have any suggestions on how we can improve the application process, form, website, customer service, or communication, please choose all that apply.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_information`, select multiple): Choose how you contacted us (phone, email, walk-in, or online chat).
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, select one): If you spoke with one of our staff, please select their name (Jim, John, Jane, or another person).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text): Enter the number you used to contact us.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address that you used to contact us.
* **Name** (`name`, text): Enter your name for contact purposes.
* **Comments** (`comment`, note): Add any additional comments about your experience. This is optional, but it helps us understand your perspective better.
