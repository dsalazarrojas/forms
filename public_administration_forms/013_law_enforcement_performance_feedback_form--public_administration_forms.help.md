# Law Enforcement Performance Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from the public about their experiences with law enforcement in the community. It helps us understand how we can improve our services and provide better support to the community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant information about your interaction with the law enforcement officer, such as "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
2. Provide a brief description of what happened during the interaction.
3. Enter the date and time of the incident, if known.
4. Specify the location of the incident.
5. Mention the officer in charge.
6. Add any additional comments or information about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Police Interaction** (`police_interaction`): Select the quality of your interaction with the law enforcement officer, using the options "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Feedback** (`feedback`): Provide a brief description of what happened during the interaction, including the officer's behavior, actions, or response to your concerns.
* **Date of Incident** (`date_of_incident`): Enter the date of the incident, if known. If you're unsure or don't know the exact date, please provide the approximate date or the date range (e.g., "Last week" or "Sometime in February").
* **Time of Incident** (`time_of_incident`): Enter the time of the incident, if known. If you're unsure or don't know the exact time, please provide the approximate time or the time range (e.g., "Late morning" or "Between 10am-12pm").
* **Location of Incident** (`location_of_incident`): Specify the location where the incident occurred.
* **Officer in Charge** (`officer_in_charge`): Identify the officer in charge during the incident, if known.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Add any extra information or comments about your experience that might not fit in the other fields. This is your opportunity to provide any additional details that can help us better understand your situation.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when providing feedback.
* Be specific about your experience, including details about the officer's behavior and actions.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or guidance.
* Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve our services and provide better support to the community.
