# Petition For Regulatory Review - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for regulatory review purposes, allowing agencies to provide a formal petition for review of a decision or action. The form serves as a means to document and track the review process, ensuring that all relevant information is collected and assessed thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form title to provide a brief description of the petition.
2. Enter the agency's name that the petition is submitted to.
3. Provide the agency's phone number for contact purposes.
4. Enter the agency's email address for communication and follow-up.
5. Select the date of the decision or action being petitioned for review, using the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (1, text, optional): A brief description of the petition.
* **Agency Name** (2, text, optional): The name of the agency the petition is submitted to.
* **Agency Phone Number** (3, text, optional): The phone number of the agency for contact purposes.
* **Agency Email** (4, email, optional): The email address of the agency for communication and follow-up.
* **Decision Date** (5, date, optional): The date of the decision or action being petitioned for review, using the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Tips

- Double-check the agency information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure the decision date is correctly formatted (MM/DD/YYYY).
- If any field requires correction or additional information, please contact the relevant agency.
