# social_care_market_failure_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback about your experience with the social care market, specifically when a failure occurred. Your input is valuable in helping us understand what went wrong and how we can improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your user information, including your name and any relevant contact details.
2. Describe the service you were interacting with when the failure occurred.
3. Provide feedback about your experience with the service, including any issues you encountered and suggestions for improvement.
4. Rate the service's policy effectiveness and identify any service gaps you experienced.
5. Rate the service's effectiveness and provide comments.
6. If you have any additional comments or observations, please include them in the 'Comments' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, required: false): Please provide your name and any relevant contact details so we can follow up with you for further clarification.
* **Service Info** (`service_info`, text, required: false): Describe the service you were interacting with when the failure occurred.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required: true): Provide a detailed description of your experience with the service, including any issues you encountered and suggestions for improvement.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): If you have an email address associated with your account, please enter it here so we can contact you for further clarification.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): If you have a phone number associated with your account, please enter it here so we can contact you for further clarification.
* **Date and time** (`date_and_time`, date, required: false): Please provide the date and time when the failure occurred.
* **Rating 1** (`rating_1`, select_one, required: true): Rate the service's overall quality (Good or Fair).
* **Rating 2** (`rating_2`, select_one, required: true): Rate the service's responsiveness (Good or Fair).
* **Policy Effectiveness** (`policy_effectiveness`, select_multiple, required: true): Indicate whether the policy was effective (Yes or No) in addressing the failure.
* **Service Gaps** (`service_gaps`, text, required: true): Identify any service gaps you experienced.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or observations you may have about the service.

Note: If you're unsure about any of the fields or their requirements, please don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.
