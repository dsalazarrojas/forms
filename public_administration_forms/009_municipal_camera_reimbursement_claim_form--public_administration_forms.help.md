# Municipal Camera Reimbursement Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Municipal Camera Reimbursement Claim Form is a tool for municipalities to submit claims for reimbursement of camera equipment used in their administration. This form collects essential information about the claimant, their contact details, camera equipment, and submission documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact details, including name, address, and phone number.
2. Describe the camera equipment and its usage in detail.
3. Upload submission documents, if required.
4. Verify your form submission and confirm its accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`): Enter your municipality's contact information, including name, address, phone number, and any other relevant details. This information will be used to contact you for further clarification or follow-up.
* **Camera Details** (`camera_details`): Describe the camera equipment used, including its type, make, model, and usage details. Be as specific as possible to ensure accurate reimbursement.
* **Submission Documents** (`submission_documents`): Upload any supporting documents for your claim, such as receipts, invoices, or certificates of ownership. Ensure they are relevant to your camera equipment and usage.
* **Upload Submission Documents** (`submission_documents_uploads` and `submission_documents_uploads_2`): You may be required to upload additional documents. These fields are for that purpose.
* **Submit Form** (`submit_form`): Once you have completed the form, click this button to submit your claim. Make sure to review your form for accuracy before submitting.
* **Confirm Form Submission** (`confirm_form_submission`): This field is not editable and serves as a confirmation message after submitting your form. Review it carefully to ensure your submission was successful.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Only upload necessary documents to support your claim.
* Double-check your submission for errors before clicking the 'Submit Form' button.
