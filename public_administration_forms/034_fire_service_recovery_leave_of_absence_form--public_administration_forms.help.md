# Fire Service Recovery Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Service Recovery Leave Of Absence Form is a crucial document used to manage employee leave of absence (LOA) requests in fire departments. This form is used to track and record essential information about an employee's leave, ensuring that the department follows the required procedures and policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Select the department name from the list of options.
3. Enter the date of the leave.
4. Enter the date of return.
5. Choose the reason for the leave from the list of options.
6. Enter the number of hours absent.
7. Enter any comments, if necessary.
8. Select the approval status, approver name and title, and review status, if applicable.
9. Choose the fire service officer responsible for the request.
10. Select the fire station and fire vehicle involved.
11. Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fire Service Form** (`form_section`, text, required): This section is for the main information about the employee's leave.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee requesting leave.
* **Department Name** (`department_name`, select_one, required): Select the department where the employee works.
* **Leave Date** (`leave_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the leave.
* **Return Date** (`return_date`, date, required): Enter the date of return.
* **Reason For Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, select_multiple, required): Choose the reason for the leave from the list of options.
* **Hours Absent** (`hours_absent`, number, required): Enter the number of hours absent.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments, if necessary.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_multiple, required): Select the current approval status.
* **Approver Name** (`approver_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the approver.
* **Approver Title** (`approver_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the approver.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the current review status.
* **Comments Reviewer** (`comments_reviewer`, text, optional): Enter any comments from the reviewer.
* **Approver Comments** (`approver_comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments from the approver.
* **Fire Service Officer** (`fire_service_officer`, select_multiple, required): Choose the fire service officer responsible for the request.
* **Fire Station** (`fire_station`, select_multiple, required): Select the fire station involved.
* **Fire Vehicle** (`fire_vehicle`, select_multiple, required): Select the fire vehicle involved.

## Tips
When filling out this form, ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information. If you're unsure about any of the fields, please ask the HR representative or the fire department's administration office for guidance.
