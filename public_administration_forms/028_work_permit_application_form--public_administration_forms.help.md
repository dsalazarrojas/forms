# Work Permit Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for employees to apply for a work permit, providing necessary details to facilitate the process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your contact information:
	* Employee Name
	* Employee Phone
	* Employee Email
	* Employee Address
2. Provide business details:
	* Business Name
	* Business Address
	* Business Phone
	* Business Email
3. Specify the work permit details:
	* Work Permit Type
	* Work Permit Start Date
	* Work Permit End Date
	* Work Permit Duration (select one of the options)
	* Work Permit Status (select one of the options)
4. Enter employee's job information:
	* Job Title
	* Employee Position
	* Work Type
	* Work Hours
5. Optional: Add any additional notes if necessary
