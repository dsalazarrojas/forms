# Case Closure Monthly Statistics Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Case Closure Monthly Statistics Report Form is used to gather and report on monthly statistics for cases that have been closed during that month, providing insight into the performance metrics and efficiency measures of the case closure process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form and understand the purpose and required fields.
2. Enter the required information in each field, including Report ID Number, Reporting Month, Reporting Year, and other relevant details.
3. Select the correct options for each field from the available choices.
4. Enter the relevant case statistics and metrics, such as cases opened, closed, and clearance rates.
5. Review and complete the performance metrics section for a detailed analysis of the case closure process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report ID Number** (`report_id`): Unique identifier for this report.
* **Reporting Month** (`report_month`): Select the month covered by this report.
* **Reporting Year** (`report_year`): Select the year covered by this report.
* **Department or Division Name** (`department_name`): Enter the name of the department or division that submitted this report.
* **Agency Name** (`agency_name`): Enter the name of the agency that submitted this report.
* **Reporting Officer Name** (`reporting_officer`): Enter the name of the officer who submitted this report.
* **Officer ID Number** (`officer_id`): Enter the ID number of the officer who submitted this report.
* **Report Submission Date** (`submission_date`): Enter the date this report was submitted.
* **Cases Open at Start of Month** (`cases_opened_start`): Enter the number of cases open at the start of the month.
* **Cases Opened During Month** (`cases_opened_during`): Enter the number of new cases opened during the month.
* **Total Cases to Work** (`total_cases`): Enter the total number of cases to work on.
* **Cases Closed During Month** (`cases_closed`): Enter the number of cases closed during the month.
* **Cases Closed by Arrest** (`cases_closed_arrest`): Enter the number of cases closed by arrest.
* **Cases Closed by Citation** (`cases_closed_citation`): Enter the number of cases closed by citation.
* **Cases Closed by Other Means** (`cases_closed_other`): Enter the number of cases closed by other means.
* **Cases Closed as Unfounded** (`cases_closed_unfounded`): Enter the number of cases closed as unfounded.
* **Cases Closed as False Report** (`cases_closed_false`): Enter the number of cases closed as false.
* **Cases Remaining Open** (`cases_remaining`): Enter the number of cases remaining open.
* **Clearance Rate Percentage** (`clearance_rate`): Enter the clearance rate percentage.
* **Median Days to Close Case** (`median_days_to_close`): Enter the median days to close case.
* **Cases Closed Within 7 Days** (`cases_closed_within_7_days`): Enter the number of cases closed within 7 days.
* **Cases Closed in 7 to 30 Days** (`cases_closed_7_to_30_days`): Enter the number of cases closed in 7 to 30 days.
* **Cases Closed in 30 to 90 Days** (`cases_closed_30_to_90_days`): Enter the number of cases closed in 30 to 90 days.
* **Cases Closed Over 90 Days** (`cases_closed_over_90_days`): Enter the number of cases closed over 90 days.
* **Aged Cases Review** (`aged_cases`): Enter any comments or notes regarding aged cases.
* **Cases Open Over 1 Year** (`cases_over_1_year`): Enter the number of cases open over 1 year.
* **Aged Cases Reviewed This Month** (`aged_cases_reviewed`): Enter the number of aged cases reviewed this month.
* **Aged Cases Closed This Month** (`aged_cases_closed`): Enter the number of aged cases closed this month.
* **Resource Allocation** (`resource_allocation`): Enter any comments or notes regarding resource allocation.
* **Number of Officers Assigned** (`officers_assigned`): Enter the number of officers assigned to case work.
* **Average Cases Per Officer** (`cases_per_officer`): Enter the average number of cases per officer.
* **Overtime Hours Used** (`overtime_hours`): Enter any overtime hours used for case work.
* **Challenges or Issues** (`challenges`): Enter any challenges or issues faced during case work.
* **Supervisor Review** (`supervisor_review`): Enter any comments or notes from the supervisor.
* **Reviewing Supervisor** (`reviewing_supervisor`): Enter the name of the supervisor who reviewed this report.
* **Supervisor Comments** (`supervisor_comments`): Enter any comments or notes from the supervisor.
* **Supervisor Signature Date** (`supervisor_signature_date`): Enter the date of the supervisor's signature.
Note: All required fields must be completed for the report to be submitted.
