# Government Public Service Community Feedback Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from the public about their experiences with government public services. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the field labeled "First Name". This is the name you'd like to be addressed by.
2. Enter your **Email Address** in the "Email" field. This is where we'll send you any updates or follow-up questions.
3. Share your **Feedback** about your interaction with our public services. Please be as detailed as possible so we can understand your concerns.
4. Provide the **Date** you interacted with our public services. This will help us track the timeline of your experience.
5. Indicate the **Time** you interacted with our public services. This will help us understand the duration of your interaction.
6. Choose the **Service Area** that best describes the public service you interacted with. Please select "Public Services", "Community Services", or "Other Services" that best fits your experience.
7. If you'd like to be contacted, please provide your **Contact Number**. This is optional, but it will help us follow up with you regarding your feedback.
8. Enter your **Address** if you'd like to provide additional context about your experience.
9. Rate your **Satisfaction Level** with the service you received. Select one of the five options: "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", "Poor", or "Unsatisfactory".
10. Finally, provide any additional **Comments** about your experience. This is your chance to give us more details about what worked and what didn't.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as you'd like to be addressed.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for us to follow up with you.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required/optional): Share your thoughts about your interaction with our public services.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Provide the date of your interaction for us to track the timeline.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Indicate the duration of your interaction for us to understand better.
* **Service Area** (`service_area`, select_one, required/optional): Select the area of service you interacted with. Options: Public Services, Community Services, Other Services.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter a number where we can contact you for follow-up questions.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Provide additional context about your experience.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_multiple, required/optional): Rate your satisfaction level with the service you received. Options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Unsatisfactory.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Share any additional thoughts about your experience.
