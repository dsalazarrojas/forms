# Government Accessibility Standards Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Government Accessibility Standards Inspection Form is a critical tool for evaluating facilities' compliance with accessibility standards. It is used by inspectors to record their findings, identify areas for improvement, and provide recommendations for rectification.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and permissions before filling out the form.
2. Select the correct options for each field, taking care to provide accurate and detailed responses.
3. Fill out each field according to the instructions provided below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Location**: Enter the physical location of the facility being inspected (e.g., Government Building A, 123 Main Street).
* **Date Of Inspection**: Enter the date of the inspection (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Inspection Team**: Enter the names of the team members who conducted the inspection.
* **Facilities Visited**: Indicate if the inspection was conducted on one or multiple facilities. Select 'Yes' or 'No' accordingly.
* **Facility ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the facility (e.g., a number or code).
* **Total Areas Inspected**: Enter the total number of areas inspected during the visit.
* **Areas With Deficiencies**: Enter the number of areas identified as requiring improvement.
* **Deficiencies Found**: Enter the number of deficiencies found during the inspection.
* **Date Of Next Inspection**: Enter the scheduled date for the next inspection (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes from the inspection team.
* **Assigned Inspector**: Enter the name of the assigned inspector or team leader.
* **Assigned Inspector Email**: Enter the email address of the assigned inspector or team leader.

Note: This form is not intended for commercial or business use. Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions and information to complete the form accurately and within the specified guidelines.
