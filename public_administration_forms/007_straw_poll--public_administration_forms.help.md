# Straw Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Straw Poll form is a tool for users to express their opinions and preferences on various topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the poll, if applicable.
2. Answer the questions in the form, selecting one or multiple options as necessary.
3. Enter any comments or additional information, if desired.
4. Set any filters to narrow down the poll results, selecting from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Straw Poll Form** (Straw Poll Form, text, required: false): This field is for entering the title of the poll.
* **Subject Title** (Subject Title, text, required: false): Enter a title for the subject of the poll.
* **Poll Subject** (Poll Subject, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the subject of the poll.
* **Level of Education** (Level of Education, select_one, required: false): Select your level of education: High school, College, University, Master's, or Other.
* **Voter Gender** (Voter Gender, select_one, required: false): Select your gender: Male, Female, or Other.
* **Voter Age** (Voter Age, number, required: false): Enter your age.
* **Voter Income** (Voter Income, number, required: false): Enter your annual income.
* **Voter Education** (Voter Education, select_one, required: false): Select your highest level of education: High school, College, University, Master's, Ph.D., or Other.
* **Voter Occupation** (Voter Occupation, select_multiple, required: false): Select all that apply: Student, Employee, Retired, or Other.
* **Voter Level of Interest** (Voter Level of Interest, select_multiple, required: false): Select all that apply: Very, Somewhat, Not very, or Not at all.
* **Straw Poll Comments** (Straw Poll Comments, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments or information.
* **Filter by Education** (Filter by Education, select_one, required: false): Select a filter for the poll results by education level: High school, College, University, Master's, or Other.
* **Filter by Age** (Filter by Age, select_one, required: false): Select a filter for the poll results by age: 18-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55-64, or 65+.
* **Filter by Income** (Filter by Income, select_one, required: false): Select a filter for the poll results by income: 0-25,000, 25,001-40,000, 40,001-60,000, 60,001-80,000, 80,001-100,000, or More than 100,000.
* **Filter by Occupation** (Filter by Occupation, select_one, required: false): Select a filter for the poll results by occupation: Student, Employee, Retired, or Other.
* **Filter by Interest Level** (Filter by Interest Level, select_one, required: false): Select a filter for the poll results by level of interest: Very, Somewhat, Not very, or Not at all.
* **Submitted By** (Submitted By, text, required: false): Enter any additional information.
* **Submitter Info** (Submitter Info, text, required: false): Enter any additional information.
* **Filter by Educational Level** (Filter by Educational Level, select_one, required: false): Select a filter for the poll results by educational level: High school, College, University, Master's, or Other.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Select only the filters that apply to you.
* Enter any additional comments or information as needed.
* Ensure you have not already completed this form, as duplicate submissions may not be accepted.
