# municipal_policy_public_opinion_survey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The municipal policy public opinion survey form is designed to gather public opinion on municipal policies and services. It aims to gather feedback from residents to help the municipality understand their needs and priorities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your resident feedback in the "Resident Feedback" field.
2. Select the services that are most important to you from the "Services Priorities" field.
3. If applicable, select your community demographics from the "Community Demographics" field.
4. Choose your policy priorities from the "Policy Priorities" field.
5. If you have a public service awareness or awareness date, please enter it in the "Public Service Awareness" field.
6. If you have information about public service access, please enter it in the "Public Service Access" field.
7. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Resident Communication" field.
8. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
9. If you have an email address, please enter it in the "Email Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Resident Feedback** (`resident_feedback`, text, required/optional): This field is where you can provide any feedback or comments about the municipality or its services.
* **Services Priorities** (`services_priorities`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the services that are most important to you, such as waste management, public transportation, or community events.
* **Community Demographics** (`community_demographics`, select_multiple, required/optional): If applicable, select your community demographics, such as age group or income level.
* **Policy Priorities** (`policy_priorities`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your policy priorities, such as public safety or environmental protection.
* **Public Service Awareness** (`public_service_awareness`, date, required/optional): If you have information about public service awareness or awareness date, please enter it here.
* **Public Service Access** (`public_service_access`, time, required/optional): If you have information about public service access, please enter it here.
* **Resident Communication** (`resident_communication`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about resident communication.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required/optional): If you have an email address, please enter it here.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when providing your feedback and information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* The more information you provide, the better we can serve our community.
