# Provincial Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Provincial Feedback Survey is designed to gather feedback from provincial administrators on their experience with public services. This form helps the government to identify areas of improvement and provide better services to the public.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions as accurately as possible.
2. Fill out the "feedback type" field to specify the type of feedback you're providing (e.g., Provincial agencies, Public service teams, or Ongoing data collection).
3. Select all relevant options in the "region" field (e.g., Alberta, British Columbia, Ontario, or Quebec).
4. If you want to rate your satisfaction with a service, use the "satisfaction rating" field.
5. Provide any additional comments in the "comments" field.
6. Fill out your "email" and "phone" fields if you have them available.
7. Finally, provide your name and the date of your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **feedback type**: This field allows you to specify the type of feedback you are providing. The options are:
  + **Provincial agencies**: Feedback on provincial services provided by agencies.
  + **Public service teams**: Feedback on public services provided by teams.
  + **Ongoing data collection**: Feedback on ongoing data collection activities.
* **region**: This field allows you to select the region where you work or reside.
* **satisfaction rating**: This field allows you to rate your satisfaction with a service on a scale of 1-5.
* **comments**: This field allows you to provide any additional comments about your experience with public services.
* **email**: This field allows you to provide your email address if you have one.
* **phone**: This field allows you to provide your phone number if you have one.
* **province**: This field allows you to select the province where you work or reside.
* **date**: This field allows you to select the date of your feedback.
* **time**: This field allows you to select the time of your feedback.
* **note**: This field is for any additional notes about your feedback.
* **file upload**: This field allows you to upload a file related to your feedback.
