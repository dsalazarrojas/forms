# Government Records Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to government records, and it is essential to provide accurate and detailed information to facilitate a smooth processing experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **General Information (Page 1)**: This section is used to collect your basic details, including your name, contact information, and other general information.
2. **Contact Details (Page 2)**: In this section, you are required to select the best contact method for communication regarding your record request.
3. **Records Request Details (Page 3)**: Please provide specific details about the records you are requesting, including any supporting information that may be required.
4. **Date Of Birth (Page 4)**: If you are requesting records related to your birth or other personal details, please provide your date of birth.
5. **Signature (Page 5)**: This is a confirmation that you are requesting the records and that you have read and understood the terms and conditions.
6. **Phone Number (Page 6)**: Please provide your phone number for easy contact and communication.
7. **Email (Page 7)**: Provide your email address for official communication and updates regarding your record request.
8. **Address (Page 8)**: This section is used to collect your address for records delivery or other purposes.
9. **File Upload (Page 9)**: If required, please upload files that support your record request.
10. **Notes (Page 10)**: This section is for any additional information you would like to provide regarding your record request.
11. **Additional Info (Page 11)**: This section is optional, providing further information that may be relevant to your record request.
12. **File Attachment (Page 12)**: If required, please attach files that support your record request.
13. **Contact Preferences (Page 13)**: Choose your preferred method of contact for communication regarding your record request.
14. **Submission Date (Page 14)**: The date you submitted your record request.
15. **Submission Time (Page 15)**: The time you submitted your record request.
16. **Submission Status (Page 16)**: This field is automatically populated after processing your record request.
17. **Submission Comments (Page 17)**: This field is for any comments or feedback about your record request.
18. **Follow Up (Page 18)**: Choose the frequency of follow-up communication regarding your record request.
19. **Assigned To (Page 19)**: This section is used to select the official or department responsible for processing your record request.
20. **Priority (Page 20)**: Choose the priority level of your record request.
21. **Status (Page 21)**: This field is automatically populated after processing your record request.
22. **Comments (Page 22)**: This field is for any comments or feedback about your record request.
23. **File Link (Page 23)**: This field is for attaching any relevant files or links related to your record request.
24. **Submission Link (Page 24)**: This field is for sharing links related to your record request.
25. **Created At (Page 25)**: The date and time your record request was created.
