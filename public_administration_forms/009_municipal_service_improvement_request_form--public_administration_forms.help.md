# Municipal Service Improvement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers to help improve municipal services. It allows customers to submit requests, provide feedback, and track the progress of their service requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Request information** section with your contact details.
2. Specify the **Service Request Type** by selecting the most relevant option from the dropdown list.
3. Provide a brief description of your **Service Request** in the dedicated field.
4. If you would like to be contacted for follow-up, select **Follow-up** options as needed.
5. Fill in the **Next Steps** field with any additional information about the follow-up process.
6. If assigned to a staff member, fill in the **Assigned to** field with their name or role.
7. Fill in the **Assigned Date** and **Completed Date** fields with dates relevant to the request progress.
8. Fill in the **Completed by** field with the name or initials of the person who completed the request.
9. Select the **Status** of the request from the dropdown list.
10. Add any **Comments**, **Follow-up Notes**, **Service Request Notes**, **Customer Comments**, or **Service Request Comments** as needed.
11. Enter your **Email**, **Phone**, **Fax**, **Address**, **City**, **State**, and **Zip** for contact information.
12. Finally, click **Submit** to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request information**: Please fill in this field with any additional information you think is relevant to your request.
* **Customer Information**: Provide your contact details so that the municipal service team can get in touch with you.
* **Service Request**: Briefly describe the service you are requesting.
* **Service Request Type**: Select the type of service you are requesting (e.g., General Inquiry, Request for Service, Complaint, Praise, or Other).
* **Follow-up**: If you would like to be contacted for follow-up, select this option as needed.
* **Next Steps**: Fill in this field with any additional information about the next steps in the process.
* **Assigned to**: If assigned to a staff member, fill in this field with their name or role.
* **Assigned Date**: Fill in the date when the request was assigned to a staff member.
* **Completed Date**: Fill in the date when the request was completed.
* **Completed by**: Fill in the name or initials of the person who completed the request.
* **Status**: Select the status of the request from the dropdown list (e.g., Open, In Progress, Completed, Closed).
* **Comments**: Add any comments related to the request.
* **Follow-up Notes**: Add any follow-up notes related to the request.
* **Service Request Notes**: Add any notes related to the service request.
* **Customer Comments**: Add any comments from the customer.
* **Service Request Comments**: Add any comments related to the service request.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact information.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact information.
* **Fax**: Enter your fax number for contact information.
* **Address**: Enter your address for contact information.
* **City**: Enter your city for contact information.
* **State**: Enter your state for contact information.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code for contact information.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your form.
