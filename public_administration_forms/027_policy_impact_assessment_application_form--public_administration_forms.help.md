# Policy Impact Assessment Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Policy Impact Assessment Application Form is a tool used to evaluate the potential effects of a policy on various stakeholders. It helps to identify the positive and negative impacts, which can inform decision-making processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the "Policy Impact Assessment" field, which is the main section of the form.
2. Next, specify the objective of the policy, which can include the goals, outcomes, or benefits it aims to achieve.
3. Describe the policy's impact on different stakeholders. You can select multiple stakeholders from the options provided.
4. If necessary, attach supporting documents that can further elaborate on the policy's details.
5. Determine the category and type of the policy by selecting from the provided options.
6. Finally, indicate who submitted the policy, which can be done by selecting from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Policy Impact Assessment (1)**: A brief description of the policy's impact. This field is not required.
* **Objective (2)**: A clear statement of the policy's goals, outcomes, or benefits.
* **Impact (3)**: A description of the policy's effect on different stakeholders, such as 'Yes', indicating that it affects them.
* **Stakeholders (4)**: Select all the stakeholders that the policy will impact.
* **Supporting Documents (5)**: Attach any relevant documents that can provide more details about the policy.
* **Category (6)**: Determine the category of the policy, such as 'Yes', indicating that it falls under that category.
* **Type (7)**: Determine the type of the policy, such as 'Yes', indicating that it is a specific type.
* **Submission By (8)**: Indicate who submitted the policy, which can be done by selecting from the options provided.
 
### Tips
- Be as specific as possible when describing the policy's impact and objective.
- Only attach relevant supporting documents that are directly related to the policy.
- Select the correct category and type of the policy based on your understanding of its characteristics. 
- Clearly indicate who submitted the policy.
