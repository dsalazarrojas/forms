# Accessibility Barriers In Public Spaces Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect data on the experiences and concerns of individuals with disabilities in public spaces. Your responses will help inform policy decisions and improvements to accessibility features in public spaces.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete the survey in a quiet and comfortable environment.
2. Select your responses carefully, as they will help inform the development of more accessible public spaces.
3. Answer all questions honestly, and do not hesitate to share your experiences and concerns.
4. If you experience any difficulties or need assistance, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
