# Petition For Improved Veteran Services - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Petition For Improved Veteran Services form is used to collect feedback and comments from veterans or their representatives on how to improve veteran services. This form is an opportunity for individuals to provide their thoughts and suggestions on how to better serve veterans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field. This is a required field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field. This is a required field.
3. Enter your Email address in the Email field. This is an optional field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field. This is an optional field.
5. Select your Signature from the Signature dropdown menu. This is a required field.
6. Enter your Name on Card in the Name on Card field. This is an optional field.
7. Enter your ID Card Number in the ID Card Number field. This is an optional field.
8. Enter your Organization in the Organization field. This is an optional field.
9. Select your Role from the Role dropdown menu. This is a required field.
10. Enter your Message in the Message field. This is an optional field.
11. Enter any additional Comments you may have in the Comments field. This is an optional field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your personal or work email address, if you wish to receive updates on this matter.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, if you wish to be contacted for further discussion.
* **Signature**: Select one of the available options: John, Jane, or Jane Doe. This is a required field as we need your signature to validate your identity.
* **Name on Card**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID card, if applicable.
* **ID Card Number**: Enter your ID card number, if you have one.
* **Organization**: If you represent an organization, enter its name here.
* **Role**: Select one of the available options: Veteran, Family Member, or Organization. This field helps us understand your capacity and perspective.
* **Message**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have, if you wish to provide more information.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have, if you wish to provide more details.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter your contact information so we can get back to you if needed.
* Please sign your name as it appears on your ID card or a valid government-issued ID.
* If you are representing an organization, please make sure to enter its correct name and address.
