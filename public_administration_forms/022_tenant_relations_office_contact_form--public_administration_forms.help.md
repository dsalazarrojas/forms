# Tenant Relations Office Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Tenant Relations Office Contact Form is a communication tool for tenants to contact the Tenant Relations Office with their concerns, issues, or requests. This form helps the office to efficiently track and manage interactions with tenants, ensuring a prompt response to their queries.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your email address in the 'Email' field. This is the primary contact information you'll be using for communication.
2. Type in your phone number in the 'Phone' field. This will help the Tenant Relations Office reach you more quickly, if needed.
3. Describe your issue or problem in the 'Issue Details' field. Be as detailed as possible to help the Tenant Relations Office understand your concern.
4. If you have specific requests or requirements, provide them in the 'Request Details' field.
5. If you're attaching relevant documents to support your issue, select 'Yes' or 'No' in the 'Attachments' field. This will help the Tenant Relations Office know what to expect in the email.
6. Optionally, include any additional comments or context about your issue in the 'Comments' field. This will help the Tenant Relations Office understand your perspective.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address to ensure the Tenant Relations Office can contact you efficiently.
* **Phone (Phone)**: Enter your phone number to facilitate quick calls or follow-ups, if needed.
* **Issue Details (tenant_relations_office_form_2)**: Describe your issue or problem in as much detail as possible. Be concise and clear to help the Tenant Relations Office quickly understand your concern.
* **Request Details (tenant_relations_office_form_3)**: If you have specific requests or requirements, provide them here.
* **Attachments (tenant_relations_office_form_4)**: Select 'Yes' if you're attaching documents to support your issue, or 'No' if not.
* **Comments (tenant_relations_office_form_5)**: Add any additional comments or context about your issue, if necessary.
