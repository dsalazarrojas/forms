# Community Development Plan Review - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Community Development Plan Review" form is designed to collect feedback from reviewers on a community development plan. Reviewers provide their opinions on various aspects of the plan, including its zoning compatibility, environmental impact, traffic effects, community benefit, and infrastructure adequacy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name and email address.
2.  Select your relationship to the community (e.g., Resident, Business Owner, Community Organization).
3.  Enter the plan's reference number or name.
4.  Choose the date you reviewed the plan.
5.  Evaluate the plan's overall support, zoning compatibility, environmental impact, traffic impact, and community benefit.
6.  Choose whether the plan's infrastructure is fully adequate, mostly adequate, somewhat adequate, or inadequate.
7.  If you have specific support or concerns, provide them in the "Specific Aspects You Support" and "Specific Concerns" fields.
8.  Suggest any modifications or changes you think the plan should have in the "Recommended Changes" field.
9.  Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`reviewer_name`, required): Enter your full name as you would like to be identified on the review.
* **Email Address** (`reviewer_email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted for further correspondence.
* **Reviewer Type** (`reviewer_type`, required): Select your relationship to the community (e.g., Resident, Business Owner, Community Organization).
* **Plan Reference Number or Name** (`plan_reference`, required): Enter the plan's reference number or name.
* **Date of Review** (`review_date`, required): Select the date when you reviewed the plan.
* **Overall Support** (`overall_support`, required): Choose whether you support or oppose the plan (Strongly Support, Support, Neutral, Oppose, Strongly Oppose).
* **Zoning Compatibility** (`zoning_compatibility`, required): Choose whether the plan is fully, mostly, somewhat, or not compatible with current zoning.
* **Environmental Impact Assessment** (`environmental_impact`, required): Choose the plan's environmental impact as minimal, moderate, significant, severe, or no opinion.
* **Traffic Impact Assessment** (`traffic_impact`, required): Choose the plan's expected traffic impact as minimal, moderate, significant, severe, or no opinion.
* **Community Benefit** (`community_benefit`, required): Choose whether the plan will have a great, some, neutral, some harm, or great harm to the community.
* **Infrastructure Adequacy** (`infrastructure_adequacy`, required): Choose whether the plan's infrastructure is fully, mostly, somewhat, or inadequate.
* **Specific Aspects You Support** (`specific_support`, optional): Provide specific aspects of the plan you support.
* **Specific Concerns** (`specific_concerns`, optional): Provide specific concerns you have about the plan.
* **Recommended Changes** (`recommended_changes`, optional): Suggest modifications or changes you think the plan should have.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the plan.
