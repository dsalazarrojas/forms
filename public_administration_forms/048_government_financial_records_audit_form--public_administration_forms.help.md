# government-financial-records-audit-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for auditing financial records in public administration.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form instructions and guidance carefully before starting to complete it.
- Only complete this form if you are authorized to do so.
- Fill in the required information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Financial Record Type** (main_page_question_2): Select the type of financial record being audited (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Auditing Agency** (main_page_question_3): Enter the name of the agency conducting the audit.
* **Date of Audits** (main_page_question_4): Enter the date of the audit in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Audits** (main_page_question_5): Enter the time of the audit in the 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00).
* **Audit Finding** (main_page_question_6): Describe any findings from the audit.
* **Financial Amount** (main_page_question_7): Enter the financial amount being audited.
* **Supporting Documents** (main_page_question_8): Attach any relevant supporting documents to the audit.
* **Comments** (main_page_question_9): Enter any additional comments or information relevant to the audit.
* **Review Date** (main_page_question_10): Enter the date the review was completed in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Review Time** (main_page_question_11): Enter the time the review was completed in the 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00).
* **Reviewer** (main_page_question_12): Select the name of the person conducting the review (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Review Status** (main_page_question_13): Select the status of the review (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4).
* **Review Comment** (main_page_question_14): Enter any comments or observations from the review.
* **Financial Review** (main_page_question_15): Enter any additional financial review information.
* **Financial Notes** (main_page_question_16): Enter any additional financial notes.
* **Reviewing** (main_page_question_17): Enter any information about the review process.
* **Auditor Notes** (main_page_question_18): Enter any additional notes from the auditor.
* **Reviewer's Name** (main_page_question_19): Enter the name of the person conducting the review.
* **Reviewer's Title** (main_page_question_20): Enter the title of the person conducting the review.
* **Reviewer's Contact** (main_page_question_21): Enter the contact information for the reviewer (e.g., phone number, email address).
* **Reviewer's Email** (main_page_question_22): Enter the email address of the person conducting the review.
* **Review Date** (main_page_question_23): Enter the date the review was completed in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Review Time** (main_page_question_24): Enter the time the review was completed in the 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00).
* **Signature Page** (signature_page): A signature page for the reviewer to sign and date the review.

Please note that the required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*). Ensure that you complete all the required fields to ensure accurate and complete information. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact the relevant authority or consult the relevant policies and procedures.
