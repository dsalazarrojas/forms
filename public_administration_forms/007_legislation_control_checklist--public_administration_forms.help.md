# Legislation Control Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legislation Control Checklist is a form designed to help organizations and individuals track and manage the control and compliance of legislation within their company. It is used to review and maintain up-to-date records of regulatory compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review compliance status of each legislation item.
2. Identify any areas of non-compliance.
3. Record your findings and track follow-up actions.
4. Update compliance status and document reviews.
5. Track review frequency (annually, quarterly, or monthly).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Review compliance** (`review_compliance`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the current compliance status of the legislation item.
* **Identify non-compliance** (`identify_non_compliance`, text, required: false): Describe any areas of non-compliance found during the review.
* **Document reviews** (`document_reviews`, text, required: false): Record any documentation or evidence related to the review, such as meeting minutes or audit reports.
* **Record findings** (`record_findings`, text, required: false): Summarize your findings and conclusions from the review.
* **Track follow-up actions** (`track_follow_up_actions`, text, required: true): Record any actions taken or planned to address areas of non-compliance.
* **Compliance status** (`compliance_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the current compliance status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Department review** (`department_review`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose which departments or teams are responsible for reviewing or maintaining the legislation item.
* **Review frequency** (`review_frequency`, select_multiple, required: false): Select how often the legislation item should be reviewed (Annually, Quarterly, or Monthly).

## Tips
* Make sure to review and update the form regularly to ensure accurate and up-to-date records.
* Use the 'Active' compliance status when the legislation item is up-to-date and compliant.
* Use the 'Inactive' compliance status when the legislation item is no longer applicable or relevant.
* Use the 'Pending' compliance status when the legislation item is under review or awaiting further action.
* Use the 'Track follow-up actions' field to record any planned or taken actions to address areas of non-compliance.
* Use the 'Department review' field to involve relevant departments or teams in the review process.
