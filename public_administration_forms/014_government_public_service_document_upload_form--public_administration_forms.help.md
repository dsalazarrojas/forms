# Government Public Service Document Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Government Public Service Document Upload Form is used by public service employees or officials to upload public service documents and provide relevant details about them.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for the document in the **document_title** field.
2. Select the type of document from the **document_type** dropdown menu.
3. Attach the document to be uploaded in the **upload** field.
4. Confirm that the upload was successful in the **confirm_upload** field (this field will display a confirmation message after a successful upload).
5. Enter the date and time of the document in the **document_date** and **document_time** fields.
6. If necessary, add a personal note about the document in the **document_note** field.
7. Enter a contact email in the **document_email** field to facilitate communication.
8. Enter a contact phone number in the **document_phone** field to facilitate communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **document_title** (`document_title`, text, required): Enter a title for the document that is descriptive and relevant to its content.
* **document_type** (`document_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of document from the provided options.
* **upload** (`upload`, text, optional): Attach the document to be uploaded.
* **confirm_upload** (, note, optional): This field displays a confirmation message after a successful upload.
* **document_date** (`document_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the document.
* **document_time** (`document_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the document.
* **document_note** (`document_note`, note, optional): Add a personal note about the document, if necessary.
* **document_email** (`document_email`, email, optional): Enter a contact email for the document.
* **document_phone** (`document_phone`, text, optional): Enter a contact phone number for the document.
