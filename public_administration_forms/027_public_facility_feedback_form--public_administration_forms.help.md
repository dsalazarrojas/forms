# public_facility_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The public_facility_feedback_form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experiences at public facilities. This form will help us understand how our facilities are performing and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the facility name to provide the name of the public facility you visited.
2. Rate your experience with the facility using the provided options.
3. Enter your comment about the facility.
4. Let us know if the facility has a good, average, or no parking options.
5. Enter your email address if you want to receive responses to your feedback.
6. Enter your phone number for follow-up purposes (not required).
7. Enter your name for our reference.
8. Enter the address of the facility for our reference.
9. Indicate if the facility is located in a specific location.
10. Report the date and time of your visit.
11. Enter the time of your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility** (Facility, text, required): Enter the name of the public facility you visited.
* **Rating** (Rating, select_one, required): Rate your experience with the facility using the provided options: Good, Fair, or No.
* **Comment** (Comment, text, required): Enter a brief description of your experience with the facility.
* **Category** (Category, select_one, required): Check if the facility has a good parking option, average parking, or no parking option.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address if you want to receive responses to your feedback.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **Name** (Name, text, required): Enter your name for our reference.
* **Address** (Address, text, optional): Enter the address of the facility for our reference.
* **Location** (Location, select_one, required): Check if the facility is located in a specific location.
* **Date of Visit/Prophets** (Date of Visit/Prophets, date, required): Report the date of your visit.
* **Time of Visit** (Time of Visit, time, required): Enter the time of your visit.
