# Recovery Court Program Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from individuals seeking to join the Recovery Court Program. Please provide your accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form accurately and completely, using your best judgment.
2. Make sure to include all required information (indicated by an asterisk [*]) and any optional information you wish to provide.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, Text, optional): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, Text, optional): Enter your primary phone number where you can be reached.
* **Address** (`address`, Text, optional): Enter your current address for correspondence purposes.
* **Court Order** (`court_order`, Select Multiple, optional):
	+ Select all relevant court orders that apply to your situation.
* **Program Services** (`program_services`, Select Multiple, optional):
	+ Choose the services you are interested in from the available options.
* **Income** (`income`, Number, optional): Enter your current annual income (before taxes) or "Not Applicable" if not applicable.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, optional): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD format (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, Select One, optional):
	+ Choose your current employment status.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, Select One, optional):
	+ Select your highest level of education completed.

Please note that not all fields are required, but providing accurate and complete information will help the Recovery Court Program team evaluate your application efficiently.
