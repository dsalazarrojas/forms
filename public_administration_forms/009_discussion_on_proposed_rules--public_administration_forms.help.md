# Discussion On Proposed Rules - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on proposed rules and regulations. It is intended to gather stakeholder feedback on draft policies, allowing for a structured and informed discussion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the draft policy title to help identify the policy being discussed.
2. Enter the name of the stakeholder providing feedback, if applicable.
3. If providing feedback as a representative of an organization, enter their email address to enable communication and follow-up.
4. Select the relevant policy category and subcategory to associate the feedback with the correct policy area.
5. Add any comments or observations related to the proposed rule.
6. Optionally, provide the date and time the feedback was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Draft Policy Title** (`draft_policy_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the draft policy being discussed.
* **Stakeholder Name** (`stakeholder_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the stakeholder providing feedback, if applicable.
* **Stakeholder Email** (`stakeholder_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the stakeholder providing feedback, if applicable.
* **Policy Category** (`policy_category`, select_one, optional): Select the category of the proposed policy (e.g., Category 1, Category 2, Category 3).
* **Policy Subcategory** (`policy_subcategory`, select_one, optional): Select the subcategory of the proposed policy (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments or observations related to the proposed rule.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, optional): Enter the date the feedback was submitted.
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, time, optional): Enter the time the feedback was submitted.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter your name or the name of the representative submitting the feedback.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing accurate and complete information will help facilitate a constructive and informed discussion.
