# water_line_material_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the water line materials used in various installations.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of property for which the water line material information is being collected.
2. Choose the year of installation from the provided options.
3. Rate the condition of the material used for the water line.
4. Choose the size of the material used for the water line.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **property_type** (PVC pipes, PEX tubing, ductile iron pipes, galvanized iron pipes, lead pipes, other): This field asks for the type of property being surveyed.
	* Select one or more of the options to describe the type of property.
*   **installation_year** (2018, 2017, 2016, 2015, 2014, 2013, 2012, 2011, 2010, 2009, 2008): This field requests the year the water line material was installed.
	*   Choose the year the water line material was installed.
*   **material_condition** (excellent, good, fair, poor, very poor): This field rates the condition of the material used for the water line.
	*   Rate the condition of the material used for the water line.
*   **material_size** (3/4 inch, 1 inch, 2 inch, other): This field selects the size of the material used for the water line.
	*   Select the size of the material used for the water line.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct type of property and installation year to provide accurate information.
* Rate the condition of the material honestly to ensure the survey results are reliable.
* Choose the correct size of the material used for the water line.
* If the size of the material is not listed, select "other" and provide a description in the additional information field.
