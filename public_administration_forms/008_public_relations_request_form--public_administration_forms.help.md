# Public Relations Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Relations Request Form is designed to collect information for public relations purposes, such as events, meetings, or other public engagements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your personal details:
	* **First name**: Enter your first name.
	* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
2. Add your **Email** and **Phone** for contact purposes.
3. Select your **Department** from the available options.
4. Provide a brief **Description** of your request.
5. Select a **Date** for the event or meeting.
6. Choose a **Time** for the event or meeting.
7. Add any additional **Note** about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your personal email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Department**: Select the department related to your request from the available options.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of your request.
* **Date**: Select the date for the event or meeting.
* **Time**: Select the time for the event or meeting.
* **Note**: Add any additional notes about your request.

## Tips
* Double-check your input for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure or have questions, contact the relevant department or team.
* If you're submitting multiple requests, be sure to provide unique information for each.
