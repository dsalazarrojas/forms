# Government Store Management Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from store managers on their performance and areas of improvement in store operations.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select your store location from the drop-down menu.
2.  Enter your name as the store manager.
3.  Choose the store hours that you are available to work.
4.  Score your manager's performance on a scale of 1-5.
5.  Provide a description of your manager's performance.
6.  List specific store operations challenges you are facing.
7.  Select all areas that require improvement in the store.
8.  Upload your performance data in a CSV file if applicable.
9.  Attach any additional files that support your feedback.
10.  Enter your email address for contact purposes.
11.  Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
12.  Provide any final comments or response from your manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Store Location**: Choose the location of your store from the options provided.
    *   This is the location of your store where feedback is being collected.
*   **Manager Name**: Enter your name as the store manager.
    *   This is your name, which is required for identification purposes.
*   **Store Hours**: Select the hours you are available to work.
    *   This shows your work schedule.
*   **Feedback Score**: Score your manager's performance on a scale of 1-5.
    *   This is a measure of how well you think your manager is performing.
*   **Comments**: Describe your manager's performance in detail.
    *   This provides more in-depth information about your manager's performance.
*   **Store Operations**: List specific store operations challenges you are facing.
    *   This is a list of challenges and issues you are experiencing.
*   **Priority Areas**: Select all areas that require improvement in the store.
    *   These are areas that you think need improvement.
*   **Performance Data**: Upload your performance data in a CSV file if applicable.
    *   This shows your performance data if applicable.
*   **Uploaded File**: Attach any additional files that support your feedback.
    *   This is for supporting evidence.
*   **Email Address**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
    *   This is your contact information.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
    *   This is your contact number.
*   **Manager Response**: Provide any final comments or responses from your manager.
    *   This is additional feedback from your manager.
