# Economic Programs Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Economic Programs Assessment form is designed to collect feedback and assessment from participants on economic programs. This form helps evaluate the effectiveness of programs, their outcomes, and the tools used to achieve those outcomes. It's a crucial tool for organizations to understand where they stand and make informed decisions to improve their programs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form is divided into sections, each focusing on a specific aspect of the economic program.
2. Fill out each section according to the guidelines provided in the Field-by-Field Explanation section.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Economic Programs Assessment** (1, note, required: false): This section is an introduction to the form, providing context and instructions.
* **Participant Feedback** (2, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the provided options to rate your feedback on the program.
* **Satisfaction Level** (3, select_multiple, required: true): Select all the options that best describe your level of satisfaction with the program.
* **Program Outcomes** (4, select_multiple, required: true): Select all the options that best describe the outcomes of the program.
* **Program Outcomes (Text)** (5, text, required: false): Provide any additional text-based feedback on the program outcomes.
* **Additional Feedback** (6, text, required: false): Share any additional comments or feedback you have about the program.
* **Assigned Tool** (7, select_multiple, required: true): Select all the tools that were assigned to you for the program.
* **Participant Name** (8, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Participant Email** (9, email, required: false): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Participant Phone** (10, text, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Program Name** (11, text, required: false): Enter the name of the program you are participating in.
* **Program Department** (12, text, required: false): Enter the department that manages the program.
* **Program Location** (13, text, required: false): Enter the location where the program is being implemented.
* **Submission Date** (14, date, required: false): Enter the date when the submission was made.
* **Submission Time** (15, time, required: false): Enter the time when the submission was made.
* **Participant Survey** (16, select_multiple, required: true): Select all the options that best describe your survey experience.
* **Survey Comments** (17, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments on your survey experience.
* **Participant Survey Comments** (18, text, required: false): Share any additional comments on your survey experience.
* **Participant Survey Comments 2** (19, text, required: false): Share any further comments on your survey experience.
* **Additional Comments** (20, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback on the program.
* **Participant Survey Comments 3** (21, text, required: false): Share any further comments on your survey experience.
* **Participant Survey Comments 4** (22, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments on your survey experience.
* **Participant Survey Comments 5** (23, text, required: false): Share any further comments on your survey experience.
* **Participant Survey Comments 6** (24, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments on your survey experience.
