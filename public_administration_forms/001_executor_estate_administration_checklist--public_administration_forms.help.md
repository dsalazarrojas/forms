# Executor Estate Administration Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Executor Estate Administration Checklist is a form used to gather information about the estate's assets, debts, and other relevant details. This form is designed to help administrators or professionals responsible for estate administration to gather all necessary information regarding the estate's status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete each page of the form in order, starting from Page 1.
2. For each field, provide the most accurate and detailed information available.
3. If you are unsure or lack information about a particular field, please indicate it as "Unknown" or "Not Applicable" and provide an explanation or reference to supporting documents or sources.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Initial Steps** (<label> `estate_administration_checklist_1`, <type> text, required: false): This field is for capturing any initial steps taken in the estate administration process. Provide a brief description of the steps taken so far, including any relevant dates and parties involved.
* **Inventory Management** (<label> `estate_administration_checklist_2`, <type> number, required: false): This field is for recording the number of items inventoried during the estate administration process. If the number of items is unknown or not applicable, please indicate it as "Unknown" or "Not Applicable".
* **Probate Court Filing** (<label> `estate_administration_checklist_3`, <type> select_one, options: Probate Court, Other, required: false): This field is for indicating whether the estate has been filed with the probate court. Select the relevant option from the list.
