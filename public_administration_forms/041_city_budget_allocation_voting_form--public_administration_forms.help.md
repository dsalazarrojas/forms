# City Budget Allocation Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather public input and feedback on the city's budget allocation priorities and overall spending levels. Your participation is crucial in helping city officials make informed decisions about budget allocation and resource distribution.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with your personal information, including your name and email address.
2. Select your top priorities for the city's budget allocation, ranking each priority area from 1 to 5, with 1 being the highest priority.
3. Indicate your opinion on the city's current budget levels, choosing one of the options provided.
4. If you have any top concerns or missing priorities, please specify them in the dedicated text field.
5. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have for the city's budget.
6. If you wish to be contacted for follow-up, please select your preferred communication method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`voter_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email Address** (`voter_email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address for follow-up purposes, if applicable.
* **City Address** (`voter_address`, `text`, required): Verify you are a resident of this city.
* **Resident Status** (`resident_status`, `select_one`, required): Select your status as a resident of this city.
* **Education and Schools** (`education_priority`, `number`, required): Rank the importance of education and schools from 1 to 5.
* **Infrastructure and Roads** (`infrastructure_priority`, `number`, required): Rank the importance of infrastructure and roads from 1 to 5.
* **Public Safety and Police** (`public_safety_priority`, `number`, required): Rank the importance of public safety and police from 1 to 5.
* **Parks and Recreation** (`parks_recreation_priority`, `number`, required): Rank the importance of parks and recreation from 1 to 5.
* **Public Health Services** (`public_health_priority`, `number`, required): Rank the importance of public health services from 1 to 5.
* **Public Transportation** (`public_transit_priority`, `number`, required): Rank the importance of public transportation from 1 to 5.
* **Housing and Community Development** (`housing_priority`, `number`, required): Rank the importance of housing and community development from 1 to 5.
* **Environmental Initiatives** (`environmental_priority`, `number`, required): Rank the importance of environmental initiatives from 1 to 5.
* **Social Services** (`social_services_priority`, `number`, required): Rank the importance of social services from 1 to 5.
* **Economic Development** (`economic_development_priority`, `number`, required): Rank the importance of economic development from 1 to 5.
* **What is Your Recommended Total City Budget?** (`total_budget`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you think the city's budget should be allocated.
* **Opinion on Current Tax Levels** (`tax_opinion`, `select_one`, optional): Share your opinion on the current tax levels in the city.
* **What is Your Top Budget Concern?** (`top_concern`, `text`, optional): Specify your top concern regarding the city's budget.
* **What Priority is Missing from This List?** (`missing_priority`, `text`, optional): If you feel a priority is missing, please specify it.
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any further comments or suggestions for the city's budget.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, `select_one`, optional): Select your age group.
* **Years Living in This City** (`years_living_in_city`, `select_one`, optional): Select how long you have been living in this city.
* **Household Size** (`household_size`, `number`, optional): Enter the size of your household.
* **How Should We Follow Up With You?** (`notification_preference`, `select_one`, optional): Select your preferred follow-up method.
