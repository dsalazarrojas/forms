# Government Engagement Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and record government engagement activities, including the number of team members participating in these activities and the members of each team involved.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your name and title as the government official responsible for filling this form.
2. Select the options for "Team Members" to indicate the number of team members who have participated in the engagement activity.
3. If you have selected "Yes" for "Team Members", please fill in the names of the team members who participated in the activity in the fields below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Government Engagement Form** (`government_engagement_audit_form`, text, required/optional): This is the main field where you need to fill in a brief description of the government engagement activity. Please be as concise as possible and provide a clear and detailed account of the activity.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if the activity involved team members and "No" otherwise. This will help in evaluating whether team members are participating in the engagement activity or not.
* **Team Member 1** (`team_member_1`, text, optional): If you selected "Yes" for "Team Members", fill in the name of the first team member who participated in the activity.
* **Team Member 2** (`team_member_2`, text, optional): If you selected "Yes" for "Team Members", fill in the name of the second team member who participated in the activity.
* **Team Member 3** (`team_member_3`, text, optional): If you selected "Yes" for "Team Members", fill in the name of the third team member who participated in the activity.
* **Team Member 4** (`team_member_4`, text, optional): If you selected "Yes" for "Team Members", fill in the name of the fourth team member who participated in the activity.
