# Strike Vote Participation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Strike Vote Participation Form is designed to gather information about union members who have participated in a strike. This form helps in assessing the level of participation and opinions of union members regarding the proposed strike action.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring that all required fields are filled out.
2. If a field is marked as required, please fill it out as it is essential for our records.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please leave it blank or contact the union representative for guidance.
4. Review your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **union_name**: Enter the name of the union that you are a member of. This field is not required.
* **proposed_strike_action**: Describe the proposed strike action that you participated in. This field is not required.
* **affiliation**: Provide your affiliation with the union, if any. This field is not required.
* **member_name**: Enter your name as it appears on your union membership card. This field is not required.
* **member_email**: Enter your email address. This field is not required.
* **vote**: Select 'Yes' if you participated in the strike, and 'No' otherwise.
* **feedback**: If you have any feedback or comments about the strike, please provide them here. This field is not required.
* **participation_by_affiliation**: Select all affiliations that you participated in the strike. You can select multiple affiliations if needed.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form honestly and accurately.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the union representative.
- Ensure that you have not already filled out this form.
- If you have filled out this form previously, please do not fill it out again.
