# Renewable Energy Policy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Renewable Energy Policy Survey is designed to gather information on the current status, challenges, and implementation of renewable energy policies in different countries or regions. The survey aims to provide insights on how governments support or hinder the development of renewable energy technologies, and the public's perception of renewable energy policies. This data will help identify areas for improvement and inform decision-making on renewable energy policy development and implementation.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, carefully read each question and select the most accurate answer from the provided options.
2.  Fill in the required information for each field, and provide brief descriptions when necessary.
3.  If you encounter any technical difficulties or have questions, contact your administrator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What is the current status of renewable energy policy in your country/region?** (`current_status`, Select One, required)
    This question aims to assess the current status of renewable energy policy in your country/region. Please choose one of the provided options: In Progress, Implemented, Not Applicable, or Other (please specify).
*   **Which renewable energy sources are currently being utilized in your country/region?** (`energy_sources`, Select Multiple, required)
    This question seeks to understand which renewable energy sources are being used in your country/region. Select all applicable options: Solar, Wind, Hydro, Geothermal, or Other (please specify).
*   **What are the main challenges facing the implementation of renewable energy policies in your country/region?** (`challenges`, Text, required)
    This question invites you to describe the primary obstacles encountered in implementing renewable energy policies in your country/region. Provide a brief description.
*   **How does the government support the development of renewable energy technologies?** (`government_support`, Select One, required)
    This question investigates the government's support for renewable energy technology development. Choose one of the provided options: Financial Support, Regulatory Support, Technical Support, Other (please specify), or Not Sufficient.
*   **What are the main benefits of renewable energy policies in your country/region?** (`benefits`, Text, required)
    This question requests a brief description of the primary advantages of renewable energy policies in your country/region.
*   **What are some potential solutions to the challenges facing renewable energy policies?** (`solutions`, Text, required)
    This question encourages you to suggest solutions to the problems encountered in implementing renewable energy policies in your country/region. Provide a brief description.
*   **How does the public perceive renewable energy policies in your country/region?** (`public_perception`, Select One, required)
    This question inquires about the public's view on renewable energy policies in your country/region. Choose one of the provided options: Positive, Negative, Neutral, or Other (please specify).
*   **What role does the media play in promoting renewable energy policies?** (`media_promotion`, Select One, required)
    This question investigates the media's role in supporting or hindering renewable energy policies. Select one of the provided options: Active Support, Passive Support, No Support, or Other (please specify).
*   **Are there any specific laws or regulations that support or hinder renewable energy policies?** (`laws_and_regulations`, Select Multiple, required)
    This question aims to understand the existing laws or regulations that impact renewable energy policies. Choose all applicable options: Supportive, Hindrance, Neutral, or Other (please specify).
*   **Can you provide any additional comments or suggestions on the current state of renewable energy policies?** (`additional_comments`, Text, optional)
    This question offers an opportunity to provide any additional insights or recommendations on the current state of renewable energy policies in your country/region. Provide a brief description if necessary.
