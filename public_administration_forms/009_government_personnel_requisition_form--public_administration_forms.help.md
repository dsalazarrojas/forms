# Government Personnel Requisition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to create a record for a new job opening within the government department. It collects information such as job title, department, and contact details for the purpose of tracking and managing personnel requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the Job Request field with a brief description of the job opening.
2. Select the Job Title from the provided options.
3. Select the Department from the provided options.
4. Select the Job Status as either Active or Inactive.
5. Select the Priority as either Yes or No.
6. Enter the Job Description in the provided text field.
7. Enter the Job Start Date in the date format.
8. Enter the Job End Date in the date format.
9. Select the Contact Person as either Yes or No.
10. Enter the Contact Email if the Contact Person is selected as Yes.
11. Enter the Contact Phone number if provided.
12. Add any additional notes about the job in the Job Notes field.


## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Request** (`Job Request`, text, required): This field is where you can provide a brief description of the job opening.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, select_one, required): Select the title of the job opening from the provided options.
* **Department** (`Department`, select_multiple, optional): Select the department that the job opening belongs to.
* **Job Status** (`Job Status`, select_one, required): Select whether the job is Active or Inactive.
* **Priority** (`Priority`, select_one, required): Select whether the job has a high priority or not.
* **Job Description** (`Job Description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the job opening.
* **Job Start Date** (`Job Start Date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the job opening in the date format.
* **Job End Date** (`Job End Date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the job opening in the date format.
* **Contact Person** (`Contact Person`, select_one, required): Select whether a contact person is assigned to the job.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, email, optional): Enter the contact email of the contact person if selected as Yes.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the contact person if provided.
* **Job Notes** (`Job Notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the job opening.

Note: All fields marked as required are mandatory and must be filled in for the form to be submitted. Fields marked as optional can be left blank if not applicable.
