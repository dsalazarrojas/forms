# government council knowledge quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to test your knowledge and understanding of government council-related concepts. It consists of multiple questions, correct answers, and a scoring mechanism to gauge your comprehension.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Provide the correct answer for each question.
3. Enter your score at the end of the quiz.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Quiz Questions** (Text, Required): Answer each question to the best of your ability. You will be asked about various government council-related concepts. Be sure to provide detailed and clear responses.
* **Correct Answers** (Text, Required): Enter the correct answer for each corresponding question. This will help us assess your level of understanding.
* **Score** (Text, Required): Enter your final score at the end of the quiz. Your score will be calculated based on the number of correct answers you provided.

## Tips

* Please answer each question to the best of your ability, and don't hesitate to ask for clarification if needed.
* This form is designed to be a self-assessment tool, so feel free to take your time and complete it at your own pace.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
