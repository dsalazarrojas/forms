# Community Safety Initiative Poll Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect your opinions and preferences on safety initiatives in the community. Your input will help us understand your concerns, needs, and expectations, and shape the direction of future safety initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly and briefly, and provide any additional information you feel is relevant to your response.
2. You can select one or multiple options for most questions, so feel free to pick the ones that best represent your opinions.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Input or Suggestions" field at the end of the form.
4. Remember that all responses are anonymous, and your email address will only be used to share poll results with the community.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resident Information**: This section is for optional demographic information about yourself. We understand that this might not be relevant to your answers, but it helps us understand who we are asking for feedback. You can skip this section if you prefer.
* **First Name**: Your first name, if you feel comfortable sharing it.
* **Last Name**: Your last name, if you feel comfortable sharing it.
* **Email Address**: We will use this to share poll results with you and other community members. Please provide a valid email address.
* **ZIP Code**: Verify your residency by providing your ZIP code. This is a required field to ensure we are collecting feedback from actual community residents.
* **Age Group**: Please select your age group to help us understand your demographics.
* **Household Type**: Choose the type of household you belong to (e.g., Single Person, Family with Children, etc.).
* **Safety Initiative Poll**: This is a note section for any comments or thoughts you have about safety initiatives.
* **Is Community Safety a Priority for You**: Select how important safety is to you, from Top Priority to Not a Priority.
* **How Would You Rate Current Safety**: Rate how you feel about the current safety situation in the community.
* **Do You Support New Safety Initiatives**: Choose how strongly you support new safety initiatives.
* **Which Safety Initiatives Do You Prefer**: Select up to three safety initiatives you would like to see implemented.
* **Would You Support Funding for Safety Programs**: Select how strongly you support funding for safety programs.
* **What Annual Amount Would You Support**: If you support funding, select how much you would be willing to pay annually.
* **Would You Volunteer for Safety Programs**: Choose if you would be willing to volunteer for safety programs.
* **How Many Hours Could You Volunteer Monthly**: If you volunteer, select how many hours you would be willing to dedicate per month.
* **Would You Attend Safety Meetings**: Select if you would attend community meetings on safety initiatives.
* **Preferred Communication Method**: Choose how you would like to receive safety updates (e.g., email, text messages, etc.).
* **How Much Do You Trust Local Safety Officials**: Select your level of trust in local safety officials.
* **Should Safety Be a Collaborative Effort**: Choose how you feel about residents and authorities working together on safety initiatives.
* **Would You Support Security Technology**: Select how strongly you support the use of security technology (e.g., cameras, sensors, etc.).
* **Do You Have Privacy Concerns**: Choose how concerned you are about privacy regarding security measures.
* **How Should Success Be Measured**: Select all applicable measures to help us understand how success should be evaluated for safety initiatives.
* **When Should Initiatives Begin**: Select when you think safety initiatives should start.
* **Additional Input or Suggestions**: Share any additional thoughts or comments you have about safety initiatives.
