# Child Support Office Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and manage visitor information for child support office check-ins. Please complete the form accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the date and time of your visit.
2. Provide your legal first and last name.
3. Enter your email address and phone number for contact.
4. Enter your full address.
5. Enter your child support case number.
6. Select the type of visit you are making.
7. Enter the appointment time if you have one.
8. Select the primary reason for your visit.
9. Provide more details about your visit.
10. Enter the number of children involved in the case.
11. Check if the other parent is cooperating with the process.
12. If you brought documents, select "Yes, all requested documents".
13. If you brought documents, list the documents you submitted.
14. Select if you need an interpreter for your visit.
15. If you need an interpreter, specify the language you need it in.
16. Select if you need any accessibility accommodations.
17. Select how you would prefer to be contacted about your case.
18. Select the best time to contact you.
19. Enter the number of visits you've had this month.
20. Select how satisfied you are with the services received so far.
21. Enter any additional comments or questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Check-In Date** (`check_in_date`, `date`, required): The date of your visit to the child support office.
* **Check-In Time** (`check_in_time`, `time`, required): The time you arrived at the office.
* **Visitor First Name** (`visitor_first_name`, `text`, required): Your legal first name.
* **Visitor Last Name** (`visitor_last_name`, `text`, required): Your legal last name.
* **Visitor Email** (`visitor_email`, `email`, required): Your email address for contact.
* **Visitor Phone** (`visitor_phone`, `text`, required): Your phone number for contact.
* **Visitor Address** (`visitor_address`, `text`, required): Your full street address.
* **Case Number** (`case_number`, `text`, required): Your child support case number.
* **Visit Type** (`visit_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of visit you are making, such as "Scheduled appointment", "Walk-in", etc.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, `time`, optional): If you have a scheduled appointment, enter the time.
* **Reason for Visit** (`reason_for_visit`, `select_one`, required): Select the primary reason for your visit, such as "Modify support order", "Payment inquiry", etc.
* **Visit Details** (`visit_details`, `text`, required): Provide more details about your visit.
* **Number of Children Involved** (`children_involved_count`, `number`, required): Enter the number of children involved in the case.
* **Other Parent Cooperation** (`other_parent_cooperation`, `select_one`, optional): Check if the other parent is cooperating with the process.
* **Documents Brought** (`documents_brought`, `select_one`, required): If you brought documents, select "Yes, all requested documents".
* **Documents List** (`documents_list`, `text`, optional): List the documents you submitted.
* **Interpreter Needed** (`interpreter_needed`, `select_one`, required): If you need an interpreter, select the language you need it in.
* **Interpreter Language** (`interpreter_language`, `text`, optional): If you need an interpreter, specify the language you need.
* **Accessibility Accommodations** (`accessibility_accommodations`, `select_one`, required): Select if you need any accessibility accommodations.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_one`, required): Select how you would prefer to be contacted about your case.
* **Best Contact Time** (`best_contact_time`, `select_one`, required): Select the best time to contact you.
* **Previous Visits This Month** (`previous_visits_this_month`, `select_one`, required): Enter the number of visits you've had this month.
* **Satisfaction with Services** (`satisfaction_with_services`, `select_one`, optional): Select how satisfied you are with the services received so far.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions.

## Tips
* Please complete the form accurately and thoroughly.
* If you need help with any of the fields, please ask for assistance.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please select "I don't know" or "Unknown" and explain in the "Additional Comments".
