# Civic Space Freedom Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about the perceived state of civic space in different regions. Civic space is the freedom and rights that individuals have to participate in civil society, including the right to peaceful assembly, free expression, and association.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your country of residence from the options provided in the first question.
2. For each subsequent question, choose the answer that best reflects your personal experience or perception.
3. If you're unsure about any question, choose the option that best represents your current situation.
4. You may skip questions that don't apply to you or are not relevant to your situation.
5. In the final question, you may add any additional comments or insights you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Country of Residence (1)**: Choose your country of residence from the dropdown list.
* **Type of Area (2)**: Select the type of area where you live. This will help us understand the context in which you experience civic freedoms.
* **Age Range (3)**: Select your age group. This will help us understand how different age groups perceive civic space in different regions.
* **Occupation Category (4)**: Choose your primary occupation type. This will help us understand how different professions experience civic space.
* **Assembly Freedom (5)**: Choose how free you feel to assemble peacefully in your region. This refers to your ability to organize and participate in public gatherings without fear of government retaliation.
* **Expression Freedom (6)**: Choose how free you feel to express opinions, including criticism of government, in your region. This refers to your ability to express yourself without fear of censorship or repercussions.
* **Association Freedom (7)**: Choose how free you feel to form organizations, such as NGOs, advocacy groups, or unions, in your region. This refers to your ability to organize and participate in collective action without obstacles.
* **Press Freedom (8)**: Choose how free the press is in your region. This refers to the ability of journalists and media to report on issues without censorship or government interference.
* **Internet Freedom (9)**: Choose how free is internet access in your region. This refers to your ability to access information and communicate without government monitoring or censorship.
* **Protest Experience (10)**: Choose if you've participated in public protests in the past 5 years. This will help us understand your personal experience with civic space.
* **Protest Safety (11)**: Choose how safe you feel when participating in public protests in your region. This refers to your perception of the risks involved in participating in public gatherings.
* **NGO Registration (12)**: Choose how difficult it is to register an NGO in your region. This refers to the obstacles you face when trying to create or join a collective action group.
* **Funding Restrictions (13)**: Choose if there are restrictions on funding for NGOs in your region. This refers to any limitations on the financial support you can receive for your work.
* **Surveillance Concern (14)**: Choose how concerned you are about government surveillance of civic activities in your region. This refers to your perception of the risks involved in expressing your opinions or participating in public gatherings.
* **Self-Censorship (15)**: Choose if you ever self-censor your opinions on civic or political matters. This refers to your tendency to limit your self-expression due to fear of repercussions.
* **Civic Space Trend (16)**: Choose if you perceive the civic space in your region as improving, declining, or staying the same over the past 5 years. This will help us understand your perspective on the current state of civic space.
* **Improvement Suggestions (17)**: Choose all the ways that could improve civic space in your region. This is an open-ended question where you can suggest any changes or reforms you think would be beneficial.
* **Additional Comments (18)**: Share any additional insights or comments you'd like to add about the civic space in your region. This is an opportunity to provide any further information or context that might be helpful.
