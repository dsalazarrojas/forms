# Petition For Urban Mobility Changes - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and concerns from citizens about urban mobility changes in the city. Your input will help city officials better understand what issues need to be addressed and how they can improve mobility for all citizens.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and email address to ensure that we can get back to you with any updates on your submitted concerns.
2. Provide a phone number (optional) so that we can reach you for further clarification if needed.
3. Choose a date and time when your concern occurred (if applicable).
4. Select your primary concern from the options listed.
5. Choose any additional concerns you have (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name**: Your name is required to ensure we can contact you with any updates on your submitted concerns.
  - Type: text
  - Required: yes
- **Email**: Your email address will be used to contact you for further clarification on your concern.
  - Type: email
  - Required: yes
- **Phone Number**: Your phone number (optional) will help us reach you for further questions.
  - Type: text
  - Required: no
- **Date**: Date when your concern occurred (if applicable).
  - Type: date
  - Required: no
- **Time**: Time when your concern occurred (if applicable).
  - Type: time
  - Required: no
- **What is your primary concern?**: Select one of the listed options that best describes your concern.
  - Type: select_one
  - Required: yes
  - Options:
    - Safer roads
    - Cleaner air
    - Reduced traffic congestion
    - Improved public transportation
- **What are your secondary concerns?**: Select any additional concerns from the listed options.
  - Type: select_multiple
  - Required: no
  - Options:
    - Safer roads
    - Cleaner air
    - Reduced traffic congestion
    - Improved public transportation
    - More bike-lanes
    - Better pedestrian paths

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when describing your concern.
- If you have any additional information or context, please provide it in the comments section.
- If you have already submitted a similar concern, please do not resubmit.
- Your input will help the city make informed decisions on urban mobility changes.
