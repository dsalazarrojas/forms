# Nomination For Judicial Positions - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect nominations for judicial positions, including necessary information and supporting documents required for a successful nomination.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all relevant documents from the options to support your nomination.
2. Provide a clear and concise reason for your nomination.
3. Enter your contact details so that the nomination committee can contact you for further information.
4. Complete any other sections as required by the nomination guidelines.
