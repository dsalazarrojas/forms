# municipal_business_tax_filing_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from municipal businesses for tax filing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your business name in the "Business Name" field.
2. Enter your business address in the "Business Address" field.
3. Enter your contact person's name in the "Contact Person" field.
4. Enter your business phone number in the "Business Phone" field.
5. Enter your business email in the "Business Email" field.
6. Select your tax type from the "Tax Type" dropdown menu.
7. Select the applicable tax period from the "Tax Period" dropdown menu.
8. Add any additional notes about your business in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name (text)**: Enter your business's name as it is registered with the municipal authorities.
* **Business Address (text)**: Enter your business's address where the tax authorities can contact you.
* **Contact Person (text)**: Enter the name of the person who can be contacted by the tax authorities.
* **Business Phone (text)**: Enter your business's phone number where the tax authorities can contact you.
* **Business Email (email)**: Enter your business's email address where the tax authorities can contact you.
* **Tax Type (select_one)**: Select the type of tax that applies to your business (Business Tax, Income Tax, or Sales Tax).
* **Tax Period (select_multiple)**: Select the tax period for which you are filing taxes (Yes or No).
* **Notes (note)**: Add any additional notes about your business that may be helpful for the tax authorities to know.

## Tips
* Please fill the form accurately and completely to avoid any discrepancies in the tax calculation.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the tax authorities for clarification.
* The form will not be completed if any field is left blank.
* This form is designed for municipal businesses only, so please do not fill it if you are not a business.
