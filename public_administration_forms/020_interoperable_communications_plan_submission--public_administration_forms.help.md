# interoperable_communications_plan_submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The interoperable_communications_plan_submission form is a submission form designed to gather information related to interoperable communications plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the submission details, including any relevant information about the submitted plan.
* Provide contact details for the submitter, including name and contact information.
* Upload any relevant documents or attachments.
* Choose the submission date and time.
* Select the submission time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submission Details** (`submission_details`, text, optional):
	+ Provide a brief description of the submission, including any relevant context or background information.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name and contact information of the person submitting the plan.
* **Document Uploader** (`document_uploader`, text, optional):
	+ Upload any supporting documents or attachments related to the submission.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional):
	+ Choose the date of the submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional):
	+ Choose the time of the submission.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and relevant information in each field to ensure a smooth and efficient review process.
* Use a clear and concise title and description for the submission.
* Attach any required or supporting documents to the submission.
* Be aware that some fields are optional, but providing them can aid in the review process.
* If you need help or have questions, do not hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
