# urban_design_public_input_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from the public for urban design input submissions. It is intended to be a public-facing form, accessible to anyone who wants to submit their thoughts and opinions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the fields with your information and comments as prompted.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and relevant information in each field.
3. If you have any questions or need help, contact our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Details** (`user_details`, `text`, optional): This field is used to collect your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
- **Street Input** (`street_input`, `text`, optional): This field is for providing feedback or suggestions related to the urban design of a specific street or area.
- **Public Space Input** (`public_space_input`, `text`, optional): This field is for providing feedback or suggestions related to public spaces, such as parks or public buildings.
- **Project Input** (`project_input`, `text`, optional): This field is for providing feedback or suggestions related to specific urban design projects.
- **Comments Input** (`comments_input`, `text`, optional): This field is for providing any additional comments or thoughts you have on the urban design of the area or project.
- **Email Input** (`email_input`, `email`, optional): This field is for providing your email address, in case we need to contact you about your submission.
- **Phone Input** (`phone_input`, `text`, optional): This field is for providing your phone number, in case we need to contact you about your submission.
- **Date Input** (`date_input`, `date`, optional): This field is for selecting a date related to your submission.
- **Time Input** (`time_input`, `time`, optional): This field is for selecting a time related to your submission.
- **Select Input** (`select_input`, `select_one`, optional): This field is a dropdown menu where you can select one option from the list.
- **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is a dropdown menu where you can select multiple options from the list.

## Tips
* Please be clear and concise in your submissions.
* Make sure to proofread your submission before submitting.
* If you have any further questions or need help, contact our support team.
