# Legislative Meeting Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legislative Meeting Feedback Survey is designed to gather information from participants on their experiences, feedback, and expectations for future meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Start by entering your Participant Name**. This is where you enter your name, as a participant of the meeting.
2. **Choose your Feedback Session**. Please select the type of session you attended (Meeting, Presentation, or Other) and provide details in the next field if you selected "Other".
3. **Rate your overall experience**. This is your rating on a scale of 1-5.
4. **Provide feedback**. Share your thoughts on what you liked and disliked about the session.
5. **Make suggestions**. If you have any suggestions for future sessions, please enter them in this field.
6. **Check if you attended**. Please select if you attended the meeting.
7. **Rate your satisfaction**. This is your rating of your overall satisfaction with the meeting.
8. **Check if you have next meeting plans**. If you plan to attend the next meeting, please select "True", otherwise, "False" or "Not decided".
9. **Provide any additional comments or feedback**. This is your opportunity to add any additional thoughts or comments about the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as a participant of the meeting.
* **Rating**: Rate your overall experience with the meeting on a scale of 1-5.
* **Feedback**: Share your thoughts on what you liked and disliked about the session.
* **Suggestion**: If you have any suggestions for future sessions, please enter them here.
* **Participant Survey**: If you participated in a participant survey, select "True", otherwise, "False" or "Maybe".
* **Participant Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the meeting on a scale of Very satisfied, Satisfied, Not satisfied, Very dissatisfied, or No answer.
* **Participant Next Meeting**: If you plan to attend the next meeting, select "True", otherwise, "False" or "Not decided".
