# Criminal Booking Process Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Criminal Booking Process Checklist Form is designed to aid in the collection and documentation of essential information related to a criminal case. This form provides a structured and organized way to gather data, making it easier to analyze and manage the case.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before starting.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled accurately and completely.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in each field.
4. Update the form regularly to reflect changes in the case.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Checklist 1**: This field is not currently active in the form, but you may need to fill it out in the future. Please check with your supervisor if you need to use this field.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the case.
* **Criminal Offence Description**: Describe the crime that occurred, including the details and circumstances surrounding it.
* **Criminal Charge**: Select 'Yes' if the suspect is charged with a crime, 'No' otherwise.
* **Date of Offence**: Enter the date when the crime occurred or is suspected to have occurred.
* **Time of Offence**: Enter the time when the crime occurred or is suspected to have occurred.
* **Location of Offence**: Describe the location where the crime occurred or is suspected to have occurred.
* **Suspect Details**: Enter any information you have about the suspect, such as their age, address, phone number, and email.
* **Suspect Photo**: Enter any description of the suspect's appearance.
* **Crime Scene Details**: Enter any details about the crime scene, such as the location and any notable features.
* **Witness Details**: Enter any information about the witness, including their contact information.
* **Suspect Name**: Enter the name of the suspect.
* **Suspect Age**: Enter the age of the suspect.
* **Suspect Address**: Enter the address of the suspect.
* **Suspect Phone**: Enter the suspect's phone number.
* **Suspect Email**: Enter the suspect's email address.
* **Booking Officer**: Enter the name and title of the officer making the booking.
* **Booking Officer ID**: Enter the ID number of the officer making the booking.
* **Crime Number**: Enter a unique identifier for the crime.
* **Crime Scene Location**: Describe the location of the crime scene.
* **Criminal Offence Details**: Enter a detailed description of the crime.
* **Suspect Status**: Select the status of the suspect, whether 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Witness Status**: Select the status of the witness, whether 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Officer Status**: Select the status of the officer making the booking, whether 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Suspect Injuries**: Enter any injuries sustained by the suspect during or after the crime.
