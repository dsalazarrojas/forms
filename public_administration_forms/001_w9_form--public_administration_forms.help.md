# W9 Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The W9 Form is a Certification of Other Income form used by employees to report income from other sources that's subject to withholding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "name" field.
2. Enter the date you received the income in the "date" field.
3. Provide your street address in the "street" field.
4. Enter your apartment number (if applicable) in the "apartment" field.
5. In the second page (if enabled), enter any additional information or comments if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents (e.g., driver's license, passport, etc.).
* **date** (date, required): Enter the date you received the income.
* **street** (text, required): Enter the street address where you live.
* **apartment** (text, required): Enter your apartment number (if you have one).
* **w9_form_page_2** (text, optional): Enter any additional information or comments (if needed).
