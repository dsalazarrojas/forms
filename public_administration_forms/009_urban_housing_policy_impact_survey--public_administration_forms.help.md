# Urban Housing Policy Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather your thoughts and experiences on the current urban housing policy and its effects on your living situation. The information collected will be used to inform and improve policies for a specific community.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

* Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
* If you have any personal experiences or opinions related to the question, feel free to elaborate in the text fields.
* You can select one or more options from the dropdown menu for question 4.
* Make sure to fill in all required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
* Click on the "Submit" button at the end of the form to send your responses.

* **Urban Housing Policy Impact Survey (Note)**: This is a heading to let you know what this form is about.
* **What are your experiences with housing conditions? (Text)**: Share your experiences with issues such as overcrowding, poor living conditions, or other housing-related challenges you've faced.
* **How do you feel policy effects have impacted your living situation? (Text)**: Describe how you've felt the impact of policies related to housing in your area, both positively and negatively.
* **Which policy impacts do you believe have affected your housing situation? (Select One)**:
	+ Option 1: Option 1
	+ Option 2: Option 2
	+ Option 3: Option 3
	+ Option 4: Option 4
	+ Option 5: Option 5
* **What policy opportunities do you believe are needed to address your housing situation? (Text)**: Suggest potential solutions or improvements to address housing-related issues you've faced.
* **Do you have any comments or suggestions regarding housing policy? (Text)**: Add any additional thoughts or ideas you have on how to improve housing policies.
* **How would you like to be contacted? (Text)**: Let us know your preferred method of contact, such as email or phone number.
* **Submit (Text)**: Click this button to send your completed survey.
