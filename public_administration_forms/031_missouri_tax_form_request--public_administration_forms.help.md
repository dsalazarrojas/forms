# Missouri Tax Form Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Missouri Tax Form Request form is used by public administration staff to collect information from citizens who are requesting tax forms or other documents from the state of Missouri.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Missouri Tax Form Request form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it appears on the official document.
2. Provide your address where you would like to receive the document.
3. Enter your phone number so we can contact you with any further questions.
4. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
5. Choose the type of document you are requesting (e.g. Tax Return, W-2, etc.).
6. Select the fiscal year the document pertains to.
7. Enter the date you would like to receive the document.
8. Enter your mailing address where you would like to receive the document.
9. Enter the city of your mailing address.
10. Enter the state of your mailing address.
11. Enter your zip code of your mailing address.
12. Enter the country of your mailing address (not required for international requests).
13. Add any comments or additional information you would like to provide to assist with your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Request** (`form_request`): Enter a brief description of the document you are requesting.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your name as it appears on the official document.
* **Address** (`address`): Provide your address where you would like to receive the document.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with any further questions.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Document Type** (`document_type`): Choose the type of document you are requesting (e.g. Tax Return, W-2, etc.).
* **Fiscal Year** (`fiscal_year`): Select the fiscal year the document pertains to.
* **Date Requested** (`date_requested`): Enter the date you would like to receive the document.
* **Date Mailed** (`date_mailed`): Enter the date the document was mailed to you.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`): Enter your mailing address where you would like to receive the document.
* **City** (`city`): Enter the city of your mailing address.
* **State** (`state`): Enter the state of your mailing address.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`): Enter your zip code of your mailing address.
* **Country** (`country`): Enter the country of your mailing address (not required for international requests).
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any comments or additional information you would like to provide to assist with your request.
