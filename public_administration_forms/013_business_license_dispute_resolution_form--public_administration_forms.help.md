# Business License Dispute Resolution Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business License Dispute Resolution Form is used to submit disputes related to business licenses. It guides you through the necessary steps to present your case and provides a structured process for the relevant authorities to assess and resolve the dispute.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately, providing as much detail as possible for each field.
2. Ensure you've selected the correct type of dispute and agency involved.
3. Attach any supporting documentation and describe it in the 'Supporting Documentation' field.
4. Indicate whether you've communicated about this issue before and describe any previous communications.
5. Choose your desired outcome and describe it in detail.
6. Select your preferred method of communication and availability for meetings or hearings.
7. Finally, provide any additional comments or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (1, text, required): Enter your business' legal name.
* **Business License Number** (2, text, required): Enter your business' license number.
* **Business Address** (3, text, required): Enter your business' full address.
* **Business Owner Name** (4, text, required): Enter the full name of the business owner.
* **Contact Email** (5, email, required): Enter your email address for correspondence.
* **Contact Phone** (6, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Type of Dispute** (7, select_one, required): Select the type of dispute you are experiencing (e.g., License denial, License suspension, etc.).
* **Date of Dispute Action** (8, date, required): Enter the date the disputed action occurred.
* **Agency Involved** (9, text, required): Identify the agency that issued the disputed action.
* **Dispute Description** (10, text, required): Describe the dispute in detail.
* **Grounds for Dispute** (11, text, required): Explain why you believe the action is incorrect.
* **Supporting Documentation** (12, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant documentation you will submit (e.g., Business license copy, Correspondence letters, etc.).
* **Previous Communications** (13, select_one, required): Indicate whether you've communicated about this issue before and how many times.
* **Communication Details** (14, text, optional): Describe any previous communications if you've answered "Yes" to the previous question.
* **Desired Outcome** (15, select_one, required): Choose the resolution you are seeking (e.g., License reinstatement, License approval, etc.).
* **Outcome Description** (16, text, required): Describe your desired resolution in detail.
* **Timeline Urgency** (17, select_one, required): Indicate the level of urgency for your desired outcome (e.g., Critical, Urgent, etc.).
* **Business Impact** (18, text, required): Describe how this issue has impacted your business.
* **Legal Representation** (19, select_one, required): Indicate whether you have legal representation for this matter.
* **Hearing Preference** (20, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred method of communication for any hearings or meetings.
* **Availability Dates** (21, text, optional): List your available dates for meetings or hearings.
* **Additional Comments** (22, text, optional): Provide any additional relevant information.
