# Decision Review Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a decision review request to the review committee. The form helps to track and manage the review process, ensuring that all necessary information is collected and reviewed before making a decision.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide the submission details, including any relevant context or background information.
2. Select the case details, if applicable.
3. Choose the review request option: Review and Approve, Send for Review, or Revoke.
4. Enter any review details, including the date for the review.
5. Choose the review status: In Progress, Complete, or Cancelled.
6. Add any additional notes for the reviewer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submission Details** (Text, Optional):
	+ A short description of the submission, providing context or background information.
* **Case Details** (Text, Optional):
	+ Additional information related to the case being reviewed.
* **Review Request** (Select One, Optional):
	+ Choose how you want the review to proceed:
		- Review and Approve: The reviewer will approve the decision without further review.
		- Send for Review: The reviewer will review and potentially make a decision after reviewing the submission.
		- Revoke: The review request will be cancelled, and no further action will be taken.
* **Review Details** (Date, Optional):
	+ The date by which the review should be completed or the review should be done by.
* **Review Status** (Select One, Optional):
	+ The current status of the review:
		- In Progress: The review is currently being processed.
		- Complete: The review has been completed, and a decision has been made.
		- Cancelled: The review request was cancelled, and no further action will be taken.
* **Notes** (Text, Optional):
	+ Additional comments or notes for the reviewer.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide enough context in the **Submission Details** field to ensure a smooth review process.
* Choose the correct **Review Request** option based on the review requirements.
* If the review status needs to be changed, make sure to update the **Review Status** field accordingly.
* Use the **Notes** field to provide any additional information that might aid the reviewer in making a decision.
