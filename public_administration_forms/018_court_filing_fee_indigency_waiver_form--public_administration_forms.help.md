# Court Filing Fee Indigency Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to assess the eligibility of an individual for a court filing fee waiver. It collects information about the individual's financial situation, including income, expenses, and financial aid status, to determine their ability to pay court fees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
2. Provide detailed information about your financial situation.
3. Check your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Legal Aid Group** (`legal_aid_group`): This field is used to specify the legal aid group that is associated with this case.
* **Indigency Waiver Reason** (`indigency_waiver_reason`): This field should be filled in with a brief explanation of why you are requesting a fee waiver.
* **Financial Hardship Status** (`financial_hardship_status`): Select all applicable options that describe your financial situation:
	+ Self-Employed
	+ Unemployed
	+ Disabled
	+ Retired
	+ Other (specify)
* **Monthly Income** (`monthly_income`): Enter your monthly income.
* **Household Size** (`household_size`): Enter the number of people living in your household.
* **Monthly Expenses** (`monthly_expenses`): Enter your monthly expenses.
* **Total Household Income** (`total_household_income`): Enter the total monthly income of your household.
* **Household Income Percent** (`household_income_percent`): Enter the percentage of your household income that is available for expenses.
* **Financial Aid Program** (`financial_aid_program`): Select if you are enrolled in a financial aid program:
	+ No Financial Aid Program
	+ Yes, I am in a financial aid program
* **Program Funding Amount** (`program_funding_amount`): Enter the amount of funding provided by your financial aid program.
* **Other Financial Aid Sources** (`other_financial_aid_sources`): Enter any additional financial aid sources, such as government benefits or assistance programs.
* **Other Financial Aid Sources Details** (`other_financial_aid_sources_details`): Provide a brief explanation of other financial aid sources.
* **Financial Aid Program Name** (`financial_aid_program_name`): Enter the name of your financial aid program.
* **Other Financial Aid Sources Percent** (`other_financial_aid_sources_percent`): Enter the percentage of funding provided by other financial aid sources.
* **Court Fee Waiver Request** (`court_fee_waiver_request`): Select whether you are requesting a fee waiver:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Court Fee Waiver Amount** (`court_fee_waiver_amount`): Enter the amount of fee waiver requested.
* **Total Expenses** (`total_expenses`): Enter the total amount of expenses you are experiencing.
* **Financial Aid Program Funding** (`financial_aid_program_funding`): Select if your financial aid program provides funding for court fees:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Court Fee Waiver Amount** (`court_fee_waiver_amount`): Enter the amount of fee waiver requested.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`): Enter your name and title.
* **Submitter Organization** (`submitter_organization`): Enter the name of your organization (if applicable).
* **Submitter Address** (`submitter_address`): Enter your address.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`): Enter your email address.
