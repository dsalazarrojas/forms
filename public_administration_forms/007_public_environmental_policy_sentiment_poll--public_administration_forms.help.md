# public-environmental-policy-sentiment-poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from the public on the effectiveness and relevance of environmental policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Environmental Policy Questionnaire section with your thoughts and opinions.
2. Choose the options that best represent your sentiment and views.
3. Answer the questions in the Comments section.
4. Enter your contact information in the Contact section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Environmental Policy Questionnaire**: This section asks about your general views on environmental policies.
	+ **Name**: This is the name you'd like to use for your comments.
	+ **Address**: This is your contact address.
	+ **Organization**: If you're representing an organization, please enter it here.
	+ **Position**: Your position in the organization.
* **Environmental Sentiment Rating**: Rate your sentiment on environmental policies from -10 to 10, where -10 is very negative and 10 is very positive.
* **Effective Pollution Control Measures**: Choose how much you agree or disagree with the effectiveness of pollution control measures.

	+ **Agree**

	+ **Strongly Disagree**

* **Policy Effectiveness**: Rate the effectiveness of environmental policies from -10 to 10, where -10 is not effective at all and 10 is very effective.
* **Environmental Sentiment**: Choose how you feel about environmental policies.
	+ **Positive**
	+ **Negative**
	+ **Neutral**
* **Policy Relevance**: Rate how relevant you think environmental policies are to you.
	+ **High**
	+ **Low**
	+ **Moderate**

* Be honest and accurate with your answers.
* Make sure to provide as much information as you can.
