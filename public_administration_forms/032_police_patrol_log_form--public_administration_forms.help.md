# Police Patrol Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and document information about police officer shifts, including shift start and end times, officer's involvement in incidents, and any relevant notes. The goal is to provide a clear and concise record of officer activities and incidents during a given shift.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety or section by section as needed.
2. Select the correct options for each field as they apply to your situation.
3. Complete all required fields to ensure accurate and complete documentation.
4. Double-check the information you enter to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shift Start** (`patrol_log_form_shift_start`): Enter the shift start time.
* **Shift Start Time** (`shift_start_time`): Indicate if this shift is a start or end of shift. Options are 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Shift End Time** (`shift_end_time`): Indicate if this shift is a start or end of shift. Options are 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Officer Number** (`officer_number`): Enter your officer number.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`): Describe the incident in as much detail as possible.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`): Enter the location of the incident.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`): Indicate the type of incident. Options are 'Yes' or 'No'. This field is likely for recording whether an incident occurred or not.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`): Enter the date of the incident (when possible).
* **Shift Notes** (`shift_notes`): Enter any relevant notes about the shift or incident.
* **Officer Signature** (`officer_signature`): Include your signature to confirm you've completed this form.

Tips:
* Be as accurate and detailed as possible when entering information into each field.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask for clarification from your supervisor or a colleague.
* Save and keep track of this form for easy reference during and after the shift.
