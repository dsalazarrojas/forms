# General Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an incident that has occurred on campus, including the date and time of the incident, witness details, and witness statements. The form is used to document incidents and facilitate reporting to relevant authorities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling the form.
2. Enter the date and time of the incident in the "Incident Date" field. The date should be in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Provide the witness's name and contact information in the "Witness Name" and "Witness Contact Info" fields respectively.
4. Enter the location of the incident in the "Incident Location" field.
5. Enter a brief description of the incident in the "Incident Description" field.
6. Provide the witness's statement in the "Witness Statement" field.
7. If relevant, enter the Police Report ID in the "Police Report ID" field.
8. Select the witness's safety status from the "Witness Safety Status" fields.
9. Click on the "Submit" button to submit the form.
