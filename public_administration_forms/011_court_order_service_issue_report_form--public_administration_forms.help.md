# Court Order Service Issue Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about incidents or issues related to court order services. Please use it to report new or ongoing problems you've experienced.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the issue(s) that best describe what happened from the "Service Attempts and Issues" dropdown menu.
2. Enter your name in the "User Name" field.
3. Choose the specific issue you're experiencing from the "Issue Details" dropdown menu.
4. Provide more details about the issue in the "Issue Reason" text field.
5. If you experienced a document service issue, check the "Document Service" box.
6. If you encountered other service issues not listed, select them from the "Other Service Issues" dropdown menu.
7. Record the date and time of the incident in the "Date of Incident" and "Time of Incident" fields.
8. Add any additional comments about the incident in the "Comments" text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Attempts and Issues** (Select Multiple): This field allows you to choose the issue you're experiencing. Select all that apply.
* **User Name** (Text): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification or as you'd like it to be displayed in our records.
* **Issue Details** (Select One): Choose the specific issue you're experiencing from the dropdown menu.
* **Issue Reason** (Text): Describe the issue you're experiencing in more detail. Be as specific as possible.
* **Document Service** (Text): Check this box if you experienced a document service issue.
* **Other Service Issues** (Select Multiple): If you've experienced other issues not listed, select them from the dropdown menu.
* **Date of Incident** (Date): Record the date of the incident.
* **Time of Incident** (Time): Record the time of the incident.
* **Comments** (Text): Add any additional comments or details about the incident here.
