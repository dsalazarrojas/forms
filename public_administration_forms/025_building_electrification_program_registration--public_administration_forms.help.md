# Building Electrification Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps the Building Electrification Program understand your building's needs and your interest in participating in the program. The information you provide will help us tailor our support and resources to your specific situation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the option that best describes how you learned about this program.
2. Provide your property owner information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
3. Specify your property type (e.g., single-family home, multi-family building, etc.).
4. Describe your current heating and water heating systems.
5. Select the priority areas you would like to electrify.
6. Choose your primary motivation for electrification.
7. Indicate your planned timeline for upgrades.
8. Choose your budget expectations.
9. Specify if you are aware of available incentives and which types of support you are interested in.
10. Select your preferred financing option.
11. If you have a contractor, indicate if you have already selected one.
12. Indicate if you would like contractor referrals or not.
13. Provide any additional information or questions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How did you learn about this program?**: This question helps us understand how you became aware of the program. Please select the option that best describes your source of information.
	+ Municipality or utility notice
	+ Community organization
	+ Social media
	+ News article
	+ Contractor recommendation
	+ Word of mouth
	+ Email
	+ Other
