# Smart Traffic Flow Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Traffic Flow Application Form is designed to collect information from applicants about their smart traffic flow applications. This form is used to gather necessary details for processing and reviewing applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all the required information before starting the form. Some fields are not mandatory, but providing accurate information will help speed up the processing of your application.
2.  Fill out the form by entering your answers in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Smart Traffic Flow Application** (`smart_traffic_flow_application_form`, text, Optional): Enter a brief description of your smart traffic flow application.
* **Locations** (`locations`, text, Optional): List the locations related to your smart traffic flow application.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, Optional): Enter the start date of your smart traffic flow application.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, Optional): Enter the end date of your smart traffic flow application.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, note, Optional): Attach any supporting documents related to your smart traffic flow application.
* **Agency** (`agency`, select_multiple, Optional): Select all applicable agencies that will be involved in your smart traffic flow application. This will be 'Yes' or 'No' options.
* **Municipality** (`municipality`, select_multiple, Optional): Select all applicable municipalities that will be involved in your smart traffic flow application. This will be 'Yes' or 'No' options.
* **Company** (`company`, select_multiple, Optional): Select all applicable companies that will be involved in your smart traffic flow application. This will be 'Yes' or 'No' options.
* **Project Manager Name** (`project_manager_name`, text, Optional): Enter the name of your project manager.
* **Project Manager Title** (`project_manager_title`, text, Optional): Enter the title of your project manager.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, Optional): Provide a description of your project.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification before submitting the form.
* Supporting documents can be attached as PDFs or images.
