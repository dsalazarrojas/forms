## Purpose
The Government Relationship Survey is a form created to collect feedback on your interactions with government services. Your responses will help us improve and provide better services for you and the community.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your input for each question. All questions are optional.
2. Select the correct answers for each question.
3. If other options are available for certain questions, please specify.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Government Relationship Survey Form** (`government_relationship_survey_form_title`, Text, Optional): This is the title of the form. You can provide a brief description.
* **What services have you interacted with in the past 6 months?** (`government_relationship_survey_services`, Select Multiple, Optional): Check all the services you have interacted with in the past 6 months.
* **How satisfied are you with the services?** (`government_relationship_survey_satisfaction`, Select One, Optional): Choose how satisfied you are with the services you interacted with.
* **How accessible were these services?** (`government_relationship_survey_services_accessibility`, Select One, Optional): Choose how accessible you found the services.
* **How transparent were the services?** (`government_relationship_survey_transparency`, Select One, Optional): Choose how transparent you found the services.
* **How often do you interact with government services?** (`government_relationship_survey_frequency`, Select One, Optional): Choose how often you interact with government services.
* **If you interact frequently, what drives this frequency?** (`government_relationship_survey_frequency_reason`, Select Multiple, Optional): If you interact frequently, choose what drives this frequency.
* **If other, please specify** (`government_relationship_survey_frequency_other`, Text, Optional): If other reasons apply, please specify.
* **Do you have any suggestions for improvement?** (`government_relationship_survey_suggestions`, Text, Optional): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please provide them.
* **How would you like to be contacted?** (`government_relationship_survey_contact_info`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted.
* **Any comments?** (`government_relationship_survey_comment`, Text, Optional): If you have any additional comments, please provide them.

## Tips
Please provide honest and constructive feedback. Your input is crucial for us to improve our services.

Note: This form is designed to be completed on a computer, tablet, or mobile device. Please ensure you are in a comfortable and private environment to complete the form.

This guide is a basic explanation of how to complete the form. If you need further assistance, feel free to contact us.
