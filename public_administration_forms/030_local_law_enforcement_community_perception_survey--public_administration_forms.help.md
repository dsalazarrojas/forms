# Local Law Enforcement Community Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and opinions from the community on their experience with the local law enforcement.

## How To Complete This Form
Please fill out this form by answering the questions as accurately as possible. Each question has a label and a corresponding explanation that will guide you through the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Set of Questions**: This section is meant to introduce the topic and get a general understanding of your experience with the local law enforcement.
* **Safety**: Please rate your level of safety in your community, from Very Poor to Excellent.
* **Trust**: Please rate how much you trust the local law enforcement, from Very Poor to Excellent.
* **Service Quality**: Please provide your feedback on the quality of service provided by the local law enforcement.
* **Officer Interactions**: Please provide your feedback on your interactions with law enforcement officers.
* **Service Quality Rating**: Please rate the quality of service provided by the local law enforcement, from 1 to 5.
* **Communication**: Please provide your feedback on the communication between the law enforcement and the community.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the space provided for Service Quality and Officer Interactions.
* If you need assistance with the form, contact form@gic.mx.
