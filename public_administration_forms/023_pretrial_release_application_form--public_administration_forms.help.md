# Pretrial Release Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pretrial Release Application Form is used to apply for pretrial release, a legal process that allows an individual to be released from pretrial detention before their trial.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Date of Birth in the Date Of Birth field.
3. Provide your Contact Number and Email address for contact purposes.
4. Enter the Release Date you are requesting for pretrial release.
5. Select the Release Time you are requesting for pretrial release.
6. Provide a reason for your pretrial release application in the Reason For Release field.
7. Enter the Case Number associated with your case.
8. If you have an attorney, enter their name and contact phone number.
9. Select the status of your pretrial release application: Released, Not Released, or Pending.
10. Provide any additional comments about your application.
11. Confirm that you have a valid pretrial release request ID (optional, but recommended for tracking purposes).
12. Confirm the Court Case Number associated with your case (optional, but recommended for tracking purposes).
13. Confirm that you have signed the release agreement (if applicable).
14. If you have an attorney, confirm that they have signed the release agreement (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): This field is used to enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): This field is used to enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): This field is used to enter your date of birth.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, number, required): This field is used to enter your contact number for communication purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This field is used to enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, required): This field is used to enter the date you are requesting for pretrial release.
* **Release Time** (`release_time`, time, required): This field is used to enter the time you are requesting for pretrial release.
* **Reason For Release** (`reason_for_release`, text, required): This field is used to provide a reason for your pretrial release application.
* **Case Number** (`case_number`, text, required): This field is used to enter the case number associated with your case.
* **Attorney Name** (`attorney_name`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the name of your attorney.
* **Attorney Phone** (`attorney_phone`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the contact phone number of your attorney.
* **Release Status** (`release_status`, select_one, required): This field is used to select the status of your pretrial release application: Released, Not Released, or Pending.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): This field is used to provide any additional comments about your application.
* **Pretrial Release Request ID** (`pretrial_release_request_id`, text, optional): This field is used to enter a pretrial release request ID (optional, but recommended for tracking purposes).
* **Court Case Number** (`court_case_number`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the court case number associated with your case (optional, but recommended for tracking purposes).
* **Pretrial Release Form ID** (`pretrial_release_form_id`, text, optional): This field is used to enter a pretrial release form ID (optional, but recommended for tracking purposes).
* **Release Agreement Signed** (`release_agreement_signed`, select_one, required): This field is used to confirm that you have signed the release agreement (if applicable).

## Tips
* Ensure that you have filled out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Review your application carefully before submission to avoid errors.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
