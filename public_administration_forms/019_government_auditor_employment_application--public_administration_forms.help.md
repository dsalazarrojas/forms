# Government Auditor Employment Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect employment applications from interested government agency staff members for auditor or analyst positions. Please fill out the form to provide your contact information, job preferences, and work details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information (pages 2 and 11-13).
2. Choose your job preferences (page 3).
3. Enter your education background (page 4).
4. Attach your resume (page 5).
5. Describe your professional experience (page 6).
6. List any relevant skills you have (page 7).
7. Mention any certifications you have obtained (page 8).
8. Specify your employment information (page 9).
9. Choose your job role (page 14).
10. Select your job type (page 15).
11. Enter your desired job location (page 16).
12. Specify your department and division (pages 17-18).
13. Specify your work type (page 19).
14. Enter your desired work location (page 20).
15. Choose your job status (page 21).
16. Enter your job level (page 22).
17. Enter your expected salary (page 23).
18. Choose your benefits options (page 24).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Information**: (page 1) - Provide your name and any other relevant details you'd like to share.
* **Contact Information**: (pages 2-3) - Enter your contact email and other relevant contact information.
* **Job Preferences**: (page 4) - Choose your preferred job type (full time, part time, or both).
* **Education**: (page 5) - Enter your educational background, including degrees and institutions attended.
* **Resume**: (page 6) - Attach your resume or CV.
* **Experience**: (page 7) - Describe your professional experience, including roles and responsibilities.
* **Skills**: (page 8) - List any skills you have that are relevant to the position.
* **Certification**: (page 9) - Mention any certifications you have obtained.
* **Employment Information**: (page 10) - Provide details about your current or previous employment.
* **Job Role**: (page 11) - Choose your desired job role (auditor or analyst).
* **Job Type**: (page 12) - Choose your desired job type (full time, part time, or both).
* **Job Location**: (page 13) - Enter your desired job location.
* **Department**: (page 14) - Specify your desired department.
* **Division**: (page 15) - Specify your desired division.
* **Work Type**: (page 16) - Choose your work type (government agency, private sector, or both).
* **Work Location**: (page 17) - Enter your desired work location.
* **Job Status**: (page 18) - Choose your job status (active, inactive, or both).
* **Job Level**: (page 19) - Enter your desired job level.
* **Salary**: (page 20) - Enter your expected salary.
* **Benefits**: (page 21) - Choose your benefits options (with benefits, without benefits, or not specified).
* **Submit**: (page 22) - Click submit to submit your application.

Note: All fields marked as required are necessary for the HR department to review your application.
