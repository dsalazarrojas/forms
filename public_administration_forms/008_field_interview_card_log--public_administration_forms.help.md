# Field Interview Card Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Field Interview Card Log form is used to record and document information related to field interviews, including details such as card number, team leader, date and time of completion, location, team member, card status, comments, contact information, and attachments.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Field Interview Card Log**: Enter the title of the field interview in this field.
2. **Card Number**: Enter the number assigned to this particular field interview.
3. **Team Leader**: Select the team leader who conducted the interview from the list.
4. **Date Completed**: Enter the date when the interview was completed.
5. **Time Completed**: Enter the time when the interview was completed (optional).
6. **Location**: Enter the location where the interview took place.
7. **Team Member**: Select the team member who attended the interview (optional).
8. **Card Status**: Select the current status of the field interview card (optional).
9. **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or observations about the interview.
10. **Email**: Enter the email of the person being interviewed or contacted.
11. **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person being interviewed or contacted (optional).
12. **Additional Comments**: Enter any further details or information about the interview that don't fit in the standard fields (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Field Interview Card Log** (`form_title`, required/optional): This field should contain a brief description of the field interview.
* **Card Number** (`card_number`, required): Enter the unique number assigned to this field interview.
* **Team Leader** (`team_leader`, required): Select the team leader who conducted the interview from the options provided.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, required): Enter the date when the interview was completed.
* **Time Completed** (`time_completed`, optional): Enter the time when the interview was completed (if applicable).
* **Location** (`location`, optional): Enter the location where the interview took place.
* **Team Member** (`team_member`, optional): Select the team member who attended the interview (if applicable).
* **Card Status** (`card_status`, optional): Select the current status of the field interview card from the options provided (if applicable).
* **Comments** (`comments`, optional): Enter any additional comments or observations about the interview.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter the email of the person being interviewed or contacted.
* **Phone** (`phone`, optional): Enter the phone number of the person being interviewed or contacted (if applicable).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, optional): Enter any further details or information about the interview that don't fit in the standard fields.
