# Law Enforcement Stress Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report and track law enforcement officers' leaves of absence due to stress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form and ensure you are submitting for a valid reason.
2. Fill out the form in its entirety, providing the required information.
3. Select the relevant options for the questions asked.
4. Submit the form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Law Enforcement Stress Leave** (`law_enforcement_stress_leave`, select_one, optional): Check if you are taking a leave of absence due to stress. If 'Yes', please select the reason for your leave (see **Reason For Leave** field).
* **Reason For Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more reasons for your leave. Please choose from the options provided.
	+ Stress
	+ Burnout
	+ Trauma
* **Number Of Days Off** (`number_of_days_off`, number, optional): Enter the number of days off you are requesting.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your leave.
* **Reviewers** (`reviewers`, select_multiple, optional): Select the reviewers for your leave. Please choose from the options provided.
	+ John Doe
	+ Jane Doe
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments related to your leave.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): The date the form is submitted.

## Tips
- Be honest when reporting the reason for your leave.
- Provide accurate information for the number of days off and end date.
- Ensure that the reviewers are aware of the situation and can provide the necessary support.
- Submit the form as soon as possible to minimize impact on your work and well-being.
