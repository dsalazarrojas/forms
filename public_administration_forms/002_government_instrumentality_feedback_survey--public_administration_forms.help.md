# Government Instrumentality Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Government Instrumentality Feedback Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from the public about their experience with government services. This feedback helps the government agencies to identify areas of improvement and provide better services to the public.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by entering your service name in the first field.
2. Rate the agency's overall performance using the scale of 1-10.
3. Select the overall satisfaction level with the service (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
4. If you would like to be contacted for follow-up, select how frequently you would like to be contacted (1, 2, or 3).
5. Choose the preferred method of follow-up contact (Email, Phone, or In Person).
6. Enter the current status of the follow-up action (In Progress, Completed, or Not Started).
7. Provide any additional comments about your experience.
8. Enter the notes for the follow-up action.
9. Finally, describe the next action to take for the follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Name**: Enter the name of the government service you are providing feedback about.
* **Agency Rating**: Rate the agency's performance on a scale of 1-10.
* **Rating Score**: Rate the overall satisfaction level with the service (1-10).
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Select the overall satisfaction level with the service (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments about your experience.
* **Follow Up Frequency**: Select how frequently you would like to be contacted for follow-up (1, 2, or 3).
* **Follow Up Method**: Choose the preferred method of follow-up contact (Email, Phone, or In Person).
* **Follow Up Status**: Enter the current status of the follow-up action (In Progress, Completed, or Not Started).
* **Follow Up Notes**: Enter notes for the follow-up action.
* **Follow Up Next Action**: Describe the next action to take for the follow-up.

## Tips

* Please provide accurate and honest feedback about your experience with the government service.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have any additional comments, please provide them in the Comments field.
* If you need help, do not hesitate to contact the support team.
