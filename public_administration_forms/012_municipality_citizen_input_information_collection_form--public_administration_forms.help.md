# Municipality Citizen Input Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Municipality Citizen Input Information Collection Form is designed to gather accurate and complete information from citizens on their concerns and issues, preventing duplication and ensuring efficient handling by the municipal authorities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the first two fields to identify yourself.
2. Provide a valid email address in the Email field to facilitate communication.
3. Enter your phone number, which will be used for contact purposes.
4. Select the Department concerned with the issue from the Department dropdown list.
5. Enter the Date of Concern, Time of Concern, and Location of the issue.
6. Provide a clear Description of the Concern.
7. Attach any relevant files or images to support your input (optional).
8. Optionally enter any Comments about the issue.
9. Fill in the Name of Contact, if applicable.
10. Enter the Phone Number and Email of the Contact, if applicable.
11. Select the Department of Contact.
12. Enter the Date and Time of Contact.
13. Provide a Name for the Submission and a valid Email address.
14. Select the Department of Submission.
15. Enter the Date and Time of Submission.
16. Optionally enter Comments of Submission.
17. If applicable, attach any relevant files or images for the Submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name for reference.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name for reference.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): A valid email address to facilitate communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Your contact phone number.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Select the department concerned with the issue from the dropdown list.
* **Concern** (`concern`, `text`, required): Describe the issue or concern.
* **Date of Concern** (`date_of_concern`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the issue.
* **Time of Concern** (`time_of_concern`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the issue.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the issue.
* **Description of Concern** (`description_of_concern`, `text`, required): Provide a clear description of the concern.
* **Attachment** (`attachment`, `text`, optional): Attach any relevant files or images to support your input.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Optionally enter any additional comments about the issue.
* **Name of Contact** (`name_of_contact`, `text`, required): If applicable, enter the name of the contact person.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Email of Contact** (`email_of_contact`, `email`, optional): If applicable, enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Department of Contact** (`department_of_contact`, `select_one`, required): Select the department of the contact person.
* **Date of Contact** (`date_of_contact`, `date`, required): Enter the date of contact.
* **Time of Contact** (`time_of_contact`, `time`, required): Enter the time of contact.
* **Name of Submission** (`name_of_submission`, `text`, required): Provide a name for the submission.
* **Email of Submission** (`email_of_submission`, `email`, required): A valid email address for the submission.
* **Department of Submission** (`department_of_submission`, `select_one`, required): Select the department of submission.
* **Date of Submission** (`date_of_submission`, `date`, required): Enter the date of submission.
* **Time of Submission** (`time_of_submission`, `time`, required): Enter the time of submission.
* **Comments of Submission** (`comments_of_submission`, `text`, optional): Optionally enter any comments for the submission.
* **Attachment of Submission** (`attachment_of_submission`, `text`, optional): If applicable, attach any relevant files or images for the submission.
