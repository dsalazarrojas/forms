# municipal_sludge_disposal_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The municipal_sludge_disposal_survey form is used to collect data on municipal sludge disposal practices, including types of methods, capacities, frequencies, and costs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Select the relevant options for each field, taking into account your municipality's sludge disposal practices.
3. Provide accurate and detailed information for each field.
4. Submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, optional): This field is a brief overview of your municipality's sludge disposal practices. You can provide any information or context that may be helpful for understanding your answers.
* **municipal_sludge_management_programs** (`2`, `select_one`, optional): This field asks you to select the type of sludge management program your municipality has in place. Please choose one of the available options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **sludge_disposal_practices** (`3`, `text`, optional): Describe the types of sludge disposal practices your municipality has in place, such as landfilling, incineration, or other methods.
* **sludge_disposal_capacity** (`4`, `number`, optional): Enter the total capacity of your sludge disposal facility, in units of your choice (e.g., cubic meters, tons, etc.).
* **sludge_disposal_frequency** (`5`, `number`, optional): Enter the frequency of sludge disposal activities, such as how often waste materials are collected, transported, or disposed of.
* **sludge_disposal_treatment** (`6`, `select_one`, optional): This field asks you to select the type of sludge treatment used in your municipality. Please choose one of the available options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **sludge_disposal_costs** (`7`, `text`, optional): Describe the costs associated with your sludge disposal practices, including any expenses related to equipment, labor, or other resources.
* **sludge_disposal_regulations** (`8`, `text`, optional): List any regulations or guidelines that govern your sludge disposal practices, such as permits, licenses, or other requirements.
* **submission** (`9`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field allows you to select which types of submissions you have made for your sludge disposal practices. Please choose one or more of the available options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3

Please ensure that you have all the necessary information before starting the form. If you are unsure about any field or option, please contact your municipal administration for guidance.
