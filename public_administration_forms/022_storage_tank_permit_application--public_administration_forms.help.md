# Storage Tank Permit Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for applicants to provide required information for a storage tank permit. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Facility Info (Optional)**: Enter a brief description of your facility, including its name and address. 
2. **Tank Type (Optional)**: Choose the type of tank that will be used for storage. 
3. **Tank Capacity (Optional)**: Enter the capacity of the tank in the desired units. 
4. **Tank Location (Optional)**: Enter the location where the tank will be stored. 
5. **Contact Info (Optional)**: Enter your name, email, and any other contact information for further communication. 
6. **Tank Inspection (Optional)**: Date of the tank inspection, if applicable. 
7. **Safety Certifications (Optional)**: Select any applicable safety certifications. 
8. **Permit Fee (Optional)**: Enter the permit fee for the tank. 
9. **Payment Info (Optional)**: Select the payment method used. 
10. **Submission Date (Optional)**: Date of submission of this application. 
11. **Review Status (Optional)**: Select the status of this application. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility Info (Facility Info)** (`facility_info`, `text`, Optional): Enter a brief description of your facility, including its name and address.
* **Tank Type (Tank Type)** (`tank_type`, `select_one`, Optional): Choose the type of tank that will be used for storage.
* **Tank Capacity (Tank Capacity)** (`tank_capacity`, `number`, Optional): Enter the capacity of the tank in the desired units.
* **Tank Location (Tank Location)** (`tank_location`, `text`, Optional): Enter the location where the tank will be stored.
* **Contact Info (Contact Info)** (`contact_info`, `email`, Optional): Enter your name, email, and any other contact information for further communication.
* **Tank Inspection (Tank Inspection)** (`tank_inspection`, `date`, Optional): Date of the tank inspection, if applicable.
* **Safety Certifications (Safety Certifications)** (`safety_certifications`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select any applicable safety certifications.
* **Permit Fee (Permit Fee)** (`permit_fee`, `number`, Optional): Enter the permit fee for the tank.
* **Payment Info (Payment Info)** (`payment_info`, `select_one`, Optional): Select the payment method used.
