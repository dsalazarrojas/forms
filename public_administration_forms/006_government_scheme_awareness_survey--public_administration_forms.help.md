This help guide is intended for end-users who need to fill out the Government Scheme Awareness Survey form. This form is designed to gather information on individuals' awareness and access to government schemes, as well as their overall satisfaction with these schemes. The form collects data on how individuals obtained information about the schemes, their access to these schemes, and their satisfaction levels. It also seeks to identify any barriers that may hinder access to these schemes.

The form is divided into multiple pages, each with a specific purpose, but no page is required, making it easy to fill out at the user's convenience. This guide will walk you through each page, explaining each field's purpose and how to complete it.

## Government Scheme Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to gather data on public awareness and access to government schemes, allowing for a better understanding of how effectively these schemes are disseminated. By completing this form, you will help us understand your experience and provide insights for future improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Government Scheme Awareness Survey, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to understand what information is requested.
2. Select the options that best fit your experience with government schemes.
3. Provide any additional comments or details where required.
4. Enter your email address and phone number (if desired) for contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Introduction

* **Introduction** (id: 1, label: Introduction, type: note): This page is an introduction to the survey, explaining its purpose and importance.
*   This page is not a required field, so feel free to skip it if you're ready to begin with the survey questions.

### Page 2: Awareness of Government Schemes

* **Awareness of Government Schemes** (id: 2, label: awareness_of_government_scheme, type: select_one): This question asks about your level of awareness of government schemes. Please select one of the options:
	+ Very aware: You know about all the government schemes and their details.
	+ Somewhat aware: You know about some government schemes, but not all of them.
	+ Not aware: You do not know much about government schemes.

### Page 3: How Obtained Scheme Info

* **How Obtained Scheme Info** (id: 3, label: how_obtained_scheme_info, type: select_one): This question asks about where you obtained information about government schemes. Please select one of the options:
	+ Social media: You learned about schemes through social media.
	+ TV: You learned about schemes through TV.
	+ Radio: You learned about schemes through radio.
	+ Friend: You learned about schemes from a friend or family member.
	+ Family: You learned about schemes from a family member.
	+ Other: You learned about schemes through other means. Please specify in the "Other (please specify)" field.

### Page 4: Source of Scheme Info

* **Source of Scheme Info** (id: 4, label: source_of_scheme_info, type: select_multiple): This question asks about the sources where you obtained information about government schemes. Please select all that apply:
	+ Social media: You learned about schemes through social media.
	+ TV: You learned about schemes through TV.
	+ Radio: You learned about schemes through radio.
	+ Friend: You learned about schemes from a friend or family member.
	+ Family: You learned about schemes from a family member.
	+ Other: You learned about schemes through other means. Please specify in the "Other (please specify)" field.

### Page 5: Access to Schemes

* **Access to Schemes** (id: 5, label: access_scheme, type: select_multiple): This question asks about your access to government schemes. Please select all that apply:
	+ Easy: You found it easy to access government schemes.
	+ Somewhat easy: You found it somewhat easy to access government schemes.
	+ Not easy: You found it difficult to access government schemes.
	+ Not possible: You could not access government schemes at all.

### Page 6: Satisfaction with Schemes

* **Satisfaction with Schemes** (id: 6, label: satisfaction_with_scheme, type: select_one): This question asks about your satisfaction level with government schemes. Please select one of the options:
	+ Very satisfied: You are very satisfied with government schemes.
	+ Somewhat satisfied: You are somewhat satisfied with government schemes.
	+ Neutral: You are neutral about government schemes.
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied: You are somewhat dissatisfied with government schemes.
	+ Very dissatisfied: You are very dissatisfied with government schemes.

### Page 7: Satisfaction with Outreach

* **Satisfaction with Outreach** (id: 7, label: satisfaction_with_outreach, type: select_one): This question asks about your satisfaction level with the outreach efforts of government schemes. Please select one of the options:
	+ Very satisfied: You are very satisfied with the outreach efforts.
	+ Somewhat satisfied: You are somewhat satisfied with the outreach efforts.
	+ Neutral: You are neutral about the outreach efforts.
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied: You are somewhat dissatisfied with the outreach efforts.
	+ Very dissatisfied: You are very dissatisfied with the outreach efforts.

### Page 8: Barriers to Access

* **Barriers to Access** (id: 8, label: barriers_to_access, type: select_multiple): This question asks about any barriers that prevented you from accessing government schemes. Please select all that apply:
	+ Language barrier: You had difficulty understanding the scheme due to language issues.
	+ Cost: You found the cost of accessing schemes too high.
	+ Lack of awareness: You were not aware of the scheme.
	+ Other: You encountered other barriers. Please specify in the "Other (please specify)" field.

### Page 9: Other Barriers

* **Other Barriers** (id: 9, label: other_barriers, type: select_multiple): This question asks about any other barriers that prevented you from accessing government schemes. Please select all that apply:
	+ Lack of interest: You did not have interest in the scheme.
	+ Lack of skills: You lacked the necessary skills to access the scheme.
	+ Other: You encountered other barriers. Please specify in the "Other (please specify)" field.

### Page 10: Suggestion for Improvement

* **Suggestions for Improvement** (id: 10, label: suggestions_for_improvement, type: text): This question allows you to provide any suggestions for improvement of government schemes. Enter your comments or feedback here.

### Page 11: Contact Information

* **Email** (id: 11, label: email, type: email, optional): This is an optional field for you to enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   Please enter a valid email address for us to get in touch with you.
*   If you do not want to provide your email address, feel free to skip this field.

* **Phone** (id: 12, label: phone, type: text, optional): This is an optional field for you to enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   Please enter a valid phone number.
*   If you do not want to provide your phone number, feel free to skip this field.

* **Date** (id: 13, label: date, type: date, optional): This is an optional field for you to enter the date for your feedback, if desired.
*   Please enter a valid date.
*   If you do not want to provide a date, feel free to skip this field.

## Tips
