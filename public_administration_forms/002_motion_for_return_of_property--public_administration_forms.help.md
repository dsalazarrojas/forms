# Motion For Return Of Property - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Motion For Return Of Property form is used to request the return of property that has been wrongly seized or seized.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the **Case Number** of the case related to the request for return of property. This is a unique identifier assigned to the case by the court or legal authority.
* Enter the **Date Return Received**, the date on which the property was returned to its rightful owner or the state.
* Enter the **Return Amount**, the amount of property that is being returned.
* Select the **Payment Method** by which the property was returned, such as Check, Wire Transfer, or Bank Draft.
* If necessary, provide **Supporting Documentation** that supports the return of property, such as receipts or invoices.
* Select the **Payment Status** of the return, such as Paid, Unpaid, or Partially Paid.
* Enter the **Reason** for the return of property, a brief description of why the property is being returned.
* If necessary, enter additional **Case Notes** or details related to the case.
* Enter any **Payment Notes** that may be relevant to the return.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Case Number** (1, number): Enter the unique identifier assigned to the case by the court or legal authority.
* **Date Return Received** (2, date): Enter the date on which the property was returned to its rightful owner or the state.
* **Return Amount** (3, number): Enter the amount of property that is being returned.
* **Payment Method** (4, select_one): Select how the property was returned, such as Check, Wire Transfer, or Bank Draft.
* **Supporting Documentation** (5, note): Provide any relevant receipts or invoices that support the return of property.
* **Payment Status** (6, select_one): Select the status of the payment for the return of property, such as Paid, Unpaid, or Partially Paid.
* **Reason** (7, text): Enter a brief description of why the property is being returned.
* **Case Status** (8, select_multiple): Select the current status of the case related to the return of property, such as Open, Closed, or Dismissed.
* **Case Notes** (9, note): Enter any additional details or notes related to the case.
* **Payment Notes** (10, note): Enter any relevant notes related to the payment for the return of property.
