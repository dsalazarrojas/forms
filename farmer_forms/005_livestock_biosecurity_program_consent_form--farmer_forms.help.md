# livestock_biosecurity_program_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The livestock_biosecurity_program_consent_form is a tool used by farmers to provide their consent for participation in a biosecurity program and to document their farm location and biosecurity measures in place.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the type of participant you are (Participant, Farmer, Other, or None).
2. Enter your farm name.
3. Enter your farm address.
4. Select the size of your farm (if applicable).
5. Enter your contact number (if applicable).
6. Enter your email (if applicable).
7. Select the biosecurity measures you have in place.
8. Enter the date and time you visited the farm.
9. Sign the biosecurity measures section.
10. Select the map that corresponds to your farm location.
11. Sign the form with your name and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **livestock_biosecurity_program_consent_form** (`title`, required/optional): This is the title of the form. It is not editable.
* **participant_information** (`participant_information`, required): Select the type of participant you are. This will determine what information is required for the other fields.
	+ Participant: Select this option if you are a participant in the program.
	+ Farmer: Select this option if you are a farmer participating in the program.
	+ Other: Select this option if you are neither a participant nor a farmer.
	+ None: Select this option if you do not wish to participate in the program.
* **farm_name** (`farm_name`, required): Enter the name of your farm.
* **address** (`address`, required): Enter your farm address.
* **farm_size** (`farm_size`, optional): Select the size of your farm (if applicable).
* **contact_number** (`contact_number`, optional): Enter your contact number (if applicable).

* **biosecurity_measures** (`biosecurity_measures`, required): Select the biosecurity measures you have in place.
* **date_visited** (`date_visited`, required): Enter the date you visited the farm.
* **time_visited** (`time_visited`, optional): Enter the time you visited the farm (if applicable).
* **biosecurity_measures_signature** (`biosecurity_measures_signature`, required): Sign this section to indicate you have biosecurity measures in place.
* **farm_map** (`farm_map`, required): Select the map that corresponds to your farm location.
