# Sustainable Farming Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Sustainable Farming Fellowship Application Form is designed to collect information from farmers and agricultural professionals to support the sustainable farming community. This form helps us understand your needs, interests, and requirements to provide you with relevant resources, training, and networking opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.
3. Submit the form to apply for the sustainable farming fellowship.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input**: (`user_input`, text, optional)
	+ Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Email**: (`contact_email`, email, optional)
	+ Enter your email address so we can contact you back.
* **Phone**: (`contact_phone`, text, optional)
	+ Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Farm Name**: (`farm_name`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the name of your farm.
* **Address Line 1**: (`address_line_1`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the first line of your address.
* **Address Line 2**: (`address_line_2`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the second line of your address.
* **City**: (`city`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the city where your farm is located.
* **State**: (`state`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the state where your farm is located.
* **ZIP**: (`zip`, number, optional)
	+ Enter the ZIP code where your farm is located.
* **Date of Birth**: (`date_of_birth`, date, optional)
	+ Enter your date of birth for age verification purposes.
* **Fellowship Programs**: (`fellowship_programs`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Select the types of fellowship programs you are interested in.
* **Years of Experience**: (`years_of_experience`, number, optional)
	+ Enter the number of years of experience you have in sustainable farming.
* **Farm Location**: (`farm_location`, select_one, optional)
	+ Select the location of your farm (Urban, Rural, Suburban).
* **Farm Size**: (`farm_size`, select_one, optional)
	+ Select the size of your farm (Small, Medium, Large).
* **Farm Type**: (`farm_type`, select_one, optional)
	+ Select the type of farm you manage (Livestock, Crop, Other).
* **Farm Description**: (`farm_description`, text, optional)
	+ Enter a brief description of your farm.
* **Farm Products**: (`farm_products`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Select the types of products your farm offers (Grains, Vegetables, Fruits).
* **Application Type**: (`application_type`, select_one, optional)
	+ Select how you would like to apply for the fellowship (Online, In-Person, Both).
* **Preferred Language**: (`preferred_language`, select_one, optional)
	+ Select your preferred language for communication.
* **Submitted By**: (`submitted_by`, select_one, optional)
	+ Select who is submitting the application (Self, Organization, Other).
* **Submitted For**: (`submitted_for`, select_one, optional)
	+ Select who the application is submitted for (Individual, Organization, Other).
* **Submitted To**: (`submitted_to`, select_one, optional)
	+ Select where the application is submitted (Self, Other).
* **Submitted Date**: (`submitted_date`, date, optional)
	+ Enter the date you submitted the application.
* **Notes**: (`notes`, text, optional)
	+ Enter any additional notes or comments about your application.
* **Review Notes**: (`review_notes`, text, optional)
	+ Enter any review notes or comments about your application.
