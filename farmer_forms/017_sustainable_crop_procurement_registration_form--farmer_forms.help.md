# Sustainable Crop Procurement Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sustainable Crop Procurement Registration Form is designed to collect information on sustainable crops from farmers. This form helps to gather data on the types of crops that are being procured, as well as the contact and address details of the farmers supplying them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Farmer Information" page to fill in the basic details about yourself as a farmer.
2. Move to the "Contact Information" page to provide your contact details.
3. On the "Address" page, enter your address.
4. In the "Sustainable Crops" page, select the types of crops you are procuring (e.g., "Yes" or "No" options).
5. The "Procurement Terms" page is for you to provide any additional information about your procurement terms.
6. In the "Notes" page, you can add any additional notes or comments about your sustainable crops.
7. Please fill in your email address in the "Email" field.
8. Finally, enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Farmer Information** (1, text, required): Enter your name as a farmer.
* **Contact Information** (2, text, required): Fill in your contact details, including your name, job title, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Address** (3, text, required): Enter your address to enable us to reach you.
* **Sustainable Crops** (4, select_multiple, required): Select the types of crops that you are procuring.
* **Procurement Terms** (5, text, required): Enter any additional information about your procurement terms.
* **Notes** (6, note, required): Add any additional notes or comments about your sustainable crops.
* **Email** (7, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (8, text, required): Fill in your phone number.
* **Assigned Tool** (9, text, required): Enter the name of the tool assigned to you.
