# Beef Rate Of Gain Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Beef Rate Of Gain Registration form is a tool used to collect data on beef cattle performance, specifically for recording growth rates and other relevant parameters. This form is designed for farmers and producers to track and monitor the health and progress of their cattle. By accurately recording information on animal weights, feed types, health status, and other relevant factors, producers can gain valuable insights to optimize their cattle farming practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the Producer Name, Email Address, and Phone Number to identify yourself as the producer.
2. Provide the Farm Address to indicate where the cattle are raised.
3. Enter the Registration Date to record the date this registration was made.
4. Select the Program Type to categorize the cattle under a specific program (e.g., Youth Program, Commercial Production, Research Study, etc.).
5. Enter the Animal ID or Tag Number to uniquely identify the animal.
6. Enter the Animal Name if applicable.
7. Select the Breed to record the primary breed of the cattle.
8. Select the Sex of the animal.
9. Enter the Birth Date and Initial Weight to record the animal's birth and initial weight.
10. Enter the Initial Weight Date to record the date the initial weight was taken.
11. Enter the Current Weight and Current Weight Date to record the animal's current weight.
12. Select the Feed Type to record the primary feed type used for the animal.
13. Enter the Daily Feed Amount if applicable.
14. Select the Supplement Type if any supplements are used.
15. Select the Water Source used for the animal.
16. Record the Health Status of the animal.
17. Select if vaccinations are up to date for the animal.
18. Enter the Target Weight and Target Date if applicable.
19. Enter the Average Daily Gain if known.
20. Provide Additional Notes for any other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Producer Name**: Enter your full name or operation name as the producer.
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your best contact number.
* **Farm Address**: Enter the full address of your farm or operation.
* **Registration Date**: Enter the date this registration was made.
* **Program Type**: Select the program type under which this animal is registered (e.g., Youth Program, Commercial Production, etc.).
* **Animal ID or Tag Number**: Enter a unique identifier for the animal.
* **Animal Name**: If applicable, enter the name of the animal.
* **Breed**: Select the primary breed of the cattle.
* **Sex**: Select the sex of the animal.
* **Birth Date**: Enter the date the animal was born.
* **Initial Weight**: Enter the starting weight of the animal in pounds.
* **Initial Weight Date**: Enter the date the initial weight was taken.
* **Current Weight**: Enter the current weight of the animal in pounds.
* **Current Weight Date**: Enter the date the current weight was taken.
* **Feed Type**: Select the primary feed type used for the animal.
* **Daily Feed Amount**: Enter the average daily feed amount in pounds if applicable.
* **Supplement Type**: Enter the type of supplement used if any.
* **Water Source**: Select the primary water source used for the animal.
* **Health Status**: Select the current health status of the animal.
* **Vaccinations Current**: Select if vaccinations are up to date for the animal.
* **Target Weight**: Enter the goal weight in pounds if applicable.
* **Target Date**: Enter the expected date to reach the target weight if applicable.
* **Average Daily Gain**: Enter the calculated average daily gain if known.
* **Additional Notes**: Provide any other relevant information about the animal.
