# Poultry Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Poultry Entry Form is used for recording the entry of poultry into the system. This form is used for both buying and selling of poultry, allowing farmers to track their poultry inventory and sales data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of poultry you are buying or selling from the options provided for "Poultry Type".
2. Enter the weight of the poultry you are buying or selling in the "Poultry Weight" field.
3. Enter the price of the poultry you are buying or selling in the "Poultry Price" field.
4. Enter the selling price of the poultry in the "Selling Price" field, if applicable.
5. Enter the quantity of poultry you are buying or selling in the "Quantity" field.
6. Select the date and time the poultry was sold in the "Date Sold" and "Time Sold" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Selling Poultry
* **Selling Poultry** (`selling_poultry`, text, required): Enter the description of the poultry you are selling.

### Poultry Type
* **Poultry Type** (`poultry_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of poultry, such as Broiler, Layer, Breeder, Turkey, or Duck.

### Poultry Weight
* **Poultry Weight** (`poultry_weight`, number, required): Enter the weight of the poultry.

### Poultry Price
* **Poultry Price** (`poultry_price`, number, required): Enter the price of the poultry.

### Selling Price
* **Selling Price** (`selling_price`, number, required): Enter the selling price of the poultry.

### Quantity
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the quantity of poultry.

### Date Sold
* **Date Sold** (`date_sold`, date, required): Select the date the poultry was sold.

### Time Sold
* **Time Sold** (`time_sold`, time, required): Select the time the poultry was sold.

## Tips
* Be accurate when filling out the form to ensure correct tracking of your poultry inventory and sales data.
* Make sure to select the correct type of poultry to ensure accurate categorization.
