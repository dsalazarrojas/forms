# Rotational Grazing Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and data from farmers about their rotational grazing experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
Please answer the questions on this form to help us better understand your rotational grazing practices, challenges, and level of satisfaction. Make sure to complete all required fields, and provide as much detail as you can in the optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Rotational Grazing Feedback Survey Form**: This is the title of the form, which will be displayed at the top of the page.
- **What are your primary goals for rotational grazing?**: Please select all the goals that apply to your rotational grazing practices. This will help us understand your objectives.
  * Daily
  * Reduced soil erosion
  * Improved water quality
  * Increased crop yields
  * Other (please specify)
- **How often do you currently rotational graze?**: This question helps us understand your rotational grazing schedule.
  * Daily
  * Weekly
  * Monthly
  * Rarely
- **How many hours per day do you rotational graze?**: Please enter the number of hours you spend rotational grazing per day.
- **What method do you use for rotational grazing?**: This question helps us understand your rotational grazing practices.
  * No-till
  * Rotational grazing
  * Other
- **What benefits do you get from rotational grazing?**: Please select all the benefits that apply to your rotational grazing practices.
  * Improved soil structure
  * Increased crop yields
  * Better nutrient cycling
  * Improved water quality
  * Other (please specify)
- **What challenges do you face with rotational grazing?**: This question helps us understand your difficulties with rotational grazing.
  * Soil erosion
  * Weed pressure
  * Pest and disease pressure
  * Other (please specify)
- **How satisfied are you with your rotational grazing experience?**: This question will help us understand your level of satisfaction with rotational grazing.
  * Very satisfied
  * Somewhat satisfied
  * Neutral
  * Somewhat dissatisfied
  * Very dissatisfied
- **Additional comments**: If you have any additional thoughts or suggestions, please share them in this text field.
- **Farmer Email**: If you would like to be contacted by us, please enter your email address.
- **Phone Number**: If you would like to be contacted by us, please enter your phone number.

Remember to complete all required fields to ensure your feedback is included in our analysis. Thank you for taking the time to share your experiences and opinions about rotational grazing!
