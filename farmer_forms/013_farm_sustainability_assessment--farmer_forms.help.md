# Farm Sustainability Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help assess a farm's current sustainability practices and provide guidance on areas for improvement. It will ask you to provide information on your farm's current practices, progress, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each section carefully and answer all questions thoroughly.
2. For each question, select the most accurate response based on your farm's current status.
3. If you are unsure about any question, contact the assigned tool or user for guidance.
4. Complete all required fields to ensure accurate and complete assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a brief overview of the form's purpose and instructions.
* **Farm Information**: This field is for providing basic information about your farm, such as farm name and contact details.
* **Farm Practices**: Select all the sustainable practices that your farm is currently using or has plans to implement in the future. Examples include:
	+ Sustainable practices (e.g., reducing waste, using renewable energy)
	+ Organic practices (e.g., using organic fertilizers, no pesticides)
	+ Integrated pest management (e.g., using natural pest control methods)
	+ Crop rotation (e.g., rotating crops to maintain soil health)
	+ Cover crops (e.g., planting cover crops to reduce soil erosion)
* **Challenges**: Indicate if your farm is currently facing any challenges in implementing sustainable practices.
* **Progress**: Provide the date of your last progress evaluation or a specific date to mark your progress.
* **Farm Name**: Enter the name of your farm or operation.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person to contact for further questions or guidance.
* **Contact Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the person to contact for further questions or guidance.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the communication method you would like to be assigned to (e.g., Chat, Video Call, Phone Call, Meeting).
* **Assigned User**: Indicate if a specific user has been assigned to you or not.
* **Assigned User (Yes/No)**: If yes, provide the name of the assigned user.
