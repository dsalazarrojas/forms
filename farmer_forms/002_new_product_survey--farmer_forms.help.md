# New Product Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "New Product Survey" form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with a new product. This form is used to understand customer demographics, product satisfaction, and overall feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Make sure to fill out all applicable fields, as indicated by the required fields marked with an asterisk (*) or an asterisk enclosed in a box.
3. Use the provided options for select fields, where applicable.

* **Title** (`title`, text, *): This is the title of the survey.
* **Customer Demographics** (`customer_demographics`, text, *): Please provide your name, age, and contact information so we can get in touch with you.
* **Age** (`age`, number, *): Please enter your age.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, *): Please provide your email address or phone number so we can contact you about your feedback.
