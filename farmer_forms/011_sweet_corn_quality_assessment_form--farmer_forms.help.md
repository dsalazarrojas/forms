# Sweet Corn Quality Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sweet Corn Quality Assessment Form is used to collect data on the quality attributes of sweet corn, allowing farmers to assess and compare the quality of their crops. This form is a crucial tool for farmers to track and analyze the yield, moisture level, color, and sweetness level of their sweet corn.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the amount of sweet corn harvested as the Corn Yield on **Page 1**.
2. Select the moisture level of the sweet corn as **Moisture Level** on **Page 1**.
3. Choose the color of the sweet corn as **Corn Color** on **Page 1** (optional).
4. Select all that apply for the sweetness level of the sweet corn as **Sweetness Level** on **Page 2** (required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Corn Yield (Number)**: This field is used to record the total amount of sweet corn harvested. Please enter the exact amount in a numerical format.
* **Moisture Level (Number)**: Select the level of moisture in your sweet corn, with options ranging from Low to High.
* **Corn Color (Select One)**: Choose one of the three options: Green, Yellow, Blue. Note that this field is optional, so feel free to leave it blank if not applicable.
* **Sweetness Level (Select Multiple)**: Select all that apply for the sweetness level of your sweet corn, with options ranging from Low to High. This field is required to ensure accuracy and thoroughness in your assessment.
