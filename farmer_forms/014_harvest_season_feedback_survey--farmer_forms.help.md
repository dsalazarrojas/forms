# harvest_season_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from farmers about their experiences during the upcoming harvest season. This feedback will be invaluable in informing decisions to improve agricultural planning and resource management.

## How To Complete This Form
Please go through each page of the form, and for each field, follow these steps:

1. **Introduction**:
This is a page to introduce yourself and the farm you're submitting feedback for.
2. **Farm Name**:
Enter the name of the farm where the harvest season took place.
3. **Farm Location**:
Enter the location of the farm.
4. **Season Length**:
Enter the length of the harvest season.
5. **Crop Type**:
Enter the type of crops grown during the season.
6. **Crop Variety**:
Enter the specific variety of crops grown during the season.
7. **Yield**:
Enter the yield of the crops during the season.
8. **Harvest Planning**:
Share your experiences with harvest planning and any challenges you faced.
9. **Team Experience**:
Indicate if the team had any experience or difficulties with harvest planning and execution.
10. **Next Year Planning**:
Share any suggestions or insights on how to improve next year's harvest planning.
11. **Comments**:
Add any additional comments or observations regarding the harvest season.
12. **Email**:
Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
13. **Phone**:
Enter your phone number for follow-up communication.
14. **Assigned User**:
Indicate if the assigned user was involved in the harvest planning and execution process.
