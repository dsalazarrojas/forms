# Crop Yield Data Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Crop Yield Data Audit Form" is designed to collect information about your farm and crop details, helping us to better understand and improve crop yield data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the "Farm Details" field with your farm's name and any relevant contact information.
2. In the "Field Details" field, provide specific information about the field where the crop was grown, including the field's name and location.
3. For "Crop Details", describe the crop type, variety, and any other relevant details about the crop.
4. In the "Notes" field, include any additional comments or observations about your farm and crop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Farm Details (1)**: Enter your farm's name and any relevant contact information, such as phone number and email address.
* **Field Details (2)**: Describe the field where the crop was grown, including its name and location.
* **Crop Details (3)**: Provide specific details about the crop, including type and variety.
* **Notes (4)**: Add any additional comments or observations about your farm and crop.
