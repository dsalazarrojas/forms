# frost_protection_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The frost_protection_application_form is designed to help farmers request frost protection services for their crops. It's a form that collects contact information, farm details, and other relevant data to ensure that the farmer receives the necessary support during frost events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user information, including your name and any other relevant details.
2. Provide your contact information, such as phone number and email.
3. Enter your farm's name and location.
4. Select the type(s) of crops you are growing.
5. Choose the method(s) you would like to use for frost protection.
6. If you have any additional information or specific requests, please include them in the "Additional Info" field.
7. Finally, confirm the name and contact method of the person or organization assigned to help you with frost protection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (User Information, text, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant personal details.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, required: false): This is where you will enter your phone number and email address.
* **Contact Method** (Contact Method, select_one, required: false): Choose how you would like to be contacted: Phone, Email, or In Person.
* **Contact Number** (Contact Number, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Farm Name** (Farm Name, text, required: false): Enter the name of your farm.
* **Farm Location** (Farm Location, text, required: false): Enter the location of your farm.
* **Crop Type** (Crop Type, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type(s) of crops you are growing (e.g., Corn, Soybeans, Wheat, Alfalfa, Other).
* **Acreage** (Acreage, number, required: false): Enter the number of acres you are farming.
* **Frost Protection Methods** (Frost Protection Methods, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the method(s) you would like to use for frost protection (e.g., Sprinklers, Wind machines, Wind machines with heating, Other).
* **Additional Info** (Additional Info, text, required: false): If you have any additional information or specific requests, please include them here.
* **Assigned Tool** (Assigned Tool, text, required: false): Enter the name of the tool assigned to help with your frost protection.
* **Assigned User** (Assigned User, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person or organization assigned to assist you with frost protection.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (if any).
* Double-check that you have selected the correct crop type and frost protection methods.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the person or organization assigned to help you.
