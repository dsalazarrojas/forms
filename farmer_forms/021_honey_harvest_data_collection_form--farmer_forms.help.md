# Honey Harvest Data Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Honey Harvest Data Collection Form is designed to collect data from farmers about their honey harvests. This form is intended for internal use within the organization and helps to track and record important information about the harvest season.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields and make sure you understand what information is being requested.
2. Fill in the relevant fields with the required information.
3. Make sure to complete all the required fields, such as "Farm ID", "Date", and "Time".
4. You can leave the optional fields blank or fill them in if you feel they are relevant to your harvest.
5. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Farm Details** (`farm_details`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about your farm, such as its name, location, or type.
* **Honey Details** (`honey_details`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of honey you harvested (Clover, Wild, or Other).
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the harvest.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the harvest.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of day of the harvest.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the farm or where the harvest took place.
* **Farm ID** (`farm_id`, number, required): Enter your farm ID number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Confirm your submission before submitting the form.
