# Crop Storage Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your crop storage management practices, capacity, and facility details. This will help us better understand your experiences and challenges in managing crop storage on your farm.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all questions honestly and accurately.
2. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose the correct answer from the options provided.
3. For text fields, provide a clear and concise description of your responses.
4. You can skip any questions that are not relevant to your farm or storage practices.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Storage Practices** (Storage Practices): This field is for you to describe your current storage practices, such as how you store your crops.
* **Storage Capacity** (Storage Capacity): Please enter the total storage capacity of your facility in units (e.g., tons, cubic meters).
* **Storage Challenges** (Storage Challenges): Describe any challenges you face in managing your storage, such as space constraints or pest infestations.
* **Storage Facility** (Storage Facility): Select 'Yes' if you have a storage facility on your farm, 'No' otherwise.
* **Storage Team** (Storage Team): Select 'Yes' if you have a dedicated storage team on your farm, 'No' otherwise.
* **Additional Storage Practices** (Additional Storage Practices): Describe any additional storage practices you use beyond the standard practices.
* **Additional Storage Challenges** (Additional Storage Challenges): Describe any additional challenges you face in managing your storage.
* **Location** (Location): Select 'Yes' if your facility is located on your farm, 'No' otherwise.
* **Facility Operators** (Facility Operators): Select 'Yes' if you have facility operators on your farm, 'No' otherwise.
* **Facility Managers** (Facility Managers): Select 'Yes' if you have facility managers on your farm, 'No' otherwise.
* **Other Storage Practices** (Other Storage Practices): Describe any other storage practices you use beyond the standard practices.
* **Other Storage Challenges** (Other Storage Challenges): Describe any other challenges you face in managing your storage.
* **Facility Location** (Facility Location): Select 'Yes' if your facility is located on your farm, 'No' otherwise.
* **Facility Name** (Facility Name): Enter the name of your storage facility.
* **Facility Address** (Facility Address): Enter the address of your storage facility.
* **Facility Operators** (Facility Operators): Select 'Yes' if you have facility operators on your farm, 'No' otherwise.
* **Facility Managers** (Facility Managers): Select 'Yes' if you have facility managers on your farm, 'No' otherwise.
* **Facility Employees** (Facility Employees): Select 'Yes' if you have facility employees on your farm, 'No' otherwise.
* **Facility Phone** (Facility Phone): Enter the phone number of your storage facility.
* **Facility Email** (Facility Email): Enter the email address of your storage facility.
* **Facility Hours** (Facility Hours): Enter the working hours of your storage facility.
* **Facility Equipment** (Facility Equipment): Enter any equipment you use in your storage facility.
* **Facility Location** (Facility Location): Select 'Yes' if your facility is located in a nearby location, 'No' otherwise.
