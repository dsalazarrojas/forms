# Milk_Distribution_Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Milk Distribution Tracker is a form used for monitoring and recording milk distribution activities. It's essential for tracking milk delivery dates, quantities, and production methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the milk delivery date, which is the date when milk was delivered to the recipient.
2. Enter the milk quantity that was delivered, including the unit of measurement (e.g., liters or gallons).
3. Select the milk receiving location from the options provided.
4. Enter the milk production date, which is the date when milk was produced.
5. Choose the milk production method from the available options (e.g., Cow, Pig, Goat, or Sheep).
6. Enter the contact information of the milk shipper, including their name and address.
7. Enter the name and contact information of the person or company that received the milk.
8. Add any additional notes or comments about the milk shipment, if necessary.
9. Enter any notes or comments from the milk collector, if applicable.
10. Enter the contact information of the person or company that received the milk, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Milk Delivery Date**: The date when milk was delivered to the recipient.
	+ Type: Date
	+ Required: False
* **Milk Quantity**: The amount of milk delivered, including the unit of measurement (e.g., liters or gallons).
	+ Type: Number
	+ Required: False
* **Milk Receiving Location**: The location where milk was received.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False
* **Milk Production Date**: The date when milk was produced.
	+ Type: Date
	+ Required: False
* **Milk Production Method**: The method used to produce milk (e.g., Cow, Pig, Goat, or Sheep).
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Required: False
* **Milk Shipper Details**: Additional information about the milk shipper, if needed.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False
* **Milk Shipper Contact**: Contact information of the milk shipper.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False
* **Milk Shipped To**: The name and contact information of the person or company that received the milk.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False
* **Milk Shipper Address**: The address of the milk shipper.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False
* **Milk Production Method**: The method used to produce milk (e.g., Cow, Pig, Goat, or Sheep).
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Required: False
* **Milk Collection Date**: The date when milk was collected.
	+ Type: Date
	+ Required: False
* **Milk Collected By**: Additional information about the milk collector.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False
* **Milk Shipper Notes**: Additional notes or comments about the milk shipment.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False
* **Milk Collector Notes**: Additional notes or comments from the milk collector.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False
* **Milk Shipped To Contact**: The contact information of the person or company that received the milk.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False
* **Milk Shipped To Company**: The name of the company that received the milk.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled in accurately to maintain proper tracking.
* Review and update this form regularly to reflect the latest milk distribution data.
* If any issues arise during milk distribution, use this form to document them for future reference.
* If you're unsure about any field, please consult with the milk distribution team or refer to the milk distribution guidelines.
