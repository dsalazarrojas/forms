## Farm Volunteer Worker Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Farm Volunteer Worker Application Form is a tool used to collect information from individuals interested in volunteering on the farm. This form is intended for those who would like to contribute their time and skills to help with farm-related tasks and activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the `first_name` field.
2. Enter your last name in the `last_name` field.
3. Provide a valid email address in the `email` field (we will use this to communicate with you).
4. Enter your phone number in the `phone` field (we will use this to contact you, if needed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your given name (e.g., John).
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your surname (e.g., Smith).
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address where we can reach you (e.g., `john.smith@example.com`).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number (e.g., 1-202-345-6789).

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
- If you are unable to complete this form, please reach out to us directly.
- We will review and respond to your application shortly after submission.
