# Livestock Sanitation Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Livestock Sanitation Support Request Form is used to report issues concerning livestock sanitation, waste management, or disease outbreaks on farms. This form helps our support team quickly understand the problem and provide assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in a new tab or window.
2. Enter your farm's address in the "farm_address" field.
3. Provide your farm's name in the "farm_name" field.
4. Select the location where the issue occurred in the "location" field.
5. Enter the date the issue was reported in the "date_reported" field.
6. Describe the issue in detail in the "issue_description" field.
7. Select the types of animals affected by the issue in the "photos" and "animal_type" fields.
8. Indicate the urgency level of the issue in the "urgency" field.
9. If an inspection tool has been assigned to the issue, select the correct tool in the "assigned_tool" field.
10. Click "Submit" to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **farm_address** (text, optional)
Enter your farm's address where the issue occurred.

### 2. **farm_name** (text, optional)
Enter your farm's name.

### 3. **location** (text, optional)
Select the location where the issue occurred.

### 4. **date_reported** (date, optional)
Enter the date the issue was reported.

### 5. **issue_description** (text, optional)
Provide a detailed description of the issue.

### 6. **photos** (select multiple, optional)
Select the types of animals affected by the issue. Options include:
* Cattle, Pigs, Goats, Sheep, and Horses
* Sheep, Goats, and Horses
* Cattle and Pigs
* Cattle
* Pigs
* Goats
* Sheep
* Horses

### 7. **urgency** (select one, optional)
Indicate the urgency level of the issue:
* High
* Medium
* Low

### 8. **animal_type** (select multiple, optional)
Select the types of animals affected by the issue. Options include:
* Beef Cattle, Pigs, Goats, Sheep, and Horses
* Goats, Sheep, and Horses
* Pigs and Sheep
* Goats
* Sheep
* Horses
* Pigs
* Cattle
* Beef and Pigs
* Sheep and Horses
* Beef Cattle

### 9. **assigned_tool** (select one, optional)
If an inspection tool has been assigned to the issue, select the correct tool:
* chatjimmy
* chatjane
* chatjohn

### 10. **Submit** (button)
Click to send the form with the collected information.
