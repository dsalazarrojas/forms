# farmer_job_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The farmer_job_application_form is a form designed to gather information from potential job applicants for farm-related positions. It is intended to be used by farmers, farmhands, and other agricultural professionals. The form collects data on personal and job-related information, including farm name, contact details, job title, work experience, job start date, hours worked, job type, work location, job status, farm equipment, work schedule, job duties, job hours, job salary, benefits, emergency contact, contact number, work phone, work email, personal email, work address, farm address, job type 2, and job notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the farm name in the designated field.
2. Provide your name, email, and any other required or optional information as specified.
3. Select the relevant job title from the options provided.
4. Enter your work experience in years.
5. Enter the job start date in the required format (mm/dd/yyyy).
6. Enter the number of hours you are willing to work.
7. Select the type of job you are applying for (Seasonal or Permanent).
8. Choose the locations where you are willing to work (Barn, Farm, Greenhouse).
9. Select your job status (Available or Unavailable).
10. Select the farm equipment you are familiar with (Tractor, Plow, Sprayer).
11. Select the days you are available to work (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday).
12. If applicable, enter any job duties you are responsible for.
13. Enter your job hours in the required format (hh:mm AM/PM).
14. If applicable, enter your job salary.
15. Select the benefits you are eligible for (Health Insurance, Paid Vacation, Retirement Plan).
16. Provide emergency contact information.
17. Enter your contact number.
18. Enter your work phone number.
19. Enter your work email.
20. Enter your personal email.
21. Enter your work address.
22. Enter your farm address.
23. Select your job type (Part-time or Full-time).
24. Enter any additional notes about the job.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Farm Name** (`farm_name`, text, required): The name of the farm you are applying for.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Your email address.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, select_one, required): The title of the job you are applying for (Farmhand, Farmer, Rancher).
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, number, required): The number of years you have worked in your field.
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, date, required): The date you would start working on the job.
* **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, number, required): The number of hours you are willing to work per week.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, required): The type of job you are applying for (Seasonal or Permanent).
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, select_multiple, required): The locations where you are willing to work (Barn, Farm, Greenhouse).
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_multiple, required): Your current job status (Available or Unavailable).
* **Farm Equipment** (`farm_equipment`, select_multiple, optional): The farm equipment you are familiar with (Tractor, Plow, Sprayer).
* **Work Schedule** (`work_schedule`, select_multiple, optional): The days you are available to work (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday).
* **Job Duties** (`job_duties`, text, optional): Any job duties you are responsible for.
* **Job Hours** (`job_hours`, time, optional): Your job hours in the required format.
* **Job Salary** (`job_salary`, number, optional): Your expected job salary.
* **Benefits** (`benefits`, select_multiple, optional): The benefits you are eligible for (Health Insurance, Paid Vacation, Retirement Plan).
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Emergency contact information.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Your contact number.
* **Work Phone** (`work_phone`, text, optional): Your work phone number.
* **Work Email** (`work_email`, email, optional): Your work email address.
* **Personal Email** (`personal_email`, email, optional): Your personal email address.
* **Work Address** (`work_address`, text, optional): Your work address.
* **Farm Address** (`farm_address`, text, optional): The farm address you work at.
* **Job Type 2** (`job_type_2`, select_one, optional): The type of job you are applying for (Part-time or Full-time).
* **Notes** (`note`, text, optional): Any additional notes about the job.
