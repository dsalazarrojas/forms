## Purpose
The Irrigation Audit Form is used to assess the status of irrigation systems on a farm. It is meant to be completed by farmers or their representatives to provide an overview of the current condition and performance of their irrigation systems. This form is used to identify areas for improvement and optimization of water usage.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Determine the status of each type of irrigation equipment, including whether it has been checked for functionality and performance. For each equipment type, select "Yes" if it has been checked, and "No" otherwise.
2. Answer "Yes" or "No" for each of the other questions regarding water flow, pump performance, and pipe leakages.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Is irrigation equipment checked (`irrigation_equipment_checked`)** (`select_one`, required: false)
	+ This field asks if irrigation equipment, such as pipes, pumps, and irrigation systems, have been inspected and checked for proper functionality.
* **Is water flow checked (`water_flow_checked`)** (`select_one`, required: false)
	+ This field asks if the flow of water through the irrigation system has been measured or assessed for proper flow rates.
* **Is pump performance checked (`pump_performance_checked`)** (`select_one`, required: false)
	+ This field asks if the performance of the pump, which supplies water to the irrigation system, has been checked for proper functionality and efficiency.
* **Are pipe leakages checked (`pipe_leakages_checked`)** (`select_one`, required: false)
	+ This field asks if any leakages or issues with the pipes in the irrigation system have been identified and addressed.
* **No further fields to complete.**

## Tips

* Make sure to answer "Yes" only if you have actually checked or measured the relevant equipment or system component.
* If you are unsure about the status of any of these systems, it's best to select "No" and provide additional comments in the form comments section (not shown here).
* This form is meant to be used as a guide to help you optimize your irrigation system, so please answer accurately and thoroughly.
