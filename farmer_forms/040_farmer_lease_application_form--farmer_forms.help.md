# Farmer Lease Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from farmers for the purpose of evaluating their application for lease. The form will be reviewed by the land management team to determine the eligibility of the farmer for the lease.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. For fields that are not required, provide information if it is relevant and useful for the evaluation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **lease_application_form**
* **lease_application_form** (`text`, required: false): This is the main text field where you can provide a brief description of your farm or lease application. 

### 2. **name**
* **name** (`text`, required: false): This field is for your name as a farmer.

### 3. **email**
* **email** (`email`, required: false): Provide your email address where you can be reached for communication purposes.

### 4. **phone**
* **phone** (`text`, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for further information.

### 5. **select_one**
* **select_one** (`select_one`, required: false): Choose either 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you have a valid driver's license.

### 6. **select_multiple**
* **select_multiple** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more options that apply to you, such as whether you have a farm experience or a farm equipment.

### 7. **date**
* **date** (`date`, required: false): Provide a valid date for any relevant farm-related events or applications.

### 8. **time**
* **time** (`time`, required: false): Enter a valid time for any relevant farm-related events or applications.

### 9. **note**
* **note** (`note`, required: false): This is an optional text field for any additional comments or notes about your farm or application.

### 10. **assigned_tool**
* **assigned_tool** (`text`, required: false): If you have any special farm tools assigned to you, enter them here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields with accurate and complete information.
* Be as detailed as possible with any additional information that might be relevant to your application.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the land management team for assistance.
