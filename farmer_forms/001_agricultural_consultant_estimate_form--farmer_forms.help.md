# Agricultural Consultant Estimate Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used by agricultural consultants to provide an estimate of their services for clients. It captures details about the consultant, client, farm, and services offered to ensure a clear and accurate estimate of the work involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and the name of your consulting company.
2. Provide the name of the client and the address or location of the farm.
3. Specify the date of the consultation visit.
4. Enter the total acreage of the farm.
5. Select the main crop being consulted on.
6. Choose all the services required.
7. Describe the current farm challenges you are facing.
8. (Optional) Enter the estimated hours of work and the hourly rate.
9. Calculate the total estimated cost for the services.
10. Select the agreed payment terms.
11. (Optional) Enter any additional notes or special conditions.
12. Sign the form digitally as the consultant and the client to confirm agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consultant Name** (Text): Full name of the agricultural consultant.
* **Consulting Company** (Text): Name of your consulting firm or organization.
* **Client Name** (Text): Farm owner or client name.
* **Farm Location** (Text): Address or location of the farm.
* **Consultation Date** (Date): Date of the consultation visit.
* **Farm Size (Acres)** (Number): Total acreage of the farm.
* **Primary Crop Type** (Select One): Main crop being consulted on.
*   - Corn
*   - Wheat
*   - Soybeans
*   - Rice
*   - Cotton
*   - Vegetables
*   - Fruits
*   - Other
* **Services Required** (Select Multiple): Select all consulting services needed.
*   - Soil Analysis
*   - Crop Planning
*   - Pest Management
*   - Irrigation Consulting
*   - Organic Certification
*   - Financial Planning
*   - Equipment Recommendations
*   - Market Analysis
* **Current Farm Challenges** (Text): Describe the main challenges you are facing.
* **Estimated Hours Required** (Number): Expected hours of consulting work. (Optional)
* **Hourly Rate (USD)** (Number): Consultant hourly rate. (Optional)
* **Total Estimate (USD)** (Number): Total estimated cost for services.
* **Payment Terms** (Select One): Agreed payment terms.
*   - Due on receipt
*   - Net 15
*   - Net 30
*   - 50% upfront, 50% on completion
*   - Custom terms
* **Additional Notes** (Text): Any additional information or special conditions. (Optional)
* **Consultant Signature** (Text): Digital signature of consultant.
* **Client Signature** (Text): Digital signature of client.
* **Estimate Valid Until** (Date): Date until which this estimate is valid.
