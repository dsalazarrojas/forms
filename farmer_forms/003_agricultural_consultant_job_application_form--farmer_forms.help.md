# Agricultural Consultant Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agricultural Consultant Job Application Form is designed to gather information from applicants seeking to work as agricultural consultants. This form helps farmers or organizations identify suitable candidates for the position of agricultural consultant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your full name and email address.
2. Provide your phone number (including country code if applicable).
3. Enter your current address (city, state, and country).
4. Select the position you are applying for (field consultant, agronomy advisor, research specialist, or project manager).
5. Enter your years of experience in agricultural consulting or related work.
6. List your relevant qualifications and certifications.
7. Enter your availability start date.
8. Provide professional references (name and contact information for up to two references).
9. Write a brief cover letter explaining why you are suitable for the role.
10. Check the consent box for background checks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Full Name** (Text): Enter your full name in the format of "John Doe".
* **Email Address** (Email): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you regarding your application.
* **Phone Number** (Text): Enter your phone number with country code (e.g., +1 202 123 4567).
* **Current Address** (Text): Enter your current city, state, and country (e.g., New York, USA).
* **Position Applied For** (Select One): Choose from the following options:
	+ Field consultant
	+ Agronomy advisor
	+ Research specialist
	+ Project manager
* **Years of Relevant Experience** (Number): Enter the total years of experience you have in agricultural consulting or related work.
* **Qualifications and Certifications** (Text): List any relevant degrees, licenses, or certifications you hold.
* **Availability Start Date** (Date): Enter the date when you can start work.
* **Professional References** (Text): Provide name and contact information for up to two professional references.
* **Consent for Background Check** (Note): Check this box to give consent for reference and background checks.
* **Cover Letter** (Text): Write a brief statement explaining why you are suitable for the position.
