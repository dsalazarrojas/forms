# Onion Shed Management Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about onion shed management practices, including the type of operation, shed location, capacity, and management conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of operation your farm does from the drop-down menu.
2. Enter the location details, including the shed's name and coordinates if necessary.
3. Input the shed's capacity, total area, and available area.
4. Choose whether the shed has temperature, light, humidity, and water supply control.
5. Determine if the shed has electrical power, lighting, ventilation, and ice packaging facilities.
6. Specify any other shed services or services provided.
7. Enter any notes about the shed.
8. Indicate if the shed uses pesticides and has fire suppression system.
9. Enter the total value and cost price of the shed.
10. Determine if the shed has insurance and specify its cost and deductible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the type of onion shed operation your farm does?** (`onion_shed_management_survey`): This field asks you to select the type of operation your farm does, such as onion farm, packing, storage, or other.
* **Shed Location** (`onion_shed_location_id`): This is the id of the shed's location. You can enter the id number if you have it.
* **Location Name** (`shed_location_name`): This is the name of the shed's location, e.g., farm name or address.
* **Shed Capacity** (`shed_capacity`): Enter the capacity of the shed in units, e.g., number of crates or boxes.
* **Total Shed Area** (`total_shed_area`): Enter the total area of the shed in square feet or meters.
* **Available Shed Area** (`available_shed_area`): Enter the available area of the shed, i.e., the area that is currently not occupied.
* **Shed Temperature Control** (`shed_temperature_control`): Choose 'True' if the shed has temperature control facilities and 'False' otherwise.
* **Shed Light Control** (`shed_light_control`): Choose 'True' if the shed has light control facilities and 'False' otherwise.
* **Shed Humidity Control** (`shed_humidity_control`): Choose 'True' if the shed has humidity control facilities and 'False' otherwise.
* **Shed Water Supply** (`shed_water_supply`): Choose 'True' if the shed has water supply facilities and 'False' otherwise.
* **Shed Electrical Power** (`shed_electrical_power`): Choose 'True' if the shed has electrical power facilities and 'False' otherwise.
* **Shed Electrical Lighting** (`shed_electrical_lighting`): Choose 'True' if the shed has electrical lighting facilities and 'False' otherwise.
* **Shed Ventilation** (`shed_ventilation`): Choose 'True' if the shed has ventilation facilities and 'False' otherwise.
* **Shed Ice Packaging** (`shed_ice_packaging`): Choose 'True' if the shed has ice packaging facilities and 'False' otherwise.
* **Other Shed Services** (`shed_other_services`): If the shed has any other services not listed above, enter them here.
* **Notes** (`shed_notes`): Enter any additional comments or observations about the shed.
* **Shed Pesticides** (`shed_pesticides`): Choose 'True' if the shed uses pesticides and 'False' otherwise.
* **Shed Fire Suppression** (`shed_fire_suppression`): Choose 'True' if the shed has fire suppression facilities and 'False' otherwise.
* **Total Shed Value** (`total_shed_value`): Enter the total value of the shed.
* **Onion Cost Price** (`onion_cost_price`): Enter the cost price of the onion.
* **Shed Insurance** (`shed_insurance`): Choose 'True' if the shed has insurance and 'False' otherwise.
* **Total Insured Value** (`total_insured_value`): Enter the total insured value of the shed.
* **Shed Insurance Cost** (`shed_insurance_cost`): Enter the cost of the shed's insurance.
* **Shed Insurance Deductible** (`shed_insurance_deductible`): Enter the deductible of the shed's insurance.
* **Shed Insurance Limit** (`shed_insurance_limit`): Enter the limit of the shed's insurance.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately to ensure the data collected is reliable.
* If you are unsure about any field, please enter 'None' or 'N/A'.
* If you need to add any additional services or notes, please use the 'Other Shed Services' field.
* Please be aware that the shed's value and cost price are important indicators of its worth and financial implications, so enter them carefully and accurately.
