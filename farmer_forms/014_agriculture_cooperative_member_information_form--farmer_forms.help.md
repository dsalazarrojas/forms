# Agriculture Cooperative Member Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agriculture Cooperative Member Information Form is designed to collect information from farmers and agricultural businesses that are interested in joining or already members of the cooperative. This form helps the cooperative understand your needs and tailor its services to support your farming operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*). These are the fields that are necessary to complete the form.
2. Choose your answers from the provided options for select type questions.
3. Make sure to enter accurate information, especially for fields like farm size, annual input spending range, and primary commodity.
4. If you have any additional comments or information that you would like to share with the cooperative, please use the "Additional Comments" field.
5. Once you have completed the form, review it carefully to ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
6. Sign the form digitally by entering your signature in the "Member Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (id: 1)**: This field is required and is your legal first name. Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name (id: 2)**: This field is required and is your legal last name. Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Business or Farm Name (id: 3)**: This field is required and is the name of your farming business or farm. Please enter the name exactly as it appears on your official documents.
* **Member Type (id: 4)**: This field is required and asks for the type of membership you are applying for. Please choose the option that best describes your farm or business.
* **Membership Status (id: 5)**: This field is required and asks whether you are a new member, an existing member, or a returning member.
* **Membership Start Date (id: 6)**: This field is required and asks for the date when you joined or want to join the cooperative. Please enter the date in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Street Address (id: 7)**: This field is required and is your primary address. Please enter the address exactly as it appears on your official documents.
* **City (id: 8)**: This field is required and is the city of your address. Please enter the city exactly as it appears on your official documents.
* **State (id: 9)**: This field is required and is the state or province of your address. Please enter the state exactly as it appears on your official documents.
* **ZIP Code (id: 10)**: This field is required and is the postal code of your address. Please enter the zip code exactly as it appears on your official documents.
* **County (id: 11)**: This field is required and is the county of your residence. Please enter the county exactly as it appears on your official documents.
* **Primary Phone (id: 12)**: This field is required and is your main contact number. Please enter the number exactly as it appears on your official documents.
* **Secondary Phone (id: 13)**: This field is optional and is an alternative contact number. Please enter the number exactly as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address (id: 14)**: This field is required and is your primary email address. Please enter the email address exactly as it appears on your official documents.
* **Farm Size (id: 15)**: This field is required and asks for the total acreage you operate. Please enter the number exactly as it appears on your official documents.
* **Primary Commodity (id: 16)**: This field is required and asks for your main agricultural product. Please choose the option that best describes your primary product.
* **Secondary Commodities (id: 17)**: This field is optional and asks for other products you grow or raise. Please choose the options that best describe your secondary products.
* **Organic Certified (id: 18)**: This field is required and asks whether you are organically certified. Please choose the option that best describes your certification status.
* **Services Interested In (id: 19)**: This field is required and asks for the cooperative services you are interested in. Please choose the options that best describe your needs.
* **Current Suppliers (id: 20)**: This field is optional and asks for where you currently purchase inputs. Please enter the answer exactly as it appears on your official documents.
* **Annual Input Spending Range (id: 21)**: This field is optional and asks for your approximate annual spending on inputs. Please choose the option that best describes your spending range.
* **Marketing Assistance Needs (id: 22)**: This field is optional and asks for the marketing help you need. Please choose the options that best describe your needs.
* **Major Equipment Owned (id: 23)**: This field is optional and asks for significant farm equipment you own. Please enter the answer exactly as it appears on your official documents.
* **Equipment Sharing Interest (id: 24)**: This field is required and asks whether you are interested in equipment sharing. Please choose the option that best describes your interest.
* **Professional References (id: 25)**: This field is optional and asks for names and contact info of references. Please enter the answer exactly as it appears on your official documents.
* **How Did You Hear About Us (id: 26)**: This field is required and asks for the source of your referral. Please choose the option that best describes how you heard about us.
* **Additional Comments (id: 27)**: This field is optional and asks for any other information you would like to share with the cooperative. Please enter the answer exactly as it appears on your official documents.
* **Member Signature (id: 28)**: This field is required and asks for your digital signature. Please enter your signature exactly as it appears on your official documents.
* **Application Date (id: 29)**: This field is required and asks for the date of your application. Please enter the date in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
