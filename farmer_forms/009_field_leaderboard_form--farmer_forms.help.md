# Field Leaderboard Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Field Leaderboard Form is designed to provide a summary of field information in a concise format. It is meant to be completed by farmers to track and manage their field activities, including the status of their fields, farm names, field names, start and end dates and times, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Field Leaderboard Form**: Start by filling out the first field, "Field Leaderboard Form". This is the title of the form, which should be left blank.
2. **Enter Farm Name**: In the "farm_name" field, type in the name of the farm you are associated with. This will help identify the farm for which you are tracking field activities.
3. **Enter Field Name**: In the "field_name" field, type in the name of the field you are tracking.
4. **Select Field Type**: In the "field_type" field, select the type of field you are tracking (e.g., corn, soybean, wheat, etc.).
5. **Enter Start Date and Time**: In the "start_date" and "start_time" fields, type in the start date and time of your field activity. Use the date format `YYYY-MM-DD` and the time format `HH:MM:SS`.
6. **Enter End Date and Time**: In the "end_date" and "end_time" fields, type in the end date and time of your field activity. Again, use the date format `YYYY-MM-DD` and the time format `HH:MM:SS`.
7. **Select Status**: In the "status" field, select the current status of your field activity from the options "active", "inactive", or "completed".
8. **Add Notes**: In the "notes" field, add any additional comments or details about your field activity.
9. **Select Contact Person**: In the "contact_person" field, select the person(s) responsible for the field activity.
10. **Submit**: Once you have filled out all the required fields, click "Submit" to save your data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Field Leaderboard Form (title)**: The title of the form, which should be left blank.
* **Farm Name (farm_name)**: The name of the farm you are associated with.
* **Field Name (field_name)**: The name of the field you are tracking.
* **Field Type (field_type)**: The type of field you are tracking (e.g., corn, soybean, wheat, etc.).
* **Start Date (start_date)**: The start date of your field activity in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Start Time (start_time)**: The start time of your field activity in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
* **End Date (end_date)**: The end date of your field activity in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **End Time (end_time)**: The end time of your field activity in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
* **Status (status)**: The current status of your field activity, which can be "active", "inactive", or "completed".
* **Notes (notes)**: Additional comments or details about your field activity.
* **Contact Person (contact_person)**: The person(s) responsible for the field activity.

Tips:
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields before submitting the form.
* Use the date and time formats specified in the instructions above.
* Select the correct status for your field activity.
* Add any additional comments or details in the "notes" field.
* Be accurate and thorough when filling out the "contact_person" field.
