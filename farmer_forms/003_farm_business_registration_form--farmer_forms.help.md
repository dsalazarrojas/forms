# Farm Business Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Farm Business Registration Form is a multi-page form used to register a farm business with the relevant authorities. This guide will walk you through each page of the form and explain the purpose of each field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, start by carefully reading each page and filling in the relevant information. Make sure to answer all required questions as accurately and completely as possible. If you're unsure about any of the questions, please seek clarification or contact the relevant authorities for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Farm Name** (`farm_name`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of your farm.
* **Farm Address** (`farm_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of your farm.
* **Farm Contact Email** (`farm_contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the person to contact for your farm.
* **Farm Contact Phone** (`farm_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the person to contact for your farm.
* **Farm Type** (`farm_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of farm you operate (small farm, large scale farm, or organic farm).
* **Farm Business Hours** (`farm_business_hours`, `time`, required): Enter the hours of operation for your farm.
* **Farm License Number** (`farm_license_number`, `text`, optional): If you have a farm license, enter the number here.
* **Farm Registration Date** (`farm_registration_date`, `date`, optional): If you've registered your farm before, enter the date of the previous registration.
* **Farm Registration Number** (`farm_registration_number`, `text`, required): Please enter a unique registration number for your farm.
* **Farm Additional Info** (`farm_additional_info`, `note`, optional): Add any additional information about your farm here.
* **Farm Business Status** (`farm_business_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the current status of your farm (active, inactive, or pending).
* **Farm Owners** (`farm_owners`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the owners of your farm (John, Jane, or Joe).
* **Farm Location** (`farm_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of your farm.
* **Farm Business Category** (`farm_business_category`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the categories of your farm (farm, ranch, or orchard).
* **Farm Description** (`farm_description`, `text`, required): Enter a description of your farm.
* **Farm Equipment** (`farm_equipment`, `text`, optional): If you have farm equipment, describe it here.
* **Farm Certifications** (`farm_certifications`, `text`, optional): If your farm has any certifications, list them here.
* **Farm Insurance** (`farm_insurance`, `text`, optional): If your farm has insurance, provide details here.
* **Farm License Expiration** (`farm_license_expiration`, `date`, optional): If you have a farm license, enter the expiration date.
* **Farm Certification Number** (`farm_certification_number`, `text`, optional): If you have farm certifications, enter the numbers here.
* **Farm Registration Fee** (`farm_registration_fee`, `number`, required): Please enter the registration fee for your farm.
* **Farm Registration Fees** (`farm_registration_fees`, `text`, optional): If you have additional fees, list them here.
* **Farm Registration Note** (`farm_registration_note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about your farm here.

Remember to review each page carefully and answer all required questions accurately and completely. If you're unsure about any of the questions, please seek clarification or contact the relevant authorities.
