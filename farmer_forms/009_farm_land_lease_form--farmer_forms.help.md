# Farm Land Lease Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Farm Land Lease Form is designed to collect essential information about a farm land lease agreement between a farmer and a land owner. The completed form will be used to store and manage farm land lease data for administrative and record-keeping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review each field carefully and ensure you have the required information available.
* Fill in the form accurately and consistently.
* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Farm Land Lease Form** (farm_land_lease_form)
* A unique identifier for the form.

* 2. **Size of Land** (size)
* This field is required. Enter the size of the land being leased in square units (e.g., hectares or acres).
* 3. **Tenant Name** (tenant_name)
* This field is required. Enter the name of the tenant (farmer) leasing the land.
* 4. **Tenant Phone** (tenant_phone)
* This field is not required. Enter the tenant's phone number for contact purposes.
* 5. **Land Owner Name** (landlord_name)
* This field is required. Enter the name of the land owner.
* 6. **Size Allowed** (size_allowed)
* This field is required. Enter the size of land the tenant is allowed to lease in square units (e.g., hectares or acres).
* 7. **Land Area** (land_area)
* This field is required. Enter the size of the land being leased in square units (e.g., hectares or acres).
* 8. **Start Date** (start_date)
* This field is required. Enter the start date of the lease in date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* 9. **End Date** (end_date)
* This field is required. Enter the end date of the lease in date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* 10. **Rent** (rent)
* This field is required. Enter the rent amount in monetary units (e.g., dollars or euros).
* 11. **Notes** (notes)
* This field is not required. Enter any additional notes or comments about the lease agreement.
