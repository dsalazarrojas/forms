# Staple Food Crops Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Staple Food Crops Survey is designed to collect brief information about staple food crop production from farmers. This data will help in understanding the agricultural landscape, making informed decisions about food security, and policy development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, please fill in your **Farmer Name** (farmer_name) and **Farm ID** (farm_id) fields.
2. Select one or more staple foods you produce from the list in the **Crop Type** (crop_type) field.
3. Provide any additional comments about your production area in the **Production Area** (production_area) field.
4. Enter the land area harvested for each crop in the **Land Area Harvested** (land_area_harvested) field.
5. Record the yield percentage of your crops in the **Yield Percentage** (yield_percentage) field.
6. Enter the size of your farm in hectares in the **Farm Size** (farm_size) field.
7. Select your farm location from the options in the **Farm Location** (farm_location) field.
8. Provide your contact number in the **Contact Number** (contact_number) field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Farmer Name (farmer_name)**: This field is used to record the name of the farmer who is filling the form.
* **farm_id (farm_id)**: This field is meant to capture a unique ID for the farm.
* **Crop Type (crop_type)**: Select one or more staple foods you produce from the provided options.
* **Production Area (production_area)**: This field is for providing any additional comments or notes about your production area.
* **Land Area Harvested (land_area_harvested)**: Enter the land area harvested for each crop.
* **Yield Percentage (yield_percentage)**: Record the yield percentage of your crops.
* **Farm Size (farm_size)**: Enter the size of your farm in hectares.
* **Farm Location (farm_location)**: Select one of the options: urban, rural, or peri-urban.
* **Contact Number (contact_number)**: Enter your contact number.
