# Greenhouse Grower Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather feedback from farmers about their experience with our greenhouse grower program. Your input is valuable in helping us understand how to improve our services and make the program more effective.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Select the appropriate page to answer, based on the section you would like to provide feedback on.
2. Fill in the text fields with your thoughts, experiences, and suggestions.
3. Make sure to answer every question thoroughly, as this will help us understand your perspective better.
4. When you've finished, click "Submit" to send us your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Operations Questionnaire** (`operations_questionnaire`, text, optional):
	+ This section is for providing feedback on your operations and activities.
* **Challenges Questionnaire** (`challenges_questionnaire`, text, optional):
	+ This section is for sharing your challenges and difficulties you face while using our program.
* **Satisfaction Questionnaire** (`satisfaction_questionnaire`, text, optional):
	+ This section is for expressing your satisfaction or dissatisfaction with our program and services.
* **Operational Changes** (`operational_changes`, text, optional):
	+ This section is for suggesting operational changes that could improve your experience with our program.
* **Operations 2** (`operations_2`, text, optional):
	+ This section is for providing feedback on operations and activities, similar to the first section.
* **Challenges 2** (`challenges_2`, text, optional):
	+ This section is for sharing challenges and difficulties, similar to the second section.
* **Satisfaction 2** (`satisfaction_2`, text, optional):
	+ This section is for expressing your satisfaction or dissatisfaction, similar to the third section.
* **Operational Changes 2** (`operational_changes_2`, text, optional):
	+ This section is for suggesting operational changes, similar to the fourth section.
* **Operations 3** (`operations_3`, text, optional):
	+ This section is for providing feedback on operations and activities, similar to the first section.
* **Challenges 3** (`challenges_3`, text, optional):
	+ This section is for sharing challenges and difficulties, similar to the second section.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when providing feedback.
* Be honest and accurate when describing your experiences and challenges.
* If you have suggestions for improvements, don't hesitate to share them.

Note: This form has multiple identical fields (e.g., operations_questionnaire and operations_2). These are meant to allow farmers to provide separate feedback for each section of the program, without feeling overwhelmed by an excessive number of questions.
