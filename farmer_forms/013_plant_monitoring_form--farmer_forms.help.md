# Plant Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect data on plant monitoring for maintenance and improvement purposes. It's essential to provide accurate and up-to-date information for better plant care.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the type of plant being monitored from the options provided.
2.  Choose the location of the monitored plant.
3.  Enter the current temperature of the plant in Celsius.
4.  Enter the current temperature of the plant in Fahrenheit.
5.  Input the current moisture level of the plant as a percentage.
6.  Select Yes or No for disease or pest presence on the plant.
7.  Choose the frequency of watering for the plant.
8.  (Optional) Enter the amount of rainwater collected from the plant.
9.  Select Yes or No for recent fertilization of the plant.
10. Select the type of fertilizer used (if applicable).
11.  Choose the current growth stage of the plant.
12.  (Optional) Provide any additional information about the plant.
13.  Enter the date of last monitoring.
14.  Select the current status of the plant (Healthy, Diseased, Damaged, or Other).
15.  (Optional) Add any additional notes about the plant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of plant is being monitored?** (`monitor_plant_type`): Please select the type of plant being monitored from the options provided.
* **Location of the monitored plant** (`monitor_plant_location`): Choose the location where the plant is being monitored.
* **Current temperature of the plant (in Celsius)** (`monitor_temperature_celsius`): Enter the current temperature of the plant in Celsius.
* **Current temperature of the plant (in Fahrenheit)** (`monitor_temperature_fahrenheit`): Enter the current temperature of the plant in Fahrenheit.
* **Current moisture level of the plant (in percentage)** (`monitor_moisture`): Input the current moisture level of the plant as a percentage.
* **Has the plant shown any signs of disease or pests?** (`monitor_disease_pests`): Select Yes or No for disease or pest presence on the plant.
* **How often is the plant watered?** (`monitor_watering_frequency`): Choose the frequency of watering for the plant.
*   (Optional) **How much rainwater is collected from the plant?** (`monitor_rainwater_collected`): Enter the amount of rainwater collected from the plant (only required if this field is visible).
* **Has the plant been fertilized recently?** (`monitor_fertilization`): Select Yes or No for recent fertilization of the plant.
* **What type of fertilizer was used?** (`monitor_fertilizer_type`): Select the type of fertilizer used (only if this field is visible).
* **What is the current growth stage of the plant?** (`monitor_growth_stage`): Choose the current growth stage of the plant.
* (Optional) **Additional information about the plant** (`monitor_additional_info`): Provide any additional information about the plant (only if this field is visible).
* **Date of last monitoring** (`monitor_date`): Enter the date of last monitoring.
* **What is the current status of the plant?** (`monitor_status`): Select the current status of the plant (Healthy, Diseased, Damaged, or Other).
* **Notes** (`monitor_notes`): Provide any additional notes about the plant (only if this field is visible).
