# Agriculture Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Agriculture Survey is a form designed to collect information from farmers about their farming practices, challenges, and goals. This form will help us understand the current state of agriculture and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please answer the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* If you're unsure about any question, please feel free to ask for clarification.
* You can complete the form in one sitting or save and return later, as you like.

* **Respondent Name** (id: 1, text, optional): Enter your full name.
* **Farm Name** (id: 2, text, required): Enter the name of your farming operation.
* **Farm Location** (id: 3, text, required): Enter the address or general location of your farm.
* **Total Acreage** (id: 4, number, required): Enter the total size of your farm in acres.
