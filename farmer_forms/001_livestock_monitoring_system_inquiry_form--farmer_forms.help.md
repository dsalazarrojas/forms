# Livestock Monitoring System Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Livestock Monitoring System Inquiry Form is a tool for farmers or their representatives to contact support or gather information about the livestock monitoring system. It is likely used for maintenance issues, general inquiries, or other assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form effectively, follow these steps:

1. Enter your farm's name in the "farm_name" field. This will help us understand which farm you are contacting us about.
2. Provide your name as the owner or representative of the farm in the "owner_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field. This will be our primary contact point with you.
4. If you would like to be contacted by phone, please enter your contact phone number in the "contact_phone" field.
5. Enter the address of the farm in the "address" field. This will help us understand the location of the farm.
6. Describe your inquiry or issue with the livestock monitoring system in the "inquiry" field.
7. Estimate the severity of the issue or the level of attention required, using the "estimate" field.
8. Indicate if you would like a follow-up on this issue by selecting "True" or "False" in the "follow_up" field.
9. If you are interested in a specific product or service related to the livestock monitoring system, please describe it in the "product" field.
10. Add any additional comments or notes about your inquiry in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Farm Name (farm_name)**: The name of the farm you are contacting us about.
* **Your Name (owner_name)**: Your name as the owner or representative of the farm.
* **Email (email)**: Your email address for us to contact you.
* **Contact Phone (contact_phone)**: Your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
* **Farm Address (address)**: The address of the farm for us to locate it.
* **Inquiry (inquiry)**: Describe your issue or question about the livestock monitoring system.
* **Issue Estimate (estimate)**: An estimate of the issue's severity or level of attention required.
* **Follow-up (follow_up)**: Indicate if you would like a follow-up on this issue, selecting "True" or "False".
* **Product (product)**: If you are interested in a specific product or service related to the system, describe it.
* **Notes (notes)**: Additional comments or notes about your inquiry.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields for your inquiry to be processed efficiently.
* If you are unsure about a field, try to provide as much information as possible.
* If you have any additional materials or attachments related to your inquiry, you can attach them to your submission.
