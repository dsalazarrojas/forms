# Local Food Producer Partnership Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from local food producers who are interested in partnering with a local food system or network.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Fill in your farm's information on the "Farmer Info" page.
2. Provide your contact information on the "Contact Info" page.
3. Describe your produce and sales channels on the "Produce Info" and "Sales Channels" pages.
4. Select your farm's certification information on the "Certification Info" page.
5. Fill in your farm's information on the "Farm Info" page.
6. Provide a brief description of your partnership goals on the "Partnership Info" page.
7. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Farmer Info (Page 1)** (`title`, text, required): Please fill in your farm's title and any other relevant information.
* **Contact Info (Page 2)** (`name`, text, required): Please provide your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Produce Info (Page 3)** (`name`, text, required): Please describe your produce, including the types and quantities of produce you grow or sell.
* **Sales Channels (Page 4)** (`select_multiple`, optional): Choose all the sales channels that apply to your farm:
	+ Food Co-op
	+ Farmers Market
	+ Restaurant
	+ CSA
* **Market Info (Page 5)** (`name`, text, required): Please describe your farm's market or sales channel presence, including any relevant websites or social media handles.
* **Certification Info (Page 6)** (`select_one`, optional): Select one of the following certifications:
	+ Organic
	+ Local
	+ Non-GMO
	+ USDA
* **Farm Info (Page 7)** (`name`, text, required): Please provide your farm's information, including its location and products sold.
* **Partnership Info (Page 8)** (`name`, text, required): Please describe your goals and expectations for partnering with the local food system or network.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact email or phone number].
