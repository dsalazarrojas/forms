# Beekeeping Hive Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beekeeping Hive Declaration Form is a comprehensive tool to collect information from beekeepers about their apiary operations. The data collected will help us understand the current state of beekeeping in the region and support effective decision-making and resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the instructions above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Beekeeper Name** (`beekeeper_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the beekeeper.
* **Email Address** (`beekeeper_email`, email, required): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`beekeeper_phone`, text, required): Enter your best phone number to reach you.
* **Apiary Name** (`apiary_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your beekeeping operation.
* **Apiary Address** (`apiary_address`, text, required): Enter the full address of your apiary location.
* **Declaration Date** (`declaration_date`, date, required): Enter the current date.
* **Total Number of Hives** (`total_hives`, number, required): Enter the total number of hives at your apiary location.
* **Hive Types** (`hive_types`, select_multiple, required): Select all the types of hives you have at your apiary.
* **Hive ID Numbers** (`hive_ids`, text, required): Enter the identification numbers of all your hives.
* **Queen Source** (`queen_source`, select_one, required): Select the source of your queen bees.
* **Bee Breed or Strain** (`bee_breed`, select_one, required): Select the primary breed or strain of bees you keep.
* **Hive Condition** (`hive_condition`, select_one, required): Select the overall condition of your hives.
* **Issues Detected** (`issues_detected`, select_multiple, required): Select all the issues you have detected with your hives.
* **Treatments Applied** (`treatment_applied`, select_multiple, required): Select all the treatments you have applied to your hives.
* **Last Inspection Date** (`last_inspection_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your last hive inspection.
* **Honey Production This Season** (`honey_production`, number, optional): If applicable, report the total honey production in pounds this season.
* **Wax Production This Season** (`wax_production`, number, optional): If applicable, report the total wax production in pounds this season.
* **Forage Availability** (`forage_available`, select_one, required): Select the quality of forage in the area.
* **Water Source Available** (`water_source`, select_one, required): Indicate if water is available for your bees.
* **Winter Preparation Status** (`winter_prep_status`, select_one, required): Report your winter preparation status.
* **Registration Fee Paid** (`registration_fee`, select_one, required): Confirm if you have paid the registration fee.
* **Beekeeping Association Member** (`association_member`, select_one, required): Indicate if you are a member of a beekeeping association.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about your operation.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you answer all questions accurately and truthfully.
- If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification before submitting the form.
- If you have any additional information not captured in this form, please use the "Additional Notes" field to provide it.
- Thank you for your cooperation and contributions to our beekeeping community!
