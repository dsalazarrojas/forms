# Horse Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Horse Registration Form is a crucial document for registering horses in a database, providing accurate and up-to-date information about each horse, its owner, and farm.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the horse's identification number in the `horse id` field.
2. Provide the horse's name in the `horse name` field.
3. Select the horse's breed from the available options (if provided).
4. Enter the owner's email address in the `owner email` field.
5. Enter the owner's phone number in the `owner phone` field.
6. Enter the owner's address in the `owner address` field.
7. Enter the horse's birthdate in the `horse birthdate` field.
8. Enter the horse's weight in the `horse weight` field.
9. Select the horse's color from the available options (if provided).
10. Enter the horse's height in the `horse height` field.
11. Enter the date of registration in the `horse registration date` field.
12. Provide any additional registration notes in the `horse registration notes` field.
13. Enter the farm's name in the `farm name` field.
14. Enter the farm's address in the `farm address` field.
15. Enter the farm's phone number in the `farm phone` field.
16. Enter the farm's email address in the `farm email` field.
17. Enter the farm's owner's name in the `farm owner` field.
18. Enter the farm owner's phone number in the `farm owner phone` field.
19. Enter the farm owner's email address in the `farm owner email` field.
20. Select the horse's type from the available options (if provided).
21. Select the horse's gender from the available options (if provided).
22. Enter the horse's date of birth in the `horse dob` field.
23. Enter the horse's microchip number (if applicable).
24. Enter the farm's license number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **horse id** (`number`, required): Enter the unique identification number assigned to the horse.
* **horse name** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of the horse.
* **horse breed** (`text`, optional): Select the breed of the horse.
* **owner email** (`email`, optional): Enter the email address of the horse's owner.
* **owner phone** (`text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the horse's owner.
* **owner address** (`text`, optional): Enter the address of the horse's owner.
* **horse birthdate** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of birth of the horse.
* **horse weight** (`number`, optional): Enter the weight of the horse.
* **horse color** (`text`, optional): Select the color of the horse.
* **horse height** (`number`, optional): Enter the height of the horse.
* **horse registration date** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of registration of the horse.
* **horse registration notes** (`text`, optional): Provide any additional notes about the horse's registration.
* **farm name** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of the farm.
* **farm address** (`text`, optional): Enter the address of the farm.
* **farm phone** (`text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the farm.
* **farm email** (`email`, optional): Enter the email address of the farm.
* **farm owner** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of the farm owner.
* **farm owner phone** (`text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the farm owner.
* **farm owner email** (`email`, optional): Enter the email address of the farm owner.
* **horse type** (`text`, optional): Select the type of horse.
* **horse gender** (`text`, optional): Select the gender of the horse.
* **horse dob** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of birth of the horse.
* **horse microchip number** (`text`, optional): Enter the microchip number of the horse (if applicable).
* **farm license number** (`text`, optional): Enter the license number of the farm (if applicable).
* **form submission note** (`text`, optional): Provide any additional information about the form submission.
