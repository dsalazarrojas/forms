# Crop Information Collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from farmers about their crops and farms. This data will be used for analysis, planning, and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form pages and fields below to understand what information is required.
2. Enter the required information for each field, using the guidance provided below.
3. Submit the form to complete your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Farmer Information (Page 1)
* **Farmer Information** (farmers_info, text, not required): Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself as a farmer.

### Crop Information (Page 1)
* **Crop Information** (crop_info, text, not required): Enter details about the specific crop you are growing.

### Aggregated Crops (Page 2)
* **Aggregated Crops** (aggregated_crops, text, not required): List the types of crops you grow on your farm.

### Crop Yield (Page 2)
* **Crop Yield** (crop_yield, number, not required): Enter the yield of your crop, if applicable.

### Seasonal Data (Page 3)
* **Seasonal Data** (seasonal_data, text, not required): Provide any seasonal data related to your crop or farm.

### Farm Location (Page 3)
* **Farm Location** (farm_location, text, not required): Enter your farm location, including country, state, or region.

### Farm Land Size (Page 4)
* **Farm Land Size** (farm_land_size, number, not required): Enter the size of your farm land in acres.

### Farm Land Type (Page 4)
* **Farm Land Type** (farm_land_type, text, not required): Select the type of land you use for farming, e.g. irrigated, non-irrigated, etc.

### Farm Land Use (Page 5)
* **Farm Land Use** (farm_land_use, text, not required): Describe how you use your land for farming, e.g. crop, livestock, etc.

### Weather Conditions (Page 5)
* **Weather Conditions** (weather_conditions, text, not required): Report any weather-related data relevant to your farm.

### Farm Soil Quality (Page 6)
* **Farm Soil Quality** (farm_soil_quality, text, not required): Evaluate the quality of your farm soil, e.g. good, poor, etc.

## Tips
* Ensure you provide accurate and honest information to help with analysis and planning.
* Use the correct units of measurement for land size (acres).
* Be as detailed as possible with seasonal data and weather conditions.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask for help or clarification.
* Review your entry before submitting to ensure it's accurate and complete.
* If you need help or have questions, reach out to us.
