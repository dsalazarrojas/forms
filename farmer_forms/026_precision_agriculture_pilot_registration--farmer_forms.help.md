# Precision Agriculture Pilot Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Precision Agriculture Pilot Registration form is a tool for farmers to register their interest and participate in the precision agriculture pilot program. This form helps us gather information about your farm and your participation in the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your name from the list of farmers (Farmer 1, Farmer 2, or Farmer 3).
2. Enter your farm details in the text field provided.
3. Select the crops you are interested in testing and implementing on your farm (you can select multiple options).
4. Provide your participant details, including any relevant information about yourself.
5. Enter your contact information, including any relevant contact details.
6. Review the terms and conditions of the program and check the box to confirm you have read and agree to them.
7. Submit the form to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Farmer
* **Farmer** (`Farmer 1`, `Farmer 2`, `Farmer 3`, required: false): Choose your name from the list of farmers.

### Farm Details
* **Farm Details** (`text`, required: false): Enter your farm's details, including any relevant information about your farm.

### Crops
* **Crops** (`select multiple`, required: false): Select all the crops you are interested in testing and implementing on your farm (you can select multiple options).

### Participant Details
* **Participant Details** (`text`, required: false): Enter any relevant information about yourself, such as your role on the farm or your experience with precision agriculture.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`text`, required: false): Enter your contact information, including any relevant contact details, such as your name, email, and phone number.

### Terms and Conditions
* **Terms and Conditions** (`note`, required: false): Review the terms and conditions of the program and check the box to confirm you have read and agree to them.

**Tips**

* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all fields are accurate and complete.
* If you have any questions or need help with the form, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* By completing this form, you will be registering your farm for the precision agriculture pilot program.
