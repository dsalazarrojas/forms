# Tractor Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tractor Inspection Form is a crucial tool for farmers to maintain and report the condition of their tractors. Completing this form helps identify any potential issues and prevent accidents or costly repairs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" for each tractor being inspected.
2. Choose the current status of the tractor ("Active" or "Inactive").
3. Check the condition of the engine oil, air filter, transmission, and brakes.
4. Report any issues with the suspension.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tractor Inspection Tractors** (`tractor_inspection_tractors`, select_multiple, Required): Select "Yes" for tractors that need to be inspected, and "No" for tractors that do not require inspection.
* **Tractor Status** (`tractor_status`, select_one, Required): Choose the current status of the tractor, either "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Engine Oil** (`engine_oil`, select_one, Required): Check the level and condition of the engine oil. Select "Yes" if it's adequate, and "No" if it's low or dirty.
* **Air Filter** (`air_filter`, select_one, Required): Check the air filter for any blockages or obstructions. Select "Yes" if it's clean, and "No" if it's dirty or clogged.
* **Transmission** (`transmission`, text, Optional): Report any issues with the transmission, such as unusual noises or leaks.
* **Brakes** (`brakes`, select_one, Required): Check the condition of the brakes. Select "Yes" if they're in good condition, and "No" if they're worn or malfunctioning.
* **Suspension** (`suspension`, text, Optional): Report any issues with the suspension, such as unusual noises or uneven ride.

## Tips
Please make sure to carefully read and understand each question carefully before answering. If you're unsure about any aspect of the form, please consult with your supervisor or the relevant guidelines for further clarification.
