# Farm Emergency Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The farm emergency contact form is a vital resource that helps farm staff and management quickly identify and contact emergency contacts during an unforeseen situation.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, please provide the requested information in the fields below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Please enter the first name of the emergency contact.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the last name of the emergency contact.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the emergency contact.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Provide the contact's phone number for emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the individual who is the emergency contact.
*   **Relationship** (`relationship`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you are a family member or friend of the emergency contact.
*   **Phone Number 2** (`phone_number_2`, `text`, optional): Enter an alternative phone number for the emergency contact.
*   **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of the emergency contact.
*   **Submission** (`submission`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe the emergency contact form submission purpose and context.

The farm staff will be using this information to contact emergency contacts in the event of a farm emergency or incident. Please provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that emergency contacts can be quickly reached in a timely and efficient manner.
