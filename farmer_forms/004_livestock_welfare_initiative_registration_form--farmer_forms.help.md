# Livestock Welfare Initiative Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Livestock Welfare Initiative Registration Form is designed to collect information from farmers and organizations that are participating in animal welfare programs. This form helps us ensure that participants are accurately recorded and their information is up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select "Yes" if you are a farmer or organization participating in the animal welfare initiative.
2. If you have any contact details (e.g., phone number, email address), please provide them.
3. Enter the name of your farm or organization.
4. Enter the address of your farm or organization.
5. Enter the city and state of your farm or organization.
6. Enter your zip code.
7. Enter your email address (if you have one).
8. Enter your phone number (if you have one).
9. Describe the program focus (e.g., animal health, animal welfare).
10. List any welfare concerns you have.
11. Enter animal details (e.g., type, number).
12. Enter the start and end dates of your program.
13. Provide any additional comments or notes about your submission.
14. Verify your email address.
15. Verify your phone number (if you have one).
16. Select the status of your verification (e.g., Verified, Not Verified, Not Required).
17. If you were not assigned to this form, select "Other" as the submitted by option.
18. Enter an animal ID (if applicable).
19. Enter another animal ID (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_info**: This is the main section for participant information.
* **participant_type**: Select "Yes" if you are a farmer or organization participating in the animal welfare initiative.
* **contact_details**: If you have any contact details, please provide them. This includes phone number and email address.
* **farm_or_organization**: Enter the name of your farm or organization.
* **farm_or_organization_name**: Enter the name of your farm or organization.
* **address**: Enter the address of your farm or organization.
* **city**: Enter the city of your farm or organization.
* **state**: Enter the state of your farm or organization.
* **zip**: Enter the zip code of your farm or organization.
* **email**: Enter your email address (if you have one).
* **phone**: Enter your phone number (if you have one).
* **program_focus**: Describe the program focus (e.g., animal health, animal welfare).
* **welfare_concerns**: List any welfare concerns you have.
* **animal_details**: Enter animal details (e.g., type, number).
* **program_start_date**: Enter the start date of your program.
* **program_end_date**: Enter the end date of your program.
* **note**: Provide any additional comments or notes about your submission.
* **email_verification**: Verify your email address.
* **phone_verification**: Verify your phone number (if you have one).
* **verification_status**: Select the status of your verification (e.g., Verified, Not Verified, Not Required).
* **submitted_by**: If you were not assigned to this form, select "Other" as the submitted by option.
* **animal_id**: Enter an animal ID (if applicable).
* **animal_id_2**: Enter another animal ID (if applicable).
* **submit**: This is the submit button to save your information.
