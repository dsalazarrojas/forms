# Crop Scouting Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Crop Scouting Report Form is a tool used to collect data about crops and farmers for agricultural research, monitoring, or management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and title as the farmer or crop scout completing the form.
2. Enter the name of the crop being observed.
3. Provide any relevant notes or observations about the crop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Farmer Name
* **farmer_name**: Enter your name as the farmer or crop scout responsible for filling out this form. This information will help us know who completed the form.

### Crop Name
* **crop_name**: Enter the name of the crop being observed. This can be a type of plant, such as wheat or corn, or a specific field or plot where the crop is located.

### Notes
* **notes**: Provide any additional information or observations you have about the crop. This can include details about its condition, growth, or any issues you've encountered.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out this form.
* Use clear and concise language to help us understand your observations.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
