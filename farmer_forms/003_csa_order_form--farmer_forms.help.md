# CSA Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CSA Order Form is a tool for customers to place their orders for Community-Supported Agriculture (CSA) shares. The form collects information about the customer, their share preferences, and payment details. Completing this form will help the farm team understand the customer's needs and provide the best possible experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Full Name in the `Full Name` field.
2. Provide your Email Address for order confirmations and updates.
3. Enter your Phone Number for contact purposes.
4. Select the Share Size that suits your household.
5. Choose how often you want to receive your share (Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, or Seasonal).
6. Select the Pickup Location where you will collect your share (Farm Pickup, Downtown Drop-off, Northside Market, or Home Delivery).
7. If desired, select your Pickup Day Preference (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, or Saturday).
8. Choose any Dietary Preferences for your share (Vegetarian-focused, Fruit-heavy, Leafy greens, Root vegetables, or Family-friendly).
9. Select any Add-On Items you'd like to include with your share (Farm eggs, Fresh flowers, Artisan bread, Local honey, Herbal bouquet, or None).
10. Choose the Payment Method (Credit Card, Debit Card, SNAP/EBT, Check, or Cash at pickup).
11. Select the Payment Plan (Full payment upfront, Monthly installments, or Pay per pickup).
12. Enter the Start Date when you'd like your share to begin.
13. Add any Special Instructions, such as allergies or delivery notes.
14. Finally, indicate how you heard about the farm (Friend or family, Social media, Farmers market, Website search, Local event, or Other).
15. If interested, express your Volunteer Interest (Yes, interested, Maybe someday, or No thanks).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Required): Enter your primary member's name.
* **Email Address** (Required): Provide a valid email address for order confirmations and updates.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your contact number for delivery issues.
* **Share Size** (Required): Select from Small (1-2 people), Medium (2-4 people), Large (4-6 people), or Extra Large (6+ people).
* **Share Frequency** (Required): Choose how often you'd like to receive your share (Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, or Seasonal).
* **Pickup Location** (Required): Select where you'll collect your share (Farm Pickup, Downtown Drop-off, Northside Market, or Home Delivery).
* **Pickup Day Preference**: If you have a preferred pickup day, select it (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, or Saturday).
* **Dietary Preferences**: Check any dietary preferences for your share (Vegetarian-focused, Fruit-heavy, Leafy greens, Root vegetables, or Family-friendly).
* **Add-On Items**: Select any additional items to include with your share (Farm eggs, Fresh flowers, Artisan bread, Local honey, Herbal bouquet, or None).
* **Payment Method** (Required): Choose how you'll pay for your share (Credit Card, Debit Card, SNAP/EBT, Check, or Cash at pickup).
* **Payment Plan** (Required): Select the payment schedule (Full payment upfront, Monthly installments, or Pay per pickup).
* **Start Date** (Required): Enter the start date of your share.
* **Special Instructions**: Provide any allergies, delivery notes, or requests.
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: Help us understand our community by selecting how you heard about the farm (Friend or family, Social media, Farmers market, Website search, Local event, or Other).
* **Volunteer Interest**: Express your interest in volunteering on the farm (Yes, interested, Maybe someday, or No thanks).
