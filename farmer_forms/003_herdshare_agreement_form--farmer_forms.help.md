# Herdshare Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Herdshare Agreement Form is a tool used to create and manage herdshare agreements between farmers and users. This form helps establish a formal partnership, ensuring that both parties understand their roles and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your Email: Enter your email address to contact you in case we need to reach out to you for further information.
2. Enter your Phone: Add your phone number where you can be contacted.
3. Provide Farm Name: Enter the name of the farm or agricultural operation you are associated with.
4. Enter User Name: Add your name to be associated with this agreement.
5. Specify Share Amount: Enter the amount of shares you have with the farm.
6. Select Start Date: Enter the date you started sharing with the farm.
7. Select End Date: Enter the date you plan to end sharing with the farm.
8. Choose Share Term: Select the term of the share agreement (6 months, 12 months, or 24 months).
9. Provide Farm Location: Enter the location of the farm or agricultural operation.
10. Enter Farm Name (if applicable): If you have a second farm, add its name.
11. Enter Farm Address: Provide the address of the farm or agricultural operation.
12. Enter Farm Phone: Add the phone number of the farm or agricultural operation.
13. Enter Farm Email: Enter the email address of the farm or agricultural operation.
14. Choose Farm Share Term: Select the term of the farm's share agreement (6 months, 12 months, or 24 months).
15. Enter Farm Start Date: Enter the date the farm started sharing.
16. Enter Farm End Date: Enter the date the farm plans to end sharing.
17. Select Status: Choose the status of the share agreement (Active or Inactive).
18. Specify Share Amount 2: Enter the amount of shares you have with the second farm.
19. Enter User Farm Name (if applicable): If you have a second farm, add its name.
20. Enter Share Agreement Signed At: Enter the date the share agreement was signed.
21. Add Farm Note (if applicable): If you have additional information about the farm, add it here.
22. Add User Note (if applicable): If you have additional information about yourself, add it here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email** (`user_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`user_phone`, text, optional): Add your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Farm Name** (`farm_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the farm or agricultural operation.
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Add your name for identification purposes.
* **Share Amount** (`share_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of shares you have with the farm.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you started sharing with the farm.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to end sharing with the farm.
* **Share Term** (`share_term`, select_one, optional): Select the term of the share agreement (6 months, 12 months, or 24 months).
* **Farm Location** (`farm_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the farm or agricultural operation.
* **Farm Name 2** (`farm_name_2`, text, optional): If you have a second farm, enter its name.
* **Farm Address** (`farm_address`, text, optional): Provide the address of the farm or agricultural operation.
* **Farm Phone** (`farm_phone`, text, optional): Add the phone number of the farm or agricultural operation.
* **Farm Email** (`farm_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the farm or agricultural operation.
* **Farm Share Term** (`farm_share_term`, select_one, optional): Select the term of the farm's share agreement (6 months, 12 months, or 24 months).
* **Farm Start Date** (`farm_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the farm started sharing.
* **Farm End Date** (`farm_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the farm plans to end sharing.
* **Status** (`agreement_status`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the status of the share agreement (Active or Inactive).
* **Share Amount 2** (`share_amount_2`, number, optional): Enter the amount of shares you have with the second farm.
* **User Farm Name** (`user_farm_name`, text, optional): If you have a second farm, add its name.
* **Share Agreement Signed At** (`farm_share_agreement_signed_at`, date, optional): Enter the date the share agreement was signed.
* **Farm Note** (`farm_note`, note, optional): Add any additional information about the farm.
* **User Note** (`user_note`, note, optional): Add any additional information about yourself.
