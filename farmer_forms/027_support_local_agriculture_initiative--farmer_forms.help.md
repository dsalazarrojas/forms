# support_local_agriculture_initiative - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "support_local_agriculture_initiative" form is designed to gather information from farmers for a support initiative for local agriculture. This form allows farmers to provide their details, volunteer information, email address, phone number, and donation details, which will help the initiative to better support local agriculture.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and any other relevant information under "Farmer Info".
2. Select your interest in volunteering by choosing one or more options under "Volunteer Signups".
3. Enter your email address under "Email" to ensure the initiative can contact you.
4. Enter your phone number under "Phone" for any further communication.
5. If you wish to donate, enter a numerical value under "Donation Amount".
6. Enter any details about your pledge under "Pledge Details".
7. Enter any details about your volunteer work under "Volunteer Details".
8. Add any additional notes about your support for the local agriculture initiative under "Notes".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Farmer Info** (Farmer Info, text, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself as a farmer.
* **Volunteer Signups** (Volunteer Signups, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more options to express your interest in volunteering for the local agriculture initiative. You can choose from "Volunteer", "Pledge", or "Donation".
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address to allow the initiative to contact you.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Donation Amount** (Donation Amount, number, optional): If you wish to donate, enter a numerical value.
* **Pledge Details** (Pledge Details, text, optional): Enter any details about your pledge.
* **Volunteer Details** (Volunteer Details, text, optional): Enter any details about your volunteer work.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your support for the local agriculture initiative.
