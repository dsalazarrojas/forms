**Farm Survey - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Farm Survey is designed to collect data from farmers on their farming practices, soil types, equipment, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions on each page carefully, making sure to provide accurate and honest information about your farm operations.
2. Each page has specific questions tailored to collect unique information. Follow the prompts and instructions on each page to ensure accurate completion.
3. If a question is not relevant or you are unsure about the answer, please provide the best effort response or leave it blank.
4. You can submit the survey once you have completed all the pages.
