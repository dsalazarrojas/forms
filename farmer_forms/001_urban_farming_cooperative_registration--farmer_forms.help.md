# urban_farming_cooperative_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering members of the Urban Farming Cooperative. It collects information about the member and their farm, such as contact details, farm location, and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling in your **Member Details**. 
2. Choose your **Member Interests** that align with the cooperative's goals.
3. Grant your consent for the cooperative's terms and conditions.
4. Select your **Member Type** from the available options.
5. Enter your **Member Address**.
6. Provide your **Zip Code**.
7. Enter your **Member Phone**.
8. Enter your **Member Organization** (if applicable).
9. Enter your **Member Position** (if applicable).
10. Choose a **Member Start Date** and a **Member End Date** (if applicable).
11. Add any additional **Member Notes**.
12. Enter the **Farm Name**.
13. Enter the **Farm Address**.
14. Enter the **Farm City**.
15. Enter the **Farm State**.
16. Enter the **Farm Zip Code**.
17. Enter the **Farm Phone**.
18. Add any additional **Farm Notes**.
19. Select your **Member Interests 2** that align with the cooperative's goals.
20. Finally, review your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Details** (Member Details): 
  * This is the section where you provide your basic contact information.
  * You can enter your name, email, or any other relevant details here.
* **Member Interests** (Member Interests): 
  * Choose from the available options that best represent your interests and involvement with the cooperative.
  * These interests might reflect your farm or project goals, skills, or needs.
* **Consent** (Consent): 
  * This is an agreement to abide by the cooperative's rules and regulations.
  * Please read and understand the terms before granting consent.
* **Member Type** (Member Type): 
  * Select the type of member you are, such as farmer, volunteer, or partner.
  * This helps the cooperative understand your role within the organization.
* **Member Address** (Member Address): 
  * Enter the physical address where you reside or work.
  * This is important for communication and logistical purposes.
* **Zip Code** (Zip Code): 
  * Enter your postal code or zip code for geographical tracking.
  * This helps the cooperative understand your location.
* **Member Phone** (Member Phone): 
  * Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
  * Make sure to include your country code if you're not in a local area code.
* **Member Organization** (Member Organization): 
  * If you are associated with an organization, enter it here.
  * This could be your farm, non-profit, or business name.
* **Member Position** (Member Position): 
  * Enter your role or title within your organization.
  * This helps the cooperative understand your responsibilities.
* **Member Start Date** (Member Start Date), **Member End Date** (Member End Date): 
  * Choose these dates if your membership has a specific start and end date.
  * These dates help the cooperative track your involvement timeline.
* **Member Notes** (Member Notes): 
  * Add any additional comments or notes about your membership.
  * This could include your goals, challenges, or expectations.
* **Farm Name** (Farm Name): 
  * Enter the name of your farm or project.
  * This is important for record-keeping and recognition.
* **Farm Address** (Farm Address): 
  * Enter the physical address of your farm or project.
  * This is crucial for logistical and communication purposes.
* **Farm City** (Farm City): 
  * Enter the city where your farm or project is located.
  * This helps the cooperative understand your farm's location.
* **Farm State** (Farm State): 
  * Enter the state where your farm or project is located.
  * This provides geographical information for the cooperative.
* **Farm Zip Code** (Farm Zip Code): 
  * Enter the postal code or zip code of your farm or project.
  * This is essential for tracking purposes.
* **Farm Phone** (Farm Phone): 
  * Enter your farm or project's phone number.
  * This is for contact and communication purposes.
* **Farm Notes** (Farm Notes): 
  * Add any additional comments or notes about your farm or project.
  * This could include your goals, challenges, or achievements.
* **Member Interests 2** (Member Interests 2): 
  * Choose from the available options that align with your interests and involvement with the cooperative.
  * These interests might reflect your farm or project goals, skills, or needs.
