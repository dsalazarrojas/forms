# Animal Breeder Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Breeder Estimate Form is used to collect information from potential clients for the purpose of providing a cost estimate for breeding services. This form helps the breeder to gather the necessary details about the client, the animal, and the services requested.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, you will need to follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your **Client Full Name**, **Email Address**, **Phone Number**, and **Address**. These fields are required for us to contact you and provide you with the estimate.
2. In the **Animal Information** field, provide any relevant details about the animal, such as its species, breed, age, and sex.
3. Select the **Services Requested** options that apply to the breeding services you require. You can choose one or more of the options provided.
4. If you have a specific **Type of Breeding** in mind, select it from the options provided.
5. If you have **Health Certifications** for the animal, select the relevant options from the list.
6. If you are a female animal, enter the **Number of Previous Litters**.
7. In the **Breeding History** field, provide any previous breeding outcomes.
8. If you have any **Special Requirements**, enter them in this field.
9. Review the estimated **Cost Breakdown** and make sure it includes all the relevant services and fees.
10. If applicable, enter the **Stud Fee**, **Artificial Insemination Fee**, **Estimated Veterinary Fees**, and **Genetic or Health Testing Fees**.
11. Enter the **Additional Fees** if any other costs are applicable.
12. Calculate the **Total Estimated Cost** by summing up all the services and fees.
13. Select the **Payment Terms** that apply to your situation.
14. Enter the **Date** when this estimate is valid.
15. Read and agree to the **Terms and Conditions**.
16. Select whether you wish to **Proceed with Services** or just receive the estimate.
17. Finally, sign and date the **Breeder Signature** and **Date** fields to confirm your acceptance of the estimate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Full Name**: Your full name as the pet owner or breeder.
* **Email Address**: Your email address for us to contact you and deliver the estimate.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact number for any questions or concerns.
* **Address**: Your street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Animal Information**: Provide any relevant details about the animal, such as its species, breed, age, and sex.
* **Services Requested**: Select all the services you require for the breeding process.
* **Type of Breeding**: Select the specific method of breeding you require.
* **Health Certifications**: Select any health certifications the animal may have.
* **Number of Previous Litters**: For female animals, enter the number of previous litters.
* **Breeding History**: Provide any previous breeding outcomes.
* **Special Requirements**: Enter any specific needs or requests you have.
* **Estimated Cost Breakdown**: Review the estimated costs for each service.
* **Stud Fee**: If applicable, enter the stud fee.
* **Artificial Insemination Fee**: If applicable, enter the artificial insemination fee.
* **Estimated Veterinary Fees**: If applicable, enter the estimated veterinary fees.
* **Genetic or Health Testing Fees**: If applicable, enter the genetic or health testing fees.
* **Additional Fees**: If any other costs are applicable, enter them here.
* **Total Estimated Cost**: Calculate the total estimated cost by summing up all services and fees.
* **Payment Terms**: Select the payment schedule that applies to your situation.
* **This estimate is valid until**: Enter the date when this estimate is valid.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Review and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Client Acceptance**: Select whether you wish to proceed with services or just receive the estimate.
* **Breeder Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm your acceptance of the estimate.
* **Date**: Enter the date when you sign the form.
