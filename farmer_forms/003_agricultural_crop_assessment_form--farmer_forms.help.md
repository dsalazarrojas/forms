# Agricultural Crop Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agricultural Crop Assessment Form is used to collect information on the health, growth, and condition of crops on a farm. It helps farmers to track the progress of their crops, identify potential issues, and make informed decisions for future growth and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and title in the "Assessor Name" field.
2. Enter the date of the assessment in the "Assessment Date" field.
3. Provide the name of the farm being assessed in the "Farm Name" field.
4. Enter the field identifier (name or number) in the "Field Identifier" field.
5. Enter the size of the field in acres in the "Field Size (acres)" field.
6. Select the specific crop variety in the "Crop Variety" field.
7. Enter the date the crop was planted in the "Planting Date" field.
8. Select the current growth stage of the crop in the "Current Growth Stage" field.
9. Select the overall health condition of the crop in the "Overall Crop Health" field.
10. Indicate the presence of pests in the "Pest Presence" field.
11. Select all pests observed in the "Pest Types Identified" field.
12. Select any disease symptoms observed in the "Disease Symptoms" field.
13. Select the current irrigation status in the "Irrigation Status" field.
14. Select the soil condition in the "Soil Condition" field.
15. Indicate any signs of nutrient deficiency in the "Nutrient Deficiency Signs" field.
16. Select the level of weed pressure in the "Weed Pressure Level" field.
17. Enter the projected yield at harvest in the "Estimated Yield (bushels per acre)" field.
18. Provide recommended actions for the farmer in the "Recommendations" field.
19. Indicate if a follow-up visit is required in the "Follow-up Assessment Needed" field.
20. If a follow-up visit is required, enter the scheduled date in the "Follow-up Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessor Name** (1, text, required): Enter your name and title.
* **Assessment Date** (2, date, required): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Farm Name** (3, text, required): Enter the name of the farm being assessed.
* **Field Identifier** (4, text, required): Enter the field name or number.
* **Field Size (acres)** (5, number, required): Enter the size of the field in acres.
* **Crop Variety** (6, text, required): Select the specific crop variety.
* **Planting Date** (7, date, required): Enter the date the crop was planted.
* **Current Growth Stage** (8, select_one, required): Select the current growth stage of the crop.
* **Overall Crop Health** (9, select_one, required): Select the overall health condition of the crop.
* **Pest Presence** (10, select_one, required): Indicate the presence of pests.
* **Pest Types Identified** (11, select_multiple, required): Select all pests observed.
* **Disease Symptoms** (12, select_multiple, required): Select any disease symptoms observed.
* **Irrigation Status** (13, select_one, required): Select the current irrigation status.
* **Soil Condition** (14, select_one, required): Select the soil condition.
* **Nutrient Deficiency Signs** (15, select_multiple, required): Indicate any signs of nutrient deficiency.
* **Weed Pressure Level** (16, select_one, required): Select the level of weed pressure.
* **Estimated Yield (bushels per acre)** (17, number, required): Enter the projected yield at harvest.
* **Recommendations** (18, text, required): Provide recommended actions for the farmer.
* **Follow-up Assessment Needed** (19, select_one, required): Indicate if a follow-up visit is required.
* **Follow-up Date** (20, date, required): If a follow-up visit is required, enter the scheduled date.
