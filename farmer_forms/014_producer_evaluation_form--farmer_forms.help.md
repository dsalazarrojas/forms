# Producer Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Producer Evaluation Form is used to collect feedback from farmers on their producers. This form gathers information about the farmer's experience, farm details, and evaluation metrics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with your information.
2. Select the relevant options for "Crop Type", "Assigned To", and "Submitted By".
3. Enter your comments and evaluation details in the "Comments" field.
4. Choose a date and time for the "Evaluation Date" and "Evaluation Time" fields.
5. Enter your email and phone number in the "Farmer Email" and "Farmer Phone" fields, respectively.
6. Submit the form once you've filled in all the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Farmer Name** (`farmer_name`, text, **optional**): Enter your name as a farmer.
* **Farm Name** (`farm_name`, text, **optional**): Enter the name of your farm.
* **Crop Type** (`crop_type`, select_one, **optional**): Choose the type of crop you produce (Wheat, Corn, Soybean).
* **Quality Score** (`quality_score`, number, **optional**): Rate the quality of your produce (1-10).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, **optional**): Enter any additional comments or notes about your produce.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, **optional**): Choose the date of your evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, **optional**): Choose the time of your evaluation.
* **Farmer Email** (`farmer_email`, email, **optional**): Enter your farm's email address.
* **Farmer Phone** (`farmer_phone`, text, **optional**): Enter your farm's phone number.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_multiple, **optional**): Choose the person assigned to your evaluation (John, Jane, Jimmy).
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, **optional**): Choose who submitted the evaluation (ChatJimmy, ChatSally, ChatAlice).
* **Producer ID** (`producer_id`, number, **optional**): Enter a unique producer ID (if applicable).
* **Assigned To** is a dropdown menu where you can select multiple people. Please choose all applicable names.
* **Submitted By** is a dropdown menu where you can select one person. Please choose the correct person who submitted the evaluation.
