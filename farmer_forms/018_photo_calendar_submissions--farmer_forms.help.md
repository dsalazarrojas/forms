# photo_calendar_submissions - Help Guide
## Purpose
The photo_calendar_submissions form is designed to collect photo submissions from farmers for a photo calendar or publication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the photo you want to upload from your device or computer.
2. Provide a description of the photo in the "Describe your photo" field.
3. Choose "Yes" in the "Your submission information" field to indicate that you are the submitter of the photo.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
6. Fill in your submission information in the "Submitter" field (note field).
7. Enter your address in the "Address" field (optional).
8. Enter your city in the "City" field (optional).
9. Enter your state in the "State" field (optional).
10. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field (optional).
11. Upload the photo in the "Photo upload" field.
12. Confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions by selecting "Yes" in the "Agreed" field.
