# Strawberry Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Strawberry Order Form is designed to collect customer information, order details, and other relevant details related to strawberry orders for internal use within a farming operation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the customer information section with your name, contact email, and phone number.
2. Specify the details of your order, including the type and quantity of strawberries you require.
3. If needed, provide any additional notes about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information**:
	+ Customer Information (`customer_info`, text, required): Please fill in your name.
* **Order Details**:
	+ Order Details (`order_details`, text, required): Please provide the quantity and type of strawberries you require.
* **Customer Contact**:
	+ Customer Contact (`customer_contact`, text, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Strawberry Type**:
	+ Strawberry Type (`strawberry_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of strawberry you require.
* **Quantity**:
	+ Quantity (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the quantity of strawberries you require.
* **Email**:
	+ Email (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone**:
	+ Phone (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Order Notes**:
	+ Order Notes (`order_notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional notes about your order.
* **Assigned Tool**:
	+ Assigned Tool (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Not currently in use, please ignore.
* **Submitted By**:
	+ Submitted By (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Not currently in use, please ignore.
* **Submitted At**:
	+ Submitted At (`submitted_at`, text, optional): Not currently in use, please ignore.
* **Updated By**:
	+ Updated By (`updated_by`, text, optional): Not currently in use, please ignore.
* **Updated At**:
	+ Updated At (`updated_at`, text, optional): Not currently in use, please ignore.
* **Created At**:
	+ Created At (`created_at`, text, optional): Not currently in use, please ignore.
* **Created By**:
	+ Created By (`created_by`, text, optional): Not currently in use, please ignore.

## Tips
- Ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
- Double-check the details you enter for accuracy.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer back to this guide for clarification.

Please note that fields not currently in use should be ignored. They are included in the form for internal use only.
