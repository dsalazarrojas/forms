# farmers_personal_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal information from farmers, likely for administrative or contact purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Farm Location (farm_location)**: A free-text field where you can enter the name or description of your farm or farm location. This field is not required to fill out.
* **Name (name)**: A field to collect your name as it appears on your personal documents such as ID or passport. This field is not required to fill out.
* **Address (address)**: A field to enter your farm's physical address, including street, apt, or rural address. This field is not required to fill out.
* **Phone number (phone_number)**: A field to enter your phone number for contact purposes. This field is not required to fill out.
* **Email (email)**: A field to enter your email address for contact purposes. This field is not required to fill out.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter your information accurately and completely, as this form may be used for official purposes.
- It is recommended to fill out all fields, even if not required, for a more comprehensive record.
- If you have any issues or concerns while filling out this form, please reach out to the contact mentioned above.
