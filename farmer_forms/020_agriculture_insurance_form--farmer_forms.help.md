# Agriculture Insurance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from farmers about their agricultural operations to apply for insurance coverage. The form will help us to assess your eligibility for insurance coverage and to determine the appropriate premium rates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the applicant's name in the "Applicant Name" field.
2. Select the type of insurance coverage you are requesting (e.g. Crop insurance, Livestock insurance, Equipment insurance, or Multi-peril insurance).
3. Enter the area of land to be insured (in hectares) and/or the value of crops to be insured (optional).
4. Enter the requested start date of the insurance coverage.
5. Select the perils to include in the policy (e.g. Drought, Flood, Pest outbreak, Fire, or Theft).
6. Enter any previous claims history (if applicable).
7. If you are using an insurance broker, enter their name.
8. Enter your contact phone number for policy communications.
9. Finally, review and sign the declaration and signature field to confirm that the information provided is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Policy Type** (`policy_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of insurance coverage you are requesting (e.g. Crop insurance, Livestock insurance, Equipment insurance, or Multi-peril insurance).
* **Insured Area in Hectares** (`insured_area_hectares`, number, optional): Enter the area of land to be insured. This field is optional, but will help us to determine your premium rates.
* **Value of Crops** (`value_of_crops`, number, optional): Enter the estimated monetary value of the crops to be insured. This field is optional.
* **Coverage Start Date** (`coverage_start_date`, date, required): Enter the requested start date of the insurance coverage.
* **Perils to Cover** (`perils_to_cover`, select_multiple, required): Select the perils you want included in the policy (e.g. Drought, Flood, Pest outbreak, Fire, or Theft).
* **Previous Claims History** (`previous_claims`, text, optional): Enter any prior insurance claims history (if applicable).
* **Broker Name** (`broker_name`, text, optional): If you are using an insurance broker, enter their name.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number for policy communications.
* **Declaration and Signature** (`declaration_signature`, note, required): Review and sign the declaration to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
