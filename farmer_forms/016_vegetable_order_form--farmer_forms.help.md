# Vegetable Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vegetable Order Form is used to collect customer information for orders of vegetables from our farm. It is meant for customers who want to place an order with our farm.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Fill out the form with your information, including your name, order date, address, email, and phone number.
* If you're not sure about any of the fields, you can leave them blank.
* Once you've filled out the form, click the submit button to send your order to our farm.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Please enter your name so we can contact you regarding your order.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date you'd like to order or would like us to deliver your vegetables. This helps us plan and process your order accordingly.
* **Address**: Enter your address so we can deliver your order to your doorstep.
* **Email**: If you'd like us to contact you via email, please enter your email address.
* **Phone**: If you'd like us to call you for any reason, please enter your phone number.

## Tips
* You can leave any fields blank if you're not sure about them.
* Please ensure that you enter your email and phone number correctly so we can contact you smoothly.
* If you have any special requests or questions, please feel free to call us or email us using the information you provided.
