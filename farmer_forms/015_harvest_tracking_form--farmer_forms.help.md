# Harvest Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a plant's harvest, including details about the plant, harvest date and time, plot and farm IDs, and additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Plant Name** field with the name of the plant that was harvested.
2. Enter the **Harvest Date** in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
3. Enter the **Harvest Time** in the format of hh:mm:ss.
4. Fill in the **Plot ID** field with the ID number for the plot where the harvest took place.
5. Enter the **Crop ID** field with the ID number for the crop that was harvested.
6. Check the **Farmer Information** field to indicate whether the farmer has provided information or not.
7. If applicable, select the **Location** field with the correct location where the harvest took place.
8. Check the **Weather** field to indicate the weather conditions at the time of harvest.
9. Enter the **Harvest Weight** field with the weight of the harvest.
10. Enter the **Farm ID** field with the ID number for the farm where the harvest took place.
11. Fill in the **Notes** fields (if any) with any additional details or comments about the harvest.
