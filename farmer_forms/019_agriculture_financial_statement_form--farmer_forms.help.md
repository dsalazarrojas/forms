# Agriculture Financial Statement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect financial information for agricultural businesses or individuals to facilitate tax returns, financial planning, and business evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Owner or business name** field with the legal name of the farm or business.
2. Enter the **Reporting period start date** and **Reporting period end date** to define the financial reporting period.
3. Enter the **Total revenue** and **Total expenses** fields with accurate figures.
4. The **Net income** field will automatically calculate as revenue minus expenses.
5. If applicable, list **Major assets** and **Liabilities summary** with accurate values.
6. Provide contact details in the **Accountant contact** field, if needed.
7. Carefully confirm the **Declaration of accuracy** with accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Owner or business name
* **Owner or business name**: Enter the legal name of the farm or business. (Required)

### Reporting period start date
* **Reporting period start date**: Enter the start date of the financial reporting period. (Required)

### Reporting period end date
* **Reporting period end date**: Enter the end date of the financial reporting period. (Required)

### Total revenue
* **Total revenue**: Enter the gross income for the period. (Required)

### Total expenses
* **Total expenses**: Enter the operating costs during the period. (Required)

### Net income
* **Net income**: Automatically calculated as revenue minus expenses. (Not required but calculated for reference)

### Major assets
* **Major assets**: Optional. List the values of land, machinery, or livestock. (Optional)

### Liabilities summary
* **Liabilities summary**: Optional. Summarize loans and outstanding debts. (Optional)

### Accountant contact
* **Accountant contact**: Optional. Provide contact details for the accountant or preparer. (Optional)

### Declaration of accuracy
* **Declaration of accuracy**: Confirm that information is accurate to the best of your knowledge. (Required)

## Tips
- Please fill in all required fields accurately.
- You may skip optional fields if not applicable.
- Review the form carefully before submission.
