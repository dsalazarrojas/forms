# Daily Agricultural Data Collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Agricultural Data Collection form is designed to collect and track daily data from farmers, allowing for the monitoring and analysis of changes in weather, soil conditions, and temperature that can impact crop growth and yield.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form for each day with the required and relevant information for your farm.
2. Make sure to select the correct "Assigned To" person, who will receive the report.
3. Use the "Submit" field to finalize and send the report to the assigned person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Farm Id (`1`, `number`, required):** Enter the unique identifier for your farm.
* **Crop Name (`2`, `text`, optional):** Enter the name of the crop you are reporting on, if applicable.
* **Date (`3`, `date`, optional):** Enter the date you are reporting for, in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Weather (`4`, `text`, optional):** Describe the current weather conditions on your farm.
* **Temperature (`5`, `number`, optional):** Enter the current temperature in your area.
* **Soil Condition (`6`, `text`, optional):** Describe the current condition of your soil.
* **Notes (`7`, `text`, optional):** Enter any additional comments or notes about your farm or observations.
* **Assigned To (`8`, `select_multiple`, required):** Select the person to whom you will be sending this report.
* **Submit (`9`, `select_one`, required):** Select the action you wish to perform with your report, such as "Submit Form", "Save Draft", or "Cancel".
