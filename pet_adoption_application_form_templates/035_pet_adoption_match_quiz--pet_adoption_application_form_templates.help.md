# Pet Adoption Match Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Adoption Match Quiz is designed to help potential pet adopters find the perfect pet that suits their lifestyle and preferences. The form asks questions about various aspects of pet care to ensure that the adopter and pet are compatible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Pet Adoption Match Quiz, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer based on your honest assessment of your pet care preferences.
2. For each question, select the answer that best matches your situation.
3. Make sure to fill in all required fields (indicated by a star *) to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Meet The Adopter (1)
* **Meet The Adopter** (text): This field is an optional description of yourself as an adopter. Please provide a brief introduction, including your age, location, and any relevant pet experience.

### What type of pet do you want to adopt? (2)
* **What type of pet do you want to adopt?** (select_one): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Dog
	+ Cat
	+ Bird
	+ Fish

### What energy level are you looking for? (3)
* **What energy level are you looking for?** (select_one): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Low
	+ Medium
	+ High

### What size pet do you want? (4)
* **What size pet do you want?** (select_multiple): Choose one or more of the following options:
	+ Small
	+ Medium
	+ Large

### How much space do you have available? (5)
* **How much space do you have available?** (select_one): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Small
	+ Medium
	+ Large

### What noise level are you comfortable with? (6)
* **What noise level are you comfortable with?** (select_one): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Quiet
	+ Moderate
	+ Loud

### Have you had a pet before? (7)
* **Have you had a pet before?** (select_one): 
	+ False
	+ True

### How many hours per day do you have to spend with your pet? (8)
* **How many hours per day do you have to spend with your pet?** (text): Provide a number of hours you have available to spend with your pet.

### How many family members are there in your household? (9)
* **How many family members are there in your household?** (number): Enter the number of family members living with you.

### What is your pet budget? (10)
* **What is your pet budget?** (number): Enter the maximum amount you are willing to spend on pet care per month.

### How would you like to be contacted? (11)
* **How would you like to be contacted?** (select_multiple): Choose one or more of the following options:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Chat

### Is there an emergency contact person? (12)
* **Is there an emergency contact person?** (select_one): 
	+ True
	+ False

By following this guide, you will be able to complete the Pet Adoption Match Quiz accurately and effectively, and find the perfect pet for your lifestyle and preferences.
