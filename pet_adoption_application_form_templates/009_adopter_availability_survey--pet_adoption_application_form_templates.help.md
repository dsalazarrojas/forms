# Adopter Availability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your availability and preferences to help us find the best match for you and your family.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Answer all questions with your best judgment.
3. If you have any doubts or questions, contact us for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. Full Name**: Please provide your full name as you would like it to be listed on the pet's adoption papers.
* **2. Email Address**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about the pet's adoption and any updates.
* **3. Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you in case of emergencies or schedule changes.
* **4. Work Schedule and Availability**: This field is a note field where you can describe your work schedule and availability in a few sentences.
* **5. Current Employment Status**: Select your current employment status.
	+ Employed full-time
	+ Employed part-time
	+ Self-employed
	+ Retired
	+ Stay-at-home
	+ Student
	+ Unemployed
* **6. Typical Work Hours**: If applicable, provide your typical work hours.
* **7. Weekday Availability for Pet Care**: Select the times of the day you are available to care for pets on weekdays.
	+ Before work (6am-9am)
	+ Mid-day (12pm-1pm)
	+ After work (5pm-7pm)
	+ Evening (7pm-10pm)
	+ Not available weekdays
* **8. Weekend Availability**: Select the times of the day you are available to care for pets on weekends.
	+ Saturday morning
	+ Saturday afternoon
	+ Saturday evening
	+ Sunday morning
	+ Sunday afternoon
	+ Sunday evening
* **9. How Often Do You Take Vacations?**: Select how often you take vacations.
	+ Never
	+ 1-2 weeks per year
	+ 2-4 weeks per year
	+ Monthly
	+ Several times per year
* **10. Who Cares for Your Pet When You Travel?**: Select who takes care of your pet when you travel.
	+ I have a trusted pet sitter
	+ I board my pet at a facility
	+ I take them with me
	+ I ask family or friends
	+ I do not travel
* **11. Contact Preferences**: This field is a note field where you can describe your preferred method of contact and frequency.
* **12. Preferred Method of Contact**: Select your preferred method of contact.
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Text message
	+ In-person
* **13. Best Time to Contact You**: Select the best time to contact you.
	+ Morning (6am-12pm)
	+ Afternoon (12pm-5pm)
	+ Evening (5pm-10pm)
	+ Weekends only
	+ Anytime
* **14. Preferred Communication Frequency**: Select the frequency of communication you prefer.
	+ As needed only
	+ Weekly updates
	+ Monthly updates
	+ Quarterly follow-ups
* **15. Please Summarize Your Typical Weekly Schedule**: In this text field, briefly describe your typical weekly schedule.
* **16. Average Hours Pet Would Be Alone per Day**: If applicable, provide the average hours the pet would be alone per day.
* **17. Do You Have Backup Care Available?**: Select if you have backup care available for your pet.
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Not sure
* **18. Can You Adjust Your Schedule for Pet Needs?**: Select how flexible you are with your schedule for pet care.
	+ Yes, very flexible
	+ Somewhat flexible
	+ Not very flexible
* **19. Are You Available for Emergency Pet Needs?**: Select if you are available for emergency pet needs.
	+ Yes, always
	+ Yes, most times
	+ Sometimes
	+ False
