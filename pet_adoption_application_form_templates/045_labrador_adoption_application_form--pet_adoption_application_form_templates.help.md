# Labrador Adoption Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Labrador Adoption Application Form is a questionnaire designed to gather essential information from potential adopters to facilitate the adoption process of Labradors. Completing this form will help us understand your preferences and determine if you're a suitable match for a labradors adoption.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Contact Info Form, which requests general contact information.
2. Provide information about the labrador you are interested in adopting in the Labrador Info Form.
3. Share your adoption preferences and expectations in the Adoption Info Form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Info Form (ID 1, Label: Contact Info Form, Type: text, Required: false)**: Please provide your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Labrador Info Form (ID 2, Label: Labrador Info Form, Type: text, Required: false)**: Share as much detail as you can about the labrador(s) you are interested in adopting, such as the breed, size, age, and color.
* **Adoption Info Form (ID 3, Label: Adoption Info Form, Type: text, Required: false)**: Explain your adoption preferences and expectations, including what you are looking for in an adoption experience and any specific needs you may have.

## Tips
* Ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* Be as specific as possible when sharing about the labradors you are interested in.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
