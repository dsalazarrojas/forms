# pet_adoption_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help individuals who are interested in adopting a pet, providing a structured and organized way to collect necessary information for the adoption process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form and understand what information is required.
2. Fill in your contact details, including your name, email, and phone number.
3. Provide information about the pet you are interested in adopting, including its name, species, and breed.
4. Enter your address to help the adoption organization verify your identity and contact you.
5. Indicate your pet preferences, such as whether you are looking for a specific type of pet or any pet-related allergies.
6. Add any additional comments about yourself or your situation that may be relevant to the adoption process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name, email, and phone number so that we can contact you about the adoption process.
* **Pet Details** (`pet_details`, `text`, required): Provide information about the pet you are interested in adopting, including its name, species, and breed.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your address to help us verify your identity and contact you.
* **Pet Preferences** (`pet_preferences`, `text`, required): Indicate your pet preferences, such as whether you are looking for a specific type of pet or any pet-related allergies.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments about yourself or your situation that may be relevant to the adoption process.

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Make sure to provide enough information to help the adoption organization understand your needs and preferences.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your interest in adopting a pet and look forward to hearing from you!
