# Simple Pet Adoption Application Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential adopters about themselves and their pet's details to facilitate a smooth pet adoption experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your personal details, including your name, contact email, and address.
2. Provide information about your household, including household income and size.
3. Choose the type and size of pet you're interested in adopting.
4. Describe your vehicle type and model (if applicable).
5. Answer any other questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false)
Your first name is the first name given to you at birth or the name you prefer to be called.

*   Please enter your first name.

### 2. **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false)
