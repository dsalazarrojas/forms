# Wolfdog Adoption Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wolfdog Adoption Application Form is a tool used to collect information from potential adopters interested in adopting a pet from the Wolfdog organization. The form is designed to ensure that adopters are matched with the best possible pet companion for their lifestyle and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the User Information section, providing your name, contact details, and any other relevant user information.
2. In the Contact Information section, provide your contact details, including a contact name, phone number, and email address.
3. The Household Information section is where you should describe your household, including the number of people living with you, their ages, and any relevant information about your living situation.
4. In the Pet Information section, indicate whether you have any pets at home and if so, provide information about them.
5. The Medical Information section is optional but recommended for those with medical conditions or concerns.
6. In the Pet Conditions section, select 'Yes' if you are willing to adopt a pet with specific conditions.
7. The Pet Species section is where you select the type of pet you are interested in adopting (e.g., dog, cat, etc.).
8. In the Adoption Date section, provide the date you are interested in adopting a pet.
9. The Adoption Type section allows you to select the type of adoption you are interested in (e.g., permanent, foster, etc.).
10. If applicable, fill out the Adoption Fee section with the agreed-upon adoption fee in the selected currency.
11. Finally, provide any additional User Notes if you have any further information or comments.
12. If desired, you can add Staff Notes for the adoption team to reference during the adoption process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (User Information, text, required): This section is where you provide your basic contact information, such as name and contact details.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, required): In this section, you are asked to provide your contact name, phone number, and email address.
* **Household Information** (Household Information, text, optional): This section is intended to provide information about those living with you, such as their ages and roles within your household.
* **Pet Information** (Pet Information, text, required): Please describe any pets currently living with you.
* **Medical Information** (Medical Information, text, optional): If you or any family members have medical conditions or concerns, you can provide this information here.
* **Pet Conditions** (Pet Conditions, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you are willing to adopt a pet with specific conditions.
* **Pet Species** (Pet Species, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of pet you are interested in adopting (e.g., dog, cat, etc.).
* **Adoption Date** (Adoption Date, date, required): Provide the date you are interested in adopting a pet.
* **Adoption Type** (Adoption Type, select_one, optional): Select the type of adoption you are interested in (e.g., permanent, foster, etc.).
* **Adoption Fee** (Adoption Fee, number, optional): If applicable, fill out the agreed-upon adoption fee in the selected currency.
* **Adoption Fee Currency** (Adoption Fee Currency, select_one, optional): Select the currency of the adoption fee.
* **User Notes** (User Notes, text, optional): Provide any additional information or comments.
* **Staff Notes** (Staff Notes, text, optional): You can add any notes for the adoption team to reference during the adoption process.
