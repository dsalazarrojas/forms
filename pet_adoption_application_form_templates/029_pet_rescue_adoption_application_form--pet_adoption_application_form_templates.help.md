# Pet Rescue Adoption Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Rescue Adoption Application Form is a crucial document that helps us get to know you and your pet better. It will be reviewed by our team to determine the best match between you and our available pets. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal information:
	* **User Name**: Enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
	* **Contact Number**: Provide your phone number where we can reach you.
	* **Email**: Enter your email address where we can send updates and information.
2. Provide information about your pet:
	* **Pet Name**: Enter the name of the pet you are interested in adopting.
	* **Pet Type**: Choose the type of pet you are interested in adopting (Cat, Dog, or Other).
3. Share your adoption story:
	* **Adoption Date**: The date you are interested in adopting your pet.
	* **Adoption Reason**: Tell us why you want to adopt a pet from our rescue.
	* **Rescue Location**: If you have a preferred location for the adoption, please let us know.
4. Information about your household:
	* **Owner Name**: If this is a joint application, enter the name of all household members.
	* **Owner Contact**: Provide contact information for all household members.
	* **Owner Address**: Enter your home address.
	* **Owner City**: Enter the city where you live.
	* **Owner State**: Choose the state where you live from the list.
	* **Owner Zip**: Enter your zip code.
	* **Owner Phone**: Provide your phone number.
	* **Owner Relationship**: Indicate how you are related to the owner (Friend, Family Member, etc.).
5. Information about your pet:
	* **Pet Breed**: If your pet has a breed, enter it here.
	* **Pet Age**: Enter your pet's age.
	* **Pet Weight**: Enter your pet's weight.
	* **Pet Description**: Provide a brief description of your pet.
6. Final steps:
	* **Pet Surrender Date**: The date you are willing to surrender your pet to our rescue (if applicable).
	* **Vet Records**: If applicable, provide veterinary records for your pet.
	* **Microchip Status**: If your pet is microchipped, let us know.
	* **Spay Status**: If your pet is spayed/neutered, let us know.
	* **Signature**: Please sign below to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
