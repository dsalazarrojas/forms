# Cat Adoption Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Cat Adoption Form is designed to gather information from potential adopters to ensure a smooth and successful adoption process for both the adopter and the cat. This form helps us understand your lifestyle, preferences, and needs, which is essential for matching you with the right feline companion.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by reviewing each section carefully and answering all the required questions.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to help us understand your situation.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name (id: 2)**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address (id: 3)**: Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes. Please ensure it's a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number (id: 4)**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you for any further information or updates. Please ensure it's a valid phone number that we can use to call or text you.
* **Mailing Address (id: 5)**: Enter your street address, city, state, and zip code for our records. This is important for us to send any necessary documents or communicate with you.
* **Type of Residence (id: 7)**: Select the type of residence you currently live in, such as a house, apartment, condo, or mobile home.
* **Do you own or rent (id: 8)**: Indicate whether you own or rent your current residence. This helps us understand your housing situation.
* **Are there children in the household (id: 9)**: If you have children, please list their ages. This information helps us ensure the cat's safety and well-being.
* **Do you currently have other pets (id: 10)**: If you have other pets, please list their species and whether they are spayed/neutered. This helps us understand your pet management experience.
* **Name of cat or breed you are interested in (id: 12)**: Enter the name of the specific cat or breed you are interested in adopting.
* **Why are you interested in adopting at this time (id: 13)**: Please explain why you want to adopt a cat at this time. This helps us understand your motivations and preferences.
* **How many hours will the cat be alone each day (id: 14)**: Select the number of hours you will be away from home each day. This helps us understand the cat's social needs.
* **Will the cat be kept indoors or outdoors (id: 15)**: Indicate whether you plan to keep your cat indoors or outdoors. This helps us ensure the cat's safety and well-being.
* **Name and Phone of your current Veterinarian (id: 16)**: Enter the name and phone number of your current veterinarian. If you don't have one, list the one you plan to use.
* **I understand that there is an adoption fee (id: 17)**: Confirm that you understand that there is an adoption fee associated with adopting a cat.
* **I agree to a follow-up visit or call after adoption (id: 18)**: Confirm that you agree to a follow-up visit or call after adopting a cat to ensure a smooth transition.

## Tips

* Please answer all required fields to ensure a complete and accurate submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask us for assistance.
* Be honest and provide accurate information to help us match you with the right feline companion.
