# Puppy Adoption Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Puppy Adoption Form is designed to collect information from potential adopters about themselves and their preferences for adopting a puppy. This form helps the adoption team assess the adopter's qualifications and ensure a successful adoption process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact details, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your address and city.
3. Select your preferences for the size and breed of the puppy you are interested in adopting.
4. Enter your veterinarian's information, including their name and whether your pet is vaccinated.
5. Sign and add any additional notes, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Info** (1): Enter your name.
* **Contact Details** (2): Enter your email address and phone number.
* **Address** (3): Enter your address.
* **Owner Preferences** (4): Describe your preferences for the size of the puppy you are interested in adopting.
* **Age** (5): Enter your age.
* **Breed** (6): Select one or multiple breeds that interest you.
* **Size** (7): Select the size of the puppy you are interested in adopting.
* **Pet Details** (8): Enter a description of the type of pet you are interested in adopting.
* **Type** (9): Select the type of pet you are interested in adopting (Dog, Cat, or Other).
* **Pet Details Breed** (10): Select one or multiple breeds for the type of pet you are interested in adopting.
* **Pet Details Size** (11): Select the size of the pet you are interested in adopting.
* **Vet Info** (12): Enter your veterinarian's name.
* **Name** (13): Enter your veterinarian's name.
* **Adopted** (14): Enter the date your pet was adopted.
* **Vaccinated** (15): Select whether your pet is vaccinated.
* **License** (16): Enter your pet's license number.
* **Owner Signature** (17): Sign your name to confirm your adoption application.
* **Owner Notes** (18): Add any additional notes about your pet's adoption.
* **Submit** (19): Select "Submit" to send your adoption application.
* **E-mail** (20): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (21): Enter your phone number.
* **Other Notes** (22): Add any other notes about your adoption application.
* **Owner Info Address** (23): Enter your address.
* **City** (24): Enter your city.
* **State** (25): Enter your state.
