# Dog Rescue Adoption Matching Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dog Rescue Adoption Matching Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in adopting a dog from a pet rescue organization. This form helps match the individual with available dogs based on their preferences and the dog's characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your name and email address.
3. Provide your phone number.
4. Enter the name of the dog you are interested in adopting.
5. Select the dog's breed.
6. Specify the dog's age.
7. Select the dog's size.
8. Check the adoption status of the dog (available for adoption or adopted).
9. Enter any additional information about the rescue organization (if applicable).
10. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Dog Name**: Enter the name of the dog you are interested in adopting.
* **Breed**: Select the breed of the dog you are interested in adopting.
* **Age**: Enter the age of the dog you are interested in adopting.
* **Size**: Select the size of the dog you are interested in adopting.
* **Adoption Status**: Check the box if the dog is available for adoption or already adopted.
* **Rescue Organization Information**: Enter any additional information about the rescue organization, if applicable.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact the rescue organization.
* The dog you are interested in adopting may not be available at the time of submission, but we will get back to you as soon as possible if they are available.
* If you are not available for adoption, please let us know and we will keep your information on file for future reference.
