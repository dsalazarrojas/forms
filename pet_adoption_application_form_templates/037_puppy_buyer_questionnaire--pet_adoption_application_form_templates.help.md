# Puppy Buyer Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Puppy Buyer Questionnaire is a form designed to gather essential information from individuals or families interested in adopting a pet from our organization. The purpose of this form is to help us better understand your household, family dynamics, and preferences to provide the best possible match between you and your new furry friend.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review this guide to understand each section and ensure you answer all required fields.
2.  Answer each question carefully and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
3.  If you are unsure about any question, don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
4.  Once you have completed the form, submit it to us and a member of our team will review your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **household_info** (Text, Optional): Please describe your home and living situation, including the number and age of household members, pets, and any previous pet experience.
* **family_info** (Text, Optional): Provide information about your family, such as their ages, occupations, and any relevant family history or dynamics.
* **household_members** (Number, Optional): If you have any family members or roommates, please list their ages.
* **household_income** (Number, Optional): This field is for estimating your household's annual income.
* **pet_experience** (Select Multiple, Optional):
	+ Option 1: You have prior experience with pets and are familiar with their care.
	+ Option 2: You have some pet experience, but are still learning.
	+ Option 3: You are new to pet ownership but willing to learn.
* **family_relationships** (Select Multiple, Optional):
	+ Option 4: You live with a partner/spouse.
	+ Option 5: You have children/dependents.
	+ Option 6: You live alone or have no dependents.
* **desired_breed** (Select One, Optional):
	+ Option 7: You prefer a small-breed dog (under 20 pounds).
	+ Option 8: You prefer a medium-breed dog (20-50 pounds).
	+ Option 9: You prefer a large-breed dog (over 50 pounds).
* **desired_size** (Select One, Optional):
	+ Option 10: You prefer a puppy/small adult dog (under 2 years).
	+ Option 11: You prefer an adult dog (over 2 years).
	+ Option 12: You are open to adopting any age or size dog.
* **adoption_date** (Date, Optional): If you have a specific date in mind for adoption, please provide it. Our team will do our best to accommodate your request.
* **contact_info** (Text, Optional): Leave any additional contact information or comments, such as your preferred method of contact or any special requests.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when answering each question to help us better understand your needs.
* If you have any additional comments or concerns, please include them in the contact info field.
* Our team will review your application carefully and contact you with any questions or concerns.
* We appreciate your patience and understanding as we process your application.
* A member of our team will contact you once your application has been processed.
