# Pig Adoption Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pig Adoption Application Form is designed to gather information about you and your household to assess your suitability for adopting a pig. This form will help us understand your personal details, household, experience with pets, contact information, adoption preferences, pet care abilities, and any medical conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details in the "Personal info" field.
2. Provide information about your household in the "Household info" field.
3. Share your experience with pets in the "Experience with pets" field.
4. Enter your contact information in the "Contact info" field.
5. Specify your adoption preference in the "Adoption preference" field.
6. Share any information about your pet care experience and abilities in the "Pet care" field.
7. List any medical conditions you or your household members may have in the "Medical conditions" and "Medical conditions info" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal info** (`Personal info`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, phone number, and email address for contact purposes.
* **Household info** (`Household info`, text, required/optional): Describe your living situation, including the number of people living in your household and any pets already living with you.
* **Experience with pets** (`Experience with pets`, text, required/optional): Share your experience with animals, including the types of animals you've cared for and any notable experiences.
* **Contact info** (`Contact info`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, address, phone number, and email address so we can contact you.
* **Adoption preference** (`Adoption preference`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of adoption you're interested in:
	+ Pet
	+ Animal
	+ Service
* **Pet care** (`Pet care`, text, required/optional): Describe your experience and abilities with caring for animals, including feeding, grooming, and veterinary visits.
* **Medical conditions** (`Medical conditions`, text, required/optional): List any medical conditions you or your household members may have.
* **Medical conditions info** (`Medical conditions info`, text, required/optional): Provide additional information about any medical conditions you or your household members may have.
