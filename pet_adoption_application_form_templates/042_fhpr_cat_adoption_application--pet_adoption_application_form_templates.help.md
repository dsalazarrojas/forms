# FHPR Cat Adoption Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This help guide explains how to complete the FHPR Cat Adoption Application form. The form is designed to gather information about you and your pet preferences, as well as your own contact information and adoption preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name (username) in the "Username" field. This will help us identify you in the adoption process.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. We will use this to contact you about your adoption application and any updates on your potential adoption.
3. Choose a name for your cat in the "Pet Name" field. This can be any name you like, but please keep it simple and not too long.
4. Select the breed of cat you are interested in adopting from the "Breed" dropdown menu. Please choose from the options provided.
5. Choose the sex of the cat you are interested in adopting from the "Sex" dropdown menu. Please choose from the options provided.
6. Enter the age of the cat you are interested in adopting in the "Age" field. Please enter the age of the cat in years.
7. Enter the weight of the cat you are interested in adopting in the "Weight" field. Please enter the weight of the cat in pounds.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This is how we will contact you for any updates or questions.
9. Enter your street address in the "Street Address" field. This is where we will send any mail about your adoption application.
10. Enter the city where you reside in the "City" field.
11. Enter the state where you reside in the "State" field.
12. Enter your zip code in the "Zip Code" field.
13. Describe why you want to adopt a cat from the "Adoption Reason" field. Please be as specific as possible.
14. Select the date you would like to adopt a cat from the "Adoption Date" field. Please enter the date you would like to adopt in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
15. Select the type of pet you are interested in adopting from the "Adoption Type" dropdown menu. Please choose from the options provided.
16. Select your adoption status from the "Adoption Status" dropdown menu. Please choose from the options provided.
17. If you have any other details that we should know about your adoption preference, please enter them in the "Other Details" field. This is optional.
18. In the "Declaration Text" field, please type a brief statement agreeing to the terms and conditions of adoption.
19. In the "Declaration Agree" field, please select "Agree" if you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of adoption.
20. In the "Declaration Date" field, please enter the date you agree to the terms and conditions of adoption in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
21. Finally, click the "Submit" button to submit your adoption application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Username** (text, required): Enter your name as you would like to be referred to in the adoption process.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your adoption application.
* **Pet Name** (text, required): Choose a name for your potential pet.
* **Breed** (text, required): Select the breed of cat you are interested in adopting.
* **Sex** (select_one, required): Choose the sex of the cat you are interested in adopting.
* **Age** (number, required): Enter the age of the cat you are interested in adopting.
* **Weight** (number, required): Enter the weight of the cat you are interested in adopting.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for any updates or questions.
* **Street Address** (text, required): Enter the address where we will send any mail about your adoption application.
* **City** (text, required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (text, required): Enter the state where you reside.
* **Zip Code** (text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Adoption Reason** (text, required): Describe why you want to adopt a cat from the FHPR organization.
* **Adoption Date** (date, required): Enter the date you would like to adopt a cat.
* **Adoption Type** (select_one, required): Choose the type of pet you are interested in adopting.
* **Adoption Status** (select_one, required): Choose your adoption status.
* **Other Details** (note, optional): If you have any other details about your adoption preference, enter them here.
* **Declaration Text** (text, required): Type a brief statement agreeing to the terms and conditions of adoption.
* **Declaration Agree** (select_one, required): Select "Agree" if you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of adoption.
* **Declaration Date** (date, required): Enter the date you agree to the terms and conditions of adoption.
* **Submit** (text, required): Click this button to submit your adoption application.
