# pet_adoption_custody_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pet adoption custody form is designed to collect information from potential adopters about their contact details, pet preferences, household circumstances, and expectations. This form helps the adopter to be matched with a suitable pet that meets their needs and lifestyle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your contact details in the first field.
2. In the second field, specify your preferences for a pet (e.g., breed, size, age, etc.).
3. Describe your household information, including the number of people living in the household and any pets you already have.
4. Provide a brief statement about your commitment to caring for a pet.
5. Finally, outline your expectations for pet care and responsibility.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details**: Please enter your contact information, including your name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Puppy Preference**: Describe the type of pet you are interested in adopting, including breed, size, age, and other relevant details.
* **Household Info**: Describe your household, including the number of people living in the household, the age and type of pets you already have, and any relevant household details.
* **Ownership Commitment**: Please provide a brief statement about your commitment to caring for a pet, including your experience with pets and why you are prepared to take on pet care responsibilities.
* **Expectations**: Outline your expectations for pet care, including the type of care and attention you expect to provide for your future pet.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete all fields accurately and honestly.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact the organization for guidance.
- Review and submit the form once you have completed it to ensure all information is accurate and complete.
