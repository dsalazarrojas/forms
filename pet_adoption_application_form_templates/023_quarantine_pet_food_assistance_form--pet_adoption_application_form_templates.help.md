# Quarantine Pet Food Assistance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to provide essential information to support pet food assistance during the quarantine period. It is intended for pet owners who are seeking help with feeding their pets while ensuring they receive the necessary care and nutrition.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete the form, please go through the following steps:
	+ Select the relevant information in each field and type it in accurately.
	+ Ensure all required fields are filled in as they are marked with \*.
	+ Submit the completed form for review and further assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Pet Food Information** (Required)
    The first field is to provide a brief description of the pet's food needs and preferences.
*   **Pet Type** (Required)
    Please select the type of pet you own from the options provided (Dog, Cat, or Other).
*   **Pet Name** (Not Required)
    Enter your pet's name so we can identify them accurately.
*   **Owner Email** (Required)
    Enter your email address so we can contact you with further assistance and updates.
*   **Owner Phone** (Required)
    Enter your phone number so we can contact you in case of any queries or updates.
*   **Food Frequency** (Required)
    Select how often you would like food assistance to be delivered to your pet (Once a day, Twice a day, or Three times a day).
*   **Special Diet** (Not Required)
    If your pet has any special dietary needs or requirements, please describe them here.
*   **Assistance Amount** (Not Required)
    If applicable, please specify the quantity of food assistance you are requesting.
*   **Assistance Period** (Not Required)
    If known, please specify the date when you will need this assistance to end.
*   **Notes** (Not Required)
    Add any additional information that can help us better assist you and your pet.

## Tips

*   Please ensure accurate and up-to-date information to help us provide the best assistance possible.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
*   We will get back to you as soon as possible with further instructions and updates.
