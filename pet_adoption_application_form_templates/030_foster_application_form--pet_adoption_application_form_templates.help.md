# Foster Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Foster Application Form is a crucial step in the animal shelter's adoption process. It helps us understand your qualifications, availability, and experience to ensure that you can provide a suitable home for a foster pet.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly.
2. Choose the options that best describe your situation and preferences.
3. Review your submission carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Basic Information
* **Basic Information**: This section is a required field where you provide your basic information, including your name and contact details. Please fill in your name, address, and phone number accurately.
### Pet Information
* **Pet Information**: Select the type of pet you're interested in fostering (Dogs or Cats).
### Pet Count
* **How many pets do you currently have in your household?**: Choose the number of pets you have, which will help us evaluate your ability to care for a foster pet.
### Address
* **What is your address?**: Enter your address accurately so we can contact you if needed.
### Phone Number
* **What is your phone number?**: Enter your phone number to ensure we can reach you in case of an emergency.
### Availability
* **What days are you available to foster a pet?**: Choose the days you're available to care for a pet, which will help us match you with a pet that fits your schedule.
* **How many hours a day are you available to care for a pet?**: Select the number of hours you can dedicate to caring for a pet each day.
### Pet Care Experience
* **What is your pet care experience?**: Choose your level of experience in caring for pets, which will help us evaluate your ability to care for a foster pet.
### Special Needs
* **Have you had any experience with pets that have special needs?**: Be honest about your experience with pets that require extra care.
### Medical Conditions
* **Do you have any medical conditions that may impact your ability to care for a pet?**: If you have any health conditions that may affect your ability to care for a pet, let us know so we can discuss it further.
### Medication
* **Do you take any medications?**: Be honest about any medications you're taking.
### Emergency Contact
* **Who is your emergency contact person?**: Provide your emergency contact person's name and phone number.
### Emergency Contact Phone
* **What is their phone number?**: Enter their phone number so we can reach them in case of an emergency.
### Returned Pets
* **Have you had any pets surrendered to a shelter in the past?**: If yes, what was the reason?
### Abuse/Neglect
* **Have you ever had a pet returned or removed due to neglect or abuse?**: Be honest about any previous situations with pets.
### Veterinary Clinic
* **Do you have a vet?**: If you have a veterinarian you regularly visit, please select Option A. 
### Insurance
* **Do you have pet insurance?**: If you have pet insurance, please select Option A.
### Review and Agreement
* **Please review your submission carefully before submitting it.**: Double-check your information and ensure it's accurate.
* **Do you agree to the terms and conditions of the foster program?**: Confirm your understanding and agreement with the foster program's terms and conditions.

Please fill out the form carefully and honestly, and we'll get in touch with you to discuss your application and the next steps in the foster program.
