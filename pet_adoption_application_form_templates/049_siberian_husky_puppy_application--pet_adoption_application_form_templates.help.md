# Siberian Husky Puppy Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a comprehensive application form for prospective pet adopters of Siberian Husky puppies. The form collects information about you and your family, including your contact details, preferences, and lifestyle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, providing all the required information.
2. Review the form before submitting to ensure all fields are completed.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Family Information (1)**: Please provide information about your family, including the number of family members.
* **Family Size (2)**: Enter the number of family members, including children and pets.
* **Contact Information (3)**: Enter your contact details, including email and phone number.
* **First Name (4)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (5)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email (6)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (7)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Puppy Preferences (8)**: Select how much energy your family has for a new puppy (e.g., Puppies with lots of energy, Puppies with low energy, Both).
* **Ownership Commitment (9)**: Indicate your willingness to own a new puppy (True or False).
* **Other Pets (10)**: If you have other pets, select True.
* **Previous Puppies (11)**: If you have previously owned a puppy, select True.
* **House Type (12)**: Select the type of house you live in (House, Apartment, Condo).
* **Yard Size (13)**: Select the size of your yard (Large, Medium, Small).
* **Fence Type (14)**: If you have a fence, select True.
* **Smoking (15)**: If you smoke, select True.
* **Pets Allowed (16)**: If you allow pets in your home, select True.
* **Home Type (17)**: Select the type of home you live in (House, Condo, Townhouse).
* **Children (18)**: If you have children living in your home, enter the number of children.
* **Employment Status (19)**: Select your employment status (Employed, Unemployed, Student).
* **Income (20)**: Select your income level (High, Medium, Low).
* **Rental (21)**: If you rent your home, select True.
* **Rent Type (22)**: If you have a rent agreement, select True.
* **Payment Plan (23)**: If you want to pay for the puppy, select True.
* **Deposit (24)**: If you are willing to pay a deposit for the puppy, select True.
* **Notes (25)**: Enter any additional information about yourself or your family.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us before submitting the form.
* This form is for your benefit, as well as for the benefit of the puppy.
