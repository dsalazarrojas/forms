# Adopt A Shelter Animal Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from individuals or families interested in adopting a shelter animal. The purpose of this form is to ensure that you are a suitable adopter for a shelter animal and to help us understand your preferences and circumstances to find the best match for you and your household.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* Applicant Name: Enter your full legal name.
	* Occupation: Provide your current employer or field of work.
	* Contact Phone: Enter the best number to reach you.
	* Contact Email: Enter your email address.
2. Provide your household information:
	* Home Address: Enter your street address, city, state, and zip code.
	* Home Ownership: Select whether you own or rent your home.
	* Landlord Permission if Renting: If you rent, we may contact your landlord to verify.
3. Specify your household details:
	* Number of Adults in Household: Include yourself.
	* Number of Children in Household: Enter the number of children in your household.
4. Choose your preferences:
	* Animal Type Interest: Select the type of animal you are interested in adopting (Dog, Cat, Small Mammal, or Other).
	* Specific Animal Name if Any: If you have a particular pet in mind from our website, enter its name.
	* Yard Type: Describe your outdoor space (Fenced, Unfenced, or No yard).
5. Additional information:
	* Reason for Adoption: Explain why you want to add a pet to your family.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name (Text, Required)**: Enter your full legal name.
* **Occupation (Text, Required)**: Provide your current employer or field of work.
* **Contact Phone (Text, Required)**: Enter the best number to reach you.
* **Contact Email (Email, Required)**: Enter your email address.
* **Home Address (Text, Required)**: Enter your street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Home Ownership (Select One, Required)**: Select whether you own or rent your home.
* **Landlord Permission if Renting (Select One, Optional)**: If you rent, we may contact your landlord to verify.
* **Number of Adults in Household (Number, Required)**: Include yourself.
* **Number of Children in Household (Number, Required)**: Enter the number of children in your household.
* **Yard Type (Select One, Required)**: Describe your outdoor space (Fenced, Unfenced, or No yard).
* **Animal Type Interest (Select One, Required)**: Select the type of animal you are interested in adopting (Dog, Cat, Small Mammal, or Other).
* **Specific Animal Name if Any (Text, Optional)**: If you have a particular pet in mind from our website, enter its name.
* **Current Pets in Home (Select Multiple, Optional)**: Select all that apply (Dogs, Cats, Birds, None).
* **Experience with Chosen Species (Select One, Required)**: Explain your experience with the chosen species (Extensive, Some, None).
* **Reason for Adoption (Text, Required)**: Explain why you want to add a pet to your family.
