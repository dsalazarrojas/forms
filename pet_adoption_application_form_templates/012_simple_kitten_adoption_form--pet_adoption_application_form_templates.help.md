# simple_kitten_adoption_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit an adoption application for a simple kitten adoption process. Please provide the required and optional information to help us match you with the purrfect kitten.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in your name and contact information (First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone).
* Provide your address where you plan to house the kitten.
* Select which breed(s) of kittens you are interested in.
* Choose the name, age, sex, and color of the kitten you'd like to adopt.
* Please note that the fields marked as required are essential for the adoption process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter the address where the kitten will be living.
* **Breed** (`breed`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the breed(s) of kittens you are interested in adopting.
* **Name Of Kitten** (`name_of_kitten`, `text`, required): Give the kitten a name that will make them special.
* **Age Of Kitten** (`age_of_kitten`, `number`, required): Enter the age of the kitten you're interested in adopting.
* **Sex Of Kitten** (`sex_of_kitten`, `select_one`, required): Choose the sex of the kitten (male or female).
* **Color Of Kitten** (`color_of_kitten`, `select_one`, required): Select the color of the kitten you're interested in.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form carefully and accurately.
* If you have any questions or need help with the form, contact our team.
* Our team will review your application and match you with the purrfect kitten based on your preferences.
