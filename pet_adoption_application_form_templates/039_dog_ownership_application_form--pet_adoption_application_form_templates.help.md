# dog_ownership_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather contact information and details about a potential pet adoption from the applicant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out each section carefully and thoroughly.
* Answer all required fields (if any).
* Use the provided hints or guidance to help you fill out the form.
* Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure everything is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): This section is for providing your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number. If you're filling out this form on behalf of someone else, please make sure to include their contact information as well.
* **Pet Info** (`pet_info`, text, required: false): In this section, please provide details about the pet you're interested in adopting, including its breed, age, and any other relevant information.
* **Adoption Info** (`adoption_info`, text, required: false): Here, you can provide additional information about your adoption process, such as how you found out about the pet and what motivated you to adopt.
* **Owner Info** (`owner_info`, text, required: false): This section is for providing information about the owner of the pet, including their name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required: false): Finally, you can provide any additional notes or comments about your application. Be sure to keep your comments concise and relevant to your application.

## Tips
* Make sure to be accurate and thorough in your responses.
