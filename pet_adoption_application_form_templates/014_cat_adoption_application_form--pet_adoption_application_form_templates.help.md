# Cat Adoption Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from potential cat adopters to ensure that they are a good fit for adopting a cat from our organization. This form helps us understand your lifestyle, experience with pets, and ability to provide a loving home for a feline friend.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please provide clear and honest answers to each question. Make sure to fill out all required fields. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1 - Applicant Details
* **Applicant Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Occupation/Employer**: Briefly describe your occupation or the name of your employer.
* **Page Note**: This section is for informational purposes only and will not be used for any decision-making process.

### Page 2 - Home and Lifestyle
* **Work Schedule/Hours away from home**: Describe your typical work schedule and how many hours you are away from home.
* **Where will the cat spend most of its time**: Choose where the cat will spend most of its time: Indoors Only, Indoors/Outdoors, Outdoors Only, or Enclosed Catio.
* **Where will the cat sleep at night**: Describe where the cat will sleep at night.
* **How do you plan to manage scratching behavior**: Describe your plan for managing the cat's scratching behavior, e.g., scratching posts, nail trimming.
* **Does anyone in the home have cat allergies**: Choose whether someone in the home has cat allergies, and if so, whether they are managed with medication or are severe.
* **What will happen to the cat if you move**: Describe what will happen to the cat if you move.

### Page 3 - Current Pets
* **Details of current pets (Breed, Age, Temperament)**: Describe your current pets, including breed, age, and temperament.
* **Are your current pets up to date on vaccinations and spayed/neutered**: Choose whether your current pets are up to date on vaccinations and spayed/neutered.

### Page 4 - References
* **Reference Name and Phone Number**: Provide the name and phone number of a personal reference (not a family member).

### Final Section
* **I certify that the information provided is true**: Typing your name acts as a digital signature, confirming that the information provided is accurate and truthful.
