# Pet Training Program Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential details from pet owners to tailor the pet training program to their pet's specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form with your pet's information, including its name, species, and age.
2. Provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
3. List your contact methods, such as email, phone, or social media.
4. Describe your pet's training needs and goals.
5. Share any concerns or questions you have about the program.
6. Enter your veterinary information, including their name, phone number, and address.
7. Provide detailed information about your pet, including its breed, size, and medical conditions.
8. Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
9. Describe the details of the training session you would like to have.
10. Enter your payment information, including the method of payment and any additional fees.
11. Read and agree to the payment terms and conditions.
12. Add any additional comments or requests you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Information**: Enter your pet's name, species, and age.
* **Owner Information**: Enter your name, email, and phone number.
* **Contact Information**: List your preferred contact methods, such as email, phone, or social media.
* **Contact Methods**: Enter multiple contact methods for us to reach you.
* **Pet Training Needs**: Describe your pet's training needs and goals.
* **Owner Concerns**: Share any concerns or questions you have about the program.
* **Veterinary Information**: Enter your veterinarian's name, phone number, and address.
* **Pet Details**: Enter detailed information about your pet, including breed, size, and medical conditions.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Training Session Details**: Describe the details of the training session you would like to have.
* **Payment Information**: Enter your payment method and any additional fees.
* **Payment Terms**: Read and agree to the payment terms and conditions.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or requests you may have.
