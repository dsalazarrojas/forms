# Dog Bio Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and update information about a dog's bio, including its behavior, personality, and health. It is intended to be used to update a dog's existing bio, not to create a new one.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields (dog_bio_update_form, dog_behavior, dog_personality, dog_training, and dog_health) with the most up-to-date information about the dog.
2. You can add special notes about the dog in the dog_special_notes field.
3. If you have images of the dog, you can upload them in the dog_image_1 and dog_image_2 fields.
4. In the dog_health_notes field, you can provide any additional information about the dog's health.
5. Review and submit the form to update the dog's bio.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dog Bio Update Form** (`dog_bio_update_form`): The title of the form is required and should be filled in with the updated information about the dog.
* **Dog Behavior** (`dog_behavior`): Provide an update on the dog's behavior, such as any changes in its activity level or any new habits it has developed.
* **Dog Personality** (`dog_personality`): Update any information about the dog's personality, such as its temperament or any changes in its affection level.
* **Dog Training** (`dog_training`): Fill in any updates on the dog's training, such as new commands it has learned or any changes in its obedience level.
* **Dog Health** (`dog_health`): Report any changes or updates on the dog's health, such as any new medical issues or recent vet visits.
* **Dog Health Notes** (`dog_health_notes`): Provide any additional information about the dog's health, such as its vaccination schedule or dietary needs.
* **Special Notes** (`dog_special_notes`): Add any special notes or comments about the dog that are not covered in the other fields.
* **Dog Image 1** (`dog_image_1`): Upload an image of the dog to document its appearance or any notable features.
* **Dog Image 2** (`dog_image_2`): Upload another image of the dog for further documentation.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and consistently to ensure that the dog's bio is up-to-date and consistent with its actual status.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact us to verify before proceeding.
* Only fill out the form for dogs that are already in the system, as this form is for updates, not new creations.
