# Animal Adoption Process Analysis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your animal adoption process. It will help identify areas for improvement and provide insights on how to streamline the adoption process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your role in the adoption process.
2. Enter the name of your organization.
3. Choose the length of time your organization has been operating.
4. Select the methods you use to receive adoption applications.
5. Indicate the approximate number of adoptions your organization processes annually.
6. Select the types of questions in your adoption application.
7. Choose the average time it takes to process an application.
8. Identify the screening steps involved in your process.
9. Select the percentage of applications that are typically approved.
10. Indicate the most common reasons for application rejection.
11. Select whether you conduct post-adoption follow-ups.
12. Choose the approximate adoption return rate.
13. Select the technology used to manage adoptions.
14. Identify the biggest challenges in your adoption process.
15. Select areas you'd like to improve in your process.
16. Choose how satisfied you are with your current process.
17. Indicate what aspects of your process are working well.
18. Add any additional comments or suggestions.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your abilities.
* If unsure of the options provided, please choose the one that best represents your current situation.
* This survey should take around 10-15 minutes to complete.
* You can save your progress and complete the survey later. 

Please note that some fields have more than 2 options, if you need to provide more details or have a custom answer, please use the "Other" or "Add additional comments" field to elaborate on your response.
