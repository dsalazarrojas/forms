# Pet Adoption Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a potential adopter and their pet. This information will be used by the pet adoption organization to determine the suitability of the adopter for pet adoption, to ensure the best possible match between the adopter and the pet. The form is divided into several sections, each focused on different aspects of the adoption process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information in the "User Information" section.
2. Provide details about the pet you're interested in adopting in the "Pet Information" section.
3. Share your contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
4. If applicable, provide medical records for you or your pet in the "Medical Records" section.
5. Write a brief adoption application in the "Adoption Application" section.
6. Sign the form in the "Signature" section.
7. Finally, confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions in the "User Agreement" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Information
* **User Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
### Pet Information
* **Pet's Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the pet you're interested in adopting.
* **Pet's Age** (`age`, `text`, required): Enter the age of the pet.
* **Pet's Breed** (`breed`, `text`, required): Enter the breed of the pet.
* **Pet's Description** (`description`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the pet you're interested in adopting.
### Contact Information
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `text`, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
### Pet Details
* **Pet's Photo** (`photo`, `text`, required): Upload a photo of the pet you're interested in adopting.
### Medical Records
* **Medical History** (`history`, `text`, required): If you or your pet have any medical conditions, describe them in this section.
### Adoption Application
* **Adoption Application** (`application`, `text`, required): Write a brief statement explaining why you're interested in adopting the pet.

* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required): Sign this form to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
