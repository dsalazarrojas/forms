# Animal Surrender Intake Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from owners surrendering their animals to our organization. This information will help us understand the animal's history, behavior, and needs, ensuring a smooth and safe transition for the animal.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, providing accurate and detailed information about the animal and yourself as the owner.
2. Be specific when answering questions, especially regarding the animal's health, behavior, and veterinary care.
3. If you have any photos or medical records, please attach them to the form or provide information on how we can obtain them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Full Name (1)**: Enter your legal name as the animal's owner.
* **Email Address (2)**: Provide your contact email address in case we need to reach out to you.
* **Phone Number (3)**: Enter your contact phone number for further communication.
* **Street Address (4)**: Enter your current address where the animal is being surrendered.
* **City (5)**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **State or Province (6)**: Enter the state or province where you reside.
* **ZIP or Postal Code (7)**: Enter the ZIP or postal code of your location.
* **Request Date (8)**: Enter the date you are requesting to surrender the animal.
* **Preferred Intake Date (9)**: Select a date when you can bring the animal to our organization.
* **Animal Name (10)**: Enter the name of the animal being surrendered.
* **Species (11)**: Choose the correct species of the animal (e.g., Dog, Cat, Rabbit, etc.).
* **Breed (12)**: If known, enter the breed of the animal.
* **Age (13)**: Enter the age of the animal in years.
* **Sex (14)**: Choose the sex of the animal (e.g., Male, Female).
* **Weight (15)**: Enter the weight of the animal in pounds.
* **Color and Markings (16)**: Describe the animal's coat color and any distinguishing features.
* **Microchip Status (17)**: Indicate whether the animal has a microchip and its status.
* **Microchip Number (18)**: If applicable, enter the microchip number.
* **Spay or Neuter Status (19)**: Indicate whether the animal is spayed or neutered.
* **Vaccination Status (20)**: Choose the vaccination status of the animal (e.g., Up to Date, Partially Vaccinated, Not Vaccinated).
* **Current Health Status (21)**: Choose the animal's current health status (e.g., Healthy, Minor Issues, Needs Medical Attention).
* **Health Details (22)**: Describe any health conditions the animal is experiencing.
* **Temperament and Behavior (23)**: Describe the animal's personality and behavior.
* **Good with Children (24)**: Indicate whether the animal is good with children.
* **Good with Dogs (25)**: Indicate whether the animal is good with dogs.
* **Good with Cats (26)**: Indicate whether the animal is good with cats.
* **Reason for Surrender (27)**: Explain the reason for surrendering the animal.
* **Daily Care Routine (28)**: Describe the animal's daily care routine, including feeding and exercise.
* **Diet Information (29)**: Describe the animal's diet, including food type and schedule.
* **Special Needs or Requirements (30)**: List any special needs or requirements the animal has.
* **Veterinarian Information (31)**: Enter the name and contact information of the animal's veterinarian.
* **Medical Records (32)**: Indicate whether you have medical records available for the animal.
* **Photos Available (33)**: Indicate whether you have photos available to share.
* **Urgency Level (34)**: Choose the urgency level for the animal's intake (e.g., Immediate, Within 1 Week, Within 2 Weeks, Flexible).
* **Additional Information (35)**: Provide any additional information about the animal or situation that may be helpful.
* **I Understand This Is a Request Only (36)**: Confirm that you understand the animal's intake is subject to approval and availability.

Please remember that this form is a request for surrender, and our organization will review the information provided to determine the best course of action for the animal's well-being and safety.
