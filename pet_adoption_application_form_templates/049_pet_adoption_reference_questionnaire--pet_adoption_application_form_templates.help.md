# Pet Adoption Reference Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Adoption Reference Questionnaire is a form designed to collect information about the user's pet adoption process. This form is used to gather details about the user's contact information, pet adoption preferences, and their current situation with pets at home.

## How To Complete This Form
Please complete the following sections to ensure that we have accurate and up-to-date information about your pet adoption needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Adoption Information** (`pet_adoption_reference_questionnaire`, text, required): This is the introduction to the form.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **Zip** (`zip`, number, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Available Date** (`available_date`, date, optional): If you're interested in adopting, enter the date you'd be available to adopt. If you're not interested in adopting, you may leave this field blank.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, optional): Select how you would like to be contacted for adoption information. You can choose from Email, Phone, or Message.
* If you selected Message, you may also enter your preferred contact method in the next field.
* **Preferred Contact Method Other** (`preferred_contact_method_other`, text, optional): If you selected Message in the previous field, enter your preferred contact method here.
* **Reason for Adopting** (`reason_for_adopting`, text, optional): Enter why you're interested in adopting a new pet.
* **Number of People** (`number_of_people`, number, optional): Enter the number of people in your household.
* **Pets in Home** (`pets_in_home`, number, optional): Enter the number of pets you currently have at home.
* **Pet Experience** (`pet_experience`, text, optional): Enter any experience you have with pets, such as training or caring for them.
* **Pet Adopted** (`pet_adopted`, select_one, optional): If you've adopted a pet in the past, select Yes. If you haven't, select No, and if you're unsure, select Maybe.
