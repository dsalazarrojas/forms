# Rescue Dog Adoption Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals interested in adopting a rescue dog from a shelter or rescue organization. The form is designed to gather personal and household details, pet experience, desired pet preferences, and any medical conditions that may impact the adoption process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Personal Details**: Enter your personal details, including your name, address, phone number, and any other relevant information.
2. **Household Details**: Describe your household, including the number of people living in the household, the age range of occupants, and any other relevant details.
3. **Pet Experience**: If you have experience with pets, please describe your experience, including the type and duration of your previous pet ownership.
4. **Desired Pet**: Select the type of dog you are interested in adopting, from the options provided (Pug, Bulldog, Chihuahua, or Golden Retriever).
5. **Conditions**: If you have any medical conditions that may impact your ability to care for a dog, please describe them in the space provided.
6. **Other**: If you have any other information that you would like to share with us, please use this space.
7. **Notes**: If you have any additional comments or thoughts that you would like to share with us, please use this space.
8. **Confirm Dog**: Indicate whether or not you are confirming that you are interested in adopting a dog.
9. **Other Notes**: If you have any other information that you would like to share with us, please use this space.
