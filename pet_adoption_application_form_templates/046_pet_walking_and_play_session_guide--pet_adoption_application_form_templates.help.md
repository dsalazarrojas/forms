# Pet Walking And Play Session Guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to guide pet owners and pet walkers through the necessary information and agreements required for a pet walking and play session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and the fields below.
2. Complete all the required fields to provide the necessary information.
3. Ensure you have the consent of the pet owner before proceeding.
4. Review the agreements and instructions provided in this form carefully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **pet walker name** (`pet_walker_name`, text, optional): This field is for the pet walker to provide their name and contact information.
* **session preferences** (`session_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the preferences you have for the pet walking and play session.
	+ Yes: This is an indication that you have preferences for the session.
	+ No: This is an indication that you do not have any preferences for the session.
* **owner details** (`owner_details`, select_one, optional): This field is for the owner to indicate if they are the pet's owner or the pet walker.
	+ True: You are the pet's owner.
	+ False: You are not the pet's owner.
* **session time** (`session_time`, time, optional): This field is for the pet owner to select the desired session time.
* **session date** (`session_date`, date, optional): This field is for the pet owner to select the desired session date.
* **pet details** (`pet_details`, text, optional): This field is for the pet owner to provide information about their pet.
* **consent** (`consent`, select_one, optional): This field is for the pet owner to provide their consent for the pet walking and play session.
	+ True: You consent to the session.
	+ False: You do not consent to the session.
* **owner contact** (`owner_contact`, email, optional): This field is for the pet owner to provide their contact information.
* **pet walker service** (`pet_walker_service`, text, optional): This field is for the pet walker to provide information about their service.
* **pet sitter phone** (`pet_sitter_phone`, text, optional): This field is for the pet walker to provide their phone number.
* **pet owner agreement** (`pet_owner_agreement`, select_one, optional): This field is for the pet owner to indicate if they agree to the terms and conditions.
	+ True: You agree to the terms and conditions.
	+ False: You do not agree to the terms and conditions.
* **pet walker phone** (`pet_walker_phone`, text, optional): This field is for the pet walker to provide their phone number.
* **pet insurance** (`pet_insurance`, select_one, optional): This field is for the pet owner to indicate if they have pet insurance.
	+ True: You have pet insurance.
	+ False: You do not have pet insurance.
* **vet contact** (`vet_contact`, text, optional): This field is for the pet owner to provide their vet's contact information.
* **pet medical history** (`pet_medical_history`, text, optional): This field is for the pet owner to provide information about the pet's medical history.
* **owner notes** (`owner_notes`, text, optional): This field is for the pet owner to provide any additional notes or comments.
* **pet walker notes** (`pet_walker_notes`, text, optional): This field is for the pet walker to provide any additional notes or comments.
* **owner address** (`owner_address`, text, optional): This field is for the pet owner to provide their address.
* **pet walker address** (`pet_walker_address`, text, optional): This field is for the pet walker to provide their address.
* **pet walker service area** (`pet_walker_service_area`, text, optional): This field is for the pet walker to provide information about their service area.
* **session location** (`session_location`, text, optional): This field is for the pet owner to select the desired session location.
* **session start time** (`session_start_time`, time, optional): This field is for the pet owner to select the desired session start time.
* **session end time** (`session_end_time`, time, optional): This field is for the pet owner to select the desired session end time.
* **session duration** (`session_duration`, text, optional): This field is for the pet owner to provide information about the desired session duration.
* **owner signature** (`owner_signature`, note, optional): This field is for the pet owner to sign their agreement, indicating their consent to the pet walking and play session.
