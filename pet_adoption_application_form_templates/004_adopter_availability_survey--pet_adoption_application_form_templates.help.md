# Adopter Availability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Adopter Availability Survey is a form designed to help match you with an adoptable pet that fits your lifestyle and preferences. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form, and we'll do our best to find a compatible match for you and your new furry family member.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer all questions carefully and honestly to ensure we can find the best match for you and your pet needs.
2. Be sure to fill out all required fields (marked with *) as they are necessary for us to find a suitable pet for you.
3. Provide accurate information about your work schedule, household size, and other relevant details to help us understand your lifestyle.
4. If you have any questions or need help, feel free to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`adopter_name`, `text`, required): Your complete name will help us know who you are and how to contact you.
* **Email Address** (`adopters_email`, `email`, required): We'll use this email address to communicate with you about your adoption application and any other pet-related matters.
* **Phone Number** (`adopter_phone`, `text`, required): Please provide your phone number so we can reach you in case of emergencies or schedule conflicts.
* **Household Size** (`household_size`, `number`, required): This will help us understand how many people are in your household, which affects the amount of attention and care your new pet will need.
* **Children in Home** (`children_present`, `select_one`, required): If you have children, please select the number of kids you have. This will help us consider your family's needs when finding a pet match.
* **Ages of Children** (`children_ages`, `text`, optional): If you have children, please list their ages so we can better understand their needs and your family's dynamics.
* **Other Pets in Home** (`other_pets`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you have other pets, please select their types (e.g., dogs, cats, birds) so we can consider their needs when finding a pet match.
* **Work Schedule** (`work_schedule`, `select_one`, required): Please select your typical work schedule to help us determine when we can contact you and when you'll be away from home.
* **Type of Home** (`home_type`, `select_one`, required): Please select the type of home you live in, which helps us consider space and living arrangements for your new pet.
* **Outdoor Space Access** (`yard_access`, `select_one`, required): If you have a yard or outdoor area, please select yes or no. This will help us determine if your home can accommodate a pet that needs regular exercise and outdoor access.
* **Yard is Fenced** (`yard_fenced`, `select_one`, optional): If you have a yard, please select if it's fenced or not. This will help us consider the safety of your new pet.
* **Available Weekday Hours** (`availability_weekday`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the times of the day when you're available to care for your new pet on weekdays.
* **Available Weekend Hours** (`availability_weekend`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the times of the day when you're available to care for your new pet on weekends.
* **Pet Care During Work** (`pet_care_plan`, `select_one`, required): Please select who will care for your new pet while you're at work, and we'll work around their schedule and needs.
* **Travel Frequency** (`travel_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you travel for extended periods so we can plan for pet care during your absences.
* **Pet Arrangements When Traveling** (`pet_travel_arrangements`, `text`, optional): If you have a regular travel schedule, please describe your plans for your pet's care during that time.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_one`, required): Please select your preferred method of contact (phone, email, or text) so we can best communicate with you about your adoption application.
* **Best Time to Contact** (`best_contact_time`, `text`, optional): If you have a preferred time for phone calls, please specify it so we can schedule calls accordingly.
