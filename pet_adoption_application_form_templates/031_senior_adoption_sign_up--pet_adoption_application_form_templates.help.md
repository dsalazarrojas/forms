# Senior Adoption Sign Up - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in adopting a senior pet from a shelter or rescue organization.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in your personal and contact information on the first page (user_info).
- Provide your contact details on the second page (contact_details).
- Enter your home information on the third page (home_info).
- Share details about the pet you are interested in adopting on the fourth page (pet_info).
- In the last page (application_info), please provide any additional information about your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_info** (Page 1, Text, Optional): This is where you provide your name and any other personal details you'd like to share about yourself.
* **contact_details** (Page 2, Text, Optional): Here, you'll enter your contact information, such as your address and phone number, so we can get in touch with you about your adoption application.
* **home_info** (Page 3, Text, Optional): Please describe your home environment and what kind of care and attention you can provide to a senior pet.
* **pet_info** (Page 4, Text, Optional): Share details about the specific type of pet you're interested in adopting, such as its age, size, or energy level.
* **application_info** (Page 5, Text, Optional): Use this space to provide any additional information about your application or your pet preferences.
