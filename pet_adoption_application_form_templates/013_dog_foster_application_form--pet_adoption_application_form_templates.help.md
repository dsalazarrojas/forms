# Dog Foster Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dog Foster Application Form is used to collect information from potential foster families about their ability to care for dogs in need. The form helps evaluate their household, pet experience, and care provider qualifications to ensure a safe and loving environment for the dogs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal contact information, including your name, address, and phone number.
2. Describe your household, including the number and type of people living with you, your living arrangement, and any pets you currently have.
3. Share your knowledge about dogs and their physical conditions, if you have any.
4. Introduce yourself as a potential dog foster caregiver, explaining your motivations, experience, and qualifications.
5. Submit the form to proceed with the application process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Info (personal-info)** (`personal_info`, Text, Not Required): Please provide your name, address, and phone number to contact you.
* **Household Details (household-details)** (`household_details`, Text, Not Required): Describe your household, including the number and type of people living with you, your living arrangement, and any pets you currently have.
* **Physical Conditions (physical-conditions)** (`physical_conditions`, Text, Not Required): Share your knowledge about dogs and their physical conditions, if you have any.
* **Pet Info (pet-info)** (`pet_info`, Text, Not Required): If you have experience caring for dogs or other animals, describe your experience.
* **Care Providers (care-providers)** (`care_providers`, Text, Not Required): Introduce yourself as a potential dog foster caregiver, explaining your motivations, experience, and qualifications.
* **Submission (submission)** (`submission`, Text, Required): Provide a brief statement about why you're interested in fostering a dog and what makes you a suitable caregiver.
* **Additional Info (additional-info)** (`additional_info`, Text, Not Required): If you have any further information that might be helpful, please include it here.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough when answering the questions.
* Use clear and concise language to describe your household, experience, and qualifications.
* Attach any supporting documentation, such as references or medical certificates, if requested.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, contact the fostering organization for guidance.
