# Rehome Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rehome Application Form is a tool used to rehome a pet that is currently being cared for in a shelter or by the previous owner. This form helps to gather essential information from the person interested in rehoming a pet to ensure a smooth and successful rehoming process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Pet Type** (Cat, Dog, or Other) of the pet you are interested in rehoming.
2. Enter your **Name** in the space provided. This will help us know who to contact in case of any questions or updates.
3. Provide your **Age** if you are 18 or older. If you are under 18, please leave this field blank.
4. Enter your **Contact Info** (phone number and/or email) so we can reach out to you with any updates.
5. Enter your **Address** to help us contact you in case of any issues with the pet's rehoming.
6. In **Pet Info**, please provide any relevant information about the pet, such as its breed, size, and behavior.
7. In **Home Info**, describe your home environment, including the number of people living there, any other pets, and any relevant details that might affect the pet's well-being.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, select_one): Select the type of pet you are interested in rehoming (Cat, Dog, or Other).
* **Name** (`name`, text): Enter your name to help us know who to contact in case of any questions or updates.
* **Age** (`age`, number): If you are 18 or older, enter your age. If you are under 18, please leave this field blank.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text): Enter your phone number and/or email to help us contact you with any updates.
* **Address** (`address`, text): Enter your address so we can contact you in case of any issues with the pet's rehoming.
* **Pet Info** (`pet_info`, text): Provide any relevant information about the pet, such as its breed, size, and behavior.
* **Home Info** (`home_info`, text): Describe your home environment, including the number of people living there, any other pets, and any relevant details that might affect the pet's well-being.
