# Foster Application For Dogs - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for potential foster families to apply for a foster home for a dog. We'll use the information you provide to get in touch with you and learn more about your lifestyle and interests to find the best match for you and your family.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form below with accurate and up-to-date information about yourself and your family.
- Make sure to include your available start and end dates for the foster period.
- If you have any additional information or questions, don't hesitate to include them in the "Why Are You Applying" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Please enter your first name as it is written on your official identification.
* **Last Name**: Please enter your last name as it is written on your official identification.
* **Address Line 1**: Enter your street address or the address you'd like to receive mail and phone calls from us.
* **City**: Enter the city where you live.
* **State**: Enter the state where you live.
* **Zip Code**: Enter the five-digit zip code of your address.
* **Home Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached at home.
* **Mobile Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached mobilely.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Available Start Date**: Enter the date when you'd be available to start fostering a dog.
* **Available End Date**: Enter the date when you'd like your foster commitment to end.
* **Why Are You Applying**: Share any additional information about why you're interested in fostering a dog, such as your experience with animals or your family's lifestyle.
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: Share how you found out about our organization.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
- If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us.
- We appreciate your interest in fostering a dog and look forward to getting in touch with you soon!
