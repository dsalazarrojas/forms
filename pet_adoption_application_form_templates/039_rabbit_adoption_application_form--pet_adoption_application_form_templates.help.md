# Rabbit Adoption Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rabbit Adoption Application Form is a tool designed to gather necessary information from potential adopters to facilitate a successful rabbit adoption process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide detailed information about yourself in the "Adopter Details" section, which includes your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. Choose the type of rabbit that you are interested in adopting from the "Rabbit Breed" dropdown menu. Select one breed from the available options: Holland Lop, Mini Lop, Dutch, English, French, Californian, Polish, or Netherland Dwarf.
3. Select the color of the rabbit you are interested in adopting from the "Rabbit Color" dropdown menu. You can choose from White, Black, Chocolate, Blue, or Other.
4. In the "Suitability Assessment" section, select all the statements that apply to your lifestyle and experience with rabbits. This will help the adoption team assess your suitability for rabbit ownership.
5. If applicable, choose a date for the adoption in the "Adoption Scheduling" section.
6. Finally, provide any additional contact information in the "Contact Info" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Adopter Details** (`Adopter Details`, text, required: false): This section is where you can provide your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Rabbit Info** (`Rabbit Info`, text, required: false): In this section, you can provide any additional information about the rabbit you are interested in adopting, such as its age, size, or health conditions.
* **Suitability Assessment** (`Suitability Assessment`, select_multiple, required: false): This section helps us understand your lifestyle and experience with rabbits. Please select all the statements that apply to you:
	+ Adopter's lifestyle can accommodate a rabbit
	+ Adopter has space for a rabbit to exercise
	+ Adopter is familiar with rabbit care and behavior
	+ Adopter has previous experience with rabbits
* **Adoption Scheduling** (`Adoption Scheduling`, date, required: false): If you have a preferred date for adoption, please select it in this field.
* **Rabbit Breed** (`Rabbit Breed`, select_one, required: false): Choose the breed of rabbit you are interested in adopting.
* **Rabbit Color** (`Rabbit Color`, select_one, required: false): Choose the color of the rabbit you are interested in adopting.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, text, required: false): Provide any additional contact information that you feel is relevant.
* **Submit Button** (`Submit Button`, text, required: false): This is where you submit your application.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will review your application and get back to you soon!
