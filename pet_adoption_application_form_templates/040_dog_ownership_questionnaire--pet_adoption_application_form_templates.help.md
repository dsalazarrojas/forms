# dog_ownership_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "dog_ownership_questionnaire" is designed to collect information about your potential new pet. It will help the shelter staff understand your lifestyle, experience, and preferences, which will help determine whether our dog is a good match for you and your family.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. For each field, please provide the most accurate and honest information about your potential new pet and yourself.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dog Breed** (text): Please enter the breed of the dog you are inquiring about adopting.
* **Dog Age**: Indicate the age of the dog you are considering adopting. Please be as accurate as possible.
* **Veterinary Visits**: If you have experience with regular veterinary visits, please indicate whether you would be able to take your dog to regular check-ups.
* **Dog Health**: Please provide any relevant information about the dog's health, such as medical conditions or allergies.
* **Dog Vet**: Indicate whether the dog has a regular vet, and if so, please provide their name and contact information.
* **Microchip**: If the dog has a microchip, please indicate whether it's implanted, and if so, which type.
* **Spayed/Neutered**: Indicate whether the dog has been spayed or neutered.
* **Training**: Indicate whether the dog has had any training, such as obedience or behavior modification.

## Tips

* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* The information you provide will help us assess the suitability of the dog for adoption and ensure it's a good fit for you and your family.
