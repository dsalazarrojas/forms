# Pet Adoption Agreement Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Adoption Agreement Extension Form is a document that is used to update or modify an existing pet adoption agreement. This form is meant to be used when an adopter wants to make changes to the original agreement, ensuring that all parties involved are aware of these changes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your pet ID (page 1).
2. Enter your pet's name (page 2).
3. Select the type of pet you are adopting (page 3).
4. Choose the adoption agreement you are applying for (page 4).
5. Enter your name (page 5).
6. Enter your contact email address (page 6).
7. Provide your phone number (page 7).
8. Enter your address (page 8).
9. Add any additional notes or comments (page 9).
10. Enter any additional information related to the adoption agreement (page 10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **pet_id** (Page 1, Number): Enter the ID number of the pet you are adopting.
* **pet_name** (Page 2, Text): Enter the name of the pet you are adopting.
* **pet_type** (Page 3, Select Multiple): Choose the type of pet you are adopting (e.g., dog, cat, bird, fish).
* **adoption_agreement** (Page 4, Select One): Select the adoption agreement you wish to apply for (e.g., Pet Adoption Agreement 1.0, 2.0, 3.0).
* **adopter_name** (Page 5, Text): Enter your name as the adopter.
* **contact_email** (Page 6, Email): Enter your contact email address.
* **phone** (Page 7, Text): Enter your phone number.
* **address** (Page 8, Text): Enter your address.
* **notes** (Page 9, Note): Add any additional comments or notes related to the adoption.
* **adopter_agreement** (Page 10, Text): Enter any additional information related to the adoption agreement.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure the adoption agreement is properly updated.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, please reach out to the relevant department or contact person for assistance.
