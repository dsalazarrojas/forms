## Purpose
The Dog Walking Report Form is used to document dog walking activities and gather information about the dog's experience. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, fill out the form with the required information, which includes:
	* The "Dog Breed"
	* "Walk Location"
2. Optional information includes:
	* The dog's "Breed", "Weight", "Size" and "Health"
	* The walk's duration in "Hours" and "Walk Type"
	* The start and end times of the walk
	* The weather conditions of the walk
	* The dog's name
	* The walk's distance and speed
	* The dog's current health status and any comments or notes about the walk
3. Ensure to enter accurate information to ensure the form is completed as accurately as possible.
