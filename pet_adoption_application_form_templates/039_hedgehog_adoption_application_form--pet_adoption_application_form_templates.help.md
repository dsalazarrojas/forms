# Hedgehog Adoption Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hedgehog Adoption Application Form is designed to help us understand who is seeking to adopt a hedgehog as a pet. Please ensure you are ready to commit to adopting a hedgehog and that the information provided is accurate.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Select the correct options from the available choices.
3. Submit your application once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Are you a pet lover** (`pet_loner`, text, required: false):
	+ Please indicate your level of pet love. If you have had pets before, describe your experience. If you don't have pets, explain why you'd like to adopt a hedgehog.
* **Are you seeking to adopt a pet?** (`seeking_to_adopt`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Check 'Yes' if you are looking to adopt a pet, or 'No' if you are just browsing.
* **What type of pet do you want to adopt?** (`pet_type`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select all the pet types you are interested in adopting, including hedgehogs.
* **What is your name?** (`name`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your name as it appears on your ID or official documents.
* **Contact number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter a number where we can contact you.

## Tips
* Please ensure the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We appreciate your honesty and commitment to providing a loving home for a hedgehog.
