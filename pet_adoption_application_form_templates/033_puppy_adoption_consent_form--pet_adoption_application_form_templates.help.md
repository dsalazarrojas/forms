# Puppy Adoption Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from individuals or families interested in adopting a puppy from an animal shelter or rescue organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your name, address, phone number, email, and contact information.
2. Review the adoption contract terms and select the corresponding option to express your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to appear on all correspondence related to the adoption.
* **Address**: Provide your address where the puppy will be kept.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for updates and communication related to the adoption.
* **Contact Details**: Enter a brief description of your contact information, including your work or preferred method of contact.
* **Contact First Name/Last Name**: Enter the first and last name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Consent**: Select one of the options to express your agreement or disagreement with the adoption contract terms.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have read and understood the adoption contract terms before completing this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
* We appreciate your honesty and understanding in this process.
