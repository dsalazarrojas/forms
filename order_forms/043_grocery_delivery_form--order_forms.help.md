# Grocery Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Grocery Delivery Form is designed to gather information from customers for their grocery orders, allowing for efficient and accurate order processing and delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Fill in the User Input Form with your details. This form is not required.
2.  In the Select One Product field, choose the product you wish to order. You can select one of the available options.
3.  In the Select Multiple Products field, choose the products you wish to order. You can select multiple options.
4.  Enter the Date of Delivery in the Date of Delivery field. This is the date you want your order to be delivered.
5.  Enter the Time of Delivery in the Time of Delivery field. This is the time you want your order to be delivered.
6.  In the Note to Driver field, provide any additional information or instructions for the delivery driver.
7.  Enter your Email Input in the Email Input field. This field is not required.
8.  In the Phone Number field, enter your phone number.
9.  Choose the Payment Method you prefer from the options provided in the Payment Method field.
10.  In the Payment Information field, select the payment details required for your order.
11.  Enter your Delivery Address in the Delivery Address field. This field is not required.
12.  Choose the Payment Status from the options provided in the Payment Status field. This field is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input Form** (text, optional): This field is a text input for any additional information you would like to provide.
* **Select One Product** (select_one, optional): Choose one product from the available options for your order.
* **Select Multiple Products** (select_multiple, optional): Choose multiple products for your order.
* **Date of Delivery** (date, optional): Enter the date you want your order to be delivered.
* **Time of Delivery** (time, optional): Enter the time you want your order to be delivered.
* **Note to Driver** (note, optional): Provide any additional information or instructions for the delivery driver.
* **Email Input** (email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Payment Method** (select_one, optional): Choose the payment method you prefer.
* **Payment Information** (select_multiple, optional): Select payment details required for your order.
* **Delivery Address** (note, optional): Enter your delivery address.
* **Payment Status** (select_one, optional): Choose the payment status for your order.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate order processing and delivery.
* Double-check your selections for accuracy to avoid any errors.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact our customer support team.
* Ensure that your payment information is correct to avoid any delays or cancellations.
