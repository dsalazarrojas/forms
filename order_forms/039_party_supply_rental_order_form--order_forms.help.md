# Party Supply Rental Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to order party supplies and related services for a party. Please fill out the form to provide us with necessary information to process your order.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by selecting the service type (balloons, tablecloths, or napkins) that you would like to rent.
2. If you select "Balloon" or "Tablecloth", enter the number of items you need in the "Items Needed" field. You can select multiple items if needed.
3. Enter the date and time of your party.
4. Provide the party location.
5. Enter a brief description of your party (optional).
6. Enter your contact name.
7. Enter your email address.
8. Enter your phone number.
9. Choose your preferred payment method (Bank Transfer, Check, or Cash).
10. If needed, enter any additional services you would like to order.
11. If needed, enter any notes or comments about your order.
12. Click the "Submit" button to complete your order.
