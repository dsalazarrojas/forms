# Custom Printing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to input their printing order details for custom printing services. Customers can select the desired printing type, size, color mode, quantity, paper quality, material, paper type, and provide their details to place an order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the desired printing type from the options provided in the "What do you want to print?" field.
2. Choose the print size that suits your needs from the available options in the "Print size" field.
3. Select the color mode you prefer from the "Color" field. You can choose between "Color" and "Black and White".
4. Enter the quantity of prints you need in the "Quantity" field.
5. Select the desired paper quality from the options in the "Paper quality" field. Choose from "Glossy", "Matte", or "Matte and Glossy".
6. Select the material for your prints from the options in the "Material" field. You can choose from "Cardstock", "Cotton", or "Vellum".
7. Choose the paper type that suits your needs from the options in the "Paper type" field. Select from "8.5 x 11", "8.5 x 14", or "Letter".
8. Enter any additional customer details in the "Customer details" field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order details**: Enter a brief description of your printing order, if desired. (text, optional)
* **What do you want to print?**: Select the type of document you want to print, such as an A5, A6, A7, A8, A10, etc. (select_one, optional)
* **Print size**: Choose the size of the print, such as A5, A6, A7, A8, A10, etc. (number, optional)
* **Color**: Select the color mode you prefer for your prints, either "Color" or "Black and White". (select_one, optional)
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of prints you need. (number, optional)
* **Paper quality**: Choose the desired paper quality for your prints, from "Glossy", "Matte", or "Matte and Glossy". (select_one, optional)
* **Material**: Select the material you prefer for your prints, from "Cardstock", "Cotton", or "Vellum". (select_multiple, optional)
* **Paper type**: Choose the paper type that suits your needs, from "8.5 x 11", "8.5 x 14", or "Letter". (select_one, optional)
* **Customer details**: Enter any additional details about your order, such as special instructions or requests. (text, optional)

## Tips
* Please select the most suitable options for your printing needs to ensure accurate processing of your order.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact our support team for assistance.
