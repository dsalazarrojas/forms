# Timed Release Supplement Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect information for timed releases, which are essential for delivering products or services to customers on a specific schedule or timeframe. The form is designed to gather customer details, delivery preferences, and payment information to ensure smooth and timely delivery of products or services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your delivery preferences, including timeframe, payment method, and any special delivery instructions.
2. Provide your payment information and confirm your order total.
3. Enter your contact details, including name, email, and phone number (if applicable).
4. Choose your preferred delivery options, such as shipping method (if applicable).
5. Confirm your payment status.
6. Add any additional customer notes or instructions.
