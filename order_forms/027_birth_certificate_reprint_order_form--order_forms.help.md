# Birth Certificate Reprint Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Birth Certificate Reprint Order Form is used to request a duplicate birth certificate. This form requires information from both the requester and the certificate holder to ensure accuracy and authenticity of the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of certificate you need (Certified Copy, Certified Abstract, Short Form, or Full Certificate).
2.  Enter your name, phone number, and email address.
3.  Choose your relationship to the certificate holder (Self, Parent, Guardian, Legal Representative, or Other Authorized Person).
4.  Fill in the certificate holder's name, date of birth, sex, and mother's name as it appears on the certificate.
5.  If applicable, provide the father's name as it appears on the certificate.
6.  Enter the place of birth and state or country of birth.
7.  Select the number of copies you need.
8.  Choose your preferred delivery method (Standard Mail, Expedited Mail, Email, or In-Person Pickup).
9.  Enter the delivery address, city, and zip code.
10.  Select your payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, Check, Money Order, Online Payment, or In-Person Cash).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Relationship to Certificate Holder** (`requester_relationship`): Select how you are related to the certificate holder.
*   **Your First Name** (`requester_first_name`): Enter your first name.
*   **Your Last Name** (`requester_last_name`): Enter your last name.
*   **Your Phone Number** (`requester_phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Your Email Address** (`requester_email`): Enter your contact email address.
*   **Certificate Holder First Name** (`holder_first_name`): Enter the certificate holder's first name.
*   **Certificate Holder Middle Name** (`holder_middle_name`): Enter the certificate holder's middle name (if applicable).
*   **Certificate Holder Last Name** (`holder_last_name`): Enter the certificate holder's last name.
*   **Date of Birth** (`holder_dob`): Enter the certificate holder's date of birth.
*   **Sex at Birth** (`holder_sex`): Select the certificate holder's sex at birth (Male or Female).
*   **Mother's Full Name at Birth** (`holder_mothers_name`): Enter the mother's name as it appears on the certificate.
*   **Father’s Full Name** (`holder_fathers_name`): Enter the father's name if it appears on the certificate.
*   **Place of Birth** (`birth_location`): Enter the city or county where the certificate holder was born.
*   **State or Country of Birth** (`birth_state_country`): Enter the state or country where the certificate holder was born.
*   **Number of Copies Needed** (`number_of_copies`): Select the number of copies you need.
*   **Type of Certificate Needed** (`certificate_type`): Select the type of certificate you need (Certified Copy, Certified Abstract, Short Form, or Full Certificate).
*   **Preferred Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`): Select how you want to receive the certificate (Standard Mail, Expedited Mail, Email, or In-Person Pickup).
*   **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`): Enter the address where you want to receive the certificate.
*   **City** (`delivery_city`): Enter the city where you want to receive the certificate.
*   **State** (`delivery_state`): Enter the state where you want to receive the certificate.
*   **Zip Code** (`delivery_zip`): Enter the postal code where you want to receive the certificate.
*   **Certificate Fee** (`certificate_fee`): Enter the cost per copy of the certificate (if applicable).
*   **Shipping Fee** (`shipping_fee`): Enter the cost for shipping (if applicable).
*   **Total Cost** (`total_cost`): Enter the total amount due.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select how you will pay (Credit Card, Debit Card, Check, Money Order, Online Payment, or In-Person Cash).
*   **Identity Verification Method** (`identity_verification`): Select how you will prove your identity (Driver's License, Passport, Government ID, Birth Certificate, or Other Valid ID).
*   **ID Number** (`id_number`): Enter your ID number.
*   **I certify that information is accurate and complete** (`authorization_signature`): Confirm that the information is accurate and complete.

Note: The fields marked as "optional" are not required to fill in, but filling them out will ensure that your information is accurate and complete.
