**Yard Sign Information Collection - Help Guide**
## Purpose
The Yard Sign Information Collection form is designed to collect specific details about yard signs. This form will help gather essential information about sign specifications, artwork, installation details, and installation location.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, fill it out one page at a time. The form is divided into four sections: Sign Specs, Artwork, Installation Details, and Installation Location. Make sure to provide as much detail as possible for each section without leaving any fields blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Sign Specs**
    *   **Sign Specs**: This section collects information about the sign's specifications.
        +   This is where you'll enter details about the sign's dimensions, material, size, and other physical properties.
*   **Artwork**
    *   **Artwork**: In this section, describe the visual aspects of the yard sign, such as colors, graphics, and design patterns.
        +   Please provide a detailed description of the sign's appearance.
*   **Installation Details**
    *   **Installation Details**: This section asks for information about how the sign will be installed.
        +   Share your knowledge about the sign's mounting method, location, and any specific installation requirements.
*   **Installation Location**
    *   **Installation Location**: This section gathers details about the physical location where the sign will be installed.
        +   Enter the accurate address or location of where the sign will be installed.

## Tips
- Please be as thorough as possible in filling out this form.
- Use clear and concise language when describing the sign's specifications, artwork, installation details, and location.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, contact the form owner (form@gic.mx) for assistance.
