# Kosher Food Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to order kosher food from a store or restaurant. It helps the store staff to efficiently manage and fulfill customer orders.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Order Details** in the text field provided.
2. Select the **Food Items** you would like to order from the available options (e.g., Apple, Banana, Carrot). You can select one or multiple items.
3. Enter your total **Order Total** (if applicable).
4. Provide your **Customer Details** in the text field.
5. Choose your preferred **Payment Method** from the available options (e.g., Cash, Card, Transfer).
6. Enter your **Shipping Address** in the text field.
7. Add any **Notes** about your order if you have any specific requests.
8. Select the **Order Status** from the available options (e.g., In Progress, Complete, Cancelled).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your order.
* **Food Items** (`food_items`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the food items you would like to order. You can select one or multiple items.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, optional): If you are ordering online or over the phone, enter the total amount of your order.
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, optional): Provide your name, contact information, and any relevant details about yourself or the person placing the order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to pay for your order (e.g., Cash, Card, Transfer).
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter your delivery address where the order should be shipped to.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any special requests or instructions for your order.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of your order (e.g., In Progress, Complete, Cancelled).

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields (e.g., Payment Method, Order Status) to ensure your order is processed correctly.
* Double-check your order details and customer information to avoid any errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the store staff.
