# Wheel Valve Extension Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a wheel valve extension order. It is designed to be completed by customers who need to extend or modify their wheel valves.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer information in the sections below, including your name, phone number, and email.
2. Specify the date of the order.
3. Provide the details of the order.
4. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of your request.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your account.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `text`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address to receive updates on your order.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, `text`, optional): Enter your address for delivery or shipping purposes.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, optional): Specify the date you would like to complete the order.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, `text`, optional): Describe the specific details of the order.

## Tips

* Please make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our customer support team.
* Please note that this form is only for wheel valve extension orders, and other services have separate forms for those specific services.
