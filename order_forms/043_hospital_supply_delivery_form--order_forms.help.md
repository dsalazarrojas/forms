# hospital_supply_delivery_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hospital supply delivery form is a document used to document and track hospital supply deliveries. It captures essential information about the delivery process, including customer details, delivery schedules, and supply status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form efficiently, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's First and Last Name in the "customer_name" field.
2. Enter the date of the delivery in the "delivery_date" field.
3. Select the supplier's name from the provided options in the "supplier_name" field.
4. Choose the order type as either "Urgent" or "Non-Urgent" in the "order_type" field.
5. Select the delivery address from the provided options in the "delivery_address" field.
6. Choose the delivery status as "Confirmed" or "Not Confirmed" in the "delivery_status" field.
7. Enter the contact person's name in the "contact_person" field.
8. Enter any relevant notes or comments in the "notes" field.
9. Enter the customer's email address in the "customer_email" field.
10. Enter the order ID in the "order_id" field.
11. Enter the supplier's email address in the "supplier_email" field.
12. Enter the contact person's phone number in the "contact_phone" field.
13. Enter the order description in the "order_description" field.
14. Select the delivery time as per your convenience in the "delivery_time" field.
15. Enter the hospital name in the "hospital_name" field.
16. Enter the facility name in the "facility_name" field.
17. Enter the contact person's name in the "contact_name" field.
18. Select the delivery date range as per your preference in the "delivery_date_range" field.
19. Enter any delivery notes in the "delivery_notes" field.
20. Enter any customer notes in the "customer_note" field.
21. Select the order status as "Open" or "Closed" in the "order_status" field.
22. Enter any supplier notes in the "supplier_notes" field.
23. Select the signature status as "Confirmed" or "Not Confirmed" in the "signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First and Last Name** (customer_name, text, required): Enter the customer's name, including first and last name.
* **Delivery Date** (delivery_date, date, required): Enter the date of the delivery.
* **Supplier Name** (supplier_name, text, required): Select the supplier's name from the provided options.
* **Order Type** (order_type, select_one, required): Choose the urgency of the delivery, either "Urgent" or "Non-Urgent".
* **Delivery Address** (delivery_address, select_multiple, required): Select the delivery address from the provided options.
* **Delivery Status** (delivery_status, select_one, required): Choose the status of the delivery, either "Confirmed" or "Not Confirmed".
* **Contact Person** (contact_person, text, required): Enter the contact person's name.
* **Notes** (notes, note, optional): Enter any relevant notes or comments about the delivery.
* **Email** (customer_email, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Order ID** (order_id, text, required): Enter the order ID number.
* **Supplier Email** (supplier_email, email, optional): Enter the supplier's email address.
* **Contact Number** (contact_phone, text, required): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Order Description** (order_description, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the order.
* **Delivery Time** (delivery_time, time, optional): Select the delivery time as per your convenience.
* **Hospital Name** (hospital_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the hospital.
* **Facility Name** (facility_name, text, optional): Enter the facility name.
* **Contact Name** (contact_name, text, optional): Enter the contact person's name.
* **Order Date** (order_date, date, optional): Enter the date of the order.
* **Delivery Date Range** (delivery_date_range, select_multiple, optional): Select the delivery date range as per your preference.
* **Delivery Notes** (delivery_notes, note, optional): Enter any delivery notes.
* **Customer Note** (customer_note, note, optional): Enter any customer notes.
* **Order Status** (order_status, select_one, optional): Choose the status of the order, either "Open" or "Closed".
* **Supplier Notes** (supplier_notes, note, optional): Enter any supplier notes.
* **Signature** (signature, select_one, required): Select the signature status, either "Confirmed" or "Not Confirmed".
