# exempt_organization_purchase_certificate - Help Guide
## Purpose
The exempt_organization_purchase_certificate form is used to report exempt organizations' purchases that are subject to special rules or regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the exempt organization type from the options provided.
2. Enter the name of the vendor or supplier.
3. Enter the date of the purchase transaction.
4. Enter the total value of the purchase transaction.
5. Provide a description of the purchase items.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exempt Organization** (Select): Choose the type of exempt organization that made the purchase (Nonprofit Organization or Other qualifying organization).
* **Vendor**: Enter the name of the organization or entity that made the purchase.
* **Purchase Date**: Enter the date on which the purchase was made.
* **Purchase Amount**: Enter the total value of the purchase transaction.
* **Purchase Items**: Briefly describe the items purchased.
* **Total Value**: Enter the total value of the purchase transaction.
