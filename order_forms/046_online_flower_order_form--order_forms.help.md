# Online Flower Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to provide information about their flower orders. Please fill in the required and optional fields to complete your order successfully.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Fill in your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Choose a date for delivery or pick-up in the "Date" field.
6. Select the type of order you would like to place in the "Order Type" field.
7. Choose the color of the flowers in the "Flower Color" field.
8. Select the shape of the flowers in the "Flower Shape" field.
9. Enter the quantity of flowers you would like to order in the "Quantity" field.
10. Fill in the price of your order in the "Price" field.
11. Choose your preferred payment method in the "Payment Method" field.
12. Confirm your order details in the "Confirm Order" field.
13. Agree to the order terms in the "Agree" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (Text, Required): Enter your name to help us identify you and fulfill your order.
* **Address** (Text, Required): Enter your address for delivery or pick-up.
* **Email** (Text, Required): Enter your email address for communication and order updates.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for any follow-up calls or questions.
* **Date** (Text, Optional): Choose a date for delivery or pick-up of your order.
* **Order Type** (Select One, Optional): Select the type of order you would like to place, such as a bouquet or a gift basket.
* **Flower Color** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the color of the flowers you would like to order.
* **Flower Shape** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the shape of the flowers you would like to order.
* **Quantity** (Number, Optional): Enter the quantity of flowers you would like to order.
* **Price** (Number, Optional): Fill in the price of your order.
* **Payment Method** (Select One, Optional): Choose your preferred payment method, such as Stripe or PayPal.
* **Total** (Number, Calculated): This field is automatically calculated based on your order details.
* **Note** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional notes or instructions for your order.
* **Confirm Order** (Text, Required): Confirm your order details to ensure accuracy.
* **Agree** (Select One, Required): Agree to the order terms before submitting your order.
