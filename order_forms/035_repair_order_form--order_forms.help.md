# Repair Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Repair Order Form is a tool used to gather essential information from customers about their repair orders. This form helps to ensure that all necessary details are captured accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information about the customer and their repair order.
2. Provide a clear and concise description of the reason for the repair.
3. Enter any additional notes or comments that may be relevant to the repair order.
4. Enter the date and time the repair order was created.
5. Review and submit the form to submit the order for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the customer's name as it appears on their order.
* **Contact Email (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address if you have one.
* **Contact Phone (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number if you have it.
* **Product Name (`product_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the product being repaired.
* **Product Model (`product_model`, text, optional): Enter the model number or description of the product being repaired.
* **Reason for Repair (`reason_for_repair`, text, optional): Enter a clear and concise description of why the repair is needed.
* **Description (`description`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the repair order.
* **Date Created (`date_created`, date, optional): Enter the date the repair order was created (will be auto-filled if not filled in).
* **Time Created (`time_created`, time, optional): Enter the time the repair order was created (will be auto-filled if not filled in).
* **Customer Notes (`customer_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional information or requests from the customer about the repair order.

Note: The fields marked as "optional" are not required to be filled, but it is recommended to provide as much information as possible to ensure efficient processing of the repair order.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information about the customer and their repair order to ensure prompt processing.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to your supervisor or the customer service team for assistance.
* Double-check the form for any errors or missing information before submitting it.
