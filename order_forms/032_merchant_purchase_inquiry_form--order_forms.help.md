# Merchant Purchase Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Merchant Purchase Inquiry Form is used by merchants to submit inquiries about purchasing products or services from other vendors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name and any other contact information in the **Buyer Name** field.
2.  Provide your contact information, including your email and phone number, in the **Buyer Contact Info** field.
3.  Enter the email address of the buyer in the **Buyer Email** field.
4.  Enter the phone number of the buyer in the **Buyer Phone** field.
5.  Enter the name of the product you are inquiring about in the **Product Name** field.
6.  Enter the product code in the **Product Code** field (if applicable).
7.  Enter the quantity of the product you are inquiring about in the **Requested Quantity** field.
8.  Enter the delivery address of the buyer in the **Delivery Address** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Inquiry Details**: Enter any additional information about your inquiry.
*   **Buyer Name**: Enter your name as it would appear on your business card or letterhead.
*   **Buyer Contact Info**: Enter your company's contact information, including your email and phone number.
*   **Buyer Email**: Enter the email address of the person who will be responsible for communication about the inquiry.
*   **Buyer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who will be responsible for communication about the inquiry.
*   **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product you are inquiring about.
*   **Product Code**: Enter the product code (if applicable).
*   **Requested Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the product you are inquiring about.
*   **Delivery Address**: Enter the delivery address of the buyer.

## Tips
*   Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date contact information.
*   Be as specific as possible when entering product details to ensure that the seller can process your request efficiently.
*   If you have any additional requests or requirements, please include them in the **Inquiry Details** field.
*   The form will be reviewed by the receiving party for further action.
