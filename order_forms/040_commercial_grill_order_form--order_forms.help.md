# Commercial Grill Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to place an order for a commercial grade grill. The form collects customer information, grill preferences, and order details to ensure accurate processing and delivery of the ordered grill.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked as "Required" in the table below.
2. Select the desired options for the grill features and accessories.
3. Choose the preferred delivery method and date.
4. Select the payment method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name or business name.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter your business name if applicable.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for order confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact number for any communication.
* **Grill Selection** (`section_header_grill_selection`, note, not required): Select the type of grill you want to order from the options below.
* **Grill Type** (`grill_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of grill you want to order (Gas, Charcoal, Electric, Pellet, Infrared, or Hybrid).
* **Grill Size** (`grill_size`, select_one, required): Select the cooking area size of the grill you want (Compact, Small, Medium, Large, or Extra Large).
* **Grill Brand** (`grill_brand`, select_one, required): Choose the preferred brand of the grill (Weber, Traeger, Napoleon, Broil King, Char-Broil, DCs, Lynx, or Other).
* **Specific Model** (`specific_model`, text, not required): Enter the model number of the grill if known.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of grills you want to order.
* **Features and Options** (`section_header_features`, note, not required): Select the features and options you want to include with your grill order.
* **Side Burner** (`side_burner`, select_one, required): Choose whether you want a side burner with your grill.
* **Rotisserie Kit** (`rotisserie_kit`, select_one, required): Select whether you want a rotisserie kit with your grill.
* **Smoker Box** (`smoker_box`, select_one, required): Choose whether you want a smoker attachment with your grill.
* **Grill Cover** (`grill_cover`, select_one, required): Select whether you want a protective grill cover with your order.
* **Grill Tool Set** (`tool_set`, select_one, required): Choose whether you want a basic or premium tool set with your grill.
* **Additional Accessories** (`section_header_accessories`, note, not required): Select any additional accessories you want to order.
* **Additional Burners** (`additional_burners`, number, not required): Enter the number of additional burners you want to order.
* **Grill Brush** (`grill_brush`, select_one, required): Select whether you want a cleaning brush with your grill.
* **Meat Thermometer** (`thermometer`, select_one, required): Choose whether you want a meat thermometer with your grill.
* **Propane Tank** (`propane_tank`, select_one, required): Select whether you want a propane tank with your grill.
* **Charcoal** (`charcoal`, select_one, required): Select whether you want charcoal with your grill.
* **Delivery and Installation** (`section_header_delivery`, note, not required): Select your preferred delivery method and delivery date.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, required): Enter your complete delivery address.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select_one, required): Choose the delivery method (Standard, Express, Store Pickup, or White Glove Delivery).
* **Installation Service** (`installation_service`, select_one, required): Select whether you want professional installation with your order.
* **Preferred Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required): Enter your preferred delivery date.
* **Payment** (`section_header_payment`, note, not required): Select your preferred payment method.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose the payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, Purchase Order, Wire Transfer, Check, or Financing).
* **Subtotal** (`subtotal`, number, required): View the calculated subtotal of your order.
* **Delivery Fee** (`delivery_fee`, number, required): View the delivery fee of your order.
* **Installation Fee** (`installation_fee`, number, not required): View the installation fee of your order.
* **Total Amount** (`total_amount`, number, required): View the final total of your order.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, not required): Enter any special requests you have for your order.

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Select all relevant options and features for your grill order.
* Contact us if you have any questions or need further assistance with your order.
* Please note that the delivery and installation fees may vary based on your location and other factors.
