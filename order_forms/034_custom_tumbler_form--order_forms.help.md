# custom_tumbler_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The custom_tumbler_form is used to collect information about custom tumbler orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the quantity of tumblers you want to order.
2. Select the color of the tumbler from the list of available options.
3. Choose the type of tumbler by selecting "Yes" or "No".
4. Select the size of the tumbler by choosing "Yes" or "No".
5. Choose the shape of the tumbler by selecting "Yes" or "No" for multiple shapes.
6. Enter any additional notes about your order.
7. Enter your email address.
8. Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **quantity** (Number): Enter the number of tumblers you want to order.
* **tumbler_type** (Select One): Choose "Yes" to order a tumbler with the selected attributes, or "No" to order a plain tumbler.
* **color** (Select Multiple): Select the colors you want for your custom tumblers. You can choose multiple colors.
* **size** (Select One): Choose "Yes" to order a large tumbler, or "No" to order a small one.
* **shape** (Select Multiple): Select the shapes you want for your custom tumblers. You can choose multiple shapes.
* **notes** (Note): Enter any additional comments or instructions about your order.
* **email** (Email): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **phone** (Text): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your order.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct quantity of tumblers you want to order.
* Double-check your selection for color, size, and shape to ensure it matches your desired custom tumbler.
* Provide accurate contact information so we can reach out to you about your order.
