# Distribution Plan Overview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you create a distribution plan for your organization. It guides you through the key components of your distribution process, timeline, and resource plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer "Yes" or "No" to whether the distribution process is in place.
2. Input your timeline details in the "timeline" field.
3. Write a plan for resources in the "resource_plan" field.
4. Select the target areas from the options provided.
5. Provide feedback or comments in the "review" field (optional).
6. Click the "Submit" button to send the form for processing and review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **distribution_process** (Yes/No): This field asks whether the distribution process is in place. Answer "Yes" if it is, and "No" if it's not.
* **timeline** (Date): This is where you input your timeline information. Please use the correct format for dates.
* **resource_plan** (Text): This field is for writing a plan for resources. Please be specific and provide enough details.
* **target_area** (Select Multiple): Select all the target areas that apply to your distribution plan.
* **review** (Note): This field is for providing feedback or comments. It's optional and for internal use only.
* **Submit** (Text): Click this button to send the form for processing and review.
