# Supplier Payment Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a supplier's payment authorization. It is used to track and verify payments made to suppliers.

## How To Complete This Form

### To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the Order Number in the "Order Number" field.
* Enter the supplier's name in the "Supplier Name" field.
* Select the "Payment Method" for the payment (Yes or No).
* Select the "Payment Terms" for the payment (Yes or No).
* Enter the "Amount Paid" for the payment.
* Select the "Payment Status" for the payment (Active or Inactive).
* Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
* Enter the supplier's email in the "Email to Supplier" field.
* Enter the supplier's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
* Select the tool assigned to the supplier in the "Assigned Tool" field (Chatjimmy or Other).
* Select the "Category" for the payment (Yes or No).
* Enter a description of the payment in the "Description" field.
* Enter the supplier's ID in the "Supplier ID" field.
* Select the date of the payment in the "Order Date" field (if required).
* Select the date of the payment in the "Payment Date" field (if required).
* Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Notes 2" field.
* Enter the payment terms for the payment (if not using the standard terms).
* Enter the amount paid for the payment (if not using the standard amount).
* Enter the supplier's name in the "Supplier Name 2" field (if required).
* Enter the supplier's ID in the "Supplier Id 2" field (if required).
* Select the date of the payment in the "Payment Date 2" field (if required).
* Select the date of the payment in the "Amount Paid 2" field (if required).
* Select the payment method for the payment in the "Payment Method 2" field (if required).
* Select the payment status for the payment in the "Payment Status 2" field (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Number** (1, text, required): The order number is a unique identifier for the order. Enter the order number to associate the payment with the correct order.
* **Supplier Name** (2, text, required): Enter the name of the supplier.
* **Date of Payment** (3, date, optional): Enter the date of the payment. If not required, leave blank.
* **Amount Paid** (4, number, required): Enter the amount paid to the supplier.
* **Payment Method** (5, select_one, optional): Select the method of payment (Yes or No).
* **Payment Terms** (6, select_multiple, optional): Select the payment terms (Yes or No).
* **Payment Status** (7, select_one, optional): Select the payment status (Active or Inactive).
* **Notes** (8, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the payment.
* **Email to Supplier** (9, email, optional): Enter the supplier's email address.
* **Phone Number** (10, text, optional): Enter the supplier's phone number.
* **Assigned Tool** (11, select_one, optional): Select the tool assigned to the supplier (Chatjimmy or Other).
* **Category** (12, select_one, optional): Select the category for the payment (Yes or No).
* **Description** (13, text, required): Enter a description of the payment.
* **Supplier ID** (14, text, required): Enter the supplier's ID.
* **Order Date** (15, date, optional): Enter the date of the order.
* **Payment Date** (16, date, optional): Enter the date of the payment.
* **Notes 2** (17, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the payment.
* **Payment Terms 1** (18, text, optional): Enter the payment terms for the payment (if not using the standard terms).
* **Amount** (19, number, optional): Enter the amount paid for the payment (if not using the standard amount).
* **Supplier Name 2** (20, text, optional): Enter the supplier's name.
* **Supplier Id 2** (21, text, optional): Enter the supplier's ID.
* **Payment Date 2** (22, date, optional): Enter the date of the payment.
* **Amount Paid 2** (23, number, optional): Enter the amount paid for the payment.
* **Payment Method 2** (24, text, optional): Enter the payment method for the payment.
* **Payment Status 2** (25, text, optional): Select the payment status for the payment (Active or Inactive).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check the accuracy of all entered information.
* Use the correct payment method and status for each payment.
* Enter any additional notes or comments that may be relevant to the payment.
