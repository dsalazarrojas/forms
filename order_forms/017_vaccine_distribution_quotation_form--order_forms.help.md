# Vaccine Distribution Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vaccine distribution quotation form is designed to facilitate the management of vaccine orders. It collects essential information for both customers and suppliers, ensuring accurate tracking and management of vaccine orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To use the vaccine distribution quotation form effectively, follow these steps:

1.  Select the **Vaccine Order Details** field to choose the type of vaccine order you wish to place.
2.  In the **Customer Details** field, select whether you are a customer or supplier.
3.  For the **Delivery Details** field, choose the delivery status of your order.
4.  Enter your **Delivery Address** in the text field provided.
5.  Select the **Delivery Date** and **Delivery Time** for your order.
6.  Fill in your **Customer Name**, **Supplier Name**, and other relevant contact information as required.
7.  Fill in the **Vaccine Name**, **Vaccine ID**, **Customer Order ID**, **Supplier ID**, **Delivery ID**, and **Customer ID** fields with the necessary information.
8.  Finally, select the **Delivery Status** to indicate whether your order has been delivered, is pending, or not delivered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Vaccine Order Details
* **Vaccine Order Details** (Select One): Choose the type of vaccine order you wish to place.
### Customer Details
* **Customer Details** (Select Multiple): Select whether you are a customer or supplier.
### Supplier Details
* **Supplier Details** (Select Multiple): Choose whether you are a supplier.
### Delivery Details
* **Delivery Details** (Select Multiple): Choose the delivery status of your order.
### Delivery Address
* **Delivery Address** (Text): Enter your delivery address to ensure accurate order tracking.
### Delivery Date
* **Delivery Date** (Date): Select the date for your order delivery.
### Delivery Time
* **Delivery Time** (Time): Select the time for your order delivery.
### Customer Name
* **Customer Name** (Text): Fill in your name as the customer.
### Supplier Name
* **Supplier Name** (Text): Fill in the supplier's name.
### Email
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
### Phone
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number for contact.
### Vaccine Name
* **Vaccine Name** (Text): Fill in the vaccine name.
### Vaccine ID
* **Vaccine ID** (Number): Enter the vaccine ID.
### Customer Order ID
* **Customer Order ID** (Number): Enter the customer order ID.
### Supplier ID
* **Supplier ID** (Number): Enter the supplier ID.
### Delivery ID
* **Delivery ID** (Number): Enter the delivery ID.
### Customer ID
* **Customer ID** (Number): Enter the customer ID.
### Supplier Customer ID
* **Supplier Customer ID** (Number): Enter the supplier customer ID.
### Order Date
* **Order Date** (Date): Select the date for your order.
### Supplier Order ID
* **Supplier Order ID** (Number): Enter the supplier order ID.
### Customer Order Date
* **Customer Order Date** (Date): Select the date for your order.
### Supplier Customer Order ID
* **Supplier Customer Order ID** (Number): Enter the supplier customer order ID.
### Delivery Status
* **Delivery Status** (Select One): Choose the delivery status of your order.

Tips:
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately to avoid any issues with order processing.
* Use the correct format for date and time entries (dd/mm/yyyy for dates and hh:mm for times).
* Double-check the supplier and customer information to ensure accuracy and avoid duplication.
