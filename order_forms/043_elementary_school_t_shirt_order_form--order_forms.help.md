# Elementary School T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact and order information from students or parents for elementary school t-shirt orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your school name in the "School Name" field.
4. Select the quantity of t-shirts you wish to order in the "Quantity" field.
5. Enter the price of the order in the "Price" field.
6. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" field (Stripe, Cash, or PayPal).
7. Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field.
8. Enter your shipping city in the "Shipping City" field.
9. Enter your shipping state in the "Shipping State" field.
10. Enter your shipping zip code in the "Shipping Zip" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, Optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, Optional): Enter your last name.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, Optional): Enter the name of your elementary school.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, Optional): Enter the number of t-shirts you wish to order.
* **Price** (`price`, number, Optional): Enter the total price of your order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, Required): Select your preferred payment method (Stripe, Cash, or PayPal).
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, Optional): Enter your shipping address.
* **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`, text, Optional): Enter the city where you wish to have your order shipped.
* **Shipping State** (`shipping_state`, text, Optional): Enter the state where you wish to have your order shipped.
* **Shipping Zip** (`shipping_zip`, text, Optional): Enter the zip code where you wish to have your order shipped.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure timely and correct order fulfillment.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
