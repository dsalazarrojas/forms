# Medical Device Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request medical devices for patients in a clinical setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the patient's information on the "patient_info" page.
2.  On the second page, provide details about the medical device being requisitioned.
3.  On the third page, specify the quantity of the device needed.
4.  Enter the date of the device request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **patient_info**: Enter the patient's name, ID number, and any other relevant details. This page is used to identify the patient for whom the device is being requisitioned.
- **medical_device_details**: Enter a description of the medical device being requisitioned, including its type, brand, and model.
- **medical_device_quantity**: Specify the quantity of the device needed. This can be a single unit or multiple units, depending on the patient's requirement.
- **request_date**: Enter the date when the device was requested by the patient or healthcare professional. This will help track the request timeline.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
- If you have already submitted a form for the same patient and device, do not resubmit.
- Please ensure that you have the patient's consent before submitting this form.
