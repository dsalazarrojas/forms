# Account Recharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Account Recharge Form is used to collect account information and recharge details for account management and payment processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review and fill out the form carefully, ensuring you provide accurate information.
2. Start by completing the account information section.
3. Select the desired recharge amount and payment method.
4. If you selected a custom amount, enter it in the corresponding field.
5. Choose whether to enable auto-recharge and set the threshold (if enabled).
6. Enter any promotional or discount code you have (if applicable).
7. Add any additional notes or comments (if needed).
8. Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Account Holder Name**: Enter the name of the account holder, which is the person associated with the account.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address associated with your account.
* **Account ID or Username**: Enter your account ID or username.
* **Phone Number (optional)**: Enter your phone number (if provided by the account holder).
* **Recharge Amount**: Select the desired recharge amount from the options provided.
* **Custom Amount (if selected above)**: Enter the custom amount if you chose to select a custom amount in the previous field.
* **Payment Method**: Choose the payment method to be used for the recharge (e.g., credit card, debit card, PayPal, bank transfer, or gift card).
* **Enable Auto-Recharge**: Select whether to automatically recharge when the balance falls below a set threshold (if enabled).
* **Auto-Recharge Threshold (if enabled)**: Enter the threshold amount below which the account will be automatically recharged (if enabled).
* **Promotional or Discount Code (optional)**: Enter any promotional or discount code you have (if applicable).
* **Additional Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the recharge (if needed).
