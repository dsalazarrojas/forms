# Fitness Studio Display Rack Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to provide details for ordering a display rack for their fitness studio. It collects essential information for delivery and management of the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Display Rack Details** section with the relevant information about the display rack you are interested in ordering.
2. Provide your **Delivery Details** in the next section, including the delivery address and any special instructions.
3. Choose your **Preferred Dates** for delivery in the section that follows.
4. Enter your **Requester Details** in the final section, including your name and any other relevant contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Display Rack Details (Display Rack Details)**: Enter a brief description of the display rack you are interested in ordering. This will help us understand your needs and preferences.
* **Delivery Details (Delivery Details)**: Provide the delivery address where you would like to receive the display rack. If there are any specific instructions or requests for delivery, please include them here.
* **Preferred Dates (Preferred Dates)**: Select the dates when you would like to receive the display rack. If you have a specific date in mind, please enter it here.
* **Requester Details (Requester Details)**: Enter your name and any other relevant contact information. This will help us get back to you with updates and information about your order.
* **Order Management (Order Management)**: Please note that this field is not currently active and is reserved for future use.
