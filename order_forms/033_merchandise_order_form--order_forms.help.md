# Merchandise Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Merchandise Order Form is designed to collect essential information from customers for ordering merchandise. It's crucial to complete this form accurately to ensure correct processing of the order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the **Customer Info** page with your name, address, and email.
2. Proceed to the **Merchandise** page to provide details about the merchandise you're ordering.
3. On the **Contact Info** page, provide your contact information, if applicable.
4. Move to the **Shipping** page to input shipping details.
5. The **Order Comments** page is optional and can be used to add any additional comments about your order.
6. On the **Payment Method** page, select your preferred payment method.
7. Finally, review all pages for accuracy before submitting your order.
