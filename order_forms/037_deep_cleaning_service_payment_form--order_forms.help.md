# Deep Cleaning Service Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Deep Cleaning Service Payment Form is designed to collect payment information for customers who wish to book a deep cleaning service.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Deep Cleaning Service Payment Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer name, service provider, service date, service time, service price, and service details.
2. Enter your customer email address and phone number for communication purposes.
3. Select the payment method you wish to use (Bank Transfer, PayPal, or Card).
4. Confirm the total price of the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name (text)**: A field to enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Service Provider (select_one)**: Choose whether or not you are the service provider.
* **Service Date (date)**: The date on which the deep cleaning service will take place.
* **Service Time (time)**: The time on which the deep cleaning service will take place.
* **Service Price (number)**: Enter the price for the service, which will be used for payment.
* **Service Details (text)**: Add any additional details about the service, such as any specific areas to be cleaned.
* **Customer Email (email)**: Enter your email address to facilitate communication about the service.
* **Customer Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Payment Method (select_one)**: Choose how you would like to make the payment (Bank Transfer, PayPal, or Card).
* **Total Price (number)**: Confirm the total price for the service before proceeding with payment.
