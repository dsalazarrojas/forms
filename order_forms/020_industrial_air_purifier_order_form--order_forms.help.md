# industrial_air_purifier_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The industrial air purifier order form is designed to collect information for purchasing industrial air purifiers. Please fill out this form to place an order.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:
1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select the desired air purifier model from the "Air Purifier Model" dropdown menu.
5. Enter the quantity of air purifiers you wish to purchase.
6. Enter your shipping details in the "Ship to" field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (text, required): Enter your name as it should be displayed on the order receipt.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address that we will use to contact you about your order.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Air Purifier Model** (select_one, optional): Select the model of air purifier that you wish to purchase from the dropdown menu.
* **Quantity** (number, optional): Enter the number of air purifiers you wish to purchase.
* **Ship to** (text, optional): Enter the shipping details for your order.
## Tips
* Please ensure that you have reviewed the available air purifier models and their specifications before filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team for assistance.
* This form is a one-time order form, so you will not need to fill it out multiple times.
