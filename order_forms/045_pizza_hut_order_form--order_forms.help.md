# Pizza Hut Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Pizza Hut Order Form is designed to collect information from customers for their orders, allowing for efficient and accurate order processing.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select your delivery option from the "Delivery Option" dropdown menu.
2. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
3. Provide your name in the "Name" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter the delivery person's name in the "Delivery Name" field.
6. Enter the delivery address in the "Delivery Address" field.
7. Enter the delivery time in the "Delivery Time" field, in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30 for 2:30 PM).
8. Add any additional order details in the "Order Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Option (Delivery Option, select_one, required/optional)**: Choose how you'd like to receive your order: Pick-up, Delivery, or Carry-Out.
* **Address (Address, text, required/optional)**: Enter your address for pick-up or delivery.
* **Name (Name, text, required/optional)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification or contact information.
* **Phone (Phone, text, required/optional)**: Enter your phone number for contact and order updates.
* **Delivery Name (Delivery Name, text, required/optional)**: Enter the name of the person receiving the delivery.
* **Delivery Address (Delivery Address, text, required/optional)**: Enter the address for delivery.
* **Delivery Time (Delivery Time, time, required/optional)**: Enter the time for delivery in 24-hour format.
* **Order Details (Order Details, note, required/optional)**: Add any special instructions or comments about your order.
