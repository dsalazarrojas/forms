# Party Equipment Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for party equipment reservations. It will help us ensure that we have all the necessary details to provide the correct equipment for your event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately. If any field is left blank, we may contact you for additional information.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for each field as instructed.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): This field is for providing a brief description of the event.
* **Equipment Needed** (`equipment_needed`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the party equipment you require for your event. This includes Microphones, Speakers, and Projectors.
* **Quantity** (`equipment_quantity`, number, optional): Enter the number of each equipment you need.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your event.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of your event.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of your event.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your event.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you regarding your event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of event (Meeting, Party, or Conference).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or details about your event.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): This is the final step to submit your form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately.
* Double-check your contact information for correct formatting.
* If you have any questions, feel free to contact us.
