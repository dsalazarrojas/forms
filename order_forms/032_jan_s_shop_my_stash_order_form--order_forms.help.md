# jans_shop_my_stash_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "jans_shop_my_stash_order_form" is a form used for customers to place orders from the Jans' shop. It collects essential information for the order process.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" from the "product" field to indicate the status of your order.
2. Enter the quantity of the products you wish to order.
3. Enter the order date in the "order_date" field.
4. Enter the total price of the order.
5. Add any customer notes regarding your order.
6. Enter your email address to receive order updates.
7. Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer** (`customer`, 4, Optional): Enter your customer information for easier order tracking and communication.
* **product** (`product`, select_one, Optional): Select "Yes" if you have products available for order, or "No" if not.
* **quantity** (`quantity`, 3, Optional): Enter the quantity of the products you wish to order.
* **order_date** (`date`, 4, Optional): Enter the date of your order.
* **total_price** (`total_price`, 5, Optional): Enter the total price of your order.
* **customer_note** (`customer_note`, 6, Optional): Add any additional notes about your order.
* **email** (`email`, 7, Optional): Enter your email address to receive order updates.
* **phone** (`phone`, 8, Optional): Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you.
## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your order details for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us through our customer support channel.
* We will be in touch with you shortly after form submission to confirm your order.
