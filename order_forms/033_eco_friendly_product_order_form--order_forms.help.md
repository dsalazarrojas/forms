# Eco Friendly Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Eco Friendly Product Order Form" is a tool designed to collect customer details, product information, and order data for eco-friendly products. This form helps customers submit orders for products, while also providing them with the opportunity to leave order notes and shipping details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Scroll down the form pages to select which sections you need to fill out.
2. Start by filling in the "Customer Details" page with your name and contact information.
3. Move on to the "Product Details" page and provide information about the products you want to order.
4. Then, proceed to the "Payment Info" page to enter your payment details.
5. Review and confirm your order on the "Order Summary" page.
6. Provide shipping information on the "Shipping Info" page.
7. Finally, leave any additional notes or comments on the "Order Notes" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details**: Enter your name, address, phone number, and other relevant contact information.
* **Product Details**: Provide a detailed description of the product you would like to order, including any specific features or quantities needed.
* **Payment Info**: Enter your payment method and details, such as credit card or bank information.
* **Order Summary**: This is a review page to confirm your order details before submitting.
* **Shipping Info**: Enter your shipping address and any additional shipping instructions.
* **Order Notes**: Leave any additional comments or instructions for the seller.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address so the seller can contact you about your order.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to avoid any issues with your order.
* Double-check your order details before submitting to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the seller directly.
* You can modify or add to this form as needed, or use it as a base for your own forms.
