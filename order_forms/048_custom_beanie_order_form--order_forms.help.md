# Custom Beanie Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Beanie Order Form is a tool used to gather information from customers for their custom beanie orders. It helps sales teams to understand the customer's preferences for their beanie and process the order efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant options in the **Customer Details** section to provide information about the customer.
2. Choose the desired options in the **Order Details** section for the beanie order.
3. Confirm your order details in the **Order Confirmation** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details (Select One)**:
    * **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's full name.
    * **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email address.
    * **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Order Details (Select Multiple)**:
    * **Color**: Choose the color of the beanie.
    * **Size**: Select the size of the beanie.
    * **Beanie Type**: Choose the type of beanie the customer wants.
    * **Beanie Color**: Select the color of the beanie.
    * **Beanie Style**: Select the style of the beanie.
* **Order Confirmation 1/2**: This field is used for additional order confirmations, please enter the number for order confirmation 1.
* **Order Confirmation 2/2**: This field is used for additional order confirmations, please enter the number for order confirmation 2.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately to ensure timely order processing.
- Ensure that the customer's contact information is correct to facilitate order delivery.
- If you are unsure about any of the options, contact the sales team for assistance.
