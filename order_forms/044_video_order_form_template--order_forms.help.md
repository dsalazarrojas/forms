# Video Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from clients for video order requests. It includes fields for client details, project specifics, and review and confirmation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the client's name.
2. Enter the client's email address.
3. Enter the client's phone number.
4. Enter the project name.
5. Select the deadline for the video project.
6. Enter your electronic signature as proof of acceptance.
7. Select whether the order is approved, rejected, or in progress.
8. Enter your electronic signature as client acknowledgement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Enter the client's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the client's email address where they can be reached.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter the client's phone number where they can be reached.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, `text`, required): Enter a name for the video project.
* **Deadline** (`deadline`, `date`, required): Select the deadline for the video project.
* **E-Signature** (`e_signature`, `text`, required): Enter your electronic signature as proof of acceptance.
* **Review and Confirm** (`review_and_confirm`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the order is approved, rejected, or in progress.
* **Client E-Sign** (`client_signature`, `text`, required): Enter your electronic signature as client acknowledgement.

## Tips
* Always ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* Please review and confirm the order before submitting it.
* If you need to make any changes, please do not hesitate to contact us.
