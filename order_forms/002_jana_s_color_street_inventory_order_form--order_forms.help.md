# Jana's Color Street Inventory Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers for inventory orders, allowing sales or inventory teams to process orders accurately and provide excellent service.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by providing your contact information, including email, phone number, name, and address.
2. Select the desired color stations for your order.
3. Enter any additional order details or notes in the "Order Details" field.
4. Specify the quantity of the item you wish to order.
5. If you have any customer notes or preferences, enter them in the "Customer Note" field.
6. Finally, review your entire form before submitting it to ensure all necessary information is provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (`Customer Info`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Customer E-Mail** (`Customer E-Mail`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`Customer Phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Name** (`Customer Name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **Color Stations** (`Color Stations`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the desired color stations for your order.
* **Customer Address** (`Customer Address`, text, required/optional): Enter your address, without dashes.
* **Order Details** (`Order Details`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or information about your order.
* **Quantity** (`Quantity`, number, required/optional): Enter the quantity of the item you wish to order.
* **Customer Note** (`Customer Note`, text, required/optional): Enter any customer notes or preferences.
* **Color Stations 2** (`Color Stations 2`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the desired color stations for this order (second part).
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date for your order.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time for your order.
* **Color Stations 3** (`Color Stations 3`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the desired color stations for this order (third part).
* **Customer Zip** (`Customer Zip`, text, required/optional): Enter your zip code without dashes.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter all necessary information, especially contact details and color stations.
* Double-check your form before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
