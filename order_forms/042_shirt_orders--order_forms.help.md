# Shirt Orders - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers for their shirt orders, which will be used for further processing and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the "Customer Details" page with the required information: name, email, phone number, order, and status.
* Enter the additional information as required or as needed.
* Select the payment method and amount for the order.
* Choose the shipping method for the order.
* Fill out any additional information fields as necessary.
* Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`customer_details`): Enter the customer's name.
* **Email** (`customer_email`): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone** (`customer_phone`): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Date** (`customer_date`): Enter the date of the order.
* **Time** (`customer_time`): Enter the time of the order.
* **Order** (`customer_order`): Select the order number.
* **Note** (`customer_note`): Enter any additional notes about the order.
* **Comments** (`customer_comments`): Enter any comments or messages from the customer.
* **Total** (`customer_total`): Enter the total amount of the order.
* **Status** (`customer_status`): Select the status of the order (Active or Inactive).
* **Status Reason** (`customer_status_reason`): Enter the reason for the status change.
* **Payment Method** (`customer_payment_method`): Select the payment method (Cash, Card, Bank Transfer).
* **Amount** (`customer_amount`): Enter the amount paid for the order.
* **Shipping Method** (`customer_shipping_method`): Select whether shipping is required or not.
* **Shipping** (`customer_shipping`): Enter any additional shipping information.
* **Additional Info 1-8** (`customer_additional_info_1-8`): Enter any additional information as needed.
* **Customer** (`customer_customer`): Enter the name of the customer.
