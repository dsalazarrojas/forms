# Custom Barcode Label Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for custom barcode label orders. It helps customers provide details about their orders and is typically filled out by the customer or customer service team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, indicated by a red asterisk (\*) in the field label.
2. Enter your information in the designated fields, using the format and instructions provided for each field.
3. Review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (text): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification or as you would like it to appear on the order.
* **Order Specifications** (text): Provide a brief description of your order, including any special requests or instructions for the fulfillment of the order.
* **Artwork** (text): Enter any relevant artwork or images associated with your order.
* **Quantity** (number): Enter the number of labels you would like to order.
* **Unit Price** (number): Enter the price per unit of the item being ordered.
* **Payment Method** (select multiple): Select one or more of the payment options provided.
* **Payment Information** (text): Enter any additional payment information required for processing the order.
* **Shipping Details** (select one): Select the shipping details that apply to your order.
* **Special Instructions** (text): Enter any special instructions or notes related to your order.
* **Order Date** (date): Enter the date you would like to receive the order.
* **Order Time** (time): Enter the time you would like to receive the order.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address to receive updates on your order.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number to receive updates on your order.
* **Customer Message** (text): Enter any additional comments or requests you have for the order.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form or your order, please contact our customer service team.
