# ubam_hostess_rewards_order_form_for_jenns_team - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ubam_hostess_rewards_order_form_for_jenns_team form is used to record orders from customers for rewards from Jenn's Team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields with your information:
	* Enter a name to identify yourself.
	* Enter an email address that we can use to contact you.
	* Enter your phone number so we can communicate with you.
	* Select the date you'd like to receive your order.
	* Select the time of day you'd like to receive your order.
2. Click the submission button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **<no label> (form_submission)**: This field is a free text area for you to describe your order.
* **Name (name)**: Enter your name so we can identify you and address the order to you.
* **Email (email)**: Enter the email address that you'd like us to contact you with updates about your order.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number so we can communicate with you about the order.
* **Order Date (order_date)**: Select the date you'd like to receive your order.
* **Order Time (order_time)**: Select the time of day you'd like to receive your order.
