# storage_service_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for inquiring about storage services. It guides the user through a series of questions to gather necessary information for storage service provision. The form is typically used by individuals or departments seeking storage space or services for their specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the basic information about your inquiry in page 1.
2. Answer the question about whether you have a specific storage service in mind on page 2.
3. Select the services you are interested in on page 3.
4. Specify the date you need the storage service for on page 4.
5. Provide a preferred time slot for the storage service on page 5.
6. Add any additional information or notes about your storage service inquiry on page 6.
7. Enter your email address on page 7.
8. Provide any additional information on page 8.
9. Confirm your storage service inquiry in page 9.
10. Provide any further details or comments on page 10.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Page 1: Storage Service Inquiry** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): This page provides a space to enter any additional information about your storage service inquiry.
	* Please enter a brief description of your storage service request.
*   Example: "I need storage space for my project files."
*   Please keep this concise and relevant to your needs.
- **Have a Specific Storage Service in Mind?** (`storage_service_inquiry_form_page_2`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question will help us understand if you have a particular storage service in mind.
	* Please answer "Yes" if you have a specific storage service in mind.
	* Please answer "No" if you are open to suggestions.
- **Services Interested In** (`storage_service_inquiry_form_page_3`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This section allows you to select the storage services you are interested in.
	* Select all the services that apply to your storage service inquiry.
*   Example: "Data Storage", "Climate Control", "Security".
*   Please note that you can select multiple services.
- **Date Needed** (`storage_service_inquiry_form_page_4`, `date`, required/optional): This field is used to specify the date you need the storage service for.
	* Please enter the date in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
*   Example: "02/15/2024".
*   Please note that you must enter a date in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
- **Preferred Time Slot** (`storage_service_inquiry_form_page_5`, `time`, required/optional): This field is used to specify a preferred time slot for the storage service.
	* Please enter the time in the format "HH:MM AM/PM".
*   Example: "09:00 AM".
*   Please note that you must enter a time in the format "HH:MM AM/PM".
- **Additional Information** (`storage_service_inquiry_form_page_6`, `note`, required/optional): This field is used to add any additional information about your storage service inquiry.
	* Please enter any additional details or notes about your storage service request.
	* Example: "I need the storage space to be climate controlled."
- **Email** (`storage_service_inquiry_form_page_7`, `email`, required/optional): This field is used to enter your email address.
	* Please enter your email address.
	* Example: "your-email@example.com".
*   Please note that this will be used as a contact method.
- **Additional Information (if needed)** (`storage_service_inquiry_form_page_8`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter any further information about your storage service inquiry.
	* Please enter any additional details or notes about your storage service request.
*   Example: "I have special equipment that needs to be stored."
- **Confirm Inquiry** (`storage_service_inquiry_form_page_9`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to confirm your storage service inquiry.
	* Please enter a confirmation of your storage service request.
	* Example: "I confirm this is a genuine storage service inquiry."
- **Any Further Comments** (`storage_service_inquiry_form_page_10`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter any further comments or details about your storage service inquiry.
	* Please enter any additional information or comments about your storage service request.
*   Example: "I would like to inquire about storage services for my company."
