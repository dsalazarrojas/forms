# Hardware Purchase Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hardware Purchase Payment Form is a tool designed to collect payment information from businesses for hardware purchases. This form is intended for business representatives to provide accurate and timely payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully:

1.  Enter the business name where the hardware purchase was made.
2.  Provide the date of the purchase.
3.  Enter the total cost of the hardware purchase.
4.  Choose the method of payment used for this purchase.
5.  Enter the company email for communication purposes.
6.  Enter the business phone number for further contact.
7.  Provide the address of the business where the purchase was made.
8.  Enter the order ID number.
9.  Enter the payment date.
10.  Leave a note about the payment (optional).
11.  Select the status of the payment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): The name of the business that made the hardware purchase.
*   **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, date, required): The date when the hardware purchase was made.
*   **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, number, required): The total cost of the hardware purchase.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): The method used for the payment, which can be Cash or Credit Card.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required): The email of the business for further communication and contact.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): The phone number of the business.
*   **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, required): The address of the business where the hardware purchase was made.
*   **Order ID** (`order_id`, text, required): The identifier for the order.
*   **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required): The date when the payment was made.
*   **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Any further comments or notes about the payment.
*   **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required): The status of the payment, which can be Pending, Paid, Partially Paid, or Failed.
*   **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required): The amount paid for the hardware purchase.

## Tips
*   Ensure all fields are accurately filled out to prevent errors in payment processing.
*   Use the payment status options accurately, as they will influence the payment process.
*   Enter the correct payment method to avoid delays or issues.
