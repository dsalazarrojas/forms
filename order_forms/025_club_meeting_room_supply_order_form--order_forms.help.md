# Club Meeting Room Supply Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Club Meeting Room Supply Order Form is a tool used by meeting organizers and staff to order supplies and equipment for club meetings and events. This form helps streamline the process of ordering necessary items and ensures that all information is accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the order date for the meeting or event.
2. Enter the name and email address of the person placing the order.
3. Enter your phone number (optional).
4. Enter the name of the club or organization.
5. Choose the date of the meeting or event.
6. Select the time of the meeting or event.
7. Enter the name of the meeting room or location.
8. Estimate the expected number of attendees.
9. Choose the supplies and equipment needed for the meeting.
10. If necessary, specify the quantity of coffee and bottled water required.
11. Select the type of snacks to be provided.
12. If required, specify any additional A/V equipment needed.
13. Provide any special requests or notes for the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Date**: Enter the date for which the meeting or event is scheduled.
* **Requester Name**: Enter your name as the person placing the order.
* **Requester Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding the order.
* **Requester Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional, but recommended for easier communication).
* **Club Name**: Enter the name of the club or organization hosting the meeting.
* **Meeting or Event Date**: Enter the date of the meeting or event.
* **Meeting or Event Time**: Enter the time of the meeting or event.
* **Meeting Room Name or Location**: Enter the name of the room or location of the meeting.
* **Expected Number of Attendees**: Estimate the total number of people attending the meeting or event.
* **Supplies Needed**: Select all the necessary supplies and equipment for the meeting, such as:
	+ Coffee and Tea
	+ Water and Beverages
	+ Snacks
	+ Paper Products
	+ Writing Supplies
	+ A/V Equipment
	+ Seating
	+ Table Setup
	+ Other
* **Coffee Service Quantity**: If you require coffee, specify the number of cups or pots needed.
* **Bottled Water Quantity**: If you require bottled water, specify the number of bottles needed.
* **Snack Preference**: Select the type of snacks you would like to have for the meeting or event.
* **A/V Equipment Needed**: If you need any additional A/V equipment, enter the specifics here.
* **Special Requests or Notes**: Provide any additional information or requests for the meeting or event.

This guide will help you complete the form accurately and effectively, ensuring that your meeting or event is well-planned and well-equipped.
