# Franchise Brand Onboarding Kit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track the onboarding process of new franchises, including the status of their onboarding kits and essential contact information for both the store and franchise manager.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
2. Enter your store name in the **Store Name** field.
3. Enter your phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
4. Enter your date of birth in the **Date of Birth** field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Enter your store address in the **Store Address 1**, **Store Address 2**, and **Store Address 3** fields.
6. Select your city from the list in the **City** field.
7. Select your state from the list in the **State** field.
8. Enter your zip code in the **Zip Code** field.
9. Enter the name of your franchise location in the **Franchise Location** field.
10. Select the type of onboarding kit you require from the list in the **Onboarding Kit Type** field.
11. Enter any additional notes for the onboarding kit in the **Notes** field.
12. Enter the name of the person submitting the form in the **Submitted By** field.
13. Enter the date and time you submitted the form in the **Date Submitted** and **Time Submitted** fields, respectively.
14. Enter your store manager's name in the **Store Manager** field.
15. Enter your store manager's phone number in the **Store Manager Phone** field.
16. Enter your store manager's email address in the **Store Manager Email** field.
17. Enter the name of your franchise manager in the **Franchise Manager** field.
18. Enter your franchise manager's phone number in the **Franchise Manager Phone** field.
19. Enter your franchise manager's email address in the **Franchise Manager Email** field.
20. Enter your franchise manager's title in the **Franchise Manager Title** field.
21. Enter your signature in the **Signature** field.
22. Enter any notes from your franchise manager in the **Franchise Manager Notes** field.
23. Enter any notes from your store manager in the **Store Manager Notes** field.
24. Select the onboarding status from the list in the **Onboarding Status** field.
25. Select the onboarding kit status from the list in the **Onboarding Kit Status** field.
26. Select the franchise status from the list in the **Franchise Status** field.
27. Enter any notes for the onboarding kit in the **Notes for Onboarding Kit** field.
28. Enter any notes for the store in the **Notes for Store** field.
