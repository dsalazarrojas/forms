# Window Tint Installation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Window Tint Installation Request Form is a tool used to collect information from customers about their window tinting needs. By completing this form, you will be able to specify your preferences for your vehicle's window tinting, including the type of tint you want, the services you require, and your preferred schedule for installation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information and vehicle details on the "Customer Details" page.
2. Specify your vehicle's details on the "Vehicle Details" page.
3. Choose your tint preferences on the "Tint Preferences" page.
4. Enter your appointment details on the "Appointment Details" page.
5. Select your preferred option for scheduling the installation on the "How would you like to proceed with your tint installation?" page.
6. Choose any additional services you require on the "What services do you require?" page.
7. Provide any additional comments or notes on the "Additional Notes" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, **required: false**): Please provide your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address. This will help us contact you to confirm your appointment and answer any questions you may have.

- **Vehicle Details** (`vehicle_details`, text, **required: false**): Please specify your vehicle's make, model, and year. This will help us determine the correct tint film and installation process for your vehicle.

- **Tint Preferences** (`tint_preferences`, text, **required: false**): Please choose the type of tint film you prefer (e.g. light, dark, or mirror finish).

- **Appointment Details** (`appointment_details`, text, **required: false**): Please provide the date and time you would like to schedule your appointment.

- **How would you like to proceed with your tint installation?** (`select_one`, select_one, **required: false**): Please select whether you would like to schedule a callback, install immediately, or not schedule an appointment at this time.

- **What services do you require?** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, **required: false**): Please choose any additional services you require, such as window film or glass film installation.

- **Additional Notes** (`note`, note, **required: false**): Please provide any additional comments or notes about your appointment or requirements.
