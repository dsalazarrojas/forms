# Manis By Dani On Hand Stock Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manis By Dani On Hand Stock Order Form is a simple form designed for users to submit orders for stock from Manis By Dani On Hand. The form collects critical information about the order, including color, size, date, time, phone number, and email.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your desired color from the available options (Gold, Rose, Red, Blue, Black).
2. Choose the size(s) that you would like to order.
3. Enter the desired date for pickup in the Date field.
4. Select the time you would like to pick up your order in the Time field.
5. Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further details.
6. Enter your email address so we can keep you informed about your order.
7. If you have any additional information or context about your order, you can include it in the Note field.
8. Click the Main button to submit your order.
