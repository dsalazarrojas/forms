# Pre Order Learning Game Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for users to pre-order a learning game. It collects basic information from users to process their pre-orders efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the field marked "First Name".
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the field marked "Last Name".
3. Enter your valid **Email** address in the field marked "Email".
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the field marked "Phone".
5. Select your desired **Order Date** for the learning game in the field marked "Order Date".
6. Select the desired **Order Time** for the learning game in the field marked "Order Time".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (First Name, text, required: false)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name (Last Name, text, required: false)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email (Email, email, required: false)**: Enter a valid email address to receive updates and notifications about your order.
* **Phone (Phone, text, required: false)**: Enter your phone number to contact you for any further details about your order.
* **Order Date (Order Date, date, required: false)**: Select the date you would like to order the learning game.
* **Order Time (Order Time, time, required: false)**: Select the time you would like to order the learning game.
