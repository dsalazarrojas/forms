# custom_patches_order_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect customer information and order details for custom patches.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.
2. Ensure that the email address is formatted correctly.
3. Double-check that the phone number is correct.
4. Enter the order total and customer details accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's name as it appears on their order.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the customer's email address in the correct format (e.g., `john.doe@example.com`).
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's phone number in the international format (e.g., `123-456-7890`).
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's address for delivery purposes.
* **Customer City** (`customer_city`, text, required/optional): Enter the city where the customer is located.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, required/optional): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Logo Image** (`logo_image`, text, required/optional): Enter the image of the logo that needs to be customised.
* **Design Details** (`design_details`, text, required/optional): Describe the design requirements for the custom patch.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information about the order.
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, required/optional): Enter any other details about the customer.
