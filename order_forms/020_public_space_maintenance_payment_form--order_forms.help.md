# public_space_maintenance_payment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and manage maintenance requests and payments for public spaces.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. Select the desired maintenance service type(s) from the options provided.
3. Enter the details of the maintenance request.
4. Choose the payment method and amount.
5. Set the payment date and time.
6. Provide any additional comments or notes about the payment.
7. Click submit to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Maintenance Request** (<span style="font-weight:normal;">maintenance_request, text, required</span>): Enter a brief description of the maintenance request.
* **Maintenance Details** (<span style="font-weight:normal;">maintenance_details, text, required</span>): Provide any additional details about the maintenance request.
* **Payment Method** (<span style="font-weight:normal;">payment_method, select_one, required</span>): Select the method of payment (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Payment Amount** (<span style="font-weight:normal;">payment_amount, number, required</span>): Enter the amount of payment.
* **Payment Date** (<span style="font-weight:normal;">payment_date, date, required</span>): Enter the date of the payment.
* **Payment Time** (<span style="font-weight:normal;">payment_time, time, required</span>): Enter the time of the payment.
* **Customer Name** (<span style="font-weight:normal;">customer_name, text, required</span>): Enter the name of the customer.
* **Customer Email** (<span style="font-weight:normal;">customer_email, email, required</span>): Enter the email address of the customer.
* **Customer Phone** (<span style="font-weight:normal;">customer_phone, text, optional</span>): Enter the phone number of the customer (optional).
* **Payment Status** (<span style="font-weight:normal;">payment_status, select_one, required</span>): Select the payment status (e.g., paid, unpaid, pending).
* **Payment Note** (<span style="font-weight:normal;">payment_note, note, optional</span>): Enter any additional comments or notes about the payment.
* **Order Form** (<span style="font-weight:normal;">order_form, select_multiple, optional</span>): Select any additional order forms (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
* **Assigned Tool** (<span style="font-weight:normal;">assigned_tool, text, optional</span>): Enter the name of the tool assigned to the customer (optional).
* **Assigned User** (<span style="font-weight:normal;">assigned_user, text, optional</span>): Enter the name of the user assigned to the customer (optional).
* **Payment Reference** (<span style="font-weight:normal;">payment_reference, text, optional</span>): Enter any payment reference information (optional).
* **Payment Total** (<span style="font-weight:normal;">payment_total, number, optional</span>): Enter the total amount of payment (optional).
* **Payment Currency** (<span style="font-weight:normal;">payment_currency, text, optional</span>): Enter the currency of the payment (optional).
* **Order ID** (<span style="font-weight:normal;">order_id, text, optional</span>): Enter the ID of the order (optional).
* **Customer Address** (<span style="font-weight:normal;">customer_address, text, optional</span>): Enter the address of the customer (optional).
* **Maintenance Service** (<span style="font-weight:normal;">maintenance_service, select_multiple, required</span>): Select the type of maintenance service (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
* **Maintenance Address** (<span style="font-weight:normal;">maintenance_address, text, required</span>): Enter the address of the maintenance request.
* **Maintenance Service Date** (<span style="font-weight:normal;">maintenance_service_date, date, required</span>): Enter the date of the maintenance service.
* **Maintenance Service Time** (<span style="font-weight:normal;">maintenance_service_time, time, required</span>): Enter the time of the maintenance service.
* **Maintenance Status** (<span style="font-weight:normal;">maintenance_status, select_one, required</span>): Select the status of the maintenance service (e.g., completed, pending, cancelled).
* **Customer Message** (<span style="font-weight:normal;">customer_message, text, optional</span>): Enter any message from the customer.
