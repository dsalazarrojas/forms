# Handmade Craft Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from customers for handmade craft delivery. It includes fields for customer information, order details, payment information, and a signature confirmation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer information field with your name, email, and contact number.
2. Provide details about your order, including the craft type, quantity, and any special requests.
3. Enter your payment information, including the payment method and amount.
4. Sign the digital signature field to confirm your order.
5. Read and agree to the terms and conditions.
6. Click the submit button to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information (Customer Information)**: Enter your name, email, and contact number to allow us to contact you about your order.
* **Order Details (Order Details)**: Describe the craft you wish to order, including the type, quantity, and any special requests.
* **Delivery (Order Details)**: This field is intended to collect delivery information, but it seems to be a duplicate of the "Order Details" field. Please provide details about delivery in the "Order Details" field instead.
* **Payment Info (Payment Info)**: Enter your payment method and amount for the order.
* **Signature (Signature)**: Sign this digital signature to confirm your order. Please use your name as your signature.
* **Terms & Conditions (Terms & Conditions)**: Read and agree to our terms and conditions to complete your order.
* **Submit (Submit)**: Click the submit button to complete and submit your order.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all fields correctly and accurately to avoid any issues with your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
