# Pumpkin Cheesecake Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pumpkin Cheesecake Order Form is designed to collect information from customers who wish to place an order for a pumpkin cheesecake. This form is intended to be a self-service tool for customers to provide their personal information, order details, and payment methods in a single form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your personal information in the "Customer Information" section.
2. Provide the order details, including the type of cheesecake you'd like to order and any special requests, in the "Order Information" section.
3. Enter your shipping information, including your address and any special delivery instructions, in the "Shipping Information" section.
4. Select your payment method in the "Payment Information" section.
5. If you have any additional comments or instructions for the baker, please include them in the "Customer Notes" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Customer Information (customer_info)** (`text`, **required**): Please enter your name and contact information to help us process your order.
- **Order Information (order_info)** (`text`, **optional**): Describe the type of pumpkin cheesecake you would like to order, including any special requests or instructions.
- **Shipping Information (shipping_info)** (`text`, **optional**): Enter your shipping address and any special delivery instructions.
- **Payment Information (payment_info)** (`text`, **optional**): Please select your preferred payment method, such as credit card or other options.
- **Order Details (order_details)** (`text`, **optional**): Add any additional information about your order, such as any specific ingredients or allergies.
- **Customer Notes (customer_notes)** (`text`, **optional**): If you have any special requests or instructions, please include them in this section.
