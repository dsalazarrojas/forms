## Form Purpose
This form is for designing logos for clients. It requires information about the client's contact details and the logo's design preferences. This form does not have any specific business logic or internal requirements that need to be checked for, so we will focus on explaining each field to the end user.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the requested information. The form is divided into two main sections: "Client Information" and "Logo Design Preferences". Fill out the "Client Information" section first, which asks for the client's contact details such as email, phone number, name, and address. Then move on to the "Logo Design Preferences" section and select the logo type, size, and color preferences for the client's logo.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Name
* **Business Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the client's business name.

### Client Name
* **Client Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the client's name.

### Contact Email
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the client's email address.

### Contact Phone
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the client's phone number.

### Contact Address
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, `text`, required): Enter the client's address.

### Logo Type
* **Logo Type** (`logo_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of logo the client wants (e.g., "Yes" or "No").

### Logo Size
* **Logo Size** (`logo_size`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the size of the logo the client wants (e.g., "Yes" or "No").

### Logo Color
* **Logo Color** (`logo_color`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the color of the logo the client wants (e.g., "Yes" or "No").

### Logo Description
* **Logo Description** (`logo_description`, `text`, optional): Enter a description of the logo.

### Logo Image
* **Logo Image** (`logo_image`, `text`, optional): Upload a logo image (if applicable).

### Logo URL
* **Logo URL** (`logo_url`, `text`, optional): Enter the URL of the logo (if applicable).

### Message
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional message for the designer.

### Tagline
* **Tagline** (`tagline`, `text`, optional): Enter a short tagline for the logo.

This form is meant to gather information for the client's logo design requirements. It's not necessary to fill out all the fields, but the required fields are marked as such. The form does not have any complex business logic, so simply focus on filling out the fields accurately and consistently.
