# Rent Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information from tenants and landlords for rent payments. It is a user-friendly interface designed to make the rent payment process easier and faster.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required and optional fields as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Information (Tenant Information, note, required: false)**: This page is where the tenant will provide their personal details for the rent payment.
* **Payment Info (Payment Info, text, required: false)**: Enter the payment amount and any other relevant payment information.
* **Payment Date (Payment Date, date, required: false)**: Enter the date of the payment.
* **Payment Method (Payment Method, select_multiple, required: false)**: Choose the method of payment (Card, Bank Transfer, or Check).
* **Payment Amount (Payment Amount, number, required: false)**: Enter the exact amount to be paid.
* **Payment Notes (Payment Notes, text, required: false)**: Add any additional notes about the payment.
* **Tenant Signature (Tenant Signature, select_multiple, required: false)**: Choose the type of signature required (Signature or Other).
* **Landlord Signature (Landlord Signature, select_multiple, required: false)**: Choose the type of signature required (Signature or Other).

## Tips
* Please fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and efficient rent payment processing.
* Ensure you choose the correct payment method and amount to avoid delays or errors.
* Double-check the information you provide before submitting the form.
