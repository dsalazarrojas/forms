# responsive_product_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The responsive product order form is a general-purpose form used to collect customer information for a responsive product order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Order details" field with the necessary details of your order.
2. If required, provide your customer's information in the "customer_info" field.
3. If necessary, fill in the "billing_info" field with your customer's billing information.
4. If necessary, provide your shipping information in the "shipping_info" field.
5. If required or applicable, fill in the "payment" field with the payment details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order details** (`order_details`, text, required): This is the main section of the form where you will enter the details of your order.
* **customer_info** (`customer_info`, text, optional): This field is used to capture your customer's information, such as name and contact details.
* **billing_info** (`billing_info`, text, optional): This field is used to capture your billing information.
* **shipping_info** (`shipping_info`, text, optional): This field is used to capture your shipping information.
* **payment** (`payment`, text, optional): This field is intended to capture payment details, but its usage is unclear without further context.

## Tips
* Please fill in all required fields to ensure accurate submission of your order.
* Use a valid email address and contact number for the customer_info field.
* Double-check your billing and shipping information to ensure accuracy.
* If the "payment" field is required or applicable, use the correct payment method to complete the transaction.
* If you need assistance or have questions, contact the form owner at form@gic.mx.
