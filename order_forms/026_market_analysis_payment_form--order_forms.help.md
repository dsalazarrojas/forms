# Market Analysis Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for collecting payment information and market analysis data. It helps in tracking sales, payments, and other financial transactions to understand market trends.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **first name**, **last name**, and **phone number**.
2. Enter your **email** (if applicable) and **company** (if applicable).
3. Select your preferred **payment gateway** from the options provided (Stripe, PayPal, or Authorize.net).
4. Fill in the **amount** of the transaction.
5. Select all applicable **prophets** (John, Mary, or Jane).
6. Select your preferred **category** and **sub category** (if applicable).
7. Enter the **quantity** of items purchased (if applicable).
8. Choose the **currency** of the transaction (USD, EUR, or JPY).
9. Enter the **price** of the transaction.
10. Choose the **payment date** of the transaction.
11. Select your preferred **payment method** (Cash, Credit Card, or Check).
12. If you selected Credit Card, fill in your **credit card number** and **credit card type** (Visa, Mastercard, or Amex).
13. If you selected Credit Card, enter the **credit card expiration** date.
14. If you selected Credit Card, choose whether your account has a **credit card code**.
15. Finally, select the **payment status** (Pending, Paid, or Reversed).
