# promotional giveaway design order form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to order a promotional giveaway design for a specific promotional giveaway. It is intended to collect information about the design requirements and details of the giveaway.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the promotional giveaway designer field with the name or ID of your preferred designer.
2. Select the promotional giveaway preference from the available options.
3. Provide a description of the promotional giveaway in the promotional giveaway description field.
4. Upload an image for the promotional giveaway in the promotional giveaway image upload field (if required).
5. Enter your promotional giveaway email address in the promotional giveaway email field.
6. Enter your promotional giveaway phone number in the promotional giveaway phone field (if required).
7. Choose the promotional giveaway date and time in the promotional giveaway date and time fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **promotional giveaway designer**: Enter the name or ID of the designer you prefer.
- **promotional giveaway preference**: Select the preferred type of giveaway from the options provided.
- **promotional giveaway description**: Enter a brief description of the promotional giveaway, including the product or service being promoted.
- **promotional giveaway image upload**: If required, upload an image for the promotional giveaway. This field is not required, so you can skip it if it's not necessary.
- **promotional giveaway email**: Enter your email address for the promotional giveaway.
- **promotional giveaway phone**: Enter your phone number for the promotional giveaway (if required).
- **promotional giveaway date**: Choose the date for the promotional giveaway.
- **promotional giveaway time**: Choose the time for the promotional giveaway.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to avoid any delays or issues with your order.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact the design team for assistance.
