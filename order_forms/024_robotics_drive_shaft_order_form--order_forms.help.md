# robotics_drive_shaft_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect order and sales information for robotics drive shafts. It captures customer details, order specifications, payment information, and other relevant details related to the drive shaft.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Order Details** section with the relevant information about the order.
2. Provide customer details in the **Customer Information** section, including their contact email, phone number, and address.
3. In the **Payment Info** section, enter any payment information related to the order.
4. Describe the drive shaft specifications in the **Drive Shaft Specs** section.
5. Add any comments or notes in the **Order Comments** section.
6. Select the condition of the drive shaft in the **Drive Shaft Condition** section (Good, Fair, or Poor).
7. Add any additional notes in the **Drive Shaft Notes** section.
8. Enter the drive shaft price in the **Drive Shaft Price** section.
9. Enter any customer notes in the **Customer Notes** section.
10. Add a customer signature in the **Customer Signature** section.
11. Upload any images, videos, audio, or documents related to the drive shaft in the **Drive Shaft Image**, **Drive Shaft Video**, **Drive Shaft Audio**, and **Drive Shaft Document** sections.
12. Upload any additional documents related to the drive shaft in the **Drive Shaft Additional Document**, **Drive Shaft Additional Document 2**, **Drive Shaft Additional Document 3**, **Drive Shaft Additional Document 4**, and **Drive Shaft Additional Document 5** sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details**: Enter a brief description of the order.
* **Customer Information**: Enter the customer's contact email, phone number, and address.
* **Payment Info**: Enter any payment information related to the order.
* **Drive Shaft Specs**: Describe the drive shaft specifications, including model, year, and mileage.
* **Order Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes related to the order.
* **Drive Shaft Condition**: Select the condition of the drive shaft (Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Drive Shaft Notes**: Add any additional notes related to the drive shaft.
* **Drive Shaft Price**: Enter the price of the drive shaft.
* **Customer Notes**: Add any additional notes from the customer.
* **Customer Signature**: Enter the customer's signature.
* **Drive Shaft Image**: Upload an image of the drive shaft.
* **Drive Shaft Video**: Upload a video related to the drive shaft.
* **Drive Shaft Audio**: Upload an audio file related to the drive shaft.
* **Drive Shaft Document**: Upload a document related to the drive shaft.
* **Drive Shaft Additional Document**: Upload an additional document related to the drive shaft.
