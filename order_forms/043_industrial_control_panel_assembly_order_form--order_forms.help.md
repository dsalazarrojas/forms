# Industrial Control Panel Assembly Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for assembling an industrial control panel. It helps businesses that manufacture or sell industrial control panels provide a clear understanding of the customer's needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2.  Provide all the necessary information for a smooth order process.
3.  Review the form carefully to avoid errors or missing information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is meant to gather basic contact and company information about the customer. Please fill this field with your company name, contact person's name, and any other relevant details.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required: false):
	+ In this field, provide a description of the order, including the specific control panel required, any modifications, or additional components needed.
* **Delivery Info** (`delivery_info`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe how you'd like the order delivered, including the delivery method and any special requirements.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the delivery address where the control panel will be shipped to. This includes the physical address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Customer Contact** (`customer_contact`, text, required: false):
	+ Provide the primary contact's name, phone number, and email to ensure smooth communication.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the name of the contact person or company representative.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date you'd like to receive the delivered panel.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required: false):
	+ Choose a suitable time for delivery, if applicable.
* **Delivery Note** (`delivery_note`, note, required: false):
	+ Add any special notes or comments for the delivery personnel.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false):
	+ Enter a valid email address for the customer or representative to facilitate communication.

## Tips
- Double-check the form for any missing information before submitting.
- Ensure that all fields are accurate to avoid delays or errors.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to the form instructions or contact the order fulfillment team.
