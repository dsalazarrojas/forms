# Subscription Management Permission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Subscription Management Permission Form is used to manage subscription services and related preferences for subscription plans. It is designed to ensure that users have the necessary permissions to manage their subscriptions effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the options that best apply to your subscription management needs.
2. Review your selections carefully to ensure they accurately reflect your preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **manage_subscription_services** (Select Multiple): 
  This field allows you to select which subscription services you need to manage. Choose from the available options, such as Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
  - **Option 1**: Select this option if you need to manage subscription services 1.
  - **Option 2**: Select this option if you need to manage subscription services 2.
  - **Option 3**: Select this option if you need to manage subscription services 3.
  
- **confirm_subscription_services** (Select One, Date, or Text): 
  This field is used to confirm your subscription services selection. You can enter a date if you need to confirm a specific date or select one of the options, such as Option 1 or Option 2, to confirm your selection.
  - **Option 1**: Select this option if you need to confirm subscription services 1.
  - **Option 2**: Select this option if you need to confirm subscription services 2.
  - **Date**: Enter a date to confirm a specific date-related subscription service.
  
- **manage_billing_processes** (Time): 
  This field is used to select the billing processes you need to manage. You can select from the available options or enter a specific time to confirm your selection.
  - **Option 1**: Select this option if you need to manage billing process 1.
  - **Option 2**: Select this option if you need to manage billing process 2.
  - **Time**: Enter a time to confirm a specific time-related billing process.
  
- **confirm_billing_processes** (Select Multiple): 
  This field is used to confirm your billing processes selection. Choose from the available options, such as Option 1 or Option 2.
  - **Option 1**: Select this option if you need to confirm billing process 1.
  - **Option 2**: Select this option if you need to confirm billing process 2.
  
- **subscription_preferences** (Note): 
  This field is used to enter your subscription preferences. Enter any additional information or comments related to your subscription preferences.
  
- **Confirm Subscription Preferences** (Text): 
  This field is used to confirm your subscription preferences. Enter any additional information or comments related to your subscription preferences.
