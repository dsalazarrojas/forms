# inventory_rescan_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a rescan of the inventory, which is used to update the accuracy and completeness of the data stored in the inventory management system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Product Code**: Enter the product's unique code.
	* **Reason For Rescan**: Explain why you need to rescan the inventory for this product.
2. Fill in the optional fields:
	* **Request Details**: Provide additional information about the rescan request.
	* **Inventory Level**: Enter the current inventory level of the product.
	* **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the product that needs to be rescanned.
	* **Location**: Enter the location of the product in the warehouse.
3. Click "Submit" to send the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Code**: <code> Enter the product's unique code to help identify the product being rescanned.
* **Request Details**: <text> Provide a brief description of the rescan request.
* **Reason For Rescan**: <text> Explain why the inventory needs to be rescanned for this product.
* **Inventory Level**: <number> Enter the current stock level of the product.
* **Quantity**: <number> Enter the quantity of the product that needs to be rescanned.
* **Location**: <text> Enter the location in the warehouse where the product is stored.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that your request is processed correctly.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, contact your supervisor or inventory team for assistance.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
