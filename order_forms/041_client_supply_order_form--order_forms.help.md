# Client Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Client Supply Order Form is a tool used to collect client information and order details for the delivery of supplies. This form ensures accurate and timely delivery of supplies by gathering essential information from clients.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Client Information**
	* Enter your name as it should appear on the order.
	* Enter your company name (if applicable).
	* Enter your email address for contact purposes.
	* Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
	* Enter your billing address for payment and delivery purposes.
2. **Order Details**
	* Enter the date you would like to receive the order.
	* Enter the required delivery date (when you need the supplies).
	* Enter the product name for the first item you would like to order.
	* Enter the quantity of the product.
	* Enter the unit price for the product.
3. **Product Add-ons**
	* If you would like to order additional products, enter their names, quantities, and unit prices.
4. **Payment and Order Information**
	* Select the payment method you would like to use (credit card, check, bank transfer, or purchase order).
	* If you have a purchase order number, enter it here.
5. **Special Instructions or Notes**
	* Enter any special requests or notes about the order.
6. **Submit**
	* Review your order details carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter your name as it should appear on the order.
* **Company Name**: Enter your company name if applicable.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Billing Address**: Enter your billing address for payment and delivery purposes.
* **Shipping Address**: Leave blank if the same as billing address.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date you would like to receive the order.
* **Required Delivery Date**: Enter the date you need the supplies by.
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the first product you would like to order.
* **Product Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the first product.
* **Unit Price**: Enter the unit price of the first product.
* **Product Name 2**/3/4: Enter the name of the additional products you would like to order (if applicable).
* **Product Quantity 2**/3/4: Enter the quantity of the additional products (if applicable).
* **Product Unit Price 2**/3/4: Enter the unit price of the additional products (if applicable).
* **Special Instructions or Notes**: Enter any special requests or notes about the order.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you would like to use.
* **Purchase Order Number**: Enter the purchase order number if applicable.

By following this guide, you should be able to accurately and efficiently complete the Client Supply Order Form. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant support team for assistance.
