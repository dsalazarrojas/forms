# Race Day Medal Printing Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for ordering medals to be printed for a race day event. Please fill out this form accurately to ensure your order is fulfilled correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the text to be printed on the medals on the "Race Day Medal Text" field.
2. Select the desired medal designs from the "Medal Designs" field. 
3. Enter the quantity of each medal design on the "Quantity per Medal" field.
4. Enter the total number of medals to be ordered on the "Total Medals Ordered" field.
5. Provide your shipping details in the "Shipping Details" field, including any specific instructions or requests.
6. Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field.
7. If necessary, enter any order details in the "Order Details" field to help us process your order.
8. Enter your payment details in the "Payment Details" field.
9. Select the payment method to be used for this order in the "Payment Method" field.
10. Finally, please enter the name of the person submitting this order in the "Order Submitted By" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Race Day Medal Text** (`race_day_medal_text`, text, required/optional): Enter the text to be printed on the medals.
*   **Medal Designs** (`medal_designs`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the desired medal designs for your order.
*   **Quantity per Medal** (`quantity_per_medal`, number, required/optional): Enter the quantity of each medal design ordered.
*   **Total Medals Ordered** (`total_medals_ordered`, number, required/optional): Enter the total number of medals to be ordered.
*   **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, required/optional): Provide your shipping details, including any specific instructions or requests.
*   **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the shipping address where the medals will be sent.
*   **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional details about your order to help us process it correctly.
*   **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your payment details, including any relevant information about your payment method.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required/optional): Select the payment method to be used for this order (Cash, Check, or Credit Card).
*   **Order Submitted By** (`order_submitted_by`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person submitting this order.
