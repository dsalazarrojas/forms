# Recycled Display Stand Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recycled Display Stand Order Form is designed to help customers place orders for recycled display stands. To use this form, please follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Form Title` field with your desired title for the order.
2. Enter the name of the business that will be receiving the recycled display stand in the `Business Name` field.
3. Specify the name of the product being ordered in the `Product Name` field.
4. Enter the quantity of the order in the `Order Quantity` field.
5. Select the type of order from the options provided in the `Order Type` field.
6. Choose the date for the order in the `Order Date` field.
7. Add any additional notes or comments regarding the order in the `Order Note` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for the order.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the business that will be receiving the recycled display stand.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the product being ordered.
* **Order Quantity** (`order_quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of the product being ordered.
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of order from the provided options.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Choose the date for the order.
* **Order Note** (`order_note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes regarding the order.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and correctly.
* Double-check the order details before submitting the form to avoid any potential issues.
