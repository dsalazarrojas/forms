**POD Submission Form - Help Guide**

**Purpose**
The POD (Proof of Delivery) Submission Form is used by our customers to submit information about their order. This form helps us to track and process their order more efficiently.

**How To Complete This Form**

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information:
	* **Submitter Name**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
	* **Business Name**: Enter the name of the business associated with the order.
	* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product being ordered.
2. Select the relevant information for your order:
	* **Product Type**: Choose the type of product being ordered (e.g., T-shirt, Mug).
	* **Quantity**: Enter the number of products being ordered.
	* **Product Size**: Select the size of the product (e.g., S, M, L, XL).
	* **Usage**: Enter the date of intended usage for the product.
	* **Usage Time**: Enter the time of day when you plan to use the product.
	* **Confirm Usage**: Confirm whether the product will be used.
	* **Quantity Printed**: Enter the number of times the product will be printed.
	* **Design File**: Enter a file containing the design for the product.
	* **Product Image**: Enter an image of the product.
