# Ticket Printing Quantity Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ticket Printing Quantity Request Form is used to gather information for ticket printing requests, ensuring efficient processing and reducing errors and duplication of work.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, select the type of ticket you need to print from the "Ticket Type" dropdown menu. If it's not a ticket, choose "Other" and include a description in the comments field.
2.  Enter the required information on page 1, including the quantity of tickets you need to print and your contact details (customer name, email, and phone number).
3.  You can add any additional comments about the request in the comments field, if needed.
4.  Review your information to ensure it is accurate before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Ticket Type**: This field allows you to select the type of ticket you need to print. Choose between "Ticket", "Non-Ticket", or "Other" if it doesn't fit into either category.
*   **Quantity**: Enter the number of tickets you need to print. Make sure to enter the correct quantity to avoid errors.
*   **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer for whom the tickets are being printed.
*   **Email**: Enter the customer's email address for communication and verification purposes.
*   **Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number for communication and verification purposes.
*   **Address**: This field is not required, but you can include the customer's address if it's necessary for the printing process.
*   **Comments**: Add any additional comments about the printing request, if needed.

## Tips
*   Be sure to review your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
*   Use the comments field to provide any additional context or instructions for the printing process.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to your supervisor for guidance.
