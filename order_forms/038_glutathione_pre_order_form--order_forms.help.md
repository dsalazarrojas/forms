# Glutathione Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers who are interested in purchasing glutathione supplements. It is intended for customers who have not yet confirmed their order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the First Name and Last Name fields, respectively.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
3. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
4. Select the product you are interested in from the Product dropdown menu. 
5. Enter the quantity of the product you want to order.
6. If you want to specify a delivery date, enter it in the Delivery Date field.
7. If you want to specify a delivery time, enter it in the Delivery Time field.
8. Enter your delivery address in the Delivery Address field.
9. If you want to provide any delivery instructions, enter them in the Delivery Instructions field.
10. Select your preferred Payment Method from the dropdown menu.
11. Enter the amount of the product you want to order.
12. Select the delivery status of your order from the dropdown menu.
13. If you want to add any notes, enter them in the Notes field.
14. If you want to specify a clinic, enter it in the Clinic field.
15. If you want to specify the clinic staff, enter it in the Clinic Staff field.
16. If you want to specify the brand, enter it in the Brand field.
17. If you want to add any additional notes, enter them in the Notes 1, Notes 2, Notes 3, or Notes 4 fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address here.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number here.
* **Product** (`Product`, select_one, required): Select the product you are interested in from the dropdown menu.
* **Quantity** (`Quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of the product you want to order.
* **Delivery Date** (`Delivery Date`, date, optional): Enter the date you want to receive your delivery.
* **Delivery Time** (`Delivery Time`, time, optional): Enter the time you want to receive your delivery.
* **Delivery Address** (`Delivery Address`, text, optional): Enter your delivery address here.
* **Delivery Instructions** (`Delivery Instructions`, text, optional): Enter any delivery instructions here.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method from the dropdown menu.
* **Amount** (`Amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the product you want to order.
* **Delivery Status** (`Delivery Status`, select_one, required): Select the delivery status of your order from the dropdown menu.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes here.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, text, optional): Enter any assigned tool here.
* **Clinic** (`Clinic`, text, optional): Enter the clinic here.
* **Clinic Staff** (`Clinic Staff`, text, optional): Enter the clinic staff here.
* **Brand** (`Brand`, text, optional): Enter the brand here.
* **Notes 1** (`Notes 1`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes here.
* **Notes 2** (`Notes 2`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes here.
* **Notes 3** (`Notes 3`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes here.
* **Notes 4** (`Notes 4`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes here.
* **Delivery Date 1** (`Delivery Date 1`, date, optional): Enter the delivery date here.
* **Delivery Time 1** (`Delivery Time 1`, time, optional): Enter the delivery time here.

Note: This form is designed to be filled out by customers who have not yet confirmed their order. If you have already placed an order, please contact our customer service team to update your order information.
