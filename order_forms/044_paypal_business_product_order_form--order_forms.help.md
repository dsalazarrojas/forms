# paypal_business_product_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for businesses to place orders with PayPal, capturing essential information for B2B transactions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Shipping Address** field with the delivery address for the order.
2. Provide a brief description of the **Product Details** in the corresponding field.
3. Enter **Customer Info**, including name and contact details.
4. Enter **Payment Info** for the order.
5. Add any additional **Order Notes** if necessary.
6. Select the **Shipping Method** (e.g. "Yes" or "No").
7. Enter the **Shipping Cost** for the order.
8. Enter the **Tax Amount** for the order.
9. Enter the **Total Order Value**.
10. Enter the date and time the **Date Ordered** and **Date Shipped**.
11. Enter the **Time Shipped**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shipping Address**: <small>This field is used to enter the shipping address for the order.</small>
* **Product Details**: <small>Enter a brief description of the product being ordered.</small>
* **Customer Info**: <small>Enter information about the customer, such as their name and contact details.</small>
* **Payment Info**: <small>Enter payment information for the order.</small>
* **Order Notes**: <small>Add any additional notes about the order.</small>
* **Shipping Method**: <small>Select the shipping method for the order (e.g. "Yes" or "No").</small>
* **Shipping Cost**: <small>Enter the cost of shipping for the order.</small>
* **Tax Amount**: <small>Enter the tax amount for the order.</small>
* **Total Order Value**: <small>Enter the total value of the order.</small>
* **Date Ordered**: <small>Enter the date the order was placed.</small>
* **Date Shipped**: <small>Enter the date the order was shipped.</small>
* **Time Shipped**: <small>Enter the time the order was shipped.</small>

## Tips

* Please fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete order processing.
* This form is intended for B2B transactions, so please only use it for business-to-business orders.
* Ensure that you enter accurate and complete information for each field.
