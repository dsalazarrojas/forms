# paypal_business_product_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for businesses to place orders with PayPal, capturing essential information for B2B transactions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Shipping Address** field with the delivery address for the order.
2. Provide a brief description of the **Product Details** in the corresponding field.
3. Enter **Customer Info**, including name and contact details.
4. Enter **Payment Info** for the order.
5. Add any additional **Order Notes** if necessary.
6. Select the **Shipping Method** (e.g. "Yes" or "No").
7. Enter the **Shipping Cost** for the order.
8. Enter the **Tax Amount** for the order.
9. Enter the **Total Order Value**.
10. Enter the date and time the **Date Ordered** and **Date Shipped**.
11. Enter the **Time Shipped**.
