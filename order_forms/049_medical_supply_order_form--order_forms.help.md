# Medical Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Medical Supply Order Form is a form used for ordering medical supplies. Its purpose is to gather accurate and up-to-date information about medical supplies, ensuring timely and efficient ordering.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's details, including any relevant medical history, allergies, or special instructions.
2. **Order Date**: Enter the date when the medical supply is required or ordered.
3. **Medical Supply Id**: Enter the unique identifier for the medical supply.
4. **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the medical supply ordered.
5. **Order Status**: Select all applicable statuses for the order, such as New, In Progress, or Completed.
6. **Supplier Information**: Enter any relevant information about the supplier, such as contact details or special instructions.
7. **Order Total**: Enter the total cost of the ordered medical supplies.
8. **Payment Method**: Select the method of payment for the order, such as Cash, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer.
9. **Payment Status**: Select the status of payment for the order, such as Paid or Unpaid.

## Tips

* Please double-check all fields before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your supervisor or the inventory team.
* This form is designed to prevent duplication of orders, so please ensure that you're not creating multiple orders for the same items.
