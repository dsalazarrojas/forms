# Business Card Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for placing orders for business cards. Please fill it out accurately to ensure timely and correct production of your cards.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before filling it out.
2. Enter your name and email address to receive order confirmations.
3. Select the quantity of cards you need.
4. Choose your preferred card size, stock type, finish style, and color printing options.
5. Decide on the design file status (provided, needed, or use a template).
6. Enter your company name, job title, and website (if applicable).
7. Select your shipping address details.
8. Choose your shipping method (speed) and payment method (type).
9. Review your order details for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Required): Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on the business card.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter a valid email address for order confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your contact phone number for order inquiries.
* **Company Name** (Required): Enter your company or business name as you would like it to appear on the card.
* **Job Title** (Required): Enter your position or job title as you would like it to appear on the card.
* **Website** (Optional): Enter your company website (if applicable).
* **Quantity** (Required): Select the quantity of business cards you need.
* **Custom Quantity** (Optional): Enter a custom quantity if you selected "Custom quantity" above.
* **Card Size** (Required): Choose your preferred card size (standard or custom).
* **Stock Type** (Required): Select the quality of card stock you prefer.
* **Finish Style** (Required): Choose the card surface finish you prefer.
* **Color Printing** (Required): Select the printing options for your cards (black and white or full color).
* **Design File Provided** (Required): Decide whether you have a design file ready, need design help, or use a template.
* **Design File Format** (Optional): If you have a design file, enter its format.
* **Street Address** (Required): Enter your complete mailing address.
* **Address Line 2** (Optional): Enter any additional address details (apartment, suite, etc.).
* **City** (Required): Enter your city name.
* **State/Province** (Required): Enter your state or province code.
* **Postal Code** (Required): Enter your postal code.
* **Shipping Method** (Required): Choose your preferred shipping speed.
* **Payment Method** (Required): Select how you will pay for your order.

Note: Please ensure you have reviewed and understand all fields before submitting the form.
