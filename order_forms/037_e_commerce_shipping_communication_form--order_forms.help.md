# e_commerce_shipping_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The e_commerce_shipping_communication_form is a flexible form designed to collect and display shipping information for ecommerce orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred shipping method using the "Shipping Preferences" field.
2. Choose your shipping service from the options provided in the "Shipping Service" field.
3. Select the current status of your order from the options in the "Order Status" field.
4. Enter the date of shipment in the "Shipping Date" field.
5. Enter the time of shipment in the "Shipping Time" field, if applicable.
6. Enter the tracking number in the "Tracking Number" field.
7. If necessary, add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
8. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shipping Preferences** (Text, Optional): Select your preferred shipping method.
* **Shipping Service** (Select One, Optional): Choose your shipping service from the options UPS, FedEx, and USPS.
* **Order Status** (Select One, Optional): Select the current status of your order from the options Placed, Picked, Shipped, and Delivered.
* **Shipping Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of shipment.
* **Shipping Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of shipment, if applicable.
* **Tracking Number** (Text, Optional): Enter the tracking number for your shipment.
* **Notes** (Note, Optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your shipment.
* **Contact Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your contact phone number, if necessary.
