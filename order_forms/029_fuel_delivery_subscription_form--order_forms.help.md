# Fuel Delivery Subscription Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fuel Delivery Subscription Form is designed to collect information from customers who are interested in receiving fuel deliveries from a business that offers this service. The form will be used to gather your personal information, delivery preferences, and payment details to ensure smooth and secure fuel deliveries.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your Name and Phone number to help our team get in touch with you.
2.  Enter your Email to ensure we can contact you with any updates or issues related to your subscription.
3.  Provide your Delivery Address where you would like to receive fuel deliveries.
4.  Indicate your Fuel Delivery preferences by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the options.
5.  Choose your preferred Payment Method and frequency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fuel Delivery Subscriptions**: This text field is used to collect information about your fuel delivery subscriptions. Please provide any relevant details about your current or past subscriptions.
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it should be used by our team to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached by our team.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted with updates and issues.
* **Delivery Address**: Enter the complete address where you would like to receive fuel deliveries.
* **Delivery Frequency**: Select "Yes" if you are interested in receiving regular fuel deliveries, "No" otherwise.
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method.
* **Payment Frequency**: Select your preferred payment frequency.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
*   Review the information before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the support team.
