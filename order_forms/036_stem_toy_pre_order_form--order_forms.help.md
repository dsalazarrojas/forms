# STEM Toy Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to place pre-orders for STEM toys.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your customer information in the `Customer Info` field. This field is not required, but it's essential to provide your name and contact details so that our team can reach out to you for any pre-order related questions or updates.
2. Enter your delivery information in the `Delivery Info` field. Again, this field is not required, but it will help us ensure that your order is delivered to the correct location.
3. Fill in the `Order Details` field with the specific STEM toy(s) you'd like to pre-order. Please make sure to provide as much detail as possible, such as the quantity and color of the toy you're interested in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info**: This field is where you can enter your name and contact details. This will help our team get in touch with you for any pre-order related questions or updates.
* **Delivery Info**: This field is where you can enter the address where you'd like your order to be delivered. Please make sure to include your street address, apartment number (if applicable), city, state, and zip code.
* **Order Details**: This field is where you can describe the STEM toy(s) you'd like to pre-order. Please be specific about the type of toy, color, and quantity you're interested in.
* **Tips**: If you have any questions or need help with your pre-order, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx). Our team is here to assist you!
