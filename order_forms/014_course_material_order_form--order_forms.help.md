# Course Material Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers for course material orders. It is designed to be used by customers to place orders for course materials, including courses, resources, and other related materials.

The form is meant to capture the following information:
- Customer details (first name, last name, email, phone number)
- Course details (course ID, quantity, total cost)
- Order details (order date, order time)
- Payment details (payment method, payment status, payment amount)

Please note that some fields are not required, and you may skip them if not necessary.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **first name** in the first name field.
2. Enter your **last name** in the last name field.
3. Enter your **email address** in the email field.
4. Enter your **phone number** in the phone field.
5. Enter the **course ID** of the course you are ordering.
6. Enter the **quantity** of the course materials you need.
7. Enter the **total cost** of the order.
8. Select your **payment gateway** from the options provided.
9. Enter any additional **order notes** you'd like to include with your order.
10. Confirm your order by selecting the **confirm** option.
11. Optionally, select any additional **order notes2** you'd like to include.
12. Enter the **order date** and **order time** of your order.
13. Enter the **course name** of the course you are ordering.
14. Select your **order status** from the options provided.
15. Optionally, select any additional **order notes2** you'd like to include.
16. Enter the **order date2** and **order time2** of your order.
17. Select your **payment method** from the options provided.
18. Select your **payment status** from the options provided.
19. Enter the **payment amount** of the order.
20. Optionally, select any additional **payment method2** you'd like to include.
21. Select your **payment status2** from the options provided.
22. Enter the **payment amount2** of the order.
23. Enter the **order date3** and **order time3** of your order.
