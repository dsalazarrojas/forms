# University Lab Safety Equipment Requisition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The University Lab Safety Equipment Requisition Form is used to request safety equipment for the lab, ensuring that staff members have access to necessary resources for safe and efficient work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the lab name and contact person responsible for the lab.
2. Provide a brief description of the equipment needed.
3. Specify the quantity of equipment required.
4. Select the type of equipment needed.
5. Update the approval status and reason for decline (if applicable).
6. Choose who should be assigned to handle the request.
7. Select the date when the equipment is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lab Name (1)**: Enter the name of the lab where the safety equipment is needed.
* **Contact Person (2)**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the lab who can be contacted for further information.
* **Lab Contact (3)**: Enter a contact number or email for the lab contact.
* **Department (4)**: Enter the department where the lab is located.
* **Equipment Type (5)**: Enter the type of safety equipment needed (e.g., fume hood, PCR machine, etc.).
* **Quantity (6)**: Enter the number of pieces of equipment needed.
* **Description (7)**: Provide a brief description of the purpose of the equipment or how it will be used.
* **Approval Status (8)**: Select the status of the request from the following options:
	+ Awaiting Approval
	+ Approved
	+ Declined
* **Reason Declined (9)**: If the request was declined, enter a brief reason why.
* **Assigned To (10)**: Select who should be assigned to handle the request from:
	+ chatjimmy
	+ admin
	+ other
* **Date Required (11)**: Select the date when the equipment is required.

## Tips
* Review the lab safety protocols and guidelines before submitting the form.
* Double-check the equipment type and quantity to ensure accurate representation of needs.
* Update the form regularly for any changes in status or requests.
