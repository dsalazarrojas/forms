# first_data_online_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The first_data_online_donation_form is used to collect information from online donors, covering donor details, donation specifics, and frequency of donations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Scroll through the form pages and fill out each required and optional field as prompted.
2.  Ensure you fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
3.  Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Donor Information** (`donor_info`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter any relevant information about the donor.
- **Donation Details** (`donation_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the donation here.
- **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, text, optional): If you're the donor or acting on behalf of the donor, please enter their name here.
- **Donor Email** (`donor_email`, email, optional): Enter your or the donor's email address for contact purposes.
- **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the donation here.
- **Donation Date** (`donation_date`, date, optional): Select a date for the donation or leave blank for one-time donations.
- **Frequency** (`frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose whether this is a one-time donation, a recurring donation, or an irregular donation.
- **Recurring Donations** (`recurring_donations`, select_multiple, optional): Mark if you or the donor are willing to make recurring donations.
- **Donor Phone** (`donor_phone`, text, optional): If you or the donor have a phone number, enter it here.
- **Donor Address** (`donor_address`, text, optional): Enter your or the donor's address for additional contact and record-keeping purposes.

## Tips
- Be accurate and thorough when filling out the form.
- Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting.
- For recurring donations, ensure you understand the commitment before agreeing.
- This form is for online donations; if you're in need of assistance, contact our support team.
