# Custom Furniture Order Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Furniture Order Intake Form is designed to collect information from customers who want to place a custom order for furniture.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select your preferences from the "Customer Preferences" page.
2. Enter your delivery details on the "Delivery Details" page.
3. Provide any custom order details on the "Custom Order Details" page.
4. Choose your payment method on the "Payment Method" page.
5. Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number on the "Customer Information" page.
6. Add any additional notes or comments on the "Customer Note" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Preferences** (`Yes/No`, select_multiple): Choose your preferences for the order, such as color, material, and style.
* **Delivery Details** (`text`, ): Enter the delivery details, including the address and any specific instructions.
* **Custom Order Details** (`text`, ): Provide any additional details about your custom order, such as specific requirements or requests.
* **Payment Method** (`Yes/No`, select_one): Choose your preferred payment method, such as credit card or bank transfer.
* **Customer Name** (`text`, ): Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
* **Customer Email** (`email`, ): Enter your valid email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`text`, ): Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Note** (`note`, ): Add any additional comments or notes about your order, such as special instructions or requests.
