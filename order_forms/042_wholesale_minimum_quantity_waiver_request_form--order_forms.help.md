# wholesale_minimum_quantity_waiver_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for customers to request a waiver for the minimum quantity required for a wholesale order. It helps the customer to communicate their reasons for the request, which is reviewed and processed accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the product SKU, quantity, and order date.
2. Provide your customer name, email, and phone number.
3. Select the reason for the waiver.
4. If necessary, provide an additional reason for the waiver.
5. Optionally, provide product description and product price.
6. Review your information and click 'Submit' to submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header**: Enter any additional information or comments about your request.
* **Product SKU**: Enter the SKU of the product you are requesting a waiver for.
* **Quantity**: Enter the quantity you are requesting to be waived.
* **Reason for Waiver**: Select why you are requesting a waiver. This will help the reviewer to understand your needs.
* **Another Reason for Waiver**: This field is optional, but if you have an additional reason for the waiver, you can select it.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on the order.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date of the original order.
* **Submit**: Review and submit your request for review and processing.
* **Product Description**: Optionally, enter a brief description of the product.
* **Product Price**: Optionally, enter the price of the product.
* **Order Number**: Optionally, enter the order number.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
