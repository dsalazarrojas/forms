# e-learning-course-order-form-template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The e-learning-course-order-form-template is a form used to order e-learning courses. It is designed to collect customer information and details about the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name and email address in the "User Information" section.
2. Select the "Course Title" and enter the quantity of the course you wish to order.
3. Choose the "Payment Method" and enter any additional "Notes" or "Comments" if needed.
4. Enter the "Date" and "Time" of the order.
5. Review and confirm the "Order Summary" to ensure accuracy.
6. Sign the "E-Signature" section to confirm your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`id: 11`, `name: user_name`, `type: text`, required: false):
	+ Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`id: 12`, `name: user_email`, `type: email`, required: false):
	+ Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`id: 13`, `name: user_phone`, `type: text`, required: false):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Course Title** (`id: 14`, `name: course_title`, `type: text`, required: false):
	+ Select the title of the course you wish to order.
* **Price** (`id: 15`, `name: course_price`, `type: number`, required: false):
	+ Enter the price you are willing to pay for the course.
* **Quantity** (`id: 16`, `name: course_quantity`, `type: number`, required: false):
	+ Enter the number of courses you wish to order.
* **Payment Method** (`id: 17`, `name: payment_method`, `type: select_one`, required: false):
	+ Select the method of payment you wish to use (e.g., Option 1, Option 2).
* **Date** (`id: 18`, `name: order_date`, `type: date`, required: false):
	+ Enter the date of the order.
* **Time** (`id: 19`, `name: order_time`, `type: time`, required: false):
	+ Enter the time of the order.
* **Notes** (`id: 20`, `name: order_notes`, `type: note`, required: false):
	+ Enter any additional notes or comments about the order.
* **Payment Notes** (`id: 21`, `name: payment_notes`, `type: note`, required: false):
	+ Enter any additional payment-related notes or comments.
* **Comments** (`id: 22`, `name: user_comments`, `type: text`, required: false):
	+ Enter any additional comments about the order.
* **Address** (`id: 23`, `name: user_address`, `type: text`, required: false):
	+ Enter your address (if necessary).
* **Status** (`id: 24`, `name: course_status`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select the status of the course (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **E-Signature** (`id: 25`, `name: e_signature`, `type: text`, required: false):
	+ Sign your order by entering your electronic signature.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth and successful order process.
* Review the "Order Summary" before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the order, contact us for assistance.
