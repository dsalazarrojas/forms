# theme_park_souvenirs_requisition_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to be completed by customers to request souvenirs from our Theme Park. It is used to gather information about their preferences and needs for souvenir items. The form will help us understand their interest and provide them with the best possible souvenir options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you have provided all the necessary information.
2. Fill in the requested information for each field accurately and completely.
3. Select any relevant options from the available dropdowns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### theme_park_souvenirs_requisition_form_page_1
* **theme_park_souvenirs_requisition_form_page_1** (`text`, required: false): This is the start of the form where you will be able to write a brief introduction about your request.

### theme_park_souvenirs_requisition_form_page_2
* **theme_park_souvenirs_requisition_form_page_2** (`text`, required: false): This is the second part of the form where you can provide any additional information about your request.

### theme_park_souvenirs_requisition_form_page_3
* **Option 1** (`select_one`, required: false): Select one of the options provided, if applicable. This field is used to narrow down the options for the souvenir.

### theme_park_souvenirs_requisition_form_page_4
* **theme_park_souvenirs_requisition_form_page_4** (`text`, required: false): This is the third part of the form where you can provide more details about your souvenir preferences.

### theme_park_souvenirs_requisition_form_page_5
* **Option 5 1** (`select_multiple`, required: false), **Option 5 2** (`select_multiple`, required: false), **Option 5 3** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select any of the options that apply to you, if applicable. This field allows you to choose multiple options for your souvenir preferences.

### theme_park_souvenirs_requisition_form_page_6
* **Option 6 1** (`select_one`, required: true), **Option 6 2** (`select_one`, required: false): Select one of the options provided. This field is used to further specify your souvenir preferences.

### theme_park_souvenirs_requisition_form_page_7
* **theme_park_souvenirs_requisition_form_page_7** (`text`, required: false): This is the final part of the form where you can write any additional comments or details about your request.

### theme_park_souvenirs_requisition_form_page_8
* **theme_park_souvenirs_requisition_form_page_8** (`number`, required: false): This is the last field where you can provide any numerical information that may be required for your souvenir request.

This guide has been prepared with the assumption that all required fields have been marked as "required: true", except for **Option 6 2** which has been marked as "required: false".
