# educational_kit_delivery_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide delivery details for educational kits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the details of your educational kit.
2. Enter the address where you would like to receive your kit.
3. Select a delivery date for your kit.
4. Choose a delivery time that suits you.
5. Enter the kit ID number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Educational Kit Delivery Details** (`educational_kit_delivery_details`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the details of your educational kit, including the type and any relevant information.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the address where you would like to receive your educational kit.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional):
	+ Select a date for your kit delivery. Please note that dates should be in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, optional):
	+ Choose a time for your kit delivery. This could be a morning, afternoon, or evening delivery, depending on your preference.
* **Kit ID** (`kit_id`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the kit ID number, which is used to identify your educational kit.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields, if any.
- Double-check your input before submitting the form.
- If you have any issues or concerns, contact our support team.
