# T Shirt Order Form With 2Checkout - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to process orders for T-Shirts with 2Checkout as the payment method. The form captures order details, customer information, and shipping details to facilitate smooth order processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the product options (color) for the T-Shirt you wish to order.
2. Enter the quantity of T-Shirts you want to purchase.
3. Enter the price of the T-Shirt.
4. Provide your customer details (email, phone number, etc.).
5. Select your preferred payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, etc.).
6. Fill in the shipping address and details (state, zip, country, etc.).
7. Choose the shipping method (FedEx, USPS, etc.).
8. Fill in any optional notes for the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details** (`order_details`): This field allows you to provide a short description of the order, but it's not a required field.
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`): Enter your email address and phone number to contact you for any order updates or queries.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select one or multiple payment methods (Credit Card, PayPal) for the order.
* **Product Options** (`product_options`): Choose the color of the T-Shirt you want to order.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`): Enter the number of T-Shirts you want to purchase.
* **Price** (`price`): Enter the price of the T-Shirt.
* **Order Note** (`order_note`): Provide any additional notes or comments about the order.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address to receive order updates or communications from us.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`): This field is not required, as the total will be calculated automatically.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`): The date when the order was placed.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`): The time when the order was placed.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`): Enter your billing address to process the payment.
* **Street Address** (`billing_address_line_1`): Enter your street address for billing purposes.
* **City** (`billing_address_line_2`): Enter your city name for billing purposes.
* **State** (`state`): Select your state of residence for billing purposes.
* **Zip** (`zip`): Enter your zip code for billing purposes.
* **Country** (`country`): Select your country of residence for billing purposes.
* **Shipping Report** (`shipping_report`): Enter any shipping reports or notes for the order.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`): Enter any shipping details or comments about the order.
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`): Choose the shipping method for the order (FedEx, USPS, etc.).
* **Shipping Cost** (`shipping_cost`): Enter the shipping cost for the order.
* **Tax Report** (`tax_report`): Enter any tax reports or notes for the order.
* **Tax Details** (`tax_details`): Enter any tax details or comments about the order.
* **Discount Report** (`discount_report`): Enter any discount reports or notes for the order.
* **Discount Details** (`discount_details`): Enter any discount details or comments about the order.
