# Factory Power Backup Battery Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Factory Power Backup Battery Order Form" is used for ordering power backup batteries from a factory. This form requires accurate and complete information to process your order correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your factory name and address in the "Factory Name" and "Address" fields, respectively.
2. Provide your preferred delivery date in the "Preferred Date" field.
3. Enter the delivery date you would like to receive the product in the "Delivery Date" field.
4. Enter the quantity of batteries you would like to order in the "Quantity" field.
5. Enter the unit price of the product in the "Unit Price" field. This value will be automatically calculated for you.
6. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.
7. Enter your contact person's name in the "Contact Person" field.
8. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
9. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
10. Optionally, enter any additional comments or notes in the "Contact Message" field.
11. Confirm your order details by selecting "I confirm the order details above" from the "Confirm" dropdown menu.
12. Finally, submit your order by filling out the "Order Number" field with a unique number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Factory Name** (`Factory Name`, Text, Required): Enter your factory name to identify your business or organization.
* **Address** (`Address`, Text, Optional): Enter the address of your factory location.
* **City** (`City`, Text, Optional): Enter the city of your factory location.
* **State** (`State`, Text, Optional): Enter the state of your factory location.
* **Zip Code** (`Zip Code`, Number, Required): Enter the zip code of your factory location.
* **Preferred Date** (`Preferred Date`, Date, Required): Enter the date you would like to receive the product.
* **Delivery Date** (`Delivery Date`, Date, Required): Enter the date you would like to receive the product on.
* **Quantity** (`Quantity`, Number, Required): Enter the number of batteries you would like to order.
* **Unit Price** (`Unit Price`, Number, Required): Enter the price per unit of the product. This value will be used to calculate the total price.
* **Total Price** (`Total Price`, Number, Required): This field will automatically calculate the total price of your order based on the unit price and quantity.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, Select One, Required): Select your preferred payment method from the dropdown menu.
* **Payment Info** (`Payment Info`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional information about your payment.
* **Order Date** (`Order Date`, Date, Required): Enter the date you would like to receive the product on.
* **Order Number** (`Order Number`, Text, Required): Enter a unique order number for your order.
* **Contact Person** (`Contact Person`, Select One, Required): Select the contact person responsible for your order.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, Email, Required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, Text, Required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Message** (`Contact Message`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes for your contact person.
* **Confirm** (`Confirm`, Select Multiple, Required): Confirm your order details by selecting "I confirm the order details above" from the dropdown menu.
* **Cancel** (`Cancel`, Text, Optional): This field is not required for the order submission.

Note: Some fields have specific requirements or constraints that must be followed. Please ensure you understand the requirements for each field before submitting your order.
