# Sports Apparel Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for customers to place orders for sports apparel. It collects necessary information to fulfill orders and gather feedback for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the requested information.
2. Be sure to review your answers before submitting the form.
3. If you have any questions or issues, contact the form administrators at form@gic.mx.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information (Page 1, Field 1)** 
  (Customer name, email, etc.): Enter your name and contact information to help us identify you as our customer.
* **Shipping Information (Page 1, Field 2)**
  (Address, phone number, etc.): Provide your shipping address and contact number so we can deliver your order correctly.
* **Order Form (Page 1, Field 3)**
  (Order details, such as product selection): Describe the sports apparel items you would like to order.
* **Payment Information (Page 1, Field 4)**
  (Payment method, total): Provide your payment method and total amount due.
* **Review (Page 1, Field 5)**
  (Text area for feedback): If you'd like to provide feedback, use this space to describe your experience with our sports apparel.
* **Review Info (Page 1, Field 6)**
  (Optional text field for additional information): If you'd like to provide more details about your review, you can use this field.
* **Review Rating (Page 1, Field 7)**
  (Rating of 1-5): Rate your experience with a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Review Date (Page 1, Field 8)**
  (Date): Enter the date you reviewed the sports apparel.
