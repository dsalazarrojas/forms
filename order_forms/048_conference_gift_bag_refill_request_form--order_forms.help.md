# Conference Gift Bag Refill Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a refill of conference gift bags. It is designed to be used by event teams to gather information necessary for coordinating and managing the refill process.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow the instructions below:

*   Make sure you are submitting this form for the purpose of requesting a refill and not for other types of requests.
*   Fill out the form completely and accurately.
*   Review your submission carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Requester name** (`Requester name`, `text`, required)
    This field is used to enter the name of the person requesting the refill.
*   **Event name** (`Event name`, `text`, required)
    This field is used to enter the name of the conference or event for which the gift bag refill is being requested.
*   **Bag items required** (`Bag items required`, `text`, required)
    In this field, list the items and quantities needed for the refill. For example, "1 water bottle, 2 pens, etc."
*   **Preferred delivery date** (`Preferred delivery date`, `date`, required)
    Enter the date when the refill is needed at the venue. This date will be used to schedule the refill delivery.
*   **Delivery location** (`Delivery location`, `text`, required)
    In this field, enter the location where the gift bag refill will be delivered, such as a venue dock, room, or a contact name.
*   **Budget code** (`Budget code`, `text`, not required)
    This field is for entering the charge code for procurement purposes.
*   **Vendor preference** (`Vendor preference`, `text`, not required)
    If you have a preferred supplier, enter their name in this field.
*   **Contact phone** (`Contact phone`, `text`, not required)
    Enter a contact phone number for delivery coordination purposes.
*   **Approval required** (`Approval required before order`, `select_one`, not required)
    If your organization requires internal approval before the order is processed, select `True` here.
*   **Additional notes** (`Additional notes`, `text`, not required)
    Enter any additional notes or instructions for the supplier in this field.

## Tips
*   Review your submission carefully before submitting the form.
*   If you need to resubmit the form, please contact the event team.
*   Make sure to use this form only for refill requests, not for new orders.
