# Construction Equipment Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you order construction equipment for your projects. Please fill out the form to request heavy machinery such as excavators, cranes, and loaders for your construction site.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name as the requester.
2. Enter your contact phone number for delivery coordination.
3. Select the type of equipment you need (e.g., excavator, crane, loader, etc.).
4. Specify the number of units required.
5. Provide your full site delivery address.
6. You may also provide optional information such as your company name, the number of rental duration days, and preferred delivery date.
7. If you have any special instructions or handling requirements, please include them in the "Special instructions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Requester Information**
* **Requester name** (`requester_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as the person placing the order.
### 2. **Company Information** (`company_name`, `text`, optional)
* **Company name** (`company_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your company name if applicable.
### 3. **Contact Information**
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your best contact phone number for delivery coordination.
### 4. **Equipment Selection**
* **Equipment type** (`equipment_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of equipment you need (e.g., excavator, crane, loader, etc.).
### 5. **Order Details**
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, required): Specify the number of units required.
### 6. **Delivery Information**
* **Delivery address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, required): Enter your full site delivery address.
### 7. **Additional Information**
* **Preferred delivery date** (`preferred_delivery_date`, `date`, optional): Enter your preferred delivery date if applicable.
* **Rental duration days** (`rental_duration_days`, `number`, optional): Specify the number of days the equipment is needed.
* **Special instructions** (`special_instructions`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional handling or site access requirements, enter them here.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled out.
- Review your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
- If you need to make changes, please contact our customer support for assistance.
