# New Novel Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Novel Pre Order Form is a tool for customers to place pre-orders for new novels. It captures essential information about the book, the customer, and the order details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Book Title** of the novel you wish to pre-order.
2. Enter your **Author's Name**.
3. Enter your **Email** address.
4. Enter your **Phone Number**.
5. Choose whether the book is a **Signed** copy or a **Gift**.
6. Enter a brief **Pre-Order Message**.
7. Enter the **Shipping Name**.
8. Enter the **Shipping Address**.
9. Enter the **Shipping City**.
10. Enter the **Shipping State**.
11. Enter the **Shipping Zip Code**.
12. Select the **Shipping Method** (UPS, USPS, or FedEx).
13. Enter the **Quantity** of books you wish to order.
14. Enter the **Signed Amount** for each book.
15. Enter the **Price** of each book.
16. Select the **Payment Method** (Credit Card or PayPal).
17. Enter the **Signed Date**.
18. (Optional) Enter the **Signed Time**.
19. Enter the **Store Name**.
20. Enter the **Store Address**.
21. Enter the **Store Phone Number**.
22. (Optional) Enter the **Store Email**.
23. (Optional) Enter the **Store URL**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Book Title**: The title of the novel you wish to pre-order. 
* **Author**: The name of the author of the novel you wish to pre-order.
* **Email**: A valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone**: A valid phone number where you can be reached.
* **Signed or Gift**: Whether this is a signed copy or a gift.
* **Signed Message**: A message to be signed in the book, optional.
* **Shipping Name**: The name to ship the order to.
* **Shipping Address**: The address to ship the order to.
* **Shipping City**: The city to ship the order to.
* **Shipping State**: The state to ship the order to.
* **Shipping Zip**: The zip code to ship the order to.
* **Shipping Method**: The shipping method to use (UPS, USPS, or FedEx).
* **Quantity**: The number of books to order.
* **Signed Amount**: The amount to be signed in the book.
* **Price**: The price of the book.
* **Payment Method**: The payment method to use (Credit Card or PayPal).
* **Signed Date**: The date to sign the book on.
* **Signed Time**: The time to sign the book on, optional.
* **Pre-Order Message**: A brief message to include with the order.
* **Store Name**: The name of the store.
* **Store Address**: The address of the store.
* **Store Phone**: The phone number of the store.
* **Store Email**: The email of the store, optional.
* **Store URL**: The website of the store, optional.
