# Purchase Order Data Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Purchase Order Data Upload Form is a tool to collect and upload vendor information related to purchase orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the relevant fields for the first vendor.
2. Upload any supporting documents required for the first vendor.
3. Repeat steps 1 and 2 for any additional vendors.
4. Add any additional information or comments for each vendor.
5. Click the submit button to finalize the upload.
