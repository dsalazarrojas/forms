# On Call Service Proposal - Help Guide
## Purpose
The On Call Service Proposal form is used by clients to submit their on-call service needs and details to the service provider. It is used to facilitate communication and ensure that both parties are on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To get started, please fill out your Client Details, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant information.
2. Next, enter the specifics about the service you are requesting in the Service Details field.
3. If there are any proposal terms you would like to include, type them in the Proposal Terms field.
4. Select the desired maintenance service option from the dropdown menu.
5. Select the desired repair service option from the dropdown menu.
6. Select the desired consulting service option from the dropdown menu.
7. If you have any agreements or terms you'd like to include, type them in the Proposal Terms Agreement field.
8. Finally, sign and date your proposal by filling out the Client Signature field.
9. The service provider will sign and date their copy of the proposal in the Service Provider Signature field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

1. **Client Details**: Enter your name, email, and any other relevant information for the service provider.
2. **Service Details**: Describe the services you are requesting in as much detail as possible.
3. **Proposal Terms**: Include any specific terms or conditions you would like the service provider to adhere to.
4. **Maintenance Service** (`maintenance_service`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the maintenance service option that best fits your needs.
	* Option 1: Regular maintenance
	* Option 2: On demand maintenance
	* Option 3: Custom maintenance
5. **Repair Service** (`repair_service`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the repair service option that best fits your needs.
	* Option 1: Urgent repairs
	* Option 2: Non-urgent repairs
	* Option 3: Maintenance repairs
6. **Consulting Service** (`consulting_service`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the consulting service option that best fits your needs.
	* Option 1: One-time consultations
	* Option 2: Ongoing consulting
	* Option 3: Specialized consulting
7. **Proposal Terms Agreement**: Type any additional terms or agreements you would like to include.
8. **Client Signature**: Sign and date your proposal to indicate your agreement to the terms.
9. **Service Provider Signature**: The service provider will sign and date their copy of the proposal to confirm their agreement to the terms.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that the service provider has all the necessary information to prepare a comprehensive proposal.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the service provider for assistance.
* Remember to carefully review the proposal terms and conditions before signing.
* This form is a template, please customize it according to your needs.
