# Bostocks Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Bostocks Order Form is designed to collect order information from customers, allowing the company to process their order efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by filling out the customer details section, which includes:
	* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
	* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you would like to receive confirmation of your order.
	* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
	* **Delivery Address**: Enter the address where you would like to receive your order.
2. In the Product Selection section, select the quantity of each product you would like to order.
	* **Product A - Quantity**: Enter the quantity of Product A you would like to order.
	* **Product B - Quantity**: Enter the quantity of Product B you would like to order.
	* **Product C - Quantity**: Enter the quantity of Product C you would like to order.
3. In the Special Instructions section, include any additional comments or special instructions you have for your order.
4. In the Delivery and Payment section, select your preferred delivery date and payment method.
	* **Preferred Delivery Date**: Select the date you would like to receive your order.
	* **Payment Method**: Select how you would like to pay for your order (Cash on Delivery, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer).
5. Finally, review your order and click "submit" to complete your order.
