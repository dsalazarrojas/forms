# Alarm Monitoring Service Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect payment information for the Alarm Monitoring Service. To complete this form, you will need to provide your account details, choose a monitoring plan and payment cycle, and specify payment and billing information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button at the bottom of the form to begin filling in the form.
2. Enter your **Customer Name** in the "Customer Name" field.
3. Enter your **Account Number** in the "Alarm Account Number" field.
4. Enter the **Service Address** where the alarm is installed.
5. Enter your **Contact Email** for payment receipt.
6. Enter your **Contact Phone** number.
7. Choose a **Monitoring Plan** that suits your needs from the options provided.
8. Select a **Payment Cycle** that suits your needs from the options provided.
9. Enter the **Amount to Pay** (in dollars).
10. Choose your **Cardholder Name**.
11. Select your **Card Type** from the options provided.
12. Decide if you want to **Enroll in Automatic Monthly Payments**.
13. Read and agree to the **Authorization Statement**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification or passport.
* **Alarm Account Number**: Enter your alarm system account number, if you have one. If you don't, please enter your account number or any other unique identification number associated with your alarm system.
* **Service Address**: Enter the address where your alarm system is installed.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address you want to receive payment receipts.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number for payment confirmation or questions.
* **Monitoring Plan**: Choose from the options:
	+ **Basic (Burglar Only)**: Basic monitoring for burglar alarms.
	+ **Standard (Burglar and Fire)**: Standard monitoring for burglar and fire alarms.
	+ **Premium (Video Monitoring and Smart Home)**: Premium monitoring with video and smart home features.
	+ **Commercial Enterprise Plan**: Customizable plan for commercial properties.
* **Payment Cycle**: Choose from the options:
	+ **Monthly**: Pay monthly.
	+ **Quarterly (5% Discount)**: Pay quarterly with a 5% discount.
	+ **Annual (10% Discount)**: Pay annually with a 10% discount.
* **Amount to Pay**: Enter the amount due for the chosen payment cycle.
* **Cardholder Name**: Enter the name of the person on the credit card.
* **Card Type**: Choose from the options:
	+ **Visa**
	+ **Mastercard**
	+ **American Express**
	+ **Discover**
* **Enroll in Automatic Monthly Payments**: Choose to be charged automatically each month.
* **I authorize the security provider to charge my card for monitoring services**: Read and agree to the authorization statement.

Please note that some fields are marked as "required" to ensure that your payment information is correctly processed.
