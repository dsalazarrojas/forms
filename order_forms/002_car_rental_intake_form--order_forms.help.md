# Car Rental Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for car rental purposes, including collecting personal information, rental details, and preferences for the rental period.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your personal details, including full name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and driver's license number.
3. Choose the pickup date and time for your rental.
4. Select your preferred vehicle type and insurance coverage level.
5. Choose any additional equipment you require during your rental.
6. Confirm that all provided information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your driver's license. 
* **Email Address**: The email address where we will send your rental confirmation.
* **Phone Number**: Required for pickup coordination.
* **Date of Birth**: Drivers must be 21 or older.
* **Driver's License Number**: Enter your driver's license number.
* **Pickup Date**: The date you will be picking up your rental vehicle.
* **Pickup Time**: The time you will be picking up your rental vehicle.
* **Return Date**: The date you will be returning your rental vehicle.
* **Vehicle Type Preference**: Select your preferred car category.
* **Insurance Selection**: Choose your coverage level.
* **Additional Equipment Needed**: Select any extras you require.
* **I certify that all information provided is accurate**: Confirm that all provided information is accurate.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.
