# AI Solution Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "AI Solution Quotation Form" is designed to help project owners provide the necessary information for a solution provider to quote a project. By filling out this form, you'll be able to gather all the required details and provide a clear understanding of your project's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To get started, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name and contact email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Describe the project in a few sentences, including any specific needs or requirements.
3. Select the preferred solution type from the available options.
4. Choose the estimated budget range for your project.
5. Specify the desired timeline, including start and completion dates.
6. If applicable, provide any procurement requirements or additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client name**: Enter the client's name, whether it's a company or an individual. This will help the solution provider get in touch with you for any further questions or clarifications.
* **Contact email**: Provide your contact email address for the solution provider to reach you for sending the quotation and any other communication. Make sure it's a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Project description**: Briefly describe the project, highlighting its key aspects, specific needs, and requirements. This will help the solution provider understand what you're looking for.
* **Preferred solution type**: Select the type of solution you're looking for, such as Custom model development, Managed service, Integration and deployment, or Consulting only. This will guide the solution provider in tailoring their quote.
* **Budget range**: Choose the estimated budget range for your project, which will help the solution provider provide a more accurate quote.
* **Desired timeline**: Specify the target start and completion dates for the project. This will help the solution provider plan and allocate resources accordingly.
* **Procurement requirements**: If you have any specific requirements or needs related to procurement, such as contractual needs or vendor onboarding, please mention them here. This will help the solution provider understand your expectations.
* **Additional information**: If you have any additional information that might be relevant to your project, feel free to include it here. This is an optional field, so don't worry if you don't have anything to add.
