# Restaurant Takeout Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect necessary information for processing a takeout order from a customer.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply navigate through the pages and fill in the required and optional fields with the relevant information. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, not required): This is the main section where you will enter the order details.
* **Name** (`name`, text, not required): Enter the customer's name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, not required): Enter the date you would like to pick up the order.
* **Time** (`time`, time, not required): Enter the time you would like to pick up the order.
* **Order Items** (`order_items`, select_multiple, not required): Select the items you would like to order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, not required): Choose your preferred payment method.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, not required): Enter the total cost of your order.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, not required): Enter any notes or comments about your order.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, not required): Enter any special requests you may have.
* **Delivery Info** (`delivery_info`, text, not required): Enter any delivery information.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, not required): Enter the date you would like to deliver the order.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, not required): Enter the time you would like to deliver the order.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, text, not required): Enter any notes or comments from the customer.
* **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, text, not required): Enter any comments about the order.
