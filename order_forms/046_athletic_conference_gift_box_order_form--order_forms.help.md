# Athletic Conference Gift Box Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athletic Conference Gift Box Order Form is used to create a custom order for a gift box to be used at a conference or event. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Conference or Event Name: Enter the name of the conference or event.
	* Date of Event: Enter the date of the event.
	* Primary Contact Person: Enter the name and contact information of the primary contact person.
	* Delivery Street Address: Enter the full address where the gift boxes will be delivered.
	* Requested Delivery Date: Enter the date by which you would like to receive the gift boxes.
	* Preferred Shipping Method: Select the shipping method that best suits your needs.
	* Preferred Billing Method: Select the method by which you would like to pay for the gift boxes.
2. Optional fields can be filled in as needed:
	* Gift Box Tier: Select the base level for your gift boxes (Essential, Premium, or Elite Executive).
	* Base Items to be Included: Choose the items to be included in the gift box (Stainless Steel Water Bottle, Branded Performance T-Shirt, Executive Notebook and Pen Set, etc.).
	* Premium Add-on Items: Select any additional items to be included in the gift box (Bluetooth Portable Speaker, Insulated 30oz Tumbler, etc.).
	* Apparel Sizing Breakdown: If ordering custom apparel, enter the sizing breakdown (e.g. 20-S, 40-M, 40-L, 10-XL).
	* Custom Message for Inclusion Card: Enter a custom message to be included in the gift box (max 200 characters).
	* Box Color and Aesthetic Theme: Select the color and theme of the gift box (Minimalist White, Executive Matte Black, Natural Kraft Paper, etc.).
	* Logo Placement Choice: Select where to place the logo on the gift box (printed on box lid, engraved on items, or both).
3. Finally, click "Submit" to complete the form and place your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Conference or Event Name** (`conference_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the conference or event.
* **Date of Event** (`event_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Primary Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): Enter the name and contact information of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Email Address** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the primary contact person.
* **Gift Box Tier** (`box_tier`, `select_one`, required): Select the base level for your gift boxes (Essential, Premium, or Elite Executive).
* **Total Quantity Required** (`total_quantity`, `number`, required): Enter the total quantity of gift boxes needed.
* **Base Items to be Included** (`base_items`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the items to be included in the gift box (Stainless Steel Water Bottle, Branded Performance T-Shirt, etc.).
* **Premium Add-on Items** (`premium_addons`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any additional items to be included in the gift box (Bluetooth Portable Speaker, Insulated 30oz Tumbler, etc.).
* **Apparel Sizing Breakdown** (`sizing_breakdown`, `text`, optional): If ordering custom apparel, enter the sizing breakdown.
* **Custom Message for Inclusion Card** (`custom_card_message`, `text`, optional): Enter a custom message to be included in the gift box (max 200 characters).
* **Box Color and Aesthetic Theme** (`box_aesthetic`, `select_one`, required): Select the color and theme of the gift box (Minimalist White, Executive Matte Black, Natural Kraft Paper, etc.).
* **Logo Placement Choice** (`logo_placement_choice`, `select_one`, required): Select where to place the logo on the gift box (printed on box lid, engraved on items, or both).
* **Delivery Street Address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, required): Enter the full address where the gift boxes will be delivered.
* **Requested Delivery Date** (`required_delivery_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date by which you would like to receive the gift boxes.
* **Preferred Shipping Method** (`shipping_method_preference`, `select_one`, required): Select the shipping method that best suits your needs (Standard Freight, Priority Overnight, Local White Glove Delivery to Venue).
* **Preferred Billing Method** (`payment_method_choice`, `select_one`, required): Select the method by which you would like to pay for the gift boxes (Corporate Invoice, Credit Card Payment, Pre-existing Corporate Account).
* **Date of Order Submission** (`order_submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the order submission.
