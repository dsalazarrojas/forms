# Employee Merchandise Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Merchandise Order Form is used to record and manage employee merchandise orders. This form is used to collect information on the order details, employee information, payment information, and order status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the order details section with the relevant information.
2. Select the merchandise products that the employee has chosen.
3. Fill in the payment information to ensure that the order is processed correctly.
4. Fill in the delivery information to ensure that the order is delivered to the correct location.
5. Add any notes or comments that the employee wishes to include.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main** (Page 1): This is the main section of the form. Enter the employee's name and ID number.
* **Order Details** (Page 2): Enter the order date and any other relevant details about the order.
* **Employee Info** (Page 3): Enter the employee's department and role within the company.
* **Order Products** (Page 4): Select the merchandise products that the employee has chosen. You can select multiple products by choosing "Yes" for each product.
* **Payment Info** (Page 5): Enter the payment method and any other relevant payment details.
* **Delivery Info** (Page 6): Enter the delivery address and any other relevant delivery details.
* **Employee Products** (Page 7): Select the merchandise products that the employee is purchasing. You can select multiple products by choosing "Yes" for each product.
* **Order Status** (Page 8): Enter the order status, which will be used to track the progress of the order.
* **Employee Notes** (Page 9): Enter any notes or comments that the employee wishes to include.

## Tips

* Make sure to select all relevant merchandise products for the employee.
* Double-check the payment information to ensure that the order is processed correctly.
* Enter the delivery information carefully to ensure that the order is delivered to the correct location.
* Include any relevant notes or comments to help track the progress of the order.
