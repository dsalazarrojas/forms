# Youth Photography Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Photography Order Form is a tool for users to provide information and preferences for a photo shoot. It is intended for youth clients, typically children, to order photo sessions with a photographer. The form collects contact information, session preferences, and print quantity requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name in the text field provided.
2. Enter your Email address in the correct format (e.g., [name]@domain.com).
3. Enter your phone number in the text field.
4. Select the desired Date for the photo session.
5. Choose the Start Time for the photo session.
6. Select the End Time for the photo session.
7. Choose the Photo Session type (Session 1, Session 2, or Session 3).
8. Select the Number of People participating in the session (1-10).
9. Select the Print Quantity (1-5).
10. Choose the Payment method (Pay Online or Pay Offline).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., [name]@domain.com).
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (date, required/optional): Select the date for the photo session.
* **Start Time** (time, required/optional): Choose the start time for the photo session.
* **End Time** (time, required/optional): Choose the end time for the photo session.
* **Photo Session** (select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of photo session you require (Session 1, Session 2, or Session 3).
* **Number of People** (select_multiple, required/optional): Select the number of people participating in the session (1-10).
* **Print Quantity** (select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the number of prints you require (1-5).
* **Payment** (select_one, required/optional): Choose the payment method (Pay Online or Pay Offline).
