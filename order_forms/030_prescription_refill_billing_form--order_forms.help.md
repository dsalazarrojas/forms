# Prescription Refill Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from patients for the refill of their prescription medication. It's intended for healthcare providers to gather details from patients and their family members or caregivers before processing the refill of their medication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your patient's name and phone number.
2. Enter your relationship with the patient (e.g., Patient, Family Member, Caregiver).
3. Select a payment method (e.g., Check, Credit Card, Electronic Funds Transfer).
4. Choose the payment method's associated date and time (if applicable).
5. Enter the payment amount (if applicable).
6. Enter any additional details or comments in the "Notes" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`): Please enter your patient's name as it appears on their identification card.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`): Enter your patient's phone number where we can reach them.
* **Patient Relationship** (`patient_relationship`): Choose "Patient", "Family Member", or "Caregiver" to describe your relationship with the patient.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`): Enter the address where you'd like the bill sent to.
* **Insurance Card Holder** (`insurance_card_holder`): Enter the holder's name as it appears on the insurance card.
* **Insurance Card Number** (`insurance_card_number`): Enter the number on the insurance card.
* **Prescription ID** (`prescription_id`): Enter the ID of the prescription being refilled.
* **Doctor** (`doctor_name`): Enter the doctor's name who prescribed the medication.
* **Doctor Phone** (`doctor_phone`): Enter the doctor's phone number.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`): Enter the patient's address as it appears on their identification card.
* **Refill Date** (`prescription_refill_date`): Enter the date for the refill of the medication.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional comments or details about the refill.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Choose "Check", "Credit Card", or "Electronic Funds Transfer" as the payment method.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`): Enter the date of payment (if applicable).
* **Payment Time** (`payment_time`): Enter the time of payment (if applicable).
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`): Enter the amount of the payment (if applicable).
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`): Enter any payment terms or agreements.
* **Relationship with Patient other** (`patient_relationship_other`): Enter any additional relationship details with the patient.
* **Billing Address other** (`billing_address_other`): Enter any additional address details for billing.
* **Insurance Card Holder other** (`insurance_card_holder_other`): Enter any additional details about the insurance card holder.
* **Payment Method other** (`payment_method_other`): Choose "Yes" or "No" if the payment method is different.
* **Payment Date other** (`payment_date_other`): Enter the date of the payment (if different).
* **Payment Amount other** (`payment_amount_other`): Enter the amount of the payment (if different).
