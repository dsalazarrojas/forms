# Online Shopping Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic information from customers interested in requesting a quote for an online shopping experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "User Info" page with your name and any other relevant details.
2. Move on to the "Product Info" page and provide a brief description of the product you're interested in.
3. Finally, go to the "Shipping Info" page and enter your shipping details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info**:
	+ **User Name** (required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
	+ **Additional Comments** (optional): Provide any additional information you'd like to share about your request.
* **Product Info**:
	+ **Product Description** (optional): Briefly describe the product you're interested in.
* **Shipping Info**:
	+ **Shipping Address** (required): Enter your shipping address to ensure accurate delivery.
	+ **Shipping City** (required): Enter the city where you'd like the product to be shipped.
	+ **Shipping Postal Code** (required): Enter your postal/zip code for shipping purposes.
	+ **Shipping Country** (required): Select the country where you'd like the product to be shipped.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled out to ensure a successful quote request.
- Be as detailed as possible in your product description to help us better understand your needs.
- Double-check your shipping details to avoid any errors or delays in delivery.
