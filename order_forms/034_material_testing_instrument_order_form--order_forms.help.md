# Material Testing Instrument Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Material Testing Instrument Order Form is designed to collect information for ordering material testing instruments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each field and its requirements.
* Select the type of instrument you need from the dropdown menu in the "Instrument Order" field.
* Enter the quantity you need for the instrument in the "Quantity Ordered" field.
* Provide any additional information or comments in the "Customer Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Instrument Order** (Select Multiple):
	+ This field allows you to select one or more types of instruments you need for material testing.
	+ Options include: Spectrophotometer, Chromatograph, Mass Spectrometer, Atomic Absorption Spectrometer, pH Meter, pH Meter with Temperature, and Thermocouple.
* **Quantity Ordered** (Number):
	+ Enter the quantity of the selected instrument you need.
* **Customer Information** (Text):
	+ Enter your name and company information.
* **Contact Person** (Select One):
	+ Select the contact person who will be responsible for the order.
	+ Options include: Dr. John Doe, PhD, and Dr. Jane Doe.
* **E-mail** (Email):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Company** (Text):
	+ Enter your company name.
* **Material Order Details** (Text):
	+ Enter any additional information about the material order.
* **Instrument Order Details** (Text):
	+ Enter any additional information about the instrument order.
* **Instrument Order Details 2** (Text):
	+ Enter any additional information about the instrument order (if needed).
* **Customer Comments** (Text):
	+ Enter any additional comments or feedback about the order.
* **Instrument Status** (Select One):
	+ Select the status of the instrument you ordered.
	+ Options include: Available, Out of Order, and In Use.
* **Quantity Status** (Select One):
	+ Select the status of the quantity ordered.
	+ Options include: Available, Out of Order, and In Use.
* **Instrument Status 2** (Select Multiple):
	+ Select the status of the instrument you ordered.
	+ Options include: Available, Out of Order, and In Use.
* **Contact Information** (Text):
	+ Enter any additional contact information.

Please complete the form carefully to ensure accurate and efficient order processing.
