# Social Media Service Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Media Service Order Form is designed to collect information from customers ordering social media services. This form will help our team understand what services you need and what to expect from your order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of social media service you need by choosing from the options on the "service_type" field.
2. Specify the duration for the service by selecting from the "service_duration" field.
3. Choose how frequently you'd like to receive the service by selecting from the "service_frequency" field.
4. Provide a price for the service by entering a numerical value in the "service_price" field.
5. Choose a payment gateway by selecting from the "payment_gateway" field.
6. Select the payment method you'd like to use by choosing from the "payment_method" field.
7. Once you've filled out all the required fields, submit the form to place your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details**: This field is where you can enter any additional information about your order. You can type in any comments or questions you have.
* **service_type** (Social Media Management, Social Media Advertising, Social Media Marketing, Social Media Consulting, Social Media Analytics): Select the type of social media service you need. This will help us understand what kind of service you're looking for.
* **service_duration** (One hour, One day, One week, One month): Choose how long you'd like the service to last.
* **service_frequency** (Daily, Weekly, Monthly): Choose how frequently you'd like to receive the service.
* **service_price**: Enter a numerical value for the price of the service. Please make sure to use a valid currency format.
* **payment_gateway** (Stripe, PayPal, Authorizenet): Select the payment gateway you'd like to use for this order.
* **payment_method** (Pay with Card, Pay with Account): Select the payment method you'd like to use for this order.
* **Tips**: Please ensure that you've filled out all the required fields before submitting the form. This will help us process your order quickly and efficiently.
