# Retail Tablet Display Stand Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect order details for a retail tablet display stand. It is designed to be completed by customers who wish to purchase a display stand for their tablets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the customer information section** (`customer_info`): Enter your name and any other contact details as required.
2. **Enter your shipping address** (`shipping_address`): Provide your address where the display stand should be shipped to.
3. **Select your preferred stand size** (`stand_choices`): Choose one or more of the available stand sizes that best fit your needs.
4. **Add any order notes** (`order_notes`): If you have any specific instructions or requests for your order, enter them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (`customer_info`)**: This field collects your name and contact details. Please enter your full name, email address, and phone number to ensure our team can contact you about your order.
* **Shipping Address (`shipping_address`):**: Provide your shipping address where the display stand should be delivered to. Please ensure it is accurate and complete to avoid any delays in delivery.
* **Stand Choices (`stand_choices`):**: Choose one or more of the available stand sizes from the dropdown list. This will determine the size of the display stand you will receive.
* **Order Notes (`order_notes`):**: If you have any specific instructions or requests for your order, enter them here. This could be anything from "deliver to the back door" to "please assemble the stand before shipping".

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your order details before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at <form@gic.mx>
* Please note that orders cannot be changed once they are submitted. If you need to modify your order, please contact us before submitting the form.
