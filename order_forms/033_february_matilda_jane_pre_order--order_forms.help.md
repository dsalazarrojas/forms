# February Matilda Jane Pre Order - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for potential customers to express interest in pre-ordering the February Matilda Jane collection. If you are a current customer, you can skip this form and contact us directly to place a stock order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select 'Yes' or 'No' from the Pre-Order Form dropdown menu if you are interested in pre-ordering the collection.
2. Fill in your name in the text field below.
3. Enter your email address in the email field, making sure to follow the email format (e.g., username@example.com).
4. Provide your phone number in the text field.
5. Enter your address in the text field.
6. Type in your city in the text field.
7. Enter your state in the text field.
8. Enter your zip code in the number field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pre-Order Form** (`order_form`, select_multiple, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your interest in pre-ordering the February Matilda Jane collection.
* **name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address in the format of username@example.com.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required): Provide your phone number.
* **address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address where the order will be shipped.
* **city** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **state** (`state`, text, required): Enter the two-letter state abbreviation where you reside.
* **zip** (`zip`, number, required): Enter the zip code for your address.

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, contact us directly.
* We will notify you when the product is available for stock order.
