# Food Pre Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to capture customer information, food orders, and payment details for food pre-orders. It is designed to gather essential data for processing customer orders efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your customer details in the "Customer Details" section.
2. Select the type of food you would like to order from the "Food Order" dropdown menu.
3. If you have any special requests, enter them in the "Special Requests" field.
4. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
5. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter the desired delivery time in the "Delivery Time" field.
7. Add any special instructions for the delivery in the "Special Instructions" field.
8. Calculate or enter the total cost of the order in the "Order Total" field.
9. Provide any additional comments about your order in the "Customer Comments" field.
10. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.
11. Enter any additional payment details in the "Payment Details" field.
12. Add any order notes or additional information in the "Order Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details**: Please enter your name, address, and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Food Order**: Choose the type of food you would like to order from the dropdown menu (Pizza, Burger, Sushi).
* **Special Requests**: If you have any special requests or instructions for your order, enter them here.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number so we can reach you for delivery confirmation and any updates.
* **Email**: Provide your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **Delivery Time**: Enter the desired delivery time for your order.
* **Special Instructions**: Add any special instructions or notes about your delivery.
* **Order Total**: Enter the total cost of your order.
* **Customer Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information about your order.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you would like to pay for your order (Credit Card, Cash, Online Payment).
* **Payment Details**: If you selected a payment method that requires additional information, enter it here.
* **Order Notes**: Add any additional notes or information about your order.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly to avoid any delays in processing your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will contact you to confirm your order details before processing your payment.
