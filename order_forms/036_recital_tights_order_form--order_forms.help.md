# recital_tights_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record orders for recital tights, a type of performance attire typically worn by dancers. It is used to collect necessary information from studios to process orders efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Studio Name** where the order is being placed.
2. Enter the **Student Name** for whom the order is being placed.
3. Choose the **Order Date** for the recital.
4. Select the **Type of Product** you are ordering (Tights, Shorts, or Skirt).
5. Choose the **Product Color** of the selected product.
6. Select the **Product Size** that fits the student.
7. Enter any additional **Order Comment** to help us process the order.
8. Provide the **Customer Email** and **Customer Phone** for billing and contact purposes.
9. Enter the **Studio Email** and **Studio Phone** for communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Studio Name (Studio Name)**: Enter the name of the studio where the order is being placed.
* **Student Name (Student Name)**: Enter the name of the student for whom the order is being placed.
* **Order Date (Order Date)**: Choose the date of the recital the order is for.
* **Type of Product (Type of Product)**: Select the type of product you are ordering (Tights, Shorts, or Skirt).
* **Product Color (Product Color)**: Choose the color of the product you selected.
* **Product Size (Product Size)**: Select the size of the product that fits the student.
* **Order Comment (Order Comment)**: Enter any additional comments to help us process the order.
* **Customer Email (Customer Email)**: Enter the email address of the customer for billing and contact purposes.
* **Customer Phone (Customer Phone)**: Enter the phone number of the customer for contact and billing purposes.
* **Studio Email (Studio Email)**: Enter the email address of the studio.
* **Studio Phone (Studio Phone)**: Enter the phone number of the studio.
* **Assigned Tool (Assigned Tool)**: Not applicable for this form.
* **Assigned User (Assigned User)**: Not applicable for this form.
* **Status (Status)**: Not applicable for this form.
* **Created At (Created At)**: Not applicable for this form.
* **Updated At (Updated At)**: Not applicable for this form.
* **Deleted At (Deleted At)**: Not applicable for this form.
* **Created By (Created By)**: Not applicable for this form.
* **Updated By (Updated By)**: Not applicable for this form.
