# Custom Beer Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for customers to provide their information and order details for beer deliveries. The form consists of three pages that help gather the necessary information for smooth order processing.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and honestly to ensure accurate information is captured.
2. Provide all required fields.
3. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (customer_info)** (`Customer Information`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Delivery Info (delivery_info)** (`Delivery Info`, text, optional):
	+ Provide your delivery address and any specific delivery instructions.
* **Order Summary (order_summary)** (`Order Summary`, text, optional):
	+ Briefly describe your order, including any special requests or notes.
