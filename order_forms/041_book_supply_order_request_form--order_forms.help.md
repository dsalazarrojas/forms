# Book Supply Order Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information for book supply orders from various customers.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Fill in your full name as it appears on your official documents.
*   Provide a valid email address where you can be reached.
*   Enter your phone number for order inquiries or communication.
*   If you are representing an organization, enter your organization's name.
*   Choose the type of your organization (e.g., School, Library, Bookstore, University, Business, Individual, or Other).
*   Enter your complete shipping address for delivery.
*   Enter the name of your city.
*   Enter your state/region.
*   Enter your postal code (zip code).
*   Choose the date of your order.
*   Enter the title and author of each book you want to order.
*   Specify the quantity of each book you want to order.
*   Enter the total quantity of all books ordered.
*   Select the shipping method (Standard 5-7 days, Express 2-3 days, Overnight, or Store Pickup).
*   Choose the payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, Check, Purchase Order, Invoice).
*   If you have any special requests, type them in this field.
*   Read and agree to the terms and conditions.
