# Lifestyle Magazine Casing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Lifestyle Magazine Casing Order Form is used to collect order details from customers for magazine casing production and shipping.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Review and verify that all fields are filled in correctly before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Magazine Casing Order** (`magazine_casing_order_form`, text, optional): This field is for the customer to enter the order details in their own words.
* **Order Number** (`order_number`, text, optional): This field is for the customer to enter their order reference number for their records.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): This field is for the customer to enter their name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): This field is for the customer to enter their email address for communication.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): This field is for the customer to enter their phone number.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, optional): This field is for the customer to enter their address for shipping purposes.
* **Magazine Title** (`magazine_title`, text, optional): This field is for the customer to enter the title of the magazine they are ordering.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): This field is for the customer to enter the number of magazine casings they are ordering.
* **Material** (`material`, select_one, optional): This field is for the customer to select the material they prefer for the magazine casing (Glass, Plastic, Wood).
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for the customer to select the shipping method they prefer (UPS, Fedex, USPS).
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): This field is for the customer to enter their shipping address.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, note, optional): This field is for the customer to add any special instructions for their order.

## Tips

* Double-check all fields before submitting the form to ensure accurate and complete information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our customer service team.
