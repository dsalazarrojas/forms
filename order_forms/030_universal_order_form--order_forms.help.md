# Universal Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Universal Order Form is a tool used to collect customer information and order details for online orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* First Name (id 1)
	* Last Name (id 2)
	* Payment Method (id 5)
	* Products/Services (id 6)
2. Provide optional fields:
	* Email (id 3)
	* Phone (id 4)
	* Date (id 7)
	* Time (id 8)
	* Note (id 9)
3. Select one payment method and at least one product/service for the order.
4. Click the "Submit Order" button (id 10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (id 1)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (id 2)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email (id 3)**: Enter a valid email address. This will help us contact you with order updates.
* **Phone (id 4)**: Enter a valid phone number. This will help us contact you if needed.
* **Payment Method (id 5)**: Choose how you want to pay for your order: Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
* **Products/Services (id 6)**: Select at least one product or service you would like to order. You can select multiple options.
* **Date (id 7)**: Select a date for your order (optional).
* **Time (id 8)**: Select a time for your order (optional).
* **Note (id 9)**: Add any additional comments or requests for your order (optional).
* **Submit Order (id 10)**: Review and submit your order details.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
* Use a valid email address and phone number.
* Select at least one product/service for your order.
* Review your order details before submitting.
