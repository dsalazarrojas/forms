# vegetables_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vegetables_order_form is a form designed for customers to place orders for vegetables. It gathers information for the customer's selection of products, delivery details, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the products you wish to order on page 1.
2. Fill in your customer details, including your name and contact information on page 2.
3. Specify your delivery information, including address and delivery time on page 3.
4. On page 4, select the quantity of each product you wish to order.
5. On page 5, enter any additional comments or requests for your order.
6. On page 6, enter any additional contact information.
7. On page 7, specify your preferred delivery time.
8. On page 8, enter your delivery address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select products**: Choose the products you wish to order from the list.
* **Customer details**: Enter your name and contact information, such as email and phone number.
* **Delivery info**: Enter your delivery address and preferred delivery time.
* **Select quantity**: Choose the quantity of each product you wish to order.
* **Contact details**: Enter any additional contact information.
* **Delivery time**: Specify your preferred delivery time.
* **Order notes**: Enter any additional comments or requests for your order.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Delivery address**: Enter your delivery address.

## Tips
- Make sure to select the correct products and quantities to ensure accurate order fulfillment.
- Double-check your delivery information to ensure it is correct.
- Use the "Order notes" field to leave any additional comments or requests for your order.
