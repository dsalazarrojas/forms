# Cookie Cakes Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place an order for a cookie cake. To place an order, please follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
To place an order, please complete the following steps:

1. Provide your customer information by filling out the "Customer Full Name", "Email Address", and "Phone Number" fields.
2. Select the "Order Date" and "Pickup or Delivery Date" fields to specify when you would like to receive your order.
3. Choose the type of order you would like to place (Pickup or Delivery).
4. Select the size and flavor of your cookie cake, as well as the type of frosting and frosting flavor you would like.
5. If you would like to add any customizations, such as a message or decorations, please describe them in the "Message to Write" and "Additional Decorations" fields.
6. Select the quantity of cookie cakes you would like to order.
7. If you would like to include any additional decorations, such as sprinkles or an edible image, please select the options and provide any additional details.
8. Finally, select your payment method and provide any special requests or allergen information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Full Name** (Customer Full Name, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Enter the email address you would like to use for order confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required): Enter your best contact number in case we need to reach you.
* **Order Date** (Order Date, date, required): Select the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Pickup or Delivery Date** (Pickup or Delivery Date, date, required): Select the date you would like to receive your order (if delivery).
* **Order Type** (Order Type, select_one, required): Choose between "Pickup" or "Delivery".
* **Cookie Cake Size** (Cookie Cake Size, select_one, required): Choose the size of your cookie cake (Small - 8 inch, Medium - 10 inch, Large - 12 inch, Extra Large - 14 inch, Custom).
* **Cookie Flavor** (Cookie Flavor, select_one, required): Select the base flavor for your cookie cake (Chocolate Chip, Double Chocolate, Sugar Cookie, Red Velvet, Oatmeal Raisin, Peanut Butter, Snickerdoodle, Birthday Cake, Other).
* **Frosting Type** (Frosting Type, select_one, required): Choose the type of frosting you would like (Buttercream, Cream Cheese, Chocolate Ganache, Royal Icing, Whipped Cream, No Frosting).
* **Frosting Flavor** (Frosting Flavor, select_one, required): Select the flavor of your frosting (Vanilla, Chocolate, Strawberry, Lemon, Cream Cheese, Mocha, Other).
* **Message to Write** (Message to Write, text, required): If you would like to add a message to your cookie cake, enter it here.
* **Message Color** (Message Color, select_one, required): Choose the color of your message (Black, Brown, Red, Blue, Pink, Purple, Green, Rainbow, Silver).
* **Border Design** (Border Design, select_one, required): Select the border design of your cookie cake (Simple Border, Scalloped Border, Shell Border, Rope Border, No Border, Custom).
* **Border Color** (Border Color, select_one, required): Choose the color of your border (White, Chocolate, Pink, Blue, Red, Purple, Green, Rainbow).
* **Quantity** (Quantity, number, required): Enter the number of cookie cakes you would like to order.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_one, required): Select the method you will use to pay for your order (Credit Card, Debit Card, Cash, Gift Card).
* **Special Requests** (Special Requests, text, required): If you have any special requests or requirements, enter them here.
* **Allergen Information** (Allergen Information, text, required): If you have any allergies we should be aware of, enter them here.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, text, required): Any other information you would like to provide about your order.
