# Coffee Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coffee Order Form is a tool to help you create the perfect cup of coffee. It guides you through the process of selecting a drink, customizing it to your taste, and providing delivery information so we can get it to you on time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **Customer Information** section with your name, email, and phone number.
2. Choose the **Delivery Type**, select a **Delivery Address**, and specify the **Desired Delivery Date** and **Preferred Delivery Time**.
3. Select your **Espresso-based Drink** from the options provided.
4. Choose the **Quantity** of the drink you want.
5. Select your **Milk Option** and **Cup Size**.
6. Choose your **Temperature** preference.
7. If desired, add **Extra Espresso Shots**, select a **Flavor Syrup**, and choose **Whipped Cream**.
8. In the **Additional Items** section, select any **Pastry or Food Item** you'd like to pair with your drink.
9. In the **Special Requests** section, add any **Special Instructions** we should know about (e.g., allergies, preferences).
10. Finally, select your **Payment Method**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as we'll use it for communication.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number in case we need to reach you.
* **Delivery Type** (`delivery_type`, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like to receive your coffee (In-store Pickup, Local Delivery, Shipping).
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, required): Enter your delivery address, including street address.
* **Desired Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required): Select the date you'd like to receive your coffee.
* **Preferred Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, optional): Choose a preferred delivery time for your convenience.
* **Espresso-based Drink** (`espresso_drink`, select_one, required): Select your desired drink (Espresso, Americano, Latte, etc.).
* **Quantity** (`espresso_quantity`, number, required): Choose the number of drinks you'd like to order.
* **Milk Option** (`milk_option`, select_one, required): Select the milk option for your drink (Regular, Skim, Soy, Almond, Oat, Coconut, No).
* **Cup Size** (`size`, select_one, required): Choose the cup size for your drink (Small, Medium, Large, Extra Large).
* **Temperature** (`temperature`, select_one, required): Select the temperature preference for your drink (Hot, Iced, Blended).
* **Extra Espresso Shots** (`extra_shots`, number, optional): If desired, add extra espresso shots to your drink.
* **Flavor Syrup** (`flavor_syrup`, select_one, optional): Select a flavor syrup to add to your drink (None, Vanilla, Caramel, etc.).
* **Whipped Cream** (`whipped_cream`, select_one, optional): Choose if you'd like whipped cream on your drink.
* **Pastry or Food Item** (`pastry_item`, select_one, optional): Select any pastry or food item you'd like to pair with your drink (None, Croissant, Muffin, etc.).
* **Pastry Quantity** (`pastry_quantity`, number, optional): Enter the number of pastry items you'd like to order.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, optional): Enter any special instructions or preferences we should know about (e.g., allergies, requests).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Cash on delivery).

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check your information, especially for delivery address and date.
* If you have any special requests or allergies, please include them in the **Special Instructions** field.
* We'll contact you to confirm your order details before processing the payment.
* Enjoy your perfect cup of coffee!
