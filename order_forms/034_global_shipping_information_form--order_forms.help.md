# Global Shipping Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Shipping Information Form is a tool used to gather essential details about your shipping address, shipping method, and delivery date for smooth order delivery. This form helps us process your order efficiently and ensure it reaches you on time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Customer Name** in the designated field.
2. Fill in your **Shipping Address**, including your street address or PO box.
3. Provide your **City** where you're located.
4. Enter your **Postal Code** to ensure correct delivery.
5. Select your **Country** of residence.
6. Choose your preferred **Shipping Method**: Standard, Express, or Air Shipping.
7. Set your desired **Delivery Date** for the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your shipping address or PO box number for correct delivery.
* **City** (`city`, text, required/optional): Enter the city where you're located.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, text, required/optional): Enter your postal code for accurate delivery.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required/optional): Select your country of residence.
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, select_one, required/optional): Choose from:
	+ Standard Shipping: Our standard shipping method with a longer delivery time.
	+ Express Shipping: Our faster shipping method with expedited delivery.
	+ Air Shipping: Our fastest shipping method with expedited delivery at an additional cost.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you'd like to receive your order by.

## Tips
* Please double-check your input for accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask our support team for assistance.
* Ensure you've entered the correct shipping method to avoid any delays or additional costs.
* You can always contact us if you need any modifications or cancellations.
