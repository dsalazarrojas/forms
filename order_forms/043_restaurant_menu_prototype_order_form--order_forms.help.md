# restaurant_menu_prototype_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The restaurant menu prototype order form is used to collect customer information for orders. It is used to record and track customer details related to their orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Select the order date using the date picker.
3. Enter the total order amount.
4. Choose the payment terms from the dropdown menu.
5. Select the status of the order from the dropdown menu.
6. Enter any additional customer information such as email, phone number, and address.
7. Enter any notes related to the customer.
8. Finally, review and submit the form to ensure all required fields are filled in.
