# Athlete Welcome Packet Printing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athlete Welcome Packet Printing Order Form is a form designed to collect information for printing Athlete Welcome Packets. This form should be used by clients (teams or organizations) to specify their requirements for the printing and delivery of the packets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your team or organization name to identify your order.
2. Provide the primary contact person's information, including their email address.
3. Select a required delivery date for the packets.
4. Specify the number of packets required.
5. Choose the included packet components.
6. Select the paper quality and weight for the packets.
7. Choose the binding or assembly type for the packets.
8. Select the cover page printing options.
9. If personalization is required, provide a link to the athlete roster data.
10. If personalization is required, specify any special printing or finishing instructions.
11. Enter your shipping address.
12. Enter your billing address (which should be the same as the shipping address if it's a standard delivery).
13. Choose the preferred shipping method.
14. Enter the date of order submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team or Organization Name**: Enter the name of your team or organization.
* **Primary Contact Person**: Enter the name and email of the person responsible for the order.
* **Contact Email Address**: Enter the email address of the primary contact person.
* **Required Delivery Date**: Select a required delivery date for the packets. Please allow 5-7 business days for production.
* **Total Number of Packets Required**: Enter the number of packets you need to print.
* **Included Packet Components**: Select all items to be included in each packet.
* **Paper Quality and Weight**: Choose the paper quality and weight for the packets.
* **Binding or Assembly Type**: Choose the binding or assembly type for the packets.
* **Cover Page Printing Options**: Select the printing options for the cover page.
* **Is individual personalization required?**: If you require individual personalization, select "Yes" and provide a link to the athlete roster data.
* **Link to Athlete Roster Data**: If individual personalization is selected, enter a link to the athlete roster data (e.g. Google Sheet or Dropbox link).
* **Special Printing or Finishing Instructions**: If personalization is required, specify any special printing or finishing instructions.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your full shipping address.
* **Billing Address**: Enter your full billing address (which should be the same as the shipping address if it's a standard delivery).
* **Preferred Shipping Method**: Choose the preferred shipping method.
* **Date of Order Submission**: Enter the date of order submission.

Please ensure that you have all necessary information before completing this form, as it will be used to produce the packets exactly as specified.
