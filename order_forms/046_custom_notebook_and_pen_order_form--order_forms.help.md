# Custom Notebook and Pen Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for customers to place an order for a custom notebook and pen.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the color of the pen you would like to order.
2.  Choose the size of the pen.
3.  Choose the size of the notebook you would like to order.
4.  Select the cover type of the notebook.
5.  Choose the color of the notebook cover.
6.  Choose the type of paper in the notebook.
7.  Enter the quantity of the notebook and pen you would like to order.
8.  Enter your email address for contact purposes.
9.  Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
10. Enter any additional comments or notes about your order.
11. Click the "Submit" button to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Custom Notebooks** (`custom_notebook_and_pen_order_form_page1`): This is the section where you can select the size of the notebook you would like to order.
* **pen_color** (`pen_color`): Choose the color of the pen from the dropdown menu. Options are blue, red, and green.
* **pen_size** (`pen_size`): Choose the size of the pen you would like to order. Options are small, medium, and large.
* **notebook_size** (`notebook_size`): Choose the size of the notebook you would like to order. Options are small, medium, and large.
* **notebook_cover_type** (`notebook_cover_type`): Select the type of cover you would like for your notebook. Options are plain, glossy, and matte.
* **notebook_cover_color** (`notebook_cover_color`): Choose the color of the notebook cover. Options are white, black, and blue.
* **paper_type** (`paper_type`): Choose the type of paper in the notebook. Options are plain, lined, and grid.
* **quantity** (`quantity`): Enter the quantity of notebooks and pens you would like to order.
* **email** (`email`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **comments** (`custom_notebook_and_pen_order_form_comments`): Enter any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Submit** (`custom_notebook_and_pen_order_form_submit`): Click this button to complete your order.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
- If you have any special requests or questions, enter them in the "Comments" field.
- If you have any issues with your order, contact us at the email address you provided.
