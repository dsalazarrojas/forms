# Domain Name Registration Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect payment details and contact information from users who wish to register their domain name.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your domain name in the "domain_name" field.
2. Provide your contact information, including your first name in the "contact_first_name" field and your last name in the "contact_last_name" field.
3. Enter your contact email address in the "contact_email" field.
4. Enter your contact address in the "contact_address" field.
5. Provide your contact phone number in the "contact_phone" field.
6. If you're paying by bank transfer, check the "Bank Transfer" option in the "payment_method" field.
7. If you're paying by check, check the "Check" option in the "payment_method" field.
8. If you're paying by credit card, check the "Credit Card" option in the "payment_method" field.
9. Select the "Submit" option in the "submit" field to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **domain_name**: Enter the domain name you wish to register (e.g., example.com).
* **contact_first_name**: Enter your first name (e.g., John).
* **contact_last_name**: Enter your last name (e.g., Doe).
* **contact_email**: Enter your email address (e.g., johndoe@example.com).
* **contact_address**: Enter your contact address (e.g., 123 Main St, Anytown, USA).
* **contact_phone**: Enter your contact phone number (e.g., 555-123-4567).
* **payment_details**: Enter any additional payment details (e.g., account number).
* **payment_method**: Select one of the available payment methods (e.g., Bank Transfer, Check, or Credit Card).
* **submit**: Select "Submit" to complete the form.
