# medicine delivery form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The medicine delivery form is used to collect information about medicine delivery, allowing medical professionals or authorized personnel to track and manage medicine delivery processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the medicine delivery. This field is not mandatory but is useful for tracking purposes.
* **order id** (`order_id`, text, required/optional): Enter the unique identification number of the order. This field is optional but helps in identifying and tracking orders.
* **patient name** (`patient_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the patient receiving the medicine. This field is optional but is useful for patient identification.
* **delivery type** (`delivery_type`, select multiple, required/optional): Select the type of delivery (Pharmacy, Home delivery, Mail order, or Clinic). This field is optional but helps in tracking delivery methods.
* **medication name** (`medication_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the medicine being delivered. This field is optional but is necessary for identifying the medicine.
