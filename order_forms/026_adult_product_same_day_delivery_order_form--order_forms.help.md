# Adult Product Same Day Delivery Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer information, delivery details, and payment information for adult products that require same-day delivery. The form ensures that customers provide accurate and complete information to facilitate a smooth and secure delivery experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the customer information section with your name, contact phone number, and contact email.
2. Select your preferred delivery time slot and provide any additional delivery instructions.
3. Choose the products you would like to order and specify the total quantity.
4. Enter the total order cost, including tax and delivery fee.
5. Confirm your age verification (18+ only) and select your payment method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (required)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Phone (required)**: Provide your contact phone number for delivery coordination.
* **Contact Email (required)**: Enter your email address for order confirmation and receipt.
* **Delivery Address (required)**: Enter your delivery address, including apartment or suite number.
* **Delivery Window (required)**: Select your preferred same-day time slot for delivery.
* **Delivery Instructions (optional)**: Provide any additional instructions for delivery, such as gate codes or drop-off location.
* **Products Ordered (required)**: Select the products you would like to order from the available options.
* **Total Quantity (required)**: Enter the total number of items you are ordering.
* **Order Total Cost (required)**: Enter the total cost of the order, including tax and delivery fee.
* **Age Verification 18 plus (required)**: Confirm that you are 18 years or older to receive adult products.
* **Payment Method (required)**: Select your preferred payment method, such as credit card, PayPal, or cash on delivery.
