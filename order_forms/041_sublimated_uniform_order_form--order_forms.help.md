# sublimated_uniform_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to provide their contact information, team details, and other relevant data for a uniform order. Please fill out this form to place an order with us.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form**: Carefully complete each field with the requested information.
2. **Submit the form**: Once you've filled out the form, click the submit button to send it to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details**: Enter your name, email, and any other contact information that we need to get in touch with you.
* **Team Details**: Provide information about the team or group you're ordering for, including the team name, jersey color, and other relevant details.
* **Player List**: List all players or team members who will be wearing the uniform. 
* **Required Date**: Choose the date when you need the uniforms by. If you have multiple dates, please provide all of them.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the address where we should ship the uniforms. Please ensure it's accurate and complete.
* **Design Details**: Provide any specific details about the design of the uniform you'd like us to follow for your order.
* **File**: If you have any supporting files (e.g. images) for your order, upload them here.
* **Review**: If you'd like to add any additional information or review your order before submitting, please do so here.
* **Order Details**: Enter any other details you'd like us to know about your order.
