# Mulch Sale Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mulch Sale Order Form is designed to collect customer information and mulch order details for mulch sales. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure a smooth and efficient ordering process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Customer Details** (Page 1)
	* Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
2. **Delivery Schedule** (Page 2)
	* Specify the date and time you would like to receive your mulch delivery.
3. **Mulch Order** (Page 3)
	* Enter the type and quantity of mulch you would like to order.
4. **Customer Address** (Page 4)
	* Enter your address where the mulch will be delivered or picked up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details** (Text): Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Delivery Schedule** (Text): Specify the date and time you would like to receive your mulch delivery.
* **Mulch Order** (Text): Enter the type and quantity of mulch you would like to order (e.g., "3 cubic yards of organic mulch").
* **Customer Address** (Text): Enter the address where the mulch will be delivered or picked up.
