# Fluffy Cakes Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fluffy Cakes Order Form is used to gather customer details for ordering purposes. This form is intended for both internal and external use cases.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Fluffy Cakes Order Form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Customer Name** (page 1) - Enter your name as the customer who will be receiving the order.
2. Enter your **Phone Number** (page 1) - Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
3. Enter your **Email Address** (page 1) - Enter your email address to receive updates and confirmation of your order.
4. Enter your **Delivery Address** (page 2) - Enter the address where you would like the order to be delivered.
5. Choose the **Order Date** (page 2) - Select the date for when you would like to receive the order.
6. Choose the **Order Time** (page 2) - Select the time of day for when you would like to receive the order.
7. Enter any **Description** (page 3) - Provide any additional comments or details about your order if needed.
8. Enter any **Order Notes** (page 3) - Enter any special requests or comments about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the customer who will be receiving the order.
* **Customer Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive updates and confirmation of your order.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, optional): Enter the address where you would like the order to be delivered.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Select the date for when you would like to receive the order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Select the time of day for when you would like to receive the order.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or details about your order if needed.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, optional): Enter any special requests or comments about your order.

## Tips
Please make sure to provide accurate information to ensure smooth processing of your order. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us!
