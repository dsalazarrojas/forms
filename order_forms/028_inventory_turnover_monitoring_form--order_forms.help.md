# Inventory Turnover Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inventory Turnover Monitoring Form is a specialized form used to track and monitor inventory levels and turnover. It is designed to collect specific data related to inventory metrics such as quantity, cost, and selling price.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category for the form (Order Forms)
2. Enter the period start date for the inventory monitoring
3. Enter the period end date for the inventory monitoring
4. Enter the item ID
5. Enter the item name
6. Enter the quantity of the item
7. Enter the cost of the item
8. Enter the selling price of the item
9. Enter the reorder level for the item
10. Enter the reorder quantity for the item
11. (Optional) Enter the average cost and average selling price if applicable
12. Enter the inventory turnover for the item
13. Enter the reorder frequency for the item (if applicable)
14. Enter the inventory turnover rate for the item (if applicable)
15. Enter the item status
16. Add any additional notes about the item
17. Enter your email address
18. Enter your phone number
19. Enter the description of the form
20. Enter a long description of the form
21. Enter the name of the person submitting the form
22. Enter the tool assigned to the form
23. Enter the user assigned to the form

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Category**: This field allows you to select the type of form this is (Order Forms) or other types of forms.
  - Type: select_one
  - Purpose: Select the category of the form
- **Period Start Date**: Enter the start date of the period being monitored.
  - Type: date
  - Purpose: Enter the start date of the inventory monitoring period
- **Period End Date**: Enter the end date of the period being monitored.
  - Type: date
  - Purpose: Enter the end date of the inventory monitoring period
- **Item ID**: Enter the unique ID of the item being monitored.
  - Type: text
  - Purpose: Enter the item ID
- **Item Name**: Enter the name of the item being monitored.
  - Type: text
  - Purpose: Enter the item name
- **Quantity**: Enter the current quantity of the item.
  - Type: number
  - Purpose: Enter the current quantity of the item
- **Cost**: Enter the cost of the item.

  - Purpose: Enter the cost of the item
- **Selling Price**: Enter the selling price of the item.
  - Type: number
  - Purpose: Enter the selling price of the item
- **Reorder Level**: Enter the level at which the item will be reordered.
  - Type: text
  - Purpose: Enter the reorder level
- **Reorder Quantity**: Enter the quantity at which the item will be reordered.
  - Type: text
  - Purpose: Enter the reorder quantity
- **Average Cost**: (Optional) Enter the average cost of the item.
  - Type: number
  - Purpose: (Optional) Enter the average cost of the item
- **Average Selling Price**: (Optional) Enter the average selling price of the item.
  - Type: number
  - Purpose: (Optional) Enter the average selling price of the item
