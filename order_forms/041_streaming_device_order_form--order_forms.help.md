# Streaming Device Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Streaming Device Order Form is used to collect information for individual or small-scale orders of streaming devices. This form is not intended for high-volume orders or commercial use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in the order you prefer, as the fields are not meant to be filled out in any specific order.
2. Begin by entering the **device** field to identify the type of streaming device you are interested in ordering.
3. Next, enter the **model** of the streaming device in the **model** field.
4. Enter the **price** of the streaming device in the **price** field. If you are unsure of the price, you can leave it blank and our team will contact you to confirm.
5. Enter the **quantity** of the streaming device you would like to order in the **quantity** field.
6. Optionally, add any additional **notes** you would like to include with your order in the **notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **device**: Enter the type of streaming device you are interested in ordering.
* **model**: Enter the model of the streaming device (e.g., model number or description).
* **price**: Enter the price of the streaming device.
* **quantity**: Enter the number of streaming devices you would like to order.
* **notes**: Add any additional information you would like to include with your order (e.g., special requests, delivery instructions).
