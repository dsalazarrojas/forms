# flower_seeds_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a customer's order for flowers and seeds.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **customer name** in the "customer name" field. This is a required field and should be filled in carefully.
2. Enter your **email address** in the "email" field. This is also a required field and should be checked for accuracy.
3. Fill in your **phone number** in the "phone" field. This is a required field and should be correct.
4. Select the **seed type** from the options provided. This is a required field and can be either "Petunia", "Viola", or "Daffodil".
5. Enter the **quantity** of seeds you are ordering. This is a required field and should be a number.
6. Fill in your **shipping address** in the "shipping address" field. This is a required field and should be correct.
7. Choose a **payment method** from the options provided. This is a required field and can be either "Credit Card" or "PayPal".
8. (Optional) Enter any additional **note** you may have. This field is not required.
9. (Optional) Specify the **flower type** and **seed color** of the seeds you are ordering. These fields are not required.
10. (Optional) Specify the **flower size** of the seeds you are ordering. This field is not required.
11. (Optional) Enter the **seed count** of seeds you are ordering. This is not required.
12. Choose the **delivery method** for your order. This field is not required.
13. (Optional) Enter the **seed price** of the seeds you are ordering. This field is not required.
14. The **total price** of your order will be automatically calculated and displayed.
15. Select the **payment status** of your order from the options provided. This is a required field and can be "Paid", "Partially paid", or "Unpaid".
16. Choose the **status** of your order from the options provided. This is a required field and can be either "Active" or "Inactive".
17. (Optional) Enter the **seed weight** of the seeds you are ordering. This field is not required.
18. (Optional) Enter the **seed length** of the seeds you are ordering. This field is not required.
19. (Optional) Enter the **seed count** of seeds you are ordering again. This is not required.
20. Enter the **delivery date** for your order. This field is not required.
21. Enter the **delivery time** for your order. This field is not required.
22. Enter your **delivery address** for your order. This field is not required.
23. (Optional) Choose whether or not to use **delivery method** for your order. This field is not required.
