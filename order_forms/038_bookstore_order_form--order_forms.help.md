# Bookstore Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information for customers to place an order for books with the bookstore. The form collects customer contact and shipping details, book title and format information, order quantity and unit price, and payment method. Please note that this form is intended for new orders only.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full Name** (`customer_name`), **Email Address** (`customer_email`), and **Phone Number** (`customer_phone`) details.
2. Provide the **Book Title** (`book_title`), **Author Name** (`book_author`), and **ISBN Number** (`isbn`) of the book you wish to order.
3. Select the desired **Book Format** (`format`) from the options provided.
4. Enter the **Quantity** (`quantity`) of books you would like to order.
5. Specify the **Unit Price** (`unit_price`) for each book.
6. Fill in your **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`) for the delivery of the books.
7. Choose the preferred **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`) for the order.
8. Select the **Payment Method** (`payment_method`) for the order.
9. If applicable, indicate if a **Bulk Order Discount** (`bulk_discount`) is required.
10. Optionally, provide any **Special Requests** (`special_requests`) for the order.
11. Calculate the total **Order Total** (`order_total`) by multiplying the **Unit Price** (`unit_price`) by the **Quantity** (`quantity`).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you would like to receive order confirmation.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your best contact number for any order updates.
* **Book Title**: Enter the title of the book you wish to order.
* **Author Name**: Enter the name(s) of the book's author(s).
* **ISBN Number**: Enter the 13-digit ISBN number if available.
* **Book Format**: Select the desired format of the book (Hardcover, Paperback, E-book, or Audiobook).
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of copies you would like to order.
* **Unit Price**: Enter the price per book.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your full delivery address for the books.
* **Shipping Method**: Choose the preferred shipping method (Standard, Expedited, Overnight, or Local pickup).
* **Payment Method**: Select the preferred payment method (Credit card, Debit card, PayPal, Bank transfer, or Check).
* **Bulk Order Discount**: Indicate if bulk discounts are applicable to your order.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any special handling or notes for the order.
* **Order Total**: Calculate the total amount by multiplying the **Unit Price** by the **Quantity**.

Note: This form requires fields marked as `required: true` to be filled in to submit the order.
