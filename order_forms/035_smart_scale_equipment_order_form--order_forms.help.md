# Smart Scale Equipment Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a smart scale equipment order. Please fill out the form to ensure your order is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct payment method for your order.
2. Enter the total payment amount for your order.
3. Fill out the Form Information field with any relevant details about your order.
4. Enter your customer name.
5. Enter your email address.
6. Enter your phone number.
7. Enter your shipping address.
8. Enter your shipping city.
9. Enter your shipping state.
10. Enter your shipping zip code.
11. Specify the quantity of smart scale equipment to be ordered.
12. Complete any additional shipping details fields, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Information (page 1)**: Provide a brief description of your order or any specific instructions.
* **Customer Name (page 1)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email (page 1)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (page 1)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Shipping Address (page 1)**: Enter your shipping address.
* **Shipping City (page 1)**: Enter your shipping city.
* **Shipping State (page 1)**: Enter your shipping state.
* **Shipping Zip (page 1)**: Enter your shipping zip code.
* **Quantity (page 1)**: Specify the number of smart scale equipment units to be ordered.
* **Payment Method (page 1)**: Select the payment method for your order (e.g., credit card, PayPal, bank transfer).
* **Payment Amount (page 1)**: Enter the total payment amount for your order.
* **Order Comments (page 1)**: Provide any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Shipping Details**:
	+ **Shipping Details 1 (page 2)**: Enter any relevant shipping details, if necessary.
	+ **Shipping Details 2 (page 3)**: Enter any additional shipping details, if necessary.
	+ **Shipping Details 3 (page 4)**: Enter any more shipping details, if necessary.
	+ ... **Shipping Details 10 (page 12)**: Enter any additional shipping details, if necessary.
	+ **Shipping Details 11 (page 13)**: Enter any more shipping details, if necessary.
	+ **Shipping Details 12 (page 14)**: Enter any additional shipping details, if necessary. 

Note: Please only fill out the shipping details fields if necessary, as they are not required fields.
