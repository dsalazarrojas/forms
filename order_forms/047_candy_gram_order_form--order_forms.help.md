# Candy Gram Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to order a candy gram for a student. Please fill out the form completely to ensure accurate and efficient processing of your order.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the sender information section, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the recipient's name and grade or department.
3. Choose the type and quantity of candy you want to send.
4. Select the delivery location and occasion theme.
5. Decide if you want to be anonymous or not.
6. Choose a payment method and preferred delivery date.
7. Confirm that you agree to the school's fundraising terms.
8. Finally, review and submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sender Name**: Your name as the sender of the candy gram.
* **Sender Email**: The email address where you can be reached for confirmation or questions.
* **Sender Phone**: Your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Sender Grade or Department**: The grade or department of the recipient.
* **Recipient Name**: The name of the student you are sending the candy gram to.
* **Recipient Grade or Homeroom**: The grade or homeroom of the recipient to assist with delivery.
* **Delivery Location**: The preferred drop-off point for the candy gram.
* **Candy Gram Type**: The type of candy you want to send (e.g. Lollipop Heart, Chocolate Kiss, etc.).
* **Quantity**: The number of grams you want to send.
* **Personalized Message**: A message to go along with the candy gram (optional, 150 characters max).
* **Occasion or Theme**: The reason for sending the candy gram (e.g. Valentine's Day, Halloween, etc.).
* **Anonymous Sender**: Whether or not you want the recipient to know you sent the candy gram.
* **Payment Method**: The method you will use to pay for the candy gram (e.g. Cash, Venmo, etc.).
* **Preferred Delivery Date**: The date you prefer for delivery.
* **School Fundraiser Terms Agreement**: Confirmation that you agree to the school's fundraising terms.
* **Order Date**: The date you are ordering the candy gram.
