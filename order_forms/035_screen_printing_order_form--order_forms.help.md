# Screen Printing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers for screen printing orders. It will be used to collect necessary details from customers, including their name, email, phone number, shipping address, any order comments, payment method, order status, and customer notes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information.
2. Make sure to select the correct payment method.
3. Choose the correct order status.
4. Add any additional comments or notes that may be helpful for the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Please enter the customer's full name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter the customer's email address to ensure we can contact them about their order.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number if you have it.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter the customer's shipping address to ensure accurate delivery.
* **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select whether the customer will pay online or offline.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the order (Active or Inactive).
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the customer that may be helpful for the order.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact our team for assistance.
* This form will be used to communicate with the customer and finalize the order, so please fill it out thoroughly and accurately.
