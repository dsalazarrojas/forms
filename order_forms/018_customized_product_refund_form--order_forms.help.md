# Customized Product Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a refund for a customized product. Please fill out the form below with the required and optional information to help us process your refund request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to provide all required information, even if you're unsure about any field.
3. Use the selection menus to choose the correct options for your refund reason and phone country code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer-name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **order-id** (number, optional): If you know your order ID, please enter it to help us locate your order quickly.
* **reason-for-return** (select one, optional): Choose the reason for returning the product. The options include:
	+ Order not received
	+ Defective product
	+ Different product received
	+ Refund policy not applied
* **order-date** (date, optional): If you know the date of your order, please enter it.
* **shipping-date** (date, optional): If you know the date of your shipment, please enter it.
* **product-name** (text, optional): Describe the product you're returning.
* **product-description** (text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the product you're returning.
* **customer-message** (text, optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please enter them here.
* **customer-email** (email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your refund.
* **customer-phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number, including your country code (e.g., +1 for the USA).
* **order-value** (number, optional): If you know the value of your order, please enter it.
* **shipping-address** (text, optional): Enter your shipping address to help us locate your order.
* **customer-country** (select one, optional): Choose your country from the list.
	+ USA
	+ Canada
	+ Mexico
* **customer-state** (select one, optional): Choose your state from the list.
	+ California
	+ New York
	+ Texas
* **customer-city** (text, optional): Enter your city or town.
* **customer-zip** (number, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **customer-phone-country-code** (select one, optional): Choose your country code from the list.
	+ +1
	+ Other

Please fill out the form accurately and completely, and we'll be happy to process your refund request.
