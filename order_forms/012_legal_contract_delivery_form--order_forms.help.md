# legal_contract_delivery_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the details of a legal contract, including contract information, delivery details, and assignment status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required and optional fields as necessary.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and relevant information for each field.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contract Details** (`contract_details`, text, optional):
  Enter a brief description of the contract.
* **Contract Type** (`contract_type`, select_one, optional):
  Select whether the contract is active or inactive.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, optional):
  Enter the address where the contract will be delivered.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional):
  Enter the date when the contract will be delivered.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, optional):
  Enter the time when the contract will be delivered.
* **Delivery Notes** (`delivery_notes`, note, optional):
  Add any additional notes about the delivery.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional):
  Select whether the contract is assigned to someone or not.
* **Delivery Status** (`delivery_status`, select_one, optional):
  Select the status of the delivery (Active or Inactive).
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select_one, optional):
  Select whether the delivery method is available or not.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional):
  Select whether a tool is assigned to the contract or not.
* **Delivery Location** (`delivery_location`, select_multiple, optional):
  Select multiple locations for the delivery.
* **Contract Document** (`contract_document`, text, optional):
  Upload or enter the contract document.

## Tips
* Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult the relevant personnel or resources.
* This form is a general-purpose template for recording contract details; you can adjust it according to your needs.
