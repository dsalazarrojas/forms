# School T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School T Shirt Order Form is designed to collect order details from customers, including order number, order details, T-shirt size, T-shirt color, customer name, email, and payment details (amount and method).

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Enter a valid order number.
3. Provide a detailed description of the order.
4. Select your preferred T-shirt size and color.
5. Enter your name and email address.
6. Enter the payment amount.
7. Choose your preferred payment method (optional).
8. Indicate whether you want to sync the order with your account.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Number** (`order_number`, number, required): Enter a valid order number to associate with this order.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the order.
* **T-Shirt Size** (`tshirt_size`, select_one, required): Select your preferred T-shirt size.
* **T-Shirt Color** (`tshirt_color`, select_one, required): Select your preferred T-shirt color.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required): Enter the payment amount.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred payment method.
* **Sync to Account** (`sync_to_account`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you want to sync this order with your account.

## Tips
* Double-check the order number to ensure it is valid.
* Provide detailed order details to help the customer service team process your order efficiently.
* Make sure to choose the correct T-shirt size and color to avoid any mistakes.
* Enter a valid email address to ensure you receive updates on your order status.
* Enter the correct payment amount to avoid any payment issues.
* If you choose to sync the order with your account, ensure your account settings are up-to-date.
