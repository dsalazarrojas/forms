# Personal Training Package Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to order a personal training package. To complete this form, follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the package type that suits your needs from the "Package Type" dropdown.
2. Choose your preferred package duration from the "Package Duration" dropdown.
3. Select your payment plan from the "Payment Plan" dropdown.
4. Provide your name, email, and phone number in the "Client Details", "Email", and "Phone" fields, respectively.
5. Enter your desired training time in the "Time" field.
6. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown.
7. Add any additional order notes in the "Order Note" field.
8. Finally, click "Submit" to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Package Type** (`Package Type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of package you are interested in. This can be Personal, Group, or Semi-Private.
* **Package Duration** (`Package Duration`, select_one, required: false): Choose the duration of your package. Options are 1 Month, 3 Months, or 6 Months.
* **Payment Plan** (`Payment Plan`, select_one, required: false): Select your preferred payment plan. Options are Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually.
* **Client Details** (`Client Details`, text, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Trainer Details** (`Trainer Details`, text, required: false): Enter any additional trainer details (e.g. trainer name, experience).
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select_one, required: false): Select your preferred payment method. Options are Bank Transfer, Credit/Debit Card, or PayPal.
* **Order Note** (`Order Note`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about your order.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required: false): Enter your desired training date.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, required: false): Enter your desired training time.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, text, required: false): Click to complete your order.
