# Tickets Purchase Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tickets Purchase Form is designed to collect information from customers about their ticket purchase preferences. This form is not for actual payment processing, but rather to gather details about the tickets the customer wishes to purchase.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select your ticket preference:
	* Choose between "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you want to purchase tickets.
2. Select the type of ticket you wish to purchase:
	* Choose between "Yes" or "No" to specify the type of ticket you are interested in purchasing.
3. Enter the event details:
	* Provide the name of the event you wish to attend.
4. Enter the price of the ticket:
	* Input the price of the ticket you are interested in purchasing.
5. Specify the quantity of tickets:
	* Enter the number of tickets you wish to purchase.
6. Provide a donation (optional):
	* This is an optional field to allow customers to make an additional donation.
7. Enter your contact information:
	* Provide your email address.
8. Enter your phone number:
	* Input your phone number for contact purposes.
9. Add any additional notes (optional):
	* This is an optional field to provide any additional information about your ticket purchase.
10. Specify the total cost (optional):
	* Input the total cost of the tickets you are purchasing.
11. Select a payment method (optional):
	* Choose between "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you will be using a payment method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **order_form**: This field is used to select whether you want to purchase tickets. 
* **ticket_type**: This field is used to specify the type of ticket you are interested in purchasing.
* **event**: Enter the name of the event you wish to attend.
* **price**: Input the price of the ticket you are interested in purchasing.
* **quantity**: Enter the number of tickets you wish to purchase.
* **donation**: This is an optional field to allow customers to make an additional donation.
* **email**: Provide your email address for contact purposes.
* **phone**: Input your phone number for contact purposes.
* **note**: This is an optional field to provide any additional information about your ticket purchase.
* **total**: Input the total cost of the tickets you are purchasing.
* **payment_method**: This field is used to specify if you will be using a payment method. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate submission of your ticket purchase preferences.
* Optional fields are not required, but they can provide valuable information to help us process your ticket purchase.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us at your convenience.
