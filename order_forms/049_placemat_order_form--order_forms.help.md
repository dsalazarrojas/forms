# placemat_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The placemat_order_form is a form designed to collect information for placing an order for a custom-made placemat. It is intended for customers or users who want to place an order for a placemat.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your First Name in the First Name field.
* Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
* Provide your Email address in the Email field.
* Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
* Choose the Placemat Type you are interested in.
* Specify the Quantity of placemats you would like to order.
* Select the Color of the placemat you desire.
* If desired, choose a Size for the placemat. Note that this field is not required.
* Optionally, include a Message for the order if you have any specific requirements or instructions.
* Choose the Date for the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you regarding your order.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number as it appears on your phone or identification.
* **Placemat Type** (select_one, required): Choose the type of placemat you are interested in. This field has three options: Placemat 1, Placemat 2, and Placemat 3.
* **Quantity** (number, required): Enter the number of placemats you would like to order.
* **Color** (select_multiple, required): Select one or more colors for the placemat. This field has three options: Red, Blue, and Green.
* **Size** (select_one, optional): Choose a size for the placemat. This field has three options: Small, Medium, and Large. Note that this field is not required.
* **Message** (text, optional): If you have any specific requirements or instructions for your order, include them in this field.
* **Order Date** (date, required): Choose the Date for the order.
