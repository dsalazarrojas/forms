# Shelf Talker Frame Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect order details from customers, including size and quantity specifications, and design preferences, to facilitate the processing of their custom Shelf Talker Frame orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information about your order.
2. Provide the necessary details for the size and quantity of the shelf frame.
3. Specify any design preferences for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Form**: This is the main page of the form where you can enter the details of your order.
	+ **Size Details**: Enter the specific size requirements for your shelf frame, including measurements or specifications.
	+ **Quantity Details**: Specify the number of shelves or frames you wish to order.
	+ **Design Details**: Describe any special design preferences or requirements for your order.
	+ **Submit Form**: Once you have filled in all the required fields, click this button to submit your order.
