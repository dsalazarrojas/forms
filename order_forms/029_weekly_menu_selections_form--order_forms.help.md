# Weekly Menu Selections Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect users' menu selection preferences for the upcoming week. It helps users to easily select their desired options from a list of available restaurants and menu items.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have filled out this form accurately and thoroughly.
* Select the restaurant(s) you would like to make selections from.
* Enter the date you ordered the menu items.
* Choose the time you ordered the menu items.
* Enter a brief description of your order details.
* Enter your contact number.
* Enter your email address.
* Select your desired menu items for the week from the provided options.
* Add any additional comments you would like to include with your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Restaurant Selections**: Select the restaurant(s) you would like to make selections from. This will allow us to know which restaurant's menu you are interested in.
* **Date Ordered**: Enter the date you ordered the menu items. This helps us to track your order history and ensure that we do not duplicate orders.
* **Time Ordered**: Choose the time you ordered the menu items. This ensures that we can schedule your order accordingly.
* **Order Details**: Enter a brief description of your order details. This helps us to understand your preferences and ensure that we can fulfill your order correctly.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number so we can reach you with any updates or queries regarding your order.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with any further questions or updates.
* **Weekly Menu Selections**: Select your desired menu items for the week from the provided options. This will allow us to know your exact preferences and ensure that we can provide you with the correct menu items.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or information you would like to include with your order. This helps us to tailor your order to your specific needs.
