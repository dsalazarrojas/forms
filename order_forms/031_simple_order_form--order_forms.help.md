# Simple Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Simple Order Form is designed for customers to provide their order information and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Customer Info section with your details.
2. Select the items you would like to order in the Select Items section.
3. Choose your preferred payment method in the Payment Method section.
4. If applicable, select your delivery method in the Delivery Method section.
5. Rate our shop in the Rate Our Shop section.
6. Finally, provide any additional feedback in the Feedback section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info**: This section is where you enter your name, address, and contact information. It is a required field to ensure we can contact you.
* **Select Items**: In this section, choose the items you would like to order from the list provided. You can select multiple items by checking each box next to the item name.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you would like to pay for your order. You can select from Bank Transfer or Meetups.
* **Delivery Method**: If you have selected items to be delivered, choose how you would like them delivered. You can select from Local Mail or Known Couriers.
* **Rate Our Shop**: Rate our shop as Excellent, Good, or Fair to help us improve our services.
* **Feedback**: Provide any additional feedback about your experience with us in this section.
