# Rental Deposit Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rental Deposit Refund Form is used for tenants to request a refund for their rental deposit. This form is to be filled out by tenants to provide the necessary information for the refund process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill out the form completely and accurately with the tenant's information.
- Enter the deposit amount and reasons for the refund.
- Select the date of when the refund was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Information** (`tenant_info`, text, optional): Enter the tenant's name, address, and any other relevant information.
* **Deposit Amount** (`deposit_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the deposit being refunded.
* **Refund Reason** (`refund_reason`, text, optional): Briefly describe the reason for the refund request.
* **Refund Date** (`refund_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the refund should be processed.
* **Tenant Signature** (`tenant_signature`, note, optional): The tenant's digital or physical signature, if applicable.
* **Landlord Information** (`landlord_info`, text, optional): Enter the landlord's name, address, and contact information.
* **Landlord Signature** (`landlord_signature`, note, optional): The landlord's digital or physical signature, if applicable.
* **Form Submitted Date** (`form_submitted_date`, date, optional): Automatically populated with the date the form was submitted.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth refund process.
- If you are unable to submit the form electronically, please contact your landlord for assistance.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your landlord or the form administrator for support.
