# organic_product_delivery_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for the delivery of organic products. Please provide the required and optional details as instructed below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the delivery address.
2. Enter the delivery zip code.
3. Select the type of product to be delivered (Organic or Non-Organic).
4. Enter the quantity of the product to be delivered.
5. Provide the delivery date.
6. Select the delivery time.
7. Enter your phone number.
8. Enter your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Address (delivery_address)**: Enter the address where you would like the organic product delivered to.
* **Delivery Zip (delivery_zip)**: Enter the zip code of the delivery address.
* **Product Type (product_type)**: Select whether the product is Organic or Non-Organic.
* **Quantity (quantity)**: Enter the quantity of the product to be delivered.
* **Delivery Date (delivery_date)**: Enter the date of the delivery. Please use the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Delivery Time (delivery_time)**: Select the time of the delivery.
* **Phone Number (phone_number)**: Enter your phone number for any follow-up or delivery confirmation purposes.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address for any follow-up or delivery confirmation purposes.

## Tips

- Double-check the accuracy of the delivery address and zip code.
- Ensure that you select the correct product type and quantity.
- If you have any special delivery instructions, please contact the delivery team.
