# Tidy Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tidy Order Form is used to collect customer information, order details, and payment methods for a seamless ordering experience. This form is designed to capture the essential data required for processing customer orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Tidy Order Form, follow these steps:

1. **Customer Information**: Enter your name and email address accurately.
2. **Order Details**: Provide your shipping address, city, state, and postal code for delivery purposes.
3. **Order Date and Time**: Enter the date and time you wish to place your order.
4. **Order Total**: Enter the total amount of your order.
5. **Payment Method**: Choose from available payment options (Cash, PayPal, Credit Card).
6. **Order Status**: Select the status of your order (Pending, Approved, Cancelled).
7. **Customer Note**: Add any additional comments or notes for the customer.
8. **Assigned Tools**: If applicable, select the assigned tools for your order.
9. **Output File**: Provide any additional file outputs required for your order.
10. **Form ID**: Enter a unique identifier for your form (if applicable).
11. **Category**: Select the category for your order (if applicable).
12. **Description**: Enter a brief description of your order (if applicable).
