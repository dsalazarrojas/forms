# Library Expansion Order Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request expansion of a library, providing the necessary details to process your request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are an authorized person from your branch, and review your branch's needs carefully to avoid duplication.
2. Fill out all required fields (*marked with an asterisk*) and provide as much detail as possible for other fields.
3. Review the form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **branch** (*required*): This field is your branch's name or location code.
* **budget** (number, not required): This is your estimated budget for the library expansion project. Please note that this field is optional; if you don't provide a value, we will contact you separately for budget discussion.
* **estimated_delivery_date** (date, not required): Provide your estimated delivery date for the library expansion project. This will help us plan accordingly.
* **delivery_details** (text, not required): Please describe any specific delivery details, such as the number of items, quantities, or conditions.
* **library_material_id** (*required*): This is a unique library material ID number. Please ensure you provide the correct number.
* **quantity** (number, not required): If applicable, please specify the quantity of items you need for the library expansion project.
* **branch_manager_signature** (text, not required): This field is for branch manager's signature. Please ensure you sign with your official signature.
* **branch_manager_name** (text, not required): This field is for branch manager's name. It's for your records, not required for processing the request.

## Tips

* Ensure you submit all necessary information, especially *required* fields.
* Be as detailed as possible in other fields to help us process your request more efficiently.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, contact us separately.
* Once submitted, we will review and contact you for any further information or discussion.
* Please allow us time to review and process your request.
