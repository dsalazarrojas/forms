# Pizza Toppings Selection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pizza Toppings Selection Form is designed to collect order information from customers. It is a multi-page form with several sections to ensure all necessary details are captured accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  On the first page, provide your customer information by filling in the text fields.
2.  Move to the next page to enter your order details.
3.  On the third page, provide any special requests you may have for your order.
4.  Select your desired toppings on the fourth page.
5.  Review and confirm your order details on the last page before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, optional): 
  Enter your name and other relevant customer information.

- **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): 
  Provide any specific information about your order, such as the type of pizza and size.

- **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, note, required): 
  If you have any special requests for your order, type them in the text field provided.

- **Toppings Selection** (`toppings_selection`, text, optional): 
  Choose your desired pizza toppings from the available options and type in the corresponding numbers or codes.

- **Order Summary** (`order_summary`, text, optional): 
  Review and confirm the order details entered above.

- **Confirm Order** (`confirm_order`, text, optional): 
  Review and confirm your order before submission to ensure accuracy.

## Tips
* Review each section carefully before proceeding to the next one.
* If you have any questions or issues, contact your local pizza establishment for assistance.
