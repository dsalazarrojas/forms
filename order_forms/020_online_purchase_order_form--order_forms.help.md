# online_purchase_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect order information from customers for online purchases. Fill out this form to provide your details and order specifics for a smooth and efficient processing process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the customer information section, which includes details such as your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
2. Proceed to the order details section and provide the specifics about your purchase, including any relevant notes.
3. In the shipping address section, enter your shipping address to ensure accurate delivery.
4. Select your preferred payment method (yes or no) and provide any additional comments or notes in the customer notes section.
5. If applicable, enter your shipping cost and tax rate to ensure accurate order totals.
6. Finally, double-check your order total to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info (customer_info)**: Enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Order Details (order_details)**: Provide specifics about your purchase, such as any notes or comments.
* **Shipping Address (shipping_address)**: Enter your shipping address to ensure accurate delivery.
* **Payment Method (payment_method)**: Select your preferred payment method (yes or no).
* **Shipping Cost (shipping_cost)**: If applicable, enter your shipping cost.
* **Tax Rate (tax_rate)**: If applicable, enter your tax rate.
* **Order Total (order_total)**: Double-check your order total to ensure accuracy.
* **Customer Notes 1 (customer_notes)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Customer Notes 2 (customer_notes_2)**: Enter any further comments or notes about your order.
