# Seasonal Gift Wrapping Set Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for customers to place orders for gift-wrapped sets. It collects information about the order, including gift sets, size, style, quantity, customer contact information, and shipping address.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the gift wrapping set(s) you would like to order.
2. Choose the size and style of the gift wrapping set.
3. Enter the quantity of gift wrapping sets you would like to order.
4. Enter your name and email address.
5. Enter your phone number and shipping address.
6. If necessary, provide any special instructions.
7. Review your order carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your order, e.g., "Birthday gift for my sister".
* **Gift Wrapping Sets** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more gift sets from the provided options, e.g., "Set 1" or "Set 1 and Set 2".
* **Wrapping Size** (`3`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the size of the gift wrapping set, e.g., "Small", "Medium", or "Large".
* **Wrapping Style** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more wrapping style options, e.g., "Solid", "Faux Fur", "Glitter", or "Metallic".
* **Quantity** (`5`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of gift wrapping sets you would like to order.
* **Customer Name** (`6`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your driver's license or ID.
* **Customer Email** (`7`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`8`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Shipping Address** (`9`, `text`, required: false): Enter your shipping address.
* **Order Date** (`10`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you would like to receive your order by.
* **Special Instructions** (`11`, `text`, required: false): Provide any special instructions for your order, e.g., "Please make sure to use eco-friendly materials."
* **Comments** (`12`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
