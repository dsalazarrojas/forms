## Gym Social Media Backdrop Order Form - Help Guide
### Purpose
The Gym Social Media Backdrop Order Form is a tool used to create and manage social media backdrop orders. It is intended to be filled out by gym staff or management to submit details about their orders, including backdrop type, image, and payment information.

### How To Complete This Form
Before filling out the form, please ensure you have all necessary information ready. This form should only be submitted once for each order. If you need to make changes or modifications to an existing order, please contact our support team.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Backdrop Type** (`id: 1`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select the type of backdrop you need from the available options: Backdrop, Logo, Both, or Other. This will help us understand the context of your order.
* **Backdrop Image** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the image you need for your backdrop. This can include details such as size, resolution, or any specific requirements you have.
* **Backdrop Image Alt** (`id: 3`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the alt text for your backdrop image, which will help visually impaired users understand the image content.
* **Logo** (`id: 4`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the logo image you need. Include details such as size, resolution, or any specific requirements you have.
* **Logo Image Alt** (`id: 5`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the alt text for your logo image, which will help visually impaired users understand the image content.
* **Payment Method** (`id: 6`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the payment method you wish to use for your order. Select one or more of the following options: Credit Card, PayPal, or Other.
* **Quantity** (`id: 7`, `type: number`, required: false): Enter the number of backdrops or logos you need.
* **Unit Price** (`id: 8`, `type: number`, required: false): Enter the price per unit of the order.
* **Shipping Cost** (`id: 9`, `type: number`, required: false): Enter any additional shipping costs, if applicable.
* **Order Notes** (`id: 10`, `type: note`, required: false): Enter any additional information or notes about your order.
* **Order Date** (`id: 11`, `type: date`, required: false): Enter the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Order Time** (`id: 12`, `type: time`, required: false): Enter the time you would like to receive your order.
* **Gym Name** (`id: 13`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your gym's name, which will be displayed on the order receipt.
* **Trainer Name** (`id: 14`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the name of the trainer or staff member submitting the order.
* **Email** (`id: 15`, `type: email`, required: false): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication about the order.
* **Phone** (`id: 16`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your phone number, which will be used for communication about the order.
* **Other Info** (`id: 17`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter any additional information you would like to provide about your order.

### Tips
Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly to avoid any delays in processing your order. If you have any questions or need assistance with submitting the form, contact our support team.
