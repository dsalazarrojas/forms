# health_supplement_launch_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers to pre-order a new health supplement product. It will help us get the customer's consent for shipping, payment method, and other details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "New Product Name" field with the name of the product you would like to pre-order.
2. Enter your email address in the "Customer Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
4. Fill in the "Shipping Address" field with your shipping address.
5. Enter the name of the city where you would like to ship the product in the "Shipping City" field.
6. Select your shipping state from the options in the "Shipping State" field.
7. Enter the zip code for your shipping address in the "Shipping Zip" field.
8. Select the country where you would like to ship the product from the options in the "Shipping Country" field.
9. Select the date you would like to receive the order from the calendar in the "Order Date" field.
10. Choose your preferred payment method from the options in the "Payment Method" field.
11. Select the type of payment card you would like to use from the options in the "Payment Card Type" field.
12. Check the boxes for any customer details you would like us to know, such as your phone number and shipping address, in the "Customer Details" field.
13. Enter any additional notes about your order in the "Order Notes" field.
14. Finally, fill in the "Customer Name" field with your name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Product Name**: A field to enter the name of the new health supplement product you would like to pre-order.
* **Customer Email**: A field to enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone**: A field to enter your phone number.
* **Shipping Address**: A field to enter your shipping address.
* **Shipping City**: A field to enter the name of the city where you would like to ship the product.
* **Shipping State**: A field to select the state where you would like to ship the product (Yes/No options).
* **Shipping Zip**: A field to enter the zip code for your shipping address.
* **Shipping Country**: A field to select the country where you would like to ship the product (Yes/No options).
* **Order Date**: A field to select the date you would like to receive the order from the calendar.
* **Payment Method**: A field to choose your preferred payment method (Yes/No options).
* **Payment Card Type**: A field to select the type of payment card you would like to use (Yes/No options).
* **Customer Details**: A field to check boxes for any additional customer details you would like us to know (Yes/No options).
* **Order Notes**: A field to enter any additional notes about your order.
* **Customer Name**: A field to enter your name.
