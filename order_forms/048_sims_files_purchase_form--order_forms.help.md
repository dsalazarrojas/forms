# Sims Files Purchase Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sims Files Purchase Form is a form designed to gather information from customers about their purchase of Sims files. This form is used to record and confirm purchase orders, ensuring that all relevant details are captured accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Email
	* Quantity
	* Delivery
	* Payment Method
	* Purchase Confirm
	* Purchase Date
	* Purchase Reason
	* Purchase Status
	* Form Submitter
	* Delivery Status

2. You may also enter additional information in the optional fields, such as:
	* Customer Information (Customer Info)
	* Contact Info
	* Address
	* Purchase Info
	* Order Notes
	* Delivery Confirm
	* Form Created
