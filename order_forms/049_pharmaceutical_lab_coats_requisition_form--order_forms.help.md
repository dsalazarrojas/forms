# Pharmaceutical Lab Coats Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request lab coats for various purposes in the pharmaceutical lab. It is intended for employees who need lab coats for their daily work. The form is used to track and manage lab coat requests, ensuring that the lab has the necessary coats for its operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of lab coat you need from the options provided.
2. Choose the size and color of the lab coat that suits your requirements.
3. Enter the quantity of lab coats you are requesting.
4. Select the date and time when you need the lab coat.
5. Specify the department and team that you are working with.
6. Indicate if the team leader is aware of the request.
7. Choose the approver for the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lab Coat Type**: Select the type of lab coat you need from the options provided. You can choose between Short Sleeve and Long Sleeve lab coats.
* **Coat Size**: Choose the size of the lab coat that suits your needs. You can select from small, medium, large, and extra large sizes.
* **Coat Color**: Select the color of the lab coat you want. You can choose from white, black, and blue lab coats.
* **Lab Coat Quantity**: Enter the number of lab coats you are requesting. This will help us track the number of coats we need to provide.
* **Date Requested**: Enter the date when you need the lab coats. This will help us schedule the delivery accordingly.
* **Time Requested**: Enter the time when you need the lab coats. This will help us ensure that we deliver the coats on time.
* **Department**: Specify the department you are working with. This will help us track the requests for each department.
* **Team Leader**: Indicate if the team leader is aware of the request. This will help us keep them informed and ensure that they are aware of the lab coat needs.
* **Approver**: Choose the approver for the request. This will help us track who has reviewed and approved the request.
* **Note**: This is a general notes section where you can provide any additional information about your lab coat request.
