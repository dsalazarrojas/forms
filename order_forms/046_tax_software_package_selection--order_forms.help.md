# Tax Software Package Selection - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you select the best tax software package for your business or clients. You will be asked to provide information about your needs and preferences, and the form will help you choose a package that suits your requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your tax profession from the options provided.
2. Choose your professional experience level.
3. Select the features and benefits that you require in a tax software package.
4. Select your preferred software seller.
5. Enter your confidence level in using tax software.
6. Enter the number of hours you expect to spend on tax tasks per month.
7. Choose your preferred payment method.
8. Enter the number of purchases you expect to make with the software.
9. Enter the number of hours you expect to spend on tax tasks per year.
10. Select your expected date of purchase.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tax Professional** (`tax_professional`, select_one, required):
	+ Choose your profession from the options provided: Tax Consultant, Tax Lawyer, Tax Accountant, or Tax Advisor.
* **Professional Experience** (`tax_professional_experience`, select_one, required):
	+ Select your level of professional experience: 0-5 years, 5-10 years, 10-20 years, or 20 years+.
* **Package Features** (`tax_package_features`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Select the features that you require in a tax software package, such as Fast and efficient data collection, Smoother online purchases, Good customer support, and Online purchases available.
* **Benefits** (`package_benefits`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Select the benefits you want in a tax software package, such as Fast and efficient data collection, Smoother online purchases, Good customer support, and Online purchases available.
* **Software Seller** (`software_seller`, select_one, required):
	+ Choose the software seller that you prefer: Highly rated, Neutral, or Low rated.
* **Confidence Level** (`confidence_level`, number, required):
	+ Enter a number from 1 to 100 to indicate your confidence level in using tax software.
* **Hours Spent** (`hours_spent`, number, required):
	+ Enter the number of hours you expect to spend on tax tasks per year.
* **Expected Date of Purchase** (`expected_date_of_purchase`, date, required):
	+ Enter the date by which you expect to purchase the software.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required):
	+ Choose your preferred payment method: Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Other.
* **Purchases Count** (`purchases_count`, number, required):
	+ Enter the number of purchases you expect to make with the software.
* **Hours Spent per Month** (`hours_spent_per_month`, number, required):
	+ Enter the number of hours you expect to spend on tax tasks per month.
* **Expected Date of Purchase 2** (`expected_date_of_purchase_2`, date, required):
	+ Enter the date by which you expect to purchase the software.

## Tips

* Be honest about your professional experience and confidence level to get accurate recommendations.
* Consider the package features and benefits that are most important to you.
* Choose a payment method that you are comfortable with.
* Double-check your expected dates of purchase to ensure you don't miss a deadline.
* Enter accurate numbers for your hours spent and purchases count.
