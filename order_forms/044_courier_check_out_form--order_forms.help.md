# Courier Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Courier Check Out Form is designed to gather necessary information for the checkout process of a courier service. This form will be used for various scenarios, including but not limited to, orders for delivery, pickup, or other special services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out each section according to the provided fields and explanations.
2. Review your answers carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
3. Submit the form once you have filled out all required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Order Information** (`order_information`, text, optional): This field is used to enter any additional information about the order, such as specific instructions or notes.
  - **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about the order, including any special requests or requirements.
  - **Payment Handling** (`payment_handling`, text, optional): This field allows you to specify how the payment should be handled (e.g., credit card, cash, etc.).
  - **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Choose the payment method from the available options (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
  - **Delivery Location** (`delivery_location`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple delivery locations from the available options (e.g., Location 1, Location 2, Location 3).
  - **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, optional): Enter the expected delivery time for the order.
  - **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the current status of the payment (e.g., paid, unpaid, partial).
  - **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, optional): Enter the total payment amount for the order.
  - **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): Enter the expected delivery date for the order.
  - **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, optional): Enter the expected delivery time for the order.
  - **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the order.
  - **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address associated with the order.
  - **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number associated with the order.
  - **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact details for the order.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this guide or consult with the relevant personnel.
- Keep the form organized and clear to facilitate smooth and efficient processing.
