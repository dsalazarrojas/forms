# Crochet Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Crochet Order Form is a form used for customers to provide their details and information for a specific order, including customer details, order information, payment information, delivery address, and order notes. This form helps ensure that all necessary information is provided to process the order correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Crochet Order Form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to ensure all required fields have been filled out correctly.
2. Fill out the fields as required, providing accurate and complete information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details**: Enter your name and any other relevant personal information.
* **Order Info**: Provide any necessary details about the order, such as date or quantity ordered.
* **Payment Info**: Enter payment information, including payment method and any other relevant details.
* **Delivery Address**: Enter the delivery address of the customer.
* **Order Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the order, if needed.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Double-check that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Ensure all information is accurate and complete to avoid any delays or errors in processing the order.
* If you need help or have any questions, feel free to contact us.
