# Product Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Product Order Form is designed for end-users to provide accurate and complete order 
information to both the customer and the business. This guide will walk you through 
each section of the form to ensure a smooth and efficient order process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Review the form before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Details** (`product_order_form`, Text, Optional):
	+ Enter the product details, such as product name, description, or product 
	number, to ensure accurate product identification.
* **Order Information** (`product_order_form_1`, Text, Optional):
	+ Provide order information, such as date, time, or order number, to 
	refer to the order later.
* **Shipping Info** (`product_order_form_2`, Text, Optional):
	+ Enter shipping details, such as address, city, or state, to ensure 
	delivery to the correct location.
* **Payment Details** (`product_order_form_3`, Text, Optional):
	+ Enter payment details, such as payment method, amount, or 
	payable date, to complete the payment process.
* **Order Confirmation** (`product_order_form_4`, Text, Optional):
	+ Verify order details for accuracy before submission, ensuring a 
	correct order confirmation.
* **Additional Info** (`product_order_form_5`, Text, Optional):
	+ Enter any additional information, such as special requests or 
	instructions, to enhance the order process.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out to avoid delays in the order process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the business for assistance.
