# App Update Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The App Update Pre-Order Form is designed to gather information from users interested in pre-ordering an app update. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide your contact information, specify your requirements and needs for the update, and choose your preferred support and training options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" section, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the number of licenses you need in the "Number of licenses needed" field.
3. Choose the app version you are interested in from the "Which app version are you interested in?" dropdown menu.
4. Select your desired deployment timeline from the "When do you plan to deploy?" dropdown menu.
5. Choose your primary use case from the provided options or provide a brief description.
6. Select all applicable features from the "Which features are most important to you?" dropdown menu.
7. Provide your expected budget range in the "Expected budget range" dropdown menu.
8. Choose your desired level of support and whether you need training or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it should be spelled.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Please enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): If you are ordering on behalf of a company, please enter the company name.
* **App Version** (`app_version`, select_one, required): Please select the app version you are interested in.
* **Number of licenses needed** (`quantity`, number, required): Please enter the number of licenses you need.
* **When do you plan to deploy?** (`deployment_timeline`, select_one, optional): Choose when you plan to deploy the updated app.
* **What is your primary use case?** (`use_case`, text, optional): Briefly describe your primary use case.
* **Which features are most important to you?** (`feature_priorities`, select_multiple, required): Select all the features that are most important to you.
* **Expected budget range** (`budget_range`, select_one, optional): Choose your expected budget range.
* **What level of support do you require?** (`support_needed`, select_one, required): Please choose the level of support you require.
* **Do you need implementation training?** (`training_required`, select_one, optional): If you need training, choose the level of training you require.
* **Additional requirements or notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Please provide any additional requirements or notes you have.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields carefully to ensure accurate pre-ordering.
* If you have any additional requirements or notes, please provide them in the "Additional requirements or notes" field.
* A representative from our team will contact you soon after submitting the form to discuss your pre-order.
