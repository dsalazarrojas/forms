# Shoes Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shoes Order Form is a tool designed to collect information for shoe orders, allowing customers to provide their preferences and details regarding their desired shoes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the requested information for your shoe order.
2. Ensure you provide accurate details for your order, such as shoe design, size, and color.
3. Fill out the "Further requests" field with any additional information or instructions for your order.
4. Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
5. Select the payment method and amount for your order.
6. Enter the delivery details, including date and time.
7. Provide any additional notes or comments for your order.
8. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shoe Design** (`Shoe Design`, text, not required): Enter the desired shoe design for your order.
* **Size** (`Size`, number, not required): Enter the desired shoe size for your order.
* **Quantity** (`Quantity`, number, not required): Enter the quantity of shoes you would like to order.
* **Color** (`Color`, text, not required): Enter the desired color for your order.
* **Further requests** (`Further requests`, text, not required): Enter any additional information or instructions for your order.
* **Contact Name** (`Contact Name`, text, not required): Enter your name for contact purposes.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Delivery Details** (`Delivery Details`, text, not required): Enter any additional delivery instructions or details.
* **Payment Details** (`Payment Details`, text, not required): Enter any additional payment information or instructions.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select multiple, not required): Select the payment method you would like to use (Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer).
* **Payment Amount** (`Payment Amount`, number, not required): Enter the payment amount.
* **Order Date** (`Order Date`, date, not required): Enter the desired order date.
* **Order Time** (`Order Time`, time, not required): Enter the desired order time.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, not required): Enter any additional notes or comments for your order.
* **Order Notes** (`Order Notes`, note, not required): Enter any additional notes or comments for your order.
* **Customer Message** (`Customer Message`, text, not required): Enter any message for the customer.
* **Customer Name** (`Customer Name`, text, not required): Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Phone** (`Customer Phone`, text, not required): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Email** (`Customer Email`, email, not required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Sales Notes** (`Sales Notes`, text, not required): Enter any additional sales notes or comments.
* **Order Status** (`Order Status`, select multiple, not required): Select the order status (Pending, In Progress, Completed, Cancelled).
* **Order Number** (`Order Number`, number, not required): Enter the order number.
* **Order Total** (`Order Total`, number, not required): Enter the total order amount.
* **Payment Status** (`Payment Status`, select multiple, not required): Select the payment status (Pending, In Progress, Completed, Cancelled).
 
## Tips

* Ensure you fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check the information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your sales representative or customer support.
