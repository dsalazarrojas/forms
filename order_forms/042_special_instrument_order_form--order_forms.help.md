# Special Instrument Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The special instrument order form is designed to collect customer information and custom instrument order details for special instrument purchases. This form helps us to understand the customer's requirements and preferences, ensuring a smooth and accurate custom order fulfillment process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are filled in and that you have provided all necessary information.
2. Select the "custom_instruments" field (page 1) to indicate whether you are interested in purchasing a custom instrument or not.
3. Fill in your contact information in the "customer_details" field (page 2).
4. Enter your delivery address in the "delivery_address" field (page 3).
5. Choose your preferred payment method in the "payment_method" field (page 4).
6. Enter the order total amount in the "order_total" field (page 4).
7. Upload files relevant to your custom instrument order in the "custom_instrument_order_file_uploads_1" and "custom_instrument_order_file_upload_2" fields (pages 6 & 7).
8. Add any additional notes or comments about your order in the "custom_instrument_order_notes" field (page 8).
9. Enter your email address in the "custom_instrument_order_email" field (page 10).
10. Enter your phone number in the "custom_instrument_order_phone" field (page 11).
11. Choose the desired delivery time for your order in the "custom_instrument_order_time_delivery" field (page 12).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **custom_instruments** (page 1): Select "Yes" if you are interested in purchasing a custom instrument, "No" otherwise.
* **customer_details** (page 2): Enter your name, address, and any other contact information that may be relevant to your order.
* **delivery_address** (page 3): Enter your delivery address, including the address of where you would like to receive your order.
* **payment_method** (page 4): Choose your preferred payment method.
* **order_total** (page 4): Enter the total amount you are willing to pay for your custom instrument order.
* **custom_instrument_order_file_upload_1** and **custom_instrument_order_file_upload_2** (pages 6 & 7): Upload any relevant files about your custom instrument order, such as pictures or audio files.
* **custom_instrument_order_notes** (page 8): Add any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **custom_instrument_order_email** (page 10): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **custom_instrument_order_phone** (page 11): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your order.
* **custom_instrument_order_time_delivery** (page 12): Choose the desired delivery time for your order.
