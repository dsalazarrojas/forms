# NCC_Wish List - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your wishes or orders. Please use it to inform the customer service team about your needs, preferences, or requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your **Customer Name** in the field provided.
2. Enter the **Order Date** in the format MM/DD/YY.
3. Select the **Time** you prefer for your order in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
4. Choose the color(s) you are interested in by selecting from the options listed.
5. Enter the **Quantity** of the items you wish to order.
6. Input the price of the items you are interested in.
7. Select the **Product** you wish to order from the options listed.
8. Choose whether you would like to have your order shipped to your address by selecting **Yes** or **No**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter your full name to help us identify you.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you would like to receive your order in MM/DD/YY format.
* **Time** (`order_time`, time, required: false): Select the time you would like to receive your order in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Color** (`color`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the color(s) you are interested in.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required: false): Enter the number of items you wish to order.
* **Price** (`price`, number, required: false): Input the price of the items you are interested in.
* **Product** (`product`, select_one, required: false): Select the product you wish to order from the options listed.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose whether you would like to have your order shipped to your address.
