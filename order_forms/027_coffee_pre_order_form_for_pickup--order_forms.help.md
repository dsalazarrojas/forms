# Coffee Pre Order Form For Pickup - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coffee Pre Order Form For Pickup is designed to gather information from customers for their pre-ordered coffee and food items for pickup at a coffee shop or store location.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2.  Enter your phone number for pickup confirmation in the "Phone Number" field.
3.  Enter your email address (optional) in the "Email Address" field.
4.  Select the date of pickup in the "Pickup Date" field.
5.  Select the time of pickup in the "Preferred Pickup Time" field.
6.  Choose the store location where you want to pick up your order in the "Pickup Location" field.
7.  Select your first drink choice in the "First Drink Choice" field.
8.  Enter the quantity of your drink choice in the "Quantity" field.
9.  Select the size of your drink choice in the "Size" field.
10. Enter any customizations for your drink choice in the "Customizations" field (if applicable).
11. If you want to order a second drink, select it in the "Second Drink Choice" field.
12. Enter the quantity of your second drink choice (if applicable) in the "Drink 2 Quantity" field.
13. Select the size of your second drink choice (if applicable) in the "Drink 2 Size" field.
14. If you want to order food, select the food item from the list in the "Food Item" field.
15. Enter the quantity of your food item (if applicable) in the "Food Quantity" field.
16. Select your milk preference in the "Milk Preference" field.
17. Select your sweetness level in the "Sweetness Level" field.
18. Enter any special instructions or notes in the "Special Instructions" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name to ensure that your order is associated with your correct identity.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for pickup confirmation and any other purposes.
*   **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address to receive any updates or notifications about your order.
*   **Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`, `date`, required): Select the date when you want to pick up your order.
*   **Preferred Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, `time`, required): Select the time when you want to pick up your order.
*   **Pickup Location** (`location`, `select_one`, required): Choose the store location where you want to pick up your order.
*   **First Drink Choice** (`drink_choice_1`, `select_one`, required): Select your first drink choice from the list.
*   **Quantity** (`drink_1_quantity`, `number`, required): Enter the quantity of your first drink choice.
*   **Size** (`drink_1_size`, `select_one`, required): Select the size of your first drink choice.
*   **Customizations** (`drink_1_customization`, `text`, optional): Enter any customizations for your first drink choice.
*   **Second Drink Choice** (`drink_choice_2`, `select_one`, optional): Select your second drink choice from the list.
*   **Drink 2 Quantity** (`drink_2_quantity`, `number`, optional): Enter the quantity of your second drink choice.
*   **Drink 2 Size** (`drink_2_size`, `select_one`, optional): Select the size of your second drink choice.
*   **Customizations for Drink 2** (`drink_2_customization`, `text`, optional): Enter any customizations for your second drink choice.
*   **Food Item** (`food_item`, `select_one`, optional): Select any food item from the list you want to order.
*   **Food Quantity** (`food_quantity`, `number`, optional): Enter the quantity of the food item you selected.
*   **Milk Preference** (`milk_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select your milk preference.
*   **Sweetness Level** (`sweetness`, `select_one`, optional): Select your desired level of sweetness.
*   **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `text`, optional): Enter any special instructions or notes about your order.

## Tips

- Please double-check your information before submitting your form.
- Make sure to select the correct store location for pickup.
- If you have any special requests or allergies, please enter them in the "Special Instructions" field.
- You can select a second drink choice if you want to order more drinks.
- You can select a food item if you want to order food.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at your selected email address or phone number.
