# Adventure Map Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Adventure Map Order Form is used to submit orders for custom maps, trail maps, or region overview maps. This form helps to gather the necessary information for processing your order accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer name and shipping address carefully, as they will be used for delivery purposes.
2. Choose the type of map you would like to order from the available options.
3. Specify the quantity of maps you would like to order.
4. Select the shipping method that suits your needs.
5. Enter your billing email address for invoice and receipt purposes.
6. Provide any special instructions or notes if needed.
7. Finally, enter the date of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the customer as you would like to be displayed on the order.
* **Shipping address** (`shipping_address`, text, required): Enter the shipping address where the map will be delivered. This should include street, city, state, and postal code.
* **Map type** (`map_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of map you would like to order from the available options: Trail map, Topographic map, Region overview map, or Custom map.
* **Quantity ordered** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of maps you would like to order. Please enter a whole number.
* **Shipping method** (`shipping_method`, select_one, required): Choose the shipping method that suits your needs: Standard, Express, or Overnight.
* **Billing email** (`billing_email`, email, required): Enter the billing email address for invoice and receipt purposes.
* **Special instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, optional): Provide any additional notes or instructions for shipping or handling.
* **Order date** (`order_date`, date, required): Enter the date of submission, which will be used to track the order.

## Tips
* Please double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our support team for assistance.
* Our team will process your order as soon as possible after submission.
