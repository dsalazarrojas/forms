# merchandise_purchase_order - Help Guide
## Purpose
The merchandise purchase order form is designed to collect necessary information from customers or suppliers for the procurement and sales process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Select the type of merchandise you wish to purchase or sell using the select options provided.
2. Enter the quantity of the merchandise you want to purchase or sell.
3. Enter the price of the merchandise if applicable.
4. Provide a detailed description of the merchandise.
5. Choose any additional services required for the order.
6. Enter the contact email of the person responsible for the order.
7. Enter the contact phone number of the person responsible for the order.
8. Enter the contact name of the person responsible for the order.
9. Enter the vendor details if applicable.
10. Click the "Submit" button to finalize the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Merchandise Type** (`merchandise_type`, select_one, Required): Select the type of merchandise you wish to purchase or sell.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, Optional): Enter the quantity of the merchandise you want to purchase or sell.
* **Price** (`price`, number, Optional): Enter the price of the merchandise if applicable.
* **Description** (`description`, text, Optional): Provide a detailed description of the merchandise.
* **Services** (`services`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose any additional services required for the order.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, Optional): Enter the contact email of the person responsible for the order.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, Optional): Enter the contact phone number of the person responsible for the order.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, Optional): Enter the contact name of the person responsible for the order.
* **Vendor** (`vendor`, text, Optional): Enter the vendor details if applicable.
* **Submit** (`submit`, note, Required): Click the "Submit" button to finalize the order.
