# thanksgiving_pie_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to provide their order details, including type of pie, customer information, and other relevant details so that the business can fulfill their order correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the `order_details` page with any additional details about your order.
2. Move on to the `customer_details` page and fill out the `customer_name`, `customer_email`, `customer_phone`, and `customer_address` fields with your information.
3. In the `pie_options` page, select the type of pie you would like to order (e.g., Apple Pie, Pumpkin Pie, etc.).
4. Finally, enter the total number of pies you would like to order in the `total_pies` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): This page is for any additional details about your order. Please provide any relevant information about your order here.
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, optional): This page is for providing your customer information, including name, email, phone number, and address.
* **Pie Options** (`pie_options`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of pie you would like to order from the available options (Apple Pie, Pumpkin Pie, etc.).
* **Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it should be listed on the order.
* **Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address as it should be used for order communication.
* **Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number as it should be used for order communication.
* **Address** (`customer_address`, text, optional): Enter your address where the pies will be delivered or picked up.
* **Total Pies** (`total_pies`, number, optional): Enter the total number of pies you would like to order.
