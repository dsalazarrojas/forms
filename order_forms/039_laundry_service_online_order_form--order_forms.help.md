# Laundry Service Online Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Laundry Service Online Order Form is a user-friendly tool designed to collect information for customers to place an order with a laundry service. This form helps the customer to easily provide details about their laundry needs and payment preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose whether you want to use our pickup service or not. If you choose "Yes", select one or both of the available options for pickup locations.
2. Select the pickup time that suits you best.
3. Choose whether you want our delivery service or not. If you choose "Yes", select one or both of the available options for delivery locations.
4. Choose the payment method you prefer. You can select "Yes" for credit card, cash, or other payment methods.
5. Enter the total amount you are willing to pay for the service.
6. Provide any special instructions or additional information about your order.
7. Confirm your order details and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pickup Service** (`pickup_service`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select "Yes" if you want us to pick up your laundry from your location.
* **Pickup Location** (`pickup_location`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the address or location where you would like us to pick up your laundry.
* **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, time, required: false):
	+ Choose the time that works best for your laundry pickup.
* **Delivery Service** (`delivery_service`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select "Yes" if you want us to deliver your clean laundry to you.
* **Delivery Location** (`delivery_location`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the address or location where you would like us to deliver your laundry.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required: false):
	+ Choose the time that works best for your laundry delivery.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter any special instructions or additional information about your order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select "Yes" for credit card, cash, or other payment methods.
* **Total Dollars** (`total_dollars`, number, required: false):
	+ Enter the total amount you are willing to pay for the service.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, required: false):
	+ Confirm the payment terms and conditions.
* **Special Instructions 1** (`special_instructions_1`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter any special instructions or additional information about your order 1.
* **Special Instructions 2** (`special_instructions_2`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter any special instructions or additional information about your order 2.
