# Beef Cut Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beef Cut Order Form is a tool used to place custom orders for beef products with specific specifications.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form completely**: Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
2. **Select the correct beef quantity**: Choose the amount of beef you wish to order.
3. **Specify steak cutting instructions**: Choose the thickness and packaging of your steaks.
4. **Choose your rib and loin section preferences**: Select from the available options for rib and loin sections.
5. **Specify roast and ground beef preferences**: Choose your desired roast and ground beef options.
6. **Add any additional extras**: Select any additional organ meats or specialties you desire.
7. **Enter any additional instructions**: Provide any specific cutting or packaging instructions.
8. **Choose your pickup or delivery preference**: Select how you wish to receive your order.
9. **Submit your order**: Click the submit button to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact and confirmation purposes.
* **Amount of Beef Ordering**: Choose the quantity of beef you wish to order from the available options.
* **Slaughter / Processing Date**: Select the date on which you would like the beef processed.
* **Desired Steak Thickness**: Choose the thickness of your steak from the available options.
* **Number of Steaks Per Package**: Enter the number of steaks you wish to have per package.
* **Rib Section Choice**: Select your preference for the rib section from the available options.
* **Loin Section Choice**: Select your preference for the loin section from the available options.
* **Chuck Section Processing**: Choose your desired chuck section processing options.
* **Ground Beef Package Size**: Choose the size of the ground beef package from the available options.
* **Ground Beef Lean / Fat Ratio**: Choose the desired lean to fat ratio for your ground beef.
* **Organ Meats or Extras**: Select any additional organ meats or specialties you desire.
* **Additional Instructions**: Enter any specific cutting or packaging instructions.
* **Pickup or Delivery Preference**: Choose how you wish to receive your order.
* **Order Submission Date**: Enter the date on which you would like to submit your order.
