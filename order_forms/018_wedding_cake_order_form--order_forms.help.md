# Wedding Cake Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your custom wedding cake order. Please fill out the form with your details to ensure that your special day is celebrated with the perfect cake.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the "Order Information" page with the required details:
	* Enter the date you would like to order your wedding cake.
	* Select the type of wedding cake you are interested in (Round, Heart, or Square).
	* Choose the size of the cake that suits your needs (Small, Medium, Large, Extra Large, or Extra Extra Large).
	* Select the filling you prefer (Strawberry or Chocolate).
2. On the second page, enter your contact information:
	* Provide your contact number where the baker can reach you.
	* Enter your email address for further communication.
	* Enter the name of the person you would like to contact.
3. Finally, add any special message to the baker and select the baker you would like to work with (Jimmy or Tom).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Date**: Enter the date you would like to order your wedding cake.
* **Order Time**: Select the time you would like to order your wedding cake (if applicable).
* **Wedding Cake Type**: Choose the style of your wedding cake (Round, Heart, or Square).
* **Wedding Cake Size**: Select the size of the wedding cake (Small, Medium, Large, Extra Large, or Extra Extra Large).
* **Wedding Cake Filling**: Select the filling you prefer for your wedding cake (Strawberry or Chocolate).
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the date you would like to receive your wedding cake (if applicable).
* **Delivery Time**: Select the time you would like to receive your wedding cake (if applicable).
* **Message to Baker**: Add any special instructions or requests to the baker.
* **Baker's Name**: Select the baker you would like to work with (Jimmy or Tom).
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number where the baker can reach you.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person you would like to contact.
* **Cake Price**: You can enter any price you are willing to pay for the cake (optional, but not required).
