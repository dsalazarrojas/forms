# Towing-Service-Request-Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a structured way for customers to submit a towing service request, including details about their vehicle, the type of service needed, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information about your vehicle and the service needed.
2. Select the correct service type from the available options.
3. Choose the desired locations for service from the provided options.
4. Enter your contact information, including phone number and email.
5. Provide any additional information about your vehicle or service request.
6. Review your submission for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name, so we can address you personally.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, optional): Enter your vehicle's make, such as Ford or Toyota.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, optional): Enter your vehicle's model, such as Mustang or Camry.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, optional): Enter the year of your vehicle's manufacture.
* **Vehicle Plate Number** (`vehicle_plate_number`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's license plate number.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of service you need, such as Towing, Recovery, or Roadside.
* **Service Location** (`service_location`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the locations where the service should be performed.
* **Service Time** (`service_time`, time, optional): Enter the time when you need the service to be performed.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Address** (`address`, note, optional): Provide any additional information about your vehicle's location or service location.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, optional): Provide any additional details about your vehicle or service request.
* **Vehicle Insurance** (`vehicle_insurance`, select_multiple, optional): Check if your vehicle is insured.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_one, optional): Check if you want to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number of an emergency contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact email of an emergency contact person.
* **Service Comments** (`service_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about your service request.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, optional): Select the payment status of your service request.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, optional): Enter the payment date of your service request.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select the payment method used for your service request.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of vehicle you are requesting service for.
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`, text, optional): Enter the color of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make-Model** (`vehicle_make_model`, text, optional): Enter the make and model of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year Range** (`vehicle_year_range`, number, optional): Enter the range of years your vehicle was manufactured.
* **Service Status** (`service_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your service request. 

Note: Some fields are optional and can be omitted if not relevant.
