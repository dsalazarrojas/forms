# Lab Chemical Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lab Chemical Delivery Form is used to document the delivery of chemicals for inventory management and other administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Chemical Name** of the substance being delivered.
2. Select the **Delivery Date** when the substance is expected to be delivered.
3. Enter the **Quantity** of the substance being delivered.
4. Enter the **Supplier Name** of the substance.
5. Select the **Delivery Type** (e.g., "Yes" or "No") to indicate whether the delivery is being made.
6. Enter the **Lab ID** of the lab associated with the delivery.
7. Enter the **Chemical ID** of the substance being delivered.
8. Select the **Shipment Date** when the substance is actually shipped.
9. Select the **Delivery Status** (e.g., "Active" or "Inactive") to indicate whether the delivery is active or not.
10. Enter the **Customer Name** of the person or organization receiving the delivery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Chemical Name** (`chemical_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the substance being delivered.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): Select the date when the substance is expected to be delivered.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of the substance being delivered.
* **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the supplier of the substance.
* **Delivery Type** (`delivery_type`, select_one, optional): Select whether the delivery is being made.
* **Lab ID** (`lab_id`, text, optional): Enter the ID of the lab associated with the delivery.
* **Chemical ID** (`chemical_id`, text, optional): Enter the ID of the substance being delivered.
* **Shipment Date** (`shipment_date`, date, optional): Select the date when the substance is actually shipped.
* **Delivery Status** (`delivery_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of the delivery (e.g., "Active" or "Inactive").
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or organization receiving the delivery.
