# Capuchin Monkey Purchase Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for individuals or organizations interested in acquiring capuchin monkeys for personal or professional purposes. It helps us assess your experience, ability to provide suitable care, and commitment to the long-term care of the animals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form carefully, providing accurate and truthful information about yourself or your organization.
2. Select the correct options for each field, and ensure that you have answered all required fields.
3. Review your submission before submitting it, ensuring that all fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name, as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Your last name, as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address**: Your official email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact number for any further questions or concerns.
* **Street Address**: Your physical address where the capuchin monkey will reside.
* **City**: The city where you live or plan to keep the capuchin monkey.
* **State/Province**: The state or province where you reside.
* **ZIP or Postal Code**: Your post code or zip code.
* **Country**: The country where you reside.
* **Experience with Primates**: Select one of the options provided to describe your level of experience with primates.
	+ No experience
	+ Minimal experience
	+ Moderate experience
	+ Extensive experience
	+ Professional experience
* **Describe Your Experience with Primates**: This field requires a detailed description of your past experiences working with, caring for, or interacting with primates.
* **Other Animals Currently Owned**: If you have other animals, please list them in this field.
* **Veterinary Reference**: Please provide the name and contact information of your veterinarian.
* **Primate Specialist Reference (if available)**: If you have a primate specialist reference, please provide their name and contact information.
* **Type of Housing Available**: Select one of the options provided to describe the type of housing you have available for the capuchin monkey.
	+ Single family home
	+ Apartment
	+ Farm/acreage
	+ Facility/sanctuary
	+ Other
* **Indoor Space Available (sq ft)**: Please provide an approximate area available for the capuchin monkey indoors.
* **Outdoor Space Available (sq ft)**: Please provide an approximate area available for the capuchin monkey outdoors.
* **Enclosure Details and Features**: Describe the setup and features of your housing in this field.
* **Enrichment and Activity Plan**: Describe how you plan to enrich and maintain the capuchin monkey's life.
* **Access to Primate-Experienced Veterinary Care?**: Select one of the options provided to describe your access to veterinary care for the capuchin monkey.
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Unsure
* **Financial Ability to Support Animal Long-term?**: Select one of the options provided to describe your financial ability to support the capuchin monkey long-term.
	+ Yes, definitely
	+ Yes, probably
	+ Maybe
	+ No
* **Commitment to Lifetime Care (20-45 years)?**: Select one of the options provided to describe your commitment to care for the capuchin monkey for its lifetime.
	+ Yes, fully committed
	+ Yes, probably committed
	+ Unsure
	+ No
* **Plan for Care During Travel/Absence**: Describe how you plan to care for the capuchin monkey during travel or absence.
* **I understand and agree to all ethical animal care standards?**: Select one of the options provided to confirm that you have read and agree to the provided guidelines.
