* Order Details: This section is for any additional notes or comments the customer wants to include with their order.
* Shipping Address: This section collects the shipping address of the customer.
* Order Status: This section is for the status of the order, which can be pending, processing, delivered, or cancelled.
* Order Date and Time: This section collects the date and time the order was placed.
* Order Notes: This section is for any additional notes or comments the customer wants to include with their order.
* Order ID: This section collects the unique ID of the order.

## Purpose

The T-Shirt Product Order Form is designed to collect information from customers to place an order for a t-shirt or other apparel product.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete the form, start by filling out the "Customer Info" section. This includes providing your name, email, phone number, and other contact information.
2. Next, select the type of t-shirt or apparel product you want to order from the "Customer T-Shirt" section.
3. Provide the billing address and name in the "Billing Info" section.
4. Choose the payment method from the "Payment Method" section.
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Order Details" section.
6. Enter the shipping address in the "Shipping Address" section.
7. Select the status of the order in the "Order Status" section.
8. Provide the date and time the order was placed in the "Order Date and Time" section.
9. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Order Notes" section.
10. The "Order ID" section is for the unique ID of the order, which will be automatically generated.
