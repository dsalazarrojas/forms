# Vehicle Wrap Design Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Wrap Design Order Form is used to gather essential details from customers to create a custom vehicle wrap design. This form will ask for information about the vehicle, design specifications, and contact details to ensure a smooth and successful design process.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

* Enter your **Client Name** in the "Client Name" field. This will help us keep track of your order.
* Provide **Vehicle Information** in the "Vehicle Info" field. Describe the type of vehicle you are interested in designing a wrap for (e.g., car, bike, or truck).
* Enter the **Vehicle Year** if you know it. This will help us provide a more accurate design.
* Select the **Wrap Type** from the dropdown menu. You can choose from "Chrome" or "Vinyl" wrap types.
* Choose the **Wrap Size** that best fits your needs. You can select one or multiple options: "Full", "Partial", or "Custom".
* Enter any **Wrap Design** notes or specifications in the "Design Specifications" field.
* Select the **Vehicle Color** from the available options.
* Choose the **Vehicle Make** from the available options.
* Enter the **Vehicle Model** if you know it.
* Enter the **Vehicle Year Range** if you have a specific range in mind.
* Select the **Wrap Location** where you'd like the design applied. You can choose from "Front", "Back", or "Sides".
* Add any additional **Wrap Design Note** in the "Wrap Design Note" field.
* Enter your **Contact Name**.
* Enter your **Contact Phone Number**.
* Enter your **Contact Email**.
* Enter a **Contact Message** if you have any specific requests or questions.
* Select your **Payment Terms**. You can either pay on delivery or pay on invoice.
* Select your **Payment Method**. You can choose from "Credit Card", "Bank Transfer", or "Cash".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name (Client Name)**: Enter your name as the person ordering the vehicle wrap design.
* **Vehicle Info (Vehicle Info)**: Provide a brief description of the vehicle you are interested in designing a wrap for.
* **Vehicle Year (Vehicle Year)**: Enter the year of your vehicle if you know it.
* **Vehicle Make (Vehicle Make)**: Select the make of your vehicle from the available options.
* **Vehicle Model (Vehicle Model)**: Enter the model of your vehicle if you know it.
* **Vehicle Color (Vehicle Color)**: Select the color of your vehicle from the available options.
* **Wrap Type (Wrap Type)**: Choose the type of wrap you'd like (Chrome or Vinyl).
* **Wrap Size (Wrap Size)**: Select the size of the wrap you need (Full, Partial, or Custom).
* **Wrap Design (Wrap Design)**: Enter any specific design notes or specifications.
* **Wrap Location (Wrap Location)**: Choose where you'd like the wrap applied (Front, Back, or Sides).
* **Wrap Design Note (Wrap Design Note)**: Add any additional notes or details about the design.
* **Contact Name (Contact Name)**: Enter your name as the contact person.
* **Contact Phone (Contact Phone)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Email (Contact Email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Contact Message (Contact Message)**: Add any specific requests or questions.
* **Payment Terms (Payment Terms)**: Select when you'd like to pay (on delivery or on invoice).
* **Payment Method (Payment Method)**: Choose your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Cash).
