# Automation Line Replacement Part Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for ordering replacement parts for automation lines. Please fill out the form with your details and information about the part you need. The form is divided into several sections to guide you through the process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your personal details in sections 3-5 (First Name, Last Name, and Email Address).
2. Select your department (section 7) from the dropdown list.
3. Provide your company details (section 10) if you need assistance from your company.
4. Fill in the line information (sections 17-18, 19-20, 21-22).
5. Select the equipment type and manufacturer (sections 19, 20, 21).
6. Select the part type (section 27).
7. Specify the quantity needed (section 28).
8. Choose the shipping method and account number (sections 37-38) if applicable.
9. Attach a purchase order and provide your budget code if applicable (sections 44-45).
10. Check if an approval is required (section 52) and specify the approver's name if so.
11. Sign with your digital signature (section 50).
12. Set the request date (section 51).
13. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`3`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official ID.
* **Last Name** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official ID.
* **Email Address** (`5`, `email`, required): Enter your work email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`6`, `text`, required): Enter your work phone number for communication.
* **Department** (`7`, `select_one`, required): Select your department (e.g., Maintenance, Engineering).
* **Company Information** (`9`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional information about your company.
* **Company Name** (`10`, `text`, required): Enter your company's legal name.
* **Facility Name** (`11`, `text`, optional): Enter the facility name if applicable.
* **Facility Address** (`12`, `text`, required): Enter the shipping address of the facility.
* **City** (`13`, `text`, required): Enter the city where the facility is located.
* **State** (`14`, `select_one`, required): Select the state where the facility is located.
* **ZIP Code** (`15`, `text`, required): Enter the 5-digit ZIP code of the facility.
* **Automation Line Information** (`16`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional information about the line.
* **Line ID** (`17`, `text`, required): Enter the line ID or identifier.
* **Line Name** (`18`, `text`, optional): Enter the line name if applicable.
* **Equipment Type** (`19`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of equipment (e.g., Conveyor System, Robotic Arm).
* **Equipment Manufacturer** (`20`, `text`, required): Enter the equipment manufacturer's brand name.
* **Equipment Model** (`21`, `text`, required): Enter the equipment model number.
* **Equipment Serial Number** (`22`, `text`, optional): Enter the equipment serial number.
* **Part Information** (`23`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional information about the part.
* **Part Name** (`24`, `text`, required): Enter the part name or OEM part number.
* **Part Number** (`25`, `text`, required): Enter the OEM part number.
* **Part Description** (`26`, `text`, optional): Enter a description of the part.
* **Part Type** (`27`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of part (e.g., Electrical Component, Mechanical Component).
* **Quantity Needed** (`28`, `number`, required): Enter the quantity of parts needed.
* **Unit of Measure** (`29`, `select_one`, required): Select the unit of measure (e.g., Each, Box, Pack).
* **Alternative Part Numbers** (`30`, `text`, optional): Enter alternative part numbers if available.
* **Part Condition Required** (`31`, `select_one`, required): Select the condition of the part (e.g., New OEM, Refurbished).
* **Order Urgency** (`32`, `select_one`, required): Select the urgency of the order (e.g., Emergency, Normal).
* **Reason for Replacement** (`33`, `select_one`, required): Select the reason for replacement (e.g., Equipment Failure, Preventive Maintenance).
* **Failure Description** (`34`, `text`, optional): Enter a description of the failure.
* **Downtime Impact** (`35`, `select_one`, required): Select the impact of downtime on the line.
* **Shipping Information** (`36`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional shipping information.
* **Shipping Method** (`37`, `select_one`, required): Select the shipping method (e.g., Standard Ground, Expedited 2-Day).
* **Shipping Account Number** (`38`, `text`, optional): Enter the shipping account number if applicable.
* **Special Shipping Instructions** (`39`, `text`, optional): Enter any special shipping instructions.
* **Receiving Contact** (`40`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person receiving the part.
* **Receiving Phone** (`41`, `text`, optional): Enter the receiving contact's phone number.
* **Pricing Information** (`42`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional pricing information.
* **Budget Code** (`43`, `text`, optional): Enter the budget code if applicable.
* **Cost Center** (`44`, `text`, optional): Enter the cost center if applicable.
* **Purchase Order Required** (`45`, `select_one`, required): Select if a purchase order is required.
* **PO Number** (`46`, `text`, optional): Enter the PO number if applicable.
* **Estimated Budget** (`47`, `select_one`, optional): Select the estimated budget for the part.
* **Vendor Preference** (`48`, `text`, optional): Enter the preferred vendor if applicable.
* **Additional Notes** (`49`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Requester Signature** (`50`, `text`, required): Enter your digital signature.
* **Request Date** (`51`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the request.
* **Approval Required** (`52`, `select_one`, required): Select if approval is required.
* **Approver Name** (`53`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the approver if approval is required.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Enter accurate and complete information to ensure smooth processing of your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the order team for assistance.
* Attach a purchase order or other required documents if applicable. 

Please review and complete the form carefully to ensure accuracy and efficiency in processing your order.
