# Personalized Ornament Pre Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Personalized Ornament Pre Order Form is used to collect personal and order-related information from customers to prepare for the order fulfillment process. This form is designed to be easy to fill out and helps the business process your order efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name by filling out the First Name and Last Name fields.
2. Enter your address, phone number, and email address in the corresponding fields.
3. Select the date you would like to order the ornament.
4. Choose the type of order (Ornament, Decoration, or Gift).
5. Select the type and size of the ornament you wish to order.
6. Indicate the quantity of the ornament you would like to order.
7. Add any additional notes or comments about your order, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your home or office address where you would like to receive your order.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for any order updates.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address where you can receive order updates and notifications.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, required): Select the date you would like to receive your order. Please note that orders for the same date will be processed in order of receipt.
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the type of order (Ornament, Decoration, or Gift).
* **Ornament Type** (`ornament_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of ornament you would like to order (Ball Ornament, Tree Ornament, or Christmas Ornament).
* **Ornament Size** (`ornament_size`, `select_one`, required): Choose the size of the ornament you would like to order (Small, Medium, or Large).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, required): Indicate how many ornaments you would like to order.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required): Add any additional comments or notes about your order, if necessary.
