# Pet Supplies Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Supplies Order Form is designed to help customers place orders for pet supplies. Fill out this form to provide us with essential details about your order and your pet.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields on each page. The form has three pages.
2. Enter your name and contact information on the first page.
3. Confirm the date you submitted the form on the second page.
4. Click "Submit" to send your order details to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Pet Supplies Order Form Page 1
* **Pet Supplies Order Form** (`pet_supplies_order_form_page_1_label`, note, required: false): This page is a general information page where you can provide any additional details about your order.

### Pet Supplies Order Form Page 2
* **Customer Name** (`pet_supplies_order_form_submitted_by`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Submission Date** (`pet_supplies_order_form_submitted_on`, date, required: false): Enter the date you submitted this form.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your submissions date to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
