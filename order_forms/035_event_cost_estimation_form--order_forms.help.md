## event_cost_estimation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The event cost estimation form is used to estimate the costs associated with an event. This form is intended to gather essential details about the event to provide an accurate cost breakdown. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the overall cost in the "Overall Cost" field.
2. Fill in the event details in the "Event Details" field.
3. Enter the estimated costs for each category (Event Estimation, Location Estimation, Travel Estimation, Food Estimation, Audio Visual Estimation, and Other Estimation).
4. Enter the event name, date, start and end times, venue, location, address, city, state, and zip code.
5. Provide the contact information, including name, phone number, email, organization, and job title.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Cost (Overall Cost)** (`number`, required): Enter the total estimated cost for the event.
* **Event Details (Event Details)** (`text`, required): A brief description of the event.
* **Event Estimation (Event Estimation)** (`number`, required): Estimated costs associated with the event (e.g. personnel, equipment, etc.).
* **Location Estimation (Location Estimation)** (`number`, required): Estimated costs associated with location (e.g. rental, etc.).
* **Travel Estimation (Travel Estimation)** (`number`, required): Estimated costs associated with travel (e.g. transportation, etc.).
* **Food Estimation (Food Estimation)** (`number`, required): Estimated costs associated with food (e.g. catering, etc.).
* **Audio Visual Estimation (Audio Visual Estimation)** (`number`, required): Estimated costs associated with audio-visual equipment (e.g. microphones, etc.).
* **Other Estimation (Other Estimation)** (`number`, required): Estimated costs associated with other event expenses (e.g. decorations, etc.).
* **Event Name (Event Name)** (`text`, required): Name of the event.
* **Event Date (Event Date)** (`date`, required): Date of the event.
* **Event Start (Event Start)** (`time`, required): Start time of the event.
* **Event End (Event End)** (`time`, required): End time of the event.
* **Event Venue (Event Venue)** (`text`, required): Venue where the event will take place.
* **Event Location (Event Location)** (`text`, optional): Location of the event (city, state, country).
* **Event Address (Event Address)** (`text`, optional): Address of the event location.
* **Event City (Event City)** (`text`, optional): City of the event location.
* **Event State (Event State)** (`text`, optional): State of the event location.
* **Event Zip (Event Zip)** (`text`, optional): Zip code of the event location.
* **Event Country (Event Country)** (`text`, optional): Country of the event location.
* **Contact Name (Contact Name)** (`text`, required): Name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone (Contact Phone)** (`text`, required): Phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email (Contact Email)** (`email`, required): Email of the contact person.
* **Contact Organization (Contact Organization)** (`text`, required): Organization of the contact person.
* **Contact Job Title (Contact Job Title)** (`text`, optional): Job title of the contact person.
* **Contact Notes (Contact Notes)** (`note`, optional): Additional notes about the contact person.
* **Travel Estimation (Travel Estimation)** (`number`, required): Estimated costs associated with travel (e.g. transportation, etc.).
* **Food Estimation (Food Estimation)** (`number`, required): Estimated costs associated with food (e.g. catering, etc.).
* **Other Estimation (Other Estimation)** (`number`, required): Estimated costs associated with other event expenses (e.g. decorations, etc.).
