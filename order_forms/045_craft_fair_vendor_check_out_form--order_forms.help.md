# Craft Fair Vendor Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to facilitate the check out process for vendors at a craft fair, ensuring accurate and efficient record-keeping and payment processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the vendor's information in the "Vendors Information" field.
2. Enter item information in the "Item Information" field.
3. Enter the total sales tax in the "Sales Tax" field.
4. Enter the sales tax percent as a percentage in the "Sales Tax Percent" field.
5. Select the payment method (e.g., Cash, Credit Card, Check) in the "Payment Method" field.
6. Enter any additional sales information in the "Sales Information" field.
7. Check the "Payment Terms Agreement" box if you agree to the payment terms.
8. Enter any notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
9. Enter the vendor's email address in the "Email" field.
10. Enter the vendor's phone number in the "Phone" field.
11. Enter the date and time of the transaction in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
12. Enter the total sales amount in the "Sales Total" field.
13. Enter the change given to the vendor in the "Change Given" field.
14. Enter the amount due from the vendor in the "Amount Due" field.
15. Enter the sales tax amount in the "Sales Tax Amount" field.
16. Enter the sales tax refund amount in the "Sales Tax Refund" field.
17. Enter the total sales amount in the "Total Sales" field.
18. Enter the payment total in the "Payment Total" field.
19. Enter the cash change given to the vendor in the "Cash Change" field.
20. Enter the check number (if applicable) in the "Check Number" field.
21. Enter the vendor's signature in the "Vendor Signature" field.
22. Enter the sales tax rate as a percentage in the "Sales Tax Rate" field.
23. Enter any additional payment terms details in the "Payment Terms Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendors Information** (vendor_info): Enter the vendor's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Item Information** (item_info): Enter details about the items sold or traded.
* **Sales Tax** (sales_tax): Enter the total sales tax amount.
* **Sales Tax Percent** (sales_tax_percent): Enter the sales tax percentage as a number (e.g., 8.5).
* **Payment Method** (payment_method): Select the payment method used (e.g., Cash, Credit Card, Check).
* **Sales Information** (sales_info): Enter any additional sales information.
* **Payment Terms** (payment_terms): Enter any payment terms or conditions.
* **Payment Terms Agreement** (payment_terms_agreement): Check the box if you agree to the payment terms.
* **Notes** (notes): Enter any notes or comments about the transaction.
* **Email** (email): Enter the vendor's email address.
* **Phone** (phone): Enter the vendor's phone number.
* **Date** (date): Enter the date of the transaction.
* **Time** (time): Enter the time of the transaction.
* **Sales Total** (sales_total): Enter the total sales amount.
* **Change Given** (change_given): Enter the amount of change given to the vendor.
* **Amount Due** (amount_due): Enter the amount due from the vendor.
* **Sales Tax Amount** (sales_tax_amount): Enter the sales tax amount.
* **Sales Tax Refund** (sales_tax_refund): Enter the sales tax refund amount.
* **Total Sales** (total_sales): Enter the total sales amount.
* **Payment Total** (payment_total): Enter the payment total.
* **Cash Change** (cash_change): Enter the amount of cash change given to the vendor.
* **Check Number** (check_number): Enter the check number (if applicable).
* **Vendor Signature** (vendor_signature): Enter the vendor's signature.
* **Sales Tax Rate** (sales_tax_rate): Enter the sales tax rate as a percentage (e.g., 8.5).
* **Payment Terms Details** (payment_terms_details): Enter any additional payment terms details.
