# Factory Equipment Grounding Kit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for ordering a Factory Equipment Grounding Kit, which is a kit used for safety grounding of electrical equipment. The form will collect information to process the order and communicate with the customer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Enter your order details in the "Order Details" field.
2. Provide the equipment id.
3. Select the type of equipment you are ordering (yes or no).
4. Select the size of the equipment you are ordering (yes or no).
5. Select the type of grounding you need (yes or no).
6. Enter the quantity of the kit you need.
7. Enter the price of the kit.
8. Select the shipping method (yes or no).
9. Enter your shipping address.
10. Enter your shipping city.
11. Enter your shipping state.
12. Enter your shipping zip code.
13. Enter your shipping country.
14. (Optional) Enter your shipping phone number.
15. (Optional) Enter your shipping email address.
16. Enter your customer name.
17. (Optional) Enter your customer phone number.
18. Enter your customer email address.
19. (Optional) Enter your company name.
20. (Optional) Enter your company phone number.
21. Enter your company address.
22. Enter your company zip code.
23. Enter your company country.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details**: Enter a brief description of your order, including any specific requirements or instructions.
* **Equipment Id**: Enter the id of the equipment you are ordering.
* **Equipment Type**: Select "Yes" if the equipment is a specific type, or "No" if it is not.
* **Equipment Size**: Select "Yes" for each size of equipment you are ordering.
* **Grounding Type**: Select "Yes" for the type of grounding you need.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of kits you need.
* **Price**: Enter the price of the kit.
* **Shipping Method**: Select "Yes" if you need shipping, or "No" if you will pick up the kit.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the address where you want the kit to be shipped.
* **Shipping City**: Enter the city where you want the kit to be shipped.
* **Shipping State**: Enter the state where you want the kit to be shipped.
* **Shipping Zip**: Enter the zip code where you want the kit to be shipped.
* **Shipping Country**: Enter the country where you want the kit to be shipped.
* **Shipping Phone**: (Optional) Enter your phone number.
* **Shipping Email**: (Optional) Enter your email address.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name.
* **Customer Phone**: (Optional) Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Company Name**: (Optional) Enter your company's name.
* **Company Phone**: (Optional) Enter your company phone number.
* **Company Address**: Enter your company's address.
* **Company Zip**: Enter your company's zip code.
* **Company Country**: Enter your company's country.
