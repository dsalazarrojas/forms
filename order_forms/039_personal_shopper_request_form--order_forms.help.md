# Personal Shopper Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Shopper Request Form is used to gather information from personal shoppers for their shopping session preferences and requirements. This form is designed to help the shopper and the store staff prepare for a smooth and efficient shopping experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's **Client Name**, which is the name of the person who will be shopping.
2. Enter the **Phone Number** of the client.
3. Provide the **Email Address** of the client.
4. Select the desired **Shopping Session** time: Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
5. Choose the **Date of Shopping** for the session.
6. Select the preferred **Shopping Time** for the session.
7. If needed, indicate the **Additional Requests** you have for the shopper.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Enter the full name of the person who will be shopping.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the client for easy communication.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the client for future reference.
* **Shopping Session**: Select the time of day for the shopping session (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
* **Date of Shopping**: Choose the date for the shopping session.
* **Preferred Shopping Time**: Select the preferred time for the shopping session.
* **Preferred Language**: Select the language you would like to have as a primary language during the shopping session (English, Spanish, or French).
* **Additional Requests**: If you have any special requests, such as product availability or specific product requests, provide them in this field.
