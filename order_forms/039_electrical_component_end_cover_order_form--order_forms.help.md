# electrical_component_end_cover_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from customers to place an order for electrical component end covers. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:
- Fill in the Form Details section with the relevant information.
- In the Delivery Preferences section, select whether you prefer delivery on a specific date or not.
- Enter your Payment Info, including any required payment details.
- Select the Suppliers and Manufacturers that you would like to order from.
- Provide the Order Total, including any applicable discounts.
- Enter your Customer Data, including a valid email address.
- Add any Notes about your order, if required.
- Finally, click Submit to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Details** (`form_details`, `text`, required): Fill in the necessary details for the form, including any relevant information.
- **Delivery Preferences** (`delivery_preferences`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you prefer delivery on a specific date or not.
- **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, `text`, required): Enter your payment information, including any applicable payment methods or details.
- **Suppliers and Manufacturers** (`suppliers_manufacturers`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable suppliers and manufacturers for your order.
- **Order Total** (`order_total`, `number`, required): Enter the total cost of your order, including any applicable discounts.
- **Customer Data** (`customer_data`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address for yourself or your representative.
- **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any relevant notes about your order, if required.
