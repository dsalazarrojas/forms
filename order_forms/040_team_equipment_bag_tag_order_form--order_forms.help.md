# Team Equipment Bag Tag Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by team members to order equipment for their team and submit a request for equipment to be tagged with a unique identifier.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the category for your order from the dropdown menu. Currently, you have options to select from "Order Forms" and "Product Order Forms".
2. Provide a detailed description of the equipment you are ordering.
3. Enter the name of the product or equipment you are ordering.
4. Enter the quantity of the equipment you need.
5. Enter the price of the equipment.
6. Select a date for the order.
7. Enter the submitter's name.
8. Enter the submitter's email address.
9. Enter the submitter's phone number.
10. Enter any additional order notes.
11. Enter any customer notes.
12. Enter the submitter's name.
13. Enter the submitter's company or organization.
14. Enter the submitter's job title.
15. Enter the submitter's department or team.
16. Enter the submitter's job function or role.
17. Enter the submitter's manager's name.
18. Enter the submitter's manager's email address.
19. Enter the submitter's manager's phone number.
20. Enter the submitter's manager's department or team.
21. Enter the submitter's manager's job title.
22. Enter the submitter's manager's email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category**: 
	+ Select one from the dropdown menu to categorize your order.
* **Description**: A detailed description of the equipment you are ordering.
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product or equipment you are ordering.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of units you need.
* **Price**: Enter the cost of the equipment.
* **Order Date**: Select a date for the order.
* **Submitter**: Enter your name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Order Notes**: Enter any additional information about the order.
* **Customer Notes**: Enter any notes for the customer.
* **Submitter Email**: Re-enter your email address for verification.
* **Submitter Phone**: Re-enter your phone number for verification.
* **Submitter Address**: Enter your address.
* **Submitter Company**: Enter your company or organization.
* **Submitter Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Submitter Department**: Enter your department or team.
* **Submitter Job Function**: Enter your job function or role.
* **Submitter Manager**: Enter your manager's name.
* **Submitter Manager Title**: Re-enter your manager's title.
* **Submitter Manager Department**: Re-enter your manager's department or team.
* **Submitter Manager Job Title**: Re-enter your manager's job title.
* **Submitter Manager Email**: Re-enter your manager's email address.
* **Submitter Manager Phone**: Re-enter your manager's phone number.
* **Submitter Manager Address**: Re-enter your manager's address.
* **Submitter Manager Company**: Re-enter your manager's company or organization.
