# 2021 Heidi's Mani Tease Color Street Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 2021 Heidi's Mani Tease Color Street Order Form is designed to collect necessary information from customers for ordering Color Street products or services. This includes contact information, order details, delivery preferences, and payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Enter your full name**: Fill in the "Full name" field with your legal name, as it appears on your identification documents.
2.  **Enter your email address**: Type your email address where you can be reached for order confirmation and updates.
3.  **Enter your phone number**: Provide your phone number for delivery coordination or any other necessary contact.
4.  **Select order date**: Choose the date you want to place your order, ensuring you receive your items on time.
5.  **Select the item(s) you want to order**: Choose the specific Color Street collection or product you wish to purchase, and specify the quantity.
6.  **Enter the unit price per set**: Provide the unit price of the item you've selected.
7.  **Choose your nail size preference**: Select the nail size that best fits your needs.
8.  **Select your preferred delivery method**: Choose between standard, express shipping, pickup, or local delivery based on your preference.
9.  **Enter your delivery address (if different from billing)**: If your delivery address is different from your billing address, enter it here.
10. **Request a delivery date (optional)**: If you prefer a specific delivery date, enter it here; otherwise, leave it blank for the earliest available delivery.
11.  **Choose your payment method**: Select how you wish to make your payment (credit card, cash, bank transfer, PayPal, Venmo, or invoice).
12.  **Enter any special instructions or customization requests**: If you have any additional requests or needs, enter them in this field.
13.  **Indicate how you heard about Heidi's Mani Tease**: If you'd like to help us understand how you discovered our business, select your preferred source (social media, friend referral, previous customer, online search, or an advertisement).
14.  **Additional comments or notes**: Provide any additional information you'd like to share about your order.
