# Makeup Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Makeup Order Form is designed to collect customer information for order fulfillment and delivery purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer details, including your name, email address, and any other relevant information.
2. Provide your delivery address, including your street address, city, state, and zip code.
3. Describe your order details, including the products you would like to purchase.
4. Select your preferred payment method (Visa, Mastercard, or PayPal).
5. Add any customer notes, such as special requests or comments.
6. Provide delivery notes, if applicable.
7. Select the desired delivery time and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Details
* **Customer Details**: This field is where you can enter your name and contact information.
* **Delivery Address**: Enter your delivery address, including your street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Order Details**: Describe the products you would like to purchase.
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method (Visa, Mastercard, or PayPal).
* **Customer Notes**: Add any special requests or comments.
* **Delivery Notes**: Provide any notes related to your delivery.
* **Delivery Time**: Select the time slot you prefer for your delivery.
* **Delivery Date**: Choose the date for your delivery.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the customer's information is not already available in the system before creating a new order.
* If you are a registered customer, you do not need to fill in this form again.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our customer support team.
* Please review and submit the form carefully to avoid any errors.
* We will contact you to confirm your order and delivery details.
