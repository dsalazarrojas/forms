# kitchen-flooring-safety-mat-order-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The kitchen-flooring-safety-mat-order-form is a form used to collect contact information and product details from customers who are interested in ordering kitchen flooring safety mats.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
2. Enter the delivery address where you would like the product to be shipped.
3. Select the specific product you are interested in.
4. Enter any payment information (such as credit card details) to complete the order.
5. Enter the total cost of the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information (1)** (`text`, **Required: No**): Enter your name, phone number, and email address to contact you.
* **Delivery Address (2)** (`text`, **Required: No**): Enter the address where you would like the product to be shipped.
* **Product (3)** (`text`, **Required: No**): Select the specific product you are interested in (e.g. "Mat A" or "Mat B").
* **Payment Information (4)** (`text`, **Required: No**): Enter any payment information required to complete the order (e.g. credit card details).
* **Order Total (5)** (`number`, **Required: No**): Enter the total cost of the order, which will be calculated automatically based on the selected product.
