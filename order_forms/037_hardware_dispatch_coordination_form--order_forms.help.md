# Hardware Dispatch Coordination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hardware Dispatch Coordination Form is a template for coordinating the dispatch of hardware equipment between a dispatcher and a requester. This form helps gather necessary information and contact details to facilitate a smooth equipment dispatch process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter the title of the dispatch**: Type a brief description of the dispatch in the "Form Title" field.
2. **Enter your contact information**: Fill in your email address and phone number in the "Dispatcher Email" and "Dispatcher Phone" fields, respectively.
3. **Enter the requester's contact information**: Enter the requester's email address and phone number in the "Requester Email" and "Requester Phone" fields, respectively.
4. **Provide equipment details**: Enter the type, description, and other relevant details about the equipment to be dispatched.
5. **Set the dispatch date and time**: Enter the date and time for the equipment dispatch.
6. **Specify the dispatch status**: Select the status of the dispatch from the options provided (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
7. **Enter additional equipment details**: Enter the equipment's serial number, model, and category.
8. **Add any notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments related to the dispatch.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title**: A brief description of the dispatch.
* **Dispatcher Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Dispatcher Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Requester Email**: Enter the requester's email address.
* **Requester Phone**: Enter the requester's phone number.
* **Equipment Type**: Select the type of equipment to be dispatched.
* **Equipment Description**: Enter a brief description of the equipment.
* **Dispatch Date**: Enter the date for the equipment dispatch.
* **Dispatch Time**: Enter the time for the equipment dispatch.
* **Dispatch Status**: Select the status of the dispatch from the options provided.
* **Requester Name**: Enter the requester's name.
* **Requester Department**: Enter the requester's department.
* **Dispatcher Name**: Enter your name.
* **Dispatcher Department**: Enter your department.
* **Equipment ID**: Enter the equipment's ID number.
* **Dispatch Location**: Enter the location where the equipment is to be dispatched.
* **Dispatch Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments related to the dispatch.
* **Request Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments from the requester.
* **Equipment Serial**: Enter the equipment's serial number.
* **Equipment Model**: Enter the equipment's model number.
* **Equipment Category**: Select the category of the equipment from the options provided.
* **Equipment Brand**: Enter the brand of the equipment.
* **Equipment Status**: Select the status of the equipment from the options provided.
* **Dispatch Date End**: Enter the end date for the equipment dispatch.
