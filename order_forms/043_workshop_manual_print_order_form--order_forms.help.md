# Workshop Manual Print Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to order workshop manuals for printing. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure your order is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2. If you need to order more than one manual, repeat the form for each manual.
3. Choose the correct delivery method for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Manual Print Order Form**: This field is the title of the form. You do not need to fill out this field.
* **Delivery Option**: Select how you would like to receive your order. Options are:
	+ Email: receive your order as an email attachment
	+ Email and Phone: receive your order as an email attachment and a phone call with instructions
* **Workshop Manual Specification**: Enter any special instructions or notes about the manual you are ordering. For example, if you need to have a specific section removed, mention it here.
* **Workshop Manual Quantity**: Enter the number of manuals you would like to order. This field is not required, but if you are ordering more than one manual, you will need to fill it out.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date you would like to have your order processed. This field is not required, but if you have a specific date in mind, enter it here.
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the date you would like to receive your order. This field is not required and will default to the order date.
* **Delivery Time**: Enter the time of day you would like to receive your order. This field is not required and will default to 00:00.
* **Delivery Method**: Select how you would like to receive your order. Options are:
	+ Print: receive a printed copy of your order
	+ Email: receive your order as an email attachment
	+ PDF: receive your order as a PDF attachment
* **Order Notes**: Enter any additional notes or instructions for your order. For example, if you need to have a specific section highlighted, mention it here.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This field is not required, but if you would like to be contacted about your order, enter it here.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number. This field is not required, but if you would like to be contacted by phone, enter it here.
