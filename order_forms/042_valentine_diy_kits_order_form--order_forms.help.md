# Valentine DIY Kits Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Valentine DIY Kits Order Form is used to collect information for custom Valentine's Day kits. This form is designed to gather details such as name, email, phone number, address, and other relevant information for processing orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your E-mail address in the format of `example@domain.com` in the "E-mail" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
6. Leave the "Note" field empty if you have no additional information to add.
7. Select the date you wish to order your kit in the "Date Ordered" field.
8. Select the time you wish to order your kit in the "Time Ordered" field.
9. Select the quantity of kits you wish to order from 1-10 in the "Quantity" field.
10. Choose your preferred color options for the kit in the "Options" field, selecting as many or as few as you like.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (id: 1)
* **First Name**: This field requires you to enter your given name.
* Required Field.

### Last Name (id: 2)
* **Last Name**: This field requires you to enter your surname.
* Required Field.

### E-mail (id: 3)
* **E-mail**: Enter your email address in the format `example@domain.com`.
* Required Field.

### Phone Number (id: 4)
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in the format of `XXX-XXX-XXXX`.
* Required Field.

### Address (id: 5)
* **Address**: Enter your full address where the kit will be shipped to.
* Required Field.

### Note (id: 6)
* **Note**: This field is optional and allows you to add any additional information about your order.
* Optional Field.

### Date Ordered (id: 7)
* **Date Ordered**: Select the date you want to order the kit on, in the format `MM/DD/YY`.
* Required Field.

### Time Ordered (id: 8)
* **Time Ordered**: Select the time you want to order the kit on, in 24-hour format `HH:MM`.
* Required Field.

### Quantity (id: 9)
* **Quantity**: Select the number of kits you want to order, from 1 to 10.
* Required Field.

### Options (id: 10)
* **Options**: Choose one or multiple colors for your kit:
	+ Red
	+ Blue
	+ Green
* Required Field.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* Ensure your address is complete and correct to avoid shipping issues.
* Select a valid date and time for your order.
* Choose your preferred color option(s) carefully, as they may affect the kit's design.
