# Holiday Cake Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holiday Cake Order Form is a digital form used to collect customer information for placing holiday cake orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of cake you would like to order (e.g., Vanilla, Chocolate, Strawberry, or Pumpkin).
2.  Choose the size of the cake that suits your needs (e.g., Small, Medium, or Large).
3.  Provide the desired delivery date for your order.
4.  Specify the delivery time for your order.
5.  Select a preferred payment method (e.g., Cash, Credit Card, or PayPal).
6.  Enter the payment amount (only if applicable).
7.  Add any additional comments or notes regarding your order.
8.  Provide your email address.
9.  Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cake Type** (`holiday_cake_form_fields`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select a delicious holiday cake flavor from the list (e.g., Vanilla, Chocolate, Strawberry, or Pumpkin). This field is optional.
* **Cake Size** (`holiday_cake_form_fields`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the size of the cake that suits your needs from the available options (e.g., Small, Medium, Large). This field is optional.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date when you would like to receive your holiday cake order. This field is optional.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, `time`, required/optional): Specify the time of day when you would like to receive your holiday cake order. This field is optional.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select how you would like to make your payment (e.g., Cash, Credit Card, or PayPal). This field is optional.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the payment amount if you have selected a payment method other than Credit Card or PayPal. This field is optional.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your order. This field is optional.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your order. This field is optional.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you regarding your order. This field is optional.

## Tips
- If you have any special requests or instructions for your order, please add them in the "Notes" field.
- Make sure to select a delivery date and time that suits your needs to ensure timely delivery.
- If you have any questions or concerns regarding your order, please do not hesitate to contact us using the provided contact information.
