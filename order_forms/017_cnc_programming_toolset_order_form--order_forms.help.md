# CNC Programming Toolset Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for the ordering of a CNC programming toolset. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure accurate ordering and delivery of the toolset.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields marked with *.
2. Select the correct toolset package that suits your needs.
3. Choose the software and hardware components required.
4. Provide accurate billing and shipping information.
5. Select the delivery method that suits your needs.
6. Choose your preferred payment method.
7. Review and accept the terms and conditions of sale.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, *): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, *): Enter your email address for order confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, *): Enter your best contact number.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your organization's name (if applicable).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): Enter your position.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, `text`, *): Enter your complete street address.
* **Billing City** (`billing_city`, `text`, *): Enter the city for your billing address.
* **Billing State** (`billing_state`, `text`, *): Enter the state or province for your billing address.
* **Billing ZIP Code** (`billing_zip`, `text`, *): Enter the postal code for your billing address.
* **Shipping Same as Billing** (`shipping_same_as_billing`, `select_one`, *): Select whether the shipping address is the same as the billing address.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the shipping address if it's different from the billing address.
* **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`, `text`, optional): Enter the city for the shipping address.
* **Shipping State** (`shipping_state`, `text`, optional): Enter the state or province for the shipping address.
* **Shipping ZIP Code** (`shipping_zip`, `text`, optional): Enter the postal code for the shipping address.
* **Toolset Package** (`toolset_package`, `select_one`, *): Select the toolset package that suits your needs.
* **Software Requirements** (`software_included`, `select_multiple`, *): Select the software you need for the toolset.
* **Hardware Components** (`hardware_components`, `select_multiple`, *): Select the physical tools you need for the toolset.
* **Machine Compatibility** (`machine_compatibility`, `text`, *): Enter the brand and model of your CNC machine.
* **Control System** (`control_system`, `select_one`, *): Select the brand of your CNC control system.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, *): Enter the number of toolsets you need.
* **Training Required** (`training_needed`, `select_one`, *): Select whether you need training included with your order.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, `select_one`, *): Select how you would like to receive your order.
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, `select_one`, optional): Select the shipping method if you need a physical shipment.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, *): Select your preferred payment method.
* **Purchase Order Number** (`purchase_order_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your PO number if applicable.
* **Estimated Total Amount** (`total_amount`, `text`, optional): This will be confirmed with the order total.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, optional): Enter any custom requirements.
* **Terms and Conditions Accepted** (`terms_accepted`, `select_one`, *): Accept the terms and conditions of sale.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any other information.

## Tips
* Double-check that you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If the shipping address is the same as the billing address, select "Shipping Same as Billing" to avoid duplicate entry.
* If you need a physical shipment, select the correct shipping method.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
