# driver_service_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The driver service inquiry form is designed to collect information for driver service inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps listed in the internal reasoning block.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User** (`1`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the "User" option that best describes your role in this inquiry.
* **Phone** (`2`, `text`, required): Provide your phone number.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Service Type** (`4`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of service you require.
* **PickUp Service** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required): If applicable, select "Yes" for pickup services.
* **Dropoff Service** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required): If applicable, select "Yes" for dropoff services.
* **Inquiry Details** (`7`, `text`, required): Provide detailed information about your inquiry.
* **Additional Comments** (`8`, `text`, required): If necessary, add additional comments about your inquiry.
* **Signature** (`9`, `note`, required): Add a signature to confirm your information.
* **Date** (`10`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are submitting the form.
* **Time** (`11`, `time`, required): Enter the time you are submitting the form.
* **Address** (`12`, `text`, required): Provide the address associated with your inquiry.
* **City** (`13`, `text`, required): Enter the city associated with your address.
* **State** (`14`, `text`, required): Enter the state associated with your address.
* **Zip** (`15`, `text`, required): Enter the zip code associated with your address.
* **Country** (`16`, `text`, required): Enter the country associated with your address.
* **Notes** (`17`, `text`, required): Add any relevant notes about your inquiry.
* **Assigned Tool** (`18`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if you have an assigned tool from the "Assigned Tool" options.
* **Notes 2** (`19`, `text`, required): Add any further comments or notes about your inquiry.
* **Signature 2** (`20`, `note`, required): Confirm if you have any further signature requirements from the "Signature 2" options.
* **Date 2** (`21`, `date`, required): Enter the date for the signature.
* **Time 2** (`22`, `time`, required): Enter the time for the signature.
* **Assigned Tool 2** (`23`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if you have an assigned tool 2 from the "Assigned Tool 2" options.
* **Email 2** (`24`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone 2** (`25`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
