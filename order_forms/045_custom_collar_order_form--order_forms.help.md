# Custom Collar Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Custom Collar Order Form is used to place an order for a custom collar from a customer. This form collects essential information to fulfill the order and establish contact with the customer.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select the Collar Type**: Choose the type of collar you want from the options provided (Type A, B, or C).
2. **Select the Collar Size**: Choose the size of the collar from the options provided (Small, Medium, or Large).
3. **Enter Customer Information**:
	* Enter the **Customer Name**.
	* Enter the **Customer Email** (a valid email address).
	* Enter the **Customer Phone** number.
4. **Submit the Form**: Once you have filled out all the required fields, click the submit button to send the order to the business or organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details (Page 1)** (`order_details`, text, not required): This is a text field where you can enter any additional information about your order.
* **Customer Info (Page 2)** (`customer_info`, text, not required): This is a text field for entering any relevant customer information.
* **Collar Type (Page 3)** (`collar_type`, select_one, not required): Choose the type of collar you want to order from the options provided.
* **Collar Size (Page 4)** (`collar_size`, select_one, not required): Choose the size of the collar you want to order from the options provided.
* **Customer Name (Page 5)** (`customer_name`, text, not required): Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Email (Page 6)** (`customer_email`, email, not required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone (Page 7)** (`customer_phone`, text, not required): Enter the customer's phone number.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all the fields correctly to ensure the order is processed correctly.
* If you have any special requests or instructions for the order, you can enter them in the **Order Details** field.
* Double-check the customer information for accuracy before submitting the form.
