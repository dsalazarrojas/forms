# down_payment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The down_payment_form is used to collect client information and payment details for clients who are paying for services or goods. This form helps to keep track of client payments and payment statuses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your client's information in the Client Info field.
2. Enter the payment amount in the Payment Amount field.
3. Select the payment due date from the calendar.
4. Choose the payment method (cash, credit card, or bank transfer).
5. Enter any additional payment details in the Payment Details field (optional).
6. Sign your signature in the Client Signature field.
7. Select the payment status (paid, pending, or declined).
8. Add any additional notes in the Additional Notes field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Info**: Enter your client's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
- **Payment Amount**: Enter the exact amount of the payment.
- **Payment Due Date**: Select the date by which the payment is due.
- **Payment Method**: Choose the method of payment (cash, credit card, or bank transfer).
- **Payment Details**: Enter any additional details about the payment, such as payment terms or conditions.
- **Client Signature**: Sign your signature to confirm the payment information.
- **Payment Status**: Select the status of the payment (paid, pending, or declined).
- **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the payment (optional).
