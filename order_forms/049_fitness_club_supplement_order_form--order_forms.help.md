# Fitness Club Supplement Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information for ordering supplements from a fitness club. It will guide you through the process of providing your details for a successful order.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Go through each page (User Information, Order Details, Payment Information, and Delivery Information) and fill out the required fields.
*   Enter your details accurately and completely in each section.
*   Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### User Information
*   **User Name** (<name>): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
*   **User Email** (<type>): Enter a valid email address you check regularly.
*   This field is not required but is recommended for communication purposes.

### Order Details
*   **Order Type** (<type>): Choose the type of supplement or product you want to order.
*   **Product Quantity** (<type>): Enter the quantity of the selected product you want to purchase.
*   **Special Instructions** (<type>): If you have specific requests or questions about your order, enter them here.
*   This field is not required but is useful for providing additional context about your order.

### Payment Information
*   **Card Number** (<type>): Enter your credit or debit card number (card type not shown).
*   **Expiration Date** (<type>): Enter the expiration date of your card (MM/YY format).
*   **Security Code** (<type>): Enter the security code found on your card's physical counterpart.
*   **Card Type** (<type>): Choose the type of your card (e.g., Visa, Mastercard).
*   **Zip/Postal Code** (<type>): Enter your card's zip or postal code.
*   **CVV** (<type>): Enter the 3 or 4-digit code found on the back of your card.
*   This field is required for payment processing.

### Delivery Information
*   **Delivery Address** (<type>): Enter your delivery address in the format 'Street Address', 'City', 'State', 'Zip'.
*   **Phone Number** (<type>): Enter a valid phone number for delivery purposes.
*   This field is not required but is useful for delivery purposes.

## Tips

*   Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   Make sure to fill out all required fields for successful order processing.
*   If you have any issues or questions, contact the fitness club's customer service department.
