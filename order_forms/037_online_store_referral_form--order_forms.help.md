# online_store_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect referrals from customers who have recommended your online store to others.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields with the customer's information.
2. Select the referral source and frequency for both the customer and store.
3. Add any additional comments or messages if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Enter the name of the customer who made the referral.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`): Enter the email address of the customer who made the referral.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`): Enter the phone number of the customer who made the referral (optional).
* **Customer Referral Link** (`customer_referral_link`): Enter any relevant link or URL associated with the customer's referral (optional).
* **Customer Referral Name** (`customer_referral_name`): Enter the name of the customer who was referred (optional).
* **Store Name** (`store_name`): Enter the name of your online store.
* **Store Email** (`store_email`): Enter the email address of your online store.
* **Store Phone** (`store_phone`): Enter the phone number of your online store.
* **Customer Referral Message** (`customer_referral_message`): Enter any additional comments from the customer about their referral (optional).
* **Store Referral Message** (`store_referral_message`): Enter any additional comments from your store about the referral (optional).
* **Form Submitted** (`form_submitted`): This field is for internal use only and is not visible to users.
* **Store Referral Link** (`store_referral_link`): Enter any relevant link or URL associated with your store's referral (optional).
* **Customer Referral Type** (`customer_referral_type`): Select how the customer was referred to your store, such as Online Store, Social Media, or Word of Mouth.
* **Customer Referral Source** (`customer_referral_source`): Select where the customer heard about your store, such as Friend, Family, or Colleague.
* **Customer Referral Frequency** (`customer_referral_frequency`): Select how often the customer refers to your store, such as Once, Several times, or Regularly.
* **Store Referral Frequency** (`store_referral_frequency`): Select how often customers refer to your store, such as Once, Several times, or Regularly.
* **Store Referral Source** (`store_referral_source`): Select where customers hear about your store, such as Friend, Family, or Colleague.
* **Store Referral Message To** (`store_referral_message_to`): Select who the referral message should be sent to, such as Customer, Customer Service Team, or Sales Team.
* **Store Referral Message To Other** (`store_referral_message_to_other`): Enter any additional comments or messages related to the referral (optional).
