### To prevent the spread of COVID-19, our organization has created a PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) Order Form to help organizations and businesses obtain the necessary protective gear. This form is designed to collect information about the order, including contact and shipping details, and the specific PPE items required. 

Before proceeding, please ensure that you are not duplicating orders and that your organization's needs are well-defined. This will help us process your order efficiently and effectively.

### COVID 19 PPE Order Form - Help Guide

### Purpose
This form is used to place orders for COVID-19 Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) for your organization. Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your order is processed correctly.

### How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the necessary information for the order, including the order date, order ID number, organization name, contact person's name, phone number, email address, and shipping address.
2. Select the organization type that best describes your organization.
3. Specify the quantity of each PPE item you require.
4. Choose the urgency of your order (Routine, Expedited, or Emergency).

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Date**: <date> - Enter the date for this order.
* **Order ID Number**: <text> - Enter a unique identifier for this order.
* **Organization Name**: <text> - Enter your organization's name as it appears on your logo or business card.
* **Organization Type**: <select_one> - Select the type of organization that best describes your organization (e.g., Hospital, Clinic, Nursing Home, School, Business, Government, Non-Profit, Other).
* **Contact Person Name**: <text> - Enter the name of the person responsible for this order.
* **Phone Number**: <text> - Enter the phone number of the person responsible for this order.
* **Email Address**: <email> - Enter the email address of the person responsible for this order.
* **Shipping Address**: <text> - Enter the shipping address for this order.
* **N95 Respirator Masks**: <number> - Enter the quantity of N95 respirator masks required.
* **Surgical Masks**: <number> - Enter the quantity of surgical masks required.
* **Face Shields**: <number> - Enter the quantity of face shields required.
* **Gloves**: <number> - Enter the quantity of gloves required.
* **Protective Gowns**: <number> - Enter the quantity of protective gowns required.
* **Head Covers**: <number> - Enter the quantity of head covers required.
* **Foot Covers**: <number> - Enter the quantity of foot covers required.
* **Safety Goggles**: <number> - Enter the quantity of safety goggles required.
* **Hand Sanitizer**: <number> - Enter the quantity of hand sanitizer required in liters.
* **Surface Disinfectant**: <number> - Enter the quantity of surface disinfectant required in liters.
* **Other PPE Items**: <text> - Enter any other PPE items required that are not listed above.
* **Required Delivery Date**: <date> - Enter the date by which you require delivery of your order.
* **Order Urgency**: <select_one> - Select the urgency level of your order (Routine, Expedited, or Emergency).
