# Simple Order Form By Jezreel Collections - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect general information about an order from a user. It is intended for a retail or e-commerce context and is meant to be simple and easy to fill out.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following fields:

*   **User Information** (User Information): This is the first section of the form. Please fill out your name.
*   **Order Information** (Order Information): This is the second section of the form. Please provide any relevant information about your order.
*   **Order Details** (Order Details): This is the third section of the form. Please provide any additional details about your order.
*   **Submit** (Submit): Once you have filled out all the relevant fields, click the "Submit" button to send your order information.

*   **Order Information (Order Information)**:
