# sports_t_shirt_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sports t-shirt order form is designed to collect information from customers to place an order for sports t-shirts. This form helps the team management gather the necessary details for the order, including customer information, order details, and team-related information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Customer Information**: Enter the customer's name, contact phone number, and email address in the respective fields. This information will help the team team contact the customer for any queries or updates.
2. **Team Information**: Select the team name and enter their contact information, which includes their email address, phone number, and address.
3. **Order Details**: Choose the color and size of the t-shirt, select the type of t-shirt (Hoodie or T-shirt), and enter the quantity of the t-shirts needed.
4. **Additional Information**: Provide any additional notes for the order if required.
5. **Confirm Order**: Review the order details and confirm them to proceed with the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: A text field to enter the customer's name.
* **Team Name**: A text field to enter the team name.
* **Order Date**: A date field to select the order date.
* **Order Time**: A time field to select the order time.
* **T-Shirt Information**: A text field to enter any additional information about the t-shirt order.
* **Shirt Color**: A dropdown menu to select the color of the t-shirt.
* **Shirt Size**: A checkbox menu to select the size of the t-shirt.
* **T-Shirt Type**: A checkbox menu to select the type of t-shirt (Hoodie or T-shirt).
* **Quantity**: A numerical field to enter the quantity of t-shirts needed.
* **Notes**: A text field for any additional notes for the order.
* **Order Number**: A dropdown menu to select the order number.
* **Team Contact**: A text field to enter the team contact person's name.
* **Team Phone**: A text field to enter the team phone number.
* **Team Email**: A text field to enter the team email address.
* **Team Address**: A text field to enter the team address.
* **Team City**: A text field to enter the team city.
* **Team State**: A text field to enter the team state.
* **Team Zip**: A text field to enter the team zip code.
* **Team Notes**: A text field for any additional notes about the team.
* **Player Names**: A text field to enter the names of players in the team.
* **Order Confirmation**: A text field for any additional notes or confirmation about the order.

**Tips**: Ensure you fill out all required fields to avoid any delays or issues with the order. If you have any doubts or queries, contact the team management for assistance.
