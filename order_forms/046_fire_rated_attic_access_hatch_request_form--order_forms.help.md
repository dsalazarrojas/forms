# Fire Rated Attic Access Hatch Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for fire-rated attic access hatch requests. It is intended to ensure that all necessary details are provided for the proper installation of the hatch.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter the fire-rated hatch specs in the "Fire Rated Hatch Specs" field.
3. Choose the installation location in the "Installation Location" field.
4. Select the fire rating for the hatch (Good or Fair) in the "Fire Rating" field.
5. If the hatch has a second fire rating, select the rating in the "Fire Rating 2" field.
6. Indicate whether the installation method will be "Yes" or "No" in the "Installation Method" field.
7. Enter any additional installation details in the "Installation Details" field.
8. Provide delivery details in the "Delivery Details" field.
9. Enter the name of the contact person in the "Contact Name" field.
10. Enter the contact person's email address in the "Contact Email" field.
11. Enter the contact person's phone number in the "Contact Phone" field (optional).
12. Enter any additional comments in the "Comments" field (optional).
13. Enter the name of the assigned user in the "Assigned User" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fire Rated Hatch Request** (`fire_rated_hatch_request`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the request.
* **Fire Rated Hatch Specs** (`fire_rated_hatch_specs`, text, required): Enter the specifications of the fire-rated hatch.
* **Installation Location** (`installation_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the hatch is to be installed.
* **Installation Date** (`installation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the installation.
* **Installation Time** (`installation_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the installation.
* **Fire Rating** (`fire_rating`, select_one, required): Choose the fire rating for the hatch (Good or Fair).
* **Fire Rating 2** (`fire_rating_2`, select_multiple, required): Select the second fire rating for the hatch (optional).
* **Installation Method** (`installation_method`, select_one, required): Indicate whether the installation method will be "Yes" or "No".
* **Installation Details** (`installation_details`, text, required): Enter any additional installation details.
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, text, required): Enter the delivery details for the installation.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person (optional).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, optional): Enter the name of the assigned user (optional).

Note: All fields marked with an asterisk (\*) are required. 

This form is designed to be completed by the person making the request. If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor for assistance. 

Please ensure that all fields are filled in before submitting the form.
