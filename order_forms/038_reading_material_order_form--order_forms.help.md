# reading_material_order_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for customers to place and manage orders for reading materials. It captures essential customer and order information to facilitate efficient order processing and communication with the customer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's information, including their name, email, and phone number (fields: 13, 14, and 15).
2. Select the delivery type (field: 12) and confirm the order (field: 5).
3. Enter the payment method(s) (field: 6).
4. Specify the book information, including type (field: 19).
5. Enter the order total and shipping cost (fields: 21 and 8).
6. Provide any special instructions or delivery information (fields: 20 and 23).
7. Add any additional order notes (field: 24).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Information** (field: 13): Enter the customer's name.
- **Contact Information** (field: 14): Enter the customer's email address.
- **Customer Phone** (field: 15): Enter the customer's phone number.
- **Shipping** (field: 3): Enter the shipping information.
- **Order Info** (field: 4): Enter any additional order details.
- **Confirm Order** (field: 5): Confirm the order details.
- **Payment Method** (field: 6): Select the payment method(s) (e.g., Cash, Credit, PayPal, Bank Transfer).
- **Book Info** (field: 7): Enter any book-related information.
- **Shipping Cost** (field: 8): Enter the shipping cost.
- **Delivery Info** (field: 9): Enter any delivery information.
- **Special Instructions** (field: 10): Enter any special instructions for the order.
- **Order Total** (field: 11): Enter the order total.
- **Delivery Type** (field: 12): Select the delivery type (e.g., Pickup, Delivery, Shipping).
- **Customer Name** (field: 13): Enter the customer's name.
- **Customer Email** (field: 14): Enter the customer's email address.
- **Customer Phone** (field: 15): Enter the customer's phone number.
- **Customer Notes** (field: 16): Enter any customer notes.
- **Order Date** (field: 17): Enter the order date.
- **Order Time** (field: 18): Enter the order time.
- **Book Type** (field: 19): Select the book type (e.g., Novel, Textbook, Other).
- **Customer Address** (field: 20): Enter the customer's address.
- **Order Total** (field: 21): Enter the order total.
- **Special Instructions** (field: 22): Enter any special instructions.
- **Delivery Info** (field: 23): Enter any delivery information.
- **Order Notes** (field: 24): Enter any order notes.
- **Form Completion** (field: 25): Enter any additional comments.

Note: This form includes fields for both customer and order information. Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure successful order processing.
