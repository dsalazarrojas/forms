# Website Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Website Order Form is used to collect information about a website, its features, and business details for website development, maintenance, or other related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the website domain in the "Website Domain" field.
2. Provide your business name and contact email in the "Business Name" and "Contact Email" fields, respectively.
3. Enter your business phone number in the "Business Phone" field.
4. Enter your business address in the "Business Address" field.
5. Specify the business city in the "Business City" field.
6. Select the business state in the "Business State" field.
7. Choose the business country in the "Business Country" field.
8. Fill in the website title in the "Website Title" field.
9. Describe the website in the "Website Description" field.
10. Enter relevant keywords for the website in the "Website Keywords" field.
11. Specify the target audience for the website in the "Website Target Audience" field.
12. List the technologies used on the website in the "Website Technologies" field.
13. Set the website deadline if applicable in the "Website Deadline" field.
14. Choose the website status as "Active" or "Inactive" in the "Website Status" field.
15. Select the website language from the available options in the "Website Language" field.
16. Enter the website URL in the "Website URL" field.
17. Fill in the domain name in the "Domain Name" field.
18. Select the domain extension (e.g., com, net, org) in the "Domain Extension" field.
19. Enter a subdomain if necessary in the "Subdomain" field.
20. Specify the website size in the "Website Size" field.
21. Describe the website layout in the "Website Layout" field.
22. Suggest a domain name if you have one in the "Domain Suggestion" field.
23. Describe your business in the "Business Description" field.
24. Select your business type in the "Business Type" field.
