# Sandwich Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a template for customers to provide their sandwich order details. It's intended for use in restaurants, cafes, or food establishments to collect customer orders efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Main Details** field (Field 2) with your name and contact information.
2. In the **Toppings** field (Field 3), select the toppings you'd like on your sandwich. You can choose from the available options or add your own.
3. In the **Beverages** field (Field 4), select the drinks you'd like to accompany your sandwich.
4. In the **Other Options** field (Field 5), specify any additional options or requests you have for your order.
5. In the **Order Details** field (Field 8), provide any comments or special requests you have for your order.
6. Review your order and submit it by clicking the **Submit** button (Field 6).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Details** (Field 2, text, required/optional): Enter your name and contact information to help us get in touch with you.
* **Toppings** (Field 3, text, optional): Select from the list of available toppings or add your own custom toppings.
* **Beverages** (Field 4, text, optional): Choose from the list of available drinks to pair with your sandwich.
* **Other Options** (Field 5, text, optional): Specify any special requests or custom options you'd like to include.
* **Order Details** (Field 7, text, optional): Add any comments or special requests for your order.
* **Submit** (Field 6, text, required/optional): Review and submit your order for processing.
* **Sandwich Order Comments** (Field 7, text, optional): Add any comments or feedback about your order.
