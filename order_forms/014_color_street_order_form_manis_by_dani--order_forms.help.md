# Color Street Order Form - Manis By Dani - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for customers to place orders for Color Street products on a monthly basis. It is intended for individuals or businesses seeking to purchase products regularly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your name, email address, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field.
3. In the "List the set names and quantities you want" field, list the specific sets you are interested in purchasing.
4. Enter the total number of sets you are ordering in the "Total Number of Sets" field.
5. Check if you have reached the "Buy 3 Get 1 Free" deal in the "Did you reach the Buy 3 Get 1 Free deal?" field.
6. Select your payment method and enter your payment handle in the "How will you be paying?" and "Payment Username" fields respectively.
7. Choose your shipping method in the "Shipping Method" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (monthly_customer_name, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (monthly_email, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (monthly_phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any necessary contact.
* **Shipping Address** (monthly_address, text, required): Enter your address where you would like your order shipped.
* **List the set names and quantities you want** (monthly_sets_list, text, required): Type in the names of the sets you are interested in purchasing and the quantity of each.
* **Total Number of Sets** (monthly_total_sets, number, required): Enter the total number of sets you are ordering.
* **Did you reach the Buy 3 Get 1 Free deal?** (monthly_b3g1, select_one, required): Check if you have reached the "Buy 3 Get 1 Free" deal.
* **How will you be paying?** (monthly_payment_method, select_one, required): Select from the options to indicate how you will be paying for your order.
* **Payment Username** (monthly_payment_handle, text, required): Enter your payment handle associated with your selected payment method.
* **Shipping Method** (monthly_shipping_choice, select_one, required): Select your preferred shipping method.
* **Special Instructions or Requests** (monthly_extra_notes, text, optional): Enter any special instructions or requests you may have.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your email address correctly for payment and shipping notifications.
* Check your shipping method carefully to ensure timely and cost-effective delivery.
* Double-check your order details before submitting to avoid errors.
