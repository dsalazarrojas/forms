# Statement Candle Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Statement Candle Order Form is used for customers to submit their order information for a statement candle. Please fill out this form to place your order and ensure that all required fields are completed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Click on the first page of the form, "Form Header", where you will see a brief introduction to the order form.
2. On the "Customer Info" page, please provide your name, which will be used as a reference for your order.
3. On the "Customer Address" page, enter your address details to ensure that your order can be delivered correctly.
4. On the "Customer Phone" page, enter your phone number for contact purposes.
5. On the "Order Details" page, select the options that best describe your order requirements.
6. On the "Delivery Info" page, select the delivery option that suits your needs.
7. On the "Special Requests" page, if you have any specific requirements for your order, please enter them here.
8. Finally, on the "Signature Required" page, sign and date your order to confirm your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header** (1, Note, Optional): This is the introduction page to the order form, providing a brief overview of the form and its purpose.
* **Customer Info** (2, Text, Optional): Enter your name to be used as a reference for your order.
* **Customer Address** (3, Text, Optional): Enter your address details to ensure correct delivery of your order.
* **Customer Phone** (4, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Order Details** (5, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the options that best describe your order requirements.
* **Delivery Info** (6, Select One, Optional): Select the delivery option that suits your needs.
* **Special Requests** (7, Text, Optional): If you have any specific requirements for your order, please enter them here.
* **Signature Required** (8, Text, Optional): Sign and date your order to confirm your submission.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed to avoid delays in processing your order.
* Double-check your order details before submitting to avoid any mistakes.
* If you are a repeat customer, refer to your previous order history to avoid duplicating efforts.
* Contact us if you have any questions or require further assistance.
