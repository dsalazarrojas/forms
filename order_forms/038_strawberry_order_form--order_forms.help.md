# strawberry_order_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for customers to submit an order for strawberries. To complete the form, please follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your name and email address.
2. Select the type of strawberry you want to order (Apple, Banana, Other, or Mixed).
3. Enter the quantity of strawberries you would like to order.
4. If you have any special message or instructions for the order, please enter it in the message field.
5. Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it should appear on the order receipt.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you with order details.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for additional contact (optional).
* **Address**: Enter your address for delivery or pickup (optional).
* **Strawberry Type**: Select the type of strawberry you would like to order (Apple, Banana, Other, or Mixed).
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of strawberries you would like to order.
* **Message**: Enter any special message or instruction for your order.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter your email address correctly to ensure we can contact you with order details.
- If you have any special requests or instructions for your order, please include them in the message field.
- Please note that the quantity field requires a numerical value.
