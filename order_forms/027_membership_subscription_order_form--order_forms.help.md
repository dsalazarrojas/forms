# membership_subscription_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a membership subscription order, enabling efficient and accurate processing of subscription requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the membership plan that best suits your needs.
2. Choose your preferred plan duration in months.
3. Enter the customer's name.
4. Provide the customer's email address.
5. Enter the customer's phone number.
6. Select your preferred payment method.
7. Optionally, select the payment gateway for your chosen payment method.
8. Enter any additional order notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Membership Subscription Order Form**: This is the title of the form.
* **Membership Plan**: Select the type of membership plan you'd like to subscribe to (monthly, yearly, biyearly).
* **Plan Duration (in months)**: Enter the duration of the membership plan in months.
* **Subscription Fee**: Enter the subscription fee for the selected plan. (Note: This field is likely to be auto-populated based on the chosen membership plan, but you may need to fill it out manually if it's not.)
* **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's name as it appears on their identification or as they would like to be known.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's valid email address.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you'd like to use (stripe, paypal, bank transfer).
* **Payment Gateway**: If you selected a payment method in the previous field, select the corresponding payment gateway (stripe payments, paypal payments). If you're unsure, you can leave this field blank.
* **Order Notes**: Enter any additional information or instructions about the order. This can be used by the support team to follow up on the order.
