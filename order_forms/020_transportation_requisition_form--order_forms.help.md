# Transportation Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transportation Requisition Form is used to request transportation-related resources for an event or project. It gathers details about the trip, vehicle needs, and other relevant information to ensure proper planning and execution of transportation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the trip details in the text box provided. This includes any relevant information about the trip, such as the location, purpose, and any specific requirements.
2. Select the vehicle needs by choosing the options that apply to your situation from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter the date of the trip.
4. Enter the time of the trip.
5. Provide a brief description of the trip.
6. Enter the email address of the person responsible for coordinating the trip.
7. Enter the phone number of the person responsible for coordinating the trip.
8. Select whether the trip has been assigned to someone or if it is not.
9. Select whether the trip has a dedicated tool or resource assigned to it.
10. Add any additional notes or comments about the trip.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trip details** (`trip_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the trip, including the location, purpose, and any specific requirements.
* **Vehicle needs** (`vehicle_needs`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the vehicle needs that apply to your situation. This includes options like "Yes" and "No".
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the trip.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the trip.
* **Description** (`description`, note, optional): Provide a brief description of the trip.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person responsible for coordinating the trip.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for coordinating the trip.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional): Select whether the trip has been assigned to someone or not.
* **Assigned tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select whether the trip has a dedicated tool or resource assigned to it.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the trip.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and clear information about the trip details, vehicle needs, and contact information.
* Double-check that you have selected all the necessary vehicle needs and assigned the trip to the correct person or tool.
* Use the description field to provide context about the trip.
* Use the notes field to add any additional information that may be helpful for the logistics team.
