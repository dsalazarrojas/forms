# Order Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Order Planning Form is designed to gather information from customers to facilitate efficient and effective order processing. It is intended for customers who have already been in contact with the company or have existing relationships.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Enter your details accurately and truthfully.
3. Fill out the form step by step, one page at a time.
4. Use the provided fields as guidance for what information to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_page (required: false)**: This is the first page of the form, which may contain introductory information or initial questions.
* **second_page (required: false)**: This is the second page of the form, which may contain additional questions or fields.
* **third_page (required: false)**: This is the third page of the form, which may contain more in-depth questions or fields.
 
## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask the company's sales team for guidance.
* The form is designed to be filled out one page at a time, so please take your time and ensure each page is complete before proceeding to the next.
