# Conveyor Roller Bearing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to guide customers through the process of placing an order for a conveyor roller bearing. Please fill out this form with the necessary information to ensure accurate and efficient processing of your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer information, including name and contact details.
2. Provide shipping address information for the order.
3. Select the type and size of the bearing needed.
4. Choose the quantity and unit of measure.
5. Choose the order priority and preferred shipping method.
6. Select the payment method and provide any additional payment details if needed.
7. Provide any additional comments or information relevant to the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name or company name.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your primary email address for order communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your best contact number.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter your business name if applicable.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required): Enter the full delivery address.
* **City** (`shipping_city`, text, required): Enter the city for delivery.
* **State or Province** (`shipping_state`, text, required): Enter the state or province for delivery.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`shipping_zip`, text, required): Enter the ZIP or postal code for delivery.
* **Country** (`shipping_country`, text, required): Enter the country for delivery.
* **Is Billing Address Same as Shipping** (`billing_same_as_shipping`, select_one, required): Choose whether the billing address is the same as the shipping address.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, text, optional): If the billing address is different from the shipping address, enter it here.
* **Bearing Type** (`bearing_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of bearing needed.
* **Bearing Model Number** (`bearing_model`, text, optional): Enter the specific model number if known.
* **Bearing Size** (`bearing_size`, text, optional): Enter the dimensions if known.
* **Roller Diameter** (`roller_diameter`, text, optional): Enter the diameter in mm or inches.
* **Roller Length** (`roller_length`, text, optional): Enter the length in mm or inches.
* **Shaft Diameter** (`shaft_diameter`, text, optional): Enter the compatible shaft size.
* **Quantity Ordered** (`quantity_ordered`, number, required): Enter the number of units ordered.
* **Unit of Measure** (`unit_of_measure`, select_one, required): Choose the unit of measure.
* **Application Type** (`application_type`, select_one, required): Choose the application type.
* **Environment Conditions** (`environment_conditions`, select_multiple, required): Choose the operating environment.
* **Material Preference** (`material_preference`, select_one, optional): Choose the material preference.
* **Seal Type** (`seal_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the seal type.
* **Order Priority** (`order_priority`, select_one, required): Choose the order priority.
* **Required Delivery Date** (`required_delivery_date`, date, optional): Enter the date needed by.
* **Preferred Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, select_one, required): Choose the preferred shipping method.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose the payment method.
* **Purchase Order Number** (`purchase_order_number`, text, optional): Enter the purchase order number if applicable.
* **Account Number** (`account_number`, text, optional): Enter the account number if you have one.
* **Technical Drawing** (`technical_drawing`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you have a technical drawing.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, optional): Enter any specific requirements.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any other information.

## Tips

* Please ensure you have all necessary information before proceeding.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact our support team for assistance.
* This form is designed to guide you through the process, but if you have any issues, contact us and we will be happy to help.
