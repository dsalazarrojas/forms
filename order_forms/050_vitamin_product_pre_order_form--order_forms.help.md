# Vitamin Product Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vitamin Product Pre Order Form is designed to collect information from customers who wish to pre-order a vitamin product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  Start by providing your **Customer Information**, including your **Customer Name**, **Customer Phone**, and **Customer Email**.
2.  In the **Delivery Information** section, enter your **Customer Address** and select your **Delivery Address** if applicable.
3.  In the **Order Items** section, choose which **item 1** to **item 15** you would like to order.
4.  In the **Order Details** section, enter any additional information about your order.
5.  If you have **Order Notes**, you can add them in this section as well.
6.  Please enter the **Order Total** for your order.
7.  In the **Delivery Preferences** section, choose your preferred **Delivery Date** and **Delivery Time**.
8.  If you have a specific **Customer Message** for the order, you can add it in this section.
9.  Finally, select who the **Assigned To** will be for your order from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Information**:
    *   **Customer Name** (text): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
    *   **Customer Phone** (text): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
    *   **Customer Email** (email): Enter your email address where we can reach you.
*   **Delivery Information**:
    *   **Delivery Date** (date): Please select the date you'd like your order delivered by.
*   **Order Items**:
    *   This field allows you to choose multiple items for your order. Select all the items you'd like to order.
*   **Order Details**:
    *   **Order Notes** (text): Add any additional comments or details about your order in this section.
    *   **Order Total** (number): Enter the total cost of your order.
*   **Order Notes**:
    *   Enter any additional comments or information about your order.
*   **Customer Address**:
    *   Enter your customer address for delivery purposes.
*   **Delivery Address**:
    *   Enter the address where you'd like to receive your order.
*   **Delivery Preferences**:
    *   **Delivery Date** (date): Please select the date you'd like your order delivered by.
    *   **Delivery Time** (time): Select your preferred delivery time.
*   **Customer Contact**:
    *   **Customer Phone** (text): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
*   **Delivery Date**:
    *   This field is for selecting the delivery date for your order.
*   **Delivery Time**:
    *   This field is for selecting the time of delivery for your order.
*   **Customer Message**:
    *   This is a section for any additional comments or messages for your order.
*   **Assigned To**:
    *   Select who will be assigned to handle your order from the options provided.

## Tips
Make sure all required fields are filled out correctly to avoid any errors in the order process. If you need assistance, do not hesitate to contact us.
