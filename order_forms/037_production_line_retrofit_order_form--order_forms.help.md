# Production Line Retrofit Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers about their production line retrofit orders. The form gathers essential details such as the production line number, retrofit date and time, and customer contact information to facilitate efficient processing and communication. Ensure you have the necessary permissions and authorizations before filling out this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields: Production Line Location and Production Line Status.
2. Select the correct Retrofit Date and Retrofit Time using the calendar and clock tools.
3. Enter the Customer Name and Customer Email.
4. Optionally, provide the Customer Phone number.
5. Review your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Production Line Number** (`production_line_number`, number, required): Enter the specific production line number that is being retrofitted.
* **Retrofit Date** (`retrofit_date`, date, required): Select the date when the retrofit is scheduled to take place.
* **Retrofit Time** (`retrofit_time`, time, required): Select the time when the retrofit is scheduled to take place.
* **Production Line Location** (`production_line_location`, select_one, required): Choose the correct production line location from the available options (100, 200, 300).
* **Production Line Status** (`production_line_status`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant production line statuses (Active, Inactive, Retired) that apply.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.

## Tips
* Double-check the Production Line Number to ensure it is accurate.
* Verify the correct Production Line Location and Status before submitting.
* Make sure to proofread for any errors before submitting the form.
