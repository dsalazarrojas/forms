# Tournament Refreshment Delivery Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tournament Refreshment Delivery Order Form is designed to collect information for tournament refreshment delivery orders. This form helps ensure that all necessary details are captured accurately to facilitate efficient order processing.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each page according to the field explanations below.
2. Review your answers carefully to ensure accuracy.
3. Submit the form once you've filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tournament Info** (`tournament_info`, required): Enter a brief introduction to the event or tournament.
* **Order Info** (`order_info`, required): Provide details about the order, including any special requirements.
* **Delivery Info** (`delivery_info`, required): Enter delivery information for the order.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, required): Enter payment details for the order.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, required): Add any additional order specifics.
* **Delivery Person** (`delivery_person`, optional): Identify the person receiving the delivery (if applicable).
* **Delivery Phone** (`delivery_phone`, optional): Enter the phone number of the person receiving the delivery (if applicable).
* **Delivery Email** (`delivery_email`, optional): Enter the email of the person receiving the delivery (if applicable).
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, required): Enter the name of the event or tournament.
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, required): Select the type of order (Food or Beverages).
* **Order Description** (`order_description`, optional): Provide a detailed description of the order.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, required): Enter the desired delivery time for the order.
* **Delivery Location** (`delivery_location`, required): Enter the location of the delivery.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, required): Select the payment method used for the order.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, required): Enter the total payment amount.
* **Additional Details** (`additional_details`, optional): Enter any additional information about the order.
* **Form 1** (`form_id_14486`, optional): Select Form 1 or Form 2 for the order.
* **Form 2** (`form_id_15659`, optional): Select Form 2 or Form 1 for the order.
* **Submit** (`submit`, note): Submit the form once you've filled out all required fields.
* **Error Message** (`error_message`, note): Displays any error message if the form is incomplete or invalid.
* **Success Message** (`success_message`, note): Displays a success message once the form is submitted successfully.

Please fill out the form carefully, ensuring all required fields are completed accurately, and submit it once you've finished.
