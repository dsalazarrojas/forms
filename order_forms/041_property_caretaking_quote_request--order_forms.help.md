# property_caretaking_quote_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect property caretaking quote requests from property owners. It's a simple form for gathering essential information about the property caretaking service quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as prompted. Each field has a specific purpose, and we'll explain each one below.
2. Please ensure that you have a clear understanding of what you're submitting before finalizing your quote request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Owner Info** (`property_owner_info`, text, optional): This field is for the property owner's contact information, including name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Quote Request** (`quote_request`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the property caretaking services you're requesting.
* **Quote Details** (`quote_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the quote, such as special requirements or requests.
* **Service Requirements** (`service_requirements`, text, optional): Describe the services you need from the caretaker, including any specific tasks or schedules.
* **Property Details** (`property_details`, text, optional): Share details about the property, like its location, size, or any unique features.
* **Service Quote** (`service_quote`, text, optional): Enter the amount or rate you're looking for in the quote.
* **Service Quote Details** (`service_quote_details`, text, optional): Add any additional information about the service quote, such as payment terms or conditions.
* **Property Caretaker Info** (`property_caretaker_info`, text, optional): Provide the contact information for the property caretaker, if applicable.
* **Caretaker Quote** (`caretaker_quote`, text, optional): Enter any additional information or comments from the property caretaker.
* **Quote Date** (`quote_date`, date, optional): Set the date you'd like the quote to be delivered or completed by.
* **Quote Time** (`quote_time`, time, optional): Specify the time you'd like the quote to be delivered or completed by.

## Tips
Please double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors or delays. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
