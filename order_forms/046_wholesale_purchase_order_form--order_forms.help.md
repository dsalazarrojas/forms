# Wholesale Purchase Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wholesale Purchase Order Form is designed to collect contact information, shipping details, purchase details, and payment details for wholesale purchases.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Contact Information
- Provide your company name, contact name, and contact number in the "Contact Information" section.
- Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately to help us process your order efficiently.

### Step 2: Shipping Details
- Enter your shipping address, city, state, and zip code in the "Shipping Details" section.
- Double-check your information to ensure it's correct, as this information is used for delivery.

### Step 3: Purchase Details
- Enter the product(s) you'd like to purchase and the quantity in the "Purchase Details" section.
- Be as specific as possible about the products you'd like to buy to avoid any confusion.

### Step 4: Payment Details
- Enter your payment method and any relevant payment information in the "Payment Details" section.
- Ensure your payment method is accurate to avoid any delays in processing your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Contact Information
- **Contact Name** (`Contact Name`, text, required): Enter your name or your company's name as the contact person for this order.
- **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for any further questions or updates.
- **Email** (`Email`, text, optional): Provide your email address for any additional information or updates about your order.

### Shipping Details
- **Shipping Address** (`Shipping Address`, text, required): Enter your shipping address where you'd like to receive your order.
- **City** (`City`, text, required): Enter the city for your shipping address.
- **State/Province** (`State/Province`, text, required): Enter the state or province for your shipping address.
- **Zip/Postal Code** (`Zip/Postal Code`, text, required): Enter the zip or postal code for your shipping address.

### Purchase Details
- **Products** (`Products`, text, required): Enter the product(s) you'd like to purchase and the quantity.
- **Product Description** (`Product Description`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the products you'd like to purchase.

### Payment Details
- **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, text, required): Enter your payment method (e.g., credit card, bank transfer).
- **Payment Information** (`Payment Information`, text, required): Enter any additional information about your payment method.
