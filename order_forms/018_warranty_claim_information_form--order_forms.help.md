# warranty_claim_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The warranty claim information form is used to collect information about a customer's warranty claim. It is essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that your claim is processed efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your **customer name** in the "Customer Name" field. This field is optional, but it helps us get in touch with you about your claim.
2. Enter your **customer email** in the "Customer Email" field. This is an optional field that we will use to communicate with you about your claim.
3. Provide the **product name** in the "Product Name" field. This is the name of the product that you are making a claim about.
4. Enter the **purchase date** of the product in the "Purchase Date" field. This is the date when you purchased the product.
5. Select the **warranty claim type** from the list provided. You can choose from "Physical Damage", "Manufacturing Defect", or "Other".
6. Provide a brief **description** of the issue you are experiencing with the product.
7. If applicable, upload **photos/videos** of the product or issue.
8. Attach **proof of purchase** documents, such as receipts or packaging materials.
9. Sign with your **customer signature** to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
10. Sign with your **business signature** (if applicable, for business customers).
11. Review the **warranty details** provided to ensure that you have filled out all required fields.
12. Check the **warranty agreement** box to confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions of the warranty.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted about your claim.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the product that you are making a claim about.
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when you purchased the product.
* **Warranty Claim Type** (`warranty_claim_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of claim you are making.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the issue you are experiencing with the product.
* **Photos/Videos** (`photos_videos`, select_multiple, optional): Select and upload photos or videos that illustrate the issue with the product.
* **Proof of Purchase** (`proof_of_purchase`, text, optional): Attach a copy of your purchase receipt or packaging material.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, text, optional): Sign with your signature to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Business Signature** (`business_signature`, text, optional): Sign with your business signature (if applicable).
* **Warranty Details** (`warranty_details`, note, optional): Review the warranty details provided and ensure that you have filled out all required fields.
* **Warranty Agreement** (`warranty_agreement`, select_multiple, optional): Check the box to confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions of the warranty.
