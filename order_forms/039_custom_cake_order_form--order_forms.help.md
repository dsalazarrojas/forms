# custom_cake_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The custom_cake_order_form is used to collect information for a custom cake order, ensuring that all required fields are filled out accurately.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
- Ensure that all fields with the label "Required" are filled out.
- Fill in the customer's name and date for the order.
- Select the desired cake type and size from the provided options.
- Enter the desired quantity and price of the cake.
- Enter any additional notes for the order, if needed.
- Select the payment method and confirm that the order has been accepted.
- Enter any payment details, payment total, and payment status.
- Choose the payment gateway to process the payment.
- Finally, confirm the accepted date and payment terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (Name, text, required): Enter the name of the customer who is placing the order.
- **Date** (Date, date, required): Select the date for the custom cake order.
- **Time** (Time, time, required): Choose the time for the custom cake order.
- **Cake Type** (Cake Type, select_one, required): Select the type of cake that the customer wants.
- **Quantity** (Quantity, number, required): Enter the desired number of cakes.
- **Notes** (Notes, note, required): Add any additional notes about the order.
- **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter the email address of the customer.
- **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter the phone number of the customer.
- **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_multiple, required): Choose the payment method to use.
- **Accepted** (Accepted, select_one, required): Confirm that the order is accepted.
- **Payment Terms** (Payment Terms, note, required): Enter the payment terms for the order.
- **Payment Details** (Payment Details, text, required): Enter any payment details.
- **Payment Total** (Payment Total, text, required): Enter the total payment amount.
- **Payment Status** (Payment Status, text, required): Confirm the payment status.
- **Payment Gateway** (Payment Gateway, select_one, required): Choose the payment gateway to use.
- **Accepted At** (Accepted At, date, required): Confirm the accepted date of the order.
- **Payment Terms At** (Payment Terms At, date, required): Confirm the payment terms date.
- **Payment Total At** (Payment Total At, date, required): Confirm the payment total date.
- **Cake Design** (Cake Design, text, required): Enter any additional design details for the cake.
- **Cake Size** (Cake Size, select_one, required): Choose the desired size of the cake.
- **Cake Flavor** (Cake Flavor, select_one, required): Select the flavor of the cake.
- **Cake Message** (Cake Message, text, required): Enter any message for the cake.
- **Cake Price** (Cake Price, text, required): Enter the price of the cake.
- **Cake Image** (Cake Image, text, required): Upload the image of the cake.
- **Payment Total** (Payment Total, text, required): Enter the total payment amount.
- **Payment Status** (Payment Status, text, required): Confirm the payment status.
- **Cake Image** (Cake Image, text, required): Upload the image of the cake.
