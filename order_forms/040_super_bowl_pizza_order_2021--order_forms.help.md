# Super Bowl Pizza Order 2021 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture information for customer pizza orders for the Super Bowl event. Customers can submit their orders using this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a date to place your order on the "Order Date" field.
2. Enter your name in the "Customer Name" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Customer Email" field.
5. Choose whether you want a pizza or not in the "Pizza Type" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to place your order. This will help us know when to prepare your pizza.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): This is your name, which we will use to identify you and prepare your order.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Pizza Type** (`pizza_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you want a pizza or not. If you choose 'Yes', we will prepare a pizza for you.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all fields carefully to ensure that your order is processed correctly.
* Double-check the order date to ensure that you don't forget to pick up your order.
