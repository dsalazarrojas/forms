# Hanukkah Gift Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a Hanukkah gift delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

* Fill in the recipient's details, including their name, email, and phone number.
* Select the desired delivery preferences, such as date and time of delivery.
* Enter the delivery location and any additional information about the gift.
* Enter your own contact information, including your email and phone number.
* Review the form for any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Recipient Details** (`recipient_details`): Enter the recipient's name, email, and phone number.
* **Preferences** (`preferences`): Select the desired delivery preferences, such as date and time of delivery.
* **Message** (`message`): Enter a personal message to be included with the gift.
* **Date of Delivery** (`date_of_delivery`): Select the date of the delivery.
* **Time of Delivery** (`time_of_delivery`): Select the time of the delivery.
* **Recipient Email** (`recipient_email`): Enter the recipient's email address.
* **Recipient Phone** (`recipient_phone`): Enter the recipient's phone number.
* **User Email** (`user_email`): Enter your own email address.
* **User Phone** (`user_phone`): Enter your own phone number.
* **Delivery Location** (`delivery_location`): Select the location of the delivery.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`): Enter any additional information about the gift or delivery.
* **Gift Details** (`gift_details`): Enter any details about the gift, such as its description or contents.
* **Recipient Notes** (`recipient_notes`): Enter any notes about the recipient.
* **User Notes** (`user_notes`): Enter any notes about yourself or the delivery process.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`): Enter any additional information about the order, such as its status or comments.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`): Select the method of delivery, such as "in-person" or "shipping".
* **Delivery Method Details** (`delivery_method_details`): Enter any additional information about the delivery method.
* **Delivery Notes** (`delivery_notes`): Enter any notes about the delivery process.
* **Order Number** (`order_number`): Enter the order number for reference.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`): Select the status of the order, such as "pending" or "delivered".
* **Order Status Details** (`order_status_details`): Enter any details about the order status.
* **Order Comments** (`order_comments`): Enter any comments about the order.
* **Delivery Status** (`delivery_status`): Select the status of the delivery, such as "pending" or "delivered".
* **Delivery Status Details** (`delivery_status_details`): Enter any details about the delivery status.
* **Gift Message** (`gift_message`): Enter a personal message to be included with the gift.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to avoid any potential issues with the delivery process.
* Double-check the recipient's details to ensure accuracy.
* Use the form to track and manage your delivery orders.
* Enter any additional information that may be helpful for the delivery process.
