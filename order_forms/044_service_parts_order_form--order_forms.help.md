# Service Parts Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to order service parts from a central service center. The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers about the parts they need and any additional details or comments related to their order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
* Enter the customer's address in the "Customer Address" field.
* Select the service parts needed from the list in the "Service Parts List" field.
* Enter the quantities of each part needed in the "Quantities" field.
* Provide any additional comments or information about the order in the "Comments" field.
* Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer E-mail" field.
* Enter the name of the service center that will fulfill the order in the "Service Center Name" field.
* Select the order date from the calendar in the "Order Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, required): Enter the customer's name as it appears on their official identification.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, required): Enter the customer's full address, including street address and any relevant postal codes.
* **Service Parts List** (`service_parts_list`, select multiple): Select one or more service parts from the available options.
	+ Part A: A specific service part available for order.
	+ Part B: A specific service part available for order.
	+ Part C: A specific service part available for order.
	+ Part D: A specific service part available for order.
	+ Part E: A specific service part available for order.
* **Quantities** (`quantities`, number): Enter the quantity of each selected service part needed.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note): Provide any additional comments or information about the order.
* **Customer E-mail** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Service Center Name** (`service_center_name`, required): Enter the name of the service center that will fulfill the order.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date): Select the date the order is to be fulfilled.
