# Taxi Service Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for booking a taxi ride with a service provider. Please provide your details and payment information to complete your booking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start with the first page of the form.
2. Enter your details in the **Customer Details** field.
3. Enter your payment information in the **Payment Info** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, optional): Enter your name, phone number, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, optional): Enter your payment method (e.g., credit card, cash) and any other relevant payment information.

## Tips
- Please make sure to enter accurate information to avoid any issues with your booking.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the taxi service provider.
