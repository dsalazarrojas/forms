# Graduation Lei Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Graduation Lei Order Form is designed to collect information from users who are ordering graduation lei for their school or university. This form will help the ordering process go smoothly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) below.
2. Make sure to select the correct delivery and payment options that apply to your order.
3. Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Graduation Lei Order Form** (1, note, not required): This is a placeholder field to help us understand your order purpose.
* **School Name** (2, text, not required): Enter the name of the school or university you are placing the order for.
* **Order Details** (3, text, not required): Please provide any additional details about your order, such as special requests or specific instructions.
* **Pickup/Delivery** (4, select_one, not required): Select either "Delivery" if you would like us to handle delivery or "Pickup" if you will pick up the order yourself.
* **Payment Method** (5, select_one, not required): Choose a payment method that suits your needs:
	+ **Credit Card**: Pay with your credit card (we accept Visa, Mastercard, and American Express).
	+ **Cash**: Pay cash upon delivery or pickup.
	+ **Invoice**: We will send you an invoice for payment.
* **Pickup Date** (6, date, not required): If you selected "Pickup", provide the date you would like to pick up your order.
* **Delivery Date** (7, date, not required): If you selected "Delivery", provide the date you would like the order delivered.
* **Delivery Name** (8, text, not required): If you are not picking up the order yourself, provide the name of the person who will be receiving the order.
* **Lei Size** (9, select_multiple, not required): Choose the size of the graduation lei:
	+ **Small**: A smaller graduation lei.
	+ **Medium**: A medium-sized graduation lei.
	+ **Large**: A larger graduation lei.
* **Lei Color** (10, select_one, not required): Select the color of the graduation lei:
	+ **Red**: A red graduation lei.
	+ **Blue**: A blue graduation lei.
	+ **Green**: A green graduation lei.
* **Graduation Date** (11, date, not required): Provide the date of graduation.
* **Order Total** (12, number, not required): This field is used for internal calculation purposes only.
* **School Address** (13, text, not required): If you are picking up the order, please provide the school address where you will be picking up the order.

Note: Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
