# Remodel Furnace Relocation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remodel Furnace Relocation form is used to gather information and schedule the relocation of a furnace in a building or home.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Review and understand the purpose of the form, as described above.
*   Fill out the form accurately and completely, providing all required information.
*   Ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals before proceeding with the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Upload File** (`upload_file`, `text`, optional): This field is not currently being used in this form, but if an attachment or a document is required for this form it would be used here.
*   **Schedule Renovation** (`schedule_renovation`, `date`, optional): Select the date and time for the furnace relocation.
*   **Provide Details** (`provide_details`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional details or notes about the furnace relocation.
*   **Furnace Type** (`furnace_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of furnace being relocated, such as 'type1', 'type2', or 'type3'.
*   **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the furnace relocation.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person for the furnace relocation.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person for the furnace relocation.
*   **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the contact person for the furnace relocation.
*   **Contact Message** (`contact_message`, `text`, optional): Enter a message to the contact person about the furnace relocation.
*   **Send Notification** (`send_notification`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether to send a notification to the contact person after completing the form.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that you have reviewed the relevant procedures and guidelines before filling out this form.
*   Review the form carefully and accurately to avoid any errors or miscommunication.
*   If you are unsure about any field or process, contact the relevant authorities or seek guidance from a supervisor or colleague.
