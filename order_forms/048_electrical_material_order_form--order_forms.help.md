# Electrical Material Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers or contacts who need to place an order for electrical materials. Please fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information to help us process your order efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form carefully before filling it out.
- Enter your company name and contact information (if it's not your personal order).
- Make sure to enter a clear description of the material you are ordering.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (Company Name, Text, optional): Enter your company's name or the name of the business or organization placing the order.
* **Contact First Name** (Contact First Name, Text, optional): Enter the first name of the contact person responsible for the order.
* **Contact Last Name** (Contact Last Name, Text, optional): Enter the last name of the contact person responsible for the order.
* **Phone** (Phone, Text, optional): Enter the contact person's phone number for communication.
* **Email** (Email, Email, optional): Enter the contact person's email address for communication.
* **Material Description** (Material Description, Text, optional): Enter a clear and detailed description of the material you are ordering. This will help us process your order correctly.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy.
- If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, contact us.
- Please use the same form for each order you place. Do not reuse or modify the form for different orders.
