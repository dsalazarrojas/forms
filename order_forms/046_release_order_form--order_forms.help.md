**Release Order Form - Help Guide**
================-----------

### Purpose

The Release Order Form is a document that helps us manage and track the sending or delivery of goods or assets to authorized recipients.

### How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*). This form requires no fields to be filled out to submit it.
2. Enter all the relevant details about the release in the corresponding fields.
3. Ensure all fields have accurate and up-to-date information.
4. Double-check for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Form**: A free-text field to enter any relevant information about the release.
* **Release Details**: A field to enter any specific details about the release, such as the product or asset being released.
* **Release Status**: A field to track the status of the release, such as "pending", "in progress", or "completed".
* **Recipient Details**: A field to enter information about the recipient, including their email and phone number.
* **Release Authorization**: A field to enter any necessary release authorization or approval information.
* **Release Notes**: A field for any additional comments or notes about the release.
* **Request to Receipt**: A field to track the request to receipt process, including any relevant dates and times.
* **Date of Release**: A field to enter the date the release was made or should be made.
* **Release Date**: A field to enter the date the release was planned or scheduled.
* **Release Time**: A field to enter the time the release was made or should be made.
* **Recipient Email**: A field to enter the recipient's email address.
* **Recipient Phone**: A field to enter the recipient's phone number.
* **Release Form Status**: A field to track the status of the release form itself, such as "submitted", "in progress", or "completed".
