# Catering Service Menu Selection - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help clients select and customize their catering menu for events such as weddings, corporate events, and other gatherings. It helps us understand their preferences, requirements, and expectations to provide a tailored catering service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information (Client Name, Email Address, and Phone Number).
2. Provide event details (Event Name, Date, and Number of Guests).
3. Choose the service style and meal time for your event.
4. Select the main course options and sides for your event.
5. Choose the beverage package and dessert options.
6. Specify any special requests or requirements, such as dietary restrictions or custom cocktails.
7. Add any additional comments or information about your event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name as the client or contact person.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter your email address for communication and event updates.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number for contact and coordination.
* **Event Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the event or occasion.
* **Event Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Guest Count** (Number, Required): Enter the expected number of guests.
* **Service Style** (Select One, Required): Choose how you want to be served (e.g., Buffet, Plated Dinner, Family Style, etc.).
* **Meal Time** (Select One, Required): Choose when you want to have the meal (e.g., Breakfast, Brunch, Lunch, Dinner, etc.).
* **Appetizer Selection** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose from the list of appetizer options. You can select multiple options.
* **Number of Appetizers** (Number, Optional): Enter the number of appetizer varieties you want to choose.
* **Soup or Salad** (Select One, Optional): Choose the first course (e.g., Caesar Salad, Garden Salad, etc.).
* **Main Course Option 1** (Select One, Required): Choose the first main course option.
* **Main Course Option 2** (Select One, Optional): Choose the second main course option.
* **Side Dishes** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose from the list of side dish options. You can select multiple options.
* **Dessert Selection** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose from the list of dessert options. You can select multiple options.
* **Beverage Package** (Select One, Required): Choose the beverage package (e.g., Non-Alcoholic Only, Beer and Wine, etc.).
* **Signature Cocktail Request** (Text, Optional): If you have a custom cocktail request, enter the name and ingredients here.
* **Dietary Requirements** (Text, Optional): If you have any dietary restrictions, enter them here (e.g., vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, etc.).
* **Vegetarian Meals Needed** (Number, Optional): Enter the number of vegetarian meals required.
* **Vegan Meals Needed** (Number, Optional): Enter the number of vegan meals required.
* **Gluten-Free Meals Needed** (Number, Optional): Enter the number of gluten-free meals required.
* **Kids Menu Needed** (Number, Optional): Enter the number of children's menu options needed.
* **Tasting Session Requested** (Select One, Optional): If you want to have a tasting session, choose 'True'.
* **Special Requests** (Text, Optional): If you have any special requests or requirements, enter them here.
* **Additional Comments** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your event.

**Tips**

* Make sure to review and double-check your selections before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to contact us.
* We'll do our best to accommodate your requests and preferences.
