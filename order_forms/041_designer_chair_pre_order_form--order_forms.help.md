# Designer Chair Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place an order for a designer chair. It collects information about the chair, delivery preferences, and customer contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the model you would like to order from the "Select Model" dropdown menu.
2. Choose your preferred delivery options from the "Delivery Preferences" dropdown menu.
3. Enter your deposit amount (if applicable).
4. Select the delivery date for your order.
5. Add any additional comments about your order.
6. Enter your contact information, including name, phone number, and address.
7. Provide your email address.
8. Enter any additional comments about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Model** (`model_selection`, select_one, required: false): Choose the model of designer chair you would like to order.
* **Delivery Preferences** (`delivery_preferences`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the delivery preference that best suits your needs, such as in-store pickup or in-home delivery.
* **Deposit** (`deposit`, number, required: false): Enter the deposit amount for your order (if applicable).
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required: false): Select the date for your order delivery.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments about your order.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your name, phone number, and address to ensure smooth communication.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Provide your email address for contact purposes.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or questions about your order.
