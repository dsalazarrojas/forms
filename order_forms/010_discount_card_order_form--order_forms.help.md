# Discount Card Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Discount Card Order Form is used to collect customer information for our discount card order process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the first name field.
2. Enter your last name in the last name field.
3. Provide your email address in the email field.
4. Select your business type from the options provided in the business type field.
5. Choose your preferred discount type by selecting one or more options from the discount type field.
6. Enter your phone number in the phone field.
7. Enter your street address in the street field.
8. Enter your city in the city field.
9. Enter your state in the state field.
10. Enter your zip code in the zip field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_one, optional): Select your type of business from the options provided.
* **Discount Type** (`discount_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more discount types by selecting from the options provided.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Street** (`street`, text, optional): Enter your street address.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city name.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter your state name.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip code.
