# Beverage Logistics Compliance Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beverage Logistics Compliance Order Form is used to gather essential information for businesses ordering beverages. This form ensures that all necessary details are collected to facilitate a smooth and compliant ordering process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your company name and contact person's name, email, and phone number.
2. Enter your business license number and tax ID number.
3. Provide your delivery address, city, state, and ZIP code.
4. Select the type of beverages you are ordering (alcoholic, non-alcoholic, or mixed selection).
5. List the products you want to order and specify the quantities.
6. Choose a preferred delivery date and time.
7. Confirm that you are authorized to order and acknowledge compliance requirements.
8. Indicate your storage compliance status.
9. Select your preferred payment method.
10. If applicable, provide a purchase order number.
11. Add any special delivery instructions.
12. Provide any additional comments about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your business name as it appears on your business license.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the contact person handling this order.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address where you want to receive order confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number for order coordination.
* **Business License Number** (`license_number`, text, required): Enter your business license or permit number.
* **Tax ID Number** (`tax_id`, text, required): Enter your business tax identification number.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, required): Enter your full delivery address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city for delivery.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state for delivery.
* **ZIP Code** (`postal_code`, text, required): Enter the postal code for delivery.
* **Beverage Category** (`beverage_category`, select_one, required): Select the type of beverages you are ordering (alcoholic, non-alcoholic, or mixed selection).
* **Order Items** (`order_items`, text, required): List the products you want to order.
* **Quantities** (`quantities`, text, required): Specify the quantity for each item.
* **Preferred Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required): Choose a date for delivery.
* **Preferred Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, optional): Choose a time that works best for delivery (if not specified, it will be determined by our team).
* **Age Verification Statement** (`age_verification`, select_one, required): Confirm that you are authorized to order beverages.
* **Compliance Acknowledgment** (`compliance_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Acknowledge that you understand and will comply with all applicable laws and regulations.
* **Storage Compliance** (`storage_compliance`, select_one, required): Indicate your storage compliance status.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method.
* **Purchase Order Number** (`purchase_order_number`, text, optional): If you have a purchase order number, enter it here.
* **Special Delivery Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, optional): Add any special delivery requirements.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about your order.

Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth and compliant ordering process.
