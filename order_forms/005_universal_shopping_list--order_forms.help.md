# Universal Shopping List - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Universal Shopping List form allows you to add, manage, and track items you need to purchase from a store or other retail outlet. Fill out the form with details such as item name, quantity, price, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the item you wish to add to your shopping list.
2. Specify the quantity of the item you need.
3. Enter the price of the item.
4. Select the date you need the item by.
5. Choose a time of day for delivery or pickup.
6. Add a description of the item (optional).
7. Include any additional notes about the item (optional).
8. Enter contact information, such as email and phone number (if applicable).
9. Select a category for the item from the available options.
10. Add tags to further categorize the item (optional).
11. Choose the status of the item (e.g., new, restocked, etc.).
12. Select the priority level of the item (e.g., high, low, etc.).
13. Add additional tags to further categorize the item (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Item Name**: Enter the name of the item you wish to add to your shopping list.
* **Item Quantity**: Enter the number of items you need to purchase.
* **Item Price**: Enter the price of the item.
* **Date**: Select the date you need the item by.
* **Time**: Choose a time of day for delivery or pickup.
* **Description**: Add a brief description of the item (if applicable).
* **Notes**: Include any additional notes about the item (if applicable).
* **Email**: Enter your contact email address (if applicable).
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number (if applicable).
* **Category**: Select a category for the item from the available options.
* **Tags**: Add tags to further categorize the item (if applicable).
* **Status**: Choose the status of the item (e.g., new, restocked, etc.).
* **Priority**: Select the priority level of the item (e.g., high, low, etc.).
* **Tags 2**: Add additional tags to further categorize the item (if applicable).
* **Tags 2**: This field is a duplicate of the "Tags" field, intended for further categorization.

Note: The form is designed to be user-friendly, but it's recommended to double-check that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
