# Market Fee Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Market Fee Payment Form is a form used to collect payment information for a market fee payment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Event Title** in the first field. This is the title of the event for which you are paying the market fee.
2. Enter the **Market Fee** in the second field. This is the amount of the market fee you are paying.
3. Enter your **Vendor Name** in the third field. This is the name of the vendor for whom you are paying the market fee.
4. Select the **Payment Method** by choosing from the options "Card" or "Bank Transfer".
5. Enter your **Card Number** in the fourth field. This is the 16-digit number on your credit or debit card.
6. Enter your **Card Expiration** in the fifth field. This is the expiration date of your credit or debit card, in the format MM/YY.
7. Enter your **Card CVV** in the sixth field. This is the three-digit code on the back of your credit or debit card.
8. You can optionally enter **Bank Transfer Details** in the seventh field, if you are using bank transfer as your payment method.
9. Click the **Submit** button to complete the form and submit your payment information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Title** (text, required): This is the title of the event for which you are paying the market fee.
* **Market Fee** (number, required): This is the amount of the market fee you are paying.
* **Vendor Name** (text, required): This is the name of the vendor for whom you are paying the market fee.
* **Payment Method** (select_multiple, required): This is the method of payment you are using to pay the market fee. You can choose from "Card" or "Bank Transfer".
* **Card Number** (text, required): This is the 16-digit number on your credit or debit card.
* **Card Expiration** (text, required): This is the expiration date of your credit or debit card, in the format MM/YY.
* **Card CVV** (text, required): This is the three-digit code on the back of your credit or debit card.
* **Bank Transfer Details** (text, optional): This is the details of your bank transfer payment method. (Note: Only required for Bank Transfer payment method)
* **Submit** (note, required): This is the button to submit your payment information.

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly and accurately to avoid delays in processing your payment. If you have any questions or issues, please contact our customer support team for assistance.
