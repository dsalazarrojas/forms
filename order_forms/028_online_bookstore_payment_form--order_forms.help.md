# Online Bookstore Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect payment information from customers for online bookstore transactions. Please fill out this form to complete your purchase.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your order details in the "Order Details" field.
2. Select your delivery preferences from the "Delivery Preferences" field.
3. Enter your payment information in the "Payment Info" field.
4. Choose your payment method from the "Payment Method" field.
5. Enter the order total in the "Order Total" field.
6. Select a delivery date from the "Delivery Date" field.
7. Select a delivery time from the "Delivery Time" field.
8. Provide any additional delivery notes in the "Delivery Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details**: Enter a brief description of your order, including the item(s) purchased and any specific instructions.
* **Delivery Preferences**: Select your preferred delivery method (e.g., in-store pickup, delivery to home or office).
* **Payment Info**: Enter your payment information, including credit card details or other accepted payment methods.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you would like to pay for your order (e.g., credit card, PayPal, etc.).
* **Order Total**: Enter the total amount of your order.
* **Delivery Date**: Select the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Delivery Time**: Select the time slot you would like to receive your order.
* **Delivery Note**: Provide any additional information or special requests you have for your delivery.
