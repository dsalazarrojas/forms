# Recital Program Dedication Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to input information for an order or performance submission. Please follow the steps below to complete the form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out each section with the required information.
2. Review your input before submitting.
3. Save or cancel your submission as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Form** (`order_form`): This is the main section where you input your order details.
* **Performer Data** (`performer_data`): Enter information about the performer(s) involved in the performance.
* **Performance Details** (`performance_details`): Provide more details about the performance.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): Enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
* **Performance Info** (`performance_info`): Enter any additional performance-related information.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`): Enter specific details about the order.
* **Order Summary** (`order_summary`): Summarize your order to ensure it's accurate.
* **Submit**: Submit your order.
* **Cancel**: Cancel your submission.
* **Save**: Save your submission for later.
* **Back**: Go back to a previous page.
* **Next**: Go to the next page.
* **Help**: Get help with the form.
* **Save & Print**: Save and print your submission.
* **Save as Draft**: Save your submission as a draft.
* **Submit & Download**: Submit and download your form.
* **Preview**: Preview your submission.
* **Cancel Submission**: Cancel your submission.
* **Reset**: Reset the form.
* **Confirm**: Confirm your submission.
* **Save and Exit**: Save and exit the form.
* **Save as PDF**: Save your submission as a PDF.
* **View Preview**: View a preview of your submission.
