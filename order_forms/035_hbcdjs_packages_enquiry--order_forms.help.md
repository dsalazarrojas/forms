# Hbcdjs Packages Enquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Hbcdjs Packages Enquiry" form is designed to help the Hbcdjs team gather information from customers who are interested in purchasing or inquiring about packages. This form is used to collect details about the customer's preferences and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the enquiry form with your information and preferences.
2. Select the options for "package_selection" and "package_type" that best match your needs.
3. Enter your name, email, phone number, and any other relevant information in the corresponding fields.
4. Choose a suitable date and time for your enquiry.
5. Optionally, provide a brief description of your enquiry in the "description" field.
6. Review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all fields are filled out accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Enquiry Form
* **enquiry_form** (`enquiry_form`, text, optional): Enter a brief message about your enquiry.
### Package Selection
* **package_selection** (`package_selection`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more options that best match your needs. 
### Name
* **name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your full name.
### Email
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address.
### Phone
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
### Date
* **date** (`date`, date, optional): Choose a date for your enquiry.
### Start Time
* **start_time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Choose a start time for your enquiry.
### End Time
* **end_time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Choose an end time for your enquiry.
### Package Type
* **package_type** (`package_type`, select_one, optional): Select a package type that best suits your needs.
### Description
* **description** (`description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your enquiry.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to reach out to the Hbcdjs team.
* Please fill out the form only once per customer.
