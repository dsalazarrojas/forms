# University Sports Uniforms Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Sports Uniforms Requisition Form is used to collect information for the university sports uniform requisition process. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that your sports uniform needs are met.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required and optional information.
2. Ensure that the Team Name and Sport Name fields are filled out as they are required.
3. Enter the quantity of uniforms you need.
4. Select the correct size and color of the uniform you need.
5. If necessary, select the sleeve type for the uniform.
6. Enter the date you would like to receive the uniforms by.
7. Fill out your contact information (Name, Email, and Phone Number) so that we can contact you regarding your uniform order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the team you are requesting the uniform for.
* **Sport Name** (`sport_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the sport or activity the team is for (e.g., Football, Basketball, etc.).
* **Team Number** (`team_number`, number, optional): Enter the team number (if applicable).
* **Sport Number** (`sport_number`, number, optional): Enter the sport number (if applicable).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the number of uniforms you need.
* **Size** (`size`, select_multiple, required): Select the size of the uniform you need from the available options (Extra Small, Small, Medium, Large, Extra Large).
* **Color** (`color`, select_one, required): Select the color of the uniform you need.
* **Sleeve Type** (`sleeve_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the sleeve type of the uniform you need (Short Sleeve, Long Sleeve, None).
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to receive the uniforms by.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department you are affiliated with.
