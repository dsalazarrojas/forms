# Soft Play Rental Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soft Play Rental Agreement Form is a document designed to collect information from customers for a soft play rental service. It is used to gather details about the customer, event, rental, and payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details, such as name and contact information.
2. Provide information about the event you are renting, including the date and time.
3. Specify the rental details, including the equipment or services you are interested in renting.
4. Choose your preferred payment method and contact method.
5. Confirm your rental confirmation and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details** (customer_details): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Event Details** (event_details): Provide details about the event you are renting for.
* **Rental Terms** (rental_terms): Specify the rental details, including equipment or services.
* **Payment Details** (payment_details): Enter payment information for your rental.
* **Contact Details** (contact_details): Enter your contact information for communication.
* **Agreement Terms** (agreement_terms): Read and agree to the rental agreement terms and conditions.
* **Payment Confirmation** (payment_confirmation): Choose the payment method for your rental.
* **Contact Confirmation** (contact_confirmation): Choose the contact method you prefer.
* **Rental Confirmation** (rental_confirmation): Confirm your rental preferences.
* **Terms and Conditions** (terms_and_conditions): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the rental service.
* **Signature** (signature): Sign and date the agreement.
* **Payment Terms** (payment_terms): Enter any additional payment information or instructions.
* **Payment Method 1**, **Payment Method 2** (payment_confirmation): Choose from available payment methods.
* **Contact Method 1**, **Contact Method 2**, **Contact Method 3** (contact_confirmation): Choose from available contact methods.
* **Rental Method 1**, **Rental Method 2** (rental_confirmation): Choose from available rental methods.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth rental process.
* Review the terms and conditions carefully before signing.
* Contact the rental service if you have any questions or concerns.
* Keep a copy of the signed agreement for your records.
