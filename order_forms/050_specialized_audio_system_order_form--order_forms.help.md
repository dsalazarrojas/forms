# Specialized Audio System Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help customers place an order for specialized audio systems, providing a tailored solution for their specific needs.
## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button when you are ready to place your order.
2. Enter your form information in the fields provided.
3. Select your preferred options for "customer_preferences" (Audio, Visual, or Both).
4. Enter your order details in the "order_details" field.
5. Provide your "customer_name" and "customer_email" to receive updates and communication related to your order.
6. Choose a "payment_gateway" (PayPal, Stripe, or Bank Transfer) for processing your payment.
7. Select the "order_status" (Open for Processing, In Progress, Shipped, or Cancelled) to reflect the current status of your order.
8. Leave any additional "order_notes" regarding your order in the "order_notes" field.
9. Enter your "phone_number" to ensure we can contact you regarding your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Information** (`form_information`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your order.
* **Customer Preferences** (`customer_preferences`, select multiple, optional): Select the type of content you require for your order (Audio, Visual, or Both).
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the order you are placing.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Payment Gateway** (`payment_gateway`, select one, optional): Choose how you want to pay for your order (PayPal, Stripe, or Bank Transfer).
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select one, optional): Select the current status of your order (Open for Processing, In Progress, Shipped, or Cancelled).
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes regarding your order.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you regarding your order.
