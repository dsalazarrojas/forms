# Generic Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Generic Product Order Form is used to gather information from customers for placing orders for generic products. This form is crucial in processing and fulfilling customer orders efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your customer details.
2. Select the product details you'd like to order.
3. Choose your preferred payment method.
4. Enter any additional customer information you'd like to share.
5. Select the date and time range for the order.
6. Enter your customer's address.
7. Enter your order address.
8. Provide any customer comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details** (`Customer Details`, text, required): Enter your name and contact information here.
* **Product Details** (`Product Details`, text, required): Select the product you'd like to order.
* **Payment Details** (`Payment Method`, select one, required): Choose how you'd like to pay for your order (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Customer Information** (`Customer Info`, select multiple, required): Select any relevant customer information (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Other Information** (`Other Info`, select multiple, required): Select any additional information about your order (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Order Date and Time** (`Order Date and Time`, date and time, required): Enter the date and time you'd like to place your order (Order Date Range and Order Time Range).
* **Customer Address** (`Customer Address`, text, required): Enter your customer address for delivery or shipping.
* **Order Address** (`Order Address`, text, required): Enter the address where the order should be delivered or shipped.
* **Customer City** (`Customer City`, text, required): Enter the city for your customer address.
* **Order City** (`Order City`, text, required): Enter the city for the order address.
* **Customer State** (`Customer State`, text, required): Enter the state for your customer address.
* **Order State** (`Order State`, text, required): Enter the state for the order address.
