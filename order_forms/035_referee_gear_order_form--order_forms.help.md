# Referee Gear Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Referee Gear Order Form is designed to help referees order equipment for events or competitions. This form collects essential contact, shipping, and payment information to ensure a smooth ordering process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your contact information, including your name and email address.
2. Select a shipping method (Ground, Air, or Other).
3. Provide shipping details, such as your address and any additional comments.
4. Indicate whether you want to order equipment (Yes or No).
5. Indicate whether you want to order uniforms (Yes or No).
6. Specify your payment terms (I have read and agreed or I have not read and agreed).
7. Enter your shipping cost (a numerical value).
8. Select your shipping cost currency (USD, EUR, CAD, AUD, JPY, CNY).
9. Enter any additional shipping details, such as your city and state.
10. Enter your contact phone number.
11. Review and confirm your order details before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your official email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Shipping Address 1** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter your shipping address.
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, select_one, optional): Select a shipping method (Ground, Air, or Other).
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional shipping details.
* **Equipment Order** (`equipment_order`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you want to order equipment (Yes or No).
* **Uniform Order** (`uniform_order`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you want to order uniforms (Yes or No).
* **Shipping Terms** (`shipping_terms`, text, optional): Enter any shipping terms or conditions.
* **Order Terms** (`order_terms`, select_one, optional): Specify your order terms (I have read and agreed or I have not read and agreed).
* **Shipping Cost** (`shipping_cost`, number, optional): Enter your shipping cost (a numerical value).
* **Shipping Cost Currency** (`shipping_cost_currency`, select_one, optional): Select your shipping cost currency (USD, EUR, CAD, AUD, JPY, CNY).
* **Shipping Address 2** (`shipping_address_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional shipping address details.
* **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`, text, optional): Enter your shipping city.
* **Shipping State** (`shipping_state`, select_one, optional): Select your shipping state (Yes or No).
* **Shipping Zip** (`shipping_zip`, text, optional): Enter your shipping zip code.
* **Shipping Country** (`shipping_country`, text, optional): Enter your shipping country.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, optional): Enter any payment terms or conditions.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, optional): Enter your payment details.
* **Payment Terms 2** (`payment_terms_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional payment terms or conditions.
* **Shipping Method 2** (`shipping_method_2`, select_one, optional): Select a shipping method (Ground, Air, or Other).
* **Shipping Terms 2** (`shipping_terms_2`, text, optional): Enter any shipping terms or conditions.
* **Order Terms 2** (`order_terms_2`, select_one, optional): Specify your order terms (I have read and agreed or I have not read and agreed).
* **Expected Shipping Date** (`shipping_date`, date, optional): Enter your expected shipping date.
* **Payment Currency** (`payment_terms_2`, text, optional): Enter any payment currency information.
