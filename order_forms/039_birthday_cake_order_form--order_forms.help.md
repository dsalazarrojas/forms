# Birthday Cake Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Birthday Cake Order Form is designed to collect information from customers to place a custom order for a birthday cake. The form is divided into several sections to make it easy for customers to provide all the required details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the required information.
2. Fill in the "Customer Information" section with your name, email, phone number, and address.
3. In the "Order Details" section, select the cake size, flavor, and decorations you desire.
4. Choose the desired delivery date and time in the "Delivery Information" section.
5. Review the "Pricing and Payment" section to understand the total cost and payment options.
6. Enter the birthday person's name, age, and theme or interests in the "Birthday Celebrant" section.
7. Review and agree to the cancellation policy in the "Agreement and Confirmation" section.
8. Finally, confirm your order by selecting the "I Confirm This Order" option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for delivery and contact purposes.
* **Delivery Address**: Enter the address where you would like the cake delivered.
* **Cake Size**: Choose the size of the cake you want, ranging from 6-inch (6-8 servings) to 12-inch (30-40 servings).
* **Cake Flavor**: Select the flavor of the cake you desire from the given options.
* **Frosting Flavor**: Choose the frosting flavor that you prefer.
* **Cake Filling**: Select the filling you want between the cake layers.
* **Decorations and Design**: Enter any specific decorations or design you want on the cake.
* **Custom Message**: Enter any custom message or text you want on the cake.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Check any dietary restrictions or allergies you have.
* **Special Requests or Preferences**: Enter any other customizations or requests you have.
* **Desired Delivery Date**: Enter the date you want the cake delivered.
* **Preferred Delivery Time**: Choose the time you prefer for delivery.
* **Delivery Method**: Select whether you want delivery to your address or pickup at the bakery.
* **Detailed Delivery Instructions**: Enter any specific delivery instructions, such as gate codes or building access.
* **Base Cake Price**: Review the base price of the cake.
* **Decoration and Customization Fee**: Review the additional cost for decorations and customizations.
* **Delivery Fee**: Review the delivery fee, if applicable.
* **Total Order Price**: Review the total cost of the order.
* **Preferred Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method.
* **Deposit Required**: Enter the amount needed to secure the order.
* **Final Payment Due Date**: Review the date when the balance is due.
* **Birthday Person's Name**: Enter the name of the birthday person.
* **Birthday Person's Age**: Enter the age of the birthday person.
* **Theme or Interests**: Enter any theme or interests related to the birthday person.
* **I Understand the Cancellation Policy**: Review and agree to the cancellation policy.
* **I Confirm This Order**: Review and confirm your order before submitting.
