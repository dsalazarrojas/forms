# Organic Farming Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from customers interested in subscribing to an organic farming service, allowing the business to tailor their services to meet the customer's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your customer details, including your name and any other relevant information.
2. Choose the subscription services that interest you (e.g., Organic Farming).
3. Select the frequency of your orders (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, etc.).
4. Enter your contact number for any follow-up communication.
5. Provide your email address for any updates or notifications.
6. Choose the location of your farm (e.g., Farm 1, Farm 2, etc.).
7. Add any additional notes or comments about your subscription.
8. If applicable, add any specific notes about your orders.
9. Select the date and time you'd like to start and end your subscription.
10. Choose when you'd like to start and end your subscription (e.g., Subscription Start, Subscription End, Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details**: Enter your name and any other relevant details for our records.
* **Subscription Services**: Choose which organic farming services interest you, such as Organic Farming.
* **Order Frequency**: Select how often you'd like to order (e.g., daily, weekly, bi-weekly).
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number for any follow-up communication.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for any updates or notifications.
* **Farm Location**: Choose the location of your farm (e.g., Farm 1, Farm 2, etc.).
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your subscription.
* **Order Notes**: If applicable, add any specific notes about your orders.
* **Date of Subscription**: Enter the date you'd like to start your subscription.
* **Time of Subscription**: Enter the time you'd like to start your subscription.
* **Subscription Start**: Choose when you'd like to start your subscription (e.g., Subscription Start, Subscription End, Other).
* **Subscription End**: Choose when you'd like to end your subscription (e.g., Subscription End, Other).
* **Subscription Length**: Enter the length of your subscription (e.g., number of weeks, months, etc.).
