# Massage Therapy Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect payment and treatment information from massage therapy clients, allowing you to efficiently bill them for services rendered.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out this form with the client's details and treatment information.
2. Provide payment information, including any necessary payment terms or conditions.
3. Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (Patient Information, text, optional):
	+ Enter the client's name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
* **Treatment Details** (Treatment Details, text, optional):
	+ Describe the massage therapy treatment provided, including the date and duration.
* **Payment Information** (Payment Information, text, optional):
	+ Enter payment details, including amount and any payment methods (e.g. check, credit card, etc.).
