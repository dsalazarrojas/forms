# Men's Grooming Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers who want to place orders for men's grooming services such as haircuts, shaves, or other treatments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

* Please fill out the Order Form section on the first page.
* Choose the Pickup or Delivery option for how you would like to receive your order.
* Select the Pick up Time or Delivery Time based on your preference.
* Choose the Payment Method and specify if you have an "Other" payment method (if applicable).
* Enter the Name of Service and specify the Product Name (if applicable).
* Enter the Service Price (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Form (order_form)**: This is the main form section where you can enter your order details.
* **Pickup or Delivery (pickup_or_delivery)**: Choose how you would like to receive your order. Options are: 
  * Pickup: We will have a staff member meet you at the store to hand over your order.
  * Delivery: We will deliver your order to your home or office.
  * Both: A combination of Pickup and Delivery options.
* **Pick up Time (pickup_time)**: If you selected Pickup, enter the time you would like to pick up your order.
* **Delivery Time (delivery_time)**: If you selected Delivery, enter the time you would like your order to be delivered.
* **Payment Method (payment_method)**: Choose how you would like to pay for your order.
  * Payment Method: This is the most common payment method used by customers.
  * Other: If you have another payment method, please specify it in the "Other" field.
* **Other Payment Method (payment_method_other)**: This field is only visible if you selected "Other" in the "Payment Method" field. Enter your alternative payment method here.
* **Name of Service (name_of_service)**: Enter the name of the service you would like to order.
* **Product Name (product_name)**: Enter the name of the product you would like to order (if applicable).
* **Service Price (service_price)**: Enter the price of the service you would like to order (if applicable). 

## Tips

* If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us.
* Please review your order carefully before submitting it to ensure it is correct.
* If you need to make any changes to your order, please contact us and we will be happy to assist you.
