# Custom Furniture Maker Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect referrals for the custom furniture maker's business. Please fill out this form if you know someone who is interested in working with the custom furniture maker.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the company that you are referring.
2. Provide the name of the contact person at the company.
3. Enter the address of the company.
4. Enter the phone number of the company.
5. Enter the email address of the company.
6. Select the type of referral (customer, partner, supplier, or other).
7. If you selected "other", please provide a brief description of the referral.
8. Enter the name of the person you are referring at the company.
9. Enter the job title of the person you are referring at the company.
10. Enter any additional contact information for the person you are referring at the company.
11. Enter the phone number of the person you are referring at the company.
12. Enter the email address of the person you are referring at the company.
13. Enter a message to pass along to the custom furniture maker.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company that you are referring.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person at the company.
* **Company Address**: Enter the address of the company.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the company.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the company.
* **Referral Type**: Select the type of referral (customer, partner, supplier, or other).
* **Referred Company**: Enter the name of the company that the person you are referring works at.
* **Referred Name**: Enter the name of the person you are referring at the company.
* **Referred Job Title**: Enter the job title of the person you are referring at the company.
* **Referred Contact Info**: Enter any additional contact information for the person you are referring at the company.
* **Referred Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person you are referring at the company.
* **Referred Email**: Enter the email address of the person you are referring at the company.
* **Message**: Enter a brief message to pass along to the custom furniture maker.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide as much information as possible about the company and the person you are referring.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can always contact the custom furniture maker directly.
* This form is for referrals only, if you are looking for information about the custom furniture maker, please visit their website or contact them directly.
* If you are referring a company, please make sure to include all of the required information.
