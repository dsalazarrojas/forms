# Local Bakery Cooperative Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect order information for the Local Bakery Cooperative. It's essential to fill this form accurately to ensure smooth order processing and delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the desired pickup date for your order.
2. Choose the pickup time for your order.
3. Enter the delivery address where you would like your order delivered.
4. Choose the delivery date for your order.
5. Enter your name and contact information (email and phone number) so we can get in touch with you.
6. Enter the name and contact information (email and phone number) of the Local Bakery Cooperative you are ordering from.
7. Provide any special instructions for your order.
8. Enter any additional pickup instructions for the customer picking up your order.
9. Enter any delivery instructions for the customer receiving your order.
10. Add any notes or comments about your order if you'd like to provide additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pickup date**: Enter the date you would like to pick up your order.
* **Pickup time**: Choose the time you would like to pick up your order.
* **Delivery address**: Enter the address where you would like your order delivered.
* **Delivery date**: Choose the date you would like to have your order delivered.
* **Customer name**: Enter your name so we can personalize your order.
* **Customer email**: Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you about your order.
* **Customer phone**: Enter your phone number so we can call you with any order updates.
* **Bakery name**: Enter the name of the Local Bakery Cooperative you are ordering from (if you're unsure, ask us for a list).
* **Bakery email**: Enter the email address of the Local Bakery Cooperative you are ordering from (if you're unsure, ask us for their contact info).
* **Bakery phone**: Enter the phone number of the Local Bakery Cooperative you are ordering from (if you're unsure, ask us for their contact info).
* **Special instructions**: Provide any additional details or instructions for your order.
* **Pickup instructions**: Enter any special instructions for the person picking up your order.
* **Delivery instructions**: Enter any special instructions for the person receiving your order.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about your order if you'd like.

Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure your order is processed correctly. If you have any questions or issues, contact us for assistance.
