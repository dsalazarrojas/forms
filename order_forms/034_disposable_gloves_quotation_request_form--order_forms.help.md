# Disposable Gloves Quotation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather customer information and preferences for custom disposable glove quotations. It is intended for sales team members to collect data for new requests or revisions to existing orders.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details as a user, including your contact person, phone number, and email address.
2. Specify the order date and time for your request.
3. Select the type of product you are interested in (you can choose multiple types).
4. Enter the quantity of gloves you require.
5. Review the required fields section for any additional information you need to provide.
6. Select the submission option for your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input**: Enter your personal details as a user, including your name, company, and other relevant information.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and title of the person you would like to be contacted about your request.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the contact person or your company.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person or your company.
* **Order Date**: Specify the date you would like to place your order.
* **Order Time**: Specify the time you would like to place your order.
* **Product Type**: Choose the type of product you are interested in (you can select multiple types).
* **Quantity Requested**: Enter the number of gloves you would like to order.
* **Required Fields**: This section is for any additional information that may be required for your request, such as specific product features or customization options.
* **Submit Form**: Select how you would like to submit your form (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate processing of your request.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please contact the sales team directly.
* If you would like to make changes to your existing order, please specify the order number or ID.
