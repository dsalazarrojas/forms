# Pet Photography Order Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pet Photography Order Form Template is designed to gather information from pet owners about their pet photography needs. This template allows pet businesses to capture relevant details about the pet owner's requirements for a session or shoot. By filling out this form, the pet owner is able to provide their pet's and their own information, including contact details and session details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category that best describes your pet photography order. Choose from the options "Photography", "Order Forms", or "Pet Forms".
2. Enter a brief description of your order, including any specific details about your pet and your photography preferences.
3. Choose the type of session you are requesting (e.g. Pet Photo Session, Pet Photo Shoot, or Pet Photo Shoot & Video).
4. Select a date and time for your session.
5. Enter the location for your session.
6. Provide your contact number, so we can easily get in touch with you.
7. Enter your email address, so we can keep you updated on your order and any important information.
8. Add any additional notes about your order or session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category (Category)**: Select the type of form that best describes your order. This will help us to understand your needs better.
	+ Select "Photography" for general photography orders.
	+ Select "Order Forms" for general orders that are not photography related.
	+ Select "Pet Forms" for pet related orders.
* **Description (Description)**: Enter a brief description of your order, including any specific details about your pet and your photography preferences.
* **Session Type (Session Type)**: Choose the type of session you are requesting. This will help us to schedule the right services for you.
	+ Select "Pet Photo Session" for a basic photography session.
	+ Select "Pet Photo Shoot" for a more in-depth photography session.
	+ Select "Pet Photo Shoot & Video" for a combination of photography and video services.
* **Date and Time (Date and Time)**: Enter the date and time for your session. Please be as specific as possible, so we can schedule the right services for you.
* **Location (Location)**: Enter the location for your session. This will help us to know where to take your photos.
* **Contact Number (Contact Number)**: Enter your contact number, so we can easily get in touch with you.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address, so we can keep you updated on your order and any important information.
* **Notes (Notes)**: Add any additional notes about your order or session. This is a good place to mention any specific requests or preferences you may have.
