# Gardening Kit Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gardening Kit Subscription Form is designed for customers to sign up for a regular gardening kit subscription service. Customers can choose how often and for how long they want to receive the gardening kit, as well as select their preferred delivery day of the week.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill out the form with your contact and shipping information (name, email, address, phone number, shipping city).
- Select the frequency of your subscription (e.g., monthly, bi-weekly, weekly).
- Choose the length of your subscription (e.g., 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, 1 year, 2 years).
- Select your preferred delivery day of the week (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email (`customer_email`):** Enter your email address, which will be used to send your subscription information, including delivery notifications and any relevant promotions or special offers.
* **Shipping Address (`shipping_address`):** Enter your complete address where you would like the gardening kit to be delivered. This should include your street address, city, state and zip code.
* **Subscription Frequency (`subscription_frequency`):** Choose how often you want to receive your gardening kit. Options include monthly, bi-weekly, and weekly.
* **Subscription Length (`subscription_length`):** Select how long you want to subscribe for. Options include 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, 1 year, and 2 years.
* **Delivery Day (`delivery_day`):** Choose the day of the week you want your gardening kit to be delivered.
* **Shipping City (`shipping_city`):** Enter your city where you would like the gardening kit to be delivered.
* **Phone Number (`phone_number`):** Enter your phone number, which can be used to contact you for any questions or issues regarding your subscription.
* **Name (`name`):** Enter your name as you would like it to be used for your subscription.
* **Address (`address`):** Enter your complete address where you would like the gardening kit to be delivered.
* **Zip Code (`zip_code`):** Enter your zip code where you would like the gardening kit to be delivered.
