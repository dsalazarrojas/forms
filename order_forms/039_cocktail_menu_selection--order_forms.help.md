# Cocktail Menu Selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cocktail Menu Selection form is designed to help you select your preferred cocktail and share your personal preferences with our bartender. This form ensures that we can craft your drink exactly to your liking.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your guest information, including your name and email address (if applicable).
3. Select your preferred cocktail, including your first and second choice.
4. Indicate your preferred spirit, sweetness level, and glass type.
5. Choose any non-alcoholic options if you do not consume alcohol.
6. Enter the number of people in your party and the number of drinks you would like per person.
7. Select your preferred garnish and any special requests.
8. Finally, select a date for your selection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (if you have one).
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if you have one).
* **Number of People in Your Party** (`party_size`, number, required): Enter the number of people in your party.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): List any food or drink allergies or dietary restrictions you have.
* **Can You Consume Alcohol?** (`alcohol_restrictions`, select_one, required): Select "True" if you can consume alcohol, "False" if you cannot, or "Non-alcoholic only" if you prefer non-alcoholic options.
* **First Cocktail Choice** (`first_choice`, select_one, required): Select your preferred cocktail from the list.
* **Alternative Selection** (`second_choice`, select_one, optional): Select your second preferred cocktail.
* **Preferred Spirit** (`spirit_preference`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred spirit type (Vodka, Gin, Rum, Tequila, Whiskey, or Brandy).
* **Preferred Sweetness Level** (`sweetness_level`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred sweetness level (Very sweet, Sweet, Balanced, Dry, Very dry, or No preference).
* **Non-Alcoholic Drink Choice** (`non_alcoholic_choice`, select_one, optional): Select a non-alcoholic drink from the list (if applicable).
* **Number of Drinks per Person** (`drink_servings`, number, optional): Enter the number of drinks you would like per person.
* **Glass Type Preference** (`glass_preference`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred glass type (Coupe glass, Rocks glass, Highball glass, Margarita glass, Martini glass, or No preference).
* **Ice Preference** (`ice_preference`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred ice type (Cubed ice, Crushed ice, Shaved ice, or No preference).
* **Garnish Preference** (`garnish_preference`, select_multiple, optional): Select all preferred garnishes (Citrus twist, Fresh mint, Berries, Olives, Cherries, Herbs, or None).
* **Special Requests or Notes** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any special requests or notes.
* **Selection Date** (`selection_date`, date, required): Select the date of your selection.

Please note that all fields marked as "required" are necessary to complete the form, while optional fields are for your convenience. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask our bartender for assistance.
