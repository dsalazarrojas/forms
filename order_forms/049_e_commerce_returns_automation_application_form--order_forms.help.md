# e_commerce_returns_automation_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The e_commerce_returns_automation_application_form is designed to collect information about a customer's return request. This form will help the customer service team to process returns efficiently and resolve any issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your customer information, including your name, email, phone, and address.
2.  Enter the details about your order, such as order date, order time, return date, and return time.
3.  Choose the reason for your return.
4.  Select the condition of the product you are returning.
5.  Enter any additional return details.
6.  Enter your return status and number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Customer Info** (`customer_name`, `email`, `phone`, `customer_address`, `customer_city`, `customer_state`, `customer_postal_code`, `customer_country`):
    *   Enter your name, email, phone number, address, city, state, postal code, and country.
*  **Order Info** (`order_date`, `order_time`, `product_id`, `product_name`, `order_number`, `return_number`):
    *   Enter the date and time of your order, as well as the product id, name, order number, and return number.
*  **Return Reason** (`return_reason`):
    *   Choose a reason for your return from the given options.
*  **Product Condition** (`product_condition`):
    *   Select the condition of the product you are returning (e.g., new, used, damaged).
*  **Return Details** (`return_note`):
    *   Enter any additional details about your return.
*  **Return Status** (`return_status`):
    *   Choose the status of your return.
*  **Return Date** (`return_date`):
    *   Enter the date of your return.
*  **Return Time** (`return_time`):
    *   Enter the time of your return.
*  **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`):
    *   Enter the shipping address of the product you are returning.
*  **Return Reason Details** (`return_reason_details`):
    *   Enter any additional details about your return reason.
*  **Product ID** (`product_id`):
    *   Enter the id of the product you are returning.
*  **Customer Message** (`customer_message`):
    *   Enter any additional message to the customer service team.
*  **Return Number** (`return_number`):
    *   Enter the number of the return.
