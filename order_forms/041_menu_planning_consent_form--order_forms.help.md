# Menu Planning Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Menu Planning Consent Form is a tool used to gather information about customers' dietary needs and restrictions. This information helps in planning menus that cater to their requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your first and last name.
2. Enter your email address (optional, but recommended for contact purposes).
3. Enter your contact phone number.
4. Select any special diets you follow (e.g., Gluten-free, Vegetarian, Vegan, etc.).
5. Mention any food allergies (if you have any).
6. List any food restrictions you have (e.g., no nuts, no dairy, etc.).
7. If you have any other restrictions not listed, you can provide that information in the "Other Restrictions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name, as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name, as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for any further questions or inquiries. This is an optional field, but it's helpful for our staff to contact you.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for any questions or inquiries.
* **Special Diets**: Select any diets you follow from the listed options. If you have a diet that's not listed, please select "Other (please specify)" and provide more information.
* **Food Allergies**: If you have any food allergies, list them here. This will help our staff to ensure your safety while dining with us.
* **Food Restrictions**: List any food restrictions you have, such as no nuts, no dairy, etc. This will help our staff to accommodate your needs.
* **Other Restrictions**: If you have any other restrictions not listed, provide that information here. This could be anything from no MSG to no spicy food, etc.

Tips:
* Be as accurate as possible when providing your information. This will ensure that we can cater to your needs properly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask our staff for assistance.
* This form will help us to create a menu that caters to your requirements, so please be as specific as possible when providing your information.
