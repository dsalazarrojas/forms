# T Shirt Deposit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The T Shirt Deposit Order Form is used to collect information for a t-shirt order, including the customer's name, email, address, t-shirt color, size, style, deposit amount, payment method, and total cost.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name in the "name" field.
2. Enter the customer's email address in the "email" field.
3. Enter the customer's address in the "address" field.
4. Select the desired t-shirt color from the drop-down menu in the "t_shirt_color" field.
5. Select the desired t-shirt size from the drop-down menu in the "t_shirt_size" field.
6. Select the desired t-shirt style from the multi-select menu in the "t_shirt_style" field.
7. Enter the deposit amount in the "deposit" field.
8. Select the payment method from the drop-down menu in the "payment_method" field.
9. Enter the total cost of the order in the "total" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name (Text)**: Enter the customer's name.
* **email (Email)**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **address (Text)**: Enter the customer's address.
* **t-shirting color (Text)**: Select the desired color of the t-shirt.
* **t-shirt size (Select One)**: Select the desired size of the t-shirt.
	+ Options: Small, Large, X-Large
* **t-shirt style (Select Multiple)**: Select the desired style(s) of the t-shirt.
	+ Options: Round-Neck, Crew-Neck
* **deposit (Number)**: Enter the deposit amount for the order.
* **payment method (Select One)**: Select the payment method to be used for the order.
	+ Options: Credit-Card, PayPal
* **total (Number)**: Enter the total cost of the order.
