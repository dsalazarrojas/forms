# Maintenance Service Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maintenance Service Renewal Form is used to provide the required information for service providers to process the renewal or modification of an existing service contract.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct service provider ID.
2. Enter the client information.
3. Enter the client name.
4. Enter the service start date.
5. Enter the service renewal date.
6. Select the service type (yes/no).
7. Select the renewal option (yes/no).
8. Enter the reason for the renewal.
9. Enter the next renewal date.
10. Provide any comments.
11. Enter contact information (if necessary).
12. Enter email address (if necessary).
13. Enter phone number (if necessary).
14. Select the assigned user.
15. Select the service provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Provider ID**: Enter the ID of the service provider you are renewing or modifying the contract with. This is a required field.
* **Client Information**: Enter any additional information about the client. This can include any relevant details that will help the service provider process the renewal or modification. (Optional)
* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client. This is a required field.
* **Service Start Date**: Enter the date when the service started. This is a required field.
* **Service Renewal Date**: Enter the date when the service will be renewed or modified. This is a required field.
* **Service Type**: Select yes or no to indicate whether the service needs to be renewed or modified. This is a required field.
* **Renewal Option**: Select yes or no to indicate whether the renewal option is applicable. This is a required field.
* **Reason for Renewal**: Enter the reason for renewing or modifying the service contract. This is a required field.
* **Next Renewal Date**: Enter the date when the service will be renewed or modified again. This is a required field.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the renewal or modification. (Optional)
* **Contact Information**: Enter any contact information for the service provider to contact you. (Optional)
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the service provider to contact you. (Optional)
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the service provider to contact you. (Optional)
* **Assigned User**: Select yes or no to indicate whether the service is assigned to a specific user. This is a required field.
* **Service Provider**: Select yes or no to indicate whether the service provider is involved in the renewal or modification. This is a required field.
