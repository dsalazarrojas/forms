# sports_photography_order_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers for sports photography services. It includes fields for package selection, customer details, order summary, payment terms, payment method, email, phone number, and order confirmation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the package that best suits your needs by choosing one of the options in the "Package Selection" field.
2. Enter your customer details in the "Customer Details" field.
3. Provide a brief summary of your order in the "Order Summary" field.
4. Specify your payment terms in the "Payment Terms" field.
5. Choose one or more payment methods for your order in the "Payment Method" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
8. Confirm your order details in the "Order Confirmation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Package Selection (`Package Selection`, `select_one`, required: false)
Choose the package that best suits your needs for sports photography services.

### Customer Details (`Customer Details`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your name, address, and other relevant details to help us better understand your order.

### Order Summary (`Order Summary`, `note`, required: false)
Provide a brief summary of your order to help us understand your requirements.

### Payment Terms (`Payment Terms`, `text`, required: false)
Specify any payment terms or conditions you would like to include in your order.

### Payment Method (`Payment Method`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Choose one or more payment methods for your order, such as Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.

### Email (`Email`, `email`, required: false)
Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.

### Phone (`Phone`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your order.

### Order Confirmation (`Order Confirmation`, `note`, required: false)
Confirm your order details to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Tips
- Please select a package that suits your needs for sports photography services.
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information in the "Customer Details" field.
- Provide clear and concise information in the "Order Summary" field.
- Clearly specify your payment terms and methods to avoid confusion.
- Enter a valid email address and phone number to ensure we can contact you about your order.
- Review your order details carefully before confirming them.
