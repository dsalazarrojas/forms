# Loading And Delivery Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect acknowledgment and details about delivery of goods or services.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form carefully and accurately.
- Make sure you have all the necessary information and documents ready.
- Select "Yes" or "No" for delivery confirmation.
- Enter the date of delivery in the appropriate field.
- Sign in the signature field.
- Add any remarks or delivery details in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Delivery Confirmation** (`Delivery Confirmation`, `select_one`, required): 
  Confirm whether the delivery was successful or not.
- **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, required): 
  Enter the date when the delivery took place.
- **Signature** (`signature`, `note`, required): 
  Sign here to confirm the delivery details.
- **Date of Signature** (`date_of_signature`, `date`, required): 
  Enter the date when the signature was applied.
- **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, `text`, optional): 
  Add any additional information about the delivery.
- **Signature Details** (`signature_details`, `text`, optional): 
  Add any additional details about the signature.
- **Delivery Date Range** (`delivery_date_range`, `time`, optional): 
  Enter the start and end time of the delivery.
- **Delivery Time Range** (`delivery_time_range`, `time`, optional): 
  Enter the start and end time of the delivery.
- **Remarks** (`remarks`, `text`, optional): 
  Add any additional remarks or comments about the delivery.
- **Signature Of** (`signature_of`, `select_one`, optional): 
  Select whether the signature is from the Buyer or Seller.
- **Date of Delivery** (`date_of_delivery`, `date`, required): 
  Enter the date of delivery.
- **Remarks** (`remarks`, `text`, optional): 
  Add any additional remarks or comments about the delivery.
