## Packaging Label Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to order packaging labels in a specific size and material. It is intended for production and fulfillment teams to create packaging labels for products being prepared for shipping.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the order form from the available options.
2. Choose the size of the label you need from the "Size" field.
3. Choose the material of the label from the "Material" field.
4. Enter the quantity of labels you need in the "Quantity" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Form**: This field is used to select the specific order form you want to use.
* **Size**: This field allows you to choose the size of the label you need. Please select one of the available sizes to ensure that your label is correctly sized for your product.
* **Material**: This field allows you to choose the material of the label you need. Please select one of the available materials to ensure that your label is durable and suitable for your product.
* **Quantity**: This field is used to enter the number of labels you need. Please enter a whole number (e.g. 5, 10, 20, etc.).

## Tips

* Make sure to select the correct size and material for your label to ensure it is correctly formatted and durable.
* If you are unsure about the size or material needed for your product, please contact our production team for guidance.
* Please enter the correct quantity of labels needed to avoid overproduction or underproduction.
* If you need to make any changes to your order, please contact our production team to request modifications.
