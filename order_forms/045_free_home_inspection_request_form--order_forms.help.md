# Free Home Inspection Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Free Home Inspection Request Form is designed to collect information from customers or clients who require a free home inspection. This form will help the inspection team to schedule a suitable inspection time and date for the requested property and capture additional information for the inspection process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your property address in the "property address" field. This is the address of the property you would like to have inspected.
2. Select a preferred date and time for the inspection in the "preferred date" and "inspection time" fields. Please note that these are your preferred options, and the inspection team will do their best to accommodate them.
3. Choose the type of property you are requesting the inspection for, by selecting "Residential", "Commercial", or "Other" from the "home type" dropdown menu.
4. Select the type of inspection you require, by choosing "Visual", "Full", or "Other" from the "inspection type" multi-select menu.
5. Enter your contact phone number in the "contact phone" field, if you have one. This will help the inspection team to contact you for any updates or questions.
6. Enter your contact email address in the "contact email" field, if you have one. This will help the inspection team to send you updates or notifications about the inspection.
7. Choose the tool that you would like to be assigned to perform the inspection, by selecting "chatjimmy" or "Other" from the "assigned tool" dropdown menu.
8. If you have any additional details or comments, please provide them in the "additional details" field. This will help the inspection team to better understand your requirements and perform the inspection more efficiently.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **property_address**: The property address where the inspection will take place.
* **preferred_date**: The date you would like the inspection to take place. Please note that this is not a fixed date and time, but rather a preferred one.
* **inspection_time**: The time you would like the inspection to take place. Like the preferred date, this is not a fixed time and is subject to change.
* **home_type**: The type of property you are requesting the inspection for (e.g., Residential, Commercial, or Other).
* **inspection_type**: The type of inspection you require (e.g., Visual, Full, or Other).
* **contact_phone**: If you have a contact phone number, please enter it here. This will help the inspection team to contact you for any updates or questions.
* **contact_email**: If you have a contact email address, please enter it here. This will help the inspection team to send you updates or notifications about the inspection.
* **assigned_tool**: The tool you would like to be assigned to perform the inspection (e.g., chatjimmy or Other).
* **additional_details**: Any additional information or comments you have regarding the inspection. This will help the inspection team to better understand your requirements and perform the inspection more efficiently.
