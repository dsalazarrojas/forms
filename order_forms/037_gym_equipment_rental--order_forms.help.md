# Gym Equipment Rental - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to provide information about the rental of gym equipment, including the type of equipment, quantity, and rental dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the **First name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Last name** field.
3. Provide your date of birth in the **Date of birth** field in the format MM/DD/YY.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone number** field.
5. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
6. Select the type of equipment being rented from the **Equipment type** field.
7. Enter the quantity of equipment being rented in the **Equipment quantity** field.
8. Select the start date of the rental in the **Rental start date** field.
9. Select the end date of the rental in the **Rental end date** field.
10. Provide any additional notes about the rental in the **Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Date of birth** (date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Phone number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Equipment type** (select multiple, optional): Select the type of equipment being rented. Options: Yes, No.
* **Equipment quantity** (number, optional): Enter the quantity of equipment being rented.
* **Rental start date** (date, optional): Select the start date of the rental.
* **Rental end date** (date, optional): Select the end date of the rental.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Provide any additional notes about the rental.

## Tips
* Be sure to enter accurate and complete information in each field.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the gym management for assistance.
* This form is designed to be completed once for each piece of equipment being rented. If you need to rent multiple pieces of equipment, please fill out the form multiple times.
