# Bulk Bag Order Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a bulk bag order for a customer or business.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the order date on which the order is being placed.
2. Enter the customer's full name or business name.
3. Add the customer's contact email address.
4. Provide the customer's phone number.
5. Enter the billing address, including street address, city, state or province, and zip or postal code.
6. Enter the shipping address, including street address, city, state, and zip or postal code.
7. Select the type of bags needed from the options provided.
8. Enter the dimensions or volume of the bags.
9. Specify the quantity of bags required.
10. Choose the unit type (e.g., individual bags, cartons, pallets, etc.).
11. If desired, select a preferred bag color.
12. Check if any bag printing or imprinting is required.
13. If printing is required, provide printing details.
14. Enter the estimated unit price per bag (if known).
15. Enter the estimated total cost (if known).
16. Choose the preferred delivery date.
17. Select the shipping method (e.g., standard, expedited, next day, will pickup, etc.).
18. Add any special instructions or requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Date** (1, date, required): Enter the date on which the order is being placed.
* **Customer Name** (2, text, required): Enter the customer's full name or business name.
* **Email Address** (3, email, required): Enter the customer's contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (4, text, required): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Billing Address** (6, text, required): Enter the customer's billing address, including street address, city, state or province, and zip or postal code.
* **Shipping Address** (10, text, required): Enter the address where the order should be shipped, including street address, city, state, and zip or postal code.
* **Bag Type** (14, select_one, required): Choose the type of bags needed from the options provided (e.g., polypropylene, plastic, paper, kraft, laminated, custom, other).
* **Bag Size** (15, text, required): Enter the dimensions or volume of the bags.
* **Quantity of Bags** (16, number, required): Enter the number of bags required.
* **Unit Type** (17, select_one, required): Choose the unit type (e.g., individual bags, cartons, pallets, etc.).
* **Bag Color** (18, text, optional): If desired, enter the preferred bag color.
* **Bag Imprinting or Printing** (19, select_one, optional): Check if any bag printing or imprinting is required.
* **Printing Details** (20, text, optional): If printing is required, provide printing details.
* **Unit Price per Bag** (21, number, optional): If known, enter the estimated unit price per bag.
* **Estimated Total Cost** (22, number, optional): If known, enter the estimated total cost.
* **Requested Delivery Date** (23, date, required): Enter the date on which the order is needed.
* **Preferred Shipping Method** (24, select_one, required): Choose the shipping method (e.g., standard, expedited, next day, will pickup, etc.).
* **Special Instructions** (25, text, optional): Add any special requests or instructions.
