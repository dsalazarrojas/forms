# Clone Of Color Street Nails In My Stock - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to clone and track orders for Color Street Nails in My Stock. It collects information from the end user for inventory purposes, including stylist's name, contact email, product code, color variant, quantity ordered, price per unit, shipment preference, delivery address, and special instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **Stylist name**, which will be used for order tracking purposes.
2.  Enter your **Contact email**, which will receive order confirmations.
3.  Enter the **Product code**, which is the SKU or product identifier for the item ordered.
4.  Describe the **Color variant**, such as the color or pattern of the nail product.
5.  Enter the **Quantity ordered**, the number of units being ordered.
6.  Enter the **Price per unit**, the decimal value of the price in the local currency.
7.  Select the **Shipment preference**, which is the preferred delivery method for the order.
8.  Enter the **Delivery address**, the full shipping address for the order.
9.  If necessary, provide **Special instructions**, such as any special handling or packaging requirements for the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Stylist name** (`stylist_name`, text, required): Enter your name for order tracking purposes.
*   **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email to receive order confirmations.
*   **Product code** (`product_code`, text, required): Enter the SKU or product identifier for the item ordered.
*   **Color variant** (`color_variant`, text, optional): Describe the color or pattern of the nail product.
*   **Quantity ordered** (`quantity_ordered`, number, required): Enter the number of units being ordered.
*   **Price per unit** (`price_per_unit`, number, optional): Enter the decimal value of the price in the local currency.
*   **Shipment preference** (`shipment_preference`, select_one, required): Select the preferred delivery method for the order.
*   **Delivery address** (`delivery_address`, text, required): Enter the full shipping address for the order.
*   **Special instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, optional): Enter any special handling or packaging requirements for the order.
