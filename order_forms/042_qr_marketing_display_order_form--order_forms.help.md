# qr_marketing_display_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and display information related to orders for marketing purposes. It helps the marketing team analyze and use this data for their campaigns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the **Customer Details** section (Page 1) with the requested information. This includes entering any relevant details about the customer.
2. In the **Product Selections** section (Page 2), choose either "Yes" or "No" to confirm the selection of specific products.
3. In the **Artwork** section (Page 3), enter any relevant information about the artwork related to the order.
4. In the **Delivery Preferences** section (Page 4), choose the preferred delivery option from the list.
5. Finally, in the **Customer Details 2** section (Page 5), enter any additional details about the customer if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about the customer.
* **Product Selections** (`product_selections`, select_multiple, optional): Choose "Yes" or "No" to confirm the selection of specific products. This will show a list of products related to the order.
* **Artwork** (`artwork`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about the artwork related to the order.
* **Delivery Preferences** (`delivery_preferences`, select_one, optional): Choose the preferred delivery option from the list of available options.
* **Customer Details 2** (`customer_details_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the customer if necessary.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields, even if they are marked as optional, to ensure accurate data collection.
* Double-check the information entered in each field to ensure it is correct and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any field or have questions, please contact your marketing team for assistance.
* This form is for marketing purposes only and is not intended for order fulfillment or order creation.
