# Takeout Bag Branding Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to manage orders and branding for takeout bags. It allows restaurants to place orders for customized takeout bags with their branding printed on them.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the restaurant's name in the "Restaurant Name" field.
2. Upload the logo for the branding in the "Logo" field.
3. Select the type of bag from the options provided in the "Bag Type" field.
4. Choose the color of the bag from the options provided in the "Bag Color" field.
5. Enter the quantity of bags needed in the "Quantity" field.
6. Enter the price of the order in the "Price" field.
7. Select the delivery date from the calendar.
8. Choose the payment method from the options provided in the "Payment Method" field.
9. Enter any additional payment details in the "Payment Details" field.
10. Enter the customer's email in the "Email" field.
11. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Phone" field.
12. Enter any additional order notes in the "Order Notes" field.
13. Confirm the customer's email in the "Confirm Email" field.
14. Confirm the customer's phone number in the "Confirm Phone" field.
15. Enter the order confirmation message in the "Order Confirmation" field.
16. Select the payment status from the options provided in the "Payment Status" field.
17. Enter any status notes in the "Status Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Restaurant Name** (`restaurant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the restaurant placing the order.
* **Logo Attachment** (`logo_attachment`, text, optional): Upload the logo for the branding.
* **Bag Type** (`bag_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of bag from the options provided.
* **Bag Color** (`bag_color`, select_one, optional): Choose the color of the bag from the options provided.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of bags needed.
* **Price** (`price`, number, optional): Enter the price of the order.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): Select the delivery date from the calendar.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Choose the payment method from the options provided.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, note, optional): Enter any additional payment details.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional order notes.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, email, optional): Confirm the customer's email address.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, text, optional): Confirm the customer's phone number.
* **Order Confirmation** (`order_confirmation`, text, optional): Enter the order confirmation message.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, optional): Select the payment status from the options provided.
* **Status Notes** (`status_notes`, text, optional): Enter any status notes.
* **Logo** (`logo`, text, optional): Enter any additional logo information.
* **Bag Image** (`bag_image`, text, optional): Enter any additional bag image information.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, optional): Enter the total price of the order.
* **Tax Amount** (`tax_amount`, number, optional): Enter any tax amount.
* **Total Price** (`total_price`, number, optional): Enter the total price of the order.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the customer's name.
* **Order ID** (`order_id`, number, optional): Enter any order ID number.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, optional): Enter the assigned user's name.
* **Confirm Email**: Enter the email address of the person confirming the order.
* **Confirm Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person confirming the order.
* **Order Confirmation**: Enter the confirmation message for the order.
