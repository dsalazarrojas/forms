# fair_trade_product_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to provide their personal information when placing an order for a Fair Trade product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **First Name** in the first field. This is an optional field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the second field. This is an optional field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the third field. This should be a valid email address.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the fourth field. This will help us contact you.
5. Enter your **Address** in the fifth field. This will help us with order delivery.
6. Enter your **City** in the sixth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Text, Optional)**: Enter your first name, as this is not a required field.
* **Last Name (Text, Optional)**: Enter your last name, as this is not a required field.
* **Email (Email, Required)**: Enter a valid email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone (Text, Optional)**: Enter your phone number to help us contact you.
* **Address (Text, Optional)**: Enter your address, which will help us with order delivery.
* **City (Text, Optional)**: Enter your city, which will help us with order delivery.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your email address correctly to ensure you receive updates on your order.
* If you have any issues with your order, please contact us at your provided email address or phone number.
