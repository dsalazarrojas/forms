# Gym Branded Keychain Bulk Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place bulk orders for customized gym branded keychains. It is intended for customers who want to order multiple keychains with their gym's logo or branding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your user information in the "User Information" page.
2. In the "Order Details" page, select the bulk item you would like to order and the desired quantity.
3. In the "Shipping Info" page, enter your shipping details to ensure timely delivery of your order.
4. In the "Billing Info" page, enter your billing details to complete the payment process.
5. Review your order total, which includes the total price and shipping cost.
6. Confirm your order by selecting "Yes" in the "Confirm Order" field.
7. Finally, submit the form to place your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information**: This page is where you enter your contact information and any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Order Details**: Select the bulk item you would like to order and enter the desired quantity.
* **Bulk Item**: Choose the type of bulk item you want to order (e.g. "Bulk Item 1", "Bulk Item 2", etc.).
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of items you would like to order.
* **Shipping**: Enter the shipping details for your order, including any shipping instructions or special requests.
* **Weight**: Enter the weight of the items being shipped (not applicable for keychains).
* **Height**: Enter the height of the items being shipped (not applicable for keychains).
* **Color**: Select the color of the keychain you would like to order (e.g. Red, Blue, Green).
* **Size**: Select the size of the keychain you would like to order (e.g. Small, Medium, Large).
* **Product ID**: Enter the product ID for the item you would like to order (not applicable for custom keychains).
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product you would like to order (not applicable for custom keychains).
* **Product URL**: Enter the URL for the product you would like to order (not applicable for custom keychains).
* **Product Description**: Enter a description of the product you would like to order (not applicable for custom keychains).
* **Price**: Enter the price of the item you would like to order (not applicable for custom keychains).
* **Discount**: Enter any applicable discounts for your order (e.g. coupon codes).
* **Shipping Cost**: Enter the shipping cost for your order.
* **Discount Code**: Select if you have a discount code to apply to your order.
* **Submit**: Select "Yes" to submit your order and "No" to cancel.
* **Confirm Order**: Confirm that you have reviewed and accepted the order details.
* **Total**: Review the total price and shipping cost of your order before submitting.
* **Payment Info**: Enter your payment information to complete the order process.
* **Order Total**: Review the final total of your order before submitting.
