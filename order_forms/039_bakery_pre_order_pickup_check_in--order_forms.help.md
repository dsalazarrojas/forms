# Bakery Pre Order Pickup Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate a smooth and accurate pickup experience for pre-ordered items at the bakery.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all fields marked as required.
2. Confirm your order details, including items and quantities.
3. Inspect the products and confirm their condition.
4. Confirm payment method and amount (if applicable).
5. Provide feedback and comments (if desired).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your order confirmation.
* **Order Number or Reference** (`order_number`, `text`, required): Enter the order number or reference provided to you.
* **Phone Number on File** (`customer_phone`, `phone`, optional): If you have a phone number on file, please enter it for our staff to contact you with any issues.
* **Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`, `date`, required): Confirm the date of your pickup.
* **Arrival Time** (`pickup_time`, `time`, required): Confirm your expected arrival time.
* **Order Items Confirmation** (`items_ordered`, `text`, required): Confirm the items in your order.
* **Quantities Confirmed** (`quantity_confirmation`, `text`, required): Confirm the quantities of the items in your order.
* **Products Condition is Satisfactory** (`condition_satisfactory`, `select_one`, required): Inspect the products and confirm their condition (Yes, No, or Needs Adjustment).
* **Special Requests Met** (`special_requests_met`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm if any special requests were met.
* **Product Quality Rating** (`quality_rating`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the quality of the products (Excellent, Good, Acceptable, Below Expectations).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your payment method (Cash, Credit Card, Debit Card, Already Paid Online).
* **Amount Paid** (`amount_paid`, `decimal`, optional): If applicable, confirm the amount paid.
* **Receipt Provided** (`receipt_provided`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if a receipt was provided.
* **Packaging and Handling** (`packaging_adequate`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm if the packaging is adequate for transport.
* **Special Handling Instructions Provided** (`special_handling_needs`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm if special handling instructions were provided.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
* **Additional Comments or Feedback** (`feedback_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Staff Member Name (Assisting with Pickup)** (`staff_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the staff member assisting with your pickup.
* **Pickup Check-In Complete** (`checkout_complete`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that the checkout process is complete (Confirmed Complete, Other).

By following this guide, you will be able to accurately and efficiently complete the bakery pre-order pickup check-in form.
