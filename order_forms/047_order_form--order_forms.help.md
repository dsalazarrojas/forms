# Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about customer orders, allowing you to gather essential details about the customer and their order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" fields is not actually required but is a valid field, you can skip it if you wish. 
3. Once you have filled in the necessary information, click the submit button to send your order details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's name as it appears on their identification, this field is not actually required but is a valid field.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email address, this is also not required but is a valid field.
