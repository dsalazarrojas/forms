# Internet Services Billing Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect billing information from customers for internet services. Please fill out the form below to ensure accurate and timely payment processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully, providing accurate information in each field.
2. Use the provided options for payment gateway selection.
3. If you have any special payment terms or notes, please include them in the "order_notes" field.
4. Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's name as it appears on their billing invoice.
* **Street Address**: Enter the customer's street address for billing purposes.
* **Zip Code**: Enter the customer's zip code for billing purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the customer's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email**: Enter the customer's email address for billing and communication purposes. Please use the format `example@example.com`.
* **Payment Gateway**: Select the payment gateway to use for this transaction (Stripe, PayPal, Authorize.net).
* **Payment Terms**: Enter any special payment terms or instructions.
* **Order Notes**: Enter any additional notes or instructions for the billing team to reference.

Note: The fields are all optional, but please fill out as much information as possible to ensure accurate billing processing.
