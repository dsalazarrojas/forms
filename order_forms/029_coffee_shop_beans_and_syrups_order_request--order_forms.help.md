# Coffee Shop Beans And Syrups Order Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather order requests from coffee shops for their business operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the introduction above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, *required*): Enter your coffee shop's name.
* **Owner or Manager Name** (`owner_name`, text, *required*): Enter the name of the person responsible for the order.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, *required*): Enter your coffee shop's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, *required*): Enter your coffee shop's phone number.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, *required*): Enter your coffee shop's address.
* **Preferred Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select_one, *required*): Choose how you'd like to receive your order (Pickup or Delivery).
* **Requested Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, *required*): Enter the date you'd like to receive your order.
* **Coffee Bean Type** (`bean_type`, select_one, *required*): Select the type of coffee bean you'd like to order (Single Origin, Blend, Dark Roast, etc.).
* **Quantity - pounds** (`bean_quantity`, number, *required*): Enter the quantity of coffee beans in pounds.
* **Bean Origin or Variety** (`bean_origin`, text, *optional*): Enter the specific origin or variety of coffee beans.
* **Grind Option** (`grind_option`, select_one, *required*): Choose the grind option for your coffee beans (Whole beans, Finely ground, etc.).
* **Order Additional Coffee Beans** (`add_more_beans`, select_one, *optional*): Select 'Yes' if you'd like to order additional coffee beans.
* **Second Coffee Bean Type** (`bean_type_2`, select_one, *optional*): Select an additional type of coffee bean you'd like to order.
* **Quantity - pounds** (`bean_quantity_2`, number, *optional*): Enter the quantity of the second type of coffee bean in pounds.
* **Syrup Type** (`syrup_type`, select_one, *required*): Select the type of syrup you'd like to order (Vanilla, Caramel, etc.).
* **Quantity - bottles or liters** (`syrup_quantity`, number, *required*): Enter the quantity of syrup in bottles or liters.
* **Bottle Size** (`bottle_size`, select_one, *required*): Choose the bottle size for your syrup order (750 ml, 1 liter, etc.).
* **Order Additional Syrups** (`add_more_syrups`, select_one, *optional*): Select 'Yes' if you'd like to order additional syrups.
* **Second Syrup Type** (`syrup_type_2`, select_one, *optional*): Select an additional type of syrup you'd like to order.
* **Quantity - bottles or liters** (`syrup_quantity_2`, number, *optional*): Enter the quantity of the second type of syrup in bottles or liters.
* **Special Requests or Notes** (`special_requests`, text, *optional*): Enter any special requests or notes for your order.

Note: The fields marked with an asterisk (\*) are required fields. Please ensure they are completed to avoid any issues with your order.
