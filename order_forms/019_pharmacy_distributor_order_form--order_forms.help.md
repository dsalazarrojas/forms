# Pharmacy Distributor Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help pharmacy distributors efficiently process orders for various products, allowing them to manage and track orders in an organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields with the necessary information for the order.
2.  Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
3.  Submit the form once you have entered all the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Order Form** (`distributor_order_form`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the order details for the pharmacy distributor to process.
*   **Product Form** (`product_order_form`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the product details for the order.
*   **Product Form 2** (`product_order_form_1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter additional product details for the order if necessary.
*   **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the payment details for the order.
*   **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the delivery details for the order.
*   **Order Summary** (`order_summary`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief summary of the order for tracking and reference.
*   **Order Status** (`order_status`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the current status of the order for tracking.
*   **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the order.
*   **Order Form 2** (`distributor_order_form_2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter additional order details if needed.
*   **Product Form 2** (`product_order_form_2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter additional product details if needed.
