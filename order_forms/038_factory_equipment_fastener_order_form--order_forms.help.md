# Factory Equipment Fastener Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the ordering of fasteners for factory equipment. Fill out this form to provide the necessary information for our team to process your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Machinery ID on page 1.
2. Select the order date on page 1.
3. Enter the type of fastener you need on page 1.
4. Specify the quantity of fasteners required on page 1.
5. Provide delivery details, including any special instructions or notes, on page 1.
6. Select the status of your order: whether it's "Order" or "Cancel" on page 1.
7. Check the boxes to select the purchasing team(s), production team(s), and maintenance team(s) that will be working on the project on page 1.
8. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Machinery ID (1)
* **Machinery ID**: Enter the unique ID of the machinery you are ordering fasteners for. This helps us ensure that the correct fasteners are ordered for the correct equipment.

### Order Date (2)
* **Order Date**: Select the date you would like the fasteners to be delivered. This ensures that your order is processed in a timely manner.

### Fastener Type (3)
* **Fastener Type**: Enter the specific type of fastener you need, such as screw, nut, or bolt.

### Fastener Quantity (4)
* **Fastener Quantity**: Specify the number of fasteners you need. This helps us accurately process your order.

### Delivery Details (5)
* **Delivery Details**: Provide any special instructions or notes about the delivery, such as delivery location or special handling requirements.

### Status (6)
* **Status**: Select "Order" to confirm your order or "Cancel" to cancel your order.

### Purchasing Team (7)
* **Purchasing Team**: Check the box if the purchasing team will be involved in your project. This helps us prioritize your order and assign the correct team members.

### Production Team (8)
* **Production Team**: Check the box if the production team will be working on your project. This helps us ensure that your order is received on time and is properly installed.

### Maintenance Team (9)
* **Maintenance Team**: Check the box if the maintenance team will be involved in your project. This helps us schedule maintenance and repairs according to your needs.

### Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting your form to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will contact you if we need any additional information to process your order.
