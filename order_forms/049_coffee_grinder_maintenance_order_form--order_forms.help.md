# Coffee Grinder Maintenance Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a maintenance order for a coffee grinder. It gathers crucial information to assess and address issues with the grinder.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Provide all requested information for each section.
- If you are unsure about any field, please describe your concerns in the field provided (e.g., "Not sure" or "Not applicable").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): This field is used to collect your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): This field is used to collect your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): This field is used to collect your phone number for further communication.
* **Business or Cafe Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): If you are a business or a cafe, please provide your name.
* **Grinder Brand** (`grinder_brand`, text, required): This field is used to collect the brand of the grinder.
* **Grinder Model** (`grinder_model`, text, required): This field is used to collect the model of the grinder.
* **Approximate Age of Grinder** (`grinder_age`, select_one, required): Please select the age of your grinder from the provided options.
* **Serial Number** (`serial_number`, text, optional): If you have the serial number of the grinder, please provide it.
* **Type of Maintenance Needed** (`maintenance_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of maintenance required from the provided options.
* **Description of Issues or Concerns** (`issue_description`, text, required): Describe the problems you are experiencing with your grinder.
* **Grind Consistency Issues** (`grind_consistency`, text, optional): If you are experiencing grind consistency issues, provide a description.
* **Unusual Sounds or Vibration** (`unusual_sounds`, text, optional): If you are experiencing unusual sounds or vibration, describe it.
* **Service Location Preference** (`service_location`, select_one, required): Select your preferred service location from the provided options.
* **Preferred Service Date** (`preferred_date`, date, optional): If you have a preferred service date, provide it.
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency`, select_one, required): Select the urgency level of your maintenance needs.
* **Budget Limit** (`budget_constraint`, number, optional): If you have a budget limit, provide an approximate amount.
* **Authorize Repair if Cost Exceeds Budget** (`authorization`, select_one, optional): If you are willing to authorize a repair that may exceed your budget, select the corresponding option.
* **Special Requests or Notes** (`special_requests`, text, optional): If you have any additional instructions or requests, provide them in this field.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required): The date on which the order is being submitted.

Note: If you are unsure about any field, please describe your concerns in the field provided (e.g., "Not sure" or "Not applicable").
