# CardioMessenger Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help healthcare organizations order CardioMessenger devices with ease. It guides you through the process of providing essential information about your organization, contact person, and device requirements, ensuring that we can tailor our support and delivery to meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your organization's name and type.
2.  Provide your contact person's name, title, and email address.
3.  Enter your contact person's phone number and organization address.
4.  Select the type of support you need for the installation.
5.  Choose the device model you wish to order.
6.  Specify the quantity of devices you need.
7.  Estimate the expected monthly usage.
8.  Select your preferred payment method.
9.  Provide any special requirements or notes.
10.  Confirm that you agree to the CardioMessenger terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, required): Enter your organization's name, e.g., "ABC Medical Center".
* **Organization Type** (`organization_type`, required): Select the type of healthcare facility, e.g., "Hospital", "Clinic", "Medical Practice", etc.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person_name`, required): Enter the primary person's name for this order.
* **Contact Person Title** (`contact_person_title`, required): Enter the contact person's title, e.g., "Manager", "Director", "Procurement Officer".
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, required): Enter a valid email address to receive order confirmation.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, required): Enter the contact person's phone number, including the area code.
* **Organization Address** (`organization_address`, required): Enter the street address for delivery.
* **City** (`city`, required): Enter the city where the organization is located.
* **State or Province** (`state_province`, required): Enter the state or province where the organization is located.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, required): Enter the postal code for the organization.
* **Country** (`country`, required): Enter the country where the organization is located.
* **CardioMessenger Device Model** (`device_model`, required): Select the device model you wish to order, e.g., "CardioMessenger Pro", "CardioMessenger Standard", etc.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, required): Specify the number of devices you need to order.
* **Expected Monthly Usage** (`expected_monthly_usage`, optional): Estimate the number of patients who will use the CardioMessenger.
* **Installation and Support Needed?** (`installation_support`, required): Select the level of support you need for installation, e.g., "Yes - Full onsite installation", "Yes - Remote technical support only".
* **Staff Training Required?** (`training_required`, required): Select the level of training your staff needs, e.g., "Yes - Comprehensive training", "Yes - Basic operation only".
* **Current Patient Monitoring System** (`current_system`, optional): Provide information about the current system you are using.
* **Integration with Existing Systems?** (`integration_requirements`, optional): Select if you need integration with existing software.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method`, required): Select your preferred payment method, e.g., "Credit Card", "Bank Transfer".
* **Purchase Order Number** (`po_number`, optional): Enter a purchase order number if applicable.
* **Any Special Requirements or Notes?** (`special_requirements`, optional): Enter any special requirements or notes about your order.
* **Estimated Deployment Date** (`estimated_deployment`, optional): Specify when you need the devices to be operational.
* **I agree to the CardioMessenger terms and conditions** (`terms_agreement`, required): Select "Yes, I agree" to proceed with the order.
