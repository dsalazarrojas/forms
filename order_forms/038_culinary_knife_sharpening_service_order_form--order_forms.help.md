## Purpose
This form is used to place an order for our culinary knife sharpening service. Please provide the required information to ensure your knives are properly sharpened and delivered on time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant options for each field and submit it. If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact us.

* **Customer** (Select One): Please choose your name from the list to identify yourself as the customer.
* **Knifetype** (Select One): Select the type of knife you have that needs sharpening.
* **Number of Knives** (Number): Enter the number of knives you would like us to sharpen.
* **Sharpening Service** (Select One): Choose from Basic, Premium, or Deluxe to select the sharpening service level you prefer.
* **Pickup Date** (Date): Select a date for when you'd like to pick up your sharpened knives.
* **Drop-off Date** (Date): If you'd like your knives returned on a different date than pickup, select this date.
* **Pickup Time** (Time): Select a time for when you'd like to pick up your sharpened knives.
* **Drop-off Time** (Time): If you'd like your knives returned on a different time than pickup, select this time.
* **Online Payment** (Select One): Choose how you'd like to pay for the service (PayPal or Stripe).
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address for communication and order confirmation.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number for any additional communication or to confirm the order details.
