# outdoor_service_quote_request - Help Guide

## Purpose
The outdoor_service_quote_request form is used to collect information from customers requesting outdoor services. This form will help you gather essential details about the customer, their project, and the required services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely, ensuring that all required fields are filled in.
2. Enter your First Name and Last Name as requested.
3. Provide a valid Email address that we can use to contact you.
4. Enter your Phone number for easy communication.
5. Describe your Project Details, including any specific requirements or needs.
6. Select the type of service you are requesting (Yes/No).
7. Enter your Address for service delivery and communication purposes.
8. Select the Start Date for the service.
9. Select the End Date for the service.
10. Specify the Quote Price for the service (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, Required): Enter your first name as per your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, Optional): Enter your last name (if applicable).
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, Required): Provide a valid email address we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, Required): Enter your phone number for easy communication.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, `text`, Required): Describe your project, including any specific requirements or needs.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, `select_multiple`, Required): Select the type of service you are requesting (Yes/No).
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, Required): Enter your address for service delivery and communication purposes.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, Required): Select the start date for the service.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, Required): Select the end date for the service.
* **Quote Price** (`quote_price`, `number`, Required): Specify the price for the service (if applicable).
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, Optional): Add any additional notes about your request.
