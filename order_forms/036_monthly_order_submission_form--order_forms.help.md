# Monthly Order Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Monthly Order Submission Form is a tool used to collect and document monthly order submissions from authorized personnel. It helps to ensure that all necessary information is provided accurately and efficiently, facilitating smooth order processing and delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the submission date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
2. Provide delivery details, including any relevant information about the delivery process.
3. Select whether the product will be delivered (Yes/No).
4. Enter the delivery address if the product will be delivered to a specific location.
5. Add any delivery notes that are relevant to the order.
6. If there are special requests, include them in the "Special Requests" field.
7. Enter the submission date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you are submitting the order for. 
* **Delivery Details**: Provide any relevant information about the delivery process, such as delivery methods or special instructions.
* **Product Orders**: Select 'Yes' to indicate that the product will be delivered, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Delivery Address**: Enter the address to which the product will be delivered, if applicable.
* **Delivery Notes**: Add any additional information that is relevant to the delivery process.
* **Special Requests**: If there are any special requests or specific requirements for the order, include them here.
