# Warehouse Space Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warehouse Space Booking Form is designed to collect information from customers to facilitate smooth warehouse operations. It helps warehouse staff to understand when and where customers want to book space, and with whom. This ensures that the warehouse can efficiently allocate space and resources according to customer needs.
## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select the "Bookings" page.
2.  Fill in your Customer Name.
3.  Choose your Date Of Arrival.
4.  Select your Time Of Arrival.
5.  Specify the duration of your stay.
6.  Choose the type of space you require (e.g., Warehouse, Storefront, Parking, Yard, or Office).
7.  Select the size of the space you need (Large, Medium, Small, or Mini).
8.  Indicate the status of the space you wish to book (Available, In Use, Reserved, or Closed).
9.  Enter your Customer Phone.
10.  Enter your Customer Email.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Bookings**: This is the main page of the form. Please select it to begin filling in the form.
* **Customer Name**: Please enter your name as the customer who is booking the space.
* **Date Of Arrival**: Choose the date you plan to arrive at the warehouse.
* **Time Of Arrival**: Select the time you plan to arrive at the warehouse.
* **Duration**: Specify the duration of your stay at the warehouse.
* **Space Type**: Select the type of space you require (e.g., Warehouse, Storefront, Parking, Yard, or Office).
* **Space Size**: Select the size of the space you need (Large, Medium, Small, or Mini).
* **Space Status**: Indicate the status of the space you wish to book (Available, In Use, Reserved, or Closed).
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
