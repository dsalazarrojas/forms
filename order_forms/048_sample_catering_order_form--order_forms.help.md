# Sample Catering Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to provide their catering order details, contact information, and payment preferences.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Contact Information** (`customer_email`, `customer_phone`, `customer_message`): <small>This section is used to collect your contact information.</small>
*   **Delivery Information** (`delivery_info`): <small>Choose whether you want to receive your order delivery or not.</small>
*   **Order Details** (`order_details`): <small>Select "Yes" if you want to place an order.</small>
*   **Date** (`date`): <small>Enter the date you'd like to receive your order.</small>
*   **Time** (`time`): <small>Enter the time you'd like to receive your order.</small>
*   **Payment** (`payment`): <small>Choose whether you want to pay on-site or through a different method.</small>
