# Holiday Gift Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holiday Gift Order Form is designed for customers to place an order for gifts for their loved ones during the holiday season. Please provide the necessary information to help us fulfill your gift request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and fill in all required fields (those marked with *).
2. Select the desired gift from the provided options.
3. Enter your email address and phone number for communication and order details.
4. Add any additional message if needed (e.g., gift card information or special requests).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Form** (`form_1`): Enter a brief description of the gift recipient's name, their relationship to you, and a short message.
* **Payment Information** (`form_2`): This field is currently empty, indicating no payment information is required at this time.
* **Buy Now Pay Later** (`form_3`): This field is not currently applicable, indicating this payment option is not available.
* **Product** (`form_4`): Select one of the available gift options: Gift 1, Gift 2, or Gift 3.
* **Quantity** (`form_5`): Enter the desired quantity of the selected gift.
* **Total** (`form_6`): This field will be automatically updated based on your selection and is not editable.
* **Email** (`form_7`): Enter your email address for communication and order details.
* **Phone** (`form_8`): Enter your phone number for further contact and confirmation.
* **Message** (`form_9`): Add any additional information or special requests for the gift, such as gift card details or personalized messages.
