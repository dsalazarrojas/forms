# Swimming Pool Construction Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Swimming Pool Construction Intake Form is designed to help contractors and clients collaborate on pool projects by collecting necessary details about the project.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, select the type of pool you are interested in from the "Pool Type" dropdown menu.
2. Choose the size of the pool you need from the "Pool Size" list.
3. Select the shape of the pool you want from the "Pool Shape" options.
4. Choose the color of the pool from the "Pool Color" options.
5. Select any features you'd like to include in your pool, such as spas, tanning decks, or waterfalls.
6. Enter the location of the pool, including the address.
7. Select your preferred contact method for communication with the contractor, such as landline, cell, or email.
8. Enter the name of the contractor you would like to work with.
9. Select the preferred contact method for the contractor, such as landline, cell, or email.
10. Choose the project status from the options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pool Type** (`Pool Type`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Select the type of pool you are interested in, such as Above Ground Pools, In-Ground Pools, or In-Ground Pools (Spas).
* **Pool Size** (`Pool Size`, `select_multiple`, required):
	+ Choose the size of the pool you need, such as Small, Medium, or Large.
* **Pool Shape** (`Pool Shape`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Select the shape of the pool you want, such as Rectangular, Freeform, or Kidney-Shaped.
* **Pool Color** (`Pool Color`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Choose the color of the pool you prefer, such as Blue, Green, or Black.
* **Pool Features** (`Pool Features`, `select_multiple`, required):
	+ Select any features you'd like to include in your pool, such as Spas, Tanning Decks, or Waterfalls.
* **Location** (`Location`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter the address where the pool will be built.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `select_multiple`, required):
	+ Select your preferred contact method for communication with the contractor, such as Landline, Cell, or Email.
* **Contractor Name** (`Contractor Name`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter the name of the contractor you would like to work with.
* **Contractor Contact** (`Contractor Contact`, `select_multiple`, required):
	+ Select the preferred contact method for the contractor, such as Landline, Cell, or Email.
* **Project Status** (`Project Status`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Choose the project status, such as Active, On Hold, or Cancelled.

Note: Please fill in all the required fields to ensure accurate project information and prevent delays.
