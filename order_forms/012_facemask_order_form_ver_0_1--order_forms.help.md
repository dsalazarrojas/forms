# facemask order form (ver 0.1) - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers who want to order facemasks. It is intended to gather customer details and preferences for efficient order processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the shipping information on the "shipping info" field.
2. Provide the "delivery info" on the next page.
3. Indicate whether you want to order any items by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each option in the "order items" field.
4. Enter your payment information in the "payment info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **shipping_info** (shipping info, text, optional): Enter your shipping address and any other information relevant to the delivery of your order.
* **delivery_info** (delivery info, text, optional): Provide details about where you would like to receive your order. This could be a physical address, email, or phone number.
* **order_items** (order items, select multiple, optional): Select "Yes" for any items you would like to order. This will allow us to process your order accordingly.
* **payment_info** (payment info, text, optional): Enter your payment method and details, such as credit card number or bank account information.
