# Quick Release Connector Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for ordering a Quick Release Connector, and it is meant to be completed by both the supplier and the customer. It is designed to collect relevant information from both parties involved in the transaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **Supplier Info** section with your contact information as the supplier.
2. In the **Customer Details** section, provide your customer's contact information.
3. In the **Delivery Preferences** section, specify the delivery details for the Quick Release Connector.
4. In the **Order Confirmation** section, confirm the order details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Supplier Info** (Supplier Info, text, required: false)
Provide your company name, address, and any other relevant contact information as the supplier.

### **Customer Details** (Customer Details, text, required: false)
Enter the customer's name, address, and any other relevant contact information.

### **Delivery Preferences** (Delivery Preferences, text, required: false)
Specify the delivery date, time, and any special instructions for delivering the Quick Release Connector.

### **Order Confirmation** (Order Confirmation, text, required: false)
Confirm the order details, including the Quick Release Connector's specifications and quantity.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check the information provided in each section to ensure accuracy.
- Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, refer to the supplier's guidelines or contact them for clarification.
