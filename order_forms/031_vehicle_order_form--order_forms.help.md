# Vehicle Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Order Form is designed to collect information about vehicles being ordered. This form is not meant for payments, but rather for recording order details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information for the vehicle being ordered.
2. Select the correct category and vehicle type for the order.
3. Choose the year and trim level of the vehicle.
4. Select the transmission and drive type for the vehicle.
5. Enter the mileage and price of the vehicle.
6. Enter the order quantity and date.
7. Choose the delivery date and time (if applicable).
8. Add any additional notes or comments about the order.
9. Enter the contact information for the customer (if not already provided).
10. Choose the report status for the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Order Form** (`form_title`, required/optional): Enter the title of the vehicle being ordered.
* **Category** (`category`, optional): Select the category of the form from the options provided.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, optional): Choose the type of vehicle being ordered from the options provided.
* **Make Model** (`make_model`, optional): Enter the make and model of the vehicle being ordered.
* **Year** (`year`, optional): Choose the year of the vehicle being ordered.
* **Trim Level** (`trim_level`, optional): Enter the trim level of the vehicle being ordered.
* **Transmission** (`transmission`, optional): Choose the transmission type of the vehicle being ordered.
* **Drive Type** (`drive_type`, optional): Select the drive type of the vehicle being ordered.
* **Vehicle Status** (`vehicle_status`, optional): Choose the status of the vehicle being ordered.
* **Mileage** (`mileage`, optional): Enter the mileage of the vehicle being ordered.
* **Price** (`price`, optional): Enter the price of the vehicle being ordered.
* **Order Quantity** (`order_quantity`, optional): Enter the quantity of the vehicle being ordered.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, optional): Enter the date the vehicle was ordered.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, optional): Enter the date the vehicle is to be delivered.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, optional): Choose the time the vehicle is to be delivered.
* **Notes** (`notes`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the order.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, optional): Enter the email address of the customer.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Report Status** (`report_status`, optional): Choose the status of the order report.
* **Customer ID** (`customer_id`, optional): Enter the ID of the customer.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, optional): Enter the name of the customer.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, optional): Enter the email address of the customer.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, optional): Enter the phone number of the customer.
* **Customer Company** (`customer_company`, optional): Enter the company name of the customer.
