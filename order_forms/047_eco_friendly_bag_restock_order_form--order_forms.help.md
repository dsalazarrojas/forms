# Eco Friendly Bag Restock Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to submit orders for the eco-friendly bag restock. It is used for internal purposes to process and update order status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the form details (Form Details) to provide us with your general information about the order.
2. Provide your customer details (Customer Details) to help us get in touch with you.
3. Submit your delivery details (Delivery Details) to ensure smooth delivery of your order.
4. Fill in your order details (Order Details) to specify what you're ordering.
5. Provide payment details (Payment Details) to complete the payment process.
6. Add any order notes (Order Notes) if needed.
7. Select the form submitted status (Form Submitted).
8. Fill in your customer name, email, phone, and zip code (Customer Details).
9. Select the order date and delivery date (Order Date and Delivery Date).
10. Provide your customer address (Customer Address).
11. Specify the order quantity, product type, color, and size (Order Quantity, Product Type, Product Color, Product Size).
12. Choose your payment method (Payment Method).
13. Fill in your customer comments (Customer Comments).
14. Specify the order total (Order Total).
15. Choose the order status (Order Status) and delivery status (Delivery Status).
16. Add any customer notes (Customer Notes).
17. Finally, select the form submitted date (Form Submitted Date).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Details (1)**: Enter a brief description of your order.
* **Customer Details (2)**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number so we can contact you.
* **Delivery Details (3)**: Enter your delivery address to ensure smooth delivery.
* **Order Details (4)**: Specify what product you're ordering (Product Type), its color, size, and quantity.
* **Payment Details (5)**: Choose how you'd like to pay for your order.
* **Order Notes (6)**: Add any extra comments about your order.
* **Form Submitted (7)**: Select whether the form has been submitted or not.
* **Customer Name (8)**: Enter your name.
* **Customer Email (9)**: Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone (10)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Order Date (11)**: Enter the date you placed the order.
* **Delivery Date (12)**: Enter the date you'd like the order delivered.
* **Customer Address (13)**: Enter your delivery address.
* **Order Quantity (14)**: Specify the quantity of the ordered product.
* **Product Type (15)**: Select the type of product you're ordering.
* **Product Color (16)**: Select the color of the product.
* **Product Size (17)**: Select the size of the product.
* **Payment Method (18)**: Choose how you'd like to pay for your order.
* **Customer Comments (19)**: Add any extra comments about your order.
* **Order Total (20)**: Enter the total cost of your order.
* **Customer Zip (21)**: Enter your zip code.
* **Order Status (22)**: Choose the status of your order (Pending, Completed, Cancelled).
* **Delivery Status (23)**: Choose the status of your delivery (Pending, Delivered, Cancelled).
* **Customer Notes (24)**: Add any extra comments about your order.
* **Form Submitted Date (25)**: This is filled in automatically.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that all date fields are in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* Make sure you've chosen the correct payment method and delivery status.
* If you have any special comments or requirements, please add them in the 'Order Notes' field.
* Double-check all the details before submitting the form.
* You can contact us if you need any help or clarification.
