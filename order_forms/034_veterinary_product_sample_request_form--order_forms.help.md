# Veterinary Product Sample Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to request veterinary product samples for various purposes, such as product testing, quality control, or research. To ensure accurate and efficient processing, please fill out the form completely and correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Form Details" page and choose the correct option (Yes or No) to indicate whether this is a new or existing request.
2. Fill out the "Clinic Info" field with your clinic's name, address, and other relevant contact information.
3. Choose the "Contact Person" option (Yes or No) to indicate whether someone will be available to receive the sample.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field to ensure we can contact you with updates and instructions.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field to receive notifications and updates.
6. Select "Sample Requests" (Yes or No) to indicate whether you need a sample for testing, quality control, or other purposes.
7. Fill out the "Assigned To" field with the name of the user or team responsible for handling the sample.
8. Fill out the "Assigned By" field with the name of the person or team responsible for assigning the task.
9. Select the "Assigned Date" and "Assigned Time" fields to schedule the sample delivery.
10. Confirm that the request has been "Submitted By" (Yes or No) and the "Submitted Time" field to record the submission time.
11. Fill out the "Form Title" field with a brief description of the requested sample.
12. Select "Form Category" (Yes or No) to indicate the category of the request.
13. In the "Form Description" field, provide a detailed description of the requested sample and its purpose.
14. Fill out the "Assigned Tool" field with the name of the tool or equipment used for the request.
15. (Optional) If you wish to assign a specific user to handle the request, fill out the "Assigned User" field.
16. (Optional) If you wish to assign a specific tool to handle the request, fill out the "Submitted Tool" field.
17. Fill out the "Form Version" field with the current version of the form.
18. Confirm that the "Updated" (Yes or No) field is "Yes" to indicate that the form has been updated.
19. (Optional) If you wish to provide any additional comments, fill out the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Details**: This page lets you choose whether this is a new or existing request. (Required: Yes/No)
* **Clinic Info**: Enter your clinic's name, address, and other relevant contact information. (Required: Yes)
* **Contact Person**: Confirm if someone will be available to receive the sample. (Optional)
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication and updates. (Required: Yes)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for notifications and updates. (Optional)
* **Sample Requests**: Choose whether you need a sample for testing, quality control, or other purposes. (Required: Yes/No)
* **Assigned To**: Identify the user or team responsible for handling the sample.
* **Assigned By**: Identify the person or team responsible for assigning the task.
* **Assigned Date**: Schedule the sample delivery date.
* **Assigned Time**: Schedule the sample delivery time.
* **Submitted By**: Confirm that the request was submitted. (Required: Yes/No)
* **Submitted Time**: Record the submission time.
* **Form Title**: Provide a brief description of the requested sample.
* **Form Category**: Confirm the category of the request. (Optional)
* **Form Description**: Provide a detailed description of the requested sample and its purpose.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the name of the tool or equipment used for the request.
* **Assigned User**: (Optional) Identify a specific user to handle the request.
* **Submitted User**: (Optional) Identify a specific user who submitted the request.
* **Submitted Tool**: (Optional) Enter the name of the tool or equipment used for the request.
* **Form Version**: Enter the current version of the form.
* **Updated**: Confirm that the form has been updated. (Optional)
