# Toilet Paper Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Toilet Paper Order Form" is a form used to collect customer information and order details for toilet paper orders.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the "Customer Details" section with your name and any other relevant contact information.
2.  Select the type of order you are placing ("Retail", "Wholesale", or "Bulk") in the "Order Type" field.
3.  Choose the type of product you are ordering ("Toilet Paper", "Hand Towels", or "Paper Towels") in the "Product Type" field.
4.  Enter the quantity of the ordered product in the "Quantity" field.
5.  Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
6.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7.  Choose a delivery date in the "Delivery Date" field.
8.  Select a delivery time in the "Delivery Time" field.
9.  Enter any additional notes or comments about your order in the "Note" field.
10. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Gateway" field.
11. If you have any custom options, select them in the "Custom Option 1", "Custom Option 2", and "Custom Option 3" fields.
12. Enter your address, city, state, zip code, and country in the "Address", "City", "State", and "Zip Code" fields.
13. Enter your customer signature in the "Customer Signature" field.
14. If you are placing an order on behalf of a company, enter the company name, phone number, and email address in the "Company", "Store Phone", and "Store Email" fields.
15. Enter your tax ID number in the "Tax ID" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details**: This section collects your name, contact information, and other relevant details.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Order Type**: Select the type of order you are placing ("Retail", "Wholesale", or "Bulk").
* **Product Type**: Choose the type of product you are ordering ("Toilet Paper", "Hand Towels", or "Paper Towels").
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of the product you are ordering.
* **Email**: Provide your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Delivery Date**: Choose a delivery date for your order.
* **Delivery Time**: Select a delivery time for your order.
* **Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Payment Gateway**: Select your preferred payment method ("PayPal", "Stripe", or "Square").
* **Custom Option 1**: Select any custom options 1.
* **Custom Option 2**: Select any custom option 2.
* **Custom Option 3**: Select any custom option 3.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **State**: Enter your state.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code.
* **Country**: Enter your country.
* **Customer Signature**: Enter your customer signature.
* **Company**: If you are placing an order on behalf of a company, enter the company name.
* **Store Phone**: If you are placing an order on behalf of a company, enter the store phone number.
* **Store Email**: If you are placing an order on behalf of a company, enter the store email address.
* **Tax ID**: Enter your tax ID number.
* **Store Name**: Enter the name of the store placing the order.
* **Store Phone**: Enter the phone number of the store placing the order.
* **Store Email**: Enter the email address of the store placing the order.

## Tips

* Ensure that you are providing accurate and complete information to avoid any delays or issues with your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* Please review your order carefully before submitting it to ensure that everything is correct.
