# Suit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from customers to place a suit order. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure that your order is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to select the correct fabric type and color for your suit.
3. Choose the correct collar style, lapel style, and button style that suits your preferences.
4. Select the type of order you would like to place (Custom or Standard).
5. Enter the desired delivery date for your suit.
6. If you have any special instructions or comments, feel free to add them in the "Special Instructions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to ensure that we can contact you with updates on your order.
* **Height**: Enter your height in inches, as this will help us determine the correct fit for your suit.
* **Waist**: Enter your waist size in inches.
* **Sleeve Length**: Enter your sleeve length in inches.
* **Fabric Type**: Select the type of fabric you would like for your suit (Suit, Dress Shirt, Pants, Jacket, or Other).
* **Fabric Color**: Select the color of the fabric you would like for your suit (Black, White, Blue, Red, Green, Yellow, Gray, Brown, Purple, Pink, Orange, Silver, Gold, or Other).
* **Collar Style**: Select the collar style you would like for your suit (Classic, Spread Collar, Mandarin, or Other).
* **Lapel Style**: Select the lapel style you would like for your suit (Small, Large, None, or Other).
* **Button Style**: Select the button style you would like for your suit (Buttonholes, No Buttons, or Other).
* **Order Type**: Select the type of order you would like to place (Custom or Standard).
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the date you would like to receive your suit.
* **Special Instructions**: If you have any special instructions or comments, enter them here.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about your order.

Remember, accuracy is key when filling out this form to ensure that your suit is made to your specifications. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
