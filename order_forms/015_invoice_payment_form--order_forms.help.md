# invoice_payment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The invoice payment form is used for users to provide payment information for invoices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your User Name.
2. Enter your Email Address.
3. Enter your Phone Number.
4. Select a Payment Method from the provided options.
5. Enter the Amount Paid.
6. Select a Payment Date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter User Name**: Enter the name you would like to be used for your payment.
* **Enter Email**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Enter Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Select Payment Method**: Choose one or more payment methods that apply to you. Available options are Credit Card, Paypal, and Bank Transfer.
* **Enter Amount**: Enter the amount you are paying, in numeric format.
* **Select Payment Date**: Choose a date when the payment should be processed.
