# Home Appliance Stock Order Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the ordering of home appliances from a stock or inventory management system. The purpose of the form is to collect relevant information from the customer or requestor about the desired appliances, including contact details, delivery address, and order specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name in the "Contact Person" field.
2. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
3. Enter your delivery address in the "Delivery Address" field.
4. Provide a brief description of your order details in the "Order Details" field.
5. Select the date for the order in the "Order Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Person**: A text field where you can enter your name.
* **Contact Number**: A text field where you can enter your contact number.
* **Delivery Address**: A text field where you can enter your delivery address.
* **Order Details**: A note field where you can enter a brief description of your order.
* **Order Date**: A date field where you can select the date for your order.
