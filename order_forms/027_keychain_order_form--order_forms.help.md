# Keychain Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to place orders for keychains. Fill in the required fields to place your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the order details such as order specifics.
2. Provide your customer information.
3. Choose the delivery information.
4. Enter your payment information.
5. Confirm your order details.
6. Submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order** (`order_form`, text, optional): This field is for entering the order specifics such as any special requests or instructions.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): In this field, you can add any additional details for your order.
* **Order Items** (`order_items`, text, optional): This field is intended for listing the items ordered. Since it's for keychains, it might be a placeholder for more specific options.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and contact details for order tracking.
* **Delivery Info** (`delivery_info`, text, optional): Fill in the delivery address and any specific instructions for shipping.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, optional): Include your payment method and details for smooth processing.
* **Order Confirmation** (`order_confirmation`, text, optional): Verify your order details are correct before submitting.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, optional): Click here to submit your order.
* **Keychain Color** (`keychain_color`, select one, optional): Choose the color of your keychain from the given options: Red, Blue, Green.
* **Keychain Size** (`keychain_size`, select multiple, optional): Select the sizes you require for your keychain from the options: Small, Medium, Large.
* **Keychain Type** (`keychain_type`, select multiple, optional): Choose the type of keychain from the given options: Engraved, Printed.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all the necessary fields to ensure accurate order processing.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
