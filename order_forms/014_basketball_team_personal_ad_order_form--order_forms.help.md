# Basketball Team Personal Ad Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Basketball Team Personal Ad Order Form is designed for parents or team representatives to place a personal ad in the season's program celebrating their child's or team member's participation in the basketball team.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Complete the form accurately and thoroughly with the required information.
2.  Ensure all fields marked as "required" are filled in before proceeding.
3.  Review your selections carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Recognition Program Personal Ads --**: This section serves as a header or introduction for the personal ad section of the program. It is a note field and does not require any further input.
* **Purchaser Name**: Please enter the name of the person placing the order.
* **Purchaser Email**: Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Player Name to be Featured**: Enter the name of the basketball player to be featured in the personal ad.
* **Player Team/Division**: Select the division or team the player belongs to.
* **Select Ad Size**: Choose the size of the ad from the available options to fit your needs and budget.
*   *Full Page ($100)*: A full-page ad is a larger and more detailed ad.
*   *Half Page ($60)*: A half-page ad is a smaller and less detailed ad.
*   *Quarter Page ($35)*: A quarter-page ad is the smallest and most budget-friendly option.
*   *Shout Out Text Only ($15)*: A text-only ad is a simple ad with just text and no images.
* **Message for the Ad**: Enter a message for the player or team to be featured in the ad, with character limits depending on the ad size chosen.
*   *Max 250 characters for full/half page, 100 for quarter page*: Please keep the message concise within the character limit.
* **-- Photo Submission --**: This section is not required for this form, but it is a note field for additional information. You are to email high-resolution photos to ads@basketballteam.org with the name in the subject line.
* **Special Layout Instructions**: If you have any specific design requests or themes for the ad, you can enter them here.
* **Payment Selection**: Choose how you wish to pay for the ad, either online or through check.
*   *Paid Online*: Pay online through our secure payment portal.
*   *Check Mailed*: Pay through a check mailed to the address provided.
*   *Invoice Me*: You will receive an invoice for payment by mail.
* **I understand that ads must be submitted by the October 1st deadline**: Confirm that you understand the deadline for submitting the ad.
*   *I Understand*: A confirmation that you have read and understood the deadline requirement.
*   *Other*: If you need a different deadline, select this option and provide your own deadline.
* **Date of Submission**: Enter the date when you submitted this form.

Note that this form ensures that all necessary details are gathered in one place to create and print the personal ads on time. By completing this form accurately and thoroughly, you will help the team create the best possible ads for your child or team member.
