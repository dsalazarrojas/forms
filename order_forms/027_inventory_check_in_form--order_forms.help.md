# Inventory Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inventory Check In Form is a crucial tool for inventory management. It enables users to accurately update item quantities, ensuring that inventory levels reflect the actual stock on hand. This form helps users to track and record items as they are received, received by, and the date they were received.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a detailed description of the item being checked in.
2. Provide the correct quantity of the item.
3. Select the person who received the item.
4. Enter the date the item was received.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Item Description (text)**: A brief description of the item being checked in. This field helps the user to accurately identify the item and its corresponding quantity.
* **Item Quantity (number)**: Enter the correct quantity of the item. This field requires a numerical value to accurately update the inventory levels.
* **Received By (select_one)**: Select the person who received the item. This field ensures that responsibility is assigned to the correct personnel.
* **Date Received (date)**: Enter the date the item was received. This field is essential for accurate historical record-keeping.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and complete data entry.
* Double-check the quantity and date fields for correctness.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult the relevant personnel or contact your supervisor for guidance.
