# Color Street On Hand Stock By Mama Osita Manis - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to track and manage on-hand stock levels for a specific product, including essential details about the product and its stock status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: Owner name, Product code, Units on hand, and Reorder needed.
2. Optionally, fill in the following fields if applicable: Product description, Retail price, Preferred supplier, Last sold date, and Notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Owner name** (`owner`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or entity responsible for the product.
* **Product code** (`product_code`, text, required): Enter the unique code or identifier for the product.
* **Product description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the product, including its color and design details.
* **Units on hand** (`units_on_hand`, number, required): Enter the exact number of units available for the product.
* **Retail price** (`retail_price`, number, optional): Enter the current retail price of the product, if applicable.
* **Reorder needed** (`reorder_needed`, select_one, required): Indicate whether the product requires reordering by selecting 'True' or 'False'.
* **Preferred supplier** (`preferred_supplier`, text, optional): Enter the name of the preferred supplier for the product, if applicable.
* **Last sold date** (`last_sold_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the product was last sold, in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional information about the product or stock status.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out to ensure accurate and complete data.
* Use specific and correct formatting for dates, such as YYYY-MM-DD for the last sold date.
* If applicable, select 'True' for Reorder needed to indicate the product requires reordering.
