# Macarons Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Macarons Order Form is designed to collect order information from customers. It's a simple form that helps you understand what they want to order and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the order details section to provide information about the macarons you want to order.
2. Add your customer information, including any relevant details about yourself or the recipient of the order.
3. Enter your shipping details, so we can get the order to you quickly and smoothly.
4. Add any additional macaron details you'd like to share.
5. Once you've filled out all the necessary fields, click on the "Submit" button to send your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details**: This section is where you provide information about the macarons you want to order. Please enter a brief description of your order in this field.
* **Customer Info**: This is where you can provide your contact information or that of the recipient of the order. This includes your name, email address, and any other details you think we should know.
* **Shipping Details**: Here, you can provide your shipping address, including any special instructions or notes.
* **Macaron Details**: If you have any additional information about the macarons you're ordering, such as special requests or preferences, please add it here.
* **Submit**: This is the button you click to send your order. Once clicked, the form will be submitted, and we'll receive your order details.
* **Macarons Order**: This field is not used in the form and seems to be a duplicate or unnecessary field. Please ignore it.
* **Customer Order**: This field is also not used in the form and seems to be a duplicate or unnecessary field. Please ignore it. 

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all the necessary fields to ensure we can process your order correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
