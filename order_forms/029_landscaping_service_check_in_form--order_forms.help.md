# Landscaping Service Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from clients about their landscaping service needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your information, including your name and any relevant details about your service needs.
2. Select the type of service you require from the drop-down menu.
3. Enter your phone number and email address, if available.
4. Add any comments or notes about your service needs, if desired.
5. Sign and submit the form to complete the check in process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter your name, as it will be displayed in our records.
* **Service Provider** (`service_provider`, text, required): Enter the name of the service provider who will be performing the service.
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time, required): Select the time of arrival for the service call.
* **Job Address** (`job_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the location where the service will be performed.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your service needs.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of service you require from the drop-down menu.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if available.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, if available.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your service needs.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, text, optional): Sign and date your signature to confirm your completion of the form.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a smooth service delivery process.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask a staff member for assistance.
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact our support team.
