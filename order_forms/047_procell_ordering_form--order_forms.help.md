# procell_ordering_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The procell_ordering_form is designed to collect information for customer orders, allowing you to efficiently manage and process orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer's name in the **Customer Name** field.
2. Enter your customer's email address in the **Customer Email** field.
3. Enter your customer's phone number in the **Customer Phone** field.
4. Choose the date of the order in the **Order Date** field.
5. Select the time of the order in the **Order Time** field.
6. Choose the type of order in the **Order Type** field.
7. Provide any additional details about the order in the **Order Details** field.
8. Enter your customer's address in the **Customer Address** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): This is where you enter your customer's name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): This field is for your customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your customer's phone number here.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required: false): Select the date of the order from the calendar.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, required: false): Choose the time of the order.
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' or 'No' depending on the type of order.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, note, required: false): Provide any additional details about the order.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, required: false): Enter your customer's address here.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are filled out correctly and accurately to avoid errors and duplication of orders.
- Double-check the **Order Type** field to ensure you've selected the correct type of order.
- Be thorough when filling out the **Order Details** field to provide enough information about the order.
- Enter your customer's address accurately in the **Customer Address** field to ensure delivery or pickup is possible.
