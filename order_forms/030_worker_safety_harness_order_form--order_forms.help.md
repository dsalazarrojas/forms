# Worker Safety Harness Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Worker Safety Harness Order Form is used to collect details for a worker safety harness order. This form helps us understand what the customer needs and ensures we get it right on the first try.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in your personal details (name, email, phone number, and order description).
2. Select the type of safety equipment you are ordering (Harness, Helmet, or Glove).
3. Specify the quantity of equipment you are ordering.
4. If necessary, enter the order date and any special instructions.
5. Sign and date the form (both customer and employee).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details**: Enter a brief description of the order.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name for verification purposes only.
* **Customer E-mail**: Enter your company email address, if applicable.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Order Description**: Enter a brief description of the order for our team to reference.
* **Safety Equipment Type**: Select the type of safety equipment you are ordering. This can be Harness, Helmet, or Glove.
* **Quantity of Equipment Ordered**: Enter the number of units you are ordering.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date you would like to receive the order. This is optional and used for internal planning purposes.
* **Special Instructions**: If you have any special requests or requirements, enter them here.
* **Customer Signature**: Sign and date this section to confirm your order.
* **Employee Signature**: Our employee will sign and date this section to confirm receipt of the order.

Note: This form is not intended for repeat orders or large project orders. If you are unsure, please contact your sales representative or refer to our project order form for more information.
