# Retail Safety Equipment Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for retail businesses to order safety equipment. It helps the organization track orders, manage inventory, and maintain customer relationships.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form correctly:

1.  Enter the necessary information for the business and contact person.
2.  Select the delivery address and state from the dropdown lists.
3.  Enter the product details and quantity.
4.  Select the delivery date and time.
5.  Choose the delivery status.
6.  Enter any additional information.
