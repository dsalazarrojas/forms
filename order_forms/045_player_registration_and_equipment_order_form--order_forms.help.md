# Player Registration And Equipment Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from players and their families. The goal of the form is to gather necessary information such as contact details, equipment preferences, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select your preferred equipment from the options provided.
3. Review your payment information carefully.
4. Confirm your submission by selecting the correct option on the submit field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Details** (`player_details`, text, required: false): This field is used to enter the player's name and any other relevant details.
* **Select Equipment** (`equipment_selection`, select multiple, required: false): Select one or more of the following equipment options:
	+ Baseball Bat
	+ Baseball Glove
	+ Baseball Pants
	+ Baseball Shoes
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, required: false): Enter the payment information for the selected equipment.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select one, required: false): Select `True` to submit the form, and `False` to cancel.
* **Notes** (`player_notes`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about your submission.
* **Email** (`player_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`player_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`player_address`, text, required: false): Enter your address.
* **City** (`player_city`, text, required: false): Enter your city name.
* **State** (`player_state`, text, required: false): Enter your state or province.
* **Zip** (`player_zip`, text, required: false): Enter your zip or postal code.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you need help or have questions, please contact us.
* If you are a coach or administrator, please submit this form for each player separately.
