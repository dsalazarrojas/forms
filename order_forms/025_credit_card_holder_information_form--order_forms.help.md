# credit_card_holder_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The credit_card_holder_information_form is a form used to collect information about a credit card holder's details for the purpose of processing a credit card-related service request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name and other relevant details in the "credit_card_holder" field.
2.  Provide your credit card number in the "credit_card_number" field.
3.  Select the expiration date of your credit card from the "expiration_date" dropdown menu.
4.  Enter the expiration year of your credit card in the "expiration_year" field.
5.  Enter the Card Verification Value (CVV) code found on the back of your credit card in the "cvv" field.
6.  Confirm your CVV code by entering it again in the "cvv_confirmation" field.
7.  Select your credit card type from the "card_type" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **credit_card_holder** (text, required: false): Enter the name of the credit card holder.
* **credit_card_number** (number, required: false): Enter the credit card number without any spaces or hyphens.
* **expiration_date** (select_multiple, required: false): Select the month your credit card expires.
* **expiration_year** (number, required: false): Enter the year your credit card expires.
* **cvv** (number, required: false): Enter the Card Verification Value (CVV) code found on the back of your credit card.
* **cvv_confirmation** (note, required: false): Confirm your CVV code.
* **card_type** (select_one, required: false): Select the type of your credit card from the list of options.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions to complete this form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for assistance.
