# prom_photography_order_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers interested in purchasing a photo package for a specific event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your contact information (First Name, Email, and Phone).
* Provide a brief description of the event (Event Details).
* Select the photo package that suits your needs (Photo Package).
* Choose the type of photo that you would like (Photo Type).
* Select the location where the photo will be taken (Photo Location).
* Choose the size and orientation of the photo (Photo Size and Photo Orientation).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Event Details** (text): Provide a brief description of the event, including any relevant details that may help us serve you better.
* **Photo Package** (select_one): Choose a photo package that suits your needs. Options include:
	+ Senior
	+ Couple
	+ Group
* **Photo Type** (select_multiple): Select the type of photo you would like. Options include:
	+ Single
	+ Couple
	+ Group
* **Photo Location** (select_one): Choose the location where the photo will be taken. Options include:
	+ School
	+ Home
	+ Outdoor
* **Photo Size** (select_multiple): Select the size of the photo you would like. Options include:
	+ Small
	+ Medium
	+ Large
* **Photo Orientation** (select_multiple): Select the orientation of the photo you would like. Options include:
	+ Landscape
	+ Portrait
	+ Square
