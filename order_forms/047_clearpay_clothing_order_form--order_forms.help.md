# Clearpay Clothing Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect order information for clothing items. It is used to gather customer and shipping details, as well as specifics about the order such as items, prices, and quantities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Customer Information**: 
   - **Full Name**: Enter your complete name as it appears on your identification.
   - **Email Address**: Enter your email address for order confirmation and updates.
   - **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
2. **Shipping Address**: 
   - **Street Address**: Enter your full shipping address.
   - **City**: Enter the city where you would like to receive your order.
   - **State/Province**: Enter the state or province where you would like to receive your order.
   - **ZIP/Postal Code**: Enter the postal code where you would like to receive your order.
   - **Country**: Enter the country of delivery.
3. **Clothing Items**: 
   - **Item 1 Description**: Enter a description of the first clothing item.
   - **Item 1 Size**: Select the size of the first clothing item from the options provided.
   - **Item 1 Color**: Enter the color of the first clothing item.
   - **Item 1 Quantity**: Enter the number of first clothing items you would like to order.
   - **Item 1 Price Per Unit**: Enter the price for each first clothing item.
   - **Item 2 Description**: Enter a description of the second clothing item (if applicable).
   - **Item 2 Size**: Select the size of the second clothing item from the options provided (if applicable).
   - **Item 2 Quantity**: Enter the number of second clothing items you would like to order (if applicable).
   - **Additional Items**: Enter any other clothing items you would like to order.
4. **Order Summary and Pricing**: 
   - **Subtotal**: This is the total before tax and shipping.
   - **Shipping Cost**: Enter the cost of delivery.
   - **Tax Amount**: Enter the sales tax amount (if applicable).
   - **Discount Code**: Enter any applicable discount code.
   - **Discount Amount**: Enter the amount discounted.
   - **Total Order Amount**: This is the final amount due.
5. **Payment Information**: 
   - **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you would like to use.
   - **Shipping Method**: Select the shipping method you would like to use.
   - **Special Instructions**: Enter any special requests or notes.
6. **Order Date**: Enter the date of this order.

## Tips

- Double-check all fields for accuracy to avoid errors.
- Use the correct format for the date of birth (YYYY-MM-DD).
- Enter all necessary information for accurate order processing.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the Clearpay team for support.
