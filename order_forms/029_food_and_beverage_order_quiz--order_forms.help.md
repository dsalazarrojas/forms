# food-and-beverage-order-quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather contact and order details for food and beverage services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Contact Details" page with your name, email, and phone number.
2. Go to the "Order Options" page and select the type of order you would like to place (e.g. "Food", "Beverage", or "Combo").
3. On the "Timing and Payment" page, choose the delivery or pick-up option and select a payment method (e.g. "Credit Card", "Cash", or "Online Payment").
4. Finally, go to the "Additional Info" page and provide any additional information you would like to include with your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details** (Page 1, <name>): 
  Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (Page 1, <name>): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (Page 1, <name>): Enter your phone number.
* **Order Options** (Page 2, <name>): Select the type of order you would like to place (e.g. "Food", "Beverage", or "Combo").
* **Timing and Payment** (Page 3, <name>): Choose your delivery or pick-up option and select a payment method (e.g. "Credit Card", "Cash", or "Online Payment").
* **Additional Info** (Page 4, <name>): Provide any additional information you would like to include with your order.
