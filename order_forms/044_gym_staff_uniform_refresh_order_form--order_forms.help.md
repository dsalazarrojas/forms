# Gym Staff Uniform Refresh Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by gym staff to request a uniform refresh for themselves.

## How To Complete This Form

- Fill out the form completely to ensure accurate processing.
- Please ensure the correct quantity of uniforms is selected.
- If unsure about any field, please contact the relevant personnel.
- Double-check the "Pickup Date" and "Pickup Time" for correct scheduling.
- Enter the correct email address for communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff Uniform Reorder Form** (`id: 1`):
  A text input field for a brief description or reference for the order.

* **Select Sizes** (`id: 2`):
  Select multiple sizes you need for the uniform refresh.
  Choose from the available options: Small, Medium, and Large.

* **Quantity** (`id: 3`):
  Enter the number of each size needed for the uniform refresh.

* **Delivery** (`id: 4`):
  Select the preferred delivery method: Delivery or Pickup.

* **Pickup Date** (`id: 5`):
  Enter the date for pickup of the uniforms. Please use the format:  YYYY-MM-DD.

* **Pickup Time** (`id: 6`):
  Enter the time for pickup of the uniforms. Please use the 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).

* **Email** (`id: 7`):
  Enter your email address for communication regarding the order.

* **Phone** (`id: 8`):
  Enter your phone number for communication regarding the order.

* **Staff ID** (`id: 9`):
  Enter your staff ID number.

* **Comments** (`id: 10`):
  Additional comments or notes for the order.

* **Gym Name** (`id: 11`):
  Enter the name of the gym where you work.

* **Gym Address** (`id: 12`):
  Enter the address of the gym where you work.

* **Gym Phone** (`id: 13`):
  Enter the phone number of the gym.

* **Gym Email** (`id: 14`):
  Enter the email address of the gym.

* **Gym Fax** (`id: 15`):
  Enter the fax number of the gym.

* **Gym Manager** (`id: 16`):
  Enter the name of the gym manager.

* **Gym Manager Phone** (`id: 17`):
  Enter the phone number of the gym manager.

* **Gym Manager Email** (`id: 18`):
  Enter the email address of the gym manager.

* **Gym Manager Fax** (`id: 19`):
  Enter the fax number of the gym manager.

* **Delivery Address** (`id: 20`):
  Enter the address where the uniforms will be delivered to.

* **Pickup Address** (`id: 21`):
  Enter the address where the uniforms will be picked up from.

* **Delivery Instructions** (`id: 22`):
  Additional instructions for delivery.

* **Pickup Instructions** (`id: 23`):
  Additional instructions for pickup.

* **Notes** (`id: 24`):
  Additional comments or notes for the order.

* **Assigned Tool** (`id: 25`):
  A text input field for any assigned tool or equipment for the order.
