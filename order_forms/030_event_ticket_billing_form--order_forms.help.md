# Event Ticket Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Ticket Billing Form is designed for users to create and manage billing information for events. This form is used by event organizers to collect payment and attendee details for events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Navigate to the Event Ticket Billing Form.
2. Fill out each page of the form accordingly:
   - Page 1: Enter the event date, select the ticket type, and enter the ticket price.
   - Page 2: Enter the order form and enter the order total.
   - Page 3: Select the payment method and fill out the required information.
3. Review each page to ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Ticket Billing Form** (`event_ticket_billing_form`, text, required): Enter a descriptive title for the event.
* **Order Form** (`order_form`, text, required): Enter the order number or form.
* **Payment Form** (`payment_form`, text, required): Enter the payment information (e.g. method and details).
* **Attendee Info** (`attendee_info`, text, required): Enter the attendee's name and contact information.
* **Ticket Type** (`ticket_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of ticket (e.g. General Admission or VIP).
* **Ticket Quantity** (`ticket_quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of tickets being purchased.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Ticket Price** (`ticket_price`, number, required): Enter the price of each ticket.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, required): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the method of payment (e.g. Credit Card or Bank Transfer).
  Note: Please ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
