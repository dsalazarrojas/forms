# Restaurant Sanitization Audit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Sanitization Audit Order Form is a critical tool used to assess a restaurant's compliance with food safety regulations. This form helps health inspectors or auditors evaluate the restaurant's sanitization status, ensuring that it meets the required standards for public health and safety.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately, providing all required information.
2. Ensure that you have reviewed the restaurant's current status before submitting the form.
3. Complete all required fields, as indicated by the (*) symbol.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Restaurant Name (1)
* **Restaurant Name** (`restaurant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the restaurant being audited.

### Date of Last Inspection (2)
* **Date of Last Inspection** (`date_of_last_inspection`, date, required): Enter the date of the last inspection or audit performed on the restaurant.

### Is the restaurant currently open? (3)
* **Is the restaurant currently open?** (`is_restaurant_open`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if the restaurant is currently open, 'No' if it is not.

### Is the kitchen clean and organized? (4)
* **Is the kitchen clean and organized?** (`kitchen_cleanliness`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable options that describe the kitchen's cleanliness and organization status:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'

### What is the current temperature of the kitchen? (5)
* **What is the current temperature of the kitchen?** (`kitchen_temperature`, number, required): Enter the current temperature of the kitchen, in degrees.

### When was the last time the restaurant's water was tested? (6)
* **When was the last time the restaurant's water was tested?** (`last_water_test_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the last time the restaurant's water was tested.

### Is the restaurant's dishwashing station properly sanitized? (7)
* **Is the restaurant's dishwashing station properly sanitized?** (`sanitization`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if the dishwashing station is properly sanitized, 'No' if it is not.

### What is the current date of the last time the restaurant's equipment was cleaned? (8)
* **What is the current date of the last time the restaurant's equipment was cleaned?** (`equipment_cleaning_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the last time the restaurant's equipment was cleaned.

### Is the restaurant's waste disposal area clean and well-maintained? (9)
* **Is the restaurant's waste disposal area clean and well-maintained?** (`waste_disposal_area_cleanliness`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable options that describe the waste disposal area's cleanliness and maintenance status:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
