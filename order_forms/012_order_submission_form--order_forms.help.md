# Order Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and store information related to order submissions, including customer details, shipping and payment information, and order totals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form fields with the required information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
3. Submit the form when you have finished entering all the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order**: Enter the order name or description.
* **Customer**: Enter the customer's name and any relevant details.
* **Shipping**: Enter the shipping information, including address, email, and phone number.
* **Payment**: Select the payment method and any relevant details.
* **Order Details**: Enter any additional details about the order, such as the total price or order number.
* **Itemized Order**: Enter a list of items ordered, including quantity and price.
* **Customer Address**: Enter the customer's address.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the shipping address.
* **Shipping Method**: Select whether shipping is included or not.
* **Payment Method**: Select whether payment is included or not.
* **Order Total**: Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Shipping Total**: Enter the shipping cost.
* **Payment Total**: Enter the total payment.
* **Tax Included**: Enter whether tax is included or not.
* **Tax Total**: Enter the tax total.
* **Date**: Select the date of the order submission.
* **Time**: Select the time of the order submission.
* **Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the order.
* **Email**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Shipping Email**: Enter the shipping contact's email address.
* **Shipping Phone**: Enter the shipping contact's phone number.
* **Sales**: Select whether sales are included or not.
