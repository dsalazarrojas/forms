# Community Race Finish Line Banner Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to order a custom banner for a community race finish line. Please fill out the form below to provide all the necessary details for your order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reviewing the form and making sure you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Enter the name of your race event in the "Race Event Name" field.
3. Choose the date of the event in the "Date of the Event" field.
4. Enter the name of the organization or individual placing the order in the "Ordering Organization or Individual Name" field.
5. Provide the contact email and phone number of the person responsible for the order in the "Contact Email Address" and "Contact Phone Number" fields.
6. Select the size of the banner from the "Banner Size Selection" options.
7. Choose the material of the banner from the "Banner Material Selection" options.
8. Enter the main text to be displayed on the banner in the "Main Text for the Banner" field.
9. Decide if you want to include a logo on the banner and provide a description in the "Logo Description or Placement Instruction" field (if applicable).
10. Select the preferred color scheme for the banner from the "Preferred Color Scheme" options (you can choose one or multiple colors).
11. If you want to specify custom colors, enter them in the "Custom Color Description" field.
12. Enter the total number of banners to order in the "Total Number of Banners to Order" field.
13. Choose the shipping method from the "Delivery or Shipping Method" options.
14. Enter the required delivery date, at least 10 days before the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Race Event Name** (`race_event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your race event.
* **Date of the Event** (`race_event_date`, date, required): Choose the date of the event.
* **Ordering Organization or Individual Name** (`ordering_organization`, text, required): Enter the name of the organization or individual placing the order.
* **Contact Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Provide the email address of the person responsible for the order.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the order.
* **Banner Size Selection** (`banner_size_selection`, select_one, required): Select the size of the banner (Small, Medium, or Large).
* **Banner Material Selection** (`banner_material_selection`, select_one, required): Choose the material of the banner (Heavy Duty Vinyl, Lightweight Mesh, or Weatherproof Fabric).
* **Main Text for the Banner** (`banner_main_text`, text, required): Enter the text to be displayed on the banner.
* **Would you like to include an organization logo?** (`include_logo_check`, select_one, required): Decide if you want to include a logo on the banner.
* **Logo Description or Placement Instruction** (`logo_description_info`, text, optional): Describe what the logo looks like (if not providing a file).
* **Preferred Color Scheme** (`color_scheme_selection`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or multiple color schemes (Blue and White, Red and White, Green and White, Black and Yellow, or Custom).
* **Custom Color Description** (`custom_colors_desc`, text, optional): Specify custom colors with HEX codes or shades.
* **Total Number of Banners to Order** (`banner_order_quantity`, number, required): Enter the total number of banners to order.
* **Delivery or Shipping Method** (`shipping_method_choice`, select_one, required): Choose the shipping method (Standard Shipping - 7 Days, Express Shipping - 3 Days, or In-Store Pick-up).
* **Required Delivery Date** (`required_delivery_date`, date, required): Enter the required delivery date at least 10 days before the event.
* **Pricing and Payment** (`pricing_payment_note`, note, optional): Note: The final pricing depends on the size and quantity of the banner. We will email an invoice once we review your order.
