# Parking Fee Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to request a refund for an overpaid parking fee. If you have paid for parking but did not use it or were overcharged, you can submit this form to initiate the refund process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Parking Fee Refund Form**: This is the main form section where you can provide your name, phone number, and email address.
2. **Parking Fee Refund Reason**: Select the reason why you are requesting a refund (e.g., "Yes" for overpaid parking fee).
3. **Order Details**: Choose the order you are requesting a refund for from the list of options (e.g., "Order ID" or "Order Description").
4. **Amount**: Enter the amount you are requesting a refund for.
5. **Amount Refund**: Enter the amount you are requesting as a refund.
6. **Payment Method**: Choose the payment method you used to pay for parking (e.g., "Card" or "Cash").
7. **Business Details**: Provide the business name, phone number, email address, and address where the parking fee was paid.
8. **Business Name**: Enter the name of the business where you paid for parking.
9. **Business Phone**: Enter the phone number of the business where you paid for parking.
10. **Business Email**: Enter the email address of the business where you paid for parking.
11. **Business Address**: Enter the address of the business where you paid for parking.
12. **Business City**: Enter the city of the business where you paid for parking.
13. **Business State**: Enter the state of the business where you paid for parking.
14. **Business Zip**: Enter the zip code of the business where you paid for parking.
15. **Business Country**: Enter the country of the business where you paid for parking.
16. **Order ID**: Enter the order ID of the parking fee you are requesting a refund for.
17. **Business Details**: Provide any additional information about the business where you paid for parking.
18. **Parking Fee Refund Reason**: Enter the reason why you are requesting a refund for the parking fee.
19. **Amount Refund Reason**: Enter the reason why you are requesting a refund for the amount.
