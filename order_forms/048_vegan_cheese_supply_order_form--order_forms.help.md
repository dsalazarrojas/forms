# Vegan Cheese Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vegan Cheese Supply Order Form is used to collect information from customers to place an order for vegan cheese supply.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with the required information.
2. Ensure all fields are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, required): Fill in your name as it will appear in our records.
* **Address** (`address`, required): Enter your address where we can deliver the order.
* **Phone** (`phone`, required): Provide your phone number for any further communication.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, required): Specify the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, required): Choose the time slot you prefer for delivery.
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, required): Check 'Yes' if you want to include additional vegan products with your order, otherwise check 'No'.
