# Art Collection Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to place pre-orders for art collections. It will guide you through the required information to complete your order.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Step 1: Customer Information**
	* Fill out your name, email address, and phone number to provide your contact details.
	* Select your type of customer from the dropdown menu.
2. **Step 2: Artwork Selection**
	* Enter the title of the artwork you wish to purchase.
	* Enter the name of the artist who created the artwork.
	* Specify the medium or type of the artwork.
3. **Step 3: Quantity and Pricing**
	* Indicate the quantity of the artwork you wish to purchase.
	* Enter the unit price of the artwork.
4. **Step 4: Delivery Information**
	* Select your preferred delivery method (Standard, Express, or Local pickup).
	* Choose whether you are interested in shipping insurance.
5. **Step 5: Payment Information**
	* Choose your preferred payment method (Credit card, Bank transfer, PayPal, or Check).
	* Specify the deposit percentage or if you will pay the full amount.
6. **Step 6: Special Requests (Optional)**
	* If you have any customization requests (e.g., framing, sizing), describe them here.
	* Add any additional comments or notes about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed for the artwork.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you with updates.
* **Customer Type**: Select the type of customer you are (e.g., Individual collector, Gallery, Institution).
* **Artwork Title**: Enter the title of the artwork you wish to purchase.
* **Artist Name**: Enter the name of the artist who created the artwork.
* **Artwork Code or Product Code**: (Optional) Enter any unique code or product code assigned to the artwork.
* **Medium or Type**: Specify the medium or type of the artwork (e.g., Print, Original, Limited edition).
* **Edition Size or Limited Quantity**: (Optional) Indicate the edition size or limited quantity of the artwork.
* **Dimensions or Size**: (Optional) Enter the dimensions or size of the artwork.
* **Quantity Ordered**: Indicate the number of artwork pieces you wish to purchase.
* **Unit Price**: Enter the price of one unit of the artwork.
* **Total Price**: Will be calculated based on the quantity and unit price.
* **Currency**: Select the currency you wish to use for payment (e.g., USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD).
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your shipping address for the artwork.
* **City, State, ZIP Code**: Enter your city, state, and ZIP code for shipping purposes.
* **Expected Delivery Date**: (Optional) Enter an estimated date for delivery.
* **Delivery Preference**: Choose your preferred delivery method (Standard, Express, Local pickup).
* **Interested in Shipping Insurance**: Choose whether you would like shipping insurance.
* **Preferred Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method (e.g., Credit card, Bank transfer, PayPal).
* **Deposit Percentage**: Enter the percentage or amount of deposit to reserve the artwork.
* **Any Customization Requests**: Describe any customization requests for the artwork (e.g., framing, sizing).
* **Additional Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
