# Weekly Lunch Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly Lunch Order Form is a digital form designed for employees to place their lunch orders. This form captures essential information for catering services to deliver the correct meal on the correct date and time. 

## How To Complete This Form
To successfully complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Employee Name** in the first field. This field is required and must be accurate.
2. Choose the **Order Date** when you would like to receive your lunch order. This date will be used to schedule your order.
3. Select the desired **Order Time** in the 24-hour clock format.
4. Indicate the **Lunch Location** where you would like to receive your order.
5. Make a **Meal Selection** by choosing one of the available options. Please select only one option.
6. If you have any **Special Instructions**, enter them in the provided field. This is an optional field.
7. Enter your **Employee Email** in the required field.
8. Enter your **Employee Phone** in the required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, Text, Required): Please enter your name as you would like it to appear on your order.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, Date, Required): Please select the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, Time, Required): Please select the time you would like to receive your order in the 24-hour clock format.
* **Lunch Location** (`lunch_location`, Text, Required): Please select the location where you would like to receive your order.
* **Meal Selection** (`meal_selection`, Select Multiple, Required): Please choose one meal option from the available choices. Selecting more than one option will not be processed.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, Text, Optional): If you have any special requests for your order, enter them here.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, Email, Required): Please enter your work email address.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, Text, Required): Please enter your work phone number.

## Tips
- Make sure to review each field carefully to avoid errors.
- Select only one option for Meal Selection.
- Ensure your contact information is accurate.
