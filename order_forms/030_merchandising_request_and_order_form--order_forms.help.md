# Merchandising Request And Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Merchandising Request And Order Form is a document designed to collect information from customers regarding their merchandising requests and purchase details. This form is intended to be used for internal purposes only and is not affiliated with any specific form platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and materials before filling out the form.
2. Fill out the form in a clear and legible manner, providing accurate information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, `text`, optional): This field is for collecting information about the customer. Please enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information that will help us serve you better.
* **Purchase Info** (`purchase_info`, `text`, optional): This field is for providing details about the purchase you are requesting. Please describe the product or service you are interested in and any specific requirements or special requests you may have.
* **Vendor Info** (`vendor_info`, `text`, optional): This field is for providing information about the vendor or supplier of the product or service you are interested in. Please enter the vendor's name, contact information, or any other relevant details that will help us facilitate the order.

## Tips
* Please double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, do not hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
* This form is for internal use only and is not intended for external sharing or distribution.
