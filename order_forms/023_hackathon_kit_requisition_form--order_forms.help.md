# Hackathon Kit Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a Hackathon Kit, a package of resources and tools to support a specific event or activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name for your contact details.
2. Provide your Email address for internal communication and contact.
3. Enter your Phone number for communication and coordination.
4. Select a Date for the event or activity.
5. Enter the name of the Event.
6. List the Items needed for the event or activity.
7. (Optional) Add any additional Notes about the event or activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **Required**): Enter your email address for internal communication and contact.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **Optional**): Enter your phone number for communication and coordination.
* **Date** (`date`, date, **Optional**): Select the date for the event or activity.
* **Time** (`time`, time, **Optional**): Select the time for the event or activity.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, **Optional**): Enter the name of the event or activity.
* **List of Items** (`item_list`, text, **Required**): List the items needed for the event or activity.
* **Notes** (`note`, note, **Optional**): Add any additional notes about the event or activity.
