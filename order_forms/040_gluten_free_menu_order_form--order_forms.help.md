# Gluten Free Menu Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gluten Free Menu Order Form is designed to capture customer information for gluten-free menu orders. This form is used to gather necessary details for preparing and delivering gluten-free meals to customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Name** in the first field.
2. Select the **Order Date** you would like to place your order for.
3. Choose the **Order Time** you would like to pick up your order.
4. Indicate if you need **Gluten Free Ingredients** in your order.
5. Select the **Order Type** of your order.
6. Type any **Customer Messages** you would like to provide.
7. Provide any **Special Requests** you may have.
8. Indicate if a specific **Assigned Tool** will be needed for your order.
9. Enter your **Customer Contact Number** for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Order Date**: Select the date you would like to pick up your order.
* **Order Time**: Choose the time you would like to pick up your order.
* **Gluten Free Ingredients**: If you need gluten-free ingredients, select 'Yes' for each type of ingredient you require.
* **Order Type**: Select 'Yes' if you need a gluten-free order.
* **Customer Message**: Type any comments or messages you would like to include with your order.
* **Special Requests**: Type any additional requests you have for your order.
* **Assigned Tool**: If you need a specific tool for your order, select 'Yes'.
* **Customer Contact Number**: Enter a number where we can contact you for follow-up.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and clear information to ensure your order is prepared correctly.
* If you have any questions or issues with your order, contact us directly.
* Our team will review your order and follow up with you to confirm the details.
* If you need a specific tool for your order, please ensure it is available before placing your order.
