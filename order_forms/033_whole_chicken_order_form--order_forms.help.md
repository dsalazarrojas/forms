# whole_chicken_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers place orders for whole chickens, providing an easy and streamlined way to gather necessary information for processing their orders.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name in the "name" field. This is a required field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field. This is a required field to ensure we can contact you with your order details.
3. Provide your phone number in the "phone" field. This is a required field for our team to contact you with updates and confirm your order.
4. Select the number of whole chickens you'd like to order in the "whole_chicken_count" field. This is a required field to ensure we have enough chickens for your order.
5. Choose any extra sides you'd like to add to your order from the "extra_sides" dropdown menu. This is an optional field.
6. Select any extra sauces you'd like to add to your order from the "extra_sauces" dropdown menu. This is an optional field.
7. If you have any special instructions or requests for your order, type them in the "special_instructions" field. This is an optional field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer (text)**: This is a required field where you can enter your name.
* **Email (text)**: This is a required field where you can enter your email address. Please ensure it's accurate to receive updates and confirmations about your order.
* **Phone (text)**: This is a required field where you can enter your phone number. Our team will use this to contact you with updates and confirm your order.
* **Whole Chicken Count (number)**: This is a required field where you select the number of whole chickens you'd like to order.
* **Extra Sides (select)**: Select up to 3 extra sides to accompany your order from the available options: Carrot, Celery, or Corn.
* **Extra Sauces (select)**: Select up to 3 extra sauces to accompany your order from the available options: BBQ, Honey Mustard, or Ranch.
* **Special Instructions (text)**: This is an optional field where you can provide any special instructions or requests for your order.
