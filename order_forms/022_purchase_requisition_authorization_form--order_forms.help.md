# Purchase Requisition Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Purchase Requisition Authorization Form is used to gather information from vendors, including purchase requests and requisitioning details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Enter the vendor's name and address.
3. Select the approved by field from the dropdown list.
4. Choose the purchase request status.
5. Enter the purchase request amount.
6. Add any notes or comments.
7. Enter the contact person's email and phone number.
8. Choose the department and category.
9. Select the location.
10. Enter the request number.
11. Enter the purchase requester's title, department, and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Purchase Request Form** (text, required/optional): This is the main section of the form where you will enter all the details of the purchase requisition.
* **Purchase Request Date** (date, required/optional): This field is used to enter the date of the purchase request.
* **Vendor Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the vendor.
* **Vendor Address** (text, required/optional): Enter the address of the vendor.
* **Purchase Requestor** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person making the purchase request.
* **Purchase Request Date** (date, required/optional): This field is used to enter the date of the purchase request.
* **Purchase Request Description** (text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the purchase request.
* **Approved By** (select one, required/optional): Select the person who approved the purchase request from the dropdown list.
* **Purchase Request Status** (select one, required/optional): Choose the status of the purchase request from the dropdown list.
* **Purchase Request Amount** (number, required/optional): Enter the amount of the purchase request.
* **Notes** (text, required/optional): Add any notes or comments about the purchase request.
* **Email** (text, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Person** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Approval Date** (date, required/optional): This field is used to enter the date of approval.
* **Approval Time** (time, required/optional): This field is used to enter the time of approval.
* **Remarks** (text, required/optional): Enter any remarks about the purchase request.
* **Purchase Requester** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the purchase requester.
* **Department** (text, required/optional): Enter the department of the purchase requester.
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the purchase requester.
* **Category** (text, required/optional): Enter the category of the purchase request.
* **Location** (text, required/optional): Enter the location of the purchase request.
* **Request Number** (text, required/optional): Enter the request number.
* **Purchase Requester Title** (text, required/optional): Enter the title of the purchase requester.
* **Purchase Requester Department** (text, required/optional): Enter the department of the purchase requester.
* **Purchase Requester Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the purchase requester.
