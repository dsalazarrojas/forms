# Cash On Delivery Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for capturing cash-on-delivery (COD) payment information from customers when making an order. It's a multi-page form that collects relevant details such as order date, customer information, delivery address, order details, payment information, and delivery preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select the relevant pages that require input, such as "Order Information", "Customer Information", "Delivery Address", "Order Details", "Payment Details", and "Delivery Preferences".
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and valid information for each page.
3. Review and complete any additional fields as required or needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Date** (id: 2): Enter the date of the order in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Customer Full Name** (id: 5): Enter the customer's full name.
* **Phone Number** (id: 6): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Email Address** (id: 7): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Street Address** (id: 9): Enter the street address where the order will be delivered.
* **City** (id: 10): Enter the city where the order will be delivered.
* **State or Province** (id: 11): Enter the state or province where the order will be delivered.
* **Zip or Postal Code** (id: 12): Enter the zip or postal code where the order will be delivered.
* **Country** (id: 13): Enter the country where the order will be delivered.
* **Item Description** (id: 15): Enter a brief description of the item being ordered.
* **Quantity** (id: 16): Enter the quantity of the item ordered.
* **Unit Price** (id: 17): Enter the unit price of the item ordered.
* **Subtotal** (id: 18): This field will be calculated automatically based on the quantity and unit price.
* **Shipping Cost** (id: 20): Enter the shipping cost, if applicable.
* **Tax Amount** (id: 21): Enter the tax amount, if applicable.
* **Total Amount Due** (id: 22): This field will be calculated automatically based on the subtotal, tax amount, and shipping cost.
* **Preferred Delivery Date** (id: 24): Enter the preferred delivery date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Special Delivery Instructions** (id: 25): Enter any special delivery instructions, such as gate codes or preferred times.

Note: Some fields have default values or calculations that are automatically updated as you fill out the form.
