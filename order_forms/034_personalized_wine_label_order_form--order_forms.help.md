# Personalized Wine Label Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personalized wine label order information from customers. It allows you to gather detailed data for an order, including customer details, label design choices, order date and time, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the Wine Type from the options provided.
2. Choose the Label Size, Label Type, and Label Color for your wine label.
3. Upload a Wine Label Image or enter the image URL.
4. Enter the Order Date and Order Time for your wine order.
5. Provide your Customer Email, Customer Phone Number, and any additional Order Notes.
6. Enter the Order Total and Wine Price for your order.
7. Select the Order Status (Active or Inactive) and Payment Method.
8. Double-check all fields for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Order Form** (`order_form`, `text`, required): This is the main form section where you enter your wine label order information.
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, not required): Enter your name as the customer who is placing the order.
- **Wine Type** (`wine_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the type of wine you'd like to order (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
- **Label Size** (`label_size`, `select_one`, required): Choose the label size you'd like for your wine label (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
- **Label Type** (`label_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the label type you'd prefer (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
- **Label Color** (`label_color`, `select_one`, required): Select the label color you'd like for your wine label (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
- **Wine Label Image** (`wine_label_image`, `text`, required): Upload a Wine Label Image or enter the image URL.
- **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you'd like to place the order.
- **Order Time** (`order_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you'd like to place the order.
- **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
- **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, `text`, required): Enter any additional order notes or comments.
- **Order Total** (`order_total`, `number`, required): Enter the total amount of the order.
- **Wine Price** (`wine_price`, `number`, required): Enter the price of the wine.
- **Order Status** (`order_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the order status (Active or Inactive).
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select the payment method (Yes or No).
