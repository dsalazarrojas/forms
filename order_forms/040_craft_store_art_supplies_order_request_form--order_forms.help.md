# Craft Store Art Supplies Order Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Craft Store Art Supplies Order Request Form is a tool for customers to request art supplies from the store. By filling out this form, customers can provide their contact information and details about the art supplies they need to place an order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and contact information in the "customer_info" field.
2. Enter your contact number in the "contact_number" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email_address" field.
4. Specify the type of art supplies you need in the "art_supplies" field.
5. Select the quantity of each art supply you need.
6. Choose your delivery option in the "delivery_option" field.
7. Add any additional notes about your order in the "order_notes" field.
8. Select a date for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_info** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **contact_number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **email_address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **art_supplies** (`art_supplies`, text, optional): Specify the type of art supply you need (e.g. Acrylic Paint, Watercolor Paints, Canvas, etc.).
* **supply_type** (`supply_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of art supply you need from the options provided (e.g. Acrylic Paint, Watercolor Paints, etc.).
* **quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of each art supply you need.
* **delivery_option** (`delivery_option`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you would like to receive your order (e.g. Pickup, Delivery).
* **order_notes** (`order_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **order_date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Select a date for your order.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all relevant fields to ensure your order is processed correctly.
- If you need multiple art supplies, separate each in a comma-separated list in the "art_supplies" field.
- If you have any additional requirements or requests for your order, please include them in the "order_notes" field.
- Please enter a valid date in the "order_date" field.
