# Quote Correction Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quote Correction Submission Form is a tool for users to correct quotes or identify potential errors in quotes. This form helps the team address any discrepancies and improve the overall accuracy of the quotes.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the quote you wish to correct or identify an error in.
2. Select the relevant quote ID from the "Quote ID" field.
3. Enter the date of the quote in the "Quote Date" field.
4. Indicate if the client is involved in the correction process by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Client" field.
5. Describe the correction or comment in the "Correction" field.
6. Specify the type of correction if applicable (yes, no, or multiple types) in the "Correction Type" field.
7. Add any additional comments or explanations in the "Comments" field.
8. Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm that you have reviewed and agree with the correction or comment in the "Submit" field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Quote ID** (Quote ID, text, optional): Enter the ID of the quote you are correcting or reviewing.
* **Quote Date** (Quote Date, date, optional): Enter the date of the quote in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Client** (Client, select one, optional): Indicate if the client is involved in the correction process by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Correction** (Correction, text, optional): Describe the correction or comment you want to make regarding the quote.
* **Correction Type** (Correction Type, select multiple, optional): Select one or multiple types of corrections if applicable. This field is meant to provide more specificity regarding the type of correction.
* **Comments** (Comments, text, optional): Add any additional comments or explanations regarding the correction or comment.
* **Submit** (Submit, select one, optional): Confirm that you have reviewed and agree with the correction or comment by selecting "Yes".
