# Student Monthly Food Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for students to order food on a monthly basis, providing an opportunity for them to specify their meal preferences, dietary needs, and other important details.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each question carefully and answer according to your preferences and dietary needs.
- Select your preferred meal frequency and cuisine options that suit your eating habits.
- If you have any dietary restrictions, select the corresponding option.
- Choose your preferred payment method (online or offline) and whether you want a receipt.
- Enter your contact information (email and phone number) accurately.
- Enter your student ID correctly.
- Select the date for your order.
- If you have any special requests, you can type them in the designated box.
- Click submit once you have filled in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Meal Preferences** (`Meal Preferences`, `select_one`, `Regularly/Occasionally/Rarely`): Choose how often you like to have meals.
  * **Regularly**: This is for students who like to have meals regularly.
  * **Occasionally**: This is for students who prefer meals occasionally.
  * **Rarely**: This is for students who have meals rarely.

- **Meal Frequency** (`Meal Frequency`, `select_one`, `1-2/3-4/5-6`): Choose how frequently you like to have meals.
  * **1-2**: This frequency is for students who have one to two meals.
  * **3-4**: This frequency is for students who have three or four meals.
  * **5-6**: This frequency is for students who have five or six meals.

- **Meal Cuisine** (`Meal Cuisine`, `select_one`, `Indian/Chinese/Italian/Others`): Choose your preferred meal cuisine.
  * **Indian**: This is for students who prefer Indian cuisine.
  * **Chinese**: This is for students who prefer Chinese cuisine.
  * **Italian**: This is for students who prefer Italian cuisine.
  * **Others**: This is for students who prefer any other cuisine.

- **Dietary Restrictions** (`Dietary Restrictions`, `select_one`, `True/False`): If you have any dietary restrictions, please select this option.
  * **True**: This is for students with dietary restrictions.
  * **False**: This is for students without dietary restrictions.

- **Discount** (`Discount`, `select_one`, `True/False`): Check if you are eligible for a discount.
  * **True**: This option is for students eligible for a discount.
  * **False**: This option is for students not eligible for a discount.

- **Contact Information - Email** (`Contact Information - Email`, `email`): Enter your email address.
- **Contact Information - Phone** (`Contact Information - Phone`, `text`): Enter your phone number.
- **Student ID** (`Student ID`, `number`): Enter your student ID number.
- **Order Date** (`Order Date`, `date`): Choose the date for your order.
- **Special Requests** (`Special Requests`, `note`): If you have any special requests, type them in this box.
- **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, `select_one`, `Online/Offline`): Choose how you want to make your payment.
  * **Online**: This is for students who want to make payments online.
  * **Offline**: This is for students who want to make payments offline.
- **Receipt** (`Receipt`, `select_one`, `True/False`): Choose if you want a receipt.
  * **True**: This is for students who want a receipt.
  * **False**: This is for students who do not want a receipt.

- **Tips**

* Please ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Use the provided options to answer each question honestly as per your preferences and dietary needs.
* For fields like Special Requests, you can type in any specific requests you might have.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact your support team.
* Review and double-check all entered information before submitting the form to avoid any mistakes.
