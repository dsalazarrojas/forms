# nft_marketplace_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather user information and preferences for potential NFT marketplace inquiries.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the NFT marketplace that interests you from the options provided in the "nft_marketplace" field.
2. Enter your name and email address in the "name" and "email" fields, respectively.
3. If you have a preferred phone number, enter it in the "phone" field.
4. Specify the date and time of interest in the "date_of_interest" and "time_of_interest" fields, if applicable.
5. Enter a brief description of your NFT needs or interests in the "nft_description" field.
6. If you have a preferred price range, enter it in the "price_range" field.
7. Select any relevant NFT tags from the list provided in the "nft_tags" field.
8. Finally, attach any relevant images related to your NFT interests or needs in the "nft_image" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **nft_marketplace** (`nft_marketplace`, select_one, optional): Select the NFT marketplace that interests you.
* **name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if applicable.
* **date_of_interest** (`date_of_interest`, date, optional): Enter the date of interest for your NFT inquiry.
* **time_of_interest** (`time_of_interest`, time, optional): Enter the time of interest for your NFT inquiry.
* **nft_description** (`nft_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your NFT needs or interests.
* **price_range** (`price_range`, number, optional): Enter a preferred price range for your NFT inquiry.
* **nft_tags** (`nft_tags`, select_multiple, optional): Select any relevant NFT tags from the list provided.
* **nft_image** (`nft_image`, text, optional): Attach any relevant images related to your NFT interests or needs.
## Tips
This form is designed to be as simple and straightforward as possible. Please fill out only the fields that are relevant to your inquiry. If you have any questions or need further assistance, please do not hesitate to contact us.
