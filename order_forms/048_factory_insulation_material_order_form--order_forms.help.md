# Factory Insulation Material Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Insulation Material Order Form is designed to facilitate the collection of information required for ordering insulation materials at the factory. This form ensures accurate and efficient communication between the factory and the ordering team.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the material to be ordered from the available options.
2. Enter the quantity of the selected material.
3. Choose the unit of measurement for the ordered quantity.
4. Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the order.
5. Provide the contact phone number for the ordering team.
6. Enter the contact email address.
7. If applicable, select the date and time when the order is required.
8. Review the form carefully before submitting it for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Material to Order** (`material_to_order`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of insulation material to be ordered from the available options: Insulation, Other, or None.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, optional): Enter the total quantity of the selected material needed.
* **Unit of Measure** (`unit_of_measure`, `select_one`, optional): Select the unit of measurement for the ordered quantity: Square Feet, Square Yards, Cubic Feet, Other, or None.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the order.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Provide the contact phone number for the ordering team.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the contact email address.
* **Date Required** (`date_required`, `date`, optional): If applicable, select the date when the order is required.
* **Time Required** (`time_required`, `time`, optional): If applicable, select the time when the order is required.
