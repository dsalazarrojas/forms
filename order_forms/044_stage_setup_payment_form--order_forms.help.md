# Stage Setup Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stage Setup Payment Form is a tool for setting up and managing payments for a particular stage or event. This form is used to collect necessary information for payment processing.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the event name and event date in the respective fields.
2. Select one or multiple service names from the options available.
3. Enter the number of service hours for the selected service(s).
4. Enter the amount due for the service(s).
5. Choose a payment method from the available options.
6. Fill in any other required fields, such as billing address and payment status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event for which you are setting up a stage.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Service Name** (`service_name`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple services for which you are setting up a stage.
* **Service Hours** (`service_hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours for the selected service(s).
* **Amount Due** (`amount_due`, number, optional): Enter the amount due for the service(s).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Choose a payment method from the available options.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, text, optional): Enter the billing address for the payment.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current payment status.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the payment.
* **Payment Time** (`payment_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the payment.
* **Event Notes** (`event_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes about the event.
* **Total Amount Due** (`total_amount_due`, number, optional): Enter the total amount due for the service(s).
* **Tax Amount** (`tax_amount`, number, optional): Enter the tax amount for the service(s).
* **Tax Exempt** (`tax_exempt`, select_one, optional): Select whether the service(s) is tax exempt.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the service(s).
* **Additional Payment Method** (`payment_method_additional`, text, optional): Enter any additional payment method information.
* **Tax Rate** (`tax_rate`, number, optional): Enter the tax rate for the service(s).
* **Amount Paid** (`amount_paid`, number, optional): Enter the amount paid for the service(s).
* **Remaining Amount** (`remaining_amount`, number, optional): Enter the remaining amount for the service(s).
* **Event Status** (`event_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the event.
* **Payment Method Additional Details** (`payment_method_additional_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional payment method details.
* **Payment Status Details** (`payment_status_details`, text, optional): Enter any payment status details.
* **Notes Details** (`notes_details`, text, optional): Enter any notes details.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes.

## Tips

* Ensure to fill all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your input for accuracy and completeness.
* Review your form carefully before submitting to avoid mistakes.
