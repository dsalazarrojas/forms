# mail_delivery_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The mail_delivery_form is used to collect and manage mail delivery information for packages being shipped. This form is used by shipping personnel to ensure accurate and efficient delivery of packages.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, select the desired shipping method from the options provided in the "Shipping Method" field.
2.  Enter the recipient's name in the "Recipient Name" field.
3.  Enter the recipient's address in the "Delivery Address" field.
4.  Enter the recipient's city in the "Delivery City" field.
5.  Enter the recipient's zip code in the "Shipping Zip Code" field.
6.  Select the delivery date and time in the "Delivery Date Time" field.
7.  Choose the current delivery status from the options provided in the "Delivery Status" field.
8.  Complete any other required fields, such as "Shipping Name", "Shipping Phone", "Shipping Email", "Package Description", "Package Weight", "Package Length", "Package Width", "Package Height", "Recipient Zip Code", "Recipient Phone", and "Recipient Email".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Shipping Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the shipping personnel completing the form.
*   **Recipient Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the recipient who will receive the package.
*   **Delivery Address** (text, optional): Enter the delivery address of the recipient.
*   **Delivery City** (text, optional): Enter the city of the delivery address.
*   **Shipping Zip Code** (text, optional): Enter the zip code of the shipping address.
*   **Recipient Zip Code** (text, optional): Enter the zip code of the recipient's address.
*   **Shipping Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the shipping personnel.
*   **Shipping Email** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the shipping personnel.
*   **Package Description** (text, optional): Enter a brief description of the package being shipped.
*   **Package Weight** (text, optional): Enter the weight of the package being shipped.
*   **Package Length** (text, optional): Enter the length of the package being shipped.
*   **Package Width** (text, optional): Enter the width of the package being shipped.
*   **Package Height** (text, optional): Enter the height of the package being shipped.
*   **Shipping Method** (select one, optional): Select the shipping method to be used (Express, UPS, USPS, FedEx).
*   **Delivery Date Time** (date, optional): Enter the date and time of the delivery.
*   **Delivery Status** (select one, optional): Select the current delivery status (Delivered, In Transit, Pending).

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
