# Factory Emergency Exit Signage Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for ordering emergency exit signage for a factory or manufacturing facility.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name and email.
2. Select the correct location where the order will be used.
3. Confirm that you wish to order exit signage.
4. Choose the type of signage you require.
5. Specify the quantity of signage needed.
6. Add any additional notes or comments.
7. Set the order date and review date.
8. Select the order status and assign a tool to the order.
9. Review and confirm your order details.
