# Authorize.Net Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for placing new orders for products that are in stock and ready for shipping. It is not for duplicate or cancelled orders. Please ensure that you have all the necessary information and options selected correctly before completing the form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the order.
2. Enter your first and last name.
3. Enter your email address and phone number.
4. Enter your shipping address, including city, state/province, and postal/zip code.
5. Enter the name of the product you are ordering.
6. Select the quantity of the product.
7. Enter the unit price of the product.
8. If applicable, select the size and color of the product.
9. Select any custom options or special requests for the product.
10. Enter the subtotal of the order (product cost before taxes).
11. Enter the tax amount (if applicable).
12. Select the shipping method and enter the shipping cost.
13. If applicable, enter a discount code and the amount of the discount.
14. Enter the total amount to be charged.
15. Select the payment method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Date** (1): Enter the date of this order.
* **First Name** (2): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (3): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (4): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (5): Enter your phone number.
* **Shipping Address** (6): Enter your shipping address.
* **City** (7): Enter the city of the shipping address.
* **State or Province** (8): Enter the state or province of the shipping address.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (9): Enter the postal/zip code of the shipping address.
* **Country** (10): Enter the country of the shipping address (if applicable).
* **Product Name** (11): Enter the name of the product you are ordering.
* **Quantity** (13): Enter the quantity of the product.
* **Unit Price** (14): Enter the price per unit of the product.
* **Product Size** (15): Select the size of the product (if applicable).
* **Product Color** (16): Select the color of the product (if applicable).
* **Customization or Special Options** (17): Enter any custom requirements for the product.
* **Subtotal** (18): Enter the subtotal of the order (product cost before taxes).
* **Tax Amount** (19): Enter the tax amount (if applicable).
* **Shipping Method** (20): Select the shipping method.
* **Shipping Cost** (21): Enter the cost of shipping.
* **Discount Code** (22): Enter a discount code (if applicable).
* **Discount Amount** (23): Enter the amount of the discount.
* **Total Amount** (24): Enter the total amount to be charged.
* **Payment Method** (25): Select the payment method.

**Tips**

* Ensure that all fields are filled in completely and accurately before submitting the form.
* If a field is not applicable to your order, enter "N/A" or do not enter anything.
* If you are unsure of any field, contact support for clarification.
