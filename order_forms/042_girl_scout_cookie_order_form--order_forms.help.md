# Girl Scout Cookie Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Girl Scout Cookie Order Form is designed to collect information from customers who place orders for Girl Scout cookies. This form helps sales teams and customer service representatives track and manage orders efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the following information:
   - **Customer**: Enter the customer's name and any relevant details.
   - **Order Type**: Select whether the order is online or offline.
   - **Cookie Type**: Choose the type of cookie being ordered (e.g., Chocolate Chip, Peanut Butter, Oatmeal Raisin).
   - **Quantity**: Enter the number of cookies being ordered.
   - **Date Ordered**: Enter the date of the order.
   - **Time Ordered**: Enter the time of the order.
   - **Cookie Description**: Write a brief description of the order.
   - **Contact**: Enter the customer's contact information (e.g., email or phone number).
   - **Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
   - **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or questions from the customer.
   - **Customer Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback from the customer.
   - **Cookie Size**: Choose the size of the cookie being ordered (e.g., Regular, King-Size, Single).
   - **Cookie Price**: Enter the price of the cookie.
   - **Total Price**: Enter the total price of the order.
   - **Order Date**: Enter the date of the order.
   - **Order Time**: Enter the time of the order.
   - **Order Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback from the sales team or customer service representative.
   - **Payment Method**: Choose the payment method used (e.g., cash, credit, debit).
   - **Payment Status**: Choose the payment status (e.g., paid, unpaid, pending).
   - **Order Status**: Choose the status of the order (e.g., in progress, completed, cancelled).
