# School Carnival Wristband Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the completion of the School Carnival Wristband Order Form. The form is designed to collect order details for wristbands to be distributed during the School Carnival event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below.

1. Fill in the order details (page 1).
2. Enter your shipping address (page 2).
3. Provide additional shipping details (page 3).
4. Add your payment details (page 4).
5. Write any order comments (page 5).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Order details
* **Order details** (`order_details`, `text`, required/optional): This field is where you will enter the details of your order, such as the quantity of wristbands you want to purchase.

### Shipping address
* **Shipping address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your shipping address where your order will be sent to. This is a required field.

### Shipping details
* **Shipping details** (`shipping_details`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional details about your shipping address, such as special instructions.

### Payment details
* **Payment details** (`payment_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any payment information or details related to your order. This is a required field.

### Order comments
* **Order comments** (`order_comments`, `text`, required/optional): Leave any comments or messages for the event staff handling your order. This is an optional field.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your order details, shipping address, and payment information to ensure accuracy.
- If you have any questions or issues with your order, please contact event staff for assistance.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
