# sports_league_fan_wristband_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sports league fan wristband order form is used to collect information for ordering custom sports-themed wristbands. This form is essential for ensuring that the user's order is processed correctly and for our administration to track and fulfill orders accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **User Name**, **Email**, and **Phone** fields with your contact details.
2. Select the **Team** you wish to order for, from the available options.
3. Enter the desired **Order Quantity** for the wristbands.
4. Provide the **Shipping Address** where you would like the order to be shipped.
5. Enter the **City** and **State** for the shipping address (optional but recommended).
6. Enter the **Zip** for the shipping address.
7. Add any additional **Order Notes** for the administrator's attention.
8. Enter the **Order Total** amount, which will be calculated automatically.
9. Select a **Payment Method** from the available options.
10. Enter the **Payment Details** for the selected payment method.
11. Enter the **Order Confirmation** and **Shipping Confirmation** dates.
12. Select the **Order Status** and **Shipping Status** of the order.
13. Choose the **Payment Status** of the order.
14. Add any **Order Comments** and **Shipping Comments** for the administrator's attention.
15. Enter the **Team Name**, **Team Number**, and **Order Date** for the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: A required field for your name.
* **Email**: A required field for your email address, which will be used for communication.
* **Phone**: A required field for your phone number.
* **Team**: A required field for selecting the team you wish to order for.
* **Order Quantity**: A required field for the quantity of wristbands you wish to order.
* **Shipping Address**: A required field for the address where the order will be shipped.
* **City**: An optional field for the city of the shipping address.
* **State**: An optional field for the state of the shipping address.
* **Zip**: A required field for the zip code of the shipping address.
* **Order Notes**: An optional field for any additional notes for the administrator.
* **Order Total**: A required field, which will be calculated automatically.
* **Payment Method**: A required field for selecting the payment method.
* **Payment Details**: A required field for the payment details.
* **Order Confirmation**: A required field for the order confirmation date.
* **Shipping Confirmation**: A required field for the shipping confirmation date.
* **Order Status**: A required field for selecting the status of the order.
* **Shipping Status**: A required field for selecting the shipping status.
* **Payment Status**: A required field for selecting the payment status.
* **Order Comments**: An optional field for any comments on the order.
* **Shipping Comments**: An optional field for any comments on the shipping.
* **Team Name**: A required field for the name of the team.
* **Team Number**: A required field for the team number.
* **Order Date**: A required field for the order date.
* **Created At**: An optional field, not required for this form but used by the administrator for tracking.
* **Shipping Details**: An optional field for additional shipping details.
* **Order Form Submission**: An optional field, not required for this form but used by the administrator for tracking.
