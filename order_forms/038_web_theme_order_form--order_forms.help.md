# Web Theme Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Web Theme Order Form is designed to collect information about the desired web theme, its specifications, and the user's contact details for the web designer.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of web theme you want from the "theme-type" field.
2. Choose the color scheme for your theme from the "color-scheme" field.
3. Select the size of your theme from the "theme-size" field.
4. Pick the custom elements you want to include in your theme from the "custom-elements" field.
5. Choose the design style for your theme from the "design-style" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "web-designer-email" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "web-designer-phone" field.
8. Enter your name in the "web-designer-name" field.
9. Enter any custom message for your theme in the "custom-message" field.
10. Review your selections before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Theme-type** (`theme-type`, select_one): Choose the type of web theme you want. Options are theme-1, theme-2, and theme-3.
* **Color Scheme** (`color-scheme`, select_multiple): Select multiple colors for your theme from color-1, color-2, and color-3.
* **Theme Size** (`theme-size`, number): Enter the size of your theme. (Note: The label is different from field 13, but they are the same field.)
* **Custom Elements** (`custom-elements`, select_multiple): Select multiple custom elements for your theme from element-1, element-2, and element-3.
* **Design Style** (`design-style`, select_one): Choose the design style for your theme from style-1, style-2, and style-3.
* **Web Designer Email** (`web-designer-email`, email): Enter your email address so the web designer can contact you.
* **Web Designer Phone** (`web-designer-phone`, text): Enter your phone number so the web designer can contact you.
* **Web Designer Name** (`web-designer-name`, text): Enter your name so the web designer can contact you.
* **Custom Message** (`custom-message`, text): Enter any custom message for your theme.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct theme type and size for your theme.
* Choose a color scheme that suits your theme's style.
* Select custom elements that fit your theme's theme type.
* Choose a design style that matches your theme's color scheme.
* Enter a valid email address and phone number for the web designer to contact you.
* Enter your name so the web designer can address you personally.
* Enter any custom message for your theme if necessary.
