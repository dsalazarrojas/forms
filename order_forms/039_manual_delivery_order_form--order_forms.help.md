# Manual Delivery Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for manual delivery orders, such as delivering items to customers or clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

### Customer Info Page

* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, Required): Enter the customer's name, address, and contact information.
* **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`, text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes from the customer.

### Delivery Info Page

* **Delivery Info** (`delivery_info`, text, Required): Enter the details of the delivery, including the delivery address and other relevant information.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, Optional): Enter the date of the delivery.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, Optional): Enter the time of the delivery.

### Order Items Page

* **Order Items** (`order_items`, text, Optional): List the items to be delivered, including quantities and any special instructions.

### Delivery Status Page

* **Delivery Status** (`delivery_status`, select, Required): Select the current status of the delivery: Available for pickup, In-transit, or Arrived.
* **Delivery Comments** (`delivery_comments`, text, Optional): Enter any comments or notes about the delivery.
* **Delivery Terms** (`delivery_terms`, text, Optional): Enter any special terms or conditions related to the delivery.
* **Delivery Notes** (`delivery_notes`, text, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the delivery.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter all required fields, such as customer info and delivery status.
* Use the "Delivery Comments" field to communicate with the customer about any issues or concerns.
* Update the "Delivery Status" field regularly to reflect the current status of the delivery.
* Use the "Delivery Notes" field to record any important information about the delivery.
* Use the "Delivery Terms" field to set any special terms or conditions for the delivery.
