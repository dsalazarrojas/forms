# DIY Kit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The DIY Kit Order Form is designed to collect information from customers to place orders for DIY kits. This form will help gather essential details such as customer contact information, order date, and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (marked as "Required") to ensure that your order is processed correctly.
2. Select the correct category for your order from the list.
3. Enter a brief description of the product you are ordering.
4. Enter your customer contact information (first name, last name, email address, phone number, and address).
5. Choose the state where you are located.
6. Select the payment method you prefer (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
7. Review and submit the form to place your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category**: Select the category of the kit you are ordering from the options provided (Kits or Other).
* **Description**: Briefly describe the kit you are ordering.
* **Customer First Name**: Enter the first name of the person placing the order.
* **Customer Last Name**: Enter the last name of the person placing the order.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the person placing the order.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person placing the order.
* **Address**: Enter the physical address where the order will be delivered.
* **City**: Enter the city where the order will be delivered.
* **State**: Select the state where the order will be delivered from the options provided (CA, NY, or IL).
* **Zip**: Enter the zip code where the order will be delivered.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Order Total**: Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you prefer to use for this order (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
* **Form Title**: This field is not actually part of the form. It appears to be an extra field that is not being used in the current schema. You can ignore it or leave it blank.
