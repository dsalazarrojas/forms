# App Integration Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The App Integration Payment Form is designed for integrating with external applications, facilitating payment processing and order management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your order details in the "Order date" field, selecting a date for your order.
2.  Enter your order ID, which will be auto-generated if not provided.
3.  Enter your customer's full name in the "Customer full name" field.
4.  Enter your customer's email address in the "Email address" field.
5.  Enter your customer's phone number in the "Phone number" field.
6.  If applicable, enter your company name in the "Company name" field.
7.  Enter the product or service being purchased in the "Product or service being purchased" field.
8.  Enter the quantity of the product or service being purchased in the "Quantity" field.
9.  Enter the unit price of the product or service in the "Unit price" field.
10. Enter the subtotal amount, which will be calculated based on the quantity and unit price.
11. Enter the tax amount in the "Tax amount" field, if applicable.
12. If applicable, enter the discount or coupon applied to the order in the "Discount or coupon applied" field.
13. Enter the final total amount due in the "Total payment amount" field.
14. Enter the full billing address in the "Billing address" field.
15. Enter the city of the billing address in the "City" field.
16. Enter the state or province of the billing address in the "State or Province" field.
17. Enter the zip or postal code of the billing address in the "Zip or Postal Code" field.
18. Enter the country of the billing address in the "Country" field.
19. Determine if the shipping address is the same as the billing address in the "Shipping address same as billing" field.
20. If the shipping address is different from the billing address, enter the shipping address in the "Shipping address" field.
21. Select the shipping method in the "Shipping method" field, which can be standard, express, overnight, or digital delivery.
22. Select the payment method in the "Payment method" field, which can be credit card, debit card, bank transfer, PayPal, Apple Pay, or Google Pay.
23. If paying by card, select the card type in the "Card type" field.
24. Enter any special instructions or notes in the "Special instructions or notes" field.
25. Finally, confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions in the "I agree to the terms and conditions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order date** (order_date, date, required): Enter the date for your order.
* **Order ID** (order_id, text, required): Enter your order ID, which will be auto-generated if not provided.
* **Customer full name** (customer_name, text, required): Enter your customer's full name.
* **Email address** (customer_email, email, required): Enter your customer's email address.
* **Phone number** (customer_phone, text, required): Enter your customer's phone number.
* **Company name** (company_name, text, required): Enter your company name, if applicable.
* **Product or service being purchased** (product_service, text, required): Enter the product or service being purchased.
* **Quantity** (product_quantity, number, required): Enter the quantity of the product or service being purchased.
* **Unit price** (unit_price, number, required): Enter the unit price of the product or service in dollars.
* **Subtotal** (subtotal, number, required): Enter the subtotal amount, which will be calculated based on the quantity and unit price.
* **Tax amount** (tax_amount, number, required): Enter the tax amount in dollars, if applicable.
* **Discount or coupon applied** (discount_applied, number, required): Enter the discount or coupon applied, if applicable.
* **Total payment amount** (total_amount, number, required): Enter the final total amount due in dollars.
* **Billing address** (billing_address, text, required): Enter the full billing address.
* **City** (billing_city, text, required): Enter the city of the billing address.
* **State or Province** (billing_state, text, required): Enter the state or province of the billing address.
* **Zip or Postal Code** (billing_zipcode, text, required): Enter the zip or postal code of the billing address.
* **Country** (billing_country, text, required): Enter the country of the billing address.
* **Shipping address same as billing** (shipping_address_same, select_one, required): Determine if the shipping address is the same as the billing address.
* **Shipping address** (shipping_address, text, required): If the shipping address is different from the billing address, enter the shipping address.
* **Shipping method** (shipping_method, select_one, required): Select the shipping method, which can be standard, express, overnight, or digital delivery.
* **Payment method** (payment_method, select_one, required): Select the payment method, which can be credit card, debit card, bank transfer, PayPal, Apple Pay, or Google Pay.
* **Card type** (card_type, select_one, required): If paying by card, select the card type, which can be Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, or Diners Club.
* **Special instructions or notes** (special_instructions, text, required): Enter any special instructions or notes.
* **I agree to the terms and conditions** (terms_agreement, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely to ensure seamless integration with the application.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the application's documentation or contact support for assistance.
* This form is a crucial part of the order management process, and filling it out accurately will help ensure that orders are processed correctly and payments are made efficiently.
