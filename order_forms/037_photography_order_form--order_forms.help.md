# Photography Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photography Order Form is designed to collect information about the customer's order for photography services. This form helps the photographer or order management system to efficiently process and fulfill the customer's request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the **Form Submission** field. This is the main entry point for your order information.
2. Fill out the **Customer Name** field with your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
3. Provide your **Customer Email** address for communication and updates.
4. Enter your **Customer Phone** number for quick contact.
5. Choose your preferred **Photography Session** option from the dropdown list.
6. Select the desired **Photography Package** from the list provided.
7. Enter the date and time for the photography session in the **Photography Date** and **Photography Time** fields respectively.
8. If necessary, add any additional **Photography Notes** in the provided field.
9. Enter the **Assigned Tool** used for the session, if applicable.
10. Fill out your **Form ID** and **Form Version** fields for record-keeping purposes.
11. Provide your **Customer Address**, **Customer City**, **Customer State**, **Customer Zip**, and **Customer Country** for delivery and location details.
12. Enter your **Customer Website** (if applicable).
13. Write a message to the customer in the **Customer Message** field.
14. Enter your **Photographer's Name**, **Photographer's Email**, and **Photographer's Phone** for contact information.
15. Select the preferred **Photography Service** from the dropdown list.
16. Review the **Photography Terms** and **Photography Agreement** fields for any additional terms or conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Submission**: The main entry point for your order information. Enter your order details accurately and thoroughly.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Customer Email**: Provide a valid email address for communication and updates.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number for quick contact.
* **Photography Session**: Choose your preferred session option from the dropdown list.
* **Photography Package**: Select the desired package from the list provided.
* **Photography Date**: Enter the date for the photography session.
* **Photography Time**: Enter the time for the photography session.
* **Photography Notes**: Add any additional notes for the session.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool used for the session, if applicable.
* **Form ID**: Fill out your form ID for record-keeping purposes.
* **Form Version**: Enter the version of the form for record-keeping purposes.
* **Customer Address**: Enter your delivery address.
* **Customer City**: Enter your city.
* **Customer State**: Enter your state or province.
* **Customer Zip**: Enter your zip or postal code.
* **Customer Country**: Enter your country.
* **Customer Website**: Enter your website (if applicable).
* **Customer Message**: Write a message to the customer.
* **Photographer's Name**: Enter your name as the photographer.
* **Photographer's Email**: Enter your email address as the photographer.
* **Photographer's Phone**: Enter your phone number as the photographer.
* **Photography Service**: Select the preferred service from the dropdown list.
* **Photography Terms**: Review the terms and conditions related to the service.
* **Photography Agreement**: Review the agreement related to the service.
*   **Photography Agreement**: This field is currently not being used.
