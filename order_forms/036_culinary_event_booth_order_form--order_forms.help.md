# Culinary Event Booth Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Culinary Event Booth Order Form is designed to gather information about a vendor's booth participation and payment details for a culinary event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant information about your booth participation by choosing between 'Yes' or 'No' for the "Booth Order" field.
2. Enter your Vendor Name to ensure that the event organizers can contact you efficiently.
3. Choose the correct date for the order and payment.
4. If applicable, provide the total cost of your booth participation.
5. Select the preferred payment method for your order.
6. Enter the payment date if applicable.
7. Fill in your payment amount.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Booth Order** (`1`, Select Multiple, Required): This field is used to decide if you would like to participate in the event as a vendor. Select 'Yes' if you're interested in taking part in the event. 
* **Vendor Name** (`2`, Text, Optional): Please fill in your name or company name that will be used for communication and order processing.
* **Order Date** (`3`, Date, Optional): If applicable, choose the date when you'd like to place your order.
* **Total Cost** (`4`, Number, Optional): Enter the total cost of your booth participation if it applies to your order.
* **Payment Method** (`5`, Select One, Optional): Choose the preferred method of payment for your order. For example, 'Yes' for credit card or 'No' for bank transfer.
* **Payment Date** (`6`, Date, Optional): If you've chosen to pay, select the date when you'd like to make the payment.
* **Payment Amount** (`7`, Number, Optional): Fill in the total payment amount for your order.
* **Event Name** (`8`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the event you'll be participating in, which will help the event organizers track your participation efficiently.
