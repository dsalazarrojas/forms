# E Commerce Store Launch Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E Commerce Store Launch Checklist Form is designed to gather essential information about an e-commerce store's launch details. This form helps store owners and administrators to document and track crucial settings and configurations before launching a new store.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields (marked as "required" in the form) with accurate and up-to-date information.
* For fields with options, choose the relevant value from the provided options.
* Enter any additional relevant information in the text fields.
* Review and validate your input before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Launch Date**: Enter the date you plan to launch your store. This field is not required, but it's helpful for scheduling and planning purposes.
* **Store Name**: Enter the name of your store, which will be used to identify and reference your store.
* **Store URL**: Enter the URL of your store's website. This field should include the full URL (e.g., `https://example.com/store`).
* **Store Category**: Enter a brief description of your store's category or niche (e.g., fashion, electronics, etc.).
* **Store Tagline**: Enter a short tagline or slogan that describes your store's purpose or unique value proposition.
* **Payment Gateway**: Select the payment gateway you will be using for transactions (e.g., Stripe, PayPal, Authorize.net).
* **Payment Terms**: Enter any payment terms or conditions that apply to your store (e.g., payment methods, processing fees, etc.).
* **Shipping Rates**: Enter the rates or shipping costs for your store. This field should be a numeric value (e.g., $10, 5% of order total).
* **Shipping Policies**: Enter any shipping policies or instructions that apply to your store (e.g., delivery times, package handling, etc.).
* **Store Owner Name**: Enter your name or the name of the store owner. This field is not required for technical purposes, but it can be useful for contact and communication.

## Tips

* Double-check your input for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* Use the options provided for fields with options to ensure consistency and standardization in your data.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your administrator or support team.
