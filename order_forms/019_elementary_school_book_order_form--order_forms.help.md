# Elementary School Book Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to update the book order records for an elementary school student. It captures essential student information and contact details for school communication. Please complete this form with accurate and up-to-date information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the student's last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter the school name in the "School Name" field.
4. Enter the parent's or guardian's email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter the parent's or guardian's phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Enter the student's home address in the "Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the student's first name as it appears on their name tag or ID card.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the student's last name as it appears on their name tag or ID card.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the student's elementary school.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for the parent or guardian to receive important updates and notifications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter a valid phone number for the parent or guardian to receive important calls and updates.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter the student's home address for delivery and contact purposes.

## Tips

- Double-check the accuracy of the student's name, school name, and contact information to ensure correct updates.
- Use a valid email address and phone number to ensure timely communication.
- Enter a detailed home address to facilitate delivery of school resources.
