# Scentsy Holiday Collection Warmers Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about new holiday collection warmers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for each field as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Holiday Collection** (`new_holiday_collection`): Enter the title of the new holiday collection.
* **Explore Warmers** (`explore_warmers`): This field is not applicable for new holiday collection warmers and can be left blank.
* **Get The Inside Scoop** (`get_the_inside_scoop`): This field is not applicable for new holiday collection warmers and can be left blank.
* **Get The Inside Scoop On Whats New** (`get_the_inside_scoop_on_what`): This field is not applicable for new holiday collection warmers and can be left blank.
* **No Coding** (`no_coding`): This field is not applicable for new holiday collection warmers and can be left blank.
* **Explore** (`explore`): This field is not applicable for new holiday collection warmers and can be left blank.
* **New Collection Items** (`new_collection_items`): Enter the name of the new collection item.
* **Item Details** (`item_details`): Enter a brief description of the new collection item.
* **Item Name** (`item_name`): Enter the name of the new collection item.
* **Item Description** (`item_description`): Enter a detailed description of the new collection item.
* **Item Price** (`item_price`): Enter the price of the new collection item.
* **Item Quantity** (`item_quantity`): Enter the quantity of the new collection item.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate information collection.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact us at form@gic.mx for assistance.
