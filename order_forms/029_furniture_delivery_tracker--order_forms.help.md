# Furniture Delivery Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Furniture Delivery Tracker form is used to track the status of furniture deliveries from the warehouse to the retailer and then to the customer. It collects information about each delivery, including delivery status, confirmation from the retailer and warehouse, and any additional notes or details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select the Delivery Status**: Choose the current status of the delivery from the options: Delivered, Delayed, Pending, or Cancelled.
2. Confirm **Retailer Confirmation**: If the delivery has been received by the retailer, select 'Yes'. If not, select 'No'.
3. Confirm **Warehouse Confirmation**: If the delivery has been received by the warehouse, select 'Yes'. If not, select 'No'.
4. Confirm **Delivery Team Confirmation**: If the delivery has been completed, select 'Yes'. If not, select 'No'.
5. **Date of Delivery**: Enter the date of the delivery in the correct format (mm/dd/yy).
6. **Time of Delivery**: Enter the time of the delivery in the format hh:mm.
7. **Delivery Notes**: Enter any additional notes or details about the delivery.
8. **Date of Delivery Notes**: Enter the date and time of when the delivery notes were made.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Delivery Status**: The current status of the delivery (Delivered, Delayed, Pending, or Cancelled).
* **Retailer Confirmation**: Confirmation from the retailer that the delivery has been received.
* **Warehouse Confirmation**: Confirmation from the warehouse that the delivery has been received.
* **Delivery Team Confirmation**: Confirmation from the delivery team that the delivery has been completed.
* **Date of Delivery**: The date of the delivery in the format mm/dd/yy.
* **Time of Delivery**: The time of the delivery in the format hh:mm.
* **Delivery Notes**: Additional notes or details about the delivery.
* **Date of Delivery Notes**: The date and time when the delivery notes were made.

## Tips
* Make sure to update the delivery status regularly to reflect the current status of the delivery.
* Ensure that the retailer, warehouse, and delivery team confirmations are accurate and up-to-date.
* Use the delivery notes field for any additional information about the delivery that may be useful for future reference.
