# Bishop Storehouse Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bishop Storehouse Order Form is a simple form used to collect order information from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Select your preferred storehouse location in the "Storehouse Location" field.
3. Choose a date for your order in the "Order Date" field.
4. Select your preferred pickup time in the "Preferred Pickup Time" field.
5. If you'd like to add any additional items or special requests, you can enter them in the "Additional Items or Special Requests" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Storehouse Location**: Select the storehouse location from which you'd like to pick up your order.
* **Order Date**: Choose a date for your order, or select a future date for delivery or pickup.
* **Preferred Pickup Time**: Select your preferred time for pickup or delivery.
* **Additional Items or Special Requests**: Enter any additional items or special requests you have.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
