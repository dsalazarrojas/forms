# Subscription Cancellation Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers who want to cancel their subscriptions and complete the offboarding process. Please fill out this form with accurate information to ensure a smooth cancellation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Answer the "Why are you canceling your subscription?" question to provide us with a clear understanding of your reason for cancellation.
2. Enter the date and time you will be canceling your subscription in the "When will you be canceling your subscription?" and "What time will you be canceling your subscription?" fields.
3. Provide a date and time for when you think you will complete the offboarding process in the "When do you think you will complete the offboarding process?" and "At what time will you complete the offboarding process?" fields.
4. If you have any comments or feedback about our offboarding process, please add them in the "Feedback about our offboarding process" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This will be the contact method we use to communicate with you about the offboarding process.
6. You can add any additional comments about the cancellation in the "Comments" field.
7. If your reason for cancellation is not listed in the "Why are you canceling your subscription?" field, you can specify it in the "Other (please specify)" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Why are you canceling your subscription?** (`cancellation_reason`, text, optional):
    This field is where you should provide a clear explanation for why you are canceling your subscription. Please be as detailed as possible so that we can better understand your reason for cancellation.
*   **When will you be canceling your subscription?** (`cancellation_date`, date, optional):
    This field is where you should enter the date you will be canceling your subscription. Please use the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
*   **What time will you be canceling your subscription?** (`cancellation_date_time`, time, optional):
    This field is where you should enter the time you will be canceling your subscription. Please use the 24-hour time format (HH:MM).
*   **When do you think you will complete the offboarding process?** (`offboarding_date`, date, optional):
    This field is where you should enter the date you think you will complete the offboarding process. Please use the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
*   **At what time will you complete the offboarding process?** (`offboarding_date_time`, time, optional):
    This field is where you should enter the time you think you will complete the offboarding process. Please use the 24-hour time format (HH:MM).
*   **Feedback about our offboarding process** (`feedback`, text, optional):
    This field is where you can add any comments or feedback about our offboarding process. Your input will help us to improve our service.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
    This field is where you should enter your email address. This will be the contact method we use to communicate with you about the offboarding process.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional):
    This field is where you can add your phone number. We may contact you over the phone to discuss the offboarding process.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional):
    This field is where you can add any additional comments or information about the cancellation.
*   **Other (please specify)** (`cancellation_reason_other`, text, optional):
    This field is where you can specify a reason for cancellation that is not listed in the "Why are you canceling your subscription?" field.
