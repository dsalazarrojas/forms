# Exhibitor Booth Upgrade Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help exhibitors upgrade their booths at an event. It collects information about the upgrade requirements, payment details, and event-related information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the basic information of your exhibitor booth in the "Basic Info" section.
2. Select any additional add-ons you would like to upgrade your booth with in the "Add-ons" section.
3. Choose your payment method and provide any required payment details in the "Payment Method" and "Payment Details" sections.
4. Provide event details such as date, start and end time, venue, and event name in the "Event Details" section.
5. Add any additional information about your upgrade requirements in the "Additional Info" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Basic Info** (id: 1, required: false): This section collects the basic information about your exhibitor booth.
* **Add-ons** (id: 2, required: false): Select any additional add-ons you would like to upgrade your booth with.
	+ Options: 1, 2, 3
* **Payment Method** (id: 3, required: false): Choose your payment method for the upgrade.
	+ Options: Bank Transfer, Credit Card, PayPal, Check
* **Payment Details** (id: 4, required: false): Provide any required payment details.
* **Date of Event** (id: 5, required: false): Enter the date of the event.
* **Start Time of Event** (id: 6, required: false): Enter the start time of the event.
* **End Time of Event** (id: 7, required: false): Enter the end time of the event.
* **Venue** (id: 8, required: false): Enter the venue where the event is taking place.
* **Event Name** (id: 9, required: false): Enter the name of the event.
* **Additional Info** (id: 10, required: false): Add any additional information about your upgrade requirements.
* **Total Value** (id: 11, required: false): Enter the total value of your upgrade requirements.
* **Payment Status** (id: 12, required: false): Choose the status of your payment.
	+ Options: Paid, Partially Paid, Pending Payment, Not Paid
* **Comments** (id: 13, required: false): Add any comments about your payment.
* **Email** (id: 14, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (id: 15, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Add-ons** (id: 16, required: false): Select any additional add-ons you would like to upgrade your booth with.
	+ Options: 1, 2, 3

* **Date of Addon** (id: 20, required: false): Enter the date of the add-on event.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields to ensure your upgrade requirements are met.
* Double-check your payment details to avoid any issues with payment processing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
