# Concert Ticket Delivery Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information for delivering concert tickets to customers. It helps ensure that tickets are delivered securely and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling in the required fields with accurate information to ensure smooth delivery.
2.  Choose the preferred delivery method and timing for your concert tickets.
3.  Enter your contact information to receive delivery-related communications.
4.  Optionally provide additional information if you have special delivery requirements.
5.  Review and submit the form to initiate ticket delivery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Order Reference** (1, `Order Reference`, note, not required): This field is not mandatory but can be used for any reference or tracking purposes.
*   **Order ID** (2, `Order ID`, text, required): Enter your order confirmation number.
*   **Event Name** (3, `Event Name`, text, required): Specify the name of the concert event.
*   **Event Date** (4, `Event Date`, date, required): Enter the date of the concert event.
*   **Purchaser Name** (5, `Purchaser Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person purchasing the concert tickets.
*   **Purchaser Email** (6, `Purchaser Email`, email, required): Enter your email address for delivery and confirmation purposes.
*   **Delivery Options** (7, `Delivery Options`, note, not required): Select any additional delivery options you may need.
*   **Preferred Delivery Method** (8, `Preferred Delivery Method`, select\_one, required): Choose how you would like to receive your concert tickets:
    *   Electronic Email Delivery
    *   Mobile Wallet Download
    *   Will Call Box Office Pickup
    *   Standard Mail
    *   Express Courier
*   **Delivery Email Address** (9, `Delivery Email Address`, email, required): Enter the email address to receive electronic tickets if selected.
*   **Mobile Phone Number** (10, `Mobile Phone Number`, text, not required): Enter your mobile number for mobile ticket delivery.
*   **Will Call Pickup Name** (11, `Will Call Pickup Name`, text, not required): Enter your name for box office pickup.
*   **Pickup ID Number** (12, `Pickup ID Number`, text, not required): Enter the ID number for verification if applicable.
*   **Mailing Address** (13, `Mailing Address`, note, not required): Provide your address for physical ticket delivery if needed.
*   **Recipient Name** (14, `Recipient Name`, text, not required): Specify who should receive the tickets if different from the purchaser.
*   **Street Address** (15, `Street Address`, text, not required): Enter the full street address.
*   **Apartment or Suite** (16, `Apartment or Suite`, text, not required): Enter the unit number if applicable.
*   **City** (17, `City`, text, not required): Enter the city name.
*   **State or Province** (18, `State or Province`, text, not required): Enter the state or province name.
*   **Postal Code** (19, `Postal Code`, text, not required): Enter the ZIP or postal code.
*   **Country** (20, `Country`, select\_one, not required): Choose the country for delivery:
    *   United States
    *   Canada
    *   United Kingdom
    *   Australia
    *   Other
*   **Delivery Timing Preference** (21, `Delivery Timing Preference`, select\_one, required): Select when you would like to receive your tickets:
    *   Send Immediately
    *   Send One Week Before Event
    *   Send Three Days Before Event
    *   Hold for Will Call
*   **Special Delivery Instructions** (22, `Special Delivery Instructions`, text, not required): Enter any specific delivery notes.
*   **Signature Required** (23, `Signature Required`, select\_one, not required): Choose whether a signature is required:
    *   Yes Signature Required
    *   No Leave at Door
*   **Delivery Insurance** (24, `Delivery Insurance`, select\_one, not required): Protect your tickets during shipping:
    *   Yes Add Insurance
    *   No Insurance Needed
*   **Delivery Confirmation** (25, `Delivery Confirmation`, select\_one, required): Choose how you should be confirmed:
    *   Email Confirmation
    *   SMS Text Confirmation
    *   Both Email and SMS
    *   No Confirmation Needed
