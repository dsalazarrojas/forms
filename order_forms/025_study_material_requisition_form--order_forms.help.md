# Study Material Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Study Material Requisition Form is a simple and straightforward form that allows you to request materials from a central inventory or repository. This form is used to submit a request for materials, providing essential information about the institution, material details, and any additional comments.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the institution requesting the material.
2. Select the date of the material requisition request.
3. Enter the time of the request, if applicable.
4. Enter the title of the material you are requesting.
5. Specify the type of material being requested.
6. Enter the quantity of material needed.
7. Enter your name and title, as well as the title of the person or department that the material is being requisitioned for.
8. Enter any relevant comments about the material request.
9. Verify and submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Institution Name** (institution_name, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the institution making the material requisition request.
* **Requisition Date** (requisition_date, date, required/optional): Select the date of the material requisition request. This date will help track and identify the request in the inventory.
* **Requisition Time** (requisition_time, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the request, if applicable. This field helps track the timing of the request for further reference.
* **Material Title** (material_title, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the material being requested. This helps the repository identify the specific material being sought.
* **Material Type** (material_type, text, required/optional): Specify the type of material being requested. This could include, but not limited to, book titles, journal articles, or other types of materials.
* **Quantity Requested** (quantity_requested, number, required/optional): Enter the number of materials being requested. This helps in determining the required quantity for the repository.
* **Requisitioned By** (requisitioned_by, text, required/optional): Enter your name and title, as well as the title of the person or department the material is being requisitioned for. This ensures proper attribution and identification of the requester.
* **Requisitioned For** (requisitioned_for, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the person or department the material is being requisitioned for. This field provides clarity on the intended use of the requested material.
* **Material ID** (material_id, text, required/optional): Enter any unique identifier for the material being requested. This could be a reference number or a unique code.
* **Comments** (comments, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or details about the material request, such as the intended use or any specific requirements. This helps the repository or inventory manager understand the request better.
