# zz_golf_togo - Help Guide
## Purpose
The zz_golf_togo form is used to capture order details, payment information, and customer contact and address information for the purpose of processing and delivering orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the "Order Form" section with your order details.
2. Select your preferred payment method from the options provided.
3. Enter your delivery date and time in the "Delivery Date" and "Delivery Time" fields.
4. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Enter the total value of your order in the "Order Total" field.
8. Enter your name in the "Customer Name" field.
9. Enter your email address in the "Customer Email" field.
10. Enter your phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
11. Enter your address in the "Customer Address" field.
12. Add any additional notes about yourself in the "Customer Note" field.
13. Provide any comments or feedback in the "Customer Comment" field.
