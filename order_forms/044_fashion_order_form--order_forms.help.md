# Fashion Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fashion Order Form is used to collect information from customers for fashion orders. It is designed to gather relevant details such as products, shipping addresses, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read through each field carefully and understand its purpose.
* Fill in your information accordingly, making sure to include all required fields.
* Select the correct options for each field where applicable.
* Review your submission before submitting the form to ensure all information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page** (`first_page`, text, optional): This field is a general information field where you can write any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Select Products** (`products`, select_multiple, optional): Select the products you would like to order. You can choose multiple products by holding down the Ctrl key (or Command key on a Mac) while selecting.
* **Add Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or special requests for your order here.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping`, select_one, optional): Choose the shipping address for your order from the provided options.
* **Total** (`total`, number, optional): If you know the total cost of your order, please enter it here. Note: This field is not required but will help us process your order more efficiently.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications about your order.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your order.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select the tool you would like to use to process your order. Please choose "chatjimmy" if you have a specific request.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.

## Tips

* Make sure to select the correct products and shipping address for your order.
* If you have any special requests or comments, use the "Add Comments" field to include them.
* Enter your email address and phone number so we can contact you about your order.
* If you know the total cost of your order, please enter it to help us process your order more efficiently.
