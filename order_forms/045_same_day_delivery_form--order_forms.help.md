# same_day_delivery_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "same_day_delivery_form" is designed to collect customer information and delivery details for a same-day delivery order. This form is intended for customers to provide their personal and delivery information for a quick and efficient order fulfillment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the "Customer Info" section.
2. Enter your phone number and email address in the "Phone" and "Email" fields, respectively.
3. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
4. Enter the delivery date and time in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
5. Enter any delivery information, such as instructions or special requests, in the "Delivery Info" section.
6. If applicable, select the "Product List" field to indicate if products are included in the order.
7. Enter the order price and discount information in the "Price" and "Discount" fields.
8. Select the payment method for the order in the "Payment Method" field.
9. If other payment methods are selected, enter the details in the "Other" field.
10. Provide any additional order information and notes in the "Order Info Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, required): Enter your name and any other personal details.
* **Phone** (`customer_info_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`customer_info_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Notes** (`customer_info_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information.
* **Delivery Info** (`delivery_info`, text, optional): Enter any delivery information or instructions.
* **Date** (`delivery_info_date`, date, optional): Enter the delivery date.
* **Time** (`delivery_info_time`, time, optional): Enter the delivery time.
* **Note** (`delivery_info_note`, text, optional): Enter any additional delivery information or notes.
* **Delivery Info Phone** (`delivery_info_phone`, text, optional): Enter the delivery phone number.
* **Delivery Info Email** (`delivery_info_email`, email, optional): Enter the delivery email address.
* **Address** (`delivery_info_address`, text, optional): Enter the delivery address.
* **Zip** (`delivery_info_zip`, text, optional): Enter the delivery zip code.
* **City** (`delivery_info_city`, text, optional): Enter the delivery city.
* **State** (`delivery_info_state`, text, optional): Enter the delivery state.
* **Country** (`delivery_info_country`, text, optional): Enter the delivery country.
* **Instructions** (`delivery_info_instructions`, text, optional): Enter any delivery instructions or information.
* **Delivery Info Note 2** (`delivery_info_note_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional delivery information or notes.
* **Order Info** (`order_info`, text, optional): Enter any order information.
* **Products** (`order_info_products`, text, optional): Enter the products included in the order.
* **Product List** (`order_info_products_list`, select_multiple, optional): Select which products are included in the order.
* **Price** (`order_info_price`, text, optional): Enter the order price.
* **Discount** (`order_info_discount`, text, optional): Enter the discount information.
* **Payment Method** (`order_info_payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select the payment method for the order.
* **Other** (`order_info_payment_method_other`, text, optional): Enter any other payment information.
* **Order Info Notes** (`order_info_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional order information or notes.
