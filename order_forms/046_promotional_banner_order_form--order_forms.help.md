# promotional-banner-order-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "promotional-banner-order-form" is used to collect information from users for promotional banner orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your user input information in the "User Input" field.
2. Select the payment method and provide necessary payment information in the "Payment Information" field.
3. Provide banner details, including any relevant information, in the "Banner Details" field.
4. Upload or link your banner image in the "Banner Image" field.
5. Enter order information, if necessary, in the "Order Information" field.
6. Enter the date and time of the order in the "Order Date" and "Order Time" fields, respectively.
7. Add any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
9. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
10. Enter your name or title in the "Form Submitter" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`User Input`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about the order.
* **Payment Information** (`Payment Information`, text, required): Enter any necessary payment information for the order.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select_one, optional): Select the payment method used for the order (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Banner Details** (`Banner Details`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the banner.
* **Banner Image** (`Banner Image`, text, required): Upload or link the banner image.
* **Order Information** (`Order Information`, text, optional): Enter any additional order information.
* **Order Date** (`Order Date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the order.
* **Order Time** (`Order Time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the order.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the order.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Form Submitter** (`Form Submitter`, text, required): Enter your name or title.
