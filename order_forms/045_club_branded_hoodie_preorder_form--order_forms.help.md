# Club Branded Hoodie Preorder Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to place a preorder for a custom-made Club Branded Hoodie. By filling out the form, users can select their preferred hoodie color, size, style, and quantity, as well as add custom details like name and number. They will also be able to provide their shipping address, delivery date, and preferred payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields, including your name, email, phone number, club affiliation, delivery address, city, state, zip, expected delivery date, price per unit, total cost, preferred payment method, and preorder confirmation.
2. For fields with options, select one of the provided options that best fits your needs.
3. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Enter your full name in this field. (Text, Required)
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address. (Email, Required)
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number. (Text, Optional)
* **Club or Organization**: Enter the club or organization you belong to. (Text, Required)
* **Hoodie Selection --**: This is an optional section where you can choose from various hoodie preferences. (Note, Optional)
	+ **Hoodie Color Preference**: Choose the color of the hoodie you prefer. (Select One, Required)
	+ **Hoodie Size**: Choose the size of the hoodie you prefer. (Select One, Required)
	+ **Hoodie Style**: Choose the style of the hoodie you prefer. (Select One, Required)
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of hoodies you wish to order. (Number, Required)
* **-- Customization Options --**: This is an optional section where you can add custom details like name and number on the hoodie. (Note, Optional)
	+ **Custom Name on Hoodie**: Enter a custom name to print on the hoodie. (Text, Optional)
	+ **Custom Number on Hoodie**: Enter a custom number to print on the hoodie. (Text, Optional)
* **-- Size Information --**: This is an optional section where you can help us get the right fit by entering your chest measurement. (Note, Optional)
	+ **Chest Measurement (inches)**: Enter your chest measurement in inches. (Number, Optional)
	+ **Length Preference**: Choose the length preference for your hoodie. (Select One, Optional)
* **-- Delivery Information --**: This is where you enter your delivery details. (Note, Optional)
	+ **Delivery Address**: Enter the address where you would like us to ship the hoodie. (Text, Required)
	+ **City, State, Zip**: Enter your city, state, and zip code. (Text, Required)
	+ **Expected Delivery Date**: Enter the date when you would like to receive the hoodie. (Date, Required)
* **-- Payment Information --**: This is where you enter your payment details. (Note, Optional)
	+ **Price Per Hoodie**: Enter the cost per unit of the hoodie. (Number, Required)
	+ **Total Cost**: The total cost for the number of hoodies you ordered. (Number, Required)
	+ **Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method. (Select One, Required)
* **Special Instructions or Notes**: Enter any special instructions or notes. (Text, Optional)
* **Preorder Confirmation**: Confirm that you have reviewed and understood the preorder details. (Select One, Required)
