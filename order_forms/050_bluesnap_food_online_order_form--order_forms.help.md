# BlueSnap Food Online Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The BlueSnap Food Online Order Form is designed to facilitate online food ordering for customers. This form is part of a larger system for managing food delivery orders, allowing customers to provide their contact information, select their preferred delivery time and meal category, and specify their order details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your Full Name**: Please provide your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. **Enter your Email Address**: Enter your valid email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. **Enter your Phone Number**: Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. **Enter your Delivery Address**: Enter your delivery address in the "Delivery Address" field.
5. **Select your City**: Select your city of delivery in the "City" field.
6. **Enter your ZIP/Postal Code**: Enter your ZIP/Postal code in the "ZIP/Postal Code" field.
7. **Select your Preferred Delivery Time**: Choose your preferred delivery time from the options provided in the "Preferred Delivery Time" field.
8. **Select your Meal Category**: Choose your meal category from the options provided in the "Meal Category" field.
9. **Select your Entrees**: List your entrees or select from the menu in the "Entrees Selected" field.
10. **Optional: Add Side Dishes and Beverages**: If you have any side dishes or beverages to order, list them in the "Side Dishes" and "Beverages" fields respectively.
11. **Enter Special Requests or Dietary Restrictions**: If you have any special requests or dietary restrictions, enter them in the "Special Requests or Dietary Restrictions" field.
12. **Choose your Number of Meals**: Enter the number of meals you'd like to order in the "Number of Meals" field.
13. **Review your Subtotal**: Review your subtotal in the "Subtotal" field.
14. **Review your Delivery Fee and Tax Amount**: Review your delivery fee and tax amount in the "Delivery Fee" and "Tax" fields.
15. **Review your Total Amount**: Review your total amount in the "Total Amount" field.
16. **Choose your Payment Method**: Select your payment method from the options provided in the "Payment Method" field.
17. **Optional: Enter Tip Amount**: If you'd like to tip your delivery person, enter the tip amount in the "Tip Amount" field.
18. **Enter Delivery Instructions**: If you have any delivery instructions (e.g., gate code, buzzer), enter them in the "Delivery Instructions" field.
19. **Select Order Date**: Choose the date you'd like to receive your order in the "Order Date" field.
