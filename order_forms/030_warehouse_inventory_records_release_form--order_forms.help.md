# warehouse_inventory_records_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers about their release of warehouse inventory records.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please select the customer from the list of available options.
2.  Choose the items that are being released.
3.  Enter the date and time of the release.
4.  Optionally enter a reason for the release.
5.  Add any additional remarks about the release.
6.  Sign and date the form as the customer.
7.  Sign and date the form as the person releasing the inventory.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Customer Info** (Select One): Select the customer from the list provided.
*  **Item Info** (Select Multiple): Choose the items being released.
*  **Date** (Date): Enter the date of the release.
*  **Time** (Time): Enter the time of the release.
*  **Reason** (Text): Optionally enter a reason for the release.
*  **Remarks** (Text): Add any additional comments or notes about the release.
*  **Customer Signature** (Number): Please sign and date this field as the customer.
*  **Employee Signature** (Number): Please sign and date this field as the person releasing the inventory.
