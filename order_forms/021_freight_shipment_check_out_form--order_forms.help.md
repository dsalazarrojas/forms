# Freight Shipment Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and verify information for customer shipments, ensuring accurate payment processing and tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the **Freight Shipment Details**.
2. Provide **Customer Info**.
3. Specify **Shipment Info**.
4. Confirm **Payment Info**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Freight Shipment Details** (`form_section_1`, text, required/optional): Enter the specific shipment information, including shipment date, weight, and any relevant notes.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's name, address, and contact information for reference and payment processing.
* **Shipment Info** (`shipment_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the shipment's details, such as shipment number, weight, and package type.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the payment method and payment amount for the shipment, ensuring accurate and secure payment processing.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* Ensure all fields are filled out according to your business's policies and procedures.
* Contact your supervisor or customer support if you have any questions or concerns while filling out this form.
