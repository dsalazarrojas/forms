# vehicle_air_intake_boot_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers to place a vehicle order. It is designed to be completed in a specific order to ensure accurate order intake and avoid duplication of effort.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide vehicle information in the "Vehicle Information" section.
2. Choose the type of vehicle in the "Vehicle Type" section.
3. Specify the engine type in the "Engine Type" section.
4. Enter intake specifications in the "Intake Specifications" section.
5. Add boot details in the "Boot Details" section.
6. Choose a date and time for your order in the "Order Date" and "Order Time" sections.
7. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
8. Add any additional notes in the "Additional Notes" section.
9. Confirm your order in the "Confirm Order" section.
10. Choose delivery options in the "Delivery Options" section.
11. Choose a payment method in the "Payment Method" section.
12. Calculate the total cost in the "Total Cost" section.
13. Enter a confirmation code in the "Confirmation Code" section.
14. Select a payment status in the "Payment Status" section.
15. Add any further notes in the "Notes 2" section (optional).
15. Confirm your order in the "Confirm" section.
16. Add any final submission information in the "Submission" section (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Information** (Vehicle Information, text, required): Enter the details of your vehicle, including make, model, and year.
* **Vehicle Type** (Vehicle Type, text, required): Choose the type of vehicle you are ordering.
* **Engine Type** (Engine Type, text, required): Specify the engine type of your vehicle.
* **Intake Specifications** (Intake Specifications, text, required): Enter any specifications related to your vehicle's intake.
* **Boot Details** (Boot Details, text, required): Provide any details about your vehicle's boot.
* **Order Date** (Order Date, date, required): Choose the date you would like to order your vehicle.
* **Order Time** (Order Time, time, required): Choose the time you would like to order your vehicle.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, required): Enter your contact information.
* **Additional Notes** (Additional Notes, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Confirm Order** (Confirm Order, checkbox, required): Confirm that you have reviewed your order.
* **Delivery Options** (Delivery Options, select multiple, required): Choose how you would like to receive your vehicle.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select one, required): Choose your preferred payment method.
* **Total Cost** (Total Cost, number, required): Calculate the total cost of your order.
* **Confirmation Code** (Confirmation Code, text, required): Enter a confirmation code to secure your order.
* **Payment Status** (Payment Status, select one, required): Select the status of your payment.
* **Notes 2** (Notes 2, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Confirm** (Confirm, radio, required): Confirm your order.
* **Submission** (Submission, text, optional): Enter any additional submission information.

Please note that some of the fields are marked as optional, but please provide as much information as possible to ensure accurate order intake.
