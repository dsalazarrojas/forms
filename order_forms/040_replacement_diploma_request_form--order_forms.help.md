# replacement_diploma_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The replacement_diploma_request_form is used to process replacement requests for diplomas. Fill out this form to provide the necessary shipping and payment information for your replacement diploma.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your shipping information, including your street, city, state, and zip code.
2. Choose how you would like to pay the fee for your replacement diploma.
3. Enter your email address and phone number for contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Replacement Request** (`replacement_request`, text, required): Enter a clear and concise reason for requesting a replacement diploma.
* **Shipping Information** (`shipping_info`, text, required): Enter your shipping details to ensure your replacement diploma reaches you promptly.
* **Name** (`shipping_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it should appear on the diploma.
* **Street** (`shipping_street`, text, required): Enter the street address where you would like your diploma to be shipped.
* **City** (`shipping_city`, text, required): Enter the city where you would like your diploma to be shipped.
* **State** (`shipping_state`, text, required): Enter the state where you would like your diploma to be shipped.
* **Zip** (`shipping_zip`, text, required): Enter the zip code where you would like your diploma to be shipped.
* **Fee Payment Method** (`fee_payment_method`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you would like to pay for your replacement diploma. Options include Credit Card, Bank Account Transfer, and Wire Transfer.
* **Fee Amount** (`fee_amount`, number, optional): Enter the fee amount for your replacement diploma. This field is not required, but providing the amount will expedite the process.
* **Fee CVV** (`fee_cvv`, text, optional): Enter the CVV for your fee payment method. This field is not required, but providing the CVV will expedite the process.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that your shipping information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us through other means.
* Providing your fee payment information will expedite the process.
