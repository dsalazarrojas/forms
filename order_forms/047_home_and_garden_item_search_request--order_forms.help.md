# Home And Garden Item Search Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a request for a home and garden item. Fill out this form to provide us with the necessary information to process and follow up on your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the product type, name, and description in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the price range you are looking for.
3. Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
4. Provide any additional details about your request in the "Product Request Details" field.
5. Choose the priority level for your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Search** (`product_search`, text, required/optional): Enter the name or keywords of the product you are looking for.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of product you are looking for. This will help us narrow down the search results.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the product you are looking for.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional details about the product you are looking for.
* **Product Image** (`product_image`, text, required/optional): If you have an image of the product, you can enter the URL or reference number for it.
* **Price Range** (`product_price_range`, number, required/optional): Enter the price range you are looking for (e.g. $0-$100).
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter your name so we know who is making the request.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you by phone.
* **Product Request Details** (`product_request_details`, text, required): Provide any additional details about your request.
* **Product Request Priority** (`product_request_priority`, select_multiple, required): Choose the priority level for your request: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4.
* **Contact Message** (`contact_message`, text, required/optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, enter them here.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): This field will be used for internal purposes only.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): This field will be used for internal purposes only.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): This field will be used for internal purposes only.
