# Subscription Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect billing and customer details for subscription orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your billing details in the corresponding fields:
	* Enter your billing address in the "Billing Address" field.
	* Provide your contact information in the "Billing Contact" field.
	* Enter your phone number in the "Billing Phone" field.
2. Enter your customer details, such as name and email, in the "Customer Details" field.
3. Review and agree to the "Billing Terms" in the field provided.
4. Submit the completed form to finalize your subscription order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Billing Address** (billing_address): Enter your billing address to ensure accurate billing and shipping information.
* **Billing Contact** (billing_contact): Provide your contact information to help us get in touch with you for any questions or issues.
* **Billing Phone** (billing_phone): Enter your phone number to facilitate communication for billing and order-related inquiries.
* **Customer Details** (customer_details): Enter your name and email to identify you as a customer and for communication purposes.
* **Billing Terms** (billing_terms): Review and agree to the terms of billing to ensure a smooth and secure transaction.
