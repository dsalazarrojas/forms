# Cross Promotion Flyer Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Cross Promotion Flyer Order Form" is used for customers to submit orders for promotional products such as promotional flyers. This form collects contact information and order details, including delivery preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
2.  Enter the details of your order, including any special instructions, in the "Order Form" field.
3.  Select your preferred delivery date and time in the "Delivery Date" and "Delivery Time" fields.
4.  Choose your delivery method in the "Delivery Method" field.
5.  If applicable, fill in the "Delivery Address" field to ensure timely delivery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Order Form** (`order_form`, text, required): Enter the details of your order, including any special instructions.
*   **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your contact information to ensure we can get in touch with you.
*   **Flyer Request** (`flyer_request`, text, required): Enter the specific flyer you would like to order.
*   **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, optional): Enter your delivery address to ensure timely delivery. Please note that this is not required but recommended.
*   **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required): Select the specific date you'd like to receive your order.
*   **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required): Select the time you'd like to receive your order.
*   **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select multiple, optional): Select the method of delivery that suits you best. Options are "Yes" or "No".
*   **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, optional): If you have any special instructions or requests, please enter them here.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your order is processed correctly. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
