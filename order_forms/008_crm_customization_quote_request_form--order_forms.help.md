# CRM Customization Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CRM Customization Quote Request Form is designed to collect information about a customer's existing CRM system and their customization requirements. This form is used by the customization team to better understand the customer's needs and prepare a quote for the customization work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your company's name and primary contact person's name and email address.
2. Select your business industry from the list.
3. Indicate your current CRM system (if any).
4. Specify your primary customization goals.
5. Estimate the number of users for your CRM system.
6. List any required third-party integrations.
7. Specify any specific custom entities needed.
8. Provide an estimated budget for the customization work.
9. Choose a target launch date for the customization project.
10. Provide any additional technical specifications for the customization work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company's name.
* **Primary Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person for your company.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the primary contact person.
* **Business Industry** (`industry`, select_one, required): Select your business industry from the list.
* **Current CRM System** (`current_crm`, text, optional): If you currently use a CRM system, enter its name.
* **Primary Customization Goals** (`primary_goals`, select_multiple, required): Select all the primary goals for your customization work. This can include Automated Workflow, Custom Reporting, Third-Party Integration, User Interface Re-design, API Development, Data Migration, and more.
* **Estimated Number of CRM Users** (`user_count`, number, required): Estimate the number of users for your CRM system.
* **Required Third-Party Integrations** (`integration_needs`, text, optional): List any third-party integrations required for your customization work.
* **Specific Custom Entities Needed** (`custom_entities`, text, optional): Specify any custom entities needed for your customization work, such as Projects, Shipments, Subscriptions, etc.
* **Estimated Budget for Customization** (`budget_estimate`, select_one, required): Choose an estimated budget range for the customization work.
* **Target Launch Date** (`desired_launch_date`, date, required): Choose a target launch date for the customization project.
* **Additional Technical Specifications** (`additional_specs`, text, optional): Provide any additional technical specifications for the customization work.
