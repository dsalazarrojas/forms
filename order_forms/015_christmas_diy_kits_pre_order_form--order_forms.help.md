# Christmas DIY Kits Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a pre-order form for Christmas DIY kits. It allows customers to provide their information, select kit preferences, and specify delivery and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your **Customer Information** section:
	* Provide your **First Name** and **Last Name** in their corresponding fields.
	* Enter your **Email Address** and **Phone Number** to ensure we can contact you for any updates.
2. Fill in your **Shipping Address** details, including:
	* **Shipping Address**
	* **City**
	* **State or Province**
	* **ZIP or Postal Code**
	* **Country**
3. Select the **DIY Kit Type** you would like to order from the options:
	* **Ornament Making Kit**
	* **Wreath Assembly Kit**
	* **Cookie Decorating Kit**
	* **Candle Making Kit**
	* **Holiday Craft Bundle**
	* **Premium Kit Collection**
4. Choose your **Skill Level** that corresponds with your abilities:
	* **Beginner**
	* **Intermediate**
	* **Advanced**
	* **Family-friendly**
5. Indicate the **Quantity to Order** you would like to purchase.
6. If you want to personalize your kit, select one of the options:
	* **Yes, with custom name**
	* **Yes, with custom message**
	* **No personalization needed**
7. Select your **Color Preferences** for the kit:
	* **Traditional red and green**
	* **Silver and gold**
	* **Blue and white**
	* **Multi-color**
	* **No preference**
8. Choose a **Shipping Method** for your order:
	* **Standard (5-7 business days)**
	* **Express (2-3 business days)**
	* **Overnight**
	* **Local pickup**
9. If you want a **Gift Wrapping Service**, select one of the options:
	* **Yes, standard wrapping**
	* **Yes, premium wrapping**
	* **No, thank you**
10. Finally, select your **Payment Method**:
	* **Credit card**
	* **Debit card**
	* **PayPal**
	* **Bank transfer**
11. Indicate your **Preferred Delivery Date** for your order.
12. Click the "Submit" button to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (Customer Information, required): This section collects your personal details to contact you about your order.
* **First Name** (First Name, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for any updates.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you if needed.
* **Shipping Address** (Shipping Address, required): Enter your shipping address to ensure your order reaches you correctly.
* **City** (City, required): Enter the city for your shipping address.
* **State or Province** (State or Province, required): Enter the state or province for your shipping address.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (ZIP or Postal Code, required): Enter the ZIP or postal code for your shipping address.
* **Country** (Country, required): Enter the country for your shipping address.
* **DIY Kit Type** (DIY Kit Type, required): Select the type of DIY kit you would like to order.
* **Skill Level** (Skill Level, required): Choose a skill level that corresponds with your abilities.
* **Quantity to Order** (Quantity to Order, required): Indicate the number of kits you would like to order.
* **Custom Text or Name** (Custom Text or Name, optional): If you want to personalize your kit, enter your custom text or name here.
* **Color Preferences** (Color Preferences, optional): Select your color preference for the kit.
* **Preferred Delivery Date** (Preferred Delivery Date, required): Choose a date you would like to receive your order.
* **Shipping Method** (Shipping Method, required): Select a shipping method for your order.
* **Gift Wrapping Service** (Gift Wrapping Service, optional): If you want a gift wrapping service, select one of the options.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, required): Choose a payment method for your order.
* **Would you like to personalize this kit?** (Would you like to personalize this kit?, optional): If you want to personalize your kit, select one of the options.
* **Customization Options** (Customization Options, optional): If you want to personalize your kit, enter your custom text or name here.
* **Local Pickup** (Local pickup, optional): Select this option if you want to pick up your order locally.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in to avoid delays in processing your order.
* If you have any questions, feel free to contact us for assistance.
