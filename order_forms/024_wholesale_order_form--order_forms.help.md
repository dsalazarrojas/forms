# Wholesale Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help you understand the "Wholesale Order Form" and its required and optional fields to successfully complete the form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form's layout, which is divided into multiple pages.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Enter your wholesale order information, including products, quantities, and order notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wholesale Order Form** (`title`, text, not required): Enter a title for your wholesale order.
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, not required): Enter your company name.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, not required): Enter your contact person's name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, not required): Enter your company name.
* **Order Products** (`order_products`, text, not required): Enter your wholesale order products.
* **Product** (`product`, select_one, not required): Choose 'Yes' if you want to add the product to your order.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, not required): Enter the quantity of the product.
* **Add to Order** (`add_to_order`, select_multiple, not required): Choose 'Yes' to add the product to your order.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, not required): Enter any additional notes about your order.
* **Order Product** (`order_product`, select_one, not required): Choose 'Yes' to add the product to your order.
* **Order Quantity** (`order_quantity`, number, not required): Enter the quantity of the product.
* **Order Note** (`order_note`, text, not required): Enter any additional notes about the order.
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, not required): Enter your customer details.
* **Customer Company Name** (`customer_company_name`, text, not required): Enter your company name.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, not required): Enter your customer address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Customer Order Notes** (`customer_order_notes`, text, not required): Enter any additional notes about the order.
* **Product Details** (`product_details`, text, not required): Enter product details.
* **Product Image** (`product_image`, text, not required): Enter a product image URL.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, not required): Enter product name.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, not required): Enter product description.
* **Product Price** (`product_price`, number, not required): Enter product price.
* **Customer Total** (`customer_total`, number, not required): Enter your total order value.
* **Customer Total Price** (`customer_total_price`, number, not required): Enter your total price.
* **Customer Total Price Calculated** (`customer_total_price_calculated`, number, not required): Enter calculated total price.
* **Add to Cart** (`add_to_cart`, select_one, not required): Choose 'Yes' to add the product to your cart.
* **Customer Submit** (`customer_submit`, select_one, not required): Choose 'Yes' to submit the order.
* **Order Submit** (`order_submit`, select_one, not required): Choose 'Yes' to submit the order.
* **Submit** (`customer_submit`, select_one, not required): Choose 'Yes' to submit the order.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Review and double-check your entries before submitting.
* Use clear and concise language in all fields.
* If you have any issues or concerns, feel free to contact us.
