# Order Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Order Cancellation Form is a tool for customers to request cancellation of their orders. This form allows customers to provide a reason for cancellation and any additional details or comments they may have. The form is typically used when a customer wants to cancel their order due to a specific reason, and it helps the support team to quickly identify the cause of the cancellation and respond accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Order Cancellation Form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Order Cancellation Form" link to open the form.
2. Select a reason for cancellation from the dropdown menu.
3. If you selected "Other" as the reason, provide a brief description of the reason in the "Reason for Cancellation Details" field.
4. Enter any additional comments or notes related to the order in the "Customer Message" field.
5. Add any relevant notes or comments about the order in the "Customer Note" field (if necessary).
6. Click the "Submit" button to send the form to the support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Cancellation Form Page 1**: This is the first page of the form, where you can select a reason for cancellation.
* **Reason for Cancellation**:
	+ Select the reason for cancellation from the dropdown menu.
	+ Options: Cancellation Request, Refund Request, Return Request, Cancel and Refund, Other.
* **Order ID**: Please enter the ID of the order you are cancelling.
* **Reason for Cancellation Details**:
	+ If you selected "Other" as the reason, provide a brief description of the reason.
* **Customer Message**:
	+ Enter any additional comments or notes related to the order.
* **Customer Note**:
	+ Add any relevant notes or comments about the order (if necessary).
* **Submit**:
	+ Click the "Submit" button to send the form to the support team.

## Tips
* Please be concise and clear when providing information in the form.
* Use the "Reason for Cancellation Details" field to provide more information about the reason if you selected "Other".
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, feel free to contact the support team.
