# Custom Simple Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Custom Simple Order Form is designed for customers to place orders, specifying products, quantities, and delivery instructions for each item.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the product ID for each item you wish to order.
2. Enter the name of each product.
3. Specify the quantity for each product.
4. Provide any special delivery instructions for each product, if necessary.
5. Click "Submit" to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product ID** (`product_id`, text, required): Enter a unique identifier for the product you wish to order.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the product being ordered.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Specify how many units of the product to order.
* **Delivery Instructions** (`delivery_instructions`, text, not required): Provide any special delivery instructions for the product, if necessary.
* **Product ID 2**-6** (`product_id_2`-`product_id_6`, text, not required): Enter a unique identifier for additional products you wish to order, if applicable.
* **Product Name 2**-6** (`product_name_2`-`product_name_6`, text, not required): Enter the name of additional products, if applicable.
* **Quantity 2**-6** (`quantity_2`-`quantity_6`, number, not required): Specify the quantity for additional products, if applicable.
* **Delivery Instructions 2**-6** (`delivery_instructions_2`-`delivery_instructions_6`, text, not required): Provide special delivery instructions for additional products, if necessary.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to complete your order.

## Tips
- Ensure to complete all required fields.
- Double-check your entries for accuracy.
- Review your form before submission to ensure all details are correct.
