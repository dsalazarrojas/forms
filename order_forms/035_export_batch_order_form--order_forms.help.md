# Export Batch Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The export batch order form is used to collect information for exporting a batch order from our system. This form is typically used when you need to export a batch order for delivery or shipping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the products you want to export by checking the "Select Products" box next to each product.
2. Fill in your First Name, Email, and Phone (if you want to be contacted).
3. Enter the quantity of products you want to export.
4. Provide a Product Code for the order (if required by your shipping carrier).
5. Enter your Shipping Address for accurate delivery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Products** (Select Multiple): 
  Choose the products you want to export.
* **First Name** (Text): Your name.
* **Email** (Email): Your email address.
* **Phone** (Text): Your phone number (if you want to be contacted).
* **Quantity** (Number): The amount of products you want to export.
* **Product Code** (Text): A code for the order that may be required by your shipping carrier.
* **Shipping Address** (Text): The address where the order will be delivered.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have selected the correct products and entered the correct shipping address for accurate delivery.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact our support team for assistance.
