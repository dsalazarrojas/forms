# fin_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "fin_order_form" form is a digital order form designed to collect essential customer and order information for processing and fulfillment. It is used to gather details about the customer and their shipping address, product preferences, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your customer name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter your valid email address in the "Customer Email" field.
3. Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field.
4. Specify your shipping city in the "Shipping City" field.
5. Select your country of origin in the "Shipping Country" field.
6. Describe your product preferences in the "Product Preferences" field.
7. Provide any necessary payment details in the "Payment Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: This field is for the customer's name. Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer Email**: This field is for the customer's email address. Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Shipping Address**: This field is for the customer's shipping address. Please enter your address in the format "Street Address, Apartment Number, City, State/Country, ZIP/Postal Code".
* **Shipping City**: This field is for the city where the customer wants their order shipped to. Please enter the city name as it is spelled.
* **Shipping Country**: This field is for the country where the customer wants their order shipped to. Please select one of the available countries: Australia, Canada, Germany, United States.
* **Product Preferences**: This field is for any special requests or instructions related to the product ordered. Please describe your preferences in detail.
* **Payment Details**: This field is for any information related to the payment method and amount. Please enter your payment details as per your preferred payment method.
