# Maintenance Spray Lubricant Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for recording and managing orders for maintenance spray lubricants. It collects contact information, order details, and scheduling information for delivery and assignment of tasks or personnel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide contact information for the person placing the order.
2. Select the type of lubricant required.
3. Specify the quantity of lubricant needed.
4. Choose the delivery location.
5. Select the delivery date and time.
6. (Optional) Enter any additional delivery notes or instructions.
7. (Optional) Enter any additional order notes or instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Person** (Contact Person): Enter your name and title, so we can contact you about your order.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number): Enter your work phone number, so we can reach you about your order.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your work email, so we can communicate with you about your order.
* **Quantity Requested** (Quantity Requested): Enter the amount of lubricant you need to complete the task.
* **Lubricant Type** (Lubricant Type): Choose the type of lubricant you require, e.g., Oil, Grease, or Other.
* **Delivery Location** (Delivery Location): Choose the location where the lubricant will be delivered, e.g., Workshop, Workshop A, or Workshop B.
* **Delivery Date** (Delivery Date): Select the date you need the lubricant to be delivered by.
* **Delivery Time** (Delivery Time): Select the time you need the lubricant to be delivered by.
* **Delivery Note** (Delivery Note): Enter any additional delivery instructions or notes (optional).
* **Order Note** (Order Note): Enter any additional order instructions or notes (optional).
* (Optional) **Assigned To** (Assigned To): Enter the name of the person or personnel assigned to complete the task.
