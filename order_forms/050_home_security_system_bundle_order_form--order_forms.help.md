# Home Security System Bundle Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect customer details and schedule the installation of a home security system bundle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the fields as required and recommended.
2. Review your details carefully before submission.
3. Once you have completed the form, click the submit button to place your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Details (id: 1)
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, optional): This field is used to enter your name and contact information.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, text, optional): This field is for your signature, please print your name and date to confirm your order.

### System Details (id: 2)
* **System Details** (`system_details`, text, optional): This field is used to enter any specific details about the system you would like to order.
* **Schedule Installation** (`schedule_installation`, text, optional): This field is used to schedule the installation of the system.

### Payment Details (id: 4)
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, optional): This field is used to enter any payment details, such as the payment method and amount.

### Confirmation (id: 5)
* **Confirmation** (`confirmation`, note, optional): This field is for the customer to confirm their order.

### Email (id: 6)
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is used to enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is used to enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter all required fields correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
* Once you have completed the form, review your details carefully before submission.
* This form is for orders only, please do not use it for any other purpose.
