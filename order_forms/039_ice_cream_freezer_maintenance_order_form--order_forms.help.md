# ice_cream_freezer_maintenance_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to submit a maintenance request for the ice cream freezer. Please fill out the form to schedule maintenance and ensure your freezer is in good working condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of freezer you are using from the "Freezer Type" field.
2. If necessary, enter the equipment model number in the "equipment_model" field.
3. Describe the issue or maintenance request in the "Maintenance Request" field.
4. Choose a date and time for the appointment in the "Appointment" field.
5. Optionally, select a technician from the "Technician" field.
6. Enter any notes or comments about the freezer's condition in the "Equipment Condition" field.
7. Provide any additional comments in the "Comments" field.
8. Enter your email address and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Freezer Type** (`freezer_type`, select_one): Select the type of freezer you are using.
*  **Equipment Model** (`equipment_model`, text): Enter the equipment model number, if applicable.
*  **Maintenance Request** (`maintenance_request`, text): Describe the issue you are experiencing with the freezer.
*  **Appointment** (`appointment`, date): Choose a date and time for the maintenance appointment.
*  **Technician** (`technician`, select_one): Select a technician from the list provided.
*  **Equipment Condition** (`equipment_condition`, number): Enter the current condition of the freezer, on a scale of 1-10.
*  **Comments** (`comments`, text): Add any additional comments or notes about the freezer's condition.
*  **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address for maintenance updates.
*  **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number for maintenance updates.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required. If you have any issues with the freezer, please describe them in the "Maintenance Request" field.
