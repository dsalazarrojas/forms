# farmers_market_vendor_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **farmers_market_vendor_check_out_form** is used to document and verify sales during a farmer's market transaction. This form captures information about the vendor and customer, including their contact details and payment specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **Vendor Information** page with your own details.
2. Move on to the **Order Details** page to record the order specifics.
3. On the **Payments** page, enter the payment amount.
4. Sign your name in the **Vendor Signature** field.
5. Have the customer sign their name in the **Customer Signature** field.
6. Enter the date and time of the transaction in the **Date** and **Time** fields, respectively.
7. If applicable, enter the customer's **Customer Email** and **Customer Phone** details.
8. Finally, record the **Order Subtotal** and **Payment Amount** to ensure accurate accounting.
