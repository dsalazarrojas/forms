# Halal Food Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Halal Food Order Form is a tool used for customers to place orders for halal food products, providing essential information for order processing and customer communication. This form helps to streamline the ordering process and ensures that all necessary details are captured for a smooth order fulfillment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Indicate if you prefer halal food by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Do you prefer Halal?" field.
5. If you have any special requests, type them in the "Special Requests?" field.
6. In the "Order Details" field, describe the specific halal food products you would like to order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Halal Food Order Form** (`form_info`, text, required): This field is the title of the form, which is displayed at the top of the page. Please leave this field blank for now.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): This field is for you to enter your name. Please type your name as you would like it to appear on your order confirmation.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address so we can communicate with you regarding your order.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Provide your phone number so we can contact you if needed for further order details.
* **Do you prefer Halal?** (`halal_preferred`, select_one, required): Please select "Yes" if you prefer halal food or "No" if not.
* **Special Requests?** (`special_requests`, text, optional): If you have any special requests or instructions for your order, type them here.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required): Describe the specific halal food products you would like to order. Please be as detailed as possible to ensure accurate order fulfillment.
