# Custom Painting Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Painting Order Form is designed to collect information from customers about their preferred painting order. This form is typically used in a business context to capture essential details required to fulfill a custom order. The form will collect basic contact information and details about the painting, such as type, colors, size, and quantity.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on the "Start" button to begin filling out the form.
2.  Enter your **First Name** in the text field. This is the first name of the person ordering the painting.
3.  Enter your **Last Name** in the text field. This is the last name of the person ordering the painting.
4.  Enter your **Email Address** in the email field. This is the email address associated with the order.
5.  Enter your **Phone Number** in the text field. This is the phone number associated with the order.
6.  Select the **Painting Type** from the available options. This is the type of painting you would like to order.
7.  Enter the **Subject** of the painting in the text field. This is a brief description of the painting.
8.  Enter the **Artist** of the painting in the text field. This is the name of the artist that created or will create the painting.
9.  Enter the **Colors** of the painting in the text field. This is the primary color of the painting.
10.  Enter the **Size** of the painting in the text field. This is the desired size of the finished painting.
11.  Enter the **Quantity** of paintings you would like to order in the number field. This is how many paintings you would like to order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name**: A text field for the first name of the person ordering the painting.
*   **Last Name**: A text field for the last name of the person ordering the painting.
*   **Email Address**: An email field for the email address associated with the order.
*   **Phone Number**: A text field for the phone number associated with the order.
*   **Painting Type**: A text field for the type of painting you would like to order.
*   **Subject**: A text field for a brief description of the painting.
*   **Artist**: A text field for the name of the artist that created or will create the painting.
*   **Colors**: A text field for the primary color of the painting.
*   **Size**: A text field for the desired size of the finished painting.
*   **Quantity**: A number field for the quantity of paintings you would like to order.
 
## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your order is processed correctly.
*   Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure it is accurate.
*   If you have any issues or questions, contact your customer support team for assistance.
