# Website Design Gift Card Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Website Design Gift Card Claim Form is a simple form designed to collect information from clients about their website design project to facilitate the gift card claim process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the required information in the corresponding fields.
2. Make sure to fill out all the fields that are marked as "required".
3. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gift Card Code** (`gift_card_code`): Enter the gift card code provided to you.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address where you would like to receive the gift card.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Project Goal** (`project_goal`): Describe your project goal for which you are claiming the gift card.
* **Deadline** (`deadline`): Enter the deadline for your project, if applicable.
* **Priority** (`priority`): Select the priority level of your project (High, Medium, or Low).

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to avoid incomplete submissions.
* If you have any issues with the form or need help, contact us at form@gic.mx.
* We will review your form submission and follow up with you regarding your gift card claim.
