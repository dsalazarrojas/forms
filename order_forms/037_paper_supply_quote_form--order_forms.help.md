# Paper Supply Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Paper Supply Quote Form is a tool used to generate quotes for paper supplies based on the customer's requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your first name and last name in the first name and last name fields.
2. Enter your business name and email address in the business name and email fields.
3. If you have a business phone number, enter it in the business phone field.
4. Select the type of paper you need from the paper type dropdown menu.
5. Enter the quantity of paper required.
6. Choose the color and size of the paper from the dropdown menus.
7. Select the orientation of the paper (portrait or landscape).
8. Choose the finish type of the paper (glossy, matte, or other).
9. Enter any additional notes or special requests.
10. Select the date you would like the quote to be valid from the quote date field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Business name** (`business_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your business name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Business phone** (`business_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your business phone number.
* **Paper type** (`paper_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of paper you need (e.g., A4, Letter, Legal, etc.).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required/optional): Enter the quantity of paper required.
* **Color** (`color`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the color of the paper you need (e.g., Black, White, Color, etc.).
* **Size** (`size`, number, required/optional): Enter the size of the paper required.
* **Orientation** (`orientation`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the orientation of the paper (e.g., Portrait, Landscape, etc.).
* **Finish** (`finish`, select_one, required/optional): Select the finish type of the paper (e.g., Gloss, Matte, etc.).
* **Paper supply quote** (`paper_supply_quote`, text, required/optional): Enter any special requirements or instructions.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or special requests.
* **Quote Date** (`quote_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date the quote is valid until.
* **Required** (`required`, select_one, required): Select whether the quote is required or not.

**Tips**

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to generate a correct quote.
* For select fields, choose the options that best match your needs.
* If you have any special requirements or requests, enter them in the additional notes field. 
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the sales representative for assistance.
