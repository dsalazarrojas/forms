# Fitness Center Apparel Reorder Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Center Apparel Reorder Form is used to track and manage reorders of apparel for employees or customers in a fitness center or gym setting. This form collects various pieces of information to help administrators or managers keep track of reorder requests and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **Email** field. This should be a valid email address.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. Select the **Order Date** for the reorder.
6. Select the **Order Time** for the reorder.
7. Choose **Yes** or **No** for **Apparel Size**, **Apparel Type**, **Reorder Type**, and **Reorder Status**.
8. Enter a date for the **Reorder Date**.
9. Enter details about the order in the **Order Details** field.
10. Enter the total value of the order in the **Order Total** field.
11. Enter the name of the gym or studio where the order was placed.
12. Enter the name of the studio or department where the order was placed.
13. Enter the name of the employee who placed the order.
14. Choose **Yes** or **No** for **Apparel Category** and **Employee Status**.
15. Enter the email address of the employee who placed the order.
16. Enter the phone number of the employee who placed the order.
17. Enter any additional notes about the order in the **Order Notes** field.
18. Enter the location of the gym or studio where the order was placed.
19. Enter any additional details about the reorder in the **Reorder Details** field.
