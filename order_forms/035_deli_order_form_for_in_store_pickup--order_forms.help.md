# Deli Order Form For In Store Pickup - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Deli Order Form For In Store Pickup is used to collect information from customers for in-store pickup orders. This form helps us understand the customer's order preferences, pickup details, and any additional comments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form carefully and accurately with the required information.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for your order.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Customer Information (Page 1)
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required): Please enter your name, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.

### Order Information (Page 1)
* **Order Information** (`order_info`, text, required): Describe your order, including the type and quantity of items you want to pick up.

### Beverage (Page 1)
* **Beverage** (`beverage`, select_one, optional): Do you want to add a beverage to your order? If yes, select one of the options.

### Additional Information (Page 1)
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, note, optional): If you have any special requests or comments, you can write them here.

### Confirmation (Page 1)
* **Confirmation** (`confirmation`, select_one, optional): Are you confirming your order details? Select yes if you're sure.

### Pickup Information (Page 2)
* **Pickup Information** (`pickup_info`, text, required): Provide your store pickup details, including the date and time of your pickup.

### Payment Information (Page 2)
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, optional): If you're paying cash, just let us know. If you have other payment methods, describe them here.

### Other Information (Page 2)
* **Other Information** (`other_info`, note, optional): Any other comments or information you'd like to share with us.

## Tips
* Please make sure to check your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask.
* Thank you for using our deli order form!
