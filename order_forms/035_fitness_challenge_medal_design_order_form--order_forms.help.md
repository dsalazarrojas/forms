# Fitness Challenge Medal Design Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for creating custom fitness challenge medals. It is typically used by participants who want to customize their medal according to their preference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the participant's information, including their design choice, ribbon color, medal shape, size, and ribbon shape.
2. Enter the event name and date.
3. Review the form to ensure that all required fields are filled and the information is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the participant, such as their name or nickname.
* **Select Participant Design** (`participant_design_choice`, select, optional): Choose the design you prefer from the list of options (Gold, Silver, Bronze).
* **Participant Ribbon Color** (`participant_ribbon_color`, select, optional): Choose the color of the ribbon from the list of options (Red, Blue, Green).
* **Participant Medal Shape** (`participant_medal_shape`, select, optional): Choose the shape of the medal from the list of options (Round, Square, Heart).
* **Participant Medal Size** (`participant_medal_size`, select, optional): Choose the size of the medal from the list of options (Small, Large).
* **Participant Ribbon Shape** (`participant_ribbon_shape`, select, optional): Choose the shape of the ribbon from the list of options (Rectangle, Circle).
* **Participant Ribbon Width** (`participant_ribbon_width`, select, optional): Choose the width of the ribbon from the list of options (1, 2, 3).
* **Participant Medal Quantity** (`participant_medal_quantity`, number, optional): If you want to order multiple medals, enter the quantity here.
* **Event Information** (`event_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you are ordering multiple medals, enter the quantity in the Participant Medal Quantity field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizer.
