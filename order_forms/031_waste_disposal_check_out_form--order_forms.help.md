# waste_disposal_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The waste_disposal_check_out_form is designed to collect information for waste disposal purposes. It will be used to gather data on orders, customer details, and waste types to efficiently track and manage waste disposal orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the order date.
2. Select the type of order from the available options.
3. Enter the customer's name.
4. Choose the type of waste (select multiple options if applicable).
5. Enter the weight of the waste.
6. Select the collection location.
7. Provide your contact email.
8. Enter your contact phone number.
9. Click on the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Date (1, Date)**: Enter the date of the order.
* **Order Type (2, Select One)**: Select the type of order from the options provided. 
* **Customer Name (3, Text)**: Enter the customer's name.
* **Waste Type (4, Select Multiple)**: Choose the type(s) of waste being disposed. 
* **Waste Weight (5, Number)**: Enter the weight of the waste. 
* **Collection Location (6, Select One)**: Select the location where the waste will be collected.
* **Contact Email (7, Email)**: Enter your contact email.
* **Contact Phone (8, Text)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Submit (9, Note)**: Click on this button to complete and submit the form.
