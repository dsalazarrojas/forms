# Corporate Gift Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Gift Delivery Form is a tool to collect information for corporate gift delivery purposes. This form will gather essential details to ensure the gift is delivered correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your contact information, which includes your name, and any other relevant details.
2. Specify your preferred delivery method and any specific delivery instructions.
3. Enter the gift details, including the gift name, description, and any additional information.
4. Enter the recipient's details, including their name, address, and any other relevant information.
5. Enter the delivery address and email of the person who will receive the gift.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Contact Information** (`name`, `text`, optional)
This field is where you enter your name and any other relevant contact details.
*   **Name**: Enter your name as you would like to be referred to in the gift delivery process.
*   **[Other details]**: If you have any other contact information, such as a phone number or office address, you can include it here.

### **Delivery Preferences** (`delivery_preferences`, `text`, optional)
This field is where you specify your preferred delivery method and any special instructions.
*   **Delivery Preferences**: Describe your preferred delivery method, such as "hand delivery" or "email."

### **Gift Details** (`delivery_details`, `text`, optional)
This field is where you enter details about the gift you are sending.
*   **Gift Details**: Describe the gift you are sending, including its name and description.

### **Recipient Details** (`recipient_details`, `text`, optional)
This field is where you enter the recipient's contact information.
*   **Recipient Details**: Enter the recipient's name, address, and any other relevant details.

### **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, optional)
This field is where you enter the address where the gift should be delivered.
*   **Delivery Address**: Enter the address where the gift should be delivered to the recipient.

### **Delivery Email** (`delivery_email`, `text`, optional)
This field is where you enter the email address of the person who will receive the gift.
*   **Delivery Email**: Enter the email address of the person who will receive the gift.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure the gift is delivered correctly.
*   If you have any special instructions or requests, include them in the **Delivery Preferences** field.
*   Double-check your contact and delivery information for accuracy.
