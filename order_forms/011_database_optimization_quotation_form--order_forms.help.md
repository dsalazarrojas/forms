# Database Optimization Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients about their database optimization needs, which will help the team create an accurate and timely quotation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the provided information about the client and their database.
2. Provide the client's name and email address in the "Client Information" section.
3. Enter the client's phone number in the "Client Phone" section.
4. Specify the type of service required in the "Service Level" section (Low, Medium, or High).
5. Select any additional services needed from the list provided in the "Additional Services" section (Backup, Cloud, or Database).
6. Provide a detailed description of the client's database in the "Database Information" section.
7. Specify the size of the database in the "Database Size" section.
8. Specify the name of the service the client is looking for in the "Service Name" section.
9. Provide a detailed description of the quotation request in the "Quotation Request" section.
10. Choose the date for the quotation in the "Quote Date" section.
11. Choose the time for the quotation in the "Quote Time" section.
12. Add any additional notes or comments regarding the client's requirements in the "Note" section.
13. Specify the tool assigned to the client in the "Assigned Tool" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (text, required/optional): This field collects general information about the client.
* **Client Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the client's name.
* **Client Email** (email, required/optional): Enter the client's email address.
* **Client Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Database Information** (text, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of the client's database.
* **Database Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the client's database.
* **Database Size** (number, required/optional): Specify the size of the client's database.
* **Service Information** (text, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of the service the client is looking for.
* **Service Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the service the client is looking for.
* **Service Level** (select_one, required/optional): Choose the level of service required (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Additional Services** (select_multiple, required/optional): Select any additional services needed (Backup, Cloud, or Database).
* **Quotation Request** (text, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of the quotation request.
* **Quote Date** (date, required/optional): Choose the date for the quotation.
* **Quote Time** (time, required/optional): Choose the time for the quotation.
* **Note** (note, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments regarding the client's requirements.
* **Assigned Tool** (text, required/optional): Specify the tool assigned to the client.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Check the "Service Level" and "Additional Services" fields for any errors or inconsistencies.
* Double-check the "Quote Date" and "Quote Time" fields for correct dates and times.
* Use the "Note" field for any additional comments or instructions.
