# League Locker Room Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The League Locker Room Supply Order Form is used to order supplies for the league locker room. This form helps team management track and process orders for essential items such as water bottles, trash cans, and cleaning products.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select the "league_locker_room_supply_order_form_page_1" page and enter your name and any relevant information in the text fields. This is where you can provide any additional context or details about your order.
2.  Proceed to the "equipment_requests" page (page 3) and select "Yes" if you need to order equipment, or "No" if you do not. This will help the team management understand your needs.
3.  On the "delivery_schedule" page (page 4), choose "delivery" to arrange for the supplies to be delivered to your location, or "pickup" to collect the supplies from the management office.
4.  On the "track_orders" page (page 5), you can add any relevant comments or notes about your order.
5.  Finally, on the "email" page (page 6), enter your email address to ensure the team management contacts you regarding your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (Page 1, text, optional): Enter your name, and any relevant information about your order in this field.
- **Equipment Requests** (Page 3, select one, optional): Select "Yes" if you need to order equipment, or "No" if you do not.
- **Delivery Schedule** (Page 4, select one, optional): Choose "delivery" to arrange for the supplies to be delivered, or "pickup" to collect from the management office.
- **Notes** (Page 5, note, optional): Add any comments or notes about your order in this field.
- **Email** (Page 6, email, optional): Enter your email address so team management can contact you regarding your order.
