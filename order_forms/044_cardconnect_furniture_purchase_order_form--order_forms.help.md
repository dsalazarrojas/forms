# CardConnect Furniture Purchase Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The CardConnect Furniture Purchase Order Form is a critical document that allows customers to place orders for furniture items. This form helps us gather necessary information for processing the order correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please ensure you have the following information ready before completing this form:**
	* Customer information (name, email, phone number)
	* Billing address (city, state, zip code)
	* Order details (product descriptions, quantities, and unit prices)
	* Payment method (credit card, debit card, bank transfer, check, or finance plan)
2. **Fill out the form as follows:**
	* **Customer Information:**
		+ Enter your name, email address, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
	* **Order Details:**
		+ Enter the product description, quantity, and unit price for each product being ordered.
		+ Ensure you fill out only the fields for the products being ordered, skipping fields for products not being ordered.
	* **Payment Method:**
		+ Select the desired payment method from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer name:** Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email address:** Enter your email address associated with your order.
* **Phone number:** Enter your phone number where you can be reached for order updates.
* **Company name (if applicable):** If you are representing a company, enter the company name.
* **Billing address:** Enter the address where you want the order delivered or where the payment will be processed.
* **City:** Enter the city for the billing address.
* **State and zip code:** Enter the state and zip code for the billing address.
* **Delivery address (if different):** Enter a different delivery address if the order needs to be delivered to a different location.
* **Order date:** Enter the date for the order.
* **Purchase order number:** If provided, enter the unique purchase order number.
* **Furniture category:** Select the category of the furniture ordered (e.g., Seating, Bedroom, Office, etc.).
* **Product 1 description:** Enter a detailed description of the first product being ordered (style, model, color, etc.).
* **Product 1 quantity:** Enter the number of the first product being ordered.
* **Product 1 unit price:** Enter the price per unit of the first product.
* **Product 2 description:** Enter a detailed description of the second product being ordered (style, model, color, etc.). 
* **Product 2 quantity:** Enter the number of the second product being ordered.
* **Product 2 unit price:** Enter the price per unit of the second product.
* **Product 3 description:** Enter a detailed description of the third product being ordered (style, model, color, etc.). 
* **Product 3 quantity:** Enter the number of the third product being ordered.
* **Product 3 unit price:** Enter the price per unit of the third product.
* **Subtotal:** The total cost of all products before tax.
* **Tax amount:** The amount of tax applied to the order.
* **Total order amount:** The total cost of the order, including tax.
* **Payment method:** Select the method of payment for the order (credit card, debit card, bank transfer, check, or finance plan).

Please note that all fields marked as required must be filled out for the form to be processed correctly. If you are unsure about any field, please contact our customer service team for assistance.
