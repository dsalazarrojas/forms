# Construction Safety Signage Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to order safety signage for construction projects. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure your safety signage needs are met.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select the type of safety signage needed from the options provided.
3. Enter the contact information for order placement and delivery coordination.
4. Specify the quantity of signs required.
5. Choose the material preference for the signs.
6. Enter the delivery address where the signs should be delivered.
7. If necessary, select the required by date for the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester name** (Requester name, text, required): Enter the name of the person placing the order.
* **Contact phone** (Contact phone, text, optional): Enter a contact phone number for delivery coordination.
* **Signage type** (Signage type, select_one, required): Select the type of safety signage needed. Options are:
	+ Danger
	+ Warning
	+ Mandatory
	+ Informational
* **Quantity** (Quantity, number, required): Enter the number of signs required for the order.
* **Material** (Material, select_one, optional): Choose the material preference for the signs. Options are:
	+ Corrugated plastic
	+ Aluminum
	+ Vinyl
* **Delivery address** (Delivery address, text, required): Enter the delivery address where the signs should be delivered.
* **Required by date** (Required by date, date, optional): Enter the date by which the signs are needed.
