# Food Innovation Lab Equipment Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Innovation Lab Equipment Order Form is used to gather essential information for the order, delivery, and status management of lab equipment. It is designed to facilitate the efficient processing of orders and ensure that all necessary details are provided.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information for the order.
2. Ensure that all fields are accurate and complete.
3. Review the form for any additional comments or notes before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Form** (`order_form`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the order, if necessary.
* **Equipment Details** (`equipment_details`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the equipment being ordered, including its intended use and any special requirements.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, email address, and/or phone number for our team to contact you regarding your order.
* **Delivery Scheduling** (`delivery_scheduling`, text, optional): Specify the preferred delivery date and any specific delivery instructions.
* **Approvals** (`approvals`, text, optional): If necessary, provide a reference to the approval process or any relevant documentation related to the order.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, text, optional): This field is not currently used in the form but is a placeholder for future development.
* **Attachments** (`attachments`, text, optional): Upload any relevant supporting documentation or files related to the order.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes regarding the order, such as special requests or concerns.
