# Fresh Produce Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers about their fresh produce delivery order, ensuring accurate and efficient processing of their request.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer information, including your name, phone numbers, and email.
2. Provide your delivery information, such as your address and preferred delivery time.
3. Enter details about your order, including the total value and any specific instructions or notes.
4. Select your preferred payment method and any additional information about your order.
5. Review and confirm your order details before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (Customer Name, text, required: false):
  The field to enter your name, which will be used to identify you as the customer.
* **Customer E-mail** (Customer E-mail, email, required: false):
  Your email address for communication and order updates.
* **Customer Phone** (Customer Phone, text, required: false):
  Your contact phone number, in case we need to reach you.
* **Customer Address** (Customer Address, text, required: false):
  Your address for delivery purposes.
* **Delivery Information** (Delivery Address, text, required: false):
  The address where you'd like to receive your delivery.
* **Delivery Time** (Delivery Time, time, required: false):
  The preferred time slot for your delivery.
* **Delivery Date** (Delivery Date, date, required: false):
  The date on which you'd like to receive your delivery.
* **Order Total** (Order Total, number, required: false):
  The total value of your order.
* **Order Notes** (Order Notes, text, required: false):
  Any specific notes or requests you have about your order.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_one, required: false):
  Choose from Cash, Credit, or PayPal as your payment method.
* **Delivery Status** (Delivery Status, select_one, required: false):
  Track the status of your delivery (Scheduled, In Progress, Delivered, Cancelled).
* **Customer Name** (Customer Name, text, required: false):
  Enter your full name for reference.
* **Customer Phone 2** (Customer Phone 2, text, required: false):
  Additional phone number for contact purposes.
* **Customer Email 2** (Customer Email 2, email, required: false):
  Your secondary email address for communication.
* **Order Type** (Order Type, select_multiple, required: false):
  Select from Single Pick Up, Subscription Order, One Time Order, or Multiple Orders.
* **Order Frequency** (Order Frequency, select_multiple, required: false):
  Choose from One Time, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly.
* **Payment Method 2** (Payment Method 2, select_one, required: false):
  Select another payment method, similar to the one above.
* **Customer Phone 3** (Customer Phone 3, text, required: false):
  Yet another phone number for contact.
* **Delivery Method** (Delivery Method, select_one, required: false):
  Choose from Pickup, Delivery, or In Store Pickup.
* **Delivery Status 2** (Delivery Status 2, select_one, required: false):
  Similar to the delivery status above, track your delivery.
* **Notes** (Notes, text, required: false):
  Additional comments or reminders for your order.

Tips: 

* Double-check your information for accuracy before submission.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact the relevant team member.
* This form is meant to be used only for customer orders, so kindly refrain from using it for other purposes.
