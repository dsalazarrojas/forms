# Wine Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect order details for a wine order. It is designed to gather information about the customer, their contact details, and order specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the field labeled "Last Name".
3. Provide your **Email** address in the field "Email".
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the field "Phone".
5. Enter your **Address** details in the fields "Address 1" and "Address 2".
6. Input your **Postal Code** in the field "Postal Code".
7. Select the type of wine you would like to order from the options "Red", "White", and "Rosé" in the field "Wine Type".
8. Specify the number of wines you would like to order in the field "Wine Count".
9. Choose a date for your order in the field "Order Date".
10. Select a time for your order in the field "Order Time".
11. Add any additional **Note** or comments about your order in the field "Note".
12. If applicable, assign your order to either "chatjimmy", "support", or "customer service" in the field "Assigned To".
13. If applicable, select a tool for your order from the options "chatjimmy", "sales", or "support" in the field "Assigned Tool".
14. Finally, add any **Order Comments** in the field labeled "Order Comments".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Address 1** (text, required: false): Enter your address line 1.
* **Address 2** (text, required: false): Enter your address line 2.
* **Postal Code** (text, required: false): Enter your postal code.
* **Wine Type** (select_multiple, required: false): Select the type of wine you would like to order ("Red", "White", "Rosé").
* **Wine Count** (number, required: false): Specify the number of wines you would like to order.
* **Order Date** (date, required: false): Choose a date for your order.
* **Order Time** (time, required: false): Select a time for your order.
* **Note** (note, required: false): Add any additional note or comments about your order.
* **Assigned To** (select_one, required: false): If applicable, assign your order to either "chatjimmy", "support", or "customer service".
* **Assigned Tool** (select_one, required: false): If applicable, select a tool for your order from "chatjimmy", "sales", or "support".
* **Order Comments** (text, required: false): Add any additional comments about your order.
