# Shop My Stash Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Shop My Stash Form" is designed to collect information from users for inventory management and customer interaction purposes. This form can be used in a variety of contexts, such as customer inquiries, orders, or other shop-related interactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the form, which will be displayed as the header of the form.
2. Choose a category for the form, such as "Order Forms" or "Other Forms".
3. Provide a brief description of the form, which will help users understand the purpose of the form.
4. Select any relevant items from the current inventory for the form.
5. Enter the customer's phone number and email address.
6. Add any additional notes about the customer.
7. The form title (Form ID) will be automatically generated based on the input data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (text): Enter a brief title for the form that will be displayed to the user.
* **Category** (select one): Choose a category for the form, such as "Order Forms" or "Other Forms".
* **Description** (text): Provide a brief description of the form to help users understand its purpose.
* **Current Inventory** (select multiple): Select any relevant items from the current inventory for the form.
* **Customer Phone** (text): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Email** (email): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Note** (note): Add any additional notes about the customer.
* **Form ID** (number): The form title will be automatically generated based on the input data. 

## Tips
* Make sure to enter a clear and concise form title that accurately represents the purpose of the form.
* Select relevant items from the current inventory to ensure that the form is properly linked to the shop's inventory.
* Enter the customer's contact information accurately to facilitate smooth communication.
* Use the customer note field to add any additional information about the customer that may be relevant to the form's purpose.
