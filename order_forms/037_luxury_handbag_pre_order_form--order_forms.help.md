# Luxury Handbag Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to pre-order luxury handbags. It collects customer contact information, delivery details, deposit details, and order notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer details in the first field.
2. Specify your delivery details in the second field.
3. Enter your deposit details in the third field.
4. Add any order notes in the fourth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number to contact you.
* **Delivery Details**: Provide your delivery address, date, or any other details for when you would like to receive your handbag.
* **Deposit Details**: Enter any deposit or payment information you would like to provide.
* **Order Notes**: Add any additional comments or instructions about your order.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate contact information to ensure timely communication.
- Clearly specify your delivery details to avoid any confusion.
- Enter a valid deposit or payment information to complete your order.
