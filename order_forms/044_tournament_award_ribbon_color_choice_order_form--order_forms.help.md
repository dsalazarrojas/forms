# Tournament Award Ribbon Color Choice Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to order tournament award ribbons for your event. Please provide the necessary details to complete your order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the ribbon style you want for your award ribbons.
2. Choose the color of the ribbon you want to order.
3. Indicate the quantity of ribbons you need.
4. Select your preferred payment method.
5. Enter your order details, including event name, date, and time (if applicable).
6. Add any additional notes for your order.
7. Enter your school and contact details.
8. Confirm your order details and submit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Ribbon Style** (`tournament_ribbon_style`, select_one, required: false): 
Select the style of the award ribbon you want to order. Please choose from the available options, which include Red, Blue, and Green.

* **Ribbon Color** (`ribbon_color`, select_one, required: false): 
Choose the color of the award ribbon you want to order. Please select from the available options, which include Red, Blue, and Green.

* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required: false): 
Enter the number of ribbons you need to order.

* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required: false): 
Select your preferred payment method. You can choose from Cash, Card, or Check.

* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required: false): 
Enter the date you plan to make the payment. Please use the MM/DD/YY format.

* **Payment Time** (`payment_time`, time, required: false): 
Enter the time you plan to make the payment. This field is optional and will be used to confirm your payment schedule.

* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required: false): 
Enter the name of your school.

* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): 
Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.

* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): 
Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your order.

* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required: false): 
Enter the name of the event associated with your order.

* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): 
Enter the date of the event for which you're ordering the award ribbons.

* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required: false): 
Enter the time of the event for which you're ordering the award ribbons. This field is optional and will be used to confirm your event schedule.

* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): 
Add any additional notes or comments about your order here.

* **Tournament Name** (`tournament_name`, text, required: false): 
Enter the name of the tournament for which you're ordering the award ribbons.

* **League Name** (`league_name`, text, required: false): 
Enter the name of the league associated with your order.

* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, required: false): 
Enter the address of your school.

* **School City** (`school_city`, text, required: false): 
Enter the city of your school.

* **School State** (`school_state`, text, required: false): 
Enter the state of your school.

* **School Zip** (`school_zip`, text, required: false): 
Enter the zip code of your school.

* **Organizer Name** (`organizer_name`, text, required: false): 
Enter the name of the event organizer.

* **Organizer Email** (`organizer_email`, email, required: false): 
Enter the email address of the event organizer.

* **Organizer Phone** (`organizer_phone`, text, required: false): 
Enter the phone number of the event organizer.

* **Certificate Name** (`certificate_name`, text, required: false): 
Enter the name of the certificate or award associated with your order.

* **Event Type** (`event_type`, text, required: false): 
Enter the type of event for which you're ordering the award ribbons.

* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required: false): 
Choose the payment status of your order. You can select Paid or Unpaid.
