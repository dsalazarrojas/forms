# nail_stripes_invoice_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information related to a nail stripes invoice request. It's used for internal purposes, such as tracking and managing orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*) on the label.
2. Enter the customer's information, including name, address, and any other relevant details.
3. Enter the order information, such as stripe type, color, size, pattern, and width.
4. Enter the stripe quantity, price, and total price.
5. Select the order status and enter any additional order comments.
6. Enter any additional notes or comments regarding the customer or order.
7. Finally, click the submit button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Information
* **Customer Information** (`Customer Information`, text, required): Enter the customer's name and any other relevant details.

### Address
* **Address** (`Address`, text, required): Enter the customer's address.

### Order Info
* **Order Info** (`Order Info`, text, required): Enter the order information, including any relevant details.

### Stripe Info
* **Stripe Info** (`Stripe Info`, text, required): Enter any additional stripe information, such as fabric or material.

### Stripe Type
* **Stripe Type** (`Stripe Type`, select_one, required): Select the type of stripe, either "Nail" or "Other".

### Stripe Color
* **Stripe Color** (`Stripe Color`, select_multiple, required): Select one or multiple colors for the stripe.

### Stripe Size
* **Stripe Size** (`Stripe Size`, number, required): Enter the size of the stripe.

### Stripe Pattern
* **Stripe Pattern** (`Stripe Pattern`, select_one, required): Select the stripe pattern, either "Striped" or "Solid".

### Stripe Width
* **Stripe Width** (`Stripe Width`, number, required): Enter the width of the stripe.

### Stripe Length
* **Stripe Length** (`Stripe Length`, number, required): Enter the length of the stripe.

### Stripe Quantity
* **Stripe Quantity** (`Stripe Quantity`, number, required): Enter the quantity of stripes.

### Price
* **Price** (`Price`, number, required): Enter the price of the stripe.

### Total Price
* **Total Price** (`Total Price`, number, required): Enter the total price of the order.

### Request Date
* **Request Date** (`Request Date`, date, required): Enter the date of the request.

### Customer Note
* **Customer Note** (`Customer Note`, note, optional): Enter any additional customer notes or comments.

### Order Status
* **Order Status** (`Order Status`, select_one, required): Select the order status, either "New", "In Progress", or "Completed".

### Order Comments
* **Order Comments** (`Order Comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional order comments or notes.

### Stripe Note
* **Stripe Note** (`Stripe Note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments regarding the stripe.

Note: This help guide is a general guide and might need to be adjusted according to the specific use case or business logic. Always review the form carefully before filling it out to ensure it's filled out correctly.
