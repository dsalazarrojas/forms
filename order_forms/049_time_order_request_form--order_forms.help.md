# Time Order Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Time Order Request Form is a tool used to request time slots for various services. It helps our team manage their schedule and ensure that services are delivered at the right time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field. Please note that this field is required for us to contact you.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. In the "Message" field, describe the service you need and any additional information that might be helpful for our team.
5. Select the services you need from the "Service" field. Please choose all applicable services.
6. Enter the start date and start time of the requested time slot in the "Start Date" and "Start Time" fields, respectively.
7. Enter the end date and end time of the requested time slot in the "End Date" and "End Time" fields, respectively.
8. Specify the duration of the time slot in the "Duration" field.
9. If you have a preferred date and time for the service, enter it in the "Preferred Date" and "Preferred Time" fields.
10. Select the morning, afternoon, and evening slots you prefer from the "Morning", "Afternoon", and "Evening" fields, respectively. Please note that these fields are mutually exclusive.
11. Enter your name in the "Submitter" field.
12. Enter the date and time you're submitting the form in the "Submit Date" and "Submit Time" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Message** (`message`, text, required): Describe the service you need and any additional information.
* **Service** (`service`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable services.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the requested time slot.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the requested time slot.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the requested time slot.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of the requested time slot.
* **Duration** (`duration`, number, required): Specify the duration of the time slot.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, required): If you have a preferred date, enter it here.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, required): If you have a preferred time, enter it here.
* **Morning** (`morning`, select_multiple, required): Select the morning slot you prefer.
* **Afternoon** (`afternoon`, select_multiple, required): Select the afternoon slot you prefer.
* **Evening** (`evening`, select_multiple, required): Select the evening slot you prefer.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, required): Enter the date you're submitting the form.
* **Submit Time** (`submit_time`, time, required): Enter the time you're submitting the form.
