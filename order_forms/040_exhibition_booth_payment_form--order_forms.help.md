# Exhibition Booth Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect payment information from exhibitors for an exhibition, typically for booth fees, merchandise, or other services. It's likely used by event organizers or staff to manage payments efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To fill out the form, please start by providing your exhibitor details in the "Exhibitor Details" section.
2.  Next, enter your payment information in the "Payment Info" section.
3.  Select your preferred payment method(s) in the "Payment Method" section.
4.  Finally, click on the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Exhibitor Details
*   **Exhibitor Details** (`exhibitor_details`, text, optional): Please enter your name, company name, or any other relevant details about yourself or your organization. This information will help the event organizers understand who you are and what you're exhibiting.

### Payment Info
*   **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, optional): Enter your payment amount, any relevant invoice numbers, or other payment-related details. You can also mention any additional information that's essential to your payment, such as a payment reference or account number.

### Payment Method
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the way you'd like to pay (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Cash). Some exhibitors may have specific payment requirements or restrictions. Make sure to select an option that suits your situation.

### Submit
*   **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, optional): Once you've filled out the form, click the "Submit" button to send your information to the event organizers. This will help them process your payment efficiently.
