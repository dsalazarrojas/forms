# shipping_liability_waiver_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a liability waiver and consent form for shipping-related activities. It's used to collect information about the customer's shipping preferences and obtain their signature on certain documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Start by reviewing the form and its sections.
2.  Complete each field with the requested information.
3.  Be sure to provide your signature in the designated signature field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **shipping_liability_waiver_consent** (1) - text
*   This field is for providing a brief description of the shipping liability waiver and consent.

### **customer name** (2) - text
*   Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.

### **customer email** (3) - email
*   Enter your email address to facilitate communication and contact.

### **shipping method** (4) - select_one
*   Choose the shipping method you prefer among the available options: UPS, USPS, FedEx, Canada Post, or Other.

### **shipping date** (5) - date
*   Enter the date you would like to ship your package by.

### **signature_confirmation** (6) - note
*   This field is for confirming that you have read and understood the shipping liability waiver and consent.

### **customer signature** (7) - text
*   Sign your name in this field to acknowledge that you have read and agreed to the shipping liability waiver and consent terms.
