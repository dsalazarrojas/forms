# Eco Friendly Diaper Pre Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for customers to provide their pre-order details for eco-friendly diapers. It collects shipping and payment information to ensure smooth delivery and processing of the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your shipping address in the `Shipping Address` field.
2. Enter the city where you'd like to receive your order in the `City` field.
3. Select your state from the list in the `State` field.
4. Enter your zip code in the `Zip` field.
5. If you're a business or have a different shipping preference, select 'Yes' in the `State` and `Country` fields.
6. Select your preferred payment method from the list in the `Payment Method` field.
7. Choose a delivery date in the `Delivery Date` field.
8. Enter a specific time for delivery in the `Delivery Time` field (if applicable).
9. Add any delivery notes in the `Delivery Note` field (if applicable).
10. Provide any pre-order note in the `Pre-Order Note` field.
11. Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
12. Add your phone number in the `Phone` field.
13. Fill in your name in the `Name` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pre Order** (`pre_order`, text, Optional): Enter your pre-order details in this field.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, Optional): Enter the physical address where you'd like to receive your order.
* **City** (`shipping_city`, text, Optional): Enter the city where you'd like to receive your order.
* **State** (`shipping_state`, select_one, Optional): Select your state from the list.
* **Zip** (`shipping_zip`, text, Optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Country** (`shipping_country`, select_one, Optional): Select your country from the list (not applicable for domestic orders).
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, Optional): Enter any additional payment information or details.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, Optional): Select your preferred payment method.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, Optional): Choose a delivery date.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, Optional): Select a specific time for delivery (if applicable).
* **Delivery Note** (`delivery_note`, text, Optional): Add any delivery notes or instructions.
* **Pre-Order Note** (`preorder_note`, text, Optional): Enter any pre-order notes or instructions.
* **Email** (`customer_email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Name** (`customer_name`, text, Optional): Fill in your name.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as Required.
