# team_coachs_toolkit_supply_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from team coaches for customized sports equipment and apparel orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as a team coach in the "Coach Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field, so we can contact you about your order.
3. Select the type of order you wish to place from the "order_type" field, which includes Team Equipment, Team Uniforms, Team Gear, or Team Apparel.
4. Enter your team's sport in the "sport" field (e.g., Soccer, Basketball, Football, etc.).
5. Specify the quantity of each item you need in the "quantity" field.
6. Enter the price of each item in the "price" field.
7. Add any additional notes about your order in the "notes" field.
8. Select your team's status from the "team" field.
9. Indicate if you are the team coach from the "team_coach_id" field.
10. Confirm if this is the main school contact from the "school" field.
11. Select your school's address in the "school_address" field.
12. Enter your school's city in the "school_city" field.
13. Enter your school's state in the "school_state" field.
14. Enter your school's zip code in the "school_zip" field.
15. Enter your school's country in the "school_country" field.
16. Enter your school's phone number in the "school_phone" field.
17. Enter your school's fax number in the "school_fax" field.
18. Enter your school's email address in the "school_email" field.
19. Enter your coach's phone number in the "coach_phone" field.
20. Enter your coach's fax number in the "coach_fax" field.
21. Enter your coach's email address in the "coach_email" field.
22. Enter your coach's address in the "team_coach_address" field.
23. Enter your coach's city in the "team_coach_city" field.
24. Enter your coach's state in the "team_coach_state" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a team coach.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of order you wish to place. Choose from Team Equipment, Team Uniforms, Team Gear, or Team Apparel.
* **Sport** (`sport`, text, required): Enter your team's sport (e.g., Soccer, Basketball, Football, etc.).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Specify the quantity of each item you need.
* **Price** (`price`, number, required): Enter the price of each item.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Add any additional notes about your order.
* **Team Coach ID** (`team_coach_id`, select_one, required): Select if you are the team coach.
* **Team** (`team`, select_one, required): Indicate if this is the main school contact.
* **School** (`school`, select_one, required): Confirm if you are the main school contact.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, required): Enter your school's address.
* **School City** (`school_city`, text, required): Enter your school's city.
* **School State** (`school_state`, text, required): Enter your school's state.
* **School Zip** (`school_zip`, text, required): Enter your school's zip code.
* **School Country** (`school_country`, text, required): Enter your school's country.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, required): Enter your school's phone number.
* **School Fax** (`school_fax`, text, required): Enter your school's fax number.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email, required): Enter your school's email address.
* **Coach Phone** (`coach_phone`, text, required): Enter your coach's phone number.
* **Coach Fax** (`coach_fax`, text, required): Enter your coach's fax number.
* **Coach Email** (`coach_email`, email, required): Enter your coach's email address.
* **Coach Address** (`team_coach_address`, text, required): Enter your coach's address.
* **Coach City** (`team_coach_city`, text, required): Enter your coach's city.
* **Coach State** (`team_coach_state`, text, required): Enter your coach's state.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately to avoid any delays in processing your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
