# Equipment Vibration Detector Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for customers to order the equipment vibration detector product or inquire about its availability.
## How To Complete This Form
1. **Equipment Vibration Detector Form** (`equipment_vibration_detector_form`): Enter a brief description of what you are looking for or inquiring about.
2. **Product Type** (`product_type`): Choose whether or not you are ordering a product (`Yes`/`No`).
3. **Model Number** (`model_number`): Enter the model number of the product you are inquiring about.
4. **Serial Number** (`serial_number`): Enter the serial number of the product.
5. **Description** (`description`): Enter a detailed description of the product.
6. **Order Date** (`order_date`): If you are ordering, please select the date you want the order to be processed by.
7. **Quantity Ordered** (`quantity_ordered`): If you are ordering, enter the number of units you want to order.
8. **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`): Enter your shipping address.
9. **Email Address** (`email_address`): Enter your email address.
10. **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter your phone number.
11. **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`): Enter any additional comments about your order.
12. **Sales Person** (`sales_person`): Enter the sales person's name who handled your order.
13. **Department** (`department`): Enter the department you are inquiring or ordering from.
14. **Order Status** (`order_status`): Choose the status of the order (`Active`/`Inactive`).
15. **Order Notes** (`order_notes`): Enter any further comments or notes about the order.
16. **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`): Choose whether or not you want to ship the product (`Yes`/`No`).
17. **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`): Enter the type of equipment you are inquiring or ordering.
18. **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Enter your name.
19. **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter your contact number.
20. **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your contact email.
21. **Order Notes** (`order_notes`): Enter any further comments or notes about the order.
22. **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`): Enter additional comments from the customer.
23. **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`): Enter the date you want the order to be delivered by.
24. **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`): Enter the city where you want the product shipped to.
25. **Equipment Specifications** (`equipment_specifications`): Enter detailed specifications about the equipment being ordered.

## Tips
- Please fill in all required fields to ensure accurate order processing.
- Enter all fields clearly and accurately to avoid errors.
- If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to contact the sales person or department for assistance.
