# Robotics End Arm Tooling Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect order information for robotics end arm tooling products. It is intended for customers who wish to place an order for these specific products.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields, if any.
2. Select the relevant tooling products from the list.
3. Enter any additional comments or instructions in the "Submit Order" field.
4. Provide your customer information, including any relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Application Information** (`application_info`, text, optional): Enter your company or organization name, and any other relevant details about your application.
- **Tooling Products** (`tooling_products`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the tooling products you wish to order from the available options.
- **Submit Order** (`submit_order`, note, optional): Enter any comments or instructions about your order.
- **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Enter your customer name, address, phone number, and email.

## Tips
- Please make sure to enter accurate information to ensure correct order processing.
- If you need help or have questions, contact our support team.
- Make sure to review your order carefully before submission to avoid any errors.
