# Online Plant Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Plant Order Form is designed to collect user information and plant selection for a smooth and efficient order process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **User Details** in the first section.
2. Select your desired **Plant Selection** in the second section.
3. Provide your **Payment Details** in the third section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details** (`user_details`, text, required: false): Fill in your name, contact information, and any other relevant details to help us process your order.
* **Plant Selection** (`plant_selection`, text, required: false): Select the type and quantity of plant you would like to order. Please be specific with the plant name or type for accurate processing.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, required: false): Enter your payment method and relevant financial information to complete the order.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for a smooth order process.
* If you have any special requests or questions, contact [Contact: form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
