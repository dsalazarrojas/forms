# Beard Product Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to place an order for beard care products, such as beard oil, balm, wax, wash, comb, and brush. It collects customer information, order details, and payment method for the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name, last name, and email address in the "First Name", "Last Name", and "Email Address" fields respectively.
2. Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you for any updates or queries.
3. Provide your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field.
4. Select the city and state/province from the dropdown menus.
5. If you want to add any items to your order, fill in the quantity and select the scent for each item.
6. Choose the gift wrap option if you want to add a personal message to your order.
7. Enter the subtotal, shipping cost, and select your payment method.
8. Review the total amount and ensure it matches your calculations.
9. If you have any special instructions or messages for us, enter them in the "Special Instructions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name here.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter your contact email address here.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter your contact phone number here.
* **Shipping Address** (Text, Required): Provide your full shipping address here.
* **City** (Text, Required): Select your city from the dropdown menu.
* **State or Province** (Text, Required): Select your state or province from the dropdown menu.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (Text, Required): Enter your postal code here.
* **Beard Oil Quantity** (Number, Optional): Enter the number of beard oil bottles you want to order.
* **Beard Oil Scent** (Select One, Optional): Select the scent of the beard oil you prefer.
* **Beard Balm Quantity** (Number, Optional): Enter the number of beard balm tins you want to order.
* **Beard Balm Scent** (Select One, Optional): Select the scent of the beard balm you prefer.
* **Beard Wax Quantity** (Number, Optional): Enter the number of beard wax containers you want to order.
* **Beard Wash Quantity** (Number, Optional): Enter the number of beard wash bottles you want to order.
* **Beard Comb Quantity** (Number, Optional): Enter the number of combs you want to order.
* **Beard Brush Quantity** (Number, Optional): Enter the number of brushes you want to order.
* **Gift Message** (Text, Optional): Enter a personal message for the recipient.
* **Gift Wrap Option** (Select One, Required): Choose whether you want gift wrap for your order.
* **Subtotal** (Number, Optional): Enter the subtotal before tax and shipping.
* **Shipping Cost** (Number, Optional): Enter the shipping cost here.
* **Total Amount** (Number, Required): This will be calculated and displayed here.
* **Payment Method** (Select One, Required): Choose your payment method.
* **Special Instructions** (Text, Optional): Enter any special instructions or messages for us here.

Please review the form carefully and ensure you have filled in all required fields before submitting your order.
