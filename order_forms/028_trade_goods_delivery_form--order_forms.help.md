# trade_goods_delivery_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and document information about trade goods deliveries, helping to ensure accurate and efficient trade transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Delivery Date" field with the date the trade goods were delivered or scheduled to be delivered.
2. Enter the delivery time in the "Delivery Time" field, if applicable.
3. Provide the delivery address in the "Delivery Address" field.
4. Enter the city of delivery in the "Delivery City" field.
5. Specify the delivery country in the "Delivery Country" field.
6. Enter the ZIP/Postal Code in the "Delivery ZIP/Postal Code" field.
7. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Delivery State" field, if applicable.
8. Optionally, add any additional delivery notes in the "Delivery Note" field.
9. Enter the Trade ID, if available.
10. Enter the trade date in the "Trade Date" field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date): Enter the date the trade goods were delivered or are scheduled to be delivered.
- **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time): Enter the delivery time if applicable.
- **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text): Enter the delivery address where the trade goods were delivered or are scheduled to be delivered.
- **Delivery City** (`delivery_city`, text): Enter the city of the delivery address.
- **Delivery Country** (`delivery_country`, text): Enter the country where the trade goods were delivered or are scheduled to be delivered.
- **Delivery ZIP/Postal Code** (`delivery_zip`, number): Enter the ZIP/Postal Code of the delivery address.
- **Delivery State** (`delivery_state`, select_one): Select "Yes" if the trade goods delivery includes a state, otherwise select "No".
- **Delivery Note** (`delivery_note`, note): Add any additional delivery notes or comments.
- **Trade ID** (`trade_id`, text): Enter the Trade ID, if available.
- **Trade Date** (`trade_date`, date): Enter the trade date if necessary. 

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
- Double-check the delivery date and time to ensure they are correct.
- Use the "Delivery Note" field for any additional comments or notes about the delivery.
- If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the relevant team or supervisor for guidance.
