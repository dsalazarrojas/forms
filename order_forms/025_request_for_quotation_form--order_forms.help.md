# request_for_quotation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "request_for_quotation_form" form is used to request quotations for a specific supplier, product, or service. It captures essential information from the supplier, such as supplier details, product description, unit price, quantity, total price, delivery date and time, and contact information (email and phone). This form helps in streamlining the quotation process and ensuring that all required information is collected in a single request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the supplier's details in the "Supplier" field.
2. Enter a brief description of the item or service being requested in the "Item Description" field.
3. Enter the unit price of the item or service in the "Unit Price" field.
4. Specify the quantity of the item or service required in the "Quantity" field.
5. Calculate the total price by multiplying the unit price with the quantity in the "Total Price" field.
6. Select a delivery date in the "Delivery Date" field.
7. Choose a delivery time in the hour and minute format in the "Delivery Time" field.
8. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
9. Enter the supplier's email address in the "Email" field.
10. Enter the supplier's phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier (Supplier, text, required: false)**: Enter the supplier's name or company name.
* **Item Description (Item Description, text, required: false)**: A brief description of the item or service being requested.
* **Unit Price (Unit Price, number, required: false)**: The price of the item or service per unit.
* **Quantity (Quantity, number, required: false)**: The number of units of the item or service required.
* **Total Price (Total Price, number, required: false)**: Calculated by multiplying the unit price with the quantity.
* **Delivery Date (Delivery Date, date, required: false)**: The date when the item or service is expected to be delivered.
* **Delivery Time (Delivery Time, time, required: false)**: The time when the item or service is expected to be delivered.
* **Note (Note, note, required: false)**: Any additional comments or notes about the request.
* **Email (Email, email, required: false)**: The supplier's email address.
* **Phone (Phone, text, required: false)**: The supplier's phone number.
