# Home Renovation Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide information and make payments related to home renovations. Fill out this form to ensure your payment is processed correctly and to confirm your agreement with the terms of service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your name**: Fill in the "user_name" field with your full name as it appears on your identification.
2. **Describe your job**: In the "job-description" field, briefly explain the scope of the renovation work you're paying for.
3. **Enter total cost**: In the "total-cost" field, enter the total amount you're paying.
4. **Choose your payment method**: Select how you'd like to make the payment, such as "Cash", "Bank", or "Credit".
5. **Select payment due date**: Choose when you'd like to make the payment. If the date isn't set, it's usually best to select "None".
6. **Enter your contact information**: In the "email" and "contact-number" fields, provide your contact details so we can get in touch with you about your payment.
7. **Check the payment terms**: In the "payment-terms" field, read and agree to the terms of service and payment terms.
8. **Confirm agreement with payment terms**: In the "payment-terms-agreement" field, select "Accept" to confirm you've read and agreed to the terms.
9. **Add any additional comments**: In the "note" field, include any comments or messages about your payment.
10. **Review and agree with payment status**: In the "payment-status" field, select how your payment is progressing (e.g., "Paid", "Partial", "Unpaid").
11. **Enter your address**: In the "user-address" field, enter your home address for payment processing purposes.
12. **Add any additional message**: In the "user-message" field, include any additional comments or messages about your payment.
13. **Enter payment method hint**: In the "payment-method-hint" field, enter a hint about your preferred payment method.
14. **Check terms of service**: In the "payment-terms-agreement-checkbox" field, select "Accept" to confirm you've checked the terms of service.
15. **Add any additional address information**: In the "client-address" field, enter your address for payment processing purposes.
16. **Enter your name**: In the "client-name" field, enter your name for payment processing purposes.
17. **Select date for signature**: In the "client-date" field, select a date for your signature.
18. **Sign with checkbox**: In the "payment-terms-agreement-checkbox" field, select "Accept" to confirm your agreement with the terms of service.
19. **Enter your address again**: In the "client-address" field, enter your address for payment processing purposes.
20. **Add any additional agreement information**: In the "payment-terms-agreement-note" field, include any additional comments or messages about your agreement with the terms of service.
