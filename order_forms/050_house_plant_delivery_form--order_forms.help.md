# House Plant Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The House Plant Delivery Form is a tool for customers or their staff to collect and submit their order information for house plant delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the First name and Last name fields with your full name.
2. Enter a valid email address in the Email field.
3. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
4. Describe the order in the Order details field.
5. Specify the Total plants to be delivered.
6. Enter your address in the Delivery address field.
7. Select the delivery date and time in the Delivery date and Delivery time fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address in the format `john.doe@example.com`.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number in the format `123-456-7890`.
* **Order details** (`order_details`, text, optional): Describe the order in a brief sentence or a few words.
* **Total plants** (`total_plants`, number, optional): Enter the total number of plants to be delivered.
* **Delivery address** (`delivery_address`, text, optional): Enter your delivery address in the format of house number, street, city, and country.
* **Delivery date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): Select the date for delivery in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Delivery time** (`delivery_time`, time, optional): Select the time for delivery in 12-hour format like `HH:MM`.
