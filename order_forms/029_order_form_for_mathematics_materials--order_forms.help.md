# Order Form For Mathematics Materials - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Order Form For Mathematics Materials" is designed to collect information for orders, including payment details and shipping instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined below:

1.  Start by filling in the "Order Information" page.
2.  Move on to the "Payment" page and provide your payment details.
3.  Finally, fill in the "Shipping" page with your shipping preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Information** (`order_form_for_mathematics_materials`, text, required): This is the first page of the form where you can provide details about your order.
* **Payment** (`payment_information`, text, required): Here, you can specify your payment method and any other relevant details.
* **Shipping** (`order_form_for_mathematics_materials`, text, required): In this page, you can provide your shipping preferences, including any special instructions or requirements.

## Tips
