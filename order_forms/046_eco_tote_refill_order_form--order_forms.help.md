# Eco Tote Refill Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eco Tote Refill Order Form is used to place an order for refills of eco-friendly totes. Fill out this form to provide your details and select your preferred products for refills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer "Yes" or "No" to "Product Selections" regarding the products you want to refill.
2. Fill in your "Delivery Details" with any additional comments or special instructions.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" to "Customer Agreements" if you have previously agreed on our customer agreements.
4. Enter the "Product Name" of the eco-tote you want to refill.
5. Fill in the "Product Price" of the product you want to refill.
6. Enter your "Phone Number" and "Email Address" for contact information.
7. Select a "Delivery Date" when you want to receive your refilled totes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Selections** (yes/no, required: no): Answer "Yes" if you want to select the products for refills, otherwise answer "No". This helps us know if you have specific products that need to be refilled.
* **Delivery Details** (text, required: no): This is a free text field where you can provide any additional comments or special instructions for your delivery.
* **Customer Agreements** (yes/no, required: no): Answer "Yes" if you have previously agreed on our customer agreements, otherwise answer "No". This helps us ensure we only serve you through this form.
* **Product Name** (text, required: no): Enter the name of the eco-friendly tote you want to refill.
* **Product Price** (number, required: no): Enter the price of the product you want to refill.
* **Delivery Address** (text, required: no): Enter the address where you want to receive your refilled totes.
* **Delivery Date** (date, required: no): Select a date when you want to receive your refilled totes.
* **Phone Number** (text, required: no): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Email Address** (email, required: no): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
