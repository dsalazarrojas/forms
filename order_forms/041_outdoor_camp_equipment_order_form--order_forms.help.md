# outdoor_camp_equipment_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The outdoor camp equipment order form is used to collect customer information and order details for outdoor camp equipment. This form helps us understand what products the customer is interested in and what they need to know for delivery and communication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Please ensure you are in the correct context (this form) to avoid duplication of effort.
* Carefully review and fill in the required and optional fields according to the guidelines below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (id: 1)
	+ The field where you can provide your name, address, and other contact information.
* **What product do you want to order?** (id: 2)
	+ Select the type of outdoor camp equipment you are interested in (e.g., "Product 1" or "Product 2").
* **Quantity** (id: 3)
	+ Enter the number of products you would like to order.
* **Price** (id: 4)
	+ Please note that this field is not currently being used in the form. If necessary, you will be able to fill this in at a later stage.
* **Date** (id: 5)
	+ Select the date for delivery or use as a reference for the order.
* **Contact Info** (id: 6)
	+ Provide additional contact information if necessary.
* **Email** (id: 7)
	+ Enter your email address where you can be reached for delivery updates.
* **Phone** (id: 8)
	+ Enter your phone number where you can be reached for delivery updates.

## Tips
* If you have any questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
* Ensure to review your entries for accuracy before submission.
