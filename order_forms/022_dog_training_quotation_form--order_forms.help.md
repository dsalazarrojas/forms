# Dog Training Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide a quick and easy way for customers to request a quote for dog training services. Fill out this form to provide us with information about your dog and your desired training needs, and we will get back to you with a personalized quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  **Client Info**: Provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
2.  **Dog Info**: Tell us about your dog, including their name, breed, age, and any relevant details about their personality or needs.
3.  **Training Type**: Select the type of training your dog needs. You can choose from our available training options, or select "Other" if you have a specific request.
4.  **Training Days**: Enter the number of days you are interested in having your dog trained.
5.  **Training Time**: Choose a date for your dog's training session.
6.  **Service Info**: Provide any additional information you think we should know about your dog's training needs.
7.  **Service Option**: Select the level of service you are interested in. This will help us tailor our quote to your needs.
8.  **Quote Info**: Enter any specific instructions or details you want us to consider when generating your quote.
9.  **Quote Price**: We will fill this in automatically based on your selections. If you want us to consider a different price range, let us know in the quote info section.
10. **Payment Method**: Choose how you would like to pay for your dog's training services. We accept PayPal, Bank Transfer, and Cash payments.
11. **Email**: Enter your contact email address so we can send you our quote and any follow-up information.
12. **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
13. **Message**: Enter any additional comments or questions you may have.
14. **Note**: Leave any additional notes or comments about your dog's training needs.
