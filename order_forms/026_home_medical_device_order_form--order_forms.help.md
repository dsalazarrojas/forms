# Home Medical Device Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to create an order for home medical devices. It helps healthcare providers and patients provide information about the patient's medical device needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Patient Info**:
	* Enter the patient's personal details in this section.
2. **Device Info**:
	* Select the medical device you want to order from the provided options.
3. **Order Date and Time**:
	* Enter the date and time the order was made.
4. **Status**:
	* Choose the current status of the order (Ordered, Cancelled, Delivered, or Pending).
5. **Comments**:
	* Enter any additional comments or notes related to the order.
6. **Patient Contact Info**:
	* Enter the patient's email address, phone number, and address.
7. **Healthcare Provider Info**:
	* Enter any notes from the healthcare provider.
8. **Signatures**:
	* Enter the patient and healthcare provider signatures.
9. **Order Notes**:
	* Enter any additional notes related to the order.
10. **Total**:
	* Enter the total cost of the medical device.
11. **Status Comments**:
	* Enter any additional comments related to the order status.
12. **Date and Time**:
	* Enter the date and time the order was received.
