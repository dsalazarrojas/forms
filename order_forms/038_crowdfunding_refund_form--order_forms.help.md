# Crowdfunding Refund Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a refund for a crowdfunded project. Please fill out this form if you believe you are eligible for a refund.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Backer Information**: This section is for you to enter your name and any other identifying information about yourself, such as your backer name.
2. **Request Date**: Enter the date you are requesting the refund. This should be the date you are filling out this form.
3. **Order Number**: Enter the order number associated with your purchase. If you're not sure what this is, you can try to look it up on your receipt or order confirmation email.
4. **Refund Amount**: Enter the amount you are requesting as a refund. Please ensure this amount is correct and matches your original purchase.
5. **Reason for Refund**: Briefly explain the reason why you are requesting a refund. This could be something like "Project not completed as expected" or "Issue with reward".
6. **Backer Name**: Enter your backer name, if different from your contact name.
7. **Contact Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted for further communication about your refund.
8. **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for further communication about your refund.
