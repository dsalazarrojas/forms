# Factory Automation Chipset Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Automation Chipset Order Form is designed to collect information about orders for a factory automation chipset. This form is intended for external customers who will be interacting with the company's services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Company Details" page to input your company information.
2. Click on the "Shipping Address" page to input your shipping address.
3. Click on the "Chipset Selection" page to select the chipsets you would like to order.
4. Click on the "Quantity" page to input the quantity of each selected chipset.
5. Click on the "Order Notes" page to input any additional notes about your order.
6. Click on the "Submit" button to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Details** (`company_details`, `text`, Optional)
	+ This field is used to input your company's name and contact information. Please enter your company's name and any relevant contact information.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, Optional)
	+ This field is used to input your shipping address. Please enter your complete shipping address, including street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Chipset Selection** (`chipset_selection`, `select_multiple`, Optional)
	+ This field allows you to select the chipsets you would like to order. Please choose one or more options from the list.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, Optional)
	+ This field is used to input the quantity of each selected chipset. Please enter the quantity for each selected chipset.
* **Chipset Quantity** (`chipset_quantity`, `number`, Optional)
	+ This field is used to input the total quantity of all selected chipsets. Please enter the total quantity.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, `note`, Optional)
	+ This field is used to input any additional notes about your order. Please enter any relevant information about your order.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `submit`, Required)
	+ This field is used to complete your order. Please click the button to submit your order.
