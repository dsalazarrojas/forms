# Academic Journal Subscription Quote Request - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from institutions, organizations, or individuals interested in obtaining a quote for subscription to our academic journal. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your name and contact information in the "Requester name", "Institution name", and "Contact email" fields.
2. Select the type of subscription you are interested in (Individual, Institutional, Consortium, Online only, or Print and online).
3. Estimate the number of users that will be accessing the journal, if applicable.
4. List the desired journals or subject areas you are interested in.
5. Specify your expected budget range for the subscription.
6. Enter the contact information for the billing contact.
7. Provide any additional requirements or special licensing or access needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester name**: This field is required for us to contact you with further information or clarification. Please enter your name.
* **Institution name**: This field is required and should be the name of your institution or organization.
* **Contact email**: This field is required and should be the email address that you want to use for quote delivery and communication.
* **Subscription type**: This field is required and allows you to select the type of subscription you are interested in (Individual, Institutional, Consortium, Online only, or Print and online).
* **Estimated number of users**: If you are requesting a subscription for an institution or consortium, please provide an estimated number of users that will be accessing the journal.
* **Desired journals or packages**: List the specific journals or subject areas you are interested in.
* **Budget range**: This field is optional, but please provide an estimated budget range for the subscription.
* **Billing contact details**: Enter the name and contact information for the billing contact.
* **Additional requirements**: This field is optional, but please provide any special licensing or access needs you may have.
