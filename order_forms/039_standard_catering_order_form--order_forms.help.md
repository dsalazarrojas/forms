# Standard Catering Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is intended for customers to provide their information for a standard catering order. The form collects essential details to ensure accurate order processing and timely communication.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form carefully, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Select the desired catering service(s) from the available options.
3. Choose the type of food the customer would like to order.
4. Specify the delivery date and time.
5. Enter the number of items to be ordered.
6. Provide contact information for the customer, including a phone number and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer's full name as they would like to be addressed.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the specific order the customer would like to place, including any special requests or instructions.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date on which the customer would like to receive their order.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, `text`, required/optional): Choose the specific time of day for delivery (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening).
* **Catering Service** (`catering_service`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select any additional catering services the customer would like to add to their order (e.g., Baked Goods, Finger Foods, Desserts).
* **Food Type** (`food_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of food the customer would like to order (e.g., Baked Goods, Finger Foods, Desserts).
* **Order Quantity** (`order_quantity`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of items the customer would like to order.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer's phone number for easy communication.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the customer's email address for further communication and updates.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Select the correct catering service and food type to ensure the order is created correctly.
* Provide a clear delivery date and time to ensure timely delivery.
* Enter accurate contact information for seamless communication.
* If any field is not relevant or required, leave it empty.
* Review and revise your order before submitting to ensure it meets your needs.
