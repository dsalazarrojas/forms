# Store Front Banner Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for store front banners, including customer details, banner specifications, and order details. It is intended for customers to place orders for banners with their desired specifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your customer information (customer_info).
2. Enter any additional text fields (text_fields).
3. Enter banner details (banner_details).
4. Provide artwork information (artwork).
5. Enter payment details (payment_details).
6. Enter the total cost of the order (total_cost).
7. Review and enter any additional review fields (review_fields).
8. Upload any artwork for the banner (artwork_upload).
9. Enter the design for the banner (artwork_design).
10. Enter the banner text (banner_text).
11. Specify the banner size (banner_size).
12. Specify the material for the banner (banner_material).
13. Write a review of the order (review).
14. Enter any additional review text (review_text).
15. Click the submit button to complete the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (customer_info)**: Enter your name and contact information to associate it with your order.
* **Additional Text Fields (text_fields)**: Enter any additional text that you would like to include in the banner.
* **Banner Details (banner_details)**: Enter the specifics of the banner you would like to order, including size and material.
* **Artwork (artwork)**: Provide a description of the artwork you would like to use for the banner.
* **Payment Details (payment_details)**: Enter your payment information to complete the order.
* **Total Cost (total_cost)**: Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Review Fields (review_fields)**: Review and enter any additional review fields to provide feedback on the order.
* **Artwork Upload (artwork_upload)**: Upload any artwork files for the banner.
* **Artwork Design (artwork_design)**: Enter the design specifics for the artwork.
* **Banner Text (banner_text)**: Enter any text that you would like to include on the banner.
* **Banner Size (banner_size)**: Specify the size of the banner you would like to order.
* **Banner Material (banner_material)**: Specify the material for the banner.
* **Review (review)**: Write a review of the order.
* **Review Text (review_text)**: Enter any additional review text to provide feedback on the order.
* **Submit**: Click the submit button to complete and send the order.
