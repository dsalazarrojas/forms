# Public Safety Equipment Requisition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Public Safety Equipment Requisition Form is a tool for end-users to request public safety equipment from the company's inventory. This form helps to manage and track equipment requests, ensuring that the right equipment is delivered to the right person.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have a legitimate need for the equipment.
2.  Review the available options in Section 4 and select the required equipment.
3.  Provide accurate and complete information in all fields.
4.  Submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Section 1: Information
*  **Name**: (`text`, optional): This field is for you to enter your name as you want to appear on the equipment's requisition.
*  **Employee ID**: (`text`, optional): If you want to be contacted for any follow-up questions, please enter your Employee ID.
*  **Contact Information**: (`text`, optional): You may enter additional contact information, such as your phone number or department, to help us contact you.

### Section 2: Section 2
*  **Section 2 Description**: (`text`, required): Please provide a brief description of why this equipment is needed.

### Section 3: Section 3
*  **Section 3 Description**: (`text`, optional): This field is for any additional information or clarification about your equipment request.

### Section 4: Equipment Selection
*  **Public Safety Equipment**: (`select_multiple`, required): Select one or more of the equipment options that you need.
    -  **Option 1**: This is the first option.
    -  **Option 2**: This is the second option.

### Section 5: Additional Information
*  **Additional Information**: (`number`, optional): If you have any additional information about your request, please provide it here.

### Section 6: Confirmation
*  **Email Address**: (`email`, required): Please enter your email address for follow-up communication.

## Tips
*  Make sure to review your information carefully before submitting the form to avoid errors.
*  If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact the relevant personnel.
*  Please allow sufficient time for the equipment to be processed and delivered to you.
