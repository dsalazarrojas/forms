# Subscription Services Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Subscription Services Intake Form is designed to gather essential information from clients regarding their subscription services needs. This form helps us understand their preferences, requirements, and any additional services they might need.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, please follow these steps:

1. Provide us with your client's information in the **Client Information** section.
2. Enter your contact number in the **Contact Number** field.
3. Share your email address with us in the **Email** field.
4. Select the types of services you are interested in from the **Services** dropdown menu.
5. If applicable, fill out the **Date** and **Time** fields for your preferred service schedule.
6. Use the **Comments** field for any additional information you'd like to share with us.
7. In the **Client Comments** field, please provide any further comments or feedback regarding your services.
8. In the **Custom Fields** field, you can add any extra information not included in the previous fields.
9. Share your **Client Preferences** in the respective field.
10. Specify your **Preferred Time** for our services.
11. In the **Other** field, you can provide any other information not covered in the previous fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information**: Provide us with your client's name, company name (if applicable), and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number to ensure we can reach you for any updates or questions.
* **Email**: Share your email address to receive notifications and updates from us.
* **Services**: Select the services you are interested in from the dropdown menu (Subscription Services, Customized Services).
* **Date**: If you have a preferred date for our services, please enter it here.
* **Time**: Enter your preferred time for our services.
* **Comments**: Use this field for any additional information or comments regarding your services.
* **Client Comments**: Provide any further comments or feedback regarding your services.
* **Custom Fields**: Add any extra information not included in the previous fields.
* **Client Preferences**: Share your client preferences with us.
* **Preferred Time**: Specify your preferred time for our services.
* **Other**: Provide any other information not covered in the previous fields.
* **Other 2**: This field is similar to the **Other** field, providing additional space for any extra details.
