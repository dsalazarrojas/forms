# Early prophets Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Early Prophets Form is a billing form used to capture information about payments for services rendered to guests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form carefully to understand which fields are required and which are optional.
2. Enter the Early Check Out Date of the guest (Page 1).
3. Select the payment methods used for the guest (Page 2).
4. Enter the payment amount (Page 3).
5. Enter the Room Number of the guest (Page 4).
6. Enter the Guest Name (Page 5).
7. Enter the Phone Number (Page 6).
8. Enter the Email Address (Page 7).
9. Select the payment type (Page 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Early Check Out Date** (Page 1, `early_check_out_date`): Enter the date the guest checked out early.
* **Payment Method** (Page 2, `payment_method`): Select all payment methods used for the guest (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
* **Payment Amount** (Page 3, `payment_amount`): Enter the payment amount for the services rendered.
* **Room Number** (Page 4, `room_number`): Enter the number of the room the guest was staying in.
* **Guest Name** (Page 5, `guest_name`): Enter the name of the guest.
* **Phone Number** (Page 6, `phone_number`): Enter the phone number of the guest.
* **Email Address** (Page 7, `email_address`): Enter the email address of the guest.
* **Payment Type** (Page 8, `payment_type`): Select the type of payment made (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate processing.
- Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
- Contact the facility administration if you have any questions or need clarification on any field.
