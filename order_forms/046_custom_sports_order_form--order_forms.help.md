# custom_sports_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The custom sports order form is designed to collect customer details, order preferences, payment details, confirm order, and contact details from users.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **customer_details** section with your name, email, and any other relevant contact information.
2. In the **order_preferences** section, select your preferred products or services you would like to order.
3. In the **payment_details** section, provide your payment method and any other payment-related information.
4. Confirm your order in the **confirm_order** section.
5. Finally, provide your contact details in the **contact_details** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, required): This is where you'll provide your name, email, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Order Preferences** (`order_preferences`, text, required): Select your preferred products or services you would like to order.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, required): Provide your payment method and any other payment-related information.
* **Confirm Order** (`confirm_order`, text, required): Confirm your order to ensure that we have all the necessary information.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required): This is where you'll provide your contact information, including your phone number and any other relevant details.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure that we can process your order successfully.
* Double-check your order preferences and contact details for accuracy.
* If you have any issues or concerns while filling out the form, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
