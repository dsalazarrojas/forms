# Vehicle Sales Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Sales Agreement Form is a document that outlines the terms and conditions of a vehicle sale between a buyer and a seller. It is designed to collect necessary information about the vehicle and the buyer, ensuring a clear understanding of the sale's details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the vehicle details (page 1) by providing the vehicle's make, model, year, and any other relevant information.
2. Provide your buyer information (page 2) by filling out the required fields with your details.
3. Review and agree to the payment terms (page 3) to understand the selling price and any payment conditions.
4. Enter the vehicle's price (page 4) to ensure you agree to the selling price.
5. Sign the buyer's signature (page 5) to confirm your agreement to purchase the vehicle.
6. Sign the seller's signature (page 6) to confirm the seller's agreement to sell the vehicle.
7. Date and time of signing (page 7 and 8) to record the date and time of the sale.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle details (page 1, text)**: Enter the vehicle's make, model, year, and any other relevant information to describe the vehicle being sold.
* **Buyer_info (page 2, text)**: Fill out your buyer information, including your name, address, and contact details.
* **Payment terms (page 3, text)**: Review and agree to the payment terms, which outline the selling price and any conditions of the sale.
* **Vehicle price (page 4, number)**: Enter the selling price of the vehicle to confirm your agreement.
* **Buyer signature (page 5, note)**: Sign this field to confirm your agreement to purchase the vehicle.
* **Seller signature (page 6, note)**: Sign this field to confirm the seller's agreement to sell the vehicle.
* **Date signed (page 7, date)**: Enter the date of the sale to record the date of the agreement.
* **Time signed (page 8, time)**: Enter the time of the sale to record the exact time of the agreement.
