# e_commerce_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to report e-commerce issues, such as defective or damaged products, or issues with the product not being as described.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information and attach any relevant attachments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_information`, Text, Optional): This is where you provide your contact information, such as your name and email address.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, Text, Optional): This field is for you to provide details about the order that's related to the issue you're reporting. Be as specific as possible about the order, including the order number, date, and any other relevant details.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, Text, Optional): In this field, please describe the issue you're experiencing with your order. Try to be as clear and concise as possible about the problem and what you've observed.
* **Issue Type** (`issue_type`, Select One, Optional): Choose the type of issue you're reporting:
	+ Defective Product
	+ Damaged Product
	+ Not as Described
	+ Other
* **Attachment** (`attachment`, Text, Optional): If you have any supporting documentation or images related to your issue, such as photos of the damaged product or a copy of your receipt, you can attach them here.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, Note, Optional): If you'd like to sign your complaint, you can add your signature here.
* **Sales Rep Signature** (`sales_rep_signature`, Note, Optional): If a sales representative has contacted you about your issue, they may ask you to sign this field to confirm that they've received your complaint and that you've agreed on a resolution.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you're filling out all the required information accurately and completely.
* If you have any supporting documentation or images, attach them to help us better understand your issue.
* If you're unsure about any part of the form, don't hesitate to contact our customer service team for assistance.
