# E Waste Collection Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The E Waste Collection Order Form is designed to collect essential information from users for waste collection services. This form helps us to provide you with the best possible service by knowing exactly what you need to be collected, when you need it collected, and where it should be taken to.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in your **Enter Your Name** (User Name): This is a required field that needs to be filled with your actual name. Make sure to enter it correctly to ensure accurate identification.
- Enter your **Email** (Email Address): This field is not required, but if you enter an email address, you will receive notifications and updates about your order.
- Provide your **Address**: This is a required field where you need to enter your address for us to know where to send your collected waste.
- Choose your **Pickup Date** (Pickup Date): Select the date you want your waste to be collected.
- Select your **Pickup Time** (Pickup Time): Choose the time you want your waste to be collected. 
- List the **Items to Dispose** (Items to Dispose): This is a required field where you need to list the items you want to be collected.
- Choose the **Collection Center** (Collection Center): Select the collection center where your waste should be taken to. You have two options: **Recycling Center** or **Landfill**.
- If you have **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments), you can enter any extra details that might be helpful for us to understand your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Your Name (User Name)** (`text`, required): Please enter your actual name for identification purposes.
* **Email (Email Address)** (`email`, optional): This is an optional field where you can enter your email address for notifications and updates.
* **Address** (`text`, required): Please enter your physical address to ensure we know where to send your collected waste.
* **Pickup Date (Pickup Date)** (`date`, required): Select the date for your waste collection.
* **Pickup Time (Pickup Time)** (`time`, required): Choose the time for your waste collection.
* **Items to Dispose (Items to Dispose)** (`text`, required): List the items you want us to collect.
* **Collection Center (Collection Center)** (`select_one`, required): Select between **Recycling Center** or **Landfill** for collection.
* **Additional Comments (Additional Comments)** (`note`, optional): Enter any extra details for us to understand your request.
