# School Pie Fundraiser Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to capture information for a school pie fundraiser, including the type of pie, quantity, and other details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the title of the order in the "School Pie Fundraiser Order Form" field.
2. Provide a brief description of the order in the "School Pie Fundraiser Description" field.
3. Select the type of pie from the "Type of Pie" dropdown menu.
4. Enter the quantity of pie needed in the "Quantity" field.
5. Enter the email address associated with the order in the "Email" field.
6. Enter the phone number associated with the order in the "Phone" field.
7. Enter the date the order is required for in the "Date" field.
8. Enter the time the order is required for in the "Time" field.
9. Enter any additional notes or comments about the order in the "Comments" field.
10. Enter the total amount of the order in the "Total" field.
11. Enter the name of the person submitting the order in the "Submitted by" field.
12. Enter the category of the order in the "Category" field.
13. Enter the date and time the order was submitted in the "Submitted at" field.
14. Enter the name of the person assigned to fulfill the order in the "Assigned to" field.
15. Add any relevant tags to the order in the "Tags" field.
