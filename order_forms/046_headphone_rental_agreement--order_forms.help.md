# Headphone Rental Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Headphone Rental Agreement form is used by schools or events teams to request headphone equipment for an event. This form helps us gather necessary information about the event and the equipment needed to ensure a smooth rental experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in the event title and dates to provide context about the event.
* Specify the type and count of headphones needed for the event.
* Provide details about the event location and equipment description.
* Enter the contact information for follow-up and communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **school name**: Enter the name of the school or organization requesting the rental.
* **event title**: Enter a brief description of the event title.
* **start date**: Enter the date the event starts.
* **end date**: Enter the date the event ends.
* **contact name**: Enter the name of the contact person for the event.
* **school address**: Enter the address of the school or organization.
* **event location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **event type**: Enter a brief description of the type of event (e.g., concert, conference, etc.).
* **event date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **event start time**: Enter the start time of the event.
* **event end time**: Enter the end time of the event.
* **event length**: Enter the length of the event.
* **equipment type**: Enter the type of headphones needed.
* **equipment count**: Enter the number of headphones needed.
* **equipment description**: Enter a brief description of the headphones needed.
* **contact phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **contact email**: Enter the email of the contact person.
* **signature 1**: Enter your signature as the contact person.
* **signature 2**: Enter the signature of the school or organization representative.
* **school signature**: Enter the name of the school or organization representative.
* **school signature date**: Enter the date of the school or organization representative's signature.
* **school signature time**: Enter the time of the school or organization representative's signature.
* **school contact name**: Enter the name of the school or organization contact person.
* **school contact phone**: Enter the phone number of the school or organization contact person.

Note: Please make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field to ensure a smooth rental experience.
