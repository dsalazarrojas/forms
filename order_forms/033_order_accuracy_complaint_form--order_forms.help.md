# Order Accuracy Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Order Accuracy Complaint Form is designed to collect information about customer complaints related to their orders. Please fill out this form if you are experiencing an issue with one of our orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields below with as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Accuracy Form** (1, number, required): This is the form itself.
* **Customer Name** (2, text, required): Please enter your name.
* **Order ID** (3, number, required): Please enter the ID of the order you are experiencing an issue with.
* **Complaint Description** (4, text, required): Describe the issue you are experiencing with your order.
* **Customer Email** (5, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Customer Phone** (6, text): If you have a preferred phone number, please enter it here.
* **Customer Notes** (7, text): If you have any additional information that might help us resolve your issue, please enter it here.
* **Business Name** (8, text): This is the name of the business associated with your order.
* **Issue Type** (9, select_one, required): Select the type of issue you are experiencing:
	+ Order was not received
	+ Product was not as expected
	+ Product is missing
	+ Product was damaged
	+ Other
* **Issue Description** (10, text, required): Please describe the issue you are experiencing with your order.
* **Resolution Status** (11, select_one, required): Select the status of the resolution:
	+ Not resolved
	+ Partially resolved
	+ Resolved
	+ On hold
* **Resolution Details** (12, text): Describe how the issue was resolved.
* **Follow-up Actions** (13, select_multiple): Select any follow-up actions that should be taken:
	+ Call customer
	+ Send follow-up email
	+ Schedule follow-up call
	+ Escalate issue
* **Resolution Date** (14, date): Enter the date the issue was resolved.
* **Resolution Time** (15, time): Enter the time the issue was resolved.
* **Business Response** (16, text): This is for internal business team use only.
* **Customer Response** (17, text): This is for internal business team use only.
* **Follow-up Details** (18, text): If you have any additional information that might help with follow-up, please enter it here.
* **Follow-up Date** (19, date): Enter the date the follow-up action was taken.
* **Follow-up Time** (20, time): Enter the time the follow-up action was taken.
* **Business Response Status** (21, select_one): Select the status of the business response:
	+ Not responded
	+ Responded
* **Business Response Date** (22, date): Enter the date the business response was received.
* **Customer Response Status** (23, select_one): Select the status of the customer response:
	+ Not responded
	+ Responded
* **Customer Response Date** (24, date): Enter the date the customer response was received.
* **Submitted By** (25, select_one): Select who submitted the complaint:
	+ Customer
	+ Business
* **Submitted By Name** (26, text): Enter the name of the person who submitted the complaint.
* **Submitted By Email** (27, email): Enter the email address of the person who submitted the complaint.
