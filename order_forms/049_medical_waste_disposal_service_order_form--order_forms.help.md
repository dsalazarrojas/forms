# Medical Waste Disposal Service Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to order medical waste disposal services for various types of medical waste. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that your medical waste is handled and disposed of correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the correct service type from the options provided.
2. Provide your facility address if you are ordering services for your business or institution.
3. Choose the desired service frequency for your waste disposal needs.
4. Select the duration of the service required.
5. Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the order.
6. Enter the email address of the contact person.
7. Enter the contact phone number of the contact person.
8. Provide the start date of the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Address** (Facility Address, text, required/optional): Enter the address of your facility, including street address, city, state, and zip code. This information is used to locate your facility and ensure that our services are delivered correctly.
* **Service Type** (Service Type, select_one, required): Select the type of medical waste you need to be disposed of, such as pharmaceuticals, biological, sharps, anatomical, or infectious waste.
* **Service Frequency** (Service Frequency, text, optional): Enter the frequency at which you need the service, e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, or daily.
* **Service Duration** (Service Duration, select_multiple, optional): Select the duration of the service required, such as 1 day, 2 days, 3 days, 4 days, or 5 days.
* **Contact Name** (Contact Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for this order.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Service Start Date** (Service Start Date, date, optional): Enter the date you would like the service to start.

## Tips

* Make sure to select the correct service type and frequency to ensure that our services are delivered correctly.
* Enter accurate contact information to ensure that we can reach you with any questions or updates.
* Double-check your form before submitting to ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately.
