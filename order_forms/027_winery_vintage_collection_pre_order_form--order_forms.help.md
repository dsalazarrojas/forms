# winery_vintage_collection_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The winery_vintage_collection_pre_order_form is used to collect information from customers for a pre-order of the winery's vintage collection. This form is required to be completed only once per order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Customer Info page.
2. Complete the Order Details page.
3. Provide the Delivery Info.
4. Enter the Payment Info.
5. Sign the Customer Signature page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Info
* **Customer Info** (1, text, required/optional): This page is where you enter your contact and address information.
### Order Details
* **Order Details** (2, text, required/optional): Enter all necessary details about your order, including any specific requests or instructions.
### Delivery Info
* **Delivery Info** (3, text, required/optional): Provide your delivery address and any specific delivery instructions.
### Payment Info
* **Payment Info** (4, text, required/optional): Enter your payment information to complete the order.
### Customer Signature
* **Customer Signature** (5, text, required/optional): Sign and date the form to confirm the order.
