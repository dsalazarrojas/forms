# Electric Vehicle Charger Installation Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for ordering electric vehicle charger installations for companies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the company details, including name, contact person, email, and address.
2. Provide contact information, including contact number and installation date.
3. Specify the vehicle make and model.
4. Choose the charger type and installation location.
5. Enter additional comments or notes if necessary.
6. Assign a tool for the installation.
7. Enter customer signature and company signature.
8. Provide installation status and comments.
9. Provide charger status and notes.
10. Enter customer rating and charger rating (if applicable).
11. Enter the submission date (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Details**: Enter the company name, address, and contact information to identify the company and its representatives.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person for the company.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the contact person.
* **Installation Date**: Enter the date of the installation.
* **Vehicle Make**: Enter the make of the vehicle to be installed with the charger.
* **Vehicle Model**: Enter the model of the vehicle to be installed with the charger.
* **Charger Type**: Select the type of charger to be installed.
* **Installation Location**: Enter the location where the charger will be installed.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool that will be used for the installation.
* **Customer Signature**: Enter the customer's signature to confirm the installation details.
* **Company Signature**: Enter the company's signature to confirm the installation details.
* **Installation Status**: Select the status of the installation (e.g., Pending, In Progress, Done).
* **Installation Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the installation.
* **Charger Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the charger.
* **Customer Rating**: Enter the customer's rating of the installation (if applicable).
* **Charger Rating**: Enter the charger's rating (if applicable).
* **Charger Status**: Select the status of the charger (e.g., Active, Inactive, Pending).
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date of submission (if applicable).
