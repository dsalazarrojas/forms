# Farm To Table Product Delivery Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Farm To Table Product Delivery Form is a key tool used to manage and coordinate the delivery of farm-to-table products from farmers to customers. This form helps to gather essential information for smooth and efficient delivery operations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by providing your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your **Email Address** to ensure we can contact you regarding any updates or changes.
3. Select a date for the **Delivery Date** when you plan to receive your order.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** so we can contact you for any delivery queries.
5. Choose the type of **Order Details** you require (e.g., Local, Organic, Seasonal, or Special Order) that best describes your product needs.
6. Type in the total number of **Total Items** to be delivered.
7. In the **Delivery Info** field, provide any relevant details about your order or delivery preferences.
8. Select a time of the day for the **Delivery Time** that suits you best.
9. Enter the **Delivery Location** where you would like the order to be delivered.
10. Choose your preferred **Payment Type** (Cash, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer).
11. Finally, add any **Notes** regarding your order or any specific requirements you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (farmer_info)**: Enter your first name to help us identify you.
* **Email Address (email_address)**: Enter your valid email address for us to contact you.
* **Delivery Date (delivery_date)**: Select a date when you would like to receive your order.
* **Phone Number (phone_number)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you for delivery queries.
* **Order Details (order_details)**: Choose the type of product that best describes your order needs.
* **Total Items (total_items)**: Enter the total number of items you are ordering.
* **Delivery Info (delivery_info)**: Provide any relevant details about your order or delivery preferences.
* **Delivery Time (delivery_time)**: Select a time for the delivery that suits you best.
* **Delivery Location (delivery_location)**: Enter the location where you would like the order to be delivered.
* **Payment Type (payment_type)**: Choose your preferred payment method.
* **Notes (notes)**: Add any additional information regarding your order or requirements.
