# nail_varnish_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for an order, including details about the customer, order total, payment method, shipping address, order date, order time, and any additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the order details in the first field.
2. Provide the customer's information in the second field.
3. Enter the total amount for the order in the third field.
4. Select the payment method for the transaction from the dropdown menu in the fourth field.
5. Enter the shipping address for the order in the fifth field.
6. Enter the date of the order in the sixth field.
7. Enter the time of the order in the seventh field.
8. If any additional notes about the order are required, enter them in the last field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details** (Order Details, text, required/optional): Enter any details about the order, such as the product being ordered or any special requests.
* **Customer Info** (Customer Info, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Order Total** (Order Total, number, required/optional): Enter the total amount of the order.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_one, required/optional): Choose the method of payment for the order from the dropdown menu:
	+ Visa
	+ Mastercard
	+ Amex
	+ Discover
	+ PayPal
	+ Bank Draft
* **Shipping Address** (Shipping Address, text, required/optional): Enter the shipping address for the order, including the address lines and any additional details.
* **Order Date** (Order Date, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the order.
* **Order Time** (Order Time, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the order.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the order.
