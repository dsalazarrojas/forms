# Paper Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Paper Order Form is used for customers to place orders for paper products. This form allows users to input information about the order, including paper size, color, quantity, unit price, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter the customer's last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter the customer's address in the "address" field (optional).
4. Select the order date from the calendar.
5. Enter the order details in the "order_details" field (optional).
6. Enter the contact phone number in the "contact_phone" field (optional).
7. Enter the contact email in the "contact_email" field (optional).
8. Select the paper size from the options in the "paper_type" field.
9. Select the paper color from the options in the "paper_color" field.
10. Enter the quantity of paper ordered.
11. Enter the unit price of the paper.
12. Enter the total amount due.
13. Select the payment method from the options in the "payment_method" field.
14. Select the payment date from the calendar.
15. Select the paper status from the options in the "paper_status" field.
16. Enter the delivery address.
17. Enter the delivery phone number.
18. Enter the delivery email.
19. Enter any additional order notes (optional).
20. Enter any delivery notes (optional).
21. Select the delivery date from the calendar.
22. Select the delivery time from the time picker.
23. Enter your signature.
24. Enter the signature date from the calendar.
25. Enter the signature time from the time picker.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's last name.
- **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter the customer's address.
- **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, optional): Select the date the order is being placed.
- **Order Details** (`order_details`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional information about the order.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the customer's contact phone number.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the customer's contact email.
- **Paper Type** (`paper_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the size of the paper from the options.
- **Paper Color** (`paper_color`, `select_one`, required): Select the color of the paper from the options.
- **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, required): Enter the quantity of paper ordered.
- **Unit Price** (`unit_price`, `number`, required): Enter the unit price of the paper.
- **Total** (`total`, `number`, required): Enter the total amount due.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select the payment method from the options.
- **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, `date`, required): Select the payment date from the calendar.
- **Paper Status** (`paper_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of the paper from the options.
- **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, required): Enter the delivery address.
- **Delivery Phone** (`delivery_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the delivery phone number.
- **Delivery Email** (`delivery_email`, `text`, required): Enter the delivery email.
- **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information about the order.
- **Delivery Notes** (`delivery_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information about the delivery.
- **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, required): Select the delivery date from the calendar.
- **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, `time`, required): Select the delivery time from the time picker.
- **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required): Enter your signature.
- **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Select the signature date from the calendar.
- **Signature Time** (`signature_time`, `time`, required): Select the signature time from the time picker.
