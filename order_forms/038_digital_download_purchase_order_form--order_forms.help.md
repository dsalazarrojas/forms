# digital_download_purchase_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The digital download purchase order form is a tool used to collect customer information and details about digital downloads or products for processing and fulfillment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, including any optional fields that are relevant to your transaction.
2. Review your information before submitting the form to ensure it is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Purchase Order Form** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This field is a general description of the purchase order form.
* **Customer Name** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the customer or the person placing the order.
* **Email Address** (`3`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the customer or contact person.
* **Phone Number** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the customer or contact person.
* **Product/Download** (`5`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Select "Download" for digital downloads.
	+ Select "Purchase" for tangible products.
	+ Select "License" for licensing purposes.
* **Purchase Order Status** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select "Active" for active orders.
	+ Select "Completed" for completed orders.
	+ Select "Inactive" for inactive orders.
* **Product Description** (`7`, `note`, required: false): This field is a note for product/download description.
* **Customer Note** (`8`, `note`, required: false): This field is a note for any customer comments or requests.

## Tips
- If you are unsure about any field, refer to this help guide or contact your support team.
- If you have any special requests or notes, use the "Customer Note" field.
- Ensure that your information is accurate and up-to-date to avoid delays in processing your order.
