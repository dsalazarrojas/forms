# Garden Design Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Garden Design Order Form is designed to collect information from clients or customers about their garden design needs. This form helps our team gather essential details about their project requirements, preferred payment method, and desired design package.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the **Project Request** field (Page 1). Please provide a brief description of your garden design needs.
2. Select your preferred **Payment Method** (Page 1). You can choose between Stripe and PayPal.
3. Select one or more **Design Package** options (Page 1) that suit your needs. You can choose from Package 1, Package 2, or Package 3.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Request (Page 1)** (`Project Request`, text, required/optional): Please provide a brief description of your garden design needs, including any specific requirements or requests you have for your garden design.
* **Payment Method (Page 1)** (`Payment Method`, select_one, required/optional): Choose how you'd like to make your payment, either through Stripe or PayPal.
* **Design Package (Page 1)** (`Design Package`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one or more of the available design packages that meet your needs: Package 1, Package 2, or Package 3.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide clear and concise information in the **Project Request** field to ensure our team can understand your needs accurately.
* Double-check your selected **Payment Method** to ensure you're aware of any associated fees or charges.
* Choose the **Design Package** that best suits your budget and requirements to get the most value for your garden design project.
