# Soccer Team T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soccer Team T Shirt Order Form is used for customers to place orders for custom t-shirts with their preferred color, size, and quantity. This form ensures accurate and efficient order fulfillment by collecting necessary customer information and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the respective fields.
2. Select your preferred shirt size from the options provided.
3. Choose the number of shirts you want to order in the quantity field.
4. Select the color of the shirt from the options provided.
5. Enter the price of the order.
6. Select the shipping cost for your order.
7. Choose a payment method from the options provided.
8. Enter the date you would like to place the order.
9. Select the payment status of your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your full name to ensure accurate order fulfillment.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you for updates.
* **Shirt Size** (`shirt_size`, select_one, optional): Choose the size of the shirt you want to order from Large, Medium, or Small.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of shirts you want to order.
* **Color** (`color`, select_one, optional): Select the color of the shirt from White, Red, or Blue.
* **Price** (`price`, number, required): Enter the price of the order to complete the order.
* **Shipping** (`shipping_cost`, number, optional): Select the shipping cost for your order.
* **Payment** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Choose the payment method from PayPal, Stripe, or Bank Transfer.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required): Enter the date you would like to place the order.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of your payment from Paid, Pending, or Cancelled.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Double-check your order details before confirming the order.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact our customer support team for assistance.
