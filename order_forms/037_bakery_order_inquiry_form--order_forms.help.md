# Bakery Order Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals or organizations to submit custom bakery order inquiries, including event and product details. The form aims to gather necessary information to provide accurate quotes and facilitate communication with the bakery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling in your personal details, including your full name and email address.
2.  Choose the type of event or occasion you are inquiring about (e.g., wedding, birthday, corporate event).
3.  Select the date of the event.
4.  Indicate the number of guests attending the event.
5.  Choose the types of products you are interested in (e.g., cakes, cupcakes, cookies, pastries, bread).
6.  Describe the type of product you are looking for in the "Detailed Product Description" field.
7.  Estimate the number of servings or pieces you will need.
8.  If applicable, provide any flavor or design ideas you have.
9.  Mention any dietary requirements or allergies.
10.  Specify any custom requests or special accommodations you need.
11.  Provide the delivery location for the order.
12.  Choose a budget range for the order.
13.  Select a timeline for when you need a quote.
14.  Choose your preferred contact method for follow-up (email, phone, text message).
15.  Finally, indicate whether you are ready to submit your inquiry and await a quote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Organization or Company (if applicable)**: If you are representing a business or organization, enter your name here.
* **Type of Event or Occasion**: Select the type of event or occasion you are inquiring about (e.g., wedding, birthday, corporate event).
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Number of Guests**: Indicate the number of guests attending the event.
* **Type of Product(s)**: Select all the types of products you are interested in (e.g., cakes, cupcakes, cookies, pastries, bread).
* **Detailed Product Description**: Describe the type of product you are looking for.
* **Estimated Quantity**: Estimate the number of servings or pieces you will need.
* **Flavor Ideas or Preferences**: If you have any flavor ideas or preferences, mention them here.
* **Design or Decoration Ideas**: If you have any design or decoration ideas, mention them here.
* **Dietary Requirements or Allergies**: If you have any dietary requirements or allergies, mention them here.
* **Custom Requests or Special Accommodations**: If you have any custom requests or special accommodations, mention them here.
* **Delivery Location**: Provide the delivery location for the order.
* **Budget Range**: Choose a budget range for the order (e.g., under 100, 100-250, 250-500, etc.).
* **Timeline for Quote**: Choose a timeline for when you need a quote (e.g., urgent, within 1 week, within 2 weeks, etc.).
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Choose your preferred contact method for follow-up (e.g., email, phone, text message).
* **Submit Inquiry and Await Quote**: Indicate whether you are ready to submit your inquiry and await a quote.
