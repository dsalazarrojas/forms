# Factory Signal Light Tower Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Signal Light Tower Order Form is designed to collect necessary information for processing new orders of factory signal light towers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields and submit it. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section for further clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required): Please provide a brief description of the order.
* **Delivery Info** (`delivery_info`, text, required): Enter any relevant information about the delivery.
* **Tower Specs** (`tower_specs`, text, required): Specify any special specifications for the tower.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, required): If you have any special requests or instructions for your order, please note them here.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required): Enter the date for delivery of the tower.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required): Enter the time of delivery.
* **Tower Type** (`tower_type`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you need an active tower or "No" if you need an inactive tower.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the amount of towers you are ordering.
* **Special Notes** (`special_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information or comments about your order.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter your name or the name of the person to contact.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address or the email address of the person to contact.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number or the phone number of the person to contact.
* **Tower Status** (`tower_status`, select_multiple, required): Select "Active" if you need an active tower and "Inactive" if you need an inactive tower.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you need any further assistance or clarification, please contact the relevant department.
