# Interactive Display Screen Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Interactive Display Screen Order Form is designed to streamline the ordering process by breaking it down into manageable sections. This form is ideal for collecting details about an order for various types of transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Scroll through the form's pages and start filling out the required fields for each section.
2. Begin with the Order Details page (Page 1), where you will find the text field for entering your order information.
3. Move on to the Customer Info page (Page 2) to enter your customer's contact and identity details.
4. Proceed to the Shipping Info page (Page 3) to fill out the delivery details.
5. Next, provide payment information on the Payment Info page (Page 4).
6. Finally, review and summarize your order details on the Order Summary page (Page 5).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Order Details (Page 1)**
* Order Details (Order Details, text, required): Here, you'll enter the core details about your order. This field allows you to provide a clear and concise description of what you're ordering.

### **Customer Info (Page 2)**
* Customer Info (Customer Info, text, required): In this field, you will input your or your customer's contact and identity information, ensuring that we can communicate with them effectively and efficiently.

### **Shipping Info (Page 3)**
* Shipping Info (Shipping Info, text, required): This field is where you'll provide the delivery address and any other relevant shipping details so we can ensure your order reaches you without issue.

### **Payment Info (Page 4)**
* Payment Info (Payment Info, text, required): Please provide payment information to complete your order. This includes any payment method and details we may need to process the payment successfully.

### **Order Summary (Page 5)**
* Order Summary (Order Summary, text, required): This is your final opportunity to review and confirm all your order details before finalizing your order.
