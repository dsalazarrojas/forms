# Catering Supplies Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Catering Supplies Requisition Form is used to request catering supplies and equipment for events, meetings, or other occasions within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your details in the required fields, including your name, department, email address, phone number, request date, and the date you need the supplies.
2. Select the department and location where the supplies will be delivered.
3. If applicable, provide the event name, date, and priority level of the request.
4. Select the type of supply category you need.
5. For each item you are requesting, enter the item name, description, quantity, and unit of measure.
6. If you know the estimated cost, enter it in the estimated cost field.
7. Add any special instructions for the delivery or handling of the supplies.
8. If required, select the approval status of the manager for the request.
9. Enter the name of the manager who approved or will approve the request.
10. Add any additional comments or information related to the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Name** (`requester_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select your department, such as Kitchen, Front of House, etc.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Needed By Date** (`needed_by_date`, date, required): Enter the date you need the supplies.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event if applicable.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event if applicable.
* **Priority** (`priority`, select_one, required): Select the priority level of the request, such as Low, Medium, High, or Critical.
* **Supply Category** (`supply_category`, select_one, required): Select the type of supply category, such as Food Items, Beverages, etc.
* **Item 1 Name** (`item_1_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the first item you are requesting.
* **Item 1 Description** (`item_1_description`, text, optional): Enter the description of the first item you are requesting.
* **Item 1 Quantity** (`item_1_quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of the first item you are requesting.
* **Item 1 Unit** (`item_1_unit`, text, optional): Enter the unit of measure for the first item you are requesting.

* **Item 3 Quantity** (`item_3_quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of the third item you are requesting.
* **Item 3 Unit** (`item_3_unit`, text, optional): Enter the unit of measure for the third item you are requesting.
* **Item 4 Name** (`item_4_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the fourth item you are requesting.
