# scissors_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scissors Order Form is designed to collect customer information and order details for processing and fulfilling orders of scissors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's information in the **customer_info** field.
2. Select the type of scissors required from the **scissors_type** dropdown menu.
3. Enter the quantity of scissors needed in the **quantity** field.
4. Choose the size of scissors required from the **size** field.
5. Select the color of scissors preferred from the **color** field.
6. Add any additional notes or comments in the **notes** field.
7. Enter the customer's email address for communication purposes in the **email** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info**: Enter the customer's name, address, and any other relevant contact information. (Text field, required: false)
* **Scissors Type**: Select the type of scissors required for the order, including options for Type A, Type B, and Type C. (Select menu, required: false)
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of scissors needed for the order. (Number field, required: false)
* **Size**: Choose the size of scissors required, including options for small, medium, and large. (Select menu, required: false)
* **Color**: Select the color of scissors preferred, including options for red, blue, and green. (Select menu, required: false)
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the order in this field. (Text field, required: false)
* **Email**: Enter the customer's email address for communication purposes. (Email field, required: false)

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly to avoid errors in processing the order.
- Use the form only for creating new orders, as duplicate orders cannot be submitted.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form or the order, contact the support team for assistance.
