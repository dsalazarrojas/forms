# Themed Party Favor Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect order information for themed party favors. It captures essential details such as contact information, party specifics, and order information to ensure accurate and efficient processing of themed party favors.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Ensure that you provide valid contact information to enable us to get in touch with you.
- Choose the correct theme for your party.
- Specify the number of favors you would like to order.
- Provide any special instructions or requests for your favors.
- Select the correct order status for your favor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
- **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your complete address.
- **Zip Code** (`zip`, `number`, required): Enter your zip code.
- **Party Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date for your themed party.
- **Party Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Enter the time for your themed party.
- **Message** (`message`, `text`, required): Enter any special instructions or requests for your favors.
- **Name of Guest** (`name_2`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the guest for whom you are ordering.
- **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, `number`, required): Enter the number of guests attending the party.
- **Number of Favors** (`number_of_favors`, `number`, required): Enter the number of favors you would like to order.
- **Favor Type** (`favor_type`, `select_one`, not required): Select a favor type (Balloons, Gift, Pinata).
- **Party Theme** (`party_theme`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select the theme for your party (Themed Party, Birthday Party, Holiday Party).
- **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `text`, not required): Provide any additional instructions or requests for your favors.
- **Order** (`order`, `select_one`, not required): Select whether to order or not.
- **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, not required): Enter any comments for your order.
- **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, not required): Enter any additional notes for your order.
- **Order Status** (`order_status`, `select_one`, not required): Select the status of your order (Ordered, Not Ordered).
- **Date of Order** (`date_of_order`, `date`, not required): Enter the date for when you would like to order.
- **Time of Order** (`time_of_order`, `time`, not required): Enter the time for when you would like to order.
- **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, not required): Not used. 
- **Order Time** (`order_time`, `time`, not required): Not used.
- **Order Number** (`order_number`, `number`, required): Enter a unique order number for your order.
- **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select the status of your payment (Paid, Unpaid, Partially Paid).
- **Order Total** (`order_total`, `number`, required): Enter the total cost of your order.
