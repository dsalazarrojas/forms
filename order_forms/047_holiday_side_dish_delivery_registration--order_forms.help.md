# Holiday Side Dish Delivery Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this Holiday Side Dish Delivery Registration form is to collect customers' preferences for holiday side dishes to accompany their orders.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Customer Details" page to enter your name and any other required information.
2. In the "Delivery Preferences" page, select your desired delivery date and time.
3. In the "Side Dish Selections" page, choose the side dishes you would like to order from the available options.
4. Once you have filled out all the required fields, click "Submit" to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details** (Customer Details, text, optional)
	+ This field is for entering your name and any other relevant customer information.
* **Delivery Preferences** (Delivery Preferences, text, optional)
	+ This field is for specifying your desired delivery date and time. Please select the date and time that suits you best.
* **Side Dish Selections** (Side Dish Selections, select multiple, required)
	+ Choose one or more side dishes you would like to order from the available options. Selecting multiple side dishes allows us to provide you with a variety of options for your holiday meal.
	+ Side dishes available for selection:
		- Side Dish 1
		- Side Dish 2
		- Side Dish 3

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled out to ensure accurate processing of your order.
- If you have any special requests or questions, please contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
