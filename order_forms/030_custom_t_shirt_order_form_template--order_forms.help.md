# Custom T Shirt Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for custom t-shirt orders. Users can input their preferences for t-shirt design, style, color, size, price, quantity, and other relevant details to create a custom t-shirt order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the desired t-shirt color from the available options.
2. Choose the desired t-shirt size from the available options.
3. Select the desired t-shirt style(s) from the available options.
4. Enter the customer's name.
5. Enter the custom text for the t-shirt (if desired).
6. Describe the custom design for the t-shirt (if desired).
7. Input the price of the t-shirt.
8. Specify the quantity of t-shirts to be ordered.
9. Select the order date.
10. Enter the customer's email address.
11. Enter the customer's phone number.
12. Select the desired T Shirt Style 2 from the available options.
13. Enter the customer's address.
14. Enter the customer's zip code.
15. Select the order status from the available options.
16. Select the phone type from the available options.
17. Select the desired T Shirt Size 2 from the available options.
18. Enter the email hint (if desired).
19. Enter the customer's phone number (if desired).
20. Select the order date 2.
21. Select the desired T Shirt Color 2 from the available options.
22. Enter the customer's name 2.
23. Enter the custom text for the t-shirt 2 (if desired).
24. Describe the custom design for the t-shirt 2 (if desired).
25. Input the quantity of t-shirts 2 to be ordered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select a color** (`t_shirt_color`, select_one): Select the desired color for your custom t-shirt from the available options.
* **Select a size** (`t_shirt_size`, select_one): Choose the desired size for your custom t-shirt from the available options.
* **Select a style** (`t_shirt_style`, select_multiple): Select one or more desired styles for your custom t-shirt from the available options.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text): Enter the customer's name as it appears on the order.
* **Shirt Text** (`t_shirt_text`, text): Enter any custom text you'd like to appear on your t-shirt (if desired).
* **Shirt Design** (`t_shirt_design`, text): Describe the custom design for your t-shirt (if desired).
* **Shirt Price** (`t_shirt_price`, number): Input the price of your custom t-shirt.
* **Quantity** (`t_shirt_quantity`, number): Specify the quantity of t-shirts to be ordered.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date): Select the order date.
* **Email** (`customer_email`, email): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone** (`customer_telephone`, text): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **T Shirt Style 2** (`t_shirt_style_2`, select_multiple): Select one or more desired styles for your custom t-shirt 2 from the available options.
* **Address** (`customer_address`, text): Enter the customer's address.
* **Zip** (`customer_zip`, text): Enter the customer's zip code.
* **Status** (`order_status`, select_one): Select the order status from the available options.
* **Phone Type** (`customer_phone_type`, select_one): Select the phone type from the available options.
* **T Shirt Size 2** (`t_shirt_size_2`, select_one): Select the desired size for your custom t-shirt 2 from the available options.
* **Email Hint** (`customer_email_hint`, text): Enter any desired hint for the customer's email (if desired).
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text): Enter the customer's phone number (if desired).
* **Order Date 2** (`order_date_2`, date): Select the order date 2.
* **T Shirt Color 2** (`t_shirt_color_2`, select_one): Select the desired color for your custom t-shirt 2 from the available options.
* **Customer Name 2** (`customer_name_2`, text): Enter the customer's name 2.
* **T Shirt Text 2** (`t_shirt_text_2`, text): Enter any custom text you'd like to appear on your t-shirt 2 (if desired).
* **T Shirt Design 2** (`t_shirt_design_2`, text): Describe the custom design for your t-shirt 2 (if desired).
* **T Shirt Quantity 2** (`t_shirt_quantity_2`, number): Input the quantity of t-shirts 2 to be ordered.
