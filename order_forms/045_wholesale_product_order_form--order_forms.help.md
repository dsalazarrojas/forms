# Wholesale Product Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for customers to place wholesale orders for products. It captures essential information for the order, including customer details and order specifics. Fill out this form to create a new order with our business.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer information: Enter your customer name, email address, and any other relevant details in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the order date for the order: Choose the date you'd like to place the order for.
3. Enter the total price: Provide the total amount for the order.
4. Choose your payment terms: Select the payment terms you're comfortable with (e.g., "Net 30" for 30-day payment terms).
5. Review and submit: Double-check your entries for accuracy and submit the form to place the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer info**: This section is where you'll enter your name, email address, and any other relevant details. Please make sure to enter accurate information so we can contact you efficiently.
* **Order date**: This is the date you'd like to place the order for. Please select a valid date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Total price**: This is the total amount for the order. Enter the exact price you're willing to pay for the product.
* **Payment terms**: This is where you'll choose your preferred payment terms. Please select one of the available options (e.g., "Net 30" for 30-day payment terms).
* **Customer name**: This is your name as the customer. Please enter your full name for our records.
* **Customer email**: This is your email address where we'll contact you for order updates and inquiries.
* **Product name**: This is the name of the product you're ordering. Please enter the exact name of the product as described on our product list.
* **Tips**:
	+ Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
	+ If you have any questions or need clarification, feel free to contact us directly.
	+ Our team will review and process your order once you submit the form.
