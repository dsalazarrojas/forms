# Catering Services Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to generate an invoice for catering services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in this guide).
2. Make sure to enter all the necessary information about the client, event, and services provided.
3. Review and fill out the cost fields (food cost, beverage cost, etc.) accurately.
4. Select the correct payment method and payment status for the invoice.
5. If you have any additional comments or billing notes, fill them out in the "Billing Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Enter the client's full name.
* **Company Name (Optional)**: If the client has a company, enter their company name.
* **Email Address**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the client's contact phone number.
* **Billing Address**: Enter the address where the invoice should be sent.
* **Invoice Date**: Enter the date of the invoice.
* **Invoice Number**: Enter the reference number of the invoice.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Service Type**: Select the type of service provided (Full Service, Drop-off, Buffet, Plated, etc.).
* **Number of Guests**: Enter the total number of guests served.
* **Food Cost**: Enter the cost for food provided.
* **Beverage Cost**: Enter the cost for beverages provided (if applicable).
* **Staff Cost**: Enter the cost for service staff (if applicable).
* **Equipment Rental Cost**: Enter the cost for equipment rental (if applicable).
* **Delivery Fee**: Enter the delivery charge (if applicable).
* **Setup Fee**: Enter the setup charge (if applicable).
* **Service Charge**: Enter the service fee percentage (if applicable).
* **Tax Amount**: Enter the applicable tax amount (if applicable).
* **Gratuity**: Enter the tip amount (if applicable).
* **Discounts Applied**: Enter any discounts applied to the total amount.
* **Total Amount Due**: Enter the final total amount due.
* **Deposit Paid**: Enter the amount already paid.
* **Balance Due**: This will automatically calculate the remaining balance due.
* **Payment Due Date**: Enter the date when payment is due.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, Check, Bank Transfer, or Cash).
* **Payment Status**: Select the current payment status (Paid, Partially Paid, Unpaid, or Pending).
* **Billing Notes**: Enter any special billing instructions.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any other information about the event or services provided.
* **Payment Status**: This will automatically calculate the payment status based on the payment method and due date.
