# eye_surgery_billing_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The eye_surgery_billing_form is used to collect billing and administrative information for eye surgery procedures. This form helps to ensure accurate and efficient processing of patient billing and payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Indicate the patient's medical insurance status by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Medical Insurance" field.
3. Enter the name of the medical provider who performed the surgery in the "Medical Provider" field.
4. Enter the date of the surgery in the "Surgery Date" field.
5. Enter the name of the surgeon who performed the surgery in the "Surgeon Name" field.
6. Enter the patient's insurance ID number in the "Insurance ID" field.
7. Enter the billing address in the "Billing Address" field.
8. Enter the patient's address in the "Patient Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their insurance card or other identification.
* **Medical Insurance** (select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate whether the patient has medical insurance or not. Select "Yes" if the patient has insurance, "No" if they do not.
* **Medical Provider** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the medical provider who performed the surgery.
* **Surgery Date** (date, required/optional): Enter the date of the surgery in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Surgeon Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the surgeon who performed the surgery.
* **Insurance ID** (number, required/optional): Enter the patient's insurance ID number.
* **Billing Address** (text, required/optional): Enter the address to which the bill should be sent.
* **Patient Address** (text, required/optional): Enter the patient's address for billing and communication purposes.
