# exhibitor_information_pack_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect exhibitor information for an order pack used in events or activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the exhibitor's company name in the "exhibitor_company" field.
2. Provide the first name of the contact person in the "first_name" field.
3. Enter the last name of the contact person in the "last_name" field.
4. Type the address of the contact person in the "address" field.
5. Enter any additional address information in the "address_2" field.
6. Provide the city of the contact person in the "city" field.
7. Enter the state of the contact person in the "state" field.
8. Enter the zip code of the contact person in the "zip" field.
9. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the "phone" field.
10. Enter the email address of the contact person in the "email" field.
11. Enter any additional email addresses in the "email_2" and "email_3" fields (optional).
12. Select the date of the order in the "order_date" field.
13. Select the time of the order in the "order_time" field.
14. Enter any notes about the order in the "order_notes" field.
15. Provide any special instructions in the "special_instructions" field.
16. Confirm the order status in the "confirm_order" field.
17. Select the status of the order in the "order_status" field.
18. Enter any additional special instructions in the "special_instructions_2" field.
19. Select the date of the order in the "order_date_2" field.
20. Select the time of the order in the "order_time_2" field.
21. Enter any notes about the order in the "order_notes_2" field.
22. Confirm the order status in the "confirm_order_2" field.
23. Select the status of the order in the "order_status_2" field.
24. Select any order confirmation options in the "confirm_order_3" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exhibitor's Company Name**: Enter the name of the exhibitor company.

### last_name

### state

### zip

### phone
* **Contact Person's Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
### email
* **Contact Person's Email Address**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
### email_2
* **Additional Email Address**: Enter any additional email addresses (optional).
### email_3
* **Second Additional Email Address**: Enter any other additional email addresses (optional).
### order_date
* **Order Date**: Select the date of the order.
### order_time
* **Order Time**: Select the time of the order.
### order_notes

### special_instructions
