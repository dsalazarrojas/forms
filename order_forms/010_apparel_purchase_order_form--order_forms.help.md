# Apparel Purchase Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record details for the purchase of apparel products from suppliers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required details for the supplier:
	* Provide the supplier's name, email, and phone number in the Supplier Information section.
2. Complete the buyer's information:
	* Enter the buyer's name, company name, and email in the Buyer Information section.
3. Enter the order details:
	* Enter the purchase order number, order date, and required delivery date.
4. Enter the product details:
	* Describe the product being purchased.
	* Enter the quantity and unit price of the product.
5. Select the payment and shipping methods:
	* Choose the payment method (e.g., Bank Transfer, Credit Card, etc.).
	* Select the shipping method (e.g., FOB Shipping Point, FOB Destination, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the supplier, as given by the supplier.
* **Supplier Email** (`supplier_email`, Email, Required): Enter the email address of the supplier.
* **Supplier Phone** (`supplier_phone`, Text, Required): Enter the phone number of the supplier.
* **Buyer Name** (`buyer_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the buyer.
* **Company Name** (`buyer_company`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the buyer's company.
* **Buyer Email** (`buyer_email`, Email, Required): Enter the email address of the buyer.
* **Purchase Order Number** (`po_number`, Text, Required): Enter the purchase order number.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date of the order.
* **Required Delivery Date** (`required_delivery_date`, Date, Required): Enter the required delivery date.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, Text, Required): Describe the product being purchased.
* **Quantity** (`item_quantity`, Number, Required): Enter the quantity of the product being purchased.
* **Unit Price** (`unit_price`, Number, Required): Enter the unit price of the product.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, Select One, Required): Choose the payment method (e.g., Bank Transfer, Credit Card, etc.).
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, Select One, Required): Choose the shipping method (e.g., FOB Shipping Point, FOB Destination, etc.).

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately to avoid delays in processing the order.
* Double-check the supplier's and buyer's contact information for accuracy.
* Select the correct payment and shipping methods for the order.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to the supplier.
