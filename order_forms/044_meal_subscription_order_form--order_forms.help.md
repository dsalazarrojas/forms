# Meal Subscription Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from customers for meal subscriptions. The form includes fields for customer diet, meal selection, delivery schedule, total number of meals, customer contact information (email and phone number), and any additional customer message.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields according to your preferences.
2. Make sure to fill in all required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
3. Enter your information clearly and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meal Subscription** (`meal_subscription`, text, required/optional): Enter your meal subscription preferences here.
* **Customer Dietary** (`customer_dietary`, text, required/optional): Indicate any dietary restrictions or preferences you have.
* **Select Meals** (`meal_selection`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the types of meals you'd like to subscribe to (Yes/No).
* **Delivery Schedules** (`delivery_schedules`, text, required/optional): Specify your preferred delivery schedule.
* **Total Meals** (`total_meals`, number, required/optional): Enter the total number of meals you'd like to order.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Customer Message** (`customer_message`, text, required/optional): Leave any additional comments or messages for the meal subscription service.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your order is processed correctly.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the meal subscription service directly.
