# sports_event_staff_uniform_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sports_event_staff_uniform_order_form is a simple form designed to collect information from staff members about their roles and uniform preferences for sports events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your role(s) from the list provided.
2. Enter your name and any additional details in the text field.
3. Choose your preferred delivery method (e.g., email, pickup, etc.).
4. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Staff Details
* **Staff Details**: (`staff_details`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter any additional details about yourself. Please provide any relevant information that will help with uniform distribution.

### Roles
* **Roles**: (`roles`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or multiple roles that you will be performing at the event. This will help determine which uniforms you need. Please select from:
	+ Event Manager
	+ Staff
	+ Security
	+ Operations
	+ Marketing

### Uniform Selections
* **Uniform Selections**: (`uniform_selections`, text, optional): If you have any specific uniform preferences or requirements, please enter them here.

### Delivery Preferences
* **Delivery Preferences**: (`delivery_preferences`, text, optional): Specify your preferred method of receiving your uniform. Please enter "email" if you would like the uniform to be sent to your email, "pickup" if you would like to collect it from the office, or any other method if applicable.

## Tips
* Please submit the form only once per staff member to avoid confusion and errors.
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the event organizers or staff administrators for assistance.
