# Video Game Release Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers who want to pre-order a video game. It collects details about the game, customer information, payment information, and shipping address.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Game Details section with the required information about the game you want to pre-order.
2. Provide your contact information in the Customer Information section.
3. Enter your payment details in the Payment Information section.
4. Complete the Shipping Address section with your address where you would like the game to be shipped.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Game Details** (`video_game_release_pre_order_form_1`, text, optional): Fill in the title and description of the game you want to pre-order.
* **Customer Information** (`video_game_release_pre_order_form_2`, text, optional): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant contact information so that we can get in touch with you.
* **Payment Information** (`video_game_release_pre_order_form_3`, text, optional): Enter your payment method (e.g. credit card, PayPal, bank transfer) and any other relevant financial details.
* **Shipping Address** (`video_game_release_pre_order_form_4`, text, optional): Enter the address where you would like the game to be shipped to. This should include your street address, city, state, and zip code.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
- Double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
