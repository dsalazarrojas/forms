# office_relocation_delivery_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The office relocation delivery form is used to manage the process of delivering office items to a new location.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the office location in the "Office Location" field.
* Enter the date for the item's relocation in the "Relocation Date" field.
* Enter the address where the item will be delivered in the "Delivery Address" field.
* Provide a brief description of the item being relocated in the "Item Description" field.
* Enter the quantity of the item being relocated in the "Item Quantity" field.
* Enter any specific instructions for the delivery of the item in the "Delivery Instructions" field.
* Enter the time for the item's delivery in the "Delivery Time" field.
* Enter the name of the contact person responsible for receiving the item in the "Contact Name" field.
* Enter the phone number of the contact person in the "Contact Phone" field.
* Enter the email of the contact person in the "Contact Email" field.
* Enter any additional comments about the contact person in the "Contact Notes" field.
* Choose the current status of the item from the "Item Status" field.
* Choose the method of delivery from the "Delivery Method" field.
* Choose whether or not a delivery service is required in the "Delivery Service" field.
* Choose whether or not the office has been relocated in the "Office Relocated" field.
* Enter the weight of the item in the "Item Weight" field.
* Enter the size of the item in the "Item Size" field.
* Enter the dimensions of the item in the "Item Dimensions" field.
* Enter the height of the item in the "Item Height" field.
* Enter the width of the item in the "Item Width" field.
* Enter the length of the item in the "Item Length" field.
* Enter the depth of the item in the "Item Depth" field.
* Enter the color of the item in the "Item Color" field.
* Enter any additional comments about the item in the "Comments" field.
