# Nail Salon Product Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers place orders for nail salon products. Please follow the instructions to complete the form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Select a product**: Choose the type of product you would like to order from the options provided (Nail Polish, Nail Gel, Nail Lacquer, or Nail Glitter).
2. **Enter product name**: Type the specific name of the product you've selected.
3. **Specify quantity**: Enter the number of the product you need.
4. **Calculate total cost**: The total cost will be calculated automatically based on your selection.
5. **Salon information (optional)**: Please note that this section is for the salon's internal use and is not required for the order. If you're an employee of the salon, you may fill in your salon's name and address for administrative purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form**: This is the header section of the form, which is not a field to fill, but rather a visual indicator that this is the main content area.
- **Product Type**: Select the type of nail product you'd like to order (e.g., Nail Polish, Nail Gel).
  * Example: Choose "Nail Polish" from the dropdown menu.
- **Product Name**: Please enter the specific name of the product you've selected (e.g., "Ruby Red").
  * Example: Type "Ruby Red" in this field.
- **Quantity**: Enter the number of products you need to order.
  * Example: Enter "5" if you need 5 units of the product.
- **Total**: This field will automatically display the total cost of your order based on the product and quantity selected. You do not need to fill this field.
- **Salon Name**: This field is for internal use by the salon and is not required for the order. Please do not fill this out unless you are an employee of the salon.
- **Address**: This field is also for internal use by the salon and is not required for the order. Please do not fill this out unless you are an employee of the salon.

## Tips
- Double-check your product selection and quantity to ensure accuracy.
- If you're an employee of the salon, please only fill in your salon's name and address if necessary.
- The total cost will be calculated automatically, so you don't need to worry about it.
