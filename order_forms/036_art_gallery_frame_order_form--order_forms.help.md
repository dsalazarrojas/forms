# Art Gallery Frame Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place orders for custom art gallery frames. It collects customer contact information, framing specifications, and order logistics details to ensure that your order is accurate and can be produced and delivered correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your contact information: Enter your full name, email address, and phone number to ensure we can contact you with any questions or updates about your order.
2. Describe your artwork: Provide a brief description of the artwork you are ordering, including its size and medium.
3. Select your frame specifications: Choose the frame material, finish, and glazing type that suits your needs.
4. Choose your shipping method: Select how you would like to receive your frame, whether in-store pickup, local courier, or ground/express shipping.
5. Confirm your order: Double-check all the details you've entered and confirm that everything is correct before proceeding with your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
- **Email Address** (Required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates about your order.
- **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted with questions or updates.
- **Billing Address** (Required): Enter your billing address where you would like the frame delivered or picked up.
- **Description of Artwork** (Required): Describe the artwork you are ordering, including its size and medium.
- **Frame Size Required** (Required): Enter the size of the frame you need, e.g., 18x24 inches.
- **Preferred Frame Material** (Required): Choose from Solid Wood, Metal - Aluminum, Polystyrene (Eco-friendly), or Ornate Gilded Wood.
- **Frame Finish or Color** (Required): Select from Matte Black, Satin White, Natural Oak, Walnut Stain, Brushed Silver, or Gold Leaf.
- **Matting Requirements** (Required): Choose Single Mat, Double Mat, No Mat (Flush mount), or Floating Mount.
- **Mat Color** (Optional): Enter the color of the mat, e.g., Off-white, Jet Black, Deep Blue.
- **Glazing (Glass/Acrylic) Selection** (Required): Choose from Standard Picture Glass, Non-Glare Glass, UV Filter Acrylic, or Museum Glass (Highest clarity).
- **How would you like to receive your frame?** (Required): Select In-store Pickup, Local Courier, Ground Shipping, or Express Shipping.
- **Any other specific requests?** (Optional): Enter any additional instructions or requests you have for your order.
- **Date of Order** (Required): Enter the date you would like to receive your frame.
- **I confirm the details above are correct and wish to proceed with the order** (Required): Double-check all the details and confirm your order before proceeding.
