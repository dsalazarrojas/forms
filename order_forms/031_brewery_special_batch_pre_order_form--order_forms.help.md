# Brewery Special Batch Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Brewery Special Batch Pre Order Form is designed to allow customers to pre-order a special batch of beer from the brewery. This form gathers essential information from the customer, including their contact details, order preferences, and payment and delivery options.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Start by filling out your contact information**:
   * Fill in your **Your Name** (`customer_name`): This is your full name.
   * Enter your **Email Address** (`email_address`): This is used for order confirmation and updates.
   * Enter your **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): This is your contact phone number.
2. **Select your membership status**:
   * Choose your **Membership Status** (`membership_status`): This determines your level of membership benefits.
3. **Enter your order details**:
   * Enter the **Special Batch Name** (`special_batch_name`): This is the name of the limited release you are ordering.
   * Select the **Expected Release Date** (`release_date`): This is when you would like to receive your order.
4. **Configure your order**:
   * Enter the **Quantity to Pre-Order** (`quantity_ordered`): This is the number of units you would like to order.
   * Select your **Package Type** (`package_type`): Choose how you would like to receive your order.
5. **Review and confirm your order**:
   * Confirm the **Estimated Total Cost** (`total_cost`): This is the total cost of your order, including any applicable taxes and fees.
6. **Select your payment and delivery options**:
   * Choose your **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select how you would like to pay for your order.
   * Select your **Payment Schedule** (`payment_timing`): Choose when you will make your payment.
7. **Finalize your order**:
   * Confirm you understand the **Cancellation Policy** (`cancellation_policy`): Review the policy and agree to its terms.
   * Choose how you would like to receive **Updates** (`notification_preference`): Select how you would like to stay informed about your order.
8. **Add any special requests**:
   * Enter any **Special Requests or Notes** (`special_requests`): This is an opportunity to add any additional information or requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for order confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Membership Status**: Select your membership level.
* **Special Batch Name**: Enter the name of the limited release you are ordering.
* **Expected Release Date**: Enter the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Quantity to Pre-Order**: Enter the number of units you would like to order.
* **Package Type**: Select how you would like to receive your order.
* **Price Per Unit**: Enter the cost per unit of your order.
* **Estimated Total Cost**: Confirm the total cost of your order.
* **Pickup or Delivery**: Select how you would like to receive your order.
* **Pickup Date**: Enter the date you would like to pick up your order.
* **Preferred Pickup Time**: Select the time you would like to pick up your order.
* **Delivery Address**: Enter the address where you would like to receive your order.
* **Delivery Special Instructions**: Enter any special delivery instructions.
* **Payment Method**: Select how you would like to pay for your order.
* **Payment Schedule**: Choose when you will make your payment.
* **I understand the Cancellation Policy**: Confirm you understand the cancellation policy.
* **Updates**: Select how you would like to stay informed about your order.
* **Special Requests or Notes**: Add any additional information or requests.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
* Review each field carefully to ensure you have entered the correct information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* This form is for ordering a special batch of beer. If you are trying to order a regular batch, please use the standard order form.
