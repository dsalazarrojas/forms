# new_line_addition_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "new_line_addition_form" is used to add new lines to an existing order, allowing authorized personnel to update the order details in a quick and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary authorization to access and update orders.
2. Select the relevant order ID from the database.
3. Enter the customer ID associated with the order.
4. Enter the item ID of the item being added to the order.
5. Provide a brief description of the item being added.
6. Enter the price of the item.
7. Specify the quantity of the item being added.
8. Enter the date and time of the order.
9. Enter any additional customer notes related to the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **order_id (1)**: Enter the ID of the order to which you want to add a new line.
* **customer_id (2)**: Enter the ID of the customer associated with the order.
* **item_id (3)**: Enter the ID of the item being added to the order.
* **description (4)**: Type a brief description of the item being added.
* **price (5)**: Enter the price of the item being added.
* **quantity (6)**: Specify the quantity of the item being added.
* **order_date (7)**: Enter the date of the order.
* **order_time (8)**: Enter the time of the order.
* **customer_notes (9)**: Enter any additional customer notes related to the order.

## Tips
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered to ensure correct order updates.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with a colleague or the relevant department for guidance.
