# Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users track and document financial transactions, such as orders or payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.
2. Enter the amount of the transaction in the "Amount" field.
3. Enter the recipient's email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter the recipient's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter the date and time of the transaction in the "Date of Transaction" and "Time of Transaction" fields, respectively.
6. Add any additional notes or comments about the transaction in the "Description" field.
7. Select the current status of the payment from the "Payment Status" dropdown menu.
8. Add any additional notes or comments about the transaction in the "Notes" field.
9. Enter the date of the payment in the "Payment Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the method of payment for this transaction, such as Bank Transfer, Credit Card, Pay Pal, or Cash App.
* **Amount** (`amount`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the amount of the transaction.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the recipient's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the recipient's phone number.
* **Date of Transaction** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the transaction.
* **Time of Transaction** (`time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the transaction.
* **Description** (`description`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the transaction.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the current status of the payment, such as Paid, Unpaid, Partially Paid, or Pending.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the transaction.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the payment.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure proper tracking and documentation of transactions.
* Use the "Payment Method" field to track the method of payment, which can be useful for reconciliation or accounting purposes.
* The "Description" field can be used to add any additional notes or comments about the transaction, such as a transaction ID or any other relevant information.
* Use the "Notes" field to add any additional notes or comments about the transaction, such as a payment receipt or any other relevant information.
* The "Payment Date" field is used to record the date of the payment, which can be useful for tracking payment due dates or payment history.
