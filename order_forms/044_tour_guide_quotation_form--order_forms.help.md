# Tour Guide Quotation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tour Guide Quotation Form is designed to collect information from users to create a quote for a tour guide service. Users are required to fill out the form with their details and preferences, and the form will generate a quote based on that information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the title of the form with a descriptive name.
2. Provide a brief description of the tour in the "Tour Details" field.
3. Estimate the price range for the tour in the "Estimated Price Range" field.
4. Choose your preferred option from the "Select a Tour" dropdown menu.
5. Provide your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
6. Review and submit the form by selecting the "Submit" option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Step**: This field is not used in the provided YAML, so let's just skip it.
* **Second Step**: This field is also not used, so we will skip it as well.
* **Tour Details**: This field is where you can provide a brief description of the tour you are interested in. Please describe the tour, including its type, duration, and any other relevant details.
* **Estimated Price Range**: In this field, please estimate the budget you are willing to pay for the tour. This will help the tour guide service quote you accordingly.
* **Select a Tour**: Please choose your preferred tour option from the dropdown menu. This will help the tour guide service tailor the tour to your needs.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so that the tour guide service can contact you with the quote and any further information.
* **Phone Number**: Provide your phone number so that the tour guide service can contact you if needed.
* **Submit**: Once you have filled out all the necessary information, select this option to submit the form and receive the quote.
* **Confirm**: After submitting the form, you will be asked to confirm whether you would like to receive the quote. Please select "Send quote to client" to receive the quote.
