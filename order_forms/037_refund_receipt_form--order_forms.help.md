# Refund Receipt Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Refund Receipt Form is used to document and track refund details for a customer after a sale has taken place. This form is used to keep track of the refund date, amount, reason for refund, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Order Form** section with the order details.
2. Enter the **Refund Date** for the refund.
3. Enter the **Refund Amount** for the customer.
4. Select the **Reason for Refund**.
5. Enter the **Customer Name**, **Customer Email**, and **Customer Phone** details.
6. Enter the **Order Number**.
7. Choose the **Refund Status** (Fully Refunded, Partially Refunded, or Not Refundable).
8. Add any **Notes** about the refund process.
9. Select the **Assigned To** (the person handling the refund).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Form** (order_form): Enter the order details as per the order form provided.
* **Refund Date** (refund_date): Enter the date of the refund.
* **Refund Amount** (refund_amount): Enter the amount to be refunded.
* **Reason for Refund** (reason_for_refund): Select the reason for the refund.
* **Customer Name** (customer_name): Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Email** (customer_email): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (customer_phone): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Order Number** (order_number): Enter the order number related to the refund.
* **Refund Status** (refund_status): Select the status of the refund (Fully Refunded, Partially Refunded, or Not Refundable).
* **Notes** (notes): Add any notes about the refund process.
* **Assigned To** (assigned_to): Select the person handling the refund.
