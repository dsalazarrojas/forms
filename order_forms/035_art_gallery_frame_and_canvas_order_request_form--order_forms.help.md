# Art Gallery Frame And Canvas Order Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers to request a quote for framing an artwork at our gallery. Please follow the instructions below to complete the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in all required fields (marked as **Required**) to the best of your abilities.
2. For fields where options are provided, select the one that best suits your needs.
3. If unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask our staff for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (Customer Name): Enter the name of the person or organization placing the order.
* **Email Address** (Email Address): Enter the email address where you can be reached for further inquiries or updates.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number): Enter your phone number for any follow-up conversations.
* **Title of the Artwork** (Title of the Artwork): Enter a brief title of the artwork being framed.
* **Medium of the Artwork** (Medium of the Artwork): Select the medium or type of material used to create the artwork (e.g., oil on canvas, watercolor on paper).
* **Exact Dimensions of the Artwork (H x W)** (Exact Dimensions of the Artwork): Enter the exact height and width of the artwork, including units (e.g., 20 x 24 inches).
* **What are you ordering?** (What are you ordering?): Select what you are ordering (e.g., custom frame, canvas, or mounting only).
* **Frame Style Preference** (Frame Style Preference): Select your preferred style of frame (e.g., modern black wood, classic gold leaf, etc.).
* **Glass/Acrylic Type** (Glass/Acrylic Type): Select the type of glass or acrylic you prefer for your frame (e.g., standard clear, UV protection, museum grade, etc.).
* **Canvas Stretcher Depth (If applicable)** (Canvas Stretcher Depth (If applicable)): Select the depth of the canvas stretcher if you are ordering a canvas (e.g., standard, gallery wrap, deep, etc.).
* **Additional Customization Notes** (Additional Customization Notes): Enter any additional customization requests or notes for your order (e.g., specific matting colors, spacers, etc.).
* **Requested Completion Date** (Requested Completion Date): Enter the date when you would like to receive your order.
* **Preference for Receiving Order** (Preference for Receiving Order): Select how you would like to receive your order (e.g., local pickup, delivery, shipping).
* **I approve the above specifications and understand this is a request for a quote** (I approve the above specifications and understand this is a request for a quote): Select this option to indicate that you have reviewed and agreed upon the specifications for your order.
* **Date of Request** (Date of Request): Enter the date you are submitting this request.

Please review your submission carefully before sending it to our gallery. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
