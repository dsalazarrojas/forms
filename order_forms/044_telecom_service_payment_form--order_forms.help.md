# Telecom Service Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telecom Service Payment Form is designed to collect payment information and details for telecom services. This form is used to process payments for billing or usage purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the service type (e.g., Billing or Usage) that the payment is related to.
2. Enter the account number for the relevant service.
3. Enter your name.
4. Enter your address.
5. Enter the amount of the payment.
6. Enter your email address.
7. Enter your phone number.
8. Choose the payment method (e.g., Credit Card or Bank Transfer).
9. Select the payment status (e.g., Paid or Unpaid).
10. Enter the date of the payment.
11. Enter the time of the payment.
12. Enter the payment reference number.
13. Enter the account balance.
14. Select the account status (e.g., Active or Dormant).
15. Enter any additional payment method details.
16. Enter your account number.
17. Enter the name of the account holder.
18. Enter the account name.
19. Enter the payment amount.
20. Enter the payment date.
21. Enter the payment time.
22. Select the second payment method (e.g., Cash or Check).
23. Select the second payment status (e.g., Paid or Unpaid).
24. Enter any payment details.
25. Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of service the payment is related to (e.g., Billing or Usage).
* **Account Number** (`account_number`, text, required): Enter the account number associated with the relevant service.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name for reference.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address for billing or other relevant details.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, required): Enter the amount to be paid.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact or billing purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact or billing purposes.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose the payment method (e.g., Credit Card or Bank Transfer).
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required): Select the payment status (e.g., Paid or Unpaid).
* **Date of Payment** (`date_of_payment`, date, required): Enter the date of the payment.
* **Time of Payment** (`time_of_payment`, time, required): Enter the time of the payment.
* **Payment Reference Number** (`payment_reference_number`, number, required): Enter the payment reference number.
* **Account Balance** (`account_balance`, number, required): Enter the account balance.
* **Account Status** (`account_status`, select_one, required): Select the account status (e.g., Active or Dormant).
* **Payment Method Details** (`payment_method_details`, text, required): Enter any additional payment method details.
* **Account Number** (`account_number`, text, required): Enter your account number.
* **Account Holder** (`account_holder`, text, required): Enter the name of the account holder.
* **Account Name** (`account_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the account.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required): Enter the payment amount.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required): Enter the payment date.
* **Payment Time** (`payment_time`, time, required): Enter the payment time.
* **Payment Method 2** (`payment_method2`, select_one, required): Select the second payment method (e.g., Cash or Check).
* **Payment Status 2** (`payment_status2`, select_one, required): Select the second payment status (e.g., Paid or Unpaid).
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, required): Enter any additional payment details.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately.
* Double-check the payment details to ensure they are correct.
* Review the form before submitting it to ensure all information is complete and accurate.
