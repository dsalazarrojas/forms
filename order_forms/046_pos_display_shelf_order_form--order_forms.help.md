# POS Display Shelf Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The POS Display Shelf Order Form is used to collect information for POS Display Shelf orders from staff members. This form should only be used by authorized staff to place orders for products on the POS display shelf.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user information in the **User Input** field. This is where you can enter your name, department, or other relevant details.
2. Provide **Order Details** about the order, such as the product description or quantity needed.
3. Enter the **Shipping Address** where the order will be delivered.
4. Choose the **Payment Method** used for the order.
5. Enter the date and time of the order in the **Date** and **Time** fields.
6. If necessary, enter a **Phone** number for communication.
7. Enter a valid **Email** address for contact.
8. Add any relevant **Note** or **Order Notes** for the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input**: Enter your name, department, or any other relevant information to help identify you as the person placing the order.
* **Order Details**: Enter a brief description of the order, including the product and quantity needed.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the address where the order will be delivered.
* **Payment Method**: Choose the payment method used for the order, such as credit card or bank transfer.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the order in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the order in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Phone**: Enter a phone number for communication with the customer or delivery team.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address for contact.
* **Note**: Enter any relevant notes or comments about the order.
* **Order Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the order that you think the customer or delivery team should be aware of.
