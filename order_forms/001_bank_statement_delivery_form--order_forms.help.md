# Bank Statement Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Bank Statement Delivery Form" is used by customers to request delivery of their bank statements. It is an essential tool to provide customers with the necessary information to access their financial history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name as it appears on your account.
2. Enter your account number without any spaces.
3. Select the correct account type (Checking, Savings, Business, or Other).
4. Choose the start and end dates for the requested statement period.
5. Select your preferred delivery method (Secure Upload, Email, or Physical Pickup).
6. If uploading, provide a secure link or reference code.
7. Enter your contact email address for delivery notifications.
8. Include your contact phone number, including the country code if applicable.
9. Read and agree to the consent and authorization terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Full Name (Required)**: Enter your full legal name as it appears on your account.
* **Account Number (Required)**: Enter the full account number without any spaces.
* **Account Type (Required)**: Select the type of account you wish to access (Checking, Savings, Business, or Other).
* **Statement Period Start Date (Required)**: Choose the start date for the requested statement period.
* **Statement Period End Date (Required)**: Choose the end date for the requested statement period.
* **Delivery Method (Required)**: Select your preferred delivery method (Secure Upload, Email, or Physical Pickup).
* **Upload Reference or File Link (Optional)**: If uploading, provide a secure link or reference code for the bank's internal reference.
* **Contact Email (Required)**: Enter your email address for delivery notifications.
* **Contact Phone Number (Optional)**: Include your country code if applicable.
* **Consent and Authorization (Required)**: Read and agree to the terms of authorization for the bank to process your request.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid delays in processing your request.
* Double-check your account number and account type to ensure they match your account information.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact our support team for assistance.
