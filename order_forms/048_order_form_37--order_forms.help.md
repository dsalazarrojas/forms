# order_form_37 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The order_form_37 is used to gather information for order processing. Fill out this form with the customer's delivery address, name, order details, shipping cost, and email address.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the order_form_37, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the required information for the customer.
2. Fill in the fields as per the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, not required): Enter the customer's delivery address, including the street address and any additional instructions for delivery.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, not required): Enter the customer's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the order, including any specific details or instructions.
* **Shipping Cost** (`shipping_cost`, number, not required): Enter the estimated shipping cost for the order.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, not required): Enter the customer's email address for communication.
