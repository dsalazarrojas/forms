# Restocking Automation Quote Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to request a quote for automated restocking services. Fill out this form to provide the necessary information for our team to prepare a quote for you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the service you are requesting a quote for from the drop-down menu in the "Automated Restocking Service ID" field.
2. Enter the quantity of the item you are requesting restocking for.
3. Select the reorder frequency from the options provided.
4. Enter the delivery frequency and delivery date for the restocked items.
5. Select the delivery time for the restocking service.
6. Enter the name and email of the person requesting the service.
7. Enter the phone number of the person requesting the service (optional).
8. Enter any additional notes about the service or order (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requesting Restocking Service**: Enter a brief description of the service you are requesting. This will help us understand the type of service you need.
* **Service Details**: Enter any additional details about the service you are requesting. This can include information about the type of items being restocked, etc.
* **Automated Restocking Service ID**: Select the specific automated restocking service you are requesting. This will help us determine which service plan to use.
* **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the item you are requesting restocking for.
* **Reorder Frequency**: Enter the frequency at which you would like the items to be reordered. For example, "monthly" or "quarterly".
* **Delivery Frequency**: Enter the frequency at which you would like the restocked items to be delivered. For example, "weekly" or "bi-weekly".
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the date on which you would like the restocking service to start.
* **Delivery Time**: Enter the time of day at which you would like the restocked items to be delivered.
* **Requested By**: Enter the name of the person requesting the service.
* **Requested Date**: Enter the date on which the service was requested.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person (optional).
* **Automated Restocking Service Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the service or order.
* **Order Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the order.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate quote preparation.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your account representative for assistance.
* Please review and verify the information you enter before submitting the form.
