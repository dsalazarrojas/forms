# University Sports Equipment Order Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help University departments request sports equipment for their activities. It is a simple and straightforward process to ensure that the equipment is properly ordered and reviewed before being issued to the department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the required fields with your information.
* Select your department from the list of options.
* Choose the type of equipment you need from the list of options.
* Select the date you would like to receive the equipment.
* Choose the location where you would like to pick up the equipment.
* Specify if the equipment is in good, bad, or fair condition.
* Enter your contact phone number and email.
* Add any additional notes about your request.
* Fill out the assigned tool field with the name of the equipment you are requesting.
* Fill out the created by and updated by fields with your name and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)**: Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name (2)**: Please enter your last name.
* **University (3)**: Please select your department name.
* **Department (4)**: Please select your department from the options provided.
* **Equipment Type (5)**: Please select the type of equipment you need.
* **Date of order (6)**: Please enter the date you would like to receive the equipment in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Pickup Location (7)**: Please enter the location where you would like to pick up the equipment.
* **Return Location (8)**: Please enter the location where you would like to return the equipment when you are done using it.
* **Equipment Condition (9)**: Please select the condition of the equipment you are returning.
* **Contact Phone (10)**: Please enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Contact Email (11)**: Please enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Notes (12)**: Please add any additional notes about your request.
* **Assigned Tool (13)**: Please enter the name of the equipment you are requesting.
* **Created By (14)**: Please enter your name and date when you create this request.
* **Updated By (15)**: Please enter your name and date when you update this request.
