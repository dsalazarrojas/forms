# E Book Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E Book Requisition Form is a form used to request e-books from a catalog. The form is designed to collect information from the requester to facilitate the processing of the e-book request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the order options to select the desired e-books.
2. Fill in the required fields:
   - E-book Title
   - Author
   - E-book Edition
   - E-book Format
   - E-book Subject
   - E-book Status
   - Requester Name
   - Requester Email
   - Requester Phone
   - Requester Institution

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order e-book** (`order_form`, Select Multiple):
	+ Choose the e-books you wish to order.
* **E-book Title** (`e_book_title`, Text):
	+ Enter the title of the requested e-book.
* **Author** (`author`, Text):
	+ Enter the author of the requested e-book.
* **E-book Edition** (`e_book_edition`, Select One):
	+ Select the desired edition of the e-book.
* **E-book Format** (`e_book_format`, Select One):
	+ Select the desired format of the e-book.
* **E-book Subject** (`e_book_subject`, Select Multiple):
	+ Choose the subject of the e-book.
* **E-book Status** (`e_book_status`, Select One):
	+ Select the status of the e-book.
* **E-book Price** (`e_book_price`, Number):
	+ Enter the price of the requested e-book.
* **E-book Publisher** (`e_book_publisher`, Text):
	+ Enter the publisher of the requested e-book.
* **E-book Year** (`e_book_year`, Number):
	+ Enter the year of the requested e-book.
* **E-book ISBN** (`e_book_isbn`, Text):
	+ Enter the ISBN of the requested e-book.
* **E-book URL** (`e_book_url`, Text):
	+ Enter the URL of the requested e-book.
* **Request Date** (`e_book_request_date`, Date):
	+ Enter the date you are requesting the e-book.
* **Due Date** (`e_book_due_date`, Date):
	+ Enter the due date for the requested e-book.
* **Order Status** (`e_book_order_status`, Select One):
	+ Select the status of the e-book order.
* **Requester Name** (`e_book_requester_name`, Text):
	+ Enter your name.
* **Requester Email** (`e_book_requester_email`, Email):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Requester Phone** (`e_book_requester_phone`, Text):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Requester Institution** (`e_book_requester_institution`, Text):
	+ Enter your institution's name.

Note: The optional fields are not required to be filled in but it is recommended to fill them for a complete and accurate e-book request.
