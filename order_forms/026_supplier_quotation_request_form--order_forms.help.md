# Supplier Quotation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a quote from a supplier for their products or services. It is a tool for the supplier to provide a detailed quote that includes all necessary information for the buying organization to make an informed decision.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the supplier_quotation_request form with the necessary information about your quote.
2. Select the options that best describe your quote in the select_one field.
3. Add any additional information you would like to provide in the note field.
4. Provide a date and time for the expected completion of the quote.
5. Click submit to send the completed form to the buying organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Quotation Request** (`supplier_quotation_request_form_1`, text, optional): This is the main quote request field. Please provide a detailed description of the products or services you are offering. This is the most important field for the buying organization to understand what you are offering.
* **Select One** (`supplier_quotation_request_form_2`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options that best describes your quote. This field helps the buying organization understand how you are offering your products or services.
	+ Option 1: This is the first option for your quote.
	+ Option 2: This is the second option for your quote.
	+ Option 3: This is the third option for your quote.
* **Note** (`supplier_quotation_request_form_3`, note, optional): This is an additional field for you to provide any additional information about your quote. You can use this field to provide any relevant details that will help the buying organization make an informed decision.
* **Date** (`supplier_quotation_request_form_4`, date, optional): Provide a date for the expected completion of the quote. This is the date when you expect to deliver the products or services.
* **Time** (`supplier_quotation_request_form_5`, time, optional): Provide a time for the expected completion of the quote. This is the time when you expect to deliver the products or services.

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide all necessary information in the supplier_quotation_request field.
* Use the select_one field to select only one option that best describes your quote.
* You can add any additional information in the note field.
* Make sure to provide a valid date and time for the expected completion of the quote.
