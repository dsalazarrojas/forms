# Promotional Counter Stand Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Promotional Counter Stand Order Form is used to place orders for promotional counter stands with your business or organization. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the order (if required).
2. Upload your artwork or provide a link to it (if applicable).
3. Enter the quantity of counter stands needed.
4. Select the material type of the counter stand.
5. Enter the dimensions of the counter stand (height, width, depth).
6. Choose the color(s) of the counter stand.
7. Specify the desired delivery date and time.
8. Determine the shipping cost (if required).
9. Set the order status (confirmed or not confirmed).
10. Choose the payment method (online or offline payment).
11. Enter the order amount (if required).
11. Add any additional order notes.
12. Enter your name and email address for communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_header** (Note): This is the title of the order. Please enter a clear and concise title to help us quickly identify the order.
* **artwork** (Text): This is the main image or design that will be printed on the counter stand. You can upload your artwork or provide a link to it if it's already online. Make sure it's in the correct format (e.g., JPEG, PNG, PDF).
* **artwork_url** (Text): If you've provided a link to your artwork, enter the URL here so we can access it.
* **quantity** (Number): Enter the number of counter stands you need.
* **material** (Select One): Choose the material type for the counter stand from the dropdown options: Counter stand, Counter stand 1, or Counter stand 2.
* **height** (Number): Enter the height of the counter stand in inches.
* **width** (Number): Enter the width of the counter stand in inches.
* **depth** (Number): Enter the depth of the counter stand in inches.
* **color** (Select Multiple): Choose the color(s) of the counter stand from the dropdown options: Red, Blue, or Green. You can select multiple colors if necessary.
* **delivery_date** (Date): Enter the desired delivery date for the counter stands.
* **delivery_time** (Time): Enter the desired delivery time for the counter stands.
* **shipping_cost** (Number): Enter the shipping cost, if required.
* **order_status** (Select One): Set the order status to "Confirmed" or "Not confirmed" depending on whether the order is confirmed or not.
* **payment_method** (Select One): Choose the payment method: Online payment or Offline payment.
* **payment_amount** (Number): Enter the payment amount, if required.
* **order_notes** (Note): Add any additional notes about the order, such as special instructions or requests.
* **customer_name** (Text): Enter your name for communication purposes.
* **customer_email** (Email): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **customer_email** (Note): This field will be used for communication regarding the order.
