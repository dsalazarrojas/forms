# primitive_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The primitive_order_form is a simple form designed to collect basic customer information and order details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer information section, including any relevant details about the customer.
2. Provide product details in the product_details section.
3. Enter payment information in the payment_info section.
4. Complete the shipping information in the shipping_info section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_info** (`customer_info`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for entering customer information such as name, email, and contact number.
* **product_details** (`product_details`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter any relevant details about the product being ordered.
* **payment_info** (`payment_info`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter payment information such as payment method and amount.
* **shipping_info** (`shipping_info`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter shipping details including shipping address and any other relevant information.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate data collection.
* If a field is not required, feel free to fill it in if you have the necessary information.
* Review your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact us at form@gic.mx for assistance.
