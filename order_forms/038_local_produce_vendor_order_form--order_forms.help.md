# Local Produce Vendor Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for local produce vendors to place orders with the company. It gathers information about the order details, shipping details, contact information, and notes relevant to the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Order Details** page (id: 1) with the specific details of the order you want to place.
2. Enter your **Shipping Details** (id: 2) to ensure that the order is delivered correctly.
3. Provide your **Contact Information** (id: 3) so we can get in touch with you.
4. Add any additional **Notes** (id: 4) you need to provide more context about the order.
5. Select the correct **Vendor Info** (id: 5) to associate with the order.
6. Choose the current **Status** (id: 6) of the order from the options.
7. Select the date and time of the order on the **Date** (id: 7) and **Time** (id: 8) fields.
8. Enter the **Order Total** (id: 9) for the order.
9. Choose the **Payment Method** (id: 10) for the order.
10. Enter your **Email** (id: 11) and **Phone** (id: 12) contact information.
11. Provide your **Address** (id: 13) for delivery.
12. Add any additional **Order Notes** (id: 14, 15, 17, 18, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, 7, 9) you need to provide more context.
13. Select the **Assigned Tool** (id: 15) to associate with the order.
14. Enter the **Vendor Name** (id: 16) associated with the order.
15. Add any additional **Comments** (id: 17, 18, 21, 22).

* **Order Details (id: 1)**: Enter the specific details of the order you want to place.

* **Vendor Info (id: 5)**: Select the correct vendor information associated with the order.
* **Status (id: 6)**: Select the current status of the order from the options.
* **Date (id: 7)**: Enter the date of the order.
* **Time (id: 8)**: Enter the time of the order.
* **Order Total (id: 9)**: Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Payment Method (id: 10)**: Select the payment method for the order.
* **Email (id: 11)**: Enter your email contact information.
* **Phone (id: 12)**: Enter your phone contact information.
* **Address (id: 13)**: Enter your address for delivery.
* **Order Notes 2 (id: 14)**: Enter any additional order notes.
* **Assigned Tool (id: 15)**: Select the tool associated with the order.
* **Vendor Name (id: 16)**: Enter the name of the vendor associated with the order.
* **Additional Notes (id: 17)**: Enter any additional notes.

* **Order Notes 2 (id: 20)**: Enter any additional notes.
