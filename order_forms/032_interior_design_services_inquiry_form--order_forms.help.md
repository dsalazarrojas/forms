# interior_design_services_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from clients for interior design services. It helps us understand their needs, provide better services, and ensure a successful collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Client" field with the client's name and other relevant details.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Contact" field.
3. Describe your project in the "Project" field, including any relevant details.
4. In the "Description" field, provide a detailed description of your project.
5. Choose the purpose of your contact from the "Purpose" field.
6. Select your preferred contact method from the "Contact Method" field.
7. Enter a valid email address in the "Email" field.
8. Specify the date and time you are available to meet or discuss.
9. Select the files you would like to upload from the "File Uploads" field.
10. Confirm your selection by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client** (`client_info`, text, required: false): Enter the client's name and other relevant details.
* **Contact** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Provide your contact information.
* **Project** (`project_details`, text, required: false): Describe your project in detail.
* **Description** (`project_description`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of your project.
* **Purpose** (`contact_purpose`, select_one, required: false): Choose the purpose of your contact.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your preferred contact method.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Specify the date and time you are available to meet or discuss.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Specify the time you are available to meet or discuss.
* **File Uploads** (`file_uploads`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the files you would like to upload.
* **File Uploads 2** (`file_uploads_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Select additional files you would like to upload.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): Confirm your selection by clicking the "Submit" button.
