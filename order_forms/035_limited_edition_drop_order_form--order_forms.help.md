# Limited Edition Drop Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for customers to place orders for limited edition products. It allows customers to provide information about themselves and their order details.

## How To Complete This Form
To place an order, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First name.
2. Enter your Last name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Select the Order Date for your order.
5. Provide Order Details about your order.
6. Enter the Product ID of the product you are ordering.
7. Select the type of drop you are ordering (Sneakers, Tees, or Hoodies).
8. Enter your Shipping Address.
9. Enter the City where you will be shipping from.
10. Select the State where you will be shipping from (California, New York, or Texas).
11. Enter the Zip code for the shipping address.
12. Select the Country where you will be shipping from (USA, Canada, or Mexico).
13. Select the Payment Method for your order (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
14. Enter the Amount of your order.
15. Enter the Quantity of the products you are ordering.
16. Select the Shipping Method for your order (Express, Standard, or Ground).
17. Add any Order Notes.
18. Confirm your order by selecting "Yes".
19. Select the date and time you confirm your order.
20. Enter a revised order date if needed.
21. Enter a time to confirm your order.
22. Enter the time your order will ship.
23. Add any additional Notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter the first name that will be used to fulfill the order.
* **Last name**: Enter the last name that will be used to fulfill the order.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address to contact you about your order.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date you would like to receive your order by.
* **Order Details**: Enter any special details about your order.
* **Product ID**: Enter the number associated with the product you are ordering.
* **Drop Type**: Select the type of drop you are ordering (Sneakers, Tees, or Hoodies).
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the address where you would like to ship the order to.
* **City**: Enter the city where you will be shipping from.
* **State**: Select the state where you will be shipping from (California, New York, or Texas).
* **Zip**: Enter the zip code for the shipping address.
* **Country**: Select the country where you will be shipping from (USA, Canada, or Mexico).
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method for your order (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
* **Amount**: Enter the total amount of the order.
* **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the product you are ordering.
* **Shipping Method**: Select the shipping method for your order (Express, Standard, or Ground).
* **Order Notes**: Add any additional notes about your order.
* **Confirm**: Confirm your order by selecting "Yes".
* **Confirm Date**: Enter the date and time you confirm your order.
* **Order Date Revised**: Enter a revised order date if needed.
* **Confirm Time**: Enter the time to confirm your order.
* **Order Time**: Enter the time your order will ship.
* **Shipping Time**: Enter the time your order will ship.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about your order.
