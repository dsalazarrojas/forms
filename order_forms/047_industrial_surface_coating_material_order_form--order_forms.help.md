# Industrial Surface Coating Material Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information for industrial surface coating material orders. It is used for business-to-business or business-to-customer communication for the purpose of fulfilling orders.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please fill out the form carefully, providing as much detail as possible to ensure accurate order fulfillment.
* If you have any additional orders or changes to existing orders, please create new forms for each.
* Review your form before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Form** (`order_form`, text, optional): This is the main section of the form where you can provide a brief description of the order.
* **Coating Form** (`coating_form`, text, optional): This section is for describing the specific coating material or type you are ordering.
* **Shipping Form** (`shipping_form`, text, optional): This section is for providing shipping information, such as shipping address or logistics details.
* **Order Form Notes** (`order_form_notes`, note, required): This section is for providing any additional notes or instructions about the order. This field is required.
