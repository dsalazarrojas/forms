# Packing Materials Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Packing Materials Requisition Form is a document used to request packing materials for a customer's order. This form is used to gather essential information from the customer, including their details, materials needed, and shipping information. It will be reviewed by the assigned staff member for processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **customer name** and **customer email**.
2. Provide a brief description of the **materials requested**.
3. Select the **packaging supplies** needed for your order (e.g., bubble mailer, bubble bag, or box).
4. Enter your **shipping address**, including **shipping city** and **shipping state**.
5. If applicable, provide additional comments about your order.
6. Assign a staff member to handle your order by selecting one of the available options (**chatjimmy** or **jenny**).
7. Finally, provide the **review comments** for the assigned staff member.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer name (1)**: Enter the name of the customer for whom the materials are being requisitioned.
* **customer email (2)**: Provide the customer's email address.
* **materials requested (3)**: Describe the packing materials you need.
* **requested date (4)**: Date when you need the packing materials.
* **packaging supplies (5)**: Select the packaging type needed for your order (bubble mailer, bubble bag, or box).
* **shipping address (6)**: Enter your shipping address.
* **shipping city (7)**: Enter the city for your shipping address.
* **shipping state (8)**: Enter the state for your shipping address.
* **shipping zip (9)**: Enter your zip code.
* **comments (10)**: Add any additional comments about your order.
* **assigned to (11)**: Select a staff member to handle your order (chatjimmy or jenny).
* **assigned date (12)**: Date when the staff member was assigned to handle your order.
* **review comments (13)**: Add comments for the staff member to review and process your order.
