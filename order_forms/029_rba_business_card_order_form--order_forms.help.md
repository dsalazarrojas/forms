# RBA Business Card Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to place an order for custom business cards with various customizations. Fill out the form to provide the necessary information to create your business cards.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your company name in the Company field.
4. Enter your email address in the Email field.
5. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
6. Enter your address in the Address field.
7. Select the type of card you want (Card Type).
8. Enter the quantity of cards you want to order.
9. Select the color of the cards (card_color).
10. Select the type of order (order_type).
11. If you want to customize the card with a custom image, enter the URL of the image in the custom_image field.
12. If you want to include a custom message on the card, enter the message in the custom_message field.
13. Select the size of the card (card_size).
14. Select the material of the card (card_material).
15. Select the finish of the card (finish).
16. Select the status of the order (order_status).
17. Finally, click submit to place your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required): Enter your company name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your address.
* **Card Type** (`card_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the type of card you want.
*   • Card Type: Choose the type of card you want (e.g., Business Card).
*   • Card Size: Choose the size of the card you want (e.g., US Business).
*   • Card Material: Choose the material of the card you want (e.g., Plastic).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, optional): Enter the quantity of cards you want to order.
* **Card Color** (`card_color`, `select_one`, optional): Select the color of the cards.
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of order.
*   • Custom: For custom order requests.
*   • Standard: For standard orders.
* **Custom Image** (`custom_image`, `text`, optional): If you have a custom image, enter the URL of the image.
* **Custom Message** (`custom_message`, `text`, optional): If you want to include a custom message on the card, enter it here.
* **Custom Image URL** (`custom_image_url`, `text`, optional): If you want to include a custom image on the card, enter the URL of the image.
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of order.
*   • Custom: For custom order requests.
*   • Standard: For standard orders.
* **Card Size** (`card_size`, `select_one`, optional): Select the size of the card.
*   • US Business: For US business cards.
*   • Square: For square cards.
*   • Standard: For standard cards.
* **Card Material** (`card_material`, `select_one`, optional): Select the material of the card.
*   • Plastic: For plastic cards.
*   • PVC: For PVC cards.
*   • Paper: For paper cards.
* **Finish** (`finish`, `select_one`, optional): Select the finish of the card.
*   • Glossy: For glossy finish.
*   • Matte: For matte finish.
*   • Embossed: For embossed finish.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of the order.
*   • In Review: For orders in review.
*   • In Progress: For orders in progress.
*   • Completed: For completed orders.
* **Order ID** (`order_id`, `text`, optional): Enter a unique order ID (not required for this form).

Note: The fields marked as `required` are mandatory fields that need to be filled out to place an order. The `optional` fields are fields that are not required to place an order but can be filled out for customizing the order.
