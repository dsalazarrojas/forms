# Job Work Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for creating new job work orders, which are essential documents for managing work orders efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the **Job Title** in the first field. This is a required field.
2. Enter the **Customer Name** in the second field. Although not required, it's useful for reference.
3. Enter the **Job Order Form Description** in the third field. This field is optional and can be used to provide more details about the job order.
4. Upload any relevant **Files** in the fourth field. The number of files that can be uploaded is not specified.
5. Enter the **Payment Amount** in the fifth field. This is a required field.
6. Select a **Payment Method** from the available options in the sixth field. This field is required and can be one of the following: Cash, Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal.
7. Enter the **Due Date** for the job work order in the seventh field. This field is required.
8. Click on the **Submit Button** in the last field to complete and submit the form. This field is not required but is necessary for submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title** (text, required): Enter the title of the job work order.
* **Customer Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the customer associated with the job work order.
* **Job Order Form Description** (text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the job work order.
* **File Uploads** (number, optional): Upload any relevant files related to the job work order.
* **Payment Amount** (number, required): Enter the amount to be paid for the job work order.
* **Payment Method** (select one, required): Select the payment method for the job work order from the available options.
* **Due Date** (date, required): Enter the due date for the job work order.
* **Submit Button** (note, not required): Click on this button to complete and submit the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields completely and accurately.
* Use the **Job Order Form Description** field to provide additional details that may be relevant to the job work order.
* The **File Uploads** field can be used to upload multiple files if necessary.
* Be careful when selecting the **Payment Method**, as it is a required field.
