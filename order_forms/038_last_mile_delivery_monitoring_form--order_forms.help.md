This help guide explains the purpose and usage of each field in the last mile delivery monitoring form. The form is used to collect information about deliveries, including customer feedback and tracking numbers, to help monitor and optimize the delivery process. The guide walks the user through the steps to complete the form, including entering delivery information, providing feedback, and reviewing the entered data. Each field is explained in a concise and practical manner, making it easy for the user to understand its purpose and usage.
