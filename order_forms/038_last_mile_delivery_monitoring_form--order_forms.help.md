# last_mile_delivery_monitoring_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The last mile delivery monitoring form is used to collect information related to deliveries, such as customer feedback and tracking numbers, to help monitor and optimize the delivery process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the required information about the delivery, such as the tracking number, in the "Delivery Info" page.
2. Provide feedback about the delivery experience to help us improve our service in the "Customer Feedback" page.
3. Enter the estimated arrival time of the delivery.
4. Review the information you entered to ensure it is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Delivery Info**: This field is used to enter the tracking number, which can help us track the delivery's status and provide you with updates.
* **Customer Feedback**: Please select all that apply to provide feedback about your delivery experience, such as whether you received your order on time and whether it was delivered as expected.
* **Tracking Number**: Enter the tracking number for your delivery, which can be found on the shipping label or provided by the courier service.
* **Estimated Arrival Time**: Enter the estimated time of arrival for your delivery, which helps us track the delivery's progress and provide you with accurate arrival information.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the correct tracking number to ensure accurate tracking.
* Provide as much feedback as possible to help us improve our service.
* If you have any issues or concerns about your delivery, please contact our support team.
