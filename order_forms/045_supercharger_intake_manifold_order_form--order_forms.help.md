# Supercharger Intake Manifold Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supercharger Intake Manifold Order Form is designed to collect order details and customer information from customers ordering a Supercharger Intake Manifold.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your order details in the "Order Details" field.
2. Provide your customer information in the "Customer Info" field.
3. Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supercharger Intake Manifold Order** (`supercharger_intake_manifold_order_form_page`, text, not required): A field to enter your order details.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, not required): A field to enter your customer information.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, not required): A field to enter additional order details.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, not required): A field to enter your shipping address. 

## Tips
- Please ensure you enter all relevant information to ensure timely and accurate order processing.
- If you need assistance with your order, contact your sales representative or customer support team.
- This form is for orders only; if you have any other inquiry, please use the contact form provided on the website.
