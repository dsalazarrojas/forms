# hoodie_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact, order, and payment information from customers, allowing for efficient and accurate processing of their orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name in the "What is your name?" field.
2. Select your order type from the "What is your order type?" dropdown menu.
3. Choose how you would like to be contacted from the "How would you like to be contacted?" dropdown menu.
4. Provide a brief description of your order in the "Can you provide a brief description of your order?" field, if desired.
5. Select your payment method from the "What is your payment method?" dropdown menu.
6. Enter your delivery address in the "What is your delivery address?" field.
7. Provide a contact phone number, if applicable, in the "Can you provide a contact phone number?" field.
8. Add any additional comments or details about your order in the "Is there anything else you would like to include in your order?" field.
9. Review and confirm your order details in the "Can you confirm your order details?" field.
10. Optionally, add any special delivery requests or notes in the "Do you have any special requests for delivery?" field.
11. Select your preferred delivery method from the "How would you like to receive your order?" dropdown menu, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is your name?** (text, required): Enter your name to identify yourself.
- **What is your order type?** (select_one, required): Select the type of order you would like to place.
- **How would you like to be contacted?** (select_multiple, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, or Message).
- **Can you provide a brief description of your order?** (text, optional): Provide a brief description of your order, if desired.
- **What is your payment method?** (select_one, required): Select the payment method you would like to use.
- **What is your delivery address?** (text, required): Enter your delivery address.
- **Can you provide a contact phone number?** (text, optional): Provide a contact phone number, if applicable.
- **Is there anything else you would like to include in your order?** (text, optional): Add any additional comments or details about your order.
- **Can you confirm your order details?** (text, required): Review and confirm your order details.
- **Do you have any special requests for delivery?** (text, optional): Add any special delivery requests or notes.
- **How would you like to receive your order?** (select_multiple, optional): Select the preferred delivery method (Delivery Method 1, Delivery Method 2, or Delivery Method 3).
- **Can you provide a note?** (checkbox, optional): Add a note or comment to your order, if desired.
