# Legal Retainer Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about payments made by clients for legal services on a retainer basis. It helps track client payments and ensures that retainer fees are paid on time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Select the correct payment method (Bank, Credit Card, Cash, or PayPal).
3. Enter the payment date.
4. Specify the payment amount (if applicable).
5. Indicate the payment status (Paid, Partially Paid, or Unpaid).
6. Include any relevant terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter the client's name as it appears in our records.
* **Payment Method**: Choose the method by which the client made the payment (Bank, Credit Card, Cash, or PayPal).
* **Payment Date**: Enter the date on which the payment was made.
* **Amount**: Enter the amount paid by the client.
* **Payment Status**: Indicate whether the payment is Paid, Partially Paid, or Unpaid.
* **Retainer Fee**: Enter the retainer fee paid by the client (if applicable).
* **Terms and Conditions**: Describe any terms and conditions related to the payment.
* **Cancellation Policy**: Describe the policy for cancelling payments.
* **Disputes**: Describe any disputes related to the payment.
* **Payment Cancellation**: Describe any cancellation of payment.
