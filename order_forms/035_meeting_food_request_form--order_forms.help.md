# Meeting Food Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meeting Food Request Form is a tool to collect information about your food preferences and dietary restrictions for meetings. This form will help us better understand your needs and provide you with suitable food options during your meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of food you would like to have at your meeting:
	* Select one or more options from the "Meeting Food" list (Food, Beverage, Snack).
2. Choose your category:
	* Select one or more options from the "Category" list (Food, Beverage, Snack).
3. Specify any dietary restrictions you have:
	* Select one or more options from the "Dietary Restrictions" list (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, nuts-free, dairy-free).
4. Indicate your food preferences:
	* Select one or more options from the "Food Preferences" list (meat, fish, chicken, vegan, vegetarian).
5. If you have any food allergies, please note them in the "Food Allergies" field.
6. Provide your name:
	* Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on meeting materials.
7. Enter your email address:
	* Provide the email address we can use to contact you about meeting details.
8. Enter your phone number:
	* Provide your phone number so we can contact you about meeting updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Food Request Form** (`meeting_food_request_form`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of food you would like to have at your meeting.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more options from the category list.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, optional): Specify any dietary restrictions you have, such as vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, or nuts-free.
* **Food Preferences** (`food_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate your food preferences, such as meat, fish, chicken, or vegan.
* **Food Allergies** (`food_allergies`, note, optional): If you have any food allergies, please note them here.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on meeting materials.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address we can use to contact you about meeting details.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about meeting updates.

## Tips
- Please select only the options that apply to you to ensure accurate and efficient processing.
- If you're unsure about any options, feel free to ask for clarification.
- This form is for your comfort and convenience during meetings; please provide honest and detailed information to help us serve you better.
