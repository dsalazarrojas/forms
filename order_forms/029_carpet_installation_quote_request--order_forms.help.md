**Carpet Installation Quote Request - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Carpet Installation Quote Request form is designed to collect essential information from customers interested in installing carpet flooring. This form will help our team create a customized quote for the project, which includes details about the customer, project requirements, preferred carpet style and material, installation specifics, and additional information. Please provide accurate and detailed responses to ensure we can deliver an accurate quote for your project.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the **Project Category** that best describes your project.
2. Choose the **Primary Area of Installation** where the carpet will be installed.
3. Enter your **Approximate Total Square Footage** of the area to be covered.
4. Select your **Desired Carpet Style** and **Preferred Material**.
5. Choose the **Padding Type** that suits your comfort and durability needs.
6. Select the **Current Flooring** type that currently exists in the area.
7. Indicate whether you **Need Flooring Removal**, **Subfloor Repairs**, and **Furniture Moving**.
8. Select the **Start Date** for the project, if desired.
9. Enter your **Estimated Budget**, if you have one.
10. Finally, confirm that you are requesting a quote and not placing an order, and acknowledge that the quote is subject to a site measurement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name, including any relevant title or company affiliation.
* **Email Address**: Enter your business or personal email address for communication.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your work or personal phone number for scheduling and coordination.
* **Installation Address**: Enter the exact address where the carpet will be installed.
* **Project Category**: Select the best description of your project: New Construction, Renovation, Commercial, Residential, or Other.
* **Primary Area of Installation**: Select all areas where the carpet will be installed (e.g., Living Room, Bedrooms, Hallways, Basement, Stairs, Office).
* **Approximate Total Square Footage**: Estimate the total area in square footage where the carpet will be installed.
* **Desired Carpet Style**: Choose a primary look for the carpet: Plush, Berber, Frieze, Patterned, or Undecided.
* **Preferred Material**: Select the material you prefer for the carpet: Nylon, Polyester, Wool, Triexta, or Not Sure.
* **Padding Type**: Choose the comfort and durability level for the carpet: Standard, Premium, Waterproof, or Moisture Barrier.
* **Current Flooring**: Select the type of flooring currently existing in the area: Carpet, Hardwood, Tile, Vinyl, Concrete, or Subfloor Only.
* **Need Flooring Removal**: Indicate whether you need existing flooring removal (Yes/No, or Some areas).
* **Subfloor Repairs Needed**: Indicate whether you need subfloor repairs (Yes/No, or Unsure).
* **Furniture Moving**: Indicate whether you need to move furniture (Yes/No).
* **Desired Start Date**: Enter a specific date when you want to start the project, or leave blank to schedule at our discretion.
* **Estimated Budget**: Enter your estimated budget for the project, if you have one.
* **Quote Acknowledgement**: Confirm that this is a quote request and not an order, acknowledging that the final quote is subject to site measurement.
