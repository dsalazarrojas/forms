# Concert Ticket Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form serves as an order summary and payment gateway for concert ticket purchases. It requires users to review their order details before proceeding with payment.

## How To Complete This Form

* Carefully review your order information to avoid errors during payment processing.
* Ensure you have the necessary details to complete the form accurately.
* Follow the step-by-step instructions below to complete the form successfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Reference Number**: A unique ID for your order, which you should have received via email or other means.
* **Event Name**: The concert event name you have chosen to attend.
* **Event Date**: The date of the concert event.
* **Event Venue**: The location of the concert event.
* **Ticket Details**: A note section for reviewing your ticket selection; ensure you have the correct tickets selected.
* **Ticket Type**: The category of ticket you've purchased (e.g., General Admission, Reserved Seating, etc.).
* **Ticket Quantity**: The number of tickets you've bought for the event.
* **Base Ticket Price**: The price per ticket before additional fees and taxes.
* **Tickets Subtotal**: A calculated field displaying the total cost of the tickets before fees and taxes.
* **Fees and Taxes**: A note section for reviewing additional charges; these will vary based on your location and order details.
* **Service Fee**: A calculated field displaying any processing fees applied to your order.
* **Facility Fee**: A calculated field displaying any venue-specific charges applied to your order.
* **Sales Tax**: A calculated field displaying applicable tax for your location.
* **Total Amount Due**: The final price to be paid for the order, including all fees and taxes.
* **Payer Information**: A note section for reviewing your billing details; ensure they match your order information.
* **Cardholder Name**: The name on your credit card.
* **Billing Email**: Your email address for receiving order confirmation and other related communications.
* **Billing Phone**: Your contact number for any queries or updates.
* **Billing Address**: Your full billing address for payment processing.
* **Payment Details**: A note section for reviewing your payment information; ensure it matches your card details.
* **Card Type**: The type of credit card you're using (e.g., Visa, Mastercard, etc.).
* **Card Number**: Your 16-digit credit card number.
* **Expiry Date**: The expiry date of your credit card (in MM/YY format).
* **Security Code**: The security code (CVV) on the back of your credit card.
* **Payment Authorization**: Confirm that you authorize this payment to complete your order.
