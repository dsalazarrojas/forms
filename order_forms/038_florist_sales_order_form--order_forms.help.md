# Florist Sales Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place an order for flowers from the florist. To complete the form, fill out the fields as described below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Order Flowers" field and enter your desired order.
2. Select the type of flowers you want from the list provided.
3. Choose the occasion for which you are ordering the flowers.
4. Enter the desired delivery date and time.
5. Enter your name, email address, and phone number for contact purposes.
6. Add any additional message or note for the florist.
7. Select the payment method you wish to use.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Flowers** (`florist_sales_order_form_form_input`, `text`, optional): Enter your desired order of flowers.
* **Flowers Type** (`flower_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of flowers you want from the list provided.
	+ Select from: Rose, Orchid, Carnation, Tulip, Lilac, Violet, Bouquet
* **Occasion** (`occasion`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the occasion for which you are ordering the flowers.
	+ Select from: Birthdays, Valentines, Anniversaries, Other, Funeral
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the desired delivery date for the flowers.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the desired delivery time for the flowers.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name for contact purposes.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Message** (`order_message`, `text`, optional): Add any additional message or note for the florist.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, `number`, optional): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, optional): Select the payment method you wish to use.
	+ Select from: Cash, Card, Transfer, Bank Account

**Tips**
- Make sure to double-check the fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Enter a valid email address and phone number for contact purposes.
- Choose the correct payment method according to your preference.
- If you have any further questions or need assistance, contact the florist directly.
