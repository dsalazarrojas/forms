# Warehouse Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warehouse Check Out Form is a critical tool for tracking and documenting items checked out from the warehouse. It helps employees and managers accurately record and maintain inventory, ensuring accurate tracking of items, dates, and times.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your employee ID number into the `Employee ID` field.
2.  Select the `Item Number` of the item you are checking out.
3.  Indicate the `Item Type` by selecting either `Yes` or `No` from the dropdown menu.
4.  Enter the correct `Quantity` of the item being checked out.
5.  Enter the `Date Checked Out` and `Time Checked Out` for the checkout process.
6.  In the `Reason` field, provide a brief description of the item's purpose or reason for checkout.
7.  In the `Comments` field, add any additional comments or notes related to the checkout.
8.  Select the `Item Status` by choosing from the dropdown menu (`Active` or `Inactive`).
9.  Enter your `Email` address and `Phone` number for contact purposes.
10.  Indicate whether the item was checked out to a location (`Yes` or `No`).
11.  Confirm `Supervisor Approval` by selecting `Yes` or `No`.
12.  Enter the `Return Date` and `Return Time` for the return of the item.
13.  In the `Return Comments` field, add any notes or comments related to the return process.
14.  Enter your supervisor's `Email` address and `Phone` number for contact purposes.
15.  Confirm the `Return Status` by choosing from the dropdown menu (`Active` or `Inactive`).
16.  Select the `Employee Return Comments` field for any additional comments or notes related to the return process.
17.  Enter any additional comments or notes from the supervisor in the `Supervisor Return Comments` field.
18.  Finally, submit the form by clicking the `Submit` button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **User ID (`employee_id`, number, required)**: Enter the unique ID number assigned to you as an employee in the warehouse.
*   **Item Number (`item_number`, number, required)**: Enter the exact number of the item being checked out.
*   **Item Type (`item_type`, select_one, required)**: Select `Yes` if the item is currently active, `No` otherwise.
*   **Quantity (`quantity`, number, required)**: Enter the number of items being checked out.
*   **Date Checked Out (`date_checked_out`, date, required)**: Enter the date you are checking the item out on.
*   **Time Checked Out (`time_checked_out`, time, required)**: Enter the time you are checking the item out on.
*   **Reason (`reason`, text, required)**: Describe the purpose or reason for checking out the item.
*   **Comments (`user_comments`, text, optional)**: Add any additional comments or notes related to the checkout.
*   **Item Status (`item_status`, select_multiple, required)**: Select `Active` if the item is currently active, `Inactive` otherwise.
*   **Email (`email`, email, required)**: Enter your company email address for contact purposes.
*   **Phone (`phone`, text, required)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Location (`location`, select_one, required)**: Select `Yes` if the item is checked out to a location.
*   **Supervisor Approval (`supervisor_approval`, select_one, required)**: Confirm that your supervisor has approved the checkout.
*   **Employee ID (`employee_id`, number, required)**: Enter the unique ID number assigned to you as an employee.
*   **Date Returned (`date_returned`, date, optional)**: Enter the date the item was returned.
*   **Comments (`comments`, text, optional)**: Add any additional comments or notes related to the return.
*   **Supervisor Notes (`supervisor_notes`, text, optional)**: Enter any notes or comments from the supervisor.
*   **Employee Notes (`employee_notes`, text, optional)**: Enter any additional comments or notes from you.
*   **Return Status (`return_status`, select_one, required)**: Select `Active` if the item is currently active, `Inactive` otherwise.
*   **Return Comments (`return_comments`, text, optional)**: Add any additional comments or notes related to the return.
*   **Supervisor Return Comments (`supervisor_return_comments`, text, optional)**: Enter any notes or comments from the supervisor.
*   **Return Date (`return_date`, date, required)**: Enter the date the item was returned.
*   **Return Time (`return_time`, time, optional)**: Enter the time the item was returned.
*   **Employee Return Comments (`employee_return_comments`, text, optional)**: Enter any additional comments or notes related to the return process.
*   **Supervisor Return Comments (`supervisor_return_comments`, text, optional)**: Enter any additional comments or notes related to the return process.
*   **Submit (`submit`, text, required)**: Click the `Submit` button to complete the form.
