# Restaurant Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Order Form is a simple form used to place orders with a restaurant. It's essential for customers to provide their pickup or delivery location, order type, payment method, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To place an order, follow these steps:

1. Choose your preferred **Pickup Location** from the list of available options.
2. Select your **Delivery Location** if you want your order delivered. Note that delivery options are the same as pickup locations.
3. Choose the type of **Order Type** you wish to place. This could be Food Pickup, Delivery, or Online Payment.
4. Choose your preferred **Payment Method** from Visa, MasterCard, or PayPal.
5. If necessary, provide any **Special Instructions** for your order.
6. Enter your **Order Date** in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
7. Choose a specific **Order Time** in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
8. Enter your **Customer Email** address.
9. Provide your **Customer Phone** number.
10. Enter your **Customer Name**.
11. Enter your **Customer Address**.
12. Select the **Restaurant Name** for your order.
13. Add any additional notes in the **Note** field.
14. (Optional) Provide any additional notes in the **Another note** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pickup Location**: Select the location where you'd like to pick up your order.
* **Delivery Location**: Select the location where you'd like to have your order delivered.
* **Order Type**: Choose the type of order you'd like to place (Food Pickup, Delivery, or Online Payment).
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you'd like to pay for your order (Visa, MasterCard, or PayPal).
* **Special Instructions**: Add any special notes or instructions for your order.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date you'd like to receive your order in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Order Time**: Enter the time you'd like to receive your order in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Customer Email**: Enter your customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your customer's phone number.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your customer's name.
* **Customer Address**: Enter your customer's address.
* **Restaurant Name**: Enter the name of the restaurant you'd like to order from.
* **Note**: Add any additional notes about your order.
* **Another note**: Provide any additional notes about your order.
* **Order ID**: This field is automatically generated and not editable.
