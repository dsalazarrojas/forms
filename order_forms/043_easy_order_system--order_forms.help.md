# easy_order_system - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "easy_order_system" form is used to collect information from customers for an order process. This form gathers details about the customer's personal information, order preferences, and order total.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Start by filling in your personal details on the first page.
* On the second page, select the products you would like to order.
* On the third page, choose your delivery preferences.
* On the fourth page, enter a summary of your order.
* On the fifth page, confirm the total cost of your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name, address, and contact information here.
* **Order Products** (`order_products`, `text`, required: false): Select the products you would like to order.
* **Delivery Preferences** (`delivery_preferences`, `text`, required: false): Choose your preferred delivery method and any special instructions.
* **Customer Order** (`customer_order`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief summary of your order to ensure accuracy.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, `number`, required: false): Confirm the total cost of your order.
