# insurance_policy_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The insurance policy order form is a crucial document used to collect client and policy information for new insurance policy orders. Its primary purpose is to ensure that all relevant details are captured during the policy ordering process, facilitating smooth communication and policy execution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Begin by filling out the client data fields, which include:
	* **Client Data**: Enter the client's name, contact information, and any other necessary details.
2. Select the **Policy Type**: Choose the type of policy being ordered, e.g., 'Yes' or 'No'.
3. Specify the **Policy Coverage**: Select all relevant policy coverages, e.g., 'Yes' or 'No'.
4. Enter the **Effective Date**, **Expiration Date**, and **Order Date**: These dates are essential for policy activation, expiration, and order processing.
5. Select the **Order Status**: Choose the order status, e.g., 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
6. Optionally, add **Policy Details**, **Agent Signature**, **Client Signature**, and **Notes** for additional comments or information.
7. Select the **Payment Terms** and **Payment Method**: Choose 'Yes' or 'No' for payment terms and select the payment method, e.g., 'Yes' or 'No'.
8. Enter **Payment Method Details** as required.
9. Add any **Order Comments**, **Additional Notes**, and **Policy Number**, **Policy Term**, **Policy Years in Force**, **Order Number**, **Client Email**, **Client Phone**, **Agent Email**, and **Agent Phone**, if applicable.
10. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Data (Client Data)**: Enter the client's name, contact information, and any other necessary details.
* **Policy Type (Policy Type)**: Choose the type of policy being ordered, e.g., 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Policy Coverage (Policy Coverage)**: Select all relevant policy coverages, e.g., 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Effective Date (Effective Date)**: Enter the date the policy becomes effective.
* **Expiration Date (Expiration Date)**: Enter the date the policy expires.
* **Order Date (Order Date)**: Enter the date the order was made.
* **Order Time (Order Time)**: Enter the time the order was made.
* **Order Status (Order Status)**: Choose the order status, e.g., 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Policy Details (Policy Details)**: Add any additional comments or information about the policy.
* **Agent Signature (Agent Signature)**: Add the agent's signature for verification.
* **Client Signature (Client Signature)**: Add the client's signature for verification.
* **Payment Terms (Payment Terms)**: Choose 'Yes' or 'No' for payment terms.
* **Payment Method (Payment Method)**: Select the payment method, e.g., 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Payment Method Details (Payment Method Details)**: Enter details about the payment method.
* **Order Comments (Order Comments)**: Add any comments about the order.
* **Additional Notes (Additional Notes)**: Add any additional comments or information.
* **Policy Number (Policy Number)**: Enter the policy number.
* **Policy Term (Policy Term)**: Enter the policy term.
* **Policy Years in Force (Policy Years in Force)**: Enter the number of policy years in force.
* **Order Number (Order Number)**: Enter the order number.
* **Client Email (Client Email)**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Client Phone (Client Phone)**: Enter the client's phone number.
* **Agent Email (Agent Email)**: Enter the agent's email address.
* **Agent Phone (Agent Phone)**: Enter the agent's phone number.
* **Notes (Notes)**: Add any additional comments or information.
