# healthy prophets form with paysafe - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "healthy prophets form with paysafe" is a form designed to collect customer information and payment preferences for orders. It is used to gather details such as customer name, email, phone number, address, payment method, order type, and date and time of the order. This form helps customers provide accurate and complete information necessary for processing their orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure all fields are relevant and accurate.
2. Fill in the required fields marked as "required."
3. Select the preferred payment method from the available options.
4. Choose the order type that best describes the order.
5. Enter any additional resources or notes related to the order.
6. Double-check the total amount and date and time of the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Healthy-juice-order-form (Order Form)** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This is the main title of the form.
* **Customer-name (Customer Name)** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Customer-email (Customer Email)** (`3`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address to enable communication and order tracking.
* **Customer-phone (Customer Phone)** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Provide your phone number for easy contact and order updates.
* **Customer-address (Customer Address)** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Enter your address for delivery and order details.
* **Payment-method (Payment Method)** (`6`, `select_one`, required: true): Select one of the available payment methods (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Order-type (Order Type)** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Choose multiple options from the list (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Resources (Resources)** (`8`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional resources or notes related to the order.
* **Total (Total)** (`9`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total amount of the order.
* **Order-date (Order Date)** (`10`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the order.
* **Order-time (Order Time)** (`11`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the order.
