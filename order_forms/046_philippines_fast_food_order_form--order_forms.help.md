# Philippines Fast Food Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information for fast food orders from customers in the Philippines. It is used to capture customer and order details, including delivery date and time, order status, and payment method. The form is intended to be completed by customers ordering fast food from a restaurant or food establishment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Select the food item you would like to order and the quantity you would like to order.
3. Provide your delivery date and time.
4. Choose the payment method you would like to use.
5. Enter any additional order notes or comments.
6. Review and submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`): Enter your name, email, and phone number.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`): Describe your order in detail.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): Enter your delivery address and any additional contact information.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`): Enter the date you would like to pick up or have your order delivered.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`): Enter the time you would like to pick up or have your order delivered.
* **Food Item** (`food_item`): Select the food item you would like to order.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`): Enter the quantity of the food item you would like to order.
* **Price** (`price`): Enter the price of your order.
* **Total Price** (`total_price`): This field is automatically populated based on your order details.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`): Choose the status of your order (Pending, Confirmed, or Cancelled).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select the payment method you would like to use (Credit Card, Pay Pal, or Cash).
* **Customer Note** (`customer_note`): Enter any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Restaurant Information** (`restaurant_info`): Enter any additional information about the restaurant or food establishment.
* **Store Number** (`store_number`): Enter the store number or location of the restaurant or food establishment.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Enter your name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`): Enter your delivery address.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`): Enter the date you would like to pick up or have your order delivered.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`): Enter the time you would like to pick up or have your order delivered.
* **Order Comment** (`order_comment`): Enter any additional comments about your order.
* **Delivery Note** (`delivery_note`): Enter any delivery instructions or notes.
* **Store Information** (`store_info`): Enter any additional information about the store or restaurant.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Enter any information about the tool or equipment used for the order.

Note: The fields marked as "required: false" are optional and can be left blank if not applicable.
