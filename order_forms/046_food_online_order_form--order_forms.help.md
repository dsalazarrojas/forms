# Food Online Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Online Order Form is a tool used to place orders with a food establishment or online food service. Fill out this form to inform us of your food preferences and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions as they appear on each page.
2. On each page, select the relevant option for your needs from the dropdown or radio buttons.
3. Continue to the next page until you've completed all the fields.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Meal Type (Page 1)
* **meal_type**: Are you ordering food for yourself or someone else? You can choose between "Yes" and "No".

### Dietary Restrictions (Page 1)
* **dietary_restrictions**: Do you have any dietary restrictions or preferences? You can choose between "Yes" and "No".

### Quantity (Page 1)
* **quantity**: How many items would you like to order? Enter a number to specify the quantity.

### Temperature (Page 1)
* **temperature**: Do you want your order to be hot or cold? You can choose between "Yes" and "No".

### Payment (Page 2)
* **payment**: How would you like to pay for your order? You can choose between "Yes" and "No" (assuming this refers to a payment method, but could be clarified in the future).

### Delivery Details (Page 2)
* **delivery_details**: Enter any additional information about your delivery requirements here.

### Order Online (Page 2)
* **order_online**: Would you like to place your order online? You can choose between "Yes" and "No".
