# videography_billing_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The videography_billing_form is used to collect billing information from clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are an authorized user.
2. Navigate to the form, which is likely located in a secure area.
3. Click on the first page, "Videography Billing Form First Page", to begin the process.
4. Complete the required fields, which include "payment_method", "payment_status", and "payment_terms".
5. Review and complete any additional fields as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **client_name** (text, required: false)
Enter the client's name.

### 2. **client_email** (email, required: false)
Enter the client's email address.

### 3. **billing_address** (text, required: false)
Enter the billing address for the client.

### 4. **payment_method** (select_one, required: true)
Select the payment method to be used for billing (Stripe, PayPal, or Square).

### 5. **total_cost** (number, required: false)
Enter the total cost of the services rendered.

### 6. **subtotal** (number, required: false)
Enter the subtotal of the services rendered.

### 7. **tax** (number, required: false)
Enter any applicable tax for the services rendered.

### 8. **discount** (number, required: false)
Enter any applicable discount for the services rendered.

### 9. **grand_total** (number, required: false)
Enter the grand total for the services rendered.

### 10. **payment_status** (select_one, required: true)
Select the payment status of the invoice (paid, pending, or overdue).

### 11. **payment_terms** (text, required: false)
Enter any payment terms or conditions that apply.

### 12. **note** (note, required: false)
Enter any additional notes or comments.

### 13. **client_note** (text, required: false)
Enter any additional notes or comments from the client.

### 14. **terms** (text, required: false)
Enter any terms or conditions that apply to the services rendered.

### 15. **date** (date, required: false)
Enter the date of the services rendered.

### 16. **time** (time, required: false)
Enter the time of the services rendered.
