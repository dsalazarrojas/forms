# industrial_pressure_valve_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The industrial pressure valve order form is designed to collect information for orders of industrial pressure valves. This form is intended for business-to-business or business-to-customer transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required and optional information as prompted.
2. Review the form for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details**: 
    * (`order_details`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the order.
* **Customer Info**:
    * (`customer_info`, text, required: false): Enter information about the customer, such as name, company name, etc.
* **Shipping**:
    * (`shipping_info`, text, required: false): Enter shipping information, such as address, city, state, etc.
* **Product Info**:
    * (`product_info`, text, required: false): Enter product information, such as product name, quantity, etc.
* **Order Notes**:
    * (`order_notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or instructions related to the order.
* **Contact Person**:
    * (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Enter the contact person's name and title.
* **Email**:
    * (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Phone**:
    * (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Address**:
    * (`address`, text, required: false): Enter the delivery address.
* **City**:
    * (`city`, text, required: false): Enter the city of delivery.
* **State**:
    * (`state`, text, required: false): Enter the state of delivery.
* **Zip**:
    * (`zip`, text, required: false): Enter the zip code of delivery.
* **Order Date**:
    * (`order_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the order.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Review the form for errors before submission.
