# Submit A Product Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a product and its submitter for review or listing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information about the product.
2. Make sure to select the correct product status (active, inactive, or pending).
3. Enter the business name, email, and phone number of the submitter.
4. Provide a URL for the product.
5. Add any additional notes about the product.
6. Choose any relevant categories and add tags for the product.
7. Add any submitter notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the product.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, required): Enter a description of the product.
* **Price** (`product_price`, number, required): Enter the price of the product.
* **Product Image** (`product_image`, text, required): Enter a URL or image link of the product image.
* **Product Status** (`product_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of the product (active, inactive, or pending).
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the business associated with the product.
* **Business Email** (`business_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the business.
* **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the business.
* **Product URL** (`product_url`, text, required): Enter the URL of the product.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, required): Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, required): Select the date the product was submitted.
* **Submit Time** (`submit_time`, time, required): Select the time the product was submitted.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Enter any additional notes about the product.
* **Categories** (`categories`, select_multiple, required): Choose the categories relevant to the product.
* **Tags** (`tags`, text, required): Enter any relevant tags for the product.
* **Submitter Notes** (`submitter_notes`, text, required): Enter any notes from the submitter.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
* Use the correct format for date and time fields.
* Use the select_multiple field for categories to choose multiple categories.
* Use the tags field to add any relevant tags for the product.
