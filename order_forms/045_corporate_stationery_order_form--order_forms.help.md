# Corporate Stationery Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Stationery Order Form is used to place orders for business stationery items such as business cards, notepads, and other printed materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Company Name and Department where prompted.
2. Select the desired Stationery Type from the provided options.
3. Enter the number of items you wish to order in the Quantity field.
4. Enter the Unit Price of the stationery item.
5. Select a delivery date for your order.
6. (Optional) Enter a delivery time for your order, if applicable.
7. Enter your contact email address.
8. Enter your phone number.
9. (Optional) Enter a fax number.
10. Provide any additional notes about your order, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name (Text)**: Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Department (Select One)**: Select the department within your company that this order is for.
* **Stationery Type (Select Multiple)**: Choose the type of stationery you wish to order from the options provided. 
* **Quantity (Number)**: Enter the number of stationery items you require.
* **Unit Price (Number)**: Enter the price per item of the stationery you wish to order.
* **Delivery Date (Date)**: Select the date you wish to receive your order.
* **Delivery Time (Time)**: (Optional) If applicable, select the time you wish to receive your order.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Fax (Text)**: (Optional) Enter your fax number.
* **Notes (Note)**: Provide any additional information about your order, if necessary.
