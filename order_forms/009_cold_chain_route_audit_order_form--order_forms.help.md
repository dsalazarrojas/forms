# Cold Chain Route Audit Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request and track the audit of a cold chain route. A cold chain is a supply chain that requires temperature-sensitive goods to be kept within a specific temperature range to prevent spoilage or damage.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on the "Start Form" button to begin.
2.  Select the type of cargo being transported from the "Type of Cargo" dropdown list.
3.  Enter the temperature range required for the cargo.
4.  Enter the origin city and country of the cargo.
5.  Enter the destination city and country of the cargo.
6.  Enter the preferred start date and time for the audit.
7.  Enter the estimated duration of the route in hours.
8.  Select the number of temperature monitoring devices needed for the audit.
9.  Enter any special instructions for the audit team.
10. Click on "Submit" to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requesting Organization**: This field is required to be filled in with the name of your organization.
* **Primary Contact Name**: This field is required to be filled in with the name of the primary contact person for this audit request.
* **Contact Email**: This field is required to be filled in with the email address of the primary contact person.
* **Origin City and Country**: This field is required to be filled in with the city and country of origin.
* **Destination City and Country**: This field is required to be filled in with the city and country of destination.
* **Type of Cargo**: Select the type of cargo being transported from the dropdown list.
	+ **Pharma**: Temperature-sensitive pharmaceuticals.
	+ **Food**: Food and beverages.
	+ **Biological**: Biological samples, such as blood or organs.
	+ **Chemicals**: Chemicals that require specific temperature control.
	+ **Electronics**: Electronic equipment or devices.
	+ **Other**: Other temperature-sensitive goods.
* **Temperature Requirement**: Select the temperature range required for the cargo.
	+ **Deep Frozen (-20C)**: For goods that require deep freezing.
	+ **Frozen (-18C)**: For goods that require freezer temperatures.
	+ **Chilled (+2C to +8C)**: For goods that require chilled temperatures.
	+ **Controlled Ambient (+15C to +25C)**: For goods that require controlled ambient temperatures.
* **Preferred Audit Date**: This field is required to be filled in with the start date and time for the audit.
* **Estimated Duration of Route**: This field is required to be filled in with the estimated duration of the route in hours.
* **Required Data Loggers**: This field is required to be filled in with the number of temperature monitoring devices needed for the audit.
* **Special Handling Instructions**: This field is optional and can be used to provide any special instructions for the audit team.
