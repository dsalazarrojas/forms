# Custom Hang Tag Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to provide their order information for custom hang tags. Please fill out this form to ensure we have all the necessary details for your order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields as best as you can.
2. If you are unsure about any field, contact our support team for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Artwork
* **Artwork** (`artwork`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the artwork you would like to create a custom hang tag for.

### Shipping
* **Shipping** (`shipping`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant shipping details for your custom hang tag.

### Artwork Details
* **Artwork Details** (`artwork_details`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information about the artwork you are ordering, such as the artist or the image's size.

### Payment
* **Payment** (`payment`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant payment information or notes about your order.

### Payment Method
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, optional): Choose from the available payment options:
	+ Credit Card
	+ PayPal
	+ Stripe

### Artwork Type
* **Artwork Type** (`artwork_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or both of the following:
	+ Digital
	+ Physical

### Tag Size
* **Tag Size** (`tag_size`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the size of the tag you would like:
	+ Small
	+ Large

### Shipping Details
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional shipping information or notes about your order.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all relevant fields to ensure a smooth and efficient order process.
* If you have any questions or need help with your order, contact our support team for assistance.
