# Online Boutique Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect payment information from customers for online orders. It is a crucial step in the ordering process, ensuring that customers can securely provide their payment details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*). In this case, the payment method field is the only required field.
2. Select your preferred payment method (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
3. Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
4. If needed, add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
5. Review your completed form before submitting it for order confirmation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Method** (*): Select your preferred payment method for the order.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to help us contact you with order updates and confirmations.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to provide an additional contact method.
* **Date**: Enter the date for the order or the date of the payment.
* **Time**: Enter the time for the payment (if applicable).
* **Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about your order.

Note that the fields "Order Form", "shipping_details", and "payment_info" are not visible to customers and are used internally for order processing purposes.
