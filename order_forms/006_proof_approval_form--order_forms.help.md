# Proof Approval Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Proof Approval Form is used to collect information and approval for an order-related proof or document.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the order details, including the order date.
2. Provide your name, email, and phone number (optional).
3. Enter the customer's name, email, and phone number.
4. Enter the customer's address.
5. Attach a proof document or note.
6. Select the approval option (if required).
7. Add any additional comments (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Order Details
* **Order Details**: This field is used to enter the order-related information, such as the order ID or description. It is an optional field.

### Order Date
* **Order Date**: Enter the date of the order.

### Customer Name
* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer.

### Customer Email
* **Customer Email**: Enter the email of the customer.

### Customer Phone
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the customer.

### Customer Address
* **Customer Address**: Enter the address of the customer.

### Proof Document
* **Proof Document**: Attach the proof document or note related to the order.

### Approval
* **Approval**: Select the approval option for the order. Options are:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4
	+ Option 5

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the order.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Double-check the information entered in the form for accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any field or process, please contact your representative for assistance.
