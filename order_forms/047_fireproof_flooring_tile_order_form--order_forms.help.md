# Fireproof Flooring Tile Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fireproof Flooring Tile Order Form is designed to collect customer information for the ordering of fireproof flooring tiles. This form helps us understand your contact details, delivery preferences, and any specific tile selections or notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information in the "customer_details" section.
2. Provide your delivery preferences in the "delivery_details" section.
3. Specify any specific tile selections or options in the "tile_selections" section.
4. Add any additional delivery notes in the "delivery_notes" section.
5. Include any relevant customer notes or instructions in the "customer_notes" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, required: false): Please enter your name, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, text, required: false): Specify your preferred delivery date and time, or any specific delivery instructions.
* **Tile Selections** (`tile_selections`, text, required: false): Choose the specific tiles you would like to order, and describe any custom options or modifications you require.
* **Delivery Notes** (`delivery_notes`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information about the delivery, such as parking restrictions or special access requirements.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or instructions for our team to consider during the order process.
