# promotional_giveaway_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The promotional giveaways order form is used to collect information from users who want to place orders for promotional items. Please fill out the form with your details and promotion preferences.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with your information and select your preferred promotion type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (required, text): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (optional, text): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (required, email): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (optional, text): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
* **Promotion** (required, select_multiple): Select one or more of the promotional items that you are interested in. You can choose from Giveaway, Contest, and Promotion.
* **Date** (optional, date): If you need to select a specific date, enter it here.
* **Time** (optional, time): If you need to select a specific time, enter it here.
