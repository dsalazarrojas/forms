# Cleaning Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for cleaning supply orders from requesters. It guides the requester to provide necessary details about themselves, the supplies needed, and the reason for the order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the Requester Information Section**: Enter your full name, department, email address, and phone number.
2. **Specify the Supplies Needed**: Choose the specific cleaning supplies required and the quantity needed.
3. **Enter Additional Items**: If any additional supplies are needed that are not listed, enter them here.
4. **Justify the Order**: Select the reason why these supplies are needed and describe the current inventory status.
5. **Set the Urgency Level**: Select the urgency level for the order.
6. **Enter Budget and Estimated Cost Information**: If known, enter the budget code and estimated cost of the supplies.
7. **Enter Supervisor Information**: Enter the name of the approving supervisor and their approval status.
8. **Sign the Form**: Provide your electronic signature.
