# Wellness Retreat Holistic Product Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wellness Retreat Holistic Product Requisition Form is designed for customers to place orders for holistic wellness products. This form gathers essential information about the customer and their order, ensuring timely and accurate delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your **Customer Name** in the "Customer Name" field.
2.  Enter your **Email** address in the "Customer Email" field.
3.  Select the type(s) of **Products** you're ordering in the "Product Type" field.
4.  Provide a brief **Product Description** in the "Product Description" field (optional).
5.  Enter the **Quantity** of the product you're ordering.
6.  Select a **Date of Receipt** for your order.
7.  Choose a **Delivery Date** for your order.
8.  Choose a **Delivery Time** for your order.
9.  Enter your **Delivery Address** where you'd like the product delivered.
10.  Enter your **Contact Number** for communication.
11.  Add any **Note** about your order (optional).
12.  Enter the email address to which you'd like us to **Send a confirmation email**.
13.  Select whether someone has been **Assigned to** handle your order.
14.  Select a **Date** when the order was **Assigned** (if applicable).
15.  Choose a **Time** when the order was **Assigned** (if applicable).
16.  Enter the name of the person who **Submitted** your order (if applicable).
17.  Select a **Date** when the order was **Submitted** (if applicable).
18.  Choose a **Time** when the order was **Submitted** (if applicable).
19.  Select whether the order was **Submitted** by someone (if applicable).
20.  Select whether the order was **Submited** at this time (if applicable).
21.  Select whether the order was **Submitted At** this time (if applicable).
