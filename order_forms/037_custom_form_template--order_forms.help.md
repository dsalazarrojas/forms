# Custom Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect customer information for orders. It consists of four sections: Order Form, Customer Details, Order Details, and Shipping Address.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, start by filling in the fields as prompted.
*   Begin with the **Order Form** section, where you can enter information about the order.
*   Proceed to the **Customer Details** section, where you can enter customer contact and personal information.
*   Next, provide **Order Details** about the product or service being ordered.
*   Finally, fill in the **Shipping Address** section with the customer's delivery information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Order Form** (`order_form`, `text`, required: false): Enter order information such as order date, order number, or any other relevant details.
*   **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter customer contact and personal information, e.g., name, email, phone number.
*   **Order Details** (`order_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide details about the product or service being ordered, such as description, price, or order quantity.
*   **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the customer's shipping address, including street, city, state, and postal code.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill in all required fields, if applicable.
*   Double-check the information provided for accuracy.
*   Use this form for a specific and unique purpose to prevent duplication.
