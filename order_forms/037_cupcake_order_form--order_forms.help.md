# Cupcake Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Cupcake Order Form is used for customers to place orders for cupcakes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the `Customer Name` field.
2. Select the type of order you would like to place in the `Order Type` field (e.g. Chocolate, Vanilla, Strawberry).
3. Enter the quantity of cupcakes you would like to order in the `Quantity` field.
4. Choose a delivery date and time that works for you in the `Delivery Date` and `Delivery Time` fields.
5. Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
6. Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: A field to enter your name for order verification and communication.
* **Order Type**: A field to select the type of cupcake order you would like to place (e.g. Chocolate, Vanilla, Strawberry).
* **Quantity**: A field to enter the number of cupcakes you would like to order.
* **Delivery Date**: A field to select the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Delivery Time**: A field to select the time you would like to receive your order.
* **Email**: A field to enter your email address for order confirmation and updates.
* **Phone**: A field to enter your phone number for order confirmation and updates.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter your name and contact information correctly to ensure we can get in touch with you for order updates and delivery confirmation.
* Please note that orders placed before the delivery date selected may not be processed until that date.
* If you have any special requests or questions, do not hesitate to contact us.
