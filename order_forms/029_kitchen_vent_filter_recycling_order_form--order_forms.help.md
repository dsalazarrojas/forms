# kitchen_vent_filter_recycling_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers about their kitchen vent filter recycling orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "customer_info" field.
2. Provide your contact information in the "contact_info" field.
3. Enter details about your order in the "order_details" field.
4. Enter more information about your order in the "order_info" field.
5. Enter additional customer information in the "customer_info_2" field.
6. Enter more contact information in the "contact_info_2" field.
7. Enter more details about your order in the "order_details_2" field.
8. Enter more order information in the "order_info_2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **customer_info (Text)**: Enter your name so we can address you properly.
- **contact_info (Text)**: Provide your contact information, such as email or phone number.
- **order_details (Text)**: Enter details about your order, including quantity, type, or any other relevant information.
- **order_info (Text)**: Enter more details about your order.
- **customer_info_2 (Text)**: Enter additional customer information that was not covered in the first set of fields.
- **contact_info_2 (Text)**: Enter additional contact information.
- **order_details_2 (Text)**: Enter more details about your order.
- **order_info_2 (Text)**: Enter more order information.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure that your order is processed correctly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
- We appreciate your cooperation and look forward to processing your order.
