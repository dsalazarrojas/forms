# Retail Buyer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used by retail buyers to provide information about their orders, including their name, contact email, phone number, delivery preferences, and any additional comments or instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Buyer Name** in the first field.
2. Provide your **Buyer Email** address to ensure we can contact you about your order.
3. Enter your **Buyer Phone** number to allow us to contact you with updates.
4. Select your preferred **Delivery Method** from the available options.
5. Enter your **Store Name** to specify the store where you want to pick up your order.
6. Type in your **Store Address** to provide the address of the store where you want to pick up your order.
7. Choose your preferred **Payment Method** from the available options.
8. Provide any **Order Comments** you may have about your order.
9. Enter any **Special Instructions** you may need for us to fulfill your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. Retail Buyer Application**: This field is where you can enter a brief description of your order.
* **2. Buyer Name**: This is your name as the buyer.
* **3. Buyer Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **4. Buyer Phone**: This is your phone number where we can reach you with updates about your order.
* **5. Delivery Method**: Select one or more of the following options to specify how you want to receive your order: In-store pickup, Delivery, or Mail.
* **6. Store Name**: Enter the name of the store where you want to pick up your order.
* **7. Store Address**: Type in the address of the store where you want to pick up your order.
* **8. Payment Method**: Choose how you want to pay for your order: PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Check.
* **9. Order Comments**: If you have any special instructions or comments about your order, type them here.
* **10. Special Instructions**: If you need any special instructions for your order, enter them here.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date contact information so we can reach you about your order.
- Double-check your delivery preferences to ensure we understand how you want to receive your order.
- Use clear and concise language in the "Order Comments" and "Special Instructions" fields to help us better understand your needs.
