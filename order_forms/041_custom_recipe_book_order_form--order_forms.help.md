# Custom Recipe Book Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a custom recipe book order. Please fill out the form with your details to place your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the text field provided. This is the name that will be used for our records.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the email field. This will be used for communication purposes.
3. Enter your **Phone Number** in the text field. This will help us contact you for any further questions.
4. Fill in the **Date of Order** using the date picker or manually input the date you prefer to receive your book.
5. Select a time slot for your order using the time picker. Please choose your preferred time for your book delivery or pickup.
6. For **Select One**, pick one option from the dropdown list. This field will ask you to choose from a set of options. 
7. For **Select Multiple**, pick one or more of the provided options from the dropdown list. This field will ask you to choose from multiple options. 
8. Once you've filled in all the necessary fields, click submit to place your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your first name here.
* **Email** (text, required/optional): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you for your order status.
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for any further questions.
* **Date of Order** (date, required/optional): Fill in the date you prefer to receive your book. 
* **Time of Order** (time, required/optional): Choose the time slot for your book delivery or pickup. 
* **Select One** (select one, required/optional): Choose one option from the list provided.
* **Select Multiple** (select multiple, required/optional): Choose one or more options from the list provided.

## Tips
- Please ensure all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
- Review all fields before submitting to ensure correctness.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us using the email provided.
