## nfl_pre_order_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The NFL Pre-Order Form is used to collect information from customers for pre-ordering tickets for upcoming games. This form is required for all customers who wish to purchase tickets for events that have not yet occurred.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the team name of the team you are interested in purchasing tickets for.
2. Select the quantity of tickets you wish to purchase.
3. Enter the price you are willing to pay for the tickets.
4. Choose the type of order you would like to place (General Admission or Premium Experience).
5. Enter the date and time of the game you are interested in.
6. Enter your phone number and email address so we can contact you to confirm your order.
7. Enter any additional notes you would like to include with your order.
8. Confirm your phone number and email address to ensure accuracy.
9. Confirm your order type and any additional notes.
10. Finally, click "Submit" to submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the team you are interested in purchasing tickets for.
* **Ticket Quantity** (`ticket_quantity`, number, optional): Enter the number of tickets you would like to purchase.
* **Ticket Price** (`ticket_price`, number, optional): Enter the price you are willing to pay for the tickets.
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, select_one, required): Choose between General Admission and Premium Experience to determine the type of order you would like to place.
* **Game Date** (`game_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the game you are interested in.
* **Game Time** (`game_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the game you are interested in.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you to confirm your order.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you to confirm your order.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes you would like to include with your order.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, text, optional): Confirm your phone number to ensure accuracy.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, email, optional): Confirm your email address to ensure accuracy.
* **Confirm Notes** (`confirm_notes`, note, optional): Confirm any additional notes you would like to include with your order.
* **Confirm Order Type** (`confirm_order_type`, select_one, required): Confirm the type of order you would like to place (General Admission or Premium Experience).
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, optional): Click "Submit" to submit your order.
* **Error Message** (`error_message`, text, optional): This field is not visible to the user and is used to display any error messages that may occur during the submission process.
* **Confirm Submit** (`confirm_submit`, text, optional): Confirm that you are ready to submit your order.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_multiple, required): Choose the status of your order from the following options: Pending, Processing, Completed, Cancelled.
* **Team Name 2** (`team_name_2`, text, optional): This field is not currently used.
* **Ticket Number** (`ticket_number`, text, optional): This field is not currently used.
* **Order Number** (`order_number`, text, optional): This field is not currently used.
* **Confirm Ticket Number** (`confirm_ticket_number`, text, optional): This field is not currently used.
* **Confirm Order Number** (`confirm_order_number`, text, optional): This field is not currently used.
* **Ticket Expiration** (`ticket_expiration`, date, optional): This field is not currently used.
* **Ticket Price 2** (`ticket_price_2`, number, optional): This field is not currently used.
* **Game Date 2** (`game_date_2`, date, optional): This field is not currently used.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the team name, phone number, and email address you enter are accurate and up-to-date.
* Double-check your order type and status to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out this form, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will review and confirm your order once you have submitted it.
* Please be patient and allow us time to process your order.
