# Flight_Booking_Extension_Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Flight_Booking_Extension_Form is used to gather information for flight bookings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Select the correct page by clicking on the corresponding tab.
2. Enter the required and optional information for each field as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, optional): Enter the customer's name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Payment Options** (`payment_options`, select_multiple, optional): Select the payment options for the flight booking, such as "Yes" or "No" for payment methods.
* **Travel Agency** (`travel_agency`, text, optional): Enter the name of the travel agency or airline used for the flight.
