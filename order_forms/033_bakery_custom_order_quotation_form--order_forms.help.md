# Bakery Custom Order Quotation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you provide detailed information about your custom bakery order. It includes fields for customer contact details, event and order specifics, customization options, delivery preferences, and budget estimates.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Step 1: Fill out the form with your details**. This form is divided into several sections, each with its own set of fields. Please fill out each section carefully to ensure that the bakery has all the necessary information for your custom order.
2.  **Review your answers carefully**. Double-check that all fields are filled out correctly, especially those marked as required.
3.  **Submit the form** once you're confident that all information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Information** (`customer_name`, text, required): Please enter your name.
*   **Email Address** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, phone, required): Enter your phone number.
*   **Company or Organization** (`company_organization`, text, optional): If you're representing a company or organization, enter the name.
*   **Event Type** (`event_type`, select\_one, required): Choose the type of event (e.g. Wedding, Birthday, etc.).
*   **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
*   **Estimated Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, integer, required): Enter the estimated number of guests attending the event.
*   **Detailed Order Description** (`order_description`, text, required): Describe the specific items you need for the order (e.g. cake, cookies, etc.).
*   **Primary Item** (`primary_item`, select\_one, required): Choose the main item you're ordering (e.g. cake, cookies, etc.).
*   **Number of Servings or Pieces Needed** (`servings_quantity`, integer, required): Enter the number of servings or pieces of the chosen item.
*   **Flavor Preferences** (`flavor_preferences`, text, required): Specify your flavor preferences for the order (e.g. chocolate, vanilla, etc.).
*   **Design or Decoration Requirements** (`design_requirements`, text, required): Mention any special design or decoration requests you have for the order.
*   **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, please mention them.
*   **Custom Toppers or Embellishments** (`custom_toppers`, select\_one, optional): Choose any custom toppers or embellishments you'd like (e.g. fresh flowers, fondant decorations, etc.).
*   **Personalization Needed** (`personalization`, text, optional): If you'd like personalization (e.g. names, messages, photos, etc.), specify the details.
*   **Delivery Option** (`delivery_option`, select\_one, required): Choose how you'd like to receive your order (e.g. bakery delivery, customer pickup, etc.).
*   **Delivery Location or Pickup Time** (`delivery_location`, text, required): Specify the delivery location or pickup time for your order.
*   **Estimated Budget** (`budget_estimate`, text, optional): Enter your estimated budget for the order (range in dollars).
*   **Special Requirements or Notes** (`special_requirements`, text, optional): If you have any special requirements or notes for the order, mention them.
*   **Quotation Request Confirmation** (`quotation_request_confirmation`, select\_one, required): Confirm that you'd like a custom quotation for this order.

## Tips

*   Make sure to check all required fields are filled out correctly.
*   Enter accurate information for all fields.
*   If unsure about any field, double-check the available options and descriptions.
*   If you need help with anything, contact us for assistance.
