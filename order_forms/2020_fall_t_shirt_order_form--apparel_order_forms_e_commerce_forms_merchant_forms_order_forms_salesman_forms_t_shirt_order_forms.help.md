# 2020 Fall T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 2020 Fall T Shirt Order Form is used for customers to place orders for customized T-shirts. This form captures essential information such as customer contact details, order details, shipping information, and payment preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form successfully, please follow these steps:

1. **Provide your Customer Information**: Enter your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. **Specify your Order Details**: Choose the design name or reference, select the T-shirt style and fabric material, and choose the primary T-shirt color(s).
3. **Define your Order Quantity**: Break down the quantity of T-shirts by size.
4. **Provide Shipping Information**: Select your preferred delivery method, enter your shipping address, and select your country.
5. **Make Payment Arrangements**: Choose your preferred payment method and enter any promotional code if applicable.
6. **Agree to Terms and Conditions**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the 2020 Fall T-Shirt Order Terms & Conditions.
7. **Add Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address for order confirmations and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you about your order.
* **Design Name or Reference**: Enter the name of your selected design or the reference number for your custom artwork.
* **T-Shirt Style**: Choose from the available styles: Unisex Crewneck Tee, Women's V-Neck Tee, Youth Size Tee, Long Sleeve Tee, Pullover Hoodie, or Tank Top.
* **Fabric Material**: Select from the available fabric materials: 100% Ringspun Cotton, 50/50 Cotton/Poly Blend, Performance Dry-Fit, or Organic Cotton.
* **Primary T-Shirt Color(s)**: Enter the primary color(s) for your selected design.
* **Quantity Breakdown by Size**: Enter the quantity of T-shirts by size, e.g., S:5, M:10, L:8, XL:5, 2XL:2, 3XL:1.
* **Special Print Instructions**: Enter any specific print instructions for your design, such as print location, size, or color changes.
* **Preferred Delivery Method**: Choose from the available delivery methods: Standard Shipping (3-7 Bus. Days), Express Shipping (1-2 Bus. Days), Local Pickup (Store Name), or Event Delivery (Specify Event).
* **Shipping Address Line 1**: Enter your shipping address, apartment/suite number, or P.O. Box.
* **City**: Enter the city for your shipping address.
* **State/Province**: Enter the state or province for your shipping address.
* **Zip/Postal Code**: Enter the zip or postal code for your shipping address.
* **Country**: Select from the available countries: United States, Canada, Mexico, United Kingdom, Germany, France, Australia, or Other.
* **Requested Delivery Date**: Enter a date for your delivery, please allow 2-3 weeks for production and shipping.
* **Preferred Payment Method**: Choose from the available payment methods: Credit/Debit Card (Online), PayPal, Bank Transfer, Invoice (Net 30, for approved accounts), or Cash (for Local Pickup).
* **Promotional Code**: Enter any valid discount or promotion code if applicable.
* **Agree to Terms & Conditions**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the 2020 Fall T-Shirt Order Terms & Conditions.
* **Additional Comments or Questions**: Enter any additional comments or questions you may have.
