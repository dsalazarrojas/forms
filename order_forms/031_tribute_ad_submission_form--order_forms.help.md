# Tribute Ad Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tribute Ad Submission Form is designed to collect personal and contact information from users who are interested in submitting a tribute advertisement for a special event or project. This form is intended to provide an easy way for individuals to provide their details to be contacted and receive further instructions on the submission process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
- Fill in your First Name
- Fill in your Last Name
- Fill in your Email
- Write a Message with your submission details

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Please enter a valid email address that you use regularly. This will help us contact you with further instructions.
- **Message** (`Message`, `text`, required: false): In this field, please provide any additional details about your submission, including the event or project name, your role, and any other relevant information. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required information to increase your chances of being contacted.
* Be clear and concise in your message to ensure we can understand your submission.
* If you have any issues filling this form or need more information, please contact the form creator at form@gic.mx.
