# Custom Ice Cream Cup Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you order a custom ice cream cup by selecting your desired flavor, size, and toppings.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your favorite flavor from the options provided.
2. Choose the size of your ice cream cup.
3. Choose any additional toppings you'd like to add to your cup.
4. Enter your email address (optional) and/or phone number (optional) for communication purposes.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, optional): A field to enter any additional information you'd like to share with us.
* **Flavor** (`flavor`, select_one, optional): Choose one of our available ice cream flavors: Apple, Strawberry, or Chocolate.
* **Size** (`size`, select_one, optional): Select the size of your ice cream cup: Small, Medium, or Large.
* **Topping 1** (`topping_1`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more from our available toppings: Sprinkles, Whipped cream, Hot Fudge, or Nuts.
* **Topping 2** (`topping_2`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more from our available toppings: Marshmallow, Cookie Dough, or Brownie bits.
* **Size 2** (`size2`, select_one, optional): Select the size of your second ice cream cup (if applicable).
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with your order details.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with your order details.

## Tips

* Please choose your flavor and size options carefully, as they cannot be changed once the order is submitted.
* You can add or remove toppings as needed.
* If you have any specific requests or allergies, please use the "Customer Info" field to let us know.
