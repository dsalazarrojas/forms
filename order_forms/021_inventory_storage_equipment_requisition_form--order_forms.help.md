# Inventory Storage Equipment Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inventory Storage Equipment Requisition Form is designed to collect information for storage equipment requisition requests. This form is used to request equipment storage and management, helping to track and manage storage needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the storage location where the equipment will be stored.
2. Choose the type of equipment being requested.
3. Enter the quantity of equipment needed.
4. Provide a brief description of the equipment.
5. Select the category of the equipment (e.g., General Use, Warehouse, or Special Use).
6. Enter the date when the equipment is needed.
7. Enter your email and contact information as the submitter.
8. Optionally, enter any notes or comments for the approver.
9. Submit the form to initiate the equipment storage and management process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Storage Location** (`storage_location`, select_one): Choose the location where the equipment will be stored. This will help determine the specific storage space for the equipment.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, select_one): Select the type of equipment being requested (e.g., Pallet, Crate, Box).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number): Enter the amount of equipment needed.
* **Description** (`description`, text): Briefly describe the equipment being requested.
* **Date Needed** (`date_needed`, date): Select the date when the equipment is needed.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text): Optionally, enter the name of the assigned tool or asset (if applicable).
* **Category** (`category`, select_one): Choose the category of the equipment (e.g., General Use, Warehouse, Special Use).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note): Enter any additional comments or notes for the approver.
* **Approver** (`approver`, text): Optionally, enter the name of the approver.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`, text): Enter your department or team.
* **Submitter Job Title** (`submitter_job_title`, text): Enter your job title.
* **Submitter Location** (`submitter_location`, text): Enter your location.
* **Approver** (`approver`, text): Optionally, enter the name of the approver.
* **Approver Email** (`approver_email`, email): Optionally, enter the approver's email address.
* **Approver Phone** (`approver_phone`, text): Optionally, enter the approver's phone number.
* **Approver Department** (`approver_department`, text): Optionally, enter the approver's department or team.
* **Approver Location** (`approver_location`, text): Optionally, enter the approver's location.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date): Select the date when the form was submitted.
* **Approve Date** (`approve_date`, date): Optionally, select the date when the form was approved.
* **Reject Date** (`reject_date`, date): Optionally, select the date when the form was rejected.
* **Submitter Notes** (`submitter_notes`, note): Enter any additional comments or notes for the submitter.
* **Approver Comments** (`approver_comments`, note): Enter any comments or feedback for the approver.
* **Submitter Status** (`submitter_status`, text): Enter the status of the submitter (e.g., Approved, Rejected, Pending).
