# Health Coaching Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to bill healthcare services provided to clients. Completing this form ensures that you accurately report services provided, payment details, and billing information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the client's name and contact information (email and phone number) in the "Client Name", "Email", and "Phone" fields.
2.  Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Payment Details" field, indicating whether the client has agreed to payment.
3.  Enter the billing address (if applicable) in the "Billing Address" field.
4.  Enter the total charges for the services provided in the "Total Charges" field.
5.  Select the tax rate applied to the services provided in the "Tax Rate" field.
6.  Select the payment method used for the services provided in the "Payment Method" field.
7.  Enter the date and time of service in the "Date of Service" and "Time of Service" fields (if applicable).
8.  Enter any additional service notes in the "Service Notes" field (if applicable).
9.  Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Order Type", "Service Type", "Client Status", and "Staff Status" fields, indicating their respective statuses.
10.  Enter the staff member's name and role in the "Staff Name" and "Staff Role" fields.
11.  Enter the second set of billing information in the "Total Charges2", "Tax Rate2", "Payment Method2", "Date of Service 2", "Time of Service 2", and "Service Notes 2" fields.
12.  Click "Submit" to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (text, required): Enter the client's full name.
* **Payment Details** (select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" if the client has agreed to payment, "No" otherwise.
* **Billing Address** (text, optional): Enter the billing address if applicable.
* **Total Charges** (number, optional): Enter the total charges for the services provided.
* **Tax Rate** (select_one, required): Select the tax rate applied to the services provided.
* **Payment Method** (select_one, required): Select the payment method used for the services provided.
* **Date of Service** (date, optional): Enter the date of service.
* **Time of Service** (time, optional): Enter the time of service.
* **Service Notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional service notes.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Order Type** (select_one, required): Select "Yes" if the order type is applicable.
* **Service Type** (select_one, required): Select "Yes" if the service type is applicable.
* **Client Status** (select_one, required): Select the client's status.
* **Staff Status** (select_one, required): Select the staff member's status.
* **Billing Date** (date, optional): Enter the billing date.
* **Billing Time** (time, optional): Enter the billing time.
* **Total Charges2** (number, required): Enter the total charges for the second set of services provided.
* **Tax Rate2** (number, required): Enter the tax rate applied to the second set of services provided.
* **Payment Method2** (select_one, required): Select the payment method used for the second set of services provided.
* **Date of Service 2** (date, required): Enter the date of service for the second set of services provided.
* **Time of Service 2** (time, required): Enter the time of service for the second set of services provided.
* **Service Notes 2** (note, required): Enter any additional service notes for the second set of services provided.
* **Staff Name** (text, required): Enter the staff member's name.
* **Staff Role** (text, required): Enter the staff member's role.
