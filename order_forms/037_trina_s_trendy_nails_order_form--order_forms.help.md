# Trina_s_Trendy_Nails_Order_Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers for nail services, including customer name, services requested, service time, number of customers, and date of visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:
1. Fill out the customer name field with the customer's name.
2. Select the service you wish to book from the services requested section.
3. Choose the service time that suits you best.
4. If you are booking for multiple customers, enter the number of customers in the number of customers field.
5. Select the date of visit for the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (customer_name, text, required): Please enter the customer's name.
* **Service Requested** (service_requested, select_one, required): Choose the service you wish to book. Options include Acrylic, Gel, and Nail art.
* **Service Time** (service_time, time, optional): Select the service time that suits you best. This field is for selecting the duration of the service.
* **Number of Customers** (number_of_customers, number, optional): If you are booking for multiple customers, enter the number of customers in this field. This field is used to ensure we have enough staff to assist you.
* **Date of Visit** (date_of_visit, date, required): Select the date of visit for the service. This field is used to schedule the service appointment. 

## Tips
- Be sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a successful form submission.
- If you are booking for multiple customers, please ensure the number of customers field is updated accordingly.
- For date of visit, select a date that suits you best, taking into account our available appointment slots.
