# Sports Shop Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to provide information about their orders for sports-related products or services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill in the required information accurately and thoroughly**. This ensures that the sports shop can efficiently process and fulfill your order.
2. **Provide detailed explanations for optional fields**. This will help the shop understand any special requirements or requests you have.
3. **Submit the form once you have completed all fields**. This will send your order information to the shop for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info (1)** (`Customer Info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name and contact details to help the shop get in touch with you for order updates.
* **Shipping Address (2)** (`Shipping Address`, `text`, required: false): Provide your address details for delivery of your order.
* **Order Details (3)** (`Order Details`, `text`, required: false): Describe the specific order you are placing, including any special instructions or requests.
* **Payment Details (4)** (`Payment Details`, `text`, required: false): Specify your payment method and details to avoid any delays in processing your order.
* **Order Items (5)** (`Order Items`, `text`, required: false): List the specific items you want to order, including quantity and any special requirements.
