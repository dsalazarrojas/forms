# Watch Design Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for customers to pre-order a custom watch design. It collects customer information, production details, and other essential data required for the production process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields (marked as required).
2. Enter your email address in the correct format (if you're asked for an email address).
3. Enter your phone number in the correct format (if you're asked for a phone number).
4. Sign your name to confirm the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Watch Details** (custom_watch_details, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the watch design you want to pre-order.
* **Customer Info** (customer_info, text, optional): Enter your name, address, and any other identifying information about yourself.
* **Deposit Details** (deposit_details, text, optional): Provide information about any deposit you have made or will make for your pre-order.
* **Production Planning** (production_planning, text, optional): Specify any production details, such as production date or material selection.
* **Order Confirmation** (order_confirmation, text, optional): Enter a brief confirmation of your pre-order, including any relevant details.
* **Customer Signature** (customer_signature, text, optional): Sign your name to confirm the pre-order, this is a requirement.
* **Email** (customer_email, email, required): Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., `john.doe@example.com`).
* **Phone** (customer_phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number in the correct format (e.g., `123-456-7890`).
* **Custom Watch Name** (custom_fields_10, text, optional): Enter a unique name for your custom watch design.
 
Note: The fields marked as required are those that you must fill in for the form to be valid. The fields marked as optional are those that you can skip if you're not providing that information.
