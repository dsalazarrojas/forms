# Home Cleaning Service Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Home Cleaning Service Subscription Form" is used to collect information from customers and service providers for home cleaning services. It is designed to help schedule and manage cleaning service appointments, handle billing and payment details, and provide additional notes and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the "Customer Info" page with your basic information.
2. On the "Service Preferences" page, select the frequency and type of cleaning service you require.
3. On the "Schedule Info" page, select the schedule time for your cleaning appointment.
4. On the "Billing Info" page, provide your billing information.
5. On the "Payment Preferences" page, choose your preferred payment method and frequency.
6. On the "Customer Notes" page, add any additional comments or notes about your request.
7. On the "Service Provider Info" page, add any information about the service provider.
8. Finally, review your form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

	+ **Email**: Enter your email address to be contacted by our service provider.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Billing Info**: Enter your billing address for payment processing.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your shipping address for service provider delivery.
* **Service Preferences**

	+ **Service Frequency**: Select how often you would like the service (Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly).

	+ **Schedule Time**: Choose the schedule time for your cleaning appointment (09:00, 10:00, 11:00, 12:00, 13:00, 14:00, 15:00, 16:00, 17:00, 18:00).
