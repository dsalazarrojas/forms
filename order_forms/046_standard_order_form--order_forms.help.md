# Standard Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Standard Order Form is a simple and straightforward form designed to collect information for a standard order. It's intended to capture order-related details, such as item numbers, products, and quantities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Item number` field with the corresponding item number for the order.
2. Select the desired `Product` from the dropdown list.
3. Enter the `Quantity` of the product being ordered.
4. Enter the `Unit price` of the product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order form** (`order_form`, text, required: false): This field is the main container for the order information.
* **Item number** (`item_number`, text, required: false): Enter the item number for the order, which helps identify the order uniquely.
* **Product** (`product`, select_one, required: false): Select the product being ordered from the list of available options.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required: false): Enter the quantity of the product being ordered.
* **Unit price** (`unit_price`, number, required: false): Enter the unit price of the product being ordered.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure the order can be processed correctly.
- If you're unsure about the product or quantity, please check with your sales representative for clarification.
- If you need to add or remove items from the order, please use our add or remove item functionality.
