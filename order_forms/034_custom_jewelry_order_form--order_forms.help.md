# Custom Jewelry Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Jewelry Order Form is a tool used by customers to provide their specifications for a custom jewelry piece. It helps our designers create a unique piece that meets their expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, fill out the form completely and accurately with your personal and order details.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you have provided the correct information.
3. Once you are satisfied that the form is complete, submit it for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info (1)**: Please enter your name and contact information to help us contact you with questions and updates about your order.
* **Jewelry Preferences (2)**: Describe the type of jewelry piece you would like to order, including any specific characteristics or features you would like to see in your custom piece. Be as specific as possible, including material, color, and style.
* **Order Details (3)**: Provide as much detail as you can about the order, including any special requests or instructions you have for our designers.
* **Payment Info (4)**: Enter your payment method and details to secure the order placement.

## Tips
* Be as clear and detailed as possible when providing your order specifications to ensure our designers can create the perfect custom piece.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can ask for clarification or guidance from our customer service team.
* Please review your form carefully before submission to avoid any errors or omissions.
