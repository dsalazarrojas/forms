# Electronics Home Entertainment Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to place a pre-order for home electronics products such as TVs, soundbars, and gaming consoles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the User Info section with your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your Delivery Info, including the address to which you want your order delivered.
3. Enter your Shipping Info, including the shipping address (if different from delivery).
4. Enter your Payment Info, including your method of payment.
5. List the items you wish to order in the Order Items section.
6. Add any additional notes about your order in the Order Notes section.
7. If you have any questions or concerns, or need additional assistance, you can contact Customer Support.
8. Finally, enter your Customer Support Phone number if you have any questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (User Info, Text, Required/Optional: Required): Enter your name and contact information so we can get in touch with you about your order.
* **Delivery Info** (Delivery Info, Text, Required/Optional: Required): Provide the address to which you want your order delivered.
* **Shipping Info** (Shipping Info, Text, Required/Optional: Required): Enter the address to which you want your order shipped (if different from delivery).
* **Payment Info** (Payment Info, Text, Required/Optional: Required): Enter your payment method and details.
* **Order Items** (Order Items, Text, Required/Optional: Required): List the items you wish to order, including product names and quantities.
* **Order Notes** (Order Notes, Note, Required/Optional: Required): Add any additional notes about your order, such as special instructions or requests.
* **Customer Support** (Customer Support, Text, Required/Optional: Required): Enter your contact information for customer support, in case you have any questions or issues.
* **Customer Support Phone** (Customer Support Phone, Text, Required/Optional: Required): Enter your phone number for customer support to contact you.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact customer support for assistance.
