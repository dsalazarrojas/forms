# shipping_documentation_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The shipping_documentation_appointment_form is designed to capture essential information for shipping documentation appointments, enabling efficient handling and processing of shipments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's sections and fields to understand what information is required for your shipping documentation appointment.
2. Select the "Schedule Appointment" options to indicate whether you need to schedule an appointment or not.
3. Answer the "Service Type" questions to select relevant services for your appointment.
4. Enter the shipping date and time in the respective fields.
5. Provide additional customer and shipping details in the text fields.
6. Enter contact information for the shipping contact person.
7. Review and update the shipping status as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **schedule_appointment** (schedule_appointment, select_one, required: false): Choose 'Yes' to schedule an appointment or 'No' if no appointment is needed.
* **service_type** (service_type, select_multiple, required: false): Select multiple services required for your shipment.
* **shipping_date** (shipping_date, date, required: false): Enter the desired shipping date.
* **shipping_time** (shipping_time, time, required: false): Enter the shipping time.
* **notes** (notes, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about your shipment.
* **contact_number** (contact_number, text, required: false): Enter the contact number of your shipping contact person.
* **email** (email, email, required: false): Enter the email of your shipping contact person.
* **customer_name** (customer_name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the customer.
* **shipping_address** (shipping_address, text, required: false): Enter the shipping address.
* **shipping_contact_name** (shipping_contact_name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the shipping contact person.
* **shipping_contact_number** (shipping_contact_number, text, required: false): Enter the contact number of the shipping contact person.
* **shipping_contact_email** (shipping_contact_email, email, required: false): Enter the email of the shipping contact person.
* **shipping_contact_relationship** (shipping_contact_relationship, select_one, required: false): Choose 'Yes' for a related shipping contact person or 'No' otherwise.
* **service_location** (service_location, text, required: false): Enter the location of the service.
* **documentation_details** (documentation_details, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about the documentation.
* **service_notes** (service_notes, note, required: false): Add any notes about the service provided.
* **shipping_status** (shipping_status, select_one, required: false): Choose 'Active' for an active shipping status or 'Inactive' otherwise.
* **shipping_status_comments** (shipping_status_comments, text, required: false): Enter comments about the shipping status.
* **assigned_to** (assigned_to, text, required: false): Enter the person assigned to handle the shipment.
* **shipping_date_2** (shipping_date_2, date, required: false): Enter a secondary shipping date.
* **shipping_time_2** (shipping_time_2, time, required: false): Enter the secondary shipping time.
* **additional_notes** (additional_notes, note, required: false): Add any additional notes.
* **shipping_contact_address** (shipping_contact_address, text, required: false): Enter the address of the shipping contact person.
* **shipping_documentation_type** (shipping_documentation_type, select_one, required: false): Choose 'Yes' for shipping documentation type or 'No' otherwise.
* **shipping_documentation_date** (shipping_documentation_date, date, required: false): Enter the shipping documentation date.
