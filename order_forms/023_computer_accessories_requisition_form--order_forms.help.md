# Computer Accessories Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from employees and departments for computer accessories requisition and approval process. It helps to ensure the right accessories are ordered and delivered to the right people in a timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and department in the first two fields.
2. Describe the computer accessory you need in the "Item requested" field.
3. Specify the quantity of items you need.
4. Provide a brief justification for your request, stating the reason you need the accessory.
5. Optionally, select a preferred vendor from the provided options (if applicable).
6. Indicate the delivery location for the accessory.
7. Leave the "Approval status" field blank for internal use.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester name** (`requester_name`, required/optional: true): This is your full name for identification purposes.
* **Department** (`department`, required/true): Please enter the department requesting the accessory.
* **Item requested** (`item_requested`, required/true): Describe the computer accessory you need, e.g., "Laptop keyboard" or "Printer ink cartridges".
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, required/true): Enter the whole number of items you need, e.g., "10" for 10 items.
* **Justification** (`justification`, required/true): Please provide a brief reason why you need this accessory, e.g., "For a meeting room presentation".
* **Preferred vendor** (`preferred_vendor`, optional/false): If applicable, select the vendor you would like to use for this order.
* **Delivery location** (`delivery_location`, required/true): Specify the location where the accessory should be delivered, e.g., your office or a specific room.
* **Approval status** (`approval_status`, optional/false): Leave this field blank for internal use, as it is automatically updated by the system.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out this form correctly to avoid duplication of work and get the accessories you need promptly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your department manager for guidance.
