# Drink Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Drink Order Form" is used to collect information from customers about their drink order. This form is typically used in a cafe or store where customers can order drinks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Drink Name" field to specify the type of drink you would like to order.
2. Select your preferred method of payment from the options provided in the "How will you be paying?" field.
3. If you have a specific drink in mind, enter the name in the "Drink Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Drink Name**: (`drink_name`, text, optional) Enter the type of drink you would like to order, e.g., "Cappuccino" or "Fresh Fruit Juice".
* **How will you be paying?**: (`drink_order_form_payment`, select_one, required) Select your preferred method of payment: "Cash", "Card", or "Online".
  - "Cash": Pay with cash at the counter.
  - "Card": Pay with a credit or debit card.
  - "Online": Pay online through our website or mobile app.
* There is no "Drink Order" field on this form. If you are unsure about how to complete the form, please ask a staff member for assistance.
