# store_opening_staff_badge_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The store_opening_staff_badge_order_form is designed to facilitate staff members in requesting store opening badges for themselves. This form ensures that the store has accurate and up-to-date information about staff badge requests, making it easier to process and manage these requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Staff Details**: Please provide your personal details, including your name, ID number, and any other relevant information about yourself.
2.  **Shipping Info**: Enter your shipping address, including the store address where you will receive your badge.
3.  **Payment**: Select your preferred payment method to process your badge request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Staff Details** (`staff_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your personal details, including your name, ID number, and any other relevant information about yourself.
*   **Shipping Info** (`shipping_info`, `text`, required): Enter your shipping address, including the store address where you will receive your badge.
*   **Payment** (`payment`, `text`, required): Select your preferred payment method to process your badge request.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your badge request, if needed.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that you have entered accurate and complete information to avoid delays or complications with your badge request.
*   If you have already submitted a request for a store opening badge, you do not need to submit another request.
*   If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact the store management for assistance.
