# Food Box Online Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect customer information and order details for online food box orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  In the **Customer Information** section, enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.
2.  In the **Contact** section, provide your address and phone number so that we can get in touch with you.
3.  In the **Order** section, specify the date and time you would like your order delivered.
4.  In the **Order Details** section, provide any special instructions or requests you may have, such as food allergies or dislikes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **Customer Information (customer_info)** (`Text`, optional)
    This field is where you can enter your contact information for us to reach out to you.
*  **Contact (contact_info)** (`Text`, optional)
    This field is where you can provide your address and phone number for us to contact you for your order.
*  **Order (order_info)** (`Text`, optional)
    This field is where you can specify the date and time you would like your order delivered.
*  **Order Details (order_details)** (`Text`, optional)
    This field is where you can provide any special instructions or requests you may have for your order, such as food allergies or dislikes.
