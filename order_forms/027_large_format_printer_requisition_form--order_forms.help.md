# Large Format Printer Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request the requisition of a large format printer for a customer's order. It captures information necessary for the printer to be shipped and delivered to the customer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Customer Name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Select the shipping method from the "shipping_method" dropdown.
3. Enter the shipping details in the "Shipping Details" field.
4. Provide the shipping cost in the "Shipping Cost" field.
5. Add any additional shipping notes in the "Shipping Notes" field.
6. Describe the product in detail in the "Product Description" field.
7. Enter the URL of the product image in the "Product Image URL" field.
8. Specify the quantity of the product ordered in the "Product Quantity" field.
9. Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field.
10. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
11. Enter the shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field.
12. Add any additional order notes in the "Order Notes" field.
13. Specify the order date in the "Order Date" field.
14. Specify the delivery date in the "Delivery Date" field.
15. Specify the order time in the "Order Time" field.
16. Enter the product weight in the "Product Weight" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header** (`form_header`, note, not required): This is a note field that provides a heading for the form.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, not required): Enter the customer's name.
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, select multiple, not required): Select the shipping method for the order (ground, express, priority mail).
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, not required): Enter any details necessary for shipping.
* **Shipping Cost** (`shipping_cost`, number, not required): Enter the shipping cost.
* **Shipping Notes** (`shipping_notes`, note, not required): Enter any additional shipping notes.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, not required): Describe the product being ordered.
* **Product Image URL** (`product_image_url`, text, not required): Enter the URL of the product image.
* **Product Quantity** (`product_quantity`, number, not required): Specify the quantity of the product ordered.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, not required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, not required): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, not required): Enter the shipping address.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, text, not required): Enter any additional order notes.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, not required): Specify the order date.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, not required): Specify the delivery date.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, not required): Specify the order time.
* **Product Weight** (`product_weight`, number, not required): Enter the product weight.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check all fields before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you've selected the correct shipping method and entered the correct shipping details.
* If you need help, don't hesitate to reach out to your team.
