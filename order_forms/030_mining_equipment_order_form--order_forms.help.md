# Mining Equipment Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for users to request mining equipment from suppliers. Please ensure the information you provide is accurate, and you are a valid contact person for your organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Supplier Name" field.
2. Provide your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
3. Select all applicable equipment types from the "Equipment Type" dropdown menu.
4. Enter the quantity of each equipment type you selected.
5. Choose a delivery date for your mining equipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your business cards or marketing materials. This is not required but is useful for our record keeping.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number for follow-up purposes. This is not required but helps us reach you for any urgent requests or updates.
* **Equipment Type**: Select all types of mining equipment you are interested in ordering. This could be anything from drilling machines to excavators.
* **Equipment Quantity**: Enter the quantity you need for each selected equipment type. Please be accurate to ensure we deliver the correct amount.
* **Delivery Date**: Select the date you would like to receive your mining equipment. Please note that this is not guaranteed but will help us schedule delivery accordingly.

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact our support team.
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting.
* If you need to order more than one type of equipment, you can select multiple options from the "Equipment Type" field.
