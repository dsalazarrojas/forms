# Album Printing Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your album printing requirements, enabling us to provide an accurate quote for your project.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the photographer or studio that will be associated with the album.
2. Provide your email address so we can contact you with any questions or updates.
3. Specify the preferred size of the album you are interested in.
4. Indicate the estimated number of spreads in the album.
5. Choose the preferred cover material for the album.
6. Select the type of paper to be used for the album printing.
7. Enter the number of albums you would like to have printed.
8. Decide if you would like custom embossing or debossing on your album.
9. If applicable, specify the color for the cover and lining of your album.
10. If you have a specific deadline for the project, please provide the date.
11. Enter your shipping destination, including zip code or city.
12. If you have a target budget for each album, please provide it.
13. In the "Additional Requests" field, feel free to include any further customization you may require.
14. If interested, select "True" for a sample album discount.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photographer or Studio Name** (`photographer_studio_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the photographer or studio associated with the album.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Provide your email address for communication purposes.
* **Preferred Album Size** (`album_size`, select_one, required): Choose from 8x8 inches, 10x10 inches, 12x12 inches, 10x14 inches (Landscape), or Custom (Specify in notes).
* **Estimated Number of Spreads** (`number_of_spreads`, number, required): Enter the number of spreads in your album (1 spread = 2 pages).
* **Cover Material Preference** (`cover_material_preference`, select_one, required): Select from Genuine Leather, Faux Leather (Vegan), Linen/Fabric, Photo Wrap (Hardcover), or Acrylic/Crystal Cover.
* **Paper Type Request** (`paper_type_request`, select_one, required): Choose from Standard Photo Paper, Premium Thick Layflat, Fine Art Paper, or Cotton Rag.
* **Quantity Requested** (`quantity_requested`, number, required): Enter the number of albums you would like to have printed.
* **Custom Embossing or Debossing** (`embossing_debossing`, select_one, required): Select from None, Studio Logo, Client Names/Date, or Both.
* **Preferred Color for Cover/Lining** (`color_preference`, text, optional): If applicable, specify the color for the cover and lining of your album.
* **Deadline Requirement** (`deadline_requirement`, date, optional): If you have a specific deadline, provide the date.
* **Shipping Destination** (`shipping_destination`, text, required): Enter your shipping destination, including zip code or city.
* **Target Budget per Unit** (`budget_per_unit`, text, optional): If you have a target budget for each album, feel free to provide it.
* **Additional Customization Requests** (`additional_requests_quote`, text, optional): In this field, include any additional customization requests you may have.
* **Sample Album Inquiry** (`sample_album_inquiry`, select_one, required): Select "True" if you are interested in a sample album discount.
