# Custom Sticker Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Sticker Order Form is designed to collect information from customers for custom sticker orders. This form helps us to create the custom order and ensures that all the necessary details are included.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. For fields with options, select the appropriate option from the dropdown list.
3. Ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details** (Order Details, note, not required): Please include any additional information that may affect the order or customization.
* **Sticker Quantity** (Sticker Quantity, number, required): Enter the number of stickers you would like to order. 
* **Shipping Address** (Shipping Address, note, not required): Enter your shipping address, this information is used to calculate shipping costs.
* **Shipping Phone** (Shipping Phone, text, not required): Enter your phone number for shipping purposes.
* **Shipping Email** (Shipping Email, email, not required): Enter your email address for shipping purposes.
* **Billing Name** (Billing Name, text, not required): Enter your name for billing purposes.
* **Billing Address** (Billing Address, note, not required): Enter your billing address.
* **Billing Phone** (Billing Phone, text, not required): Enter your phone number for billing purposes.
* **Billing Email** (Billing Email, email, not required): Enter your email address for billing purposes.
* **Order Total** (Order Total, number, required): Enter the total value of the order.
* **Shipping City** (Shipping City, text, not required): Enter your city for shipping purposes.
* **Shipping State** (Shipping State, text, not required): Enter your state for shipping purposes.
* **Shipping Zip** (Shipping Zip, text, not required): Enter your zip code for shipping purposes.
* **Shipping Country** (Shipping Country, text, not required): Enter your country for shipping purposes.
* **Billing City** (Billing City, text, not required): Enter your city for billing purposes.
* **Billing State** (Billing State, text, not required): Enter your state for billing purposes.
* **Billing Zip** (Billing Zip, text, not required): Enter your zip code for billing purposes.
* **Billing Country** (Billing Country, text, not required): Enter your country for billing purposes.
* **Sticker Design** (Sticker Design, select_one, required): Choose whether you would like the sticker to be custom or not.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_one, required): Choose the payment method for your order.

## Tips
* Please make sure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
