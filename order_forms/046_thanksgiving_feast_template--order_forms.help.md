# Thanksgiving Feasts Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Thanksgiving Feasts Template is a form designed to collect information for a Thanksgiving event. This form helps gather essential details such as guest lists, meal preferences, event specifics, and contact information to make your Thanksgiving celebration smooth and enjoyable.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing the title of your event, which will serve as a heading for the form.
2. Select the guests who will be attending your Thanksgiving feast.
3. Choose the main dish for the event, such as Turkey, Ham, or Fish.
4. If you'd like, add a side dish and any additional side dish information.
5. Select a dessert for the occasion.
6. Enter the time of your event, which will help guests know when to arrive.
7. Provide the location where the event will take place.
8. Enter the name and contact information (e.g., email, phone number) of the host or contact person.
9. Add any additional notes or comments you'd like to share with your guests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Main** (`text`, required: false): The heading for your event.
- **Guest List** (`select_multiple`, required: true): Select the guests attending your Thanksgiving celebration.
- **Main Dish** (`select_one`, required: true): Choose the main dish for the event.
- **Side Dish 1** (`text`, required: false): Add an optional side dish to complement your main dish.
- **Side Dish 2** (`text`, required: false): Add another optional side dish.
- **Dessert** (`text`, required: false): Choose a sweet treat for the occasion.
- **Event Time** (`time`, required: false): Enter the time of your event, so guests know when to arrive.
- **Location** (`text`, required: false): Provide the address or location of your event.
- **Contact Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name of the host or contact person.
- **Contact E-mail** (`email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the host or contact person.
- **Contact Phone** (`text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the host or contact person.
- **Notes** (`text`, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments you'd like to share with your guests.

## Tips
- Make sure to select the correct guest list, main dish, and event time to ensure a smooth and enjoyable celebration.
- You can add or remove fields as per your needs, but keep in mind that the more information you provide, the more helpful it will be for your guests.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the host or contact person listed in the form.
