# September Good Hart Cloth Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The September Good Hart Cloth Pre Order Form is a digital form designed for customers to pre-order their September collection from Good Hart Cloth. This form collects essential information necessary for the pre-order process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the required information for each field. The form requires all fields to be completed accurately and truthfully.
2. Select the pre-order items you wish to purchase from the list provided.
3. Enter the preorder date (MM/DD/YYYY) accurately.
4. Enter your store name, email, and phone number.
5. Write any additional order notes if necessary.
6. Review and confirm the information you've entered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Preorder Details
* **Preorder details** (`preorder_details`, text, optional): Please enter a brief description of your preorder.

### Select Preorder Items
* **Select Preorder Items** (`select_preorder_items`, select_multiple, required): Select all the items you wish to preorder from the list provided. You can select multiple items.

### Preorder Date
* **Preorder Date** (`preorder_date`, date, optional): Enter the preorder date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Store Name
* **Store Name** (`store_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the store you'll be collecting your preorder from.

### Order Notes
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, optional): If you have any additional information or notes to share with the store, enter them here.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address associated with your preorder.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number associated with your preorder.

### Confirm Preorder Details
* **Confirm Preorder Details** (`confirm_preorder_details`, text, optional): Confirm the preorder details you've entered.

### Confirm Store Name
* **Confirm Store Name** (`confirm_store_name`, text, optional): Confirm the store name you've entered.

### Confirm Order Notes
* **Confirm Order Notes** (`confirm_order_notes`, text, optional): Confirm the order notes you've entered.

### Confirm Email
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, text, optional): Confirm your email address.

### Confirm Phone
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, text, optional): Confirm your phone number.

### Tips
* Please ensure all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* Select all required fields to complete the form.
* Review your form carefully before submitting it.
