# Cosmetics Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Cosmetics Order Form" is used for customers to order cosmetics and provide necessary information for the order process.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by filling out the required fields: `Size` and any other fields marked as required.
2.  Enter your `First Name`, `Last Name`, and `Email` to identify yourself as a customer.
3.  Provide your `Address` and `Phone` for contact purposes.
4.  Enter the `Cosmetics Type` of product you are ordering (if applicable).
5.  Select the `Quantity` of product you need.
6.  Choose the `Size` of the product you need (if applicable).
7.  Enter the `Date of Birth`, `Order Date`, and `Assigned Date` for recordkeeping.
8.  Select the `Time of Visit` and `Order Time` for scheduling purposes.
9.  Enter any additional `Customer Messages` (up to 4) for further clarification or comments.
10. Finally, fill out the `Order Number` and `Assigned Tool` fields for tracking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Size** (`Size`, select_multiple, required): Choose the size of the product you need from the dropdown options.
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name to identify yourself as a customer.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name to identify yourself as a customer.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, required): Provide your address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Quantity** (`Quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of products you need.
* **Cosmetics Type** (`Cosmetics Type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of cosmetics you are ordering from the dropdown options.
* **Order Number** (`Order Number`, text, required): Enter the order number for tracking.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, text, required): Enter the tool assigned for the order.
* **Order Status** (`Order Status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of the order from the dropdown options.
* **Customer Message** (`Customer Message`, text, optional): Add any additional messages or comments for the customer.
* **Customer Message 2** (`Customer Message 2`, text, optional): Add any additional messages or comments for the customer.
* **Customer Message 3** (`Customer Message 3`, text, optional): Add any additional messages or comments for the customer.
* **Customer Message 4** (`Customer Message 4`, text, optional): Add any additional messages or comments for the customer.
