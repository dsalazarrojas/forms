# halloween_cookie_decorating_kit_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Halloween cookie decorating kit order form is used to collect customer information and order details for a custom cookie decorating kit order. This form helps us process orders efficiently and ensures that we have all the necessary information to fulfill the order correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your shipping address, including city and state.
2. Enter your customer name and email address.
3. If you have any special instructions or requests for your cookie decorations, please let us know in the "Order Notes" field.
4. Select the type of cookie you would like to order (e.g., cookie 1, cookie 2, or cookie 3).
5. Enter the quantity of cookie decorations you would like to order.
6. Fill in the product name and price.
7. Enter the shipping total, order subtotal, tax, and total amounts.
8. Choose your payment type (Yes or No).
9. Enter the product price range for the custom cookie decorations.
10. Fill in any additional information about your order, such as cookie decorations and quantity total.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order**: Enter a brief description of your order.
* **Shipping**: Enter your shipping address.
* **City**: Enter the city for your shipping address.
* **State**: Enter the state for your shipping address.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date you would like to place your order.
* **Order Notes**: Enter any special requests or instructions for your custom cookie decorations.
* **Cookie Type**: Select the type of cookie you would like to order (e.g., cookie 1, cookie 2, or cookie 3).
* **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of cookie decorations you would like to order.
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product you are ordering.
* **Product Price**: Enter the price of the product.
* **Shipping**: Enter the shipping cost.
* **Order Subtotal**: Enter the subtotal of your order.
* **Tax**: Enter the tax amount.
* **Total**: Enter the total amount of your order.
* **Payment Type**: Choose your payment type (Yes or No).
* **Product Price Range**: Enter the price range for the custom cookie decorations.
* **Shipping Cost**: Enter the shipping cost.
* **Order Total**: Enter the total amount of your order.
* **Product Price Total**: Enter the total price of all products in your order.
* **Cookie Decorations**: Enter any additional information about your cookie decorations.
* **Quantity Total**: Enter the total quantity of cookie decorations in your order.
* **Form Footer**: This field is for any additional information or comments.
