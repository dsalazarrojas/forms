# Craft Kit Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Craft Kit Subscription Form is designed to collect contact information from users who want to subscribe to our craft kit service. This form helps us gather essential details to provide updates, notifications, and efficient service delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Email in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your Address in the "Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, Required): Enter your first name to help us personalize communication.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, Required): Enter your last name for our records.
* **Email** (Email, email, Required): Enter your primary email address for us to send updates and notifications.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, Optional): Provide your phone number for us to contact you in case of any issues or updates.
* **Address** (Address, text, Optional): Enter your address for our records and potential delivery purposes.

## Tips
* Ensure you enter accurate and up-to-date information to help us communicate with you efficiently.
* If you have any special requests or comments, please let us know in your message field (not included in this form). 
* Please submit this form only once to avoid duplicate records.
* Our team will process your subscription, and we'll contact you soon!
