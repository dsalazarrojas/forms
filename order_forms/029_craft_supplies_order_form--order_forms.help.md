# craft_supplies_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The craft supplies order form is used to place a new order for craft supplies. Fill out the form to provide the necessary information for the order to be processed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the product you would like to order by clicking on the "Select a Product" dropdown.
2. Enter the quantity of the product you would like to order.
3. Choose the delivery date for your order.
4. Select your payment method: either card or cash.
5. Enter any additional notes or information about your order, if needed.
6. Enter your email address so we can contact you with order updates.
7. Enter your phone number, if you would like to be contacted about your order.
8. Click "Submit" to complete and submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select a Product (product_select)**: Choose the product you would like to order.
* **Quantity (quantity)**: Enter the number of the selected product you would like to order.
* **Delivery Date (delivery_date)**: Enter the date you would like your order to be delivered.
* **Payment Method (payment_method)**: Select your payment method: card or cash.
* **Notes (notes)**: Enter any additional notes or information about your order.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with order updates.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number, if you would like to be contacted about your order.
* **Submit (submit)**: Click to complete and submit your order.
* **Confirm (confirm)**: This is not a required field and will be visible only to ensure that you are aware that the form has been submitted.
* **Customer Information (customer_info)**: This field is not visible on the form but is used for customer information.
