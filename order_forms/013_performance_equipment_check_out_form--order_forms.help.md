# Performance Equipment Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Performance Equipment Check Out Form" is used to track the status of performance equipment being checked out for use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Order Form" section to indicate whether or not the equipment is being ordered.
2. Enter the borrower's information in the "borrower_info" field. This field is not required, but it's recommended to provide additional context about who the equipment is being checked out to.
3. In the "equipment_info" field, select the type of equipment being checked out. This field is not required, but it helps to ensure that the correct equipment is being checked out.
4. Enter the order date in the "order_date" field. This date should be the date when the equipment was first ordered or requested.
5. Enter the return date in the "return_date" field. This date should be the date when the equipment is expected to be returned.
6. Enter the order time in the "order_time" field. This time should be the time when the equipment was first ordered or requested.
7. Enter the return time in the "return_time" field. This time should be the time when the equipment is expected to be returned.
8. Select the order status in the "order_status" field. This field is not required, but it helps to track the current status of the equipment.
9. Enter any additional notes in the "notes" field. This field is not required, but it's a good place to provide extra information about the equipment being checked out.
10. Enter the borrower's email in the "email" field. This field is not required, but it helps to ensure that the borrower can be reached for any updates or reminders.
11. Enter the borrower's phone number in the "phone" field. This field is not required, but it helps to ensure that the borrower can be reached for any updates or reminders.
12. If you're checking out a specific piece of equipment, enter its ID in the "equipment_id" field. This field is not required, but it helps to track the specific equipment being checked out.
13. If you're checking out a specific piece of equipment, enter its name in the "equipment_name" field. This field is not required, but it helps to track the specific equipment being checked out.
14. If you're checking out a piece of equipment of a specific type, select the corresponding type in the "equipment_type" field.
15. If you're checking out a piece of equipment to someone, select "Yes" or "No" in the "assigned_to" field to indicate whether the equipment is being assigned to that person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Form** (1, select_multiple, required/optional): This field allows you to indicate whether the equipment is being ordered or not.
* **borrower_info** (2, text, required/optional): This field is where you enter the borrower's information.
* **equipment_info** (3, select_multiple, required/optional): This field is where you select the type of equipment being checked out.
* **order_date** (4, date, required): This field is where you enter the date when the equipment was first ordered or requested.
* **return_date** (5, date, required): This field is where you enter the date when the equipment is expected to be returned.
* **order_time** (6, time, required): This field is where you enter the time when the equipment was first ordered or requested.
* **return_time** (7, time, required): This field is where you enter the time when the equipment is expected to be returned.
* **order_status** (8, select_one, required/optional): This field is where you select the current status of the equipment.
* **notes** (9, text, required/optional): This field is where you enter any additional notes or comments about the equipment being checked out.
* **email** (10, email, required/optional): This field is where you enter the borrower's email address.
* **phone** (11, text, required/optional): This field is where you enter the borrower's phone number.
* **equipment_id** (12, number, required/optional): This field is where you enter the ID of the equipment being checked out.
* **equipment_name** (13, text, required/optional): This field is where you enter the name of the equipment being checked out.
* **equipment_type** (14, select_multiple, required/optional): This field is where you select the type of equipment being checked out.
* **assigned_to** (15, select_multiple, required/optional): This field is where you select whether the equipment is being assigned to the borrower or not.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information in each field.
* Double-check the fields before submitting the form.
