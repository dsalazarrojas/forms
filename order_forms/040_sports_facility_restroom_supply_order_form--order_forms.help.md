# Sports Facility Restroom Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the ordering of restrooms supplies for sports facilities, ensuring that staff and players have access to clean and hygienic facilities. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure accurate orders are placed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide a brief description of your order in the text field at the top of the form.
2. Select 'Yes' if you need to order restroom supplies, or 'No' if you don't.
3. Enter the quantity of supplies needed for each order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Form Text** (text, required): A short description of your order, e.g., "Ordering paper towels and toilet paper for the main restroom".
* **Restroom Supplies** (select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you need to order restroom supplies, or 'No' if you don't.
* **Quantity** (number, required): Enter the quantity of supplies needed, e.g., "10 paper towels".
