# product_purchase_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers for a product purchase order. It is intended for both B2B and B2C transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your **Customer Info**, including your name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
2. Enter your **Shipping Info**, including the delivery address and any special instructions.
3. Enter your **Payment Info**, including the payment method and any relevant payment details.
4. Enter your **Product Info**, including the product name, description, and quantity.
5. Enter your **Product Order**, including the order details and any specific requirements.
6. Review the **Order Terms**, to ensure you understand the terms and conditions of the order.
7. Add any additional **Note** for the order.
8. Enter your **Email** address.
9. Enter your **Phone** number.
10. Enter the **Date** of the order.
11. Enter the **Time** of the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (`Customer Info`, text, required: false):
This field is where you provide your personal or company contact information. Enter your name, company name, and any other relevant details.
* **Shipping Info** (`Shipping Info`, text, required: false):
Enter the delivery address and any special instructions for the order.
* **Payment Info** (`Payment Info`, text, required: false):
Enter the payment method and any relevant payment details.
* **Product Info** (`Product Info`, text, required: false):
Enter the product name, description, and quantity.
* **Product Order** (`Product Order`, text, required: false):
Enter the order details and any specific requirements.
* **Order Terms** (`Order Terms`, text, required: false):
Review the order terms and conditions to ensure you understand them.
* **Note** (`Note`, note, required: false):
Add any additional notes or comments for the order.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required: false):
Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required: false):
Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required: false):
Enter the date of the order.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, required: false):
Enter the time of the order.
* **Submit Form** (`Submit Form`, text, required: false):
Click this button to submit the form.
