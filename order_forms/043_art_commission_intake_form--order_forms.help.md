# Art Commission Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Art Commission Intake Form is designed to collect essential information from clients to ensure that their custom art commission requirements are accurately understood and met. It serves as a communication tool for clients and artists, helping to clarify expectations and prevent misunderstandings. Please fill out this form thoroughly and accurately to ensure a successful art commission process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your contact information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Describe how you found the artist's work to help us understand your connection with them.
3. In the "Commission Vision and Concept" section, provide a detailed description of the artwork you envision, including the subject matter and any specific requirements.
4. Specify your artistic style preference and the medium you prefer for the artwork.
5. Provide information on the artwork's specifications, such as the preferred width, height, and unit of measurement.
6. Indicate your color preferences or palette.
7. Specify your preferred payment schedule and budget range.
8. Indicate the number of revision rounds you expect and how you would like to proceed with the approval process.
9. Add any additional notes or special requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Your full name is required to contact you and confirm your identity.
* **Email Address**: Your email address is required to communicate with you regarding your commission.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number is required for any urgent matters or schedule coordination.
* **Address**: This field is optional but helps us understand your location.
* **How did you find my work**: This helps us understand how you discovered the artist and if there are any specific reasons why you're interested in their work.
* **Describe Your Commission Vision**: This is the most important section, providing a detailed description of the artwork you envision, including subject matter, style, and any specific requirements.
* **Inspiration or Reference Images**: If you have any reference images or links to specific artworks that inspire you, please share them here.
* **Artistic Style Preference**: Describe the style of art you prefer, e.g., realistic, abstract, modern, traditional, etc.
* **Preferred Medium**: Specify the medium you would like the artwork to be created in, e.g., painting, sculpture, digital, etc.
* **Preferred Width**: Enter the preferred width of the artwork.
* **Preferred Height**: Enter the preferred height of the artwork.
* **Unit of Measurement**: Select the unit of measurement for the artwork's dimensions.
* **Color Preferences or Palette**: If you have any color preferences or a specific palette in mind, describe them here.
* **Subject Matter or Theme**: Specify the subject matter or theme of the artwork.
* **When do you need this completed**: Enter the deadline for the completion of the artwork.
* **Budget Range**: Select the budget range you are comfortable with for the commission.
* **Preferred Payment Schedule**: Select the payment schedule you prefer, e.g., full payment upfront, 50% deposit, etc.
* **Revision Expectations**: Indicate the number of revision rounds you expect and how you would like to proceed with the approval process.
* **Revision Expectations**: This field is required to ensure we understand your expectations for the revision process.
* **How will approval process work**: Describe how you would like the approval process to work, e.g., in-person meetings, digital proofs, etc.
* **Additional Notes or Special Requirements**: Provide any additional notes or special requirements for the commission.
