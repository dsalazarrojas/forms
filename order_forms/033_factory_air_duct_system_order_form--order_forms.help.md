# Factory Air Duct System Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Air Duct System Order Form is designed to collect information required for the fabrication of custom air duct systems for factories. This form is crucial for accurately capturing the specifications of the system, ensuring that the manufactured product meets the customer's requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Start" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Fill out the required fields on the first page ("main-page") with the necessary information about the system you would like to order:
	* Provide a title for your order.
3. Proceed to the second page ("system-info") to input system-specific details:
	* Enter the required information.
4. Move on to the third page ("order-info") to enter additional order-specific information:
	* Enter the details as requested.
5. Double-check all fields to ensure accuracy and completeness before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Factory Air Duct System Order Form** (`title`, required): Enter a brief and descriptive title for your order.
* **System Info** (`system-info`, required): Provide detailed information about the system you would like to order, including any relevant specifications or technical details.
* **Order Info** (`order-info`, required): Enter any additional order-specific information that is required.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out to avoid submission errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
