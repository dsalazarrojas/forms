# Retro Sports Shirts Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retro Sports Shirts Order Form is designed to collect information from customers to place an order for a retro sports shirt.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your **Customer Name** in the first field. This is a required field to ensure we have your name on record.
2. Enter your **Shipping Address** in the second field. This field should include your full shipping address.
3. Choose your **Payment Method** from the available options. You can select between Bank Transfer, Credit Card, or PayPal.
4. Select your desired **Shirt Size** from the options provided (Small, Medium, Large, X-Large).
5. Choose the colors you'd like for your shirt from the **Color Choice** field. You can select multiple colors.
6. Enter the **Order Date** and **Order Time** for your desired order.
7. Provide your **Phone Number** for any follow-up or communication.
8. Enter your **Email** address so we can contact you for any updates or notifications.
9. Add any **Special Instructions** or notes about your order in the last field. This is an optional field for any additional comments you'd like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (Text Field, Required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Shipping Address** (Text Field, Optional): Enter your full shipping address for delivery.
* **Payment Method** (Select One, Optional): Choose how you'd like to pay for your order (Bank Transfer, Credit Card, or PayPal).
* **Shirt Size** (Select One, Optional): Select your desired shirt size (Small, Medium, Large, X-Large).
* **Color Choice** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the colors you'd like for your shirt. You can choose one or multiple colors.
* **Order Date** (Date Field, Optional): Enter the date you'd like to place your order.
* **Order Time** (Time Field, Optional): Enter the time you'd like to place your order.
* **Phone Number** (Text Field, Optional): Enter your phone number for any follow-up or communication.
* **Email** (Email Field, Optional): Enter your email address for any updates or notifications.
* **Special Instructions** (Note Field, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
