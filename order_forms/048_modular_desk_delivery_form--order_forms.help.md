# Modular Desk Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and manage desk delivery orders, providing a structured way to capture customer and delivery information. It's intended to be used for creating new orders, updating existing ones, or viewing details of a specific order.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1.  Begin by filling out the Desk Order Details section, providing information about the desk being delivered.
2.  Proceed to the Delivery Information section, entering the required details such as delivery method, status, and address.
3.  Move on to the Customer Information section, filling out the required fields with the customer's details.
4.  In the Order Details section, enter any relevant notes, order number, date, status, and total.
