# Delivery Service Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Delivery Service Intake Form is used to collect information about a customer's delivery service request.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read through each field to ensure you have filled out all required and optional fields.
- Fill out the form with your service details.
- Make sure to submit the form once you have completed all necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Details (1)** (`Service Details`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the service you require.
* **Service Location (2)** (`Service Location`, select, optional): Select the service location from the provided options.
* **Delivery Location (3)** (`Delivery Location`, text, optional): Specify where the delivery should be made to.
* **Delivery Time Frame (4)** (`Delivery Time Frame`, time, optional): Choose a specific time frame for the delivery.
* **Delivery Date (5)** (`Delivery Date`, date, optional): Pick the date for the delivery.
* **Service Details (6)** (`Service Details`, text, optional): Further describe the service you require.
* **Service Type (7)** (`Service Type`, text, optional): Specify the type of service you need (e.g., package, item, etc.).
* **Service Frequency (8)** (`Service Frequency`, select multiple, optional): Choose from the options (daily, weekly, monthly) for the frequency of the service.
* **Contact Phone (9)** (`Contact Phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the customer.
* **Contact Email (10)** (`Contact Email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the customer.
* **Additional Details (11)** (`Additional Details`, text, optional): Enter any extra information related to the service.
* **Notes (12)** (`Notes`, note, optional): Leave any comments or remarks for the delivery team.
* **Delivery Notes (13)** (`Delivery Notes`, text, optional): Enter any delivery-specific notes or comments.
