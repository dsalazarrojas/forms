# Clearpay Furniture Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for customers to place an order for furniture by providing their personal and shipping information, selecting the items they wish to purchase, and providing any additional details or customization requests.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out all applicable fields to provide the necessary information for your order.
- Ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any delays or issues with your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information**: Please enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact details as per your preference.
* **Billing Address**: Provide the address where you would like to receive your invoice or payment information. If different from your shipping address, please ensure to include this field.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the address where you would like your ordered furniture to be delivered.
* **Furniture Items**: Select the items you wish to purchase from our catalog. You can enter one or multiple items, separated by commas.
* **Customization Options**: If you have any requests for customization (e.g., color, size, material), please specify them here.
* **Order Summary**: Summarize your order to ensure it matches your expectations (e.g., item quantity, price, delivery date).
* **Delivery Options**: Choose your preferred delivery method and date.
* **Additional Information**: If you have any special requests or comments, please provide them here.

## Tips
- Double-check that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any issues with your order, please contact our customer support team for assistance.
- If you need to make changes to your order, please note them down in the "Additional Information" field.
