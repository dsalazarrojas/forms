# Apparel Selection And Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Apparel Selection And Order Form" is a comprehensive digital tool designed to gather customer information and preferences for a seamless apparel ordering experience. This form is crucial for customers to provide accurate details about their desired garments, including type, size, color, and customization options. On the other hand, it allows order processors to efficiently process customer requests, ensuring timely deliveries and satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your **Full Name** in the "Full Name" field.
2.  Enter your **Email Address** and **Phone Number**.
3.  Specify your **Organization Name** (if applicable).
4.  Choose the desired **Garment Type** from the provided options.
5.  Select the desired **Size** for your chosen garment.
6.  Choose the **Color** for your garment.
7.  Select your **Material Preference** (if applicable).
8.  Specify the desired **Quantity** of the garment.
9.  If you want custom options, provide them in the "Design or logo details" field.
10.  Enter your **Shipping Address** details, including **City**, **State/Province**, and **ZIP/Postal Code**.
11.  Choose a **Preferred Delivery Date**.
12.  Finally, review your selections carefully and submit the form to initiate your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`full_name`, **Text**, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, **Email, required): Enter a valid email address for communication.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, **Text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Organization Name** (`organization`, **Text, optional): If you're ordering on behalf of an organization, provide the name here.
*   **Garment Type** (`garment_type`, **Select One, required): Choose from the options: T-Shirt, Polo Shirt, Hoodie, Sweatshirt, Jacket, Cap, or Long Sleeve Shirt.
*   **Size** (`size_selection`, **Select One, required): Select your size from the available options: XS, S, M, L, XL, 2XL, or 3XL.
*   **Color** (`color_selection`, **Select One, required): Choose the color for your selected garment from the available options: Black, White, Navy, Blue, Red, Green, Gray, or Custom.
*   **Material Preference** (`material_preference`, **Select One, optional): Select from 100 percent Cotton, Cotton Blend, Polyester, Performance Fabric, or Organic Cotton.
*   **Quantity Ordered** (`quantity_ordered`, **Number, required): Indicate the amount of the garment you wish to purchase.
*   **Add Custom Logo or Design?** (`add_custom_logo`, **Select One, optional): If you want to add a custom logo or design, select 'True'. Otherwise, select 'False'.
*   **Design or Logo Details** (`design_details`, **Text, optional): If you've selected 'True' for the previous field, describe the design or logo here.
*   **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, **Text, required): Enter your shipping address.
*   **City** (`city`, **Text, required): Enter the city where you want the order delivered.
*   **State/Province** (`state`, **Text, required): Enter the state or province.
*   **ZIP/Postal Code** (`postal_code`, **Text, required): Enter the ZIP or postal code for the delivery address.
*   **Preferred Delivery Date** (`preferred_delivery_date`, **Date, required): Choose the preferred date for your order delivery.

**Tips**:

*   Review your selections carefully to ensure accuracy.
*   Double-check the address entered in the **Shipping Address**, **City**, **State/Province**, and **ZIP/Postal Code** fields for correct delivery.
*   If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact our order processing team.
