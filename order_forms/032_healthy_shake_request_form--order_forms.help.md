# Healthy Shake Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Healthy Shake Request Form is a form designed to gather information from users for orders of healthy shakes. This form is used to collect data for processing orders and providing services to users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your user information in the "User Information" section.
2. Select your address type from the "Address Information" section.
3. Choose your shake order from the "Order Information" section.
4. If you have any special payment information, fill it in the "Payment Information" section (this is optional).
5. Choose your delivery preference from the "Delivery Information" section (this is optional).
6. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" section (this is optional).
7. Fill in your contact email and phone number in the "Contact Information" section (this is optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Information (<user_input>)
* **User Information** (text, required): Fill in your name, email, and any other relevant information about yourself.

### Address Information (<address>)
* **Address Information** (select multiple, required): Choose where you would like your order to be delivered: Home or Work. Please note that delivery options may vary based on your selection.

### Order Information (<order>)
* **Order Information** (select one, required): Select one of the following shakes: Mango Madness or Other. Please choose according to your preference.

### Payment Information (<payment>)
* **Payment Information** (number, optional): Fill in any additional payment information, such as gift card number or other payment details, if applicable. Note that this field is not required.

### Delivery Information (<delivery>)
* **Delivery Information** (select multiple, optional): Choose from the following options: Pickup or Delivery. Select "Pickup" if you prefer to collect your order in person. Select "Delivery" if you prefer to have it delivered to your home or office.

### Notes (<notes>)
* **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your order, if you have any.

### Contact Information (<contact_email> and <contact_phone> and <contact_phone2>)
* **Contact Email** (email, optional): Fill in your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (text, optional): Fill in your contact phone number.
* **Contact Phone 2** (text, optional): Fill in your secondary contact phone number.
