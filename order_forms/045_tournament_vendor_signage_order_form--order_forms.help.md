# Tournament Vendor Signage Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about tournament vendor signage orders for processing and management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email**. These are required fields for order tracking purposes.
2. Enter your **Phone** number for easy contact.
3. Choose a **Date** for the order (MM/DD/YYYY format).
4. Provide a brief **Signage Description** of the requested signage.
5. If applicable, enter your **Vendor ID** to help us track your account.
6. If needed, include **Special Instructions** for any specific requests.
7. Add **Event Details** for further reference (e.g., event name, location).
8. If assigned a specific **Tool**, enter the tool's name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your surname.
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, **required**): Enter the email address we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, **required**): Choose the date for the order (MM/DD/YYYY format).
* **Signage Description** (`signage_description`, `text`, **optional**): Provide a brief description of the requested signage.
* **Vendor ID** (`vendor_id`, `text`, **optional**): If you have a vendor account, enter your unique ID.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `text`, **optional**): Add any special instructions for the order.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, `text`, **optional**): Enter any additional event details, such as name and location.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, **optional**): If assigned a specific tool, enter the tool's name.
