# sports_event_ticket_portal_access_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from event organizers and attendees for sports events. It gathers essential details for event management, ticket sales, and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with the required and optional information as prompted.
2. Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
3. Review your entries before submission to ensure correctness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Organizer Info** (`event_organizer_info`, text, optional): This field is for the event organizer's contact information. Please provide the name of the person or organization responsible for the event.
* **Attendee Info** (`attendee_info`, text, optional): This field is for the attendee's details. Please provide any relevant information about the attendee, such as name, email, or phone number.
* **Ticket Info** (`ticket_info`, text, optional): This field is for ticket-related information. Please enter ticket prices, availability, or any other relevant details about the tickets.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): This field is for the contact information of the event organizer or representative. Please provide a name, email, or phone number for communication purposes.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): This field is for event-related information. Please provide any details about the event, such as date, time, location, or description.
* **Ticket Sales** (`ticket_sales`, text, optional): This field is for ticket sales information. Please enter ticket sales data, such as quantity sold or remaining stock.
