# Print Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Print Order Form is used to collect information from customers for printing services orders.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the order details.
2. Specify if the order should be printed.
3. Choose the paper type and size.
4. Select the print resolution and orientation.
5. Enter the date and time needed for the order.
6. Add any additional notes about the order.
7. Enter your contact information: email, phone number, name, company, address, city, state, zip, and country.
8. Enter your vendor information: name, phone number, fax number, and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details** (print_order_form_1, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the order.
* **Print** (print_order_form_2, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" to confirm that the order should be printed.
* **Paper Type** (print_order_form_3, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of paper for the order.
* **Paper Size** (print_order_form_4, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the size of the paper for the order.
* **Paper Color** (print_order_form_5, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the color of the paper for the order.
* **Quantity** (print_order_form_6, number, required/optional): Enter the quantity of the order.
* **Print Resolution** (print_order_form_7, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the print resolution for the order.
* **Print Orientation** (print_order_form_8, select_one, required/optional): Choose the orientation of the print.
* **Date Needed** (print_order_form_9, date, required/optional): Enter the date needed for the order.
* **Time Needed** (print_order_form_10, time, required/optional): Enter the time needed for the order.
* **Notes** (print_order_form_11, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes about the order.
* **Email** (print_order_form_12, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (print_order_form_13, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Name** (print_order_form_14, text, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **Company** (print_order_form_15, text, required/optional): Enter your company name.
* **Address** (print_order_form_16, text, required/optional): Enter your address.
* **City** (print_order_form_17, text, required/optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (print_order_form_18, text, required/optional): Enter your state.
* **Zip** (print_order_form_19, text, required/optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Country** (print_order_form_20, text, required/optional): Enter your country.
* **Vendor** (print_order_form_21, text, required/optional): Enter your vendor's name.
* **Vendor Phone** (print_order_form_23, text, required/optional): Enter your vendor's phone number.
* **Vendor Fax** (print_order_form_24, text, required/optional): Enter your vendor's fax number.
* **Vendor Email** (print_order_form_25, email, required/optional): Enter your vendor's email address.
