# Key Handover Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Key Handover Check In Form is designed to document the process of handing over keys, items, or equipment between authorized personnel. This form helps to track the status of items, ensures accountability, and prevents loss or misplacement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the User ID of the person checking in the item.
2. Enter the Item ID of the item being checked in.
3. Select the type of item being checked in (Key, Vehicle, or Equipment).
4. Enter the date of the item check-in.
5. If applicable, enter the date the item is expected to be checked out (Check Out Date).
6. If applicable, enter the name of the person who checked the item out (Checked Out By).
7. Enter any additional comments or information about the handover.
8. Enter your phone number and email for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User ID** (`user_id`, `number`, required): Enter the unique ID assigned to you as a user.
* **Item ID** (`item_id`, `number`, required): Enter the unique ID assigned to the item being checked in.
* **Item Type** (`item_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of item being checked in. Options are Key, Vehicle, or Equipment.
* **Check In Date** (`check_in_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the item was checked in.
* **Check Out Date** (`check_out_date`, `date`, required): If the item is being checked out, enter the date it is expected to be checked out.
* **Checked Out By** (`checked_out_by_k`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person who checked the item out (if applicable).
* **Check Out By** (`check_out_by`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person who will be checking the item out (if applicable).
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, optional): Enter any assigned tool or device number (if applicable).
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the handover.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.

## Tips
- Ensure that you enter accurate information for each field to prevent errors and ensure consistency in the data collection.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or team lead for assistance.
- Keep the form up-to-date and accurate to maintain a precise record of key handovers.
