# Weather Station Equipment Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weather Station Equipment Delivery Form is designed to collect information for the delivery of weather station equipment. This form helps users schedule and coordinate the delivery of weather station equipment to the desired location.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the date for the delivery of the weather station equipment.
2. Select the type of equipment being delivered (Weather Station, Weather Monitor, or Anemometer).
3. Enter the address where the delivery will take place.
4. Provide the contact information for the client or person responsible for receiving the equipment.
5. Specify the delivery terms (yes or no, indicating if any special handling instructions are required).
6. Add any additional notes or comments about the delivery.
7. Enter the name of the client or person receiving the equipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **delivery_date**
`delivery_date` is the date when the equipment will be delivered.

### 2. **equipment_type**
`equipment_type` is the type of weather station equipment being delivered. This can be Weather Station, Weather Monitor, or Anemometer. Choose the one that best describes the equipment being delivered.

### 3. **delivery_address**
`delivery_address` is the address where the weather station equipment will be delivered. Enter the complete address, including the street number and any additional information.

### 4. **delivery_contact**
`delivery_contact` is the name and contact information of the person receiving the equipment or the client's representative.

### 5. **delivery_terms**
`delivery_terms` indicates if any special handling instructions are required. Choose 'yes' if there are any specific instructions for handling the equipment, and 'no' otherwise.

### 6. **delivery_notes**
`delivery_notes` is a field for any additional comments or notes about the delivery. You can add any relevant details or instructions that may be helpful for the delivery team.

### 7. **client_name**
`client_name` is the name of the client or person receiving the weather station equipment. Enter the correct name to ensure accurate records and communication.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the information you enter in each field to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the support team for help.
* Please note that the delivery date and time will be confirmed once the form is submitted.
