# Meal Kit Delivery Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meal Kit Delivery Order Form is designed to collect information from customers for meal kit delivery orders. This form will help us process orders efficiently and ensure that we have all the necessary details to deliver the meal kits correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in your User Information (Page 1, User Information)
* Provide your delivery details (Page 2, Delivery Details)
* Enter your payment information (Page 3, Payment Information)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (Page 1, User Information)** (`name: User Information`, `type: text`, required: false): Please fill in your name and any other relevant contact information so we can reach out to confirm your order.
* **Delivery Details (Page 2, Delivery Details)** (`name: delivery_details`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your delivery address, including your name, address, and any special instructions for delivery.
* **Payment Information (Page 3, Payment Information)** (`name: payment_info`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your payment method and details to complete the order.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately to avoid delays in processing your order.
* If you have any issues or questions, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* We will respond to your order as soon as possible after receiving your completed form.
