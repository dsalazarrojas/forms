# University Research Chemicals Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request chemicals for research purposes within the university. It is intended for use by faculty, staff, and Principal Investigators (PIs) to provide essential information about the chemicals they need.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Ensure that you have the necessary purchase authorization before submitting your request.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the lab contact person or your lab phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Form**: Enter a brief description of the chemical you need.
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: No
* **Chemical Name**: Enter the name of the chemical you are requesting.
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: No
* **Chemical ID**: Provide the unique identifier for the chemical you are requesting, if available.
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: No
* **Category**: Choose the category of the chemical (e.g. Solids, Liquids, Gases).
	+ Type: Select menu
	+ Required: No
* **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the chemical you need.
	+ Type: Number field
	+ Required: No
* **Unit Price**: Enter the price per unit of the chemical.
	+ Type: Number field
	+ Required: No
* **Purchase Authorization**: Specify who has authorized the purchase (Faculty, Staff, PI).
	+ Type: Select menu
	+ Required: No
* **Lab Contact**: Enter the contact information for the laboratory or contact person.
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: No
* **Lab Phone**: Enter the phone number of the laboratory or contact person.
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: No
* **Purchase Request Date**: Enter the date of the purchase request.
	+ Type: Date field
	+ Required: No
* **Submission Form**: A secondary form for additional comments or details.
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: No

## Tips

* Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to facilitate a smooth ordering process.
* If you have any doubts or uncertainties about the chemicals you need, please reach out to the lab contact or your research department.
