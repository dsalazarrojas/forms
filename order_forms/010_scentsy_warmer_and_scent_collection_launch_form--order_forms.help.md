# Scentsy Warmer And Scent Collection Launch Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the launch of new Scentsy warmers and scent collections. It is intended for internal use by authorized personnel to track and record important information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the title of the new product in the **Scentsy Warmers And Scent Collections** field. This is the main product name that will be used for tracking and documentation.
2. In the **Launch Form** field, enter the form used for this launch (e.g. "New Product Launch").
3. Select **Yes** or **No** in the **Collection** field to indicate whether this launch is for a new scent collection or not.
4. Enter the date of the launch in the **Launch Date** field. This should be a date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
5. Select the time of the launch in the **Launch Time** field. This should be a time in the format HH:MM (e.g. 14:30).
6. Add any additional notes or comments in the **Notes** field. This is optional but recommended for any important details or comments.
7. If applicable, enter the name of the consultant who is handling the launch in the **Consultant Name** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Scentsy Warmers And Scent Collections**: This field is used to enter the title of the new product that is being launched.
* **Launch Form**: This field is used to enter the type of form used for this launch (e.g. "New Product Launch").
* **Collection**: This field is used to select whether this launch is for a new scent collection or not. Selecting **Yes** will indicate that a new scent collection is being launched.
* **Scentsy Warmers And Scent Collections Launched**: This field is used to enter a numerical value indicating how many Scentsy warmers and scent collections were launched.
* **Launch Date**: This field is used to enter the date of the launch in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Launch Time**: This field is used to select the time of the launch in the format HH:MM.
* **Notes**: This field is used to add any additional notes or comments about the launch. This is optional but recommended for any important details or comments.
* **Consultant Name**: This field is used to enter the name of the consultant who is handling the launch, if applicable.
