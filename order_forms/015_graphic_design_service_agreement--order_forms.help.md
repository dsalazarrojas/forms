# Graphic Design Service Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a service agreement that clients use to input information about their graphic design project. The form is used to collect details about the client's project, payment terms, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill in your name and contact information in the first section.
*   Describe the service you are inquiring about and provide any necessary details.
*   Choose a payment option that suits your needs.
*   Select the service type you are interested in and provide a project deadline.
*   Enter a detailed description of your project.
*   Finally, confirm your project deadline and provide any further details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, optional): Please fill in your name and any other names you wish to be associated with this project.
* **What service are you inquiring about?** (`service_description`, `text`, optional): Describe the service you are interested in, such as logo design or web design.
* **Project Deadline** (`project_deadline`, `date`, optional): Enter a deadline for when you would like the project to be completed by. Please use the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Email** (`client_contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
*   **Phone** (`client_contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter a phone number where you can be reached.
*   **Service Type** (`service_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of service you are interested in from the provided options: Logo Design, Branding, Web Design, Print Design, or Other.
*   **Project Description** (`project_description`, `text`, optional): Enter a detailed description of your project, including any requirements or specifics.
*   **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose payment terms that apply to your project. This will be used to discuss payment with the designer.
*   **Service Deadline** (`service_deadline`, `date`, optional): Confirm the deadline for when the project is to be completed.
*   **Client Address** (`client_address`, `text`, optional): Enter a valid address where you can be reached.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that the information you enter is accurate and complete.
*   If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
*   This form is a service agreement, and any information entered here will be used to provide the best possible service for your project.
