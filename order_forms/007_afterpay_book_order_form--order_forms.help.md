# Afterpay Book Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Afterpay Book Order Form is used by customers to order books in installments. This form is designed to collect relevant information such as customer's name, email address, phone number, shipping details, book titles, quantity, book format, payment method, and estimated total amount for the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field, which is required.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field, which is required for order confirmation and digital receipts.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field, which is required.
4. Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field, including street, city, state, and ZIP, which is required.
5. Select the "Book Titles" you wish to purchase by listing them in the text field.
6. Enter the total number of books you wish to purchase in the "Quantity" field, which is required.
7. Choose the format of the book(s) you selected, such as Hardcover, Paperback, E-book, or Audiobook, from the "Format" dropdown menu.
8. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu, which is required.
9. Enter the estimated total amount for the order, including shipping, in the "Estimated Total Amount" field, which is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address for order confirmation and digital receipts.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required): Enter your shipping address including street, city, state, and ZIP.
* **Book Titles** (`book_titles`, text, required): List the titles of the books you wish to purchase.
* **Format** (`book_format`, select_one, required): Choose the format of the book(s) you selected, such as Hardcover, Paperback, E-book, or Audiobook.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the total number of books you wish to purchase.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method, which is required.
* **Estimated Total Amount** (`total_amount`, number, required): Enter the estimated total amount for the order, including shipping.
