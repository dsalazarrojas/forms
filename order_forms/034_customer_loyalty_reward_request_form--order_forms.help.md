# Customer Loyalty Reward Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to capture the required and optional information from customers to process their reward request.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in the required fields: customer_first_name, customer_last_name, customer_email, customer_phone, and loyalty_program.
2.  Choose your preferred contact method: Email, Phone, or Text.
3.  Review the form carefully and click "Submit" to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Customer First Name** (`customer_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
*   **Customer Last Name** (`customer_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
*   **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
*   **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
*   **Loyalty Program** (`loyalty_program`, select_one, required): Choose the loyalty program you are a member of.
*   **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_multiple, required): Choose your preferred contact method: Email, Phone, or Text.
*   **More options will be added as needed**

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
*   Double-check your email address for accuracy.
*   If you are unsure about your loyalty program status, contact our customer support for verification.
