# Hospice Medical Equipment Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for ordering medical equipment for hospice patients, ensuring the collection of necessary information for the order, delivery, and status of the equipment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring that all required fields are filled in accurately and consistently.
2. Select the correct equipment type from the provided options.
3. Provide accurate contact information for delivery and follow-up purposes.
4. Review and update the order status as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Form 1** (`order_form_1`, text, optional): Enter any additional information or comments about the order.
* **Patient Name** (`patientName`, text, required): Enter the patient's name for whom the equipment is intended.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipmentType`, select_one, optional): Select the type of medical equipment needed from the provided options (Oxygen, CPAP, BiPAP, Vent, Nebulizer, Other).
* **Equipment Count** (`equipmentCount`, number, required): Enter the quantity of equipment ordered.
* **Delivery Date** (`deliveryDate`, date, required): Enter the date for delivery of the equipment.
* **Delivery Time** (`deliveryTime`, time, required): Enter the time for delivery of the equipment.
* **Contact Name** (`contactName`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person for the order.
* **Contact Phone** (`contactPhone`, text, required): Enter the phone number for the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contactEmail`, email, required): Enter the email address for the contact person.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the order.
* **Special Requests** (`specialRequests`, select_multiple, optional): Select any special requests for the order (Other, Medical Supplies, Delivery, Setup).
* **Special Requests 2** (`specialRequests_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional special requests for the order (Other, Setup, Delivery).
* **Medical Supplies** (`medicalSupplies`, select_multiple, optional): Select any medical supplies needed from the provided options (Other, Oxygen Tanks, Oxygen Regulators, Oxygen Sensors, CPAP Machines, BiPAP Machines, Nebulizers, Vents).
* **Delivery Instructions** (`deliveryInstructions`, text, optional): Enter any special instructions for delivery.
* **Medical Supplies 2** (`medicalSupplies_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional medical supplies needed from the provided options (Other, Oxygen Tanks, Oxygen Regulators, Oxygen Sensors, CPAP Machines, BiPAP Machines, Nebulizers, Vents).
* **Medical Supplies 3** (`medicalSupplies_3`, select_multiple, optional): Select if any additional medical supplies are needed.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Enter your signature for the order.
* **Order Status** (`orderStatus`, select_one, optional): Update the order status as needed (In Process, Complete, Cancelled).
* **Delivery Status** (`deliveryStatus`, select_one, optional): Update the delivery status as needed (In Progress, Completed, Cancelled).
* **Equipment Status** (`equipmentStatus`, select_one, optional): Update the equipment status as needed (Active, Inactive, Lost).
* **Notes 2** (`notes_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the order.
* **Medical Staff** (`medicalStaff`, select_multiple, optional): Select the medical staff involved in the order (RN, LPN, CNA, Other).
* **Delivery Date 2** (`deliveryDate_2`, date, optional): Enter the date for delivery of the equipment.
* **Medical Supplies 4** (`medicalSupplies_4`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional medical supplies needed from the provided options (Yes, No).
* **Delivery Time 2** (`deliveryTime_2`, time, optional): Enter the time for delivery of the equipment.

## Tips

* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Review and update the form regularly for new developments or changes.
* Contact support if there's anything unclear or missing.
* This form is intended for ordering medical equipment for hospice patients, so ensure that the selected equipment type is correct.
* Be cautious when handling the order status, delivery status, and equipment status as they are critical for tracking and follow-up.
