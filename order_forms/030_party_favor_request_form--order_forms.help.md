**Party Favor Request Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Party Favor Request Form is a simple form designed to collect information from employees regarding their party requests. This form is meant for internal use only within the organization and is used to gather details about party dates, times, and other relevant information for planning and organization purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your **First Name** in the first text box.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the second text box.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the third text box.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the fourth text box.
5. Choose the **Order Date** for the party in the fifth date field.
6. Select the **Order Time** for the party in the sixth time field.
7. Enter the desired **Party Date** for the event in the seventh date field.
8. Choose the desired **Party Time** for the party in the eighth time field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, Required): Enter your first name for reference purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, Required): Enter your last name for reference purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Required): Enter your official email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, Required): Enter your primary phone number for contact purposes.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, Required): Choose the date you would like to order your party favor.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, Required): Choose the time you would like to order your party favor.
* **Party Date** (`party_date`, date, Required): Choose the date of the party.
* **Party Time** (`party_time`, time, Required): Choose the time of the party.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any issues during the party.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
