# Lost Package Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Lost Package Complaint Form is used to report lost packages to our team. This form helps us to gather necessary information to investigate and resolve the issue efficiently. Please complete the form to report a lost package, and we will get back to you as soon as possible.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling the form.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
3. Enter your details and the package's details accurately.
4. Review the form before submission to ensure all fields are filled correctly.
5. Click on the "Submit" button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Lost Package (1)**: Enter a brief description of the lost package, including its condition and any relevant details.
* **Order Number (2)**: Enter the order number of the lost package. This helps us identify the package in our system.
* **Customer Name (3)**: Enter the customer's name who received the package or is associated with the order.
* **Contact Number (4)**: Enter the customer's contact number, so we can reach them for further information.
* **Email (5)**: Enter the customer's email address, so we can communicate with them about the lost package.
* **Address (6)**: Enter the address where the package was delivered or should have been delivered.
* **Description (7)**: Enter a detailed description of the lost package, including its contents and any specific issues with it.
* **Package Details (8)**: Enter any additional details about the package, such as its size, color, or packaging material.
* **Report Date (9)**: Enter the date you are reporting the lost package, using the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Report Time (10)**: Enter the time you are reporting the lost package, using the format HH:MM AM/PM.

## Tips

* Please fill in the form accurately and completely to help us investigate the issue efficiently.
* If you have any supporting documents or photos of the lost package, please attach them to the form or send them separately to our team.
* We will get back to you as soon as possible with further instructions or updates on the status of your lost package.
