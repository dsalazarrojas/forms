# Bar Beverage Pump Replacement Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Bar Beverage Pump Replacement Order Form is designed to assist bar and restaurant establishments in placing a pump replacement order. This form will guide you through the necessary steps to ensure that all required information is collected for a smooth and efficient replacement process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the date of the order and ensure it is accurate.
2.  Enter your establishment's name and address to identify your location.
3.  Provide the manager's name and contact email and phone number.
4.  Choose the type of pump you are requesting to replace.
5.  Select the pump size and capacity needed.
6.  If applicable, enter your preferred pump brand or model.
7.  Specify the location where the pump is installed and the quantity needed.
8.  Choose the reason for the replacement and describe the current condition of the pump.
9.  Select the urgency level of the order.
10.  Provide the date you need the pump by.
11.  Indicate if you require installation service and if you have a service contract.
12.  If applicable, enter your service contract number and approved budget amount.
13.  If applicable, provide your purchase order number.
14.  Enter any special instructions or requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Date** (Required): The date of this replacement order.
* **Establishment Name** (Required): Name of your bar or restaurant.
* **Location or Address** (Required): Address of your establishment.
* **Manager Name** (Required): Name of manager placing the order.
* **Email Address** (Required): Contact email for communication.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Contact phone number for communication.
* **Pump Type Needed** (Required): Select the type of pump you are replacing.
* **Pump Size** (Required): Select the size or capacity needed.
* **Brand Preference** (Optional): If applicable, enter your preferred pump brand or model.
* **Current Pump Model** (Optional): If replacing a pump, enter the current model.
* **Pump Location** (Required): Where the pump is installed.
* **Quantity Needed** (Required): Number of pumps to order.
* **Reason for Replacement** (Required): Choose the reason for the replacement.
* **Current Pump Condition** (Optional): Describe the condition of the pump or issue.
* **Order Urgency Level** (Required): Select the urgency level of the order.
* **Date Needed By** (Required): Specify when you need the pump(s) by.
* **Installation Required** (Required): Indicate if you require professional installation.
* **Service or Warranty Contract** (Optional): If you have a service contract, check applicable.
* **Service Contract Number** (Optional): If applicable, enter your service contract number.
* **Approved Budget Amount** (Optional): If applicable, enter your approved budget amount.
* **Purchase Order Number** (Optional): If applicable, enter your purchase order number.
* **Special Instructions** (Optional): Enter any specific requirements or notes.

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Review all information carefully to avoid errors.
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to reach out for clarification.
