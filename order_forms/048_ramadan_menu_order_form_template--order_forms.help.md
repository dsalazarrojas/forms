# ramadan_menu_order_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers for orders during the month of Ramadan. This information includes customer details, order specifics, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and any other relevant customer information on page 1 (Customer Information).
2. On page 2 (Order Details), provide details about your order, including any specific instructions or requests.
3. On page 3 (Payment Information), enter your payment method and any relevant payment details.
4. If you have any additional notes or comments for the customer, you can add them on page 4 (Customer Notes).
5. On the last page (Email), enter your email address and any other relevant contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name and any other relevant customer information.
* **Order Details** (`text`, required/optional): Provide details about your order, including any specific instructions or requests.
* **Payment Information** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your payment method and any relevant payment details.
* **Customer Notes** (`note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments for the customer.
* **Email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address and any other relevant contact information.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your input for accuracy and completeness.
