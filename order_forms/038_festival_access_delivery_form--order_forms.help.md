# festival_access_delivery_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The festival_access_delivery_form is used to collect information for festival access delivery, including attendee details, ticketing, payment, and delivery preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., passport, driver's license).
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
3. **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
4. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
5. **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
6. **Festival Ticket Type**: Select the type of festival ticket you are purchasing (e.g., standard, premium, VIP).
7. **Festival Ticket Quantity**: Enter the number of festival tickets you are purchasing.
8. **Access Type**: Select the type of access you require (e.g., standard, premium, VIP).
9. **Payment Method**: Choose how you want to pay for your festival tickets (e.g., cash, card).
10. **Notes**: Enter any additional information or comments you'd like to share with us.
11. **Confirm Access**: Confirm that you are accessing the festival (e.g., yes, no).
12. **Delivery Method**: Choose how you want to receive your festival tickets (e.g., email, in-person).
13. **Delivery Address**: Enter your delivery address where you'd like to receive your festival tickets.
14. **Delivery City**: Enter the city where you'd like to receive your festival tickets.
15. **Delivery State**: Enter the state where you'd like to receive your festival tickets.
16. **Delivery Zip**: Enter your zip code for delivery purposes.
17. **Delivery Country**: Enter your country of residence.
18. **Delivery Instructions**: Enter any special delivery instructions or requests.
19. **Festival Date**: Enter the date of the festival you are attending.
20. **Festival Time**: Enter the time of the festival you are attending.
21. **Festival Venue**: Enter the venue of the festival you are attending.
22. **Festival Description**: Enter a brief description of the festival you are attending.
23. **Payment Details**: Enter any payment details or references.
24. **Confirm Festival**: Confirm the festival you are attending.
25. **Confirm Access**: Confirm your access to the festival.
