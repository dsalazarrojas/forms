# Hostess Order Form 2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture contact information and order details from a hostess who is interested in placing an order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the fields on the form, paying attention to the labels and instructions for each field.
2. Be sure to provide accurate and complete information in each field.

- **Hostess Order Form 2** (`form_1`, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the form, which is already provided as "Hostess Order Form 2".
- **Contact Information** (`form_2`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact details to ensure that we can get in touch with you effectively.
- **Phone** (`form_3`, text, required/optional): Provide your phone number so that we can contact you regarding your order.
- **lots of offers** (`form_4`, text, required/optional): Enter a description of the offers you are interested in.

- **reads** (`form_8`, text, required/optional): Enter any details about what you read.
- **to offer** (`form_9`, text, required/optional): Describe what you want to offer.
- **organize** (`form_10`, text, required/optional): Enter any information about organizing your offers.
- **the offers** (`form_11`, text, required/optional): Provide a list of offers you are interested in.
- **Form 12** (`form_12`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information about Form 12.
- **template** (`form_13`, text, required/optional): Provide details about a template related to your offers.
- **useful** (`form_14`, text, required/optional): Enter any information about what is useful to you.
- **for** (`form_15`, text, required/optional): Describe for what purpose.
