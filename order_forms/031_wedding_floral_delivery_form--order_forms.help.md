# Wedding Floral Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients about their wedding order requirements. It is used by our team to understand the specifics of the order, including couple details, delivery details, payment method, and other essential information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter the couple's details in the "Couple Details" field.
2. Provide information about the delivery of the order in the "Delivery Details" field.
3. If applicable, enter the payment details in the "Payment Details" field.
4. Fill in the "Order Details" field with any specific requirements or instructions for the order.
5. In the "Order Comments" field, include any additional comments or messages that the client would like to include with the order.
6. For the "Payment Method" field, select the desired payment method (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Cash).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wedding Floral Delivery** (text): Enter a brief description of the order, for example, "Wedding Bouquet for the Bride and Groom."
* **Couple Details** (text): Enter the details of the couple, such as names, ages, and contact information.
* **Delivery Details** (text): Enter the delivery address, date, and time of the order.
* **Payment Details** (text): Enter the payment method and amount for the order.
* **Order Details** (text): Include any specific requirements or instructions for the order, such as "Please include a red ribbon on the bouquet."
* **Order Comments** (text): Include any additional comments or messages that the client would like to include with the order.
* **Payment Method** (select one): Select one of the following payment methods: Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Cash.
* **Confirm Order** (text): Confirm the order by re-entering the order details for accuracy and completeness.
* 
Note: This form is designed to collect essential information from clients about their wedding order requirements. Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely to guarantee proper order fulfillment.
