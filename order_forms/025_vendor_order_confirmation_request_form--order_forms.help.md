# Vendor Order Confirmation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Vendor Order Confirmation Request Form is used to request confirmation of vendor orders. This form ensures that orders are accurately processed and communicated with vendors, reducing errors and misunderstandings.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the order details in the "Order Details" field. This includes any relevant information about the order, such as order number, date, or customer name.
2. Provide delivery details in the "Delivery Details" field. This includes information about the delivery date, time, or any special instructions.
3. Enter payment details in the "Payment Details" field. This includes any payment information, such as amount, method, or due date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter the order details, such as order number, date, or customer name, to provide context for the order confirmation.
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter delivery details, such as delivery date, time, or any special instructions, to ensure accurate delivery coordination.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter payment information, such as amount, method, or due date, to confirm payment arrangements.

## Tips

* Double-check the accuracy of the order, delivery, and payment details before submitting the form.
* Make sure to clearly describe any special instructions or requirements for delivery.
* Ensure payment information is complete and up-to-date to avoid delays or issues with payment processing.
