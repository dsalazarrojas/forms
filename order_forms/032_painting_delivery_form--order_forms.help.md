# Painting Delivery Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about painting deliveries, including the recipient's name, address, and shipping details. It will be used to ensure timely and accurate delivery of artworks and collectibles.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Provide Recipient Information
Complete the "Recipient Name" field with the name of the person or organization receiving the painting.
*Note: This field is optional, but required for tracking and addressing purposes.*

### Step 2: Enter Recipient Address
Provide the complete address of the recipient, including their street address, city, state, and zip code.
*Note: This field is optional, but recommended for efficient delivery.*

### Step 3: Specify Shipping Details
In the "Shipping Details" field, provide any specific instructions or requests for shipping, such as "Signature required upon delivery" or "Leave at door".


### Step 4: Choose Delivery Date (Optional)
If you are certain of the delivery date, you can select it from the calendar. If not, you can leave this field blank and we will contact you to confirm.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Recipient Name** (`recipient_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or organization receiving the painting.
* **Recipient Address** (`recipient_address`, text, optional): Enter the complete address of the recipient, including street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, optional): Provide any specific instructions or requests for shipping, such as "Signature required upon delivery" or "Leave at door".
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): Select a date for delivery or leave blank for us to contact you to confirm.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any special requests or instructions for delivery, please include them in the "Shipping Details" field.
* If you are unsure of the delivery date, please leave it blank and we will contact you to confirm.
