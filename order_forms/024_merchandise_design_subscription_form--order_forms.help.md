# merchandise_design_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The merchandise_design_subscription_form is designed to collect customer information for processing and managing subscriptions for merchandise orders. This form is intended to be completed by customers to provide their contact details and subscription preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the 'form_info' field and enter any relevant information about your order, if necessary.
2. In the 'name' field, enter your name as it appears on the order.
3. Enter your email address in the 'email' field. This is where your subscription information and order updates will be sent.
4. In the 'phone' field, enter your phone number for any additional contact purposes.
5. Select your preferred subscription type in the 'subscription_type' field from the available options.
6. In the 'subscription_date' field, select the date you would like your subscription to begin.
7. In the 'subscription_time' field, select the time you would like your subscription to begin.
8. In the 'payment_method' field, select the payment method that best suits your needs from the available options.
9. In the 'notes' field, enter any additional comments or notes about your order or subscription.
10. Review your information in the 'submit' field to ensure everything is correct before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_info** (text, optional): This field is where you can enter any additional information about your order, if necessary.
* **name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on the order.
* **email** (email, required): Enter your email address where your subscription information and order updates will be sent.
* **phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for any additional contact purposes.
* **subscription_type** (select_one, optional): Select your preferred subscription type from the available options.
* **subscription_date** (date, optional): Select the date you would like your subscription to begin.
* **subscription_time** (time, optional): Select the time you would like your subscription to begin.
* **payment_method** (select_multiple, optional): Select the payment method that best suits your needs from the available options.
* **notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your order or subscription.
* **submit** (text, optional): Review your information in this field to ensure everything is correct before submitting.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date contact information and subscription preferences.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
- Please review your information carefully before submitting to avoid any errors or misunderstandings.
