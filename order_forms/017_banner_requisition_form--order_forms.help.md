# Banner Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Banner Requisition Form is a tool used to request banners for various events, promotions, or general advertising purposes. This form collects essential information to ensure that your banner requirements are accurately met.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all necessary information.
2. Ensure that the "Order Date" is the date you would like the banners to be available for use.
3. If you have an existing order number, please provide it to help us track your request.
4. Enter your contact details, including name, email, and phone number, so we can get in touch with you.
5. Select the department responsible for the order.
6. Choose the type of banner you need (Vinyl, Fabric, Mesh, Outdoor, or Digital).
7. Specify the width and height of the banner in feet.
8. Indicate the number of banners required.
9. Provide specific color requirements or specifications.
10. Enter the main content or message for the banner.
11. Add any additional text or taglines if necessary.
12. Choose whether you need artwork or design assistance.
13. Select where your logo should be placed on the banner.
14. Specify where the banners should be delivered.
15. Select the required delivery date.
16. Enter the authorized budget in USD.
17. Check the current approval status.
18. Add any special instructions or custom requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Date** (1): Enter the date you would like the banners to be available for use.
* **Order Number** (2): If you have an existing order number, please provide it.
* **Requestor Name** (3): Enter the name of the person placing the order.
* **Email Address** (4): Provide your contact email.
* **Phone Number** (5): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Department** (6): Select the department responsible for the order.
* **Cost Center** (7): Enter the billing cost center code.
* **Approval Manager Name** (8): Enter the name of the manager for approval.
* **Banner Type** (9): Choose the type of banner you need (Vinyl, Fabric, Mesh, Outdoor, or Digital).
* **Banner Width** (10): Enter the width of the banner in feet.
* **Banner Height** (11): Enter the height of the banner in feet.
* **Quantity** (12): Indicate the number of banners required.
* **Color Specification** (13): Provide specific color requirements or specifications.
* **Primary Text or Message** (14): Enter the main content or message for the banner.
* **Secondary Text** (15): Add any additional text or taglines if necessary.
* **Artwork or Design Included** (16): Choose whether you need artwork or design assistance.
* **Logo Placement** (17): Select where your logo should be placed on the banner.
* **Delivery Address** (18): Specify where the banners should be delivered.
* **Delivery City** (19): Enter the delivery city.
* **Delivery ZIP Code** (20): Enter the delivery ZIP Code.
* **Required Delivery Date** (21): Select the required delivery date.
* **Budget Approval Amount** (22): Enter the authorized budget in USD.
* **Approval Status** (23): Check the current approval status.
* **Special Instructions** (24): Add any special instructions or custom requirements.
