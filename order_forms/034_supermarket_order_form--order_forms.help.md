# Supermarket Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supermarket Order Form is used by customers and supermarkets to collect and manage order information. It's a straightforward form that helps customers place orders with their preferred supermarket and provides a simple way for supermarkets to track and fulfill orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Name**, **Customer Email**, and **Customer Phone** to help us identify you.
2. Choose your preferred **Delivery Date** for your order.
3. Select the items you'd like to order from the **Order Items** dropdown menu. You can choose multiple items if needed.
4. Indicate your preferred **Payment Method**.
5. Add any additional notes you'd like to include in the **Customer Note** field.
6. Enter the **Store Name**, **Store Address**, **Store Email**, and **Store Phone** to help us contact the store if needed.
7. Enter your preferred **Delivery Status** for your order.
8. Choose the **Delivery Date Range** for your order if applicable.
9. Provide a brief description of the items you're ordering in the **Order Items Description** field.
10. If you have a preferred **Customer Signature**, please enter it in the corresponding field.
11. If you have a preferred **Store Signature**, please enter it in the corresponding field.
12. Enter the **Store Manager Name** for the store where you're ordering from.
13. Add any additional **Order Notes** you'd like to include.
14. Enter the **Order Total** if applicable.
15. Enter your **Customer Order ID** if you already have one.
16. Choose the **Order Status** for your order.
17. Enter the **Store Order ID** if you already have one.
18. Enter the **Store Order Number** if you already have one.
19. Enter the **Customer Order Number** if you already have one.
20. Enter the **Order Item Quantity** if you're ordering multiple items.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (text, required): Enter your name as you would like to be referred to.
* **Customer Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Customer Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Delivery Date** (date, optional): Choose a date you'd like your order to be delivered on.
* **Order Items** (select multiple, optional): Select the items you'd like to order. Please choose multiple items if needed.
* **Payment Method** (select one, optional): Indicate your preferred payment method.
* **Customer Note** (note, optional): Add any additional notes about your order.
* **Store Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the store where you're ordering from.
* **Store Address** (text, optional): Enter the address of the store where you're ordering from.
* **Store Email** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the store where you're ordering from.
* **Store Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the store where you're ordering from.
* **Delivery Status** (select one, optional): Choose the status of your order.
* **Delivery Date Range** (select multiple, optional): Choose the range of dates for your order if applicable.
* **Order Items Description** (text, optional): Describe the items you're ordering.
* **Customer Signature** (number, optional): If you'd like to add a customer signature, enter it here.
* **Store Signature** (number, optional): If you'd like to add a store signature, enter it here.
* **Store Manager Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the store manager.
* **Order Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional notes about your order.
* **Order Total** (number, optional): Enter the total cost of your order if applicable.
* **Customer Order ID** (text, optional): Enter your customer order ID if you already have one.
* **Order Status** (select one, optional): Choose the status of your order.
* **Store Order ID** (text, optional): Enter the store order ID if you already have one.
* **Store Order Number** (number, optional): Enter the store order number if you already have one.
* **Customer Order Number** (text, optional): Enter the customer order number if you already have one.
* **Order Item Quantity** (number, optional): Enter the quantity of each item if you're ordering multiple items. 

Please note that some fields may not be visible depending on the specific configuration of the form. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
