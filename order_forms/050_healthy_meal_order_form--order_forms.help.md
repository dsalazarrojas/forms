# Healthy Meal Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Healthy Meal Order Form is designed to gather user preferences for healthy meal options. This information will help provide personalized recommendations for healthy meals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections for each meal period: Breakfast, Snack, Lunch, and Dinner.
2. Select your preferences for each meal period by choosing "Yes" or "No" for the corresponding options.
3. Scroll down to review your selections and click "Submit" to finalize your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Breakfast Options**: Select "Yes" if you would like healthy breakfast options, such as oatmeal or fruit salad.
* **Snack Options**: Choose "Yes" to select healthy snack options, such as nuts or carrot sticks, and you can select multiple options by clicking the corresponding checkboxes.
* **Lunch Options**: Select "Yes" for healthy lunch options, like grilled chicken or vegetable salad.
* **Dinner Options**: Choose "Yes" to select healthy dinner options, such as salmon or roasted vegetables, and select multiple options by clicking the corresponding checkboxes.

## Tips
* Be sure to select your preferences carefully, as this will help provide accurate recommendations for your healthy meal order.
* If you have any specific dietary restrictions or preferences, please contact our customer support for assistance.
