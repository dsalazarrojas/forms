# vip_ticket_payment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Vip Ticket Payment Form** is designed for event organizers to collect payment information for VIP tickets.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the **Payment Method** using the options available (Stripe, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
2. Enter the **Ticket Price** and **Payment Amount**, which should match to avoid errors.
3. Enter any additional **Note** if necessary.
4. Confirm the **Submitted** status of the payment (True or False).
5. Select the **Payment Type** (One-off, Subscription, or Donation).
6. Enter the **Event Name** and **Event Start Date** and **Event End Date** to associate with the payment.
7. Choose the **Payment Status** (Paid, Pending, or Refunded).
8. Enter any additional **User Note** if necessary.
9. Enter the **Payment Date** and **Transaction ID** for tracking purposes.
10. Select the **Payment User** (Stripe, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
11. Enter the **Event Status** (Active, Cancelled, or Closed).
12. Enter any additional **Payment User Note** if necessary.
13. Enter the **Order ID** for tracking.
14. Enter the **Event Start Time** and **Event End Time** for scheduling.
15. Enter the **Event Location** for reference.
16. Enter the **Ticket Type** for categorization.
17. Choose the **Payment Confirmation** status (True or False).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Organizer**: Enter the email address of the event organizer.
- **Payment Method**: Select the payment method used for the ticket sale (Stripe, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
- **Ticket Price**: Enter the price of the ticket.
- **Payment Amount**: Enter the total amount paid for the ticket.
- **Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about the payment.
- **Submitted**: Confirm if the payment has been submitted (True or False).
- **Payment Type**: Choose the type of payment (One-off, Subscription, or Donation).
- **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event associated with the payment.
- **Event Start Date**: Enter the start date of the event.
- **Event End Date**: Enter the end date of the event.
- **Payment Status**: Choose the status of the payment (Paid, Pending, or Refunded).
- **User Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about the payment.
- **Payment Date**: Enter the date of the payment.
- **Transaction ID**: Enter the transaction ID for tracking purposes.
- **Payment User**: Select the payment user (Stripe, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
- **Event Status**: Choose the status of the event (Active, Cancelled, or Closed).
- **Payment User Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about the payment.
- **Order ID**: Enter the order ID for tracking purposes.
- **Event Start Time**: Enter the start time of the event.
- **Event End Time**: Enter the end time of the event.
- **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event.
- **Ticket Type**: Enter the type of ticket.
- **Payment Confirmation**: Choose the confirmation status of the payment (True or False).

Please review each field carefully to ensure accurate input, and make sure to enter the correct information for the event and payment details.
