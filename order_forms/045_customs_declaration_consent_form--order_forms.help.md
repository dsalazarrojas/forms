**Form Purpose**
The Customs Declaration Consent Form is a form used to gather information from travelers to declare their goods and obtain consent for customs clearance. This form is crucial for ensuring that travelers comply with customs regulations and rules.

**How To Complete This Form**

1.  Ensure you have the required information and documents before starting the form.
2.  Carefully read the instructions and questions in each section before answering.
3.  Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email Address accurately and completely.
4.  Choose the correct Goods Type for your declared items (Goods, Package, or Baggage).
5.  Enter the weight of the goods you are declaring.
6.  Indicate whether you declare the goods truthfully (True) or not (False).
7.  Sign the form to confirm your declaration.

**Field-by-Field Explanation**

*  **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your passport or identification document.
*  **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your passport or identification document.
*  **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your valid email address that we can contact you with if we need more information.
*  **Goods Type** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose the category that best describes the items you are declaring:
	+  Goods: for packaged or shipped items, such as electronics or clothing
	+  Package: for items sent through parcel services, like USPS or UPS
	+  Baggage: for personal items you are carrying on your person, like luggage or hand luggage
*  **Goods Weight** (Number, Required): Enter the total weight of the items you are declaring in kilogram.
*  **Declaration** (Select One, Required): Check True if you declare the goods truthfully. Check False if you don't declare all the goods truthfully.
*  **Signature** (Text, Required): Sign your name to confirm that the information provided is accurate and true.
