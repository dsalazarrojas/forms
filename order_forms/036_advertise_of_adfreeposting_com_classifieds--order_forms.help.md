# Advertise Of Adfreeposting.com Classifieds - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for advertisers to provide information and details for their advertisement on Of Adfreeposting.com Classifieds.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Business Name**, which should be the name of your business or organization.
2. Enter the **Contact Person Name**, which is the name of the person responsible for advertising.
3. Provide a **Valid Email Address** for communication purposes.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** for contact purposes.
5. Enter your **Business Address**, which should be the complete address of your business.
6. Select the type of **Ad Type** you want to advertise (e.g., Banner Ad, Text Ad, Image Ad, Video Ad, or Featured Listing).
7. If you're advertising a Banner Ad, select the desired **Banner Size** from the options provided.
8. Choose the **Ad Duration** you want your ad to run (e.g., 1 Week, 2 Weeks, 1 Month, 3 Months, 6 Months, or 1 Year).
9. Enter the **Target Audience** you're trying to reach.
10. Choose the **Campaign Start Date** when you want your ad campaign to start.
11. Write a brief **Ad Message** that will be displayed in your ad.
12. Specify a **Call to Action** that you want viewers to take.
13. If applicable, upload your **Logo or Image** for your ad.
14. Add any **Special Requirements** you have for your advertisement.
15. Select the desired **Pricing Package** from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (Required): Name of your business or organization.
* **Contact Person Name** (Required): Name of the person responsible for advertising.
* **Email Address** (Required): Valid email for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Contact phone number.
* **Business Address** (Required): Complete address of your business.
* **Ad Type** (Required): Type of advertisement (e.g., Banner Ad, Text Ad, Image Ad, Video Ad, or Featured Listing).
* **Banner Size** (Optional): If selecting banner ad, choose the desired size from the options provided.
* **Ad Duration** (Required): Duration of your ad campaign (e.g., 1 Week, 2 Weeks, 1 Month, 3 Months, 6 Months, or 1 Year).
* **Target Audience** (Required): Who is your target audience.
* **Campaign Start Date** (Required): Date when you want your ad campaign to start.
* **Ad Message** (Required): Brief message to be displayed in your ad.
* **Call to Action** (Required): Action you want viewers to take.
* **Logo or Image Upload** (Optional): Upload your logo or ad image (if applicable).
* **Special Requirements** (Optional): Any special requirements for your advertisement.
* **Pricing Package** (Required): Select the desired pricing package from the options provided.
* **Terms Agreement** (Required): Agree to the terms and conditions of advertising.
