# Afterpay Household Products Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate household product orders through Afterpay, allowing customers to select and purchase products in interest-free installments.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Enter your **Full Name** (required) in the "Full Name" field, which is a text field that requires your name to be filled out completely.
2.  Enter your **Contact Email** (required) in the "Contact Email" field, which is an email address that will be used for communication regarding your order.
3.  Provide your **Delivery Address** (required) in the "Delivery Address" field, which should include your street, suburb, and postcode.
4.  Select the **Product Name** (required) of the household item you would like to order from the options provided.
5.  Choose the **Pack Size** (required) of the product from the available options (Single Item, 3-Pack Bundle, 6-Pack Bundle, Bulk / Case).
6.  Enter the **Quantity** (required) of the product you would like to order.
7.  Enter the **Unit Price** (required) of the product.
8.  (Optional) Select additional items you would like to **Frequently Bought Together** from the available options (Dish Soap, Kitchen Sponges, Trash Bags, All-purpose Cleaner).
9.  Choose your **Payment Option** (required) from the available options (Afterpay, Debit Card, Credit Card), selecting Afterpay for interest-free installments.
10. Enter the **Total Order Amount** (required) in the field, which includes tax.
11. Provide **Delivery Instructions** (optional) on how you would like your order to be left at your doorstep, for example, "Leave behind the planter".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **-- Household Essentials Order --**: A heading that guides you through the order form.
*   **Full Name**: A text field requiring your name for successful order processing.
*   **Contact Email**: A required email field for order-related communication.
*   **Delivery Address**: Enter your delivery address including your street, suburb, and postcode for successful delivery.
*   **-- Item Details --**: A subheading that guides you to product selection.
*   **Product Name**: A field requiring you to select the specific household item you'd like to order.
*   **Pack Size**: Select the packaging size of the product from the given options.
*   **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the product you'd like to order.
*   **Unit Price**: Enter the price per item.
*   **Frequently bought together items**: Select additional items you'd like to add to your order from the available options.
*   **Payment Option**: Select your preferred payment method, offering interest-free Afterpay or traditional debit/credit card payment.
*   **Total Order Amount**: Include tax in this field.
*   **Leave at door instructions**: Enter any specific delivery instructions, if needed.

This form is designed to guide you through the process of placing a household order through Afterpay, ensuring a smooth and efficient shopping experience.
