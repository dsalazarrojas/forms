# Portable AC Unit Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from customers for the successful booking and delivery of portable air conditioning units.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Follow the instructions and explanations provided in each field for a clear understanding of what information is required.
* Please provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* Ensure that you have a basic understanding of the form's purpose and available options.
* Take a few minutes to review the form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Portable AC Unit**: 
	+ Please select "Yes" if you are interested in booking a portable air conditioning unit. 
	+ Please select "No" if you are not interested in booking a portable air conditioning unit.
* **Customer Name**: 
	+ Please enter your name as it appears on your identification. 
	+ This is a required field and must be filled out accurately.
* **Unit Size**: 
	+ Please select "Yes" if you are interested in a large unit, "No" for a small unit.
* **Start Date**: 
	+ Please enter the date you would like to start using the portable AC unit. 
	+ This can be a date in the past or a date in the future. 
	+ Please ensure that this date is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Additional Information** (not provided in the YAML, but assumed to be there as it usually is in such forms):
	+ Please provide any additional details or comments about your booking request.

## Tips
* Please take your time to ensure that you have completed the form accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it. 

This form is designed to guide customers through the booking process for portable AC units, ensuring accurate and up-to-date information is collected for successful booking and delivery.
