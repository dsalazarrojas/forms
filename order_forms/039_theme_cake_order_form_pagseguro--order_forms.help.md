# Theme Cake Order Form PagSeguro - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the theme cake order form. The form is designed to gather information about your order, including flavor, size, custom design, and delivery details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required and optional information.
2. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Form** (`order_form`, text, optional): This field is a placeholder where you can enter any information about your order.
* **Upload Style** (`style_upload`, text, optional): Here you can upload your desired cake style or design.
* **Flavor Name** (`flavor_name`, text, optional): Enter the flavor of cake you would like to order.
* **Cake Size** (`size_name`, number, optional): Select the size of cake you would like to order.
* **Custom Design** (`custom_design`, text, optional): Upload a picture of your custom cake design.
* **Custom Message** (`custom_message`, text, optional): Enter any custom message you would like to have on the cake.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): Select the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, optional): Select the time you would like to receive your order.
* **Order Number** (`order_number`, text, optional): Enter your order number.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your order is processed correctly.
* If you have any special requests or customizations, please include them in the Custom Message field.
* If you have any issues with your order, please contact us at your customer contact information.
