# qr_shelf_label_printing_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The qr_shelf_label_printing_order_form is a form used to collect information for QR shelf label printing orders. This form helps our team process and prepare labels efficiently. Please fill this form out with accurate and up-to-date information about the order to avoid any errors or delays in printing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Order ID" field to confirm or find the correct order you're working on.
2. Fill out the "Customer Information" section with accurate details about the customer, including their name, phone number, and email (if available).
3. Enter the "Product Information" section with details about the product, including the name, description, and quantity required.
4. Provide the "Delivery Information" section with the date and time for delivery, as well as any additional delivery notes.
5. Enter the "Payment Information" section with payment amounts and methods (credit or cash).
6. Review and fill out the "Order Status" and "Payment Status" fields with the current status.
7. Fill out the "Customer Notes" section with any additional information or comments from the customer.
8. Review the "Delivery Notes" section for any special delivery instructions or requirements.
9. Fill out the "Assigned Tool" field with the tool assigned for this order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Form** (required/optional: false)
  This field is not used in the explanation, as it is the form title.
* **Customer Information** (required/optional: false)
  + **Customer Email** (email): Enter the customer's email address, if available.
  + **Customer Phone** (text): Enter the customer's phone number.
  + **Customer Address** (text): Enter the customer's address.
* **Product Information** (required/optional: false)
  + **Product Name** (text): Enter the product name.
  + **Product Description** (text): Enter a brief description of the product.
  + **Product Quantity** (number): Enter the quantity of the product.
  + **Product Price** (number): Enter the price of the product.
* **Delivery Information** (required/optional: false)
  + **Delivery Date** (date): Enter the date for delivery.
  + **Delivery Time** (time): Enter the time for delivery.
  + **Delivery Notes** (text): Enter any additional delivery instructions or requirements.
* **Payment Information** (required/optional: false)
  + **Payment Amount** (number): Enter the amount of payment made.
  + **Payment Method** (select_one: credit/cash): Select the payment method, either credit or cash.
* **Additional Notes** (required/optional: false)
  + **Customer Notes** (text): Enter any additional comments or information from the customer.
  + **Delivery Notes** (text): Enter any special delivery instructions or requirements.
* **Order Status** (select_one: pending/completed): Select the current order status, either pending or completed.
* **Payment Status** (select_one: paid/unpaid): Select the current payment status, either paid or unpaid.
* **Product Status** (select_one: available/unavailable): Select the product status, either available or unavailable.
* **Order ID** (number): Enter the order ID for reference.
* **Payment ID** (number): Enter the payment ID for reference.
* **Assigned Tool** (text): Enter the tool assigned for this order.
