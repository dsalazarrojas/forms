# Material Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Material Order Form is a form designed to collect information from customers about material orders. This form is used to gather necessary details such as customer contact information, material type, quantity, and pricing information to facilitate efficient order processing and delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Complete the form with accurate and up-to-date information about yourself**:
	* Enter your name in the "Customer Name" field.
	* Enter your email address in the "Customer Email" field.
2. **Select the material type**:
	* Choose the type of material you are ordering from the "Material Type" dropdown menu.
3. **Enter material quantity and pricing information**:
	* Enter the quantity of material you are ordering in the "Material Quantity" field.
	* Enter the unit price of the material in the "Material Unit Price" field.
	* The "Material Total Price" field will automatically be populated with the calculated total price.
4. **Add any additional notes or comments**:
	* Enter any relevant notes or comments about your order in the "Customer Notes" field.
5. **Enter order dates and times**:
	* Enter the date you ordered the material in the "Date Ordered" field.
	* Enter the time you ordered the material in the "Time Ordered" field.
6. **Enter your contact information (optional)**:
	* Enter your phone number in the "Customer Phone" field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name (Customer Name)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer Email (Customer Email)**: Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Material Type (Material Type)**: Select the category of material you are ordering (e.g., concrete, lumber, paints, or tiles).
* **Material Quantity (Material Quantity)**: Enter the number of units of material you are ordering.
* **Material Unit Price (Material Unit Price)**: Enter the price per unit of material.
* **Material Total Price (Material Total Price)**: The calculated total price of the material order, automatically populated based on the quantity and unit price.
* **Customer Notes (Customer Notes)**: Enter any relevant notes or comments about your order.
* **Date Ordered (Date Ordered)**: Enter the date you placed your order.
* **Time Ordered (Time Ordered)**: Enter the time you placed your order.
* **Customer Phone (Customer Phone)**: Enter your phone number, if applicable.
