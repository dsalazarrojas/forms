# sports_equipment_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to check out sports equipment. Fill out the form to record the details of the equipment being checked out, including the date and time, equipment ID, and staff member's information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, indicated by *.
2. Enter the date and time you wish to check out the equipment.
3. Enter the ID number of the equipment being checked out.
4. Enter a brief description of the equipment being checked out.
5. Enter the quantity of the equipment being checked out.
6. Enter your name and contact information so we can contact you with questions or reminders.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_info** (`text`, optional): Enter a brief description of yourself, so we can verify your identity.
* **date_out** (`date`, optional): Enter the date you wish to check out the equipment.
* **time_out** (`time`, optional): Enter the time you wish to check out the equipment.
* **equipment_id** (`number`, optional): Enter the ID number of the equipment being checked out. This will help us track the equipment's location.
* **item_description** (`text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the equipment being checked out. This will help us identify what was checked out.
* **quantity** (`number`, optional): Enter the quantity of the equipment being checked out.
* **staff_name** (`text`, optional): Enter your name so we can verify your identity.
* **staff_phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **staff_email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with questions or reminders.
* **return_date** (`date`, optional): Enter the date you expect to return the equipment by. Make sure this is a date you can realistically keep in mind.
* **return_time** (`time`, optional): Enter the time you expect to return the equipment by.
 
## Tips
- Make sure to check in on the return date and time specified above to ensure the equipment is returned promptly.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask a staff member for help.
- Please return the equipment in the same condition it was in when you checked it out.
