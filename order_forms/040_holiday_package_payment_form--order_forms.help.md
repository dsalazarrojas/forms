# Holiday Package Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holiday Package Payment Form is designed to collect payment information from customers for holiday packages. This form is used to facilitate the payment process for customers who have selected a holiday package.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter the name of the holiday package for which you are making a payment.
2. Enter the details of the holiday package, including any relevant information such as dates and destinations.
3. Select the date and time for the payment.
4. Enter the amount of money to be paid for the holiday package.
5. Choose the payment method, which can be Cash, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
6. Enter any additional payment notes or instructions.
7. Enter the customer's email address.
8. Enter the customer's phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Name** (`form_name`, Text, Required: No): Enter the name of the holiday package for which you are making a payment.
* **Holiday Package Details** (`holiday_package_details`, Text, Required: No): Enter any relevant details of the holiday package, such as dates and destinations.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, Date, Required: No): Enter the date when the payment is being made.
* **Payment Time** (`payment_time`, Time, Required: No): Enter the time when the payment is being made.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, Text, Required: No): Enter the amount of money to be paid for the holiday package.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, Select One, Required: No): Choose the payment method from the following options: Cash, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
* **Payment Notes** (`payment_notes`, Text, Required: No): Enter any additional payment notes or instructions.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required: No): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Required: No): Enter the customer's phone number.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information in the form to ensure smooth processing of your payment.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact our support team for assistance.
