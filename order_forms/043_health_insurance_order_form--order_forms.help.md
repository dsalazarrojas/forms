# Health Insurance Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Insurance Order Form is a form designed to collect information necessary for insurance providers to process and manage insurance policies. This form gathers personal details, medical conditions, and policy information to ensure accurate and efficient policy management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Health Insurance Order Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* User Name
	* Email
	* Phone
	* Address
2. Provide medical information:
	* Date of Birth
	* Date of Visit
	* Medical Provider
	* Medical Condition (Yes/No)
3. Enter policy details:
	* Insurance Plan (Yes/No)
	* Coverage Start Date
	* Coverage End Date
	* Policy Number
	* Policy Effective Date
4. Complete policy holder information (if applicable):
	* Policy Holder
	* Policy Holder Phone
	* Policy Holder Email
	* Policy Holder Address
	* Policy Holder Date of Birth
	* Policy Holder Medical Condition (Yes/No)
	* Policy Holder Coverage Start Date
	* Policy Holder Coverage End Date

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official ID.
* **Email** (`email`, text, optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your current address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, optional): Enter the date of your last visit to the medical provider.
* **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of your medical provider.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, select multiple, optional): Check if you have a medical condition.
* **Insurance Plan** (`insurance_plan`, select one, optional): Check if you have an insurance plan.
* **Coverage Start Date** (`coverage_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your coverage.
* **Coverage End Date** (`coverage_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your coverage.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, text, optional): Enter your policy number.
* **Policy Effective Date** (`policy_effective_date`, date, optional): Enter the effective date of your policy.
* **Policy Status** (`policy_status`, select one, optional): Check the status of your policy (Active/Inactive).
* **Policy Type** (`policy_type`, select one, optional): Check if you have a policy type (Yes/No).
* **Policy Holder** (`policy_holder`, text, optional): Enter the policy holder's name.
* **Policy Holder Phone** (`policy_holder_phone`, text, optional): Enter the policy holder's phone number.
* **Policy Holder Email** (`policy_holder_email`, email, optional): Enter the policy holder's email address.
* **Policy Holder Address** (`policy_holder_address`, text, optional): Enter the policy holder's address.
* **Policy Holder Date of Birth** (`policy_holder_date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the policy holder's date of birth.
* **Policy Holder Medical Condition** (`policy_holder_medical_condition`, select multiple, optional): Check if the policy holder has a medical condition.
* **Policy Holder Coverage Start Date** (`policy_holder_coverage_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the policy holder's coverage start date.
* **Policy Holder Coverage End Date** (`policy_holder_coverage_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the policy holder's coverage end date.
* **Policy Holder Notes** (`policy_holder_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the policy holder.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Add your signature.

Note: The fields marked as `optional` can be left blank if not applicable.
