# Seasonal Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for customers to place orders for seasonal products.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether you want to place an order for a seasonal product.
2. Enter your name and email address so we can contact you about your order.
3. Provide your phone number so we can contact you with any questions or updates.
4. Enter the product name you're interested in purchasing.
5. Choose the quantity you'd like to buy.
6. Enter the product price (if different from the listed price).
7. Enter the total order amount (if different from the listed price).
8. Enter any applicable discount (if any).
9. Enter the tax amount (if any).
10. Enter the shipping cost (if any).
11. Choose the status of your order (e.g., Shipping, Processing, Delivered, Canceled).
12. Select whether you've paid for your order.
13. Add any additional notes or comments about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **order_form**: Select whether you're interested in placing an order for a seasonal product.
	+ This is a yes/no option to determine if you want to place an order.
* **customer_name**: Enter your name.
	+ This is your personal name, which we'll use to contact you about your order.
* **customer_email**: Enter your email address.
	+ This is the address we'll use to contact you with order updates and questions.
* **customer_phone**: Enter your phone number.
	+ This is the number we'll use to contact you with any questions or updates.
* **product_name**: Enter the name of the product you're interested in purchasing.
	+ This is the product name you'll be ordering.
* **quantity**: Choose the quantity you'd like to buy.
	+ This is the number of products you're interested in purchasing.
* **product_price**: Enter the price of the product (if different from the listed price).
	+ This is the price you'll pay for the product.
* **order_total**: Enter the total order amount (if different from the listed price).
	+ This is the total amount you'll pay for the product.
* **discount**: Enter any applicable discount (if any).
	+ This is any applicable discount you'd like to apply.
* **tax**: Enter the tax amount (if any).
	+ This is the tax amount that applies to your order.
* **shipping_cost**: Enter the shipping cost (if any).
	+ This is the cost of shipping your order.
* **order_status**: Choose the status of your order.
	+ This is the current status of your order (e.g., Shipping, Processing, Delivered, Canceled).
* **payment_method**: Select whether you've paid for your order.
	+ This is confirmation of payment for your order.
* **customer_note**: Add any additional notes or comments about your order.
	+ This is a field for any additional information you'd like to provide about your order.
