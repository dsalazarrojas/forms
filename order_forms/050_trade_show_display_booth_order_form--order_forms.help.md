# Trade Show Display Booth Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers interested in ordering a display booth for a trade show. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure your order is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Provide your Phone Number.
4. Enter your Date of Birth (this field is not required for trade show purposes, but it's included for internal record-keeping).
5. Select the date and time you would like to order.
6. Choose the type of order you would like to place (Standard or Custom).
7. Provide any additional details about your order.
8. Enter the total value of your order.
9. Select the method of payment (Online or Offline).
10. Choose the status of your order (New, In Progress, or Delivered).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address to contact you for order updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for follow-up contact.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): This field is not required for trade show purposes but is included for internal record-keeping. Please enter your Date of Birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you would like to order a display booth.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you would like to order a display booth.
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose between Standard or Custom Order.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional information about your order, such as specific requests or special requests.
* **Total Order Value** (`total_order_value`, `number`, required): Enter the total value of your order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose between Online or Offline payment methods.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the current status of your order (New, In Progress, or Delivered).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Be accurate when filling out your Date of Birth and Phone Number.
- If you have any questions or concerns about your order, contact our team for assistance.
