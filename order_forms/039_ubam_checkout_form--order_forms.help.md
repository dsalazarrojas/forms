# UBAM Checkout Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The UBAM Checkout Form is used to collect information from customers for an e-commerce platform. This form helps in processing and managing customer orders.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the customer's information: 
	* Customer Name: Enter the customer's name as it appears on their ID or as they prefer to be addressed. 
	* Email: Enter the customer's email address. 
	* Phone Number: Enter the customer's phone number for contact purposes. 
	* Customer Address: Enter the customer's physical address for shipping and contact purposes. 
	* Phone Number 2: Enter any additional phone number for the customer. 
2. Fill in the order details:
	* Order Total: Enter the total amount for the order. 
	* Order Date: Enter the date of the order. 
	* Order Time: Enter the time of the order. 
	* Order Date 2: Enter the second date of the order. 
	* Order Time 2: Enter the time of the second date. 
3. Select payment method and status:
	* Payment Method: Select the payment method used for the order from the provided options (Visa, Mastercard, American Express). 
	* Payment Status: Select the payment status from the provided options (Paid, Unpaid, Partially Paid). 
4. Fill in the order status and customer details:
	* Order Status: Select the status of the order from the provided options (Pending, Processing, Shipped, Cancelled). 
	* Customer ID: Enter the customer's ID for reference. 
	* Assigned To: Select the staff member handling the order from the provided options. 
	* Customer ID 2: Enter any additional customer ID. 
	* Assigned To 2: Select any additional staff member handling the order. 
5. Optional fields for additional customer information:
	* Customer Notes: Enter any additional notes about the customer. 
	* Customer Address 2: Enter any additional address for the customer. 
	* Customer Name 2: Enter any additional name for the customer. 
	* Customer Email 2: Enter any additional email for the customer.
