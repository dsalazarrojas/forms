# Bridal Package Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from brides to be able to provide personalized bridal services. You will need to fill out this form to book and customize your wedding services with us.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*) in the label.
2. Enter your name and contact information (email address and phone number) to ensure we can reach out to you.
3. Select your wedding date and the date you want the services to be performed.
4. Choose the bridal package that suits your needs and budget.
5. If you need additional services or have any special requests, such as hair services or additional add-ons, please let us know.
6. Confirm your payment method and schedule.
7. Review and agree to our cancellation policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Bride Name** (1): Please enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (2): Enter your email address so we can contact you about the booking and any updates.
* **Phone Number** (3): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for any questions or updates.
* **Wedding Date** (4): Choose the date of your wedding.
* **Bridal Package Selection** (5): Select the package that best suits your needs and budget.
* **Additional Attendants** (6): If you need services for more than the included attendants, let us know the number of extra people.
* **Hair Services Included** (7): Choose the hair services you need, such as just hair styling or a full hair package.
* **Trial Session** (8): Decide if you need a trial session before the wedding.
* **Preferred Trial Date** (9): If you need a trial session, choose a date for it.
* **Wedding Day Service Date** (10): Choose the date of your wedding service.
* **Service Start Time** (11): Select the time we should arrive on your wedding day.
* **Service Location** (12): Choose where the services will be performed (e.g., studio, your home, wedding venue, etc.).
* **Service Location Address** (13): Enter the full address for the service location.
* **Names of Attendants** (14): List the names of your bridesmaids or other attendants that will need services.
* **Makeup Style Preferences** (15): Describe your desired makeup style and look.
* **Inspiration or Reference Photos** (16): If you have photos of makeup looks you like, please bring them with you to the trial session.
* **Skin Type** (17): Let us know about your skin type (e.g., dry, oily, combination, sensitive, etc.).
* **Makeup Allergies or Sensitivities** (18): If you have any allergies or sensitivities to certain products, please let us know.
* **Touchup Service During Event** (19): Decide if you want us to stay for touch-ups during the event.
* **Total Package Cost** (20): Enter the total package cost after selecting your package.
* **Additional Add-Ons** (21): Select any additional services you would like to include.
* **Payment Method** (22): Choose how you will pay for the services (e.g., credit card, debit card, check, etc.).
* **Payment Schedule** (23): Decide when the payment will be made.
* **I understand the cancellation policy** (24): Please agree to our cancellation policy.
* **Special Requests or Notes** (25): If you have any additional information or requests, please let us know.
