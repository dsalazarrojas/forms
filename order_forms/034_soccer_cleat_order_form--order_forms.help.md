# Soccer Cleat Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers or end-users to order soccer cleats. To complete the form, follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cleat Order** (`cleat_order`, `text`, optional): This field is where you can type in your cleat order details.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, optional): Please select the payment method you prefer:
	+ Cash
	+ Card
* **Delivery Preferences** (`delivery_preferences`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your delivery preferences:
	+ In Person
	+ At Desk
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, `text`, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional customer details.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields (if any) to ensure your order is processed correctly.
- You can leave optional fields blank if they do not apply to your situation.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form owner at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
