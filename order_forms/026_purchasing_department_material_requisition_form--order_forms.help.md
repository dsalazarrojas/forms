# Purchasing Department Material Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request materials from the purchasing department. It is intended for internal use only and is not for new material introductions or purchasing requests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure all information is accurate and complete.
2. Enter your name and department as the requisitor in the "Requisitor" field.
3. Select the correct material type from the options provided.
4. Enter a detailed description of the material in the "Material Description" field.
5. Enter the quantity of the material requested.
6. Enter the unit cost of the material.
7. Select whether this material was requested by the department or not.
8. Select whether the approver has approved this request or not.
9. Select the department(s) affected by this material request.
10. Enter the date the material was ordered.
11. Enter the time the material was ordered.
12. Add any additional comments or notes about this requisition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Material Type** (text):
	+ Enter the type of material being requisitioned (e.g. "Office Supplies", "Electronics", etc.).
* **Material Description** (text):
	+ Provide a detailed description of the material being requisitioned.
* **Quantity** (number):
	+ Enter the quantity of the material requested.
* **Unit Cost** (number):
	+ Enter the cost of the unit of the material (e.g. the cost of one unit of the material).
* **Requested By** (select_one):
	+ Select "Yes" if this material was requested by the department, otherwise select "No".
* **Approver** (select_one):
	+ Select "Yes" if the approver has approved this request, otherwise select "No".
* **Department** (select_multiple):
	+ Select all departments that will be affected by this material request (e.g. "Marketing", "Finance", etc.).
* **Requisitor** (text):
	+ Enter your name and department that will be using the material.
* **Date Ordered** (date):
	+ Enter the date the material was ordered (in the format "YYYY-MM-DD").
* **Time Ordered** (time):
	+ Enter the time the material was ordered (in the 24-hour time format).
* **Notes** (note):
	+ Add any additional comments or notes about this requisition.
