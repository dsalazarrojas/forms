# Pharmacy Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to streamline the process of ordering medications for patients, ensuring accurate and secure interactions between patients and pharmacists.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Patient Information**: Enter your name, date of birth, and any other relevant medical information.
2. **Medical History**: Describe your medical history, including any previous medical conditions or allergies you may have.
3. **Medication Order**: Specify the medication you are requesting, its dosages, and any instructions.
4. **Dosing Instructions**: Provide detailed dosing instructions, including how often to take the medication and any specific guidelines.
5. **Allergies**: List any allergies or sensitivities you have to certain medications.
6. **Medical Conditions**: Describe any ongoing medical conditions that may affect your medication.
7. **Medication Side Effects**: Report any side effects you experience while taking the medication.
8. **Medication Interactions**: Specify any medications you are currently taking that may interact with the prescribed medication.
9. **Medication Refills**: Request any refills or adjustments to your medication.
10. **Medication Dosages**: Confirm your medication dosages and instructions.
11. **Patient Prescription**: Review and confirm your prescription.
12. **Pharmacy Contact**: Enter your contact information for the pharmacy.
13. **Doctor Signature**: Sign your name as your doctor, verifying the accuracy of the prescription.
14. **Doctor Name**: Enter your name as the doctor.
15. **Doctor Title**: Enter your title as the doctor.
16. **Pharmacy Name**: Enter the name of the pharmacy.
17. **Pharmacy Signature**: Sign as the pharmacist, confirming the accuracy of the prescription.
18. **Submitted By**: Enter your name as the person submitting the form.
