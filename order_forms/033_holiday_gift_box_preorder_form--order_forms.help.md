# Holiday Gift Box Preorder Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to pre-order a custom gift box with specific details and requirements. It allows customers to specify the number of guests, type of gift box, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Gift Box Type" option from the dropdown menu to indicate if you want a standard gift box or a custom one.
2. Enter the "Number of Guests" to specify how many people the gift box is intended for.
3. Choose the "Preorder Type" option to indicate if the preorder is for a "Gift Box", "Custom", or "Other" purpose.
4. Fill in the "Preorder Details" with any additional information about the preorder.
5. Select the "Preorder Status" option to mark the status of the preorder as "Active", "Inactive", or "Pending".
6. Enter the "Email" and "Phone" of the customer to contact them.
7. Choose the "Address" option to indicate if the gift box will be delivered to the customer's address.
8. Fill in the "City", "State", and "Zip" of the customer's address.
9. Choose the "Country" option to select the country where the customer is located.
10. Enter the "Gift Box Name", "Gift Box Type", and "Gift Box Price" for the custom gift box.
11. Fill in the "Gift Box Description" and "Gift Box Image" if applicable.
12. Enter the "Total Gift Boxes" and "Total Guests" to specify the quantity of gift boxes and guests.
13. Fill in the "Preorder Date" to specify the date of the preorder.
14. Select the "Status" option to mark the status of the preorder as "Active", "Inactive", or "Pending".
15. Fill in the "Message" and "Notes" with any additional comments or instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Gift Box Preorder Form** (Text): This is the title of the form.
* **Preorder Date** (Date, optional): The date when the customer preordered the gift box.
* **Number of Guests** (Number, optional): The number of guests the gift box is intended for.
* **Preorder Type** (Select One, optional): The type of preorder, which can be "Gift Box", "Custom", or "Other".
* **Preorder Details** (Note, optional): Additional information about the preorder.
* **Preorder Status** (Select One, optional): The status of the preorder, which can be "Active", "Inactive", or "Pending".
* **Email** (Email, optional): The email of the customer to contact them.
* **Phone** (Text, optional): The phone number of the customer.
* **Address** (Select Multiple, optional): A yes/no option to indicate if the gift box will be delivered to the customer's address.
* **City** (Text, optional): The city where the gift box will be delivered.
* **State** (Text, optional): The state where the gift box will be delivered.
* **Zip** (Text, optional): The zip code where the gift box will be delivered.
* **Country** (Text, optional): The country where the customer is located.
* **Gift Box Name** (Text, optional): The name of the custom gift box.
* **Gift Box Type** (Text, optional): The type of the custom gift box.
* **Gift Box Price** (Number, optional): The price of the custom gift box.
* **Gift Box Description** (Note, optional): A description of the custom gift box.
* **Gift Box Image** (Select Multiple, optional): A yes/no option to indicate if an image is provided for the custom gift box.
* **Total Gift Boxes** (Number, optional): The quantity of custom gift boxes.
* **Total Guests** (Number, optional): The total number of guests.
* **Preorder Date** (Date, optional): The date of the preorder.
* **Status** (Select One, optional): The status of the preorder, which can be "Active", "Inactive", or "Pending".
* **Message** (Note, optional): Additional comments or instructions for the customer.
