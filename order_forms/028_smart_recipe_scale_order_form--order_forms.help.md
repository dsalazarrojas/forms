# Smart Recipe Scale Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Smart Recipe Scale Order Form is designed to collect information from customers for orders of recipes scaled for their specific needs. This form will help us gather essential details such as customer contact and order details to process and fulfill the order efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the relevant fields with accurate information for the customer's order details.
2. Ensure that all required fields (identified by a red star) are filled in, even if they do not have an option selected or a value entered.
3. Select the shipping method and order status from the provided options.
4. Verify the order total before submitting the form.
5. Choose the payment method.
6. Review the form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, not required): Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, not required): Enter the customer's address.
* **Customer City** (`customer_city`, text, not required): Enter the customer's city.
* **Customer Postal Code** (`customer_postal_code`, text, not required): Enter the customer's postal code.
* **Customer State** (`customer_state`, text, not required): Enter the customer's state.
* **Customer Country** (`customer_country`, text, not required): Enter the customer's country.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, not required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, not required): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, note, not required): Enter any additional notes or comments for the customer.
* **Customer Shipping** (`customer_shipping`, text, not required): Enter the shipping information (e.g., apartment number, floor, etc.).
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, not required): Enter the shipping details.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, not required): Enter the payment method and any relevant details.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, not required): Enter the order details.
* **Order Confirmation** (`order_confirmation`, text, not required): Enter any confirmation or reference number for the order.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_one, not required): Select the status of the order (e.g., new, in progress, completed).
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, select_one, not required): Select the shipping method (e.g., ground, air, etc.).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, not required): Select the payment method (e.g., credit, cash, etc.).
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, not required): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, not required): Enter the time of the order.
* **Customer Shipping Name** (`shipping_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the customer's shipping contact.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, not required): Enter the tool assigned to process the order.
