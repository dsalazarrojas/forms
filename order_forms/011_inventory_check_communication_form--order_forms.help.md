# inventory_check_communication_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to communicate inventory information and check on order status. It is designed to collect detailed information on orders, including order details, inventory levels, and communication methods. This form is crucial for ensuring accurate and timely communication with customers and internal stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant form section from the top section dropdown menu. The form has two sections, but you can only complete one at a time.
2. Fill in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*).
3. Select one or multiple options from the dropdown menus as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Section* (Form Section, text, required):** This is the title of the form section you are completing. Select the relevant section from the dropdown menu.
* **Order Details* (Order Details, select_one, required):** Choose the correct option from the list to indicate the order details.
* **Inventory Level* (Inventory Level, select_multiple, required):** Select one or multiple options to indicate the current inventory level.
* **Communication Method* (Communication Method, select_multiple, required):** Choose the correct method to communicate with the customer.
* **Additional Comments (Additional Comments, text, optional):** Enter any additional comments or notes about the order.
* **Date Ordered* (Date Ordered, date, required):** Enter the date the order was placed.
* **Quantity Ordered* (Quantity Ordered, number, required):** Enter the quantity ordered.
* **Unit Cost (Unit Cost, number, optional):** Enter the unit cost of the order, if applicable.
* **Status* (Status, select_one, required):** Choose the status of the order.
* **Follow-up Action (Follow-up Action, select_multiple, optional):** Select any follow-up actions to take on the order.
* **Contact Number (Contact Number, text, optional):** Enter the contact number for the customer.
* **Email* (Email, email, required):** Enter the email address of the customer.
* **Date Order Received* (Date Order Received, date, required):** Enter the date the order was received.
* **Ship To* (Ship To, text, required):** Enter the shipping address.
* **Ship From (Ship From, text, optional):** Enter the shipping address from.
* **Comments (Comments, text, optional):** Enter any comments about the order.
* **Order Number* (Order Number, text, required):** Enter the order number.
* **Ship Method* (Ship Method, select_multiple, required):** Choose the shipping method.
* **Ship Address (Ship Address, text, optional):** Enter the shipping address.
* **Ship City (Ship City, text, optional):** Enter the city for the shipping address.
* **Ship State (Ship State, text, optional):** Enter the state for the shipping address.
* **Ship Zip (Ship Zip, text, optional):** Enter the zip for the shipping address.
* **Ship Country (Ship Country, text, optional):** Enter the country for the shipping address.
* **Form Section 2 (Form Section 2, text, optional):** This is not currently used in the form.
