# Material Handling Crane Component Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information for placing a new order for material handling crane components. Please complete this form with your order details for our team to process and fulfill your request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct crane model from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the serial number of the item you are ordering.
3. Enter the purchase price of the order.
4. Choose the date of the order (MM/DD/YYYY).
5. Enter the quantity ordered.
6. Enter the name of the customer placing the order.
7. Enter the email address of the customer (for contact purposes).
8. Select the material manufacturer of the item from the dropdown menu.
9. Add any additional notes or comments about the order.
10. Enter the phone number of the customer (for follow-up).
11. Choose the status of the order from the dropdown menu.
12. Select the type of order (e.g., new order, replacement, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Crane Model** (`crane_model`, `select_one`, optional): Select the correct model of the crane component being ordered.
* **Serial Number** (`serial_number`, `number`, optional): Enter the serial number of the item being ordered (if applicable).
* **Purchase Price** (`purchase_price`, `number`, optional): Enter the purchase price of the item being ordered.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, optional): Choose the date of the order in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Quantity Ordered** (`quantity_ordered`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of items ordered.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the customer placing the order.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the customer for contact purposes.
* **Crane Manufacturer** (`crane_manufacturer`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the manufacturer(s) of the material handling crane component.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the order.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the customer for follow-up.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the status of the order from the dropdown menu.
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of order (e.g., new, replacement, etc.).
