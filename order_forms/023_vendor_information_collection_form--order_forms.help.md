# Vendor Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about vendors, including their name, company name, email, phone number, services offered, address, website, vendor status, and creation and update dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the vendor's name in the `Vendor Name` field.
2.  Enter the company's name in the `Company Name` field.
3.  Enter the vendor's email address in the `Email` field.
4.  Enter the vendor's phone number in the `Phone` field.
5.  Describe the services offered by the vendor in the `Services Offered` field.
6.  Enter the vendor's physical address in the `Address` field.
7.  Enter the vendor's website in the `Website` field.
8.  Select the vendor's status as either `Active` or `Inactive` in the `Vendor Status` field.
9.  Enter the date the vendor was created in the `Created At` field.
10. Enter the date the vendor's information was last updated in the `Updated At` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Vendor Name** (`Vendor Name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the vendor, such as John Doe.
*   **Company Name** (`Company Name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the company associated with the vendor, such as ABC Corporation.
*   **Email** (`Email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the vendor.
*   **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the vendor.
*   **Services Offered** (`Services Offered`, text, required/optional): Describe the services offered by the vendor, such as web development and consulting.
*   **Address** (`Address`, text, required/optional): Enter the vendor's physical address.
*   **Website** (`Website`, text, required/optional): Enter the vendor's website.
*   **Vendor Status** (`Vendor Status`, select one, required/optional): Select the vendor's status as either `Active` or `Inactive`.
*   **Created At** (`Created At`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the vendor was created.
*   **Updated At** (`Updated At`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the vendor's information was last updated.

## Tips
*   Be accurate when entering the vendor's information to ensure that the correct data is stored in the system.
*   Double-check the vendor's status to ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
*   Use the correct format for dates to avoid errors.
