# Content Writing Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Content Writing Subscription Form is used to provide information about your content needs and preferences to receive professional content writing services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal and company information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Choose your subscription plan and content types.
3. Select your industry focus and tone preference.
4. Determine your SEO requirements and keywords.
5. Specify your delivery frequency and billing cycle.
6. Choose your payment method and brand guidelines.
7. Provide any additional services or special requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Subscriber Name** (Full name or company name): Enter your name or your company's name to help us understand who you are and what your organization is about.
* **Email Address** (Primary contact email): Enter your primary email address where you can be reached for correspondence.
* **Phone Number** (Contact number): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for urgent matters.
* **Company Name** (Organization name): Enter your company's name to help us understand your brand.
* **Subscription Plan**: Choose the plan that best suits your content needs (Basic, Standard, Premium, or Enterprise).
* **Content Types Needed**: Select all the content types you need (Blog Posts, Website Copy, Social Media Posts, etc.).
* **Industry Focus**: Select your industry to help us understand your context (Technology, Healthcare, Finance, etc.).
* **Average Word Count Preference**: Choose the average word count for your articles (500-800, 800-1200, 1200-2000, etc.).
* **Preferred Tone**: Select the tone you prefer for your content writing (Professional, Conversational, Technical, etc.).
* **SEO Requirements**: Choose whether you need SEO optimization for your content (Yes, Basic Keywords Only, No SEO needed).
* **Target Keywords**: Enter your primary keywords to focus on.
* **Delivery Frequency**: Choose how often you need content delivery (Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, Quarterly).
* **Desired Start Date**: Enter the date when you want our content creation to begin.
* **Billing Cycle**: Choose your payment frequency (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, PayPal, Check).
* **Brand Guidelines**: If you have brand guidelines, select Yes; otherwise, No.
* **Content Calendar Input**: Choose whether you'll provide topics in advance (Yes, monthly calendar; Yes, weekly topics; No, you choose topics).
* **Revision Needs**: Select the number of revisions you expect for each article (1 revision, 2 revisions, 3 revisions, Unlimited).
* **Additional Services**: Select any extra services you need (Image sourcing, Meta descriptions, Social media snippets, etc.).
* **Special Requests**: Enter any additional requirements or special requests you have.
