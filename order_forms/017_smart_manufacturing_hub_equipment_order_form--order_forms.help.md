# Smart Manufacturing Hub Equipment Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place orders for equipment in the Smart Manufacturing Hub. It allows users to provide their input, details about the equipment, and contact information. The form also includes fields for equipment status and order reason.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the user input fields (User Input, User Input 2, User Input 3, User Input 4) with any relevant information you'd like to provide about your order.
2. Enter the equipment details (Equipment ID, Equipment Name, Equipment Type, Serial Number, Location, Condition, Equipment Status, Order Reason).
3. Provide contact information (Contact Name, Contact Company, Contact Email, Contact Phone).
4. Select the correct equipment status (Operational, Non-operational, In Maintenance, In Storage, Unknown).
5. Finally, submit the form to place your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter any additional information you'd like to provide about your order.
- **Equipment Details** (`equipment_details`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about the equipment you're ordering.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information so we can reach out to you about your order.
- **Submit Form** (`submit_form`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your completed form.
- **User Notes** (`user_notes`, note, optional): Enter any notes or additional information you'd like to attach to your order.
- **Contact Notes** (`contact_notes`, note, optional): Enter any notes or additional information from the contact person.
- **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the date of your visit to review the equipment.
- **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, optional): If applicable, enter the time of your visit to review the equipment.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your order.
- **User Input 2** (`user_input_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional information you'd like to provide about your order.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's phone number.
- **User Input 3** (`user_input_3`, text, optional): Enter any additional information you'd like to provide about your order.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact person's email address.
- **Equipment ID** (`equipment_id`, number, optional): Enter the unique identifier for the equipment you're ordering.
- **Equipment Name** (`equipment_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the equipment you're ordering.
- **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of equipment you're ordering.
- **Serial Number** (`serial_number`, text, optional): Enter the serial number of the equipment you're ordering.
- **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the equipment is currently located.
- **Condition** (`condition`, text, optional): Enter the current condition of the equipment.
- **Equipment Status** (`equipment_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the equipment (Operational, Non-operational, In Maintenance, In Storage, Unknown).
- **Order Reason** (`order_reason`, text, optional): Enter the reason for your order.
- **User Input 4** (`user_input_4`, text, optional): Enter any additional information you'd like to provide about your order.
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
- **Contact Company** (`contact_company`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person's company.

Note: Some fields are optional, so you only need to fill out the ones that are relevant to your order.
