# tyb_nail_bar_subscribe_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users who wish to subscribe to our email list for marketing and promotional purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please make sure it's a valid email address.
2. If you wish to subscribe, select "Yes" from the "Opt-in to email newsletters" field.
3. If you wish to receive a free sample of a product from our nail bar, select one or multiple options from the "Free Sample" field.
4. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
5. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
6. You may select "Yes" or "No" for the "country" field, but this field is not required.
7. You may select "Yes" or "No" for the "state" field, but this field is not required.
8. Enter your city in the "city" field.
9. Enter your address in the "address" field.
10. Select a date from the "date" field, if applicable.
11. Select a time from the "time" field, if applicable.
12. If you have a preferred phone type, select from the options in the "phone_type" field.
13. Enter your phone extension in the "phone_ext" field.
14. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
15. Enter your zip code in the "zip_code" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your valid email address to subscribe to our email list.
* **Opt-in to email newsletters** (email_opt_in, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" to receive email newsletters.
* **Free Sample** (free_sample, select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple free samples to receive from our nail bar.
* **First name** (first_name, text, optional): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last name** (last_name, text, optional): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Opt-in to emails** (email_opt_in, select_one, optional): This is a duplicate of the "Opt-in to email newsletters" field. Please ignore it.
* **Free Nail Polish** (free_sample, select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple free samples of nail polish to receive from our nail bar.
* **Free Nail Polish Kit** (free_sample, select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple free samples of nail polish kits to receive from our nail bar.
* **Free Nail Brush** (free_sample, select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple free samples of nail brushes to receive from our nail bar.
* **Comments** (comments, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number for identification purposes.
* **Zip Code** (zip_code, text, optional): Enter your zip code for identification purposes.
* **Country** (country, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" to indicate you are from our country.
* **State** (state, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" to indicate you are from our state.
* **City** (city, text, optional): Enter your city for identification purposes.
* **Address** (address, text, optional): Enter your address for identification purposes.
* **Date** (date, date, optional): Select a date if applicable.
* **Time** (time, time, optional): Select a time if applicable.
* **Comments** (comments, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Phone Type** (phone_type, select_one, optional): Select your phone type (Home, Work, or Cell).
* **Phone Extension** (phone_ext, text, optional): Enter your phone extension.
* **Phone Number** (phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number for identification purposes.

Note: Fields marked as optional are not required to complete the form.
