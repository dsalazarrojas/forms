# Retail Shelf Mirror Display Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Shelf Mirror Display Order Form is used to collect information from retailers and customers for order purposes. This form is used to create or manage orders, track order status, and gather relevant details about the retailer and customer.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Retailer Information" page (page 1) and fill out the form with the following fields:
	* "Retailer Comments": This field is optional, but if you wish to provide additional comments or notes about the order, you can do so here.
2. Proceed to the "Customer Information" page (page 2) and fill out the form with the following fields:
	* "Customer Comments": This field is also optional, but provides space for additional comments or notes about the customer.
3. Next, move to the "Order Details" page (page 3) and enter the following information:
	* "Order Total": Enter the total amount of the order.
4. On the "Order Products" page (page 4), select from the options provided to indicate the status of the order:
	* "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the order is active or inactive.
5. Proceed to the "Shipping" page (page 5) and provide the following information:
	* "Shipping Method": Select the shipping method used for the order, whether it's "Yes" or "No".
6. Enter the "Shipping Cost" of the order.
7. On the "Payment" page (page 6), select the payment method used:
	* "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether payment has been made.
8. Move to the "Retailer Comments" page (page 7) and fill out the form with:
	* "Retailer Comments": This field is for the retailer to leave comments or notes about the order.
9. The "Customer Comments" page (page 8) is also for additional comments or notes from the customer.
10. On the "Order Status" page (page 9), select from the options provided to indicate the status of the order:
	* "Active" or "Inactive".
11. Enter the "Sales Tax" of the order, whether it's "Yes" or "No".
12. Finally, move to the "Payment Method" page (page 10) to select:
	* "Yes" or "No" to indicate the payment method used.
13. On the "Retail Shelf Mirror ID" page (page 11), enter the:
	* ID number for the retail shelf mirror.
14. Enter the "Retail Store Name" on the next page (page 12).
15. On the "Customer Information" page (page 13), enter the following:
	* "Customer Name".
	* "Customer Phone".
	* "Customer Email".
	* "Customer Street".
	* "Customer City".
	* "Customer State".
	* "Customer Zip".
	* "Customer Country".
	* "Customer Region".
16. Finally, enter the "Date Created" on the last page (page 14).
