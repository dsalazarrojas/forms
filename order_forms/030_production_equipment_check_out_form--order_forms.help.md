# Production Equipment Check Out Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Production Equipment Check Out Form is used to check out production equipment for clients. This form helps to reserve and track the status of equipment, ensuring accurate and efficient usage.


## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the client for which you are checking out the equipment.
2.  Enter the client's email address.
3.  Enter the equipment ID number.
4.  Provide a description of the equipment being checked out.
5.  Enter the start and end dates and times for the rental period.
6.  Specify the rental duration.
7.  Choose the status of the equipment rental (active, inactive, or pending).
8.  Add any comments or notes regarding the rental.
9.  Select the person assigned to the rental.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **client_name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the client for whom the equipment is being checked out.
*   **client_email** (`client_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the client.
*   **equipment_id** (`equipment_id`, number, optional): Enter the equipment ID number.
*   **equipment_description** (`equipment_description`, text, optional): Provide a description of the equipment being checked out.
*   **rental_start_date** (`rental_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the rental period.
*   **rental_start_time** (`rental_start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the rental period.
*   **rental_end_date** (`rental_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the rental period.
*   **rental_end_time** (`rental_end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the rental period.
*   **rental_duration** (`rental_duration`, text, optional): Enter the rental duration.
*   **rental_status** (`rental_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of the equipment rental (active, inactive, or pending).
*   **comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any comments or notes regarding the rental.
*   **assigned_to** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional): Select the person assigned to the rental.

## Tips

*   Make sure to complete all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
*   Double-check the rental start and end dates and times for precision.
*   Clearly communicate any comments or notes with the client.
*   Verify the assigned person is aware of the rental.
*   Review the rental status to ensure it accurately reflects the equipment's current state.
*   Update the form as needed to reflect any changes in the equipment's status or rental duration.
