# Change Order - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Change Order form is used to document modifications to existing orders, ensuring accurate tracking and approval of changes. It helps to record details of the change, including the type of change, description, affected item, and original and new quantities, prices, and totals. This form is essential for maintaining accurate records and facilitating approval processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Change Order form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the "Change Order Date" field and enter the date the change order was issued.
2.  Enter the "Change Order Number", a unique identifier for the change order.
3.  Provide the "Original Purchase Order Number" and "Original PO Date" to reference the original order being modified.
4.  Enter the name of the customer or individual placing the order.
5.  Enter the name of the vendor or supplier fulfilling the order.
6.  Describe the "Original Order Description" to clarify what was originally ordered.
7.  Select the type of change (Addition, Deletion, Modification, Substitution, Quantity Change, or Scope Change) from the "Type of Change" dropdown menu.
8.  Enter a detailed description of the change in the "Description of Change" field.
9.  Specify the item or service affected by the change in the "Item or Service Affected" field.
10. Enter the original quantity, if applicable.
11. Enter the new quantity, if applicable.
12. Enter the original unit price, if applicable.
13. Enter the new unit price, if applicable.
14. Enter the original total amount, if applicable.
15. Enter the new total amount, if applicable.
16. Indicate the "Cost Impact" of the change, if applicable.
17. Provide a reason for the change, if necessary.
18. Select the current "Approval Status" (Pending Approval, Approved, Rejected, or Requires Further Review).
19. If applicable, enter the name of the person who approved the change.
20. Select "Agreed" or "Pending" for "Both Parties Acknowledge", confirming change acceptance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Change Order Date**: Date the change order was issued.
* **Change Order Number**: Unique identifier for the change order.
* **Original Purchase Order Number**: Reference to the original order being modified.
* **Original PO Date**: Date of the original order.
* **Customer Name**: Company or individual placing the order.
* **Vendor or Supplier Name**: Company fulfilling the order.
* **Original Order Description**: Description of what was originally ordered.
* **Type of Change**: Type of modification (Addition, Deletion, Modification, Substitution, Quantity Change, or Scope Change).
* **Description of Change**: Detailed description of the change.
* **Item or Service Affected**: Specify the item or service being modified.
* **Original Quantity**: Original amount.
* **New Quantity**: Modified amount.
* **Original Unit Price**: Original cost per unit.
* **New Unit Price**: Modified cost per unit.
* **Original Total Amount**: Total original cost.
* **New Total Amount**: Total after change.
* **Cost Impact**: Amount increase or decrease.
* **Reason for Change**: Why the change is needed.
* **Approval Status**: Current approval status (Pending Approval, Approved, Rejected, or Requires Further Review).
* **Authorized By**: Name of person who approved the change.
* **Both Parties Acknowledge**: Confirm change acceptance (Agreed, Pending, or Disputed).
