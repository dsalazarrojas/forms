## Purpose
The Scentsy Holiday Collection Order Form is designed to collect customer information and order details for the holiday season. This form is meant to be a simple and straightforward way to capture key data from customers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your information in the "Customer Information" section, including [insert name, address, and other relevant details].
2. Select the items you would like to order from the "Order Items" section. You may choose one or multiple items.
3. Provide your delivery information in the "Delivery Information" section, including [insert delivery details].
4. Enter your payment information in the "Payment Information" section, including [insert payment details].
5. Confirm your order and payment in the "Confirm Order" and "Confirm Payment" sections, respectively.
6. Sign the "Customer Signature" and "Representative Signature" sections, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, optional): This section is where you can enter your personal details, such as your name and address.
* **Order Items** (`order_items`, select_multiple, optional): Select the items you would like to order from the available options. You may choose one or multiple items.
* **Delivery Information** (`delivery_info`, text, optional): Enter your delivery details, such as your shipping address and any special instructions.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, optional): Enter your payment details, such as your credit card number and expiration date.
* **Confirm Order** (`confirm_order`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm your order details, ensuring that all information is accurate.
* **Confirm Payment** (`confirm_payment`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm your payment details, ensuring that all information is accurate.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, text, optional): Sign this section to confirm that you have reviewed and agreed to the order details.
* **Representative Signature** (`representative_signature`, text, optional): If a representative is placing the order on your behalf, they must sign this section to confirm that they have reviewed and agreed to the order details.

## Tips
- Ensure that you have entered accurate information in all sections.
- Double-check your order and payment details before confirming.
