# Precision Balancing Weight Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers about their orders, specifically the Precision Balancing Weight order. The form helps to gather essential details such as customer name, order ID, order date, and order status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Customer Name** in the `customer_name` field.
2. Provide your **Customer Order ID** in the `customer_order_id` field.
3. Enter your **Customer Email** in the `customer_email` field.
4. Enter your **Customer Phone Number** in the `customer_phone` field.
5. Select the **Order Date** for your order using the date picker.
6. Select the **Order Time** for your order using the time picker.
7. Choose the status of your order from the options: **Active** or **Inactive**.
8. Enter any additional comments about your order in the `order_comment` field.
9. Enter any relevant notes about your order in the `order_note` field.
10. Provide the details of your **Weight Order** in the `weight_order` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer_name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): This is the customer's name associated with the order. Please enter the customer's full name.
* **Customer Order ID** (`customer_order_id`, number, required/optional): This is a unique identifier for the customer's order. Enter a number or alphanumeric code for easy reference.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required/optional): This is the email address of the customer associated with the order. Please enter a valid email address.
* **Customer Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, text, required/optional): This is the customer's phone number for any communication purposes. Enter a numeric string with a standard phone number format (e.g., +1-123-456-7890).
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date you would like to associate with your order using the built-in date picker.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, required/optional): Select the time you would like to associate with your order using the built-in time picker.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the status of your order from the options: **Active** or **Inactive**.
* **Order Comment** (`order_comment`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments about your order if you have any.
* **Order Note** (`order_note`, note, required/optional): Enter any relevant notes about your order if you have any.
* **Weight Order** (`weight_order`, text, required/optional): Provide the details of your weight order, including the type and quantity of items.
