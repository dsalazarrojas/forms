# Tournament Participant Wristband Reorder Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tournament Participant Wristband Reorder Form is a tool for participants who have purchased wristbands and need to reorder them due to issues with their initial order. This form aims to collect necessary information to facilitate the reordering process.

## How To Complete This Form
To successfully complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your reorder form information in the "Reorder Form" field.
2. Provide your shipping details in the "Shipping Details" field.
3. Enter your reordering payment information in the "Reorder Payments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reorder Form** (`reorder_form`, text, Optional): Describe the issue with your original order here.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_form`, text, Optional): Please provide your shipping details, including any specific instructions or requests for delivery.
* **Reorder Payments** (`payment_form`, text, Optional): Provide any additional payment information for the reordered items.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required information to ensure a smooth reordering process.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the tournament organisers directly.
- Only fill out this form if you have already purchased wristbands and need to reorder them.
