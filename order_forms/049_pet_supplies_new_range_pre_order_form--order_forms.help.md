# pet_supplies_new_range_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "pet_supplies_new_range_pre_order_form" is a form used to collect information from customers to facilitate the ordering of pet supplies. This form helps the customer to provide essential details about their order, including delivery preferences and pet type information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Pre Order Form (Page 1) with any information about your pet supplies pre-order.
2. Select your delivery details (Page 2): Choose between 'Yes' or 'No' for delivery of your pet supplies.
3. Select your pet type (Page 3): Choose multiple pet types that apply to your order.
4. Fill out the contact information (Page 4): Provide your contact details for further communication.
5. Enter your email address (Page 5): Enter your email address for communication and order updates.
6. Fill out the phone number (Page 6): Enter your phone number for further communication.
7. Select the desired date for delivery (Page 7): Choose the date for your pet supplies delivery.
8. Set the desired time for delivery (Page 8): Choose the time for your pet supplies delivery.
9. Additional information (Page 9): Fill in any additional information about your pet supplies order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pre Order Form** (`Page 1`, `text`, optional): This section is for providing any information about your pet supplies pre-order.
* **Delivery Details** (`Page 2`, `select_one`, optional): Select 'Yes' if you want to have your pet supplies delivered, 'No' otherwise.
* **Pet Type** (`Page 3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose multiple pet types that apply to your order (e.g., cat, dog, fish, etc.).
* **Contact** (`Page 4`, `text`, optional): Fill out your contact information for further communication.
* **Email** (`Page 5`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for communication and order updates.
* **Phone** (`Page 6`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Date** (`Page 7`, `date`, optional): Select the desired date for your pet supplies delivery.
* **Time** (`Page 8`, `time`, optional): Set the desired time for your pet supplies delivery.
* **Additional information** (`Page 9`, `text`, optional): Fill in any additional information about your pet supplies order.
