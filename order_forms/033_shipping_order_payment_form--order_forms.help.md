# Shipping Order Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect shipping order payment information from customers. It's used to ensure accurate and timely payment processing and order tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the delivery date for your order in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
2. Enter the delivery time for your order in the format of HH:MM AM/PM.
3. Provide your delivery address in the "Delivery Address" field.
4. Select the payment method from the dropdown options.
5. Enter any additional notes about your order in the "Order Notes" field.
6. Enter your contact name in the "Contact Name" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Contact Email" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to schedule the delivery of your order.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to schedule the delivery of your order.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, optional): Enter your delivery address to ensure accurate order tracking.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select from the available payment methods, which are Visa, Mastercard, PayoneTouch, PayPal, American Express, and Discover.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the contact name for your order.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact email for your order.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number for your order.
