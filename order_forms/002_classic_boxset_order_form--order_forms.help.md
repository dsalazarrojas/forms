# Classic Boxset Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers place orders for boxsets, providing a streamlined way to input their details and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the customer information section, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the shipping address to ensure correct delivery.
3. In the Boxset Selection section:
   * Choose the type of boxset you'd like to order.
   * Select the edition variant.
   * Choose the quantity.
4. For Pricing and Payment, enter the unit price, discount code (if applicable), select your preferred shipping method, and payment method.
5. If you have any special instructions or requests, add them in the Additional Information section.
6. Finally, enter the date of the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name to identify yourself as the customer.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address for order confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact number for delivery coordination.
* **Street Address**: Provide your full shipping address for delivery purposes.
* **City**: Enter the city for correct shipping delivery.
* **State/Province**: Enter the state or province for correct shipping delivery.
* **Postal Code**: Enter the postal code for correct shipping delivery.
* **Country**: Enter the country of delivery.
* **Boxset Type**: Select the classic boxset you wish to order (e.g., film, music, book, documentary).
* **Edition**: Choose the edition variant of the selected boxset.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of boxsets you'd like to order.
* **Unit Price**: Enter the price per boxset.
* **Discount Code**: If you have a discount code, enter it here.
* **Shipping Method**: Select your preferred shipping method (standard, express, overnight, in-store pickup).
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you'd like to pay (credit card, debit card, PayPal, bank transfer, check).
* **Special Instructions**: Add any special requests or handling instructions.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date of the order.

Note: This form is designed to guide you through the process of placing an order for a boxset. Please review each field carefully and ensure that you've filled out all required fields accurately.
