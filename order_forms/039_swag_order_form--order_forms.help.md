# Swag Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for a swag order, allowing customers to provide details about their order, including their contact information, shipping details, payment options, and order items. The form is meant to be completed by the customer to ensure accurate and timely processing of their order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the requested information. Make sure to provide accurate and clear details, especially for the shipping and payment sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Form**: This field is where you can enter any additional details about your order. (Required: false)
* **Customer Info**: Please enter your customer information, including your name and any other relevant details. (Required: false)
* **Shipping Details**: Enter your shipping details, including address and any other relevant information. (Required: false)
* **Payment Options**: Choose your preferred payment method and any other options relevant to your payment. (Required: false)
* **Order Items**: Enter the details about the items you are ordering. (Required: false)
* **Order Comments**: Enter any comments or questions you have about your order. (Required: false)
* **Total Cost**: Enter the total cost of your order. (Required: false)
* **Payment Status**: Select the status of your payment. (Required: false)
* **Order Status**: Select the status of your order. (Required: false)
* **Payment Gateway**: Choose whether or not you want to use a payment gateway. (Required: false)
* **Order Type**: Select the type of order you are placing. (Required: false)
* **Customer Address**: Enter your customer address. (Required: false)
* **Order Date**: Enter the date of your order. (Required: false)
* **Order Time**: Enter the time of your order. (Required: false)
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your customer phone number. (Required: false)
* **Customer Email**: Enter your customer email. (Required: false)
* **Customer Note**: Enter any additional notes about your customer. (Required: false)
* **Customer Comments**: Enter any comments from your customer. (Required: false)
* **Order Notes**: Enter any notes about your order. (Required: false)
* **Payment Method**: Choose whether or not you want to use a payment method. (Required: false)
* **Order Items Price**: Enter the price of the items in your order. (Required: false)
* **Order Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the items in your order. (Required: false)
* **Order Total**: Enter the total of your order. (Required: false)
* **Payment Confirmation**: Select whether or not you want to confirm your payment. (Required: false)

Note: This form is designed to be flexible and adaptable to your needs, but be sure to fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly for proper processing.
