# custom_heat_transfer_patch_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a custom heat transfer order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions above for each page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (7, text, required): Enter your name to be associated with the order.
- **Email** (8, email, required): Enter your email address to contact you regarding the order.
- **Phone** (9, text, required): Enter your phone number to contact you regarding the order.
- **Custom 1** (10, select_one, required): Select one of the available options for this custom field.
- **Custom 2** (11, select_multiple, required): Select multiple options from the available list for this custom field.
- **Date** (12, date, required): Enter the date for the order.
- **Time** (13, time, required): Enter the time for the order.
- **Amount** (14, number, required): Enter the amount for the order.
- **Currency** (15, select_one, required): Select the currency for the order.
- **Custom 3-10** (16-22, note, optional): These custom fields are for additional information. You can enter any text or note regarding your order.
- **Confirm** (25, text, required): Confirm that you have filled out the form correctly.
