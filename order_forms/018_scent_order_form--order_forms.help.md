# Scent Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scent Order Form is a tool for customers to place orders for products in a retail or e-commerce setting. It gathers necessary information from the customer, such as their email address, product details, and quantity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Order Details" section with any relevant information about your order.
2. Enter the **Product Name** of the product you would like to order.
3. Specify the **Quantity** of the product you would like to purchase.
4. (Optional) Enter the **Price** if you would like to set a custom price for your order.
5. Enter your **Customer Email**, where the order receipt or invoice will be sent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Order Details
* **Order Details**: A free-text field where you can enter any notes about your order. This could be any additional information or special requests.
### Product Name
* **Product Name**: The name of the product you would like to order.
### Quantity
* **Quantity**: The number of the product you would like to purchase.
### Price
* **Price**: The price of the product you would like to order. (Optional field, default price will be used if not filled)
### Customer Email
* **Customer Email**: Your email address, where the order receipt or invoice will be sent.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the "Customer Email" field, as this is required to process your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to our customer support team.
