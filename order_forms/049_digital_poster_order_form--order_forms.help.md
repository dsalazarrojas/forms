# Digital Poster Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to place digital poster orders, providing customers with a comprehensive platform to submit their requests. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure your order is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Customer Details** section with your contact information (fields 1-3).
2. Select the desired **Product Selection** (fields 4-6).
3. Enter the **Delivery Details** (fields 7-9).
4. Confirm your **Order** (fields 10-12).
5. Specify **Shipping Method**, **Delivery Date**, and **Delivery Time** (fields 13-15).
6. Add any **Order Notes**, **Billing Information**, and **Customer Information** (fields 16-18).
7. Enter **Customer Phone** and **Customer Comments** (fields 19-20).
8. Choose your **Payment Gateway** and enter any **Payment Information** (fields 21-23).
9. Confirm your **Order Status** and **Payment Gateway Confirmation** (fields 24-25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details (1)**: Enter your name, email, and phone number for contact purposes.
* **Product Selection (2)**: Select the digital poster(s) you would like to order.
* **Quantity (3)**: Enter the number of digital posters you would like to order.
* **Delivery Details (4)**: Enter any special delivery instructions or notes.
* **Shipping Address (5)**: Enter the address where you would like to receive your order.
* **Order Comments (6)**: Add any additional comments about your order.
* **Confirm Order (7)**: Confirm that you would like to place this order.
* **Order Confirmation (8)**: Enter your order confirmation email address.
* **Email Order Confirmation (9)**: Confirm that you would like to receive email order confirmations.
* **Shipping Method (10)**: Choose your preferred shipping method.
* **Delivery Date (11)**: Enter the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Delivery Time (12)**: Enter the time you would like to receive your order.
* **Delivery Note (13)**: Add any special delivery instructions or notes.
* **Order Total (14)**: This field is automatically calculated and displayed for your reference.
* **Customer Email (15)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Customer Phone (16)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Customer Comments (17)**: Add any additional comments about your order.
* **Customer Signature (18)**: Sign your name for verification purposes.
* **Order Status (19)**: Select the current status of your order.
* **Payment Gateway (20)**: Choose your preferred payment gateway.
* **Payment Information (21)**: Enter any relevant payment information.
* **Customer Information (22)**: Enter any additional customer information.
* **Order Notes (23)**: Add any additional notes about your order.
* **Payment Gateway Confirmation (24)**: Confirm your payment gateway confirmation email.
* **Billing Information (25)**: Enter your billing address or any billing information.
