# School Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This School Supply Order Form is a tool for school staff and parents/guardians to order school supplies for students. The form is designed to collect important information about the order, including contact details and the list of school supplies to be purchased.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. On the "School Supply Order" page (page 1), provide a brief description of the order, including the reason for the order.
2. On the "Parent/Guardian Information" page (page 2), select the relevant option for the parent/guardian's information.
3. On the "School Supply List" page (page 3), select the school supplies needed for the order.
4. On the "School Supplies" page (page 4), enter the quantity of each school supply selected in the previous step.
5. On the "Total Cost" page (page 5), enter the total cost of the order.
6. On the "School Supplies List" page (page 6), view a list of selected school supplies for verification.
7. On the "Comments" page (page 7), enter any additional comments or notes for the order.
8. On the "Email" page (page 8), enter your email address for communication.
9. On the "Phone" page (page 9), enter your phone number for communication.
10. On the "Parent/Guardian Name" page (page 10), enter the parent/guardian's name.
11. On the "School" page (page 11), enter the name of the school.
12. On the "Class" page (page 12), enter the name of the class.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Supply Order** (`school_supply_order_form_page_1`, `text`, not required): A brief description of the order, including the reason for the order.
* **Parent/Guardian Information** (`school_supply_order_form_page_2`, `select_one`, not required): Select the relevant option for the parent/guardian's information.
* **School Supply List** (`school_supply_order_form_page_3`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select the school supplies needed for the order.
* **School Supplies** (`school_supply_order_form_page_4`, `number`, not required): Enter the quantity of each school supply selected in the previous step.
* **Total Cost** (`school_supply_order_form_page_5`, `number`, not required): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **School Supplies List** (`school_supply_order_form_page_6`, `note`, not required): View a list of selected school supplies for verification.
* **Comments** (`school_supply_order_form_page_7`, `time`, not required): Enter any additional comments or notes for the order.
* **Email** (`school_supply_order_form_page_8`, `email`, not required): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone** (`school_supply_order_form_page_9`, `text`, not required): Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Parent/Guardian Name** (`school_supply_order_form_page_10`, `text`, not required): Enter the parent/guardian's name.
* **School** (`school_supply_order_form_page_11`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the school.
* **Class** (`school_supply_order_form_page_12`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the class.

# Tips

* Please ensure that you have permission to order school supplies before completing this form.
* Double-check your selections and information before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact the relevant school staff or parent/guardian.
