# Makeup Palette Pre Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Makeup Palette Pre Order Form is a tool used to collect information from customers for a custom makeup order. This form helps us understand your personal details and preferences for your order.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the "Name" and "Email" fields.
2. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
3. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
4. Add any order notes or comments in the "Order Notes" field.
5. Enter the total amount of your order in the "Total" field.
6. Choose your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown.
7. Select your preferred delivery options from the "Delivery Preferred" dropdown.
8. Choose how you'd like us to collect your data from the "Data Collection" dropdown.
9. Finally, read and agree to the payment terms in the "Payment Terms" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief title for your order.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full name for us to address you.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your physical address for delivery purposes.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments for your order.
* **Total** (`total_order`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total amount of your order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose from Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer as your preferred payment method.
* **Delivery Preferred** (`delivery_preferred`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more options for your preferred delivery time frame: Next Day, Standard (5-7 days), or Within a week.
* **Data Collection** (`data_collection`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose how you'd like us to collect your data: Contact, Marketing, or None.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `text`, required: false): Read and agree to the payment terms for your order.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure a smooth order process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* Our team will review your order details and get back to you as soon as possible.
