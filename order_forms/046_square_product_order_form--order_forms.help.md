# Square Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Square Product Order Form is designed to capture information about Square products and their associated payment terms. This form is intended for use in a commercial or business setting, such as when ordering or purchasing Square products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand what information is required and what information is optional.
2. Select the relevant payment terms that apply to your order.
3. Choose the desired Square products from the available options.
4. Submit the completed form to initiate the order or process the payment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payment Terms** (`square_product_order_form_payment_terms`): Select the desired payment terms from the options provided. This field determines the payment schedule for the order.
* **Product** (`square_product_order_form_product`): Choose the Square product you wish to order from the available options.
* **Product 2** (`square_product_order_form_product_2`): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you wish to order this product.
* **Product 3** (`square_product_order_form_product_3`): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you wish to order this product.
* **Product 4** (`square_product_order_form_product_4`): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you wish to order this product.
* ...and so on, for each product field. Note that each field has only two options: 'Yes' or 'No'.
