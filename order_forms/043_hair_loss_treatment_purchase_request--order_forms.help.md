# Hair Loss Treatment Purchase Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers for the purpose of processing a hair loss treatment purchase request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, phone number, and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your delivery address, city, and zip code.
3. Select the type of treatment you are interested in.
4. Choose the payment method and date for your order.
5. Select the product you would like to purchase and specify the quantity.
6. Provide any additional notes about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: A field to enter your name. This is a required field.
* **Phone**: A field to enter your phone number. This is a required field.
* **Email**: A field to enter your email address. This is a required field.
* **Address**: A field to enter your delivery address. This is an optional field.
* **City**: A field to enter the city where your delivery address is located. This is an optional field.
* **State**: A field to enter the state where your delivery address is located. This is an optional field.
* **Zip**: A field to enter your delivery zip code. This is a required field.
* **Type**: A field to select the type of treatment you are interested in. This is a required field.
* **Date**: A field to select the date for your order. This is a required field.
* **Method**: A field to select the payment method for your order. This is a required field.
* **Terms**: A field to select the payment terms for your order. This is a required field.
* **Notes**: A field to enter any additional notes about your order. This is an optional field.
* **Product**: A field to select the product you would like to purchase. This is a required field.
* **Quantity**: A field to specify the quantity of the product you would like to purchase. This is a required field.
* **Notes**: A field to enter any additional notes about your order. This is an optional field.
