# Personal Nail Strip Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for customers who wish to place a personalized nail strip order. It will capture their contact and shipping information, order details, and payment and shipping methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the required information. Fields marked as required must be filled out before proceeding to the next page. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Enter the customer's name and contact information.
* **Shipping Info** (`shipping_info`, text, optional): Enter the shipping address and contact information.
* **Order Info** (`order_info`, text, optional): Enter the details of the order.
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, select_one, optional): Select the shipping method to be used for the order (e.g. "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3").
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select the payment method to be used for the order (e.g. "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3").
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the order.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the order.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the order (e.g. "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3").
* **Shipping Status** (`shipping_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the shipping (e.g. "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3").
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, optional): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Payment Total** (`payment_total`, number, optional): Enter the total cost of the payment.
* **Payment Method Total** (`payment_method_total`, number, optional): Enter the total cost of the payment method.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes from the customer.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes for the order.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, text, optional): Enter the customer's signature.
* **Seller Notes** (`seller_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes from the seller.
* **Order Date 2** (`order_date_2`, date, optional): Enter the date of the second order.
* **Customer Email 2** (`customer_email_2`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address for the second order.
* **Customer Phone 2** (`customer_phone_2`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number for the second order.
* **Order Time 2** (`order_time_2`, time, optional): Enter the time of the second order.
* **Shipping Method 2** (`shipping_method_2`, select_one, optional): Select the shipping method for the second order (e.g. "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3").
* **Payment Method 2** (`payment_method_2`, select_one, optional): Select the payment method for the second order (e.g. "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3").

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields before proceeding.
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
