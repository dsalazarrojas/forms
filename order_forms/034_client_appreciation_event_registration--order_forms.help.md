# Client Appreciation Event Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Client Appreciation Event Registration form is designed to gather information from clients and attendees for a Client Appreciation Event. This form helps us understand your preferences, dietary needs, and other event-related requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please review each field carefully and provide accurate information.
2. If you're attending with a guest, please ensure you provide their information as well.
3. Select the correct dietary restrictions for both you and your guest.
4. Confirm your transportation requirements and any accessibility needs.
5. Provide a song request for the evening (optional).
6. Review and confirm your RSVP details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Full Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official government-issued ID or passport.
* **Company/Organization** (`client_organization`, text, required): Enter your company or organization name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address for event confirmation and follow-up communication.
* **Will you be attending?** (`attendance_status`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes, I'll be there!' to confirm your attendance.
* **Will you be bringing a guest?** (`bringing_guest`, select_one, required): Select 'True' if you'll be bringing a guest, and 'False' otherwise.
* **Guest Full Name** (`guest_name`, text, required if attending with a guest): If you selected 'True' for the previous field, please enter your guest's full name.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`attendee_dietary`, select_multiple, required): Select all dietary restrictions that apply to yourself (e.g., Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, etc.).
* **Guest Dietary Restrictions** (`guest_dietary`, select_multiple, required if attending with a guest): Select all dietary restrictions that apply to your guest (e.g., Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, etc.).
* **Do you require shuttle transportation from the hotel?** (`transportation_needed`, select_one, required): Select 'True' if you need shuttle transportation from the hotel, and 'False' otherwise.
* **Any specific accessibility requirements?** (`accessibility_needs`, text, optional): If you have any accessibility requirements, please describe them in detail.
* **Song Request for the evening** (`song_request`, text, optional): If you'd like to request a song for the evening, please enter the title or artist.
* **Registration Date** (`rsvp_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you're registering for the event.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (Day of Event)** (`emergency_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person or their phone number for the day of the event.

## Tips
* Please review each field carefully and provide accurate information.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact us for assistance.
* We look forward to seeing you at the Client Appreciation Event!
