# gym_locker_nameplate_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The gym_locker_nameplate_order_form is used to place an order for a gym locker nameplate. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the following information:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Member Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the member's name as it appears on their membership card or the name they would like to have displayed on the locker nameplate.
- **Member Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the member's email address, which will be used to contact them regarding their order.
- **Member Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the member's phone number, which will be used for additional communication regarding their order.
- **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select 'Yes' to indicate the member is paying for the order upfront or 'No' to arrange for payment at a later time.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the method of payment for the order, either 'Cash' or 'Online Payment'.
- **Order Type** (`order_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the type of order, either 'Regular' or 'Gift'.
- **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the order is intended for, in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
- **Order Time** (`order_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time the order is intended for, in the format 'HH:MM'.
- **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, required: false): Enter the quantity of locker nameplates being ordered.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Review the form for any required fields and ensure they are properly completed.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, do not hesitate to contact the gym administration.
