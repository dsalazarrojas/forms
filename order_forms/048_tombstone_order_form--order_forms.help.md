# Tombstone Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tombstone Order Form is used to gather information for a tombstone order. It is designed to be filled out by a customer or contact person for a company that specializes in monument and tombstone production or installation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of tombstone you would like to order from the options provided.
2. Enter your name and contact information, including phone number and email.
3. Provide any additional instructions for the order.
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tombstone Type** (`tombstone_type`, `select_one`): Select the type of tombstone you would like to order from the options provided.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`): Enter the name of the person or contact person who will be receiving the tombstone.
* **Monument Type** (`monument_type`, `select_one`): Select the type of monument you would like to order from the options provided.
* **Headstone** (`headstone`, `select_one`): Select the type of headstone you would like to order from the options provided.
* **Marker** (`marker`, `select_one`): Select the type of marker you would like to order from the options provided.
* **Other Product** (`other_product`, `select_one`): Select any other products you would like to order from the options provided.
* **Name On Headstone** (`name_on_headstone`, `text`): Enter the name of the person or contact person who will be receiving the headstone.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`): Enter the date of birth of the person or contact person who will be receiving the tombstone.
* **Date Of Death** (`date_of_death`, `date`): Enter the date of death of the person or contact person who will be receiving the tombstone.
* **Date Of Installation** (`date_of_installation`, `date`): Enter the date of installation of the tombstone.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`): Enter the location where the tombstone will be installed.
* **Order Instructions** (`order_instructions`, `text`): Enter any special instructions for the order.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`): Enter the name of the contact person for the order.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`): Enter the phone number of the contact person for the order.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `text`): Enter the email of the contact person for the order.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`): Enter the address of the contact person.
* **City** (`city`, `text`): Enter the city of the contact person.
* **State** (`state`, `text`): Enter the state of the contact person.
* **Zip** (`zip`, `text`): Enter the zip code of the contact person.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`): Enter any additional notes or comments about the order.
* **Tombstone Order Form Comments** (`tombstone_order_form_comments`, `text`): Enter any comments or notes about the tombstone order form.
* **Monument Type Comments** (`monument_type_comments`, `text`): Enter any comments or notes about the monument order.
* **Tombstone Type Comments** (`tombstone_type_comments`, `text`): Enter any comments or notes about the tombstone type.
* **Headstone Comments** (`headstone_comments`, `text`): Enter any comments or notes about the headstone.

## Tips
* Please review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required information is filled out.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact the company's customer support team.
