# Sustainable Steel Raw Material Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainable Steel Raw Material Order Form is used to collect information for orders of sustainable steel raw materials. This form is intended to be used by sales teams or customer service representatives to gather relevant details from customers before fulfilling an order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review each field for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Data** (`order_form_data`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant details about the order.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, `text`, optional): Please provide detailed customer information.
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, `text`, optional): Enter delivery details.
* **Notes** (`order_notes`, `note`, optional): Add any notes related to the order.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the delivery date of the order.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the delivery time of the order.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Choose the type of order (Aluminum, Copper, Galvanized, Stainless Steel, or Steel).
* **Comments** (`order_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, optional): Enter the order quantity.
* **Unit Price** (`unit_price`, `number`, optional): Enter the unit price of the order.
* **Total Price** (`total_price`, `number`, optional): Enter the total price of the order.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Review the form before submitting to ensure all fields are correct.
* Use clear and concise language when filling in the form.
