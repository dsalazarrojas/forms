# Retail Rebate Receipt Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers or employees to upload and track retail rebate receipts for rebates claimed. It is used for internal auditing and record-keeping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Upload Rebate Receipt" field and select the rebate receipt file to upload.
2. Fill out the "Product Code", "Product Code Value", "Purchase Date", "Claim Amount", "Claim Status", and "Claim Notes" fields as required.
3. Enter your "Email" address if you want to receive updates about your rebate claim.
4. Review the form and click the "Submit" button to upload and track your rebate receipt.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Upload Rebate Receipt**: Upload the retail rebate receipt by clicking on this field. Select the correct file from your computer or email attachment.
* **Product Code**: Enter the unique product code for the product you are claiming the rebate for. This helps us to track and verify the product.
* **Product Code Value**: Enter the product code value for the product you are claiming the rebate for. This is the value associated with the product code.
* **Purchase Date**: Enter the date you purchased the product. This helps us to track the purchase date.
* **Claim Amount**: Enter the amount you are claiming for the rebate. This amount will be verified with the store records.
* **Claim Status**: Select the current status of your rebate claim from the dropdown menu:
  + Claim Pending: The rebate claim has been made, but has not been verified.
  + Claim Approved: The rebate claim has been approved.
  + Claim Denied: The rebate claim has been denied.
* **Claim Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your rebate claim.
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you want to receive updates about your rebate claim. This ensures you receive notifications about any changes to your claim.
