## Graduation Regalia Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Graduation Regalia Order Form is designed for ordering graduation regalia (dresses) for students. This form requires providing personal and financial information to process the order. Fill out the form accurately to ensure a smooth and efficient ordering process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Complete the required fields**: All fields marked as "Required" must be filled in to submit the form.
2.  **Choose regalia details**: Select the type of regalia you wish to order.
3.  **Select payment method**: Choose how you wish to pay for your regalia.
4.  **Enter contact information**: Provide your email and phone number for contact purposes.
5.  **Submit the form**: Once all required fields are filled in, click "Submit" to complete the order.
