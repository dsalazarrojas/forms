# Afterpay Fitness Equipment Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Afterpay Fitness Equipment Order Form is a tool for customers to purchase fitness equipment through the Afterpay program. This form collects customer details, equipment selections, and payment plan preferences to facilitate smooth delivery and payment processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer information: Enter your full name, contact number, and email address in the required fields.
2. Choose your preferred payment plan: Select "Full Payment Upfront" or "Afterpay Installments" to determine your payment schedule.
3. Select your equipment: Choose the specific fitness equipment you wish to purchase from the options provided.
4. Add any additional details: If applicable, enter the weight or size of the equipment, and specify any assembly service requirements.
5. Confirm your order: Review your details and ensure everything is correct before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your mobile or landline number that we can use to contact you regarding your order.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address for digital receipt and tracking purposes.
* **Delivery Address**: Enter the address where you would like us to deliver the equipment.
* **Equipment Name - e.g., Treadmill, Dumbbells**: Choose the specific fitness equipment you wish to purchase.
* **Weight or Size - if applicable**: Enter the weight or size of the equipment, if applicable. For example, enter "20kg" for weight or "Large" for size.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of units you wish to purchase.
* **Assembly Service Required?**: Select "Yes, please assemble" for professional setup or "No, I will assemble myself" to save on costs.
* **Item Price**: Enter the price per unit of the selected equipment.
* **Payment Plan - Afterpay or Full**: Choose your preferred payment schedule between "Full Payment Upfront" or "Afterpay Installments".
* **Delivery access notes - Stairs, Elevator, etc.**: Enter any details about access requirements for delivery, such as stairs or elevator access.

## Tips
* Double-check your contact and delivery information to ensure accuracy and avoid any issues with delivery or communication.
* If you have any queries or concerns, contact our customer support team for assistance.
