# Fresh Produce Quality Sensor Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fresh Produce Quality Sensor Order Form is a form for customers or businesses to place an order for fresh produce. The form collects information about the product, sensor details, contact information, and delivery details to fulfill the order accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the order form section with the relevant details.
2. Provide product details in the Product Details section.
3. Enter sensor details in the Sensor Details section.
4. Fill in the contact information section with your relevant details.
5. Enter order info in the Order Info section.
6. Provide shipping details in the Shipping Details section.
7. Enter the delivery date and time in the Delivery Date and Time section.
8. Enter the product name in the Product Name section.
9. Enter the quantity ordered in the Quantity Ordered section.
10. Enter the unit price in the Unit Price section.
11. Enter the total cost in the Total Cost section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Form** (`order_form`, text, optional): This section is for customers or businesses to enter their order details. It's a general text field to describe the order.
* **Product Details** (`product_details`, text, optional): This section is for customers or businesses to enter the product details they want to order. This could include the type of produce, quantity, and any other relevant details.
* **Sensor Details** (`sensor_details`, text, optional): This section is for customers or businesses to enter the sensor details. This could include sensor model, serial number, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): This section is for customers or businesses to enter their contact information. This could include name, email, phone number, and any other relevant details.
* **Order Info** (`order_info`, text, optional): This section is for customers or businesses to enter order information. This could include order type, order date, and any other relevant details.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, optional): This section is for customers or businesses to enter shipping details. This could include shipping address, shipping method, and any other relevant details.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): This section is for customers or businesses to enter the delivery date. This could include the date of the delivery.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, optional): This section is for customers or businesses to enter the delivery time. This could include the time of the delivery.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, optional): This section is for customers or businesses to enter the product name.
* **Quantity Ordered** (`quantity_ordered`, number, optional): This section is for customers or businesses to enter the quantity ordered.
* **Unit Price** (`unit_price`, number, optional): This section is for customers or businesses to enter the unit price of the ordered product.
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, number, optional): This section is for customers or businesses to enter the total cost of the ordered product.

## Tips
* Double check all information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
