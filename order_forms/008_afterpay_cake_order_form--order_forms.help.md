# Afterpay Cake Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for an "Afterpay Cake Order Form" used by a bakery. Please fill out the form to provide the necessary details for your custom cake order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Customer Name** in the provided field.
2. Enter your **Email** in the provided field.
3. Select the **Pickup or Delivery Date** and **Pickup or Delivery Time** that suits you best.
4. Choose the **Event Type** that applies to your order (e.g., birthday, wedding, etc.).
5. Select the **Cake Size** that you prefer (e.g., 6 inch Round, 8 inch Round, 10 inch Round, Half Sheet, or Full Sheet).
6. Choose the **Flavor Selection** for your cake (e.g., Vanilla Bean, Chocolate Ganache, Red Velvet, Lemon Zest, or Carrot Cake).
7. Select the **Frosting Type** you prefer (e.g., Buttercream, Cream Cheese, Fondant, or Whipped Cream).
8. If you have any special **Message on the Cake**, you can write it in the provided text field.
9. If you have any **Specific Design Instructions**, you can provide them in the provided text field.
10. Select the **Payment Option** that suits your needs (e.g., "Afterpay" for installment payments, Full Payment (Credit Card), or Cash on Pickup).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be printed on the cake.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates on your order.
* **Pickup or Delivery Date**: Select the date you would like to pickup or receive your cake. Please note that we will contact you to confirm the availability of the date.
* **Pickup or Delivery Time**: Select the time slot you prefer for pickup or delivery. 
* **Event Type**: Choose the type of event for which you are ordering the cake (e.g., birthday, wedding, anniversary, etc.).
* **Cake Size**: Choose the size of cake that suits your needs (6 inch Round, 8 inch Round, 10 inch Round, Half Sheet, or Full Sheet).
* **Flavor Selection**: Choose the flavor for your cake (e.g., Vanilla Bean, Chocolate Ganache, Red Velvet, Lemon Zest, or Carrot Cake).
* **Frosting Type**: Choose the type of frosting for your cake (e.g., Buttercream, Cream Cheese, Fondant, or Whipped Cream).
* **Message on the Cake**: If you have any message you'd like to be written on the cake, enter it in this field.
* **Specific Design Instructions**: If you have any special design instructions or requests, enter them in this field.
* **Payment Option**: Select the payment option that suits your needs (e.g., "Afterpay" for installment payments, Full Payment (Credit Card), or Cash on Pickup).

**Tips**

* Make sure to select the correct event type and cake size for your order.
* If you select "Afterpay" as your payment option, we will contact you with the payment schedule and amounts.
* Please review your order carefully before submitting to ensure all the details are accurate.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
