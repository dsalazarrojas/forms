# Student Catering Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for student events, such as parties, functions, or other activities, to determine the catering requirements and options for the event. This form will help you to provide an accurate and efficient catering service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Menu** (Student Catering Order Form): Enter a brief description of the event or a note to the catering team.
2. **School Name** (School Name): Enter the name of the school hosting the event.
3. **Date of Event** (Date of Event): Enter the date of the event in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
4. **Time of Event** (Time of Event): Enter the time of the event in the format `HH:MM:SS` or the format `HH:MM`.
5. **Total Guests** (Total Guests): Enter the estimated number of guests attending the event.
6. **Catering Option** (Catering Option): Select the type of catering option required, such as `Option 1`, `Option 2`, or `Option 3`.
7. **Special Dietary Requirements** (Special Dietary Requirements): Select any special dietary requirements, such as `Option 1`, `Option 2`, or `Option 3`, applicable to the event.
