# Campus and Co. Meal Kit - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Campus and Co. Meal Kit form is designed for customers to order meal kits with ease. The form collects essential information to facilitate a successful delivery of the ordered meal kits.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details such as name, email address, and phone number in the **Customer Details** section.
2. Select the type of order you'd like to place - for yourself or as a gift.
3. If it's a gift order, provide the recipient's name and delivery address.
4. Select your preferred meal kit type and quantity from the **Meal Kit Selection** section.
5. Choose a delivery date that works for you.
6. Select a payment method (credit card, PayPal, or Campus Card) and ensure you're aware of our delivery instructions.
7. Confirm that you agree with our delivery policies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or document. This is required for verification purposes.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address to receive order confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you about your delivery.
* **Delivery Address**: Provide your full delivery address for accurate delivery purposes.
* **Order for Self or Gifting**: Select whether this order is for yourself or as a gift.
*   - If gifting, enter the recipient's name and delivery address for accurate delivery.
*   - You can add a personalized gift message if you'd like.
* **Select Your Meal Kit**: Choose your preferred meal kit type from options like Vegetarian Feast, Classic Meat, etc.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of meal kits you'd like to order.
* **Delivery Date**: Select a date that works for you to receive the meal kit.
* **Dietary Preferences and Allergies**: If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, let us know so we can adjust the meal kit accordingly.
* **Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method from credit card, PayPal, or Campus Card.
* **Special Instructions for Delivery**: If you have any special delivery instructions, such as leaving the kit at the door, please provide them here.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Confirm that you've read and agree to our delivery policies and terms.

## Tips

- Ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
- Double-check your order details before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
