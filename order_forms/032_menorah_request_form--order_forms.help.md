# Menorah Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Menorah Request Form is a tool used by Menorah organizations to submit requests for resources and support to a central hub. It's a streamlined process to ensure that your organization's needs are met, and you receive the necessary assistance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Menorah Request Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your Menorah organization's information.
2. Select the appropriate Request Type to indicate the purpose of your request.
3. Provide any additional context and details in the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Menora Request (1)** (`text`, required: false): This field is the primary description of your request. Please provide a clear and concise summary of your Menorah organization's needs.
* **Organization Name (2)** (`text`, required: false): Enter your Menorah organization's name as it is recognized in the community.
* **Organization Address (3)** (`text`, required: false): If your organization has a physical address, enter it here. This helps the central hub know where to send information and resources.
* **Synagogue Name (4)** (`text`, required: false): If your organization is associated with a synagogue, enter the name here. This helps the central hub understand your organization's connection to the synagogue community.
* **Request Type (5)** (`select_one`, required: false): Select one of the following options:
	+ Chanukah Programs: You are requesting resources for Chanukah-related programs and events.
	+ Home Use: You are requesting resources for personal or home use within your organization.
	+ Community Outreach: You are requesting resources to support community outreach and engagement.
* **Purpose (6)** (`text`, required: false): Explain the purpose of your request in a few sentences.
* **Contact Person (7)** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person who can be contacted for more information.
* **Contact Phone (8)** (`text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Email (9)** (`email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Request Date (10)** (`date`, required: false): If you know the date you need the resources by, enter it here. This helps the central hub plan ahead.
* **Additional Notes (11)** (`note`, required: false): Provide any additional context or information that may aid the central hub in understanding and processing your request.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your request is specific and detailed, as this helps the central hub process your request efficiently.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the central hub directly.
* If you need to resubmit a request, make sure to reference your previous request ID so that it can be easily tracked and updated.
