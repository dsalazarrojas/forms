# School T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School T Shirt Order Form is used to collect information from customers to place an order for a school T shirt. The form is used to gather details on the customer's preferences for T shirt size and color.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Customer Name field with the name of the person placing the order.
2. Enter the Customer Email field with the customer's email address.
3. Enter the Customer Phone field with the customer's phone number.
4. Fill in the Customer Address field with the customer's address.
5. Select the preferred T-Shirt Size (Small or Large) in the T-Shirt Size field.
6. Select the preferred T-Shirt Color (Blue or Black) in the T-Shirt Color field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): The Customer Name field is where you should enter the name of the person placing the order.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the customer's email address in this field.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's phone number in this field.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's address in this field.
* **T-Shirt Size Small** (`tshirt_size_s`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if the customer prefers a small T shirt, or 'No' otherwise.
* **T-Shirt Size Large** (`tshirt_size_l`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if the customer prefers a large T shirt, or 'No' otherwise.
* **T-Shirt Color** (`tshirt_color`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all preferred T shirt colors from the list.
* **Blue** (`tshirt_color_blue`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if the customer prefers a blue T shirt, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Black** (`tshirt_color_black`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if the customer prefers a black T shirt, or 'No' otherwise.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the customer's details are accurate to ensure timely and correct order fulfillment.
* If the customer has any additional requests or preferences, please make a note of these in the order notes field (not shown).
* This form is for order requests only, and is not for general contact or inquiries.
