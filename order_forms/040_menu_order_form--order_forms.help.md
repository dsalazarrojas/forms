# Menu Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record and store information about menu orders for our restaurant. It is intended to be filled out by customers after they have placed an order and to provide us with essential details about their order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out all required fields (marked as **Required**).
2. For fields with multiple options, select the correct answer or enter your information as prompted.
3. Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, Text, **Required**): Enter your name to associate with your order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, Select One, **Optional**): 
	+ Select "Yes" if you have selected to pay using our payment method.
	+ Select "No" otherwise.
* **Date** (`date`, Date, **Optional**): Enter the date you would like to have your order delivered on. Please use the standard date format (e.g. YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Time** (`time`, Time, **Optional**): Select the time you would like to have your order delivered on.
* **Note** (`note`, Note, **Optional**): Add any special instructions or requests you would like to include with your order.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, **Optional**): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues with your order, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will contact you via email if we need further information from you.
