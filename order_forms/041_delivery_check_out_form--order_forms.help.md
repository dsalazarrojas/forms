# Delivery Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect important details about the delivery process and customer information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your delivery details in the "Delivery Details" section.
2. Select your preferred payment option from the "Payment Options" section.
3. Provide your customer information in the "Customer Info" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, text, optional): Enter your delivery details, such as address, contact number, or any other relevant information.
* **Payment Options** (`payment_options`, select multiple, optional): Choose your preferred payment method, which could be Credit Card, PayPal, Cash on Delivery, or Bank Transfer.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, note, optional): Add a note about the customer, such as a brief description or any special instructions.
