# Postpartum Meal Plan Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Postpartum Meal Plan Request Form is a tool designed to collect information from users about their postpartum experience and preferences for support during this period. It helps healthcare providers or support teams tailor their services to the individual user's requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Details on page 1 of the form.
2. Select your preferred method of delivery and support on page 3.
3. Choose any follow-up methods you would like to receive from us on page 4.
4. If you have any additional comments or requests, add them in the Notes section on page 5.
5. Finally, click the Submit button on page 6 to send your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Details (Page 1)
* **User Details** (`User Details`, text, required): Fill in your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.

### Delivery Details (Page 2)
* **Delivery Details** (`Delivery Details`, text, optional): If you have any special instructions or details about your delivery preferences, please include them here.

### Meal Preferences (Page 3)
* **Meal Preferences** (`Meal Preferences`, select one, required): Please select one of the following options:
	+ Breastfeeding Support
	+ Meal Delivery
	+ Phone Support

### Follow Up (Page 4)
* **Follow Up** (`Follow Up`, select multiple, optional): Please select any of the following methods for follow-up communication:
	+ Phone
	+ Text
	+ Email
	+ Other (please specify in the Notes section)

### Notes (Page 5)
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or requests, please add them here.

### Submit (Page 6)
* **Submit** (`Submit`, text, optional): Click this button to send your completed form to us.
