# Native Plants Sale - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Native Plants Sale form is designed to record and manage information about native plant sales, including customer details, plant selection, and order status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "User Name" field, which is required for tracking and identification purposes.
2. Enter the customer's email address in the "Email Address" field to ensure accurate contact information.
3. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Phone" field for easy communication.
4. Select the native plant(s) desired by the customer in the "Native Plant Selection" field.
5. Enter the quantity of the selected plant(s) in the "Quantity" field.
6. Enter the price of the sale in the "Price" field.
7. Choose the payment method in the "Payment Method" field, which can be cash, credit card, or cheque.
8. Enter the date of the sale in the "Date" field.
9. Enter the time of the sale in the "Time" field.
10. If necessary, add any additional notes in the "Note" field.
11. Enter the order ID in the "Order ID" field for tracking purposes.
12. Enter the order date in the "Order Date" field.
13. Enter the total order amount in the "Order Total" field.
14. Choose the payment status in the "Payment Status" field, which can be paid or unpaid.
15. Choose the order status in the "Order Status" field, which can be active or inactive.
16. Enter the quantity of native plant(s) selected in the "Native Plant Quantity" field.
17. Enter any additional order comments in the "Order Comments" field.
18. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
19. Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field.
20. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
21. Enter the customer's address in the "Customer Address" field.
22. Enter the customer's province in the "Customer Province" field.
23. Enter the customer's city in the "Customer City" field.
24. Enter the customer's postal code in the "Customer Postal Code" field.
