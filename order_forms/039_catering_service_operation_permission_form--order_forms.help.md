# Catering Service Operation Permission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect essential information about your catering business's operations, which will be reviewed and approved by the relevant authorities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out your business name, contact person, and email address accurately.
2. Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
3. Enter your business address for verification.
4. Specify the date of your request.
5. Choose the type of operation you are undertaking.
6. Enter the location where you will operate.
7. Indicate the geographic area you serve.
8. Provide your business license number.
9. Enter your food service permit number.
10. Specify your health department permit number.
11. Choose the level of liability insurance you possess.
12. Indicate if you have food safety certification.
13. Provide a list of major equipment you have.
14. Enter the number of staff.
15. Choose the services you will offer.
16. Specify the types of cuisine you will serve.
17. Enter your operating hours, if applicable.
18. Describe your parking arrangements for staff and deliveries.
19. Outline your waste disposal plan.
20. Describe your noise control measures.
21. Indicate if you have notified your neighbors.
22. Agree to comply with regulations.
23. Date and sign the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your catering business.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name and title of the person responsible for this operation.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, required): Enter your business address for verification.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your request.
* **Operation Type** (`operation_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of operation you are undertaking (New Catering Business, Expansion of Services, Location Change, Menu Expansion, Equipment Addition, or Other).
* **Operation Location** (`operation_location`, text, required): Enter the location where you will operate.
* **Service Area** (`service_area`, text, required): Enter the geographic area you serve.
* **Business License Number** (`business_license`, text, required): Enter your business license number.
* **Food Service Permit** (`food_service_permit`, text, required): Enter your food service permit number.
* **Health Permit** (`health_permit`, text, required): Enter your health department permit number.
* **Liability Insurance** (`liability_insurance`, select_one, required): Choose the level of liability insurance you possess (Up to $1 Million, $1 to $2 Million, Over $2 Million, Not Yet Obtained).
* **Food Safety Certification** (`food_safety_certification`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have food safety certification (Yes - All Staff, Yes - Manager Only, False).
* **Equipment List** (`equipment_list`, text, optional): Provide a list of major equipment you have.
* **Number of Staff** (`staff_count`, number, optional): Enter the total number of employees.
* **Service Types** (`service_types`, select_multiple, required): Choose the services you will offer (Full Service Catering, Drop-off Catering, Buffet Service, Plated Service, Cocktail Reception, Food Truck).
* **Cuisine Types** (`cuisine_types`, select_multiple, required): Choose the types of cuisine you will serve (American, Italian, Mexican, Asian, Mediterranean, French, Fusion).
* **Operating Hours** (`operating_hours`, text, optional): Enter your business hours, if applicable.
* **Parking Arrangements** (`parking_arrangements`, text, optional): Describe your parking arrangements for staff and deliveries.
* **Waste Disposal Plan** (`waste_disposal_plan`, text, optional): Describe your waste disposal plan.
* **Noise Control Measures** (`noise_control_measures`, text, optional): Describe your noise control measures.
* **Neighbors Notified** (`neighbor_notification`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you have notified your neighbors (True, False, Not Applicable).
* **Compliance Agreement** (`compliance_agreement`, select_one, required): Agree to comply with regulations (Yes - I Agree, False).
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Date and sign the form.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any other information.
