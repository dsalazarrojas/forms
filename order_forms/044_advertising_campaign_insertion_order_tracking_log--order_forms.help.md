# Advertising Campaign Insertion Order Tracking Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Advertising Campaign Insertion Order Tracking Log form is used to track and manage advertising campaigns insertion orders. It helps ensure that all stakeholders involved in the campaign are aware of the key details and status of the insertion order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the Insertion Order Number (IO Number) in the field "IO Number".
2.  Enter the name of the campaign in the field "Campaign Name".
3.  Enter the name of the advertiser in the field "Advertiser Name".
4.  Enter the name of the agency (if applicable) in the field "Agency Name".
5.  Enter the date the insertion order was created in the field "IO Date".
6.  Enter the start date of the campaign in the field "Campaign Start Date".
7.  Enter the end date of the campaign in the field "Campaign End Date".
8.  Enter the location where the advertisement will be placed in the field "Platform Publication Details".
9.  Enter the format of the advertisement in the field "Ad Format Specifications".
10. Enter the total budget for the insertion order in the field "Total Budget".
11. Enter the rate per unit (impression, click, or other) in the field "Rate Per Unit".
12. Enter the expected impressions or clicks in the field "Projected Impressions Clicks".
13. Enter the date the creative materials are due in the field "Creative Due Date".
14. Select the current status of the insertion order from the options in the field "IO Status".
15. Enter any additional notes or comments in the field "Notes Comments".
16. Enter the name of the person who approved the insertion order in the field "IO Approved By".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **IO Number** (`io_number`, `text`, required): Enter the unique number of the insertion order.
* **Campaign Name** (`campaign_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the advertising campaign.
* **Advertiser Name** (`advertiser_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the advertiser.
* **Agency Name** (`agency_name`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the advertising agency.
* **IO Date** (`io_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the insertion order was created.
* **Campaign Start Date** (`campaign_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of the campaign.
* **Campaign End Date** (`campaign_end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of the campaign.
* **Platform Publication Details** (`platform_publication_details`, `text`, required): Enter the location where the advertisement will be placed.
* **Ad Format Specifications** (`ad_format_specifications`, `text`, required): Enter the format of the advertisement.
* **Total Budget** (`total_budget`, `number`, required): Enter the total budget for the insertion order.
* **Rate Per Unit** (`rate_per_unit`, `number`, required): Enter the rate per unit (impression, click, or other).
* **Projected Impressions Clicks** (`projected_impressions_clicks`, `number`, required): Enter the expected impressions or clicks.
* **Creative Due Date** (`creative_due_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the creative materials are due.
* **IO Status** (`io_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the current status of the insertion order from the options.
* **Notes Comments** (`notes_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the IO.
* **IO Approved By** (`io_approved_by`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person who approved the IO.
