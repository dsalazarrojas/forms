# Cosmetic Product Reorder Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for reorder operations of cosmetic products. It helps gather necessary information for inventory management and shipping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your company's name in the **Company Name** field.
2. Enter your **User Id** (a unique identifier for your account).
3. Enter the **Product Id** of the product you want to reorder.
4. Select the desired **Quantity** to be reordered.
5. Enter the date and time for the **Reorder Date** and **Reorder Time**.
6. Optionally, provide a brief **Reason For Reorder**.
7. Choose the **Reorder Frequency** (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
8. Select the desired **Shipping Method** (Ship Now or Ship Later).
9. Add any **Additional Comments** about the reorder.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (text, required): Enter your company's name to identify your account.
* **User Id** (number, required): Enter your unique account identifier.
* **Product Id** (number, required): Enter the product ID of the item you want to reorder.
* **Quantity** (number, required): Select the quantity of the product to be reordered.
* **Reorder Date** (date, required): Enter the date for the reorder operation.
* **Reorder Time** (time, required): Enter the time for the reorder operation.
* **Reason For Reorder** (text, optional): Provide a brief reason for the reorder (e.g., low stock, customer demand).
* **Reorder Frequency** (select multiple, required): Select the frequency of the reorder operation (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Shipping Method** (select one, required): Choose the shipping method for the reordered product (Ship Now or Ship Later).
* **Additional Comments** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments or instructions for the reorder operation.
