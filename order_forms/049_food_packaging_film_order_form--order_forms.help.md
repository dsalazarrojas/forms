# Food Packaging Film Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from customers for food packaging film orders. It helps businesses or organizations to gather order details, customer information, and shipping addresses, as well as product specifications and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Customer Information** (Page 1): Enter your name and email address to identify yourself as the customer.
2. **Order Details** (Page 2): Describe the type and quantity of food packaging film you are ordering.
3. **Shipping Address** (Page 3): Enter your shipping address where you would like to receive your order.
4. **Product Info** (Page 4): Provide any additional product information you want to include with your order.
5. **Product Specifications** (Page 5): Describe any specific requirements or specifications you have for your food packaging film order.
6. **Payment Gateway** (Page 6): Choose your preferred payment method, such as credit card or bank transfer.
7. **Review Order** (Page 7): Double-check your order details for accuracy.
8. **Payment Gateway 2** (Page 8): Confirm your payment information.
