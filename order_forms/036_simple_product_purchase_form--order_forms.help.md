# Simple Product Purchase Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to place an order for a simple product. Please fill out the form with your information and details of your purchase.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the required information. The form is split into several sections, each with its own explanation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
*   **First Name**: Enter your first name.

### Last Name
*   **Last Name**: Enter your last name.

### Email
*   **Email**: Enter your email address.

### Address
*   **Address**: Enter your delivery address.

### Product ID
*   **Product ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the product you are purchasing.

### Quantity
*   **Quantity**: Enter the number of products you are purchasing.

### Price
*   **Price**: Enter the price of the product.

### Payment Method
*   **Payment Method**: Select how you will pay for the product. Options include Credit Card, PayPal, and Bank Transfer.

### Payment Terms
*   **Payment Terms**: Select the terms of payment, such as one-time or recurring payments.

### Delivery Date
*   **Delivery Date**: Enter the date you would like to receive the product.

### Delivery Time
*   **Delivery Time**: Enter the time you would like to receive the product.

### Notes
*   **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your order.

### Confirm
*   **Confirm**: Confirm that the information you have entered is correct.

## Tips
*   Please make sure to enter accurate information, as it will be used to process your order.
*   If you have any issues or questions, please contact us for assistance.
