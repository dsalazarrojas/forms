## Purpose

1. Fill out the **User Information** section with your name and any other relevant details.
2. In the **Order Details** section, provide a brief description of your order.
3. In the **Payment Info** section, choose your preferred **Payment Method** and provide any **Payment Method Details**.

10. Confirm your order by checking the **Confirm** box and providing your name in the **Confirm Name** field.
