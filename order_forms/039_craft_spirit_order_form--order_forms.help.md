# Craft Spirit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for creating custom craft spirit orders from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Carefully review each field and its label to ensure accuracy.
* Fill out all optional fields that apply to your order.
* Double-check the form for any errors or missing information before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (customer_info, text, not required): This section is used to collect basic customer contact information.
* **Order Details** (order_details, text, not required): Here, you can provide more details about the order you'd like to place.
* **Payment Info** (payment_info, text, not required): This field is used to securely process your payment.
* **Event Details** (event_details, text, not required): This section is used to understand any special event-related requirements for your order.
* **Shipping Information** (shipping_info, text, not required): This field helps us ensure accurate delivery of your order.

## Tips
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for assistance.
- Be accurate with all the information to ensure smooth order processing.
