# Café Loyalty Program Materials Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to place an order for loyalty program materials (such as loyalty cards, posters, flyers, or stickers) for a specific organization or cafe branch. Please provide accurate information to ensure proper materials distribution and communication with you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **Requester Name**. This is the name of the person or department requesting the materials.
2. Enter your **Organization name**. This is the name of the cafe branch or organization placing the order.
3. Select your **Material type**: Choose the type of material you need, such as loyalty cards, posters, flyers, or stickers.
4. Enter the **Quantity required**: Provide the exact amount of materials needed.
5. Enter your **Delivery address**: Provide the address where the materials should be sent.
6. If applicable, select the **Required by date**: Enter the date by which the materials need to be delivered.
7. Check if **artwork is attached**: If you need to include artwork or additional files with your order, select "True" here.
8. Enter your **Contact phone**: Provide a phone number for communication and delivery queries.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester name** (id: 1, required): Enter the name of the person or department requesting the materials.
* **Organization name** (id: 2, required): Enter the name of the cafe branch or corporate organization placing the order.
* **Material type** (id: 3, required): Choose from the options: Loyalty cards, Posters, Flyers, Stickers.
* **Quantity required** (id: 4, required): Enter a numeric value indicating the amount of materials needed.
* **Delivery address** (id: 5, required): Enter the address where the materials should be sent.
* **Required by date** (id: 6, optional): Enter the date by which the materials need to be delivered.
* **Is artwork attached** (id: 7, optional): Select "True" if artwork or additional files are needed with the order.
* **Contact phone** (id: 8, required): Enter a phone number for communication and delivery queries.

## Tips
- Double-check that all required fields are filled correctly to avoid delays.
- If artwork is attached, ensure to provide accurate file references.
- If any issues arise during the ordering process, contact the form owner or support team for assistance.
