# Collision Repair Notification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to notify the repair team of a new collision repair job, ensuring that all necessary information is captured and communicated efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Repair Order Number**, a unique identifier for the repair job.
2. Update the **Repair Status** to reflect the current status of the repair (e.g., "Received and Inspected", "Parts on Order", etc.).
3. Enter the **Notification Date** and **Notification Time** for when the notification is being sent.
4. Provide the **Customer First Name**, **Customer Last Name**, **Customer Email Address**, and **Customer Phone Number** to ensure effective communication.
5. Enter the **Vehicle Year**, **Vehicle Make**, **Vehicle Model**, and **Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)** to accurately identify the vehicle.
6. Describe the **Date of Accident or Damage**, the **Damage Description**, and indicate the **Damage Severity** (e.g., Minor, Moderate, Severe, or Total Loss).
7. Estimate the **Repair Completion Date** and **Estimated Repair Cost**.
8. List any **Parts on Order** and **Insurance Claim Number** (if applicable).
9. Select your **Preferred Contact Method** (e.g., phone call, email, text message).
10. Add any **Notes or Messages for the Customer**.
11. Confirm **Authorization Approval for Repairs** (e.g., Approved, Pending Approval, Changes Requested, Declined).
12. Enter the **Assigned Technician Name** (if assigned).
13. Indicate if a **Follow-Up Communication is Required** (e.g., Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Repair Order Number**: A unique identifier for this repair job. This is a crucial field that helps us track and manage the repair efficiently.
* **Repair Status Update**: The current status of the repair, indicating where the repair is in the process. This helps us prioritize and manage our workload effectively.
* **Notification Date**: The date of this status update.
* **Notification Time**: The time of this status update.
* **Customer First Name**: The first name of the customer.
* **Customer Last Name**: The last name of the customer.
* **Customer Email Address**: The email address of the customer for effective communication.
* **Customer Phone Number**: The phone number of the customer for follow-up communication.
* **Vehicle Year**: The year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make**: The make of the vehicle (e.g., Ford, Toyota, Honda).
* **Vehicle Model**: The model of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)**: A unique identifier for the vehicle.
* **License Plate Number**: The license plate number of the vehicle.
* **Date of Accident or Damage**: The date of the accident or damage.
* **Damage Description**: A description of the damage.
* **Damage Severity**: The extent of the damage (e.g., Minor, Moderate, Severe, or Total Loss).
* **Estimated Repair Cost**: An estimate of the repair cost.
* **Parts on Order**: A list of any parts being ordered for the repair.
* **Insurance Claim Number**: If applicable, the insurance claim number.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: The preferred method of contact (e.g., phone call, email, text message).
* **Notes or Messages for Customer**: Additional information or updates for the customer.
* **Authorization Approval for Repairs**: Confirmation of customer approval for the repair plan.
* **Assigned Technician Name**: The name of the technician assigned to the repair.
* **Follow-Up Communication Required**: Indication of whether the customer needs to provide additional information for follow-up.
