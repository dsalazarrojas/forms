# cruise_ticket_refund_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help you complete the cruise ticket refund form. The form is used to process refunds for cruise tickets due to various reasons such as cancellation, rescheduling, or other reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the passenger information fields:
	* Passenger Name
	* Passenger Email
	* Passenger Phone
	* Passenger Address
	* Passenger City
	* Passenger State
	* Passenger Zip
2. Select the ticket details:
	* Refund Reason (choose one: Cancellation, Rescheduling, or Other)
	* Refund Amount
	* Refund Currency (choose one: USD, EUR, or CAD)
3. Select the payment method:
	* Payment Method (choose one: Bank Transfer, Credit Card, or PayPal)
4. Choose the order status:
	* Order Status (choose one: Pending, Approved, or Declined)
5. Fill in the order ID:
	* Order ID (choose one: 123456, 789012, or 345678)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Passenger Name** (`passenger_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the passenger.
* **Passenger Email** (`passenger_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the passenger.
* **Passenger Phone** (`passenger_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the passenger.
* **Passenger Address** (`passenger_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the passenger.
* **Passenger City** (`passenger_city`, text, optional): Enter the city of the passenger's address.
* **Passenger State** (`passenger_state`, text, optional): Enter the state of the passenger's address.
* **Passenger Zip** (`passenger_zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of the passenger's address.
* **Refund Reason** (`refund_reason`, select one, optional): Choose the reason for the refund (Cancellation, Rescheduling, or Other).
* **Refund Amount** (`refund_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount to be refunded.
* **Refund Currency** (`refund_currency`, select one, optional): Choose the currency of the refund (USD, EUR, or CAD).
* **Prophets Note** (`prophets_note`, note, optional): This is a note field for any additional information.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city of your address.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state of your address.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of your address.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time.
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, select one, optional): Choose the time zone (EST, EDT, CST, MST, or PST).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select one, optional): Choose the payment method (Bank Transfer, Credit Card, or PayPal).
* **Order ID** (`order_id`, select one, optional): Choose the order ID (123456, 789012, or 345678).
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select one, optional): Choose the order status (Pending, Approved, or Declined).

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or issues, contact us for assistance.
