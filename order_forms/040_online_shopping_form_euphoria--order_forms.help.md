# Online Shopping Form Euphoria - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Shopping Form Euphoria is a tool to help customers complete a purchase online. It collects information about the customer, product, and payment options for the transaction.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

*   Start by reviewing the form to ensure you are filling out the correct fields.
*   Enter your contact information, which will be used to communicate with you about your order.
*   Provide your shipping address, where you want your order to be delivered.
*   Specify the product you want to purchase, including its details.
*   Choose your preferred delivery option and payment method.
*   Select the size of the product, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Information (Contact Information, Text, Optional)**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number to enable us to contact you about your order.
*   **Shipping Address (Shipping Address, Text, Optional)**: Provide your address where you want your order to be delivered.
*   **Product Details (Product Details, Text, Optional)**: Describe the product you want to purchase, including any specific features or requirements.
*   **Delivery Option (Delivery Option, Select Multiple, Optional)**: Choose one or more delivery options that fit your needs, such as in-store pickup, home delivery, or delivery to a specific location.
*   **Payment Option (Payment Option, Select Multiple, Optional)**: Choose the payment method you prefer, such as credit card, bank transfer, or other available options.
*   **Size (Size, Select One, Optional)**: Select the size of the product you wish to purchase, if applicable.
