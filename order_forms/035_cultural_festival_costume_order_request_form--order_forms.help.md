# Cultural Festival Costume Order Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Cultural Festival Costume Order Request Form" is designed to capture information from vendors or individuals requesting costumes for an event. This form helps event organizers gather details about the costume order, including sizing, delivery, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Festival Costume Request" field with a brief description of the costume order.
2. Provide sizing details for the costume in the "Sizing Details" field.
3. Select the delivery method for the costume (Delivery, Pickup, or Other) and enter any additional delivery preferences in the "Delivery Preferences" field.
4. Enter any order notes or special requests in the "Order Notes" field.
5. Confirm your order status by selecting "Confirmed", "Declined", or "Pending" in the "Vendor Confirmation" field.
6. Confirm the order status by selecting "Confirmed", "Declined", or "Pending" in the "Order Confirmation" field.
7. Fill in your contact details, including name and phone number, in the "Contact Details" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Contact Email" field.
9. Confirm your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Festival Costume Request (text)**: A brief description of the costume order.
* **Sizing Details (text)**: Enter the sizing details for the costume, including measurements or specific needs.
* **Delivery or Pickup (select_multiple)**: Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Delivery: The costume will be delivered to your location.
	+ Pickup: The costume will be picked up from our location.
	+ Other: Specify an alternative delivery method.
* **Delivery Preferences (text)**: Enter any additional delivery preferences, such as time windows or specific instructions.
* **Order Notes (text)**: Enter any additional notes or special requests for the order.
* **Vendor Confirmation (select_multiple)**: Confirm your order status as:
	+ Confirmed: The order is confirmed and ready for processing.
	+ Declined: The order is declined or cancelled.
	+ Pending: The order is pending confirmation.
* **Order Confirmation (select_multiple)**: Confirm your order status as:
	+ Confirmed: The order is confirmed and ready for processing.
	+ Declined: The order is declined or cancelled.
	+ Pending: The order is pending confirmation.
* **Contact Details (text)**: Enter your contact name and phone number.
* **Contact Email (email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number.
