# Reputation Management Client Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reputation Management Client Order Form is a tool used to gather information for processing and fulfilling orders for reputation management services. This form is designed to capture client contact details and order specifics to ensure accurate processing and timely delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Fill in your Last Name.
3. Enter your valid Email address.
4. Provide your Phone number (optional).
5. Select the desired Order Type (Yes/No).
6. Select the Product(s) you require (Yes/No).
7. Leave a Comment (optional).
8. Choose a specific Date for the order (optional).
9. Choose a Time for the order (optional).
10. Select the Assigned Tool (optional).
11. Enter the Form ID (if provided).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
• **First Name** (`first_name`, text): Enter your first name for identification purposes.

### Last Name
• **Last Name** (`last_name`, text): Enter your last name for identification purposes.

### Email
• **Email** (`email`, email): Enter a valid email address to enable communication and order updates.

### Phone
• **Phone** (`phone`, text): If you have a preferred contact method, provide your phone number (optional).

### Order Type
• **Order Type** (`order_type`, select_one): Choose 'Yes' if you require the reputation management service, 'No' otherwise.

### Product
• **Product** (`product`, select_multiple): Select 'Yes' for each product you require for your reputation management service.

### Comment
• **Comment** (`comment`, note): Leave any additional information or special requests for our team (optional).

### Date
• **Date** (`date`, date): Select a specific Date for the order if desired (optional).

### Time
• **Time** (`time`, time): Choose a Time for the order if required (optional).

### Assigned Tool
• **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text): If assigned, indicate the tool you will be using for the order (optional).

### Form ID
• **Form ID** (`form_id`, number): Enter the Form ID if provided (optional).

### Tips
* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately to avoid delays in processing your order.
* Double-check your contact information for successful communication.
* If unsure about any field, contact our support team for guidance.
