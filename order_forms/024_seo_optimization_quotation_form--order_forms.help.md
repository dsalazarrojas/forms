# SEO Optimization Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us better understand your SEO needs and provide a quotation for our services. Please complete the form accurately to help us tailor our proposal to your specific requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your business name in the field labeled "business_name".
2. Select the service type that best describes the SEO services you are seeking.
3. Choose the current location of your business from the list of options.
4. Enter your contact email address in the field labeled "contact_email".
5. Enter your phone number in the field labeled "contact_phone", following the format +1 (123) 456-7890.
6. Fill in your service address in the field labeled "service_address", including the format 123 Main St, Anytown USA 12345.
7. Enter the city where your service is located in the field labeled "service_city".
8. Fill in the province where your service is located in the field labeled "service_province".
9. Enter your service zip code in the field labeled "service_zip", as a numeric value.
10. Fill in your billing address in the field labeled "billing_address".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **business_name** (text, required): Enter the name of your business as it appears on your website and marketing materials.
* **service_type** (select_one, required): Select the type of SEO service you are seeking, such as SEO Audit, Link Building, Content Optimization, etc.
* **current_location** (select_multiple, required): Choose the location where your business is currently operating, such as US, Canada, Mexico, etc.
* **contact_email** (email, required): Enter your contact email address in the format [name@example.com](mailto:name@example.com).
* **contact_phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number in the format +1 (123) 456-7890.
* **service_address** (text, required): Enter your service address in the format 123 Main St, Anytown USA 12345.
* **service_city** (text, required): Enter the city where your service is located.
* **service_province** (text, required): Enter the province where your service is located.
* **service_zip** (number, required): Enter your service zip code as a numeric value.
* **billing_address** (text, optional): Fill in your billing address if you wish to receive invoices and other documents from us at this address.
