# Regulatory Violation Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report and pay for regulatory violations. It is a crucial step in the process of rectifying any issues related to regulatory non-compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the date of the violation.
2. Enter the amount you are paying for the violation.
3. Indicate if you are paying for the violation (Yes/No).
4. Provide any additional notes or comments related to the violation.
5. Enter your email address.
6. Enter your phone number.
7. Enter your address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Violation Date
**violation_date** (`date`, required: false) - The date on which the regulatory violation occurred. Enter the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

### 2. Amount
**amount** (`number`, required: false) - The amount you are paying for the regulatory violation.

### 3. Payment Method
**payment_method** (`select_multiple`, required: false) 
* Yes
* No - Check one or both of the above options to indicate if you are paying for the violation.

### 4. Notes
**notes** (`note`, required: false) - Enter any additional comments or details related to the violation.

### 5. Email
**email** (`email`, required: false) - Enter your email address for contact purposes.

### 6. Phone
**phone** (`text`, required: false) - Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### 7. Address
**address** (`text`, required: false) - Enter your address for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to avoid errors.
* Ensure you check one or both options in the payment method field as necessary.
* Enter your contact information correctly to ensure we can reach you for follow-up.
