# Face Mask Customization Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Face Mask Customization Order Form is designed to collect information for creating personalized face masks. This form will help you create a customized face mask that suits your preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the base form, which is the basic design or type of face mask you want to create.
2. Choose your preferred color for the face mask from the options provided.
3. Select the size of the face mask you want, such as 3x5 or 4x6.
4. Choose the design for your face mask from the available options.
5. Fill in your name and email address.
6. Confirm your email address to ensure correct submission.
7. Add any additional notes about your order in the "Order Note" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Base Form (<code>base_form</code>):** Enter the basic design or type of face mask you want to create.
* **Color Select (<code>color_select</code>):** Choose your preferred color for the face mask from the options: red, blue, or green.
* **Size Select (<code>size_select</code>):** Select the size of the face mask you want, such as 3x5 or 4x6.
* **Design Select (<code>design_select</code>):** Choose the design for your face mask from the available options.
* **Order Form (<code>order_form</code>):** Fill in any additional information about your order.
* **Name (<code>name</code>):** Enter your name.
* **Email (<code>email</code>):** Enter your email address.
* **Confirm Email (<code>confirm_email</code>):** Confirm your email address to ensure correct submission.
* **Order Note (<code>order_note</code>):** Add any additional notes about your order.
