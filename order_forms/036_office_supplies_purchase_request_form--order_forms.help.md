# office-supplies-purchase-request-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The office-supplies-purchase-request-form is a form designed to collect information from employees to request office supplies. It is used by the procurement team to fulfill these requests and ensure timely delivery of the supplies ordered.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you have a legitimate reason for ordering office supplies.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
3. Be specific about the office supplies you need.
4. Enter your employee name and email address.
5. Enter your department name, if applicable.
6. Confirm the date and time you need the supplies ordered.
7. Add any additional notes about the order.
8. Review and submit the form for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: <employee_name> (`Employee Name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as an employee of the office.
* **Employee Email**: <employee_email> (`Employee Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address in the format of: `example@example.com`.
* **Employee Department**: <employee_department> (`Employee Department`, `text`, optional): Enter your department name if applicable.
* **Office Supplies Ordered**: <office_supplies_ordered> (`Office Supplies Ordered`, `text`, required): Be specific about the type and quantity of office supplies you need.
* **Order Date**: <order_date> (`Order Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you need the supplies ordered in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Order Time**: <order_time> (`Order Time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you need the supplies ordered in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Order Notes**: <order_notes> (`Order Notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or instructions about the order, if necessary.

## Tips
* Be accurate when filling out the form.
* Use the correct format for dates and times.
* Ensure that you have permission to order the supplies.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the procurement team for assistance.
* Do not submit duplicate orders for the same type of supply.
