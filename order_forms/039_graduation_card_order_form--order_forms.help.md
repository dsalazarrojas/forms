# Graduation Card Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Graduation Card Order Form is designed to capture relevant details from students and their parents/guardians to ensure a smooth graduation ceremony. This form should be filled out by students to provide accurate information for their graduation card.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter your name on the first field, "Order Form".
2. Provide the requested graduation details on the second field, "Graduation Details".
3. Fill out any additional order information in the third field, "Order Information".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Order Form** (`order_form`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official ID or driver's license.
### **Graduation Details** (`graduation_details`, text, optional): Please provide any additional details regarding your graduation, such as the date of the ceremony or your expected graduation date.
### **Order Information** (`order_info`, text, optional): If you have any additional requests or special instructions for your graduation order, please write them here.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields (Order Form) to ensure accurate and timely processing of your graduation card.
- Double-check your graduation details for accuracy before submitting.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your school administration or contact us at form@gic.mx.
