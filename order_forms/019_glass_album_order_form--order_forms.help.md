# glass_album_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers to place an order for a glass album.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields as prompted. You can select options from the dropdown menus where applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details** (`customer_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as the customer who is placing this order.
* **Album Type** (`album_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of album you would like to order, such as Glass or Leather.
* **Album Size** (`album_size`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the size of the album you would like to order, such as 8x10 or 10x10.
* **Album Pages** (`album_pages`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of pages you would like in your album.
* **Album Orientation** (`album_orientation`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the orientation of the album, such as Portrait or Landscape.
* **Album Coverage** (`album_coverage`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the coverage of the album, such as the size of the images or text.
* **Album Cover Type** (`album_cover_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of cover you would like for your album, such as Matte or Glossy.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as the customer who is placing this order.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address as the customer who is placing this order.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number as the customer who is placing this order.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time you would like to receive your order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the payment method you would like to use, such as PayPal or Bank Transfer.
* **Order Quantity** (`order_quantity`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of albums you would like to order.
