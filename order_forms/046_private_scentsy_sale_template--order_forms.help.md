# Private Scentsy Sale Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Private Scentsy Sale Template is designed to facilitate private sales between consultants and customers. It ensures that all necessary information is collected and recorded for tracking and reference purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in the form with the customer's details:
	* **Consultant name**: Enter your name as the consultant conducting the sale.
	* **Private Sale Link**: Enter the private sale link, if applicable.
	* **Customer Message**: Enter any notes or comments regarding the sale, such as customer preferences or concerns.
	* **Status of Sale**: Indicate the status of the sale, whether it is Active or Inactive.
	* **Sale Price**: Enter the agreed-upon sale price for the private sale.
	* **Sale Date**: Enter the date of the sale.
	* **Sale Time**: Enter the time of the sale.
	* **Sale Timezone**: Enter the time zone of the sale.
	* **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email address.
	* **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
	* **Consultant Name**: Enter your name as the consultant conducting the sale.
	* **Consultant Email**: Enter your email address as the consultant.
	* **Consultant Phone**: Enter your phone number as the consultant.
2. Check the box for **Sale Terms** that apply to the sale:
	* Choose any additional sale terms that are relevant to the sale, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.
3. If additional sale terms need to be documented, fill in the text boxes below:
	* **Sale Terms 2** to **Sale Terms 10**: Enter any other sale terms that are not listed in the previous step.
