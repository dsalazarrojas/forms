# Sports Tournament Volunteer Badge Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Tournament Volunteer Badge Order Form is designed to collect information from volunteers who need badges for a sports tournament. This form ensures that volunteers receive the correct badges for the event they are volunteering for.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the Event Name to match the event you are volunteering for.
2. Fill in your details:
	* Name
	* Email
	* Phone
3. Specify your delivery options:
	* If you need to receive a badge via delivery, select 'Yes' for Delivery Option.
	* If you need to pick up your badge, select 'Yes' for Pickup Option.
4. Enter your payment method:
	* If you are paying for your badge, select 'Yes' for Payment Method.
	* Enter your payment details in the Payment Details field.
5. Confirm your badge preferences:
	* Choose the badge type and quantity you need.
	* Select your badge color preference.
6. Enter any additional comments or special requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you are volunteering for. This is to ensure that you receive the correct badge for the event.
* **Name**: Enter your name as it should be displayed on your badge.
* **Email**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address**: (Optional) Enter your address for delivery purposes.
* **Delivery Option**: If you need to receive your badge via delivery, select 'Yes'. If you are picking up your badge, select 'No'. This will determine how your badge is delivered.
* **Name on Delivery**: If you selected 'Yes' for Delivery Option, enter your name as it should be displayed on the delivery label.
* **Delivery Details**: If you selected 'Yes' for Delivery Option, enter any delivery details, such as special instructions or delivery location.
* **Pickup Option**: If you are picking up your badge, select 'Yes'. If not, select 'No'. This will determine how your badge is distributed.
* **Name on Pickup**: If you selected 'Yes' for Pickup Option, enter your name as it should be displayed on the pickup label.
* **Pickup Details**: If you selected 'Yes' for Pickup Option, enter any pickup details, such as pickup location or time.
* **Payment Method**: If you are paying for your badge, select 'Yes'. If not, select 'No'. This will determine how your badge is paid for.
* **Payment Details**: If you selected 'Yes' for Payment Method, enter your payment details.
* **Payment Card Details**: (Optional) Enter any additional payment card details.
* **Order Total**: Enter the total number of badges you need.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date you need your badges by.
* **Order Time**: Enter the time you need your badges by.
* **Event ID**: (Optional) Enter the event ID for this order.
* **Volunteer ID**: (Optional) Enter the volunteer ID for this order.
* **Badge Type**: Choose the type of badge you need.
* **Badge Quantity**: Enter the number of badges you need.
* **Badge Color**: Choose the color of your badge.
* **Volunteer Name**: (Optional) Enter the name of the volunteer managing this order.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event this order is for.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or special requests.
