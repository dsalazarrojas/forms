# PayPal Clothing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to enter their order details when placing a PayPal clothing order.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name.
2. Enter your last name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Enter your shipping address.
6. Enter your shipping city.
7. Select your shipping state from the list of options.
8. Enter your shipping zip code.
9. Enter your order total.
10. Select the items you are ordering from the list of options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first name (text)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **last name (text)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **email (email)**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **phone (text)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **shipping address (text)**: Enter the address where your order will be shipped.
* **shipping city (text)**: Enter the city where your order will be shipped.
* **shipping state (select one)**: Select the state from the list of options where your order will be shipped.
* **shipping zip (number)**: Enter the zip code where your order will be shipped.
* **order total (number)**: Enter the total cost of your order.
* **items ordered (select multiple)**: Select the items you are ordering from the list of options.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure correct order fulfillment.
* Double-check your selections and entries before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact our customer support team.
