# industrial_heat_shield_panel_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect order details from customers, including order information, customer details, and delivery details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the **order_details** field on the first page with the relevant information about the order.
2. On the second page, provide your **customer_details** in the corresponding field.
3. Finally, enter your **delivery_details** on the third page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **order_details** (order_details, text, optional): Enter the details of the order, including any relevant information about the product, quantity, and other relevant details.
* **customer_details** (customer_details, text, optional): Enter your customer details, such as name, address, and contact information.
* **delivery_details** (delivery_details, text, optional): Enter the delivery details, including the shipping address, date, and any other relevant information.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (although none are marked as required in this form, it is assumed that customers will provide some identifying information).
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact form@gic.mx.
