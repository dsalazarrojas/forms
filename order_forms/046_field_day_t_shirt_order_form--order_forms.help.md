The Field Day T Shirt Order Form is a tool used to collect order information from customers. This form is used to gather details about the order, including the design, type, color, and size of the t-shirt, as well as the customer's contact information and order comments.

1. To complete this form, please fill out each field with the required information.
2. The "Order Details" field should include the details about the order, such as the type of design, color, and size of the t-shirt.

4. Choose the desired "Shirt Type" and "Shirt Color" from the drop-down menus.
5. If you have any additional comments about your order, you can enter them in the "Order Comments" field.
6. Enter your contact email, phone number, shipping information, name, and school name (if applicable) in the corresponding fields.
7. Finally, click the submit button to complete your order.
