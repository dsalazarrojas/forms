# Event Equipment Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Equipment Delivery Form is used to collect information from customers or internal stakeholders about equipment delivery preferences and details. This form helps us better understand their needs and ensures timely and accurate delivery of equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the desired response for each question by clicking on the radio buttons or selecting from the dropdown menus.
2. For the "select_equipment" field, choose the specific equipment you are interested in.
3. In the "delivery_details" field, provide any additional information about the delivery, such as dates, times, or specific instructions.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, type them in the "additional_comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you need the equipment delivered:**
	+ "Yes" means you would like to receive the equipment.
	+ "No" means you do not require the equipment.
* **Choose the specific equipment you would like to receive:**
	+ Select one of the available options from the dropdown menu.
* **Provide any additional delivery details:**
	+ Type any relevant information about the delivery, such as dates, times, or specific instructions.
* **Additional Comments:**
	+ Share any additional feedback or questions you have about the delivery.

## Tips

* Make sure to select the correct equipment you would like to receive.
* Double-check your answers to ensure they are accurate and complete.
* If you are unsure about anything, feel free to ask and we will be happy to assist you.
