# office_furniture_delivery_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The office_furniture_delivery_form is a tool used by suppliers to provide delivery updates to customers. This form is essential for keeping track of delivery schedules, contact information, and status updates.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields with the relevant information.
2. Select the correct delivery status from the options provided.
3. Enter the delivery dates and times in the correct format.
4. Enter the contact information for the customer and supplier.
5. Select the delivery method for the order.
