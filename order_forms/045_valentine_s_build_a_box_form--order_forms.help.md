# valentines-build-a-box-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a custom order form for customers to place an order for Valentine's Day gifts. It is used to collect information about the recipient and the gift details, including the type of gift, quantity, and total price.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the form in the "title" field.
2. Select the category of the gift from the options provided.
3. Enter a brief description of the gift in the "description" field.
4. Enter the recipient's name in the "recipient-name" field.
5. Enter the recipient's email address in the "recipient-email" field.
6. Enter the recipient's phone number in the "recipient-phone" field.
7. Enter the recipient's address in the "recipient-address" field.
8. Enter a message for the gift card or box in the "message" field.
9. Select the type of payment method from the options provided.
10. Enter the quantity of the gift in the "quantity" field.
11. Enter the total price of the gift in the "total-price" field.
12. Enter your name in the "customer-name" field.
13. Enter your email address in the "customer-email" field.
14. Enter your phone number in the "customer-phone" field.
15. Enter your address in the "customer-address" field.
16. Check the "terms-and-conditions" field to agree to the terms and conditions.
17. Enter any additional message for the recipient in the "recipient-message" field.
18. Select whether or not the gift is a gift card or box from the options provided.
19. Enter the price of the gift card or box in the "gift-card-price" field.
20. Select whether or not to include a gift tin with the gift.
21. Enter a review of the gift in the "review" field.
