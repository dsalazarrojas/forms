# Online Cookie Order Form WorldPayUS Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Cookie Order Form WorldPayUS Payment Form is designed to gather information from customers for placing cookie orders. This guide will walk you through how to fill out the form and what each field is used for.

## How To Complete This Form
To place an order for cookies, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and confirm your name to ensure accuracy.
2. Enter a valid email address to receive order updates and confirm your email to confirm accuracy.
3. Select the type of cookie you'd like to order (e.g., Chocolate Chip, Oatmeal Raisin, Peanut Butter).
4. Choose a payment method (e.g., WorldPay US, Stripe).
5. Add any additional order notes if needed.
6. Enter the order date and time.
7. Confirm your phone number.
8. Enter the total order amount.
9. Confirm your email address again to ensure accuracy.
10. Select the order status (e.g., New, Processing, Shipped, Cancelled).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cookie Order Form (`cookie_order_form`, text, not required)**: This is the title of the form. Please ignore this field.
* **Name (`name`, text, not required)**: Please enter your full name to help us verify your identity.
* **Email (`email`, email, not required)**: Enter a valid email address to receive updates about your order. We'll send you a confirmation email to ensure accuracy.
* **Quantity (`quantity`, number, not required)**: Enter the number of cookies you'd like to order.
* **Cookie Type (`type`, select_one, not required)**: Select the type of cookie you'd like to order (e.g., Chocolate Chip, Oatmeal Raisin, Peanut Butter).
* **Payment Method (`payment_method`, select_one, not required)**: Choose how you'd like to pay for your cookies (e.g., WorldPay US, Stripe).
* **Order Notes (`order_notes`, note, not required)**: If you have any special requests or messages for the baker, enter them here.
* **Order Date (`order_date`, date, not required)**: Enter the date you'd like to receive your cookies.
* **Order Time (`order_time`, time, not required)**: Enter the time you'd like to receive your cookies.
* **Confirm Phone (`confirm_phone`, text, not required)**: Enter your phone number to confirm your order.
* **Order Total (`order_total`, number, not required)**: Enter the total amount of your order.
* **Confirm Email (`confirm_email`, email, not required)**: Confirm your email address to ensure accuracy.
* **Confirm Phone (`confirm_phone`, text, not required)**: Confirm your phone number to ensure accuracy.
* **Order Status (`order_status`, select_multiple, not required)**: Select the order status you'd like to associate with your order (e.g., New, Processing, Shipped, Cancelled).

## Tips

* Please ensure that your email address and phone number are accurate to ensure we can contact you with order updates.
* If you have any questions or concerns about your order, please contact our support team.
* The form is designed to guide you through the process, so take your time and fill it out carefully.
