# Brochure Order Form 2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place an order for brochures. It gathers necessary information for brochure production, delivery, and any additional products you may want to order.

## How To Complete This Form
This form is a step-by-step guide to help you provide all required information to successfully order brochures and any other products.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`Full Name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as you would like it to appear on the order.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `email`, required): Provide your email address for order confirmation and any updates.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for order-related communication.
* **Company Name** (`Company Name`, `text`, required): If applicable, enter your company name for order processing.
* **Street Address** (`Street Address`, `text`, required): Provide your street address for delivery purposes.
* **City** (`City`, `text`, required): Enter your city for shipping purposes.
* **State or Province** (`State or Province`, `text`, required): Select your state or province for shipping purposes.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`ZIP or Postal Code`, `text`, required): Enter your ZIP or postal code for shipping purposes.
* **Country** (`Country`, `text`, required): Select your country for shipping purposes.
* **Brochure Type** (`Brochure Type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of brochure you want to order (e.g., Product Catalog, Service Overview, etc.).
* **Quantity** (`Quantity`, `number`, required): Enter the number of brochures to order.
* **Paper Size** (`Paper Size`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred paper size (e.g., Letter, Legal, A4, A5, or Custom Size).
* **Paper Type** (`Paper Type`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred paper quality (e.g., Standard, Premium, Cardstock, etc.).
* **Color Option** (`Color Option`, `select_one`, required): Choose between Full Color, Black and White, or Spot Color.
* **Folding Option** (`Folding Option`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred folding style (e.g., No Folding, Half Fold, Tri-Fold, etc.).
* **Design Files Upload** (`Design Files Upload`, `text`, required): Upload your brochure design files if available.
* **Special Instructions** (`Special Instructions`, `text`, required): Enter any special requests or instructions for your order.
* **Delivery Method** (`Delivery Method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you want to receive your order (e.g., Standard Shipping, Express Shipping, Pickup, Digital Download).
* **Also Interested In** (`Also Interested In`, `select_multiple`, required): Select any additional products you may want to order (e.g., Flyers, Business Cards, Postcards, etc.).
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, `text`, required): Provide any other comments about your order.
