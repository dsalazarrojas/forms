# Athletic Tape And Wrap Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athletic Tape And Wrap Supply Order Form is used to collect information for a new order of athletic tape and wrap supplies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your organization's information.
2. Select the departments and personnel involved in the order.
3. Provide your contact information and order details.
4. Choose your preferred shipping method and address.
5. Confirm your payment method and any special instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter your organization's name, such as a school or clinic.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select the department placing the order, such as Athletics or Sports Medicine.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the primary contact for the order.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address for order communications.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number for order questions.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required): Enter the date the order is needed by.
* **Needed By Date** (`needed_by_date`, date, required): Enter the date the order is actually needed.
* **Athletic Tape Type** (`athletic_tape_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the types of tape needed, such as Standard White Tape or Water-Resistant Tape.
* **Tape Width Preference** (`tape_width_preference`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred tape width, if desired.
* **Tape Quantity** (`tape_quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of tapes needed.
* **Wrap Type** (`wrap_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the types of wraps needed, such as ACE Bandage or Elastic Wrap.
* **Wrap Size** (`wrap_size`, select_one, optional): Choose the preferred size of wrap, if desired.
* **Wrap Quantity** (`wrap_quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of wraps needed.
* **Additional Supplies Needed** (`additional_supplies`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional supplies that may be needed.
* **Brand Preference** (`brand_preference`, text, optional): Enter any preferred brand, if applicable.
* **Color Preference** (`color_preference`, select_multiple, optional): Select preferred colors for any colored tape or wraps.
* **Estimated Unit Price** (`estimated_unit_price`, number, optional): Enter the estimated unit price for the order.
* **Total Budget** (`total_budget`, number, optional): Enter the total budget for the order.
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, select_one, required): Select the shipping method for the order, such as Standard Shipping or Express Shipping.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, required): Enter the address to ship the order to.
* **Delivery Instructions** (`delivery_instructions`, text, optional): Enter any special delivery instructions.
* **Budget or Account Code** (`budget_code`, text, optional): Enter any budget or account code, if applicable.
* **Purchase Order Number** (`purchase_order_number`, text, optional): Enter any purchase order number, if applicable.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the payment method for the order, such as Credit Card or Purchase Order.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, optional): Enter any special instructions or notes for the order.
* **Rush Order** (`rush_order`, select_one, required): Indicate if this is a rush order or not.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate order processing.
* If you have any specific delivery instructions or requests, include them in the **Delivery Instructions** field.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all details are accurate.
