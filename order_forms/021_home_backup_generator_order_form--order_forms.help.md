# Home Backup Generator Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to place an order for a home backup generator. Users will need to provide contact information, select a backup generator, choose power and feature options, and enter delivery details. The form is used to collect all necessary information for the order and ensure accurate delivery and billing information.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, fill in your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number. This information will be used to contact you with order updates and delivery notifications.
2.  Choose the desired backup generator by selecting from the available options. You can also choose power and feature options for your order.
3.  Enter your delivery address, including city, state, and zip code. This will ensure that the generator is delivered to the correct location.
4.  Select the desired payment method. You can choose between credit card, PayPal, or check.
5.  Finally, review the order details, including the total price and delivery information. Make sure to enter any delivery notes or special instructions.
