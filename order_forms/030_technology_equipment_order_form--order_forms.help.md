# Technology Equipment Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for technology equipment orders, which will be reviewed and processed by the relevant teams.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please select "User Input" as the field label for this form.
- Fill out the form accurately with the requested information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Input** (`User Input`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the relevant information about your order request.
  - Enter the equipment type (e.g., Laptops, Desktops, Monitors).
  - Enter the equipment make (e.g., Dell, HP, Apple).
  - Enter the equipment model (e.g., Dell Inspiron 15, HP Envy).
  - Enter the quantity of equipment needed.
  - Select the department that requested this equipment (e.g., IT, Procurement, Admin).
  - Select the location of the delivery (e.g., On-site, Remote, Hybrid).
  - Enter any order comments or notes.

  <hr/>


  - **Approval Status** (`Approval Status`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field is currently not used, but is intended for the order's status.
  - **Delivery Details** (`Delivery Details`, `text`, required/optional): This field is currently not used, but is intended to collect delivery information.
  - **Delivery Date** (`Delivery Date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date for the delivery.
  - **Delivery Time** (`Delivery Time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time for the delivery.
  - **Notes** (`Notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional information related to the delivery.
  - **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact information.
  - **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
  - **Department** (`Department`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the department associated with this order.
  - **Location** (`Location`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the location of the delivery.
  - **Order Comments** (`Order Comments`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any comments for this order.
  - **Delivery Address** (`Delivery Address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the delivery address.
  - **Delivery Location** (`Delivery Location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location of delivery.
  - **Equipment Description** (`Equipment Description`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a description of the equipment.
  - **Approval Comment** (`Approval Comment`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any approval comments.
  - **Order Status** (`Order Status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the status of the order (e.g., Open, Closed).
  - **User Comments** (`User Comments`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any comments from the user.
  - **Equipment Serial Number** (`Equipment Serial Number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the equipment serial number.
  - **Delivery Notes** (`Delivery Notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter delivery notes.
  - **Assigned User** (`Assigned User`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the user assigned to this order.
  - **Delivery Time Zone** (`Delivery Time Zone`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time zone for the delivery.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor for clarification.
- Double-check your information before submitting the form.
- Make sure to review the order comments and status before submitting the form.
