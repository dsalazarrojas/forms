3. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select the box type from the "Box Type" dropdown menu.
6. Choose the box size from the "Box Size" dropdown menu.
7. If you have custom dimensions, enter them in the "Custom Dimensions" field.
8. Select the box material from the "Box Material" dropdown menu.
9. Enter the quantity of boxes to be printed in the "Quantity to Order" field.
10. Choose the design option from the "Design Option" dropdown menu.
11. Select if you want to include your company logo or branding.
12. If you have any specific color specifications, enter them in the "Color Specification" field.
