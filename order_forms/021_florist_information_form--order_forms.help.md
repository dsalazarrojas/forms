# Florist Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to capture detailed information about a florist business, including business details, services offered, and other relevant contact and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields with accurate and up-to-date information about your florist business.
2. For each field, ensure that you are authorized to fill out the form on behalf of the business.
3. If you need help or clarification on any field, contact us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Florist Business Details**: Enter a brief description of your florist business, including any notable features or services offered.
* **Services Offered**: Choose the services that your business provides, such as wedding flower arrangements, bouquets, etc. (Select all that apply.)
* **Store Location**: Enter the physical address of your store location.
* **Business Hours**: Enter the hours of operation for your business. Format is HH:MM, e.g., 09:00 or 12:00.
* **Store Hours**: Note: This field is likely intended to capture the store hours of operation for customers to visit, not the hours you work. Enter the hours of operation for your store. Format is HH:MM, e.g., 09:00 or 12:00.
* **Contact Details**: Enter the name, title, and contact information for the person filling out the form on behalf of the business.
* **Email**: Enter the email address associated with your business.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number associated with your business.
* **Fax**: Enter the fax number associated with your business.
* **Address**: Enter the physical address of your business location.
* **Payment Terms**: Note: This field is likely intended to capture any special payment terms or policies for your business. Enter any relevant information, such as payment methods accepted or payment due dates.
* **Payment Methods**: Choose the payment methods accepted by your business, such as cash, credit card, etc. (Select all that apply.)
* **Delivery Info**: Enter any additional delivery information, such as delivery areas or delivery fees.
* **Delivery Time**: Enter the estimated time of delivery for orders.
* **Delivery Location**: Note: This field is likely intended to capture the location where deliveries take place. Enter any additional delivery location information, such as a physical address or drop-off points.

**Tips**

* Ensure that all information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or need help filling out the form, contact us for assistance.
* Please fill out one form per florist business.
