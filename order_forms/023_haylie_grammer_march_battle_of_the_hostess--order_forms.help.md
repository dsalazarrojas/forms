# haylie_grammer_march_battle_of_the_hostess - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "haylie_grammer_march_battle_of_the_hostess" form is designed to collect personal and event-related information from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone Number in the "Phone Number" field (this is optional).
5. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date" field.
6. Enter your Time of Birth in the "Time" field.
7. Enter any additional note or information in the "Note" field (this is optional).
8. Enter your Address in the "Address" field.
9. Enter your City in the "City" field.
10. Select one or more options from the "State" field.
11. Enter your Zip Code in the "Zip Code" field.
11. Select one option from the "Event Preferences" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
- **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number (if you want to be contacted).
- **Date** (date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Time** (time, optional): Enter your time of birth.
- **Note** (note, optional): Enter any additional information or notes about yourself.
- **Address** (text, optional): Enter your address.
- **City** (text, optional): Enter your city.
- **State** (select multiple, required): Select one or more states.
- **Zip Code** (number, required): Enter your zip code.
- **Event Preferences** (select one, required): Select whether you prefer to attend the event.
