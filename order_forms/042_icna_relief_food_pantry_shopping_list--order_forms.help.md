# icna_relief_food_pantry_shopping_list - Help Guide

## Purpose

The icna_relief_food_pantry_shopping_list form is designed to collect information from customers about their order for the ICNA Relief Food Pantry shopping list. This form helps us understand the customer's needs and preferences to provide the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you have all the required information.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for the `customer_info` field, indicating whether or not you are a customer.
3. Enter your name in the `customer_name` field.
4. Enter your email address in the `customer_email` field.
5. Enter your phone number in the `customer_phone` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Form (icna_relief_food_pantry_shopping_list.order_form)** (`note`, required/optional): This field is a placeholder for the actual order form content. You can add your order details here.
* **Customer Info (icna_relief_food_pantry_shopping_list.customer_info)** (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose "Yes" if you are a customer of the ICNA Relief Food Pantry, and "No" otherwise.
* **Customer Name (icna_relief_food_pantry_shopping_list.customer_name)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your name so we can identify you.
* **Customer Email (icna_relief_food_pantry_shopping_list.customer_email)** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Customer Phone (icna_relief_food_pantry_shopping_list.customer_phone)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter your name and email address accurately to ensure we can contact you effectively.
* If you have any specific requests or questions, please let us know in the "Order Form" field.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us at your preferred contact method.
