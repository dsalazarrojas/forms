# express_package_pickup_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The express package pickup booking form is designed to gather information from customers for efficient and accurate package delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred pickup date for your package.
2. Choose your preferred pickup time for your package.
3. Enter your name.
4. Provide your phone number.
5. Enter your email address.
6. Add any additional notes about your package.
7. Choose one option from the available options.
8. Select multiple options from the available options.
9. Enter a decimal value for your package.
9. Enter an integer value for your package.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pickup date**: Enter the date you would like to schedule the package pickup.
* **Pickup time**: Select the time you would like to schedule the package pickup.
* **Name**: Enter your name, so we can contact you regarding your package.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, so we can contact you for any updates or questions.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address, so we can send you updates and notifications.
* **Note**: Add any additional information about your package, such as special requests or instructions.
* **Select one option**: Choose one option from the available options to help us understand your package preferences.
* **Select multiple options**: Choose multiple options from the available options to help us understand your package preferences.
* **Decimal value**: Enter a decimal value for your package, such as the weight or size.
* **Integer value**: Enter an integer value for your package, such as the quantity or number of packages.

## Tips
* Please ensure you enter accurate and valid information, especially for date and time fields.
* If you have any special requests or instructions for your package, please add them in the "Note" field.
* If you select multiple options, ensure you choose options that are mutually exclusive and do not conflict with each other.
* If you enter a decimal value, ensure it is a valid number between 0 and 1.
* If you enter an integer value, ensure it is a whole number.
* Please review your form carefully before submitting, to avoid errors and ensure accurate information.
