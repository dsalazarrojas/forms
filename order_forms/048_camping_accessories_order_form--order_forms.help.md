# Camping Accessories Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Camping Accessories Order Form is designed to collect customer information and order details for camping accessories. The form is used to create orders for various camping items, including camping gear, accessories, and other essentials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your customer information**: Fill in the fields "Customer First Name", "Customer Last Name", and "Email Address" with your personal details.
2. **Enter contact information**: Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
3. **Select shipping information**: Choose the shipping method and address for your order.
4. **Order your camping accessories**: Select the quantities and types of camping gear you need, such as tent stakes, camping lanterns, sleeping bags, and more.
5. **Configure shipping and payment**: Select a payment method and review the order subtotal and total, including the shipping cost.
6. **Accept terms and conditions**: Confirm that you have read and accepted the terms and conditions.
7. **Add order notes and signature**: Enter any special notes or instructions for your order, and sign the form with your full name and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer First Name**: Your first name.
* **Customer Last Name**: Your last name.
* **Email Address**: Your contact email.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your shipping address where the order will be delivered.
* **City**: The city or town where the order will be delivered.
* **State/Province**: The state or province where the order will be delivered.
* **Zip Code/Postal Code**: The postal code where the order will be delivered.
* **Tent Stakes Quantity**: Number of packs of tent stakes needed.
* **Tent Stakes Price (per pack)**: The price per pack of tent stakes.
* **Camping Lantern Quantity**: Number of camping lanterns needed.
* **Camping Lantern Type**: Select the type of camping lantern you need.
* **Sleeping Bag Quantity**: Number of sleeping bags needed.
* **Sleeping Bag Temperature Rating**: Select the temperature rating for the sleeping bags.
* **Camping Chair Quantity**: Number of camping chairs needed.
* **Camping Table Quantity**: Number of camping tables needed.
* **Cooler Quantity**: Number of coolers needed.
* **Cooler Size**: Select the size of the cooler you need.
* **Camp Stove Quantity**: Number of camp stoves needed.
* **Camp Stove Type**: Select the type of camp stove you need.
* **Fuel Canister Quantity**: Number of fuel canisters needed.
* **Cookware Set Quantity**: Number of cookware sets needed.
* **Water Filter Quantity**: Number of water filters needed.
* **First Aid Kit Quantity**: Number of first aid kits needed.
* **Flashlight Quantity**: Number of flashlights needed.
* **Headlamp Quantity**: Number of headlamps needed.
* **Camping Hammock Quantity**: Number of camping hammocks needed.
* **Tarp Quantity**: Number of tarps needed.
* **Rope Quantity**: Number of ropes needed.
* **Fire Starter Quantity**: Number of fire starters needed.
* **Camping Utensil Set Quantity**: Number of camping utensil sets needed.
* **Portable Shower Quantity**: Number of portable showers needed.
* **Order Subtotal**: Calculated order total before shipping.
* **Shipping Method**: Select the shipping method.
* **Shipping Cost**: Calculated shipping cost.
* **Order Total**: Final order total.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method.
* **Promo Code**: If you have a promo code, enter it here.
* **Order Notes**: Special instructions for the order.
* **I Accept Terms and Conditions**: Confirm that you have read and accepted the terms and conditions.
* **Customer Signature**: Type your full name.
* **Date**: Date of order.
 
Please review this guide carefully and contact us if you need further assistance.
