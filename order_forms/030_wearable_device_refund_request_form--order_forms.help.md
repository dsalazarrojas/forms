# Wearable Device Refund Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers initiate a refund request for a wearable device that they have purchased from our store. It gathers essential information to expedite the refund process.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name in the "User Name" field.
2. Enter the "Order ID" number for the order you would like to request a refund for.
3. Enter the date and time of the original order.
4. Enter the reason for requesting the refund.
5. Select the type of device you are requesting a refund for (Wearable or Other).
6. Enter the serial number of the device.
7. (Optional) If you have any attachments or supporting documents, you may upload them in the "Attachment 1" to "Attachment 7" fields.
8. Enter your customer details, such as name, email, phone number, address, city, region, and country of residence.
9. Select your preferred return method from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your account or order information.
* **Order ID**: Enter the order number for the purchase you would like to request a refund for.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date you made the original order.
* **Order Time**: Enter the time you made the original order.
* **Reason**: Please provide a clear and concise reason for your refund request.
* **Device Type**: Select whether the device is a Wearable or Other.
* **Serial Number**: Enter the serial number of the device you are requesting a refund for.
* **Attachment 1** to **Attachment 7**: If you have any documents or images that support your refund request, you may upload them here. (Optional)
* **Customer**: Enter your customer details, such as name.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Address**: Enter your address.
* **Customer City**: Enter your city.
* **Customer Region**: Enter your region.
* **Customer Country**: Select the country of your residence.
* **Return Method**: Select your preferred return method from the available options.
