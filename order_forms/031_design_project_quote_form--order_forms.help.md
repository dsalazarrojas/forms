# Design Project Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Design Project Quote Form is a tool used to collect project quotes from designers or agencies. It's a comprehensive form that gathers all necessary information for the quote, ensuring that designers or agencies have a clear understanding of the project requirements and can provide accurate quotes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields with accurate and truthful information.
2. Select relevant options for project type and status.
3. Enter contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
4. Provide project details and notes.
5. Enter quote date and estimated time.
6. Enter estimated cost and quote terms.
7. Fill in contact information for the agency or designer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Project Details** (id: 1, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the project.
- **Project Type** (id: 2, select_multiple, required: false): Select all applicable project types from the dropdown menu.
- **Project Status** (id: 3, select_one, required: false): Select the current project status from the dropdown menu.
- **Quote Date** (id: 4, date, required: false): Enter the date of the quote.
- **Designer** (id: 6, email, required: false): Enter the name and email of the designer.
- **Agency** (id: 7, text, required: false): Enter the agency name and website.
- **Contact Details** (id: 8, text, required: false): Enter contact details, including address and phone number.
- **Project Budget** (id: 9, number, required: false): Enter the project budget.
- **Estimated Time** (id: 10, text, required: false): Enter the estimated time required for the project.
- **Estimated Cost** (id: 11, number, required: false): Enter the estimated cost of the project.
- **Quote Terms** (id: 12, text, required: false): Enter any additional quote terms.
- **Project Terms** (id: 13, text, required: false): Enter any project terms.
- **Contact Info** (id: 14, text, required: false): Enter contact information for the agency or designer.
- **Client Name** (id: 15, text, required: false): Enter the client name.
- **Agency Info** (id: 16, text, required: false): Enter agency information.
- **Quote Date2** (id: 17, date, required: false): Enter the date of the quote.
- **Quote Time** (id: 18, time, required: false): Enter the quote time.
- **Contact Name** (id: 19, text, required: false): Enter the contact name.
- **Contact Phone** (id: 20, text, required: false): Enter the contact phone number.
- **Contact Email** (id: 21, email, required: false): Enter the contact email.
- **Project Notes** (id: 22, text, required: false): Enter project notes.
- **Project Notes2** (id: 23, text, required: false): Enter additional project notes.
- **Project Status2** (id: 24, text, required: false): Enter the project status.
- **Project Status3** (id: 25, text, required: false): Enter the project status.

Note: Please fill in all required fields accurately and truthfully to ensure a smooth quote process.
