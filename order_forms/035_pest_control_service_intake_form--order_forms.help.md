# pest_control_service_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to submit a request for pest control services.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your company name in the Company Name field.
2. Enter your street address in the Street Address field.
3. Enter your city in the City field.
4. Enter your state in the State field.
5. Enter your zip code in the Zip Code field.
6. Enter your first name and last name in the Contact First Name and Contact Last Name fields.
7. Enter your contact email in the Contact Email field.
8. Enter your phone number in the Contact Phone field.
9. Enter a brief description of the service you need in the Service Request field.
10. Select the type of pest you are seeking control for in the Pest Type field.
11. Enter a detailed description of the pest issue in the Pest Description field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Street Address** (text, required): Enter the street address of your location.
* **City** (text, required): Enter the city of your location.
* **State** (text, required): Enter the state of your location.
* **Zip Code** (text, required): Enter the zip code of your location.
* **Contact First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Contact Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Contact Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Service Request** (text, required): Enter a brief description of the service you need.
* **Pest Type** (select one, required): Select the type of pest you are seeking control for. Options include:
    + Ants
    + Bed Bugs
    + Cockroaches
    + Fleas
    + Rats
    + Spiders
    + Wasps
    + Termites
    + Ticks
* **Pest Description** (text, required): Enter a detailed description of the pest issue you are experiencing.
 
## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
- If you have any additional information or details about the pest issue, please provide them in the Pest Description field.
