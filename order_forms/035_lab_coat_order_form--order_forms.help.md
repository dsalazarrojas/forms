# Lab Coat Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help employees order lab coats for their department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This field is not required.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field. This field is not required.
3. Enter your **Job Title** in the "Job Title" field. This field is not required.
4. Select your department from the list of options in the "Department" field. This field is required.
5. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone" field. This field is not required.
6. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field. This field is not required.
7. Select the **Lab Coat Size** you need in the "Lab Coat Size" field. This field is not required.
8. Select the **Lab Coat Type** you need from the list of options in the "Lab Coat Type" field. This field is not required.
9. Enter the number of **Quantity** you need in the "Quantity" field. This field is not required.
10. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the "Date of Birth" field. This field is not required.
11. Enter the **Date of Visit** you need a lab coat for in the "Date of Visit" field. This field is not required.
12. Enter the **Time of Visit** you need a lab coat for in the "Time of Visit" field. This field is not required.
13. Enter any additional **Note** you have in the "Note" field. This field is not required.
14. Enter your department's **Phone Number** in the "Department Phone" field. This field is not required.
15. Enter your department's **Email** in the "Department Email" field. This field is not required.
16. Re-enter your department name in the "Department" field. This field is not required but will be validated for consistency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, not required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, not required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, not required): Enter your job title as it appears on your identification.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select the department you are ordering the lab coat for.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number if you need to be contacted.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address if you need to be contacted.
* **Lab Coat Size** (`lab_coat_size`, number, not required): Select the size of the lab coat you need.
* **Lab Coat Type** (`lab_coat_type`, select_one, not required): Select the type of lab coat you need.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, not required): Enter the number of lab coats you need.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, not required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, not required): Enter the date you plan to visit.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, not required): Enter the time you plan to visit.
* **Note** (`note`, note, not required): Enter any additional information about your visit.
* **Department Phone** (`department_phone`, text, not required): Enter your department's phone number if you need to be contacted.
* **Department Email** (`department_email`, email, not required): Enter your department's email address if you need to be contacted.
* **Department** (`department`, text, not required): Re-enter your department name for validation purposes.
