# Fall Pre Order - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fall Pre Order form is designed to collect customer information and order details for pre-orders during the fall season. This form will be used to process and ship orders accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out each page of the form carefully**. Each page has a specific set of fields that require information from you.
2. **Review each field carefully** before proceeding to the next page.
3. **Submit the form once you have filled out all the required fields**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (Page 1)**: This page is for general customer information. You will need to provide your:
	+ **Customer Information**: Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant details that you would like to share with us.
* **Shipping Information (Page 2)**: This page is for your shipping details. You will need to provide:
	+ **Shipping Information**: Enter your shipping address, including street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Product Information (Page 3)**: This page is for your pre-order product details. You will need to provide:
	+ **Product Information**: Enter the product(s) you would like to pre-order, including quantity and any other relevant details.
* **Payment Information (Page 4)**: This page is for your payment details. You will need to provide:
	+ **Payment Information**: Enter your payment method, including card type and card details.
* **Additional Information (Page 5)**: This page is for any additional details you would like to provide. You can enter anything you feel is relevant.
* **Confirmation (Page 6)**: This page is for review and confirmation of your order. Please review your order carefully before submitting.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out each field accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We will process and ship your order according to your provided information.
