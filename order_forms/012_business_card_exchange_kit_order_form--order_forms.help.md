# Business Card Exchange Kit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a business card exchange kit order. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure that all necessary details are provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your personal and business contact information in the "Customer Details" section.
2. Select the type of business card you need (Standard, Mini, Rounded Corners, Premium Stock, or Eco-Friendly).
3. Specify the quantity of each card type you need.
4. Choose the card stock type (Standard 14pt, Premium 16pt, Thick 32pt, Recycled Paper, or Plastic).
5. Select the finish type (Matte, Gloss, Satin, Textured, or Spot UV).
6. Choose the color printing option (Single Sided Full Color, Double Sided Full Color, Black and White, or Spot Color).
7. If you need design assistance, select the level of design help you need (I have my own design, Need basic design, Need professional design, or Choose from templates).
8. Specify how quickly you need the design turnaround time (1-2 business days, 3-5 business days, 1-2 weeks, or No rush).
9. Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping Information" section.
10. Select the shipping method (Standard 7-10 days, Expedited 3-5 days, Rush 1-2 days, or Overnight).
11. Provide any special instructions or notes in the "Special Instructions" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your business card.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your business card.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or position.
* **Email Address**: Enter your business email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your business phone number.
* **Card Type**: Select the type of business card you need (Standard, Mini, Rounded Corners, Premium Stock, or Eco-Friendly).
* **Quantity - Standard Size**: Specify the number of standard-sized business cards you need.
* **Quantity - Mini Size**: Specify the number of mini business cards you need.
* **Card Stock Type**: Select the paper stock quality (Standard 14pt, Premium 16pt, Thick 32pt, Recycled Paper, or Plastic).
* **Finish Type**: Select the card surface finish (Matte, Gloss, Satin, Textured, or Spot UV).
* **Color Printing**: Select the color printing option (Single Sided Full Color, Double Sided Full Color, Black and White, or Spot Color).
* **Design Service**: Select the level of design help you need (I have my own design, Need basic design, Need professional design, or Choose from templates).
* **Design Turnaround Time**: Specify how quickly you need the design turnaround time (1-2 business days, 3-5 business days, 1-2 weeks, or No rush).
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your complete mailing address for delivery.
* **City**: Enter the city for delivery.
* **State/Province**: Enter the state or province for delivery.
* **ZIP/Postal Code**: Enter the postal code.
* **Shipping Method**: Select the shipping method (Standard 7-10 days, Expedited 3-5 days, Rush 1-2 days, or Overnight).
* **Special Instructions**: Provide any special requests or notes for your order.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to avoid any issues with your order.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact support for assistance.
- Please review your order carefully before submitting to ensure everything is correct.
