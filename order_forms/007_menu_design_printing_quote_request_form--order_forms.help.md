# Menu Design Printing Quote Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for quote requests related to menu design printing. The form captures essential details such as customer name, business type, menu design requirements, and other relevant information to help the printing team prepare a quote for the customer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and contact information, including phone number or email.
2. Select the type of business you represent (Restaurant, Food Truck, or Catering Service).
3. Provide any specific requirements for the menu design.
4. Include any special requests or additional information for the quote.
5. Choose the date and time for the quote request (if applicable).
6. Select the team member you would like to be assigned to your quote request.
7. Update the quote status as it changes (e.g., from Pending to Approved).
8. Set a due date for the quote.
9. Set a delivery date for the quote.
10. Update the quote status date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as the person requesting the quote.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of business you represent (Restaurant, Food Truck, or Catering Service).
* **Menu Design Requirements** (`menu_design_requirements`, text, required: false): Describe any specific requirements or preferences for the menu design.
* **Quote Request** (`quote_request`, text, required: false): Provide any additional information or requests for the quote.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, required: false): Enter your business address.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number or email.
* **Quote Request Date** (`quote_request_date`, date, required: false): Choose the date for the quote request (Format: MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Quote Request Time** (`quote_request_time`, time, required: false): Choose the time for the quote request (Format: 12H).
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, note, required: false): Add any special requests or notes for the quote.
* **Quote Details** (`quote_details`, note, required: false): Provide any further information for the quote.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the team member you would like to be assigned to your quote request.
* **Quote Status** (`quote_status`, select_one, required: false): Update the quote status as it changes (e.g., from Pending to Approved).
* **Quote Notes** (`quote_notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes for the quote.
* **Quote Due Date** (`quote_due_date`, date, required: false): Set a due date for the quote.
* **Quote Delivery Date** (`quote_delivery_date`, date, required: false): Set a delivery date for the quote.
* **Quote Status Date** (`quote_status_date`, date, required: false): Update the quote status date.

This form is designed to collect relevant information from customers for quote requests related to menu design printing. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that the printing team can prepare an adequate quote for your business.
