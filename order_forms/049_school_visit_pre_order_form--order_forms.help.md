# School Visit Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help schools pre-order and manage their visits by providing a simple and structured way to collect information about the visit, order details, and payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On **Page 1: School Visit Pre Order Form**, fill in the basic details of the visit, such as the school name, date, and time.
2. On **Page 2: Order Form**, provide information about the order, including the order type and quantity.
3. On **Page 3: Appearance Component**, describe the appearance of the visit, including the location and number of visitors.
4. On **Page 4: Contact Form**, provide contact information for the school representative.
5. On **Page 5: Shipping Form**, specify the shipping details, such as the shipping method and date.
6. On **Page 6: Notes**, add any additional notes or comments about the visit.
7. On **Page 7: Review Form**, fill in the review and feedback section.
8. On **Page 8: Payment Form**, specify the payment method and details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Visit Pre Order Form** (`school_visit_pre_order_form_page_1`, text, required): This page is where you provide basic details about the visit, including the school name, date, and time.
* **Order Form** (`school_visit_pre_order_form_page_2`, text, required): Fill in the order type and quantity to complete this order.
* **Appearance Component** (`school_visit_pre_order_form_page_3`, text, required): Describe the appearance of the visit, including the location and number of visitors.
* **Contact Form** (`school_visit_pre_order_form_page_4`, text, required): Provide contact information for the school representative.
* **Shipping Form** (`school_visit_pre_order_form_page_5`, text, required): Specify the shipping method and date.
* **Notes** (`school_visit_pre_order_form_page_6`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the visit.
* **Review Form** (`school_visit_pre_order_form_page_7`, text, required): Fill in the review and feedback section.
* **Payment Form** (`school_visit_pre_order_form_page_8`, text, required): Specify the payment method and details.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure a smooth and successful visit experience. If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form administrator at form@gic.mx.
