# Sales Funnel Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Funnel Checklist Form is a tool to help sales teams evaluate their lead generation and conversion processes. It is designed to identify strengths and weaknesses in each stage of the sales funnel, from lead generation to conversion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each stage of the sales funnel and answer the questions in each section based on your current processes.
2. Provide clear and concise answers for each field.
3. Make sure to answer all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Lead Generation (Page 1)
* **Lead Generation** (`lead_generation`, text, required: false): Describe your current lead generation strategies and tactics, such as advertising, social media, or referrals.

### Lead Qualification (Page 2)
* **Lead Qualification** (`lead_qualification`, text, required: false): Explain your process for evaluating and qualifying leads, including criteria for determining lead potential.

### Needs Assessment (Page 3)
* **Needs Assessment** (`needs_assessment`, text, required: false): Describe how you assess the needs of your leads, including gathering information about their challenges and pain points.

### Solution Preservation (Page 4)
* **Solution Preservation** (`solution_preservation`, text, required: false): Outline your strategies for maintaining a strong relationship with leads and customers, including ongoing communication and support.

### Solution Presentation (Page 5)
* **Solution Presentation** (`solution_presentation`, text, required: false): Explain how you present your solutions to leads and customers, including the types of materials and information used.

### Conversion Plan (Page 6)
* **Conversion Plan** (`conversion_plan`, text, required: false): Describe your conversion strategy, including the process for moving leads through each stage of the sales funnel.

### Lead Follow-up (Page 7)
* **Lead Follow-up** (`lead_follow_up`, text, required: false): Outline your lead follow-up process, including the frequency and method of follow-up activities.

### Lead Nurturing (Page 8)
* **Lead Nurturing** (`lead_nurturing`, text, required: false): Explain how you nurture leads, including ongoing communication and engagement strategies.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers to ensure accurate assessments of your sales funnel processes.
* Review and update your answers periodically to reflect changes in your processes.
* Use this form as a starting point for discussions and planning with your team to improve your sales funnel performance.
