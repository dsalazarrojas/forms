# The Mani Man Wish List - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "The Mani Man Wish List" form is a tool for customers to provide their wishes or requests to be fulfilled. This form captures essential details about the customer and their order, allowing us to effectively manage and track the order process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Provide Customer Information**
	* The "Customer Info" field is a drop-down menu where you can select "Yes" to indicate that the customer information is correct.
	* Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
	* Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field.
	* Enter the customer's phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
	* Enter the customer's address in the "Customer Address" field.
2. **Enter Order Details**
	* Select the order date in the "Order Date" field.
	* Select the order time in the "Order Time" field.
	* Enter the "Color Street Product" field with the desired product.
3. **Add Additional Information**
	* Check "Active" or "Inactive" in the "Order Status" field as needed.
	* Check "Yes" or "No" in the "Order Priority" field to indicate the urgency of the order.
	* Check "Yes" or "No" in the "Order Type" field to indicate the type of order.
	* Select the customer's order status ("Active" or "Inactive") in the "Customer Order Status" field.
	* Select the customer's order priority ("Yes" or "No") in the "Customer Order Priority" field.
4. **Assign and Follow-up**
	* Enter the name of the person assigned to the order in the "Assigned To" field.
	* Enter any additional comments from the customer in the "Customer Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info**: Select "Yes" to indicate that the customer information is correct.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Address**: Enter the customer's address.
* **Order Date**: Select the date the order is requested.
* **Order Time**: Select the time the order is requested.
* **Color Street Product**: Enter the product desired.
* **Order Status**: Check "Active" or "Inactive" as needed.
* **Order Priority**: Check "Yes" or "No" to indicate the urgency of the order.
* **Order Type**: Check "Yes" or "No" to indicate the type of order.
* **Customer Order Status**: Select the customer's order status ("Active" or "Inactive").
* **Customer Order Priority**: Select the customer's order priority ("Yes" or "No").
* **Assigned To**: Enter the name of the person assigned to the order.
* **Customer Comments**: Enter any additional comments from the customer.
* **Order Notes**: Enter any additional information about the order.
* **Customer Tags**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the customer has tags.
* **Customer Notes**: Enter any additional information about the customer.
* **Customer Comments**: Enter any additional comments from the customer.
