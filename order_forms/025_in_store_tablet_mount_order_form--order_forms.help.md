# In Store Tablet Mount Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for customers who wish to place an order for a tablet mount in-store.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Start by navigating to the "Customer details" section.
2. Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
3. Proceed to the "Customer address" section, if you feel comfortable providing your address.
4. Move on to the "Shipping details" section and provide any relevant shipping information.
5. In the "Order products" section, enter details about the products you wish to order, including quantities and any specific product information.
6. Finally, enter any additional comments or notes about your order in the "Order notes" section.
7. Once you've completed all sections, submit the form to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Customer Details
* **Customer Details** (optional): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.

### Customer Address
* **Customer Address** (optional): Provide your address for any potential delivery or returns.

### Shipping Details
* **Shipping Details** (optional): Provide any relevant shipping information, such as delivery dates or special instructions.

### Order Products
* **Order Products** (optional): Enter details about the products you wish to order, including quantities and any specific product information.

### Order Notes
* **Order Notes** (optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your order.
