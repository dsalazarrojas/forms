# Food Sampling Equipment Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to place orders for sampling equipment for food manufacturers and suppliers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information ready before starting the form.
2. Fill in the order details section with the specific equipment required and the sampling method.
3. Select the sampling tools and lab equipment needed for the order.
4. Choose the delivery method for the sampling equipment.
5. Enter the order date and delivery date.
6. Provide the orderer's contact information (email, phone number, and address).
7. Fill in the shipping address.
8. Optionally, add any delivery notes or order comments.
9. Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submission.
