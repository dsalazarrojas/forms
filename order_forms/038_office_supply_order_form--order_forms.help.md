# Office Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for customers or salespeople to place orders for office supplies. It is a centralized platform for managing and tracking office supply orders, ensuring accuracy and consistency in the ordering process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
2. Select the category and item type for the office supply order.
3. Enter the item name, quantity, price, and date for the order.
4. Choose the payment method and amount.
5. Enter the customer's contact information, including email, phone, address, city, state, zip, and country.
6. Optionally, add any additional comments or notes about the order.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`Category`, `select_one`, required): Choose the category for the office supply order.
* **Item Type** (`Item Type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of office supply item being ordered.
* **Item Name** (`Item Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the office supply item being ordered.
* **Quantity** (`Quantity`, `number`, required): Enter the quantity of the office supply item being ordered.
* **Price** (`Price`, `number`, required): Enter the price of the office supply item.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date for the order.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, `select_one`, required): Choose the method of payment for the order.
* **Payment Amount** (`Payment Amount`, `number`, required): Enter the payment amount for the order.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Address** (`Address`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's address.
* **City** (`City`, `text`, required): Enter the city where the customer is located.
* **State** (`State`, `text`, required): Enter the state where the customer is located.
* **Zip** (`Zip`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's zip code.
* **Country** (`Country`, `text`, required): Enter the country where the customer is located.
* **Order Comments** (`Order Comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the order.
* **Order Total** (`Order Total`, `number`, required): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Customer Comments** (`Customer Comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes from the customer.
* **Sales Person** (`Sales Person`, `text`, optional): Enter the salesperson's name.
* **Sales Email** (`Sales Email`, `text`, optional): Enter the salesperson's email address.
* **Sales Phone** (`Sales Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the salesperson's phone number.
* **Customer Name** (`Customer Name`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's name.
* **Order Status** (`Order Status`, `select_one`, required): Set the status of the order as active or inactive.

Note: Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely before submitting the form.
