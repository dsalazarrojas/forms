# Smart Robot Maintenance Kit Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Smart Robot Maintenance Kit Order Form is a comprehensive guide for users to place orders for robot maintenance kits and services. This form helps streamline the order process by gathering essential details about the order, payment, and delivery.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section carefully and fill in the required fields.
2. Select the relevant options from the dropdown menus for order type, robot model, and other fields as needed.
3. Enter accurate and detailed information in the text fields.
4. Double-check your entries for errors before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Type** (`order_type`, Select One): Select the type of order you are placing, such as Robot Service, Robot Upgrade, or Kit Order.
* **Robot Model** (`robot_model`, Select One): Choose the specific robot model you are ordering.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, Date): Enter the date you are placing the order.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, Text): Enter the name of the customer.
* **Customer E-mail** (`customer_email`, Email): Enter the email address of the customer.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, Text): Enter the phone number of the customer.
* **Address** (`address`, Text): Enter the delivery address of the customer.
* **City** (`city`, Text): Enter the city of the customer's address.
* **State** (`state`, Text): Enter the state or province of the customer's address.
* **Zip** (`zip`, Text): Enter the postal code or zip code of the customer's address.
* **Country** (`country`, Text): Enter the country of the customer's address.
* **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, Text): Add any additional comments about the order.
* **Shipping Cost** (`shipping_cost`, Number): Enter the shipping cost associated with the order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, Select One): Choose how the payment will be made, such as Online Payment, Offline Payment, or Bank Transfer.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, Text): Enter any special payment terms or conditions.
* **Total Amount** (`total_amount`, Number): Enter the total amount for the order.
* **Notes** (`notes`, Text): Add any additional notes or information about the order.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, Text): Enter the customer's signature.
* **Robot Service Kit Parts** (`robot_service_kit_parts`, Select One): Select whether the order includes service kit parts.
* **Robot Upgrades** (`robot_upgrades`, Select One): Choose the type of robot upgrade for the order.
* **Robot Service Kit Price** (`robot_service_kit_price`, Number): Enter the price of the robot service kit.
* **Customer Service Kit Price** (`customer_service_kit_price`, Number): Enter the price of the service kit for the customer.
* **Kit Shipping Cost** (`kit_shipping_cost`, Number): Enter the shipping cost for the service kit.
* **Customer Service Cost** (`customer_service_cost`, Number): Enter the customer service cost.
* **Customer Reported Cost** (`customer_reported_cost`, Number): Enter any additional costs reported by the customer.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, Select One): Choose the current status of the order, such as New Order, In Progress, or Completed.
* **Robot Status** (`robot_status`, Select One): Select the status of the robot, such as Available, In Maintenance, or Sold.
* **Robot Location** (`robot_location`, Select One): Choose the location of the robot, such as Warehouse, Customer Location, or Repair Center.
* **Robot Model Status** (`robot_model_status`, Select One): Select the status of the robot model, such as Active, Inactive, or Retired.
* **Customer Name Status** (`customer_name_status`, Select One): Choose the status of the customer's name, such as Verified or Not Verified.
