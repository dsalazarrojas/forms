# Matilda Jane February 2022 Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to place pre-orders for the February 2022 collection from Matilda Jane.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by providing your first and last name in the First Name and Last Name fields.
- Enter your email address in the Email field, following the standard email format (e.g., [email@example.com](mailto:email@example.com)).
- If you have a phone number, provide it in the Phone field for any necessary contact.
- If you know your date of birth, enter it in the Date of Birth field.
- Select the correct Order Type from the dropdown menu, indicating the type of item you wish to pre-order.
- Specify the Quantity of items you would like to pre-order.
- Fill in your address, city, state, and Zip Code for shipping purposes.
- Provide any additional Order Comments if needed.
- Optionally, provide Shipping Instructions for specific delivery requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your standard email address for contact and order updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for any necessary contact.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth for age-related purposes (if required for your order).
* **Order Type**: Select the type of item you wish to pre-order from the available options.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of items you would like to pre-order.
* **Address**: Enter your address for shipping purposes.
* **City**: Enter the city for shipping purposes.
* **State**: Enter the state for shipping purposes.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code for shipping purposes.
* **Order Comments**: Add any additional comments or requests for your order.
* **Shipping Instructions**: Provide any specific shipping requirements or instructions.
