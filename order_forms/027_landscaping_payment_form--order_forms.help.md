# Landscaping Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Landscaping Payment Form is used to collect and record customer payments for landscaping services. Customers can submit their payments through this form, which includes various fields for contact information, service details, and payment status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Customer Email" field.
3. Provide a description of the service you are paying for in the "Service Description" field.
4. Enter the service charge amount in the "Service Charge" field.
5. Select the method you want to use for payment in the "Payment Method" field.
6. If necessary, add additional details about your payment method in the "Payment Method Details" field.
7. Select the type of service you are paying for in the "Service Type" field.
8. Check the status of your payment in the "Payment Status" field.
9. You can add any additional notes about your payment in the "Note" field.
10. Finally, review your information and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (Customer Name, text, required): Enter your full name as the customer.
* **Customer Email** (Customer Email, email, required): Enter your email address to be contacted by us.
* **Service Description** (Service Description, text, required): Describe the service you are paying for.
* **Service Charge** (Service Charge, number, required): Enter the service charge amount.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_one, required): Select how you want to make the payment (e.g., Bank Draft, Check, Credit Card, Online Payment).
* **Payment Method Details** (Payment Method Details, text, optional): Provide additional details about your payment method if needed.
* **Payment Date/Time** (Payment Date/Time, date, optional): Enter the date and time of the payment.
* **Payment Amount** (Payment Amount, number, optional): Enter the payment amount.
* **Payment Status** (Payment Status, select_one, required): Select the status of your payment (e.g., Pending, Approved, Declined, Cancelled).
* **Service Address** (Service Address, text, optional): Enter the address of the service location, if necessary.
* **Service Type** (Service Type, select_one, required): Select the type of service you are paying for (e.g., Lawn Care, Gardening, Other).
* **Service Status** (Service Status, select_one, required): Select the status of the service (e.g., Pending, Approved, Declined).
* **Assigned Tool** (Assigned Tool, select_one, optional): Select the tool that was used to perform the service.
* **Customer Phone** (Customer Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Note** (Note, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your payment.
* **Service ID** (Service ID, text, optional): Enter the service ID, if known.
* **Customer ID** (Customer ID, text, optional): Enter your customer ID, if known.
* **Form ID** (Form ID, text, optional): Enter the form ID, if known.
* **Created By** (Created By, text, optional): Identify who created this payment record.
* **Created On** (Created On, text, optional): Date and time this payment record was created.
* **Updated On** (Updated On, text, optional): Date and time this payment record was last updated.
* **Payment Note** (Payment Note, note, optional): Add any additional notes about this payment.
* **Payment ID** (Payment ID, text, optional): Enter the payment ID, if known.
* **Form ID** (Form ID, text, optional): Enter the form ID of this payment record.
