# Tiny Home Purchase Order - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers who are interested in purchasing a tiny home. The form helps us understand their preferences and requirements for the perfect tiny home model, price range, and other details for a successful transaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your desired tiny home model in the "Model Name" field.
2. Choose your desired model year from the "Model Year" field.
3. Specify your price range for the tiny home in the "Price Range ($)" field.
4. List the features you're looking for in the "Features" field.
5. Enter your contact email address in the "Buyer's Email" field.
6. Enter your name in the "Buyer's Name" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Buyer's Phone" field.
8. Select your desired delivery date for the tiny home in the "Delivery Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Model Name** (`Model Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the tiny home model you're interested in.
* **Model Year** (`Model Year`, `number`, required: false): Choose the year you're interested in for the tiny home model.
* **Price Range ($)** (`Price Range ($)` `number`, required: false): Enter the price range you're comfortable with for the tiny home.
* **Features** (`Features`, `text`, required: false): List the features you're looking for in the tiny home.
* **Buyer's Email** (`Buyer's Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Buyer's Name** (`Buyer's Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Buyer's Phone** (`Buyer's Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Delivery Date** (`Delivery Date`, `date`, required: false): Choose the date you'd like the tiny home to be delivered.

## Tips
- Please ensure that your email address and phone number are accurate to enable us to contact you with updates and information about your order.
- Be as specific as possible when listing your desired features for the tiny home.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, feel free to include them in the "Features" field.
