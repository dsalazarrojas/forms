# School Photography Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Photography Order Form Template is designed to collect order information from customers. It is intended for use in schools or educational institutions that offer photography services. This form helps customers provide necessary details about their order, including order information, payment, and delivery options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form fields with the required and optional information.
2. Ensure you provide accurate and complete details in each field.
3. Review your order summary to ensure it accurately reflects your order.
4. Submit the form to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Data** (`form_data`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your order, such as "Photography services for school event" or "Senior class portraits."
* **Order Info** (`order_info`, text, optional): Provide details about the order, including date, time, and location of the event or shoot.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, optional): Enter payment information, including method and amount of payment.
* **Delivery Options** (`delivery_options`, text, optional): Choose delivery options, such as "Email" or " Pickup at school office."
* **Order Summary** (`order_summary`, text, optional): Review and confirm the order details provided. Ensure it accurately reflects your order.

## Tips
* Be sure to proofread your form before submitting to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the school's photography department for assistance.
* Use this form to place orders for school photography services.
* You can modify the form to suit your specific needs, but be aware that some fields may be required for processing orders.
