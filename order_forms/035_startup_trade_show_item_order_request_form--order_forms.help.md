# Startup Trade Show Item Order Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Startup Trade Show Item Order Request Form is a tool for exhibitors to provide detailed information about the items they require for the trade show, including contact details, trade show specifics, item specifics, and delivery details. This form allows the trade show management team to accurately plan and arrange for the items requested.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your exhibitor information, including your company name, contact name, email, and phone number.
2. Next, select the trade show you are attending.
3. Then, provide a detailed description of the item you are requesting, including its name and any relevant details.
4. Specify the quantity of the item you require.
5. Enter the unit price of the item.
6. Choose a delivery date and time for your order.
7. Select the delivery location.
8. Finally, add any additional notes about your order, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exhibitor Information (exhibitor_info)**: This field is for you to provide your company name, contact name, email, and phone number. Please fill in your details accurately to ensure we can contact you about your order.
* **Trade Show Information (trade_show_info)**: This field is for you to select the trade show you are attending. Please choose the correct trade show to avoid any confusion or errors.
* **Item Name (item_name)**: This field is for you to provide a detailed description of the item you are requesting. Please be specific about the item and its details.
* **Item Description (item_description)**: This field is also for you to provide more details about the item, if needed.
* **Item Quantity (item_quantity)**: This field is for you to specify the number of items you require.
* **Item Unit Price (item_unit_price)**: This field is for you to enter the unit price of the item.
* **Delivery Date (delivery_date)**: This field is for you to choose a delivery date for your order.
* **Delivery Time (delivery_time)**: This field is for you to select a delivery time for your order.
* **Delivery Location (delivery_location)**: This field is for you to choose the location where you would like to receive your order.
* **Notes (notes)**: This field is for you to add any additional comments or notes about your order, if needed.
