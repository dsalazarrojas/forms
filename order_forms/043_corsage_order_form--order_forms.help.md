# Corsage Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to create an order for a corsage. It gathers information about the customer, the order details, and the payment status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Select the date of the order in the "Order Date" field.
5. Choose the type of corsage you want from the "Corsage Type" dropdown.
6. Select the type of flower you want in the "Flower" dropdown.
7. Choose the size of the corsage in the "Corsage Size" dropdown.
8. Select a ribbon color from the "Ribbon Color" dropdown.
9. If you want to add a note to the order, enter it in the "Note" field.
10. Select "Yes" or "No" if you want to assign a tool to the order in the "Assigned Tool" dropdown.
11. Enter a description of the corsage in the "Corsage Description" field.
12. Enter any additional information about the order in the "Additional Info" field.
13. Enter a message for the corsage in the "Corsage Message" field.
14. Enter the price of the corsage in the "Corsage Price" field.
15. Select the payment status of the order in the "Payment Status" dropdown.
16. Enter the date of payment in the "Payment Date" field (optional).
17. Enter your signature in the "Customer Signature" field (optional).
18. Enter the signature of the florist in the "Florist Signature" field (optional).
19. Enter the delivery date in the "Delivery Date" field (optional).
20. Enter any delivery information in the "Delivery Info" field (optional).
21. Enter any notes about the corsage in the "Corsage Note" field (optional).
22. Select the status of the corsage in the "Corsage Status" dropdown (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Order Date** (`Order Date`, `date`, optional): Select the date of the order.
* **Corsage Type** (`Corsage Type`, `select multiple`, optional): Choose the type of corsage.
*   - **Bouquet**
*   - **Casket**
*   - **Combination**
*   - **Casket and Urn**
*   - **Urn and Corsage**
*   - **Casket and Urn and Corsage**
* **Flower** (`Flower`, `select one`, optional): Select the type of flower.
*   - **Roses**
*   - **Lilies**
*   - **Tulips**
*   - **Orchids**
*   - **Mixed**
* **Corsage Size** (`Corsage Size`, `select one`, optional): Choose the size of the corsage.
*   - **Small**
*   - **Medium**
*   - **Large**
* **Ribbon Color** (`Ribbon Color`, `select one`, optional): Select the ribbon color.
*   - **Pink**
*   - **Blue**
*   - **Red**
* **Note** (`Note`, `note`, optional): Enter any note for the order.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, `select one`, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" if you want to assign a tool to the order.
*   - **Yes**
*   - **No**
* **Corsage Description** (`Corsage Description`, `text`, optional): Enter a description of the corsage.
* **Additional Info** (`Additional Info`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information about the order.
* **Corsage Message** (`Corsage Message`, `text`, optional): Enter a message for the corsage.
* **Corsage Price** (`Corsage Price`, `text`, optional): Enter the price of the corsage.
* **Total** (`Total`, `text`, optional): (This field is not currently used in the form)
* **Payment Status** (`Payment Status`, `select one`, optional): Select the payment status of the order.
*   - **Paid**
*   - **Not Paid**
* **Payment Date** (`Payment Date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of payment (if not paid).
* **Customer Signature** (`Customer Signature`, `text`, optional): Enter your signature.
* **Florist Signature** (`Florist Signature`, `text`, optional): Enter the signature of the florist.
* **Delivery Date** (`Delivery Date`, `date`, optional): Enter the delivery date.
* **Delivery Info** (`Delivery Info`, `text`, optional): Enter any delivery information.
* **Corsage Note** (`Corsage Note`, `note`, optional): Enter any notes about the corsage.
* **Corsage Status** (`Corsage Status`, `select one`, optional): Select the status of the corsage.
*   - **Delivered**
*   - **Not Delivered**

## Tips
- Please make sure to complete this form carefully and accurately.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the support team.
- This form is for creating a single corsage order. Do not complete this form multiple times for the same order.
