# Freight Booking Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Freight Booking Appointment Form is used to schedule appointments for freight pickups and deliveries. It gathers essential information from customers, including their pickup and delivery locations, dates, and times. This form helps our team manage freight bookings efficiently and ensures that all necessary details are collected accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the location where the freight will be picked up.
2. Enter the name of the location where the freight will be delivered.
3. Select the date of the pickup and delivery for the freight.
4. Select the time of the pickup and delivery for the freight.
5. Enter the name of the person booking the freight service.
6. Enter the email address of the customer for communication purposes.
7. Enter the phone number of the customer for contact purposes.
8. Enter any additional shipping details, such as special instructions or requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pick up location**: Enter the complete address where the freight will be picked up. This can include the building number, street name, city, state, and zip code.
* **Delivery location**: Enter the complete address where the freight will be delivered. This can include the building number, street name, city, state, and zip code.
* **Pick up date**: Select the date when you want the freight to be picked up. Please ensure that this date is within our business hours.
* **Pick up time**: Select the time when you want the freight to be picked up. Please ensure that this time is within our business hours.
* **Delivery date**: Select the date when you want the freight to be delivered. Please ensure that this date is within our business hours.
* **Delivery time**: Select the time when you want the freight to be delivered. Please ensure that this time is within our business hours.
* **Customer name**: Enter the name of the person booking the freight service.
* **Customer email**: Enter the email address of the customer for communication purposes. This is how we will contact you with updates and information about your booking.
* **Customer phone**: Enter the phone number of the customer for contact purposes.
* **Shipping details**: Enter any additional information about the shipping, such as special instructions or requirements.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately to prevent errors and ensure efficient processing of your booking.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please enter them in the "Shipping details" field.
* Our team will review your booking and contact you with any questions or concerns.
* If you need to make changes to your booking, please contact our customer service team.
