# Holiday Meal Planning Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The "Holiday Meal Planning Form" is designed to gather information about holiday meal preferences, guest count, dietary restrictions, and other essential details to plan and host a successful holiday event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the **Holiday Meal Preferences** section with your preferences for the holiday meal.
2. Enter the **Guest Count** for the expected number of attendees.
3. Select your **Dietary Restrictions**, if any, to help the host plan suitable options.
4. Choose your preferred **Meal Preferences** from the list.
5. Enter the **Date** and **Time** of the event.
6. Add any **Note** for the host.
7. Provide your **Email** and **Phone** contact information.
8. Enter your **Address** (if you're planning to send gifts or host the event at your location).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Holiday Meal Preferences** (Page 1, holiday_meal_preferences):
	+ This is the main section where you can express your meal preferences for the holiday event. 
* **Guest Count** (Page 1, guest_count):
	+ Enter the number of guests you expect to attend the holiday event.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (Page 1, dietary_restrictions):
	+ Select any dietary restrictions you have, such as Gluten-Free, Vegan, Vegetarian, or Kosher, to help the host plan suitable options.
* **Meal Preferences** (Page 1, meal_preferences):
	+ Choose your preferred meal options from the list, such as Turkey, Ham, Fish, Roast Beef, or Vegetarian.
* **Date** (Page 6, date):
	+ Enter the date of the holiday event.
* **Time** (Page 6, time):
	+ Enter the time of the holiday event.
* **Note** (Page 7, note):
	+ Add any notes or special instructions for the host.
* **Email** (Page 8, email):
	+ Enter your email address so the host can contact you for any further information.
* **Phone** (Page 8, phone):
	+ Enter your phone number for any last-minute communications.
* **Address** (Page 9, address):
	+ Enter your address if you plan to send gifts or host the event at your location.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when filling out the **Holiday Meal Preferences** section to ensure the host can plan a suitable meal.
* If you have any dietary restrictions, make sure to select them in the **Dietary Restrictions** section.
* Double-check the **Date** and **Time** before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* Enter your contact information, **Email** and **Phone**, so the host can reach you if needed.
* Provide your **Address** if you're planning to send gifts or host the event at your location.
