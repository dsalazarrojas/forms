# Refund Eligibility Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to check if a customer is eligible for a refund on an order. Please fill out the form with the required information to help us process your refund request.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with all the required fields.
2. Review your information for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order ID** (`order_id`, `text`, required): Enter the ID of the order you are requesting a refund for.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the order.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the customer who placed the order.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, `number`, required): Enter the total amount of the order.
* **Refund Reason** (`refund_reason`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the reason for the refund request.
* **Refund Amount** (`refund_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the amount of the refund you are requesting.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate processing of your refund request.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
* Please double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Our system will review your refund request and contact you with further instructions or updates.
