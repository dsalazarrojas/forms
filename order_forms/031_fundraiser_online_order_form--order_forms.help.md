# Fundraiser Online Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fundraiser Online Order Form is used to collect information from customers when making online orders or donations to a fundraiser. This form is designed to gather essential details for processing and fulfilling orders, as well as collecting donation information for a cause.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your order details in the "Order Form" field.
2. Provide a description of your order in the "Order Details" field.
3. Enter the total cost of your order in the "Total Cost" field.
4. Select your preferred payment method and order type from the "Payment Method" and "Order Type" fields.
5. If making a donation, enter the donation amount and select your donation frequency.
6. Enter your name and contact information, including email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Form** (1, text, required): Enter a brief description of your order.
* **Order Details** (2, text, required): Provide a detailed description of your order, including any specific requests or special instructions.
* **Order Summary** (3, note, required): Enter a summary of your order, including any relevant details.
* **Total Cost** (4, number, required): Enter the total cost of your order, including tax and shipping.
* **Payment Method** (5, select_one, required): Select one of the following payment methods:
	+ Cash
	+ Credit Card
	+ Check
* **Order Type** (6, select_multiple, required): Select one or both of the following order types:
	+ Donation
	+ Order
* **Donation Amount** (7, number, required for donations): Enter the amount of your donation, if applicable.
* **Donation Frequency** (8, select_one, required for donations): Select one of the following donation frequencies:
	+ One Time
	+ Ongoing
* **Donor Name** (9, text, optional): Enter your name if you're making a donation.
* **Contact Email** (10, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (11, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If making a donation, ensure that the donation amount and frequency are correct.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our support team for assistance.
