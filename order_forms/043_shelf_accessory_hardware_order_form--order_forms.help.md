# Shelf Accessory Hardware Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect general information and order details from customers when ordering shelf accessory hardware. It's meant to be a straightforward and efficient way to gather essential data from clients for the purpose of fulfilling their orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by providing general information about yourself and your order.
2.  Enter your contact details to ensure we can get in touch with you.
3.  Fill in the order details, including any specific products or services you need.
4.  Add payment information to complete the order.
5.  Calculate the total order value.
6.  Finally, provide any additional product or customer information if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional)
	* This field is an optional text input for general information about yourself and your order.
 
### 2. **Contact** (`contact`, text, optional)
	* This field is for your contact details, such as your name and phone number, to ensure we can get in touch with you.
 
### 3. **Order Info** (`order_info`, text, optional)
	* This field is used to input specific order details, like products or services needed.
 
### 4. **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, optional)
	* This field is for payment information, such as credit card or bank details.
 
### 5. **Order Total** (`order_total`, text, optional)
	* This field is used to calculate the total order value.
 
### 6. **Product Info** (`product_info`, text, optional)
	* This field is optional input for product specific information.
 
### 7. **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, optional)
	* This field is optional for customer specific information.
 
### 8. **Order Review** (`order_review`, text, optional)
	* This field is an optional review for the order.
 
## Tips
- Be as thorough as possible when filling out this form to ensure we can process your order correctly.
- Double-check your contact and payment details for accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
