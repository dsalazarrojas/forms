# Santa Photo Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Santa Photo Order Form is used to collect information for a photo shoot event or session. This form helps us understand the event's details and ensures that the photo shoot is conducted efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields with the requested information.
2. Make sure to provide all the necessary details to ensure accurate photo shoot arrangements.
3. Review the form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photo Order Form** (`photo_order_form`, text, optional): This field is used to provide a brief description of the photo shoot event or session. You can describe the purpose, occasion, or theme of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event when the photo shoot is scheduled to take place.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Input the start time of the event or photo shoot session.
* **Name Of Child** (`name_of_child`, text, optional): If the photo shoot is for a child, enter their name here.
* **Name Of Adult** (`name_of_adult`, text, optional): If the photo shoot is for an adult, enter their name here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address so we can contact you for updates or to confirm the photo shoot arrangements.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number in case we need to reach you for any reason.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Briefly describe the event or occasion for the photo shoot.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the event or occasion for the photo shoot.
* **Event Date/Time** (`event_date_time`, date, optional): Input the date and time of the event or photo shoot session.
* **Session Length** (`session_length`, number, optional): Indicate how long the photo shoot session will last.
* **Session Length (Holiday Special)** (`session_length_holiday_special`, number, optional): If the photo shoot session is a holiday special, indicate the length of the session.
* **Number Of Photos** (`number_of_photos`, number, optional): Specify the number of photos to be taken during the session.

**Tips**

* Make sure to review the form carefully before submission to avoid missing any important details.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
* We look forward to capturing your special moments!
