# KayleighsLipsenseOrderFormFINAL - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to provide their order details, payment information, and shipping addresses for Kayleigh's Lipsense product orders. It's essential to fill in all required fields accurately to ensure smooth processing of your order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and fill in the required information for a successful order submission.
2. If you're unsure about any field, please refer to this guide or contact our support team for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details (Field 1)** (`order_details`, `text`, optional): This field is for providing more information about your order, if needed. You can type any additional details about your order in this field.
* **Shipping (Field 2)** (`shipping_details`, `text`, optional): If you have any special shipping requests or instructions, please fill in this field.
* **Payment (Field 3)** (`payment_details`, `text`, optional): This field is for any payment-related information or questions you might have. Please note that payment information will be processed securely through our payment gateway.
* **Order Notes (Field 4)** (`order_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any order-specific notes or comments in this field, if needed.
* **Contact Number (Field 5)** (`contact_number`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to be contacted about your order, please provide your phone number or contact information.
* **Email (Field 6)** (`email`, `email`, optional): If you'd like to be contacted about your order via email, please provide your email address.
* **Order Type (Field 7)** (`order_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of order you're placing. Please choose from the available options: [Option 1], [Option 2], or [Option 3].
* **Shipping Address (Field 8)** (`shipping_address`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your shipping address from the available options: [Option 1], [Option 2], or [Option 3].
* **Order Comments (Field 9)** (`order_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any further comments or questions about your order in this field.
