# Salad Delivery Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Salad Delivery Order Form is designed to collect order information from customers for a salad delivery service. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter a valid email address in the "Customer Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
4. Select the type of salad you would like to order from the "Salad Name" dropdown menu.
5. Choose any additional protein options from the "Protein" dropdown menu (if desired).
6. Fill in the "Base Salad Price" field with the price of the salad (automatically filled in based on the selected salad).
7. Review and fill in the "Total Price" field, which will automatically be calculated based on the selected salad and added protein.
8. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (Text, Required): Enter your name for order processing purposes.
* **Customer Email** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address so we can contact you with order updates and delivery details.
* **Customer Phone** (Text, Required): Enter a phone number so we can contact you if needed for order delivery or updates.
* **Salad Name** (Select One, Optional): Choose your salad type from the available options.
* **Protein** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose any additional protein options to add to your salad.
* **Base Salad Price** (Number, Required): The price of the salad will automatically be filled in based on your salad selection.
* **Total Price** (Number, Required): This field will automatically be calculated based on the selected salad and added protein.
* **Payment Method** (Select One, Optional): Select how you would like to pay for your order, either by credit card or cash.
