# Popcorn Pickup Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Popcorn Pickup Registration form is designed for users to register their pickup information and confirm their order details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
2. Choose a pickup time and date that suits you.
3. Provide your school information, if applicable.
4. Select your order type (yes/no).
5. Confirm your email and phone number for communication.
6. Confirm that you have received an email or phone call about your order.
7. Confirm that you have been informed about your pickup details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, time, required): Select a time for your popcorn pickup.
* **Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`, date, required): Choose a date for your popcorn pickup.
* **School Information** (`school_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional school-related information, if necessary.
* **School ID** (`school_id`, number, optional): Enter your school ID, if you are a student or staff member.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your school.
* **Pickup Time Comment** (`pickup_time_comment`, text, optional): Add any comments or notes about your pickup time.
* **Policies** (`policies`, select_multiple, optional): Select any policies that apply to your order.
* **Policies Info** (`policies_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your policies selection.
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, select_one, required): Confirm whether you are a yes or no order.
* **Order Type Info** (`order_type_info`, text, optional): Add any additional information about your order type.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of popcorn you are ordering.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, email, required): Re-enter your email address for confirmation.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, text, optional): Re-enter your phone number for confirmation.
* **Confirm Email Sent** (`confirm_email_sent`, select_one, optional): Confirm whether you have received an email about your order.
* **Confirm Phone Sent** (`confirm_phone_sent`, select_one, optional): Confirm whether you have received a phone call about your order.
* **Pickup Confirmation** (`pickup_confirmation`, select_one, optional): Confirm that you have been informed about your pickup details.

## Tips
Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
