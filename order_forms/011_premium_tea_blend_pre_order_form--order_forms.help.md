# Premium Tea Blend Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Premium Tea Blend Pre Order Form is designed to help customers place orders for premium tea blends. This form collects necessary information to complete a transaction, including the customer's name, email, order date, total price, quantity, shipping address, payment method, product name and description, customer notes, phone number, and order status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the required fields.
2. Choose the date for your order.
3. Enter the total price for your order.
4. Specify the quantity of the premium tea blend you wish to order.
5. If you have a shipping address, fill it in. Otherwise, you can leave it empty.
6. Select your preferred payment method from Visa, Mastercard, or American Express.
7. Optionally, you can add a product name and description if you wish to customize your order.
8. Add any customer notes for our team to assist you with your order.
9. If you have a phone number, please fill it in so we can contact you if needed.
10. Choose the order status as New, Shipped, Delivered, or Cancelled.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (1, text, required): Please fill in your name as it will be displayed on your order confirmation and any correspondence.
* **Email** (2, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates on your order.
* **Order Date** (3, date, required): Choose the date for your order, this will help us schedule your order accordingly.
* **Total Price** (4, number, required): Enter the total price of your order.
* **Quantity** (5, number, required): Specify the quantity of the premium tea blend you wish to order.
* **Shipping Address** (6, text, optional): If you have a shipping address, please fill it in. This will help us get your order to you quickly and efficiently.
* **Payment Method** (7, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method from Visa, Mastercard, or American Express.
* **Product Name** (8, text, optional): If you have a specific product name, please let us know so we can look it up in our database.
* **Product Description** (9, text, optional): Add a product description to help us understand your order better.
* **Customer Notes** (10, text, optional): Add any customer notes that will help us assist you with your order.
* **Phone Number** (11, text, optional): If you have a phone number, please fill it in so we can contact you if needed.
* **Order Status** (12, select_one, required): Choose the order status as New, Shipped, Delivered, or Cancelled.

## Tips
* Please double-check your order details before submitting the form.
* Make sure to choose the correct payment method and order status.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
