# Custom Cufflink Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place custom orders for cufflinks. Fill out the form with your details to ensure your order is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields with your information. Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Custom Cufflink Order** (`custom_cufflink_order_form`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your custom cufflink order.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or passport.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where we can reach you for order updates or questions.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where we can contact you for order updates or questions.
* **Custom Message** (`custom_message`, text, optional): If you have any special requests or messages for the recipient, enter them here.
* **Custom Cufflink Preferences** (`custom_cufflink_preferences`, select_one, optional): Choose whether this order is for a custom cufflink or not.
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of order you are placing.
* **Gift Type** (`gift_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose any gift options you'd like to include with your order.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date for the order.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time for the order.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
