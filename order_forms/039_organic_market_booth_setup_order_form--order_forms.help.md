# Organic Market Booth Setup Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from vendors for organic market booth setup. It includes various fields to capture vendor details, payment terms, setup dates, and other related information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling in the **Vendor Information** field, providing your company name and contact details.
2. Select your **Booth Selections** preferences from the provided options.
3. Enter your **Setup Dates** for the market setup.
4. In the **Payment Details** field, provide any additional payment information.
5. Confirm your **Vendor Payment Terms** agreement.
6. Enter your **Vendor Address**, **Vendor Phone**, and **Vendor Email**.
7. Choose your **Payment Method** preference.
8. Enter the **Vendor Fee** and **Total Setup Fee**.
9. Select the **Payment Status** as Active or Inactive.
10. Add any **Vendor Notes** if necessary.
11. Choose the **Setup Type** as Yes or No.
12. Enter the **Setup Date** and **Setup Time**.
13. Provide any **Payment Terms**.
14. Select your **Vendor Status** as Active or Inactive.
15. Choose the **Booth Type** as Yes or No.
16. Enter the **Market Information**.
17. Enter the **Market Address** and **Market Hours**.
18. Enter the **Market Fee** and **Market Fee Details**.
19. Provide the **Vendor Information**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Information** (Text): Enter your company name and contact details.
* **Booth Selections** (Select Multiple): Choose your booth preferences.
* **Setup Dates** (Date): Enter the dates for your market setup.
* **Payment Details** (Text): Provide any additional payment information.
* **Vendor Payment Terms** (Select Multiple): Confirm your payment terms agreement.
* **Vendor Address** (Text): Enter your address for payment purposes.
* **Vendor Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Vendor Email** (Email): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Payment Method** (Select One): Choose your payment method preference.
* **Vendor Fee** (Number): Enter the vendor fee.
* **Total Setup Fee** (Number): Enter the total setup fee.
* **Payment Status** (Select One): Select Active or Inactive as the payment status.
* **Vendor Notes** (Note): Add any additional vendor notes.
* **Setup Type** (Select One): Choose Yes or No for setup type.
* **Setup Date** (Date): Enter the setup date.
* **Setup Time** (Time): Enter the setup time.
* **Payment Terms** (Text): Provide any additional payment terms.
* **Vendor Status** (Select One): Select Active or Inactive as the vendor status.
* **Booth Type** (Select One): Choose Yes or No for booth type.
* **Market Information** (Text): Enter market information.
* **Market Address** (Text): Enter the market address.
* **Market Hours** (Text): Enter the market hours.
* **Market Fee** (Number): Enter the market fee.
* **Market Fee Details** (Text): Enter market fee details.
* **Vendor Information** (Text): Provide additional vendor information.
