# Product And Equipment Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product And Equipment Agreement form is designed to collect information related to the sale or lease of a product or equipment. This form is intended for businesses and individuals who need to document the terms and conditions of a product or equipment sale or lease.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the relevant fields with the required information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure that you are providing accurate and complete information.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Product and Equipment Agreement Page 1
* **Product and Equipment Agreement**: (`product_and_equipment_agreement`, text, Optional) This field is for capturing the details about the product or equipment being sold or leased.
### Contact Information
* **Contact Information**: (`contact_information`, text, Optional) This field is for capturing the contact information of the person completing the form, typically the buyer or lessor.
### Product Details
* **Product Details**: (`product_details`, text, Optional) This field is for capturing the specific details about the product or equipment being sold or leased.
### Equipment Agreement
* **Equipment Agreement**: (`equipment_agreement`, text, Optional) This field is for capturing the terms of the agreement, including any specific conditions or warranties.
### Terms and Conditions
* **Terms and Conditions**: (`terms_and_conditions`, text, Optional) This field is for capturing the terms and conditions of the agreement, including any penalties or consequences for non-compliance.
### Agreement Status
* **Agreement Status**: (`agreement_status`, text, Optional) This field is for capturing the current status of the agreement, such as "Active" or "Inactive".
### Signature
* **Signature**: (`signature`, text, Optional) This field is for capturing the signature of the person completing the form, typically the buyer or lessor.
### Contact Person Signature
* **Contact Person Signature**: (`contact_person_signature`, text, Optional) This field is for capturing the signature of the contact person involved in the agreement.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have reviewed and understood the terms and conditions of the agreement before signing.
* Double-check the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
* Keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
* Update the form as necessary to reflect changes to the agreement.
