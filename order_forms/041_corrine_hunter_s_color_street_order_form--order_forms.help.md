# Corrine Hunters Color Street Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect orders from customers for products or services related to Color Street. It collects customer information and order details in a structured way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your **Customer Name** in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field.
3. Enter your **Email Address** in the "Email Address" field.
4. Select the color you'd like to order from the "Color Selection" dropdown menu.
5. Choose the **Order Date** for your order using the date picker.
6. Select the **Order Time** for your order using the time picker.
7. If you have any additional comments or instructions for your order, please enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification or as you would like to be addressed.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Color Selection** (`color_selection`, select_one, required/optional): Select the color you'd like to order from the available options.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required/optional): Choose the date you'd like to receive your order by using the date picker.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, required/optional): Select the time you'd like to receive your order using the time picker.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or instructions you may have for your order.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you enter accurate information to avoid any issues with your order.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact us for assistance.
