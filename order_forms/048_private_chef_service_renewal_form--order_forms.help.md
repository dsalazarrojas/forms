# Private Chef Service Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Private Chef Service Renewal Form is designed to collect necessary information from clients to efficiently manage the renewal process for private chef services. This form helps the client provide their details, allowing for a smooth and efficient renewal process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the requested information. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date details to ensure a successful renewal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (id: 1)** (`text`, **required: false**): Enter the client's name and basic details.
* **Subscription Information (id: 2)** (`text`, **required: false**): Describe the existing subscription or service agreement with the client, including any relevant details or changes.
* **Contact Information (id: 3)** (`select_multiple`, **required: false**): Select the contact information you wish to gather from the client, such as First Name, Last Name, Email, or Phone.
* **Additional Notes (id: 4)** (`note`, **required: false**): Provide any additional information or comments regarding the client's subscription renewal.
* **Date (id: 5)** (`date`, **required: false**): Enter the date you'd like to schedule the renewal.
* **Time (id: 6)** (`time`, **required: false**): Enter the time you'd like to schedule the renewal.
* **Email (id: 7)** (`email`, **required: false**): Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone (id: 8)** (`text`, **required: false**): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Address (id: 9)** (`text`, **required: false`): Enter the client's address.

## Tips
- Ensure accurate and up-to-date information is provided for a successful renewal process.
- If a field is optional, only fill it in if the information is required or relevant to the renewal process.
