# Tournament Catering Service Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tournament Catering Service Order Form is used by customers to place an order for catering services for a tournament event. It gathers information necessary to fulfill the order, including event date, number of guests, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. Select the correct options for the catering service and payment method.
3. Confirm that the order is accurate by checking the "Confirm Submit" box.
4. Review and confirm the payment information, including payment amount and method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Info
* **General Info**: A text field to enter any additional information about the order.
* **Tournament Name**: A text field to enter the name of the tournament.
* **Event Date**: A date field to enter the date of the tournament event.
* **Number of Guests**: A number field to enter the number of guests attending the tournament.
* **Contact Info**: A text field to enter contact information for the customer.

### Order Details
* **Catering Service**: A select_one field to select the type of catering service desired.
* **Special Request**: A text field to enter any special requests for the order.
* **Email**: An email field to enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone**: A text field to enter the customer's phone number.

### Payment Information
* **Payment Method**: A select_one field to select the payment method (Cash, Bank Transfer, Online Payment).
* **Payment Info**: A text field to enter additional payment information.
* **Payment Amount**: A number field to enter the total payment amount.
* **Confirm Payment**: A select_one field to confirm that the order is paid.
* **Confirm Payment Info**: A text field to enter any additional payment information.

### Confirmations
* **Confirm Submit**: A select_one field to confirm that the order is submitted.
* **Confirm Payment Date**: A date field to enter the payment date.
* **Confirm Payment Time**: A time field to enter the payment time.
* **Confirm Payment Amount**: A number field to enter the confirmed payment amount.
* **Privacy Policy**: A note field to acknowledge the privacy policy of the tournament catering service.
* **Terms and Conditions**: A note field to acknowledge the terms and conditions of the tournament catering service.

### Final Steps
* **Confirm Submit Date**: A date field to enter the date the order was submitted.
* **Confirm Submit Time**: A time field to enter the time the order was submitted.
* **Order Comments**: A text field to enter any comments or feedback about the order.
