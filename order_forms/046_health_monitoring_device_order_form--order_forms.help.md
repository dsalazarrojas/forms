# Health Monitoring Device Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Monitoring Device Order Form is designed to collect information from patients to place an order for a health monitoring device. This form is used to gather details such as patient information, device requirements, and billing information to process the order accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information as instructed by the healthcare professional.
2. Ensure you have the patient's consent and authorization to collect the necessary details.
3. Review and confirm the details before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Form** (`order_form`, `text`, required: false): This field is the main form where you will enter your order details.
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter the patient's basic information such as name, address, and contact details.
* **Device Info** (`device_info`, `text`, required: false): Describe the specific health monitoring device ordered.
* **Billing Info** (`billing_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter the billing details for the order.
* **Order Review** (`order_review`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select one or more options for this field as instructed by the healthcare professional.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the order placement.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the order placement.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the patient's name.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the patient's phone number.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are completed accurately and clearly.
* Double-check the form before submitting.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, do not hesitate to ask.
