# Equipment Asset Sale Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register equipment assets that are being sold. The purpose of this form is to collect information from both the seller and the buyer, including details about the equipment, sale price, and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the necessary information in the "sale_registration" field.
2. Provide the buyer's information in the "buyer_info" field.
3. Enter the equipment details in the "equipment_details" field.
4. Specify the sale price of the equipment in the "sale_price" field.
5. Select the sale status of the equipment in the "sale_status" field.
6. Enter the buyer's name in the "buyer_name" field.
7. Enter the buyer's email in the "buyer_email" field.
8. Select any additional sale status details in the "sale_status_details" field.
9. Specify whether you want to include seller details in the "seller_details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sale Registration** (`sale_registration`): Enter a brief description of the sale.
* **Buyer Information** (`buyer_info`): Provide the buyer's contact information.
* **Equipment Details** (`equipment_details`): Enter a brief description of the equipment being sold.
* **Sale Price** (`sale_price`): Enter the sale price of the equipment (in currency).
* **Sale Status** (`sale_status`): Select the current sale status (Active or Inactive).
* **Buyer Name** (`buyer_name`): Enter the buyer's name.
* **Buyer Email** (`buyer_email`): Enter the buyer's email address.
* **Sale Status Details** (`sale_status_details`): Select any additional sale status details (Active or Inactive).
* **Seller Details** (`seller_details`): Specify whether to include seller details (Yes or No).
