# Snack Bar Product Replenishment Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Snack Bar Product Replenishment Order Form is used to place an order for snack bar products. This form collects information to help us fulfill your order accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and fill out the form accordingly.
2. Make sure to select the correct product type and ship method for your order.
3. Provide a valid delivery date and time if applicable.
4. Enter a valid email and phone number for contact purposes.
5. Add any additional notes for our team to process your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details (1)**: Enter a brief description of your order.
* **Delivery Details (2)**: Enter the delivery date and time if you have selected a delivery option.
* **Select One Product Type (3)**: Choose the type of product you want to replenish.
* **Select Multiple Products (4)**: Choose multiple products for your order if applicable.
* **Delivery Date (5)**: Enter the date you would like to deliver the order.
* **Delivery Time (6)**: Enter the time you would like to deliver the order.
* **Select One Ship Method (7)**: Choose your preferred ship method.
* **Select Multiple Ship Methods (8)**: Choose multiple ship methods if applicable.
* **Notes (9)**: Add any additional notes for our team to process your order.
* **Select One Email (10)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Select Multiple Emails (11)**: Enter multiple email addresses if applicable.
* **Phone (12)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email (13)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Select One Note (14)**: Enter a note for your order.
* **Select Multiple Notes (15)**: Enter multiple notes if applicable.
* **Date (16)**: Enter the date you would like to deliver the order.
* **Time (17)**: Enter the time you would like to deliver the order.
* **Select One Delivery Date (18)**: Choose whether you want to deliver on a specific date.
* **Select Multiple Delivery Dates (19)**: Choose multiple delivery dates if applicable.
* **Select One Delivery Time (20)**: Choose whether you want to deliver at a specific time.
* **Select Multiple Delivery Times (21)**: Choose multiple delivery times if applicable.
* **Select One Phone (22)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Select Multiple Phones (23)**: Enter multiple phone numbers if applicable.
* **Select One Email 2 (24)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Select Multiple Emails 2 (25)**: Enter multiple email addresses if applicable.
* **Select One Phone 2 (26)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Select Multiple Phones 2 (27)**: Enter multiple phone numbers if applicable.
