## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Team Branded Water Jug Order Form, follow these steps:
1. On the first page, please enter your team's name and contact information (team email address).
2. On the second page, select the size of water jug you would like to order (Small, Large, or XL).
3. On the third page, enter the number of water jugs you would like to order.
4. On the fourth page, enter your team's email address for order confirmation.
5. On the fifth page, enter your team's phone number (optional).
6. On the sixth page, select the date and time you would like to order the jug for (MM/DD/YYYY HH:MM).
7. On the seventh page, confirm that the order is correct.
8. On the eighth page, select any additional options you would like to add (e.g., "Yes" or "No") for each option listed.
9. On the ninth page, select any team branded water jug options you would like to add ("Yes" or "No") for each option listed.
10. On the tenth page, confirm that this is the final order confirmation.
11. Finally, select any additional options you would like to add ("Yes" or "No") for each option listed.
