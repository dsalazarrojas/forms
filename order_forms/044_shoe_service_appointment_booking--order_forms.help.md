# shoe_service_appointment_booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book an appointment for shoe-related services. Fill out this form to schedule a time slot for a repair, polish, or resole service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your customer details, including name, contact preferences, and any other relevant information.
2. Select your preferred contact method (phone, email, or SMS).
3. Enter the schedule details for your appointment.
4. Provide additional notes if needed.
5. Choose the type of service required (repair, polish, or resole).
6. Select a service date and time.
7. Specify the type of shoe you want to be serviced (sneaker, sandal, or boot).
8. Enter the quantity of shoes to be serviced.
9. Enter the size of the shoe.
10. Enter a unique appointment ID (if you have it).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Customer details
* **Customer details**: This is the name field where you can enter your name.
### 2. Contact preferences
* **Contact preferences**: Here, select your preferred way to be contacted (phone, email, or SMS).
### 3. Schedule details
* **Schedule details**: Enter a brief description of your schedule requirements.
### 4. Additional notes
* **Additional notes**: Enter any further information that would be helpful for the service provider to know.
### 5. Service details
* **Service details**: Enter any specific details about the service you require.
### 6. Service type
* **Service type**: Select the type of service you need (repair, polish, or resole).
### 7. Service date
* **Service date**: Enter the date on which you want to receive the service.
### 8. Service time
* **Service time**: Enter the time at which you want to receive the service.
### 9. Shoe type
* **Shoe type**: Select the type of shoe you want to be serviced (sneaker, sandal, or boot).
### 10. Quantity
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of shoes you want to be serviced.
### 11. Size of shoe
* **Size of shoe**: Select the size of the shoe that needs service.
### 12. Appointment ID
* **Appointment ID**: Enter a unique ID for your appointment (if you have one).

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate information to ensure proper service.
* If you have any special requests or instructions, include them in the additional notes field.
* Review your information before submitting the form to ensure it's accurate and complete.
