# 1:1 Diet By CWP - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "1:1 Diet By CWP" form is used to collect information for customer orders, ensuring accurate and efficient processing and delivery of products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **Full name**, **Email address**, and **Phone number** in the corresponding fields.
2. Select a **Preferred delivery method** (Standard shipping, Express shipping, Pickup, or Local delivery).
3. Enter the **Order date** you'd like to receive the order.
4. Provide the **Item or product name**, **Quantity**, and **Unit price** for the order.
5. Choose your **Packaging preference** (Standard packaging, Eco-friendly packaging, or Gift packaging).
6. Select whether you'd like **Gift wrapping** (yes or no).
7. Enter your **Payment method** (Credit card, Cash, Bank transfer, PayPal, or Invoice).
8. Review and confirm the **Order total** (automatically calculated based on the submitted data).
9. Enter any **Additional comments or notes** if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name**: Enter your legal name for order processing and communication.
* **Email address**: We'll use this to send order confirmation and updates.
* **Phone number**: Optional, for delivery updates or any other communication.
* **Order date**: Date when the order is placed.
* **Item or product name**: Specify the exact product you want to order.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of items you'd like to order.
* **Unit price**: Price per item in your local currency.
* **Packaging preference**: Choose how you'd like the product packaged (Standard, Eco-friendly, or Gift).
* **Gift wrap**: Add gift wrapping for special occasions.
* **Preferred delivery method**: Choose how you'd like the product delivered (Standard shipping, Express shipping, Pickup, or Local delivery).
* **Delivery address**: Enter full address if shipping or local delivery.
* **Requested delivery date**: Preferred date for receiving your order.
* **Special instructions or customization notes**: Any special handling or customization requests.
* **Payment method**: Select how you will pay (Credit card, Cash, Bank transfer, PayPal, or Invoice).
* **Order total**: Automatically calculated based on submitted data.
* **Additional comments or notes**: Enter any extra information you'd like us to know.

Note: This help guide is designed to be comprehensive and clear, but if you have any specific questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
