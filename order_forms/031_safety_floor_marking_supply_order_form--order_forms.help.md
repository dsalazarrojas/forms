# Safety Floor Marking Supply Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from customers regarding their safety floor marking supply order. This form will help the customer provide their contact information, delivery details, and confirm their order details. It is a simple form that aims to collect relevant data in a user-friendly manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the following steps:

1. Fill out the **Customer Info** section (page 1) with your contact information.
2. Provide your delivery details in the **Delivery Details** section (page 1).
3. Confirm your order details in the **Confirm Details** and **Confirm Order** sections (pages 3 and 4).
4. Indicate whether this is a repeat order or not in the **Is this a repeat order?** section (page 5).
5. Finally, select the **Confirm** or **Cancel** option in the **Confirm** section (page 6) to finalize or abandon your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info (page 1)** (`customer_info`, text, required/optional): This is a field where you can enter your contact information, such as your name, email, and phone number.
* **Delivery Details (page 1)** (`delivery_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your delivery information, such as the address, delivery date, and any special instructions.
* **Confirm Details (page 3)** (`confirm_details`, select_one, required/optional): Please confirm the details of your order. If you have made a mistake, please select 'Yes' to correct it.
* **Confirm Order (page 4)** (`confirm_order`, select_one, required/optional): Confirm your order to proceed with the payment.
* **Is this a repeat order? (page 5)** (`repeat_order`, select_one, required/optional): If this is a repeat order, please select 'Yes' to indicate so. This will help our team process your order more efficiently.
* **Confirm (page 6)** (`confirm_button`, radio, required/optional): Finally, select 'Confirm' to finalize your order, or 'Cancel' to abandon it.

## Tips
* Double-check your information before confirming your order.
* If you are unsure about any of the details, please contact our team for assistance.
* If this is a repeat order, please ensure that you have the correct information filled out accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us and we will be happy to help.
