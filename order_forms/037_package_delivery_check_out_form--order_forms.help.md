# Package Delivery Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Package Delivery Check Out Form is a user-facing form designed to collect information from customers for efficient package delivery. This form is intended to ensure accurate and timely delivery of packages, allowing customers to communicate their delivery preferences and any special instructions for their packages.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of your package in the `package_id` field.
2. Provide your delivery information, including your delivery method (e.g., Delivery, Pickup, On-site) and any additional details in the `delivery_address`, `contact_phone`, and `contact_number` fields.
3. Enter your email address in the `email` field.
4. If required, confirm your signature for delivery in the `signature_confirmation` field.
5. Indicate your package type (e.g., Standard, Express, Economy) using the `package_type` field.
6. Provide any special instructions for your package in the `special_instructions` field.
7. Enter your preferred delivery time window using the `delivery_time_window` field.
8. Enter any delivery notes in the `delivery_note` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **delivery_info**: Enter information about your delivery, including the delivery method and any additional details.
	+ Enter the delivery method (e.g., Delivery, Pickup, On-site) in this field.
* **delivery_time**: Enter the expected delivery time for your package.
* **delivery_date**: Enter the date of delivery for your package.
* **package_id**: Enter the title of your package.
* **customer_name**: Enter your name.
* **shipping_method**: Enter your preferred shipping method (e.g., Delivery, Pickup, On-site).
* **delivery_address**: Enter the address where you would like to receive your package.
* **contact_phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **contact_number**: Enter your contact number (e.g., cell phone).
* **email**: Enter your email address.
* **package_weight**: Enter the weight of your package.
* **shipping_method_details**: Enter any additional details about your shipping method (e.g., tracking number).
* **signature_confirmation**: Confirm whether a signature is required for delivery.
* **notes**: Enter any additional notes about your package.
* **package_type**: Select the type of package (e.g., Standard, Express, Economy).
* **special_instructions**: Enter any special instructions for your package.
* **delivery_time_window**: Enter your preferred time window for delivery.
* **delivery_note**: Enter any additional delivery notes.

Note: This form is designed to be filled out by customers, so please enter accurate and complete information to ensure smooth and efficient delivery of your package.
