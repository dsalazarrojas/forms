# holiday_brunch_table_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The holiday brunch table booking form is designed to gather essential information from customers to facilitate a smooth and enjoyable experience at the holiday brunch event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact number in the "Guest Name" and "Contact Number" fields, respectively.
2. Choose a date for your booking from the calendar provided in the "Date" field.
3. Select a time slot for your booking from the available time slots in the "Time" field.
4. Indicate the number of people attending with you in the "Party Size" field.
5. Choose your preferred diet option from the "Preferred Diet" field.
6. If you have any special requests or preferences, enter them in the "Special Requests" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person booking the holiday brunch.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date you would like to book the holiday brunch.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Choose a time slot for your booking.
* **Party Size** (`party_size`, number, required): Indicate the number of people attending with you.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Preferred Diet** (`preferred_diet`, select_one, required): Select your preferred diet option (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free).
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): If you have any special requests or preferences, enter them here.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): If you have any special requests or preferences, enter them here.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you complete all required fields to ensure that we can process your booking successfully.
- If you have any specific dietary requirements, please select your preferred diet option from the list.
- If you have any special requests or preferences, please enter them in the "Special Requests" field for our team to take note.
