# Medical Equipment Rental Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for medical professionals or individuals to order medical equipment. Please fill out this form thoroughly and accurately to ensure that your medical equipment rental request is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Order Form Details** section (Page 1) with your name and contact information. This section is not required but recommended for our records.
2. On **Rental Request** (Page 2), select 'Yes' if you are requesting a rental, and 'No' otherwise. This will help us understand your rental needs.
3. In the **Payment Information** section (Page 3), enter your payment details. Please note that payment information is not required but recommended to expedite the processing of your order.
4. The **Confirmation** section (Page 4) is informational, and you can read our terms and conditions before proceeding.
5. In the **Contact Information** section (Page 5), enter your contact details, including name, phone number, and email.
6. The **Medical Equipment Rental Order Form** sections (Pages 6-17) are for selecting the specific medical equipment you need to rent. Each page asks if you need a particular type of equipment, and you can select 'Yes' or 'No' accordingly.
7. The **Order Details** section (Page 21) is for providing more detailed information about your order.
8. In the **Additional Information** section (Page 23), enter any additional comments or requests you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Form Details** (Page 1): This section is for entering your name and contact information. It's not required but recommended for our records.
	+ **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
	+ **Contact Information**: Enter your phone number and email address for us to contact you.
* **Rental Request** (Page 2): This section asks if you are requesting a rental. Select 'Yes' if you are, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Payment Information** (Page 3): This section is for entering your payment details. Although it's not required, it will help expedite the processing of your order.
	+ **Payment Details**: Enter your payment method and information.
* **Confirmation** (Page 4): This section is for reading our terms and conditions. Please take a moment to review before proceeding.
* **Contact Information** (Page 5): This section is for entering your contact details, including name, phone number, and email.
	+ **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
	+ **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
	+ **Email**: Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Medical Equipment Rental Order Form** (Pages 6-17): These sections are for selecting specific medical equipment you need to rent. Each page asks if you need a particular type of equipment, and you can select 'Yes' or 'No' accordingly.
* **Order Details** (Page 21): This section is for providing more detailed information about your order.
* **Additional Information** (Page 23): This section is for entering any additional comments or requests you may have.

Note: Some fields have identical options, and others are missing labels. I have provided clear explanations for each field based on the available information.
