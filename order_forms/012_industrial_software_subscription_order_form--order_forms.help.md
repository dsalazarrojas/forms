# Industrial Software Subscription Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Industrial Software Subscription Order Form is a tool for customers to provide necessary information to place an order for industrial software subscriptions. This form collects and verifies order data to ensure accurate processing and delivery of services.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the General Information section with your name and any additional information you'd like to provide.
2. In the Contact Information section, select the relevant contact details for the order (Company name, Contact Name, Phone Number, Email, or Company Address and City).
3. In the Order Details section, select a subscription plan (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually) and provide an order date and time.
4. Choose the payment type (Credit Card or PayPal) and fill out the amount field with the total order value.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (General Information, Text, Required/Optional): Enter your name and any other information you'd like to provide about yourself or your organization.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, Select Multiple, Required/Optional): Select the relevant contact details for the order. This may include:
  + Company name: The name of your company.
  + Contact Name: The name of the person handling the order.
  + Phone Number: The contact number for your organization.
  + Email: The email address to contact your organization.
  + Company Address: The address of your organization's location.
  + City: The city where your organization is located.
* **Name** (Name, Text, Required/Optional): Enter the name to be associated with the order.
* **Address** (Address, Text, Required/Optional): Enter the address associated with the order.
* **Contact Name** (Contact Name, Text, Required/Optional): Enter the name of the contact person handling the order.
* **Email** (Email, Email, Required/Optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone** (Phone, Text, Required/Optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Subscription Plan** (Subscription Plan, Select One, Required/Optional): Select the subscription plan you are interested in (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
* **Order Date** (Order Date, Date, Required/Optional): Enter the date of the order.
* **Order Time** (Order Time, Time, Required/Optional): Enter the time of the order.
* **Payment Type** (Payment Type, Select One, Required/Optional): Choose the payment method you will use to complete the order (Credit Card or PayPal).
* **Amount** (Amount, Number, Required/Optional): Enter the total amount of the order value.
## Tips
* Please make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure efficient order processing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team for assistance.
