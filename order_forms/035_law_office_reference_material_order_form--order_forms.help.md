# Law Office Reference Material Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Law Office Reference Material Order Form is designed to collect information for ordering reference materials such as legal reference books and court cases. This form is used to gather necessary details to process and fulfill the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the materials you wish to order from the list of options (Materials Requested).
2.  Enter the date you need the materials by (Date Needed).
3.  Enter your contact information for the person responsible for following up on the order.
4.  Enter the desired priority level for the order (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Order Form (Page 1)
* **Order Form** (`order_form`, text, not required): This is the main text area where you can enter any additional information related to your order.

### Materials Requested (Page 2)
* **Materials Requested** (`materials_requested`, select_multiple, not required): Use this field to select the type of materials you would like to order. Please choose from the available options: Legal Reference Book or Court Cases.

### Date Needed (Page 3)
* **Date Needed** (`date_needed`, date, required): Enter the date you need the materials by. This is a required field to ensure timely delivery.

### Contact Person (Page 4)
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, not required): Enter the name and title of the person responsible for following up on your order.

### Contact Email (Page 5)
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, not required): Enter the email address of the contact person.

### Contact Phone (Page 6)
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, not required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.

### Priority (Page 7)
* **Priority** (`priority`, select_one, not required): Select the priority level for your order: Low, High, or Urgent.

### Notes (Page 8)
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required): This is a text area where you can enter any additional comments or notes about your order.
