# Subscription Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Subscription Renewal Form is used to update and renew customer subscriptions. This form helps customers to choose a new plan, select a payment method, and set the subscription expiration date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your customer name in the "customer_name" field.
2. Provide your customer's email address in the "customer_email" field.
3. Select the current subscription status of your customer (Active, Inactive, or Trial) in the "subscription_status" field.
4. Enter the date when your customer's subscription is set to expire in the "subscription_expiration_date" field.
5. Choose your customer's new plan (Basic, Premium, or Enterprise) in the "new_plan" field.
6. Select the payment method for your customer's new subscription (Card, PayPal, or Stripe) in the "payment_method" field.
7. Enter the price of your customer's new plan in the "new_plan_price" field.
8. Set the date when your customer's new subscription is set to start in the "subscription_start_date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your customer's name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your customer's email address.
* **Subscription Status** (`subscription_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current subscription status of your customer (Active, Inactive, or Trial).
* **Subscription Expiration Date** (`subscription_expiration_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when your customer's subscription is set to expire.
* **New Plan** (`new_plan`, select_one, optional): Choose your customer's new plan (Basic, Premium, or Enterprise).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select the payment method for your customer's new subscription (Card, PayPal, or Stripe).
* **New Plan Price** (`new_plan_price`, number, optional): Enter the price of your customer's new plan.
* **Subscription Start Date** (`subscription_start_date`, date, optional): Set the date when your customer's new subscription is set to start.

## Tips
- Ensure you have permission to renew subscriptions before completing this form.
- Enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any errors.
- Review all fields carefully before submitting the form.
