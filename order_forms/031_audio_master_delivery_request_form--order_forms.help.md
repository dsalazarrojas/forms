> # Audio Master Delivery Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Audio Master Delivery Request Form is a form designed to collect information for the audio mastering process. This form is used to gather essential details from clients and artists to ensure accurate and efficient audio mastering delivery. Please ensure that all fields are filled out correctly to avoid delays or mistakes in the mastering process.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately with the required information.
2.  Ensure that you have the correct links and files attached.
3.  Review your submission before submitting to confirm that all fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): This field is used to collect the client's name, which is essential for identification and communication purposes.
*   **Artist Name** (`artist_name`, text, required): This field is used to collect the artist's name, which is also essential for identification and communication purposes.
*   **Project or Album Title** (`project_title`, text, required): This field collects the title of the project or album, which helps in organizing and tracking the audio files.
*   **Number of Tracks** (`track_count`, number, required): This field indicates the number of tracks included in the project or album.
*   **Target Loudness (LUFS)** (`target_loudness`, text, required): This field specifies the target loudness level for the audio mastering process, which helps in achieving the desired sonic quality.
*   **Sample Rate and Bit Depth** (`sample_rate_depth`, select_one, required): This field allows you to choose from various combinations of sample rates and bit depths for the audio files. Please select the best option for your project.
*   **Delivery Formats Required** (`delivery_formats`, select_multiple, required): In this field, select all the delivery formats you need (e.g., WAV, AIFF, MP3, DDP Image, Vinyl Pre-master) for your project.
*   **Link to Reference Tracks** (`reference_tracks`, text, optional): If you have reference tracks that you'd like us to emulate, please provide links to them.
*   **Required Delivery Deadline** (`delivery_deadline`, date, required): This field is used to specify the deadline for the delivery of the mastered audio files.
*   **Link to Mix Files** (`mix_file_link`, text, required): Please provide a link to the mix files for us to work with.
*   **Specific Mastering Notes** (`additional_mastering_notes`, text, optional): If you have any specific instructions or requests for the mastering process, please include them here.
*   **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): This field is used for communication and invoicing purposes.

Note: Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly to avoid delays or mistakes in the mastering process. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
