The Gift Basket Order Form Template is designed to collect information for a gift order. It guides the user through a series of steps to ensure the order is accurate and complete.

## Purpose
This form is used to collect order details, customer information, shipping information, payment, gift details, and order summary for a gift order.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Order Details**: Enter the order details, such as gift information (if applicable).
2. **Customer Information**: Enter your name, email, phone number, and address as the customer.
3. **Shipping Information**: Enter the recipient's name, email, phone number, and address.
4. **Payment**: Select the payment method (PayPal or Credit card).
5. **Gift Details**: Enter gift details, such as gift card number (if applicable).
6. **Order Summary**: Review the order summary to ensure everything is accurate.
7. **Gift Basket Products**: Enter the gift basket products.
8. **Gift Basket Total**: Enter the gift basket total price.
