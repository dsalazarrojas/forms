# Artwork Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers place orders for art pieces with specific preferences for size, frame, color, and other customizations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details: Enter your first name, last name, email address, phone number, shipping address, city, state or province, and zip or postal code.
2. Specify the artwork: Enter the title or name of the artwork you want to order, and provide a brief description of what you want to order.
3. Choose your preferences: Select the size or format of the artwork, frame or mounting option, and preferred printing method.
4. Confirm your order: Enter the number of pieces you want to order and the unit price per piece.
5. Payment and delivery: Select your preferred payment method and the requested delivery date.
6. Add any special instructions: If you have any special instructions or notes for the order, enter them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name. Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Your last name. Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: The email address we will use to contact you and confirm your order.
* **Phone Number**: The phone number we will use to communicate with you and track your order.
* **Shipping Address**: The address where you want the artwork to be shipped to.
* **City**: The city where the artwork is to be shipped to.
* **State or Province**: The state or province where the artwork is to be shipped to.
* **Zip or Postal Code**: The zip or postal code for shipping purposes.
* **Artwork Title or Name**: The title or name of the artwork you want to order.
* **Artwork Description**: A brief description of what you want to order.
* **Quantity**: The number of pieces you want to order.
* **Size or Format**: The size or format of the artwork, which can be selected from available options or custom dimensions provided.
* **Custom Dimensions (if applicable)**: Optional custom dimensions for the artwork, if not using a standard size.
* **Frame or Mounting Option**: The frame or mounting option for the artwork, which can be selected from available options.
* **Color Preferences or Variations**: Any color preferences or variations you want for the artwork.
* **Preferred Printing Method**: The preferred printing method for the artwork.
* **Unit Price**: The price per unit of the artwork.
* **Total Order Price**: The total price for the order.
* **Requested Delivery Date**: The date you want the artwork delivered by.
* **Payment Method**: The payment method you want to use.
* **Special Instructions or Notes**: Any special instructions or notes you have for the order.

## Tips

* Please make sure to double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* You can always contact us if you have any questions or need further clarification on any field.
* We will be in touch with you soon after receiving your order to confirm the details and answer any questions you may have.
