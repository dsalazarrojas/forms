# Artwork Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers place orders for art pieces with specific preferences for size, frame, color, and other customizations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details: Enter your first name, last name, email address, phone number, shipping address, city, state or province, and zip or postal code.
2. Specify the artwork: Enter the title or name of the artwork you want to order, and provide a brief description of what you want to order.
3. Choose your preferences: Select the size or format of the artwork, frame or mounting option, and preferred printing method.
4. Confirm your order: Enter the number of pieces you want to order and the unit price per piece.
5. Payment and delivery: Select your preferred payment method and the requested delivery date.
6. Add any special instructions: If you have any special instructions or notes for the order, enter them here.
