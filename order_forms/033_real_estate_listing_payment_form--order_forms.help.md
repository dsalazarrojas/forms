# Real Estate Listing Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Listing Payment Form is used to collect payment information from clients for real estate listings. This form helps to efficiently process payments and keep track of payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the real estate listing in the **Real Estate Listing Payment Form** field.
2. Fill in the **Client Name** field with the client's name.
3. Select the **Package Type** for the payment from the available options.
4. Enter the **Property Price** for the listing.
5. Enter the **Down Payment** amount.
6. Choose the **Payment Method** from the available options.
7. Enter the **Property Address** of the listing.
8. (Optional) Enter any additional **Property Details** about the listing.
9. If applicable, enter the **Package Price** and **Package Discount** amounts.
10. Select the **Payment Status** from the available options (if applicable).
11. Enter the **Payment Terms** for the payment.
12. Enter the client's **Client Email** and **Client Phone**.
