# restaurant_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for restaurants to take orders from customers and manage their orders. It collects information such as pickup location, pickup time, order notes, customer name, email, phone, delivery option, payment method, online options, offline options, delivery address, delivery instructions, order ID, restaurant ID, created at, updated at, deleted at, user ID, form ID, online total, offline total, and delivery status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your pickup location in the "Pickup Location" field.
2. Select your preferred pickup time from the "Pickup Time" field.
3. Add any additional notes about the order in the "Order Notes" field.
4. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Select your preferred delivery option from the "Delivery Option" field (e.g., Delivery, Pickup).
8. Choose your payment method from the "Payment Method" field (e.g., Online, Offline).
9. If paying online, select your preferred online payment option from the "Online Options" field (e.g., Card Number, PayPal).
10. If paying offline, select your preferred offline payment option from the "Offline Options" field (e.g., Cash, Bank Transfer).
11. Enter any additional notes for online payment in the "Online Note" field.
12. Enter any additional notes for offline payment in the "Offline Note" field.
13. Enter your delivery address in the "Delivery Address" field.
14. Add any delivery instructions in the "Delivery Instructions" field.
15. Enter the order ID in the "Order ID" field.
16. Enter the restaurant ID in the "Restaurant ID" field.
17. Select the date and time the order was created from the "Created At" field.
18. Select the date and time the order was last updated from the "Updated At" field.
19. Select the date and time the order was deleted from the "Deleted At" field.
20. Enter the user ID in the "User ID" field.
21. Enter the form ID in the "Form ID" field.
22. Enter the online total in the "Online Total" field.
23. Enter the offline total in the "Offline Total" field.
24. Select the delivery status from the "Delivery Status" field (e.g., Pending, Shipped).
