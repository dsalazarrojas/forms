# Factory Soundproof Panel Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture information for the "Factory Soundproof Panel Order Form" used by customers to place orders for soundproof panels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Select the date you want the soundproof panels to be delivered on the **delivery_date** field.
2. Enter any relevant notes about the installation in the **installation_notes** field.
3. If there are additional delivery details, enter them in the **delivery_details_1** and **delivery_details_2** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **delivery_date** (Date): The date you want the soundproof panels to be delivered on.
* **installation_notes** (Note): Any notes about the installation, such as specific installation requirements or access restrictions.
* **delivery_details_1** (Number): Enter any additional delivery details, such as room numbers or floor levels.
* **delivery_details_2** (Number): Enter any further delivery details, such as specific delivery notes or comments.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the delivery date accurately to ensure timely delivery of the soundproof panels.
* Use the installation notes field to provide any relevant details about the installation, such as access requirements or contact information for the site supervisor.
* Use the additional delivery details fields to provide any other relevant information that will aid in the delivery and installation of the soundproof panels.
