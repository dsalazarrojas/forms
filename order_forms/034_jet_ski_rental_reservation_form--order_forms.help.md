# Jet Ski Rental Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for jet ski rental reservations. It helps the rental service to understand the customer's needs and preferences for a smooth and efficient rental experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Customer Name** in the text field provided.
2. Select the **Start Date** and **End Date** for your rental period.
3. Choose your preferred **Jet Ski Model** from the list:
	* Kawasaki
	* Sea-Doo
	* Yamaha
4. Select the desired **Location** for your rental:
	* Beach
	* Lake
	* River
5. Choose your **Payment Method**:
	* Card
	* Cash
6. Enter your **Customer Email** for communication purposes.
7. Provide your **Customer Phone** number for easy contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: A field to input your name so we can address you accordingly.
* **Start Date**: The date you'd like to start your rental period.
* **End Date**: The date your rental period ends.
* **Jet Ski Model**: Please select your preferred jet ski model from the provided options.
* **Location**: Choose where you'd like to rent your jet ski (beach, lake, river, etc.).
* **Payment Method**: Select the method you'd like to use for payment (card or cash).
* **Customer Email**: A field to input your email address for communication purposes.
* **Customer Phone**: A field to input your phone number for easy contact.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your details before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask our staff for assistance.
* Our team will review your submission and be in touch with you shortly to finalize your reservation.
