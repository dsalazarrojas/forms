# Comprehensive Menu Selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Comprehensive Menu Selection form is designed to collect all necessary information for a catering order, ensuring that the customer's needs are met accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the menu package that suits your needs.
2. Enter your contact name and contact phone number.
3. Choose the event date.
4. Provide the estimated number of guests.
5. List any dietary restrictions or allergies.
6. Add any special delivery instructions or requests.
7. Review and submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact name** (Contact name, text, required): Enter the name of the person placing the order.
* **Contact phone** (Contact phone, text, required): Provide a phone number for the day of the event in case we need to contact you.
* **Event date** (Event date, date, required): Choose the date the catering is required.
* **Number of guests** (Number of guests, number, required): Enter the estimated headcount for the event.
* **Menu package** (Menu package, select_one, required): Choose from Standard, Premium, Vegetarian, or Custom menu package options.
* **Dietary restrictions** (Dietary restrictions, text, optional): List any allergies or restrictions you or your guests may have.
* **Delivery instructions** (Delivery instructions, text, optional): Provide any special instructions for delivery, including setup location and time.
* **Special requests** (Special requests, text, optional): Add any other preferences or requests you may have.
* **-- Catering Order --** (section_catering_info, note, optional): This section is a note field for collecting menu selections and dietary needs. You can add any additional comments or questions here.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate information for a smooth and efficient delivery process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
