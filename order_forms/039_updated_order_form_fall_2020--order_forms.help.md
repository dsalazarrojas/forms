> # Updated Order Form Fall 2020 - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect customer information for orders in a simple and efficient way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Fall 2020 Order Form** field with the necessary information.
2. Provide your **Customer Info**, including any relevant details about the customer.
3. Enter your **Customer Address**, including the street address, city, state, and zip code.
4. Enter the **Customer Name**, including the first and last names of the customer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Fall 2020 Order Form** (`Fall 2020 Order Form`, text, optional): This field is for providing a brief description of the order.
* **Customer Info** (`Customer Info`, text, optional): This field is for collecting any relevant information about the customer, such as order notes or special requests.
* **Customer Address** (`Customer Address`, text, optional): This field is for collecting the customer's address, including the street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Customer Name** (`Customer Name`, text, optional): This field is for collecting the customer's full name, including the first and last names.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate information for the **Customer Address** field, as this will be used for shipping purposes.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can always contact the form creator for assistance.
* The form is designed to be used in any industry, so feel free to customize it as needed.
