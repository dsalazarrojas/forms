# Not Profit Plant Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture the details of a plant order. Please fill it out accurately and completely for efficient processing.
### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill out all required fields. Fields marked as **required** (denoted by **\*** or bold text in field descriptions) must be filled before submission.
- Enter your first and last name correctly to ensure proper identification.
- Enter a valid email address in the format of `yourname@yourdomain.com` or `yourname@yourisp.net`.
- For the phone number field, enter the number in the format of `XXX-XXXX-XXXX` or simply `XXX-XXXX` for international formats.
- Select one of the listed options for Plant Type.
- Enter the quantity of plants you want to order.
- Fill out the Address, City, and Zip fields with your correct address details.
- Select the States you would like to deliver or pick up your plant order.
- Enter any additional comments or instructions in the comments field, if required.
- Finally, submit the form to send it to the processing team for further action.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, \*): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, \*): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, \*): Enter a valid email address in the format of `yourname@yourdomain.com` or `yourname@yourisp.net`.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, ): This is the phone number field. Enter the number in the format of `XXX-XXXX-XXXX` or simply `XXX-XXXX` for international formats.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, \*): Enter the date when you would like to receive or pick up your plant order.
* **Plant Type** (`plant_type`, `select_one`, \*): Select one of the listed options for the type of plant you want to order.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, \*): Enter the number of plants you want to order.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, \*): Enter your correct address details for delivery or pickup.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, \*): Enter the city where you are located or would like to receive your plant order.
* **State** (`state`, `select_multiple`, \*): Select the states where you would like to deliver or pick up your plant order.
* **Zip** (`zip`, `text`, \*): Enter your zip code for correct delivery or pickup.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, ): This field is optional and can be used for additional comments or instructions.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, ): This field is optional and is used to assign a tool to the order.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, `text`, ): This field is optional and is used to assign a unique ID to the order.
* **Category** (`category`, `text`, ): This field is optional and is used to categorize the order.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, ): This field is optional and is used for additional description of the order.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, ): This field is optional and is used to identify who submitted the order.
