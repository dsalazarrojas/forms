# Prescription Glass Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a simple order form for prescription glass products. It allows customers to input their information and confirm their orders.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your details in the "User Input" field (Field 1).
2. Select the product(s) you wish to order from the "Product Selection" field (Field 2).
3. Provide shipping details in the "Shipping Details" field (Field 3).
4. Enter your payment information in the "Payment Info" field (Field 4).
5. Confirm your order in the "Order Confirmation" field (Field 5).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input (Field 1)**:
	+ A text field where you can enter your name, email address, or any other relevant information.
* **Product Selection (Field 2)**:
	+ A text field where you can enter the name or code of the product(s) you wish to order.
* **Shipping Details (Field 3)**:
	+ A text field where you can enter your shipping address, city, or any other shipping-related information.
* **Payment Info (Field 4)**:
	+ A text field where you can enter your payment method and any relevant payment details.
* **Order Confirmation (Field 5)**:
	+ A text field where you can review and confirm your order details.

## Tips

- Please make sure to fill out all required fields correctly to ensure a smooth order process.
- If you're unsure about any field, you can always ask our customer support for assistance.
- Double-check your order details before confirmation to avoid errors.
