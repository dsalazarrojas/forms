# Restaurant Safety Certification Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Safety Certification Order Form is a critical part of the certification process, which helps restaurants meet the necessary health and safety standards. This form is used to order a restaurant safety certification package, which is required for restaurants to operate.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the desired package from the "Package Selection" field. This will determine the type and scope of the certification package you are ordering.
2. Enter your basic business information in the "Basic Business Info" field. This includes your restaurant name, address, and contact information.
3. In the "Order Details" field, provide a description of your order, including any special requests or requirements.
4. Select the date and time you would like to schedule the order in the "Order Date" and "Order Time" fields.
5. Enter the total price of the order in the "Total Price" field.
6. Provide payment information in the "Payment Info" field.
7. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Package Selection** (`Package Selection`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type and scope of the certification package you require.
	+ Select one of the available options (Safety Package 1, Safety Package 2, or Safety Package 3) to determine the level of certification you need.
* **Basic Business Info** (`Basic Business Info`, `text`, optional): Enter your restaurant's basic information.
	+ This includes your restaurant name, address, and contact information.
* **Order Details** (`Order Details`, `text`, required): Provide a description of your order.
	+ This is where you can add any special requests or requirements for your order.
* **Order Date** (`Order Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you would like to schedule the order.
	+ This is the date you would like to complete the certification process.
* **Order Time** (`Order Time`, `time`, required): Select the time you would like to schedule the order.
	+ This is the time you would like to complete the certification process.
* **Total Price** (`Total Price`, `number`, required): Enter the total price of the order.
	+ This is the cost of the certification package you are ordering.
* **Payment Info** (`Payment Info`, `text`, required): Provide payment information for the order.
	+ This includes any payment details or methods you would like to use.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
	+ This is where you can add any additional information or clarification about your order.

## Tips

* Make sure to select the correct package that meets your restaurant's needs.
* Double-check your basic business information for accuracy.
* Be specific when describing your order details.
* Choose a date and time that works for your certification process.
* Enter the correct total price for the order.
* Provide clear payment information to avoid any delays in processing your order.
