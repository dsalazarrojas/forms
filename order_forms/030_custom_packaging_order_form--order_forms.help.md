# Custom Packaging Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Packaging Order Form is designed to collect customer and order information for custom packaging services. This form will help you create and manage custom packaging orders efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "customer-info" section with your contact details.
2. Describe the "order-details" and specify the "order-date" and "order-time" for the order.
3. Provide "shipping-address" information for the order.
4. Specify the "payment-details" and choose your preferred "payment-gateway" (if applicable).
5. Choose the "packaging-materials" and "custom-packaging-options" that suit your needs.
6. Confirm whether you want to use a "payment-gateway" and specify the type of "packaging-type" needed.
7. Provide your "customer-phone" and "customer-email" for communication purposes.
8. Optionally, add any additional "order-note" if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Custom Packaging Specifications** (`custom-packaging-specifications`): This field is for specifying the custom packaging requirements and specifications for your order.
* **Order Details** (`order-details`): Describe the order details, including the type of packaging needed and any special requirements.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping-address`): Enter your shipping address to ensure timely and efficient delivery of your custom packaging.
* **Payment Details** (`payment-details`): Provide your payment information for the order.
* **Payment Gateway** (`payment-gateway`): Choose whether you want to use a payment gateway for this order (if applicable).
* **Packaging Materials** (`packaging-materials`): Select the materials you want to use for your custom packaging.
* **Custom Packaging Options** (`custom-packaging-options`): Choose any additional custom packaging options you need.
* **Customer Info** (`customer-info`): Provide your contact information, including your phone and email, for us to get in touch with you.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer-phone`): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Customer Email** (`customer-email`): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Order Date** (`order-date`): Enter the date when you would like to receive your custom packaging.
* **Order Time** (`order-time`): Enter the time when you would like to receive your custom packaging.
* **Order Note** (`order-note`): Provide any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Packaging Type** (`packaging-type`): Choose the type of packaging you need.
* **Packaging Yes/No** (`packaging-type`): This field is a confirmation of the packaging type chosen.
* **Yes/No Payment Gateway** (`payment-gateway`): Confirm whether you want to use a payment gateway. 

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* We will contact you if we need any additional information or clarification.
