# Nails With Liz Wish List - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a user's preferred nail characteristics, including color, type, length, and shape.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred color from the options provided in the "What is your preferred color?" section.
2. If you prefer a specific nail type, select the corresponding option in the "What type of nail?" section. If you don't have a preference, simply leave this field blank.
3. If you prefer a specific nail length, enter your selection in the "What is your preferred nail length?" field. This field is not required, so you can leave it blank if you don't have a preference.
4. If you prefer a specific nail width, enter your selection in the "What is your preferred nail width?" field. This field is not required, so you can leave it blank if you don't have a preference.
5. Select your preferred nail shape from the options provided in the "What shape do you like your nails to be?" section.
6. If you have a specific type of nail in mind, enter it in the "What type of nail do you like?" field. This field is not required, so you can leave it blank if you don't have a preference.
7. Select your preferred nail length and shape for the second set of nails, if you have one, from the corresponding fields.
8. Finally, enter any additional information about your nail preferences in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is your preferred color?** (`color_stickynote`, `select_one`):
This field allows you to select your preferred color from the following options: Black, Red, Blue.
- **What type of nail?** (`nail_type`, `select_multiple`):
Select the types of nails you prefer. This field allows you to choose from multiple options, if any.
- **What is your preferred nail length?** (`nail_length`, `text`):
Enter your preferred nail length, or leave it blank if you don't have a preference.
- **What is your preferred nail width?** (`nail_width`, `text`):
Enter your preferred nail width, or leave it blank if you don't have a preference.
- **What shape do you like your nails to be?** (`nail_shape`, `select_one`):
Select the shape of your nails from the following options: Oval, Round.
- **What type of nail do you like?** (`nail_nail_type`, `select_multiple`):
Select the types of nails you prefer. This field is not required, so you can leave it blank if you don't have a preference.
- **Nail Nail Length** (`nail_nail_length`, `select_one`):
Select the length of your preferred nail from the following options: Short, Medium, Long.
- **Nail Nail Width** (`nail_nail_width`, `select_one`):
Select the width of your preferred nail from the following options: Narrow, Wide.
- **Nail** (`nail_nail_shape`, `select_one`):
Select the shape of your preferred nail from the following options: Oval, Round.
- **Nail** (`nail_nail_shape_2`, `select_one`):
Select the shape of your second preferred nail from the following options: Oval, Round.
- **Nail Nail Length 2** (`nail_nail_length_2`, `text`):
Enter the length of your second preferred nail, or leave it blank if you don't have a preference.
- **Nail Nail Shape 2** (`nail_nail_shape_2`, `select_one`):
Select the shape of your second preferred nail from the following options: Oval, Round.
- **Nail** (`nail_special_nail`, `text`):
Enter any additional information about your nail preferences.
- **Nail** (`nail_nail_length_2`, `text`):
Enter any additional information about your second preferred nail.
