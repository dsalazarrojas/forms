# left_handed_golf_grip_training_aids_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from customers who are interested in ordering a left-handed golf grip training aid.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the type of training aid you need from the list provided.
2.  Enter your shipping address to ensure your training aid is delivered to the correct location.
3.  Provide your customer details in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Select your training aid**: Choose the type of training aid you need for your left-handed golf grip from the list below:
    *   aid type 1
    *   aid type 2
    *   aid type 3
*   **shipping_address**: Enter your shipping address to ensure your training aid is delivered to the correct location.
*   **Customer details**: Provide any additional information about yourself that you believe will be helpful for us to process your order correctly.

## Tips

*   If you are unsure about any of the information required, please don't hesitate to contact us.
*   Ensure you enter the correct shipping address to avoid any delivery issues.
*   If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the customer details field.
