# Payment Plan Adjustment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payment Plan Adjustment Request Form is used to request changes to a customer's payment plan. This form gathers information about the customer's payment plan and the reasons for the adjustment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Customer Name
2. Enter your Payment Plan ID (if known)
3. Describe the Requested Change
4. Specify the New Payment Terms (optional)
5. Provide a Reason for the Request (optional)
6. Sign and date the form
7. Enter your Email and Phone Number (optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Name
* **Customer Name**: Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification document.
* This field is optional, but it is required for proper identification.

### Payment Plan ID
* **Payment Plan ID**: Enter the number of your existing payment plan (if known). This is not required to complete the form, but it may be helpful to speed up the processing of your request.
* This field is a numeric value.

### Requested Change
* **Requested Change**: Describe the changes you would like to make to your payment plan. Be as detailed as possible.
* This field is optional, but it's recommended to provide a clear understanding of your request.

### New Payment Terms
* **New Payment Terms**: If you are requesting changes to your payment plan, enter the new terms you would like to apply. This is optional.
* This field is only applicable if you are requesting changes to your payment plan.

### Reason for Request
* **Reason for Request**: Provide a brief explanation for your request. This is optional, but it helps us to understand your circumstances.
* This field is optional.

### Customer Signature
* **Customer Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm that you are making the request.
* This field is optional, but it's required for formal submission.

### Date of Request
* **Date of Request**: Enter the date you are making the request.
* This field is optional, but it's recommended for proper record keeping.

### Time of Request
* **Time of Request**: Enter the time you are making the request.
* This field is optional.

### Email
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your request.
* This field is optional.

### Phone
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your request.
* This field is optional.
