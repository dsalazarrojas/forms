# Audio Cable Supply Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to book audio cables for events or activities. Please provide your details to ensure a smooth and efficient booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your contact details, including your name, email, and phone number, so we can get in touch with you to confirm your booking.
2. Provide the event name and schedule details to help us understand your needs.
3. Specify the audio cables you need, including quantities for each type.
4. Choose your delivery preference, whether you want to pick up the equipment locally or have us set it up on-site.
5. Confirm that you accept responsibility for lost or damaged equipment.
6. Sign and date the agreement to confirm your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_name`): Your full name as the contact person for this booking.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Yes
* **Company or Organization Name** (`company_org_name`): If you're representing a company or organization, please enter their name.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address so we can contact you about the booking.
	+ Type: Email
	+ Required: Yes
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`): Your phone number for any further communication.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Yes
* **Event Name** (`event_name`): Name of the event or activity you're booking the equipment for.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Yes
* **Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`): Date you want to collect the equipment.
	+ Type: Date
	+ Required: Yes
* **Return Date** (`return_date`): Date you plan to return the equipment.
	+ Type: Date
	+ Required: Yes
* **XLR Cables (Microphone)** (`xlr_quantity`): Enter the number of XLR cables you need.
	+ Type: Number
	+ Required: No
* **1-4 inch TRS Cables (Instrument)** (`trs_quantity`): Enter the number of 1-4 inch TRS cables you need.
	+ Type: Number
	+ Required: No
* **HDMI Cables** (`hdmi_quantity`): Enter the number of HDMI cables you need.
	+ Type: Number
	+ Required: No
* **PowerCon/IEC Cables** (`power_con_quantity`): Enter the number of PowerCon/IEC cables you need.
	+ Type: Number
	+ Required: No
* **Stage Snake Needed?** (`stage_snake_needed`): Choose if you need a stage snake.
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Required: Yes
* **Delivery Preference** (`delivery_preference`): Choose your preferred delivery option.
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Required: Yes
* **Damage Waiver** (`damage_waiver`): Confirm that you accept responsibility for lost or damaged equipment.
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Required: Yes
* **Date of Agreement** (`signature_date`): Sign and date the agreement to confirm your booking.
	+ Type: Date
	+ Required: Yes
