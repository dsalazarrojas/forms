# Jerseys And Hoodies Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for customers to place orders for jerseys and hoodies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields: Name, Email, Phone, Type, Color, Size, Quantity, Order Number, Order Date, Order Time, and Order Status.
2. Select the appropriate type and color of the jersey and hoodie you want to order.
3. Provide order notes if needed.
4. Choose your payment method.
5. Review and submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`customer_details_1`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your ID.
* **Email** (`customer_details_2`, email, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`customer_details_3`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number (optional).
* **Type** (`jersey_details_1`, select_one, required): Select the type of jersey you want to order.
* **Color** (`jersey_details_2`, select_multiple, required): Select the color of the jersey you want to order.
* **Size** (`hoodie_details_1`, text, required): Please enter the size of the hoodie you want to order.
* **Quantity** (`hoodie_details_2`, number, required): Please enter the quantity of hoodies you want to order.
* **Order Summary** (`order_summary`, text, required): Please provide a brief description of your order.
* **Order Notes** (`order_summary_1`, text, optional): Please provide any additional notes about your order (optional).
* **Total Items** (`order_summary_2`, number, optional): Please enter the total number of items in your order (optional).
* **Total Price** (`order_summary_3`, number, optional): Please enter the total price of your order (optional).
* **Total Discount** (`order_summary_4`, number, optional): Please enter the total discount on your order (optional).
* **Total Amount Due** (`order_summary_5`, number, required): Please enter the total amount due for your order.
* **Payment Method** (`order_summary_8`, select_one, required): Please select your payment method.
* **Order Number** (`order_summary_7`, note, optional): Your order number will be provided after you submit your order.
* **Order Date** (`order_summary_3`, date, required): The date and time of your order will be provided after you submit your order.
* **Order Time** (`order_summary_4`, time, required): The date and time of your order will be provided after you submit your order.
* **Order Status** (`order_summary_5`, select_one, required): The status of your order will be updated after you submit your order.

## Tips
- Please fill in all required fields before submitting your order.
- Double-check your order details before submitting.
- If you have any issues or questions, contact our customer support team.
