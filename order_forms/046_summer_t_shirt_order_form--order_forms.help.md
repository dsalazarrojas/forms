# Summer T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers to process their order for a summer T shirt. Please complete the form accurately to ensure timely processing of your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (marked with an asterisk *).
2. Choose your preferred product image from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter the quantity of T shirts you wish to order.
4. Select the size of T shirt you prefer.
5. Fill out your shipping address.
6. Choose your country of residence.
7. Select your state or region.
8. Enter your city.
9. Provide your zip code.
10. Choose your credit card type.
11. Enter your credit card number and CVV (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Customer Name (*)
* This field is for your contact name.
* Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
### 2. Product Image (*)
* Select the image of the T shirt you wish to order from the dropdown menu.
* This will help us understand which product you're interested in purchasing.
### 3. Quantity (*)
* Enter the number of T shirts you wish to order.
* Please ensure you enter a valid number.
### 4. Size Preference (*)
* Select the size of T shirt you prefer from the options: Small, Medium, Large.
* This will ensure we get the correct size for your order.
### 5. Shipping Address (*)
* Enter your complete shipping address.
* This includes your street address or post office box number.
### 6. Country (*)
* Choose your country of residence.
* This will help us determine shipping costs and logistics.
### 7. State (*)
* Select your state or region from the available options.
* If you don't see your region listed, please contact us for assistance.
### 8. City
* Enter your city or town.
* This will help us understand your shipping location.
### 9. Zip Code (*)
* Enter your postal code or zip code.
* Please ensure you enter a valid zip code.
### 10. Credit Card Type (*)
* Select your credit card type from the available options.
* This will help us process your payment securely.
### 11. Credit Card Number (*)
* Enter your credit card number.
* Please ensure you enter a valid credit card number.
### 12. CVV
* Enter your credit card verification value (CVV) for added security.
* If you're not sure what this is, please contact us for assistance.
### 13. CVC
* Enter your credit card verification code (CVC) for added security.
* If you're not sure what this is, please contact us for assistance.
