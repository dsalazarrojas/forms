# paypal_purchase_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form "paypal_purchase_order_form" is designed to assist customers in completing a purchase transaction securely and efficiently. It collects necessary information such as product name, quantity, price, payment method, and additional details to facilitate a smooth purchase process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form with the required information, including the "Payment Method" field, which is mandatory to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the product you are purchasing.
- **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required/optional): Input the number of units of the product you are purchasing.
- **Price** (`price`, number, required/optional): Enter the price of the product.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose between "PayPal" and "Credit Card" as the payment method.
- **E-mail** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for communication and order confirmation.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Input your phone number for any follow-up communication.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Select the date for the order.
- **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Choose the time for the order.
- **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes for the order.

## Tips
- Ensure that you complete all mandatory fields to ensure your order is successfully processed.
- If you experience any issues while completing the form, please contact our support team for assistance.
