# Sports Club Refreshment Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to place refreshment orders for the sports club. It's designed to gather essential information about the order, including contact details, order type, quantity, and submission confirmation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your contact information in the "Contact Person", "Email", and "Phone" fields.
2. Select the type of order you want to place by choosing "Food", "Beverages", or "Both" in the "Order Type" field.
3. Enter the quantity of food and beverages you need in the "Quantity of Food" and "Quantity of Beverages" fields, respectively.
4. If you have any comments or special requests, type them in the "Comments" field.
5. Confirm that you have submitted the order by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Confirm" field.
6. Enter the date and time of the order in the "Order Date" and "Order Time" fields.
7. If you need to submit the order on a specific date and time, select "Yes" or "No" in the "Submit" field.
8. Choose whether the order is placed by a "Member" or "Staff" member.
9. You may need to provide an order number and ID for tracking purposes, but this is not required.
10. Finally, click the "Submit" button to submit the form.
