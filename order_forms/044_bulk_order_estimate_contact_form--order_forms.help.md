# Bulk Order Estimate Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bulk Order Estimate Contact Form is a comprehensive order form designed to collect detailed information from customers for a bulk order. This form will help us better understand your needs and provide an accurate quote for your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the desired date for the inquiry in the "Inquiry Date" field.
2. Provide your company's full name in the "Company Name" field.
3. Enter the full name of the contact person in the "Contact Person Name" field.
4. Enter the contact person's title in the "Contact Title" field (optional).
5. Enter the contact person's email address in the "Email Address" field.
6. Enter the contact person's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
7. Enter the company's address in the "Company Address" field.
8. Enter the city where the company is located in the "City" field.
9. Enter the state or province where the company is located in the "State or Province" field.
10. Enter the postal code in the "Zip or Postal Code" field.
11. Select the industry or business type of your company in the "Industry or Business Type" field.
12. Choose the category of the product you are interested in from the "Product Category of Interest" dropdown menu.
13. Enter a detailed description of the product in the "Product Description" field.
14. Enter the estimated quantity needed for the order in the "Estimated Quantity Needed" field.
15. Select the unit of measure for the order in the "Unit of Measure" dropdown menu.
16. Choose the approximate budget range for the order from the "Budget Range" dropdown menu (optional).
17. Select the desired delivery timeline from the "Desired Delivery Timeline" dropdown menu.
18. Enter the delivery address for the order in the "Delivery Address" field.
19. Indicate if this will be an ongoing order in the "Will This Be an Ongoing Order" field (optional).
20. Check if you currently have a supplier and why you are looking for a new one (optional).
21. Provide any additional information or requirements for the order in the "Additional Information" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inquiry Date** (`inquiry_date`, date, required): Enter the date you submitted your inquiry. This will help us track the timeline of your order.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company's full name as it appears on your business documents.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the contact person for your company.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's job title or position.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, required): Enter the company's address where the order will be shipped.
* **City** (`company_city`, text, required): Enter the city where the company is located.
* **State or Province** (`company_state`, text, required): Enter the state or province where the company is located.
* **Zip or Postal Code** (`company_zip`, text, required): Enter the postal code for the company's address.
* **Industry or Business Type** (`industry`, text, required): Select the industry or business type of your company.
* **Product Category of Interest** (`product_category`, select_one, required): Choose the category of the product you are interested in from the dropdown menu.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the product you are interested in.
* **Estimated Quantity Needed** (`quantity_estimate`, text, required): Enter the estimated quantity of the product needed.
* **Unit of Measure** (`unit_of_measure`, select_one, required): Choose the unit of measure for the order from the dropdown menu.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, optional): Choose the approximate budget range for the order from the dropdown menu (if applicable).
* **Desired Delivery Timeline** (`delivery_timeline`, select_one, required): Select the desired delivery timeline from the dropdown menu.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, required): Enter the delivery address for the order.
* **Will This Be an Ongoing Order** (`ongoing_orders`, select_one, optional): Indicate if this will be an ongoing order (if not, please select "One-Time Order").
* **Currently Have a Supplier** (`current_supplier`, select_one, optional): Check if you currently have a supplier and why you are looking for a new one.
* **Why Looking for New Supplier** (`comparison_reason`, text, optional): Enter the reason for looking for a new supplier.
* **Quality or Specification Requirements** (`quality_requirements`, text, optional): Enter any specific quality or specification requirements for the order.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information or requirements for the order.
