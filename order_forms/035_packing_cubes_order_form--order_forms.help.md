# Packing Cubes Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Packing Cubes Order Form is used to collect customer information and order details for packing cubes. This form helps our business to efficiently process orders and ensure accurate delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Customer Info**: Enter your name, which will be used to address the order and any future correspondence.
2. **Customer Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
3. **Customer Email**: Enter your email address to contact you for order updates.
4. **Shipping Info**: Enter your shipping information, including any necessary notes for the delivery person.
5. **Order Details**: Provide any relevant order details, such as special requests or instructions.
6. **Customer Note**: Optional field for any additional notes from the customer.
7. **Shipping Address**: Enter your shipping address for accurate delivery.
8. **Cube Quantity**: Enter the quantity of packing cubes you are ordering.
9. **Cube Type**: Select the type of packing cube(s) you are ordering (e.g. "Yes" for a standard cube, "No" for no cube).
10. **Shipping City**: Enter the city for the shipping address.
11. **Shipping State**: Enter the state for the shipping address.
12. **Shipping Zip**: Enter the zip code for the shipping address.
13. **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number to contact you for order updates.
14. **Shipping Country**: Select the country for the shipping address (e.g. "Yes" for domestic, "No" for international).
15. **Shipping Method**: Select the shipping method (e.g. "Yes" for ground, "No" for air).
16. **Order Comments**: Optional field for any additional comments from the customer.
17. **Customer Comments**: Optional field for any additional comments from the customer.
18. **Shipping Date**: Enter the desired shipping date for the order.
19. **Shipping Time**: Enter the desired shipping time for the order.
20. **Cube Note**: Enter any specific instructions or notes for the packing cubes.
21. **Order Number**: Enter the order number for the order.
22. **Order Status**: Select the status of the order (e.g. "Active", "Inactive").
23. **Customer Status**: Select the status of the customer (e.g. "Active", "Inactive").
24. **Shipping Cost**: Enter the shipping cost for the order.
25. **Order Total**: Enter the total cost of the order.
