# Freight Service Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a new freight request for shipping a package or cargo. Please fill out the form with accurate information to help us better serve your freight needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the shipper's name in the "Shipper Name" field.
2. Enter the shipper's phone number in the "Shipper Phone" field.
3. Enter the shipper's email address in the "Shipper Email" field (optional).
4. Enter the shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field.
5. Enter a detailed description of the freight in the "Freight Details" field.
6. Choose the delivery date in the "Delivery Date" field.
7. Choose the delivery time in the "Delivery Time" field.
8. Enter the delivery address in the "Delivery Address" field.
9. (Optional) Enter the delivery phone number in the "Delivery Phone" field.
10. (Optional) Enter the delivery email address in the "Delivery Email" field.
11. Select the payment method in the "Payment Method" field.
12. Enter the payment amount in the "Payment Amount" field.
13. Choose the payment date in the "Payment Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shipper Name** (`shipper_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person or company responsible for shipping the freight.
* **Shipper Phone** (`shipper_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the person or company responsible for shipping the freight.
* **Shipper Email** (`shipper_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the person or company responsible for shipping the freight. This field is optional, but it helps us to contact you with any updates or questions.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address where the freight will be picked up from.
* **Freight Details** (`freight_details`, `text`, required): Enter a detailed description of the freight, including its contents and any special requirements.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, required): Choose the date on which you would like the freight to be delivered.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, `time`, required): Choose the time of day on which you would like the freight to be delivered.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address where the freight should be delivered.
* **Delivery Phone** (`delivery_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the person who will receive the freight at delivery.
* **Delivery Email** (`delivery_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the person who will receive the freight at delivery.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select the method of payment for the freight service (Bank Transfer, Cash, or Check).
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the total amount to be paid for the freight service.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, `date`, required): Choose the date on which you would like to make the payment for the freight service.

Note: This form is used for a specific business purpose, if you are unsure about any of the fields or need clarification, please contact us at your earliest convenience.
