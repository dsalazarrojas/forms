# temperature-controlled-delivery-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for temperature-controlled delivery, ensuring that packages are transported safely and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the contact information: Shipping Address, Shipping Email, and Shipping Phone.
2.  Select the delivery date and time that suits your needs.
3.  Choose the delivery time zone and period that apply to your package.
4.  Enter the package details: Package Temperature, Package Weight, and Package Length.
5.  Fill in the tracking number and any delivery notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Shipping Address** (`8`, `text`): Enter the address where the package will be shipped from and to.
*   **Shipping Email** (`9`, `email`): Enter the email address of the person or business responsible for shipping and receiving the package.
*   **Shipping Phone** (`10`, `text`): Enter the phone number of the person or business responsible for shipping and receiving the package.
*   **Delivery Date** (`1`, `date`): Select the date when the package will be delivered.
*   **Delivery Time** (`2`, `text`): Enter the time of delivery in the format 24 hours.
*   **Delivery Time Zone** (`3`, `select_multiple`): Choose the time zone for the package delivery.
*   **Delivery Time Period** (`4`, `select_one`): Select the time period for delivery (e.g. AM or PM).
*   **Package Temperature** (`5`, `number`): Enter the temperature range in degrees Celsius that the package requires.
*   **Package Weight** (`6`, `number`): Enter the weight of the package in grams or kilograms.
*   **Package Length** (`7`, `number`): Enter the length of the package in meters or centimeters.
*   **Shipping Address** (`8`, `text`): Enter the shipping address in the same format as the previous field.
*   **Tracking Number** (`11`, `text`): Enter the tracking number for the package.
*   **Delivery Status** (`12`, `select_one`): Select the status of the package (e.g. Pending, In Transit, Delivered, Cancelled).
*   **Delivery Temperature Range** (`13`, `number`): Enter the temperature range in degrees Celsius that the package requires.
*   **Delivery Notes** (`14`, `note`): Enter any notes or instructions for the delivery.

The form also includes tips to help you get the best experience while using it. If you still have questions or concerns, please contact us.
