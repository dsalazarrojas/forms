# tech_product_launch_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The tech_product_launch_pre_order_form is designed to allow customers to express their interest in pre-ordering a tech product and provide preliminary shipping and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "User Input" field with your name or username.
2. Enter your shipping details, including your address, phone number, and email.
3. Select your preferred payment method: Card Number, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
4. Choose one or more shipping methods: Shipping via Courier, Shipping via Carrier, or Shipping via Pickup.
5. Enter any additional order and product details.
6. Enter any additional payment details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`<text>`, required: false): Enter your name or username to associate with the pre-order.
* **Shipping Details** (`<text>`, required: false): Enter your shipping address, including any necessary information for the courier or carrier.
* **Shipping Phone** (`<text>`, required: false): Enter your phone number to contact you regarding shipping updates.
* **Shipping Email** (`<email>`, required: false): Enter your email address to receive shipping updates.
* **Shipping Country** (`<text>`, required: false): Enter your country of origin.
* **Order Details** (`<text>`, required: false): Enter any additional information about your order, such as specific requirements or requests.
* **Product Info** (`<text>`, required: false): Enter any additional information about the product you are pre-ordering, such as the product name or model number.
* **Payment Method** (`<select_one>`, required: false): Choose how you would like to pay for your pre-ordered product: Card Number, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
* **Shipping Method** (`<select_multiple>`, required: false): Choose one or more shipping methods from Shipping via Courier, Shipping via Carrier, or Shipping via Pickup.
* **Shipping Address** (`<text>`, required: false): Enter your shipping address for the pre-ordered product.
* **Payment Details** (`<text>`, required: false): Enter any additional payment details, such as payment terms or conditions.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields (if any) before submitting the form.
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
- Only pre-order if you are certain about the product and details.
