# Machinery Lubrication Tube Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Machinery Lubrication Tube Order Form is used to record the details of an order for machinery lubrication tubes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the form data in the text field.
2. Enter the customer's information, including their name, email address, and phone number.
3. Indicate whether or not the customer requires lubrication tubes.
4. Enter the quantity of lubrication tubes required.
5. Enter the price per tube.
6. Calculate the total price by multiplying the quantity by the price per tube.
7. Enter any additional notes or comments about the order.
8. Enter any additional notes or comments about the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Data** (`form_data`, text, required: false): This field is used to capture any additional information related to the order.
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required: false): This field captures the customer's information, including their name, email address, and phone number.
* **Lubrication Tubes** (`lubrication_tubes`, select_multiple, required: false): This field indicates whether the customer requires lubrication tubes. Select 'Yes' if the customer needs lubrication tubes, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required: false): Enter the quantity of lubrication tubes required.
* **Price per Tube** (`price_per_tube`, number, required: false): Enter the price per lubrication tube.
* **Total Price** (`total_price`, number, required: false): This field is calculated automatically by multiplying the quantity by the price per tube. However, if you need to enter it manually, enter the total price of the order.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer E-mail** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the order.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): This field is used to capture any additional notes or comments about the order.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, required: false): This field is used to capture any additional notes or comments about the order.
