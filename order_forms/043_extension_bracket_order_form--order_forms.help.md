# Extension Bracket Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Extension Bracket Order Form is used to collect order details and payment information for an extension bracket order. This form is typically used by customers or suppliers in a business-to-business or business-to-customer setting.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **ExtensionBracketOrderForm**: This is the main field where you can enter a brief description of the order.
2. **shipping_details**: Enter the shipping details for the order, such as shipping address, city, state, and zip code.
3. **billing_details**: Enter the billing details for the order, such as billing address, city, state, and zip code.
4. **payment_details**: Enter the payment method and details for the order.
5. **customer_details**: Enter the customer's name, address, city, state, zip code, phone number, and email.
6. **order_details**: Enter any additional order details, such as order total or order status.
7. **shipping_method**: Select the shipping method for the order (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
8. **supplier_info**: Enter any supplier information, such as supplier name, address, phone number, and email.
9. **payment_method**: Enter the payment method and details for the order.
10. **customer_info**: Enter any additional customer information, such as customer phone number.
11. **contract_details**: Enter any contract details for the order.
12. **order_note**: Enter any additional notes about the order.
13. **customer_phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
14. **customer_email**: Enter the customer's email address.
15. **customer_name**: Enter the customer's name.
16. **customer_address**: Enter the customer's address.
17. **customer_city**: Enter the customer's city.
18. **customer_state**: Enter the customer's state.
19. **customer_zip**: Enter the customer's zip code.
20. **customer_country**: Enter the customer's country.
21. **supplier_phone**: Enter the supplier's phone number.
22. **supplier_email**: Enter the supplier's email address.
23. **supplier_address**: Enter the supplier's address.
