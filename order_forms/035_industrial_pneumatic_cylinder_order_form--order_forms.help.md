# industrial_pneumatic_cylinder_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers for industrial pneumatic cylinder orders.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the customer name and address.
2. Provide a brief description of the order.
3. Select the type of product required.
4. Choose the size of the pneumatic cylinder.
5. Enter the quantity of pneumatic cylinders ordered.
6. Select the payment method for the order.
7. Enter the payment amount.
8. Fill in the invoice number (if known).
9. Select the due date for payment.
10. Confirm the payment status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer placing the order.
* **Customer Address**: Enter the address of the customer placing the order.
* **Order Description**: Briefly describe the purpose or reason for the order.
* **Delivery Date**: Select the date on which the order is to be delivered.
* **Delivery Time**: Select the time on which the order is to be delivered.
* **Product Type**: Select the type of pneumatic cylinder ordered.
* **Cylinder Size**: Select the size of the pneumatic cylinder ordered.
* **Quantity Ordered**: Enter the number of pneumatic cylinders ordered.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method for the order.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount paid for the order.
* **Invoice Number**: Enter the invoice number related to the order.
* **Due Date**: Select the date on which payment is due.
* **Payment Status**: Confirm the status of payment for the order.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the sales or customer service team for assistance.
