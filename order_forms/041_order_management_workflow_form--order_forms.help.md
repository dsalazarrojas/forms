# Order Management Workflow Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Order Management Workflow Form" is a digital form designed to collect and manage order-related information. It is intended for use in order management workflows, such as retail, e-commerce, or B2B environments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form fields with the required information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure that you are entering accurate and complete information.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`title`, `text`, **required**): Enter a brief title for the order.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, `text`, **required**): Enter the customer's information, including name, address, and contact details.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, **required**): Enter the customer's shipping address.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, `text`, **required**): Enter the order details, including product information and any specific instructions.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, `text`, **required**): Select the current status of the order (e.g., pending, shipped, delivered).
* **Order Products** (`order_products`, `text`, **required**): List the products included in the order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `text`, **required**): Select the payment method used for the order (e.g., credit card, PayPal).
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, `text`, **required**): Enter any additional notes or comments about the order.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date the order was placed.

## Tips
