# Stock Availability Confirmation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stock Availability Confirmation Request Form is used to request confirmation on stock availability for a specific order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields with the necessary information.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure smooth order fulfillment.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to our team for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name**: Enter your company's name as it appears on your business cards or invoices.
* **Current Stock Level**: Enter the current stock level for the product in question.
* **Supplier**: Select the supplier of the stock from the provided options.
* **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the stock you are requesting.
* **Unit Price**: Enter the unit price of the stock.
* **Order Fulfillment Date**: Enter the date you expect to fulfill the order.
* **Delivery Fulfillment Date**: Enter the date you expect to deliver the stock.
* **Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the request.
* **Email**: Enter your contact email address, in case we need to follow up.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number, in case we need to reach you.

- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Double-check the information before submitting the form.
