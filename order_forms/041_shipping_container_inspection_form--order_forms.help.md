# shipping_container_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for inspectors to document the condition and certification status of a shipping container. It is used for regulatory compliance, risk assessment, and quality control purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

### General Guidelines

- Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- Use the options provided for each field to ensure accurate classification.
- Enter dates in the format `yyyy-mm-dd` (e.g., `2023-02-01`).
- Enter numbers exactly as they appear on the container or certification documents.
- Sign and certify the form if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Container Type** (`container_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the correct type of container (Standard, Reefer, Tanker, or Flat Rack).
* **Container Condition** (`container_condition`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all applicable conditions (Damaged, Leaky, Clean, or Soiled).
* **Condition Description** (`condition_description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the container's condition.
* **Weight Capacity** (`weight_capacity`, `number`, required: false): Enter the weight capacity of the container.
* **Container Damage** (`container_damage`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all applicable damages (No Damage, Scratch, Bend, Dented, or Hole).
* **Damage Description** (`damage_description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the damage.
* **Safety Features** (`safety_features`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all applicable safety features (Safety Harness, Fire Extinguisher, or Reflective Tape).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments regarding the condition of the container.
* **Date Inspected** (`date_inspected`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Inspector's Name** (`inspector_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the inspector.
* **Inspector ID** (`inspector_id`, `text`, required: false): Enter the ID number of the inspector.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the company.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact number of the company.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email of the company.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose between a Signature or a Stamp.
* **Certification** (`certification`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose between Certified or Non-Certified.
* **Certification Number** (`certification_number`, `number`, required: false): Enter the certification number.
* **Certification Date** (`certification_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the certification date.
* **Certification Expiration** (`certification_expiration`, `date`, required: false): Enter the certification expiration date.
* **Certification Status** (`certification_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose between Active, Inactive, or Expired.
* **Certification Type** (`certification_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose between ISPS, ISO, or OSHA.
* **Additional Comments 2** (`additional_comments_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments regarding the certification.
* **Certification Number 2** (`certification_number_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the second certification number.
* **Certification Date 2** (`certification_date_2`, `date`, required: false): Enter the second certification date.
* **Certification Expiration 2** (`certification_expiration_2`, `date`, required: false): Enter the second certification expiration date.

### Tips

* Use the options provided for each field to ensure accurate classification.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Enter dates and numbers exactly as they appear on the container or certification documents.
* Sign and certify the form if applicable.
* Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.
