# Custom Furniture Order Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Furniture Order Checklist Form is a tool used by customers to provide detailed information about their custom furniture order, including order details, customer information, and payment terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details**: This field is used to provide a brief description of the order. Please fill in the required details to ensure that your order is processed correctly.
* **Customer Information**: This field is used to collect contact information for the customer, including their name, email, phone number, and address. Please enter accurate and up-to-date information to facilitate communication and delivery.
* **Product Options**: Select the product options you would like to include in your order, such as Wooden Legs or Metal Frame.
* **Delivery Information**: Select the delivery method for your order, such as Ground Floor or Staircase.
* **Payment Method**: Choose the payment method for your order, such as Cash, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer.
* **Customer Specifications**: Provide any additional specifications or requirements for your order, such as specific materials or colors.
* **Order Date and Time**: Enter the date and time you would like to receive your order.
* **Delivery Date and Time**: Enter the date and time you would like to receive your delivery.
* **Delivery Note**: Add any additional delivery instructions or information.
* **Order Status**: Select the current status of your order, such as Open, In Progress, or Completed.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer placing the order.
* **Customer Note**: Add any additional comments or information about the customer.
* **Order Price**: Enter the price of the order.
* **Customer Comments**: Add any comments or feedback about the order.
* **Sales Notes**: Add any additional notes or information for the sales team.
* **Payment Terms**: Select the payment terms for your order, such as Net 30 or Net 60.
* **Order Terms**: Add any additional terms or conditions for the order.
* **Sales Person**: Enter the name of the sales person handling your order.
* **Customer Signature**: Select if the customer's signature is required or not for the order.

Note: Fields marked as required are mandatory and must be filled in for the form to be submitted correctly.
