# Quality Inspection Gauge Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quality Inspection Gauge Order Form is a document used to order quality inspection gauges, which are essential for monitoring and maintaining the quality of products. This form ensures that all necessary information is collected to complete the inspection process accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the shipping details that apply to your order.
2. Add special instructions if any specific requirements or conditions need to be considered during the inspection process.
3. Enter the gauge order number to identify the specific gauge being ordered.
4. Enter the quantity of the gauge to be ordered.
5. Provide the customer's name and any relevant contact information.
6. Enter the date and time of the order.
7. Enter the customer's email address and phone number (if available).
8. Enter any additional customer notes or comments.
9. Enter the shipping address (if different from the customer's address).
10. Provide any additional special instructions (up to 5 options are available).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shipping details** (Select One, Required: False): This field allows you to select the shipping details that apply to your order. Choose from the available options to ensure accurate shipping.
* **Special instructions** (Text, Required: False): Add any special instructions or requirements for the inspection process.
* **Gauge order** (Number, Required: True): Enter the specific gauge order number to identify the gauge being ordered.
* **Gauge quantity** (Number, Required: True): Enter the quantity of the gauge to be ordered.
* **Customer name** (Text, Required: False): Enter the customer's name for identification purposes.
* **Order date** (Date, Required: False): Enter the date of the order.
* **Order time** (Time, Required: False): Enter the time of the order.
* **Email** (Email, Required: False): Enter the customer's email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (Text, Required: False): Enter the customer's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Customer notes** (Text, Required: False): Add any additional notes or comments from the customer.
* **Shipping address** (Text, Required: False): Enter the shipping address (if different from the customer's address).
* **Special instructions 1-5** (Text, Required: False): Add any additional special instructions or requirements for the inspection process.
