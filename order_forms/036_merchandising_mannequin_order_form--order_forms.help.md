# Merchandising Mannequin Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Merchandising Mannequin Order Form is a critical tool for collecting relevant information related to mannequin orders. This form ensures that all necessary details are captured accurately, ensuring efficient order fulfillment and minimizing the risk of errors or miscommunication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully and ensure you have all required information before submitting.
* Select the correct options from the dropdown menus or checkboxes where applicable.
* Provide accurate and relevant information in the text fields.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Mannequin Orders** (`mannequin_orders`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable mannequin orders from the options provided. This field helps us understand the scope of the order and prioritize the fulfillment process.
- **Custom Requests** (`custom_requests`, text, optional): If you have special requirements or requests for the order, please provide a clear and concise description. This field helps our team understand your specific needs.
- **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, optional): Enter any relevant shipping information, such as special handling instructions or delivery addresses. This field ensures we can process your order efficiently.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Indicate your preferred payment method for this order. This field helps us facilitate payment processing and reduces the risk of delays.
- **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, optional): If you need to provide additional payment information, please enter it here. This field is useful for specific payment scenarios.
- **Shipping Cost** (`shipping_cost`, number, optional): Enter the shipping cost associated with this order. This field helps us accurately calculate the total order value.
- **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Provide any relevant customer information, such as contact details or contact preferences. This field ensures we can communicate effectively with you regarding order updates.
- **Order Total** (`order_total`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm whether this order is a part of a larger order or has a specific total value. This field helps us understand the order context.
- **Tax Amount** (`tax_amount`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the tax amount associated with this order. This field ensures accurate tax calculations.
- **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, select_one, optional): Indicate your preferred shipping method for this order. This field helps us plan efficient shipping and reduce costs.

**Tips**

* Please ensure all fields are completed accurately and consistently with your order requirements.
* If you are unsure about any field or have questions, contact our support team for guidance.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you need to make changes to your order, please submit a new form with the updated information.
