# Season Ticket Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Season Ticket Order Form is designed to gather information from customers who are interested in purchasing season tickets. This form will help the season ticket office process orders efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by navigating to the first page, "Season Ticket Details".
2. Fill out the "Season Ticket Details" page with the required information.
3. Proceed to the second page, "Customer Info", and fill out the customer's contact and order information.
4. On the last page, "Payment Info", enter the payment details.
5. Review each page carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Season Ticket Details** (Season Ticket Details, text, required): Enter the season ticket information, including the ticket type, price, and any other relevant details.
* **Customer Info** (Customer Info, text, required): Enter the customer's name, email address, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Payment Info** (Payment Info, text, required): Enter the payment method and any other relevant payment details.

## Tips
* Double-check the information entered on each page before submitting the form.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate order processing.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the season ticket office.
