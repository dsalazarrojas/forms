# Good Hart September Release Pre Order - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is for customers who want to pre-order items from the September release of Good Hart. It captures necessary details such as release date, customer contact information, and order specifics to process the pre-order.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **Release Date** in the date format mm/dd/yyyy.
2. Enter your **Name** as it appears on your identification.
3. Provide your **Email** address so we can contact you regarding your order.
4. Enter your **Phone** number so we can reach you if needed.
5. Fill in your **Shipping Address**, **Shipping City**, and **Shipping Zip** as per your shipping details.
6. Select the specific **Item** you're pre-ordering from the provided options.
7. Enter the **Quantity** of the item you want to pre-order.
8. If needed, add any **Note** related to your order.
9. Fill in the required **Name** fields corresponding to the item you're pre-ordering, but please do not repeat any name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Release Date** (Required: false): Enter the date you want to pre-order the item from the September release.
  * Format: mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Name** (Required: false): Please fill in your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (Required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your order.
* **Phone** (Required: false): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Shipping Address** (Required: false): Enter your shipping address where we should send the item.
* **Shipping City** (Required: false): Enter the city where you want the item shipped to.
* **Shipping Zip** (Required: false): Enter the zip code for your shipping address.
* **Item** (Required: true): Select the specific item you want to pre-order from the options.
* **Quantity** (Required: false): Specify the quantity of the item you want to pre-order.
* **Note** (Required: false): Add any specific notes regarding your order.
* **Name 1-16** (Required: false): Fill in your name corresponding to the selected item, as each field is specific to the item you've chosen. Ensure to fill in each name without duplication.
