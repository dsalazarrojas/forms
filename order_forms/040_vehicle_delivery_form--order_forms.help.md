# vehicle_delivery_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vehicle delivery form is used to collect information from customers for the vehicle delivery process. This includes collecting details from the customer, their vehicle, and the delivery instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's details.
2. Provide the vehicle details.
3. Enter the delivery instructions and date/time.
4. Enter the customer's email and phone number.
5. Choose the assigned user to handle the delivery.
6. Confirm the assigned user's role.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details**: Please fill in the customer's name and other relevant information.
* **Vehicle Details**: Enter the vehicle's make, model, and any other relevant information.
* **Delivery Instructions**: Enter any specific instructions or notes for the delivery.
* **Email**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Delivery Date**: Choose the date of delivery.
* **Delivery Time**: Choose the time of delivery.
* **Assigned User**: Choose whether a user is assigned to handle this delivery.
* **Assigned User Name**: This is an optional field for entering the assigned user's name.
* **Assigned User Role**: Choose the role of the assigned user in handling this delivery.
* **Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the date of delivery.
* **Delivery Time**: Enter the time of delivery.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and complete information.
- Double-check the customer's contact information for accuracy.
- If a customer has specific delivery instructions, make sure to include them in the form.
- Choose the correct assigned user and their role to ensure proper handling of the delivery.
- If an assigned user's name is not provided, use the customer's contact information to try to reach out to them.
