# Restroom Supplies Reorder Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by staff to reorder essential supplies for the restroom, helping to maintain a clean and hygienic environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the location where the supplies are needed.
2. Select whether delivery is preferred or not.
3. Choose the supplies required.
4. Enter the desired quantity.
5. Provide a reason for the reordering.
6. Optionally, enter the staff member who will receive the supplies.
7. Select a delivery date.
8. Choose a delivery time.
9. Enter any additional remarks.
10. Enter your email address.
11. Enter your phone number.
12. Enter the assigned tool (not applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the supplies are needed.
* **Delivery** (`delivery_preferred`, select_one, optional): Select whether you prefer delivery or not. This will help us determine the best course of action for your supply delivery.
* **Supply** (`supply_items`, select_multiple, optional): Select the supplies required from the list provided.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the desired quantity of the selected supplies.
* **Reason** (`reorder_reason`, text, optional): Provide a reason for the reordering of the supplies.
* **Staff** (`staff_member`, text, optional): Optionally, enter the staff member who will be receiving the supplies.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): Select a date for the delivery.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, optional): Choose a time for the delivery.
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, note, optional): Enter any additional remarks for the delivery.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the assigned tool (not applicable).
* **Created By** (`created_by`, text, optional): Enter the staff member who created this form entry.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, date, optional): This field will automatically be populated when the form is created.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, date, optional): This field will automatically be populated when the form is updated.
* **Deleted At** (`deleted_at`, date, optional): This field will be populated when the form is deleted.
* **Deleted By** (`deleted_by`, text, optional): Enter the staff member who deleted this form entry.
* **Deleted At 2** (`deleted_at_2`, date, optional): This field will be populated when the form is deleted.
* **Deleted By 2** (`deleted_by_2`, text, optional): Enter the staff member who deleted this form entry.
* **Created By 2** (`created_by_2`, text, optional): Enter the staff member who created this form entry.
* **Created At 2** (`created_at_2`, date, optional): This field will automatically be populated when the form is created.
