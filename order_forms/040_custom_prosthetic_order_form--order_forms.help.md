# Custom Prosthetic Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Prosthetic Order Form is used to gather information for the ordering of custom prosthetics. This form is required for all patients seeking custom prosthetics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Patient DOB" field.
3. Enter the doctor's name in the "Doctor Name" field.
4. Select the desired "Device Type" from the list provided.
5. If applicable, select the preferred "Device Color" from the list provided.
6. Select the "Prosthetic Size" that best fits the patient's needs.
7. Select the "Material Type" that the patient prefers.
8. Enter the patient's address in the "Patient Address" field (if required).
9. Enter the patient's insurance number in the "Insurance Number" field (if required).
10. Enter the patient's phone number in the "Patient Phone" field (if required).
11. Provide any additional "Notes" that may be helpful for the custom prosthetic order process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Patient DOB** (`patient_dob`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the doctor who will be using the custom prosthetic.
* **Device Type** (`device_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of prosthetic that the patient requires, such as Microprocessor-Controlled Lower Limb or Microprocessor-Controlled Upper Limb.
* **Device Color** (`device_color`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more colors for the prosthetic.
* **Prosthetic Size** (`prosthetic_size`, select_one, required): Select the size that best fits the patient's needs.
* **Material Type** (`material_type`, select_one, required): Select the material type that the patient prefers.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, optional): Enter the patient's address if it is required for the order process.
* **Insurance Number** (`insurance_number`, text, required): Enter the patient's insurance number if it is required for the order process.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number if it is required.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional information that may be helpful for the custom prosthetic order process.
