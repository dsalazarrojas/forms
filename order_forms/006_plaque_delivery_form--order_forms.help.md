# Plaque Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Plaque Delivery Form is designed to collect information for delivering plaques. The form is used to gather essential details from customers for successful plaque delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the delivery address in the "Delivery Address" field.
2.  Enter the recipient's name in the "Recipient name" field.
3.  If desired, provide delivery instructions in the "Delivery Instructions" field.
4.  Enter the recipient's email address in the "Email" field to receive a delivery confirmation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Delivery Address (delivery_address)
*   **Delivery Address**: A field to capture the address of the recipient.
    Enter the address where the plaque will be delivered.

### Recipient name (recipient_name)
*   **Recipient name**: A field to capture the name of the recipient.
    Enter the name of the person receiving the plaque.

### Delivery Instructions (delivery_instructions)
*   **Delivery Instructions**: A field to capture any special instructions for the delivery.
    Enter any additional notes or instructions for the delivery team.

### Email (email)
*   **Email**: A field to capture the recipient's email address for delivery confirmation.
    Enter the email address where you'd like to receive a delivery confirmation.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure correct delivery.
*   If you provide delivery instructions, please be as detailed as possible to avoid any confusion.
*   Ensure you enter a valid email address to receive delivery confirmation.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx.
