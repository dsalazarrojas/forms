# Subscription Cancellation Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Subscription Cancellation Claim Form is a tool for customers to report issues with their subscription orders. This form helps us to identify and resolve problems related to your order, such as incorrect order fulfillment or order discrepancies, and to process your cancellation claim accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it appears on your order.
2. Provide a detailed reason for your cancellation claim.
3. Enter the order ID associated with your claim.
4. Select the date when you placed the order.
5. If you have already cancelled your order, enter the date when you cancelled it.
6. Enter your email address.
7. Enter your phone number.
8. Provide any additional notes about your claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (Customer Name, Text, Required): Enter your name as it appears on your order.
* **Reason** (Reason, Text, Optional): Provide a detailed description of the problem with your order. This could include information about incorrect order fulfillment, order discrepancies, or any other issues.
* **Order ID** (Order ID, Number, Required): Enter the exact ID of your order as it appears in your account or on your order confirmation email.
* **Order Date** (Order Date, Date, Required): Select the date when you placed the order.
* **Cancellation Date** (Cancellation Date, Date, Optional): If you have already cancelled your order, enter the date when you cancelled it.
* **Email** (Email, Email, Optional): Enter your email address associated with your account.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted regarding your claim.
* **Note** (Note, Note, Optional): Provide any additional information about your claim that may be helpful for us to process it efficiently.
