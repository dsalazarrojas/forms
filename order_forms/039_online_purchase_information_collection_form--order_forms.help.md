# Online Purchase Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Purchase Information Collection Form is designed to collect information from customers about their online purchases. This form is meant to gather essential details that help us understand the customer's order and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and fill in the required fields.
2.  Provide the requested information in the fields as instructed.
3.  Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Online Order Details
*   **Online Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional):
    This field is for entering any additional comments or details about the online order.

### Delivery Preferences
*   **Delivery Preferences** (`delivery_preferences`, text, optional):
    This field is for specifying any delivery preferences or instructions.

### Customer Details
*   **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, optional):
    This field is for entering any additional customer details or information.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill in all the required fields to ensure your submission is complete.
*   Double-check your entries for accuracy to avoid any issues with your order.
*   If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to contact us.
