# Sample Change Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and track project changes, updates, and modifications. It's essential for maintaining project transparency and ensuring that all stakeholders are informed of changes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Project Name**: Provide the name of the project in the project dashboard. This will help you quickly locate the project's related information.
2. **Brief Description**: Enter a short description of the project to provide context for the change.
3. **Change Details**: Provide a detailed description of the change, update, or modification.
4. **Change Type**: Select the type of change from the options provided.
5. **Date and Time**: Enter the date and time of the change, if applicable.
6. **Customer Information**: Enter the customer's name, contact details, and any relevant contact information.
7. **Contractor Information**: Enter the contractor's name, contact details, and any relevant contact information.
8. **Agreement**: Enter the agreement text, if applicable.
9. **Approval and Rejection**: Record the approval and rejection processes, including the dates and times.
10. **Change Request Status**: Select the status of the change request.
11. **Comments**: Optionally, enter any comments or notes related to the change.
12. **Assigned To**: Identify the person or team assigned to handle the change.
13. **Created By**: Enter the person who created the form.
14. **Date and Time Created**: Record the date and time the form was created.
15. **Updated At**: Record the date and time the form was last updated.
16. **Approved By**: Identify the person who approved the change.
17. **Approved At**: Record the date and time the change was approved.
18. **Approved Time**: Record the time the change was approved.
19. **Rejected By**: Identify the person who rejected the change.
20. **Rejected At**: Record the date and time the change was rejected.
21. **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments related to the change.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the project in the project dashboard.
* **Brief Description** (`description`, text, required): Enter a short description of the project to provide context for the change.
* **Change Details** (`change_details`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the change, update, or modification.
* **Change Type** (`change_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of change from the options provided.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the change, if applicable.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the change, if applicable.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Contact** (`customer_contact`, text, required): Enter the customer's contact details.
* **Contractor Name** (`contractor_name`, text, required): Enter the contractor's name.
* **Contractor Contact** (`contractor_contact`, text, required): Enter the contractor's contact details.
* **Agreement Text** (`agreement_text`, text, required): Enter the agreement text, if applicable.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, required): Record the date the change was approved.
* **Approval Time** (`approval_time`, time, required): Record the time the change was approved.
* **Change Request Status** (`change_request_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the change request.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or notes related to the change.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, required): Identify the person or team assigned to handle the change.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, text, required): Enter the person who created the form.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, date, optional): Record the date the form was created.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, date, optional): Record the date the form was last updated.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, text, required): Identify the person who approved the change.
* **Approved At** (`approved_at`, date, required): Record the date the change was approved.
* **Approved Time** (`approved_time`, time, required): Record the time the change was approved.
* **Rejected By** (`rejected_by`, text, optional): Identify the person who rejected the change.
* **Rejected At** (`rejected_at`, date, optional): Record the date the change was rejected.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments related to the change.

## Tips

* Be thorough when filling out the form to ensure accurate information is captured.
* Double-check for accuracy and consistency in dates and times.
* Make sure to keep all relevant stakeholders informed of changes.
* Use this form as a reference for project planning and execution.
