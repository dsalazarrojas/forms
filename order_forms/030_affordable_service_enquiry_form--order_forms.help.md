# Affordable Service Enquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for customers to enquire about our services, such as cleaning, repair, installation, and more. It helps us understand your needs and provides us with the necessary information to assist you effectively. Please fill out this form to initiate our service and we will be in touch to discuss further details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these simple steps:

1. Fill in your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field to ensure we can contact you for updates and information.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field for contact purposes.
4. If applicable, enter your company name in the "Company Name" field.
5. Enter your address in the "Address" field for service delivery purposes.
6. Select the type of service you require from the "Type of Service Required" dropdown menu.
7. Describe the specific service you need in the "Service Description" field.
8. Specify the location where you would like the service to be performed in the "Service Location" field.
9. If you have a preferred date for the service, enter it in the "Preferred Service Date" field.
10. Select the urgency level of your service requirement from the "Urgency Level" dropdown menu.
11. Choose your budget range for the service from the "Budget Range" dropdown menu.
12. Select your preferred payment method from the "Preferred Payment Method" dropdown menu.
13. If you have any specific requirements or preferences for the service, enter them in the "Specific Requirements" field.
14. If you have any special instructions for the service provider, enter them in the "Special Instructions" field.
15. If you have used similar services before, mark "True" in the "Previous Experience with Service" field.
16. If you have used similar services before, provide details about your experience in the "Details of Previous Experience" field.
17. If you have any preferences for the service provider, enter them in the "Service Provider Preference" field.
18. Enter any additional details or information that might be helpful for the service provider in the "Additional Details" field.
19. Choose how you would prefer to be contacted from the "Preferred Contact Method" dropdown menu.
20. Specify the best time to contact you in the "Best Time to Contact" field.
21. Finally, select how you heard about us from the "How Did You Hear About Us?" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (2)**: Enter your complete name.
* **Email Address (3)**: Enter your email address for service updates and communication.
* **Phone Number (4)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Company Name (5)**: If you are contacting us on behalf of a company, enter your company's name.
* **Address (6)**: Provide your address for service delivery purposes (if applicable).
* **Type of Service Required (8)**: Select the type of service you require (e.g., Cleaning, Repair, Installation, etc.).
* **Service Description (9)**: Describe the specific service you need.
* **Service Location (10)**: Specify the location where you would like the service to be performed.
* **Preferred Service Date (11)**: Enter the date when you would like the service to be performed (optional).
* **Urgency Level (12)**: Select the urgency level of your service requirement (e.g., Immediate, Within a week, Within a month, etc.).
* **Budget Range (14)**: Choose your budget range for the service.
* **Preferred Payment Method (15)**: Select your preferred payment method (e.g., Cash, Credit Card, Bank Transfer, etc.).
* **Specific Requirements (17)**: If you have any specific requirements or preferences for the service, enter them here.
* **Special Instructions (18)**: If you have any special instructions for the service provider, enter them here.
* **Previous Experience with Service (19)**: If you have used similar services before, mark "True" here.
* **Details of Previous Experience (20)**: If you have used similar services before, provide details about your experience here.
* **Service Provider Preference (21)**: If you have any preferences for the service provider, enter them here.
* **Additional Details (23)**: Enter any additional details or information that might be helpful for the service provider.
* **Preferred Contact Method (24)**: Select how you would prefer to be contacted (e.g., Phone Call, Email, Text Message, etc.).
* **Best Time to Contact (25)**: Specify the best time to contact you.
* **How Did You Hear About Us? (26)**: Select how you heard about us (e.g., Google Search, Social Media, Friend/Family Referral, etc.).
* **Electronic Signature (27)**: Sign to submit your enquiry.
