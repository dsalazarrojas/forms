# Wire Mesh Material Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to inquire about wire mesh material. It is designed to gather information about the material, its specifications, price, and other related details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the first page with the required information about the wire mesh material. This includes the Specifications, Price, and Availability.
2. On the second page, select the relevant availability and inquiry type for the material.
3. On the third page, fill in the contact information, including the Contact Name, Email, and Additional Info.
4. On the fourth page, you can add any additional comments or notes about the material.
5. Finally, review all the information filled in and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wire Mesh Material**: (`1`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is where you can enter the name of the wire mesh material being inquired about.
* **Specifications**: (`2`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is where you can enter any additional specifications about the material, such as its dimensions or material type.
* **Price**: (`3`, `number`, required: false)
	+ This field is where you can enter the price of the wire mesh material.
* **Availability**: (`4`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select "Yes" if the material is available, or "No" if it is not.
* **Inquiry Type**: (`5`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Select all relevant inquiry types for the material, such as "yes" for available or "No" for not available.
* **Contact Info**: (`6`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is where you can enter any contact information for the material, such as a phone number or sales person's name.
* **Contact Name**: (`7`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is where you can enter the name of the person to contact for more information.
* **Email**: (`8`, `email`, required: false)
	+ This field is where you can enter an email address to contact for more information.
* **Additional Info**: (`9`, `note`, required: false)
	+ This field is where you can add any additional comments or notes about the material.
* **Assigned Tool**: (`10`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is where you can enter the tool or equipment assigned for the material.
* **Assigned Form**: (`11`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is where you can enter the form associated with the material.
* **Output File**: (`12`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is where you can enter any output files associated with the material.

	+ This field is where you can enter the category of the material.
