# Quality Testing Instrument Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather essential information for ordering quality testing instruments from our organization. This includes shipping details, payment terms, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Instrument Order**: Enter the name of the instrument you wish to order in the "Instrument Order" field.
2. **Shipping Details**: Provide your shipping address and any other relevant details.
3. **Additional Notes**: If you have any special requests or instructions, enter them here.
4. **Payment Details**: Enter your payment method and any relevant payment terms.
5. **Contact Info**: Enter your name and contact information to ensure we can reach out to you regarding your order.
6. **Shipping Cost**: Enter the shipping cost, which will be calculated based on the shipping details provided.
7. **Shipping Cost Currency**: Select the currency of the shipping cost.
8. **Payment Terms**: Enter your payment terms and conditions.
9. **Payment Terms Terms 1**: If necessary, provide any additional payment terms or conditions.
10. **Payment Terms Terms 2**: If necessary, provide any further payment terms or conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Instrument Order** (text, required: false): Enter the name of the instrument you wish to order.
* **Shipping Details** (text, required: false): Provide your shipping address and any other relevant details.
* **Additional Notes** (note, required: false): If you have any special requests or instructions, enter them here.
* **Payment Details** (text, required: false): Enter your payment method and any relevant payment terms.
* **Contact Info** (text, required: true): Enter your name and contact information to ensure we can reach out to you regarding your order.
* **Shipping Cost** (number, required: false): Enter the shipping cost, which will be calculated based on the shipping details provided.
* **Shipping Cost Currency** (select_one, required: false): Select the currency of the shipping cost.
* **Payment Terms** (text, required: true): Enter your payment terms and conditions.
* **Payment Terms Terms 1** (note, required: false): If necessary, provide any additional payment terms or conditions.
* **Payment Terms Terms 2** (note, required: false): If necessary, provide any further payment terms or conditions.

## Tips
- Ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any delays or issues with your order.
- Double-check your contact information to ensure we can reach out to you regarding your order.
- Clearly state any special requests or instructions in the "Additional Notes" field.
- Clarify any payment terms or conditions in the "Payment Terms" field.
