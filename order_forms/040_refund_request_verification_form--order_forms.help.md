# Internal note to reviewers:
The purpose of this form is to verify refund requests from customers. This form is used to gather information about the refund request, including the customer's name, email, phone number, order details, and reason for return. To avoid duplication, it is essential to ensure that this form is not filled out multiple times by the same customer. The form is intended for one-time use and should only be submitted once.

## Purpose
The refund_request_verification_form is used to collect information about a refund request from a customer. This form is necessary to verify the customer's identity and the reasons for their refund request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the customer_name field.
2. Enter your email address in the customer_email field.
3. Enter your phone number in the customer_phone field.
4. Select the date of the refund request in the refund_date field.
5. Enter the order number in the order_number field.
6. Select the status of the order in the order_status field.
7. Enter the reason for return in the reason_for_return field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **refund_request_verification_form**: This form is used to collect information about a refund request from a customer.
* **customer_name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **customer_email**: Enter your email address associated with your identification documents or order.
* **customer_phone**: Enter your phone number as it appears on your identification documents or order.
* **refund_date**: Select the date of the refund request.
* **order_number**: Enter the number of the order for which you are requesting a refund.
* **order_status**: Select the current status of the order.
* **reason_for_return**: Enter a detailed description of the reason for your refund request.
