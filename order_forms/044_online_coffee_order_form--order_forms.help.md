# Online Coffee Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Coffee Order Form is intended for customers to place orders or provide feedback, allowing businesses to gather specific information and understand customer preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill in the form with your order details or feedback as needed.
- Select the required fields to ensure you can place your order or submit feedback.
- If you're unsure about any field, please refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name (`customer_name`, text, optional):** Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
- **Customer Email (`customer_email`, email, optional):** Enter your email address to allow us to contact you.
- **Coffee Order (`coffee_order`, select multiple, optional):** Select the type of drink you would like to order (e.g., Coffee, Tea, Juice).
- **Customer Phone (`customer_phone`, text, optional):** Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Customer Message (`customer_message`, note, optional):** Leave a message or feedback about your order or experience.
- **Customer Category (`customer_category`, select one, optional):** Choose how you interacted with the form (Online Form, Offline Form, Other).
- **Customer Location (`customer_location`, select one, optional):** Select the store location you visited or interacted with.
- **Customer Preferences (`customer_preferences`, select multiple, optional):** Indicate your preferred methods of communication (Yes, No).
- **Form Submission (`form_submission`, text, optional):** Enter any additional information or comments.
- **Customer Address (`customer_address`, select one, optional):** Choose whether you would like us to have your address on file (Yes, No).
- **Customer City (`customer_city`, text, optional):** Enter your city for reference and contact purposes.
- **Customer State (`customer_state`, select one, optional):** Select your state for reference and contact purposes.

Note: Please refer to the specific business's instructions or guidelines if you have any doubts about any field.
