# Nail Order Form Tiff - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nail Order Form Tiff is used to collect information from customers for nail appointments. This form allows customers to provide their name, contact details, and appointment preferences. It is essential to fill in all required fields accurately to ensure proper communication and appointment scheduling.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first and last name in the "First name" and "Last name" fields.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select a date for your appointment from the calendar icon in the "Date" field.
5. Choose a time for your appointment from the dropdown list in the "Time" field.
6. If necessary, add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
7. Provide your address in the "Street" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your given name.
- **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your surname.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select a date for your appointment from the calendar icon.
- **Time** (`time`, time, required): Choose a time for your appointment from the dropdown list.
- **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your appointment.
- **Street** (`street`, text, optional): Enter the address where you would like to receive the appointment services.
