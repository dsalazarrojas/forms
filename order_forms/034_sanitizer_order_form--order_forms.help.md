# Sanitizer Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sanitizer Order Form is a form used for customers to place orders for sanitizers. The purpose of this form is to collect relevant information from customers to process their orders efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Address 1** in the "Address 1" field.
4. Enter your **Address 2** in the "Address 2" field.
5. Enter your **City** in the "City" field.
6. Enter your **State** in the "State" field.
7. Enter your **Zip** in the "Zip" field.
8. Enter your **Phone** in the "Phone" field.
9. Enter your **Email** (if applicable) in the "Email" field. Please note that an email address is not required to place an order.
10. Enter any **Order Notes** you may have in the "Order Notes" field.
11. Select the desired **Sanitize Type** from the dropdown menu.
12. Enter the **Quantity** of the sanitizer you want to order.
13. Enter the **Shipping Cost** (if applicable) in the "Shipping Cost" field.
14. Select the desired **Payment Gateway** from the dropdown menu.
15. Select the preferred **Payment Method** from the dropdown menu.
16. Choose whether or not to **Sync** your order to a specific platform (Shopify, WooCommerce, or BigCommerce).
17. Confirm that your order will be synced to the selected **Sync Platform**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Address 1**: Enter your physical address line 1.
* **Address 2**: Enter your physical address line 2.
* **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **State**: Enter your state or province where you reside.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip or postal code.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (optional).
* **Order Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Sanitize Type**: Select the type of sanitizer you wish to order, including 100ml, 250ml, and 500ml options.
* **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the sanitizer you wish to order.
* **Shipping Cost**: Enter the shipping cost (if applicable).
* **Payment Gateway**: Select the payment gateway to use for your order, including PayPal, Stripe, and Square options.
* **Payment Method**: Select the method of payment you prefer, including Bank Transfer, Credit Card, and Cash options.
* **Sync**: Choose whether or not to sync your order with a specific platform.
* **Sync Platform**: Confirm that your order will be synced with the selected platform.

Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure efficient and accurate order processing.
