# Luxury Watch Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for ordering luxury watches from our online store.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Name** in the text field provided.
2. Select your preferred **Watch Type** from the drop-down menu.
3. Enter the **Model Number** of the watch you would like to order, if known.
4. Select the **Color** of the watch you would like to order by checking the corresponding box(es).
5. Enter the **Price** you are willing to pay for the watch.
6. Select the **Date** you would like to receive the watch by.
7. Enter the **Time** of day you would like to receive the watch.
8. Enter your **Customer Email** for order fulfillment.
9. Enter your **Customer Phone** for any follow-up questions or concerns.
10. Enter your full **Shipping Address**, including street address, city, state, and zip code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Watch Type**: Select the luxury watch brand you would like to order from, such as Rolex, Omega, Cartier, or Audemars.
* **Model Number**: If you know the model number of the specific watch you would like to order, enter it here.
* **Color**: Select the color of the watch you would like to order by checking the box next to the corresponding color.
* **Price**: Enter the price you are willing to pay for the watch. Please note that prices may vary based on the selected watch brand and model.
* **Date**: Select the date you would like to receive the watch by. This is the date we will ship the watch to you.
* **Time**: Select the time of day you would like to receive the watch. Please note that shipping times are not guaranteed, but we will do our best to meet your request.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address where you would like to receive updates on your order.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number for any follow-up questions or concerns.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your full shipping address, including street address, city, state, and zip code. This information will be used to ship the watch to you.
