# Theme Cake Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Theme Cake Order Form is designed to collect information about your cake order preferences, including the theme, name, contact information, order details, payment method, and date of visit. This form is intended to be completed by customers ordering a cake from our bakery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the theme of your cake from the "Theme" dropdown menu.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
5. Provide a brief description of your order in the "Order Details" field (optional).
6. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment" dropdown menu.
7. Enter the date and time of your visit in the "Date of Visit" and "Time of Visit" fields (optional).
8. Select the payment method for your order from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.
9. Enter the payment amount (optional).
10. Check the status of your payment in the "Payment Status" field (optional).
11. Enter the payment date and time (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Theme** (`theme_cake_order_form_1`): Select the theme of your cake from the provided options (Theme A, Theme B, Theme C).
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your name as it appears on the order.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Order Details** (`order_details`): Provide a brief description of your order (optional).
* **Payment** (`payment`): Select your preferred payment method (Pay with credit card, Pay with PayPal, Pay with bank transfer, Pay with cash).
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`): Enter the date of your visit (optional).
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`): Enter the time of your visit (optional).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select your preferred payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, Cash).
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`): Enter the amount of your payment (optional).
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`): Check the status of your payment (Completed, In Progress, Pending).
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`): Enter the date of your payment (optional).
* **Payment Time** (`payment_time`): Enter the time of your payment (optional).
* **Theme Cake Order Form 2** (`theme_cake_order_form_2`): Select the theme of your second cake (optional).
* **Name 2** (`name_2`): Enter the name of the person receiving the second cake (optional).
* **Email 2** (`email_2`): Enter the email address of the person receiving the second cake (optional).
* **Phone 2** (`phone_2`): Enter the phone number of the person receiving the second cake (optional).
* **Order Details 2** (`order_details_2`): Provide a brief description of your second cake order (optional).
* **Payment 2** (`payment_2`): Select the payment method for your second cake (optional).
* **Date of Visit 2** (`date_of_visit_2`): Enter the date of your second visit (optional).
* **Time of Visit 2** (`time_of_visit_2`): Enter the time of your second visit (optional).
* **Payment Method 2** (`payment_method_2`): Select the payment method for your second cake (optional).
* **Payment Amount 2** (`payment_amount_2`): Enter the amount of your second payment (optional).
* **Payment Status 2** (`payment_status_2`): Check the status of your second payment (optional).
* **Payment Date 2** (`payment_date_2`): Enter the date of your second payment (optional).
* **Payment Time 2** (`payment_time_2`): Enter the time of your second payment (optional).

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter your contact information accurately to ensure proper communication.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* If you are ordering multiple cakes, please complete each section accurately for each cake.
* If you have any additional information about your order, please provide it in the "Order Details" field.
