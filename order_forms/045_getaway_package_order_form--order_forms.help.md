# Getaway Package Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Getaway Package Order Form is a simple form used to gather necessary information to book a package getaway. This form collects package selections, booking details, payment information, and contact details from the user.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your desired package (Package A, Package B, or Package C) by choosing one or multiple options in the first field.
2. Enter your booking details in the second field.
3. Enter your payment details in the third field.
4. Add any additional notes regarding your booking in the fourth field.
5. Enter your email address in the fifth field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
6. Enter your phone number in the sixth field.
7. Select the category of your package (Category A, Category B, or Category C) by choosing one option in the seventh field.
8. Enter your name in the last field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Package Selections** (`geteway_package_order_form_page_1`, select_multiple): Select one or multiple packages (Package A, Package B, or Package C) that you wish to book.
* **Booking Details** (`geteway_package_booking_form`, text): Enter the details of your booking, such as dates and time.
* **Payment Details** (`geteway_package_payment_form`, number): Enter your payment amount, ensuring that it aligns with the selected package's cost.
* **Notes** (`geteway_package_notes`, note): Add any additional notes or comments regarding your booking.
* **Email** (`geteway_package_email`, email): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted for booking updates or communications.
* **Phone** (`geteway_package_phone`, text): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for booking updates or communications.
* **Category** (`geteway_package_category`, select_one): Select the category of your package (Category A, Category B, or Category C).
* **Name** (`geteway_package_name`, text): Enter your name for reference and contact purposes.
