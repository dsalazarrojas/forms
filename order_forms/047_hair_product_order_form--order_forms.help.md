# hair_product_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The hair product order form is used to gather information for a hair product order, including details about the customer, order, and products selected.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the products you want to purchase in the **Product Selection** field.
2. Enter your **Shipping Address** and **Billing Address** fields. These are required fields.
3. Fill out the **Customer Details**, **Customer Email**, and **Customer Phone** fields with your information if you wish.
4. If you have any additional information about your order, enter it in the **Order Details**, **Shipping Details**, and **Payment Details** fields.
5. Select the **Order Date** and **Order Time** fields if you want to specify a date and time for your order.
6. Enter any additional information about your order in the **Order Note** field.
7. Enter any additional information about yourself in the **Customer Note** field.
8. Review your form and submit it when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Selection**: Select the products you want to purchase. This field is required.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your shipping address information. This field is required.
* **Billing Address**: Enter your billing address information. This field is required.
* **Order Details**: Enter any additional information about your order if you have it. This field is optional.
* **Shipping Details**: Enter any additional information about your shipping if you have it. This field is optional.
* **Payment Details**: Enter any additional information about your payment if you have it. This field is optional.
* **Customer Details**: Enter any additional information about yourself if you have it. This field is optional.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address. This field is optional.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number. This field is optional.
* **Order Date**: Select the date for your order if you want to specify one. This field is optional.
* **Order Time**: Select the time for your order if you want to specify one. This field is optional.
* **Order Note**: Enter any additional information about your order if you have it. This field is optional.
* **Customer Note**: Enter any additional information about yourself if you have it. This field is optional.
