## Packing Cube Set Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers for packing cube set orders. This form is designed to gather necessary details such as customer email, name, shipping address, city, and country to process the order efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your customer email address in the "Customer Email" field.
2. Provide your name in the "Customer Name" field.
3. Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field.
4. Enter your shipping city in the "Shipping City" field.
5. Enter your shipping country in the "Shipping Country" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Email
* **Customer Email (customer_email)**: Enter your valid email address.

### Customer Name
* **Customer Name (customer_name)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.

### Shipping Address
* **Shipping Address (shipping_address)**: Enter your complete shipping address, including street, apartment number, and any other relevant details.

### Shipping City
* **Shipping City (shipping_city)**: Enter the city where your order will be shipped to.

### Shipping Country
* **Shipping Country (shipping_country)**: Enter the country where your order will be shipped to.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information in all fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, feel free to contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
