# packaged_product_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The packaged_product_order_form is a form designed to collect information from customers for ordering packaged products. It is intended to facilitate the ordering process and ensure that the customer's needs are met.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Order Form" section by providing the required information.
2. Fill out the "Product Form" section by providing the necessary details about the product being ordered.
3. Fill out the "Customer Form" section by providing your personal and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Order Form
* **Order Form** (`order_form`, `text`, not required): This section is where you can enter any additional comments or notes about your order. If you have any special requests or instructions, you can enter them here.

### Product Form
* **Product Form** (`product_form`, `text`, not required): This section is where you can enter any additional details about the product being ordered. If you have any specific requirements or specifications, you can enter them here.

### Customer Form
* **Customer Form** (`customer_form`, `text`, not required): This section is where you can enter your personal and contact information. Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that your order is processed correctly.
