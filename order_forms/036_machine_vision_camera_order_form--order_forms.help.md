# machine_vision_camera_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for ordering machine vision cameras, which are used for various applications such as machine vision, surveillance, and automation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the camera type from the options provided.
2. Choose the desired resolution for your camera.
3. Provide shipping details, including address, city, and zip code.
4. Enter any additional application details and sales information as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details** (Order Details, `order_details`): Enter a brief description of your order.
* **Camera Type** (Camera Type, `camera_type`): Choose the type of camera you want to order, such as Machine Vision Camera or Camera.
* **Resolution** (Resolution, `resolution`): Select the resolution of your camera, which is the number of pixels that can be displayed on the screen.
* **Shipping Details** (Shipping Details, `shipping_details`): Enter the shipping details for your order, including the address, city, and zip code.
* **Shipping Address** (Shipping Address, `shipping_address`): Enter the shipping address.
* **Shipping City** (Shipping City, `shipping_city`): Enter the city for your shipping address.
* **Shipping State** (Shipping State, `shipping_state`): Enter the state for your shipping address.
* **Shipping Zip** (Shipping Zip, `shipping_zip`): Enter the zip code for your shipping address.
* **Application Details** (Application Details, `application_details`): Enter any additional details about your application.
* **Sales Info** (Sales Info, `sales_info`): Enter any sales information.
* **Sales Contact** (Sales Contact, `sales_contact`): Enter the sales contact information.
* **Sales Email** (Sales Email, `sales_email`): Enter the sales email.
* **Sales Phone** (Sales Phone, `sales_phone`): Enter the sales phone number.
* **Sales Notes** (Sales Notes, `sales_notes`): Enter any sales notes.
* **Logistics Info** (Logistics Info, `logistics_info`): Enter any logistics information.
* **Logistics Contact** (Logistics Contact, `logistics_contact`): Enter the logistics contact information.
* **Logistics Email** (Logistics Email, `logistics_email`): Enter the logistics email.
* **Logistics Phone** (Logistics Phone, `logistics_phone`): Enter the logistics phone number.
* **Logistics Notes** (Logistics Notes, `logistics_notes`): Enter any logistics notes.
