# Goods Receipt Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Goods Receipt Acknowledgement Form is used to confirm that goods or products have been received from a supplier or vendor in good condition. This form helps ensure accurate inventory management and facilitates smooth communication with suppliers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form correctly, follow these steps:

1.  Complete all the fields as required and ensure all information is accurate.
2.  If goods were received in a used condition, select "Used" from the drop-down menu for the "Goods Condition" field.
3.  Enter the value of the received goods in the "Goods Value" field.
4.  Ensure the form is submitted once all fields are complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Goods Received** (id: 2, type: text, required: false): This field is used to enter a brief description of the goods or products received.
*   **Goods Description** (id: 3, type: text, required: false): This field is used to provide a detailed description of the goods or products received.
*   **Goods Condition** (id: 4, type: select_one, required: false): This field allows you to select the condition of the received goods. Choose "New" if the goods are brand new, "Used" if they are pre-owned, or "Refurbished" if they are used but have been restored to a like-new condition.
*   **Goods Value** (id: 5, type: number, required: false): Enter the value of the received goods, including any relevant monetary values such as weight or quantity.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are complete and accurate to avoid any discrepancies or errors.
- If you need to add additional information not covered by this form, please contact your supervisor or team lead.
- This form is intended for internal use only, please keep it secure and confidential.
