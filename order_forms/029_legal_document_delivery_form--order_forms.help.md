# legal_document_delivery_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "legal_document_delivery_form" is a form used to collect information for the delivery of legal documents. It gathers necessary details such as delivery date, document type, and recipient contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the delivery date for the document.
2. Select the type of document being delivered.
3. Enter a brief description of the document.
4. Input the recipient's email address.
5. Enter the recipient's phone number.
6. Provide the delivery address.
7. Add any additional delivery instructions.
8. Attach any relevant documents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Date** (id: 1, date, required: false): Enter the date when the document is to be delivered.
* **Document Type** (id: 2, select_one, required: false): Select the type of document being delivered.
* **Document Description** (id: 3, text, required: false): Briefly describe the document being delivered.
* **Recipient Email** (id: 4, email, required: false): Enter the recipient's email address.
* **Recipient Phone** (id: 5, text, required: false): Enter the recipient's phone number.
* **Delivery Address** (id: 6, text, required: false): Provide the delivery address for the document.
* **Delivery Instructions** (id: 7, text, required: false): Add any additional delivery instructions.
* **Document Attachment** (id: 8, text, required: false): Attach any relevant documents to be delivered with the document.
