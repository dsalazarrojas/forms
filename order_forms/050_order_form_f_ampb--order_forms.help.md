# Order Form F&ampB - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers about their orders. It has three main sections, each designed to capture specific details about their orders.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on each section to begin filling out the form.
2. For **ps** (product/service selection), choose one or multiple options from the dropdown list.
3. For **ml** (option selection), select only one option from the dropdown list.
4. Review each section to ensure all required and optional information is entered before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **ps** (select multiple): This field allows you to choose one or multiple options for "ps". Choose from the available options to reflect your preferences.
* **ml** (select one): This field is used to select a single option for "ml". Choose the one that best suits your needs.

## Tips
