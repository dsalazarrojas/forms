# easter_lily_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about Easter Lily orders. It is intended for customers to provide details about their orders, including delivery dates, times, addresses, and status updates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Click on the form title "Easter Lily Order Form".
2. Fill out the form fields with the required information (marked as "required" in the form).
3. Review your completed form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details** (`order_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your Easter Lily order.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, optional): Select a date for your order delivery. This is an optional field, but it's helpful for our team to plan the delivery process.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, `time`, optional): Choose a time for your order delivery. This field is also optional, but it helps us to schedule the delivery more accurately.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, optional): Enter your address where you would like to receive your Easter Lilies. This field is optional, but it's useful for our team to know the correct delivery location.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you about your order. This field is optional, but it helps us to stay in touch with you about your order status.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number where we can contact you about your order. This field is optional, but it's helpful for our team to reach you directly.
* **Status** (`order_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the current status of your order from the options "Active", "Cancelled", or "Completed". This field is optional, but it helps us to track the order progress.
* **Notes** (`order_notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about your order. This field is optional, but it's a good place to provide any extra information about your order.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as the customer placing the order. This field is optional, but it's helpful for our team to know who the order is for.
* **Customer Order Number** (`customer_order_number`, `text`, optional): Enter a unique number assigned to your order by the customer. This field is optional, but it's helpful for our team to reference the order easily.

## Tips
- Make sure to review your completed form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out.
- If you have any questions or issues with your order, please don't hesitate to contact us.
- Our team will review and process your order as soon as possible after submitting the form.
