# Custom Neon Sign Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers to facilitate the ordering process of custom neon signs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Customer Info** section with your name, email, and phone number.
2. In the **Sign Details** section, provide a description of the sign you would like to order.
3. Choose a **Delivery Method** from the options provided (In-store pickup, Delivery, or Ship to address).
4. If you selected "Ship to address", fill out the **Delivery Address** field with the address you would like to ship the sign to.
5. Choose a **Payment Method** from the options provided (Cash, Credit card).
6. Review the **Total** section to ensure the order is correct.
7. Leave any additional **Notes** in the **Notes** field if you have any special requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (Text, Required): This field is for your name, email, and phone number.
* **Sign Details** (Text, Required): Describe the type of sign you would like to order.
* **Delivery Method** (Select, Required): Choose how you would like to receive your order (In-store pickup, Delivery, or Ship to address).
* **Delivery Address** (Text, Optional): Enter the address where you would like to ship the sign to, if you selected Ship to address.
* **Payment Method** (Select, Required): Choose how you would like to pay for your order (Cash, Credit card).
* **Total** (Text, Required): Review and confirm the total cost of your order.
* **Notes** (Text, Optional): Add any additional comments or special requests for your order.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us directly.
* We appreciate your custom and look forward to processing your order!
