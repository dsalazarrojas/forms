# Software Promo Items Order Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit order requests for software promo items. Please fill out this form to submit your order request, and our team will review and process it accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the Category for your order request.
2. Provide a brief Description of the item you are ordering.
3. Enter the Event Name and Order Date for the order.
4. Fill out the Shipping Details section with your shipping information.
5. Select the Shipping Method and enter any additional Shipping Method information if necessary.
6. Choose a Needed By Date and Time for your order.
7. Enter your Shipping Address, City, State, and Zip.
8. Provide your Contact Name, Phone, and Email.
9. Add any Order Comments if necessary.
10. Select Team Review options if applicable.
11. Fill out the Team Name, Email, Phone, and Team Comments if you are submitting on behalf of a team.
12. Finally, enter your Submitter Name and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (optional): Choose the category for your order request.
* **Description** (optional): Briefly describe the item you are ordering.
* **Event Name** (optional): Enter the name of the event for the order.
* **Order Date** (optional): Enter the date for the order.
* **Shipping Details** (optional): Enter your shipping information (name, address, city, state, zip).
* **Shipping Method** (optional): Choose how you want your item shipped.
* **Shipping Method Other** (optional): Enter any additional shipping method information if not listed.
* **Needed By Date** (optional): Enter the date by which you need the item shipped.
* **Needed By Time** (optional): Enter the time by which you need the item shipped.
* **Shipping Address** (optional): Enter your shipping address.
* **City** (optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (optional): Enter your state.
* **Zip** (optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Contact Name** (optional): Enter your name.
* **Phone** (optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (optional): Enter your email address.
* **Order Comments** (optional): Add any comments about your order.
* **Team Review** (optional): Choose team review options if applicable.
* **Team Name** (optional): Enter your team name.
* **Team Email** (optional): Enter your team email address.
* **Team Phone** (optional): Enter your team phone number.
* **Team Comments** (optional): Add any comments about your team.
* **Submitter Name** (optional): Enter your name.
* **Submitter Name** (optional): This field is a duplicate of the previous field; please fill out **Submitter Name** instead.

Note: Please make sure to fill out all required fields and provide accurate information to ensure efficient processing of your order request.
