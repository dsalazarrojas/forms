# Automated Order Routing Pilot Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Automated Order Routing Pilot Application Form is a tool used to collect information from businesses interested in participating in the Automated Order Routing Pilot program. The form helps the development team to understand the needs and processes of potential participants and tailor the pilot to meet their specific requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the sections below to ensure you have all the necessary information to complete the form.
2.  Enter your full legal name in the **Full Name** field.
3.  Enter your primary contact email in the **Email Address** field.
4.  Enter the name of your registered business in the **Company Name** field.
5.  Enter the average number of orders you process per month in the **Monthly Order Volume** field.
6.  Select all applicable **Primary Order Sources** that apply to your business operations.
7.  Choose the current **Routing Method** used in your business.
8.  Select the **Pilot Objectives** you would like to achieve.
9.  Enter the reason you are interested in participating in the pilot program in the **Reason for Participating** field.
10.  Choose the target **Implementation Timeline** for your business.
11.  Enter your preferred start date for the pilot program in the **Preferred Pilot Start Date** field.
12.  Enter the name and contact information of your **Technical Point of Contact**.
13.  Select **Yes, I agree** to the terms and conditions of the pilot program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full legal name.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your primary contact email.
*   **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your registered business.
*   **Monthly Order Volume** (`current_order_volume`, number, required): Enter the average number of orders you process per month.
*   **Primary Order Sources** (`order_sources`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable order sources that apply to your business operations.
*   **Current Routing Method** (`routing_method`, select_one, required): Choose the current routing method used in your business.
*   **Pilot Objectives** (`pilot_objectives`, select_multiple, optional): Select the objectives you would like to achieve.
*   **Reason for Participating** (`participation_reason`, text, required): Enter the reason you are interested in participating in the pilot program.
*   **Target Implementation Timeline** (`implementation_timeline`, select_one, required): Choose the target implementation timeline for your business.
*   **Preferred Pilot Start Date** (`pilot_start_date`, date, required): Enter your preferred start date for the pilot program.
*   **Technical Point of Contact** (`technical_contact`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of your technical point of contact.
*   **Do you agree to the pilot program terms?** (`terms_agreement`, select_one, required): Select **Yes, I agree** to the terms and conditions of the pilot program.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you have the necessary information and approvals to complete the form accurately.
*   Review each section carefully to ensure that you are providing unique and distinct information.
*   Do not duplicate the submission process.
*   If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please contact us.
