# university_dining_bulk_food_order_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for bulk food orders at the university's dining facilities. It is designed to be completed by a reporter, who will be responsible for coordinating the food order, and allows them to specify details such as the date of delivery, delivery location, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields as follows:
   - **Food Order Details** (`food_order_details`, `text`, required): Enter a description of the food order.
   - **reporter** (`delivery_reporter`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the reporter who is coordinating the food order.
   - **Food Order Items** (`food_order_items`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the items to be ordered.
   - **Date Of Delivery** (`date_of_delivery`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the delivery.
   - **Delivery Location** (`delivery_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location where the food will be delivered.
   - **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
   - **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
   - **Contact** (`contact_reporter`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the contact person.
   - **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about the food order.
   - **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required): Enter the tool assigned to the reporter.
2. If applicable, fill in the optional fields:
   - **Food Order Details 2** (`food_order_details_2`, `text`): Enter any additional details about the food order.
   - **reporter 2** (`delivery_reporter_2`, `text`): Enter the name of the secondary reporter who is coordinating the food order.
   - **Food Order Items 2** (`food_order_items_2`, `select_multiple`): Select additional items to be ordered.
   - **Date Of Delivery 2** (`date_of_delivery_2`, `date`): Enter the date of the delivery.
   - **Delivery Location 2** (`delivery_location_2`, `text`): Enter the location where the food will be delivered.
   - **Contact Name 2** (`contact_name_2`, `text`): Enter the name of the secondary contact person.
   - **Contact Phone 2** (`contact_phone_2`, `text`): Enter the phone number of the secondary contact person.
   - **Contact 2** (`contact_reporter_2`, `select_multiple`): Select the secondary contact person.
   - **Notes 2** (`notes_2`, `note`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the food order.
   - **Assigned Tool 2** (`assigned_tool_2`, `text`): Enter the tool assigned to the secondary reporter.
3. Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting it.
