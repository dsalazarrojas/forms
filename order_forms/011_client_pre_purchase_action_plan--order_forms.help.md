# Client Pre Purchase Action Plan - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from clients before finalizing a purchase, ensuring that their needs and expectations are understood and met.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email Address** in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the **Product or Service of Interest** that you are considering purchasing.
3. Choose when you plan to **Purchase** by selecting one of the options.
4. Select your **Budget Range**.
5. Indicate if you are the **Decision Maker** or part of the **Decision Committee**.
6. If you are currently using a similar product or service, describe your **Current Solution**.
7. Select the **Challenges** you are trying to solve (select all that apply).
8. List your **Must-Have Features** or requirements.
9. (Optional) List your **Nice-to-Have Features** or requirements.
10. If applicable, describe your **Integration Requirements**.
11. Enter the **Number of Users** that will be using this product or service.
12. Select your **Desired Implementation Timeframe** (if applicable).
13. Select your **Training Requirements** (if applicable).
14. Indicate if you are **Considering Other Vendors** (if applicable).
15. If applicable, list the names of the **Other Vendors** you are considering.
16. If you have **Previous Experience** with similar products or services, select your experience level.
17. If you have any **Concerns or Questions**, please describe them.
18. If you would like a product demo, mention your preferred time frame.
19. Describe how you **Hear About Us** (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Header**: This is a note field where you can add any additional information about your purchase needs and expectations.
- **First Name**: Enter your first name.
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
- **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
- **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
- **Company Name**: If you are representing a company, enter your company name (if applicable).
- **Product or Service of Interest**: Select the product or service you are considering purchasing from the provided options.
- **When Do You Plan to Purchase**: Select when you plan to purchase the product or service.
- **Budget Range**: Select your budget range for the purchase.
- **Are You the Decision Maker**: Indicate if you are the decision maker or part of the decision committee.
- **Current Solution**: If you are currently using a similar product or service, describe it.
- **Challenges**: Select all the challenges you are trying to solve with the product or service (select all that apply).
- **Must-Have Features**: List your non-negotiable requirements for the product or service.
- **Nice-to-Have Features**: List features that would be a bonus for the product or service.
- **Integration Requirements**: If applicable, describe the systems that need to integrate with the product or service.
- **Number of Users**: Enter the number of users that will be using the product or service.
- **Desired Implementation Timeframe**: Select your desired implementation timeframe.
- **Training Requirements**: Select your training requirements (if applicable).
- **Considering Other Vendors**: Indicate if you are considering other vendors (if applicable).
- **Other Vendors**: If you are considering other vendors, list their names.
- **Previous Experience**: If you have experience with similar products or services, select your experience level.
- **Concerns or Questions**: If you have any concerns or questions, describe them.
- **Preferred Demo Time**: If you would like a product demo, mention your preferred time frame.
- **How Did You Hear About Us**: Select how you heard about us (if applicable).
- **Additional Comments**: Use this field to add any additional information about your purchase needs and expectations.
