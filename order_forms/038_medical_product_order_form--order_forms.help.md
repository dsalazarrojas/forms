# Medical Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit orders for medical products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Choose if you would like to order medical products by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Order Form" field.
2.  Enter your customer information in the "Customer Info" field.
3.  Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.
4.  Choose the items you would like to order from the "Order Items" dropdown menu.
5.  Enter your shipping details in the "Shipping Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Order Form** (`Yes/No`, yes/no, false): This field allows you to determine if you want to order medical products or not. Select either "Yes" or "No" to proceed with your order.
*   **Customer Info** (`text`, false): Enter your details as required for us to process your order.
*   **Payment Method** (`select_one`, false): Choose one of the available payment options to complete your order: Card Payments, PayPal, or Stripe.
*   **Order Items** (`yes/no`, false): Choose the medical products you want to order.
*   **Shipping Details** (`text`, false): Enter your shipping details for your order.
