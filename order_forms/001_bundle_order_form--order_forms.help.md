# Bundle Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Bundle Order Form is used to place orders for specific bundles or packages of services. This form gathers customer information and order details to ensure accurate and efficient fulfillment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Full Name**, which is a required field to ensure our records accurately reflect your identity.
2. Provide a valid **Email Address**, which we use for correspondence and order updates.
3. Enter your **Phone Number**, which we use to contact you with any questions or updates.
4. Enter your **Delivery Address**, which we use to ship the bundles to you.
5. Select the desired **Bundle Type**, which determines the specific services included in your order.
6. Choose the **Quantity** of bundles you wish to order.
7. Select a **Preferred Delivery Date** for fulfillment.
8. If needed, provide **Special Instructions or Notes** to clarify any specific requirements for this order.
9. Lastly, select your preferred **Payment Method**, which can be Credit Card, Bank Transfer, Purchase Order, or Monthly Invoicing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`Customer Full Name`, Text, Required): Enter your complete legal name for our records.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, Email, Required): Enter a valid email address for us to contact you and send order updates.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, Text, Required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you with any questions or updates.
* **Delivery Address** (`Delivery Address`, Text, Required): Enter your full street address, city, state, and zip for bundle delivery.
* **Select Bundle Type** (`Bundle Selection`, Select One, Required): Choose the bundle type that best fits your needs from the available options.
* **Quantity** (`Order Quantity`, Number, Required): Enter the number of bundles you wish to order.
* **Preferred Delivery Date** (`Preferred Delivery Date`, Date, Required): Select a date for bundle fulfillment.
* **Special Instructions or Notes** (`Special Instructions`, Text, Optional): Provide any specific requirements or notes for this order.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`Payment Method Preference`, Select One, Required): Choose how you would like to pay for your order (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, Purchase Order, Monthly Invoicing).
