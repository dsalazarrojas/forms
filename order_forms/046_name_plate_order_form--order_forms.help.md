# Name Plate Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Name Plate Order Form is a tool used by customers to create and customize their name plates, which can be applied to a variety of objects or machines, such as vehicles, laptops, or other items that require identification or branding.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start the order process, begin by filling out the "Order Details" field.
2.  Next, select the "Type of Order" field to specify the type of name plate you want.
3.  Then, choose a "Name Plate Type" and "Material" as desired.
4.  Select your preferred font for the name plate.
5.  Choose the quantity and size of the name plate as needed.
6.  Fill out your name, email, and other contact information to help us with any further communication about your order.
7.  If you have any special requests for your order, you can fill out the "Special Request" field.
8.  Finally, click on the "Submit" button to submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your name as you would like to be displayed on the name plate.
* **Order Details**: Provide a description of the object or machine that the name plate will be applied to.
* **Type of Order**: Choose the type of name plate you want to order. This will help us understand your needs and requirements.
* **Date**: Enter the date when the order is needed.
* **Time**: Enter the time when the order is needed.
* **Name Plate Type**: Choose the type of name plate you want. This will affect the design and appearance of the plate.
* **Material**: Select the material you would like for the name plate.
* **Font**: Choose the font style for your name plate.
* **Size**: Choose the size of the name plate as needed.
* **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of name plates you need.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with any further information about your order.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for any additional contact.
* **Address**: Enter your address for shipping purposes.
* **Name Plate Number**: Enter a number for your name plate as needed.
* **Name Plate Size**: Enter the size of the name plate as needed.
* **Special Request**: If you have any special requests, such as rush order, enter them here.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or information about your order.
* **Custom Code**: Enter any custom code for your order (e.g. for internal use).
* **Order Type**: Choose the type of order you want to place.
* **Order Note**: Enter any notes or information about your order.
* **Name Plate Type**: Choose the type of name plate you want (this is different from the "Name Plate Type" field).
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your order.
* **Error Message**: This field is not displayed by default but will appear if there are any issues with your order.
* **Success Message**: This field is not displayed by default but will appear if your order is successful.
* **Custom Code**: This field is not displayed by default but will appear for internal use.
