# Detox Drinks Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Detox Drinks Order Form is a contact information and order form used to collect details from users for order processing and communication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Fill in your Last Name.
3. Provide your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, required/optional): Fill in your first name to identify yourself. 
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address so we can contact you about your order. This will be used for order updates and confirmation.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your order if needed.

## Tips
- Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
- Be aware that this form will help our team communicate with you effectively and ensure a smooth ordering process.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to this help guide for clarification.
