# Concert Organizer Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from concert attendees to complete their order details for tickets and add-ons.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the concert event you wish to attend from the "Select Event" field.
2. Choose your preferred date from the "Event Date" field.
3. Specify the number of tickets you need in the "Number of Tickets" field.
4. Select the type of ticket you require from the "Ticket Type" field.
5. Choose your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" field.
6. Enter your billing address in the "Billing Address" field.
7. Review and agree to the terms and conditions in the "Terms and Conditions" field.
8. Confirm that you want to receive a confirmation email in the "Send Confirmation Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Event** (2, Select Event, required): Choose the concert event you wish to attend.
* **Event Date** (3, Event Date, required): Select your preferred date from the available options.
* **Number of Tickets** (4, Number of Tickets, required): Specify the number of tickets you need.
* **Ticket Type** (5, Ticket Type, required): Select the type of ticket you require.
* **Payment Method** (19, Payment Method, required): Choose how you want to pay for your tickets.
* **Billing Address** (23, Billing Address, required): Enter your billing address for payment purposes.
* **Terms and Conditions** (24, Terms and Conditions, required): Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the concert organizer.
* **Send Confirmation Email** (25, Send Confirmation Email, required): Confirm that you want to receive an order confirmation email.
* **Primary Attendee Name** (9, Primary Attendee Name, required): Enter the full name of the ticket purchaser.
* **Primary Attendee Email** (10, Primary Attendee Email, required): Enter the email address associated with the ticket purchaser.
* **Primary Attendee Phone** (11, Primary Attendee Phone, required): Enter the contact phone number for the ticket purchaser.
* **Additional Attendee Names** (12, Additional Attendee Names, optional): Enter the names of any additional ticket holders.
* **Special Requests** (13, Special Requests, optional): Enter any special requests or seating preferences.
* **Add-On Services** (14, Add-On Services, optional): Choose any add-on services you would like to include with your order.
* **Parking Pass** (15, Parking Pass, optional): Choose if you would like to add a parking pass to your order.
* **Merchandise Package** (16, Merchandise Package, optional): Choose a merchandise package to add to your order.
* **Program Booklet** (17, Program Booklet, optional): Choose if you would like to add a program booklet to your order.
* **Payment Information** (18, Payment Information, optional): Enter your secure payment details.
* **Card Number** (20, Card Number, optional): Enter your card details securely.
* **Card Expiry** (21, Card Expiry, optional): Enter the expiry date of your card in MM/YY format.
* **Card CVV** (22, Card CVV, optional): Enter the 3-digit security code of your card.
* **Additional Attendee Information** (Additional Attendee Names, optional): This field is only visible when additional attendees are selected.
* **Price Per Ticket** (6, Price Per Ticket, optional): This is an automatic calculation based on your order details. 
* **Order Total** (7, Order Total, optional): This is an automatic calculation based on your order details.

## Tips
* Make sure to review all fields carefully before submitting the form.
* Double-check your order details to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any special requests or add-ons, be sure to select them accordingly.
* Review the terms and conditions before agreeing to them.
* Confirm that you want to receive an order confirmation email.
