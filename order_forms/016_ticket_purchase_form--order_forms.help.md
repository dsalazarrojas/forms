# Ticket Purchase Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ticket Purchase Form is a user interface designed to collect information from individuals or businesses interested in purchasing tickets for a specific event or event series.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. If you're unsure about any of the fields, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification or official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification or official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted for updates or confirmation of your ticket purchase.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your primary phone number where you can be contacted for updates or confirmation of your ticket purchase.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, optional): Read and acknowledge the terms and conditions of the ticket purchase, including any applicable laws or regulations.
* **Ticket Quantity** (`ticket_quantity`, number, optional): Select the number of tickets you wish to purchase. Note that ticket availability may be limited, and this field will be used to determine how many tickets can be allocated to you.
* **Ticket Price** (`ticket_price`, number, optional): This field is informational only and will display the price of the ticket(s) you are purchasing. You do not need to fill out this field.
* **Purchase** (`purchase_button`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" to confirm your ticket purchase or "No" to cancel.
* **Purchase Options** (`purchase_button_options`, select_multiple, optional): If you are purchasing multiple tickets or would like to add any additional options to your order, please select your desired options here. You can choose multiple options by selecting "Yes" for each option that applies to you.

## Tips
* Be sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the event organizers for assistance.
* Ticket availability and prices are subject to change, so please be aware of these changes before making your purchase.
