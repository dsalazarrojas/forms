# hamilton_prom_2021 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the Hamilton Prom 2021 event. It will be used to gather details such as participant's first name, last name, email address, phone number, order date, time, and any additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Click on the "Start Form" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Select the "Pages" tab to view the form sections.
3. Fill in the relevant information for each field, following the instructions below for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (text, required: false): Enter your first name.

### Last Name
* **Last name** (text, required: false): Enter your last name.

### Email
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter your email address in the format of `example@example.com`.

### Phone
* **Phone** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number.

### Order Date
* **Order Date** (date, required: false): Select the date you would like to place your order.

### Time
* **Time** (time, required: false): Select the time you would like to place your order.

### Note
* **Note** (note, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes you would like to include with your order.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out this form, don't hesitate to contact us.
