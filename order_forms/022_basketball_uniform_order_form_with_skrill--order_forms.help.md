# Basketball Uniform Order Form With Skrill - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information for ordering basketball uniforms with Skrill payment processing. It is intended for players who are ordering uniforms for themselves or others.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your player's full name in the "Player Full Name" field.
2. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email Address" field.
3. Select the desired jersey number in the "Desired Jersey Number" field.
4. Select the name to be printed on the back of the jersey in the "Name on Back of Jersey" field.
5. Choose the size of the jersey and shorts in the "Jersey Size" and "Shorts Size" fields, respectively.
6. Enter the number of sets ordered in the "Number of Sets Ordered" field.
7. Enter the email associated with your Skrill account in the "Your Skrill Account Email" field.
8. Enter the total amount due in the "Total Amount to Pay" field.
9. Select the date of the order in the "Date of Order" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Full Name** (`player_full_name`, text, required): Enter the player's full name as it should appear on the uniform.
* **Contact Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address associated with your contact information.
* **Desired Jersey Number** (`jersey_number`, number, required): Select the desired jersey number for the player.
* **Name on Back of Jersey** (`back_name`, text, required): Enter the name that should appear on the back of the jersey.
* **Jersey Size** (`jersey_size`, select one, required): Choose the size of the jersey.
* **Shorts Size** (`shorts_size`, select one, required): Choose the size of the shorts.
* **Number of Sets Ordered** (`quantity_ordered`, number, required): Enter the number of sets ordered, where one set includes one jersey and one pair of shorts.
* **Your Skrill Account Email** (`skrill_email`, email, required): Enter the email associated with your Skrill account.
* **Total Amount to Pay** (`total_amount_due`, number, required): Enter the total amount due for the order.
* **Date of Order** (`order_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the order.
