# Winter Skin Care Wish List - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Winter Skin Care Wish List form is used to collect information about users' skin types, concerns, and preferences to provide personalized guidance for winter skin care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant options from the "Winter Skin Care Wish List" dropdown menu to indicate if this form is relevant to you.
2. Enter your name in the "customer_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Choose your skin type from the "skin_type" dropdown menu.
6. Select any skin concerns you are currently experiencing from the "skin_concern" dropdown menu.
7. Choose your preferred type of skin care product from the "product_preference" dropdown menu.
8. If you have any additional comments or instructions, you can add them in the "Additional Comments" field.
9. Finally, click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Winter Skin Care Wish List** (Select)
	+ Choose "Yes" if you want to be considered for this wish list.
* **customer_name** (Text)
	+ Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **email** (Email)
	+ Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **phone** (Text)
	+ Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **skin_type** (Select)
	+ Choose your skin type: Normal Skin, Dry Skin, Oily Skin, Combination Skin, or Sensitive Skin.
* **skin_concern** (Select Multiple)
	+ Select any skin concerns you are currently experiencing from the list: Acne, Aging, Dark spots, Fine lines, Hyper-pigmentation, Pore Size, Redness, Sagging, Scarring, Stretchmarks, or Uneven skin tone, Wrinkles.
* **product_preference** (Select)
	+ Choose your preferred type of skin care product: Moisturizer, Serum, Eye Cream, Face Mask, Face Wash, Sunscreen, Toner, Exfoliant.
* **Additional Comments** (Note)
	+ Enter any additional comments or instructions you have about your skin care wish list.
* **Submit** (Text)
	+ Click to submit your wish list for processing.
