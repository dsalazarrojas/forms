# Work Order Completion Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work Order Completion Acknowledgement Form is used to obtain a client's acknowledgement of work completion, ensuring clear communication and documentation of the work's status. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as the client.
2. Enter the order number associated with the work.
3. Provide the date when the work was completed.
4. Sign below to confirm that you acknowledge the work order was completed.
5. Have your representative sign and date the completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the client.
* **Order Number** (`order_number`, text, optional): Enter the order number associated with the work. If you don't have an order number, leave this field blank.
* **Work Order Date** (`work_order_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the work was completed or started. If you don't have this information, leave this field blank.
* **Completed By** (`completed_by`, text, required): Enter your representative's name that completed the work.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or notes about the completion of the work.
* **Acknowledgement** (`acknowledgement`, text, required): Check and sign that you acknowledge the work order was completed.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, text, optional): This field is intended for your signature, if you wish to sign the form electronically or manually.
* **Completed By Signature** (`completed_by_signature`, text, required): Enter your representative's signature who completed the work.
* **Completed Date** (`completed_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the work was completed.
* **Work Order Completion Acknowledgement** (`work_order_completion_acknowledgement`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the work order completion. 

## Tips
- Please make sure to enter accurate information to ensure correct documentation and communication.
- This form is a one-page document.
- If you need to make any changes or updates, please contact form@gic.mx. 

Note: This guide is designed to be simple, yet comprehensive enough to help end-users understand the form's purpose, how to complete it, and what each field means without requiring additional context or technical knowledge.
