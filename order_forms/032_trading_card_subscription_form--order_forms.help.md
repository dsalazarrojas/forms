# Trading Card Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains how to complete the Trading Card Subscription Form.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select your preferred trading cards from the options provided.
2. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
3. Enter your first name.
4. Enter your last name.
5. Enter your address.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Trading Cards** (`trading_card_subscription_form`, select_one, required/optional): Select your preferred trading cards. Please choose one or more cards from the list.
* **Collecting Preferences** (`collecting_preferences`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your collecting preferences.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred payment options. Please choose one or more options from the list.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address for delivery purposes.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or issues, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
