# Breakout Session Material Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help breakout session organizers and attendees provide necessary information for their sessions, including details about event and session specifics, material and equipment needs, and contact information. The form is used to ensure accurate planning and preparation for the event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information (Requestor Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number).
2. Provide details about your organization or company.
3. Enter event and session details (Event Name, Session Name, Session Date, Session Time, Room Number).
4. Specify the expected number of attendees.
5. Choose any necessary audio-visual equipment and printing materials.
6. Choose any refreshments needed.
7. Determine the room setup preference.
8. Specify any special requirements.
9. Enter the required setup time for the room.
10. Provide the name and contact information of the onsite contact person.
11. If applicable, enter the budget code or PO number.
12. Add any additional notes or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requestor Full Name** (`requestor_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`requestor_email`, email, **Required**): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`requestor_phone`, text, **Required**): Enter your phone number.
* **Company or Organization** (`company_or_organization`, text, **Required**): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, **Required**): Enter the name of the event or conference.
* **Breakout Session Name** (`session_name`, text, **Required**): Enter the name or title of your session.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, **Required**): Enter the date of your session.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, **Required**): Enter the time of your session.
* **Room Number** (`room_number`, text, **Required**): Enter the room or location of your session.
* **Expected Number of Attendees** (`expected_attendees`, number, **Required**): Enter the expected number of attendees.
* **AV Equipment Needed** (`av_equipment`, select multiple, **Required**): Choose any audio-visual equipment needed (Projector, Screen, Microphone, Speakers, Laptop, HDMI Cables, Laser Pointer, Whiteboard, Flip Chart, None).
* **Printing Materials** (`printing_materials`, select one, **Required**): Choose if you need printed materials (Yes, Handouts, Yes, Workbooks, Yes, Name Tags, Yes, Certificates, No Printing Needed).
* **Print Quantity** (`print_quantity`, number, **Optional**): Enter the number of copies needed (if selected to print materials).
* **Stationery Supplies** (`stationery_supplies`, select multiple, **Required**): Choose any stationery supplies needed (Pens, Pencils, Notepads, Sticky Notes, Markers, Highlighters, Name Tag Stickers, Lanyards, None).
* **Refreshments** (`refreshments`, select one, **Required**): Choose if you need refreshments (Coffee and Tea Service, Water Bottles Only, Light Snacks, Full Catering, No Refreshments).
* **Room Setup Preference** (`room_setup`, select one, **Required**): Choose the room setup preference (Theater Style, Classroom Style, U-Shape, Round Tables, Boardroom Style, Hollow Square).
* **Signage Needed** (`signage_needed`, select one, **Required**): Choose if you need any signage (Yes, Room Sign, Yes, Directional Signs, Yes, Both, No Signage Needed).
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, text, **Optional**): Enter any special requirements or needs.
* **Required Setup Time** (`setup_time`, time, **Required**): Enter the time when the room should be ready.
* **Onsite Contact Person** (`contact_person_onsite`, text, **Required**): Enter the name and contact information of the onsite contact person.
* **Budget Code or PO Number** (`budget_code`, text, **Optional**): Enter the budget code or PO number (if applicable).
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, **Optional**): Enter any additional notes or information.
