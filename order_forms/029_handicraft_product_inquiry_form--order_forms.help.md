# Handicraft Product Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Handicraft Product Inquiry Form is designed to gather information from customers about their interest in a specific product. Please fill out this form to help our team better understand your needs and provide you with more information about the product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information, including your email address and phone number.
2. Enter the name of the product you're inquiring about.
3. Provide a brief message with any specific questions or concerns you have about the product.
4. Enter your email address and phone number to ensure we can contact you with more information about the product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as you would like it to be addressed.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the product you're inquiring about.
* **Product Price** (`product_price`, number, optional): If you're inquiring about the price of a specific product, please enter the price.
* **Inquiry Message** (`inquiry_message`, text, optional): Provide a brief message with any specific questions or concerns you have about the product.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with more information about the product.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with more information about the product.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): If you're inquiring on behalf of someone else, please enter their email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): If you're inquiring on behalf of someone else, please enter their phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, unless otherwise specified.
* If you're inquiring on behalf of someone else, please make sure to include their information as well.
* Our team will review your inquiry and respond to you as soon as possible.
