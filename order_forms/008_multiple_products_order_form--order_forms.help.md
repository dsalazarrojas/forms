# Multiple Products Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Multiple Products Order Form is used to collect information from customers regarding their order preferences. This form is designed to be completed by customers to provide their input on multiple products, including whether they want to purchase or not.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "order_form" field and enter the name of the product or a brief description of the product.
2. In the "customer_info" field, provide your name, contact number, and any other relevant information about yourself that we need to know.
3. In the "products" field, select all the products you would like to order by clicking on the "Yes" option next to each product. If you're unsure about a product, you can leave it blank.
4. In the "comments" field, enter any additional comments or questions you may have about your order.
5. Click on the "submit" field to submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **order_form** (Text Field): Enter the name of the product or a brief description of the product you would like to order.
* **customer_info** (Text Field): Provide your name, contact number, and any other relevant information about yourself that we need to know.
* **products** (Select Multiple Field):
	+ "Yes" option: Select this if you would like to purchase the product.
	+ Leave blank if you are unsure or do not want to purchase the product.
* **comments** (Note Field): Enter any additional comments or questions you may have about your order.
* **submit** (Text Field): Click on this field to submit your order.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will review your order and get back to you as soon as possible.
