# Mortgage Payment Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mortgage Payment Authorization Form is a crucial document that financial institutions use to verify the identity and creditworthiness of borrowers applying for a mortgage. This form collects information from the borrower to ensure a smooth and secure mortgage application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
* Fill in the borrower's name and contact information, including their email address and phone number.
* Enter the loan amount and payment date, if applicable.
* Choose the payment method and authorization type.
* Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and honestly, as this information will be used for verification purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Borrower's Name** (`borrower_name`, text, required)
* This field requires the borrower's full name as it appears on their identification documents, such as a passport or driver's license.
### 2. **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, number, required)
* Enter the exact amount of the loan you are applying for.
### 3. **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, optional)
* If you have chosen a specific payment date, enter the date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
### 4. **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional)
* Choose the payment method that applies to your loan, such as 'Yes' for an in-person payment or 'No' for an online payment.
### 5. **Email** (`email`, email, optional)
* Enter your email address where you can be contacted for updates on your loan application.
### 6. **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional)
* Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for updates on your loan application.
### 7. **Address** (`address`, note, optional)
* This field is for additional information about your address, if necessary.
### 8. **Authorization Type** (`authorization_type`, select_one, optional)
* Choose the authorization type that applies to your loan, such as 'Yes' for authorization by a parent or 'No' for authorization by a guardian.

## Tips
* Please fill in the form accurately and honestly, as this information will be used for verification purposes.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your financial institution for clarification.
* Ensure that all fields are filled in before submitting the form to avoid any delays or rejection of your application.
