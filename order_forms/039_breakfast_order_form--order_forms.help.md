# Breakfast Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Breakfast Order Form is designed to collect customer information and their preferences for breakfast orders. It is used to gather details such as customer name, contact information, order type, and order preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **Customer Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number** to ensure we can contact you regarding your order.
2. Select the **Order Type** of "Pickup" or "Delivery" to choose how you want to receive your order.
3. Choose a date and time for your **Order Date** and **Order Time**.
4. Select your preferred **Main Item** from the list.
5. Add a **Protein Addition** to your order if desired.
6. Select your preferred **Sides** and **Beverages**.
7. Choose the quantity of your order in the **Quantity** field.
8. Indicate any **Dietary Preferences** you have.
9. If applicable, provide **Special Instructions** for your order.
10. If you selected delivery, enter your **Delivery Address** and any **Delivery Notes**.
11. Choose a **Payment Method** for your order.
12. If you have a **Promo Code**, enter it here.
13. Finally, confirm that you have read and accept our **Terms and Conditions**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for order confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Order Type**: Choose between "Pickup" and "Delivery" to select how you want to receive your order.
* **Order Date**: Choose a date for when you need your order.
* **Order Time**: Choose a time for when you want to receive your order.
* **Main Item**: Select your preferred breakfast item from the list.
* **Protein Addition**: Add a protein source to your order if desired.
* **Sides**: Choose your preferred sides from the list.
* **Beverages**: Select your preferred drinks from the list.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of orders you are placing.
* **Dietary Preferences**: Select any dietary needs you have.
* **Special Instructions**: Enter any special cooking instructions or requests.
* **Delivery Address**: Enter your full address if you selected delivery.
* **Delivery Notes**: Enter any special delivery instructions.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you will pay for your order.
* **Promo Code**: Enter any promo code you have.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Confirm that you have read and accept our terms and conditions.
