# graphic_design_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact and subscription information from graphic design clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Enter your company name.
4. Select your preferred contact method (phone, email, or in-app).
5. Choose your subscription type (basic, premium, or enterprise).
6. Select the services you would like to subscribe to (web design, print media, or branding).
7. Enter the start date of your subscription.
8. Enter the end date of your subscription.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name.
* **E-mail** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, optional): Enter your company name.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Phone: Select to receive contact via phone.
	+ E-mail: Select to receive contact via email.
	+ In App: Select to receive contact within the app.
* **Subscription Type** (`subscription_type`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Basic: Select for basic subscription.
	+ Premium: Select for premium subscription.
	+ Enterprise: Select for enterprise subscription.
* **Services** (`services`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Web Design: Select for web design services.
	+ Print Media: Select for print media services.
	+ Branding: Select for branding services.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the start date of your subscription.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the end date of your subscription.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (e-mail).
* Select all applicable services.
* Enter accurate start and end dates for your subscription.
