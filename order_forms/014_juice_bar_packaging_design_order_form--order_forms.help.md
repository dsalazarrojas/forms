# Juice Bar Packaging Design Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Juice Bar Packaging Design Order Form is designed to collect information for custom packaging design orders. This form helps the design team create a unique and effective design for your product.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on the "Submit" button at the bottom of the form to start the ordering process.
2.  Fill out the "General Info" field with your name and contact information.
3.  Choose the type of packaging you are interested in by selecting one of the options in the "Packaging Type" dropdown menu.
4.  Enter the name of the product you are interested in customizing.
5.  Select the color scheme of the background and text by choosing from the "Label Color" and "Text Color" dropdown menus.
6.  Specify the font styles by entering the font family and size in the corresponding fields.
7.  Provide your design review and preferences in the "Design Review" field.
8.  Enter the order date and time in the "Order Date" and "Order Time" fields.
9.  Enter your email address and phone number for further communication and order tracking.
10.  Click on the "Submit" button to complete the form and send it to the design team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (`general_info`, text, required): This field is used to collect your name and contact information. Please fill it out accurately and thoroughly.
* **Packaging Type** (`packaging_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of packaging you are interested in (Juice Boxes, Bottled, Carton, or Other).
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the product you are interested in customizing.
* **Label Color** (`label_color`, select_multiple, optional): Select the label color for your packaging design.
* **Design File** (`design_file`, text, optional): Please provide any design files or references that you would like the design team to consider.
* **Font Family** (`font_family`, text, optional): Specify the font family you would like to use for the design.
* **Font Style** (`font_style`, text, optional): Enter the font style you would like to use for the design.
* **Background Image** (`background_image`, text, optional): If you have a background image you would like to use, please provide it here.
* **Background Color** (`background_color`, text, optional): Select the background color for your design.
* **Text Color** (`text_color`, text, optional): Choose the text color for your design.
* **Font Size** (`font_size`, text, optional): Enter the font size you would like to use.
* **Font Weight** (`font_weight`, text, optional): Specify the font weight you would like to use.
* **Text Align** (`text_align`, text, optional): Choose the text alignment you prefer.
* **Line Height** (`line_height`, text, optional): Enter the line height you would like to use.
* **Spacing Between Lines** (`spacing_between_lines`, text, optional): Specify the spacing between lines.
* **Padding** (`padding`, text, optional): Enter the padding you would like to use.
* **Margin** (`margin`, text, optional): Specify the margin you would like to use.
* **Border** (`border`, text, optional): Choose the border type you prefer.
* **Corner Radius** (`corner_radius`, text, optional): Enter the corner radius you would like to use.
* **Design Review** (`design_review`, note, optional): This is a free-text field where you can provide feedback on your design preferences.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Enter the order date.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Enter the order time.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to complete the form and send it to the design team.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all fields accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the design team has all the necessary information to deliver a satisfactory design.
* If you have any specific design preferences or references, please provide them in the "Design File" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the design team for further assistance.
