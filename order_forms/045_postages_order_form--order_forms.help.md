# Postages Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Postages Order Form is used to collect customer information and order details for postage services. This form helps us understand your needs and provide the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all the required fields (marked as required) accurately and completely.
2. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.
3. Use the provided options for select fields (such as First Language).
4. Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **First Language** (`first_language`, select_one, required): Select your first language from the provided options (Chinese or English).
* **Order Detail** (`order_detail`, text, optional): Describe the order you would like us to process.
* **Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`customer_address`, text, optional): Enter your physical address.
* **Postal Code** (`customer_postal_code`, text, optional): Enter your postal code or zip code.
* **City** (`customer_city`, text, optional): Enter your city name.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this guide for clarification.
- Contact our support team if you need further assistance.
