# custom_apparel_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The custom apparel order form is used for customers to place orders for custom-made apparel items.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of apparel you would like to order (hoodie, t-shirt, hat, etc.).
2. Choose the color(s) of the apparel item.
3. Enter your size and quantity of the desired apparel item.
4. Select your preferred date and time for the order to be fulfilled.
5. Choose your time zone for the selected date and time.
6. Enter your name, email, phone number, and any additional message you would like to include with your order.
7. Finally, select the "submit" button to complete your order, or choose "save for later" to save your order for later completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **apparel_type** (select_one): Select the type of apparel you would like to order (hoodie, t-shirt, hat, etc.).
* **color** (select_multiple): Choose the color(s) of the apparel item.
* **size** (number): Enter the size of the desired apparel item.
* **quantity** (number): Enter the quantity of the desired apparel item.
* **date_time** (select_one): Select the date and time for the order to be fulfilled.
* **time_zone** (select_multiple): Choose your time zone for the selected date and time.
* **name** (text): Enter your name for the order.
* **email** (email): Enter your email address for the order.
* **phone** (text): Enter your phone number for the order.
* **message** (note): Enter any additional message you would like to include with your order.
* **notes** (note): Enter any additional notes for your order.
* **submit** (select_one): Select the "submit" button to complete your order, or choose "save for later" to save your order for later completion.
