# Home Health Physician Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Health Physician Order Form is designed to provide a structured way for healthcare professionals to place orders for home health services. This form gathers essential information about the patient, their medical history, and the ordered services.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please fill in all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2.  Select the relevant options for each field.
3.  Ensure all pages are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Order Details** (`page_1`, `text`, optional): This field is intended for any additional details related to the order.
*   **Medical History** (`page_2`, `number`, optional): This field is for recording the patient's medical history, which may include any previous illnesses, allergies, or medical conditions.
*   **Physician Orders** (`page_3`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the type of services you want to order (e.g., '1', '2', '3', '4', '5', '6').
*   **Patient Info** (`page_5`, `email`, required): Enter the patient's email address to ensure they can receive updates and notifications about their care.
*   **Physician Order Confirmation** (`page_23`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that the physician has reviewed and ordered the services.
*   **Additional Information** (`page_24`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information related to the order.
*   **Order Confirmation** (`page_23`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that the order has been placed correctly.
*   **Service Selection 1** (`page_11`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the first set of services to be ordered.
*   **Service Selection 2** (`page_14`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose additional services to be ordered.
*   **Service Type** (`page_22`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of service for the order.
*   **Date** (`page_9`, `date`, optional): Record the date of the order.
*   **Time** (`page_10`, `time`, optional): Record the time of the order.
*   **Additional Notes** (`page_12`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes or information related to the order.
*   **Additional Fields** (`page_12`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information related to the order.
*   **Patient Contact** (`page_13`, `text`, optional): Enter the patient's contact information.
*   **Patient Contact Number** (`page_21`, `number`, optional): Enter the patient's contact number.
*   **Other Medical History** (`page_8`, `text`, optional): Enter any other medical history information.
*   **Order Type** (`page_23`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of order.
*   **Service Type Confirmation** (`page_23`, `select_one`, required): Confirm the service type.
*   **Additional Information 1** (`page_25`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information related to the order.
*   **Additional Information 2** (`page_25`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information related to the order.
*   **Service Type 1** (`page_24`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the service type for the order.
*   **Other Service Type** (`page_24`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the other service type for the order.
*   **Service Type 2** (`page_25`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the service type for the order.
