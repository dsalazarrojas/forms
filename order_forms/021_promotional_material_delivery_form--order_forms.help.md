# promotional_material_delivery_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The promotional material delivery form is designed for collecting information about promotional materials and their recipients. It is likely used in industries such as marketing, sales, or advertising.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the delivery information in the "Delivery Info" field.
2. Enter details about the promotional material in the "Material Info" field.
3. Enter the recipient's email address in the "Recipient Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Info (delivery_info)** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the promotional material, such as a brochure or flyer.
* **Material Info (material_info)** (Text, Optional): Describe the promotional material, including any relevant details or features.
* **Recipient Email (recipient_email)** (Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the person or organization that will receive the promotional material.
