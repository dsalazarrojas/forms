# Custom Label Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for customers to input their custom label order details. It guides you through the process of creating a customized label with specific customer preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the customer's information, including their name, email, phone number, and shipping address.
2. Enter the order details, including the order date, order time, and customer note.
3. Select the payment method and status.
4. Enter the shipping details, including the shipping method and status.
5. You can also add custom fields and labels as needed.
6. Finally, upload a custom image for the label.
7. Review your order summary before submitting the form.
