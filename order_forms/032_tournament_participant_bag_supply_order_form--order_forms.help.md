# Tournament-Participant-Bag-Supply-Order-Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information for the participant bag ordering process in a tournament. It requires accurate input to facilitate proper ordering and minimize errors.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure that you have the required information for the participant bags and the tournament.
2. Select the correct participant bags and their corresponding participant info.
3. Choose a valid payment method (Cash, Bank Transfer, or PayPal).
4. Enter the order date and participant payment details (if applicable).
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tournament Participant Bags** (`1`, `number`, `no`): Enter the number of participant bags needed.
* **Participant Bags** (`2`, `number`, `no`): Enter the number of participant bags needed.
* **Order Date** (`3`, `date`, `no`): Enter the date you want the participant bags to be ordered by.
* **Participant Info** (`4`, `select_multiple`, `no`): Select the participant(s) the bags are for. Choose from participant1, participant2, participant3.
* **Payment Method** (`5`, `select_one`, `yes`): Choose how the participant will pay for their bags. Select from Cash, Bank Transfer, or PayPal.
* **Participant Payment** (`6`, `number`, `no`): Enter the amount the participant is paying for their bags.
