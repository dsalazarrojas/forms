# Legal Supply Order Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their legal supply order requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the type of order you want to make from the options provided.
2. Provide details about the order, including any notes.
3. Select the status of the order by choosing multiple options.
4. Enter the date of the request.
5. Enter the time of the request.
6. List the supply items being ordered.
7. Provide delivery details.
8. Enter your contact name.
9. Enter your contact email.
10. Enter your contact phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Legal Supply Order Request Form** (`legal_supply_order_request_form`): This is the main section of the form where you should enter the details of your legal supply order request.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`): Please provide any relevant notes about your order.
* **Order Type** (`order_type`): Select the type of order you want to make from the options provided. This will direct the supply team to the correct processing team.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`): Select the status of your order by choosing multiple options. This will help the supply team prioritize and process your order.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`): Enter the date of your request. This will help the supply team process your order on time.
* **Request Time** (`request_time`): Enter the time of your request. This will help the supply team contact you at the correct time.
* **Supply Items** (`supply_items`): List the items being ordered. Be specific and detailed in your list.
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`): Enter any delivery details, such as delivery address or schedule.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter your name as the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

Please note that this form requires no specific formatting or structure, so feel free to enter your information as you see fit. However, please be clear and concise in your entries to ensure that your request is accurately processed.
