# pharmacy_prescription_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pharmacy_prescription_communication_form is a tool used to share prescription information between patients and pharmacies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form fields carefully and ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Enter relevant patient information in the "Patient Information" section.
* List the patient's prescribed medications in the "Medication List" section.
* Provide details of the patient's prescription in the "Prescription Information" section.
* Indicate if the patient has any medication allergies in the "Patient Medication Allergies" section.
* Enter the pharmacy's name and details in the "Pharmacy Information" section.
* Add any important notes for the patient in the "Patient Notes" section.
* Add any notes for the pharmacy staff in the "Pharmacy Notes" section.
* Complete the "Order Information" section with the patient's order details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (patient_info): This is the section where patients or healthcare professionals enter patient details such as name, address, and contact information.
* **Medication List** (medication_list): List the patient's prescribed medications, including dosage and frequency.
* **Prescription Information** (prescription_info): Provide details of the patient's prescription, including date and time.
* **Patient Medication Allergies** (patient_medication_allergies): Check if the patient has any known allergies to medications.
*   *Yes/No* options allow users to quickly select if the patient has any medication allergies.
* **Pharmacy Information** (pharmacy_info): Enter the pharmacy's name, address, and contact information.
* **Patient Notes** (patient_notes): Add any important information about the patient that the pharmacy needs to be aware of.
* **Pharmacy Notes** (pharmacy_notes): Add any notes or comments for the pharmacy staff.
* **Order Information** (order_info): Enter the patient's order details, such as order date and quantity.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately to avoid errors.
* Double-check patient medication allergies for accurate and safe dispensing.
* Communicate clearly and concisely with the pharmacy using this form.
