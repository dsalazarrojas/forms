# Exhibition Booth Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exhibition Booth Check Out Form is designed to collect relevant information from customers at the point of checkout. This form ensures that all necessary details are captured accurately and efficiently, reducing the risk of errors or misunderstandings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary details ready before completing the form.
2. Follow the instructions provided on each page to guide you through the process.
3. Fill out each field according to the explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Booth Number** (`booth_number`, number, required): Enter the number of your exhibition booth.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name, or the name of the person representing your company.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your company's email address or the email address associated with your company's registration.
* **Sales Representative** (`sales_rep`, select_one, required): Select the sales representative you interacted with during the exhibition. Please choose one of the options provided.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter the contact number of your company or the number associated with your company's registration.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, select_multiple, optional): If applicable, select the type of product you are interested in or have inquired about.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the product you are interested in or have inquired about.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the payment method you would like to use for the transaction.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, number, required): Enter the payment terms or amount due for the transaction. Please ensure this is accurate to the best of your knowledge.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure you have the necessary details before proceeding with the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask your sales representative for assistance.
