# Cheap Sports Jersey Payment Form With Sofort - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed for customers to order a cheap sports jersey with the option of customizing their purchase. The form collects contact and shipping information, customizes the jersey with a name and number, and processes payment through Sofort.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your customer contact information (full name, email address, and phone number) in the "Customer Contact Details" section.
2. Select the team and size of your preferred jersey in the "Jersey Order Details" section.
3. Enter the quantity of jerseys you wish to order.
4. If desired, add a custom name and number to the jersey.
5. Review the calculated total amount due based on the quantity selected.
6. Read and agree to the refund policy.
7. You will be redirected to Sofort to complete payment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Full Name (`customer_full_name`, text, required)

• Full Name: Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on the custom jersey.

### Email Address (`customer_email`, email, required)

• Email Address: Enter your email address where you would like to receive updates on your order.

### Phone Number (`customer_phone`, text, required)

• Phone Number: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### Street Address (`shipping_street`, text, required)

• Street Address: Enter your street address where the order will be shipped to.

### City (`shipping_city`, text, required)

• City: Enter the city where the order will be shipped to.

### Postal Code (`shipping_zip`, text, required)

• Postal Code: Enter the postal code (ZIP code) where the order will be shipped to.

### Country (`shipping_country`, text, required)

• Country: Select the country where the order will be shipped to.

### Select Team (`jersey_team`, select_one, required)

• Select Team: Choose the sports team you prefer to order a jersey for.

### Jersey Size (`jersey_size`, select_one, required)

• Jersey Size: Select the size of the jersey you prefer.

### Quantity (`order_quantity`, number, required)

• Quantity: Enter the number of jerseys you wish to order.

### Custom Name on Back (`customization_name`, text, optional)

• Custom Name on Back: Leave blank if you don't want a custom name, otherwise enter the name to be printed on the jersey.

### Custom Number (`customization_number`, number, optional)

• Custom Number: Enter a number between 0 and 99 to be printed on the jersey. Leave blank if you don't want a custom number.

### Total Amount Due (`total_amount_due`, number, required)

• Total Amount Due: This field will be calculated based on the quantity selected.

### Note - You will be redirected to Sofort to complete payment (`payment_method_note`, note, required)

• Note: This field is a note to let you know that you will be redirected to Sofort to complete payment.

### I agree to the Refund Policy (`terms_acceptance`, select_one, required)

• I agree to the Refund Policy: Select "I Agree" to confirm you have read and agreed to the refund policy.
