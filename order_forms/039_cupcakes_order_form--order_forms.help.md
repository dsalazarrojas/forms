# Cupcakes Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cupcakes Order Form is a tool for customers to provide their order details. Fill out this form to place an order for a cupcake order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer information (Customer Information).
2. Select the delivery date (Delivery Date).
3. Enter your delivery address (Delivery Address).
4. Specify the intended amount of cupcakes you want to order (Intended Amount).
5. Choose your preferred flavors (Flavors).
6. Add any additional comments (Comments).
7. Enter your contact number (Contact Number).
8. Enter your email (Email).
9. Select the delivery time (Delivery Time).
10. If necessary, specify any assigned tools (Assigned Tool).
