# Cupcakes Order - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather order information from customers. It allows customers to provide their name, email, phone, order type, and any additional notes or messages related to their order. The form is intended to be completed by customers who want to place an order with our cupcake shop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **Customer Name** in the text field provided.
2. Select a valid **Order Date** using the calendar tool.
3. Choose a time for your **Order Time** using the time selector.
4. Select either **Yes** or **No** for **Order Type**.
5. Enter your **Customer Email** in the format of a valid email address.
6. Enter your **Customer Phone** in the format of a phone number.
7. Provide any additional **Order Message** in the text field.
8. Add any **Order Notes** in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: A text field to enter your name.
* **Order Date**: A date field to select a valid date for your order.
* **Order Time**: A time field to select a time for your order.
* **Order Type**: A select one field with options "Yes" and "No".
* **Customer Email**: A field to enter a valid email address.
* **Customer Phone**: A field to enter a phone number.
* **Order Message**: A text field to provide any additional message related to your order.
* **Order Notes**: A text field to add any additional notes related to your order.
