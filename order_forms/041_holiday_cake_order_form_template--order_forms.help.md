# Holiday Cake Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the fields and instructions for the Holiday Cake Order Form Template. The form is designed to collect order details from customers for their holiday cakes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete the form, simply fill out each field with the required information.
2. Make sure to review each field carefully to ensure you are providing accurate information.
3. Once you have filled out all the required fields, submit the form to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name for the order.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name for the order.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with order updates.
* **Address Line 1** (`address_line_1`, `text`, optional): Enter your address line 1 for delivery purposes.
* **Address Line 2** (`address_line_2`, `text`, optional): Enter your address line 2 for delivery purposes.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, optional): Enter the city for delivery purposes.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, `number`, optional): Enter your zip code for delivery purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with order updates.
* **Cake Flavor** (`cake_flavor`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the type of cake flavor you would like for your order. Options include:
	+ Chocolate
	+ Vanilla
	+ Strawberry
	+ Red Velvet
	+ Carrot
	+ Other
* **Delivery Date** (`date_delivery`, `date`, optional): Select the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Delivery Time** (`time_delivery`, `time`, optional): Select the time you would like to receive your order.
* **Order Note** (`order_note`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes about your order.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, optional): Enter any special requests you may have for your order.
* **Birthday** (`birthday`, `date`, optional): Enter the birthday of the person you are ordering for, if applicable.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `text`, optional): Enter any special instructions for the order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, optional): Select the payment method for your order. Options include:
	+ Cash
	+ Credit Card
	+ PayPal
	+ Bank Transfer
* **Gift** (`gift`, `select_one`, optional): Check the box if this order is a gift.
