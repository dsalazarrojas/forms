# Equipment Borrowing Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equipment Borrowing Communication Form is used to track and manage the borrowing process of equipment within your organization. It allows users to request or offer equipment, and to communicate about the status and details of equipment loans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate fields and provide required information as prompted.
2. Choose the correct option for each field to ensure accurate tracking and communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equipment Status** (`equipment_status`): Select the current status of the equipment, which can be `Available`, `Not Available`, `On Loan`, or `In Maintenance`.
* **Communication Plan** (`communication_plan`): Describe how you plan to communicate with the requester, including any relevant details or deadlines.
* **Loan Period** (`loan_period`): Enter the duration of the loan in days, weeks, or months.
* **Due Date** (`due_date`): Enter the date when the equipment is due to be returned.
* **Loaned By** (`loaned_by`): Select the user who loaned the equipment.
* **Returned By** (`returned_by`): Select the user who returned the equipment.
* **Status Comments** (`status_comments`): Add any additional comments about the equipment status.
* **Loan Extension** (`loan_extension`): Select whether the loan period can be extended.
* **Form Completion** (`form_completion`): Confirm that the form is complete and accurate.
* **Equipment Return** (`equipment_return`): Select whether the equipment has been returned.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional comments about the equipment.
* **Requester Email** (`requester_email`): Enter the email of the person requesting the equipment.
* **Requested By** (`requested_by`): Select the user who requested the equipment.
* **Requested Date** (`requested_date`): Enter the date when the equipment was requested.
* **Loaned** (`loaned`): Enter the number of times the equipment has been loaned.
* **Returned** (`returned`): Enter the number of times the equipment has been returned.
* **Loan Duration** (`loan_duration`): Enter the duration of each loan in days, weeks, or months.
* **Loan Start Date** (`loan_start_date`): Enter the date when the loan started.
* **Loan End Date** (`loan_end_date`): Enter the date when the loan ended.
* **Last Updated** (`last_updated`): Enter the date when the form was last updated.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`): Select the user who is assigned to manage or update the form.
* **Assigned By** (`assigned_by`): Select the user who assigned the form to be managed or updated.
* **Form Submission Status** (`form_submission_status`): Select the status of the form, which can be `Approved`, `Rejected`, or `Pending`.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any comments about the form submission.
