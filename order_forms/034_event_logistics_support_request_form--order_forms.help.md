# event_logistics_support_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The event logistics support request form is designed for event planners to submit support requests related to event finances, equipment, and other logistical details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields carefully and ensure you have a legitimate support request.
2. Fill out the form with brief descriptions or notes related to your support request.
3. Submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Planner Details**: This field is for you to provide your name and title or any other relevant details about yourself as the event planner.
* **Vendor Details**: Enter any information related to the vendor or supplier you are inquiring about, such as their name, contact person, or contact information.
* **Event Schedule**: Describe your event schedule, including dates, times, and locations. Be as specific as possible to ensure we understand your needs.
* **Team Member Details**: Provide any relevant information about your team members, including their names, roles, and contact information.
* **Vendor Invoices**: Attach any invoices or receipts related to your event expenses or services. This will help us process your request efficiently.
* **Event Finances**: Describe your event finances, including any relevant budgets, costs, or financial constraints.
* **Event Equipment**: Specify any special equipment or services you need for the event, including their location, quantity, or any other relevant details.
* **Event Logistics**: Describe any logistical challenges or concerns you have regarding the event, such as transportation, accommodation, or security arrangements.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when filling out the form to ensure we understand your support request accurately.
* Do not submit duplicate requests, as they will not be processed.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
