# Tabletop Gaming Event Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from participants for a tabletop gaming event. It covers event details, participant information, order requests, and payment details. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure smooth event management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Scroll through the form and select the "Page 1: Event Details" tab.
2. Fill in the required and optional fields with your accurate information.
3. Proceed to "Page 2: Participant Details" and repeat the process.
4. If you have any orders or requests, navigate to "Page 3: Order" and fill in the necessary information.
5. Finally, provide payment details in "Page 4: Payment".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Details
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, `text`, required: false): This section is for providing information about the event you're attending. Describe the event in a few sentences, including any necessary details such as date, time, and location.

### Participant Details
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `text`, required: false): This section is for providing personal information about yourself, the participant. Include your name, email address, and any other relevant details.

### Order
* **Order** (`order`, `text`, required: false): If you have any orders or requests, list them in this section. Be specific about what you want to purchase or reserve.

### Payment
* **Payment** (`payment`, `text`, required: false): This section is for providing payment details. Include your payment method (e.g., cash, credit card, or check) and any other relevant information.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
- If you're unsure about any field, ask the event organizers for clarification.
- Review your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
- If you have any issues with the form, contact us at form@gic.mx.
