# Award Trophy Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Award Trophy Order Form is a tool designed to collect information for custom trophies that will be awarded to customers. This form will guide you through the process of ordering a custom trophy, including selecting the trophy type, quantity, size, and other customization options. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure that your order is processed accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields in the "Customer and Event Information" section:
	* Enter your full name as it appears on your business card or ID.
	* Enter your email address for contact purposes.
	* Enter your phone number as the best contact number.
	* Enter your organization or business name.
2. Select the event name for tracking purposes.
3. Proceed to the "Trophy Specifications" section:
	* Choose the primary trophy style from the options provided (Classic Cup, Glass Plaque, Acrylic Stand, Medal, or Custom Sculpture).
	* Select the quantity of trophies to order.
	* Choose the standard size for the trophy from the options provided (Small, Medium, Large, or Extra Large).
	* Select the base material for the trophy from the options provided (Solid Wood, Black Marble, Weighted Plastic, or Polished Metal).
4. Add custom text to be engraved on the trophy using the text field.
5. Select the font style for the engraving text from the options provided (Arial, Times New Roman, Script, or Modern Bold).
6. Proceed to the "Delivery and Payment" section:
	* Select the required delivery date, ensuring at least 14 days for processing.
	* Choose the delivery method from the options provided (Standard Shipping, Expedited Shipping, or Local Pickup).
	* If shipping address is required, enter the full address.
	* Select the preferred payment method from the options provided (Credit/Debit Card, Corporate Invoice, or PayPal Account).
7. Add any special instructions or comments in the text field if needed.
