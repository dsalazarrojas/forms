# e_commerce_single_purchase_payment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a single purchase order payment. It captures the customer's details and order summary for processing the payment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Customer Details section with accurate information about the customer.
2. Enter the Shipping Details section with relevant information about the delivery.
3. Provide the Payment Details in the respective section.
4. Review the Order Summary to ensure all information is correct.
5. Confirm that the order is accurate in the Confirm Order section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Details (customer_details)**: This is the section where you enter the customer's name and contact information.
- **Shipping Details (shipping_details)**: Enter the shipping address and other relevant details to ensure timely and correct delivery.
- **Payment Details (payment_details)**: Provide the payment method and amount to complete the order.
- **Order Summary (order_summary)**: Review all the information entered to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- **Confirm Order (confirm_order)**: Verify that all the entered details are correct, then confirm the order to proceed with payment processing.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields for accuracy before confirming the order.
- Ensure all fields are filled in correctly to avoid any errors.
- If unsure about any field, refer to our contact information below for further assistance.
