# state_football_championship_t_shirt_order_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to place an order for a state football championship t-shirt. It requires information about the customer such as their first name, team name, championship year, t-shirt size, t-shirt color, order quantity, email, and phone number.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your team name in the "Team Name" field.
3. Select the year of the state championship in the "State Championship Year" field.
4. Choose your preferred t-shirt size from the "T-Shirt Size" dropdown menu.
5. Choose your preferred t-shirt color from the "T-Shirt Color" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the quantity of t-shirts you would like to order in the "Order Quantity" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Team Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your team name.
* **State Championship Year** (Number, Optional): Select the year of the state championship.
* **T-Shirt Size** (Select One, Required): Choose your preferred t-shirt size (Small, Medium, Large).
* **T-Shirt Color** (Select One, Required): Choose your preferred t-shirt color (Black, White, Gray).
* **Order Quantity** (Text, Optional): Enter the number of t-shirts you would like to order.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
