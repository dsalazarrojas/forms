# Business Letterhead Design Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a business's letterhead design preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Please fill out each field with the requested information.
*   Be detailed in your responses, as this will help ensure that we can accurately create a design that meets your needs.
*   If you have any additional requirements or requests, please include them in the "Additional items" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company name**: This is the name of the company that the letterhead will be designed for. Please enter the full name of the company.
* **Contact name**: This is the person responsible for approving the final design. Please enter their name and title (e.g., Marketing Manager).
* **Contact email**: This is the email address where you would like to receive proofs and feedback on the design. Please ensure that this is a valid email address.
* **Logo provided**: If you have a logo for the letterhead, please select "True". If not, select "False".
* **Color preferences**: If you have specific color preferences for the letterhead, enter them here. This can include the primary colors or a brand palette.
* **Typography preferences**: If you have specific font style or guidance for the letterhead, enter it here.
* **Quantity of variants**: If you require multiple versions of the letterhead (e.g., different sizes or orientations), enter the number of variants you need.
* **Additional items**: If you require additional design elements, such as business card or envelope design, select the options that apply.
* **Deadline date**: If you have a specific deadline for the final design files, enter the date here.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification before submitting the form.
*   We will do our best to meet your needs and deliver a design that meets your expectations.
