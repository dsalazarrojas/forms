# Online Cupcake Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to place orders for cupcakes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Customer Contact** section with your name and contact information.
2. Choose between **Pick up** or **Delivery** options.
3. Select the **Flavor** of your cupcake (Vanilla, Chocolate, Strawberry, Blueberry).
4. Enter the **Quantity** of cupcakes you would like to order.
5. Select the **Payment Method** (Cash, Card, Online).
6. Select the **Payment Type** (Single, Multiple).
7. If you choose **Delivery**, fill out the **Delivery Address**, **Delivery Details**, and **Delivery Instructions**.
8. If you choose **Pick up**, fill out the **Pick up Location**, **Pick up Time**, and **Pick up Instructions**.
9. Add any **Special Instructions** or **Order Notes**.
10. Sign with your **Customer Signature**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Contact**: Enter your name and contact information so we can reach out to you about your order.
* **Pick up or Delivery**: Choose how you would like to receive your order (Pick up or Delivery).
* **Flavor**: Select one or more flavors of cupcakes you would like to order (Vanilla, Chocolate, Strawberry, Blueberry).
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of cupcakes you would like to order.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you would like to pay for your order (Cash, Card, Online).
* **Payment Type**: Choose if you are paying for single or multiple orders.
* **Date**: Enter the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Time**: Enter the time you would like to receive your order.
* **Address**: Enter your delivery address if you chose **Delivery**.
* **Pick up Time**: Enter the time you would like to pick up your order if you chose **Pick up**.
* **Pick up Location**: Enter the location where you would like to pick up your order.
* **Delivery Details**: Enter any additional details about your delivery order.
* **Delivery Instructions**: Enter any specific instructions for your delivery order.
* **Delivery Status**: Check the status of your delivery order (In Transit, Delivered, Missed Pickup).
* **Delivery Time Window**: Enter the time window for your delivery order.
* **Delivery Contact**: Enter the contact information for your delivery contact.
* **Special Instructions**: Enter any special instructions for your order.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Customer Signature**: Sign with your name to confirm your order.
