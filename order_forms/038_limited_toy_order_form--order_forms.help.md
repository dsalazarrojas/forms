# limited_toy_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information for a toy order from customers, including customer contact information, selected toy preferences, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Customer Information" section and enter your name and contact information.
2. Click on the "Toy Selection" section, select the toys you would like to order by ticking the corresponding boxes. You can choose multiple toys.
3. In the "Order Details" section, enter any additional order information if required.
4. In the "Payment Information" section, enter the payment method and details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information (id: 1, name: customer_info, type: text, required: false)** 
	+ This section is for providing your name and contact information, including email and phone number.
* **Toy Selection (id: 2, name: toy_selection, type: select_multiple, required: false)**
	+ Please select the toy(s) you would like to purchase by ticking the box(es) next to the name(s) of the toy(s).
* **Order Details (id: 3, name: order_details, type: text, required: false)**
	+ If there is any special message or instruction you would like to include with your order, please enter it in this section.
* **Payment Information (id: 4, name: payment_info, type: text, required: false)**
	+ Please enter the payment method and details (e.g. credit card number, expiration date, CVV). 

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and complete information for each field to ensure efficient processing of your order.
