# School Uniform Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and process school uniform orders. It contains multiple pages and fields that require completion by authorized users to gather accurate information for administrative tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On each page, fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Review each field for any additional instructions or hints provided.
3. Move to the next page by clicking the "Next" or navigation buttons.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Order Details (Page 1)
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required):
  Enter a brief description of the order, including any relevant details or notes.

### Customer Information (Page 2)
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required):
  Enter the customer's name, contact information, and any relevant details.

### Payment Details (Page 3)
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, select_multiple, optional):
  Select one or more payment options:
    + Option 1
    + Option 2
    + Option 3

### Notes (Page 4)
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional):
  Add any additional notes or comments about the order.

### School Products (Page 5)
* **School Products** (`school_products`, select_one, optional):
  Select the school product(s) ordered:
    + Product 1
    + Product 2
    + Product 3

### Total Cost (Page 6)
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, number, optional):
  Enter the total cost of the order (if applicable).

### Confirm Order (Page 7)
* **Confirm Order** (`confirm_order`, select_one, optional):
  Confirm the order:
    + Confirm
    + Decline

### Submit (Page 8)
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, select_one, optional):
  Complete and submit the form:
    + Submit
    + Cancel

## Tips
* Double-check each field for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
* Ensure all required fields are filled in.
* If unsure about any field or option, refer to the provided instructions or contact the relevant authorities.
* Use the submit button to complete and submit the form.
