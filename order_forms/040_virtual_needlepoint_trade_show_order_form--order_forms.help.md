# Virtual Needlepoint Trade Show Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Needlepoint Trade Show Order Form is a tool for event organizers to collect order information from customers during trade shows. This form helps gather essential details such as event name, customer contact information, order date and time, quantity, price, product selection, and additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Event Name** where you will be attending the trade show.
2. Enter your **Customer Name** as the representative for this order.
3. Choose the **Order Date** and **Order Time** for the trade show.
4. Enter the **Quantity** of each product you would like to order.
5. Select the **Price** for each product.
6. Choose the products you would like to **order (product_name)** from the list of options.
7. Enter your **Customer Email** and **Customer Phone** for communication purposes.
8. Add any additional **Customer Note** regarding your order.
9. Select the **Assigned Tool** to be used for communication (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you will be attending.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as the representative for this order.
* **Order Date**: Choose the date you would like to place your order for the trade show.
* **Order Time**: Choose the time you would like to place your order for the trade show.
* **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of each product you would like to order.
* **Price**: Enter the price for each product.
* **Product Name**: Choose the products you would like to order from the options provided.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Customer Note**: Add any additional note regarding your order.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool to be used for communication (e.g. chatjimmy, chatty, chatbot).
