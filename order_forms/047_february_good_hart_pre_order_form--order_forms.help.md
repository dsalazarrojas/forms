# February Good Hart Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer information for pre-orders of the "February Good Hart" product. Please fill in the required fields to provide us with the necessary details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Provide a brief description of your order in the "Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last name** (`customer_last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you about your pre-order.
* **Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number if you would like us to contact you directly.
* **Description** (`product_description`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your order to help us understand your preferences.
