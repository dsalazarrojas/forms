# Moving Service Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering moving services, including details about the type of move, dates, and times, as well as contact information for the company and the person responsible for the move.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your move details, such as the type of move and any additional information in the "What Is Your Move" section.
2. Select the type of move by choosing between "Local" or "Interstate" from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter your pickup address and date for the move.
4. Enter the pickup time for the move.
5. Enter the move date and time.
6. Provide additional details about the move in the "Move Details" sections.
7. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
8. Confirm the contact person's information for the move.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What Is Your Move** (Text): Enter a brief description of your move.
* **Move Type** (Select One): Choose between "Local" or "Interstate" to indicate the type of move.
* **Pickup Address** (Text): Enter the address where you need to pick up your belongings.
* **Pickup Date** (Date): Enter the date when you would like to pick up your belongings.
* **Pickup Time** (Time): Enter the time when you would like to pick up your belongings.
* **Move Date** (Date): Enter the date when you would like to move your belongings.
* **Move Time** (Time): Enter the time when you would like to move your belongings.
* **Additional Move Details** (Text): Enter any additional information about your move.

* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address for contact.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Company** (Text): Enter the name of the company moving your belongings.
* **Contact Person** (Text): Enter the name of the person responsible for the move.
* **Contact Email** (Email): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the move.
* **Contact Phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the move.
