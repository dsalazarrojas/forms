# Grill Box Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place orders for catering, delivery, or pickup from a grill box service. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your order is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the delivery option that suits your needs: <strong>delivery</strong>, <strong>pickup</strong>, or <strong>catering</strong>.
2. Enter your <strong>customer name</strong> as it appears on your ID or credit card.
3. Enter your <strong>customer email</strong> to receive updates on your order.
4. Enter your <strong>customer phone number</strong> in case we need to contact you.
5. Specify the <strong>order type</strong> you would like to place: <strong>Burgers</strong>, <strong>Sandwiches</strong>, <strong>Salads</strong>, <strong>Desserts</strong>, or <strong>Specialties</strong>.
6. Enter the <strong>quantity</strong> of each item you would like to order.
7. If you have any <strong>special instructions</strong>, please provide them in this field.
8. Choose the <strong>delivery date</strong> for your order.
9. Select the <strong>delivery time</strong> for your order.
10. Enter the <strong>pickup time</strong> for your order if you are picking up at our location.
11. Provide the <strong>delivery address</strong> if you are having your order delivered.
12. Provide the <strong>pickup address</strong> if you are picking up at our location.
13. If you are placing a catering order, enter the <strong>catering location</strong>.
14. Enter the <strong>catering number</strong> for your order if applicable.
15. If you have any <strong>special requests</strong>, please provide them in this field.
16. If you have any <strong>catering special instructions</strong>, please provide them in this field.
17. If you have any <strong>delivery notes</strong>, please provide them in this field.
18. If you have any <strong>pickup notes</strong>, please provide them in this field.
19. If you have any <strong>catering notes</strong>, please provide them in this field.
20. If you have any <strong>billing address</strong>, please provide the city and state in this field.
21. If you have any <strong>billing zip</strong>, please provide the zip code in this field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Option** (`delivery_option`, `select_one`): Select how you would like to receive your order: delivery, pickup, or catering.
* **Customer Name** (`name`, `text`): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or credit card.
* **Customer Email** (`email`, `email`): Enter your email to receive updates on your order.
* **Customer Phone** (`phone`, `text`): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, `select_one`): Select the type of order you would like to place: Burgers, Sandwiches, Salads, Desserts, or Specialties.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`): Enter the quantity of each item you would like to order.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, `text`): If you have any special requests or details for your order, please provide them in this field.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `note`): If you have any special instructions, please provide them in this field.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`): Choose the date for your order delivery.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, `time`): Select the time for your order delivery.
* **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, `time`): If you are picking up at our location, select the time for your order pickup.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, `text`): If you are having your order delivered, enter the delivery address.
* **Pickup Address** (`pickup_address`, `text`): If you are picking up at our location, enter the pickup address.
* **Catering Location** (`catering_location`, `text`): If you are placing a catering order, enter the location.
* **Catering Number** (`catering_number`, `number`): If you are placing a catering order, enter the catering number.
* **Catering Special Instructions** (`catering_special_instructions`, `note`): If you are placing a catering order, enter any special instructions.
* **Delivery Note** (`delivery_note`, `note`): If you have any delivery notes, enter them in this field.
* **Pickup Note** (`pickup_note`, `note`): If you have any pickup notes, enter them in this field.
* **Catering Note** (`catering_note`, `note`): If you have any catering notes, enter them in this field.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, `text`): Enter the address for billing purposes.
* **Billing City** (`billing_city`, `text`): Enter the city for billing purposes.
* **Billing State** (`billing_state`, `text`): Enter the state for billing purposes.
* **Billing Zip** (`billing_zip`, `text`): Enter the zip code for billing purposes.
