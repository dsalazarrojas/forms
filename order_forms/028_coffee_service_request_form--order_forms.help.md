# Coffee Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coffee Service Request Form is used to request coffee services for events. This form is to be completed by event organizers or planners to provide coffee services that cater to the needs of their attendees. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the requestor's information (Section: Requester Information).
2. Enter the event details, including date, time, location, and expected guest count (Section: Event Details).
3. Select the type of coffee service desired (Section: Service Requirements).
4. Specify the style of coffee and beverages to be served (Section: Coffee and Beverage Preferences).
5. Indicate any special requirements or requests (Section: Special Requirements).
6. Confirm the budget and payment terms (Section: Budget and Payment).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (requester_name, text, required): Enter your full name as the requester.
* **Organization or Business** (organization, text, required): Provide the name of your company or the event.
* **Email Address** (email, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (phone, text, required): Enter your phone number for any further communication.
* **Event Date** (event_date, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (event_time, time, required): Enter the start time of the event.
* **Event Duration - hours** (event_duration, number, required): Enter the duration of the event in hours.
* **Event Location** (event_location, text, required): Enter the full address or venue name of the event.
* **Type of Event** (event_type, select_one, required): Select the type of event (e.g., Corporate Meeting, Conference, etc.).
* **Expected Number of Guests** (expected_guests, number, required): Estimate the approximate number of attendees.
* **Coffee Service Type** (coffee_service_type, select_one, required): Choose the type of coffee service desired (e.g., Self-service Station, Full-service Barista, etc.).
* **Service Style** (service_style, select_one, required): Select the style of service (e.g., Breakfast service, Mid-morning break, etc.).
* **Setup and Cleanup Included** (coffee_service_setup, select_one, required): Choose whether setup and cleanup are included or not.
* **Coffee Style Preference** (coffee_style, select_multiple, required): Select the types of coffee and beverages to be served.
* **Milk Options to Provide** (milk_options, select_multiple, required): Choose the types of milk to be provided (e.g., Regular milk, Skim milk, etc.).
* **Additional Items** (sections_preferences, note, required): Select if you wish to include pastries or snacks.
* **Pastry or Snack Preferences** (pastry_type, select_multiple, optional): If you selected to include additional items, choose the types of pastries or snacks.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (dietary_restrictions, select_multiple, optional): Specify any dietary restrictions to accommodate.
* **Special Requests or Preferences** (special_requests, text, optional): Enter any special requests or preferences.
* **Budget and Payment** (budget, note, optional): Choose the budget per person you are willing to spend.
* **Budget per Person** (budget_per_person, select_one, optional): Select the approximate cost you are willing to spend per person.
