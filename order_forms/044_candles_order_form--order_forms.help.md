# Candles Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to place orders for candles. It collects customer information, order details, and payment information to ensure accurate and timely delivery.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the ordering process.
2. Select the options for each field, and ensure that all required fields are filled before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Full Name** (`order_customer_name`, text, **Required**): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`order_customer_email`, email, **Required**): Enter your email address for order confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`order_customer_phone`, text, **Required**): Enter your phone number for delivery and contact purposes.
* **Delivery Address** (`order_delivery_address`, text, **Required**): Enter your full street address for delivery.
* **Select Candle Scent** (`candle_scent_choice`, select_one, **Required**): Choose the desired candle scent from the options provided.
* **Select Candle Size** (`candle_size_choice`, select_one, **Required**): Choose the size of the candle from the options provided.
* **Quantity** (`order_quantity_candle`, number, **Required**): Enter the number of candles in this scent and size.
* **Preferred Wick Type** (`candle_wick_type`, select_one, **Required**): Choose the type of wick you prefer for your candle.
* **Is this a gift?** (`is_gift_check`, select_one, **Required**): Choose whether you would like to include a gift message with your order.
* **Gift Wrap Needed** (`gift_wrap_interest`, select_one, **Required**): Choose whether you would like the order wrapped.
* **Gift Message** (`gift_message_text`, text, **Optional**): Enter a personalized message to include with your gift.
* **Preferred Delivery Date** (`preferred_delivery_date_val`, date, **Required**): Choose the desired delivery date for your order.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method_choice`, select_one, **Required**): Choose the speed of shipment for your order.
* **Payment Method** (`order_payment_method_choice`, select_one, **Required**): Choose how you would like to pay for your order.
* **Special Instructions for Delivery** (`delivery_instructions_field`, text, **Optional**): Enter any special instructions for delivery.
* **I agree to the terms of sale** (`sales_terms_agreement`, select_one, **Required**): Confirm your agreement with the terms of sale.
* **Order Date** (`final_order_date`, date, **Required**): This is the date the order was made.

Note: All required fields must be filled to complete the order. If you have any questions or issues, please contact our customer support team.
