# Construction Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Construction Quotation Form is used to collect detailed information for construction project quotations. It gathers essential details from the customer and project specifics to ensure accurate and comprehensive quotations are provided.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the first page with the Quotation Date and Quotation Number.
2. On the following pages, enter the customer's information: Customer Name, Customer Company (if applicable), Customer Address, Customer Email, and Customer Phone.
3. Fill out the project details: Project Name, Project Location, and Scope of Work.
4. List and detail each item in the project, including Item Description, Quantity, Unit, Price, and Line Total for each item.
5. Calculate the Subtotal by summing up the Line Totals of all items.
6. Enter the tax rate and calculate the Tax Amount.
7. Apply any applicable discount and calculate the Total Amount.
8. Select the Payment Terms and enter the Quotation Valid Until date.
9. Enter the Delivery or Completion Timeline.
10. Add any special Terms and Conditions.
11. Sign and date the form with your name and title.
12. Finally, sign with your authorized signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Quotation Date** (1): Enter the date of this quotation. (Required)
* **Quotation Number** (2): Enter a unique quotation reference. (Required)
* **Customer Name** (3): Enter the client or company name. (Required)
* **Customer Company** (4): Enter the customer's company name if applicable. (Optional)
* **Customer Address** (5): Enter the customer's mailing address. (Required)
* **Customer Email** (6): Enter the customer's email address. (Required)
* **Customer Phone** (7): Enter the customer's contact phone number. (Required)
* **Project Name** (8): Enter the name of the project. (Required)
* **Project Location** (9): Enter where the work will be done. (Required)
* **Scope of Work** (10): Enter a detailed description of the work to be performed. (Required)
* **Item 1 Description** (11): Enter the description for the first line item. (Required)
* **Item 1 Quantity** (12): Enter the quantity or hours for the first line item. (Required)
* **Item 1 Unit** (13): Select the unit of measure for the first line item. (Required)
* **Item 1 Unit Price** (14): Enter the price per unit for the first line item. (Required)
* **Item 1 Total** (15): Calculate the quantity times unit price. (Required)
* **Item 2 Description** (16): Enter the description for the second line item. (Optional)
* **Item 2 Quantity** (17): Enter the quantity for the second line item. (Optional)
* **Item 2 Unit Price** (18): Enter the price per unit for the second line item. (Optional)
* **Item 2 Total** (19): Calculate the quantity times unit price. (Optional)
* **Subtotal** (24): Sum up the line totals of all items. (Required)
* **Tax Rate** (25): Enter the sales tax percentage. (Optional)
* **Tax Amount** (26): Calculate the total tax. (Optional)
* **Discount** (27): Enter any discount applied. (Optional)
* **Total Amount** (28): Calculate the final total. (Required)
* **Payment Terms** (29): Select the payment terms. (Required)
* **Quotation Valid Until** (30): Enter the expiration date. (Required)
* **Delivery or Completion Timeline** (31): Enter the estimated time to complete. (Required)
* **Terms and Conditions** (32): Enter any special terms. (Optional)
* **Prepared By** (33): Enter the name of the person preparing the quote. (Required)
* **Authorized Signature** (34): Sign with your name and title. (Required)
