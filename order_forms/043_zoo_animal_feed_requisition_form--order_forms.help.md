# zoo_animal_feed_requisition_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The zoo animal feed requisition form is used to request feed for zoo animals. This form is meant to be completed by zoo staff to submit requests for animal feed supplies. Please make sure to complete this form carefully and accurately to ensure efficient feed delivery and animal care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the species of animals that require feed.
2. Choose the appropriate habitats for the selected species.
3. Enter the quantity of feed needed for each species.
4. Specify the date by which the feed is needed.
5. Choose the type of feed required.
6. Select the frequency at which the feed is needed.
7. Enter the delivery date and time for the feed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Species** (`species`, `select_multiple`): Select the species of animals that require feed.
  This field allows you to choose one or multiple species that need feed, such as Zebra, Gorilla, or Kangaroo.
- **Habitat** (`habitat`, `select_multiple`): Choose the habitat where the selected species reside.
  Select the habitats where the selected species live, such as Grassland, Jungle, or Desert.
- **Quantity** (`species_quantity`, `number`): Enter the quantity of feed needed for each species.
  Enter the exact amount of feed required for each selected species.
- **Needed By Date** (`needed_by_date`, `date`): Specify the date by which the feed is needed.
  Enter the date by which the feed is required, and it will be used for delivery scheduling.
- **Feed Type** (`feed_type`, `select_multiple`): Choose the type of feed required.
  Select the type of feed that the animals need, such as Dry Food, Green Food, or Meat Food.
- **Feed Frequency** (`feed_frequency`, `select_one`): Select the frequency at which the feed is needed.
  Choose how often the animals need feed, such as Every 1st, Every 2nd, or Every 3rd.
- **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`): Enter the date of delivery.
  Enter the date when the feed will be delivered to the zoo.
- **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, `time`): Enter the time of delivery.
  Enter the time of day when the feed will be delivered to the zoo.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the form is completed accurately and thoroughly to avoid any confusion or mistakes.
* Double-check the quantities and frequencies of feed required for each species.
* Verify that the delivery date and time are correct to ensure timely feed delivery.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the zoo staff for assistance.
