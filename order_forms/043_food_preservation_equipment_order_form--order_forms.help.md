# Food Preservation Equipment Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers order food preservation equipment from our company. Please fill out this form accurately to ensure that you are ordering the right equipment for your business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the **customer_info** field with your contact information.
2.  In the **equipment_selection** field, select the equipment that you would like to order.
3.  Enter your **shipping_address** in the **shipping_address** field.
4.  In the **payment_details** field, provide any additional payment instructions.
5.  Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Order Form (#1)**: This field should be left blank unless you have a specific order in mind. If you have a unique request, fill out this field to provide more information to our sales team.
- **Customer Information (#2)**: Enter your full name, company name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
  - This field is for internal use only, so please keep it accurate and up-to-date.
- **Equipment Selection (#3)**: Select the preservation equipment that you would like to order. Please be sure to select all the options that apply.
  - Please note that each selected item will be ordered separately, and you will be responsible for payment and shipping.
- **Contact Details (#4)**: Enter your email address and phone number so that we can contact you with any questions or concerns.
  - This field is required to ensure that we can get in touch with you.
- **Shipping Address (#5)**: Enter the shipping address where you would like the equipment to be delivered.
  - Please ensure that this address is correct to avoid any delivery issues.
- **Order Notes (#6)**: If you have any special instructions or comments, please enter them here.
  - This field is optional, but it will help our sales team better understand your needs.
- **Payment Details (#7)**: Enter any additional payment instructions or requests.
  - This field is optional, but it will help us process your payment accurately.

## Tips
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
- Be sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
- Please review each field carefully to ensure that you are ordering the right equipment.
