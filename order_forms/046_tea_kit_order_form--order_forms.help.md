# tea_kit_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "tea_kit_order_form" is used to order a tea kit. This form is designed to gather information from the customer to process and fulfill their order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your customer information in the "Customer Info" field.
2. Select the type of tea you would like to order by selecting "Yes" or "No" for "Tea Selection".
3. Enter the quantity of tea kits you would like to order in the "Quantity" field.
4. Enter your shipping information in the "Shipping Info" field.
5. If you have any additional information about your order, please enter it in the "Order Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, address, phone number, and any other relevant information.
* **Tea Selection** (`tea_selection`, select multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" to order a tea kit with tea, or "No" to order a tea kit without tea.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of tea kits you would like to order.
* **Shipping Info** (`shipping_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your shipping address, shipping method, and any other shipping information.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information about your order, such as special requests or instructions.
