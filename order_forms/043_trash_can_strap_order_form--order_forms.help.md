# Trash Can Strap Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for customers to place orders for trash cans. Fill out the form with your order details, and we'll process your order accordingly.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information for the order.
2. Select your preferred strap size from the available options.
3. Choose a payment method from the available options.
4. Optionally, provide your shipping address and phone number if required for order processing.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date you'd like to process your order.
* **Strap Size** (`strap_size`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select one or multiple strap sizes from the available options: Large, Medium, Small.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, **required**): Enter the number of trash cans you'd like to order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose how you'd like to pay for your order: Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your shipping address if it's different from your billing address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for delivery or payment verification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **optional**): Enter your email address for order updates and delivery notifications.
## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately to avoid any delays in processing your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
