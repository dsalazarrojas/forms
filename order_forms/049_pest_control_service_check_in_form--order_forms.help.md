# Pest Control Service Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pest Control Service Check In Form is a tool used to record and verify important information about pest control services provided to customers. This form helps to ensure accurate and consistent data collection, making it easier to manage and analyze customer interactions. By filling out this form, customers can provide their contact information, appointment details, and service requirements, which will help our staff to provide timely and efficient services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out all the required fields marked as "Required".
2. Ensure that you fill out all the fields in the correct format (e.g., use a date in the format "YYYY-MM-DD").
3. If a field is not relevant to your situation, simply leave it blank or fill out a brief note to indicate why it's not applicable (e.g., "No phone number available").

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, Optional)
 Enter the customer's name and any other relevant details about the individual or organization.

### 2. **Service Date** (`service_date`, date, **Required**)
Enter the date when the pest control service was provided or is scheduled to be provided.

### 3. **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, **Required**)
Enter the specific time of the appointment, e.g., 02:30 PM.

### 4. **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_multiple, **Optional**)
Select all the services provided to the customer (e.g., "Yes" for all services or specific services such as "Bait removal" and "Termite treatment").

### 5. **Notes** (`notes`, note, **Optional**)
Enter any additional comments or notes about the service, e.g., "Additional treatments required" or "Special instructions for pets".

### 6. **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, **Optional**)
Enter any relevant contact information, such as phone numbers or email addresses.

### 7. **Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, text, **Required**)
 Enter the customer's phone number. Please ensure it is in the correct format (XXX-XXX-XXX).

### 8. **Email** (`email`, email, **Optional**)
Enter the customer's email address (if available).

### 9. **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, **Optional**)
Enter the customer's street address or PO box (if available).

### 10. **Service Details** (`service_details`, text, **Required**)
Enter a brief description of the services provided, e.g., "Treatments for ants, cockroaches, and fleas".
