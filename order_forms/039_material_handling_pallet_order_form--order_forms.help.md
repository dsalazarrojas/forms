# Material Handling Pallet Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Material Handling Pallet Order Form is used to collect information from customers for material handling purposes. This form is intended to streamline the ordering process, ensuring that all necessary details are captured efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Customer Info section with your contact details.
2. Provide your contact information in the Contact Info section.
3. Specify your delivery preferences in the Delivery Info section.
4. Choose your shipping options (yes/no) in the Shipping Options section.
5. Select your shipping address (yes/no) in the Shipping Address section.
6. Enter your payment details in the Payment Info section.
7. List your order items in the Order Items section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info**: (1, text, required) Enter your name and contact details to ensure we can contact you effectively.
* **Contact Info**: (2, text, required) Provide your contact information, including phone number and email address, so we can reach out to you.
* **Delivery Info**: (3, text, required) Specify your delivery preferences, such as date and time of delivery.
* **Shipping Options**: (4, select_multiple, required) Choose whether you want to ship your order yes or no.
* **Shipping Address**: (5, select_multiple, required) Choose whether you need to ship your order address yes or no.
* **Payment Info**: (6, text, required) Enter your payment details, including method and amount.
* **Order Items**: (7, text, required) List the specific items you are ordering, including quantity and details.
