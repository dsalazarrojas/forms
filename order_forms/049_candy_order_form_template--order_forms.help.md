# Candy Order Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and order details for a successful candy order submission. Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately to ensure correct delivery and order tracking.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the personal details section (2-5) with your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. In the candy selection section (7-8), choose the type of candies you would like to order and specify the quantity per item.
3. Select the overall box size and packaging style in the box size selection (9-10) and preferred packaging style sections.
4. If you have any dietary restrictions, select them in the dietary restrictions section (12).
5. Indicate if the order is a gift and add a gift note in the gift note section (14), if applicable.
6. Choose a delivery method and date in the delivery and payment section (17-18).
7. Finally, select a payment method and agree to the terms of service in the final sections (20-21).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (2): Enter your full name as it appears on your payment account.
* **Email Address** (3): Enter a valid email address for order tracking and receipt.
* **Phone Number** (4): Enter your phone number for delivery and order confirmation.
* **Shipping Address** (5): Enter your full shipping address for accurate delivery.
* **Choose Your Sweets** (7): Select all the type of candies you would like to order.
* **Order Quantity per Item** (8): Specify the quantity of each candy type.
* **Total Box Size** (9): Choose the overall size of your order.
* **Preferred Packaging Style** (10): Select the preferred packaging style for your order.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (12): Select any dietary restrictions that apply to your order.
* **Is this a Gift** (13): Indicate if the order is a gift.
* **Gift Note** (14): Add a personalized message for the gift recipient.
* **Preferred Delivery Date** (16): Choose a preferred delivery date for your order.
* **Shipping Method** (17): Select the shipping method for your order (Standard, Express, or Local Pickup).
* **Payment Method** (18): Choose a payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, Stripe, or Apple Pay).
* **Special Instructions** (19): Add any specific delivery or packaging instructions.
* **I Agree to the Terms of Service** (20): Accept the terms of service before submitting your order.
* **Date of Order** (21): Specify the date of your order.
