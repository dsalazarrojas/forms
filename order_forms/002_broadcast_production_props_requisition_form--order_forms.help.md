# Broadcast Production Props Requisition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is intended for use by employees requesting props for broadcast production projects. Fill out this form to request props for your upcoming projects.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your name and department in the respective fields.
2.  Provide the specific name or description of the prop you need.
3.  Indicate how many items you need.
4.  Give a detailed description of the prop and its purpose in the production.
5.  If the prop is urgent, mark it as such.
6.  Choose how you would like the prop to be delivered (pick up or delivery).
7.  Enter the date you need the prop to be delivered or ready for pick-up.
8.  Specify the location where you would like the prop delivered.
9.  Enter the email of your supervisor to receive approval notifications.
10.  Add any additional notes for the logistics team, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   *Requester Name* (`requester_name`, **text**, required): Full name of the person making the request.
*   *Department* (`department`, **select_one**, required): Select the department making the request (e.g., Broadcast Production, Newsroom, Technical Operations, etc.).
*   *Prop Name* (`prop_name`, **text**, required): The specific name or description of the prop needed.
*   *Quantity* (`quantity`, **number**, required): Number of items needed for the production.
*   *Description* (`description`, **text**, optional): Detailed description of the prop and its purpose in the production.
*   *Urgent Request* (`urgent`, **select_one**, required): Check if the prop is urgent.
*   *Delivery Method* (`delivery_method`, **select_one**, required): Choose between "Pick Up" or "Delivery".
*   *Delivery Date* (`delivery_date`, **date**, required): Date when the prop should be ready or delivered.
*   *Delivery Location* (`location`, **text**, required): Location address or studio name where the prop should be delivered.
*   *Supervisor Email* (`supervisor_email`, **email**, required): Enter the email of your supervisor for approval notifications.
*   *Additional Notes* (`additional_notes`, **note**, optional): Any extra information for the logistics team to know.

## Tips

*   Be as specific as possible when requesting a prop to avoid misunderstandings.
*   Ensure you have the necessary approval from your supervisor before submitting this form.
*   Double-check the delivery date and method to ensure it meets your production schedule.
*   Enter your correct email address for supervisor approval notifications.
*   Keep additional notes concise and clear for the logistics team to understand.
