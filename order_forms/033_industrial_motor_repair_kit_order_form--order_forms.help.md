# Industrial Motor Repair Kit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers to process their orders for industrial motor repair kits accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Order Form**
* **Order Form**: This is the main section where you can enter your order details. Please type in your order information here.

### 2. **Shipping Information**
* **Shipping Information**: This section is where you enter your shipping details. Please ensure that your shipping address is up to date and accurate.

### 3. **Customer Contact**
* **Customer Contact**: This section is where you can enter your contact details. Please include your name and phone number here.

### 4. **Customer Contact Info**
* **Customer Contact Info**: This section is where you can enter any additional contact information you may have.

### 5. **Customer Shipping Address**
* **Customer Shipping Address**: This section is where you enter your shipping address. Please ensure that it is accurate and up to date.

### 6. **Customer Shipping Info** and **Customer Payment Info**
* **Customer Shipping Info** and **Customer Payment Info**: These sections are where you can enter any additional shipping or payment information you may have.

### 7. **Customer Notes**
* **Customer Notes**: This is a text area where you can add any additional notes or comments you may have. This is not required, but it can be helpful for our team to process your order accurately.

### 8. **Customer Payment Info** (this field was labeled as "Customer Payment Info" but it seems like it should be part of "Customer Shipping Info")
* **Customer Payment Info**: This section was labeled as "Customer Payment Info" but it actually seems like it should be part of "Customer Shipping Info". Please disregard this field.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in correctly to avoid any issues with processing your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* If you would like to make any changes to your order, please contact us directly.
