# golf_course_equipment_order_request - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "golf_course_equipment_order_request" form is used to request golf course equipment, allowing staff to submit a request for specific equipment to be picked up or delivered.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields and determine the equipment you need.
2. Choose your preferred option for pickup or delivery.
3. Select the date and time you would like to receive the equipment.
4. Specify the type and quantity of equipment you need.
5. Add any special instructions for the request.
6. Provide contact information (name, phone number, and email) for the person requesting the equipment.
7. Enter the name, contact information, and manager's name for the pro shop (if applicable).
8. Specify the model and brand of the equipment you need.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pickup Option**: 
  • `pickup_option` (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose whether you would like to pick up the equipment at the golf course or have it delivered. Options are 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Delivery Option**: 
  • `delivery_option` (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose whether you would like to have the equipment delivered or not. Options are 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Preferred Date**: 
  • `preferred_date` (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to receive the equipment.
* **Preferred Time**: 
  • `preferred_time` (`time`, required/optional): Enter the time you would like to receive the equipment.
* **Equipment Type**: 
  • `equipment_type` (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the type of equipment you need. Options are 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Equipment Quantity**: 
  • `equipment_quantity` (`number`, required/optional): Enter the quantity of equipment you need.
* **Special Instructions**: 
  • `special_instructions` (`note`, required/optional): Add any special instructions for the request.
* **Contact Name**: 
  • `contact_name` (`text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person making the request.
* **Contact Phone**: 
  • `contact_phone` (`text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the person making the request.
* **Contact Email**: 
  • `contact_email` (`email`, required/optional): Enter the email of the person making the request.
* **Pro Shop Name**: 
  • `pro_shop_name` (`text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the pro shop.
* **Pro Shop Contact**: 
  • `pro_shop_contact` (`text`, required/optional): Enter the contact information for the pro shop.
* **Pro Shop Phone**: 
  • `pro_shop_phone` (`text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the pro shop.
* **Pro Shop Email**: 
  • `pro_shop_email` (`email`, required/optional): Enter the email of the pro shop.
* **Equipment Model**: 
  • `equipment_model` (`text`, required/optional): Enter the model of the equipment you need.
* **Equipment Brand**: 
  • `equipment_brand` (`text`, required/optional): Enter the brand of the equipment you need.
* **Pro Shop Manager**: 
  • `pro_shop_manager` (`text`, required/optional): Enter the manager's name of the pro shop.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate contact information to ensure timely communication.
* If you need help with the form, ask your supervisor or colleague for assistance.
* Double-check your selections before submitting the form to avoid errors.
