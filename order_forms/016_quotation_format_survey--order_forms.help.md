# Quotation Format Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quotation Format Survey is designed to capture and store information about quotations or quotes submitted to clients. This form helps you track quote submissions, their status, and related details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter client information, including their contact details and any relevant notes.
2. Add project information, such as project name, date, and time.
3. Provide budget information, including any relevant costs or estimates.
4. Enter contact information, including phone numbers and email addresses.
5. Confirm your submission by checking the "Confirm Submission" box.
6. Finally, review and verify all the information entered before submitting.
