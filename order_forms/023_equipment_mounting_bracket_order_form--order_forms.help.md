# Equipment Mounting Bracket Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect order information for mounting brackets, such as customer details, delivery preferences, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form header section with any necessary details.
2.  Provide customer information, including their name and contact details.
3.  Specify your delivery preferences.
4.  Enter equipment information, including details about the equipment you are ordering.
5.  Add any relevant order information.
6.  Select one of the submission options: Submit All, Submit Partial, or Submit None.
7.  If you choose to Submit Partial, please provide a brief explanation.
8.  Sign the form to confirm.
9.  Add any additional information, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header** (`form_header`, `note`, required: false): This section serves as a placeholder for any additional text that you want to include at the top of the form. You can add any information you feel is relevant or important.
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name, title, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): This is another field for you to provide contact details for the customer.
* **Delivery Preferences** (`delivery_preferences`, `text`, required: false): Specify any special delivery instructions or preferences here.
* **Equipment Information** (`equipment_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter details about the equipment you are ordering, including any relevant specifications or serial numbers.
* **Order Information** (`order_info`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional information about the order, such as any custom requirements or special instructions.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter payment details, including the amount and method of payment.
* **Delivery Confirmation** (`delivery_confirmation`, `text`, required: false): Confirm that you have received the order details accurately.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one of the following options:
	+ Submit All: Submit the entire form as is.
	+ Submit Partial: Submit only a portion of the form, with a brief explanation of why.
	+ Submit None: Do not submit the form.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required: false): Sign the form to confirm that it is accurate and complete.
* **Additional Information** (`note`, `note`, required: false): This is a text field for you to add any additional comments or information about the order.
