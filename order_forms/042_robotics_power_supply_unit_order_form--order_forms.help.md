# Robotics Power Supply Unit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture essential information from customers when placing orders for robotics power supply units. It helps us process their orders efficiently and ensure accurate delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Customer Details" tab and fill in the required information, including your name, email address, and any other relevant details.
2. Move to the "Shipping Info" tab and provide your shipping address, including your name, address, and any other shipping instructions.
3. In the "Delivery Preferences" tab, select your preferred delivery date and any special instructions for delivery.
4. Finally, move to the "Order Total" tab and enter the total cost of your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, required): This is the main contact information for the customer, including their name and email address. Please fill in this section with accurate information to enable us to contact you regarding your order.
* **Shipping Info** (`shipping_info`, text, required): This section collects your shipping address and any special instructions for delivery. Please make sure to fill in this information accurately to avoid any delays or issues with delivery.
* **Delivery Preferences** (`delivery_preferences`, text, required): In this section, you can select your preferred delivery date and provide any special instructions for delivery.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, required): This is the total cost of your order, which you need to fill in accurately to ensure that we can process your order correctly.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately and completely to avoid any issues with processing your order.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure that everything is correct.
* If you have any special requests or instructions for delivery, please fill in the "Delivery Preferences" section accordingly.
