# Procurement Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Procurement Order Form is a tool for employees to create new procurement orders for their team or department. This form helps gather essential information to process and fulfill these orders efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the contact person responsible for the order.
2. Choose the type of order being made (e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3").
3. Enter the date and time of the order.
4. Select the products or services being ordered (multiple selections are allowed).
5. Enter the delivery address for the order.
6. If necessary, enter any additional notes about the order.
7. Enter the contact number of the person placing the order.
8. Finally, enter the total price for the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Person (contact_person)**: Enter the name of the person who is responsible for this procurement order.
* **Order Type (order_type)**: Choose the type of order being made from the available options.
* **Order Date (order_date)**: Enter the date on which the order is to be fulfilled.
* **Order Time (order_time)**: Enter the time of day for the order (e.g., 08:00, 12:00, etc.).
* **Products/Services (products_services)**: Select the products or services being ordered. You can choose multiple options.
* **Delivery Address (delivery_address)**: Enter the address where the ordered products or services are to be delivered.
* **Total Price (total_price)**: Enter the total price of the ordered products or services.
* **Procurement Department (procurement_department)**: Enter the department responsible for this procurement order.
* **Notes (notes)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about this order.
* **Contact Number (contact_number)**: Enter the contact number of the person placing the order.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check the accuracy of the information entered before submitting the form.
* Ensure that the contact person is informed about the order and its status.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, do not hesitate to reach out to the procurement team for assistance.
