# Screen Printing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to request screen printing services for their order, including custom artwork and production preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
  1. Fill in your name and email address in the respective fields. 
  2. Select the correct design type (T-shirt, Hoodie, Hat, etc.) and production method (Screen Printing, Direct-to-Dye, Emboss, etc.).
  3. Choose the artwork type(s) that apply to your order (vector, raster, hand-drawn, digital).
  4. Provide a detailed description of your artwork in the "Artwork Details" field.
  5. Set the deadline for your order in the format of "YYYY-MM-DD".
  6. Select the correct quantity and color(s) for your order.
  7. Enter the price for your order.
  8. Choose the currency for your order (USD, CAD, EUR).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Name (text) (Required: false)
Name: Enter your name for contact purposes.

### 2. Email (email) (Required: false)
Email: Enter your email address in the format of "example@email.com".

### 3. Phone (text) (Required: false)
Phone: Enter your phone number for communication.

### 4. Design Type (select_one) (Required: false)
Design Type: Choose the type of design you would like to screen print (T-shirt, Hoodie, Hat, etc.).

### 5. Artwork Type (select_multiple) (Required: false)
Artwork Type: Choose the type(s) of artwork that apply to your order (vector, raster, hand-drawn, digital).

### 6. Artwork Details (text) (Required: false)
Artwork Details: Provide a detailed description of your artwork.

### 7. Deadline (date) (Required: false)
Deadline: Set the deadline for your order in the format of "YYYY-MM-DD".

### 8. Production Method (select_one) (Required: false)
Production Method: Choose the production method for your order (Screen Printing, Direct-to-Dye, Emboss, etc.).

### 9. Quantity (number) (Required: false)
Quantity: Enter the quantity of the printed item(s).

### 10. Color (select_multiple) (Required: false)
Color: Choose the color(s) for your order (Red, Blue, Green, Yellow).

### 11. Price (number) (Required: false)
Price: Enter the price for your order.

### 12. Currency (select_one) (Required: false)
Currency: Choose the currency for your order (USD, CAD, EUR).
