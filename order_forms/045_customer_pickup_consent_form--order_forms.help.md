# Customer Pickup Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to obtain consent from customers for pickup services. It captures essential information such as pickup location, date and time, contact details, and customer declaration for pickup services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "true" in the form schema) with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Fill in the pickup location and date fields with the correct information.
3. Select the appropriate contact method from the options provided for the contact number and contact email fields.
4. Provide a clear and concise description in the pickup details field.
5. Sign and date the customer declaration and business declaration fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pickup Consent**:
  * This is the main section of the form where you need to confirm that you understand and agree to the pickup terms.
  * Please provide a clear and concise answer, e.g., "I consent to the pickup service."
* **Pickup Location**:
  * Enter the exact location where the pickup is scheduled to take place.
  * Make sure to enter the correct address, including any specific details such as floor or suite number.
* **Pickup Date**:
  * Enter the date on which the pickup is scheduled to take place.
  * Use the format MM/DD/YYYY (Month, Day, Year).
* **Pickup Time**:
  * Enter the time of the pickup.
  * Use the format 12:00 AM - 11:59 PM.
* **Contact Number**:
  * Select the method of contact you prefer.
  * Options include phone, email, or other.
* **Contact Email**:
  * Enter your email address if you selected email as the contact method.
  * Make sure to enter a valid email address.
* **Pickup Details**:
  * Provide a clear and concise description of the pickup details.
  * Include any relevant information that the pickup staff might need to know.
* **Customer Declaration**:
  * This is a required field that needs to be signed and dated.
  * Please sign and date the declaration after reading and understanding the pickup terms.
  * Make sure to print and keep a copy for your records.
* **Business Declaration**:
  * This is a required field that needs to be signed and dated.
  * Please sign and date the declaration after reading and understanding the pickup terms.
  * Make sure to print and keep a copy for your records.
