# Priority Boarding Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Priority Boarding Access Form is used to collect essential information for priority boarding access purposes. This form is intended for customers who require priority boarding on their flights. It is crucial to provide accurate and complete information in this form to ensure a smooth and efficient boarding experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) as you go.
2. For the "Priority Boarding" field, select the desired priority boarding option (Priority 1, 2, or 3).
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, making sure to use the format `example@email.com`.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, including the country code.
5. For the "Date of Travel" field, select the date of your flight in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
6. Select the time of your flight in the correct format (HH:MM).
7. For the "Travel Agency" fields, enter the name of your travel agency and their contact information if available.
8. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
9. Confirm that the information provided is accurate by selecting the correct options for "Travel Agency Confirmation", "Customer Confirmation", "Confirm Email", "Priority Confirmation", and "Payment Terms".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address in the format `example@email.com`.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, including the country code.
* **Priority Boarding**: Select the desired priority boarding option (Priority 1, 2, or 3).
* **Boarding Number**: Enter your boarding number as provided by the travel agency.
* **Travel Agency**: Enter the name of your travel agency.
* **Travel Agency Phone**: Enter the phone number of your travel agency.
* **Travel Agency Email**: Enter the email address of your travel agency.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your travel arrangement.
* **Date of Travel**: Select the date of your flight in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Time of Travel**: Select the time of your flight in the correct format (HH:MM).
* **Customer Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your travel arrangements.
* **Travel Agency Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes from your travel agency.
* **Travel Agency Confirmation**: Confirm that the information provided by the travel agency is accurate.
* **Customer Confirmation**: Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Confirm Email**: Confirm that the email address provided is correct.
* **Priority Confirmation**: Confirm that the priority boarding selection is accurate.
* **Payment Terms**: Confirm the payment terms of your travel arrangement.
* **Credit Card Number**: Enter your credit card number (not required for payment).
* **Credit Card Security Code**: Enter the security code for your credit card (not required for payment).
* **Credit Card Expiration**: Enter the expiration date of your credit card (not required for payment).
* **Credit Card CVV**: Enter the CVV of your credit card (not required for payment).
* **Credit Card Month**: Enter the month of your credit card expiration (not required for payment).
* **Credit Card Year**: Enter the year of your credit card expiration (not required for payment).

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the travel agency or customer support for assistance.
* This form is for priority boarding access purposes only and is not intended for general travel information.
