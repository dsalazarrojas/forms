# Shipping Discount Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shipping Discount Registration Form is used to register businesses that wish to participate in shipping discounts. This form helps us verify the identity and legitimacy of the business, and ensure that they are able to receive and process the shipping discounts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your Shipping Discount Business Name in the "Shipping Discount Business Name" field.
2. Provide your Business Address in the "Business Address" field.
3. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shipping Discount Business Name** (text, required): Enter the name of your business as it appears on your company registration or business card.
* **Business Address** (text, optional): Enter your business address where we can send any important information and documents.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for managing the business or the contact person for shipping discounts.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter the email address associated with your business, where we can send any updates and information about shipping discounts.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for further assistance.
* This form is only for businesses that are not already registered. If you are already registered, do not submit this form again.
