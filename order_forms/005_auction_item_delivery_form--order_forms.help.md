# Auction Item Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather buyer information and delivery preferences for the successful delivery of auction items.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
* Fill in your buyer information:
	+ **Buyer Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
	+ **Bidder Number**: Enter your unique buyer number.
	+ **Email Address**: Enter your active email address.
	+ **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* Provide purchase details:
	+ **Invoice Number**: Enter your invoice number for this purchase.
	+ **Items for Delivery**: List the lot numbers or short descriptions of items to be delivered.
* Select your delivery preferences:
	+ **Preferred Delivery Method**: Choose from options like Local Pickup, Local Delivery (Carrier), Domestic Shipping (FedEx/UPS), International Shipping, and White Glove Moving Service.
	+ **Delivery Address**: Enter your delivery address in the format of Street, City, State, ZIP.
* Optional fields:
	+ **Requested Delivery Date**: If you have a specific date in mind for delivery, enter it here.
	+ **Special Handling Instructions**: If you need special handling for fragile items or have specific instructions for delivery, enter them here.
* Additional options:
	+ **Would you like to add Shipping Insurance?**: Choose Yes or No.
	+ **Require Signature on Delivery?**: Choose Yes or No.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Buyer Full Name**: Your official name as per your documents.
* **Bidder Number**: Your unique buyer number.
* **Email Address**: Your active email address.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number.
* **Invoice Number**: Your invoice number for this purchase.
* **Items for Delivery**: List the lot numbers or short descriptions of items to be delivered.
* **Preferred Delivery Method**: Choose a delivery option that suits your needs.
* **Delivery Address**: Enter your address in the format of Street, City, State, ZIP.
* **Requested Delivery Date**: Enter a date if you have a specific preference.
* **Special Handling Instructions**: Enter special instructions for delivery if required.
* **Would you like to add Shipping Insurance?**: Choose Yes or No.
* **Require Signature on Delivery?**: Choose Yes or No.
