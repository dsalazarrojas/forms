# electronics_installation_delivery_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers about their electronics installation and delivery requests. This form helps ensure smooth and efficient delivery of customer-purchased electronics.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your customer information in the "customer_info" field.
2. Enter details about the delivery information in the "delivery_info" field.
3. Enter details about the electronic device in the "electronic_device_info" field.
4. Provide information about the installation process in the "installation_info" field.
5. Enter any additional details about the order in the "order_details" field.
6. Confirm your order by entering a confirmation in the "confirmation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (customer_info, text, required/optional): Please enter your name, address, and contact information so we can get in touch with you.
* **Delivery Information** (delivery_info, text, required/optional): Enter details about the delivery date, time, and location to ensure we can deliver your electronics on time.
* **Electronic Device Information** (electronic_device_info, text, required/optional): Describe the electronic device you would like to be installed, including its serial number or model number.
* **Installation Information** (installation_info, text, required/optional): Provide details about where you would like the electronic device installed, including room or location.
* **Order Details** (order_details, text, required/optional): Enter any additional details about your order, such as the reason for purchase or any special requests.
* **Confirmation** (confirmation, text, required/optional): Enter a confirmation code or message to confirm your order.
