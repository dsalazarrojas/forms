# Industrial Cutting Fluid Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Industrial Cutting Fluid Order Form" is designed for customers to place orders for industrial cutting fluids, providing contact information, delivery details, product selection, and shipping information for processing the order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your contact information, including name, email, and any other relevant details.
2. Specify delivery details, such as date and time.
3. Select the product you wish to order from the available options.
4. Provide shipping information, including address, city, state, and zip code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant contact details.
- **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the date and time of the desired delivery.
- **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, required/optional): Select a date for the delivery of the product.
- **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, `time`, required/optional): Choose a time for the delivery of the product.
- **Product Selection** (`product_info`, `text`, required/optional): Select the industrial cutting fluid product you wish to order from the options provided.
- **Product ID** (`product_id`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the ID of the product you've selected.
- **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the product you've chosen.
- **Product Quantity** (`product_quantity`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the quantity of the product you wish to order.
- **Product Price** (`product_price`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the price of the product.
- **Delivery Info** (`delivery_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional delivery instructions or information.
- **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address where the product will be delivered.
- **Delivery City** (`delivery_city`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the city of the delivery address.
- **Delivery State** (`delivery_state`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the state of the delivery address.
- **Delivery Zip** (`delivery_zip`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the zip code of the delivery address.
- **Shipping Info** (`shipping_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any shipping instructions or information.
- **Shipping Name** (`shipping_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person or organization receiving the order.
- **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the shipping location.
- **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the city of the shipping address.
- **Shipping State** (`shipping_state`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the state of the shipping address.
- **Shipping Zip** (`shipping_zip`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the zip code of the shipping address.
- **Delivery Instructions** (`delivery_instructions`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional delivery instructions.
- **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any comments or notes about the order.
- **Order Total** (`order_total`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the total cost of the order.

## Tips
* Double-check your contact and shipping information for accuracy to avoid delays or errors.
* Make sure to provide clear delivery and shipping instructions to ensure a smooth delivery process.
* Review your order total carefully to ensure it matches your expectations.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our customer support team for assistance.
