# Live Plant Delivery Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate live plant delivery orders. It gathers essential information to ensure a smooth and efficient delivery process. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Select the desired payment method.
3. Choose the shipping option that suits your needs.
4. Provide any additional order notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details** (`order_details`): Enter a brief description of the order.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_method`): Select the payment method you prefer. This may include Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Stripe, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or Amazon Pay.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`): Type in your shipping address for efficient delivery.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`): Provide any additional shipping instructions or requests.
* **Total Price** (`total_price`): Enter the total price of the order.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`): Enter any comments or instructions you have for the order.
* **Shipping Options** (`shipping_method`): Choose your preferred shipping method: Ground Shipping, Next Day Shipping, Two Day Shipping, or Three Day Shipping.
* **Shipping Cost** (`shipping_cost`): This field will be auto-filled based on your selected shipping option.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_details`): Enter any additional payment details or information.
* **Payment Gateway** (`payment_gateway`): Select the payment gateway to use. This may include Stripe, PayPal, Authorize, Bank Transfer, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or Amazon Pay.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`): Enter the date of the order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`): Enter the time of the order.
* **Plant Type** (`plant_type`): Select the type of plant you are ordering. This may include Succulents, Bulbs, Fruits, Herbs, Ferns, Grasses, or Trees.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`): Enter the quantity of the plant you are ordering.
* **Plant Price** (`plant_price`): Enter the price of the plant.
