# Beverage CO₂ Cylinder Refill Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect essential information to process and refill CO₂ cylinders. Please ensure that you complete this form accurately, as any errors may lead to delays or inaccuracies in the refill process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your personal details in the **Personal Information** section.
2. Choose the correct **Primary selection** from the drop-down menu that best applies to your order.
3. Enter your **Full name**, **Email address**, and **Phone number** for contact purposes.
4. Select the relevant **Date** for your order.
5. If applicable, provide the **Quantity or rating** and **Estimated value** for the ordered items.
6. Optionally, include any **Additional details** that might aid in processing your order.
7. Confirm your agreement by choosing from the options in the **Agreement** section.
8. If necessary, specify the **Preferred time** for scheduling purposes.
9. Finally, provide any **Final comments or notes** in the **Final comments** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: Enter your legal full name as per your official records.
* **Email address**: Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number including the country code if you are outside of our local region.
* **Date**: Select the date for which you are submitting this order.
* **Primary selection**: Choose the option from the list that best applies to your order.
* **Quantity or rating**: Enter a whole number for the quantity or rating of the items you are ordering.
* **Additional details**: Provide any additional information that might be helpful for us to process your order correctly.
* **Agreement**: Select all the options that apply to your order.
* **Estimated value**: Enter the estimated value of the items you are ordering in decimal format if applicable.
* **Preferred time**: Enter a time if scheduling is required for your order.
* **Final comments or notes**: Provide any final comments or notes regarding your order.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately to prevent delays in processing your order.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification before proceeding.
* You can submit this form multiple times if you have multiple orders, but please ensure each submission is accurate and complete.
