# Roofing Materials Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Roofing Materials Requisition Form is used to request roofing materials from a supplier. This form gathers information about the customer's needs and the supplier's contact details. The form helps to ensure that the supplier has all the necessary information to fulfill the material requisition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your customer information, including your name and any other relevant details.
2. Provide your contact person's name and their contact email address.
3. Enter your contact phone number.
4. Select the type of material you are requesting from the options provided.
5. Enter the quantity of the material needed.
6. Enter the unit price of the material.
7. Enter the supplier's address where the material will be delivered from.
8. Provide the supplier's contact name and phone number.
9. Choose the date the material will be delivered.
10. Choose the date the material will be picked up.
11. Select the status of the material (e.g., "In Stock", "Out of Stock", "On Order").
12. Add any additional notes about the material.
13. Add any notes for the customer.
14. Sign and date the form to confirm the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (id: 1):
	+ This field is for the customer's contact information.
	+ Please enter your name and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Person** (id: 2):
	+ Enter the name of the person who should be contacted for more information about the material requisition.
* **Contact E-mail** (id: 3):
	+ Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (id: 4):
	+ Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Material Type** (id: 5):
	+ Select the type of material you are requesting from the options provided.
* **Quantity** (id: 6):
	+ Enter the quantity of the material needed.
* **Unit Price** (id: 7):
	+ Enter the unit price of the material.
* **Supplier Address** (id: 8):
	+ Enter the address where the material will be delivered from.
* **Supplier Contact** (id: 9):
	+ Enter the name and phone number of the supplier's contact person.
* **Material Delivery Date** (id: 10):
	+ Choose the date the material will be delivered.
* **Material Pickup Date** (id: 11):
	+ Choose the date the material will be picked up.
* **Material Status** (id: 12):
	+ Select the status of the material (e.g., "In Stock", "Out of Stock", "On Order").
* **Material Note** (id: 13):
	+ Add any additional notes about the material.
* **Customer Note** (id: 14):
	+ Add any notes for the customer.
* **Customer Signature** (id: 15):
	+ Sign and date the form to confirm the request.
* **Supplier Signature** (id: 16):
	+ Sign and date the form to confirm receipt of the request.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and honestly. If you are unsure about any field, please contact the supplier or contact person for clarification.
