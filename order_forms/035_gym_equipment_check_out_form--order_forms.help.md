# gym_equipment_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to check out gym equipment for members. It gathers information about the member's details, the equipment they want to rent, the rental period, and other relevant details for internal tracking and reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your Member Details section with your name, phone number, and email address.
2. Choose the equipment you want to rent from the Equipment Rental dropdown menu.
3. Select the rental period for the equipment.
4. (Optional) Provide any additional information or comments in the Note field.
5. Choose who the equipment is assigned to from the Assigned To dropdown menu.
6. Review your submission for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Details** (`Member Details`, text, optional): Enter your name, phone number, and email address to identify yourself as the member renting the equipment.
* **Equipment Rental** (`Equipment Rental`, select_one, optional): Choose the equipment you want to rent from the available options.
* **Rental Period** (`Rental Period`, date, optional): Select the duration of time you want to rent the equipment for.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Equipment Status** (`Equipment Status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the current status of the equipment (e.g., available, in use, maintenance).
* **Note** (`Note`, note, optional): Add any additional information or comments about the rental.
* **Assigned To** (`Assigned To`, select_one, optional): Choose who the equipment is assigned to from the available options.

## Tips
Before submitting the form, make sure to review all fields for accuracy and completeness. If you're unsure about any field, refer to your gym's equipment management policies or consult with a staff member for assistance.
