# Product Distribution Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for customers to schedule a product distribution appointment, providing essential details to facilitate a successful delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **First name** in the designated field.
2. Enter your **Last name** in the designated field.
3. Enter your **Email** in the designated field. This is an email address to contact you.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the designated field.
5. Select the **Date** for your appointment from the calendar or input in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
6. Choose the **Time** for your appointment in the format of 12:00 PM.
7. Enter the **Product name** you wish to have distributed.
8. Specify the **Quantity** of the product needed.
9. Enter your **Address** for the product delivery.
10. Add any **Notes** regarding your appointment or product distribution requirements.
11. Select who the **Assigned to** will be - either ChatJIMMY or Other.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name** (text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Last name** (text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address for us to contact you regarding your appointment.
- **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you if needed.
- **Date** (date, optional): Select the date for your appointment from the calendar or input in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Time** (time, optional): Choose the time for your appointment in the 12-hour format (e.g., 12:00 PM).
- **Product name** (text, optional): Please specify the name of the product you wish to have distributed.
- **Quantity** (number, optional): Enter the number of products you need.
- **Address** (text, optional): Enter your delivery address where you can receive the products.
- **Notes** (text, optional): Add any additional comments or requirements for your appointment or product distribution.
- **Assigned to** (select_one, optional): Choose between ChatJIMMY and Other for who will be handling your product distribution.

## Tips
- Please ensure all required fields are filled out correctly to avoid form errors.
- For date and time fields, use the formats suggested to avoid any confusion.
- If you have any additional questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us at the provided email or phone number.
