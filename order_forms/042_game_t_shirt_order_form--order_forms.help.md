# Game T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Game T Shirt Order Form is designed to collect necessary information from customers for ordering game-themed t-shirts. It helps in processing orders efficiently and accurately, ensuring that the customer receives the correct product with their desired specifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Game T-shirt Order" page from the form.
2. Enter your game t-shirt order in the provided text field on the first page.
3. Proceed to the next page, "Customer Info", to provide your details.
4. Fill in your customer information, including any necessary contact details.
5. Move on to "T-shirt size" and select your preferred size from the provided options.
6. Similarly, select your preferred t-shirt color from the options available.
7. On the next page, "Payment Info", enter your payment details.
8. Provide your shipping and card information for successful delivery.
9. Finally, add any order comments or special requests in the "Order Comments" field.
10. Review your form carefully before submitting it for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Game T-shirt Order**: This field is used to describe your order details, such as the game theme or design you prefer.
* **Customer Info**: This page is used to input your personal details, which will aid in processing and delivery of your order. Please fill this in accurately.
* **T-shirt size**: Select your preferred size for your t-shirt from the provided options.
* **T-shirt color**: Select your preferred color for your t-shirt from the options provided.
* **Payment Info**: This field is used to input your payment details for successful processing.
* **Shipping Info**: Provide your shipping details, which will help in delivering your order to the correct location.
* **Card Info**: Input your card information for secure payment processing.
* **Order Comments**: This field is used to include any special comments or requests you may have for your order.
