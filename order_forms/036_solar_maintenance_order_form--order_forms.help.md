# Solar Maintenance Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Solar Maintenance Order Form is used to schedule and plan for solar maintenance tasks. It is intended to be completed by customers or users to request a service visit for their solar panel system.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the maintenance address where the solar panel system is located.
2. Choose a date for the maintenance visit.
3. Describe the issue with your solar panel system.
4. Select a preferred schedule appointment time for the maintenance visit.
5. Add any additional notes about the issue or maintenance visit.
6. Enter your email address so we can contact you about the maintenance visit.
7. Enter your phone number so we can contact you about the maintenance visit.
8. Select a service technician to be assigned to the maintenance visit.
9. Choose a date for the maintenance visit.
10. Choose a time for the maintenance visit.
11. Add any additional notes about the maintenance visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **maintenance_address** (`maintenance_address`, text, required): Enter the address where the solar panel system is located.
* **maintenance_date** (`maintenance_date`, date, required): Choose a date for the maintenance visit.
* **maintenance_description** (`maintenance_description`, text, required): Describe the issue with your solar panel system.
* **schedule_appt** (`schedule_appt`, select multiple, not required): Select a preferred schedule appointment time for the maintenance visit.
* **customer_notes** (`customer_notes`, note, not required): Add any additional notes about the issue or maintenance visit.
* **customer_email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about the maintenance visit.
* **customer_phone** (`customer_phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about the maintenance visit.
* **service_technician** (`service_technician`, select one, not required): Select a service technician to be assigned to the maintenance visit.
* **service_date** (`service_date`, date, not required): Choose a date for the maintenance visit.
* **service_time** (`service_time`, time, not required): Choose a time for the maintenance visit.
* **service_notes** (`service_notes`, note, not required): Add any additional notes about the maintenance visit.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide as much detail as possible when describing the issue with your solar panel system.
* Choose a schedule appointment time that works best for you.
* If you need to contact us about the maintenance visit, please be sure to include your email address and phone number.
* If you have any additional notes or information about the maintenance visit, please include them in the customer notes field.
* If you are selecting a service technician, please make sure they are available on the date and time you choose.
* If you are scheduling a maintenance visit for a date in the future, please ensure that the service technician is also available on that date.
* Be sure to proofread your form before submitting it for accuracy and completeness.
