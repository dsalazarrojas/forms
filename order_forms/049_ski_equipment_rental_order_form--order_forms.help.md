# ski_equipment_rental_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ski equipment rental order form is used to collect customer information and preferences for renting ski equipment. This form is designed to ensure accurate and efficient processing of rental requests. To fill out this form, please follow the guidelines below.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the equipment type you would like to rent (Snowboards, Skis, Snowshoes, or Other).
2.  Choose the size of the equipment you need (Small, Medium, Large, or X-Large).
3.  Select the duration of your rental (1 day, 2 days, 3 days, or 4 days).
4.  Choose a pickup time for the equipment.
5.  Select a delivery date for the equipment.
6.  Provide your contact information.
7.  Enter your email address.
8.  If necessary, add any additional comments about your rental.
9.  Select the payment method for your order (Cash, Credit, Debit, or Other).
10. Enter the payment amount.
11. Repeat steps 9 and 10 for multiple payment methods if needed.
12. Review and submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Equipment Type** (`Equipment Type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of equipment you need to rent, such as Snowboards, Skis, Snowshoes, or Other.
*   **Equipment Size** (`Equipment Size`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the size of the equipment you need, such as Small, Medium, Large, or X-Large.
*   **Rental Duration** (`Rental Duration`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the duration of your rental, such as 1 day, 2 days, 3 days, or 4 days.
*   **Pickup Time** (`Pickup Time`, `date`, required/optional): Select the time you would like to pick up your rented equipment.
*   **Delivery Date** (`Delivery Date`, `date`, required/optional): Choose the date you would like to receive your rented equipment.
*   **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
*   **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
*   **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or special requests about your rental.
*   **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose how you would like to pay for your order, such as Cash, Credit, Debit, or Other.
*   **Payment Amount** (`Payment Amount`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the amount you will pay for your order.
*   **Payment Method 2** (`Payment Method 2`, `select_one`, required/optional): Repeat the payment method selection if you need multiple payment methods.
*   **Payment Amount 2** (`Payment Amount 2`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the amount for the second payment method.
*   **Payment Method 3** (`Payment Method 3`, `select_one`, required/optional): Repeat the payment method selection for the third payment method.
*   **Payment Amount 3** (`Payment Amount 3`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the amount for the third payment method.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
*   Double-check your order before submitting it to ensure everything is correct.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact our customer support team for assistance.
