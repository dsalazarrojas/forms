# Takeaway Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a straightforward way for customers to place takeaway orders with your restaurant. Please fill it out with your details and preferences to place your order for food to be picked up or delivered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and contact information.
2. Enter your address for delivery or pick-up.
3. Specify your food preference.
4. Add any additional order comments or instructions.
5. Select the type of order you would like to place (Order, Takeaway, Delivery).
6. Review and submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name** (Text, Optional): Please enter your first name.
* **last_name** (Text, Optional): Please enter your last name.
* **contact_number** (Text, Optional): Enter your contact number for communication.
* **address** (Text, Optional): Enter your address for delivery or pick-up.
* **delivery_time** (Time, Optional): Specify a time for delivery. Please format as a 24-hour clock (e.g. 13:00).
* **delivery_date** (Date, Optional): Specify a date for delivery. Please use the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **food_preference** (Text, Optional): Enter any specific food preferences or requests.
* **order_comments** (Note, Optional): Add any additional comments or instructions for your order.
* **email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address for communication.
* **order_details** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the type of order you would like to place:
	+ **Order**: Pickup in-store only.
	+ **Takeaway**: Pickup in-store, but not to be cooked or prepared by us.
	+ **Delivery**: Delivery to your specified address.
* **order_comments** is actually the field for "order_comments" and will be removed in the next revision.
	+ **order_details** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the type of order you would like to place.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your address and contact information is accurate to facilitate smooth order delivery or pick-up.
* If you select delivery, please ensure you have a valid email address for communication.
* Please use the 24-hour clock for the delivery time format.
* If you have specific requests or preferences, please include them in the food preference field.
