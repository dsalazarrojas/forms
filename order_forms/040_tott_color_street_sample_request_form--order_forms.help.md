# tott_color_street_sample_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "tott_color_street_sample_request_form" is a form used to request samples of different colors for street lighting purposes. The form collects information from customers, including their contact information, sample color selection, and order details. This guide will walk you through the process of filling out this form.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer information, including your name, address, and contact email and phone number.
2. Select the sample color you would like to request.
3. Choose the quantity of samples you need.
4. Fill in any additional comments or notes you would like to include.
5. If you have any specific notes or requests for the customer, please enter them in the "Customer Notes" field.
6. Finally, review your information and submit the form.
