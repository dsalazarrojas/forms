# Material Handling Equipment Vendor Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather contact information and product details from vendors of material handling equipment. It will help us connect with vendors and facilitate communication for potential business opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the vendor's information on the "Vendor Information" page.
2. Provide details about the product you are offering on the "Product Information" page.
3. Enter your contact information on the "Contact Information" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Information (Page 1)** (`text`, optional): This page is where you will enter your vendor's name, address, and other identifying details. Please provide as much information as possible to help us get in touch with you.
* **Product Information (Page 2)** (`text`, optional): This page is where you will describe your product, including its features, benefits, and any relevant details that make it unique. Be as detailed as possible to help us understand your offering.
* **Contact Information (Page 3)** (`text`, optional): This page is where you will enter your name, phone number, and email address so we can get in touch with you about your product. Make sure to enter accurate information to ensure we can effectively communicate.

## Tips

* Please provide clear and concise information about your product and contact details.
* If you have any supporting documents or attachments, you can attach them to this form.
* If you have any questions or need help with this form, please don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
