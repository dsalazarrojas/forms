# Graduation Apparel Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for ordering graduation apparel for students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's information, including student name and student grade level.
2. Select the graduation date for the order.
3. Choose the type of graduation apparel to order.
4. Select the color and size of the graduation apparel.
5. Provide any additional order details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Enter the student's name as it appears on their diploma.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the student's name as it is preferred to be displayed on the graduation apparel.
* **Student Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, `number`, optional): Enter the student's grade level (e.g. 8th, 10th, etc.).
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the student's school.
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the graduation.
* **Graduation Date Alt** (`graduation_date_alt`, `date`, optional): Enter the alternate graduation date.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, `text`, optional): Enter any special instructions for the order.
* **Graduation Apparel Type** (`graduation_apparel_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of graduation apparel to order, such as a cap and gown or a hood.
* **Graduation Apparel Color** (`graduation_apparel_color`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the colors for the graduation apparel.
* **Graduation Apparel Size** (`graduation_apparel_size`, `text`, optional): Enter the size of the graduation apparel.
* **Size 1** (`graduation_apparel_size_1`, `number`, optional): Enter the first size of the graduation apparel.
* **Size 1 Alt** (`graduation_apparel_size_1_alt`, `number`, optional): Enter the alternate size of the graduation apparel.
* **Size 2** (`graduation_apparel_size_2`, `number`, optional): Enter the second size of the graduation apparel.
* **Size 2 Alt** (`graduation_apparel_size_2_alt`, `number`, optional): Enter the alternate size of the graduation apparel.
* **Size 3** (`graduation_apparel_size_3`, `number`, optional): Enter the third size of the graduation apparel.
* **Size 3 Alt** (`graduation_apparel_size_3_alt`, `number`, optional): Enter the alternate size of the graduation apparel.
* **Size 4** (`graduation_apparel_size_4`, `number`, optional): Enter the fourth size of the graduation apparel.
* **Size 4 Alt** (`graduation_apparel_size_4_alt`, `number`, optional): Enter the alternate size of the graduation apparel.
* **Order Details 1** (`order_details_1`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional order details.
* **Order Details 1-1** (`order_details_1_1`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional order details.
* **Order Details 1-2** (`order_details_1_2`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional order details.

Note that some fields may be optional, depending on the specific needs of the order. Make sure to fill in all required fields and provide any necessary additional details for the order.
