# Charity Merchandise Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Charity Merchandise Payment Form is designed for customers to order merchandise from a charity. This form gathers contact information, merchandise details, and payment details in a clear and concise manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Email Address
	* Phone Number
	* Mailing Address
	* City
	* State
	* ZIP Code
2. Select your merchandise item:
	* Choose the item you want to purchase from the list
3. Specify order details:
	* Select your size and color preference
	* Choose the quantity you want to order
	* Make sure you've entered the unit price correctly
4. Review and finalize your order:
	* Calculate the subtotal based on the selected item and quantity
	* Select your shipping method
	* Enter the shipping cost
	* Add a donation to your order if you wish to support the charity
	* Confirm your payment method

## Field-by-Field Explanation
Here's a detailed explanation of each field:

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name for contact purposes.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name for contact purposes.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter your email address for contact and order notifications.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number for contact and order status updates.
* **Mailing Address** (Text, Required): Enter your full mailing address for delivery purposes.
* **City** (Text, Required): Enter your city for delivery purposes.
* **State** (Text, Required): Enter your state for delivery purposes.
* **ZIP Code** (Text, Required): Enter your ZIP code for delivery purposes.
* **Select Merchandise Item** (Select One, Required): Choose the item you want to purchase from the list.
* **Size** (Select One, Required): Select your preferred size for the merchandise item.
* **Color** (Select One, Required): Select your preferred color for the merchandise item.
* **Quantity** (Number, Required): Enter the number of items you want to order.
* **Unit Price** (Number, Required): Ensure you've entered the correct unit price for the merchandise item.
* **Subtotal** (Number, Required): Review the subtotal based on the selected item and quantity.
* **Shipping Method** (Select One, Required): Choose your shipping method, such as standard or express delivery.
* **Shipping Cost** (Number, Required): Enter the shipping cost for your order.
* **Add a Donation** (Select One, Optional): Add a donation to support the charity if you wish.
* **Total Amount** (Number, Required): Review the total amount including the donation, if any.
* **Payment Method** (Select One, Required): Choose how you want to pay for your order, such as credit or debit card.
* **Gift Message** (Text, Optional): If this is a gift, enter a message to be included with the order.
* **Special Instructions** (Text, Optional): Enter any special instructions or notes for your order.

## Tips
- Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
- Make sure to select the correct merchandise item and size.
- Review your order details carefully to avoid errors.
- Ensure you've entered the correct unit price and subtotal.
- Confirm your shipping method and cost.
- If you're adding a donation, review the total amount.
- Choose your payment method carefully.
- Review your order before submitting it.
