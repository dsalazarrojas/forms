# Glove Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Glove Order Form is a tool used to collect information from suppliers for order purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To use the form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about the supplier before starting the form.
2. Select the supplier from the available options (if applicable).
3. Fill in the supplier's name.
4. Choose the type of glove you wish to order (Leather, Synthetic, Latex, or Others).
5. Select the color of the glove you wish to order (Black, White, Red, or Blue).
6. Enter the quantity of gloves you wish to order.
7. Enter the date and time you wish to have the order ready (in date and time format, respectively).
8. Provide any additional comments or notes about the order (optional).
9. Enter the supplier's email address (if you have it).
10. Enter the supplier's phone number (if you have it).
11. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form and submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Id** (`supplier_id`, number, optional): Enter the supplier's ID number if you have it.
* **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, text, optional): Enter the supplier's name as they are known to you.
* **Glove Type** (`glove_type`, select multiple, optional): Choose one or more types of gloves you wish to order (Leather, Synthetic, Latex, or Others).
* **Glove Color** (`glove_color`, select one, optional): Select the color of the glove you wish to order (Black, White, Red, or Blue).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of gloves you wish to order.
* **Date Ordered** (`date_ordered`, date, optional): Enter the date you wish to have the order ready (in date format, YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Time Ordered** (`time_ordered`, time, optional): Enter the time you wish to have the order ready (in time format, HH:MM).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about the order.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the supplier's email address (if you have it).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the supplier's phone number (if you have it).
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to complete and submit your order.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have all the necessary information about the supplier before starting the form.
* Be precise when entering dates and times to avoid any confusion.
