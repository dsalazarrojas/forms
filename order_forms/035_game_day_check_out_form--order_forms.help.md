# Game Day Check Out Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Game Day Check Out Form is used to gather important information about an individual's Game Day experience. It's designed to help staff members understand the attendee's needs and requirements during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in your personal details in the User Information section.
* Answer the questions in the Attendee Details section about your attendance and any medical concerns.
* Provide contact information and emergency contact details.
* Answer questions about your Game Day experience, including any emergency services you may need and any first aid concerns.
* Select the date and time of the Game Day.
* Provide the location of the event.
