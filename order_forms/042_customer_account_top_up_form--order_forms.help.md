# Customer Account Top Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for topping up a customer's account from internal staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the customer's information to understand the changes required to be made to their account.
2. Fill in the relevant details in the form, following the format as shown.
3. Ensure all fields are filled in correctly and accurately.
4. Verify the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_account_top_up_form_1** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief explanation for the account top up.
* **customer_account_top_up_form_2** (Text, Optional): Provide the reason for the account top up.
* **customer_account_top_up_form_3** (Text, Optional): Enter the current credit balance on the customer's account.
* **customer_account_top_up_form_4** (Text, Optional): Enter the desired new credit balance for the customer.
* **customer_account_top_up_form_5** (Text, Optional): Enter any relevant customer details that need to be noted.
* **customer_account_top_up_form_6** (Text, Optional): Enter any further notes or comments about the customer's account.
* **customer_account_top_up_form_7** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional information to update the customer's account.
* **customer_account_top_up_form_8** (Text, Optional): Enter any specific instructions for updating the customer's account.
* **customer_account_top_up_form_9** (Text, Optional): Enter any other details that need to be updated on the customer's account.
* **customer_account_top_up_form_10** (Text, Optional): Enter the current status of the customer's account.
* **customer_account_top_up_form_11** (Text, Optional): Enter the desired status of the customer's account after the top up.
* **customer_account_top_up_form_12** (Text, Optional): Enter any notes about the customer's account activity.
* **customer_account_top_up_form_13** (Text, Optional): Enter any relevant account information that needs to be updated.
* **customer_account_top_up_form_14** (Text, Optional): Enter the customer's account type.
* **customer_account_top_up_form_15** (Text, Optional): Enter any other relevant account details.
* **customer_account_top_up_form_16** (Text, Optional): Enter any further notes or comments about the customer's account.
* **customer_account_top_up_form_17** (Text, Optional): Enter the customer's account limit.
* **customer_account_top_up_form_18** (Text, Optional): Enter any other account details that need to be updated.
* **customer_account_top_up_form_19** (Text, Optional): Enter any specific instructions for updating the customer's account limit.
* **customer_account_top_up_form_20** (Text, Optional): Enter any other relevant account information.
* **customer_account_top_up_form_21** (Text, Optional): Enter any notes about the customer's payment history.
* **customer_account_top_up_form_22** (Text, Optional): Enter the customer's payment method.
* **customer_account_top_up_form_23** (Text, Optional): Enter any other account details that need to be updated.
* **customer_account_top_up_form_24** (Text, Optional): Enter any final notes or comments about the customer's account.
* **customer_account_top_up_form_25** (Text, Optional): Enter any other relevant account information.
