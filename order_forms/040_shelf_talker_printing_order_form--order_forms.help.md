# Shelf Talker Printing Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers place an order for printing services. It is meant to be filled out by customers to provide their printing needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Shelf Talker Order" page with your order details, including any specific requirements and requests.
2. Upload your artwork files to the "Artwork Files" page.
3. Provide your delivery details on the "Delivery Details" page.
4. Make the payment on the "Payment" page.
5. Review and confirm your order on the "Order Confirmation" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shelf Talker Order**: This page requests the essential information for your order, such as any specific requirements and requests. (Required: False)
* **Artwork Files**: Upload your artwork files to this page. This should be a clear and concise file format to ensure smooth processing. (Required: False)
* **Delivery Details**: Enter your delivery details, including date and time of delivery if applicable. (Required: False)
* **Payment**: Enter your payment information in this page. Ensure it is accurate to avoid issues with payment processing. (Required: False)
* **Confirm**: Confirm your order details before proceeding to the next step.
* **Order Confirmation**: Review and confirm your order details before submission.
* **Cancel**: Cancel your order if you need to make any changes.
* **Error**: This field is not meant to be filled out by the user. It is used internally to display any errors that may occur during the submission process.
* **Success**: This field is also not meant to be filled out by the user. It is used internally to display a success message after submission.
* **Error Details**: This field is not meant to be filled out by the user. It is used to display any error messages that may occur during the submission process.
