# Gym Custom Wall Mirror Installation Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you place an order for a custom wall mirror installation. It gathers necessary information for your mirror order, including location, mirror specifications, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the fields provided.
2. Select the desired location where the mirror will be installed.
3. Choose the size, material, type, and mounting method of the mirror.
4. Select the mirror's surface finish and color.
5. Choose the desired shipping method and delivery date for your mirror.
6. Enter any additional order notes or instructions.
7. Enter the price and payment method for your order.
