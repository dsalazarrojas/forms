# Food Presentation Plate Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Food Presentation Plate Order Form is designed for clients to provide their order preferences and food item choices for their custom plate creation. This form helps us better understand their needs and preferences to create a personalized dish that will delight their palate.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, select the "Order preferences" page by clicking on the "Next" button at the bottom of the page.
2. On this page, you can choose your preferences from the "Food items" section. Select all the food items you would like to have on your plate.
3. In the "Quantities" section, specify the quantity of each food item you selected in step 2.
4. Please enter your "Client name" in the text field provided.
5. Optionally, you can add a hint to your "Client name" in the text field provided.
6. Enter your "Email" address in the text field provided.
7. Review your order and submit it by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order preferences** (`order_preferences`, note, not required): This section is where you can provide any special requests or comments about your order. Please feel free to add any notes or instructions that will help us create the perfect plate for you.
* **Food items** (`food_items`, select multiple, not required): Select all the food items you would like to have on your plate. You can choose from the list of available options provided.
* **Quantities** (`quantities`, select one, not required): For each food item you selected, please specify the quantity you would like to have on your plate.
* **Client name** (`client_name`, text, not required): Please enter your name as it will be listed on your order receipt.
* **Client name hint** (`client_name_hint`, text, not required): This is an optional field where you can add a brief hint or note about your name.
* **Email** (`client_email`, email, not required): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your order.
