# Gourmet Market Festival Order Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an order for the Gourmet Market Festival. Please provide the required information to ensure a smooth ordering process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the first page with your order preferences (pickup or delivery) and basic information.
- Move to the second page and enter the event details (date, time, location, etc.).
- Fill out the vendor information (vendor name, email, phone, address, etc.).
- Optionally provide additional notes if needed.
- Confirm that the order is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pickup or Delivery? (1)**: Choose whether you would like to pick up or have your order delivered.
* **Scheduled Fulfillment (2)**: Enter the event date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* *Name (3)*: Enter the name of the person or organization placing the order.
* *Vendor (4)*: Enter the name of the vendor providing the services or products for this order.
* *Contact (5)*: Enter any additional contact information for the vendor or person placing the order.
* *Order Details (6)*: Enter any additional details about the order if needed.
* *Email (7)*: Enter the email address of the person or organization placing the order.
* *Phone (8)*: Enter the phone number of the person or organization placing the order.
* *Quantity (9)*: Enter the quantity of the order.
* *Price (10)*: Enter the price of the order (not required but recommended for reference).
* *Pickup Address (11)*: Enter the address where the order will be picked up.
* *Delivery Address (12)*: Enter the address where the order will be delivered.
* *Notes (13)*: Enter any additional notes or comments about the order.
* *Event Date (14)*: Enter the date of the event in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* *Event Time (15)*: Enter the time of the event in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* *Event Location (16)*: Enter the location of the event.
* *Organizer (17)*: Enter the name of the person or organization organizing the event.
* *Organizer Email (18)*: Enter the email address of the event organizer.
* *Organizer Phone (19)*: Enter the phone number of the event organizer.
* *Vendor Email (20)*: Enter the email address of the vendor.
* *Vendor Phone (21)*: Enter the phone number of the vendor.
* *Vendor Address (22)*: Enter the address of the vendor.
* *Vendor Notes (23)*: Enter any additional notes or comments about the vendor.
* *Customer Notes (24)*: Enter any additional notes or comments from the customer.
* *Confirm (25)*: Confirm that the order is correct and complete.
