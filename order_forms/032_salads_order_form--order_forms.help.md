# Salads Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to place salad orders. It is used to collect order details, customer information, and order status updates.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field (ID: 2).
2. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (ID: 3).
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field (ID: 4).
4. Select your order type from the options (ID: 5).
5. Choose a date for your order (ID: 6).
6. Select a time for your order (ID: 7).
7. Specify the quantity of salads you would like to order (ID: 8).
8. Enter the total price of your order (ID: 20).
9. Add any additional notes or comments (ID: 23).
10. If you'd like to assign a tool to assist with your order, select it from the options (ID: 14).
11. Confirm that the form has been submitted (ID: 16).
12. Review your order details and status (ID: 12, 17, 19).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header** (`form_header`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your order (ID: 1).
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name (ID: 2).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (ID: 3).
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (ID: 4).
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of order you'd like to place (ID: 5).
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Choose a date for your order (ID: 6).
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Choose a time for your order (ID: 7).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of salads you'd like to order (ID: 8).
* **Total** (`total`, number, optional): Enter the total price of your order (ID: 20).
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments (ID: 23).
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your order (ID: 11).
* **Order Confirmation** (`order_confirmation`, text, optional): Confirm that you have submitted the form (ID: 12).
* **Order Summary** (`order_summary`, text, optional): Review a summary of your order (ID: 13).
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_multiple, optional): If you'd like to assign a tool to assist with your order (ID: 14).
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the form (ID: 15).
* **Form Submitted** (`form_submitted`, select_one, optional): Confirm that the form has been submitted (ID: 16).
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_one, optional): Check the status of your order (ID: 17).
* **Customer ID** (`customer_id`, text, optional): Enter your customer ID (ID: 18).
* **Order ID** (`order_id`, text, optional): Enter your order ID (ID: 19).
* **Total Price** (`total_price`, number, optional): Enter the total price of your order (ID: 20).
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Choose a date for your order (ID: 21).
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Choose a time for your order (ID: 22).
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments (ID: 23).
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, optional): Enter the name of the user assigned to assist with your order (ID: 24).
* **Form Completed** (`form_completed`, text, optional): Confirm that the form has been completed (ID: 25).
