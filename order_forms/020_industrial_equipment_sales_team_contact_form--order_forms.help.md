# industrial_equipment_sales_team_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The industrial equipment sales team contact form allows users to contact the sales team with inquiries related to industrial equipment sales.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
2. Select the type of inquiry you have, such as Quote, Order, or Support, from the "Inquiry Type" dropdown menu.
3. Choose the sales team member(s) you would like to contact from the "Sales Team" dropdown menu.
4. Enter your email address and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone Number" fields, respectively.
5. Describe your inquiry in the "Message" field.
6. If applicable, select your company size and industry from the "Company Size" and "Industry" dropdown menus.
7. If applicable, select the equipment you are interested in from the "Equipment Requested" dropdown menu.
8. Finally, enter your name and email address in the "Contact Requester Name" and "Contact Requester Email" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information**: This field is used to enter your name and contact information.
* **Inquiry Type**: Select the type of inquiry you have, such as Quote, Order, or Support.
* **Sales Team**: Choose the sales team member(s) you would like to contact.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter a valid phone number.
* **Message**: Describe your inquiry in this field.
* **Company Name**: Enter your company name.
* **Company Size**: Select your company size from the dropdown menu.
* **Industry**: Select your industry from the dropdown menu.
* **Equipment Requested**: Select the equipment you are interested in.
* **Contact Requester Name**: Enter your name.
* **Contact Requester Email**: Enter your email address.
