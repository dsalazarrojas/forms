# Organic Pet Food Pre-Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organic Pet Food Pre-Order Form is designed to collect necessary information from pet owners to place a pre-order for organic pet food. This form helps us understand your pet's needs and preferences to provide the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select your pet type (Fish, Cat, or Dog).
2. Enter your contact information (Email, Phone, or Address).
3. Choose your preferred delivery date.
4. Select your preferred delivery time.
5. Choose your payment method (Online Payment or Offline Payment).
6. Add any order comments or notes (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Pet Food Pre-Order** (`pet_food_pre_order`): Enter a brief description of your pre-order request.
- **Pet Details** (`pet_details`): Select your pet type (Fish, Cat, or Dog).
- **Owner Details** (`owner_details`): Enter your name and any additional information about yourself (optional).
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): Select your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, or Address).
- **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`): Enter the date you would like to receive your order.
- **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`): Select your preferred delivery time.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Choose how you would like to make payment (Online Payment or Offline Payment).
- **Order Comments** (`order_comments`): Add any additional comments about your order (optional).
- **Order Status** (`order_status`): This field will be updated automatically after order processing.
- **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
- **Address** (`address`): Enter your address.
- **Pet Type** (`pet_type`): Select your pet type (Fish, Cat, or Dog).
- **Pet Size** (`pet_size`): Select the size of your pet (Small, Medium, Large).
- **Pet Breed** (`pet_breed`): Enter your pet's breed (optional).
- **Pet Age** (`pet_age`): Enter your pet's age.
- **Order Frequency** (`order_frequency`): Select how often you would like to receive orders (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
- **Order Amount** (`order_amount`): Enter the quantity of food you would like to order.
- **Delivery Instructions** (`delivery_instructions`): Add any specific delivery instructions (optional).
- **Order Notes** (`order_notes`): Add any additional notes about your order (optional).
- **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`): Enter any additional information about yourself (optional).
- **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`): Choose how you would like to receive your order (Pickup, Delivery, Shipping).
- **Payment Status** (`payment_status`): This field will be updated automatically after payment processing.
- **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional comments (optional).
- **Created At** (`created_at`): This field will be updated automatically after form submission.

Tips:
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure efficient order processing.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for support.
- We will update you on your order status after processing.
