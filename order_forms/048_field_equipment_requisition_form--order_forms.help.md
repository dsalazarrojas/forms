# Field Equipment Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Field Equipment Requisition Form is used to request field equipment for work or tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Business Unit" field with the department or team requesting the equipment.
2. In the "Item Requested" field, enter a clear description of the equipment needed.
3. In the "Description" field, provide any additional information about the equipment or the reason for the request.
4. In the "Requested By" field, enter your name and/or title to associate the request with your identity.
5. In the "Status" field, select the current status of the request (Active or Inactive).
6. In the "Equipment Type" field, enter the type of equipment being requested.
7. In the "Quantity Requested" field, enter the number of units of equipment needed.
8. In the "Priority" field, indicate if the request has high priority ("Yes" or "No").
9. In the "Delivery Date" field, select the date the equipment is expected to be delivered.
10. In the "Delivery Time" field, select the estimated time of delivery.
11. In the "Comments" field, enter any additional comments or notes about the request.
12. In the "Assigned To" field, enter the name of the person or team responsible for fulfilling the request.
13. In the "Assigned Date" field, select the date the request was assigned to someone.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Unit** (1, text, required): Enter the department or team requesting the equipment.
* **Item Requested** (2, text, required): Enter a clear description of the equipment needed.
* **Description** (3, text, required): Provide any additional information about the equipment or the reason for the request.
* **Requested By** (4, text, required): Enter your name and/or title to associate the request with your identity.
* **Status** (5, select_one, required): Select the current status of the request (Active or Inactive).
* **Equipment Type** (6, text, required): Enter the type of equipment being requested.
* **Quantity Requested** (7, number, required): Enter the number of units of equipment needed.
* **Priority** (8, select_one, required): Indicate if the request has high priority ("Yes" or "No").
* **Delivery Date** (9, date, required): Select the date the equipment is expected to be delivered.
* **Delivery Time** (10, time, required): Select the estimated time of delivery.
* **Comments** (11, text, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about the request.
* **Assigned To** (12, text, required): Enter the name of the person or team responsible for fulfilling the request.
* **Assigned Date** (13, date, required): Select the date the request was assigned to someone.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Review your request carefully to ensure all information is accurate and complete.
* If you are not sure about the status or priority of your request, consult with your team or manager for guidance.
