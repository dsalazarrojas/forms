# Factory Air Filter Cartridge Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for ordering factory air filter cartridges. It guides you through the process of providing necessary details for a seamless ordering experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Order Form** section with your order details.
2. Provide your **Customer Info**, including any relevant contact information.
3. Enter your **Contact Info**, which will be used for communication and updates.
4. List your **Order Items**, specifying the products you want to order.
5. Fill out the **Shipping Info** section, including address and delivery details.
6. Enter your **Payment Info** to complete the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Form**: Fill out this section with the specifics of your order, including product codes or descriptions.
* **Customer Info**: Enter your business name, contact person, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Contact Info**: Provide your address, phone number, and any other contact details for communication and updates.
* **Order Items**: List the products you want to order, including quantities and any special instructions.
* **Shipping Info**: Enter your shipping address, delivery date, and any special delivery instructions.
* **Payment Info**: Enter your payment method, amount, and any other payment details.
* **Confirmation**: Review your order details for accuracy before submitting.
* **Order Total**: This field will display the total cost of your order once all information is filled out.
