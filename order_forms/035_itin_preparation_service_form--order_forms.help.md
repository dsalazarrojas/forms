# ITIN Preparation Service Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ITIN Preparation Service Form is used to collect information from customers who are applying for an Individual Taxpayer Identification Number (ITIN) through our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Enter your business details, if applicable, including the business name and address.
3. Select your Date of Birth and TIN Status.
4. Indicate the reason for your ITIN application or inactivation.
5. Fill out any additional required fields and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (text): This field is used to enter your personal name.
* **Client Email** (email): This field is used to enter your email address.
* **Client Phone** (text): This field is used to enter your phone number.
* **Business Name** (text): This field is used to enter the name of your business, if applicable.
* **Business Name Control** (select_one): This field is used to indicate whether the business name is a control (e.g., a trust, partnership, or sole proprietorship).
* **Client Address** (text): This field is used to enter your address.
* **Business Address** (text): This field is used to enter your business address, if applicable.
* **Business Phone** (text): This field is used to enter your business phone number, if applicable.
* **Date of Birth** (date): This field is used to enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Date of Birth Control** (select_multiple): This field is used to select multiple dates of birth, if applicable.
* **TIN Status** (select_one): This field is used to indicate the status of your TIN (Active, Invalid, Not Active, or None).
* **TIN Number** (number): This field is used to enter your TIN number, if applicable.
* **Reason for Application** (select_multiple): This field is used to indicate the reason for your ITIN application.
* **Reason for Inactivation** (select_one): This field is used to indicate the reason for inactivation of your TIN.
* **ITIN Application Date** (date): This field is used to enter the date of your ITIN application.
* **ITIN Preparation Date** (date): This field is used to enter the date when the ITIN preparation service is requested.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have filled out all required fields accurately to avoid delays in processing your application.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
