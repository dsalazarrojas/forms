# photo_album_printing_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The photo_album_printing_order_form is a user-facing form that collects information for ordering a photo album.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. The form is divided into several sections. Please fill out each section according to the instructions below.
2. Please fill in your photo album preferences in the "album_preferences" section by selecting your desired album type, size, and shape.
3. In the "image_uploader" section, provide the URL of the image you want to upload.
4. In the "image_list" section, select the image you want to print from the list.
5. In the "payment_details" section, provide your payment information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Album Preferences (album_preferences)**: Choose the type, size, and shape of your album from the drop-down menu. 
* **Image Uploader (image_uploader)**: Enter the URL of the image you want to print. 
* **Image List (image_list)**: Select one of the images listed from the drop-down menu.
* **Payment Details (payment_details)**: Enter your payment information, including [insert payment details, e.g., credit card number, expiration date]. 
* **Form Footer (form_footer)**: This field is currently not used in the current form configuration. 

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank, but please be aware that this may affect the processing of your order. 
* If you're unsure about any field, you can refer back to this guide for clarification. 
* Please review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure that you have provided all necessary information.
