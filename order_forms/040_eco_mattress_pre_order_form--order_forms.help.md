# Eco Mattress Pre Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Eco Mattress Pre Order Form is a step-by-step form designed to collect information from customers to reserve a mattress before it is available for purchase. This form will collect product selection, shipping information, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Select Your Eco Mattress**: Choose the type of Eco Mattress you would like to pre-order. Please select from the options provided.
2.  **Enter Shipping Address**: Enter your shipping address in the text field.
3.  **Enter City**: Enter your city in the text field.
4.  **Enter Zip Code**: Enter your 5 or 6 digit zip code in the text field.
5.  **Enter Customer Name**: Enter your name as it should be displayed on the order.
6.  **Enter Email Address**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
7.  **Enter Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
8.  **Update Message**: If you have any additional comments or questions, please enter them in this text field.

* **Product Selection (select_one)**: A dropdown menu where you select the type of Eco Mattress you would like to pre-order.
* **Shipping Address (text)**: A text field where you enter your shipping address.
* **City (text)**: A text field where you enter your city.
* **Zip (text)**: A text field where you enter your 5 or 6 digit zip code.
* **Customer Name (text)**: A text field where you enter your name as it should be displayed on the order.

* **Customer Phone (text)**: A text field where you enter your phone number where you can be reached.
