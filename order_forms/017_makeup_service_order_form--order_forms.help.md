# makeup_service_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for booking a makeup service for a specific event or appointment. It collects essential information to ensure that your makeup service request is processed correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Contact Information**: Provide your basic contact details to help us process your order efficiently.
2. **Enter Event Details**: Please provide information about the event or appointment you are booking the makeup service for.
3. **Choose Your Makeup Preferences**: Select your preferred type of makeup service (Airbrushed, Watercolor, Gel Polish, or Mineral).
4. **List Any Allergies**: If you have any allergies or sensitivities, please list them here.
5. **Enter Order Details**: Provide a brief description of the service you are booking.
6. **Select Service Location**: Choose the location where the service will be provided.
7. **Choose Service Date and Time**: Select the date and time for the service appointment.
8. **Confirm Service Start and End Time**: Double-check your service start and end times to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information**: Enter your name and contact information, including your phone number and email. This helps us get in touch with you if there are any last-minute changes or updates.
* **Event Information**: Please provide the event name, date, and venue (if applicable). This helps us understand the context of your request.
* **Makeup Preferences**: Select your preferred type of makeup service from the options provided. This ensures that we can tailor the service to your needs.
* **Allergies Information**: If you have any allergies or sensitivities, please list them here. This helps us take necessary precautions to ensure your safety and comfort.
* **Order Details**: Provide a brief description of the service you are booking, including any specific requirements or requests you may have.
* **Service Location**: Choose the location where the service will be provided.
* **Service Date**: Select the date for the service appointment.
* **Service Start Time**: Select the start time for the service appointment.
* **Service End Time**: Select the end time for the service appointment.
* **Notes**: If you have any additional comments or requirements, please enter them here. This helps us provide the best possible service.
