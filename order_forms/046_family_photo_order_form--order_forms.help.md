# Family Photo Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers ordering family photographs. It gathers essential details such as customer and product information, allowing you to efficiently process orders and provide exceptional customer service.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Family Photo Order Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Order Form** section, providing any desired comments or special requests for your order.
2. In the **Customer Info** section, enter your name and contact information so we can get in touch with you to confirm your order.
3. In the **Product Info** section, enter the product details such as type, size, or any other relevant information you want to include.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Form (order_form)** (Text, Optional): This is the main text field for customers to provide any additional comments or special requests they may have for their order.
* **Customer Info (customer_info)** (Text, Optional): Enter your name and contact information so we can get in touch with you to confirm your order. This is crucial for us to process your order efficiently.
* **Product Info (product_info)** (Text, Optional): Enter details about the product you are ordering, such as its type, size, or any other relevant information you'd like to provide.

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide accurate contact information in the **Customer Info** field so we can reach out to you to confirm your order.
* If you have any specific requirements or requests for your order, please mention them in the **Order Form** field.
