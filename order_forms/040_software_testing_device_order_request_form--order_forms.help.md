# Software Testing Device Order Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting software testing devices for internal use within our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the submit date and time when you would like to submit the request.
2. Enter the name of the device you are requesting.
3. Enter the testing group or department that will be using the device.
4. Enter your name and contact information so we can get in touch with you about your request.
5. Enter the department and title of the person requesting the device.
6. If you would like to ship the device to someone else, select the ship method and provide the necessary address information.
7. Specify the priority level of your request (e.g. Low, Medium, High, or Critical).
8. If you have any additional requests or comments, enter them in the reason field.
9. Finally, review and submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submit Date** (`form_submit_date`): The date and time when you would like to submit your request.
* **Device Name** (`form_device_name`): The name of the device you are requesting.
* **Testing Group** (`form_testing_group`): The department or team that will be using the device.
* **Requester** (`form_requester`): Your name and contact information so we can get in touch with you about your request.
* **Department** (`form_department`): The department or team of the person requesting the device.
* **Device ID** (`form_device_id`): An optional field for tracking and identifying the device.
* **Reason** (`form_reason`): If you have any additional requests or comments, enter them here.
* **Ship To** (`form_ship_to`): Select yes if you would like to ship the device to someone else. If you select yes, provide the necessary address information below.
* **Ship Method** (`form_ship_method`): If you selected "Yes" to ship to someone else, select the preferred shipping method.
* **Ship Address** (`form_ship_address`): The address where the device will be shipped.
* **Ship City** (`form_ship_city`): The city where the device will be shipped.
* **Ship State** (`form_ship_state`): The state where the device will be shipped.
* **Ship Zip** (`form_ship_zip`): The zip code where the device will be shipped.
* **Ship Country** (`form_ship_country`): The country where the device will be shipped.
* **Ship Contact** (`form_ship_contact`): The contact person's name and phone number.
* **Ship Email** (`form_ship_email`): The contact person's email.
* **Ship Phone** (`form_ship_phone`): The contact person's phone number.
* **Requester Email** (`form_requester_email`): Your email address.
* **Requester Phone** (`form_requester_phone`): Your phone number.
* **Requester Department** (`form_requester_department`): Your department or team.
* **Requester Title** (`form_requester_title`): Your title or position.
* **Priority** (`form_priority`): The priority level of your request (e.g. Low, Medium, High, or Critical).

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your request is processed correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We will review and process your request as soon as possible.
