# shelf_label_adhesive_refill_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the ordering of shelf label refills. It gathers necessary information for the order to be processed and ensures accurate communication with the customer regarding their order status and any potential issues that may arise during the refill process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Provide your order ID in the "order_id" field.
4. Enter your customer email in the "customer_email" field.
5. Enter your customer phone number in the "customer_phone" field.
6. Choose a date for the order in the "order_date" field.
7. Choose a time for the order in the "order_time" field.
8. Add any additional comments or notes regarding your order in the "order_notes" field.
9. Specify the location where you would like the order delivered in the "order_location" field.
10. Choose the type of adhesive to be used in the "adhesive_type" field.
11. Enter the shipping address in the "ship_address" field.
12. Enter the shipping city in the "ship_city" field.
13. Enter the shipping state or province in the "ship_state" field.
14. Enter the shipping zip code in the "ship_zip" field.
15. Enter the shipping phone number in the "ship_phone" field.
16. Enter the shipping email in the "ship_email" field.
17. Choose a review date for the order in the "review_date" field.
18. Choose a rating for the order in the "review_rating" field.
19. Provide any comments for the review in the "review_comments" field.
20. Choose the payment method for the order in the "payment_method" field.
21. Enter your payment card number in the "payment_card_number" field.
22. Enter the expiration date for your payment card in the "payment_exp_date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
- **Order ID** (`order_id`, `text`, required): Provide your unique order ID to help us track your order.
- **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached regarding your order status.
- **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where we can reach you regarding your order status.
- **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, required): Choose a date for the order delivery.
- **Order Time** (`order_time`, `time`, required): Choose a time for the order delivery.
- **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, `note`, required): Add any additional comments or notes regarding your order.
- **Order Location** (`order_location`, `text`, required): Specify the location where you would like the order delivered.
- **Adhesive Type** (`adhesive_type`, `text`, required): Choose the type of adhesive to be used in the refill.
- **Ship Address** (`ship_address`, `text`, required): Enter your shipping address to ensure accurate delivery.
- **Ship City** (`ship_city`, `text`, required): Enter the city where you would like the order shipped to.
- **Ship State** (`ship_state`, `text`, required): Enter the state or province where you would like the order shipped to.
- **Ship Zip** (`ship_zip`, `text`, required): Enter the zip or postal code for the shipping address.
- **Ship Phone** (`ship_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your shipping phone number to reach you.
- **Ship Email** (`ship_email`, `email`, required): Enter your shipping email address.
- **Review Date** (`review_date`, `date`, required): Choose a review date for the order.
- **Review Rating** (`review_rating`, `select_one`, required): Choose a rating for the order from the provided options.
- **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, `text`, required): Provide any comments for the review.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose a payment method from the provided options.
- **Payment Card Number** (`payment_card_number`, `text`, required): Enter your payment card number.
- **Payment Exp Date** (`payment_exp_date`, `date`, required): Enter the expiration date for your payment card.
