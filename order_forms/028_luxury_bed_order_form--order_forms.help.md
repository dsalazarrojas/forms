## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers for orders in an online store or e-commerce platform. Customers can use this form to provide their details, choose their preferred luxury bed and mattress, select their preferred color and price options, and specify their payment and delivery details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: 
   - Customer name and contact information (Customer Info)
   - Luxury bed and mattress preferences (Bed Info)
   - Payment method and delivery details (Payment Method and Delivery Info)
   - Optional fields can be filled in if applicable

2. Choose your preferred luxury bed and mattress by selecting the options in the "Bed Info" field.

3. Select your preferred color and price option by choosing from the available options in the "Color Option" and "Price Option" fields.

4. Specify your payment method and delivery details in the "Payment Method" field.

5. Enter your address and phone number in the "Address" and "Phone" fields for delivery purposes.

6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field for order tracking and communication.

7. Enter any additional notes in the "Note" field.
