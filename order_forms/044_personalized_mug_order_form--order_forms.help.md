# Personalized-Mug-Order-Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to order a personalized mug with your name and a custom message, as well as select a category that reflects your interests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the first name field with your given name.
2. Enter your last name in the second field.
3. Type in a custom message for your personalized mug in the third field.
4. Select a category for your mug from the options provided (Food and Beverage, Tech, or Sports).
5. Optionally, enter the product name if you want to specify it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name that you want to appear on your mug. 
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter the surname that you want to appear on your mug.
* **Message** (3, text, optional): Type in a custom message that you want to appear on your mug.
* **Category** (4, select_one, optional): Select a category that reflects your interests (Food and Beverage, Tech, or Sports).
* **Product Name** (5, text, optional): Enter the name of the product you want to order if you have specific requirements.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly to ensure successful order submission.
* You may leave the Product Name field empty if you don't want to specify the product.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact our customer support team for assistance.
