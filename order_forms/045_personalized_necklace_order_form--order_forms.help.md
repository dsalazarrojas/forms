# Personalized Necklace Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personalized Necklace Order Form is used to gather customer information and details for creating a customized necklace. This form is designed to be completed by the customer to ensure accurate and precise order fulfillment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, fill in the required fields:
	* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
	* **Customer Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for easy contact and follow-up communication.
	* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates and notifications.
	* **Order Name**: Enter a unique and descriptive name for your order.
	* **Order Description**: Optionally, provide a brief description of your order for our reference.
	* **Order Total**: Enter the total cost of your order.
	* **Chain Type**: Select the type of chain you prefer.
	* **Chain Material**: Select the materials for your chain.
	* **Chain Length**: Enter the length of the chain.
2. If you'd like to customize your order further, you can add additional details:
	* **Gemstone Type** (optional): Select or enter the type of gemstone you'd like to use.
	* **Chain Material 2** (optional): Select additional materials for your chain.
	* **Custom Text** (optional): Add any custom notes or instructions for your order.
3. To review and submit your form, click on the "Submit" button.
