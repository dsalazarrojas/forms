# Software Company Trade Show Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to manage trade show orders, track sales contacts, and schedule follow-up activities for potential customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the company name in the "Company" field.
2. Provide your name and title in the "Name" and "Job Title" fields.
3. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
4. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
5. Select the sales contact for the trade show order in the "Sales Contact" field.
6. Enter the sales email address in the "Sales Email" field.
7. Enter the sales phone number in the "Sales Phone" field.
8. Choose the product you are interested in from the "Product" dropdown menu.
9. Enter the quantity of the product you wish to order in the "Quantity" field.
10. Enter the unit price of the product in the "Unit Price" field.
11. Provide any additional order details in the "Order Details" field (optional).
12. Enter your company address in the "Company Address" field (optional).
13. Select the payment terms for the trade show order from the "Payment Terms" dropdown menu.
14. Add any notes about the order in the "Notes" field (optional).
15. Schedule a follow-up activity with the customer by entering a date in the "Follow up" field.
16. Choose the time for the follow-up activity in the "Follow up At" field.
17. Enter the name of the person to follow up with in the "Follow up Contact" field.
18. Enter the email address of the person to follow up with in the "Follow up Email" field.
19. Enter a message to be sent to the customer during the follow-up activity in the "Follow up Message" field.
20. Select the status of the follow-up activity from the "Follow up Status" dropdown menu.
21. Enter the date of the follow-up activity in the "Follow up Date" field.
22. Choose the time for the follow-up activity in the "Follow up Time" field.
23. Add any additional notes for the follow-up activity in the "Follow up Notes" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company**: Enter the name of the company you represent.
* **Name**: Enter your name.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Sales Contact**: Select the sales contact for the trade show order.
* **Sales Email**: Enter the email address of the sales contact.
* **Sales Phone**: Enter the phone number of the sales contact.
* **Product**: Choose the product you wish to order from the dropdown menu.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of units you wish to order.
* **Unit Price**: Enter the price per unit of the product.
* **Order Details**: Provide any additional details about the order (optional).
* **Company Address**: Enter your company address (optional).
* **Payment Terms**: Select the payment terms for the trade show order from the dropdown menu.
* **Notes**: Add any notes about the order (optional).
* **Follow up**: Schedule a follow-up activity with the customer by entering a date.
* **Follow up At**: Choose the time for the follow-up activity.
* **Follow up Contact**: Enter the name of the person to follow up with.
* **Follow up Email**: Enter the email address of the person to follow up with.
* **Follow up Message**: Enter a message to be sent to the customer during the follow-up activity.
* **Follow up Status**: Select the status of the follow-up activity from the dropdown menu.
* **Follow up Date**: Enter the date of the follow-up activity.
* **Follow up Time**: Choose the time for the follow-up activity.
* **Follow up Notes**: Add any additional notes for the follow-up activity (optional).

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure efficient order management.
* Review and proofread your form before submitting it to ensure it is complete and correct.
* If you have any questions or need assistance with the form, contact the sales team for support.
