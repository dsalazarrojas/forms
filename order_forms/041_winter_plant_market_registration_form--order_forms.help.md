# Winter Plant Market Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Winter Plant Market Registration Form is designed to collect information from customers who want to participate in the winter plant market. This form is used to gather essential details about the customers, such as their name, contact information, and product list.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your email address in the Email field.
3. Provide your phone number in the Phone field.
4. List the products you are interested in or offering in the Product List field.
5. Select all the types of products you have (e.g. plants, seeds, soil) in the Product Types field.
6. Add any additional notes or comments about yourself or your products in the Vendor Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Vendor Name** (text): Enter the name of your business or organization (if applicable).
* **Email** (email): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Product List** (text): List the products you are interested in or offering. This can include plants, seeds, soil, or other products.
* **Product Types** (select multiple):
	+ **Plants**: Check if you are offering plants for sale.
	+ **Seeds**: Check if you are offering seeds for sale.
	+ **Soil**: Check if you are offering soil for sale.
	+ **Other**: Check if you are offering other products.
* **Vendor Notes** (note): Add any additional information about yourself or your products.
