# Gym Poster And Banner Printing Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from customers for gym poster and banner printing orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Form Submission Details section with a unique id for each order submission.
2. In the Customer Info section, enter your name.
3. In the Order Details section, provide your order details.
4. In the Print Order section, specify your print order.
5. In the Upload Design Files section, upload your design files if needed.
6. In the Payment section, enter your payment information.
7. In the Order Review section, review your order details.
8. In the Confirm section, select your confirmation option.
9. In the Name section, enter your name.
10. In the Email section, enter your email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Submission Details** (`Form Submission Details`, text, required: false): This is the first section of the form where you need to enter a unique id for each order submission. Please make sure to enter a unique id for every order.
* **Customer Info** (`Customer Info`, text, required: false): Enter your name in this field. This will help us to contact you in case we need more information.
* **Order Details** (`Order Details`, text, required: false): In this section, please provide your order details. This can include any additional information you want to provide about your order.
* **Print Order** (`Print Order`, text, required: false): Specify your print order in this section.
* **Upload Design Files** (`Upload Design Files`, text, required: false): If you need to upload any design files, please do so in this section.
* **Payment** (`Payment`, text, required: false): Enter your payment information in this section.
* **Order Review** (`Order Review`, text, required: false): This is an opportunity to review your order details.
* **Confirm** (`Confirm`, select_one, required: false): Select your confirmation option here.
* **Name** (`Name`, text, required: false): Enter your name in this field.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address in this field.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all the necessary fields to ensure a smooth order process.
* If you need to upload any design files, please make sure they are in the correct format.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at contact@yourwebsite.com.
