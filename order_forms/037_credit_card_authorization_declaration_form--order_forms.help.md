# credit_card_authorization_declaration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The credit card authorization form is used to collect information from the customer for the order process.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
2.  Double-check the information before submitting to ensure accuracy.
3.  Review the fields provided for any additional information required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Name**: A required field that asks for the name of the customer.
    *   <small>Please enter the customer's name.</small>
*   **Customer Email**: A required field that asks for a valid email address for the customer.
    *   <small>Please enter a valid email address.</small>
*   **Customer Phone**: A required field that asks for the customer's phone number.
    *   <small>Please enter the customer's phone number.</small>
*   **Billing Address**: A required field that asks for the billing address for the order.
    *   <small>Please enter the billing address for the order.</small>
*   **Card Number**: A required field that asks for the credit card number without any spaces or dashes.
    *   <small>Please enter the credit card number without any spaces or dashes.</small>
*   **Security Code**: A required field that asks for the security code found on the back of the card.
    *   <small>Please enter the security code found on the back of your card.</small>
*   **Expiration Date**: A required field that asks for the expiration date of the credit card.
    *   <small>Please enter the expiration date of the credit card.</small>
*   **Order ID**: A required field that asks for the ID for the order.
    *   <small>Please enter the ID for the order.</small>
*   **Shipping Address**: An optional field that asks for the shipping address for the order.
    *   <small>Please enter the shipping address for the order, if applicable.</small>
*   **Credit Card Type**: A required field that asks for the type of credit card being used.
    *   <small>Please enter the type of credit card being used.</small>
*   **Order Total**: A required field that asks for the total amount of the order.
    *   <small>Please enter the total amount of the order.</small>
*   **Credit Card Card Verification Value**: A required field that asks for the credit card card verification value.
    *   <small>Please enter the credit card card verification value.</small>
*   **Order Status**: A required field that asks for the status of the order (Active or Inactive).
    *   <small>Please select the status of the order (Active or Inactive).</small>
*   **Customer Signature**: An optional field that asks for the customer's signature as a guarantee of the order details.
    *   <small>Please provide your signature as a guarantee of the order details.</small>
*   **Shipping Cost**: A required field that asks for the shipping cost for the order.
    *   <small>Please enter the shipping cost for the order.</small>
