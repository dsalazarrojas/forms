# Product Recall Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers to verify product recalls. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to help us investigate and resolve any issues with your product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Customer Name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the product you are inquiring about in the "Product Name" field.
3. Provide your Order ID in the "Order ID" field.
4. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
5. Enter your Contact Number in the "Contact Number" field.
6. Enter your Email in the "Email" field.
7. Enter your Address in the "Address" field.
8. Provide a brief description of the product you are inquiring about in the "Product Description" field.
9. Describe the reason for the recall in the "Reason for Recall" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the product you are inquiring about.
* **Order ID** (`order_id`, number, required/optional): Enter your order ID number, if available.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number or other contact information.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your full address.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the product you are inquiring about.
* **Reason for Recall** (`reason_for_recall`, text, required/optional): Describe the reason you are requesting the product recall.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and complete.
* If you have any additional comments or details, please include them in the "Reason for Recall" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
