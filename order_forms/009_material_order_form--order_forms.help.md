# Material Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to collect customer information and material order details for efficient processing.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the customer's basic details: customer name, email, and phone number. 
    - **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): This field is meant to capture the customer's name for reference purposes. 
    - **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the customer's email address for contact purposes. 
    - **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): This field is meant for the customer's phone number for communication.
2. Choose the material order type and select the materials ordered from the provided options.
    - **Material Order Type** (`material_order_type`, select_one, optional): This field allows you to specify the type of material being ordered. Select the relevant option from the dropdown menu. 
    - **Materials Ordered** (`materials_ordered`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to list the specific materials ordered. Choose the relevant options by selecting them from the provided list. 
3. Input the quantity and date for the ordered material.
    - **Quantity Ordered** (`quantity_ordered`, number, optional): This field is meant to capture the quantity of the material ordered. Enter a whole number. 
    - **Date Ordered** (`date_ordered`, date, optional): This field is used to record the date the material was ordered. Enter the date in the correct format.
4. Provide any special instructions and customer address for the order.
    - **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, note, optional): This field is meant to capture any special instructions or requests from the customer for the order. 
    - **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, optional): This field is meant to record the customer's address for delivery purposes. 

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled out correctly to prevent errors in processing.
- Double-check your selections in the material order type and materials ordered fields to select the correct options.
- Use the special instructions field for any specific notes or requests from the customer.
