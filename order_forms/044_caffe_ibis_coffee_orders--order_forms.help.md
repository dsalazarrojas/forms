# Caffe Ibis Coffee Orders - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect order information from customers for efficient order processing and fulfillment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer name in the field provided.
2. Provide your contact phone number in the format "123-456-7890" or "1234567890".
3. Describe your order details, such as the type and size of coffee you would like.
4. If you have a preferred pickup time, select it from the available options.
5. Choose your payment method:
	* Card: To pay with a credit or debit card.
	* Cash: To pay with cash at the time of pickup.
	* Mobile app: To pay through the Caffe Ibis mobile app.
6. If you have any special instructions or allergies, please include them in the field provided.
7. If you are a loyalty program member, please enter your membership ID.
8. For internal staff reference, please enter a brief description of your order (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer name** (customer_name, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact phone** (contact_phone, text, required): Enter your phone number in the format "123-456-7890" or "1234567890".
* **Order details** (order_details, text, required): Describe your order, including the type and size of coffee you would like.
* **Pickup time** (pickup_time, time, optional): Choose your preferred pickup time from the available options.
* **Payment method** (payment_method, select_one, required): Select how you would like to pay for your order.
* **Special instructions** (special_instructions, text, optional): Enter any special requests or allergies you have.
* **Loyalty program member** (loyalty_member, text, optional): If you are a loyalty program member, enter your membership ID.
* **Internal order reference** (order_reference, text, optional): For internal staff reference, enter a brief description of your order.
