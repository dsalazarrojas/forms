# Customer Menu Preference Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect customer menu preferences for an event or meal, ensuring that customers' needs are met during their dining experience.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information about yourself and your menu preferences.
- Make sure to include all required fields (customer name, phone number, email, preferred dish, dietary restrictions, event details, and date and time) to ensure that your request can be processed correctly.
- If you have any special requests, please provide them in the "Special Requests" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your ID or business card.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you to confirm your request.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so that we can confirm your request and send you any necessary updates.
* **Preferred Dish** (`preferred_dish`, select_one, required): Choose the dish you would prefer to be included in the menu.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, required): Please list any dietary restrictions you may have (e.g. vegetarian, gluten-free, etc.).
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): If you have any special requests or preferences that are not listed, please provide them here.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required): Provide as much detail as possible about the event or meal you are attending.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event or meal.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of the event or meal.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): This field is not for customer use. 

Note: This form is only intended for customers to request to be included in the menu rotation or who have specific needs that require accommodation. If you are a staff member and have already been removed from the menu rotation, please do not fill out this form.
