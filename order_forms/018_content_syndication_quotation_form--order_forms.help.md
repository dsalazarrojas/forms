# Content Syndication Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from clients or customers about their content syndication projects. The goal is to gather essential details to create an accurate quote for the project.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your contact information, including your company name, name, and business email.
2. Define the details of your content syndication project, including the type of content you want to syndicate and the target demographics.
3. Choose the desired outcomes and geographic reach for your project.
4. Determine your budget and timeline requirements.
5. Select any additional services needed.
6. Set a deadline for the quote.
7. Add any comments or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Requester Name**: Enter the name of the person requesting the quote.
* **Business Email**: Enter the email address associated with your business.
* **What content do you want to syndicate**: Provide titles or links to samples of the content you want to syndicate.
* **Content Type**: Choose the type of content you want to syndicate (Technical Whitepapers, Case Studies, Video Content, etc.).
* **Target Demographics/Industries**: Describe the target audience for your content.
* **Geographic Reach**: Select the geographic area where you want to reach your audience (Global, North America Only, EMEA, etc.).
* **Primary Success Metric**: Choose the main goal for your content syndication (Net New Leads, Brand Impressions, etc.).
* **Desired Campaign Duration**: Select the duration of your content syndication project (30 Days, 90 Days, 6 Months, etc.).
* **Monthly Budget Range (USD)**: Choose your budget range for the project.
* **Additional Services Required**: Select any extra services you need for your project (Lead Nurturing Emails, Custom Landing Page Design, etc.).
* **When do you need the quote by**: Set a date for the quote deadline.
* **Any other comments or questions**: Add any extra comments or questions you may have.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and clear information to create an accurate quote.
* If you have any additional questions or comments, use the designated field to communicate with the business.
* A detailed and well-structured quote will be provided once all necessary information is collected.
