# Tenant Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Billing Form is a critical component of the system used to collect and manage tenant billing information. This form is essential for processing and tracking billing status, rent amounts, and any additional notes related to a tenant's account.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Tenant Billing Form title and select the relevant information for the tenant.
2. Provide accurate billing information, including the rent amount and billing status (Active or Inactive).
3. Add any additional notes if necessary.
4. Review and submit the form for processing.

- **Tenant Billing Form** (`tenant_billing_form`, text, required): This is the form title. Enter a descriptive name for the form to identify it easily.
- **Billing Info** (`billing_info`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the billing information, such as the billing address or contact details.
- **Rent Amount** (`rent_amount`, number, optional): Enter the rent amount for the tenant, if applicable.
- **Billing Status** (`billing_status`, select_one, optional): Select the billing status of the tenant (Active or Inactive).
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes related to the tenant's account, if necessary.
