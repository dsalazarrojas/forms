# Custom Automation Arm Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Custom Automation Arm Order Form is designed to gather specific information for order-related tasks. This form is intended to collect details about the order requirements, file uploads, payment information, and other relevant details. Please fill out this form carefully to ensure that your order is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, begin by filling out the "Order Requirements" page.
    This page will have the fields that are required for the order.
2.  Next, proceed to the "File Uploads" page, where you can upload the necessary files for the order.
3.  The "Payment Information" page is where you will confirm the payment details for the order.
4.  On the "Delivery Preferences" page, you can select your desired delivery options.
5.  Finally, review and submit the form to ensure that you have filled out all the required fields correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Requirements** (`custom_automation_arm_order_form_page_1`, `text`, not required): Please fill out this field with the specific requirements of your order.
* **File Uploads** (`custom_automation_arm_order_form_page_2`, `text`, not required): Upload any necessary files related to your order here.
* **Payment Information** (`custom_automation_arm_order_form_page_3`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your payment details for the order. Please select "Yes" to proceed with payment.
* **Delivery Preferences** (`custom_automation_arm_order_form_page_4`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select your preferred delivery options for the order.
* **Delivery Time** (`custom_automation_arm_order_form_page_6`, `select_one`, required): Select the desired delivery time for the order. Please choose "Yes" to confirm the delivery time.
* **Custom Option 1** (`custom_automation_arm_order_form_page_5`, `date`, not required): This field is for any additional date information related to the order.
* **Custom Option 2** (`custom_automation_arm_order_form_page_7`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select any additional options related to the order.
* **Custom Option 3** (`custom_automation_arm_order_form_page_9`, `number`, not required): This field is for any numerical information related to the order.
* **Custom Option 4** (`custom_automation_arm_order_form_page_10`, `text`, not required): This field is for any additional text information related to the order.
* **Email** (`custom_automation_arm_order_form_page_11`, `email`, not required): Please fill out this field with your email address.
* **Delivery Time** (`custom_automation_arm_order_form_page_12`, `time`, not required): This field is for any time-related information about your order.
* **Note** (`custom_automation_arm_order_form_page_13`, `note`, not required): Add any additional notes about your order here.
* **File 1** (`custom_automation_arm_order_form_page_14`, `text`, not required): Upload a file related to your order.
* **File 2** (`custom_automation_arm_order_form_page_15`, `text`, not required): Upload another file related to your order.
* **File 3** (`file_3`, `text`, not required): Upload yet another file related to your order.
* **File 4** (`file_4`, `text`, not required): Upload another file related to your order.
* **File 5** (`file_5`, `text`, not required): Upload another file related to your order.
* **Payment Confirmation** (`custom_automation_arm_order_form_page_19`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your payment details by selecting "Yes".
* **Delivery Confirmation** (`custom_automation_arm_order_form_page_20`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm your delivery preferences by selecting "Yes".
* **Special Options** (`custom_automation_arm_order_form_page_21`, `number`, not required): This field is for any special options related to the order.
* **Additional Information** (`custom_automation_arm_order_form_page_22`, `text`, not required): Please fill out this field with any additional information related to the order.
* **Special Email** (`custom_automation_arm_order_form_page_23`, `email`, not required): Fill out this field with a special email address related to the order.
* **Special Time** (`custom_automation_arm_order_form_page_24`, `time`, not required): This field is for any special time information related to the order.
