# Event Day Wristband Printing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Day Wristband Printing Order Form is used to collect information for printing event wristbands. This form helps ensure that wristbands are accurate and delivered correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the event title in the "Event Title" field. This is the name of the event that will be printed on the wristband.
2. Enter the event date in the "Event Date" field. This is the date of the event.
3. Enter the event location in the "Event Location" field. This is the location of the event.
4. Enter the attendee's name in the "Name" field. This is the name of the person who will be picking up the wristband.
5. Enter the attendee's email in the "Email" field. This is the email address of the person who will be picking up the wristband.
6. Enter the attendee's phone number in the "Phone" field. This is the phone number of the person who will be picking up the wristband.
7. Enter the shipping name in the "Name" field. This is the name of the person or company that will be receiving the wristbands.
8. Enter the shipping address in the "Address" field. This is the address where the wristbands will be sent.
9. Enter the shipping city in the "City" field. This is the city where the wristbands will be shipped to.
10. Select whether the wristband should include shipping instructions in "State" field. Select "Yes" to include shipping instructions or "No" to exclude them.
11. Select the payment method in the "Payment Method" field. Select "Yes" to include payment instructions or "No" to exclude them.
12. Enter the payment amount in the "Payment Amount" field. This is the amount that will be paid for the wristband.
13. Enter any additional notes in the "Notes" field. This is any additional information that needs to be printed on the wristband.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Title** (`event_title`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the event that will be printed on the wristband.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the event.
* **Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person who will be picking up the wristband.
* **Email** (`attendee_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the person who will be picking up the wristband.
* **Phone** (`attendee_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the person who will be picking up the wristband.
* **Name** (`shipping_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person or company that will be receiving the wristbands.
* **Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address where the wristbands will be sent.
* **City** (`shipping_city`, text, required: false): Enter the city where the wristbands will be shipped to.
* **State** (`shipping_state`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" to include shipping instructions or "No" to exclude them.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" to include payment instructions or "No" to exclude them.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the amount that will be paid for the wristband.
* **Notes** (`payment_notes`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information that needs to be printed on the wristband.
