# Sample Pack Request Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect information about a sample pack order. It is intended for users to provide details about themselves and their order requests.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **User Info** (`user_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, title, and department (if applicable).
2. **Sample Type** (`sample_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of sample you are requesting.
3. **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the number of samples you require.
4. **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
5. **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
6. **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to receive your order.
7. **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to receive your order.
8. **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments about your order.
9. **Sample Request ID** (`sample_request_id`, number, optional): Enter the ID of the sample request.
10. **Sample Order Date** (`sample_order_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the sample order.
11. **Sample Order Time** (`sample_order_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the sample order.
12. **Sample Note** (`sample_note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments about the sample order.
13. **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
14. **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your company name.
15. **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter your department name.
16. **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
17. **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
18. **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city.
19. **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter your state.
20. **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip code.
21. **Fax** (`fax`, text, optional): Enter your fax number.
22. **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Enter your country.
23. **Other** (`other`, text, optional): Add any additional information about your request.
24. **Sample Request ID 2** (`sample_request_id_2`, number, optional): Enter the ID of the second sample request.
25. **Sample Order Date 2** (`sample_order_date_2`, date, optional): Enter the date of the second sample order.

**Tips:**

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely, as this will help us process your order efficiently.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
* Ensure that you have entered the correct sample ID and dates for your order.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, it's best to ask for clarification before proceeding.
