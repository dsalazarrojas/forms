# Warehouse Storage Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to refer their products to our warehouse for storage. It captures essential details that will help our staff process and manage the request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form with your customer information.
2. Provide clear and accurate details about your product(s) and storage requirements.
3. Double-check that all required fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data 1** (`form_data_1`, text, optional): This field is a free-form text area where you can enter any additional information you would like to share with our staff.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you about your referral.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where we can reach you if needed.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, note, optional): This field is for any extra notes about shipping, such as any specific handling requirements or special packaging needs.
* **Product Info** (`product_info`, text, optional): Describe your product(s) and their size and weight for our storage staff to accurately assess the referral.
* **Storage Capacity** (`storage_capacity`, number, optional): If you have specific requirements for the storage space, such as a certain amount of floor space or rack requirements, enter the details here.
 

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields (if any) are filled out correctly to avoid any issues with processing your referral.
* If you have any questions or need help with your referral, contact our customer support team for assistance.
