# Self Employed Purchase Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Self-Employed Purchase Order Form is designed to help self-employed individuals, such as freelancers, consultants, and independent contractors, to accurately track and report their work hours, clients, and financial information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out your **Client Details** (Field 1): Provide the client's name, address, and any other relevant information required for your work relationship.
2.  Enter the **Date** of the purchase order (Field 2): The date you completed the work or the date you plan to complete it.
3.  Enter the **Invoice Number** (Field 3): A unique identifier for this purchase order, which will be used for billing and record-keeping purposes.
4.  Describe the **Description of Work** (Field 4): A brief summary of the work you performed for the client.
5.  Enter the number of **Hours** (Field 5): The total hours you spent working on the project.
6.  Enter the **Rates** (Field 6): The rate at which you charge the client per hour. Note: This field is optional.
7.  Enter the **Total** (Field 7): The total amount due to you for the work completed.
8.  Enter the **Total Additional Rates** (Field 8): Any additional charges, such as taxes or fees, applicable to the work.
9.  Obtain your **Client Signature** (Field 9): Confirm the client has reviewed and agreed to the purchase order.
10.  Sign and date the **Date Signed** (Field 11): As the self-employed individual, confirm that you have reviewed and agreed to the purchase order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Details** (Field 1): Enter the client's name and any other relevant contact information.
*   **Date** (Field 2): Enter the date of the purchase order, either the completion date or the planned completion date.
*   **Invoice Number** (Field 3): A unique identifier for the purchase order, used for billing and record-keeping purposes.
*   **Description of Work** (Field 4): A brief description of the work completed or to be completed for the client.
*   **Hours** (Field 5): Enter the total hours spent on the project.
*   **Rates** (Field 6): Enter the rate you charge the client per hour (optional).
*   **Total** (Field 7): Enter the total amount due to you for the work completed.
*   **Total Additional Rates** (Field 8): Enter any additional fees or taxes applicable to the work.
*   **Client Signature** (Field 9): Obtain the client's signature to confirm their agreement with the purchase order.
*   **Employee Signature** (Field 10): As the self-employed individual, sign to confirm your understanding of the purchase order.
*   **Date Signed** (Field 11): Date and sign to confirm your understanding and agreement with the purchase order.
