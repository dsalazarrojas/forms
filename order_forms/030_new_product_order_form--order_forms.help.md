# New Product Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The New Product Order Form is designed to collect information from customers when they order a new product. This form should only be used for this specific purpose and not for any other type of data collection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "Customer First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Customer Last Name" field.
3. Enter the name of the product you want to order in the "Product Name" field.
4. Enter a brief description of the product you're interested in purchasing in the "Product Description" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Customer Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer First Name (text, required/optional)**: Enter your first name in this field.
* **Customer Last Name (text, required/optional)**: Enter your last name in this field.
* **Product Name (text, required/optional)**: Enter the name of the product you want to order.
* **Product Description (text, required/optional)**: Enter a brief description of the product you're interested in purchasing.
* **Customer Email (email, required/optional)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your order.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your order is processed correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
