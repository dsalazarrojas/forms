# Customer Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect customer information for check-out purposes, including name, contact details, and order ID.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Ensure it matches your active email address used to place the order.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field. This is optional.
5. Enter your address in the "Address" field. This is optional.
6. Enter the Order ID associated with your purchase in the "Order ID" field. This is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (required: false)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email (required: false)**: Enter your active email address used to place the order. This will help us contact you regarding your order or provide updates.
* **Phone Number (required: false)**: This is an optional field where you can enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Address (required: false)**: Enter your address if you would like to receive information about your order at your home or office.
* **Order ID (required: false)**: If you have an Order ID, please enter it to ensure we can locate your order accurately.
