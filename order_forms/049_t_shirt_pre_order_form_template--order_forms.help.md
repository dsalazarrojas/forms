# T-Shirts Pre Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for pre-ordering t-shirts. Please fill out the form with your details to help us process your order efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Customer Info** (Page 1). Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. Provide your **Shipping Info** (Page 1). Enter your shipping address, so we can deliver your order correctly.
3. Fill in your **Payment Info** (Page 1). This is where you can enter any payment information you're willing to share for your order.
4. Specify the **Order Details** (Page 1). Describe your order, including the item(s) and any special instructions.
5. Choose the **Quantity** (Page 1). Select the number of t-shirts you'd like to order.
6. Select the **Size** of your t-shirt from the options S, M, L, or XL.
7. Choose the **Color** of your t-shirt from the options Red, Blue, Green, or Yellow.
8. If you have a specific **T-Shirt Design**, please describe it in the **T-Shirt Design** field.
9. Select the **T-Shirt Color Option** from the options White, Black, or Grey.
10. Enter your **Shipping Address** (Page 1). Provide your shipping address so we can ensure correct delivery of your order.
