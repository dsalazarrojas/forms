# Custom Furniture Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Furniture Delivery Form is used to collect information for custom furniture delivery requests from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. First, enter your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last name" fields.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
5. Select a delivery date and time in the "Delivery Date" and "Delivery Time" fields.
6. Enter your delivery address in the "Delivery Address" field.
7. Choose how you would like to receive your delivery (Pickup at store, Delivery to home) from the "Delivery Method" field.
8. Select a delivery service (UPS, USPS) from the "Delivery Service" field.
9. Enter a Customer ID if applicable.
10. If you have any additional information, enter it in the "Order Notes" field.
11. If you have any special requests, enter them in the "Customer Notes" field.
12. Finally, review your information and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last name** (text, required): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address here.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your phone number here.
* **Address** (text, required): Enter your delivery address here.
* **Delivery Date** (date, required): Select a date for your delivery.
* **Delivery Time** (time, required): Select a time for your delivery.
* **Delivery Address** (text, required): Enter the address where you want your delivery to be made.
* **Delivery Note** (note, optional): Enter any additional information about your delivery here. 
* **Delivery Method** (select multiple, required): Choose how you want to receive your delivery (Pickup at store, Delivery to home).
* **Delivery Service** (select one, required): Choose a delivery service (UPS, USPS) to deliver your item.
* **Customer ID** (text, required): Enter a customer ID (if applicable).
* **Order Notes** (text, optional): Enter any additional information about your order here.
* **Customer Notes** (text, optional): Enter any additional notes about yourself here.
* **Special Requests** (text, optional): Enter any special requests here.
