# Worker Ear Protection Kit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to order ear protection kits for workers. Please complete this form carefully and honestly to ensure that the correct quantity of kits are sent to the correct departments.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your worker's name and company in the first page.
2. On the second page, select the department where you work.
3. Check if you need a hearing protection kit. If yes, select "Yes" and fill out the quantity required.
4. Enter your delivery address, including city, state, and postal code.
5. Finally, click submit to send your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Worker Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your ID or company records.
* **Company**: Please enter your company name as it appears on your ID or company records.
* **Department**: Please enter the name of your department or team.
* **Hearing Protection Kit**: If you need a hearing protection kit, select "Yes". If not, select "No".
* **Quantity**: Please enter the number of hearing protection kits you require.
* **Delivery Address**: Please enter your full delivery address, including street address.
* **Delivery City**: Please enter the city of your delivery address.
* **Delivery State**: Please enter the state of your delivery address (e.g., California, New York).
* **Delivery Postal Code**: Please enter your postal code (zip code).
* **Delivery Country**: Please enter your country of delivery (e.g., USA).
* **Notes**: This is an optional field for you to provide any additional information.
* **Submit**: Click this button to send your order. Please ensure that all fields are filled out before submitting.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter the correct quantity of kits required to avoid overspending.
* Double-check your delivery address for accuracy.
* If you need help with this form, please contact your supervisor or HR department.
