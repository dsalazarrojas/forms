# Grocery Preorder Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Grocery Preorder Form is used to collect information from customers for a grocery order, including contact details and order-related information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the customer information section with your contact details.
2. Enter the order details, including the date and time of the order.
3. List the items you would like to order, separated by commas.
4. Add any additional notes for the staff to know about your order.
5. Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., "name@domain.com").
6. Enter your phone number in the text field.
7. Enter your address to help the staff locate you or deliver your order.
8. Review and submit your form to place your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (Customer Information, text, required: false): Enter your name, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Order Details** (Order Details, text, required: false): Enter the date and time of your order, and any other details about your order.
* **List of Items** (List of Items, text, required: false): Enter a list of items you would like to order, separated by commas. For example, "apples, bananas, carrots".
* **Notes** (Notes, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or information for the staff to know about your order.
* **Order Date** (Order Date, date, required: false): Enter the date of your order, in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Order Time** (Order Time, time, required: false): Enter the time of your order, if applicable.
* **Email** (Email, email, required: false): Enter your email address in the format "name@domain.com".
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (Address, text, required: false): Enter your address to help the staff locate or deliver your order.

## Tips
* Please format your address correctly to ensure accurate delivery.
* Use commas to separate items in the List of Items field.
* Enter your email address in the correct format to ensure it is recognized by the system.
* Double-check your order details to ensure accuracy.
