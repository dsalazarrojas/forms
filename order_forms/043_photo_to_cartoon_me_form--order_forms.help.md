# Photo To Cartoon Me Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photo To Cartoon Me Form is a tool used to transform any uploaded photo into a custom cartoon. It's used to create a fun and creative representation of the user, suitable for personal, entertainment, and other related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:
1. Upload a photo that you'd like to convert into a cartoon.
2. Select a color for your cartoon by choosing one of the provided options.
3. Enter a description of what you'd like your cartoon to look like.
4. Choose a style for your cartoon by selecting one of the provided options.
5. Select a format for your cartoon by choosing one of the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Upload A Photo** (photo_to_cartoon_me_form_1, text, optional): Upload a photo that you'd like to convert into a cartoon. This is the starting point for your custom cartoon creation.
* **Select A Color** (photo_to_cartoon_me_form_2, select_one, optional): Choose a color for your cartoon. This option affects the overall aesthetic of your creation.
* **Enter A Description** (photo_to_cartoon_me_form_3, text, optional): Enter a description of what you'd like your cartoon to look like. This helps the system better understand your preferences.
* **Select A Style** (photo_to_cartoon_me_form_4, select_one, optional): Choose a style for your cartoon. This determines the overall look and feel of your creation.
* **Select A Size** (photo_to_cartoon_me_form_6, select_multiple, optional): Select the sizes you'd like your cartoon to come in.
