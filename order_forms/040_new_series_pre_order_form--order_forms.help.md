# New Series Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Series Pre Order Form is designed to collect information from customers who wish to pre-order a new series. This form helps to facilitate the pre-order process and ensures that the customer's order is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the payment type (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal) for your pre-order.
2. Enter your delivery address if you would like to receive the series at a different address.
3. Enter your name as it appears on your ID or official documents.
4. Enter your email address so we can contact you about your pre-order.
5. Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you about your order.
6. Choose the quantity of the series you would like to pre-order.
7. Enter the price of the series you are pre-ordering.
8. Select the launch date for the series, or enter a custom date if the series is not yet launched.
9. Select the launch time for the series, or enter a custom time if the series is not yet launched.
10. Add any additional notes or comments about your pre-order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Payment type** (`payment_type`, `select_one`, required: false)
This field allows you to select the payment method you would like to use for your pre-order. Options include Credit Card, Bank Transfer, and PayPal.

### **Delivery address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, required: false)
This field is optional, but if you would like to receive the series at a different address than your billing address, enter the address here.

### **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your name as it appears on your ID or official documents.

### **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false)
Enter your email address so we can contact you about your pre-order.

### **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you about your order.

### **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, required: false)
Choose how many series you would like to pre-order.

### **Price** (`price`, `number`, required: false)
Enter the price of the series you are pre-ordering.

### **Launch date** (`date`, `date`, required: false)
Select the launch date for the series, or enter a custom date if the series is not yet launched.

### **Launch time** (`time`, `time`, required: false)
Select the launch time for the series, or enter a custom time if the series is not yet launched.

### **Additional notes** (`note`, `note`, required: false)
This field is for any additional notes or comments about your pre-order.
