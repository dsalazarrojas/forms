# digital_menu_screen_order_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect order information from customers for the digital menu screen order process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the type of visit (Dine in, Pickup, or Delivery) from the "Digital Menu Screen Order Form" dropdown menu.
2. Enter the time of visit in the "Time of Visit" field.
3. Enter the order time in the "Order Time" field.
4. (Optional) Provide any special instructions for your order in the "Instructions" field.
5. Select the pickup location from the "Pickup Location" field.
6. Select the order items (Yes or No) from the "Select Order Items" field.
7. Enter the total cost of your order in the "Total Cost" field.
8. Enter your name in the "Customer Name" field.
9. Enter your email address in the "Customer Email" field.
10. Enter your phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
11. (Optional) Add any special instructions for your order in the "Special Instructions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Digital Menu Screen Order Form** (`select_one`, required): Select the type of visit you would like to make (Dine in, Pickup, or Delivery).
* **Time of Visit** (`date`, required): Enter the date and time you would like to visit the establishment.
* **Order Time** (`number`, required): Enter the time you would like to order.
* **Instructions** (`text`, optional): Provide any special instructions for your order, if needed.
* **Pickup Location** (`text`, required): Select the location from where you would like to pick up your order.
* **Select Order Items** (`select_multiple`, required): Select the order items you would like to order (Yes or No).
* **Total Cost** (`number`, required): Enter the total cost of your order.
* **Customer Name** (`text`, required): Enter your name.
* **Customer Email** (`email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Special Instructions** (`note`, optional): Provide any special instructions for your order, if needed.
