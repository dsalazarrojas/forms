# online_taxi_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book an online taxi service. You will be able to provide your pickup and drop-off locations, select the type of taxi you would like to book, choose the fare type, and enter your contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have filled in all required fields.
2. Enter your address in the field labeled "Address".
3. Select the type of trip you are making (Pick up, Drop off, or Both).
4. Choose the type of taxi you would like to book (Standard, Premium, or Luxury).
5. Enter the start and end times of your trip.
6. Enter your name, email, and phone number in the fields labeled "customer_name", "customer_email", and "customer_phone", respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Address**: Enter your pickup address. This will help us navigate to your location.
* **trip_type**: Choose the type of trip you are making (Pick up, Drop off, or Both). This will help us provide the best service for your needs.
* **taxi_fare**: Select the type of taxi you would like to book (Standard, Premium, or Luxury). This will determine the level of service and amenities you will receive.
* **start_time**: Enter the time you would like to be picked up.
* **end_time**: Enter the time you would like to be dropped off.
* **customer_name**: Enter your name. This is the name we will use to contact you.
* **customer_email**: Enter your email address. This is the email we will use to communicate with you.
* **customer_phone**: Enter your phone number. This is the number we will use to contact you in case of any changes or updates to your booking.
