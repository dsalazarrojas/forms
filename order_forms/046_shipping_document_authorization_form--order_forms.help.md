# Shipping Document Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shipping Document Authorization Form is used to authorize the shipping of a package or document to a customer. This form is a critical part of our shipping process, ensuring that all necessary information is accurately and securely captured before shipping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Choose whether the document should be shipped or not.
2. Enter the name of the customer to whom the shipment is intended.
3. Select the date you plan to ship the document.
4. Choose the time of shipment, if relevant.
5. Enter the shipping address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shipping Document Authorization** (`shipping_document_authorization_form`, `select_one`, **Required**): Choose whether the document should be shipped or not.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter the name of the customer to whom the shipment is intended.
* **Shipping Date** (`shipping_date`, `date`, **Optional**): Select the date you plan to ship the document.
* **Shipping Time** (`shipping_time`, `time`, **Optional**): Choose the time of shipment, if necessary.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter the shipping address for the document.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all information entered is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor for assistance.
- This form is a mandatory part of our shipping process, and completion is required to initiate shipping.
