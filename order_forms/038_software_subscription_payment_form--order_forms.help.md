# Software Subscription Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Software Subscription Payment Form is designed to collect payment and subscription information from customers for managing their software subscriptions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Name** in the first field. This is a required field to identify you as a customer.
2. Enter your **Email** in the second field. This is also a required field to verify your account.
3. If you have a **Phone Number**, you can fill it out in the third field. This is an optional field for additional contact information.
4. Enter your **Card Number** in the fourth field. This is a required field for payment processing.
5. Select your **Expiration Month** in the fifth field. Choose the month when your payment expires.
6. Select your **Expiration Year** in the sixth field. Choose the year when your payment expires.
7. Choose your **Payment Method** in the seventh field. You can choose between **Card**, **Bank Transfer**, and **PayPal** for payment processing.
8. Enter the **Amount** for your subscription in the eighth field. This is a required field for payment processing.
9. If you have a **Recurring Payment** schedule, select it in the ninth field. You can choose **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly**, **Quarterly**, or **Yearly** for recurring payment.
10. Choose your **Billing Cycle** in the tenth field. You can select **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly**, **Quarterly**, or **Yearly** for billing cycles.
11. Enter the **Start Date** of your subscription in the eleventh field. This is a required field for subscription activation.
12. Enter the **End Date** of your subscription in the twelfth field. This is a required field for subscription expiration.
13. Enter the **Time** of your subscription in the thirteenth field. This is a required field for subscription scheduling.
14. Select your **Subscription Type** in the fourteenth field. You can choose between **Basic**, **Premium**, and **Enterprise** for subscription plans.
15. Choose the status of your **Subscription Status** in the fifteenth field. You can select **Active**, **Inactive**, or **Canceled** for subscription status.
16. Select your **Payment Status** in the sixteenth field. You can choose **Paid**, **Unpaid**, or **Partially Paid** for payment status.
17. You can add a **Payment Method Description** in the seventeenth field. This is an optional field for additional payment information.
18. You can add notes for additional information in fields **Note**, **Note 2**, **Note 3**, **Note 4**, **Note 5**, **Note 6**, **Note 7**, and **Note 8**.
