# Taproom Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to take orders from customers in a taproom setting, collecting information on beer type, size, and quantity, as well as contact information for the orderer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the desired beer type (Lager, IPA, Ale, Pilsner, or Sour) in the "Beer Type" field.
* Choose the size of beer you would like (16oz, 32oz, 64oz, or 128oz) in the "Beer Size" field.
* Enter the number of beers you would like to order in the "Quantity" field.
* Select the type of glassware you would like (pint, mug, or growler) in the "Glassware" field.
* Enter the name you would like to have on the card for your order in the "Name on Card" field (optional).
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field (optional).
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
* Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field (optional).
* Enter a brief description of your order in the "Form Submit" field (optional).
* Finally, click the "Form Submit" button to complete and submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Beer Type**: Select the type of beer you would like to order.
* **Beer Size**: Choose the size of beer you would like.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of beers you would like to order.
* **Glassware**: Select the type of glassware you would like.
* **Name on Card**: Enter the name you would like to have on the card for your order.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address**: Enter your address (not currently being used).
* **Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Form Submit**: Enter a brief description of your order to complete and submit it.
