# Smart Safety Helmet Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to order a smart safety helmet. Please fill out the form with your shipping details, delivery preferences, and payment method to complete your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the shipping details, including your shipping address.
2. Choose your preferred delivery method (e.g., standard, express, or same-day).
3. Select your payment method (e.g., credit card, PayPal, or bank transfer).
4. Fill out the order form with any special requests or notes for your smart helmet.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, optional): Enter your shipping address where you want the helmet to be delivered.
* **Delivery Preferences** (`delivery_preferences`, text, optional): Specify any special delivery preferences, such as "deliver to a specific room" or "leave at the door".
* **Smart Helmet Order Form** (`order_form`, text, optional): Fill in any special requests or notes for your smart helmet, such as "with a particular feature" or "in a specific color".
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter the address where you want the helmet to be shipped to.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more delivery methods from the list:
	+ Option 1: Standard delivery (3-5 business days)
	+ Option 2: Express delivery (1-2 business days)
	+ Option 3: Same-day delivery (if available)
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more payment methods from the list:
	+ Option 4: Credit card payment
	+ Option 5: PayPal payment
	+ Option 6: Bank transfer payment

## Tips
* Double-check your shipping address and delivery method to ensure they match your needs.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (if any).
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our customer support team.
