# Wedding Catering Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Wedding Catering Quotation Form is designed to collect information about your wedding event for our catering services. Please fill out this form to provide us with the necessary details so we can tailor our services to make your special day unforgettable.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the required details about the couple (Couple Details).
2.  Choose your catering preferences and menu options (Catering Preferences & Menu Preferences).
3.  Enter the number of guests attending the event (Number of Guests).
4.  Select the date and time of the wedding event (Date of Event & Time of Event).
5.  Complete any additional preferences or details as requested in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **WeddingCateringForm**: This is the form header, which you've already landed on.
*   **CoupleDetails**: A text field where you can enter details about the couple getting married. This could include their names, ages, or any other relevant information.
*   **CateringPreferences**: A multiple selection field where you can choose from the options "Yes" or "No" for various catering services. Select "Yes" for services you're interested in, and "No" for those you're not.
*   **CateringPreferences1**: Another multiple selection field similar to the previous one, where you can choose from "Yes" or "No" for different services.
*   **NumberOfGuests**: A numerical field to enter the total number of guests attending the wedding event.
*   **NumberOfGuests1**: Another numerical field for additional guest details (though not explicitly required).
*   **DateOfEvent**: A date field to select the date of the wedding event. Please use the correct date format to ensure accurate processing.
*   **TimeOfEvent**: A time field to select the time of the wedding event. Use the 24-hour format for accurate processing.
*   **MenuPreferences**: A multiple selection field where you can choose from "Yes" or "No" for different menu options. Select "Yes" for services you're interested in, and "No" for those you're not.
*   **MenuPreferences1**: A single selection field similar to the previous one, where you can choose from "Yes" or "No" for different menu options. Select "Yes" for services you're interested in, and "No" for those you're not.
