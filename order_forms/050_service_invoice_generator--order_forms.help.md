# service_invoice_generator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The service invoice generator form is designed to create invoices for services provided to clients. This form is used to collect relevant information from both the service provider and the client, ensuring accurate and complete invoicing.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **User Details** section with your name and contact information.
2. In the **Billing Details** section, enter your billing name and contact information.
3. In the **Service Details** section, provide details about the services you are billing for.
4. Enter the **Date Services Billed**, which is the date the services were performed.
5. Enter the **Hours Billed**, which is the number of hours worked on the services.
6. Enter the **Service Rate**, which is your rate per hour for the services.
7. Calculate the **Subtotal** by multiplying the **Hours Billed** by the **Service Rate**.
8. Enter the **Tax Rate**, which is the applicable tax rate for the services.
9. Calculate the **Subtotal with Tax** by adding the **Subtotal** and the tax amount (calculated by multiplying the **Subtotal** by the **Tax Rate**).
10. Enter the **Discount Amount**, which is any applicable discount for the services.
11. Calculate the **Total** by subtracting the **Discount Amount** from the **Subtotal with Tax**.
12. Choose your **Payment Terms**, which determines when the payment is due.
13. Select the **Payment Method**, which is how the payment will be made.
14. Enter any **Notes** about the invoice or payment.
15. Enter your **Email** and **Phone** for contact information.
16. Sign with your **Client Signature** to confirm receipt of the invoice.
17. Enter the **Invoice Number**, which is a unique identifier for the invoice.
18. If applicable, enter the **Service Address**, **Client Address**, and **Billing Address** for billing and contact information.
19. Enter the **Date Invoiced**, which is the date the invoice was created.
20. Enter the **Date Paid**, which is the date payment was received (optional).
21. Review and agree to the **Terms and Conditions**, which are the rules and regulations for payment and services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details (1)**: Enter your name and contact information, such as name, email, and phone number.
* **Billing Details (2)**: Enter your billing name and contact information, such as name, email, and phone number.
* **Service Details (3)**: Enter details about the services you are billing for, such as description and quantity.
* **Date Services Billed (4)**: Enter the date the services were performed.
* **Hours Billed (5)**: Enter the number of hours worked on the services.
* **Service Rate (6)**: Enter your rate per hour for the services.
* **Subtotal (7)**: Calculate the subtotal by multiplying the hours billed by the service rate.
* **Tax Rate (8)**: Enter the applicable tax rate for the services.
* **Subtotal with Tax (9)**: Calculate the subtotal with tax by adding the subtotal and tax amount.
* **Discount Amount (10)**: Enter any applicable discount for the services.
* **Total (11)**: Calculate the total by subtracting the discount amount from the subtotal with tax.
* **Payment Terms (12)**: Choose when the payment is due.
* **Payment Method (13)**: Select how the payment will be made.
* **Notes (14)**: Enter any notes about the invoice or payment.
* **Email (15)**: Enter your email address for contact information.
* **Phone (16)**: Enter your phone number for contact information.
* **Client Signature (17)**: Sign with your signature to confirm receipt of the invoice.
* **Invoice Number (18)**: Enter a unique identifier for the invoice.
* **Service Address (19)**: Enter the service address (optional).
* **Client Address (20)**: Enter the client address (optional).
* **Billing Address (21)**: Enter the billing address (optional).
* **Date Invoiced (22)**: Enter the date the invoice was created.
* **Date Paid (23)**: Enter the date payment was received (optional).
* **Terms and Conditions (24)**: Review and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Service Provider (25)**: Enter your service provider information (optional).
