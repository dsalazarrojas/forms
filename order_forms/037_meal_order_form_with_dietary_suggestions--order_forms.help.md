**Meal Order Form With Dietary Suggestions - Help Guide**
=====================================================

**Purpose**
----------

This form is designed to collect information from customers about their meal orders, including dietary restrictions and preferences. It will help the assigned staff members to prepare and fulfill the orders accurately and efficiently.

**How To Complete This Form**
-------------------------

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
3. Select the order date and time from the calendar.
4. Choose the customer's dietary restrictions from the list (if applicable).
5. Select the meal suggestions from the available options.
6. Enter the customer's phone number, email, and address (if applicable).
7. Choose the assigned staff member to handle the order.
8. Select the order status (if applicable).
9. Enter the total cost and order type (if applicable).
10. Complete any additional notes or comments in the "Order Notes" field.
