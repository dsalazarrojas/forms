# Scentsy Holiday Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scentsy Holiday Collection Form is designed to collect customer information for the Scentsy Holiday Collection. This form is intended to gather details from customers, which will be used for marketing or sales purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the form title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a title for your submission, if desired.
2. **Enter your customer name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Provide your name or the name of the person submitting on your behalf.
3. **Provide your email address** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you or send you updates.
4. **Enter your phone number** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for further contact or communication.
5. **Enter your address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address for delivery or shipping purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form title**: Enter a brief title for your submission, if desired.
* **Customer name**: Provide the name of the person submitting on your behalf, or your own name.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address to allow us to contact you or send you updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your valid phone number for further communication.
* **Address**: Enter your valid address for delivery or shipping purposes.
