# Sports Event Food Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sports Event Food Order Form is designed to collect information from attendees for food and drink orders at a sports event. The information collected will be used for event management and order processing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Fill out the form completely and accurately**. Ensure that all required fields are filled out, and provide as much detail as possible.
2.  **Use a valid email address** for the email field.
3.  **Choose the correct food type** from the dropdown list to ensure that your order is processed correctly.
4.  **Enter the correct event date and time**, as this is crucial for order processing and event management.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name**: Your name as it will be used to identify you for order processing and communication.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address to receive any updates or confirmations related to your order.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of food items you would like to order.
* **Price**: Enter the price of the ordered food items.
* **Food Type**: Select from the dropdown list to choose the type of food you want to order. The available options are:
	+ Hot Dogs
	+ Burgers
	+ Nachos
	+ Popcorn
	+ Water
	+ Soda
* **Order Notes**: If you have any special requests or comments about your order, enter them here.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the sports event.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the sports event.
* **Venue**: Enter the venue where the sports event will take place.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the sports event.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled out correctly and accurately to avoid errors and issues with your order.
* If you need to add any special requests or comments to your order, use the "Order Notes" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns about your order, contact the event management team for assistance.
