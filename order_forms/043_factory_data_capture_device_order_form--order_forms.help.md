# Factory Data Capture Device Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to capture information for factory data capture devices, including customer details, order information, and device specifications. It helps in processing and managing orders efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the `first_name` field.
2. Enter your last name in the `last_name` field.
3. Provide your email address in the `email` field.
4. Enter your phone number in the `phone` field.
5. Enter the date of the order in the `order_date` field.
6. Select the type of data capture device you need from the `data_capture_device_type` field.
7. Enter any additional notes or comments about the order in the `order_notes` field.
8. Fill in your company name in the `company` field.
9. Provide your address in the `address` field.
10. Enter your city in the `city` field.
11. Enter your state in the `state` field.
12. Enter your zip code in the `zip` field.
13. Enter the name of the tool assigned to you in the `assigned_tool` field.
14. Enter the category of the device in the `category` field.
15. Enter a brief description of the device in the `description` field.
16. Enter any additional notes or comments about the order in the `user_notes` field.
17. Enter the current status of the order in the `order_status` field.
18. Select the type of order from the `order_type` field.
19. Enter the type of customer in the `customer_type` field.
20. Enter the ID of the customer in the `customer_id` field.
21. Enter the ID of the order in the `order_id` field.
22. Enter the name of the customer in the `customer_name` field.
23. Enter the order number in the `order_number` field.
24. Enter the date of the order in the `date` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name (Text, required: false)**: Enter your first name.
* **last_name (Text, required: false)**: Enter your last name.
* **email (Email, required: false)**: Enter your email address.
* **phone (Text, required: false)**: Enter your phone number.
* **order_date (Date, required: false)**: Enter the date of the order.
* **data_capture_device_type (Select Multiple, required: false)**: Select the type of data capture device you need. Options: Barcode Scanner, Digital Camera, Signature Pad.
* **order_notes (Note, required: false)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the order.
* **company (Text, required: false)**: Enter your company name.
* **address (Text, required: false)**: Enter your address.
* **city (Text, required: false)**: Enter your city.
* **state (Text, required: false)**: Enter your state.
* **zip (Text, required: false)**: Enter your zip code.
* **assigned_tool (Text, required: false)**: Enter the name of the tool assigned to you.
* **category (Text, required: false)**: Enter the category of the device.
* **description (Text, required: false)**: Enter a brief description of the device.
* **user_notes (Note, required: false)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the order.
* **order_status (Text, required: false)**: Enter the current status of the order.
* **order_type (Text, required: false)**: Select the type of order. Options should be provided in a separate field or in a list of options in the field, but not both.
* **customer_type (Text, required: false)**: Enter the type of customer.
* **customer_id (Text, required: false)**: Enter the ID of the customer.
* **order_id (Text, required: false)**: Enter the ID of the order.
* **customer_name (Text, required: false)**: Enter the name of the customer.
* **order_number (Text, required: false)**: Enter the order number.
* **date (Date, required: false)**: Enter the date of the order.

Note: The fields marked as required: false can be left blank if not applicable.
