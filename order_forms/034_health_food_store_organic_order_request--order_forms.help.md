# Health Food Store Organic Order Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Health Food Store Organic Order Request" form is designed for customers to request organic orders from our health food store. This form helps us understand your delivery requirements and fulfill your orders accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you have filled out all required fields (if any).
2. Fill out the "Delivery Request" section with your delivery details.
3. In the "Select Products" section, choose "Yes" if you are requesting organic products, otherwise choose "No".
4. In the "Delivery Date" and "Delivery Time" sections, select the date and time you would like to receive your order.
5. Enter your "Customer Phone", "Customer Email", "Store Name", "Store Phone", and "Store Address" details as required.
6. Add any additional "Order Notes" and "Delivery Notes" as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Delivery Request** (`delivery_request`, text, required: false): Please provide a brief description of your delivery request, including any specific details or instructions.
* **Select Products** (`selected_products`, select multiple, required: false): Choose "Yes" if you are requesting organic products, and "No" otherwise.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required: false): Select the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required: false): Select the time you would like to receive your order.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for us to contact you with order updates.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for us to send order updates.
* **Store Name** (`store_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the store where your order is being picked up or delivered from.
* **Store Phone** (`store_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the store where your order is being picked up or delivered from.
* **Store Address** (`store_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the store where your order is being picked up or delivered from.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional order details or notes as needed.
* **Delivery Notes** (`delivery_notes`, text, required: false): Add any additional delivery details or notes as needed.
