# School Lunch Preorder Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for the school lunch preorder system. Students are required to fill out this form to select their desired food option and quantity for the school meal. The form helps the school manage orders and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your school name and other relevant school information in the **School Information** field.
2. Enter your student details in the **Student Information** field.
3. Provide your parent or guardian details in the **Parent or Guardian Details** field.
4. Select your preferred food options from the available **Food Menu**.
5. Enter the quantity of food you would like to preorder in the **Food Quantity** field.
6. If applicable, enter the price of the food in the **Food Price** field.
7. Add any gift registry items in the **Gift Registry** field (optional).
8. Provide your payment details in the **Payment Details** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Information (Text, Required)**: Enter the name of your school and any other relevant information.
* **Student Information (Text, Required)**: Enter your student's name, grade level, and any other relevant details.
* **Parent or Guardian Details (Text, Required)**: Enter your name, phone number, and any other relevant details.
* **Food Menu (Select Multiple, Not Required)**: Select one or more food options from the available choices (Chicken, Beef, Pork, Fish, Vegetarian Option, Vegan Option).
* **Food Quantity (Number, Required)**: Enter the quantity of food you would like to preorder.
* **Food Price (Number, Optional)**: If you would like to pay for the food at the school, enter the price of the food. If you would like to pay by another means, do not fill out this field.
* **Gift Registry (Text, Optional)**: Add any gift items you would like to purchase for the school (optional).
* **Payment Details (Text, Required)**: Provide your payment method and any relevant details.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields (School Information, Student Information, Parent or Guardian Details, Food Quantity, Payment Details).
- Be accurate when filling out your school information to ensure the school can deliver the correct meals.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact the school's food administration for assistance.
- Review your order carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy.
