# Hotel Room Service Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to allow hotel staff to efficiently process and manage guest orders for room service.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the guest's name in the **Guest Name** field. This is the primary contact name for the order.
2. Enter the room number where the guest is staying in the **Room Number** field.
3. Choose the meal time you wish to serve the guest in the **Meal Time** field.
4. In the **Order Details** field, describe the meal or services requested by the guest.
5. If you have any additional instructions or requests, you can add them in the **Special Requests** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the guest making the order.
* **Room Number** (`room_number`, number, required: false): Enter the room number where the guest is staying.
* **Meal Time** (`meal_time`, text, required: false): Choose the time you wish to serve the meal to the guest (e.g., breakfast, lunch, dinner).
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required: false): Describe the meal or services requested by the guest.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, required: false): Add any additional instructions or requests from the guest.
