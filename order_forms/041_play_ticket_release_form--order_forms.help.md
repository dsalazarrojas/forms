# Play Ticket Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Play Ticket Release Form" is used to gather necessary information for releasing play tickets, ensuring a smooth and accurate process. This form will help us process your tickets efficiently.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the performance details.
2. Enter the quantity of tickets being released.
3. Choose your preferred delivery method (Email, In-person pick-up, or Mail).
4. Provide any special requests you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performance** (`play_ticket_release_form_performance`, text, required): Enter the performance details, such as the date, time, and title of the performance.
* **Quantity** (`play_ticket_release_form_quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of tickets you are releasing.
* **Delivery Preference** (`play_ticket_release_form_delivery_preference`, select_multiple, required): Choose how you would like to receive your tickets (Email, In-person pick-up, or Mail).
* **Special Requests** (`play_ticket_release_form_special_requests`, text, optional): If you have any special requests, such as seat preferences or accessibility needs, describe them here.
* **Special Requests 2** (`play_ticket_release_form_special_requests_2`, text, optional): Provide additional special requests, if needed.
* **Special Requests 3** (`play_ticket_release_form_special_requests_3`, text, optional): More special requests can be listed here.
