# medication_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "medication_order_form" is a form used to order medications. This guide will walk you through the process of completing the form, explaining each field's purpose and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the medication name in the "Medication Name" field.
2. Select the medication type from the "Medication Type" dropdown menu.
3. Provide a reason for the medication order in the "Reason for Order" field.
4. Enter the prescription number in the "Prescription Number" field.
5. Specify the medication dose in the "Medication Dose" field.
6. Enter the medication frequency in the "Medication Frequency" field.
7. Enter the medication duration in the "Medication Duration" field.
8. Add any additional contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
9. Enter any additional comments about the medication order in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medication Name** (`medication_order_form_medication_name`, text, Optional): Enter the name of the medication being ordered.
* **Medication Type** (`medication_order_form_medication_type`, select_one, Optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the medication is ordered or not.
* **Reason for Order** (`medication_order_form_medication_reason`, text, Optional): Provide a brief explanation for the reason the medication is being ordered.
* **Prescription Number** (`medication_order_form_prescription_number`, text, Optional): Enter the number of the prescription.
* **Medication Dose** (`medication_order_form_medication_dose`, text, Optional): Specify the dose of the medication.
* **Medication Frequency** (`medication_order_form_medication_frequency`, text, Optional): Enter the frequency at which the medication is to be taken.
* **Medication Duration** (`medication_order_form_medication_duration`, text, Optional): Enter the duration of the medication treatment.
* **Contact Info** (`medication_order_form_contact_info`, text, Optional): Add any relevant contact information.
* **Additional Comments** (`medication_order_form_additional_comments`, text, Optional): Enter any additional comments about the medication order.

Note: This form is not required to be completed in full, as some fields are optional. However, please ensure to provide accurate and relevant information for each field whenever possible.
