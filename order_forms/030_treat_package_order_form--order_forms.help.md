# Treat Package Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Treat Package Order Form is designed to collect information from customers for order processing and delivery purposes. It is likely used by businesses that offer treat packages, such as bakeries or food establishments.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Customer Info**: Fill in your name and other relevant details in the Customer Info field. This section is not required, but it will help us to address you properly.
2. **Choose Delivery Date**: Select a date for your order. This will be the date you want to receive your order on.
3. **Select Delivery Time**: Pick a time for your order. This will be the time you want to receive your order.
4. **Select Order Type**: Choose the type of order you want to place. Options include "Treat Bundle" and "Dessert Box".
5. **Select Treat Type**: Choose the type of treat you want to order. Options include "Chocolate Cake", "Fruit Tarts", and "Fruit Salad".
6. **Enter Customer Address**: You can type in any additional information about your address in this field. This is not required, but it will help us to know your location.
