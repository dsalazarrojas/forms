# Seafood Fresh Catch Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Seafood Fresh Catch Order Form is used by customers to place orders for seafood, including details about their contact information, catch preferences, payment terms, and order notes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the designated fields.
2. Enter your contact email address in the contact-email field.
3. If you need to contact you via phone, enter your phone number in the contact-phone field (optional).
4. Specify the date of the catch using the date-of-catch field.
5. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for catch-type to indicate whether you would like to receive live catch or frozen catch.
6. For payment-method, select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your preferred payment method.
7. In the payment-terms field, enter your payment terms according to the given description.
8. Add any additional notes about your order in the order-details field (optional).
9. Enter the total amount for the order in the order-total field.
10. Finally, add any additional order notes in the order-notes field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name.
*   **Contact Email** (3, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
*   **Contact Phone** (4, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted via phone, enter your phone number.
*   **Date of Catch** (5, date, required): Specify the date of the catch.
*   **Catch Type** (6, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' to receive live catch or 'No' for frozen catch.

*   **Order Details** (9, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your order.
*   **Order Total** (10, number, required): Enter the total amount for your order.
