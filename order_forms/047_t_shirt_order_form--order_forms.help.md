# T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "T Shirt Order Form" is a digital form designed to collect essential information from customers who want to place an order for a T shirt. This form helps us understand the customer's preferences and requirements for their order, ensuring we can process their request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, fill in your personal details in the "Customer Information" page.
2.  Next, select the type of T shirt you want to order from the "Product Select" dropdown menu.
3.  Enter the quantity of T shirts you wish to purchase in the "Quantity" field.
4.  If you have a email address, please enter it in the "Email" field.
5.  If you have a phone number, please enter it in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Information** (Customer Information, Text, Required/Optional): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant information about yourself.
*   **Product Select** (Product Select, Select One, Required/Optional): Choose the type of T shirt you want to purchase from the dropdown menu.
*   **Quantity** (Quantity, Number, Required/Optional): Type in the number of T shirts you want to buy.
*   **Email** (Email, Email, Required/Optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you for order updates.
*   **Phone** (Phone, Text, Required/Optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for any urgent order updates or questions.
*   **Submit** (Submit, Text, Required/Optional): Once you've filled in all the required fields, click this button to submit your order for processing.
*   **Cancel** (Cancel, Text, Required/Optional): If you've changed your mind about ordering, click this button to cancel your submission.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all fields are filled in correctly to avoid delays in processing your order.
*   If you have any questions or concerns about your order, please don't hesitate to contact us.
*   We will respond to your email or phone number as soon as possible to confirm your order details.
