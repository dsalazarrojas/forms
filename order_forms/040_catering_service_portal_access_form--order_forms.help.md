# Catering Service Portal Access Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Catering Service Portal Access Form is designed to be a simple and efficient way for individuals to request access to our catering service portal. By filling out this form, you will be able to request access to specific areas of the portal based on your role and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details such as your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Choose the type of access you need to our portal by selecting from the options provided.
3. Provide the date of your request.
4. Choose how you would like to be notified when there are updates to your account.
5. Select whether you want to enable two-factor authentication.
6. Choose the level of data access you need based on your role.
7. Agree to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
8. If you are a new user, you may be assigned an account manager.
9. If you need training on how to use the portal, select the type of training you prefer.
10. If you have any special requirements or emergency contact information, provide them in the fields provided.
11. Finally, provide any additional comments or information about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you would like to receive notifications and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number so we can reach you if needed.
* **Company Name**: Enter your company name if you are a representative of a business.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or position within your company.
* **Request Date**: Enter the date you would like to request access to the portal.
* **Access Type Requested**: Choose the type of access you need to our portal. Options include:
	+ Client Portal
	+ Vendor Portal
	+ Event Planner Portal
	+ Admin Portal
	+ Full Access
* **Reason for Access**: Choose the reason why you need access to our portal. Options include:
	+ Place Orders
	+ View Event Details
	+ Manage Bookings
	+ Access Invoices
	+ Update Profile
	+ Other
* **Event Access Needed**: If you need access to specific events, enter the event name or code.
* **Preferred Username**: Enter a preferred username for your account.
* **Notification Preference**: Choose how you would like to receive notifications:
	+ Email Only
	+ SMS Only
	+ Both Email and SMS
	+ No Notifications
* **Two-Factor Authentication**: Choose whether you want to enable two-factor authentication.
* **Data Access Level**: Choose the level of data access you need:
	+ Read Only
	+ Read and Write
	+ Full Access
	+ Restricted
* **Terms of Service Accepted**: Agree to our Terms of Service.
* **Privacy Policy Accepted**: Agree to our Privacy Policy.
* **Assigned Account Manager**: If you are a new user, enter your account manager's name.
* **Training Needed**: If you need training on how to use the portal, choose whether you want training:
	+ True
	+ No - I am Familiar
* **Training Preference**: If you need training, choose the type of training you prefer:
	+ Video Call
	+ In-Person
	+ Self-Guided Tutorial
	+ Documentation Only
	+ Not Applicable
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter your emergency contact name and phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter your emergency contact phone number.
* **Special Requirements**: Enter any special requirements or accessibility needs you have.
* **Referral Source**: Choose how you heard about us:
	+ Website
	+ Referral
	+ Social Media
	+ Advertisement
	+ Other
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about your request.
