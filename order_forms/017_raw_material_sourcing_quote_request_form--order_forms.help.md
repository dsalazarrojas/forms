# Raw Material Sourcing Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Raw Material Sourcing Quote Request Form is used to collect information from customers to request quotes from suppliers for raw materials. This form helps the purchasing team or procurement department to gather essential details about the material needed, the supplier, and the terms of the quote.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the customer's name and contact information in the "Customer Information" section (Page 1).
2. Enter the supplier's name and contact information in the "Supplier" section (Page 2).
3. Provide a brief description of the material needed in the "Material Description" field (Page 3).
4. Specify the quantity of material required in the "Material Quantity" field (Page 4).
5. Enter the supplier's quote price for the material in the "Material Price" field (Page 5).
6. Enter the supplier's quote delivery time in the "Supplier Quote Delivery Time" field (Page 6).
7. Confirm the supplier's quote terms and conditions in the "Supplier Quote Terms and Conditions" field (Page 7).
8. Add any additional comments or information from the supplier in the "Supplier Quote Additional Comments" field (Page 8).
9. Obtain the customer's signature and contact information in the "Customer Signature" and "Customer Contact" fields (Page 9).
10. Obtain the supplier's signature and contact information in the "Supplier Signature" and "Supplier Contact" fields (Page 10).
11. Enter the customer's address in the "Customer Address" field (Page 11).
12. Enter the supplier's address in the "Supplier Address" field (Page 12).
13. Attach any additional files or information from the supplier in the "Supplier Quote Additional Files" field (Page 13).
14. Attach any additional files or information from the customer in the "Customer Quote Additional Files" field (Page 14).
15. Enter any notes or comments in the "Notes" field (Page 15).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer`, text, required): Enter your company name and contact information.
* **Supplier** (`supplier`, text, optional): Enter the supplier's name and contact information.
* **Material Description** (`material_description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the material needed.
* **Material Quantity** (`material_quantity`, number, required): Specify the quantity of material required.
* **Material Price** (`material_price`, number, required): Enter the supplier's quote price for the material.
* **Supplier Quote Price** (`supplier_quote_price`, number, required): Confirm the supplier's quote price for the material.
* **Supplier Quote Delivery Time** (`supplier_quote_delivery_time`, time, required): Enter the supplier's quote delivery time for the material.
* **Supplier Quote Terms and Conditions** (`supplier_quote_terms_and_conditions`, text, optional): Confirm the supplier's quote terms and conditions.
* **Supplier Quote Additional Comments** (`supplier_quote_additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information from the supplier.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, text, required): Obtain the customer's signature.
* **Supplier Signature** (`supplier_signature`, text, required): Obtain the supplier's signature.
* **Customer Contact** (`customer_contact`, text, required): Enter the customer's contact information.
* **Supplier Contact** (`supplier_contact`, text, optional): Enter the supplier's contact information.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, required): Enter the customer's address.
* **Supplier Address** (`supplier_address`, text, optional): Enter the supplier's address.
* **Supplier Quote Additional Files** (`supplier_quote_additional_files`, text, optional): Attach any additional files or information from the supplier.
* **Customer Quote Additional Files** (`customer_quote_additional_files`, text, optional): Attach any additional files or information from the customer.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes or comments.

Please note that some fields are marked as optional, but it's highly recommended to fill them out for a complete quote request.
