# 3D Printer Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This 3D Printer Order Form is designed to collect essential information for the efficient processing and delivery of your 3D printing order. It gathers customer details, order specifics, shipping information, and payment method to ensure accurate and prompt execution of your order.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out your **Customer Information**: Enter your full name and email address in the **Full name** and **Email address** fields, respectively. This information will be used for order confirmation and communication purposes.
2.  Select your preferred **3D Printer Model** from the available options. Choose the correct model for your specific needs.
3.  Specify the **Quantity** of 3D printers you wish to order.
4.  Choose your preferred **Payment Method** from the available options.
5.  Select your preferred **Delivery Method** from the available options.
6.  Enter your **Delivery Address** and specify the **Requested Delivery Date** if applicable.
7.  Add any **Special Delivery Instructions** or **Additional Comments** regarding your order.
8.  Finally, review and submit your completed form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full name** (Required): Enter your legal name as it should be used for order processing and communication.
*   **Email address** (Required): Enter your email address where you'll receive order confirmations and updates.
*   **Phone number** (Optional): Provide your phone number for delivery coordination purposes.
*   **Company or organization** (Optional): If you're ordering for business use, enter the name of your company or organization.
*   **3D Printer Model** (Required): Select the correct 3D printer model for your specific needs.
*   **Quantity** (Required): Specify the number of 3D printers you wish to order.
*   **Unit price** (Required): Enter the price per printer in USD.
*   **Filament Type** (Optional): If applicable, choose the type of filament you prefer.
*   **Filament Quantity** (Optional): Enter the amount of filament needed in kg.
*   **Additional Accessories** (Optional): Select any extra accessories required for your 3D printers.
*   **Delivery Method** (Required): Choose your preferred shipping option.
*   **Delivery Address** (Required): Enter the full address for shipping.
*   **Requested Delivery Date** (Optional): Specify the date you'd like to receive your order.
*   **Special Instructions** (Optional): Add any special delivery instructions or notes.
*   **Payment Method** (Required): Choose your preferred payment method.
*   **Tax Exemption Status** (Optional): Check if you are exempt from taxes.
*   **Additional Comments** (Optional): Add any other information about your order.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all fields with a star (*) are completed accurately to avoid delays or errors in the order processing.
*   If you have any issues or questions, please contact us for assistance.
