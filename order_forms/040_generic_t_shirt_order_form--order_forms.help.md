# Generic T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect order information for T Shirts from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the T Shirt size you want to order from the size options (Small, Medium, Large, XL).
2. Choose the color of the T Shirt you want to order from the color options (White, Black, Red, Blue).
3. Select the design of the T Shirt you want to order from the design options (Graphic T Shirt, Funny T Shirt, T Shirt with a logo).
4. Enter the quantity of the T Shirt you want to order.
5. Enter your name so we can address your order correctly.
6. Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your order.
7. Select the payment method you prefer (Credit Card or PayPal).
8. Click "Submit Order" to send in your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **T Shirt Size** (Small, Medium, Large, XL): Select the size of the T Shirt you want to order.
* **T Shirt Color** (White, Black, Red, Blue): Choose the color of the T Shirt you want to order.
* **T Shirt Design** (Graphic T Shirt, Funny T Shirt, T Shirt with a logo): Select the design of the T Shirt you want to order.
* **T Shirt Quantity**: Enter the number of T Shirts you want to order.
* **Name**: Enter your name so we can address your order correctly.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your order.
* **Payment Method** (Credit Card, PayPal): Select your preferred payment method.
* **Submit**: Click "Submit Order" to send in your order.
