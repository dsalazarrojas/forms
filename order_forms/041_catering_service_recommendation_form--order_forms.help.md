# Catering Service Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from clients about their catering needs for a specific event. By completing this form, you will help us understand your requirements and preferences, allowing us to provide you with tailored recommendations for your catering service.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out each section carefully, making sure to provide accurate and complete information.
2. Use the provided options for select fields, and don't hesitate to ask if you need help.
3. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank, but make sure to provide any additional comments or explanations if necessary.
4. Review your form before submitting to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as the person responsible for this event.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you regarding your form submission and any follow-up questions.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number in case we need to reach you directly.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of event (e.g., Wedding, Corporate Event, Birthday Party).
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your event.
* **Number of Guests** (`guest_count`, number, required): Enter the expected number of guests for your event.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, required): Select the budget range per person (e.g., Under $25, $25 to $50).
* **Cuisine Preferences** (`cuisine_preferences`, select_multiple, required): Choose the types of cuisine you prefer (e.g., American, Italian, Mexican).
* **Service Style Preference** (`service_style_preference`, select_one, required): Choose the way you prefer your food to be served (e.g., Buffet, Plated Dinner).
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, required): If you have any dietary restrictions or preferences, please list them here.
* **Favorite Foods** (`favorite_foods`, text, required): Share with us your favorite foods so we can incorporate them into your menu.
* **Foods to Avoid** (`foods_to_avoid`, text, required): If there are any foods you dislike or cannot consume, please list them here.
* **Beverage Preference** (`beverage_preference`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred beverage option (e.g., Full Bar, Beer and Wine, Non-Alcoholic).
* **Formality Level** (`formality_level`, select_one, required): Select the formality level of your event (e.g., Very Formal, Semi-Formal, Casual).
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, select_one, required): Choose the time of day for your event (e.g., Morning, Midday, Afternoon).
* **Venue Type** (`venue_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of venue for your event (e.g., Indoor, Outdoor, Both).
* **Season** (`season`, select_one, required): Choose the season of your event (e.g., Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter).
* **Special Occasion Details** (`special_occasion`, text, required): If this event has any special significance or details, please share them with us.
* **Past Catering Experience** (`past_catering_experience`, text, required): Share with us any previous catering experiences that might help us tailor our recommendations.
* **Inspiration Sources** (`inspiration_sources`, text, required): If there are any sources that inspired your catering preferences (e.g., Pinterest, magazines), please list them here.
* **What Recommendations Do You Need** (`recommendation_type`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of recommendations you need from us (e.g., Menu Suggestions, Service Style, Beverage Pairing).
* **When Do You Need Recommendations** (`decision_timeline`, date, required): Enter the deadline for when you need our recommendations.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required): Share any additional information or comments about your event, and we'll do our best to accommodate your needs.

## Tips

* Please review your form carefully before submitting to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We are committed to providing you with exceptional service and recommendations tailored to your specific needs.
