# Sports Tournament Security Pass Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains how to complete the Sports Tournament Security Pass Order Form, which is designed for event organizers to manage access and permissions for various roles within the event, such as staff, vendors, vendors' staff, media, and guests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Event Details" page with the necessary information about the event.
2. On the "Staff Details" page, provide the required information about the staff members who need access to the event premises.
3. In the "Vendor Details" page, enter the information about the vendors and their staff who require access.
4. On the "Contact Info" page, provide your contact information for any follow-up inquiries or requests.
5. If you are a staff member, vendor, or vendor's staff, please provide a brief explanation for why you need access on the "Request Reason" page.
6. Fill out the "Staff Requesting" page with your name and other relevant details.
7. On the "Vendor Requesting" page, enter your name and other necessary information.
8. If you are a member of the media, please fill out the "Media Requesting" page with your name and other necessary information.
9. Finally, fill out the "Guest Requesting" page with your name and other details.
