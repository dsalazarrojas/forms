# Sports Tournament Security Pass Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains how to complete the Sports Tournament Security Pass Order Form, which is designed for event organizers to manage access and permissions for various roles within the event, such as staff, vendors, vendors' staff, media, and guests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Event Details" page with the necessary information about the event.
2. On the "Staff Details" page, provide the required information about the staff members who need access to the event premises.
3. In the "Vendor Details" page, enter the information about the vendors and their staff who require access.
4. On the "Contact Info" page, provide your contact information for any follow-up inquiries or requests.
5. If you are a staff member, vendor, or vendor's staff, please provide a brief explanation for why you need access on the "Request Reason" page.
6. Fill out the "Staff Requesting" page with your name and other relevant details.
7. On the "Vendor Requesting" page, enter your name and other necessary information.
8. If you are a member of the media, please fill out the "Media Requesting" page with your name and other necessary information.
9. Finally, fill out the "Guest Requesting" page with your name and other details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Details**: Enter the title, date, and any other relevant information about the event.
    - Type: text
    - Required: false
- **Staff Details**: Provide the full name, role, and any other necessary information about the staff members.
    - Type: text
    - Required: false
- **Vendor Details**: Enter the vendor's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
    - Type: text
    - Required: false
- **Contact Info**: Enter your full name, email, and phone number for any follow-up inquiries or requests.
    - Type: text
    - Required: false
- **Request Reason**: Briefly explain why you need access to the event premises (for staff, vendors, or vendor's staff).
    - Type: text
    - Required: false
- **Staff Requesting**: Enter your name and any other necessary details (e.g., role, department).
    - Type: text
    - Required: false
- **Vendor Requesting**: Enter your name and any other necessary information (e.g., company name).
    - Type: text
    - Required: false
- **Media Requesting**: Enter your name, email, and any other contact information (for media representatives).
    - Type: text
    - Required: false
- **Guest Requesting**: Enter your name and any other necessary details (e.g., company, affiliation).
    - Type: text
    - Required: false

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- Double-check the information entered for any errors or discrepancies.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the event organizers for help.
