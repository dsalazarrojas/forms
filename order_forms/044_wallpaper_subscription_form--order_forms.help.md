# Wallpaper Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wallpaper Subscription Form is designed to collect information from customers who want to subscribe to our wallpaper services. This form is used to gather details required for the subscription process, including subscription plans, customer contact information, and billing details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your subscription plan from the options provided.
2. Enter your customer name, email address, and other required contact details.
3. Choose your preferred payment method.
4. Enter your billing address and any other relevant details for the subscription.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wallpaper Subscription Details**: Select your preferred subscription plan. This is the type of subscription you are interested in.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Subscription Plan**: Choose your preferred subscription plan from the options provided.
* **Start Date**: Enter your start date in the YYYY-MM-YYYY format for the subscription.
* **Frequency**: Select how frequently you want to receive the wallpaper.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date in the YYYY-MM-YYYY format for the subscription to end.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount you want to pay for the subscription.
* **Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments related to the subscription.
* **Billing Frequency**: Check if you want to receive billing notifications for the subscription.
* **Billing Date**: Check if you want to receive billing notifications on a specific date.
* **Billing Method**: Check if you want to receive billing notifications via your preferred method.
* **Billing Address**: Enter your billing address.
* **Billing City**: Enter your billing city.
* **Billing State**: Enter your billing state.
* **Billing Zip**: Enter your billing zip code.
* **Billing Country**: Enter your billing country.
* **Payment Terms**: Enter any additional payment terms or conditions.
* **Additional Notes**: Add any additional comments or information related to the subscription.
* **Subscription Plan 2**: Check if you want to select a different subscription plan for additional services.
* **Billing Frequency 2**: Check if you want to receive billing notifications for the additional services.
* **Billing Date 2**: Check if you want to receive billing notifications on a specific date for the additional services.
* **Billing Method 2**: Check if you want to receive billing notifications via your preferred method for the additional services.
* **Billing Address 2**: Enter your billing address for the additional services.
* **Billing City 2**: Enter your billing city for the additional services.
* **Billing State 2**: Enter your billing state for the additional services.
* **Billing Zip 2**: Enter your billing zip code for the additional services.
* **Billing Country 2**: Enter your billing country for the additional services.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled out all relevant fields accurately and completely.
* Check your subscription plan details carefully before submitting the form.
* Review your payment method and billing information carefully.
* If you have any questions or need help with the form, contact our support team.
