# Funeral Flower Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers who want to place an order for funeral flowers. The form helps us understand the customer's requirements and preferences for the order, including their contact information, order details, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide details about the order, such as the product name, quantity, and delivery information.
3. Select the order type (in-person, online, or phone) and the order method (online, phone, or in-person).
4. Enter your payment details, including the tax and total amount.
5. Provide any additional comments or notes about the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`Customer Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person placing the order.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, required): Enter your address for delivery purposes.
* **City** (`City`, text, required): Enter the city where you would like the order delivered.
* **State** (`State`, select_one, required): Select the state where you would like the order delivered (CA, NY, TX).
* **Zip** (`Zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code for delivery purposes.
* **Note** (`Note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the order.
* **Product Name** (`Product Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the product you would like to order.
* **Product Image** (`Product Image`, text, optional): Enter the image of the product you would like to order.
* **Product Amount** (`Product Amount`, text, optional): Enter the amount of the product you would like to order.
* **Quantity** (`Quantity`, text, required): Enter the quantity of the product you would like to order.
* **Tax** (`Tax`, text, optional): Enter the tax amount for the order.
* **Shipping** (`Shipping`, text, required): Enter the shipping information for the order.
* **Total Amount** (`Total Amount`, text, required): Enter the total amount for the order.
* **Order Type** (`Order Type`, select_one, required): Select the type of order (in-person, online, or phone).
* **Order Method** (`Order Method`, select_multiple, required): Select the method of order (online, phone, or in-person).
