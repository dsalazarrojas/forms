# COVID 19 Rapid PCR Test Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you order COVID-19 Rapid PCR test kits. Please complete this form carefully to ensure accurate and timely delivery of your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Primary Contact Name field with the name of the person responsible for the order.
2. Enter the Company or Facility Name if you are ordering on behalf of a business or organization.
3. Provide the Delivery Address where you would like the order to be shipped to.
4. Specify the quantity of Rapid PCR Test Kits you would like to order.
5. Select your preferred payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, Stripe, Square, or Purchase Order).
6. Choose the shipping speed you prefer (Standard, Expedited, or Overnight).
7. Enter the Billing Contact Email for the payment and billing purposes.
8. Enter the Billing Contact Phone number for the payment and billing purposes.
9. If you are ordering on behalf of a business, enter your Tax ID Number.
10. Confirm that you are authorized to place this order on behalf of the organization.
11. Agree to the terms of sale and refund policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Primary Contact Name (1)**: This is the name of the person responsible for the order.
* **Company or Facility Name (2)**: If you are ordering on behalf of a business or organization, enter the name here.
* **Delivery Address (3)**: Enter the address where you would like the order to be shipped to.
* **Rapid PCR Test Kit Quantity (4)**: Specify the number of kits you would like to order.
* **Preferred Payment Method (5)**: Select from the list of available payment options to pay for your order.
* **Shipping Speed (6)**: Choose how quickly you would like your order delivered.
* **Billing Contact Email (7)**: Enter the email address associated with the billing contact.
* **Billing Contact Phone (8)**: Enter the phone number of the billing contact.
* **Tax ID Number (9)**: If you are ordering on behalf of a business, enter your tax ID number for tax purposes.
* **Order Authorization (10)**: Confirm that you are authorized to place this order on behalf of the organization.
* **Terms of Agreement (11)**: Agree to the terms of sale and refund policy before submitting the form.
