# Food Truck Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Truck Check Out Form is a document used by food truck staff to collect customer information and feedback after a sale is completed.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (Customer Information, text, required):
    This field is for the customer's basic details such as name and contact information.
* **Order Details** (Order Details, text, required):
    Enter a brief description of the order including any special requests or modifications.
* **Order Status** (Order Status, text, required):
    Describe the current status of the order, e.g. "Paid", "Unpaid", "Delivered", etc.
* **Customer Feedback** (Customer Feedback, text, optional):
    Share your thoughts and opinions about your experience at the food truck.
* **Additional Information** (Additional Information, text, optional):
    Enter any additional comments or information about your order.
* **Payment Information** (Payment Information, text, optional):
    Include payment details such as method and amount.
* **Customer Contact Info** (Customer Contact Info, text, required):
    Enter the customer's contact details such as phone number and email.
* **Customer Contact Info (2)** (Customer Contact Info (2), text, optional):
    Enter any additional contact information such as address.
* **Order Comments** (Order Comments, text, optional):
    Add any notes or comments about the order.
 
 ## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check the accuracy of your information before submitting.
* This form is only for customers who have completed their food truck transaction.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact food truck staff directly.
