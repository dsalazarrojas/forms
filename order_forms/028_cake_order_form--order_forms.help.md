# Cake Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Cake Order Form, a comprehensive form used to collect essential information for your cake order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Name**, a required field to identify you as the customer.
2. Enter your **Email Address**, a required field for order confirmation purposes.
3. Enter your **Phone Number**, a required field for contact.
4. Choose a **Order Date**, the date on which you would like to pick up or receive your cake.
5. Select a **Pickup or Delivery Date**, the date you would like to receive your cake.
6. Choose a **Preferred Time** for pickup or delivery, if applicable.
7. Select the **Receive Method**, how you would like to receive your cake. You can choose from:
	* Pickup in store
	* Home delivery
	* Event delivery
8. If you have chosen **Home delivery**, enter your **Delivery Address**, the full address where you would like to receive your cake.
9. Choose the **Cake Shape**, the shape of the cake you would like:
	* Round
	* Square
	* Rectangular
	* Heart
	* Custom shape
10. Select the **Cake Size**, the size of the cake you would like:
	* 6 inch
	* 8 inch
	* 10 inch
	* 12 inch
	* Sheet cake
	* Custom size
11. Choose the **Flavor** of the cake you would like:
	* Vanilla
	* Chocolate
	* Red Velvet
	* Lemon
	* Strawberry
	* Carrot
	* Marble
	* Coconut
12. Select the **Frosting Flavor** you would like:
	* Vanilla
	* Chocolate
	* Cream cheese
	* Strawberry
	* Lemon
	* Mocha
13. Choose the **Filling Flavor** you would like:
	* Same as frosting
	* Raspberry
	* Strawberry
	* Lemon curd
	* Chocolate ganache
	* Vanilla custard
	* No filling
14. Enter the **Quantity** of cakes you would like to order.
15. Enter a **Cake Message**, any message you would like to have written on the cake.
16. Select your **Color Scheme**, if you have any specific color preferences.
17. Describe your desired **Design Style** for the cake, if applicable.
18. If you have any **Dietary Needs**, please select from:
	* None
	* Gluten-free
	* Dairy-free
	* Vegan
	* Nut-free
19. Select the **Occasion** for your cake order:
	* Birthday
	* Wedding
	* Anniversary
	* Graduation
	* Baby shower
	* Holiday
	* Other
20. Choose the **Payment Method** for your order:
	* Credit card
	* Debit card
	* Cash
	* Gift card
21. If you have any **Special Instructions**, please provide any additional information you would like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for order confirmation purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Order Date**: Select the date on which you would like to pick up or receive your cake.
* **Pickup or Delivery Date**: Select the date you would like to receive your cake.
* **Preferred Time**: Select your preferred time for pickup or delivery, if applicable.
* **Receive Method**: Select how you would like to receive your cake.
* **Delivery Address**: If you have chosen home delivery, enter your full address where you would like to receive your cake.
* **Cake Shape**: Choose the shape of the cake you would like.
* **Cake Size**: Select the size of the cake you would like.
* **Flavor**: Choose the flavor of the cake you would like.
* **Frosting Flavor**: Select the frosting flavor you would like.
* **Filling Flavor**: Choose the filling flavor you would like.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of cakes you would like to order.
* **Cake Message**: Enter any message you would like to have written on the cake.
* **Color Scheme**: If you have any specific color preferences, enter them here.
* **Design Style**: Describe your desired design style for the cake, if applicable.
* **Dietary Needs**: If you have any dietary restrictions, select from the options provided.
* **Occasion**: Select the occasion for your cake order.
