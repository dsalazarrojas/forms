# Digital Marketing Campaign Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to help service agencies gather information from clients about their digital marketing projects. The agency will use this information to create a proposal or quotation for the project.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill in your name**: Please enter your name and any other names that will be working on the project.
2. **Project Details**: Describe the project in a few sentences. This will help the agency understand the scope and requirements of the project.
3. **Service Offers**: Select one or more services that the agency can offer for your project. This can include services like Graphic Design, Content Writing, Social Media Management, Video Production, Web Development, Digital Advertising, SEO, and PPC Advertising.
4. **Submit your quotation request**: Once you have filled in the required information, click the submit button to send your quotation request to the agency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Quote**: This is a field for you to enter any other relevant information about your project. This can include any additional details that will help the agency understand your requirements.
* **Client Name**: Please enter your name or the name of your company/organization. This will help the agency know who they are sending the proposal to.
* **Project Details**: Describe your project in a few sentences. This will help the agency understand the scope and requirements of your project.
* **Service Agency**: Please enter the name of the service agency that will be working on your project.
* **Service Offers**: Select one or more services that the agency can offer for your project. This can include services like Graphic Design, Content Writing, Social Media Management, Video Production, Web Development, Digital Advertising, SEO, and PPC Advertising.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when filling out this form, as this will help the agency create a more accurate proposal.
* Make sure to select all the relevant services that you think the agency will need to fulfill your project requirements.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact the agency.
