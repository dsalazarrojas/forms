# Beverage Delivery Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for customers to subscribe to a beverage delivery service. Please fill out the form to provide your contact and delivery information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full name" field.
2. Enter a valid email address in the "Email address" field for contact purposes.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone number" field, including the country code if applicable.
4. Select the relevant date in the "Date" field.
5. Choose the primary selection that best applies to your order in the "Primary selection" field.
6. Enter a whole number for quantity or rating in the "Quantity or rating" field.
7. Provide any additional relevant information in the "Additional details" field.
8. Select the options that apply from "Please confirm agreement" field.
9. If required, enter a decimal value for the "Estimated value" field.
10. Enter a time if scheduling is required in the "Preferred time" field.
11. Enter any final comments or notes in the "Final comments or notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (Full name, text, required): Enter your legal full name.
* **Email address** (Email address, email, required): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone number** (Phone number, text, optional): Include country code if outside local region.
* **Date** (Date, date, required): Select the relevant date.
* **Primary selection** (Primary selection, select_one, required): Choose the option that best applies.
* **Quantity or rating** (Quantity or rating, number, optional): Provide a whole number for quantity or rating.
* **Additional details** (Additional details, text, optional): Provide any additional relevant information.
* **Please confirm agreement** (Please confirm agreement, select_multiple, required): Select the options that apply.
* **Estimated value** (Estimated value, number, optional): If required, enter a decimal value.
* **Preferred time** (Preferred time, time, optional): Enter a time if scheduling is required.
* **Final comments or notes** (Final comments or notes, text, optional): Enter any final comments or notes.
