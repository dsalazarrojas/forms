# Cement Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cement Requisition Form is a tool for construction project managers to request cement from suppliers. It helps the supplier understand the project's specific needs and ensures accurate delivery of the required materials.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the project details section with the project's name and location.
2. Select the type of cement required and the quantity needed.
3. Provide the requisitioner's name and contact information.
4. Specify the delivery date and time (if applicable).
5. Enter the name and contact information of the approving manager.
6. Select the priority of the requisition (urgent, standard, or routine).
7. Add any additional notes or specifications if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name** (Required): Enter the name of the construction project.
* **Project Location** (Required): Enter the full address or site coordinates of the project location.
* **Requisitioner Name** (Required): Enter the name of the person requesting the cement.
* **Requisition Date** (Required): Enter the date the cement is required.
* **Type of Cement** (Required): Select the specific type of cement required (e.g., Portland, Rapid Hardening, Low Heat, Sulfate Resisting, White Cement).
* **Quantity Required** (Required): Enter the number of bags or units of cement needed.
* **Unit of Measure** (Required): Select the unit of measure (50kg Bag, 25kg Bag, Bulk Ton).
* **Required Delivery Date** (Required): Enter the date by which the cement is required to be delivered.
* **Preferred Delivery Time** (Optional): Enter the preferred time for delivery (if applicable).
* **Delivery Instructions** (Optional): Mention any unloading site details or access restrictions.
* **Approving Manager Name** (Required): Enter the name of the person authorized to approve this requisition.
* **Priority** (Required): Select the urgency of the requisition (High - Urgent, Medium - Standard, Low - Routine).
* **Other Order Specifications** (Optional): Enter any other details or specifications about the order.

Note: All required fields are marked as "Required" in the form, and it is essential to fill them out accurately to ensure successful delivery of the cement.
