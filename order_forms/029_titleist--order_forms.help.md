# Titleist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your information and preferences for Titleist golfing products or services. Please fill out the form below to ensure we can better understand your needs and provide you with an optimal experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **First Name**: Enter your first name in this field.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name in this field.
3. **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
4. **Phone**: Enter your phone number for further contact or follow-up purposes.
5. **Confirm Email**: Re-enter the email address you provided earlier for verification purposes.
6. **What type of club?**: Select the type of club you are interested in purchasing or inquiring about (e.g., Titleist Golf Balls, Titleist Golf Clubs, Titleist Golf Shoes).
7. **Which clubs would you like to purchase?**: Select the clubs you would like to purchase from the options provided (e.g., Driver, Fairway Woods, Hybrid, Irons, Wedges, Putter).
8. **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format "mm/dd/yyyy" (e.g., 01/02/1990).
9. **Additional Information**: Provide any additional information or comments you would like to share with us.
10. **What type of golfer are you?**: Select your skill level as a golfer (e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
11. **Which services would you like to add?**: Select the services you would like to add to your purchase (e.g., Lessons, Fitting, Repair).
12. **Email 2**: Enter a secondary email address for further communication or follow-up purposes.
13. **Phone 2**: Enter a secondary phone number for further contact or follow-up purposes.
