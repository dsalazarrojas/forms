# Nail Polish Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who wish to place an order for nail polish. It will be used by a business to fulfill the user's order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** (Page 1, **First Name** field)
2. Fill in your **Last Name** (Page 1, **Last Name** field)
3. Enter your **Address** (Page 1, **Address** field)
4. Enter your **City** (Page 1, **City** field)
5. Enter your **Zip Code** (Page 1, **Zip** field)
6. Enter your **Phone Number** (Page 1, **Phone** field)
7. Enter your **Email** (Page 1, **Email** field)
8. Select your preferred **Color** (Page 1, **Color** field)
9. Select the **Size** of the nail polish (Page 1, **Color Size** field)
10. Add any additional **Notes** (Page 1, **Notes** field)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Page 1, **First Name**, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Page 1, **Last Name**, text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Address** (Page 1, **Address**, text, required): Please enter your address for shipping purposes.
* **City** (Page 1, **City**, text, required): Please enter the city for your address.
* **Zip** (Page 1, **Zip, text, required): Please enter your zip code.
* **Phone** (Page 1, **Phone, text, required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Email** (Page 1, **Email, text, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Color** (Page 1, **Color, text, required): Please select the color of the nail polish you would like to order.
* **Color Size** (Page 1, **Color Size, text, required): Please select the size of the nail polish you would like to order.
* **Notes** (Page 1, **Notes, text, required): Please add any additional notes about your order, if necessary.
