# Sender and recipient information form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sender and Recipient Information Form is used to collect and organize information about senders and recipients of an order. This form is used to ensure clear and accurate communication between parties involved in the order process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your information as the sender in the **sender info** field.
2. Enter your name in the **sender name** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **sender email** field, if applicable.
4. Enter the recipient's information in the **recipient info** field.
5. Enter the recipient's name in the **recipient name** field.
6. Enter the recipient's email address in the **recipient email** field, if applicable.
7. Enter the recipient's phone number in the **recipient phone** field.
8. Enter any additional notes for the recipient in the **recipient notes** field.
9. Enter the delivery date and time in the **delivery date** and **delivery time** fields.
10. Select the delivery method from the list in the **delivery method** field.
11. Enter the delivery address in the **delivery address** field.
12. Enter your company name in the **sender company** field, if applicable.
13. Enter the recipient's company name in the **recipient company** field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **sender info**: Enter a brief description of yourself as the sender.
* **sender name**: Enter your full name as the sender.
* **sender email**: Enter your email address as the sender.
*   Note: This field is optional and is not required for form submission.
* **recipient info**: Enter a brief description of the recipient.
* **recipient name**: Enter the recipient's full name.
* **recipient email**: Enter the recipient's email address.
*   Note: This field is optional and is not required for form submission.
* **recipient phone**: Enter the recipient's phone number.
* **recipient notes**: Enter any additional notes or information about the recipient.
* **delivery details**: Enter any additional information about the delivery process.
* **delivery date**: Enter the date of the delivery.
*   Format: DD/MM/YY (e.g., 01/02/20).
* **delivery time**: Enter the time of the delivery.
*   Format: HH:MM AM/PM (e.g., 12:00 PM).
* **delivery method**: Select the method of delivery from the list.
*   Options: delivery method1, delivery method2, delivery method3, delivery method4, delivery method5.
*   Note: This field is optional and is not required for form submission.
* **delivery address**: Enter the address of the delivery location.
* **sender company**: Enter your company name as the sender.
*   Note: This field is optional and is not required for form submission.
* **recipient company**: Enter the recipient's company name.
*   Note: This field is optional and is not required for form submission.
*   Note: All fields are optional unless otherwise specified as required. It is recommended to fill out as much information as possible for accurate and efficient processing.
