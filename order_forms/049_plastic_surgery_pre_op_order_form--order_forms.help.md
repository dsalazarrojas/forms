# Plastic Surgery Pre Op Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect necessary information for a plastic surgery procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the patient's name and date of birth.
2. Provide the patient's identification number.
3. Enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact.
4. Indicate if the emergency contact is a relative or not.
5. Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
6. Enter the emergency contact's email address.
7. Select if the patient requires laboratory tests before surgery.
8. Select if the patient has any medical conditions.
9. Enter the name of the surgeon performing the procedure.
10. Select the name of the procedure to be performed.
11. Indicate if the patient requires any pre-operative medications.
12. Enter the anesthesia plan for the patient.
13. Enter the name of the surgeon performing the procedure.
14. Select the surgeon's specialty.
15. Enter the surgeon's ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Please enter the patient's full name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Identification Number**: Enter the patient's identification number (e.g., driver's license number).
* **Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Relation**: Indicate if the emergency contact is a relative or not (Yes/No).
* **Phone**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Email**: Enter the emergency contact's email address.
* **Laboratory Tests**: Select if the patient requires laboratory tests before surgery (Yes/No).
* **Condition**: Select if the patient has any medical conditions (Yes/No).
* **Surgeon Name**: Enter the name of the surgeon performing the procedure.
* **Procedure Name**: Select the name of the procedure to be performed (Yes/No).
* **Pre Operative Medications**: Select if the patient requires any pre-operative medications (Yes/No).
* **Anesthesia Plan**: Enter the anesthesia plan for the patient.
* **Name**: Enter the name of the surgeon performing the procedure ( duplicate of id 14).
* **Speciality**: Select the surgeon's specialty (Yes/No).
* **Surgeon ID**: Enter the surgeon's ID.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any information, consult with a medical professional.
* This form must be completed in full before the surgery can proceed.
