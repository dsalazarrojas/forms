# Afterpay Cosmetics Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Afterpay Cosmetics Order Form is designed to collect order information from customers for delivery of professional cosmetic and skincare products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Review each field carefully to ensure you provide accurate information.
2. Fill out the required fields (denoted as "true" in the table below):
	* Full Name
	* Contact Email
	* Phone Number
	* Product Category - Skincare, Makeup, Haircare, etc.
	* Item Name or Shade
	* Quantity
	* Payment Method - Afterpay, Card, PayPal
	* Total Order Amount
3. Optionally, select complimentary samples from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Full Name:
	* **Full Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on the order and any subsequent communications.
### 2. Contact Email:
	* **Contact Email** (email, required): Enter your email address for tracking and communication purposes.
### 3. Phone Number:
	* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your phone number for contact and delivery purposes.
### 4. Shipping Address:
	* **Shipping Address** (text, required): Enter your full street address for delivery purposes.
### 5. Product Category:
	* **Product Category - Skincare, Makeup, Haircare, etc.** (select_one, required): Choose the category of the product you are ordering (e.g., Skincare, Makeup, etc.).
### 6. Item Name or Shade:
	* **Item Name or Shade** (text, required): Enter the name or shade of the product you are ordering (e.g., Matte Lipstick - Rose Pink).
### 7. Quantity:
	* **Quantity** (number, required): Enter the quantity of the product you are ordering.
### 8. Samples to Include:
	* **Samples to include - limit 3** (select_multiple, optional): Choose up to 3 complimentary samples from the options provided.
### 9. Payment Method:
	* **Payment Method - Afterpay, Card, PayPal** (select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method (e.g., Afterpay, Credit Card, PayPal).
### 10. Total Order Amount:
	* **Total Order Amount** (number, required): Enter the total amount of the order, including tax and shipping.

## Tips
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Review your order carefully before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our customer support team.
