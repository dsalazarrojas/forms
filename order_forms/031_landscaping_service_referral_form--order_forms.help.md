# Landscaping Service Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about customers or clients who refer others to our landscaping service. The data collected will help us improve our service and provide better experiences for both our clients and new customers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are filled out.
2. Enter your own information for "Name", "Email", and "Phone" fields.
3. In the "Referral" field, provide a description of the referred client's needs or preferences.
4. Enter the "Client Name", "Client Email", and "Client Phone" fields with the information of the referred client.
5. Select all services provided in the "Service Provided" field that you believe will be relevant to the referred client.
6. Fill out the "Contact Info" field with your own contact information.
7. Enter the name of the person who referred the client in the "Referred By" field.
8. Finally, fill out the "Service Address" field with the address where the service will be performed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`1`, `text`, required): This field is required to capture the name of the person filling out the form.
* **Email** (`2`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`3`, `text`, optional): Provide your phone number so we can reach out to you for any questions or updates.
* **Message** (`4`, `text`, required): This field is for any additional information or description of the referred client.
* **Service** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all services that you think will be relevant to the referred client.
* **Referral** (`6`, `text`, optional): Enter a description of the referred client's needs or preferences.
* **Client Name** (`7`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the client being referred.
* **Client Email** (`8`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the client being referred.
* **Client Phone** (`9`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the client being referred.
* **Service Provided** (`10`, `text`, required): Select all services that you believe will be relevant to the referred client.
* **Contact Info** (`11`, `text`, required): Enter your name and any other contact information so we can reach back to you.
* **Referred By** (`12`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person who referred the client.
* **Service Address** (`13`, `text`, optional): Enter the address where the service will be performed.
