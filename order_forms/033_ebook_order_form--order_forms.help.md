# Ebook Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for customers to submit their ebook orders. It collects information for the order and customer details.

## How To Complete This Form
To place an order, fill out this form completely and accurately. Ensure that you provide all necessary information for both the order and customer details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **order_form** (`order_form`, text, not required):
    This field is the main text area where customers can type a short description or message to go with their order.
* **customer_info** (`customer_info`, text, not required):
    Enter information about the customer, if desired.
* **book_info** (`book_info`, text, not required):
    Provide details about the book ordered, if applicable.
* **ebook_details** (`ebook_details`, text, not required):
    Describe the ebook being ordered, including any special instructions.
* **order_info** (`order_info`, text, not required):
    Enter information about the order, if needed.
* **order_subtotals** (`order_subtotals`, text, not required):
    Enter any subtotals for the order, if applicable.
* **payment_info** (`payment_info`, text, not required):
    Provide payment information, if needed.
* **email** (`email`, email, not required):
    Enter the customer's email address for communication.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, not required):
    Enter the customer's phone number for contact.
* **company** (`company`, text, not required):
    Enter the customer's company name, if applicable.
* **first_name** (`first_name`, text, not required):
    Enter the customer's first name.
* **last_name** (`last_name`, text, not required):
    Enter the customer's last name.
* **book_title** (`book_title`, text, not required):
    Enter the title of the book being ordered.
* **book_author** (`book_author`, text, not required):
    Enter the author of the book being ordered.
* **book_price** (`book_price`, number, not required):
    Enter the price of the book.
* **quantity** (`quantity`, number, not required):
    Enter the quantity of the book being ordered.
* **total** (`total`, number, not required):
    Enter the total cost of the order.
* **order_date** (`order_date`, date, not required):
    Enter the date of the order.
* **order_time** (`order_time`, time, not required):
    Enter the time of the order.
* **payment_type** (`payment_type`, select_multiple, required):
    Select the payment type (Cheque, EFT, Credit Card).
* **payment_details** (`payment_details`, select_one, required):
    Select the specific payment detail (Cheque, EFT, Credit Card).
* **customer_address** (`customer_address`, text, not required):
    Enter the customer's address.
* **zip_code** (`zip_code`, text, not required):
    Enter the customer's zip code.
* **city** (`city`, text, not required):
    Enter the customer's city.
* **country** (`country`, text, not required):
    Enter the customer's country.

## Tips
Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your order is processed correctly.
