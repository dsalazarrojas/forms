# Tire Replacement Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tire Replacement Requisition Form is used to request replacement tires for a vehicle. It gathers information about the order, the customer, the tire details, and any payment information associated with the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the necessary information for each field. The form is divided into several sections, each with its own set of fields. Please follow the instructions below for each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Order Details
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): This field is used to gather information about the order, such as the reason for the replacement, the expected date of delivery, and any other relevant details.

### Customer Info
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, optional): This field is used to gather information about the customer, such as their name, contact information, and any other relevant details.

### Tire Info
* **Tire Info** (`tire_info`, text, optional): This field is used to gather information about the tire to be replaced, such as its make, model, and size.

### Service Details
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, text, optional): This field is used to gather information about any additional services required for the replacement, such as wheel alignment or balancing.

### Payment Info
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, optional): This field is used to gather information about any payment methods or details, such as the payment amount and any applicable discounts.

### Order Confirmation
* **Order Confirmation** (`order_confirmation`, text, optional): This field is used to confirm that the order has been received and understood.

## Tips
* Please provide clear and concise answers to each field.
* Do not include any attachments or images.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* We will review and process your order as soon as possible.
