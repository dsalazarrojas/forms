# fundraising_package_delivery_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the delivery details of a fundraising package.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Form Data** (`form_data`, `text`, not required): Enter a brief description of the package, if necessary.
2. **Fundraising Package** (`fundraising_package`, `select_one`, not required): Choose 'Yes' to indicate that this is a fundraising package.
3. **Package ID** (`package_id`, `text`, not required): Enter the package ID (if available).
4. **Name** (`name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the recipient of the package.
5. **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): Enter the email address of the recipient (if available).
6. **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required): Enter the phone number of the recipient (if available).
7. **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date of the delivery.
8. **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, `time`, not required): Enter the time of the delivery.
9. **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, not required): Enter the delivery address of the package.
10. **Delivery Zip** (`delivery_zip`, `text`, not required): Enter the delivery zip code (if available).
11. **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, not required): Choose the payment method used for delivery (e.g. Credit Card, Bank Transfer, Check, Online Payment).
12. **Package Details** (`package_details`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional details about the package.
13. **Delivery Instructions** (`delivery_instructions`, `note`, not required): Enter any special delivery instructions (if necessary).
14. **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional comments about the delivery.
15. **Order Status** (`order_status`, `select_one`, not required): Choose the order status (e.g. New Order, Pending, Shipped, Delivered).
16. **Delivery Date Ship** (`delivery_date_ship`, `date`, not required): Enter the date of delivery for the shipped package (if applicable).
17. **Delivery Time Ship** (`delivery_time_ship`, `time`, not required): Enter the time of delivery for the shipped package (if applicable).
18. **Delivery Zip Ship** (`delivery_zip_ship`, `text`, not required): Enter the zip code of the shipped package (if applicable).
19. **Delivery Address Ship** (`delivery_address_ship`, `text`, not required): Enter the address of the shipped package (if applicable).
20. **Payment Method Ship** (`payment_method_ship`, `select_one`, not required): Choose the payment method used for shipping (if applicable).
21. **Package Details Ship** (`package_details_ship`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional details about the shipped package.
22. **Delivery Instructions Ship** (`delivery_instructions_ship`, `note`, not required): Enter any special delivery instructions for the shipped package (if necessary).
23. **Additional Comments Ship** (`additional_comments_ship`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional comments about the shipped package.
24. **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional notes about the delivery.
25. **Submit** (`submit`, `select_multiple`, not required): Click 'Yes' to confirm that the information is correct and ready for submission.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure accurate information.
* If you're unsure about a field, you can always leave it blank or enter a brief description.
* Review the form carefully before clicking 'Submit' to ensure that the information is correct.
