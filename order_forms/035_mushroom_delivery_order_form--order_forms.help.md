# Mushroom Delivery Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for customers or users to place orders for mushroom delivery. It collects necessary information to ensure accurate and efficient order processing.

## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Provide Contact Information
- Complete the first name and last name fields with your valid name.
- Enter a valid email address in the Email field.

### Step 2: Choose Order Details
- Select the desired Order Date for your delivery.
- Choose the desired Delivery Date for your order.
- Select the Pickup Time for your delivery.

### Step 3: Specify Delivery Details
- Choose the desired Delivery Type for your order.
- Enter the delivery address in the Delivery Address field.

### Step 4: Add Order Items
- Enter the Item Name for each item in your order.
- Specify the quantity of each item in the Item Quantity field.
- Enter the price of each item in the Item Price field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Order Date** (required): Select the desired date for your delivery order.
* **Delivery Date** (required): Choose the desired date for your delivery.
* **Pickup Time** (required): Select the desired time for your delivery pickup.
* **Delivery Type** (optional): Choose the desired type of delivery service.
*   Options: 
    - Option 1
    - Option 2
    - Option 3
* **Delivery Address** (required): Enter the delivery address where you would like to receive your order.
* **Item Name** (required): Enter the name of each item in your order.
* **Item Quantity** (required): Specify the quantity of each item in your order.
* **Item Price** (required): Enter the price of each item in your order.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting your order.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the support team for assistance.
