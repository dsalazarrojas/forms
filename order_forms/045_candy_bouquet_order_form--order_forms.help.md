# Candy Bouquet Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to order a custom candy bouquet with various customization options, including size, candy selection, color theme, container type, occasion, and more. Please follow these steps to complete the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the customer details section with your information.
2. Select the desired bouquet details, such as size, candy types, color theme, and container type.
3. Choose the occasion and add a gift message if needed.
4. Provide delivery information, including the recipient's name, phone number, and delivery date and time.
5. Select the payment method and special instructions (if any).
6. Finally, confirm your order by reviewing and submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`bouquet_customer_name`, Required): Please enter your name as the customer.
* **Email Address** (`bouquet_customer_email`, Required): Enter your email address for order status updates.
* **Phone Number** (`bouquet_customer_phone`, Required): Enter your phone number for any delivery-related inquiries.
* **Delivery Address** (`bouquet_delivery_address`, Required): Enter the full street address for delivery purposes.
* **Bouquet Size** (`bouquet_size_choice`, Required): Choose from the options: Small ($25), Medium ($45), Large ($65), or Deluxe ($85).
* **Primary Candy Types** (`primary_candy_selections`, Required): Select multiple types of sweets, including Chocolate Bars, Gummy Bears, Sour Candies, Hard Candies, Lollipops, or Other.
* **Preferred Color Theme** (`bouquet_color_theme`, Required): Choose from the options: Rainbow, Pastel, Red or Pink, Blue or Gold, Seasonal, or Custom.
* **Container Type** (`bouquet_container_type`, Required): Select from the options: Ceramic Mug, Basket, Decorative Box, or Glass Vase.
* **Occasion** (`bouquet_occasion_type`, Required): Choose an occasion: Birthday, Anniversary, Get Well Soon, Thank You, Graduation, or Other.
* **Gift Message** (`bouquet_gift_message`, Optional): Add a personalized card message if desired.
* **Recipient Name** (`recipient_full_name`, Required): Enter the name of the recipient.
* **Recipient Phone** (`recipient_phone_number`, Required): Enter the recipient's phone number.
* **Delivery Date** (`bouquet_delivery_date`, Required): Select the delivery date for the bouquet.
* **Preferred Delivery Time** (`delivery_time_slot`, Required): Choose a time slot for delivery: Morning (9 AM-12 PM), Afternoon (12 PM-4 PM), or Evening (4 PM-7 PM).
* **Delivery Method** (`bouquet_delivery_method`, Required): Select from the options: Local Delivery ($10), Curbside Pickup (Free).
* **Payment Method** (`bouquet_payment_method`, Required): Choose from the options: Credit Card, PayPal, or Cash on Delivery.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_bouquet_instructions`, Optional): Enter any special instructions or requests for the bouquet.
* **Allergen Acknowledgment** (`allergen_acknowledgment`, Required): Confirm that you understand the potential allergens in the candies.
* **Order Date** (`final_order_timestamp`, Required): This field is automatically populated upon form submission.
* **I understand the ingredients may contain allergens** (`allergen_acknowledgment`, Required): This field is not displayed on the form but is required for submission. Please confirm you understand the potential allergens in the candies when submitting the form.
