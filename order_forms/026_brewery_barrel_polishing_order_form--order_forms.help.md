# Brewery Barrel Polishing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Brewery Barrel Polishing Order Form is used to gather information from breweries for the polishing of their barrels.

## How To Complete This Form
* Please ensure you have all necessary information and instructions before proceeding.
* Carefully review each field and provide accurate information.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Brewery or Company Name**: Enter your brewery's official name and address.
* **Contact Person Name**: Enter the name of the primary contact person for this order.
* **Email Address**: Provide your brewery's business email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your brewery's contact phone number.
* **Business Address**: Enter your complete business address for delivery and pickup.
* **Order Date**: Select the date for the order.
* **Number of Barrels to Polish**: Specify the number of barrels that require polishing.
* **Barrel Type**: Select the type of barrels you have (e.g., oak, stainless steel, French oak).
* **Barrel Size**: Choose the typical size of your barrels (e.g., 5 gallons, 10 gallons).
* **Current Barrel Condition**: Describe the current condition of the barrels (e.g., good condition with light oxidation).
* **Type of Polishing Needed**: Select the polishing service required (e.g., standard polish and restoration, deep cleaning, sanitization).
* **Service Priority**: Choose the level of service urgency (e.g., standard, expedited, emergency).
* **Preferred Service Date**: Select the desired start date for the service.
* **Pickup or Delivery**: Decide on the service option (e.g., pickup from brewery, delivery to brewery).
* **Barrel Transport Method**: Choose the method of transporting the barrels (e.g., our truck, your transportation).
* **Special Requirements or Instructions**: Specify any special handling or instructions.
* **Barrel Identification or Marks**: Provide any specific identification or marks on the barrels.
* **Preferred Polishing Materials**: Select the preferred materials to be used for polishing.
* **Request Volume Discount**: Inquire about bulk pricing for large orders.
* **Contract or Recurring Service**: Choose if you have ongoing polishing needs.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method (e.g., credit card, bank transfer, check).
* **Estimated Budget**: Specify your estimated budget range for the service.
