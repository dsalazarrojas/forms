# custom_cabinet_door_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create an order for a custom cabinet door. It gathers information from customers for the production and delivery of custom cabinet doors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the style of your custom cabinet door.
2. Select the details for your cabinet, including the color and type.
3. Provide the details for the door's sizing, including the hinge type.
4. Choose the delivery date and time for your custom cabinet door.
5. Select the payment method for your order.
6. Confirm your order details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Custom Cabinet Door Order Form** (`custom_cabinet_door_order_form`, text, required/optional): This is the title of the form.
* **Style Details** (`style_details`, select_one, required/optional): Select the style of your custom cabinet door. Options include `cabinet style`, `cabinet color`, and `cabinet type`.
* **Sizing Details** (`sizing_details`, text, required/optional): Provide the details for the door's sizing, including the hinge type.
* **Cabinet Style** (`cabinet_style`, select_one, required/optional): Select the style of your custom cabinet.
* **Cabinet Color** (`cabinet_color`, select_one, required/optional): Select the color of your custom cabinet.
* **Cabinet Type** (`cabinet_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of your custom cabinet.
* **Door Hinge Type** (`door_hinge_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the hinge type for your custom cabinet door.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required/optional): Choose the delivery date for your custom cabinet door.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required/optional): Choose the delivery time for your custom cabinet door.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required/optional): Select the payment method for your order. Options include `credit card`, `bank transfer`, and `paypal`.
* **Is Paid** (`is_paid`, text, required/optional): This field is not currently used in the form.
* **Is Saved** (`is_saved`, text, required/optional): This field is not currently used in the form.
* **Is Submitted** (`is_submitted`, text, required/optional): This field is not currently used in the form.
* **Is Confirmed** (`is_confirmed`, text, required/optional): This field is not currently used in the form.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select_one, required/optional): Select the delivery method for your custom cabinet door. Options include `pickup` and `delivery`.

Note: Some fields are not currently used in the form and can be removed from this guide.
