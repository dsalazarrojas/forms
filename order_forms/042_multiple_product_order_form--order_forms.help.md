# multiple_product_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "multiple_product_order_form" form is designed to collect information for multiple product orders. This form is used to gather details about customers' orders, including products chosen, shipping information, and other relevant details. It is essential to fill out the form accurately to ensure that orders are processed correctly and communicated effectively with customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary details and permissions to complete the form.
2. Fill in the "shipping" field with the correct shipping details.
3. In the "product_1", "product_2", etc., fields, select the products that the customer has chosen to order.
4. If a product is not ordered, select "No" in the corresponding field.
5. Enter the customer's email address in the "customer_email" field.
6. Enter any additional customer information or notes in the "order_notes" field.
7. If necessary, enter the customer's phone number in the "customer_phone" field.
8. In the "order_date" field, enter the date the order was received.
9. If necessary, enter the order time in the "order_time" field.
10. Enter the customer's name in the "customer_name" field.
11. Finally, review and submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **shipping (3)**: Enter the shipping details of the order, such as shipping address and method.
* **product_1 (1)**: Select the product the customer has chosen to order from the dropdown options.
* **product_2 (2)**: Select the product the customer has chosen to order from the dropdown options.
* **order_notes (4)**: Enter any additional information or notes about the order.
* **customer_email (5)**: Enter the customer's email address for communication and order tracking.
* **customer_phone (6)**: Enter the customer's phone number for further communication if needed.
* **customer_note (7)**: Enter any additional notes about the customer or order.
* **order_date (8)**: Enter the date the order was received.
* **order_time (9)**: Enter the time the order was received.
* **customer_name (10)**: Enter the customer's name for order tracking and communication.
* **total_cost (11)**: This field is currently not used in the current form schema and is intended for future use. Please ignore for now.
