# Colorful Cupcake Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Colorful Cupcake Order Form is used to place custom cupcake orders online. This form gathers necessary information from customers to facilitate order processing and fulfillment.

## How To Complete This Form
To place an order, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **Customer name** in the "Customer name" field. This is your full name as it appears on your order.
2. Provide your **Contact email** in the "Contact email" field. This will be used for confirmation and pickup details.
3. Choose your desired **Flavor selection** from the dropdown list. Options include Vanilla, Chocolate, Red velvet, Lemon, and Assorted.
4. Indicate the number of **Cupcakes required** in the text field.
5. If you have any **Decoration notes** or custom themes, enter them in the "Decoration notes" field. This is optional.
6. Choose your preferred **Pickup or delivery** option. You can select either "Pickup" or "Delivery".
7. If you have chosen delivery, provide your **Delivery address** in the text field.
8. Select your preferred **Preferred pickup or delivery date** in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
9. Finally, choose your **Payment method**. Options include Cash, Card on pickup, and Online payment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Cupcake Order --**: This is the form title, providing a brief description of the order form.
* **Customer name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your order.
* **Contact email**: Provide the email address you can be reached at for confirmation and pickup details.
* **Flavor selection**: Choose the desired flavor for your custom cupcake order.
* **Cupcake count**: Enter the number of cupcakes required for your order.
* **Decoration notes**: Provide any custom messages or themes for your cupcake decoration.
* **Pickup or delivery**: Choose whether you want to pickup your order or have it delivered.
* **Delivery address**: If you have chosen delivery, provide the address where you want your order delivered.
* **Preferred pickup or delivery date**: Select the date and time for pickup or delivery in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Payment method**: Choose how you will make payment for your order (Cash, Card on pickup, or Online payment).
