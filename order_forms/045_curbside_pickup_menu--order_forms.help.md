# Curbside Pickup Menu - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Curbside Pickup Menu form is used to create a menu that customers can view when they are picking up their orders at your store. This form helps you to easily add or remove items from the menu, and to update the pickup time and day information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). These fields are marked as "required" in the form.
2. Fill out the optional fields as needed.
3. Review your completed form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pickup Title** (`pickup_title`, text, optional): The title of the menu that will be displayed to customers.
* **Item Type** (`item_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of item you are offering, such as "Option 1" or "Option 2".
* **Item Name** (`item_name`, text, optional): The name of the item that will be available for curbside pickup.
* **Item Price** (`item_price`, number, optional): The price of the item.
* **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, select_one, optional): Select the time of day you will be offering the item for pickup, such as "Monday" or "Tuesday".
* **Pickup Day** (`pickup_day`, select_one, optional): Select the day of the week you will be offering the item for pickup, such as "Monday" or "Tuesday".
* **Pickup Time 2** (`pickup_time_2`, select_one, optional): Select the time of day you will be offering the item for pickup, such as "08:00" or "960".
* **Item Type 2** (`item_type_2`, select_one, required): Select the type of item 2, such as "Option 1" or "Option 2".
* **Item Name 2** (`item_name_2`, text, optional): The name of item 2.
 
## Tips
* If you are unsure about the format of the form, please refer to the form's title for guidance.
* Please make sure to review your completed form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are filled out and that each field is filled out only once.
