# Safety Barrier System Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safety Barrier System Order Form is used to gather information from customers for the purchase and installation of safety barrier systems. This form is designed to collect essential details about the customer, their order, and delivery preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your contact information, including your phone number and email address.
2. Provide your customer information, such as your name, company (if applicable), and address.
3. Fill in the order details, including the type of safety barrier system you are interested in, the delivery method, and the desired delivery date and time.
4. Specify any additional notes or comments you would like to add to your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Information
* **Contact Information**: Enter your phone number and email address to ensure we can contact you regarding your order.

### Customer Information
* **Customer Information**: Enter your name, company (if applicable), and address.
* **Customer Note**: Add any relevant notes or comments about your order.

### Order Details
* **Order Type**: Select the type of safety barrier system you are interested in (Safety Barrier, Gate, or Other).
* **Order Status**: Indicate the status of your order (Open, Closed, or Cancelled).
* **Order ID**: Enter your unique order ID.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date you would like to receive your safety barrier system.
* **Order Time**: Indicate the time you would like to receive your safety barrier system.

### Delivery Information
* **Delivery Method**: Choose how you would like to receive your safety barrier system (Courier or Pickup).
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the date you would like to receive your safety barrier system.
* **Delivery Time**: Indicate the time you would like to receive your safety barrier system.
* **Delivery Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about your delivery preferences.

### Other Information
* **Barriers**: Select the number of barriers you would like to order.
* **Customer Orders**: Enter any additional orders you would like to place.
* **Safety Barrier System ID**: Enter your unique safety barrier system ID.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Customer Address**: Enter your address.
* **Submit**: Once you have filled out all the required fields, click submit to complete your order.

## Tips
* Double-check all the information you enter carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at your preferred contact information.
* We will review and process your order promptly after receiving your submission.
