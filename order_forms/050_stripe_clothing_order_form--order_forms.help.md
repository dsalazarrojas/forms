# Stripe Clothing Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Stripe Clothing Order Form is a tool for customers to place an order for clothing items. This form collects customer contact information and payment method preferences to process the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the form to begin.
2. Select your preferred customer information (name, email, and phone number) using the dropdown menu.
3. Enter any additional order details in the text box.
4. Provide your shipping details in the text box.
5. Choose your preferred payment method (online or offline) using the dropdown menu.
6. Click on the submit button to finalize your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header** (`form_header`, text, not required): This is the title of the form that appears at the top of the page.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, select_multiple, not required): Select your preferred customer information (name, email, and phone number) using the dropdown menu. You can choose one or more of these options.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, note, not required): Enter any additional information about your order in this text box. For example, you can mention any special requests or notes about your order.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, not required): Enter your shipping details, such as your address or any specific delivery instructions.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, not required): Choose your preferred payment method (online or offline) using the dropdown menu. This will determine how your payment will be processed.
* **Form Footer** (`form_footer`, text, not required): This is the text at the bottom of the form that appears when you click submit.
