# Homework Help Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Homework Help Order Form is designed to collect information from educators for ordering homework help for students. It is intended for use in educational settings, such as universities, colleges, or online courses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your student's information, including their name and email.
2. Provide details about the homework assignment, including the subject and question.
3. Specify the assigned tool and its corresponding ID.
4. Add any additional comments or requests.
5. Review your entry carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Info** (`student_info`, text, optional): Enter your student's name, school ID, or any other relevant identifying information.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your student's full name.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter your student's valid email address.
* **Homework Details** (`homework_details`, note, optional): Provide additional information about the homework assignment, such as the course name, instructor's name, or assignment number.
* **Subject** (`subject`, text, required): Enter the subject of the homework assignment (e.g., math, science, history).
* **Question** (`question`, text, required): Enter the specific question or topic to be addressed in the homework assignment.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required): Specify the tool or resource assigned to the student for the homework assignment.
* **Tool ID** (`assigned_tool_id`, select_one, required): Select the specific tool or resource ID assigned to the student (e.g., chatjimmy, chatjimmy2, chatjimmy3).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or requests from the student or instructor.

## Tips
* Double-check the student's email address to ensure it is valid.
* Verify the subject and question are accurate to avoid confusion.
* Use the correct tool ID to ensure the student has access to the correct resource.
* If you have any issues or questions while using the form, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
