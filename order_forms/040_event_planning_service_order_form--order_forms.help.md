# event_planning_service_order_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for clients to provide details about themselves and their event planning service orders. It helps our team gather the necessary information to efficiently process and fulfill their orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the fields with the required information. Make sure to provide all the requested details to ensure our team can process your order correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Details**: Provide your name, contact information, and any other relevant details that will help us understand your needs.
  * Please fill in your name, email, and phone number to help us contact you about your order.
  * You can also add any additional comments or questions you have.
- **Event Details**: Share information about the event you are planning, such as the date, location, and number of attendees.
  * Describe the event you are planning, including the date, time, and location.
  * If you have any specific requirements or requests, please let us know.
- **Services**: Indicate which services you would like us to provide for your event.
  * Select all the services you need, such as event planning, coordination, or decorations.
  * You can also add any specific services not listed.
- **Service Orders**: Provide any additional information about your service orders.
  * Describe each service order separately, including the date and time it is scheduled for.
  * You can also add any notes or comments about each service order.
- **Payment Details**: Share any payment information related to your order.
  * You can enter any payment details, such as a deposit or payment method.
  * If you have any questions or concerns about payment, please let us know.
- **Order Notes**: Add any additional comments or questions you have about your order.
  * You can write any notes or comments you have about your order.
  * This is the last field, so review your information before submitting the form.
- **Assigned Tool**: This field is currently not used for this form.
  * This field is reserved for potential future use.
  * Please ignore it for now.
- **Form ID**: This is a unique identifier for the form.
  * This field is automatically generated and not editable by users.
  * Do not fill in this field.

## Tips
* Double-check all the information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you need help or have any questions, feel free to contact us.
* Our team will review and process your order promptly after receiving this form.
