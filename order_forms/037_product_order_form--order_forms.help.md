# Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Order Form is designed to collect information for product orders. It is intended for customers to provide details about the products they would like to order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the product name field with the name of the product you are interested in ordering.
2. Enter the quantity of the product you would like to order in the quantity field.
3. Enter the price of the product you would like to order in the price field.
4. Select the shipping date for your order in the shipping date field.
5. Choose the product category for your order from the product category field.
6. Add any additional product description if necessary in the product description field.
7. Enter your name in the customer name field.
8. Add any order notes in the order notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Name**: The product name field allows you to enter the name of the product you are ordering.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of units or items of the product you would like to order.
* **Price**: Enter the price of the product you are ordering.
* **Shipping Date**: Select a date for when you would like to have your order shipped.
* **Product Category**: Choose the category of the product from the dropdown list.
* **Product Description**: Enter any additional details about the product you are ordering.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as the customer placing the order.
* **Order Notes**: Add any additional comments or instructions about your order.
