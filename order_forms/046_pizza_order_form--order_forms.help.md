# Pizza Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pizza Order Form is used to collect information for a pizza order. Customers can specify their preferred toppings, pizza size, crust type, and additional toppings. The form also collects customer's contact information such as name, email, phone number, and address.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the desired toppings for your pizza. You can choose one or multiple options from the available toppings list.
2. Choose the size of the pizza you want to order. Options are Small (S), Medium (M), and Large (L).
3. Select the crust type for your pizza. Options are Thin and Thick.
4. Add any additional toppings you want to your pizza.
5. Enter your customer name.
6. Enter your customer email address.
7. Enter your customer phone number.
8. Enter your customer address.
9. Add any additional notes or instructions for your order if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Toppings** (`Select Multiple`): Choose one or multiple toppings from the list to add to your pizza. The available topping options are:
	+ Pepperoni
	+ Sausage
	+ Mushroom
	+ Onion
	+ Green Pepper
	+ Extra Cheese
	+ Anchovies
	+ Olive Oil
	+ Spicy Italian
* **Size** (`Select One`): Choose the size of the pizza you want to order. Options are:
	+ S: Small
	+ M: Medium
	+ L: Large
* **Crust** (`Select One`): Choose the type of crust for your pizza. Options are:
	+ Thin
	+ Thick
* **Additional Toppings** (`Select Multiple`): Add any additional toppings you want to your pizza. The available topping options are the same as in the Toppings field.
* **Customer Name** (`Text`): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer Email** (`Email`): Enter your valid email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`Text`): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`Text`): Enter your address for delivery or take-out purposes.
* **Notes** (`Note`): Add any additional notes or instructions for your order if needed.
