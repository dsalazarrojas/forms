# Liquor Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Liquor Order Form allows customers to select their preferred liquors and specify the quantities they'd like to order. This form is designed to be simple and easy to use, ensuring a smooth ordering experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred liquors from the list provided.
2. Enter the quantity you'd like for each selected liquor.
3. Click the "Submit" button to review and submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Liquor List** (`Liquor List`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose from the available liquors, such as Bourbon, Vodka, Whiskey, Rum, Gin, or Tequila.
* **Quantities** (`Quantities`, number, required: false): Enter the quantity for each selected liquor. Please note that this field will only appear if you have selected at least one liquor from the list.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, select_one, required: false): Review your order and select "Submit" to finalize your selection. You can also choose "Cancel" to go back and make changes.

## Tips

* Make sure to select at least one liquor from the list to proceed.
* Double-check your quantities to ensure accuracy.
* Review your order before submitting to avoid errors.
* If you have any issues or concerns, feel free to contact our support team for assistance.
