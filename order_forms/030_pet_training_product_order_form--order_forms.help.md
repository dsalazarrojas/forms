# Pet Training Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to place orders for pet training products. It gathers the necessary information for the order, including customer details, product selection, and order specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Name** in the text field provided.
2. Enter your **Customer Email** in the email field. Ensure it is a valid email address.
3. Select the **Product ID** of the product you wish to order from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter the **Product Name** of the product you selected in the text field provided.
5. Enter the **Quantity** you wish to order.
6. Enter the **Price** you are willing to pay for the product.
7. Fill in any additional **Order Notes** if you need to specify anything specific for your order.
8. Choose the **Select Products** you wish to order. Choose "Yes" for each product you wish to purchase and "No" for products you do not wish to purchase.
9. Confirm your order by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the **Confirm** field.
10. Select the **Date** for your order in the date field. Ensure it is a valid date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
11. Select the **Time** for your order in the time field. Ensure it is a valid time in the format 12:00 AM - 12:59 PM or 1:00 AM - 12:59 PM.
12. Review your form for accuracy and correctness before submitting. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name (1)**: Enter the name of the customer placing the order.
* **Customer Email (2)**: Enter a valid email address for the customer.
* **Product ID (3)**: Select the ID of the product from the dropdown menu.
* **Product Name (4)**: Enter the name of the product you selected.
* **Quantity (5)**: Enter the number of units of the product you wish to order.
* **Price (6)**: Enter the price you are willing to pay for the product.
* **Order Notes (7)**: Provide any additional information about your order, if necessary.
* **Select Products (8)**: Choose "Yes" to select each product you wish to purchase and "No" for products you do not wish to purchase.
* **Confirm (9)**: Confirm your order by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Date (10)**: Select a valid date for your order in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time (11)**: Select a valid time for your order in the format 12:00 AM - 12:59 PM or 1:00 AM - 12:59 PM.
