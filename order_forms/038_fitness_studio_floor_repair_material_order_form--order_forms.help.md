# Fitness Studio Floor Repair Material Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Fitness Studio Floor Repair Material Order Form" is used to collect information about the maintenance and repair needs of a fitness studio's floor materials. This form helps the studio to order new materials and coordinate the delivery process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, select whether the form is for a "Gym" or "Studio".
2. Enter your name, phone number, and email address for contact purposes.
3. Select the delivery method you prefer (if applicable).
4. Specify the quantity of materials you need to order.
5. Choose a delivery date and delivery time for your order.
6. Provide any additional delivery instructions or order details in the "Order Details" field.
7. If applicable, select which tool is assigned to handle your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gym or Studio** (Select)
	+ Choose whether this form is for a fitness studio or a gym.
* **Name** (Text)
	+ Enter your name for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (Text)
	+ Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (Email)
	+ Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Delivery Method** (Select)
	+ Select whether you prefer in-house or external delivery services.
* **Quantity Ordered** (Number)
	+ Specify the quantity of materials you need to order.
* **Delivery Date** (Date)
	+ Choose a date for delivery of the materials.
* **Delivery Time** (Time)
	+ Choose a time for delivery of the materials.
* **Delivery Instructions** (Text)
	+ Provide any additional instructions for the delivery of the materials.
* **Order Details** (Text)
	+ Enter any additional details about your order.
* **Assigned Tool** (Text)
	+ If applicable, select which tool is assigned to handle your order.

## Tips
* Ensure that you select the correct delivery method and quantity of materials needed.
* Be sure to enter accurate contact information for delivery purposes.
* If you have any special delivery instructions or details, make sure to include them in the "Order Details" field.
* If you have a preference for a specific delivery time, choose the appropriate date and time for delivery.
