# Image Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for placing an order for an image or a set of images. It's a straightforward and user-friendly tool to gather the necessary details required for a successful image ordering process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the following fields with the required information:
	* `image_details`: Provide a brief description or details about the image you want to order.
	* `contact_details`: Enter your name, email, and any other contact information for communication.
	* `order_details`: Specify the order details, including size, resolution, and other relevant specifications.
	* `payment_info`: Share the payment information, such as payment method and amount.
	* `review_request`: If you have any specific requirements or requests for the review process, mention them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **image_details**: Enter a brief description or details about the image you want to order. This will help the service provider understand what you're looking for.
- **contact_details**: Please provide your name and email address for smooth communication during the image ordering process.
- **order_details**: Specify the order details such as the image size, resolution, and any other specifications you may have. This ensures the service provider delivers the correct product according to your needs.
- **payment_info**: Share the payment information, including the payment method and amount. This helps the service provider process your order accurately.
- **review_request**: If you have any specific requirements or requests for the review process, mention them here. This helps the service provider understand your expectations and deliver a satisfactory product.
