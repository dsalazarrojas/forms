# herbs_and_foods_crop_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers place orders with our agricultural business for herbs, vegetables, fruits, and other crops.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the type of crops you wish to purchase (Herbs, Vegetables, Fruits) and select all applicable options.
2. Enter the quantities of each selected crop.
3. Provide order details in the text field.
4. Select your preferred customer information (John Doe, Jane Doe, or Other).
5. Choose your preferred payment method (Cash, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer) and note that this is optional.
6. Select your delivery preference (Same Day, Next Day, or Later).
7. Indicate if you have any additional items to add to your order (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **crops** (Herbs, Vegetables, Fruits): Select all the types of crops you wish to purchase.
* **quantities**: Enter the quantity of each selected crop.
* **order_details**: Enter any additional details about your order.
* **customer_info**: Select your name for our records.
* **payment_info**: Select your preferred payment method (optional).
* **delivery_info**: Choose your delivery preference.
* **submit_info**: Indicate if you have additional items to add to your order (optional).
