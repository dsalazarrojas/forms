# Custom Bakery Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Bakery Order Form is designed to collect information from customers for their custom bakery orders. This form will guide you through the process of submitting your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Customer Info section with your name, address, and contact details.
2. In the Delivery Info section, provide your delivery address and any special delivery instructions.
3. In the Order Details section, specify the type of order you would like to place (e.g., cake, cookies, etc.) and any custom requests.
4. Finally, in the Payment Info section, enter your payment method and details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Info
* **Customer Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please fill in your name as you would like to be referred to.
* **Customer Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your residential or commercial address where we can deliver your order.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Provide your phone number so we can contact you for any order updates.
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, required): Enter your email address for order confirmations and updates.

### Delivery Info
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address where you would like your order delivered.
* **Special Delivery Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `text`, optional): If you have any specific delivery instructions, please provide them here.

### Order Details
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, `text`, required): Specify the type of order you would like to place (e.g., cake, cookies, etc.).
* **Custom Requests** (`custom_requests`, `text`, optional): If you have any special custom requests, please provide them here.

### Payment Info
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `text`, required): Please select your preferred payment method (e.g., credit card, bank transfer, etc.).
* **Card Number** (`card_number`, `text`, optional): If using a credit card, please enter the card number.
* **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, `text`, optional): If using a credit card, enter the expiration date.
