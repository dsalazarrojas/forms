# parcel_delivery_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for the shipping of parcels or packages. It's designed to gather essential details from customers or users to facilitate the delivery process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the information for "Shipping From" - This is the origin of the shipment, where the parcel is being sent from.
2. Enter the information for "Shipping To" - This is the destination of the shipment, where the parcel is being sent to.
3. Select the "Ship Date" for the parcel's delivery. Please use the date format as prompted.
4. Enter the "Shipping Address" of the parcel's destination. This includes the street address, house number, and any additional information that might be required.
5. Enter the "City" where the parcel will be delivered.
6. Enter the "State" where the parcel will be delivered.
7. Enter the "Zip" or postal code of the delivery location.
8. Enter the "Country" of the delivery location.
9. Enter the contact "Email" for the recipient or delivery person.
10. Enter the contact "Phone" number for the recipient or delivery person.
11. Provide any additional "Shipping Instructions" that might be necessary for the delivery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shipping From** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the address of where the parcel is being shipped from.
* **Shipping To** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the address of where the parcel is being shipped to.
* **Ship Date** (Date, Required/Optional): Select the date on which the parcel is to be delivered.
* **Shipping Address** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the detailed address of the destination where the parcel will be delivered, including street name, house number, and any other relevant details.
* **City** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the city name where the parcel is to be delivered.
* **State** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the state or province where the parcel is to be delivered.
* **Zip** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the zip or postal code of the delivery location.
* **Country** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the country where the parcel is to be delivered.
* **Email** (Email, Required/Optional): Enter the contact email for the recipient or delivery person.
* **Phone** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the contact phone number for the recipient or delivery person.
* **Shipping Instructions** (Text, Required/Optional): Provide any special instructions that might be necessary for the delivery process.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately to avoid any issues with delivery.
- If you're unsure about any field, you can leave it blank or contact the shipping service for clarification.
- Always review your completed form carefully before submitting it.
