# 2021 Holiday Collection Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 2021 Holiday Collection Pre Order Form is a tool for customers to pre-order items from the holiday collection. It captures customer information, product details, and order preferences to ensure a smooth and efficient pre-ordering experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's full name in the "Full name" field.
2. Enter a valid email address in the "Email address" field to receive order confirmations.
3. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone number" field.
4. Provide the customer's billing address, including street, city, state/province, and postal code.
5. Select the date the pre-order is placed in the "Order date" field.
6. Enter the product title exactly as advertised in the "Item or product name" field.
7. If you have a supplier SKU, enter it in the "Item SKU or code" field.
8. Enter the quantity of units to pre-order.
9. Enter the unit price per single unit in USD.
10. The estimated total price will be calculated automatically, but if you need to make manual adjustments, enter the estimated total price.
11. If a discount code is applicable, enter it in the "Discount or promo code" field.
12. Choose the preferred delivery method from the available options.
13. If you have chosen shipping or local delivery, provide the delivery address.
14. Select the preferred date for delivery or pickup.
15. If required, enter special instructions or customization notes in the "Special instructions or customization notes" field.
16. Choose the payment method that suits your needs.
17. Select any additional fulfillment options required.
18. Agree to the pre-order terms, deposit, and estimated ship windows.
19. If you have any additional comments or notes, enter them in the "Additional comments or notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: Enter the customer's legal name.
* **Email address**: Enter a valid email address for order confirmations.
* **Phone number**: Enter the customer's phone number (including country code if outside domestic region).
* **Billing address**: Enter the customer's billing address, including street, city, state/province, and postal code.
* **Order date**: Select the date the pre-order is placed.
* **Item or product name**: Enter the product title exactly as advertised.
* **Item SKU or code**: Enter the supplier SKU (optional).
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of units to pre-order.
* **Unit price**: Enter the price per single unit in USD (including decimals).
* **Estimated total price**: The estimated total price will be calculated automatically, but if you need to make manual adjustments, enter the estimated total price.
* **Discount or promo code**: Enter any applicable discount code.
* **Preferred delivery method**: Choose how you'd like to receive the order.
* **Delivery address**: Enter the address where the order will be delivered (required for shipping or local delivery).
* **Requested delivery date**: Select the preferred date for delivery or pickup.
* **Special instructions or customization notes**: Enter any special instructions or customization notes (gift messages, engraving text, or packaging requests).
* **Payment method**: Choose the payment method that suits your needs.
* **Additional fulfillment options**: Select any additional services required (gift wrap, rush fulfillment, signature on delivery, Saturday delivery, consolidated shipping).
* **I acknowledge the pre-order terms, deposit and estimated ship windows**: The customer must agree to the pre-order terms before proceeding.
* **Additional comments or notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes not captured above.
