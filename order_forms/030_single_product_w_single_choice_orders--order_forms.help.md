# Single Product W Single Choice Orders - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Single Product W Single Choice Orders" form is used for placing orders for a single product with a single-choice payment method. This form gathers basic customer information, order details, and payment methods for processing single-product orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*).
2. Enter your customer information, including name, email, phone number, and shipping address.
3. Select the payment method you prefer (e.g., Payment by Card, Payment by Bank, or Payment by Cash).
4. Choose the order status (e.g., Pending, Confirmed, Rejected, Cancelled, Finished, Paid, Shipping, Failed, Lost).
5. Select the shipping method (e.g., Pickup, Shipping, Express Shipping).
6. Enter the order quantity, price, and total.
7. Fill in the customer shipping address and phone number (if different from the main address and phone number).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Single Product W Single Choice Orders
* **Single Product W Single Choice Orders** (`single_product_w_single_choice_orders_form`): This is the title of the form.
* **Single Product ID** (`single_product_id`): Enter the ID of the single product you want to order.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Customer Note** (`customer_note`): Enter any additional comments or information about your order.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`): Enter your address for delivery purposes.
* **Customer Shipping Address** (`customer_shipping_address`): If different from your main address, enter your shipping address.
* **Customer Shipping Phone** (`customer_shipping_phone`): If different from your main phone number, enter your shipping address phone number.
* **Customer Shipping Note** (`customer_shipping_note`): Enter any additional comments or information about your shipping.
* **Order Quantity** (`order_quantity`): Enter the number of products you want to order.
* **Order Price** (`order_price`): Enter the total price of the order.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`): This will be calculated automatically based on the order price and quantity.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select the method you prefer for payment (Payment by Card, Payment by Bank, or Payment by Cash).
* **Customer ID** (`customer_id`): Select one or more customer IDs from the dropdown list.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`): Choose the status of your order (e.g., Pending, Confirmed, Rejected, Cancelled, Finished, Paid, Shipping, Failed, Lost).
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`): Choose the method for shipping (e.g., Pickup, Shipping, Express Shipping).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately to avoid delays in processing your order.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact us for assistance.
* Review your order details carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
