# Port Departure Tax Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Port Departure Tax Payment Form is used to facilitate the payment of taxes for vessels departing from a port. This form serves as a means to capture essential information and payment details for such payments.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  Enter your voyage details (yes or no).
3.  Select the method of payment (online payment, bank transfer, or bank draft).
4.  Enter the payment date.
5.  Enter the payment amount.
6.  Enter any notes or comments (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Port Departure Tax Payment** (`port_departure_tax_payment_form`, text, **required**): Enter the payment for taxes for your vessel.
*   **Voyage Details** (`port_voyage_details`, select_one, **required**): Select whether your voyage has details or not.
*   **Payment Method** (`port_payment_method`, select_one, **required**): Choose how you want to pay for your port departure tax, which is either online, by bank transfer, or by bank draft.
*   **Payment Date** (`port_payment_date`, date, **required**): Enter the date you want to make the payment on.
*   **Payment Amount** (`port_payment_amount`, number, **required**): Fill out the amount of tax you want to pay for your port departure.
*   **Notes** (`port_notes`, note, **optional**): Provide any additional comments or notes about your payment for the port departure tax.

## Tips

-   Please make sure to fill the form accurately to avoid any processing issues.
-   Ensure that your payment method is compatible with your selected payment option.
-   If you are unsure about any field or payment method, contact your port administration for guidance.

This help guide is intended to make it easy to complete the form and ensure a smooth payment experience for your vessel.
