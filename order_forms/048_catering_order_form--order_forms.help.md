# Catering Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Catering Order Form is designed to gather information from customers to process their catering order efficiently. This guide will walk you through each step of the form, so you can complete it accurately and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name, email address, and phone number in the Customer Contact section.
2. Select the date of the event and choose a delivery or pickup option in the Event Details section.
3. Choose your preferred payment method and add any additional comments or instructions in the Additional Comments or Instructions section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (Contact Name, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (Contact Email, required): Enter your email address. We will use this to send you order confirmations and updates.
* **Phone Number** (Contact Phone, required): Enter your phone number. We will use this for delivery confirmations and any follow-up inquiries.
* **Date of Event** (Event Date, required): Select the date of your event.
* **Delivery or Pickup** (Delivery Method, required): Choose between delivery to your location or pickup from our kitchen. If you choose delivery, provide your delivery address.
* **Delivery Address** (Delivery Address, optional): If you selected delivery, enter your delivery address here.
* **Requested Time** (Requested Time, required): Choose a time for delivery or pickup.
* **Select Food Items** (Products Selected, required): Choose the food items you'd like to order. You can select multiple items.
* **Quantity for each selected item** (Quantity for each selected item, required): Enter the quantity for each selected item.
* **Special Dietary Requests** (Special Dietary Requests, optional): If you have any special dietary requests, such as nut-free or vegan options, enter them here.
* **Do you need disposable plates and cutlery** (Disposable Ware Needed, required): Choose whether you need disposable plates and cutlery for your event.
* **Add Beverage Package** (Drinks Package, optional): Choose a beverage package to add to your order, if desired.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (Preferred Payment Method, required): Choose your preferred payment method. We will send an invoice after reviewing your order.
* **Additional Comments or Instructions** (Additional Comments or Instructions, optional): Enter any additional comments or instructions for our staff.

## Tips
* Make sure to select all the food items you'd like to order and enter the corresponding quantities.
* If you have any special dietary requests, please let us know so we can accommodate your needs.
* If you need any additional services, such as disposable plates and cutlery, please select the option to include it in your order.
* Our staff will review your order and send you a confirmation with details. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
