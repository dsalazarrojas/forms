# Freight Forwarder Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Freight Forwarder Referral Form is a tool used to refer your business partners, shippers, or other relevant entities to a freight forwarder's services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the requested information. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the business or individual you are referring.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Shipper Name** (Shipper Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the shipper (if applicable).
* **Shipper Reference Id** (Shipper Reference Id, text, optional): Enter the shipper reference id (if applicable).
* **Shipper Address** (Shipper Address, text, optional): Enter the address of the shipper (if applicable).
* **Shipper Phone** (Shipper Phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the shipper (if applicable).
* **Business Name** (Business Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the business (if applicable).
* **Business Reference Id** (Business Reference Id, text, optional): Enter the business reference id (if applicable).
* **Business Contact Name** (Business Contact Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person (if applicable).
* **Business Address** (Business Address, text, optional): Enter the address of the business (if applicable).
* **Business Phone** (Business Phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the business (if applicable).
* **Comments** (Comments, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information about the referral.
* **Referral Type** (Referral Type, select_one, optional): Select the type of referral (Shipper, Forwarder, Brokerage).
* **I have read and agree to the terms and conditions** (Terms, select_one, required): Select "True" to confirm you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
