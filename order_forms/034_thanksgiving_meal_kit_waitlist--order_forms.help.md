# thanksgiving_meal_kit_waitlist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers who want to be notified when a specific type of meal kit becomes available. Please provide your contact information to be added to our waitlist.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name.
2. Provide your email address so we can notify you when your preferred meal kit is available.
3. If you have a phone number, please enter it so we can contact you.
4. Optionally, provide any additional notes or comments.
5. Select the type of meal kit you are interested in (Thanksgiving Feast, Holiday Meal Kit, or Dinner for 2).
6. Choose one or more meal kits that interest you (Roasted Turkey, Baked Ham, Veggie Options, or Thanksgiving Favorites).
7. Enter the date you would like the meal kit for.
8. Select the time of day you would like the meal kit to arrive at.
9. (Optional) Add any additional notes or comments about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name so we can contact you.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can notify you when your preferred meal kit is available.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it so we can contact you.
* **Notes/Comments** (`notes`, note, optional): Any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Meal Type** (`meal_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of meal kit you are interested in: 
  * Thanksgiving Feast
  * Holiday Meal Kit
  * Dinner for 2
* **Meal Kit** (`meal_kit`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more meal kits that interest you:
  * Roasted Turkey
  * Baked Ham
  * Veggie Options
  * Thanksgiving Favorites
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like the meal kit for.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time of day you would like the meal kit to arrive at.
* **Additional Notes/Comments** (`additional_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your order.

## Tips
Please note that this form is for customers who want to be notified when a specific type of meal kit becomes available. Please ensure that your email and phone number are accurate so we can contact you.
