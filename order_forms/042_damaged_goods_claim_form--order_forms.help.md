# damaged_goods_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Damaged Goods Claim Form is used to report any issues with a product from a customer's order. This form helps us to efficiently process claims and resolve any problems with the product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Order ID, which is a unique identifier assigned to your order.
2. Select the Product ID of the item that is damaged or defective.
3. Provide a detailed description of the issue with the product.
4. Choose whether an image or attachment is required to support your claim.
5. Enter your name and email address so we can contact you about your claim.
6. Enter your phone number and a message to communicate with you about your claim.
7. Choose the date you received your order and the date it was delivered.
8. Select the condition of the product as damaged or not.
9. Choose whether the shipping carrier and method were correct.
10. Select the status of the product as active or inactive.
11. Choose whether the reason for the refund is related to the product or not.
12. Enter the amount you are requesting as a refund.
13. Enter the amount you have already paid.
14. Select the Order Item IDs of the products that have issues.
15. Enter a description of the product, if necessary.
16. Enter a message to communicate with us about your claim.
17. Enter your phone number, if you want to be contacted.
18. Enter your email address, if you want to be contacted via email.
19. Choose whether a specific tool is assigned to resolve the issue.
20. Click "Submit" to submit your claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order ID (1)**: Enter a unique identifier assigned to your order.
* **Product ID (2)**: Select the Product ID of the item with the issue.
* **Describe the issue (3)**: Provide a detailed description of the problem with the product.
* **Image or attachment (4)**: Choose whether to attach an image or document to support your claim.
* **Claimant Name (5)**: Enter your name so we can contact you about your claim.
* **Email Address (6)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your claim.
* **Phone (7)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your claim.
* **Message (8)**: Enter a message to communicate with us about your claim.
* **Order Date (9)**: Enter the date you received your order.
* **Delivery Date (10)**: Enter the date your order was delivered.
* **Product Condition (11)**: Select the condition of the product as damaged or not.
* **Shipping Carrier (12)**: Choose whether the shipping carrier and method were correct.
* **Product Status (13)**: Select the status of the product as active or inactive.
* **Reason for Refund (14)**: Choose whether the reason for the refund is related to the product or not.
* **Refund Amount (15)**: Enter the amount you are requesting as a refund.
* **Amount Paid (16)**: Enter the amount you have already paid.
* **Order Item IDs (17)**: Select the Order Item IDs of the products that have issues.
* **Product Description (18)**: Enter a description of the product, if necessary.
* **Message (19)**: Enter a message to communicate with us about your claim.
* **Customer Phone (20)**: Enter your phone number, if you want to be contacted.
* **Customer Email (21)**: Enter your email address, if you want to be contacted via email.
* **Assigned Tool (22)**: Choose whether a specific tool is assigned to resolve the issue.

Note: This form is for the purpose of efficiently processing claims for damaged or defective products. Completing this form will help us understand your issue and provide the best possible solution for your claim. Please be as detailed as possible in providing your description and information.
