# Plant Based Milk Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Plant Based Milk Order Form is a digital form designed to collect information from customers for their plant-based milk orders. The form allows users to select their preferred type of milk, specify the quantity they want, choose their delivery options, and provide any additional notes or instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of milk you'd like to order from the "Select milk type" dropdown menu.
2. Choose the quantity of milk you want to order using the quantity input field.
3. Select your preferred delivery options from the "Delivery options" dropdown menu (e.g., home delivery, office pickup, or store pickup).
4. Enter your address, city, and zip code to facilitate delivery or pickup (if applicable).
5. Provide your phone number for communication purposes.
6. Enter your email address for communication and order confirmation.
7. Select a date and time for delivery or pickup (if applicable).
8. Add any additional notes or instructions in the "Order note" field.
9. Confirm your order by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select milk type**: Choose the type of plant-based milk you'd like to order (almond, oat, or coconut).
* **Quantity**: Enter the amount of milk you'd like to order.
* **Delivery options**: Select one or more of the delivery options: home delivery, office pickup, or store pickup.
* **Address**: Enter your address for delivery or pickup purposes.
* **City**: Enter the city where you'd like to receive your order.
* **State**: Enter the state where you'd like to receive your order.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code for accurate delivery or pickup.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication and order confirmation.
* **Date**: Select a date for delivery or pickup (if applicable).
* **Time**: Select a time for delivery or pickup (if applicable).
* **Note**: Add any additional notes or instructions about your order.
* **Order note**: Enter any additional comments or instructions about your order.
* **Home delivery**: Select this option if you'd like to receive your order at your home.
* **Store pickup**: Select this option if you'd like to pick up your order at a store.
* **Office pickup**: Select this option if you'd like to pick up your order at our office.
* **Weekdays**: Select this option if you'd like to receive your order on weekdays (Monday to Friday).
* **Weekend**: Select this option if you'd like to receive your order on weekends (Saturday and Sunday).
* **Delivery time**: Select a time for delivery or pickup (if applicable).
* **Delivery address**: Enter the address where you'd like to receive your order.
* **Delivery phone**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Delivery email**: Enter your email address for order confirmation and communication.
* **Confirmation**: Add any additional comments or instructions about your order confirmation.

Note that some fields are not required, but it's best to provide as much information as possible to ensure accurate and smooth processing of your order.
