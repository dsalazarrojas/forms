# Lockdown Nutrition Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for ordering food products from customers during a lockdown. It collects the customer's name, email, phone number, selected products, order date, time, and any notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Customer Name** in the text field provided.
2. Enter your **Customer Email** in the format of a valid email address.
3. Enter your **Customer Phone** in the format of a phone number.
4. Select the products you would like to order from the dropdown list. You can select multiple products.
5. Enter the name of the product you have chosen in the **Product Name** text field.
6. Enter the price of the product in the **Product Price** field.
7. Select the date for your order from the date picker.
8. Select the time for your order from the time picker.
9. Add any additional notes about your order in the **Notes** text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Your name is required to be associated with your order.
* **Customer Email**: Enter a valid email address to allow us to contact you about your order.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Product List**: Select the products you would like to order. You can select multiple products.
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product you have chosen to order.
* **Product Price**: Enter the price of the product. This is required for payment processing.
* **Order Date**: Select the date you would like to have your order fulfilled.
* **Order Time**: Select the time you would like to have your order fulfilled.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or instructions about your order.
