# Facemask Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Facemask Order Form is used to collect information from customers for the purpose of placing an order for facemasks. This form is used to gather customer details, delivery information, and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Customer Name`, `Customer Email`, and `Delivery Address` fields with the customer's contact information.
2. Enter the `Delivery City` and `Delivery Postal Code` for the delivery location.
3. Provide the `Customer Phone` number for the customer.
4. Specify the `Delivery Date` and `Delivery Time` for the order delivery.
5. Enter the `Total Items` and `Total Price` for the order.
6. Choose the `Payment Method` by selecting either `Credit Card`, `PayPal`, or `Bank Transfer`.
7. Select the `Assigned Tool` by choosing either `chatjimmy` or `other`.
8. (Optional) Enter any `Customer Note` to provide additional information to the customer.
9. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name (Customer Name)** (`customer_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the customer's name as you would like it to be displayed on the order.
* **Customer Email (Customer Email)** (`customer_email`, Email, Optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Delivery Address (Delivery Address)** (`delivery_address`, Text, Optional): Enter the delivery address where the order will be delivered.
* **Delivery City (Delivery City)** (`delivery_city`, Text, Optional): Enter the city for the delivery location.
* **Delivery Postal Code (Delivery Postal Code)** (`delivery_postal_code`, Text, Optional): Enter the postal code for the delivery location.
* **Customer Phone (Customer Phone)** (`customer_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Delivery Date (Delivery Date)** (`delivery_date`, Date, Optional): Specify the date for the order delivery.
* **Delivery Time (Delivery Time)** (`delivery_time`, Time, Optional): Specify the time for the order delivery.
* **Total Items (Total Items)** (`total_items`, Number, Optional): Enter the total number of items in the order.
* **Total Price (Total Price)** (`total_price`, Number, Optional): Enter the total price of the order.
* **Payment Method (Payment Method)** (`payment_method`, Select One, Required): Choose the payment method for the order, either `Credit Card`, `PayPal`, or `Bank Transfer`.
* **Assigned Tool (Assigned Tool)** (`assigned_tool`, Select One, Required): Choose the tool assigned to the order, either `chatjimmy` or `other`.
* **Notes (Customer Note)** (`customer_note`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional notes for the customer.
* **Notes (Notes)** (`notes`, Text, Optional): This field is not actually used in the form and can be removed.
