# Nutrition Supplement Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a simple order form for customers to place orders for nutrition supplements. It includes fields for customer information, order details, and payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Customer Name** field with your full name.
2. Enter your **Customer Email** address to ensure we can contact you about your order.
3. Provide your **Customer Phone** number in case we need to reach you.
4. Select the desired **Supplement Type** from the list provided.
5. Enter the **Order Date** in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
6. Enter your **Delivery Address** where you would like to receive your order.
7. Enter the **Quantity** of the supplement you would like to order.
8. If applicable, add any **Special Instructions** regarding your order.
9. Select your preferred **Payment Method** (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
10. If you have any **Customer Message**, feel free to add it here.
11. If you have a **Billing Address** that is different from your shipping address, please provide it here.
12. Enter your **Shipping Address** where you would like to receive your order.
13. Enter the **Total Amount** of your order.
14. Select your **Payment Status** (Paid, Pending, or Refunded).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: The name of the customer placing the order. Please fill out your full name.
* **Customer Email**: The email address where we can contact you about your order.
* **Customer Phone**: The phone number where we can reach you if needed.
* **Supplement Type**: The type of supplement you are interested in purchasing (Supplement 1, Supplement 2, Supplement 3).
* **Order Date**: The date you would like to receive your order (format YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Delivery Address**: The address where you would like to receive your order.
* **Quantity**: The amount of the supplement you would like to order.
* **Special Instructions**: Any special requests or instructions you have regarding your order.
* **Payment Method**: The method you would like to use to pay for your order (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
* **Customer Message**: Any message you would like to include with your order.
* **Billing Address**: The address where your payment will be billed (only if different from shipping address).
* **Shipping Address**: The address where you would like to receive your order.
* **Total Amount**: The total amount of your order.
* **Payment Status**: The status of your payment (Paid, Pending, or Refunded).

## Tips
Please ensure that you have filled out all required fields and that your order details are accurate. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
