# Shipping Service Drop Off Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shipping Service Drop Off Check In Form is used by employees to check in packages at a shipping service location. This form is meant to gather information about the packages being dropped off, including package details and shipper contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the "Package Info" page to choose the package details.
2.  Fill out the package weight, length, and width fields.
3.  Select the shipper's email and phone number from the dropdown options.
4.  Fill out the drop off date and time fields.
5.  Enter any additional drop off notes in the "Drop Off Note" field.
6.  Review and submit the form to complete the check-in process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shipping Service Drop Off Check In** (text): This is the title of the form that will be displayed on the page.
* **Package Info** (select multiple):
	+ This field allows you to select the package details.
	+ Options:
		- 1
		- 2
* **Ship From Location** (text): Enter the location from which the package is being shipped from.
* **Ship To** (text): Enter the location to which the package is being shipped.
* **Drop Off Date** (date): Enter the date on which the package is being dropped off.
* **Drop Off Time** (time): Enter the time of drop off.
* **Drop Off Note** (note): Enter any additional notes about the drop off process.
* **Shipper Email** (email): Enter the shipper's email address.
* **Shipper Phone** (text): Enter the shipper's phone number.
* **Package Weight** (number): Enter the weight of the package.
* **Package Length** (number): Enter the length of the package.
* **Package Width** (number): Enter the width of the package.
