# Yoga Mat Customization Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help customers place orders for custom-made yoga mats. The form collects information on the customer's details, order preferences, and payment details to process the order accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Name** (`customer_name`) in the text field.
2. Enter your **Email** (`customer_email`) in the format `example@domain.com`.
3. Select a **Order Date** (`order_date`) for your order.
4. Choose your desired **Design Color** (`design_color`) from the drop-down list: Blue, Red, Green, Yellow, or Purple.
5. Select the **Size** (`size`) you would like for your mat in the text field. Note: This is a numerical input, so you will need to enter a number.
6. Select one or more **Shape** (`shape`) options from the drop-down list: Square, Circle, Oval.
7. Choose your preferred **Material** (`material`) options from the drop-down list: Eco-friendly, Synthetic, Hybrid.
8. Enter the **Amount** (`amount`) of mats you would like to order.
9. Select your preferred **Payment Method** (`payment_method`) from the drop-down list: Cash, Credit Card, PayPal.
10. Choose your **Payment Status** (`payment_status`) from the drop-down list: Approved, Pending, Refused.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Enter your full name to help us process your order accurately.
* **Email** (`customer_email`): Enter your email address to stay updated on your order status.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`): Select a date for your order, this will help us to schedule your order processing.
* **Design Color** (`design_color`): Choose your desired color for your custom-made mat.
* **Size** (`size`): Enter the size of the mat you would like to order in a numerical value.
* **Shape** (`shape`): Select one or more shapes for your custom-made mat.
* **Material** (`material`): Choose your preferred material for your mat.
* **Amount** (`amount`): Enter the quantity of mats you would like to order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select how you would like to pay for your order.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`): Choose the status of your payment, this will help us to process your order accurately.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate order processing.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
