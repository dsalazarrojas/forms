# thank_you_insert_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback or comments from customers about their order experience. It allows customers to provide details about their order, contact information, feedback, and order status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your order details in the **Order Details** field.
2. Provide your customer contact information in the **Customer Contact Info** field.
3. Share any feedback or comments about your experience with us in the **Feedback** field.
4. Select the current status of your order from the **Order Status** dropdown menu.
5. Choose the type of media you'd like to attach to your order from the **Order Photos** dropdown menu.
6. Enter your email address in the **Email** field (e.g. example@example.com).
7. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field (e.g. 123-456-1234).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details** (text, required: false): Enter a brief description of your order.
* **Customer Contact Info** (text, required: false): Enter your contact information, such as name, address, and phone number.
* **Feedback** (text, required: false): Share any feedback or comments about your experience with us. This is a good place to mention any issues you had with your order or suggestions for improvement.
* **Order Status** (select_one, required: false): Choose the current status of your order from the following options:
	+ Not Started
	+ In Progress
	+ Completed
* **Order Photos** (select_multiple, required: false): Choose the type of media you'd like to attach to your order from the following options:
	+ Attach Photos
	+ Attach Videos
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter your email address in the format example@example.com.
* **Phone** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number in the format 123-456-1234.
