# Advertising Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Advertising Order Form is used to collect information from clients about their advertising needs and preferences. This form should only be filled out if you are interested in placing an order for advertising services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Client / Company Name** in the "Client / Company Name" field. This is the name of the company or individual you represent.
2. Enter your **Contact Person** in the "Contact Person" field. This is the person who will be in charge of the advertising order.
3. Enter your **Email Address** in the "Email Address" field. This is the email address we will use to contact you.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field. This is the phone number we will use to contact you.
5. Choose your **Select Service Package** from the options provided. Choose a package that best suits your needs.
6. Select any **Additional Services** you would like to add to your order. This will include any extra services you need, such as graphic design or copywriting.
7. Choose your **Preferred Ad Placement** from the options provided. This is where you would like your ads to appear.
8. Enter the **Campaign Duration** in weeks. This is how long you would like the advertising campaign to run.
9. Enter your **Total Budget** for this order. This is the total amount you are willing to pay for the advertising services.
10. Enter your **Target Start Date** for the campaign. This is when you would like the campaign to start.
11. Choose your **Payment Method** from the options provided. This is how you would like to pay for the services.
12. If you have any **Special Instructions**, enter them in the "Special Instructions" field. This is any additional information you would like to provide to our team.
