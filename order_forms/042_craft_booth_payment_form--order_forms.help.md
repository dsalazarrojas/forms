# Craft Booth Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record and manage payment information for craft booth transactions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the craft booth ID in the "craft_booth_id" field.
2. Enter the order ID in the "order_id" field.
3. Select the payment method from the "payment_method_id" dropdown menu.
4. Enter the amount of the transaction in the "amount" field.
5. Enter the tax rate in the "tax_rate" field.
6. Calculate the tax amount by multiplying the amount by the tax rate.
7. Enter the tax amount in the "tax_amount" field.
8. Enter the total amount in the "total" field.
9. Add any additional notes about the transaction in the "notes" field.
10. Enter the customer's email address in the "email" field.
11. Enter the customer's phone number in the "phone" field.
12. Enter the customer's first name in the "first_name" field.
13. Enter the customer's last name in the "last_name" field.
14. Add any comments or notes about the customer in the "customer_note" field.
15. Select the payment status from the "payment_status" dropdown menu.
16. Enter the date the transaction was created in the "created_at" field.
17. Enter the date the transaction was updated in the "updated_at" field.
18. Enter the date the transaction was deleted in the "deleted_at" field.
19. Enter the user who created the transaction in the "created_by" field.
20. Enter the user who updated the transaction in the "updated_by" field.
21. Select the payment type from the "payment_type" dropdown menu.
22. Select the status of the transaction from the "status" dropdown menu.
23. Enter the transaction reference number in the "transaction_reference" field.
24. Enter the customer reference number in the "customer_reference" field.
25. Enter the payment ID in the "payment_id" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_id** (`form_id`, number, optional): This is the form ID.
* **craft_booth_id** (`craft_booth_id`, number, optional): Enter the craft booth ID.
* **order_id** (`order_id`, number, optional): Enter the order ID.
* **payment_method_id** (`payment_method_id`, select_one, optional): Select the payment method from the dropdown menu.
* **amount** (`amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the transaction.
* **tax_rate** (`tax_rate`, number, optional): Enter the tax rate.
* **tax_amount** (`tax_amount`, number, optional): The tax amount is calculated by multiplying the amount by the tax rate.
* **total** (`total`, number, optional): Enter the total amount.
* **notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the transaction.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **first_name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the customer's first name.
* **last_name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter the customer's last name.
* **customer_note** (`customer_note`, text, optional): Add any comments or notes about the customer.
* **payment_status** (`payment_status`, select_one, optional): Select the payment status from the dropdown menu.
* **created_at** (`created_at`, date, optional): Enter the date the transaction was created.
* **updated_at** (`updated_at`, date, optional): Enter the date the transaction was updated.
* **deleted_at** (`deleted_at`, date, optional): Enter the date the transaction was deleted.
* **created_by** (`created_by`, note, optional): Enter the user who created the transaction.
* **updated_by** (`updated_by`, note, optional): Enter the user who updated the transaction.
* **payment_type** (`payment_type`, select_one, optional): Select the payment type from the dropdown menu.
* **status** (`status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the transaction from the dropdown menu.
* **transaction_reference** (`transaction_reference`, text, optional): Enter the transaction reference number.
* **customer_reference** (`customer_reference`, text, optional): Enter the customer reference number.
* **payment_id** (`payment_id`, note, optional): Enter the payment ID.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
* Double-check the payment method and payment type to ensure they match the customer's preferences.
* Verify the customer's contact information before making a payment.
* Use the notes field to add any additional comments or notes about the transaction.
