# trophy_and_award_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "trophy_and_award_order_form" form is used to submit requests for trophies and awards. It collects information such as name, organization, event details, award type, quantity, date awarded, date ordered, email, and phone number.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your organization's name in the "Organization" field.
3. Provide details about the event in the "Event Details" field.
4. Select the type of award you're requesting (if applicable).
5. Enter the quantity of awards you're requesting.
6. Enter the date when you received the award in the "Date Awarded" field (if applicable).
7. Enter the date when you ordered the award in the "Date Ordered" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
9. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (2)**: Enter your name as it appears on your award.
* **Organization (3)**: Enter your organization's name.
* **Event Details (4)**: Provide a brief description of the event related to your award.
* **Award Type (5)**: Choose the type of award you're requesting (Trophy, Medal, Certificate).
* **Quantity (6)**: Enter the number of awards you're requesting.
* **Date Awarded (7)**: Enter the date when you received the award (if applicable).
* **Date Ordered (8)**: Enter the date when you ordered the award.
* **Email (9)**: Enter a valid email address for communication.
* **Phone (10)**: Enter your phone number for follow-up.
