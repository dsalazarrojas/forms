# Raw Material Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Raw Material Order Form is used to gather information for raw material orders, facilitating the purchasing or procurement process for production or supply chain teams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name in the `First Name` field.
2.  Enter your last name in the `Last Name` field.
3.  Input your company's name in the `Company Name` field.
4.  Provide the address of your company in the `Company Address` field.
5.  Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
6.  Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
7.  Select whether you are looking for a specific "Material Type" from the drop-down options.
8.  Enter the quantity of the material you are interested in purchasing.
9.  Enter the unit price of the material.
10.  Enter the total price for the order.
11.  Select the date you would like to receive the order (if applicable).
12.  If applicable, select the date you would like to place the order.
13.  Enter the name of the person or team you would like to be assigned to handle this order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, not required): Enter your first name here.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, not required): Enter your last name here.
- **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, not required): Enter your company's name here.
- **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, not required): Enter your company's address here.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number here.
- **Email** (`email`, text, not required): Enter your email address here.
- **Material Type** (`material_type`, select_one, not required): Select whether you are interested in purchasing a material of type 'Yes' or 'No'.
- **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, not required): Enter the quantity of material you are interested in purchasing.
- **Unit Price** (`unit_price`, number, not required): Enter the price per unit of material.
- **Total Price** (`total_price`, number, not required): Enter the total price for the order.
- **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, not required): Select the date you would like to receive the order (if applicable).
- **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, not required): Select the date you would like to place the order (if applicable).
- **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, not required): Enter the name of the person or team you would like to be assigned to handle this order.
