# Team Recognition Plaque Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to order custom team recognition plaques with personalized messages, acknowledgments, or rewards for team members. Team leaders or administrators can use this form to provide details for the order, including team name, team leader, order date, quantity ordered, and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Team Name** of the team being recognized.
2. Enter the name of the **Team Leader** or person responsible for the order.
3. Enter the **Plaque Text**, which will be the personalized message on the plaque.
4. Provide **Order Details** to help us prepare the order (if needed).
5. Enter the **Order Date** for the order.
6. Enter the **Quantity Ordered** and **Price Per Item** for calculation of the **Total Price**.
7. Choose the **Payment Method** (Cash, Cheque, or Other).
8. Enter your **Contact Info** (email address) to contact you.
9. Enter the **Shipping Address** where you want the plaques to be shipped.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name** (team_name): Enter the name of the team being recognized.
* **Team Leader** (team_leader): Enter the name of the team leader or person responsible for the order.
* **Plaque Text** (plaque_text): Enter the text that will appear on the plaque.
* **Order Details** (order_details): Provide any additional information needed to prepare the order (e.g., occasion).
* **Order Date** (order_date): Enter the date of the order.
* **Quantity Ordered** (quantity_ordered): Enter the number of plaques ordered.
* **Price Per Item** (price_per_item): Enter the price per item for the plaques.
* **Total Price** (total_price): Calculated automatically based on the quantity ordered and price per item.
* **Payment Method** (payment_method): Choose how you want to pay (Cash, Cheque, or Other).
* **Contact Info** (contact_info): Enter your email address so we can contact you for order updates.
* **Shipping Address** (shipping_address): Enter the address where you want the plaques shipped.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate team and team leader details to ensure correct delivery and acknowledgement.
- If you have any further questions or need help, please contact us for assistance.
- Ensure accurate shipping address to prevent delays in delivery.
