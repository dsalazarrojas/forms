# COVID 19 Restaurant Online Ordering Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is created for restaurants to manage online orders during the pandemic while maintaining social distancing and contactless order handling. It's a crucial tool for customers to place orders with confidence and for restaurants to manage their orders efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your customer's name.
2. Enter their phone number for order status updates.
3. Enter their email address for order confirmation and updates.
4. Select the type of order (one-time, weekly, or bi-weekly subscription).
5. Choose a subscription plan (if applicable).
6. Select a pickup or delivery date.
7. Choose a preferred time slot for pickup or delivery.
8. Select your main course and side dishes.
9. Add any special dietary instructions.
10. If delivering, provide your delivery address.
11. Enter a tip amount for the driver or staff.
12. Confirm the estimated total order amount.
13. Select a payment method (credit card, PayPal, pay at pickup, or business account).
14. Acknowledge that orders must be placed at least 24 hours in advance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name (1)**: Enter the customer's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Phone Number (2)**: Enter the customer's phone number for order status updates.
* **Email Address (3)**: Enter the customer's email address for order confirmation and updates.
* **Order Type (4)**: Select the type of order you want to place (one-time, weekly, or bi-weekly subscription).
* **Subscription Plan Selections (5)**: Choose a subscription plan if you're placing a subscription order.
* **Select Pickup or Delivery Date (6)**: Select a date for pickup or delivery.
* **Preferred Time Slot (7)**: Choose a time slot for pickup or delivery.
* **Main Course Selections (8)**: Select all applicable main courses from the list.
* **Side Dishes (9)**: Select all applicable side dishes from the list.
* **Special Dietary Instructions (10)**: Enter any special dietary instructions (e.g., no gluten, nut allergy, extra spicy).
* **Delivery Address (11)**: Enter your delivery address if choosing delivery.
* **Driver or Staff Tip ($) (12)**: Enter a tip amount for the driver or staff.
* **Estimated Total Order Amount ($) (13)**: Confirm the estimated total order amount.
* **Payment Method (14)**: Select a payment method (credit card, PayPal, pay at pickup, or business account).
* **I understand that orders must be placed at least 24 hours in advance (15)**: Acknowledge that orders must be placed at least 24 hours in advance.

Note: Ensure to review the entire form carefully before submitting it to avoid any duplication or confusion. If you have any questions, feel free to contact our support team for assistance.
