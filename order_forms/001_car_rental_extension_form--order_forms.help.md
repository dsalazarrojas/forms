# Car Rental Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to request an extension to their existing car rental agreement. Please use this form to provide your new return date and any additional information required for the extension.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form and ensure you have all the required information before proceeding.
2. Enter the Rental Agreement Number from your current rental contract in the "Rental Agreement Number" field.
3. Enter the current return date that you were originally scheduled to return the vehicle in the "Current Return Date" field.
4. Select the new return date you wish to return the vehicle in the "New Return Date" field.
5. If applicable, provide the reason for the extension in the "Reason for Extension" field.
6. If applicable, list any additional drivers that need to be added for the extension period in the "Additional Drivers" field.
7. Fill in your contact information:
	* "Contact Email Address" and "Phone Number" are required fields. Please ensure they are entered correctly.
8. Finally, confirm your agreement to the revised rental terms and additional daily charges by selecting "Yes, I agree" next to "I agree to the revised rental terms and additional daily charges".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rental Agreement Number** (Rental Agreement Number, text, required): The unique identifier for your current rental contract.
* **Current Return Date** (Current Return Date, date, required): The date you were originally scheduled to return the vehicle.
* **New Return Date** (New Return Date, date, required): Select the new date you wish to return the vehicle.
* **Reason for Extension** (Reason for Extension, text, optional): Briefly explain why you need more time.
* **Additional Drivers** (Additional Drivers, text, optional): List any new drivers that need to be added for the extension period.
* **Contact Email Address** (Contact Email Address, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **I agree to the revised rental terms and additional daily charges** (I agree to the revised rental terms and additional daily charges, select_one, required): Confirm your agreement to the revised rental terms and additional daily charges.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your account representative for assistance.
