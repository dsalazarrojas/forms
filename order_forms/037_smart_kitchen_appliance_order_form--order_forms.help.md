# Smart Kitchen Appliance Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Kitchen Appliance Order Form is a tool designed to collect information from customers for ordering kitchen appliances and gadgets. This form guides users through a series of fields to ensure accurate and complete order submission.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select up to 3 kitchen gadgets you would like to order from the provided options (KitchenAid, Bosch, Breville, Instant Pot).
2. Choose up to 2 appliances you would like to order from the provided options (Fridge, Stove, Oven, Dishwasher).
3. Enter the quantities of each selected kitchen gadget and appliance.
4. Enter your email address to receive updates on your order.
5. Enter your phone number for any additional contact information.
6. Add any additional comments or instructions for the order.
7. Review and submit the form to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Kitchen Gadget** (Kitchen Gadget): Select up to 3 kitchen gadgets you would like to order from the options provided.
* **Appliances** (Appliances): Choose up to 2 appliances you would like to order from the options provided.
* **Quantities** (Quantities): Enter the quantities for the selected kitchen gadgets and appliances.
* **Kitchen Gadget Quantities** (Kitchen Gadget Quantities): Enter the quantity for each selected kitchen gadget.
* **Appliances Quantities** (Appliances Quantities): Enter the quantity for each selected appliance.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address to receive updates on your order.
* **Phone** (Phone): Enter your phone number for any additional contact information.
* **Comments** (Comments): Add any additional comments or instructions for the order.
* **Submit** (Submit): Review and submit the form to complete your order.
* **Notes** (Notes): Add any additional notes or information for the order.
