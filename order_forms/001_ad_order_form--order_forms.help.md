# Ad Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ad Order Form is used by advertisers to place orders for their advertisements. It collects necessary information for the sales team to process the order and prepare the ad for publication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your Advertiser Name in the "Advertiser Name" field.
2.  Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email Address" field.
3.  Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone Number" field.
4.  Select the advertisement category from the "Advertisement Category" dropdown menu.
5.  Enter a compelling headline for your advertisement in the "Ad Headline" field.
6.  Enter the main content of your advertisement in the "Advertisement Body Text" field.
7.  Enter the total number of words in your advertisement in the "Word Count" field.
8.  Select the desired publication date in the "Desired Publication Date" field in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
9.  Select the duration of the ad in days in the "Duration in Days" field.
10. If you have a preference for placement, select it from the "Preferred Placement" dropdown menu.
11. If desired, select any special formatting options in the "Special Formatting Options" field.
12. Enter your estimated cost based on word count and duration in the "Estimated Cost" field.
13. Select the payment method for this order in the "Payment Method" field.
14. Enter your billing address in the "Billing Address" field.
15. If you have any special requests or notes, enter them in the "Special Requests or Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Advertiser Name** (Advertiser Name, text, required): Enter your full name or business name as the advertiser.
* **Contact Email Address** (Contact Email Address, email, required): Enter your primary email address for order confirmation.
* **Contact Phone Number** (Contact Phone Number, text, required): Enter your business or personal phone number for contact purposes.
* **Advertisement Category** (Advertisement Category, select_one, required): Select the category that best fits your advertisement.
* **Ad Headline** (Ad Headline, text, required): Enter a compelling title for your advertisement.
* **Advertisement Body Text** (Advertisement Body Text, text, required): Enter the main content of your advertisement.
* **Word Count** (Word Count, number, required): Enter the total number of words in your advertisement.
* **Desired Publication Date** (Desired Publication Date, date, required): Select the date you would like the ad to run in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Duration in Days** (Duration in Days, number, required): Select the number of days the ad is to run.
* **Preferred Placement** (Preferred Placement, select_one, optional): Select your preferred placement for the ad, if any.
* **Special Formatting Options** (Special Formatting Options, select_multiple, optional): Select any desired special formatting options for your advertisement.
* **Estimated Cost** (Estimated Cost, number, optional): Enter your estimated cost based on word count and duration.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_one, required): Select the payment method for this order.
* **Billing Address** (Billing Address, text, required): Enter your full address for billing purposes.
* **Special Requests or Notes** (Special Requests or Notes, text, optional): Enter any additional information for the sales team.
