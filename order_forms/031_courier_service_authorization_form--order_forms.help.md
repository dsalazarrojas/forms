# Courier Service Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Courier Service Authorization Form is used to authorize shipments with a designated courier service. This form is typically required for customers who need to ship packages or goods via courier services such as Fedex, UPS, or USPS. The form helps to ensure that all necessary information is collected and authorized before shipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill out the form with your shipping address, package details, shipping date and time, and contact information.
3. Select the desired courier service from the options provided.
4. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your shipping address where the package will be sent from. This includes your name, street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Package Details** (`package_details`, note, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the package, including its weight, dimensions, and any other relevant details.
* **Shipping Date** (`shipping_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when you would like to ship the package. This should be in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Shipping Time** (`shipping_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time when you would like to ship the package. This should be in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Courier Service** (`courier_service`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the courier service you would like to use for shipping. Select from the options provided, which are Fedex, UPS, or USPS.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person or organization responsible for the shipment.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person or organization responsible for the shipment.
