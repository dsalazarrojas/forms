# Graduation Cap And Gown Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for ordering graduation caps and gowns for students graduating from a university or college.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have all the necessary information required to fill out the form.
2. Read each field carefully and enter the correct information.
3. Submit the form once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Graduation Date** (id: 8, required): Enter the date of your graduation ceremony.
* **Graduation Time** (id: 9, required): Enter the time of your graduation ceremony.
* **Graduation Location** (id: 10, required): Enter the location of your graduation ceremony.
* **Graduation Faculty** (id: 11, optional): Enter the faculty that you will be graduating from (e.g. College of Arts and Sciences, College of Engineering).
* **Graduation School** (id: 12, optional): Enter the name of your graduating school (e.g. University of XYZ).
* **Graduation Program** (id: 13, optional): Enter the name of your graduation program (e.g. Bachelor of Arts, Master's Degree).
* **Graduation Year** (id: 14, optional): Enter the year of your graduation.
* **Graduation Semester** (id: 15, optional): Enter the semester of your graduation (e.g. Spring 2022).
* **Comments** (id: 16, optional): Enter any additional comments or remarks you would like to include with your order.
* **Cap Size** (id: 2, required): Select the size of your graduation cap (8, 10, or 12).
* **Gown Size** (id: 3, optional): Select the size of your graduation gown (8, 10, or 12).
* **Sleeve Length** (id: 4, optional): Select the length of your graduation gown's sleeve (short or long).
* **Collar Type** (id: 5, required): Select the type of collar you would like on your graduation gown (open or closed).
* **Sleeve Type** (id: 6, optional): Select the type of sleeve on your graduation gown (single or double).
* **Hood Type** (id: 7, optional): Select the type of hood you would like on your graduation gown (full, half, or no hood).
* **Graduation Ceremony** (id: 18, optional): Enter a brief description of your graduation ceremony.
* **Comments for School** (id: 19, optional): Enter any comments or remarks you would like to include for your school.
* **Graduation Announcement Message** (id: 20, optional): Enter a message to be included with your graduation announcement.
* **Comments for Ceremony** (id: 21, optional): Enter any additional comments or remarks you would like to include for your graduation ceremony.
* **Graduation Faculty Email** (id: 22, optional): Enter the email address of your faculty representative.
* **Graduation School Email** (id: 23, optional): Enter the email address of your school's email.
* **Graduation Announcement Email** (id: 24, optional): Enter a email address to receive graduation announcements.
* **Graduation School Admin** (id: 25, optional): Enter the name of your school's administration contact.

Note: The required fields are marked as such, and it's essential that these fields are filled out correctly to ensure that your order is processed accurately.
