# Factory Floor Lighting System Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Factory Floor Lighting System Order Form is designed for factory staff to place orders for a lighting system for their work area. This form gathers information for the processing of the order, ensuring timely delivery and installation of the lighting system.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2.  Provide the team name and leader's name for the team requiring the lighting system.
3.  Specify the quantity of the lighting system being ordered.
4.  Select the type of lighting system you need (Lamps, Fixtures, or Ballasts).
5.  Enter the delivery date you expect for the lighting system.
6.  Enter the installation date you expect for the lighting system.
7.  Provide the delivery address for the lighting system.
8.  Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the installation.

*   **Enter Factory Floor Lighting System Information** (`factory_floor_lighting_system_order_form_1`, `note`, required: false):
    This field is for a brief description of the lighting system you require. Please provide a clear and concise overview of your needs.
*   **Team Name** (`factory_floor_lighting_system_order_form_2`, `text`, required: false):
    Enter the name of the team or department that requires the lighting system.
*   **Team Leader** (`factory_floor_lighting_system_order_form_3`, `text`, required: false):
    Enter the name of the person in charge of the team or department.
*   **Quantity** (`factory_floor_lighting_system_order_form_4`, `number`, required: false):
    Enter the number of units or quantities of the lighting system being ordered.
*   **Lighting Type** (`factory_floor_lighting_system_order_form_5`, `select_one`, required: false):
    Select the type of lighting system you need (Lamps, Fixtures, or Ballasts).
*   **Delivery Date** (`factory_floor_lighting_system_order_form_6`, `date`, required: false):
    Enter the date you expect for delivery of the lighting system.
*   **Installation Date** (`factory_floor_lighting_system_order_form_7`, `date`, required: false):
    Enter the date you expect for installation of the lighting system.
*   **Delivery Address** (`factory_floor_lighting_system_order_form_8`, `text`, required: false):
    Enter the address where the lighting system is to be delivered.
*   **Installation Contact** (`factory_floor_lighting_system_order_form_9`, `text`, required: false):
    Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the installation.

## Tips

*   Double-check the information you provide to ensure accuracy.
*   If you have any questions or need further assistance, please contact your supervisor or the order processing team.
