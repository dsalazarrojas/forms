# Safety Lighting Control Unit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect order information for Safety Lighting Control Units from customers. It ensures that all necessary details are captured accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Provide customer information, including contact details and any additional notes.
2. Specify the unit quantity and type required.
3. Select any additional safety features or equipment needed.
4. Enter delivery information, including address and date.
5. Capture the order status and status change reason (if applicable).
6. Sign and date the form to confirm order completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Please enter your company name and contact information.
- **Safety Lighting Unit Info** (`safety_lighting_unit_info`, text, optional): Describe the unit or equipment being ordered.
- **Delivery Info** (`delivery_info`, text, optional): Enter the address where the unit will be delivered.
- **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): Select the desired delivery date.
- **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, optional): Choose the preferred delivery time.
- **Unit Quantity** (`unit_quantity`, number, optional): Specify the number of units required.
- **Unit Type** (`unit_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of unit being ordered.
- **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments from the customer.
- **Supplier Notes** (`supplier_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes or comments from the supplier.
- **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_one, optional): Track the current status of the order.
- **Customer Contact** (`customer_contact`, text, optional): Enter the customer's contact information.
- **Supplier Contact** (`supplier_contact`, text, optional): Enter the supplier's contact information.
- **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, optional): Enter the delivery address.
- **Unit Model Number** (`unit_model_number`, text, optional): Enter the unit's model number.
- **Unit Serial Number** (`unit_serial_number`, text, optional): Enter the unit's serial number.
- **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, note, optional): Sign and date the form to confirm order completion.
- **Supplier Signature** (`supplier_signature`, note, optional): Sign and date the form to confirm order completion.
- **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Track the date the order was placed.
- **Order Number** (`order_number`, text, optional): Enter a unique order number.
- **Order ID** (`order_id`, text, optional): Enter a unique ID for the order.
- **Order Status Date** (`order_status_date`, date, optional): Track the date the order status was last updated.
- **Order Status Change Reason** (`order_status_change_reason`, text, optional): Enter the reason for any status changes.
- **Delivery Info Comment** (`delivery_info_comment`, text, optional): Add any comments about the delivery.
- **Safety Features** (`safety_features`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional safety features required.
- **Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional safety equipment needed.

## Tips
- Always review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy.
- Ask your supervisor or team lead if you're unsure about any field or requirement.
- Keep in mind that some fields are optional, but it's essential to complete all required fields.
