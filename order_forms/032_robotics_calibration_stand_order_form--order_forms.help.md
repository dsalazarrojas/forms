# Robotics Calibration Stand Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers interested in acquiring a robotics calibration stand to provide their details and specifications for the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields as prompted.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Details** (`form_details`, text, optional): This field is for any additional comments or notes about your order. Please provide any relevant details about your purchase, such as specific models or configurations you are interested in.
- **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide any additional details about yourself or your company, such as your business name, address, or contact person.
- **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide any specific requirements or specifications for your order, such as delivery date or time.
- **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): This field is for you to select the date you would like to receive your order.
- **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, optional): This field is for you to select the time you would like to receive your order.
- **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide the address where you would like your order to be shipped to.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide your contact number, so we can reach out to you regarding your order.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is for you to provide your email address, so we can contact you regarding your order.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to our sales team for assistance.
