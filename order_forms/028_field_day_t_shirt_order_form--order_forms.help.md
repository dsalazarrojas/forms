# Field Day T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Field Day T Shirt Order Form is designed to gather information for ordering t-shirts from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Make sure to enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
3. Select your preferred shirt size from the "Shirt Size" dropdown menu.
4. Select your preferred shirt colors from the "Color" multi-select dropdown menu.
5. Enter the date you would like to order the t-shirts in the "Date" field.
6. Choose your preferred payment type from the "Payment Type" dropdown menu.
7. If you have a message for the t-shirt, enter it in the "T-Shirt Text" field.
8. If you're ordering more than one t-shirt, enter the total number of shirts you'd like to order in the "Total Shirts" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter a valid email address.
* **Shirt Size** (`shirt_size`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred shirt size: XS, S, M, L, or XL.
* **Color** (`shirt_color`, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred t-shirt colors: Red, Blue, Green, or Yellow.
* **Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to order the t-shirts.
* **Payment Type** (`payment_type`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred payment type: Card, Bank Transfer, or Check.
* **T-Shirt Text** (`tshirt_text`, note, optional): Enter any special message for your t-shirt.
* **Total Shirts** (`total_shirts`, number, optional): If you're ordering more than one t-shirt, enter the total number of shirts you'd like to order.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields for a smooth order process.
* If you have any special requests or messages for your t-shirt, use the "T-Shirt Text" field.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact our customer support for assistance.
