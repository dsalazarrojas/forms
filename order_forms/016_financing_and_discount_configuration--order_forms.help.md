# Financing And Discount Configuration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers for financing and discount configurations. It will be used by sales teams to create a financing proposal for the customer.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select a title for the form.
2. Enter your name as the customer.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Enter the amount being financed.
6. Read and agree to the terms and conditions.
7. Select a category for the form.
8. Provide a description of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a title for the form that will be used for identification purposes only.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the customer.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount being financed.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, optional): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the financing proposal.
* **Form Category** (`form_category`, text, optional): Select a category for the form that will help identify the proposal.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, optional): Provide a description of the form that will be used for reference.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the financing proposal is created correctly.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the sales team for assistance.
* This form is for internal use only and should not be shared with customers directly.
