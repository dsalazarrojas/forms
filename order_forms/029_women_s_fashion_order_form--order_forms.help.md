# Women's Fashion Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record customer information and product orders for women's fashion items. It captures basic contact details such as name, address, and phone number, as well as product information like product type and quantity ordered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the first page of the form with your information:
	* First Name: Enter your first name as you would like to be addressed.
	* Last Name: Enter your last name as you would like to be addressed.
	* Address: Enter your mailing address to ensure smooth delivery of your order.
2. Enter your contact information:
	* Zip Code: Enter your postal zip code.
	* Phone Number: Enter your primary phone number.
	* Email: Enter your email address for any communication related to your order.
3. Select your product type:
	* Product: Choose the type of women's fashion item you would like to order (e.g., Women's Top, Women's Bottom, Women's Dress).
4. Add order details:
	* Quantity: Enter the number of items you would like to order.
	* Date: Optional date for special delivery or pick-up.
	* Time: Optional time for special delivery or pick-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your mailing address to ensure smooth delivery of your order.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, `number`, required: false): Enter your postal zip code.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your primary phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for any communication related to your order.
* **Product** (`product`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of women's fashion item you would like to order (e.g., Women's Top, Women's Bottom, Women's Dress).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of items you would like to order.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Optional date for special delivery or pick-up.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Optional time for special delivery or pick-up.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about your order.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out to ensure accurate processing of your order.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy to ensure smooth communication.
* Review your order details carefully before submitting the form.
* Contact us if you have any questions or concerns about your order.
