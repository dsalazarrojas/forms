# Product Return Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to assist customers in initiating the return or refund process for a product. Please complete this form to provide the necessary information for our team to process your return or refund.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select the category**: Choose the relevant category from the options provided to indicate the nature of the return or refund.
2. **Enter product details**: Provide the product name or description.
3. **Specify the reason for return**: Choose the reason for the return from the options provided.
4. **Add image (optional)**: If necessary, upload an image to support your return request.
5. **Enter a message**: Provide a brief description of the issue with the product.
6. **Enter customer details**: Provide your contact information (name, email, phone number, and address).
7. **Enter order details**: Enter the order ID and status.
8. **Select the status**: Choose the status of your return request.
9. **Enter form ID**: Enter the ID of the form.
10. **Submit the form**: Click the submit button to send your return request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer**: Enter your name.
* **Product**: Enter the product name or description.
* **Reason**: Choose the reason for the return from the options provided.
* **Image**: If necessary, upload an image to support your return request.
* **Message**: Enter a brief description of the issue with the product.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **Order ID**: Enter the order ID number.
* **Order Status**: Choose the status of your order.
* **Status**: Choose the status of your return request.
* **Form ID**: Enter the form ID number.
* **Category**: Choose the relevant category from the options provided.
* **Customer Category**: Choose your category type (business, consumer, or other).
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool assigned to process your return request.
* **Assigned Tool Name**: Enter the name of the tool assigned.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Address**: Enter your address.
* **Category 2**: Choose the relevant category from the options provided.
* **Output File**: This field is not required and can be left blank.
* **Output File Name**: This field is not required and can be left blank.
* **Note**: This field is not required and can be left blank.
