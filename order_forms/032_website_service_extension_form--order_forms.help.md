# Website Service Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Website Service Extension Form is designed to collect information from users about their website service extension needs and preferences. This form helps us understand what services users are interested in and how we can better serve them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your response to the `form_data` field.
2. Provide a brief description of your needs in the `form_description` field.
3. Select any services you are interested in from the `form_services` field.
4. If you have any additional information or comments, you can add them in the `form_service_details` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form Data (`form_data`)
* **Form Data** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your response to the question being asked here.

### Form Description (`form_description`)
* **Form Description** (`text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of your needs or requirements for the service.

### Form Services (`form_services`)
* **Form Services** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose any services you are interested in:
	+ Service 1
	+ Service 2
	+ Service 3

### Form Service Details (`form_service_details`)
* **Form Service Details** (`note`, required/optional): Add any additional information or comments about the services you selected.

## Tips
* Please provide as much detail as possible to help us better understand your needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
