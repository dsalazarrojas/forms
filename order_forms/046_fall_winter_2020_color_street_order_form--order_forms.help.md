# Fall/Winter 2020 Color Street Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to submit their Fall/Winter 2020 Color Street order. Please fill it out with accurate and complete information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter your **Name** in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your **Address** in the "Address" field.
3. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
5. Choose a **Date** for your order in the "Date" field.
6. Select a **Time** for your order in the "Time" field.
7. Add any **Note** about your order in the "Note" field.
8. Indicate the **Quantity** of items you are ordering in the "Quantity" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (form_1_name)**: The field to enter your full name.
* **Address (form_1_address)**: The field to enter your address for delivery or pick-up.
* **Phone (form_1_phone)**: The field to enter your phone number for contact.
* **Email (form_1_email)**: The field to enter your email address for contact.
* **Date (form_1_date)**: The field to select a date for your order. Please use the format "MM/DD/YYYY" (e.g., 12/01/2020).
* **Time (form_1_time)**: The field to select a time for your order. Please use the format "HH:MM AM/PM" (e.g., 03:00 PM).
* **Note (form_1_note)**: The field for any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Quantity (form_1_quantity)**: The field to indicate how many items you are ordering.
