# P CARD/CREDIT CARD ORDER FORM With Payment Authorization - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a P-Card/Credit Card order process, including payment authorization. It has multiple sections for users to enter their details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Select "Order Form"** (`main_page`, `text`, not required): This is the main section of the form where you can enter any additional information related to your order.
2. **Enter P-Card/Credit Card Information** (`paypal_form`, `text`, not required): Here, you can enter your P-Card/Credit Card details as requested.
3. **Enter Payment Information** (`payment_info`, `text`, not required): This section is for additional payment details, if needed.
4. **Submit** (`submit_button`, `text`, not required): Once you have filled out all the necessary information, click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select "Order Form"** (`main_page`, `text`, not required): This is the initial section of the form for you to enter any information about your order.
* **Enter P-Card/Credit Card Information** (`paypal_form`, `text`, not required): Enter your P-Card/Credit Card number, expiry date, and any other relevant details as asked.
* **Enter Payment Information** (`payment_info`, `text`, not required): If there are any additional instructions or requirements for the payment, enter that here.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, `text`, not required): Clicking this button will process and send the submitted information.
