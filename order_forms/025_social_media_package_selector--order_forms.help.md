# social media package selector form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help clients select a suitable social media package for their specific needs. It will guide them through choosing the right social media platform, selecting a package type (Basic, Premium, or Enterprise), and finalizing the payment method. The form also includes a section for additional comments and package notes, as well as a checkbox to confirm agreement with the terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select the social media service needed**: Choose the social media platform you want to work with from the options provided (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn, or TikTok).
2. **Enter client name (optional)**: Provide your company's name or your personal name, depending on the situation.
3. **Enter client email (optional)**: If you have an email address related to the client, enter it here. This is not mandatory.
4. **Specify the social media goal**: Select one or more goals for your social media efforts, such as increasing followers, engagement, or brand awareness.
5. **Select a package**: Choose a package type (Basic, Premium, or Enterprise) that suits your needs.
6. **Additional comments**: Type any additional information you want to provide about your package selection.
7. **Package notes**: Add any extra details about your package.
8. **Confirm terms and conditions**: Select "Yes" to agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the social media service needed?**: This field helps us understand which social media platform you want to work with. Please select the platform that best fits your goals and audience.
* **Client Name**: This is the name of your company or yourself. It will be used to reference you in our communication.
* **Client Email**: If you have a relevant email address, please enter it here. We will use this email for any necessary communication.
* **What is the client's social media goal?**: Here, you can choose one or more goals for your social media efforts, such as increasing followers, engagement, or brand awareness. This will help us tailor a plan that meets your objectives.
* **Select a package**: Our packages cater to different needs. The Basic package is ideal for small-scale social media management, the Premium package offers more features, and the Enterprise package is designed for large-scale businesses.
* **Package Details**: In this field, you can add any extra details about your package selection. This could include specific requirements or expectations you have.
* **How would you like to pay?**: You can choose from three payment methods: Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal. Please select the one that suits you best.
* **Additional Comments**: Type any other relevant information you'd like to provide us about your package selection.
* **Package Notes**: This is an optional field for you to add any extra details about your package.
* **Have you read and agreed to our terms and conditions?**: Please select "Yes" to confirm that you have read and agreed to our terms and conditions before we proceed with your package selection.
