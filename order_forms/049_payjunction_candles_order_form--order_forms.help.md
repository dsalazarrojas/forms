# Payjunction Candles Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect order details, customer information, and payment details for candle orders. It is intended for customers and order administrators to input and review information related to the order process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Order Details section with the necessary information about the order, including the order date and time, total amount, and order status.
2. Provide your customer information, including email, phone number, address, and any relevant notes.
3. Choose a payment method and confirm whether the payment has been made.
4. Sign the form to confirm your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details** (Order Details, text, required/optional): Enter the details of the order, including the order date and time, total amount, and order status. This section helps us understand the scope of the order.
* **Order Date** (Order Date, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the order, if known. This will help us to track the order timeline.
* **Order Time** (Order Time, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the order, if known. This will help us to track the order timeline.
* **Total Amount** (Total Amount, number, required/optional): Enter the total amount of the order. This will help us to calculate the total cost of the order.
* **Customer E-mail** (Customer E-mail, email, required/optional): Enter the customer's email address. This will help us to contact the customer with order updates.
* **Customer Phone** (Customer Phone, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's phone number. This will help us to contact the customer with order updates.
* **Customer Address** (Customer Address, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's address. This will help us to send order updates and shipping information.
* **Customer Note** (Customer Note, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the customer. This will help us to understand their preferences and requirements.
* **Order Status** (Order Status, select_one, required/optional): Select the current status of the order (Pending, Approved, Rejected).
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_one, required/optional): Select the payment method used (PayPal, Bank Transfer, Cash).
* **Payment Details** (Payment Details, text, required/optional): Enter any additional payment information, such as transaction IDs or payment references.
* **Payment Confirmation** (Payment Confirmation, select_one, required/optional): Confirm whether the payment has been made (True, False).
* **Customer Signature** (Customer Signature, text, required/optional): Enter your signature to confirm the order.
* **Order ID** (Order ID, text, required/optional): Enter a unique identifier for the order. This will help us to track the order in our system.
* **Order Notes** (Order Notes, text, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the order. This will help us to track the order's progress and history.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact our support team for assistance.
* Please be sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
