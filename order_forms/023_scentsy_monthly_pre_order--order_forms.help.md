# Scentsy Monthly Pre Order - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Scentsy Monthly Pre Order" form is used to place monthly pre-orders with Scentsy for sale associates to use during their daily sales activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are authorized to complete the form.
2. Fill out each field according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Password** (4, text, required): Enter your password.
* **Phone** (5, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Select a product** (6, select_one, required): Choose one product from the list provided:
	+ Product A
	+ Product B
	+ Product C
* **Select multiple products** (7, select_multiple, required): Choose multiple products from the list provided:
	+ Product D
	+ Product E
	+ Product F
* **Date** (8, date, optional): Choose a date for the order (if applicable).
* **Time** (9, time, optional): Choose a time for the order (if applicable).
* **Note** (10, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the order.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out each field accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact your supervisor or the IT department for assistance.
