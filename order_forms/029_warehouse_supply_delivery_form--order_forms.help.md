# Warehouse Supply Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warehouse Supply Delivery Form is a tool for warehouse staff to record and track delivery details. This form is used to document the delivery date, time, customer name, and order ID.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the delivery date for the supply delivery.
2. Enter the delivery time in the designated field.
3. Type the customer's name in the designated field.
4. Enter the order ID number in the designated field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required/optional): This field is for entering the date of the supply delivery. Please select the date from the calendar picker or manually enter it in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
  - **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required/optional): This field is for entering the time of the supply delivery. Please enter the time in the format "HH:MM:SS".
  - **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): This field is for entering the name of the customer who received the supply delivery. Please type the customer's name as it appears on their records.
  - **Order ID** (`order_id`, text, required/optional): This field is for entering the ID number of the order being delivered. Please enter the order ID as it appears on the customer's order records.

## Tips
* Ensure the delivery date and time are accurate to prevent delays or mix-ups.
* Double-check the customer's name and order ID to ensure correct identification and processing.
* Review and confirm the information before submission to prevent errors.
