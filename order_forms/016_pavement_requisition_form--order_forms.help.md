# Pavement Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a pavement requisition request. It is used to track and manage such requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:
1. Fill out the form in the relevant fields as instructed below.
2. Ensure that the form is filled out correctly and accurately.
3. Submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Requested Material
* **Requested Material** (`material_requested`, text, optional): Enter the material requested for the pavement requisition.

### Material Type
* **Material Type** (`material_type`, select_one, optional): Select whether the material is a "Yes" or "No".

### Service Requested
* **Service Requested** (`service_requested`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether the service is a "Yes" or "No" for multiple options.

### Location
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the material is needed.

### Quantity
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of material needed.

### Unit Price
* **Unit Price** (`unit_price`, number, optional): Enter the unit price of the material.

### Total Cost
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, text, optional): Enter the total cost of the material.

### Assigned User
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user_id`, select_one, optional): Select the user assigned to handle the request.

### Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out correctly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to contact the relevant authorities.
* Make sure to submit the form once complete.
