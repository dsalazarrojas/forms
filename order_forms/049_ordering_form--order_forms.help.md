# Ordering Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Ordering Form is designed to collect information for orders placed by customers. This form is used to gather the necessary details required to process an order, including product number, order number, customer name, and customer email.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the "Ordering Form" page.
2. Fill in the required and optional fields as needed:
	* Product Number
	* Order Number
	* Customer Name
	* Customer Email

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Number** (`product_number`, `number`, optional): Enter the product number of the item being ordered.
* **Order Number** (`order_number`, `number`, optional): Enter the order number associated with the order.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the customer placing the order.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the customer.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate order processing.
* Double-check the product number and order number for correctness.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask a colleague or contact support for assistance.
