# Forestry Equipment Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Forestry Equipment Check Out Form is a tool used to request and manage forestry equipment usage. It helps foresters and other forestry professionals to keep track of equipment availability, usage, and maintenance schedules.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your forester's information, including your name and contact details.
2. Provide a clear and concise description of the equipment you require, including the type and quantity needed.
3. Specify the date and time you need the equipment for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Forester Info (forester_info)**:
 	+ Type: Text Input
 	+ Required: No
 	+ Explanation: Enter your name and contact details, including your phone number and email address. This will help us get in touch with you for any questions or updates about your equipment request.
* **Equipment Request (equipment_request)**:
 	+ Type: Text Input
 	+ Required: No
 	+ Explanation: Describe the equipment you need, including the type, quantity, and any specific details about the request.
* **Date & Time (date_time)**:
 	+ Type: Text Input
 	+ Required: No
 	+ Explanation: Enter the date and time you need the equipment for, including the start and end times if applicable.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and clear information to ensure smooth processing of your equipment request.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* This form is designed to be used by foresters and forestry professionals for forestry-related activities only. If you're unsure about the form's purpose or usage, please refer back to the form's title and description.
* If you're using this form for non-forestry purposes, please note that your request may not be processed.
