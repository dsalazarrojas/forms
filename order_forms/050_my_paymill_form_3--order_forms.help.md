# My Paymill Form 3 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect order details and customer information from customers, making it an essential tool for business owners and employees to manage orders and provide excellent customer service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the payment method for the order.
2. Enter the total price of the order.
3. Enter the Paymill amount for the order.
4. Enter the discount amount for the order.
5. Enter the shipping price for the order.
6. Enter the tax for the order.
7. Enter the subtotal of the order.
8. Fill in the customer details, including name, email, phone, address, country, city, state, zip, and any additional notes or messages.
