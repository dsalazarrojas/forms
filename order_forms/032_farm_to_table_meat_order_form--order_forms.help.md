# Farm To Table Meat Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Farm To Table Meat Order Form is for customers to place orders for farm-to-table meat products. To complete this form, please follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
3. Select the type of product you are interested in (Meat, Dairy, or Poultry) from the "product-type" dropdown menu.
4. Choose the specific type of product you want to order from the "product-sub-type" dropdown menu (Ground, Sausages, or Cuts).
5. Enter the quantity of the product you want to order.
6. Enter the price of the order.
7. Select the delivery date and time for your order.
8. Add any additional comments or requests in the "order-notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Product Type**: Select the type of product you are interested in (Meat, Dairy, or Poultry).
* **Product Sub-Type**: Select the specific type of product you want to order (Ground, Sausages, or Cuts).
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of products you want to order.
* **Price**: Enter the total price of the order.
* **Delivery Date**: Select the date you want to receive your order.
* **Delivery Time**: Select the time you want to receive your order.
* **Order Notes**: Add any additional comments or requests you have for your order.
