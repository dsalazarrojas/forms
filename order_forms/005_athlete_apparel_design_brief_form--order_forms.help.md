# Athlete Apparel Design Brief Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather essential information from clients or teams to create custom apparel designs that meet their specific needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide the required information for the client and project, including the team or club name, primary contact person, contact email, and required delivery date.
2. Select the types of apparel needed, including jersey, shorts, warm-up suit, socks, headwear, and hoodies/fleece.
3. Specify the primary and secondary team colors, including any Pantone codes if available.
4. Choose the placement of the primary logo on the apparel.
5. Select the fabric preference based on performance needs.
6. Determine the sizing requirements, including the size range and estimated quantity.
7. Indicate any special design requests or patterns.
8. Provide any style keywords that describe the desired design style.
9. If desired, describe any reference styles or existing designs that the client likes.
10. Specify any special requests or details that are important to the design.
11. If applicable, select an estimated budget range for the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team or Club Name** (`team_club_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the team or club.
* **Primary Contact Person** (`primary_contact`, text, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the primary contact person.
* **Required Delivery Date** (`project_deadline`, date, required): Enter the required delivery date for the project.
* **Types of Apparel Needed** (`apparel_types`, select_multiple, required): Select all types of apparel needed, such as jersey, shorts, warm-up suit, etc.
* **Primary Team Color** (`primary_color`, text, required): Enter the primary team color, including any Pantone codes if available.
* **Secondary Team Color** (`secondary_color`, text, optional): Enter the secondary team color (if applicable).
* **Accent Color** (`accent_color`, text, optional): Enter any accent color used in the design.
* **Primary Logo Placement** (`logo_placement`, select_one, required): Choose where the primary logo should be placed on the apparel.
* **Fabric Preference** (`fabric_preference`, select_one, required): Select the fabric preference based on performance needs.
* **Sizing Requirements** (`sizing_range`, text, required): Enter the size range and estimated quantity of the apparel.
* **Estimated Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, optional): Select an estimated budget range for the project.
* **Style Keywords** (`style_keywords`, text, optional): Enter any style keywords that describe the desired design style.
* **Reference Description** (`reference_description`, text, optional): Describe any reference styles or existing designs that the client likes.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any special requests or details that are important to the design.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps_note`, note, optional): Enter any final comments or instructions for the design team.
