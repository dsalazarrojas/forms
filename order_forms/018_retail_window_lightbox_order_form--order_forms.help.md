# retail_window_lightbox_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information for orders and customer interactions within a retail window setting.

## How To Complete This Form
This form is divided into sections for easy navigation. Fill in each section as instructed, and make sure you have access to the necessary information and permissions before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Product Information
* **Product Info** (`product_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the product details or reference the product being ordered.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, required/optional): Provide the customer's contact details or information relevant to the order.
* **Shipping Info** (`shipping_info`, text, required/optional): Enter shipping details or options, if applicable.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, required/optional): Provide payment methods or details, if necessary.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required/optional): Describe the order specifics or requirements.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, required/optional): Add any special requests or instructions for the order.
* **Review Info** (`review_info`, text, required/optional): Include any relevant review information or feedback.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required/optional): Confirm the order or submission.

### Additional Information
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Select the relevant date for the order.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Select the time for the order.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, required/optional): Choose one from the provided options.

### Tips
- Always check the form for required fields before submission.
- If unsure about any field, refer to the relevant help guides or ask for assistance.
- Double-check the provided information before confirming the order.
