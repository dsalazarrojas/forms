# Sales Tax Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Tax Calculator form is designed to estimate the total sales tax for a given subtotal and sales tax rate. This form is intended for sales professionals, business owners, and customers who need to calculate the sales tax for a specific transaction. 

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the subtotal of the sale or transaction in the "Subtotal" field.
2. Enter the sales tax rate as a percentage in the "Sales Tax Rate" field.
3. Click the "Calculate" button to generate the sales tax amount and total price.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Subtotal**: Enter the total cost of the goods or services being sold before tax. This is the base amount that will be used to calculate the sales tax.
* **Sales Tax Rate**: Enter the sales tax rate as a percentage. For example, if the sales tax rate is 8.5%, enter 8.5.
* **Total Amount**: This field is automatically populated with the subtotal plus sales tax amount.
* **Sales Tax Amount**: This field is automatically populated with the calculated sales tax amount.
* **Total Inclusive Price**: This field is automatically populated with the total price, including both the subtotal and sales tax.

## Tips
- Ensure that you enter the sales tax rate as a percentage value, without the percentage sign (%).
- If the sales tax rate is not provided, please enter 0% as a default value.
- The form will only generate an accurate result if the subtotal is greater than 0.
- If the subtotal or sales tax rate is not a number, the form will not function correctly.
- If you need to calculate sales tax for a large or complex transaction, please consult a tax professional for more accurate results.
- Sales tax rates may vary by jurisdiction; if you are unsure about the sales tax rate for your location, please consult your local tax authority.
