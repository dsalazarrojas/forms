# Catering Services Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for catering services orders, including customer and event details, order information, and payment and deposit details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the customer name, email address, and phone number. These fields are required.
2. Enter the company name (if applicable).
3. Select the event name and type from the provided options.
4. Choose the date and time of the event.
5. Enter the venue name and address.
6. Specify the number of guests attending the event.
7. For each menu item, select the item and the number of servings you would like to order.
8. If there are additional items ordered, enter the details in the "Additional Items" field.
9. Enter any dietary restrictions and special requests.
10. Calculate the subtotal, tax, service charge, delivery fee, and total amount.
11. Select the payment method and confirm if a deposit has been paid.
12. Set the balance due date.
13. Add any additional comments or information about the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Your full name.
* **Email Address**: Your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number.
* **Company Name**: Your company name (if applicable).
* **Order Date**: Date of order.
* **Event Name**: Name of event.
* **Event Type**: Type of event (e.g. Wedding, corporate event, birthday party, etc.).
* **Event Date**: Date of event.
* **Event Time**: Start time of event.
* **Venue Name**: Location name.
* **Venue Address**: Complete address of the venue.
* **Number of Guests**: Total number of guests attending the event.
* **Meal Item 1**, **Meal Item 2**, **Meal Item 3**, **Meal Item 4**: Select the menu items you would like to order and the number of servings.
* **Additional Items**: Enter any additional items ordered.
* **Additional Items Cost**: Total cost for additional items.
* **Dietary Requirements**: Any food restrictions or allergies.
* **Special Requests**: Any special requests or instructions for the order.
* **Subtotal**: Total before tax and fees.
* **Tax**: Applicable taxes.
* **Service Charge**: Service fee.
* **Delivery Fee**: Delivery charge.
* **Total Amount**: Final total amount due.
* **Deposit Required**: Deposit amount required (if applicable).
* **Deposit Paid**: Confirm if deposit has been paid.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method (e.g. credit card, debit card, etc.).
* **Balance Due Date**: Date when final payment is due.
* **Additional Comments**: Any other information about the order.

Note: The fields marked with an asterisk (\*) are required fields. Make sure to fill in all required fields to complete the form properly.
