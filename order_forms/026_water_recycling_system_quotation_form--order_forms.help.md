# water_recycling_system_quotation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for quoting a water recycling system, which helps in gathering all the necessary information from both the customer and the supplier to process a quotation. The form includes various fields to capture customer and supplier details, quotation details, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the supplier information section:
	* Select the supplier from the list provided.
	* Enter the supplier's address, phone number, and email.
2. Fill in the customer information section:
	* Enter the customer's address, phone number, and email.
	* Select the quotation details, such as the date and time.
3. Fill in the quotation information section:
	* Enter the quotation amount and notes.
	* Select the quotation status (Active, Inactive, or Cancelled).
4. Optionally, select the attachment and supplier comments fields if necessary.
5. Click the "Form Submit" button to submit the form.
