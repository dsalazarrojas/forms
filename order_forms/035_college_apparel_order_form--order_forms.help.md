# College Apparel Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The College Apparel Order Form is designed to gather information for your college apparel order. Please fill out this form carefully to ensure accurate processing of your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out all required fields**: Make sure to enter information in all the fields marked as required.
2. **Select relevant options**: Choose the correct options for your order details, such as customer type and item category.
3. **Enter shipping details**: Provide your shipping address, city, state, and zip code accurately.
4. **Choose your item**: Select the item you'd like to order, including size, quantity, and color.
5. **Add customization if needed**: If you'd like to add a name or number to your item, enter the details accordingly.
6. **Choose payment method**: Select how you'd like to pay for your order.
7. **Review and submit**: Carefully review your form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Full Name**: Your name.
- **Email Address**: Your contact email.
- **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number.
- **Customer Type**: Select how you are affiliated with the college (Student, Alumni, Faculty/Staff, etc.).
- **Student ID Number**: If applicable, enter your student ID number.
- **Graduation Year**: If you're an alumni, enter your graduation year.
- **Shipping Address**: Enter your complete shipping address.
- **City**: Enter your city name.
- **State or Province**: Enter your state or province.
- **ZIP or Postal Code**: Enter your postal code.
- **Country**: Select your country of residence.
- **Item Category**: Choose the type of apparel you'd like to order (t-shirts, polo shirts, etc.).
- **Item Name**: Enter the specific item you'd like to order.
- **Color**: Select the color of the item you've chosen.
- **Size**: Select the size of the item you've chosen.
- **Quantity**: Enter how many items you'd like to order.
- **Additional Items**: Indicate if you'd like to add more items to your order.
- **Second Item Name**, **Second Item Color**, **Second Item Size**, and **Second Item Quantity**: If you're ordering more than one item, enter these details for the second item.
- **Customization Needed**: Select if you need customization, such as a name or number.
- **Name to Add**: If you need customization, enter the name to be added.
- **Number to Add**: If you need a number, enter it.
- **Gift Wrapping**: Indicate if the order is a gift.
- **Gift Message**: Enter a message to include with the gift.
- **Shipping Method**: Choose the shipping method you prefer (standard, express, etc.).
- **Payment Method**: Select how you'd like to pay for your order.
- **Card Number**: If paying by card, enter the card number.
- **Expiry Date**: Enter the expiry date of the card (MM/YY).
- **CVV**: Enter the security code.
- **Cardholder Name**: Enter the name on the card.
- **Promo Code**: If you have a promo code, enter it.
- **Special Instructions**: Enter any special requests.
- **Terms and Conditions**: Confirm that you've read and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Tips
- Double-check all required fields before submitting.
- Ensure accurate spelling and grammar in all text fields.
- Be thorough when entering shipping details.
- Review the terms and conditions before submitting.
- Contact us if you have any questions or concerns.
