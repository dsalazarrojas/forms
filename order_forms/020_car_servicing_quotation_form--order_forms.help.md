# Car Servicing Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a car servicing quotation from customers. Please fill it out accurately and completely to receive a reliable quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer name" field.
2. Enter the customer's email address in the "Email address" field.
3. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Phone number" field.
4. Enter the customer's street address in the "Street address" field (if applicable).
5. Enter the vehicle details in the "Vehicle details" field (e.g., year, make, model).
6. Select the type of service required in the "Type of service required" field.
7. Enter any specific issues or concerns in the "Specific issues or concerns" field.
8. Check if any parts replacement is needed and provide details if applicable.
9. Select if any fluid checks and refills are required.
10. Check if the vehicle has warranty coverage.
11. Select the service urgency (optional).
12. If applicable, enter the preferred completion date.
13. Enter the estimated labor cost and parts cost.
14. Enter any additional fees or charges.
15. Select the quotation validity period.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer name**: Enter the customer's name as it is in their official documents.
* **Email address**: Enter the customer's email address where they can be reached for updates or queries.
* **Phone number**: Enter the customer's phone number for further communication.
* **Street address**: Enter the customer's street address (if different from their email address).
* **Vehicle details**: Enter the vehicle details such as year, make, and model.
* **Vehicle year**: Enter the vehicle's year.
* **Vehicle make**: Enter the vehicle's make.
* **Vehicle model**: Enter the vehicle's model.
* **Registration number**: Enter the vehicle's registration number (if known).
* **Current mileage**: Enter the current mileage of the vehicle (if known).
* **Type of service required**: Select the type of service required (e.g., Major Service, Minor Service).
* **Specific issues or concerns**: Enter any issues or concerns with the vehicle.
* **Any parts replacement needed**: Check if any parts replacement is needed and provide details.
* **Fluid checks and refills required**: Check if any fluid checks and refills are required.
* **Warranty coverage status**: Check if the vehicle has warranty coverage.
* **Service urgency**: Select the service urgency level (e.g., Emergency, Urgent, Standard).
* **Preferred completion date**: Enter the preferred completion date of the service.
* **Estimated labor cost**: Enter the estimated labor cost of the service.
* **Estimated parts cost**: Enter the estimated parts cost of the service.
* **Additional fees or charges**: Enter any additional fees or charges.
* **Quotation validity period**: Select the quotation validity period (e.g., 7 days, 14 days, 30 days, 60 days).

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to receive a reliable quote. If you have any issues or concerns, please mention them in the "Specific issues or concerns" field. If you have any questions, please contact us at [insert contact info].
