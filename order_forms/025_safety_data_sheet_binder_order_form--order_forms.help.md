# safety_data_sheet_binder_order_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The safety_data_sheet_binder_order_form is used to collect contact information for the purpose of ordering safety data sheets binders. This form is used to ensure that all necessary information is collected correctly for efficient and accurate processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Fill in your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone number.
5. Fill in your Organization.
6. Fill in your Job Title.
7. Fill in your Street address.
8. Fill in your City.
9. Select your State from the options provided.
10. Fill in your Zip code.
11. Fill in your Shipping First Name.
12. Fill in your Shipping Last Name.
13. Fill in your Shipping Organization.
14. Fill in your Shipping Street address.
15. Fill in your Shipping City.
16. Select your Shipping State from the options provided.
17. Fill in your Shipping Zip code.
18. Fill in any Comments (if applicable).
19. Fill in your Shipping Phone number.
20. Fill in your Shipping Email address.
21. Select your Delivery Method from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Organization** (5, text, required): Enter your organization name.
* **Job Title** (6, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Street** (7, text, required): Enter your street address.
* **City** (8, text, required): Enter your city.
* **State** (9, select_one, required): Select the state where you are located.
* **Zip** (10, number, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Shipping First Name** (12, text, required): Enter the first name of the person or organization to ship to.
* **Shipping Last Name** (13, text, required): Enter the last name of the person or organization to ship to.
* **Shipping Organization** (14, text, required): Enter the name of the organization or company to ship to.
* **Shipping Street** (15, text, required): Enter the street address of the person or organization to ship to.
* **Shipping City** (16, text, required): Enter the city of the person or organization to ship to.
* **Shipping State** (17, select_one, required): Select the state of the person or organization to ship to.
* **Shipping Zip** (18, number, required): Enter the zip code of the person or organization to ship to.
* **Shipping Phone** (20, text, required): Enter the phone number of the person or organization to ship to.
* **Shipping Email** (21, email, required): Enter the email address of the person or organization to ship to.
* **Comments** (19, note, optional): Add any additional comments about the order or shipment.
* **Delivery Method** (22, select_one, optional): Select the delivery method from the options provided.
* **Shipping First Name** (12) can be skipped if not applicable.
* **Shipping Last Name** (13) can be skipped if not applicable.
* **Shipping Organization** (14) can be skipped if not applicable.
* **Shipping Street** (15) can be skipped if not applicable.
* **Shipping City** (16) can be skipped if not applicable.
* **Shipping Zip** (18) can be skipped if not applicable.
* **Shipping Phone** (20) can be skipped if not applicable.
* **Shipping Email** (21) can be skipped if not applicable.
* **Comments** (19) can be skipped if not applicable.
* **Delivery Method** (22) can be skipped if not applicable.
