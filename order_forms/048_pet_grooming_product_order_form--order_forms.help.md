# Pet Grooming Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers who want to order pet grooming products for their pets. It helps our team process their orders accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, please select the type of pet you are ordering products for from the dropdown menu.
2.  Enter your pet's name in the provided field.
3.  If your pet has an age, please enter its age.
4.  Choose the type of product you want to order from the dropdown menu.
5.  Specify the quantity of the product you want to order.
6.  If you have any special instructions for our team, please enter them in the field provided.
7.  Enter your contact email to ensure we can get in touch with you to discuss your order.
8.  Finally, enter your contact phone number so we can contact you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Type** (`Pet Type`, select_one, required): Please select the type of pet you are ordering products for (e.g. Dog, Cat, etc.).
* **Pet Name** (`Pet Name`, text, required): Enter your pet's name.
* **Pet Age** (`Pet Age`, number, optional): If your pet has an age, please enter it.
* **Product Type** (`Product Type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of product you want to order (e.g. shampoo, conditioner, etc.).
* **Quantity** (`Quantity`, number, required): Specify the quantity of the product you want to order.
* **Special Instructions** (`Special Instructions`, text, optional): If you have any special instructions for our team, please enter them here.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, email, required): Enter your contact email to ensure we can get in touch with you to discuss your order.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number so we can contact you if needed.

## Tips

* Please ensure you fill out all required fields to ensure accurate processing of your order.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to our team for assistance.
* Our team will review your order and get in touch with you promptly to discuss any further details.
