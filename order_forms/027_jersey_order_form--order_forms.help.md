# Jersey Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Jersey Order Form is used to create a custom jersey order for a specific team. This form is designed for team members and staff to submit the necessary information for the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the team information (team name, team email, and team phone number).
2.  Enter the player information (player number, player name, player position, and player size).
3.  Select the jersey type (V-Neck, T-Neck, Long Sleeve, or Short Sleeve).
4.  Choose the color of the jersey.
5.  Select the team color.
6.  Select the player's size.
7.  Enter the order details (order date, order time, and order status).
8.  Enter the payment type (Cash or Credit).
9.  Enter any additional order comments or notes.
10.  Submit the form after ensuring all fields are filled in correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Information** (`team_info`, text, optional): Enter the name of the team.
* **Player Information** (`player_info`, text, optional): Enter the number of the player, name, position, and size.
* **Jersey Information** (`jersey_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the jersey order.
* **Size Information** (`size_info`, text, optional): Enter any size-specific details about the order.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, optional): Enter any payment-related information.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional order details.
* **Player Number** (`player_number`, number, optional): Enter the number of the player.
* **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the player.
* **Player Position** (`player_position`, text, optional): Enter the position of the player in the team.
* **Player Size** (`player_size`, select_one, optional): Choose the size of the jersey for the player.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the order.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the status of the order (New, In Progress, Finished, Cancelled).
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the team.
* **Team Email** (`team_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the team.
* **Team Phone** (`team_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the team.
* **Jersey Type** (`jersey_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of jersey (V-Neck, T-Neck, Long Sleeve, Short Sleeve).
* **Color Choice** (`color_choice`, select_one, optional): Choose the color of the jersey.
* **Team Color** (`team_color`, select_one, optional): Choose the color of the team.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, optional): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Player Notes** (`player_notes`, note, optional): Enter any notes or comments about the player.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person submitting the order.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the order.
* **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the order.
* **Payment Type** (`payment_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the payment type (Cash, Credit).
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the status of the order (New, In Progress, Finished, Cancelled).
