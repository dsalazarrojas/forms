> # culinary_partnership_equipment_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the ordering of equipment and tools for culinary partnerships. It is meant to be used by partners who need to place an order for specific equipment and tools required for their kitchen or restaurant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form instructions to understand the purpose and expected information to be submitted.
2. Select the "form_instructions" field and type in a brief description of the partnership and the type of equipment being requested.
3. In the "form_ingredients" field, specify the quantity of equipment needed.
4. In the "form_equipment_types" field, select the type of equipment required (yes or no).
5. If necessary, select additional equipment types in the "form_equipment_type" field.
6. Enter the date for the order in the "form_date" field.
7. If applicable, select the time of day for the order in the "form_time" field.
8. In the "form_note" field, include any additional comments or instructions for the order.
9. Enter the partner's email address in the "form_email" field.
10. Enter the partner's phone number in the "form_phone" field.
11. For other equipment, select "yes" or "no" as needed.
12. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "form_equipment_order_form_10", "form_equipment_order_form_12", "form_equipment_order_form_13", "form_equipment_order_form_14", "form_equipment_order_form_15", "form_equipment_order_form_16", "form_equipment_order_form_17", "form_equipment_order_form_18", "form_equipment_order_form_19", "form_equipment_order_form_20", "form_equipment_order_form_21", "form_equipment_order_form_22", "form_equipment_order_form_23", and "form_equipment_order_form_24" fields.
