# Purchase Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to facilitate the purchase request and tracking process within your organization.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Complete the form title by entering a brief description of the purchase request.
*   Enter the purchase order date in the "Purchase Order Date" field.
*   In the "Order Description" field, describe the purchase request in detail.
*   In the "Item List" field, list the items you need to purchase, one per line.
*   Enter the name of the person who requested the purchase in the "Requested By" field.
*   Enter the name of the department that handles purchasing in the "Purchasing Department" field.
*   Enter a unique purchase requisition number in the "Purchase Requisition Number" field.
*   Enter the name of the purchasing manager responsible for the purchase order in the "Purchasing Manager" field.
*   Select the current status of the purchase order in the "Purchase Order Status" field. Options are "Approved", "Rejected", and "In Progress".
*   In the "Remarks" field, add any additional comments or notes about the purchase request.

*   **Purchase Requisition Form** (Form Title, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the purchase request.
*   **Purchase Order Date** (Date, required: false): Enter the date the purchase order was issued.
*   **Order Description** (Text, required: false): Describe the purchase request in detail.
*   **Item List** (Text, required: false): List the items you need to purchase, one per line.

*   **Purchasing Department** (Text, required: false): Enter the name of the department that handles purchasing.

*   **Purchase Order Status** (Select One, required: false): Select the current status of the purchase order. Options are "Approved", "Rejected", and "In Progress".
*   **Remarks** (Text, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the purchase request.
