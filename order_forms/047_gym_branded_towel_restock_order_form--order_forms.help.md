# Gym Branded Towel Restock Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Gym Branded Towel Restock Order Form is used to collect information for ordering branded towels from a gym. This form is essential for ensuring that the gym's branded towels are restocked in a timely and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by selecting the "Gym Details" page**, where you will enter your gym's information, including its name and address.
2. **Select the "Towel Selection" page**, where you will choose the type of towels you want to order (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
3. **Move to the "Delivery Details" page**, where you will select the desired delivery date and time.
4. **Add any additional delivery instructions in the "Delivery Note" field** if needed.
5. **On the "Order Details" page**, select whether you want to place an order ("Yes") or not ("No").
6. **Enter your contact details on the "Contact Details" page**, including your name and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gym Details (Gym Details)** (`gym_details`, text, required): Enter your gym's name and address to associate the order with your gym.
* **Towel Selection (Towel Selection)** (`towel_selection`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of towels you want to order by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Delivery Details (Delivery Details)** (`delivery_details`, date, optional): Select the desired delivery date and time for your order.
* **Delivery Note (Delivery Note)** (`delivery_note`, text, optional): Enter any additional delivery instructions or notes, such as "Please deliver to the back door".
* **Order Details (Order Details)** (`order_details`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to place an order for the towels or "No" to cancel the order.
* **Contact Details (Contact Details)** (`contact_details`, text, required): Enter your name and contact information, including phone number and email address, to ensure that we can contact you regarding the order.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information to avoid any issues with delivery or order fulfillment.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us directly.
* Please review your order carefully before submitting it to ensure that all fields are complete and accurate.
