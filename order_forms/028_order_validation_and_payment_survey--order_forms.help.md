# Order Validation And Payment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data for order validation and payment survey, which is essential for e-commerce or retail businesses to track orders, payments, and customer interactions. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully, ensuring all required fields are completed accurately.
2. For fields with options (e.g., Status, Payment Status, Shipping Status), select the relevant option from the provided list.
3. Ensure you provide accurate information for all fields, especially fields with hints (e.g., customer name, customer email, customer phone).

* **Order Details**: Enter a brief description of the order, e.g., the products or services ordered. 
* **Payment Details**: Enter the payment method and any additional details about the payment. 
* **Payment Feedback**: Enter any comments or feedback regarding payment issues or concerns.

* **Confirm Order**: Confirm the order details accurately.

* **Customer Details**: Enter the customer's name, email, and phone number for communication.

* **Payment Date**: Enter the date of payment. 
* **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's name. 
* **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email. 
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number. 
* **Order ID**: Enter the order ID. 
* **Order Date**: Enter the date the order was placed. 
* **Status**: Select the order status (e.g., Ordered, Paid, Pending, Cancelled). 
* **Payment Status**: Select the payment status (e.g., Paid, Not Paid).
