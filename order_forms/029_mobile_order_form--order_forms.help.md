# Mobile Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mobile Order Form is a tool used to collect order details and customer information from customers. This form is used for orders that your organization will be fulfilling.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the "Order" section, which is the first page of the form. You can enter any relevant details or information related to the order.
2. On the second page, you will find the "Customer Info" section. This is where you can provide information about the customer, such as whether they would like to receive a follow-up call.
3. Next, you will come to the "Order Item" section, where you can select whether the order includes an item or not.
4. The "Additional Info" section is a place for any extra notes or comments.
5. The "Name", "Email", "Phone", and "Message" fields are all optional and can be filled out as necessary.
6. Finally, the last two sections are "Order Item 9" and "Note 10". These are optional and can be filled out if necessary.
7. Once you have filled out all the sections, review your form to ensure you have filled out all required fields before submitting.
