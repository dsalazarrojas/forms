# Custom Mug Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Mug Order Form is designed to collect information from customers who want to order custom mugs. This form allows customers to provide their details, select a design preferences, and confirm their order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your name in the "What is your name?" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, if applicable.
4. Select your preferred mug color from the "Select a Mug Color" dropdown menu.
5. Choose your preferred order option from the "How would you like to proceed with your order?" dropdown menu.
6. If you have any additional information about your order, please enter it in the "Additional Information" field.
7. Enter your email address again in the "Confirm Email" field to confirm your order details.
8. Click "Submit" to place your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details**: This section captures basic information about the customer, including name, email, and phone number. Enter your name, email, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
* **What is your name?** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter your email address here. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it here.
* **Select a Mug Color** (`design_preferences_2`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred mug color from the dropdown menu.
* **How would you like to proceed with your order?** (`order_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred order option from the dropdown menu.
* **Personal Message** (`personal_message`, `text`, optional): If you have any personal message you'd like to include with your order, enter it here.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): This is an optional field where you can provide any additional information about your order.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirmation_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to confirm your order details.
* **Design Preferences** (`design_preferences`, `text`, optional): This field is currently not used in this form but is available for future enhancements.
