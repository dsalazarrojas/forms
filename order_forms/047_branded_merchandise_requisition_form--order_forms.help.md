# Branded Merchandise Requisition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information for ordering branded merchandise for internal events, promotional activities, or departmental use. The form helps ensure that the request is properly evaluated, approved, and ordered according to company policies and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as such in the form.
2. Ensure that the Department Name, Requisitioner Name, Email Address, Merchandise Item Description, Quantity Required, Logo Placement, Department Budget Code, Approving Supervisor Name, and Date of Requisition fields are completed accurately.
3. For merchandise requiring a specific event name, provide the Event Name in the If Yes - Event Name field.
4. Indicate if the merchandise is for a specific event by selecting 'True' for the Is this for a specific event? field.
5. Enter the required date for the requisition in the Date of Requisition field.
6. If the merchandise requires approval, ensure that the Approving Supervisor Name field is filled out correctly.
7. Review the form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Department Name** (`dept_name`): Enter the name of the department requesting branded merchandise. This field is required.
* **Requisitioner Name** (`req_name`): Enter your name as the person submitting the requisition request. This field is required.
* **Email Address** (`req_email`): Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes. This field is required.
* **Merchandise Item Description** (`merch_desc`): Briefly describe the item you are requesting, e.g., "Logo T-shirts." This field is required.
* **Quantity Required** (`qty_req`): Enter the number of items required. This field is required.
* **Logo Placement** (`logo_placement_select`): Choose where the logo will be placed on the item:
	+ Front Center
	+ Left Chest
	+ Back
	+ Sleeve
	+ Custom
* **Size Breakdown** (`sizes_text`): Enter a breakdown of sizes, e.g., "10S, 20M, 5L." This field is optional.
* **Is this for a specific event?** (`event_merch_status`): Indicate if the merchandise is for a specific event. This field is required.
* **If Yes - Event Name** (`event_name_val`): If the merchandise is for a specific event, enter the name of that event. This field is optional.
* **Required by Date** (`need_dt`): Enter the date by which the merchandise is required. This field is required.
* **Department Budget Code** (`budget_code_val`): Enter the department's budget code for budgeting and approval purposes. This field is required.
* **Approving Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_sign_name`): Enter the name of the supervisor who needs to approve the requisition. This field is required.
* **Date of Requisition** (`req_dt`): Enter the date the requisition was submitted. This field is required.

Please note that failure to complete this form accurately may delay or affect the processing of your requisition request.
