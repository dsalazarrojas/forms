# Airbnb Reservation Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and update information about an existing or new reservation on Airbnb. It is essential to fill it out accurately to ensure correct booking data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Fill out each field carefully, paying attention to the required fields marked with an asterisk (*) or indicated as "required" in the help guide.
3. If you need to make changes, you can update the form and click "Submit" again.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Reservation name (1)
* **Reservation name**: The name given to the reservation by the host or guest.
* **Type**: Text field.
* **Required**: Yes.
* **Hint**: Name on the reservation.

### Reservation number (2)
* **Reservation number**: A unique reference ID assigned to the reservation.
* **Type**: Text field.
* **Required**: No.
* **Hint**: Booking reference ID.

### Check in date (3)
* **Check in date**: The date the guest is arriving at the accommodation.
* **Type**: Date field.
* **Required**: Yes.
* **Hint**: Arrival date.

### Check out date (4)
* **Check out date**: The date the guest is departing from the accommodation.
* **Type**: Date field.
* **Required**: Yes.
* **Hint**: Departure date.

### Number of guests (5)
* **Number of guests**: The total number of people staying in the accommodation.
* **Type**: Number field.
* **Required**: Yes.
* **Hint**: Total guests in the reservation.

### Payment status (6)
* **Payment status**: Select one of the options:
	+ **Paid**: Payment has been made.
	+ **Pending**: Payment is still pending.
	+ **Refund requested**: A refund has been requested.
* **Type**: Select-one field.
* **Required**: Yes.
* **Hint**: Indicate whether payment is complete.

### Special requests (7)
* **Special requests**: Any additional requests or comments about the booking.
* **Type**: Text field.
* **Required**: No.
* **Hint**: Any additional booking requests.

### Contact email (8)
* **Contact email**: The email address to contact the guest or host.
* **Type**: Email field.
* **Required**: Yes.
* **Hint**: For reservation updates.
