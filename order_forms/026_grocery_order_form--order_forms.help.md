# Grocery Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Grocery Order Form is designed to collect all the necessary information from customers to process their orders efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the order details, including any relevant product information.
2. Provide store details, such as the store location and any specific store instructions.
3. Enter customer information, including name, contact details, and any other relevant contact information.
4. Enter customer address to ensure accurate delivery.
5. Add any order notes or special instructions to the order.
6. Choose a delivery time that suits your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Form** (`order_form`, text, not required): This is the main section where you will enter the order details. Please make sure to include all relevant information about the products being ordered.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, not required): Provide any additional information about the order that is not included in the product information.
* **Store Details** (`store_details`, text, not required): Enter any specific store instructions or information that is relevant to the order.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, not required): Enter customer name, contact number, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, not required): Enter the customer's address to ensure accurate delivery.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, text, not required): Add any notes or special instructions to the order.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, date, not required): Choose a delivery time that suits your needs and is convenient for the customer.
