# Album Order Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Album Order Confirmation" form is used to confirm and finalize an album order. It ensures that all necessary details and approvals are in place before proceeding with production.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your **Order Number** (`order_reference`) in the first field to confirm the order.
2. Provide your **Customer Full Name** (`customer_full_name`) to ensure accurate record keeping.
3. Select the **Album Type and Style** (`album_type_style`) to confirm the album's type and style.
4. Review the **Page Layout Review** (`section_page_reviews`) section and confirm that the proofs sent via email or portal are satisfactory.
5. Choose whether the **Cover Design and Material** (`cover_approval`) meets your requirements, and if not, select "Revision Requested" to initiate a change request.
6. If "Revision Requested" was selected, describe the **Cover Revision Requests** (`cover_revisions`) in the text field.
7. Confirm the **Internal Pages Layout** (`internal_pages_approval`) to ensure all pages from start to finish are correct.
8. Select **Internal Pages Layout Approval** (`internal_pages_approval`) to confirm that all pages are approved or request specific revisions.
9. If revisions are requested, list **Specific Page Numbers and Requested Edits** (`page_revision_details`) in the text field.
10. Confirm the **Finishing and Paper** (`section_finishing`) details for the album.
11. Select the **Paper Type** (`paper_type_confirmed`) to confirm the paper type used for the album.
12. Choose the **Edge Gilding Selection** (`gilding_options`) for the album's edges.
13. Enter your **Delivery Address** (`shipping_address_check`) to confirm the shipping address.
14. Select a **Approval Date** (`final_approval_date`) for the order.
15. Confirm your **Approval and Authorization** (`approval_authorization`) to proceed with production.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Number (order_reference)** (`1`)
	+ Enter your order number to confirm the order details.
* **Customer Full Name (customer_full_name)** (`2`)
	+ Enter the full name of the customer as it appears on your records.
* **Album Type and Style (album_type_style)** (`3`)
	+ Select the type and style of the album to confirm your requirements.
* **Page Layout Review (section_page_reviews)** (`4`)
	+ Review the proofs provided via email or portal to ensure they meet your expectations.
* **Cover Design and Material (cover_approval)** (`5`)
	+ Choose whether the cover design and material meet your requirements. If not, select "Revision Requested" for further changes.
* **Cover Revision Requests (cover_revisions)** (`6`)
	+ Describe the cover revisions needed if "Revision Requested" was selected.
* **Internal Pages Layout (internal_pages_approval)** (`7`)
	+ Confirm that all internal pages from start to finish are correct.
* **Internal Pages Layout Approval (internal_pages_approval)** (`8`)
	+ Select this field to confirm that all internal pages are approved or request specific revisions.
* **Specific Page Numbers and Requested Edits (page_revision_details)** (`9`)
	+ List specific page numbers and edits if revisions are requested.
* **Finishing and Paper (section_finishing)** (`10`)
	+ Review the finishing and paper details for the album.
* **Confirm Paper Type (paper_type_confirmed)** (`11`)
	+ Select the paper type used for the album production.
* **Edge Gilding Selection (gilding_options)** (`12`)
	+ Choose the edge gilding selection for the album.
* **Confirm Delivery Address (shipping_address_check)** (`13`)
	+ Enter your shipping address to confirm delivery.
* **Approval Date (final_approval_date)** (`14`)
	+ Select a date to confirm the order's approval.
* **Approval and Authorization (approval_authorization)** (`15`)
	+ Confirm your approval and authorization to proceed with production.
