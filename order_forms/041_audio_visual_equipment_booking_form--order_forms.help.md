# Audio Visual Equipment Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Audio Visual Equipment Booking Form is designed to help you request audio-visual equipment for events, meetings, or other purposes. Please use this form to book the equipment you need.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your organization or company name.
2. Enter the name of the event.
3. Select the date of the event.
4. Choose the equipment you need (Projector, Projector Screen, LED Display, Document Camera, Laptop/Macbook).
5. Choose the equipment you need (Powered Speaker Pair, Wireless Handheld Mic, Wireless Lavalier/Lapel Mic, Audio Mixer, Bluetooth Audio Receiver).
6. Specify the quantities for each selected equipment item.
7. Choose the logistics option (pick up from Warehouse, Request Delivery to Venue, Delivery and Setup required).
8. Enter the date you would like to pick up or have the equipment delivered.
9. Enter the expected return date for the equipment.
10. Enter an internal budget code (if applicable).
11. Agree to the audio-visual equipment rental terms.
12. Enter your name and contact phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization or Company Name** (`organization_name`): Enter the name of your organization or company.
* **Name of Event** (`event_name`): Enter the name of the event.
* **Date of Event** (`event_date`): Enter the date of the event in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Visual Equipment** (`visual_equipment`): Select all the visual equipment you need (Projector, Projector Screen, LED Display, Document Camera, Laptop/Macbook).
* **Audio Equipment** (`audio_equipment`): Select all the audio equipment you need (Powered Speaker Pair, Wireless Handheld Mic, Wireless Lavalier/Lapel Mic, Audio Mixer, Bluetooth Audio Receiver).
* **Please specify quantities for each item selected** (`quantity_notes`): Enter the quantity of each equipment item you selected.
* **Pickup or Delivery Choice** (`delivery_option`): Choose how you would like to receive the equipment (Pick up from Warehouse, Request Delivery to Venue, Delivery and Setup required).
* **Requested Pickup/Delivery Date** (`pickup_date`): Enter the date you would like to pick up or have the equipment delivered.
* **Expected Return Date** (`return_date`): Enter the date you expect to return the equipment.
* **Internal Budget Code** (`budget_code`): Enter an internal budget code (if applicable).
* **I agree to the AV Equipment Rental Terms** (`rental_terms_agreement`): Select "I Agree" to confirm you understand the rental terms.
* **Authorized Representative Name** (`contact_name`): Enter the name of the person responsible for the equipment.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
