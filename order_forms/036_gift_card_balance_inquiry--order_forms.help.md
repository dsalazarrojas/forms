## Gift Card Balance Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gift Card Balance Inquiry form is designed to help us quickly and accurately check the balance of a gift card. This allows us to provide customers with up-to-date information about their gift card balances.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the gift card number for the card you want to check the balance for. This can be found on the back or on the packaging of the gift card. You can also refer to our FAQs for more information on finding the gift card number.
2. Enter the balance you think the card has, if known. This will help us narrow down our search and speed up the process.
3. Provide the customer's name associated with the gift card. If you're checking the balance for yourself, you can enter your own name here.
4. Enter the customer's email address or phone number, if available. This will help us reach out to the customer with any updates or questions we may have.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about the gift card or the customer. This can be helpful for our team to understand the context of the inquiry.
6. Review and submit the form. Make sure to double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting.
7. Once submitted, our team will review the form and contact the customer with the balance or any updates we find.
