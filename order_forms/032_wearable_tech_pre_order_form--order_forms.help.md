# Wearable Tech Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wearable Tech Pre Order Form is designed to collect information from customers about their wearable technology pre-order requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer information on Page 1.
2. Provide your shipping address on Page 2.
3. Enter your payment information on Page 3.
4. Describe your order details on Page 4.
5. Enter the product information on Page 5.
6. Fill in your billing address on Page 6.
7. Add any additional order notes on Page 7.
8. Enter the total payment amount on Page 8.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (Page 1, `title: `Wearable Tech Pre Order Form`): Enter your name, email, and phone number to help us contact you about your order.
* **Shipping Address** (Page 2, `label: `Shipping Address`): Provide the address where you'd like to receive your order. Make sure it's accurate so we can deliver your order correctly.
* **Payment Info** (Page 3, `label: `Payment Info`): Enter your preferred payment method (e.g., credit card, PayPal) and any additional details required for processing.
* **Order Details** (Page 4, `label: `Order Details`): Describe the specific wearable tech item you'd like to pre-order. Include any customizations or special requests you have.
* **Product Info** (Page 5, `label: `Product Info`): Enter the details of the wearable tech product you're pre-ordering, such as the model number or product description.
* **Billing Address** (Page 6, `label: `Billing Address`): Enter the address where you'd like to receive your bill or invoice for the pre-ordered item.
* **Order Notes** (Page 7, `label: `Order Notes`): Add any additional comments or instructions about your order, such as specific shipping dates or special requests.
* **Payment Total** (Page 8, `label: `Payment Total`): Enter the total amount you're paying for the pre-ordered item.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
