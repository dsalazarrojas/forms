# lics_customer_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture customer order information for Lics. The purpose of this form is to gather relevant details about the customer's purchase, including their information, product selection, order type, delivery preferences, and any notes related to the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's information in the "customer_info" field.
2. Select the products the customer wants to order from the "product_list" dropdown menu.
3. Enter any additional product details in the "product_details" field.
4. Choose the type of order the customer is placing from the "order_type" dropdown menu.
5. Select the preferred delivery type from the "delivery_type" dropdown menu.
6. Specify the delivery date and time in the "delivery_date" and "delivery_time" fields.
7. Add any additional notes about the delivery in the "delivery_note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer_info** (text, required): This is where you enter the customer's name and contact information.
* **product_list** (select multiple, required): This is a dropdown menu where you select the products the customer wants to order. You can choose one or multiple options.
* **product_details** (text, optional): If the customer has any additional information about the products they are ordering, such as special requests or instructions, enter it here.
* **order_type** (select one, required): Choose the type of order the customer is placing, such as "one-time" or "recurring".
* **delivery_type** (select one, required): Select the preferred delivery method, such as "in-person" or "online".
* **delivery_date** (date, required): Specify the date you want to deliver the order.
* **delivery_time** (time, required): Specify the time you want to deliver the order.
* **delivery_note** (note, required): Enter any additional notes or instructions about the delivery, such as parking or access restrictions. 

## Tips
* Double-check that you have filled in all required fields to avoid any errors or issues with processing the order.
* Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information to ensure smooth order processing and delivery.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form or the order, contact the relevant personnel for assistance.
