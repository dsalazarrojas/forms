# seo_services_payment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The seo_services_payment_form is used to collect information from clients for SEO services payment processing and tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **Client Info** field with the client's name and contact information.
2. Enter the **Amount** of the payment.
3. Select the desired **Payment Method** (e.g., PayPal, Stripe, or Credit Card).
4. Provide **Card Info** if paying by credit card.
5. Enter the **Order ID** (if applicable).
6. Choose the **Service Type** (e.g., One-Time or Subscription).
7. Add a brief **Description** of the service provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Info** (Text, Optional): This field is used to collect the client's name and contact information.
* **Amount** (Number, Optional): Enter the total amount of the payment.
* **Payment Method** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the payment method used for this transaction (e.g., PayPal, Stripe, or Credit Card).
* **Card Info** (Text, Optional): This field is used to collect credit card information (only required if paying by credit card).
* **Order ID** (Number, Optional): Enter the order ID for this transaction (if applicable).
* **Service Type** (Select One, Optional): Choose the type of service provided (e.g., One-Time or Subscription).
* **Description** (Note, Optional): Provide a brief description of the service provided.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields for accurate processing.
* Double-check the payment method and amount before submission.
* If paying by credit card, ensure card info is accurate to avoid processing errors.
