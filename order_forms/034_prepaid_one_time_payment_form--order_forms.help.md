# prepaid_one_time_payment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record one-time payments for products or services, ensuring accurate and complete information is captured for each new payment.

## How To Complete This Form
### 1. Enter Your Details
- Fill out all required fields, denoted by an asterisk (\*).
- Provide accurate and clear information for all fields.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name (Customer Name)**: Enter the name of the customer.
* **Order Date (Order Date)**: Enter the date of the order or payment.
* **Product Name (Product Name)**: Enter the name of the product or service being paid for.
* **Product Price (Product Price)**: Enter the price of the product or service being paid for.
* **Payment Method (Payment Method)**: Select the method through which the payment was made (e.g. Bank Transfer, Credit/Debit Card, Mobile Wallet, or Other (Specify)).
* **Payment Amount (Payment Amount)**: Enter the amount paid for the product or service.
* **Payment Method (Payment Method)**: This is a duplicate field of "Payment Method", please ignore if already filled.
* **Order Status (Order Status)**: Select all the statuses that apply for the order, separated by commas. This is typically "Confirmed" if payment is complete, "Replaced" if there is an issue or need for a refund, and "Cancelled" if the payment is invalid or cannot be processed.
* **Customer Signature (Customer Signature)**: Enter your signature, confirming you've filled out the form accurately and truthfully.
* **Signature Date (Signature Date)**: Enter the date you signed the form.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting.
- Ensure you've selected all relevant statuses for "Order Status".
- If you need to make a payment for the same product or service, use this form for each new payment.
