# Girl Scout Uniform Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers to process their Girl Scout Uniform orders. It will help the Girl Scout organization understand what the customer needs, including size, color, and quantity of uniforms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your customer information in the `Customer Info` section.
2. Enter the details of your order in the `Order Info` section.
3. Specify the size of the uniform(s) you need in the `Size Info` section.
4. Indicate the quantity of the uniform(s) you need in the `Quantity Info` section.
5. Select the color of the uniform(s) you need in the `Color Info` section.
6. Enter your shipping details in the `Shipping Info` section.
7. Provide payment information in the `Payment Info` section.
8. Confirm your order by entering the information in the `Confirm Info` section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info (Customer Info)**: Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Order Info (Order Info)**: Provide the type and number of uniforms you need, and any special requests or notes.
* **Size Info (Size Info)**: Specify the size of the uniform(s) you need, including any specific measurements or requirements.
* **Quantity Info (Quantity Info)**: Enter the number of uniforms you need, including any special requests or quantities.
* **Color Info (Color Info)**: Select the color of the uniform(s) you need, including any specific colors or patterns.
* **Shipping Info (Shipping Info)**: Enter your shipping address, including any special requests or instructions.
* **Payment Info (Payment Info)**: Enter your payment method and details, including any payment options or instructions.
* **Confirm Info (Confirm Info)**: Double-check your order details to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely to ensure timely and correct processing of your order.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
- If you need to make any changes to your order, please contact us and we will assist you in the processing.
