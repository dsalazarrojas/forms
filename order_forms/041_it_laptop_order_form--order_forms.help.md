# it_laptop_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from customers for the purpose of ordering a laptop. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your order is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out all required fields. While the form is not strictly mandatory, providing accurate and complete information will help our team process your order efficiently.
2. Answer each field according to your preference and needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, required: false)
The customer info field is used to capture the customer's name and contact information. Please enter your name and any other relevant contact details.

### 2. **Laptop Type** (`laptop_type`, select_one, required: false)
This field allows you to choose whether you want to order a laptop or not. Select 'Yes' if you wish to order a laptop, and 'No' otherwise.

### 3. **Laptop Size** (`laptop_size`, select_multiple, required: false)
For this field, select the size(s) of laptop you are interested in ordering. If you're unsure of the size, please choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate that you want to discuss size options with the sales team.

### 4. **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required: false)
Enter the number of laptops you wish to order.

### 5. **Color** (`color`, select_one, required: false)
Choose the color of the laptop you want to order.

### 6. **Cost** (`cost`, number, required: false)
Enter the approximate cost of the laptop you wish to order.

### 7. **Order Details** (`order_details`, note, required: false)
This field is for any additional information you want to provide about your order. Please feel free to include any relevant comments or questions you may have.

## Tips
Please ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to our sales team.
