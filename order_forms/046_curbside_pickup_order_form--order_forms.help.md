# curbside_pickup_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "curbside_pickup_order_form" is a form designed for customers to provide their information for curbside pickup orders. This form helps us process your order more efficiently and ensure that we have the correct information for delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your customer name, email address, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
2. Select your preferred delivery date and time from the available options.
3. Add any notes or comments about your order if necessary.
4. Choose your payment method (Card, Cash, or Check).
5. Enter the amount of your order.
6. If you have a credit card, you can enter your card number for easy payment processing.
7. Click "Submit" to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your valid email address where we can contact you about your order.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for any questions or updates.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required): Select the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required): Choose the time slot you would like for your order delivery.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any comments or special instructions about your order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to pay for your order: Card, Cash, or Check.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, required): Enter the total amount of your order.
* **Card Number** (`card_number`, text, optional): If you are paying by card, enter your credit card number here.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, not required): Click to complete and submit your order.

## Tips
* Please be sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns about your order, you can contact us directly.
* We accept all major credit cards and payment methods.
* Orders are processed and ready for pickup on the chosen delivery date and time.
