# Packaging Prototype Sample Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Packaging Prototype Sample Request Form" is a form used by customers to request packaging prototype samples from your company. This form is designed to collect necessary information from customers to fulfill their requests efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the required information before submitting it.
2. Fill in the "Packaging Prototype Sample Request" field with a brief description of the packaging prototype you are requesting.
3. In the "Mockup Packaging Request Details" field, provide any additional information about the mockup packaging request, such as the product type or description.
4. Fill in the "Product Information" field with details about the product you are requesting, including its type and description.
5. In the "Customer Information" field, provide your company's details, including your name, company name, department, job title, job description, job function, and email.
6. Fill in the "Shipping Address" field with the shipping address where the packaging prototype sample will be shipped to.
7. Select the shipping method you prefer from the "Shipping Method" dropdown menu.
8. Provide your contact person's name and contact information in the "Contact Person" field.
9. In the "Additional Comments" field, include any additional comments or requests you may have.
10. If the form is submitted correctly, you will receive a follow-up email with further instructions on the status of your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Packaging Prototype Sample Request**: Enter a brief description of the packaging prototype you are requesting.
* **Mockup Packaging Request Details**: Provide any additional information about the mockup packaging request, such as the product type or description.
* **Product Information**: Enter details about the product you are requesting, including its type and description.
* **Customer Information**:
	+ **Name**: Enter your name.
	+ **Company**: Enter your company name.
	+ **Department**: Enter your department name.
	+ **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
	+ **Job Description**: Enter a brief description of your job.
	+ **Job Function**: Enter your job function.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the shipping address where the packaging prototype sample will be shipped to.
* **Shipping Method**: Select the shipping method you prefer.
* **Contact Person**: Provide your contact person's name and contact information.
* **Additional Comments**: Include any additional comments or requests you may have.
* **Sample Request Status**:
	+ **Waiting**: This status indicates that your request is pending.
	+ **Approved**: This status indicates that your request has been approved.
	+ **Rejected**: This status indicates that your request has been rejected.
* **Sample Request ID**: This is a unique identifier for your request.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Sample Request Form Submitted**: Select "True" if you have submitted the form correctly.
* **Order Form Submitted**: Select "True" if you have submitted the order form correctly.
* **Quote Submitted**: Select "True" if you have submitted a quote for the request.
* **Quote Accepted**: Select "True" if the quote has been accepted.
* **Quote Rejected**: Select "True" if the quote has been rejected.
