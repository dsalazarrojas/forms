# Shipping Waybill Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shipping Waybill Form is used to collect important information about the shipper and the shipment. This form is a critical part of the order fulfillment process and should be completed accurately and carefully.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the shipper's name and information in the "Shipper Info" field.
2. Provide the shipper's address in the "Shipper Address" field.
3. Enter the shipper's city in the "Shipper City" field.
4. Enter the shipper's state or province in the "Shipper State" field.
5. Enter the shipper's postal or zip code in the "Shipper Postal Code" field.
6. Enter the shipper's country in the "Shipper Country" field.
7. Enter the shipper's phone number in the "Shipper Phone" field.
8. Enter the shipper's email address in the "Shipper Email" field.
9. Provide more information about the shipment in the "Shipment Info" field.
10. Enter the shipper's company name in the "Shipper Company" field.
11. Provide the shipper's address again in the "Shipper Address 2" field.
12. Enter the shipper's phone number again in the "Shipper Phone 2" field.
13. Enter the shipper's email address again in the "Shipper Email 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shipper Info** (Shipper Info, text, required): This field collects the shipper's name and other identifying information.
* **Shipper Address** (Shipper Address, text, required): This field collects the shipper's address, including street, suite, or apartment number.
* **Shipper City** (Shipper City, text, required): Enter the shipper's city or town where the shipment is being sent.
* **Shipper State** (Shipper State, text, required): This field collects the shipper's state or province.
* **Shipper Postal Code** (Shipper Postal Code, text, required): This field collects the shipper's postal or zip code.
* **Shipper Country** (Shipper Country, text, required): This field collects the shipper's country.
* **Shipper Phone** (Shipper Phone, text, required): This field is used to collect the shipper's phone number.
* **Shipper Email** (Shipper Email, email, required): This field collects the shipper's email address.
* **Shipment Info** (Shipment Info, text, required): This field provides more information about the shipment.
* **Shipper Company** (Shipper Company, text, required): This field collects the shipper's company name.
* **Shipper Address 2** (Shipper Address 2, text, required): This field is a duplicate of the shipper's address.
* **Shipper Phone 2** (Shipper Phone 2, text, required): This field is a duplicate of the shipper's phone number.
* **Shipper Email 2** (Shipper Email 2, text, required): This field is a duplicate of the shipper's email address.
