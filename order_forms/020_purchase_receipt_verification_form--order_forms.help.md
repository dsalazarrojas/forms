# purchase_receipt_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify the purchase receipt of a customer for a purchase made. It is used for internal purposes, such as for accounting or inventory management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with the relevant information about the customer, date of the purchase, total cost, payment method, items purchased, and receipt confirmation from the customer and business.
2. Fill in the required fields with the correct information, indicated by a `*` symbol in the field label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details**: This section asks for information about the customer, such as name and contact details. Please provide the customer's full name and contact information.
* **Purchase Date**: This field is for recording the date of the purchase. Please enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Total Cost**: This field is for recording the total cost of the items purchased. Please enter the total amount in the currency of the purchase.
* **Payment Method**: This field is for selecting the payment method used for the purchase. Please choose one or more of the following options: credit card, paypal, cash, bank transfer.
* **Items Purchased**: This field is for listing the items purchased. Please provide a detailed list of the items purchased, including quantities and prices.
* **Receipt Confirmation**: This field is for recording the confirmation from the customer and business that the receipt is accurate. Please provide a brief note with the details of the confirmation.
* **Customer Signature**: This field is for capturing the customer's signature. Please have the customer sign and date their signature.
* **Business Signature**: This field is for capturing the business owner's or authorized representative's signature. Please have them sign and date their signature.

## Tips
Please review the form carefully before submission to ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
