# Delivery Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to provide delivery information, including their details and order details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your delivery order details in the "order_details" field.
2. Select the options for "order_items" as required.
3. Enter your delivery address and contact information in the "delivery_address" and "delivery_contact" fields.
4. Set the "payment_terms" and "payment_status" to the applicable options.
5. Enter your delivery instructions and order notes in the "delivery_instructions", "delivery_instructions_2", "delivery_notes", and "notes" fields.
6. Enter the order total in the "order_total" field.
7. Enter your email and phone number in the "customer_email" and "customer_phone" fields.
8. Enter your delivery date and time in the "delivery_date" and "delivery_time" fields.
