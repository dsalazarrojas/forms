# Sample Product Order - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for placing a sample product order. It will help us gather essential details about the customer and the order they wish to place.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out all required fields (indicated by *) with your information.
* For fields with options, select the relevant value from the provided list.
* Double-check your input for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the customer.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the customer's full name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the product being ordered.
* **Product SKU** (`product_sku`, text, optional): Enter the stock keeping unit (SKU) number of the product.
* **Product Price** (`product_price`, number, optional): Enter the price of the product.
* **Product Quantity** (`product_quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of the product being ordered.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the order.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, optional): Enter the customer's address.
* **Customer Postal Code** (`customer_postal`, text, optional): Enter the customer's postal code.
* **Customer City** (`customer_city`, text, optional): Enter the customer's city.
* **Customer State** (`customer_state`, text, optional): Enter the customer's state or region.
* **Customer Zip** (`customer_zip`, text, optional): Enter the customer's zip code.
* **Customer Country** (`customer_country`, text, optional): Enter the customer's country.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, note, optional): Enter any notes or comments about the customer.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select one of the following payment methods:
		- PayPal
		- Bank Transfer
		- Credit Card
* **Product Details** (`product_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional product details.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select one or more of the following order status:
		- pending
		- shipped
		- canceled
* **Order Date Shipped** (`order_date_shipped`, date, optional): Enter the date the order was shipped.
* **Order Date Canceled** (`order_date_canceled`, date, optional): Enter the date the order was canceled.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, optional): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the order.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the tool assigned to the order.
* **Assigned Tool Version** (`assigned_tool_version`, text, optional): Enter the version of the assigned tool.

This help guide should provide a clear and concise explanation of the form and its fields.
