# Landscaping Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients about their landscaping services for billing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section with the required information. Start by filling out the Client Information section, then the Service Details section, and finally the Payment Info section. If you are unsure about any of the information requested, please ask your administrator for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Information
* **Client Information** (`client_info`, Text, Optional): Enter the client's name, phone number, and other relevant contact information. This field allows you to store a single entry per client.

### Service Details
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, Text, Optional): Provide details about the services provided to the client, including any special requests or notes. This field is free-form, allowing you to input whatever is necessary to capture the service information.

### Payment Info
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, Text, Optional): Enter payment information, such as payment method (e.g., credit card, bank transfer) or other relevant details. This field should be used for billing purposes only and is meant to store payment information.
