# high_precision_cutting_blade_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect necessary information from customers for a high-precision cutting blade order.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **customer_name** field with the customer's full name.
2. Enter the customer's email address in the **customer_email** field.
3. Enter the customer's phone number in the **customer_phone** field.
4. Select the desired delivery address from the drop-down menu in the **delivery_address** field.
5. If there are any special instructions or requests, type them in the **special_instructions** field.
6. Finally, click the **Submit** button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header** (`form_header`, `note`, required: false): This section is just a header for the form and does not require any input.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the customer's full name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the desired delivery address from the drop-down menu.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `text`, required: false): If there are any special instructions or requests, type them in this field.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required: false): Click this button to submit the form and complete the order.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid any delays or errors in the order process.
* Double-check the form before submitting to ensure that all required fields are filled out.
* If you need to make any changes to the order, please contact the sales team directly.
