# Pre Purchase Inspection Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is meant to provide a clear understanding of the Pre Purchase Inspection Agreement form and its required information. 

## How To Complete This Form

*   Review the form carefully to ensure you understand all the sections and fields.
*   Answer all required fields to the best of your ability and truthfully.
*   Double-check for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Buyer Name (buyer_name)**: The name of the person or entity purchasing the vehicle.
    *   This field is required.
*   **Seller Name (seller_name)**: The name of the individual or entity selling the vehicle.
    *   This field is required.
*   **Vehicle Year (vehicle_year)**: The year the vehicle was manufactured.
    *   This field is not required.
*   **Vehicle Make (vehicle_make)**: The brand of the vehicle (e.g., Toyota, Honda, etc.).
    *   This field is not required.
*   **Vehicle Model (vehicle_model)**: The model of the vehicle (e.g., Corolla, Civic, etc.).
    *   This field is not required.
*   **Year of Manufacture (vehicle_year_of_manufacture)**: The year the vehicle was manufactured.
    *   This field is not required.
*   **Seller Address (seller_address)**: The address of the seller, if applicable.
    *   This field is not required.
*   **Seller Phone (seller_phone)**: The contact phone number of the seller, if applicable.
    *   This field is not required.
*   **Buyer Phone (buyer_phone)**: The contact phone number of the buyer.
    *   This field is not required.
*   **Vehicle VIN (vehicle_vin)**: The Vehicle Identification Number assigned by the manufacturer.
    *   This field is not required.
*   **Vehicle License Plate (vehicle_license_plate)**: The license plate number of the vehicle.
    *   This field is not required.
*   **Registration Status (vehicle_registration_status)**: The status of the vehicle's registration (e.g., valid, suspended, etc.).
    *   This field is not required.
*   **Engine Number (vehicle_engine_number)**: The serial number of the vehicle's engine.
    *   This field is not required.
*   **Transmission (vehicle_transmission)**: The type of transmission in the vehicle (e.g., manual, automatic, etc.).
    *   This field is not required.
*   **Vehicle Color (vehicle_color)**: The color of the vehicle (e.g., black, white, etc.).
    *   This field is not required.
*   **Odometer Reading (vehicle_odometer)**: The reading of the vehicle's odometer.
    *   This field is required.
*   **Mileage Reading (vehicle_mileage)**: The total mileage of the vehicle.
    *   This field is required.
*   **Vehicle Condition (vehicle_condition)**: The overall condition of the vehicle (e.g., excellent, fair, etc.).
    *   This field is required.
*   **Warranty (vehicle_warranty)**: If the vehicle has any active warranty.
    *   This field is not required.
*   **Notes (vehicle_notes)**: Any additional notes or comments about the vehicle.
    *   This field is not required.
*   **Year of Last Servicing (vehicle_year_of_last_servicing)**: The year the vehicle was last serviced.
    *   This field is not required.
*   **Year of Last Servicing Mileage (vehicle_year_of_last_servicing_mileage)**: The mileage of the vehicle at the last servicing.
    *   This field is not required.
*   **Inspection Date (vehicle_inspection)**: The date of the vehicle inspection.
    *   This field is required.
*   **Inspection Result (vehicle_inspection_result)**: The outcome of the vehicle inspection.
    *   This field is required.
*   **Seller Signature (seller_signature)**: The signature of the seller, indicating they have agreed on the terms of the form.
    *   This field is required.
*   **Additional Tips:**
    *   Be honest and accurate in your answers.
    *   Read and understand the terms before signing the form.
