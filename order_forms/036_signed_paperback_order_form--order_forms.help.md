# signed_paperback_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from submitters about their orders and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Submitter Details section with your name, title, department, and job title.
2. Provide your contact information: email and phone number.
3. Answer the order-related questions: order item, order description, quantity, and price.
4. Choose your order and payment method (credit card, cash, or PayPal).
5. Confirm your payment status (paid, pending, or unpaid).
6. Add any additional submitter notes.
7. Sign the form to confirm your submission.
8. Date and confirm your submission.
