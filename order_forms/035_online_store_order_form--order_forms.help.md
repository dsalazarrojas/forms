## Purpose
The Online Store Order Form is used for customers to place orders for products from our online store. This form collects customer information, order details, and payment information to facilitate the order process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Details** on the first page.
2. Provide your **Order Date** and **Product Quantity** for the ordered item(s).
3. Enter your **Email** and **Phone Number** (if applicable) for contact purposes.
4. Choose your preferred **Payment Method** (Cash or Credit Card).
5. Select the status of the **Product** (In Stock, Out of Stock, or Discontinued).
6. Indicate the status of the **Customer** (Active, Inactive, or Verified).
