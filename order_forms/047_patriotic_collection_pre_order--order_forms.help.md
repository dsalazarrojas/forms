# patriotic_collection_pre_order - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect customer information and order details for a patriotic collection.

## How to Complete This Form

1. Fill in your first name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your last name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field.
4. (Optional) Enter your date of birth in the Date of Birth field.
5. Fill in your address in the Address field.
6. (Optional) Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
7. Specify the quantity of items or services you wish to order in the Quantity field.
8. Choose your preferred shipping method from the options provided in the Shipping Method field.
9. Choose your preferred payment method from the options provided in the Payment Method field.
10. (Optional) Add any additional comments or notes about your order in the Order Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, required: false)
	+ Collects the customer's first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required: true)
	+ Collects the customer's last name.
* **Email** (Email, email, required: true)
	+ Collects the customer's email address.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required: false)
	+ Collects the customer's date of birth (likely for age verification or other purposes).
* **Address** (Address, text, required: true)
	+ Collects the customer's address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required: false)
	+ Collects the customer's phone number.
* **Quantity** (Quantity, number, required: true)
	+ Collects the number of items or services ordered.
* **Shipping Method** (Shipping Method, select, required: false)
	+ Collects the customer's preferred shipping method (FedEx, UPS, or USPS).
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select, required: true)
	+ Collects the customer's preferred payment method (Cash, Credit, or Debit).
* **Order Comments** (Order Comments, note, required: false)
	+ Collects any additional comments or notes about the order.

## Tips

* Ensure you enter the correct information for each field to ensure accurate processing of your order.
* Make sure to choose the correct shipping method and payment method that suits your needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the customer support team.
