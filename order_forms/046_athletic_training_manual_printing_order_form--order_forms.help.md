# Athletic Training Manual Printing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for ordering athletic training manuals in print. It captures all necessary information for accurate processing and fulfillment of your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your organization's name, department, and contact person's name and email address.
2. Specify the manual title, type, and quantity needed.
3. Choose the paper size, type, and finish, as well as the binding and cover type.
4. Choose color options, including full color or black and white only.
5. Determine if you need to review a proof of the manual before printing.
6. Select your preferred shipping method and address.
7. Specify your payment method and any special instructions or requests.
8. Indicate if this is a rush order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name** (1, text, required): Enter your school, clinic, or organization name.
* **Department** (2, select_one, required): Choose from Athletics, Sports Medicine, Athletic Training, Education, or Other.
* **Contact Person Name** (3, text, required): Enter the primary contact person's name for this order.
* **Contact Email** (4, email, required): Enter the contact email for order communications.
* **Contact Phone** (5, text, required): Enter the primary contact person's phone number for order questions.
* **Order Date** (6, date, required): Enter the date of the order.
* **Needed By Date** (7, date, required): Enter the date by which you need the manual.
* **Manual Title** (8, text, required): Enter the title of the manual to be printed.
* **Manual Type** (9, select_one, required): Choose from Training Procedures Manual, Emergency Care Manual, Rehabilitation Guide, Sports Medicine Reference, Custom Manual, or Existing Manual Reprint.
* **Quantity** (10, number, required): Enter the number of manuals to print.
* **Estimated Page Count** (11, number, required): Enter the estimated number of pages per manual.
* **Paper Size** (12, select_one, required): Choose from Letter (8.5 x 11), Legal (8.5 x 14), A4, Half Letter (5.5 x 8.5), or Custom Size.
* **Paper Type** (13, select_one, required): Choose from Standard Copy Paper, Premium Bond Paper, Cardstock Cover, Glossy Finish, or Matte Finish.
* **Color Options** (14, select_one, required): Choose from Full Color Throughout, Black and White Only, Color Cover, B and W Inside, or Spot Color Only.
* **Binding Type** (15, select_one, required): Choose from Spiral Bound, Saddle Stitched, Perfect Bound, Three-Hole Punched, Comb Bound, or Wire-O Bound.
* **Cover Type** (16, select_one, required): Choose from Soft Cover, Hard Cover, Laminated Cover, Cardstock Cover, or No Cover.
* **Cover Finish** (17, select_one, optional): Choose from Glossy, Matte, or Soft Touch. (Optional, but recommended if choosing a cover type.)
* **Tabs and Dividers** (18, select_one, optional): Choose from Yes - Color Tabs, Yes - White Tabs, or No. (Optional, but recommended for easier manual use.)
* **Customization Needed** (19, select_one, required): Choose from No - Print As Is, Yes - Add Organization Name, Yes - Add Logo, or Yes - Custom Sections.
* **Logo File** (20, text, optional): If you need to upload a logo or provide a description for customizing the manual.
* **Content Source** (21, select_one, required): Choose from Provide Digital File, Use Existing Template, Need Content Created, or Update Previous Version.
* **File Format** (22, select_one, optional): Choose from PDF, Word Document, InDesign, or Not Applicable. (Optional, but recommended if providing a digital file.)
* **Proof Required** (23, select_one, required): Choose from Yes - Email Proof, Yes - Physical Proof, or No - Print Directly.
* **Estimated Unit Price** (24, number, optional): Enter the estimated unit price in USD. (Optional, but recommended for accurate billing.)
* **Total Budget** (25, number, optional): Enter the total budget for the order. (Optional, but recommended for accurate billing.)
* **Shipping Method** (26, select_one, required): Choose from Standard Shipping, Express Shipping, Overnight Shipping, Pickup at Print Shop, or Local Delivery.
* **Delivery Address** (27, text, required): Enter the address where the order should be delivered.
* **Delivery Instructions** (28, text, optional): Enter any special delivery instructions or notes.
* **Budget or Account Code** (29, text, optional): Enter the budget or account code for billing purposes.
* **Purchase Order Number** (30, text, optional): Enter the purchase order number if applicable.
* **Payment Method** (31, select_one, required): Choose from Credit Card, Purchase Order, Check, Wire Transfer, or Net 30 Account.
* **Special Instructions** (32, text, optional): Enter any special requests or notes.
* **Rush Order** (33, select_one, required): Choose from No - Standard Processing or Yes - Rush Processing.
