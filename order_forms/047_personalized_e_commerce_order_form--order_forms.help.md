# Personalized E Commerce Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer information for an e-commerce order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required information for each field as accurately as possible.
2.  Double-check your entries to ensure they are correct before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info (customer_info)**: Enter your contact details, such as name and email.
* **Shipping Address (shipping_address)**: Enter your shipping address, including street address and any other relevant details.
* **Product Info (product_info)**: Enter the product or products you are ordering, including any relevant details.
* **Payment Info (payment_info)**: Enter your payment method or method details. (Note: This field may not be used for actual payment processing.)
* **Order Info (order_info)**: Enter any additional order details, such as order comments or special requests.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled in accurately to avoid delays or errors in order fulfillment.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact us for assistance.
