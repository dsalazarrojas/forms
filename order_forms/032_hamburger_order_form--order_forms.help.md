# Hamburger Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hamburger Order Form is designed to collect information from the customer to process and fulfill their hamburger order. This form is split into several sections to guide the customer through the ordering process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. The customer will be presented with the main page, where they can review the form's instructions and proceed with their order.
2. On the customer info page, select one or more of the available options for the customer's name.
3. On the order details page, select the type of patty for their order (Beef, Veggie, Chicken, or None).
4. On the order options page, select any additional options for their order (Small, Medium, or Large). This selection will only be required if the customer has chosen an order detail type that has options (e.g., selecting a patty type that has no options will skip this page).
5. On the payment info page, select the payment method for their order (Cash, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer).
6. On the final page, enter the order total, which will be confirmed by the customer to finalize their order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (customer_info): On this page, select your name from the list of available options.
* **Order Details** (order_details): Choose the type of patty for your order.
* **Order Options** (order_options): If you selected a type of patty with options, choose any additional order options here.
* **Payment Info** (payment_info): Select the payment method for your order.
* **Order Total** (order_total): Enter the total amount for your order.

## Tips

* Please double-check your selections before proceeding to the final page to ensure accuracy.
* If you encounter any issues or have questions, please contact us for assistance.
