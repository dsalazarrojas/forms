# massageTherapySubscriptionForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients or businesses who wish to subscribe to massage therapy services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information and any other details relevant to your subscription.
2. Choose your massage therapy plan (if applicable).
3. Enter your payment information (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Client Info**
* **Client Info**: <code>Client Info</code> (text, optional): Please enter your name, address, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.

### 2. **Plan**
* **Plan**: <code>Plan</code> (text, optional): Choose your preferred massage therapy plan (if you have one). If you don't have a plan, simply enter "Not Applicable".

### 3. **Payment Info**
* **Payment Info**: <code>Payment Info</code> (text, optional): Enter your payment method and any other details necessary for payment processing (if applicable).

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately to facilitate smooth subscription processing.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact our support team for clarification.
* You can modify this form as needed to suit your purposes.
