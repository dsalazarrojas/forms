# Trading Cards Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect pre-order information from users, allowing us to fulfill their requests correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your **User Information** in the first field. This includes your name, address, and any other relevant contact details.
2. Enter your **Order Information**, such as the number of cards you wish to order and any special requests.
3. Enter your **Payment Information**, including the payment method and amount.
4. If you have any additional comments or requests, you can add them in the **Comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (User Information, text, required)**: Enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Order Information (Order Information, text, optional)**: Enter the number of cards you wish to order and any special requests.
* **Payment Information (Payment Information, text, required)**: Enter the payment method and amount.
* **Comments (Comments, text, optional)**: Enter any additional comments or requests you may have.
