# Product And Service Listing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data from users for listing products and services. The form is intended for internal use within the organization to manage the listing of products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the service type that best describes your product or service.
2. Select all applicable product types related to your product or service.
3. Provide a brief description of your product or service.
4. If applicable, add a URL to an image related to your product or service.
5. Enter a category for your product or service.
6. Enter a price for your product or service.
7. Enter a contact phone number for your product or service.
8. Enter a contact email address for your product or service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product and Service Form** (`product_and_service_form`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your product or service. This field allows you to provide a short summary of your product or service.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, optional): Select the service type that best describes your product or service. This field is used to categorize your service type.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable product types related to your product or service. This field is used to categorize your product type.
* **Description** (`description`, note, optional): Enter a detailed description of your product or service. This field allows you to provide a more extensive summary of your product or service.
* **Category** (`category`, text, optional): Enter a category for your product or service. This field is used to further categorize your product or service.
* **Price** (`price`, number, optional): Enter a price for your product or service. This field is used to specify the price of your product or service.
* **Image URL** (`image_url`, text, optional): Enter a URL to an image related to your product or service. This field is used to add a visual representation of your product or service.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter a contact phone number for your product or service. This field is used to contact you for further information.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter a contact email address for your product or service. This field is used to contact you for further information.
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, text, optional): Enter additional service details. This field allows you to provide more specific information about your service.
* **Product Details** (`product_details`, text, optional): Enter additional product details. This field allows you to provide more specific information about your product.
