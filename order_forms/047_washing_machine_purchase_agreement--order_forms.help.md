# washing_machine_purchase_agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information for a washing machine purchase agreement. It is used to gather details from customers regarding their purchase experience, including sales and delivery terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal and contact information on the "Customer Information" page.
2. Specify the sales terms you agree to on the "Sales Terms" page.
3. Enter the delivery details you require on the "Delivery Details" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information**: (`id: 1`, `type: text`, not required): Please enter your personal and contact information such as your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Sales Terms**: (`id: 2`, `type: text`, not required): Describe the sales terms you agreed upon or accepted for the purchase. This could include any discounts, payment terms, or other conditions.
* **Delivery Details**: (`id: 3`, `type: text`, not required): Enter the delivery details you require, such as the delivery address, delivery date, and any specific instructions for the delivery.
