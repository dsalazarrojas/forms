# Adaptive Underwear Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adaptive Underwear Order Form is designed to collect information for adaptive underwear orders, ensuring accurate and personalized garment selection for customers with sensitive skin or medical requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address.
2. Select your preferred shipping address.
3. Choose your style and type of underwear from the options provided.
4. Select your preferred color.
5. Enter the number of packs you would like to order.
6. If you have any sensitive skin or material allergies, please mention them in the Special Requirements / Material Allergies field.
7. Choose a payment method for your order.
8. Confirm that you consent to the processing of this personal data for order fulfillment.
9. Select an order date for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Caregiver / Representative Name (if applicable)**: If you are not the customer, please enter the name of the person you are representing.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your shipping address as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for order confirmation and tracking purposes.
* **Style of Adaptive Closure**: Select the type of adaptive closure you require (e.g. Side-Opening (Magnetic), Side-Opening (Velcro / Hook and Loop), Front-Opening, Post-Surgery Snap Style, or Easy-Grip Loops).
* **Underwear Type**: Select the type of underwear you require (e.g. Briefs (Men's), Boxers (Men's), Panties (Women's), High-Waist Support, or Unisex Absorbent Style).
* **Size**: Select your size from the options provided, please refer to our size guide link for more information.
* **Color Preference**: Select your preferred color for the underwear.
* **Quantity (Packs of 3)**: Enter the number of packs you would like to order.
* **Special Requirements / Material Allergies**: If you have any sensitive skin or material allergies, please mention them here (e.g. Latex-free, 100% cotton only).
* **Delivery Instructions**: If you have any specific delivery instructions, please enter them here.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you prefer (Online Credit Card, PayPal, Invoice to Care Facility, NDIS / Insurance Billing).
* **I consent to the processing of this personal data for order fulfillment**: Confirm that you consent to the processing of this personal data for order fulfillment.
* **Order Date**: Select an order date for your order.

Note: This form is designed to be completed in conjunction with our size guide link, available on our website. Please refer to it for more information on sizing.
