# Serum Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Serum Order Form is a straightforward form used to collect information from customers or users about their serum orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and select your options as necessary. The required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*). Start by filling in the form, then submit your order when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Page
This is the first page of the form. It is not a field, but rather a page indicator.

### Name
\* Name (text field)
Provide your name as it appears on your identification.

### Phone
Phone (text field, optional)
Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted.

### Email
\* Email (email field, optional)
Enter your email address to receive order updates.

### Product
\* Product (select one)
Select the type of serum product you would like to order from the available options.

### Submit
Submit (submit button)
Click this button to finalize your order and submit it to our team.

## Tips
- Please fill in all required fields (\*) before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or issues, feel free to contact our customer support team.
- Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
