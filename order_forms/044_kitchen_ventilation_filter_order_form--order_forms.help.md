# Kitchen Ventilation Filter Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kitchen Ventilation Filter Order Form is designed to gather information from customers to process their kitchen ventilation filter orders. This form is intended for customers who want to place an order for kitchen ventilation filters.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your personal details:
   * **First Name**: Enter your first name.
   * **Last Name**: Enter your last name. This field is required.
   * **Order Date**: Enter the date you want to order the kitchen ventilation filter.
   * **Order Time**: Enter the time you want to place the order. This field is not required.
   * **Order Notes**: Add any additional notes about your order.
2. Provide your contact information:
   * **Email**: Enter your email address.
   * **Phone**: Enter your phone number. (This field is not required.)
3. Specify shipping information:
   * **Order Address**: Enter your order address.
   * **Shipping Method**: Choose the shipping method you prefer.
   * **Ship to State**: Select the state where you want to ship the order. This field is required.
   * **Ship to Zip**: Enter the zip code for the shipping destination. (This field is not required.)
   * **Ship to City**: Enter the city where you want to ship the order.
4. Additional shipping options:
   * **Ship To State 2**: Select additional states where you want to ship the order. (This field is not required.)
   * **Ship To Country**: Select whether you want to ship to countries. (This field is not required.)
   * **Ship To Province**: Select additional provinces where you want to ship the order. (This field is not required.)
   * **Ship To Region**: Enter the region where you want to ship the order. (This field is not required.)
   * **Ship To Zip 2**: Enter the zip code for the shipping destination. (This field is not required.)
   * **Ship To City 2**: Enter the city where you want to ship the order. (This field is not required.)
   * **Ship To State 3**: Select additional states where you want to ship the order. (This field is not required.)
   * **Ship To Country 2**: Select whether you want to ship to countries. (This field is not required.)
   * **Ship To Province 2**: Select additional provinces where you want to ship the order. (This field is not required.)
   * **Ship To Region 2**: Enter the region where you want to ship the order. (This field is not required.)
