# Catering Equipment Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the check out process for catering equipment used for events. It ensures that equipment is accurately tracked and recorded for return and potential fees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and the name of the person or organization you are representing.
2. Provide the event name or reference number.
3. Select the rental period, including the date equipment is checked out and due to be returned.
4. List the equipment categories and specific items selected for rental.
5. Specify any pre-existing damage or imperfections on the equipment.
6. Confirm that you understand and agree to the rental terms, including the cleanliness agreement and liability responsibility.
7. Enter the total rental fee and any security deposit amount.
8. Confirm your payment status (paid, deposit paid, internal charge).
9. Sign and date your name, as well as have a warehouse staff member witness your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Renter or Event Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person or organization checking out the equipment.
* **Organization or Catering Team**: Enter the name of the organization or team you are representing.
* **Event Name/Reference**: Enter the name of the event or a reference number for tracking.
* **Date of Check-Out**: Enter the date you are checking out the equipment.
* **Scheduled Return Date**: Enter the date you expect to return the equipment.
* **Select Equipment Categories**: Choose all applicable categories of equipment being rented.
* **Itemized List and Quantities**: List and count specific items rented, e.g. "4x Large Chafers, 2x 5-Gal Urns".
* **Condition of Equipment at Check-out**: Describe any existing scratches, dents, or imperfections on the equipment.
* **Total Rental Fee**: Enter the total fee for the rental period.
* **Security Deposit Amount**: Enter the deposit amount, which is refundable upon return of clean equipment.
* **Payment Status**: Confirm payment status: fully paid, deposit paid with balance due, or an internal charge.
* **I Agree to Return Equipment Clean and Dry**: Confirm you understand the cleanliness agreement.
* **I Accept Responsibility for Any Loss or Damage**: Confirm you understand the liability agreement.
* **Renter Signature (Type Name)**: Sign and date your name, as well as have a warehouse staff member witness your signature.
* **Warehouse Staff Witness**: Sign and date the name of the staff member witnessing your signature.
