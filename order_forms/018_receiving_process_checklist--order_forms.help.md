This form is designed to document and record the receiving process for goods or shipments at a warehouse or distribution center. The purpose of this form is to ensure accurate and efficient tracking of incoming deliveries, and to provide relevant information for inventory management, quality control, and other relevant processes. This form is not intended to be a comprehensive order management system, but rather a tool to support the receiving process. 

# Receiving Process Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document and record the receiving process for goods or shipments at a warehouse or distribution center.

## How To Complete This Form
This form is divided into several sections, each corresponding to a different aspect of the receiving process. To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Delivery Note**: Enter a brief note about the delivery, such as the delivery date, time, or any special instructions.
2.  **Supplier Info**: Provide basic information about the supplier, such as their name and contact details.
3.  **Order Info**: Enter details about the order, including the order number and any relevant information about the order.
4.  **Shipment Info**: Document the shipment details, such as the shipment number and any other relevant information.
5.  **Item Info**: Provide information about the items received, including their description and quantity.
6.  **Receiving Date**: Enter the date of the receiving process.
7.  **Receiving Time**: Record the time of receiving.
8.  **Warehouse Info**: Enter details about the warehouse where the items were received.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Delivery Note** (`delivery_note`, text, optional): Enter a brief note about the delivery, such as the delivery date, time, or any special instructions.
* **Supplier Info** (`supplier_info`, text, optional): Provide basic information about the supplier, such as their name and contact details.
* **Order Info** (`order_info`, text, optional): Enter details about the order, including the order number and any relevant information about the order.
* **Shipment Info** (`shipment_info`, text, optional): Document the shipment details, such as the shipment number and any other relevant information.
* **Item Info** (`item_info`, text, optional): Provide information about the items received, including their description and quantity.
* **Receiving Date** (`receiving_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the receiving process.
* **Receiving Time** (`receiving_time`, time, optional): Record the time of receiving.
* **Warehouse Info** (`warehouse_info`, text, optional): Enter details about the warehouse where the items were received.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete this form as accurately and thoroughly as possible to ensure accurate inventory tracking and other relevant processes.
* Double-check all fields for completeness and accuracy before submitting the form.
* This form is for internal use only, do not share with external parties. 
Note that some fields are not required, but completing all sections will provide a more complete record of the receiving process.
