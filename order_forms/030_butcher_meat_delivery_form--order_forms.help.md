# Butcher Meat Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for their meat delivery order. It includes fields for customer contact details, delivery preferences, order type and quantity, and additional notes or special instructions.

### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide your delivery address, city, and ZIP code.
3. Choose a preferred delivery date and time window.
4. Select the type of order you would like to place (one-time order, regular subscription, or wholesale order).
5. Choose the type(s) of meat you desire (beef, pork, chicken, etc.).
6. Select the specific cuts or portions you would like for each type of meat.
7. Estimate the total quantity needed.
8. If applicable, indicate any special requests or preparation preferences.
9. Select your preferred packaging preference.
10. Mention any dietary or allergen concerns.
11. Choose your preferred payment method.
12. Add any additional notes or special instructions.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (Required)
    * Enter your full name for contact purposes.
* **Email Address** (Required)
    * Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (Required)
    * Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Delivery Address** (Required)
    * Enter your street address where you would like the delivery to be made.
* **City** (Required)
    * Enter the city of your delivery address.
* **ZIP Code** (Required)
    * Enter your ZIP code for delivery purposes.
* **Preferred Delivery Date** (Required)
    * Choose a date when you would like to receive your delivery.
* **Preferred Delivery Time** (Required)
    * Select a time window for delivery (e.g., 8:00 AM - 10:00 AM).
* **Order Type** (Required)
    * Choose the type of order you would like to place (one-time order, regular subscription, or wholesale order).
* **Types of Meat Desired** (Required)
    * Select all the types of meat you would like to order.
* **Beef Cuts or Portions** (Optional)
    * Select your preferred cuts or portions for beef.
* **Pork Cuts or Portions** (Optional)
    * Select your preferred cuts or portions for pork.
* **Chicken Specifications** (Optional)
    * Select your preferred specifications for chicken.
* **Estimated Total Quantity Needed** (Required)
    * Enter the estimated total quantity of meat needed in pounds or number of portions.
* **Quality or Grade Preference** (Optional)
    * Choose the quality or grade preference for your meat.
* **Special Requests or Preparation** (Optional)
    * Enter any special requests or preparation instructions for your order.
* **Packaging Preference** (Optional)
    * Select your preferred packaging preference.
* **Dietary or Allergen Concerns** (Optional)
    * Enter any special dietary or allergen concerns.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (Required)
    * Choose your preferred payment method (credit card, check, bank transfer, cash on delivery, or account billing).
* **Additional Notes or Special Instructions** (Optional)
    * Enter any additional notes or special instructions for your order.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
