# Supplier Order Amendment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Supplier Order Amendment Form is used to update or modify existing orders that have been placed with suppliers. This form helps to ensure accuracy and clarity in the ordering process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Order Number associated with the order you'd like to update.
2. Provide the name of the supplier for the order.
3. Briefly describe the product for which you're amending the order.
4. Specify the reason for the change in the order.
5. Click 'Submit' to submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Number** (`order_number`, number, Optional): Enter the Order Number of the order you'd like to amend.
* **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, text, Optional): Enter the name of the supplier associated with the order.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, Optional): Briefly describe the product for which you're amending the order.
* **Reason for Change** (`reason_for_change`, text, Optional): Specify the reason for the change in the order.

## Tips

* Please make sure to only fill out the necessary fields for the amendment.
* Review your entry carefully before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any part of the process, don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or the relevant team for guidance.
