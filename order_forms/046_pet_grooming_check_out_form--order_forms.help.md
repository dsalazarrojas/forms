# Pet Grooming Check Out Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pet Grooming Check Out Form is used to collect information and make payments for pet grooming services provided to pet owners. This form is essential for accurate record keeping and ensuring that all relevant details are captured.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select the Pet Owner Info tab** to provide your name, address, and other relevant details about yourself as the pet owner.
2. **Select the Payment Info tab** to enter your payment method and any additional payment details.
3. **Select the Services Rendered tab** to indicate which services were performed on your pet.
4. **Select the Payment Method tab** to choose how you would like to pay for the services rendered.
5. **Select the Service Cost tab** to enter the cost of the services performed.
6. **Select the Tax Rate tab** to enter the tax rate applied to the service cost (if applicable).
7. **Select the Service Tips tab** to leave a tip for the groomer (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Owner Info (pet_owner_info)** (`Text`, Optional): This field is where you will provide your name and address as the pet owner.
* **Payment Info (payment_info)** (`Text`, Optional): This field is where you will enter your payment details, such as your name, address, and any additional payment information.
* **Services Rendered (services_rendered)** (`Select Multiple`, Optional): This field is where you will select the services that were performed on your pet, such as nail trimming, bathing, or nail polishing.
* **Payment Method (payment_method)** (`Select One`, Optional): This field is where you will choose how you would like to pay for the services rendered, such as Visa, Mastercard, or Apple Pay.
* **Service Cost (service_cost)** (`Number`, Optional): This field is where you will enter the total cost of the services performed.
* **Tax Rate (tax_rate)** (`Number`, Optional): This field is where you will enter the tax rate applied to the service cost (if applicable).
* **Service Tips (service_tips)** (`Number`, Optional): This field is where you will leave a tip for the groomer (optional).

## Tips

* Make sure to complete all fields accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask a staff member for assistance.
* This form is for your convenience and to ensure accurate record keeping.
