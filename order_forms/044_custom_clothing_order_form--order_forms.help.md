# Custom Clothing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Custom Clothing Order Form is used to create a personalized clothing item for a customer by collecting their order details such as custom clothing type, size, color, fabric, price, and delivery information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the custom clothing order details such as custom_clothing_order, size, color, and fabric in the respective fields.
2. Select the customer information, including customer_name, customer_email, customer_phone, and shipping_address, shipping_city, shipping_state, and shipping_postal_code.
3. Enter the order details such as order_details, date_of_birth, time_of_order, and order_status.
4. Enter the payment information such as price.
5. Add any additional customer notes or vendor notes as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Custom Clothing Order**: Select the type of custom clothing order you would like to make.
* **Size**: Choose the size of the custom clothing item you would like to order.
* **Color**: Select the color of the custom clothing item you would like to order.
* **Fabric**: Choose the fabric type for the custom clothing item, including Cotton, Silk, and Wool.
* **Price**: Enter the price of the custom clothing item.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the customer's date of birth for age verification purposes.
* **Time of Order**: Enter the time of the order for delivery purposes.
* **Order Status**: Select the status of the order, including Pending, Processing, and Delivered.
* **Customer Information**: Enter the customer's name, email, phone number, and shipping address, city, state, and post office code.
* **Payment Information**: Enter the payment details for the custom clothing item.
* **Customer Note**: Add any additional customer notes or requests for the order.
* **Vendor Note**: Add any additional notes or requests for the vendor.
* **Order Note**: Add any additional notes or requests for the order.
* **Order Confirmation Note**: Add any additional notes or requests for the order confirmation.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the shipping address for the custom clothing item.
* **Shipping City**: Enter the shipping city for the custom clothing item.
* **Shipping State**: Enter the shipping state for the custom clothing item.
* **Shipping Post Office Code**: Enter the post office code for the shipping address.
