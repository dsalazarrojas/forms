# Floral Arrangement Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect billing information for floral arrangements ordered by customers. It will help you accurately process and manage orders, ensuring all necessary details are included.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Customer Info (Page 1)**: Enter the customer's name, contact details, and any relevant comments or instructions.
2. **Order Details (Page 2)**: Provide information about the order, including the type of arrangement, quantity, and any special requests.
3. **Billing Info (Page 3)**: Enter the billing address and other contact details for the customer's payment method.
4. **Payment Terms (Page 4)**: Select the payment method from the options provided (Credit Card, Cash, or Bank Transfer).
5. **Date and Time (Page 5)**: Enter the date and time the service will be provided.
6. **Service Charge and Subtotal (Page 6)**: Enter the service charge and subtotal for the order.
7. **Tax and Grand Total (Page 7)**: Enter the tax and grand total for the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info (Customer Info, text, required: false)**: Enter the customer's name, address, and contact details.
* **Order Details (Order Details, text, required: false)**: Enter information about the order, including the type and quantity of arrangement.
* **Billing Info (Billing Info, text, required: false)**: Enter the billing address and other contact details for the payment method.
* **Payment Terms (Payment Terms, select_multiple, required: false)**: Select the payment method from the options provided (Credit Card, Cash, or Bank Transfer).
* **Date (Date, date, required: false)**: Enter the date the service will be provided.
* **Time (Time, time, required: false)**: Enter the time the service will be provided.
* **Service Charge (Service Charge, number, required: false)**: Enter the service charge for the order.
* **Subtotal (Subtotal, number, required: false)**: Enter the subtotal for the order.
* **Tax (Tax, number, required: false)**: Enter the tax for the order.
* **Grand Total (Grand Total, number, required: false)**: Enter the grand total for the order.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* Ensure you fill in all necessary fields to avoid missing any important details.
* Use the correct payment terms and service charge to ensure accurate billing.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all information is correct.
