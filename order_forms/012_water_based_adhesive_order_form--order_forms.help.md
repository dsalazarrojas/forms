# Water-Based-Adhesive-Order-Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for ordering water-based adhesive products. It captures customer information, product details, and order specifics to facilitate the sales or inventory management process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter a title for the order.
2. Provide your name and contact information.
3. Enter the product name and code.
4. Enter the quantity of the product to be ordered.
5. Select a delivery date and time.
6. Add any additional notes or comments about the order.
7. Enter the tool (if any) assigned to this order.
8. Optionally, you can enter a form ID for reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Water-Based-Adhesive-Order-Form (1)**: Enter a brief title for this order, e.g., "Order for Project X".
* **customer-name (2)**: Enter your name or the name of the person ordering the product.
* **email (3)**: Enter your or the customer's email address for communication.
* **customer-phone (4)**: Enter your or the customer's phone number for contact purposes.
* **product-name (5)**: Enter the name of the product being ordered.
* **product-code (6)**: Enter the product code or identifier for tracking purposes.
* **product-quantity (7)**: Enter the quantity of the product to be ordered.
* **customer-quantity (8)**: This field seems redundant with "product-quantity". Please confirm if it should be removed or used for another purpose.
* **delivery-date (9)**: Select a date for delivery of the product.
* **delivery-time (10)**: Select a time for delivery of the product.
* **note (11)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the order.
* **assigned-tool (12)**: Enter the tool (if any) assigned to this order for tracking purposes.
* **form-id (13)**: Enter a unique identifier for the form, which may be used for reference purposes.
 
## Tips
* Ensure to fill in all required fields for accurate processing.
* Double-check the product code for correct product identification.
* Enter the delivery date and time carefully to avoid errors. 
* Use the "note" field to add any additional information that may be relevant to the order.
