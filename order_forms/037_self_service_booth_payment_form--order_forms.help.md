The purpose of this form is to collect self-service booth payment information for orders. It is meant to be filled out by the customer to provide their payment details and order status after completing a self-service payment at one of our booths. This form helps us to efficiently record the customer's order details and payment method, reducing human errors and ensuring accurate records.

## Self Service Booth Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Self Service Booth Payment Form is designed to collect self-service booth payment information for orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Double-check your order details, including the order status, to ensure accuracy.
3. Choose your preferred payment method from the options provided.
4. If you have any customer notes or comments, please include them in the customer notes field.
5. Review and submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
- **Customer Telephone** (`customer_telephone`, `text`, optional): Enter the customer's telephone number.
- **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the customer's address for order delivery or other purposes.
- **Customer City** (`customer_city`, `text`, optional): Enter the customer's city for order delivery or other purposes.
- **Customer State** (`customer_state`, `text`, optional): Enter the customer's state or province for order delivery or other purposes.
- **Customer Postal Code** (`customer_postal_code`, `text`, optional): Enter the customer's postal code or zip code for order delivery or other purposes.
- **Order Description** (`order_description`, `text`, optional): Describe the order being processed.
- **Order Total** (`order_total`, `number`, optional): Enter the total value of the order.
- **Order Status** (`order_status`, `text`, required): Select the current status of the order (e.g., "Paid", "Pending", "Delivered").
- **Payment Type** (`payment_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the payment method used for this order (e.g., "Credit Card", "PayPal", "Bank Transfer").
- **Order Total Currency** (`order_total_currency`, `select_one`, optional): Select the currency of the order total (e.g., "USD", "CAD", "EUR").
- **Date of Purchase** (`date_of_purchase`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the purchase.
- **Time of Purchase** (`time_of_purchase`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the purchase.
- **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, `text`, optional): Capture the customer's signature for payment verification.
- **Customer Note** (`customer_note`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments from the customer.
- **Form Note** (`form_note`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments for our team.
- **Submit Notice** (`submit_notice`, `note`, optional): This field is not meant to be filled out by customers.

Please note that the payment terms field (`payment_terms`, `note`, optional) is also not meant to be filled out by customers and is intended for our internal use only.

By filling out this form accurately and completely, you will help us to efficiently process your order and provide the best possible service. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
