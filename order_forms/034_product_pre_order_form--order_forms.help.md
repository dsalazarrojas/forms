# Product Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Pre Order Form is used to collect information from customers who want to pre-order products before they are available for immediate purchase. This information includes their contact details, shipping and payment preferences, and product-specific information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer email address in the "Customer Email" field. This will be used as your contact information.
2. Select your preferred shipping method from the "Shipping Method" dropdown list.
3. Choose your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown list.
4. Enter the desired date for your order in the "Desired Date" field.
5. If you have any notes or special requests, you can enter them in the "Customer note" field.
6. Enter your customer name in the "Customer Name" field.
7. Enter your customer phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
8. Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field.
9. Describe the product you are ordering in the "Product Details" field.
10. Enter the product price in the "Product Price" field.
11. Enter the shipping cost in the "Shipping Cost" field.
12. Enter your tax ID in the "Tax ID" field (if applicable).
13. Enter your customer IP address in the "Customer IP" field (if applicable).
14. Enter a description of the product in the "Product Description" field.
15. Select your shipping method for the second item in the "Shipping Method 2" field.
16. Choose your payment method for the second item in the "Payment Method 2" field.
17. Enter your customer address in the "Customer Address" field.
18. Enter your customer ID in the "Product ID" field (if applicable).
19. Select the shipping status of the order in the "Shipping Status" field.
20. Enter your customer country in the "Customer Country" field.
21. Enter your customer region in the "Customer Region" field.
22. Enter your customer city in the "Customer City" field.
23. Enter your customer zip code in the "Customer Zip" field.
24. Enter your customer state in the "Customer State" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for order updates.
* **Shipping Method**: Select how you would like to receive your order. Choose from Shipping via Post, Shipping via Courier, Shipping via Express, or Shipping via Local pickup.
* **Payment Method**: Select how you would like to pay for your order. Choose from Credit Card, PayPal, Check, or Bank Transfer.
* **Desired Date**: Enter the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Customer note**: Enter any notes or special requests you have for your order.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your customer name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your customer phone number.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your shipping address where you want to receive your order.
* **Product Details**: Describe the product you are ordering.
* **Product Price**: Enter the price of the product you are ordering.
* **Shipping Cost**: Enter the shipping cost for your order.
* **Tax ID**: Enter your tax identification number (if applicable).
* **Customer IP**: Enter your customer IP address (if applicable).
* **Product Description**: Enter a description of the product you are ordering.
* **Shipping Method 2**: Select how you would like to receive your second item (if applicable).
* **Payment Method 2**: Select how you would like to pay for your second item (if applicable).
* **Customer Address**: Enter your customer address.
* **Product ID**: Enter your product ID (if applicable).
* **Shipping Status**: Select the shipping status of your order.
* **Customer Country**: Enter your customer country.
* **Customer Region**: Enter your customer region.
* **Customer City**: Enter your customer city.
* **Customer Zip**: Enter your customer zip code.
* **Customer State**: Enter your customer state.
