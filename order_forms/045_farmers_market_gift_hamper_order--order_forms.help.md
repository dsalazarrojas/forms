# Farmers Market Gift Hampers Order - Help Guide
## Purpose
To place an order for a gift hamper, you need to fill out this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To place an order, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the recipient's name in the "recipient name" field. This is a mandatory field.
2. Enter the recipient's email address in the "recipient email" field. This is also a mandatory field.
3. If you want to provide the recipient's phone number, enter it in the "recipient phone" field.
4. Select the preferred hamper type by choosing one or more from the "Select Hamper" field.
5. Enter the desired delivery date in the "delivery date" field.
6. Choose the delivery time from the list of available times in the "delivery time" field.
7. If you want to provide a note or message with the order, type it in the "Note" field.
8. Review your order details before pressing the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`Form Title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief title or description of your order.
* **Recipient's Name** (`recipient name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person or organization you are ordering the gift hamper for.
* **Recipient's Email** (`recipient email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the recipient.
* **Recipient's Phone** (`recipient phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the recipient's phone number if you want to provide it.
* **Select Hamper** (`Select Hamper`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose one or more of the available hampers:
	+ Hamper1
	+ Hamper2
	+ Hamper3
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you want the hamper to be delivered.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery time`, `time`, required): Choose the time of delivery from the available options.
* **Note** (`Note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional note or message with the order.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, `text`, required/optional): Press this button to confirm your order details.
