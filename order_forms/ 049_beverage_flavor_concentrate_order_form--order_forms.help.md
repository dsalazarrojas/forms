# Beverage Flavor Concentrate Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for ordering beverage flavor concentrates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your legal full name in the **Full name** field.
2. Enter a valid email address for contact in the **Email address** field.
3. Select the relevant date in the **Date** field.
4. Choose the primary selection that best applies to your order in the **Primary selection** field.
5. If required, enter a whole number for quantity or rating in the **Quantity or rating** field.
6. If necessary, provide any additional details in the **Additional details** field.
7. Select any applicable options in the **Please confirm agreement** field.
8. Enter a decimal value if applicable in the **Estimated value** field.
9. If scheduling is required, enter a time in the **Preferred time** field.
10. Optionally, enter any final comments or notes in the **Final comments or notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: Enter your legal full name to identify yourself as the order initiator.
* **Email address**: Enter a valid email address for contact, which will be used for communication and updates.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number, including the country code, to facilitate communication.
* **Date**: Select the date relevant to your order, such as the order date or delivery date.
* **Primary selection**: Choose the primary selection that best applies to your order. This field is typically used to select a specific product or flavor.
* **Quantity or rating**: If applicable, enter a whole number for quantity or rating, such as a rating out of 5.
* **Additional details**: Provide any additional relevant information about your order, such as special requests or notes.
* **Please confirm agreement**: Select any applicable options to confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Estimated value**: Enter an estimated value for your order, using decimal format if applicable.
* **Preferred time**: If scheduling is required, enter a time in 24-hour format.
* **Final comments or notes**: Optionally, enter any final comments or notes about your order.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number, including the country code, to facilitate communication.
* **Agreement**: Select any applicable options to confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions.
