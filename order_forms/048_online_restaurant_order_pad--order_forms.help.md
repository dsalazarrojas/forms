# online-restaurant-order-pad - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for taking orders from customers in a restaurant setting. It helps the staff to collect necessary information for processing and fulfilling customer orders efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the customer's name in the customer name field.
2. Select the correct date and time for the order.
3. Enter the order number, which is a unique identifier for the order.
4. Enter the customer's quantity, if applicable.
5. Enter the customer's phone number for contact purposes.
6. Select the specific items to be ordered.
7. If required, enter any additional comments or instructions for the order.
8. Select who the order is assigned to, which could be a staff member or a team.
9. Enter any additional comments for the staff member handling the order, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer name** (`customer_name`, required/optional): Enter the customer's full name.
* **Date and time** (`date_and_time`, required): Select the date and time for the order.
* **Order number** (`order_number`, required): Enter a unique identifier for the order.
* **Customer quantity** (`customer_quantity`, required): Enter the number of customers placing the order.
* **Customer phone** (`customer_phone`, required): Enter the customer's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Order items** (`order_items`, required): Select the specific items to be ordered from the available options.
* **Order comment** (`order_comment`, optional): Enter any additional comments or instructions for the order, but it's recommended to use the "order items" field for specific items instead.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, required): Select who the order is assigned to, which could be a staff member or a team.
* **Waiter comment** (`waiter_comment`, optional): Enter any additional comments for the staff member handling the order, if necessary.
