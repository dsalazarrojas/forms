# Caramel Chicken Meal Kit - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Caramel Chicken Meal Kit order form is used to place an order for a meal kit delivery service. Fill out this form to provide all necessary information for a successful delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select your preferred options for meal kit quantity, spice level, dietary restrictions, and cooking method.
3. Provide any special instructions for delivery, if necessary.
4. Choose a payment method and confirm the total amount due.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your name** (id: 2, *): Enter your name to be associated with the order.
* **Email address** (id: 3, *): Enter your email address to be used for communication and delivery updates.
* **Phone number** (id: 4, *): Enter your phone number for delivery coordination and updates.
* **Delivery address** (id: 5, *): Enter the address where you would like to receive the meal kit.
* **City** (id: 6, *): Enter the city for the delivery address.
* **State and zip code** (id: 7, *): Enter the state and zip code for the delivery address.
* **Number of servings** (id: 9, *): Select the number of servings you would like to order (2, 4, 6, 8, 10+).
* **How many meal kits do you want?** (id: 10, *): Enter the number of meal kits you would like to order.
* **Dietary restrictions or preferences** (id: 11, ): Select all that apply (Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Vegan, Vegetarian, Nut-Free, Low-Sodium, Low-Carb, Keto).
* **Preferred spice level** (id: 12, ): Select your preferred spice level (Mild, Medium, Spicy, Extra Spicy, No Preference).
* **Any ingredient substitutions or changes?** (id: 13, ): If you have any dietary restrictions or preferences, provide details on ingredient substitutions or changes.
* **Food allergies** (id: 14, ): If you have any food allergies, list them here.
* **Preferred cooking method** (id: 15, ): Select your preferred cooking method (Stovetop, Oven, Slow Cooker, Instant Pot, Grilling, No Preference).
* **Your cooking skill level** (id: 16, ): Select your cooking skill level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Professional).
* **Preferred delivery date** (id: 18, *): Enter the date you would like to receive the meal kit.
* **Preferred delivery time** (id: 19, *): Select the time of day you would like to receive the meal kit (Morning 8am-12pm, Afternoon 12pm-5pm, Evening 5pm-9pm, Anytime).
* **Special delivery instructions** (id: 20, ): Provide any special delivery instructions (e.g., gate code, safe spot to leave).
* **Subtotal amount** (id: 22, *): Confirm the subtotal amount due for the order.
* **Delivery fee** (id: 23, ): Enter the delivery fee for the order.
* **Total amount due** (id: 24, *): Confirm the total amount due for the order.
* **Payment method** (id: 25, *): Select the payment method to use for the order (Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Cash on Delivery).
