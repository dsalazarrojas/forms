# Training Material Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Training Material Refund Form" is used to request a refund for training materials that have been received but not used or found to be unsuitable. If you have a valid reason for requesting a refund, please fill out this form accurately to initiate the refund process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your details: first name, last name, and email.
2. Provide the order ID number associated with the training material.
3. Enter the reason for your refund request.
4. Select whether the material is of type "Yes" or "No".
5. Select the date you received the material.
6. (Optional) Enter the time you received the material.
7. Enter your phone number (this will help us contact you for further clarification).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (`first name`, text, required: false)
* First name is the first name you were given at birth or the name you prefer to be known by.

### Last Name (`last name`, text, required: false)
* Last name is the surname or last name you were given at birth or the name you prefer to be known by.

### Order ID (`order id`, number, required: false)
* Please enter the number of the order associated with the training material you are requesting a refund for.

### Reason for Refund (`reason`, text, required: false)
* Please provide a brief explanation of the reason you are requesting a refund. This could be due to the material not meeting your expectations, being unsuitable for your needs, or any other valid reason.

### Material Type (`material type`, select_multiple, required: false)
* Please select "Yes" if the material type is relevant to your refund request, "No" otherwise.

### Date Received (`date`, date, required: false)
* Enter the date you received the training material.

### Time Received (`time`, time, required: false)
* Enter the time you received the training material. (Optional, but recommended for clarity.)

### Email (`email`, email, required: false)
* Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your refund request.

### Phone (`phone`, text, required: false)
* Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further clarification or to provide updates on your refund status.
