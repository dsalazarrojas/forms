# Holiday Gift Certificate Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect details for a holiday gift certificate. It is intended for customers or employees to request or provide information on gifts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant details.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Double-check your information for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details** (`order_details`): This field is for entering order details. Enter relevant details about the order you are requesting or providing.
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`): Enter details about the delivery of the gift, such as date and time.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`): Enter payment details for the gift, if applicable.
* **Note** (`note`): Enter any additional notes or comments about the gift.
* **Gift Details** (`gift_details`): Enter details about the gift, such as the type or description.
* **Fulfillment Details** (`fulfillment_details`): Enter details about the fulfillment of the gift, such as the method of delivery.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the email address for the gift recipient.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter the phone number for the gift recipient.
* **Order Details 2** (`order_details_2`): This field is for entering order details for a second part of the order. This might be for a gift recipient with multiple orders.
* **Delivery Details 2** (`delivery_details_2`): Enter details about the delivery of the second part of the order.
* **Payment Details 2** (`payment_details_2`): Enter payment details for the second part of the order, if applicable.
* **Note 2** (`note_2`): Enter any additional notes or comments about the second part of the order.
* **Gift Details 2** (`gift_details_2`): Enter details about the second part of the gift, such as the type or description.
* **Fulfillment Details 2** (`fulfillment_details_2`): Enter details about the fulfillment of the second part of the order.
* **Email 2** (`email_2`): Enter the email address for the second gift recipient.
* **Phone 2** (`phone_2`): Enter the phone number for the second gift recipient.
* **Order Details 3** (`order_details_3`): This field is for entering order details for a third part of the order. This might be for a gift recipient with multiple orders.
* **Delivery Details 3** (`delivery_details_3`): Enter details about the delivery of the third part of the order.
* **Payment Details 3** (`payment_details_3`): Enter payment details for the third part of the order, if applicable.

* **Fulfillment Details 3** (`fulfillment_details_3`): Enter details about the fulfillment of the third part of the order.
* **Email 3** (`email_3`): Enter the email address for the third gift recipient.
* **Phone 3** (`phone_3`): Enter the phone number for the third gift recipient.
* **Submit**: Once you have filled in all the required details, click the **Submit** button to send in your request.
