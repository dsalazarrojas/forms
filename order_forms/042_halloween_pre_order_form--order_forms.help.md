# halloween_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Halloween Pre-Order Form is used to collect pre-order information from customers for Halloween-themed orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the product selection by choosing from the available options.
2. Enter your email address in the email field.
3. Enter the quantity you want to order.
4. Enter the shipping cost.
5. Enter your name.
6. Add any additional order notes.
7. Enter your phone number.
8. Choose your preferred product.
9. Select the nail art design you want.
10. Enter the date and time for the pre-order.
11. Choose the product ID.
12. Select any custom options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **product** (text, required): Enter the product you want to pre-order.
* **email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **quantity** (number, required): Enter the quantity you want to order.
* **shipping** (number, required): Enter the shipping cost.
* **customer** (text, optional): Enter your name.
* **order** (text, optional): Add any additional order notes.
* **phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **product** (select_one, required): Choose your preferred product.
* **nail** (select_multiple, optional): Select the nail art design you want.
* **date** (date, required): Enter the date for the pre-order.
* **time** (time, required): Enter the time for the pre-order.
* **product** (select_one, required): Choose the product ID.
* **custom** (select_one, required): Select any custom options.
