# Costume Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Costume Order Form Template is a form designed for customers to place and manage orders for custom-made costumes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your costume type (e.g., medieval costume, superhero costume, etc.).
2. Enter your customer name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Describe the costume details (e.g., description, color scheme, size, etc.).
6. Specify the quantity of the costume(s) you need.
7. Enter the price of the costume(s).
8. Choose the shipping method: Ground, Express, or None.
9. Choose the payment method: Cash, Credit, or PayPal.
10. Enter the date and time you would like the costume to be ready by.
11. Add any additional notes or comments about your order.
12. Assign the order to either Jimmy or Jane.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Costume Type
* **Costume Type** (`costume_type`, text, required): Please enter the type of costume you are interested in (e.g., medieval costume, superhero costume, etc.).

### Customer Name
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.

### Costume Details
* **Costume Details** (`costume_details`, note, required): Describe the costume details (e.g., description, color scheme, size, etc.).

### Quantity
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the quantity of the costume(s) you need.

### Price
* **Price** (`price`, number, required): Enter the price of the costume(s) you need.

### Shipping
* **Shipping** (`shipping`, select_one, required): Choose the shipping method: Ground, Express, or None.

### Payment
* **Payment** (`payment`, select_multiple, required): Choose the payment method: Cash, Credit, or PayPal.

### Date
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date you would like the costume to be ready by.

### Time
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time you would like the costume to be ready by.

### Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Add any additional notes or comments about your order.

### Assigned To
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required): Assign the order to either Jimmy or Jane.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Add any additional comments about your order.

## Tips
* Please ensure you fill out all required fields.
* Use the correct format for date and time entries (mm/dd/mm hh:mm).
* Double-check your entries for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.
