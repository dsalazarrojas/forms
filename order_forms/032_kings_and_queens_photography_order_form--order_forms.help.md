# Kings and Queens Photography Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The kings-and-queens-photography-order-form is a simple order form designed for customers to provide their details and package choices for photography services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as prompted with your accurate and complete information. Make sure to select the correct package options that suit your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Info** (Customer Info, text, required/optional): This field is for you to provide your name and any other relevant contact information.
- **Package Choice** (Package Choice, select_one, required/optional): Please select one of the package options provided. This will help the photographer understand your preferences.
- **Date** (Date, date, required/optional): Enter the date for your photography session.
- **Time** (Time, time, required/optional): Enter the time for your photography session.
- **Email** (Email, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so the photographer can contact you.
- **Phone** (Phone, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number so the photographer can contact you.
- **Comments** (Comments, note, required/optional): Leave any additional comments or special requests you may have.
- **Package 1** (Package 1, select_one, required/optional): Select this package option for your photography session.
- **Package 2** (Package 2, select_multiple, required/optional): Select multiple package options for your photography session.
- **Total Packages** (Total Packages, number, required/optional): Enter the total number of packages you have selected.

## Tips
- Please make sure to double check your information before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or special requests, please include them in the Comments field.
- Select the correct package options that suit your needs.
