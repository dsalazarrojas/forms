## PPE Safety Kit Drive Thru - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PPE Safety Kit Drive Thru form is designed to collect information about PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) safety kits for a drive-thru setup. This form helps track and manage the kits, including the category, description, assigned personnel, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the category of the PPE safety kit from the provided options.
2. Provide a brief description of the PPE safety kit.
3. Select the person assigned to handle the PPE safety kit from the provided options.
4. Enter the date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
5. Specify the number of PPE kits ordered.
6. Specify the number of PPE kits assigned.
7. Enter the email address for communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`Category`, select_one, required/optional): Select the category of the PPE safety kit from the available options (Order Forms or Other).
* **Description** (`Description`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the PPE safety kit, explaining its purpose and contents.
* **Assigned To** (`Assigned To`, select_one, required/optional): Select the person responsible for handling the PPE safety kit from the available options (chatjimmy or chatjim).
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **PPE Kits** (`PPE Kits`, number, required/optional): Specify the number of PPE kits ordered for the selected category.
* **PPE Kits Ordered** (`PPE Kits Ordered`, number, required/optional): Specify the number of PPE kits assigned to the selected category.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address for communication purposes.

## Tips
* Ensure that the category and description are accurate and complete to ensure proper tracking and management of the PPE safety kits.
* Select the correct person assigned to handle the PPE safety kit to ensure timely and efficient handling.
* Use the correct format for the date of birth to avoid errors.
* Specify the correct number of PPE kits ordered and assigned to maintain accurate inventory.
* Enter the correct email address for communication to ensure prompt responses.
