# Luxury Service Refund Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is created to help you request a refund for a luxury service you have purchased.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, indicated by an *asterisk* (e.g., `*Title*`).
2. Provide accurate information about the luxury service you are requesting a refund for.
3. Submit the form once you have filled in all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details* (`order_details`, `text`, required): *Please enter a brief description of the luxury service you are requesting a refund for, including the order number, date, and any other relevant details.*  
* **Order Type* (`service_type`, `select_one`, required): *Select the type of luxury service you have purchased from the dropdown options.* 
* **Reason for Refund* (`reason_for_refund`, `text`, required): *Explain the reason you are requesting a refund. Be as detailed as possible to help the customer service team process your request efficiently.* 
* **Refund Amount* (`refund_amount`, `number`, optional): *If you are requesting a refund, please enter the amount you are requesting. This field is optional and will not affect the processing of your refund request.* 
* **Order ID* (`order_id`, `number`, optional): *Enter the order ID associated with your luxury service purchase. This is not required but will help the customer service team process your request more efficiently.* 

## Tips
- Make sure to check with the customer service team before filling this form to confirm the luxury service order exists and you are not duplicating a refund request. 
- If you are unsure about any information requested in this form, contact the customer service team before proceeding.
