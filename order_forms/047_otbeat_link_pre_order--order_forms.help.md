# OTBeat Link Pre Order - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for "OTBeat Link Pre Order" from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the relevant information:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select a date in the "Date" field.
5. Choose a time in the "Time" field.
6. Add any additional order notes in the "Order Notes" field.
7. Enter your link information in the "Link" field.
8. Confirm your link information in the "Confirm Link" field by selecting "Yes" or "No".
9. Finally, enter your confirmation for "Confirm Link Confirmation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: A text field for the customer's first name.
* **Email**: A field for the customer's email address.
* **Phone**: A text field for the customer's phone number.
* **Date**: A date field for the customer's preferred date.
* **Time**: A time field for the customer's preferred time.
* **Order Notes**: A note field for the customer to add any additional order notes.
* **Link**: A text field for the customer's link information.
* **Confirm Link**: A selection field to confirm the customer's link information by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Confirm Link Confirmation**: A field for the customer to enter a confirmation message for "Confirm Link".
* **Confirm Link (2)**: Another selection field to confirm the customer's link information by selecting "Yes" or "No" (this field seems to be a redundant field that duplicates field 8 "Confirm Link", and we recommend ignoring or removing it).
* **Order Notes (2)**: A redundant field that seems to be a duplicate of "Order Notes", and we recommend ignoring or removing it.
