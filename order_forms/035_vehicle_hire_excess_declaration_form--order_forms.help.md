# Vehicle Hire Excess Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about vehicle hire excess declarations. It's a critical document for customers or vehicle hirers to provide details about the vehicle being hired and their declaration. Please ensure all required fields are completed accurately to avoid any issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in all the relevant fields with accurate information.
2. Ensure you have all the required details about the vehicle being hired.
3. Double-check your responses for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Vehicle Hire Excess Declaration Form
* **vehicle_hire_excess_declaration_form**: This is the main form section. Please provide a brief description of the vehicle being hired and the purpose of the declaration.
* **customer_declaration**: A statement from the customer declaring the vehicle details and the hiring period.
* **vehicle_details**: A short description of the vehicle being hired, including its characteristics.
* **vehicle_registration_number**: The vehicle registration number.
* **excess_insurance_coverage**: If applicable, please describe the excess insurance coverage.
* **excess_insurance_value**: The value of the excess insurance coverage.
* **customer_signature**: A signature from the customer acknowledging the declaration.
* **date**: The date the vehicle was hired or the hiring period began.
* **time**: The time the vehicle was hired or the hiring period began.
* **note**: Any additional comments or notes about the vehicle hire.

## Tips
* Double-check the accuracy of all provided information before submitting the form.
* Ensure all required fields are completed.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask for assistance.
