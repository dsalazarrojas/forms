# Supplier Delivery Schedule Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Delivery Schedule Request Form is used to request updates to a supplier's delivery schedule. This form helps to communicate with suppliers and ensure that delivery schedules are up-to-date and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant supplier from the supplier list.
2. Choose a new delivery date and frequency for the supplier.
3. Enter any relevant contact information (name, number, address, email).
4. Provide a brief description of the change in the delivery note field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **supplier** (`Supplier A`, `Supplier B`, Select one of the listed suppliers): Choose the supplier whose delivery schedule you wish to update.
* **requested_delivery_date** (`YYYY-MM-DD`): Enter the new delivery date you are requesting.
* **required_time** (integer): Enter the new delivery time (in 24-hour format, e.g. 08:00).
* **delivery_frequency** (Daily, Weekly, Monthly): Choose the new delivery frequency you are requesting.
* **delivery_status** (Pending, Approved, Rejected): Choose the new delivery status you are requesting (note: this field is not used for new delivery requests, but rather for updating existing deliveries).
* **contact_name** (text): Enter the contact name of the person making the request.
* **contact_number** (text): Enter the contact number of the person making the request.
* **supplier_address** (text): Enter the supplier's address (note: this field is optional).
* **delivery_address** (text): Enter the delivery address (note: this field is optional).
* **contact_email** (email): Enter the contact email of the person making the request.
* **delivery_note** (text): Enter a brief description of the change in the delivery schedule.
* **supplier_note** (text): Enter any additional notes or comments for the supplier.
* **required_date_2** (`YYYY-MM-DD`): Enter the new delivery date for the second supplier (if applicable).
* **delivery_frequency_2** (Daily, Weekly, Monthly): Choose the new delivery frequency for the second supplier (if applicable).
* **delivery_status_2** (Pending, Approved, Rejected): Choose the new delivery status for the second supplier (if applicable).
