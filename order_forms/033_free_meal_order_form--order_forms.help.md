# Free Meal Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Free Meal Order Form is used to collect information about a customer's meal order, including their contact details, order details, and order status. This form is designed to be completed by customers who wish to order a free meal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the legend below.
2. Enter your order details, such as date and time of order.
3. Provide any relevant notes or comments about your order.
4. Choose whether you would like freebies (if available).
5. Select the status of your order (Active or Inactive).
6. Enter your order number and total.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meal Order Form** (1): Enter a brief description of your meal order.
* **Customer Info** (2): Enter your name, email, and phone number for communication purposes.
* **Contact Details** (3): Enter any additional contact information you would like to be reached at.
* **Order Details** (4): Enter the date and time of your order.
* **Order Date** (5): Enter the date of your order. (Format: YYYY-MM-DD)
* **Order Time** (6): Enter the time of your order. (Format: HH:MM)
* **Order Note** (7): Enter any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Customer Email** (8): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Customer Phone** (9): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Customer Name** (10): Enter your name.
* **Order Total** (11): Enter the total cost of your order.
* **Customer Notes** (12): Enter any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Order Comments** (13): Enter any additional comments or questions about your order.
* **Freebies** (14): Select "Yes" if you would like to receive freebies with your order.
* **Order Status** (15): Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the status of your order.
* **Order Number** (16): Enter your order number.
* **Order Total** (17): Enter the total cost of your order. (Note: This field is a duplicate of Field 11, but with a different name.)

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate processing of your order.
* Use the correct format for date and time fields to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us through the provided email or phone number.
