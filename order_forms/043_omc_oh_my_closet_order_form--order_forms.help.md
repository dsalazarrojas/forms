# Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Order Form is designed to collect information from customers about their shipping details and order preferences. This form is used to gather essential data for order fulfillment and communication with customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the Shipping Info section with your shipping details.
2. Select your preferred shipping method in the Shipping Method section.
3. Enter your order details in the Products section, selecting the products you'd like to order.
4. Enter your billing and order date and time in the Billing Info and Order Date & Time sections.
5. Fill in any additional comments or information in the Comments section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shipping Info** (Shipping Info, note, required: false): This section is for providing your shipping address details. Enter your shipping information in the space provided.
* **Products** (Products, select_multiple, required: false): Select the products you'd like to order from the available options.
* **Product 1** (Product 1, select_one, required: false): Select the specific product from the available options you'd like to order.
* **Shipping Method** (Shipping Method, select_one, required: false): Choose your preferred shipping method.
* **Shipping Address** (Shipping Address, text, required: false): Enter your shipping address.
* **City** (City, text, required: false): Enter the city of your shipping address.
* **State** (State, text, required: false): Enter the state of your shipping address.
* **Zip** (Zip, number, required: false): Enter your zip code.
* **Country** (Country, text, required: false): Enter the country of your shipping address.
* **Billing Info** (Billing Info, note, required: false): This section is for providing billing information. Enter any relevant details.
* **Name** (Name, text, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Email** (Email, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Order Date** (Order Date, date, required: false): Enter the date of your order.
* **Order Time** (Order Time, time, required: false): Enter the time of your order.
* **Comments** (Comments, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or information about your order in this section.
