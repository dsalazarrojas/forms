# Specialty Oil Procurement Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Specialty Oil Procurement Order Form is a form designed to help customers place orders for specialty oils. It captures essential details such as oil type, order date, delivery address, and customer contact information to facilitate the ordering process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name in the "customer name" field.
2. Select the type of oil required in the "oil type" field.
3. Enter the order date in the "order date" field.
4. Provide the delivery address in the "delivery address" field.
5. Enter the customer's contact information in the "customer contact" field.
6. Enter the customer's email address in the "customer email" field (if applicable).
7. Enter any additional order details in the "order details" field.
8. Specify the total amount of oil ordered in the "total oil ordered" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer name** (optional): Enter the name of the customer who is placing the order.
* **oil type** (required): Select the type of specialty oil required for the order.
	+ Select one of the available options: "oil type 1", "oil type 2", or "oil type 3".
* **order date** (optional): Enter the date the order is requested to be placed.
* **delivery address** (optional): Provide the address where the oil will be delivered.
* **customer contact** (optional): Enter any contact information for the customer, such as a phone number or additional notes.
* **customer email** (optional): Enter the customer's email address for any additional communication.
* **order details** (optional): Enter any additional details about the order, such as specific requirements or special instructions.
* **total oil ordered** (optional): Enter the total quantity of oil ordered (currently not used for business logic, but may be used in the future).
