# raffle_ticket_generation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to create a raffle ticket for an event. It is used to collect information from users to generate a raffle ticket.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the Event Name to identify the event for which you are generating the raffle ticket.
2. Enter the Ticket Price if you wish to specify the price of the ticket.
3. Choose the Ticket Quantity if you want to buy multiple tickets.
4. Select the Ticket Type to choose which type of ticket you want to purchase.
5. Enter Order Notes if you want to include any additional information with your order.
6. Select the Payment Method to choose how you want to pay for the tickets.
7. Enter Order Email to provide a contact email address.
8. Enter Contact Number to provide a contact phone number (optional).
9. Enter Order Phone to provide a phone number for the order (optional).
10. Enter Order Comments if you want to include any additional comments with your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name (Text)** (Optional): Enter the name of the event for which you are generating the raffle ticket.
* **Ticket Price (Number)** (Optional): Enter the price of the ticket if you wish to specify it.
* **Ticket Quantity (Number)** (Optional): Choose how many tickets you want to buy.
* **Ticket Type (Select One)** (Optional): Select which type of ticket you want to purchase. Options: Ticket A, Ticket B, Ticket C.
* **Order Notes (Text)** (Optional): Enter any additional information you want to include with your order.
* **Payment Method (Select One)** (Optional): Choose how you want to pay for the tickets. Options: Online Payment, Offline Payment.
* **Order Email (Email)** (Optional): Enter a contact email address for your order.
* **Contact Number (Text)** (Optional): Enter a contact phone number for your order.
* **Order Phone (Text)** (Optional): Enter a phone number for your order.
* **Order Comments (Text)** (Optional): Enter any additional comments you want to include with your order.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your order is processed correctly.
- If you experience any issues with your order, contact us for assistance.
- The information collected from this form is for the purpose of generating a raffle ticket and will be used to fulfill your order.
