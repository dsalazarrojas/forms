# DriveThruMealOrderForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
The DriveThruMealOrderForm is used by customers to place their orders for drive-thru pickup or delivery from a restaurant. It captures essential information such as order ID, pickup type, order items, customer name, phone number, and email address.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the pickup time of your order from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter a unique order ID, if you have one.
3. Choose the type of pickup (Pickup Window, DriveThru, Delivery, or Curbside) that suits your needs.
4. Select the items you would like to order from the available options (Hamburger, Fries, Sandwich, etc.).
5. Type in your name as it appears on your identification card or driver's license.
6. Enter your phone number for easy contact.
7. Provide your email address for communication about your order.
8. Add any additional notes or comments in the customer note field.
9. Finally, review your order summary and submit the form to place your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pickup Time**: Select the time you would like to pick up your order. This will help the staff to prepare your order accordingly.
* **Order ID**: Enter a unique order ID if you have one. This is useful for your order tracking.
* **Pickup Type**: Choose the type of pickup that suits your needs. You can select from Pickup Window, DriveThru, Delivery, or Curbside.
* **Order Items**: Select the items you would like to order from the available options. You can choose one or multiple items.
* **Customer Name**: Type in your name as it appears on your identification card or driver's license.
* **Phone**: Enter a valid phone number for easy contact about your order.
* **Customer Email**: Provide your email address for communication about your order.
* **Customer Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **DriveThru Order Form**: This is a text field for any additional comments or messages about your order.
