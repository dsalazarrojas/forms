# Stationery Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stationery Delivery Form is designed to collect information from customers for delivery orders, including customer details, delivery specifics, and order preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required and optional fields as per the instructions below.
2. Review and submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details** (customer_details): Enter the customer's name and any other relevant details.
* **Delivery Details** (delivery_details): Enter the delivery address and any other relevant delivery information.
* **Shipping Details** (shipping_details): Enter any additional shipping information, such as shipping instructions or special delivery requests.
* **Stationery Items** (stationery_items): Select the stationery items you would like to have delivered.
* **Order Details** (order_details): Select the order type or package you would like to have delivered.
* **Notes** (notes): Enter any additional notes or comments about the order.
* **Customer Email** (customer_email): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (customer_phone): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Delivery Date** (delivery_date): Select the date for delivery.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
- Double-check the accuracy of all entered information to avoid errors.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
