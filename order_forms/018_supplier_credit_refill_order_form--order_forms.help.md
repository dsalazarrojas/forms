# Supplier Credit Refill Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Credit Refill Order Form is a tool to initiate a refill of a supplier's credit account with a specific amount of credit.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the supplier's credit account status to ensure it is due for refilling.
2. Fill in the supplier's name in the `supplier_name` field.
3. Enter the date of the order in the `order_date` field.
4. Enter the amount of credit to be refilled in the `credit_amount` field.
5. Select the type of order from the options provided in the `order_type` field.
6. Provide any additional details about the order in the `order_details` field (optional).
7. Enter any notes about the order in the `order_note` field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **supplier_name**: Enter the name of the supplier whose credit account is to be refilled.
* **order_date**: Enter the date of the order. This date should be the date when the supplier's credit account is due for refilling.
* **credit_amount**: Enter the amount of credit to be refilled.
* **order_type**: Select the type of order from the options provided. The options are:
	+ Supplier Credit Refill: Refill the supplier's credit account.
	+ Credit Refill: Refill the supplier's credit account for a specific reason or condition.
	+ Other: Use this option for any other type of order.
* **order_details**: Enter any additional details about the order, such as the reason for refilling or any specific instructions.
* **order_note**: Enter any notes or comments about the order. This field is optional and is for additional information about the order that may not be included in the `order_details` field.
