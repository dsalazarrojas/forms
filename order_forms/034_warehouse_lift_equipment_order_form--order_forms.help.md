# Warehouse Lift Equipment Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warehouse Lift Equipment Order Form is used to request the ordering of lift equipment for a warehouse setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the "First Name", "Last Name", "Email", and "Company" fields with your details.
2. Select the type of equipment you require from the "Equipment Type" dropdown menu.
3. Specify the quantity of equipment you need.
4. Enter the date you would like to receive the equipment.
5. Provide your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email.
6. Add any delivery instructions or notes regarding your order.
7. Optionally, add any comments or additional information about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Company**: Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Equipment ID**: Enter the ID number of the equipment you are ordering.
* **Equipment Type**: Select the type of lift equipment you require from the dropdown menu:
  + Lift Truck
  + Pallet Jack
  + Scissor Lift
  + Forklift
* **Quantity**: Specify the number of pieces of equipment you need.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date you would like to receive the equipment.
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the date you would like to receive the delivery.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person to be contacted.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter a phone number where you can be reached.
* **Contact Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Delivery Instructions**: Add any special instructions for delivery, such as a specific location.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information about your order.
* **Comments**: Add any comments or special requests regarding your order.
* **Order Comments**: Add any additional comments about your order.
* **Delivery Instructions**: Add any special instructions for delivery, such as a specific location.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information about your order.

Tips:
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your order is processed correctly.
* Use a valid email address and phone number to ensure we can contact you about your order.
* Enter accurate and detailed information to help us deliver your equipment correctly.
