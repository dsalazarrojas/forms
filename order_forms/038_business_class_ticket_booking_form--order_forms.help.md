# Business Class Ticket Booking Form - Help Guide
 
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from travelers for booking business class tickets. It helps us understand your preferences, needs, and requirements for a smooth and comfortable travel experience.
 
## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form carefully, providing accurate and up-to-date information.
- Ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
- Select options for preferences like meal, seat, and cabin class.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, use the "Special Requests" field.
 
## Field-by-Field Explanation
 
- **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your full name as it appears on your passport or ID.
- **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your email address for booking confirmation and updates.
- **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter your contact phone number.
- **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, **optional**): If you are traveling on company business, enter your company name.
- **Frequent Flyer Number** (`frequent_flyer_number`, `text`, **optional**): If you have a frequent flyer program number, enter it here.
- **Passport Number** (`passport_number`, `text`, **optional**): For international travel, enter your passport number.
- **Trip Details** (`trip_details`, `note`, **optional**): Briefly describe your trip details, such as travel dates and destinations.
- **Trip Type** (`trip_type`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the type of trip you are booking (One Way, Round Trip, Multi-City, Open Jaw).
- **Departure City** (`departure_city`, `text`, **required**): Enter the city you are departing from.
- **Arrival City** (`arrival_city`, `text`, **required**): Enter your destination city.
- **Departure Date** (`departure_date`, `date`, **required**): Select the date you want to depart.
- **Return Date** (`return_date`, `date`, **optional**): If applicable, select the date you want to return.
- **Preferred Airline** (`preferred_airline`, `text`, **optional**): If you have a preferred airline, enter it here.
- **Cabin Class** (`cabin_class`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the class of service you desire (Business Class, First Class, Premium Economy, Flexible).
- **Flight Preferences** (`flight_preferences`, `note`, **optional**): Describe any additional flight preferences or requirements.
- **Seat Preference** (`seat_preference`, `select_one`, **required**): Select where you would like to sit in the plane (Window, Aisle, Middle, No Preference).
- **Meal Preference** (`meal_preference`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your meal preference (No Preference, Vegetarian, Vegan, Kosher, Halal, Gluten-Free, Low Sodium, Diabetic, Other).
- **Special Meal Notes** (`special_meal_notes`, `text`, **optional**): If you have specific dietary needs or requirements, describe them here.
- **Baggage Requirements** (`baggage_requirements`, `select_one`, **required**): Select how many bags you will be bringing (Carry-On Only, 1 Checked Bag, 2 Checked Bags, 3+ Checked Bags).
- **Lounge Access** (`lounge_access`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose whether you want airport lounge access (True, False, Maybe).
- **Ground Transportation** (`ground_transport`, `select_one`, **required**): Select whether you need airport transfers (False, Pickup Only, Drop-off Only, Both Pickup and Drop-off).
- **Hotel Booking** (`hotel_booking`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose whether you need hotel accommodation (False, Yes - Need Booking, Yes - Have Own Booking).
- **Travel Insurance** (`travel_insurance`, `select_one`, **required**): Select whether you want travel insurance (True, False, Maybe).
- **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, **optional**): If you have any other special requests, describe them here.
- **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your expected budget range (Economy, Moderate, Flexible, Premium - Best Available).
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose your payment method (Corporate Credit Card, Personal Credit Card, Bank Transfer, Invoice, Company Account).
- **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, `text`, **optional**): If you need to provide a billing address, enter it here.
- **Additional Travelers** (`additional_travelers`, `text`, **optional**): If anyone else is traveling with you, describe their details.
 
## Tips
 
- Be accurate and detailed when filling out the form.
- Double-check your selections and information for correctness.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
