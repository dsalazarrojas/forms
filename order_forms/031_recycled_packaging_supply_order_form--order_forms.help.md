# Recycled Packaging Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recycled Packaging Supply Order Form is a tool for customers or users to place orders for recycled packaging supplies. It helps gather essential information for processing orders internally.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with your contact information and order details.
2. Select the desired order items.
3. Choose your preferred payment and shipping methods.
4. Add any additional notes or instructions.
5. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, optional):
  This field is for you to enter your name and contact details, such as email and phone number.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional):
  Enter your contact name and details, including email and phone number.
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, text, optional):
  Specify any special delivery instructions or requirements.
* **Order Items** (`order_items`, select_multiple, optional):
  Select the recycled packaging supplies you want to order. Options are:
    - Yes
    - No
* **Order Quantity** (`order_quantity`, number, optional):
  Enter the quantity of each order item you need.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional):
  Choose how you want to pay for your order. Options are:
    - Yes
    - No
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, select_one, optional):
  Select the shipping method you prefer. Options are:
    - Yes
    - No
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, text, optional):
  Provide any additional information about your order, such as special requests or instructions.
* **Total** (`total`, number, optional):
  This field will automatically calculate the total cost of your order based on your selections.
