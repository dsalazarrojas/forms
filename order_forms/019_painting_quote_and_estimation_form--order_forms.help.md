# Painting, quote and estimation form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Painting, quote and estimation form is used to gather information from customers to create a quote and estimation for painting services. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure that the internal team can create a correct quote and estimation for the customer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your first name and last name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your valid email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select the service(s) you require from the "Service" dropdown menu.
5. If you have a preferred time of service, select it from the "Time" dropdown menu. You can also select "24/7" if you are unsure of your preferred time.
6. Enter your contact time in the "Contact time" field, if applicable.
7. In the "Message" field, write a brief message to provide more information about your project.
8. If you have any additional project details, enter them in the "Project details" field.
9. If you have any comments, enter them in the "Customer comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Service** (`service`, select_one, required): Select the service you require from the dropdown menu.
* **Time** (`time`, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred time of service (AM, PM, or 24/7).
* **Contact time** (`contact_time`, time, optional): Enter your contact time.
* **Message** (`message`, text, required): Enter a brief message to provide more information about your project.
* **Project details** (`project_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional project details.
* **Customer comments** (`customer_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback you have.
