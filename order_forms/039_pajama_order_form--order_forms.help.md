# Pajama Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pajama Order Form is a crucial tool for customers to provide essential information related to their orders, including customer details, product specifics, and payment methods. It helps ensure accurate and complete data exchange between the customer and the business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your customer information, including your name and any other relevant details.
2. Provide shipping information, such as the address and any other necessary details.
3. Specify the product information, including any specific products you're ordering.
4. Enter your total price for the order.
5. Choose your preferred payment method from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant details.
* **Shipping Information** (`shipping_info`, text, optional): Enter your shipping address and any other necessary details.
* **Product Information** (`product_info`, text, optional): Enter the specific product(s) you're ordering.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter the shipping address for the order.
* **Total Price** (`total_price`, number, optional): Enter the total price for the order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred payment method from the options provided: credit card, PayPal, or bank transfer.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
