# Automotive Part Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information about your automotive part delivery, including order details, customer information, delivery address, parts information, delivery details, and condition and signature.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields with accurate information.
2. Ensure you have all necessary details before submitting the form.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Number** (`order_number`, `text`, required): Enter the number of your order to track your delivery.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your order.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, `number`, required): Enter the total cost of your order.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the customer.
* **Email Address** (`customer_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Company Name** (`customer_company`, `text`, optional): Enter your company name if applicable.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, required): Enter the delivery address where the parts will be received.
* **City** (`delivery_city`, `text`, required): Enter the city where the parts will be delivered.
* **State** (`delivery_state`, `text`, required): Enter the state where the parts will be delivered.
* **Zip Code** (`delivery_zip`, `text`, required): Enter the zip code where the parts will be delivered.
* **Number of Parts in Order** (`part_count`, `number`, required): Enter the number of parts in your order.
* **Types of Parts** (`part_types`, `text`, required): Briefly describe the types of parts ordered.
* **Packaging Condition Upon Receipt** (`packaging_condition`, `select_one`, required): Choose the condition of the packaging upon receipt (Excellent, Good, Acceptable, Damaged, Missing Items).
* **Expected Delivery Date** (`expected_delivery_date`, `date`, required): Enter the expected delivery date.
* **Actual Delivery Date** (`actual_delivery_date`, `date`, required): Enter the actual delivery date.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose the delivery method (Standard Shipping, Express Shipping, Overnight Shipping, Local Pickup, Will Call).
* **Received By (Name)** (`received_by`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person receiving the delivery.
* **Any Damage or Issues with Delivery** (`condition_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any damage or issues with the delivery.
