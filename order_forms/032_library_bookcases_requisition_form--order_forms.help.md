# Library Bookcases Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Library Bookcases Requisition Form is a request for library bookcases. It is used to gather information about the customer's needs and preferences for bookcase deliveries.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the "Customer Name" and "Customer Email" fields respectively.
2. Enter the title of the book you are interested in.
3. Enter the author of the book you are interested in.
4. Enter the price you are willing to pay for the book.
5. Enter the quantity of books you are interested in.
6. Enter any special message or notes for the customer in the "Customer Message" field.
7. Select the delivery date you prefer.
8. Select the delivery time you prefer.
9. Choose the delivery location.
10. Choose the delivery status (Active or Inactive).
11. Select the delivery method (Yes or No).
12. Enter any additional delivery notes in the "Delivery Note" field.
13. Enter the customer's phone number.
14. Enter the customer's address.
15. Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for delivery in the "Delivery Contact" field.

* Double-check your entries for accuracy and consistency.
