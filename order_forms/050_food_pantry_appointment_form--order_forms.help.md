# Food Pantry Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for food pantry appointments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Name, which is used to identify you on the form.
2. Provide your Email address, which is used to communicate with you about your appointment.
3. Enter your Phone Number, which is used to contact you if needed.
4. Choose a Date for your appointment.
5. Select a Time for your appointment.
6. Type in any additional information about your address.
7. Add any notes about your appointment in the Notes field.
8. If you have any specific tools assigned to you, select one from the list.
9. Fill in your Form ID, which is a unique identifier for this form.
10. If multiple users are submitting on your behalf, select their names from the list.
11. Enter the date when you submitted this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name (text)**: Your name as it appears on this form.
* **Email (email)**: A contact email address for you.
* **Phone Number (number)**: Your phone number for any follow-up communications.
* **Date (date)**: The date of your appointment.
* **Time (time)**: The time of your appointment.
* **Address (text)**: Any additional address information for the appointment.
* **Notes (note)**: Optional notes about your appointment.
* **Assigned Tool (select_one)**: The tool assigned to you for the appointment (Chatjimmy, Chatjane, or Chatjohn).
* **Form ID (number)**: A unique identifier for this form.
* **Submitted By (select_multiple)**: If you're submitting on behalf of others, select their names here (e.g., John Doe, Jane Doe, or John Smith).
* **Submitted On (date)**: The date you submitted this form.

**Tips**

* Please ensure you only fill out the required fields for your specific situation.
* Review your information carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your contact for clarification.
