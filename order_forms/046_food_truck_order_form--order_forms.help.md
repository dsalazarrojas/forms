# Food Truck Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers about their orders from a food truck. It helps the food truck staff to accurately process and fulfill customer requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  On the first page, enter the "Food Truck Order" in the text field. This is where you can describe the order you would like to place.
2.  On the second page, provide your name as the "Customer" in the text field. This will help the food truck staff know who is making the order.
3.  On the third page, add any additional details about your order in the "Order Details" text field. You can use this space to specify any special requests or requests that you would like to make.
4.  On the fourth page, confirm the "Order Total" in the text field, which is likely where you will be asked to confirm the total cost of your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Food Truck Order** (`food_truck_order`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the order you would like to place. For example, "1 x Burger and 1 x Fries."
*   **Customer** (`customer`, `text`, optional): Provide your name as the person making the order. This will help the food truck staff know who to address the order to.
*   **Order Details** (`order_details`, `text`, optional): If you have any special requests or additional information about your order, such as dietary restrictions or allergy concerns, enter them in this field.
*   **Order Total** (`order_total`, `text`, optional): Confirm the total cost of your order. This ensures that the food truck staff accurately processes your payment.
