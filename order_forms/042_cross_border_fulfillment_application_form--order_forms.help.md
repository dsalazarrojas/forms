# Cross Border Fulfillment Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cross Border Fulfillment Application Form is used to apply for the Cross Border Fulfillment service, a international shipping service that helps businesses send packages across borders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
- Click on the "Submit" button after filling out all the required fields.
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
- The form has multiple pages, please fill out one page at a time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Page
* **First Page** (1, page, not required): This page is the introduction page of the form, and serves as a placeholder for other pages to follow.

### First Page (1)
* **Contact Person** (2, select_one, required): This field is for selecting the contact person of the company. Please choose "Mr", "Mrs", or "Ms" depending on the contact person's identity.
* **Email** (3, email, required): This is the contact email address of the company. Please enter a valid email address.
* **Date of Birth** (4, date, required): This field is for the date of birth of the contact person. Please enter the date in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Phone** (5, text, required): This is the contact phone number of the company. Please enter a valid phone number.
* **Company Name** (6, text, required): This is the name of the company. Please enter the company's name as it appears on its official documents.
* **Company Type** (7, select_one, required): This field is for selecting the type of company. Please choose "Sole Trader", "Partnership", or "Company" depending on the company type.
* **Address** (8, text, required): This is the address of the company. Please enter a valid address.
* **City** (9, text, required): This is the city of the company's address. Please enter the city name as it appears on the address.
* **State/Province** (10, text, required): This is the state or province of the company's address. Please enter the state or province name as it appears on the address.
* **Zip/Postal Code** (11, text, required): This is the zip or postal code of the company's address. Please enter a valid zip or postal code.
* **Country** (12, select_one, required): This field is for selecting the country of the company's address. Please choose "Yes" or "No" depending on the country of the company's address.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for assistance.
* This form is intended for business customers, please do not submit this form if you are not a business customer.
