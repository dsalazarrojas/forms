# 3D Prototype Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for ordering 3D prototypes. It gathers contact information, order details, and shipping and payment information from the user. The form is divided into sections for easier navigation and understanding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contact information section with your full name, company name (if applicable), email address, and phone number (including country code).
2. Provide the order details: order date, item or product name, primary material, desired surface finish, quantity, unit price, and part dimensions (length, width, height, and wall thickness).
3. Select the preferred delivery method (standard, express, pickup, or local delivery) and enter the delivery address (street, city, state/province, and postal code) if applicable.
4. Choose a payment method (credit card, bank transfer, PayPal, or invoice).
5. Confirm the order total (calculated as quantity × unit price).
6. Enter the estimated lead time in business days.
7. Add any additional comments or notes helpful for production or delivery.
