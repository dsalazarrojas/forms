# Fireproof Cabinet Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fireproof Cabinet Order Form is used to place orders for fireproof cabinets. It collects information from customers, including delivery address, cabinet selections, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **Customer Details**. This includes your name, contact information, and any other relevant details that you'd like to share with us.
2. Enter your **Delivery Address** to ensure that we can ship your cabinet to the correct location.
3. Choose your preferred **Cabinet Type**. We offer Standard, Premium, and Deluxe options.
4. Select a **Cabinet Color** from our available options.
5. Decide on a **Delivery Date** and **Delivery Time** that suits you.
6. Add any **Delivery Notes** if you have specific instructions or requests.
7. Choose your preferred **Payment Method**. We accept Credit Card, PayPal, and Bank Transfer.
8. Enter the **Payment Amount** that you'd like to pay.
9. Fill in your **Customer Notes** if you have any special requests or comments.
10. Confirm that you **Agree to Terms**.
11. Choose to **Pay Now** or **Pay Later**.
12. Sign your name on the **Signature** line to confirm your order.
13. Confirm that you've received the **Cabinet Type** and **Cabinet Color** you've chosen.
14. Confirm that you've received the **Delivery Date** and **Delivery Time**.
15. Add any **Customer Signature** to confirm your order.
16. Finally, confirm that you've received the **Cabinet** and **Delivery** details.

* **Customer Details**: Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details that you'd like to share with us.
* **Delivery Address**: Enter your delivery address so we can ship your cabinet to the correct location.
* **Cabinet Selections**: Choose your preferred cabinet type, color, and other customizations.
* **Delivery Date**: Choose a delivery date that suits you.
* **Delivery Time**: Choose a delivery time that suits you.
* **Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount you'd like to pay.

* **Agree to Terms**: Confirm that you agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Pay Now** / **Pay Later**: Choose to pay immediately or later.
* **Signature**: Sign your name to confirm your order.
* **Customer Signature**: Add your customer signature to confirm your order.
* **Confirm Cabinet**: Confirm that you've received the cabinet type and color you've chosen.
* **Confirm Delivery**: Confirm that you've received the delivery date and time you've chosen.
* **Confirm Delivery**: Confirm that you've received the delivery details.
* **Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount you'd like to pay.
* **Customer Notes**: Add any special requests or comments.
