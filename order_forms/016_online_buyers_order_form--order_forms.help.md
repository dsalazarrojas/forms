# Online Buyers Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online buyer's order form is designed to help customers place orders or requests for goods or services. Please fill out this form to initiate a purchase or inquiry.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Choose your payment method (LBC, Cebuana Lhuillier, M Lhuillier, or Western Union).
2. Enter your customer name.
3. Enter your customer phone number.
4. Enter your customer address.
5. Enter your customer email address.
6. Provide a brief description of your order details.
7. Indicate if you have a second payment method (yes or no).
8. Enter the order amount (in numerical value).
9. Select the date of your order.
10. Select the time of your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **LBC** (`payment_method`, select_one, required: false): Choose your preferred payment method for this order.
* **Customer Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as the customer placing this order.
* **Customer Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your order.
* **Customer Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your physical address for shipping or delivery purposes.
* **Customer Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for updates on your order.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required: false): Describe the item or service you are ordering, including any special instructions.
* **Second Payment Method** (`payment_method2`, select_multiple, required: false): If you have a second payment method, select "Yes" to continue.
* **Order Amount** (`order_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the amount of the order.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required: false): Select the date of your order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, required: false): Select the time of your order.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields, if any.
* Double-check your order details and payment method to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* This form is intended for single-item or single-quantity orders. If you have multiple items or quantities, please contact us directly for assistance.
