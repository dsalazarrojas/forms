# Online Return Envelope Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Return Envelope Order Form is used to gather information about an order and prepare a return envelope for an upcoming shipment. This form helps the customer provide necessary details about their order, payment, and shipping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you are entering the correct information.
3. Submit the form once you have filled out all the necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Form (1)**: This is the main order information section. Enter your order number, which can be found on your order confirmation email or receipt.
* **Shipping (2)**: This field is for entering shipping information such as the shipping address and any additional shipping instructions.
* **Payment (3)**: Here you can enter payment details such as the payment method and any additional payment information.
* **Customer Info (4)**: Enter your customer name and any other relevant information about your order.
* **Shipping Info (5)**: Enter shipping information such as the shipping date and any additional shipping instructions.
* **Return Info (6)**: Enter return information such as the return date and any additional return instructions.
* **Order Details (7)**: Enter any additional information about your order that you feel is necessary.
* **Return Forms (8)**: Enter any additional information about the return envelope you need.
* **Payment Details (9)**: Enter payment details such as the payment total.
* **Customer Form (10)**: This field is currently not in use.
* **Shipping Details (11)**: Enter shipping details such as shipping notes.
* **Return Envelopes (12)**: Enter return envelope information such as the number of envelopes needed.
* **Payment Summary (13)**: Enter payment summary information such as any additional payment information.
* **Customer Address (14)**: Enter your customer address.
* **Order Status (15)**: Enter order status information such as any additional status updates.
* **Payment Methods (16)**: Enter payment methods such as credit card or bank transfer.
* **Order Notes (17)**: Enter any additional notes about your order.
* **Payment Status (18)**: Enter payment status such as payment confirmation.
* **Shipping Status (19)**: Enter shipping status such as shipping confirmation.
* **Shipping Notes (20)**: Enter shipping notes such as any additional shipping instructions.
* **Order ID (21)**: Enter your order ID number.
* **Envelope ID (22)**: Enter your envelope ID number.
* **Order Date (23)**: Enter the date of your order.
* **Shipping Date (24)**: Enter the shipping date of your order.
* **Payment Total (25)**: Enter the total payment amount.

Note: Please double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
