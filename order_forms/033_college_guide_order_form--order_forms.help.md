# College Guide Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to order college guides that cater to your specific needs and preferences. Please fill out the form to provide us with your information and preferences so we can tailor the guides to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your full name and email address to contact you regarding your order.
2. Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
3. Specify your relationship to the student (e.g., parent, student, counselor, etc.).
4. Choose the type of guide you are ordering (e.g., general college guide, engineering school guide, etc.).
5. Indicate the quantity of guides you would like to order.
6. Select the format of the guides (print, digital, or both).
7. Enter the price per guide (if applicable).
8. Enter the total cost of the order (if applicable).
9. Provide your shipping address to receive the guides.
10. Enter your city, state, and country for shipping.
11. Choose your shipping method (e.g., standard mail, expedited mail, etc.).
12. Decide if your billing address is the same as your shipping address.
13. If your billing address is different, enter the billing address and city, state, and country.
14. Select your payment method (e.g., credit card, debit card, etc.).
15. Enter any special delivery instructions or notes.
16. Agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name to contact you regarding your order.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address to contact you regarding your order.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
* **Relationship to Student**: Specify your relationship to the student (e.g., parent, student, counselor, etc.).
* **Which College Guide Are You Ordering**: Choose the type of guide you are ordering (e.g., general college guide, engineering school guide, etc.).
* **Quantity Ordered**: Enter the number of guides you would like to order.
* **Guide Format**: Select the format of the guides (print, digital, or both).
* **Price Per Guide**: Enter the price per guide (if applicable).
* **Total Cost**: Enter the total cost of the order (if applicable).
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your shipping address to receive the guides.
* **City**: Enter your city for shipping.
* **State/Province**: Enter your state or province for shipping.
* **Postal Code**: Enter your postal code for shipping.
* **Country**: Enter your country for shipping.
* **Shipping Method**: Choose your shipping method (e.g., standard mail, expedited mail, etc.).
* **Billing Address Same As Shipping Address**: Decide if your billing address is the same as your shipping address.
* **Billing Address - if different**: If your billing address is different, enter the billing address and city, state, and country.
* **Payment Method**: Select your payment method (e.g., credit card, debit card, etc.).
* **Student Academic Interests**: Enter any academic interests or fields that may help us tailor the guides to your needs.
* **College Size Preference**: Choose the size of college you prefer (e.g., small, medium, large, etc.).
* **Special Delivery Instructions**: Enter any special delivery instructions or notes.
* **I agree to the terms and conditions**: Agree to the terms and conditions.

## Tips

* Please make sure to enter your information accurately to ensure timely and correct processing of your order.
* If you have any special requests or instructions, please enter them in the "Special Delivery Instructions" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us through the provided email address.
* We will process your order and contact you regarding any issues or questions.
