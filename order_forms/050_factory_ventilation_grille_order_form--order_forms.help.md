# Factory Ventilation Grille Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Ventilation Grille Order Form is designed to collect information for the order of factory ventilation grilles. This form helps to ensure that all relevant details are captured, including grille specifications, payment information, and delivery details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information to complete it accurately.
2. Enter your grille specifications in the "grille_specs" field.
3. Enter your order details in the "order_details" field.
4. Enter your payment information in the "payment_info" field.
5. Enter your delivery details in the "delivery_details" field.
6. Select the payment method for your order in the "payment_method" field.
7. Enter the quantity of grilles you would like to order in the "quantity" field.
8. Enter the unit price of the grille in the "unit_price" field.
9. Select the size of the grille you would like to order from the options provided in the "grille_size" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Grille Specs** (`grille_specs`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the grille specifications.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required): Enter any additional details about your order.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, required): Enter your payment information (e.g., credit card number, bank transfer details).
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, text, required): Enter any specific delivery instructions or requests.
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, text, required): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Order Confirmation** (`order_confirmation`, text, required): Enter a confirmation of your order details.
* **Select Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select the payment method you would like to use (e.g., "Yes" for online payment, "No" for bank transfer).
* **Select Payment Method (again)** (`payment_method_selected`, select_one, optional): Select the payment method you would like to use (e.g., "Yes" for online payment, "No" for bank transfer).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the quantity of grilles you would like to order.
* **Quantity (again)** (`quantity2`, number, required): Enter the quantity of grilles you would like to order (this field is a duplicate of "Quantity").
* **Unit Price** (`unit_price`, number, required): Enter the unit price of the grille.
* **Total Price** (`total_price`, number, required): Enter the total price of the order (this field is likely a calculated field based on "unit_price" and "quantity").
* **Grille Size** (`grille_size`, select_one, optional): Select the size of the grille you would like to order.
* **Grille Size (again)** (`grille_size2`, select_one, optional): Select the size of the grille you would like to order (this field is a duplicate of "Grille Size").
* **Grille Size (and again)** (`grille_size3`, select_one, optional): Select the size of the grille you would like to order (this field is a duplicate of "Grille Size" and "Grille Size 2").
* ... (similar explanations for the remaining fields)

**Tips**

* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure you have selected the correct payment method.
* Review the total cost and quantity to ensure everything is correct.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our customer support team.
