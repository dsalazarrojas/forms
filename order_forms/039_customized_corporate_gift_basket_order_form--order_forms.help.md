**Customized Corporate Gift Basket Order Form - Help Guide**
## Purpose
This form is used to create customized corporate gift baskets for corporate events. It captures information about the gift, including company name, gift choice, quantity, payment method, and shipping details.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company to be gifted.
2. **Email**: Enter the email address of the company contact.
3. **Gift Choice**: Select one or more gift options from the available options.
4. **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the gift.
5. **Order Date**: Select the date for the order.
6. **Payment Method**: Select the payment method for the order.
7. **Shipping Address**: Enter the shipping address details.
8. **Shipping City**: Enter the city of the shipping address.
9. **State**: Enter the state of the shipping address.
10. **Zip**: Enter the zip code of the shipping address.
11. **Country**: Enter the country of the shipping address.
12. **Gift Message**: Enter a gift message for the gift.
13. **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the company contact.
14. **Order Notes**: Enter any additional order notes.
15. **Gift Card Option** (optional): Select one or more gift card options.
16. **Gift Card Value** (optional): Enter the value of the gift card.
17. **Gift Card PIN** (optional): Enter the gift card PIN.
18. **Gift Card Design** (optional): Select the design of the gift card.
19. **Custom Gift Option** (optional): Select one or more custom gift options.
20. **Payment Confirmation** (required): Confirm the payment method for the order.
