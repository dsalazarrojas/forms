# Flower Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Flower Delivery Form is a tool used to collect information for flower delivery orders. It is used to determine the best time and type of flower for delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether you want to schedule a delivery time or date.
2. Choose the type of flower you would like to be delivered.
3. Enter any additional details for the delivery.
4. Enter the quantity of flowers you would like to order.
5. Enter the price you are willing to pay.
6. Select your payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Delivery Form** (`delivery-form`, select multiple, optional): 
  This field is used to select whether you would like to schedule a delivery time or date.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery-date`, date, optional): 
  This field is for selecting the date you would like to receive your delivery.
* **Order Time** (`order-time`, time, optional): 
  This field is for selecting the time you would like to receive your delivery.
* **Personalized Message** (`personalized-message`, text, optional): 
  This field is for adding any additional details to your delivery order.
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery-details`, select one, required): 
  This field is used to specify the type of delivery you would like (e.g. Delivery Time or Delivery Date).
* **Flower Type** (`flower-type`, select one, required): 
  This field is used to choose the type of flower you would like to be delivered (e.g. Rose, Daisies, Lilies, or Tulips).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): 
  This field is for entering the number of flowers you would like to order.
* **Price** (`price`, number, optional): 
  This field is for entering the price you are willing to pay for the flowers.
* **Payment Method** (`payment-method`, select one, required): 
  This field is used to choose how you would like to pay for your flowers (e.g. Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).

## Tips
- Make sure to select the correct delivery details and flower type.
- Enter the correct quantity and price for your order.
- Select your preferred payment method carefully, as some methods may have additional requirements or restrictions.
