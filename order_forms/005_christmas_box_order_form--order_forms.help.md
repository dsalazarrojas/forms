# Christmas Box Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a Christmas Box order. It includes fields for customer details, shipping information, order details, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field. Please ensure it's a valid email address.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. If applicable, enter the name of the company or organization you represent in the "Company Name" field.
6. Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field.
7. Enter the city where you'd like to have the order shipped to in the "City" field.
8. Enter the state or province where you'd like to have the order shipped to in the "State or Province" field.
9. Enter the ZIP or postal code of the shipping address in the "ZIP or Postal Code" field.
10. Enter the country of the shipping address in the "Country" field.
11. Select the type of Christmas box you'd like to order from the "Christmas Box Type" field.
12. Indicate how many boxes you'd like to order in the "Quantity" field.
13. Select any box customization options you'd like to apply in the "Box Customization Options" field.
14. Choose your preferred delivery date in the "Preferred Delivery Date" field.
15. If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, please mention them in the "Any dietary restrictions or allergies?" field.
16. If you'd like to include a gift message, please enter it in the "Gift Message" field.
17. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" field.
