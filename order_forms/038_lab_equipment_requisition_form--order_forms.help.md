# Lab Equipment Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lab Equipment Requisition Form is a tool used by lab administrators or researchers to request new lab equipment or supplies. This form ensures that all necessary information is collected in an organized manner, allowing the lab manager to track and process equipment requisitions efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the first page, you will be asked to enter a title for your requisition. This will help the lab manager to identify the purpose of the equipment.
2. On the second page, you will be asked to provide details about the equipment you are requesting.
3. On the third page, you will be asked to provide more information about the order itself, such as the order date and expected delivery date.
4. On the fourth page, you will be asked to provide delivery details, such as delivery address and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Lab Equipment Requisition Form (Page 1)
* **Lab Equipment Requisition Form** (`title`, required): Enter a brief description of the equipment you are requesting, such as a microscope or a centrifuge.

### Equipment Details (Page 2)
* **Equipment Details** (`equipment`, optional): Describe the equipment you are requesting, including its brand, model, and any special features.

### Order Details (Page 3)
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, required): Enter the date when you would like to receive the equipment.
* **Expected Delivery Date** (`expected_delivery_date`, optional): Enter the date when you expect to receive the delivery.

### Delivery Details (Page 4)
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, required): Enter the address where the equipment should be delivered.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, required): Enter your name and contact information so that the supplier can contact you to arrange delivery.
