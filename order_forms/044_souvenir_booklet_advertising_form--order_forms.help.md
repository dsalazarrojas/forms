# Souvenir Booklet Advertising Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Souvenir Booklet Advertising Form is designed to collect information from businesses and individuals who wish to advertise their products or services in a souvenir booklet. This form helps in processing and preparing the advertising content for publication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form correctly, follow the steps outlined below:

1. Ensure you have all the required information before proceeding to fill out the form.
2. Fill out the form in a single sitting to avoid errors and inconsistencies.
3. Double-check your submissions for accuracy before clicking the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Details** (`customer_details`): Enter the name, contact number, and email address of the individual or business placing the order.
  - This field is not required.
- **Select Ad Size** (`souvenir_ad_size`): Select the size of the souvenir booklet required for your advertising content.
  - Options: 100x100, 150x150, 200x200
  - This field is not required.
- **Select Business** (`select_business`): Select the business type that will be advertised.
  - Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
  - This field is not required.
- **Payment Details** (`payment_details`): Enter any additional payment details if required.
  - This field is not required.
- **Select Payment Method** (`select_payment_method`): Choose the method of payment for your advertising content.
  - Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
  - This field is required.
- **Business Name** (`business_name`): Enter the name of your business.
  - This field is not required.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter your contact number.
  - This field is not required.
- **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
  - This field is required.
- **Business Address** (`business_address`): Enter your business address.
  - This field is not required.
- **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`): Enter the amount to be paid for the advertising content.
  - This field is required.
- **Payment Method Notes** (`payment_method_notes`): Add any additional notes about the payment method.
  - This field is not required.
- **Date of Advert** (`date_of_advert`): Select the date when the advert will be displayed.
  - This field is not required.
- **Time of Advert** (`time_of_advert`): Select the time when the advert will be displayed.
  - This field is not required.
- **Advert Image** (`advert_image`): Upload an image of the product or service to be advertised.
  - This field is required.
- **Advert Description** (`advert_description`): Briefly describe the product or service being advertised.
  - This field is required.

Note: Please review each field carefully and fill out the form according to the provided instructions. If you have any further queries or concerns, contact our support team for assistance.
