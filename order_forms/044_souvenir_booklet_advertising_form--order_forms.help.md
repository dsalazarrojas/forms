# Souvenir Booklet Advertising Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form
## Field-by-Field Explanation
  - **Customer Details**: Enter the name, contact number, and email address of the individual or business placing the order.
  - **Select Ad Size**: Select the size of the souvenir booklet required for your advertising content.
  - **Select Business**: Select the business type that will be advertised.
  - **Payment Details**: Enter any additional payment details if required.
  - **Select Payment Method**: Choose the method of payment for your advertising content.
  - **Business Name**: Enter the name of your business.
  - **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number.
  - **Email**: Enter your email address.
  - **Business Address**: Enter your business address.
  - **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount to be paid for the advertising content.
  - **Payment Method Notes**: Add any additional notes about the payment method.
  - **Date of Advert**: Select the date when the advert will be displayed.
  - **Time of Advert**: Select the time when the advert will be displayed.
  - **Advert Image**: Upload an image of the product or service to be advertised.
  - **Advert Description**: Briefly describe the product or service being advertised.
