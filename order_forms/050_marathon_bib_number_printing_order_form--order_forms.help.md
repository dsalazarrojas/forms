# marathon_bib_number_printing_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to order marathon bib number printing services. Please provide the required information to place your order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the event name in the "event_name" field.
2. If you have a design uploaded, please provide it in the "design_upload" field.
3. Enter your shipping information in the "shipping_info" field.
4. Provide your address in "shipping_address_1", "shipping_address_2", "shipping_city", and "shipping_state" fields.
5. Enter your shipping zip in the "shipping_zip" field.
6. Select your country in the "shipping_country" field.
7. Provide payment information in the "payment_info" field.
8. Choose your payment method from the "payment_method" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **event_name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event where you are ordering for.
* **upload_design** (`design_upload`, text, required): If you have an uploaded design, please provide the file name and path.
* **shipping_info** (`shipping_info`, text, required): Enter any specific shipping instructions or special requests.
* **shipping_address_1** (`shipping_address_1`, text, required): Enter your shipping address line 1.
* **shipping_address_2** (`shipping_address_2`, text, required): Enter your shipping address line 2.
* **shipping_city** (`shipping_city`, text, required): Enter the city of your shipping address.
* **shipping_state** (`shipping_state`, text, required): Enter the state of your shipping address.
* **shipping_zip** (`shipping_zip`, text, required): Enter the zip code of your shipping address.
* **shipping_country** (`shipping_country`, text, required): Enter the country of your shipping address.
* **payment_info** (`payment_info`, text, required): Enter any payment information or special instructions.
* **payment_method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose your payment method from the options:
	+ Card Number
	+ Bank Transfer
	+ Check

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need help, please contact our support team.
* Please note that we will contact you if any additional information is required to complete your order.
* We will process your order as soon as possible after receiving your submission.
