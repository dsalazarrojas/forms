# Brownies Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Brownies Order Form is used to place an order for a custom brownie. This form collects information about the customer's details, order preferences, and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Customer Name** in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Provide your **Email Address** in the required "Email Address" field.
3. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Enter your **Delivery Address**, including the street address, in the "Delivery Address" field.
5. Select the **City** where you would like to receive your order.
6. Select the **State** where you would like to receive your order.
7. Enter your **Zip Code** where you would like to receive your order.
8. Choose the **Desired Delivery Date** for your order.
9. Select the **Brownie Flavor** (up to 1 option) you would like to order. Select one from the list: Classic Chocolate, Fudge, Walnut, Caramel, Cheesecake Swirl, Mint Chocolate Chip.
10. Enter the **Quantity** of brownies you would like to order.
11. If applicable, select additional flavors you would like to add to your order from the "Any Additional Flavors" field.
12. If you have any **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies**, provide a brief description in the "Dietary Restrictions or Allergies" field.
13. If you have any **Special Requests or Customizations**, provide a brief description in the "Special Requests or Customizations" field.
14. Select the **Gift Wrapping** option for your order. You can choose from No Gift Wrap, Standard Gift Wrap, or Premium Gift Wrap.
15. Enter the estimated **Order Total** based on the quantity and flavor selected.
16. Finally, select the **Payment Method** for your order. You can choose from Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Check, or Cash.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (1): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification or credit card.
* **Email Address** (2): A valid email address is required for order updates and notifications.
* **Phone Number** (3): Your contact phone number for communication.
* **Delivery Address** (4): Your address where the order will be delivered.
* **City** (5): The city where you would like to receive your order.
* **State** (6): The state where you would like to receive your order.
* **Zip Code** (7): Your zip code where the order will be delivered.
* **Desired Delivery Date** (8): The date you would like to receive your order.
* **Brownie Flavor** (9): Select your favorite brownie flavor from the list.
* **Quantity** (10): The number of brownies you would like to order.
* **Any Additional Flavors** (11): Select all additional flavors you would like to add to your order.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (12): If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, provide a brief description.
* **Special Requests or Customizations** (13): If you have any special requests or customizations, provide a brief description.
* **Gift Wrapping** (14): Select the gift wrapping option for your order.
* **Order Total** (15): Enter the estimated order total based on the quantity and flavor selected.
* **Payment Method** (16): Select the method of payment for your order.
