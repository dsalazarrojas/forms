# Beauty Supplies Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beauty Supplies Order Form is used to collect customer information and order details for a beauty supply order. This includes customer contact details, product quantities, shipping method, and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **Full Name or Salon Name** in the "Full Name or Salon Name" field.
2.  Enter your **Email Address** in the "Email Address" field.
3.  Enter your **Shipping Address** in the "Shipping Address" field, including street, city, state, and ZIP.
4.  Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field.
5.  Select the **Shipping Method** from the dropdown list that best suits your needs.
6.  Select the **Payment Method** from the dropdown list that best suits your needs.
7.  If you have any **Special Shipping or Delivery Instructions**, enter them in the "Special Shipping or Delivery Instructions" field.
8.  Enter the **Order Date** for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name or Salon Name**: Enter your name or the name of your salon.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your shipping address, including street, city, state, and ZIP.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Shipping Method** (Standard Ground, Expedited, Overnight, Local Pickup): Select the shipping method that best suits your needs.
* **Payment Method** (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, Pay on Delivery, On Account): Select the payment method that best suits your needs.
* **Special Shipping or Delivery Instructions**: If you have any special instructions for shipping or delivery, enter them here.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date for your order. 

Please note that all fields marked as required are necessary for us to process your order. If you have any questions or need further assistance, please contact us.
