# Music Track Mastering Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information for music track mastering requests from artists or their representatives. The goal is to ensure that the artist's work meets the desired level of quality for release.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the artist information field with your name, email, and any other relevant contact details.
2. Upload or provide a link to your audio files for mastering.
3. Specify your mastering preferences, including the desired level of compression, EQ, and other relevant details.
4. Provide any relevant deadlines or timelines for the mastering process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Information** (Page 1, Field 1): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant contact information to ensure that we can reach you for any questions or updates during the mastering process.
* **Audio Files** (Page 1, Field 2): Upload your audio files or provide a link to them. This can be a mix or stem, but please ensure that the files are in the correct format and bitrate for mastering.
* **Mastering Preferences** (Page 1, Field 3): Specify your desired level of mastering, including any specific settings or styles you prefer. This can include EQ, compression, and other relevant details.
* **Deadlines** (Page 1, Field 4): Provide any relevant deadlines or timelines for the mastering process, including any specific dates or deliverables.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your audio files are in the correct format and bitrate for mastering.
* Be as specific as possible with your mastering preferences to ensure that your desired outcome is achieved.
* If you have any questions or concerns during the mastering process, please do not hesitate to reach out to us at [contact email].
