# Mask Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mask Order Form is used to collect information from customers about their order preferences for custom face masks. This includes selecting the type of mask, any specific face shape considerations, and other details to fulfill their order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Mask Order Form, follow these steps:

1. **Select the type of mask** that you want to order. Choose from the available options.
2. **Indicate if you have a specific face shape** that requires special consideration. Select multiple options if applicable.
3. **Enter your customer details**:
	* **Customer Name**
	* **Customer Email**
	* **Customer Phone**
	* **Customer Address**
	* **Customer City**
	* **Customer Country**
	* **Customer State**
	* **Customer ZIP**
4. **Specify any additional comments** about your order.
5. **Choose your shipping preference** (yes/no).
6. **Confirm your order details** by checking that everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mask Type**: Select the type of mask you want to order. This will help us determine the best fit for you.
* **Face Shape**: Indicate if you have a specific face shape that requires special consideration. We'll do our best to accommodate your needs.
* **Face Shape ID**: This field is currently unused but might be related to the Face Shape field.
* **Mask Size**: Choose the size of mask you prefer. Please note that this field might be related to the Face Mask Size ID field.
* **Face Mask Size ID**: This field is currently unused but might be related to the Mask Size field.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of masks you want to order.
* **Order Type**: Select whether this is a new or existing order.
* **Shipping**: Choose if you want to ship your order.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as the customer.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Address**: Enter your address.
* **Customer City**: Enter your city.
* **Customer Country**: Enter your country.
* **Customer State**: Enter your state or province.
* **Customer State/Province (Abbreviation)**: Enter the abbreviated state or province.
* **Customer ZIP**: Enter your ZIP code.
* **Customer Phone (Abbreviated)**: Enter your phone number (abbreviated).
* **Customer Email (Abbreviated)**: Enter your email address (abbreviated).
* **Order Comments**: Add any additional comments about your order.
* **Customer Name (Abbreviated)**: Enter your name (abbreviated).
* **Mask Type (Abbreviated)**: Select the type of mask (abbreviated).
* **Face Shape (Abbreviated)**: Indicate if you have a specific face shape (abbreviated).
* **Face Shape (Abbreviated) ID**: This field is currently unused but might be related to the Face Shape field.
* **Mask Size (Abbreviated)**: Choose the size of mask you prefer (abbreviated).
* **Face Mask Size (Abbreviated) ID**: This field is currently unused but might be related to the Mask Size field.
