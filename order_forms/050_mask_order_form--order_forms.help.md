# Mask Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mask Order Form is used to collect information from customers about their order preferences for custom face masks. This includes selecting the type of mask, any specific face shape considerations, and other details to fulfill their order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Mask Order Form, follow these steps:

1. **Select the type of mask** that you want to order. Choose from the available options.
2. **Indicate if you have a specific face shape** that requires special consideration. Select multiple options if applicable.
3. **Enter your customer details**:
	* **Customer Name**
	* **Customer Email**
	* **Customer Phone**
	* **Customer Address**
	* **Customer City**
	* **Customer Country**
	* **Customer State**
	* **Customer ZIP**
4. **Specify any additional comments** about your order.
5. **Choose your shipping preference** (yes/no).
6. **Confirm your order details** by checking that everything is correct.
