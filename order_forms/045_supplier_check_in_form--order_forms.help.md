# Supplier Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture information from suppliers about their order status. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure that your information is up-to-date and correctly recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information about your supplier.
2. Ensure you select the correct delivery status for each order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Supplier Details** (1)
* **(text)**: Enter a brief description of your supplier.

### **Name** (2)
* **(text)**: Enter the name of your company.

### **Contact Name** (3)
* **(text)**: Enter the name of the contact person responsible for this order.

### **Company** (4)
* **(text)**: Enter the name of the company associated with this order.

### **Email** (5)
* **(email)**: Enter the email of the contact person responsible for this order.

### **Contact Email** (6)
* **(email)**: Enter the email of the contact person responsible for this order.

### **Phone** (7)
* **(text)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person responsible for this order.

### **Contact Phone** (8)
* **(text)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person responsible for this order.

### **Address** (9)
* **(text)**: Enter the address of your supplier.

### **Order Number** (10)
* **(text)**: Enter the number of your order.

### **Delivery Details** (11)
* This field allows you to select the status of your delivery. Select one of the following:
	+ **Delivered**: The order has been delivered successfully.
	+ **Pending**: The order is still in progress.
	+ **Delayed**: The order is delayed due to unforeseen circumstances.

### **Supplier Arrival Time** (12)
* **(time)**: Enter the time of your supplier's expected or actual arrival.
