# Online Subscription Sample Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for an online subscription sample order. The form is used to collect personal and order-specific details such as name, email, phone number, subscription type, sample request, delivery date, delivery method, payment method, and address. This information will be used to process the order and ensure its successful delivery.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **First Name** (id: 1) and **Last Name** (id: 2) in the respective fields.
2. Enter your **Email** (id: 3) address to receive order updates and notifications.
3. Provide your **Phone Number** (id: 4) for delivery and order tracking purposes.
4. Select your preferred **Subscription Type** (id: 5) for the online sample:
	* 12-month: For a subscription of 12 months.
	* 6-month: For a subscription of 6 months.
	* 3-month: For a subscription of 3 months.
5. Specify your **Sample Request** (id: 6) in the field provided.
6. Choose a **Delivery Date** (id: 7) for the sample delivery.
7. Select the **Delivery Method** (id: 8) for your order:
	* Shipping: For delivery via postal mail.
	* Pickup: For self-pickup at our location.
8. Choose your preferred **Payment Method** (id: 9) for the order:
	* Credit Card: For online payment via credit card.
	* PayPal: For online payment via PayPal account.
	* Bank Transfer: For offline payment via bank transfer.
9. Fill in your **Payment Info** (id: 10) if you're using a payment method that requires it.
10. Provide your **Address** (id: 11) for delivery purposes.
11. Add any **Note** (id: 12) you'd like to include with your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (id: 1)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (id: 2)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email (id: 3)**: Enter your email address for order updates and notifications.
* **Phone (id: 4)**: Enter your phone number for delivery and order tracking.
* **Subscription Type (id: 5)**: Select the length of your subscription.
* **Sample Request (id: 6)**: Describe your sample request.
* **Delivery Date (id: 7)**: Choose a date for sample delivery.
* **Delivery Method (id: 8)**: Choose how you'd like your sample delivered.
* **Payment Method (id: 9)**: Select how you'd like to pay for your order.
* **Payment Info (id: 10)**: If you're using a payment method that requires it, fill in the payment information.
* **Address (id: 11)**: Enter your address for delivery purposes.
* **Note (id: 12)**: Include any additional note with your order.
