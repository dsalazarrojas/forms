# Team Branded Accessory Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the ordering of branded accessories for teams. It is intended for team members to submit their order requests, including team name, school name, order items, quantity, delivery date, delivery time, payment method, and any additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Team Name** in the first field. This is a required field that will help us identify the team you are representing.
2. Enter the **School Name** in the second field. This is also a required field that will help us confirm the school affiliation.
3. Select the **Order Items** that you would like to order. Choose from the available options to confirm whether you will be ordering each item.
4. Enter the **Quantity** of each item you are ordering.
5. Select the **Delivery Date** for your order.
6. Select the **Delivery Time** for your delivery.
7. Confirm your **Payment Method** for the order.
8. Add any **Payment Details** if you have any special payment instructions.
9. Add any **Notes** you have regarding your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name** (Required, Text): Enter the name of the team you are representing.
* **School Name** (Required, Text): Enter the name of the school affiliated with your team.
* **Order Items** (Required, Select Multiple): Choose from the available options to confirm whether you will be ordering each item.
* **Quantity** (Required, Number): Enter the number of each item you are ordering.
* **Delivery Date** (Required, Date): Select the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Delivery Time** (Required, Time): Select the time you would like to receive your order.
* **Payment Method** (Required, Select One): Confirm your payment method for the order.
* **Payment Details** (Optional, Text): Add any special payment instructions if necessary.
* **Notes** (Optional, Note): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
