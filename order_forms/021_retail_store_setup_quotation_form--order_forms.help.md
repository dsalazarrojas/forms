# Retail Store Setup Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Store Setup Quotation Form is a tool used by store employees to collect and organize data from vendors and customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the store setup information on page 1.
2. Provide vendor information on page 2.
3. Enter the vendor quote on page 3.
4. Enter the store quote on page 4.
5. Enter vendor payment terms on page 5.
6. Enter store payment terms on page 6.
7. Sign the form on page 7.
8. Sign the store signature on page 8.
9. Enter additional store setup information on page 9.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store Setup Information**: Enter the name and address of the store. (page 1)
* **Vendor Information**: Enter the vendor's name, address, and contact information. (page 2)
* **Vendor Quote**: Enter the vendor's quote for the store. (page 3)
* **Store Quote**: Enter the store's quote for the vendor. (page 4)
* **Vendor Payment Terms**: Enter the payment terms agreed upon with the vendor. (page 5)
* **Store Payment Terms**: Enter the payment terms agreed upon with the store. (page 6)
* **Signature**: Sign the form to confirm that you have reviewed and agree with the information provided. (page 7)
* **Store Signature**: Sign the form to confirm that the store has reviewed and agree with the information provided. (page 8)
* **Additional Store Setup Information**: Enter any additional information that is relevant to the store setup. (page 9) 

**Tips**: Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly. If you are unsure about any field, please ask a supervisor or manager for guidance.
