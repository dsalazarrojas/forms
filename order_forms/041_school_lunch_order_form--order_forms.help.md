# School Lunch Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for students and staff members to place orders for school lunches, allowing them to choose from various meal options and add extra selections as needed.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Start by selecting your preferred mains option from the "Select mains" section.
* Choose two snacks from the "Select snacks" section.
* Select a drink from the "Select drinks" section.
* Optionally, select an extra juice from the "Select juices" section.
* If desired, select an extra fruit from the "Select fruit" section.
* Choose a side from the "Select sides" section.
* Select a date and time for your order in the "Date" and "Time" sections.
* Add any additional notes in the "Note" section.
* Enter your email address in the "Email" section so we can contact you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select mains**: Choose one or more from the available mains options (Chicken nuggets, Ham sammies, Fish sticks, Veggie burger).
* **Select snacks**: Choose two from the available snack options (Apple, Carrot sticks, Chips).
* **Select drinks**: Choose one from the available drink options (Orange juice, Apple juice, Juice 1).
* **Select juices (extra)**: If you'd like an extra juice, choose from the available options (Orange juice, Apple juice).
* **Select fruit (extra)**: If you'd like an extra fruit, choose from the available options (Apple, Banana).
* **Select sides**: Choose one from the available side options (Carrot sticks, Salad, Rice).
* **Date**: Select a date for your order.
* **Time**: Select a time for your order.
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or requests.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.

## Tips

* Make sure to select all necessary fields to ensure your order is complete.
* Double-check your selections before submitting your order.
* If you have any special requests or questions, don't hesitate to contact us through the email provided.
