# second_hand_item_price_proposal_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a price proposal for a second-hand item. It captures information about the item being sold or purchased, including its condition, price, and contact information for the buyer and seller.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Second Hand Item" field with a brief description of the item you are selling or purchasing.
2. Select the "item_condition" field to indicate the condition of the item (new, used, or refurbished).
3. Enter the price of the item in the "item_price" field.
4. Enter the buyer's email address in the "Buyer Email" field.
5. Enter the buyer's phone number in the "Buyer Phone" field.
6. Enter any additional message from the buyer in the "Buyer Message" field.
7. Enter any additional message from the seller in the "Seller Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Second Hand Item** (`second_hand_item`, text, required/optional): A brief description of the item being sold or purchased.
- **item_condition** (`item_condition`, select_one, required/optional): Select the condition of the item (new, used, or refurbished).
- **item_price** (`item_price`, number, required/optional): The price of the item.
- **Buyer Email** (`buyer_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the buyer's email address.
- **Buyer Phone** (`buyer_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the buyer's phone number.
- **Buyer Message** (`buyer_message`, text, required/optional): Any additional message from the buyer.
- **Seller Message** (`seller_message`, text, required/optional): Any additional message from the seller.
