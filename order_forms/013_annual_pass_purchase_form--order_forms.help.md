# Annual Pass Purchase Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Annual Pass Purchase Form is used to purchase and manage annual passes for oneself or one's organization. It collects necessary information for pass processing, billing, and marketing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields (marked as 'required' in the form).
2. Select the type of pass you wish to purchase from the 'Select Pass Type' field.
3. Choose the number of passes to purchase from the 'Number of Passes' field.
4. Set your pass start date.
5. For family passes, list additional cardholders.
6. Select benefits packages (if applicable) and choose to upgrade to premium benefits.
7. Enter any discount code (if applicable).
8. Choose your payment method.
9. Agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter the name as it will appear on the pass.
* **Email Address**: Provide a valid email for pass delivery and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter a contact number for confirmation and any further communication.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth for age verification.
* **Billing Address**: Enter your billing address, including street address.
* **City**: Enter the city for your billing address.
* **State or Province**: Enter the state or province for your billing address.
* **Zip or Postal Code**: Enter your zip or postal code.
* **Select Pass Type**: Choose the type of pass you wish to purchase (Individual, Family, Student, etc.).
* **Number of Passes**: Enter the number of passes to buy.
* **Pass Start Date**: Set the effective date for the pass.
* **Additional Cardholders (for Family Pass)**: List names of family members if purchasing a family pass.
* **Benefits Selected**: Choose benefits packages (Unlimited visits, Guest privileges, etc.).
* **Upgrade to Premium Benefits**: Choose to upgrade to premium features if applicable.
* **Premium Upgrade Cost**: Enter any additional cost if upgrading to premium features (if applicable).
* **Pass Price**: Enter the base price of the selected pass.
* **Discount Code**: Enter any promotional code (if applicable).
* **Estimated Tax**: Estimated tax amount (calculated automatically).
* **Total Amount Due**: Sum of all charges, including any premium upgrade cost and tax.
* **Payment Method**: Choose your payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, etc.).
* **Auto-renew Next Year**: Choose to automatically renew the pass next year.
* **Opt in to Promotional Emails**: Choose to receive promotional emails or not.
* **I Agree to Terms and Conditions**: Confirm agreement to terms and conditions.
