# Telecommunications Service Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telecommunications Service Renewal Form is designed to collect information necessary to renew your telecommunications services, including your service type, contract details, and billing information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **renewal_service_id**, which is a unique identifier for your service.
2. Enter your **customer_id**, which is a required field for your customer information.
3. Select the **service_id** of the service you would like to renew.
4. Enter the **renewal_date** and **renewal_time** to schedule your renewal.
5. Provide a valid reason for **reason_for_renewal** (optional).
6. Choose your desired **service_type** from the dropdown menu (Voice, Data, Internet).
7. Enter your **service_contract_id** if applicable.
8. Add any additional comments in the **additional_comments** field.
9. Enter your **billing_address**, **billing_city**, **billing_state**, **billing_zip**, and **billing_country**.
10. Enter your **shipping_address**, **shipping_city**, **shipping_state**, and **shipping_zip**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **renewal_service_id**: This is a unique identifier for your service. Please enter your service's unique number.
* **customer_id**: This is a required field for your customer information.
* **service_id**: Select the ID of the service you would like to renew.
* **renewal_date**: Enter the date you would like to renew your service.
* **renewal_time**: Enter the time you would like to schedule your renewal.
* **reason_for_renewal**: Please enter a valid reason for renewing your service (optional).
* **service_type**: Choose your desired service type from the dropdown menu (Voice, Data, Internet).
* **service_contract_id**: This is the ID of your service contract. If applicable, please enter it.
* **renewal_terms**: Enter any additional comments or terms for your renewal.
* **customer_service_level**: Select your desired customer service level from the dropdown menu (Bronze, Silver, Gold).
* **customer_service_level_other**: If your service level is not listed, please enter your desired service level.
* **billing_cycle**: Choose your desired billing cycle from the dropdown menu (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
* **billing_cycle_other**: If your billing cycle is not listed, please enter your desired billing cycle.
* **renewal_status**: Select your desired renewal status from the dropdown menu (Active, Inactive).
* **renewal_status_other**: If your renewal status is not listed, please enter your desired status.
* **additional_comments**: Please add any additional comments or instructions for your renewal.
* **billing_address**: Enter your billing address.
* **billing_city**: Enter your billing city.
* **billing_state**: Enter your billing state.
* **billing_zip**: Enter your billing zip code.
* **billing_country**: Enter your billing country.
* **shipping_address**: Enter your shipping address.
* **shipping_city**: Enter your shipping city.
* **shipping_state**: Enter your shipping state.
* **shipping_zip**: Enter your shipping zip code.
* **shipping_country**: Enter your shipping country.
