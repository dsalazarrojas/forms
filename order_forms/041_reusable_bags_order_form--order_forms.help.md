# Reusable Bags Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information for reusable bag orders. Please fill it out carefully to ensure accurate processing of your order.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information (customer name, email, and phone number)
2. Select the type of reusable bags you would like to order
3. Provide the order date
4. Specify the quantity of bags needed
5. Select the payment method
6. Choose the order status
7. Add any additional notes or comments
8. Enter your shipping and billing addresses
9. Enter the order total, tax, shipping cost, and subtotal

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted for updates on your order.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for updates on your order.
* **Reusable Bags** (`reusable_bags`, select multiple, optional): Select the type of reusable bags you would like to order (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, etc.).
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to receive your order by.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the number of bags you would like to order.
* **Total Price** (`total_price`, number, optional): Enter the total price of the order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select one, optional): Select how you would like to pay for your order (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, etc.).
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select one, optional): Choose the status of your order (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, etc.).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter your shipping address where you would like to receive your order.
* **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`, text, optional): Enter the city of your shipping address.
* **Shipping State** (`shipping_state`, text, optional): Enter the state of your shipping address.
* **Shipping Zip** (`shipping_zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of your shipping address.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, text, optional): Enter your billing address where you would like to receive your invoice.
* **Billing City** (`billing_city`, text, optional): Enter the city of your billing address.
* **Billing State** (`billing_state`, text, optional): Enter the state of your billing address.
* **Billing Zip** (`billing_zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of your billing address.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Shipping Notes** (`shipping_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about shipping.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the customer.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, optional): Enter the total price of the order.
* **Order Total Tax** (`order_total_tax`, number, optional): Enter the tax amount of the order.
* **Order Total Shipping** (`order_total_shipping`, number, optional): Enter the shipping cost of the order.
* **Order Subtotal** (`order_subtotal`, number, optional): Enter the subtotal of the order.
