# Photography Session Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photography Session Order Form is used to gather necessary information from clients for a photography session. This form helps the client communicate their preferences and needs, allowing the photography studio to schedule and prepare for the session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Photography Session Order Form" field to indicate your interest in a photography session.
2. Enter your name in the "Client Name" field.
3. Provide your contact information (phone number and email) in the "Contact Number" and "Email" fields.
4. Choose a date and time for the photography session in the "Session Date" and "Session Time" fields.
5. Provide any additional session details in the "Session Details" field.
6. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Payment Option" field to indicate whether you wish to pay for the session upfront or have it charged to your account.
7. Choose a payment method in the "Payment Method" field.
8. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Payment Terms" field to indicate whether you agree to the payment terms.
9. Sign and date the form to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Photography Session Order Form** (`id: 1`, Select One, Required: No)
This field indicates whether you are interested in a photography session. Select "Yes" if you wish to schedule a session.

*   **Photography Session Order Form**: Select "Yes" to indicate your interest in a photography session.
*   **No**: Select "No" to decline a photography session.

### 2. **Client Name** (`id: 2`, Text, Required: Yes)
Enter your name in this field.

*   **Client Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.

### 3. **Contact Number** (`id: 3`, Text, Required: No)
Provide your contact number.

*   **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.

### 4. **Email** (`id: 4`, Email, Required: No)
Enter your email address.

*   **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.

### 5. **Session Date** (`id: 5`, Date, Required: No)
Choose a date for the photography session.

*   **Session Date**: Choose a date for the photography session.

### 6. **Session Time** (`id: 6`, Time, Required: No)
Choose a time for the photography session.

*   **Session Time**: Choose a time for the photography session.

### 7. **Session Details** (`id: 7`, Note, Required: No)
Provide any additional session details.

*   **Session Details**: Add any notes or preferences for the photography session.

### 8. **Payment Option** (`id: 8`, Select One, Required: No)
Select whether you wish to pay for the session upfront or have it charged to your account.

*   **Payment Option**: Select "Yes" to pay for the session upfront or "No" to have it charged to your account.

### 9. **Payment Method** (`id: 9`, Select One, Required: No)
Choose a payment method.

*   **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method.

### 10. **Payment Terms** (`id: 10`, Select One, Required: No)
Select whether you agree to the payment terms.

*   **Payment Terms**: Select "Yes" to agree to the payment terms or "No" to decline.

### 11. **Client Signature** (`id: 11`, Select One, Required: No)
Sign and date the form to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms.

*   **Client Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms.

### 12. **Photographer Signature** (`id: 12`, Select One, Required: No)
Sign and date the form to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms.

*   **Photographer Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms.

### 13. **Date of Session** (`id: 13`, Date, Required: No)
Choose the date of the photography session.

*   **Date of Session**: Choose the date of the photography session.

### 14. **Session Notes** (`id: 14`, Select Multiple, Required: No)
Select any additional session notes.

*   **Session Notes**: Select any additional session notes.

### 15. **Client Address** (`id: 15`, Select One, Required: No)
Select whether the client's address is required.

*   **Client Address**: Select "Yes" to require the client's address or "No" to decline.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the photography studio.
* The form is designed to be completed by the client, but it is the client's responsibility to ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete.
