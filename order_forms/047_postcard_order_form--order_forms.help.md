# postcard_order_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The postcard_order_form is used to collect information from customers when ordering postcards. This form helps us understand their preferences and requirements for the postcard order.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Determine whether you want to order a postcard (stationery_product) or not.
2.  Enter the quantity of postcards you would like to order.
3.  Choose your preferred payment method.
4.  Enter your email address.
5.  Provide your phone number.
6.  Select the date you would like to order the postcard.
7.  Choose the time of delivery (if different from the current date).
8.  Add any additional order notes (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Stationery Product** (`stationery_product`, Select One, Optional): Choose whether you want to order a postcard.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, Number, Required): Enter the number of postcards you would like to order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose how you would like to pay for your order (e.g., credit card, bank transfer, etc.).
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you (if needed).
* **Date** (`date`, Date, Optional): Select the date you would like to order the postcard.
* **Time** (`time`, Time, Optional): Choose the time of delivery (if different from the current date).
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, Note, Optional): Add any additional information about your order (e.g., special requests, delivery instructions, etc.).
