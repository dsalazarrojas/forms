# warranty_renewal_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information needed to renew a customer's warranty. It is only intended for existing customers who are renewing their warranty.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field accurately and completely, as indicated by the labels.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. If you need help, do not hesitate to ask a customer support staff member for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Warranty Details** (`warranty_details`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the warranty you are renewing.
* **Warranty Type** (`warranty_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of warranty you are renewing (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Warranty Date** (`warranty_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of your current warranty.
* **Warranty Expiration Date** (`warranty_expiration_date`, date, required): Enter the expiration date of your current warranty.
* **Reason for Renewal** (`reason_for_renewal`, text, required): Indicate why you are renewing your warranty (e.g., upgrade, downgrade, change).
* **Warranty Term** (`warranty_term`, text, optional): If you have a specific warranty term in mind, please enter it here.
* **Warranty Status** (`warranty_status`, text, optional): If you have any additional warranty status information, please enter it here.
* **Warranty Notes** (`warranty_notes`, text, optional): Enter any relevant notes or comments about your warranty.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you enter is accurate and complete.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask a customer support staff member for clarification.
* Submit the form only after all required fields are completed.
