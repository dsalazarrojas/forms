# Nails With Ashie Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for nail-related orders. It typically includes customer details and preferences for nail-related services or products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Provide the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field.
3. Select the desired order date from the calendar provided in the "Order Date" field.
4. Choose the type of nail service or product desired from the "Nail Type" dropdown list.
5. Select the color of the nails from the "Nail Color" dropdown list.
6. Enter the size of the nail in the "Nail Size" field.
7. Choose the shape of the nail from the "Nail Shape" dropdown list.
8. Select the desired finish for the nail from the "Nail Finish" dropdown list.
9. Add any additional notes or comments for the customer in the "Customer Notes" field.
10. Add any technician comments or notes in the "Technician Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Select the desired order date from the calendar.
* **Nail Type** (`nail_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of nail service or product desired.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Nail Color** (`nail_color`, select_multiple, optional): Select the color of the nails.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Nail Size** (`nail_size`, number, optional): Enter the size of the nail.
* **Nail Shape** (`nail_shape`, select_one, optional): Choose the shape of the nail.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Nail Finish** (`nail_finish`, select_multiple, optional): Select the desired finish for the nail.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments for the customer.
* **Technician Comments** (`technician_comments`, note, optional): Add any technician comments or notes.
