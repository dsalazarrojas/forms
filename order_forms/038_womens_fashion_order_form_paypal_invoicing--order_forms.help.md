# Womens Fashion Order Form Paypal Invoicing - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Womens Fashion Order Form Paypal Invoicing is a form designed to collect customer, shipping, and payment information for women's fashion orders. This form is intended to be filled out by customers placing an order with a business that accepts PayPal payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the customer information section with your details (name, email, phone number, etc.).
2. Provide your shipping information in the shipping information section (shipping address, shipping method, etc.).
3. Enter your payment information in the payment information section, ensuring you have a valid PayPal account or a payment method accepted by the seller.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Information (customer_info)** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Fill in your personal details (name, email, phone number, etc.) so we can better serve you.
- **Shipping Information (shipping_info)** (`shipping_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your shipping address, including any special instructions or delivery requests you may have.
- **Payment Information (payment_info)** (`payment_method`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your PayPal account information or other accepted payment methods to complete the order.
- **Order Details (order_details)** (`order_details`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your order, including the date of the order, items ordered, and any other relevant details.
