# Event Vendor Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Vendor Check Out Form is used to collect information from event vendors for checkout purposes. This form ensures that the vendor's details are accurate and complete, facilitating a smooth checkout process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields.
2. Enter the vendor's name in the "Vendor Name" field.
3. Enter the phone number of the vendor in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter the vendor's email address in the "Email" field.
5. Select the event date in the "Event Date" field.
6. Select the event time in the "Event Time" field.
7. Enter the total cost of the event in the "Total Cost" field.
8. Sign and date the "Vendor Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Vendor Info (Text)**: Enter a brief description of the event or the reason for the checkout.
* **Vendor Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the vendor, as it appears on their business card or invoice.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter the phone number of the vendor to facilitate communication.
* **Email (Text)**: Enter the email address of the vendor for easy contact.
* **Event Date (Date)**: Select the date of the event using the calendar tool or enter the date manually.
* **Event Time (Time)**: Select the time of the event using the time picker or enter the time manually.
* **Total Cost (Number)**: Enter the total cost of the event.
* **Vendor Signature (Text)**: Sign and date the vendor's signature to confirm their agreement and approval.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete information.
- Double-check the event date and time for consistency with the event schedule.
- Ensure the total cost is accurate and complete.
- The vendor's signature is a critical component of this form, as it confirms their agreement and approval.
- Keep the event vendor info section brief and to the point, as it will be used to facilitate communication during the checkout process.
