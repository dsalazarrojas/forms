# Art Services Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Services Order Form is a document used to collect information from clients for art services they are requesting. This form helps ensure that the company accurately captures the client's requirements for each service, allowing for efficient and high-quality service delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Client Name** and **Email Address** fields with your accurate contact information.
2. Select the **Type of Art Service Requested** that best describes your project.
3. Provide a detailed **Project Description**, including any specific themes, colors, or style preferences.
4. If applicable, specify the **Desired Dimensions** for the art service.
5. Estimate the **Budget** for the project, providing a range in USD.
6. Choose a **Preferred Payment Method** for your order.
7. Finally, confirm that you agree to the **Service Terms and Conditions**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Name**: A required field for identifying your name as a client.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true
  - Explanation: Enter your name so we can address you personally and efficiently.
- **Email Address**: A required field for contacting you about your order.
  - Type: email
  - Required: true
  - Explanation: Enter your email address so we can communicate with you about your project.
- **Type of Art Service Requested**: A required field to determine the service you need.
  - Type: select_one
  - Required: true
  - Explanation: Choose the service that best matches your project requirements.
  - Options: Custom Illustration, Graphic Design, Portrait Painting, Digital Art, Logo Creation
- **Detailed Project Description**: A required field for providing a clear understanding of your project requirements.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true
  - Explanation: Include themes, colors, and style preferences to ensure we understand your project needs.
- **Desired Dimensions**: An optional field to specify your desired art piece size.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
  - Explanation: If you want to ensure your project meets specific size requirements, please provide the dimensions (e.g., 11x14 inches).
- **Estimated Budget**: A required field to set expectations for your project cost.
  - Type: number
  - Required: true
  - Explanation: Enter a range in USD to help us estimate the project cost and ensure you are comfortable with the final price.
- **Requested Completion Date**: An optional field to specify when you need the project completed.
  - Type: date
  - Required: false
  - Explanation: If you have a specific deadline for your project, please provide the date you need the service completed by.
- **Preferred Payment Method**: A required field to facilitate payment processing.
  - Type: select_one
  - Required: true
  - Explanation: Choose your preferred method for payment, such as credit card, PayPal, or bank transfer.
  - Options: Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, Check
- **I Agree to the Service Terms and Conditions**: A required field to acknowledge our service agreement.
  - Type: select_one
  - Required: true
  - Explanation: Read and agree to the terms and conditions before proceeding with your order.
