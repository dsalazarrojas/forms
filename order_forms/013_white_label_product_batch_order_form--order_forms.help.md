# White Label Product Batch Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers place a bulk order for white label products. Please fill out the form with your details and we will get back to you with a quote and instructions on how to proceed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the `Contact Information` page with your name, email address, and phone number.
2. On the `Delivery Address` page, enter your delivery address details.
3. On the `Order Details` page, provide a brief description of the products you wish to order and the quantity.
4. On the `Payment Information` page, select your preferred payment method from the options provided.
5. On the `Payment Terms` page, enter any relevant payment terms or notes.
6. On the `Shipping Details` page, enter the details of your shipping method (e.g. ground, air, or courier).
7. On the `Payment Method` page, select your preferred shipping method from the options provided.
8. Finally, review your entire form and submit it to us once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, email address, and phone number so we can get in touch with you about your order.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your delivery address details, including street address, city, state, zip code, and country.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the products you wish to order and the quantity.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, required/optional): Enter any payment information you would like to provide, such as bank account details or credit card information.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, required/optional): Enter any relevant payment terms or notes that you would like to provide.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the details of your shipping method, including shipping speed and any special instructions.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required/optional): Select your preferred payment method from the options provided, which are:
	+ Stripe
	+ PayPal
	+ Bank Transfer
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your preferred shipping method from the options provided, which are:
	+ Ground Shipping
	+ Air Shipping
	+ Courier
