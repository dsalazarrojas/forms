# haptic_gaming_vest_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers to place an order for haptic gaming vests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:
1. Fill in the **Customer Information** field with your name, email, and other relevant contact details.
2. In the **Product Information** field, describe the type and model of the haptic gaming vest you are interested in purchasing.
3. Enter your **Payment** details, including the payment method and amount.
4. In the **Shipping Preferences** field, specify your preferred shipping method and address.
5. Choose your preferred **Shipping Options**, such as expedited shipping or different shipping locations.
6. Review and summarize your **Order Summary** at the end of the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (Customer Information, Text, Required): Enter your name, email, and other relevant contact details.
* **Product Information** (Product Information, Text, Optional): Describe the type and model of the haptic gaming vest you are interested in purchasing.
* **Payment** (Payment, Text, Required): Enter your payment method and amount.
* **Shipping Preferences** (Shipping Preferences, Text, Required): Specify your preferred shipping method and address.
* **Shipping Option** (Shipping Option, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose your preferred shipping options, such as expedited shipping or different shipping locations.
* **Order Summary** (Order Summary, Text, Optional): Review and summarize your order details for accuracy.
