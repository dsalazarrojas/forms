# Trade Appraisal CW - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trade Appraisal CW form is designed to collect information about trade-in vehicles and the terms of the trade.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the vehicle's VIN ( Vehicle Identification Number ) in the "vehicle_vin" field.
2. Enter the vehicle's year, make, and model in the "vehicle_year", "vehicle_make", and "vehicle_model" fields, respectively.
3. Enter the vehicle's year manufactured and last serviced in the "vehicle_year_manufactured" and "vehicle_year_last_serviced" fields, respectively.
4. Enter the appraiser's name, phone number, and email in the "appraiser_name", "appraiser_phone", and "appraiser_email" fields, respectively.
5. Enter the trade date and time in the "trade_date" and "trade_time" fields, respectively.
6. Enter any comments or notes about the vehicle in the "appraiser_comments" field.
7. Enter the purchase price of the vehicle and the trade-in value in the "vehicle_purchase_price" and "vehicle_trade_in_value" fields, respectively.
8. Enter the trade-out price in the "vehicle_trade_out_price" field.
9. Select "Accept" or "Decline" in the "trade_terms_accepted" field, depending on the terms of the trade.
10. Enter any notes about the trade terms in the "trade_terms_notes" field.
11. Enter the date and time the trade terms were accepted in the "trade_terms_date" and "trade_terms_time" fields, respectively.
12. Finally, sign and title the appraiser's name in the "appraiser_signature" and "appraiser_name_title" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **vehicle_vin** (`vehicle_vin`, text, required): Enter the vehicle's VIN (Vehicle Identification Number).
* **vehicle_year** (`vehicle_year`, number, required): Enter the vehicle's year.
* **vehicle_make** (`vehicle_make`, text, required): Enter the vehicle's make.
* **vehicle_model** (`vehicle_model`, text, required): Enter the vehicle's model.
* **vehicle_year_manufactured** (`vehicle_year_manufactured`, number, required): Enter the vehicle's year manufactured.
* **vehicle_year_last_serviced** (`vehicle_year_last_serviced`, number, required): Enter the vehicle's year last serviced.
* **appraiser_name** (`appraiser_name`, text, required): Enter the appraiser's name.
* **appraiser_phone** (`appraiser_phone`, text, required): Enter the appraiser's phone number.
* **appraiser_email** (`appraiser_email`, email, required): Enter the appraiser's email.
* **trade_date** (`trade_date`, date, required): Enter the trade date.
* **trade_time** (`trade_time`, time, required): Enter the trade time.
* **appraiser_comments** (`appraiser_comments`, text, required): Enter any comments or notes about the vehicle.
* **vehicle_purchase_price** (`vehicle_purchase_price`, number, required): Enter the purchase price of the vehicle.
* **vehicle_trade_in_value** (`vehicle_trade_in_value`, number, required): Enter the trade-in value of the vehicle.
* **vehicle_trade_out_price** (`vehicle_trade_out_price`, number, required): Enter the trade-out price.
* **trade_terms_accepted** (`trade_terms_accepted`, select_one, required): Select "Accept" or "Decline" to indicate the trade terms.
* **trade_terms_notes** (`trade_terms_notes`, text, required): Enter any notes about the trade terms.
* **trade_terms_date** (`trade_terms_date`, date, required): Enter the date the trade terms were accepted.
* **trade_terms_time** (`trade_terms_time`, time, required): Enter the time the trade terms were accepted.
* **appraiser_signature** (`appraiser_signature`, text, required): Sign the appraiser's name.
* **appraiser_name_title** (`appraiser_name_title`, text, required): Title the appraiser's name.
* **appraiser_title_designate** (`appraiser_title_designate`, text, required): Enter the appraiser's title or designation.
* **vehicle_year_purchased** (`vehicle_year_purchased`, number, required): Enter the year the vehicle was purchased.
* **trade_terms** (`trade_terms`, text, required): Enter any other notes about the trade terms.

**Tips**

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information about the vehicle and the trade terms.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the dealership's trade department.
