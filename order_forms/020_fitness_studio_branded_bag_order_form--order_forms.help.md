# Fitness Studio Branded Bag Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fitness Studio Branded Bag Order Form is designed to collect bag order information and payment details from customers. This form helps us understand their preferences for bag color, size, and type, as well as their preferred delivery and payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Order Details** section with any relevant comments or information about your order.
2. In the **Customer Information** section, provide your phone number and email address so we can contact you regarding your order.
3. Choose your preferred **Delivery Method** from the options provided (Pickup or Delivery).
4. Select your preferred **Payment Method** from the options provided (PayPal or Credit Card).
5. In the **Bag Info** section, choose one or more **Bag Color(s)** that you prefer.
6. Select your preferred **Bag Size** from the options provided (Small, Medium, Large, or X-Large).
7. Choose your preferred **Bag Type** from the options provided (Duffel, Drawstring, or Tote).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details** (1): A text field where you can provide any comments or information about your order.
* **Customer Information** (2): A text field where you can enter your phone number and email address for contact purposes.
* **Bag Info** (3): A text field where you can choose one or more bag colors (Black, Red, Blue).
* **Order Total** (4): A numeric field where you can enter your order total (if applicable).
* **Delivery Method** (5): A select-one field where you choose your preferred delivery method (Pickup or Delivery).
* **Customer Phone Number** (6): A text field where you can enter your phone number.
* **Customer Email** (7): A text field where you can enter your email address.
* **Payment Method** (8): A select-one field where you choose your preferred payment method (PayPal or Credit Card).
* **Bag Color** (9): A select-multiple field where you can choose one or more bag colors (Black, Red, Blue).
* **Bag Size** (10): A select-one field where you choose your preferred bag size (Small, Medium, Large, or X-Large).
* **Bag Type** (11): A select-one field where you choose your preferred bag type (Duffel, Drawstring, or Tote).
