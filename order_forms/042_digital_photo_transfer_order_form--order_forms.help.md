# digital_photo_transfer_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to place orders for digital photos. It collects information about the customer and their photo transfer requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Scroll through each page and read the label for each field.
2. Answer the questions and fill in the required information for each field.
3. Review your submissions before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, not required): This field is for the customer to provide their contact information, such as name, email, and phone number.
* **Transfer Information** (`transfer_info`, text, not required): This field is for the customer to describe their photo transfer requirements, such as the file format and resolution needed.
* **Design Details** (`design_details`, text, not required): This field is for the customer to provide any additional design details they may need for their photo transfer.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, not required): This field is for the customer to provide any additional contact information they may need to be reached back with.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, note, not required): This field is for the customer to leave any additional comments or notes they may have about their order.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields correctly to ensure your order is processed smoothly.
- If you have any questions or concerns about your order, please reach out to us directly.
