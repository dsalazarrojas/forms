# Coffee Beans Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Coffee Beans Order Form is designed for customers to submit orders for coffee beans, specifying their details such as customer information, delivery information, coffee bean selection, grind preference, roast date preference, special requests, and delivery method.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the customer information section, providing your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Choose your preferred delivery method.
3. Select the variety of coffee beans you'd like to order.
4. Specify the quantity of each coffee bean variety.
5. Choose your preferred grind type and roast date preference.
6. Enter any special requests or notes.
7. Finally, select the order date and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Your full name, used to identify you as a customer.
* **Email Address**: Your email address, used for communication and order updates.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number, used for contact and order tracking.
* **Shipping Address**: The address where you'd like your order delivered.
* **City**: The city where you'd like your order delivered.
* **State or Province**: The state or province where you'd like your order delivered.
* **ZIP Code or Postal Code**: The zip or postal code of your delivery address.
* **Country**: The country where you'd like your order delivered (if different from the country of your address).
* **Coffee Bean Variety 1**: Choose the type of coffee bean you'd like to order.
* **Quantity (pounds)**: Specify the quantity of the chosen coffee bean variety.
* **Coffee Bean Variety 2**: Choose an optional second type of coffee bean.
* **Quantity (pounds)**: Specify the quantity of the optional second coffee bean variety (if applicable).
* **Grind Type**: Choose the grind type you'd like for your coffee beans (Whole beans, Coarse grind, Medium grind, Fine grind, Extra fine grind).
* **Roast Date Preference**: Choose your preferred roast date preference (Most recent, 1-2 weeks old, 2-4 weeks old, No preference).
* **Special Requests or Notes**: Enter any additional preferences or customizations for your order.
* **Preferred Delivery Method**: Choose your preferred delivery method (Standard shipping, Express shipping, Priority mail, Local pickup).
* **Order Date**: Select the date you'd like your order to be processed.
