# credit_card_renewal_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the credit card renewal process. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the required information in the relevant fields.
2. Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all fields are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Card Number** (`Card Number`, text, required/optional): Enter the 16-digit credit card number.
* **Expiration Date** (`Expiration Date`, date, required/optional): Enter the expiration date of your credit card in the format `MM/YY`.
* **Expiration Month** (`Expiration Month`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the expiration month of your credit card.
* **Expiration Year** (`Expiration Year`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the expiration year of your credit card.
* **CVV** (`CVV`, text, required/optional): Enter the Card Verification Value (CVV) as a 3-digit number on the back of your credit card.
* **Zip Code** (`Zip Code`, text, required/optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required/optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, required/optional): Enter your address.
* **City** (`City`, text, required/optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (`State`, text, required/optional): Enter your state.
* **Country** (`Country`, text, required/optional): Enter your country.
* **CVV2** (`CVV2`, text, required/optional): Enter a value, this field will not be used in our application.
* **CVV3** (`CVV3`, text, required/optional): Enter a value, this field will not be used in our application.
* **CVV4** (`CVV4`, text, required/optional): Enter a value, this field will not be used in our application.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submission.
* Be cautious when entering sensitive information like CVV.
* Do not enter fake or test data.
* If you encounter any issues during the submission, please contact our support team.
