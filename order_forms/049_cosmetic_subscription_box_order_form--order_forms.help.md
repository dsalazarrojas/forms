# Cosmetic Subscription Box Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect order information from customers for a cosmetic subscription box service.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the field-by-field explanation.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (Customer Name, Text, Optional): Enter the customer's name and any other relevant contact details.
* **Select Subscription Plan** (Select from Monthly, Yearly, Optional): Choose between Monthly and Yearly subscription plans.
* **Select Products** (Select Multiple from Product 1, Product 2, Product 3, Optional): Pick the products they would like to include in their subscription box.
* **Shipping Details** (Shipping Address, Text, Optional): Enter the shipping address where the box should be sent.
* **Contact Information** (Additional Contact Information, Text, Optional): Provide additional contact information if necessary.
* **Payment Information** (Payment Details, Text, Optional): Enter payment details if applicable.
* **Order Details** (Additional Order Details, Text, Optional): Add any additional order details or instructions.
* **Review** (Feedback, Note, Optional): Enter any feedback or comments about the order.
* **Order Notes** (Additional Notes, Note, Optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the order.
* **Submit** (Submit Form, Text, Optional): Click to submit the form.
