# Above 1.0 Meter Floating Timber Shelf - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers about their custom floating timber shelf orders. It gathers essential details about the customer, shelf specifications, and installation requirements to ensure accurate production and delivery of the shelves.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your full name and email address to identify yourself as the customer.
2.  Provide your phone number for us to contact you in case of any issues.
3.  Enter your delivery address for accurate shipping and handling.
4.  Specify the shelf length, depth, and thickness according to your requirements.
5.  Choose the type of timber and finish you prefer for your shelf.
6.  Select the edge profile you like for your shelf.
7.  Indicate the number of shelves you want to order.
8.  Choose the type of wall to be installed on and whether you require professional installation.
9.  If required, select a preferred delivery date for your order.
10. Finally, provide any special instructions or custom requests you have for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Full name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
*  **Email address** (`customer_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about your order.
*  **Phone number** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for us to reach out in case of any issues.
*  **Delivery address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, required): Enter your delivery address for accurate shipping and handling.
*  **Shelf length** (`shelf_length`, `number`, required): Enter the length of the shelf you want in meters, making sure it's above 1.0 meter.
*  **Shelf depth** (`shelf_depth`, `number`, required): Enter the depth of the shelf in centimeters.
*  **Shelf thickness** (`shelf_thickness`, `select_one`, required): Choose the thickness of your shelf from 25mm, 30mm, 40mm, or 50mm.
*  **Timber type** (`timber_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of timber you prefer (Oak, Walnut, Pine, Maple, Recycled timber, or Custom).
*  **Finish type** (`finish_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the finish type for your shelf (Natural oil, Matte lacquer, Gloss lacquer, Raw or unfinished, or Stained).
*  **Edge profile** (`edge_profile`, `select_one`, required): Select the edge profile you like (Square edge, Rounded edge, Beveled edge, or Live edge).
*  **Number of shelves** (`quantity`, `number`, required): Indicate how many shelves you want to order.
*  **Wall type** (`wall_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of wall where the shelves will be installed (Brick, Plasterboard or drywall, Concrete, Timber stud, or Not sure).
*  **Do you require professional installation** (`installation_required`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you require professional installation for your shelves (Yes or No - self install).
*  **Preferred delivery date** (`preferred_delivery_date`, `date`, optional): Enter a preferred delivery date for your order if applicable.
*  **Special instructions or custom requests** (`special_instructions`, `text`, optional): Add any special instructions or custom requests you have for your order.
