# Wedding Invitation Printing Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a quote for wedding invitation printing services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the style of the wedding invitation by choosing one of the available options.
2. Enter the quantity of invitations you need.
3. Choose the paper type for the invitations.
4. Select the finish for the invitations (Matte, Glossy, or Foiled).
5. Choose the printing technology used to print the invitations (Inkjet, Digital, or Letterpress).
6. Select the ink color for the invitations (Monochrome, 2-3 Color, or 4-6 Color).
7. Enter the quantity of invitations in the unit price field.
8. Choose the unit of measurement for the invitations (Pieces, Sheets, or Rolls).
9. Enter the unit price for the invitations.
10. Select any additional services you would like (Lamination, Filing, or Foil Stamping).
11. Enter any comments or special instructions for the print job.
12. Enter the date and time you would like to have the invitations delivered.
13. Enter your phone number and email.
14. Enter your name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Invitation Style**: Choose the style of the wedding invitation. Options include:
  * Invitation Style 1
  * Invitation Style 2
  * Invitation Style 3

* **Quantity**: Enter the number of invitations you need.

* **Quantity per Unit**: Enter the number of invitations per unit of measurement.

* **Paper Type**: Choose the type of paper to use for the invitations. Options include:
  * 8x11
  * 8x13
  * 8x14

* **Finish**: Choose the finish for the invitations. Options include:
  * Matte
  * Glossy
  * Foiled

* **Printing Technology**: Choose the printing technology to use for the invitations. Options include:
  * Inkjet
  * Digital
  * Letterpress

* **Ink Color**: Choose the ink color for the invitations. Options include:
  * Monochrome
  * 2-3 Color
  * 4-6 Color

* **Quantity of Invitations**: Enter the total number of invitations you need.

* **Unit of Measurement**: Choose the unit of measurement for the invitations. Options include:
  * Pieces
  * Sheets
  * Rolls

* **Unit Price**: Enter the price per unit of measurement for the invitations.

* **Additional Services**: Choose any additional services you would like to have included with the invitations. Options include:
  * Lamination
  * Filing
  * Foil Stamping

* **Comments**: Enter any comments or special instructions for the print job.

* **Date**: Enter the date you would like to have the invitations delivered.

* **Time**: Enter the time you would like to have the invitations delivered.

* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.

* **Email**: Enter your email address.

* **Name**: Enter your name.

Note: This form is for wedding invitation printing quote requests only. Please do not use this form for other purposes.
