# Inventory Rotation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to track the inventory rotation process, including reviewing and updating product usage, inventory levels, and other relevant details. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a product from the options provided in the "Select a product" field.
2. Enter the start date of the product's usage in the "Usage start date" field.
3. Enter the end date of the product's usage in the "Usage end date" field.
4. Indicate whether the product is on rotation or not by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Rotation interval" field.
5. Update the rotation status of the product by selecting "Active" or "Inactive" in the "Rotation status" field.
6. Add any additional notes or comments about the product's usage or inventory levels in the "Notes" field.
7. Enter the contact email for the responsible person or team in the "Contact email" field.
8. Enter the contact phone number for the responsible person or team in the "Contact phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select a product** (`product_selection`, select_one, required/optional): This field allows you to choose the product that needs to be reviewed.
* **Usage start date** (`usage_start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the product started being used.
* **Usage end date** (`usage_end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the product stopped being used.
* **Rotation interval** (`rotation_interval`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" if the product is on a rotation schedule, "No" otherwise.
* **Rotation status** (`rotation_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Active" if the product is currently being used, "Inactive" otherwise.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any comments or notes about the product's usage or inventory levels.
* **Contact email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the contact email for the responsible person or team.
* **Contact phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact phone number for the responsible person or team.
