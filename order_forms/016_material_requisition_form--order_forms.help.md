# material_requisition_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The material requisition form is designed to collect brief descriptions or details about materials or supplies that are required.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each page with your information in the provided text fields.
2. Make sure to provide clear and concise descriptions to ensure your request is processed efficiently.
3. Review your filled-out form before submitting to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **material_requisition_form_page_1_label** (`material_requisition_form_page_1`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the material or supply you are requesting.
- **material_requisition_form_page_2_label** (`material_requisition_form_page_2`, text, required/optional): Provide additional details or specifications about the material or supply.
- **material_requisition_form_page_3_label** (`material_requisition_form_page_3`, text, required/optional): Add further information or context about your material or supply request.
- **material_requisition_form_page_4_label** (`material_requisition_form_page_4`, text, required/optional): Enter any relevant details or specifications about the material or supply.
- **material_requisition_form_page_5_label** (`material_requisition_form_page_5`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional context or information about your material or supply request.
- **material_requisition_form_page_6_label** (`material_requisition_form_page_6`, text, required/optional): Enter further details or specifications about the material or supply.
- **material_requisition_form_page_7_label** (`material_requisition_form_page_7`, text, required/optional): Add any remaining information or context about your material or supply request.
- **material_requisition_form_page_8_label** (`material_requisition_form_page_8`, text, required/optional): Enter any final details or specifications about the material or supply.
- **material_requisition_form_page_9_label** (`material_requisition_form_page_9`, text, required/optional): Review and verify your filled-out form before submission.
- **material_requisition_form_page_10_label** (`material_requisition_form_page_10`, text, required/optional): This is the last page; ensure all details are accurate before submitting the form.

## Tips
* Be concise and clear in your descriptions to ensure efficient processing.
* Review your filled-out form carefully before submission.
* Ensure you are not duplicating an existing requisition.
* If you need further assistance, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
