# wrestling_event_banner_selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The wrestling_event_banner_selection form is used to collect and manage the details of a wrestling event banner, including event title, date, location, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Event Title** field with the title of the event.
2. Select the **Event Date** of the event.
3. Enter the **Event Location** where the event will take place.
4. If applicable, provide the **Sponsor Name** and **Sponsor Artwork**.
5. Choose the **Event Banner Size** from the available options (Small, Medium, Large).
6. Select the **Banner Orientation** (Horizontal or Vertical).
7. Choose the positions where the banner will be displayed (Front, Back, Side).
8. Determine if **Sponsor Approval** is Required, Conditional, or Not Required.
9. Choose the **Banner Artwork** (if applicable).
10. Select if **Artwork Approval** is Required, Conditional, or Not Required.
11. Provide any **Additional Comments** about the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Title**: Enter the title of the event.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Sponsor Name**: Enter the name of the sponsor if applicable.
* **Sponsor Artwork**: If applicable, provide the sponsor's artwork.
* **Event Banner Size**: Choose the size of the event banner (Small, Medium, Large).
* **Banner Orientation**: Choose the orientation of the banner (Horizontal or Vertical).
* **Banner Position**: Choose the position where the banner will be displayed (Front, Back, Side).
* **Sponsor Approval**: Determine if sponsor approval is Required, Conditional, or Not Required.
* **Banner Artwork**: If applicable, provide the banner artwork.
* **Artwork Approval**: Determine if artwork approval is Required, Conditional, or Not Required.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments about the event.
* **Banner Approval Date**: Enter the date when the banner will be approved.
* **Banner Approval Time**: Enter the time when the banner will be approved.
* **Artwork Approval Date**: Enter the date when the artwork will be approved.
* **Artwork Approval Time**: Enter the time when the artwork will be approved.
* **Banner Delivery Date**: Enter the date when the banner will be delivered.
* **Banner Delivery Time**: Enter the time when the banner will be delivered.
* **Artwork Delivery Date**: Enter the date when the artwork will be delivered.
* **Artwork Delivery Time**: Enter the time when the artwork will be delivered.
* **Manager Approval**: Determine if manager approval is Required, Conditional, or Not Required.
* **Approval Process**: Choose the approval process (Email, Phone, Online Submission).
* **Event Manager**: Choose if the event manager is Yes or No.
* **Manager Approval**: Determine if manager approval is Required, Conditional, or Not Required.
