# Astrophotography Licensing Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Astrophotography Licensing Agreement is a document that outlines the terms and conditions of licensing astrophotography images between a photographer and a client. This form ensures that both parties understand their obligations and rights regarding the use of the licensed images.


## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Choose the correct options for each select field carefully.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all fields are completed accurately and completely.


## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, required): This is the date when the licensing agreement was made.
* **Agreement ID Number** (`agreement_id`, required): This is a reference number for the licensing agreement.
* **Photographer Name** (`photographer_name`, required): This is the full name of the photographer licensing the images.
* **Photographer Email** (`photographer_email`, required): This is the contact email of the photographer.
* **Photographer Phone** (`photographer_phone`, required): This is the contact phone number of the photographer.
* **Photographer Company/Studio Name** (`photographer_company`, optional): This is the name of the business or studio the photographer represents.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, required): This is the full name of the client.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, required): This is the contact email of the client.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, required): This is the contact phone number of the client.
* **Client Company (if applicable)** (`client_company`, optional): This is the name of the client's organization.
* **Image Description** (`image_description`, required): This is a description of the astrophotography images being licensed.
* **Number of Images Licensed** (`image_quantity`, required): This is the number of images being licensed.
* **Type of Image Usage** (`usage_type`, required): Choose how the images will be used (e.g., Commercial, Editorial, Educational, etc.).
* **Duration of License** (`usage_duration`, required): Choose how long the license will last (e.g., One-time use, 1 year, 2 years, etc.).
* **Geographic Territory** (`usage_territory`, required): Choose where the licensed images can be used (e.g., Local, Regional, National, etc.).
* **Exclusive/Non-Exclusive** (`exclusive_non_exclusive`, required): Choose between an Exclusive or Non-Exclusive license.
* **Image Modification Allowed** (`modification_allowed`, required): Choose if the client is allowed to modify the images.
* **Photographer Credit Required** (`credit_required`, required): Choose if the photographer must be credited for their work.
* **Licensing Fee** (`licensing_fee`, required): This is the total licensing cost for the images.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, required): Choose how the payment will be made (e.g., Credit Card, Bank Transfer, etc.).
* **Payment Due Date** (`payment_due_date`, required): This is the date when the payment is due.
* **Resale Rights** (`resale_rights`, required): Choose if the client can resell the images.
* **How Images Will Be Distributed** (`distribution_method`, required): Choose how the images will be delivered to the client (e.g., Digital Download, CD/USB, etc.).
* **I Agree to the Licensing Terms** (`terms_acceptance`, required): Confirm that the client agrees to the licensing terms.
* **Agreement Signature Date** (`agreement_signature_date`, required): This is the date when both parties agree to the terms.

Please review the form carefully and fill out all fields accurately and completely.
