# Custom Coupon Booklet Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Coupon Booklet Order Form is a tool used to collect information from customers who are interested in ordering a custom coupon booklet. Please fill out this form to provide your details and create a personalized coupon booklet.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name.
2. Fill in your last name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)** (`first_name`, required/optional): Please enter your first name, as this will be used to personalize your coupon booklet.
* **Last Name (2)** (`last_name`, required/optional): Please enter your last name, as this will be used to personalize your coupon booklet.
* **Email (3)** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address to receive any order confirmations or updates about your custom coupon booklet.
* **Phone Number (4)** (`phone`, required/optional): Enter your phone number to be contacted in case of any issues with your order or for further communication.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information, as this will be used to create your personalized coupon booklet.
* If you have any issues or questions, feel free to contact us for assistance.
