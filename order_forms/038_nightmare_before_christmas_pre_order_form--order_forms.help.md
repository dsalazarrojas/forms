# Nightmare Before Christmas Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nightmare Before Christmas Pre Order Form is a form used to collect information from customers to complete a pre-order for a specific product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) symbol.
2. Select the correct product from the "Select product" field.
3. Confirm the email address and date of birth in the respective confirmation fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer name** (`customer_name`, text, \*): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **E-mail** (`email`, email, \*): Enter your email address that you would like to use for communication.
- **Shipping address** (`shipping_address`, text, \*): Enter your complete shipping address.
- **Shipping postal code** (`shipping_postal_code`, text, \*): Enter your postal code.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number.
- **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, \*): Enter your date of birth.
- **Select product** (`select_product`, select multiple,): Select the product you would like to pre-order.

  - Option 2: [Insert product description]
  - Option 3: [Insert product description]
- **Submit** (`submit`, select one, \*): Choose how you would like to receive your pre-ordered product.
  - optionA: [Insert delivery option]
  - optionB: [Insert delivery option]
  - optionC: [Insert delivery option]
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, ): Enter any additional comments or special instructions for your pre-order.
- **E-mail confirmation** (`email_confirmation`, email, \*): Confirm your email address for order updates.
- **Confirm date of birth** (`confirm_date_of_birth`, date, \*): Confirm your date of birth.

Note: Please double-check your email address and date of birth before submitting the form. This will help prevent errors and ensure that your order is processed correctly.
