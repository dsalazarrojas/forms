# Freelance Service Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Freelance Service Quote Form is designed to help clients quickly and easily provide their details and preferences to your freelance services. This form is intended to help gather essential information to create an accurate quote for your services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. **Client Details** (`client_details`): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant information about yourself.
2. **Service Details** (`service_details`): Describe the services you're interested in, including any specific requirements or needs you have.
3. **Quote Details** (`quote_details`): Provide any additional details about the quote you'd like to receive, such as budget constraints or timeframes.
4. **Client Preferences** (`client_preferences`): Share any personal preferences or requirements you have for the quote, such as communication channels or deadlines.
5. **Quote Submission** (`quote_submission`): Enter any relevant details about your quote submission, including the expected format or content.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Details**: Enter your contact information to allow us to get in touch with you about your quote.
- **Service Details**: Provide a clear description of the services you're interested in, including any specific requirements or needs you have.
- **Quote Details**: Enter any additional details about the quote you'd like to receive, such as budget constraints or timeframes.
- **Client Preferences**: Share any personal preferences or requirements you have for the quote, such as communication channels or deadlines.
- **Quote Submission**: Enter any details about your quote submission, including the expected format or content.

## Tips
