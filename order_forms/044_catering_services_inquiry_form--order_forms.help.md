# Catering Services Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Catering Services Inquiry Form is designed to collect information from customers who are interested in our catering services. This form is meant to be used for internal business purposes and to provide personalized service and follow-up communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your information. Please make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure that we can better serve your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`inquirer_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where we can contact you with more information.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number where we can reach you.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): If you are representing a company, please enter your company name.
* **Inquiry Date** (`inquiry_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your inquiry.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required): Please select the type of event you are planning (e.g. Wedding, Corporate Event, Birthday Party, etc.).
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): If you have a specific start time for your event, please enter it here.
* **Estimated Guest Count** (`guest_count`, number, required): Enter the estimated number of guests for your event.
* **Venue Name** (`venue_name`, text, optional): If you have a specific venue in mind, please enter its name.
* **Venue Address** (`venue_address`, text, optional): If you have a specific address for the venue, please enter it here.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, optional): Please select your estimated budget for the event.
* **Services Interested In** (`service_interest`, select_multiple, required): Please select the services you are interested in (e.g. Full Service Catering, Drop-off Catering, etc.).
* **Cuisine Preferences** (`cuisine_preferences`, text, optional): If you have any specific cuisine preferences, please enter them here.
* **Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, text, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions or requirements, please enter them here.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_heard`, select_one, optional): Please select how you heard about us (e.g. Website, Social Media, etc.).
* **Decision Timeline** (`timeline`, select_one, optional): Please select when you would like to make a decision on our services.
* **Considering Other Caterers** (`other_caterers`, select_one, optional): Please select if you are considering other catering services.
* **Interested in Tasting** (`tasting_requested`, select_one, optional): Please select if you would like to schedule a tasting.
* **Your Questions** (`questions`, text, optional): If you have any questions or concerns, please enter them here.
* **Best Time to Contact** (`best_time_to_contact`, select_one, optional): Please select the best time to contact you.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any other information or requests, please enter them here.
