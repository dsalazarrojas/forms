# Custom Invoicing System Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request modifications or additions to the custom invoicing system. It ensures that the system is accurately updated with the requested modifications or additions, reducing the risk of errors or missed changes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name and contact number to identify yourself and your business.
2. Provide your email address for communication purposes.
3. Enter your business name as it appears on your invoices.
4. Describe the modification or addition you are requesting in detail.
5. If the modification or addition is related to an existing system, provide its system ID.
6. If you have any questions or need further clarification, enter your internal system name.
7. Specify the type of modification or addition you are requesting (addition or modification).
8. Select the status of the system after the modification or addition (active, inactive, or pending).
9. Enter the name of the person the modification or addition is being assigned to.
10. Enter the name of the person creating this request.
11. Date and time of when this request was created.
12. Enter the name of the person making the last change to this request.
13. Date and time of the last update to this request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User**: This field is for your name and contact number. Please enter your name as it appears on your invoices and your contact number.
- **Contact Number**: This field is for your contact number. This is where your company will reach out to you for any further discussion or questions.
- **Email**: This field is for your email address. This will be used for communication purposes only.
- **Business Name**: Enter your business name as it appears on your invoices. This is important for accurate identification.
- **Description**: Describe the modification or addition you are requesting in detail. Be as clear and concise as possible.
- **System ID**: If the modification or addition is related to an existing system, please enter its system ID. This will help us identify which system you are referring to.
- **System Name**: If you have any questions or need further clarification, enter your internal system name. This is not a required field.
- **Modification Type**: Specify the type of modification or addition you are requesting (addition or modification).
- **System Status**: Select the status of the system after the modification or addition (active, inactive, or pending).
- **Assigned To**: Enter the name of the person the modification or addition is being assigned to.
- **Created By**: Enter the name of the person creating this request.
- **Created At**: This field is for the date and time of when this request was created.
- **Modified By**: Enter the name of the person making the last change to this request.
- **Modified At**: This field is for the date and time of the last update to this request.
