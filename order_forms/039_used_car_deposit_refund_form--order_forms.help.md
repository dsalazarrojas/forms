# used_car_deposit_refund_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to process the refund of a customer's deposit for a used car they were planning to purchase, but for some reason, they cannot complete the transaction.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required and optional fields.
2. Provide accurate and clear information in each field.
3. Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **used_car_deposit_refund_form_input** (`used_car_deposit_refund_form_input`, text, optional): A brief description of the reason for the refund request.
  • This field is not required, but it's helpful for us to understand the context of the refund request.
- **customer_name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): The customer's name as it appears on their account or the name they used when making the original deposit.
  • This field is not required, but it helps us identify the customer accurately.
- **deposit_amount** (`deposit_amount`, number, optional): The amount of deposit the customer wants to be refunded.
  • This field is not required, but it's essential to ensure accurate refund processing.
- **vehicle_make** (`vehicle_make`, select one, optional): The make of the used car the customer was planning to purchase.
  • This option will help us understand which vehicle was involved in the transaction.
- **vehicle_model** (`vehicle_model`, text, optional): The model of the used car the customer was planning to purchase.
  • This field is optional but helps us understand which specific vehicle was involved.
- **vehicle_year** (`vehicle_year`, number, optional): The year of the used car the customer was planning to purchase.
  • This field is optional but helps us understand which year's model was involved.
- **reason_for_refund** (`reason_for_refund`, note, optional): A detailed explanation of the reason for the refund request.
  • This field is optional but provides valuable context for the refund process.
- **customer_email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): The customer's email address associated with their account or the one they used for the original deposit.
  • This field is not required but helps us to contact the customer if needed.

## Tips
- Double-check the accuracy of the provided information.
- Make sure to submit the form with all the required fields.
- If you're unsure about any part of the form, contact us for assistance.
