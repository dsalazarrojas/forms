# shipping_fee_refund_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The shipping fee refund form is a tool used to process a refund for shipping fees associated with an order. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **shipping fee details** in the first field to provide context for the refund request.
2. Type in the **order number** to identify the specific order the refund is related to.
3. Specify the **reason for refund** to explain why the refund is necessary.
4. Enter the **refund amount** to calculate the total refund due.
5. Leave a **customer message** if you'd like to provide any additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shipping fee details**: Provide a brief description of the shipping fee being refunded. This is a text field for you to enter any details about the shipping fee.
* **Order number**: Enter the unique identifier assigned to your order. This helps us quickly locate and process your refund request.
* **Reason for refund**: Please explain the reason why you are requesting a refund for the shipping fee. This helps us understand the circumstances and process the refund accordingly.
* **Refund amount**: Enter the amount of the refund you are seeking. Make sure to calculate the total refund amount accurately.
* **Customer message**: If you have any additional comments or requests regarding the refund, you can type them here.

## Tips
- Please make sure to enter accurate and correct information to avoid any delays in processing your refund. 
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to our support team.
