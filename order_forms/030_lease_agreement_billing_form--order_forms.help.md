# Lease Agreement Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from tenants regarding their lease agreement, including their personal details, lease terms, and payment information. The form is intended to be used by property managers or landlords to gather essential data for billing and administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details in the "tenant_info" section, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. Provide an overview of the lease agreement in the "lease_details" section, including the lease start and end dates, rent amount, and any other relevant terms.
3. Enter payment information in the "payment_info" section, including the payment method and frequency.
4. Review and acknowledge the "agreement_terms" section, which outlines the terms and conditions of the lease agreement.
5. Finally, sign and date the "tenant_signature" section to confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **tenant_info** (text, required: false): This section is where you will enter your personal details, such as your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **lease_details** (text, required: false): In this section, please provide an overview of the lease agreement, including the lease start and end dates, rent amount, and any other relevant terms.
* **payment_info** (text, required: false): Enter your payment information, including the payment method and frequency.
* **agreement_terms** (text, required: false): Review and acknowledge the terms and conditions of the lease agreement.
* **tenant_signature** (text, required: false): Sign and date this section to confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information in the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all required information is completed.
* Contact the property manager or landlord if you have any questions or concerns.
