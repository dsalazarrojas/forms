# custom_panel_table_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The custom_panel_table_order_form is designed to collect information for custom panel table orders. It helps customers provide details about their order, including size, material, payment options, and shipping information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button at the end of the form to send in your order.
2. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
3. Select the options for table size, table material, and payment details as per your preference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **User Input**
 (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter any additional information or notes about your order.
### 2. **Table Size**
 (`table_size`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the size of the table you want to order.
### 3. **Table Material**
 (`table_material`, select_one, required): Select the material for your table.
### 4. **Number of Legs**
 (`num_legs`, number, optional): Enter the number of legs for your table (if applicable).
### 5. **Height**
 (`height`, number, optional): Enter the height of your table.
### 6. **Width**
 (`width`, number, optional): Enter the width of your table.
### 7. **Color**
 (`color`, text, optional): Enter the color of your table.
### 8. **Order Date**
 (`order_date`, date, optional): Enter the desired date for your order.
### 9. **Order Time**
 (`order_time`, time, optional): Enter the desired time for your order.
### 10. **Notes**
 (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your order.
### 11. **User Email**
 (`user_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
### 12. **User Phone**
 (`user_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
### 13. **Table Type**
 (`table_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of table you want to order.
### 14. **Payment Gateway**
 (`payment_gateway`, select_one, required): Select the payment method for your order.
### 15. **Payment Method**
 (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you prefer to pay (if applicable).
### 16. **Shipping Address**
 (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter your shipping address.
### 17. **Shipping City**
 (`shipping_city`, text, optional): Enter the city of your shipping address.
### 18. **Shipping State**
 (`shipping_state`, text, optional): Enter the state of your shipping address.
### 19. **Shipping Postal**
 (`shipping_postal`, text, optional): Enter your postal code.
### 20. **Payment Terms**
 (`payment_terms`, select_one, required): Select the payment terms for your order.
### 21. **Custom Note**
 (`custom_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your order.
### 22. **Customer Info**
 (`customer_info`, text, optional): Enter information about the customer.
### 23. **Order Number**
 (`order_number`, text, optional): Enter the order number (if applicable).
### 24. **Shipping Zip**
 (`shipping_zip`, text, optional): Enter the postal code of your shipping address.
### 25. **Order Date 2**
 (`order_date_2`, date, optional): Enter the desired date for your order.
### 26. **Customer Email**
 (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
### 27. **Customer Phone**
 (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
### 28. **Shipping Address 2**
 (`shipping_address2`, text, optional): Enter the shipping address.
### 29. **Customer Note**
 (`customer_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the customer.
### 30. **Payment Terms 2**
 (`payment_terms2`, select_one, required): Select the payment terms for your order.
### 31. **Shipping Terms**
 (`shipping_terms`, text, optional): Enter the shipping terms for your order.
### 32. **Order Status**
 (`order_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your order.
### 33. **Shipping Terms 2**
 (`shipping_terms2`, text, optional): Enter any additional shipping terms for your order.
