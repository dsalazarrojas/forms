# Baseball Uniform Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the production and shipment of baseball uniforms. Please fill out this form to provide us with the necessary details about your team and order requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the team information section with your team's name, manager's name, email, and phone number.
2. Select the uniform style, primary color, and secondary color for your team's uniform.
3. Specify the quantity of jerseys, pants, hats, and socks you need.
4. Choose whether or not to include player names and numbers on the jerseys.
5. Provide a breakdown of the jersey and pant sizes needed.
6. Select the logo placement and type.
7. Choose the belt and piping colors.
8. Enter the shipping address and payment method.
9. Specify the delivery deadline and preferred delivery method.
10. If you need a rush order, select this option.
11. Add any additional custom requests or special instructions.
12. Calculate the total estimate (not visible on the form, for internal use only).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name**: (`team_name`, text, required) Enter your team's name.
* **Team Manager Name**: (`team_manager_name`, text, required) Enter your team manager's name.
* **Team Manager Email**: (`manager_email`, email, required) Enter the email of your team manager for order confirmation.
* **Team Manager Phone**: (`manager_phone`, text, required) Enter your team manager's phone number.
* **Order Date**: (`order_date`, date, required) Select the current date.
* **Uniform Style**: (`uniform_style`, select_one, required) Choose from: Traditional button up, Modern pullover, Reversible practice, Custom design.
* **Primary Color**: (`primary_color`, select_one, required) Choose from: White, Black, Navy blue, Gray, Red, Royal blue, Forest green, Maroon, Orange, Yellow, Custom.
* **Secondary Color**: (`secondary_color`, select_one, required) Choose from: White, Black, Navy blue, Gray, Red, Royal blue, Forest green, Maroon, Orange, Yellow.
* **Jersey Quantity**: (`jersey_quantity`, number, required) Enter the total number of jerseys needed.
* **Pant Quantity**: (`pant_quantity`, number, required) Enter the total number of pants needed.
* **Hat Quantity**: (`hat_quantity`, number, required) Enter the total number of hats needed.
* **Sock Quantity**: (`sock_quantity`, number, required) Enter the total number of socks needed.
* **Player Names on Jerseys**: (`player_name_jerseys`, select_one, required) Choose from: Yes, all jerseys, Yes, some jerseys, No names.
* **Player Numbers on Jerseys**: (`player_number_jerseys`, select_one, required) Choose from: Yes, all jerseys, Yes, some jerseys, No numbers.
* **Jersey Size Breakdown**: (`jersey_sizes`, text, required) Enter the quantity per size needed.
* **Pant Size Breakdown**: (`pant_sizes`, text, required) Enter the quantity per size needed.
* **Hat Size Breakdown**: (`hat_sizes`, text, required) Enter the quantity per size needed.
* **Logo Placement**: (`logo_placement`, select_one, required) Choose from: Front left chest, Front center, Sleeve, Back, Multiple locations.
* **Logo Type**: (`logo_type`, select_one, required) Choose from: Embroidered, Screen printed, Heat transfer, Patch, None.
* **Belt Color**: (`belt_color`, select_one, required) Choose from: Black, Brown, Navy, Red, White, Gray.
* **Piping Color**: (`piping_color`, select_one, required) Choose from: None, White, Black, Navy, Red, Matching secondary color.
* **Shipping Address**: (`shipping_address`, text, required) Enter the complete shipping address.
* **Payment Method**: (`payment_method`, select_one, required) Choose from: Credit card, Debit card, Team check, Purchase order.
* **Delivery Deadline**: (`delivery_deadline`, date, required) Select the date by which you need the uniforms.
* **Delivery Method**: (`delivery_method`, select_one, required) Choose from: Ship to address, Pickup in store, Deliver to field.
* **Rush Order**: (`rush_order`, select_one, required) Choose from: Yes, rush order, No, standard timeline.
* **Special Instructions**: (`special_instructions`, text, false) Add any additional custom requests or special instructions.
* **Total Estimate**: (`total_estimate`, number, false) This field is not visible on the form, for internal use only.

Note: This help guide is intended to assist end-users in filling out the form correctly. It is not meant to be a comprehensive guide to the production and shipment process. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please contact the production team.
