# Toy Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Toy Order Form is a tool for customers to submit their toy orders with details such as soft toys, books, board games, payment information, and customer contact details. This form is designed to gather the necessary information for our team to process and fulfill the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Soft Toy Input field to specify the type of soft toy you would like to order.
2. Enter the required information in the Book Input field to provide more context.
3. Provide details about the Board Game you wish to purchase.
4. Enter your payment information in the Payment Details field, taking note that this field is a text area for additional notes.
5. Select your preferred Customer Details option (if provided).
6. Enter your Customer Email address in the Customer Email field.
7. Fill in your Customer Phone number in the Customer Phone field.
8. Review your order details and click the Submit button to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Soft Toy Input** (`soft_toy_input`, text, required: false): Enter the type of soft toy you would like to order.
* **Book Input** (`book_input`, text, required: false): Provide more information about the book you would like to purchase.
* **Board Game Input** (`board_game_input`, text, required: false): Enter details about the board game you wish to buy.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, note, required: false): Please enter your payment details or any additional notes about your order.
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, select_one, required: false): If provided, select your preferred customer details option.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): Enter your customer email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required: false): Fill in your customer phone number.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): Click this button to complete and submit your order.
