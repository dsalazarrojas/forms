# Industrial Cleaning Chemical Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to order industrial cleaning chemicals from the company. It is intended to gather information about the customer's needs and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Determine if you are a supplier by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Supplier" field.
2. Select the products you wish to order in the "Product Selections" field.
3. Enter the quantity of each product you wish to order in the "Quantity" field.
4. Enter the unit price of each product in the "Unit Price" field.
5. Calculate the total price of your order in the "Total Price" field.
6. Check the "Delivery Details" field to indicate if you wish to receive delivery details from us.
7. Acknowledge the "Terms and Conditions" by typing your agreement in the text box.
8. Enter your order number in the "Order Number" field (if you have one).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier (id: 1)** (`select_one`: yes/no): This field determines if you are a supplier or not. Select "Yes" if you are a supplier, and "No" otherwise.
* **Product Selections (id: 2)** (`select_multiple`: yes/no, multiple selection): Select the products you wish to order from the available options.
* **Quantity (id: 3)** (`number`): Enter the quantity of each product you wish to order.
* **Unit Price (id: 4)** (`number`): Enter the unit price of each product.
* **Total Price (id: 5)** (`number`): Calculate the total price of your order by multiplying the quantity and unit price of each product.
* **Delivery Details (id: 6)** (`select_multiple`: yes/no, multiple selection): Check this field to indicate if you wish to receive delivery details from us.
* **Terms and Conditions (id: 7)** (`text`): Read and acknowledge our terms and conditions by typing your agreement in this field.
* **Order Number (id: 8)** (`text`): Enter your order number if you have one.

**Tips**

* Make sure to select all the necessary products and quantities to ensure accurate calculation of the total price.
* If you are a supplier, ensure you have selected "Yes" in the "Supplier" field.
* Double-check your calculations and selections before submitting the form.
