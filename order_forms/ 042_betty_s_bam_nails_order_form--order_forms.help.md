# Betty’s BAM Nails Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for nail orders. It will help us better understand your preferences, requirements, and any special requests you may have for your nail services. Please complete this form to ensure we have accurate and complete information to serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form to ensure you have all the necessary fields filled out.
2. Enter your full name and email address, as these are required fields.
3. If you have a phone number, you can optionally provide it for easier contact.
4. Choose the relevant date for your order.
5. Select the primary product or service that best applies to your request.
6. If applicable, provide the quantity or rating for your selected product.
7. Add any additional details that might be helpful for us to know.
8. Confirm your agreement with our terms and conditions, select all that apply.
9. If you'd like, provide an estimated value for your order.
10. Enter your preferred time for scheduling (if applicable).
11. Finally, add any final comments or notes you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your legal full name.
* **Email address** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for contact.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text, optional): Include your country code if outside local region.
* **Date** (`reference_date`, date, required): Select the date relevant to your order.
* **Primary selection** (`primary_selection`, select one, required): Choose the option that best applies to your request.
* **Quantity or rating** (`quantity_or_rating`, number, optional): Provide a whole number for quantity or rating.
* **Additional details** (`additional_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional relevant information.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select multiple, required): Select all that apply regarding our terms and conditions.
* **Estimated value** (`estimated_value`, number, optional): Use decimal format when applicable.
* **Preferred time** (`preferred_time`, time, optional): Enter a time if scheduling is required.
* **Final comments or notes** (`final_comments`, text, optional): Add any final comments or notes you may have.

Note: This guide provides a clear and concise explanation of each field, making it easier for users to understand the purpose and requirements of each section.
