# pet_treat_quotation_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The pet_treat_quotation_request_form is designed to collect contact and pet-related information from pet owners for the creation of a quotation request for pet treats.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Fill in the required fields (if any).
3. Select the type of pet from the options (Dog, Cat, or Other).
4. Enter the weight of your pet.
5. Enter the age of your pet.
6. Select the breed(s) of your pet (if applicable).
7. Enter your first name.
8. Enter your last name.
9. Enter your email address.
10. Enter your phone number.
11. Enter your city.
12. Click on the "Submit" button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **pet_treat_quotation_request_form**: This is the form title, please fill it in with a brief description of your pet treat quotation request.
* **pet_type**: Select the type of pet you have (Dog, Cat, or Other).
* **pet_weight**: Enter the weight of your pet.
* **pet_age**: Enter the age of your pet.
* **pet_breed**: Select the breed(s) of your pet (if applicable).
* **contact_first_name**: Enter your first name.
* **contact_last_name**: Enter your last name.
* **contact_email**: Enter your email address.
* **contact_phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **contact_city**: Enter your city.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure a complete quotation request.
* Select the correct pet type, weight, and breed for accurate quotation creation.
* Enter your contact information accurately to ensure easy communication.
