# Factory Access Control System Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Access Control System Order Form is used to request access to specific factory facilities. This form collects information from the user, such as their name, facility, and access level, to determine the appropriate access rights and schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the User Information section with your full name and other identifying details.
2. Select the Facility Information section and choose the specific facility you wish to access.
3. Choose your access level from the options provided:
	* Administrator: High-level access, typically only granted to authorized personnel.
	* Supervisor: Mid-level access, often granted to supervisors or managers.
	* Employee: Regular access, granted to employees who need to perform specific tasks.
	* Guest: Temporary access, granted for short-term visits or events.
4. Select a start date and end date for your access period.
5. Choose a schedule time for your access period.
6. Add any additional comments or notes in the Order Comments section.
7. Enter the Facility ID, if applicable.
8. Enter the Assigned Tool, if applicable.
9. Enter the User ID, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (1): Enter your full name, username, and any other identifying details as prompted.
* **Facility Information** (2): Choose the specific facility you wish to access. This will help us determine your access level and schedule.
* **Access Level** (3): Select the access level that best suits your needs. This will determine your privileges and schedule.
* **Start Date** (4): Enter the date you wish to begin your access period. If you are unsure, select a date as soon as possible.
* **End Date** (5): Enter the date you wish to end your access period. If you are unsure, select a date as soon as possible.
* **Schedule Time** (6): Choose a specific time for your access period. This will help us determine your schedule and availability.
* **Order Comments** (7): Add any additional comments or notes about your access request. This is a good place to provide context or clarify your needs.
* **Facility ID** (8): If you are accessing a specific facility, enter its corresponding ID. This will help us verify your request.
* **Assigned Tool** (9): If you are accessing a specific tool or equipment, enter its ID. This will help us verify your request.
* **User ID** (10): Enter your unique user ID, if applicable. This will help us track your access and activities.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure your access request is processed efficiently.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your supervisor or facility manager.
* If you need to cancel or modify your access request, please contact us with your order number.
