# Return Shipping Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Return Shipping Claim Form is a tool for customers to provide detailed information about their return shipping claim. This form helps us to process the return efficiently and ensure that we can resolve the issue as quickly as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, please follow these steps:
* Enter your name and contact information in the **Customer** field.
* Provide a detailed description of the product you are returning in the **Product Details** field.
* Select the reason for the return from the dropdown menu in the **Reason for Return** field.
* Enter the order number and date of the original purchase in the **Order Number** and **Order Date** fields, respectively.
* Choose the shipping method used for the original delivery in the **Shipping Method** field.
* If you have any supporting documents (e.g. photos, receipts), you can upload them in the **Supporting Documents** field.
* Check your signature status in the **Customer Signature** field. If you have signed the form, select **Signed**. If you haven't, select **Unsigned** or **Unknown**.
* Date and time of your signature in the **Customer Signature Date** field.
* If you have any additional comments or notes about your return, you can enter them in the **Customer Signature Note** field.
* Select the current status of the return shipping process in the **Return Shipping Status** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer**: This field is where you enter your name and contact information.
* **Product Details**: This field is where you describe the product you are returning.
* **Reason for Return**: This field allows you to select the reason for the return from the dropdown menu. Please choose the reason that best describes your situation.
* **Order Number**: This field is where you enter the order number of the original purchase.
* **Order Date**: This field is where you enter the date of the original purchase.
* **Shipping Method**: This field is where you choose the shipping method used for the original delivery.
* **Supporting Documents**: This field is where you can upload any supporting documents (e.g. photos, receipts) related to your return.
* **Customer Signature**: This field is where you check your signature status.
* **Customer Signature Date**: This field is where you enter the date and time of your signature.
* **Customer Signature Note**: This field is where you can enter any additional comments or notes about your return.
* **Return Shipping Status**: This field is where you select the current status of the return shipping process. Please choose the status that best describes the current state of your return.
