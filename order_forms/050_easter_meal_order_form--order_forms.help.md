# easter_meal_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The easter_meal_order_form is a user-facing form for customers to place an Easter meal order. It gathers contact information, meal preferences, special requests, Easter basket selections, and order notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
2. Select your preferred meal from the "Easter Meal Order" dropdown menu.
3. If you have any special requests, enter them in the "Special Requests" field.
4. Choose your desired Easter basket from the "Easter Baskets" dropdown menu (if applicable).
5. If necessary, add any additional order notes in the "Order Notes" field.
6. Choose a delivery date and time in the "Delivery Date" and "Delivery Time" fields, respectively.
7. Finally, provide any special instructions in the "Special Instructions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information, including name, email address, and phone number.
- **Easter Meal Order**: Select your preferred meal from the dropdown menu. If you don't see your preferred meal, please contact our support team.
- **Special Requests**: If you have any special requests or dietary restrictions, enter them here. Please be as detailed as possible to ensure we meet your needs.
- **Easter Baskets**: Select one or more Easter baskets that you would like to order. You can choose from multiple baskets, and we'll make sure to deliver them to you accordingly.
- **Order Notes**: If you need to add any additional notes about your order, enter them here. This can include any specific preferences or requests that we haven't covered in the form.
- **Delivery Date**: Choose the date you would like to receive your order.
- **Delivery Time**: Choose the time you would like to receive your order.
- **Special Instructions**: If you have any additional information or requests that you haven't mentioned earlier, enter them here.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely to avoid any issues with your order submission.
- If you are unsure about any field or have any questions, please contact our support team for assistance.
