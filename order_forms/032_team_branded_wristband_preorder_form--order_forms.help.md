# team_branded_wristband_preorder_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for pre-ordering team-branded wristbands. It is intended to gather all necessary details for the order, including personal and team information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select the quantity of wristbands you want to order in the "Quantity" field.
6. Choose the size of the wristband from the "Size" dropdown menu.
7. Select the color of the wristband from the "Color" dropdown menu.
8. Enter the date of the event or occasion for which you are ordering the wristbands in the "Date" field.
9. Enter the time of the event or occasion for which you are ordering the wristbands in the "Time" field.
10. Add any additional notes or instructions in the "Note" field.
11. Enter the name of the team for which you are ordering the wristbands in the "Team Name" field.
12. Enter the date of the event or occasion for which the team is ordering the wristbands in the "Event Date" field.
13. Enter the time of the event or occasion for which the team is ordering the wristbands in the "Event Time" field.
14. Enter the location of the event or occasion for which the team is ordering the wristbands in the "Event Location" field.
15. Add the team logo image in the "Team Logo" field.
16. Add the team description in the "Team Description" field.
17. Add the team URL in the "Team URL" field.
18. Add the team Facebook URL in the "Team Facebook" field.
19. Add the team Twitter URL in the "Team Twitter" field.
20. Add the team Instagram URL in the "Team Instagram" field.
21. Add the team email address in the "Team Email" field.
22. Add the team phone number in the "Team Phone" field.
23. Add the team address in the "Team Address" field.
24. Click the "Submit" button to submit your order.
