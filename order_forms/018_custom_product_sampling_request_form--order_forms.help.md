# custom_product_sampling_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "custom_product_sampling_request_form" is designed for customers or potential customers to request samples of custom-made products from your business. This form helps us understand your needs and provides a way for us to efficiently process and respond to your requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as requested, and make sure to provide the necessary information for us to best assist you. You will need to:

* Provide your company name
* Share your contact number
* Enter your contact email
* Indicate if you are requesting a sample or not
* Specify the product(s) you are interested in sampling, if applicable
* Add any additional notes about your request

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Sample Request** (`1`, `text`, required: false): 
Enter the name of the product you are interested in sampling.
* **Company Name** (`2`, `text`, required: false): 
Provide the name of your company.
* **Contact Number** (`3`, `text`, required: false): 
Enter your business contact number for us to get in touch with you.
* **Sample Requested** (`4`, `select_one`, required: false): 
Check 'Yes' if you are requesting a sample, and 'No' if you are not.
* **Product Sample Requested** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
Select 'Yes' for each product you are interested in sampling.
* **Contact Email** (`6`, `email`, required: false): 
Enter your contact email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Notes** (`7`, `note`, required: false): 
Provide any additional information or comments about your request that will help us better understand your needs.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are completed for us to efficiently process your request.
- You may select multiple products for sampling by checking the boxes under "Product Sample Requested".
- For any issues or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us using the provided contact email address.
