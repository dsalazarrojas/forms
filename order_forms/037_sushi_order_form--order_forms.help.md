# Sushi Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sushi Order Form is designed for customers to provide their information and preferences for their sushi orders. This form helps us gather essential details to ensure that your order is processed accurately and delivered on time.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select the desired pickup date for your order.
2.  Fill in your name as it appears on your identification.
3.  Enter your contact phone number to enable us to contact you in case of any updates or changes.
4.  Provide your email address (optional), which will help us keep you updated about your order.
5.  Choose the desired pickup time for your order.
6.  Select your preferred sushi type from the options provided.
7.  Enter the quantity of sushi you wish to order (optional).
8.  If you have any extra instructions or comments, type them in the designated field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Pickup Date (1)** (date, required): This field is used to select the date when you would like to pick up your sushi order. Please choose a date that suits you best.
*   **Customer Name (2)** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Contact Phone (3)** (text, required): Enter your contact phone number for any updates or changes.
*   **Email (4)** (email, optional): This field is used to keep you updated about your order. If you wish to receive updates, please provide your email address.
*   **Pickup Time (5)** (time, required): Choose the time that suits you best for picking up your order.
*   **Sushi Type (6)** (select_one, optional): Select the type of sushi you prefer from the options provided.
*   **Quantity (7)** (text, optional): Enter the quantity of sushi you wish to order.
*   **Extra Instructions (8)** (note, optional): If you have any extra instructions or comments, please type them in this field.

## Tips
*   Please check that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
*   If you have any special requests or instructions, please make sure to include them in the extra instructions field.
*   If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please contact our customer service team.
*   Please ensure that the pickup date and time selected are realistic and doable for your pickup.
*   If you need to cancel or modify your order, please contact us as soon as possible to avoid any inconvenience.
