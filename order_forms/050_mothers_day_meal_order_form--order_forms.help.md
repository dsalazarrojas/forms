# Mothers Day Meal Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mothers Day Meal Order Form is designed to collect information for Mother's Day meal orders. This form helps gather details about the order, including delivery date and time, meal preferences, and contact information, to ensure that the order is fulfilled correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the delivery date and time.
2. Choose the type of meal you would like to order (Breakfast or Other).
3. Specify the number of guests attending.
4. Enter your contact details, including email and phone number.
5. Provide any additional instructions or special requests.
6. Review your order carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **delivery_date** (`delivery_date`, date, required: false): Select the date you would like to have the meal delivered on.
* **delivery_time** (`delivery_time`, time, required: false): Choose the time you would like to have the meal delivered.
* **meal_details** (`meal_details`, text, required: false): Describe the type of meal you would like to order (e.g., "Italian food" or "Vegetarian option").
* **order_type** (`order_type`, select multiple, required: false): Select the type of meal you would like to order (Breakfast or Other).
* **number_of_guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, required: false): Specify the number of guests attending the event.
* **contact_details** (`contact_details`, note, required: false): Enter any relevant contact details to ensure that we can reach you if there are any issues with your order.
* **email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so that we can contact you with updates about your order.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you directly.
* **additional_instructions** (`additional_instructions`, text, required: false): Provide any additional information or special requests you have for your order.
* **order_notes** (`order_notes`, note, required: false): Enter any notes or comments you have about your order.
* **special_requests** (`special_requests`, text, required: false): Make any special requests or add any notes about your order.
