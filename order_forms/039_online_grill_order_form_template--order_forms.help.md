# online_grill_order_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers for their orders with the restaurant. It's designed to gather essential details about the customer and their order preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form pages sequentially, starting from the first page.
2. Enter your customer details, including name, address, and phone number.
3. Select the grill type(s) you'd like to order.
4. Add any additional order notes if needed.
5. Enter the restaurant name (if applicable).
6. Set the order date (if applicable).
7. Review your order before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Order Form (Page 1)
* **Grilled Chicken** (Grill Type): Select all applicable grill types for your order.
* **Order Notes** (Order Notes): Add any additional comments or instructions for your order.
* **Restaurant Name** (Restaurant Name): Enter the name of the restaurant you're ordering from (if applicable).
* **Order Date** (Order Date): Set the date you'd like to pick up or have your order delivered on (if applicable).

### Customer Details (Page 2)
* **Customer Details** (Customer Details): Enter your details, including name, address, and phone number.
* **Customer Email** (Customer Email): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Customer Address** (Customer Address): Enter your address for delivery or pick-up purposes.
* **Customer Phone** (Customer Phone): Enter your phone number for contact.

### Order Details (Page 3)
* **Order Total** (Order Total): Enter the total cost of your order (if applicable).
* **Customer Details** (Customer Details): Enter your details again, as this field is for confirmation purposes.

### Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Double-check your order details before submitting.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact the restaurant's support team.
