# Custom POS Display Stand Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow customers to place an order for a custom Point of Sale (POS) display stand. The form collects information about the order, including order details, artwork, quantity, color, payment method, due date, and total cost.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **order_form** field with a brief description of your order.
2. Provide more details about your order in the **specs** field.
3. Upload your artwork in the **artwork** field.
4. Specify the quantity you need in the **quantity** field.
5. Choose the color of the display stand in the **color** field.
6. Select the payment method you'd like to use (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Check) in the **payment_method** field.
7. Enter the due date for your order in the **due_date** field.
8. Enter the total cost of your order in the **total_cost** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **order_form**: A brief description of your order (required).
* **specs**: More details about your order (optional).
* **artwork**: Upload your custom artwork for the display stand (optional).
* **quantity**: The number of display stands you need (optional).
* **color**: The color of the display stand (optional).
* **payment_method**: The payment method you'd like to use (optional, choose from Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Check).
* **due_date**: The date by which you'd like to receive your order (optional).
* **total_cost**: The total cost of your order (optional).

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required. If you're unsure about any of the fields, please contact our support team for assistance.
