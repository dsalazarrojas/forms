# SEO Services Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture all relevant information for billing purposes when rendering SEO services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Order Details** in the text field, including any relevant information about the service being rendered.
2. Select the **Services Selected** options for the specific services being offered.
3. Choose the **Payment Preferences** options to determine payment terms.
4. Enter the **Client Information**, including their **Email**, **Phone**, and **Address**.
5. Enter any additional **Order Notes**.
6. Select the **Billing Status** for the order.
7. Set the **Due Date** and **Due Time**, if applicable.
8. Enter the **Billing Amount**, **Payment Amount**, **Tax Rate**, **Tax Amount**, **Discount Rate**, and **Discount Amount**.
9. Enter the **Sub-Total**, **Total**, and any **Notes** for the order.
10. Review the form for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Order Details
* **Order Details** (required, text): Enter a brief description of the order.
### Service Details
* **Service Details** (required, text): Enter any additional information about the service being rendered.
### Payment Preferences
* **Payment Preferences** (required, text): Select the payment terms for the order.
### Billing Information
* **Billing Information** (required, text): Enter the billing information for the order.
### Client Information
* **Email** (required, email): Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone** (required, text): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Address** (optional, text): Enter the client's address (if applicable).
### Order Notes
* **Order Notes** (required, note): Enter any additional notes about the order.
### Services Selected
* **Services Selected** (required, select multiple): Select the services being offered for the order.
### Payment Terms
* **Payment Terms** (required, select one): Choose the payment terms for the order.
### Payment Method
* **Payment Method** (required, select one): Choose the payment method for the order.
### Due Date
* **Due Date** (optional, date): Enter the due date for the order.
### Due Time
* **Due Time** (optional, time): Enter the due time for the order.
### Billing Status
* **Billing Status** (required, select one): Choose the billing status for the order.
### Billing Amount
* **Billing Amount** (required, number): Enter the billing amount for the order.
### Payment Amount
* **Payment Amount** (required, number): Enter the payment amount for the order.
### Tax Rate
* **Tax Rate** (required, number): Enter the tax rate for the order.
### Tax Amount
* **Tax Amount** (required, number): Enter the tax amount for the order.
### Discount Rate
* **Discount Rate** (optional, number): Enter the discount rate for the order.
### Discount Amount
* **Discount Amount** (optional, number): Enter the discount amount for the order.
### Sub-Total
* **Sub-Total** (required, number): Enter the sub-total for the order.
### Total
* **Total** (required, number): Enter the total for the order.
### Notes
* **Notes** (required, note): Enter any additional notes for the order.
### Assigned Tool
* **Assigned Tool** (required, text): Enter the tool assigned to the order.
