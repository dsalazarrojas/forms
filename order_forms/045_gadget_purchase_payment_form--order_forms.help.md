# Gadget Purchase Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gadget Purchase Payment Form is a simple form used to collect payment and shipping information from customers for the purpose of processing a gadget purchase.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your order details in the "Order Details" field. This is where you will provide information about the gadget you would like to purchase.
2. Enter your customer information in the "Customer Info" field.
3. Enter your shipping information in the "Shipping Info" field.
4. Enter your payment information in the "Payment Info" field.
5. If you have any additional information to share with our team, you can include a customer note in the "Customer Note" field.
6. If you have any specific shipping instructions, you can include a shipping note in the "Shipping Note" field.
7. If you have any additional order notes, you can include an "Order Note" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Customer Email" field.
9. Enter your phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
10. Enter your shipping phone number in the "Shipping Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details** (`1`, `text`, required: false):
	+ This field is where you will provide information about the gadget you would like to purchase.
* **Customer Info** (`2`, `text`, required: false):
	+ This field is where you will provide information about yourself, the customer.
* **Shipping Info** (`3`, `text`, required: false):
	+ This field is where you will provide information about the shipping address and any specific shipping instructions.
* **Payment Info** (`4`, `text`, required: false):
	+ This field is where you will provide payment information such as credit card number, etc.
* **Customer Note** (`5`, `note`, required: false):
	+ This field is where you can include any additional information you want to share with our team.
* **Shipping Note** (`6`, `note`, required: false):
	+ This field is where you can include any specific shipping instructions.
* **Order Note** (`7`, `note`, required: false):
	+ This field is where you can include any additional order notes or information.
* **Customer Email** (`8`, `email`, required: false):
	+ This field is where you will enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`9`, `text`, required: false):
	+ This field is where you will enter your phone number.
* **Shipping Phone** (`10`, `text`, required: false):
	+ This field is where you will enter the phone number of the shipping address.
