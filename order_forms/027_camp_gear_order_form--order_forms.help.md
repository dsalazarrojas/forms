# Camp Gear Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The camp gear order form is used to place orders for gear and merchandise from the camp store. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the delivery method for your order.
3. Choose the type of gear or merchandise you want to order from the available categories.
4. Select your primary item and size (if applicable).
5. Specify the quantity of the item you want to order.
6. If necessary, list any additional items and sizes you want to order.
7. Select your preferred payment method.
8. Provide any special instructions or gift notes.
9. Finally, indicate that you agree to the order terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address where you can be reached for order confirmation.
* **Contact Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Delivery Method**: Choose how you want to receive your order: Ship to Home, Pick up at Camp Office, or Deliver to Camper (On-site).
* **Shipping - Delivery Address**: If shipping to home, enter your address here. This field is only required if shipping to home.
* **Product Category**: Select the type of gear or merchandise you want to order: Apparel, Camping Equipment, Accessories, or Youth Sizes.
* **Select Your Primary Item**: Choose the main item you want to order from the available options.
* **Size (If Applicable)**: If the item has multiple sizes, select the size you need.
* **Quantity**: Specify how many of the item you want to order.
* **List Additional Items and Sizes**: If you want to add more items or sizes, list them here.
* **Preferred Payment Method**: Choose how you want to pay for your order: Credit Card (Online), PayPal, Apple Pay - Google Pay, or Invoice (for organizations only).
* **Special Instructions or Gift Note**: Provide any special instructions or gift notes you want to include with your order.
* **Order Submission Date**: This field is automatically populated with the current date.
* **Order Terms and Consent**: Confirm that you agree to the order terms and refund policy.
