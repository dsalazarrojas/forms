# Robotics End Effector Custom Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect custom order information for robotics end effectors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your order details in the "Order Form Page 1" section.
2. Provide customer information, such as name and email, in the "Customer Info" section.
3. Enter shipping details in the "Shipping Details" section.
4. Fill out the "Shipping Address" section.
5. Add product information in the "Product Info" section.
6. Specify payment terms in the "Payment Terms" section.
7. Provide shipping instructions in the "Shipping Instructions" section.
8. Confirm your order in the "Order Confirmation" section.
9. Enter customer contact information, such as email and phone number, in the "Customer Info" section.
10. Add any additional customer notes in the "Customer Note" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **OrderFormPage1** (`Order Form Page 1`, text, required: false)
    This is the first page of the order form. Please fill in your order details here.
* **customer_info** (`Customer Info`, text, required: false)
    This section is for you to provide information about yourself or the company placing the order.
* **shipping_details** (`Shipping Details`, text, required: false)
    Enter any relevant shipping information, such as shipping method or any special handling instructions.
* **shipping_address** (`Shipping Address`, text, required: false)
    Enter the shipping address where the order will be delivered.
* **product_info** (`Product Info`, text, required: false)
    Provide information about the product being ordered, including any special requirements or specifications.
* **payment_terms** (`Payment Terms`, text, required: false)
    Specify the payment terms, such as payment method or due date.
* **shipping_instructions** (`Shipping Instructions`, text, required: false)
    Add any special shipping instructions, such as delivery date or special handling requirements.
* **order_confirmation** (`Order Confirmation`, text, required: false)
    Confirm your order details before submitting the form.
* **customer_email** (`Customer Email`, email, required: false)
    Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **customer_phone** (`Customer Phone`, text, required: false)
    Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **customer_note** (`Customer Note`, text, required: false)
    Add any additional customer notes or comments.
