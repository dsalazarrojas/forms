# Reusable Takeout Container Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reusable Takeout Container Order Form is designed to collect information from customers to place takeout orders with a restaurant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Customer Name" field with the customer's name.
2. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
3. Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field.
4. Select the restaurant for the takeout in the "Restaurant Name" field.
5. Choose the type of container needed in the "Container Type" field.
6. Select the delivery window in the "Delivery Window" field.
7. Choose the pickup window in the "Pickup Window" field.
8. Enter the total cost of the order in the "Order Total" field.
9. Enter the restaurant's email address in the "Restaurant Email" field.
10. Enter the restaurant's phone number in the "Restaurant Phone" field.
11. Enter the delivery address in the "Delivery Address" field.
12. Select the payment method in the "Payment Method" field.
