# Tree Watering Bag Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to place an order for tree watering bags, a service provided by our gardening or landscaping business. It will collect the necessary information to fulfill the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** (required).
2. Enter your **Email** (optional, but recommended for communication purposes).
3. Specify the number of **Trees** you would like to order (optional, but recommended for accurate pricing).
4. Choose your **Payment Method** (required):
	* Credit Card
	* PayPal
	* Bank Transfer
5. Select the **Order Date** and **Order Time** (required).
6. Enter the **Total Cost** (required, automatically calculated based on the order details).
7. Add any additional **Note** (optional, but recommended for special requests).
8. Choose the **Category** of the order (required, e.g., nurseries, land scaping, etc.).
9. Select the **Payment Status** (optional, but recommended for tracking purposes):
	* Paid
	* Unpaid
	* Pending
10. Finally, select the **Order Status** (required, e.g., Confirmed, Shipped, Delivered):
	* Confirmed
	* Shipped
	* Delivered

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Please enter your first name.
* **Email**: Optional, but recommended for communication purposes. Please enter your email address if you'd like to receive updates on your order.
* **Tree Count**: If known, please enter the number of trees you'd like to order. This will help with accurate pricing.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you'd like to pay for your order:
	+ Credit Card
	+ PayPal
	+ Bank Transfer
* **Order Date**: Choose the date when you'd like to receive your order.
* **Order Time**: Choose the time of day when you'd like to receive your order.
* **Total Cost**: This will be automatically calculated based on the order details.
* **Note**: If you have any special requests or instructions, please add them here.
* **Category**: Choose the category of your order (e.g., nurseries, land scaping, etc.).
* **Payment Status**: If you've already paid, select "Paid". If you're waiting for payment confirmation, select "Pending".
* **Order Status**: Select the status of your order:
	+ Confirmed
	+ Shipped
	+ Delivered

## Tips

* Please make sure to double-check your order details before submitting.
* If you have any questions, feel free to contact us.
* We'll review your order carefully and will be in touch soon.
* If you'd like to add or remove anything, please let us know and we'll accommodate your needs.
