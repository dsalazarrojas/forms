# Team Branded Face Mask Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Branded Face Mask Order Form is used to place orders for team-branded face masks. Fill out this form to provide details about your order, and a representative will be in touch with you to finalize the order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you have permission to represent the team for which you're placing the order.
3. Review your order carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Masked** (`masked`, Number, not required):
	+ Enter the masked part of the form (e.g., 1, 2, or 3).
* **Size** (`size`, Select One, not required):
	+ Choose the size of the face mask you'd like to order (Small, Medium, or Large).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, Number, not required):
	+ Enter the number of masks you'd like to order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, Text, not required):
	+ Enter the payment method you'd like to use (e.g., credit card, PayPal).
* **Email** (`email`, Email, not required):
	+ Enter your email address to receive updates about your order.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, not required):
	+ Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, Date, not required):
	+ Enter the date you'd like to place your order by (optional).
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, Time, not required):
	+ Enter the time you'd like to place your order by (optional).
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, Note, not required):
	+ Enter any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, Text, not required):
	+ Enter the name of the team for which you're placing the order.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, Number, not required):
	+ Enter the size of the team for which you're placing the order (not used in this form, but provided for future use).
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, Select One, not required):
	+ Choose whether the order is for a Team or Individual (though this field is redundant with `order_type2`, it's included for consistency).
* **Order Type2** (`order_type2`, Select Multiple, not required):
	+ Select whether the order is for a Team or Individual.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, Select One, not required):
	+ Choose the status of the order (Open or Closed, but this field is not used in this form, as this form is only for placing orders).
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, Select One, not required):
	+ Choose the status of the payment (Paid or Unpaid, but this field is not used in this form, as this form is only for placing orders).
 
Note: Some fields are not used in this form (e.g., `team_size`, `order_status`, `payment_status`), but they are included for future use in potential extensions to this form.
 
### Tips
- Ensure that your contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any questions or concerns about your order, contact a representative.
- Review your order carefully before submitting it to avoid errors.
