# Customer Checkout And Shipping Form - Help Guide

To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Enter the requested information in each field and make sure to fill all required fields.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
* Submit the form once you have filled in all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer (1)**: Enter your name as the customer.
* **Shipping (2)**: Enter your shipping address, including the address line 1, address line 2 (if applicable), city, state, and zip code.
* **Payment (3)**: Enter your payment details, including the payment method and other required information.
* **Order Summary (4)**: Enter a brief summary of your order, such as the order number, products purchased, and shipping instructions.
