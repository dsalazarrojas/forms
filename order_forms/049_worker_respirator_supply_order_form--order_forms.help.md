# Worker Respirator Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Worker Respirator Supply Order Form is used to order respirators and other supplies for workers in their designated areas. This form helps ensure that workers receive the necessary equipment to perform their tasks safely and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand what information is required.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
3. Ensure you are requesting supplies for your designated area.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Page 1**: This page is the first step in the form process. It is optional, but helps to get you started if you need to reference other pages later on.
* **Page 2: Respirator Type** (optional):
	+ **Select One (Respirator Type)**: Choose 'Yes' if you need respirators, otherwise select 'No'. This helps us determine if you need respirators for your area.
* **Page 3: Quantity Required** (optional):
	+ **Quantity Required**: Enter the number of respirators you need. Please note that this field is for the quantity required, not available or total quantity. If you need help with quantities or have questions, feel free to ask.
* **Page 4: Contact Information** (optional):
	+ **Contact Information**: Enter your name, job title, and contact information so we can get back to you regarding your order. Please be thorough and accurate in providing your details.

## Tips
Remember to review this form carefully and fill it out thoroughly. If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to reach out.
