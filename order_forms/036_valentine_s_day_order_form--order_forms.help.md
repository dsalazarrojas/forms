# valentines-day-order-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Valentine's Day Order Form is used to collect personal contact information, order details, and payment options from customers for orders related to Valentine's Day.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill in your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email in the "Email" field.
4. Choose the Order Date in the "Order Date" field.
5. Choose the Delivery Date in the "Delivery Date" field.
6. Choose the Order Time in the "Order Time" field.
7. Fill in your Phone in the "Phone" field.
8. Write any additional comments or special requests in the "Order Message" field.
9. Choose the payment method in the "Delivery Date/Time" field if it applies.
10. Confirm whether you have a Card, Cash, or Online payment method (if applicable).
11. Enter the Order Amount.
12. Select the status of your order (In Progress, Completed, or Not Completed).
13. Fill in any Customer Notes if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Text, Required)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (Text, Required)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email (Email, Required)**: Enter your email address.
* **Order Date (Date, Required)**: Choose the date for your order.
* **Delivery Date (Date, Required)**: Choose the date for delivery.
* **Order Time (Time, Required)**: Choose the time for your order.
* **Phone (Text, Optional)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Order Message (Text, Optional)**: Write any additional comments or special requests.
* **Delivery Date/Time (Date, Optional)**: Choose the date and time for delivery.
* **Payment Method (Select One, Required)**: Choose your payment method (Yes, No).
* **Payment Method Card (Select One, Optional)**: Choose if you're paying with a Card (if applicable).
* **Payment Method Online (Select One, Optional)**: Choose if you're paying Online (if applicable).
* **Order Amount (Number, Required)**: Enter the total amount for your order.
* **Order Status (Select Multiple, Optional)**: Select the status of your order (In Progress, Completed, Not Completed).
* **Customer Notes (Text, Optional)**: Fill in any notes or comments for your order.
