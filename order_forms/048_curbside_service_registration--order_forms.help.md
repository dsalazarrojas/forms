# curbside_service_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer information for curbside service requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form with the required information, starting with your name and order number.
2.  Enter your phone number, making sure to include the area code.
3.  Provide your email address to contact you.
4.  Select the preferred pickup time for your curbside service.
5.  Choose the date for the pickup.
6.  Enter any additional curbside service details if needed.
7.  Ensure you submit the form correctly with all the required fields filled out.
