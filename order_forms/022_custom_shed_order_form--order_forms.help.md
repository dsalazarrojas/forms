# custom_shed_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for custom shed orders. Fill out this form to provide details about the shed you want to order, including the delivery date and time, size, location, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the `custom_shed_order` field with a brief description of the shed you want to order.
2. Select the desired delivery date for your shed using the date field.
3. Choose the delivery time for your shed using the time field.
4. Select the size of the shed from the options provided (small, medium, large, or extra large).
5. Enter the location where you would like to have the shed delivered.
6. Enter your name as the customer.
7. Enter your contact number.
8. Enter your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Custom Shed Order** (`custom_shed_order`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the shed you want to order.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): Select the date you would like to have the shed delivered.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, optional): Choose the time you would like to have the shed delivered.
* **Shed Size** (`shed_size`, select_one, optional): Select the size of the shed you want to order (small, medium, large, or extra large).
* **Shed Location** (`shed_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you would like to have the shed delivered.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the customer.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address.

## Tips
Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and completely to ensure a smooth ordering process.
