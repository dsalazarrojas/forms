# Color Street SPRING 2021 PRE ORDER - Flawless Nails With Jen - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow customers to place pre-orders for the "Flawless Nails With Jen" product offering for Spring 2021. It will gather necessary information from customers, including contact and shipping details, product selection, and payment options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **Customer Name** in the "Customer Name" field.
2.  Enter your **Email Address** in the "Email Address" field. This will be the email address we use to contact you regarding your order.
3.  Provide your **Shipping Address** in the "Shipping Address" field.
4.  Select your preferred **Spring Solids Selection** from the available options.
5.  Select your preferred **Spring Glitters Selection** from the available options.
6.  Select your preferred **Spring Design Selection** from the available options.
7.  If applicable, enter the **Hostess Name** in the "Hostess Name" field.
8.  Enter any **Message for Jen** in the "Message for Jen" field.
9.  Select whether you are participating in the **B3G1 Promotion**. If you are, select "True", otherwise "False".
10. Enter the total number of sets you are **Pre-Ordering** in the "Total Sets Pre-Ordered" field.
11. Select your desired **Payment Method and Handle** in the "Payment Method and Handle" field (e.g., Venmo @User).
12. Choose your desired **Shipping Option** from the available options.
13. Confirm your order by submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`jen_customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it should be displayed on your order.
* **Email Address** (`jen_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address we will use to contact you about your order.
* **Shipping Address** (`jen_shipping_address`, `text`, required): Enter your shipping address for your order delivery.
* **Spring Solids Selection** (`spring_solids_2021`, `select_multiple`, required): Select from the list of Spring Solids options.
* **Spring Glitters Selection** (`spring_glitters_2021`, `select_multiple`, required): Select from the list of Spring Glitters options.
* **Spring Design Selection** (`spring_designs_2021`, `select_multiple`, required): Select from the list of Spring Designs options.
* **Hostess Name (If applicable)** (`jen_hostess_name`, `text`, required): If you are ordering for someone else, enter their name.
* **Message for Jen** (`jen_extra_comments`, `text`, required): Enter any additional comments or message for Jen.
* **Are you participating in the B3G1 Promotion?** (`jen_b3g1_promo`, `select_one`, required): Select "True" if you are participating, otherwise "False".
* **Total Sets Pre-Ordered** (`jen_total_sets`, `number`, required): Enter the total number of sets you are pre-ordering.
* **Payment Method and Handle** (`jen_payment_handle`, `text`, required): Enter your payment method and handle (e.g., Venmo @User).
* **Shipping Option** (`jen_shipping_speed`, `select_one`, required): Select your shipping option from the available options.
* **Order Summary** (`jen_order_math`, `note`, required): This section is a summary of your order details.
* **Order Confirmation** (`jen_checkout`, `note`, required): This is where you review and confirm your order details before submission.
