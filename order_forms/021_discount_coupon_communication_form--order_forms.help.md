# discount_coupon_communication_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer information, discount coupon details, and communication preferences for orders. It is intended for businesses to communicate with customers and manage their discount coupons.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information, making sure to provide accurate and complete answers to each field.
2. Start by entering the customer information, including any details necessary for the order.
3. Next, input the coupon details, such as the discount amount and any relevant information about the coupon.
4. Then, specify the communication preferences, including how the customer would like to receive updates and information about their order.
5. After that, provide the order information, including any relevant details about the order.
6. If applicable, add any relevant business information, such as the company name and contact details.
7. Finally, provide any customer and business notes, which can be helpful for reference and communication.
8. Once all the fields are filled out, submit the form to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Info** (Customer Info, text, required: false): Provide the customer's name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
- **Coupon Details** (Coupon Details, text, required: false): Enter the details about the discount coupon, including the discount amount and any other relevant information.
- **Communication Preferences** (Communication Preferences, text, required: false): Specify how the customer would like to receive updates and information about their order, such as email or phone calls.
- **Order Info** (Order Info, text, required: false): Enter any relevant details about the order, such as the order date and time.
- **Business Info** (Business Info, text, required: false): Provide any relevant business information, such as the company name and contact details.
- **Customer Notes** (Customer Notes, text, required: false): Add any additional notes or information about the customer.
- **Business Notes** (Business Notes, text, required: false): Add any additional business-related notes or information.
- **Submission** (Submission, text, required: false): This is the final step, which submits the form.
