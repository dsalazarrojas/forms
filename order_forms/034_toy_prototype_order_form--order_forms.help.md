# Toy Prototype Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for customers or users to provide details for ordering a toy prototype. It collects information about the toy prototype being ordered, the design requirements, delivery preferences, payment information, customer details, shipping address, and any additional order notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in each field with the requested information. You will need to provide the following:

1. **Toy Prototype Details**: Enter a brief description of the toy prototype you want to order.
2. **Design Requirements**: Describe the design requirements for the toy prototype, including any specific features or functionalities you need.
3. **Delivery Preferences**: Specify your preferred method of delivery, such as pickup or shipping.
4. **Payment Information**: Provide your payment details, including the amount to be paid.
5. **Customer Information**: Enter your customer name, email address, and any other relevant information.
6. **Shipping Address**: Fill in the shipping address where you want the toy prototype to be delivered.
7. **Order Notes**: Add any additional comments or instructions for the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Toy Prototype Details** (text, required): Enter the name of the toy prototype you want to order.
* **Design Requirements** (text, optional): Describe the design requirements for the toy prototype, including any specific features or functionalities you need.
* **Delivery Preferences** (text, optional): Specify your preferred method of delivery, such as pickup or shipping.
* **Payment Information** (text, optional): Provide your payment details, including the amount to be paid.
* **Customer Information** (text, optional): Enter your customer name, email address, and any other relevant information.
* **Shipping Address** (text, optional): Fill in the shipping address where you want the toy prototype to be delivered.
* **Order Notes** (text, optional): Add any additional comments or instructions for the order.
