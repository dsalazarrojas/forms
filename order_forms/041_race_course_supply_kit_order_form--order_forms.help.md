# Race Course Supply Kit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Race Course Supply Kit Order Form is designed to collect details for an order of a Race Course Supply Kit from customers or clients. This form is used to gather essential information such as delivery details, contact information, and order comments to process the order efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the form with the required information.
2.  Choose the delivery date and time from the calendar or select a date.
3.  Enter any special instructions or comments for the order.
4.  Provide your contact name, phone number, and email.
5.  Add any additional event details (if applicable).
6.  Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Delivery Details**: Enter the details for the delivery of the Race Course Supply Kit.
*   **Delivery Date**: Select the date of delivery or choose from the calendar.
*   **Delivery Time**: Choose the time of delivery (morning or afternoon).
*   **Special Instructions**: Enter any special instructions or comments for the order.
*   **Contact Name**: Enter your name for contact purposes.
*   **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Contact Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   **Order Comments**: Add any additional comments or information about the order.
*   **Event Details**: Enter any additional event details (if applicable).

## Tips
*   Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure timely delivery of the Race Course Supply Kit.
*   Use the calendar to select a date if you want to choose from available dates.
*   Enter any specific instructions or comments to ensure that the order is processed correctly.
