# Custom Proof - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Proof form is used to collect details about a custom proof from clients. This form is intended to gather relevant information about the client's custom proof, including the company's slogan, mission statement, proof size, client value alignment, client comments, and any additional proof notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the company's slogan or mission statement in the "company slogan mission statement" field.
2. Provide the size of the proof in the "proof size" field.
3. Describe how the client's value aligns with the custom proof in the "client value alignment" field.
4. Share any comments or feedback from the client in the "client comments" field.
5. Add any additional notes or observations about the proof in the "proof notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Logo (1)**: Enter the company logo, if applicable.
* **Company Slogan / Mission Statement (2)**: Briefly describe the company's slogan or mission statement.
* **Proof Size (3)**: Specify the size of the custom proof.
* **Client Value Alignment (4)**: Explain how the client's value proposition or objectives are met by the custom proof.
* **Client Comments (5)**: Share any comments or feedback from the client regarding the custom proof.
* **Proof Notes (6)**: Add any additional notes or observations about the custom proof.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields (if any) to ensure accurate data collection.
- Double-check your inputs for accuracy and consistency.
- This form can be modified or customized to suit your specific use case.
