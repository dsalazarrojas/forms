# Afterpay Furniture Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Afterpay Furniture Order Form is designed to collect information from customers to process their orders for premium furniture items. The form is divided into several sections to ensure that all relevant details are captured.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **Customer Name**: This is the name of the person placing the order.
2.  Enter your **Email Address**: This is the email address to which the order confirmation and updates will be sent.
3.  Provide your **Residential Address**: Enter your street address, suburb, and postcode to ensure accurate delivery.
4.  Select the **Furniture Item**: Choose the type of furniture you want to order, e.g., sofa, dining table.
5.  Select the **Room Category**: Choose the room where the item will be used, e.g., living room, bedroom.
6.  Choose the **Material or Fabric Choice**: Select the type of wood or fabric for your furniture item.
7.  Enter the **Quantity**: Specify the number of items you want to order.
8.  Choose **Professional Installation Service**: Decide if you want our team to install the furniture.
9.  Enter the **Total Furniture Cost**: This will be calculated based on your selections.
10.  Select **Payment via Afterpay**: Choose how you want to pay for your order.
11.  Choose a **Preferred Delivery Date**: Select a date for delivery, subject to availability.
