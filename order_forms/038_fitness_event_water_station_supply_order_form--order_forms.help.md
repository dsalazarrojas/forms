# Fitness Event Water Station Supply Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to order supplies for a fitness event water station. It helps the water station staff to prepare the necessary supplies for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the name of the fitness event in the "Event Name" field.
2. Select the type of water supply you need from the "Water Type" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the quantity of water supply you need.
4. Enter the date you would like to pick up or have the order delivered on in the "Order Date" field.
5. Enter the time you would like to pick up or have the order delivered on in the "Order Time" field.
6. Add any additional notes about your order in the "Note" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
• **Event Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the fitness event.
• **Water Type** (select_one, required): Choose from Aek, Water, Juice, or Sports Drink.
• **Quantity** (number, required): Enter the quantity of water supply you need.
• **Order Date** (date, required): Enter the date you would like to pick up or have the order delivered on.
• **Order Time** (time, required): Enter the time you would like to pick up or have the order delivered on.
• **Note** (note, optional): Add any additional notes about your order.
• **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
• **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter the correct event name to ensure the correct supplies are prepared.
- Select the correct water type to ensure it is delivered on time.
- Enter the correct quantity to avoid over or under ordering.
- Double-check the date and time to ensure timely delivery or pickup.
- Use the "Note" field to add any special instructions or requests.
- Ensure your email address is valid and up-to-date.
- Enter your phone number correctly to ensure contact.
