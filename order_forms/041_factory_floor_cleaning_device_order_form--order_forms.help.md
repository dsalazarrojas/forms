# Factory Floor Cleaning Device Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Factory Floor Cleaning Device Order Form is used to gather information from customers about their orders for factory floor cleaning devices. This form helps in efficiently processing and managing orders for these devices, including logistics and delivery details.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Fill in Customer Information
- **Company**: Please enter the customer's company name or organization.
- **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person at the company who will be receiving the device.
- **Phone**: Provide the contact person's phone number.
- **Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
- **Contact Person**: Select 'Yes' if you are the contact person, or 'No' if not.

### Step 2: Specify Order Details
- **Device Type**: Select the type of factory floor cleaning device you wish to order ('Yes' or 'No'). 
- **Device Model**: Select the specific model of the device you want to order ('Yes' or 'No'). 
- **Quantity**: Enter the number of devices you wish to order.
- **Notes**: Provide any additional comments or instructions for your order.
- **Date Of Delivery**: Enter the date on which you would like to receive your order.
- **Time Of Delivery**: Enter the time of day you would like to receive your order.
- **Delivery Address**: Enter the address where you would like to receive your order.

### Step 3: Confirm Order and Specify Additional Details
- **City**: Enter the city for the delivery address.
- **State**: Enter the state for the delivery address.
- **Zip**: Enter the zip code for the delivery address.
- **Order Notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes about your order.
- **Total Amount**: Enter the total amount you are willing to pay.
- **Payment Terms**: Enter any specific payment terms you have.
- **Terms and Conditions**: Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the order.
- **Order Confirmation**: Select 'Yes' if this information is correct, or 'No' to correct or cancel the order.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate contact information so we can efficiently contact you with order updates.
- For delivery details, please ensure you are available to receive the device during the specified time.
- Review all fields carefully to ensure accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
- This form is for factory floor cleaning devices, so please select the correct device type and model to ensure you receive the correct item.
