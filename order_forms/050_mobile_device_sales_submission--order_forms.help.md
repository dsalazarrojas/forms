# Mobile Device Sales Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for sellers to submit sales information about mobile devices, including details about the seller, device specifications, and pricing. This form helps to track and process sales effectively, ensuring accuracy and reducing duplication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all required fields (marked as **Required** in this guide).
2. Provide accurate and relevant information for each field.
3. Review and submit the form for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Seller Details (Page 1, id: 1)**
	+ This field requires your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Device Specs (Page 1, id: 2)**
	+ Describe the mobile device you are selling, including model number, storage, and other important specs.
* **Photos (Page 1, id: 3)**
	+ Attach high-quality images of the mobile device to help with sales verification.
* **Pricing (Page 1, id: 4)**
	+ Enter the sale price of the mobile device, including any promotions or discounts.
* **Sales Status (Page 1, id: 5)**
	+ Choose the status of the sale from the options: **Pending**, **Approved**, or **Rejected**.
* **Seller Comments (Page 1, id: 6)**
	+ Provide any additional comments or notes about the sale, such as payment methods or special conditions.
* **Note:** Photos is an optional field and can be left blank if not applicable.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Attach clear and high-quality images for Photos field.
* Review and double-check all information before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to [contact email: form@gic.mx] for help and guidance.
