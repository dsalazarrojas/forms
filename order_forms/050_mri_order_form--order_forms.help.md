# mri_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mri_order_form is used for ordering and scheduling an MRI procedure for patients, gathering essential information for the appointment and payment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient prophets" field.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
3. Enter the patient's address in the "Address" field.
4. Enter the date of the appointment in the "Date of Appointment" field.
5. Enter the time of the appointment in the "Time of Appointment" field.
6. Select the duration of the procedure in the "Duration" field.
7. Choose the payment method in the "Payment Method" field.
8. Optionally, enter the payment amount in the "Payment Amount" field.
9. Add any additional notes in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient prophets**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address**: Enter the patient's street address.
* **Date of Appointment**: Enter the date of the appointment in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Appointment**: Enter the time of the appointment in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Duration**: Enter the expected duration of the MRI procedure.
* **Payment Method**: Select the method of payment for the procedure (e.g., credit card, insurance).
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount to be paid for the procedure (optional).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or instructions for the procedure (optional).

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are completed before proceeding with the submission.
- Double-check the accuracy of patient information entered, as it may affect appointment scheduling and payment processing.
- Use the "Payment Method" field to select the payment method that best suits the patient's preferences or circumstances.
