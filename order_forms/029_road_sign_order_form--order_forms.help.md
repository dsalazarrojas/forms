# Road Sign Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Road Sign Order Form is a user-friendly form designed to collect information from customers to process their orders and deliver road signs. The form is a vital part of our order management process, ensuring we receive all necessary details to create and ship orders accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Road Sign Order Form, follow these simple steps:

1.  Ensure you have all required information before filling out the form.
2.  Select the correct options for each field as instructed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Details
• **Customer Details** (`customer_details`): Enter your name, contact information, and any relevant notes in this text field.

### Delivery Details
• **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`): Provide any additional delivery instructions or details in this text field.

### Sign Orders
• **Sign Orders** (`sign_orders`): Select one or more order options from the dropdown menu to specify which signs you would like to order.

### Artwork Details
• **Artwork Details** (`artwork_details`): Enter any specific requests or details about the artwork in this text field.

### Payments
• **Payments** (`payments`): Enter any payment information or details in this text field.

### Customer Info
• **Customer Info** (`customer_info`): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you have any specific customer information to include in the order.

### Order Notes
• **Order Notes** (`order_notes`): Add any additional notes or requests about the order in this text field.

### Customer Phone
• **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`): Enter the customer's phone number for further communication if required.

Tips:
* Be as specific as possible when filling out the form to ensure accurate order processing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to our customer support team.
