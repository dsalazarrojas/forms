**Industrial Network Hub Order Form - Help Guide**
=============================================

## Purpose
The Industrial Network Hub Order Form is used to collect information from customers who want to place orders with the Industrial Network Hub. It's a straightforward form that helps us understand the customer's needs and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field.
3. Enter the customer's contact number in the "Customer Contact No" field.
4. Provide a brief description of the order in the "Order Description" field.
5. Specify the delivery date in the "Requested Delivery Date" field.
6. Enter the delivery location in the "Delivery Location" field.
7. If applicable, specify any special requirements in the "Special Requirements" field.
8. Enter any additional customer notes in the "Customer Notes" field.
9. Describe the product being ordered in the "Product Ordered" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the customer as they would like to be known.
- **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, Email, Optional): Enter the customer's email address for communication purposes.
- **Customer Contact No** (`customer_contact_no`, Text, Optional): Enter the customer's contact number for easy contact.
- **Order Description** (`order_description`, Note, Optional): Briefly describe the order being made to ensure clarity.
- **Requested Delivery Date** (`requested_delivery_date`, Date, Optional): Specify the date the order is needed by.
- **Delivery Location** (`delivery_location`, Text, Optional): Enter the location where the delivery is to be made.
- **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, Text, Optional): If any special requirements or requests, enter them here.
- **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional information or notes from the customer.
- **Product Ordered** (`product_ordered`, Text, Optional): Describe the product being ordered, including any specific details or specifications.
