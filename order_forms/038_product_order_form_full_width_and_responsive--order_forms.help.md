# Product Order Form Full Width And Responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Order Form Full Width And Responsive is a tool for customers to provide information to place a product order. It's designed to be easily accessible and displayed in a full-width and responsive format.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the product from the list.
2. Choose the quantity of the product.
3. Choose the shipping option (if applicable).
4. Provide any additional comments for the order.
5. Enter the customer's email address.
6. Select the customer's phone number.
7. Date and time of the order should be automatically filled in as per default.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Product** (`select_product`, required/optional): Select the product you wish to order from the provided options.
  This field allows you to choose the product you're interested in purchasing.
- **Quantity** (`select_quantity`, required/optional): Select the quantity you want to order.
  This field determines how many of the selected product you wish to order.
- **Shipping** (`shipping`, required/optional): Enter the shipping address and other details if necessary.
  This field allows for any additional shipping instructions or address details.
- **Customer** (`select_customer`, required/optional): Select the customer's details (email, phone) as per the options.
  This field helps you to provide the customer's contact information for the order.
- **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or instructions for the order.
  This field allows you to provide any additional comments about the order.
- **Email** (`select_email`, required/optional): Enter the customer's email address.
  This field helps you to provide the customer's email address for communication.
- **Phone** (`select_phone`, required/optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
  This field allows you to provide the customer's phone number for communication.
- **Order Date** (`order_date`, required/optional): The date of the order should be filled in automatically.
  This field displays the order's date and time.
- **Time** (`select_time`, required/optional): The time of the order should be filled in automatically.
  This field displays the order's time.
