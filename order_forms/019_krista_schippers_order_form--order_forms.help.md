# Krista Schippers Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Krista Schippers Order Form is a tool for customers to provide their order details to the business. The form is divided into two pages, each page has a specific set of fields that the customer needs to fill in.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information ready before starting the form.
2. Fill out the first page with your first name, last name, email, phone number, and date you would like to order.
3. Fill out the second page with any additional notes or comments you may have.
4. Click "Submit" to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification.

### Email
* **Email** (email, required): Please enter a valid email address where you can be reached.

### Phone
* **Phone** (text, required): Please enter your phone number where you can be reached.

### Date
* **Date** (date, required): Please enter the date you would like to order.

### Time
* **Time** (time, required): Please enter the time you would like to order.

### Note
* **Note** (note, optional): Please enter any additional notes or comments about your order.

### First Name Surname
* **First Name Surname** (text, optional): This field is not required and can be left blank.
