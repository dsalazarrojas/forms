# Supplier Quotation Update Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to update information related to supplier quotations. The form is intended to gather updates from suppliers regarding their quotations, such as the updated quotation price, date, or other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information to update your supplier quotation.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
3. Select the correct options for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Quotation Update Contact Form** (`title`, text, not required): This field is the title of the form, providing a descriptive name to identify the supplier's update.
* **Updated Quotation** (`updated_quotation`, text, not required): Enter the updated details of your supplier's quotation, if any.
* **Quotation Date** (`quotation_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the updated quotation, if applicable.
* **Updated Quotation Price** (`updated_quotation_price`, number, not required): Enter the price of the updated quotation, if changed.
* **Updated Quotation Total** (`updated_quotation_total`, number, not required): Enter the total of the updated quotation, if changed.
* **Supplier Update Reason** (`supplier_update_reason`, select one, not required):
	+ 'Yes': This option indicates that there is an update to the quotation.
	+ 'No': This option indicates that there is no update to the quotation.
* **Supplier Update Comment** (`supplier_update_comment`, text, not required): Enter any additional comments related to the supplier update.
* **Supplier Contact** (`supplier_contact`, select multiple, not required):
	+ 'Yes': This option indicates that the supplier's contact information is up to date.
	+ 'No': This option indicates that the supplier's contact information is not up to date.

Note: All fields are optional, and you can leave them blank if they do not apply to your update.
