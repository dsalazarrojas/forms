# School T Shirt Purchase Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect purchase requests for school t-shirts. It is intended for students, teachers, and staff to submit their t-shirt purchasing needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and fill in the required information as prompted.
2. Provide your name and any relevant details in the 'school_t_shirt_purchase_form' field.
3. List your t-shirt purchasing needs in the 't_shirt_purchases' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **School T Shirt Purchase Form (`school_t_shirt_purchase_form`, text, required: no)**: Enter your name and any other relevant details you'd like to include for your t-shirt purchase request.
- **T Shirt Purchases (`t_shirt_purchases`, text, required: no)**: Describe your t-shirt purchasing needs, including the design or color of the t-shirt you're interested in.
- **Submit T Shirt Purchase Form (`submit_t_shirt_purchase_form`, select_one, required: yes)**: Choose to either "Submit" to submit your request or "Cancel" to start over.
