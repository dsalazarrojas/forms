# Reusable Carton Program Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to capture detailed information about your order for the Reusable Carton Program. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure efficient processing and delivery of your cartons.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on each field to select the appropriate information for your order.
2. Fill out all required fields.
3. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details**: Enter a brief description of your order.
* **Contact Information**: Provide your name, address, phone number, and email address to contact you about your order.
* **Delivery Details**: Specify the delivery date and any special delivery instructions.
* **Payment Options**: Choose your preferred payment method (e.g., credit card, bank transfer).
* **Shipping Details**: Enter the carton size, type, and quantity, as well as the unit of measurement (inches, centimeters, or feet).
* **Carton Size**: Select the width and height of the carton.
* **Carton Type**: Choose the type of carton (e.g., Carton A, Carton B, Carton C).
* **Carton Color**: Select the color of the carton (e.g., White, Black, Blue).
* **Carton Print Type**: Choose whether to print on the front and back, front only, or back only.
* **Custom Print**: Enter any custom print information.
* **Quantity**: Specify the number of cartons required.
* **Carton Quantity Type**: Choose whether to measure quantity per unit, per box, or per carton.
* **Carton Size Unit**: Select the unit of measurement for the carton size (e.g., inches, centimeters, feet).
* **Carton Width**, **Carton Height**, **Carton Depth**, **Carton Length**, **Carton Weight**: Enter the dimensions and weight of the carton.
* **Carton Note**: Add any additional comments or notes about the carton.
* **Order Date**: Specify the date when you would like to place your order.
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the desired delivery date.
* **Order By**: Enter any specific person or department that will be ordering the cartons.
* **Order Notes**: Add any additional comments or instructions about your order.
* **Carton Print Color**: Select the color for the print on the carton (e.g., None, White, Black, Blue).
* **Order Status**: Indicate the status of your order (e.g., Not Started, In Progress, Completed).

Please review the information entered and submit the form accurately.
