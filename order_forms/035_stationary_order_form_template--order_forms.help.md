# Stationary Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a new order request for stationary items. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the product you wish to order from the **Product Selection** dropdown menu.
2. Choose the desired quantity from the **Quantity Selection** dropdown menu.
3. Select the payment method from the **Payment Selection** dropdown menu.
4. Enter your **Customer Email** and **Customer Phone**.
5. Enter your **Shipping Address**, **Billing Address**, and any other optional address fields.
6. Select the order status by choosing **In Progress**, **Completed**, or **Canceled** from the **Order Status** dropdown menu.
7. Optionally, you can add any additional order notes in the **Order Notes** field.
8. Finally, review your information and submit the form to create a new order request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Selection**: Select the product you wish to order from the dropdown menu.
* **Quantity Selection**: Choose the desired quantity of the selected product.
* **Payment Selection**: Choose the payment method for your order (Cash or Credit).
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address to contact you regarding your order.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your shipping address to ensure accurate delivery.
* **Billing Address**: Enter your billing address for payment purposes.
* **Shipping City**: Enter your shipping city for accurate delivery.
* **Shipping State**: Enter your shipping state for accurate delivery.
* **Shipping Zip**: Enter your shipping zip code for accurate delivery.
* **Billing City**: Enter your billing city for accurate delivery.
* **Billing State**: Enter your billing state for accurate delivery.
* **Billing Zip**: Enter your billing zip code for accurate delivery.
* **Order Date**: Optionally, enter the date of the order.
* **Order Time**: Optionally, enter the time of the order.
* **Order Status**: Select the order status by choosing **In Progress**, **Completed**, or **Canceled**.
* **Assigned User**: Optionally, enter the assigned user for your order.
* **Created At**: Displays the date and time the order was created.
* **Updated At**: Displays the date and time the order was last updated.
* **Assigned Tool**: Optionally, enter the tool assigned to your order.
* **Form ID**: Optionally, enter a form ID for tracking purposes.
* **Output File**: Optionally, enter an output file for your order.
* **Form Title**: Optionally, enter a title for your order.
* **Order Notes**: Optionally, enter any additional notes regarding your order.
