# Nail On Hand Stock Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nail On Hand Stock Order Form is designed to collect essential information about a customer's stock order. This form helps the sales team to efficiently track and manage stock orders, enabling accurate inventory tracking and timely product delivery to customers. 

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Type the customer's email address in the "Email" field.
3. Select the order date from the calendar in the "Order Date" field.
4. Enter the quantity of the product being ordered in the "Quantity" field.
5. Enter the name of the product being ordered in the "Product Name" field.
6. Enter the name of the supplier providing the product in the "Supplier Name" field.
7. Enter the customer's shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field.
8. Enter the city of shipment in the "Shipping City" field.
9. Enter the state of shipment in the "Shipping State" field.
10. Enter the shipping zip code in the "Shipping Zip" field.
11. Select the country of shipment in the "Shipping Country" field.
12. Choose a payment method from the "Payment Method" select list.
13. Optionally, add any additional notes about the order in the "Order Notes" field.
14. Identify the sales representative assigned to this order in the "Assigned User" field.
15. Confirm that this order was created by the sales representative who filled out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer placing the order.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the customer.
* **Order Date**: Select the date when the order was placed.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of products being ordered.
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product being ordered.
* **Supplier Name**: Enter the name of the supplier providing the product.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the customer's shipping address.
* **Shipping City**: Enter the city where the order will be shipped.
* **Shipping State**: Enter the state where the order will be shipped.
* **Shipping Zip**: Enter the zip code where the order will be shipped.
* **Shipping Country**: Select the country where the order will be shipped.
* **Payment Method**: Choose the payment method for this order.
* **Order Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the order.
* **Assigned User**: Identify the sales representative assigned to this order.
* **Created By**: Confirm that this order was created by the sales representative who filled out the form.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
* Double-check the information entered to avoid errors.
* Save your progress frequently to avoid losing unsaved changes.
