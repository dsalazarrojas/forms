# Title Insurance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Title Insurance Request Form is used to collect information for title insurance requests. It ensures that the property's title is valid and free from any defects or liens.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the title insurance request form with accurate and complete information.
2. Select the correct property type (Residential or Commercial).
3. Choose your preferred shipment method (Email or Mail).
4. Select the title insurer (Title Company A or Title Company B).
5. Provide any additional order notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title Insurance Request Form**: Enter a brief description of the title insurance request. 
* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client.
* **Property Address**: Enter the address of the property.
* **Loan Amount**: Enter the amount of the loan.
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property (Residential or Commercial).
* **Shipment Method**: Select how you want to receive the title insurance (Email or Mail).
* **Title Insurer**: Select the title insurance company (Title Company A or Title Company B).
* **Insurance Amount**: Enter the amount of insurance coverage desired.
* **Insurance Type**: Enter the type of insurance coverage desired (optional).
* **Order Notes**: Enter any additional information or notes about the order.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the client (optional).
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the client (optional).
* **Contact Email**: Enter the contact email of the client (optional).
