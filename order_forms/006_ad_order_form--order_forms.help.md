# Ad Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ad Order Form is a tool used to create and manage advertisements for publishing in various media outlets. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure your ad is displayed correctly and reaches its intended audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Ad Order Form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your Advertiser Name and Email Address to identify you as the advertising representative.
2. Provide your company or organization's name if applicable (this is not required).
3. Enter your billing address, city, and ZIP or Postal Code to facilitate payment and delivery.
4. Choose the correct advertisement type (Classified, Display, Announcement, Legal Notice, Obituary, Directory, or Other).
5. Select the desired publication date for your ad.
6. Determine the ad run frequency (One-Time Publication, Weekly for 4 weeks, Weekly for 8 weeks, Monthly for 3 months, Monthly for 6 months, or Monthly for 12 months).
7. Enter the number of words in your advertisement.
8. Write and submit your advertisement copy.
9. Optionally, provide a title or heading for your ad.
10. If relevant, enter a contact number to display in your ad.
11. Select a category for your ad (Real Estate, Jobs, For Sale, Services, Notices, Announcements, or Other).
12. Leave the estimated cost blank, as it will be calculated based on word count and frequency.
13. Choose your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Check, Bank Transfer, Cash, or Invoice).
14. If necessary, provide any special requests for your ad (e.g., bold, italics, or other formatting).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Advertise Name** (`advertiser_name`, text, required): Enter your or your company's name as the advertiser.
* **Email Address** (`advertiser_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication and payment purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`advertiser_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact and payment purposes.
* **Company or Organization Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter your company or organization's name if applicable.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, text, required): Enter your billing address to facilitate payment and delivery.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where you want your ad to be published.
* **State or Province** (`state_province`, text, required): Enter the state or province where you want your ad to be published.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter the ZIP or Postal Code for your billing address.
* **Advertisement Type** (`ad_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of advertisement you want to create (Classified, Display, Announcement, Legal Notice, Obituary, Directory, or Other).
* **Desired Publication Date** (`publication_date`, date, required): Choose the date you want your ad to be published.
* **Run Frequency** (`run_frequency`, select_one, required): Determine how many times you want your ad to run (One-Time Publication, Weekly for 4 weeks, Weekly for 8 weeks, Monthly for 3 months, Monthly for 6 months, or Monthly for 12 months).
* **Word Count** (`word_count`, number, required): Enter the number of words in your advertisement.
* **Advertisement Copy** (`ad_copy`, text, required): Enter the text of your advertisement.
* **Title or Heading** (`heading`, text, optional): Enter a title or heading for your ad.
* **Contact Number to Display** (`phone_number_in_ad`, text, optional): Enter a contact number to display in your ad.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Choose a category for your ad (Real Estate, Jobs, For Sale, Services, Notices, Announcements, or Other).
* **Estimated Cost** (`estimated_cost`, number, optional): This field will be calculated based on word count and frequency.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Check, Bank Transfer, Cash, or Invoice).
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any special requests for your ad (e.g., bold, italics, or other formatting).
