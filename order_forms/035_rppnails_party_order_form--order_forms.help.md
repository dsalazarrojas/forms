# RPPNails PARTY Order Form! - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect party information, user details, and other relevant details for planning a party. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your information in the **User Information** section.
2. Provide details about the party in the **PARTY Information** section.
3. Specify the **Party Date** and **Start Time** for the party.
4. Choose the **Party Type** from the available options.
5. Enter any additional **Party Details**.
6. Select the **ASSIGNED TOOL** from the available options.
7. Enter any **PARTY Notes** (up to 12 fields).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required): Enter your name and contact information.
- **PARTY Information** (`party_info`, text, required): Describe the party in a few words.
- **Party Date** (`party_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the party.
- **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the party.
- **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of the party.
- **PARTY Details** (`party_details`, text, required): Add any additional details about the party.
- **PARTY Type** (`party_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of party from the available options.
- **Additional PARTY Details** (`party_details_2`, text, required): Enter any extra details.
- **PARTY Address** (`party_details_3`, text, required): Enter the address of the party.
- **PHONE** (`party_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the party host.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the party host.
- **ASSIGNED TOOL** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required): Choose the tool assigned to the party from the available options.
- **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, required): Submit the party details.
- **PARTY Notes** (`party_notes`, text, required): Enter any notes about the party (1/12).
- **Additional PARTY Notes** (`party_notes_2`, text, required): Enter any additional notes.
- **PARTY Notes 2** (`party_notes_3`, text, required): Enter any notes about the party.
- **More PARTY Notes** (`party_notes_4`, text, required): Enter additional notes.
- **Last PARTY Notes** (`party_notes_5`, text, required): Enter the last notes.
- **PARTY Notes 3** (`party_notes_6`, text, required): Enter any notes.
- **PARTY Notes 4** (`party_notes_7`, text, required): Enter any notes.

- **PARTY Notes 6** (`party_notes_9`, text, required): Enter any notes.
