# Retail Snack Shelf Display Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to manage and order snacking products for retailers and their suppliers. It collects information about the retailer's store, the supplier's details, and order specifics like delivery date, time, and quantity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of form you want to create or manage: 
	* Order Forms
	* Delivery Forms
	* Merchandising Forms
2. Enter your store information: 
	* Store name
	* Store address
	* Store telephone number
	* Store email
3. Enter your supplier information: 
	* Supplier name
	* Supplier address
	* Supplier telephone number
	* Supplier email
4. Select the order details:
	* Delivery date
	* Delivery time
	* Delivery note
	* Merchandising quantity

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **order-forms** (Category, Select One)
Select the type of form you want to create or manage. This option helps to categorize and filter forms for easier searching and management.

### 2. **form_title** (Title, Text)
Enter a title for the form, such as "Snack Order Form for Retailer XYZ".

### 3. **description** (Description, Text)
Enter a brief description of the form, explaining its purpose and relevance.

### 4. **store_name** (Store Name, Text)
Enter your store name, as this will be displayed in the form title.

### 5. **store_address** (Store Address, Text)
Enter your store address, which will be used for delivery and communication purposes.

### 6. **store_telephone** (Store Telephone, Text)
Enter your store telephone number, which will be used for contact and communication purposes.

### 7. **store_email** (Store Email, Text)
Enter your store email address, which will be used for contact and communication purposes.

### 8. **supplier_name** (Supplier Name, Text)
Enter the supplier's name, who will be delivering the snacking products.

### 9. **supplier_address** (Supplier Address, Text)
Enter the supplier's address, which will be used for delivery and communication purposes.

### 10. **supplier_telephone** (Supplier Telephone, Text)
Enter the supplier's telephone number, which will be used for contact and communication purposes.

### 11. **supplier_email** (Supplier Email, Text)
Enter the supplier's email address, which will be used for contact and communication purposes.

### 12. **delivery_date** (Delivery Date, Date)
Select the date you would like to receive the snacking products.

### 13. **delivery_time** (Delivery Time, Time)
Select the time you would like to receive the snacking products.

### 14. **delivery_note** (Delivery Note, Note)
Enter any special instructions or notes about the delivery.

### 15. **merchandising_quantity** (Merchandising Quantity, Number)
Enter the quantity of snacking products to be delivered.

This help guide explains how to complete the Retail Snack Shelf Display Order Form, which is used for managing snacking products orders between retailers and suppliers. By understanding the form's purpose and each field's purpose, users can efficiently and accurately complete the form, reducing errors and improving overall efficiency.
