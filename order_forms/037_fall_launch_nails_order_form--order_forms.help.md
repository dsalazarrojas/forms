# Fall Launch Nails Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fall Launch Nails Order Form is designed to collect order information, select products, and gather customer contact details. This guide will walk you through each section of the form, explaining what to expect and how to fill out the form correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the "Order Information" section with any relevant details about your order.
2. In the "Select Products" section, choose the products you would like to order.
3. Enter the name of the product you have selected in the "Nail Product Name" field.
4. Choose the color of the nail product you have selected from the options provided.
5. Select the size of the nail product you have selected from the options provided.
6. Enter the quantity of the nail product you would like to order.
7. Enter the price of the nail product you have selected.
8. If applicable, enter a discount code or select "Yes" if you have already been provided with a discount.
9. Enter the total cost of the order.
10. Choose the payment method for your order (e.g. credit card, cash, etc.).
11. Enter any additional comments or notes about your order.
12. Enter your email address.
13. Enter your phone number.
14. Enter your address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Information (1)**: This section is for any additional comments or details about your order.
* **Select Products (2)**: Choose the products you would like to order.
* **Nail Product Name (3)**: Enter the name of the product you have selected.
* **Nail Color (4)**: Choose the color of the nail product you have selected.
* **Nail Size (5)**: Choose the size of the nail product you have selected.
* **Quantity (6)**: Enter the quantity of the nail product you would like to order.
* **Price (7)**: Enter the price of the nail product you have selected.
* **Discount (8)**: If you have already been provided with a discount, select "Yes" here. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Total (9)**: Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Payment Method (10)**: Choose the payment method for your order (e.g. credit card, cash, etc.).
* **Comments (11)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Email (12)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (13)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address (14)**: Enter your address.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your order is processed correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* For any special requests or instructions, please enter them in the "Comments" field.
