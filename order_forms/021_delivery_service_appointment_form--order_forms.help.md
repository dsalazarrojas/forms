# delivery_service_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer information and delivery schedule preferences for delivery services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your customer details in the first field.
2. Select a delivery date for your appointment.
3. Enter any special instructions for your delivery service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details** (customer_details, text, required: false): Enter your customer details, such as name and contact information.
* **Delivery Date** (delivery_date, date, required: false): Select a date for your delivery appointment.
* **Special Instructions** (special_instructions, text, required: false): Enter any specific instructions for your delivery service, such as "please bring up the stairs" or "leave in a specific location".

## Tips
* Please ensure you have provided accurate and relevant information for your delivery service appointment.
* Double-check your delivery date to avoid any last-minute issues.
* If you have any special instructions, please enter them clearly and concisely for our delivery staff to follow.
