# Halloween Candy Gram Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for placing a Halloween candy gram order, which is a small gift of candy given to express appreciation or celebration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your information and selections. Make sure to provide accurate details, especially the address and phone number, as this information will be used to contact you and fulfill your order correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it should be displayed on the order.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it should be displayed on the order.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address. This will be used to contact you regarding your order.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for further information.
* **Select First** (select_one, required): Choose the primary item from the options provided.
* **Select Multiple** (select_multiple, required): Select any additional items to include with the primary selection.
* **Date** (date, required): Enter the date for when you would like the order to be delivered or sent.
* **Note** (note, required): Enter any special instructions or notes for the candy gram, such as a birthday message or gift message.
* **Assigned Tool** (text, required): Enter the name of the person or team that will be assigned to fulfill your order.
* **Category** (select_one, required): Choose the category for the candy gram.
* **Address** (text, required): Enter the address where the candy gram should be delivered or sent.
* **Quantity** (number, required): Enter the quantity of the candy gram to be sent.
* **Description** (text, required): Enter any additional information or message to be included with the candy gram.
