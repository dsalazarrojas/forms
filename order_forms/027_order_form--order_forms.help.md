## Purpose
The Order Form is designed to collect information from customers for processing orders. It is used by e-commerce or retail businesses to gather customer details, order info, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer details, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide your address and delivery details.
3. Enter your order information, including order items and payment method.
4. Choose your preferred shipping method and delivery time.
5. Enter any additional contact information, such as your email and phone number.
6. Add any customer notes or comments.
7. Select your preferred payment method and order status.
8. Review and submit the form to complete your order.
