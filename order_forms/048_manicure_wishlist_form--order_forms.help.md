# manicure_wishlist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your preferences for a manicure treatment. Please provide your choices to ensure we can provide you with the best possible experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred options for each field, or leave them blank if none are applicable.
2. Fill out any text fields, such as `special_requests` and `additional_comments`.
3. Confirm that you have reviewed and agree with your selected options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wishlist Items**: Please select all the items you are interested in for your manicure treatment.
* **Quantity**: Indicate how many of each item you would like to have.
* **Manicure Style**: Choose your preferred style for the treatment.
* **Color Scheme**: Select the color scheme that suits your preference.
* **Manicure Color**: Choose the color you would like for your treatment.
* **Manicure Nails**: Select the type of nail design you are interested in.
* **Nail Polish**: Choose the type of nail polish you would like to have.
* **Manicure Length**: Select the length of the treatment you are comfortable with.
* **Manicure Price**: Indicate the price range you are willing to pay for the treatment.
* **Special Requests**: If you have any special requests, please provide them here.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any further comments or questions, please provide them here.
* **Confirm Wishlist**: Confirm that this is your preferred wishlist.
* **Confirm Order**: Confirm that this is your preferred order.
* **Review**: Review your selected options to ensure they are correct.

This form will help us understand your preferences and provide you with a personalized experience. Please take your time and fill it out accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
