# Bartending Service Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about an event's details, including client information, event schedule, and service requirements. The form will help our team prepare for the event and ensure that we can provide the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name or the name of the client.
2. Enter the date of the event.
3. Provide the address of the event location.
4. Enter the start and end times of the service period.
5. Estimate the number of guests attending the event.
6. Choose the bar package that suits your needs.
7. Specify the number of bartenders you require (if necessary).
8. Confirm if you need setup and teardown services.
9. Choose your preferred glassware type.
10. Enter the total contract amount.
11. Confirm the deposit amount paid.
12. Confirm the 48-hour cancellation policy.
13. Sign and date the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name or Organization** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the client or the name of your organization.
* **Event Date** (2, date, required): Enter the date of the event in the required format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Event Location Address** (3, text, required): Enter the address of the event location.
* **Service Start Time** (4, time, required): Enter the start time of the service period in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Service End Time** (5, time, required): Enter the end time of the service period in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Estimated Number of Guests** (6, number, required): Estimate the number of guests attending the event.
* **Selected Bar Package** (7, select_one, required): Choose one of the following bar packages:
	+ Beer and Wine Only
	+ Standard Full Bar
	+ Premium Full Bar
	+ Signature Cocktails Package
* **Number of Bartenders Requested** (8, number, optional): Specify the number of bartenders you require. If not specified, we will assume the standard number of bartenders.
* **Do you require setup and teardown services?** (9, select_one, required): Confirm if you need setup and teardown services:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Glassware Preference** (10, select_one, required): Choose one of the following glassware preferences:
	+ Disposable Plastic
	+ Rental Glassware
	+ Provided by Client
* **Total Agreed Contract Amount** (11, number, required): Enter the total amount agreed upon in the contract.
* **Deposit Amount Paid** (12, number, required): Enter the deposit amount paid.
* **I acknowledge the 48-hour cancellation policy** (13, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand and accept the 48-hour cancellation policy:
	+ I Accept
	+ Other
* **Date of Agreement** (14, date, required): Sign and date the agreement.

Note: Make sure to review all fields carefully before submitting the form.
