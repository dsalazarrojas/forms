# Custom Carpeting Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for custom carpeting orders, including customer details, carpet specifications, and order status updates.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill in the Customer Name, Contact Number, Email, and Customer Email fields** with the relevant information for your order.
2. **Select the Carpet Type** to indicate whether you want a Rug or a Carpet.
3. **Enter the Custom Dimensions** if your order requires a non-standard size.
4. **Choose the Carpet Color** that suits your preferences.
5. **Set the Delivery Date and Time** for the order, if applicable.
6. **Enter the Custom Carpet Price, Custom Carpet Width Price, Custom Carpet Length Price, and Custom Carpet Total Price** with accurate values.
7. **Update the Order Status** to reflect the current status of your order.
8. **Add any Special Requirements or Custom Order Comments** as needed.
9. **Finally, provide your **Customer Signature** for verification purposes.**

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the person placing the order.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the phone number of the person or business placing the order.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the person or business placing the order.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the email address of the person or business placing the order. (Note: This is different from the contact email, which may be an alternate contact method.)
* **Custom Dimensions**: Enter the custom dimensions for your order if it's not a standard size.
* **Carpet Type**: Select between "Rug" or "Carpet" to indicate the type of product needed.
* **Carpet Width**: Enter the width of the carpet in the required units (e.g., feet, yards, etc.).
* **Carpet Length**: Enter the length of the carpet in the required units (e.g., feet, yards, etc.).
* **Custom Message**: Enter any additional comments or instructions for your order.
* **Special Requirements**: Enter any special requirements or notes for your order.
* **Report Date**: The date the order was reported (automatically populated by the system).
* **Order Date**: The date the order was placed (automatically populated by the system).
* **Carpet Color**: Select one or more colors for the carpet from the provided options.
* **Custom Order Comments**: Enter any additional comments or instructions for your order.
* **Custom Order Notes**: Enter any additional notes or information for your order.
* **Customer Signature**: Enter your signature for verification purposes.
* **Order Status**: Select the current status of your order from the provided options.
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the date for delivery of the order, if applicable.
* **Delivery Time**: Enter the time for delivery of the order, if applicable.
* **Custom Carpet Price**: Enter the custom price for your order.
* **Custom Carpet Width Price**: Enter the custom width price for your order.
* **Custom Carpet Length Price**: Enter the custom length price for your order.
* **Custom Carpet Total Price**: Enter the total custom price for your order.
* **Custom Order Status**: Select the status of your custom order from the provided options.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the name of the tool or person assigned to handle your order.
