# Annual Inventory Clearance Sale Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers place orders during the annual inventory clearance sale. It collects necessary information for processing the order and provides options for customers to choose their preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your contact information: 
   - Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
   - Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
   - Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
   - If you are a business customer, enter your company name in the "Company Name" field.

2. Select your sale preferences: 
   - Choose the year of the inventory clearance sale you are interested in from the "Sale Year" dropdown menu.
   - Select your customer type from the "Customer Type" dropdown menu (Retail, Wholesale, Business, Reseller, or Other).

3. Choose your product preferences: 
   - Select the categories of products you are interested in from the "Product Category" multi-select field.
   - List the items you want to order in the "Item Selection" field.
   - Enter the quantity of each item in the "Quantities" field.

4. Select your delivery and payment preferences: 
   - Choose your preferred delivery method from the "Delivery Method" dropdown menu (Standard Shipping, Express Shipping, Store Pickup, Warehouse Pickup, or Freight Delivery).
   - Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu (Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, Check, or Invoice).
   - If you are picking up your order, choose your preferred pickup date from the "Preferred Pickup Date" field.
   - If you are shipping your order, choose your preferred delivery date from the "Preferred Delivery Date" field.

5. Review and accept terms: 
   - Read and agree to the terms and conditions by selecting "Yes, I Accept" in the "Terms and Conditions" field.
   - If you are interested in receiving marketing communications, select "Yes, Please" in the "Marketing Communications" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Your full name as it should appear on the order and any communication.
* **Email Address**: Your email address for order confirmation and any communication.
* **Phone Number**: Your best contact number for any questions or updates.
* **Company Name**: If you are a business customer, enter your company name. (Optional)
* **Billing Address**: Your full billing address for payment and invoicing purposes.
* **Shipping Address**: Your full shipping address where the order will be delivered.
* **Sale Year**: Choose the year of the inventory clearance sale you are interested in.
* **Customer Type**: Select your type of customer (Retail, Wholesale, Business, Reseller, or Other).
* **Product Category**: Select the categories of products you are interested in.
* **Item Selection**: List the items you want to order.
* **Quantities**: Enter the quantity of each item.
* **Size and Color Preferences**: If applicable, enter any size and color preferences for your order. (Optional)
* **Acceptable Condition**: Choose the condition of the items you are willing to accept (New Only, New or Open Box, New or Refurbished, or Any Condition).
* **Minimum Discount**: Select the minimum discount you are looking for.
* **Order Total**: Enter the estimated total amount of your order. (Optional)
* **Delivery Method**: Choose your preferred delivery method.
* **Preferred Pickup Date**: Choose your preferred pickup date if you are picking up your order. (Optional)
* **Preferred Delivery Date**: Choose your preferred delivery date if you are shipping your order. (Optional)
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Marketing Communications**: Select if you are interested in receiving marketing communications.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any other information or comments about your order. (Optional)
* **Bulk Discount**: Select if you are ordering in bulk.
* **Special Instructions**: Enter any special requests or instructions for your order. (Optional)
