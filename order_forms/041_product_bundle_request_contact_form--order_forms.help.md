# Product Bundle Request Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers or internal staff to request a product bundle. It will help us understand your requirements and facilitate the process of getting you the bundle you need.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Select the correct options from the dropdown menus.
3. Provide a clear and concise description in the text fields.
4. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name for our records.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name for our records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address so we can contact you about your product bundle request.
* **Use Case** (`use_case`, text, optional): Describe the use case or scenario where the product bundle will be used. This will help us better understand your needs.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Please enter your contact number for us to get in touch with you about your request.
* **Product Bundle** (`product_bundle`, text, optional): Describe the product bundle you are requesting, including any specific products or features you require.
* **SaaS Provider** (`saas_provider`, select_one, required): Choose the SaaS provider from the dropdown menu. This will help us narrow down the options for your bundle.
* **Agency** (`agency`, select_multiple, required): Select all the agencies that will be using the product bundle.
* **Team** (`team`, select_one, optional): If you have a specific team in mind, select the team from the dropdown menu.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, date, optional): If you would like to be contacted about your bundle request after a specific date, enter the date in this field.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, note, optional): If you have any additional information or next steps regarding your request, please describe them in this field.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about your product bundle request.
* **Follow Up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): If you would like to be followed up about your request after a specific date, enter the date in this field.
* **Lead Qualification** (`lead_qualification`, select_one, required): Choose the lead qualification from the dropdown menu to help us better understand your request.
