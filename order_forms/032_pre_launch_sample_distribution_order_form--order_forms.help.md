# Pre Launch Sample Distribution Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Launch Sample Distribution Order Form is designed to collect information for pre-launch sample distribution orders. This form will help the distribution process by gathering customer and order details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer information.
2. Provide contact details.
3. Enter order details, including order date and time.
4. Specify shipping details.
5. Select sample requests.
6. Identify the submitter.
7. Enter email and phone number (if available).
8. Add any notes or comments.
9. Confirm the order.
10. Sign and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required/optional): Please enter the customer's name and relevant contact information.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): Provide the customer's contact details.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the order details, including any special instructions.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the order.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, required/optional): Specify the shipping details.
* **Sample Requests** (`sample_requests`, select multiple, required/optional): Select the samples requested.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, required/optional): Identify the person submitting the order.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's phone number (if available).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, select one, required/optional): Confirm the order details.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required/optional): Sign and date the form.
* **Customer Info 2** (`customer_info_2`, text, required/optional): Repeat customer information for secondary purposes.
* **Contact Details 2** (`contact_details_2`, text, required/optional): Repeat contact details.
* **Order Details 2** (`order_details_2`, text, required/optional): Repeat order details.
* **Order Date 2** (`order_date_2`, date, required/optional): Repeat order date.
* **Order Time 2** (`order_time_2`, time, required/optional): Repeat order time.
* **Shipping Details 2** (`shipping_details_2`, text, required/optional): Repeat shipping details.
* **Sample Requests 2** (`sample_requests_2`, select multiple, required/optional): Repeat sample requests.
* **Submitter 2** (`submitter_2`, text, required/optional): Repeat submitter information.
* **Email 2** (`email_2`, email, required/optional): Repeat email address.
* **Phone 2** (`phone_2`, text, required/optional): Repeat phone number (if available).
* **Customer Info 2** (`customer_info_2`, text, required/optional): Repeat customer information.
* **Contact Details 2** (`contact_details_2`, text, required/optional): Repeat contact details.

## Tips
- Please fill in the form accurately and carefully.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant department.
- Review your responses before submission to avoid duplication of effort.
