# Frame Order Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Frame Order Submission Form is a form used to collect information about frame orders, including frame details, artwork upload, and delivery preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the required details about the frame you are ordering, including any necessary information in the Frame Order Details section.
2. Upload the artwork associated with the frame, either by using the Artwork Upload field or providing a URL.
3. Choose your delivery preference from the available options.
4. Enter your delivery information, including your name, email, phone number, address, city, state, and zip code.
5. Optionally, add any notes about your delivery in the Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Frame Order Details** (Frame Order Details): This section collects required information about the frame, but it is optional, so you can skip this section if you don't want to provide any additional details.
* **Artwork Upload** (Artwork Upload): Upload your artwork for the frame, or provide the URL if you want to link it instead.
* **Delivery Preferences** (Select your delivery preference): Choose between In-Store Pickup or Delivery for your frame order.
* **Name** (Name): Enter your name for delivery purposes.
* **Email** (Email): Provide your email address for delivery purposes.
* **Phone** (Phone): Enter your phone number for delivery purposes.
* **Address** (Address): Enter your delivery address.
* **City** (City): Enter the city for your delivery address.
* **State** (State): Enter the state for your delivery address.
* **Zip** (Zip): Enter the zip code for your delivery address.
* **Notes** (Notes): Add any additional notes about your delivery.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure smooth delivery of your frame order.
* If you have any special requests or questions, you can add them in the Notes field.
* If you are choosing delivery, double-check that your delivery information is accurate and up-to-date.
