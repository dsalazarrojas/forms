# Franchise Marketing Material Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to order marketing materials from the marketing team. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your request is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with the required information.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for each field.
3. Review your submission carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requested materials** (`franchise_marketing_material_order_form_1`): Enter the type and quantity of marketing materials you need. This could include brochures, flyers, or other promotional materials.
* **Customization needs** (`franchise_marketing_material_order_form_2`): Select "Yes" if you need any customizations made to the marketing materials, such as text or image changes. Select "No" if you don't need any customizations.
* **Location** (`franchise_marketing_material_order_form_3`): Enter the location where the marketing materials should be delivered.
* **Number of copies** (`franchise_marketing_material_order_form_4`): Enter the number of copies of the marketing materials you need.
* **Delivery date** (`franchise_marketing_material_order_form_5`): Select a date for when you would like to receive the marketing materials.
* **Delivery time** (`franchise_marketing_material_order_form_6`): Select a time for when you would like to receive the marketing materials.
* **Comments** (`franchise_marketing_material_order_form_7`): Enter any additional comments or instructions for the marketing team.
* **Email** (`franchise_marketing_material_order_form_8`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`franchise_marketing_material_order_form_9`): Enter your phone number.
* **Requester's name** (`franchise_marketing_material_order_form_10`): Enter your name.
* **Requester's title** (`franchise_marketing_material_order_form_11`): Enter your title.
* **Requester's department** (`franchise_marketing_material_order_form_12`): Select your department. Options: "Yes", "No".
* **Requester's role** (`franchise_marketing_material_order_form_13`): Select your role. Options: "Yes", "No".
* **Requester's email** (`franchise_marketing_material_order_form_14`): Enter your email address.
* **Requester's phone** (`franchise_marketing_material_order_form_15`): Enter your phone number.
* **Location** (`franchise_marketing_material_order_form_16`): Enter the location where the marketing materials will be used.
* **Address** (`franchise_marketing_material_order_form_17`): Enter the address where the marketing materials should be delivered.
* **City** (`franchise_marketing_material_order_form_18`): Enter the city where the marketing materials will be used.
* **Zip** (`franchise_marketing_material_order_form_19`): Enter the zip code where the marketing materials should be delivered.
* **State** (`franchise_marketing_material_order_form_20`): Enter the state where the marketing materials will be used.
* **Country** (`franchise_marketing_material_order_form_21`): Enter the country where the marketing materials will be used.
* **Comments** (`franchise_marketing_material_order_form_22`): Enter any additional comments or instructions for the marketing team.
* **Marketing team notes** (`franchise_marketing_material_order_form_23`): Enter any additional information for the marketing team.
* **Marketing team** (`franchise_marketing_material_order_form_24`): Enter the name of the marketing team member handling your request.
* **Submission** (`franchise_marketing_material_order_form_25`): Select "Yes" to submit your request.
