# Amphibian Specimen Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for ordering amphibian specimens from our institution. It is intended for individuals and institutions to order amphibian specimens for educational or research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in each section:
	* Section 1: Order Information
	* Section 2: Institution Information
	* Section 3: Contact Information
	* Section 4: Shipping Information
	* Section 5: Specimen Information
	* Section 6: Quality and Quality Requirements
	* Section 7: Educational Use
	* Section 8: Shipping Preferences
	* Section 9: Payment Information
	* Section 10: Permits and Compliance
	* Section 11: Additional Information
2. Review each section carefully and ensure all required fields are filled in.
3. Double-check for any special requests or additional information needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Section 1: Order Information
* **Order Date**: The date you are requesting the specimens to be delivered.
* **Order Number**: A reference number for your order, for our office use only.
* **Section 2: Institution Information**
* **Institution Name**: The name of your institution or organization.
* **Institution Type**: The type of institution or organization (e.g., K-12 School, College/University, Museum, etc.).
* **Department**: The department within your institution related to the specimens.
* **Section 3: Contact Information**
* **Contact First Name**: The first name of the contact person.
* **Contact Last Name**: The last name of the contact person.
* **Contact Title/Position**: The title or position of the contact person.
* **Email Address**: The email address of the contact person, for order confirmation.
* **Phone Number**: The phone number of the contact person.
* **Section 4: Shipping Information**
* **Street Address**: The shipping address of the specimens.
* **City**: The city where the specimens will be shipped from.
* **State/Province**: The state or province where the specimens will be shipped from.
* **ZIP/Postal Code**: The postal code where the specimens will be shipped from.
* **Country**: The country where the specimens will be shipped from.
* **Section 5: Specimen Information**
* **Type of Specimens**: The type of specimens you are requesting (e.g., preserved frogs, preserved toads, etc.).
* **Frog Species Requested**: The species of frog you are requesting, if applicable.
* **Salamander Species Requested**: The species of salamander you are requesting, if applicable.
* **Reptile Species Requested**: The species of reptile you are requesting, if applicable.
* **Quantity Needed**: The number of specimens you need.
* **Preferred Specimen Size**: The preferred size of the specimens (e.g., small, medium, large).
* **Preservation Method**: The method of preservation you prefer (e.g., formalin, alcohol, etc.).
* **Section 6: Quality Requirements**
* **Quality Grade**: The quality level you require (e.g., premium, standard, economy).
* **Intact Specimens**: Whether the specimens need to be intact or have minor defects.
* **Labeling Requirements**: The labeling requirements for the specimens (e.g., species name only, scientific and common name, etc.).
* **Section 7: Educational Use**
* **Intended Use**: The intended use of the specimens (e.g., classroom demonstration, research, etc.).
* **Grade/Education Level**: The level of education or grade of the students using the specimens.
* **Approximate Number of Students**: The approximate number of students using the specimens, if applicable.
* **Section 8: Shipping Preferences**
* **Shipping Method**: The shipping method you prefer (e.g., standard ground, expedited, etc.).
* **Required Delivery Date**: The specific date you need the specimens delivered, if applicable.
* **Section 9: Payment Information**
* **Payment Method**: The payment method you prefer (e.g., purchase order, credit card, etc.).
* **Purchase Order Number**: The purchase order number, if applicable.
* **Billing Address**: The billing address, if different from the shipping address.
* **Section 10: Permits and Compliance**
* **Do You Have Required Permits**: Whether you have the required permits for the specimens.
* **Permit Number**: The permit number, if applicable.
* **Institutional Approval Obtained**: Whether you have obtained the required institutional approval, if applicable.
* **Section 11: Additional Information**
* **Additional Materials Needed**: Any additional materials needed for the specimens (e.g., dissection kits, etc.).
* **Special Requests**: Any special requests or requirements for the specimens.
* **Section 12: Authorization**
* **Authorized By**: The name of the authorized person.
* **Authorization Signature**: The signature of the authorized person.
* **Authorization Date**: The date of authorization.
