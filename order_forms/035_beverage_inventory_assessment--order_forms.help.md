# Beverage Inventory Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beverage Inventory Assessment form is designed to help bar and restaurant establishments assess their current stock levels of various beverages, including beer, wine, and spirits. This form helps identify inventory status, products that need reordering, and potential storage issues.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your business name and contact information on the first page.
2. Select the type of inventory assessment you are conducting (e.g., Full Inventory, Partial Inventory, etc.).
3. For each category (beer, wine, and spirits), select the current stock level and list any products that need reordering.
4. Identify any expired products, storage issues, or notes about suppliers.
5. Specify the urgency of the reorder (e.g., Immediate, High, Medium, Low).
6. Estimate the approximate cost of the needed inventory.
7. Provide any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of your bar or restaurant.
* **Contact Person Name** (2, text, required): Enter the full name of the person completing the assessment.
* **Email Address** (3, email, required): Enter the email address where you would like to receive inventory reports.
* **Phone Number** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number for inventory coordination.
* **Assessment Date** (5, date, required): Enter the date of this inventory assessment.
* **Inventory Type** (6, select_one, required): Select the type of inventory assessment you are conducting (e.g., Full Inventory, Partial Inventory, etc.).
* **Beer Inventory Status** (7, select_one, required): Select the current status of your beer stock level (e.g., Well Stocked, Adequate, Running Low, Needs Reorder, Out of Stock).
* **Beer Items to Reorder** (8, text, required): List the beer products that need reordering.
* **Wine Inventory Status** (9, select_one, required): Select the current status of your wine stock level (e.g., Well Stocked, Adequate, Running Low, Needs Reorder, Out of Stock).
* **Wine Items to Reorder** (10, text, required): List the wine products that need reordering.
* **Spirits Inventory Status** (11, select_one, required): Select the current status of your spirits stock level (e.g., Well Stocked, Adequate, Running Low, Needs Reorder, Out of Stock).
* **Spirits Items to Reorder** (12, text, required): List the spirits products that need reordering.
* **Mixer Inventory Status** (13, select_one, required): Select the current status of your mixer and soda stock level (e.g., Well Stocked, Adequate, Running Low, Needs Reorder, Out of Stock).
* **Mixer Items to Reorder** (14, text, required): List the mixers that need reordering.
* **Non-Alcoholic Inventory Status** (15, select_one, required): Select the current status of your non-alcoholic beverage stock (e.g., Well Stocked, Adequate, Running Low, Needs Reorder, Out of Stock).
* **Non-Alcoholic Items to Reorder** (16, text, required): List the non-alcoholic products that need reordering.
* **Storage Issues** (17, text, required): Describe any storage problems you are experiencing.
* **Expired Products Found** (18, text, required): List any expired items discovered.
* **Reorder Priority** (19, select_one, required): Select the urgency of reordering (e.g., Immediate, High, Medium, Low).
* **Estimated Order Value** (20, select_one, required): Estimate the approximate cost of the needed inventory (e.g., Under $500, $500 to $1,000, etc.).
* **Supplier Notes** (21, text, required): Enter any notes about suppliers or deliveries.
* **Additional Comments** (22, text, required): Enter any other inventory-related information.

Note: Please fill out this form as accurately as possible to help us provide you with the most accurate inventory reports.
