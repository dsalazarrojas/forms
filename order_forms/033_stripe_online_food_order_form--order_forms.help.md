# Stripe Online Food Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be a simple online ordering system for customers to provide their details and order information. It consists of four pages for collecting customer details, payment information, shipping details, and order specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the 'Start' button to begin the form.
2. On the 'Customer Details' page, enter your name, address, and any other relevant customer information.
3. On the 'Payment Details' page, provide your payment method and any additional payment information.
4. On the 'Shipping Details' page, enter your shipping address and any special shipping instructions.
5. On the 'Order Details' page, describe your order and any specific requests or comments.
6. Review your information on each page and submit the form when ready.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Details
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your name and any other relevant customer information.
### Payment Details
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your payment method (e.g., credit card, PayPal) and any additional payment information.
### Shipping Details
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your shipping address and any special shipping instructions.
### Order Details
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional):
	+ Describe your order and any specific requests or comments.
