# Lipstick Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to place an order for lipstick. It collects relevant customer information and preferences to process their order.

## How To Complete This Form
To place an order, follow these steps:

1. Select the lipstick color from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the quantity of lipstick you would like to order.
3. Enter your order date (if applicable).
4. Provide your contact information, including phone number, name, email, and address.
5. Select your shipping preferences, including country, state, city, and zip code.
6. If applicable, provide shipping address and name.
7. Enter any additional shipping instructions or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lipstick Color** (`lipstick_color`, `select_one`, required): Select the color of lipstick you would like to order.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, optional): Enter the amount of lipstick you would like to order.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you would like to place your order.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your address.
* **Country** (`country`, `select_one`, optional): Select your country of residence.
* **State** (`state`, `select_one`, optional): Select your state or region.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, optional): Enter your city.
* **Zip** (`zip`, `number`, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, `select_one`, optional): Select your preferred shipping method.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, optional): Enter your shipping address.
* **Shipping Name** (`shipping_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the shipping name.
* **Shipping Phone** (`shipping_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the shipping phone number.
* **Shipping Country** (`shipping_country`, `select_one`, optional): Select your shipping country.
* **Shipping State** (`shipping_state`, `select_one`, optional): Select your shipping state or region.
* **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`, `text`, optional): Enter your shipping city.
* **Shipping Zip** (`shipping_zip`, `number`, optional): Enter your shipping zip code.
* **Order Comments 2/3/4/5** (`order_comments2`, `order_comments3`, `order_comments4`, `order_comments5`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your order ( duplicate fields can be removed for brevity).

## Tips
Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form to avoid any errors or delays in processing your order.
