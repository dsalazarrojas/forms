# Door Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Door Order Form is used to collect customer information and order preferences for door orders within the organization. This form is intended for internal use only.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Customer Information** page with the relevant details.
2. Proceed to the **Name** page and enter the customer's name.
3. Next, move on to the **Address** page and enter the customer's address.
4. Select the **Order Date** for the order.
5. Enter the customer's **Email** and **Phone** details.
6. Choose the desired **Order Preference** from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (text): This page is for collecting general customer details.
* **Name** (text, required): Enter the customer's name to associate it with the order.
* **Address** (text): Enter the customer's address to facilitate delivery and communication.
* **Order Date** (date, required): Select the date for the order.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter the customer's email address to facilitate communication.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number for communication purposes.
* **Order Preference** (select_one, optional): Choose the desired order preference from the provided options.
