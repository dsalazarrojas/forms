# Lab Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect order information from lab suppliers. It helps the supplier to gather details of the customer and their requested lab supplies, and it also allows them to provide order items and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Page 1: Customer Information**
    Fill in the customer's name, contact information, and any other relevant details in the first page.
2.  **Page 2: Order Items**
    List the lab supplies you want to order, including their quantities and any specific requests.
3.  **Page 3: Payment Information**
    Enter your payment details, such as the payment method and the amount due.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name** (`customer_info`, text, required): Enter the customer's name.
*   **Contact Information** (`customer_info`, text, required): Enter the customer's contact information (phone number and/or email).
*   **Order Items** (`order_items`, text, required): List the lab supplies you want to order, including their quantities and any specific requests.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_info`, text, required): Choose the payment method (e.g., credit card, bank transfer).
*   **Amount Due** (`payment_info`, text, required): Enter the total amount due for the order.
*   **Additional Instructions** (`payment_info`, text, required): Enter any additional instructions or comments for the supplier.

## Tips

*   Make sure to provide accurate contact information to ensure smooth communication with the supplier.
*   Clearly list the lab supplies you want to order, including their quantities and any specific requests.
*   Confirm the payment details carefully to avoid any disputes.
*   If you have any special requests or comments, include them in the "Additional Instructions" field.
