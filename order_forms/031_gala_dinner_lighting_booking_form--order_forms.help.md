# gala_dinner_lighting_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a gala dinner lighting service. It collects information required for arranging lighting for an event, including client details, event schedule, venue address, and other essential details.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the relevant details of the client, such as their name or company name.
2. Choose the event date on which the gala dinner will take place.
3. Provide the address of the venue where the event will be held.
4. Select a suitable lighting package from the options provided.
5. Agree to the terms and conditions of the service.
6. Enter your contact details, including your name, phone number, and email address.
7. Provide any additional event details that may be relevant to the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details** (`Client Details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the details of the client, such as company name or personal name.
* **Event Date** (`Event Date`, `date`, required/optional): Choose the date on which the event will take place.
* **Venue Address** (`Venue Address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the venue where the event will be held.
* **Lighting Package** (`Lighting Package`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select a suitable lighting package from the options provided, which are:
	+ Standard
	+ Premium
	+ Ultimate
* **Terms and Agreement** (`Terms and Agreement`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or more of the options provided to show that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions. Options include:
	+ I have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
	+ Yes, I accept the terms and conditions.
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your details, including your name, company name, or any other relevant information.
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name in case you want to be contacted directly regarding the event.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number so that we can reach you for any further communication.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address so that we can send you any necessary information or updates.
* **Address** (`Address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your address if you want to be contacted at your workplace or office.
* **Event Details** (`Event Details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional details about the event that may be relevant to the service.
* **Venue** (`Venue`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the venue where the event will take place.
* **Event Date** (`Event Date`, `date`, required/optional): Choose the date on which the event will take place.
## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields correctly to avoid any issues with the booking.
* Double-check the details you provide before submission to ensure accuracy.
* Contact us if you need any clarification or have any doubts about the form.
