# Sample Gift Registry For On Hand Stock - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and manage the on-hand stock of items across multiple categories.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the category (Form 1, Form 2, etc.) that the item belongs to.
2. Enter the name of the item.
3. Enter the price of the item.
4. Enter the quantity of the item.
