# supplier_pricing_structure_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a supplier's pricing structure. It is used to gather data on suppliers and their pricing strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and ensure you understand the purpose of each field.
2. Complete each field with the relevant information about the supplier's pricing structure.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Supplier Form** (`1`, `text`, optional): Enter the name or ID of the supplier being evaluated.
- **Pricing Info** (`2`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information about the supplier's pricing structure.
- **Comparison Info** (`3`, `text`, optional): Enter any comparison information about the supplier's pricing structure.
- **Notes** (`4`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes about the supplier's pricing structure.
- **Selected Supplier** (`5`, `select_one`, optional): Select the supplier that you are evaluating (Supplier A, Supplier B, or Supplier C).
- **Selected Date** (`6`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when the supplier's pricing structure was evaluated.
- **Selected Time** (`7`, `time`, optional): Enter the time when the supplier's pricing structure was evaluated.
- **Price** (`8`, `number`, optional): Enter the price of the supplier's product or service.
- **Discount** (`9`, `number`, optional): Enter any discount information about the supplier's pricing structure.
- **Tax Rate** (`10`, `number`, optional): Enter any tax rate information about the supplier's pricing structure.
- **Shipping Rate** (`11`, `number`, optional): Enter any shipping rate information about the supplier's pricing structure.
- **Notes 2** (`12`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes about the supplier's pricing structure.
- **Assigned Tool** (`13`, `text`, optional): Enter the tool or system used to evaluate the supplier's pricing structure.
- **Form ID** (`14`, `number`, optional): Enter the ID of the form submission.
- **Category** (`15`, `text`, optional): Enter the category of the supplier's product or service.
- **Description** (`16`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the supplier's product or service.
- **Output File** (`17`, `text`, optional): Enter the location where the supplier's pricing structure will be output.
- **Form Title** (`18`, `text`, optional): Enter the title of the form.
- **Category Name** (`19`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the category for the supplier.
- **Submitted By** (`20`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
- **Submitted Date** (`21`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the form was submitted.
- **Assigned Tool 2** (`22`, `text`, optional): Enter the tool or system used to evaluate the supplier's pricing structure (if different from `Assigned Tool`).
- **Form ID 2** (`23`, `number`, optional): Enter the ID of the form submission (if different from `Form ID`).
- **Form Title 2** (`24`, `text`, optional): Enter a different title for the form (if necessary).
- **Submitted By 2** (`25`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form (if different from `Submitted By`).
