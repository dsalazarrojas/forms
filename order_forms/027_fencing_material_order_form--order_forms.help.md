# Fencing Material Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fencing Material Order Form is designed to collect information from customers to place a fencing material order with the business. This form helps the customer to specify the type of material, dimensions, and quantity needed for their order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of material you need by choosing from the available options (Wood, Metal, or Vinyl).
2. Enter the width of the material in the required format (e.g., 8 ft, 10 ft, etc.).
3. Enter the length of the material in the required format (e.g., 8 ft, 10 ft, etc.).
4. Enter the height of the material in the required format (e.g., 8 ft, 10 ft, etc.).
5. Specify the quantity of material needed.
6. Enter your name and contact email for communication purposes.
7. Enter your business name and phone number for business-to-business communication purposes.
8. Provide any additional notes about the order in the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of material do you need? (material_type)**: Select the type of material you need for your order (Wood, Metal, or Vinyl).
* **What is the material width? (material_width)**: Enter the width of the material in feet (e.g., 8 ft, 10 ft, etc.).
* **What is the material length? (material_length)**: Enter the length of the material in feet (e.g., 8 ft, 10 ft, etc.).
* **What is the material height? (material_height)**: Enter the height of the material in feet (e.g., 8 ft, 10 ft, etc.).
* **How many pieces do you need? (quantity_ordered)**: Specify the quantity of material needed for your order.
* **What is your name? (customer_name)**: Enter your name for communication purposes.
* **Contact Email (contact_email)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **What is your business name? (business_name)**: Enter your business name for business-to-business communication purposes.
* **Business Phone (business_phone)**: Enter your business phone number for business-to-business communication purposes.
* **Additional notes (order_notes)**: Provide any additional information or special requests for your order.
* **What is the material description? (material_description)**: Enter a brief description of the material you need (e.g., for custom orders or special requirements).
