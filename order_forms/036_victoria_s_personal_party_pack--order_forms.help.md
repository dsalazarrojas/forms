# Victoria's Personal Party Pack - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the user's personal details for a specific purpose. To ensure that you provide accurate and relevant information, please review the form carefully before submitting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the "User Interests" field, where you can provide a brief description of your hobbies and interests.
2. Next, choose the Mystery Product from the dropdown menu. This will help us understand your preferences.
3. If you have more than one Mystery Product to order, please specify the quantity in the Mystery Product Count field.
4. Select the Date and Time of your order.
5. In the Notes field, you can add any additional comments or instructions for your order.
6. Fill in your Contact Information, including Name, Email, Phone, Address, Zip, City, State, Province, and Country Region.
7. Finally, provide your Gender, Age, Language, and Zip Code.
