# Cake Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your preferences regarding cakes, including your favorite flavors, preferred frostings, sweetness levels, texture, and dietary restrictions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please start by providing your information in the "Your Name" and "Email Address" fields.
2. Select your preferred purchase frequency in the "How often do you purchase cakes" dropdown menu.
3. In the "Your Favorite Cake Flavors" section, select all the flavors you enjoy.
4. In the "Flavors You Dislike" section, select all the flavors you don't enjoy.
5. Choose your preferred frosting type and amount in the "Preferred Frosting Type" and "How much frosting do you prefer" select menus.
6. Indicate your preferred sweetness level and cake density in the "Preferred Sweetness Level" and "Cake Density" select menus.
7. Choose whether you prefer cakes with or without filling in the "Do you prefer cake with filling" select menu.
8. In the "Favorite Cake Toppings" section, select all the toppings you enjoy.
9. If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, please select all that apply in the "Dietary Restrictions or Allergies" section.
10. Finally, indicate the types of occasions you purchase cakes for in the "Types of Occasions You Buy Cakes For" section.
11. If you have any additional preferences or suggestions, please type them in the "Additional Preferences or Suggestions" text box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Your Name** (Text): Your name as it appears on the form is required for identification purposes.
- **Email Address** (Email): Your email address is required for any follow-up communication or surveys.
- **How often do you purchase cakes?** (Select One): Choose your preferred purchase frequency from the options provided.
  * Weekly: You purchase cakes every week.
  * Monthly: You purchase cakes once a month.
  * Quarterly: You purchase cakes every 3 months.
  * Annually: You purchase cakes every year.
  * Rarely: You rarely purchase cakes.
- **Your Favorite Cake Flavors** (Select Multiple): Select all the flavors you enjoy, e.g. Chocolate, Vanilla, Strawberry, Red Velvet, etc.
- **Flavors You Dislike** (Select Multiple): Select all the flavors you don't enjoy, e.g. Almond, Coconut, Coffee, Licorice, etc.
- **Preferred Frosting Type** (Select One): Choose your preferred frosting type from the options provided.
  * Buttercream: A classic frosting made from butter and sugar.
  * Cream Cheese: A tangy frosting made from cream cheese and sugar.
  * Chocolate Ganache: A rich, dark chocolate frosting.
  * Fondant: A sweet, decorative frosting.
  * Whipped Cream: A light, airy frosting.
  * No frosting: You don't prefer any frosting.
- **How much frosting do you prefer?** (Select One): Choose your preferred amount of frosting from the options provided.
  * Light: A small amount of frosting is preferred.
  * Moderate: A moderate amount of frosting is preferred.
  * Heavy: A lot of frosting is preferred.
- **Preferred Sweetness Level** (Select One): Choose your preferred sweetness level from the options provided.
  * Not very sweet: You prefer cakes that are not very sweet.
  * Moderately sweet: You prefer cakes that are moderately sweet.
  * Very sweet: You prefer very sweet cakes.
- **Preferred Cake Density** (Select One): Choose your preferred cake density from the options provided.
  * Light and fluffy: A light and fluffy cake is preferred.
  * Moist and dense: A moist and dense cake is preferred.
  * Somewhere in between: A cake that is not too light or too dense is preferred.
- **Do you prefer cake with filling?** (Select One): Choose whether you prefer cakes with filling or not.
  * Yes, definitely: You always prefer cakes with filling.
  * Sometimes: You sometimes prefer cakes with filling.
  * No, plain is better: You prefer plain cakes without filling.
- **Favorite Cake Toppings** (Select Multiple): Select all the toppings you enjoy, e.g. Fresh fruit, Sprinkles, Nuts, Chocolate chips, Caramel, Whipped cream, etc.
- **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (Select Multiple): Select all the dietary restrictions or allergies you have, e.g. Gluten-free, Dairy-free, Vegan, Nut-free, Sugar-free, etc.
- **Types of Occasions You Buy Cakes For** (Select Multiple): Select all the occasions you buy cakes for, e.g. Birthdays, Anniversaries, Weddings, Corporate events, Personal enjoyment, Gifts, etc.
- **Additional Preferences or Suggestions** (Text): If you have any additional preferences or suggestions, please type them here.
