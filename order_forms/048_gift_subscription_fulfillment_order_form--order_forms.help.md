# Gift Subscription Fulfillment Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help businesses process and manage gift subscription orders, including their status and related details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the gift type from the options provided.
2. Enter the subscription period, gift value, and gift recipient details.
3. Enter the business email, phone number, and order ID.
4. Choose the gift subscription status and order status.
5. Select the order date and order value status.
6. If necessary, add any additional notes or comments.
7. Submit the form after filling out all required fields.

* **Subscription Start Date** (`id: 5`): Enter the start date of the subscription.

* **Gift Message** (`id: 8`): Add a message to be sent to the gift recipient.
* **Business Email** (`id: 9`): Enter your business email address.
* **Business Phone** (`id: 10`): Enter your business phone number.
