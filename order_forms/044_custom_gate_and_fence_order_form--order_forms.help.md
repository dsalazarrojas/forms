## Custom Gate And Fence Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect project details and contact information from customers who are interested in ordering custom gates and fences. It is intended to be completed by customers or their representatives to provide the necessary information for our team to process their order.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Project Details** field with the relevant project information.
2. Enter your **Contact Info**, including your name and contact method.
3. Select the type of gate you wish to order (e.g., **Gate**, **Other**).
4. Select the type of fence you wish to order (you may choose multiple options).
5. If you are paying via a different method, please provide the **Payment Details**.
6. Enter your **Home Address** (optional).
7. Enter your **Customer Name**.
8. Enter your **Customer Email**.
9. Enter your **Customer Phone**.
10. Enter a brief **Project Description** (optional).
11. Add any additional **Order Notes** (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details**: Enter the project name, location, and other relevant details.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your name, contact method (email or phone), and other contact information.
* **Gate Type**: Select the type of gate you wish to order (e.g., wooden, metal, etc.).
* **Fence Type**: Select the type(s) of fence you wish to order (e.g., post and panel, etc.).
* **Payment Details**: If you are not paying via bank transfer, enter the relevant payment details (e.g., credit card number, etc.).
* **Home Address**: Enter your home address if you wish to have the order delivered or installed there.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Project Description**: Enter a brief description of the project.
* **Order Notes**: Add any additional notes about your order, such as special requests or concerns.
