# Wedding Flower Order Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you input information about your wedding flowers. You can use it to order or inquire about wedding flowers for your big day.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name.
2. Enter your Email address so we can contact you back.
3. Provide your Phone number in case we need to discuss further.
4. Describe your Ceremony Details (e.g., date, time, location).
5. Choose your Style Preferences (e.g., Classic, Modern, Traditional) for the flower arrangement.
6. Select the type of flowers you'd like to have (e.g., Roses, Lilies, Sunflowers).
7. Decide on the quantity of flowers you'd like to have.
8. Choose the color of the flowers (e.g., Pink, Red, White).
9. If you have any Special Requests (e.g., delivery, setup), let us know in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (Required: False)
* **First Name**: A text field to input your first name.

### Last Name (Required: False)
* **Last Name**: A text field to input your last name.

### Email (Required: False)
* **Email**: A field to input your email address, so we can contact you back.

### Phone (Required: False)
* **Phone**: A text field to input your phone number for further contact.

### Ceremony Details (Required: False)
* **Ceremony Details**: A text field to describe the details of your ceremony (e.g., date, time, location).

### Style Preferences (Required: False)
* **Style Preferences**: A multiple-choice field to choose your preferred style for the flower arrangement (e.g., Classic, Modern, Traditional).

### Flower Choices (Required: False)
* **Flower Choices**: A multiple-choice field to select the type of flowers you'd like to have (e.g., Roses, Lilies, Sunflowers).

### Flower Quantity (Required: False)
* **Flower Quantity**: A numerical field to decide on the quantity of flowers you'd like to have.

### Flower Color (Required: False)
* **Flower Color**: A single-choice field to select the color of the flowers (e.g., Pink, Red, White).

### Special Requests (Required: False)
* **Special Requests**: A text field to input any special requests you have (e.g., delivery, setup).

Note: All fields are optional, but please fill them in according to your needs.
