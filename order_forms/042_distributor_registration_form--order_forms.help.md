# Distributor Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for distributors to register themselves and their representative(s) information for ordering purposes. It is intended to be filled out by the distributor or their representative(s) to provide accurate and complete contact information for communication and order processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out your representative's name, role, and email in the "Representative" section.
* Enter your recruiter's name, role, and email in the "Recruiter" section.
* Fill out your order details and payment terms in the "Order Details" and "Payment Terms" sections.
* Review and accept the terms and conditions.
* Fill out your contact information (name, phone, and email) in the "Contact Information" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Representative Name** (`representative_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the representative who will be handling the order.
* **Representative Role** (`representative_role`, select_one, required): Choose the representative's role (Admin, Manager, or Owner).
* **Representative Email** (`representative_email`, email, required): Enter the representative's email address.
* **Representative Phone** (`representative_phone`, text, required): Enter the representative's phone number.
* **Representative Address** (`representative_address`, text, required): Enter the representative's address.
* **Recruiter Name** (`recruiter_name`, text, required): Enter the recruiter's name who will be coordinating the order.
* **Recruiter Role** (`recruiter_role`, select_one, required): Choose the recruiter's role (Admin, Manager, or Owner).
* **Recruiter Email** (`recruiter_email`, email, required): Enter the recruiter's email address.
* **Recruiter Phone** (`recruiter_phone`, text, required): Enter the recruiter's phone number.
* **Recruiter Address** (`recruiter_address`, text, required): Enter the recruiter's address.
* **Order ID** (`order_id`, text, required): Enter the order ID for the order being placed.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, required): Enter any payment terms or conditions related to the order.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, required): Review and accept the terms and conditions of the order.
* **Contact Information (Name)** (`representative_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Contact Information (Phone)** (`representative_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Information (Email)** (`representative_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.

# Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete registration information.
* Review and accept the terms and conditions to ensure you understand the order terms.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your representative or recruiter for assistance.
