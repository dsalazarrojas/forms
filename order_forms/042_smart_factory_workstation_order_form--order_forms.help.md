# Smart Factory Workstation Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Factory Workstation Order Form is a request form used to order workstations for specific needs and requests. It is meant to be used for internal use within the factory.
 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the type of workstation you need from the options provided in the 'Requesting Workstation' section.
2. Optionally, provide a description of your workstation request.
3. In the 'Workstation Type' section, select one or more types of workstations you need.
4. In the 'Description' field, enter a brief description of your requested workstation.
5. In the 'Requested By' field, enter the name of the person requesting the workstation.
6. In the 'Contact Info' field, enter any contact information for the person requesting the workstation.
7. Enter your phone number in the 'Phone' field.
8. Enter your email address in the 'Email' field.
9. In the 'Order Notes' field, enter any additional information or notes for the order.
10. Optionally, select the name of the person submitting the form from the 'Submitted By' field.
11. Finally, enter the date you are submitting the form in the 'Submitted Date' field.
 
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. Requesting Workstation**: Select the type of workstation you need. Available options are 1, 2, and 3.
* **2. Workstation Type**: Select one or more types of workstations you need.
* **3. Description**: Enter a brief description of your requested workstation.
* **4. Requested By**: Enter the name of the person requesting the workstation.
* **5. Contact Info**: Enter any contact information for the person requesting the workstation.
* **6. Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **7. Email**: Enter your email address.
* **8. Order Notes**: Enter any additional information or notes for the order.
* **9. Submitted By**: Optionally, select the name of the person submitting the form.
* **10. Submitted Date**: Enter the date you are submitting the form.
 
## Tips
* Please ensure that you select the correct type of workstation you need.
* Make sure to enter a clear and concise description of your requested workstation.
* If you have any contact information, please enter it in the 'Contact Info' field.
* If you have any additional notes or information for the order, please enter it in the 'Order Notes' field.
* Finally, ensure that you enter the correct date and name of the person submitting the form.
