# Art Gallery Exhibition Supplies Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Gallery Exhibition Supplies Order Form is used to document and process orders for supplies needed for exhibitions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the exhibition's name and the person requesting the supplies in the "Exhibition Name" and "Person Requesting Supplies" fields, respectively.
2. Select the department responsible for the exhibition from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter the date supplies are needed by in the "Date Supplies are Needed By" field.
4. For each of the five categories of supplies, provide a brief description of the supplies required.
5. Select the preferred payment method from the dropdown menu.
6. Fill in the digital approval of the order, entering your name as required.
7. Enter the date of the order.
8. (Optional) Provide any special delivery instructions.
9. (Optional) Provide an internal order tracking reference for office use.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exhibition Name** (`exhibition_title`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of the exhibition.
* **Person Requesting Supplies** (`requester_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of the person requesting the supplies.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the department responsible for the exhibition.
* **Date Supplies are Needed By** (`required_by_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date supplies are needed by.
* **Hardware and Hanging Supplies** (`category_1`, `text`, `optional`): Enter a brief description of the hardware and hanging supplies required.
* **Lighting and Electrical** (`category_2`, `text`, `optional`): Enter a brief description of the lighting and electrical supplies required.
* **Labeling and Signage** (`category_3`, `text`, `optional`): Enter a brief description of the labeling and signage supplies required.
* **Packing and Shipping** (`category_4`, `text`, `optional`): Enter a brief description of the packing and shipping supplies required.
* **Other Supplies** (`category_5`, `text`, `optional`): Enter a brief description of any other supplies required.
* **Estimated Total Cost** (`total_estimated_cost`, `number`, `optional`): Enter an estimated total cost of the supplies.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the preferred payment method from the dropdown menu.
* **Special Delivery Instructions** (`delivery_instructions`, `text`, `optional`): Enter any special delivery instructions.
* **Digital Approval (Type Name)** (`digital_approval_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your digital approval of the order, including your name.
* **Date of Order** (`order_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date of the order.
* **Internal Order Tracking Reference (Office Use Only)** (`tracking_reference`, `text`, `optional`): Enter an internal order tracking reference for office use.

## Tips
* Be sure to provide accurate and detailed descriptions of the supplies required for each category.
* Double-check your preferred payment method and digital approval before submitting the form.
* Make sure to enter the correct date of the order and your digital approval name.
