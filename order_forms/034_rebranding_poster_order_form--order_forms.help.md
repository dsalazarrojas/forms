# Rebranding Poster Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by agencies to order posters for rebranding purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your agency's name in the "Agency Name" field.
2.  Provide your agency's website URL in the "Agency URL" field.
3.  Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
4.  Choose whether you want a poster of a certain size ("Poster Size") and type ("Poster Type").
5.  Specify the quantity of posters you need.
6.  Select a price for the order ("Price").
7.  Choose a payment method for your order ("Payment Method").
8.  Add any additional notes about your order in the "Order Note" field.
9.  Set the order date and time ("Order Date" and "Order Time") for your order.
10.  Enter your agency's email address ("Agency Email").
11.  Provide your agency's phone number ("Agency Phone").
12.  Update the order status ("Order Status") and payment status ("Payment Status") fields accordingly.
13.  Record the date and time when the order status was last updated ("Order Status Updated At") and the user who last updated it ("Order Status Updated By") and the payment status ("Payment Status Updated At") and the user who last updated it ("Payment Status Updated By").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Agency Name** (`Agency Name`, `text`, required): Enter your agency's name to be associated with this order.
*   **Agency URL** (`Agency URL`, `text`, required): Enter your agency's website URL.
*   **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `text`, required): Enter your contact information.
*   **Poster Size** (`Poster Size`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the size of poster you need.
*   **Poster Type** (`Poster Type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of poster you need.
*   **Quantity** (`Quantity`, `number`, required): Specify the number of posters you need.
*   **Price** (`Price`, `number`, required): Set the price for your order.
*   **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, `select_one`, required): Choose a payment method for your order.
*   **Order Note** (`Order Note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about your order.
*   **Order Date** (`Order Date`, `date`, required): Set the date of your order.
*   **Order Time** (`Order Time`, `time`, required): Set the time of your order.
*   **Agency Email** (`Agency Email`, `email`, required): Enter your agency's email address.
*   **Agency Phone** (`Agency Phone`, `text`, required): Enter your agency's phone number.
*   **Order Status** (`Order Status`, `select_one`, required): Set the order status for your order.
*   **Order Status Updated At** (`Order Status Updated At`, `date`, required): Record the date when the order status was last updated.
*   **Order Status Updated By** (`Order Status Updated By`, `text`, required): Record the user who last updated the order status.
*   **Payment Status** (`Payment Status`, `select_one`, required): Set the payment status for your order.
*   **Payment Status Updated At** (`Payment Status Updated At`, `date`, required): Record the date when the payment status was last updated.
*   **Payment Status Updated By** (`Payment Status Updated By`, `text`, required): Record the user who last updated the payment status.
*   **Order Updated At** (`Order Updated At`, `date`, required): Record the date and time when the order was last updated.
*   **Order Updated By** (`Order Updated By`, `text`, required): Record the user who last updated the order.
