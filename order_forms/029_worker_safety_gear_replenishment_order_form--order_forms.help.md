# worker_safety_gear_replenishment_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and manage orders for safety gear and equipment. It helps to gather necessary information for the replenishment process.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form pages and sections to understand what information is required.
2. Select the options for each field based on your needs.
3. Fill out the form completely and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hires** (Yes/No): Indicate whether you are ordering safety gear for new hires.
* **Personal Protective Equipment Orders** (Yes/No): Check this option if you need to order personal protective equipment (PPE).
* **Damaged Gear** (Yes/No): Indicate if you have damaged safety gear that needs replacement.
* **Safety Equipment Orders** (Yes/No): Check this option to order safety equipment.
* **Order Notes** (Text): Enter any additional notes or comments about the order.
* **Order Date** (Date): Enter the date of the order.
* **Assigned To** (Yes/No): Indicate whether the order is assigned to someone else.
* **Tips**: Double-check your selections before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
