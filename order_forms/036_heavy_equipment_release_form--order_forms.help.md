# Heavy Equipment Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Heavy Equipment Release Form is a documentation tool used to record the release of heavy equipment to customers. It helps in ensuring that all necessary information is captured, and it serves as a record for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Customer Information**: Fill out this field with the customer's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. **Equipment Information**: Provide information about the equipment being released, including its description, model number, and any other details that may be relevant.
3. **Return Status**: Select the correct status of the equipment being returned from the options provided (Equipment has been inspected and released to the customer, Equipment is being returned to the vendor, or Equipment is being repaired).
4. **Remarks**: If there are any additional comments or remarks about the release, enter them in this field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (Customer Information, text, required: false)**
	+ This field is used to capture the customer's name, contact information, and any other relevant details. Please provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* **Equipment Information (Equipment Information, text, required: false)**
	+ This field is used to provide information about the equipment being released, including its description, model number, and any other relevant details. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Return Status (Return Status, select_one, required: true)**
	+ This field is used to select the status of the equipment being returned from the options provided. Select the correct status from the available options.
* **Remarks (Remarks, text, required: false)**
	+ This field is used to capture any additional comments or remarks about the release. If there are any notable issues or concerns, please enter them in this field.
