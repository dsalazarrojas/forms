### Home Accent Pre Order Form - Help Guide

#### Purpose
The Home Accent Pre Order Form is used to collect information from customers to facilitate home accent pre orders.

#### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **customer_name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **customer_email** field. (Please ensure your email address is valid and formatted correctly for the best chance of receiving a response.)
3. Enter the name of the item(s) you would like to order in the **item_name** field.
4. Enter the quantity of the item(s) you would like to order in the **quantity** field.
5. Enter your shipping address in the **shipping_address** field. (Please ensure your shipping address is accurate and formatted correctly for delivery purposes.)
6. Enter your phone number in the **phone** field. (This is optional but will help us contact you if there are any issues with your order.)
7. Select the date you would like to receive your order in the **order_date** field. (Please ensure the date is in the correct format.)
8. If you have any comments or requests regarding your order, enter them in the **order_comment** field. (This is optional but will be taken into account while preparing your order.)

#### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name (customer_name)**: The name of the customer placing the order.
* **Email Address (customer_email)**: A valid email address for communication.
* **Item Name (item_name)**: The name of the item(s) being ordered.
* **Quantity (quantity)**: The number of items being ordered.
* **Shipping Address (shipping_address)**: The address where the order will be shipped to.
* **Phone (phone)**: The phone number of the customer for any necessary contact.
* **Order Date (order_date)**: The date the customer would like to receive their order.
* **Order Comment (order_comment)**: Any comments or requests the customer may have about their order.
