# Club Annual Report Printing Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Club Annual Report Printing Order Form is a tool for clubs and organizations to place orders for printing of their annual reports. The form helps to gather all necessary information to process the order efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your club's name and contact person's name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your contact email address and phone number to ensure we can get back to you with any questions or updates.
3. Provide details about your annual report, including the report title, report year, and document format.
4. Choose the printing specifications, including the number of copies, approximate page count, color or black and white printing, paper type, and binding type.
5. Select your preferred delivery method, delivery address, and the date you need the reports by.
6. Choose your payment method and confirm the order information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Club Name (1)** (`text`, **required**): Enter your club's name.
* **Contact Person Name (2)** (`text`, **required**): Enter the name of the person placing the order.
* **Email Address (3)** (`email`, **required**): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number (4)** (`text`, **required**): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Document Details (5)** (`note`, **not required**): This field can be used to provide additional information about your annual report.
* **Report Year (6)** (`number`, **required**): Enter the year the report is for.
* **Report Title (7)** (`text`, **required**): Enter the title of the annual report.
* **Document Format (8)** (`select_one`, **required**): Choose the format of your document: PDF, Word, InDesign, or Other.
* **Printing Specifications (9)** (`note`, **not required**): This field can be used to provide additional information about your printing specifications.
* **Number of Copies (10)** (`number`, **required**): Enter the number of copies you want to print.
* **Approximate Page Count (11)** (`number`, **required**): Enter the approximate page count of your document.
* **Color or Black and White (12)** (`select_one`, **required**): Choose whether you want to print in color or black and white.
* **Paper Type (13)** (`select_one`, **required**): Choose the type of paper you want to use: Standard white, Premium white, Colored paper, Glossy stock, or Other.
* **Binding and Finishing (14)** (`note`, **not required**): This field can be used to provide additional information about your binding and finishing preferences.
* **Binding Type (15)** (`select_one`, **required**): Choose the binding type: Stapled, Spiral bound, Perfect bound, Saddle stitched, or Other.
* **Covers (16)** (`select_one`, **required**): Choose the type of covers you want: No covers, Card stock covers, Laminated covers, Heavy duty covers.
* **Delivery Information (17)** (`note`, **not required**): This field can be used to provide additional information about your delivery specifications.
* **Delivery Address (18)** (`text`, **required**): Enter the address where you want the reports sent to.
* **Needed By Date (19)** (`date`, **required**): Enter the date you need the reports by.
* **Delivery Method (20)** (`select_one`, **required**): Choose how you want to receive your reports: Standard shipping, Express shipping, Local pickup, or Other.
* **Estimated Cost (21)** (`number`, **not required**): This field will display the estimated cost of your order.
* **Payment Method (22)** (`select_one`, **required**): Choose how you want to pay: Invoice, Credit card, Check, Cash, or Other.
* **Special Requests or Instructions (23)** (`text`, **not required**): Enter any special requests or instructions you may have.
* **I Confirm This Order Information Is Correct (24)** (`select_one`, **required**): Confirm that the order information is correct.
