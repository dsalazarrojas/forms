# Soccer Team T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the Soccer Team T-shirt order process. It is used to gather customer details for accurate order processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your First Name in the first text field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the second text field.
3. Enter your Email address in the third text field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the fourth text field.
5. Select your preferred Shirt Size from the options provided.
6. Choose your preferred Quantity of shirts.
7. Select your Payment Method.
8. Choose your preferred Shirt Color (if applicable).
9. Enter your Date of Birth.
10. Enter the Date of Sale.
11. Select the Sale Type.
12. Enter the Team Name (optional).
13. Upload a T-shirt image (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Shirt Size** (`shirt_size`, select_one, true/required): Choose the shirt size you prefer.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of shirts you would like to order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, true/required): Choose your preferred payment method.
* **Shirt Color** (`shirt_color`, select_multiple, false/optional): Select your preferred shirt colors.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Date of Sale** (`date_of_sale`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the sale.
* **Sale Type** (`sale_type`, select_one, true/required): Choose the type of sale.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the team name (if applicable).
* **T-shirt Image** (`tshirt_image`, text, required/optional): Upload a T-shirt image (if applicable).
