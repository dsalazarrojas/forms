# Payment Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to provide payment information for a purchase or service. It collects various details to facilitate secure and efficient transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form with the requested information in each field.
- Make sure to enter accurate and relevant details in each field.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name to identify yourself.
- **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required: false): Enter your shipping address for delivery purposes.
- **Mobile Number** (`mobile_number`, text, required: false): Enter your mobile number for contact purposes.
- **Facebook URL** (`facebook_url`, text, required: false): If you have a Facebook account, enter your Facebook URL.
- **Payment Mode** (`payment_mode`, select_one, required: false): Choose how you want to pay (e.g., Stripe, PayPal, Bank Transfer).
- **Date of Customer** (`date_of_customer`, date, required: false): Enter the date you became a customer, if applicable.
- **Schedule Payment** (`schedule_payment`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose your payment schedule (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
- **Payment Mode Options** (`payment_mode_1`, select_multiple, required: false): Check if you have any payment mode options.
- **Schedule Payment Options** (`schedule_payment_1`, select_multiple, required: false): Check your payment schedule options.

## Tips
- Be accurate and complete when filling out the form.
- Review each field carefully to ensure you are providing the correct information.
- If you are unsure about any field, ask for help or clarification.
