# Cloud Computing Subscription Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for existing customers to renew their current cloud computing subscription plans. It gathers information required to process the renewal and provide the customer with the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the account information section, which includes:
	* Account Number: Enter your existing subscription account ID.
	* Account Holder Name: Enter the name of the primary account contact.
	* Email Address: Enter the billing contact email address.
2. Move on to the current subscription details section and fill out:
	* Current Cloud Service: Select the name and type of your current cloud service.
	* Current Subscription Plan: Choose your current subscription plan.
	* Renewal Date: Enter the date when your current subscription is set to expire.
	* Current Monthly Cost - USD: Enter your current monthly cost.
3. Choose your renewal options:
	* Renewal Plan Choice: Select whether you want to continue with your current plan or change to a different one.
	* New Plan if Changing: If you choose to change plans, select the new plan and specify the estimated new cost.
	* Required Features for Renewal: Select the features required for your new plan.
4. In the billing information section, fill out:
	* Billing Contact Name: Enter the name of the person responsible for payments.
	* Billing Email Address: Enter the email address where invoices will be sent.
	* Payment Method: Choose your preferred payment method.
5. Finally, if you have any special requests or comments, use the additional information section to provide them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Account Number** (1, `text`, required): Enter your existing subscription account ID to identify your account.
* **Account Holder Name** (3, `text`, required): Enter the name of the primary account contact.
* **Email Address** (4, `email`, required): Enter the billing contact email address where invoices will be sent.
* **Current Cloud Service** (7, `text`, required): Select the name and type of your current cloud service.
* **Current Subscription Plan** (8, `text`, required): Choose your current subscription plan.
* **Renewal Date** (9, `date`, required): Enter the date when your current subscription is set to expire.
* **Current Monthly Cost - USD** (10, `number`, required): Enter your current monthly cost.
* **Renewal Plan Choice** (12, `select_one`, required): Select whether you want to continue with your current plan or change to a different one.
* **New Plan if Changing** (13, `text`, required): If you choose to change plans, select the new plan and specify the estimated new cost.
* **Required Features for Renewal** (17, `select_multiple`, required): Select the features required for your new plan.
* **Billing Contact Name** (21, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for payments.
* **Billing Email Address** (22, `email`, required): Enter the email address where invoices will be sent.
* **Payment Method** (23, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred payment method.
* **Special requests or comments** (25, `text`, required): Enter any specific requirements or notes you may have.
