# Racing Bikes Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect customer information and order details for bike orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer information section with your name, contact details, and any other relevant customer information.
2. Provide details about the order, including the type of bike and any special requests.
3. Enter delivery details, including the delivery address and any specific delivery instructions.
4. Summarize the order in the last section to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, required/optional): This is the customer's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the type of bike and any special requests for the order.
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the delivery address and any specific delivery instructions.
* **Order Summary** (`order_summary`, text, required/optional): Summarize the order in this section to ensure accuracy.

## Tips
* Please fill in the form accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any special requests or questions, contact the dealership's customer service.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all fields are filled correctly.
