# Material Delivery Validation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about material delivery, including the delivery date, material details, shipping information, and related remarks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the delivery date from the calendar picker.
2. Enter the material ID or other relevant material information.
3. Enter the quantity received.
4. Choose the delivery method (e.g., By Air, By Sea, By Land).
5. Enter the shipping cost.
6. Select the delivery status (e.g., Delivered, In Transit, Pending).
7. Add any comments.
8. Enter the shipping address.
9. Enter the contact person's name and phone number.
10. Enter the email address.
11. Enter the order ID.
12. Enter the customer ID.
13. Enter the material type.
14. Add any delivery notes.
15. Add any remarks (up to three separate sections).
16. (Optional) Enter additional material ID and other related information (use with caution and clear instructions).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Delivery Date (id: 1)**: A calendar picker to select the delivery date.
* **Material Id (id: 2)**: Enter the material ID or other relevant material information.
* **Material Condition (id: 3)**: Select the condition of the material (e.g., Excellent, Fair, Damaged).
* **Quantity Received (id: 4)**: Enter the quantity received.
* **Delivery Method (id: 5)**: Choose the delivery method (e.g., By Air, By Sea, By Land).
* **Shipping Cost (id: 6)**: Enter the shipping cost.
* **Delivery Status (id: 7)**: Select the delivery status (e.g., Delivered, In Transit, Pending).
* **Comments (id: 8)**: Add any comments related to the delivery.
* **Shipping Address (id: 9)**: Enter the shipping address.
* **Contact Person (id: 10)**: Enter the contact person's name.
* **Phone Number (id: 11)**: Enter the phone number.
* **Email Address (id: 12)**: Enter the email address.
* **Order Id (id: 13)**: Enter the order ID.
* **Customer Id (id: 14)**: Enter the customer ID.
* **Material Type (id: 15)**: Enter the material type.
* **Delivery Notes (id: 16)**: Add any delivery notes.
* **Remarks 1 (id: 24)**: Add any remarks.
* **Remarks 2 (id: 24)**: Add any additional remarks.
* **Delivery Person (id: 23)**: Enter the delivery person's name.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill up all relevant fields.
* If you need to enter a material ID, use the field labeled "Material Id" (id: 2).
* If you need to enter a delivery method or status, select the relevant option from the drop-down menus.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
