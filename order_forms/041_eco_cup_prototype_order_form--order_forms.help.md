# Eco Cup Prototype Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers interested in ordering "Eco Cup" products or services. Please fill out the form below to submit your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the required information. All fields are text input types, so you can type in your response directly into each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Field 1**: This field is for your name.
* **Field 2**: This field is for your email address.
* **Field 3**: This field is for your phone number.
* **Field 4**: This field is for your physical address.
* **Field 5**: This field is for your order description.
* **Field 6**: This field is for your order quantity.
* **Field 7**: This field is for your desired delivery date.
* **Field 8**: This field is for your special instructions.
* **Field 9**: This field is for your additional comments.
* **Field 10**: This field is for your name again (likely for verification purposes).
* **Field 11**: This field is for your email address again (likely for verification purposes).
* **Field 12**: This field is for your phone number again (likely for verification purposes).
* **Field 13**: This field is for your payment method (e.g. credit card, paypal).
* **Field 14**: This field is for your payment date.
* **Field 15**: This field is for your total cost.
* **Field 16**: This field is for your order status (this field seems out of place, it would be more logical to have this field at the end of the form).
* **Field 17**: This field is for your order notes.
* **Field 18**: This field is for your order total (this field seems to be a duplicate of Field 15).
* **Field 19**: This field is for your order subtotal.
* **Field 20**: This field is for your tax (this field is likely for taxes to be applied to the order).
* **Field 21**: This field is for your shipping address.
* **Field 22**: This field is for your shipping city.
* **Field 23**: This field is for your shipping state.
* **Field 24**: This field is for your shipping zip code.
* **Field 25**: This field is for your signature (this field is likely for verification purposes).

## Tips
* Please fill out each field accurately and completely.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
