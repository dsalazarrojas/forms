# Bhuntas Collection/E Store Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide necessary information to onboard new stores and collections onto our platform, helping us to better understand your store's needs and ensure a successful experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section and field as instructed below:

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store or Collection Name** (`store_name`, `text`, required): This is the official name of your store or collection. It's the name that will be displayed on our platform and is used to identify your store.
* **Store Category or Niche** (`store_category`, `text`, required): This is the type of products or services you offer, e.g., clothing, electronics, food, etc.
* **Store Owner or Manager Name** (`owner_name`, `text`, required): This is the name of the person responsible for managing your store or collection.
* **Email Address** (`owner_email`, `email`, required): This is your primary contact email for communication and support.
* **Phone Number** (`owner_phone`, `text`, required): This is the phone number we can use to contact you for support or questions.
* **Types of Products You Will Sell** (`product_types`, `text`, required): List the categories of products you will be selling on our platform.
* **How Do You Source Products** (`product_sourcing`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you source your products, e.g., self-manufactured, wholesale purchasing, etc.
* **Quality Standards or Certifications** (`quality_standards`, `text`, optional): If your products have any special quality standards or certifications, please list them here.
* **Pricing Strategy** (`price_strategy`, `select_one`, required): This is how you price your products, e.g., cost-plus markup, market-based pricing, etc.
* **Target Customer Demographic** (`target_demographic`, `text`, required): Describe the ideal customer for your store or collection.
* **How Do You Position Your Brand** (`market_positioning`, `text`, required): Describe how you position your brand, e.g., premium, budget-friendly, eco-conscious, etc.
* **Geographic Reach** (`customer_reach`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the locations where you serve customers.
* **Order Processing Capability** (`order_processing`, `select_one`, required): This is how you handle orders, e.g., manual processing, partially automated, etc.
* **Average Order Processing Time** (`average_processing_time`, `select_one`, required): This is how long it takes you to prepare orders, e.g., same day, 1-3 days, etc.
* **Shipping Partners** (`shipping_partners`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the shipping partners you use, e.g., USPS, FedEx, etc.
* **Return and Refund Policy** (`return_policy`, `select_one`, required): This is your return and refund policy for customers.
* **Current Inventory Size** (`inventory_size`, `number`, optional): If you have an inventory management system, please estimate the number of items in your inventory.
* **Inventory Management System** (`inventory_system`, `select_one`, required): This is how you track your inventory, e.g., manual tracking, spreadsheet, etc.
* **Product Restock Frequency** (`restock_frequency`, `select_one`, required): This is how often you restock your products.
* **Marketing Channels** (`marketing_channels`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the marketing channels you use, e.g., social media, email marketing, etc.
* **Social Media Accounts** (`social_media_presence`, `text`, optional): Link to your social media accounts.
* **Goals on This Platform** (`platform_goals`, `text`, required): Describe your goals for selling on our platform.
* **Projected Monthly Sales** (`sales_projection`, `select_one`, optional): Estimate your projected sales on our platform.
* **I Agree to Marketplace Terms and Conditions** (`platform_agreement`, `select_one`, required): You must agree to our marketplace terms and conditions.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): If you have any other information about your store or collection, feel free to add it here.

Note: The required fields are marked as "required" in the form, which means they are necessary to complete the onboarding process. Please make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure a successful onboarding experience.
