# Custom Cake Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for users who want to create a custom cake order. It collects information about their preferred cake size, design, and other features.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select the date of the desired cake delivery date in the "Date" field.
6. Choose the time of the desired cake delivery in the "Time" field.
7. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
8. Select the size of the cake you would like (Large, Medium, or Small) in the "Cake size" field.
9. Choose the desired design of the cake (Classic, Cartoon, or Wedding) in the "Cake design" field.
10. Enter any special message you would like to be written on the cake in the "Special message" field.
11. Select the type of cake (Chocolate, Vanilla, or Red Velvet) in the "Cake type" field.
12. Choose the desired flavors (Strawberry, Blueberry, or Lemon) in the "Flavor" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (date, required): Select the date of the desired cake delivery.
* **Time** (time, required): Choose the time of the desired cake delivery.
* **Address** (text, required): Enter your address.
* **Cake size** (select_one, optional): Choose the size of the cake (Large, Medium, or Small).
* **Cake design** (select_multiple, optional): Select one or more desired design options (Classic, Cartoon, or Wedding).
* **Special message** (text, optional): Enter any special message you would like to be written on the cake.
* **Cake type** (select_one, optional): Select the type of cake (Chocolate, Vanilla, or Red Velvet).
* **Flavor** (select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more desired flavors (Strawberry, Blueberry, or Lemon).
