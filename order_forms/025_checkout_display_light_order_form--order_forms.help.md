# Checkout Display Light Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Checkout Display Light Order Form" is used to capture essential details for your display light order. This form ensures that our team receives all necessary information for processing your order efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your Brand/Organization Name.
2. Provide your Store Location/ID.
3. Enter your Store Manager's Full Name.
4. Enter your Contact Email Address.
5. Enter your Contact Phone Number.
6. Select your desired Display Light Model.
7. Specify the quantity of units ordered.
8. Decide if you want to include a Mounting Hardware Kit with your order.
9. Add any Custom Branding Details, if applicable.
10. Provide your complete shipping address.
11. Select a requested delivery date for your order.
12. Indicate if a Professional Installation is required.
13. Choose your preferred payment method.
14. (Optional) Enter a Purchase Order Number, if you have one.
15. Confirm your order by indicating you authorize this order on behalf of the store.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Brand / Organization Name (Required)**: Enter your company's name as it appears on your business cards, invoices, or other official documents.
* **Store Location / ID (Required)**: Provide your store's location, which could be a physical address or a unique identifier used within your organization.
* **Store Manager Name (Required)**: Enter the name of the person managing your store or contact person responsible for the order.
* **Contact Email Address (Required)**: Enter the email address associated with your store or contact person.
* **Contact Phone Number (Required)**: Enter your store's or contact person's phone number.
* **Display Light Model (Required)**: Select the model of display light you wish to order. Please note that each model has its specifications, so select carefully.
* **Quantity Requested (Required)**: Enter the number of units of the display light you are ordering.
* **Include Mounting Hardware Kit (Required)**: Decide if you want a Mounting Hardware Kit included with your order, and if so, be aware of the additional fee.
* **Custom Branding Details (Optional)**: If you need custom branding such as logo engraving or specific color schemes, provide more details in this field.
* **Complete Shipping Address (Required)**: Enter your complete shipping address where you'd like to receive your order.
* **Requested Delivery Date (Required)**: Choose a date for the delivery of your order.
* **Professional Installation Required (Required)**: Indicate if a professional installation is needed, and we will quote the service if so.
* **Payment Method (Required)**: Select your preferred payment method.
* **Purchase Order Number (Optional)**: If you have a Purchase Order Number, enter it here. This is useful for our billing and accounting purposes.
* **Estimated Total Amount (Optional)**: This field shows an estimated total, subject to tax and shipping fees.
* **I Authorize this Order (Required)**: Confirm that you authorize this order on behalf of your store, which will serve as a signature of your agreement with our company.
