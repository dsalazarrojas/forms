# Concert Ticket Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a user-friendly way to order concert tickets for various events. It guides the user through selecting the event, date, and seating options, and provides an opportunity to enter contact information and select delivery preferences. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the event you wish to attend by selecting the "Select Concert" field.
2. Select your preferred date by choosing the "Concert Date" field.
3. Choose your preferred seating area by selecting the "Seating Section" field.
4. Enter the number of tickets you would like to purchase by filling out the "Number of Tickets" field.
5. Enter your contact information, including first and last name, email address, and phone number.
6. Select your preferred delivery option by choosing the "Ticket Delivery Preference" field.
7. Choose your preferred payment method by selecting the "Payment Method" field.
8. Accept the terms and conditions by selecting the "Terms Agreement" field.
9. Click "Submit Order" to complete your purchase.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Concert** (Concert Selection, select_one, required): Choose the concert event you wish to attend.
* **Concert Date** (Concert Date, date, required): Select your preferred date for the concert.
* **Seating Section** (Seating Section, select_one, required): Choose your preferred seating area for the concert.
* **Number of Tickets** (Number of Tickets, number, required): Enter the number of tickets you would like to purchase.
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter your family name.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Enter your email address for order confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Ticket Delivery Preference** (Ticket Delivery Preference, select_one, required): Select how you would like to receive your tickets (e.g., E-Ticket via Email, Mobile App Download, etc.).
* **Pickup or Delivery Address** (Pickup or Delivery Address, note, required): Enter your delivery address if applicable.
* **Street Address** (Street Address, text, required): Enter your street address for delivery.
* **City** (City, text, required): Enter your city for delivery.
* **State or Province** (State or Province, text, required): Enter your state or province for delivery.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (ZIP or Postal Code, text, required): Enter your postal code for delivery.
* **Optional Add-Ons** (Optional Add-Ons, note, optional): Choose to add any optional items to enhance your concert experience.
* **Parking Pass** (Parking Pass, select_one, optional): Choose to add parking to your order.
* **Merchandise Bundle** (Merchandise Bundle, select_one, optional): Choose to add concert merchandise to your order.
* **Payment Information** (Payment Information, note, optional): Review your payment details before submission.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method.
* **Terms Agreement** (Terms Agreement, select_one, required): Accept the terms and conditions before submission.
* **Submit Order** (Submit Order, select_one, required): Click to complete your purchase.
