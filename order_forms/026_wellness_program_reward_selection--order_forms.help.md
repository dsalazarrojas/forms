# Wellness Program Reward Selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form allows you to select a reward for participating in the wellness program and provide any additional feedback about your experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the reward options provided and select your preferred rewards. You can choose multiple rewards by checking the boxes next to each option.
2. If you have any additional feedback or comments about your experience with the wellness program, you can type them in the "Participant Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Reward Preferences
* **Reward Preferences** (`reward_preferences`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Check the boxes next to the rewards you would like to receive. You can choose multiple rewards by checking multiple boxes.
### Delivery Details
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, note, required: false)
	+ This field is a text box where you can provide any additional information about your delivery preferences or needs.
### Participant Feedback
* **Participant Feedback** (`participant_feedback`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is where you can provide any comments or feedback about your experience with the wellness program. This can include positive or negative feedback.
