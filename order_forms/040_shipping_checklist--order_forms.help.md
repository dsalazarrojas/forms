# shipping_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The shipping checklist is used to track and document the shipping process, including the status of shipments, the method of shipping, and customer interactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it.
3. Enter all relevant information, including customer details, shipping dates, and package contents.
4. Use the shipping method and service provider fields to indicate the chosen shipping method and service provider.
5. Use the shipping notes field to add any additional comments or notes related to the shipment.
6. Use the customer notes field to add any additional comments or notes from the customer.
7. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Address Label** (`address_label`, text, optional): Enter the label for the address where the package will be shipped to or from.
* **Ship Date** (`ship_date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the package will be shipped.
* **Tracking Number** (`tracking_number`, text, optional): Enter the tracking number for the shipment.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the customer receiving the shipment.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the customer receiving the shipment.
* **Product Weight** (`product_weight`, number, optional): Enter the weight of the product being shipped.
* **Product Dimensions** (`product_dimensions`, text, optional): Enter the dimensions of the product being shipped.
* **Ship Method** (`ship_method`, select_one, optional): Select the method of shipping (Yes or No).
* **Ship Service Provider** (`ship_service_provider`, select_one, optional): Select the service provider used for shipping (Yes or No).
* **Shipping Status** (`shipping_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the shipment (Active or Inactive).
* **Shipping Notes** (`shipping_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes related to the shipment.
* **Ship From Location** (`ship_from_location`, text, optional): Enter the location from which the package will be shipped.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the customer receiving the shipment.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the customer receiving the shipment.
* **Ship To Location** (`ship_to_location`, text, optional): Enter the location to which the package will be shipped.
* **Ship From Contact** (`ship_from_contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact person or department responsible for shipping.
* **Ship From Person** (`ship_from_person`, text, optional): Enter the person responsible for shipping.
* **Package Contents** (`package_contents`, text, optional): Enter a description of the package contents.
* **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or notes from the customer.
* **Ship From Notes** (`ship_from_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes from the shipper.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes from the customer.
* **Ship Date Range** (`ship_date_range`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the shipment period.
* **Ship Date Range End** (`ship_date_range_end`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the shipment period.
* **Package Weight Unit** (`package_weight_unit`, text, optional): Enter the unit of measurement for the package weight.
* **Ship Method Notes** (`ship_method_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes related to the shipping method.
