# Request Your Free Design - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Request Your Free Design" form is intended for customers or users to submit their requests for free design services or consultations. This form is not meant for sales or support inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form successfully, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Business Name** in the field labeled "Business Name".
2. Provide your **Email** address in the correct format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
3. Enter your **Phone Number** if you'd like to be contacted by phone.
4. Describe your **Description** of the design request or consultation needed.
5. If you have a file to attach, upload it in the **File Upload** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name**: Enter the name of your business or company.
* **Email**: Enter your email address in the correct format (`example@example.com`).
* **Phone Number**: If you'd like to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number here.
* **Description**: Briefly describe the design request or consultation needed. Be as specific as possible.
* **File Upload**: Attach any relevant files or images related to your design request or consultation.
