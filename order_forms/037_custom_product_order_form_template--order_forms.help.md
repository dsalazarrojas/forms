# Custom Product Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers create and manage custom product orders with ease. It gathers necessary details about the product and customer contact information, as well as order status to ensure smooth order processing and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as necessary.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
3. Review and submit the form to place your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Product Details
* **Product Name**: Type the name of the product you are ordering.
* **Product Price**: Enter the price of the product.
* **Product Description**: Briefly describe the product you are ordering.
* **Quantity**: Select the quantity of the product you want to order.

### Product Order Information
* **Product Order Status**: Select the current status of the order from the options: Processing, On Hold, Delivered, Cancelled.

### Customer Information
* **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone Number**: Type the customer's phone number.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the shipping address for the ordered product.
* **Order Notes**: Add any additional notes about the order.
* **Custom Fields**: Select up to 5 custom fields that apply to this order.

### Date and Time
* **Delivery Date**: Select the desired delivery date for the product.
* **Order Total**: Enter the total cost of the order, including any applicable taxes and shipping costs.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your order details before submitting the form.
* Ensure the delivery date is accurate and feasible.
* If there are any issues with the order, contact the sales team for assistance.
