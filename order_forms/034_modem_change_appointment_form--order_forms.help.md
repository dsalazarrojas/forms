# Modem Change Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule and confirm appointments for modem changes. It helps to ensure that the necessary information is collected from customers, technicians, and other stakeholders to facilitate efficient and effective modem changes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's information in the **customer_info** field.
2. Provide details about the appointment in the **appointment_details** field.
3. Select the appointment time in the **appointment_time** field (if applicable).
4. Enter the technician's information in the **tech_info** field.
5. Enter the customer's phone number in the **customer_phone** field (if applicable).
6. Enter the customer's email address in the **customer_email** field (if applicable).
7. Select the appointment date in the **appointment_date** field (if applicable).
8. Enter the technician's notes about the appointment in the **technician_notes** field (if applicable).
9. Select the desired outcome for the appointment from the **confirm_appointment** field.
10. (Optional) Confirm the appointment with the technician in the **tech_confirm_appointment** field.
11. Indicate whether the modem change is required by the **technician_id** field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_info** (Text): Enter the customer's name and contact information.
* **appointment_details** (Text): Provide a brief description of the appointment.
* **appointment_time** (Date): Select the time of the appointment.
* **tech_info** (Text): Enter the technician's name and contact information.
* **customer_phone** (Number): Enter the customer's phone number (if applicable).
* **customer_email** (Email): Enter the customer's email address (if applicable).
* **appointment_date** (Date): Select the date of the appointment (if applicable).
* **technician_notes** (Note): Enter notes about the appointment for the technician.
* **confirm_appointment** (Select Multiple): Select the desired outcome for the appointment:
	+ Confirm Appointment
	+ Reschedule
	+ No Show
* **tech_confirm_appointment** (Select Multiple): (Optional) Confirm the appointment with the technician:
	+ Confirm Appointment
	+ Reschedule
	+ No Show
* **technician_id** (Select Multiple): Indicate whether the modem change is required:
	+ Yes
	+ No

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately to avoid delays or cancellations.
- Use the **confirm_appointment** field to communicate the desired outcome to the technician.
- If the appointment is rescheduled, update the fields accordingly.
- Keep the **technician_notes** field for the technician's reference.
