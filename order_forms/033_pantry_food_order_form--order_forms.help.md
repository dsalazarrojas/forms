# Pantry Food Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pantry Food Order Form is used to place an order for food products, providing customers with a convenient way to submit their order details and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Order Form**:
	* Enter any order-specific details you would like to mention.
2. **Order Details**:
	* Provide any additional information about your order.
3. **Contact Info**:
	* Enter your email address so that we can contact you about your order.
4. **Order Status**:
	* This field is not currently required, but you can use it to track the status of your order.
5. **Payment Info**:
	* Although this field is not required, you can use it to provide payment information if needed.
6. **Order Notes**:
	* Enter any additional comments or notes about your order.
7. **Food Items**:
	* Select the food items you would like to order from the list.
8. **Food Items 2**:
	* This is another list of available food items. Select any additional items you would like to order.
9. **Phone**:
	* Enter your phone number so that we can contact you about your order.
10. **Email**:
	* Confirm your email address for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Form** (`Order Form`, text, required/optional): Enter any order-specific details you would like to mention.
* **Order Details** (`Order Details`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional information about your order.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, text, required/optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you about your order.
* **Order Status** (`Order Status`, text, required/optional): This field is not currently required, but you can use it to track the status of your order.
* **Payment Info** (`Payment Info`, text, required/optional): Although this field is not required, you can use it to provide payment information if needed.
* **Order Notes** (`Order Notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Food Items** (`Food Items`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the food items you would like to order from the list.
* **Food Items 2** (`Food Items 2`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any additional food items you would like to order from this list.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you about your order.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required/optional): Confirm your email address for contact purposes.

Note: This form is designed to help you place an order for food products. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure your order is processed correctly.
