# Sample Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sample Request Form is designed to collect information from users for a specific purpose. This form is used to gather details about the user's request, including the type of example they would like, quantity, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Sample Request Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your desired example type (e.g., product or service) in the "Product or Service" field.
2. Enter the quantity of the example you would like.
3. Provide any additional comments about your request in the "Comments" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. If applicable, select a date and time for your request in the "Date" and "Time" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sample Request** (sample_request_form, text, required): Enter a brief description of your sample request.
* **Product or Service** (product_service, text, required): Select the type of example you would like to request (e.g., product or service).
* **What type of example would you like?** (example_type, select_one, required): Choose between product or service.
* **Quantity** (quantity, number, required): Enter the quantity of the example you would like.
* **Comments** (comments, text, required): Provide any additional comments about your request.
* **Email** (email, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date** (date, date, required): Select the date for your request.
* **Time** (time, time, required): Select the time for your request.
* **Product or Service 2** (product_service_2, text, required): Enter any additional information about the second product or service you would like to request.
