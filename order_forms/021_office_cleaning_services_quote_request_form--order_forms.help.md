# office_cleaning_services_quote_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request office cleaning services. It collects information about the contact person and their contact information (phone, email), location, services offered, dates and frequency of cleaning, and any special requests or comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including name, phone number, and email address.
2. Select the services you are interested in.
3. Choose the start and end dates for the cleaning services.
4. Select the frequency of the cleaning services (e.g., one-time, weekly, bi-weekly, etc.).
5. Choose the location where the cleaning services will be provided.
6. If you have any special requests, type them in the comments field.
7. Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **contact_info**: Enter your name, company, or organization name. This will help us identify you and provide the necessary services.
* **contact_person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for this quote request.
* **contact_email**: Enter your email address where we can send you quotes, invoices, and updates.
* **contact_phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you for any further questions or clarification.
* **services**: Select the services you are interested in, such as window cleaning, carpet cleaning, etc.
* **services_selected**: Select one option from the list of services you selected.
* **start_date**: Enter the start date for the cleaning services.
* **end_date**: Enter the end date for the cleaning services.
* **frequency**: Select the frequency of the cleaning services, such as one-time, weekly, bi-weekly, etc.
* **frequency_selected**: Select one frequency from the list.
* **location**: Choose the location where the cleaning services will be provided.
* **special_requests**: If you have any special requests or comments, type them in this field.
* **comments**: If you have any additional comments or concerns, type them here.
* **assigned_tool**: Enter any assigned tool or equipment needed for the cleaning services.
* **form_id**: Enter a unique identifier for this form submission.
* **assigned_tool**: This field is currently empty and does not need to be filled.

## Tips
* Please fill in all required fields to ensure we can process your request efficiently.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* We will review your request and provide you with a quote or confirmation of your booking.
* Note that we may contact you for further clarification or details before finalizing your quote or booking.
