# product_stock_restocking_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help you check stock levels and determine if products need restocking.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the products that need restocking by checking the "Yes" box next to each product.
2. Enter the current stock levels for each product in the corresponding field.
3. Enter the units for each stock level in the corresponding field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **restock_products** (`product_stock_restocking_checklist_page_1`, select_multiple): Select the products that need restocking.
* **stock_levels** (`stock_levels`, number): Enter the current stock level for the selected product.
* **stock_levels_units** (`stock_levels_units`, number): Enter the units for the current stock level.

Note: This form has multiple fields for stock levels and units, but all of them are optional and seem to be duplicates. It's recommended to only fill out the fields that are required and make sense for your specific product and stock management process.
