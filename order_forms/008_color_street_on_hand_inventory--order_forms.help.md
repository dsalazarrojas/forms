# Color Street On Hand Inventory - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and manage Color Street's on-hand inventory, ensuring accurate stock levels and historical data for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the "Inventory Manager Name" field with your name or title.
2. Enter the "Date of Count" field with the date of the current inventory count.
3. For each section, enter the total number of sets available. These sections include:
	* Total Solid Sets
	* Total Glitter Sets
	* Total Art and Design Sets
	* Total French Sets
	* Total Pedicure Sets
	* Total Holiday/Seasonal Sets
4. If you have any sample items available, enter the number of "Total Twosie Samples" in the respective section.
5. Fill in the "Inventory Notes" field with any additional information about the current inventory state (e.g., missing items, damaged sets, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inventory Manager Name** (`inventory_manager`, text, required): This field is required to be filled in with your name or title.
* **Date of Count** (`date_of_count`, date, required): This field is required to be filled in with the date of the current inventory count.
* **Total Solid Sets** (`total_solid_sets`, number, required): Enter the total number of solid sets available.
* **Total Glitter Sets** (`total_glitter_sets`, number, required): Enter the total number of glitter sets available.
* **Total Art and Design Sets** (`total_art_sets`, number, required): Enter the total number of art and design sets available.
* **Total French Sets** (`total_french_sets`, number, required): Enter the total number of French sets available.
* **Total Pedicure Sets** (`total_pedi_sets`, number, required): Enter the total number of pedicure sets available.
* **Total Holiday/Seasonal Sets** (`total_holiday_sets`, number, required): Enter the total number of holiday/seasonal sets available.
* **Total Twosie Samples** (`twosie_samples_count`, number, required): Enter the total number of twosie samples available.
* **Inventory Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Fill in any additional information about the current inventory state.
