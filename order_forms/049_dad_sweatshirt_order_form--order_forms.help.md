# Dad Sweatshirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Dad Sweatshirt Order Form is a form used to gather information for customized dad sweatshirts. This form helps customers provide their order details, including their name, contact information, order details, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter a valid email address in the "Email" field. This will help us contact you with order updates and information.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select a date and time for your order in the "Order Date" and "Order Time" fields.
6. Enter any additional order notes in the "Order Notes" field.
7. Select the color of your sweatshirt from the "Color" field.
8. Select the size of your sweatshirt from the "Size" field.
9. Enter the quantity of sweatshirts you would like to order.
10. Enter the total amount for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Form** (`order_form`): This is the main form that collects all order information. Fill out this form to provide your order details.
* **First Name** (`first_name`): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter a valid email address to receive order updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number for order follow-up.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`): Select a date for your order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`): Select a time for your order.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`): Provide any additional notes for your order.
* **Color** (`color`): Select a valid color code for your sweatshirt (Red, Blue, Green).
* **Size** (`size`): Select a valid size code for your sweatshirt (S, M, L, XL).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`): Enter the number of sweatshirts you would like to order.
* **Total** (`total`): Enter the total amount for your order.
* **Submit** (`submit`): Select "Submit" to complete and send your order.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`): Select "Cancel" to discard your order.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter a valid email address and phone number to ensure you receive order updates and follow-up.
* Double-check your order details, especially the color and size selections.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact our customer service team.
