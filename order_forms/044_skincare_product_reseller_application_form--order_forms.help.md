# Skincare Product Reseller Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for skincare product resellers to provide information about their products, business, and payment preferences. It will help us understand your business and process your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below.

1. Review each field carefully and provide accurate information.
2. Enter your contact information in the "contact_information" field.
3. Select your sales channel from the options provided in the "sales_channel" field.
4. Enter your storefront URL and description in the "storefront_url" and "storefront_description" fields.
5. Select the products you are interested in selling from the "products" field.
6. Describe your shipping and pricing policies in the "shipping_and_pricing" field.
7. Enter your store information in the "store_info" field.
8. Specify your payment methods and terms in the "payment_methods" and "payment_terms" fields.
9. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **product_inquiries** (`product_inquiries`, text, required: false): This field is for any additional product inquiries or questions you have.
* **contact_information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): Please enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
* **sales_channel** (`sales_channel`, select_one, required: false): Select the sales channel you are using (e.g., online marketplace, physical store, etc.).
* **storefront_url** (`storefront_url`, text, required: false): Enter your storefront URL.
* **storefront_description** (`storefront_description`, text, required: false): Describe your storefront.
* **products** (`products`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the products you are interested in selling.
* **shipping_and_pricing** (`shipping_and_pricing`, note, required: false): Describe your shipping and pricing policies.
* **store_info** (`store_info`, text, required: false): Enter your store information.
* **payment_methods** (`payment_methods`, text, required: false): Specify your payment methods.
* **payment_terms** (`payment_terms`, text, required: false): Describe your payment terms.
* **notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Provide any additional notes or comments.
