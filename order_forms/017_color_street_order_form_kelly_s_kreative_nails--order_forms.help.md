# Color Street Order Form - Kelly's Kreative Nails - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Color Street Order Form is designed to allow customers to place an order for nail polish sets from Kelly's Kreative Nails. This form is used to collect essential information from the customer, including their name, address, and payment details, to process and fulfill the order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Name" field and type in your name.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field.
4. In the "Name of Sets you want to order" field, type in the name of the sets you wish to purchase.
5. Enter the total number of sets you want to order in the "Total Number of Sets" field.
6. If applicable, answer "True" for the "Is this a Buy 3 Get 1 Free deal?" field.
7. Choose your payment method (Venmo, PayPal, or Cash) in the "Payment Method" field.
8. Enter the username associated with your chosen payment method in the "Payment Username" field.
9. Select your delivery option (Ship or Pick-up) in the "Delivery Option" field.
10. If applicable, enter the name of the hostess in the "Hostess Name" field.
11. Leave a message for Kelly in the "Message for Kelly" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name** (`kelly_name`, `text`, required): Type in your name.
*   **Email Address** (`kelly_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
*   **Shipping Address** (`kelly_shipping`, `text`, required): Enter your shipping address.
*   **-- Your Selection --** (`section_kelly_order`, `note`, required): This section is for selecting the sets you wish to purchase. Please enter the name of the sets in the next field.
*   **Name of Sets you want to order** (`kelly_set_names`, `text`, required): Type in the name of the sets you want to purchase.
*   **Total Number of Sets** (`kelly_total_count`, `number`, required): Enter the total number of sets you wish to order.
*   **Is this a Buy 3 Get 1 Free deal?** (`kelly_b3g1_check`, `select_one`, required): Answer "True" if you are taking advantage of the buy 3 get 1 free promotion.
*   **-- Payment and Shipping --** (`section_kelly_payment`, `note`, required): This section contains the payment and shipping details.
*   **Payment Method** (`kelly_payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose between Venmo, PayPal, and Cash as your payment method.
*   **Payment Username** (`kelly_payment_id`, `text`, required): Enter the username associated with your chosen payment method.
*   **Delivery Option** (`kelly_delivery`, `select_one`, required): Select between shipping ($4.00) and pick-up (free).
*   **Hostess Name (if applicable)** (`kelly_hostess`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the hostess (if applicable).
*   **Message for Kelly** (`kelly_message`, `text`, optional): Leave a message for Kelly.

## Tips

*   This form is designed to be completed by the customer to place an order for nail polish sets from Kelly's Kreative Nails.
*   Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete to ensure timely processing of the order.
*   If you are unsure about any part of the form, please do not hesitate to contact Kelly's Kreative Nails support team for assistance.
