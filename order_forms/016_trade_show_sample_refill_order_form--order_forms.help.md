# Trade Show Sample Refill Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow customers to place orders for sample refills for trade show exhibits. The form gathers information about the order, shipping details, and any additional comments or notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Order Details page with the required information about your order.
2. In the Shipping Info page, provide your shipping details for the ordered samples.
3. In the Sample Request page, enter the details about the sample you are requesting.
4. In the Sample Type page, select the type of sample you are requesting.
5. Enter the quantity of the sample you need.
6. Choose the shipping method you prefer (Ground or Air).
7. Select the ship-to address from the options provided.
8. Enter the date and time you would like to receive the order.
9. Enter your email address and phone number for communication purposes.
10. Enter any additional address details if necessary.
11. Enter any special instructions or comments about your order.
12. Provide any additional comments or notes about the order.

- **Order Details**: A page where you can enter the details about your order.
- **Shipping Info**: A page where you can provide your shipping details for the ordered samples.
- **Sample Request**: A page where you can enter details about the sample you are requesting.
- **Sample Type**: A page where you can select the type of sample you are requesting.
  - **Product 1**
  - **Product 2**
  - **Product 3**
- **Quantity**: The quantity of the sample you need.
- **Shipping Method**: A page where you can choose the shipping method you prefer (Ground or Air).
- **Ship To**: A page where you can select the ship-to address.
  - **Ship To 1**

- **Phone**: A page where you can enter your phone number for communication purposes.
