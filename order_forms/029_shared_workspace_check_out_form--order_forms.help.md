# Shared Workspace Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shared Workspace Check Out Form is a tool used to record checkout times and gather information about shared workspaces.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Check In Time** (1) - This field is used to record the time you checked in to the shared workspace.
2. Enter the **Check In Date and Time** (3) - This field is used to record the date and time you checked in to the shared workspace.
3. Enter the **Checkout Time** (4) - This field is used to record the time you plan to checkout of the shared workspace.
4. Enter the **Checkout Date and Time** (5) - This field is used to record the date and time you plan to checkout of the shared workspace.
5. Enter the **Workspace Name** (6) - This field is used to identify the shared workspace you are checking out of.
6. Enter **Event Venues** (7) - This field is used to report the venues you visited during the event.
7. Enter **Co-Working Spaces** (8) - This field is used to report which co-working spaces you used during the event.
8. Enter **Shared Workspace Check Out Form Comments** (9) - This field is used to provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience in the shared workspace.
9. Enter your **Feedback** (10) - This field is used to provide any additional feedback or suggestions for future improvements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Check In Time (1)**: Enter the time you checked in to the shared workspace. (Required)
* **Check In Date and Time (3)**: Enter the date and time you checked in to the shared workspace. (Required)
* **Checkout Time (4)**: Enter the time you plan to checkout of the shared workspace. (Required)
* **Checkout Date and Time (5)**: Enter the date and time you plan to checkout of the shared workspace. (Required)
* **Workspace Name (6)**: Enter the name of the shared workspace you are checking out of. (Required)
* **Event Venues (7)**: Report the venues you visited during the event. 
* **Co-Working Spaces (8)**: Report which co-working spaces you used during the event. 
* **Shared Workspace Check Out Form Comments (9)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience in the shared workspace. 
* **Feedback (10)**: Provide any additional feedback or suggestions for future improvements. 

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the correct date and time for both check-in and checkout.
* If you have any issues or concerns while using the form, contact us at form@gic.mx.
