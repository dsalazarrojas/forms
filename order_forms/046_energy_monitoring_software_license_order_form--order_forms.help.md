# Energy Monitoring Software License Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to order a license for energy monitoring software. Please fill out the form with your information to place an order.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your information.
2. Choose the type of license you are interested in.
3. Select the license term that applies to your situation.
4. Indicate whether your company is of type A or B.
5. If your company type is not listed, provide a brief description of your company type.
6. Select the order date for your license.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Address**: Enter the address of your company.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **License Type**: Select the type of license that you are interested in. Options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **License Term**: Select the term length of your license. Options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **License Term Other**: If you have a specific license term in mind, enter it here.
* **Company Type**: Select the type of company you have. Options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Company Type Other**: If you have a company type that is not listed, enter it here.
* **Order Date**: Select the date you would like to order the license.
