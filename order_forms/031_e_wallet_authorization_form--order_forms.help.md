# e_wallet_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E-Wallet Authorization Form is used for managing and authorizing E-Wallets for payment and refund purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant Category from the "Category" field.
2. Briefly describe the purpose of the E-Wallet Authorization in the "Description" field.
3. Choose the E-Wallet Form Type from the "E-Wallet Form Type" field.
4. Select the E-Wallet Merchant Info from the "E-Wallet Merchant Info" field.
5. Enter the E-Wallet Form ID in the "E-Wallet Form ID" field (this is a numerical value).
6. If necessary, provide details about who created and updated the E-Wallet Authorization in the "Created By" and "Updated By" fields, and when it was created and updated in the "Created At" and "Updated At" fields.
7. Finally, select the E-Wallet Version from the "E-Wallet Version" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`form_category`): Select the category that best describes the purpose of this E-Wallet Authorization.
* **Description** (`form_description`): Briefly describe the purpose of the E-Wallet Authorization.
* **E-Wallet Form Type** (`e_wallet_form_type`): Choose the type of E-Wallet Form (Payment or Refund).
* **E-Wallet Merchant Info** (`merchant_info`): Choose the status of the E-Wallet (Test or Live).
* **E-Wallet Form ID** (`e_wallet_form_id`): Enter a unique numerical value for this E-Wallet Authorization.
* **Created By** (`created_by`): Optionally, enter the name of the individual who created this E-Wallet Authorization.
* **Created At** (`created_at`): Optionally, enter the date and time this E-Wallet was created.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`): Optionally, enter the name of the individual who updated this E-Wallet Authorization.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`): Optionally, enter the date and time this E-Wallet was updated.
* **E-Wallet Version** (`e_wallet_version`): Choose the E-Wallet Version.

Note: Fields marked as "optional" can be left blank if not applicable.
