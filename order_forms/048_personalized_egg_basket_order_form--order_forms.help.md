# Personalized Egg Basket Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a personalized egg basket order. It captures details such as customer information, egg basket type, quantity, unit price, and delivery instructions to ensure accurate and efficient processing of orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter the order date in the "Order Date" field.
3. Enter the order ID and order number in the "Order ID" and "Order Number" fields, respectively.
4. Enter the customer's email and phone number in the "Customer Email" and "Customer Phone" fields.
5. Enter the order total, shipping cost, and customer note in the "Order Total", "Shipping Cost", and "Customer Note" fields, respectively.
6. Select the status of the order by choosing from the "Order Status" dropdown menu.
7. Enter the customer's address in the "Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order ID** (`order_id`, text, required): Enter a unique identifier for the order.
* **Order Number** (`order_number`, number, required): Enter a unique number for the order.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the order.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, text, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, required): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Shipping Cost** (`shipping_cost`, number, required): Enter the shipping cost for the order.
* **Customer Note** (`customer_note`, note, required): Enter any additional notes about the customer.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of the order from the available options.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the customer's address.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments about the order.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, optional): Choose the method of contact from the available options.
* **Delivery Instructions** (`delivery_instructions`, note, optional): Enter any delivery instructions for the order.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of delivery.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of delivery.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of eggs ordered.
* **Unit Price** (`unit_price`, number, optional): Enter the unit price of the eggs.
* **Egg Basket Type** (`egg_basket_details`, text, optional): Enter details about the egg basket.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Enter information about the customer.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, optional): Choose the method of contact from the available options.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, note, optional): Enter any notes about the customer.
