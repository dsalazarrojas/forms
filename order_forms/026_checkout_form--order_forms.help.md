# Checkout Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect the necessary information for a successful checkout process. It's divided into sections that correspond to specific parts of the checkout process: shipping details, customer information, and payment preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your shipping details in the "Shipping Details" section.
2. Enter your customer information, including full name and email address.
3. In the "Billing Information" section, choose whether your billing address is the same as your shipping address or input different billing details.
4. Select your preferred payment method from the list.
5. If you have a promo code, enter it in the "Promo Code" field.
6. Review and agree to the "Terms and Conditions" and "Privacy Policy" sections.
7. Finally, enter your order total and delivery instructions if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (`customer_full_name`, required): Enter your full name as it should be displayed on the invoice and for communication.
- **Email Address** (`customer_email_address`, required): Enter your email address to receive order updates and notifications.
- **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address_street`, required): Enter your street address where the order will be delivered.
- **City** (`shipping_address_city`, required): Enter the city where your shipping address is located.
- **State / Province** (`shipping_address_state`, required): Enter the state or province where your shipping address is located.
- **Zip / Postal Code** (`shipping_address_zip`, required): Enter the zip or postal code of your shipping address.
- **Country** (`shipping_country_name`, required): Enter the country where your shipping address is located.
- **Billing Address (same as Shipping)** (`billing_same_as_shipping`, required, select_one): Choose whether your billing address is the same as your shipping address or select "False".
- **Preferred Payment Method** (`selected_payment_method`, required, select_one): Choose your preferred payment method from the list.
- **Promo Code** (`applied_promo_code`, optional): Enter your discount code, if applicable.
- **Order Total ($)** (`order_total_amount`, required): Enter the total amount of the order, including taxes and shipping.
- **Delivery Instructions** (`special_delivery_instructions`, optional): Enter any specific delivery instructions, such as "leave at front door".
- **I Agree to Terms and Conditions** (`terms_conditions_agreement`, required, select_one): Agree to the terms and conditions of the order.
- **I Agree to Privacy Policy** (`privacy_policy_agreement`, required, select_one): Agree to the privacy policy of the order.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
- Double-check your shipping and billing addresses for correct formatting and spelling.
- Choose the correct payment method and review the terms and conditions carefully before submitting the form.
- Use promo codes and delivery instructions as needed to ensure a smooth checkout process.
