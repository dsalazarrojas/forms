# Private Label Packaging Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for customers to provide their information and preferences for a private label packaging order. It is used to gather necessary details such as contact information, product specifications, and shipping instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer information in the "Customer Information" section.
2. Provide details about the product you are ordering in the "Product Info" section.
3. Specify your delivery preferences in the "Delivery Info" section.
4. Enter your shipping information in the "Shipping" section.
5. Specify any special packaging requirements in the "Packaging Specifications" section.
6. Enter your payment information in the "Payment Info" section.
7. Leave any additional comments or instructions in the "Customer Comments" section.
8. Review and confirm your billing information in the "Billing Info" section.
9. Provide the order details you would like to purchase in the "Order Details" section.
10. Enter your delivery address in the "Delivery Address" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information**: Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Product Info**: Describe the product you would like to order, including any specific features or requirements you need.
* **Delivery Info**: Specify your preferred delivery date, time, and any special delivery instructions.
* **Shipping**: Enter your shipping address and any shipping preferences, such as expedited shipping.
* **Packaging Specifications**: Specify any special packaging requirements, such as custom packaging or labeling.
* **Payment Info**: Enter your payment information, including your payment method and amount.
* **Customer Comments**: Leave any additional comments or instructions for our team.
* **Billing Info**: Review and confirm your billing information to ensure accuracy.
* **Order Details**: Provide the details of the order, including the product(s) being ordered and any relevant information.
* **Delivery Address**: Enter your delivery address, including any relevant details or instructions.
