# Seafood Platter Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seafood Platter Pre Order Form is used to gather information from customers to pre-order their desired seafood platter for pickup or delivery. This form helps us prepare for your special occasion or event, ensuring we have your preferred platter ready for you when you need it.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Pre Order Details page with information about your order, such as any special requests or instructions.
2. On the Pickup Details page, specify your preferred pickup location or method (if applicable).
3. On the Platter Type page, select the type of platter you would like to order (Platter or Other).
4. On the Delivery Details page, provide any additional information about your delivery preferences (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pre Order Details (text)**: Here, you can provide any special requests or instructions related to your platter order.
* **Pickup Details (text)**: If you prefer to pick up your platter at our location, specify the date, time, and location you would like to pick up your order.
* **Platter Type (select one)**: Choose between "Platter" (a standard platter option) and "Other" (if you have a custom or specific platter request).
* **Delivery Details (text)**: If you have chosen to have your platter delivered, provide the address where you would like it to be delivered, and any additional information to ensure smooth delivery.
