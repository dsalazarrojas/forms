To complete this form, follow these steps:

2. Choose the size of the item you need (e.g., extra small, small, medium, etc.).
3. Enter the color of the item.
4. Select the type of fabric the item is made of.
5. Enter the quantity of items you need.
6. Enter the delivery date for the order.
7. Enter the delivery time for the order.
8. Enter the customer's email address.
9. Enter the customer's phone number.
10. Enter the customer's address.
11. Enter the customer's city.
12. Enter the customer's state.
13. Enter the customer's zip code.
14. Enter the customer's country.
