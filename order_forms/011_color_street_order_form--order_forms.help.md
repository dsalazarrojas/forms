# Color Street Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Color Street Order Form is used to place an order for Color Street products. 
This form collects customer information and order details, which will be used to process your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your **Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Shipping Address**. These are required fields.
2. Select your preferred **Payment Method** (Venmo, PayPal, or Facebook Pay) and enter your associated **Payment ID/Username**.
3. Indicate if this order is a **Gift** and enter a **Gift Message** if applicable.
4. Choose the **Sets** you'd like to order and enter the **Total Number of Sets**.
5. Click the submit button to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, required): Enter your email address for contact and order updates.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, required): Enter your shipping address in the format "Street, City, State, ZIP" (e.g., "123 Main St, Anytown, CA, 12345").
* **List the sets you would like to order** (`sets_selection`, required): Enter the sets you would like to order, along with the quantity for each set.
* **Total Number of Sets** (`total_sets_count`, required): Enter the total number of sets you are ordering.
* **Is this order a gift?** (`is_this_a_gift`, required): Indicate if this order is a gift.
* **Gift Message** (`gift_message`, optional): Enter a gift message if this order is a gift.
* **How would you like to pay?** (`payment_choice`, required): Select your preferred payment method.
* **Payment ID/Username** (`payment_id`, required): Enter your associated payment ID or username.
* **Anything else I should know?** (`comments`, optional): Add any additional information or comments about your order.
