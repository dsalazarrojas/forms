# Custom Packaging Tape Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect all the necessary details for a custom packaging tape order, including order information, shipping details, art work, customer shipping info, customer info, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Provide all required details for the order, including any artwork.
2. Fill in the shipping details to ensure timely and accurate delivery.
3. Enter the customer's shipping information for delivery verification.
4. Enter the customer's information for record keeping purposes.
5. Confirm the payment details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details**: This field is for the order information and should include the order number, customer name, and order date (if available).
* **Shipping Details**: This section is used to provide the shipping information, including name, address, city, state, zip, country, and phone number (if applicable).
* **Artwork**: This field is for any additional art work or instructions that need to be included with the order.
* **Customer Shipping Info**: This field is for the customer's shipping address, used for delivery verification.
* **Customer Info**: This field is for the customer's contact information, used for record keeping purposes.
* **Payment Details**: This field is for the payment amount and method used for the order.
* **Total**: This field is for the total amount of the order, automatically calculated and displayed after filling out the form.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields (if any) and provide accurate information for a smooth order process.
- Double-check the information before submitting the form to avoid any errors or discrepancies.
