# Scout's Popcorn Sales Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for Scout leaders to order popcorn for their troops. It gathers essential information about each order, such as the Scout's name, order details, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Scout's name in the "Scout name" field.
2. Select the order date and time in the "Order date" and "Order time" fields.
3. Choose the type of popcorn ordered in the "Popcorn type" field.
4. Enter the quantity of popcorn ordered.
5. Enter the Scout leader's contact information, including their email address and phone number in the "Scout leader" fields.
6. Enter the customer's contact information, including their name, email address, and phone number in the "Customer" fields.
7. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Customer notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Scout name** (`scout_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the Scout who is ordering the popcorn.
* **Order date** (`order_date`, `date`, required): Select the date on which the order is scheduled.
* **Order time** (`order_time`, `time`, required): Select the time on which the order is scheduled.
* **Popcorn type** (`popcorn_type`, `text`, required): Choose the type of popcorn ordered.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, required): Enter the number of units of popcorn ordered.
* **Scout leader** (`scout_leader`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the Scout leader who is submitting the order.
* **Scout leader email** (`scout_leader_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the Scout leader.
* **Scout leader phone** (`scout_leader_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the Scout leader.
* **Customer name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the customer.
* **Customer email** (`customer_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the customer.
* **Customer phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the customer.
* **Customer notes** (`customer_notes`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Customer ID** (`customer_id`, `number`, required): Enter the ID number of the customer.
* **Total** (`total`, `number`, required): Enter the total cost of the order.
