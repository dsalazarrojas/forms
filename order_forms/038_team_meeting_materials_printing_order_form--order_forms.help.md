# Team Meeting Materials Printing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for printing team meeting materials.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Select Meeting Materials
Select the type of meeting materials you need printed. Options include "Option 1" and "Handouts". Choose the one that best suits your needs.

### Step 2: Specify File Requests
Enter the number of files you need printed. This field is required and should be a whole number.

### Step 3: Enter Quantity
Enter the quantity of meeting materials you need printed. This field is required and should be a whole number.

### Step 4: Provide Delivery Date and Time
Enter the delivery date and time you prefer for your meeting materials. This field is required.

### Step 5: Enter Delivery Location
Enter the location where you would like to receive your meeting materials. (Optional)

### Step 6: Provide Contact Person Information
Enter the name of the person responsible for receiving the meeting materials. This field is required and should be in the format "First Name Last Name".

### Step 7: Specify Office/Department
Enter the name of the office or department responsible for the meeting. (Optional)

### Step 8: Add Delivery Notes
Enter any additional information you would like to convey to the person delivering the meeting materials. (Optional)

### Step 9: Enter Email and Phone Number
Enter your email address and phone number for communication purposes. These fields are required.

### Step 10: Enter Print Request Comments
Enter any comments or requests related to the meeting materials. This field is required.

### Step 11: Specify Meeting Location
Enter the location of the meeting where the meeting materials will be used. This field is required.

### Step 12: Provide Meeting Date and Time
Enter the date and time of the meeting where the meeting materials will be used. This field is required.

### Step 13: Specify Meeting Start and End Time
Enter the start and end time of the meeting. (Optional)

### Step 14: Add Meeting Location Notes
Enter any additional notes about the meeting location. (Optional)

### Step 15: Enter Meeting Date Notes
Enter any notes about the meeting date. (Optional)

### Step 16: Enter Meeting Time Notes
Enter any notes about the meeting time. (Optional)

### Step 17: Assign to a Team Member
Enter the name of the team member responsible for the meeting. This field is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Materials** (Select One): Select the type of meeting materials you need printed.
* **File Requests** (Number): Enter the number of files you need printed.
* **Quantity** (Number): Enter the quantity of meeting materials you need printed.
* **Delivery Date** (Date): Enter the delivery date you prefer for your meeting materials.
* **Delivery Time** (Time): Enter the delivery time you prefer for your meeting materials.
* **Delivery Location** (Text): Enter the location where you would like to receive your meeting materials.
* **Contact Person** (Text): Enter the name of the person responsible for receiving the meeting materials.
* **Office/Department** (Text): Enter the name of the office or department responsible for the meeting.
* **Delivery Notes** (Text): Enter any additional information you would like to convey to the person delivering the meeting materials.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Print Request Comments** (Text): Enter any comments or requests related to the meeting materials.
* **Meeting Location** (Text): Enter the location of the meeting where the meeting materials will be used.
* **Meeting Date** (Date): Enter the date of the meeting where the meeting materials will be used.
* **Meeting Time** (Time): Enter the time of the meeting where the meeting materials will be used.
* **Meeting Start Time** (Time): Enter the start time of the meeting. (Optional)
* **Meeting End Time** (Time): Enter the end time of the meeting. (Optional)
* **Meeting Location Notes** (Text): Enter any additional notes about the meeting location. (Optional)
* **Meeting Date Notes** (Text): Enter any notes about the meeting date. (Optional)
* **Meeting Time Notes** (Text): Enter any notes about the meeting time. (Optional)
* **Meeting Materials Request** (Text): Enter any specific request for meeting materials.
* **Meeting Materials Request Notes** (Text): Enter any additional information related to the meeting materials request. (Optional)
* **Assigned To** (Text): Enter the name of the team member responsible for the meeting. This field is required.
