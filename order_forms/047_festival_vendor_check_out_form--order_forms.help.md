# festival_vendor_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The festival_vendor_check_out_form is used for vendors to check out from the festival. It collects information from vendors for administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the vendor name in the first field.
2. Enter the vendor's address in the second field.
3. Provide the vendor's contact number in the third field.
4. Enter the vendor's contact email in the fourth field.
5. Enter the payment amount in the fifth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **vendor name (vendor name, text, required)**: Enter the name of the vendor.
* **vendor address (vendor address, text, required)**: Enter the address of the vendor.
* **vendor contact number (vendor contact number, text, required)**: Enter the contact number of the vendor.
* **vendor contact email (vendor contact email, email, required)**: Enter the contact email of the vendor.
* **payment amount (payment amount, number, required)**: Enter the payment amount due from the vendor.
