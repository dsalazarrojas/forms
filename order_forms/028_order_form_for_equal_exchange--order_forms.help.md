# Order Form For Equal Exchange - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer information and order details for the Equal Exchange platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Customer Details" page and fill in your name, email, and any other relevant details.
2. Move on to the "Order Details" page and provide your order information, including product or service name, quantity, and price.
3. If applicable, move to the "Payment Details" page and fill in your payment information.
4. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Order Notes" and "Customer Note" fields, if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details** (`Customer Details`, text, optional): Fill in your name, email, and any other relevant details.
* **Order Details** (`Order Details`, text, optional): Provide your order information, including product or service name, quantity, and price.
* **Payment Details** (`Payment Details`, text, optional): Fill in your payment information, if applicable.
* **Order Notes** (`Order Notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Customer Note** (`Customer Note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or questions for the platform.
