# Food Delivery Service Opt In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Delivery Service Opt In form is a tool for customers to express their interest in receiving food delivery from our service. This form helps us understand your preferences and needs for a personalized delivery experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" if you are interested in our food delivery service.
2. Enter your Customer Name.
3. Provide your Email address.
4. Enter your Contact Number (phone number).
5. Choose your preferred Delivery Date and Time.
6. Enter your Delivery Address.
7. Provide Order Details (e.g., what you'd like to order).
8. Enter the total value of your Order.
9. Choose your preferred Payment Method.
10. Add any additional Customer Notes.
11. If you have a preferred Food Type, please let us know.
12. If you have a special Restaurant Choice, select "Yes" and we'll try to accommodate it.
13. Check the Order Status box if your order is Active or Inactive.
14. Enter Date of Birth and Time of Birth for your records.
15. Enter your Age for age verification purposes.
16. Enter the Food Item you'd like to order.
17. Choose the Order Date and Time you'd like to order.
18. Leave any Delivery Note for our team.
19. Choose the Food Delivery Time.
20. Provide any additional Order Notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Food Delivery Service** (Select One): 
  Select "Yes" if you are interested in our food delivery service.
* **Customer Name** (Text): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Number** (Text): Enter your phone number.
* **Delivery Date** (Date): Enter your preferred delivery date.
* **Delivery Time** (Time): Enter your preferred delivery time.
* **Delivery Address** (Text): Enter your delivery address.
* **Order Details** (Text): Provide the details of your order (e.g., what you'd like to order).
* **Order Total** (Number): Enter the total value of your order.
* **Payment Method** (Select Multiple): Choose your preferred payment method.
* **Customer Notes** (Note): Leave any additional customer notes or requests.
* **Food Type** (Text): If you have a preferred food type, please let us know.
* **Restaurant Choice** (Select One): If you have a special restaurant preference, select "Yes" and we'll do our best to accommodate it.
* **Order Status** (Select One): Check the box if your order is Active or Inactive.
* **Date of Birth** (Date): Enter your date of birth.
* **Time of Birth** (Time): Enter your time of birth.
* **Age** (Number): Enter your age for age verification purposes.
* **Food Item** (Text): Enter the food item you'd like to order.
* **Order Date** (Date): Enter the date you'd like to order.
* **Order Time** (Time): Enter the time you'd like to order.
* **Delivery Note** (Note): Leave any delivery notes for our team.
* **Food Delivery Time** (Time): Enter the time you'd like your food delivered.
* **Order Notes** (Text): Provide any additional order notes.
* **Customer Choice** (Text): Leave any additional customer choice or preference information.
