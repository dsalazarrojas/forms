# Chicken Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chicken Order Form is designed to collect information from customers to place an order for chicken products. It helps gather details about the customer, their order preferences, and payment methods to streamline the ordering process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the instructions and information provided in this guide before filling out the form.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
- Choose your options for each field based on your preferences.
- Double-check your submission for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Provide your full name or business name.
- **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): If you are ordering for a business, provide your business name.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication and order updates.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number for delivery and order inquiries.
- **Delivery Address** (`address`, text, required): Provide your full delivery address for efficient delivery.
- **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required): Choose the date you need the order delivered.
- **Required Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required): Choose the date you need the order delivered.
- **Customer Type** (`customer_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of customer you are (Individual, Restaurant, etc.).
- **Type of Chicken** (`chicken_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of chicken you want to order (Broiler/Fryer, etc.).
- **Chicken Parts** (`chicken_parts`, select_multiple, required): Select the chicken parts you need (Whole chicken, Breast, etc.).
- **Fresh or Frozen** (`fresh_frozen`, select_one, required): Choose whether you want fresh or frozen chicken.
- **Skin Preference** (`skin_on_off`, select_one, required): Select whether you want skin on or off chicken.
- **Bone Preference** (`bone_in_out`, select_one, required): Choose whether you want bone-in or boneless chicken.
- **Quantity in Pounds** (`quantity_pounds`, number, required): Enter the total pounds of chicken needed.
- **Quantity in Pieces** (`quantity_pieces`, number, optional): Enter the total pieces if applicable.
- **Packaging Preference** (`packaging_preference`, select_one, required): Choose how you want the chicken packaged (Bulk, Individual, etc.).
- **Grade or Quality** (`grade_quality`, select_one, required): Choose the quality grade you prefer (Grade A, Premium, etc.).
- **Brand Preference** (`brand_preference`, text, optional): If you have a preferred brand, enter it here.
- **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you want the order delivered (Standard, Express, etc.).
- **Delivery Instructions** (`delivery_instructions`, text, optional): Provide any special delivery instructions.
- **Storage Requirements** (`storage_requirements`, text, optional): Enter any special storage needs.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you want to pay (Credit card, Debit card, etc.).
- **Account Number** (`account_number`, text, optional): If you have an account with us, enter the account number.
- **Purchase Order Number** (`purchase_order_number`, text, optional): If you have a PO number, enter it here.
- **Recurring Order** (`recurring_order`, select_one, required): Select whether this is a recurring order (Yes, No, etc.).
- **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any special requirements.
- **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_accepted`, select_one, required): Select whether you accept the terms and conditions.

## Tips
- Double-check your submission for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
- Ensure you have provided all required fields correctly.
- Read the terms and conditions before accepting them.
- Contact customer support if you have any questions or need further clarification.
