## Streaming Package Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from customers interested in purchasing a streaming package.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name in the "customer_name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "customer_email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Indicate if you are interested in a streaming package by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "streaming_package" field.
5. Enter any additional details about your pre-order in the "pre_order_details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **customer_name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
- **customer_email** (email, required): Enter your valid email address.
- **phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
- **streaming_package** (select multiple, required): Select "Yes" if you are interested in purchasing a streaming package, otherwise select "No".
- **start_date** (date, optional): Enter the start date for your streaming package (if applicable).
- **pre_order_details** (note, optional): Enter any additional details or comments about your pre-order.
