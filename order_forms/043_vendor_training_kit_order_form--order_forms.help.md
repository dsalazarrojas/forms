# Vendor Training Kit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Training Kit Order Form is a tool for vendors to place orders for training kits from an online platform or system. This form captures necessary information for processing the order, including vendor details, shipping information, and delivery method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your details and select the desired kits and materials for your order.
2.  Select the shipping method and address for delivery.
3.  Enter your email address to receive notifications and confirmations.
4.  Enter your contact number for customer support purposes.
5.  Enter the order date and time in the correct format.
6.  Add any additional order notes if necessary.
7.  Review your order before submitting it to ensure accuracy and correctness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Vendor Details**: This field is for you to enter your vendor name and other identifying information. Please enter accurate and up-to-date details to facilitate order processing.
- **Shipping Details**: Enter the shipping details, including the address and other relevant information for delivery.
- **Kit Selection**: Select one or more kits from the available options. Please ensure you select the correct kits for your order.
- **Shipping Method**: Choose the shipping method that best suits your needs, including ground, air, or courier services.
- **Delivery Method**: Select the delivery method that you prefer, and it will be used to process your order.
- **Training Materials**: Select any additional training materials required for your kit.
- **Order Notes**: Enter any additional information or comments about your order in this field.
- **Vendor Email**: Enter your email address to receive notifications and confirmations related to your order.
- **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number for customer support purposes.
- **Order Date**: Enter the date of your order in the correct format.
- **Order Time**: Enter the time of your order in the correct format.
- **Order Notes**: Enter any additional comments or information about your order in this field.
