# delivery_order_form_pedro - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from customers for delivery purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact details.
2. Provide delivery instructions for a smooth delivery process.
3. Enter your delivery address.
4. If different from the contact details, provide the contact person's name.
5. Enter your contact number.
6. Provide your email address.
7. If necessary, add any order notes.
8. Select the payment terms for your order.
9. Specify the delivery date for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details**: This field is required to capture the customer's contact information.
* **Delivery Instructions**: This field is required to collect special delivery instructions.
* **Delivery Address**: This field is required for delivery address.
* **Contact Person**: This optional field is used to capture the name of the contact person responsible for the order.
* **Contact Number**: This field is required for contact number.
* **Email**: This field is required for customer's email address.
* **Order Notes**: This optional field is used to collect any additional information about the order.

* **Delivery Date**: This field is required to schedule the delivery date.
* **Payment Terms**: This field has three options: Cash, Credit Card, and Other.
