# fashion_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "fashion_order_form" is a form designed for customers to provide information necessary to place an order. The form gathers customer details, contact information, and specifics about the order, ensuring that all required information is collected in a structured manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. On the first page, please enter your **Customer Name**. This is a mandatory field.
2. On the second page, enter the **Order Date** and **Order Time**. These fields are also required.
3. On the next page, provide your **Contact Phone Number** and **Email**. Again, these are mandatory fields.
4. On the following page, enter your **Address**, **City**, and **State**. These are also required fields.
5. Next, enter your **Zip Code**. This is a mandatory field.
6. On the final page, enter **Order Details** and choose the **Assigned Tool**. Please be as detailed as possible when providing order specifics. Finally, if you have any additional **Customer Message**, you can enter it here. This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Order Date**: The date of the order.
* **Order Time**: The time of the order.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Address**: Enter your full address where the order will be delivered or received.
* **City**: Enter the city where you would like to receive the order.
* **State**: Enter the state where the order will be received.
* **Zip Code**: Enter the zip code of the address.
* **Order Details**: Provide detailed information about the order, including any special instructions or requirements.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool assigned for the order.
* **Customer Message**: If you have any specific messages or comments for the person handling the order, enter them here.
