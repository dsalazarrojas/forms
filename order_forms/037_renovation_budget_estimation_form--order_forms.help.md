# Renovation Budget Estimation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users estimate the budget for a renovation project. It is intended for renovation projects only and should not be used for other types of projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the contractors you would like to consider for your project from the list provided.
2. Enter the location of your project.
3. Choose the type of project you are undertaking (e.g., kitchen renovation, bathroom renovation, etc.)
4. Estimate the total cost of your project.
5. Provide a breakdown of your budget, including any specific costs you would like to include (e.g., materials, labor, etc.)
6. Enter the start and end dates of your project.
7. Specify the area of your project that will be renovated.
8. Enter the total costs of materials and labor for your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Contractor** (`contractor_selection`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the contractors you would like to consider for your project. You can choose one or more contractors from the provided list.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of your project. This could be a specific room or area within a building.
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, text, required: false): Choose the type of project you are undertaking (e.g., kitchen renovation, bathroom renovation, etc.)
* **Estimated Cost** (`estimated_cost`, number, required: false): Enter the total cost of your project.
* **Budget Breakdown** (`budget_breakdown`, text, required: false): Provide a breakdown of your budget, including any specific costs you would like to include (e.g., materials, labor, etc.)
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the start date of your project.
* **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the end date of your project.
* **Renovation Area** (`renovation_area`, text, required: false): Specify the area of your project that will be renovated.
* **Materials Costs** (`materials_costs`, number, required: false): Enter the total costs of materials for your project.
* **Labor Costs** (`labor_costs`, number, required: false): Enter the total costs of labor for your project.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all the required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can always contact us for clarification.
* This form is only intended for renovation projects, so please do not fill it out for other types of projects.
