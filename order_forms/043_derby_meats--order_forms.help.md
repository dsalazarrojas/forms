# Derby Meats - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Derby Meats form is used to collect customer and order information for processing and fulfillment.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your order details in the "Order Form" section.
2. Provide your customer information in the "Customer Information" section.
3. Enter your shipping information in the "Shipping Information" section.
4. Select the date and time for your order in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about your order in the "Customer Note" field.
6. Enter your email address and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone" fields.
7. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
8. Add any additional comments about your order in the "Order Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Form** (`order_form`, text, required:false): Enter your order details in this field. Please provide a clear and concise description of what you are ordering.
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required:false): Enter your name and any other relevant customer information in this field.
* **Shipping Information** (`shipping_info`, text, required:false): Enter the shipping details for your order in this field.
* **Date** (`order_date`, date, required:false): Select the date for your order.
* **Time** (`order_time`, time, required:false): Select the time for your order.
* **Customer Note** (`customer_note`, text, required:false): Add any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Please enter a note about this order** (`customer_note_hint`, note, required:false): This field is a reminder to add any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Email** (`customer_email`, email, required:false): Enter your email address in this field.
* **Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required:false): Enter your phone number in this field.
* **Address** (`customer_address`, text, required:false): Enter your address in this field.
* **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, text, required:false): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
