# direct_debit_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The direct debit authorization form is used to set up and manage direct debit authorizations for customer accounts. This form collects necessary information for creating and updating direct debit authorizations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name in the **Customer Name** field (Field 3).
2. Enter the customer's email in the **Email** field (Field 4). Ensure the email format is correct (e.g., `example@example.com`).
3. Enter the customer's phone number in the **Phone** field (Field 5). Please format the number without any special characters or spaces (e.g., `1234567890`).
4. Enter the routing number for the customer's account in the **Routing Number** field (Field 6).
5. Enter the account number for the customer's account in the **Account Number** field (Field 7).
6. Enter the customer's address in the **Customer Address** field (Field 8).
7. Select the **Account Type** (Field 11) of the customer's account (e.g., Checking, Savings, or Savings/Check).
8. Select the **Account Ending** (Field 12) of the customer's account (e.g., 0, 1, or 2).
9. Select the **Debit Amount** (Field 13) for the direct debit authorization (e.g., 10, 20, or 30).
10. Choose the **Start Date** (Field 14) for the direct debit authorization.
11. Choose the **End Date** (Field 15) for the direct debit authorization.
12. Select the **Recurring Frequency** (Field 16) for the direct debit authorization (e.g., Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
13. Enter the **Bank Transit** number for the customer's account (Field 17).
14. Enter the routing number ending for the customer's account in the **Routing Number Ending** field (Field 18).
15. Enter any additional notes for the customer in the **Customer Note** field (Field 19).
16. Enter any additional notes for the bank in the **Bank Name Ending** field (Field 20).
17. Enter the email ending for the customer in the **Email Ending** field (Field 21).
18. Enter the phone ending for the customer in the **Phone Ending** field (Field 22).
19. Enter the account number ending for the customer in the **Account Number Ending** field (Field 23).
20. Enter the account type ending for the customer in the **Account Type Ending** field (Field 24).
