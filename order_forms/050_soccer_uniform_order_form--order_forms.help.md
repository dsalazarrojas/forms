# Soccer Uniform Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soccer Uniform Order Form is used by teams or organizations to collect orders for team uniforms. This form gathers essential information about the order, including contact information, order details, and payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the `first_name` field.
2. Enter your last name in the `last_name` field.
3. Provide your contact email address in the `contact_email` field.
4. Specify your team name in the `team_name` field.
5. Enter the date of the order in the `order_date` field.
6. Select the type of order you would like to place (Online, In-store, or Phone) in the `order_type` field.
7. Enter the total cost of the order in the `order_total` field.
8. Select your preferred payment method (Square, PayPal, or Bank transfer) in the `payment_method` field.
9. Enter the shipping address in the `shipping_address` field.
10. Provide your phone number in the `phone` field.
11. Enter the date for shipping in the `shipping_date` field.
12. Add any comments about the order in the `comments` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Team/Club** (`team_name`, text, required): Enter your team name.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the order.
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of order (Online, In-store, or Phone).
* **Total** (`order_total`, number, required): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method (Square, PayPal, or Bank transfer).
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required): Enter the shipping address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Shipping Date** (`shipping_date`, date, required): Enter the date for shipping.
* **Comments/Additional Information** (`comments`, text, required): Add any comments about the order.

Note: Some fields may be optional, but they are still required for the form to be completed correctly. Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure a successful order submission.
