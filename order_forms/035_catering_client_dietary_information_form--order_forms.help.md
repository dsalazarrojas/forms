# Catering Client Dietary Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect detailed information about each guest's dietary requirements and restrictions for an upcoming event. This information is crucial for the catering team to provide safe and suitable food options for all guests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Event Details** section with the event name and date.
2. Enter the **Primary Host/Client Name** to identify the person responsible for the event.
3. For each guest, fill in their **Full Name**, **Dietary Restrictions or Preferences**, **Severe Food Allergies**, and **Severity of Allergy**.
4. If a guest has a severe food allergy, indicate the severity level using the **Allergy Severity** options.
5. Select **Yes/No** for **Does the guest carry an EpiPen or medication?** and provide the **Guest Contact Phone Number** if needed.
6. Enter any **Additional Notes** or **Alternative Meal Preference** if required.
7. Finally, confirm the accuracy of the provided information by selecting **I Agree** for **I certify that the dietary information provided is accurate**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event or meeting.
* **Date of Event**: Enter the date of the event or meeting.
* **Primary Host/Client Name**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the event.
* **Guest Full Name**: Enter the full name of each guest.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Preferences**: Select all applicable dietary options for each guest (e.g., Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, etc.).
* **Severe Food Allergies**: Select all applicable severe food allergies (e.g., Peanuts, Tree Nuts, Shellfish, etc.).
* **Allergy Severity**: Indicate the severity level of the allergy (Mild, Moderate, Severe).
* **Does the guest carry an EpiPen or medication?**: Select **Yes** if the guest carries an EpiPen or medication, **No** otherwise.
* **Guest Contact Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the guest, if needed.
* **Alternative Meal Preference**: Enter any alternative meal preference or special request for the guest.
* **Additional Notes for the Chef**: Enter any additional information or notes for the chef, such as cross-contamination concerns or ingredients to avoid.
* **I certify that the dietary information provided is accurate**: Select **I Agree** to confirm the accuracy of the provided information.
* **Form completed by**: If different from the guest, enter the name of the person completing the form.

Note: This form is designed to be filled out by the guest or their representative, and the **Form completed by** field is meant to capture the name of the person completing the form on behalf of the guest.
