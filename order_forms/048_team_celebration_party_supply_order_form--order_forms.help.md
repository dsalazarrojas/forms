# Team Celebration Party Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Celebration Party Supply Order Form is a tool to gather information about team celebrations and provide supplies for the occasion. This form will help us understand the theme, size, and preferences of the party to make it a memorable event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your team celebration details, including estimated guests, budget, and party theme.
2. Select your party supplies preferences, such as theme and dessert options.
3. Choose your delivery preferences, including date, time, and method.
4. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
5. Add any additional comments or requests for your order.
6. Submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Celebration Party Details** (`team_celebration_party_details`): Provide a brief description of the team celebration, including estimated number of guests and any other relevant details.
* **Select Party Supplies** (`supply_selections`): Select the party supplies you require, such as "Yes" or "No" for each option.
* **Delivery Preferences** (`delivery_preferences`): Choose your preferred delivery date and time.
* **Party Theme** (`party_theme`): Select the party theme you want to decorate with.
* **Party Color Scheme** (`party_color_scheme`): Select your preferred party color scheme.
* **Party Color Schemes** (`party_color_schemes`): Select additional color schemes you'd like to use.
* **Budget** (`budget`): Enter your budget for the party supplies.
* **Budget Amount** (`budget_amount`): Enter the specific amount of your budget.
* **Estimated Guests** (`estimated_guests`): Enter the estimated number of guests attending the party.
* **Estimated Guests Range** (`estimated_guests_range`): Select the guest range, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Party Dessert** (`party_dessert`): Select your preferred party dessert option.
* **Party Dessert Option** (`party_dessert_option`): Select your preferred dessert option, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`): Enter the preferred delivery date for the party supplies.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`): Enter the preferred delivery time for the party supplies.
* **Delivery Time Range** (`delivery_time_range`): Select the delivery time range, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`): Select your preferred delivery method, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`): Enter your delivery address for the party supplies.
* **Delivery Address Option** (`delivery_address_option`): Enter any additional delivery address options.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Order Comments** (`order_comments`): Add any additional comments or requests for your order.
* **Team Leads** (`team_leads`): Select the team leads who will be attending the party.
* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`): Select the team lead responsible for the party.
* **Party Supplies** (`party_supplies`): Select the party supplies you require, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Submit** (`submit`): Review and submit your order.
