# Basic Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer information and order details for processing orders. It consists of several sections that guide the customer through the ordering process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer information in the first section, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the item you would like to order and enter its number or code.
3. Provide a description of the item you are ordering.
4. Enter the quantity of the item you would like to order.
5. In the "Additional Items" section, you can add any extra items you would like to include in your order (optional).
6. Enter your shipping address, including city, state/province, and ZIP/postal code.
7. Review the order summary to ensure everything is accurate.
8. Confirm the order is accurate and complete by selecting the "I Confirm" option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Customer Information --** (`section_header_1`): This section collects your customer information.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`customer_email`): Enter your valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`customer_phone`): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **-- Item Selection --** (`section_header_2`): This section allows you to select the item you would like to order.
* **Item Number or Code** (`item_number`): Enter the number or code of the item you would like to order.
* **Item Description** (`item_description`): Provide a brief description of the item you are ordering.
* **Quantity Ordered** (`item_quantity`): Enter the number of items you would like to order.
* **Unit Price** (`unit_price`): Enter the price of the item per unit (optional).
* **Line Total** (`line_total`): Calculated based on the item quantity and unit price (hidden field, auto-calculated).
* **-- Additional Items --** (`section_header_3`): This section allows you to add any extra items to your order (optional).
* **Additional Item Number** (`additional_item_1`): Enter any extra items you would like to include in your order.
* **Quantity for Additional Item** (`additional_qty_1`): Enter the quantity of the additional item.
* **-- Shipping Address --** (`section_header_4`): This section collects your shipping address.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`): Enter your shipping address.
* **City** (`shipping_city`): Enter the city where you would like to have your order shipped.
* **State/Province** (`shipping_state`): Enter the state or province where you would like to have your order shipped.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (`shipping_zip`): Enter the ZIP or postal code where you would like to have your order shipped.
* **-- Order Summary --** (`section_header_5`): This section summarizes your order details.
* **Subtotal** (`order_subtotal`): Calculated based on the item quantity and unit price (hidden field, auto-calculated).
* **Shipping Cost** (`shipping_cost`): Calculated based on shipping address and other factors (hidden field, auto-calculated).
* **Tax Amount** (`tax_amount`): Calculated based on shipping address and other factors (hidden field, auto-calculated).
* **Order Total** (`order_total`): Calculated based on subtotal, shipping cost, and tax amount (hidden field, auto-calculated).
* **-- Order Confirmation --** (`section_header_6`): This section confirms your order details.
* **I Confirm This Order is Accurate and Complete** (`order_confirmed`): Select this option to confirm that your order is accurate and complete.
