# Last-Mile-Delivery-Intake-Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Last-Mile-Delivery-Intake-Form is a form used to collect information about a customer's delivery needs. This form is designed to gather essential details such as customer name, address, email, phone number, delivery date and time, and any special instructions or notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter the customer's delivery address in the "Delivery Address" field.
3. Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field.
4. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
5. Select the delivery date from the calendar icon next to the "Delivery Date" field.
6. Select the delivery time from the time picker next to the "Delivery Time" field.
7. Choose the delivery status from the dropdown menu next to the "Delivery Status" field.
8. Enter any special instructions or notes in the "Special Instructions" field.
9. Optionally, enter the delivery date and time in the "Delivery Date and Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`Customer Name`, Text, required/optional): Enter the customer's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Delivery Address** (`Delivery Address`, Text, required/optional): Enter the customer's delivery address, including street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Customer Email** (`Customer Email`, Email, required/optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`Customer Phone`, Text, required/optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Delivery Date** (`Delivery Date`, Date, required/optional): Select the date for the delivery from the calendar icon.
* **Delivery Time** (`Delivery Time`, Time, required/optional): Select the time for the delivery from the time picker.
* **Delivery Status** (`Delivery Status`, Select One, required/optional): Choose the status of the delivery from the dropdown menu:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
* **Delivery Notes** (`Delivery Notes`, Note, required/optional): Enter any notes or comments about the delivery.
* **Special Instructions** (`Special Instructions`, Text, required/optional): Enter any special instructions or requirements for the delivery.
* **Delivery Date and Time** (`Delivery Date and Time`, Date, required/optional): Select the date and time for the delivery from the calendar icon.
