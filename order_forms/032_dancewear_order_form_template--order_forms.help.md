# dancewear_order_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from dancers to process their orders for dancewear correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Order Form
* Fill out the Order Form section with the required information, such as any relevant details about the order.

### Step 2: Dancer Info
* Enter your name in the Dancer Info Name field.
* Enter your phone number in the Dancer Info Phone field.
* Enter your email address in the Dancer Info Email field.
* Select your date of birth (DOB) from the calendar.
* Enter your school or affiliation in the Dancer Info School field.
* Select your level of dance experience in the Dancer Info Level field.
* Select your gender from the options.
* Enter any additional notes in the Dancer Info Notes field.

### Step 3: Dancewear Items
* Select the dancewear item you are ordering.
* Enter the size of the dancewear item in the Dancewear Item Size field.
* Enter the color of the dancewear item in the Dancewear Item Color field.
* Enter the quantity of the dancewear item in the Dancewear Item Quantity field.

### Step 4: Payment Method
* Select your preferred payment method from the options.
* If you choose 'Other', enter the details of your payment method in the Other Payment Method field.

### Step 5: Dancer Info (continued)
* Enter your address in the Dancer Info Address field.
* Enter your city in the Dancer Info City field.
* Enter your province or state in the Dancer Info Province field.
* Enter your country in the Dancer Info Country field.
* Enter your postal code in the Dancer Info Postal Code field.
* Enter any additional notes in the Dancer Info Notes field.

### Step 6: Date and Time
* Select the date and time for the order to be processed.
