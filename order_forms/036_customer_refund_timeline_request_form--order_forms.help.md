# Customer Refund Timeline Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about customers' refund requests, enabling us to process their requests accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter the "Order ID" associated with the order you'd like to refund.
3. Provide a brief description of the issue with your order in the "Issue Description" field.
4. Specify the refund amount and type in the "Refund Amount" and "Refund Type" fields.
5. Enter the date and time of purchase in the "Date of Purchase" and "Time of Purchase" fields.
6. Select the status of your refund request from the "Refund Status" options.
7. Add any additional comments or notes about your refund request in the "Notes" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
9. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your order. This will help us identify you as the customer and process your refund request.
* **Order ID**: Enter the unique ID of the order you'd like to refund. This helps us locate the correct order in our system.
* **Issue Description**: Provide a brief description of the issue with your order. This will help us understand why you're requesting a refund.
* **Refund Amount**: Enter the amount you'd like to refund. This is the amount we'll refund you, in the currency of your original purchase.
* **Refund Type**: Select the type of refund you'd like to receive, either a Refund Amount or Refund Store Credit.
* **Date of Purchase**: Enter the date you made the purchase. This helps us determine when the refund should be processed.
* **Time of Purchase**: Enter the time you made the purchase. This helps us verify the purchase details.
* **Refund Status**: Select the status of your refund request from the options provided: Pending, Approved, or Denied.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your refund request. This helps us understand your situation and process your request efficiently.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This is how we'll contact you about your refund status.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number. This is how we'll contact you about your refund status, if necessary.
