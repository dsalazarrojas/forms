# organic_produce_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The organic_produce_subscription_form is a comprehensive form designed to collect customer information and preferences for managing organic produce subscriptions. 

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read the form sections below to understand each field's purpose.
- Complete each field accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **customer** (`customer`, text, required): Enter your name and contact information as per your preference. 
- **delivery_address** (`delivery_address`, text, required): Provide the address where you would like your organic produce to be delivered. 
- **produce_selection** (`produce_selection`, select_multiple, required): Select the types of organic produce you are interested in subscribing to. 
  * Choose from the options provided, e.g., fruits, vegetables, or herbs.
  * Multiple selections are allowed.
- **subscription_plan** (`subscription_plan`, select_one, required): Choose a subscription plan that suits your needs. 
  * This might include options like frequency of delivery, subscription duration, etc.
- **start_date** (`start_date`, date, required): Select the date you would like your subscription to start. 
  * Enter the date in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
- **end_date** (`end_date`, date, required): Select the date you would like your subscription to end. 
  * Enter the date in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
- **frequency** (`frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how frequently you would like to receive your organic produce. 
  * Options might include weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly.
- **payment_method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method. 
  * Options might include credit card, bank transfer, or online payment.
- **comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or requests you have regarding your subscription. 
- **notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or details about your subscription. 
- **billing_address** (`billing_address`, text, required): Enter your billing address. 
  * This is usually the same as your delivery address, but might be different in case you want to set up a different billing address.
- **delivery_frequency** (`delivery_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose how frequently you would like to receive your organic produce. 
  * Options might include weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly.
- **delivery_date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): Select the date you would like to receive your organic produce. 
  * Enter the date in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
- **subscription_type** (`subscription_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of subscription you would like to have. 
  * Options might include standard, premium, or organic.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- Choose wisely when selecting your produce preferences, subscription plan, and payment method.
- Use the comments and notes fields to provide any additional information that might help the customer management team process your request efficiently. 
- Ensure your billing address is accurate to avoid any payment processing issues.
