# Steepted Tea Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Steepted Tea Order Form is used to collect customer information and preferences for their tea orders. This form allows customers to select their preferred type of tea, flavor, and other essential details for processing their orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of tea you'd like to order from the options provided.
2. Choose the desired flavor(s) for your selected tea.
3. Enter your customer name as you'd like it to be displayed on your order.
4. Select a delivery date for your order.
5. Enter your shipping address, city, and zip code for delivery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product**: Select the type of tea you'd like to order. You can choose from:
	+ Black Tea
	+ Green Tea
	+ Herbal Tea
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of tea units you'd like to order (optional).
* **Flavor**: Choose the flavor(s) you'd like to have with your tea. You can select one or multiple flavors:
	+ Vanilla
	+ Lemon
	+ Mint
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as you'd like it to be displayed on your order.
* **Delivery Date**: Select a date for your order delivery.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your shipping address where you'd like your tea to be delivered.
* **Shipping City**: Enter the city of your shipping address.
* **Shipping State**: Enter the state of your shipping address.
* **Shipping Zip**: Enter the zip code of your shipping address.

## Tips
- Make sure to select the correct product type and flavor(s) you'd like to order.
- Ensure your shipping address is accurate to avoid delays or errors with delivery.
- If you have any special requests or instructions for your order, please include them in the "shipping address" field.
