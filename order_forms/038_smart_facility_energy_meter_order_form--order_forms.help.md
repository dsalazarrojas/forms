# Smart Facility Energy Meter Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Facility Energy Meter Order Form is a tool used to submit requests for new energy meter installations or upgrades for facilities. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Name and Email to identify yourself and the facility associated with the order.
2. Enter your Phone number for contact purposes.
3. Select the Facility Type (Office, Home, or Other) that the meter will be installed in.
4. Choose the type of meter you need (Smart or Standard).
5. Provide the Address of the facility where the meter will be installed.
6. Enter the Order Date when the meter is needed.
7. Enter the Total Amount for the order.
8. Optionally, add Comments if you have any further information about the order.
9. If needed, enter the Meter Count and the Installation Type (In-house or External).
10. Select the Priority (High, Medium, or Low) for the order.
11. Enter the Contact Person and their Email for facility contact purposes.
12. Optionally, enter the Installation Date and Completion Date for the order.
13. Select the Status of the order (Pending, Done, or Cancelled).
14. If needed, enter the Assigned Tool and Assigned User.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (1, text, required): Enter your name and the facility name for this order.
* **Email** (2, email, required): Enter your work or personal email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (3, text, optional): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Facility Type** (4, select_one, required): Select the type of facility where the meter will be installed (Office, Home, or Other).
* **Meter Type** (5, select_one, optional): Choose between a Smart or Standard meter.
* **Address** (6, text, optional): Enter the address of the facility where the meter will be installed.
* **Order Date** (7, date, required): Enter the date when the meter is needed.
* **Total Amount** (8, number, required): Enter the total amount for the order.
* **Comments** (9, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information about the order.
* **Meter Count** (10, number, optional): If needed, enter the number of meters to be installed.
* **Installation Type** (11, select_one, optional): Select between In-house or External installation.
* **Priority** (13, select_multiple, optional): Choose the priority of the order (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Contact Person** (14, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person for the facility.
* **Facility Contact** (15, email, optional): Enter the email of the facility contact person.
* **Installation Date** (16, date, optional): Enter the date when the meter will be installed.
* **Completion Date** (17, date, optional): Enter the completion date for the order.
* **Status** (18, select_one, optional): Select the status of the order (Pending, Done, or Cancelled).
* **Assigned Tool** (19, text, optional): Enter the tool assigned for the order if needed.
* **Assigned User** (20, text, optional): Enter the user assigned for the order if needed.
* **Comments** (21, text, optional): Add any additional comments for the order.
* **Form ID** (22, text, optional): Enter the unique ID for the form.
* **Submitted Date** (23, date, optional): Enter the date when the form was submitted.
* **Updated At** (24, date, optional): Enter the date when the form was last updated.
* **Created At** (25, date, optional): Enter the date when the form was created.

Note: Fields marked as required must be filled in for the form to be valid. Optional fields are for further information or details.
