# Catering Event Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Catering Event Order Form Template is a tool for clients to provide information about their catering event. 
## How To Complete This Form
Please fill out the form carefully, providing all required information for a successful event planning process. 
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Event** (`event_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the event, e.g. "Wedding Reception" or "Corporate Party".
* **Event Theme or Occasion** (`event_theme`, `text`, optional): If the event has a specific theme or occasion, enter it here, e.g. "Birthday Party" or "Graduation Ceremony".
* **Date of Event** (`event_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the event in MM/DD/YY or DD/MM/YY format.
* **Start Time** (`event_start_time`, `time`, required): Enter the start time of the event in 12-hour format (AM/PM).
* **End Time** (`event_end_time`, `time`, required): Enter the end time of the event in 12-hour format (AM/PM).
* **Expected Number of Guests** (`guest_count_final`, `number`, required): Enter the estimated number of guests attending the event.
* **Venue Name and Full Address** (`venue_location_details`, `text`, required): Enter the name and full address of the venue where the event will take place.
* **Specific Room or Hall Name** (`room_hall_name`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the specific room or hall name where the event will be held.
* **Menu Selections** (`selected_menu_items`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable menu items that you would like to include in your event package. Examples include Assorted Finger Sandwiches, Hot Hors d oeuvres Platter, and Garden Salad with Vinaigrette.
* **Beverage Selection** (`beverage_package_choice`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the beverage options: Tea and Coffee Service Only, Non-Alcoholic Soft Drink Bar, Standard Beer and Wine Package, or Premium Full Bar Service.
* **Total Estimated Event Cost** (`total_estimated_cost`, `number`, required): Enter the total estimated cost of the event.
* **Current Payment Status** (`payment_status_field`, `select_one`, required): Select the current payment status: Full Payment Received, Deposit Received - Balance Pending, Awaiting Deposit, or Invoiced.
* **Billing Contact Person** (`billing_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the billing contact person.
* **Billing Contact Email** (`billing_contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the billing contact person.
