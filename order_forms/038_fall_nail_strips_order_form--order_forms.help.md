# Fall Nail Strips Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fall Nail Strips Order Form is used for customers to place orders for nail strips, a type of beauty product. This form is designed to collect customer information, shipping details, and payment information in order to process the order efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer information section with the customer's name, phone number, and email.
2. Provide the shipping information, including the customer's address, state, city, and zip code.
3. Specify the order details, including the order date, shipping date, and delivery date.
4. Choose a payment method (cash, credit card, or PayPal) and provide any additional payment notes or instructions.
5. Review and submit the form to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Shipping Information** (`shipping_info`, `text`, required): Enter the shipping address.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, `text`, required): Enter the billing address.
* **State** (`customer_state`, `select_one`, required): Select the customer's state from the dropdown list.
* **City** (`customer_city`, `select_one`, required): Select the customer's city from the dropdown list.
* **Zip** (`billing_zip`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's zip code.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, required): Specify the order date.
* **Shipping Date** (`shipping_date`, `date`, optional): Specify the shipping date.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, optional): Specify the delivery date.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, `time`, optional): Specify the delivery time.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, `text`, required): Enter any additional order details or notes.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select the payment method (cash, credit card, or PayPal).
* **Payment Notes** (`payment_notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional payment notes or instructions.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, `number`, required): Enter the total amount of the order.
* **Shipping Cost** (`shipping_cost`, `number`, required): Enter the shipping cost.
* **Tax Rate** (`tax_rate`, `number`, required): Enter the tax rate.
* **Subtotal** (`subtotal`, `number`, required): Enter the subtotal.
* **Total** (`total`, `number`, required): Enter the total amount of the order.
