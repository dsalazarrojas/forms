# Soup Sale Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soup Sale Registration form is used to register for a soup sale event. It collects information from customers, including fundraiser details, pickup or delivery options, soup information, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Fundraiser Info field with the relevant information.
2. Select the Pickup or Delivery option that applies to you. If you select "Other (specify below)", enter the address in the Other Pickup Address field.
3. Enter the Soup Info field with the name of the soup you are interested in.
4. Enter the Quantity Ordered field with the number of soups you would like to purchase.
5. Choose a Pickup Date for your order.
6. Enter your Contact Email and Contact Phone fields with your relevant contact information.
7. Add any additional Notes about your order in the Notes field.
8. Finally, click the Submit button to submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fundraiser Info**: Enter any information about the fundraiser you are registering for.
* **Pickup or Delivery**: Select whether you would like to pick up your order at the store or have it delivered to your home. If you select "Other (specify below)", enter the address for delivery in the Other Pickup Address field.
* **Other Pickup Address**: Enter the address for delivery if you selected the "Other (specify below)" option in the Pickup or Delivery field.
* **Soup Info**: Enter the name of the soup you would like to order.
* **Quantity Ordered**: Enter the number of soups you would like to purchase.
* **Pickup Date**: Select the date you would like to pick up your order.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your order.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your order.
