# heritage_flag_purchase_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Heritage Flag Purchase Form is used to gather information from customers to process their heritage flag purchases. This form is not required for every purchase, but it is necessary for customers who are purchasing a heritage flag.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Customer Details page with the relevant information about the customer.
2. Move on to the Address page to enter the customer's address.
3. Proceed to the Flag Details page to enter the specific details about the flag being purchased.
4. On the Shipping Details page, provide the necessary shipping information for the flag.
5. Enter the Order Details to specify what is being purchased.
6. Lastly, enter the Payment Details page to complete the payment information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Details
* **Customer Details (customer_details)**: Enter the customer's name, email, and other relevant contact information to ensure that we have the correct contact details for them.

### Address
* **Address (address_details)**: Enter the customer's address, including their street address, city, state, zip code, and any other necessary details to facilitate shipping and communication.

### Flag Details
* **Flag Details (flag_details)**: Provide specific details about the flag being purchased, such as the flag's name, description, and any customization options.

### Shipping Details
* **Shipping Details (shipping_details)**: Enter the shipping information, including the shipping address, contact person, and any additional details to ensure timely and correct delivery.

### Order Details
* **Order Details (order_details)**: Specify what is being purchased, including the product name, quantity, and price.

### Payment Details
* **Payment Details (payment_details)**: Enter the payment information, including the payment method, amount, and any other necessary details to complete the transaction.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely to avoid any delays in processing your heritage flag purchase.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
* This form is for informational purposes only and will not be used for any other purpose other than processing your heritage flag purchase.
