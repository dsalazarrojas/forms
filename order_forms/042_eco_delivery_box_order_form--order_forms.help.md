# Eco Delivery Box Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for the Eco Delivery Box service. Please use this form to place orders for delivery of products, including groceries and food.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your delivery details in the **Delivery Details** field.
2. Indicate if this order is a **Recurring Order**.
3. Enter your **Payment** method or details.
4. Provide your **Email** address.
5. Enter your **Phone** number.
6. Enter your **Delivery Address** where you would like to receive the order.
7. Set the **Delivery Date** for when you would like to receive the order.
8. Select the **Delivery Time** when you would like to receive the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, text, required/optional):
  Enter the details of the order you would like to place, such as the type and quantity of products.
- **Recurring Order** (`recurring_order`, text, required/optional):
  Check this box if this order is recurring, such as a weekly or monthly subscription.
- **Payment** (`payment`, text, required/optional):
  Enter your payment method or details, such as credit card information or cash on delivery.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional):
  Enter your email address, which will be used for communication and confirmation of the order.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional):
  Enter your phone number, which will be used for communication and confirmation of the order.
- **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, required/optional):
  Enter the address where you would like to receive the delivery.
- **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required/optional):
  Set the date when you would like to receive the delivery.
- **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required/optional):
  Set the time when you would like to receive the delivery.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check the accuracy of the information entered.
* Review and confirm your order before submission.
* Contact your customer service representative if you have any questions or issues with the form.
