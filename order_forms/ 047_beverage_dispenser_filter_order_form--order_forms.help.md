# Beverage Dispenser Filter Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Beverage Dispenser Filter Order Form" is a data collection tool used to gather relevant information from customers to process their orders efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the "Full name" field with your legal full name.
2. Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
3. Provide a phone number, including country code if necessary.
4. Select the relevant date for your order.
5. Choose the primary selection from the available options.
6. Enter a whole number for quantity or rating, if applicable.
7. Provide any additional details that might be helpful for your order.
8. Confirm your agreement with the selected options.
9. Enter an estimated value in decimal format, if necessary.
10. Enter a preferred time for scheduling, if required.
11. Add any final comments or remarks about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your legal full name for identification purposes.
* **Email address** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email for contact purposes.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text, optional): Include country code if outside local region.
* **Date** (`reference_date`, date, required): Select the relevant date for your order.
* **Primary selection** (`primary_selection`, select_one, required): Choose the option that best applies to your needs.
* **Quantity or rating** (`quantity_or_rating`, number, optional): Provide a whole number for quantity or rating, if applicable.
* **Additional details** (`additional_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional relevant information to help with your order.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_multiple, required): Select any that apply to confirm your agreement.
* **Estimated value** (`estimated_value`, number, optional): Enter an estimated value in decimal format, if necessary.
* **Preferred time** (`preferred_time`, time, optional): Enter a time if scheduling is required.
* **Final comments or notes** (`final_comments`, text, optional): Enter any final comments or remarks about your order.
