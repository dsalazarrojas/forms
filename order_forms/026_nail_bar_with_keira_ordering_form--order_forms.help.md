# Nail Bar With Keira Ordering Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers place orders with Nail Bar with Keira by providing essential details and preferences for processing the order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled in correctly.
3. Review your details before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Start**: This is the starting point of the form. Please fill in your basic information.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer placing the order.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the email address of the customer. This will be used for correspondence and order tracking.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the customer.
* **Order Details**: Write a brief description of the order.
* **Service Type**: Choose whether the order is for a 'Yes' or 'No' service. Please be aware that not all services are available for every order.
* **Nail Bar Name**: Select the name of the nail bar providing the service.
* **Nail Bar Address**: Enter the address of the nail bar.
* **Nail Bar City**: Enter the city of the nail bar.
* **Nail Bar State**: Enter the state of the nail bar.
* **Nail Bar Zip**: Enter the zip code of the nail bar.
* **Nail Bar Notes**: Add any additional notes about the nail bar.
* **Keira Name**: Enter the name of Keira.
* **Keira Email**: Enter the email address of Keira.
* **Keira Phone**: Enter the phone number of Keira.
* **Keira Address**: Enter the address of Keira.
* **Keira City**: Enter the city of Keira.
* **Keira State**: Enter the state of Keira.
* **Keira Zip**: Enter the zip code of Keira.
* **Keira Notes**: Add any additional notes about Keira.
* **Order Date**: Choose the date for the order.
* **Order Time**: Choose the time for the order.
* **Assigned Tool**: Choose how the order should be assigned (Chat, Email, or Phone).
* **Submit**: Confirm that all details are correct and submit the form to place the order. Please be aware that submission is final and the order cannot be edited after submission.
