# International Digital Services Invoice - Help Guide
## Purpose
The international digital services invoice form is used to manage and track various aspects of billing and payment processes for international digital services. It covers customer information, billing details, payment methods, and notification settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant pages and fields as necessary for your specific task.
2. Fill out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*) in this guide.
3. Review and adjust your input as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Billing Process (Page 1)
* **Billing Process (id: 1)**: This field is used to describe the billing process for the international digital services.
* **Customer Info (id: 2)**: Enter the customer's name in this field.
* **Billing Details (id: 3)**: Enter the billing details, such as the billing address and other relevant information.
* **Track Payments (id: 4)**: This field is used to track payments related to the billing process.

### Payment Process (Page 2)
* **Payment Methods (id: 5)**: Select the relevant payment methods for this invoice.
* **Payment Terms (id: 6)**: Enter the payment terms and conditions.
* **Notes (id: 7)**: Add any additional notes or comments related to the payment process.

### Payment Settings (Page 3)
* **Send Notification (id: 8)**: Select whether to send a notification for this invoice.
* **Email (id: 9)**: Enter the email address to receive notifications.
* **Phone (id: 10)**: Enter the phone number to receive notifications.

### Status and Settings (Page 4)
* **Payment Status (id: 11)**: Select the current payment status for this invoice.
* **Payment Amount (id: 12)**: Enter the payment amount.
* **Billing Address (id: 13)**: Enter the billing address.
* **Customer Address (id: 14)**: Enter the customer's address.

### Payment Terms and Status (Page 5)
* **Payment Terms Date (id: 15)**: Select the date for payment terms.
* **Payment Terms Time (id: 16)**: Select the time for payment terms.
* **Payment Terms Date Range (id: 17)**: Select the date range for payment terms.
* **Payment Terms Time Range (id: 18)**: Select the time range for payment terms.

### Status and Settings (Page 6)
* **Billing Process Status (id: 19)**: Select the current status of the billing process.
* **Customer Info Status (id: 20)**: Select the status of the customer information.
* **Payment Info Status (id: 21)**: Select the status of the payment information.
* **Notification Status (id: 22)**: Select the status of the notification settings.

Tips:

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Review your input before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field or option, consult your supervisor or colleague for guidance.
