# Scarf Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you place a pre-order for a scarf. You will be able to choose from different styles, select the quantity and size of your scarf, and provide your contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your **Customer Name** in the text field at the top of the form.
2. Select the style of scarf you would like to order from the **Scarf Style** dropdown menu.
3. Choose the desired quantity of your scarf using the **Quantity** field.
4. Select the size of your scarf from the **Scarf Size** dropdown menu.
5. Enter your **Email** address where you would like to receive updates on your order.
6. Optionally, provide your **Phone Number** for faster communication.
7. Select a **Date** for your order.
8. Select a **Time** for your order.
9. Leave any additional information or comments in the **Note** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
- **Scarf Style** (`scarf_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the style of scarf you would like to order. Options: style1, style2, style3.
- **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required: false): Enter the quantity of scarves you would like to order.
- **Scarf Size** (`scarf_size`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the size of the scarf you would like to order. Options: small, medium, large.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address where you would like to receive updates on your order.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for faster communication.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Select the date you would like to have your order delivered.
- **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Select the time you would like to have your order delivered.
- **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Leave any additional information or comments about your order here.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your order is processed correctly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us directly.
- Orders are subject to availability and may take up to 3-5 business days to process.
- We will send a confirmation email to the email address provided after submission of this form.
- Please allow 24-48 hours for delivery of your order.
