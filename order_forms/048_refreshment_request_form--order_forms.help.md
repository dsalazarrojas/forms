# Refreshment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request refreshments for events, conferences, or meetings. It will help you provide essential information about the event and the refreshments you need.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required information about the event.
2. Choose the refreshments you need.
3. Provide contact details.
4. Add any additional notes or comments.
5. Submit the form to request the refreshments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **order_details** (text): Describe the order you are requesting refreshments for.
* **event_date** (date): Enter the date of the event.
* **refreshment_time** (time): Enter the time of the event.
* **refreshment_items** (select_multiple): Choose the refreshments you need. Select one or more from the following: Water, Soda, Juice, Coffee, Tea, Cake, Cookies, Fruit.
* **event_organizers** (select_one): Check if this is an event organized by your team or not.
* **order_instructions** (text): Add any additional instructions for the refreshments order.
* **contact_person** (select_one): Choose the contact person for this event. Select one from: John, Jane, Jim, Janice.
* **contact_email** (email): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **contact_phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **event_details** (text): Describe the event in more detail.
* **event_description** (text): Provide a description of the event.
* **refreshment_notes** (text): Add any additional notes about the refreshments.
* **event_type** (select_one): Choose the type of event. Select one from: Conference, Meeting, Party, Workshop.
* **refreshment_budget** (number): Enter the budget for the refreshments.
* **refreshment_quantity** (number): Enter the quantity of refreshments you need.
* **event_location** (text): Enter the location of the event.
* **refreshment_special_requests** (text): Add any special requests for the refreshments.
* **contact_title** (text): Enter the title of the contact person.
* **refreshment_special_instructions** (text): Add any special instructions for the refreshments order.
* **event_start_time** (time): Enter the start time of the event.
* **event_end_time** (time): Enter the end time of the event.
* **event_duration** (number): Enter the duration of the event.
* **event_date** (date): Enter the date of the event again.
* **refreshment_catering** (text): Add any catering instructions.
* **form_submitted** (text): This field is automatically populated and should not be edited.

Note: Please fill in the form carefully and provide accurate information. This will help us to prepare the refreshments according to your needs.
