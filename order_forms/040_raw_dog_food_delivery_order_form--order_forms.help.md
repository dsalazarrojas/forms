# Raw Dog Food Delivery Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from pet owners for the delivery of raw dog food. It will help the delivery service process your order efficiently and ensure that your pet receives the correct food at the right time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the Raw Dog Food option to begin.
2. Choose your delivery preferences from the options provided.
3. Select your payment method and type.
4. If you have a specific delivery schedule, enter the date.
5. Enter the total value of your order.
6. Choose the type of raw food your pet prefers (Chicken, Fish, or Beef).
7. Enter the quantity of raw dog food you would like to order.
8. Select the delivery time that suits you best.
9. Enter your pet's name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Raw Dog Food** (`Raw Dog Food`, `text`, required): Enter the type of raw dog food you would like to order.
* **Delivery Preferences** (`Delivery Preferences`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose from the options provided to specify how you would like your order to be delivered.
* **Payment Info** (`Payment Info`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred payment method and type.
* **Delivery Schedule** (`Delivery Schedule`, `date`, optional): If you have a specific delivery schedule, enter the date here.
* **Order Total** (`Order Total`, `number`, required): Enter the total value of your order.
* **Raw Food Type** (`Raw Food Type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of raw food your pet prefers (Chicken, Fish, or Beef).
* **Raw Dog Food Quantity** (`Raw Dog Food Quantity`, `number`, required): Enter the quantity of raw dog food you would like to order.
* **Delivery Time** (`Delivery Time`, `time`, required): Select the delivery time that suits you best.
* **Pet Name** (`Pet Name`, `text`, required): Enter your pet's name.
 
## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your order details before submitting.
- If you have any special requests or questions, please contact our support team.
- We strive to deliver your order on time and to your preference.
