# nail_slay_with_niki_&_co_order_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for customers who want to place an order with Niki & Co. It collects essential information for processing the order and ensures that the order is processed correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all required fields with accurate and truthful information. The form is divided into multiple pages, and each page is explained below.
2. Start by filling in the "Order Form" page.
3. On the "Order Form" page, provide your order details.
4. Proceed to the "Customer Info" page, where you will fill in your personal details.
5. Provide your email address on the "Customer Email" page.
6. Fill in your phone number on the "Customer Phone" page.
7. Finally, confirm your order items on the last page and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Form** (`Order Form`, `text`, optional):
	+ This page is where you can enter your order details. You can type in the information you need to place your order.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter your personal details here, which will be used to verify your identity.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, `text`, required):
	+ Provide your address to ensure accurate shipping and delivery.
* **Order Items** (`order_items`, `select_multiple`, required):
	+ Select the items you wish to order from the options provided.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, required):
	+ Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required):
	+ Provide your phone number so we can contact you if needed.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields carefully and accurately to ensure a smooth ordering process.
* Double-check your order details before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us directly.
* Once you submit the form, you will receive a confirmation email with your order details.
