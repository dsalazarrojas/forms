# Biscuit Delivery Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Biscuit Delivery Order Form is designed for customers to place orders for biscuit deliveries. This form helps gather information required for processing and delivering the order efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your name, email, phone number, and delivery address.
2. Select the desired biscuit varieties and quantities.
3. Specify the desired delivery date and time.
4. Enter any special requests or dietary requirements.
5. Choose a payment method and payment timing.
6. Confirm your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (Customer Name): Your full name as we will address the order.
*   **Email Address** (Customer Email): Your contact email for delivery confirmations and updates.
*   **Phone Number** (Customer Phone): Your contact phone number for delivery confirmations and updates.
*   **Delivery Address** (Customer Address): Your home or office address where biscuits will be delivered.
*   **City** (Customer City): The city where the delivery address is located.
*   **State or Province** (Customer State): The state or province where the delivery address is located.
*   **ZIP or Postal Code** (Customer ZIP): The postal code of the delivery address.
*   **Desired Delivery Date** (Desired Delivery Date): The date you want your biscuits delivered.
*   **Preferred Delivery Time** (Preferred Delivery Time): Morning, afternoon, or evening delivery time you prefer.
*   **Biscuit Variety 1** (Biscuit Variety 1): First biscuit variety you would like.
*   **Quantity of Variety 1** (Biscuit Quantity 1): Number of dozen biscuits of the first variety you need.
*   **Biscuit Variety 2** (Biscuit Variety 2): Second biscuit variety you would like, or select 'None' if you don't want any more variety.
*   **Quantity of Variety 2** (Biscuit Quantity 2): Number of dozen biscuits of the second variety you need, or 'None' if not applying.
*   **Biscuit Variety 3** (Biscuit Variety 3): Third biscuit variety you would like, or select 'None' if you don't want any more variety.
*   **Quantity of Variety 3** (Biscuit Quantity 3): Number of dozen biscuits of the third variety you need, or 'None' if not applying.
*   **Dietary Requirements** (Special Dietary): Specify any dietary requirements like gluten-free, vegan, etc.
*   **Baking Preferences** (Baking Preferences): How you like your biscuits baked - lightly, medium, fully, or extra crispy.
*   **Packaging Preference** (Packaging Preference): How you want your biscuits packaged.
*   **Special Instructions** (Special Instructions): Enter any special requests or comments.
*   **Biscuit Price** (Biscuit Price): Cost of biscuits.
*   **Delivery Fee** (Delivery Fee): Cost for delivery.
*   **Packaging Fee** (Packaging Fee): Cost if applicable.
*   **Discount Code** (Discount Code): If you have a valid discount code.
*   **Total Amount Due** (Total Amount Due): Sum of all costs.
*   **Payment Method** (Payment Method): How you will pay for the biscuits.
*   **When Will You Pay** (When Will You Pay): When you want to pay for the order.
*   **Order Confirmation** (Order Confirmation): Confirm or cancel your order.
*   **Contact me to confirm delivery details** (Contact For Confirmation): If you want us to contact you after confirming the order.

## Tips
Please note that the desired delivery date and time should be considered as estimates. The actual delivery may vary based on availability of biscuits and delivery schedules.

This guide helps customers complete the form efficiently and accurately, ensuring a smooth biscuit delivery experience.
