# Spirit Wear Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Spirit Wear Order Form is designed to capture critical information needed to place an order for custom spirit wear.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **order_details** field with a brief description of your order.
2. Enter your **shipping_address** in the provided text field.
3. In the **select_tshirt_size** field, choose the size of t-shirt you'd like to order (X-Large, Large, Medium, or Small).
4. Select the **select_color** field with the desired color of your t-shirt (Red, Blue, Green, or Yellow).
5. Choose the **select_designs** field with the design you'd like for your t-shirt (Logo, Mascot, or Text).
6. Enter the quantity of t-shirts you'd like to order in the **quantity** field.
7. If you have any additional notes about your order, type them in the **notes** field.
8. Review the **payment_terms** field for any specific payment instructions or terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **order_details** (Text, Optional): A brief description of your order.
* **shipping_address** (Text, Optional): Enter your shipping address for accurate delivery.
* **select_tshirt_size** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose the size of t-shirt you'd like to order (X-Large, Large, Medium, or Small).
* **select_color** (Select One, Required): Select the color of your t-shirt (Red, Blue, Green, or Yellow).
* **select_designs** (Select One, Required): Choose the design for your t-shirt (Logo, Mascot, or Text).
* **quantity** (Number, Required): Enter the quantity of t-shirts you'd like to order.
* **notes** (Note, Optional): Add any additional notes about your order.
* **payment_terms** (Text, Optional): Review and acknowledge any specific payment instructions or terms.

## Tips

* Double-check your order details to ensure accuracy.
* Select the correct size and color to avoid mistakes.
* Enter the correct quantity to avoid overselling.
* Use the **notes** field for any special requests or instructions.
* Review the **payment_terms** field for any specific payment instructions or terms.
