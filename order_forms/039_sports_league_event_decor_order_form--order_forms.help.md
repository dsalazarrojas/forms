# Sports League Event Decor Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for sports league event decor orders, including event details, payment information, and other relevant specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the event details you'd like to order (Select Decor Items).
2. Input the event date (Event Date).
3. Enter the event venue (Event Venue).
4. Provide the event location (Event Location).
5. Specify the delivery instructions for your order (Delivery Instructions).
6. Select your payment method (Payment Method), and enter the payment amount (Payment Amount) and currency (Payment Currency).
7. Confirm the payment information (Confirm Payment).
8. Select the event time (Event Time).
9. Optionally, add event attendees, notes, external links, file uploads, and email addresses for your event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Decor Items (Yes/No)**: Select the decor items you would like to order for your sports league event.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Event Venue**: Enter the venue where the event will take place.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Delivery Instructions**: Provide any specific delivery instructions for your order.
* **Payment Information**: Enter the payment information for your order.
* **Payment Method (Yes/No)**: Select the method of payment you would like to use.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the payment amount for your order.
* **Payment Currency (Yes/No)**: Select the currency for your payment.
* **Event Time**: Select the time of the event in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Confirm Payment**: Confirm that the payment information is correct.
* **Back to Previous Step (Yes/No)**: If you need to review your previous entries or make changes, select this option to return to the previous step.
* **Event Attendees (Yes/No)**: If applicable, enter the attendees for your event.
* **Event Notes**: Add any additional notes for your event.
* **Event External Links**: If applicable, enter any external links for your event.
* **Event File Uploads**: If applicable, upload any files related to your event.
* **Event Email**: Enter the email address for your event contact.
* **Submit**: Review and submit your order.
* **Thank You**: A thank you message appears after a successful submission.
* **Error Message**: In case of any errors or issues with your submission.
* **Payment Status (Active/Inactive)**: The status of your payment (Active or Inactive).
* **Back to Previous Step (Yes/No)**: If you need to review your previous entries or make changes, select this option to return to the previous step.
* **Payment Info Back to Previous Step (Yes/No)**: If you need to review your payment information, select this option to return to the previous step.
