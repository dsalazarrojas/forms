# Catering Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Catering Appointment Form is designed to collect necessary information from clients for a meeting with a catering service provider. This form gathers details such as client contact information, appointment scheduling, and event specifics to ensure that the catering service provider is well-prepared for the meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the **Full Name** field (required).
2. If applicable, enter your company or organization name in the **Company or Organization Name** field.
3. Provide your email address in the **Email Address** field (required).
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone Number** field (required).
5. Select a date and time for your appointment in the **Requested Appointment Date** and **Preferred Time Slot** fields (required).
6. Choose the purpose of your appointment in the **Purpose of the Appointment** field (required).
7. Enter your estimated event date in the **Estimated Event Date** field (optional).
8. Choose the type of event in the **Type of Event** field (required).
9. Enter an estimated guest count in the **Estimated Guest Count** field (required).
10. If applicable, provide the name of the venue where you have booked the meeting (if already booked).
11. Lastly, indicate what you would like to focus on during the meeting in the **What would you like to focus on during this meeting** field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Full Name** (`client_name`, text, required)
* This field is where you enter your full name for the meeting.

### 2. **Company or Organization Name** (`company_org_name`, text, optional)
* If you represent a company or organization, enter the name here.

### 3. **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required)
* Enter your email address where you can be reached.

### 4. **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required)
* Enter your phone number where the catering service provider can contact you.

### 5. **Requested Appointment Date** (`preferred_appointment_date`, date, required)
* Select a suitable date for your appointment with the catering service provider.

### 6. **Preferred Time Slot** (`preferred_time_slot`, select_one, required)
* Choose a time slot from the options provided (morning, lunchtime, afternoon, late afternoon).

### 7. **Purpose of the Appointment** (`appointment_type`, select_one, required)
* Select the reason for your appointment (initial consultation, menu tasting, venue site visit, final logistics meeting, contract signing).

### 8. **Estimated Event Date** (`estimated_event_date`, date, optional)
* If known, enter an estimated event date.

### 9. **Type of Event** (`event_type`, select_one, required)
* Choose the type of event (wedding, corporate gala, business lunch/meeting, birthday/private party, memorial service, other).

### 10. **Estimated Guest Count** (`expected_guest_count`, number, required)
* Enter the estimated number of guests.

### 11. **Estimated Catering Budget** (`budget_estimate`, number, optional)
* If applicable, provide an estimated budget for the event.

### 12. **Event Venue (if already booked)** (`venue_name_location`, text, optional)
* If the event venue is already booked, enter the name here.

### 13. **What would you like to focus on during this meeting** (`specific_meeting_goals`, text, optional)
* If necessary, specify what you'd like to focus on during the meeting.

This form ensures that the catering service provider has all the necessary information for a productive meeting.
