# Textbook Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Textbook Order Form is designed to collect order information from customers, including bookstore details, order time and date, book information, customer contact information, payment methods, and order status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the **Bookstore Name** in the designated field. This is the name of the bookstore where the book will be purchased.
2. Enter the **Order Date** in the designated field. This is the date the order was placed.
3. Enter the **Order Time** in the designated field. This is the time the order was placed.
4. Enter the **Book Title** in the designated field. This is the title of the book being ordered.
5. Enter the **Book Price** in the designated field. This is the price of the book.
6. Enter the **Quantity** of books being ordered in the designated field.
7. Enter the **Customer Name** in the designated field. This is the name of the customer placing the order.
8. Enter the **Customer Email** in the designated field. This is the email address of the customer.
9. Enter the **Customer Phone** in the designated field. This is the phone number of the customer.
10. Select the **Payment Method** from the drop-down menu. This is the method used to process the payment.
11. Enter the **Coupon Code** in the designated field. This is any applicable discount code.
12. Enter the **Total Price** in the designated field. This is the total price of the order after any discounts.
13. Select the **Order Status** from the drop-down menu. This is the current status of the order.
14. Select the **Payment Status** from the drop-down menu. This is the status of the payment.
15. Enter the **Book Photo URL** in the designated field. This is the URL of the book photo.
16. Select the **Payment Gateway** from the drop-down menu. This is the payment gateway used to process the payment.
17. Enter **Order Notes** in the designated field. This is any additional information about the order.
18. Enter **Customer Notes** in the designated field. This is any additional information for the customer.
19. Enter the **Order Date 2** in the designated field. This is the second date of the order.
20. Enter the **Customer Name 2** in the designated field. This is the second name of the customer.
21. Enter **Book Title 2** in the designated field. This is the second book title.
22. Select the **Order Status 2** from the drop-down menu. This is the status of the second book.
23. Select the **Payment Method 2** from the drop-down menu. This is the method used to pay for the second book.
24. Select the **Order Notes 2** in the designated field. This is any additional notes for the second book.
25. Select the **Customer Notes 2** in the designated field. This is any additional notes for the second customer.

* **Total Price**: Enter the total price of the order after any discounts.
* **Order Status**: Select the current status of the order.

* **Book Photo URL**: Enter the URL of the book photo.
* **Payment Gateway**: Select the payment gateway used to process the payment.
* **Order Notes**: Enter any additional information about the order.
* **Customer Notes**: Enter any additional information for the customer.
* **Order Date 2**: Enter the date of the second order.
