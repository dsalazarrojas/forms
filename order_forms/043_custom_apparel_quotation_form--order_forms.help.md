# Custom Apparel Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Apparel Quotation Form is used to gather information from customers for custom apparel orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name to identify yourself as the customer.
2. Provide your phone number for communication purposes.
3. Enter your address to ensure accurate delivery and billing.
4. Select the fabric color for your custom apparel order.
5. Select the fabric pattern for your custom apparel order.
6. Choose the type of product you are interested in.
7. Enter your email address for correspondence.
8. Select the custom quotation date for your order.
9. Select the custom quotation time for your order.
10. Specify the custom quotation duration in minutes.
11. Enter the custom quotation total cost for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification or passport.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False
* **Customer Address**: Enter your full address for accurate delivery and billing.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False
* **Fabric Color**: Select the color of fabric you are interested in for your custom apparel order.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False
* **Fabric Pattern**: Select the pattern of fabric you are interested in for your custom apparel order.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False
* **Product Type**: Choose the type of product you are interested in (e.g., t-shirt, dress, etc.).
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address for correspondence.
	+ Type: Email
	+ Required: False
* **Custom Quotation Date**: Select the date you would like to receive your custom quotation.
	+ Type: Date
	+ Required: False
* **Custom Quotation Time**: Select the time you would like to receive your custom quotation.
	+ Type: Time
	+ Required: False
* **Custom Quotation Duration**: Specify the duration of the custom quotation in minutes.
	+ Type: Number
	+ Required: False
* **Custom Quotation Total Cost**: Enter the total cost of your custom quotation.
	+ Type: Number
	+ Required: False

## Tips

* Please make sure to provide accurate and detailed information to ensure a smooth processing of your custom apparel order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* Our team will review your custom quotation and provide you with a quote within the selected duration.
