# Streetwear Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Streetwear Order Form Template is a comprehensive form designed to process orders for streetwear merchandise in an efficient and accurate manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill in your name in the `customer_name` field.
2. Enter your shipping address, including city and country, in the `shipping_address`, `shipping_city`, `shipping_state`, and `shipping_zip` fields.
3. Select your preferred payment method from the `payment_method` dropdown menu.
4. Optional: Enter the date and time you'd like to place your order in the `order_date` and `order_time` fields, respectively.
5. Enter the total cost of your order in the `order_total` field.
6. Enter your email address in the `customer_email` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_name** (`text`, required): Enter your name to identify yourself as the customer.
* **shipping_address** (`text`, required): Enter your shipping address for accurate delivery.
* **shipping_city** (`text`, required): Enter the city where you'd like your order delivered.
* **shipping_state** (`text`, required): Enter the state where you'd like your order delivered.
* **shipping_zip** (`number`, required): Enter your ZIP code for shipping purposes.
* **shipping_country** (`text`, required): Enter your country for international orders.
* **order_date** (`date`, optional): Enter the date you'd like to receive your order.
* **order_time** (`time`, optional): Enter the time you'd like to receive your order.
* **order_total** (`number`, required): Enter the total cost of your order.
* **payment_method** (`select_one`, required): Select your preferred payment method.
* **customer_email** (`email`, required): Enter your email address for order confirmation and updates.

## Tips
* Double-check your contact information for accurate order processing.
* If you're ordering internationally, ensure you select the correct country and enter your correct ZIP code.
* Selecting the correct payment method will facilitate a smooth checkout process.
