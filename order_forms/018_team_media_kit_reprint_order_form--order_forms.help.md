# Team Media Kit Reprint Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for requesting a media kit reprint. A media kit is a collection of materials used for internal or external use, such as sales collateral or marketing materials. This form will guide you through the necessary steps to create a successful reprint order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  **Fill out the form fields**:
    a. **Team**: Enter the name of the team that is responsible for the media kit.
    b.  **Name**: Enter your name.
    c. **Email**: Enter your email address.
    d. **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
    e. **Order Reason**: Select the reason for the reprint. You can choose either "Internal Reprint" for internal use or "External Use" for external use.
    f. **Quantity**: Enter the number of reprints required.
    g. **File Format**: Select the format for the reprint. You can choose between "PDF", "Excel", "CSV", or "XML".
    h. **Delivery Method**: Choose how you want to receive the reprint. You can select either "Email" or "Print".
    i. **Delivery Date**: Enter the date you want to receive the reprint. This is optional.
    j. **Delivery Time**: Enter the time you want to receive the reprint. This is optional.
    k. **Order Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments regarding the order.
    l. **Assigned To**: Select the person or team responsible for the assigned tool.
    m. **Output File**: Enter the name of the output file.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team**: Enter the team name responsible for the media kit.
* **Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Order Reason**: Choose "Internal Reprint" for internal use or "External Use" for external use.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of reprints required.
* **File Format**: Select the format for the reprint, e.g., "PDF", "Excel", "CSV", or "XML".
* **Delivery Method**: Choose how you want to receive the reprint, either via "Email" or "Print".
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the date you want to receive the reprint.
* **Delivery Time**: Enter the time you want to receive the reprint.
* **Order Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments regarding the order.
* **Assigned To**: Select the person or team responsible for the assigned tool, e.g., "ChatJIMMY" or "Other".
* **Output File**: Enter the name of the output file.
