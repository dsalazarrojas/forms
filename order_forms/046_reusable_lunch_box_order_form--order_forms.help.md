# Reusable Lunch Box Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reusable Lunch Box Order Form is designed to capture user information for a lunch box order. This form will help streamline the ordering process, allowing users to input their meal preferences and payment details in an efficient and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details in the `User Input` field.
2. Select your desired `Lunch Order` from the options provided.
3. Choose your meal `Meal Preferences` from the list.
4. Indicate any `Meal Restrictions` you have.
5. Select your preferred `Payment Method` from the available options.
6. Review and confirm your order in the `Order Confirmation` field.
7. Click submit to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`text`, required: false): Fill in your name or any other relevant details you would like to share with the lunch box service.
* **Order Details** (`text`, required: false): Briefly describe your order or any special requests you have.
* **Order Summary** (`text`, required: false): Review and confirm your order before submission.
* **Lunch Order** (`select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Apple
	+ Banana
	+ Carrot
	+ Donut
	+ Grapes
	+ Ham
	+ Orange
	+ Pear
	+ Watermelon
* **Meal Preferences** (`select_one`, required: false):
	+ Grilled Chicken
	+ Salad
	+ Sushi
	+ Tacos
	+ Veggies
* **Meal Restrictions** (`select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Gluten-free
	+ Nut-free
	+ Vegan
* **Payment Method** (`select_one`, required: false):
	+ Apple Pay
	+ Google Pay
	+ PayPal
	+ Venmo

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields to ensure a smooth order process.
* Review your order carefully before clicking submit to avoid any errors.
* If you have any specific requests or instructions, please use the `Order Details` field to communicate them.
* If you have any food restrictions, please select them in the `Meal Restrictions` field to ensure the lunch box service can prepare your order accordingly.
