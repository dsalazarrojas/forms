# Sports League Flag Printing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports League Flag Printing Order Form is a tool for customers to place custom orders for sports league flag printing. This form collects information about the league name, flag type, quantity, order type, artwork, color scheme, payment type, payment amount, artwork size, team name, team name size, shipping address, and other details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the league name and team name information in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the flag type, quantity, order type, and artwork that you want to order.
3. Choose a payment type and enter the payment amount.
4. Enter the shipping address details, including city, state, zip, and country.
5. Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
6. Enter your email address to contact you about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **League Name**: Enter the name of the sports league that you are ordering for. 
- **Flag Type**: Select the type of flag you want to order (Custom or Stock). 
- **Quantity**: Enter the number of flags you want to order. 
- **Order Type**: Select the type of order you want to place (Flag, Banner, or Banners). 
- **Artwork**: Select the type of artwork you want to display on your flag (Flag, Number, or Logo). 
- **Color Scheme**: Select the color scheme you want to use for your flag (Monochrome or Multicolor). 
- **Payment Type**: Select the method of payment you want to use (Payment or Deposit). 
- **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount you want to pay for your order. 
- **Artwork Size**: Select the size of the artwork you want to display on your flag (Small, Large, or Extra Large). 
- **Quantity Artwork**: Enter the number of flags with artwork you want to order. 
- **Shipping Address**: Enter the shipping address where you want the flags to be delivered. 
- **City**: Enter the city where the shipping address is located. 
- **State**: Enter the state where the shipping address is located. 
- **Zip**: Enter the zip code where the shipping address is located. 
- **Country**: Enter the country where the shipping address is located. 
- **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your order. 
- **Order Comments**: Enter any additional comments about your order. 
- **Team Name**: Enter the name of the team for which you are ordering flags. 
- **Team Name Other**: Enter any other information about the team name. 
- **Team Name Abbrev**: Enter the team name abbreviation. 
- **Team Name Number**: Enter the team name number. 
- **Team Name Size**: Select the team name size (Single, Dual, or Both). 
- **Team Name Size Other**: Enter any other information about the team name size. 
- **Email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you about your order.
