# Rice Bowl Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rice Bowl Order Form is designed for customers to order a customized rice bowl meal from a restaurant. The form is used to gather information about the customer's preferences for the meal, contact details, and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Rice Bowl Details section with your preferred meal options.
2. Select your preferred pickup or delivery method.
3. Choose your preferred pickup or delivery time.
4. Select your preferred type of rice bowl (veggie or non-veggie), protein, vegetables, and grains.
5. Choose your preferred sauce.
6. Enter your contact information (email and phone number).
7. Select your preferred payment method.
8. Enter any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rice Bowl Details** (`Rice Bowl`, `select_multiple`): Choose your preferred type of rice bowl (veggie or non-veggie) and its toppings.
* **Pick Up or Delivery** (`Pick Up or Delivery`, `select_one`): Select whether you want to pick up your order or have it delivered.
* **Pick Up Time** (`Pick Up Time`, `time`): Choose the time for pickup.
* **Delivery Time** (`Delivery Time`, `time`): Choose the time for delivery.
* **Rice Bowl** (`Rice Bowl`, `select_multiple`): Choose your preferred type of protein (Chicken, Tofu, Fish).
* **Protein** (`Protein`, `select_multiple`): Choose your preferred protein (Chicken, Tofu, Fish).

* **Sauces** (`Sauces`, `select_multiple`): Choose your preferred sauce (Sriracha Sauce, Peanut Sauce, Soy Sauce).
