# AA Technique Generic - Help Guide

## Purpose
The AA Technique Generic form is a standardized template for capturing essential information for orders and purchases. It gathers details from customers, order specifics, delivery preferences, and payment information. This guide will walk you through the form fields and their intended purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form accurately, follow these steps:

*   Ensure you have all the necessary information about the customer and the order at hand.
*   Start by filling in the customer's information: provide their full name, email address, and phone number if applicable.
*   If you're ordering on behalf of a business, include the company or organization name.
*   Next, enter the order date, item or product name, and item description. You may also specify the quantity and unit price of the items ordered.
*   Calculate the total amount as quantity times unit price.
*   Choose a preferred delivery method: standard shipping, express shipping, pickup, or local delivery.
*   If needed, provide a delivery address.
*   Optionally, specify a requested delivery date and include any special instructions or customizations.
*   Select a payment method: credit card, cash, bank transfer, PayPal, or invoice.
*   Enter the applicable tax rate for the order.
*   Add any additional comments or notes about the order.
*   Finally, confirm acceptance of the terms and conditions by selecting 'True'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full name** (2): Enter your complete legal name.
*   **Email address** (3): This will be used for order confirmation.
*   **Phone number** (4): Optional, for delivery updates.
*   **Company or organization name** (5): If you're ordering on behalf of a business.
*   **Order date** (7): The date of the order.
*   **Item or product name** (8): Specify the exact product or service.
*   **Item description** (9): Brief description of the product or service.
*   **Quantity** (10): Enter the number of items.
*   **Unit price** (11): Price per item in USD.
*   **Total amount** (12): Calculated as quantity × unit price.
*   **Preferred delivery method** (14): Choose from standard shipping, express shipping, pickup, or local delivery.
*   **Delivery address** (15): Full address for delivery.
*   **Requested delivery date** (16): Optional, for specific delivery timing.
*   **Special instructions or customization notes** (17): Any specific requirements or customizations.
*   **Payment method** (19): Select from credit card, cash, bank transfer, PayPal, or invoice.
*   **Tax rate percentage** (20): Applicable tax rate for the order.
*   **Additional comments or notes** (21): Any other information about the order.
*   **I accept the terms and conditions** (22): Confirm by selecting 'True'.
