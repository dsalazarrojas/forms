# Makeup Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Makeup Order Form is a tool used to collect information from customers for their makeup orders. It is used to gather the customer's details, photo references, and order preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Makeup Order Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the `First Name` field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the `Last Name` field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the `Email` field.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the `Phone` field.
5. Select a date for your **Front photo**, which is a photo of the front of your face.
6. Select a date for your **Back photo**, which is a photo of the back of your face.
7. Select a date for your **Side profile photo**, which is a photo of your profile.
8. Select a date for your **Top profile photo**, which is a photo of your profile from the top.
9. Choose a **Product** that you would like to order from the drop-down list.
10. Enter the **Quantity** of the product you would like to order.
11. Select a **Color** for your order from the drop-down list.
12. If you have any **Comments**, you can type them in the provided field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: A text field to enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: A text field to enter your last name.
* **Email**: A text field to enter your email address.
* **Phone**: A text field to enter your phone number.
* **Front photo**: A date field to select the date of your front photo.
* **Back photo**: A date field to select the date of your back photo.
* **Side profile photo**: A date field to select the date of your profile photo from the side.
* **Top profile photo**: A date field to select the date of your profile photo from the top.
* **Product**: A drop-down list to select a product to order.
* **Quantity**: A number field to enter the quantity of the product you would like to order.
* **Color**: A drop-down list to select a color for your order.
* **Comments**: A text field to enter any additional comments or notes about your order.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct product and quantity to ensure accurate order fulfillment.
* Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, you can reach out to us directly.
