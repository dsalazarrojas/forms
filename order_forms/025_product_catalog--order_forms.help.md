# Product Catalog - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your products, including whether you have products, a brief description of your products, and an image of your product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the products that you have from the list of options.
2. Type a brief description of your product in the text field.
3. Upload or enter the image of your product in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product List** (`select_multiple`, `product_list`, required: false): Select all the products that you have from the list of options. This will help us understand what products you have available.
* **Description** (`text`, `product_description`, required: false): Type a brief description of your product in this field. Please be as detailed as possible to help us understand your product better.
* **Image** (`text`, `product_image`, required: false): Upload or enter the image of your product in this field. Please ensure that the image is clear and high-quality.

## Tips
* Make sure to select all the products that you have accurately.
* Be concise and clear in your product description.
* Use the correct format for uploading or entering your product image.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for assistance.
