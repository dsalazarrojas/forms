# Security System Delivery Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Security System Delivery Form is designed to collect vital information for the successful delivery of security systems. Please fill out this form to ensure accurate and efficient delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's details, including their name, in the "Client Information" section.
2. Choose the delivery method in the "Delivery Type" section.
3. Provide product information in the "Product Information" section.
4. Enter the quantity of the security system to be delivered.
5. Add any additional notes in the "Notes" section.
6. Enter the client's contact email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Delivery Information** (`1`, text, optional): Enter the delivery details, such as date, time, and location.
* **Client Information** (`2`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more clients from the list: John Doe, Jane Doe, Bob Smith.
* **Delivery Type** (`3`, select_one, optional): Select the delivery method: Delivery, Pickup, In Person.
* **Product Information** (`4`, text, optional): Enter the product details, including model, serial number, and any other relevant information.
* **Quantity** (`5`, number, optional): Enter the number of security systems to be delivered.
* **Notes** (`6`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or instructions for the delivery team.
* **Email** (`7`, email, optional): Enter the client's contact email address.
* **Phone** (`8`, text, optional): Enter the client's contact phone number.
