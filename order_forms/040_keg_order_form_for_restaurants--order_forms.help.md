# Keg Order Form For Restaurants - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Keg Order Form For Restaurants is a tool used by customers to place keg orders with their local restaurants. This form will guide you through the process of providing the necessary information for your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your customer preferences, such as any specific requests or allergies you may have.
2.  Enter your delivery details, including the date and time you would like your order delivered.
3.  Enter your customer contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
4.  Specify the delivery notes, if any.
5.  Enter the desired delivery date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Customer Preferences (Customer Preferences)** (`customer_preferences`, text, no): This field allows you to specify any special requests or allergies you may have.
*   **Delivery Details (Delivery Details)** (`delivery_details`, text, no): Enter the date and time you would like your order delivered.
*   **Keg Details (Keg Details)** (`keg_details`, text, no): Please enter any details about the keg you would like to order.
*   **Customer Info (Customer Info)** (`customer_info`, text, no): This field is used for customer information such as name and any other relevant details.
*   **Delivery Address (Delivery Address)** (`delivery_address`, text, no): Enter your delivery address for the keg to be delivered to.
*   **Customer Phone (Customer Phone)** (`customer_phone`, number, no): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
*   **Customer Email (Customer Email)** (`customer_email`, email, no): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
*   **Delivery Notes (Delivery Notes)** (`delivery_notes`, text, no): This field is used for any additional delivery notes.
*   **Delivery Time (Delivery Time)** (`delivery_time`, time, no): Enter the time you would like your order delivered.
*   **Delivery Date (Delivery Date)** (`delivery_date`, date, no): Enter the date you would like your order delivered.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that you have provided all the required information to ensure a smooth delivery process.
*   If you have any special requests, please specify them in the Customer Preferences field.
*   Please note that all fields are optional unless marked as required.
