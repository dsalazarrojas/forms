# home_cleaning_estimate_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The home cleaning estimate form is designed to collect information from customers to provide an estimate of the time required to clean their home. This form is used to determine the scope of work and time needed to complete the cleaning tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer's details in the `Customer Details` section.
2. Enter the customer's address in the `address` section.
3. Indicate if any services are required in the `Services Required` section.
4. Enter the estimated time required to complete the cleaning tasks in the `Estimated Time` section.
5. Select the date for the scheduled cleaning in the `Date` section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details**: Enter the customer's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **address**: Enter the customer's address where the cleaning will be performed.
* **Services Required**: Choose the services that are required, such as "Yes" for cleaning, "No" for other services.
* **Estimated Time**: Enter the estimated time required to complete the cleaning tasks in the format HH:MM (e.g., 2:30).
* **Date**: Select the date for the scheduled cleaning.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure an accurate estimate.
* If services other than cleaning are required, select "No" for services and add a note or comment for clarification.
