# Food Brand Sampling Booth Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Brand Sampling Booth Order Form is a tool that helps event organizers and vendors communicate and coordinate the logistics of food brand sampling events. This form is designed to collect necessary information from vendors and event teams to ensure a successful event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring all required fields are filled in.
3. Submit the form to the event organizer or team once you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendors Requests**: This field is a text box where you can provide any requests or inquiries you have about the event or sampling process.
* **Event Details**: Enter the date and start/end times of the event. This will help us determine the event schedule.
* **Booth Placement**: Select the placement of your booth at the event. This will help us plan the event layout.
* **Sampling Items**: Select the sampling items you wish to offer at the event. This will help us plan the inventory and logistics of the sampling process.
* **Sampling Dates**: This field is not currently used, but you can add any additional dates that are relevant to the sampling process.
* **Vendor Contact**: Provide your name and contact information so we can get in touch with you about any questions or concerns.
* **Event Team**: Enter your event team members' names and contact information. This will help us know who to contact about event-related matters.
* **Booth Size**: Select the size of your booth to help us plan the event layout.
* **Sampling Items Notes**: If you have any special instructions or notes about the sampling items, please enter them here.
* **Vendor Requirements**: If you have any additional requirements or requests, please enter them here.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check all fields before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
- This form is only for food brand sampling events, so please make sure to fill it out accordingly.
