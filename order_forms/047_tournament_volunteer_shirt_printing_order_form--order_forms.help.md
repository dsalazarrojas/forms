# Tournament Volunteer Shirt Printing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from volunteers regarding their t-shirt printing needs for a tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: volunteer name, email, phone, confirm email, confirm printing, and email status.
2. Select the volunteer's shirt size, color, and type.
3. Provide any special instructions if necessary.
4. Confirm the order date and confirm the printing method.
5. Select the confirmation method (Mailchimp, Email, Phone, or In Person).
6. Confirm that the order has been printed or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter the volunteer's name.
* **Shirt Size** (`2`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the volunteer's preferred t-shirt size.
* **Shirt Color** (`3`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the volunteer's preferred t-shirt color.
* **Print Name On Back** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any name you want to print on the back of the shirt.
* **Special Instructions** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any special instructions for the print.
* **Email** (`6`, `email`, required): Enter the volunteer's email address.
* **Phone** (`7`, `text`, required): Enter the volunteer's phone number.
* **Quantity** (`8`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of shirts ordered.
* **Shirt Type** (`9`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of t-shirt (e.g., short sleeve, long sleeve).
* **Organization** (`10`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the organization's name.
* **Volunteer Position** (`11`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the volunteer's position.
* **Order Date** (`12`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the order.
* **Confirm Quantity** (`13`, `number`, required/optional): Confirm the quantity of shirts ordered.
* **Order Notes** (`14`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the order.
* **Confirm Email** (`15`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that the email address entered is accurate.
* **Confirmation Method** (`16`, `select_one`, required): Select the method used for confirmation (Mailchimp, Email, Phone, or In Person).
* **Print Method** (`17`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the printing method (Mail, Local Print, or Online Print).
* **Confirmation Date** (`18`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of confirmation.
* **Printed By** (`19`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the person who printed the shirt.
* **Confirm Printing** (`20`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that the shirt has been printed.
* **Print Status** (`21`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the print status (In Queue, Printed, or Not Printed).
* **Confirm Email Sent** (`22`, `select_one`, required/optional): Confirm that the email has been sent.
* **Email Status** (`23`, `select_one`, required): Select the email status (Sent or Not Sent).
* **Volunteer Tshirt Printing Status** (`24`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the volunteer's t-shirt printing status (Complete or Not Complete).
* **Confirm Print** (`25`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that the print has been confirmed.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately.
* Double-check the information entered to ensure it's correct.
* If unsure about any field, consult with the tournament organizers for clarification.
