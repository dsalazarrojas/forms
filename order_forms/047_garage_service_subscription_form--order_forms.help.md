# Garage Service Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Garage Service Subscription Form is a tool used to capture essential information from customers who wish to subscribe to our garage services. This form helps us understand their preferences, requirements, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone" field.
3. Enter your **Address** in the "Address" field.
4. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field. (Note: This should be a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you.)
5. Select a **Start Date** for your garage service subscription.
6. Choose your **Frequency** of service (Monthly, Quarterly, or Bi-annually).
7. Select the **Service Provider(s)** you would like to use (Company A, Company B, or Company C).
8. Add any additional **Notes** you would like to include.
9. If applicable, enter your **Vehicle Year**, **Vehicle Make**, **Vehicle Model**, and **Vehicle Type** details.
10. Select your preferred **Service Frequency** (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Fortnightly).
11. Enter your **Customer Vehicle** details.
12. Choose your **Payment Method(s)** (Credit Card, Check, or Bank Transfer).
13. Enter the **Payment Amount** for your subscription.
14. Select the **Payment Cycle** (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
15. Check the status of your **Payment Status** (Paid, Due, or Pending).
16. Add any additional **Notes 2** you would like to include.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Start Date**: Select the date you would like to start your garage service subscription.
* **Frequency**: Choose how often you would like to receive garage service (Monthly, Quarterly, or Bi-annually).
* **Service Provider**: Select the company that will be providing your garage service (Company A, Company B, or Company C).
* **Notes**: Add any additional information you would like to include.
* **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make**: Enter the make of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model**: Enter the model of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Type**: Enter the type of vehicle you have.
* **Vehicle License**: Enter your vehicle license number.
* **Service Frequency**: Choose how often you would like to receive service (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Fortnightly).
* **Customer Vehicle**: Enter your vehicle details.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you prefer (Credit Card, Check, or Bank Transfer).
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount you would like to pay.
* **Payment Cycle**: Choose when you would like to make payments (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
* **Payment Status**: Check the status of your payment (Paid, Due, or Pending).
* **Notes 2**: Add any additional information you would like to include.
