# insurance_policy_delivery_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients or customers about their insurance policy delivery preferences. It helps the delivery team understand how their policy should be delivered, making the process smoother and more efficient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions for each field. Please ensure that all required fields (marked as "required") are filled in correctly before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **client_info** (text, required: false): Please enter your name and any other relevant information that will help us identify you.
* **delivery_method** (select_one, required: false): Choose how you would like to receive your insurance policy: Insurance Policy, Policy Documents, Policy Certificate, or Policy Card.
* **delivery_address** (text, required: false): Enter the address where you would like to receive your policy delivery.
* **email** (email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your policy delivery.
* **phone** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
* **delivery_date** (date, required: false): Select the date you would like to receive your policy delivery.
* **delivery_time** (time, required: false): Select the time you would like to receive your policy delivery.
* **additional_info** (note, required: false): If there's any additional information you'd like to provide about your policy delivery, please enter it here.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure we can process your request efficiently.
* If you need assistance with any of the fields, feel free to contact us.
* We'll do our best to deliver your policy according to your selected preferences.
