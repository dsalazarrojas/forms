# Craft Event Lunch Pre Order - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Craft Event Lunch Pre Order form is designed to collect pre-order requests from attendees for the Craft Event. It helps us plan and prepare for the lunch service and ensures that all attendees have an option that suits their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Event Details (Page 1). 
2. Select your Lunch Choices and any relevant dietary needs on the next page.
3. Confirm your order and provide any additional information or notes.
4. Confirm your order and sign your name to finalize your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (Page 1):
	+ This is the first page of the form where you can provide any relevant information about your attendance to the event.
* **Lunch Choices** (Page 2):
	+ Select one or more of the lunch options provided:
		- Grilled Cheese
		- Salad
		- Soup
		- Fruit
		- Yogurt Parfait
		- Vegetarian Quiche
		- Vegan Quesadilla
* **Dietary Needs** (Page 2):
	+ If you have any dietary restrictions, please select one or more of the options:
		- Dairy Free
		- Vegan
		- Gluten Free
		- None
* **Payment Details** (Page 3):
	+ Provide any relevant payment information, such as the amount paid or any specific notes about your payment.
* **Additional Info** (Page 3):
	+ If you have any additional information or notes about your order, you can provide them here.
* **Order Notes** (Page 4):
	+ This is an optional field for any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Order Confirmation** (Page 4):
	+ Confirm that you have completed and reviewed your order.
* **Attendee Signature** (Page 4):
	+ Sign your name to finalize your order.
* **Report** (Page 4):
	+ This field is not currently used and can be ignored.
* **Payment Status** (Page 5):
	+ Confirm the status of your payment.
* **Payment Confirmation** (Page 5):
	+ This field is not currently used and can be ignored.
* **Order Status** (Page 5):
	+ Confirm the status of your order.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your lunch choices and dietary needs are accurate to ensure we can provide the right meal for you.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us.
* Thank you for using our form to pre-order your lunch!
