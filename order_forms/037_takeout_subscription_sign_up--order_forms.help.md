# Takeout Subscription Sign Up - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to sign up for a takeout subscription service. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your customer information: Enter your name and other details in the "Customer Information" page.
2. Select your subscription information: Choose your preferred delivery frequency and time on the "Subscription Information" page.
3. Enter your payment information: Provide your payment method and details on the "Payment Information" page.
4. Enter your delivery information: Enter your delivery address and other details on the "Delivery Information" page.
5. Review your order information: Review your order details, including frequency and time, on the "Order Information" page.
6. Review your fees: Review any applicable fees associated with your subscription on the "Order Fees" page.
7. Review your subscription terms and payment terms: Review the terms and conditions of your subscription on the "Subscription Terms" and "Payment Terms" pages.
8. Select your order and delivery frequencies: Choose your preferred order and delivery frequency on the "Order Frequency" and "Delivery Frequency" pages.
9. Enter your order time and delivery date: Enter your preferred order time and delivery date on the "Order Time" and "Delivery Date" pages.
10. Enter your contact information: Enter your email and phone number on the "Email" and "Phone" pages.
11. Select your payment method: Choose your preferred payment method on the "Payment Method" page.
12. Enter your card information: Enter your card number, expiration date, and CVC on the "Card Number", "Card Expiration", and "Card CVC" pages.
13. Enter your address information: Enter your address, city, state, and zip code on the "Address", "City", "State", and "ZIP" pages.
