# Legal Benefits Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request legal benefits, such as certificates or documents, from a legal benefits ordering system. It captures essential information from the requestor, including their contact details, the type of documents they are requesting, and any additional notes or instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the requested information in the corresponding fields.
2. For each field, ensure that the required fields are filled in and that the correct type of input (e.g., email, date, etc.) is used.
3. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. legal_benefits_order_form**: This field is not a field to fill in, but rather a label for the form itself.
* **2. benefits_requester_name**: This field is for the name of the person requesting the legal benefits.
* **3. benefits_requester_email**: This field is for the email address of the person requesting the legal benefits. Please use a valid email address in the format of `username@example.com`.
* **4. benefits_requester_phone**: This field is for the phone number of the person requesting the legal benefits.
* **5. legal_benefits_ordered_by**: This field is for the name of the person or organization that is ordering the legal benefits.
* **6. legal_benefits_ordered_date**: This field is for the date on which the legal benefits are ordered. Please use the date format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **7. legal_benefits_ordered_time**: This field is for the time when the legal benefits are ordered. 
* **8. legal_benefits_requested_reason**: This field is for a brief description of why the legal benefits are being requested.
* **9. legal_benefits_requested_documents**: This field is for the type(s) of documents being requested (e.g., "Birth Certificate", "Marriage Certificate", etc.).
* **10. legal_benefits_requested_documents_type**: This field is for selecting the type of document(s) being requested (e.g., Birth Certificate, Marriage Certificate, Divorce Document, etc.).
* **11. legal_benefits_requesting_organization**: This field is for the name of the organization requesting the legal benefits.
* **12. legal_benefits_requesting_contact**: This field is for the contact information of the person or organization requesting the legal benefits.
* **13. legal_benefits_requesting_address**: This field is for the address of the person or organization requesting the legal benefits.
* **14. legal_benefits_requesting_country**: This field is for the country where the legal benefits are being requested.
* **15. legal_benefits_requesting_region**: This field is for the region where the legal benefits are being requested.
* **16. legal_benefits_requesting_city**: This field is for the city where the legal benefits are being requested.
* **17. legal_benefits_requesting_state**: This field is for the state where the legal benefits are being requested.
* **18. legal_benefits_requesting_zip**: This field is for the zip code of the location where the legal benefits are being requested.
* **19. legal_benefits_requesting_street**: This field is for the street address where the legal benefits are being requested.
* **20. legal_benefits_requesting_street_number**: This field is for the number of the street where the legal benefits are being requested.
* **21. legal_benefits_ordering_instructions**: This field is for any additional instructions or comments related to the legal benefits order.
* **22. legal_benefits_ordering_notes**: This field is for any additional comments or notes related to the legal benefits order.
* **23. legal_benefits_ordering_comments**: This field is for any additional comments or notes from the user.
* **24. legal_benefits_ordering_status**: This field is for selecting the status of the legal benefits order (e.g., Ordered, Unordered, Cancelled).
* **25. legal_benefits_ordering_date**: This field is for the date when the legal benefits were ordered. Please use the date format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Review your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* Use the correct format for dates and times as specified above.
