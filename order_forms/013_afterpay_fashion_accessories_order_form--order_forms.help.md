# Afterpay Fashion Accessories Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers for an order of fashion accessories.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your full mailing address in the "Mailing Address" field.
4. Select the type of accessory you are interested in from the "Accessories Type" field.
5. Enter the name or code of the specific item you wish to purchase in the "Item Name or Code" field.
6. Select the material or finish of the item from the "Material or Finish" field.
7. Enter the quantity of the item you wish to purchase in the "Quantity" field.
8. Select your preferred gift wrapping option from the "Gift Wrap Option" field.
9. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, Text, Required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address for order updates.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, Text, Required): Enter your full mailing address for delivery purposes.
* **-- Accessory Details --** (`details_header`, Note, Required): This section provides a header for the next fields.
* **Accessories Type** (`accessory_type`, Select One, Required): Select the category of accessory you are interested in (e.g. Luxury Watch, Fine Jewelry, etc.).
* **Item Name or Code** (`item_name_code`, Text, Required): Enter the name or code of the specific item you wish to purchase.
* **Material or Finish** (`material_finish`, Select One, Required): Select the material or finish of the item (e.g. 14k Gold, Sterling Silver, etc.).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, Number, Required): Enter the quantity of the item you wish to purchase.
* **Gift Wrap Option** (`gift_wrap`, Select One, Optional): Select whether you would like complimentary gift wrapping for your purchase.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, Select One, Required): Select your preferred payment method (e.g. Afterpay, Visa, etc.).

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your name and email address correctly.
* Enter your mailing address in a format that is easy to read.
* Select the correct accessory type and material/finish.
* Enter the correct item name or code.
* Ensure you select the correct payment method.
* If you have any questions, do not hesitate to contact us.
