# Baby Shower Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information for a baby shower supply order. It is intended for event organizers to provide details of the event, including the date, decorations, tableware, party favors, and payment method preference. This form helps streamline the ordering process and ensures that all necessary information is collected for successful order fulfillment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the event details: Enter the customer's name, date of the baby shower, and requested delivery date.
2. Choose a decoration package: Select the desired decoration package from the options provided.
3. Order tableware: Enter the quantity of tableware sets needed.
4. Specify tableware theme: Choose a tableware theme from the available options or select custom.
5. Add party favors: Enter the quantity of party favors required.
6. Add special instructions: Provide any special instructions or custom theme details.
7. Choose a payment method: Select the preferred payment method for the order.
8. Enter contact information: Enter a phone number to contact you in case of any issues.
9. Provide delivery address: Enter the address where the order will be delivered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Baby Shower Event Supply Order --**: This is the header for the form, providing a brief description of what the form is for.
* **Organizer or Customer Name** (`customer_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person organizing the baby shower or the customer's name if you're representing them.
* **Date of Baby Shower** (`event_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the baby shower in `MM/DD/YYYY` format.
* **Requested Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you'd like to receive the order, typically at least 2 weeks in advance.
* **Decoration Package** (`decorations_order`, `select_one`, required): Choose from Basic (Balloons and Streamers), Deluxe (Themed Backdrop and Centerpieces), Premium (Custom Banners and Floral Arrangements), or No Decorations Needed.
* **Tableware Set Quantity (Packs of 20)** (`tableware_count`, `number`, optional): Enter the quantity of tableware sets (plates, cups, and napkins) needed. Each set contains 20 items.
* **Tableware Color/Theme** (`tableware_theme`, `select_one`, optional): Choose from Pastel Blue, Pastel Pink, Gender Neutral (Yellow/Green), or specify a custom theme in the special instructions field.
* **Party Favor Quantity** (`party_favor_quantity`, `number`, optional): Enter the quantity of party favors needed.
* **Special Order Instructions or Custom Theme Details** (`special_instructions`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information or custom theme details.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose from Credit Card, Bank Transfer, PayPal, or Cash on Delivery.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter a phone number to contact you in case of any issues.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address where the order will be delivered.

## Tips
* Please double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any special requests or custom needs, please provide them in the special instructions field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at the provided phone number.
