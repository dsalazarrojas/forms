# diamond jewelry order form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for customers to provide their order details for diamond jewelry. It's used to collect information that will enable our team to process and fulfill their orders correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you've filled out the form for the correct purpose and context.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and detailed information about your order.
3. Review each field carefully to ensure you've provided the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Name
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.

### Customer Email
* **Customer email** (`customer_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you about your order.

### Customer Phone
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for order updates.

### Customer Address
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, `text`, optional): Enter your home or physical address where your order will be shipped to.

### Customer City
* **Customer City** (`customer_city`, `text`, optional): Enter your city where your order will be shipped to.

### Customer State
* **Customer State** (`customer_state`, `text`, optional): Enter your state where your order will be shipped to.

### Customer Zip
* **Customer Zip** (`customer_zip`, `text`, optional): Enter your zip code where your order will be shipped to.

### Order Total
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, `number`, optional): Enter the total amount of your order, including tax and shipping.

### Payment Method
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ **Visa**
	+ **Mastercard**
	+ **PayPal**
	Select your preferred payment method for your order.
