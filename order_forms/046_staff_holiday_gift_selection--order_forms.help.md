# Staff Holiday Gift Selection - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect staff holiday gift preferences and other relevant details such as delivery options and gift amount.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the available gift options and select the gifts you would like to receive.
2. Choose your preferred delivery options.
3. Confirm whether you would like to receive the gift.
4. Enter your name.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Enter your email address.
7. Provide a message for the gift (optional).
8. Select the delivery date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gift Preferences** (`gift_preferences`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the gifts you would like to receive. This field allows you to choose from multiple gift options, which are listed as "Yes" or "No".
* **Delivery Choices** (`delivery_choices`, select_one, required: false): Choose your preferred delivery option, which are listed as "Gift 1", "Gift 2", or "Gift 3".
* **Confirm Delivery** (`confirm_delivery`, select_one, required: false): Confirm whether you would like to receive the gift. Choose "True" or "False".
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Gift Message** (`gift_message`, text, required: false): Provide a message for the gift (optional).
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required: false): Select the delivery date for the gift.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required: false): Select the delivery time for the gift.
* **Gift Amount** (`gift_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the gift amount (optional). 

## Tips
* Please review the available gift options carefully before selecting your preferences.
* Make sure to enter your contact information accurately.
* If you have any special requests for the gift, please include them in the gift message field.
