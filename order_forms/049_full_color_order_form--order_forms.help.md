# Full Color Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a customer's order for full color printing services. It is used to gather details such as color, paper size, paper type, quantity, unit price, and total price. Customers will also need to provide their contact information, shipping methods, payment terms, and any special requests they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer name, email, and phone number.
2. Select the color details and paper type for your order.
3. Choose the paper size and quantity you want.
4. Input the unit price and calculate the total price.
5. Select the shipping method and input any additional shipping details.
6. Choose your payment terms.
7. Input any special requests or notes for your order.
8. Input any additional notes or details about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **color_details**: Input the specific color you want your order printed on.
* **paper_size**: Select the size of the paper you want your order printed on.
* **paper_type**: Select the type of paper you want your order printed on.
* **quantity**: Input the number of orders you want.
* **unit_price**: Input the price per unit of your order.
* **total_price**: Calculate the total price based on the quantity and unit price.
* **shipping_method**: Select the shipping method you want for your order.
* **shipping_details**: Input any additional shipping details, such as shipping address and shipping instructions.
* **payment_terms**: Select your payment terms, such as whether you want to pay in advance or on delivery.
* **special_request**: Input any special requests you may have for your order.
* **customer_name**: Input your customer name.
* **email**: Input your email address.
* **phone**: Input your phone number.
* **date**: Input the date you want your order delivered.
* **time**: Input the time you want your order delivered.
* **note**: Input any additional notes for your order.
* **shipping_address**: Input your shipping address.
* **billing_address**: Input your billing address.
* **order_note**: Input any additional notes about your order.
* **order_details**: Input any additional details about your order.
* **order_number**: Input your order number.
* **order_date**: Input the date of your order.
* **order_time**: Input the time of your order.

## Tips
* Be accurate and thorough when inputting your order details to ensure your order is processed correctly.
* Double-check your input for any errors or inconsistencies.
* If you have any special requests or questions, do not hesitate to contact us.
