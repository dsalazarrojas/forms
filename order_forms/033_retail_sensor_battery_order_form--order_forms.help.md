# retail_sensor_battery_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The retail sensor battery order form is used to collect information from customers for orders of retail sensor batteries. This form is meant for individual or small business purchases and is not suitable for bulk or wholesale orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Read through the form carefully and ensure you have the required information ready.
- Choose the relevant options for each field based on your order details.
- Fill in the shipping details, including whether you want to ship your order or not.
- Select the type of shipping you prefer.
- Choose the type of sensor battery you need.
- Enter the quantity of sensor batteries you require.
- Provide your shipping address.
- Fill in your shipping city.
- Select your shipping state.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Shipping details (<name>):** This field allows you to specify whether you want to ship your order or not.
  - Select 'Yes' if you want to ship the order.
  - Select 'No' if you don't want to ship the order.

- **Shipping Type (<name>):** This field allows you to choose the shipping type for your order.
  - Select 'Yes' for expedited shipping (e.g., next-day or express shipping).
  - Select 'No' for regular shipping (e.g., ground or standard shipping).

- **Sensor Battery Type (<name>):** This field allows you to select the type of sensor battery you need.
  - Select 'Yes' for the sensor battery type you require.
  - Select 'No' for a different type.

- **Quantity (<name>):** This field allows you to enter the quantity of sensor batteries you need.
  - Enter the number of sensor batteries you require.

- **Shipping Address (<name>):** This field allows you to provide your shipping address.
  - Enter your shipping address in the format 'Street Address, City, State ZIP'.

- **Shipping City (<name>):** This field allows you to fill in your shipping city.
  - Enter the city where you want to ship your order.

- **Shipping State (<name>):** This field allows you to select your shipping state.
  - Select 'Yes' for the state you're shipping to.
  - Select 'No' for a different state.

## Tips
- Double-check your selections before submitting the form.
- Ensure you have entered accurate information to avoid delays in processing your order.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact support.
