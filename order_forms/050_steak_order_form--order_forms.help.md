# Steak Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Steak Order Form is a tool used to place an order for steaks. It allows customers to select their preferred steaks, enter their contact details, make a payment, and add any additional notes about their order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the first page, select the option to "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you would like to order steaks.
2. If you selected "Yes", you will be directed to the next page. If you selected "No", the form will be complete.
3. On the second page, enter your contact details in the "Contact Details" field.
4. On the third page, enter your payment details in the "Make a Payment" field.
5. On the final page, add any additional notes about your order in the "Notes" field.
6. Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Steaks** (`order_form_steak_preference`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" to place an order for steaks, and "No" to cancel your order.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your name, email, and phone number to ensure efficient communication and order fulfillment.
* **Make a Payment** (`order_form_steak_payment`, text, optional): Enter your payment information, such as credit card number or other accepted payment methods.
* **Notes** (`order_form_steak_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or instructions about your order.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have provided all required information to complete your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our customer support team.
* We accept all major credit cards for payment.
