# Invoice Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Invoice Information Collection Form is a tool used to gather essential details from a company or business being invoiced. It helps to ensure accurate and complete information is exchanged between the parties involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

*   Enter the company's name and address in the corresponding fields.
*   Choose the city from the dropdown list.
*   Select the state from the provided options (e.g., CA, CO, FL).
*   Enter the 5-digit zip code.
*   Provide the contact person's name.
*   Enter the contact person's phone number.
*   Enter the contact person's email address (ensure it's in the correct format, e.g., example@example.com).
*   Provide the company website (if applicable).
*   Choose the type of company (Business or Private).
*   If the company type is not listed, you can describe it in the "Other Company Type" field.
*   Enter the 13-digit tax ID.
*   Enter the invoice date (mm/dd/yyyy).
*   Enter the due date (mm/dd/yyyy).
*   Enter the invoice amount (2 digits after decimal point).
*   Choose the payment method (Online Payment, Bank Transfer, or Check).
*   Enter the payment terms.
*   Enter any additional notes about the invoice.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Company Name
*   **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the company or business being invoiced.
*   **Company Address** (`company_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the company or business being invoiced.
*   **City** (`city`, `text`, required/optional): Choose the city from the dropdown list.
*   **State** (`state`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the state from the provided options.
*   **Zip** (`zip`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the 5-digit zip code.
*   **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required/optional): Provide the contact person's name.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the contact person's phone number.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the contact person's email address (ensure it's in the correct format).
*   **Company Website** (`company_website`, `text`, required/optional): Provide the company website (if applicable).
*   **Company Type** (`company_type`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the type of company (Business or Private).
*   **Other Company Type** (`company_type_other`, `text`, required/optional): If the company type is not listed, describe it in this field.
*   **Tax ID** (`tax_id`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the 13-digit tax ID.
*   **Invoice Date** (`invoice_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the invoice date (mm/dd/yyyy).
*   **Due Date** (`due_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the due date (mm/dd/yyyy).
*   **Invoice Amount** (`invoice_amount`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the invoice amount (2 digits after decimal point).
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the payment method (Online Payment, Bank Transfer, or Check).
*   **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the payment terms.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the invoice.

## Tips
*   Be accurate and complete when filling out the form to ensure smooth processing.
*   Review the form before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled out.
*   If a field is not applicable, do not fill it out.
*   Use the "Other Company Type" field if the company type is not listed.
