# Newsletter Subscription Management - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow users to manage their newsletter subscriptions, customize their email delivery preferences, and set specific dates and times for delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required information, such as your email address and any other required fields.
2. Select your preferred email frequency (e.g. Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
3. Choose specific dates and times for delivery (e.g. start and end dates, delivery date, and delivery time).
4. Add any additional notes or comments if needed.
