# e_commerce_agreement_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers regarding their online shopping experience, specifically their agreement with the ecommerce site's terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. If you have any issues or concerns, make sure to include them in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information**:
	+ **Customer Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
	+ **Email Address**: Enter your email address associated with your purchase.
	+ **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number associated with your purchase.
* **Business Information**:
	+ **Business Name**: If you are a business owner, please enter your company's name.
	+ **Business Address**: Enter your company's address.
* **Agreement Terms**:
	+ **Have you reviewed the agreement terms?**: Yes/No, indicate whether you have reviewed the terms.
	+ **Do you have any issues with the terms?**: If yes, please elaborate.
* **Payment Terms**:
	+ **Payment Method**: How do you prefer to pay? (e.g. credit card, bank transfer, etc.)
* **Shipping Terms**:
	+ **Shipping Method**: How do you prefer to receive your order? (e.g. standard shipping, express shipping, etc.)
* **Delivery Terms**:
	+ **Delivery Date**: What date would you like to receive your order by?
* **Return Terms**:
	+ **Return Policy**: Have you reviewed the return policy? Yes/No, indicate whether you have reviewed it.
	+ **Return Issues**: If yes, please elaborate.
* **E-Signature**:
	+ **E-Signature**: An electronic signature is a digital representation of your handwritten signature.
	+ **Signature Image**: Upload a copy of your handwritten signature.
* **Customer Agreement**:
	+ **Have you reviewed the customer agreement?**: Yes/No, indicate whether you have reviewed it.
	+ **Agreement Issues**: If yes, please elaborate.

Note: This form is for feedback purposes only and is not a comprehensive review of the site's policies.
