# Seed Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seed Requisition Form is a tool for suppliers to submit their seed orders, enabling us to track and manage seed inventory accurately. This form is essential for preventing stockouts, ensuring timely deliveries, and maintaining accurate records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Client Name and Product Code accurately to identify the seed order.
2. Select the Seed Type from the options provided (New, Old, Damaged, or Missing).
3. Enter the quantity and unit price of the seeds.
4. Calculate the Total Cost by multiplying the Quantity by the Unit Price.
5. Enter the Order Date when the seeds are expected to be delivered.
6. Select the Seed Status (New, Old, Damaged, or Missing) to indicate the seeds' condition.
7. Add any additional Notes if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seed Requisition Form**: This is the title of the form, which should not be changed.
* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client who is requesting the seeds.
* **Product Code**: Enter the product code of the seeds being ordered.
* **Seed Type**: Select the type of seeds (New, Old, Damaged, or Missing) to indicate their condition.
* **Quantity**: Enter the amount of seeds ordered.
* **Unit Price**: Enter the price per unit of the seeds.
* **Total Cost**: This field will automatically calculate the total cost by multiplying the Quantity and Unit Price. Please ensure this field is accurate.
* **Seed Status**: Select the status of the seeds (New, Old, Damaged, or Missing).
* **Seed Description**: Enter a brief description of the seeds if necessary.
* **Seed Image**: Upload or enter a link to the seed image if required.
* **Seed Status**: Select the seed status (New, Old, Damaged, or Missing).
* **Order Date**: Enter the date when the seeds are expected to be delivered.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or information regarding the seed order, if required.
