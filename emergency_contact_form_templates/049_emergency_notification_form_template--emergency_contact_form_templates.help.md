# emergency_notification_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The emergency_notification_form_template is a form designed to collect information about emergency contacts and their status.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Click on the "Next" button to navigate to the first page.
2.  Select the first page by clicking on the "Page 1".
3.  Fill in the required fields on page 1.
4.  Click the "Save and Continue" button to proceed to the next page.
5.  Repeat steps 3-4 for each page until completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Contact Info

*   **Contact Info**: This is a text field where you can enter a brief description of the contact person.
    *   **Type**: Text
    *   **Required**: No
*   **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person.
    *   **Type**: Text
    *   **Required**: No
*   **Contact Email**: Enter the contact person's email address.
    *   **Type**: Email
    *   **Required**: No
*   **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact person's phone number.
    *   **Type**: Text
    *   **Required**: No
*   **Contact Location**: Enter the contact person's location.
    *   **Type**: Text
    *   **Required**: No

### Emergency Status

*   **Notification Status**: Select the status of the notification from the available options (Emergency, Non-emergency, or Cancelled).
    *   **Type**: Select One
    *   **Required**: No
*   **Notification Message**: Enter a brief message to provide further details about the emergency.
    *   **Type**: Text
    *   **Required**: No

## Tips
*   Please fill in the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the necessary information is collected.
*   Make sure to select the correct notification status to indicate the urgency of the emergency.
*   Enter a clear and concise message in the notification message field.
*   Review each page carefully before proceeding to the next page.
*   Use the "Save and Continue" button to navigate to the next page.
*   Do not forget to click on the "Save and Continue" button to save your progress.
