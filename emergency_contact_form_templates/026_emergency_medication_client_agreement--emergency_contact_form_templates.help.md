# Emergency Medication Client Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a client's medical history, particularly their medication regimen, in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the client's **parent's name** in the `parent_name` field.
2. Enter the client's **date of birth** in the `date_of_birth` field in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
3. In the `medications` field, select all the medications the client is taking from the list.
4. In the `frequency` field, select how often the client takes each medication from the list.
5. Enter the **dosage** of each medication in the corresponding field.
6. Select the **dosage unit** (e.g., mg, mL, etc.) for each medication in the corresponding field.
7. In the `medical_condition` field, enter any relevant medical conditions the client has.
8. Select the method of **medication administration** (e.g., oral, injection, topical, etc.).
9. In the `allergies` field, select any allergies the client has from the list.
10. Enter the **emergency contact** information, including their phone number and email.
11. Select the **contact relationship** with the emergency contact (e.g., parent/guardian, grandparent, etc.).
12. Sign and date the form to confirm the information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Parent's Name**: Enter the name of the client's parent or guardian.
	+ Required: Not required but recommended for emergency purposes.
- **Date of Birth**: Enter the client's date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
	+ Required: False
- **Medications**: List the medications the client is taking, selecting from the options provided.
	+ Required: False
- **Frequency**: Select how often each medication is taken.
	+ Required: False
- **Dosage**: Enter the dosage of each medication.
	+ Required: False
- **Dosage Unit**: Select the unit of measurement for the dosage (e.g., mg, mL, etc.).
	+ Required: False
- **Medical Condition**: Enter any relevant medical conditions the client has.
	+ Required: False
- **Medication Administration**: Select the method of administration (e.g., oral, injection, etc.).
	+ Required: False
- **Allergies**: List any allergies the client has.
	+ Required: False
- **Emergency Contact**: Enter the contact information for the emergency contact.
	+ Required: False
- **Contact Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
	+ Required: False
- **Contact Email**: Enter the email of the emergency contact.
	+ Required: False
- **Contact Relationship**: Select the relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., parent/guardian, etc.).
	+ Required: False
- **Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm the information is accurate.
	+ Required: False

## Tips

- Make sure to select all relevant medications and their frequencies.
