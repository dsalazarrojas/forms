# emergency_contact_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect emergency contact information from owners or individuals who may need to be contacted in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Emergency Contact Name" field with the owner's or individual's name.
2. Enter the "Owner Phone" field with the owner's or individual's phone number.
3. Enter the "Emergency Contact Name" field with the name of the emergency contact person (if applicable).
4. Enter the "Owner Phone" field with the phone number of the emergency contact person (if applicable).
5. Select the category of the emergency contact from the "Emergency Contact Category" dropdown (Fire, Police, or Medical).
6. Add any additional information about the emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Note" field (optional).
7. Select the category of the owner/individual from the "Category" dropdown (Business or Residential).
8. Enter the "Emergency Contact Address" field with the address of the emergency contact person (if applicable).
9. Enter the "Emergency Contact City" field with the city of the emergency contact person (if applicable).
10. Enter the "Emergency Contact State" field with the state of the emergency contact person (if applicable).
11. Enter the "Emergency Contact Zip" field with the zip code of the emergency contact person (if applicable).
