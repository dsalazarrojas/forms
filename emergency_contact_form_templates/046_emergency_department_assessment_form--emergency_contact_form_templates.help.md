# Emergency Department Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Emergency Department Assessment Form is designed to gather essential information from patients and their emergency contacts in a hospital setting. This form helps healthcare professionals assess the patient's medical condition, gather contact information, and provide necessary care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. **Arrival Time**: Enter the time of the patient's arrival at the emergency department.
2. **Medical Condition**: Describe the patient's current medical condition or any relevant medical history.
3. **Emergency Contact Information**:
	* **Emergency Contact Name**: Provide the name of the patient's emergency contact.
	* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
	* **Relationship to Patient**: Specify the relationship between the emergency contact and the patient (e.g., family member, caregiver, etc.).
	* **Emergency Contact Name 2** and **Relationship to Patient 2**: Repeat the same information for a second emergency contact, if applicable.
	* **Emergency Contact Name 3** and **Relationship to Patient 3**: Repeat the same information for a third emergency contact, if applicable.
4. **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
5. **Emergency Contact Address**: Provide the address of the emergency contact.
6. **Medical History**:
	* **Medical History**: Describe any relevant medical history or conditions.
	* **Other Medical History**: Provide any additional medical history or conditions not listed above.
7. **Insurance**: Enter any relevant insurance information.
8. **Additional Information**:
	* **Medical Treatment**: Describe any current medical treatment or medications.
	* **Medications Allergies**: List any allergies or sensitivities related to medications.
	* **Other Medical Treatment**: Provide any additional medical treatment or procedures not listed above.
