# emergency_contact_information_validation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The emergency_contact_information_validation_form is a template for collecting emergency contact information from employees. This form is designed to gather essential details that can be used in emergency situations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields, such as First Name, Last Name, Address Line 1, City, Zip Code, Phone Number, and Emergency Contact Name.
2. For the Relation To Employee field, choose the correct relationship from the options provided.
3. For the Emergency Contact Relationship field, select one or more relationships that apply to the emergency contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full first name of the person.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full last name of the person.
* **Address Line 1** (`address_line_1`, `text`, required): Enter the full address line 1 of the person.
* **Address Line 2** (`address_line_2`, `text`, optional): Enter the full address line 2 of the person. (Optional field)
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city where the person lives.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, optional): Enter the state where the person lives. (Optional field)
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, `number`, required): Enter the zip code of the person's address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the person.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the person. (Optional field)
* **Relation To Employee** (`relation_to_employee`, `select_one`, required): Choose the relationship to the employee from the options provided.
* **Other Description** (`relation_other_description`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the relationship if Other is chosen. (Optional field)
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more relationships to the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email Address** (`emergency_contact_email_address`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the emergency contact. (Optional field)

## Tips
* Please fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete emergency contact information.
* If you're not sure about the relationship options, refer to your emergency contact for guidance. 
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submission.
* You may be able to use this template for other purposes, but the original intent was for emergency situations, so please adjust accordingly.
