## Tips

### emergency_contact_form_fields (Page 1)
* **Emergency Contact Form Fields**: Enter the emergency contact person's name.
* **emergency_contact_phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **emergency_contact_relationship**: Describe the relationship between you and the emergency contact person.
* **contact_relationship_other**: If the relationship is not one of the listed options, specify it here.
* **contact_relationship_other_other**: If necessary, provide additional information about the relationship.
* **emergency_contact_phone_work**: Enter the work phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **emergency_contact_address**: Enter the address of the emergency contact person.

### emergency_contact_pdf_form_fields (Page 2)
* **emergency_contact_relationship_other**: Enter any other contact information about the relationship.
