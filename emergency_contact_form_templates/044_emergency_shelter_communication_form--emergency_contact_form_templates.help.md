# Emergency Shelter Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect and track emergency information for a shelter or facility. It's meant to ensure that emergency contacts are registered and that staff can respond quickly and effectively in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have the necessary information before submitting.
2. Enter the facility's name and address in the respective fields.
3. Provide the emergency contact's name, phone number, email, and address.
4. Select the type of emergency contact relationship (e.g., staff, resident, volunteer).
5. Choose the type of emergency (e.g., medical, fire, natural disaster).
6. Describe the emergency situation briefly.
7. Enter the number of people seeking shelter and the available services.
8. Enter the time it took to respond to the emergency.
9. Select the status of the emergency (e.g., open, closed, partial).
10. Review and submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
