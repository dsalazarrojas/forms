# Crisis Management Specialist Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about a crisis management specialist referral for individuals or organizations that require support during a crisis.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Determine whether a specialist referral is needed by answering "Yes" or "No" to the first question.
2. If a referral is needed, provide the necessary information about the specialist, including their name, contact number, email address, and specialist type.
3. Select the specialist location (Local, Regional, or National) where the specialist is located.
4. Enter the specialist's description and certifications.
5. Enter the referral date.
6. Enter the specialist's name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Refer Specialist**: Determine whether a referral is needed by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the options.
* **Specialist Referral**: Enter the name of the specialist being referred.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the specialist.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the specialist.
* **Specialist Type**: Select the type of specialist being referred (Crisis Management Specialist, Crisis Counsellor, or Other).
* **Specialist Location**: Select the location of the specialist (Local, Regional, or National).
* **Specialist Description**: Enter a brief description of the specialist's services or expertise.
* **Referral Date**: Enter the date when the referral was made.
* **Specialist Name**: Enter the name of the specialist being referred.
* **Specialist Certifications**: Enter any relevant specialist certifications or qualifications.

## Tips

* Be sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information about the specialist.
* Use the form to refer individuals or organizations that require support during a crisis.
* If you are unsure about the specialist's qualifications or experience, please do not refer them through this form.
* This form is a quick reference guide, and it's recommended to perform a thorough assessment or evaluation of the specialist's qualifications before making a referral.
