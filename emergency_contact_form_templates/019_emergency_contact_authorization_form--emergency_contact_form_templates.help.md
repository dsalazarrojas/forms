# Emergency Contact Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect the contact information for emergency situations. The information collected will be used to ensure that the right contact person is reached in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the following fields with the requested information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: This is the first name of the emergency contact person.
* **Last Name**: This is the last name of the emergency contact person.
* **Email**: This is the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Phone**: This is the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Address**: This is the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship**: Select the relationship you have with the emergency contact person (e.g. Mom, Dad, Brother, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact's Name**: This is the name of the person you are designating as an emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact's Phone**: This is the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship (Emergency Contact)**: Select the relationship between the contact person and the emergency contact (e.g. Mom, Dad, Brother, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact's Address**: This is the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Permission**: Check the box to grant or deny permission for the contact person to be contacted in an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact's Message**: Leave a message for the emergency contact person, this will be sent to them in case of an emergency.
