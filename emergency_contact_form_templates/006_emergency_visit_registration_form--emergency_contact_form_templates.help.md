# Emergency Visit Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Visit Registration Form is used to gather information about an emergency visit to provide appropriate care and services to the patient.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Emergency Visit Registration** (`emergency_visit_registration_form_first_page`, Select Multiple, Required: False):
	+ This question asks whether the visit was an emergency or not. Please select "Yes" if the visit was an emergency, and "No" if it was not.
* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, Text, Required: False):
	+ Enter any relevant information about the patient, such as their name, date of birth, or any other relevant details.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, Text, Required: False):
	+ Provide the name, phone number, and relationship of the emergency contact person (e.g., spouse, parent, friend).
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, Select One, Required: False):
	+ Please select "Yes" if the patient has a relevant medical history, such as a previous medical condition or surgery, and "No" if they do not.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, Select Multiple, Required: False):
	+ Select any allergies or sensitivities the patient has, such as medications, foods, or substances.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions carefully and accurately to ensure we can provide the best care for the patient.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact the medical staff for clarification.
* If you need to add additional information or details not covered in this form, please attach a separate sheet or contact the medical staff.
