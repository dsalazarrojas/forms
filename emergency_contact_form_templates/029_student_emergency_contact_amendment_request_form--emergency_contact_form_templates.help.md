# Student Emergency Contact Amendment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an amendment to a student's emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form with the following information:
   - **Student Name**: This field is required for identification purposes.
   - **Guardian 1 First Name**: Optional, for listing the first name of the first emergency contact person.
   - **Guardian 1 Last Name**: Optional, for listing the last name of the first emergency contact person.
   - **Guardian 1 Relationship**: Optional, to specify the relationship between the student and the first emergency contact person.
   - **Guardian 1 Phone**: Optional, for listing the phone number of the first emergency contact person.
   - **Guardian 1 Email**: Optional, for listing the email address of the first emergency contact person.
   - **Student Email**: Required, for listing the student's email address.
   - **Student Phone**: Required, for listing the student's phone number.
   - **Emergency Contact Method**: Optional, to specify the method of emergency contact (e.g., first name, last name, email, or phone number).
   - **Guardian 2 First Name**: Optional, for listing the first name of the second emergency contact person.
   - **Guardian 2 Last Name**: Optional, for listing the last name of the second emergency contact person.
   - **Guardian 2 Phone**: Optional, for listing the phone number of the second emergency contact person.
   - **Guardian 2 Email**: Optional, for listing the email address of the second emergency contact person.
   - **Emergency Contact Person**: Required, for listing the name of the emergency contact person.
   - **Student Address**: Optional, for listing the student's address.
   - **Student City**: Optional, for listing the city of the student's address.
   - **Student State**: Optional, for listing the state of the student's address.
   - **Student Postal Code**: Optional, for listing the postal code of the student's address.
   - **Guardian 1 Address**: Optional, for listing the address of the first emergency contact person.
   - **Guardian 1 City**: Optional, for listing the city of the first emergency contact person's address.
   - **Guardian 1 State**: Optional, for listing the state of the first emergency contact person's address.
   - **Guardian 1 Postal Code**: Optional, for listing the postal code of the first emergency contact person's address.
   - **Guardian 2 Address**: Optional, for listing the address of the second emergency contact person.
   - **Guardian 2 City**: Optional, for listing the city of the second emergency contact person's address.
   - **Guardian 2 State**: Optional, for listing the state of the second emergency contact person's address.
   - **Guardian 2 Postal Code**: Optional, for listing the postal code of the second emergency contact person's address.
