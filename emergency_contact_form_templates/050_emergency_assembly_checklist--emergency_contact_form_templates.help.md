# Emergency Assembly Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Emergency Assembly Checklist form is used to collect information related to emergency situations that require a team's assembly. It is essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure effective emergency response.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by reviewing the form structure and understanding the fields that need to be completed. The form is divided into two main sections: Team Information and Incident Details.
2.  For each section, follow the instructions provided to ensure accurate and complete information is collected.
3.  Fill in the required and optional fields as instructed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Muster Attendance
**Muster Attendance** (`muster_attendance`, select_multiple, Optional): 
Check the muster attendance status of your team members. Select 'Yes' if all team members are present, 'No' if some team members are not present, or 'Unknown' if you are unsure.

### Has Incident Occurred
**Has Incident Occurred** (`incident_report`, select_one, Optional): 
Check if an incident has occurred. Select 'Yes' if an incident has occurred, 'No' if it has not occurred, or 'Unknown' if you are unsure.

### Team Leader
**Team Leader** (`team_leader`, text, Optional): 
Enter the name of your team leader.

### Team Location
**Team Location** (`team_location`, select_one, Optional): 
Select the location where your team is currently located. Choose from the options provided in the dropdown list.

### Contact
**Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, Optional): 
Enter the contact information (name, phone number, email, etc.) of your team leader or emergency contact person.

### Incident Details
**Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, Optional): 
Provide a short description of the incident, including any relevant details such as the cause, duration, and severity.

### Roll Call Status
**Roll Call Status** (`roll_call_status`, select_multiple, Optional): 
Check the status of the roll call. Select 'Roll Call Completed' if the roll call is completed, 'Roll Call Not Started' if it has not started, or 'Roll Call Suspended' if it is suspended.

### Roll Call Time
**Roll Call Time** (`roll_call_time`, time, Optional): 
Enter the time of the roll call in the format 'HH:MM AM/PM'.

### Roll Call Date
**Roll Call Date** (`roll_call_date`, date, Optional): 
Enter the date of the roll call.

### Roll Call Location
**Roll Call Location** (`roll_call_location`, select_one, Optional): 
Select the location of the roll call from the options provided.

### Emergency Contact Number
**Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, Optional): 
Enter the contact number of your team leader or emergency contact person.

### Emergency Contact Email
**Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, Optional): 
Enter the email address of your team leader or emergency contact person.

### Incident Description
**Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, Optional): 
Provide a detailed description of the incident, including any relevant details such as the cause, duration, and severity.

**Tips**

*   Ensure that you have the necessary information and permissions to complete the form accurately.
*   Double-check the fields for completeness and accuracy before submitting.
*   If unsure about any field, refer to your team leader or emergency contact person for guidance.
