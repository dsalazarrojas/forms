# Hospital Emergency Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from a patient's emergency contact to provide medical staff with vital information in case of an emergency. The form is typically filled out when a patient is admitted to the hospital and is intended to be used in conjunction with other medical records.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Personal Details** page with your name, address, phone number, and any other relevant personal information.
2. Move on to the **Contact Details** page and provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
3. If applicable, indicate any **Medical History** that may be relevant to your stay in the hospital.
4. Choose the **Emergency Contact** from the list of options, e.g., Parent, Spouse, or Other Relative.
5. Provide the name of your emergency contact.
6. Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
7. If you have any **Medical Conditions**, please select the relevant option from the list.
8. If you have any **Medication Allergies**, please select the relevant option from the list.
9. Indicate if you have any **Medical Equipment** with you, e.g., oxygen tank or pacemaker.
10. If applicable, provide a description of the medical equipment.
11. If applicable, provide a description of your medical condition.
12. Choose the **Relationship** with your emergency contact (e.g., Parent, Spouse, or Other).
13. If applicable, provide a description of your relationship with your emergency contact.
14. Provide the **Hospital Name** and **Hospital Room** number (if applicable).
15. Indicate if you have a **Medical Staff** member assigned to you (e.g., Doctor, Nurse, or Other).
16. If applicable, provide a description of the medical staff member.
17. Choose if you need **Emergency Medical Services** (e.g., True or None).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details**: This page is for entering your personal information, such as your name, address, and phone number.
* **Contact Details**: This page is for entering the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Medical History**: This page is for providing any relevant medical history that may be important for your stay in the hospital.
* **Emergency Contact**: This is the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: This is the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: This is the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Medical Condition**: This page is for indicating any medical conditions you have, such as diabetes or a heart condition.
* **Medication Allergies**: This page is for indicating any medication allergies you have.
* **Medical Equipment**: This page is for indicating if you have any medical equipment with you, such as an oxygen tank.
* **Medical Equipment Description**: This page is for providing a description of the medical equipment.
* **Medical Condition Description**: This page is for providing a description of your medical condition.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: This page is for indicating the relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Other**: This page is for providing a description of your emergency contact relationship.
* **Hospital Name**: This is the name of the hospital where you are being admitted.
* **Hospital Room**: This is the room number where you will be staying in the hospital.
* **Medical Staff**: This page is for indicating if you have a medical staff member assigned to you.
* **Medical Staff Description**: This page is for providing a description of the medical staff member.
* **Emergency Medical Services**: This page is for indicating if you need emergency medical services.
