# Emergency Department Patient Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from patients about their experience in the emergency department. This feedback will help us identify areas of improvement and provide better care to our patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Select the corresponding option for each question from the provided choices.
3. Add any additional comments or feedback in the "Any Other Comments" section if you have any further thoughts or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Emergency Department Patient Satisfaction Survey Page 1 (<code>)**
  * **Emergency Department Patient Satisfaction Survey Page 1 Label (<code>):** This page is the first part of the survey and asks questions about your experience in the emergency department.
* **How Long Did You Wait? (<code>rating_of_wait_time</code>):** 
  + Select one of the following options:
    - Less than 30 minutes
    - 30-60 minutes
    - 60-90 minutes
    - More than 90 minutes
  This question is to gauge the wait time in the emergency department.
* **What Was The Level Of Nurse Satisfaction? (<code>rating_of_nurse</code>):** 
  + Select one of the following options:
    - Excellent
    - Good
    - Average
    - Fair
    - Poor
  This question is to gauge the level of satisfaction with the nurses in the emergency department.
* **What Was The Level Of Doctor Satisfaction? (<code>rating_of_doctor</code>):** 
  + Select one of the following options:
    - Excellent
    - Good
    - Average
    - Fair
    - Poor
  This question is to gauge the level of satisfaction with the doctors in the emergency department.
* **Were All Safety Procedures Properly Followed? (<code>patient_safety_procedure</code>):** 
  + Select one of the following options:
    - True
    - False
  This question is to determine if all safety procedures were followed during your visit.
* **What Was Your Overal Experience Of The Hospital? (<code>hospital_environment</code>):** 
  + Select one of the following options:
    - Excellent
    - Good
    - Average
    - Fair
    - Poor
  This question is to gauge your overall experience with the hospital.
* **How Helpfull Was The Staff During Your Visit? (<code>staff_helpfulness</code>):** 
  + Select one of the following options:
    - Very helpful
    - Somewhat helpful
    - Not helpful at all
  This question is to determine how helpful the staff was during your visit.
* **Overall Rating Of Your Experience (<code>overall_satisfaction</code>):** 
  + Rate your overall experience with a number.
  This question is to gauge your overall satisfaction with your experience.
* **Any Other Comments (<code>emergency_department_patient_satisfaction_survey_note</code>):** 
  + Type any other comments or feedback you have about your experience.
  This question is to provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience.
