# Emergency Contact Update Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Contact Update Confirmation Form is used to confirm updates to an individual's emergency contact information. This form is intended for updating new or existing emergency contact details, ensuring that the information is accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the individual's new or updated emergency contact information.
2. Double-check that all fields are filled out correctly before submitting the form.
3. Make sure to save a copy of the completed form for your records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **emergency_contact_form**: 
  * Description: A free-form text field for confirming the new or updated emergency contact information.
  * Required: No
  * Type: text
  * Hint: Provide a brief description of the new or updated emergency contact information, such as a phone number or email address.
* **name**: 
  * Description: The name of the emergency contact person.
  * Required: No
  * Type: text
  * Hint: Enter the name of the person you are updating as an emergency contact.
* **email**: 
  * Description: The email address of the emergency contact person.
  * Required: No
  * Type: email
  * Hint: Enter the email address of the person you are updating as an emergency contact.
* **phone_number**: 
  * Description: The phone number of the emergency contact person.
  * Required: No
  * Type: text
  * Hint: Enter the phone number of the person you are updating as an emergency contact.
