# Emergency Contact Information Update Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and update emergency contact information from individuals. It is intended for updating contact details such as phone numbers, addresses, and email addresses. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options from the dropdown menu for "Relationship" to indicate the type of emergency contact you are submitting.
2. Enter the contact person's phone number in the "Phone" field.
3. Enter the contact person's email address in the "Email" field.
4. Fill in the "Address" field with the contact person's address.
5. Enter the contact person's city in the "City" field.
6. Enter the contact person's state in the "State" field.
7. Enter the contact person's zip code in the "Zip" field.
8. You can add any additional comments in the "Comments" field.
9. If you would like to attach any files or documents, you can do so using the "Attachments" field.
10. Finally, enter any relevant tags or keywords in the "Tags" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`emergency_contact_form`): Enter a brief description of the emergency contact.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`): Enter a unique identifier for the contact person.
* **Relationship** (`relationship`): Select the type of emergency contact (Emergency Contact 1, Emergency Contact 2, etc.).
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter the contact person's address.
* **City** (`city`): Enter the contact person's city.
* **State** (`state`): Enter the contact person's state.
* **Zip** (`zip`): Enter the contact person's zip code.
* **Comments** (`emergency_contact_form_comments`): Enter any additional comments about the emergency contact.
* **Attachments** (`emergency_contact_form_attachments`): You can attach any files or documents to support the emergency contact.
* **Tags** (`emergency_contact_form_tags`): Enter any relevant tags or keywords for the emergency contact.
* **Notes** (`emergency_contact_form_note`): Enter any additional notes about the emergency contact.

Note: This form requires no prior knowledge of the fields, so please fill them in carefully to ensure that the emergency contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
