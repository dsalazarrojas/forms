# Emergency Contact Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and store emergency contact information for individuals. This information can be used in case of an emergency, such as a medical emergency or a crisis situation. The form is designed to be completed by the individual or their authorized representative.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, start by filling out the first name and last name fields, which are used to identify the individual.
2.  Enter a phone number that can be used to contact the individual or their emergency contact.
3.  Enter the individual's email address, which can be used to contact them or their emergency contact.
4.  Select the relationship between the individual and their emergency contact from the provided options (e.g., Emergency Contact, Family Member, Spouse, or Other).
5.  Enter the emergency contact's name, phone number, and email address.
6.  Enter the emergency contact's address, city, province, and country.
7.  Enter the alternate contact's name, phone number, and email address.
8.  Enter the alternate contact's address, city, province, and country.
