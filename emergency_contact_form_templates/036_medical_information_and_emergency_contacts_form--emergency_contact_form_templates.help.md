# Medical Information And Emergency Contacts Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather important medical information and emergency contact details for individuals. The purpose is to provide a standardized and efficient way to collect this information, ensuring that you have the necessary data in case of a medical emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

* Click on the "Emergency Contact" section and choose whether the individual has emergency contacts or not.
* If they do, click on the "Medical Information" section and enter any relevant medical details.
* In the "Allergies" and "Medication List" fields, list any known allergies and medications the individual takes.
* In the "Medical History" field, provide any relevant medical history or conditions.
* In the "Emergency Contact" section, fill in the details of the contact person's name and phone number.
* In the "Emergency Contact Relationship" field, specify your relationship with the contact person.
* If you have additional medical emergency contacts, you can list them in the "Medical Emergency Contact" field.
* In the "Medical Emergency" field, describe the emergency situation that would require contact.
* In the "What To Do" field, provide instructions on what to do in case of a medical emergency.
* In the "Contact Method" field, specify the method you would use to contact the emergency contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact_form`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose whether the individual has emergency contacts or not.
* **Medical Information** (`medical_info_form`, Text, Optional): Enter any relevant medical information, such as medical conditions, treatments, or other relevant details.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_condition`, Text, Optional): List any known medical conditions or conditions that may require medical attention.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, Text, Optional): List any known allergies the individual has.
* **Medication List** (`medication_list`, Text, Optional): List any medications the individual is currently taking.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, Text, Optional): Provide any relevant medical history or conditions.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, Text, Optional): Specify your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Emergency Contact** (`medical_emergency_contact`, Text, Optional): If you have additional emergency contacts, list them here.
* **Medical Emergency** (`medical_emergency`, Text, Optional): Describe the emergency situation that would require contact.
* **What To Do** (`medical_emergency_what_to_do`, Text, Optional): Provide instructions on what to do in case of a medical emergency.
* **Contact Method** (`medical_emergency_contact_method`, Text, Optional): Specify the method you would use to contact the emergency contact.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* If there are multiple emergency contacts, list them in the order of priority.
* If the individual has a medical emergency contact, ensure that the contact person is aware of their role.
* Review the form carefully to ensure all necessary fields are completed.
