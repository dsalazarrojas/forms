# Emergency Procedures Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Procedures Form is a vital tool to document and maintain emergency contact information, procedure descriptions, and status updates within an organization. It's designed to be completed by employees, managers, or other authorized personnel to ensure that critical contact information is accurate and easily accessible in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form's sections carefully to ensure that you're filling out the correct information.
2. Fill out the form with the required and optional fields, depending on the specific emergency procedure being documented.
3. Double-check that you've entered all relevant details accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Emergency Contact Form Template** (`emergency_contact_form_template`): A text field where you can enter a template or template-related information for the emergency contact form.
* **Emergency Procedure Name** (`emergency_procedure_name`): Enter a clear and concise name for the emergency procedure being documented.
* **Emergency Procedure Location** (`emergency_procedure_location`): Provide the location relevant to the emergency procedure, if applicable.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`): Enter the name of the emergency contact person or person in charge.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person or person in charge.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`): Enter the email address of the emergency contact person or person in charge. Please use the correct format for an email address (e.g., `john.doe@example.com`).
* **Emergency Procedure Description** (`emergency_procedure_description`): Describe the procedure in detail, including any necessary steps or actions required in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Procedure Type** (`emergency_procedure_type`): Select the type of emergency procedure being documented, such as Emergency Contact Form or Safety Inspection Form.
* **Emergency Procedure Priority** (`emergency_procedure_priority`): Choose the priority level for the emergency procedure, such as High, Medium, or Low.
* **Emergency Procedure Status** (`emergency_procedure_status`): Select the status of the emergency procedure, such as Active, Inactive, or Pending.

## Tips
* Double-check that you've entered all required and optional fields accurately and completely.
* Review the form thoroughly before submitting to ensure that all information is correct and up-to-date.
* Keep the form in a secure and accessible location, such as a centralized intranet or shared drive, to maintain the most recent version of the emergency procedures.
