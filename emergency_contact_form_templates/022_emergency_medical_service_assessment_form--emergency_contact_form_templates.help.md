# Emergency Medical Service Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Emergency Medical Service Assessment Form is designed to gather relevant information about an individual's medical condition, symptoms, and emergency contact details. It's essential to complete the form accurately and honestly to provide the best possible assistance.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Review the form thoroughly and understand each field's purpose.
*   Complete the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
*   Ensure that you provide the necessary contact details for emergency situations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, `required: false`): Enter your first name.
*   **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, `required: false`): Enter your last name.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, `required: false`): Enter your email address for emergency contact purposes.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, `required: false`): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
*   **Date** (`date`, `date`, `required: false`): Enter the date of the medical condition or symptom.
*   **Time** (`time`, `time`, `required: false`): Enter the time of the medical condition or symptom.
*   **Medical Condition** (`condition`, `text`, `required: false`): Describe your current medical condition or illness.
*   **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `text`, `required: false`): List your current symptoms or any discomfort you're experiencing.
*   **Emergency contact** (`emergency_contact`, `select_one`, `required: false`): Choose the primary emergency contact from the options provided.
*   **2** (`emergency_contact_2`, `select_one`, `required: false`): Choose the secondary emergency contact from the options provided.
*   **3** (`emergency_contact_3`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): Select all applicable emergency contacts from the options provided.
*   **Address** (`address`, `text`, `required: false`): Enter your current address for emergency contact purposes.
*   **Medical provider** (`medical_provider`, `text`, `required: false`): Enter the name of your medical provider or doctor.
*   **Medical history** (`medical_history`, `text`, `required: false`): Describe your medical history, including any previous conditions or allergies.
*   **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, `required: false`): List any allergies or medical conditions that may affect your treatment.

## Tips
*   Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
*   Use clear and concise language when describing your medical condition and symptoms.
*   Double-check your contact details to ensure they are up-to-date and correct.
*   If you're unsure about any field or information, consult with a medical professional or emergency contact for guidance.
