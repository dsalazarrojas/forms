# Employee Emergency Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Emergency Contact Form is a crucial document that provides essential information about your emergency contacts. It's designed to help us quickly reach out to your loved ones in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. Enter your phone number, if you have one.
3. Enter your email address.
4. List your emergency contacts, such as family members or close friends.
5. Enter their phone numbers and email addresses.
6. Select the relationship you have with each contact (e.g., Mother, Father, Brother).
7. Ensure you have accurately completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Phone Number**: If you have a phone number, enter it. This will be used to quickly reach out to you in an emergency.
* **Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used to reach out to you in non-emergency situations.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: List your emergency contacts, such as family members or close friends.
* **Relationship**: Select the relationship you have with each emergency contact (e.g., Mother, Father, Brother).
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of each emergency contact.
* **Contact Info**: Enter any additional information about each emergency contact (e.g., their address, workplace).
* **Emergency Phone Number**: If you have an emergency phone number, enter it. This will be used to quickly reach out to you in an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: If you have an emergency contact email, enter it. This will be used to reach out to you in non-emergency situations.

## Tips

* Ensure you have accurately completed all the required fields.
* Double-check the information you provide to ensure it's up-to-date and accurate.
* Review and update this form regularly to ensure your emergency contacts are current.
