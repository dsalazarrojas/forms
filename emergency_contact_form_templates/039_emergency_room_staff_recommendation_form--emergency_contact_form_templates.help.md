# emergency_room_staff_recommendation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The emergency room staff recommendation form is used to collect feedback on a staff member's performance during an emergency situation. It helps to document their ability to remain calm, provide clear communication, and take appropriate action in high-stress situations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the staff member's first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Select the staff member's job title from the "Staff Title" field.
3. Provide the date of the emergency event in the "Date" field.
4. Rate the staff member's overall performance on a scale of 0-4 in the "Overall Performance Rating" field.
5. Rate the staff member's communication skills on a scale of 0-4 in the "Communication Skills" field.
6. Rate the staff member's empathy towards patients and colleagues on a scale of 0-4 in the "Empathy" field.
7. Rate the staff member's ability to work as a team on a scale of 0-4 in the "Teamwork" field.
8. Rate the staff member's professionalism on a scale of 0-4 in the "Professionalism" field.
9. Rate the staff member's time management skills on a scale of 0-4 in the "Time Management" field.
10. Rate the staff member's adaptability on a scale of 0-4 in the "Adaptability" field.
11. Rate the staff member's leadership skills on a scale of 0-4 in the "Leadership" field.
12. Describe the emergency situation in the "Emergency Situation" field.
13. Enter any staff recommendation in the "Staff Recommendation" field.
14. Indicate if feedback was given to the staff member in the "Feedback Given" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`staff_name`, text, optional): Enter the staff member's first name.
* **Staff Title** (`staff_title`, text, optional): Select the staff member's job title.
* **Date** (`feedback_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the emergency event.
* **Overall Performance Rating** (`staff_performance`, number, optional): Rate the staff member's overall performance on a scale of 0-4.
* **Communication Skills** (`communication`, number, optional): Rate the staff member's communication skills on a scale of 0-4.
* **Empathy** (`empathy`, number, optional): Rate the staff member's empathy towards patients and colleagues on a scale of 0-4.
* **Teamwork** (`teamwork`, number, optional): Rate the staff member's ability to work as a team on a scale of 0-4.
* **Professionalism** (`professionalism`, number, optional): Rate the staff member's professionalism on a scale of 0-4.
* **Time Management** (`time_management`, number, optional): Rate the staff member's time management skills on a scale of 0-4.
* **Adaptability** (`adaptability`, number, optional): Rate the staff member's adaptability on a scale of 0-4.
* **Leadership** (`leadership`, number, optional): Rate the staff member's leadership skills on a scale of 0-4.
* **Emergency Situation** (`emergency_situation`, text, optional): Describe the emergency situation.
* **Staff Recommendation** (`staff_recommendation`, text, optional): Enter any staff recommendation.
* **Feedback Given** (`feedback_given`, text, optional): Indicate if feedback was given to the staff member.

## Tips
* Ensure you rate the staff member's performance on a scale of 0-4 for each category.
* Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your rating.
* Use the "Emergency Situation" field to describe the context of the emergency event.
* Enter a clear and concise staff recommendation in the "Staff Recommendation" field.
* Indicate if feedback was given to the staff member in the "Feedback Given" field.
