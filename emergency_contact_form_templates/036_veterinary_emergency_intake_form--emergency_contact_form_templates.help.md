# Veterinary Emergency Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for veterinary clinics to quickly gather essential information from pet owners in emergency situations. Its purpose is to collect vital data to provide the best possible care for the pet.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please take a few minutes to carefully fill out this form. This will help the veterinary staff understand the situation and provide the best possible care for your pet.
- Ensure you answer all the required fields (if applicable) to help the vet assess the situation accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **pet_info**: Enter your pet's basic information, such as species, age, and any distinguishing features.
- **owner_info**: Provide your contact details, including your name, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
- **contact_info**: Enter the contact information of the person or facility responsible for the pet's care in case the owner cannot be reached.
- **medical_history**: Describe your pet's medical history, including any previous illnesses, allergies, or treatments.
- **emergency_info**: Briefly explain the reason for the emergency visit, including any symptoms or concerns.
- **treatment_plan**: Describe any treatment or medication the pet is currently receiving, and any specific needs or allergies.
- **veterinary_info**: Enter the name and contact details of the pet's regular vet or a nearby emergency vet for follow-up care.
- **contact_person_info**: Provide the name and contact information of the person responsible for the pet's care during this emergency.

## Tips
- Double-check the information you enter is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you're unsure about any field, consult with the vet staff member or assistant collecting the form.
- This form's accuracy and completeness will help the vet team provide the best possible care for your pet.
