# Emergency Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Claim Form is designed to collect information about an emergency claim, typically related to a car accident or other incidents where compensation is being sought.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information to fill out the form completely.
2.  Fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
3.  Be prepared to provide as much detail as possible for each question, as this will help the reviewer process the claim efficiently.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Claim Details** (claim_details): A description of the claim, including any relevant details about the incident.
- **Claimant Info** (claimant_info): Provide your personal contact information to be reached for further questions or clarification.
- **Injury Description** (injury_description): A detailed description of the injuries sustained during the incident.
- **Claimant Phone** (claimant_phone): Your phone number where you can be contacted.
- **Insurance Provider** (insurance_provider): The name of your insurance company or other relevant entity.
- **Car Details** (car_details): Provide details about the vehicle involved in the incident, including make, model, and year.
- **Injury Date** (injury_date): The date of the injury, or when the incident occurred.
- **Provider Name** (insurance_provider_name): The name of the insurance provider or company.
- **Contact Method** (contact_method): How the claimant would like to be contacted (e.g., phone, email, in-person).
- **Claim Number** (claim_number): A unique identifier for the claim, if provided by the insurance company or other relevant entity.
- **Incident Details** (incident_details): Additional details about the incident, including any circumstances surrounding it.
- **Incident Date** (incident_date): The date of the incident.
- **Provider Name** (insurance_provider_name): The name of the insurance company or other relevant entity (note: duplicate field of same type).
 
Please ensure to complete the form thoroughly and accurately to ensure that the emergency claim is processed efficiently. If you are unsure about any information, please contact the relevant authorities or your insurance company for clarification.
