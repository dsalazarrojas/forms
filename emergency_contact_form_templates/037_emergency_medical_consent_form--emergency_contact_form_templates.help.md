# Emergency Medical Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's emergency medical history, including their medical conditions, allergies, medications, and contact information for emergency contacts. This form is intended to be completed by the patient or their representative, and will be used to help emergency medical staff respond to medical emergencies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the patient information section with the patient's details.
2. List the patient's emergency contact information, including their name, relationship, and contact number.
3. Describe the patient's medical conditions, allergies, and medications.
4. Indicate the reason for the visit to the emergency medical facility.
5. Provide the patient's next of kin information, including their name, relationship, and contact details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information.
	+ This is the initial section where you will enter basic patient information.
* **Emergency Contact**: List the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.
	+ This person will be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Info**: Enter additional contact information for the emergency contact.
	+ Include their address, phone number, and email.
* **Emergency Info**: Describe the patient's emergency contact information in detail.
	+ This could include things like medical conditions, medications, and allergies.
* **Relationship with Emergency Contact**: Select the relationship between the patient and the emergency contact.
	+ Options include '1', '2', '3'.
* **Other (if not listed above)**: If none of the above relationships apply, describe the relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions**: Select the patient's medical conditions from the options provided.
	+ This will help emergency medical staff understand the patient's medical history.
* **Allergies**: Describe the patient's allergies.
* **Medications**: List the patient's medications.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship with Emergency Contact**: Select the relationship between the patient and the emergency contact.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Last Seen**: Enter the date and time the patient was last seen.
* **Last Seen Time**: Enter the time the patient was last seen.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the emergency contact.
* **Reason for Visit**: Select the reason for the visit to the emergency medical facility.
	+ Options include '1', '2', '3', '4', '5'.
* **Medical Facility**: Describe the medical facility the patient visited.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Enter the address of the emergency contact.
* **Next of Kin (Name)**: Enter the name of the next of kin.
* **Next of Kin (Relation)**: Describe the relationship between the patient and the next of kin.
* **Next of Kin (Phone)**: Enter the contact phone number of the next of kin.
* **Next of Kin (Email)**: Enter the email address of the next of kin.
* **Notes**: Leave any additional notes about the patient's emergency medical history.
	+ This is an optional field for any additional information.
