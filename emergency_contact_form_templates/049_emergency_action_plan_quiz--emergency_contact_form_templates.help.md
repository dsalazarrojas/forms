# emergency_action_plan_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to create an emergency action plan for a team or organization, gathering important contact information of its members in case of emergency.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. First, please make sure you have all the necessary information before starting the form.
2. Enter the First Name and Last Name of the team member.
3. If applicable, select all the contact numbers (Yes) for the team member.
4. Select the Employee Title of the team member.
5. If applicable, select the Emergency Contact Name, Emergency Contact Relationship, and Emergency Contact Email for the team member.
6. Enter the Emergency Contact Work Number, Emergency Contact Home Number, and Emergency Contact Cell Number for the team member.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the first name of the team member.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the last name of the team member.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select whether you want to include contact numbers (Yes or No).
* **Employee Title** (`employee_title`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the job title of the team member.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the relationship between the team member and the emergency contact (e.g. spouse, parent, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Work Number** (`emergency_contact_work_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the work number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Home Number** (`emergency_contact_home_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the home number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Cell Number** (`emergency_contact_cell_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the cell number of the emergency contact.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Review your information for any errors or discrepancies before submitting.
* Double-check your contact information before saving your answers.
* If you have any questions or doubts, please contact the team/organization administrator.
