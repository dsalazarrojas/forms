# Emergency Location Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Location Request Form is designed to collect contact information and details about your location in case of an emergency. This information will help us provide assistance and support when you need it most.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your address, including the street and city where you are located.
2. Choose the type of location you are currently at (e.g., Building, House, Apartment, or Other).
3. If you are currently in a region, select the region you are in (e.g., North, South, East, West).
4. Add any additional notes about your location that may be helpful for assistance (location_note).
5. Provide your emergency contact's name and their contact information, including their phone number and email address.
6. Add any additional notes about your emergency contact, such as their role or relationship to you (emergency_contact_note).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Emergency Location Request** (emergency_location_request): This field is where you should enter your current address, including any apartment or suite numbers if applicable.
* **Address** (address): Enter your address as it appears on a physical map or mail delivery address.
* **Location Type** (location_type): Choose the type of location you are currently at, such as a Building, House, Apartment, or Other.
* **City** (city): Enter the city where you are currently located.
* **Region** (region): Select the region you are in, such as North, South, East, or West.
* **Location Note** (location_note): Add any additional notes or details about your location that may be helpful for assistance.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (emergency_contact_name): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (emergency_contact_number): Enter the contact number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (emergency_contact_email): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Note** (emergency_contact_note): Add any additional notes or details about your emergency contact, such as their relationship to you or their role.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information to help us provide the best possible assistance.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can always ask for help or clarification.
