# emergency_contact_directory_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The emergency contact directory consent form is used to collect contact information for emergency purposes, such as in case of an emergency or crisis situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your accurate and truthful information.
2. Select the correct relationship between you and the emergency contact person.
3. Confirm your consent to be listed in the emergency contact directory.
4. Provide any additional notes or comments if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Please enter your email address that you use for official correspondence.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Please enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted by phone.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Please enter your date of birth as it appears on your official documents.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Please enter your address where you can be contacted if necessary.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, optional): Please enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship** (`relationship`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select the relationship between you and the emergency contact person.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, required): Please confirm your consent to be listed in the emergency contact directory.
* **Confirm Consent** (`confirm_consent`, `select_one`, required): Please confirm that you have read and understood the terms of the consent.
* **Confirm Date** (`confirm_date`, `date`, optional): Please enter the date you confirmed your consent.
* **Confirm By** (`confirm_by`, `time`, optional): Please enter the time you confirmed your consent.
* **Confirm Location** (`confirm_location`, `text`, optional): Please enter the location where you confirmed your consent.
* **Confirm Time** (`confirm_time`, `time`, optional): Please enter the time you confirmed your consent.
* **Confirm Details** (`confirm_details`, `text`, optional): Please enter any additional details about your confirmation.
* **Confirm Notes** (`confirm_notes`, `text`, optional): Please enter any additional notes or comments about your confirmation.
* **Confirm Consent By** (`confirm_consent_by`, `text`, optional): Please enter the name of the person who confirmed your consent.
* **Confirm Consent At** (`confirm_consent_at`, `date`, optional): Please enter the date you confirmed your consent.
* **Confirm Location 2** (`confirm_location_2`, `text`, optional): Please enter the location where you confirmed your consent (if different from previous location).
* **Confirm Time 2** (`confirm_time_2`, `time`, optional): Please enter the time you confirmed your consent (if different from previous time).
* **Confirm Details 2** (`confirm_details_2`, `text`, optional): Please enter any additional details about your confirmation (if different from previous details).
* **Confirm Notes 2** (`confirm_notes_2`, `text`, optional): Please enter any additional notes or comments about your confirmation (if different from previous notes).
* **Confirm Consent By 2** (`confirm_consent_by_2`, `text`, optional): Please enter the name of the person who confirmed your consent (if different from previous person).
* **Confirm Consent At 2** (`confirm_consent_at_2`, `date`, optional): Please enter the date you confirmed your consent (if different from previous date).

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the person or organization administering the form.
* If you need to update your information, please do not submit duplicate forms.
