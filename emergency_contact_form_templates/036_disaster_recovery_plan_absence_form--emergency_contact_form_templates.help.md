# Disaster Recovery Plan Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report employee absences during a disaster recovery plan. It captures essential information about the employee's emergency contact details, absence status, and other relevant details. Please use this form to report your absence accurately and in a timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the required information.
2. Enter the employee's emergency contact details, including their name and phone number.
3. Select the employee's absence status (e.g., On Site, Off Site, or Remote).
4. Choose your preferred method of contact (e.g., Email, Phone, or Text).
5. Enter the employee's name and date of absence.
6. Select the contact status (e.g., Contacted, Not Contacted, or No Response).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Emergency Contact Details
* **Emergency Contact Details** (`emergency_contact_details`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's details, including their name and phone number. This will be used to reach out to the contact in case of an emergency.

### Employee Absence
* **Employee Absence** (`employee_absence`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your absence status (e.g., On Site, Off Site, or Remote).

### Contact Preference
* **Contact Preference** (`contact_preference`, select_one, optional): Select how you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency (e.g., Email, Phone, or Text).

### Emergency Contact Name
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.

### Emergency Contact Phone
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.

### Employee Name
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it should be displayed on the form.

### Employee Absence Date
* **Employee Absence Date** (`employee_absence_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your absence.

### Disaster Status
* **Disaster Status** (`disaster_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether you are On Site, Off Site, or Remote during the disaster.

### Employee Location
* **Employee Location** (`employee_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of your absence (e.g., building number or office floor).

### Contact Status
* **Contact Status** (`contact_status`, select_one, optional): Select whether you have been contacted (e.g., Contacted, Not Contacted, or No Response).

### Tips
* Please ensure you have the required information before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can refer to the help guide for more information.
* If you need help with submitting the form, please contact your supervisor or HR representative.
