# Farm Emergency Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather emergency contact information for farm operations, allowing farm owners, managers, or workers to provide essential details in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Emergency Contacts" field, indicating if there are emergency contacts listed.
3. Fill out each field with accurate and up-to-date information. All fields are optional, but providing accurate information is highly recommended.
4. Select a contact type from the options provided in the "Contact Type" field to indicate the type of contact person.
5. Review and submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Emergency Contacts**: This field allows you to select if emergency contacts are listed. If you select "Yes," you will have the opportunity to list the contacts below.
* **Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number for the emergency contact person.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship**: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Address**: Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Notes**: Provide any additional notes or information about the emergency contact.
* **Contact Type**: Select a contact type from the options provided, such as Farm Owner, Farm Manager, or Farm Worker.
* **Farm Location**: Enter the location of the farm.
* **Emergency Contact Person**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
