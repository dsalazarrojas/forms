# power_outage_survival_guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact information and household details in case of a power outage. It will help us respond quickly and effectively in an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the emergency contact information, including the contact name, phone number, email, and address.
2. Specify the contact relationship (e.g., spouse, family member, neighbor).
3. Provide household details, including the household name, type, size, and number of people.
4. Specify any additional contact information for alternate contacts, if applicable.
5. Provide any additional information about the household's needs in case of a power outage.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): This field is for the primary contact person's name.
* **Contact Relationship** (`contact_relationship`, `text`, required): This field is for the relationship between the primary contact and the household (e.g., spouse, family member, neighbor).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): This field is for the primary contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): This field is for the primary contact person's email address.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, `text`, required): This field is for the primary contact person's address.
* **Alternate Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_alternate_name`, `text`, optional): This field is for an alternate contact person's name.
* **Alternate Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_alternate_phone`, `text`, optional): This field is for an alternate contact person's phone number.
* **Alternate Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship_alternate`, `text`, optional): This field is for the relationship between the alternate contact and the household (e.g., spouse, family member, neighbor).
* **Alternate Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_alternate_email`, `email`, optional): This field is for an alternate contact person's email address.
* **Alternate Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_alternate_address`, `text`, optional): This field is for an alternate contact person's address.
* **Household Name** (`household_name`, `text`, required): This field is for the household's name.
* **Household Type** (`household_type`, `select_one`, required): This field is for the type of household (e.g., single-family home, multi-family home, apartment).
* **Household Size** (`household_size`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is for the number of people in the household.
* **Household Needs** (`household_needs`, `text`, required): This field is for any additional information about the household's needs in case of a power outage.
* **Household Needs Details** (`household_needs_details`, `text`, optional): This field is for any additional information about the household's needs in case of a power outage.
* **Alternate Contact Name 2** (`emergency_contact_alternate_name_2`, `text`, optional): This field is for an alternate contact person's name.
* **Alternate Contact Relationship 2** (`emergency_contact_relationship_2`, `text`, optional): This field is for the relationship between the alternate contact and the household (e.g., spouse, family member, neighbor).
* **Alternate Contact Phone 2** (`emergency_contact_alternate_phone_2`, `text`, optional): This field is for an alternate contact person's phone number.
* **Alternate Contact Email 2** (`emergency_contact_alternate_email_2`, `email`, optional): This field is for an alternate contact person's email address.
* **Alternate Contact Address 2** (`emergency_contact_alternate_address_2`, `text`, optional): This field is for an alternate contact person's address.
* **Household Needs Urgent** (`household_needs_urgent`, `number`, optional): This field is for the household's level of urgent needs in case of a power outage.
* **Household Needs Emergency** (`household_needs_emergency`, `number`, optional): This field is for the household's level of emergency needs in case of a power outage.
* **Household Needs Non-Emergency** (`household_needs_non_emergency`, `number`, optional): This field is for the household's level of non-emergency needs in case of a power outage.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have multiple emergency contacts or alternate contacts, fill out multiple sections accordingly.
* If you have any additional information about the household's needs, provide it in the "Household Needs Details" field.
