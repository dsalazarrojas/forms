# Little Acres Learning Academy Emergency Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is for collecting emergency contact information from students' parents/guardians. It is used to gather essential contact details in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully before filling it out.
2. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Make sure to provide emergency contact information for each student.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Emergency Form** (`emergency_form`): A brief description of the purpose of this form.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): The name of the person filling out this form, typically the parent or guardian.
* **Relationship** (`contact_relationship`): The relationship between the contact person and the student (e.g., Mother, Father, Grandparent).
* **Home Phone** (`home_phone`): The contact person's home phone number.
* **Work Phone** (`work_phone`): The contact person's work phone number.
* **Email** (`email`): The contact person's email address.
* **Address** (`address`): The contact person's address.
* **City** (`city`): The city where the contact person lives.
* **State** (`state`): The state where the contact person lives.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`): The contact person's zip code.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (`emergency_contact_person`): The person to contact in case of an emergency (e.g., Mother, Father, Grandparent).
* **Emergency Contact Person Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`): The phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Person Email** (`emergency_contact_email`): The email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Person Address** (`emergency_contact_address`): The address of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship to Student** (`emergency_contact_relationship`): The relationship between the emergency contact person and the student (e.g., Mother, Father, Grandparent).
* **Student Name** (`student_name`): The name of the student.
* **Student Date of Birth** (`student_dob`): The date of birth of the student.
* **Grade Level** (`student_grade`): The grade level of the student (e.g., Preschool/Pre-K, K-1, 1st, 2nd, etc.).
* **School Year** (`student_school_year`): The current school year of the student.
* **Student Gender** (`student_gender`): The gender of the student (e.g., Male, Female, Other).
* **Student Ethnicity** (`student_ethnicity`): The ethnicity of the student (e.g., African American, American Indian/Alaska Native, Asian, etc.).
* **Student Disability** (`student_disability`): The disability of the student (e.g., None, Physical, Learning, Other).
* **School** (`school`): The school where the student attends.
* **Teacher** (`teacher`): The teacher who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Guardian Signature** (`guardian_signature`): A signature from the student's guardian or parent.

## Tips

- Make sure to update the form regularly to reflect any changes in emergency contact information.
- Verify the accuracy of the information provided.
- Review this form with students' parents/guardians before submission.
