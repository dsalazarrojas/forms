# Employee Emergency Contact List - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the emergency contacts of employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the employee's last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter the employee's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Enter the employee's email address in the "Email" field (if applicable).
5. Enter the employee's work phone number in the "Work Phone" field (if applicable).
6. Enter the employee's cell phone number in the "Cell Phone" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter the employee's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter the employee's last name.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the employee's phone number.
* **Email**: Enter the employee's email address (if applicable).
* **Work Phone**: Enter the employee's work phone number (if applicable).
* **Cell Phone**: Enter the employee's cell phone number (if applicable).

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* This form will help the organization to have a list of emergency contacts that can be reached in case of an emergency.
* Please ensure that this form is completed only once for each employee.
