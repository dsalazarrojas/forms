# Evacuation Alert Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Evacuation Alert Communication Form is a template used to collect and store information about emergency contacts of employees or individuals who need to be notified in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the necessary information for the emergency contact.
2. Ensure that you have already checked if the individual is already listed in the emergency contact directory, to avoid duplication of effort.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Emergency Contact Form** (`emergency_contact_form`, text, optional): This is a free-form text field where you can provide any additional information about the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Role** (`emergency_contact_role`, select_one, optional): Select whether the emergency contact is a "Yes" or "No" to indicate their role in relation to the employee or individual.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Cell** (`emergency_contact_cell`, text, optional): Enter the cell phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Other** (`emergency_contact_other`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Location** (`emergency_contact_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the emergency contact.

# Tips
* Be sure to double-check the information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and avoid errors.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can always reach out to the emergency contact or the relevant authorities for clarification.
* Remember to keep the information up-to-date and accurate to ensure that emergency responders can contact the right person in case of an emergency.
