# disaster_response_team_media_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain media consent from disaster response team members or participants for various media-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions truthfully and accurately.
2. Select the correct options for each field.
3. Provide your signature and date when prompted.
4. Review your responses carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Media Consent
* **Media Consent** (`media_consent`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you consent to media release (Yes/No). This will determine what kind of media can be used featuring you.

### Media Type
* **Media Type** (`media_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of media you are willing to be featured in (Yes/No).

### Release Consent
* **Release Consent** (`release_consent`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you consent to the release of your media content (Yes/No).

### Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required): Provide your contact information for emergency purposes.

### Media Permissions
* **Media Permissions** (`media_permissions`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the specific permissions you grant for media use (Yes/No).

### Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you have an emergency contact (Yes/No).

### Media Release
* **Media Release** (`media_release`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you release your media content (Yes/No).

### Signature
* **Signature** (`signature`, `note`, required): Provide your signature to confirm your consent.

### Date
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you complete this form.

### Time
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you complete this form.

### Media Usage
* **Media Usage** (`media_usage`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information about how you wish to use media.

### Training Session
* **Training Session** (`training_session`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you have attended a training session (Yes/No).

### Deployment Event
* **Deployment Event** (`deployment_event`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you were involved in a deployment event (Yes/No).

### Public Event
* **Public Event** (`public_event`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you were featured in a public event (Yes/No).

### Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or explanations.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Provide your email for further contact.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Provide your phone number for further contact.

### Media File
* **Media File** (`media_file`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide a URL or file link.

### URL
* **URL** (`url`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide a URL.

### Notes 2
* **Notes 2** (`notes_2`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or explanations.

### Email 2
* **Email 2** (`email_2`, `email`, optional): Provide your email for further contact.

### Phone 2
* **Phone 2** (`phone_2`, `text`, optional): Provide your phone number for further contact.

### File
* **File** (`file`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide a URL or file link.

### URL 2
* **URL 2** (`url_2`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide a URL.

### Notes 3
* **Notes 3** (`notes_3`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or explanations.
