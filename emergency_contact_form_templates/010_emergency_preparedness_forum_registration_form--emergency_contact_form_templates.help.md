# Emergency Preparations Forum Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from participants about their emergency preparations. The goal is to collect relevant details that can help us understand your needs and preferences in case of an emergency. This form is not intended to replace official emergency registration processes but rather serve as a supplementary tool to enhance our understanding of your emergency situations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Read the instructions carefully and make sure you understand the purpose of this form.
2. Enter your details in the designated fields, making sure to fill out all required fields.
3. Review your entries before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full name as you would like to be referred to.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Provide your valid email address to ensure we can contact you if needed.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your current address to help us locate you during an emergency.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number to enable us to reach you quickly in case of an emergency.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your emergency preparations, if applicable.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of your emergency preparations, if applicable.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Provide the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure we can effectively assist you during emergencies.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for help or clarification.
* This form is not a substitute for official emergency registration processes, and we recommend that you complete these processes as per usual procedures.
