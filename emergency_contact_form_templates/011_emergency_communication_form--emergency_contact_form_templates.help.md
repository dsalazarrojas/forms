# Emergency Communication Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect basic information for emergency communication in a quick and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are filled.
2. Provide accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Ensure that the contact method and contact relationship are relevant to the incident or emergency situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info** (<type:text>): Enter the contact information of the person filling out the form.
* **Incident Details** (<type:text>): Describe the incident or emergency situation in brief.
* **Emergency Contact** (<type:text>): Identify the emergency contact person's name.
* **Contact Method** (<type:select_one>): Select the method by which the contact was made (First Name, Last Name, Email, or Phone).
* **Contact Relationship** (<type:text>): Define the relationship between the contact and the person involved in the incident (e.g., colleague, client, etc.).
* **Location** (<type:text>): Specify the location where the incident occurred.
* **Contact Info** (<type:text>): Provide any additional contact information (e.g., phone number, address, etc.).
* **Incident Date** (<type:date>): Enter the date the incident occurred.

## Tips

* Please provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure efficient emergency communication.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to this help guide or contact the designated emergency contact.
* Fill out the form only once per incident or emergency situation to avoid duplication.
