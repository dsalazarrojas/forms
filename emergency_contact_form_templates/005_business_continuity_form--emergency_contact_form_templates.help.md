# Business Continuity Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture essential information about your organization's business continuity and disaster recovery plan. It will help us understand how your organization prepares for and responds to emergencies, identify potential risks and threats, and provide necessary support and resources for recovery.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly.
2. Use the provided hints for guidance on each field.
3. Ensure that you have the necessary authorization to fill out this form on behalf of your organization.
4. Do not duplicate your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (organization_name): This is the name of your organization. Please enter the exact name as it appears in your official records.
* **Organization Type** (organization_type): Select the type of organization you are representing. This helps us understand your organization's specific needs and risks.
* **Department Name** (department_name): If your organization has departments, enter the name of the department you are representing. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **Number of Employees** (number_of_employees): Please estimate the approximate number of employees in your organization.
* **Primary Contact Name** (contact_name): Enter the name of your main point of contact for continuity planning.
* **Contact Title** (contact_title): Enter the title or position of your main point of contact.
* **Email Address** (contact_email): Enter the email address of your main point of contact.
* **Phone Number** (contact_phone): Enter the phone number of your main point of contact.
* **Alternate Contact Name** (alternate_contact_name): If you have a backup contact, enter their name. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Alternate Contact Phone** (alternate_contact_phone): If you have a backup contact, enter their phone number. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Critical Functions** (critical_functions): List the key functions or services that must continue to operate during an emergency or disaster. Examples: Customer service, emergency response, critical infrastructure, etc.
* **Recovery Time Objective (RTO)** (recovery_time_objective): Estimate the number of hours needed to resume full operations.
* **Recovery Point Objective (RPO)** (recovery_point_objective): Estimate the maximum amount of data loss in hours before recovery.
* **Backup Location** (backup_location): Indicate where your organization stores backups.
* **Identified Risks** (identified_risks): Select the risks and threats that you have identified as potential hazards.
* **Existing Plan** (existing_plan): Indicate whether your organization has an existing continuity plan.
* **Plan Last Tested** (plan_last_tested): If your organization has a plan, indicate when it was last tested or drilled.
* **Next Review Date** (next_review_date): Suggest when your organization should review and update its continuity plan.
* **Assistance Needed** (support_needs): If your organization needs support or resources for continuity planning, describe what is required.
