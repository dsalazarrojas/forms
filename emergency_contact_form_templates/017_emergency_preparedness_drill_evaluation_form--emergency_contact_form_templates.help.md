# Emergency Preparedness Drill Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from participants and observers to evaluate the effectiveness of an emergency preparedness drill.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have attended the emergency preparedness drill.
2. Fill in your participant information:
	* Enter your participant name.
	* Select your participant position (e.g., Fire, Police, Medical, Other).
3. Fill in the drill details:
	* Enter the emergency preparedness drill ID.
	* Select the observation date.
	* Rate the drill effectiveness (e.g., Good, Fair, Poor).
	* Select your communication (e.g., Good, Fair, Poor).
	* Rate your participant satisfaction (select one or multiple options).
4. Complete the observer information:
	* Rate the observer's satisfaction with the drill (select multiple options).
	* Select your participant's emergency contact (e.g., email, phone, address).
5. Provide any additional suggestions for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Emergency Preparedness Drill ID** (1, text, required): Enter the unique ID of the emergency preparedness drill you participated in.
- **Participant Name** (2, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or badge.
- **Participant Position** (3, select_one, required): Select your role or position during the drill (e.g., Fire, Police, Medical).
- **Observation Date** (4, date, required): Enter the date you observed the emergency preparedness drill.
- **Drill Effectiveness** (5, text, required): Rate the overall effectiveness of the emergency preparedness drill (e.g., Good, Fair, Poor).
- **Communication** (6, select_one, required): Rate your communication with the emergency response team (e.g., Good, Fair, Poor).
- **Participant Satisfaction** (7, select_multiple, required): Select how satisfied you are with the emergency preparedness drill (select one or multiple options, e.g., 1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
- **Observer Satisfaction** (8, select_multiple, required): Select how satisfied you are with the emergency preparedness drill as an observer (select multiple options, e.g., 1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
- **Suggestions** (9, text, optional): Provide any suggestions for improving future emergency preparedness drills.
- **Emergency Contact** (10, email, required): Enter the contact information of the person to reach out in case of an emergency (e.g., email).
- **Phone** (11, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person to reach out in case of an emergency.
- **Address** (12, text, optional): Enter the address of the person to reach out in case of an emergency.
