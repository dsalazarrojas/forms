# Employee Support Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Support Intake Form is designed to collect information from employees about their support requests. This form helps the support team to understand the employee's concerns and allocate resources effectively to resolve the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Employee Name and Employee Email (if applicable).
2. Select your Department and Assigned To (if applicable).
3. Describe the issue you are facing in the Issue Description field.
4. If necessary, indicate your Priority level.
5. Enter your Employee Phone number (if applicable).
6. Choose your Support Department.
7. Enter the Due Date (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): If you are unsure about your department, leave this field blank.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, optional): Describe the issue you are facing in a few words.
* **Priority** (`priority`, select_one, optional): Please choose the level of priority for your issue, such as "Yes" if it's urgent or "No" if it's not.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, optional): Enter your official email address.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, optional): If you prefer to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number.
* **Support Department** (`support_department`, select_one, optional): Choose the department that best addresses your issue.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required): Please select "Yes" if you would like the support team to assign someone to your issue.
* **Due Date** (`due_date`, date, optional): If you have a specific deadline for resolution, enter the date.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to your supervisor or support team for guidance.
- Please note that this form is for employee support requests only. If you are a manager or HR representative, please use the corresponding form for your department.
