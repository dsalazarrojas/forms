# Media Training Reimbursement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Media Training Reimbursement Request Form is designed for employees to submit requests for reimbursement related to media training expenses. This form helps ensure all necessary information is collected and processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in your details and submit the form.
2.  Ensure you provide accurate information about your media training expenses.
3.  Double-check your entries before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Media Training Reimbursement Request Form** (`1`, `text`, optional): This is the form title.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official records.
* **Project Title** (`project_title`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe the project you're undergoing media training for.
* **Media Type** (`media_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of media training you've attended (e.g., video, audio, etc.).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the start date of your media training.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the end date of your media training.
* **Total Hours** (`total_hours`, `number`, optional): Enter the total hours you spent on media training.
* **Reimbursed Amount** (`reimbursed_amount`, `number`, optional): Enter the amount you're requesting for reimbursement.
* **Expense Description** (`expense_description`, `text`, optional): Describe your media training expenses.
* **Expense Amount** (`expense_amount`, `number`, optional): Enter the expense amount for your media training.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, `select_one`, optional): This field might be used by the approver to track status (though this could be optional).
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments related to your media training.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, optional): This field might be used to record the person submitting the form (though this could be automatically populated).
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, `date`, optional): This field is likely filled in automatically when you submit the form.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, `text`, optional): This field might be used to record the person approving the form (though this could be automatically populated).
