# Injection Therapy Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Injection Therapy Intake Form is a crucial document used to gather essential information from patients prior to starting injection therapy treatment. This form helps healthcare professionals understand the patient's medical history, current condition, and contact information, ensuring a safe and effective treatment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  Provide your first name and last name as they appear on your official documents.
2.  Enter your date of birth.
3.  Enter your contact number and email address where you can be reached for any updates or concerns.
4.  If you have an insurance provider, please enter their name and policy number.
5.  Share your medical history, including any existing medical conditions or allergies.
6.  Select the medications you are currently taking.
7.  Confirm your allergies, if any.
8.  Agree to the terms and conditions of treatment.
9.  Provide information about your emergency contact, including their name, number, and relationship.
10.  Enter the address of your emergency contact.
11.  Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact.
12.  If applicable, enter the name and contact number of your therapist.
13.  Enter any additional comments or notes for your therapist.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Contact Number**: Enter a contact number where you can be reached for any updates or concerns.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can receive updates or concerns from your healthcare team.
* **Insurance Provider**: If you have an insurance provider, enter their name. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Insurance Policy Number**: If you have an insurance policy number, enter it here. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Medical History**: Share any existing medical conditions or concerns.
* **Medical History Date**: If you have medical history, enter the date you first experienced the condition.
* **Medication List**: Select the medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies**: Select any allergies you have.
* **Consent**: Confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions of treatment.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relation**: Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact City**: Enter the city of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact State**: Enter the state of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Country**: Enter the country of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact.
* **Therapist Name**: Enter the name of your therapist, if applicable.
* **Therapist Number**: Enter the contact number of your therapist, if applicable.
* **Therapist Address**: Enter the address of your therapist, if applicable.
* **Therapist Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes for your therapist.
* **Therapist Notes Additional**: Enter any further comments or notes for your therapist, if applicable.

Note: Please complete this form carefully and accurately. If you have any doubts or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to your healthcare team.
