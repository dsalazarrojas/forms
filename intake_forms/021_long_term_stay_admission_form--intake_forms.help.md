# Long Term Stay Admission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to capture information about an individual's long term stay, including their application details, contact information, and medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
Before beginning, please ensure that you have the necessary permission to fill out this form and that you are providing true and accurate information. The form consists of multiple pages, each with its own set of fields. You may need to complete each page separately, but the fields on each page should be self-explanatory.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information** (`long_term_stay_form_1`): This page collects basic information about the applicant, including their name, email, and phone number. Please ensure that the name is spelled correctly, and the email and phone number are accurate and up-to-date.
* **Applicant's Name** (`applicant_name`): Enter the full name of the applicant.
* **Applicant's Email** (`applicant_email`): Enter the applicant's email address.
* **Applicant's Phone** (`applicant_phone`): Enter the applicant's phone number.
* **Stay Start Date** (`stay_start_date`): Enter the date when the applicant's stay is scheduled to begin.
* **Stay End Date** (`stay_end_date`): Enter the date when the applicant's stay is scheduled to end.
* **Stay Duration** (`stay_duration`): Select whether the applicant will be staying for a short-term or long-term stay.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`): Enter the name of the facility where the applicant will be staying.
* **Facility Address** (`facility_address`): Enter the address of the facility where the applicant will be staying.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`): Enter the name of the program the applicant will be participating in, if applicable.
* **Program Type** (`program_type`): Enter the type of program the applicant will be participating in, if applicable.
* **Stay Notes** (`stay_notes`): Enter any additional notes or comments about the applicant's stay.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`): Enter any relevant medical history or conditions the applicant may have.
* **Medication List** (`medication_list`): Enter a list of any medications the applicant is currently taking.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`): Enter any allergies or sensitivities the applicant has.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Relation** (`emergency_relation`): Enter the relationship between the applicant and the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Address** (`emergency_address`): Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
 

Please note that some fields may not be required, but it's always best to fill out as much information as possible to ensure the applicant's stay is well-documented and safe.
