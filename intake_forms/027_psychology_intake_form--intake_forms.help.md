# Psychology Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Psychology Intake Form is a collection tool used to gather patient information and medical history for psychology-related services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. Provide clear and concise answers for each field.
3. Ensure you complete all fields marked as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required/optional): Describe any previous medical conditions or surgeries that may impact your treatment.
* **Family History** (`family_history`, text, required/optional): Share any relevant family medical history or genetic conditions.
* **Mental Status** (`mental_status`, text, required/optional): Explain your current mental health status, including any mental health diagnoses or conditions.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, required/optional): List any prescription medications, herbal supplements, or vitamins you're currently taking.
* **Medical Info** (`medical_info`, text, required/optional): Report any significant medical events, allergies, or sensitivities.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required/optional): List any allergies or sensitivities you have.
* **Social History** (`social_history`, text, required/optional): Share information about your personal and social background, including education, employment, and relationships.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, text, required/optional): Add any additional medical notes or comments.
* **Insurance Info** (`insurance_info`, text, required/optional): Provide your insurance information, including policy numbers and provider names.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Be thorough but concise in your responses.
* If you're unsure about a question or section, consult with your healthcare provider before proceeding.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your healthcare provider or the form creators.
