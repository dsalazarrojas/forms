# Mileage Reimbursement Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to claim reimbursement for mileage expenses. Please fill out this form accurately and honestly to ensure a smooth approval process.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Make sure you have all necessary information to complete the form accurately.
*   If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to reach out to your supervisor for clarification.
*   Please fill out the form in its entirety before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Company Name** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Your company name.
    *   Please enter your company name.
*   **Employee Name** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Your name.
    *   Please enter your name.
*   **Date Traveled** (`3`, `date`, required/optional): Date of the travel.
    *   Please enter the date you traveled.
*   **Start Location** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): Location where you started your trip.
    *   Please enter the location where you started your trip.
*   **End Location** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): Location where you ended your trip.
    *   Please enter the location where you ended your trip.
*   **Odometer Reading** (`6`, `number`, required/optional): Odometer reading at the start of the trip.
    *   Please enter the odometer reading at the start of your trip.
*   **Total Miles** (`7`, `number`, required/optional): Total miles traveled.
    *   Please enter the total miles you traveled.
*   **Reimbursement Amount** (`8`, `number`, required/optional): Amount of reimbursement you are claiming.
    *   Please enter the amount of reimbursement you are claiming.
*   **Expense Type** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): Type of expense (e.g., business, personal).
    *   Please enter the type of expense (e.g., business, personal).
*   **Notes** (`10`, `note`, required/optional): Any additional comments or details.
    *   Please enter any additional comments or details.
*   **Expense Type Options** (`11`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the type of expense (e.g., Business Travel, Personal Travel, Other).
    *   Select the type of expense (e.g., Business Travel, Personal Travel, Other).
*   **Category** (`12`, `select_one`, required/optional): Category of the expense (e.g., Business, Personal, Other).
    *   Select the category of the expense (e.g., Business, Personal, Other).
*   **Mileage Rate** (`13`, `number`, required/optional): The mileage rate.
    *   Please enter the mileage rate.
*   **Submitted By** (`14`, `text`, required/optional): Name of the person submitting the form.
    *   Please enter the name of the person submitting the form.
*   **Submitted Date** (`15`, `date`, required): Date the form was submitted.
    *   Please enter the date the form was submitted.
*   **Approved By** (`16`, `text`, required/optional): Name of the person who approved the form.
    *   Please enter the name of the person who approved the form.
*   **Approved Date** (`17`, `date`, required): Date the form was approved.
    *   Please enter the date the form was approved.
*   **Reimbursed Amount** (`18`, `number`, required/optional): The amount reimbursed.
    *   Please enter the amount reimbursed.
*   **Comments** (`19`, `text`, required/optional): Any comments or feedback from the approver.
    *   Please enter any comments or feedback from the approver.
*   **Status** (`20`, `text`, required): Status of the reimbursement (e.g., pending, approved).
    *   Please enter the status of the reimbursement (e.g., pending, approved).
*   **Payment Method** (`21`, `text`, required/optional): Method of payment (e.g., check, online transfer).
    *   Please enter the method of payment (e.g., check, online transfer).
*   **Payment Date** (`22`, `date`, required/optional): Date of payment.
    *   Please enter the date of payment.
*   **Payment Status** (`23`, `text`, required/optional): Status of payment (e.g., paid, pending).
    *   Please enter the status of payment (e.g., paid, pending).
*   **Employee ID** (`24`, `text`, required/optional): ID number of the employee.
    *   Please enter your ID number.
*   **Company ID** (`25`, `text`, required/optional): ID number of the company.
    *   Please enter your company ID number.

## Tips

*   Double-check all fields before submitting the form.
*   If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
*   Make sure to keep your answers accurate and truthful.
*   The form will only be approved if it is completed accurately and honestly.
*   If the form is not approved, you will be notified and asked to correct any inaccuracies.
