# Personal Trainer Client Intake And Liability Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Trainer Client Intake And Liability Form is designed to gather essential information about you, the client, to ensure that our trainers are aware of any potential risks or health concerns. This information will help us provide personalized guidance and support throughout your training program.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions honestly and accurately.
2. Provide as much information as possible, but only if you feel comfortable sharing it.
3. If a field is marked as optional, please provide information only if it's relevant to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Client Information (Page 1)

* **Client Information** (`client_info`, text, required): Please provide your name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Please provide the name and contact information of someone the trainer can contact in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Information** (`medical_info`, text, required): Please describe any medical conditions or allergies that may affect your training.
* **Injury History** (`injury_history`, text, required): Please describe any past injuries or medical conditions that may affect your training.

### Medical Clearance (Page 2)

* **Medical Clearance** (`medical_clearance`, text, required): Please provide a doctor's note or clearance letter if you have a pre-existing medical condition.
* **Medical Clearance 2** (`medical_clearance2`, text, required): Please provide a second medical clearance letter or note if applicable.

### Risk Assessment (Page 3)

* **Risk Assessment** (`risk_assessment`, text, required): Please describe any high-risk activities you're planning to engage in during your training.
* **Risk Assessment 2** (`risk_assessment2`, text, required): Please describe any high-risk activities you're planning to engage in during your training.

### Emergency Contact (Page 4)

* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, required): Please provide the name and contact information of someone the trainer can contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Minimalist Info** (`emergency_contact_minimalist_info`, text, required): Please provide a brief description of your emergency contact's relationship to you.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Please provide the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Please describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, required): Please provide the address of your emergency contact.

### Consent (Page 5)

* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Please indicate whether you have signed the consent form (yes/no).
* **Consent Form Signed** (`consent_form_signed`, select_one, required): Please indicate whether you have signed the consent form (yes/no).
* **Consent Form Date** (`consent_form_date`, date, required): Please provide the date you signed the consent form.
* **Consent Form Date Signed** (`consent_form_date_signed`, date, required): Please provide the date you signed the consent form.
* **Consent Form Signed By** (`consent_form_signed_by`, select_multiple, required): Please select the person who signed the consent form (Trainer/Gym).
* **Consent Form Signed By Phone** (`consent_form_signed_by_phone`, text, required): Please provide the phone number of the person who signed the consent form.

### Additional Information (Page 6)

* **Exercise Frequency** (`exercise_frequency`, text, required): Please describe your exercise frequency and routine.
* **Exercise Type** (`exercise_type`, text, required): Please describe the type of exercises you're planning to engage in during your training.
* **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, select_one, required): Please select your risk level (Low/Medium/High).
* **Risk Assessment 2** (`risk_assessment2`, text, required): Please describe any high-risk activities you're planning to engage in during your training.
* **Medical Info 2** (`medical_info_2`, text, required): Please provide any additional medical information.
