# High Dose IV Therapy Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from patients undergoing high dose IV therapy for assessment and potential treatment. It will help clinicians and healthcare staff evaluate the patient's medical history, condition, and other relevant information to provide the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's age in the "Age" field.
3. Enter the patient's medical record number, if available, in the "Medical Record Number" field.
4. Describe the patient's medical condition in the "Medical Condition" field.
5. Enter any relevant information about the patient's current medication regimen in the "Medication" field.
6. Answer the question about the patient's medical history by selecting "True" or "None" from the "Medical History" dropdown menu.
7. Select all relevant options for the "Consent" field, indicating that the patient has given consent for treatment.
8. Enter the patient's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
9. Enter the patient's email address in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Please enter the patient's first name as it is typically spelled.
* **Age**: Enter the patient's age in years.
* **Medical Record Number**: If the patient has a medical record number, enter it here. This will help us keep track of their medical history.
* **Medical Condition**: Describe the patient's current medical condition, including any allergies, sensitivities, or other relevant information.
* **Medication**: If the patient is currently taking any medication, please list it here.
* **Medical History**: Answer "True" if the patient has a known medical history, and "None" if they do not. This will help us assess their current health status.
* **Consent**: Select "I consent" to indicate that the patient has given consent for treatment.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the patient's phone number so we can contact them with any updates or questions.
* **Email**: Enter the patient's email address so we can contact them with any important information or updates.

## Tips

* Ensure you have the patient's informed consent before completing this form.
* Be as thorough as possible when filling out the "Medical History" and "Medical Condition" fields.
* If you're unsure about any information, please ask the patient or their primary care physician for clarification.
