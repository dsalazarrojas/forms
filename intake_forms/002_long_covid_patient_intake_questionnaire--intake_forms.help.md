# Long COVID Patient Intake Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients who have been diagnosed with Long COVID. The goal is to gather accurate and complete data on the patient's symptoms, medical history, and other relevant details to help healthcare professionals provide the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and accurately, based on your personal experiences with Long COVID.
2. For each field, enter your response in the corresponding text box or select from the available options.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Long COVID Patient Intake Questionnaire Form**: This is the form's title and introduction.
* **Long COVID History**: Please provide a brief explanation of your Long COVID experience, including any relevant medical history.
* **Long COVID Symptoms**: Indicate if you have experienced any of the following symptoms:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Long COVID Symptom Impact**: Indicate the severity of your symptoms, if you have experienced them:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Patient Contact**: Provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Patient Address**: Enter your address, including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Physician Contact**: Provide your physician's contact information, including their name, email, and phone number.
* **Physician Address**: Enter your physician's address, including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Visit Date**: Enter the date of your visit to the healthcare provider.
* **Long COVID Diagnosis**: Indicate the diagnosis made by your healthcare provider:
	+ Long COVID
	+ Not Long COVID
	+ Unknown
* **Long COVID Symptoms Duration**: Enter the duration of your Long COVID symptoms in days.
* **Long COVID Symptom Severity**: Describe the severity of your symptoms.
* **Long COVID Vaccination Status**: Indicate your vaccination status:
	+ Vaccinated
	+ Not vaccinated
	+ Unknown
* **Long COVID Medications**: List any medications you are taking for Long COVID.
* **Long COVID Other Health Conditions**: Describe any other health conditions you have.
* **Long COVID Medication Allergies**: List any medication allergies you have.
* **Long COVID Allergies**: Describe any allergies you have related to Long COVID.
* **Long COVID Other Conditions**: Describe any other conditions related to Long COVID.

## Tips

* Please answer all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult your healthcare provider for guidance.
* If you have filled out this form previously, please do not submit this form again.
