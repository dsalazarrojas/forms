# Plastic Surgery Patient Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Plastic Surgery Patient Intake Form is a crucial document that helps us gather essential information about your medical history, condition, and treatment preferences. By filling out this form, you will help us understand your needs better and ensure that we provide the best possible care for you during your surgical procedure.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the page tabs at the top of the form to navigate through each section.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review each section carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Patient Information
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text): Provide your primary phone number where we can reach you.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text): Enter your current address to ensure we send bills and updates to the correct location.

### Page 2: Health Condition
* **Health Condition** (`health_condition`, text): Describe your current medical condition, including any recent illnesses or injuries.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text): Share any previous medical conditions or surgeries you've had.
* **Understanding of Procedure** (`understanding_of_procedure`, text): Tell us about your understanding of the surgical procedure and any questions or concerns you may have.

### Page 3: Medical Information
* **Medical Specialties** (`medical_specialties`, select_multiple): If applicable, select any medical specialties you have.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text): List any known allergies, including medications and substances.
* **Meds** (`medications`, text): Provide a list of your current medications.
* **Medical Tests** (`medical_tests`, text): Share any recent medical tests or results.
* **Medical History Review** (`medical_history_review`, text): Summarize any recent medical history or events.

### Page 4: Surgical Procedure
* **Surgical Procedure** (`surgical_procedure`, select_one): Select whether you are ready to proceed with the surgery.

### Page 5: Insurance and Contacts
* **Insurance** (`insurance`, text): Provide your insurance information, including provider and policy number.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text): Name a person or contact we can contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, text): Share emergency contact information, including phone number and address.

### Page 6: Signatures and Notes
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text): Sign this form to confirm you have read and understand the information provided.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date): Date and time of your signature.
* **Signature Time** (`signature_time`, time): Date and time of your signature.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, text): Provide any additional medical notes or comments.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, text): This is an additional note field for any further comments.

Please review each field carefully and provide accurate information. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask your doctor or our team for assistance.
