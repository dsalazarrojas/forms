# Lease Assistance Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lease Assistance Intake Form is designed to collect essential information from individuals seeking help with their lease-related concerns. This form aims to gather contact, applicant, and rental information to provide effective assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your contact information in the first section, "Contact Information."
2. Proceed to the second section, "Applicant Information," and fill in the details about the person applying for assistance.
3. In the third section, "Rental Information," enter the details about the rental property.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, not required): This is the contact information section where you can enter your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Contact Information** is a general field for you to provide your contact details.

### Applicant Information
* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`, text, not required): Enter the details about the person applying for assistance.
* **Applicant Information** is a field for you to provide information about the person seeking assistance.

### Rental Information
* **Rental Information** (`rental_info`, text, not required): This is the section for you to enter details about the rental property.
* **Rental Information** is a field for you to provide information about the rental property.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately to provide effective assistance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the form administrators at form@gic.mx.
