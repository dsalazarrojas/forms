# Family Capital Orientation Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about the family's capital orientation, including contact details, address, family goals, family history, and other relevant background information. Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the contact details such as name, contact number, and email address to ensure that we can reach out to you with any further information or queries.
2.  Enter the address of the family member or person of interest, which will help us in understanding the geographical context.
3.  Clearly state the family's goal, which will enable us to provide better support and services.
4.  Provide detailed information about the family history, which will aid in understanding the family's dynamics and background.
5.  Confirm that you have read and understood the consent information provided.
6.  Enter the date and time of birth, if applicable, to help us in better understanding the individual's age and identity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your full name.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
*   **Address** (`address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your address.
*   **Family Goal** (`family_goal`, `text`, required/optional): Clearly state your family's goal.
*   **Family Goal Details** (`family_goal_details`, `text`, required/optional): Provide detailed information about the family's goal.
*   **Family History** (`family_history`, `text`, required/optional): Share your family's history.
*   **Consent** (`consent`, `text`, required/optional): Confirm that you have read and understood the consent information provided.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of birth (if applicable).
*   **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of birth (if applicable).
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the contact person's name.

Please note that some fields are optional and may not be required for your submission. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
