# Behavior Support Service Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Behavior Support Service Agreement form is designed to collect vital information for service providers to tailor their support services to the participant's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the participant's full name in the "Participant Full Name" field.
2. Choose the start date of the service in the "Agreement Start Date" field.
3. Select the end date of the service in the "Agreement End Date" field.
4. Choose the main category of support services in the "Primary Service Type" field (Individual Behavior Support, Group Support Sessions, etc.).
5. Describe the primary goals and objectives of the service in the "Primary Goals and Objectives" field.
6. Select the frequency of service in the "Frequency of Service" field (Multiple times per week, Weekly, etc.).
7. Enter the agreed-upon hourly rate in the "Agreed Hourly Rate" field.
8. Acknowledge the cancellation policy by selecting "I agree to the cancellation terms" in the "Cancellation Policy Acknowledgment" field.
9. Choose how you would like to proceed with confidentiality (I understand and agree to confidentiality terms, Other) in the "Confidentiality Agreement" field.
10. Select any data collection and storage methods you're comfortable with in the "Consent for Data Collection and Storage" field.
11. If applicable, enter the participant's representative name and their relationship with the participant.
12. Enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.
13. Add any additional notes or special requirements in the "Additional Notes or Special Requirements" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name (Participant Full Name)**: Enter the participant's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Date of Agreement (Agreement Start Date)**: Enter the date on which this service agreement comes into effect.
* **Agreement End Date (Agreement End Date)**: Enter the date on which this service agreement will end.
* **Agreement Start Date (Service Start Date)**: Enter the date on which services are scheduled to begin.
* **Agreement End Date (Service End Date)**: Enter the scheduled review or termination date of the service.
* **Primary Service Type (Primary Service Type)**: Select the main category of support services you're seeking (Individual Behavior Support, Group Support Sessions, etc.).
* **Primary Goals and Objectives (Primary Goals and Objectives)**: Outline the main outcomes expected from this service.
* **Frequency of Service (Frequency of Service)**: Choose the frequency of service (Multiple times per week, Weekly, etc.).
* **Agreed Hourly Rate (Agreed Hourly Rate)**: Enter the amount in USD for the hourly rate.
* **Cancellation Policy Acknowledgment (Cancellation Policy Acknowledgment)**: Select "I agree to the cancellation terms" to acknowledge the 24-hour cancellation policy.
* **Confidentiality Agreement (Confidentiality Agreement)**: Choose how you'd like to proceed with confidentiality terms.
* **Consent for Data Collection and Storage (Consent for Data Collection and Storage)**: Select any data collection and storage methods you're comfortable with.
* **Participant Representative Name (Participant Representative Name)**: If applicable, enter the participant's representative name.
* **Relationship to Participant (Relationship to Participant)**: If applicable, describe the relationship between the representative and participant.
* **Emergency Contact Name (Emergency Contact Name)**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (Emergency Contact Phone)**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Additional Notes or Special Requirements (Additional Notes or Special Requirements)**: Add any additional notes or special requirements.
