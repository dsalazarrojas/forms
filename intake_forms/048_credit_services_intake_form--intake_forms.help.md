# Credit Services Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from borrowers who are applying for credit services, such as loans, credit cards, or other financial products. This form helps loan officers and financial analysts assess borrowers' creditworthiness and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all required information.
2. If you have a co-borrower, please include their information in the Co-Borrower Information section.
3. Ensure that you have the necessary permission from your co-borrower to share their information with the lender.
4. If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Borrower Information
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Please enter your personal email address.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Please enter your physical address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Please enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Please enter your state of residence.
* **Zip** (`zip`, `number`, required): Please enter your zip code.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Please enter your contact phone number.

### Loan Information
* **Loan Type** (`loan_type`, `select_one`, optional): Please select the type of loan you are applying for. Options include:
	+ Home Equity
	+ Home Improvement
	+ Personal
	+ Vehicle
	+ Other
* **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, `number`, optional): Please enter the amount you are requesting for the loan.
* **Credit Score** (`credit_score`, `number`, optional): Please enter your current credit score.
* **Income** (`income`, `number`, optional): Please enter your annual income.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `text`, optional): Please select your current employment status. Options include:
	+ Employed
	+ Self-Employed
	+ Retired
	+ Unemployed
* **Employment Length** (`employment_length`, `number`, optional): Please enter the length of your current employment.
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, `text`, optional): Please enter the name of your employer.
* **Employer Address** (`employer_address`, `text`, optional): Please enter the address of your employer.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, `text`, optional): Please select your employment type. Options include:
	+ Full-time
	+ Part-time
	+ Seasonal
	+ Contract
* **Employment Title** (`employment_title`, `text`, optional): Please enter your employment title.

### Co-Borrower Information
* **Co Name** (`co_name`, `text`, optional): Please enter the name of your co-borrower.
* **Co Address** (`co_address`, `text`, optional): Please enter the address of your co-borrower.
* **Co City** (`co_city`, `text`, optional): Please enter the city where your co-borrower resides.
* **Co State** (`co_state`, `text`, optional): Please enter the state where your co-borrower resides.
* **Co Zip** (`co_zip`, `number`, optional): Please enter the zip code of your co-borrower.
* **Co Phone** (`co_phone`, `text`, optional): Please enter the phone number of your co-borrower.
