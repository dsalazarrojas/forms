# Virtual Intake Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Intake Survey is a form designed to collect general information, session details, and client preferences for virtual sessions. It is intended to gather essential data for a better understanding of clients' needs and goals, allowing for more personalized and effective services to be provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill in each field with your relevant information.
3. Make sure to answer all required fields (indicated by a red asterisk \* or a "required" label).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **title (General Information, text, optional)**: This field is for providing your name or a brief introduction to help us understand your context.
* **session_details (Session Details, text, optional)**: Here, you can provide any information about the session you are seeking, such as its purpose or objective.
* **session_preferences (Session Preferences, text, optional)**: If there are any specific preferences or requirements you have for the session, please let us know in this field.
* **client_consent (Client Consent, text, optional)**: Please acknowledge your understanding and consent for the virtual session by typing your initials or signature in this field.
* **session_goals (Session Goals, text, optional)**: This field is for describing the main goals you aim to achieve during the session.
* **session_topics (Session Topics, text, optional)**: If there are any specific topics or areas you would like to focus on during the session, please specify them here.
* **session_notes (Session Notes, text, optional)**: This field is for jotting down any additional notes or questions you may have before the session.
* **client_info (Client Information, text, optional)**: You can provide any additional information about yourself that may be relevant to the session.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions as thoroughly as possible, as this will help us better understand your needs.
* If you are unsure about any field or have any questions, do not hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your time and effort in completing this form accurately.
