# Hernia Mesh Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for patients to report any complications or concerns they have regarding their hernia mesh surgery. It is intended to help healthcare providers quickly gather information and provide appropriate care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information, including your email and phone number.
2. Provide the name of the surgeon who performed your hernia mesh surgery.
3. Enter the date of your surgery.
4. Describe any complications or concerns you are experiencing related to your hernia mesh.
5. Choose how you would like to be followed up with regarding your inquiry (e.g. phone, email, or mail).
6. Provide a date for follow up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (1)**: Enter your name, as it should appear on your medical records.
* **Surgeon Name (2)**: Enter the name of the surgeon who performed your hernia mesh surgery.
* **Date of Surgery (3)**: Enter the date you underwent hernia mesh surgery.
* **Follow Up Question (4)**: Describe any complications or concerns you are experiencing related to your hernia mesh surgery.
* **Patient Email (5)**: Enter your email address so that healthcare providers can easily contact you.
* **Patient Phone (6)**: Enter your phone number so that healthcare providers can reach you by phone.
* **Surgeon Email (7)**: Enter the email address of your surgeon, if you have it.
* **Surgeon Phone (8)**: Enter the phone number of your surgeon, if you have it.
* **Medical Office (9)**: Enter the name of your medical office or clinic where you received treatment.
* **Support Team (10)**: Choose how you would like to be supported during the follow up process. Select all that apply.
* **Follow Up Method (11)**: Choose how you would like to be contacted for follow up (e.g. phone, email, or mail).
* **Follow Up Date (12)**: Enter a date for follow up, or leave this field blank for the healthcare provider to schedule a follow up at their discretion.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when describing any complications or concerns you are experiencing.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, do not hesitate to contact your healthcare provider for guidance.
- If you have already completed this form, please do not attempt to fill it out again.
