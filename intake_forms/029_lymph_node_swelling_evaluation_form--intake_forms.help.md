# Lymph Node Swelling Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lymph Node Swelling Evaluation Form is designed to collect patient information regarding swollen lymph nodes. This form helps healthcare professionals assess the severity and characteristics of swollen lymph nodes, enabling them to make informed decisions for diagnosis and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read the questions and answer them accurately and thoroughly.
- Fill out the form according to your best knowledge and understanding of your swollen lymph nodes.
- If you are unsure about any question, consult with a healthcare professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Swollen Lymph Node Concerns** (`id: 1, label: Swollen Lymph Node Concerns`): Describe any concerns you have about your swollen lymph nodes.
* **Patient Name** (`id: 2, label: Patient Name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your medical record or identification documents.
* **Medical History** (`id: 3, label: Medical History`): Provide any relevant medical history, including previous treatments or conditions that may be related to your swollen lymph nodes.
* **Current Medication** (`id: 4, label: Current Medication`): List any medications you are currently taking that may be relevant to your swollen lymph nodes.
* **Allergies** (`id: 5, label: Allergies`): List any allergies you have that may be related to your swollen lymph nodes.
* **Surgical History** (`id: 6, label: Surgical History`): Provide any recent or past surgical history that may be related to your swollen lymph nodes.
* **Symptoms** (`id: 7, label: Symptoms`): Describe any other symptoms you are experiencing, such as pain, itching, or burning.
* **Pain Level** (`id: 8, label: Pain Level`): Rate your pain level on a scale of 0-10, where 0 is no pain and 10 is severe pain.
* **Swelling Size** (`id: 9, label: Swelling Size`): Describe the size of your swollen lymph nodes.
* **Swelling Color** (`id: 10, label: Swelling Color`): Choose the color of your swollen lymph nodes from the provided options.
* **Swelling Shape** (`id: 11, label: Swelling Shape`): Choose the shape of your swollen lymph nodes from the provided options.
* **Swelling Texture** (`id: 12, label: Swelling Texture`): Choose the texture of your swollen lymph nodes from the provided options.
* **Fever** (`id: 13, label: Fever`): Indicate whether you have a fever.
* **Chills** (`id: 14, label: Chills`): Indicate whether you have chills.
* **Rash** (`id: 15, label: Rash`): Indicate whether you have a rash.
* **Swollen Lymph Nodes Location** (`id: 16, label: Swollen Lymph Nodes Location`): Describe the location of your swollen lymph nodes.
* **Swollen Lymph Nodes Duration** (`id: 17, label: Swollen Lymph Nodes Duration`): Estimate how long you have been experiencing swollen lymph nodes.
* **Swollen Lymph Nodes Recurring** (`id: 18, label: Swollen Lymph Nodes Recurring`): Indicate whether your swollen lymph nodes have recurred.
* **Swollen Lymph Nodes Pain** (`id: 19, label: Swollen Lymph Nodes Pain`): Indicate whether you experience pain with your swollen lymph nodes.
* **Swollen Lymph Nodes Redness** (`id: 20, label: Swollen Lymph Nodes Redness`): Indicate whether your swollen lymph nodes are red.
* **Swollen Lymph Nodes Itchiness** (`id: 21, label: Swollen Lymph Nodes Itchiness`): Indicate whether your swollen lymph nodes are itchy.
* **Swollen Lymph Nodes Burning** (`id: 22, label: Swollen Lymph Nodes Burning`): Indicate whether your swollen lymph nodes are burning.
* **Swollen Lymph Nodes Temperature** (`id: 23, label: Swollen Lymph Nodes Temperature`): Choose whether your swollen lymph nodes are cooler or warmer than normal.
* **Swollen Lymph Nodes Discoloration** (`id: 24, label: Swollen Lymph Nodes Discoloration`): Indicate whether your swollen lymph nodes have discoloration.
* **Swollen Lymph Nodes Bleeding** (`id: 25, label: Swollen Lymph Nodes Bleeding`): Indicate whether you experience bleeding with your swollen lymph nodes.
* **Swollen Lymph Nodes Other Symptoms** (`id: 26, label: Swollen Lymph Nodes Other Symptoms`): Describe any other symptoms you are experiencing with your swollen lymph nodes.
* **Swollen Lymph Nodes Other Info** (`id: 27, label: Swollen Lymph Nodes Other Info`): Provide any additional information about your swollen lymph nodes.

## Tips
- Answer each question carefully and to the best of your ability.
- If you are unsure about any question, ask a healthcare professional for guidance.
- This form is designed to help healthcare professionals assess your swollen lymph nodes.
