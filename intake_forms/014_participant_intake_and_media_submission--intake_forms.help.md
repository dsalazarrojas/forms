# Participant Intake And Media Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information and media submissions for data collection activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the participant's name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Enter the participant's email address in the "Participant Email" field. Please ensure that the email address is valid and in a format that can be easily recognized by email providers (e.g., `example@example.com`).
3. Upload any relevant media files, such as images or videos, in the "Upload" field.
4. Provide a description of the uploaded media file in the "Description" field.
5. Read and agree to the terms of the "Consent" field (this is a text field where you can type in your consent).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (Participant Name, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Participant Email** (Participant Email, email, optional): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Upload** (Upload, text, optional): Upload any relevant media files that support your participation. This could include images, videos, or other types of media files.
* **Description** (Description, text, optional): Provide a description of the media file you uploaded, including any relevant details about its context.
* **Consent** (Consent, note, optional): Review and agree to the terms of the consent, then type in your consent.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when providing your information and uploading media files.
* Use a valid email address that you can access and manage.
* Only upload files that are relevant to your participation.
* Provide a clear and concise description of the uploaded media file.
* Review and agree to the consent terms before submitting the form.
