# Permanent Makeup Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients about their personal details, medical history, and preferences for permanent makeup procedures. 

## How To Complete This Form
This form has multiple fields that require information about the client's contact details, medical history, and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Intake Form**: This field is the starting point for the form, and it's a text field where you can enter any information about yourself that you feel is relevant.
* **Client Information**: This field is where you can enter your name, date of birth, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Contact Information**: This field is where you can enter your address, phone number, and email address so that we can contact you for further questions or to confirm your appointment.
* **Medical Information**: In this field, please provide us with your medical history, including any allergies or conditions that may affect your treatment.
* **Medical History**: Please provide us with any medical history that you think is relevant to your treatment, such as past injuries or illnesses.
* **Permanent Makeup Preferences**: Here you can specify your preferences for your permanent makeup treatment, such as the design you want, colors, and any other details.
* **Payment Information**: This field is where you can enter your payment information, such as your credit card number and expiration date.
* **Billing Address**: Enter your billing address so that we can send you an invoice for your treatment.
* **Emergency Contact**: Please provide us with the contact information of an emergency contact person in case of any urgent situation.
* **Allergy**: If you have any allergies, please specify them here.
* **Medical Notes**: If you have any medical notes or previous medical conditions that we should know about, please provide them here.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out this form completely and accurately to ensure that we can provide you with the best possible service. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
