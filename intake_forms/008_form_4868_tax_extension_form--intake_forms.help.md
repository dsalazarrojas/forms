# Form 4868 Tax Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a tax extension for your business. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your request is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the applicable option for "Fill" to indicate your intention to request a tax extension.
2. Fill in your name as it appears on your official documents.
3. Enter your business name as it appears on your official documents.
4. Provide your business address.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Enter your email address.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Fill** (`form_4868_tax_extension_form`, select_multiple, required): This field indicates your intention to request a tax extension. Select 'Yes' to indicate that you would like to request an extension.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Business** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter your business name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Provide the address of your business.
* **Phone** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your business phone number.
* **Email** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your official email address.
* **Reason** (`reason_for_extension`, text, required): Please provide a clear and concise reason for your tax extension request.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date for which you are requesting the tax extension.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are accurately and completely filled out to avoid processing delays.
- If you have any issues or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.
