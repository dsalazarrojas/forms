# Aesthetic Surgery Clinic Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Aesthetic Surgery Clinic Intake Form is designed to collect essential information from patients before their consultation and treatment. This form helps our medical team understand your medical history, current health, and treatment goals, ensuring a safe and effective treatment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your personal information, including your first name, last name, date of birth, mobile phone number, email address, and home address.
2. If applicable, provide your emergency contact name and phone number.
3. Indicate how you heard about our clinic (e.g., Google Search, Social Media, Doctor Referral).
4. If you have any current medications or supplements, list them in the "Current Medications / Supplements" field.
5. If you have any drug allergies, list them in the "Drug Allergies" field.
6. Answer the questions about your smoking status and alcohol consumption habits.
7. If you have had any past surgeries, list them in the "Past Surgeries" field.
8. Describe your primary aesthetic concern and what you hope to achieve with treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text): Your given name.
* **Last Name** (text): Your surname.
* **Date of Birth** (date): Your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Mobile Phone** (text): Your mobile phone number.
* **Email Address** (email): Your email address.
* **Home Address** (text): Your home address.
* **Emergency Contact Name & Phone** (text): The name and phone number of a contact person we can reach in case of an emergency.
* **How did you hear about us?** (select_one): Choose how you heard about our clinic (e.g., Google Search, Social Media, Doctor Referral).
* **Current Medications / Supplements** (text): List any medications or supplements you are currently taking.
* **Drug Allergies** (text): List any allergies you have to specific medications or substances (e.g., Penicillin, Latex).
* **Do you smoke?** (select_one): Answer whether you currently smoke, occasionally smoke, or are a former smoker.
* **Alcohol Consumption** (select_one): Indicate how often you consume alcohol per week.
* **Past Surgeries** (text): List any previous surgeries, including dates.
* **What is your primary aesthetic concern?** (text): Describe your main area of concern (e.g., nose shape, wrinkles, body contouring).
* **What is your desired outcome?** (text): Describe your ideal result.

## Tips

* Be accurate and honest when filling out this form to ensure a safe and effective treatment process.
* If you are unsure about any information, do not hesitate to ask our medical team for guidance.
* Please do not fill out this form more than once; each time you visit our clinic, we will update your information accordingly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask our medical team for assistance.
