# Drug Delivery Research Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Drug Delivery Research Intake Form is designed to collect essential information about drug delivery research projects. This information will help the relevant teams to understand the project's requirements and plan accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the correct Research Type for your project. Choose between "Clinical" and "Non-Clinical" based on the nature of your project.
2. Choose the Study Status from "Active", "Inactive", or "Pending" based on the current status of your project.
3. Enter the name of your project in the Study Name field.
4. Enter the name of the sponsor or organization funding your project in the Sponsor Name field.
5. Enter the name of the lead sponsor or contact person responsible for your project in the Lead Sponsor field.
6. Enter the contact information of the person responsible for your project, including their email address in the Contact E-mail field and their phone number in the Contact Phone field.
7. Enter the location of your project in the Location (City, State) field.
8. Enter a brief description of your project in the Description field.
9. Enter the address of the project location in the Contact Address field.
10. Enter the project deadline in the format of "yyyy-mm-dd" in the Project Deadline field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research Type** (`Research Type`, select_one, required): Select the type of research project. This will help us understand the nature of your study.
* **Study Status** (`Study Status`, select_one, required): Choose the current status of your project. This will help us understand the current state of your project.
* **Study Name** (`Study Name`, text, required): Enter a unique name for your project.
* **Sponsor Name** (`Sponsor Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the organization or person funding your project.
* **Lead Sponsor** (`Lead Sponsor`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person or lead sponsor responsible for your project.
* **Contact Person** (`Contact Person`, text, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for your project.
* **Contact E-mail** (`Contact E-mail`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Location (City, State)** (`Location (City, State)`, text, required): Enter the location of your project, including the city and state.
* **Description** (`Description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your project.
* **Contact Address** (`Contact Address`, text, required): Enter the address of the project location.
* **Project Deadline** (`Project Deadline`, date, required): Enter the deadline of your project in the format of "yyyy-mm-dd". 

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please contact the relevant team for assistance.
* This form is for internal use only, and the collected information will be reviewed by authorized personnel to ensure its accuracy and completeness.
* Please note that this form is not intended for ongoing or previously submitted projects.
