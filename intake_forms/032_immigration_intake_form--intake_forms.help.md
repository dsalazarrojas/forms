# Immigration Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for clients to provide personal and immigration-related information to assist with the intake process. It is intended for use by immigration experts, lawyers, or officials to gather essential details for further evaluation and action.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the form, section by section, to ensure accuracy and clarity.
2. Enter the required information for each field, as indicated by the label.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the client as it appears on their official documents, such as passport or ID card.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the client's date of birth in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Citizenship Status** (`citizenship_status`, select_one, optional): Select the client's current citizenship status:
	+ US Citizen
	+ Permanent Resident
	+ Non-Resident Alien
* **Reason for Intake** (`reason_for_intake`, text, optional): Provide a brief explanation of the reason for the intake process.
* **Interview Details** (`interview_details`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about the interview, such as its purpose or location.
* **Immigration Status** (`immigration_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the client's current immigration status:
	+ Green Card
	+ Visa
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, date, optional): Enter the next action or appointment date for the client.
* **Interview Date** (`interview_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the client's next scheduled interview.
* **Interview Time** (`interview_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the client's next scheduled interview.

## Tips
- Double-check the form fields for accuracy and completeness before submission.
- If you are unsure about any field, please consult the relevant immigration expert or official.
- This form is designed to assist with the intake process, but it is not a substitute for professional advice or official documentation.
