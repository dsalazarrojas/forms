# Stem Cell Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stem Cell Intake Form is designed to collect comprehensive information about a patient's medical history, medical conditions, and social history to aid medical professionals in providing personalized care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
2. Ensure that all relevant information is provided.
3. Complete each section in the order it appears.
4. Answer all questions honestly and accurately.
5. Double-check your responses for errors.
