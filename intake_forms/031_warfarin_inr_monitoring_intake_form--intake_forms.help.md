# Warfarin INR Monitoring Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather essential information from patients on warfarin therapy to monitor their INR levels, adjust their medication dose as needed, and ensure safe treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the following steps:

1.  Enter your name and medical record number in the corresponding fields.
2.  Provide the date of birth to ensure we have an accurate representation of your health history.
3.  Enter the medications you are currently taking, including Warfarin.
4.  Report the result and date of your last INR test to enable our team to assess your current INR levels.
5.  Choose the target INR range that your healthcare provider has set for you, as this will guide adjustments to your medication.
6.  Enter the contact information for your healthcare provider and the clinic where you receive care.
7.  Schedule your next appointment or test date with us.
8.  Provide any additional information or comments about your medical history and treatment.

*   **Warfarin INR Monitoring Intake Form** (Required: False):
    *   Enter a brief description of the purpose of the form.
*   **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, Text, Required: False):
    *   Enter your full name.
