# Pet Medical Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect important information about a pet's medical condition and treatment. This information helps our veterinarians understand the pet's needs and provide the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Pet Medical Info** (pet_medical_info)
	* This field is where you should enter any existing medical conditions or concerns about your pet.
2. **Owner Info** (owner_info)
	* Please provide us with your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
3. **Contact Details** (contact_details)
	* Enter any additional contact information or notes that we should be aware of, such as emergency contact numbers or special instructions.
4. **Treatment Consent** (treatment_consent)
	* Please select "Yes" if your pet requires treatment, and "No" if your pet does not.
5. **Owner Notes** (owner_notes)
	* Share any relevant information about your pet's behavior, habits, or medical history that may help us provide the best care.
6. **Veterinary Notes** (veterinary_notes)
	* Enter any relevant information about your pet's medical history or previous treatments.
7. **Medical History** (medical_history)
	* Select "Yes" if your pet has a medical history, and "No" if not.
8. **Allergies** (allergies)
	* Select "Yes" if your pet has any allergies, and "No" if not.
9. **Medications** (medication)
	* Enter any medications your pet is currently taking, including dosage and frequency.
10. **Medical Conditions** (medical_conditions)
	* Select "Yes" if your pet has any medical conditions, and "No" if not.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have answered all required fields truthfully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask a veterinarian for guidance.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
