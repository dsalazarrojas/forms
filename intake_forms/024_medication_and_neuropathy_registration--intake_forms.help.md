# Medication And Neuropathy Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who are experiencing neuropathy symptoms and wish to report their medication usage. The form will help us better understand their condition and medication regimen, which will aid in providing more effective treatment and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your address in the text field.
2. Enter your name in the text field.
3. Enter your age in the number field. This is a required field.
4. Select your current medication from the dropdown list. You can choose one or more from the options provided.
5. If necessary, enter your symptoms in the note field. This is optional.
6. Enter your email address in the email field. This is a required field.
7. Enter your phone number in the text field. This is a required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Address** (`Address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address where you would like to be contacted.
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full name.
* **Age** (`Age`, `number`, required: true): Enter your age.
* **Medication** (`Medication`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the medication you are currently taking.
	+ Options: Medication 1, Medication 2, Medication 3.
* **Symptom** (`Symptom`, `note`, required: false): Enter any symptoms you are experiencing.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: true): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: true): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
