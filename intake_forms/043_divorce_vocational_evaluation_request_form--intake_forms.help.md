# Divorce Vocational Evaluation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Divorce Vocational Evaluation Request Form is designed to gather information about your financial situation and employment status in preparation for a divorce. This form is a requirement for the divorce process and will help the court and your attorney understand your financial circumstances.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the first name field.
2. Enter your last name in the last name field.
3. Enter your email address in the email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the phone field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the date of birth field in the format MM/DD/YY.
6. Enter the date of your marriage in the date of marriage field in the format MM/DD/YY.
7. Enter your number of dependents.
8. Select your employment status from the employment status field (Employed, Unemployed, Retired).
9. Enter your job title in the job title field.
10. Enter your income in the income field.
11. Enter the time of divorce in the time of divorce field.
12. Enter the date of separation in the date of separation field in the format MM/DD/YY.
13. Select your reason for divorce from the reason for divorce field (Irreconcilable differences, Abuse, Other).
14. Enter any additional assets in the assets field.
15. Enter any liabilities in the liabilities field.
16. Enter income for non-earner spouse in the income for non-earner field.
17. Enter income for non-earner spouse percentage in the income for non-earner percentage field.
18. Select employment status for non-earner spouse from the employment status for non-earner field (Employed, Unemployed, Retired).
19. Enter job title for non-earner spouse in the job title for non-earner field.
20. Enter assets for non-earner spouse in the assets for non-earner field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Date of marriage** (`date_of_marriage`, date, required): Enter the date of your marriage in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Reason for divorce** (`reason_for_divorce`, select_multiple, optional): Select the reason for your divorce (Irreconcilable differences, Abuse, Other).
* **Number of dependents** (`number_of_dependents`, number, required): Enter the number of dependents you have.
* **Employment status** (`employment_status`, select_one, optional): Select your employment status (Employed, Unemployed, Retired).
* **Job title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Income** (`income`, number, required): Enter your income.
* **Time of divorce** (`time_of_divorce`, time, required): Enter the time of divorce in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Date of separation** (`date_of_separation`, date, required): Enter the date of separation in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Income earner** (`income_earner`, text, optional): Enter income for the earner spouse.
* **Income non-earner** (`income_non_earner`, text, optional): Enter income for the non-earner spouse.
* **Income non-earner percentage** (`income_non_earner_percentage`, number, optional): Enter the percentage of income for the non-earner spouse.
* **Employment status earner** (`employment_status_earner`, select_one, optional): Select the employment status for the earner spouse (Employed, Unemployed, Retired).
* **Employment status non-earner** (`employment_status_non_earner`, select_one, optional): Select the employment status for the non-earner spouse (Employed, Unemployed, Retired).
* **Job title earner** (`job_title_earner`, text, optional): Enter the job title for the earner spouse.
* **Job title non-earner** (`job_title_non_earner`, text, optional): Enter the job title for the non-earner spouse.
* **Assets earner** (`assets_earner`, text, optional): Enter assets for the earner spouse.
* **Assets non-earner** (`assets_non_earner`, text, optional): Enter assets for the non-earner spouse.
