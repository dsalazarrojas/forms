# resource_navigation_guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users to provide personalized guidance on navigating resources.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the program name, which should be a brief description of the program or service you are seeking guidance on.
2. Enter your contact details, including your email address and phone number, so that we can get in touch with you for further support.
3. Select the required service from the list provided, as this will help us tailor our guidance to your specific needs.
4. Enter the date you would like to access the resource (prophets_date) and, if applicable, the date for any additional dates you would like to access (prophets_date_2).
5. Provide any additional comments or notes in the text box provided under "notes".
6. If none of the provided services match your needs, please select "other" and describe the service you require in the field "service_needed_other".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program name** (program_name): A brief description of the program or service you are seeking guidance on.
* **Contact details** (contact_details): Enter your email address and phone number, so we can get in touch with you for further support.
* **Required Service** (service_needed): Select the service that best matches your needs from the provided options.
* **Date** (date_prophets_date): Enter the date you would like to access the resource, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Contact Phone** (contact_phone): Enter your phone number.
* **Notes** (notes): Provide any additional comments or notes you would like to share with us.
* **Other Service Needed** (service_needed_other): If none of the provided services match your needs, select "other" and describe the service you require.
* **Other Service Details** (service_needed_other): Describe the service you require if none of the provided options match your needs.
* **Prophets Date 2** (prophets_date_2): If you need guidance on multiple dates, enter the second date you would like to access.
* **Contact Phone 2** (contact_phone_2): Enter your alternative phone number.

## Tips
- Please ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and correctly.
- If you need guidance on multiple services or dates, please provide all relevant information in the provided fields.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
