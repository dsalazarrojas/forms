# Microdose Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants in a microdose program, allowing for efficient registration and data collection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the Participant Details section with your name, date of birth, and any other relevant details.
2. Provide your health information in the Health Information section.
3. Specify your intention(s) in the Intention section.
4. Choose your consent status in the Consent section.
5. Enter your microdose registration information in the Microdose Registations section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Details (participant_details)
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, required): Enter your name, date of birth, and any other relevant details to identify you as a participant in the microdose program.

### Health Information (health_info)
* **Health Information** (`health_info`, optional): Provide any health-related information that may be relevant to your participation in the microdose program.

### Intention (intentions)
* **Intention** (`intentions`, optional): Specify your intention(s) for registering in the microdose program, such as treatment, prevention, or education.

### Consent (consent)
* **Consent** (`consent`, optional): Choose whether you consent to participate in the microdose program by selecting "Yes" or "No".

### Microdose Registations (microdose_registrations)
* **Microdose Registations** (`microdose_registrations`, optional): Enter your microdose registration information, such as date, time, and location of registration.

## Tips
* Make sure to review your information carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the microdose program administrators.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification before proceeding.
