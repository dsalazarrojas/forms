# Acanthamoeba Infection Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients about their contact lens use, symptoms, and exposure to potential sources of infection. The information gathered will help healthcare professionals assess the risk of Acanthamoeba infection and identify potential risk factors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2. Select the appropriate response for each question.
3. Ensure you provide your full name and correct email and phone number for assessment follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Identification --** (`section_patient_id`): This section is for patient identification and is not required.
* **Full Name** (`patient_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID. (Required)
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address for assessment follow-up. (Required)
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`): Enter your primary phone number for assessment follow-up. (Required)
* **Symptom History --** (`section_symptoms`): This section is for describing your symptom history and is not required.
* **When did your symptoms first appear?** (`symptoms_start_date`): Enter the approximate date when your symptoms first appeared. (Required)
* **Select all current eye symptoms** (`current_symptoms`): Choose all the symptoms you are currently experiencing:
	+ Redness
	+ Pain
	+ Blurred Vision
	+ Light Sensitivity (Photophobia)
	+ Excessive Tearing
	+ Sensation of something in the eye (Required)
* **Rate your eye pain intensity 1-10** (`pain_intensity`): Rate your eye pain intensity from 1 (minimal) to 10 (unbearable). (Required)
* **Contact Lens History --** (`section_lens_history`): This section is for describing your contact lens history and is not required.
* **Do you wear contact lenses?** (`wear_contact_lenses`): Answer yes or no to whether you wear contact lenses. (Required)
* **If yes, what type of lenses?** (`lens_type`): If you wear contact lenses, select the type you use. (Not required)
* **Do you ever rinse lenses with tap water?** (`hygiene_practices`): Answer yes or no to whether you rinse your lenses with tap water. (Not required)
* **Do you swim or shower while wearing lenses?** (`swim_with_lenses`): Answer yes or no to whether you swim or shower while wearing lenses. (Not required)
* **Environmental Exposure --** (`section_exposure`): This section is for describing your environmental exposure and is not required.
* **Recent exposure to** (`water_exposure`): Check all the places you were recently exposed to water: 
	+ Hot Tubs
	+ Lakes or Rivers
	+ Wells
	+ Public Swimming Pools (Not required)
* **Previous Treatment --** (`section_treatment`): This section is for describing your previous treatment and is not required.
* **List any eye drops or medications you are currently using** (`current_medications`): List all eye drops or medications you are currently using. (Not required)
* **Urgent Notice --** (`section_urgent`): This section is for acknowledging that this assessment is not a substitute for an emergency clinical exam and is required.
* **I understand this assessment is not a substitute for an emergency clinical exam** (`emergency_acknowledgement`): Acknowledge that this assessment is not a substitute for an emergency clinical exam. (Required)

## Tips
- Please answer all questions truthfully to ensure accurate assessment.
- If you have any concerns or questions, contact your healthcare professional.
