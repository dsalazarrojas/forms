# Daily Food Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Food Intake Form is designed to collect information about an individual's daily food intake habits.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the items you consumed from the list of available options.
2. Enter the quantity of each item you consumed.
3. Choose how many calories you burned through your daily activities.
4. Indicate whether you had sufficient water intake today.
5. Select the frequency of your food consumption for the items listed.
6. Provide any additional notes about your food intake.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Food Items** (<name> `food_items`, <type> `select_multiple`, required/optional: optional): Select all the items you consumed today from the available options.
* **Quantity** (<name> `quantity`, <type> `number`, required/optional: optional): Enter the quantity of each item you consumed.
* **Calories Burned** (<name> `calories_burned`, <type> `number`, required/optional: optional): Estimate how many calories you burned today through your daily activities.
* **Water Intake** (<name> `water_intake`, <type> `select_one`, required/optional: optional): Indicate whether you had sufficient water intake today.
* **Food Frequency** (<name> `food_frequency`, <type> `select_one`, required/optional: optional): Choose the frequency of your food consumption for each item.
* **Notes** (<name> `notes`, <type> `text`, required/optional: optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about your food intake today.
