# Muscle Building Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Muscle Building Intake Form is designed to collect necessary information from clients about their muscle building goals and training details. This form helps our team understand the client's expectations and preferences, allowing us to provide tailored services and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your client details on page 1.
2. Indicate your goals for muscle building on page 2.
3. Specify your training goals on page 3.
4. Share your program details on page 4.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details** (`client_details`, text, required: false): 
Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details you'd like to share with us.
* **Goals** (`goals`, select_multiple, required: false): 
Choose all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Training Goals** (`training_goals`, select_one, required: false): 
Choose one:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Program Details** (`program_details`, text, required: false): 
Describe your current workout routine, diet, and any other relevant information about your muscle building program.

## Tips
Please fill out the form carefully, as your input will help us provide the best possible service for you. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask!
