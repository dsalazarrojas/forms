# Vehicle Maintenance Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Maintenance Intake Form is a tool for customers or users to report any issues or concerns they have with their vehicle's condition, such as maintenance requests or problems they have encountered while driving.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide **Vehicle Details** information, including any relevant details about your vehicle.
2.  Describe the **Vehicle Status**, including any issues or concerns you have with your vehicle's condition.
3.  Specify the **Service Request**, if you have any maintenance requests or concerns.
4.  Report the **Vehicle Condition**, including any notable characteristics or issues with your vehicle.
5.  Click **Submit** to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Vehicle Details**: Provide any relevant details about your vehicle. This could include the make, model, year, mileage, or any other information you think would be helpful. This field is not required, so feel free to enter as much or as little information as you like.
*   **Vehicle Status**: Describe the current status of your vehicle, including any issues or concerns you have. This could include problems with the engine, transmission, or any other notable characteristics that might be relevant to the form's purpose.
*   **Service Request**: If you have any maintenance requests or concerns, specify them here. This could include things like oil changes, tire rotations, or other service needs.
*   **Vehicle Condition**: Report the current condition of your vehicle, including any notable issues or characteristics. This could include anything from minor issues like a small dent to more serious problems like engine failure.
*   **Submit**: Once you have filled out all the necessary fields, click **Submit** to send your information to the relevant parties for action.
