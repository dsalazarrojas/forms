# urgent_care_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The urgent_care_intake_form is designed to collect basic information from patients in a medical setting. This form is used to gather contact and insurance details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and other patient information in the "patient_info" field.
2. Provide any relevant medical history in the "medical_history" field.
3. Enter your insurance information in the "insurance_info" field.
4. Enter your contact information in the "contact_info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required/optional): Describe any past medical conditions or treatments that may be relevant to your current visit.
* **Insurance Info** (`insurance_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your insurance provider and policy number for billing purposes.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your address, phone number, and any other contact information for follow-up.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your healthcare provider for assistance.
* This form is for your safety and convenience, so please fill it out as completely as possible.
