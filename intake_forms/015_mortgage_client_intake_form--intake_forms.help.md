# Mortgage Client Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from mortgage clients for loan applications. It is intended to be completed by the client and submitted to the lender for review.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Review and understand each field before providing information.
3. Sign and date the form as required by the client and lender.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mortgage Client Intake Form Title**: Enter a brief title for the mortgage client intake form, e.g., "John Smith - Loan Application".
* **Loan Amount**: Enter the amount of the loan being applied for.
* **Loan Term**: Enter the term of the loan in months.
* **Occupation**: Enter your occupation or type of work.
* **Income**: Enter your monthly income.
* **Property Type**: Enter the type of property being used as collateral, e.g., house, condo, etc.
* **Property Value**: Enter the value of the property being used as collateral.
* **Property Location**: Enter the location of the property being used as collateral.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your name, email, phone number, and address.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the loan application.
* **Loan Status**: Enter the current status of the loan application, e.g., pending, approved, denied.
* **Client Signature**: Sign and date your signature as the mortgage client.
* **Lender Signature**: Sign and date your signature as the lender.
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date and time the client and lender signed the form.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **City**: Enter the city where you live.
* **State**: Enter the state where you live.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the loan application.
* **Loan Approved Status**: Enter the status of the loan approval, e.g., approved, denied.
* **Lender Comments**: Enter any comments from the lender about the loan application.
* **Date Created**: The date the form was created.
* **Lender Comments**: Enter any comments from the lender about the loan application.

Please make sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly, and if you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant department or team for assistance.
