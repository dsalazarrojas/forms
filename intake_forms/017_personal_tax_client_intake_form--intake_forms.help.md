# Personal Tax Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal details from tax clients, including contact information and emergency contacts. It will be used to provide personalized advice or representation for tax planning and consulting services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to enter the correct type of contact information (e.g., email, phone, address).
3. For emergency contacts, ensure you have their permission to collect their information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First name (`1`, `text`, required: false)
- Enter your first name.

### Last name (`2`, `text`, required: false)
- Enter your last name.

### Email (`3`, `email`, required: false)
- Enter your work or personal email address.

### Phone (`4`, `text`, required: false)
- Enter your phone number.

### Social Security Number (`5`, `text`, required: false)
- Enter your Social Security number.

### Occupation (`6`, `text`, required: false)
- Enter your profession or job title.

### Employer name (`7`, `text`, required: false)
- Enter the name of your current or past employer.

### Employer address (`8`, `text`, required: false)
- Enter the address of your current or past employer.

### Client notes (`9`, `note`, required: false)
- Add any additional information about yourself that may be helpful for our services.

### Date of birth (`10`, `date`, required: false)
- Enter your date of birth.

### Years employed (`11`, `text`, required: false)
- Enter the number of years you have been employed.

### Income earned 2021 (`12`, `number`, required: false)
- Enter your income for the year 2021.

### Dependents (`13`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
- Select any dependents who may be affected by your tax situation.

### Address (`14`, `text`, required: false)
- Enter your residential or mailing address.

### City (`15`, `text`, required: false)
- Enter the city where you live.

### State (`16`, `text`, required: false)
- Enter the state where you live.

### Zip (`17`, `text`, required: false)
- Enter your zip code.

### County (`18`, `text`, required: false)
- Enter the county where you live.

### Country (`19`, `text`, required: false)
- Enter the country where you live.

### Emergency contact name (`20`, `text`, required: false)
- Enter the name of the emergency contact person.

### Emergency contact relationship (`21`, `select_one`, required: false)
- Select the relationship of the emergency contact person (e.g., Mother, Father, Brother, Sister, Other).

### Emergency contact phone (`22`, `text`, required: false)
- Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.

### Emergency contact address (`23`, `text`, required: false)
- Enter the emergency contact person's address.

### Emergency contact date of birth (`24`, `date`, required: false)
- Enter the emergency contact person's date of birth.

### Emergency contact social security number (`25`, `text`, required: false)
- Enter the emergency contact person's Social Security number.

Tips:
- Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
