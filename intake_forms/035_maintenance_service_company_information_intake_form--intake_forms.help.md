# Maintenance Service Company Information Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended to help you complete the Maintenance Service Company Information Intake Form. This form is designed to collect information about your company, including its contact details, services offered, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the company information section (fields 1-5).
2. Provide contact information (fields 6-7).
3. Answer questions about services offered and payment terms (fields 8-9).
4. Select relevant certifications and licenses (fields 10-13).
5. Answer questions about safety records and equipment inventory (fields 14-15).
6. Provide emergency contact information (field 16).
7. Select key personnel (fields 17-23).
8. Fill out key personnel details (fields 24-25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Company Information (maintenance_service_company_info)
* **Company Name** (`title`): Enter your company's name.
* **Company Information** (`maintenance_service_company_info`): Describe your company's information.

### 2. Contact Information (contact_info)
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your company's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your company's email address.

### 3. Contact Information
* **Website** (`website`): Enter your company's website URL.

### 4. Services Offered
* **Services Offered** (`services_offered`): Select the services your company offers.

### 5. Payment Terms
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`): Describe your company's payment terms.

### 6. Services Offered (Yes/No)
* **Yes/No** (`services_offered`): Select 'Yes' if you offer this service, 'No' otherwise.

### 7. Payment Terms (Yes/No)
* **Yes/No** (`work_order_process`): Select 'Yes' if you offer payment on work orders, 'No' otherwise.

### 8. Certifications
* **Certifications** (`certifications`): Select certifications your company holds.

### 9. Licenses
* **Licenses** (`licenses`): Select licenses your company holds.

### 10. Safety Record
* **Safety Record** (`safety_record`): Select 'Yes' if you have a good safety record, 'No' otherwise.

### 11. Equipment Inventory
* **Equipment Inventory** (`equipment_inventory`): Select 'Yes' if you have an equipment inventory, 'No' otherwise.

### 12. Insurance Policies
* **Insurance Policies** (`insurance_policies`): Select 'Yes' if you have insurance policies, 'No' otherwise.

### 13. Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): Select 'Yes' if you have an emergency contact, 'No' otherwise.

### 14. Key Personnel
* **Key Personnel** (`key_personnel`): Select key personnel.
* **Title** (`key_personnel_title`): Enter key personnel title.
* **Relationship** (`key_personnel_relationship`): Enter key personnel relationship.
* **Email** (`key_personnel_email`): Enter key personnel email.
* **Phone** (`key_personnel_phone`): Enter key personnel phone number.
* **Address** (`key_personnel_address`): Enter key personnel address.

### 15. Additional Key Personnel (if necessary)
* **Key Personnel 2 Title** (`key_personnel_title_2`): Enter key personnel 2 title.
* **Key Personnel 2 Relationship** (`key_personnel_relationship_2`): Enter key personnel 2 relationship.
* **Key Personnel 2 Email** (`key_personnel_email_2`): Enter key personnel 2 email.
* **Key Personnel 2 Phone** (`key_personnel_phone_2`): Enter key personnel 2 phone number.
* **Key Personnel 2 Address** (`key_personnel_address_2`): Enter key personnel 2 address.

## Tips
* Double-check your answers for accuracy.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the relevant department for assistance.
