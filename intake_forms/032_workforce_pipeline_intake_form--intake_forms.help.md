# Workforce Pipeline Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workforce Pipeline Intake Form is designed to collect information about your work experience, availability, and other relevant details that can be used in a workforce pipeline process. This form will help us understand your skills, work type, and availability, which will assist us in finding the best fit for you in our pipeline.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide a link to your resume, which should include a brief overview of your work experience.
3. Select your relevant work experience (1-2 years, 2-5 years, 5-10 years, or 10+ years).
4. If you have experience that doesn't fit into the provided options, please describe it in the "Other Work Experience" field.
5. Select your available date, if applicable.
6. Choose your preferred contact method (Phone, Email, Text, or Video call).
7. Provide your contact information.
8. Select your work type (Full-time, Part-time, Freelance, Internship, Volunteer, or Contract).
9. Choose your work status (Available, Available in 1 week, Available in 2 weeks, or Not Available).
10. Indicate your language proficiency.
11. If you have other relevant work experience, please describe it in the "Other Work Experience" field.
12. Select your education level (High School, Associate, Bachelor, Master, Ph.D., or Other).
13. List your relevant skills.
14. Choose your availability (Flexible or Fixed).
15. If you have specific days and hours available, please enter them in the corresponding fields.
