# Neuromuscular Massager Therapy Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from clients before their session with a neuromuscular massager therapist. The data collected will help the therapist understand the client's medical history, treatment goals, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the first page with your personal information, including your first name, last name, date of birth, and contact details.
2. Provide information about any medications or allergies you are currently taking.
3. Share details about any chronic conditions or medical conditions you have.
4. Indicate your current pain level and location.
5. Specify your treatment goals for the sessions.
6. Share your client history and any relevant information about your health.
7. Provide emergency contact information, including the name, phone number, and email of a contact person.
