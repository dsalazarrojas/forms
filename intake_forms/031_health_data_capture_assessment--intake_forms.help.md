# Health Data Capture Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Data Capture Assessment form is a tool used to collect information on a user's health, medical history, and related topics. This form helps healthcare professionals gather essential data for accurate diagnosis, treatment, and care planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your general information, including name and contact details.
2. Share your medical history, including any previous illnesses, conditions, or allergies.
3. Describe your lifestyle habits, such as dietary preferences or exercise routines.
4. Give consent for the collection of your health data.
5. Provide detailed information on your health status, including symptoms, treatments, and diagnoses.
6. Report on any social determinants that may affect your health.
7. List any emergency contacts that should be notified in case of an emergency.
8. Mention any medications you are currently taking.
9. Specify any allergies you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (text, required/optional): This section is for you to provide your basic details, such as your name, contact number, or email address.
* **Medical History** (text, required/optional): Share any previous illnesses, conditions, or allergies you may have.
* **Lifestyle Insights** (text, required/optional): Describe your daily habits, such as your diet, exercise routine, or smoking status.
* **Consent** (text, required/optional): Grant consent for the collection and use of your health data.
* **Health Details** (text, required/optional): Provide detailed information on your current health status, including symptoms, treatments, or diagnoses.
* **Social Determinants** (text, required/optional): Share any social factors that may affect your health, such as living situation or economic status.
* **Contact Information** (text, required/optional): Enter your contact details, including phone numbers and emergency contact names.
* **Emergency Contacts** (text, required/optional): List any emergency contacts that should be notified in case of an emergency.
* **Medications** (select multiple, required/optional): Check any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies** (select one, required/optional): Specify any allergies you may have.

## Tips
* Please ensure you complete all required fields to provide accurate and comprehensive information.
* If a field is not applicable, please select "None" or leave it blank.
* Be honest and thorough in your responses to ensure accurate data collection.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please consult a healthcare professional before proceeding.
