# Healthcare Necessities Analysis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's healthcare necessities. It will help healthcare providers understand the patient's medical history, allergies, and other relevant information to provide better care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the patient's name in the "patient name" field.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "date of birth" field in YYYY-MM-DD format.
3. Enter the patient's ID number in the "patient id" field.
4. If applicable, enter the patient's medical condition in the "medical condition" field.
5. Select "Yes" or "No" for the medications the patient is taking in the "medication list" field.
6. Enter any allergies the patient has in the "allergies" field.
7. Enter the patient's medical provider's name in the "medical provider" field.
8. Select whether the patient has a medical specialty in the "medical specialty" field.
9. Select "Yes" or "No" for the patient's medical history in the "medical history" field.
10. Select the patient's contact method in the "contact method" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient name (1)**: Enter the patient's full name in this field.
* **date of birth (2)**: Enter the patient's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1990-02-20).
* **patient id (3)**: Enter the patient's unique ID number.
* **medical condition (4)**: Enter any medical conditions the patient has (e.g., diabetes, heart failure).
* **medication list (5)**: Select "Yes" for each medication the patient is taking.
* **allergies (6)**: Enter any allergies the patient has (e.g., penicillin, latex).
* **medical provider (7)**: Enter the patient's medical provider's name (e.g., Dr. Smith).
* **medical specialty (8)**: Select "Yes" if the patient has a medical specialty.
* **medical history (9)**: Select "Yes" for each medical history the patient has (e.g., heart attack, surgery).
* **contact method (10)**: Select the method of contact the patient prefers (e.g., phone, email).
