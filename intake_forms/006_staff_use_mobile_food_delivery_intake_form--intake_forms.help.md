# Staff Use Mobile Food Delivery Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for staff members to record information about a mobile food delivery order, including the order date, customer details, and delivery details. This form helps staff to efficiently track and manage food delivery orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date when the order was placed.
2. Enter the name of the customer.
3. Enter the customer's phone number.
4. Enter the order number.
5. Enter the total cost of the order.
6. Enter the delivery address.
7. Select the delivery date and time.
8. Provide any additional comments about the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, **Required**): This field is used to record the date when the mobile food delivery order was placed. Please select the correct date from the calendar picker.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, **Required**): Enter the name of the customer who placed the order.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, **Required**): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Order Number** (`order_number`, number, **Required**): Enter the order number assigned to the delivery.
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, number, **Required**): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, **Required**): Enter the delivery address.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, **Required**): Select the date for delivery.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, **Optional**): Add any additional comments about the order, if necessary.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate information for all fields to ensure efficient processing of the order.
* If the customer's phone number is not provided, please try to obtain it from the customer or their representative.
* If you're unsure about any information, please contact the relevant department or team for assistance.
