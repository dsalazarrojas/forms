# Eligibility Meeting Sign In - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from attendees for an eligibility meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Ensure all fields are accurate and complete.
3. Submit the form to schedule your eligibility meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### School
*   **School** (`school`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the school you're attending.
*   **First** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
*   **Last** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
*   **Contact** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number.
*   **Attendee Reason** (`reason_for_attendee`, `select_one`, required): Select the reason for attending the eligibility meeting.
*   **Additional Reason** (`reason_for_attendee_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any additional reasons for attending the meeting.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (DDMM/YYYY).
*   **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the meeting.
*   **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the meeting.
*   **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the meeting.
*   **Meeting Duration** (`meeting_duration`, `number`, optional): Enter the duration of the meeting in hours.
*   **Attendee Type** (`attendee_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of attendee you are (School, Agency, Non-Profit, or HR Team).
*   **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the school you're associated with.
*   **Agency** (`agency_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the agency you're associated with.
*   **Organization** (`organization_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the organization you're representing.
*   **Department** (`department_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the department you're from.
*   **Role** (`role`, `text`, optional): Enter your role within the organization.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): Enter your job title.
*   **Job Title 2** (`job_title_2`, `text`, optional): Enter another job title.
*   **Job Title 3** (`job_title_3`, `text`, optional): Enter another job title.
*   **Job Title 4** (`job_title_4`, `text`, optional): Enter another job title.
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
*   **Department** (`department`, `text`, optional): Enter the department you're from.
*   **Job Title 5** (`job_title_5`, `text`, optional): Enter another job title.

## Tips
*   Make sure to double-check the information before submitting the form.
*   If you're unsure about any field, contact the meeting coordinator for assistance.
*   Ensure you're filling out the form accurately and completely to avoid delays in the meeting process.
