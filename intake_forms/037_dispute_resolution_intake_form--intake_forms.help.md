# Dispute Resolution Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dispute Resolution Intake Form is designed to collect information from parties involved in a dispute to facilitate a smooth and efficient resolution process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the purpose and the information required.
2. Complete each field according to your relationship with the other party involved in the dispute.
3. Answer all required fields (indicated by a *) to ensure that your submission is complete.
4. If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities or seek advice from a qualified professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dispute Resolution Party One Info**: Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you are the party involved in this dispute.
* **Dispute Resolution Party One Name**: Enter the name of the party involved in this dispute.
* **Dispute Resolution Party One Address**: Enter the address of the party involved in this dispute.
* **Dispute Resolution Party One Email**: Enter the email address of the party involved in this dispute.
* **Dispute Resolution Party One Phone**: Enter the phone number of the party involved in this dispute.
* **Dispute Resolution Party One Relationship**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the relationship between you and the other party involved in the dispute.
* **Dispute Resolution Party Two Info**: Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the other party is involved in this dispute.
* **Dispute Resolution Party Two Name**: Enter the name of the other party involved in the dispute.
* **Dispute Resolution Party Two Address**: Enter the address of the other party involved in the dispute.
* **Dispute Resolution Party Two Email**: Enter the email address of the other party involved in the dispute.
* **Dispute Resolution Party Two Phone**: Enter the phone number of the other party involved in the dispute.
* **Dispute Resolution Party Two Relationship**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the relationship between you and the other party involved in the dispute.
* **Dispute Resolution Party Info**: Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether all parties have been involved in this dispute.
* **Dispute Resolution Party Name**: Enter the name of all parties involved in this dispute.
* **Dispute Resolution Address**: Enter the address of all parties involved in this dispute.
* **Dispute Resolution Email**: Enter the email address of all parties involved in this dispute.
* **Dispute Resolution Phone**: Enter the phone number of all parties involved in this dispute.
* **Dispute Resolution Relationship**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the relationship between all parties involved in this dispute.
* **Additional Info**: Provide any additional information that may be helpful in resolving the dispute.
* **Supporting Documents**: Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have any supporting documents related to the dispute.
* **Dispute Resolution Date**: Enter the date of the dispute resolution.
* **Dispute Resolution Time**: Enter the time of the dispute resolution.
* **Dispute Resolution Comments**: Provide any comments or notes related to the dispute resolution.
* **Dispute Resolution Other**: Provide any other relevant information related to the dispute resolution.

Note: This guide is for your reference only, and it is recommended to review the form carefully before filling it out.
