# Medical Patient Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect medical history and contact details of patients to help healthcare professionals assess and provide adequate care.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form accurately:
* Review the form questions carefully and answer them truthfully.
* For medical history, include as much detail as possible.
* For contact information, ensure accuracy and up-to-date information.
* For date fields (Medical History Date and Birth Date), use the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical History**: This is a free-text field for patients to describe their medical history, including any chronic illnesses, allergies, or previous medical conditions.
* **Medical History Date**: This field is for recording the date of the patient's medical history, if available.
* **Medical History Note**: This is a free-text field for healthcare professionals to note any additional information about the patient's medical history.
* **E-Signature**: This field is for the patient's electronic signature, confirming that they have reviewed and agreed to the information provided.
* **Contact Info**: Select one or more options to indicate the patient's preferred contact methods (e.g., phone, email, address).
* **Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Email**: Enter the patient's email address.
* **Address**: Enter the patient's address.
* **Birth Date**: This field is for recording the patient's birth date.
* **Medical History Additional Info**: This is another free-text field for patients to provide any additional information about their medical history.
* **E-Signature 1**: This field is for a second electronic signature from the patient, ensuring they have reviewed and agreed to the information provided.
 
## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your healthcare professional or our support team.
