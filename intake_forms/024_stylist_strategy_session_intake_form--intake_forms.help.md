# Stylist Strategy Session Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help stylists and consultants gather information about a client's goals and preferences for their strategy session. The purpose of this form is to ensure that both parties are on the same page before the session begins.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have a clear understanding of the client's goals and preferences.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as required.
3. If you're unsure about any field, ask your client for clarification.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Session Goals** (<label>Session Goals, <type>text, required/optional)
* This field is used to collect the client's goals for the session.

### 2. **Personal Goals** (<label>Personal Goals, <type>text, required/optional)
* This field is used to capture the client's personal goals.

### 3. **Preferred Outcome** (<label>Preferred Outcome, <type>select_multiple, required/optional)
* Select the preferred outcome(s) for the session from the dropdown options.

### 4. **Stylist Goals** (<label>Stylist Goals, <type>select_multiple, required/optional)
* Select the goals of the stylist from the dropdown options.

### 5. **Context** (<label>Context, <type>text, required/optional)
* Provide any relevant context that may affect the session.

### 6. **Consultant Goals** (<label>Consultant Goals, <type>select_one, required/optional)
* Select the goal of the consultant from the dropdown options.

### 7. **Consultant Preferences** (<label>Consultant Preferences, <type>text, required/optional)
* Share any preferences or requirements you have as a consultant.

### 8. **Stylist Preferences** (<label>Stylist Preferences, <type>text, required/optional)
* Share any preferences or requirements you have as a stylist.

### 9. **Pre-Session Questions** (<label>Pre-Session Questions, <type>text, required/optional)
* Ask any questions you have before the session.

### 10. **Pre-Session Goals** (<label>Pre-Session Goals, <type>text, required/optional)
* Set any goals or expectations you have before the session.

Note: The required fields are marked as required in the field-by-field explanation. Please ensure you complete these fields for a successful and comprehensive session.
