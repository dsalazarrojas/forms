# Patient Symptom Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Symptom Intake Form is a tool used by healthcare professionals to collect information about patients' symptoms and medical history. This form helps healthcare professionals understand the patient's condition and provide accurate diagnoses and treatment plans.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Read the form carefully**: Before starting, please make sure you have all the required information and materials, such as a pen or keyboard and a stable internet connection.
- **Answer all fields**: Complete all the fields as accurately as possible, even if you're not sure about something.
- **Select all relevant symptoms**: If you're experiencing multiple symptoms, select all that apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1 - Patient Information
- **Patient Information** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): This is the title of the form, and it cannot be edited. You can read it to understand what this form is for.
- **Patient Information** (page_1, text, required/optional): This is a free text field where you can enter your name, date of birth, or any other relevant information about yourself. Please enter your name, date of birth, and any other relevant details.

### Page 2 - Symptoms
- **Symptoms** (page_2, select_multiple, required/optional): This page lists various symptoms. Please select all the symptoms you are experiencing. The options include:
  - **Fever**: A high body temperature, usually above 100.4°F (38°C).
  - **Headache**: Pain or discomfort in the head.
  - **Nausea**: Feeling sick to your stomach.
  - **Fatigue**: Feeling tired or lacking energy.
  - **Joint pain**: Pain in your joints.
  - **Muscle pain**: Pain in your muscles.
  - **Shortness of breath**: Feeling like you can't catch your breath.
  - **Cough**: A dry or phlegmy cough.
  - **Sore throat**: Pain or irritation in the throat.
  - **Diarrhea**: Loose, watery stools.
  - **Vomiting**: Bringing up fluid or food through your mouth.
  - **Rash**: Abnormal skin rash or lesion.
  - **Loss of appetite**: Not feeling hungry or interested in food.
  - **Loss of consciousness**: Unresponsive or unaware of your surroundings.
  - **Seizure**: Sudden, uncontrolled muscle contractions.
  - **Numbness or weakness**: Losing feeling or strength in parts of your body.
  - **Weakness**: General feeling of being unable to do things.
  - **Loss of balance**: Feeling like you might fall or stumble.
  - **Speech difficulty**: Struggling to speak or communicate.
  - **Blurred vision**: Difficulty focusing on objects.
  - **Double vision**: Seeing two images instead of one.
  - **Eye redness**: Redness or swelling in your eyes.
  - **Eye pain**: Pain in or around your eyes.
  - **Eye discharge**: Discharge or fluid from your eyes.
  - **Yellowed skin**: Yellowing of your skin.
  - **Yellowed eyes**: Yellowing of your eyes.
  - **Swollen lips**: Swelling in your lips.
  - **Swollen face**: Swelling in your face.
  - **Swollen joints**: Swelling in your joints.
  - **Sore tongue**: Pain or discomfort in your tongue.
  - **Bad taste**: Unpleasant taste or smell.
  - **Bad smell**: Unpleasant odor.
  - **Runny nose**: Nasal congestion or runny nose.
  - **Loss of taste**: Difficulty tasting food or drinks.
  - **Sweating**: Excessive sweating.
  - **Hiccups**: Sudden, repetitive contractions of the diaphragm.
  - **Heart burn**: Burning sensation in your chest.

### Page 3 - Additional Comments
- **Additional Comments** (page_3, text, required/optional): This is a free text field where you can enter any additional information or comments about your condition. Please feel free to provide as much detail as possible.

## Tips
- **Be honest and accurate**: Please provide as much detail as possible, even if you're unsure about something.
- **Ask for help**: If you need help or clarification about any symptom or field, don't hesitate to ask.
- **Review your answers**: Double-check your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
