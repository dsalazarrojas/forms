# Digestive Health Provider Information Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about digestive health providers, including their contact details, practice size, and specialty. The data collected will be used to manage and improve healthcare services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* `Provider Name`
	* `Provider Type`
	* `Refferal Type`
2. Select the options that apply to you:
	* `Specialty`
	* `Insurance Payers`
	* `Payment Methods`
	* `Languages`
	* `Provider Status`
	* `Practice Size`
	* `Provider Network`
	* `Provider Location`
	* `Provider Distance`
	* `Provider Rating`
3. Enter your `Address` details.
4. Enter your `Phone` and `Fax` numbers.
5. Enter your `Email` and `Website` (if applicable).
6. Add any additional `Notes` about your practice or yourself as a provider.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Provider Name** (`1`): Please enter your full name as a provider.
* **License Number** (`2`): Enter your medical license number.
* **Address** (`3`): Enter your practice address.
* **Address 2** (`4`): Enter additional address information (e.g., suite number, floor).
* **Address 3** (`5`): Enter further address information (e.g., street address, building).
* **Phone** (`6`): Enter your work or mobile phone number.
* **Fax** (`7`): Enter your fax number (if applicable).
* **Email** (`8`): Enter your email address.
* **Website** (`9`): Enter your professional website (if applicable).
* **Specialty** (`10`): Select the area of expertise you specialize in.
* **Provider Type** (`11`): Select your profession (e.g., Physician, Nurse Practitioner).
* **Refferal Type** (`12`): Select the type of referrals you handle (e.g., Primary Care, Specialty Care).
* **Insurance Payers** (`13`): Select the insurance plans you accept.
* **Payment Methods** (`14`): Select the payment methods accepted by your practice.
* **Languages** (`15`): Select the languages you can communicate with patients.
* **Provider Status** (`16`): Select your practice status.
* **Practice Size** (`17`): Select the size of your practice.
* **Provider Network** (`18`): Select if you are part of a network.
* **Provider Location** (`19`): Select the location of your practice (e.g., City, County).
* **Provider Distance** (`20`): Select the distance of your practice from patients.
* **Provider Rating** (`21`): Select your patient satisfaction rating.
* **Practice Hours** (`22`): Select the hours you operate your practice.
* **Referral Window** (`23`): Select the time period for referrals.
* **Provider Rating Notes** (`24`): Enter any comments about your rating.
* **Notes** (`25`): Add any additional comments about your practice or yourself as a provider.
