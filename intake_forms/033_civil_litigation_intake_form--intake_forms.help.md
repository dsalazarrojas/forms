# Civil Litigation Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from prospective clients about their civil litigation matter. This information will be used to evaluate the case and determine the best course of action for representation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Please ensure that you are providing accurate and honest information about your legal issue.
2. Answer all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
3. Use a brief description for the "Briefly describe your legal situation" field.
4. Ensure that you select the correct options for each select_one field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Legal Name** (`potential_client_name`, text, \*): Enter your full name as it is spelled.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, \*): Enter your email address.
* **Primary Phone Number** (`client_phone`, text, \*): Enter your primary phone number.
* **Preferred Method of Contact** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone Call, Text Message, or In-Person Meeting).
* **Briefly describe your legal situation** (`legal_issue_description`, text, \*): Provide a brief description of the legal issue.
* **Date of Incident or Dispute** (`incident_date`, date, \*): Enter the date of the incident or dispute.
* **Have you been served with any legal papers?** (`court_papers_served`, select_one): Choose one of the following options:
	+ True: Yes, I have been served with legal papers.
	+ False: No, I have not been served with legal papers.
	+ Not Sure: I am not sure.
* **List names of other parties involved** (`other_parties_involved`, text, \*): List the names of all parties involved in the legal issue.
* **What is your desired outcome from this litigation?** (`desired_outcome`, text, \*): Specify your desired outcome (e.g., Compensation, Injunction, Specific Performance).
* **Have you previously consulted an attorney regarding this matter?** (`prior_representation`, select_one): Choose one of the following options:
	+ True: Yes, I have previously consulted an attorney.
	+ False: No, I have not previously consulted an attorney.
* **Are you aware of any upcoming deadlines or court dates?** (`urgency_deadlines`, text, false): If you are aware of any upcoming deadlines or court dates, please list them.
* **Do you have insurance that might cover this claim?** (`insurance_coverage`, select_one): Choose one of the following options:
	+ True: Yes, I have insurance that might cover this claim.
	+ False: No, I do not have insurance that might cover this claim.
	+ Not Sure: I am not sure.
* **How did you hear about our law firm?** (`hear_about_firm`, select_one): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Online Search: I found you through an online search.
	+ Referral (Friend/Family): A friend or family member referred me to your firm.
	+ Bar Association: I saw your firm through a bar association.
	+ Advertisement: I saw your ad in a magazine or online.
	+ Other: Please specify.
* **Date** (`intake_date`, date, \*): Enter the current date.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and honest information about your legal issue.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our firm directly.
