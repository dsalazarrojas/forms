# Orthotic Prescription Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Orthotic Prescription Survey form is designed to collect patient information and medical history for the prescription of orthotic devices. This form will help healthcare professionals understand the patient's needs and preferences, ensuring the best possible outcomes for the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the introduction page, which explains the purpose and importance of the form.
2. Answer the questions on each page in a clear and concise manner, providing as much detail as possible.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please refer to the "Contact" section at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What brings you here today** (`introduction`): This is the first page of the form, which explains the purpose and importance of the form.
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`): Please provide a brief description of the patient's medical history, including any relevant medical conditions or allergies.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`): Describe the patient's symptoms, including any pain, discomfort, or other issues related to their condition.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`): Provide a detailed account of the patient's medical history, including any previous injuries or conditions that may be relevant to their orthotic device prescription.
* **What do you want to achieve** (`goals`): Describe the patient's goals and expectations for their orthotic device, including any specific activities or goals they hope to achieve with the device.
* **Orthotic Device** (`orthotic_device`): Select the type of orthotic device that the patient is interested in, or has been prescribed in the past.
* **Device Type** (`device_type`): Select the type of orthotic device that the patient needs, such as AFO, Orthotic, or Other.
* **Left Foot** (`left_foot`): Indicate whether the patient has any issues with their left foot, and select the relevant option.
* **Right Foot** (`right_foot`): Indicate whether the patient has any issues with their right foot, and select the relevant option.
* **Left Ankle Knee** (`left_ankle_knee`): Select whether the patient has any issues with their left ankle knee.
* **Left Knee Elbow** (`left_knee_elbow`): Select whether the patient has any issues with their left knee elbow.
* **Right Ankle Knee** (`right_ankle_knee`): Select whether the patient has any issues with their right ankle knee.
* **Right Knee Elbow** (`right_knee_elbow`): Select whether the patient has any issues with their right knee elbow.
* **Left Sole Length** (`left_sole_length`): Describe the length of the patient's left sole.
* **Left Toe** (`left_toe`): Describe the patient's left toe.
* **Left Ankle Knee** (`left_ankle_knee`): Select whether the patient has any issues with their left ankle knee.
* **Right Foot** (`right_foot`): Describe the patient's right foot.
* **Right Ankle Knee** (`right_ankle_knee`): Select whether the patient has any issues with their right ankle knee.
* **Right Knee Elbow** (`right_knee_elbow`): Select whether the patient has any issues with their right knee elbow.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional comments or information that may be relevant to the patient's prescription.
* **Doctor** (`doctor`): Provide the name and contact information of the patient's doctor.
* **Doctor Title** (`doctor_title`): Provide the title of the patient's doctor.
* **Contact No** (`contact_no`): Provide the contact information for the patient's doctor or healthcare provider.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Provide the email address of the patient's doctor or healthcare provider.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Provide the phone number of the patient's doctor or healthcare provider.
* **Contact Notes** (`contact_notes`): Add any additional comments or information that may be relevant to the patient's contact information.
* **Submit** (`submit`): Once you have completed all the fields, click the "Submit" button to send the form to the patient's healthcare provider.
