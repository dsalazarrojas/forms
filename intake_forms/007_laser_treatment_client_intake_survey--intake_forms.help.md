# laser_treatment_client_intake_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients prior to laser treatment. It gathers essential details such as contact information, medical history, and any allergies they may have. The data collected will be used for administrative purposes, including scheduling appointments and communicating with the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start on the HomePage, which is the first page of the form.
2. Enter your First Name in the FirstName field.
3. Enter your Last Name in the LastName field.
4. Provide your Email address in the Email field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
5. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
6. Share your Medical History in the MedicalHistory field.
7. If you have any allergies, list them in the Allergies field.
8. Once you have filled out all the necessary fields, review your information for accuracy and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **FirstName** (Required/Optional: Required)
	* Enter your first name.
- **LastName** (Required/Optional: Required)
	* Enter your last name.
- **Email** (Required/Optional: Required)
	* Enter your valid email address that will be used for communication with you.
- **Phone** (Required/Optional: Required)
	* Enter your contact phone number.
- **MedicalHistory** (Required/Optional: Required)
	* Provide a brief description of your medical history, including any relevant conditions or medications.
- **Allergies** (Required/Optional: Required)
	* List any allergies you have, including medications, food, or other substances.

## Tips
* Ensure you answer all the required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your email address for correctness.
* Be as detailed as possible when sharing your medical history and allergies.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* Once you submit the form, you will receive a confirmation email with the next steps in your treatment plan.
