# Aromatherapy Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Aromatherapy Client Intake Form is designed to gather essential information about your health, skin, and preferences to provide safe and effective aromatherapy treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully and accurately.
2. Ensure that you fill in all required fields.
3. If you're unsure about any information, please ask your aromatherapist for guidance.
4. Review and revise your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (2)** (`client_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name, including any middle name or initials.
* **Date of Birth (3)** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format `month/day/year` (e.g., 02/12/1990).
* **Phone Number (4)** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number, including the area code.
* **Email Address (5)** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Date of Intake (6)** (`intake_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your intake session.
* **Current Medical Conditions (8)** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): If you have any current medical conditions, such as hypertension, please list them here.
* **List of Current Medications (9)** (`medication_list`, text, optional): If you take any prescription or over-the-counter medications, please list them here.
* **Major Surgeries or Procedures (10)** (`surgical_history`, text, optional): If you have had any major surgeries or procedures, please list them here.
* **Allergies or Sensitivities (11)** (`allergies_or_sensitivities`, text, optional): If you have any allergies or sensitivities to food, medications, or essential oils, please list them here.
* **History of Adverse Reactions (12)** (`reaction_history`, text, optional): If you have had any adverse reactions to treatments, medications, or products, please list them here.
* **Skin Type (14)** (`skin_type`, select_one, required): Select your skin type from the options: Normal, Dry, Oily, Combination, Sensitive, or Other.
* **Skin Conditions or Sensitivities (15)** (`skin_conditions`, text, optional): If you have any skin conditions or sensitivities, such as eczema or psoriasis, please list them here.
* **Cosmetic or Skincare Product Use (16)** (`cosmetic_use`, text, optional): If you use any cosmetics or skincare products, please list them here.
* **Pregnancy Status (18)** (`pregnancy_status`, select_one, optional): Select your current pregnancy status from the options: False, True, Not sure, or Prefer not to say.
* **Breastfeeding (19)** (`breastfeeding`, select_one, optional): Select your current breastfeeding status from the options: False, True, or Prefer not to say.
* **Menstrual Cycle Status (20)** (`menstrual_cycle`, select_one, optional): Select your current menstrual cycle status from the options: Regular, Irregular, Irregular periods, Menopausal, or Post-menopausal.
* **Contraindications Check (21)** (`contraindications_understand`, select_one, required): Select "Yes, I understand" if you're aware of aromatherapy contraindications.
* **Awareness of Potential Medication Interactions (22)** (`medication_interactions`, select_one, required): Select "Yes, aware" if you're aware of potential medication interactions.
* **Informed Consent (24)** (`informed_consent`, select_one, required): Select "Yes, I consent" to aromatherapy treatment based on the information provided above.

## Tips
* If you're unsure or have any questions, please ask your aromatherapist for guidance.
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Review and revise your answers before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you provide all required information to ensure safe and effective treatment.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please discuss them with your aromatherapist.
