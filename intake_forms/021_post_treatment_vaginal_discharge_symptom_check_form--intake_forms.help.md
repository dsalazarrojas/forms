# Post Treatment Vaginal Discharge Symptom Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients regarding their post-treatment symptoms after undergoing treatment for vaginal discharge.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of when the patient's symptoms started.
2. Choose the duration of the patient's symptoms.
3. Select all the discharge colors that the patient is experiencing.
4. Select all the discharge textures that the patient is experiencing.
5. Select the frequency of the patient's symptoms.
6. Choose all the symptoms that the patient is experiencing (check all that apply).
7. Select the outcome of the patient's treatment.
8. Check if the patient's follow-up is scheduled.
9. Enter the date of the next scheduled follow-up appointment.
10. Enter the number of reminders the patient needs for follow-up appointments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **symptom_date** (`symptom_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of when the patient's symptoms started.
* **symptom_duration** (`symptom_duration`, number, required: false): Choose the duration of the patient's symptoms. 
* **discharge_color** (`discharge_color`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the discharge colors that the patient is experiencing.
* **discharge_texture** (`discharge_texture`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the discharge textures that the patient is experiencing.
* **symptom_frequency** (`symptom_frequency`, select_one, required: false): Select the frequency of the patient's symptoms.
* **symptoms** (`symptoms`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all the symptoms that the patient is experiencing (check all that apply).
* **treatment_outcome** (`treatment_outcome`, select_one, required: false): Select the outcome of the patient's treatment.
* **follow_up_scheduled** (`follow_up_scheduled`, select_one, required: false): Check if the patient's follow-up is scheduled.
* **follow_up_next** (`follow_up_next`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the next scheduled follow-up appointment.
* **follow_up_reminders** (`follow_up_reminders`, number, required: false): Enter the number of reminders the patient needs for follow-up appointments.
