# Eviction Diversion Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals who are eligible and invited to participate in the Eviction Diversion Program. The program is designed to provide temporary assistance to individuals who are facing eviction and need help getting back on their feet.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information in the corresponding fields. Make sure to review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required/optional): Please enter your first name as it is spelled on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required/optional): Please enter your last name as it is spelled on your identification documents.
* **Email** (3, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that you have access to. This will be your point of contact for program updates and communications.
* **Phone** (4, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Street Address** (5, text, required/optional): Enter your physical address where you currently reside.
* **Apartment** (6, text, required/optional): If you have an apartment or suite number, please enter it here.
* **City** (7, text, required/optional): Enter the city where you currently reside.
* **State** (8, text, required/optional): Enter the state where you currently reside.
* **Zip** (9, text, required/optional): Enter your postal code (zip code).
* **Date of Birth** (10, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Employment Status** (11, select_one, required/optional): Select your current employment status:
	+ **Unemployed**: You are not currently employed.
	+ **Working**: You are currently employed.
	+ **Self-Employed**: You are self-employed.
* **Annual Income** (12, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your annual income:
	+ 0-15,000
	+ 15,000-25,000
	+ 25,000-35,000
	+ 35,000-50,000
	+ 50,000-75,000
	+ 75,000+
Note: This form is best completed on a computer or tablet with a compatible web browser. If you experience any issues, please contact the program administrator for assistance.
