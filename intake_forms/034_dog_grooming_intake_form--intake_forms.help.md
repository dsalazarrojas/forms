# Dog Grooming Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dog grooming intake form is designed to collect information from dog owners about their pets and their needs for grooming services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out each section accurately and thoroughly.
2. Answer all relevant questions and provide detailed information whenever possible.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dog Owner Information** (`dog_owner_information`):
	+ This section collects information about yourself as the dog owner.
* **Dog Details** (`dog_details`):
	+ This section is about your dog's specific needs and characteristics.
* **Dog Hygiene** (`dog_hygiene`):
	+ Please indicate how often you bathe your dog, with options: Morning, Afternoon, and Evening.
* **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`):
	+ Select your preferred contact time with options: Morning, Afternoon, and Evening.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`):
	+ Provide your contact information, such as phone number and email.
* **Veterinary Info** (`veterinary_info`):
	+ If your dog has a veterinarian, please provide their name and contact information.
* **Medical Info** (`medical_info`):
	+ Provide any relevant medical information about your dog, such as allergies or health issues.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`):
	+ Select any medical conditions that apply to your dog with options: None, Allergies, Medications, and Health Issues.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`):
	+ If you have any additional information or concerns, please provide them here.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`):
	+ Please provide your payment information, such as method and amount.
* **Dog Photo ID** (`dog_photo_id`):
	+ If you have a photo ID of your dog, please provide it here.
* **Client Photo ID** (`client_photo_id`):
	+ If you have a photo ID of yourself, please provide it here.

Please complete this form accurately and thoroughly to help us provide the best possible experience for both you and your pet.
