# Annual Business Report Filing Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information for the Annual Business Report Filing, which is a critical document required for businesses to report their financial and business information to the relevant authorities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure that the form is filled in completely and correctly before submission.
3. Review your answers carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): 
Fill in the legal name of the company.

- **Contact Person Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): 
Enter the name of the person responsible for filing the report.

- **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): 
Enter a valid email address of the contact person.

- **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): 
Enter the best contact number of the contact person.

- **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, required): 
Enter the registered business address of the company.

- **Business Entity Type** (`business_entity_type`, select_one, required): 
Choose the legal structure of the business (Corporation, S Corporation, LLC, Partnership, Sole Proprietorship, Nonprofit, or Other).

- **State of Incorporation** (`state_of_incorporation`, select_one, required): 
Select the state where the business is incorporated from the list (Delaware, California, New York, Texas, Florida, Nevada, Wyoming, or Other).

- **EIN Number** (`ein_number`, text, required): 
Enter the Employer Identification Number of the business.

- **State ID Number** (`state_id_number`, text, optional): 
If applicable, enter the state business ID number.

- **Filing Year** (`filing_year`, select_one, required): 
Select the year this report is for from the list ('2026', '2025', '2024', '2023').

- **Fiscal Year End Date** (`fiscal_year_end`, date, required): 
Enter the date when the fiscal year ends.

- **Report Type** (`report_type`, select_one, required): 
Choose the type of report (Annual Report, Statement of Information, Franchise Tax Report, Combined Report, Amendment, or Other).

- **Officers and Directors** (`officers_directors`, text, required): 
List the current officers and directors of the business.

- **Registered Agent** (`registered_agent`, text, required): 
Enter the name of the registered agent.

- **Registered Agent Address** (`registered_agent_address`, text, required): 
Enter the address of the registered agent.

- **Authorized Shares** (`authorized_shares`, number, optional): 
If applicable, enter the number of authorized shares.

- **Outstanding Shares** (`outstanding_shares`, number, optional): 
If applicable, enter the number of outstanding shares.

- **Principal Business Activity** (`principal_business_activity`, text, required): 
Describe the main business activity of the company.

- **NAICS Code** (`naics_code`, text, optional): 
If applicable, enter the industry classification code.

- **Gross Receipts** (`gross_receipts`, number, optional): 
If applicable, enter the total gross receipts for the year.

- **Total Assets** (`total_assets`, number, optional): 
If applicable, enter the total company assets.

- **Filing Method** (`filing_method`, select_one, required): 
Choose the method of filing (Electronic Filing, Paper Filing, Expedited Filing, Standard Processing).

- **Filing Fee Payment** (`filing_fee_payment`, select_one, required): 
Choose the method of payment (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, Check, Invoice, Prepaid Account).

- **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, optional): 
If applicable, list any supporting documentation.

- **Signature Name** (`signature_name`, text, required): 
Enter the name of the person signing the form.

- **Signature Title** (`signature_title`, text, required): 
Enter the title of the signing person.

- **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): 
Enter the date of signing.

- **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): 
If applicable, enter any other relevant information.

Note: Fields marked as "required" are mandatory and need to be filled in for the form to be valid, while those marked as "optional" can be filled in or left blank.
