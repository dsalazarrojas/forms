# Speech And Language Service Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a patient's background, medical history, and communication concerns to help service providers offer tailored speech and language services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief overview of the patient's background, including their personal and family history.
2. Describe the patient's medical history, including any relevant conditions or allergies.
3. If applicable, specify any communication concerns or difficulties the patient experiences.
4. Mention any speech or language disorders the patient has.
5. Share details about the patient's family background and how it may be relevant to their condition.
6. Enter the date and time of the patient's medical history.
7. Provide information about the patient's communication history, including any relevant details.
8. Share the patient's concerns or questions about their condition or treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient Background** (`patient_background`, text, optional)
* This field is for providing a brief overview of the patient's background, including their personal and family history.
* Please include any relevant details that may be helpful for the service provider to know.

### 2. **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional)
* This field is for describing the patient's medical history, including any relevant conditions or allergies.
* Please provide a concise and accurate account of the patient's medical history.

### 3. **Medical Conditions** (`medical_history_condition`, text, optional)
* This field is for listing any medical conditions or allergies that are relevant to the patient's condition.
* Please ensure to be as detailed as possible, but avoid repeating information already provided in the "Medical History" field.

### 4. **Communication Concerns** (`communication_concerns`, text, optional)
* This field is for expressing any concerns or difficulties the patient experiences with communication.
* Please provide any relevant details or examples that may aid the service provider in understanding the patient's needs.

### 5. **Speech or Language Disorder** (`speech_language_disorder`, text, optional)
* This field is for mentioning any speech or language disorders the patient has.
* Please be as accurate and detailed as possible when describing the patient's condition.

### 6. **Patient Family Background** (`patient_family_background`, text, optional)
* This field is for sharing details about the patient's family background and how it may be relevant to their condition.
* Please provide any information that may be helpful for the service provider to know.

### 7. **Medical History Date** (`medical_history_date`, date, optional)
* This field is for entering the date of the patient's medical history.
* Please ensure to format the date according to your local date format.

### 8. **Medical History Time** (`medical_history_time`, time, optional)
* This field is for entering the time of the patient's medical history.
* Please format the time according to your local time format.

### 9. **Communication History** (`communication_history`, text, optional)
* This field is for providing information about the patient's communication history, including any relevant details.
* Please be as thorough and accurate as possible when describing the patient's communication patterns.

### 10. **Patient Concerns** (`patient_concerns`, text, optional)
* This field is for expressing any concerns or questions the patient has about their condition or treatment.
* Please provide any relevant details or examples that may aid the service provider in understanding the patient's needs.

## Tips
* Please ensure to answer all fields accurately and thoroughly to help the service provider understand the patient's needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the service provider for clarification.
* This form is for patient use only and should not be used for general public submission.
