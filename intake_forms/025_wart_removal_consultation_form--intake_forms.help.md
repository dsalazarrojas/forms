# Wart Removal Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wart Removal Consultation Form is designed to gather necessary information from patients to prepare for their wart removal consultation or appointment scheduling.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the patient details section with your personal information.
2. Provide your medical history to help our staff better understand your situation.
3. Select the preferred appointment time from the available options or enter your own time.
4. If you have multiple preferred times, please list them in the fields provided.
5. If you have any medical conditions that may affect your treatment, select the relevant option.
6. Enter any relevant notes or information in the notes field.
7. Provide your contact information, including phone numbers and email, to ensure we can reach out to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Details**: Please fill out your personal details, including your name, etc.
* **Medical History**: Describe any medical conditions or allergies you have that may be relevant to your treatment.
* **Appointment Time**: Select the time you would prefer for your appointment. If you don't see a time that works for you, you can enter a custom time.
* **Preferred Time 1-5**: If you have multiple preferred appointment times, please list them in these fields.
* **Prophets**: This field is currently empty and does not seem to be used. Please ignore it.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your primary phone number for us to contact you.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so we can reach out to you.
* **Medical Conditions**: Select the medical conditions that apply to you from the options provided.
* **Note**: Enter any relevant notes or information about your consultation or appointment.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your contact information.
* **Contact Name 2-4**: Enter the names of additional contacts, if applicable.
* **Contact Name Report**: Enter the name of the person or organization we should report to in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone Number 2-7**: Enter the phone numbers of additional contacts, if applicable.

Note: Some fields have been left out due to redundancy or lack of clear purpose. Please review the form carefully and only fill out the fields that are relevant to your situation.
