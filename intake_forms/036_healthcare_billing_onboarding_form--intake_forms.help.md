# Healthcare Billing Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Healthcare Billing Onboarding Form is a tool to gather essential information about patients and track their billing status. This form is designed for healthcare professionals to quickly and accurately collect required data to onboard new patients and ensure smooth billing processing.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Patient Info" section with the patient's details, if necessary.
2. Enter the "Billing Info" section with the relevant billing details.
3. Select the "Onboarding Status" in the dropdown menu to indicate the patient's status.
4. Optionally, add any additional comments or notes about the patient's onboarding in the "Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, optional): Enter the patient's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
- **Billing Info** (`billing_info`, text, optional): Enter the billing address, contact person, and any other billing-related information.
- **Onboarding Status** (`onboarding_status`, select_multiple, required): Choose the patient's onboarding status from the available options: Active, Inactive, or Incomplete.
- **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the patient's onboarding process.
