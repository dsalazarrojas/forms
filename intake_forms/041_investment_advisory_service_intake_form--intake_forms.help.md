# Investment Advisory Service Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Investment Advisory Service Intake Form is designed to collect information about you and your investment goals. Please answer each question accurately to help our advisors provide personalized investment advice tailored to your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section as instructed. Make sure to answer all required questions, and if you need help or clarification, do not hesitate to contact our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter your valid email address where you can be reached for updates and communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further questions or updates.
* **Address** (`address`, required): Enter your current address for our records.
* **Investment Objectives** (`investment_objectives`, required): Describe your investment goals, such as saving for retirement, a down payment on a house, or education expenses.
* **Financial History** (`financial_history`, required): Provide a brief overview of your financial situation, including income, expenses, and any significant financial events.
* **Risk Tolerance** (`risk_tolerance`, required): Select the level of risk you are willing to take with your investments, such as conservative, moderate, or aggressive.
* **Investment Experience** (`investment_experience`, required): Describe your past experience with investments, including any successful or unsuccessful experiences.
* **Asset Allocation** (`asset_allocation`, optional): If you have existing investments, list the types of assets you currently own, such as stocks, bonds, or real estate.
* **Investment Experience (Years)** (`investment_experience_years`, required): Enter the number of years you have been investing.
* **Net Worth** (`net_worth`, optional): Enter your current net worth if you have one.
* **Investment Goals** (`investment_goals`, optional): Select whether you have investment goals, such as saving for retirement or a down payment.
* **Investment Frequency** (`investment_frequency`, required): Select how often you invest, such as monthly, quarterly, or annually.
* **Asset Classes** (`asset_classes`, optional): If you have existing investments, list the types of assets you currently own, such as stocks, bonds, or real estate.
* **Investment Approach** (`investment_approach`, required): Select the investment approach you prefer, such as growth, income, or value investing.
* **Financial Institution** (`financial_institution`, optional): If you have an account with a financial institution, select it from the list.
* **Account Holder** (`account_holder`, optional): If you have an account holder for your investments, select it from the list.
* **Investment Type** (`investment_type`, optional): If you have existing investments, list the type of investments you currently own, such as stocks, bonds, or ETFs.
* **Investment Location** (`investment_location`, required): Select where you plan to invest, such as domestic or international.
* **Investment Status** (`investment_status`, required): Select the current status of your investments, such as active or inactive.
* **Risk Attitude** (`risk_attitude`, required): Select your attitude towards risk, such as cautious or aggressive.
* **Investment Return** (`investment_return`, optional): Enter the expected return on your investments.
* **Time Period** (`time_period`, required): Enter the time period you plan to invest, such as short-term or long-term.
* **Investment Amount** (`investment_amount`, optional): Enter the amount you plan to invest.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, optional): If you have any additional comments or information, please provide them here.

**Tips**

* Be honest and accurate when answering each question.
* If you are unsure about any question, do not hesitate to contact our support team for clarification.
* Please review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
