# Therapy Program Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Therapy Program Admission Form is used to collect information from clients that will be used by the therapy team to provide personalized support and care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the first section with your personal details: 
   - First Name
   - Last Name
   - Email
   - Phone
2. Enter the intake date and time in the format specified.
3. Select the appropriate options for "Consent to Treatment" and "Payment Details" as necessary.
4. Provide a brief note on your medical history and schedule details.
5. If you have other payment details that are not listed, please select "Yes" to indicate this.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): This is the first name of the client. Please enter your first name.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): This is the last name of the client. Please enter your last name.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This is the email address of the client. Please enter your valid email address.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): This is the phone number of the client. Please enter your valid phone number.

### Intake Date
* **Intake Date** (`intake_date`, date, optional): This is the date of the client's intake appointment. If you are unsure of the date, please leave it blank.

### Intake Time
* **Intake Time** (`intake_time`, time, optional): This is the time of the client's intake appointment. If you are unsure of the time, please leave it blank.

### Therapist Name
* **Therapist Name** (`therapist_name`, text, required): This is the name of the therapist assigned to the client. Please enter the name of the therapist.

### Therapist Phone
* **Therapist Phone** (`therapist_phone`, text, optional): This is the phone number of the therapist. Please enter the phone number of the therapist.

### Practice Name
* **Practice Name** (`practice_name`, text, required): This is the name of the practice where the client will be receiving therapy. Please enter the name of the practice.

### Practice Address
* **Practice Address** (`practice_address`, text, optional): This is the address of the practice. Please enter the address of the practice.

### Practice Phone
* **Practice Phone** (`practice_phone`, text, optional): This is the phone number of the practice. Please enter the phone number of the practice.

### Practice Email
* **Practice Email** (`practice_email`, text, optional): This is the email address of the practice. Please enter the email address of the practice.

### Consent to Treatment
* **Consent to Treatment** (`consent_to_treatment`, select multiple, optional): Please select "Yes" if you consent to treatment, "No" if you do not consent, or "Maybe" if you are unsure. 

### Medical History
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, note, optional): This is a note on your medical history. Please provide a brief description of your medical history.

### Schedule Time
* **Schedule Time** (`schedule_time`, time, optional): This is the time of your scheduled appointment. If you are unsure of the time, please leave it blank.

### Schedule Date
* **Schedule Date** (`schedule_date`, date, optional): This is the date of your scheduled appointment. If you are unsure of the date, please leave it blank.

### Schedule Note
* **Schedule Note** (`schedule_note`, note, optional): This is a note on your schedule. Please provide a brief description of your schedule.

### Payment Details
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, select one, optional): Please select "Online Payment" or "Offline Payment" as necessary.

### Other Payment Details
* **Other Payment Details** (`other_payment_details`, select multiple, optional): If you have other payment details that are not listed, please select "Yes" to indicate this.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that the therapy team has the necessary information to provide the best possible care for you.
* If you are unsure of any information, please leave it blank and discuss it with the therapy team.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the therapy team for assistance.
