# Urgent Care Patient Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture patient information for urgent care settings. It helps ensure that all necessary details are collected efficiently, reducing errors and improving the accuracy of patient records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with the required and optional information.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as `required: true`).
3. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose the most accurate option from the dropdown list.
4. Use the "Additional info here" field for any extra details or notes.
5. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter the patient's first name. (Text input, Required)
- **Last Name**: Enter the patient's last name. (Text input, Required)
- **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format. (Date input, Required)
- **Address**: Enter the patient's home address. (Text input, Not required)
- **Emergency Contact First Name**: If you have an emergency contact, enter their first name. (Text input, Not required)
- **Emergency Contact Last Name**: If you have an emergency contact, enter their last name. (Text input, Not required)
- **Relationship**: Choose how you are related to the emergency contact (e.g., mother, father, sibling, etc.). (Select_one, Required)
- **Allergy**: If you have any medical allergies, enter them here. (Text input, Not required)
- **Symptoms**: Choose any of the symptoms you are experiencing or have experienced. (Select_multiple, Required)
- **Medical Condition**: If you have any pre-existing medical conditions, enter them here. (Text input, Not required)
- **Consent**: Check if you have given informed consent or assent for treatment. (Select_one, Required)
- **Emergency Contact Phone**: If you have an emergency contact, enter their phone number. (Text input, Not required)
- **Emergency Contact Email**: If you have an emergency contact, enter their email address. (Email input, Not required)
- **Note**: Any additional information about your visit or treatment. (Note input, Not required)
- **Emergency Contact Relationship**: If you have an emergency contact, describe your relationship to them. (Text input, Not required)
- **Consent Date**: The date you provided consent or assent for treatment. (Date input, Required)
- **Signature**: Please sign to confirm that you have read and understand this form. (Text input, Required)

Note: This guide is meant to be read in conjunction with the actual form, which will be filled out online or on paper. Always review your answers carefully and ensure they accurately reflect your situation.
