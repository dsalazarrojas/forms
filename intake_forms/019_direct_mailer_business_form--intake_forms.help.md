# direct_mailer_business_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The direct_mailer_business_form is used to gather information about mailers, customers, and mailing status for direct mailer business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for each field, which includes:
	* Business Form
	* Customer Data
	* Mailing List
	* Mailing Date
	* Mailing Time
2. Select the relevant options for each field from the provided lists.
3. Enter any additional information in the text fields, such as Customer Notes and Business Notes.
4. Ensure you've reviewed all the fields carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Form** (`business_form`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the business purpose of the mailer.
* **Customer Data** (`customer_data`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the relevant customer data for the mailer. This field is optional, but provides additional context to the mailer.
* **Mailing List** (`mailing_list`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the mailing list associated with the mailer.
* **Mail Piece Type** (`mail_piece_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of mail piece being used.
* **Mail Piece Size** (`mail_piece_size`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the size of the mail piece.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes about the customer.
* **Mailing Date** (`mailing_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date for the mailing.
* **Mailing Time** (`mailing_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time for the mailing.
* **Business Notes** (`business_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional business notes about the mailer.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address for the customer.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number for the customer.
* **Mailing Status** (`mailing_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of the mailing.
* **Customer ID** (`customer_id`, `number`, optional): Enter the customer ID number.
* **Mail Piece ID** (`mail_piece_id`, `number`, optional): Enter the mail piece ID number.
* **Mail Piece Name** (`mail_piece_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the mail piece.
* **Mail Piece Description** (`mail_piece_description`, `text`, optional): Enter a description of the mail piece.
* **Mail Piece Status** (`mail_piece_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of the mail piece.
* **Mail Piece Image** (`mail_piece_image`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the image associated with the mail piece.
* **Mail Piece Cost** (`mail_piece_cost`, `number`, optional): Enter the cost of the mail piece.
* **Mail Piece Price** (`mail_piece_price`, `number`, optional): Enter the price of the mail piece.
* **Mail Piece Size Width** (`mail_piece_size_width`, `number`, optional): Enter the width of the mail piece.
* **Mail Piece Size Height** (`mail_piece_size_height`, `number`, optional): Enter the height of the mail piece.
* **Mail Piece Size Depth** (`mail_piece_size_depth`, `number`, optional): Enter the depth of the mail piece.
* **Mail Piece Size Length** (`mail_piece_size_length`, `number`, optional): Enter the length of the mail piece.
* **Mail Piece Size Width Unit** (`mail_piece_size_width_unit`, `select_one`, optional): Select the unit of measurement for the mail piece width.
* **Mail Piece Size Height Unit** (`mail_piece_size_height_unit`, `select_one`, optional): Select the unit of measurement for the mail piece height.
* **Mail Piece Size Depth Unit** (`mail_piece_size_depth_unit`, `select_one`, optional): Select the unit of measurement for the mail piece depth.
* **Mail Piece Size Weight** (`mail_piece_size_weight`, `number`, optional): Enter the weight of the mail piece.
* **Mail Piece Size Weight Unit** (`mail_piece_size_weight_unit`, `select_one`, optional): Select the unit of measurement for the mail piece weight.
* **Mail Piece Status Date** (`mail_piece_status_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the mail piece status.
* **Mail Piece Status Time** (`mail_piece_status_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the mail piece status.
* **Mail Piece Status Note** (`mail_piece_status_note`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes about the mail piece status.
