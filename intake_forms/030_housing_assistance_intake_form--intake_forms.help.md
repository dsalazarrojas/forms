# Housing Assistance Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals seeking housing assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
- To get started, select the "Housing Assistance Services" option that best describes your need.
- Fill in the remaining fields with the required information, making sure to be as accurate as possible.
- If any of the fields are marked as "Optional" you can fill them in if you feel it is necessary or if it is relevant to your situation.
- Please note that some fields are required to be filled in order to proceed with the application process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information**: A field to input some personal details about you.
  * **Name** (text): Enter your name.
  * **Age** (text): Enter your age.
* **Contact Information**: A field to input contact information about you.
  * **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
  * **Email** (text): Enter your email address.
* **Housing Information**: A field to input information about your housing status.
  * **Address** (text): Enter your current address.
* **Housing Assistance History**: A field to input any previous housing assistance you have received.
  * **Provider** (text): Enter the name of the provider.
  * **Date** (date): Enter the date you received assistance.
* **Medical Information**: A field to input any medical information.
  * **Condition** (text): Enter a brief description of your medical condition.
  * **Medication** (text): Enter any medication you are currently taking.
* **Housing Status**: A field to input your current housing status.
  * **Housing Type** (text): Enter the type of housing you are currently in.
* **Household Information**: A field to input information about your household.
  * **Members** (text): Enter the number of people in your household.
* **Emergency Contact**: A field to input information about your emergency contact.
  * **Name** (text): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
  * **Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Housing Assistance Services**: A field to select the type of housing assistance you are seeking.
  * **Homelessness Prevention**: Select this option if you are at risk of becoming homeless.
  * **Emergency Assistance**: Select this option if you are in immediate need of housing assistance.
  * **Housing Stabilization**: Select this option if you need help maintaining your current housing.
  * **Permanent Supportive Housing**: Select this option if you need long-term housing support.
  * **Rapid Rehousing**: Select this option if you are transitioning from one housing situation to another.
  * **Transitional Housing**: Select this option if you are staying in temporary housing while seeking long-term housing.
* **Housing Assistance Funding**: A field to input the funding amount for your housing assistance.
  * **Dollar Amount** (number): Enter the dollar amount of assistance you are seeking.
* **Housing Assistance Amount**: A field to input the amount of assistance you are seeking.
  * **Dollar Amount** (number): Enter the dollar amount of assistance you are seeking.
* **Housing Assistance Start Date**: A field to input the start date of your housing assistance.
  * **Date** (date): Enter the date you began receiving assistance.
* **Housing Assistance End Date**: A field to input the end date of your housing assistance.
  * **Date** (date): Enter the date you will no longer be receiving assistance.
* **Housing Assistance Provider**: A field to select the provider of your housing assistance.
  * **Provider 1** (select_one): Select your current provider.
  * **Provider 2** (select_one): Select another provider.
  * **Provider 3** (select_one): Select another provider.
  * **Provider 4** (select_one): Select another provider.
  * **Provider 5** (select_one): Select another provider.
  * **Provider 6** (select_one): Select another provider.
  * **Other** (select_one): If none of the above options are your current provider.
* **Additional Notes**: A field for any additional information.
  * **Notes** (note): Enter any additional information you feel is relevant.
