# Mental Health Program Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients for mental health services. Please note that this form is intended for individuals seeking mental health support and services. If you're unsure if this form is for you, please contact us to confirm.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each question.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. Be as detailed as possible when providing information about your mental health history, medical conditions, and previous therapy experiences.
4. If you're unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
5. Submit the form once you've completed all sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Client Data**: This section is for general identifying information about you.
  * **Client Data** (`client_data`, text, required: false): Enter your name, date of birth, and other relevant contact information.
  * **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Describe any medical conditions you have that may be relevant to your mental health.
  * **Family History** (`family_history`, text, required: false): Share information about your family's mental health history.
  * **Mental Health History** (`mental_health_history`, text, required: false): Share your mental health history, including any previous diagnoses or treatment.

- **Mental Health Programs**: This section asks about your experiences with mental health programs.
  * **Mental Health Programs** (`mental_health_programs`, select multiple, required: false): Check all programs you're interested in or have completed.
  * **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, select one, required: false): Choose the language you'd like to communicate in.
  * **Mental Health Provider** (`mental_health_provider`, text, required: false): If applicable, list your current mental health provider.
  * **Mental Health Programs Completed** (`mental_health_programs_completed`, text, required: false): Describe any mental health programs you've completed.
  * **Mental Health Programs In Progress** (`mental_health_programs_in_progress`, text, required: false): Describe any mental health programs you're currently participating in.
  * **Mental Health Programs Planned** (`mental_health_programs_planned`, text, required: false): Share your planned mental health programs or goals.
  * **Mental Health Programs Completed Other** (`mental_health_programs_completed_other`, text, required: false): For programs not listed, please describe them in detail.
  * **Mental Health Programs In Progress Other** (`mental_health_programs_in_progress_other`, text, required: false): For programs not listed, describe them in detail.
  * **Mental Health Programs Planned Other** (`mental_health_programs_planned_other`, text, required: false): For programs not listed, describe them in detail.

- **Emergency Contact**: This section is for emergency contact information.
  * **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false): Identify your emergency contact person.
  * **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, required: false): Provide their contact information.

- **Medical Conditions**: This section is for any medical conditions you'd like to share.
  * **Medical Conditions** (`medical_history_conditions`, text, required: false): List any relevant medical conditions.
  * **Medications** (`medications`, text, required: false): List your current medications.
  * **Mental Health Status** (`mental_health_status`, text, required: false): Describe your current mental health status.
  * **Mental Health Goals** (`mental_health_goals`, text, required: false): Share your mental health goals.

- **Therapy Experiences**: This section is for your therapy experiences.
  * **Current Therapy** (`current_therapy`, text, required: false): Describe your current therapy experiences.
  * **Previous Therapy** (`previous_therapy`, text, required: false): Share your previous therapy experiences.
  * **Mental Health Counseling** (`mental_health_counseling`, text, required: false): Share your mental health counseling experiences.
  * **Counseling Notes** (`counseling_notes`, text, required: false): Record any notes from your counseling sessions.
  * **Mental Health Counseling Sessions** (`mental_health_counseling_sessions`, text, required: false): Share the number of mental health counseling sessions you've had.

- **Additional Information**: This section is for any additional information you'd like to share.
  * **Mental Health Programs Completed Other** (`mental_health_programs_completed_other`, text, required: false): Share programs not listed, in detail.
  * **Mental Health Programs In Progress Other** (`mental_health_programs_in_progress_other`, text, required: false): Share programs not listed, in detail.
  * **Mental Health Programs Planned Other** (`mental_health_programs_planned_other`, text, required: false): Share programs not listed, in detail.
