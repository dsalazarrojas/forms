# tattoo_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The tattoo_inquiry_form is used to capture customer inquiries related to tattoos, providing essential information for processing and responding to customer requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Name** in the text field provided.
2. Enter your **Tattoo Inquiry Number** in the required field.
3. Provide your **Phone Number** (if applicable).
4. Enter your **Email Address** (if applicable).
5. Select your **Tattoo Inquiry Date** from the date picker.
6. Select your **Tattoo Inquiry Time** (if applicable) from the time picker.
7. Enter any additional **Tattoo Inquiry Details** you would like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Tattoo Inquiry Number** (`tattoo_inquiry_number`, number, required: true): This is the unique identifier assigned to your tattoo inquiry.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): If you have a phone number, please enter it so we can contact you.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required: false): If you have an email address, please enter it so we can communicate with you.
* **Tattoo Inquiry Date** (`tattoo_inquiry_date`, date, required: true): Select the date of your tattoo inquiry.
* **Tattoo Inquiry Time** (`tattoo_inquiry_time`, time, required: false): If applicable, select the time of your tattoo inquiry.
* **Tattoo Inquiry Details** (`tattoo_inquiry_details`, text, required: false): Provide any additional information about your tattoo inquiry.
