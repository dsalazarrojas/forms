# Tax Dispute Resolution Services Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Tax Dispute Resolution Services Intake Form is designed to help capture tax dispute-related information and provide a structured approach to resolving tax disputes. This form serves as a guide for authorized representatives or taxpayers to input relevant details about their tax dispute, including the type of dispute, date, and resolution status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully before starting to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Select the appropriate options for each field based on the information relevant to your tax dispute.
3. Enter the tax dispute details, taxpayer information, and any other required fields accurately.
4. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tax Dispute Details (1)**: A text field where you can enter the brief description of the tax dispute. This field is optional.
* **Taxpayer Information (2)**: A select-one field that asks whether you are the taxpayer involved in the dispute. The options are "Yes" or "No".
* **Dispute Type (3)**: A select-one field that helps identify the type of tax dispute. The options are "Yes" or "No", although this field seems contradictory and might need further clarification.
* **Dispute Date (4)**: A date field where you can input the date when the tax dispute occurred. This field is optional.
* **Resolution Method (5)**: A select-one field that indicates how the tax dispute was resolved. The options are "Yes" or "No".
* **Resolution Status (6)**: A select-one field that shows the current resolution status of the tax dispute. The options are "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Tax Authorization (7)**: A select-one field that indicates whether you are authorized to represent the taxpayer. The options are "Yes" or "No".
* **Tax Documents (8)**: A note field where you can attach any supporting tax documents related to the dispute. This field is optional.
* **Client Follow-up (9)**: A text field where you can enter any follow-up information or questions you have regarding the tax dispute. This field is optional.
* **Authorized Representative (10)**: A select-one field that asks whether you are an authorized representative of the taxpayer. The options are "Yes" or "No".
* **Authorized Representative Email (11)**: An email field where you can input the authorized representative's email address. This field is optional.
* **Authorized Representative Phone (12)**: A text field where you can input the authorized representative's phone number. This field is optional.
* **Authorized Representative Extension (13)**: A select-one field that indicates whether you need to extend the authorized representative's contact duration. The options are "Yes" or "No". 

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your authorized representative or tax dispute resolution services team for guidance.
* The form can be submitted multiple times, but please only update the relevant fields that have changed.
* If you have any issues with the form, please report them to your tax dispute resolution services team.
