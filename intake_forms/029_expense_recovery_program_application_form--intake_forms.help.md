# Expense Recovery Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expense Recovery Program Application Form is a document used by employees to request reimbursement for expenses incurred during work-related activities. This form helps to keep track of expenses, ensures transparency, and prevents duplication of claims.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Make sure to select the correct expense type from the options provided.
3. Enter a clear and concise description of the expense.
4. Enter the correct amount of the expense.
5. Select the correct employee who submitted the expense.
6. Select the correct employee who approved the expense.
7. Enter the date and time of submission and approval.
8. Add any relevant notes or comments if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User (1)**: Enter your name.
* **Company (2)**: Enter your company name.
* **Date of Claim (3)**: Enter the date when you incurred the expense in MM/DD/YY format.
* **Expense Type (4)**: Select the type of expense (Business Trip, Business Meeting, or Other).
* **Description (5)**: Enter a clear and concise description of the expense.
* **Amount (6)**: Enter the total amount of the expense.
* **Submitted By (7)**: Select the employee who submitted the expense (Jane Doe or John Doe).
* **Approved By (8)**: Select the employee who approved the expense (Jane Doe or John Doe).
* **Submitted (9)**: Enter the date and time of submission.
* **Approved (10)**: Enter the date and time of approval.
* **Notes (11)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the expense.
* **Email (12)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (13)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address (14)**: Enter your address.
* **Date Claimed (15)**: Enter the date you are claiming the expense.
* **Claimant Signature (16)**: Enter your signature.
* **Approver Signature (17)**: Enter the approver's signature.
* **Approver (18)**: Select the employee who approved the expense (Jane Doe or John Doe).
* **Date Approved (19)**: Enter the date of approval.
* **Reason for Rejection (20)**: Enter the reason for rejection if the expense was denied.
* **Reason (21)**: Enter any additional reason or explanation.
* **Notes Approver (22)**: Enter any additional notes or comments from the approver.
* **Comments (23)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback.
* **Approver Comments (24)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback from the approver.
