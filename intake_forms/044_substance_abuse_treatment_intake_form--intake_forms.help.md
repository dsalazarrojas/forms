# Substance Abuse Treatment Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential information from individuals seeking substance abuse treatment. This information will be used to provide personalized care and treatment for the individual.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **User Information**: Please provide your basic contact information, including your name, address, phone number, and email. This will help us to contact you for any follow-up questions or to schedule appointments.
2. **Contact Information**: Enter your contact information, including your emergency contact person's name and phone number. This will help us to reach out to your emergency contact in case of an emergency.
3. **Medical History**: Describe your medical history, including any pre-existing medical conditions or allergies that may impact your treatment.
4. **Substance Abuse History**: Provide details about your substance abuse history, including the substances you have used and how long you have used them.
5. **Treatment History**: Describe your previous treatment experiences, including any successes or challenges you have faced.
6. **Family Background**: Share information about your family, including any family history of substance abuse or mental health issues.
7. **Mental Health**: Discuss your mental health history, including any previous diagnoses or treatments you have received.
8. **Legal History**: Disclose any legal issues related to substance abuse or mental health treatment.
9. **Medical Treatment**: If you have received medical treatment in the past, provide details about your treatment, including the date and duration of treatment.
10. **Discharge Information**: If you have previously been discharged from treatment, provide information about your discharge date and reason.
