# Mpox Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from patients for the diagnosis and treatment of Mpox. Please follow this guide to complete the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand the questions asked.
2. Select the exposed areas where you were bitten or scratched by a rodent.
3. Describe your symptoms and any prophetic symptoms you are experiencing.
4. Share your travel history for the last two weeks.
5. List any contact traces, if applicable.
6. Provide the date you were exposed to Mpox.
7. If you have a healthcare provider, please enter their name and contact information.
8. Enter your phone number and email address for further contact.
9. Select the medical facility where you would like to be treated.
10. Specify who submitted the form on your behalf (e.g., patient, physician, or other).
11. Select the time of submission.
12. Double-check all information provided before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Exposed Areas** (Exposed Areas): A list of areas where you were bitten or scratched by a rodent.
- **Symptoms and Prophets** (Symptoms and Prophets): List any symptoms you are experiencing, such as fever, rash, swelling, muscle ache, etc.
- **Travel History** (Travel History): Share your travel history for the last two weeks, including countries and cities visited.
- **Contact Traces** (Contact Traces): List any contact traces, if applicable.
- **Exposure Date** (Exposure Date): Enter the date you were exposed to Mpox.
- **Healthcare Provider** (Healthcare Provider): If you have a healthcare provider, enter their name and contact information.
- **Phone Number** (Phone Number): Enter your contact phone number.
- **Email** (Email): Enter your email address.
- **Prophetic Symptoms** (Prophetic Symptoms): Select any prophetic symptoms you are experiencing, such as fever, rash, etc.
- **Medical Facility** (Medical Facility): Select the medical facility where you would like to be treated.
- **Submitted By** (Submitted By): Specify who submitted the form on your behalf (e.g., patient, physician, or other).
- **Submitted Time** (Submitted Time): Select the time of submission.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and complete information to ensure proper treatment and diagnosis.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult the healthcare provider or a relevant authority.
* Double-check all information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
