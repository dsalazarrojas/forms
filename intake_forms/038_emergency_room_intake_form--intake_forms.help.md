# emergency_room_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient information for emergency room intake. It is intended to be used by patients to provide essential details to medical staff during emergency visits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Fill out each field** with your personal details and medical history.
2. **Be as thorough as possible** in providing accurate information to help the medical staff assist you.
3. **Submit the form** once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Patient Info**
* **Patient info** (`patient_info`, `text`, required/optional): Please fill in your personal details, such as your name, date of birth, and any other relevant information.
* **Medical info** (`medical_info`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your current medical conditions, allergies, or medications that may be relevant to your emergency room visit.
* **Medical history** (`medical_history`, `text`, required/optional): Share any relevant medical history or previous hospitalizations that may affect your treatment.
* **Contact info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact information, such as your address and phone number, so we can stay in touch with you.
* **Emergency contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, `text`, required/optional): List any emergency contacts, such as family members or caregivers, who we can reach out to in case of an emergency.
