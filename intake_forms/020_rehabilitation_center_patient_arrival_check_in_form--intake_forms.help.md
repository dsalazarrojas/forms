# Rehabilitation Center Patient Arrival Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect patient information for rehabilitation center arrivals.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete this form:

1. Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth.
3. Enter the patient's contact number.
4. Select the arrival time of the patient.
5. Enter the date of the patient's visit.
6. Indicate if the patient has a therapist assigned to them.
7. Enter the appointment number for the patient.
8. Enter the patient's ID.
9. Enter any additional notes about the patient's visit.
10. Indicate who submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, Required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Patient Dob** (`patient_dob`, date, Optional): Enter the patient's date of birth in the correct date format.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, Optional): Enter the patient's contact number.
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time, Optional): Select the arrival time of the patient.
* **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, date, Optional): Enter the date of the patient's visit.
* **Therapist** (`therapist`, select_one, Optional): Indicate if the patient has a therapist assigned to them.
* **Appointment Number** (`appointment_number`, text, Optional): Enter the appointment number for the patient.
* **Patient Id** (`patient_id`, text, Optional): Enter the patient's ID.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, Optional): Enter any additional notes about the patient's visit.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, select_multiple, Optional): Indicate who submitted the form.
