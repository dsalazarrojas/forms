# Food Photo Nutrition Analysis Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a food item from a photo. This data will be used for nutrition analysis and comparison.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Take a clear and high-quality photo of the food item.
2.  Upload the photo URL.
3.  Fill out the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
4.  Review the form for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <table>
| Field Label | Field Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Food Details** | This field is where you can enter any additional information about the food item, such as its name and description. |
| **Upload Photo** | Enter the URL of the photo of the food item. Please ensure the photo is clear and high-quality. |
| **Date of Intake** | Enter the date when the food was taken into the database. |
| **Serving Size** | Enter the weight of the serving portion of the food item. Please ensure it is in a valid numeric value. |
| **Food Name** | Enter the name of the food item. If it's a generic or unknown name, please provide any available information. |
| **Nutrition Review** | Enter a brief review or note about the nutrition of the food item, if available. |
| **Comments** | Enter any additional comments about the food item, such as its origin or processing methods. |
| **Photo Upload URL** | Enter the URL of the uploaded photo of the food item. Please ensure it is a valid URL. |
| **Serving Weight** | Enter the weight of the serving portion of the food item. Please ensure it is a valid numeric value. |
| **Food Category** | Select one category for the food item from the list: Fruits, Vegetables, Grains, Dairy, Protein, or Other. This will help in classifying and comparing the food items. |
| **Serving Portion** | Select one or multiple serving options from the list: Yes, No. This will indicate if the food item is served as a portion size or not. |

### Tips
*   Ensure the photo is clear and high-quality for accurate analysis.
*   Enter accurate and valid data for proper classification and comparison.
*   Do not resubmit existing data.
