# IVF Patient Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about patients undergoing in-vitro fertilization (IVF) treatments. The form will help our clinic's medical team gather essential details about the patient's medical history, insurance, and other relevant information to provide them with the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are in a quiet and private area where you can focus on filling out the form accurately.
2. Read each section carefully and answer all questions thoroughly.
3. Enter your patient's information in the relevant fields, and make sure to select the correct options for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Patient Name (`patient_name`)
 * This is the name of the patient, and it should be entered exactly as it appears on their identification documents.

### 2. Date of Birth (`date_of_birth`)
 * Please enter the patient's date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD or MM/DD/YY).

### 3. Email (`email`)
 * This is the patient's email address, where we can contact them for any updates or questions.

### 4. Phone (`phone`)
 * Enter the patient's phone number, including the country code and any other relevant details.

### 5. Address (`address`)
 * Provide the patient's home address, including the street address, city, state, and zip code.

### 6. Medical History (`medical_history`)
 * Describe the patient's medical history, including any relevant medical conditions, allergies, or previous treatments.

### 7. Consent (`consent`)
 * Confirm that the patient has given their consent for our medical team to access their information and provide them with treatment.

### 8. Other Consents (`other_consents`)
 * If the patient has given consent for other treatments or medical procedures, select them from the list.

### 9. Medical Insurance (`medical_insurance`)
 * Confirm if the patient has medical insurance, and if so, provide the insurance provider's name.

### 10. Insurance Provider (`insurance_provider`)
 * Enter the name and contact information of the patient's insurance provider, if applicable.

### 11. Patient Notes (`patient_notes`)
 * Add any additional notes or comments about the patient, such as previous medical conditions or concerns.

Tips:

* Please double-check that all fields are filled out accurately before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact our clinic's staff for guidance.
* Remember to keep all information confidential and secure.
