# intramuscular_prescription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The intramuscular_prescription_form is designed to collect patient data and prescription information from medical professionals for intramuscular injections. This form aims to standardize the intake process for healthcare providers, ensuring accurate and complete patient information is collected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's data in the "patient_data" field, including any relevant medical history or allergies.
2. Provide the prescription information in the "prescription_info" field, including the medication name, dosage, and any specific instructions.
3. If necessary, provide additional details about the prescription in the "prescription_details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Data (patient_data)**: Enter the patient's name, medical history, or any relevant medical conditions that need to be considered when administering the intramuscular injection.
* **Prescription Info (prescription_info)**: Enter the name of the medication, dosage, and any special instructions or precautions that need to be taken.
* **Prescription Details (prescription_details)**: Use this field to provide any additional information about the prescription, such as specific medical conditions that necessitate the intramuscular injection or any other relevant details.

## Tips
* Double-check the patient's data for accuracy before proceeding with the prescription.
* Ensure that you have all necessary information about the medication and the patient's medical history before administering the intramuscular injection.
* If you're unsure about any part of the prescription or patient data, consult with a colleague or a medical professional for guidance.
