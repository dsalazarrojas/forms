# Hypnotherapy Intake And Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather essential information from clients before their hypnotherapy consultation. The form aims to understand the client's background, preferences, and requirements for the session, ensuring a successful and effective consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your information.
2. Provide as much detail as you can, but do not worry if some fields are not applicable.
3. If you need help or clarification, do not hesitate to reach out to your hypnotherapist.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **client_info** (`client_info`, text, optional): A brief introduction or background information about yourself.
* **name** (`name`, text, optional): Your name (first and last names).
* **date_of_birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Your date of birth (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **contact_details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Your contact information (email, phone number, address, etc.).
* **address** (`address`, text, optional): Your home address.
* **medical_history** (`medical_history`, text, optional): A brief account of your medical history, including any allergies or health conditions.
* **medication** (`medication`, text, optional): Any medications you are currently taking.
* **medical_condition** (`medical_condition`, select_one, required): Please select the medical condition you are experiencing (Anxiety, Depression, ADHD, or Other).
* **therapist_info** (`therapist_info`, text, optional): Any relevant information about your previous hypnotherapy sessions or experiences.
* **name_of_thereapist** (`name_of_thereapist`, text, optional): The name of your hypnotherapist (if known).
* **experience** (`experience`, number, optional): The number of sessions you have attended before.
* **consultation_goals** (`consultation_goals`, text, optional): Your goals and expectations for the consultation.
* **session_frequency** (`session_frequency`, number, optional): The frequency of sessions you would like to attend (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, etc.).
* **session_length** (`session_length`, number, optional): The length of each session you would like to attend (e.g., 45 minutes, 1 hour, etc.).
* **follow_up** (`follow_up`, text, optional): Any follow-up information or instructions from your hypnotherapist.
* **next_appointment** (`next_appointment`, date, optional): The date and time of your next appointment.
* **appointment_time** (`appointment_time`, time, optional): The time of your next appointment.
* **notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Any notes or comments from your hypnotherapist.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Your email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Your phone number.
* **additional_comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Any additional comments or information about your consultation.
* **signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Your signature (optional but recommended for official purposes).
