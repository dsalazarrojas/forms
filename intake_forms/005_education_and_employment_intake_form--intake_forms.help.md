# Education And Employment Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a person's education and employment background, including work experience, education, relevant coursework and projects, certifications, skills, volunteer activities, language abilities, and previous college or university attended information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out each section of the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Ensure that you provide the most up-to-date and relevant information.
3. Review each section carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Education And Employment Intake Form
* **Education And Employment Intake Form** (`education_and_employment_intake_form_page`, text, not required): This is the title of the form.
### Education And Employment Background
* **Education And Employment Background** (`education_and_employment_background`, text, not required): Please provide a brief description of your education and employment background.
### Education
* **Education** (`education_and_employment_education`, text, not required): Describe your educational background, including any relevant coursework or projects.
### Education And Employment Education 1
* **Education And Employment Education 1** (`education_and_employment_education_1`, text, not required): Describe your education and employment history for the first instance.
### Employment
* **Employment** (`education_and_employment_employment`, text, not required): Describe your employment history, including job title and duration.
### Education And Employment Employment 1
* **Education And Employment Employment 1** (`education_and_employment_employment_1`, text, not required): Describe your employment history for the first instance.
### Education And Employment Employment 2
* **Education And Employment Employment 2** (`education_and_employment_employment_2`, text, not required): Describe your employment history for the second instance.
### Previous Positions
* **Previous Positions** (`education_and_employment_previous_positions`, select_multiple, not required): Select the years of experience you had in your previous positions.
	+ None
	+ 1-2 years
	+ 2-5 years
	+ 5-10 years
	+ 10+ years
### Duration Of Previous Position
* **Duration Of Previous Position** (`education_and_employment_previous_positions_duration`, date, not required): Enter the duration of your previous position if you selected a specific number of years in the previous field.
### Description Of Previous Position
* **Description Of Previous Position** (`education_and_employment_previous_positions_description`, text, not required): Describe your previous position in as much detail as possible.
### Relevant Coursework Projects Or Research
* **Relevant Coursework Projects Or Research** (`education_and_employment_relevant_coursework_projects_or_research`, text, not required): List any relevant coursework, projects, or research you have completed.
### Relevant Professional Certifications
* **Relevant Professional Certifications** (`education_and_employment_relevant_professional_certifications`, text, not required): List any relevant professional certifications you hold.
### Relevant Skills
* **Relevant Skills** (`education_and_employment_relevant_skills`, text, not required): List any relevant skills you possess.
### Relevant Volunteer Activities
* **Relevant Volunteer Activities** (`education_and_employment_relevant_volunteer_activities`, text, not required): List any relevant volunteer activities you have participated in.
### Relevant Language Abilities
* **Relevant Language Abilities** (`education_and_employment_relevant_language_abilities`, select_one, not required): Select the languages you are proficient in.
	+ English
	+ French
	+ Spanish
	+ Mandarin
	+ Other
### Relevant Proficiency Levels
* **Relevant Proficiency Levels** (`education_and_employment_relevant_proficiency_levels`, select_one, not required): Select your proficiency level in each language you selected.
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Proficient
	+ Native
	+ Other
### Previous College Or University Attended
* **Previous College Or University Attended** (`education_and_employment_previous_college_or_university_attended`, text, not required): Describe the college or university you attended.
### Previous College Or University Attended Degree
* **Previous College Or University Attended Degree** (`education_and_employment_previous_college_or_university_attended_degree`, select_one, not required): Select the degree you earned.
	+ Bachelor
	+ Master
	+ PhD
	+ Other
### Previous College Or University Attended Institution
* **Previous College Or University Attended Institution** (`education_and_employment_previous_college_or_university_attended_institution`, text, not required): Describe the institution where you earned your degree.
### Previous College Or University Attended Graduated
* **Previous College Or University Attended Graduated** (`education_and_employment_previous_college_or_university_attended_graduated`, date, not required): Enter the date you graduated, if applicable.
### Previous College Or University Attended Graduated Year
* **Year** (`education_and_employment_previous_college_or_university_attended_graduated_year`, number, not required): Enter the year you graduated.
### Previous College Or University Attended Graduated Month
* **Month** (`education_and_employment_previous_college_or_university_attended_graduated_month`, number, not required): Enter the month you graduated.
### Previous College Or University Attended Graduated Day
* **Day** (`education_and_employment_previous_college_or_university_attended_gradaded_day`, number, not required): Enter the day you graduated.
### Previous College Or University Attended Graduated Time
* **Time** (`education_and_employment_previous_college_or_university_attended_gradaded_time`, time, not required): Enter the time you graduated.
### Previous College Or University Attended Graduated AM/PM
* **AM/PM** (`education_and_employment_previous_college_or_university_attended_gradaded_am_pm`, select_one, not required): Select the time of day you graduated.
	+ AM
	+ PM
	+ Other

## Tips
* Ensure that you answer each question accurately and thoroughly.
