# website_client_requirement_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The website_client_requirement_intake_form is designed to collect information from clients about their website requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the 'Client Info' field with your name and contact information.
2. Provide a detailed description of your project requirements in the 'Project Requirements' field.
3. Fill out the 'Technical Details' field with any specific technical requirements for your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Info**: A text field for entering your name and contact information.
* **Project Requirements**: A text field for describing your project's objectives and expectations.
* **Technical Details**: A text field for specifying any technical requirements for your project.
