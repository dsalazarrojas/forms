# mental health information form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect mental health information to help healthcare professionals provide targeted support.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Complete the Intake Form (Page 1)
- Please fill in your details in the text box.

### Step 2: Confirm Your Mental Health Support Needs (Page 1)
- Select 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm if you need mental health support.
- If you select 'Yes', please provide more information in the mental health history, goals, and concerns text boxes.

### Step 3: Specify Your Mental Health Goals and History (Page 1)
- In the mental health history text box, describe your mental health history in a few sentences.
- In the mental health goals text box, describe your mental health goals.
- In the mental health concerns text box, specify your mental health concerns.

### Step 4: Confirm Your Mental Health Assistance and Services Needs (Page 2)
- Select 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm if you need mental health goals assistance and support services.
- Select 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm if you need mental health crisis services.

### Tips
- Please provide clear and concise answers to each question.
- If you need help or have questions, refer back to this guide or consult with a healthcare professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intake Form** (Page 1, text): Please fill in your details here, including your name, date of birth, and any other relevant information.
* **Mental Health Support** (Page 1, text): This is where you confirm if you need mental health support. Select 'Yes' if you need help with your mental health.
* **Mental Health Support Needed** (Page 1, select one): This is where you specify if you need mental health support. If you select 'Yes', please provide more information in the mental health history, goals, and concerns text boxes.
* **Mental Health History** (Page 1, text): This is where you describe your mental health history, including any past experiences or treatments.
* **Mental Health Goals** (Page 1, text): This is where you describe your mental health goals, including what you hope to achieve.
* **Mental Health Concerns** (Page 1, text): This is where you specify your mental health concerns, including any areas of difficulty or worry.
* **Mental Health Goals Assistance** (Page 2, select multiple): This is where you confirm if you need mental health goals assistance. Select 'Yes' if you need help achieving your goals.
* **Mental Health Support Services** (Page 2, select multiple): This is where you confirm if you need mental health support services. Select 'Yes' if you need help with services such as counseling or therapy.
* **Mental Health Crisis Services** (Page 2, select multiple): This is where you confirm if you need mental health crisis services. Select 'Yes' if you are experiencing a crisis and need immediate help.
