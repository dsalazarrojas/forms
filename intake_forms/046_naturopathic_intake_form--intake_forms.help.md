# Naturopathic Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from patients for Naturopathic consultations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields.
2. Choose the correct medical condition that applies to you.
3. Provide any medical history or allergies that you have.
4. Enter your treatment goals and preferred treatment duration.
5. Select your preferred payment method and type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter your full name as it is written on any identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Medical Condition**: Select the medical condition that you are experiencing (e.g. Migraines and Headaches).
* **Medication**: If you are currently taking any medication, enter the name and dosage of each medication.
* **Allergy**: If you have any allergies, enter the type and severity of each allergy.
* **Medical History**: Enter any significant medical history that you have, including previous treatments and conditions.
* **Family History**: Enter any significant medical history in your family, including your parents and siblings.
* **Medical History Family Member**: Enter any medical history of a family member.
* **Treatment Plan**: Enter a brief description of the treatment you are seeking.
* **Treatment Goals**: Enter your treatment goals, such as reducing pain or improving overall health.
* **Treatment Duration**: Select the desired duration of your treatment.
* **Treatment Frequency**: Enter the number of times you would like to come in for treatment per week.
* **Practitioner Name**: Enter the name of the practitioner you would like to work with.
* **Practitioner License**: Enter the type of license that your practitioner has (optional).
* **Practitioner Contact Info**: Enter the contact information for your practitioner.
* **Payment Type**: Select the method of payment you would like to use (e.g. cash, insurance, etc.).
* **Payment Method**: Select the specific method of payment you would like to use (e.g. check, credit card, etc.).
* **Insurance**: If you have insurance, enter your insurance provider and policy number.
* **Policy ID**: Enter your insurance policy ID number.
* **Insurance Effective Date**: Enter the effective date of your insurance policy.
* **Insurance Provider**: Enter the name of your insurance provider.
* **Billing Address**: Enter the address where you would like to receive your bills.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the address where you would like to receive any shipments.
* **Medical Record Number**: Enter the number of your medical record.
* **Date**: Enter the date of your appointment.
* **Time**: Enter the time of your appointment.
* **Practitioner Naturopathic License**: Enter the type of license that your practitioner has.
* **Naturopathic Doctor**: Enter the name of your naturopathic doctor.
* **Insurance Provider**: Enter the name of your insurance provider.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have all necessary information and documents before completing this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your practitioner for guidance.
* This form is for your personal medical consultation, so please ensure that you are filling it out for yourself.
