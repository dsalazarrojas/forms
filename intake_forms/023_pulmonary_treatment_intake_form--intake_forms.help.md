# Pulmonary Treatment Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pulmonary Treatment Intake Form is a patient information sheet designed to capture a patient's medical history, current medical conditions, and treatment goals. This form is meant to provide a comprehensive understanding of the patient's pulmonary health, enabling healthcare professionals to better assess and tailor treatment plans accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Enter Patient Details**: Answer the questions in the `patient_details` section to provide basic information about the patient.
2.  **List Symptom History**: Describe any symptoms related to the patient's respiratory system in the `symptom_history` section.
3.  **Document Medication History**: List the patient's current medications, including any asthma, cystic fibrosis, or pneumonia medications in the `medication_history` section.
4.  **Medical History**: Provide a brief overview of the patient's medical history in the `medical_history` section.
5.  **Allergy History**: Describe any allergies the patient has in the `allergy_history` section.
6.  **List Medical Conditions**: Select any relevant medical conditions (e.g., Cystic Fibrosis, Asthma, Pneumonia) in the `medical_conditions` section.
7.  **List Medications**: Select any medications the patient is currently taking related to their medical conditions in the `medication_list` section.
8.  **Other Medical Conditions**: If the patient has any other medical conditions not listed, describe them in the `medical_conditions_other` section.
9.  **Treatment Goals**: Explain the patient's treatment goals and objectives in the `treatment_goals` section.
10. **Consent**: Indicate whether the patient has granted consent to receive treatment or further information. Select either 'True' or 'False' for the `consent` field.
11.  **Consent Details**: If the patient has granted consent, provide any additional details regarding their consent in the `consent_details` section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Details (1)**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information to help healthcare professionals identify the correct patient and contact them.
* **Symptom History (2)**: Describe any symptoms related to the patient's respiratory system, such as coughing, difficulty breathing, or wheezing.
* **Medication History (3)**: List any medications the patient is currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.
* **Medical History (4)**: Provide a brief overview of the patient's medical history, including any previous surgeries or health conditions.
* **Allergy History (5)**: Describe any allergies the patient has, including any reactions or sensitivities.
* **Medical Conditions (6)**: Select any relevant medical conditions from the list provided, such as Cystic Fibrosis, Asthma, or Pneumonia.
* **Medications (7)**: Select any medications the patient is taking that are related to their medical conditions.
* **Other Medical Conditions (8)**: If the patient has any other medical conditions not listed, provide a description.
* **Treatment Goals (9)**: Explain the patient's treatment goals and objectives, such as improving lung function or managing symptoms.
* **Consent (10)**: Indicate whether the patient has granted consent to receive treatment or further information.
* **Consent Details (11)**: If the patient has granted consent, provide any additional details regarding their consent.
