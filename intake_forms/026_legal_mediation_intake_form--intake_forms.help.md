# Legal Mediation Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Mediation Intake Form is a tool used to gather essential information from individuals seeking legal mediation services. This form helps our team understand your specific needs and requirements for the mediation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review each page carefully to understand the questions and fields that follow.
2.  Enter your contact information, which includes your name and any other relevant contact details.
3.  Provide information about the party involved in the mediation, if applicable.
4.  List all issues that need to be addressed in the mediation process.
5.  Select the individuals involved in the mediation process.
6.  Choose your preferred contact method for future communication.
7.  Describe your legal background if necessary.
8.  Specify your goals for the mediation process.
9.  Select your primary goal for mediation from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Information
*  **Contact Information** (id: 1):
  +   Label: Contact Information
  +   Type: text
  +   This field is used to collect your contact information, including your name and any other relevant details. Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.

### Party Information
*  **Party Information** (id: 2):
  +   Label: Party Information
  +   Type: text
  +   This field is used to provide information about the party involved in the mediation process, if applicable.

### Legal Mediation Issues
*  **Legal Mediation Issues** (id: 3):
  +   Label: Legal Mediation Issues
  +   Type: select_multiple
  +   Options: 'Yes', 'No'
  +   This field is used to list all issues that need to be addressed in the mediation process. Please select all relevant options.

### Party Participants
*  **Party Participants** (id: 4):
  +   Label: Party Participants
  +   Type: select_multiple
  +   Options: 'Yes', 'No'

### Contact Preference
*  **Contact Preference** (id: 5):
  +   Label: Contact Preference
  +   Type: select_one

  +   This field is used to choose your preferred contact method for future communication.

### Contact Details

  +   Label: Contact Details

### Legal Background

### Mediation Goals

  +   This field is used to specify your goals for the mediation process. Please select all relevant options.
