# Financial Counseling Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Counseling Intake Form is a collection of questions designed to gather essential information from clients to provide personalized financial guidance. This form helps our financial advisors understand your financial situation, goals, and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Client Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. **Contact Number**: If you have a phone number, please provide it so we can reach you for follow-up conversations.
3. **Email Address**: This is the email address we will use to communicate with you about your financial plan and progress.
4. **Financial Advisor**: Indicate if you currently have a financial advisor or if you need help finding one.
5. **Counselor Date**: If you have a specific date for your financial counseling session, please let us know.
6. **Available Time**: Choose the times you are available for your counseling session.
7. **Financial History**: Provide a brief overview of your current financial situation, including any notable expenses, income, or debts.
8. **Financial Goals**: Describe your short-term and long-term financial goals, such as saving for a specific purpose or paying off debt.
9. **Income Range**: Indicate your current income range (e.g., below poverty line, middle-class, or higher-income bracket).
10. **Assets Range**: Select the types of assets you own or have access to (e.g., cash, property, investments).
11. **Debt Range**: Indicate the types of debt you have (e.g., credit cards, loans, mortgages).
12. **Financial Status**: Describe your current financial status, including any financial challenges you're facing.
