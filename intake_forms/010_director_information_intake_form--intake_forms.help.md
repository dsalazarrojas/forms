# Director Information Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Director Information Intake Form is designed to collect personal and professional details from directors, which will be used for various purposes such as communication, record-keeping, and reporting. It is crucial that you provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that your data is correct and reliable.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review the fields for any additional details or instructions provided in the hints.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents, such as your passport or driver's license.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Select your department from the list provided, if it is applicable.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your official email address that you regularly use for communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted or if you need to contact us.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): Enter your job title or position.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `note`, optional): Enter a brief description of your job or responsibilities.

Note: This form is for the directors to provide information about themselves. If you are not a director, please do not fill out this form.
