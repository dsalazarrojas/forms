# Tax Debt Bankruptcy Discharge Eligibility Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tax Debt Bankruptcy Discharge Eligibility Intake Form is a tool to help individuals assess their eligibility for bankruptcy discharge. This form gathers information about tax debt, bankruptcy details, and other relevant factors to determine whether an individual is eligible for discharge.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Tax Debt Details" section, providing information about your tax debt, including the tax year and debt amount.
2. Select the relevant bankruptcy details, such as Chapter 7, Chapter 11, or Chapter 13.
3. List the financial institutions involved in your tax debt.
4. Provide your current tax debt status.
5. Enter the date of your bankruptcy filing and discharge.
6. Add any additional notes or comments about your situation.
7. Confirm whether you have reported your tax debt to the relevant authorities.
8. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
9. Select the person reporting this information.
10. Choose the correct intake form.
11. Confirm who submitted the form.
12. Enter the date of submission.
13. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tax Debt Details (1)** (`tax_debt_details`, text, required): This section requires information about your tax debt, including the tax year and debt amount.
* **Tax Year (2)** (`tax_year`, number, required): Please enter the tax year related to your tax debt.
* **Bankruptcy Details (3)** (`bankruptcy_details`, select_one, required): Select the relevant bankruptcy chapter: Chapter 7, Chapter 11, or Chapter 13.
* **Financial Institutions Involved (4)** (`financial_institutions_involved`, select_multiple, required): List the financial institutions involved in your tax debt.
* **Debt Amount (5)** (`debt_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of your tax debt.
* **Tax Debt Status (6)** (`tax_debt_status`, select_one, required): Select your current tax debt status: active, inactive, or pending.
* **Bankruptcy Filing Date (7)** (`bankruptcy_filing_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your bankruptcy filing.
* **Discharge Date (8)** (`discharge_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your bankruptcy discharge.
* **Notes (9)** (`notes`, note, required): Add any additional comments or notes about your situation.
* **Reported (10)** (`reported`, select_one, required): Confirm whether you have reported your tax debt to the relevant authorities.
* **Email (11)** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone (12)** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Reported By (13)** (`reported_by`, select_one, required): Select the person reporting this information.
* **Intake Form (14)** (`intake_form`, select_one, required): Choose the correct intake form.
* **Submitted By (15)** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required): Confirm who submitted the form.
* **Submission Date (16)** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date of submission.
* **Signature (17)** (`signature`, note, required): Sign the form to confirm your information.
* **Signature Date (18)** (`signature_date`, date, required): Date the signature.
* **Signed By (19)** (`signed_by`, select_one, required): Select the person signing the form.
* **Verified By (20)** (`verified_by`, select_one, required): Select the person verifying the form.
* **Verified Date (21)** (`verified_date`, date, required): Enter the date of verification.
* **Reported (22)** (`reported`, select_one, required): Confirm whether the form has been reported.
