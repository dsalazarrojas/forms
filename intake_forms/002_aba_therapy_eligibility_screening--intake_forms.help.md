# ABA Therapy Eligibility Screening - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to determine a patient's eligibility for ABA therapy, a treatment approach for individuals with autism spectrum disorder or other behavioral concerns. This form gathers essential information about the patient's personal details, medical history, and reasons for referral.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the patient's personal information (Patient full name, Date of birth, Guardian or parent name, and Contact phone number).
2. Provide a brief description of the Reason for referral.
3. Select any Developmental concerns that apply to the patient.
4. List any Previous diagnoses or evaluations (if applicable).
5. Choose the Preferred service model (In clinic, In home, or Telehealth).
6. Provide Availability notes for initial sessions (if applicable).
7. Determine the Screening conclusion (Eligible for ABA assessment, Not eligible at this time, or Requires further evaluation).
8. Include Screening notes and recommendations from the clinician.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient full name**: Enter the patient's legal name.
* **Date of birth**: Enter the date of birth for age verification.
* **Guardian or parent name**: If the patient is under 18, enter the name of the primary caregiver.
* **Contact phone number**: Enter the best phone number to reach the guardian or patient.
* **Reason for referral**: Provide a brief description of the presenting concerns.
* **Developmental concerns**: Select any areas of concern (Communication, Social skills, Repetitive behaviors, Adaptive skills, Attention and focus).
* **Previous diagnoses or evaluations**: List any prior diagnoses or professional evaluations.
* **Insurance provider**: Enter the primary insurance for billing (if applicable).
* **Preferred service model**: Choose the preferred mode of therapy delivery (In clinic, In home, or Telehealth).
* **Availability notes**: Provide days and times available for initial sessions (if applicable).
* **Screening conclusion**: Determine if the patient is Eligible for ABA assessment, Not eligible at this time, or Requires further evaluation.
* **Screening notes and recommendations**: Include any observations and next steps from the clinician.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, do not hesitate to reach out to the relevant authorities.
