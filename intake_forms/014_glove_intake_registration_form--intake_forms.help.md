# Glove Intake Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about glove intake registrations for medical or industrial purposes.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the department from the options provided.
* Specify the quantity of gloves needed.
* Select an assigned individual to receive the gloves.
* Enter the intake time in the correct time zone (UTC).
* Enter the intake date in the format MM/DD/YY.
* Enter a phone number for communication.
* Enter an email for communication (optional).
* Enter the submitted time (UTC).
* Enter the submitted offset information (UTC).

* **Quantity**: This field is for specifying the number of gloves needed.
* **Notes**: This is an optional field for any additional comments or details.
* **Assigned To**: This field allows you to select the individual assigned to receive the gloves. Please ensure that you select the correct individual.
* **Intake Date**: This field collects the date when the gloves were taken in. Please ensure that you enter the correct date in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Email**: This field is for entering an email address for communication (if available).
* **Phone**: This field is for entering a phone number for communication (if available).
* **Submitted At**: This field collects the time when the form was submitted.
* **Submitted To**: This field allows you to select the individual to whom the form was submitted.

* **Submitted By Phone**: This field is for entering a phone number for the individual who submitted the form (if available).
* **Submitted At UTC Offset**: This field collects the offset information for the submitted time (UTC).
