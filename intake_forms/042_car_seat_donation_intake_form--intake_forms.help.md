# Car Seat Donation Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the car seat you are donating, ensuring that the seat is safe and suitable for use in our program. Please answer all questions accurately and truthfully.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. For each field, use the provided options or type in the information requested.
3. If a field is not applicable, leave it blank or mark the "N/A" option.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Donor Information (Page 1)
* **Donor Information** (Donor Information, note, optional): This is a note section for your contact details.
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Your last name.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): For donation communications.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required): Your contact number.
* **Street Address** (Street Address, text, required): Your address.
* **City** (City, text, required): Your city.
* **State** (State, text, required): Your state.
* **ZIP Code** (ZIP Code, text, required): Your ZIP code.

### Donor Information (Page 2)
* **Donation Details** (Donation Details, note, optional): Information about the car seat.
* **Donation Date** (Donation Date, date, required): When are you donating?
* **Number of Car Seats** (Number of Car Seats, select_one, required): How many seats?
* **Seat Type** (Seat Type, select_one, required): Type of car seat.
* **Seat Brand** (Seat Brand, text, optional): Manufacturer.
* **Seat Model** (Seat Model, text, optional): Model name/number.
* **Seat Condition** (Seat Condition, select_one, required): Overall condition.
* **Manufacturing Date** (Manufacturing Date, text, optional): If known.
* **Expiration Date** (Expiration Date, date, optional): Usually 6-10 years from manufacture.
* **Seat History** (Seat History, note, optional): Important safety information.

### Donor Information (Page 3)
* **Accident History** (Accident History, select_one, required): Was the seat in an accident?
* **Recall Status** (Recall Status, select_one, required): Any recalls?
* **Reason for Donation** (Reason for Donation, select_one, required): Why are you donating?
* **Accessories Included** (Accessories Included, select_multiple, optional): What comes with the seat?
* **Cleanliness** (Cleanliness, select_one, required): Has it been cleaned?
* **Visible Damage** (Visible Damage, select_one, required): Any damage?
* **Damage Description** (Damage Description, text, optional): Describe any damage.
* **Missing Parts** (Missing Parts, text, optional): List any missing parts.

### Donor Information (Page 4)
* **Drop-Off or Pickup** (Drop-Off or Pickup, select_one, required): How will you donate?
* **Drop-Off Location** (Drop-Off Location, select_one, optional): Preferred location.
* **Pickup Address** (Pickup Address, text, optional): If pickup needed.
* **Preferred Pickup Time** (Preferred Pickup Time, time, optional): What time?
* **Donation Receipt** (Donation Receipt, select_one, required): Do you need a receipt?
* **Estimated Value** (Estimated Value, number, optional): For tax purposes.
* **Volunteer Interest** (Volunteer Interest, select_one, optional): Would you like to volunteer?
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (How Did You Hear About Us, select_one, optional): Your referral source.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, text, optional): Any other information.

### Terms and Conditions (Page 5)
* **Terms Agreement** (Terms Agreement, select_one, required): Confirm donation terms.
* **Submission Date** (Submission Date, date, required): Today's date.

## Tips
* Please review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.
* We appreciate your donation and your contribution to our program!
