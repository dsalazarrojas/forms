# Data Privacy Violation Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Data Privacy Violation Intake Form is used to document and investigate incidents related to data privacy violations, such as data breaches or misuse. It is essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to initiate the investigation and resolution process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the Report Type that best describes the incident.
2. Provide a detailed Description of the Incident, including any relevant dates and times.
3. Enter the Date of Incident in the format MM/DD/YY.
4. Enter the Time of Incident in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
5. Identify the affected Individual(s) involved in the incident.
6. Enter the Contact Name and Contact Email of the person to contact for more information.
7. Enter the Contact Phone number of the contact person.
8. Provide additional Incident Details, if necessary.
9. Attach any relevant Incident Images or documents.
10. Identify who Reported the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report Type**: Select the category that best describes the incident (Report of data breach, Report of data misuse, or Report of data misuse or breach).
* **Description of Incident**: Provide a detailed account of what happened, including any relevant dates and times.
* **Date of Incident**: Enter the date the incident occurred, in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Time of Incident**: Enter the time the incident occurred, in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Affected Individual**: Identify who was affected by the incident (Jane, John, or both).
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person to contact for more information.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Incident Details**: Provide any additional relevant information about the incident.
* **Incident Image**: If you have any supporting images or documents related to the incident, please attach them here.
* **Reported By**: Identify who reported the incident (User 1, User 2, or both).

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are submitting accurate and truthful information.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, please seek clarification from your supervisor or the designated data protection officer.
* Check the fields carefully to avoid duplication of effort and ensure that all relevant information is captured.
