# Guided Meditation Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients to provide them with personalized guided meditation services. Please ensure that you are a client seeking these services and have not already submitted a similar form in the past.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields below with your information and answer the questions to the best of your abilities. Each field has a label that explains what we are looking for. If you are unsure about a field, please refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What are the client goals** (`client_goals`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of what you hope to achieve through meditation, such as reducing stress, improving focus, or enhancing overall well-being.
- **What are the client's needs** (`meditation_needs`, text, optional): Describe the specific areas where you would like help with, such as anxiety, sleep, or relationships.
- **How often will the client use meditation** (`frequency_of_use`, number, optional): Indicate how frequently you plan to use meditation in your daily life, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
- **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address where we can contact you.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number where we can reach you.
- **Preferred time for meditation session** (`preferred_time`, time, optional): Indicate a specific time that suits you best for our meditation sessions, such as morning, afternoon, or evening.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when answering the questions to help us provide the best services tailored to your needs.
- Ensure that your email and contact number are accurate to ensure smooth communication.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us directly.
