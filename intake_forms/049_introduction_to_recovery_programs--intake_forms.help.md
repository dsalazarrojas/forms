# Introduction To Recovery Programs - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Introduction To Recovery Programs" form is used to gather information from individuals seeking recovery programs or services. This form helps us understand their interests, goals, and needs to provide personalized support and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name of the recovery center you are interested in.
2.  Provide your phone number so we can contact you.
3.  Enter your email address so we can communicate with you.
4.  Describe your recovery goals.
5.  Select your interests (Yes/No).
6.  Choose your preferred contact method (Yes/No).
7.  Enter the name of the person you would like to be in contact with.
8.  Select the recovery center services you are interested in (Yes/No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Recovery Center Name** (`recovery_center_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the recovery center you are interested in. This could be a specific program or organization that provides recovery services.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Provide a number where we can contact you. This will help us reach out to you for further discussion or updates.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, text, optional): Enter your email address so we can communicate with you about your recovery progress and needs.
* **Recovery Goals** (`recovery_goals`, text, optional): Describe your short-term and long-term recovery goals. This will help us understand your needs and provide guidance.
* **Interests** (`interests`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the interests that apply to you (Yes/No).
	+ "Yes" indicates your interest in the corresponding option.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred contact method (Yes/No).
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person you would like to be in contact with.
* **Recovery Center Services** (`recovery_center_services`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the recovery center services you are interested in (Yes/No).
