# Mediation Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from clients in order to better understand their situation and provide them with appropriate mediation services. Please note that this form is not a substitute for legal advice, and the information collected here will be used solely for the purpose of providing mediation services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Client Email" field.
3. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, **optional**): Enter your name so we can address you correctly and ensure that we can communicate effectively.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, **optional**): Enter your email address so we can reach you for any necessary communication.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, **optional**): Enter your contact number so we can reach you for any necessary communication.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you need assistance with filling out this form, please don't hesitate to contact us.
