> # Neuro Rehabilitation Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients undergoing neuro rehabilitation. It gathers details about the patient's medical history, neurological history, physical exam, medications list, treatment plans, patient outcome, and additional comments. The form is intended to help healthcare professionals assess and manage patient care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Leave optional fields blank unless required or if you have additional information to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, required): Enter the patient's basic information, such as name, date of birth, and contact details.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the patient's medical history, including any relevant medical conditions or allergies.
* **Neurological History** (`neurological_history`, text, optional): Enter the patient's neurological history, including any past diagnoses, conditions, or treatments related to the nervous system.
* **Physical Exam** (`physical_exam`, text, optional): Describe the patient's physical exam, including any relevant observations or findings.
* **Medications List** (`medication_list`, text, optional): List the patient's current medications, including dosage and frequency.
* **Treatment Plans** (`treatment_plans`, text, optional): Outline the patient's treatment plans, including any ongoing or upcoming therapies.
* **Patient Outcome** (`patient_outcome`, text, optional): Report on the patient's current outcome or progress.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional information that is not covered in the other fields.

## Tips
* Be accurate and honest when filling out this form to ensure accurate assessment and management of the patient's care.
* Review and revise the form as necessary to ensure all fields are complete and accurate.
* If you have any questions or concerns about completing this form, contact form@gic.mx.
