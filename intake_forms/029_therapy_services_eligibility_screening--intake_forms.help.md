# Therapy Services Eligibility Screening - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather information from clients who are interested in seeking therapy services. The purpose of this form is to help our team determine which therapy services are most suitable for you and to ensure that we can provide the best possible support.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out this form in its entirety, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (`client_info`, text, optional): Please provide your name, date of birth, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Please provide the name, phone number, and relationship of your emergency contact person.
* **Therapy Services** (`therapy_services`, select_one, optional): Select one or more therapy services that you are interested in, such as Cognitive Therapy, Family Therapy, Individual Therapy, or Support Groups.
* **Available Dates and Times** (`dates_and_times`, date, optional): Please provide the dates and times that you are available to attend therapy sessions.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, optional): Please indicate your preferred time for therapy sessions.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, note, optional): Please provide any relevant medical history or concerns.
* **Do you have any medical conditions?** (`medical_condition`, select_one, optional): Answer "True" if you have any medical conditions that may impact your therapy sessions.
* **Current Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): Please list any current medications you are taking.
* **Have you had any major medical procedures?** (`medical_procedure`, select_multiple, optional): Check all that apply, such as Surgery, Chemotherapy, or Radiotherapy.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact, please list their name, phone number, and relationship.

## Tips

* Please be as thorough as possible when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please don't hesitate to ask.
* Our team is here to support you and will review your form thoroughly.
