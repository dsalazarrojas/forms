## Purpose
The interior design intake questionnaire is a tool for clients to provide essential information about their interior design project. This information will be used by designers to get a better understanding of their needs and preferences, allowing them to create a design that meets their expectations. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reading through the form carefully and ensuring you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Complete each section of the form thoroughly and accurately, using the options provided or adding any additional information that is relevant to your project.
3. Don't hesitate to ask for help if you're unsure about any of the fields or questions.
