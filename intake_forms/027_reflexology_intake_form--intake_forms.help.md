# Reflexology Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from patients or clients for reflexology appointments. It helps the reflexologist to have a better understanding of the patient's health status and requirements before the session.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that the reflexologist has all necessary information.
- If you need help or have any questions, do not hesitate to ask the reflexologist for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Schedule Appointment
* **Schedule Appointment** (`schedule_appointment`, text, optional): Enter the date and time you would like to schedule the appointment.
*   Please use the format `MM/DD/YYYY` for the date and `HH:MM AM/PM` for the time.

### 2. Provide Health Info
* **Provide Health Info** (`provide_health_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant health information you think the reflexologist should know.
*   Please provide as much detail as possible to help the reflexologist understand your health status.

### 3. Patient Info
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, optional): Enter your patient information (e.g., medical conditions, allergies).
*   Please be as accurate as possible when providing this information.

### 4. Reflexologist Name
* **Reflexologist Name** (`reflexologist_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the reflexologist who will be attending to you.
*   This information will be helpful for the reflexologist to know who will be treating you.

### 5. Appointment Date
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your appointment.
*   Please use the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.

### 6. Appointment Time
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your appointment.
*   Please use the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.

### 7. Appointment Duration
* **Appointment Duration** (`appointment_duration`, number, optional): Enter the expected duration of your appointment.
*   Please enter the number of minutes you expect the appointment to last.

### 8. Reflexology Treatment
* **Reflexology Treatment** (`reflexology_treatment`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of reflexology treatment you are interested in (e.g., Reflexology, Massage, Acupuncture, Chiropractic).
*   Please select one of the options.

### 9. Patient Notes
* **Patient Notes** (`patient_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments you would like to share with the reflexologist.
*   This is a good place to provide any further information that is not covered in the other fields.

### 10. Medical Conditions
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_multiple, optional): If you have any medical conditions, please mark the ones that apply to you.
*   Please select all that apply.

### 11. Medication List
* **Medication List** (`medication_list`, text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
*   This information will help the reflexologist understand your health status better.

### 12. Allergies
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, optional): If you have any allergies, please mark the ones that apply to you.
*   Please select all that apply.

### 13. Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
*   This information will be helpful in case of any emergencies.

### 14. Emergency Contact Number
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
*   Please make sure to include the area code.

### 15. Medical Insurance
* **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance`, text, optional): If you have medical insurance, enter the name of your insurance provider.
*   This information will help the reflexologist to fill out any necessary paperwork.

### 16. Medical Insurance Number
* **Medical Insurance Number** (`medical_insurance_number`, text, optional): Enter your medical insurance number.
*   Please make sure to enter the correct number.

### 17. Payment Method
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to pay for your appointment (e.g., cash, credit card, etc.).
*   Please select one of the options.

### 18. Payment Amount
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount you are paying for your appointment.
*   Please enter the correct amount.

### 19. Payment Status
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of your payment (e.g., Active, Inactive).
*   Please select one of the options.

### 20. Note
* **Note** (`note`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes for the reflexologist.
*   This is a good place to provide any further information that is not covered in the other fields.

### 21. Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
*   This information will help the reflexologist to contact you.

### 22. Contact Method
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, text, optional): Enter the method of contact (e.g., phone, email, etc.).
*   Please enter the correct method.

### 23. Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Enter your contact information (e.g., address, phone number, etc.).
*   This information will help the reflexologist to contact you.

### 24. Appointment Confirmation
* **Appointment Confirmation** (`appointment_confirmation`, text, optional): Enter your appointment confirmation (e.g., yes/no).
*   Please confirm your appointment.

### 25. Signature
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Sign this form to confirm you have read and understood the information provided.
*   Please sign this form to confirm your understanding.
