# Home Insurance Claim Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Insurance Claim Intake Form is a form used to collect information about a home insurance claim. This form is designed to be completed by the claimant (the policyholder) and should be filled out carefully to ensure all necessary information is collected.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please complete the form in a clear and concise manner, filling out all required fields as accurately as possible.
- If you are unsure about any information, do not hesitate to ask for help or clarification.
- If you are unable to complete the form online, please contact us to arrange an alternative method of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Claimant Information**: This section is used to collect basic contact information about the claimant, including their name, phone number, and email address. Please enter your name, phone number, and email address in the corresponding fields.
  - **Claimants Phone**: Enter the phone number of the claimant.
  - **Claimants Email**: Enter the email address of the claimant.
  - **Claimant Name**: Enter the name of the claimant as it appears on the insurance policy.
- **Address**: This section is used to collect the address where the incident or loss occurred.
  - **Policy Number**: Enter the policy number associated with the policyholder's insurance policy.
  - **Policy Start Date**: Enter the start date of the policy.
  - **Policy End Date**: Enter the end date of the policy.
  - **Claim Type**: Enter a brief description of the type of claim being made (e.g. theft, water damage, etc.).
  - **Claim Description**: Enter a detailed description of the claim, including any relevant circumstances or events that led to the claim.
  - **Claim Details**: Enter any additional information that may be relevant to the claim.
  - **Claim Attachment**: This is an optional field for attaching any supporting documentation or evidence related to the claim.
  - **Claimant Address**: Enter the address where the incident or loss occurred.

## Tips
- Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- If you are unable to attach any supporting documentation, please provide a detailed description of what happened and why it is relevant to the claim.
