# Stepparent Adoption Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from Stepparents for the adoption process. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth and efficient process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully before starting.
2. Fill out each field according to the instructions provided for each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Page

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.

### First Name

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.

### Last Name

* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.

### Contact Information

* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the person to contact.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the person to contact.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (`emergency_contact_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter the first name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (`emergency_contact_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter the last name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required): Describe the relationship between the emergency contact person and you.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.

### Social Worker Information

* **Social Worker First Name** (`social_worker_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter the first name of the social worker.
* **Social Worker Last Name** (`social_worker_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter the last name of the social worker.
* **Social Worker Agency** (`social_worker_agency`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the agency the social worker is working with.
* **Court Order Number** (`court_order_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the court order number.

### Date Information

* **Date Of Service** (`date_of_service`, `date`, required): Enter the date you received service.
* **Date Of Submission** (`date_of_submission`, `date`, required): Enter the date you submitted this form.

### Additional Information

* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your address.
* **County** (`county`, `select_one`, required): Select the county you reside in.
* **State** (`state`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the state(s) you reside in.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Relationship with Child** (`emergency_contact_relationship_other`, `text`, optional): If the relationship between you and the emergency contact person is not listed, describe it here.
