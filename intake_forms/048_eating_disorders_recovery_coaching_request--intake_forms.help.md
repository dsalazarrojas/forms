# Eating Disorders Recovery Coaching Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients who are seeking recovery coaching services for eating disorders. Please fill out this form to provide necessary background, goals, and contact details for your recovery coach.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the Client Background section, which will provide your recovery coach with essential information about you.
2. In the Goals And Objectives section, select all the goals and objectives that apply to you.
3. Enter your contact details in the Contact Details section.
4. In the Recovery Coach section, indicate if you have a recovery coach and provide their email and phone number.
5. Enter the dates and times of your first and last sessions with your recovery coach.
6. Report your progress by entering the total number of sessions completed and total hours spent coaching.
7. Finally, enter the current date and time and any additional notes or files as needed.
