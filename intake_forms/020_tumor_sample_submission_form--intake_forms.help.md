# Tumor Sample Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about tumor samples submitted for analysis. It is used to gather essential details about the specimens, including their type, collection method, and clinical information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Specimen Details** section with the required information.
2. Select the correct **Specimen Type** from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter the **Received Date** of the sample.
4. Fill out the **Clinical Information** section with relevant medical history.
5. Enter the **Specimen ID** and **Specimen Weight** (if applicable).
6. Select the status of the **Specimen** (e.g., Received, In Transit, Not Received).
7. Enter any additional **Clinical Notes** and **Documentation**.
8. Select the **Clinical Team** members involved.
9. Enter the **Specimen Collection Date** and **Time**.
10. Describe the **Specimen Collection Method**.
11. Enter the **Documentation URL** (if applicable).
12. Add any additional **Clinical Notes**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Specimen Details**: Enter a brief description of the specimen.
* **Specimen Type** (Tissue, Cell Block, Fluid): Choose the type of specimen submitted.
* **Received**: Enter the date the specimen was received.
* **Clinical Information**: Enter any relevant medical history or treatment information.
* **Specimen ID**: Enter the unique ID number of the specimen.
* **Specimen Weight**: Enter the weight of the specimen (if applicable).
* **Specimen Condition** (Tumor, Normal, Other): Choose the condition of the specimen.
* **Specimen Description**: Enter a detailed description of the specimen.
* **Lab Information**: Enter any additional information about the lab or testing process.
* **Clinical Information 2**: Enter any additional medical history or treatment information.
* **Specimen Status**: Choose the status of the specimen (e.g., Received, In Transit, Not Received).
* **Documentation**: Enter any additional documentation or notes.
* **Clinical Team**: Select the healthcare professionals involved in the case.
* **Clinical Notes**: Enter any additional clinical notes or comments.
* **Specimen Collection Date**: Enter the date the specimen was collected.
* **Specimen Collection Time**: Enter the time the specimen was collected.
* **Specimen Collection Method**: Describe the method used to collect the specimen.
* **Documentation URL**: Enter the URL of any relevant documentation (if applicable).
* **Clinical Team 2**: Select the healthcare professionals involved in the case.
* **Clinical Notes 2**: Enter any additional clinical notes or comments.
* **Specimen ID 2**: Enter any additional specimen ID information.
* **Specimen ID 3**: Enter any additional specimen ID information (if applicable).
* **Specimen ID 4**: Enter any additional specimen ID information (if applicable).
