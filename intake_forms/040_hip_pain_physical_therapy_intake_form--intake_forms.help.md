# Hip Pain Physical Therapy Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hip Pain Physical Therapy Intake Form is used to gather essential information from patients to help our therapists understand their hip pain and create a personalized treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete each section accurately and honestly.
2. If you are unsure about any information, please ask your primary care physician or other healthcare professional for clarification.
3. Use the provided options for fields with specific instructions, such as "Select One" or "Select Multiple".
4. Do not leave any required fields blank.

- **Patient History** (`patient_history`, `note`, required): This section is used to gather information about your medical history and any previous treatments you've received.
  - Please provide a brief description of your medical history, including any previous surgeries, injuries, or medical conditions.
  - Be as detailed as possible to help the therapist understand your overall health status.
- **Pain Details** (`pain_details`, `note`, required): This section is used to gather information about your hip pain.
  - Please describe your hip pain, including its location, duration, and any factors that exacerbate or alleviate it.
  - Be as detailed as possible to help the therapist understand your pain.
