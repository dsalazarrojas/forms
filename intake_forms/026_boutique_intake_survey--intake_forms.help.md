# Boutique Intake Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your preferences, interests, and shopping habits to help us better understand your needs and provide you with a more personalized experience at our boutique. This information will be used to tailor our suggestions and recommendations to suit your style, fit, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand its purpose and relevance.
2. Fill in each field with the most accurate information you can provide.
3. Use the available options for select-type fields to choose your preferred responses.
4. Don't worry if you're not sure about a particular field; you can always skip it or provide a note in the feedback or suggestions section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`customer_first_name`, `text`, **required**):
	+ This field is used to collect your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`customer_last_name`, `text`, **required**):
	+ This field is used to collect your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email Address** (`customer_email`, `email`, **required**):
	+ This field is used to communicate with you about updates, promotions, and events.
* **Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, `text`, **optional**):
	+ This field is used for phone calls and messages, but feel free to skip it if you prefer not to receive calls.
* **Age Range** (`customer_age_range`, `select_one`, **required**):
	+ This field is used to determine your age group for style and product suggestions.
* **Gender** (`customer_gender`, `select_one`, **optional**):
	+ This field is used to tailor our suggestions based on your gender.
* **Shopping Frequency** (`shopping_frequency`, `select_one`, **required**):
	+ This field is used to understand how often you shop at our boutique.
* **Style Preferences** (`style_preferences`, `select_multiple`, **required**):
	+ This field is used to determine your preferred styles and genres.
* **Color Preferences** (`color_preferences`, `select_multiple`, **required**):
	+ This field is used to understand your preferred colors and color schemes.
* **Size Range** (`size_range`, `select_one`, **optional**):
	+ This field is used to determine your typical size range for fitting purposes.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, **required**):
	+ This field is used to understand your budget range for purchasing decisions.
* **Product Interests** (`product_interests`, `select_multiple`, **required**):
	+ This field is used to determine your preferred product categories and interests.
* **Brand Preferences** (`brand_preferences`, `text`, **optional**):
	+ This field is used to collect your favorite brands or labels.
* **Shopping Motivation** (`shopping_motivation`, `select_one`, **required**):
	+ This field is used to understand what motivates your shopping behavior.
* **Fit Challenges** (`fit_challenges`, `select_multiple`, **optional**):
	+ This field is used to identify any fit challenges you face.
* **Fabric Preferences** (`fabric_preferences`, `select_multiple`, **optional**):
	+ This field is used to understand your preferred fabric types.
* **Sustainability Importance** (`sustainability_importance`, `select_one`, **required**):
	+ This field is used to determine how important sustainability is to you.
* **Communication Preference** (`communication_preference`, `select_multiple`, **required**):
	+ This field is used to decide how you'd like to receive communications from us.
* **Referral Source** (`referral_source`, `select_one`, **required**):
	+ This field is used to understand how you heard about our boutique.
* **Personal Shopper Interest** (`personal_shopper_interest`, `select_one`, **required**):
	+ This field is used to determine if you'd be interested in personal shopping services.
* **Styling Session Interest** (`styling_session_interest`, `select_one`, **optional**):
	+ This field is used to understand if you'd be interested in styling sessions.
* **Upcoming Special Occasions** (`special_occasions`, `text`, **optional**):
	+ This field is used to identify any upcoming events that require outfit suggestions.
* **Wishlist Items** (`wishlist_items`, `text`, **optional**):
	+ This field is used to collect any specific items you're looking for.
* **Feedback or Suggestions** (`feedback_suggestions`, `text`, **optional**):
	+ This field is used to provide any feedback or suggestions on how we can improve your experience.

## Tips
Take your time to fill out this form, and feel free to ask if you have any questions or concerns. If you're unsure about any field, simply leave it blank or provide a note in the feedback or suggestions section. We'll do our best to accommodate your needs and preferences.
