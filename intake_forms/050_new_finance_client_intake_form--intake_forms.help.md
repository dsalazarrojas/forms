# New Finance Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Finance Client Intake Form is used to collect information from new finance clients from various financial institutions. This form helps us understand the client's needs and preferences, ensuring we provide the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Choose the financial institution from the provided options.
2. Enter the client's name and any other relevant details in the text fields.
3. Enter the client's email address and phone number to ensure we can contact them efficiently.
4. Provide your contact name and any additional notes or comments in the note field.
5. Select the date and time of the scheduled appointment or meeting.
6. Click submit to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Financial Institution (financial-institution)**: Select the financial institution your client belongs to. This will help us to know who the client is related to.
  * Select one of the options provided.
* **Finance Client Intake Form Name (finance-client-intake-form-name)**: Enter the name of your client.
  * This field is optional but highly recommended for client identification.
* **Contact Name (contact-name)**: Enter your name and title/position in your institution.
  * This field is optional but recommended for internal communication.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
  * This field is optional but recommended for communication.
* **Phone Number (phone-number)**: Enter your phone number for easy contact.
  * This field is optional but recommended for communication.
* **Address (address)**: Enter your institution's address for easy location identification.
  * This field is optional but recommended for internal records.
* **Date (date)**: Select the date of the scheduled appointment or meeting.
  * This field is optional but recommended for scheduling purposes.
* **Time (time)**: Select the time of the scheduled appointment or meeting.
  * This field is optional but recommended for scheduling purposes.
* **Note (note)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the client or meeting.
  * This field is optional but recommended for internal records.
