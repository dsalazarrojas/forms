# Mental Health Safety Plan - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Safety Plan is a form designed to help individuals create a comprehensive plan for managing their mental health needs. It collects information about their general information, mental health history, crisis history, coping strategies, support system, emergency contact, and mental health safety plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the General Information section, which asks for basic details such as name, contact information, and demographics.
2. Move on to the Mental Health History section, where you can provide information about your mental health experiences, including any diagnoses, treatments, or medications.
3. The Crisis History section allows you to describe any previous crises or emergency situations you have faced.
4. In the Crisis Coping Strategies section, you can outline the techniques and methods you use to cope with crises.
5. The Support System section is where you can list people you can rely on for emotional support, such as family, friends, or mental health professionals.
6. Provide information about your emergency contact, including their name, phone number, and email address.
7. Finally, outline your mental health safety plan, which should include details about how you will stay safe and manage crises.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, required): This field collects basic details such as name, contact information, and demographics.
* **Mental Health History** (`mental_health_history`, text, required): This field asks for information about your mental health experiences, including any diagnoses, treatments, or medications.
* **Mental Health History Details** (`mental_health_history_details`, note, required): This field provides space to elaborate on your mental health history.
* **Crisis History** (`crisis_history`, text, required): This field describes any previous crises or emergency situations you have faced.
* **Crisis Coping Strategies** (`crisis_coping_strategies`, text, required): This section allows you to outline the techniques and methods you use to cope with crises.
* **Support System** (`support_system`, text, required): This field lists people you can rely on for emotional support, such as family, friends, or mental health professionals.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): This field asks for information about your emergency contact, including their name and phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): This field describes your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): This field asks for your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, text, required): This field asks for your emergency contact's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, required): This field asks for your emergency contact's address.
* **Mental Health Safety Plan** (`mental_health_safety_plan`, text, required): This field outlines your mental health safety plan, including details about how you will stay safe and manage crises.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when providing information about your mental health history and crisis history.
* Make sure to list all individuals you can rely on for support, including mental health professionals.
* Clearly outline your crisis coping strategies and mental health safety plan.
* Double-check that your emergency contact information is up-to-date and accurate.
* Review this form carefully before submitting it, ensuring all required fields are completed.
