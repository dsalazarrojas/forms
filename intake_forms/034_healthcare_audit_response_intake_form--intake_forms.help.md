# Healthcare Audit Response Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and document information about healthcare audits. It is intended to track and record the progress of audits, including patient identifiers, audit details, and corrective actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and authorizations to complete this form.
2. Review each field carefully and enter the required information.
3. Fill in the patient identifier field with the relevant patient information.
4. Provide detailed information about the audit details, including any corrective actions taken.
5. Enter any supporting documentation related to the audit.
6. Select the status of the audit from the dropdown options.
7. Enter any notes or comments about the audit.
8. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
9. Schedule a follow-up date and time for review.
10. Select the reviewer and reviewer submitter from the dropdown options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Identifier**: Enter the patient's unique identifier, such as a medical record number or ID number.
* **Audit Details**: Provide a detailed description of the audit, including any issues identified and corrective actions taken.
* **Corrective Actions**: List any corrective actions taken as a result of the audit.
* **Supporting Documentation**: Attach any supporting documents related to the audit, such as photographs, reports, or other relevant files.
* **Internal Review**: Enter any internal review or comments related to the audit.
* **Follow-up**: Schedule a follow-up date and time for review.
* **Healthcare Team**: Enter the name and title of the healthcare team member responsible for the audit.
* **Submitted By**: Enter your name and title as the submitter of the audit.
* **Date**: Enter the date you submitted the audit response.
* **Time**: Enter the time you submitted the audit response.
* **Submitted**: Enter the date and time the audit response was submitted.
* **Reviewed By**: Select the name of the reviewer who reviewed the audit response.
* **Review Date**: Enter the date the audit response was reviewed.
* **Review Time**: Enter the time the audit response was reviewed.
* **Status**: Select the status of the audit from the options: Completed, In Progress, Not Started.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the audit.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Follow-up Date**: Schedule a follow-up date for review.
* **Review Follow-up**: Schedule a review follow-up date.
* **Submitter**: Enter your name and title as the submitter of the audit response.
* **Reviewer Submitter**: Select the name and title of the reviewer who reviewed the audit response.
* **Reviewer**: Enter the name and title of the reviewer.
* **Reviewed Reviewer**: Select the name and title of the reviewer who reviewed the audit response.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the healthcare team for assistance.
