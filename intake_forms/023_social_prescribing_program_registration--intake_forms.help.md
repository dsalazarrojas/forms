# Social Prescribing Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who are interested in participating in our social prescribing program. The program aims to support the health and wellbeing of individuals by providing non-medical interventions and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you have answered all required fields.
3. Review your submission for completeness and accuracy before submitting.
4. Submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Information
• **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional): This field is for general user information that will be stored in our database.

### Interests
• **Interests** (`interests`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more options that best describe your interests. The options are: Option 1, Option 2, and Option 3.

### Consent
• **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, optional): Select one option for consent. The options are: Option 1, Option 2.

### Wellbeing Information
• **Wellbeing Information** (`wellbeing`, time, optional): This field is for recording wellbeing information.

### Wellbeing Information
• **Wellbeing Information** (`wellbeing_info`, note, optional): This field is for additional wellbeing information.

### Submission Information
• **Submission Information** (`submission_info`, date, optional): Record the date of submission.

### Email
• **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.

### Phone
• **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
