# Home Care Worker Intake Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Care Worker Intake Survey is designed to collect information about home care workers to ensure accuracy and consistency in our records. This information will be used to identify and contact home care workers for future services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Enter your Home Care Worker ID, which can be your ID number, name, or any unique identifier.
3. Enter your name as it is spelled and formatted correctly.
4. Enter your complete address to ensure we can send you any necessary documents or notifications.
5. Enter your phone number and email address so we can contact you easily.
6. Enter your date of birth for age verification purposes.
7. Enter the date you started working as a home care worker.
8. Enter the number of hours you work per week to help us schedule and plan services.
9. Enter the number of hours you spend per visit with our clients.
10. Enter your current rate per hour for our payment records.
11. Select one of the payment methods you prefer (Bank Transfer, Check, or Cash).
12. If you have any additional comments or information, please provide them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Care Worker Id**: Your ID number, name, or any unique identifier that represents you as a home care worker. This helps us to identify and contact you for future services.
  * Required
  * Type: number/text

* **Home Care Worker Name**: Enter your full name as it is spelled and formatted correctly. This will help us to know who you are and what services you provide.
  * Required
  * Type: text

* **Home Care Worker Address**: Enter your complete address where we can send documents or notifications. This ensures we can reach you at the right time.
  * Required
  * Type: text

* **Home Care Worker Contact Number**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you easily for any updates or instructions.
  * Required
  * Type: text

* **Home Care Worker Email**: Enter your email address so we can send you notifications and updates about your services.
  * Required
  * Type: email

* **Home Care Worker Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth for age verification purposes. This ensures we understand who you are and what services you can provide.
  * Required
  * Type: date

* **Home Care Worker Employment Date**: Enter the date you started working as a home care worker. This helps us to know when you joined our services.
  * Required
  * Type: date

* **Home Care Worker Hours per Week**: Enter the number of hours you work per week to help us schedule and plan services for our clients.
  * Required
  * Type: number

* **Home Care Worker Hours per Visit**: Enter the number of hours you spend per visit with our clients. This helps us to plan and schedule visits effectively.
  * Required
  * Type: number

* **Home Care Worker Rate per Hour**: Enter your current rate per hour for our payment records. This ensures we pay you accurately for your services.
  * Required
  * Type: number

* **Home Care Worker Payment Method**: Select one of the payment methods you prefer (Bank Transfer, Check, or Cash). This helps us to process your payments efficiently.
  * Required
  * Type: select_one

* **Home Care Worker Additional Info**: If you have any comments or additional information, please provide them here. This helps us to understand your preferences and needs better.
  * Optional
  * Type: text
