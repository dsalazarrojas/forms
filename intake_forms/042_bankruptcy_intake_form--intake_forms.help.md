# Bankruptcy Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bankruptcy Intake Form is designed to collect detailed information about an individual's financial situation in order to assess their eligibility for bankruptcy protection. This form gathers information about the debtor's identification, employment, income, expenses, assets, and debts to provide a comprehensive understanding of their financial status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Debtor Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. **Email Address**: Provide your contact email address.
3. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
4. **Mailing Address**: Enter your street address, city, state, and ZIP code.
5. **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
6. **Social Security Number**: Enter your full Social Security number.
7. **Drivers License Number**: Enter your state and driver's license number.
8. **Marital Status**: Select your current marital status (Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Separated).
9. **Spouse Name** (if applicable): Enter your spouse's name.
10. **Spouse Social Security Number** (if applicable): Enter your spouse's full Social Security number.
11. **Number of Dependents**: Enter the number of dependents you have.
12. **Bankruptcy Chapter**: Select the type of bankruptcy chapter you are filing for (Chapter 7, Chapter 13, Chapter 11, Not Sure).
13. **Employment Status**: Select your current employment status (Employed, Self-Employed, Unemployed, Retired, Disabled).
14. **Employer Name** (if applicable): Enter your employer's name.
15. **Employer Address** (if applicable): Enter your work address.
16. **Gross Monthly Income**: Enter your income before taxes.
17. **Net Monthly Income**: Enter your income after taxes.
18. **Total Monthly Expenses**: Enter the total of all your living expenses.
19. **List of Creditors**: Enter the names and amounts of all your creditors.
20. **Total Debt Amount**: Enter the sum of all your debts.
21. **List of Assets**: Enter details about all your assets, including property and vehicles.
22. **Real Estate Owned** (if applicable): Enter details about any real estate you own.
23. **Vehicles Owned** (if applicable): Enter details about any vehicles you own.
24. **Bank Accounts**: Enter details about all your bank accounts and their balances.
25. **Prior Bankruptcy Filing**: Select if you have filed for bankruptcy within the past 8 years or if it was more than 8 years ago.
26. **Pending Legal Actions**: If you have any pending lawsuits, garnishments, or other legal actions, describe them.
27. **Financial Courses Completed** (if applicable): Select if you have completed credit counseling.
28. **Attorney Representation**: Select if you have an attorney, need one, or will represent yourself.
29. **Additional Comments**: Provide any other relevant information about your financial situation.
30. **Signature Date**: Enter the date you sign this form.
