# Emergency Department Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to help emergency department staff members assess patients' medical conditions and provide effective care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the **Patient Information** section, which includes basic details about the patient such as their name, age, and medical history.
2. Move on to the **Chief Complaint** section, where you should describe the reason for the patient's visit.
3. Fill out the **Medical Condition** section with any relevant medical conditions or concerns the patient may have.
4. If applicable, list the patient's **Allergies** in the corresponding section.
5. For each question in the **Med History** sections, choose all that apply from the available options.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out each section completely and accurately.
* If you're unsure about any information, ask the patient or their caregiver for clarification.
* Use the **Med History** sections to gather detailed medical history information.
* If a field is not applicable, leave it blank and proceed with the next section.
