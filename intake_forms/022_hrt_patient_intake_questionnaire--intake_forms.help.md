# HRT Patient Intake Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about patients' medical history and treatment preferences for Hormone Replacement Therapy (HRT).

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please enter your name accurately and completely in the "patient_name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Describe your medical history in the "medical_history" field.
5. Choose when your medical history began in the "medical_history_date" field.
6. Choose the time of day when your medical history began in the "medical_history_time" field.
7. If your doctor has referred you to this clinic, select "Yes" in the "hrt_doctor_referral" field.
8. If you have any additional information about your medical history, enter it in the "medical_history_additional_info" field.
9. Enter your notes about your medical history in the "patient_notes" field.
10. If you have any additional information to share with the healthcare provider, enter it in the "additional_info" field.
11. Select "Yes" or "No" for each option in the "hrt_treatment_goals" field.
12. Select "Yes" or "No" for each option in the "blood_test_results" field.
13. Enter the name of your doctor who referred you to this clinic in the "hrt_doctor_name" field.
14. Enter your nurse's name, if applicable, in the "hrt_nurse_name" field.
15. Enter your doctor's phone number in the "hrt_doctor_phone" field.
16. Enter your doctor's email address in the "hrt_doctor_email" field.
17. Enter your clinic's phone number in the "hrt_clinic_phone" field.
18. Enter your clinic's email address in the "hrt_clinic_email" field.
19. Enter your clinic's address in the "hrt_clinic_address" field.
20. Enter your clinic's operating hours in the "hrt_clinic_hours" field.
21. Enter any notes about your clinic in the "hrt_clinic_notes" field.
22. Enter any other notes about your clinic in the "hrt_other_clinic_notes" field.
23. Sign and date your form with your signature in the "hrt_patient_signature" field.
24. Date and time of completion of this form in the "hrt_date_completed" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Heading** (`form_heading`, `note`, not required): This is the heading of the form.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name accurately and completely.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, not required): Provide your phone number.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required): Describe your medical history, including any relevant details.
* **Medical History Date** (`medical_history_date`, `date`, required): Choose when your medical history began.
* **Medical History Time** (`medical_history_time`, `time`, required): Choose the time of day when your medical history began.
* **HRT Doctor Referral** (`hrt_doctor_referral`, `select_one`, required): If your doctor has referred you to this clinic, select "Yes".
* **Medical History Additional Info** (`medical_history_additional_info`, `text`, not required): If you have any additional information about your medical history, enter it here.
* **Patient Notes** (`patient_notes`, `note`, not required): Enter any notes about your medical history.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `text`, not required): If you have any additional information to share with the healthcare provider, enter it here.
* **HRT Treatment Goals** (`hrt_treatment_goals`, `select_multiple`, required): Select "Yes" or "No" for each option.
* **Blood Test Results** (`blood_test_results`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" or "No".
* **HRT Doctor's Name** (`hrt_doctor_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your doctor who referred you to this clinic.
* **HRT Nurse's Name** (`hrt_nurse_name`, `text`, not required): Enter your nurse's name, if applicable.
* **HRT Doctor's Phone Number** (`hrt_doctor_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your doctor's phone number.
* **HRT Doctor's Email** (`hrt_doctor_email`, `email`, required): Enter your doctor's email address.
* **HRT Clinic Phone Number** (`hrt_clinic_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your clinic's phone number.
* **HRT Clinic Email** (`hrt_clinic_email`, `email`, required): Enter your clinic's email address.
* **HRT Clinic Address** (`hrt_clinic_address`, `text`, required): Enter your clinic's address.
* **HRT Clinic Hours** (`hrt_clinic_hours`, `text`, not required): Enter your clinic's operating hours.
* **HRT Clinic Notes** (`hrt_clinic_notes`, `text`, not required): Enter any notes about your clinic.
* **HRT Other Clinic Notes** (`hrt_other_clinic_notes`, `text`, not required): Enter any other notes about your clinic.
* **HRT Patient Signature** (`hrt_patient_signature`, `text`, required): Sign and date your form with your signature.
* **Date Completed** (`hrt_date_completed`, `date`, required): Date and time of completion of this form.

Please note that this form has multiple pages, each with its own set of fields. Ensure that you complete all required fields before proceeding. Your responses will be used to guide the healthcare provider's treatment plan and ensure that you receive the best possible care.
