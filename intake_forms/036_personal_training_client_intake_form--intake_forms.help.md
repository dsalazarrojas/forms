# personal_training_client_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential details about you as our client, allowing us to provide the best possible experience and training. Please fill out this form completely and accurately to ensure we have a clear understanding of your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your contact information, choosing one of the following methods to contact us: Email, Phone, or In Person.
2. If applicable, provide the contact information of an emergency contact, including their relation to you, phone number, and email address.
3. Share any relevant medical history or notes that might affect your training.
4. If necessary, provide medical notes or information that will aid in your training.
5. Add any additional notes about your training or personal goals.
6. Enter your date of birth for our records.
7. If applicable, provide training information or any specific requirements.
8. Finally, confirm your understanding of our payment terms for your training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Info (`client_info`, text, optional)**: Enter any identifying information about yourself.
* **Contact Method (`contact_method`, select_one, **required**)**: How you would prefer to contact us for any questions or concerns.
	+ Please select one: Email, Phone, or In Person.
* **Emergency Contact Info (`emergency_contact`, text, optional)**: If you have someone we should contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relation (`emergency_contact_relation`, select_one, **required**)**: Who is this emergency contact to you (e.g., Spouse, Parent, Child, Other).
* **Relation** options: Please choose from: Spouse, Parent, Child, Other.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional)**: The phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional)**: The email address of your emergency contact.
* **Medical History (`medical_history`, text, optional)**: Any medical conditions or concerns you would like to share with us.
* **Medical Notes (`medical_notes`, text, optional)**: Any additional medical information that might impact your training.
* **Relation** options: Please choose from: Spouse, Parent, Child, Other.
* **Relation** (same as above).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (same as above).
* **Emergency Contact Email** (same as above).
* **Medical Info (`medical_info`, text, optional)**: Any additional medical information you wish to provide.
* **Notes (`notes`, text, optional)**: Any additional information or comments about your training or goals.
* **Date of Birth (`date_of_birth`, date, optional)**: Your date of birth for our records.
* **Training Info (`training_info`, text, optional)**: Any specific training information or requirements.
* **Payment Terms (`payment_terms`, text, optional)**: Please confirm your understanding of our payment terms.
