# life_coach_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic information from clients for life coaching services. It aims to provide a comprehensive overview of the client's needs, preferences, and medical history. This information is used to create a personalized coaching plan tailored to their requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide basic information about yourself as a client, including name and contact details.
2. Share your medical history, including any allergies, medications, and previous surgeries.
3. If necessary, specify any relevant medical conditions or injuries that may impact your coaching journey.
4. Choose your preferred contact methods (phone, email, or both).
5. If applicable, provide any additional medical information not covered in the previous sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Info**: This field is a brief introduction to yourself as a client. Please fill out your name and any other relevant information you'd like to share.
  - **Contact Info**: Enter your contact details, including phone number and email address.
  - **Medical History**: Describe your medical history, including any allergies, medications, and previous surgeries.
  - **Medical History Condition**: If you have any medical conditions, please specify them here.
  - **Medical History Date**: For any relevant medical history dates, please enter them in this field.
  - **Medical History Note**: If you have any additional medical notes or comments, please share them here.
  - **Contact Methods**: Select one or more contact methods (phone, email, or both) that you would prefer for communication.
  - **Medical History Allergies**: If you have any allergies, please list them here.
  - **Medical History Medication**: If you're currently taking any medications, please list them here.
  - **Medical History Surgeries**: If you've had any surgeries, please list them here.
  - **Medical History Injuries**: If you've experienced any injuries, please describe them here.
  - **Medical History Other**: If there's anything else you'd like to share about your medical history, please use this field.
  - **Contact Method Phone**: If you'd like to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number.
  - **Contact Method Email**: If you'd like to be contacted by email, please provide your email address.
  - **Contact Method Note**: If you have any specific preferences for contact (e.g., best time to call, etc.), please share them here.

## Tips
- Please provide as much detail as possible to ensure accurate and effective coaching.
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask your life coach for guidance.
- This form is for internal use only, and your information will be kept confidential and secure.
