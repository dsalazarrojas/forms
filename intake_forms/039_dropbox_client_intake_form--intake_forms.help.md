# Dropbox Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dropbox Client Intake Form is designed to collect essential information from clients or customers who are reaching out to Dropbox for support. This form helps our support team understand your needs and provide the best assistance possible.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each field with the required information.
2. Make sure to include any relevant attachments or files as requested.
3. Review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure everything is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Information** (`1`, `text`, required: `false`): This section is where you can provide basic information about yourself, such as your name, company name, and other relevant details.
  * Please enter your name and any other relevant information that will help us identify you.
- **Contact Details** (`2`, `text`, required: `false`): This section is where you can provide your contact information, such as email address and phone number.
  * Enter your email address and phone number so we can reach out to you efficiently.
- **Business Details** (`3`, `text`, required: `false`): This section is where you can provide any relevant business information that is related to your issue or inquiry.
  * Please include any company names, titles, or department names that are relevant to your request.
- **Communication Preferences** (`4`, `text`, required: `false`): This section is where you can let us know your preferred communication method (e.g., email, phone, or support ticket).
  * Choose how you would like us to contact you and resolve your issue.
- **File Attachments** (`5`, `text`, required: `false`): This section is where you can attach any relevant files or documents that support your request.
  * Only attach files that are necessary for us to understand and resolve your issue.

## Tips
- Make sure to proofread your form carefully before submitting it.
- If you're unsure about any field or option, please ask a support agent for guidance.
- If you have any additional questions or concerns, feel free to contact us directly.
