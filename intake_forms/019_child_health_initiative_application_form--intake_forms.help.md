# Child Health Initiative Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from parents or guardians to determine which programs their child is eligible for. This information will be used to assess the child's needs and select the most suitable program for them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Parent or Guardian's name in the "Parent or Guardian Name" field.
2. Provide the contact email address in the "Contact Email Address" field for application status updates.
3. Enter the contact phone number in the "Contact Phone Number" field.
4. Select the initiative program that you think your child needs in the "Initiative Program Requested" field.
5. Enter the child's full name in the "Child Full Name" field.
6. Enter the child's date of birth in the "Child Date of Birth" field.
7. Select the household income range in the "Household Income Range" field.
8. Indicate the number of dependents in the household in the "Number of Dependents in Household" field.
9. Provide a brief description of why you are applying for this initiative in the "Why are you applying for this initiative?" field.
10. Agree to the terms and conditions in the "Agree to program terms and conditions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the parent or guardian of the child.
* **Contact Email Address** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can keep you updated on application status.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your daytime phone number for any updates.
* **Initiative Program Requested** (`initiative_program`, `select_one`, required): Choose the program that you think your child needs.
  • Nutrition and Meal Support: For families struggling with meal provision and nutrition.
  • Dental Health Access: For children with dental issues.
  • Mental Wellness Services: For children with mental health concerns.
  • Physical Fitness Grants: For physical fitness programs for children.
* **Child Full Name** (`child_name`, `text`, required): Enter the child's full name for enrollment purposes.
* **Child Date of Birth** (`child_dob`, `date`, required): Enter the child's date of birth to confirm program age limits.
* **Household Income Range** (`income_range`, `select_one`, required): Choose the household income range.
  • Under $30,000: For families with lower incomes.
  • $30,000 to $60,000: For middle-income families.
  • Above $60,000: For higher-income families.
* **Number of Dependents in Household** (`dependent_count`, `select_one`, required): Indicate the number of dependents in the household.
  • 1 to 2: For small households.
  • 3 to 4: For medium-sized households.
  • 5 or more: For larger households.
* **Why are you applying for this initiative?** (`application_reason`, `text`, required): Describe how this program will benefit your child.
* **Agree to program terms and conditions** (`terms_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Agree to the terms and conditions of the program.
  • I Agree: Accept the terms and conditions.
  • I Disagree: Decline the terms and conditions.
