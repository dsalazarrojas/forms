# Child Relocation (Move Away) Request Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request relocation of a child. Please fill out the form below to provide the required information.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply go through each section and provide the requested information. Make sure to answer all required questions and submit the form once you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (1. Applicant Name, text, required): This is your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Your role in the case** (3. Applicant Role, select_one, required): Select your role in the case from the given options:
	+ Parent
	+ Legal Representative
	+ Mediator
* **Case Number - if applicable** (5. Case Number, text, optional): If you are referring to an existing court case, please provide the case number here.
* **Child Name and Age** (6. Child Details, text, required): This section is for the name and age of the child subject to the relocation request.
* **Proposed New City and State** (8. New Location, text, required): Specify the city and state to which you will be moving, providing a detailed description if necessary.
* **Anticipated Date of Move** (9. Move Date, date, required): Enter the proposed date for the move, in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Primary reason for move** (11. Primary Reason, select_one, required): Choose the core reason for the relocation from the provided options:
	+ New Employment Opportunity
	+ Move closer to Family Support
	+ Higher Education
	+ Other Reason
* **Detailed explanation for relocation request** (12. Detailed Explanation, text, required): Provide a clear and concise explanation for the relocation request, including the necessity and context.
* **Proposed new visitation schedule** (14. Visitation Proposal, text, required): Describe the proposed arrangements for visitation, explaining how the other parent will be able to visit and interact with the child in the new location.
* **Status of communication with the other parent** (15. Parent Notified, select_one, required): Indicate whether the other parent has been notified about the relocation request and their response:
	+ Yes - Notified and Agrees
	+ Yes - Notified and Disagrees
	+ No - Not yet notified
* **Intake Date** (16. Today Date, date, required): The date on which this form is completed, which will be automatically populated.

## Tips
Please ensure that you have reviewed and understood the implications of this form before submitting it. This form is a formal document and its completion is subject to the relevant laws and regulations of the jurisdiction in which it is submitted. If you are unsure about any part of the form or its implications, please consult with your legal representative before submission.
