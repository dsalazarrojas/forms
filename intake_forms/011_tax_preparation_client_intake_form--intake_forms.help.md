# Tax Preparation Client Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tax Preparation Client Intake Form is a critical tool for tax professionals to collect vital information from clients. This form ensures that tax returns are prepared with accuracy and completeness, reducing the risk of errors or missed deductions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1.  Review each field carefully to understand the required information.
2.  Enter your accurate details for each field.
3.  Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Information** (1): This field is for providing general details about yourself as a client. Please enter your basic information such as First Name, Last Name, and Email.
    *   **First Name** (2): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
    *   **Last Name** (3): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
    *   **Email** (4): Enter your active email address for communication purposes.
*   **Phone** (5): Enter your contact number for further communication.
*   **Address** (6): Enter your current address for tax purposes.
*   **Tax Year** (7): Enter the year your tax return is for.
*   **Total Income** (8): Enter your total income for the selected tax year.
*   **Dependents** (9): If you have dependents, enter their number.
*   **Marital Status** (10): Select your current marital status.
    *   **Single**: You are single.
    *   **Married**: You are married.
    *   **Widowed**: You are widowed.
    *   **Divorced**: You are divorced.
*   **Tax ID Number** (11): Enter your Tax ID Number for tax purposes.
*   **Employment Status** (12): Select your current employment status.
    *   **Employed**: You are currently employed.
    *   **Self-Employed**: You are self-employed.
    *   **Retired**: You are retired.
    *   **Unemployed**: You are unemployed.
    *   **Disabled**: You are disabled.
*   **Date Of Birth** (13): Enter your date of birth.
*   **Note** (14): Provide any additional comments or notes for your tax professional.
*   **Assigned Tax Professional** (15): If you have been assigned a tax professional, please select their name.
*   **Tax Deductions** (16): Enter your total tax deductions for the selected tax year.
*   **Tax Withholdings** (17): Enter your tax withholdings for the selected tax year.
*   **Signature** (18): If you have multiple signatures, select all applicable signatures.
    *   **Signature 1**: Sign for this purpose.
    *   **Signature 2**: Sign for this purpose.
    *   **Signature 3**: Sign for this purpose.
*   **Question 1** (19): Answer Question 1 as prompted.
*   **Question 2** (20): Answer Question 2 as prompted.
*   **Question 3** (21): Answer Question 3 as prompted.
*   **Question 4** (22): Answer Question 4 as prompted.
*   **Question 5** (23): Answer Question 5 as prompted.
*   **Question 6** (24): Answer Question 6 as prompted.
*   **Question 7** (25): Answer Question 7 as prompted.
*   **Note** (14): Add any additional comments or notes for your tax professional. This field is for providing any additional information that may be relevant to your tax return.
*   **Assigned Tax Professional** (15): If you have been assigned a tax professional, select their name from the list. This is an important field as it ensures that your tax return is prepared by the professional assigned to you.
*   **Tax Deductions** (16): Enter your total tax deductions for the selected tax year. This field is crucial as it affects the accuracy of your tax return.
*   **Tax Withholdings** (17): Enter your tax withholdings for the selected tax year. This field is essential for ensuring the accuracy of your tax return.
*   **Signature** (18): Sign this form to confirm that you have reviewed and understand the information provided. You may have multiple signatures if required by your tax professional or for multiple purposes.
    *   **Signature 1**: Sign for this purpose.
    *   **Signature 2**: Sign for this purpose.
    *   **Signature 3**: Sign for this purpose.

## Tips
*   Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   If you are unsure about any field, consult your tax professional for clarification.
*   Ensure that you have provided all required information before submitting the form.
*   Review the form carefully before finalizing your submission.
*   If you have any additional comments or questions, you can add them in the "Note" field for your tax professional's reference.
