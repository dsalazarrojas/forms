# Chiropractic Treatment Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chiropractic Treatment Intake Form is a vital document designed to collect information from patients to help chiropractors accurately diagnose and treat their conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review and understand the form content before beginning.
2.  Fill out the patient demographics and contact information sections accurately.
3.  Provide accurate medical history and previous treatments for your condition.
4.  List any current medications and allergies.
5.  Confirm that the information provided is accurate with the 'I confirm this information is accurate' field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name**: A field for you to enter your full name as you would like it to be recorded.
    *   **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format 'mm/dd/yyyy'.
    *   **Age**: Enter your age.
    *   **Gender**: Select your gender from the options provided.
    *   **Phone Number**: Enter your contact number.
    *   **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
    *   **Street Address**: Enter your complete street address.
    *   **City**: Enter the name of the city where you live.
    *   **State**: Enter the name of the state where you live.
    *   **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code.
    *   **Pain Location**: Select all areas where you experience pain or discomfort.
    *   **Pain Severity**: Rate your pain from 1 (minimal) to 10 (severe).
    *   **When Did Pain Start**: Select the time frame when your pain started.
    *   **What Caused the Pain**: Describe the cause of your pain (if known).
    *   **Previous Chiropractic Treatment**: Select if you have previously received chiropractic treatment.
    *   **Previous Treatments**: List any previous treatments for your condition.
    *   **Current Medications**: List all medications you are currently taking.
    *   **Known Allergies**: List any known allergies.
    *   **I confirm this information is accurate**: Confirm that the information you have provided is accurate.

## Tips

*   Review the form carefully before submission.
*   Ensure all fields are filled in with accurate and up-to-date information.
*   Confirm accuracy of the information provided with the 'I confirm this information is accurate' field.
*   If you have completed this form before, be prepared to confirm the accuracy of your information.
