# Credit Services Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Credit Services Intake Form is designed to collect information from borrowers to assess and manage credit services requests. This form helps the credit services team make informed decisions regarding loan approval or denial.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand what information is required and what type of questions are being asked.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
3. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Loan type** (`loan_type`, text, optional): Please select the type of loan you are applying for.
* **Loan amount** (`loan_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the loan you are applying for.
* **Credit card number** (`credit_card_number`, text, optional): Enter your credit card number if you have one.
* **Income** (`income`, number, optional): Enter your annual income.
* **Employment status** (`employment_status`, select_one, optional): Select your current employment status from the options provided: Active, Inactive, Retired, Disabled.
* **Employment length** (`employment_length`, number, optional): Enter the length of your employment.
* **Credit score** (`credit_score`, number, optional): Enter your current credit score.
* **Loan term length** (`loan_term_length`, number, optional): Enter the length of the loan term.
* **Loan interest rate** (`loan_interest_rate`, number, optional): Enter the loan interest rate.
* **Loan terms and conditions** (`loan_terms_and_conditions`, note, optional): Enter any additional loan terms and conditions.
* **Borrower name** (`borrower_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your credit report.

## Tips
- Ensure you fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
- Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the credit services team for assistance.
