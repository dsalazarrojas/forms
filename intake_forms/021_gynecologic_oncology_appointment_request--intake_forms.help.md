# gynecologic_oncology_appointment_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an appointment with a gynecologic oncology doctor. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure efficient processing and effective communication with the hospital's medical team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your patient information (Name, Phone Number, and Email Address) in the "Patient Info" section.
2. Select a date and time for your appointment in the "Appointment Date" and "Appointment Time" fields, respectively.
3. Choose the department you are requesting a visit to (Oncology, Gynecology, or Other) in the "Hospital Department" field.
4. Provide a brief medical history in the "Medical History" field.
5. Select your preferred doctor (Dr. Smith, Dr. Johnson, or Dr. Doe) in the "Doctor Preference" field.
6. Add any additional notes about your appointment in the "Appointment Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Info (Patient Info, text, required)**: This section requires your full name, phone number, and email address. Please ensure that your contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
- **Appointment Date (Appointment Date, date, required)**: Select a date for your appointment from the calendar. Make sure the date is in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
- **Appointment Time (Appointment Time, time, required)**: Choose a time for your appointment. Please note that appointment times may be subject to change based on doctor availability.
- **Doctor Name (Doctor Name, text, required)**: This field is used to select the doctor you would like to see for your appointment. Please choose from the available options or provide the name of an alternate doctor if not listed.
- **Phone Number (Phone Number, text, required)**: Enter your phone number so that the hospital can contact you with appointment reminders or updates.
- **Email Address (Email Address, email, required)**: This field is used to contact you via email for appointment scheduling and updates.
- **Hospital Department (Hospital Department, select_one, required)**: Choose the department where you would like to be seen (Oncology, Gynecology, or Other). If you select "Other," please provide a brief explanation in the "Medical History" field.
- **Medical History (Medical History, text, required)**: Briefly describe your medical history, including any relevant health conditions or previous treatments.
- **Doctor Preference (Doctor Preference, select_one, required)**: Select the doctor you would like to see for your appointment. Please note that availability may vary based on doctor schedules.
- **Appointment Notes (Appointment Notes, note, required)**: Add any additional notes or comments regarding your appointment. This field is optional but can help ensure that your doctor has all the necessary information for your visit.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly. If you have any questions or concerns about this form, contact the relevant hospital department or authority for assistance.
