# Early Pregnancy Symptom And Concern Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a patient's general health and well-being, including their pregnancy status, symptoms, concerns, and medical history.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each field carefully and answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2.  Enter your general contact information in the "General Information" field.
3.  Provide details about your pregnancy in the "Pregnancy Info" field.
4.  List any symptoms or issues you are experiencing in the "Symptoms" field.
5.  Share any concerns or questions you have about your pregnancy in the "Concerns" field.
6.  Provide information about your medical history, including any allergies or sensitivities, in the "Medical History" field.
7.  List any medications you are currently taking in the "Medications" field.
8.  Enter your contact information for the patient or their representative in the "Contact Info" field.
9.  Review the form to ensure all fields are completed and accurate.
10.  Sign and date the form, and have it signed by your medical provider.
