# Prenatal Massage Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prenatal Massage Client Intake Form is a vital document for our prenatal massage clients to provide us with essential information about their health and consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your first name, email, and phone number to enable us to contact you effectively.
2. For "Medical Conditions", select "Yes" if you have any pre-existing medical conditions that may affect your massage experience.
3. For "Allergies" and "Medications", select "Yes" for any substances or conditions that you are allergic to or currently taking.
4. Choose your massage frequency (how often you want to receive massage) and area (which areas you want to be massaged) by selecting "Yes" from the respective options.
5. Provide your consent date and time by selecting a date and time in the corresponding fields.
6. Choose who will give consent for your massage (e.g., Client, Client's Spouse, Guardian, or Someone Else).
7. Please review your selections carefully and ensure all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **FirstName** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name for us to address you effectively.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to enable us to contact you with updates and reminders.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to ensure we can reach you when needed.
* **MedicalConditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_multiple, optional): If you have any pre-existing medical conditions that may affect your massage experience, please select "Yes" from the options.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, optional): If you are allergic to any substances or have any sensitivities, please select "Yes" for each applicable option.
* **Medications** (`medications`, select_multiple, optional): If you are currently taking any medications, please select "Yes" for each applicable option.
* **MassageFrequency** (`massage_frequency`, select_one, optional): Please choose how often you want to receive massages.
* **MassageArea** (`massage_area`, select_one, optional): Please select the areas you want to be massaged (e.g., back, neck, etc.).
* **ConsentDate** (`consent_date`, date, optional): Please select the date you consent to receive a massage.
* **ConsentTime** (`consent_time`, time, optional): Please select the time you consent to receive a massage.
* **ConsentSignature** (`consent_signature`, select_one, optional): Please choose who will give consent for your massage.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid any mistakes.
