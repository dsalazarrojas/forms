# tcpa_violation_claim_intake_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to intake violations of TCPA (Telephone Consumer Protection Act) claims. The purpose of this form is to gather relevant information and details about the claim, including claimant and claimant's representative contact information, company information, and case details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Please fill out the form fields with the required information.
2. Select the relevant options from the dropdown menus where applicable.
3. Upload any necessary files as attachments.
4. Sign and date the form where required.
5. Click the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the claimant's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the claimant's last name.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter the company name.
* **Claim Type** (`claim_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of claim being filed.
* **Date Received** (`date_received`, date, optional): Enter the date the claim was received.
* **Time Received** (`time_received`, time, optional): Enter the time the claim was received.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, optional): Select the method used to contact the claimant.
* **Call Transcription** (`call_transcription`, note, optional): Enter any relevant details about the call transcription.
* **Text Transcription** (`text_transcription`, note, optional): Enter any relevant details about the text transcription.
* **Claim Details** (`claim_details`, note, optional): Enter any relevant details about the claim.
* **Company Represented** (`company_represented`, select_multiple, optional): Select the company or entity being represented.
* **Case Number** (`case_number`, text, optional): Enter the case number.
* **Claimant Phone** (`claimant_phone`, text, optional): Enter the claimant's phone number.
* **Claimant Email** (`claimant_email`, email, optional): Enter the claimant's email address.
* **Claimant Represented** (`claimant_represented`, select_multiple, optional): Select the individual or entity representing the claimant.
* **Case Manager** (`case_manager`, select_one, optional): Select the case manager.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of review.
* **Reviewed By** (`reviewed_by`, select_one, optional): Select the individual or entity that reviewed the claim.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any relevant comments or notes.
* **Claimant Signature** (`claimant_signature`, text, optional): Enter the claimant's signature.
* **Case Manager Signature** (`case_manager_signature`, text, optional): Enter the case manager's signature.
* **Company Signature** (`company_signature`, text, optional): Enter the company's signature.
* **File Uploads** (`file_uploads`, text, optional): Upload any relevant files as attachments.
* **File Uploads 2** (`file_uploads_2`, text, optional): Upload any additional files as attachments.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click to submit the form.
