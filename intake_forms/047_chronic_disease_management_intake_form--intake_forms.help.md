# Chronic Disease Management Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chronic Disease Management Intake Form is designed to gather essential information about your medical history, symptoms, and lifestyle habits to help healthcare providers develop an effective management plan for your chronic disease.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your demographic information (First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, Email Address, Phone Number).
2. List all your chronic conditions and how long you've had them.
3. Provide a list of any previous treatments or medications you're currently taking.
4. List any medications you're allergic to or have adverse reactions to.
5. Provide a family medical history, including any family members with chronic diseases.
6. Answer questions about your lifestyle habits, including smoking status, alcohol consumption, exercise frequency, and dietary patterns.
7. Describe your current symptoms and their severity.
8. Indicate how symptoms affect your daily activities.
9. Specify your healthcare goals and disease management goals.
10. If applicable, mention any barriers to managing your condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, required): Please enter your given name as it is spelled.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, required): Please enter your surname as it is spelled.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address** (`email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, optional): This is a note field for any relevant medical history information.
* **Primary Chronic Conditions** (`primary_conditions`, required): List all your chronic conditions, e.g., diabetes, hypertension, or asthma.
* **How long have you had these conditions?** (`condition_duration`, required): Describe how long you've had your chronic conditions.
* **Previous Treatments or Medications** (`previous_treatments`, optional): List any previous treatments or medications you've taken.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, required): List all current medications you're taking.
* **Medication Allergies or Adverse Reactions** (`allergies`, required): List any medications you're allergic to or have adverse reactions to.
* **Family Medical History** (`family_history`, optional): This is a note field for any family medical history information.
* **Family History of Chronic Diseases** (`family_conditions`, optional): List any family members with chronic diseases.
* **Lifestyle Factors** (`lifestyle_factors`, optional): This is a note field for any lifestyle factors that may impact your condition.
* **Smoking Status** (`smoking_status`, required): Select your smoking status: Never smoked, Former smoker, or Current smoker.
* **Alcohol Consumption** (`alcohol_use`, required): Select your alcohol consumption frequency: None, Occasional, Moderate, or Heavy.
* **Exercise Frequency Per Week** (`exercise_frequency`, optional): Enter how often you exercise per week.
* **Dietary Pattern** (`diet_type`, optional): Select your dietary pattern: Regular diet, Vegetarian, Vegan, Low-sodium, Diabetic diet, or Other.
* **Symptoms & Functional Status** (`symptoms_assessment`, optional): This is a note field for any symptoms or functional status information.
* **Current Symptoms** (`current_symptoms`, required): Describe your current symptoms.
* **Symptom Severity (1-10)** (`symptom_severity`, required): Rate the severity of your symptoms on a scale of 1-10.
* **How do symptoms affect daily activities?** (`daily_activities`, optional): Describe how your symptoms affect your daily activities.
* **Healthcare Goals** (`healthcare_goals`, optional): This is a note field for any healthcare goals you may have.
* **Disease Management Goals** (`management_goals`, required): Specify your disease management goals.
* **Barriers to Managing Your Condition** (`barriers_to_care`, optional): If applicable, mention any barriers to managing your condition.
