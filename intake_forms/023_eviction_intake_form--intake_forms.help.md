# eviction_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eviction Intake Form is designed to gather information about a tenant's eviction process, including details about the reason for eviction, date and time of eviction, and witness information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Eviction Intake Form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide Tenant Information**: Fill in the tenant's information, including name, and other relevant details.
2. **Choose Reason for Eviction**: Select the reason for eviction from the provided list.
3. **Enter Date and Time of Eviction**: Input the date and time of eviction.
4. **Enter Witness Information**: Fill in the witness's information, including name, title, and other relevant details.
5. **Choose Protections**: Select the protections in place, if any.
6. **Add Additional Information**: Enter any additional witness statements, notes, or other relevant details.
