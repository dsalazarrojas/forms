# Direct Primary Care Membership Enrollment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for enrolling individuals into a Direct Primary Care (DPC) membership program. The purpose of this form is to collect relevant information from prospective members, allowing them to choose a clinic, select a plan that suits their needs, and provide necessary contact details, email, phone number, and other information to process their enrollment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a clinic from the list of provided options.
2. Choose a plan that suits your needs from the available options.
3. If applicable, select "Yes" to indicate you want to join the membership program.
4. Enter your contact details, including your email and phone number.
5. Provide your date and time of enrollment.
6. Enter any additional notes or physician details (if applicable).
7. Add any additional notes or comments to finalize your enrollment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select a clinic** (`clinic_selection`, select_one, required: false): Choose a clinic where you would like to receive care from. Select one of the three available options: Clinic A, Clinic B, or Clinic C.
* **Select a plan** (`membership_plan`, select_one, required: false): Choose a plan that suits your needs from the available options: Basic Plan, Premium Plan, or Deluxe Plan.
* **Enter member details** (`member_details`, select_multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" if you want to be contacted for further details, or "No" if you do not want to be contacted.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, note, required: false): Enter any additional contact information you would like to share with us.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for communication and updates.
* **Date of Enrollement** (`date`, date, required: false): Select the date of your enrollment.
* **Time of Enrollement** (`time`, time, required: false): Select the time of your enrollment.
* **Physician Details** (`physician_details`, note, required: false): Enter any additional details about your physician (if applicable).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments to finalize your enrollment.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately to ensure a smooth enrollment process.
* Review your selections carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
