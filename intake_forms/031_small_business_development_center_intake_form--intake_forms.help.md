# small_business_development_center_intake_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for entrepreneurs and small business owners to submit their information and needs to the Small Business Development Center (SBDC). The SBDC provides various services such as business planning, market research, and financial planning to support the development of small businesses. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the required information in the **Entrepreneur information** field (page 1). 
2. Select one or more services you are interested in from the **services** field (page 2). 
3. Enter your business details in the **Business Name**, **Email**, **Phone**, and **Business Type** fields (pages 3-6). 
4. Provide a brief description of your business and its goals in the **Business Description** and **SBDC Goals** fields (pages 7-8). 
5. Select the services you are interested in using from the **SBDC Services Used** field (page 9). 
6. Enter the name and contact information of the business owner in the **Business Owner** field (page 10). 
7. If applicable, list any programs or services you are interested in from the **SBDC Programs** field (page 11). 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Entrepreneur information
* **Entrepreneur information** (Entrepreneur_info): A text field where you can enter your personal information, such as your name and title. 

### Page 2: SBDC Services
* **SBDC Services** (sbdc_services): A field where you can select one or more services you are interested in, such as Business Planning and Strategy, Market Research, etc. 

### Page 3: Business Name
* **Business Name** (business_name): Enter the name of your business. This is a required field. 

### Page 4: Email
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address. This is a required field. 

### Page 5: Phone
* **Phone** (phone): Enter your phone number. This is a required field. 

### Page 6: Business Type
* **Business Type** (business_type): Select the type of business you have, such as Retail, Service, or Online. This is not a required field. 

### Page 7: Business Location
* **Business Location** (business_location): Enter the location of your business. This is not a required field. 

### Page 8: Business Description
* **Business Description** (business_description): Provide a brief description of your business. This is a required field. 

### Page 9: SBDC Goals
* **SBDC Goals** (sbdc_goals): Enter your goals for using the SBDC services. This is a required field. 

### Page 10: SBDC Services Used
* **SBDC Services Used** (sbdc_services_used): Select the services you are interested in using, such as Business Planning and Strategy, Market Research, etc. This is a required field. 

### Page 11: Business Owner
* **Business Owner** (business_owner): Enter the name and contact information of the business owner. This is a required field. 

### Page 12: SBDC Programs
* **SBDC Programs** (sbdc_programs): If applicable, list any programs or services you are interested in. This is not a required field.
