# Liquid Biopsy Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Liquid Biopsy Request Form is used to collect information for liquid biopsies, a minimally invasive diagnostic procedure. This form is used to gather details about the patient, the sample, and the biopsy process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the required information in each field. Required fields are marked as "required".
2. Fill in the liquid biopsy information (Field 1).
3. Provide patient information (Fields 2-5).
4. Specify the sample type (Field 4).
5. Enter the date and time of the biopsy (Fields 5 and 6).
6. Provide details about the sample (Fields 7-9).
7. Specify the sample condition (Field 12).
8. Update the sample status (Field 13).
9. Enter any additional comments or information about the sample (Field 11).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Liquid Biopsy Information (Field 1)** (`Liquid Biopsy Information`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the liquid biopsy being requested.
* **Patient Information (Field 2)** (`Patient Information`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about the patient, such as patient ID or date of birth.
* **Sample Information (Field 3)** (`Sample Information`, text, optional): Enter any details about the sample, such as sample type or volume.
* **Sample Type (Field 4)** (`Sample Type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of liquid biopsy (Liqui Biopsy, Solid Tumor, CTC, or Circulating Tumor Cells).
* **Date of Biopsy (Field 5)** (`Date of Biopsy`, date, optional): Enter the date of the biopsy.
* **Time of Biopsy (Field 6)** (`Time of Biopsy`, time, optional): Enter the time of the biopsy.
* **Biopsy Location (Field 7)** (`Biopsy Location`, text, optional): Enter any details about the location of the biopsy.
* **Sample Weight (Field 8)** (`Sample Weight`, number, optional): Enter the weight of the sample.
* **Sample Volume (Field 9)** (`Sample Volume`, number, optional): Enter the volume of the sample.
* **Sample Concentration (Field 10)** (`Sample Concentration`, number, optional): Enter the concentration of the sample.
* **Comments (Field 11)** (`Comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the sample.
* **Sample Condition (Field 12)** (`Sample Condition`, select_multiple, optional): Select the condition of the sample (Normal or Abnormal).
* **Sample Status (Field 13)** (`Sample Status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the sample (Requested, Received, In Progress, or Completed).
* **Patient ID (Field 14)** (`Patient ID`, text, optional): Enter the patient ID.
* **Form 15** (`Form 15`, text, optional): This field is currently not used and can be ignored.
* **Biopsy Date (Field 16)** (`Biopsy Date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the biopsy.
* **Time Sample (Field 17)** (`Time Sample`, time, optional): Enter the time of the sample collection.
* **Time Sample Received (Field 18)** (`Time Sample Received`, time, optional): Enter the time the sample was received.
* **Sample Location (Field 19)** (`Sample Location`, text, optional): Enter any details about the location of the sample.
* **Form 20** (`Form 20`, select_multiple, optional): This field is currently not used and can be ignored.
* **Form 21** (`Form 21`, select_one, optional): This field is currently not used and can be ignored.
