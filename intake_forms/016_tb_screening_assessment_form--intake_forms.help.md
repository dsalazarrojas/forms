# tb_screening_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The tb_screening_assessment_form is used to gather essential information about patients suspected of having tuberculosis. This form helps healthcare providers identify potential TB cases and ensure accurate diagnosis and treatment.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's identification information on **Patient Identification**.
2. Provide a brief summary of the patient's **medical history**.
3. Select all applicable options for **tuberculosis_exposure** that match the patient's condition.
4. Select all applicable options for **symptoms** that match the patient's symptoms.
5. Provide details about the patient's travel history on **travel_history**.
6. Fill in the patient's contact information on **contact_info**.
7. Enter the name and contact information of the patient's healthcare provider on **healthcare_provider_name** and **healthcare_provider_contact** (if applicable).
8. Add any additional notes or comments on **additional_notes** (if applicable).
9. Enter the last date the patient was seen on **date_last_seen** (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Identification**: This field is used to input the patient's name, date of birth, or any other identifying information.
* **medical_history**: Please provide a brief summary of the patient's medical history, including any relevant allergies, medical conditions, or previous treatments.
* **tuberculosis_exposure**: Select all options that apply to the patient's exposure to tuberculosis, such as exposure to someone with TB or traveling to a high-risk area.
* **symptoms**: Select all options that apply to the patient's symptoms, such as coughing or weight loss.
* **travel_history**: Provide details about the patient's travel history, including dates and destinations.
* **contact_info**: Select the patient's contact method, such as phone or email.
* **healthcare_provider_name**: Enter the name of the patient's healthcare provider, if applicable.
* **healthcare_provider_contact**: Enter the contact information of the patient's healthcare provider, if applicable.
* **additional_notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the patient's case.
* **date_last_seen**: Enter the last date the patient was seen by the healthcare provider.
