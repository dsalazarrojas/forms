# Real Estate Marketing Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Marketing Intake Form is designed to collect information for real estate marketing purposes. This form is meant to gather data about properties, including their characteristics, marketing status, and target audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name, last name, and email address in the first three fields.
2. Enter your phone number to ensure we can reach you for any further questions or updates.
3. Provide your date of birth and address to help us understand your background and location.
4. Enter your zip code to help us narrow down your target location.
5. Choose whether or not you have a real estate license.
6. Provide a brief description of the property.
7. Enter the property price.
8. Choose the property status (Active or Inactive).
9. Choose the marketing status (Active or Inactive).
10. Select the types of marketing you would like to engage in (Yes or No).
11. Enter the marketing budget for the property.
12. Set the marketing start date.
13. Set the marketing end date.
14. Indicate the target audience for the marketing campaign.
15. Specify the target location for the marketing campaign.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **Zip code**: Enter your zip code.
* **Real estate license**: Check Yes if you have a real estate license, No otherwise.
* **Property description**: Provide a brief description of the property.
* **Property price**: Enter the price of the property.
* **Property status**: Choose Active if the property is currently active, Inactive otherwise.
* **Marketing status**: Choose Active if the marketing campaign is active, Inactive otherwise.
* **Marketing type**: Select Yes for marketing engagement, No otherwise.
* **Marketing budget**: Enter the budget for the marketing campaign.
* **Marketing start date**: Set the start date for the marketing campaign.
* **Marketing end date**: Set the end date for the marketing campaign.
* **Marketing target audience**: Indicate the target audience for the marketing campaign.
* **Marketing target location**: Specify the target location for the marketing campaign.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately to avoid any potential issues with the marketing campaign.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* The information provided will be used to create a comprehensive marketing strategy for your property.
