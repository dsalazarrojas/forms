# holistic_health_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holistic Health Intake Form is a comprehensive form used to gather information about a patient's medical history, contact details, and symptoms. This form helps healthcare professionals assess a patient's overall health status and create a personalized treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Patient Name**, **Contact Number**, and **Email** in their respective fields.
2. In the **Medical History** section, provide a brief summary of your medical history, including any previous illnesses, allergies, or health conditions.
3. If you have any additional medical history that doesn't fit in the provided fields, you can use the **Medical History Other** fields to provide more information.
4. Describe your **Symptoms** in the designated field.
5. List any **Medical Conditions** you are currently experiencing or have experienced in the past.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your primary phone number where you can be contacted for follow-up questions.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Medical History**: Briefly describe any previous medical conditions, allergies, or health issues you have.
* **Medical History Other**: If you have additional medical history not covered in the previous field, provide more details here.
* **Medical History Other 2**: If you have more medical history to report, provide it here.
* **Medical History Other 3**: If you have any other medical history to share, enter it here.
* **Symptoms**: Describe any symptoms you are currently experiencing, such as pain, fatigue, or discomfort.
* **Medical Condition 1**: Enter any current or past medical conditions, such as diabetes, hypertension, or allergies.
* **Medical Condition 2**: Enter any additional medical conditions you have.
* **Medical Condition 3**: Enter any other medical conditions.
* **Medical Condition 4**: Enter any more medical conditions.

## Tips
- Be as thorough as possible when filling out the form to ensure accurate information is collected.
- If you have any doubts or questions, please contact the healthcare professional for clarification.
- The information collected in this form will help our team create a personalized treatment plan tailored to your needs.
