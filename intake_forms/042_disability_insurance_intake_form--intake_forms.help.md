# Disability Insurance Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disability Insurance Intake Form is a crucial tool for collecting essential information about an individual's disability insurance needs. This form helps determine the suitability of an individual for disability insurance coverage, ensuring that their needs are accurately assessed and met.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Begin by filling in your personal details on Page 1, including your contact information and occupation.
2.  On Page 2, you will be asked about your disability type, insurance details, and employment status.
3.  Page 3 will require you to provide information about your medical history, medical certification, and insurance coverage.
4.  Page 4 will ask you about your income and employment status, as well as your occupation duration.
5.  On Page 5, you will need to provide information about your education and occupation duration.
6.  Page 6 will ask you about your income, insurance coverage, and insurance coverage duration.
7.  Page 7 will require you to provide information about your insurance agent and contact information.
8.  Page 8 will ask you about your medical provider and any necessary medical certification information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **1. Disability Insurance Intake Form** (Text, Optional): This section is the title of the form, which you do not need to fill in.
*   **2. Client Information** (Text, Optional): Provide your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
*   **3. Disability Type** (Select One, Optional): Select the type of disability you are applying for (Physical, Mental Health, Medical).
*   **4. Insurance Details** (Text, Optional): Enter any existing insurance details or policies you have.
*   **5. Contact Information** (Text, Optional): Provide your contact information, including phone numbers and email addresses.
*   **6. Insurance Agent Info** (Text, Optional): If you are working with an insurance agent, enter their name and contact information.
*   **7. Medical History** (Text, Optional): Describe your medical history, including any relevant medical conditions or procedures.
*   **8. Medical Certification Info** (Text, Optional): Provide any necessary medical certification information.
*   **9. Employment Status** (Number, Optional): Enter your current employment status, with 1 indicating currently employed and 0 indicating not employed.
*   **10. Income Employment Status** (Number, Optional): Enter your employment status regarding income, with 1 indicating employed and 0 indicating not employed.
*   **11. Income Employment Status Frequency** (Number, Optional): Indicate how often you are employed, with 1 indicating full-time and 0 indicating part-time or irregular.
*   **12. Occupation** (Text, Optional): Enter your occupation or profession.
*   **13. Occupation Duration** (Number, Optional): Enter the duration of your occupation, in years.
*   **14. Education** (Text, Optional): Enter your level of education, such as Bachelor's degree or Master's degree.
*   **15. Education Duration** (Number, Optional): Enter the duration of your education, in years.
*   **16. Income** (Number, Optional): Enter your annual income.
*   **17. Insurance Coverage** (Text, Optional): Enter any existing insurance coverage you have.
*   **18. Insurance Coverage Duration** (Number, Optional): Enter the duration of your current insurance coverage.
*   **19. Insurance Agent** (Text, Optional): Enter the name and contact information of your insurance agent.
*   **20. Contact Number** (Text, Optional): Enter your contact phone number.
*   **21. Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address.
*   **22. Address** (Text, Optional): Enter your address.
*   **23. Date of Birth** (Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
*   **24. Date of Birth Format** (Text, Optional): Enter the format of your date of birth.
*   **25. Medical Provider** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of your medical provider.
