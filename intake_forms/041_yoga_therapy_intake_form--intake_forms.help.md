# yoga_therapy_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from clients for a comprehensive understanding of their medical history, contact information, and other relevant details for a smooth and effective yoga therapy session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Start by filling out the client information section with your name, date of birth, and other relevant details.
2.  In the contact information section, provide your current contact details, including phone number and email.
3.  The medical history section is where you provide any previous medical conditions or surgeries that may be relevant to your therapy.
4.  Please list any allergies you have in the allergies section.
5.  If you have an emergency contact, please provide their name and contact details.
6.  In the health information section, you can provide any other relevant health information that may affect your therapy.
7.  Please indicate your yoga preferences, including any specific styles or levels of practice you enjoy.
8.  Finally, provide your payment information, including any relevant payment details.
9.  Use the notes section to provide any additional comments or information that may be helpful for your therapy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information**: Please provide your name, date of birth, and other relevant details.
* **Contact Info**: Provide your current contact details, including phone number and email.
* **Medical History**: List any previous medical conditions or surgeries that may be relevant to your therapy.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions that may be relevant to your therapy.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies you have.
* **Emergency Contact**: If you have an emergency contact, please provide their name and contact details.
* **Health Information**: Provide any other relevant health information that may affect your therapy.
* **Yoga Preferences**: Indicate your yoga preferences, including any specific styles or levels of practice you enjoy.
* **Payment Information**: Provide your payment information, including any relevant payment details.
* **Notes**: Use this section to provide any additional comments or information that may be helpful for your therapy.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth and effective therapy process.
* If any field is not relevant to you, please fill in "N/A" or "Not Applicable."
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
