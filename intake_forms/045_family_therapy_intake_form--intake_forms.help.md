# Family Therapy Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Therapy Intake Form is designed to collect essential information from family members or caregivers for the purpose of initiating therapy sessions. This form helps therapists understand the family's history, current concerns, and support systems, ultimately aiding in the development of an effective treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the provided text fields with accurate and detailed information.
2. Ensure you answer all questions thoroughly, as this information will help the therapist create a personalized treatment plan.
3. If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask your therapist for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Family Therapy Intake Form** (1): This is the title of the form and is not a field to fill in. Please ignore it.
- **Family History** (2): Describe the family's medical, psychological, or behavioral history, including any relevant diagnoses, treatments, or hospitalizations.
- **Contact Info** (3): Provide your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address (if applicable).
- **Family Counselor Info** (4): Share any relevant information about your family's previous therapy experiences, including the name of the therapist, dates of treatment, and reasons for termination (if applicable).
- **Presenting Problem** (5): Describe the primary concerns or issues that bring you to therapy, including any symptoms or problems that affect the family's daily life.
- **Family History with Therapist** (6): If applicable, provide the name and contact information of the family therapist, including the dates and details of previous sessions.
- **Family Support System** (7): Describe the people who provide emotional or practical support to the family, including their relationship to you and the family.
- **Therapist Counselor Info** (8): Share any relevant information about the family's current or previous therapists, including their name, specialty, and contact information.
- **Family Support System Coping** (9): Describe how the family's support system helps or hinders your coping mechanisms, including any positive or negative effects of support on your mental health.

## Tips

* Please be honest and thorough when filling out this form.
* If you're unsure about any field, don't hesitate to ask your therapist for clarification.
* This form is designed to be a starting point for your therapy sessions, and your therapist will work with you to create a personalized treatment plan.
