# Music Therapy Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and medical conditions of clients seeking music therapy services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "last name" field.
3. If you have any medical conditions, please provide a brief description in the "condition" field.
4. If you have any additional comments or instructions for the music therapist, please enter them in the "music therapist notes" field.
5. Fill in your contact information in the "contact info" field.
6. If you have a valid email address, please enter it in the "email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Condition** (`condition`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions that may affect your music therapy sessions, please provide a brief description here.
* **Music therapist notes** (`music_therapist_notes`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or instructions for the music therapist, please enter them here.
* **Contact info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Please enter your contact information, including phone number and address, for the music therapist to reach you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you have a valid email address, please enter it here for the music therapist to contact you.
