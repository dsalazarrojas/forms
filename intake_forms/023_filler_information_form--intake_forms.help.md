# Filler Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential details about a fillers' business information to facilitate smooth interactions and operations within our organization. It's not meant to be a comprehensive business registration form but a supplementary form to collect specific information to improve communication and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Please fill out each field according to the instructions provided for each section.
- Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure the best possible outcome.
- You can fill out the form on a single page, and it will be automatically saved once you click the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Filler Information** (`filler_information`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the filler.
* **Filler Business Type** (`filler_business_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of business (Business, Nonprofit, Clinic) that accurately represents the filler.
* **Filler Business Name** (`filler_business_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the business.
* **Filler Address** (`filler_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the business.
* **Filler City** (`filler_city`, text, required: false): Enter the city where the business is located.
* **Filler State** (`filler_state`, text, required: false): Enter the state where the business is located.
* **Filler Zip** (`filler_zip`, text, required: false): Enter the zip code of the business location.
* **Filler Phone** (`filler_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the business.
* **Filler E-mail** (`filler_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the business.
* **Filler Date** (`filler_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the filler.

## Tips
Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions or authority to fill out this form on behalf of the filler. If you're unsure, please consult with the relevant authorities or the filler directly.
