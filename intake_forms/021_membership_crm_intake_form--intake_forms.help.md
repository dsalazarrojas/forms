### Membership CRM Intake Form

#### Form Purpose:
The Membership CRM Intake Form is a crucial document used to collect and store membership information from potential or existing members. This form helps the membership committee accurately gather and verify the necessary details required to manage the membership process.

#### How To Complete This Form:
To complete the Membership CRM Intake Form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the "Basic Info" section with your personal details.
2. Provide your "Contact Info" (if applicable).
3. Select your membership type and payment type from the respective dropdowns.
4. Enter your payment date and details.
5. Fill in the "Billing Info" section, which may be required for some payment methods.
6. Select your payment method from the dropdown.
7. Fill in the "Additional Info" section with any relevant details.
8. Enter the "Member Since" and "Expiration Date" (if applicable).
9. If the membership is not active, fill in the "Membership Status" field.
10. Add any additional notes (if necessary).
11. Fill in the "Created By" and "Updated By" fields (if applicable).

#### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Basic Info (1)**: Enter your basic personal information, such as your name, contact number, or other identifying details.
* **Contact Info (2)**: Enter your contact information, such as email or phone number, if applicable.
* **Membership Type (3)**: Select "Member" if you are a registered member, or "Non-Member" if you are not.
* **Payment Type (4)**: Select the type of payment you are using, such as cash or credit card.
* **Payment Date (5)**: Enter the date of the payment.
* **Billing Info (6)**: Enter any relevant billing information, such as your account number or payment method details.
* **Payment Method (7)**: Select the method of payment, such as credit card or bank transfer.
* **Payment Method Details (8)**: Enter any additional details about the payment method, such as card number or bank account information.
* **Membership Status (9)**: Select the status of your membership, such as active or inactive.
* **Notes (10)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your membership.
* **Additional Info (11)**: Enter any additional information about your membership, such as special requests or comments.
* **Member Since (12)**: Enter the date you became a member.
* **Expiration Date (13)**: Enter the date your membership expires (if applicable).
* **Created By (14)**: Enter the name of the person who created this membership record.
* **Updated By (15)**: Enter the name of the person who last updated this membership record.
* **Basic Info 1-4 (16-19)**: Enter additional basic information about yourself, such as your address or other details.

#### Tips:
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your details before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact the membership committee for assistance.
