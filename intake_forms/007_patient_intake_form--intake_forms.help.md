# Patient Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Patient Intake Form is a crucial tool for healthcare providers to collect essential information about patients. This form is used to gather details about the patient's medical history, contact information, and other relevant data to ensure the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please ensure you have all the necessary information before starting to complete the form.
2.  Review each field carefully and fill in the required fields marked as required.
3.  If a field is optional, use your best judgment to determine if it's applicable to your situation.
4.  Double-check your work before submitting the form to avoid any errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name as they would like to be addressed.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the patient's email address for contact purposes.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter the patient's phone number for communication.
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, note, optional): Provide a detailed account of the patient's medical history, if applicable.
*   **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the medical conditions that apply to the patient.
*   **Medical Test Results** (`medical_test_results`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the patient has medical test results available.
*   **Medication Allergies** (`medication_allergies`, select_multiple, optional): List all the medications the patient is allergic to.
*   **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, select_one, optional): Identify the medical provider who prescribed the patient's medication.
*   **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, select_one, optional): Indicate the patient's insurance provider.
*   **Practice Name** (`practice_name`, text, optional): Enter the practice name where the patient is receiving treatment.
*   **Practice Address** (`practice_address`, text, optional): Enter the practice address where the patient is receiving treatment.
*   **Street** (`street`, text, optional): Enter the practice street address.
*   **Suite** (`suite`, text, optional): Enter the practice suite number.
*   **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city of the practice location.
*   **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state of the practice location.
*   **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter the ZIP code of the practice location.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the practice phone number.
*   **Fax** (`fax`, text, optional): Enter the practice fax number.
*   **Website** (`website`, text, optional): Enter the practice website address.

## Tips

*   Please ensure you complete the form accurately and thoroughly.
*   Check your work before submitting to avoid errors.
*   If you're unsure about any field or information, do not hesitate to ask for help.
