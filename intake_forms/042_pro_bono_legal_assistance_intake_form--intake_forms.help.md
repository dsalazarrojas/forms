# Pro Bono Legal Assistance Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pro Bono Legal Assistance Intake Form is designed to collect relevant information from individuals seeking legal assistance. The form helps in identifying the problem, gathering contact details, and understanding the scope of assistance required.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Start by providing your User Information.
*   Describe your legal problem in detail.
*   Select your preferred contact method.
*   Provide your contact number and email address (if applicable).
*   Enter your date of birth.
*   Specify the date and time of the intake.
*   Enter a brief description of your legal issue.
*   Add any additional notes you'd like to provide about yourself or your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Information** (1, `text`, `false`): This field is used to collect basic user information.
    *   Please provide your name and any other relevant information that you feel would be helpful for the intake process.
*   **Legal Problem** (2, `text`, `false`): Describe your legal problem in as much detail as possible. 
    *   Be specific about the issue you are facing, including any relevant dates and circumstances. 
*   **Contact Method** (3, `select_one`, `false`): This field is used to specify how the organization would like to contact you for the intake.
    *   Select your preferred contact method: Home, Office, or Mobile.
*   **Contact Number** (4, `text`, `false`): Provide your contact number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
    *   If you do not have a contact number, please leave this field blank.
*   **Email** (5, `email`, `false`): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted by email.
    *   Please ensure that your email address is accurate and active.
*   **Date of Birthday** (6, `date`, `false`): Provide your date of birth.
    *   This will help us to better understand your age and potentially identify any relevant age-related laws or regulations.
*   **Date of Intake** (7, `date`, `false`): Specify the date on which the intake will take place.
    *   Please use the date format as provided in the instructions. 
*   **Time of Intake** (8, `time`, `false`): Enter the time of the intake.
    *   Use the time format as provided in the instructions. 
*   **Legal Issue Description** (9, `note`, `false`): Write a brief description of your legal issue.
    *   This field is meant to be a general description of your problem. 
*   **User Notes** (10, `note`, `false`): Add any additional notes or information about yourself or your situation.
    *   This field is optional and meant to provide more context or details about your case. 

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you complete the form accurately and thoroughly. 
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the relevant authorities. 
*   We take data privacy seriously, and we will not share your information with anyone without your consent. 
*   Please review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid any errors. 
*   If you need help or clarification on any part of the form, please do not hesitate to ask. 
*   We are here to assist you in the best way we can.
