# Emergency Medical Services Negligence Claim Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Medical Services Negligence Claim Intake Form is a document used to report and document incidents of medical negligence or malpractice. It is designed to gather information about a patient's experience with healthcare providers and to facilitate the handling of claims related to medical negligence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Emergency Medical Services Negligence Claim Intake Form** (Page 1):
	* This is the first page of the form. Please read through the form carefully before proceeding.
2. **Category** (Page 2):
	* Select one of the following categories:
		+ First Name
		+ Last Name
		+ Organization
3. **Claim Description** (Page 3):
	* Provide a detailed description of the incident, including the date and time of the incident.
4. **Date of Incident** (Page 4):
	* Enter the date on which the incident occurred.
5. **Time of Incident** (Page 5):
	* Enter the time at which the incident occurred.
6. **Location of Incident** (Page 6):
	* Enter the location where the incident occurred.
7. **Medical Condition** (Page 7):
	* Describe the medical condition related to the incident.
8. **Description of Injuries** (Page 8):
	* Provide a detailed description of the injuries sustained during the incident.
9. **Witness Statements** (Page 9):
	* Include any witness statements or statements from other relevant individuals.
10. **Medical Experts** (Page 10):
	* Select any medical experts who may be involved in the case.
11. **Supporting Evidence** (Page 11):
	* Attach any supporting evidence, such as medical records or witness statements.
12. **Claim Status** (Page 12):
	* Select the current status of the claim:
		+ Open
		+ Closed
		+ Denied
13. **Follow-up Date** (Page 13):
	* Enter the date for follow-up on the claim.
14. **Follow-up Time** (Page 14):
	* Enter the time for follow-up on the claim.
15. **Follow-up Location** (Page 15):
	* Enter the location for follow-up on the claim.
16. **Follow-up Remarks** (Page 16):
	* Enter any remarks or comments related to the follow-up.
17. **Follow-up Status** (Page 17):
	* Select the current status of the follow-up:
		+ Open
		+ Closed
		+ Denied
