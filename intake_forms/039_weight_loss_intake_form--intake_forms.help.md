# Weight Loss Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Weight Loss Intake Form is used to gather information about your weight loss goals and medical history. This form will help our staff understand your needs and provide you with the best possible support for achieving your weight loss goals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all the required questions, indicated by an asterisk (\*) symbol.
2. Select all relevant medical history and treatment needs that apply to you.
3. Specify your weight loss goals, including your preferred contact method (e.g., phone, email, or text message).
4. Enter your height and weight in the corresponding fields.
5. Select the unit of measurement for your height and weight.
6. Enter your age.
7. Ensure you enter your contact information accurately.
8. Answer any additional medical history questions that apply to you.
9. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, select_multiple): This field asks about your medical history, which may include any previous surgeries, medical conditions, or allergies. Please select all that apply.
* **Treatment Needs** (`treatment_needs`, select_multiple): This field asks about any medical treatments or therapies you have undergone or are currently undergoing. Please select all that apply.
* **Weight Loss Goals** (`weight_loss_goals`, select_multiple): This field asks about your weight loss goals, including any specific objectives you have in mind (e.g., to lose a certain amount of weight, to improve overall health, etc.). Please select all that apply.
* **Vital Information** (`vital_information`, time): This field collects information about your vital signs, such as your height and weight. Enter your height and weight in the corresponding fields.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, email): This field collects your contact information, such as your email address or phone number. Ensure that you enter this information accurately, as it will help us contact you for further support.
* **Height** (`height`, number): Enter your height in the corresponding field.
* **Weight** (`weight`, number): Enter your weight in the corresponding field.
* **Blood Pressure** (`blood_pressure`, number): This field asks about your blood pressure reading. Please enter your reading accurately.
* **Height Unit** (`height_unit`, select_one): Select the unit of measurement for your height (feet, inches, or cm).
* **Weight Unit** (`weight_unit`, select_one): Select the unit of measurement for your weight (kg or lbs).
* **Age** (`age`, number): Enter your age.
* **Contact Preferred Contact Method** (`contact_preferred_contact_method`, select_multiple): Select your preferred contact method for communication (phone, email, or text message).
* **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, select_multiple): This field asks about any additional medical history or conditions that may impact your weight loss. Please select all that apply.
* **Medical History 3** (`medical_history_3`, select_multiple): This field asks about any further medical history or conditions that may impact your weight loss. Please select all that apply.
* **Weight Loss Goals 2** (`weight_loss_goals_2`, select_multiple): This field asks about any additional weight loss goals or objectives you have. Please select all that apply.
* **Contact Information 2** (`contact_info_2`, email): This field is a repeat of the Contact Information field, ensuring the accuracy of your contact information.
* **Weight Loss Goals 3** (`weight_loss_goals_3`, select_multiple): This field asks about any additional weight loss goals or objectives you have. Please select all that apply.
* **Medical History 4** (`medical_history_4`, select_multiple): This field asks about any further medical history or conditions that may impact your weight loss. Please select all that apply.
