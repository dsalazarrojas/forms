# LLC Filing Intake Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from businesses for the purpose of the LLC (Limited Liability Company) filing process. The goal is to gather accurate and complete data from business owners to facilitate a smooth and efficient filing process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please carefully review each field and provide accurate information. The form is divided into multiple sections, each with specific fields that correspond to different aspects of your business. Follow these steps:

1. **User Input**: Enter your basic contact information in the first field.
2. Select your response to the Filing Type question to determine the type of LLC filing required.
3. Provide your business's details, including name, address, phone number, email, and other relevant information.
4. Specify your business's type, status, and entity type.
5. Confirm the date your business was opened.
6. Enter any additional notes or comments about your business.
7. Finally, submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. User Input (Required)
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, required): Enter your name and contact information to allow our team to contact you with any questions or to update you on the status of your LLC filing.

### 2. Filing Type (Not required)
* **Filing Type** (`filing_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of LLC filing you are submitting: Yes/No. If you are unsure, you can consult our resources for guidance.

### 3. Business Name (Not required)
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your business as it appears on all official documents.

### 4. Business Address (Not required)
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, optional): Provide the physical address of your business location.

### 5. Business Phone (Not required)
* **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your business.

### 6. Business Email (Not required)
* **Business Email** (`business_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address associated with your business.

### 7. Attorney Name (Not required)
* **Attorney Name** (`attorney_name`, text, optional): If you are working with an attorney, enter their name here.

### 8. Attorney Phone (Not required)
* **Attorney Phone** (`attorney_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number associated with your attorney.

### 9. Business Type (Not required)
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_one, optional): Select Yes/No to indicate the type of your business (e.g., Corporation, Partnership, etc.).

### 10. Date Opened (Not required)
* **Date Opened** (`date_opened`, date, optional): Enter the date your business was opened.

### 11. Status (Not required)
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, optional): Select Active/Inactive to indicate the status of your business.

### 12. Business Entity (Not required)
* **Business Entity** (`business_entity`, select_multiple, optional): Select Yes/No to indicate the type of business entity (e.g., Corporation, Partnership, etc.).

### 13. State (Not required)
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state where your business is located.

### 14. Zip (Not required)
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter the postal code associated with your business's location.

### 15. City (Not required)
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city associated with your business's location.

### 16. State Abbr (Not required)
* **State Abbr** (`state_abbreviation`, text, optional): Enter the abbreviation for the state where your business is located.

### 17. Country (Not required)
* **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Enter the country associated with your business.

### 18. County (Not required)
* **County** (`county`, text, optional): Enter the county associated with your business's location.

### 19. Fax (Not required)
* **Fax** (`fax`, text, optional): Enter the fax number associated with your business.

### 20. Business Entity Type (Not required)
* **Business Entity Type** (`business_entity_type`, select_one, optional): Select Yes/No to indicate the type of business entity (e.g., Corporation, Partnership, etc.).

### 21. Business Entity Number (Not required)
* **Business Entity Number** (`business_entity_number`, text, optional): Enter the number associated with your business entity.

### 22. Business Entity Formation Method (Not required)
* **Business Entity Formation Method** (`business_entity_formation_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select Yes/No to indicate the method used to form your business entity.

### 23. Notes (Not required)
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your business.

### 24. Submitted By (Not required)
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting this form.

### 25. Submitted Date (Not required)
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, optional): Enter the date and time you submitted this form.

## Tips
* Please double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact our support team for guidance.
* Our team will review your submission and contact you with any questions or to update you on the status of your LLC filing.
