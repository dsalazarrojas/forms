# Footwear Wear Analysis Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Footwear Wear Analysis Intake Form is designed to collect comprehensive information about a user's footwear wear, including pain reports, gait assessment, and other relevant factors. This form helps our team understand the user's footwear wear and provide informed recommendations for prevention and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review each field carefully and provide detailed information about your footwear wear.
2.  Fill in all required fields, indicated by a red asterisk (*).
3.  Use the provided options for select fields, and type your answer in the text fields.
4.  Submit the form when you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Footwear Wear Analysis Intake** (1): This is the main title of the form. You can leave it blank.
* **Footwear Photos** (2): Please provide pictures or descriptions of your footwear, including the brand, model, and condition.
* **Pain Reports** (3): Describe any pain or discomfort you experience while wearing your footwear.
* **Gait Assessment** (4): Rate your gait, from 1 (very poor) to 10 (very good).
* **Footwear Wear** (5): Describe your footwear usage, including duration and frequency.
* **Footwear Usage** (6): Describe how you use your footwear, e.g., walking, running, or standing.
* **Other Issues** (7): If you have any other issues or concerns related to your footwear wear, describe them here.
* **User Info** (8): Provide any additional information about yourself that might be relevant to your footwear wear analysis.
* **Medical Conditions** (9): Check if you have any medical conditions that may affect your footwear wear.
* **Medical Procedure** (10): Check if you have undergone any medical procedure that may affect your footwear wear.
* **Shoe Info** (11): Check if you have any information about your shoes, such as brand and model.
* **Shoe Brand** (12): Check if you know the brand of your shoes.
* **Shoe Model** (13): Check if you know the model of your shoes.
* **Shoe Size** (14): Type in your shoe size.
* **Shoe Width** (15): Type in your shoe width.
* **Shoe Condition** (16): Describe the condition of your shoes, including any damage or wear.
* **User Height** (17): Type in your height.
* **User Weight** (18): Type in your weight.
* **User Age** (19): Type in your age.
* **User Gender** (20): Check if you identify as male, female, or other.
* **User Height Unit** (21): Check if your height is in feet and inches or centimeters.
* **User Weight Unit** (22): Check if your weight is in pounds or kilograms.
* **User Weight Range** (23): If you have a range of weights, type it in.
* **User Notes** (24): Provide any additional information or comments about your footwear wear.
* **User Comments** (25): Provide any final comments or feedback about your footwear wear.

Note: This guide will help you understand the form's structure and requirements to ensure accurate and complete submission. Please review each field carefully and provide detailed information to get the best results from your footwear wear analysis.
