# Elderly Care Resident Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic information about elderly care residents, including their name, date of birth, address, and contact phone number. This information will be used to provide personalized care and services to the residents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined below:

1.  Start by filling in the "Resident Information Form" field with the resident's name.
2.  Enter the resident's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
3.  Enter the resident's address in the "Address" field.
4.  Enter the resident's phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Resident Information Form** (`elderly_care_resident_info`, Text, Optional):
    This field is for providing a brief description of the resident or any additional relevant details.
*   **Resident Name** (`elderly_care_resident_name`, Text, Optional):
    Enter the resident's full name.
*   **Date of Birth** (`elderly_care_resident_dob`, Date, Optional):
    Enter the resident's date of birth in the format "YYYY-MM-DD" (Year-Month-Day).
*   **Address** (`elderly_care_resident_address`, Text, Optional):
    Enter the resident's address, including street number, street name, city, state, and zip code.
*   **Phone** (`elderly_care_resident_phone`, Text, Optional):
    Enter the resident's phone number.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
*   You can save and edit the form to make changes before submitting it.
*   If the resident has any medical conditions or allergies, please consult with the care team before filling out this form.

Note: This form is not a formal medical record and is used solely for the purpose of providing elderly care services.
