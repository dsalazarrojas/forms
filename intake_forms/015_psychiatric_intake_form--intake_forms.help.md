# psychiatric_intake_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information from patients for psychiatric intake purposes. It's crucial to provide accurate and complete answers to ensure proper assessment and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Answer each question carefully and thoroughly.
2. Provide as much detail as possible for each field.
3. If you're unsure about any question or feel that a field is not relevant, please contact your healthcare provider or staff member for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Information
* **Patient Information (Text)**: This is the first step in the intake process. Please enter your name and any other identifying information as instructed.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information (Text)**: This field is used to gather your contact information, which may be used for follow-up communication with your healthcare provider.

### Emergency Contact Information
* **Emergency Contact Information (Text)**: This field is for providing contact information for a family member or friend who can be reached in case of an emergency.

### Medical History
* **Medical History (Text)**: Please describe any previous medical conditions or allergies you have.

### Psychiatric Medical History
* **Psychiatric Medical History (Text)**: This field is for detailing any previous psychiatric treatment or mental health issues.

### Social History
* **Social History (Text)**: Please provide a brief overview of your social background, including any significant events or relationships that may be relevant to your treatment.

### Family History
* **Family History (Text)**: This field is used to gather information about your family medical history, including any mental health issues or illnesses.

### Mental Status
* **Mental Status (Text)**: Please describe your current mental status, including any symptoms or concerns you may be experiencing.

### Psychiatric Diagnosis
* **Psychiatric Diagnosis (Select Multiple)**: Choose any applicable psychiatric diagnoses from the provided options.

### Medication List
* **Medication List (Text)**: List any current medications you are taking, including dosages and frequencies.

### Allergy
* **Allergy (Text)**: This field is for listing any allergies you have.

### Treatment Plan
* **Treatment Plan (Text)**: This field is for outlining any specific treatment plans or goals you have for your mental health care.

Note: This form may be subject to changes or updates. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your healthcare provider or staff member.
