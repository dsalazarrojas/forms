# Kink Lifestyle Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Kink Lifestyle Submission Form is a tool used by the Kink Community to gather information about its members' kink interests and boundaries. This form is designed to help the community build a better understanding of its members' needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your name and email address, as well as any other information you feel comfortable sharing.
2. Select your kink interests from the drop-down menu, choosing all that apply.
3. Indicate your boundaries and any other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Details (member_details)**: This is a text field where you can provide any additional details about yourself.
* **Kink Interests (kink_interests)**: This is a drop-down menu where you can select all the kink activities or interests that apply to you. Please select all that apply.
* **Boundaries (boundaries)**: This is a text field where you can indicate your boundaries or any other relevant details.
* **Consent (consent)**: This field is for indicating whether you consent to certain activities or not. Please select "True" if you are consenting to all activities, "False" if you are not consenting to any activities, or "Maybe" if you need more information.
* **Interests (member_interests)**: This is a drop-down menu where you can select all the interests or activities that apply to you. Please select all that apply.
* **Name (name)**: This is the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Email (email)**: This is the email address of the person submitting the form.
* **Phone (phone)**: This is the phone number of the person submitting the form.
* **Name Confirmation (name_confirmation)**: This field is a place to confirm the name provided.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form completely and honestly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the Kink Community moderators for assistance.
* Remember to keep your personal and kink-related information private.
* Only submit one form per person.
* Make sure to double-check your selections before submitting the form.

Please note that this form is intended for kink-identified individuals and is not a survey. The information collected will be used by the Kink Community to better understand its members' needs and preferences.
