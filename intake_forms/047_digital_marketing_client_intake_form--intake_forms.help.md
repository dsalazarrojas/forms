# Digital Marketing Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients about their digital marketing needs and goals. The purpose of this form is to gather essential details that will help us understand your requirements and provide you with the best possible solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form in a new tab or window to avoid losing your progress.
2. Start with the first page, "Client Information", where you will be asked to provide your name and contact details.
3. Proceed to the second page, "Job Details", where you will be asked to describe your marketing needs and goals.
4. Finally, move to the third page, "Additional Information", where you can add any extra details or notes that you would like to include.
5. Once you have completed all the pages, review your form and make sure to click the submit button to send it to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Information
* **Name** (Client Information 1, Text, Required): Enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Contact Information** (Client Information 2, Text, Required): Provide your email address and/or phone number so we can get in touch with you.
### Job Details
* **Job Details** (Job Details 1, Text, Required): Describe your marketing needs and goals, so we can understand what you're looking for.
* **Job Description** (Job Details 2, Text, Optional): Add any additional details about your job that will help us serve you better.
### Additional Information
* **Additional Information** (Additional Information 1, Text, Optional): Include any extra information that you think is relevant to your marketing needs.
* **Notes** (Additional Information 2, Text, Optional): Add any notes or comments that you would like to share with us.

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide as much information as possible, so we can better understand your needs.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask us for clarification.
* You can always reach out to us if you need assistance with anything related to your marketing needs.
