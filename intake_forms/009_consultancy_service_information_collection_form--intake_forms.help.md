# Consultancy Service Information Collection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us better understand your consultancy service needs. By completing this form, you will be providing us with valuable information that will allow us to tailor our services to meet your specific requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
2. Be accurate and truthful when providing information.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`client_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`client_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, required): Provide a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, text, required): Enter your primary contact number.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of your organization.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, optional): Select the number of employees in your organization.
	+ 1-10
	+ 11-50
	+ 51-200
	+ 201-500
	+ 501-1000
	+ 1000+
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, required): Select the industry sector your company operates in.
	+ Technology
	+ Healthcare
	+ Finance
	+ Manufacturing
	+ Retail
	+ Education
	+ Government
	+ Nonprofit
	+ Energy
	+ Other
* **Service Type Needed** (`service_type`, select_one, required): Select the consultancy service you require.
	+ Strategic Planning
	+ Business Analysis
	+ Process Improvement
	+ Change Management
	+ Technology Consulting
	+ Financial Consulting
	+ HR Consulting
	+ Marketing Consulting
	+ Risk Management
	+ Other
* **Project Scope** (`project_scope`, select_one, required): Select the scope of the project.
	+ Small - Single Department
	+ Medium - Multiple Departments
	+ Large - Organization Wide
	+ Enterprise - Multiple Locations
* **Project Objectives** (`project_objectives`, text, required): Describe the objectives you hope to achieve.
* **Current Challenges** (`current_challenges`, text, required): Describe the challenges you are facing.
* **Desired Timeline** (`project_timeline`, select_one, required): Select the desired project timeline.
	+ Urgent - Within 2 Weeks
	+ 1 Month
	+ 2-3 Months
	+ 3-6 Months
	+ 6+ Months
	+ Flexible
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, optional): Select your estimated budget for this project.
	+ Under $10,000
	+ $10,000 - $25,000
	+ $25,000 - $50,000
	+ $50,000 - $100,000
	+ $100,000 - $250,000
	+ Over $250,000
	+ To Be Determined
* **Decision Makers Involved** (`decision_makers`, text, optional): List the people involved in the decision-making process.
* **Have You Used Consultants Before** (`previous_consulting`, select_one, optional): Select if you have prior consultancy experience.
	+ True
	+ False
* **Previous Consulting Experience** (`previous_experience`, text, optional): Describe your past experience with consultants.
* **Key Stakeholders** (`stakeholders`, text, optional): List key people who should be involved.
* **Success Criteria** (`success_criteria`, text, required): Describe how you will measure success.
* **Preferred Start Date** (`preferred_start_date`, date, optional): Select the date you would like to begin the project.
* **Additional Requirements** (`additional_requirements`, text, optional): Enter any special requirements or considerations.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, optional): Select the source of your referral.
	+ Website
	+ Social Media
	+ Referral
	+ Conference
	+ Advertisement
	+ Search Engine
	+ Other
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any other information you would like to share.
