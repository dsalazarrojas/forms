# Church Intake Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is to collect personal and church-related information from new members of the church.
## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, start by filling in your personal details and contact information on the first part of the form, then proceed to the household and family section, followed by your church context and membership status, and finally, the last section about your interests and skills.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Personal Information --** 
  • *Full Name* (Required, text): Fill in your full name.
  • *Date of Birth* (Required, date): Enter your date of birth in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
  • *Phone Number* (Required, text): Enter your phone number in the international format (country code + number, e.g., +1-XXX-XXX-XXXX).
  • *Email Address* (Required, email): Enter your email address (e.g., johndoe@domain.com).
  • *Residential Address* (Required, text): Enter your current home address (e.g., street number and name + city + state/region + postal code).
  • *Preferred Name/Nickname* (Optional, text): If you prefer to be called by a nickname, enter it here.

* **-- Household and Family --** 
  • *Marital Status* (Optional, select one): Choose your current marital status from the options provided.
  • *Family Members (Living in Household)* (Optional, text): List the names and relationships of family members living with you.
  • *Best Time to Reach You* (Required, select one): Choose a time when you are usually available to receive calls or messages (e.g., Weekday Morning, Weekday Afternoon, Weekday Evening, Weekend).

* **-- Church Context --** 
  • *Current Membership Status* (Required, select one): Indicate your current status in the church (e.g., Seeking Membership, Registered Member, Regular Attendee, Occasional Visitor).
  • *Ministries you're interested in Joining* (Optional, select multiple): Choose any ministries that you would like to be part of (e.g., Worship, Children, etc.).
  • *Skills or Talents you'd like to Contribute* (Optional, text): If you have a skill or talent you'd like to share with the church, list it here.
  • *Current Prayer Requests* (Optional, text): If you have any prayer requests, list them here.
  • *Any other information for the ministry team* (Optional, text): Add any additional comments or information that the ministry team should know.

* **Today's Date** (Required, date): Enter the current date in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
 
This form helps the church intake process by collecting vital information to create a complete member profile. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your information is correctly updated. 
Note: All required fields are marked as "Required, <field_type>" for clarity.
