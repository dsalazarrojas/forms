# Family Law Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Law Intake Form is designed to collect essential information from clients seeking family law services. This form helps us understand their personal and financial situation, allowing us to provide effective support and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Enter your name and contact details (if applicable).
3. Provide your address and date of birth.
4. Select your marital status and number of dependents.
5. List any family members you would like to include in the support process.
6. If applicable, provide your emergency contact's name, relationship, and address.
7. Select your client counsel type (Family Law or Probate).
8. If necessary, provide additional comments or explanations.
9. Upload any relevant documents (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter your full name as you would like to be referred to.
* **Contact Number**: If you have a contact number, please enter it. This can be a phone number or any other contact method (e.g., mobile phone number).
* **Email Address**: Enter your personal email address, if applicable.
* **Address**: Provide your current address.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the standard date format (DD/MM/YY).
* **Marital Status**: Select one of the following options: Single, Married, Divorced.
* **Employment Status**: Enter your current employment status.
* **Number of Dependents**: If you have dependents (e.g., children, spouse), enter the number of people who depend on you for support.
* **Employment Income**: If you have income from employment, enter the amount.
* **Family Members**: Select all family members you would like to include in the support process (e.g., Spouse, Children, Parents).
* **Emergency Contact Name**: If you have an emergency contact, enter their name.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Describe the relationship between you and your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: If applicable, provide your emergency contact's address.
* **Client Counsel Type**: Select one of the following options: Family Law, Probate.
* **Additional Comments**: If necessary, add any additional comments or explanations to provide context about your situation.
* **Document Upload**: If required, upload any relevant documents to support your case.

## Tips
* Double-check your details before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* Make sure to provide all required fields marked with an asterisk (*). 
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification or guidance.
* The form is designed to be completed by the client, so please be honest and accurate in your responses.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
