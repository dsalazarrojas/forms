## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully and fill out the required information accurately.
2. For fields with options, select the relevant choice that best describes your situation.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
4. If you need help or clarification on any field, do not hesitate to ask the medical staff for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **patient_info** (`patient_info`, text, required: false): This field is for providing general information about yourself, such as your name, age, and contact details. Please ensure that the information provided is up-to-date and accurate.
- **medical_history** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): This field is for describing your medical history, including any previous medical conditions, surgeries, or allergies. Please be as detailed as possible, but concise.
- **contact_info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): This field is for providing your contact details, including your address, phone number, and email. Please ensure that this information is accurate and up-to-date.
- **appointment_date** (`appointment_date`, date, required: false): This field is for selecting the date and time of your scheduled appointment. Please choose a date and time that is convenient for you.
- **medical_condition** (`medical_condition`, select_one, required: false): This field is for selecting any medical conditions that you have, such as Hypertension, Diabetes, or Other. Please choose the one that best describes your situation.
- **medical_insurance** (`medical_insurance`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is for selecting your medical insurance coverage, such as Blue Cross, Blue Shield, or Medicare. Please choose all that apply.
- **emergency_contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false): This field is for providing information about your emergency contact, including their name, phone number, and relationship to you. Please ensure that this information is accurate and up-to-date.
- **patient_relationship** (`patient_relationship`, text, required: false): This field is for describing the relationship between the emergency contact and you, such as family member or friend. Please choose the one that best describes your situation.

## Tips

* Take your time and fill out the form carefully to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask the medical staff for clarification.
* Please provide as much detail as possible for each field to ensure effective care.
* If you need to make any changes to your information, please inform the medical staff immediately.
