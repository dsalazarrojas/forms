# Medical Outreach Vaccination Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for patients to provide their consent for medical outreach and vaccination services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each section thoroughly and accurately.
2. Review the provided options for select fields.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Details**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and other demographic information.
* **Medical History**: Provide a detailed description of the patient's medical history.
* **Consent**: Indicate whether the patient gives their consent for medical outreach and vaccination services.
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the correct format (yyyy-mm-dd).
* **Vaccination Type**: Select 'Yes' if the patient is due for vaccination, 'No' otherwise.
* **Vaccination Status**: Select 'Active' if the patient is up-to-date with vaccinations, 'Inactive' otherwise.
* **Vaccination Date**: Enter the date of the last vaccination if any.
* **Doctor Name**: Enter the name of the doctor who is treating the patient.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the patient's contact number for follow-up.
* **Email**: Enter the patient's email address.
* **Medical Notes**: Enter any relevant medical notes or instructions for healthcare professionals.
* **Tips**:
	+ Double-check the patient's information for accuracy.
	+ Ensure that all fields are completed before submitting the form.
	+ If you're unsure about any field, consult with healthcare professionals or seek clarification.
