# Kinesiology Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Kinesiology Intake Form is designed to collect essential information about a client's medical and physical history, goals, and treatment plan for kinesiology purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the first name and last name fields with the client's full name.
2. Enter the client's email address in the Email field.
3. Provide the client's phone number in the Phone Number field.
4. In the Medical History field, provide a brief description of the client's medical history.
5. In the Medical History Details field, enter a detailed description of the client's medical history.
6. In the Medical Notes field, enter any additional medical notes or comments.
7. In the Physical History field, enter a detailed description of the client's physical history.
8. In the Injury Details field, provide a detailed description of the client's injury.
9. Enter a detailed description of the client's treatment plan in the Treatment Plan field.
10. In the Client Goals field, enter a detailed description of the client's goals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the client's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter the client's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, note, optional): Enter a brief description of the client's medical history.
* **Medical History Details** (`medical_history_details`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the client's medical history.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional medical notes or comments.
* **Physical History** (`physical_history`, note, optional): Enter a detailed description of the client's physical history.
* **Injury Details** (`injury_details`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the client's injury.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the client's treatment plan.
* **Client Goals** (`client_goals`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the client's goals.
* **Therapist Notes** (`therapist_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments from the therapist.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the therapist has a complete understanding of the client's needs.
* This form is intended for clients who are seeking kinesiology services, such as physical therapy or rehabilitation. If you are not a client, please do not complete this form.
