# Tax Preparation Virtual Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Preparation Virtual Client Intake Form is designed to collect essential information from clients to prepare their taxes efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with all required and optional fields as necessary.
2. Be accurate and thorough in providing your information.
3. Review and submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information** (client_info, text, not required): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Tax Year** (tax_year, number, not required): Specify the year for which you are preparing taxes.
* **Tax Return Type** (tax_return_type, select_one, not required):
	+ Choose the type of tax return you require:
		- Item 1
		- Item 2
		- Item 3
* **Income Information** (income_info, text, not required): Provide any relevant details about your income, such as income from employment or investments.
* **Expenses Information** (expenses_info, text, not required): List any expenses you wish to claim on your tax return.
* **Tax Return Status** (tax_return_status, select_multiple, not required):
	+ Select the status of your tax return:
		- Active
		- Pending
		- Completed
		- Canceled
* **Tax Return Note** (tax_return_note, note, not required): Add any additional comments or notes about your tax return.
* **Client E-mail** (client_email, email, not required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Client Phone** (client_phone, text, not required): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Assigned Tool** (assigned_tool, select_one, not required):
	+ Choose the tool assigned to you for tax preparation:
		- chatjimmy
		- other
* **Other Tool** (other_assigned_tool, text, not required): If you selected "other" for the assigned tool, specify the tool name.
* **Tax Return Deadline** (tax_return_deadline, date, not required): Enter the deadline for submitting your tax return.
* **Tax Return Time** (tax_return_time, time, not required): Enter the time of submission for your tax return.
* **Tax Return Date** (tax_return_date, date, not required): Enter the date of submission for your tax return.

Note: This form is designed to collect information to facilitate the tax preparation process. Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly to avoid any issues during the tax preparation process.
