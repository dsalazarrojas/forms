# Dependent Tax Credit Eligibility Intake - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from clients regarding their dependent tax credit eligibility, which can help provide financial benefits to families with dependents.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select the relevant tax year for the form.
2. Choose whether you are eligible or ineligible for dependent tax credits.
3. Identify the type of dependent(s) you have.
4. Provide more information about your dependents.
5. Specify the amount of tax credit you are applying for.
6. Sign and date the form to confirm your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tax Year** (id: 2, select_one, required: false): Select the relevant tax year for the form.
* **Tax Credit Eligibility** (id: 3, select_multiple, required: false): Choose whether you are Eligible, Ineligible, or Pending Review for dependent tax credits.
* **Dependents** (id: 4, select_multiple, required: false): Identify the type of dependent(s) you have, such as Child, Spouse, Parent, or Other.
* **Dependent Details** (id: 5, text, required: false): Provide more information about your dependent(s).
* **Tax Credit Amount** (id: 6, number, required: false): Specify the amount of tax credit you are applying for.
* **Signature** (id: 7, note, required: false): Sign and date the form to confirm your information.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submission to ensure all fields are complete.
* If you need assistance or have questions, please reach out to financial aid or other support staff for help.
