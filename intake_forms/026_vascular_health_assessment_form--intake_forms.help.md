# Vascular Health Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from patients to help healthcare professionals assess their vascular health. It covers various aspects, including patient information, medical history, symptoms, consent, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Patient Info**: Fill out the basic patient information section with the patient's name, age, and any other relevant details.
2. **Medical History**: Provide a brief summary of the patient's medical history, including any previous diagnoses, treatments, or allergies.
3. **Vascular Symptoms**: Select all the symptoms that apply to the patient, such as chest pain, arm weakness, shortness of breath, and so on.
4. **Consent**: Confirm that the patient has provided their consent for the assessment.
5. **Vascular Ratings**: If required, fill out the vascular ratings section with any relevant details.
6. **Blood Pressure**: Record the patient's blood pressure reading (if applicable).
7. **Medications**: List any medications the patient is currently taking.
8. **Allergies**: List any allergies the patient has.
9. **Medical Conditions**: List any pre-existing medical conditions the patient may have.
10. **Vital Signs**: Record the patient's vital signs, if necessary.
11. **Patient Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the patient's condition or treatment.
