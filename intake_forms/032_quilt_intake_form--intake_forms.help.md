# Quilt Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Quilt Intake Form is designed for customers seeking to create custom quilts. It allows you to provide information about your project, including your design preferences, quilt specifications, and other details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully before filling out the sections.
2. Complete each field according to the explanations provided.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Design Preferences** (1) (`design_preferences`, `text`, required/optional)

* A text box where you can describe your design preferences for the quilt. This can include colors, patterns, and any specific design elements you like or dislike.

### 2. **Quilt Specifications** (2) (`quilt_specifications`, `text`, required/optional)

* Describe the specific requirements and specifications you have for the quilt, including size, material type, and any other details.

### 3. **Timeline Requirements** (3) (`timeline_requirements`, `text`, required/optional)

* Provide information about your expected timeline for completion, including any deadlines or specific dates for delivery.

### 4. **Desired Finish Date** (4) (`desired_finish_date`, `date`, required/optional)

* Enter the desired date for the quilt to be completed.

### 5. **Fabric Color Scheme** (5) (`fabric_color_scheme`, `select_multiple`, required/optional)

* Select from the following options: Light, Dark, Custom.
* Choose one or more options that best describe the color scheme you have in mind for your quilt.

### 6. **Fabric Type** (6) (`fabric_type`, `select_multiple`, required/optional)

* Select from the following options: Cotton, Linen, Silk, Rayon, Wool, Synthetic.
* Choose one or more fabric types that best suit your needs.

### 7. **Quilt Size** (7) (`quilt_size`, `number`, required/optional)

* Enter the desired size for your quilt.

### 8. **Material Type** (8) (`material_type`, `select_multiple`, required/optional)

* Select from the following options: Quilts, Tapestries, Bed Quilts.
* Choose one or more material types that best fit your needs.

### 9. **Thread Color** (9) (`thread_color`, `select_multiple`, required/optional)

* Select from the following options: Color 1, Color 2, Color 3, Color 4, Color 5, Color 6.
* Choose one or more thread colors that best match your design preferences.

### 10. **Customer Name** (10) (`customer_name`, `text`, required/optional)

* Enter your name for reference and contact purposes.

### 11. **Custom Quilts** (11) (`custom_quilts`, `select_multiple`, required/optional)

* Select 'True' if you have any custom requirements or requests, or 'False' if you don't have any.

### 12. **Fabric Width** (12) (`fabric_width`, `number`, required/optional)

* Enter the width of the fabric you plan to use.

### 13. **Design Type** (13) (`design_type`, `select_multiple`, required/optional)

* Select from the following options: Hand, Machine, Both.
* Choose the design method that best suits your needs.

### 14. **Material Width** (14) (`material_width`, `number`, required/optional)

* Enter the width of the material you plan to use.

### 15. **Customer E-mail** (15) (`customer_email`, `email`, required/optional)

* Enter your email address for contact purposes.

### 16. **Customer Phone** (16) (`customer_phone`, `select_multiple`, required/optional)

* Select from the following options: Mobile, Home, Other.
* Choose how you would like to be contacted.

### 17. **Customer Address** (17) (`customer_address`, `text`, required/optional)

* Enter your address for delivery purposes.
