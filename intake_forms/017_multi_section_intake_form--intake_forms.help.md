# Multi Section Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Multi Section Intake Form is a comprehensive form designed to collect various types of information from clients in a single document. This form is intended to gather essential details about clients, including their contact information, medical history, family background, social support, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the type of information being requested.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
3. Use the provided fields to gather the necessary information from the client.
4. Submit the completed form for further review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details (1)**: Enter the client's basic details such as name, address, and date of birth.
* **Contact Info (2)**: Provide the client's contact information, including phone number and email address.
* **Contact Info 2 (3)**: Enter any additional contact information for the client (e.g., work phone number, work email address).
* **Medical History (4)**: Describe the client's medical history, including any previous conditions or allergies.
* **Family Background (5)**: Enter information about the client's family, including family members and their relationships.
* **Social Support (6)**: Identify any social support systems the client has in place, such as support groups or caregivers.
* **Emergency Contact (7)**: Enter the name and contact information of the client's emergency contact.
* **Medical History 2 (8)**: Provide any additional medical history information, if applicable.
* **Family Background 2 (9)**: Enter any further information about the client's family.
* **Social Support 2 (10)**: List any additional social support systems the client has.
* **Emergency Contact 2 (11)**: Enter the name and contact information of the client's emergency contact 2.
* **Medical History 3 (12)**: Enter any further medical history information, if applicable.

## Tips
- Ensure you have the client's consent to collect this information.
- Review the form carefully to ensure accurate and complete information is collected.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant authorities or professionals for guidance.
