# Business Coaching Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather essential information from you so we can understand your business goals, challenges, and expectations. Please fill out this form to help us tailor our coaching services to your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply scroll through the pages and fill in the required fields with your personal and business information. You can skip or leave blank any optional fields that don't apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your personal documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your personal documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter the primary email address you use for contact and communication.
* **Phone Number**: Enter a best contact number where you can be reached.
* **Business Name**: Enter the name of your company or venture.
* **Type of Business**: Describe the type of business you operate.
* **Years in Business**: If applicable, enter the number of years your business has been in operation.
* **Current Annual Revenue**: Select an approximate annual revenue range for your business.
* **Primary Goal**: Describe the specific goal you most want to achieve through coaching.
* **Specific Challenges**: Describe the key issues you face that you'd like to address.
* **Main Focus Areas**: Select all the focus areas that apply to your business goals.
* **Desired Timeline**: Choose when you'd like to see progress on your goals.
* **Have you worked with a Coach Before?**: Answer yes, no, or not sure if you've worked with a coach before.
* **Business Management Experience**: Enter the number of years you've managed a business.
* **Commitment Level**: Indicate how serious you are about the coaching process.
* **Preferred Coaching Format**: Choose how you'd like to work with a coach.
* **Availability**: Enter your general availability for coaching sessions.
* **Additional Information**: Use this space to provide any additional context or questions you have.
