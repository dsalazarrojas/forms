# Initial Inpatient Physician Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Initial Inpatient Physician Evaluation Form is a document used to assess and record the patient's medical status, treatment plan, and other relevant information during an inpatient stay.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the patient's medical history and vital signs.
2. Complete each field accurately and completely.
3. Use the space provided for any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the patient.
* **Date of Birth (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Physician Name (`physician_name`, text, required): Enter your name and credentials (e.g., MD, DO).
* **Medical Department (Medical Department, text, required): Enter the department or unit where you are working (e.g., ICU, ER).
* **Medical Record Number (Medical Record Number, text, required): Enter the patient's unique medical record number.
* **Allergies (allergies, text, required): List any known allergies the patient has.
* **Medications (medications, text, required): List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Medical Therapy (medical_therapy, text, required): Describe the patient's current medical therapy or treatment plan.
* **Surgical History (surgical_history, text, required): Describe any past surgical history or procedures the patient has undergone.
* **Blood Pressure (blood_pressure, text, required): Record the patient's current blood pressure reading.
* **Temperature (temperature, text, required): Record the patient's current temperature reading.
* **Pulse (pulse, text, required): Record the patient's current pulse reading.
* **Blood Oxygen Saturation (blood_oxygen_saturation, text, required): Record the patient's current blood oxygen saturation level.
* **Lab Test 1 (lab_test_1, text, required): Record any relevant lab test results for this patient.
* **Lab Test 2 (lab_test_2, text, required): Record any additional lab test results for this patient.
* **Lab Test 3 (lab_test_3, text, required): Record any other lab test results for this patient.
* **Diagnosis (diagnosis, text, required): Describe the patient's current diagnosis.
* **Treatments (treatment, text, required): Describe the patient's current treatment plan.
* **Follow-up (follow_up, text, required): Schedule any follow-up appointments or care for the patient.
* **Team Name (team_name, text, required): Enter the name of the healthcare team caring for this patient.
* **Team Role (team_role, text, required): Describe the role of the team member.
* **Comments (comments_9, text, required): Leave any additional comments or notes about the patient's case.
* **Discharge Status (discharge_status, select_one, required): Choose whether the patient has been discharged, admitted, or deceased.
* **Insurance Provider (insurance_provider, text, required): Enter the patient's insurance provider information.
* **Patient Address (patient_address, text, required): Record the patient's current address.
* **Date of Visit (date_of_visit, date, required): Record the date of the visit.
* **Time of Visit (time_of_visit, time, required): Record the time of the visit.
* **Physician Specialty (physician_specialty, text, required): Enter your specialty or area of expertise.
* **Physician License (physician_license, text, required): Enter your professional license number or type.
* **Physician Address (physician_address, text, required): Record your address.
* **Physician Phone (physician_phone, text, required): Record your phone number.
