# Buyer Profile Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is meant to capture the essential details of a potential buyer's profile, including their contact information, search preferences, and property requirements. Completing this form ensures that the sales team has a thorough understanding of your needs and can offer tailored support throughout the home buying process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your personal contact information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Specify the co-buyer's name and phone number, if applicable.
3. Choose a start date for your property search and an estimated closing date, if you have a target date in mind.
4. Select your budget range and indicate whether financing options are available to you.
5. Indicate your preferred property types, including primary and alternative types.
6. Enter your required number of bedrooms and bathrooms.
7. Choose your key amenities and features for the property you're looking for.
8. If school district quality is an important factor, select your level of importance.
9. Specify your acceptable commute distance.
10. Decide if you'd like to receive notifications about open houses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your complete name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to ensure you receive updates and notifications.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number for direct communication.
* **Co-Buyer Name** (`cobuyer_name`, `text`, optional): If you're purchasing with another person, enter their name.
* **Co-Buyer Phone Number** (`cobuyer_phone`, `text`, optional): If you're purchasing with another person, enter their phone number.
* **When Do You Want to Start Searching** (`search_start_date`, `date`, required): Choose a start date for your property search.
* **Estimated Closing Date** (`estimated_closing_date`, `date`, optional): If you have a target closing date, select it here.
* **Price Range** (`price_range`, `select_one`, required): Select your desired budget range for the property.
* **Is Financing Available** (`financing_available`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you have pre-approval or are paying cash.
* **Primary Property Type Sought** (`property_type_primary`, `select_one`, required): Choose the main type of property you're looking for (e.g., single-family home, condo).
* **Alternative Property Types** (`property_type_alternative`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select other types of properties you would also consider.
* **Preferred Neighborhoods or Areas** (`preferred_neighborhoods`, `text`, required): Enter specific neighborhoods or areas you're interested in.
* **Number of Bedrooms Needed** (`bedrooms_needed`, `number`, required): Enter the minimum number of bedrooms you require.
* **Number of Bathrooms Needed** (`bathrooms_needed`, `number`, required): Enter the minimum number of bathrooms you require.
* **Key Amenities or Features** (`key_amenities`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select essential features you must have in the property.
* **School District Important** (`school_important`, `select_one`, required): Choose how important school district quality is to you.
* **Acceptable Commute Distance** (`commute_distance`, `text`, optional): Specify your maximum acceptable commuting distance.
* **Notify Me of Open Houses** (`open_house_notifications`, `select_one`, required): Decide if you'd like to receive notifications about open houses.

## Tips

* Please answer all required fields, as they help the sales team tailor their support to your needs.
* If you have any questions or need help filling out this form, feel free to ask your sales associate.
* This form should be completed only once, and the information will be used to guide your home buying process.
* If you're unsure about any field, try to answer based on your honest preferences, and we'll discuss any doubts or concerns during our consultation.
