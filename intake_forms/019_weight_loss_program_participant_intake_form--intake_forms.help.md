# Weight Loss Program Participant Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from participants who are interested in or are currently enrolled in our weight loss program. The purpose of this form is to collect demographic and health-related data to help our team better understand the participant's weight loss history, medical history, and treatment goals. This information will be used to provide personalized guidance and support to help participants achieve their weight loss goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields as instructed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, number, optional): Enter your contact number (phone or other contact information).
* **Weight** (`weight`, number, optional): Enter your current weight.
* **Height** (`height`, number, optional): Enter your height in inches or centimeters.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): List any medical conditions you are currently dealing with.
* **Medsications** (`medications`, text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical Procedure Results** (`medical_procedure_results`, text, optional): Describe any medical procedures you have undergone or are planning to undergo.
* **Blood Test Results** (`blood_test_results`, text, optional): Describe any blood test results you have or are planning to get.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Describe your medical history.
* **Medical Family History** (`medical_family_history`, text, optional): Describe your family's medical history.
* **Medical Family History Notes** (`medical_family_history_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional notes or details about your family's medical history.
* **Medical Family History Other** (`medical_family_history_other`, text, optional): Describe any other relevant medical history not listed.
* **Medical Family History Family Members** (`medical_family_history_family_members`, text, optional): List any family members with medical conditions or history.
* **Medical Family History Family Members Notes** (`medical_family_history_family_members_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional notes or details about your family members' medical history.
* **Weight Loss History** (`weight_loss_history`, text, optional): Describe your past weight loss attempts and successes/failures.
* **Weight Loss Method** (`weight_loss_method`, select_one, optional): Select the method you used for weight loss (Crash diets, Meal planning apps, Nutritionists, Personal trainers, Other).
* **Weight Loss History Notes** (`weight_loss_history_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional notes or details about your weight loss history.
* **Weight Loss Goal** (`weight_loss_goal`, text, optional): Describe your weight loss goal.
* **Weight Loss Goal Notes** (`weight_loss_goal_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional notes or details about your weight loss goal.
* **Weight Loss Goal Weight** (`weight_loss_goal_weight`, number, optional): Enter your weight loss goal in kg or lbs.
* **Weight Loss Goal Unit** (`weight_loss_goal_unit`, select_one, optional): Select the unit for your weight loss goal (lbs, kg, inches, percent).
