# rehabilitation_initial_documentation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The rehabilitation_initial_documentation form is used to gather essential information for patients undergoing rehabilitation. This form helps healthcare professionals collect and document the patient's medical history, details, and treatment preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have the necessary information and permissions to complete this form.
2. Review the form fields to ensure you are providing accurate and relevant information.
3. Enter the required and optional information as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Please enter a brief summary of the patient's medical history.
* **Patient Details** (`patient_details`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about the patient, such as their name, date of birth, or contact information.
* **Medications List** (`medication_list`, text, optional): List all medications the patient is taking, including dosages and frequencies.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_history_conditions`, text, optional): List any medical conditions the patient has, such as diabetes, heart disease, etc.
* **Rehab Goals** (`rehab_goals`, select_multiple, optional): Select the primary goals of the rehabilitation plan, such as Pain Management or Other.
* **Consent To Treatment** (`consent_to_treatment`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the patient has given consent for treatment.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide the name and contact information for the patient's emergency contact.
* **Insurance Information** (`insurance_info`, text, optional): Enter the patient's insurance provider name, policy number, and claim date.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when completing this form to ensure the patient's needs are met.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the patient's medical records or consult with the healthcare team.
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and approvals before sharing this form with the patient or other healthcare professionals.
