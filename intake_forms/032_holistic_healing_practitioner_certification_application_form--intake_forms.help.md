# Holistic Healing Practitioner Certification Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information from holistic healing practitioners who wish to be certified. It is meant to be completed by individuals who have experience in their field and want to demonstrate their expertise in the certification process. The form is used to verify the applicant's qualifications, experience, and supporting documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by providing the title of your application form.
2. Indicate whether you have supporting documents to submit with your application.
3. Provide detailed information about your experience in the field, including relevant qualifications and credentials.
4. Submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Title (Application Form) 
* **Title** (`title`, text, required): Enter the title of your application form, which will be used to identify you throughout the process.

### Supporting Documents 
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, select_multiple, required: false): 
  This field allows you to indicate if you have any supporting documents to submit with your application. Select 'Yes' if you have documents to submit, and 'No' if you do not have any documents to submit.

### Experience Details 
* **Experience Details** (`experience_details`, note, required: false): 
  This field is for providing detailed information about your experience in the field. Please provide a clear and concise description of your qualifications and relevant experience.

### Submit Application 
* **Submit Application** (`submit_application`, select_one, required: false): 
  This field indicates whether you are ready to submit your application. Select 'Yes' to submit your application, and 'No' if you are not ready.
