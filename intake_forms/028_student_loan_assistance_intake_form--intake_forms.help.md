**Student Loan Assistance Intake Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Student Loan Assistance Intake Form is designed to collect information from students seeking help with their student loans. This form helps us understand your situation and provide you with guidance and support in managing your student loans.

### How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form accurately and completely, providing all required information.
2. Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the respective fields.
3. Enter your Date of Birth in the format 'MM/DD/YYYY'.
4. If you have attended a school, provide the name of the school in the School Attended field.
5. Select your Student Loan Type from the Federal, Private, or State options.
6. Choose your Student Loan Provider from Navient, Wells Fargo, or Sallie Mae.
7. Enter your Current Debt and Income (Monthly) values.
8. Indicate your Loan Status as True or False.
9. Enter the Follow-up Date and Time for your last contact with your student loan provider.
10. Provide a Follow-up Note about your last contact.
11. Enter the Financial Counselor's name and contact information.
12. If you have any comments or concerns, use the Borrower Comments field.
13. If you have any comments or feedback for the Financial Counselor, use the Financial Counselor Comments field.
14. Enter the Follow-up Details and Outcomes of your last contact.
15. Enter the Follow-up Recommendations for your next steps.
16. Enter the Next Follow-up Date and Time.
17. Enter the Next Follow-up Note.
