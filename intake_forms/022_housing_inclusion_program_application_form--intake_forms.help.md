# Housing Inclusion Program Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Housing Inclusion Program aims to provide assistance and support to those in need of housing. This form is designed to collect individual and household information to help us assess your eligibility for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly, providing accurate and relevant information.
2. Make sure to review each field for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Individual Info** (`text`, not required)
This field is for you to provide your name and any other relevant details about yourself.

* Please enter your name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* If you have any other names or aliases, please include them as well.

### 2. **Contact Info** (`text`, not required)
This field is for you to provide your contact information.

* Please enter your phone number and email address where you can be reached.
* This will help us get in touch with you about your application.

### 3. **Household Info** (`text`, not required)
This field is for you to provide information about your household.

* Please describe your household composition, including the number of family members and their relationships to you.
* If you are a single person living alone, please indicate this.

### 4. **Income Info** (`text`, not required)
This field is for you to provide information about your income.

* Please list all sources of income, including employment, benefits, and any other regular income.
* You can also attach supporting documents, such as pay stubs or bank statements, if possible.

### 5. **Housing History** (`text`, not required)
This field is for you to provide information about your housing history.

* Please describe any previous housing experiences, including evictions, foreclosures, or other relevant details.
* If you have a good landlord or property manager, please provide their contact information.

### 6. **Employment Info** (`text`, not required)
This field is for you to provide information about your employment.

* Please list all your current and previous employers, including job titles, dates of employment, and reasons for leaving.
* If you are self-employed, please describe your business and its income.

### 7. **Education Info** (`text`, not required)
This field is for you to provide information about your education.

* Please list all your educational institutions, degrees, and relevant courses.
* If you have any relevant certifications or training, please include them as well.

### 8. **Medical Info** (`text`, not required)
This field is for you to provide information about your medical history.

* Please disclose any medical conditions, allergies, or disabilities that may impact your housing needs.
* If you have any medical documents, such as doctor's notes or medical records, please attach them.

### 9. **Family Status** (`text`, not required)
This field is for you to provide information about your family composition.

* Please describe your relationships with family members, including their ages and relationships (e.g., spouse, child, parent).
* If you have any family members with disabilities or medical conditions, please indicate their details.

### 10. **Housing Status** (`text`, not required)
This field is for you to provide information about your current housing situation.

* Please describe your current living arrangements, including type of accommodation and location.
* If you are currently housed by someone else, please explain the circumstances.

### 11. **Support Info** (`text`, not required)
This field is for you to provide information about any support systems in place.

* Please list any social workers, counselors, or other support contacts who can assist with your application.
* If you have any relevant community resources or support groups, please include them as well.

### 12. **Emergency Contact** (`text`, not required)
This field is for you to provide information about an emergency contact.

* Please list a contact person who can reach you or your household in case of an emergency.
* If you have any out-of-country emergency contacts, please provide their information.

Please review all fields carefully and answer each question accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
