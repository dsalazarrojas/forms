# Ancestor Healing Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Ancestor Healing Agreement form is a crucial part of our spiritual healing process, and it's essential to complete it accurately to avoid any miscommunication or misunderstandings. This form will gather information about your intentions, preferences, and expectations, ensuring a clear understanding between you and our team before starting the healing session.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Client Full Name** (Client Full Name, text, required): Please provide your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. **Client Email Address** (Client Email Address, email, required): Enter your valid email address where you can be reached for communication.
3. **Client Phone Number** (Client Phone Number, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted via phone, please provide your phone number.
4. **Primary Focus or Intention for this Healing Session** (What is your primary focus or intention for this healing session?, text, required): Briefly describe your main focus or intention for this healing session.
5. **Specific Ancestral Lineages to Address (if known)** (Specific ancestral lineages you wish to address (if known.), text, optional): If you're aware of any specific ancestral lineages you'd like to address during this session, please list them here.
6. **Session Goals** (What are your goals for this session?, select_multiple, required): Select one or more goals for this session from the provided options:
	* Clearing Trauma
	* Reclaiming Gifts
	* Forgiveness
	* Understanding Patterns
	* General Peace
7. **Consent and Terms** (Consent and Terms, note, required): Please review the terms of this form, and if you're ready to proceed, type your full name to confirm your consent.
8. **Consent to Spiritual Work** (I consent to participate in spiritual/energetic ancestor healing work., select_one, required): Choose to indicate your consent to participate in spiritual/energetic ancestor healing work.
9. **Understanding of Non-Medical Nature** (I understand this work is spiritual in nature and not a substitute for medical/psychological care., select_one, required): Confirm your understanding that this work is spiritual in nature and not a substitute for medical or psychological care.
10. **Preferred Session Format** (Preferred Session Format, select_one, required): Choose your preferred session format:
	* In-Person
	* Virtual
	* Remote / Distance
11. **Requested Session Date** (Requested Session Date, date, required): Provide the date you'd like to schedule your healing session.
12. **Digital Signature Confirmation** (Digital Signature Confirmation, text, required): Type your full name to confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have provided accurate and clear information to ensure a smooth and effective healing session.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact our support team.
* Review each section carefully before submitting the form to avoid any misunderstandings.
