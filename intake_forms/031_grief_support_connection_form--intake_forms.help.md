# Grief Support Connection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Grief Support Connection Form is designed to provide a connection between individuals experiencing grief and available support services. This form helps us understand your situation, needs, and preferences to provide the best possible support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the intake form with your personal details.
2. Provide accurate and up-to-date information about your loved one, including their date and time of death.
3. If applicable, provide information about the social worker or counselor involved.
4. Connect with a next of kin (relative) to receive support and updates.
5. Indicate your agreement to share information with the next of kin.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Intake Form (`id: 1`)
Intake Form: Provide a brief personal overview, including your name, email, and phone number.

### 2. Name (`id: 2`)
Name: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 3. Email (`id: 3`)
Email: Enter your email address where you can be reached for support and updates.

### 4. Phone (`id: 4`)
Phone: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for support and updates.

### 5. Zip (`id: 5`)
Zip (Zip Code): Enter your zip code to help us locate you geographically.

### 6. Date of Death (`id: 6`)
Date of Death: Enter the date your loved one passed away.

### 7. Time of Death (`id: 7`)
Time of Death: Enter the time your loved one passed away (optional).

### 8. Cause of Death (`id: 8`)
Cause of Death: Provide a brief description of the circumstances surrounding your loved one's passing (optional).

### 9. Social Worker (`id: 9`)
Social Worker: If you're working with a social worker, provide their name and contact information (optional).

### 10. Counselor (`id: 10`)
Counselor: If you're working with a counselor, provide their name and contact information (optional).

### 11. Connection (`id: 11`)
Connection: Enter a short message connecting you to a support service or resource.

### 12. Follow-up (`id: 12`)
Follow-up: Enter a follow-up message or any additional information about your situation.

### 13. Intake Form 2 (`id: 13`)
Intake Form 2: This is a secondary intake form for additional information (optional).

### 14. Notes (`id: 14`)
Notes: Add any additional information or comments about your situation (optional).

### 15. Next of Kin (`id: 15`)
Next of Kin: Indicate that you have a next of kin (relative) who can be contacted for support.

### 16. Relationship (`id: 16`)
Relationship: Describe your relationship to the next of kin (optional).

### 17. Next of Kin Name (`id: 17`)
Next of Kin Name: Enter the name of your next of kin (if selected).

### 18. Next of Kin Phone (`id: 18`)
Next of Kin Phone: Enter your next of kin's phone number (if selected).

### 19. Next of Kin Email (`id: 19`)
Next of Kin Email: Enter your next of kin's email address (if selected).

### 20. Next of Kin Relationship (`id: 20`)
Next of Kin Relationship: Describe your relationship to your next of kin (if selected).

### 21. Next of Kin Notes (`id: 21`)
Next of Kin Notes: Add any notes about your next of kin (if selected).

### 22. Next of Kin Agree (`id: 22`)
Next of Kin Agree: Confirm that you have a next of kin's permission to share information with them.

### 23. Next of Kin Message (`id: 23`)
Next of Kin Message: Enter a message to be sent to your next of kin (if selected).

### 24. Next of Kin Message 2 (`id: 24`)
Next of Kin Message 2: Enter a second message to be sent to your next of kin (if selected).

### 25. Next of Kin Message 3 (`id: 25`)
Next of Kin Message 3: Enter a third message to be sent to your next of kin (if selected).

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [support email].
- We will review and follow up with you based on your submitted information.
