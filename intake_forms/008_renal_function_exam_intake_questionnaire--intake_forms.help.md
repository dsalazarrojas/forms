# Renal Function Exam Intake Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients regarding their renal function examination. It helps healthcare professionals assess the patient's kidney function and provide necessary care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your general information (name, age, address, etc.) on the first page.
2. Provide your medical history, including any past surgeries or medical conditions, on the second page.
3. List any kidney-related diseases you have or have had in the past on the third page.
4. Mention all the medications you are currently taking on the fourth page.
5. Note any allergies you have on the fifth page.
6. Describe your medical conditions, including any recent medical tests, on the sixth page.
7. Record your vital signs (blood pressure, pulse, etc.) on the seventh page.
8. Share laboratory results, such as blood tests, on the eighth page.
9. List any medications or allergies you have that may affect your kidney function on the ninth page.
10. Provide information about your family medical history on the tenth page.
11. Include contact details of your family members or caregivers on the eleventh page.
12. Record your renal clearance results on the twelfth page.
13. Report your creatinine levels on the thirteenth page.
14. Document your urine output and frequency on the fourteenth page.
15. Record your blood pressure and any other health-related metrics on the fifteenth page.
16. Report your weight and height measurements on the sixteenth page.
17. Calculate your body mass index (BMI) on the seventeenth page.
18. Answer questions about your smoking habits on the eighteenth page.
19. Describe your exercise routine on the nineteenth page.
