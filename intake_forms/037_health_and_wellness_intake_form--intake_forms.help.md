# Health And Wellness Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about a client's health and wellness status to help healthcare providers understand their medical background and provide personalized care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form by typing in your information in the corresponding fields.
2. Ensure you complete all required fields (indicated by the "required" label).
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Name (`client_name`)
* **Client Name** (text): Please enter your full name as you would like it to be used in your records.
### Birth Date (`birth_date`)
* **Birth Date** (date): Enter your birth date (mm/dd/yyyy) to help us understand your age and medical history.
### Email (`email`)
* **Email** (email): Enter your preferred contact email address, if you have one.
### Phone Number (`phone_number`)
* **Phone Number** (text): Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted.
### Medical Conditions (`medical_conditions`)
* **Medical Conditions** (text): List any known medical conditions you have, such as diabetes, high blood pressure, etc.
### Medical History (`medical_history`)
* **Medical History** (text): Describe any past medical procedures, surgeries, or illnesses.
### Health Insurance (`health_insurance`)
* **Health Insurance** (select multiple): Indicate if you have health insurance coverage.
### Emergency Contact (`emergency_contact`)
* **Emergency Contact** (select one): Choose the emergency contact you want us to contact in case of an emergency.
### Emergency Phone Number (`emergency_phone_number`)
* **Emergency Phone Number** (text): Enter the contact information of your emergency contact.
### Medical Procedures (`medical_procedures`)
* **Medical Procedures** (text): Describe any past medical procedures or treatments you've undergone.
 
## Tips
* Please ensure accuracy and completeness in your answers.
* If you're unsure or have questions, please ask your healthcare provider for assistance.
* If you've filled out this form previously, you should not submit it again unless instructed otherwise.
