# Microdermabrasion Client Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Microdermabrasion Client Intake Form is used to gather essential information from clients before their treatment sessions. This form helps our medical staff to identify and assess the client's medical history, allergies, and other relevant factors that may impact their treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the **Client Name** field.
2. Provide the client's address in the **Client Address** field.
3. Enter the client's email in the **Email** field, if applicable.
4. If the client has a phone number, enter it in the **Phone** field.
5. If the client has any medical conditions, please list them in the **Medical Condition** field.
6. If the client has allergies, select all that apply from the **Allergies** field.
7. Select the **Medical Procedure** that the client is undergoing from the drop-down menu.
8. In the **Medical History** field, provide any relevant medical history that may impact their treatment.
9. If necessary, add any additional medical notes in the **Medical Notes** field.
10. Enter the client's date of birth in the **Date of Birth** field.
11. Enter the date of their service in the **Date of Service** field.
12. If applicable, enter the time of service in the **Time of Service** field.
13. Enter the name of the doctor and/or medical staff member attending to the client in the **Doctor Name** and/or **Staff Name** fields.
14. If applicable, add the client's signature in the **Client Signature** field.
15. Finally, add the staff member's signature in the **Staff Signature** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the client's name as they want to be addressed during their treatment.
* **Client Address** (`client_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the client's address for future correspondence or treatment scheduling.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the client's email address for future communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the client's phone number for future scheduling or communication.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, required: false): If the client has a medical condition, list it here.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the allergies that apply to the client.
* **Medical Procedure** (`medical_procedure`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the medical procedure that the client is undergoing.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Provide any relevant medical history that may impact their treatment.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional medical notes if necessary.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter the client's date of birth.
* **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of their service.
* **Time of Service** (`time_of_service`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of service if applicable.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the doctor attending to the client.
* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the staff member attending to the client.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required: false): Add the client's signature to confirm their understanding of the treatment.
* **Medical License** (`medical_license`, `text`, required: false): Enter the client's medical license number (if applicable).
* **Medical License State** (`medical_license_state`, `text`, required: false): Enter the state where the client's medical license is from (if applicable).
* **Medical License Number** (`medical_license_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the client's medical license number (if applicable).
* **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the expiration date of the client's medical license (if applicable).
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `text`, required: false): Add the client's signature to confirm their understanding of the treatment.
* **Staff Signature** (`staff_signature`, `text`, required: false): Add the staff member's signature to confirm their understanding of the treatment.
* **Medical Specialty** (`medical_specialty`, `text`, required: false): Enter the medical specialty of the doctor or staff member attending to the client.
* **Practice Location** (`practice_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the practice location where the treatment is taking place.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact number for future communication or scheduling.
* **Medical Board** (`medical_board`, `text`, required: false): Enter the client's medical board information (if applicable).

Note: Some fields may be optional or not applicable to the client's case. Please fill in only the required and relevant information.
