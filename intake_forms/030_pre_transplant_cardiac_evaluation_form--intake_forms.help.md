# Pre Transplant Cardiac Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre-Transplant Cardiac Evaluation Form is designed to collect information about a patient's cardiac history, medical history, and other relevant factors related to a potential transplant. This form will be used by the medical team to assess the patient's risk factors and make informed decisions about their suitability for transplant.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully to understand what information is being requested.
2.  Answer each question honestly and accurately, using the options provided.
3.  Ensure that you have permission to share the requested information.
4.  Use the provided hints, if any, to help guide your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (1)**: Please enter your name, date of birth, and any other relevant demographic information. This information will help the medical team understand your overall health profile.
* **Medical History (2)**: Describe your past medical history, including any previous surgeries, illnesses, or allergies. Be as detailed as possible, but do not include information that is not relevant to your cardiac history.
* **Cardiac History (3)**: Provide a detailed account of your cardiac history, including any heart conditions, surgeries, or other cardiac-related events. This will help the medical team understand your current cardiac status.
* **Cardiac Assessments (4)**: If you have undergone any cardiac assessments or evaluations, please describe them in detail. This will help the medical team understand your current cardiac status.
* **Medical Team Contacts (5)**: List the names and contact information of your primary care physician and other relevant medical contacts.
* **Transplant Recipient Information (6)**: Please provide any relevant information about the recipient of the transplant, if applicable. This will help the medical team understand the context of the transplant.
* **Cardiac Risks (7)**: If you have any cardiac risks or concerns, please describe them in detail. This will help the medical team understand any potential risks associated with the transplant.
* **Cardiac Risk Factors (8)**: List any cardiac risk factors you have, such as high blood pressure, diabetes, or other conditions.
* **Cardiac Surgical Procedure (9)**: If you have undergone any cardiac surgery, please describe the procedure and any relevant details.
* **Transplant Center Contacts (10)**: List the names and contact information of the transplant center and other relevant contacts.
* **Cardiac Surgical Reports (11)**: If you have any cardiac surgical reports, please attach them or describe them in detail.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Use clear and concise language.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult with your healthcare provider.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the medical team at the transplant center.
