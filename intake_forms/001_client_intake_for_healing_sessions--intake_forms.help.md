# Client Intake For Healing Sessions - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from clients for their healing sessions. It gathers personal details, preferred session dates and times, and their goals for the healing process. Please fill out this form to provide the practitioner with the necessary information to create a personalized and effective plan for your healing sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information, including your full name, date of birth, phone number, and email address.
2. Select your preferred session type (e.g., Energy Healing, Reiki, etc.).
3. Choose a date for your first session and indicate your preferred time of day.
4. Provide a brief health history and description of your current concerns.
5. Describe your goals for the healing sessions.
6. Select your consent preference for receiving holistic treatment and follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): This is your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Preferred Name** (`preferred_name`, text, optional): Enter a name that you are comfortable with being called or addressed by the practitioner. This can be different from your full name if you prefer a nickname or a pen name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Provide the best phone number to reach you, including area code and extension (if applicable).
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter the email address that you would like the practitioner to use for appointment confirmations and future communications.
* **Emergency Contact Name and Phone** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact person, provide their name and phone number.
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, select_one, required): Select one or more of the healing modalities that interest you, such as Energy Healing, Reiki, Sound Therapy, etc.
* **Preferred Session Date** (`preferred_session_date`, date, required): Choose a date for your first session.
* **Preferred Session Time** (`preferred_session_time`, time, optional): Indicate your preferred time of day for the session.
* **Brief Health History and Current Concerns** (`brief_health_history`, text, optional): Provide any relevant medical or emotional information that may be helpful for the practitioner.
* **Session Goals** (`session_goals`, text, required): Describe your desired outcomes or focus areas for the healing sessions.
* **Consent to Treatment** (`consent_to_treatment`, select_one, required): Select your preference for receiving holistic treatment and follow-up, or choose to discuss further with the practitioner.

Tips:

* Please fill in all required fields to ensure that the practitioner can provide you with the best possible care.
* Be as detailed as possible when describing your brief health history and session goals.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask the practitioner for guidance.
