# Discharge To Home Planning Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect important information about the patient's discharge from the hospital or care facility, ensuring a smooth transition to home and providing necessary follow-up care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please review the patient's medical chart to ensure that all necessary information is accurate and up-to-date.
2. Answer each section carefully, providing as much detail as possible for each field.
3. If a field is not applicable, leave it blank or select "None" as the answer.
4. Make sure to review the completed form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant identifying information.
* **Discharge Date**: Enter the date of the patient's discharge from the hospital or care facility.
* **Follow Up Dates**: Enter any scheduled follow-up appointments or visits for the patient, including the date and time.
* **Follow Up Times**: Enter the expected duration of each follow-up visit.
* **Medications**: Enter the patient's current medications, including dosages and frequency.
* **Medical Status**: Enter a brief description of the patient's current medical status, including any current symptoms or concerns.
* **Medical Condition**: Select the patient's primary medical condition (e.g. Heart Failure, Hypertension, Diabetic, etc.).
* **Patient Goals**: Enter any specific goals or objectives set for the patient's recovery or care.
* **Patient Outcomes**: Enter any expected outcomes or results for the patient's care or treatment.
* **Follow Up Person**: Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for follow-up care.
* **Follow Up Phone**: Enter the phone number of the follow-up contact person.
* **Follow Up Email**: Enter the email address of the follow-up contact person.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any information or have questions, please ask your supervisor or healthcare team for guidance.
