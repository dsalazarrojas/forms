# Nutrition And Wellness Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather information about your health and wellness status. Please answer each question thoroughly and honestly to ensure that your healthcare practitioner has the necessary information to create a personalized nutrition and wellness plan for you.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out each section of the form as completely and accurately as possible.
2. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your healthcare practitioner for guidance.
3. Once you have completed the form, review it carefully to ensure that all information is accurate and up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (`client_info`):** Please provide your name and any other contact information you would like to share with your healthcare practitioner.
* **Contact Information (`contact_info`):** This section is where you will enter your contact information, including your phone number and email address, so that your healthcare practitioner can reach out to you for follow-up or to answer any questions.
* **Medical History (`medical_history`):** Please provide any relevant medical history, including previous illnesses, surgeries, or allergies.
* **Allergies (`allergies`):** List any allergies you have, including any medications or foods you are allergic to.
* **Medications (`medications`):** List any medications you are currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.
* **Medical Conditions (`medical_conditions`):** Please list any current or past medical conditions, such as diabetes, hypertension, or asthma.
* **Nutrition Goals (`nutrition_goals`):** What are your goals for nutrition, such as weight loss or improved energy levels?
* **Wellness Goals (`wellness_goals`):** What are your overall wellness goals, such as improved mental health or reduced stress?
* **Dietary Restrictions (`dietary_restrictions`):** Do you have any dietary restrictions, such as vegan or gluten-free?
* **Height & Weight (`height_weight`):** Please enter your height and weight.
* **Blood Tests Results (`blood_tests_results`):** If you have any blood test results, please enter them here.
* **Lab Results (`lab_results`):** If you have any lab test results, please enter them here.
* **Medical Tests (`medical_tests`):** If you have any medical test results, please enter them here.
* **Nutrition Plan (`nutrition_plan`):** Please describe your current nutrition plan, including any dietary changes you have made.
* **Health Risk Factors (`health_risk_factors`):** Please list any health risk factors, such as family history or smoking history.
* **Medical Family History (`medical_family_history`):** Please list any relevant medical history from your family members.
* **Nutrition Plan Status (`nutrition_plan_status`):** Please indicate the current status of your nutrition plan, such as on-going or completed.
* **Wellness Plan Status (`wellness_plan_status`):** Please indicate the current status of your wellness plan, such as on-going or completed.
* **Client Signature (`client_signature`):** Please sign and date this form to acknowledge that you have completed it accurately and truthfully.
* **Practitioner Signature (`practitioner_signature`):** Please sign and date this form to confirm that you have reviewed and agreed to the information provided.
* **Date (`date`):** Enter the date you completed this form.
* **Time (`time`):** Enter the time you completed this form.
* **Email (`email`):** Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone (`phone`):** Enter your phone number for follow-up or to answer any questions.
* **Notes (`notes`):** Please add any additional notes or comments about your health and wellness status.
