# Community Housing Initiative Onboarding - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from new applicants for the Community Housing Initiative. It ensures that all necessary details are gathered for efficient communication and effective case management. 

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read through each section and question to ensure you understand what information is being requested.
- Fill out the form completely and accurately, paying attention to each field's requirements.
- Make sure to review your answers before submitting to ensure everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Intake Coordinator Name**:
  - (Text, Required): This field is for the name of the intake coordinator who is handling your application.
  - Enter your name as it appears on your documents, such as your ID or driver's license.
- **Applicant Full Name**:
  - (Text, Required): This is the name of the applicant, as it appears on their identification documents.
  - Please enter your name as it is written on your ID or driver's license.
- **Date of Intake**:
  - (Date, Required): This field is for the date of your initial meeting or contact with the Community Housing Initiative.
  - Select the date you first contacted the initiative.
- **Urgency Level for Housing**:
  - (Select One, Required): This question asks about the urgency of your housing situation.
  - Choose the level that best describes your current situation:
    - **Immediate Crisis - Homeless tonight**: You have an immediate housing crisis and are homeless or in danger of becoming homeless.
    - **High - Within 30 days**: You anticipate being homeless within the next 30 days due to a housing crisis or instability.
    - **Medium - Stable but seeking**: You are stable in your current housing situation but are seeking help or planning for the future.
    - **Low - Long-term planning**: You are planning ahead for long-term housing needs.
- **Identified Housing Barriers**:
  - (Select Multiple, Required): This is a list of potential barriers that may hinder your housing situation.
  - Choose all that apply:
    - **Credit History Issues**: You've had problems with credit or financial stability.
    - **Low or Unstable Income**: You have a low income or unstable income sources.
    - **Previous Eviction Record**: You have had an eviction record or been previously evicted.
    - **Physical Accessibility Needs**: You require special assistance due to a disability or physical limitations.
    - **Lack of Legal Documentation**: You lack proper identification or documentation needed for housing.
    - **Criminal Record**: You have a criminal record that may impact your housing situation.
- **Support Services Required**:
  - (Select Multiple, Optional): This is a list of support services you may need or require.
  - Choose all that apply:
    - **Legal Aid and Advocacy**: You need help with legal services or advocacy.
    - **Financial Literacy Coaching**: You require coaching on financial literacy or money management.
    - **Job Placement Assistance**: You need assistance with finding employment or job placement.
    - **Mental Health Support**: You need mental health support or counseling.
    - **Substance Use Recovery**: You require support with substance use recovery.
    - **Childcare Services**: You need help with childcare or childcare services.
- **Documentation Provided at Intake**:
  - (Select Multiple, Required): This is a list of documents you've provided during your intake session.
  - Choose all that apply:
    - **State Issued ID**: You've provided a valid state-issued ID.
    - **Birth Certificate**: You've provided a birth certificate or proof of birth.
    - **Social Security Card**: You've provided a Social Security card or proof of Social Security number.
    - **Proof of Income**: You've provided proof of your income or financial stability.
    - **Recent Utility Bill**: You've provided a recent utility bill or proof of utility services.
- **Preferred Frequency of Case Management Contact**:
  - (Select One, Required): This question asks about your preferred frequency of case management contact.
  - Choose the frequency that best suits your needs:
    - **Daily Check-ins**: You'd like to have daily check-ins with the initiative.
    - **Weekly Check-ins**: You'd like to have weekly check-ins with the initiative.
    - **Bi-weekly Check-ins**: You'd like to have bi-weekly check-ins with the initiative.
    - **Monthly Check-ins**: You'd like to have monthly check-ins with the initiative.
- **Agreed Upon Next Steps and Action Items**:
  - (Text, Required): This is a field for agreeing upon the next steps and action items for your case management plan.
  - Enter a clear description of the agreed-upon next steps and action items.
- **Counselor Electronic Signature**:
  - (Text, Required): This field is for the electronic signature of the counselor.
  - Type your name as it appears on your documents, such as your ID or driver's license.
- **Confidential Coordinator Notes**:
  - (Text, Optional): This is a field for confidential notes from the intake coordinator.
  - Enter any additional information that might be helpful for your case management, but be aware that this is for internal use only.
