# Loan Application Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Loan Application Intake Form is designed to collect basic information from loan applicants to facilitate the loan application review process. This guide will walk you through each section of the form to ensure accurate and efficient submission.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary information about the loan applicant before proceeding.
2. Carefully fill out each field, and if you are unsure about any field, please reach out to our loan team for assistance.
3. Review the form for accuracy before submitting it.
4. Submit the form, and our loan team will review and follow up with further questions or instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, Required/Optional): This field is required for identifying the loan applicant.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, Required/Optional): This field is also required for identification purposes.
* **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, `number`, Required/Optional): Enter the loan amount being applied for.
* **Interest Rate** (`interest_rate`, `number`, Required/Optional): This field is used to calculate interest rates for the loan.
* **Loan Term** (`loan_term`, `text`, Required/Optional): Describe the loan term or duration in words.
* **Loan Status** (`loan_status`, `select_multiple`, Required/Optional): Select all applicable loan statuses (Active/Inactive).
* **Loan Type** (`loan_type`, `select_one`, Required/Optional): Choose the type of loan being applied for.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, Required/Optional): Select the employment status of the loan applicant.
* **Annual Income** (`annual_income`, `text`, Required/Optional): Enter the loan applicant's annual income.
* **Loan Date** (`loan_date`, `date`, Required/Optional): Enter the date of the loan application.
* **Loan Time** (`loan_time`, `time`, Required/Optional): Enter the time of the loan application.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, Required/Optional): Enter the loan applicant's address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, Required/Optional): Enter the loan applicant's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, Required/Optional): Enter the loan applicant's email address.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, Required/Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the loan application.
* **Loan Applicant Name** (`loan_applicant_name`, `text`, Required/Optional): Enter the name of the loan applicant.
* **Loan Applicant Address** (`loan_applicant_address`, `text`, Required/Optional): Enter the loan applicant's address.
* **Employment Status of Loan Applicant** (`loan_applicant_employment_status`, `select_one`, Required/Optional): Select the employment status of the loan applicant.
* **Employment Start Date of Loan Applicant** (`loan_applicant_employment_start_date`, `date`, Required/Optional): Enter the date the loan applicant started employment.
* **Employment End Date of Loan Applicant** (`loan_applicant_employment_end_date`, `date`, Required/Optional): Enter the date the loan applicant's employment ended.
* **Loan Applicant Income** (`loan_applicant_income`, `text`, Required/Optional): Enter the loan applicant's income.
* **Loan Applicant Employer Name** (`loan_applicant_employer_name`, `text`, Required/Optional): Enter the loan applicant's employer's name.
* **Loan Applicant Job Title** (`loan_applicant_job_title`, `text`, Required/Optional): Enter the loan applicant's job title.
* **Loan Applicant Employer Address** (`loan_applicant_employer_address`, `text`, Required/Optional): Enter the loan applicant's employer's address.
* **Loan Applicant Phone** (`loan_applicant_phone`, `text`, Required/Optional): Enter the loan applicant's phone number.
