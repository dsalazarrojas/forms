# Financial Budgeting Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Budgeting Client Intake Form is used to collect detailed information about a client's financial situation, allowing financial advisors, credit counselors, or other professionals to provide personalized guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the relevant sections with your financial information, including income sources, expenses, debt, and financial goals.
2. Be as detailed and accurate as possible in your responses.
3. If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask your financial advisor for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Income source** (Income Source, text, optional): Please describe your primary source of income, such as employment or self-employment.
* **Income frequency** (Income Frequency, select_one, optional): Do you receive a regular income? If so, how often? (Yes/No)
* **Income Amount** (Income Amount, number, optional): What is your monthly income?
* **Expense source** (Expense Source, text, optional): Describe your primary source of expenses, such as rent or utilities.
* **Expense frequency** (Expense Frequency, select_one, optional): Do you have regular expenses? If so, how often? (Yes/No)
* **Expense Amount** (Expense Amount, number, optional): What is your monthly expense amount?
* **Debt Total** (Debt Total, number, optional): What is your total debt amount?
* **Debt Description** (Debt Description, text, optional): Please describe your debt, including type (e.g., credit card, loan) and lender.
* **Financial Goal** (Financial Goal, text, optional): What is your financial goal, such as saving for a specific purpose?
* **Financial Goal Description** (Financial Goal Description, text, optional): Please describe your financial goal in more detail.
* **Financial Goal Type** (Financial Goal Type, select_multiple, optional): What type of financial goal do you have? (Yes/No)
* **Financial Institution** (Financial Institution, select_one, optional): Do you work with a financial institution, such as a bank or credit union? (Yes/No)
* **Financial Institution Type** (Financial Institution Type, text, optional): Please describe the type of financial institution you work with.
* **Financial Institution Description** (Financial Institution Description, text, optional): Please describe your financial institution and its services.
* **Financial Institution Address** (Financial Institution Address, text, optional): What is the address of your financial institution?
* **Financial Institution Contact** (Financial Institution Contact, text, optional): Who is your primary contact at your financial institution?
* **Financial Institution Phone** (Financial Institution Phone, text, optional): What is the phone number of your financial institution?
* **Financial Institution Email** (Financial Institution Email, email, optional): What is the email address of your financial institution?
* **Financial Institution Fax** (Financial Institution Fax, text, optional): What is the fax number of your financial institution?
* **Financial Institution Hours** (Financial Institution Hours, time, optional): What are the hours of operation for your financial institution?
* **Financial Institution Website** (Financial Institution Website, text, optional): What is the website of your financial institution?
* **Financial Institution Facebook** (Financial Institution Facebook, text, optional): What is the Facebook page of your financial institution?
* **Financial Institution Instagram** (Financial Institution Instagram, text, optional): What is the Instagram page of your financial institution?
* **Financial Institution Twitter** (Financial Institution Twitter, text, optional): What is the Twitter page of your financial institution?
* **Financial Institution YouTube** (Financial Institution YouTube, text, optional): What is the YouTube channel of your financial institution?

## Tips
* Please be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, feel free to ask your financial advisor for guidance.
* This form is for informational purposes only and is not a substitute for professional financial advice.
