# Employment Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about an individual's employment history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
3. Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
4. Enter your email address in the **Email** field. Please use a valid email format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
5. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
6. Select your employment start date in the **Employment Start Date** field.
7. Select your employment end date in the **Employment End Date** field (if applicable).
8. Enter any relevant information about your education in the **Education 1 1** and **Education 1 2** fields (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Employment Start Date** (`employment_start_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date you started working.
- **Employment End Date** (`employment_end_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date you stopped working (if applicable).
- **Education 1 1** (`education_1_1`, text, required/optional): Enter any relevant information about your education.
- **Education 1 2** (`education_1_2`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information about your education (if applicable).

## Tips
- Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any issues or questions, contact your HR representative or support team for assistance.
