# Client Session Preference Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects essential information to help us better understand and tailor your session experience to your needs. Please provide accurate and thorough answers to the following questions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name, email address, and phone number in the Personal Information section.
2. Select your preferred session frequency (e.g., Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, or As Needed).
3. Choose your preferred days of the week for sessions (select all that apply).
4. Select your preferred time of day for sessions (e.g., Early Morning, Morning, Afternoon, Late Afternoon, Evening).
5. Choose your preferred session duration (e.g., 30 minutes, 45 minutes, 1 hour, 90 minutes).
6. If you prefer in-person sessions, select your preferred location.
7. Choose your preferred method of communication for reminders (e.g., Email, Phone call, SMS text, No reminders).
8. If you have a language preference, select from the available options.
9. Describe your main goals for these sessions.
10. If applicable, mention any accessibility needs you may have.
11. Provide any additional preferences or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required):
  * This is the first field on the form, and it's essential to provide your full name for accurate identification and communication.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required):
  * Enter your email address so we can keep you informed about session schedules and reminders.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required):
  * Provide your phone number for us to contact you about sessions and any updates.
* **Preferred session frequency** (`session_frequency`, `select_one`, required):
  * Select your preferred frequency of sessions (e.g., Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, As Needed).
* **Preferred days for sessions** (`preferred_days`, `select_multiple`, required):
  * Choose all the days you are available for sessions (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
* **Preferred time of day** (`preferred_time`, `select_one`, required):
  * Select your preferred time of day for sessions (e.g., Early Morning, Morning, Afternoon, Late Afternoon, Evening).
* **Preferred session duration** (`session_duration`, `select_one`, required):
  * Choose your preferred session duration (e.g., 30 minutes, 45 minutes, 1 hour, 90 minutes).
* **If in-person, preferred location** (`location`, `text`, optional):
  * If you prefer in-person sessions, provide the location you would like to have sessions.
* **Preferred contact method for reminders** (`contact_preference`, `select_one`, required):
  * Select your preferred method of communication for reminders (e.g., Email, Phone call, SMS text, No reminders).
* **Language preference** (`language_preference`, `select_one`, optional):
  * If you have a language preference, select from the available options.
* **What are your main goals for these sessions?** (`session_goals`, `text`, optional):
  * Describe your main goals and objectives for these sessions.
* **Do you have any accessibility needs?** (`accessibility_needs`, `select_one`, optional):
  * If you have any accessibility needs, select 'Yes' and describe your needs in the next field.
* **Accessibility details** (`accessibility_details`, `text`, optional):
  * If you selected 'Yes' for accessibility needs, provide a description of your needs.
* **Additional preferences or notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional):
  * Provide any additional preferences or notes that you feel are relevant to your sessions.
