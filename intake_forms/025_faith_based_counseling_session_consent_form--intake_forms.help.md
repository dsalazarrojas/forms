# Faith Based Counseling Session Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients for faith-based counseling sessions, ensuring their consent and understanding of the session details.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by providing your name and contact information, including your first name, last name, email address, and contact number.
2.  Enter your date of birth and social security number for verification purposes.
3.  Specify the date of your last visit to the counseling service.
4.  Describe the reason you're seeking counseling.
5.  Define your session goals and whether you would like to schedule sessions once, weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly.
6.  Choose the duration of each session, select the start and end times, and specify the length of each session.
7.  Indicate if you require flexible session frequency.
8.  Review and submit your form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Form Details** (`form_details`, text, optional): This section provides a brief description of the form and its purpose.
*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates and correspondence.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number where you can be reached for emergencies or updates.
*   **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
*   **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your current address where you can be reached for sessions or updates.
*   **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, text, optional): Enter your social security number for verification purposes.
*   **Date Of Last Visit** (`date_of_last_visit`, date, optional): Enter the date of your last visit to the counseling service.
*   **Reason For Counseling** (`reason_for_counseling`, text, optional): Describe the reason you're seeking counseling.
*   **Session Goals** (`session_goals`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the goals you would like to achieve through the counseling sessions.
*   **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, number, optional): Choose the duration of each session in minutes.
*   **Session Frequency** (`session_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose the frequency of sessions once, weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly.
*   **Session Start Time** (`session_start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of each session.
*   **Session End Time** (`session_end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of each session.
*   **Session Length (min.)** (`session_length`, number, optional): Choose the length of each session in minutes.
*   **Flexible Session Frequency** (`session_frequency_flexible`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you require flexible session frequency.

Note: The optional fields are not required but recommended for a comprehensive understanding of your needs and requirements.
