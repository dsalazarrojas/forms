# upper_respiratory_infection_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a patient's upper respiratory symptoms, medical history, and other relevant details to aid in the diagnosis and treatment of an upper respiratory infection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all the symptoms that the patient is experiencing.
2. Provide information about the patient's medical history, including any allergies or medical conditions that may be relevant to their condition.
3. Indicate the patient's vaccination status, including any recent vaccinations or immunizations.
4. Report any test results that are relevant to the patient's condition.
5. Enter the diagnosis and treatment provided to the patient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **upper_respiratory_infection_assessment** (`upper_respiratory_infection_assessment`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the patient's upper respiratory symptoms.
* **symptoms** (`symptoms`, select_multiple, required): Select all the symptoms that the patient is experiencing, such as fever, sore throat, or runny nose.
* **patient_info** (`patient_info`, text, required): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other identifying information.
* **contact_tracing** (`contact_tracing`, text, required): Provide information about the patient's contact with others who may have been exposed to the patient's infection.
* **medical_history** (`medical_history`, text, required): Provide information about the patient's medical history, including any allergies or medical conditions that may be relevant to their condition.
* **allergies** (`allergies`, text, required): List any allergies that the patient has, including any medications or substances to which they are allergic.
* **vaccination_status** (`vaccination_status`, text, required): Indicate the patient's vaccination status, including any recent vaccinations or immunizations.
* **test_results** (`test_results`, text, required): Report any test results that are relevant to the patient's condition.
* **diagnosis** (`diagnosis`, text, required): Enter the diagnosis and treatment provided to the patient.
* **treatment** (`treatment`, text, required): Enter the treatment provided to the patient.
