# Last Will Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Last Will Intake Form is a document that helps individuals ensure that their wishes are respected after they pass away. It collects personal details of the individual to create a comprehensive document that can be used in the event of their demise.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Please review each field carefully before proceeding.
2. Enter your personal details accurately and honestly.
3. Ensure that you have read and understand the terms and conditions before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, Required/Optional: Optional): The first name of the individual who is creating the last will and testament. Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, Required/Optional: Optional): The last name of the individual who is creating the last will and testament. Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Age
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, Required/Optional: Optional): The age of the individual who is creating the last will and testament. Please enter your age as it is currently being recorded.
