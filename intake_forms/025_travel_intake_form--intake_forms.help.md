**Travel Intake Form - Help Guide**
================================

## Purpose

This form is for collecting information about travel-related activities. It is intended for travel agencies to intake information about travel requests from clients. The form will help gather essential details about the client's preferences, travel plans, and requirements.

Before proceeding, please ensure that you are using this form for its intended purpose and not duplicating existing records. If you have already collected information about a client, please do not submit the form again.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant details about your travel plans, including destinations, travel dates, and preferred mode of transportation.
2. Choose the travel agency you are associated with or would like to use for your travel needs.
3. Indicate your client type (Adult, Child, or Senior) and preferred language for communication.
4. Provide contact information for the travel agency, including phone number and email.
5. Optionally, add any additional travel-related comments or preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Details (text)**:
  This field allows you to enter a brief description of your travel plans.
* **Travel Dates (date)**:
  Enter the dates of your intended trip, in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Destinations (select)**:
  Select one or more destinations for your travel, such as Paris, New York, or Tokyo.
* **Preferred Mode of Transportation (select multiple)**:
  Choose the modes of transportation you prefer, including Air, Train, Bus, Car, or Motorcycle.
* **Preferred Language (select)**:
  Select the language you prefer to communicate in, such as English, Spanish, Mandarin, French, or Japanese.
* **Travel Budget (number)**:
  Enter your estimated budget for the travel, in numerical format.
* **Travel Preferences (text)**:
  Add any additional travel preferences or requirements here.
* **Client Preferences (text)**:
  Enter any client-specific preferences or requirements.
* **Travel Agency (select multiple)**:
  Select the travel agency you are associated with or would like to use for your travel needs.
* **Client Type (select)**:
  Indicate whether you are an Adult, Child, or Senior.
* **Contact Number (text)**:
  Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email (email)**:
  Enter your contact email address.
* **Travel Agent (text)**:
  Enter the name of your travel agent.
* **Travel Agent Title (text)**:
  Enter the title of your travel agent.
* **Travel Agency Address (text)**:
  Enter the address of your travel agency.
* **Travel Agency Phone (text)**:
  Enter the phone number of your travel agency.
* **Travel Agency Email (email)**:
  Enter the email address of your travel agency.
* **Travel Agency Website (text)**:
  Enter the website of your travel agency.
* **Travel Agency Fax (text)**:
  Enter the fax number of your travel agency.

## Tips

Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to the best of your abilities. If you have any doubts or questions, please reach out to your travel agent for assistance.
