# lash_extension_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients for lash extensions services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Client Information**: Please fill in the client's personal details such as name, address, city, state, and zip code.
2. **Lash Extension Type**: Select the type of lash extension service the client is looking for (Lash Lift, Classic Lash, Volume Lash, etc.).
3. **Lash Length**: Choose the length of the lashes the client prefers.
4. **Number of Lashes**: If applicable, enter the number of lashes the client is looking for.
5. **Technician Name**: Enter the name of the technician performing the service.
6. **Appointment Date and Time**: Schedule the appointment date and time with your technician.
7. **Payment Method**: Choose how you would like to pay for the service (Credit Card, Cash, or Check).
8. **Payment Amount**: Enter the total payment amount.
9. **Payment Date**: Record the date of payment.
10. **Payment Time**: Record the time of payment.
11. **Client Signature**: Sign the form to confirm.
12. **Technician Signature**: Sign the form to confirm the appointment details.
13. **Date and Time**: Record the date and time of the appointment.
14. **Phone Number**: Enter the client's phone number.
15. **Email**: Enter the client's email.
16. **Address**: Enter the client's address.
17. **City**: Enter the client's city.
18. **State**: Enter the client's state.
19. **Zip**: Enter the client's zip code.
20. **Country**: Enter the client's country.
21. **Client Name**: Enter the client's name.
22. **Additional Notes**: Leave any additional notes or comments for the technician.
