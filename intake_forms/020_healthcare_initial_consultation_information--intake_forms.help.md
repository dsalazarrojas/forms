# Healthcare Initial Consultation Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information for new patient consultations. Please complete this form to provide healthcare providers with the necessary details to review before and during your consultation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out this form carefully, providing accurate and complete information about yourself.
2. Ensure that you have not already submitted this form for the same patient.
3. Review each field carefully to provide the most relevant information for your consultation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Patient Information** (`new_patient_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, address, and any other identifying information as requested.
* **Consultation Request** (`consultation_request`, text, optional): Briefly describe the reason for your consultation, including any specific issues or concerns you'd like the healthcare provider to be aware of.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Provide a brief summary of your medical history, including any previous medical conditions, treatments, or surgeries.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact details, including phone number and email address, so we can reach you with any further questions or concerns.
* **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance`, text, optional): Provide your medical insurance information, including your insurance ID number.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): List the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Allergies and Medications** (`allergies_and_medications`, text, optional): List any allergies or medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical History Details** (`medical_history_details`, text, optional): Provide more detailed information about your medical history, including any relevant medical conditions or treatments.
* **Medical Tests** (`medical_tests`, text, optional): List any recent medical tests or procedures you have undergone.
* **Medical Procedure** (`medical_procedure`, text, optional): Describe any medical procedures you have undergone or will undergo.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, optional): Sign your name, indicating that you have filled out this form accurately and voluntarily.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of your healthcare provider.
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about yourself that is relevant to your consultation.
* **Medical History Notes** (`medical_history_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about your medical history.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or concerns you'd like to share with your healthcare provider.
* **Medical History Date** (`medical_history_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your last medical history event.
* **Medical History Time** (`medical_history_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your last medical history event.
* **Medical Tests Date** (`medical_tests_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your last medical tests event.
* **Medical Procedure Date** (`medical_procedure_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your last medical procedure event.
* **Patient Signature Date** (`patient_signature_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Patient Signature Time** (`patient_signature_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you signed this form.
* **Medical Tests Time** (`medical_tests_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your last medical tests event.
* **Medical Procedure Time** (`medical_procedure_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your last medical procedure event.
* **Healthcare Provider Time** (`healthcare_provider_time`, time, optional): Enter the time your healthcare provider was seen.
* **Medical Insurance ID** (`medical_insurance_id`, number, optional): Enter your medical insurance ID number.

Note: Some fields are marked as optional, but please ensure that you complete all required fields to avoid any issues with your consultation.
