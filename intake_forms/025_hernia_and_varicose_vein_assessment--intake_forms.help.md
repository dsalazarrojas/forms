# Hernia And Varicose Vein Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Hernia And Varicose Vein Assessment" form is designed to collect patient information for further medical evaluation and diagnosis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each question.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and completely.
3. Provide clear and concise information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Information (Page 1)
* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, optional): Fill in your name, date of birth, and other relevant patient information.

### Symptoms
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, optional): Describe any symptoms or issues you are experiencing related to hernia and varicose veins.

### Vein Assessment
* **Vein Assessment** (`vein_assessment`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your vein condition, including any relevant medical history or concerns.

### Consent
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, optional): Sign and date this form to acknowledge that you have read, understand, and consent to the collection and use of your data for medical evaluation and diagnosis.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide your address, phone number, and other contact information for follow-up or medical correspondence.

### Medical History
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Share any relevant medical history, including previous medical conditions, surgeries, or treatments.

### Medication
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking or have taken in the past.

### Patient Notes
* **Patient Notes** (`patient_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional information you think is relevant to your vein condition or medical history.

### Doctor Notes
* **Doctor Notes** (`doctor_notes`, text, optional): Your healthcare provider may add notes or comments here.

### Insurance
* **Insurance** (`insurance`, text, optional): Provide your insurance information or any relevant insurance claims.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your responses to ensure accurate and reliable data.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your healthcare provider.
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure you have answered all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult your healthcare provider.
