# Procedure Side Effects Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Procedure Side Effects Intake Form is used to collect information about potential side effects or issues experienced by users after a medical procedure. This form helps medical professionals gather and track data on adverse reactions or unexpected consequences following a specific treatment or intervention.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read the form carefully and only fill out the form for relevant and non-duplicate cases.
2. Enter your information in the provided text fields as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input 1** (`User Input 1`, `text`, optional): Please enter a brief description of the medical procedure or treatment that you have undergone or are undergoing.
* **User Input 2** (`User Input 2`, `text`, optional): Please describe any symptoms or issues you have experienced after the medical procedure or treatment.
* **User Input 3** (`User Input 3`, `text`, optional): If you have reported any side effects or issues, please provide more details about when you first experienced them and how long they have been ongoing.
* **User Input 4** (`User Input 4`, `text`, optional): Please provide any additional comments or information about your experience with the medical procedure or treatment.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to provide accurate information.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your healthcare provider or our support team for guidance.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
