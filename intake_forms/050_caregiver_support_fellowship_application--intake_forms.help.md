# Caregiver Support Fellowship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide is designed to walk you through the process of completing the Caregiver Support Fellowship Application form. The form is used to collect information from caregivers and family members who want to apply for the fellowship. By the end of this guide, you should understand the form's purpose, how to complete it, and what each field means.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Read through the form carefully and fill in the required fields. Each section is labeled and explained in detail below.
2.  Answer each question to the best of your abilities. If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
3.  Make sure to sign and agree to the fellowship terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`applicant_name`, Text, **Required**): Please enter your first and last name in this field.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, Email, **Required**): This is your primary contact email. Please use a valid email address that you check frequently.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text, **Required**): Enter your phone number including the area code.
*   **Street Address** (`address`, Text, **Required**): Enter your full street address where you can be reached.
*   **City** (`city`, Text, **Required**): Enter the name of the city where you live.
*   **State or Province** (`state_province`, Text, **Required**): Enter the name of your state or province.
*   **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, Text, **Required**): Enter your postal code or zip code.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, **Required**): Enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
*   **Fellowship Program Type** (`fellowship_type`, Select One, **Required**): Choose the type of fellowship program you're applying for. You can select one of the following:
    *   Caregiver Support and Training
    *   Mental Health and Wellness
    *   Leadership Development
    *   Community Advocacy
    *   Professional Development
    *   Specialized Care Training
*   **Application Date** (`application_date`, Date, **Required**): Enter the date you're submitting this application.
*   **Years of Caregiving Experience** (`caregiving_experience`, Number, **Required**): Enter the number of years you've been caregiving.
*   **Relationship to Care Recipient** (`care_recipient_relationship`, Select One, **Required**): Choose your relationship to the care recipient:
    *   Spouse or partner
    *   Adult child
    *   Parent
    *   Sibling
    *   Grandparent
    *   Grandchild
    *   Other family
    *   Professional caregiver
    *   Multiple relationships
*   **Type of Care Provided** (`caregiving_type`, Select Multiple, **Required**): Select all the types of care you've provided:
    *   Personal hygiene and grooming
    *   Mobility and transfer assistance
    *   Medication management
    *   Medical care or nursing
    *   Meal preparation
    *   Financial management
    *   Emotional or psychological support
    *   Dementia or cognitive care
    *   Palliative or end-of-life care
    *   Multiple types of care
*   **Caregiving Intensity** (`caregiving_intensity`, Number, **Required**): Enter the number of hours per week you spend caregiving.
*   **Current Challenges or Struggles** (`current_challenges`, Text, **Required**): Describe any challenges you're currently facing.
*   **Why Are You Applying for Fellowship?** (`fellowship_motivation`, Text, **Required**): Describe why you're applying for this fellowship program.
*   **Learning Goals and Objectives** (`learning_goals`, Text, **Required**): Describe what you hope to learn from this fellowship.
*   **Type of Support You Need Most** (`support_need`, Select Multiple, **Required**): Select the top three types of support you need:
    *   Mental health or emotional support
    *   Stress management and wellness
    *   Caregiving skills training
    *   Financial planning or assistance
    *   Legal and estate planning
    *   Healthcare navigation
    *   Respite care options
    *   Peer support and community
    *   Professional development
    *   Leadership training
*   **Highest Level of Education** (`education_background`, Select One, **Required**): Choose your highest level of education:
    *   High school or equivalent
    *   Some college
    *   Associate degree
    *   Bachelor's degree
    *   Master's degree
    *   Professional degree
    *   Other training
*   **Professional or Work Background** (`professional_background`, Text, **Optional**): Briefly describe your professional or work background.
*   **Community or Volunteer Involvement** (`community_involvement`, Text, **Optional**): Describe your involvement in community service or volunteering.
*   **Leadership or Advocacy Experience** (`leadership_experience`, Text, **Optional**): Describe any experience you have in leadership roles or advocacy work.
*   **Commitment to Fellowship Program** (`commitment_level`, Select One, **Required**): Choose your commitment level:
    *   Fully committed - will prioritize
    *   Very committed - high priority
    *   Committed - medium priority
    *   Interested - flexible commitment
    *   Exploring - may change plans
*   **Time Available for Fellowship Activities** (`time_availability`, Number, **Required**): Enter the number of hours per week you have available for fellowship activities.
*   **Professional or Personal References** (`references`, Text, **Required**): Provide names and contact information of two professional or personal references.
*   **Additional Qualifications or Skills** (`additional_qualifications`, Text, **Optional**): List any relevant certifications or special skills you have.
*   **Personal Statement** (`personal_statement`, Text, **Required**): Write a brief personal statement about yourself and your vision.
*   **How Will You Define Success?** (`success_definition`, Text, **Optional**): Describe how you would define success in this fellowship program.
*   **I Agree to Fellowship Terms and Conditions** (`terms_agreement`, Select One, **Required**): Select "Yes, I agree" to indicate your agreement to the fellowship terms and conditions.
*   **Additional Information or Comments** (`additional_information`, Text, **Optional**): If you have any additional information or comments, please provide them here.

## Tips

*   Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
*   Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
*   If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
*   Make sure to sign and agree to the fellowship terms and conditions.
