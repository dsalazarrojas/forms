# legalConsultationIntakeForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients and intake new cases for lawyers or paralegals based on their specialization and case type.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client information section (pages 1-2).
2. Select the relevant case details in the case details section (page 3).
3. Choose the legal specialization and contact method of the assigned lawyer or paralegal (pages 4-5).
4. Enter contact information, including phone number and email (pages 6-7).
5. Add any additional notes or comments about the case (page 8).
6. Select the case type and priority (pages 9-10).
7. Enter a description of the case (page 11).
8. Assign a lawyer or paralegal to the case (pages 12-13).
9. Enter the intake date, time, and location (pages 14-15).
10. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Info (page 1)**: Enter the client's name, address, and any other identifying information.
* **Case Details (page 2)**: Describe the nature of the case and any relevant background information.
* **Legal Specialization (page 3)**: Select the area of law that applies to this case, such as family law or criminal law.
* **Contact Info (page 4)**: Choose the method of contact, such as phone or email.
* **Contact Person (page 5)**: Select the person who will be handling the case.
* **Case Type (page 6)**: Select the type of case, such as civil or criminal.
* **Contact Number (page 7)**: Enter the client's phone number.
* **Contact Email (page 8)**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Case Notes (page 9)**: Add any additional notes or comments about the case.
* **Case Priority (page 10)**: Indicate the priority of the case, such as urgent or non-urgent.
* **Case Description (page 11)**: Describe the case in detail.
* **Assigned Lawyer/Paralegal (page 12)**: Select the lawyer or paralegal assigned to the case.
* **Lawyer/Paralegal Name (page 13)**: Enter the name of the assigned lawyer or paralegal.
* **Intake Date (page 14)**: Enter the date the case was intaked.
* **Intake Time (page 15)**: Enter the time the case was intaked.
* **Intake Location (page 16)**: Enter the location where the case was intaked.
* **Case File Number (page 17)**: Enter the case file number.
* **Case Status (page 18)**: Select the current status of the case.
* **Case File (page 19)**: Enter any additional case file information.
* **Case Priority (page 20)**: Indicate the priority of the case.
* **Assigned Paralegal (page 21)**: Enter the name of the assigned paralegal.
* **Notes (page 22)**: Add any additional notes or comments about the case.
