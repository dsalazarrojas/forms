# Vial Vault Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Vial Vault Registration Form is used for registering vials in a storage facility or vault. It helps manage and track vials throughout their storage period, including storage start and end dates, quantities, locations, and contact information for handling.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Vial Vault Registration Form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of vial from the "Vial Type" dropdown menu.
2. Enter the storage start date when the vial was placed in storage.
3. Input the quantity of vials being stored.
4. Choose the storage location from the options provided.
5. Enter your contact name and email address for communication.
6. Provide your contact phone number (optional).
7. Enter a contact message or any notes related to the vial (optional).
8. Record the storage start and end times, if applicable (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vial Type** (`vial_type_form`): Select the type of vial from the available options. This helps identify and categorize vials in storage.
* **Storage Start Date** (`storage_start_date`): Enter the date when the vial was placed in storage.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`): Input the number of vials being stored.
* **Storage Location** (`storage_location`): Choose the location where the vial is stored.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter your name for communication purposes.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Input your phone number for communication (optional).
* **Contact Message** (`contact_message`): Enter a message or note related to the vial (optional).
* **Storage Start Date/Time** (`storage_start_date_time`): Record the start date and time of storage (optional).
* **Storage End Date** (`storage_end_date`): Enter the planned date for removing or retrieving the vial from storage (optional).

## Tips

* Ensure that you accurately select the vial type for correct categorization.
* Enter storage start and end dates precisely for tracking purposes.
* Keep contact information up-to-date to facilitate communication and coordination.
* Use the contact message field for any notes or comments related to the vial.
