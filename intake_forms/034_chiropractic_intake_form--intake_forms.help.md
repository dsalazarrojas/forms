# Chiropractic Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Chiropractic Intake Form is a comprehensive document used to gather essential information about a patient's personal and medical history, symptoms, and health habits. This form helps our chiropractors understand your needs and provide personalized care. Please fill out this form thoroughly and accurately to ensure we can provide you with the best possible treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Carefully read the entire form to understand each section and question.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information about your personal details, medical history, and symptoms.
3. Use the options provided for each field to select the most accurate answers.
4. Review and edit your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (Required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Street Address** (Required): Enter your street address where you can be reached.
* **City** (Required): Enter the city where you can be reached.
* **State** (Required): Enter your state where you can be reached.
* **Zip Code** (Required): Enter your zip code where you can be reached.
* **Current Complaints or Symptoms** (Required): Describe the reasons for your visit, including any symptoms or health concerns.
* **Location of Pain or Discomfort** (Required): Select all areas where you experience pain or discomfort.
* **How Long Have You Had These Symptoms** (Required): Choose how long you have experienced these symptoms.
* **Previous Chiropractic Care** (Required): Indicate if you have received chiropractic care recently or long ago.
* **Previous Injuries or Accidents** (Optional): If applicable, describe any previous injuries or accidents.
* **Surgical History** (Optional): If applicable, list any previous surgeries.
* **Medications** (Optional): List all medications you are currently taking.
* **Medication Allergies** (Optional): If you have any allergies or sensitivities to medications.
* **Other Allergies** (Optional): If you have any allergies or sensitivities to other substances.
* **Current Occupation** (Optional): Describe your current occupation.
* **Exercise or Physical Activity Level** (Optional): Describe your exercise or physical activity level.

This guide should provide users with a clear understanding of the form's purpose, how to complete it, and what each field means. The concise explanations of each field will help ensure accurate and comprehensive data entry.
