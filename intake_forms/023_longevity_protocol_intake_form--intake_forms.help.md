# Longevity Protocol Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Longevity Protocol Intake Form is designed to collect essential information about a patient's client information, medical history, and contact details. This form will help us better understand the patient's needs and provide the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on each field to enter the required information.
2. Fill in the client information (page 1).
3. Provide any relevant medical history information (page 2).
4. Answer the question about participating in wellness programs (page 3).
5. Enter any additional contact information (page 4).
6. Add any additional comments or notes about the patient's medical history (pages 5-9 and 11-15).
7. Add any additional comments or notes about the patient's medical history (page 10 and 16-20).
8. Add any additional comments or notes about the patient's medical history (page 21-23).
9. Add any additional comments or notes about the patient's medical history (page 24).
10. Click the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (page 1): Enter the patient's client information, including their name, address, and contact details.
* **Medical History** (page 2): Provide any relevant medical history information about the patient.
* **Wellness Programs** (page 3): Answer "Yes" if the patient is participating in any wellness programs, "No" otherwise.
* **Contact Information** (page 4): Enter any additional contact information about the patient.
* **Medical Notes 1-10** (pages 5-10 and 16-15): Add any additional comments or notes about the patient's medical history.
* **Medical History 2** (page 21): Provide any additional medical history information about the patient.
* **Contact Information 2** (page 22): Enter any additional contact information about the patient.
* **Medical Notes 2-4** (pages 23-24): Add any additional comments or notes about the patient's medical history.
* **Submit** (page 25): Click this button to submit the form.
