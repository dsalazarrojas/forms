# New Guest Digital Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Guest Digital Consultation Form is used to collect information from guests who have requested a digital consultation, coaching, or therapy session. This form is designed to be completed only once per guest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your first name and last name in the `first_name` and `last_name` fields, respectively.
2. Enter your email address in the `email` field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
3. Enter your phone number in the `phone` field. This will be used for communication purposes.
4. Select your date of birth in the `date_of_birth` field.
5. Choose your preferred time for the consultation in the `time_of_visit` field.
6. Select the type of session you are interested in (Consultation, Coaching, or Therapy) in the `consultation_type` field.
7. Add any additional notes or comments in the `notes` field.
8. Select the person to whom you would like to be assigned (Chat Jimmy or Other) in the `assigned_to` field.
9. The `created_at` field will be automatically generated and is not editable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Select your date of birth.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, optional): Choose your preferred time for the consultation.
* **Consultation Type** (`consultation_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of session you are interested in (Consultation, Coaching, or Therapy).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_multiple, required): Select the person to whom you would like to be assigned (Chat Jimmy or Other).
* **Created At** (`created_at`, date, optional): This field will be automatically generated and is not editable.
