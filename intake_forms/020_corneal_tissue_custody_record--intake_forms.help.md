# corneal_tissue_custody_record - Help Guide
## Purpose
The corneal_tissue_custody_record form is used to document and record information about the collection and custody of corneal tissue samples. This form helps ensure accurate and consistent recording of essential details, such as collection method, date, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form carefully before starting to ensure you understand the purpose and required information.
2. Select the correct collection method from the list of options provided.
3. Enter the date and time of collection, if applicable.
4. Provide the name of the collector and any relevant information about the case.
5. Enter any additional remarks or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Collection Method** (1, select_one):
	+ Select the method used to collect the corneal tissue sample.
* **Date** (2, date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date of collection, if known.
* **Time** (3, time, required: false):
	+ Enter the time of collection, if known.
* **Collector** (4, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the name of the person who collected the sample.
* **Case Number** (5, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the case number associated with the sample.
* **Case Name** (6, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the name of the case.
* **Chain of Custody** (7, text, required: false):
	+ Describe the sequence of events and people involved in the custody of the sample.
* **Remarks** (8, text, required: false):
	+ Enter any additional comments or details about the sample.
* **Submitted By** (9, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted Date** (10, date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date the form was submitted.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure the integrity of the record.
- Review the form for completeness before submitting.
- If unsure about any information, consult with the relevant authorities or experts.
