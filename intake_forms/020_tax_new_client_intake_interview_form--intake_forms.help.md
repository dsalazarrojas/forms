# tax_new_client_intake_interview_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for new clients who are not registered in the system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter your date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter your social security number in the "social_security_number" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "phone_number" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
7. Enter your physical address in the "address" field.
8. Enter your occupation in the "occupation" field.
9. Select your marital status from the options: Married, Single, Divorced, or Widow(er).
10. Enter the number of dependents you have in the "dependents" field.
11. Enter the number of years you have resided in the current address in the "years_of_residence" field.
12. Enter the number of years you have resided in the current address in the "years_of_residence_2" field.
13. Enter your phone number for secondary contact in the "phone_number_2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **last_name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **date_of_birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **social_security_number** (`social_security_number`, text, required: false): Enter your social security number.
* **phone_number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your physical address.
* **occupation** (`occupation`, text, required: false): Enter your occupation.
* **marital_status** (`marital_status`, select_one, required: false): Select your marital status from the options: Married, Single, Divorced, or Widow(er).
* **dependents** (`dependents`, number, required: false): Enter the number of dependents you have.
* **years_of_residence** (`years_of_residence`, number, required: false): Enter the number of years you have resided in the current address.
* **years_of_residence_2** (`years_of_residence_2`, number, required: false): Enter the number of years you have resided in the current address again for verification purposes.
* **phone_number_2** (`phone_number_2`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for secondary contact. 

## Tips
* Double-check all the information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Use the same format for date of birth (MM/DD/YYYY).
* Enter correct social security number and phone numbers for primary and secondary contact.
* Select the correct marital status.
* Enter accurate number of dependents.
* Enter accurate years of residence for both fields.
