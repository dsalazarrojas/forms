# Tow Truck Insurance Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from customers who are interested in insuring their tow trucks. The form helps the insurance provider to gather required details about the truck, its driver, and other relevant information. Please fill out this form carefully, providing accurate and detailed answers.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form to understand what each field is asking for.
2.  Complete the form by providing the requested information for each field.
3.  Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
4.  Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Tow Trunk Information (2)**: Enter the make, model, and year of your tow truck.
*   **Tow Trunk License Plate (3)**: Provide the license plate number of your tow truck.
*   **Insurance Information (4)**: Select from the list to specify which type of insurance information you are providing.
	+   Full Name: This is the full name of the policyholder or vehicle owner.
	+   Address: This is the address associated with the policy.
	+   Phone Number: This is the phone number of the policyholder or vehicle owner.
	+   Email Address: This is the email address of the policyholder or vehicle owner.
*   **Towing Location (5)**: Enter the location where the tow truck was towed.
*   **Towing Date (6)**: Enter the date when the tow truck was towed.
*   **Towing Time (7)**: Enter the time when the tow truck was towed.
*   **Towed Vehicle Information (8)**: Select from the list to specify which information about the towed vehicle you are providing.
	+   Full Name: This is the full name of the vehicle owner.
	+   Address: This is the address associated with the vehicle.
	+   Phone Number: This is the phone number of the vehicle owner.
	+   Email Address: This is the email address of the vehicle owner.
*   **Additional Notes (9)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the incident.
*   **Insurance Quote (10)**: Enter any insurance quotes or relevant notes about the policy.
*   **Follow Up (11)**: Schedule a follow up date if needed.
*   **Tow Truck Insurance Provider (12)**: Select from the list to specify which insurance provider you are using.
*   **Policy Number (13)**: Enter the policy number associated with the insurance.
*   **Agent (14)**: Enter the name of the agent associated with the policy.
*   **Agent Phone (15)**: Enter the phone number of the agent.
*   **Customer Phone (16)**: Enter the phone number of the customer.
*   **Customer Email (17)**: Enter the email address of the customer.
*   **Policy Effective Date (18)**: Enter the date when the policy becomes effective.
*   **Policy Expiration Date (19)**: Enter the date when the policy expires.
*   **Customer Signature (20)**: Sign this field with your name.
*   **Agent Signature (21)**: Sign this field with your name.
*   **Date Submitted (22)**: Date this form was submitted.
*   **Submitted By (23)**: Enter the name of who submitted this form.
*   **Towed Vehicle (24)**: Enter any additional details about the vehicle that was towed.
*   **Insurance Quote 1 (25)**: Enter any additional insurance quotes or notes.
