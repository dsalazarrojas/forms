# QDRO Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and document QDRO information for accurate and efficient processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start with the first page, "Participant Information", and provide the necessary details about the participant involved in the QDRO.
2. Proceed to the next page, "Alternate Payee Information", and provide the necessary details about the alternate payee.
3. On the third page, "Retirement Plans", enter the relevant details about the retirement plans involved.
4. On the fourth page, "Participant Income", enter the participant's income details.
5. Finally, on the last page, "Alternate Payee Address", enter the address of the alternate payee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Information (Page 1)
• **Participant Information** (`qdro_participant`, text, required/optional): Enter the name and other relevant details about the participant involved in the QDRO.

### Alternate Payee Information (Page 2)
• **Alternate Payee Information** (`qdroalternatepayee`, text, required/optional): Enter the name and other relevant details about the alternate payee involved in the QDRO.

### Retirement Plans (Page 3)
• **Retirement Plans** (`retirement_plans`, text, required/optional): Enter the relevant details about the retirement plans involved in the QDRO.

### Participant Income (Page 4)
• **Participant Income** (`participant_income`, number, required/optional): Enter the participant's income details.

### Alternate Payee Address (Page 5)
• **Alternate Payee Address** (`alternate_payee_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the alternate payee.

## Tips
- Double-check the information entered to ensure accuracy and consistency.
- If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the relevant department for clarification.
- Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Save a copy of the completed form for future reference.
