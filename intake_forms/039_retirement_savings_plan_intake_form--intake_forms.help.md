# retirement_savings_plan_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Retirement Savings Plan Intake Form" is designed to collect essential information about an individual's financial situation, goals, and current investments for financial planning and analysis purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Please provide your personal details** under "User Information".
2. Choose your **Financial Goals** and **Institutional Investment** options.
3. Write down your **Financial History** in the provided note field.
4. Select your current **Income Frequency** and choose your **Financial Advisor**.
5. Select your **Other Income Sources** and your **Other Retirement Planning** options.
6. Provide details about your **Investment Experience** and **Investment Return** (if applicable).
7. Select your desired **Risk Tolerance** and **Investment Return** (if applicable).
8. **Retirement Goals** can be selected from the provided options.
9. **Investment Approaches** can also be selected from the options.
10. Enter your current **Retirement Savings** amount and select your **Retirement Age**.
11. Choose your **Retirement Status** and select the correct **Retirement Planning** option.
12. Finally, enter your desired **Investment Frequency** and **Investment Return** (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Information (required)**: Please provide your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself.
  - **Financial Goals (required)**: Choose one or more financial goals, such as saving for a house, paying off debt, or achieving a specific income level.
  - **Financial History (required)**: Write down your financial history, including any relevant events or circumstances that have affected your financial situation.
  - **Income (required)**: Select your current income frequency, whether it's monthly, annually, or bi-annually.
  - **Investment History (optional)**: Select a date for your investment history if you have any previous investment experience.
  - **Assets (required)**: Choose one or more assets you're invested in, such as stocks, bonds, or real estate.
  - **Risk Tolerance (optional)**: Select your risk tolerance level, with options like conservative, moderate, or aggressive.
  - **Investment Experience (optional)**: Provide details about your investment experience and any relevant credentials.
  - **Investment Assets (optional)**: Enter the current value of your invested assets.
  - **Investment Frequency (optional)**: Select the frequency of your investment activity, such as monthly, quarterly, or annually.
  - **Income Frequency (optional)**: Select the frequency of your income, such as monthly, bi-monthly, or annually.
  - **Financial Advisor (optional)**: Choose one or more financial advisors you've consulted or plan to consult.
  - **Other Income Sources (optional)**: Select any other income sources you have, such as self-employment or freelance work.
  - **Investment Objectives (required)**: Write down your investment objectives, such as long-term growth or short-term gains.
  - **Investment Approaches (optional)**: Select one or more investment approaches that align with your objectives.
  - **Retirement Goals (optional)**: Choose one or more retirement goals, such as early retirement or retirement in a specific location.
  - **Investment Return (optional)**: Enter the expected return on your investments, if applicable.
  - **Investment Risk (required)**: Select your current investment risk level, with options like low, moderate, or high.
  - **Financial Institutions (optional)**: Choose one or more financial institutions you've invested with.
  - **Institutional Investment (optional)**: Select one or more institutional investment options.
  - **Retirement Savings (required)**: Enter your current retirement savings amount.
  - **Retirement Age (required)**: Select your desired retirement age.
  - **Retirement Status (required)**: Select your current retirement status, such as not retired or already retired.
  - **Retirement Planning (optional)**: Choose your retirement planning strategy, such as self-directed or assisted.
  - **Other Retirement Planning (optional)**: Select one or more additional retirement planning options.
  - **Investment Institution (optional)**: Enter the value of your current investments.
  - **Investment Frequency (optional)**: Select the frequency of your investment activity.
  - **Investment Return (optional)**: Enter the expected return on your investments.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out this form, as the information will be used for financial planning and analysis.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to the provided options or consult with a financial advisor.
- Review each field carefully to ensure you've filled out all required and optional fields.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for further assistance.
