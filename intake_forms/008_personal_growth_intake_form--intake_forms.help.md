# Personal Growth Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Personal Growth Intake Form is designed to help individuals share their thoughts, feelings, and goals related to personal growth and development. It is intended to gather information that will help in better understanding and addressing individual needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields based on your personal information and experiences.
2. Provide as much detail as possible for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1

- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name, if you have one.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name, if you have one.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
- **What is challenging you?** (`challenge1`, text, optional): Briefly describe the most challenging obstacle you are facing right now.
- **Challenges** (`challenge2`, text, optional): Describe any other challenges you are facing.
- **Goal** (`goal`, text, optional): Share your personal or professional goal(s) you are striving for.
- **Readiness for change** (`readiness`, text, optional): Rate your readiness to make a change on a scale of 1-5 or simply state your readiness for change.

## Tips

- Please be as honest and accurate as possible in filling in the form.
- You can skip fields if they are not relevant to you.
- If you need help with anything, feel free to ask.
