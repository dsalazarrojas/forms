# Client Orientation For Autism Services - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form helps us understand your needs and coordinate the best care for you and your client. It's a crucial part of our intake process, and we need this information to provide effective services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete each section thoroughly and accurately.
2. Use the provided instructions and examples for each field.
3. If you are unsure about any field, contact us for assistance.
4. Please submit one form per client.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client First Name** (Client First Name, text, required): Enter your client's first name.
* **Client Last Name** (Client Last Name, text, required): Enter your client's last name.
* **Client Date of Birth** (Client Date of Birth, date, required): Enter your client's date of birth (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Client Age** (Client Age, number, required): Enter your client's age.
* **Primary Caregiver Name** (Primary Caregiver Name, text, required): Enter the name of the primary caregiver.
* **Caregiver Relationship to Client** (Caregiver Relationship to Client, select_one, required): Select the relationship between the caregiver and client (Parent, Guardian, Sibling, Other Family Member, Professional Caregiver, Other).
* **Caregiver Email Address** (Caregiver Email Address, email, required): Enter the email address of the primary caregiver.
* **Caregiver Phone Number** (Caregiver Phone Number, text, required): Enter the phone number of the primary caregiver.
* **Home Address** (Home Address, text, required): Enter the street address, city, state, and zip code of the client's home.
* **Date of Autism Diagnosis** (Date of Autism Diagnosis, date, optional): Enter the approximate date of the client's autism diagnosis (mm/dd/yyyy). 
* **Diagnosing Professional or Clinic** (Diagnosing Professional or Clinic, text, optional): Enter the name of the professional or clinic that diagnosed the client's autism.
* **Current Services or Therapies Received** (Current Services or Therapies Received, select_multiple, required): Select all the services or therapies the client is currently receiving (Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA), Speech Therapy, Occupational Therapy, Physical Therapy, Social Skills Training, Counseling, Educational Support, None, Other).
* **Current Service Frequency** (Current Service Frequency, select_one, required): Select how often the client receives services (Daily, Several times per week, Once per week, 2-3 times per month, Once per month, Not currently receiving services).
* **Primary Concerns or Challenges** (Primary Concerns or Challenges, select_multiple, required): Select all the areas that need the most support (Communication, Social Interaction, Behavioral Challenges, Daily Living Skills, Sensory Processing, Academic Skills, Emotional Regulation, Motor Skills, Other).
* **Client Communication Methods** (Client Communication Methods, select_multiple, required): Select all the methods the client uses to communicate (Verbal Speech, Sign Language, Picture Exchange System (PECS), Communication Device or App, Written Communication, Gestures, Other).
* **Known Sensory Sensitivities** (Known Sensory Sensitivities, text, optional): Describe any known sensitivities to sounds, textures, lights, etc.
* **Additional Medical Conditions or Diagnoses** (Additional Medical Conditions or Diagnoses, select_multiple, optional): Select all the medical conditions or diagnoses the client has (ADHD, Anxiety, Epilepsy, Sleep Disorders, Gastrointestinal Issues, Food Allergies, None, Other).
* **Current Medications** (Current Medications, text, optional): List the name and dosage of any medications the client is currently taking.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Emergency Contact Name, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (Emergency Contact Phone Number, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Preferred Service Location** (Preferred Service Location, select_one, required): Select the preferred location for services (Home-based Services, Clinic-based Services, School-based Services, Community-based Services, Telehealth, No Preference).
* **Insurance Provider** (Insurance Provider, text, optional): Enter the name of the client's insurance provider.
* **Insurance Policy Number** (Insurance Policy Number, text, optional): Enter the client's insurance policy number.
* **How Did You Hear About Our Services** (How Did You Hear About Our Services, select_one, optional): Select how the client heard about our services (Physician Referral, School District, Other Provider Referral, Friend or Family, Online Search, Social Media, Other).
* **What Are Your Primary Goals for Services** (What Are Your Primary Goals for Services, text, required): Enter your primary goals for services.
* **Additional Information** (Additional Information, text, optional): Enter any additional information that would help us better serve the client.

## Tips
* Be as accurate as possible when completing this form.
* If you have questions or concerns, contact us.
* We may follow up with you after submitting this form to discuss your answers.
* Please submit one form per client.
