# Medical Tour Service Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a medical tour service agreement. The agreement requires users to provide personal and medical information to ensure the safety and effectiveness of the tour.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button after filling out all required fields.
2. Enter your name and email address on the second page to confirm your identity.
3. Answer each question accurately and truthfully.
4. Select the correct options for the questions that have multiple options.
5. Upload a photo of your medical ID, if required.
