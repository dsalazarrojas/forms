# Appendicitis Symptoms Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Appendicitis Symptoms Assessment form is used to collect and assess symptoms associated with appendicitis, a medical condition that requires prompt attention. This form is designed for patients or clinicians to input information that will help healthcare professionals make informed decisions about patient care.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, fill out the required fields: Pain location, Symptom onset date, and Pain severity.
2.  Select the symptom that best matches the patient's symptoms from the Associated symptoms list.
3.  For Recent abdominal trauma or surgery, select 'True' if the patient has experienced abdominal trauma or surgery within the last year. Otherwise, select 'False'.
4.  In the Allergies and current medications field, list any current medications or allergies the patient is taking.
5.  Leave the Triage recommendation field for the clinician to fill out after assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient name (patient_name)**:
    *   Optional for anonymous intake, otherwise enter the patient's name.
*   **Symptom Onset Date (onset_date)**:
    *   Enter the date the patient's symptoms began.
*   **Pain Location (pain_location)**:
    *   Select where the patient is feeling the pain:
    *   Periumbilical
    *   Right lower quadrant
    *   Generalized
    *   Other
*   **Pain Severity (pain_severity)**:
    *   Rate the current pain level, from 0 to 10.
*   **Associated Symptoms (associated_symptoms)**:
    *   Select all symptoms that apply:
    *   Nausea or vomiting
    *   Fever
    *   Loss of appetite
    *   Diarrhea or constipation
    *   None
*   **Recent Abdominal Trauma or Surgery (recent_trauma_or_surgery)**:
    *   Select 'True' if the patient has experienced abdominal trauma or surgery within the last year. Otherwise, select 'False'.
*   **Allergies and Current Medications (allergies_and_medications)**:
    *   List any current medications or allergies the patient is taking.
*   **Triage Recommendation (triage_recommendation)**:
    *   This field is intended for the clinician to fill out after assessment.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately for proper triage and assessment.
*   If a patient's symptoms do not match any of the provided options, select 'Other' for Associated Symptoms.
*   Leave the Triage recommendation field for the clinician to fill out after assessment.
