# Medical Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Intake Form is a collection of questions designed to gather essential medical information from patients for the purpose of assessment and treatment planning. This form is meant to be completed by patients or their authorized representatives and is intended for use by medical professionals.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Complete each field as thoroughly and accurately as possible.
2.  Review and ensure that you understand each field's purpose before providing information.
3.  Do not skip any fields, as the information is necessary for comprehensive treatment planning.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Provide a detailed account of your medical history, including any previous medical conditions, treatments, or surgeries you have undergone.
*   **Patient Identification** (`patient_identification`, select_one, required): Please select the correct patient from the list provided. If you are an authorized representative, select 'Other' to provide more information about your relationship with the patient.
*   **Past Surgeries** (`past_surgeries`, select_multiple, required): List any past surgeries you have undergone, including dates and details of procedures.
*   **Medical Symptoms** (`medical_symptoms`, select_multiple, optional): List any current or recurring medical symptoms you are experiencing.
*   **Genetic Information** (`genetic_information`, text, required): Provide information about any genetic conditions or family medical history that may be relevant to your case.
*   **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, required): List any allergies you have, including medications, food, or environmental factors.
*   **Medications** (`medications`, select_multiple, optional): List any medications you are currently taking or have taken in the past.
*   **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional information that may be helpful for your treatment.

## Tips

*   If you are unsure about any field, please consult with a healthcare professional for guidance.
*   Ensure that you complete all required fields to receive comprehensive care.
