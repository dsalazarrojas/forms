# Business Information Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential business information to help us understand your company's needs and provide effective support.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please answer each field according to the instructions provided.
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company Name** (1): Enter your full business name as it is registered.
- **Type of Business** (2): Select the category that best describes your business (Retail, Service, Manufacturing, Technology, Consulting, or Other).
- **Years in Business** (3): Provide the number of years your business has been operating.
- **Estimated Annual Revenue** (4): Choose the approximate annual revenue of your business (Under $100,000, $100,000 - $500,000, $500,000 - $1,000,000, $1,000,000 - $5,000,000, Over $5,000,000).
- **Primary Contact Name** (5): Enter the full name of the main contact person.
- **Email Address** (6): Provide the business email address.
- **Phone Number** (7): Enter the primary business phone number.
- **Business Address** (8): Enter the full street address of your business location.
- **Number of Employees** (9): If applicable, enter the total current workforce.
- **Main Products or Services** (10): Briefly describe what your business offers.
- **Business Improvement Goals** (11): Select all areas you would like to improve (Marketing and Sales, Operations, Customer Service, Financial Management, Technology, Staff Training).
- **Current Business Challenges** (12): Describe any challenges your business is currently facing.
- **Budget for Improvements** (13): Choose the approximate budget available for improvements (Under $5,000, $5,000 - $15,000, $15,000 - $50,000, Over $50,000, Not Determined Yet).
- **Implementation Timeline** (14): Select the desired implementation time frame (Immediate (0-3 months), Short-term (3-6 months), Medium-term (6-12 months), Long-term (12+ months)).
- **Decision Authority** (15): Select yes or no to indicate if you have authority to make purchasing decisions, or partially if you have partial authority.

Note: If you have any questions or need help with this form, don't hesitate to ask.
