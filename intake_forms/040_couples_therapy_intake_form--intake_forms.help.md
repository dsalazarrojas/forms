# Couples Therapy Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Couples Therapy Intake Form is a crucial tool for providing personalized couples therapy services. By completing this form, couples can provide essential information about their relationship, partner backgrounds, and individual goals, which helps the therapist understand their situation better.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide general information about you and your partner (couple_info).
2. Enter details about each partner's background, including their history, relationship status, and counseling experiences (partner1_info, partner1_history, partner1_support, partner1_support_history, partner2_info, partner2_history, partner2_support, and partner2_support_history).
3. Describe the relationship dynamics and any issues you are experiencing (issues).
4. Specify your goals for therapy (goals).
5. Provide information about the relationship duration and status (relationship_status and relationship_duration).
6. Indicate the frequency of therapy sessions per week (sessions_per_week).
7. Confirm the relationship status and duration for each partner (partner1_relationship_status, partner1_relationship_duration, partner2_relationship_status, partner2_relationship_duration).
8. Outline any previous counseling experiences for each partner (partner1_counseling_goals, partner1_counseling_history, partner2_counseling_goals, partner2_counseling_history).
9. Enter any notes for the therapist's reference (therapist_notes).
10. Indicate the current therapy status (completed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **couple_info**: A text field to provide general information about you and your partner.
* **partner1_info**: A text field for each partner to enter their background details.
* **partner1_history**: A text field to describe each partner's history.
* **partner1_support**: A number field to indicate each partner's support level (1-10).
* **partner1_support_history**: A text field to describe each partner's support history.
* **partner2_info**: A text field for each partner to enter their background details.
* **partner2_history**: A text field to describe each partner's history.
* **partner2_support**: A number field to indicate each partner's support level (1-10).
* **partner2_support_history**: A text field to describe each partner's support history.
* **relationship_info**: A text field to describe the relationship dynamics and any issues.
* **issues**: A select_one field to select any specific issues (e.g., Anger Issues, Trust Issues, Communication Issues).
* **goals**: A text field to specify your goals for therapy.
* **history**: A text field to provide additional historical context.
* **partner1_relationship_status**: A select_one field to confirm each partner's relationship status (Single, In a Relationship, Engaged).
* **partner1_relationship_duration**: A select_multiple field to select each partner's relationship duration (Less than 1 month, 1-3 months, More than 3 months).
* **partner2_relationship_status**: A select_one field to confirm each partner's relationship status (Single, In a Relationship, Engaged).
* **partner1_counseling_goals**: A text field to describe each partner's counseling goals.
* **partner1_counseling_history**: A text field to describe each partner's counseling history.
* **partner2_counseling_goals**: A text field to describe each partner's counseling goals.
* **partner2_counseling_history**: A text field to describe each partner's counseling history.
* **therapist_notes**: A text field for the therapist's notes.
* **completed**: A select_one field to confirm the therapy status (Not Started, Completed).
