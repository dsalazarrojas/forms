# New Client Group Session Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about new clients for group sessions. Please answer all required questions and provide as much detail as possible for our staff to review.

## How To Complete This Form
* Please answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any question, contact us for clarification.
* Please fill in all required fields (indicated by *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **client_group_session_intake_form_1** (`Yes`, `No`): 
  Please select "Yes" if you are interested in participating in a group session.
* **client_details_1** (`text`): 
  Please provide us with your name and any other identifying information.
* **health_history_1** (`text`): 
  Share any relevant health-related information about yourself.
* **consent_1** (`Yes`, `No`): 
  Select "Yes" to confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions of the group sessions.
* **client_contact_1** (`text`): 
  Provide your contact information for us to reach out to you about the sessions.
* **email_1** (`email`): 
  Please enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **session_date_1** (`date`): 
  Select a date for your desired group session.
* **session_time_1** (`time`): 
  Choose a time for your group session.
* **form_notes_1** (`text`): 
  Add any additional information that might be relevant to your session.
* **assigned_instructor_1** (`Yes`, `No`): 
  Please select "Yes" to have an assigned instructor for your session.
* **additional_notes_1** (`text`): 
  Provide any other information that might be relevant to your session.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your contact information is up-to-date and accurate.
* If you have any concerns or questions, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We'll review your form submission and be in touch with you soon.
