# Massage Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from patients for massage therapy services, including appointment details, symptoms, and massage preferences.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the appointment time and date according to your actual schedule.
2. Select the type of appointment (Massage, Physical Therapy, Chiropractic, or Other).
3. If you have any symptoms, select the ones that apply to you.
4. Describe your symptoms in the text field.
5. Enter your massage preferences, including the duration of the massage.
6. Enter your name and email address for our records.
7. Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
8. Enter any additional message or comments about your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Appointment Details** (id: 1): Enter the date and time of your appointment.
* **Symptoms Selected** (id: 2): Select any symptoms that apply to you (Yes or No).
* **Describe Symptoms** (id: 3): Enter a detailed description of your symptoms.
* **Massage Details** (id: 4): Enter any specific massage details you have (e.g., focus areas).
* **Appointment Time** (id: 5): Enter the actual time of your appointment.
* **Appointment Date** (id: 6): Enter the actual date of your appointment.
* **Patient Name** (id: 7): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (id: 8): Enter your email address for our records.
* **Phone Number** (id: 9): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Message** (id: 10): Enter any additional comments or questions you have.
* **Appointment Type** (id: 11): Select the type of appointment you have (Massage, Physical Therapy, Chiropractic, or Other).
* **Massage Duration** (id: 12): Select the length of time you would like your massage to be.
