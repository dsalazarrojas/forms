# hospital patient status check in form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to check the status of patients in a hospital setting. It is intended to collect information about a patient's ID, name, date and time of admission, medical history, allergies, medications, and the nurse's name and ID (if available). This form is likely used by medical staff to quickly and efficiently track patient information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary information about the patient before starting the form.
2. Enter the patient's ID in the "patient id" field. This is a unique identifier for the patient.
3. Enter the patient's name in the "name" field.
4. Enter the patient's date of admission in the "date of admission" field in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
5. Enter the patient's time of admission in the "time of admission" field in the format "HH:MM:SS".
6. In the "medical history" field, describe the patient's relevant medical history, if any.
7. In the "allergies" field, list any allergies the patient has.
8. In the "medications" field, list any medications the patient is currently taking.
9. In the "nurse name" field, enter the name of the nurse who is caring for the patient, if known.
10. In the "nurse id" field, enter the ID number of the nurse who is caring for the patient, if known.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient ID** (patient_id, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's unique ID number.
* **Name** (name, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's name.
* **Date of Admission** (date of admission, date, required/optional): Enter the date the patient was admitted in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Time of Admission** (time of admission, time, required/optional): Enter the time the patient was admitted in the format "HH:MM:SS".
* **Medical History** (medical history, text, required/optional): Describe the patient's relevant medical history, if any.
* **Allergies** (allergies, text, required/optional): List any allergies the patient has.
* **Medications** (medications, text, required/optional): List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Nurse Name** (nurse name, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the nurse who is caring for the patient, if known.
* **Nurse ID** (nurse id, number, required/optional): Enter the ID number of the nurse who is caring for the patient, if known.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check the patient's information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you are unsure about a field, consult with a medical professional or the hospital's protocol for patient information.
* Remember to submit the form to complete the patient's status check.
