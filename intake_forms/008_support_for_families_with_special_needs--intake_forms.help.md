# Support For Families With Special Needs - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about families with special needs to provide support and services. It helps us understand their requirements and provide the necessary assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Family Information (Page 1)**: Fill out the "Family Information" page with general details about the family.
2. **Family Members (Page 2)**: Select all family members who require support.
3. **Member Details (Page 3)**: Enter additional details about each family member.
4. **Family History (Page 4)**: Provide any relevant family history that may be helpful.
5. **Family Crisis Services (Page 5)**: Select the type of crisis services the family requires.
6. **Family Support Services (Page 6)**: Select the types of support services the family needs.
7. **Family Needs (Page 7)**: Enter any specific needs or concerns the family has.
8. **Follow-up (Page 8)**: Select the follow-up frequency and enter any follow-up notes.
9. **Notes (Page 9)**: Enter any additional notes about the family.
10. **Contact Information (Page 10)**: Enter the family's contact email and phone number.
11. **Follow-up Details (Page 11)**: Enter the follow-up date and time.
12. **Follow-up User (Page 12)**: Enter the user responsible for follow-up.
13. **Follow-up Comments (Page 13)**: Enter any follow-up comments.
14. **Family Needs Status (Page 14)**: Select the family's needs status.
15. **Family Needs Notes (Page 15)**: Enter any additional notes about the family's needs.
16. **Family Needs Counselor (Page 16)**: Enter the counselor's name.
17. **Assistance (Page 17)**: Select the type of assistance the family requires.
18. **Family Needs Comments (Page 18)**: Enter any additional comments about the family's needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Family Information**: Enter the family's name, address, and other general details.
* **Family Members**: Select all family members who require support.
* **Member Details**: Enter additional details about each family member, such as their needs and concerns.
* **Family History**: Enter any relevant family history that may be helpful.
* **Family Crisis Services**: Select the type of crisis services the family requires.
* **Family Support Services**: Select the types of support services the family needs.
* **Family Needs**: Enter any specific needs or concerns the family has.
* **Family Needs Details**: Enter any additional details about the family's needs.
* **Family Needs Status**: Select the family's needs status (Active, Inactive, or Archived).
* **Family Needs Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the family's needs.
* **Family Needs Counselor**: Enter the name of the family counselor.
* **Assistance**: Select the type of assistance the family requires.
* **Family Needs Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the family's needs.
* **Contact E-mail**: Enter the family's email address.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the family's phone number.
* **Follow-up Frequency**: Enter the follow-up frequency.
* **Follow-up Notes**: Enter any follow-up notes.
* **Follow-up Date**: Enter the follow-up date.
* **Follow-up Time**: Enter the follow-up time.
* **Follow-up User**: Enter the user responsible for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Comments**: Enter any follow-up comments.
* **Family Needs Additional Info**: Enter any additional information about the family's needs.
* **Signature**: Enter your signature.
* **Additional Info**: Enter any additional information about the family.
