# Animal Shelter Digital Platform Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Shelter Digital Platform Registration Form is designed to collect information from users who wish to register on our platform. This includes adopters, foster parents, volunteers, donors, service providers, partner organizations, and staff members. The form will help us understand your needs and preferences, ensuring a smooth and personalized experience on our platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full Name**, which should include your first and last name.
2. Enter your **Email Address**, which will be your primary contact email.
3. Provide your **Phone Number**, which will be used for communication.
4. Enter your **Date of Birth**, ensuring you are 18 or older.
5. Fill in your **Street Address**, including your house number and street name.
6. Enter your **City** where you reside.
7. Choose your **State or Province** where you live.
8. Enter your **ZIP or Postal Code** for accurate delivery.
9. Select your **Type of Registration**, indicating your primary role on the platform.
10. Choose a **Desired Username** for platform login.
11. Create a **Password** for secure login access.
12. Indicate what you will use the platform for by selecting all relevant options from **What Will You Use This Platform For**.
13. Choose your **Notification Preferences**, such as email, SMS, or platform messages.
14. Confirm that we may **Contact You About Shelter Activities**.
15. Select how you **Heard About Our Platform**, if applicable.
16. Indicate if you have **Interacted with Our Shelter Before**, including previous adoptions, volunteering, or donations.
17. Disclose if you have **Accessibility Requirements**, and if so, provide more details.
18. Select your **Preferred Language** for platform communication.
19. Confirm that you **Accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy**.
20. Consent to your **Data Being Used for Shelter Operations**.
21. Optionally, choose to **Receive Marketing Communications**.
22. Provide the **Emergency Contact Name** and **Phone Number** (if applicable).
23. Add any **Additional Information** about yourself.
