# Insect Bite Physical Exam Documentation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to document the physical examination of a patient for an insect bite, gathering essential information to aid in diagnosis, treatment, and patient care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and resources before starting the examination.
2. Complete each section of the form in the following order:
	* First page: Provide a brief description of the patient's condition and the examination process.
	* Patient info: Enter the patient's personal details, such as name, ID number, and contact information.
	* Bite info: Record the details of the insect bite, including location, size, and any other relevant information.
	* Symptoms: Describe the patient's symptoms and complaints related to the bite.
	* Location: Note the location of the bite on the body.
	* Bite size: Record the size of the bite using the provided scale.
	* Patient condition: Select the patient's condition (allergic or non-allergic) based on your examination and medical history.
	* Medical history: Select any relevant medical conditions or allergies the patient may have.
	* Follow up: Schedule a follow-up appointment or note any further actions required.
	* Examiner: Record your name, contact information, and qualifications.
	* Date examined: Document the date of the examination.
	* Examiner signature: Sign and date your signature as the examiner.
	* Examiner contact: Provide your contact information for future reference.
	* Examiner notes: Record any additional notes or comments about the examination.
	* Patient signature: Have the patient sign and date their agreement to the examination and documentation.
	* Patient agreement: Select the patient's agreement status (agreed or not agreed).
	* Patient agreement date: Record the date of the patient's agreement.
	* Patient contact: Note the patient's contact information.
	* Patient address: Record the patient's address.
	* Examiner licence: Record your professional qualifications and licence number.
	* Examiner position: Record your position or title.
	* Examiner workplace: Record your workplace or clinic.
	* Examiner workplace address: Record your workplace address.
	* Patient ID: Record the patient's ID number.
	* Other comments: Provide any additional comments or notes about the examination.
