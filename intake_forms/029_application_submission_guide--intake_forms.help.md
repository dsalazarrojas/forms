# Application Submission Guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Application Submission Guide is a form designed to help applicants provide accurate and complete information for their application. This guide will walk you through each section of the form and help you understand what is required.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and provide the requested information.
2. Ensure you answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. If you are unsure about any section, do not hesitate to ask for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`Full Name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, email, required): Enter your valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Mailing Address** (`Mailing Address`, text, required): Enter your mailing address.
* **Program Name** (`Program Name`, text, required): Select the name of the program you are applying to.
* **Application Deadline** (`Application Deadline`, date, required): Enter the date by which you plan to apply.
* **Intended Enrollment Date** (`Intended Enrollment Date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to enroll in the program.
* **Resume - Uploaded?** (`Resume - Uploaded?`, select_one, required): Select the status of your resume upload.
* **Academic Transcripts - Uploaded?** (`Academic Transcripts - Uploaded?`, select_one, required): Select the status of your academic transcript upload.
* **Letters of Recommendation - Uploaded?** (`Letters of Recommendation - Uploaded?`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your letters of recommendation upload.
* **Statement of Purpose or Essay - Completed?** (`Statement of Purpose or Essay - Completed?`, select_one, required): Select the status of your statement of purpose or essay.
* **Certifications or Credentials - Uploaded?** (`Certifications or Credentials - Uploaded?`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your certifications or credentials upload.
* **Statement of Purpose or Personal Essay** (`Statement of Purpose or Personal Essay`, text, required): Write a statement explaining your goals and reasons for applying.
* **Additional information or special circumstances** (`Additional information or special circumstances`, text, optional): Provide any additional information or special circumstances that may be relevant to your application.
* **I certify that all information provided is accurate** (`I certify that all information provided is accurate`, select_one, required): Confirm that all information provided is accurate.
