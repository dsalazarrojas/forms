# Criminal Defense Consultation Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients seeking consultation on criminal defense matters. It is intended to provide the consultant with a comprehensive understanding of the client's case and circumstances.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's information in the "Client Information" section.
2. Provide details about the case in the "Case Information" section.
3. Specify the representation details, if applicable, in the "Representation Details" section.
4. Enter the contact information of the person filling out the form in the "Contact Information" section.
5. If applicable, describe the current case status in the "Case Status" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Information
• **Client Information** (`client_info`, text, optional): Enter the client's name, date of birth, and any other relevant identifying information.

### Case Information
• **Case Information** (`case_info`, text, optional): Describe the nature of the case, including the charges, court dates, and any other relevant details.

### Representation Details
• **Representation Details** (`representation_details`, text, optional): If you are being represented by an attorney, provide their name, contact information, and any other relevant details.

### Contact Information
• **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, email address, and phone number so that the consultant can contact you for further information.

### Case Status
• **Case Status** (`case_status`, text, optional): Describe the current status of the case, including any upcoming court dates, hearings, or other relevant updates.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
- If you are unsure about any part of the form, consult with the consultant before submitting.
- Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or need further clarification, do not hesitate to contact the consultant.
