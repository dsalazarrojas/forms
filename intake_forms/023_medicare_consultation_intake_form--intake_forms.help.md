# Medicare Consultation Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from patients before a consultation with a healthcare provider. It will help the healthcare provider understand the patient's medical history, current condition, and reason for the consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name
2. Enter your last name
3. Input your date of birth
4. Provide your contact number
5. Enter your email address
6. Enter your current address
7. Describe your medical history
8. List any medications you are currently taking
9. Describe the medical procedure you are undergoing
10. Enter your Medicare card number (if applicable)
11. Enter the name of your doctor
12. Enter the specialty of your doctor
13. Describe your current medical condition
14. Enter the name of the medical facility you are visiting
15. Enter the department of the medical facility you are visiting
16. Describe the reason for your visit
17. Enter the date of your visit
18. Enter the time of your visit
19. Enter the length of your consultation
20. Provide a signature (patient signature)
21. Provide a signature (doctor signature)
22. Enter the medical record number (if applicable)
23. Enter the billing number (if applicable)
24. Provide any additional comments

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your official identification.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your official identification.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Address**: Enter your current address.
* **Medical history**: Describe any previous medical conditions or surgeries you have had.
* **Medication**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical procedure**: Describe the medical procedure you are undergoing.
* **Medicare card number**: Enter your Medicare card number if you have one.
* **Doctor name**: Enter the name of your doctor.
* **Doctor specialty**: Enter the specialty of your doctor.
* **Medical condition**: Describe your current medical condition.
* **Medical facility**: Enter the name of the medical facility you are visiting.
* **Medical department**: Enter the department of the medical facility you are visiting.
* **Reason for visit**: Describe the reason for your visit.
* **Visit date**: Enter the date of your visit in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Visit time**: Enter the time of your visit.
* **Appointment time**: Enter the scheduled appointment time.
* **Consultation length**: Estimate the length of your consultation.
* **Patient signature**: Provide a signature to confirm that you have reviewed and understood the information provided.
* **Doctor signature**: Provide a signature from the healthcare provider to confirm that they have reviewed and understood the information provided.
* **Medical record number**: Enter the medical record number if applicable.
* **Billing number**: Enter the billing number if applicable.
* **Additional comments**: Provide any additional comments or information that may be helpful for the healthcare provider.
