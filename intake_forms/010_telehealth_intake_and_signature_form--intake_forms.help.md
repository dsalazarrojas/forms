Telehealth Intake And Signature Form
==========================

This form is designed for patients to provide essential information and consent for telehealth services. It serves as a digital intake form for healthcare providers to gather patient data and obtain necessary signatures. When completing this form, please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and truthfully.

The form is divided into several sections, each with its own purpose. Please note that some fields are optional and only applicable in specific situations.

### How To Complete This Form

1.  **Patient Information** (Page 1)
    *   Please provide your basic patient information, including your name, contact details, and medical history.
2.  **Consent** (Page 2)
    *   Select 'Yes' to agree to receive telehealth services.
3.  **Signatures** (Page 3)
    *   Enter the date and time you sign this form.
4.  **Provider Information** (Page 4)
    *   Enter your name (if you are a patient or a provider).
    *   Enter the name of your healthcare practice.
5.  **Signatures** (Page 5)
    *   Enter your number (not applicable).
    *   Enter the provider's number (not applicable).
    *   Enter the practice's number (not applicable).
6.  **Consent Details** (Page 6)
    *   Enter the text of your consent.
    *   Enter the date you consented to services.
    *   Enter the time you consented to services.
    *   Enter your signature (not applicable).
7.  **Telehealth Signature** (Page 7)
    *   Enter your number (not applicable).
