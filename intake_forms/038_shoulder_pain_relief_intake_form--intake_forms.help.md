## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from patients who are experiencing shoulder pain, allowing medical professionals to provide personalized care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and detailed information about your shoulder pain experience.
2. Ensure that you are a patient or a medical professional authorized to fill out this form.
3. Do not duplicate your submission.
