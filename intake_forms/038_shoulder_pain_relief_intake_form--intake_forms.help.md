# Shoulder Pain Relief Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from patients who are experiencing shoulder pain, allowing medical professionals to provide personalized care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and detailed information about your shoulder pain experience.
2. Ensure that you are a patient or a medical professional authorized to fill out this form.
3. Do not duplicate your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info (id: 1)** (`text`, required: false)
	+ This field is used to collect your basic information, such as name and contact details.
* **Pain Details (id: 2)** (`text`, required: false)
	+ This field is used to describe your shoulder pain experience, including the duration and intensity of the pain.
* **Medical History (id: 3)** (`text`, required: false)
	+ This field is used to record any previous injuries or surgeries related to your shoulder.
* **Contact Info (id: 4)** (`text`, required: false)
	+ This field is used to collect your contact information, such as address and emergency contact details.
* **Submit (id: 5)** (`text`, required: false)
	+ Pressing this button will submit your completed form for review and processing.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure that your condition is properly understood.
- If you have any medical emergencies, please contact your medical provider immediately.
- This form is intended for shoulder pain relief, if you have any other medical condition, please seek appropriate care.
