# Patient Starter Kit Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient information for access to the Patient Starter Kit, a resource that supports patients during the initial stages of a medical treatment program. To complete this form, you will need to provide basic patient details, including their name, contact information, and delivery preferences. This guide will walk you through each field and what to expect.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Patient Starter Kit Access Form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **First Name** and **Last Name** fields with your full name as it appears on your identification documents. These fields are required.
2. Enter your **Email Address** (if you have one). While not required, this helps us contact you with further instructions and updates.
3. Provide your **Phone Number** (if you have one). This is optional, but it allows us to contact you if needed.
4. Enter your **Date of Birth**. This is not required, but it helps us tailor your experience.
5. Enter your **Address** and **City**. These fields are required.
6. Choose your **State** of residence. This is a required field.
7. Choose your preferred **Delivery Method** for receiving the Patient Starter Kit. You can choose from:
	* Home
	* Clinic
	* Other (please specify)
8. If you selected "Other" for delivery method, please provide a brief explanation in the **Delivery Note** field.
9. Indicate if you prefer **Delivery Location** at your home or not.
10. Enter any **Patient Preferences** you may have (e.g., language, accessibility needs).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Patient Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Patient Email**: Enter your email address, if you have one. This helps us contact you with further instructions and updates.
* **Patient Phone**: Enter your phone number, if you have one. This is optional, but it allows us to contact you if needed.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth, if you wish to share this information. This helps us tailor your experience.
* **Address**: Enter your address as it appears on your identification documents.
* **City**: Enter your city of residence. This is a required field.
* **State**: Choose your state of residence. This is a required field.
* **Country**: You do not need to select a country for this form.
* **Delivery Method**: Choose how you would like to receive the Patient Starter Kit. You can choose from:
	+ Home
	+ Clinic
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Delivery Time**: You do not need to select a delivery time for this form.
* **Delivery Date**: You do not need to select a delivery date for this form.
* **Delivery Location**: Indicate if you prefer delivery at your home or not.
* **Delivery Note**: If you selected "Other" for delivery method, provide a brief explanation.
* **Patient Preferences**: Enter any preferences you may have, such as language or accessibility needs.

Note: Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date. If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us.
