# Monthly Healing Support Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather user details, contact preferences, support preferences, and consent for monthly healing support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user details in the **User Details** field.
2. Select your contact preferences in the **Contact Preferences** field.
3. Choose your support preferences in the **Support Preferences** field.
4. Provide consent in the **Consent** field by typing your response.
5. Review your answers carefully and click the **Submit** button to submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details** (Text, Optional): Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant details you'd like to share with our support team.
* **Contact Preferences** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose how you'd like to be contacted for support, such as phone, email, or in-person.
* **Support Preferences** (Select One, Optional): Select the type of support you're requesting, such as counseling or coaching.
* **Consent** (Note, Optional): Check the box to indicate your consent for healing support. Please be aware that this is a required field for support to proceed.
* **Submit** (Text, Required): Enter a brief description of your healing support request. This field is required to submit the form.
