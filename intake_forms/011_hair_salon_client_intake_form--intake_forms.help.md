# Hair Salon Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a crucial part of the booking process for new clients. It helps the salon staff gather essential information about the client, their appointment schedule, and their payment preferences. Completing this form ensures a smooth and enjoyable experience for both the client and the salon staff.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and contact information (Client Name, Contact Number, Email).
2. Select a date and time for your appointment (Appointment Date, Appointment Time).
3. Choose the services you would like to receive during your visit (What services do you need?).
4. Indicate your preferred payment method (Payment Method).
5. If there are any special instructions or requests, please provide in the "Special Instructions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Client**: Enter your name to identify yourself as a new client. (text)
* **Client Name**: Enter your full name (first and last) as it will be used by the salon staff for future interactions. (text, required)
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number so the salon staff can contact you for any updates or confirmations. (text, required)
* **Email**: Enter your email address so the salon staff can send you reminders and updates about your appointment. (email, required)
* **Appointment Date**: Select a date for your appointment from the calendar provided. (date, required)
* **Appointment Time**: Select a time for your appointment from the provided options. (time, required)
* **What services do you need?**: Choose the services you would like to receive during your visit. You can select multiple services if needed. (select_multiple, required)
* **Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method for the services. (select_one, required)
* **Special Instructions**: If there's anything specific you'd like to inform the salon staff about your visit, please provide in this field. (note, optional)

## Tips
* Double-check that all required fields are filled in accurately before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the salon staff for assistance.
* Please be aware that submitting this form will not confirm your appointment. You will need to follow up with the salon to confirm your visit.
