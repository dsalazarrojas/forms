# Leak Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leak Intake Form is used to collect information about a leak that has been observed or reported. This form is intended to be completed by individuals who have witnessed or reported a leak and need to report it to the assigned team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Leak Report" field with a brief description of the leak.
2. Select the type of leak from the "Leakt Type" field.
3. If applicable, indicate the size of the leak in the "pipe_size" field.
4. Identify the location of the leak in the "pipe_location" field.
5. If you have taken a photo of the leak, add it in the "Photo" field.
6. Add any additional notes about the leak in the "Notes" field.
7. Provide your contact information, including your email and phone number, in the "Email" and "Phone" fields, respectively.
8. Indicate whether the leak is assigned to a specific team in the "Assigned Team" field.
9. Set a follow-up date and time in the "Follow-up" and "Follow-up Time" fields, respectively.
10. Finally, confirm the date and time of submission in the "Submitted At" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Leak Report** (Leak Report, text, required/optional): This field is where you should provide a brief description of the leak.
* **Leakt Type** (Leak Type, select_one, optional): Choose the type of leak from the provided options.
* **pipe_size** (pipe_size, number, optional): If you know the size of the pipe, you can indicate it here. However, if you are not sure, you can leave this field blank.
* **pipe_location** (pipe Location, select_multiple, optional): Select where the leak is located: Front, Back, Both, or Other.
* **Photo** (Photo, text, optional): If you have taken a photo of the leak, add it here.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Any additional information about the leak can be added in this field.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Provide your email address so that the assigned team can contact you.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Add your phone number so that the assigned team can contact you if needed.
* **Assigned Team** (Assigned Team, select_one, required): Check "Yes" if the leak is assigned to a team.
* **Follow-up** (Follow-up, date, optional): Set a date for the follow-up.
* **Follow-up Time** (Follow-up Time, time, optional): Set a time for the follow-up.
* **Submitted At** (Submitted At, date, optional): Confirm the date and time of submission.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when describing the leak in the "Leak Report" field.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can always leave them blank.
* If you have any additional information or clarification about the leak, add it in the "Notes" field.
* Ensure that the "Assigned Team" field is checked if the leak is assigned to a specific team.
* Use 24-hour clock for the follow-up time in the "Follow-up Time" field.
