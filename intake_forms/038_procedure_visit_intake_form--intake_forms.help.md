# Procedure Visit Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Procedure Visit Intake Form is used to collect information about patient visits to a medical practice. This form helps in organizing and processing visit details for the practice.

## How To Complete This Form

- To complete the form, fill out the required information for each field and submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (Patient Name): Enter the name of the patient, including their first name, last name, and any middle name if applicable.
* **Patient Email** (Patient Email): Enter the patient's email address. If the patient does not have an email address, you can leave this field blank.
* **Patient Phone** (Patient Phone): Enter the patient's phone number, including any area code or country code as necessary. If the patient does not have a phone number, you can leave this field blank.
* **Medical Practice** (Medical Practice): Enter the name of the medical practice where the patient is visiting.
* **Procedure Name** (Procedure Name): Enter the name of the procedure or treatment the patient is undergoing or receiving.
* **Date of Visit** (Date of Visit): Enter the date of the patient's visit using the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Time of Visit** (Time of Visit): Enter the time of the patient's visit using the standard time format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Medical Practice Location** (Medical Practice Location): Enter the location of the medical practice where the patient is visiting.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth): Enter the patient's date of birth using the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Medical Practice ID** (Medical Practice ID): Enter the unique ID of the medical practice where the patient is visiting.
* **Medical Practice Address** (Medical Practice Address): Enter the address of the medical practice where the patient is visiting.

## Tips

- Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely for a successful intake process.
- If a patient's information has already been entered, please do not create a duplicate entry.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the practice management team for assistance.
- This form is designed for one-time entry of patient visit information and is not intended for continuous updates or edits.
