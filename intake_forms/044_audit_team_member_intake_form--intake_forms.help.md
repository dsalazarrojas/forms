# Audit Team Member Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from potential candidates who wish to join the audit team. It is essential that you fill out this form accurately and truthfully to ensure that we can review your application efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section of the form carefully, ensuring that you provide accurate and complete information.
2. Review each field for its relevance and importance.
3. Make sure to select the correct options for each field, especially for dropdown fields.
4. Sign the form with your electronic signature at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name**: Please enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your personal email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Current Department**: Please enter your current department.
* **Current Position**: Enter your current job title.
* **Application Date**: Enter the date you are applying for the position.
* **Desired Role**: Select the role you are interested in applying for from the given options:
	+ Staff Auditor
	+ Senior Auditor
	+ Audit Manager
	+ IT Auditor
	+ Compliance Auditor
	+ Forensic Auditor
	+ Other
* **Availability Start**: Enter the earliest date you can start working.
* **Employment Type**: Select your preferred employment type:
	+ Full-Time
	+ Part-Time
	+ Contract
	+ Temporary
	+ Internship
* **Education Level**: Select your highest level of education:
	+ High School
	+ Associate Degree
	+ Bachelor Degree
	+ Master Degree
	+ Doctorate
	+ Professional Certification
* **Field of Study**: Enter your field of study.
* **Years of Experience**: Select the total years of relevant experience:
	+ Less than 1 year
	+ 1-3 years
	+ 4-6 years
	+ 7-10 years
	+ Over 10 years
* **Audit Experience**: Briefly describe your audit experience.
* **Certifications Held**: Select all certifications you hold from the given options:
	+ CPA
	+ CIA
	+ CISA
	+ CFE
	+ ACCA
	+ Other
	+ None
* **Technical Skills**: Select all technical skills you possess:
	+ Data analysis
	+ Audit software
	+ Excel advanced
	+ SQL
	+ Python
	+ ACL
	+ IDEA
* **Industry Knowledge**: Select all industries you have experience in:
	+ Financial services
	+ Healthcare
	+ Manufacturing
	+ Technology
	+ Retail
	+ Government
	+ Non-profit
	+ Other
* **Travel Availability**: Select your travel frequency:
	+ Yes, frequent travel
	+ Yes, occasional travel
	+ Yes, limited travel
	+ No, local only
* **Why Audit Team**: Please explain why you wish to join the audit team.
* **Strengths**: List your key strengths.
* **Development Goals**: If applicable, describe your development goals.
* **References Available**: Select if you have professional references:
	+ Yes, will provide
	+ Yes, attached
	+ False
* **Applicant Signature**: Type your full name as electronic signature.
