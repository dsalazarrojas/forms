# ligament_release_surgery_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the intake information for a patient's ligament release surgery. It helps to ensure that all relevant details are captured and that the patient has provided their informed consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

- Enter the patient's name in the "patient_name" field.
- Enter the patient's email address in the "patient_email" field (if available).
- Enter the patient's phone number in the "patient_phone" field (if available).
- In the "medical_history" field, provide a brief summary of the patient's medical history, including any relevant medical conditions, allergies, or medications.
- Enter the name of the surgeon performing the surgery in the "surgeon_name" field.
- Enter the date of the surgery in the "surgical_date" field (in MM/DD/YY format).
- Enter the time of the surgery in the "surgical_time" field.
- Check the "consent" field to confirm that the patient has provided their informed consent.
- Enter any pre-surgical instructions or guidance provided to the patient in the "pre_surgical_instructions" field.
- Enter any post-surgical instructions or guidance provided to the patient in the "post_surgical_instructions" field.
- In the "follow_up_care" field, describe any follow-up care instructions or appointments the patient needs to schedule.
- Indicate whether the patient has agreed to the ligament release in the "ligament_release" field (yes/no).
- Enter the date the patient signed the consent form in the "consent_date" field (in MM/DD/YY format).
- Check the "patient_signature" field to confirm that the patient has signed the consent form (yes/no).
- Check the "surgeon_signature" field to confirm that the surgeon has signed the consent form (yes/no).
- Enter the date the patient is available to be seen in the "patient_date" field (in MM/DD/YY format).
- In the "pre_operating_room_checklist" field, provide a list of items that the patient needs to do or bring with them before surgery.
- In the "intraoperative_complications" field, describe any complications that may have occurred during the surgery.
- In the "post_operating_room_checklist" field, provide a list of items that the patient needs to do or bring with them after surgery.
- In the "post_operating_room_comments" field, describe any post-operative comments or concerns.
- Indicate whether the patient will receive anesthesia during the surgery in the "anesthesia_type" field (yes/no).
- Enter the dose of anesthesia to be used in the "anesthesia_dose" field.
- In the "pain_management" field, describe any pain management strategies or medications that will be used.
- In the "post" field, provide any additional post-operative comments or instructions.

- Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- If a field has an "optional" label, it is not required but may be necessary for certain procedures.

- If a field has a "date" or "time" label, use the MM/DD/YY or MM:HH:MM format, respectively.
