# Wills Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wills Information Collection Form is a crucial document used to gather information about a client's wills-related details. This form is essential in ensuring that the client's wishes are accurately reflected in the will, and that the legal process is completed efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Choose the client's relationship status from the options provided (Married, Single, Divorced, or Widowed).
3. Select the client's marital status from the options provided (Married, Single, Divorced, or Partnered).
4. Enter the client's address in the "Address" field.
5. Provide a contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
6. Enter any additional instructions or information regarding the will in the "Instructions" field.
7. Confirm the instructions provided in the "Confirm Instructions" field.
8. Confirm the client's name in the "Confirm Client Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): The client's name is the name of the person who is creating the will.
* **Relationship Status** (`relationship_status`, `select_one`, required): This field determines the client's relationship status, such as being married, single, divorced, or widowed.
* **Marital Status** (`marital_status`, `select_one`, required): This field determines the client's marital status, such as being married, single, divorced, or partnered.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): The client's address is the physical location where the will can be served or delivered.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): This field should be filled in with the client's contact number for any additional information or communication.
* **Instructions** (`instructions`, `text`, required): This field is for any additional information or instructions the client would like to include in the will.
* **Confirm Instructions** (`confirm_instructions`, `text`, required): Confirm the instructions provided in the "Instructions" field.
* **Confirm Client Name** (`confirm_client_name`, `text`, required): Confirm the client's name as it is entered in the "Client Name" field.
