# Counseling Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from patients for the purpose of providing counseling services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email and Phone number for contact purposes.
3. Provide your Birth Date, Birth Time, and Medical History in the specified fields.
4. If you have any Medical History, Medication, Allergies, or Medical Condition, please describe them in the respective fields.
5. If you have a Family Doctor, enter their name and contact information.
6. If you have Medical Insurance, select your type of insurance from the options provided.
7. If you have referred to a Doctor in the past, select their name from the options provided.
8. If you have a Medical Group, select your group type from the options provided.
9. If you have any Doctor Specialties, select their specialty from the options provided.
10. If you have any Doctor Degrees or Years of Experience, select their relevant information from the options provided.
11. Finally, if you have any Doctor Notes or Languages spoken, enter them in the respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Birth Date**: Enter your date of birth in the format dd-mm-yyyy (e.g., 01-01-1990).
* **Birth Time**: Enter your time of birth in 12-hour format (e.g., 12:00 PM).
* **Medical History**: Describe any previous medical conditions or procedures you have had.
* **Medication**: Describe any medication you are currently taking.
* **Allergies**: Describe any allergies you have.
* **Medical Condition**: Describe any current medical conditions you are experiencing.
* **Family Doctor**: Enter the name and contact information of your family doctor.
* **Family Doctor Phone**: Enter your family doctor's phone number.
* **Medical Insurance**: Select your type of medical insurance.
* **Medical Insurance Provider**: Enter your medical insurance provider's name.
* **Medical Insurance Number**: Enter your medical insurance number.
* **Doctor Referrals**: Select the name of any doctor you have referred to in the past.
* **Medical Group**: Select your medical group type.
* **Medical Group Type**: Enter your medical group type (e.g., name and address).
* **Doctor Specialties**: Select your doctor's specialty.
* **Doctor Degree**: Select your doctor's degree.
* **Doctor Years of Experience**: Enter your doctor's years of experience.
* **Doctor Languages**: Enter any languages your doctor speaks.
* **Doctor Notes**: Enter any additional notes about your doctor.
* **Doctor Notes**: Enter any additional notes about your doctor.
