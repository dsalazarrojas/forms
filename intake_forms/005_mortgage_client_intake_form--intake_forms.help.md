# Mortgage Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mortgage Client Intake Form is designed to collect necessary information from clients seeking a mortgage loan. This information includes financial data, loan requirements, and other relevant factors to assess their creditworthiness for a mortgage loan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
*   Enter the client's contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
*   Enter the client's email address in the "Email" field.
*   Enter the client's address in the "Address" field.
*   Enter the loan amount in the "Loan Amount" field.
*   Select the loan term from the dropdown menu in the "Loan Term" field.
*   Select the type of property the client is interested in purchasing or refinancing from the dropdown menu in the "Property Type" field.
*   Enter the value of the property in the "Property Value" field.
*   Enter the client's statement in the "Client Statement" field (optional).
*   Select the type of loan the client is interested in from the dropdown menu in the "Loan Type" field.
*   Enter the length of the loan term in the "Loan Term Length" field.
*   Enter the interest rate in the "Interest Rate" field (optional).
*   Select the amortization schedule from the dropdown menu in the "Amortization Schedule" field.
*   Select the payment frequency from the dropdown menu in the "Payment Frequency" field.
*   Select the type of collateral the client is using as security for the loan from the dropdown menu in the "Collateral Type" field.
*   Enter the value of the collateral in the "Collateral Value" field.
*   Enter the client's employment status in the "Employment Status" field.
*   Select the source of the client's income from the dropdown menu in the "Income Source" field.
*   Enter the client's annual income in the "Annual Income" field.
*   Select the client's financial history from the dropdown menu in the "Financial History" field.
*   Enter the client's credit score in the "Credit Score" field.
*   Enter the debt-to-income ratio in the "Debt-to-Income Ratio" field.
*   Select the credit bureau the client uses from the dropdown menu in the "Credit Bureau" field.
*   Enter the client's credit age in the "Credit Age" field.
*   Select if the client has provided credit references from the dropdown menu in the "Credit References" field.
*   Select if the client has provided financial statements from the dropdown menu in the "Financial Statements" field.
*   Select if the client has a credit agreement from the dropdown menu in the "Credit Agreement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Name** (Client Name, Text, Optional): This is the field where the client enters their name. Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Contact Number** (Contact Number, Text, Optional): This field is used to collect the client's contact number. Please enter your phone number to ensure we can reach you for any follow-up questions or loan updates.
*   **Email** (Email, Email, Optional): This field is used to collect the client's email address. Please enter your email address to receive updates and notifications about your loan status.
*   **Address** (Address, Note, Optional): This field is used to collect the client's address. Please enter your current address to ensure we can mail any loan documents or correspondence.
*   **Loan Amount** (Loan Amount, Number, Optional): This field is used to collect the loan amount the client is interested in. Please enter the exact amount you are interested in borrowing.
*   **Loan Term** (Loan Term, Select One, Required): This field is used to select the length of the loan term. Please choose from the options 6, 12, 24, or 48 months.
*   **Property Type** (Property Type, Select One, Required): This field is used to select the type of property the client is interested in purchasing or refinancing. Please choose from the options Single Family, Multi Family, Condo, or Townhouse.
*   **Property Value** (Property Value, Number, Required): This field is used to enter the value of the property. Please enter the exact value of the property.
*   **Client Statement** (Client Statement, Note, Optional): This is a field for the client to enter any additional information or comments about their loan requirements. Please feel free to include any relevant details about your financial situation.
*   **Loan Type** (Loan Type, Select One, Required): This field is used to select the type of loan the client is interested in. Please choose from the options Fixed Rate, Variable Rate, or Balloon.
*   **Loan Term Length** (Loan Term Length, Number, Required): This field is used to enter the length of the loan term. Please enter the exact number of months.
*   **Interest Rate** (Interest Rate, Number, Optional): This field is used to enter the interest rate. Please enter the exact interest rate.
*   **Amortization Schedule** (Amortization Schedule, Select One, Required): This field is used to select the amortization schedule. Please choose from the options Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly.
*   **Payment Frequency** (Payment Frequency, Select One, Required): This field is used to select the payment frequency. Please choose from the options Monthly, Bi-Monthly, or Quarterly.
*   **Collateral Type** (Collateral Type, Select One, Required): This field is used to select the type of collateral the client is using as security for the loan. Please choose from the options Cash, Asset, or Other.
*   **Collateral Value** (Collateral Value, Number, Required): This field is used to enter the value of the collateral. Please enter the exact value of the collateral.
*   **Loan Status** (Loan Status, Note, Optional): This field is used to provide additional information about the loan status. Please feel free to include any relevant details.
*   **Employment Status** (Employment Status, Select One, Required): This field is used to select the client's employment status. Please choose from the options Employed, Self-Employed, or Un-Employed.
*   **Income Source** (Income Source, Select One, Required): This field is used to select the source of the client's income. Please choose from the options Salary, Commission, or Bonus.
*   **Annual Income** (Annual Income, Number, Required): This field is used to enter the client's annual income. Please enter the exact annual income.
*   **Financial History** (Financial History, Select One, Required): This field is used to select the client's financial history. Please choose from the options Good, Fair, or Poor.
*   **Credit Score** (Credit Score, Number, Required): This field is used to enter the client's credit score. Please enter the exact credit score.
*   **Debt-to-Income Ratio** (Debt-to-Income Ratio, Number, Required): This field is used to enter the debt-to-income ratio. Please enter the exact debt-to-income ratio.
*   **Credit Bureau** (Credit Bureau, Select One, Required): This field is used to select the credit bureau the client uses. Please choose from the options Equifax, TransUnion, or Experian.
*   **Credit Age** (Credit Age, Number, Required): This field is used to enter the client's credit age. Please enter the exact credit age.
*   **Credit References** (Credit References, Select One, Required): This field is used to select if the client has provided credit references. Please choose from the options Yes or No.
*   **Financial Statements** (Financial Statements, Select One, Required): This field is used to select if the client has provided financial statements. Please choose from the options Yes or No.
*   **Credit Agreement** (Credit Agreement, Select One, Required): This field is used to select if the client has a credit agreement. Please choose from the options Yes or No.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely to avoid any delays or issues with your loan application.
*   If you are unsure about any field or have questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to our team for assistance.
*   We will review your application carefully and contact you with any additional questions or concerns we may have.
