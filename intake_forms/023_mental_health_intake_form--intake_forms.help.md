# Mental Health Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients for mental health services.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
2.  Provide a brief medical history for the last 30 days.
3.  Select any relevant supporting documents, such as ID or insurance cards.
4.  Enter the payment fee for services.
5.  Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
6.  If applicable, identify your emergency contact and their relation to you.
7.  Enter your date of birth.
8.  Choose your relation to the patient (e.g., spouse, child, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Patient Name** (Patient Name): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Medical History** (Medical History): Describe your medical history for the last 30 days.
*   **Supporting Documents** (Supporting Documents): Select any relevant supporting documents, such as ID or insurance cards.
*   **Fee Payment** (Fee Payment): Enter the payment fee for services.
*   **Contact Info** (Contact Info): Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
*   **Emergency Contact** (Emergency Contact): Identify your emergency contact, if applicable.
*   **HIPAA Consent** (HIPAA Consent): Sign the HIPAA consent form to allow us to access your medical information.
*   **Insurance Provider** (Insurance Provider): Enter your medical insurance provider, if applicable.
*   **Address** (Address): Enter your current address.
*   **Date Of Birth** (Date Of Birth): Enter your date of birth.
*   **Relation To Patient** (Relation To Patient): Choose the relation between you and the patient (e.g., spouse, child, etc.).
*   **Medical Insurance** (Medical Insurance): Enter your medical insurance information, if applicable.
*   **Medical Condition** (Medical Condition): Describe any medical conditions you may have.
*   **Medication List** (Medication List): List any medications you are currently taking.
*   **Allergies** (Allergies): Describe any allergies you have.
*   **Medical Device** (Medical Device): Specify any medical devices you use.
*   **Other Medical Info** (Other Medical Info): Provide any other relevant medical information not mentioned above.
*   **Medical History Info** (Medical History Info): If required, provide more detailed information about your medical history.

## Tips
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
*   Provide accurate and honest information to ensure the best possible treatment.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
