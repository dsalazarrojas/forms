# Home Health Intake Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Health Intake Form Template is a tool used to gather essential information from patients in the home health care setting. It helps healthcare providers collect and document crucial patient data, ensuring accurate and comprehensive care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Patient Information (Page 1)**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information.
2. **Home Health Information (Page 1)**: Describe the patient's home health situation, including any concerns or challenges.
3. **Medical History (Page 1)**: Record the patient's medical history, including previous surgeries, allergies, or medical conditions.
4. **Medications (Page 1)**: Select all medications the patient is currently taking.
5. **Patient Goals (Page 1)**: List the patient's goals and objectives for their care.
6. **Patient Status (Page 1)**: Select the patient's current status, such as "Homebound" or "Hospitalized".
7. **Care Team (Page 1)**: Select all members of the patient's care team, including nurses, therapists, or other healthcare providers.
8. **Discharge Diversions (Page 2)**: Describe any discharge instructions or diversions.
9. **Patient Discharge (Page 2)**: Enter the date of the patient's discharge or planned discharge date.
10. **Discharge Status (Page 2)**: Select the patient's discharge status.
11. **Care Instructions (Page 2)**: Provide any additional care instructions for the patient.
