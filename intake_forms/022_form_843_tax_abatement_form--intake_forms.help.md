# Form 843 Tax Abatement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Form 843 Tax Abatement Form is used to document tax abatement claims submitted by claimants and taxpayers. This form captures relevant information about the claimant and taxpayer, including their contact details, the amount and date of the claim, and the reason for the claim. The form helps ensure that all required information is collected and processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Claimant Information** section:
	* Enter the claimant's name.
	* Enter the claimant's phone number (optional).
	* Enter the claimant's email address (optional).
	* Enter the claimant's address.
	* Enter the claimant's city.
	* Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the claimant is a resident of the state.
	* Enter the claimant's zip code (optional).
2. Fill out the **Tax Claim Details** section:
	* Enter the amount of tax claimed by the claimant.
	* Select a date for the tax claim.
	* Enter the reason for the tax claim (optional).
3. Fill out the **Taxpayer Information** section:
	* Enter the taxpayer's name.
	* Enter the taxpayer's phone number (optional).
	* Enter the taxpayer's email address (optional).
	* Enter the taxpayer's address.
	* Enter the taxpayer's city.
	* Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the taxpayer is a resident of the state.
	* Enter the taxpayer's zip code (optional).
	* Enter the amount of tax claimed by the taxpayer.
	* Select a date for the tax claim.
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Claimant Information
* **Claimant**: Enter the claimant's name.
* **Claimant Phone**: Enter the claimant's phone number (optional).
* **Claimant Email**: Enter the claimant's email address (optional).
* **Claimant Address**: Enter the claimant's address.
* **Claimant City**: Enter the claimant's city.
* **Claimant State**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the claimant is a resident of the state.
* **Claimant Zip**: Enter the claimant's zip code (optional).
* **Claimant Amount**: Enter the amount of tax claimed by the claimant.
* **Claimant Date**: Select a date for the tax claim.
* **Claimant Reason**: Enter the reason for the tax claim (optional).
* **Claimant Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the claimant (optional).
* **Taxpayer**: Enter the taxpayer's name.
* **Taxpayer Phone**: Enter the taxpayer's phone number (optional).
* **Taxpayer Email**: Enter the taxpayer's email address (optional).
* **Taxpayer Address**: Enter the taxpayer's address.
* **Taxpayer City**: Enter the taxpayer's city.
* **Taxpayer State**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the taxpayer is a resident of the state.
* **Taxpayer Zip**: Enter the taxpayer's zip code (optional).
* **Taxpayer Amount**: Enter the amount of tax claimed by the taxpayer.
* **Taxpayer Date**: Select a date for the tax claim.
* **Taxpayer Reason**: Enter the reason for the tax claim (optional).
* **Taxpayer Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the taxpayer (optional).
* **Tax Type**: Enter the type of tax (optional).
* **Submit**: Click to submit the form.

### Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out correctly before submitting the form.
* Double-check the claimant and taxpayer information for accuracy.
* Enter dates in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* If you have any issues or questions while completing the form, contact your tax authority for assistance.
