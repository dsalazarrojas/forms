# Lost & Found Dog Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us gather information about a lost dog, its owner, and any relevant details that might aid in the dog's recovery. Please provide as much detail as possible to increase the chances of reuniting the dog with its owner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the form fields with the relevant information about the lost dog and its owner. You can refer to the field-by-field explanations below for more details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Owner Information** (Page 1, **owner_info**, required): Enter the name, address, email, and phone number of the dog's owner. Please make sure to include any relevant contact information.
* **Pet Information** (Page 1, **pet_info, required): Enter a brief description of the pet (dog) that has gone missing.
* **Lost Location** (Page 1, **lost_location, required): Describe the location where the pet was last seen.
* **Lost Date** (Page 1, **lost_date, required): Enter the date when the pet went missing.
* **Dog Type** (Page 1, **dog_type, optional): Select the type of pet (dog or cat), or choose "Other" if it's neither.
* **Microchip Info** (Page 1, **microchip_info, optional): If the pet has a microchip, provide any relevant information about it.
* **Collar Color** (Page 1, **collar_color, optional): Describe the color of the pet's collar.
* **Collar Size** (Page 1, **collar_size, optional): Select the size of the pet's collar.
* **Microchip Number** (Page 1, **microchip_number, optional): If the pet has a microchip, enter its number.
* **Owner Contact** (Page 1, **owner_contact, optional): Select how the owner would like to be contacted (email or phone).
* **Owner Name** (Page 1, **owner_name, required): Enter the owner's name.
* **Owner Address** (Page 1, **owner_address, optional): Enter the owner's address.
* **Owner Phone** (Page 1, **owner_phone, optional): Enter the owner's phone number.
* **Owner Email** (Page 1, **owner_email, optional): Enter the owner's email address.
* **Dog Breed** (Page 1, **dog_breed, optional): If the pet has a breed, enter it here.
* **Dog Weight** (Page 1, **dog_weight, optional): If known, enter the pet's weight.
* **Dog Color** (Page 1, **dog_color, optional): Describe the color of the pet.
* **Dog Description** (Page 1, **dog_description, optional): Enter a brief description of the pet.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form as thoroughly as possible to increase the chances of reuniting the dog with its owner.
* If you're unsure about any information, please leave it blank and we'll do our best to help.
* You can also attach any supporting documents, such as photos or vet records, to the form if you wish.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us at your convenience.
