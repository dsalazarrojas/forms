# tanf_information_disclosure_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The tanf_information_disclosure_form is designed to collect and record essential information for TANF programs. It serves as a crucial tool for tracking client and provider data, ensuring accurate and efficient program management.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out this form completely and accurately to avoid any potential issues with the TANF program.
- Review each field carefully to understand its purpose and relevance to the TANF program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (`client_information`, `text`, optional): Please provide the client's basic information. This field is optional but helps in identifying the client.
* **Authorization** (`authorization`, `text`, optional): This field is for the social services provider's name and information. It is optional but recommended for record-keeping.
* **Disclosure Date** (`disclosure_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the disclosure or the date this form was filled out. This helps track the timing of client and provider interactions.
* **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, `date`, optional): Record the date the client's authorization expires. This field is also optional but essential for tracking client authorization status.
* **Social Services Provider** (`social_services_provider`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether the social services are provided by an 'Agency', 'Organization', or an 'Individual'. This choice indicates who the social services are being provided by.
* **Client Consent** (`client_consent`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select 'True' if the client has given consent, 'False' if they have not, or 'Other' for any other reason. This field indicates the client's consent status.
* **Authorization List** (`authorization_list`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select 'True' if the client has authorized you, 'False' if they have not, or 'Other' for any other reason. This field is related to client authorization.
* **TANF Information** (`tanf_information`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional TANF-related information not covered in other fields. This field is optional and meant for extra details.
* **TANF Program Name** (`tanf_program_name`, `text`, optional): Provide the name of the TANF program being applied for or participated in. This field is also optional but helpful for tracking program involvement.
* **Authorization Name** (`authorization_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person authorizing on behalf of the client. This field is optional but recommended for identification purposes.
* **Authorization Address** (`authorization_address`, `text`, optional): Record the address of the person authorizing on behalf of the client. This field is optional but recommended for contact and identification purposes.

## Tips
- Always review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all fields are filled out correctly and completely.
- If a field is marked as 'required' but you are unsure about what to fill, refer to the provided instructions or contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
- Accuracy and completeness are crucial in filling out this form, so take your time and review each field carefully.
