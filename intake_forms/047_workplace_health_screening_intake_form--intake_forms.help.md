# Workplace Health Screening Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from employees for workplace health screening purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address.
2. Provide your Date of Birth.
3. Answer questions about your medical history, including any medical conditions, health concerns, and allergies.
4. Report any medications you are currently taking.
5. List any medical procedures you have undergone.
6. Provide information about your medical history in your family and at the workplace.
7. Describe any work-related exposures or hazards you are concerned about.
8. Share your medical screening questionnaire and results.
9. Discuss next steps and follow-up actions.
10. Sign and acknowledge the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (2, text): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or government-issued ID.
* **Email** (3, email): Enter your work email address.
* **Date of Birth** (4, date): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Workplace Location** (5, text): Enter the name of your workplace or department.
* **Medical Condition** (6, text): List any medical conditions you have or are concerned about.
* **Health Concerns** (7, text): Describe any health concerns you have.
* **Emergency Contact** (8, text): Enter the name and phone number of a contact to reach in case of an emergency.
* **Medical History** (9, text): Describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses or treatments.
* **Health Risk Factors** (10, text): List any factors that may affect your health at the workplace.
* **Allergies** (11, text): List any allergies you have.
* **Medications** (12, text): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical Procedures** (13, text): Describe any medical procedures you have undergone.
* **Medical History Family** (14, text): Describe your family medical history.
* **Medical History Workplace** (15, text): Describe your workplace medical history.
* **Work Exposures** (16, text): List any work-related exposures or hazards you are concerned about.
* **Work Exposures Description** (17, text): Describe any work exposures or hazards.
* **Medical History Illness** (18, text): Describe any recent or recurring illnesses.
* **Health Screening Questionnaire** (19, text): Share the results of any health screening questionnaires.
* **Medical Screening Questionnaire** (20, text): Share the results of any medical screening questionnaires.
* **Health Screening Results** (21, text): Describe the results of your health screening.
* **Next Steps** (22, text): Discuss any next steps or follow-up actions.
* **Medical Screening Follow-up** (23, text): Describe any follow-up actions or next steps.
* **Medical Screening Follow-up Description** (24, text): Describe the purpose or goal of the medical screening follow-up.
* **Signed** (25, select_one): Sign and acknowledge this form by selecting "Employee Acknowledgement" or "Employee Decline."
