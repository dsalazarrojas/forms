# Placenta Encapsulation Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive intake form for placenta encapsulation clients, aiming to gather essential information for providing tailored services.

## How To Complete This Form
Complete this form by filling out each page and field according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**
	+ <label>General Information</label> (<type>text</type>, <required>false>): This page collects general information about you and your pregnancy or postpartum experience. Please provide any relevant details about your pregnancy, birth, and postpartum journey.
* **Placenta Information**
	+ <label>Placenta Information</label> (<type>text</type>, <required>false>): This field is for any relevant information about your placenta, such as its size or any concerns you have about it.
* **Medical History**
	+ <label>Medical History</label> (<type>text</type, <required>false>): Please provide any relevant medical information about yourself or your baby, such as allergies or conditions that may affect our services.
* **Contact Information**
	+ <label>Contact Information</label> (<type>text</type, <required>false>): Please provide your contact information, including your email address and phone number, so we can communicate effectively.
* **Birth Information**
	+ <label>Birth Information</label> (<type>text</type, <required>false>): This field is for any relevant information about your baby's birth, such as the date and time of birth or any complications during delivery.
* **Services**
	+ <label>Services</label> (<type>text</type, <required>false>): Please specify the services you are interested in, such as encapsulation or other related services.
* **Medical Clearance**
	+ <label>Medical Clearance</label> (<type>text</type, <required>false>): If you have any medical clearances or certificates, please attach them or provide relevant information.
* **Billing Information**
	+ <label>Billing Information</label> (<type>text</type, <required>false>): Please provide your billing information, including your address, to ensure we can process payments correctly.
* **Signature**
	+ <label>Signature</label> (<type>text</type, <required>false>): Please sign this form to acknowledge that you have read and understand the terms of our services.
* **Consent**
	+ <label>Consent</label> (<type>text</type, <required>false>): Please provide your consent for us to use the information you have provided for our services.
* **Other Inquiries**
	+ <label>Other Inquiries</label> (<type>text</type, <required>false>): If you have any other inquiries or requests, please provide them here.
