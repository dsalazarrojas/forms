# # surgical_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **surgical_intake_form** is a patient intake form used to gather necessary information for surgical procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information in the **Patient Information** page.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth in the **Date of Birth** field.
3. Provide the patient's contact information in the **Patient Contact** field.
4. Describe the patient's medical history in the **Medical History** field.
5. List any known allergies in the **Allergies** field.
6. List the patient's current medications in the **Medication List** field.
7. Select the medical procedure(s) the patient will be undergoing in the **Medical Procedure** field.
8. Select the patient's medical specialty(ies) in the **Medical Specialty** field.
9. Select the patient's medical condition(s) in the **Medical Condition** field.
10. Choose the surgical team member(s) assigned to the patient in the **Surgical Team** field.
11. List any additional surgery details in the **Surgery Details** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (<name>)** (`<name>`, text, required/optional): This section collects the patient's basic information.
* **Date of Birth (<label>)** (`<date>`, date, optional): Enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Patient Contact (<label>)** (`<text>`, text, optional): Enter the patient's contact information, including name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Medical History (<label>)** (`<text>`, text, optional): Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous medical conditions or treatments.
* **Allergies (<label>)** (`<select_multiple>`, select multiple, optional): Select any known allergies the patient has.
* **Medication List (<label>)** (`<select_multiple>`, select multiple, optional): List the patient's current medications.
* **Medical Procedure (<label>)** (`<select_one>`, select one, optional): Select the medical procedure(s) the patient will be undergoing.
* **Medical Specialty (<label>)** (`<select_one>`, select one, optional): Select the patient's medical specialty(ies).
* **Medical Condition (<label>)** (`<select_one>`, select one, optional): Select the patient's medical condition(s).
* **Surgical Team (<label>)** (`<select_one>`, select one, optional): Choose the surgical team member(s) assigned to the patient.
* **Surgery Details (<label>)** (`<select_multiple>`, select multiple, optional): List any additional surgery details.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure complete patient information.
* Double-check the patient's information for accuracy and completeness.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid errors.
