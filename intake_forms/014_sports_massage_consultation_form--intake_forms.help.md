# Sports Massages Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Massages Consultation Form is a crucial tool for us to understand your health status and medical history before we can provide you with the best possible massage experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the step-by-step guide below:

1.  Select the correct client type and status from the options provided in the New Client Form section.
2.  Fill out the Contact Information section with your contact details.
3.  Answer the Medical History Form questions to inform us about any medical conditions you may have.
4.  Select your current health status from the options provided in the Health Status Form section.
5.  Choose the correct medical condition(s) you are currently experiencing from the Medical Conditions Form section.
6.  Select the massage goals and frequencies you're interested in.
7.  Fill out the Medical History Form section to provide further information about your medical history.
8.  Enter the date of your next appointment in the Contact Form section.
9.  Choose the time of your next appointment in the Time Form section.
10.  Add any additional notes about your massage experience in the Note Form section.
11.  Enter your email address in the Email Form section.
12.  Enter your phone number in the Phone Number Form section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **New Client Form** (Client Type: Active, Inactive, Returning/Client Type: New, Existing)
    Select the correct client type that best describes your current status.
*   **Contact Information** (Required)
    Enter your current address, phone number, and any other contact details we need to know about you.
*   **Medical History Form** (Required)
    Inform us about any medical conditions you may have, such as pregnancy, allergies, or past injuries.
*   **Health Status** (Required)
    Choose your current health status from the options provided.
*   **Medical Condition** (Required)
    Select the medical condition(s) you are currently experiencing.
*   **Massage Goals** (Required)
    Choose the massage goals you're interested in, such as muscle relaxation or improving circulation.
*   **Medical Conditions** (Required)
    Select any medical conditions you may have that we should be aware of before providing a massage.
*   **Massage Frequencies** (Required)
    Choose the frequency of massage you'd like to receive.
*   **Medical History** (Required)
    Provide further information about your medical history.
*   **Contact Form** (Required)
    Enter the date of your next appointment.
*   **Time** (Required)
    Choose the time of your next appointment.
*   **Note** (Required)
    Add any additional notes about your massage experience.
*   **Email** (Required)
    Enter your email address so we can contact you about your appointment.
*   **Phone Number** (Required)
    Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your appointment.

## Tips

*   Please double-check all required fields before submitting the form.
*   If you have any doubts or concerns, don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
*   Our team will review your form and get back to you with any further questions or concerns.
