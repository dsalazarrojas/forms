# Recruiting Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recruiting Intake Form is designed to collect essential information from companies about their recruiting needs. This form is used to gather details about the company, job description, and candidate requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the Company Information section with details about your company.
2.  Move on to the Job Details section and provide information about the job you are looking to fill.
3.  In the Contact Information section, provide details about the person responsible for the recruiting process.
4.  Finally, add any additional comments or information that might be helpful.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Info** (`company_info`, Text, Optional): Enter your company's name, description, and location.
* **Job Details** (`job_details`, Text, Optional): Provide a brief description of the job, including job title, job type, and job category.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, Text, Optional): Enter the contact person's name, email, and phone number.
* **Candidate Info** (`candidate_info`, Text, Optional): Describe the candidate's background, experience, and qualifications.
* **Candidate Requirements** (`candidate_requirements`, Text, Optional): List the skills, qualifications, or requirements needed for the candidate.
* **Job Posting** (`job_posting`, Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the job posting.
* **Candidate Profile** (`candidate_profile`, Text, Optional): Describe the desired candidate profile and background.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, Text, Optional): Enter a detailed description of the job duties and responsibilities.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, Text, Optional): Enter a brief job title.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, Select, Optional): Select the type of job, which includes Senior Software Engineer, Junior Software Engineer, Senior Manager, Junior Manager, or Other.
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, Select, Optional): Select the category of the job, which includes Software, Hardware, or Other.
* **Job Tags** (`job_tags`, Text, Optional): Enter any relevant job tags or keywords.
* **Job Skills** (`job_skills`, Text, Optional): List the necessary job skills.
* **Job Requirements** (`job_requirements`, Text, Optional): Describe the job requirements and qualifications.
* **Candidate Requirements Note** (`candidate_requirements_note`, Text, Optional): Add any additional requirements for the candidate.
* **Recruiter Info** (`recruiter_info`, Text, Optional): Enter the recruiter's name, email, and phone number.
* **HR Manager** (`hr_manager`, Text, Optional): Enter the HR Manager's name, email, and phone number.
* **Hiring Manager** (`hiring_manager`, Text, Optional): Enter the hiring manager's name, email, and phone number.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, Text, Optional): Enter the contact person's name, email, and phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, Text, Optional): Enter the contact email.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter the contact phone number.

## Tips

* This form is for general information purposes only and does not guarantee any response or outcome.
* Please be as detailed as possible to ensure we can provide the best possible response.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
