# iv_therapy_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The iv_therapy_intake_form is a comprehensive form designed to collect patient information, medical history, and treatment details for intravenous therapy. It's used to create accurate patient profiles for healthcare professionals to provide personalized care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the medical history section (page 1) by providing a detailed account of your past medical experiences, including any allergies, medications, or previous medical conditions.
2. On page 2, enter your patient information, such as your name, contact details, and any relevant personal data.
3. On page 3, fill out the contact information section, which includes your phone number and email address.
4. On page 4, describe your treatment details, including your goals and any specific requirements or concerns.
5. On page 5, report any allergies or medical history conditions that may impact your treatment.
6. On page 6, select the treatment outcome you expect from the intravenous therapy (IV) treatment.
7. Finally, review your submission to ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): A detailed account of your past medical experiences, including any allergies, medications, or previous medical conditions.
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, required): Enter your name, contact details, and any relevant personal data.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Fill out your phone number and email address for communication purposes.
* **Treatment Details** (`treatment_details`, text, required): Describe your treatment goals and any specific requirements or concerns.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): Report any allergies or medical history conditions that may impact your treatment.
* **Medication History** (`medication_history`, text, optional): Report any medications you're currently taking or have taken in the past.
* **Medical History Condition** (`medical_history_condition`, text, required): A detailed account of any ongoing medical conditions.
* **Treatment Outcome** (`treatment_outcome`, select_one, required): Select the expected treatment outcome from the IV treatment (IV, Oral, or Other).
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, text, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* If you're unsure about any field, please consult with the healthcare team before proceeding.
* Please review your submission carefully before submitting it.
