# Shelter Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information from individuals seeking services from a shelter or organization. It helps our staff to efficiently assess your needs and provide the required support.

## How To Complete This Form
Please fill out the form completely and accurately. You are required to provide the following information:

- Enter the name of the shelter or organization you are seeking help from.
- Enter your name.
- Enter your email address.
- Enter the date and time you are requesting services.
- Enter your phone number.
- Enter your address.
- Select the type of service you are seeking.
- Provide a brief description of the services you need.
- Enter your date of birth.
- Enter your age.
- Select your gender.
- List any medical conditions you have.
- Enter any additional medical notes.
- Enter the name of your emergency contact.
- Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
- Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
- Enter any additional notes about your emergency contact.
- Select your current service status.
- Enter the date services started.
- Enter the date services ended.
- Enter the date you last made contact.
- Select how you last made contact (e.g. phone, in-person, email).
</pre>

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shelter Name**: Enter the name of the shelter or organization providing services.
* **Client Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates or information.
* **Date**: Enter the date you are requesting services.
* **Time**: Enter the time you are requesting services.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Address**: Enter your current address.
* **Service Type**: Select the type of service you are seeking (e.g. Shelter, Housing, Support, Other).
* **Service Description**: Provide a brief description of the services you need.
* **Client DOB**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Age**: Enter your age.
* **Gender**: Select your gender.
* **Client Race**: Select your racial or ethnic background.
* **Medical Condition**: List any medical conditions you have.
* **Medical Notes**: Enter any additional medical notes.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship**: Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Notes**: Enter any additional notes about your emergency contact.
* **Service Status**: Select your current service status.
* **Service Start Date**: Enter the date services started.
* **Service End Date**: Enter the date services ended.
* **Last Seen Date**: Enter the date you last made contact.
* **Last Contact Method**: Select how you last made contact (e.g. phone, in-person, email).

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and completely.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask for assistance.
- If you need help with filling out the form, please contact our staff for support.
