# health_insurance_client_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Insurance Client Intake Form is used to collect essential information from clients about their health insurance. This form helps our team gather the necessary details to provide the best possible service and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the following details:

-   Ensure you fill in all required fields (none are marked as required, but please ensure you fill in all fields for a complete submission).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Insurance Provider** (text): 
    Enter the insurance provider's name or ID, as provided by the policy holder.
- **Insurance Plan** (text): 
    Enter the name or ID of the insurance plan the client is enrolled in.
- **Policy Holder** (text): 
    Enter the policy holder's name and date of birth.
- **Client Details** (text): 
    Enter any additional information about the client, such as their address or contact number.
- **Contact Info** (text): 
    Enter the contact information (phone number, email, etc.) of the client.
- **Policy Application** (text): 
    Enter details about the policy application, such as the date submitted or application status.
- **Policy Documents** (text): 
    If applicable, upload or enter details about policy documents required for the application.
- **Policy Dropped** (number): 
    If the policy has been dropped, enter the reason and date of the drop.

## Tips
- Please ensure you fill in all fields for a complete submission.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact our support team.
- This form is for your information only; please do not share it with others without permission.
- The information collected through this form will be used to provide the best possible service and support.
