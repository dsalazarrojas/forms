# Diabetes Prevention Program Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients interested in participating in our Diabetes Prevention Program. The goal is to understand the patient's risk factors, medical history, and other relevant information to provide personalized support and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections below and answer the questions according to your personal experiences and medical history.
2. For select-type questions, choose the options that apply to you.
3. Ensure you answer all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is intended to collect basic information about yourself. Please provide your full name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false)
	+ In this field, please describe any medical conditions or treatments you are currently undergoing or have undergone in the past.
* **Medications** (`medications`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Check the options below to indicate if you are currently taking any medications. If yes, please specify what kind of medication and why you are taking it.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Provide your contact information, including phone number and email, so we can get in touch with you about your participation in the program.
* **Healthcare Providers** (`healthcare_providers`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Check the options below to indicate if you have a primary care physician or other healthcare providers who have knowledge about your medical history.
* **Health Insurance** (`health_insurance`, `text`, required: false)
	+ If you have health insurance, please provide the name of your insurance provider and policy number.
* **Diabetes Screening** (`diabetes_screening`, `text`, required: false)
	+ If you've undergone diabetes screening or know your diabetes status, describe the results.
* **Family History** (`family_history`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Check the options below to indicate if you have a family member with diabetes or a history of diabetes in your family.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Check the options below to indicate if you have a medical condition that may increase your risk of developing type 2 diabetes.
* **Risk Factors** (`risk_factors`, `text`, required: false)
	+ In this field, please list any risk factors you believe you have that may increase your risk of developing type 2 diabetes.
