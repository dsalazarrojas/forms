# dental_website_development_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from dental practices about their website development projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each field accurately and completely. Make sure to review the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, not required): This field is for your practice's contact information. Please fill out your name, address, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Project Scope** (`project_scope`, `text`, not required): This field is for describing the scope of your website development project in as much detail as possible.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, `number`, not required): Enter a value with decimal places for your project budget.
* **Expected Start Date** (`expected_start_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the expected start date of your project in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select the contact methods you prefer (Phone, Email, Mail).
* **Type of Practice** (`dental_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of practice (General Dentist, Specialist, Orthodontist, Pediatric, Periodontal, Other).
* **Practice Size** (`practice_size`, `text`, not required): Enter the number of employees in your practice.
* **Technology Stack** (`technology_stack`, `text`, not required): List your current technology stack (e.g., WordPress, React, etc.).
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, `text`, not required): Describe your target audience.
* **Website Features** (`website_features`, `text`, not required): Specify the features you would like on your website.
* **Website Launch Date** (`website_launch_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the expected launch date of your website in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the contact person for your practice.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your practice's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your practice's email address.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, not required): Enter your practice's address.
* **Website** (`website`, `text`, not required): Enter your current website URL.
* **Current Technology Stack** (`current_technology_stack`, `text`, not required): List your current technology stack (e.g., WordPress, React, etc.).
* **Current Platform** (`current_platform`, `text`, not required): Enter your current platform.
* **Current Content Management System** (`current_content_management_system`, `text`, not required): Enter your current content management system.
* **Current Website URL** (`current_website_url`, `text`, not required): Enter your current website URL.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required): Enter any additional notes or comments about your project.
* **Website Development Preferences** (`website_development_preferences`, `text`, not required): Specify your website development preferences.

Please make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
