# Body Contouring Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients about their body contouring goals, medical history, and preferences. The information gathered will help the medical professional understand the client's expectations, medical status, and preferred communication method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
6. Enter your current address in the "Address" field.
7. Enter your city, state, and zip in the "City - State - Zip" field.
8. Select all areas you are interested in for body contouring, such as face, neck, chin, arms, abdomen, waist, hips, thighs, buttocks, legs, and calves, in the "Which areas are you interested in" field.
9. Choose your current body type from the options: Apple, Pear, Hourglass, Rectangle, Inverted Triangle, or Other.
10. If you have had previous body contouring procedures, enter the details in the "Previous Body Contouring Procedures" field.
11. Select your satisfaction level with previous procedures from the options: Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Not Applicable.
12. If you are taking any medications, enter them in the "Current Medications" field.
13. If you have any medical conditions, enter them in the "Relevant Medical Conditions" field.
14. If you have allergies, enter them in the "Known Allergies" field.
15. If you have any skin conditions or sensitivities, enter them in the "Skin Conditions or Sensitivities" field.
16. If you are pregnant or nursing, select the corresponding option in the "Are you currently pregnant or nursing" field.
17. Select your smoking status from the options: Non-smoker, Former Smoker, Current Smoker, or Occasional Smoker.
18. Enter your current exercise routine in the "Current Exercise Routine" field.
19. Enter your dietary habits in the "Dietary Habits" field.
20. Select your realistic expectations about results from the options: True, Mostly, Somewhat, or False.
21. Select your expected timeline for results from the options: Immediate Results, 1-2 Weeks, 2-4 Weeks, 1-3 Months, More than 3 Months, or Unsure.
22. Enter your desired appointment date in the "Desired Appointment Date" field.
23. Select your preferred contact method from the options: Phone, Email, Text Message, or In Person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (Email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (Text): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (Date): Enter your date of birth.
* **Address** (Text): Enter your current address.
* **City - State - Zip** (Text): Enter your city, state, and zip code.
* **What are your body contouring goals** (Text): This field is used to understand your body contouring objectives. Please enter any specific areas you are interested in, such as face, neck, chin, arms, abdomen, waist, hips, thighs, buttocks, legs, and calves.
* **Which areas are you interested in** (Select Multiple): Select all areas you are interested in for body contouring. This field is for your convenience, and you can select multiple areas.
* **Current Body Type** (Select One): Choose your current body type from the options: Apple, Pear, Hourglass, Rectangle, Inverted Triangle, or Other.
* **Previous Body Contouring Procedures** (Text): If you have had previous body contouring procedures, enter the details in this field.
* **Satisfaction with Previous Procedures** (Select One): Select your satisfaction level with previous procedures from the options: Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Not Applicable.
* **Current Medications** (Text): If you are taking any medications, enter them in this field.
* **Relevant Medical Conditions** (Text): If you have any medical conditions, enter them in this field.
* **Known Allergies** (Text): If you have any allergies, enter them in this field.
* **Skin Conditions or Sensitivities** (Text): If you have any skin conditions or sensitivities, enter them in this field.
* **Are you currently pregnant or nursing** (Select One): Select your status from the options: Yes - Pregnant, Yes - Nursing, False, Unsure, or Not Applicable.
* **Smoking Status** (Select One): Select your smoking status from the options: Non-smoker, Former Smoker, Current Smoker, or Occasional Smoker.
* **Current Exercise Routine** (Text): Enter your current exercise routine in this field.
* **Dietary Habits** (Text): Enter your dietary habits in this field.
* **Do you have realistic expectations about results** (Select One): Select your level of realistic expectations from the options: True, Mostly, Somewhat, or False.
* **Expected Timeline for Results** (Select One): Select your expected timeline for results from the options: Immediate Results, 1-2 Weeks, 2-4 Weeks, 1-3 Months, More than 3 Months, or Unsure.
* **Desired Appointment Date** (Date): Enter your desired appointment date in this field.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (Select One): Select your preferred contact method from the options: Phone, Email, Text Message, or In Person.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and complete information to ensure the best possible experience for your body contouring procedures.
* If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please inform your healthcare provider to ensure your safety during the procedures.
* Be aware that your desired appointment date may be subject to changes based on the availability of the medical professional.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact your healthcare provider.
