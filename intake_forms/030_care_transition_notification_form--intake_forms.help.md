This form is designed to facilitate the transfer of care between healthcare teams, services, or units. It captures essential information to ensure a smooth transition, including patient details, care teams involved, and relevant medical information. The form's purpose is to provide a structured way to document the transition of care, reducing potential errors and ensuring continuity of care.

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the necessary information for the patient's **Patient name**, specifying the name of the patient undergoing the transition.
2. Provide the **From service or unit**, which is the current care team or unit the patient is currently under.
3. Identify the **To service or unit**, which is the receiving care team or unit that will take care of the patient after the transition.
4. Enter the **Transition date**, marking the date when the change in care occurs.
5. Specify the **Reason for transition**, providing a clear clinical or administrative explanation for the transfer.
6. Add any **Important notes for receiving team**, including allergies, limitations, or care preferences that are essential for the receiving team to know.
7. Ensure the **Responsible provider** information is accurate, including their name and contact details.
8. Confirm whether the **Family has been informed** about the transition status.
9. Finally, list any **Follow-up actions required**, specifying the tasks for the receiving team to prioritize.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient name** (`patient_name`, text, required): A field to specify the name of the patient transitioning care.
* **From service or unit** (`from_service`, text, required): The current care team or unit providing care for the patient.
* **To service or unit** (`to_service`, text, required): The care team or unit taking over the care of the patient.
* **Transition date** (`transition_date`, date, required): The date when the care transition happens.
* **Reason for transition** (`reason_for_transition`, text, required): A field to explain the clinical or administrative reason for the care transfer.
* **Important notes for receiving team** (`important_notes`, text, required): A field to document any allergies, limitations, or care preferences the receiving team should be aware of.
* **Responsible provider** (`responsible_provider`, text, required): The transferring provider's details, including name and contact information.
* **Family informed** (`family_informed`, select_one, required): A checkbox to confirm if the family has been informed about the transition status.
* **Follow-up actions required** (`follow_up_actions`, text, optional): Any tasks or actions the receiving team should perform after the care transition.

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Review the form carefully before submitting.
* Use clear and concise language in fields like Reason for transition and Important notes for receiving team.
* If unsure about any field, consult with the relevant healthcare team or unit.
