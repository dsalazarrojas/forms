# Hydrogen Therapy Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients who are interested in hydrogen therapy, a non-traditional treatment method. The form will help us assess the client's needs and provide a personalized experience for their therapy session. Please note that this form is for intake purposes only and does not guarantee a therapy session.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully and provide accurate information.
2. For the required fields, please ensure you fill out as much relevant information as possible.
3. If you are unable to answer a question, please explain why in the corresponding text box.
4. Once you've completed all the sections, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details** (`client_details`, text, required): Please provide your name, address, and contact information. This helps us to communicate with you effectively and prepare for your therapy session.
* **Health History** (`health_history`, text, required): Please describe your medical history, including any allergies, medical conditions, or previous treatments. This information is crucial for us to understand your needs and provide the best possible care.
* **Goals** (`goals`, text, required): Please specify the reasons you want to undergo hydrogen therapy. This will help us tailor the therapy to your specific needs.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Please select one of the two options provided, indicating whether you are at least 18 years old or not. This is a mandatory field to ensure our therapy sessions are conducted in a safe and responsible manner.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Please provide your phone number or any other contact information. This helps us to keep in touch with you and make any necessary arrangements for your therapy session.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please provide your email address. This is an optional field, but it will help us to communicate with you more efficiently.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, optional): Please provide the date of your therapy session. If you're unsure, you can leave this field blank and we'll schedule a date for you.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, optional): Please provide the time of your therapy session. If you're unsure, you can leave this field blank and we'll schedule a time for you.
