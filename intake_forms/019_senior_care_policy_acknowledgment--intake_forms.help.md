# Senior Care Policy Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about the Senior Care Policy Acknowledgment from end-users in a healthcare setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the purpose and questions being asked.
2. Answer each question carefully and accurately.
3. Ensure that you are completing the form for the correct purpose and not for any other reason.
4. If you are unsure about any of the questions, please contact the relevant authorities for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Care Recipient** (`care_recipient`, Select One, **Required:** No): This field allows you to specify whether or not you are a care recipient. Choose 'Yes' if you are a care recipient and 'No' if you are not.
* **Responsible Party** (`responsible_party`, Select One, **Required:** No): This field asks you to specify whether or not you are the responsible party for the care. Choose 'Yes' if you are the responsible party and 'No' if you are not.
* **Policy Date** (`policy_date`, Date, **Required:** No): This field asks for the date when the policy was acknowledged.
* **Care Facility** (`care_facility`, Text, **Required:** No): This field allows you to enter any care facility information related to the care.
* **Policy Signature** (`policy_signature`, Select Multiple, **Required:** No): This field asks you to specify whether or not you signed the policy. Choose 'Yes' if you signed the policy and 'No' if you did not.
* **Notes** (`notes`, Note, **Required:** No): This field is a text box for any additional notes or comments related to the care policy.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, Date, **Required:** No): This field asks for the date when the policy was signed.
* **Care Giver** (`care_giver`, Select One, **Required:** No): This field asks you to specify whether or not you are a care giver. Choose 'Yes' if you are a care giver and 'No' if you are not.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
* If you need help or clarification about any of the questions, contact the relevant authorities.
* This form is for the purpose of acknowledging and recording policies related to care recipient, responsible party, policy date, care facility, policy signature, notes, signature date, and care giver.
* This form should not be used for any other purpose.
