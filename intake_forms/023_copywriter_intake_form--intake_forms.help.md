# Copywriter Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Copywriter Intake Form is designed to gather essential information about your business to create tailored and effective content.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each field with the required information about your business.
2. For each field, follow the instructions and hints provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): Enter your business name.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter your company name, if different from your business name.
* **About Your Business** (`business_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe your business.
* **Products/Services** (`products_services`, text, optional): List your products or services.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, text, optional): Identify your target audience.
* **Business Website** (`business_website`, text, optional): Enter your website URL, if you have one.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your business phone number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your business email address.
* **Facebook** (`business_facebook`, text, optional): Enter your Facebook URL, if you have one.
* **Twitter** (`business_twitter`, text, optional): Enter your Twitter URL, if you have one.
* **Instagram** (`business_instagram`, text, optional): Enter your Instagram URL, if you have one.
* **LinkedIn** (`business_linkedin`, text, optional): Enter your LinkedIn URL, if you have one.
* **Short Description** (`business_description_long`, text, optional): Briefly describe your business.
* **Keywords** (`business_keywords`, text, optional): Enter keywords related to your business, separated by commas.
* **Tags** (`business_tags`, text, optional): Enter tags related to your business, separated by commas.
* **Image** (`business_image`, text, optional): Upload a relevant image.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your social media presence from the list.
* **Social Media Links** (`social_media_link`, text, optional): Enter links to your social media profiles.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, optional): Enter your business address.
* **City** (`business_city`, text, optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (`business_state`, text, optional): Enter your state or region.
* **ZIP** (`business_zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your social media presence from the list.
* **Submit** (`submit`, note, required): Click to submit your completed form.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, note, optional): Cancel your submission and start over.

Please note that some fields are optional, but filling out as much information as possible will help the copywriter create the best content for your business.
