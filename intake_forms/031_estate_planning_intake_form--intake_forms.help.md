# Estate Planning Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Estate Planning Intake Form is designed to collect essential information from clients or customers for estate planning purposes. This form aims to gather details that will help facilitate effective estate planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the purpose and scope of the information being collected.
2. Complete each field accurately and thoroughly, using the provided instructions as guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Client Information** (client_information)
* A text field where you can provide your name and any other relevant details about yourself or your representative.
* Please enter your name and any other necessary information.

### **Family History** (family_history)
* A text field for providing details about your family, such as family members' names, ages, and relationships.
* Please list any family members who are relevant to your estate planning.

### **Contact Preferences** (contact_preferences)
* A text field for specifying your contact preferences, such as preferred contact method (e.g., phone, email, or mail).
* Please indicate your preferred method of communication for estate planning purposes.
