# Psychological Appointment Pre Screening - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from clients before their psychological appointment. It helps us understand your needs, medical history, and any potential risks or concerns that may impact your treatment. Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to help us prepare for your appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email, phone number, and preferred language.
2. Provide details about your schedule requests and availability.
3. If applicable, mention any medical conditions, medications, allergies, and emergency medical devices you need to be aware of.
4. Confirm your insurance provider, number, and plan (if applicable).
5. Share your medical history, mental health provider, and any other relevant information.
6. Finally, confirm that you understand and agree to the client agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Please fill in your full name as per your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to contact you for further information or to schedule your appointment.
* **Phone Number**: Provide your phone number so we can reach you for any urgent matters.
* **Preferred Language**: Select your preferred language for communication (e.g., English, Spanish, French).
* **Schedule Request**: Let us know your preferred schedule for the appointment (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening).
* **Intake Details**: Describe the reason for your appointment and any specific concerns you'd like to address.
* **Available Time**: Enter your availability for the appointment (e.g., weekdays, weekends).
* **Mental Health Provider**: If you have a mental health provider, please specify their name and contact information.
* **Location**: Enter the location where you'd like to have your appointment (e.g., office, home).
* **Session Goals**: Describe your goals for the session (e.g., stress management, therapy).
* **Emergency Contact Person**: Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relation**: Specify your relationship with the emergency contact person (e.g., family member, friend).
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Enter the address of your emergency contact person.
* **Client Agreement**: Read and agree to the client agreement carefully, which includes our policies and procedures.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any ongoing medical conditions, such as diabetes, hypertension, etc.
* **Medication**: If applicable, mention any ongoing medications or treatments.
* **Allergies**: If you have any allergies, please list them (e.g., penicillin, etc.).
* **Emergency Medical Device**: If applicable, mention any emergency medical devices you need to carry (e.g., EpiPen).
* **Insurance Provider**: If you have insurance, please specify your provider name and contact information.
* **Insurance Number**: Enter your insurance number (if applicable).
* **Insurance Plan**: If you have an insurance plan, enter the plan name (if applicable).
* **Medical History**: Provide a detailed history of your medical and psychological history, including any relevant events or diagnoses.
* **Other Health Insurance**: If you have other health insurance, please specify.

Note: Please make sure to fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to help us prepare for your appointment. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
