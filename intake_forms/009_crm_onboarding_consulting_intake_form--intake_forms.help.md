# CRM Onboarding Consulting Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information about your company and project requirements for a successful CRM onboarding process. Please fill out this form to help us understand your needs and provide a tailored onboarding experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to the best of your ability.
2. For each field, please provide the most up-to-date and relevant information.
3. If you are unsure about any field, contact us for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_full_name`, text, required): Enter your company's full name.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter your organization's name.
* **Office Phone** (`office_phone`, text, required): Enter your office phone number.
* **Selected CRM Platform** (`crm_platform`, select_one, required): Select the CRM platform you are onboarding to (e.g., Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, etc.).
* **Is Data Migration Required?** (`migration_required`, select_one, required): Indicate if you need to migrate data from a legacy system (Yes - from another CRM, Yes - from spreadsheets, or No - new setup).
* **Estimated Number of Records to Migrate** (`existing_records_estimate`, number, optional): Enter the total number of leads, contacts, and accounts you need to migrate.
* **Technical Complexity Assessment** (`technical_complexity`, select_one, required): Rate the expected difficulty of integrations (Standard - No custom code, Moderate - Basic API use, High - Extensive Customization).
* **Internal Project Lead Name** (`internal_project_lead`, text, required): Enter the name of your internal project lead.
* **Number of Staff Requiring Training** (`total_trainees`, number, required): Enter the number of staff members requiring training.
* **Preferred Training Method** (`training_preferred_method`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred training method (Live Remote Workshops, On-site training, Pre-recorded videos, One-on-one coaching).
* **Target Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, date, required): Enter the target start date for the project.
* **Success Criteria** (`success_criteria`, text, required): Describe what a successful onboarding looks like for you.
* **Project Priority Level** (`priority_level`, select_one, required): Set the project's priority level (Low, Medium, High, Critical).
