# Buyer Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Buyer Intake Form is a crucial tool used by real estate professionals to gather essential information about potential buyers. This form helps in creating a personalized experience by understanding the buyer's needs, preferences, and requirements for a home purchase.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by providing your name as the buyer, which is a required field. Please enter your full name, including any relevant titles or prefixes.
2. Enter your contact email address, which is a required field. This will help the real estate professional get in touch with you for any further assistance.
3. Enter your contact phone number, which is a required field. This will help in scheduling meetings or calls for further discussions.
4. If applicable, provide the name of your real estate agent or representative.
5. Please select your marital status from the provided options.
6. If there's a co-buyer or co-borrower, please enter their name.
7. Enter the number of people in your household.
8. Choose your employment status from the options provided.
9. Estimate your approximate annual income in USD or your local currency.
10. Select your preferred home buying timeline.
11. Choose the type of property you are interested in from the options provided.
12. Enter your preferred location, neighborhood, or area for the home purchase.
13. Select your preferred price range for the home purchase.
14. If you're not pre-approved for a mortgage, you can leave this field blank.
15. Enter the amount you have available for a down payment or the percentage of the home's price you're willing to pay as a down payment.
16. If you're pre-approved for a mortgage, select the option that applies to you.
17. Choose your preferred loan type from the options provided.
18. Enter any must-have features or priorities you have for the home, such as a garage or a yard.
19. If there are any deal-breakers or features you'd like to avoid, enter them here.
20. Finally, add any additional information or notes about your home buying process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: This is the primary contact name of the buyer.
* **Email Address**: This is the email address where you can be reached for further discussions.
* **Phone Number**: This is your contact phone number for scheduling meetings or calls.
* **Agent Assigned**: If you have a real estate agent or representative, enter their name here.
* **Marital Status**: Choose your marital status from the provided options.
* **Co-Buyer/Borrower Name**: If there's a co-buyer or co-borrower, enter their name here.
* **Household Size**: Enter the number of people in your household.
* **Employment Status**: Choose your employment status from the provided options.
* **Approximate Annual Income**: Enter your approximate annual income in USD or your local currency.
* **Home Buying Timeline**: Choose when you'd like to buy a home from the provided options.
* **Property Type Preference**: Select the type of property you're interested in from the provided options.
* **Location Preference**: Enter your preferred location, neighborhood, or area.
* **Price Range for Purchase**: Select your preferred price range for the home purchase.
* **Down Payment Amount**: Enter the amount you have available for a down payment or the percentage of the home's price you're willing to pay as a down payment.
* **Mortgage Pre-Approval**: If you're pre-approved for a mortgage, select the option that applies to you.
* **Preferred Loan Type**: Choose your preferred loan type from the provided options.
* **Must-Have Features or Priorities**: Enter any must-have features or priorities you have for the home.
* **Deal-Breakers or Things to Avoid**: If there are any features or situations you'd like to avoid, enter them here.
* **Additional Information or Notes**: Add any additional information or notes about your home buying process.

Note: Please fill out this form as accurately and completely as possible to ensure the best experience for you and your real estate professional.
