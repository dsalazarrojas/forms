# Pre Complaint Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Complaint Intake Form is a collection tool used to gather information about incidents or complaints that have occurred. This form helps us to efficiently process and address these issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the form fields to understand what information is required for each step.
2. Fill out the form by providing accurate and relevant information for each field.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Incident Details (1)
* **Incident Details**: A brief description of the incident.
	+ This field is used to provide a concise and clear account of what happened.
	+ Please provide a brief summary of the incident, avoiding unnecessary details.
### Date Occurred (2)
* **Date Occurred**: The date the incident occurred.
	+ This field is used to determine the chronological order of events.
	+ Please enter the date in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
### Time Occurred (3)
* **Time Occurred**: The time the incident occurred.
	+ This field is used to further refine the timeline.
	+ Please enter the time in the format 'HH:MM AM/PM'.
### Description (4)
* **Description**: A detailed description of the incident.
	+ This field is used to gather a comprehensive account of what happened.
	+ Please provide as much information as possible, but avoid unnecessary details.
### Category (5)
* **Category**: Select 'Yes' if the incident is related to a specific category.
	+ This field helps us to categorize and prioritize incidents.
	+ Please choose the category that best describes the incident.
### Location (6)
* **Location**: The location where the incident occurred.
	+ This field is used to determine the location of the incident.
	+ Please provide the exact location where the incident happened.
### Customer Details (7)
* **Customer Details**: Any relevant customer information.
	+ This field is used to gather information about the customer involved.
	+ Please provide any relevant details about the customer.
### Agency (8)
* **Agency**: The agency involved in the incident.
	+ This field is used to determine the agency's involvement.
	+ Please provide the name of the agency involved.
### Staff (9)
* **Staff**: Select 'Yes' if staff were involved in the incident.
	+ This field helps us to determine staff involvement.
	+ Please choose 'Yes' if staff were directly or indirectly involved.
### Email (10)
* **Email**: The email address associated with the customer or incident.
	+ This field is used to contact the customer or gather more information.
	+ Please enter a valid email address.
### Phone (11)
* **Phone**: The phone number associated with the customer or incident.
	+ This field is used to contact the customer or gather more information.
	+ Please enter a valid phone number.
