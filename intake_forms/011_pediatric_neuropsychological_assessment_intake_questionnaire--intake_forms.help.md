# Pediatric Neuropsychological Assessment Intake Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about the patient's medical history, family history, and behavioral information to aid in the diagnosis and treatment of pediatric neuropsychological conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Name" field with the patient's full name.
2. Enter the patient's age.
3. Provide contact information, if necessary.
4. Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous treatments or conditions.
5. Provide information about the patient's family history, including any relevant medical conditions.
6. Describe any behavioral information that may be relevant to the patient's condition.
7. List any medical conditions the patient has.
8. Indicate if the patient is taking any medications.
9. List any allergies the patient has.
10. Provide any additional medical information not covered in other fields.
11. Finally, provide any additional notes about the patient's condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter the patient's full name.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, required): Enter the patient's age.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional): Please provide any contact information for the patient, such as phone numbers or email addresses.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required): Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous treatments or conditions.
* **Family History** (`family_history`, `text`, required): Provide information about the patient's family history, including any relevant medical conditions.
* **Behavioral Information** (`behavioral_info`, `text`, required): Describe any behavioral information that may be relevant to the patient's condition.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `select_multiple`, required): Check any medical conditions that the patient has, such as ADHD or Autism Spectrum Disorder.
* **Medications** (`medication`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate if the patient is taking any medications.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, required): List any allergies the patient has.
* **Other Medical Conditions** (`other_medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional medical information not covered in other fields.
* **Mental Health Information** (`mental_health_info`, `text`, required): Provide information about the patient's mental health, such as any past traumas or mental health conditions.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional notes about the patient's condition.
