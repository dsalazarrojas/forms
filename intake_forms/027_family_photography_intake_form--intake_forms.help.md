# Family Photography Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Photography Intake Form is designed to collect information about your family's photography session needs. This form will help our team understand your preferences, requirements, and expectations, ensuring we create the perfect experience for your family. Please ensure this form is completed accurately and thoroughly to get the best results from your session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each section carefully and fill out all required fields.
2. Be as specific and detailed as possible when answering questions.
3. Ensure your contact information is up-to-date and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Family Name** (`family_name`, `text`, required: false)
This field is your family's name. Please enter the name of all family members who will be participating in the session.

### 2. **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false)
This field requires your email address. Please enter the email address that you would like us to use for communication regarding your session.

### 3. **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the phone number where you can be reached for session-related inquiries or updates.

### 4. **Photographer** (`photographer_name`, `text`, required: false)
This field is not currently used, but if you would like to specify a photographer, please enter their name here.

### 5. **Session Date** (`session_date`, `date`, required: false)
Choose a specific date for your family photography session.

### 6. **Session Time** (`session_time`, `time`, required: false)
Choose a specific time for your family photography session.

### 7. **Session Location** (`session_location`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the location where you would like to have your family photography session.

### 8. **Session Length** (`session_length`, `number`, required: false)
Please select the duration of your session.

### 9. **Number of Family Members** (`number_of_family_members`, `number`, required: false)
Enter the number of family members participating in the session.

### 10. **Number of Children** (`number_of_children`, `number`, required: false)
Enter the number of children participating in the session.

### 11. **Child Ages** (`child_ages`, `text`, required: false)
List the ages of the children participating in the session.

### 12. **Session Type** (`session_type`, `select_one`, required: false)
Select the type of session you are interested in:
* Family Portrait
* Family Fun
* Family Adventure

### 13. **Photo Locations** (`photo_locations`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Choose the locations where you would like to have your photos taken:
* Home
* Outdoor
* Studio

### 14. **Photo Styles** (`photo_styles`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Select the lighting styles you prefer:
* Natural Light
* Artificial Light
* Mixed Light

### 15. **Family Photography Session Details** (`family_photography_session_details`, `note`, required: false)
Enter any additional details or requests you have for your session. This is a free-form text field for you to provide any specific requirements or expectations.
