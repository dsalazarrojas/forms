# Hair Growth Supplement Daily Use Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hair Growth Supplement Daily Use Intake Form is a collection of data about your daily use of hair growth supplements. Your responses will help us understand how you use the supplement and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read each page carefully and answer all the questions.
* Enter your responses in the required fields.
* If a question is not applicable to you, please select "Not Applicable" in the corresponding field.
* Make sure to submit your responses accurately and honestly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Daily Use** (Page 1)
	+ This page asks about your daily use of the hair growth supplement. Please indicate how often you use the supplement.
* **Start Date** (Page 2)
	+ This page asks for the start date of your supplement use. Please enter the date you first started using the supplement.
* **Dosage** (Page 3)
	+ This page asks about the amount of the supplement you take each day. Please enter the dosage as instructed by your healthcare professional.
* **Frequency** (Page 4)
	+ This page asks how often you use the supplement (Daily, Every Other Day, or Weekly). Please select the option that best describes your usage.
* **Overall Experience** (Page 5)
	+ This page is for you to describe your overall experience with the supplement. Please enter any comments or feedback you have about the supplement.
* **Side Effects** (Page 6)
	+ This page is for you to describe any side effects you have experienced while using the supplement. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Adherence** (Page 7)
	+ This page asks about your adherence to the supplement schedule. Please select how well you follow the recommended usage (Almost Always, Most Days, or Few Days).
* **Comments** (Page 8)
	+ This page is for any additional comments or feedback you have about the supplement. Please use this space to share your thoughts.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us for support.
* Your responses will help us improve the hair growth supplement and provide better products and services.
