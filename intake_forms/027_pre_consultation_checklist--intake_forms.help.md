# Pre Consultation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients before a consultation, typically for healthcare or medical professionals. It's a pre-consultation checklist that helps gather relevant details to provide the best possible care and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions thoroughly and accurately to provide the best possible information for the consultation.
2. If you're unsure about any question, please don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
3. Ensure you fill out the form completely before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Client Information (page 1)
* **Client Information** (`title`, `required`: false): This is the starting point of the form. Please provide your basic contact information to help us know who you are and how to contact you.

### Symptoms (page 2)
* **Symptoms** (`title`, `required`: false): Describe any health issues or problems you're experiencing. Please be as detailed as possible so we can better understand your situation.

### Consent (page 3)
* **Consent** (`title`, `required`: false): Acknowledge that you've read and understood the terms and conditions of the consultation. Please read through the provided information and agree to the terms to proceed.

### Additional Information (page 4)
* **Additional Information** (`title`, `required`: false): Provide any other relevant details that might be helpful for the consultation. This is an open-ended field, so please feel free to add anything you think is important.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure the best possible outcome from the consultation.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask.
* This form is for your convenience and should take about 5-10 minutes to complete. If you're unable to complete it, please contact us for assistance.
