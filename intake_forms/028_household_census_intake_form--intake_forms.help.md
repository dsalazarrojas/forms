# Household Census Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Household Census Intake Form is a tool used to collect data on households in the community. It helps us understand the demographics and socioeconomic characteristics of households, which informs our planning and resource allocation decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the Household Number, which is a unique identifier for each household.
2. Enter the name of the household, if applicable.
3. Provide the street address of the household.
4. Enter the house number (if applicable).
5. Enter the household income.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Household Number**: Enter the unique identifier for your household. This is usually a number assigned by the local government or a unique identifier created by the household itself.
* **Household Name**: Enter the name of the household, if it has one (e.g., "Smith Family" or "Johnson Household").
* **Street Address**: Enter the street address where the household is located.
* **House Number**: Enter the house number (if your household has a separate house number).
* **Household Income**: Enter the total monthly income of all household members.
