# criminal-defense-intake-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The criminal-defense-intake-form is a document used to collect information from defendants, attorneys, and other stakeholders involved in a criminal case. This form helps to gather essential details about the case, including defendant information, charges, case status, trial date, and contact information. The purpose of this form is to streamline the intake process and ensure accurate and comprehensive data collection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by entering the defendant's information, including their name, contact details, and any relevant notes.
2. Provide information about the charges, including the charge description and any relevant details.
3. Enter the case information, such as the case status and trial date.
4. Add the attorney's information, including their name and any relevant notes.
5. Add the contact information for the case, including phone, email, and address.
6. Enter the witness and victim information, if applicable.
7. Add any additional notes or comments about the case.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Defendant Information
* **Defendant Name** (`id: 1`, `label: Defendant Information`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the name of the defendant.
* **Defendant Information** (`id: 2`, `label: Charges Information`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter a brief description of the charges against the defendant.
* **Case Information** (`id: 3`, `label: Case Information`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter any relevant information about the case, such as the case number or docket number.
* **Attorney Information** (`id: 4`, `label: Attorney Information`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the name and contact information of the attorney representing the defendant.
* **Contact Information** (`id: 5`, `label: Contact Information`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the contact information for the person completing this form, including their name, phone number, and email address.
* **Witness Information** (`id: 6`, `label: Witness Information`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter any information about witnesses involved in the case, including their names and contact details.
* **Victim Information** (`id: 7`, `label: Victim Information`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter any information about the victim, including their name and contact details.
* **Case Status** (`id: 8`, `label: Case Status`, `type: number`, `required: false`): Select the current status of the case, such as "open" or "closed".
* **Trial Date** (`id: 9`, `label: Trial Date`, `type: date`, `required: false`): Enter the trial date for the case.
* **Lawyer Name** (`id: 10`, `label: Lawyer Name`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the name of the lawyer representing the defendant.
* **Contact Name** (`id: 11`, `label: Contact Name`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the name of the contact person for the case.
* **Contact Phone** (`id: 12`, `label: Contact Phone`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`id: 13`, `label: Contact Email`, `type: email`, `required: false`): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Address** (`id: 14`, `label: Address`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the address where the case will be tried.
* **City** (`id: 15`, `label: City`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the city where the case will be tried.
* **State** (`id: 16`, `label: State`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the state where the case will be tried.
* **Zip** (`id: 17`, `label: Zip`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the zip code where the case will be tried.
* **Attorney Notes** (`id: 18`, `label: Attorney Notes`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter any notes or comments from the attorney.
* **Case Notes** (`id: 19`, `label: Case Notes`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter any notes or comments about the case.
* **Client Notes** (`id: 20`, `label: Client Notes`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter any notes or comments from the client.
