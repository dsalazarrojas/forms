# Inbound Contact Center Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inbound Contact Center Intake Form is designed to collect customer contact information and preferences for efficient processing by customer service representatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring that all required fields are filled.
2. Select the relevant options for Phone, Customer Service Rep, and Customer Name.
3. Enter the correct Contact Date and Contact Time.
4. Enter the Customer Email address (if applicable).
5. Submit the form to initiate the contact process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information (Page 1)**:
	+ **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the customer.
	+ **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address (if applicable).
* **Phone** (Page 1):
	+ **Phone** (`phone`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more phone numbers that the customer may contact you on.
* **Customer Service Rep** (Page 1):
	+ **Customer Service Rep** (`customer_service_rep`, select_one, optional): Choose the customer service representative who will handle the customer's query.
* **Contact Date** (Page 2):
	+ **Contact Date** (`contact_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the customer's contact.
* **Contact Time** (Page 2):
	+ **Contact Time** (`contact_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the customer's contact.
* **Customer Name** (Page 2):
	+ **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the customer.
* **Phone** (Page 2):
	+ **Phone** (`phone`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more phone numbers that the customer may contact you on.
* **Customer Service Rep** (Page 2):
	+ **Customer Service Rep** (`customer_service_rep`, select_one, optional): Choose the customer service representative who will handle the customer's query.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate contact information to ensure efficient processing.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the customer service team for assistance.
* This form is designed to be completed in a timely manner to ensure prompt response to the customer's query.
