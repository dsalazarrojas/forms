# Mothers And Children Acceptance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a mothers and children acceptance form designed to collect information from parents and guardians about their consent, contact details, and child information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read the form instructions and explanations carefully before starting.
2. Select "Parent Consent" from the options provided in the "Parent Consent" field.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" from the options provided in the "Contact Information" field.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" from the options provided in the "Child Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Consent** (`Parent Consent`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please indicate your consent to participate in the program by selecting either "Yes" or "No".
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `select_one`, required: true): Indicate whether you have contact information or not by selecting "Yes" or "No". Please note that selecting "Yes" is required.
* **Child Information** (`Child Information`, `select_one`, required: true): Indicate whether you have child information or not by selecting "Yes" or "No". Please note that selecting "Yes" is required.

## Tips
* Please fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the form administrator for assistance.
* Ensure that you have provided accurate and honest information to avoid any delays or issues with your submission.
