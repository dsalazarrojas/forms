# Rehabilitation Insurance Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rehabilitation Insurance Verification Form is a crucial tool for healthcare professionals to verify and update patient insurance information. This form helps ensure accurate and up-to-date patient records, facilitating efficient and effective patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
2. Enter the provider's name and name of the facility (provider's name) in the "Provider Name" field.
3. Enter the date of the visit or service provided in the "Date Of Visit" field.
4. Fill in the patient's insurance number in the "Insurance Number" field.
5. Select the type of insurance coverage in the "Insurance Type" field (e.g., Yes or No).
6. Enter the start date of the coverage in the "Coverage Start Date" field.
7. Enter the end date of the coverage in the "Coverage End Date" field (if applicable).
8. Provide any additional coverage details in the "Coverage Details" field.
9. Enter the provider's license number in the "Provider License Number" field.
10. Enter the state where the provider is licensed in the "Provider License State" field.
11. Enter the provider's contact phone number in the "Provider Contact Number" field.
12. Enter the facility's name in the "Facility Name" field.
13. Enter the facility's address in the "Facility Address" field.
14. Enter the facility's city in the "Facility City" field.
15. Enter the facility's zip code in the "Facility Zip" field.
16. Enter the provider's National Provider Identifier (NPI) in the "Provider NPI" field.
17. Enter the facility's address in the "Provider Address" field.
18. Enter the state where the facility is located in the "Facility State" field.
19. Enter the facility's phone number in the "Phone" field.
20. Enter the facility's fax number in the "Fax" field.
21. Enter the facility's email address in the "Email" field.
22. Enter the facility's website in the "Website" field.
23. Enter the facility's license number in the "Facility License" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Provider Name**: The name of the healthcare provider submitting this form. This field is required.
* **Provider Contact Number**: The contact phone number of the healthcare provider. This field is optional.
* **Provider License Number**: The license number of the healthcare provider. This field is optional.
* **Provider License State**: The state where the healthcare provider is licensed. This field is optional.
* **Provider NPI**: The National Provider Identifier (NPI) of the healthcare provider. This field is optional.
* **Provider Address**: The address of the healthcare provider. This field is optional.
* **Date Of Visit**: The date of the visit or service provided. This field is optional.
* **Insurance Number**: The patient's insurance number. This field is optional.
* **Insurance Type**: The type of insurance coverage (Yes or No). This field is optional.
* **Coverage Start Date**: The start date of the coverage. This field is optional.
* **Coverage End Date**: The end date of the coverage. This field is optional.
* **Coverage Details**: Any additional details about the coverage. This field is optional.
* **Coverage Status**: The status of the coverage. This field is optional.
* **Coverage Note**: Any additional notes about the coverage. This field is optional.
* **Facility Name**: The name of the facility providing the rehabilitation services. This field is optional.
* **Facility Address**: The address of the facility providing the rehabilitation services. This field is optional.
* **Facility City**: The city where the facility is located. This field is optional.
* **Facility State**: The state where the facility is located. This field is optional.
* **Facility Zip**: The zip code of the facility. This field is optional.
* **Facility License**: The license number of the facility. This field is optional.
* **Phone**: The facility's phone number. This field is optional.
* **Fax**: The facility's fax number. This field is optional.
* **Email**: The facility's email address. This field is optional.
* **Website**: The facility's website. This field is optional.
