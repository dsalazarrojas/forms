# Vestibular Rehabilitation Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vestibular Rehabilitation Registration form is used to collect patient information for vestibular rehabilitation treatment. This form gathers essential details about the patient, their medical history, and their insurance information to provide personalized treatment plans.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the patient's details, such as name, date of birth, and contact phone number.
2. Provide any relevant medical history, including conditions, medications, and medical history dates.
3. Describe the patient's symptoms and their relationship with the doctor.
4. Outline the treatment goals and any additional comments.
5. Enter insurance information, including name and number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, optional): Enter the patient's contact phone number.
* **Medical License** (`Medical License`, text, optional): Check 'Yes' if the patient has a medical license, 'No' otherwise.
* **Medical License State** (`Medical License State`, select_one, optional): Select the state where the patient holds their medical license.
* **Doctor Name** (`Doctor Name`, text, optional): Enter the doctor's name.
* **Doctor Title** (`Doctor Title`, text, optional): Enter the doctor's title or profession.
* **Doctor Department** (`Doctor Department`, text, optional): Enter the doctor's department or specialty.
* **Patient Relationship** (`Patient Relationship`, text, optional): Describe the patient's relationship with the doctor.
* **Medical History Date** (`Medical History Date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the patient's medical history.
* **Medical History Description** (`Medical History Description`, text, optional): Describe the patient's medical history in detail.
* **Medical History Condition** (`Medical History Condition`, select_multiple, optional): Select any conditions associated with the patient's medical history.
* **Medication Name** (`Medication Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of any medications the patient is taking.
* **Medication Dosage** (`Medication Dosage`, text, optional): Enter the dosage of any medications the patient is taking.
* **Symptoms** (`Symptoms`, select_multiple, optional): Select any symptoms the patient is experiencing.
* **Symptoms Description** (`Symptoms Description`, text, optional): Describe the symptoms in detail.
* **Treatment Goals** (`Treatment Goals`, text, optional): Outline the treatment goals for the patient.
* **Treatment Goals Description** (`Treatment Goals Description`, text, optional): Describe the treatment goals in detail.
* **Insurance Name** (`Insurance Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the patient's insurance provider.
* **Insurance Number** (`Insurance Number`, text, optional): Enter the patient's insurance number.
* **Insurance Provider** (`Insurance Provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of the insurance provider.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required. Please fill out as much information as possible to ensure accurate and effective treatment planning.
