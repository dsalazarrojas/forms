# Therapy Walk In Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients before their therapy sessions. 
It helps the therapist and the treatment team gather necessary details about the 
client's intake and any emergency contact.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for each field.
2. Review the form for accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Info**: This field is for the client's personal details. Please enter your 
  name, address, and any other relevant information. 
  *Note: This field is not mandatory.*
 
* **Intake Details**: In this field, describe your reason for seeking therapy, your 
  goals for treatment, and any medical or mental health concerns you'd like to 
  share with your therapist. 
  *Note: This field is not mandatory.*

* **Contact Info**: Provide your contact information, including phone number and 
  email address, in case your therapist needs to reach out to you for any 
  reason. 
  *Note: This field is not mandatory.*

* **Emergency Contact**: List the name, phone number, and relationship of your 
  emergency contact, in case of any urgent situations. 
  *Note: This field is not mandatory.*

* **Consent**: Please indicate your agreement to be registered for therapy and 
  provide any other consent information as required. 
  *Note: This field is not mandatory.*

## Tips
- Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and completely.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid any mistakes.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask your therapist.
- The information collected in this form will be kept confidential and used for 
  the purpose of your therapy sessions.
