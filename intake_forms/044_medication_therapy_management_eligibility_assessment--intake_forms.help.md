# medication_therapy_management_eligibility_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assist clinicians in assessing a patient's eligibility for medication therapy management, a process of reviewing and optimizing a patient's medication regimen to ensure they are receiving the most effective and safe treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the patient's medical history, including their current medications and medical conditions.
2. Complete each page of the form, providing information about the patient's medical history, medical conditions, and medication list.
3. Select the most accurate answers for each question, using the options provided.
4. Complete the Follow-Up Information section to provide additional information about the patient's follow-up care.

* **Patient Details (Page 1)** (1): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant demographic information.
* **Patient Contact Information (Page 2)** (2): Enter the patient's contact information, including their phone number and email address.
* **Family History (Page 3)** (3): Enter any relevant family medical history, including any family members with medical conditions or allergies.
* **Medical History (Page 4)** (4): Enter the patient's medical history, including any relevant medical conditions or allergies.
* **Medication List (Page 5)** (5): Select the medications the patient is currently taking, using the options provided.
