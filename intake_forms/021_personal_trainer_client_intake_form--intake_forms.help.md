### Personal Trainer Client Intake Form - Help Guide

#### Purpose
The personal trainer client intake form is designed to collect information about you, the client, and your goals, so that your personal trainer can create a customized training plan tailored to your needs.

#### How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your general information, including your name and any other relevant details.
2. Select your primary goal for working with your personal trainer.
3. Enter your contact information, including your address and phone number.
4. Provide the contact information for an emergency contact, in case of an urgent situation.
5. Share any payment information, such as your payment method and frequency.
6. Add any additional notes you'd like to share with your personal trainer.

#### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_info`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide your full name, address, and any other relevant details. This will help your personal trainer get to know you better.
* **Goals** (`goals`, `select_one`, optional): Select your primary goal for working with your personal trainer. You can choose one or multiple options from the list.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your address and phone number to ensure your personal trainer can reach you.
* **Emergency Contact Information** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional): Please provide the name and contact information for a person the personal trainer can reach in case of an emergency.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, `text`, required/optional): Share any information regarding your payment method and frequency.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments you'd like to share with your personal trainer.
