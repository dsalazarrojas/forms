# Myotherapy Or Remedial Massage Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be completed by clients before attending a myotherapy or remedial massage therapy session. It collects essential information about the client's health details, health goals, medical conditions, and health history. Please ensure you fill out this form thoroughly and accurately to help our therapists provide you with the best possible treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section of the form carefully and accurately.
2. Answer each question thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. If you have any questions or concerns about a particular field, please don't hesitate to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Health Details**: (`client_health_details`, text, required: false) This section gathers information about your general health and wellness. Please describe your current health and fitness level, any ongoing health issues, or previous medical procedures. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Health Goals**: (`health_goals`, text, required: false) In this section, please outline your short-term and long-term health goals, including any specific areas you'd like to focus on during your treatment sessions. Be specific about what you hope to achieve through myotherapy or remedial massage therapy.
* **Medical Conditions**: (`medical_conditions`, text, required: false) If you have any medical conditions that may impact your treatment, please list them in this section. This includes any allergies, sensitivities, or previous surgeries.
* **Health History**: (`health_history`, text, required: false) Here, you can provide any relevant health history, such as previous injuries, illnesses, or medical treatments. This will help our therapists understand your overall health picture and tailor your treatment accordingly.

## Tips

* Please ensure you answer each question truthfully and accurately.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
* This form is an essential part of the intake process. Please complete it thoroughly to receive the most effective and safe treatment.
* If you have any questions or concerns before, during, or after completing this form, don't hesitate to contact us.
