# emotional_wellness_client_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **emotional_wellness_client_intake_form** is a comprehensive form designed to collect information from clients to support their emotional well-being. This form is meant to be used by professionals in the mental health industry to gather essential details about their clients' history, contact information, and treatment plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form's purpose and instructions.
2. Answer all the questions in the following pages: Client Info, Contact Info, Medical History, Mental Health History, Treatment Plans, Emergency Contacts, Emergency Services, and Mental Health Services.
3. Ensure you complete all required fields (indicated by *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Info
* **Client Info** (`1`, `text`, optional): This page is where you can enter any general information about the client, such as their name, address, and any other relevant details.

### Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`2`, `text`, optional): This page is where you can enter the client's contact information, including their phone number and email.

### Medical History
* **Medical History** (`3`, `text`, optional): In this section, you can document any significant medical events or conditions affecting the client.

### Mental Health History
* **Mental Health History** (`4`, `text`, optional): This section is where you can document any significant mental health events or conditions affecting the client.

### Treatment Plans
* **Treatment Plans** (`5`, `text`, optional): In this page, you can outline any existing or planned treatments the client is undergoing.

### Emergency Contacts
* **Emergency Contacts** (`6`, `text`, optional): This page is for recording important contact information in case of an emergency, such as the client's emergency contacts' names and phone numbers.

### Emergency Services
* **Emergency Services** (`7`, `text`, optional): Here, you can document any emergency services the client is receiving or needs to receive.

### Mental Health Services
* **Mental Health Services** (`8`, `text`, optional): In the last page, you can document any mental health services the client is receiving or has received in the past.

## Tips
* Make sure to tailor the form to the specific needs of your clients.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out.
* Use the form as a tool to support your clients' emotional well-being.
