# Participant Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for capturing information about program participants. It is designed to collect details such as program name, contact information, and participant details to ensure accurate and efficient participant management.

## How to Complete This Form
To fill out the form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the program name in the "Program Name" field.
2. Provide the contact person's name in the "Contact Person" field.
3. Enter the contact person's email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter the contact person's phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
5. If applicable, select the start and end dates for the program using the date fields.
6. Enter the duration of the program in the "Program Duration" field.
7. Enter your name and email address in the "Participant Name" and "Participant Email" fields, respectively.
8. Finally, enter your phone number in the "Participant Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the program you are participating in or interested in.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person to contact regarding the program.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): If applicable, select the start date of the program.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): If applicable, select the end date of the program.
* **Program Duration** (`program_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the program in number of weeks or months.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the participant.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address as the participant.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number as the participant.
