# Low Vision Patient Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a patient's low vision status, medical history, and other relevant details to provide accurate and effective care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the patient's first name and last name in the respective fields.
2.  Enter the patient's date of birth.
3.  Enter the date of the visit.
4.  Select the patient's primary vision problem.
5.  Enter the patient's phone number.
6.  Choose the patient's current vision level (Normal, Mild, Moderate, or Severe).
7.  If necessary, enter the name of the referring doctor, phone number, and email address.
8.  Choose the patient's vision goals (Improve vision, Improve daily activities, Improve independence).
9.  Provide a detailed description of the patient's vision goals.
10. Enter the patient's medical history, including any relevant medical conditions.
11. Enter the date of the last visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **patient_first_name** (text, required): Enter the patient's first name.
*   **patient_last_name** (text, required): Enter the patient's last name.
*   **date_of_birth** (text, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
*   **date_of_visit** (date, required): Enter the date of the visit in MM/DD/YYYY format.
*   **primary_vision_problem** (text, required): Select the patient's primary vision problem.
*   **patient_phone** (text, required): Enter the patient's phone number.
*   **vision_level** (select one, required): Choose the patient's current vision level (Normal, Mild, Moderate, or Severe).
*   **referring_doctor** (text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the referring doctor.
*   **referring_doctor_phone** (text, optional): If applicable, enter the referring doctor's phone number.
*   **referring_doctor_email** (email, optional): If applicable, enter the referring doctor's email address.
*   **referring_clinician** (text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the clinician.
*   **patient_vision_goals** (select multiple, optional): Choose the patient's vision goals (Improve vision, Improve daily activities, Improve independence).
*   **vision_goals_detail** (text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the patient's vision goals.
*   **medical_history** (text, optional): Enter the patient's medical history, including any relevant medical conditions.
*   **patient_medical_conditions** (text, optional): Enter any relevant medical conditions that may affect the patient's vision.
*   **date_of_last_visit** (date, optional): Enter the date of the last visit in MM/DD/YYYY format.

## Tips
*   Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information about the patient's vision status and medical history.
*   If you're unsure about any of the fields, please ask the patient for clarification or consult with the referring doctor.
