# Scar Treatment Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scar Treatment Evaluation Form is a tool for healthcare professionals to collect data and information about scars. It helps evaluate the characteristics of a scar and its treatment history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the relevant fields with the requested information about the scar.
2. Choose the correct options from the select fields.
3. Enter your additional comments and notes if needed.
4. Submit the form for review and evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is the scar location?** (`scar_location`, text, optional): Please enter a description of the location of the scar, such as the body part or area affected.
- **Scar type** (`scar_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of scar from the options provided, such as Atrophic or Hypertrophic.
- **Scar size (cm)** (`scar_size`, number, optional): Enter the size of the scar in centimeters.
- **Scar color** (`scar_color`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the colors of the scar from the options provided, such as White, Red, Blue, or Purple.
- **Scar depth (mm)** (`scar_depth`, number, optional): Enter the depth of the scar in millimeters.
- **Scar surface** (`scar_surface`, select_one, optional): Choose the surface type of the scar from the options provided, such as Flat or Wavy.
- **Patient info** (`patient_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant patient information, such as medical history or allergies.
- **Medical history** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Enter any relevant medical history related to the scar.
- **Scar treatment** (`scar_treatment`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of treatment received from the options provided, such as Surgery or Chemical peeling.
- **Scar treatment duration (months)** (`scar_treatment_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the treatment in months.
- **X-axis** (`scar_location_x`, number, optional): Enter the X-axis location of the scar.
- **Y-axis** (`scar_location_y`, number, optional): Enter the Y-axis location of the scar.
- **Z-axis** (`scar_location_z`, number, optional): Enter the Z-axis location of the scar.
- **X-axis orientation** (`scar_orientation_x`, number, optional): Enter the X-axis orientation of the scar.
- **Y-axis orientation** (`scar_orientation_y`, number, optional): Enter the Y-axis orientation of the scar.
- **Z-axis orientation** (`scar_orientation_z`, number, optional): Enter the Z-axis orientation of the scar.
- **Scar area (cm²)** (`scar_area`, number, optional): Enter the area of the scar in square centimeters.
- **Scar diameter (cm)** (`scar_diameter`, number, optional): Enter the diameter of the scar in centimeters.
- **Scar width (cm)** (`scar_width`, number, optional): Enter the width of the scar in centimeters.
- **Scar length (cm)** (`scar_length`, number, optional): Enter the length of the scar in centimeters.
- **Scar height (cm)** (`scar_height`, number, optional): Enter the height of the scar in centimeters.
- **Scar diagnosis** (`scar_diagnosis`, text, optional): Enter a diagnosis related to the scar.
- **Scar treatment status** (`scar_treatment_status`, text, optional): Enter the current status of the scar treatment.
- **Scar treatment outcome** (`scar_treatment_outcome`, select_one, optional): Choose the treatment outcome from the options provided, such as Improved or Worsened.
- **Additional comments** (`scar_additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the scar.
