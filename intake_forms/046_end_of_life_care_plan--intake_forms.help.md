# End Of Life Care Plan - Help Guide
## Purpose
The End Of Life Care Plan form is designed to gather essential information about the patient's health status, medical history, and current care needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient's Name" field (page 1).
2. Provide personal details in the "Personal Information" field (page 2).
3. Describe the patient's medical history in the "Medical History" field (page 3).
4. Indicate whether there are any medical caregivers involved in the patient's care (page 4).
5. Specify any medical conditions the patient has (page 5).
6. List any medications the patient is taking (page 6).
7. Identify any allergies the patient has (page 7).
8. Check if the patient has any advance directives (page 8).
9. List other caregivers involved in the patient's care (page 9).
10. Report any other medical conditions the patient may have (page 10).
11. Indicate whether the patient is taking any other medications (page 11).
12. Review and acknowledge the form's contents (page 12).
13. Provide any additional comments or information about the patient (page 13).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient's Name** (`Patient's Name`, text, required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their ID card or identification documents.
* **Personal Information** (`Personal Information`, text, optional): Provide any additional personal details that are relevant to the patient's care, such as address, contact information, etc.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, text, required): Describe the patient's medical history, including any chronic conditions, surgeries, or other relevant health information.
* **Medical Caregivers** (`Medical Caregivers`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether there are any medical caregivers involved in the patient's care, such as doctors, nurses, or family members.
* **Medical Caregivers List** (`Medical Caregivers List`, text, optional): List the names and contact information of the medical caregivers involved.
* **Medical Conditions** (`Medical Conditions`, select_multiple, optional): Check any conditions the patient has, such as heart disease, diabetes, or respiratory issues.
* **Medical Conditions List** (`Medical Conditions List`, text, optional): List any other medical conditions the patient has.
* **Medications** (`Medications`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether the patient is taking any medications, such as pain medication or blood thinners.
* **Medications List** (`Medications List`, text, optional): List any other medications the patient is taking.
* **Allergies** (`Allergies`, select_multiple, optional): Check any allergies the patient has, such as food or environmental allergies.
* **Allergies List** (`Allergies List`, text, optional): List any other allergies the patient has.
* **Advance Directives** (`Advance Directives`, select_multiple, optional): Check if the patient has any advance directives, such as a living will or do-not-resuscitate order.
* **Advance Directives List** (`Advance Directives List`, text, optional): List any other advance directives the patient has.
* **Other Caregivers** (`Other Caregivers`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about other caregivers involved in the patient's care.
* **Other Caregivers List** (`Other Caregivers List`, text, optional): List the names and contact information of other caregivers.
* **Other Medical Conditions** (`Other Medical Conditions`, select_multiple, optional): Check any other medical conditions the patient may have.
* **Other Medical Conditions List** (`Other Medical Conditions List`, text, optional): List any other medical conditions the patient may have.
* **Other Medications** (`Other Medications`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether the patient is taking any other medications.
* **Other Medications List** (`Other Medications List`, text, optional): List any other medications the patient is taking.
* **Review and Acknowledgement** (`Review and Acknowledgement`, checkbox, required): Confirm that you have reviewed the form and its contents.
* **Review and Acknowledgement List** (`Review and Acknowledgement List`, text, optional): No explanation.
* **Other Information** (`Other Information`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the patient that may be relevant to their care.
* **Other Information List** (`Other Information List`, text, optional): No explanation.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about the patient's care.

## Tips
* Please review your responses carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult with the patient or their caregiver.
* This form is meant to be a comprehensive overview of the patient's care needs; please answer all fields to the best of your ability.
