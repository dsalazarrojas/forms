# Dermal Filler Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients about their dermal filler treatments, including medical history, contact details, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information. The form has multiple pages with various fields that need to be completed.
2. Make sure to answer all the questions truthfully and accurately.
3. If you are unsure about any question, ask your practitioner for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Client Information** (`client_info`, text, optional)
* This section is for providing your basic contact information such as name, phone number, and email.

### **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional)
* This section is for providing your contact information such as address, phone number, and email.

### **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional)
* This section is for providing information about your past medical history, including any previous surgeries, allergies, or medical conditions.

### **Medications** (`medications`, text, optional)
* This section is for listing any medications you are currently taking or have taken in the past.

### **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional)
* This section is for listing any allergies you have, including medications, foods, or substances you are allergic to.

### **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional)
* This section is for listing any medical conditions you have or have had in the past, such as diabetes, high blood pressure, etc.

### **Family History** (`family_history`, text, optional)
