This help guide will assist users in filling out the Special Education Intake Form, a document used to collect relevant information about students' needs and circumstances.

**Purpose**
-----------

The Special Education Intake Form is a crucial tool for educators and support staff to gather essential details about students' situations, enabling them to provide personalized assistance and accommodations. This form ensures that students receive the support they need to succeed academically and socially.

**How To Complete This Form**
-------------------------

1. **Student Information**: Enter the student's name, age, and grade level to help us understand their basic demographics.
2. **Contact Information**: Provide the student's address, phone number, and email address to enable us to contact them or their guardians.
3. **Student Details**: Describe the student's strengths, weaknesses, and any medical conditions that may affect their learning.
4. **Family Members**: List the names and contact information of the student's family members, including parents or guardians.
5. **Services Needed**: Select the therapies or services the student requires, such as Physical Therapy, Occupational Therapy, or Speech Therapy.
6. **Additional Services**: Choose any further services the student might need, like Adaptive Technology or Assistive Technology.
7. **Special Education Services**: Choose the type of special education service the student requires, such as General Special Education, Early Childhood, or High School.
8. **Education Background**: Provide any relevant information about the student's educational background, such as past special education experiences.
9. **Medical History**: Describe any medical conditions or allergies that may impact the student's learning.
10. **Student Performance**: Describe the student's academic performance, including strengths and areas of improvement.
11. **Behavioral Concerns**: List any behavioral concerns or difficulties the student may face.
12. **Student Behavior**: Describe how the student interacts with peers and adults.
13. **Parent Participation**: Share the parents or guardians' level of involvement in the student's special education process.
14. **Student Goals**: Identify the student's short-term and long-term goals.
15. **Progress Tracking**: Report on how the student's progress will be monitored.
16. **Communication Methods**: Describe how the student prefers to communicate with educators or support staff.
17. **School Participation**: Outline any school-related programs or activities the student participates in.
18. **School Supports**: List any school resources or services the student uses.
19. **Teacher Supports**: Describe any teacher-led programs or activities that support the student.
20. **Student Supports**: Identify any student-led or self-directed activities.
21. **Additional Comments**: Share any further information or concerns about the student's situation.
22. **Date Completed**: Enter the date the form was completed.
23. **Time Completed**: Enter the time the form was completed.
24. **Completed By**: Identify who completed the form.
25. **Completed On**: Enter the date the form was completed.
