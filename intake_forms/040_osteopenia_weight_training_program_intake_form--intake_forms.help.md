# Osteopenia Weight Training Program Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather important information from clients before they start participating in the Osteopenia Weight Training Program. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure a safe and successful experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information, including your name, contact details, and medical information.
2. Read and understand all the terms and conditions, including the safety acknowledgement and waiver.
3. Sign the waiver and submission forms to acknowledge that your information is true and accurate.
