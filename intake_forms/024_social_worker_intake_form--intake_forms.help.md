# Social Worker Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information for social workers to provide proper care and support to patients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring all required fields are filled.
2. Review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.
3. Submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Basic Info**: Enter your basic information, including your name, phone number, and email.
	+ This field is used to collect your personal contact details.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information for communication with the social worker.
	+ This field is used to establish a contact point for further communication.
* **Medical History**: Describe your medical history, including any previous conditions or treatments.
	+ This field is used to gather information about your medical past.
* **Insurance Info**: Provide your insurance details, including any relevant policy numbers.
	+ This field is used to verify insurance coverage.
* **Family History**: Describe your family medical history, including any conditions or treatments.
	+ This field is used to gather information about your family's medical history.
* **Medical Info**: Enter any relevant medical information, such as allergies or previous surgeries.
	+ This field is used to gather detailed medical information.
* **Medications**: List any current medications or supplements.
	+ This field is used to track your current medications.
* **Medical Notes**: Enter any important medical notes or comments.
	+ This field is used to record important medical information.
* **HIPAA**: Select one option indicating your HIPAA preference.
	+ This field is used to determine your HIPAA status.
* **Insurance Payers**: Select all applicable insurance payers.
	+ This field is used to verify insurance coverage.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the contact information of your emergency contact.
	+ This field is used to gather contact information for emergency situations.
* **Social Worker**: Enter the name and contact information of your assigned social worker.
	+ This field is used to establish communication with your social worker.
* **Signature**: Sign the form to confirm submission.
	+ This field is used to confirm form submission.
* **Date**: Enter the current date.
	+ This field is used to timestamp the form submission.
* **Time**: Enter the current time.
	+ This field is used to timestamp the form submission.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
	+ This field is used for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
	+ This field is used for communication purposes.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
	+ This field is used for contact purposes.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments.
	+ This field is used for general information.
* **Medical Condition**: Enter any relevant medical conditions.
	+ This field is used to gather medical information.
* **Social Work Info**: Enter any relevant social work information.
	+ This field is used to provide social work information.
* **Medical History 2**: Enter any additional medical history information.
	+ This field is used to gather more medical history information.
* **Social Work Notes**: Enter any additional social work notes.
	+ This field is used to record social work information.
* **Insurance Info 2**: Enter any additional insurance information.
	+ This field is used to verify insurance coverage.
* **Signature 2**: Sign the form to confirm submission.
	+ This field is used to confirm form submission.

Please note that some fields may require additional information or attachments. If unsure, please contact the relevant authorities or your assigned social worker for guidance.
