# Graduation Recognition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Graduation Recognition Form is used to document and recognize academic achievements, graduations, and awards. It captures essential information about students, their graduation, and recognition details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the student's information, including their student ID, name, and contact details.
2. Select the graduation date and degree or award type.
3. Choose the type of recognition and date for the recognition.
4. Provide additional details about the recognition, such as the recognition reason and note.
5. Upload a photo of the graduate (optional).
6. Enter any additional information, such as the school name, location, and graduation term.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Information** (`student_info`): Enter the student's name, student ID, or any other unique identifier.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter the student's name as it appears on their graduation documents.
* **Student Number** (`student_number`): Enter the student's number or ID as per your institution's system.
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`): Enter the date of graduation or completion of studies.
* **Graduation Degree** (`graduation_degree`): Select the degree or award type (e.g., Bachelor's, Master's, Ph.D.).
* **Student Name** (`student_name`): Enter the student's name as per your institution's records.
* **Recognition Type** (`recognition_type`): Choose the type of recognition (e.g., Graduation, Award, Honor).
* **Recognition Date** (`recognition_date`): Enter the date of recognition or award.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`): Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`): Enter the student's address.
* **School Name** (`school_name`): Enter the name of the school or institution.
* **School Location** (`school_location`): Enter the location of the school or institution.
* **Recognition By** (`recognition_by`): Enter the name or title of the person or organization granting recognition.
* **Recognition Reason** (`recognition_reason`): Provide a brief reason for the recognition.
* **Recognition Note** (`recognition_note`): Add any additional comments or notes about the recognition.
* **Photo URL** (`photo_url`): Upload a photo of the graduate (optional).
* **Photo Alt Text** (`photo_alt_text`): Enter a brief description of the photo.
* **Photo Caption** (`photo_caption`): Enter a caption for the photo.
* **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`): Enter the year of graduation.
* **Graduation Term** (`graduation_term`): Select the term of graduation (e.g., Spring, Fall).
* **Award Type** (`award_type`): Select the type of award or recognition.
* **Award Year** (`award_year`): Enter the year of the award.
* **Award Recognition Year** (`award_recognition_year`): Enter the year of award recognition.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`): Enter the name of the person or team assigned to recognize the graduate.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
* Double-check your entries for formatting and spelling errors.
* Consult with the relevant authority if you are unsure about any field or requirement.
* Use clear and concise language when providing details and recognition reason.
