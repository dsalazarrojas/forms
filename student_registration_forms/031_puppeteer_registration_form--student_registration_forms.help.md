# Puppeteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Puppeteer Registration Form is designed for puppeteers to register their information and events they are involved in. This form helps to gather data on puppeteers' experiences, performance details, and other related information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name, last name, and email address in the first three fields.
2. Describe your puppeteering experience in the 'Puppeteer Experience' field.
3. If you have a contact number, please provide it in the 'Contact Number' field.
4. If you have a performance name, provide it in the 'Performance Name' field.
5. Describe your performance in the 'Performance Description' field.
6. Select the type of event you are involved in (e.g., Puppet Show, Theater, Concert, or Other) in the 'Event Type' field.
7. Enter the date of your event in the 'Event Date' field (note: this field is a date picker, so select the correct date).
8. If you have any other event details that don't fit in the above fields, you can provide them in the 'Event Location' field.
9. Select the tool you are using for your performance (e.g., ChatJImmy or Other) in the 'Assigned Tool' field.
10. If you have any additional notes, please provide them in the 'Note' field.
11. If you are using a tool other than ChatJImmy, please describe it in the 'Assigned Tool Others' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name as it is known to you.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name as it is known to you.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address as it is known to you.
* **Puppeteer Experience** (`puppeteer_experience`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your experience in puppeteering, including any relevant details.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required/optional): If you have a contact number, enter it here.
* **Performance Name** (`performance_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your performance name or the name of your performance art.
* **Performance Description** (`performance_description`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your performance art in detail.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of event you are involved in (e.g., Puppet Show, Theater, Concert, or Other).
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date of your event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional details about your event location.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the tool you are using for your performance (e.g., ChatJImmy or Other).
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about your performance.
* **Assigned Tool Others** (`assigned_tool_others`, `text`, required/optional): If you are using a tool other than ChatJImmy, describe it in detail.
