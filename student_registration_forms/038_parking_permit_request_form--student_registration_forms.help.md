# Parking Permit Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parking Permit Request Form is a crucial part of the parking permit issuance process for students. It helps provide necessary details for a smooth and efficient permit process. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee details: This section is required for us to identify you as a student and process your request.
2. Choose your parking location: Select where you would like to park your vehicle.
3. Select your parking preference: Choose if you need a parking permit.
4. Enter your employee name and email: These details are necessary for us to contact you about your parking permit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, text, required): Please fill in your employee name and ID number to identify you as a student.
* **Parking Spaces** (`parking_spaces`, text, required): This field is used to specify if you need parking spaces.
* **Parking Location** (`parking_location`, select_one, required): Choose where you would like to park your vehicle from the options provided.
* **Parking Location ID** (`parking_location_id`, text, required): Enter the parking location ID corresponding to your chosen location.
* **Parking Permit** (`parking_permit`, select_multiple, required): Select if you need a parking permit.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your employee name as it appears on your ID.
* **Employee E-mail** (`employee_email`, email, required): Enter your employee email address for us to contact you.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your employee details are accurate to avoid delays in processing your request.
* If you have any questions or issues with completing this form, feel free to contact us.
* Please note that parking permits are subject to availability.
