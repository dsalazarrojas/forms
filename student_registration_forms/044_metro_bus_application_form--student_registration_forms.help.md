# Metro Bus Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Metro Bus Application Form is a tool used to collect necessary details from students applying for a Metro Bus scholarship. Please fill out this form carefully, ensuring all required and optional fields are completed accurately.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to ensure you understand what information is being collected.
2. Fill out each field according to the instructions provided for each field.
3. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **student_information**: Enter a brief description of why you are applying for the Metro Bus scholarship. Please provide a short paragraph explaining your background and how you believe you qualify for this opportunity.
* **student_name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **student_address**: Provide your current address. If you are a student living on campus, you can enter your dormitory's address.
* **student_id_number**: Enter your government-issued ID number (such as your passport or driver's license number).
* **student_email**: Enter your valid email address that you check regularly.
* **contact_number**: Enter your contact number that we can use to reach you for any further questions or updates.
* **student_birthdate**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
