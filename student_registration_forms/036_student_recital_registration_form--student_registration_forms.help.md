# Student Recital Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Recital Registration Form is designed to collect information from students participating in a recital event. This form is essential for event organizers to gather accurate and complete details about each performer.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure a smooth registration process, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form fields below to understand what information is required.
2.  Fill in the form with accurate and complete details for each student participating in the recital.
3.  Double-check your answers before submitting the form to avoid mistakes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Student Information** (`student_recital_registration_form_1`)
*   **Student Name**: Enter the student's full name as it appears on their identification or school records.
*   **Grade Level**: Select the student's current grade level or year of study.
*   **School/Institution**: Choose the student's school or institution name from the options provided or type it in manually.
*   **Performance Title**: Enter the title of the recital performance, including any relevant details (e.g., piece, composer, etc.).

### **Recital Information** (`student_recital_registration_form_2`)
*   **Recital Date**: Enter the date of the recital performance.
*   **Time**: Select the start time of the recital performance.
*   **Location**: Provide the address or location of the recital event.
*   **Performance Type**: Choose the type of performance (e.g., piano, vocal, instrumental, etc.).

### **Contact Information** (`student_recital_registration_form_3`)
*   **Parent/Guardian Name**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian contact person.
*   **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
*   **Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
*   **Address**: Enter the physical address of the contact person.

### **Student Performance** (`student_recital_registration_form_4`)
*   **Performance Details**: Write a brief description of the performance, including any special notes or requirements.

## Tips
*   Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
*   Double-check your answers for any inconsistencies before submission.
*   If you're unsure about any information, contact the event organizers for clarification.
