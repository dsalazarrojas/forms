# Homework Help Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Homework Help Order Form is designed to collect information from students who need help with their homework. This form is used to guide students in submitting orders for homework help, and the information collected will be used to provide them with assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone number.
5. Describe the problem you need help with.
6. Select the subject of your problem.
7. Choose the level of help you need.
8. Select your preferred contact method.
9. Choose the tool you would like to use for help.
10. Select a date and time for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
- **Problem Description** (`problem_description`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the problem you need help with.
- **Subject** (`subject`, `text`, required/optional): Select the subject of your problem.
- **Help Needed** (`help_needed`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the level of help you need (Help with everything, Just check my work, Explain it to me, Do it for me).
- **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, Live Chat, In-Person Meeting).
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the tool you would like to use for help (chatjimmy, Other).
- **Date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): Select a date for help.
- **Time** (`time`, `time`, required/optional): Select a time for help.
