# Artworks Studio Day Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Artworks Studio Day Application Form is designed to collect information from students for the Artworks Studio Day event. This form is used to create student profiles and manage student attendance, provide necessary accommodations, and ensure their safety during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the "Student Information" section.
2. Select your preferred studio day date in the "Select Your Studio Day Date" section.
3. Choose your preferred mediums to work with in the "Which mediums are you most interested in" section.
4. If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please describe them in the "Food Allergies or Medical Conditions" section.
5. If you have previous art experience, please describe it in the "Briefly describe your previous art experience" section.
6. Choose how you heard about us in the "How did you hear about us" section.
7. Finally, provide your emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact Phone" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name (Student Full Name)** (`student_full_name`, text, required): This field asks for your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth (student_dob)** (`date`, required): Please enter your date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Current Grade Level (grade_level_student)** (`select_one`, required): Please select your current grade level from the options: Elementary School, Middle School, High School, or Post-secondary.
* **Parent or Guardian Name (parent_guardian_details)** (`text`, required): Please enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (emergency_phone_studio)** (`text`, required): Please enter your emergency contact phone number.
* **Select Your Studio Day Date (studio_date_selection)** (`select_one`, required): Please select the date you would like to attend the Artworks Studio Day event.
* **Which mediums are you most interested in (preferred_mediums_studio)** (`select_multiple`, optional): Please select the mediums you are interested in working with.
* **Food Allergies or Medical Conditions (allergy_medical_notes)** (`text`, optional): If you have any food allergies or medical conditions, please describe them in this section.
* **Briefly describe your previous art experience (past_art_exp)** (`text`, optional): If you have previous art experience, please describe it briefly.
* **How did you hear about us (discovery_studio)** (`select_one`, optional): Please select how you heard about us.
* **I consent to my child being picked up by authorized guardians (pick_up_consent)** (`select_one`, required): Please confirm that you consent to your child being picked up by authorized guardians.
