# Reverse Career Fair Student Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reverse Career Fair Student Registration Form is designed to collect information from students about their interests, preferences, and availability for a reverse career fair event. This form will help the event organizers to better understand student needs and plan the event accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your information in the fields provided, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select your preferred contact method (yes or no).
3. If you have selected "yes" for the preferred contact method, provide a brief description of your preferred contact method.
4. Indicate your availability for the event by selecting the available time.
5. Specify your preferred career area (yes or no).
6. Select your preferred dress code for the event (yes or no).
7. Provide any additional information about yourself that you think would be helpful for the event organizers.
8. Enter the name of the person submitting the form (in case of group registration).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information** (`reverse_career_fair_student_registration`, Text, Optional): Enter your student ID or any other identifier assigned to you by the university.
* **Name** (`student_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (`student_email`, Email, Optional): Enter your university email address.
* **Phone** (`student_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Major** (`student_major`, Text, Optional): Enter your major or academic program.
* **Career Fair Participation** (`career_fair_participation`, Select One, Optional): Indicate if you plan to participate in the career fair (yes or no).
* **Preferred Career Area** (`preferred_career_area`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select all the career areas that interest you.
* **Available Time** (`available_time`, Time, Optional): Select the time you are available for the event.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, Select One, Optional): Select your preferred method of contact (yes or no).
* **Contact Method Description** (`preferred_contact_method_description`, Text, Optional): If you have selected "yes" for the preferred contact method, provide a brief description of your preferred contact method.
* **Dress Code** (`career_fair_dress_code`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the dress code you prefer for the event (yes or no).
* **Presentation Info** (`career_fair_presentation_info`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional information about yourself that you think would be helpful for the event organizers.
* **Additional Info** (`career_fair_additional_info`, Text, Optional): Provide any other comments or information about yourself that you think would be helpful for the event organizers.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the form (in case of group registration).

## Tips

* Please fill in all the fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask the event organizers for clarification.
* Make sure to check your preferred contact method and dress code selections carefully.
* If you have any special requirements or needs, do not hesitate to provide additional information in the "Presentation Info" field.
