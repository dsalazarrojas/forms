# Loan Repayment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The loan repayment form is used to document and track loan repayments made to a student.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined above. Review the loan agreement and ensure you understand the repayment terms before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **loan_amount** (Loan Amount, Number, Required/Optional: False): The total amount of the loan being repaid. This should be filled out accurately and clearly.
* **issuer** (Issuer, Select One, Required/Optional: False): The name of the issuer of the loan. Please select the appropriate issuer from the dropdown list.
* **interest_rate** (Interest Rate, Number, Required/Optional: False): The interest rate of the loan. This field is not applicable for many types of loans.
* **repayment_date** (Repayment Date, Date, Required/Optional: False): The date of the loan repayment. Please ensure this date is accurate and corresponds to the actual repayment date.
* **repayment_frequency** (Repayment Frequency, Select Multiple, Required/Optional: False): The frequency of the loan repayment. Please select one or more of the options provided.
* **total_repayment** (Total Repayment, Number, Required/Optional: False): The total amount repaid for the loan. This field is not necessary for many types of loans.
* **additional_info** (Additional Information, Note, Required/Optional: False): Any additional information about the loan repayment. Please use this field to provide any relevant details.
* **issuer_email** (Issuer Email, Email, Required/Optional: False): The email address of the issuer of the loan. Please ensure this email is accurate and up-to-date.
* **issuer_phone** (Issuer Phone, Text, Required/Optional: False): The phone number of the issuer of the loan. Please enter the correct phone number.
* **issuer_name** (Issuer Name, Text, Required/Optional: False): The name of the issuer of the loan. Please enter the correct name.
* **issuer_address** (Issuer Address, Text, Required/Optional: False): The address of the issuer of the loan. Please enter the correct address.
* **issuer_note** (Issuer Note, Note, Required/Optional: False): Any additional information about the issuer. Please use this field to provide any relevant details.

## Tips
- Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
- Ensure you have all necessary information about the loan before filling out the form.

Note: This form does not need to be filled out for every single type of loan. However, it's always recommended to fill out the form for all loans to ensure accurate and thorough documentation.
