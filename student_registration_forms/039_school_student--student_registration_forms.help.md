# School Student - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is for completing the School Student registration form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the student's first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the student's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the student's phone number.
* **School year** (`school_year`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the student's school year.
* **Group** (`student_group`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select whether the student is affiliated with a group.
* **Class** (`student_class`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the student's class affiliation.
* **Gender** (`student_gender`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the student's gender.
* **Age** (`student_age`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the student's age.
* **Height** (`student_height`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the student's height in feet and inches.
* **Weight** (`student_weight`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the student's weight in pounds.

## Tips
- Please double-check your entries for accuracy.
- If you need to make changes, you can edit your submission before submission.
- If you have any issues or doubts, contact your designated support person.
