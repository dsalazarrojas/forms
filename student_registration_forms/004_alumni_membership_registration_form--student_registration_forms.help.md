# Alumni Membership Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Alumni Membership Registration Form is designed for alumni to register for membership with the institution. It collects essential information for member profiling and benefits distribution.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your membership type from the available options: Recent Graduate, Standard Alumni, Senior Alumni, Life Member, International Alumni, or Retired Faculty/Staff.
2. Enter your personal details: First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, and Gender.
3. Provide your contact information: Email Address, Phone Number, and Mailing Address.
4. Share your academic background: Institution Name, Graduation Year, and Degree Earned.
5. Select membership benefits: Alumni Newsletter Subscription, Event Discounts Enrollment, and Career Services Access.
6. Choose your preferred payment method and frequency.
7. Confirm your emergency contact information (optional).
8. Accept the membership terms and conditions, privacy policy, and communication consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Membership Type** (`membership_category`): Select the type of membership that applies to you.
* **Membership Category** (`membership_category`): Choose your membership type from the options provided.
* **First Name** (`first_name`): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender** (`gender`): Select your gender from the provided options.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): Enter your email address, which will be your primary contact method.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter your phone number.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`): Enter your street address for membership materials.
* **City** (`city`): Enter your city.
* **State/Province** (`state`): Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (`zip_code`): Enter your zip code or postal code.
* **Country of Residence** (`country`): Select your country of residence from the options provided.
* **Institution Name** (`institution_name`): Enter the name of the school you graduated from.
* **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`): Enter your graduation year.
* **Degree Earned** (`degree_earned`): Select your degree earned from the options provided.
* **Major/Field of Study** (`major_field_study`): Enter your major or field of study.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id_number`): If available, enter your student ID number.
* **Alumni Newsletter Subscription** (`newsletter_subscription`): Select whether you want to receive the alumni newsletter (digital, print, or both).
* **Event Discounts Enrollment** (`event_discounts`): Select whether you want to receive event discounts.
* **Career Services Access** (`career_services`): Select whether you want to access career services.
* **Library Access Request** (`library_access`): Select whether you want to access institutional library resources.
* **Membership Fee Amount** (`membership_fee`): Enter your annual membership dues.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select your preferred payment method.
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`): Select how often you want to pay (annually, quarterly, monthly, or one-time life membership).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`): Enter the full name of your emergency contact (optional).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact (optional).
* **Relationship to Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact_relationship`): Enter your relationship with your emergency contact (optional).
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_acceptance`): Agree to the membership terms and conditions.
* **Privacy Policy Acknowledgment** (`privacy_policy`): Acknowledge the privacy policy.
* **Communication Consent** (`communication_consent`): Consent to receive communications from the alumni association.
* **Other Benefits Selection** (`newsletter_subscription`, `event_discounts`, `career_services`): Select additional membership benefits.
* **Other Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select an alternative payment method (if not using credit card).

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields marked as "required" are filled out correctly.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the provided help text or contact the institution's administration.
* If you have any issues with the form, please report them to the institution's technical support team.
* Make sure to review and understand the membership terms and conditions, privacy policy, and communication consent before accepting them.
