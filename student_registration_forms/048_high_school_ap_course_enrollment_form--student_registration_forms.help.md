# High School AP Course Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect student information and course preferences for Advanced Placement (AP) courses at high school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Student ID.
2. Enter your Student Name.
3. Choose your Grade Level (High or Low).
4. Select the Course ID.
5. Enter the Course Name.
6. Fill in your Parent Name (if applicable).
7. Choose the date you requested the course.
8. Indicate whether you have requested the course (Yes or No).
9. Check if the course has been approved (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID**: This is your unique identifier as a student. Enter your student ID number.
* **Student Name**: This is your name. Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Grade Level**: This is your current grade level in the school. Choose between High and Low.
* **Course ID**: This is the unique identifier for the course you are enrolling in.
* **Course Name**: This is the name of the course you are enrolling in. Be sure to spell it correctly.
* **Parent Name**: This is your parent's name. If you are under 18, this field may be filled in by your parent or guardian.
* **Course Request Date**: This is the date you requested to enroll in the course. Be sure to use a valid date.
* **Course Requested**: Indicate whether you have requested to enroll in the course (Yes or No).
* **Course Approved**: Check if the course has been approved or not (Yes or No).
