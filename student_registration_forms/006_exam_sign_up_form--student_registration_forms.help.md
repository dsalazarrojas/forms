# Exam Sign Up Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to allow students to sign up for an exam. It is intended for students who want to participate in exams offered by the institution. This form is not meant to be used for any other purpose, and filling it out will only register you for the exam specified in the "Enter Exam Code" field.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the exam code in the "Enter Exam Code" field.
2. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Fill in your last name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Choose a date for the exam you wish to take.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Exam Code**: This is the unique code for the exam you wish to take. Please enter the code exactly as it appears on your exam schedule or invitation.
* **First Name**: This is where you enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Last Name**: This is where you enter your last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Date**: This is where you select the date you wish to take the exam. Please use the date format `mm/dd/yyyy` (e.g., 06/12/2024).
