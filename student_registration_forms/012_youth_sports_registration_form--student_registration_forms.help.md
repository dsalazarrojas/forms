# Youth Sports Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to register students for youth sports programs. It collects essential information from students, such as their name, school affiliation, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the "name" field with the student's name.
2. Next, select the student's "grade_level" from the dropdown list.
3. Enter the student's "school_name" and "school_id" if applicable.
4. Provide the student's "email" and "phone" contact details.
5. Enter the "guardian_name", "guardian_email", and "guardian_phone" details for the student's guardian.
6. Finally, indicate your consent by filling out the "consent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter the student's name.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the student's grade level from the dropdown list.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the student's school name (if applicable).
* **School ID** (`school_id`, `text`, optional): Enter your school's ID (if applicable).
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the student's email address in the format "example@example.com".
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the student's guardian.
* **Guardian Email** (`guardian_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the guardian's email address in the format "example@example.com".
* **Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the guardian's phone number.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `text`, required): Indicate your consent by filling out this field.

## Tips

* Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
* Ensure you have the necessary permissions to register the student for the youth sports program.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the program administrators for assistance.
