# Dorm Room Change Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dorm Room Change Request Form is a tool used by students to request a change in their dorm room assignment. This form is designed to gather relevant information from students who wish to move to a different room or have special housing requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately, providing all the required information.
2. If you have already submitted a request for the same room type and size, please do not fill out this form again.
3. Make sure to submit the form before the requested date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Room Request**: This is the main field where you can request a change in your dorm room. Please be specific about the type of room you are requesting.
- **Student ID**: Please enter your unique student ID number.
- **Room Type**: Choose 'Yes' if you are requesting a change in room type (e.g., single, double, triple).
- **Room Size**: Select 'Yes' if you are requesting a change in room size (e.g., small, medium, large).
- **Reason**: Provide a clear and detailed reason for your room change request.
- **Notes**: If you have any additional information or comments, please add them here.
- **Email**: Please enter your university email address.
- **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
- **Date**: Enter the date you are requesting to move to your new room.
- **Time**: Enter the time you would like to move to your new room.

## Tips
- Make sure to submit the form accurately and thoroughly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the housing administration.
- Please allow 3-5 business days for your request to be processed.
- If you need to make any changes to your request, please contact the housing administration before the requested date and time.
