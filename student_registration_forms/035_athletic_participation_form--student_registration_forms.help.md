# Athletic Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The athletic participation form is used to gather information about students participating in school sports activities. It is essential to collect accurate and complete information about the student's personal details, medical status, and other relevant information to ensure safe and enjoyable participation in sports activities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name, date of birth, and student ID number.
2. Select your grade level and school name.
3. Provide your address, city, state, and zip code.
4. Select your contact phone number and email (if applicable).
5. Provide your parent or guardian's name, phone number, and email (if applicable).
6. Select your emergency contact's name and relationship.
7. Choose the sport you wish to participate in.
8. Select the season you wish to participate in.
9. Indicate if you have previously participated in this sport before.
10. List any medical conditions or allergies.
11. List any current medications.
12. List any previous significant sports injuries.
13. Provide your insurance provider's name and policy number.
14. Enter the date of your last sports physical examination.
15. Provide the name of your doctor who performed the physical examination.
16. Select your medical clearance status.
17. Select your transportation plan.
18. Indicate if you need a fee waiver.
19. Indicate if you agree to follow the athletic code of conduct.
20. Indicate if you understand and accept the risks of athletic participation.
21. Select if you allow media coverage.
22. Provide your student signature.
23. Provide your parent or guardian signature.
24. Enter the date of your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Athlete Full Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name, including your first and last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM-DD-YYYY.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required): Select your gender from the available options (Male, Female, Non-Binary, Prefer Not to Say).
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id`, `text`, required): Enter your school identification number.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your school.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your current grade level from the available options (9th Grade, 10th Grade, 11th Grade, 12th Grade).
* **Street Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your home address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city of your residence.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter the state or province of your residence.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your zip or postal code.
* **Student Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact phone number (if applicable).
* **Student Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your contact email (if applicable).
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (`parent_guardian_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the contact phone number of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`parent_guardian_email`, `email`, required): Enter the contact email of your parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required): Enter your relationship to your emergency contact.
* **Sport Selection** (`sport_selection`, `select_one`, required): Select the sport you wish to participate in.
* **Season** (`season`, `select_one`, required): Select the season you wish to participate in.
* **Previous Participation** (`previous_participation`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you have previously participated in this sport before.
* **Medical Conditions or Allergies** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, required): List any medical conditions or allergies you have.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, `text`, optional): List any current medications you are taking.
* **Previous Sports Injuries** (`previous_injuries`, `text`, required): List any previous significant sports injuries.
* **Health Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your insurance provider.
* **Insurance Policy Number** (`insurance_policy_number`, `text`, required): Enter your insurance policy number.
* **Physical Examination Date** (`physical_exam_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your last sports physical examination.
* **Physician Name** (`physician_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the doctor who performed your physical examination.
* **Medical Clearance Status** (`medical_clearance`, `select_one`, required): Select your medical clearance status.
* **Transportation Plan** (`transportation_plan`, `select_one`, required): Select your transportation plan.
* **Participation Fee** (`fee_payment`, `number`, optional): Enter the fee amount if applicable.
* **Fee Waiver Needed** (`fee_waiver_needed`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you need a fee waiver.
* **Code of Conduct Agreement** (`code_of_conduct`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you agree to follow the athletic code of conduct.
* **Assumption of Risk** (`assumption_of_risk`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you understand and accept the risks of athletic participation.
* **Media Release** (`media_release`, `select_one`, required): Select if you allow media coverage.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, `text`, required): Sign with your full name as the student.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature** (`parent_guardian_signature`, `text`, required): Sign with your full name as the parent or guardian.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your signature.

## Tips
1. Ensure you complete the form accurately and thoroughly.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting.
3. Ask for help if you need it.
