# FERPA Notice Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The FERPA Notice Form is used to collect and update personal information and data sharing consent from students, as per the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA).

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Student Name** as it appears on your student record.
2. Enter your **Student Email** address as it appears on your student record.
3. Enter your **Student Phone Number** as it appears on your student record.
4. Select your **School Records** as per your student record (optional).
5. Select your **Data Purposes** as per your student record (optional).
6. Check the box to give your **FERPA Consent** to data sharing.
7. Enter any **Additional Comments** about your student record (optional).
8. Enter the **Date** as it appears on your student record.
9. Enter the **Time** as it appears on your student record.
10. Enter your **Student Signature** as it appears on your student record.
11. Enter your **School Signature** as it appears on your student record.
12. Check the box to agree to **Agreements** to data sharing.
13. Check the box for **FERPA Agreement 1** to data sharing (optional).
14. Check the box for **FERPA Agreement 2** to data sharing (optional).
15. Check the box for **FERPA Acknowledgement 1** to data sharing (optional).
16. Check the box for **FERPA Acknowledgement 2** to data sharing (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (1)** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your student record.
* **Student Email (2)** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter your university email address.
* **Student Phone Number (3)** (`student_phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number as it appears on your student record.
* **School Records (4)** (`school_records`, select_multiple, optional): Select your school records as per your student record.
* **Data Purposes (5)** (`data_purposes`, select_one, optional): Select your data purposes as per your student record.
* **FERPA Consent (6)** (`ferpa_consent`, checkbox, required): Check the box if you consent to data sharing.
* **Additional Comments (7)** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about your student record.
* **Date (8)** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date as it appears on your student record.
* **Time (9)** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time as it appears on your student record.
* **Student Signature (10)** (`student_signature`, text, required): Enter your signature as it appears on your student record.
* **School Signature (11)** (`school_signature`, text, required): Enter your school signature as it appears on your student record.
* **Agreements (12)** (`agreements`, checkbox, required): Check the box if you agree to data sharing.
* **FERPA Agreement 1 (13)** (`ferpa_agreement_1`, radio, optional): Check the box if you agree to data sharing.
* **FERPA Agreement 2 (14)** (`ferpa_agreement_2`, radio, optional): Check the box if you agree to data sharing.
* **FERPA Acknowledgement 1 (15)** (`ferpa_acknowledgement_1`, checkbox, required): Check the box if you agree to data sharing.
* **FERPA Acknowledgement 2 (16)** (`ferpa_acknowledgement_2`, checkbox, required): Check the box if you agree to data sharing.
