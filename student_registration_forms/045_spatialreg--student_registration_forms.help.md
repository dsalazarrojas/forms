# spatialreg - Help Guide

## Purpose
The spatialreg form is used to collect information from students for registration purposes. This form is required for all students to provide accurate and up-to-date information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the spatialreg form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. If applicable, enter the emergency contact's name, email, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
6. Enter the student ID number in the "student_id" field.
7. Select a registration date from the calendar.
8. Enter the fee amount in the "fee" field.
9. Select the fee frequency from the options provided (monthly or one-time).
10. Choose the type of fee (campus or off-campus).
11. If applicable, enter any additional information about your accommodation in the "accommodation" field.
12. Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact from the options provided (parent, guardian, or friend).
13. Enter any notes about your emergency contact in the "emergency_contact_note" field.
14. Enter any additional notes about your situation.
