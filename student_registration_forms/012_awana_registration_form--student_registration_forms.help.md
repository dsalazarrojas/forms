# Awana Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Awana Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from parents or guardians for their child's registration in the Awana program. The form is to be completed by a parent or guardian for each child being registered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

* Carefully read each field description and provide accurate information for your child.
* Fill in the required fields with your child's information.
* Select the correct options for each field from the provided list.
* Sign and certify the accuracy of the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Year** (id: 1): Select the current program year for your child.
	+ This field is used to determine which Awana program your child is eligible for.
* **Church or Organization Name** (id: 2): Enter the name of the church or organization your child is registered with.
	+ This field helps us know which church or organization is associated with your child's registration.
* **Parent/Guardian First Name** (id: 3): Enter your first name.
	+ This field is used to identify the primary parent or guardian associated with your child.
* **Parent/Guardian Last Name** (id: 4): Enter your last name.
	+ This field is used to identify the primary parent or guardian associated with your child.
* **Home Address** (id: 5): Enter your home address.
	+ This field is used to determine your child's address for Awana program purposes.
* **City** (id: 6): Enter the city where you reside.
	+ This field is used to identify the city associated with your child's registration.
* **State** (id: 7): Enter the state or province where you reside.
	+ This field is used to identify the state or province associated with your child's registration.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (id: 8): Enter your postal code.
	+ This field is used to identify the postal code associated with your child's registration.
* **Home Phone Number** (id: 9): Enter your primary home phone number.
	+ This field is used as an emergency contact number.
* **Mobile Phone Number** (id: 10): Enter your cell phone number.
	+ This field is used as an emergency contact number.
* **Email Address** (id: 11): Enter your primary email address.
	+ This field is used as an emergency contact email.
* **Child First Name** (id: 12): Enter your child's first name.
	+ This field is used to identify your child.
* **Child Last Name** (id: 13): Enter your child's last name.
	+ This field is used to identify your child.
* **Child Date of Birth** (id: 14): Enter your child's birth date.
	+ This field is used to determine your child's age.
* **Child Gender** (id: 15): Select your child's gender.
	+ This field is used to identify your child's gender.
* **Current Grade Level** (id: 16): Select your child's current grade level.
	+ This field is used to determine which Awana program your child is eligible for.
* **Awana Program Level** (id: 17): Select which Awana program your child is interested in.
	+ This field is used to determine which Awana program your child is eligible for.
* **Registering Additional Children** (id: 18): Indicate if you are registering additional children.
	+ This field is used to determine if you are registering multiple children.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (id: 19): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
	+ This field is used to identify the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (id: 20): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
	+ This field is used to contact the emergency contact person in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (id: 21): Describe the relationship between the emergency contact person and your child.
	+ This field is used to understand the relationship between the emergency contact person and your child.
* **Medical Conditions or Allergies** (id: 22): Enter any medical conditions or allergies your child has.
	+ This field is used to identify any medical conditions or allergies your child may have.
* **Current Medications** (id: 23): Enter any medications your child is taking.
	+ This field is used to identify any medications your child is taking.
* **Physician Name** (id: 24): Enter the name of your child's doctor.
	+ This field is used to identify your child's doctor.
* **Physician Phone** (id: 25): Enter your child's doctor's phone number.
	+ This field is used to contact your child's doctor.
* **Health Insurance Provider** (id: 26): Enter the name of your child's health insurance provider.
	+ This field is used to identify your child's health insurance provider.
* **Authorized Pickup Persons** (id: 27): Enter the names of people authorized to pick up your child.
	+ This field is used to identify people authorized to pick up your child.
* **Photo/Video Permission** (id: 28): Indicate if you give permission for us to use photos and videos of your child for promotional materials.
	+ This field is used to determine if you give permission for photos and videos of your child to be used.
* **Medical Release Authorization** (id: 29): Indicate if you authorize medical treatment for your child in case of an emergency.
	+ This field is used to determine if you authorize medical treatment for your child in case of an emergency.
* **Liability Waiver Agreement** (id: 30): Indicate if you agree to the liability waiver terms.
	+ This field is used to determine if you agree to the liability waiver terms.
* **Volunteer Interest** (id: 31): Indicate if you are interested in volunteering with Awana.
	+ This field is used to determine if you are interested in volunteering.
* **How Did You Hear About Awana** (id: 32): Select how you heard about Awana.
	+ This field is used to understand how you heard about Awana.
* **Registration Fee Payment** (id: 33): Select how you intend to pay the registration fee.
	+ This field is used to determine how you intend to pay the registration fee.
* **Parent/Guardian Signature** (id: 34): Sign and certify the accuracy of the information provided.
	+ This field is used to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Registration Date** (id: 35): Enter the current date.
	+ This field is used to record the date of registration.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
* If you are registering multiple children, please complete this form for each child.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* We appreciate your cooperation in providing accurate and complete information.
