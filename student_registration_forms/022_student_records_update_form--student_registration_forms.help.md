# Student Records Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to update a student's records. It is used to collect updated information about a student, including details from the student and their parent or guardian.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
- Ensure that all fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the 'Submit' field to confirm whether to update the records or not.

- **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the school the student attends.
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the student's name.
- **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, `text`, optional): Enter the student's current grade level.
- **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the parent or guardian's contact phone number.
- **Parent E-mail** (`parent_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the parent or guardian's email address.
- **Update Reason** (`update_reason`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief reason for the update.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter the student's date of birth.
- **Date of Last Grade** (`date_of_last_grade`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the student last attended a grade level.
- **Update Notes** (`update_notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes related to the update.
- **Parent Address** (`parent_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the parent or guardian's home address.
- **Parent City** (`parent_city`, `text`, optional): Enter the parent or guardian's city of residence.
- **Parent State** (`parent_state`, `text`, optional): Enter the parent or guardian's state of residence.
- **Parent Zip** (`parent_zip`, `text`, optional): Enter the parent or guardian's zip code.
- **Parent Country** (`parent_country`, `text`, optional): Enter the parent or guardian's country of residence.
- **Student Address** (`student_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the student's address.
- **Student City** (`student_city`, `text`, optional): Enter the student's city of residence.

- **Student Notes** (`student_notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes related to the student.
- **Parent Notes** (`parent_notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes related to the parent or guardian.
