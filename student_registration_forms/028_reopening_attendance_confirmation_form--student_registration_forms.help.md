# resources/reopening-attendance-confirmation-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reopening Attendance Confirmation Form is used to confirm a student's physical attendance at a school event or activity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are using this form for the correct event or activity.
2. Enter your name and email (if you have one) to identify yourself.
3. Confirm your physical attendance by selecting "Yes" or "No" for the "Prophets physical attendance?" field.
4. Enter the attendance date you attended the event.
5. If desired, enter the time you attended the event.
6. If you have any additional information, you can enter it in the "Additional Notes" field.
7. Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (if you have one).
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if you have one).
* **Prophets physical attendance?** (`physical_attendance`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you attended the event physically, or "No" if you did not.
* **Attendance Date** (`attendance_date`, date, required): Enter the date you attended the event.
* **Attendance Time** (`attendance_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you attended the event (if desired).
* **School Location** (`school_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the school or event venue.
* **Additional Notes** (`student_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional information you believe is necessary to confirm your attendance.
