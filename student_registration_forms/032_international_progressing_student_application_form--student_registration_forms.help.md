# International Progressing Student Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The International Progressing Student Application Form is a document designed to collect information about students who are progressing or have progressed from one institution to another. This form is used to track and manage student progression.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring all required fields are filled.
* Review each field carefully and ensure you understand what is being asked.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student Info (id: 1)
* **Student Info** (`Student Info`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of yourself as a student.
### Student Name (id: 2)
* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
### Email (id: 3)
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Provide your valid email address.
