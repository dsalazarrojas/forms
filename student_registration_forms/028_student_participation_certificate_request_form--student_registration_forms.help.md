# Student Participation Certificate Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Participation Certificate Request Form is a tool that allows students to request participation certificates for courses they have attended. It is used to gather necessary information from the students for the creation of their participation certificates.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review and confirm that your information is accurate before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as per your official documents, including first and last name(s).
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required): Enter your student ID number as per your student ID card or official documents.
* **Institution Name** (`institution_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the institution you attended, as per your official documents.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the course you attended, as per your official documents.
* **Participation Date** (`participation_date`, date, required): Enter the date you participated in the course, in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter your valid student email address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter your valid student phone number.
* **Institution Contact** (`institution_contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact information for the institution you attended.

## Tips
* Be accurate when filling out the form with information from your official documents.
* Make sure to review and confirm that your information is accurate before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact your institution's administration.
