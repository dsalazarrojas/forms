# Student Merchandise Proposal - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from students about their product proposals, including categories, descriptions, and other relevant details. Please provide clear and concise information to help us better understand your proposal.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Start Form" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Provide a brief title for your proposal in the "Student Merchandise Proposal" field.
3. Select the category that best fits your proposal from the "Category" dropdown menu.
4. Write a detailed description of your proposal in the "Description" field.
5. If you have an attached file, upload it in the "File" field.
6. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
8. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
9. Select the date and time for your proposal in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
10. Choose one option from the "Select One" dropdown menu.
11. Choose multiple options from the "Select Multiple" dropdown menu.
12. Enter a numerical value in the "Integer" field.
13. Enter a decimal number in the "Decimal" field.
14. Select a date and time for your proposal in the "Date Time" field.
15. Enter a date range in the "Date Range" field.
16. Enter a time range in the "Time Range" field.
17. Enter a boolean value in the "Boolean" field.
18. Enter any additional text information in the "Text" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Merchandise Proposal** (`student_merchandise_proposal_form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for your proposal.
* **Category** (`student_merchandise_proposal_category`, select_one, optional): Select the category that best fits your proposal.
* **Description** (`student_merchandise_proposal_description`, text, optional): Write a detailed description of your proposal.
* **File** (`student_merchandise_proposal_file`, text, optional): If you have an attached file, upload it here.
* **Note** (`student_merchandise_proposal_note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Email** (`student_merchandise_proposal_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`student_merchandise_proposal_phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number.
* **Date** (`student_merchandise_proposal_date`, date, optional): Select the date for your proposal.
* **Time** (`student_merchandise_proposal_time`, time, optional): Select the time for your proposal.
* **Select One** (`student_merchandise_proposal_select_one`, select_one, optional): Choose one option from the dropdown menu.
* **Select Multiple** (`student_merchandise_proposal_select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional): Choose multiple options from the dropdown menu.
* **Integer** (`student_merchandise_proposal_integer`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value.
* **Decimal** (`student_merchandise_proposal_decimal`, number, optional): Enter a decimal number.
* **Date Time** (`student_merchandise_proposal_date_time`, text, optional): Enter a date and time for your proposal.
* **Date Range** (`student_merchandise_proposal_date_range`, text, optional): Enter a date range.
* **Time Range** (`student_merchandise_proposal_time_range`, text, optional): Enter a time range.
* **Boolean** (`student_merchandise_proposal_boolean`, text, optional): Enter a boolean value.
* **Text** (`student_merchandise_proposal_text`, text, optional): Enter any additional text information.

## Tips
- Please be specific and clear when filling out the form.
- Provide any supporting files or documents as attachments.
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form administrators.
