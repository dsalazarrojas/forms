# mentee_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mentee_intake_form is designed to collect necessary information from students for the purpose of assigning a successful mentorship match. This includes gathering details about their interests, college experience, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the Personal Info section, which includes fields for student name, phone number, and email.
2. In the Student Interests section, select all the interests that apply to you.
3. For the College Experience section, select your current college level and major.
4. In the Additional Information section, enter your student ID, department name, and college name.
5. Finally, review all the information you entered to ensure accuracy before submitting the form.
