# esl_and_computer_skills_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students for registration purposes, particularly for ESL and computer skills courses. It will help the institution or organization to keep track of student details and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the "User Input" field.
2. Provide course information in the "Course Info" field.
3. Select your proficiency levels in the "Proficiency Levels" field.
4. Enter your class roster in the "Class Roster" field.
5. If you have any other information, you can enter it in the "Other Info" field.
6. Finally, confirm that the information provided is accurate by selecting "Confirm" from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input**: Enter your personal details, such as your name and contact information.
* **Course Info**: Provide any relevant course information, such as course name, date, or time.
* **Proficiency Levels**: Select your proficiency levels in a specific skill or subject.
* **Class Roster**: Enter your class roster, which may include your class number, instructor's name, or class schedule.
* **Course Preferences**: Enter any other relevant course information or preferences.
* **Other Info**: Enter any additional information that is not covered in the other fields.
* **Confirm**: Confirm that the information provided is accurate before submission.
