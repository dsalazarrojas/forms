# Study Abroad Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect personal details and background information from students interested in applying for a study abroad program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your personal details.
2. Ensure you answer all required fields.
3. If you need help with any field, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)**: Your given name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name (2)**: Your surname as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date Of Birth (3)**: Your date of birth, in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Email (4)**: Your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number (5)**: Your contact phone number.
* **Address (6)**: Your current or permanent address.
* **Country Of Citizenship (7)**: Your country of origin or citizenship.
* **Preferred Language (8)**: Select the language you would like to use for communication.
* **Program Of Interest (9)**: Select the study abroad program you are interested in.
* **Highest Level Of Education (10)**: Select your highest level of education completed.
* **Previous Institution (11)**: Select your previous institution of study or work.
* **Current Residence (12)**: Select your current place of residence.
* **Country Of Residence (13)**: Select your current country of residence.
* **Passport Number (14)**: Your passport number (if applicable).
* **Passport Expiration Date (15)**: The expiration date of your passport (if applicable).
* **Passport Scan (16)**: Upload a scan of your passport page (if applicable).
* **Student Passport Image (17)**: Upload your student passport image (if applicable).
* **Visa Type (18)**: Select your current visa type (if applicable).
* **Visa Number (19)**: Enter your visa number (if applicable).
* **Visa Expiration Date (20)**: Enter the expiration date of your visa (if applicable).
* **Visa Scan (21)**: Upload a scan of your visa page (if applicable).
* **Student ID (22)**: Enter your student ID number (if applicable).
* **Student ID Scan (23)**: Upload a scan of your student ID (if applicable).
* **Student Card (24)**: Upload a scan of your student card (if applicable).
* **Signature (25)**: Sign your name for verification purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Use the language you select as your preferred language for communication purposes.
* Upload required documents such as passport scans and student ID scans as instructed.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the program guidelines or contact your program representative.
