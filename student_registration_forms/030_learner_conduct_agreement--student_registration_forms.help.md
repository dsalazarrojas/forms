# Learner Conduct Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Learner Conduct Agreement form is a crucial document that ensures learners understand and agree to specific conduct rules and guidelines in a learning environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. The first field, "LearnerConductAgreement," is a text field where you need to type or copy and paste your acknowledgement statement. Please describe the rules and guidelines you agree to follow during the learning process.
2. In the "AcknowledgeDate" field, select the date you have read and understood the conduct rules. This date should be in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. In the "AcknowledgeSignature" field, type your full name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., ID card).
4. In the "LearnerSignature" field, type your full name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., ID card).
5. In the "AcknowledgeDateSigned" field, select the date you signed the conduct agreement.
6. In the "AcknowledgeSigned" field, type "yes" to confirm that you have signed the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **LearnerConductAgreement**: This is a text field where you need to type or copy and paste your acknowledgement statement. Please describe the rules and guidelines you agree to follow during the learning process.
* **AcknowledgementDate**: Select the date you have read and understood the conduct rules. This date should be in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **AcknowledgementSignature**: Type your full name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., ID card).
* **LearnerSignature**: Type your full name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., ID card).
* **AcknowledgeDateSigned**: Select the date you signed the conduct agreement.
* **AcknowledgeSigned**: Type "yes" to confirm that you have signed the agreement.

## Tips
- Make sure to read and understand the conduct rules before completing the form.
- Ensure that your acknowledgement and signature are accurate and legible.
- If you are unsure about any part of the form, please ask your instructor or institution's administration for clarification.
- Remember to sign the agreement only after you have read and understood the conduct rules.
