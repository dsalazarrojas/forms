# student_information_form_dark_and_responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended to assist students in filling out the Student Information Form, which is used to collect demographic data, contact details, and academic credentials for registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant information for each field.
2. Enter your First Name and Last Name.
3. Enter your Student ID.
4. Select your birthday.
5. Enter your Phone Number.
6. Enter your Email address.
7. (Optional) Enter a Second Email address.
8. Enter your Address.
9. Select your Gender.
10. Enter your Birth Date and Birth Time.
11. Enter your Birth Place.
12. Enter your School Name.
13. Enter your Department Name.
14. (Optional) Select your Student Level.
15. Select your Student Status.
16. (Optional) Enter your Course Name and Code.
17. (Optional) Upload a Student Image.
18. (Optional) Enter your Faculty Name.
19. (Optional) Enter your Student Number and Faculty ID.
20. (Optional) Enter your Graduation Date and Graduate Degree.
21. (Optional) Enter your Academic Year.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it is displayed on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it is displayed on your official documents.
* **Student ID** (`3`, `number`, required): Enter your student ID as assigned by the institution.
* **Birthday** (`4`, `date`, required): Select your date of birth using the calendar format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Phone Number** (`5`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number as a series of digits.
* **Email** (`6`, `email`, required): Enter your primary email address.
* **Second Email** (`7`, `email`, optional): If you have an alternative email address, enter it here.
* **Address** (`8`, `note`, optional): Enter your home or office address.
* **Gender** (`9`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your gender from the provided options.
* **Birth Date** (`10`, `date`, optional): Select your birth date using the calendar format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Birth Time** (`11`, `time`, optional): Select your birth time in 12-hour format (e.g., 03:00 PM).
* **Birth Place** (`12`, `text`, optional): Enter the city, state, or country where you were born.
* **School Name** (`13`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your school or institution.
* **Department Name** (`14`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your department or faculty.
* **Student Level** (`15`, `select_one`, optional): Select your current student level from the provided options.
* **Student Status** (`16`, `select_one`, optional): Select your current student status from the provided options.
* **Course Name** (`17`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your course or program.
* **Course Code** (`18`, `text`, optional): Enter the course code or identification number.
* **Student Image** (`19`, `text`, optional): Upload a photo or image of yourself.
* **Faculty Name** (`20`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your faculty or department.
* **Student Number** (`21`, `text`, optional): Enter your student ID or number as assigned by the institution.
* **Faculty ID** (`22`, `number`, optional): Enter your faculty ID as assigned by the institution.
* **Graduation Date** (`23`, `date`, optional): Select your graduation date in the calendar format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Graduate Degree** (`24`, `text`, optional): Enter your graduate degree or field of study.
* **Academic Year** (`25`, `text`, optional): Enter the academic year of your program or degree.
