# small_group_leader_training_registration_form - Help Guide

This form is for students to register for small group leader training sessions. The training sessions are typically offered on specific dates and students must register with their full name, email, and phone number to secure their spot. The form also collects date of birth and additional notes about the student.

Before proceeding, please ensure that you are not already registered for this training session and that you have not filled this form previously.

The form is divided into multiple sections, each with a specific field that requires attention. Below is a step-by-step guide on how to complete this form correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field. This is not required but recommended for communication purposes.
4. If you have a date of birth, enter it in the "Date of Birth" field. This is not required but may be needed for specific training session prerequisites.
5. Select your preferred training session date from the "Training Session" dropdown menu.
6. If you have been assigned a tool, enter it in the "Assigned Tool" field. This is not required but may be relevant for training purposes.
7. If you have any additional notes, enter them in the "Additional Notes" field. This is not required but recommended for providing context.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for updates and information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for easier communication.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth if it's required for this training session.
* **Training Session** (`training_session`, `select_one`, required): Select the date of your preferred training session from the dropdown menu.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, optional): If you have been assigned a specific tool, enter it here.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional information that may be relevant for your training session.

## Tips

* Ensure that you are not already registered for this training session.
* Double-check that you have entered the correct information in each field.
* If you need help or have questions, contact your instructor or the training coordinator.

This form is designed to collect necessary information for your small group leader training session. Please complete it accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth experience.
