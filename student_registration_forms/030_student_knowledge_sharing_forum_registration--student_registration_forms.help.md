# Student Knowledge Sharing Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Knowledge Sharing Forum Registration form is designed to collect essential information from students to facilitate their participation in the Student Knowledge Sharing Forum.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your student ID number in the "Student ID" field.
5. Choose your school name from the provided options in the "School Name" field.
6. Select your department from the provided options in the "Department" field.
7. List your program name in the "Program Name" field.
8. Select your roles from the provided options in the "Role" field.
9. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
10. Enter your registration date in the "Registration Date" field.
11. Select your time zone from the provided options in the "Time Zone" field.
12. Add any additional notes about your participation in the "Forum Participation" field.
13. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
14. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
15. Select your student programs from the provided options in the "Student Programs" field.
16. Select your groups from the provided options in the "Student Groups" field.
17. Enter your language in the "Language" field.
18. Enter your location in the "Location" field.
19. Select whether you are in a time zone from the provided options in the "Time Zone" field.
20. Select your student status from the provided options in the "Student Status" field.
21. Enter the name of the forum you are participating in in the "Forum Name" field.
22. Enter the link to the forum in the "Forum Link" field.
23. Enter a description of your student program in the "Student Program Description" field.
24. Enter the email of your school in the "School Email" field.
25. Enter the phone number of your school in the "School Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Text)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name (Text)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your student email address where you can be reached.
* **Student ID (Number)**: Enter your student identification number.
* **School Name (Text)**: Enter the name of your school.
* **Department (Select)**: Select your department from the provided options.
* **Program Name (Text)**: Enter your academic program name.
* **Role (Select Multiple)**: Select your roles from the provided options.
* **Date of Birth (Date)**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Registration Date (Date)**: Enter your registration date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time Zone (Select)**: Select your time zone from the provided options.
* **Forum Participation (Note)**: Add any additional notes about your participation in the forum.
* **Contact Number (Text)**: Enter your contact number.
* **Address (Text)**: Enter your address.
* **Student Programs (Select Multiple)**: Select your student programs from the provided options.
* **Student Groups (Select Multiple)**: Select your student groups from the provided options.
* **Language (Text)**: Enter the language you are fluent in.
* **Location (Text)**: Enter your location.
* **Time Zone (Select)**: Select whether you are in a time zone from the provided options.
* **Student Status (Select)**: Select your current student status from the provided options.
* **Forum Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the forum you are participating in.
* **Forum Link (Text)**: Enter the link to the forum.
* **Student Program Description (Text)**: Enter a description of your student program.
* **School Email (Email)**: Enter the email of your school.
* **School Phone (Text)**: Enter the phone number of your school.
