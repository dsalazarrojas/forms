# student_id_card_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The student ID card registration form is designed to collect information from students to create an ID card for identification and verification purposes. This form should only be filled out by authorized students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your Student Name.
2. Enter your Student Email.
3. Upload your Student Photo (if required).
4. Enter your Student Phone.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents. (Student Name)
* **Student Email**: Enter your official student email address. (Student Email)
* **Student Photo**: Upload a recent, clear, and frontal photo of yourself. (Student Photo)
* **Student Phone**: Enter your student-issued phone number. (Student Phone)

## Tips
* Ensure that your photo is clear and well-lit.
* Make sure to enter your email address correctly to ensure that we can contact you.
* Enter your phone number as it appears on your student-issued ID.

Note: The fields are not required, but the student name and email are necessary for the ID card process to work correctly.
