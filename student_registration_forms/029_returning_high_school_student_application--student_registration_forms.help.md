# Returning High School Student Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Returning High School Student Application form is used to collect information about high school students who are reapplying for their enrollment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Enter the student's name as it appears on their official documents.
3. Provide the student's school name and address accurately.
4. Enter the student's grade level and start and end dates of their previous enrollment.
5. Select the student's current status (Active or Inactive).
6. Answer the question about the staff name and reason for reapplying.
7. Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name as it appears on their official documents. This is a required field.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the high school the student is reapplying from. This is a required field.
* **Grade**: If applicable, enter the student's grade level at the time of their previous enrollment. This field is not required.
* **Parent Email**: Enter the parent's email address. This is a required field.
* **Parent Phone**: Enter the parent's phone number. This is a required field.
* **Student ID**: Enter the student's unique identifier. This is a required field.
* **Student Address**: Enter the student's home address. This is a required field.
* **Student Grade Level**: Select the student's grade level at the time of their previous enrollment (High or Low). This field is not required.
* **Student Start Date**: Enter the date the student started their previous enrollment. This is a required field.
* **Student End Date**: Enter the date the student ended their previous enrollment. This is a required field.
* **Student Status**: Select the student's current status (Active or Inactive). This is a required field.
* **Staff Name**: Answer whether the student has any staff members who can vouch for their enrollment. This field is required.
* **Reason**: Provide a brief explanation for reapplying. This field is not required.
* **Notes**: If there's anything else you'd like to add about the student's reapplication, enter it here (optional).
