# Admission Form For Secondary School - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from parents/guardians for the admission process of their child to the secondary school. It includes details about the student's personal and academic background, medical conditions, and special needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields accurately.
2. Make sure to enter the correct format for dates (e.g., MM/DD/YY).
3. If you have any special needs or medical conditions, please provide detailed explanations.
4. Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent/Guardian Full Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Parent/Guardian Email**: Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address**: Enter the current residential address.
* **City**: Enter the city of residence.
* **State/Province**: Enter the state or province of residence.
* **ZIP/Postal Code**: Enter the postal code of residence.
* **Country**: Enter the country of residence.
* **Student Full Name**: Enter the student's full name.
* **Student Date of Birth**: Enter the student's birth date (MM/DD/YY).
* **Student Gender**: Select the student's gender (Male, Female, Other, Prefer not to say).
* **Grade Applying For**: Select the grade level the student is applying for.
* **Previous School**: Enter the name of the student's previous school.
* **Previous Grade Completed**: Select the grade level completed last year.
* **Academic Transcript Attached**: Select if the academic transcript is attached to this application.
* **Standardized Test Scores**: Enter any standardized test scores.
* **Special Educational Needs**: Select if the student has any special educational needs.
* **Special Needs Details**: Enter detailed explanations of any special needs.
* **Medical Conditions**: Select if the student has any medical conditions.
* **Medical Conditions Details**: Enter detailed explanations of any medical conditions.
* **Medications Required**: Select if the student requires any medications during school hours.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Enter the relationship to the student.
* **Transportation Needs**: Select the transportation needs.
* **Extracurricular Interests**: Enter any extracurricular activities the student is interested in.
* **Application Date**: Enter the date of submitting this application.
* **Parent/Guardian Signature**: Electronically sign to confirm the application.

Note: Please make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the school administration.
