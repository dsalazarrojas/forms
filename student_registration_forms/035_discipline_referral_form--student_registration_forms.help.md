# Discipline Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Discipline Referral Form is a document used by teachers to report incidents or behavior issues that require immediate attention. It is meant to provide a clear and concise report of the incident, ensuring that necessary actions are taken to address the situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1.  **Discipline Referral**: This is the main section where you can provide a brief description of the incident or behavior issue.
2.  **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student involved in the incident.
3.  **Student Identifier**: Enter any unique identifier associated with the student, such as their student ID number.
4.  **Teacher Name**: Enter your name as the referring teacher.
5.  **Date of Incident**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
6.  **Incident Type**: Describe the type of incident or behavior issue that occurred.
7.  **Description of Incident**: Provide a detailed description of what happened.
8.  **Student Response**: Allow the student to provide their response to the incident.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
*   Use the form for serious incidents or behavior issues that require immediate attention.
*   Review the form before submitting it to ensure all required information is included.
*   If you have already filled out a discipline referral form for the same student recently, review the previous form and update it instead of filling out a new one.
