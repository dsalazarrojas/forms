# Online Course Student Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Course Student Onboarding Form is used to register students for online courses. The form collects necessary information to onboard students into the course and provide a clear understanding of the course terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your student name and email address.
2. Select the online course you wish to enroll in.
3. Provide your payment information (if applicable).
4. Accept the course agreement.
5. Choose whether to submit your form or save it for later.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name to help us identify you as a student.
- **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter your active email address so we can contact you about course updates and notifications.
- **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with any urgent course updates.
- **Student Registration** (`student_registration`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of yourself or your background information.
- **Select Course** (`course_selection`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the available courses to enroll in.
- **Enrollment Date** (`enrollment_date`, date, optional): Select the date you plan to start the course.
- **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Select the date you plan to begin the course.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional): Choose a payment method that applies to you (e.g. Cash, PayPal, Bank Transfer).
- **Student Notes** (`student_notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or background information about yourself that is not covered in the previous fields.
- **Agreement** (`agreement`, number, optional): Accept or decline the course agreement.
- **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, optional): Choose whether to submit your form or save it for later.
