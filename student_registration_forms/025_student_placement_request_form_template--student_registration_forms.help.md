# Student Placement Request Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for students to request placement in a school or class, and for school staff to record and track the status of these requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select your school id from the list of options.
2. Enter your student name and student id.
3. Choose your grade level.
4. Select your student placement status (if you have already been placed or have another status).
5. Enter your school name (if applicable).
6. Choose your placement date (if applicable).
7. Select your teacher(s) who will be handling your placement request.
8. Select your class(es) you are requesting placement in.
9. Enter a comment or reason for your placement request.
10. Enter your teacher's comment (if applicable).
11. Enter your email address and phone number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### School Id (id: 1)
* School Id: **School Id** (`number`, required: false)
	+ This field is used to select the school where you are requesting placement.
### Student Name (id: 2)
* Student Name: **Student Name** (`text`, required: false)
	+ Enter your name as it is written on your official records.
### Grade Level (id: 3)
* Grade Level: **Grade Level** (`number`, required: false)
	+ Choose your current grade level (e.g., 9, 10, 11, etc.).
### Student Id (id: 4)
* Student Id: **Student Id** (`number`, required: false)
	+ Enter your student id (e.g., 123456).
### Placement Request Reason (id: 5)
* Placement Request Reason: **Placement Request Reason** (`text`, required: false)
	+ Enter a brief explanation or reason for your placement request.
### Student Placement Status (id: 6)
* Student Placement Status: **Student Placement Status** (`select_one`, required: false)
	+ Choose the status of your placement request (Pending, Accepted, Rejected, Placed, Withdrawn).
### School Name (id: 7)
* School Name: **School Name** (`text`, required: false)
	+ Enter the name of the school you are requesting placement in.
### Placement Date (id: 8)
* Placement Date: **Placement Date** (`date`, required: false)
	+ If applicable, enter the date of your placement request.
### Teacher (id: 9)
* Teacher: **Teacher** (`select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Select the teacher(s) who will be handling your placement request.
### Class (id: 10)
* Class: **Class** (`select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Select the class(es) you are requesting placement in.
### Student Comment (id: 11)
* Student Comment: **Student Comment** (`note`, required: false)
	+ Enter a comment or note about your placement request.
### Teacher Comment (id: 12)
* Teacher Comment: **Teacher Comment** (`note`, required: false)
	+ Enter a comment or note from your teacher.
### Student Email (id: 13)
* Student Email: **Student Email** (`email`, required: false)
	+ Enter your student email address.
### Student Phone (id: 14)
* Student Phone: **Student Phone** (`text`, required: false)
	+ Enter your student phone number.
### Class Name (id: 15)
* Class Name: **Class Name** (`text`, required: false)
	+ Enter the name of the class you are requesting placement in.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* You can always contact school staff if you have any questions or need help with your placement request.
* This form is used for internal tracking and reporting purposes only.
