# Preschool Initial Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Preschool Initial Application Form is used to gather essential information from parents or guardians about their child's enrollment in a preschool program. This information will help the preschool administration process admissions and tailor programs according to the child's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and the name of your child.
2. Enter your email address and phone number to enable us to contact you.
3. Choose your child's date of birth.
4. Indicate your child's gender.
5. Select your state and country of residence.
6. Choose the start and end dates of the preschool program (if applicable).
7. Fill in your address.
8. Enter your zip code and city.
9. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a parent or guardian.
* **Child Name** (`child_name`, text, required): Enter your child's name.
* **Child Birthday** (`child_birthday`, date, optional): Enter your child's date of birth, if applicable.
* **Child Gender** (`child_gender`, select one, required): Choose your child's gender.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Preschool Name** (`preschool_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the preschool your child is enrolling in.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address for our records.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city of your residence.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **State** (`state`, select one, required): Choose your state of residence.
* **Country** (`country`, select one, required): Choose your country of residence.
* **Preschool Start Date** (`preschool_start_date`, date, required): Select the start date of the preschool program.
* **Preschool End Date** (`preschool_end_date`, date, optional): Select the end date of the preschool program (if applicable).
