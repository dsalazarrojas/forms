# Roommate Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by students to apply to be a roommate in a shared housing arrangement. The purpose of this form is to gather information about each applicant's personal details, living preferences, and living arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details:
   * First name: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
   * Last name: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
   * Email: Enter your email address that you check regularly.
   * Phone: Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
   * Bio: Write a brief description about yourself to help us get to know you better.
2. Select your availability:
   * If you have a specific move-in date, enter it. If you don't, you can leave this field blank.
3. Choose your roommates:
   * Select all the roommates you will be living with from the provided options.
4. Choose your room type and size:
   * Room type: Choose how you would like to be listed (Private, Shared).
   * Room size: Choose the number of roommates you will be living with (Small, Medium, Large).
5. Answer questions about smoking and pets:
   * Smoking: Choose whether you smoke or not.
   * Pets: Choose whether you have pets or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
- **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
- **Email** (3, email, required): Enter your email address that you check regularly.
- **Phone** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
- **Why are you applying for this house** (5, text, required): Write a brief description about yourself to help us get to know you better.
- **When are you available** (6, date, optional): If you have a specific move-in date, enter it. If you don't, you can leave this field blank.
- **Move-in Date** (7, date, required): Enter the date you will be moving into the house.
- **Move-out Date** (8, date, optional): If you have a specific move-out date, enter it. If you don't, you can leave this field blank.
- **Roommates** (9, select multiple, required): Select all the roommates you will be living with from the provided options.
- **Room type** (10, select one, required): Choose how you would like to be listed (Private, Shared).
- **Room size** (11, select one, required): Choose the number of roommates you will be living with (Small, Medium, Large).
- **Smoking** (12, select one, required): Choose whether you smoke or not.
- **Pets** (13, select one, required): Choose whether you have pets or not.
