# College Application Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from applicants interested in attending our college. It will help us get to know you better and understand your interests. Please provide accurate information to ensure a smooth application process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on each page to proceed to the next section of the form.
- Answer each field according to the guidance provided below.
- Submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your identification or government records.
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your identification or government records.
- **Email Address**: Provide your preferred contact email where we can reach you.
- **Phone Number**: Enter your mobile or landline number.
- **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YY format (e.g., 07/12/1990).
- **Gender Identity**: Select the option that best describes your identity. Please choose from the provided options.
- **Current Address**: Enter your current address as it is written on your identification or government records.
- **City**: Enter the city where you currently reside.
- **State or Province**: Enter the state or province where you currently reside.
- **Postal Code**: Enter your postal code as it is written on your identification or government records.
- **School or College of Interest**: Select the field of study that interests you the most. Please choose from the provided options.
- **Degree Level Sought**: Select the degree level you are interested in. Please choose from the provided options.
- **Preferred Start Date**: Select the semester or year you would like to start.
- **Highest Level of Education Completed**: Select the highest level of education you have completed. Please choose from the provided options.
- **Current or Previous GPA**: Enter your current or previous GPA on the 4.0 scale.
- **Standardized Test Results Submitted**: If you have taken any standardized tests (e.g., SAT, ACT, GRE), select 'Yes', otherwise choose 'No' or 'Plan to submit'.
- **English as Second Language**: If you are not a native English speaker, select 'Yes', otherwise choose 'No'.
- **Require Accessibility Accommodations**: If you have any accessibility needs, select 'Yes', otherwise choose 'No'.
- **Accommodation Details**: If you selected 'Yes' for the above option, provide details about your needs.
- **Interested in Financial Aid Information**: If you would like to know more about financial aid, select 'Yes', otherwise choose 'No'.
- **International Student Status**: Select the status that best describes you. Please choose from the provided options.
- **How did you hear about our college**: Select all the ways you found out about our college. You can select multiple options.
- **Additional Information or Comments**: If you would like to provide any additional information or comments, please do so.
