# Student Allergy Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Allergy Registration Form is a vital document that helps the school administration provide necessary support and accommodations for students with allergies. This form collects information about a student's allergies, medical conditions, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Student Allergy Registration Form accurately and effectively, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the **Student Allergy Registration** section (page 1). Please provide your name, and any other relevant personal information in this section.
2. Move on to the **Allergy Type** section (page 2). Select one of the allergy types listed, such as Allergic to food, Allergic to insect stings, Allergic to latex, or Allergic to medication.
3. If you have a medication allergy, select the medications that you are allergic to in the **Medication Allergy** section (page 3).
4. Check the **Food Allergy** section (page 4) to list any food allergies you may have, such as Peanuts, Tree Nuts, Fish, or Soy.
5. If you have an insect sting allergy, select the specific insect that triggers your allergy in the **Insect Sting Allergy** section (page 5).
6. Check the **Other Food Allergies** section (page 6) for any other food allergies you may have.
7. In the **Latex Allergy** section (page 7), select Natural Latex or Synthetic Latex if you are allergic to either.
8. In the **Other Allergies** section (page 8), provide any other allergies or medical conditions you may have.
9. If applicable, fill out the **Additional Information** section (page 9) with any relevant notes or comments.
10. Finally, provide your contact information, including Email and Phone number, in the last section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Allergy Registration** (page 1): This section is to provide your name and any other relevant personal information.
* **Allergy Type** (page 2): Select one of the listed allergy types that applies to you.
* **Medication Allergy** (page 3): If you are allergic to medications, select the ones that trigger your allergy.
* **Food Allergy** (page 4): List any food allergies you may have, such as Peanuts, Tree Nuts, Fish, or Soy.
* **Insect Sting Allergy** (page 5): Select the specific insect that triggers your allergy.
* **Other Food Allergies** (page 6): Provide any other food allergies you may have.
* **Latex Allergy** (page 7): Select Natural Latex or Synthetic Latex if you are allergic to either.
* **Other Allergies** (page 8): Provide any other allergies or medical conditions you may have.
* **Additional Information** (page 9): Fill out this section with any relevant notes or comments.
* **Email** (page 10): Enter your Email address.
* **Phone** (page 11): Enter your Phone number.

## Tips
Please take your time to fill out this form accurately and thoroughly. If you are unsure about any field or section, do not hesitate to ask for assistance.
