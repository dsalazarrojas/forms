# Open Ended Student Response Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Open Ended Student Response Form is a tool used to collect responses and reflections from students. This form is used by teachers to obtain insights into student perspectives and thoughts. It can also be used by students to provide feedback and share their thoughts and reflections on various topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required fields (marked as required).
2. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
3. Enter your Email and Phone number for contact purposes.
4. Provide your Date of Birth.
5. Select your Gender.
6. Enter your Student ID.
7. Choose your Grade Level.
8. Enter the subject area you are responding to.
9. Enter your School Name.
10. Write a brief Student Reflection.
11. Provide Teacher Comments.
12. Enter your School Official Email.
13. Enter the name and Title of your Teacher.
14. Enter the name and Title of any additional Teachers involved.
15. Enter the School Name (if different from the previous School Name field).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your Email address that you want to be reached at.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your Date of Birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Gender**: Select your Gender as Male, Female, or Other.
* **Student ID**: Enter your student ID number (if applicable).
* **Grade Level**: Choose your grade level from the options provided.
* **Subject Area**: Enter the subject area you are responding to.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school you attend.
* **Student Reflection**: Provide a brief reflection or response to the question.
* **Teacher Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback from your teacher.
* **School Official Email**: Enter the school official's email address.
* **Teacher**: Enter the name and Title of your teacher.
* **Title**: Enter your teacher's title.
* **Teacher 1**: Enter the name and Title of any additional Teachers involved.
* **Title 1**: Enter the title of the additional Teacher.
* **Teacher 2**: Enter the name and Title of any additional Teachers involved.
* **Title 2**: Enter the title of the additional Teacher.
* **School Name 2 (optional)**: Enter the name of the school (if different from the previous School Name field).
* **Student Response**: This field is for any additional comments or responses from the student.
* **Grade Level**: Choose your grade level from the options provided.
* **Teacher Name**: Enter the name of your teacher.
* **Teacher Title**: Enter your teacher's title.
* **Teacher 1 Name**: Enter the name of any additional teacher involved.
* **Title 1**: Enter the title of the additional teacher.
* **Teacher 2 Name**: Enter the name of any additional teacher involved.
* **Title 2**: Enter the title of the additional teacher.
* **School**: Enter the name of the school you attend.

Note: The fields marked as required must be filled out.
