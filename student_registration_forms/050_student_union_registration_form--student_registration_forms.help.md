# Student Union Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for students to register themselves with the student union, providing basic information required for the union's records and relevant communication.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read this guide carefully before starting the form.
- Fill in your details accurately and completely.
- If you need to add any additional comments or information, use the 'note' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name**: <name> - Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
- **email**: <email> - Enter your valid email address for the student union to contact you.
- **date_of_birth**: <date> - Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
- **program**: <program> - Select the level of your academic program (Undergraduate, Graduate, Postgraduate, etc.).
- **semester**: <semester> - Select your current academic semester (if applicable).
- **campus**: <campus> - Select the campus you belong to (if applicable).
- **phone**: <phone> - Enter your contact phone number for emergency or additional contact purposes.
- **note**: <note> - Use this field to add any relevant additional comments or information not covered by other fields.
