# new_international_student_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new international student application form is designed to collect personal and contact information from international students who are applying for programs or services offered by the institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the personal details section with your first and last name.
2. Provide your contact information, including a valid email address and phone number.
3. Enter your housing information, if applicable.
4. List any required documents you need to submit.
5. Acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms of the application.
6. Select your nationality.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First and last name**: Please enter your complete name, including your first and last names.
* **Contact details**: Enter your current contact information, including a valid email address and phone number.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **Phone number**: Enter a valid phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Housing information**: If you have a housing arrangement, provide the address and details of your accommodations.
* **List of documents**: Please list all required documents you need to submit with your application.
* **Acknowledgement**: Please select "Yes" to confirm that you have read and understood the terms of the application.
* **Required documents**: Please list any additional documents you need to submit with your application.
* **Nationality**: Select your country of origin from the provided list.
