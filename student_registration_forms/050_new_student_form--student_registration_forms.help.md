# New Student Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The New Student Form is a tool used to collect and store student's information for academic purposes. This form helps the school administration to register new students and add them to the database.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To fill out the form, start by clicking on the "New Student Form" link, either on the school's website or through any other authorized channel.
2. Review the form carefully to understand what information is required from you.
3. Click on each field to access its input options.
4. Fill in the required information for each field.
5. Once you have completed the form, submit it to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Student Information (Page 1)
* **Student Information (student_info)**: This field is used to collect the student's personal details. Please enter your full name, student ID, and other relevant information as prompted.

### Course Registration (Page 2)
* **Course Registration (course_registration)**: This field is used to register the student for a specific course. Please enter the course code, course name, or any other relevant details as required.

### Contact Information (Page 3)
* **Contact Information (contact_info)**: This field is used to collect the student's contact details. Please enter your address, phone number, and email address as prompted.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid any issues with registration.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all required information is correct.
* If you encounter any issues during the form submission process, contact the school administration for assistance.
