# Phd Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PhD Admission Form is designed to collect information from students who are interested in PhD programs at University A, University B, or University C. This form is used to streamline the application process and ensure that all necessary information is provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the university where you are interested in pursuing your PhD.
3. Provide your current GPA.
4. Choose the research areas that interest you.
5. Select a date and time to submit your application.
6. Once you have completed all the fields, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **University** (`University`, select_one, required: false): Select the university where you are interested in pursuing your PhD. Please choose one of the following options: University A, University B, or University C.
* **Degree** (`Degree`, text, required: false): Enter your current degree level (Bachelor's, Master's, etc.).
* **GPA** (`GPA`, number, required: false): Enter your current GPA.
* **Research Area** (`Research Area`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the research areas that interest you. Please select all that apply.
* **Submit Date** (`Submit Date`, date, required: false): Select a date to submit your application.
* **Submit Time** (`Submit Time`, time, required: false): Select a time to submit your application.
