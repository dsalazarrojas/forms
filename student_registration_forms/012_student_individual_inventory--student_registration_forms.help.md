# Student Individual Inventory - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Student Individual Inventory" form is designed to collect essential student data, including student ID, name, and academic status. This form helps in maintaining accurate records and facilitates easier tracking of students' progress.

## How To Complete This Form

### To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the student's **Student Id**. This is a unique identifier for the student.
2.  Enter the student's **Student Name** in the text field provided.
3.  Select the correct **Vocation** for the student from the available options ('Yes' or 'No').
4.  If applicable, enter the student's **Student Vocation** in the text field.
5.  Choose the student's **Student Program** ('Yes' or 'No').
6.  Select the student's **Student Course** ('Yes' or 'No').
7.  Choose the student's **Student Level** (High or Low).
8.  Select the student's **Student Status** (Active or Inactive).
9.  Choose the student's **Student Year** ('Yes' or 'No').
10. Select the student's **Student Section** ('Yes' or 'No').

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Id (1)** (`number`, required: false): Enter the student's unique identifier.
*   **Student Name (2)** (`text`, required: false): Enter the student's name.
*   **Vocation (3)** (`select_one`, required: false): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the student has a vocation.
*   **Student Vocation (4)** (`text`, required: false): Enter the student's vocation if they have one.
*   **Student Program (5)** (`select_one`, required: false): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the student is in a program.
*   **Student Course (6)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each course the student is enrolled in.
*   **Student Level (7)** (`select_one`, required: false): Choose High or Low to indicate the student's level.
*   **Student Status (8)** (`select_one`, required: false): Select Active or Inactive to indicate the student's status.
*   **Student Year (9)** (`select_one`, required: false): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the student is in their first year.
*   **Student Section (10)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each section the student is enrolled in.

## Tips

*   Make sure to enter accurate information for each field, as this will ensure that the student's records are up-to-date and accurate.
*   If you're unsure about any field, please refer to your institution's policies or consult with the relevant authorities.
*   This form is designed to be user-friendly and easy to navigate. If you have any issues or need help, please do not hesitate to contact us.
