# course_planning_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect course planning information from instructors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with clear and concise text.
2. Ensure you're filling out the correct information and in the correct context.
3. Don't worry if you're unsure about any field - it's better to ask for clarification than to leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Objectives** (`course_objectives`, text, optional): This field is for instructors to describe the course objectives, which should be clear and concise statements about what students will learn or achieve.
* **Lesson Plans** (`lesson_plans`, text, optional): This field is for instructors to outline their lesson plans, which should include a general idea of how the course will be structured and presented.
* **Resources** (`resources`, text, optional): This field is for instructors to list any resources they plan to use or recommend for the course, such as textbooks, websites, or other materials.
* **Instructors** (`instructors`, text, optional): This field is for instructors to provide their name and contact information, which should be accurate and up-to-date.
* **Description** (`course_description`, text, optional): This field is for instructors to describe the course in a few sentences, providing an overview of its content and scope.
* **Student Regsitrations** (`student_registrations`, text, optional): This field is for instructors to record any student registrations, which should include student names, IDs, or other relevant details.

## Tips
* Be sure to proofread your answers for accuracy and clarity.
* If you're unsure about any field, it's better to ask for clarification than to leave it blank.
* Remember that this form is for course planning, not student registration.
