# Student Organization College Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register students for a college organization or club, collecting essential information for administrative and logistical purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field.
4. Confirm your email address by re-entering it in the Confirm Email field.
5. Enter your Student ID number in the Student ID field.
6. Enter the name of the college you are registering for in the College Name field.
7. Check the Committee field to specify whether you are a committee member (if applicable).
8. Select your Role within the organization (Student, Member, or Leader).
9. For additional roles within the organization (like President, Vice President, Secretary, or Treasurer), select from the list.
10. If applicable, enter the start and end dates for your term in the Start Date and End Date fields.
11. Enter any additional notes about your registration in the Notes field.
12. Finally, review your form to ensure all information is accurate before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name as it is listed on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter your last name as it is listed on your official documents.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address that we will use to contact you.
* **Confirm Email** (Confirm Email, email, required): Re-enter your email address to verify it.
* **Student ID** (Student ID, text, required): Enter your student ID number.
* **College Name** (College Name, text, required): Enter the name of the college you are registering for.
* **Committee** (Committee, select_multiple, optional): Check if you are a member of any committees.
* **Role** (Role, select_one, required): Select your role within the organization (Student, Member, or Leader).
* **Role 2** (Role 2, select_one, optional): Select an additional role within the organization, such as President, Vice President, Secretary, or Treasurer.
* **Term** (Term, time, required): Enter the date when your term starts.
* **Start Date** (Start Date, date, optional): Enter the start date of your term.
* **End Date** (End Date, date, optional): Enter the end date of your term.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your registration.
* **Submit** (Submit, text, optional): Enter your submission for review.
* **Confirm Submission** (Confirm Submission, text, optional): Re-enter your submission for review.
* **Review** (Review, text, optional): Enter any review or comments about your submission.
