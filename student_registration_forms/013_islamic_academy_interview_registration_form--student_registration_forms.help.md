# Islamic Academy Interview Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Islamic Academy Interview Registration Form is a mandatory form to be completed by applicants who wish to register for an interview session. This form captures essential information to ensure each applicant meets the minimum requirements and eligibility criteria for the interview session.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure you have an active internet connection.
- Read all instructions carefully to avoid missing any required fields.
- Complete all the required fields.
- Review the form before submitting to ensure accuracy.
- Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Basic Info
* **Basic Info** (`basic_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of yourself.
* **Applicant Info** (`applicant_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the applicant.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter the contact details of the applicant.
* **Education History** (`education_history`, text, optional): Enter the education history of the applicant.
* **References** (`references`, text, optional): Enter any relevant references for the applicant.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the applicant.

### Verification
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the applicant's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, number, required): Enter the applicant's phone number.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, select_one, required): Confirm that the email address is correct.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, select_one, required): Confirm that the phone number is correct.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the date of birth of the applicant.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, required): Enter the time of birth of the applicant.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Confirm the gender of the applicant.
* **Nationality** (`nationality`, select_one, required): Confirm the nationality of the applicant.
* **Marital Status** (`marital_status`, select_one, required): Confirm the marital status of the applicant.
* **Religion** (`religion`, select_one, required): Confirm the religion of the applicant.
* **Language** (`language`, select_one, required): Confirm the language of the applicant.

### Upload Documents
* **Upload Certificate** (`upload_certificate`, text, required): Upload a copy of the applicant's certificate.
* **Upload Document** (`upload_document`, text, required): Upload a copy of a document relevant to the applicant.
* **Upload Passport Copy** (`upload_passport_copy`, text, required): Upload a copy of the applicant's passport.
* **Upload National ID** (`upload_national_id`, text, required): Upload a copy of the applicant's national ID.

### Signature
* **Signature** (`signature`, select_one, required): Confirm that the applicant's information is accurate.
