# Primary School Pre-Registration Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The primary purpose of this form is to collect information from parents or guardians for a primary school student's pre-registration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully and fill out the required information for your child.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
3. If you are enrolling your child in the school for the first time, you will need to fill out the entire form. If you have already enrolled your child in the school, you can skip some sections and provide the necessary documents directly to the school administration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Details** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This section is for your child's basic information such as name, date of birth, and student ID number.
* **Parent Details** (`2`, `text`, required: false): This section is for your contact information and address.
* **Guardian Details** (`3`, `text`, required: false): This section is for your spouse's contact information and address.
* **Medical Certification** (`4`, `number`, required: false): This section is for any medical certifications or special needs your child may have.
* **Medical Record** (`5`, `text`, required: false): This is a brief summary of your child's medical history.
* **Student Status** (`6`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select whether your child is a new or existing student.
* **Admission Status** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select whether your child is admitted or not admitted to the school.
* **Student Type** (`8`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the type of student your child is, whether day scholar, boarder, or other.
* **Student Type Other** (`9`, `text`, required: false): If your child is of a type that is not listed, please provide a brief description.
* **Student Address** (`10`, `text`, required: false): This is your child's address.
* **Student Address Line 2** (`11`, `text`, required: false): This is the second line of your child's address.
* **Postal Code** (`12`, `number`, required: false): This is the postal code of your child's address.
* **Contact Number** (`13`, `text`, required: false): This is your contact number.
* **Email** (`14`, `email`, required: false): This is your email.
* **Emergency Contact** (`15`, `text`, required: false): This is the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`16`, `text`, required: false): This is the emergency contact number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`17`, `email`, required: false): This is the emergency contact email.
* **Student Document** (`18`, `text`, required: false): This is where you can provide any supporting documents for your child's registration.
* **Medical Report** (`19`, `text`, required: false): This is any additional medical information for your child.
* **Birth Certificate** (`20`, `text`, required: false): This is your child's birth certificate.
* **Student Image** (`21`, `text`, required: false): This is your child's image.
* **Signature** (`22`, `text`, required: false): This is the parent or guardian's signature.
* **Student Signature** (`23`, `text`, required: false): This is your child's signature (if applicable).
* **Date** (`24`, `date`, required: false): The date of completion of this form.
* **Time** (`25`, `time`, required: false): The time of completion of this form.
