To create a user-facing help guide for the Course Registration Form IICST, we will follow these steps:

1.  **Form Purpose**:  The form is used for students to register for courses.
2.  **How To Complete This Form**:  To complete this form, follow these steps:
    *   Enter the name of the course you wish to register for in the "Course Name" field.
    *   Select the semester you wish to register for in the "Semester" field.
    *   Enter your name in the "Student Name" field.
    *   Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
    *   Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
    *   Enter the course id in the "Course ID" field.
    *   Enter the course code in the "Course Code" field.
    *   Enter the course title in the "Course Title" field.
    *   Enter the date you registered for the course in the "Student Registration Date" field.
    *   Enter the start date of the course in the "Course Start Date" field.
    *   Enter the end date of the course in the "Course End Date" field.
    *   Enter the start date of your course in the "Student Start Date" field.
    *   Enter the end date of your course in the "Student End Date" field.
    *   Select your status in the "Status" field.
    *   Enter the number of hours you plan to dedicate to the course in the "Course Hours" field.
    *   Enter the ECTS (European Credit Transfer System) points for the course in the "Course ECTS" field.
    *   Enter the duration of the course in the "Course Duration" field.
    *   Select your level of experience with the course subject in the "Course Level" field.
    *   Select your level of experience with the course subject in the "Student Level" field.
    *   Enter your student id in the "Student ID" field.
    *   Enter any additional names of students registering for the course in the "Student Name 2", "Student Name 3", "Student Name 4", and "Student Name 5" fields.
    *   Enter any additional information about the student(s) registering for the course in the "Student Name 6" field.
3.  **Field-by-Field Explanation**:  Below is a detailed explanation of each field in the form.

**Field-by-Field Explanation**

*   **1.  Course Name**: This is the name of the course you wish to register for. Enter the name of the course exactly as it is displayed.
*   **2.  Semester**: This field is used to select the semester you wish to register for.
*   **3.  Student Name**: This is your name, and you should enter it exactly as it is displayed on your student ID.
*   **4.  Email**: Enter your email address to be used for course-related communications.
*   **5.  Phone**: Enter your phone number for course-related contact.
*   **6.  Course ID**: Enter the id of the course you are registering for.
*   **7.  Course Code**: Enter the code of the course you are registering for.
*   **8.  Course Title**: Enter the title of the course you are registering for.
*   **9.  Student Registration Date**: Enter the date you registered for the course.
*   **10.  Course Start Date**: Enter the start date of the course you are registering for.
*   **11.  Course End Date**: Enter the end date of the course you are registering for.
*   **12.  Student Start Date**: Enter your start date of the course you are registering for.
*   **13.  Student End Date**: Enter your end date of the course you are registering for.
*   **14.  Status**: Select your status as a student. This could be Active, Inactive, or Pending.
*   **15.  Course Hours**: Enter the number of hours you plan to dedicate to the course.
*   **16.  Course ECTS**: Enter the ECTS points for the course.
*   **17.  Course Duration**: Enter the duration of the course.
*   **18.  Course Level**: Select your level of experience with the course subject. This could be Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
*   **19.  Student Level**: Select your level of experience with the course subject. This could be Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
*   **20.  Student ID**: Enter your student ID.
*   **21.  Student Name 2**: Enter any additional name of a student registering for the course.
*   **22.  Student Name 3**: Enter any additional name of a student registering for the course.
*   **23.  Student Name 4**: Enter any additional name of a student registering for the course.
*   **24.  Student Name 5**: Enter any additional name of a student registering for the course.
*   **25.  Student Name 6**: Enter any additional information about the student(s) registering for the course.

**Tips**: Please make sure to enter all required fields and accurate information. If you are unsure about any field, please ask your instructor or refer to the course syllabus for more information. Also, please make sure to review all fields before submission to avoid any errors.
