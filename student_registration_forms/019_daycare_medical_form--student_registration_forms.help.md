# Daycare Medical Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect sensitive medical information about the student, which will be kept confidential and only used for emergency purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out your student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter your student's date of birth in the "Student DOB" field in MM/DD/YYYY format.
3. Select your student's gender from the options provided in the "Student Gender" field.
4. List any allergies your student has in the "Student Allergies" field, selecting multiple options as necessary.
5. Enter your name in the "Contact Name" field.
6. Specify your relationship to the student in the "Contact Relationship" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Contact Phone" field in the format "XXX-XXX-XXXX".
8. Enter the address of the emergency contact in the "Contact Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your student's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Student DOB** (`student_dob`, date, required): Enter your student's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/01/1999).
* **Student Gender** (`student_gender`, select_one, required): Select your student's gender from the options provided.
* **Student Allergies** (`student_allergies`, select_multiple, required): List any allergies your student has, including peanuts, eggs, and dairy products.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the emergency contact for the student.
* **Contact Relationship** (`contact_relationship`, select_one, required): Specify your relationship to the student (e.g., mother, father, other).
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter a valid phone number for emergency contact.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the emergency contact, including street, city, state, and zip code.
