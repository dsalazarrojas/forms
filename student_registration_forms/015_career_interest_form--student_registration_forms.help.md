# Career Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Career Interest Form is designed to help students identify their career interests and goals, allowing them to reflect on their aspirations and provide information about their background, skills, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the "Student Information" section, which includes your name, ID, and date of birth.
2.  In the "Career Interests" section, select your top three career paths from the list provided.
3.  In the "Skills and Experience" section, list any relevant coursework, training, or work experience.
4.  In the "Career Goals" section, describe your desired salary range, preferred work environment, and specific career or job title.
5.  In the "Preferences" section, choose your preferred work environment, values, and geographic location for work.
6.  Finally, add any additional information you feel is relevant to your career goals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Name** (`student_name`): Enter your full name.
*   **Student ID** (`student_id`): Enter your student ID number.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YY`.
*   **Grade Level** (`grade_level`): Select your current grade level or level of study.
*   **School or University Name** (`school_name`): Enter the name of your school or university.
*   **Current Major or Intended Major** (`current_major`): Enter your current or intended major.
*   **Primary Career Interests** (`primary_career_interests`): Select up to three career paths that interest you the most.
*   **Specific Career or Job Title** (`specific_career`): Enter the specific career or job title you are interested in.
*   **Career Motivation** (`career_motivation`): Describe why you are interested in this career.
*   **Key Skills and Strengths** (`key_skills`): Select any skills or strengths you have that are relevant to your career goals.
*   **Work or Volunteer Experience** (`work_experience`): Briefly describe any work or volunteer experience you have.
*   **Relevant Coursework or Training** (`relevant_coursework`): Describe any relevant coursework or training you have completed.
*   **Interest in Professional Certifications** (`certification_interest`): List any professional certifications you are interested in pursuing.
*   **Desired Salary Range** (`salary_expectations`): Select your desired salary range.
*   **Preferred Work Environment** (`work_environment`): Select any environment in which you prefer to work.
*   **What Matters Most to You in a Career?** (`work_values`): Select up to three values that matter most to you in a career.
*   **Geographic Work Preferences** (`geographic_preference`): Select your preferred geographic location for work.
*   **Preferred Role or Position Level** (`role_position`): Select your preferred role or position level.
*   **Plans for Further Education** (`further_education`): Describe any plans for further education.
*   **Concerns or Challenges About This Career** (`career_concerns`): Describe any concerns or challenges you have about this career.
*   **Interested in Career Mentorship?** (`mentor_interest`): Indicate your interest in career mentorship.
*   **Additional Information About Your Career Goals** (`additional_information`): Provide any additional information about your career goals.

## Tips

*   Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
*   Use the space provided to provide detailed explanations.
*   You can always ask for help if you are unsure about any section.
*   Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
