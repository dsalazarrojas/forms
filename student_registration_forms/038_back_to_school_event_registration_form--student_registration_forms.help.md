# Back To School Event Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect information from parents or guardians to register their child for a back to school event. This event is designed to help students reconnect with teachers, staff, and peers at the start of a new school year. The event will offer various activities, such as orientation, a curriculum fair, meet the teacher sessions, and a clubs fair. 

To avoid duplicate registrations, we will only accept one registration per student. 

Please ensure the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date, as it will be used to create a student profile and ensure their participation in the event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) in the title.
2. Select the sessions the student will be attending from the options provided.
3. Provide any accommodations or supports the student may need.
4. Enter the name and contact information for the emergency contact.
5. If the student will be picked up from school, enter the pickup arrangements.
6. Check the box to indicate consent for photos and media.
7. Select the date of registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent name**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
* **Student name**: Enter the full name of the student.
* **Grade level**: Enter the student's grade level.
* **Contact email**: Enter the email address where event updates will be sent. (Optional)
* **Sessions attending**: Select the sessions the student will be attending (e.g., Orientation, Curriculum fair, Meet the teacher, Clubs fair).
* **Any accommodations needed**: List any accommodations or supports the student may need, such as accessibility requirements.
* **Emergency contact name and phone**: Enter the name and phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Pickup arrangements**: Enter the name of the person or organization that will be picking up the student. (Optional)
* **Consent for photos and media**: Check the box to indicate if the student can be photographed or recorded during the event.
* **Registration date**: Enter the date the student is registered for the event. (Note: This field is automatically populated based on the current date.)
