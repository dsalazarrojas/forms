# nxt_gen_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for students to register and provide necessary information for a program. It helps us better understand who is participating and tailor our support and services to meet their needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Participant Details section with your basic information, including your email and phone number.
2. Check the Participant Interests section to select how you can be reached during the program.
3. Choose your preferred option in the Participant Consent section.
4. Enter your school name and contact information in the School Info section, if required.
5. Provide your Date of Birth and time zone in the Date of Birth and Time sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required): Enter your basic information, including your name, as required.
* **Participant Interests** (`interests`, select_multiple, required): Please select how you can be reached during the program.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you are interested in regular communication and updates.
	+ Select 'No' if you are not interested.
* **Participant Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred option for communication and participation.
	+ Option 1: You are willing to communicate and participate in the program.
	+ Option 2: You are not willing to communicate and participate in the program.
* **School Info** (`school_info`, text, optional): Enter your school name and contact information, if required.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Date of Birth** (`date`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth to provide demographic information.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter your time zone for scheduling purposes.

## Tips

* Please ensure you complete all required fields to ensure accurate capture of information.
* Use a valid email address and phone number to facilitate communication.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the program administrators.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid any errors. 

Please note that the fields marked as optional can be left blank if not applicable to you.
