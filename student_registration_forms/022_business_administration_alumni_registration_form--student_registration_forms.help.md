# Business Administration Alumni Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from graduates of the Business Administration program to help us stay in touch and provide opportunities for mentorship, professional development, and volunteering.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary information ready before starting.
2. Fill in all required fields (marked as such).
3. Double-check your information for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (2, text, required): This is your full name as it appeared at graduation.
* **Maiden or Previous Name** (3, text, required): If different from your current name.
* **Date of Birth** (4, date, required): Your birth date.
* **Gender** (5, select_one, required): Your gender (Male, Female, Non-Binary, etc.)
* **Email Address** (7, email, required): Your current email.
* **Phone Number** (8, text, required): Your contact number.
* **Program Name** (15, select_one, required): Which program did you complete (e.g., Bachelor of Business Administration).
* **Graduation Year** (16, number, required): When did you graduate.
* **Concentration or Major** (17, select_one, required): Your area of specialization.
* **Current Employer** (19, text, required): Name of your organization.
* **Current Job Title** (20, text, required): Your position.
* **Industry** (21, select_one, required): Your industry sector.
* **Years of Experience** (22, select_one, required): Total years of professional experience.
* **LinkedIn Profile** (23, text, required): URL to your LinkedIn profile.
* **Alumni Engagement** (24, note, required): How would you like to stay connected (e.g., newsletter, events).
* **Subscribe to Alumni Newsletter** (25, select_one, required): Would you like to receive our newsletter.
* **Mentorship Interest** (26, select_one, required): Would you like to participate in mentorship (as a mentor, mentee, or both).
* **Event Interest** (27, select_multiple, required): Types of events you are interested in.
* **Volunteering Interest** (28, select_one, required): Would you like to volunteer.
* **Additional Comments** (29, text, required): Any other information you would like to share.

Note: If a field has a list of options, you can only select one or more from those options, depending on the field type. 

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
* Be honest and accurate when filling this form.
* This form should not be used for multiple submissions by the same person. 
* If you've already submitted your information, please do not submit again.
