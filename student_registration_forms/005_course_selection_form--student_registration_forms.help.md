# Course Selection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Selection Form is designed to help students select courses for their academic program. This form will guide you through the process of selecting courses that align with your interests and academic goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred courses from the list provided on each page. You can select multiple courses on some pages.
2. If required, select a specific course from the list provided on each page.
3. Enter your preferred time for managing your course schedule.
4. If applicable, provide your email address for receiving notifications.
5. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
6. Write a note if you have any additional information or comments.
7. Review and submit the form when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Selection** (`course_selection`, select_multiple, required: false): Select up to 2 courses that you are interested in from the options provided.
* **Select Course** (`select_course`, select_one, required: false): Select one course that you are interested in from the options provided.
* **Manage Time** (`time_management`, date, required: false): Enter the date and time you prefer to manage your course schedule.
* **Manage Projects** (`project_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you prefer to manage your projects.
* **Receive Email** (`email_notification`, email, required: false): Enter your email address to receive notifications about your courses.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Write a note if you have any additional information or comments.
* **Note 2** (`note_2`, note, required: false): This is another note field where you can provide additional information or comments.
* **Course Selection 2** (`course_selection_form_10`, select_multiple, required: false): Select up to 2 courses from the options provided. 
* **Select Course 1** (`select_course_form_1`, select_one, required: false): Select one course from the options provided.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when selecting courses to ensure accurate representation of your preferences.
* Make sure to review your selections before submitting the form.
* If you need assistance with any field, please don't hesitate to ask an admin or support staff.
