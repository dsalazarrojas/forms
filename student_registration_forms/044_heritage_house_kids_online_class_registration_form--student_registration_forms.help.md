# Heritage House Kids Online Class Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect relevant information from students to ensure they are registered correctly for online classes. It helps us understand your student's needs and preferences to provide the best possible experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your Student Name, email address, and age in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your phone number and the parents' name in the respective fields.
3. Choose the student's language from the select_one options.
4. Select the class interest(s) from the given options.
5. Choose the parents' work status and education level from the select_one options.
6. Select the student's education level.
7. Choose the class date and time from the date and time fields.
8. Enter the class duration in minutes.
9. Select the class frequency from the options provided.
10. Enter the student's date of birth (DOB) in the correct format.
11. Optionally, enter any note or message for the parents or students.
12. Select the payment method and payment date.
13. Optionally, enter the class location and language.
14. Select the folklore and poems for the class.
15. Select the songs to be included in the class.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (1)**: Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Email (2)**: Enter your email address for communication and updates.
* **Age (3)**: Enter your age in years.
* **Phone (4)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Parents Name (5)**: Enter your parents' name as it appears on their government-issued ID or passport.
* **Parents Email (6)**: Enter your parents' email address for communication and updates.
* **Student Language (7)**: Select your native language.
* **Class Interest (8)**: Select the class topic(s) you are interested in.
* **Parents Work Status (9)**: Select the status of your parents' employment.
* **Parents Education (10)**: Select your parents' education level.
* **Student Education (11)**: Select your education level.
* **Class Date (12)**: Enter the date of the class in the correct format.
* **Class Time (13)**: Enter the time of the class in the correct format.
* **Class Duration (14)**: Enter the duration of the class in minutes.
* **Class Frequency (15)**: Select the frequency of the class.
* **Student DOB (16)**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format.
* **Parents Note (17)**: Optionally, enter any note or message for the parents.
* **Student Note (18)**: Optionally, enter any note or message for the student.
* **Payment Method (19)**: Select the payment method for the class.
* **Payment Date (20)**: Enter the date of the payment in the correct format.
* **Class Location (21)**: Optionally, enter the location of the class.
* **Class Language (22)**: Optionally, enter the language of the class.
* **Class Folklore (23)**: Select the folklore(s) for the class.
* **Class Poem (24)**: Select the poem(s) for the class.
* **Class Songs (25)**: Select the song(s) for the class.

## Tips
- Ensure that you provide accurate information to avoid any issues with your registration.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid any errors.
- Contact the support team if you have any questions or concerns.
