# Graduation Eligibility Validation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to validate the eligibility of students for graduation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your student information: 
   - Student name
   - Student ID
   - Date of birth
   - Anticipated date of graduation

2. Select your program(s):
   - Choose your major and minor from the options provided.

3. Check your academic status:
   - Current academic status (full-time or part-time)
   - Previous academic status (previous full-time or part-time status)

4. Select your previous programs:
   - If you have completed previous degree(s) or programs.

5. Choose your degree(s):
   - The type of degree you are pursuing or have already completed (e.g., Bachelor's, Master's, etc.)

6. Select your concentration:
   - The area of concentration you are or will be concentrating on in your degree program.

7. Fill in any additional information:
   - Provide any additional comments or information that may be helpful for the graduation committee to consider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Year Student** (`graduation_eligibility_validation_form_student`, Text, Optional): This field is used to identify whether you are a first-year student or not.
* **Programs** (`graduation_eligibility_validation_form_programs`, Select One, Optional): Select the program you are or will be enrolled in.
* **Outstanding Requirements** (`graduation_eligibility_validation_form_outstanding`, Text, Optional): This field is used to note any outstanding requirements that you have not yet met.
* **GPA** (`graduation_eligibility_validation_form_gpa`, Number, Optional): Enter your current GPA.
* **GPA Scale** (`graduation_eligibility_validation_form_gpa_scale`, Select One, Optional): Select the scale used to calculate your GPA.
* **Semesters** (`graduation_eligibility_validation_form_semesters`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the number of semesters you have or will complete.
* **Credits** (`graduation_eligibility_validation_form_credits`, Number, Optional): Enter the number of credits you have or will complete.
* **Recommended By** (`graduation_eligibility_validation_form_recommended_by`, Select One, Optional): If there is someone who recommended you for graduation, select them from the provided options.
* **Comments** (`graduation_eligibility_validation_form_comments`, Text, Optional): Fill in any comments that may be helpful for the graduation committee to consider.
* **Additional Comments** (`graduation_eligibility_validation_form_additional_comments`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional comments you want to make.
* **Student Name** (`graduation_eligibility_validation_form_student_name`, Text, Optional): Fill in your name as it should appear on your diploma.
* **Student ID** (`graduation_eligibility_validation_form_student_id`, Text, Optional): Enter your student ID number.
* **Date of Birth** (`graduation_eligibility_validation_form_date_of_birth`, Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Date of Graduation** (`graduation_eligibility_validation_form_date_of_graduation`, Date, Optional): Enter the date you are expected to graduate.
* **Major** (`graduation_eligibility_validation_form_major`, Text, Optional): Select or type in your major.
* **Minor** (`graduation_eligibility_validation_form_minor`, Text, Optional): Select or type in your minor.
* **Program** (`graduation_eligibility_validation_form_program`, Text, Optional): Select or type in your program.
* **Degree** (`graduation_eligibility_validation_form_degree`, Text, Optional): Select or type in your degree.
* **Concentration** (`graduation_eligibility_validation_form_concentration`, Text, Optional): Select or type in your concentration.
* **Anticipated Date of Graduation** (`graduation_eligibility_validation_form_anticipated_date_of_graduation`, Date, Optional): Enter the date you expect to graduate.
* **Current Academic Status** (`graduation_eligibility_validation_form_current_academic_status`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select your current academic status (full-time or part-time).
* **Previous Academic Status** (`graduation_eligibility_validation_form_previous_academic_status`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select your previous academic status (full-time or part-time).
* **Previous Programs** (`graduation_eligibility_validation_form_previous_programs`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select your previous programs.
* **Previous Degrees** (`graduation_eligibility_validation_form_previous_degrees`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select your previous degrees.
* **Additional Information** (`graduation_eligibility_validation_form_additional_information`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional information you want to make.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Select the correct options for all fields.
* Double check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the graduation committee.
