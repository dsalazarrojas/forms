# Student Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Information Collection Form is designed to collect and store student and teacher details for school administration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) symbol.
2. Enter your student's or teacher's name, date of birth, and student ID (for students).
3. Provide your school name and teacher ID (for teachers).
4. Select your grade level or status from the given options.
5. If applicable, provide your contact information (email and phone number).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Student, Text, *): Enter your student's first name.
* **Last Name** (Student, Text, *): Enter your student's last name.
* **Date Of Birth** (Student, Date, *): Enter your student's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Email** (Optional, Email): Enter your student's email address (only if you have one).
* **Phone** (Optional, Text): Enter your student's phone number (only if you have one).
* **Address** (Optional, Text): Enter your student's address (only if you have one).
* **School Name** (Optional, Text): Enter your school's name.
* **Grade Level** (Student, Select One, *): Select your student's grade level from the given options.
* **Student ID** (Student, Number, *): Enter a unique student ID number.
* **Teacher Name** (Teacher, Text, *): Enter your teacher's name.
* **Teacher Email** (Optional, Email): Enter your teacher's email address (only if you have one).
* **Teacher Phone** (Optional, Text): Enter your teacher's phone number (only if you have one).
* **Student Notes** (Student, Text, *): Enter any additional notes about your student.
* **Teacher Notes** (Optional, Note): Enter any additional notes about your teacher.
* **Student Grade Level** (Student, Select One, *): Select your student's grade level from the given options.
* **Teacher ID** (Teacher, Number, *): Enter a unique teacher ID number.
* **Teacher Grade Level** (Optional, Select One): Select your teacher's grade level from the given options.
* **Student Attendance** (Optional, Select Multiple): Select whether your student attends school regularly (True), or not (False).
* **Teacher Attendance** (Optional, Select Multiple): Select whether your teacher attends school regularly (Yes), or not (No).
* **Student Progress** (Optional, Select Multiple): Select your student's current progress (Excellent, Good, Fair).
* **Teacher Progress** (Optional, Select Multiple): Select your teacher's current progress (Excellent, Good, Fair).
* **Student Status** (Student, Select One, *): Select your student's status (Active, Inactive).
* **Teacher Status** (Optional, Select One): Select your teacher's status (Active, Inactive).
* **Student Image** (Optional, Text): Upload an image of your student.
* **Teacher Image** (Optional, Text): Upload an image of your teacher.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (\*).
* If a field is optional, only fill it in if it's necessary for your specific situation.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy.
* If you need help, reach out to your school administration for assistance.
