# Student Pre-Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Pre-Registration Form is designed to collect necessary information from students to facilitate the registration process. Please complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth and efficient registration experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully review each field to understand its purpose and relevance to your registration.
2. Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Complete all required fields to avoid any delays in the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information** (`student_information`, text, required: false): This field collects general information about the student, including their name.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, required: false): Enter a unique student ID assigned to the student.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Enter the student's full name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter the student's date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter the student's contact phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the student's email address.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the student's emergency contact.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_or_guardian_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the parent or guardian responsible for the student.
* **Parent or Guardian Contact Number** (`parent_or_guardian_contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter the parent or guardian's contact phone number.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`parent_or_guardian_email`, email, required: false): Enter the parent or guardian's email address.
* **Academic History** (`academic_history`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if the student has any academic history or 'No' otherwise.
* **Student Attitude** (`student_attitude`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all applicable traits that describe the student's attitude (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
* **Parent Attitude** (`parent_attitude`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all applicable traits that describe the parent or guardian's attitude (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
* **School History** (`school_history`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if the student has any school history or 'No' otherwise.
* **Student Interests** (`student_interests`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the student's interests.
* **Student Objectives** (`student_objectives`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the student's objectives.
* **School Attendance** (`school_attendance`, number, required: false): Enter the student's expected school attendance (in hours per day).
* **Student Attendance** (`student_attendance`, number, required: false): Enter the student's expected attendance (in hours per day).

## Tips
1. Be accurate and thorough when completing the form.
2. Use clear and concise language when filling out the 'Student Interests' and 'Student Objectives' fields.
3. Ensure all required fields are accurately filled to avoid any delays in the registration process.
