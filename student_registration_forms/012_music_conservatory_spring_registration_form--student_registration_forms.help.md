# Music Conservatory Spring Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Music Conservatory Spring Registration Form is used to collect information from students and parents for the music conservatory's spring registration process. This form gathers essential details, including student and parent information, and music-related data to facilitate a smooth registration experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate music school from the options provided in the "Music School" dropdown menu.
2. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
3. Enter the student's age in the "Student Age" field.
4. Enter the student's email address in the "Student Email" field.
5. Enter the parent's name in the "Parent Name" field.
6. Enter the parent's phone number in the "Parent Phone" field.
7. Enter the student's address in the "Student Address" field.
8. Select one or more instruments from the "Instrument" field, where options include Piano, Guitar, Violin, and Drums.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Music School**: Choose the music school that the student is currently attending.
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Student Age**: Enter the student's age in years.
* **Student Email**: Enter the student's email address.
* **Parent Name**: Enter the parent's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Parent Phone**: Enter the parent's phone number.
* **Student Address**: Enter the student's home address.
* **Instrument**: Select one or more instruments that the student is interested in playing. Options include Piano, Guitar, Violin, and Drums.

## Tips
- Ensure that the student's information is accurate and up-to-date.
- Make sure to select the correct music school from the options provided.
- Enter the student's contact information (email and phone number) to ensure that we can contact them regarding registration and other important updates.
- If the student is interested in playing multiple instruments, select all relevant options in the "Instrument" field.
