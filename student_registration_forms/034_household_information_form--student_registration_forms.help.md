# household_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about the household's economic status, size, and income frequency. This information will help in understanding the household's financial situation and provide a better assessment of their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Select the most accurate options for your household's income frequency and size.
3. Choose the correct head of the household and their employment status.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Household Information Form Page 1**: 
	+ **Household Information Form Page 1** (`household_information_form_page_1`, text, optional)
		- This field is not currently being used, so you can skip it.
* **Household Income** (`household_income`, number, optional)
	+ Enter the total household income.
* **Household Assets** (`household_assets`, text, optional)
	+ Describe the household's assets, such as savings, investments, or property.
* **Household Income Frequency** (`household_income_frequency`, select_one, optional)
	+ Select one of the options to indicate how often the household's income is received:
		- Once
		- Several times a month
		- Several times a year
		- Never
* **Household Rental** (`household_rental`, number, optional)
	+ Enter the total household rental income (if applicable).
* **Household Size** (`household_size`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Select all that apply to indicate the number of people in the household:

* **Head of the Household** (`household_head_of_household`, select_one, optional)
