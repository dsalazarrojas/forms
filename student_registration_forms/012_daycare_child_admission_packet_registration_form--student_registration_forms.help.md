# Daycare Child Admission Packet Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daycare Child Admission Packet Registration Form is used to collect information from parents or guardians about their child's enrollment in our daycare facility. This form helps us to better understand their child's needs, medical history, and payment terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your child's information, including their date of birth and any medical conditions or allergies they may have.
2. Choose your payment term (e.g. Month-to-Month, 3-Months, etc.).
3. Select your preferred payment method (e.g. Cash, Credit Card, etc.).
4. If applicable, provide your medical insurance information, including the type of insurance and policy number.
5. List any emergency contact details and their relationship to the child.
6. Sign the form to confirm that you have reviewed and understand the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Daycare Child Admission Packet Registration Form**
This is the main form title and heading.

### 2. **Parent Information**
Enter your name and contact information as a parent or guardian.

### 3. **Child Information**
Provide your child's name, date of birth, and any additional comments about their medical history or special needs.

### 4. **Date of Birth**
Enter your child's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### 5. **Date of Service Start**
Choose the date when your child will start our daycare services.

### 6. **Date of Service End**
Choose the date when your child's services with us will end.

### 7. **Payment Term**
Select the payment term that best suits your needs (e.g. Month-to-Month, 3-Months, etc.).

### 8. **Payment Method**
Choose how you will pay for our services (e.g. Cash, Credit Card, etc.).

### 9. **Medical Insurance**
If applicable, select any medical insurance that your child has.

### 10. **Medical Insurance Provider**
If your child has medical insurance, enter the name of their provider.

### 11. **Emergency Contact Name**
List the name of an emergency contact person.

### 12. **Relationship to the Child**
Choose how the emergency contact is related to the child (e.g. Parent/Guardian, Grandparent, etc.).

### 13. **Emergency Contact Phone**
Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.

### 14. **Medical Insurance Card Number**
If your child has medical insurance, enter the card number.

### 15. **Medical Insurance Group Number**
If your child has medical insurance, enter the group number.

### 16. **Child Medical Conditions**
List any medical conditions or concerns about your child.

### 17. **Child Allergies**
List any allergies your child may have.

### 18. **Child Medical Devices**
List any medical devices your child uses (e.g. EpiPen, etc.).

### 19. **Child Additional Comments**
Add any additional comments or information about your child.

### 20. **Facility Signature**
The facility will sign to confirm that we have received your information.

### 21. **Parent Signature**
You, the parent or guardian, must sign to confirm that you have reviewed and understand the information provided.

### 22. **Facility License Number**
Enter our facility license number.

### 23. **Facility License State**
Enter the state where our facility is licensed.

### 24. **Facility License Expiration Date**
Enter the date when our facility license expires.

### 25. **Facility License Status**
Choose the status of our facility license (e.g. Active, Inactive, Pending).
