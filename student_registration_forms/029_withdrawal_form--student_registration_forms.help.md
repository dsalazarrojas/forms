# Withdrawal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Withdrawal Form is designed for students to formally notify the institution of their intention to leave the course, program, or university. This form helps students, instructors, and administrators manage the withdrawal process efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Withdrawal Form, follow these steps:
1. Select the appropriate option for "student" to indicate your current student status.
2. Choose the withdrawal date from the calendar.
3. Provide a reason for your withdrawal in the "Reason for Withdrawal" field (select multiple options if necessary).
4. Add any additional comments regarding your withdrawal.
5. Sign the form with your name (Student Signature) and have a counselor sign the form (Counselor Signature).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **student**: Indicates your current student status (Yes/No).
	+ If you have not yet started classes, select "No".
* **Withdrawal Date**: Enter the date you plan to withdraw from the course, program, or university.
* **Reason for Withdrawal**: Choose the reason(s) for your withdrawal from the available options or provide additional details.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional information you feel is relevant to your withdrawal.
* **Student Signature**: Provide your name to confirm your intention to withdraw.
* **Counselor Signature**: A counselor will sign to confirm receipt of your withdrawal request.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth for administrative purposes (required only for official records).
* **Counselor Signature**: A counselor will sign to confirm receipt of your withdrawal request (optional but required for official records).
