# Freshers’ Fest Play Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for plays submitted to the Freshers' Fest, a platform for new students to showcase their talents. Please ensure that you are submitting a new play that you have created, and not a previously submitted one. Additionally, please do not submit multiple plays at once. Each play should have a unique title, name, and description.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the respective fields. These are required fields.
2. Enter your university name in the "university" field.
3. Enter the name of your play in the "play_name" field.
4. Select the date and time of your play in the "play_date" and "play_time" fields.
5. Enter the duration of your play in the "play_duration" field. This should be in minutes.
6. Describe your play in the "play_description" field.
7. Enter the name of the director of your play in the "play_director" field.
8. Enter the venue where your play will be performed in the "play_venue" field.
9. Enter any relevant tags or keywords for your play in the "play_tags" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **last_name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **university** (`university`, text, required): Enter the name of the university you are associated with.
* **play_name** (`play_name`, text, optional): Enter the title of your play.
* **play_date** (`play_date`, date, optional): Select the date of your play. Format should be mm/dd/yyyy.
* **play_time** (`play_time`, time, optional): Select the time of your play. Format should be hh:mm.
* **play_duration** (`play_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of your play in minutes. For example, 60 for a 1 hour play.
* **play_description** (`play_description`, text, optional): Describe your play briefly.
* **play_director** (`play_director`, text, optional): Enter the name of the director of your play.
* **play_venue** (`play_venue`, text, optional): Enter the venue where your play will be performed.
* **play_tags** (`play_tags`, text, optional): Enter any relevant tags or keywords for your play.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your play is original and not copied from elsewhere.
* Please be accurate and truthful while filling in the form.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact the festival organizers.
