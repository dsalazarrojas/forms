# Student Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Form is used to collect essential details about a student for academic activities or services. This form is only to be filled out for new students or when a student's existing information needs to be updated.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Select the student's date of birth from the calendar.
3. Enter the name of the school the student attends.
4. Enter the student's contact number.
5. Enter the student's ID number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the student's full name.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Optional): Select the student's date of birth from the calendar.
- **School Name** (`school_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the school the student attends.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, Text, Optional): Enter the student's contact number.
- **Student ID** (`student_id`, Number, Optional): Enter the student's ID number.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
- Double-check for any errors before submission.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to the student's ID card or other documentation for verification.
- Only fill out this form for new students or when updating an existing student's information.
