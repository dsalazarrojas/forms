# Choir Audition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from students who wish to audition for the choir. The form will help us understand your musical background, experience, and availability for an audition. Please fill out this form thoroughly and accurately to ensure a successful audition process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to include your preferred audition date and time.
3. If you have any additional information or comments, please include them in the "Additional Comments or Interests" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it is written on your school ID.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Grade Level**: Select your current grade level from the given options (6th Grade, 7th Grade, 8th Grade, 9th Grade, 10th Grade, 11th Grade, 12th Grade, or Other).
* **School Name**: Enter the name of your school.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: Enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of your parent or guardian.
* **Years of Singing Experience**: Enter the number of years you have been singing.
* **Previous Choir Experience**: Select your previous choir experience from the given options (None, School choir, Church choir, Community choir, Multiple choirs).
* **Vocal Range or Voice Type**: Select your vocal range or voice type from the given options (Soprano, Alto, Tenor, Bass, Unsure).
* **Can you read music?**: Select if you can read music from the given options (Yes, Proficiently, Yes, basic level, No).
* **Other Instruments Played**: If you play other instruments, enter them in this field.
* **Prepared Piece Title**: Enter the title of the song or piece you will be auditioning with.
* **Genre of Prepared Piece**: Select the genre of the prepared piece from the given options (Classical, Musical Theater, Jazz, Pop, Sacred, Folk, Other).
* **Accompaniment for Audition**: Select the accompaniment type you will be using for the audition from the given options (A cappella, Piano accompaniment, Recorded backing track, Accompanist provided).
* **Preferred Audition Date**: Enter your preferred date for the audition in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Preferred Audition Time**: Select your preferred time for the audition from the given options (Morning (8am-12pm), Afternoon (12pm-5pm), Evening (5pm-8pm), Flexible).
* **Additional Comments or Interests**: Enter any additional comments or interests you have for the audition.
