# prom_guest_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The prom_guest_form is a registration form for guests attending a promotion event. It collects essential details such as names, contact information, and other relevant data for the event's organization and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Select your grade level from the available options in the "student_grade" field.
6. If you have a student ID, enter it in the "student_id" field.
7. If your grade level is not listed in the "student_grade" field, provide more information about your grade level in the "student_grade_other" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address associated with your contact information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, required/optional): If you have a student ID, enter it here.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, select_one, required/optional): Select your grade level from the options provided (9th, 10th, 11th).
* **Student Grade Other** (`student_grade_other`, text, required/optional): If your grade level is not listed, provide more information about your grade level here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields correctly.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* If you have any issues or need help, contact the event organizers for assistance.
