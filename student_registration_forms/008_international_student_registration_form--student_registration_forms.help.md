# international_student_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect essential information from international students for registration purposes. It helps administrators verify student identity, track attendance, and provide relevant services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's personal details, such as name, date of birth, and other relevant information.
2. Provide accurate contact information for the student, including address and other contact preferences.
3. Review and submit the form for verification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information** (Text, Optional): This field is for the student to enter their name, which will help administrators identify them.
* **Personal Details** (Text, Optional): This field is for the student to provide any additional personal details that they feel are relevant for the purpose of their registration.
* **Contact Information** (Text, Optional): Enter your email address and phone number to ensure you can be reached for important updates and notifications.
* **Address** (Text, Optional): Provide your address for delivery or pickup purposes.
* **Date Of Birth** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you were born on (mm/dd/yyyy).
