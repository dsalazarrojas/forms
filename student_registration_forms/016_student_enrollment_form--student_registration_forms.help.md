# Student Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information for student enrollment, including personal details, program selection, and fee payment options.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure you have all necessary information before starting the form.
2. Fill out each field accurately and completely.
3. If you need assistance with a specific field, don't hesitate to reach out to your administrator or support staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your active and valid email address for communication.
* **Student Number** (`student_number`, select_multiple, optional): Select your student number from the provided options.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, optional): Enter your birth date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Program** (`program`, select_multiple, optional): Select the program you are enrolling in from the provided options.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, optional): Select the department you are enrolling in from the provided options.
* **Fee Payment** (`fee_payment`, select_multiple, optional): Select your fee payment option from the provided choices.
* **Other Fees** (`other_fees`, text, optional): Enter any additional fees you need to pay.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address for billing purposes.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city for billing purposes.
* **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Enter your country for billing purposes.
* **State/Province** (`state_province`, text, optional): Enter your state or province for billing purposes.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, text, optional): Enter your postal code for billing purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, optional): Enter your birth date in the format mm/dd/yyyy. This field is likely an error and should be replaced with a single field instance. 
* **Program** (`program`, select_multiple, optional): Select your program from the provided options.
