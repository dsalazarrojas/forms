# Editable Activities For Students - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about student activities, interests, and participation in a school or educational setting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field accurately and truthfully.
2. Review the fields carefully to ensure that they are completed correctly.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the administrator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Interests**: A text field where you can describe your interests.
* **Activity Participation**: A field where you can select if you are participating in the activity, if not, or both if applicable.
* **Student Comments**: A field for any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Student Uploads**: Select "Files uploaded" if you have uploaded a file, otherwise select "No files uploaded".
* **Date Of Interest**: The date when you became interested in the activity. Use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time Of Interest**: The time when you became interested in the activity. Use the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Email**: Your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number.
* **Date Of Participation**: The date when you participated in the activity. Use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Educator Comments**: Additional comments from the educator.
