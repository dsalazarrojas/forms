# University Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Admission Form is a tool used to collect information from prospective students who wish to be admitted into the university. The form will guide you through the process of providing necessary details about yourself and the university you are applying to.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Make sure to select the correct student status from the dropdown menu.
3. Click the submit button to send your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Name** (`student name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as you would like it to be listed on university records.
- **Student Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide your contact information, including phone numbers and email addresses.
- **Academic Background** (`academic_background`, text, optional): Share information about your previous educational background, including degrees completed and institutions attended.
- **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, text, optional): Include any other relevant personal details you think would be helpful for the admissions team.
- **University Info** (`university_info`, text, optional): Provide information about the university you are applying to.
- **Student Status** (`student_status`, text, required): Select your current student status.
- **Course Selection** (`course_selection`, text, optional): Indicate the course you are interested in applying to.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (dd/mm/yyyy).
- **Time of Application** (`time_of_application`, time, optional): Indicate the time you submitted your application.
- **Email** (`email`, text, optional): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Provide your current address.
- **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Select your country of origin.
- **State** (`state`, text, optional): Select your state or province.
- **Pin Code** (`pin_code`, text, optional): Enter your pin code.
- **A Level** (`a_level`, text, optional): Provide your A-level results or other relevant academic achievements.
- **Status ID** (`student_status_id`, text, optional): Enter your status ID (if applicable).
- **Status** (`student_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your application.
- **University Name** (`university_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the university you are applying to.
- **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, optional): Indicate the course you are interested in applying to.
- **University Address** (`university_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the university you are applying to.
- **University Phone** (`university_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the university.
- **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your application.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required.
