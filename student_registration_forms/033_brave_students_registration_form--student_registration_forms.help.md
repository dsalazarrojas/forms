# BRAVE Students Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The BRAVE Students Registration Form is designed to collect information from students who are interested in participating in the BRAVE program. This form helps us to understand the student's personal details, school information, and program preferences, ensuring a smooth and personalized experience for the student.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Select the correct grade level and program of interest.
3. Provide your parent/guardian's contact information, including their name, email, and phone number.
4. Answer the additional questions to help us understand your interests, strengths, and needs.
5. Review the consent agreement carefully and select the correct option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your student's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your student's last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your student's date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **School Name**: Enter your school's name.
* **Grade Level**: Select the grade level your student is currently in.
* **Parent/Guardian Name**: Enter your full name as the parent/guardian.
* **Relationship to Student**: Describe your relationship to the student (e.g., mother, father, guardian).
* **Parent/Guardian Email**: Enter your primary email address.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone**: Enter your primary phone number.
* **Program of Interest**: Describe what the student is interested in participating in the program for.
* **Academic Strengths**: List the subject areas where your student excels.
* **Areas for Growth**: List the subject areas where your student needs support.
* **Special Needs or Accommodations**: Describe any learning disabilities or accessibility needs.
* **Prior BRAVE Program Participation?**: Indicate if your student has participated in the program before.
* **What Are Your Goals?**: Describe what your student hopes to achieve through the program.
* **Parent/Guardian Consent**: Select the option to agree to the program's terms and conditions.
