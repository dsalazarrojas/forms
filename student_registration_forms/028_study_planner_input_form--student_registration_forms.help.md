# study_planner_input_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The study_planner_input_form is designed to collect information from students about their study goals, preferences and schedules. This form helps students set clear goals and schedule their study time and dates, providing personalized support and resources to aid in their study planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required and optional fields according to your study goals and preferences.
2. Review each field for its relevance to your study plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **study_goals** (`study_goals`, text, required: false): Please enter your short-term and long-term study goals. This will help us understand what you want to achieve in your studies.
* **study_scheduling_preferences** (`study_scheduling_preferences`, number, required: false): Enter a number that reflects how much time you have available to dedicate to studying each day/week.
* **Study Planning Goals** (`study_planning_goals`, text, required: false): Describe your specific goals for this study planning period. This could include exam dates, project deadlines, or other relevant targets.
* **Study Planning Preferences** (`study_planning_preferences`, text, required: false): Specify any additional preferences you have for your study planning, such as studying at a specific time of day or studying in a particular location.
* **Scheduling Time** (`scheduling_time`, time, required: false): Enter your availability for study time, specifying a start and end time of your study period. For example, "Monday to Friday, 8am-12pm".
* **Scheduling Date** (`scheduling_date`, date, required: false): Enter a date for when you plan to start your studies. This will help us provide you with the most up-to-date resources and support.
