# Student Accommodation Plan Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your accommodation preferences and details for a student registration process. It will be used by the student services team to better understand your needs and preferences for accommodation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out each field carefully and accurately**: Make sure to fill out each field with the correct information.
2. **Select "Yes" or "No" for select options**: When you see "Yes" or "No" options, select the corresponding answer.
3. **Choose the correct option from the list**: When you see a list of options, choose the one that best suits your needs.
4. **Enter your name, email, phone, and address**: Make sure to enter your full name, email, phone number, and address accurately.
5. **Select your status and dorm type**: Choose your status and dorm type from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Name**: Enter your full name.
- **Email**: Enter your official email address.
- **Student Phone**: Enter your phone number.
- **Student Address**: Enter your current address.
- **Student Status**: Select your status from the available options.
- **Student Dorm Status**: Select your dorm status from the available options.
- **Student Dorm Type**: Select your dorm type from the available options.
- **Move In Details**: Enter your move-in details, including date and time.
- **Preferred Language**: Select your preferred language from the available options.
- **Preferred Location**: Select your preferred location from the available options.
- **Move In Method**: Select the method of your move-in from the available options.
- **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Select the relationship of your emergency contact person from the available options.
- **Move Out Date**: Enter your move-out date if applicable.
- **Move Out Time**: Enter your move-out time if applicable.
- **Move In Time AM/PM**: Select the time of day for your move-in.
- **Move Out Time AM/PM**: Select the time of day for your move-out.
- **Roommate Preferences**: Enter any additional information about your roommate preferences.
- **Accommodation Type**: Select whether you have a preference for accommodation type.
- **Notes**: Enter any additional information or comments about your accommodation preferences.
- **Student ID**: Enter your student ID number.
