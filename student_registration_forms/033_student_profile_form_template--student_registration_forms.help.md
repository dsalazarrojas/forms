# student_profile_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The student profile form template is a collection of information about a student, aimed at providing a comprehensive understanding of their background, education, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the profile form, providing your name, surname, and email address.
2. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
3. Select your date of birth and birthplace.
4. Choose your gender.
5. Select your nationality and address.
6. Enter the name of the course you are enrolled in.
7. Enter your student ID.
8. Select your current student status (active or inactive).
9. Add any relevant notes about yourself.
10. If applicable, select your student group and level (High or Low).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Profile Form** (`profile_form`, text, optional): This field allows you to enter a brief description of yourself.
*   **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your first and last name.
*   **Surname** (`surname`, text, optional): Enter your last name or surname.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Select your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
*   **Birth Place** (`birth_place`, text, optional): Enter the place where you were born.
*   **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional): Select your gender (Male, Female, or Other).
*   **Nationality** (`nationality`, select_multiple, optional): Select your country of origin (American, British, Canadian, or Australian).
*   **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your current address.
*   **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the course you are currently enrolled in.
*   **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, optional): Enter your student ID for identification purposes.
*   **Student Status** (`student_status`, select_one, optional): Select your current student status (Active or Inactive).
*   **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any relevant notes about yourself.
*   **Student Group** (`student_group`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether you are part of a student group ('Yes' or 'No').
*   **Student Level** (`student_level`, select_multiple, optional): Select your student level (High or Low).

## Tips
- Double-check the accuracy of the information entered before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, consult the relevant authorities or seek clarification.
- Ensure that all fields are completed accurately to provide a comprehensive profile.
