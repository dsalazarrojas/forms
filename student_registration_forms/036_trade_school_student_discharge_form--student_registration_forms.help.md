# Trade School Student Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the discharge of a student from a trade school program. It collects relevant information about the student, the program they were enrolled in, and the reason for their discharge.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the correct trade school student discharge form type (if applicable).
2. Fill out the following fields:
	* Student Name: This is the name of the student being discharged.
	* Student ID: This is the unique identifier assigned to the student.
	* Date Of Birth: This is the date of birth of the student (optional).
	* Trade School Name: This is the name of the trade school where the student was enrolled.
	* Program ID: This is the unique identifier for the program the student was enrolled in.
	* Enrollment Date: This is the date the student enrolled in the program (optional).
	* Trade School Address: This is the address of the trade school where the student was enrolled.
	* Reason For Discharge: This is the reason why the student is being discharged from the program.
	* Date Of Discharge: This is the date of the discharge.
