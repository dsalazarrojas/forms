# outdoor-activity-terms-agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The outdoor-activity-terms-agreement form is designed to obtain participant consent and gather emergency contact information for outdoor activities that may pose some level of risk or liability to the participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully to understand the information required.
2.  Fill out the form with the necessary information.
3.  Sign and date the form as required.
4.  Ensure that you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Participant Consent**: This field is a note field where you can record any information about the participant's consent to participate in the activity.
*   **Emergency Contacts**: Select all emergency contacts that apply.
*   **Emergency Contact 1**: Enter the name and contact information for the first emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact 2**: Enter the name and contact information for the second emergency contact.
*   **Signatures**: This field is a note field where you can record signatures of participants.
*   **Waiver Form**: Please fill out this section if necessary.
*   **Additional Information**: Enter any additional information about the participant or the activity.
*   **Emergency Medical**: Select 'Yes' if medical conditions apply.
*   **Medical Conditions**: Select all medical conditions that apply.
*   **Allergies**: Select all allergies that apply.
*   **Emergency Contact 3**: Enter the name and contact information for the third emergency contact.
*   **Participant Information**: Enter any participant information.
*   **Participant Consent Confirmation**: Select 'Yes' to confirm participant consent.
*   **Participant Acceptance**: Select 'Yes' to confirm participant acceptance.
*   **Participant Terms Acknowledgement**: Select 'Yes' to confirm participant acknowledgement of terms.

## Tips
*   Make sure to review and understand all the information required in the form.
*   Fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
*   Ensure that you have obtained participant consent before allowing them to participate in the activity.
