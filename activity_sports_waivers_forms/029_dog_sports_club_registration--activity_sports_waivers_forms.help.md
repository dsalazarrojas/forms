# Dog Sports Club Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from dog owners who wish to register their dogs for a sports club activity. This form is used to gather essential details about the dog and its owner, ensuring a smooth and enjoyable experience for both.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select the correct options for the "Dog Owner" field. This field is used to indicate whether the dog's owner is registering or not.
2. Enter the dog's information in the "Dog Information" field. This is a text field where you can provide any additional details about your dog.
3. In the "Owner Information" field, you can add any relevant notes about the owner.
4. Choose the contact method for communication in the "Contact Method" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, if applicable.
6. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field, if required.
7. Enter the address and city in their respective fields.
8. Select the state or province where you reside in the "State/Province" field.
9. For the "Dog Type", "Dog Breed", "Dog Size", "Dog Weight", "Dog Age", fields, select the correct option from the dropdown menu.
10. Finally, enter the class time and date in their respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dog Owner**: This field indicates whether the dog's owner is registering or not. Select "Yes" if you are the owner or "No" if you are not.
* **Dog Information**: Enter any additional details about your dog in this text field.
* **Owner Information**: Add any relevant notes about the owner in this note field.
* **Contact Info**: Choose how you would like to be contacted, "Yes" or "No".
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if it's required for contact.
* **Address**: Provide your address for contact purposes.
* **City**: Enter your city for contact purposes.
* **State/Province**: Select the state or province where you reside.
* **Dog Type**: Select the type of dog you are registering.
* **Dog Breed**: Select the breed of your dog.
* **Dog Size**: Select the size of your dog.
* **Dog Weight**: Select the weight range of your dog.
* **Dog Age**: Select the age of your dog.
* **Contact Method**: Choose how you would like to be contacted.
* **Class Time**: Enter the class time you wish to register for.
* **Class Date**: Enter the date of the class you wish to register for.
* **Activity**: Select whether you are interested in joining an activity.
* **Notes**: Add any notes about your dog or the activity.
* **Owner Name**: Enter your name for contact purposes.
* **Class Name**: Enter the name of the class you are registering for.
* **E-Signature**: Enter your electronic signature.
* **Owner Signature**: Enter the owner's signature.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the class you are registering for.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the class you are registering for.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

Tips:
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Ensure the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
