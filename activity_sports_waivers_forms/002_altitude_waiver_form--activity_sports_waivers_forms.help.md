# Altitude Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Altitude Waiver Form is a document that helps individuals understand and acknowledge the risks associated with high-altitude activities. It's a necessary step to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full Name** (page 1) to confirm your identity.
2. Enter your **Date of Birth** (page 1) to verify your age and eligibility for the activity.
3. Provide your **Emergency Contact Name** (page 1) and **Emergency Contact Phone** (page 1) in case of an emergency.
4. Select all applicable **Medical Conditions** (page 2) from the list to ensure we're aware of any potential health risks.
5. Choose the type of **Activity Type** (page 2) you'll be participating in (e.g., Rock Climbing, High Altitude Trekking, etc.).
6. Read and **Risk Acknowledge** (page 2) that you understand the risks involved in the activity.
7. Select whether you **Agree** (page 3) with the waiver terms or **Do Not Agree**. Please note that by selecting 'I Agree', you waive certain legal rights.
8. If you're under 18, enter your **Parent or Guardian Name** (page 3) if required.
9. Finally, sign and date the form by filling in the **Date of Signature** (page 3).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (page 1): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (page 1): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (page 1): Enter the name of the person you want us to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (page 1): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Medical Conditions** (page 2): Select all the medical conditions that apply to you from the list. Please note that selecting 'None' does not imply you have no health issues, but rather that none of the listed conditions apply to you.
* **Activity Type** (page 2): Choose the type of high-altitude activity you'll be participating in. If unsure, please consult with your activity provider.
* **Risk Acknowledge** (page 2): Read the risks involved in high-altitude activities and select 'I Acknowledge and Accept Risks' if you understand and accept them.
* **Waiver Agreement** (page 3): Select whether you **Agree** or **Do Not Agree** with the waiver terms. Please be aware that selecting 'I Agree' waives certain legal rights.
* **Date of Signature** (page 3): Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (page 3): If you're under 18, enter the name of your parent or guardian if required.
* **Signature Date** (page 3): Date of signature, which is used to confirm that you've read and agreed to the terms of the waiver.
