# Craft Event Indemnification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Craft Event Indemnification Form is used to collect waiver information from participants in a craft event. It is essential for event organizers to fill out this form to ensure that participants understand and consent to the waiver terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field carefully and thoroughly.
2. Select the relevant options for each field.
3. Make sure all required fields are filled out before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required): Enter the participant's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Waiver Consent** (`waiver_consent`, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to indicate whether the participant has given their consent to the waiver terms.
* **Waiver Signature** (`waiver_signature`, note, optional): If the participant is present, ask them to sign the waiver form.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required): Enter the name of the event, date, time, and other relevant event details.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, note, optional): If the participant is present, ask them to sign the form to confirm their consent.
* **Workshop Details** (`workshop_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional workshop details if required.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to indicate the type of event.
* **Workshop Duration** (`workshop_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the workshop if required.
* **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, number, optional): Enter the participant's age if required.
* **Participant Gender** (`participant_gender`, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to indicate the participant's gender.
* **Waiver Expires** (`waiver_expires`, date, optional): Enter the date the waiver expires if required.
* **Workshop Venue** (`workshop_venue`, text, optional): Enter the venue of the workshop if required.

Tips:

* Make sure to obtain the participant's signature if they are present.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
* Keep the participant's copy of the form for record-keeping purposes.
