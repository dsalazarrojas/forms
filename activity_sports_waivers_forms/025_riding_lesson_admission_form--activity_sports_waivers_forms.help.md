# Riding Lesson Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about students before they begin riding lessons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select "Yes" if you are prepared to complete the riding lesson admission form.
2. Fill in your **Student Name** and **Contact Person**.
3. Enter your **Student Age**.
4. Choose your **Student Experience Level** from the options: Horse riding prophets, Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
5. Select all the equipment you will be using for the riding lesson from the options: None, Saddle, Bit, Bridle, Blanket, Halter, or Other.
6. Enter the **Instructor Name**.
7. Choose the date and **Start Time** and **End Time** for the riding lesson.
8. Enter your **Instructor Phone** number.
9. Enter the **Emergency Contact Number** and choose the relationship to the student.
10. Enter your **Instructor E-mail**.
11. Enter the total time for the riding lesson.
12. Check "Waiver Signed" to confirm you have read and agreed to the terms of service.
13. Read through the **Terms of Service**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Riding Lesson Admission Form** (Select One): This field is used to determine if you are prepared to complete the riding lesson admission form. Please select "Yes" to confirm.
* **Student Name**: Please enter the name of the student.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the student.
* **Student Age**: Enter the student's age.
* **Student Experience Level**: Choose the student's experience level from the options: Horse riding prophets, Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Riding Equipment**: Select all the equipment you will be using during the riding lesson from the options: None, Saddle, Bit, Bridle, Blanket, Halter, or Other.
* **Instructor Name**: Please enter the name of the instructor.
* **Date**: Choose the date for the riding lesson.
* **Start Time**: Choose the start time for the riding lesson.
* **End Time**: Choose the end time for the riding lesson.
* **Instructor Phone**: Enter the instructor's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship**: Choose the relationship to the student from the options: Mother or Father, Brother or Sister, Spouse, or Friend.
* **Instructor E-mail**: Enter the instructor's email.
* **Total Time**: Enter the total time for the riding lesson.
* **Waiver Signed**: Please check this field to confirm you have read and agreed to the terms of service.
* **Terms of Service**: Please read through the terms of service carefully before proceeding.
