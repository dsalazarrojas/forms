# Experience Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Experience Waiver Form" is used to collect participant details, medical information, and signatures for experiences or activities. This form helps us gather essential information about each participant, ensuring their safety and well-being during the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Complete each section thoroughly**: Please fill out all the required fields, including participant details, medical history, and emergency contact information.
2. **Use the provided hints**: If you need help with a field, refer to the hint text for guidance.
3. **Sign the form**: Once you've completed all the fields, sign the form to confirm your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details**:
	+ **<label>Participant Details</label>**:
		- This section is for collecting basic information about the participant, such as name, age, and contact details.
* **Experience Participant Details**:
	+ **<label>Experience Participant Details</label>**:
		- This section is for collecting specific information about the experience or activity the participant is participating in.
* **Parent Details**:
	+ **<label>Parent Details</label>**:
		- This section is for collecting information about the parent or guardian of the participant, if applicable.
* **Emergency Contact**:
	+ **<label>Emergency Contact</label>**:
		- This section is for providing contact information for a family member or emergency contact, in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Details**:
	+ **<label>Medical Details</label>**:
		- This section is for reporting any medical conditions or allergies that may affect the participant's participation in the experience.
* **Participant Signature**:
	+ **<label>Participant Signature</label>**:
		- Please sign and date the form to confirm your participation in the experience.
* **Parent Signature**:
	+ **<label>Parent Signature</label>**:
		- If the participant is a minor, please sign and date the form to confirm your consent as the parent or guardian.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when completing the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask for help or clarification.
* Remember to sign the form once you've completed all the fields.
