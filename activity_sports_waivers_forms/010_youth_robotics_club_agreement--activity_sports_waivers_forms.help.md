# Youth Robotics Club Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Robotics Club Agreement form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians and their children participating in the Youth Robotics Club. The form will be used to obtain consent and agreement from the parent/guardian regarding the child's participation in the club.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the Parent Name**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
2. **Enter the Participant Name**: Enter the name of the child participating in the club.
3. **Enter the Participant Age**: Enter the age of the child.
4. **Enter the Parent Phone**: Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
5. **Enter the Parent Email**: Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
6. **Enter the Participant Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the child.
7. **Agreement**: Check "Yes" to indicate that you have read, understand, and agree to the Youth Robotics Club Code of Conduct.
8. **Enter the Guardian Name**: Enter the name of the guardian (if applicable).
9. **Enter the Relationship to Participant**: Select the relationship between the guardian and the child (e.g. Father or Mother, Brother or Sister, Guardian).
10. **Enter the Guardian Phone**: Enter the phone number of the guardian (if applicable).
11. **Enter the Guardian Email**: Enter the email address of the guardian (if applicable).
12. **Enter the Participant Address**: Enter the address of the child.
13. **Enter the Participant City**: Enter the city where the child lives.
14. **Enter the Participant State**: Enter the state where the child lives.
15. **Enter the Participant Zip**: Enter the zip code where the child lives.
16. **Enter the Participant Country**: Enter the country where the child lives.
17. **Parent Signature**: Sign with your name to indicate that you have read and agree to the Youth Robotics Club Code of Conduct.
18. **Guardian Signature**: If the guardian is signing, enter their name.
19. **Participant Signature**: Sign with your name to indicate that you have read and agree to the Youth Robotics Club Code of Conduct.
20. **Date and Time**: Enter the date and time of signing.
21. **Staff Approval**: Select "Approve" if you agree with the participation of the child.
22. **Staff Signature**: Sign with your name.
23. **Staff Phone**: Enter the phone number of the staff member.
24. **Staff Email**: Enter the email address of the staff member.
