# Sports Training Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients participating in sports training activities at a facility. It ensures that clients are properly informed about the risks and procedures of the training activity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your personal contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
2. If you have an emergency contact, please provide their name, relationship to you, phone number, and email address.
3. If you have any medical conditions that may affect your participation in the training activity, please list them in the "Medical Conditions" field.
4. If you have any specific medical certifications or certifications related to the training activity, please indicate them in the "Facility Medical Certifications" and "Facility Certifications" fields.
5. Please provide the facility's address, city, state, zip code, phone number, and email address.
6. If the facility has a medical certificate or liability insurance, please indicate them in the "Facility Medical Certificate" and "Facility Certifications" fields.
7. Finally, please sign the "Facility Signature" field to confirm that you have read and understood the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sports Training Liability Waiver** (1): This field is the main title of the form.
* **Contact Person** (2): Please provide your personal contact information.
* **Contact Phone** (3): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Email** (4): Enter your email address.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (5): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (6): Select the relationship between you and the emergency contact (e.g., Parent, Spouse, Partner, Friend, Family).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (7): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (8): Enter the email address of the emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions** (9): List any medical conditions that may affect your participation in the training activity.
* **Medical Conditions Note** (10): Provide any additional information about your medical conditions.
* **Emergency Medical Certificate** (11): Indicate whether you have a medical certificate or not.
* **Facility Address** (12): Enter the address of the facility where you will be participating in the training activity.
* **Facility City** (13): Enter the city of the facility.
* **Facility State** (14): Enter the state of the facility.
* **Facility Zip** (15): Enter the zip code of the facility.
* **Facility Phone** (16): Enter the phone number of the facility.
* **Facility Email** (17): Enter the email address of the facility.
* **Facility License Number** (18): Enter the facility's license number.
* **Facility License Type** (19): Enter the type of facility license.
* **Medical Provider** (20): Enter the name of your medical provider.
* **Facility Medical Provider** (21): Enter the name of the facility's medical provider.
* **Facility Medical Certificate** (22): Indicate whether the facility has a medical certificate or not.
* **Facility Certifications** (23): Indicate whether the facility has any certifications or not.
* **Facility Liabilities** (24): List any liabilities that may affect your participation in the training activity.
* **Facility Signature** (25): Sign the form to confirm that you have read and understood the form.

Note: Please review each field carefully and fill out the form accurately to ensure that you are properly informed about the risks and procedures of the training activity.
