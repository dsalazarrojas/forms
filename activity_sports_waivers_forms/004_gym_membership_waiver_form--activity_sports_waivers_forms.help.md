# gym_membership_waiver_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from new members of a gym or fitness center, allowing them to provide their medical history, emergency contact information, and any other required details for liability release and safety precautions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the **New Member Form** with your details.
2. Provide **Emergency Contact Information** for a trusted person who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
3. Specify your **Emergency Contact Relationship** with the person you listed above.
4. Fill out the **Medical Information** section to disclose any pre-existing medical conditions.
5. In the **Injury Information** section, list any injuries you have had in the past 12 months.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Member Form** (`new_member_form`, `text`, required/optional): This section is the initial form where you provide your details, such as your name and contact information.
* **Emergency Contact Information** (`emergency_contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): Provide the name and contact details (phone number, address, email) of a person who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required/optional): Specify your relationship with the emergency contact person (e.g. spouse, friend, parent).
* **Medical Information** (`medical_info`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for disclosing any pre-existing medical conditions that may impact your ability to participate in activities at the gym/fitness center.
* **Injury Information** (`injury_info`, `text`, required/optional): List any injuries you have had in the past 12 months, including the nature of the injury and the date it occurred.

## Tips

* Please answer each question carefully and truthfully.
* If you have any medical conditions or injuries that may impact your ability to participate in activities, make sure to disclose them in the **Medical Information** and **Injury Information** sections.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the gym/fitness center staff for guidance.
