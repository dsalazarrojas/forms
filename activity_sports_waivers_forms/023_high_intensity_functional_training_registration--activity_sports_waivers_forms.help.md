# High Intensity Functional Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The High Intensity Functional Training Registration form is designed to collect information from participants in a high-intensity functional training program. This form helps us to assess each participant's eligibility and suitability for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete all required fields to ensure accurate and successful registration.
2. Enter your date of registration in the "Registration Date" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
6. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
7. Enter your age in the "Participant Age" field.
8. Choose the start date of your program in the "Program Start Date" field.
9. Choose the end date of your program in the "Program End Date" field.
10. Indicate your participant level in the "Participant Level" field.
11. Enter the name of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
12. Enter the phone number of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
13. Specify your relationship to the participant in the "Relationship to Participant" field.
14. Enter the email address of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Email" (optional) field.
15. Choose the type of your program in the "Program Type" field.
16. Indicate your program level in the "Program Level" field.
17. Select the frequency of your training in the "Training Frequency" field.
18. Select the duration of your workouts in the "Workout Duration" field.
19. Choose the time of your training sessions in the "Training Time" field.
20. Select the days you are available to train in the "Training Days" field.
21. List any medical conditions that may affect your training in the "Medical Conditions" field.
22. List any previous injuries that may impact your training in the "Injuries" field.
23. List any medical devices you use in the "Medical Devices" field.
24. List any medications you are currently taking in the "Medications" field.
25. Add any additional medical notes in the "Medical Notes" field (optional).
26. Add any notes about your relationship with your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Relationship Note" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you complete this form.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications about the program.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, `number`, required): Enter your age.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, `date`, required): Choose the start date of your program.
* **Program End Date** (`program_end_date`, `date`, required): Choose the end date of your program.
* **Participant Level** (`participant_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose your participant level (Novice, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship to Participant** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `select_one`, required): Specify your relationship to the participant (Parent, Spouse, Sibling, Friend, Other).
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of your emergency contact (optional).
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of your program (Strength Training, Cardiovascular Training, Flexibility Training, Balance and Flexibility, Other).
* **Program Level** (`program_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose your program level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Training Frequency** (`training_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the frequency of your training sessions.
* **Workout Duration** (`workout_duration`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the duration of your workouts.
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the time of your training sessions.
* **Training Days** (`training_days`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the days you are available to train.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `select_multiple`, required): List any medical conditions that may affect your training.
* **Injuries** (`injuries`, `select_multiple`, required): List any previous injuries that may impact your training.
* **Medical Devices** (`medical_devices`, `select_multiple`, required): List any medical devices you use.
* **Medications** (`medications`, `select_multiple`, required): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, `note`, required): Add any additional medical notes.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Note** (`emergency_contact_relationship_note`, `note`, required): Add any notes about your relationship with your emergency contact.
