# Snowmobile Tour Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
This Snowmobile Tour Check In form is designed to collect crucial information from snowmobile participants, including emergency contact details, medical information, and tour specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form correctly:
1. Provide accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
2. Fill out the form completely, especially if you are participating in a guided tour.
3. Review each section carefully before submitting your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Information
* **Participant Name** (`participant_information`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on your tour documents.
* **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, `number`, required/optional): Please enter your age to help us understand your physical capabilities.
* **Participant Age 2** (`participant_age_2`, `number`, required/optional): This field is for secondary participants, please enter their age.

### Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name and relationship of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a 10-digit emergency contact phone number.
* **Emergency Contact 2** (`emergency_contact_2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name and relationship of your secondary emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Info 2** (`emergency_contact_info_2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter contact information for your secondary emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone 2** (`emergency_contact_phone_2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a 10-digit emergency contact phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email 2** (`emergency_contact_email_2`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of your secondary emergency contact person.

### Medical Details
* **Medical Details** (`medical_details`, `text`, required/optional): Describe any medical conditions you have that may affect your ability to participate in the tour.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, required/optional): List any medical conditions you have that may be relevant to your participation.
* **Medical Condition Description** (`medical_condition_description`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of your medical condition.
* **Medical Procedure** (`medical_procedure`, `text`, required/optional): Describe any medical procedures you are currently undergoing.
* **Medical Procedure 2** (`medical_procedure_2`, `text`, required/optional): Describe any secondary medical procedures you are currently undergoing.
* **Medical Insurance Provider** (`medical_insurance_provider`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your medical insurance provider name.
* **Medical Insurance Provider 2** (`medical_insurance_provider_2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your secondary medical insurance provider name.

### Tour Information
* **Snowmobile Tour Type** (`snowmobile_tour_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of tour you are participating in (Snowmobile Tour, Guided Snowmobile Tour, Self Guided Snowmobile Tour, Private Snowmobile Tour).
* **Snowmobile Tour Date** (`snowmobile_tour_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of your tour.
* **Snowmobile Tour Time** (`snowmobile_tour_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the tour start time.
* **Snowmobile Tour Location** (`snowmobile_tour_location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location of your tour.
* **Snowmobile Tour Start Time** (`snowmobile_tour_start_time`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the tour start time in 24-hour format.
* **Snowmobile Tour Operator** (`snowmobile_tour_operator`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your tour operator.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Review each section carefully before submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your tour operator directly.
* If you need to update any information, kindly do so and resubmit the form.
* Providing accurate information is crucial for the tour operators to take necessary precautions and ensure your well-being.
