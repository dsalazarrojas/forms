# Fitness Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Liability Waiver is a mandatory form that must be completed by individuals participating in fitness activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have reviewed the form carefully and have all the necessary information available.
2. Complete all required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
3. Enter your name, contact information, and emergency contact details accurately.
4. If you have any medical conditions, please specify them in the "Medical Conditions" field.
5. Select "True" if you grant permission for your emergency contact to be notified in case of an emergency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (`emergency_contact_first_name`, text, optional): Enter the first name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (`emergency_contact_last_name`, text, optional): Enter the last name of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact (e.g., parent, spouse, friend).
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, note, optional): List any medical conditions or allergies that may affect your participation in the fitness activity.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Permission** (`emergency_contact_permission`, select one, optional): Select "True" if you grant permission for your emergency contact to be notified in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Procedure** (`medical_procedure`, text, optional): Specify the medical procedure you are undergoing or have undergone.
* **Other Medical Procedure** (`medical_procedure_other`, text, optional): If you have undergone a medical procedure not listed above, describe it in this field.
* **Other** (`medical_procedure_other`, text, optional): If you have any other medical information that is not covered above, provide details in this field.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are completed accurately and honestly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask the staff or instructor for assistance.
* If you have any medical information that is not listed, please provide details in the "Medical Conditions" field.
* If you grant permission for your emergency contact to be notified, please ensure they are available and willing to receive notifications.
