# Air Jump Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Air Jump Registration Form is a mandatory form that needs to be completed by all participants before attending an air jump event. It collects vital information about the participant, including personal details, jump experience, and medical clearance. This form helps us to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for everyone involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your first and last name in the respective fields.
3. Provide your date of birth, age, and current weight.
4. Select your gender and preferred jump type.
5. Enter your height and weight in feet and pounds, respectively.
6. Enter your contact email address and phone number.
7. Enter your mailing address, city, state, and zip code.
8. Select your emergency contact name, relationship, and phone number.
9. Choose your jump experience level and type.
10. Select your preferred jump date and time.
11. Choose your alternative jump date and time (if applicable).
12. List any medical conditions that may affect your jump experience.
13. Indicate if you have medical clearance or if it's not required.
14. List any current medications that may impact your jump experience.
15. Indicate if you have any previous jump injuries.
16. Select your harness size based on your weight and height.
17. Choose if you want to purchase a photo or video package after your jump.
18. Select your T-shirt size if you're planning to purchase one.
19. Acknowledge the liability waiver agreement.
20. Confirm that you understand and accept the risks involved in the jump.
21. Confirm your health declaration.
22. Sign and date the form digitally.
