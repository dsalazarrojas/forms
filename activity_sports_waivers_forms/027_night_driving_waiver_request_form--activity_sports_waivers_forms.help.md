# Night Driving Waiver Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from drivers to verify their qualifications for night driving. It is used to request a waiver to drive at night, allowing users to specify their vehicle and driving experience information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct option for the Night Driving Waiver Request question (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
2. Enter your name in the Name field.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field.
4. If applicable, enter your phone number in the Phone field.
5. Enter your address in the Address field (note: this is a text area for additional information).
6. Accept the Waiver Agreement by typing out the full agreement provided.
7. Provide details on your driving experience, including your driving license number, expiration date, and vehicle details (make, model, year, and plate number).
8. Specify the total hours you have driven at night, the hours you practice driving each week, and the practice experience you have.
9. Select the type of vehicle you drive (SUV, Car, or Truck).
10. Enter your vehicle color and insurance information.
11. Enter your current driving license number and expiration date.
12. Enter the total hours you have driven and the hours you practice each week.
13. Select your practice experience (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
14. Finally, click Submit to submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Night Driving Waiver Request** (`night_driving_waiver_request_form`): Select the correct option to request a waiver to drive at night.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your driver's license.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter your date of birth or the date your driver's license was issued. (Optional)
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address where we can reach you.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes. (Optional)
* **Address** (`address`): Enter your home address or any other relevant information.
* **Waiver Agreement** (`waiver_agreement`): Accept this waiver by typing out the full agreement provided.
* **Driving Experience** (`driving_experience`): Describe your driving experience, including your driving license number, expiration date, and vehicle details.
* **Driver License** (`driving_license`): Enter your driver's license number.
* **Driving License Exp** (`driving_license_exp`): Enter your driver's license expiration date.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`): Enter your vehicle's make.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`): Enter your vehicle's model.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`): Enter your vehicle's year.
* **Vehicle Plate** (`vehicle_plate`): Enter your vehicle's license plate number.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact.
* **Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact. (Optional)
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`): Select the type of vehicle you drive (SUV, Car, or Truck).
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`): Enter your vehicle's color.
* **Vehicle Insurance** (`vehicle_insurance`): Enter your vehicle's insurance information.
* **Driving License** (`driving_license_number`): Enter your driver's license number.
* **Driving License Exp** (`driving_license_exp`): Enter your driver's license expiration date.
* **Hours Driven Night** (`hours_driven_night`): Enter the total hours you have driven at night.
* **Hours Driven Total** (`hours_driven_total`): Enter the total hours you have driven.
* **Hours Practice** (`hours_practice`): Enter the hours you practice driving each week.
* **Practice Experience** (`practice_experience`): Select your practice experience (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Submit** (`submit`): Click the Submit button to submit your request.
