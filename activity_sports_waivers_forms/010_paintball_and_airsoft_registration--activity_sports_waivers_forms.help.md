# Paintball And Airsoft Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for paintball and airsoft events. It is a registration form that captures essential details from participants, including their contact information, scheduling preferences, and safety acknowledgments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Click on the "Complete Registration" button** to begin.
2. Provide your **Registration Details** in the first field. This is a free-text field where you can enter any relevant information about your registration.
3. Choose your **Participant Details** in the second field. Enter your name and any other relevant details.
4. Select your **Scheduling Preferences** in the third field. Choose from Morning, Afternoon, or Evening to indicate your preferred time slot.
5. Read and acknowledge the **Safety Acknowledgements** in the fourth field. This is a text field where you can confirm you have read and understood the safety guidelines.
6. Enter your **Participant Email** in the fifth field. This is the email address where we will contact you for event updates and information.
7. Enter your **Participant Phone** in the sixth field. This is your contact phone number for emergencies or last-minute updates.
8. If you have any **Participant Notes**, enter them in the seventh field. This is an optional field for any additional information you wish to provide.
9. Finally, select your **Event Scheduling** in the eighth field. Choose your preferred time slot for the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Registration Details (text)**: Enter any relevant information about your registration.
- **Participant Details (text)**: Enter your name and any other relevant details.
- **Scheduling Preferences (select_multiple)**: Choose your preferred time slot for the event (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
- **Safety Acknowledgements (text)**: Confirm you have read and understood the safety guidelines.
- **Participant Email (email)**: Enter your valid email address for event updates and information.
- **Participant Phone (text)**: Enter your contact phone number for emergencies or last-minute updates.
- **Participant Notes (note)**: Enter any additional information you wish to provide.
- **Event Scheduling (time)**: Choose your preferred time slot for the event.

## Tips
- Ensure you provide valid email and phone numbers for effective communication.
- Choose your scheduling preferences carefully to ensure you are available for the event.
- Read and understand the safety guidelines carefully before proceeding.
- Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you need assistance or have questions, contact the event organizers at your registered email address.
