# Demo Ride Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a liability waiver for participants in a demo ride event. It is intended to confirm the participant's consent to participate and gather contact information for emergency purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the purpose and requirements.
2. Fill out the "Participant Details" page with your personal information.
3. Provide the emergency contact information, including the contact person's name and phone number.
4. Confirm your participation by selecting the "Waiver Consent" option.
5. Sign the form electronically to confirm your participation.
6. Verify that all information is accurate and complete before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details**: This page collects your personal information, including your name and any other details that we may need to know about you. Please fill out this page carefully and accurately.
* **Emergency Contact**: If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their name and phone number. This information will be used in case of an emergency.
* **Waiver Consent**: This field requires you to confirm whether you consent to participate in the demo ride event. Select "I confirm my participation" if you are willing to participate, and "I do not confirm my participation" if you are not.
* **Participant Signature**: This field is for you to sign the form electronically, confirming that you have read and understood the terms of the waiver.
* **Participant Name**: Please fill out your name accurately and completely.
* **Participant Email**: If you have an email address, please provide it. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Participant Phone**: If you have a phone number, please provide it.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their name.
* **Relationship**: If the emergency contact person is a family member or friend, please describe your relationship with them.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you need to fill out this form multiple times, ensure that you have received approval from the form administrator or are using the same email address each time you submit the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask the form administrator for clarification.
