# Robot Activity Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a liability waiver from participants in robot activities. The waiver is a legal document that protects both the participant and the robot owner/parent from any potential liability in case of accidents or injuries during robot activity events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Participant Information page with your details.
2. Move on to the Contact Information page and provide the contact details of a trusted individual in case of emergencies.
3. On the Parent/Guardian Information page, provide the contact information of your parent or guardian (if applicable).
4. Move on to the Robot Information page, where you will provide details about your robot.
5. On the Waiver page, acknowledge that you have read and understood the waiver terms and conditions.
6. Sign and date the signature field.
7. Finally, review and submit the form to complete it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (page 1)
	+ **Participant Name**, **Age**, and **Contact Information**:
		- Fill out your name, age, and contact details (phone number, email, or address) for emergency purposes.
* **Contact Information** (page 2)
	+ **Name**, **Phone**, and **Email**:
		- Provide the contact details of a trusted individual who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Parent/Guardian Information** (page 3)
	+ **Name**, **Phone**, and **Email**:
		- If you are under 18, provide the contact information of your parent or guardian.
* **Robot Information** (page 4)
	+ **Robot Type**, **Age**, **Description**, **Serial Number**, and **Model**:
		- Choose the type of robot you are participating with (e.g., robot, robot kit, etc.).
		- Select the age of your robot.
		- Describe your robot in a few words.
		- Enter the serial number of your robot.
		- Enter the model of your robot.
* **Waiver** (page 5)
	+ **Is this Robot under warranty?**, **Warranty Expiration Date**, and **Return Policy**:
		- Indicate if your robot is under warranty or not.
		- If under warranty, enter the expiration date of the warranty.
		- Choose the return policy for your robot (e.g., self, parent, etc.).
* **Second Signature** (page 6)
	+ **Signature** and **Date Signed**:
		- Sign and date the second signature field to confirm that you have read and understood the waiver terms and conditions.
* **Who is the Robot Owner?** (page 7)
	+ **Self**, **Parent**, or **Guardian**:
		- Choose who owns the robot (e.g., self, parent, etc.).
* **Address of Robot Owner** (page 8)
	+ **Address**:
		- Enter the address of the robot owner.
* **Phone of Robot Owner** (page 9)
	+ **Phone Number**:
		- Enter the phone number of the robot owner.
* **Email of Robot Owner** (page 10)
	+ **Email Address**:
		- Enter the email address of the robot owner.
* **Name of Robot** (page 11)
	+ **Robot Name**:
		- Enter the name of your robot.
* **Model of Robot** (page 12)
	+ **Robot Model**:
		- Enter the model of your robot.
* **Serial Number of Robot** (page 13)
	+ **Serial Number**:
		- Enter the serial number of your robot.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and carefully to avoid any errors.
* If you are under 18, ensure that you have the permission of your parent or guardian to participate in robot activities.
* Review the waiver terms and conditions carefully before signing.
* Keep a copy of the signed waiver form for your records.
