# Quad Bike Safety Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quad Bike Safety Assessment form is designed to gather information about a rider's safety level and provide guidance on areas for improvement. This form is used to ensure that riders are properly equipped and prepared for quad bike riding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Enter your first name in the `Rider First Name` field.
* Enter your last name in the `Rider Last Name` field.
* Select your date of birth in the `Date of Birth` field.
* Enter your contact number in the `Contact Number` field.
* Select the type of vehicle you are riding in the `Vehicle Type` field.
* Enter the make of your vehicle in the `Vehicle Make` field.
* Enter the year of your vehicle in the `Vehicle Year` field.
* Select whether you have a bike license in the `Rider Bike Licence` field.
* Select whether you wear a safety helmet in the `Safety Helmet Worn` field.
* Select whether you wear a safety jacket in the `Safety Jacket Worn` field.
* Select whether you wear safety boots in the `Safety Boots Worn` field.
* Review and complete the `Pre-Ride Checklist` field, which is a text area where you can provide any additional information or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rider First Name (Text)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Rider Last Name (Text)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth (Date)**: Select your date of birth to ensure that you are eligible to ride a quad bike.
* **Contact Number (Text)**: Enter a contact number where you can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Vehicle Type (Select One)**: Select the type of vehicle you are riding, such as "Quad Bike" or "Other".
* **Vehicle Make (Text)**: Enter the make of your vehicle, such as "Honda" or "Yamaha".
* **Vehicle Year (Number)**: Enter the year of your vehicle, such as 2005 or 2020.
* **Rider Bike Licence (Select One)**: Select whether you have a bike license, which is required to ride a quad bike.
* **Safety Helmet Worn (Select One)**: Select whether you wear a safety helmet while riding.
* **Safety Jacket Worn (Select One)**: Select whether you wear a safety jacket while riding.
* **Safety Boots Worn (Select One)**: Select whether you wear safety boots while riding.
* **Pre-Ride Checklist (Note)**: Provide any additional information or comments that may be relevant to your safety assessment.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have all the necessary information and equipment ready before filling out this form.
* Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please contact our support team for assistance.
