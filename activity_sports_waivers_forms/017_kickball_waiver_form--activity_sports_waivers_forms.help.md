# Kickball Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants to sign a waiver before playing kickball, acknowledging the risks associated with the sport.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields.
2. Ensure you have the necessary information about your emergency contact.
3. Sign and date the waiver.
4. Have a witness sign and date the waiver below your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person you want as your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose how you know the emergency contact (e.g., parent, guardian, friend).
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): If you have any medical conditions that may affect your participation, enter them here.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, optional): If you have a medical history that may affect your participation, enter it here.
* **Description** (`medical_history_description`, `text`, optional): Provide a description of your medical history.
* **Treatment** (`medical_history_treatment`, `text`, optional): List any treatments you have undergone for your medical history.
* **Waiver Signed** (`waiver_signed`, `date`, required): Sign and date this waiver to confirm you have read and understood the terms.
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, `text`, required): Sign here to verify the participant's signature.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person witnessing your signature.

Note: The fields with `required: true` must be filled out to complete the form.
