# Adventure Sports Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants to acknowledge and accept the risks associated with adventure sports activities. It requires participants to provide personal information, confirm their participation, and release the operator from liability in case of accidents or injuries.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely.
2. Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review each field carefully before proceeding.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your acknowledgment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name**: Enter your legal full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your birthdate in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address**: Enter your current residential address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter a valid phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address for communication.
* **Activity Name**: Enter the name of the adventure sport activity you'll be participating in.
* **Activity Date**: Enter the date when the activity will take place.
* **Activity Location**: Enter the location where the activity will occur.
* **Inherent Risks Acknowledged**: Confirm that you understand that adventure sports activities have inherent risks.
* **Injury Assumption**: Confirm that you understand that injuries can occur during the activity.
* **Death Risk Acknowledgment**: Confirm that you acknowledge the possibility of death during the activity.
* **Equipment Use Acknowledgment**: Confirm that you agree to use provided equipment properly.
* **Liability Release**: Confirm that you release the operator from liability for injuries or accidents.
* **Indemnification Agreement**: Confirm that you agree to indemnify the operator against claims.
* **Photography Consent**: Confirm that you consent to being photographed during the activity. (Note: You can select "Only with permission" if you want to be asked for permission before being photographed.)
* **Signature Date**: Date and sign the form to confirm your acknowledgment.
