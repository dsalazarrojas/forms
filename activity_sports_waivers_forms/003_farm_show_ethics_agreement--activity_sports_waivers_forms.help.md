# Farm Show Ethics Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Farm Show Ethics Agreement" is a crucial document that outlines the rules and guidelines for attendees of a farm show. By signing this form, you acknowledge that you have read, understand, and will comply with the specified rules, ensuring a safe and enjoyable experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully to ensure you understand the expectations and rules outlined.
2. Fill out the required information accurately and honestly.
3. For questions that don't apply to you, leave them blank or fill in "N/A" (Not Applicable).
4. Sign the "Signature" field to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Info** (General Info, text, optional):
	+ Enter a brief description about yourself, including your name, farm/organization name, and contact information.
- **Event Rules** (Event Rules, text, optional):
	+ Provide details about your understanding of the event rules, such as arrival and departure times, prohibited activities, and emergency contact information.
- **Animal Welfare** (Animal Welfare, text, optional):
	+ Describe how you will handle animal welfare during the event, such as proper handling, care, and emergency procedures.
- **Conduct Standards** (Conduct Standards, text, optional):
	+ Outline your plan for adhering to conduct standards, including behavior, communication, and respect for others.
- **Signature** (Signature, select_one, optional):
	+ Select one of the provided options to confirm your agreement:
		+ Option 1: I have read, understand, and will abide by the event rules and guidelines.
		+ Option 2: I have not read or understand the event rules and guidelines.
		+ Option 3: I have questions or concerns regarding the event rules and guidelines.
- **Date Signed** (Date Signed, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date you sign this form.
- **Employee Info** (Employee Info, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select any relevant employee information:
		+ Option 1: Farm Manager
		+ Option 2: Farmhand
		+ Option 3: Other (please specify)
- **Contact Info** (Contact Info, text, optional):
	+ Provide emergency contact information, including name and phone number.
- **Emergency Info** (Emergency Info, text, optional):
	+ Describe any emergency procedures, such as emergency exit routes and first aid kits.
- **Farm Info** (Farm Info, text, optional):
	+ Give a brief description of your farm, including name and location.
- **Farm Show Info** (Farm Show Info, text, optional):
	+ Provide any necessary farm show information, such as event dates, start and end times, and gate hours.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you're unsure about any of the rules or guidelines, ask a show representative for clarification.
* Remember to sign the form after you've reviewed and agreed to the terms.
