# Personal Training Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a waiver for personal training, used to collect information about the participant, their emergency contacts, and any medical conditions or allergies they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and provide clear and accurate information for each field.
2. Enter your name and date of birth in the "Participant Name" and "Date of Birth" fields, respectively.
3. Provide the name, phone number, and relationship of your emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Name", "Emergency Contact Phone", and "Emergency Contact Relationship" fields, respectively.
4. If applicable, list any medical conditions, procedures, or surgeries you have in the "Medical Conditions", "Medical Procedure or Surgery", and "Medication" fields.
5. If applicable, list any allergies you have in the "Allergies" field.
6. If you have an alternate contact person, provide their name and phone number in the "Alternate Contact Name" and "Alternate Contact Phone" fields.
7. Review your information carefully and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Training Waiver Form** (`personal_training_waiver_form`, text, required): This is the title of the form. You do not need to fill in this field.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it should be printed on the waiver.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Please enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Please enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Please describe your relationship to the emergency contact person (e.g., "Parent", "Spouse", etc.).
* **Alternate Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name_2`, text, required): If you have an alternate contact person, please enter their name.
* **Alternate Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone_2`, text, required): If you have an alternate contact person, please enter their phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, required): If you have an emergency contact person's email address, please enter it.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions or procedures, please list them here.
* **Medical Procedure or Surgery** (`medical_procedure`, text, optional): If you have had any medical procedures or surgeries, please list them here.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, optional): If you are taking any medication, please list it here.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): If you have any allergies, please list them here.

## Tips
* Please review your information carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification.
* If you need help with completing the form, please contact us.
