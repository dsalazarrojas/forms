# Youth Sports Publicity Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Sports Publicity Consent Form is designed to collect consent from parents or guardians for the media representation of their child participating in youth sports. This form is a requirement for participating in youth sports programs and ensures that the parents or guardians are aware of and agree to the publication of their child's likeness in media.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the child's **First Name** in the first field.
2.  Enter the child's **Last Name** in the second field.
3.  Optionally, enter the child's **Birthdate** if available.
4.  Enter your **Address** where you can be reached.
5.  Enter your **Email** address where you can be reached.
6.  Enter your **Phone Number** where you can be reached.
7.  Select **Consent** to publication by choosing "True" or "False".
8.  Select up to three **Publicity Images** that your child is comfortable with being represented by.
9.  Optionally, select up to three **Publicity Audio** recordings that your child is comfortable with being represented by.
10. Select up to three **Publicity Videos** that your child is comfortable with being represented by.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your child's first name.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your child's last name.
*   **Birthdate** (`birthdate`, date, optional): Enter your child's birthdate if available.
*   **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address where you can be reached.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
*   **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Select "True" to agree to publication or "False" to decline.
*   **Publicity Images** (`publicity_image`, select_multiple, required): Select up to three publicity images that your child is comfortable with being represented by.
*   **Publicity Audio** (`publicity_audio`, select_multiple, optional): Select up to three publicity audio recordings that your child is comfortable with being represented by.
*   **Publicity Videos** (`publicity_video`, select_multiple, required): Select up to three publicity videos that your child is comfortable with being represented by.
