# sports_facility_marketing_event_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect consent and other relevant information from individuals participating in sports facility marketing events. It ensures that participants are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions of the event, including marketing permissions and waiver requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Provide Your Participant Details**: Please fill out the "Participant Details" page, including any required information about yourself.
2. **List Your Emergency Contacts**: Enter the contact information of a person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
3. **Check Your Marketing Permissions**: Read and agree to the marketing permissions section, which describes how your data may be used.
4. **Provide Event Details**: Enter any relevant information about the event you are participating in.
5. **Accept the Waiver**: Read and accept the waiver carefully, as it outlines your understanding of the risks associated with participating in the event.
6. **Understand and Agree to the Participant Agreement**: Carefully read the participant agreement, which outlines your responsibilities and expectations during the event.
7. **Understand and Agree to the Marketing Agreement**: Read and understand the marketing agreement, which describes how your data will be used for marketing purposes.
8. **Provide Information about Your Data**: Enter any relevant information about your data that you are willing to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details (Page 1)**: Enter your name, email, and any other relevant information about yourself as a participant.
* **Emergency Contacts (Page 2)**: Enter the name, email, phone number, and any other contact information of a person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Marketing Permissions (Page 3)**: This section describes how your data may be used for marketing purposes, including social media, email, and other promotional activities. Read carefully and agree to the terms.
* **Event Details (Page 4)**: Enter any relevant information about the event you are participating in, including dates, times, and locations.
* **Waiver (Page 5)**: Read and accept the waiver carefully, as it outlines your understanding of the risks associated with participating in the event.
* **Participant Agreement (Page 6)**: Carefully read and agree to the participant agreement, which outlines your responsibilities and expectations during the event.
* **Marketing Agreement (Page 7)**: Understand and agree to the marketing agreement, which describes how your data will be used for marketing purposes.
* **Participant Data (Page 8)**: Enter any information you are willing to share about your data, including any specific details or preferences.
