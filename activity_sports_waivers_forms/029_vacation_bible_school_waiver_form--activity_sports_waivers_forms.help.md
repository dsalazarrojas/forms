# Vacation Bible School Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a waiver for students participating in Vacation Bible School activities. It is used to obtain consent from parents or guardians for their child's participation and to gather necessary contact and medical information in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with the required information.
2. Review the form carefully to ensure you have provided all necessary details.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent's Name** (parent_name): This is the name of the parent or guardian filling out this form.
* **Parent Contact Information** (parent_contact_info): This is an optional field for providing additional contact information, such as email or phone number, in case we need to reach out to you.
* **Student's Name** (student_name): This is the name of the student participating in Vacation Bible School.
* **Student's Date of Birth** (student_date_of_birth): This is the date of birth of the student participating in Vacation Bible School.
* **Student's School** (student_school): This is an optional field to specify which school the student attends. Please select from the available options (First Day of School, Second Day of School, Third Day of School, Full Week, Full Month, Full Year).
* **Student's Grade Level** (student_grade): This is an optional field to specify the grade level of the student participating in Vacation Bible School. Please select from the available options (Pre-K, K-1, K-2, First Grade, Second Grade, Third Grade, Fourth Grade, Fifth Grade, Sixth Grade, Seventh Grade, Eighth Grade, Ninth Grade, Tenth Grade, Eleventh Grade, Twelfth Grade, Thirteenth Grade).
* **Parent's Signature** (parent_signature): Please sign this section to confirm that you have reviewed and agree to the terms of this waiver.
* **Guardian Consent** (guardian_consent): This is an optional field to indicate if you have or have not given consent for your child to participate in Vacation Bible School activities. Please select 'Yes', 'No', or 'Decline'.
* **Emergency Contact** (emergency_contact): This is an optional field to provide emergency contact information, such as name, phone number, and relationship to the student.
* **Medical Information** (medical_info): This is an optional field to list any medical conditions or allergies the student may have.
* **Medication List** (medication_list): This is an optional field to list any medications the student takes.
* **Medical Conditions** (medical_conditions): This is an optional field to provide any additional medical conditions or concerns.
* **Emergency Medical Instructions** (emergency_medical_instructions): This is an optional field to provide any emergency medical instructions.
* **Medical Release** (medical_release): This is an optional field for you to authorize the student to receive medical treatment in case of an emergency.
