# Surf School Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather participant information, including contact details, medical conditions, and emergency contact information from participants or their parents/guardians for a surf school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, date of birth, and any medical conditions.
2. Provide the name, address, phone number, and email of a parent or guardian if you are under 18.
3. List the name and relationship of the emergency contact person.
4. Enter the contact information for the emergency contact person, including phone number and email.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm you have read and understood the waiver.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information**: Enter your full name and date of birth.
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Parent or Guardian Information**: Enter the name and address of a parent or guardian who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Parent Name**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian who signed below.
* **Parent Relationship**: Describe your relationship with the parent or guardian (e.g., mother, father, custodian).
* **Contact Information**: Enter the contact information for the parent or guardian, including phone number and email.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Email**: Enter the email of the parent or guardian.
* **Medical Conditions**: If you have any medical conditions that may affect your surfing ability, list them here.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Describe the relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Signature**: Sign and date below to confirm you have read and understood this waiver.
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Date Received**: Enter the date this form was received by the surf school.
* **Instructor Signature**: If the instructor who signed below was not the original signatory, please enter their name and title.
* **Instructor Name**: Enter the name of the instructor who signed this form.
* **Title**: Enter the title of the instructor who signed this form.
* **Instructor Phone**: Enter the phone number of the instructor who signed this form.
* **Instructor Email**: Enter the email of the instructor who signed this form.

Note: This form is not a legal contract, but rather a waiver to be signed by the participant or their parent/guardian before engaging in surfing activities.
