# Trail Maintenance Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain liability waivers from participants in trail maintenance activities. It helps to ensure that participants are aware of and acknowledge the risks associated with these activities and provides emergency contact information in case of an accident.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: Participant Name, Email, and Emergency Contact Name (if applicable).
2. If applicable, fill out the emergency contact information (Emergency Contact Relationship, Emergency Contact Name, Emergency Contact Phone Number, and Emergency Contact Address).
3. Check the box for Parent/Guardian Signature and Participant Signature to acknowledge that you have read and understand the waiver.
4. Review and sign the waiver form (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trail Maintenance Liability Waiver**: This is the main content of the waiver form, which outlines the risks and responsibilities associated with trail maintenance activities. Please review this carefully before completing the form.
* **Participant Name**: This is the name of the participant who will be involved in the trail maintenance activity. Please enter the participant's name accurately.
* **Email**: This is the email address of the participant. If you have an email address, please enter it.
* **Phone Number**: This is the phone number of the participant. If you have a phone, please enter it.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Check this box if the emergency contact is a relative or family member of the participant.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: This is the name of the person who will be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number**: This is the phone number of the person who will be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: This is the address of the person who will be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Parent/Guardian Signature**: Check this box if you are a parent or guardian of the participant and are signing on their behalf.
* **Participant Signature**: Check this box if you are the participant and are signing on your own behalf.
* **Participant Signature**: This box is checked to acknowledge that the participant has read and understood the waiver form.
* **Participant Signature**: This box is checked to confirm that the participant has agreed to the terms of the waiver form. 

Note: Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and truthfully. If you have any doubts or questions, please contact the trail maintenance team.
