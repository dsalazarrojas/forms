# Swimming Championship Participation Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a participation agreement for the swimming championship, where swimmers need to provide personal details, medical information, emergency contacts, and consent to participate in the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the personal details of the swimmer on the first page, including any relevant information.
2. Provide medical information on the second page to ensure the safety and well-being of the swimmer during the event.
3. List emergency contacts on the third page, including contact names, addresses, and phone numbers.
4. Select the relevant options for consent on the fourth page, indicating who can consent for the swimmer.
5. Sign and date the form to indicate completion and agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Swimmer Details** (`swimmer_details`, `text`, required: false):
    Provide the swimmer's name, age, contact number, and any other relevant details.
* **Medical Information** (`medical_info`, `text`, required: false):
    Enter any relevant medical information that may affect the swimmer's participation, such as allergies or medical conditions.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, `text`, required: false):
    List the names, addresses, and contact numbers of people who should be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
    Select one or more of the options: "Swimmer and Parent/Guardian," "Swimmer," or "Coach/Trainer" to indicate who can consent for the swimmer.
* **Signatures** (`signatures`, `text`, required: false):
    Sign and date the form to confirm completion and agreement.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully to ensure the swimmer's safety and well-being during the event.
- If the swimmer is under 18, please ensure that a parent or guardian signs the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the event organizers or officials.
