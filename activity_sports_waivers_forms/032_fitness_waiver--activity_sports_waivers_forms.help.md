# Fitness Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals participating in fitness activities or programs. This information will be used to ensure their safety and liability. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to help us better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "First Name" field with your full first name.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field. Make sure to follow the email format (e.g., username@domain.com).
4. Enter your birth date in the "Birth Date" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your full first name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Last Name**: Enter your full last name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Email**: Enter your email address in the format of username@domain.com. This will be used to contact you with important information or updates.
* **Birth Date**: Enter your birth date in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in the format (###)#####, with no dashes or parentheses. 

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields for a smooth and accurate processing of your information.
- If you have any issues or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us at your provided email address.
