# Team Video Analysis Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is required for team members and their parents or guardians to provide consent for the analysis of video recordings. The form collects information about the team, parent, and recording details, including type, duration, and purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Enter the name of your team in the first field.
* Provide the parent's name and their contact information (email and phone number).
* Select the type of video recording (e.g., Video Recording, Audio Recording, etc.).
* Choose the purpose of the recording (e.g., practice, game, etc.).
* Provide the date and time of the recording.
* Enter the duration of the recording.
* Review and agree to the terms, privacy policy, and disclaimer.
* Sign the form as a parent or guardian to confirm consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team**: Enter the name of your team.
* **Parent**: Enter the parent's name.
* **Guardian Consent**: Select "Yes" to confirm that the parent or guardian has given consent for the recording.
* **Date of Video Recording**: Enter the date of the video recording.
* **Time of Video Recording**: Enter the time of the video recording.
* **Video Recording Duration**: Enter the duration of the recording in minutes.
* **Video Recording Type**: Select the type of recording (e.g., Video Recording, Audio Recording, etc.).
* **Consent Type**: Select "Yes" to confirm that the parent or guardian has given consent for the recording.
* **Recording Purposes**: Select the purpose of the recording (e.g., practice, game, etc.).
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the recording.
* **Email**: Enter the parent's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the parent's phone number.
* **Team Video Analysis Consent Form Terms**: Review the terms and conditions of the consent form.
* **Team Video Analysis Consent Form Privacy**: Review the privacy policy of the consent form.
* **Team Video Analysis Consent Form Disclaimer**: Review the disclaimer of the consent form.
* **Team Video Analysis Consent Form Agreement**: Review the agreement of the consent form.
* **Parent Signature**: Sign the form as a parent or guardian to confirm consent.
* **Parent Date**: Enter the date of the signature.
* **Parent Time**: Enter the time of the signature.
* **Parent Phone**: Enter the parent's phone number.
* **Note 2**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the recording (optional).
* **Email 2**: Enter the parent's email address (optional).
* **Phone 2**: Enter the parent's phone number (optional).
* **Terms 2**: Review the terms and conditions (optional).
* **Privacy 2**: Review the privacy policy (optional).
* **Terms 2**: Review the disclaimer (optional).
