# sports_co_op_registration_and_waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering and obtaining waiver from participants for various sports co-operative activities. It collects participant information, emergency contact details, and consent for participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the participant's details in the **Participant Details** section.
2. Select which activities the participant will be participating in by choosing options in the **Activity 1** and **Activity 2** sections.
3. Provide emergency contact information for the participant in the **Participant Emergency Contact** section.
4. Confirm the participant's name and phone number in the **Participant Emergency Contact Name** and **Participant Emergency Contact Phone** fields.
5. Read and agree to the terms and conditions in the **Consent** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details**: Enter the participant's basic information.
* **Activity 1**: Select which activity the participant will be participating in.
* **Activity 2**: Select which activity the participant will be participating in.
* **Participant Emergency Contact**: Provide the participant's emergency contact information.
* **Participant Emergency Contact Name**: Confirm the participant's name.
* **Participant Emergency Contact Phone**: Confirm the participant's phone number.
* **Consent**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Signed Consent**: Select whether you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions or not.
