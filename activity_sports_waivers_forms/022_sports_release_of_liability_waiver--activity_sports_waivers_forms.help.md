# sports_release_of_liability_waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Release of Liability Waiver form is used to collect information from participants and their parents or legal guardians to ensure their safety and well-being during sports activities. This form helps to identify potential medical conditions, emergency contacts, and other important details that are necessary for providing proper care and attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant's name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Provide your name (parent or legal guardian) in the "Parent or Legal Guardian Name" field.
3. Enter your participant's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field (if required).
4. Fill in your participant's address in the "Address" field.
5. Enter your participant's phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Provide your email address (if available).
7. Identify your relationship with the participant in the "Emergency Contact" field.
8. Enter your emergency contact's phone number in the "Emergency Phone" field.
9. Confirm if your emergency contact has any medical conditions by answering "Yes" or "No" in the "Relationship" field.
10. Enter the date and time of the waiver in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
11. Sign your signature in the "Signature" field.
12. Sign on behalf of your participant in the "Participant Signature" field.
13. Enter your participant's date of birth in the "Participant Date" field.
14. Enter the time of the waiver for your participant in the "Participant Time" field.
15. Enter your participant's age in the "Age" field.
16. Select your participant's sex ("Yes" or "No" in the "Sex" field).
17. Provide your participant's height in feet and inches in the "Height (in ft and in)" field.
18. Enter your participant's weight in pounds in the "Weight (in lbs)" field.
19. If your participant has any medical conditions, enter them in the "Medical Condition" field (if required).
20. Provide more details about your participant's medical conditions in the "Medical Condition Details" field (if required).
21. Confirm if your emergency contact has any medical conditions by entering "Yes" or "No" in the "Emergency Contact Medical Condition" field.
22. Enter more details about your emergency contact's medical conditions in the "Emergency Contact Medical Condition" field (if required).
23. Enter the name of the activity your participant will be participating in in the "Activity Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your participant's name.
* **Parent or Legal Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter your name (parent or legal guardian).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your participant's date of birth (if required).
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your participant's address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your participant's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your participant's email address (if available).
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Identify your relationship with the participant.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, select_one, required): Confirm if your emergency contact has any medical conditions by answering "Yes" or "No".
* **Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the waiver.
* **Time** (`signature_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the waiver.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required): Sign your signature.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, text, required): Sign on behalf of your participant.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, required): Sign on behalf of your participant.
* **Participant Date** (`participant_date`, date, required): Enter your participant's date of birth.
* **Participant Time** (`participant_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the waiver for your participant.
* **Age** (`participant_age`, text, required): Enter your participant's age.
* **Sex** (`participant_sex`, select_one, required): Select your participant's sex ("Yes" or "No").
* **Height (in ft and in)** (`participant_height`, text, required): Enter your participant's height in feet and inches.
* **Weight (in lbs)** (`participant_weight`, text, required): Enter your participant's weight in pounds.
* **Medical Condition** (`participant_medical_condition`, text, optional): If your participant has any medical conditions, enter them here.
* **Medical Condition Details** (`participant_medical_condition_details`, text, optional): Provide more details about your participant's medical conditions.
* **Emergency Contact Medical Condition** (`emergency_medical_condition`, text, optional): Confirm if your emergency contact has any medical conditions by answering "Yes" or "No".
* **Activity Name** (`activity_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the activity your participant will be participating in.
