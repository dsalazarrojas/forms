# Liability And Terms Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Liability And Terms Agreement form is used to gather essential information and obtain consent from participants in a sports-related activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your participant details, including your name and any other relevant information.
2. Enter your contact information, so we can reach you if needed.
3. Indicate your consent for the activities you are participating in.
4. Read and acknowledge the terms and conditions of the activity.
5. Sign your name as a form of agreement and confirmation.
6. Provide your business information if you represent an organization or company.
7. Choose whether you understand and agree to the terms.
8. Finally, provide the date you are completing this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details (text)**: Please enter your name, participant ID (if applicable), or any other identifying information.
* **Contact Information (text)**: Enter your contact information, including phone number and email address, so we can reach you if needed.
* **Waiver Consent (select_multiple)**: Indicate your consent for the participant by selecting from the provided options.
* **Terms and Conditions (text)**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the activity. Make sure you understand what you are agreeing to.
* **Signature (note)**: Sign your name to confirm your agreement and understanding of the terms.
* **Business Information (text)**: If you represent an organization or company, enter your business information such as company name and address.
* **Terms Acknowledgment (select_one)**: Choose whether you understand and agree to the terms by selecting from the provided options.
* **Date (date)**: Enter the date you are completing this form, which will help in tracking the agreement and understanding.

## Tips
- Make sure to read and understand each field carefully before proceeding.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask.
- Ensure that you have the necessary information and consent before completing the form.
- If you need to make any changes or modifications, please let us know.
- Keep a copy of the completed form for your records.
