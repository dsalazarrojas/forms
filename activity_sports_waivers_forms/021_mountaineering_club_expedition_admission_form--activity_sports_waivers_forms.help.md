# mountaineering_club_expedition_admission_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mountaineering Club Expedition Admission Form is used to collect information about you and your emergency contact for an upcoming expedition. This form helps us ensure that we have the necessary information to provide a safe and enjoyable experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and submit it. Please make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information about yourself and your emergency contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **required**): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **required**): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **required**): Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you during the expedition.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **optional**): Please enter a phone number where we can reach you in case of an emergency.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, **required**): Please enter your date of birth.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (`emergency_contact_first_name`, text, **optional**): If you have an emergency contact, please enter their first name.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (`emergency_contact_last_name`, text, **optional**): If you have an emergency contact, please enter their last name.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, **optional**): If you have an emergency contact, please enter their email address.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, **optional**): If you have an emergency contact, please enter their phone number.
* **Expedition Leader** (`expedition_leader`, text, **optional**): If you will be leading an expedition, please enter your name.
* **Expedition Start Date** (`expedition_start_date`, date, **required**): Please enter the start date of your expedition.
* **Expedition End Date** (`expedition_end_date`, date, **required**): Please enter the end date of your expedition.
* **Expedition Duration** (`expedition_duration`, number, **required**): Please enter the number of days your expedition will last.
* **Expedition Type** (`expedition_type`, select_one, **optional**): Please select the type of expedition you will be participating in:
	+ Mountaineering
	+ Hiking
	+ Climbing
	+ Skiing
	+ Other
* **Expedition Location** (`expedition_location`, text, **optional**): Please enter the location of your expedition.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, **optional**): Please enter any additional information about your expedition.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, **optional**): If you have an emergency contact, please describe your relationship with them.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, **optional**): If you have any medical conditions, please list them.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, **optional**): If you have any allergies, please list them.
* **Medical Certificate** (`medical_certificate`, text, **optional**): If you have any medical conditions, please attach a medical certificate as proof.
* **Expedition Experience** (`expedition_experience`, text, **optional**): Please describe your previous expedition experience.
* **Expedition Experience Type** (`expedition_experience_type`, select_multiple, **optional**): Please select the types of expeditions you have experience in:
	+ Rock Climbing
	+ Mountaineering
	+ Hiking
	+ Climbing
	+ Skiing
	+ Other
* **Safety Plan** (`safety_plan`, text, **optional**): Please describe your safety plan for the expedition.
* **Trip Leaders** (`trip_leaders`, text, **optional**): Please list the names and contact information of your trip leaders.
* **Emergency Contact Phone 2** (`emergency_contact_phone_2`, text, **optional**): If you have an emergency contact, please enter their phone number.
