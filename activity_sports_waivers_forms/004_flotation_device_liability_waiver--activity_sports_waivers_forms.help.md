# Flotation Device Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect liability waiver information from participants in a flotation device activity. It's essential to sign this waiver to acknowledge and accept the risks associated with the activity.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

2. **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, text, required: false): Enter the participant's residential or mailing address.
3. **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, required: false): If the participant is under 18 years old, please fill in the name of the guardian or parent who is accompanying them.
4. **Guardian Contact Number** (`guardian_contact_number`, text, required: false): Provide the contact number of the guardian or parent.
5. **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter the participant's date of birth in the format DD/MM/YY (e.g., 01/01/20).
6. **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required: false): Enter the participant's email address (e.g., participant@example.com).
7. **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required: false): Provide the participant's phone number.
8. **Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`, text, required: false): Provide the guardian's phone number.
9. **Guardian Email** (`guardian_email`, email, required: false): Enter the guardian's email address.
10. **Participant Signature** (`participant_sign_date`, date, required: false): Sign and date this section to acknowledge that you understand and agree to the waiver terms.
11. **Guardian Signature** (`guardian_sign_date`, date, required: false): Sign and date this section if the participant is under 18 years old, and you are their guardian or parent.
12. **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, required: false): Please sign your name to confirm that you agree with the waiver terms.
13. **Participant Waiver Text** (`participant_waiver_text`, text, required: false): This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or notes related to the waiver.
14. **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agree`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one of the two options: "I agree" or "I disagree" to confirm that you agree with the waiver terms.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you fill in all the required fields accurately and honestly.
- If the participant is under 18 years old, please provide their guardian or parent's contact information.
- Make sure to review the waiver terms carefully before signing.
