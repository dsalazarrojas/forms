# Youth Sports Concussion Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather information from parents or guardians about their child's involvement in sports activities that may involve risk of head injury. It is an important part of ensuring the safety and well-being of young athletes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you, as the parent or guardian, give your consent for your child to participate in the specified sports activities.
2. If you select "Yes", please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
3. If you select "No", please provide a brief explanation in the space below.
4. Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether your child may be subject to head injuries.
5. If you select "Yes", please enter your child's name.
6. Enter your email address so we can contact you with important information.
7. Enter your phone number so we can contact you with important information.
8. Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether your child participates in sports.
9. Provide a brief explanation about the potential risks and consequences of head injuries in sports activities.
10. Provide a brief explanation about what you, as the parent or guardian, can do to reduce the risk of head injuries in sports activities.
11. Please provide any other information you want to share about head injuries and safety measures.
 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Parent Consent** (`parent_consent`, select_one, required: false): This field asks you to select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you, as the parent or guardian, give your consent for your child to participate in the specified sports activities.
*   **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required: false): If you select "Yes" for the above field, please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Parent Contact Information** (`parent_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you with important information.
*   **Parent Contact Information** (`parent_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with important information.
*   **Child's Sport Participation** (`youth_sport`, select_one, required: false): This field asks you to select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether your child participates in sports activities.
*   **Concussion Information** (`concession_info`, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide a brief explanation about the potential risks and consequences of head injuries in sports activities.
*   **Concussion Information 2** (`concussion_info_2`, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide a brief explanation about what you, as the parent or guardian, can do to reduce the risk of head injuries in sports activities.
*   **Concussion Information 3** (`concussion_info_3`, text, required: false): Please provide any other information you want to share about head injuries and safety measures.
*   **Concussion Information 4** (`concussion_info_4`, text, required: false): Please note that this field is not required.
 
## Tips

*   Please answer all required questions.
*   Please provide accurate and clear information about your child's participation in sports activities.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us with your email or phone number. 

Note: In some cases, fields might be not displayed if a parent/guardian selects "No" in `parent_consent`.
