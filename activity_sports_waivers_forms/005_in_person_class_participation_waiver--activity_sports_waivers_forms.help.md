# In Person Class Participation Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect personal and medical information from students participating in an in-person class or activity. This information is used to ensure that the student's safety and well-being are protected during the class.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow the steps outlined above in the internal reasoning block.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Emergency contact name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Emergency contact relationship**: Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact address**: Enter the address of your emergency contact person.
* **Medical condition**: Select any medical conditions you have.
* **Medical condition details**: Provide details about your medical conditions.
* **Allergies**: Select whether you have any allergies.
* **Allergies**: This field is a duplicate of the previous one, please select whether you have any allergies.
* **Medical condition medication**: Select whether you have any medical condition medications.
* **Medical condition medication other**: If you have medical condition medications, enter the name of the medication.
* **Medical condition medication frequency**: If you have medical condition medications, enter the frequency of the medication.
* **Medical condition medication dosage**: If you have medical condition medications, enter the dosage of the medication.
* **Medical condition medication side effects**: If you have medical condition medications, enter any side effects of the medication.
* **Medical condition medication last administered**: If you have medical condition medications, enter the last time you administered the medication.
* **Emergency contact permission**: Select whether you have permission for instructors or emergency contacts to administer medication.
* **Instructor consent**: Select whether you have given your instructor consent to participate in the class.
* **Instructor signature**: If you have given your instructor consent, sign this field.
* **Participant signature**: If you have participated in the class, sign this field.
* **Date and time**: Enter the date and time of the class.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all the fields accurately and truthfully.
* Some fields are not required but are recommended for your safety and well-being.
* If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please provide detailed information about them.
* If you have any medical condition medications, provide information about them, including the frequency, dosage, and side effects.
