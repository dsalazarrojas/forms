# Air Show Team Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from participants in an air show team event. It includes fields to capture participant details, team information, and waiver acknowledgment. The form helps ensure a smooth and safe experience for all participants by gathering necessary data for event organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Participant Name** in the designated field.
2. If your team has a name, enter it in the **Team Name** field. This is optional.
3. Provide the **Event Date** of the event in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
4. Enter your **Emergency Contact Name and Phone Number** in case of any emergency during the event.
5. If you have any **Medical Conditions or Medications**, please list them in the provided field.
6. Read and acknowledge the **Liability Waiver** by typing your name in the designated field.
7. If applicable, provide **Insurance Details** for any relevant coverage.
8. Finally, date and sign the **Date Signed** field to acknowledge your waiver.
9. If you have any **Additional Notes** for the event organizers, use this field to share them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or personal documents.
* **Team name** (optional): Enter your team name, if applicable.
* **Event date**: Provide the date of the event in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-04-15).
* **Emergency contact name and phone**: Enter the name and phone number of a person who can be contacted in case of an emergency during the event.
* **Medical conditions or medications**: List any medical conditions or medications you are currently under.
* **Liability waiver acknowledgement**: Type your name in this field to indicate your understanding and acceptance of the liability waiver.
* **Insurance details**: If you have insurance, provide the provider's name and policy number for any relevant coverage.
* **Date signed**: Date and sign the waiver to acknowledge your acceptance.
* **Additional notes**: Share any special requests or safety concerns with the event organizers.
