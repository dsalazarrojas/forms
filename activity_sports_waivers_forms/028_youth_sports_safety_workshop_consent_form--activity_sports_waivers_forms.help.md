# Youth Sports Safety Workshop Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Sports Safety Workshop Consent Form is a crucial document that requires parents or guardians to provide their consent and emergency contact information for their children participating in youth sports workshops. This form ensures the child's safety and well-being during the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review each page carefully and ensure you understand the required information.
2.  Fill out the form accurately and honestly, as it is a legal document.
3.  If you are unsure about any information, please ask for clarification from the workshop organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Parent Permissions
*   **Parent Permissions** (`parent_permissions`, select_multiple, required: false): This field asks you to confirm whether you have permission to grant your child access to the workshop activities.
    -   Please select 'Yes' if you have given your child permission to participate in the workshop activities.
    -   Select 'No' if you have not given your child permission.

### Page 2: Emergency Contacts
*   **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, select_one, required: false): This field is used to identify who would be contacted in case of an emergency.
    -   Select 'Parent/Guardian' if you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
    -   Choose 'Emergency Contact' if you have another contact that should be reached first.
    -   If none of the above options apply, select 'Other' and provide details in the text field below.

### Page 3: Health Details
*   **Health Details** (`health_details`, text, required: false): This field is used to provide any health-related information about your child that may be relevant to their safety.
    -   Please fill this field with any medical conditions, allergies, or concerns that might impact your child's participation in the workshop.

### Page 4: Waiver
*   **Waiver** (`waiver`, select_multiple, required: false): This field confirms that you acknowledge and confirm the risks associated with participating in the workshop activities and accept responsibility for your child's actions.
    -   Select 'Yes' to confirm you understand and accept the risks and responsibilities associated with the workshop.
    -   Select 'No' to decline your child's participation in the workshop.

## Tips

*   Please fill out this form carefully and accurately to ensure your child's safety.
*   If you have any concerns or questions, contact the workshop organizers for assistance.
*   This form is a requirement for your child's participation in the workshop.
