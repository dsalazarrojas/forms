# Sports League Data Collection Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports League Data Collection Consent Form is used to collect participant information, emergency contact details, and signature and date for the sports league. This form is essential for data collection and record-keeping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant details (Name, Email, Phone).
2. Enter the emergency contact information (Name, Email, Phone, Relationship, Address).
3. Select the consent options (Full Name, Email, Phone Number, or Emergency Contact 1, 2, or 3).
4. Sign and date the form by entering your signature and the date.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (Participant name): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (Participant phone): Enter your phone number.
* **Emergency Contacts** (Relationship, Name, Email, Phone):
	+ **Relationship**: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., mother, father, sister).
	+ **Name**: Enter the emergency contact's name.
	+ **Email**: Enter the emergency contact's email address.
	+ **Phone**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **I Consent**:
	+ Select all the ways you consent to communicate with you (e.g., phone, email, or both).
* **Signature**: Sign your name to confirm your consent.
* **Date**: Enter the date you sign the form.

## Tips

* Ensure you fill in all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* Review the form carefully before submission.
* Keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
