# Parental Consent For Athletic Health Screening - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for parents or legal guardians to provide consent and information for their child's athletic health screening.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, required): This field captures the guardian's name. Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Guardian Contact Information** (`guardian_contact_info`, text, required): This field captures the guardian's contact information. Please ensure that it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Guardian Relationship** (`guardian_relationship`, select_one, required): Select the relationship between you and the child (Parent, Emergency Contact, Other).
* **Guardian Permission** (`guardian_permission`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you have given or not given permission for your child to participate in sports.
* **Guardian Signature** (`guardian_signature`, note, required): This is a note field for the guardian to sign and date.
* **Doctor Info** (`doctor_info`, text, optional): This field captures any doctor's notes or information related to the child's health.
* **Medical Notes** (`health_notes`, text, optional): This field is for additional medical notes.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): This field captures the emergency contact's name, address, phone number, and relationship with the child.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): This field captures the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This field captures the emergency contact's email.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, text, optional): This field is for additional medical notes.
