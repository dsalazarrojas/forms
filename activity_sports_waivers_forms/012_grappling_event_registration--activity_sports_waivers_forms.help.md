# grappling_event_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "grappling_event_registration" form collects information from participants in a grappling event, such as a jiu-jitsu tournament or a grappling competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your Participant Info, including your name and email address.
2. Enter your Name (First and Last Name) to register for the event.
3. Enter your Participant Phone number for contact purposes.
4. Choose your Experience Level: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
5. Select your Division: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
6. Read and Accept the Waiver to confirm your understanding of the event's terms.
7. Provide Event Details as required by the organizer.
8. Sign and submit your Participant Signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Info (Participant Info)**: Enter your name to be identified as a participant in the event.
* **Name (First and Last Name)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for event communications.
* **Participant Phone (participant_phone)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Experience Level (experience_level)**: Select your grappling experience level: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Division (division)**: Select your grappling division: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Waiver Acceptance (waiver_acceptance)**: Please review and accept the waiver agreement to participate in the event.
* **Event Details (event_details)**: Provide any additional information required by the event organizer.
* **Participant Signature (participant_signature)**: Sign and submit your signature to confirm your participation.
