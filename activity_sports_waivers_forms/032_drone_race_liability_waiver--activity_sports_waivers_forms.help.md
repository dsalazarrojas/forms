# Drone Race Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for obtaining a waiver and liability release from participants in a drone racing event. The purpose of this form is to ensure that participants understand and agree to the risks involved in the event and to release and hold harmless all parties responsible for the event and the drone.

## How To Complete This Form

- Read through the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
- Fill out the personal information section (pages 1-7) with your (or your parent/guardian's) information.
- Select the correct drone type in the "Drone Type" field. This will automatically skip some fields.
- Fill out the event information section (pages 8-16) with the event details.
- Review the waiver and liability release terms in the "Waiver Agreement" field and select the correct options.
- Sign and date the waiver and liability release agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Name**: Enter your name or your parent/guardian's name.
*   **Parent or Guardian Name**: Enter your parent or guardian's name if you are a minor.
*   **Participant Email**: Enter your email address.
*   **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number.
*   **Participant Address**: Enter your address.
*   **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person you would like to contact in case of an emergency.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
*   **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
*   **Parent or Guardian Signature**: Sign this field to confirm you are the parent or guardian of the participant.
*   **Participant Signature**: Sign this field to confirm you are the participant and have read and understood the waiver and liability release agreement.
*   **Emergency Contact Signature**: Sign this field to confirm you are the emergency contact person.
*   **Participant Waiver**: Review and agree to the waiver and liability release terms.
*   **Participant Waiver Date**: Date the waiver and liability release agreement.
*   **Waiver Date**: Date the waiver and liability release agreement.
*   **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
*   **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event.
*   **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event.
*   **Drone Type**: Select the type of drone you will be operating.
*   **Drone Size**: Select the size of the drone you will be operating.
*   **Drone Speed**: Select the speed of the drone you will be operating.
*   **Drone Experience**: Select your level of experience with drones.
*   **Drone License**: Select your type of pilot license.
*   **Event Organizer**: Select the type of event organizer.
*   **Waiver Agreement**: Review and agree to the waiver and liability release terms.
*   **Waiver Time**: Date the waiver and liability release agreement.

## Tips

- Make sure to review the waiver and liability release terms carefully before signing.
- If you are a minor, make sure a parent or guardian signs the waiver and liability release agreement.
- If you are an event organizer, make sure to review the waiver and liability release terms carefully before signing.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, ask an event organizer for clarification.
