# Fitness Transformation Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Transformation Release Form is designed to gather essential information from participants in a fitness or wellness program. This form helps to ensure a smooth and successful transformation journey for the participant.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out this form completely and accurately, providing all requested information.
2. Review the signed consents carefully and sign where required.
3. Ensure that all fields are filled out correctly, as this information will be used to track and support your progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Details
* **Participant Name**: This is the name of the participant who is filling out the form.
* **Participant Phone Number**: This is the contact phone number of the participant.
* **Participant Email**: This is the contact email of the participant.
* **Participant Date of Birth**: This is the date of birth of the participant.
* **Participant Phone Number**: This is the contact phone number of the participant.
* **Participant Email**: This is the contact email of the participant.

### Coach Details
* **Coach Name**: This is the name of the coach assigned to the participant.
* **Coach Phone Number**: This is the contact phone number of the coach.
* **Coach Email**: This is the contact email of the coach.

### Program Enrollment
* **Program Enrollment**: This is where the participant selects their program enrollment options.
* **Program Start Date**: This is the start date of the program.
* **Program End Date**: This is the end date of the program.
* **Participant Program Length**: This is the length of the program that the participant is enrolled in.
* **Program Type**: This is the type of program that the participant is enrolled in.
* **Participant Goals**: This is the goal that the participant is working towards during the program.
* **Program Goals**: This is the goal that the program aims to achieve.

### Signed Consents
* **Signed Consent 1**: This is where the participant signs the first consent agreement.
* **Signed Consent 2**: This is where the participant signs the second consent agreement.
* **Signed Consent 3**: This is where the participant signs the third consent agreement.

### Program Notes
* **Program Notes**: This is the additional information about the program that the participant wants to share.

### Signatures
* **Participant Signature**: This is where the participant signs the form, indicating they have read and understood the contents.
* **Coach Signature**: This is where the coach signs the form, indicating they have reviewed and understood the contents.

### Additional Information
* **Participant Phone Number**: This is the contact phone number of the participant.
* **Coach Phone Number**: This is the contact phone number of the coach.
* **Participant Email**: This is the contact email of the participant.
* **Coach Email**: This is the contact email of the coach.
