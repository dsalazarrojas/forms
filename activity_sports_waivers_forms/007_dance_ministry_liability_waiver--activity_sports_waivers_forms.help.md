# Dance Ministry Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information and obtain consent from participants, parents, and emergency contacts for a dance ministry program. The form aims to ensure that all parties are aware of and agree to the dance ministry's liability terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide information about the participant, including their name, contact details, and emergency contact information.
2. Enter the parent or guardian's contact information, including their name, address, and contact details.
3. Provide the emergency contact's name, phone number, and relationship to the participant.
4. Enter the emergency contact's address.
5. List any medical conditions that the participant may have.
6. List any allergies that the participant may have.
7. Obtain the emergency contact's signature to confirm that they are aware of and agree to the participant's involvement in the dance ministry program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`): Enter the participant's full name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
* **Parent Contact Information** (`parent_contact_information`): Enter the parent or guardian's full name, contact address, and contact details.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): Enter the emergency contact's full name, phone number, and relationship to the participant.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`): Enter the emergency contact's address.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`): List any medical conditions that the participant may have, such as allergies, diabetes, or other health concerns.
* **Allergy List** (`allergy_list`): List any allergies that the participant may have, such as food or insect allergies.
* **Emergency Contact Signature** (`emergency_contact_signature`): Obtain the emergency contact's signature to confirm that they are aware of and agree to the participant's involvement in the dance ministry program.
* **Dance Ministry Liability Statement** (`dance_ministry_liability_statement`): Read and understand the dance ministry's liability terms and conditions.
* **Guardian Signature** (`guardian_signature`): Obtain the participant's guardian's signature to confirm that they are aware of and agree to the participant's involvement in the dance ministry program.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`): Obtain the parent's signature to confirm that they are aware of and agree to the participant's involvement in the dance ministry program.
