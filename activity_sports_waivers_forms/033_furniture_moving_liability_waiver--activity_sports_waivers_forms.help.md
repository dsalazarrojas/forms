# Furniture Moving Liability Waiver - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain essential information and consent from clients and facility managers for a furniture moving service. It ensures a smooth and successful move by clarifying details and terms of the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form title: Enter the name of the move in the "Furniture Moving Liability Waiver" field.
2. Select the Category: Check the correct category that applies to your move.
3. Enter a Description: Provide a brief description of the move.
4. Enter Move Details: Choose the date of the move.
5. Select Facilities Manager: Choose whether a facilities manager will be involved in the move.
6. Enter Facilities Information: Enter the name, phone number, email, and address of the facilities manager.
7. Enter Move Time and End Time: Enter the start and end time of the move.
8. Enter Move Location: Enter the location of the move.
9. Enter Client Information: Enter the client's name, phone number, email, and address.
10. Enter Notes: Add any additional notes regarding the move.
11. Enter Facilities Notes: Add any additional notes from the facilities manager.
12. Sign and Date: Have the client and facility manager sign and date the form to confirm their acknowledgment of the move details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Furniture Moving Liability Waiver** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter the name of the move.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the move involves a category.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the move.
* **Move Details** (`move_details`, date, required): Enter the date of the move.
* **Facility Manager** (`facility_manager`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether a facilities manager will be involved.
* **Facilities Name** (`facilities_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the facilities manager.
* **Facilities Phone** (`facilities_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the facilities manager.
* **Facilities Email** (`facilities_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the facilities manager.
* **Facilities Address** (`facility_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the facilities manager.
* **Move Date** (`move_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the move.
* **Move Time** (`move_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the move.
* **Move End Time** (`move_end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of the move.
* **Move Location** (`move_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the move.
* **Move Start Time** (`move_start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the move.
* **Move End Time** (`move_end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of the move.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, text, optional): Optional field for client signature.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the client's name.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, required): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter the client's email address.
* **Client Address** (`client_address`, text, required): Enter the client's address.
* **Client Notes** (`client_notes`, note, required): Enter any additional notes regarding the move.
* **Facilities Notes** (`facilities_notes`, note, required): Enter any additional notes from the facilities manager.
* **Facility Signature** (`facility_signature`, text, required): Enter the facilities manager's signature.
* **Move Description** (`move_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the move.
* **Move License** (`move_license`, select_multiple, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether a move license is required.
* **Move License Terms** (`move_license_terms`, text, required): Enter the terms of the move license.
