# sports_covid_19_and_release_of_liability_waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from patients regarding their COVID-19 vaccination status, including their medical organization details, vaccine dosage, and consent for treatment. This form is designed to ensure that patients understand and agree to the risks associated with the COVID-19 vaccine.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your patient information in the "Patient Information" field.
2. Provide your name in the "patient_name" field.
3. Enter your date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field.
4. Enter your contact number in the "contact_number" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
6. Enter the name of the medical organization where you received your vaccination in the "medical_organization" field.
7. Enter the name of your doctor in the "doctor_s_name" field.
8. Enter your doctor's contact number in the "doctor_s_contact_number" field.
9. Select the dose of the COVID-19 vaccine you have taken from the options provided in the "vaccine_dosage" field.
10. Enter the date when the vaccine was administered in the "vaccine_administered_date" field.
11. Check the boxes in the "consent" field to indicate your agreement to the risks associated with the COVID-19 vaccine.
12. Check the box in the "release_of_liability" field to release the liability.
13. Select the options in the "signature" field to indicate your understanding of the risks associated with the COVID-19 vaccine.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (1)** (`text`, optional): Enter your patient information.
* **patient_name (2)** (`text`, optional): Enter your name as a patient.
* **date_of_birth (3)** (`date`, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **contact_number (4)** (`text`, optional): Enter your contact number.
* **email (5)** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **medical_organization (6)** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of the medical organization where you received your vaccination.
* **doctor_s_name (7)** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of your doctor.
* **doctor_s_contact_number (8)** (`text`, optional): Enter your doctor's contact number.
* **vaccine_dosage (9)** (`select_one`, optional): Select the dose of the COVID-19 vaccine you have taken. Options: 10 mcg, 15 mcg, 20 mcg, 30 mcg.
* **vaccine_administered_date (10)** (`date`, optional): Enter the date when the vaccine was administered.
* **consent (11)** (`select_multiple`, optional): Check the boxes to indicate your agreement to the risks associated with the COVID-19 vaccine. Options: I agree to accept the risks associated with the COVID-19 vaccine, I do not agree to accept the risks associated with the COVID-19 vaccine.
* **release_of_liability (12)** (`note`, optional): Check the box to release the liability.
* **signature (13)** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select the options to indicate your understanding of the risks associated with the COVID-19 vaccine. Options: I have read and understood the risks associated with the COVID-19 vaccine, I have not read and understood the risks associated with the COVID-19 vaccine.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your healthcare provider.
