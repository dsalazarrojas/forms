# Snowmobile Ride Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a liability waiver for participants in a snowmobile ride event. It is crucial to fill it out accurately to ensure your safety and the safety of others on the ride.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with your information accurately and completely.
2. If you are a participant, fill out your name, email, and contact number.
3. If you are a driver, fill out your driver's license number, vehicle registration number, and driver experience.
4. List any medical conditions that could affect your ability to participate in activities.
5. Sign the participant signature and driver signature fields to confirm you have read and understood the waiver.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email**: Enter your personal email address.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number where you can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., parent, friend, spouse).
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Enter the address of the emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions that could affect your ability to participate in activities.
* **Insurance Status**: If you have insurance, enter your status (e.g., insured, uninsured).
* **Insurance Policy Number**: If applicable, enter your insurance policy number.
* **Vehicle License Number**: Enter your vehicle's license number.
* **Vehicle Registration Number**: Enter your vehicle's registration number.
* **Participant Signature**: Sign this field to confirm you have read and understood the waiver.
* **Participant DOB**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Participant Age**: Enter your age.
* **Driver Signature**: Sign this field to confirm you have read and understood the waiver.
* **Driver License Number**: Enter your driver's license number.
* **Driver Vehicle Number**: Enter your vehicle's registration number.
* **Driver Experience**: Enter your driving experience (e.g., hours, years).
* **Vehicle Type**: Enter the type of vehicle you will be driving (e.g., snowmobile).
* **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year your vehicle was manufactured.
* **Vehicle Make**: Enter the make of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model**: Enter the model of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle License**: Enter the license plate number of your vehicle.

## Tips

* Please double-check the accuracy of your information before submitting the form.
* Ensure all fields are completed if you are a participant and a driver.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact the event organizers for assistance.
