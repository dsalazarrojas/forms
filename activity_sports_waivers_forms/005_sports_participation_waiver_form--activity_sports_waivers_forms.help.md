# sports_participation_waiver_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain a written agreement from parents or guardians for their child's participation in sports activities. It ensures that they understand the rules and conditions of the activity and assume responsibility for their child's participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the Participant Information Section**: Enter your child's name, your name (if you are a parent or guardian), and your contact information.
2. **Select the Activity Type**: Choose the specific sport activity your child is participating in (Soccer, Volleyball, or Tennis).
3. **Acknowledge Participant Agreement**: Read and sign the **participant_agreement** section, which states that you understand the rules and conditions of the activity and are responsible for your child's actions.
4. **Acknowledge Parent/Guardian Agreement**: If you are a parent or guardian, read and sign the **parent_guardian_agreement** section, which states that you understand the rules and conditions of the activity and are responsible for your child's actions.
5. **Provide Emergency Contact Information**: Enter your name and contact information if you are a parent or guardian.
6. **Specify Emergency Contact Relationship**: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.

- Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date contact information.
