# Media Participation Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent or waiver from participants for media participation in a specific activity or event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer all questions truthfully.
3. Sign and date the form where indicated.
4. Make sure to obtain a signature from a parent or guardian if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`Participant Details`, text, required: false): Please enter your name, age, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Media Usage** (`Media Usage`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" if you agree to allow your image or voice to be recorded for media purposes. Select "No" if you do not want to be recorded.
* **Consent To Use** (`Consent To Use`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all applicable consent options, such as "Yes" for allowing your image or voice to be recorded, and "Yes" for allowing your image to be posted on the internet.
* **Consent To Publication** (`Consent To Publication`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" if you agree to allow your image or voice to be published on the internet.
* **Participant Signature** (`Participant Signature`, note, required: false): Please sign and date below to indicate that you have read and understood this agreement.
* **Media Participation Waiver** (`Media Participation Waiver`, text, required: false): If you are a participant, you may want to write down any additional comments or concerns you have.
* **Parent Or Guardian Consent** (`Parent Or Guardian Consent`, select_one, required: false): If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must select "Yes" to confirm their consent.
* **Parent Or Guardian Name** (`Parent Or Guardian Name`, text, required: false): Please enter your parent or guardian's name if they are consenting on your behalf.
* **Parent Or Guardian Signature** (`Parent Or Guardian Signature`, note, required: false): Please sign and date below to confirm that you are the parent or guardian and that you grant consent for media participation.
* **Participant Age Range** (`Participant Age Range`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your age range to confirm whether you are a minor or an adult.
