# Positive Vibes Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Positive Vibes Waiver Form is designed to collect information from participants in an activity or event, ensuring they are aware of and agree to the rules and risks associated with the activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant details section with your information.
2. Provide your emergency contacts' information in the corresponding section.
3. Select the consent options that apply to you.
4. Sign the waiver and agreements sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant details**: Enter your name, date of birth, age, email, and phone number.
* **Participant signature**: Provide a note or signature to confirm your details.
* **Participant date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Participant age**: Enter your age.
* **Participant email**: Enter your email address.
* **Participant phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Participant address**: Enter your home or mailing address.
* **Participant emergency contacts name**: Enter the name of the person or people you would like us to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Participant emergency contacts phone**: Enter the phone number of the person or people you would like us to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Participant emergency contacts email**: Enter the email address of the person or people you would like us to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Participant emergency contacts address**: Enter the address of the person or people you would like us to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Participant emergency contacts relationship**: Describe your relationship with the person or people listed above (e.g., parent, guardian, sibling).
* **Participant consent**: Select the option(s) that apply to you, indicating your understanding of and agreement to the rules and risks associated with the activity.
* **Participant agreement**: Select the option(s) that apply to you, indicating your understanding of and agreement to the rules and risks associated with the activity.
* **Waiver**: Select the option that apply to you, indicating your understanding of and agreement to the rules and risks associated with the activity.
* **Emergency contacts consent**: Select the option that apply to you, indicating your understanding of and agreement to the rules and risks associated with the activity.
* **Activity agreement**: Select the option that apply to you, indicating your understanding of and agreement to the rules and risks associated with the activity.
* **Participant signature 2-8**: Provide a note or signature to confirm your details.
* **Emergency contacts signature**: Provide a note or signature to confirm your emergency contacts' details.
* **Participant signature 9-10**: Provide a note or signature to confirm your agreement.
* **Participant signature 11**: Provide a note or signature to confirm your emergency contacts' agreement.
* **Participant signature 11**: Provide a note or signature to confirm your waiver.
