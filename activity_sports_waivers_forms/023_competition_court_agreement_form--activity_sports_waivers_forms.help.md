**Competition Court Agreement Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and agreements from participants before a competition. It ensures that all participants understand the rules, regulations, and liabilities involved in the competition. By filling out this form, participants confirm their understanding of the competition's rules, regulations, and requirements, and agree to follow them.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your information accurately and completely for the following fields:
	* **Competition Name**: The official name of the competition.
	* **Competition Date**: The date of the competition.
	* **Venue Name**: The name of the venue where the competition will take place.
	* **Venue Address**: The full address of the venue.
	* **Participant Full Name**: Your legal name.
	* **Participant Email**: Your contact email.
	* **Participant Phone**: Your contact phone number.
	* **Team Name** (if applicable): The name of the team you are representing.
	* **Competition Division**: The division or category you are participating in (e.g., Junior, Senior, Open, etc.).
	* **Event Category**: The specific event category you are participating in (e.g., Individual, Doubles, Team, etc.).
	* **Court Assignment** (if assigned): The assigned court number or name.
	* **Match Schedule** (if scheduled): The scheduled match times.
2. Agree to the following statements:
	* **I have read the competition rules**: Confirm that you have read and understood the competition rules.
	* **I will follow all rules**: Agree to follow all competition rules and regulations.
	* **I accept officials' authority**: Accept the decisions made by officials.
	* **I will maintain good sportsmanship**: Agree to maintain good sportsmanship during the competition.
	* **My equipment is fully compliant**: Confirm that your equipment meets the competition's regulations.
	* **I understand late policy**: Understand the consequences of being late to the competition.
	* **I understand forfeit policy**: Understand the rules about forfeitures.
	* **I understand protest procedures**: Understand how to file a protest.
	* **I waive liability**: Agree to waive liability for injuries.
	* **I consent to medical treatment**: Consent to medical treatment in case of an emergency.
	* **I consent to photography**: Consent to event photography.
	* **I consent to results publication**: Consent to publish your results.
3. Sign and date the form:
	* **Participant Signature**: Type your full name as a signature.
	* **Signature Date**: Date of signing.
	* **Witness Name** (if required): Name of the witness (if required).

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure you are registered for the competition and understand your responsibilities as a participant.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask the competition organizers for clarification.
* Remember to sign and date the form before submitting it to the competition organizers.
