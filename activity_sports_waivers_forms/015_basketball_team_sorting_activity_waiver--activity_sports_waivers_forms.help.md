# Basketball Team Sorting Activity Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by participants to sign a waiver and consent form before participating in the basketball team sorting activity. It includes personal information, medical history, and consent for photos and media use.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have read and understood the waiver text on page 8.
2. Fill out your personal information on pages 2-4 (Participant Full Name, Date of Birth, and Current Grade Level).
3. If you are under 18, provide your Parent or Legal Guardian's name on page 5.
4. If necessary, list any known medical conditions or allergies on page 7.
5. Sign the waiver and consent form (page 9).
6. Consent to emergency medical treatment if needed (page 10).
7. Grant permission for photo and media use (page 11).
8. Sign and date the form on page 12.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Date of Birth** (`participant_dob`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Current Grade Level** (`grade_level`, number, required): Select your current grade level (e.g., 6, 9, 12).
* **Parent or Legal Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, text, required if under 18): If you are under 18, enter your parent or legal guardian's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Known Medical Conditions or Allergies** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): List any known medical conditions or allergies (e.g., Asthma, Heart conditions, Nut allergies).
* **Waiver Text** (`waiver_text`, note, required): Read and understand the liability waiver on this page.
* **I have read and agree to the liability waiver** (`waiver_acceptance`, select_one, required): Select I Agree to confirm you have read and understood the waiver text.
* **I consent to emergency medical treatment if necessary** (`medical_treatment_consent`, select_one, required): Select I Consent to agree to emergency medical treatment.
* **Photo and Media Release** (`photo_release`, select_one, required): Select I Grant Permission to allow photos and media use.
* **Date Signed** (`signing_date`, date, required): Enter the date you signed this form in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Relationship to Participant** (`relationship_to_participant`, text, required): Enter how you are related to the participant (e.g., Mother, Father, Self).
