# Stunt Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stunt Participation Consent Form is designed to gather essential information from stunt participants. This form is intended to be filled out by individuals who wish to participate in stunts, such as sports events or activities that involve risks. The form helps the organizer to ensure the participant's safety and obtain their consent before allowing them to participate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your information, including your name, stunt participation status, stunt type, waiver agreement, emergency contact information, and consent to share your information.
2. Select the correct stunt type(s) that apply to your participation.
3. Enter your email address for emergency contact purposes.
4. Select whether you consent to share your information.
5. Provide any additional information about your participation, if necessary.
6. Finally, select the date of your stunt participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Stunt Participation Consent Form**: This is the title of the form, which is also the first field to fill out. It's simply the name of the form.
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Have Participated in Stunt**: Select 'Yes' if you have participated in a stunt previously, or 'No' if you haven't. This helps the organizer understand your stunt experience.
* **Stunt Type**: Select all the stunt types that apply to your participation, such as Stunt, Stunt Activity, etc. This helps the organizer understand the nature of the stunt you're participating in.
* **Waiver Agreement**: This is a note field where you can agree to the waiver terms. Please read and understand the waiver before signing it.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person you'd like us to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person you'd like us to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for emergency contact purposes.
* **Consent to Share**: Select 'Yes' if you agree to share your information with others, 'No' otherwise.
* **Additional Information**: Provide any additional details about your stunt participation, such as your experience level or any medical conditions.
* **Stunt Participation Date**: Enter the date of your stunt participation.
