# Courier Partner Realignment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help courier partners request realignment of their routes, ensuring they can efficiently manage their deliveries and improve their performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and make sure you understand the questions and options available.
2. Select the correct options for each question based on your current situation and preferences.
3. Fill in the required information, such as your email address and phone number.
4. Provide a detailed description of your realignment request in the "realignment_request_note" field.
5. Submit the form to initiate your realignment request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Prophets 
* **Prophets** (`Prophets`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
  This field is used to indicate whether you currently have prophets or not. Select 'Yes' if you have any prophets, or 'No' if you do not have any prophets.

### pickup_location 
* **pickup_location** (`pickup_location`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  This field allows you to select your current pickup location. Choose your city from the available options: New York, Los Angeles, or Chicago.

### dropoff_location 
* **dropoff_location** (`dropoff_location`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
  This field is used to select your desired dropoff location. You can choose multiple locations that you would like to have your packages dropped off at. Select 'Yes' for each location you are interested in.

### pickup_time 
* **pickup_time** (`pickup_time`, `time`, required: false): 
  This field is for specifying your desired pickup time. Enter the time of day you would like to start picking up packages, if applicable.

### dropoff_time 
* **dropoff_time** (`dropoff_time`, `time`, required: false): 
  Similar to the pickup time field, this is for specifying your desired dropoff time. Enter the time of day you would like to finish dropping off packages, if applicable.

### realigned_courier 
* **realigned_courier** (`realigned_courier`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  This field is used to select your desired realigned courier. Choose from the available options: John or Jane.

### realignment_request_note 
* **realignment_request_note** (`realignment_request_note`, `note`, required: false): 
  This field requires a detailed description of your realignment request. Provide as much information as possible to ensure your request is processed efficiently.

### email 
* **email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): 
  This field is for entering your email address. Please ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.

### phone 
* **phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): 
  This field is for entering your phone number. Please ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.

### category 
* **category** (`category`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
  This field is used to select your current category status. Choose 'Yes' if you have any category, or 'No' if you do not have any category.

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide accurate and complete information in all fields.
* If you are unsure about any question, refer to the form instructions or ask your supervisor for guidance.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, contact your supervisor or our support team.
