# Catering Quantity Adjustment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for requesting adjustments to catering quantities for an event or order. You will need to provide information about your event, the quantity adjustments you are requesting, and the reason for the change.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details (Requester Name, Email Address, and Phone Number) to ensure that we can contact you to confirm and discuss your request.
2. Enter the date of your event or order.
3. Provide the order confirmation number for the original order.
4. Enter the name of your event or company.
5. Enter the original guest count and the new guest count you are requesting.
6. Choose the type of adjustment you are requesting (increase or decrease).
7. Select the menu items affected by the adjustment.
8. Specify the reason for the change.
9. If applicable, provide more details about the reason for the change.
10. If necessary, indicate any dietary changes or substitutions.
11. Set a deadline for when you need to have this adjustment made.
12. Confirm if you understand that there may be price changes.
13. Check if manager approval is required for this request.
14. Indicate the level of urgency for this request.
15. Confirm if substitutions are acceptable.
15. Add any additional comments or information that may be helpful for us to process your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Name**: Enter your full name as it should appear on the request.
* **Email Address**: Enter your work or personal email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number for follow-up.
* **Company Name**: If you are representing a company, enter the name of the company. This is not required but helps us identify your company.
* **Request Date**: Enter the date you are making this request.
* **Order Number**: Enter the original order confirmation number for reference.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of your event or meeting.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event or order.
* **Original Guest Count**: Enter the number of guests originally ordered.
* **New Guest Count**: Enter the updated number of guests you are requesting.
* **Adjustment Type**: Choose whether you are increasing or decreasing the quantity.
* **Items Affected**: Select the menu items that will be affected by the adjustment.
* **Reason for Change**: Select the reason for the change (e.g. RSVP changes, budget adjustments, etc.).
* **Reason Details**: If necessary, provide more information about the reason for the change.
* **Dietary Requirements**: If there are any new dietary changes or requirements, please specify.
* **Deadline**: Set a deadline for when you need this adjustment made.
* **Price Impact Understood**: Confirm if you understand that there may be cost changes.
* **Manager Approval Needed**: Check if manager approval is required for this request.
* **Urgency**: Indicate the level of urgency for this request.
* **Substitutions Acceptable**: Confirm if substitutions are acceptable.
* **Additional Comments**: Any other information that may be helpful for us to process your request.

Note: Please ensure that all information provided is accurate and up-to-date to avoid delays or errors in processing your request.
