# Invoice Duplication Removal Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request the removal of duplicate invoices from your account. If you have invoices that you believe are duplicates, please fill out this form to initiate the removal process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your base information in the "Base Info" field.
2. Enter your invoice information in the "Invoice Info" field.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the invoices are duplicated in the "Duplicate Invoices" field.
4. Choose the supporting documents required for the removal process in the "Supporting Documents" field.
5. Provide additional details about the base information in the "Base Info Details" field.
6. Enter more information about the invoices in the "Invoice Details" field.
7. Enter any relevant information about the duplicate invoices in the "Duplicate Info" field.
8. Leave any additional comments or requests in the "Request Info" field.
9. Use the "Notes" field for any additional comments or explanations.
10. Confirm that you want to submit your request by selecting "Yes" in the "Base Info Submit" field.
11. Finally, confirm that you are ready to submit your request by selecting "Yes" in the "Request Submit" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Base Info** (Page 1): Enter your name and contact information for the account where the duplicate invoices are located.
* **Invoice Info** (Page 1): Enter the details of the duplicated invoices, including the invoice number, date, and amount.
* **Duplicate Invoices** (Page 1): Select "Yes" if you believe the invoices are duplicated, "No" otherwise.
* **Supporting Documents** (Page 1): Select the required supporting documents for the removal process, such as copies of the invoices or proof of payment.
* **Base Info Details** (Page 2): Provide any additional details about the base information, such as the account type or billing address.
* **Invoice Details** (Page 2): Enter more information about the invoices, such as the invoice number, date, and amount.
* **Duplicate Info** (Page 2): Enter any relevant information about the duplicate invoices.
* **Request Info** (Page 2): Leave any additional comments or requests related to the removal process.
* **Notes** (Page 2): Use this field to provide any additional comments or explanations about your request.
* **Base Info Submit** (Page 3): Confirm that you want to submit your request by selecting "Yes".
* **Request Submit** (Page 3): Confirm that you are ready to submit your request by selecting "Yes".

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and clear information about the duplicate invoices.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your account manager or customer support for assistance.
* Please note that submitting this form will initiate the removal process, and we will review your request and contact you with further instructions.
