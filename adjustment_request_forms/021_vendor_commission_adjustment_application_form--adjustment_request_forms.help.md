# vendor_commission_adjustment_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vendor_commission_adjustment_application_form is a form designed for vendors to request adjustments to their commissions. It captures information about the request, such as the date and time of the request, the type of adjustment, and the amount of the adjustment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Make sure to select the correct adjustment type (Yes or No).
3. Enter the date and time of the request in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY and HH:MM AM/PM).
4. Enter your name as it appears on your contract or agreement with us.
5. Provide a clear and concise reason for the adjustment in the text area.
6. Enter the amount of the adjustment, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Commission Adjustment** (`vendor_commission_adjustment`, text, optional): A brief description of the reason for the adjustment request. This field is optional but recommended for context.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, optional): The date when the adjustment request was made. Please format the date in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Request Time** (`request_time`, time, optional): The time when the adjustment request was made. Please format the time in the correct format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Adjustment Type** (`adjustment_type`, select_one, optional): Select Yes or No to indicate whether you are requesting an adjustment.
* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, optional): Your name as it appears on your contract or agreement with us.
* **Reason for Adjustment** (`reason_for_adjustment`, text, optional): A clear and concise explanation for the adjustment request.
* **Adjustment Amount** (`adjustment_amount`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the amount of the adjustment.
