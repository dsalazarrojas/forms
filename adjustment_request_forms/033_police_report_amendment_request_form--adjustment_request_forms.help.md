# Police Report Amendment Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request an amendment to a police report. It is intended for individuals or organizations seeking to modify or update information contained in a police report. The form is used to provide details about the amendment, including the reason for the request and any additional information required.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* First Name: Enter your first name.
	* Last Name: Enter your last name.
	* Date Of Birth: Enter your date of birth in the required format (mm/dd/yyyy).
	* Address: Enter your current address.
	* Phone Number: Enter your phone number.
	* Email: Enter your email address.
2. Enter your contact information:
	* Police Report Number: Enter the police report number associated with the report you wish to amend.
	* Department: Enter the department that issued the original police report.
	* Officer Name: Enter the name of the officer who submitted the original report.
	* Officer Designation: Enter the designation of the officer (e.g., Badge Number).
	* Police Station: Enter the police station where the original report was submitted.
3. Provide details about the amendment:
	* Reason For Amendment: Enter a brief description of the reason for the amendment.
	* Additional Details: Enter any additional information relevant to the amendment.
4. Select the follow-up status:
	* Follow Up Status: Select whether the follow-up is Active or Inactive.
	* Follow Up Date: Enter the date for follow-up (optional).
	* Follow Up Details: Enter any additional information for follow-up (optional).
	* Follow Up Officer Name: Enter the name of the officer responsible for follow-up (optional).
	* Follow Up Status Notes: Enter any notes about the follow-up status (optional).
	* Follow Up Status: Select the follow-up status again (optional, but must match the previously selected status).
	* Follow Up Officer Name: Select the name of the officer responsible for follow-up again (optional, but must match the previously selected officer).
	* Follow Up Status Reason: Enter any reason for the follow-up status (optional).
5. Finalize your request:
	* Date Amendment Submitted: Enter the date you are submitting the amendment.
	* Submitted By: Enter your name.
	* Submitted To: Enter the name of the person or organization submitting the request.
	* Follow Up Status: Select the follow-up status one last time.
