# Retail Price Adjustment Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a retail price adjustment request for a specific product. Please provide accurate and detailed information to facilitate the processing of your request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring that all required and optional fields are filled in.
2. Review your submissions for accuracy before submitting.
3. Attach any necessary supporting documents to your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Information (Product Information, text, required)**: Enter the product title and description, including the brand and model number, for the product being adjusted.
* **Reason for Change (Reason for Change, text, optional)**: Provide a brief explanation for the price adjustment request.
* **Date of Change Request (Date of Change Request, date, optional)**: Enter the date when the price adjustment was requested.
* **Current Price (Current Price, number, optional)**: Enter the current retail price of the product.
* **New Price (New Price, number, optional)**: Enter the proposed new retail price for the product.
* **Reason for Requesting (Reason for Requesting, text, optional)**: Explain the reason for the price adjustment request.
* **Supporting Documents (Supporting Documents, note, optional)**: Attach any relevant supporting documents to aid in the price adjustment process.
* **Vendor Contact (Vendor Contact, text, optional)**: Provide the contact information for the vendor or supplier.
* **Vendor Email (Vendor Email, email, optional)**: Enter the email address for the vendor or supplier.
* **Vendor Phone (Vendor Phone, text, optional)**: Enter the phone number for the vendor or supplier.
* **Retailer Contact (Retailer Contact, text, optional)**: Provide the contact information for the retailer.
* **Retailer Email (Retailer Email, email, optional)**: Enter the email address for the retailer.
* **Retailer Phone (Retailer Phone, text, optional)**: Enter the phone number for the retailer.
* **Pricing Team Contact (Pricing Team Contact, text, optional)**: Provide the contact information for the pricing team.
* **Pricing Team Email (Pricing Team Email, email, optional)**: Enter the email address for the pricing team.
* **Pricing Team Phone (Pricing Team Phone, text, optional)**: Enter the phone number for the pricing team.
* **Reason for Decline (Reason for Decline, text, optional)**: Provide a brief explanation if the price adjustment request is declined.
* **Notes (Notes, note, optional)**: Add any additional notes or comments regarding the price adjustment request.
