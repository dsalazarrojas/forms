# Cost Of Living Adjustment Update Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cost of Living Adjustment Update Request Form is used by employees to request a change in their salary due to external factors such as inflation, rent increases, or other economic changes that affect their living costs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
2. If you have already submitted a request for a salary adjustment, please do not resubmit it as a new request.
3. If you are not the actual employee, please do not fill out this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee name (employee_name)**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your official personnel documents.
* **Request date (request_date)**: This is the date you are requesting the salary adjustment. 
* **Current salary (current_salary)**: Please enter your current salary as of the request date. 
* **New salary (new_salary)**: Enter the new salary you are requesting. 
* **Reason for adjustment (reason_for_adjustment)**: Please provide a clear and concise explanation for the reason you are requesting the salary adjustment. 
* **Department (department)**: Select the department where you work. 
* **Manager's signature (manager_signature)**: This field is for your manager to sign and date once the adjustment is approved. It is not required to be filled out by the employee. 
* **Approved manager name (approved_manager_name)**: This field is for your manager to enter their name once the adjustment is approved. It is not required to be filled out by the employee. 
* **Approved date (approved_manager_date)**: This field is for your manager to enter the date the adjustment was approved. It is not required to be filled out by the employee. 
* **Notes (notes)**: Please enter any additional information that your manager may need to know in order to process your request. 
* **Submitted by (submitted_by)**: This field is not editable and will be automatically populated with the employee's name who submitted the form.
