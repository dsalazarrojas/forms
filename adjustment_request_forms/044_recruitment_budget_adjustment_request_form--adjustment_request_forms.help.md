# Recruitment Budget Adjustment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Recruitment Budget Adjustment Request Form" is a digital form designed to collect information about requests for adjustments to the recruitment budget. This form is meant to be filled out by employees who need to request changes to their budget allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the "Request details" field with a brief description of the reason for your budget adjustment request.
2. Select the type of adjustment you are requesting (Budget Increase, Budget Decrease, or Budget Adjustment).
3. Choose the category of the budget you are adjusting (salary, non-salary, or other).
4. Enter the amount of the budget adjustment you are requesting.
5. Choose the date you would like to submit your request.
6. Optionally, enter any additional comments or notes regarding your request.
7. Select the user who is requesting the adjustment (John, Jane, or Jim).
8. Choose the department(s) affected by the request (Accounting, Marketing, or IT).
9. Select the employee who submitted the original request (John, Jane, or Jim).
10. Choose the review status of your request (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
11. Enter any review comments regarding your request.
12. Select the employee who reviewed your request (John, Jane, or Jim).
13. Finally, enter the date the request was updated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request details** (`request_details`, text, optional): A brief description of the reason for your budget adjustment request.
* **Adjustment type** (`adjustment_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of adjustment you are requesting (Budget Increase, Budget Decrease, or Budget Adjustment).
* **Budget category** (`budget_category`, select_one, optional): Choose the category of the budget you are adjusting (salary, non-salary, or other).
* **Budget amount** (`budget_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the budget adjustment you are requesting.
* **Request date** (`request_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to submit your request.
* **Comment** (`comment`, note, optional): Optionally enter any additional comments or notes regarding your request.
* **User requesting** (`user_requesting`, select_one, optional): Select the user who is requesting the adjustment (John, Jane, or Jim).
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the department(s) affected by the request (Accounting, Marketing, or IT).
* **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`, select_one, optional): Select the employee who submitted the original request (John, Jane, or Jim).
* **Submitted date** (`submitted_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the request was submitted.
* **Review status** (`review_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the review status of your request (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
* **Review comments** (`review_comments`, note, optional): Enter any review comments regarding your request.
* **Review by** (`review_by`, select_one, optional): Select the employee who reviewed your request (John, Jane, or Jim).
* **Updated date** (`updated_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the request was updated.

## Tips
Please make sure to provide accurate and clear information in each field to ensure that your request is processed correctly. If you have any doubts or questions, please do not hesitate to reach out to your HR representative for assistance.
