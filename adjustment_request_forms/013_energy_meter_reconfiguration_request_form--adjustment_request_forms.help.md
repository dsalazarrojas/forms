# Energy Meter Reconfiguration Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Energy Meter Reconfiguration Request Form is used to collect information about energy meter reconfigurations. This form is completed by an individual submitting a request for reconfiguration and is used to track and manage the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form with your information, including:
	*   Name
	*   Email
	*   Phone
	*   Department (if applicable)
2.  Provide details about the energy meter, including:
	*   Serial number
	*   Current and proposed meter readings
	*   Type of meter (Smart or Standard)
3.  Specify the date and time of the reconfiguration
4.  Choose the status of the reconfiguration request:
	*   Awaiting Approval
	*   Approved
	*   Declined

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Input** (`user_input`): This field is used to enter any additional information about the request.
*   **Reconfiguration Request** (`reconfiguration_request`): Enter a brief description of the reconfiguration request.
*   **Supporting Document** (`supporting_document`): Attach any relevant documents that support the reconfiguration request.
*   **Reconfiguration Reason** (`reconfiguration_reason`): Explain the reason for the reconfiguration request.
*   **Reconfiguration Date** (`reconfiguration_date`): Enter the date of the proposed reconfiguration.
*   **Reconfiguration Time** (`reconfiguration_time`): Enter the time of the proposed reconfiguration.
*   **Reconfiguration Note** (`reconfiguration_note`): Add any additional notes about the reconfiguration request.
*   **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter your name as the contact person.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address as the contact person.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter your phone number as the contact person.
*   **Energy Meter Serial Number** (`energy_meter_serial_number`): Enter the serial number of the energy meter.
*   **Current Meter Reading** (`current_meter_reading`): Enter the current reading of the energy meter.
*   **Proposed Meter Reading** (`proposed_meter_reading`): Enter the proposed reading of the energy meter.
*   **Meter Type** (`meter_type`): Choose the type of meter (Smart or Standard).
*   **Reconfiguration Status** (`reconfiguration_status`): Choose the status of the reconfiguration request (Awaiting Approval, Approved, Declined).
*   **Assigned To** (`assigned_tool`): If assigned to a specific tool, enter the name of the tool.
*   **Assigned Tool Name** (`assigned_tool_name`): Enter the name of the tool assigned to.
*   **Assigned Tool Email** (`assigned_tool_email`): Enter the email of the tool assigned to.
*   **Assigned Tool Phone** (`assigned_tool_phone`): Enter the phone number of the tool assigned to.
*   **Assigned Tool Department** (`assigned_tool_department`): Enter the department of the tool assigned to.
*   **Reconfiguration Date** (`reconfiguration_date`): Enter the date of the reconfiguration (optional).
*   **Reconfiguration Time** (`reconfiguration_time`): Enter the time of the reconfiguration (optional).
*   **Reconfiguration Note** (`reconfiguration_note`): Add any additional notes about the reconfiguration request (optional).
*   **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`): Enter the date you submitted the request.
*   **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`): Enter the time you submitted the request.
*   **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): Enter your name as the submitter.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a complete request.
*   Attach relevant documents to support your request.
*   Clearly specify the reconfiguration date and time.
*   Be accurate when entering meter readings and type.
*   Review the status of your request and follow up on any updates.
