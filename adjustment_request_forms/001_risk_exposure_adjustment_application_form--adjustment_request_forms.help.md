# Risk Exposure Adjustment Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Risk Exposure Adjustment Application Form is used by users to request adjustments to their risk exposure levels. This form helps to ensure that adjustments are made in a structured and documented manner, reducing the risk of misunderstandings or errors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the risk exposure adjustment application details in the text field provided.
2. Enter the adjustment amount in the numerical field, making sure it is accurate and complete.
3. Provide a justification for the adjustment in the text field, explaining why the change is necessary.
4. If applicable, attach any supporting documents that validate the adjustment request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### risk_exposure_adjustment_application
* **Risk Exposure Adjustment Application**: Enter a brief description of the risk exposure adjustment you are requesting. This field is optional, but it helps to provide context for the adjustment.

### adjustment_amount
* **Adjustment Amount**: Enter the numerical value of the adjustment you are requesting. This field is required and must be a whole number.

### justification
* **Justification**: Provide a clear and concise explanation for the adjustment request. This field is required and should clearly state the reason for the adjustment.

### supporting_documents
* **Supporting Documents**: If you have any documents that support your adjustment request, such as receipts or proof of payment, attach them here. This field is optional but recommended for clarity and transparency.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for all fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or the risk department for guidance.
* Ensure that your justification is clear and concise, and that it supports your adjustment request.
* Attach any supporting documents that validate your request.
