# Employee Time Log Adjustment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Time Log Adjustment Request Form is used to request adjustments to previously recorded time logs. This form helps employees correct errors or omissions in their time logs, ensuring accurate representation of their work hours.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form accurately and honestly, providing all required information.
2. Ensure that you have a valid reason for requesting an adjustment.
3. If applicable, provide comments to support your request.
4. Select the correct status for your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your employee identification.
* **Time Log ID**: Enter the ID number of the time log entry you are requesting an adjustment for.
* **Date**: Enter the date on which the time log was originally recorded.
* **Reason**: Briefly explain the reason for requesting an adjustment (e.g., error, omission, or correction).
* **Comments** (optional): Add any additional comments or supporting information for the request.
* **Submitted By**: This field will automatically populate with your employee name. You can review and confirm it if necessary.
* **Submitted Date**: This field will automatically populate with the current date and time. You can review and confirm it if necessary.
* **Updated By**: This field will automatically populate with the name of the person who updates the status of your request.
* **Updated Date**: This field will automatically populate with the date and time of the status update.
* **Status**:
	+ **Pending**: Your request is waiting for review and processing.
	+ **Approved**: Your request has been approved, and the adjustment has been made.
	+ **Rejected**: Your request has been denied due to insufficient information or an incorrect reason.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
- Use the correct status to track the progress of your request.
- Ensure that your request is processed efficiently by providing clear and concise information.
