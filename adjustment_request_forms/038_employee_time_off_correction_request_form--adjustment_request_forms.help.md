# Employee Time Off Correction Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to correct mistakes or discrepancies in previously submitted time off requests. It's a collaborative effort between the employee and their manager to rectify any errors or inconsistencies.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you're submitting a valid correction to your original time off request.
2. Fill in your employee's name as the primary contact for this request.
3. Provide your manager's name, as they'll be handling the correction request.
4. Clearly explain the reason for the correction in the 'Reason' field.
5. Enter the date of the original time off request.
6. If you're correcting a time off request, provide the original time.
7. Specify the type of adjustment you're requesting (Leave, Shift Swap, or Shift Adjustment).
8. Enter the date of the adjustment request.
9. Enter the adjusted time for the new request.
10. Add any additional comments or clarifications in the 'Notes' field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID or company records.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of your direct manager, as they'll be handling your correction request.
* **Reason**: Explain the reason for the correction. Be specific and clear, and include any relevant details.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the original time off request.
* **Time**: If you're correcting a time off request, enter the original time.
* **Original Request Date**: Enter the date of the original time off request, if it's not already populated.
* **Original Request Time**: Enter the original time of the time off request, if applicable.
* **Correction Reason**: Explain the reason for the correction, including any supporting information.
* **Adjustment Request Type**: Choose the type of adjustment you're requesting (Leave, Shift Swap, or Shift Adjustment).
* **Adjustment Request Date**: Enter the date of the adjustment request.
* **Adjustment Request Time**: Enter the adjusted time for the new request.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or clarifications to support your correction request.

## Tips

* Ensure all fields are filled accurately and completely to prevent delays in processing your correction request.
* Use clear and concise language when explaining the reason for the correction.
* Be mindful of your manager's time and respond promptly to their inquiries.
* Keep your 'Notes' field for any supporting information that may not fit in other fields.
