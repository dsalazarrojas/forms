# Compensation Band Adjustment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Compensation Band Adjustment Form is used to request a change to a new pay grade or compensation band for an employee. This form is used to gather information about the employee's current and proposed compensation details, including their current job title, salary, and requested band change. The purpose of this form is to assist in making an informed decision about an employee's compensation band adjustment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your information**: Complete the form with your personal details and the employee's details whose compensation band you are adjusting.
2. **Request date**: Enter the date of submission of the request.
3. **Provide job and department information**: Enter the employee's current job title, department, and employee ID.
4. **Current compensation details**: Enter the employee's current compensation band, salary, and length of time in the current role.
5. **Proposed band change**: Select the proposed new compensation band and the reasons for the change.
6. **Supporting documents**: Attach any relevant documents that support the request.
7. **Manager and HR review**: Ensure that the manager and HR review the request and provide feedback.
