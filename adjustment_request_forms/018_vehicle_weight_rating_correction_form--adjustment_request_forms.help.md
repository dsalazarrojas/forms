# vehicle_weight_rating_correction_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The vehicle_weight_rating_correction_form is used to report any discrepancies or errors in the weight rating of a vehicle. This form is used to provide a detailed report of the weight rating issue, including the vehicle type, year, weight, and any additional comments or notes. The form is used to help ensure accurate and consistent weight ratings across all vehicles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Review the current weight rating of the vehicle and verify that it is incorrect or needs to be updated.
2. Select the correct vehicle type and make from the provided options.
3. Enter the correct vehicle year.
4. Enter the correct weight of the vehicle.
5. Select the status of the vehicle's weight rating (Good or Fair).
6. Provide any additional comments or notes regarding the weight rating issue.
7. Enter the name and contact information of the reviewer.
8. Enter the date and time of the review.
9. Sign the form with your name.
