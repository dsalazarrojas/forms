# Pool Heating Adjustment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pool Heating Adjustment Request Form is used to submit requests for changes to your pool heating settings. Please use this form to request adjustments to your pool heating.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select your name from the options provided in the "Pool Heating Adjustment Requestor Name" field.
2. Enter the date of your request in the "Pool Heating Adjustment Request Date" field. You can format the date in the desired format.
3. Enter your desired pool heating temperature in the "Pool Heating Adjustment Request Temperature" field.
4. Explain your reason for the request in the "Pool Heating Adjustment Request Reason" field. This will help the pool management team understand your request.
5. Provide your contact information (name, phone number, and email) so that we can get in touch with you to discuss your request.
6. Add any additional notes or comments if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Pool Heating Adjustment Request Form (Text)
* This is the main section of the form where you can enter your request for pool heating adjustment.
* **Pool Heating Adjustment Request Date (Date)**: The date when you are requesting the adjustment.
*  **Pool Heating Adjustment Request Temperature (Number)**: The desired temperature for your pool heating.
* **Pool Heating Adjustment Request Reason (Text)**: Explain the reason for your request.
* **Pool Heating Adjustment Requestor Name (Select One)**: Choose your name from the options provided.
* **Pool Heating Adjustment Requestor Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number so that we can contact you to discuss your request.
* **Pool Heating Adjustment Requestor Email (Email)**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you about your request.
* **Pool Heating Adjustment Requestor Note (Note)**: Add any additional comments or information that might be helpful for the pool management team.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your submission is accurate and complete.
* Check your pool's schedule and settings before submitting your request.
* If you are unsure about anything, do not hesitate to contact the pool management team for assistance.
