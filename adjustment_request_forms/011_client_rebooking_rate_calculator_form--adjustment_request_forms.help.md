# Client Rebooking Rate Calculator Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on client rebooking rates for service businesses, helping them to track performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please select the reporting period and year you would like to analyze. Then, provide the required information for each field. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank or add any additional notes if you have any information to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reporting Period** (reporting_period): Select the time frame you would like to analyze (e.g., January, February, etc.).
* **Year** (reporting_year): Enter the year you would like to analyze.
* **Total Number of Clients Served** (total_clients_served): Enter the total number of unique clients you served during this period.
* **Number of New Clients** (new_clients_count): Enter the number of clients who were served for the first time during this period.
* **Number of Returning Clients** (returning_clients_count): Enter the number of clients who returned for another booking during this period.
* **Total Repeat Bookings** (repeat_bookings): Enter the total number of repeat bookings in this period.
* **Number of Cancellations** (cancellations_count): (Optional) Enter the number of clients who cancelled after their first booking.
* **Clients Who Did Not Rebook** (did_not_rebook_count): (Optional) Enter the number of clients who completed service but did not return.
* **Average Time Between Bookings** (average_time_between_bookings): (Optional) Enter the average time between bookings in days or weeks.
* **Primary Service Type** (service_type): Select the main service type you offer (e.g., Hair and Beauty, Personal Training, etc.).
* **Calculated Rebooking Rate** (rebooking_rate_calculated): (Optional) This field will be populated automatically after you input other required fields. 
* **Retention Goal** (retention_goal): (Optional) Enter the percentage rebooking rate you aim to achieve.
* **Key Factors Affecting Rebooking Rates** (key_factors): (Optional) Describe any factors that influence your clients' return rates.
* **Strategies to Improve Rebooking Rates** (improvement_strategies): (Optional) Share your plans to increase client retention.
* **Additional Notes** (notes_additional): (Optional) Add any other relevant information to help us understand your performance.
