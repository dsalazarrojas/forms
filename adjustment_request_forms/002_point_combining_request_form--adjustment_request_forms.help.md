# Point Combining Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Point Combining Request Form is used to initiate or track the combining or transfer of points related to customers or accounts. This form is intended to help customers or business users update customer or account points and manage their transfer status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's name, email, and phone number.
2. Enter the customer's points and the points to be transferred.
3. Select the transfer type (Points Transfer, Points Merge, Points Redemption, or Points Expiration).
4. Choose the account(s) to transfer the points to.
5. Select the reason for the request.
6. Enter the transfer date and time.
7. Optionally, enter the points earned or issued.
8. Select the status of the request (Open, Closed, Rejected, or In Progress).
9. Assign the request to a specific user.
10. Add any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Points**: Enter the customer's current points.
* **Points to Transfer**: Enter the points to be transferred.
* **Transfer Type**: Select the type of transfer (Points Transfer, Points Merge, Points Redemption, or Points Expiration).
* **Account ID**: Select the account(s) to transfer the points to.
* **Reason for Request**: Select the reason for the request (Test, Test 1, or Test 2).
* **Transfer Date**: Enter the date of the transfer.
* **Transfer Time**: Enter the time of the transfer.
* **Points Earned**: Optionally, enter the points earned (if applicable).
* **Points Issued**: Optionally, enter the points issued (if applicable).
* **Status**: Select the status of the request (Open, Closed, Rejected, or In Progress).
* **Assigned to**: Select the user responsible for the request.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the request.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check the customer's information to ensure accuracy.
* Review the transfer details carefully before submitting the form.
* Check the status of the request regularly to ensure timely completion.
* Use the notes field to track any additional information or progress related to the request.
