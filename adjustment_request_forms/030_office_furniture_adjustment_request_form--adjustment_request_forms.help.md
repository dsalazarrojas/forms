# Office Furniture Adjustment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The office furniture adjustment request form is used by employees to request adjustments to office furniture items such as chairs, desks, shelving, filing cabinets, and other office equipment. This form helps the management team to identify the needs of employees and allocate resources accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the "What type of adjustment would you like to request?" field with the type of furniture you need, such as "Chair" or "Desk".
2.  Describe the item you are requesting in the "Item Description" field.
3.  Select the reason for your request from the "Reason for Request" dropdown, such as "Broken" or "Dirty".
4.  Choose the location where the adjustment is needed from the "Location" dropdown, such as "Office 1" or "Office 3".
5.  Select the priority level of your request from the "Priority" dropdown, such as "High" or "Low".
6.  Enter the date and time you submitted the request in the "Date Submitted" and "Time Submitted" fields.
7.  Enter your employee name and email address in the "Employee Name" and "Employee Email" fields.
8.  Enter your phone number in the "Employee Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Office Furniture Adjustment Request Form** (`office_furniture_adjustment_request_form`): This is the main title field for the form.
* **What type of adjustment would you like to request?** (`office_furniture_adjustment_details`): Choose the type of furniture you need, such as a chair or a desk.
* **Item Description** (`office_furniture_adjustment_item_description`): Briefly describe the item you are requesting.
* **Reason for Request** (`office_furniture_adjustment_reason`): Select the reason for your request from the following options: Broken, Dirty, Missing, Other, or Not Needed.
* **Location** (`office_furniture_adjustment_location`): Choose the location where the adjustment is needed, such as Office 1 or Office 3.
* **Priority** (`office_furniture_adjustment_priority`): Select the priority level of your request from the following options: High, Medium, or Low.
* **Date Submitted** (`office_furniture_adjustment_date_submitted`): Enter the date you submitted the request.
* **Time Submitted** (`office_furniture_adjustment_time_submitted`): Enter the time you submitted the request.
* **Employee Name** (`office_furniture_adjustment_employee_name`): Enter your employee name.
* **Employee Email** (`office_furniture_adjustment_employee_email`): Enter your employee email address.
* **Employee Phone** (`office_furniture_adjustment_employee_phone`): Enter your employee phone number.
