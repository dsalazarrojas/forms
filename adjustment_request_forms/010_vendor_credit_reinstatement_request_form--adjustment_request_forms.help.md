# Vendor Credit Reinstatement Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request reinstatement of a vendor credit, which is a payment to a vendor for goods or services provided to an organization. The form is used to gather information necessary to facilitate the request.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide general information about the request.
2. Enter contact details of the person requesting the credit.
3. Enter credit and vendor information.
4. Provide order, product, shipping, and payment details.
5. Select a signature option and provide additional comments or notes if necessary.
6. Choose relevant options for date and time of the request.
7. Select one or multiple options from the provided list.
8. Enter email and phone number information.
9. Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info (1)**: Enter a brief description of the request.
* **Contact Details (2)**: Enter your name, title, and contact information.
* **Credit Info (3)**: Enter details about the credit being requested.
* **Vendor Info (4)**: Enter vendor name and contact information.
* **Order Info (5)**: Enter order number and other relevant order details.
* **Product Info (6)**: Enter product details, such as product name and quantity.
* **Shipping Info (7)**: Enter shipping details, such as shipping address and tracking number.
* **Payment Info (8)**: Enter payment information, such as payment method and amount.
* **Submission (9)**: This field is for any additional comments or notes.
* **Signature (10)**: Choose your signature from the provided list.
* **Date-Time (11)**: Enter the date and time of the request.
* **Date-Time Two (12)**: Enter an additional date and time.
* **Time (13)**: Enter the time of the request.
* **Select One (14)**: Choose one option from the provided list.
* **Select Multiple (15)**: Choose multiple options from the provided list.
* **Email (16)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (17)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Note (18)**: Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Decimal (19)**: Enter a decimal value.
* **Integer (20)**: Enter an integer value.
* **Text (21)**: Enter text information.
* **Text Area (22)**: Enter a larger text area for additional comments.
* **Text Area Two (23)**: Enter an additional text area for comments.
* **Text Area Three (24)**: Enter another text area for comments.
* **Text Area Four (25)**: Enter the final text area for comments.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact the vendor's customer support.
