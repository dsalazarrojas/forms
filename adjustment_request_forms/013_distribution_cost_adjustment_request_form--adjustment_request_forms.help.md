# Distribution Cost Adjustment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Distribution Cost Adjustment Request Form is used to request adjustments to the distribution of costs within a company or organization. This form allows users to provide details about the adjustment, including their justification, date, time, and any additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name.
2. Provide a detailed justification for the cost adjustment request in the Justification field.
3. Enter the Date and Time of the request.
4. Select the Cost Unit affected by the request from the dropdown list.
5. Enter the Amount of the adjustment.
6. Add any additional notes or comments in the Notes field.
7. Click on the Submit button to send the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Justification** (Justification, text, optional): Provide a detailed explanation for the cost adjustment request.
* **Date** (Date, date, optional): Enter the date of the request.
* **Time** (Time, time, optional): Enter the time of the request.
* **Amount** (Amount, number, optional): Enter the amount of the adjustment.
* **Cost Unit** (Cost Unit, select_one, required): Select the cost unit affected by the request from the dropdown list.
* **Submit** (Submit, text, optional): Click this button to send the request.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about this request.
