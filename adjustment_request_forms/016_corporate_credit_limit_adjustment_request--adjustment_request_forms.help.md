# Corporate Credit Limit Adjustment Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request an adjustment to a corporate credit limit. Fill out this form to submit a request for a credit limit adjustment, providing necessary details and supporting documents for review and approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Submitting Company" field with the name of the company requesting the credit limit adjustment.
2. Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the request.
3. Provide a brief reason for the credit limit adjustment in the "Reason for Adjustment" field.
4. Enter the new credit limit amount in the "New Credit Limit" field.
5. Upload supporting documents as needed in the "Supporting Documents" field.
6. Choose the status of the request from the "Status" dropdown menu.
7. Select the submitter's name from the "Submitter" field.
8. Choose the date and time of submission in the "Submit Date" and "Submit Time" fields.
9. Enter any additional review notes in the "Review Note" field.
10. Select the date and time of review in the "Review Date" and "Review Time" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitting Company** (Submitting Company, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the company requesting the credit limit adjustment.
* **Contact Person** (Contact Person, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the request.
* **Reason for Adjustment** (Reason for Adjustment, Text, Optional): Provide a brief reason for the credit limit adjustment.
* **Adjustment Amount** (Adjustment Amount, Number, Optional): Enter the amount of the credit limit adjustment. (Note: This field is not actually present on the form; it seems to be a duplicate of "New Credit Limit" and is not used in this guide.)
* **Current Credit Limit** (Current Credit Limit, Number, Optional): Enter the current credit limit.
* **New Credit Limit** (New Credit Limit, Number, Optional): Enter the new credit limit amount.
* **Supporting Documents** (Supporting Documents, Text, Optional): Upload supporting documents for the credit limit adjustment.
* **Submit Date** (Submit Date, Date, Optional): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submit Time** (Submit Time, Time, Optional): Enter the time of submission.
* **Review Note** (Review Note, Text, Optional): Enter any additional review notes.
* **Review Date** (Review Date, Date, Optional): Enter the date of review.
* **Review Time** (Review Time, Time, Optional): Enter the time of review.
* **Status** (Status, Select One, Optional): Choose the status of the request from the dropdown menu.
* **Submitter** (Submitter, Text, Optional): Select the submitter's name.
* **Submitted By** (Submitted By, Text, Optional): This field seems to be a redundant field; it's likely not actually used in this guide.
* **Submitted On** (Submitted On, Date, Optional): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submitted At** (Submitted At, Time, Optional): Enter the time of submission.
* **Created By** (Created By, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person who created the request.
* **Created On** (Created On, Date, Optional): Enter the date the request was created.
* **Created At** (Created At, Time, Optional): Enter the time the request was created.
* **Updated By** (Updated By, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person who updated the request.
* **Updated On** (Updated On, Date, Optional): Enter the date the request was updated.
* **Updated At** (Updated At, Time, Optional): Enter the time the request was updated.
* **Notes** (Notes, Text, Optional): Enter any additional notes.
