# Distribution Group Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Distribution Group Request Form is a tool used to request resources, assets, or personnel within an organization. It helps to coordinate and document the distribution of these items efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your company name in the "Company Name" field. This is a required field and will help identify your organization.
2. Select the request date from the calendar when filling out the "Request Date" field. This date should be the date you are requesting the distribution for.
3. Provide the request time in the "Request Time" field. This is an important piece of information to help plan the distribution process.
4. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" field. This will enable us to get in touch with you regarding your request.
5. Select the department responsible for the request in the "Department" field. This will help us route your request efficiently.
