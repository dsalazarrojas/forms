# Payroll Correction Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a correction to an employee's payroll, including errors or discrepancies in paychecks or benefits. Complete this form to report any issues that need to be addressed and ensure your pay is accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out your personal information, including your error description, error date, and job title.
*   Specify whether the error is yes or no by selecting the correct answer from the options provided.
*   If you have any additional documentation or evidence to support your claim, you may attach it to this form using the "Supporting Docs" field.
*   Enter the date you submitted this form to track and date any subsequent corrections.
*   If you are reporting the error through this form, please indicate who submitted it.
*   Enter the department or relevant information related to your job or position.
*   Specify the amount of the error.
*   Enter more details about the error.
*   Describe any actions taken to resolve the issue.
*   Add any additional notes or information about the issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Employee Info** (employee_info, text, required): Please enter your name, employee ID, or other unique identifier to help us identify you in our records.
*   **Error Description** (error_description, text, optional): Describe the error or discrepancy you have experienced in your paycheck or benefits.
*   **Error Date** (error_date, date, optional): Enter the date the error was discovered.
*   **Error Type** (error_type, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if the error is still present or 'No' if it has been resolved.
*   **Attachment** (attachment, text, optional): If you have any supporting documentation or evidence, you can attach it here.
*   **Supporting Docs** (supporting_docs, text, optional): Similar to the Attachment field, but with a more specific label for clarity.
*   **Submission Date** (submission_date, date, optional): Enter the date you submitted this form to help us track any subsequent corrections.
*   **Submitted By** (submitted_by, text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the form, which is you.
*   **Department** (department, text, optional): Enter the department or relevant information related to your job or position.
*   **Job Title** (job_title, text, optional): Enter your job title.
*   **Error Amount** (error_amount, number, optional): Enter the amount of the error.
*   **Error Details** (error_details, text, optional): Enter more details about the error.
*   **Action Taken** (action_taken, text, optional): Describe any actions taken to resolve the issue.
*   **Notes** (notes, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or information about the issue.
*   **Attachment 2** (attachment_2, text, optional): Another field for attaching additional documentation or evidence.
*   **Attachment 3** (attachment_3, text, optional): Another field for attaching further documentation or evidence.
