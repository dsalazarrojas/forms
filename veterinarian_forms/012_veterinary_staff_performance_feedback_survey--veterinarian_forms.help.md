# Veterinary Staff Performance Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the performance of veterinary staff members. It is an essential tool to identify areas of strength and weakness, and to provide guidance for future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review each question carefully to ensure you have the necessary information.
2.  Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3.  Do not leave any questions blank, as this may affect the overall evaluation.
4.  Once you have completed all the questions, click the "Submit" button to finalize your evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (`general_info`, text, optional): This is a text field for any general comments or information you would like to share about the staff member's performance.
* **Veterinarian Info** (`veterinarian_info`, text, optional): This field is for providing any additional information about the staff member, such as their name or title.
* **Performance Rating** (`performance_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the staff member's performance as:
	+ Outstanding
	+ Good
	+ Satisfactory
	+ Needs Improvement
	+ Poor
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any specific comments or suggestions for improvement for the staff member.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you would like to provide your email address for the staff member to contact you, please enter it here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you would like to provide your phone number for the staff member to contact you, please enter it here.
* **Manager Info** (`manager_info`, text, optional): If you are the manager of the staff member, please provide any relevant information about their performance that you would like to share.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Once you have completed all the questions, click this button to submit your evaluation.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback, using concrete examples or situations where the staff member has demonstrated strength or weakness.
* Avoid making assumptions or general statements, as these can be misleading or unclear.
* Remember that this feedback is for the staff member's growth and development, so please provide constructive and actionable suggestions for improvement.
