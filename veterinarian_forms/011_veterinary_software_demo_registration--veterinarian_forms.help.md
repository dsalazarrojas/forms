# Veterinary Software Demo Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Software Demo Registration form is a required step for clinics interested in participating in a demo of the veterinary software. By filling out this form, you will be able to reserve a time slot and provide your clinic and contact information for updates and followup on the demo.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting.
3. Review and confirm your contact information for accuracy.
4. Submit the form to reserve your time slot and receive updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Veterinary Software Demo Registration Form** (Text, Optional): A title for your registration.
- **Clinic Name** (Text, Optional): The name of your veterinary clinic.
- **Contact Name** (Text, Optional): The name of the person completing the form.
- **Clinic Location** (Text, Optional): The location of your veterinary clinic.
  - This field is helpful for us to know where your clinic is located in case we need to schedule a followup or provide further assistance.
- **Contact Email** (Email, Optional): The email address of the person to contact for updates and followup.
- **Contact Phone** (Text, Optional): The phone number of the person to contact for updates and followup.
- **Demo Date** (Date, Optional): The date you would like to schedule the demo.
  - Please format the date as MM/DD/YY to help us schedule your demo on a suitable time slot.
- **Demo Time** (Time, Optional): The time you would like to schedule the demo.
  - Please format the time as 24-hour format (HH:MM).
- **Preferred Demo Time** (Time, Optional): If you have a preferred time for the demo, please let us know.
  - We will try our best to accommodate your preferred time, but we will contact you if there are conflicts.
- **Demo Length** (Text, Optional): The length of the demo you would like to schedule.
- **Demo Length Units** (Text, Optional): Please specify if you would like the demo to be in minutes or hours.
- **Software Interests** (Text, Optional): What specific features or functions of the software are you interested in learning about?
- **Followup** (Text, Optional): Any additional information or followup questions you have for the demo.

## Tips

- This form is only required once per clinic. If your clinic is already registered, please do not fill out the form again.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at <your contact email>.
- We will contact you via the contact email and phone number you provide to schedule and confirm your demo time slot. 

Please note that the format of the form fields may change, so it is best to review and update your contact information before the demo date to ensure accuracy and continuity.
