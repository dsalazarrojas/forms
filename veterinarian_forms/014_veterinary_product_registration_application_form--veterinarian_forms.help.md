# Veterinary Product Registration Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Product Registration Application Form is designed to collect information for the registration of veterinary products. This form helps the manufacturer and supplier provide necessary data for the registration and approval process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the product name, manufacturer name, and product code fields with the relevant information.
2. Describe the product in the product description field.
3. Select the correct animal uses from the multiple selection options.
4. Select the correct product label approval option.
5. Fill out the supplier details, including contact person, contact number, email, fax, and website.
6. Fill out the product category, subcategory, and subsubcategory fields.
7. Fill out the product class, type, family, brand, and regulatory authority fields.
8. Choose the submission date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the veterinary product.
* **Manufacturer Name** (`manufacturer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the manufacturer of the product.
* **Product Code** (`product_code`, `text`, required): Enter the product code or any identifying code for the product.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, `text`, required): Describe the product in detail.
* **Supplier Details** (`supplier_details`, `text`, required): Provide the contact information of the supplier, including their contact person, contact number, email, fax, and website.
* **Product Formulation** (`product_formulation`, `text`, required): Specify the formulation of the product.
* **Animal Uses** (`animal_uses`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the animal uses of the product.
* **Product Label** (`product_label`, `text`, optional): Enter the product label.
* **Product Label Approval** (`product_label_approval`, `select_one`, required): Select the product label approval option.
* **Manufacturer Address** (`manufacturer_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of the manufacturer.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and title of the contact person.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact number of the supplier.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email of the supplier.
* **Fax** (`fax`, `text`, optional): Enter the fax number of the supplier.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the supplier.
* **Website** (`website`, `text`, optional): Enter the website of the supplier.
* **Product Category** (`product_category`, `text`, optional): Select the product category.
* **Product Subcategory** (`product_subcategory`, `text`, optional): Select the subcategory of the product.
* **Product SubsubCategory** (`product_subsubcategory`, `text`, optional): Select the subsubcategory of the product.
* **Product Class** (`product_class`, `text`, optional): Select the class of the product.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, `text`, optional): Select the type of the product.
* **Product Family** (`product_family`, `text`, optional): Select the family of the product.
* **Product Brand** (`product_brand`, `text`, optional): Select the brand of the product.
* **Regulatory Authority** (`regulatory_authority`, `text`, optional): Select the regulatory authority of the product.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of submission.
