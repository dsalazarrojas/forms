# Dog Rescue Adoption Matching Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from adopters to facilitate the adoption process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details in the "Adopter Details" section.
2. Provide information about the dog you are interested in adopting in the "Dog Details" section.
3. Specify the criteria you are looking for in a match in the "Matching Criteria" section.
4. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" section.
5. Select the dog you are interested in adopting and any additional dogs from the "Adopted Dog" section.
6. Enter your email address and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone" sections respectively.
7. Click submit to save your input.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Adopter Details** (`adopted_details`): This section is where you can fill in your details such as your name, address, and contact information.
* **Dog Details** (`dog_details`): This section is where you can provide information about the dog you are interested in adopting.
* **Matching Criteria** (`matching_criteria`): This section is where you can specify the criteria you are looking for in a match, such as size, age, and breed.
* **Notes** (`notes`): This section is where you can add any additional notes or comments about the dog or adoption process.
* **Email** (`adopter_email`): This is a valid email address for follow-up communications.
* **Phone** (`adopter_phone`): This is a valid phone number for follow-up communications.
* **Adopted Dog** (`select_one_adopted_dog`): This is where you can select the dog you are interested in adopting.
* **Additional Dogs** (`select_multiple_additional_dogs`): This is where you can select any additional dogs you are interested in adopting if there are more than one.
* **Email** (`adopter_email`): This is a valid email address for follow-up communications.
* **Phone** (`adopter_phone`): This is a valid phone number for follow-up communications.
