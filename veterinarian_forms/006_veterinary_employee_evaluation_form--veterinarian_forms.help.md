# Veterinary Employee Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and evaluation of veterinary employees' performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's name
2. Select their job title
3. Rate their performance (High, Medium, Low)
4. Provide feedback
5. Select the evaluation date
6. Choose the evaluation period (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4)
7. Add any comments
8. Enter the employee's email address
9. Choose their category (Veterinarian, Veterinary Assistant, Veterinarian Assistant Manager)
10. Select the person submitting the evaluation (Manager, Team Member, Veterinarian Manager)
11. Choose the person receiving the evaluation (Manager, Team Member, Veterinarian Manager)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the employee being evaluated.
* **Job Title** (text, required/optional): Select the job title of the employee being evaluated.
* **Performance Rating** (select_one, required/optional): Rate the employee's performance as High, Medium, or Low.
* **Feedback** (text, required/optional): Provide feedback on the employee's performance.
* **Evaluation Date** (date, required/optional): Select the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Period** (select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the evaluation period (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4).
* **Comments** (note, required/optional): Add any additional comments about the employee's performance.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter the employee's email address.
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter the employee's phone number.
* **Category** (select_one, required/optional): Select the category of the employee (Veterinarian, Veterinary Assistant, Veterinarian Assistant Manager).
* **Submitted By** (select_one, required/optional): Select the person submitting the evaluation (Manager, Team Member, Veterinarian Manager).
* **Submitted To** (select_one, required/optional): Select the person receiving the evaluation (Manager, Team Member, Veterinarian Manager).

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information collected is accurate and up-to-date.
* Complete the form for the correct evaluation period.
* Use the feedback section to provide constructive feedback to the employee.
* Enter the employee's email address and phone number for contact purposes.
* Select the correct category and submitter/receiver for internal evaluation purposes.
