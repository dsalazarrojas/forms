# Pet Relinquishment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Pet Relinquishment Form" is used to capture information about pets that need to be relinquished due to various reasons. This form helps ensure that all relevant details are collected accurately and consistently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand the required information.
2. Select the correct pet type(s) from the available options.
3. Fill in the animal's name, date found, reason for relinquishment, owner information, contact information, and any medical information related to the pet.
4. Indicate if an adoption agreement is needed for the pet.
5. Provide the date for when the pet will be relinquished.
6. Double-check the form for completeness and accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Type**: 
  * (<name>) (Pet Type)

* **Animal Name**: 
  * (<label>) (Animal Name)
  Enter the name of the animal you are submitting for relinquishment.

* **Date Found**: 
  * (<label>) (Date Found)
  Enter the date when you found or acquired the pet. This field is optional but helps track the pet's history.

* **Reason Found**: 
  * (<label>) (Reason Found)
  Briefly describe the reason you found or acquired the pet.

* **Owner Information**: 
  * (<label>) (Owner Information)
  Provide any relevant details about the pet's owner, if applicable.

* **Contact Information**: 
  * (<label>) (Contact Information)
  Enter any contact information for the owner or person submitting the form, if available.

* **Medical Information**: 
  * (<label>) (Medical Information)
  Share any relevant medical information about the pet, if available.

* **Adoption Agreement**: 
  * (<label>) (Adoption Agreement)
  Indicate if an adoption agreement is required for the pet's safekeeping.

* **Relinquish Date**: 
  * (<label>) (Relinquish Date)
  Enter the date when the pet will be relinquished or removed from your care.
 
## Tips
- Double-check the form for completeness and accuracy before submission.
- Review the form carefully to ensure all required information is captured.
- If you need any assistance or have questions, reach out to your supervisor or support team.
