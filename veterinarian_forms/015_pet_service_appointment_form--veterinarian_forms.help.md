# Pet Service Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from pet owners for scheduling appointments with the veterinarian. The purpose of this form is to provide a seamless and efficient way to gather necessary details from pet owners before their appointment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your pet's information:
   - Select the service type you would like to receive for your pet (Bathing, Grooming, Nail Trimming, Vaccinations, Consultation).
   - Choose the type of pet you are visiting with (Dog, Cat, Other).
2. Schedule your appointment:
   - Enter the date and time of your desired appointment.
3. Contact and confirm your appointment:
   - Enter your contact number so that we can reach you to confirm your appointment.
   - Enter your email address so that we can contact you for any updates or changes.
4. Add your pet's details:
   - Enter your pet's name.
   - Provide the reason for your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Pet owner info** (`Pet owner info`, text, optional)
This field is used to collect any additional information from the pet owner that may be relevant to the appointment.

### 2. **Appointment Date** (`Appointment Date`, date, optional)
Enter the date you would like to schedule your appointment. This will help us to ensure that we have availability on the desired date.

### 3. **Service Type** (`Service Type`, select_one, optional)
Choose the type of service you would like to receive for your pet. This could be Bathing, Grooming, Nail Trimming, Vaccinations, or Consultation.

### 4. **Pet Type** (`Pet Type`, select_one, optional)
Select the type of pet you are visiting with. This will help us to tailor the appointment to your pet's specific needs.

### 5. **Appointment Time** (`Appointment Time`, time, optional)
Enter the time you would like to schedule your appointment.

### 6. **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, text, required)
Enter your contact number so that we can reach you to confirm your appointment.

### 7. **Email** (`Email`, email, required)
Enter your email address so that we can contact you for any updates or changes.

### 8. **Pet Name** (`Pet Name`, text, required)
Enter your pet's name to help us to identify them during the appointment.

### 9. **Reason for Visit** (`Reason for Visit`, text, required)
Provide the reason for your visit to help us to better understand your pet's needs.

### 10. **Veterinarian Notes** (`Veterinarian Notes`, text, optional)
This field is available for the veterinarian to take any notes during the appointment. You can enter any additional information that may be relevant to your pet's care.
