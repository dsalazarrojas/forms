# Pet Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pet Survey form is designed to collect information about pets and their owners for the purpose of providing personalized veterinary care and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **What is your purpose for visiting us tonight?** (Page 1)
	* Enter a brief description of the reason for your visit.
2. **What is the name of your pet?** (Page 2)
	* Enter the name of your pet.
3. **What type of pet do you own?** (Page 3)
	* Select the type of pet you own from the options provided: dog, cat, or other.
4. **What is your pet's age?** (Page 4)
	* Enter the age of your pet in years.
5. **Who does the pet belong to?** (Page 5)
	* Select who the pet belongs to: self, family, or other.
6. **Contact Information** (Page 6)
	* Enter your contact information for future follow-up and treatment purposes.
7. **Contact Phone** (Page 7)
	* Enter your phone number.
8. **Contact Email** (Page 8)
	* Enter your email address.
9. **Owner Email** (Page 9)
	* Enter the owner's email address.
10. **Owner Phone** (Page 10)
	* Enter the owner's phone number.
