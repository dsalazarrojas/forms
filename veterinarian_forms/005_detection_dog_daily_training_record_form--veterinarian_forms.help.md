# Detection Dog Daily Training Record Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record and document the daily training sessions for detection dogs. It captures essential information on the dog's name, training type, date and time, training location, duration, trainer's name, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the correct training type from the available options (Obedience, Protection, Tracking).
2. Enter the dog's name and ensure it is spelled correctly.
3. Enter the date and time of the training session.
4. Choose the correct duration of the training session.
5. Enter the trainer's name.
6. Enter the location where the training took place.
7. Provide any notable equipment or tools used during the session (e.g., harness, leash, ball, etc.).
8. Enter any other equipment not listed in the previous field.
9. Provide any notes from the handler or trainer.
10. Enter the temperature, if recorded during the session.
11. Enter the training area used (e.g., room, field, etc.).
12. Select any additional equipment used during the session (e.g., Harness, Leash, Ball, Clicker, Target stick, Treats).
13. Provide a brief description of other equipment used (if not listed in the previous field).
14. Enter feedback from the handler.
15. Enter feedback from the trainer.
16. Enter any health issues with the dog (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training session** (`training_session`, `text`, required): Briefly describe the training session, including any notable events or outcomes.
* **Training type** (`training_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of training conducted (Obedience, Protection, Tracking).
* **Date and time** (`date_and_time`, `date`, required): Enter the date and time of the training session.
* **Dog name** (`dog_name`, `text`, required): Enter the dog's name accurately.
* **Training level** (`training_level`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the level of training (Basic, Intermediate, Advance).
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the session.
* **Training duration** (`training_duration`, `time`, required): Enter the duration of the training session.
* **Trainer name** (`trainer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the trainer's name accurately.
* **Training location** (`training_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location where the training took place.
* **Weather** (`weather`, `text`, optional): Record any notable weather conditions during the session.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the session.
* **Temperature** (`temperature`, `number`, optional): Enter the temperature recorded during the session (if applicable).
* **Training area** (`training_area`, `text`, optional): Enter the area where the training took place (e.g., room, field, etc.).
* **Equipment used** (`equipment_used`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any equipment or tools used during the session (e.g., Harness, Leash, Ball, Clicker, Target stick, Treats).
* **Other equipment used** (`other_equipment_used`, `text`, optional): Enter any other equipment not listed in the previous field.
* **Handler feedback** (`handler_feedback`, `text`, optional): Enter feedback from the handler.
* **Trainer feedback** (`trainer_feedback`, `text`, optional): Enter feedback from the trainer.
* **Dog health** (`dog_health`, `text`, optional): Enter any health issues with the dog (if applicable).
