# New Client And Pet Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The New Client And Pet Information Form is designed to collect essential data from new clients and their pets at a veterinary clinic or hospital. The information gathered will be used to provide personalized care and services to the client and their pet.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form with accurate and detailed information about yourself and your pet.
- Make sure to answer all required fields.
- If you need help with any field, don't hesitate to ask the staff for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Client And Pet Information Form** (`new_client_and_pet_information_form`, text, required: false): This section is the main title of the form and provides a brief description of the form's purpose.
* **Veterinarian** (`veterinarian`, text, required: true): Enter the name and contact information of the veterinarian who will be attending to your pet.
* **Pet Medical History** (`pet_medical_history`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed account of your pet's medical history, including any previous illnesses, allergies, or sensitivities.
* **Pet Vaccination Record** (`pet_vaccination_record`, text, required: false): Enter your pet's vaccination records, including dates and types of vaccinations received.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, required: false): If you have any additional information about your pet that you would like to share with the veterinarian, please include it here.

## Tips
- Please take your time to fill out the form accurately and completely.
- If you're unsure about any field, double-check the form carefully before submitting.
- If you need help or clarification on any field, don't hesitate to ask the staff for assistance.
