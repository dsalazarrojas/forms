# Pet Care Reminder Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help pet owners schedule and keep track of veterinary care reminders for their pets.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the type of pet you own (e.g., dog, cat, etc.).
2. Select the date for the reminder.
3. Choose the type of procedure (e.g., surgery, check-up, vaccination) that requires the reminder.
4. Enter the name of your pet.
5. Indicate whether a reminder has been sent or not for each procedure.
6. If applicable, enter the follow-up date for the procedure.
7. If you are unsure about any of the information, please contact your veterinarian for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Type (Pet Type)**: Select the type of pet that requires the reminder.
* **Reminder Date (Reminder Date)**: Enter the date for the reminder, in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Veterinarian Name (Vet Name)**: Enter the name of the veterinarian who will perform the procedure.
* **Procedure Type (Procedure Type)**: Select the type of procedure that requires the reminder (e.g., surgery, check-up, vaccination).
* **Pet Name (Pet Name)**: Enter the name of your pet.
* **Reminder Sent (Reminder Sent)**: Indicate whether a reminder has been sent for each procedure by selecting "True" or "False".
* **Follow-up Date (Follow-up Date)**: If applicable, enter the date for the follow-up visit with your veterinarian.
