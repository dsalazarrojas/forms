# Veterinary Disaster Preparedness Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and store critical information about a pet's owner and their animal's details, emergency contacts, and medical history. The form serves as a record-keeping tool for veterinarians, ensuring they have accurate and up-to-date information about the pet and owner in the event of a disaster or emergency.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Owner Details**: Please fill in your personal details, including your name, address, phone number, and email.
2. **Animal Information**: Provide details about your animal, including their name, breed, type, and medical conditions.
3. **Emergency Contacts**: Select your emergency contacts from the options provided.
4. **Signatures**: Sign the form to confirm you are the pet owner and that you have provided accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Details** (`Owner Name`, `Text`, `Required`): Please fill in your full name as the pet owner.
* **Animal Info** (`Animal Name`, `Text`, `Required`): Enter your pet's name and breed.
* **Animal Type** (`Cat/Dog/Horse`, `Select One`, `Required`): Select the type of animal you own.
* **Owner Contacts** (`Select One`, `Required`): Select your emergency contact from the provided options (Mother, Father, Brother).
* **Owner Address** (`Text`, `Required`): Enter your home address.
* **Owner Phone** (`Text`, `Required`): Enter your phone number.
* **Owner Email** (`Email`, `Optional`): Enter your email address (optional).
* **Owner Title** (`Dr./Mr./Mrs.`, `Select One`, `Optional`): Select your title (Dr., Mr., Mrs.).
* **Owner Date of Birth** (`Date`, `Optional`): Enter your date of birth.
* **Owner Year of Birth** (`Number`, `Optional`): Enter the year you were born.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Select One`, `Optional`): Select your emergency contact (Mother, Father, Brother).
* **Veterinary Clinic** (`Text`, `Optional`): Enter the name of your veterinarian's clinic.
* **Emergency Phone** (`Text`, `Optional`): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Email** (`Email`, `Optional`): Enter your emergency contact's email address (optional).
* **Medical Condition** (`Text`, `Optional`): Describe any medical conditions your pet may have.
* **Medication** (`Text`, `Optional`): Describe any medications your pet is taking.
* **Disaster Preparedness Plan** (`Text`, `Optional`): Describe your disaster preparedness plan for your pet.
* **Owner Signed Date** (`Date`, `Required`): Sign and date this form to confirm you have provided accurate information.
* **Veterinarian Signed Date** (`Date`, `Required`): Sign and date this form to confirm you have reviewed and agree with the provided information.
* **Veterinarian Signature** (`Note`, `Optional`): This is a note field for the veterinarian to add any comments.
* **Owner Signature** (`Note`, `Required`): Sign this form to confirm you are the owner of the pet and that you have provided accurate information.

## Tips

* Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information about your pet and emergency contact.
* Review and sign the form to confirm the accuracy of the information.
* Keep a copy of this form for your records.
* Update this form as needed to reflect any changes in your pet's information or emergency contact details.
