# Veterinary Product Sample Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Veterinary Product Sample Request Form is a tool used to collect information from veterinarians who would like to request samples of our products. This form will help us understand their needs and preferences for our products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact information section with your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the product details you are interested in.
3. Choose the product samples you would like to request.
4. Select the shipping details for your samples.
5. Enter any additional shipping address information.
6. Write a note if you have any additional comments or requests.
7. Finally, click the "Submit Request" field to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (Contact Information): Enter your name, email address, and phone number to help us contact you about your request.
* **Product Details** (Product Details): Select the product(s) you are interested in. This will help us determine what samples we should send you.
* **Product Sample Request** (Product Sample Request): Choose the product samples you would like to request. Make sure to select only what you need.
* **Shipping Details** (Shipping Details): Select how you would like your samples shipped. This will help us determine the best shipping method for you.
* **Shipping Address** (Shipping Address): Enter the shipping address where you would like to receive your samples.
* **Submit Request** (Submit Request): Enter a brief note if you have any additional comments or requests.
* **Form Comments** (Form Comments): Enter any additional comments or requests you have about your request.
