# Veterinary Faculty Presentation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Faculty Presentation Request Form is designed to allow faculty members to request a presentation opportunity at the university. This form helps to gather necessary information about the event, contact details, and presentation specifics, which will be reviewed and approved internally.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of event you will be presenting at: Choose between a lecture and a talk.
2. Select the universities, schools, or organizations where the event will take place. You can choose multiple options if necessary.
3. Provide details about the event, including date and time (note: use YYYY-MM-DD format for dates).
4. Specify the start and end dates of the event.
5. Enter the location of the event.
6. Enter the contact person's name and email address.
7. Provide the contact person's phone number.
8. If you are using a specific tool for the presentation, enter the tool's name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Submission**: Enter a brief description of your presentation topic and request (max 255 characters).
* **Event Details**: Select the type of event you will be presenting at. This will help us understand the nature of your presentation.
* **University School**: Select the university, school, or organization where the event will take place. You can choose multiple options if necessary. Note: This field is optional but helps us to track your event's location.
* **Presentation Details**: Enter details about your presentation, including date and time (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of your presentation.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of your presentation.
* **Event Location**: Enter the physical location where the event will take place.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the contact person's name responsible for this presentation.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the contact person's email address (format: `username@example.com`).
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Assigned Tool**: If you are using a specific tool for your presentation, enter the tool's name.
* **Form ID**: This field is not required and is used for internal tracking purposes only. You can leave it blank.
