# Veterinary Maternity Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Maternity Leave Of Absence Form is used to request a leave of absence for maternity-related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee ID number in the `employee_id` field.
2. Type your name in the `employee_name` field.
3. Enter the start date of your leave in the `start_date` field.
4. Select the type of leave you are requesting (e.g. Maternity Leave, Parental Leave, Paternity Leave, Adoption Leave, Medical Leave, or Other) in the `leave_type` field.
5. Enter the end date of your leave in the `end_date` field.
6. Provide a brief reason for your leave in the `reason` field (optional).
7. Confirm that the information you provided is accurate in the `confirmation` field.
8. Provide any additional comments regarding your leave request in the `comments` field (optional).
9. If you have already discussed your leave with your manager, provide their confirmation in the `manager_comments` field (optional).
10. Enter any further comments or instructions for staff in the `staff_confirmation` field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_id** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter your employee ID number to identify yourself as an employee.
* **employee_name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Type your name as it appears on your employee ID card.
* **start_date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to start your leave.
* **leave_type** (`leave_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of leave you are requesting.
* **end_date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to return to work after your leave.
* **reason** (`reason`, text, optional): Provide a brief reason for your leave request, if applicable.
* **confirmation** (`confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm that the information you provided is accurate.
* **comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments regarding your leave request, if applicable.
* **manager_comments** (`manager_comments`, text, optional): If you have already discussed your leave with your manager, provide their confirmation, if applicable.
* **staff_confirmation** (`staff_confirmation`, text, optional): Enter any further comments or instructions for staff, if applicable.

Note: The information provided on this form will be reviewed and processed by your manager or HR representative. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to them for assistance.
