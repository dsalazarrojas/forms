# Veterinary Diagnostic Evaluation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Diagnostic Evaluation Consent Form is used to collect information from the owner of a pet and obtain consent for any procedures that will be performed on the pet.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Owner Details**: Provide your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
2. **Owner Phone**: Enter your phone number so that we can contact you with any updates or concerns.
3. **Owner Email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you with any updates or concerns.
4. **Symptoms**: Describe the symptoms you have observed in your pet.
5. **Consent**: Indicate whether you give your consent for the procedures that we will perform on your pet. Please select 'Yes' or 'No' from the dropdown menu.
6. **Diagnostic Procedures**: Please indicate which diagnostic procedures you are willing to have performed on your pet. You can select one or more options from the list.
7. **Veterinarian's Notes**: Enter any additional information that you feel is relevant to your pet's condition.
8. **Owner's Notes**: Enter any additional information that you feel is relevant to your pet's condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Owner Details (Owner Phone)**: Enter your phone number so that we can contact you with any updates or concerns.
* **Owner Email (Owner Email)**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you with any updates or concerns.
* **Symptoms (Symptoms)**: Describe the symptoms you have observed in your pet. This will help the veterinarian make an accurate diagnosis.
* **Consent (Consent)**: Indicate whether you give your consent for the procedures that we will perform on your pet. Please select 'Yes' or 'No' from the dropdown menu.
* **Diagnostic Procedures (Diagnostic Procedures)**: Please indicate which diagnostic procedures you are willing to have performed on your pet. You can select one or more options from the list.
* **Veterinarian's Notes (Veterinarian's Notes)**: Enter any additional information that you feel is relevant to your pet's condition.
* **Owner's Notes (Owner's Notes)**: Enter any additional information that you feel is relevant to your pet's condition.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when describing your pet's symptoms.
* Make sure to select all relevant diagnostic procedures that you are willing to have performed on your pet.
* Please indicate your contact information accurately so that we can contact you with any updates or concerns.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask your veterinarian.
