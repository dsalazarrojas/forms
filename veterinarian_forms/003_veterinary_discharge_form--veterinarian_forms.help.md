# Veterinary Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Discharge Form is a document used to collect information for the discharge process of a pet from the veterinary clinic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Pet Name** in the first field. This is the name of the pet that is being discharged.
2. Enter the **Owner Name** in the second field. This is the name of the person who owns the pet.
3. Enter the **Owner Email** in the third field. This is the email address of the pet owner.
4. Select the **Pet Species** in the fourth field. This is the type of animal (e.g., dog, cat, etc.).
5. Enter the **Reason For Visit** in the fifth field. This is a brief description of why the pet was brought to the veterinary clinic.
6. Enter the **Treatment** provided to the pet in the sixth field. This is a description of any medical treatment given to the pet.
7. Enter the **Follow Up Visit Date** in the seventh field. This is the date and time scheduled for the pet's next visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Name**: A text field to enter the name of the pet.
* **Owner Name**: A text field to enter the name of the pet owner.
* **Owner Email**: An email field to enter the contact information of the pet owner.
* **Pet Species**: A text field to select the type of animal.
* **Reason For Visit**: A text field to enter a brief description of why the pet was brought to the veterinary clinic.
* **Treatment**: A text field to enter any medical treatment provided to the pet.
* **Follow Up Visit Date**: A date field to schedule the next visit for the pet.
