# veterinary_operations_participant_information - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a pet and its owner's details for veterinary operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of pet from the options provided.
2. Choose the breed of the pet from the options provided.
3. Fill in the owner's name, email, phone number, and address details.
4. Choose whether to participate in the study or not.
5. Provide any additional notes about the pet or owner if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Details** (`pet_details`, `text`, required: false): This field is for providing any additional information about the pet.
* **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose the species of the pet, such as Dog, Cat, or Other.
* **Pet Breed** (`pet_breed`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Choose one or more breeds of the pet, such as Labrador, Golden Retriever, or Pug.
* **Owner Details** (`owner_details`, `text`, required: false): This field is for providing any additional information about the owner.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, `text`, required: true): Enter the name of the pet's owner.
* **Owner Email** (`owner_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email of the pet's owner.
* **Owner Phone** (`owner_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the pet's owner.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose whether to participate in the study or not.
* **Date** (`consent_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the consent.
* **Time** (`consent_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the consent.
* **Pet Weight** (`pet_weight`, `number`, required: false): Enter the weight of the pet.
* **Pet Length** (`pet_length`, `number`, required: false): Enter the length of the pet.
* **Pet DOB** (`pet_dob`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of birth of the pet.
* **Owner Address** (`owner_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of the pet's owner.
* **Owner City** (`owner_city`, `text`, required: false): Enter the city of the pet's owner.
* **Owner State** (`owner_state`, `text`, required: false): Enter the state of the pet's owner.
* **Owner Zip** (`owner_zip`, `text`, required: false): Enter the zip code of the pet's owner.
* **Pet Description** (`pet_description`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the pet.
* **Owner Notes** (`owner_notes`, `text`, required: false): Enter any notes about the pet's owner.
* **Pet History** (`pet_history`, `text`, required: false): Enter any medical history of the pet.
* **Pet Diagnosis** (`pet_diagnosis`, `text`, required: false): Enter any diagnosis of the pet.
* **Veterinary Clinic** (`veterinary_clinic`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the veterinary clinic.
* **Procedure Details** (`procedure_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter any details about the procedure.
* **Procedure Date** (`procedure_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the procedure.
* **Procedure Time** (`procedure_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the procedure.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
* This form is for the purpose of collecting information about a pet and its owner's details for veterinary operations.
