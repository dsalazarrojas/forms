# Veterinary Relief Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Relief Program Application Form is designed to gather information from clinics applying for relief support. This form helps us understand their operational needs and medical requirements to allocate resources effectively. Please ensure you're completing this form to submit an application for relief support, not for other purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand the types of information we require.
2. For each section, provide accurate and relevant details about your clinic.
3. Ensure you've completed all required fields (indicated by *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Clinic Information (Page 1)
* **Clinic Information** (clinic_info, note, optional): This is a space for you to provide any information about your clinic, such as its name, address, and a brief description. Please keep it concise and relevant.

### Requested Staffing (Page 2)
* **Requested Staffing** (requested_staffing, select_multiple, optional): This question asks whether your clinic needs staff relief. You can select 'Yes' if you need staff assistance, and 'No' if you don't. 

### Operational Support (Page 2)
* **Operational Support** (operational_support, select_one, optional): This question asks whether your clinic requires operational support, such as equipment or supplies. You can select 'Yes' if you need operational support, and 'No' if you don't.

### Application Information (Page 2)
* **Application Information** (application_info, select_one, optional): This question helps us understand your application's status. You can select 'Yes' if your application is still active, and 'No' if it's not.

### Contact Details (Page 3)
* **Contact Details** (contact_details, select_multiple, optional): This section is for your contact information. Please select 'Yes' if you'd like us to contact you, and 'No' if you don't. 

### Contact Information (Page 3)
* **Contact Information** (contact_info, note, optional): This is a space for you to provide your contact name, phone number, and email. Please ensure you've filled this out correctly to receive updates about your application.

### Veterinary Information (Page 3)
* **Veterinary Information** (veterinary_info, select_multiple, optional): This question helps us understand your clinic's veterinary needs. You can select 'Yes' if you need veterinary assistance, and 'No' if you don't. 

### Medical Information (Page 4)
* **Medical Information** (medical_info, note, optional): This is a space for you to provide any medical information relevant to your application. Please keep it concise and clear.

### Medical Certification (Page 4)
* **Medical Certification** (medical_certification, select_one, optional): This question asks whether you have the necessary medical certifications for the program. You can select 'Yes' if you do, and 'No' if you don't.

### Application Attachments (Page 4)
* **Application Attachments** (application_attachments, select_multiple, optional): This section is for uploading supporting documents for your application. Please select 'Yes' if you need to upload attachments, and 'No' if you don't. 

Note that the provided form fields are not exhaustive and might require further review and validation before submission.
