## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **FirstName** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **ContactNumber** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number, if you have one.
* **DateOfBirth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **TimeOfBirth** (`time_of_birth`, time, required): Enter your time of birth in the format HH:MM.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your home address as it appears on your identification.
* **DogName** (`dog_name`, text, required): Enter your dog's name.
* **DogDescription** (`dog_description`, text, optional): Describe your dog's breed, size, color, and any other relevant details.
* **EmergencyContact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (if you have one).
* **OwnerAgreement** (`owner_signature`, text, required): Sign with your owner's signature to confirm that you have read and understood the form.
* **VetName** (`vet_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the vet who will be providing care to your pet.
* **VetAgreement** (`vet_signature`, text, required): Sign with the vet's signature to confirm that they have read and understood the form.
* **VeterinariansLicense** (`vet_license`, text, required): Enter the vet's license number.
* **DateOfSignature** (`owner_signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Notes** (`owner_signature_note`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes you may have about your pet's care.
* **VetNotes** (`vet_signature_note`, text, optional): Enter any notes from the vet.
* **EmergencyNotes** (`emergency_notes`, text, required): Enter any emergency contact information or notes.
* **DogLicense** (`dog_license`, text, required): Enter your dog's license number.
* **OwnerLicense** (`owner_license`, text, required): Enter your owner's license number.
* **LicenseExpiryDate** (`dog_license_expiration`, date, required): Enter your dog's license expiration date.
* **LicenseStatus** (`dog_license_status`, select_multiple, required): Select the status of your dog's license (Active or Inactive).
* **Declaration** (`owner_declaration`, text, required): Make a declaration as an owner.
* **VetDeclaration** (`vet_declaration`, text, required): Make a declaration as a vet.
* **DeclarationOfEmergency** (`emergency_declaration`, text, required): Make a declaration of emergency.
* **OwnerAgreement** (`owner_agreement`, text, required): Sign with the owner's agreement to confirm that you have read and understood the form.
