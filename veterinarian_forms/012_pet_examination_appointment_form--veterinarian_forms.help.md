# Pet Examination Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pet Examination Appointment Form is a tool for scheduling examinations for pets. This form is used to collect necessary information from pet owners to schedule an appointment with a veterinarian. It helps to streamline the process of creating and managing examination records.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, start by selecting the veterinarian you would like to see from the Veterinarian dropdown menu.
2. Enter the examination date and time in the Examination Date and Examination Time fields respectively.
3. Select any medical history that may be relevant to the examination, such as Heartworm, Flea, or Tick.
4. List any allergies that the pet may have, such as Food, Environmental, or Medication.
5. Enter any medications that the pet is currently taking.
6. If necessary, enter any special instructions for the examination, such as "Please administer a sedative before examination."
7. Provide emergency contact information for the pet's owner.
8. Finally, provide the pet's name and type, and add any examination notes or results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Examination Form** (Page 1): This is the main form page where you will enter the necessary information for the examination.
* **Veterinarian** (Page 1): Choose a veterinarian to conduct the examination. This will be the person who will see your pet.
* **Examination Date** (Page 1): Enter the date of the examination. This is the day your pet will see the veterinarian.
* **Examination Time** (Page 1): Enter the time of the examination. This is the time slot you have chosen for the examination.
* **Examination Duration** (Page 1): Optional field for entering the examination duration.
* **Medical History** (Page 1): Select any medical history that may be relevant to the examination, such as Heartworm, Flea, or Tick.
* **Allergies** (Page 1): List any allergies that the pet may have, such as Food, Environmental, or Medication.
* **Medications** (Page 1): Enter any medications that the pet is currently taking.
* **Special Instructions** (Page 1): Enter any special instructions for the examination, such as "Please administer a sedative before examination."
* **Emergency Contact Info** (Page 1): Provide emergency contact information for the pet's owner.
* **Pet Name** (Page 1): Enter the name of the pet being examined.
* **Pet Type** (Page 1): Enter the type of pet being examined.
* **Examination Notes** (Page 1): Add any examination notes or results.
* **Medical Records** (Page 1): Enter any medical records related to the examination.
* **Examination Result** (Page 1): Enter the examination result.
* **Examination Recommendation** (Page 1): Enter any examination recommendations.
* **Next Steps** (Page 1): Enter any next steps or follow-up information.
* **Upload File** (Page 1): Upload any files related to the examination.
* **Submit To** (Page 1): Choose where to submit the form to, such as the Doctor or Receptionist.
* **Submit** (Page 1): Save or submit the form.
* **Cancel** (Page 1): Cancel the form and start over.
* **Back** (Page 1): Go back to the previous page.
* **Save** (Page 1): Save the form for later.
