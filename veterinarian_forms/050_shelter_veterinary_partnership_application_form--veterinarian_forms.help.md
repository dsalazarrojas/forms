# Shelter Veterinary Partnership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shelter Veterinary Partnership Application Form is designed for veterinarians or clinics to partner with shelters. This partnership enables veterinarians to provide services to shelter animals and promotes collaboration between the two organizations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your clinic's details, including address and contact information.
2. Select the services you offer, such as cat services, dog services, or other services.
3. Indicate your collaboration preferences, including regular check-ins, regular meetings, email updates, or phone updates.
4. Enter your clinic's address and additional address details.
5. Provide any additional notes or review comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Clinic Details**: Enter your clinic's name, address, and contact information, including email and phone number.
	+ **Clinic Name**: Enter your clinic's name.
	+ **Clinic Email**: Enter your email address.
	+ **Clinic Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Services**: Select the services you offer, such as cat services, dog services, or other services.
* **Collaboration Preferences**: Select the types of collaboration you prefer, including regular check-ins, regular meetings, email updates, or phone updates.
* **Additional Collaboration Preferences**: Enter any additional information about your collaboration preferences.
* **Clinic Address**: Enter your clinic's address, including street, postal code, province, and country.
* **Review Notes**: Enter any review notes or comments.
* **Review Comments**: Select any review comments, such as positive, negative, or neutral.
* **Clinic Province 2**: Enter your clinic's province (second part of the address).
* **Clinic Province 3**: Enter your clinic's province (third part of the address).
* **Clinic Region**: Enter your clinic's region.
* **Clinic City**: Enter your clinic's city.
* **Clinic Street**: Enter your clinic's street address.
* **Clinic Postal Code 2**: Enter your second postal code.
* **Clinic Postal Code 3**: Enter your third postal code.
* **Additional Review Notes**: Enter any additional review notes.
* **Review Comments 2**: Select any review comments, such as positive, negative, or neutral.
* **Collaboration Preferences 2**: Select the types of collaboration you prefer.
