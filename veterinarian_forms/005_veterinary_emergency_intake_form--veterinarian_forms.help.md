# Veterinary Emergency Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Emergency Intake Form is designed to gather essential information about pets that require urgent care or attention. This form helps veterinary staff quickly and accurately document the pet's details, ensuring that the animal receives the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant information about the pet and owner.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Information** (`pet_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the pet, including the pet's name, species, and any relevant medical history.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter the owner's contact information, including their name, phone number, and email address.
* **Owner Information** (`owner_info`, text, optional): Enter the owner's information, including their name, phone number, and email address.
* **Visit Information** (`visit_info`, text, optional): Enter the details of the visit, including the date and time of the visit.
* **Emergency Type** (`emergency_type`, select_one, optional): Select whether the pet is experiencing a 'Yes' or 'No' emergency.
* **Emergency Info** (`emergency_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the emergency situation.
* **Treatment** (`treatment`, text, optional): Enter the treatment or procedure performed during the visit.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the visit.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, optional): Enter the owner's name.
* **Owner Phone** (`owner_phone`, text, optional): Enter the owner's phone number.
* **Owner Email** (`owner_email`, text, optional): Enter the owner's email address.
* **Owner Address** (`owner_address`, text, optional): Enter the owner's address.
* **Relationship** (`owner_relationship`, text, optional): Enter the relationship between the owner and the pet.
* **Pet Name** (`pet_name`, text, optional): Enter the pet's name.
* **Pet Breed** (`pet_breed`, text, optional): Enter the pet's breed.
* **Pet Age** (`pet_age`, number, optional): Enter the pet's age.
* **Pet Weight** (`pet_weight`, number, optional): Enter the pet's weight.
* **Pet Species** (`pet_species`, select_multiple, optional): Select the pet's species, or enter 'Yes' or 'No' if applicable.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's name.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the visit.
* **Visit Time** (`visit_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the visit.
