# veterinarian_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for individuals applying to become veterinarians. It collects information about the applicant's personal details, qualifications, and experience for internal purposes, such as the hiring process or evaluation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone number in the respective fields.
2. Provide information about two references, including their Name, Title, Organization, and Relationship.
3. List your Qualifications 1 and 2, including their Title and Description.
4. Enter your Date of Birth.
5. Describe your Experience, Education, Certifications, Skills, Work Experience, Military Experience, Previous Clinics, and Emergency Contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Required: No): Enter your first name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Required: No): Enter your last name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required: No): Enter a valid email address you frequently use.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Required: No): Enter your phone number.
* **Reference 1 Name** (`reference_1_name`, Text, Required: No): Enter the name of your first reference.
* **Reference 1 Title** (`reference_1_title`, Text, Required: No): Enter the title of your first reference.
* **Reference 1 Organization** (`reference_1_organization`, Text, Required: No): Enter the organization your first reference is associated with.
* **Reference 1 Relationship** (`reference_1_relationship`, Text, Required: No): Describe your relationship with your first reference.
* **Reference 2 Name** (`reference_2_name`, Text, Required: No): Enter the name of your second reference.
* **Reference 2 Title** (`reference_2_title`, Text, Required: No): Enter the title of your second reference.
* **Reference 2 Organization** (`reference_2_organization`, Text, Required: No): Enter the organization your second reference is associated with.
* **Reference 2 Relationship** (`reference_2_relationship`, Text, Required: No): Describe your relationship with your second reference.
* **Qualifications 1 Title** (`qualifications_1_title`, Text, Required: No): Enter the title of your first qualification.
* **Qualifications 1 Description** (`qualifications_1_description`, Text, Required: No): Describe your first qualification.
* **Qualifications 2 Title** (`qualifications_2_title`, Text, Required: No): Enter the title of your second qualification.
* **Qualifications 2 Description** (`qualifications_2_description`, Text, Required: No): Describe your second qualification.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Required: No): Enter your date of birth in the standard format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Experience** (`experience`, Text, Required: No): Describe your relevant experience in the field of veterinary medicine.
* **Education** (`education`, Text, Required: No): Describe your educational background and relevant courses.
* **Certifications** (`certifications`, Text, Required: No): List any relevant certifications or licenses you hold.
* **Skills** (`skills`, Text, Required: No): List your relevant skills or areas of expertise.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, Text, Required: No): Describe your work experience in the field of veterinary medicine.
* **Military Experience** (`military_experience`, Text, Required: No): If applicable, describe your military experience.
* **Previous Clinics** (`previous_clinics`, Text, Required: No): If applicable, list any clinics or hospitals you have worked at.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, Text, Required: No): Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all fields accurately and completely.
- Use standard formats for dates and contact information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant authority for assistance.
