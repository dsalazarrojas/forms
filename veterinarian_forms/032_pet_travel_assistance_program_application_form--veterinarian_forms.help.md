# Pet Travel Assistance Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Travel Assistance Program Application Form is designed to collect information about your pet and its travel plans, as well as any medical information relevant to its care during travel. This information will be used by veterinarians and animal care professionals to provide the best possible care and assistance to your pet.

## How To Complete This Form
### Before You Start
* Make sure you have all necessary information about your pet, including its medical history, vaccination records, and any special needs or requirements.
* Ensure you have all necessary contact information about your pet, including your pet's name, age, species, and a current photo.
* If you are traveling with a veterinary professional, please ensure you have their contact information as well.

### To Complete the Form
1. **Pet Information**: Please provide a brief description of your pet, including its name, age, species, weight, and a current photo.
	* If your pet has any medical conditions or allergies, please provide detailed information about them.
2. **Contact Information**: Please provide your contact information, including your name, address, phone number, and email address.
3. **Pet Travel Details**: Describe the travel plans for your pet, including the destination, mode of transportation, and expected duration of travel.
4. **Travel Details**: Provide any relevant information about your travel plans, including travel dates, flight information, or any special accommodations for your pet.
5. **Veterinarian Information**: If applicable, please provide the contact information of your veterinarian or a veterinary professional who will be caring for your pet during travel.
6. **Signature**: Please sign and date this form to indicate that the information provided is accurate and complete.
7. **Veterinarian Signature**: If you are traveling with a veterinary professional, please have them sign and date this form to confirm their involvement in your pet's care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Information (ID 1)**: A text field where you can provide a brief description of your pet, including its name, age, species, weight, and a current photo.
* **Contact Information (ID 2)**: A text field where you can provide your contact information, including your name, address, phone number, and email address.
* **Pet Travel Details (ID 3)**: A text field where you can describe the travel plans for your pet, including the destination, mode of transportation, and expected duration of travel.
* **Travel Details (ID 4)**: A text field where you can provide any relevant information about your travel plans, including travel dates, flight information, or any special accommodations for your pet.
* **Veterinarian Information (ID 5)**: A text field where you can provide the contact information of your veterinarian or a veterinary professional who will be caring for your pet during travel.
* **Signature (ID 6)**: A text field where you can sign and date this form to indicate that the information provided is accurate and complete.
* **Veterinarian Signature (ID 7)**: A text field where your veterinarian or a veterinary professional can sign and date this form to confirm their involvement in your pet's care.
* **Submit (ID 8)**: A text field where you can submit your completed form for review and assistance.

## Tips
* Make sure to include all necessary information about your pet and travel plans to ensure the best possible care and assistance during travel.
* If you are unsure about any information or have questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* Please keep a copy of this form for your records and ensure that your veterinarian or veterinary professional has a copy as well.
