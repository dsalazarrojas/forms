# Veterinary Policy Information Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Veterinary Policy Information Request Form" is designed for veterinarians and pet owners to provide detailed information about policies related to pet care, insurance, and other veterinary-related matters. This form aims to gather essential data for efficient policy handling and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the provided fields with relevant information about the policy.
2. Provide detailed descriptions for each field to ensure that you have provided accurate and complete information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Policy Information** (`policy_information`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the policy you are requesting information about.
* **2. Pet Details** (`pet_details`, text, required): Provide the type and breed of the pet, along with any relevant medical history or special needs.
* **3. Owner Details** (`owner_details`, text, required): Enter the owner's name and contact information for policy verification purposes.
* **4. Policy Verification** (`policy_verification`, text, required): Verify that you are the policy owner or authorized representative.
* **5. Policy Coverage** (`policy_coverage`, text, required): Specify the policy's coverage details (e.g., insurance coverage, policy scope).
* **6. Policy Status** (`policy_status`, text, required): Indicate the current status of the policy (e.g., active, inactive, pending).
* **7. Policy Expiration** (`policy_expiration`, text, required): Enter the policy's expiration date.
* **8. Policy Effectiveness** (`policy_effectiveness`, text, required): Describe the policy's effectiveness (e.g., when it started, how long it will last).
* **9. Policy Notes** (`policy_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes related to the policy.
* **10. Policy Coverage Notes** (`policy_coverage_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about policy coverage.
* **11. Policy Effectiveness Notes** (`policy_effectiveness_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes related to policy effectiveness.
* **12. Policy Expiration Notes** (`policy_expiration_notes`, text, optional): Note any expiration date-related information.
* **13. Policy Verification Notes** (`policy_verification_notes`, text, optional): Add any verification-related comments or notes.
* **14. Policy Holder** (`policy_holder`, text, required): Enter the policy's holder's name and contact information.
* **15. Policy Holder Notes** (`policy_holder_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the policy holder.
* **16. Policy Holder Verification** (`policy_holder_verification`, text, required): Verify that you are the policy holder or authorized representative.
* **17. Owner Signature** (`owner_signature`, text, required): Sign the form with the owner's signature.
* **18. Owner Date** (`owner_date`, text, required): Enter the date of signing.
* **19. Owner Notes** (`owner_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes from the owner.
* **20. Policy Number** (`policy_number`, text, required): Enter the policy number (if applicable).

## Tips

* Ensure that you complete all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check the information provided to avoid errors.
* If you need help, contact the veterinary clinic or office staff for assistance.
