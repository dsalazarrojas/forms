# Pet Eye Contact Observation Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Eye Contact Observation Log is a form designed to record observations and notes for pet eye contact. It is intended for pet owners or veterinarians to document the details of pet eye contact, including the date and time of the observation, owner contact information, and pet type.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the pet eye contact observation log date and time.
2. Enter the details of the observation in the pet observation log field.
3. Add any additional notes or comments in the pet observation log note field.
4. Enter the owner's name and contact information.
5. Specify the pet type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Eye Date** (`pet_eye_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the observation.
* **Pet Eye Time** (`pet_eye_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the observation.
* **Pet Observation Log** (`pet_observation_log`, text, required): Record the details of the observation.
* **Pet Observation Log Note** (`pet_observation_log_note`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the observation.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the pet owner.
* **Owner Contact Info** (`owner_contact_info`, text, required): Enter the contact information for the pet owner.
* **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, text, required): Specify the type of pet being observed.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter all required fields accurately and completely.
* Use the pet observation log field to record specific details about the pet eye contact.
* Use the pet observation log note field to add any additional comments or notes about the observation.
* If the pet type is not specified, the form will not be complete.
* If you are a pet owner, make sure to inform your veterinarian about any changes or updates to the pet eye contact observation log.
