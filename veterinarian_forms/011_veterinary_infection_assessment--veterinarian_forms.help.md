# Veterinary Infection Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect general information about a pet's infection. The form is for public use and is meant to help pet owners provide details about their pet's current health condition.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide details about your pet, including their name, age, and type.
2. Describe the symptoms you have observed in your pet.
3. Indicate whether the pet has been infected at any of the following sites:
	* Yes (tick one or multiple)
	* No
4. If applicable, provide details about any treatments or medications your pet is currently receiving.
5. Enter the date of the visit to the veterinary clinic.
6. Enter the name of the veterinarian who treated your pet.
7. Provide the name of the veterinary clinic where your pet was treated.
8. Enter any relevant contact information for the pet's owner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet details** (`pet_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your pet, including their name, age, and type (e.g., dog, cat, etc.).
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, optional): Describe the symptoms you have observed in your pet, such as vomiting, diarrhea, etc.
* **Infection sites** (`infection_sites`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether the pet has been infected at any of the following sites:
	+ Yes (tick one or multiple)
	+ No
* **Treatments** (`treatments`, text, optional): If applicable, provide details about any treatments or medications your pet is currently receiving.
* **Date of visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, optional): Enter the date of the visit to the veterinary clinic.
* **Veterinarian name** (`veterinarian_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the veterinarian who treated your pet.
* **Veterinary clinic** (`veterinary_clinic`, text, optional): Enter the name of the veterinary clinic where your pet was treated.
* **Owner contact** (`owner_contact`, text, optional): Provide any relevant contact information for the pet's owner.

## Tips
This form is designed to gather general information about your pet's infection. Please fill out as much or as little information as you need, depending on your situation.
