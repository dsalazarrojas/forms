# Blackjack Animal Rescue Online Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Blackjack Animal Rescue Online Application Form is designed to help match you with the perfect animal from our rescue. This form collects information about you and your lifestyle to ensure we can find the best fit for you and your new companion.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each field carefully and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before proceeding.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name.
* **Last Name**: Your last name.
* **Age**: Your age.
* **Email Address**: A valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number**: A valid phone number where we can contact you.
* **Home Address**: A complete address where you live.
* **Housing Type**: The type of housing you live in, such as a house, apartment, or condo.
* **Do you own or rent your home?**: If you rent, do you have permission from your landlord to have a pet? If yes, we can proceed. If not, we may not be able to place a pet with you.
* **If renting, does landlord allow pets?**: If you rent, please select Yes or No.
* **Do you have an enclosed yard?**: If you have a yard, is it fully or partially fenced? If not, this may impact the type of animal we can match with you.
* **Do you have other pets?**: If you have other pets, please select Yes or No.
* **Current Pets (if applicable)**: Please provide details about your current pets, including species, age, and temperament.
* **Experience with pets**: How much experience do you have with pets?
* **Type of animal interested in**: Select all the types of animals you are interested in adopting.
* **Primary purpose of adoption**: What is the main reason for adopting a new animal?
* **Veterinary Reference (if applicable)**: If you have had previous experience with animals, please provide the name and contact information of your previous veterinarian.
* **Commitment Level**: How long do you plan to keep the animal?
* **Application Date**: The date you are submitting this application.

Note: This form is for general guidance and may not cover every possible scenario. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask.
