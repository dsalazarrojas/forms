# Zoo Veterinarian Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Zoo Veterinarian Referral Form is used to collect and document information about animal health concerns and care requirements from zoon-related organizations. This information will be used by veterinarians to provide proper care and treatment to the animals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields: <u>Animal ID</u>, <u>Animal Health Concerns</u>, <u>Animal ID</u> (if applicable), <u>Vet Name</u>, <u>Vet Contact Info</u>, and <u>Submitted By</u>.
2. If required, fill in the <u>Animal ID</u> of the animal and any other relevant information.
3. In the <u>Animal Health Concerns</u> field, describe the health issues or concerns you are reporting.
4. In the <u>Care Requirements</u> field, specify any special care the animal requires.
5. In the <u>Submitted By</u> field, select the organization or individual who submitted this form (Zoo or Vet).
6. Select the <u>Submitted Date</u> to indicate when the form was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Animal ID** (`animal_id`, number, required): Please fill in the unique identifier for the animal. If the animal is new, leave this field blank.
* **Zoo Name** (`zoo_name`, text, optional): If you are submitting on behalf of the zoo, enter the name of the zoo.
* **Vet Name** (`vet_name`, text, optional): If you are submitting on behalf of a vet, enter the name of the vet.
* **Animal Health Concerns** (`animal_health_concerns`, text, optional): Describe any health concerns or issues you are reporting.
* **Care Requirements** (`care_requirements`, text, optional): Specify any special care the animal requires.
* **Zoo Contact Info** (`zoo_contact_info`, text, optional): If you are submitting on behalf of the zoo, enter the contact information.
* **Vet Contact Info** (`vet_contact_info`, text, optional): If you are submitting on behalf of a vet, enter the contact information.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether you are submitting on behalf of the Zoo or Vet.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, optional): Select the date when this form was submitted.
