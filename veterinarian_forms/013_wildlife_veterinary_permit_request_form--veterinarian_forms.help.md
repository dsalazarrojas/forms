# Wildlife Veterinary Permit Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wildlife Veterinary Permit Request Form is a tool for requesting a permit that authorizes the holder to care for and transport wildlife in a specific species. This form is designed to gather necessary information for the permit issuance process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully review each field to ensure you have completed all required and relevant information.
- Enter accurate information for each field.
- Ensure you have filled out the form completely before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (1, text, optional): Enter a brief title describing the reason for the permit request.
* **Veterinarian's Name** (2, text, optional): Enter the name of the veterinarian who will be caring for the wildlife.
* **Contact Number** (3, text, optional): Enter the contact number of the veterinarian or facility representative.
* **Email Address** (4, email, optional): Enter the email address of the veterinarian or facility representative.
* **Species** (5, text, optional): Select the species of wildlife being cared for or transported.
* **Permit Type** (6, select_one, optional): Choose the type of permit being requested:
	+ Permit for Wildlife Rehabilitation
	+ Permit for Wildlife Relocation
	+ Permit for Wildlife Transport
* **Wildlife Species** (7, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable species of wildlife being cared for or transported.
	+ Mammal
	+ Reptile
	+ Amphibian
* **Permit Number** (8, text, optional): Enter a reference or identifier for the permit request.
* **Date of Capture** (9, date, optional): Enter the date when the wildlife was captured.
* **Time of Capture** (10, time, optional): Enter the time when the wildlife was captured.
* **Date of Release** (11, date, optional): Enter the date when the wildlife was released.
* **Time of Release** (12, time, optional): Enter the time when the wildlife was released.
* **Release Location** (13, text, optional): Enter the location where the wildlife was released.
* **Wildlife Caregiver Name** (14, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or facility caring for the wildlife.
* **Wildlife Caregiver Contact Number** (15, text, optional): Enter the contact number of the wildlife caregiver.
* **Wildlife Caregiver Email Address** (16, email, optional): Enter the email address of the wildlife caregiver.
* **Permit Status** (17, text, optional): Enter the current status of the permit request.
* **Comments** (18, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the permit request.
* **Veterinary Facility Name** (19, text, optional): Enter the name of the veterinary facility issuing the permit.
* **Veterinary Facility License** (20, text, optional): Enter the license number or ID of the veterinary facility.
* **Permittee Facility License** (21, text, optional): Enter the license number or ID of the permittee's facility.
* **Permittee Name** (22, text, optional): Enter the name of the permittee.
* **Wildlife Veterinary Permit Note** (23, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the permit request.
* **Submitter's Name** (24, text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the permit request.
* **Submitter's Email** (25, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person submitting the permit request.
