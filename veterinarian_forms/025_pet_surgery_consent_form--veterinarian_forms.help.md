# Pet Surgery Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be used by pet owners to provide consent and essential information for their pet's surgery. It includes sections to acknowledge risks, provide contact details, and specify veterinary services required.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through each section carefully to ensure you understand what information is required.
2. Fill in the relevant sections with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Consent** (`owner_consent`, text, optional): This section is for you to provide consent for your pet's surgery. Please enter a simple "yes" or "no" to indicate your agreement.
* **Risks Explained** (`risks_explained`, text, optional): Briefly describe any risks or concerns you have regarding your pet's surgery. This will help the veterinarian understand your pet's specific situation.
* **Authorization Document** (`authorization_document`, text, optional): Upload a copy of the authorization document, if required, to support your pet's surgery.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Provide your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email, so we can reach you with updates and questions.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name, phone number, and email of the person to be contacted in case of emergencies.
* **Veterinary Services** (`veterinary_services`, select_multiple, optional): Select any services that your pet requires during surgery, such as surgery, anesthesia, or an anatomical examination.
* **Anesthesia Risks** (`anesthesia_risks`, text, optional): Describe any risks or concerns you have regarding anesthesia for your pet's surgery.
* **Anesthesia Type** (`anesthesia_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of anesthesia that will be used during your pet's surgery, such as general anesthesia, sedation, or local anesthesia.
* **Surgeon Experience** (`surgeon_experience`, text, optional): Briefly describe the experience and qualifications of the veterinarian performing the surgery.

## Tips
- Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information to help with a successful surgery process.
- Review your responses before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask the veterinarian.
