# Veterinary Technology Prototype Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from veterinary institutions and researchers about their veterinary technology prototype applications. The form aims to gather essential details about the submitter, institution, development stage, submission description, supporting files, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Submitter Information section with your name and contact details.
2. Enter the name of the institution submitting the veterinary technology prototype application.
3. Select the development stage of the application (Development, Pilot, Testing, or Launch).
4. Provide a brief description of the submission.
5. Choose the type of supporting files you are providing (video, image, or document).
6. Enter your contact information (name, email, and contact).
7. Select the submission status (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
8. Enter your name and institution as the reviewer.
9. Enter your contact information (name, email, and contact).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitter Information** (`submitter_info`, text, required): Enter the name and contact details of the person submitting the form.
* **Institution** (`institution`, text, required): Enter the name of the institution submitting the form.
* **Development Stage** (`development_stage`, select_one, required): Select the current development stage of the veterinary technology prototype application (Development, Pilot, Testing, or Launch).
* **Submission Description** (`submission_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the submission (max 1000 characters).
* **Supporting Files** (`supporting_files`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of supporting files you are providing (video, image, or document).
* **Submitter Contact** (`submitter_contact`, text, required): Enter your contact information (name and email).
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Submission URL** (`submission_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL of the submission (if applicable).
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the submission (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
* **Reviewers Comments** (`reviewers_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments from the reviewer(s) (max 1000 characters).
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Institution** (`reviewer_institution`, text, required): Enter the name of the institution where you are reviewing from.
* **Reviewer Contact** (`reviewer_contact`, text, required): Enter your contact information (phone number or other contact details).
* **Reviewer Email** (`reviewer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Submission ID** (`submission_id`, text, optional): Enter a unique ID for the submission (if applicable).
