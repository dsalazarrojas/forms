# Animal Hospital Estimate Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for veterinarians to create an estimate for animal hospital services. It gathers information about the client, pet, and services required for a detailed cost estimate. The form also includes sections for services, medications, hospitalization, and payment options, ensuring a comprehensive estimate is provided to the client.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Client Information**: Fill in the client's name, email, phone number, and address.
2.  **Pet Information**: Enter the pet's name, species, age, weight, and reason for visit.
3.  **Services**: Select the services required for the pet, such as vaccinations, dental cleaning, and blood work.
4.  **Estimate Details**: Enter the estimated costs for examination, diagnostic tests, procedures, medications, hospitalization, and additional services.
5.  **Payment Information**: Choose the payment options and any deposit required for services.
6.  **Authorization**: Confirm if you approve the estimate and proceed with services.
7.  **Veterinarian Information**: Enter your name and signature as the attending veterinarian.
8.  **Client Authorization**: Sign and date the form to confirm your approval of the estimate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Full Name**: Enter the pet owner's name.
*   **Email Address**: Enter the client's email address for contact purposes.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter the client's phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Address**: Enter the client's street address, city, state, and zip code.
*   **Client Name**: Enter the pet owner's name.
*   **Estimated Services**: Enter a detailed cost estimate for veterinary services.
*   **Estimate Date**: Enter the date of the estimate.
*   **Estimate Number**: Enter a reference ID for the estimate.
*   **Pet's Name**: Enter the name of the pet.
*   **Species**: Select the type of animal (e.g., dog, cat, bird, etc.).
*   **Age**: Enter the age of the pet in years.
*   **Weight**: Enter the weight of the pet in pounds.
*   **Reason for Visit**: Enter the reason for the pet's visit.
*   **Diagnostic Tests**: Select all applicable diagnostic tests (e.g., blood work, urinalysis, etc.).
*   **Estimated Diagnostic Costs**: Enter the total cost of the diagnostic tests.
*   **Procedures**: Select all applicable procedures (e.g., vaccination, dental cleaning, etc.).
*   **Estimated Procedure Costs**: Enter the total cost of procedures.
*   **Medications**: Enter any prescribed medications for the pet.
*   **Estimated Medication Costs**: Enter the total cost of medications.
*   **Hospitalization Required**: Select if hospitalization is required and for how many days.
*   **Estimated Hospitalization Costs**: Enter the total cost of hospitalization.
*   **Additional Services**: Enter any additional services required.
*   **Additional Costs**: Enter the total cost of additional services.
*   **Estimate Totals**: Enter a summary of the estimate.
*   **Subtotal**: Enter the subtotal of the estimate before tax.
*   **Tax**: Enter any tax applicable for the services.
*   **Total Estimated Cost**: Enter the grand total of the estimate.
*   **Estimate Validity**: Enter the date until which the estimate is valid.
*   **Payment Options**: Select all applicable payment options (e.g., cash, credit card, etc.).
*   **Is a Deposit Required**: Select if a deposit is required.
*   **Deposit Amount**: Enter the deposit amount if required.
*   **Additional Notes**: Enter any important information for the client.
*   **Do You Approve This Estimate**: Confirm approval of the estimate.
*   **Veterinarian Name**: Enter your name as the attending veterinarian.
*   **Veterinarian Signature**: Sign with your name as the attending veterinarian.
*   **Date**: Enter the date of the veterinarian's signature.
*   **Client Signature**: Sign with your name as the pet owner.
*   **Deposit Required**: Select if a deposit is required for payment.
*   **Deposit Amount**: Enter the deposit amount if required.
*   **Additional Costs**: Enter the total cost of additional services.
*   **Total Estimated Cost**: Enter the grand total of the estimate.

## Tips

*   Always review the client's information carefully to ensure accuracy.
*   Ensure all services and costs are detailed and clear for the client.
*   Keep the estimate up-to-date and review it regularly for changes.
*   Verify the client's payment options and deposit requirements.
*   Sign and date the form to confirm your approval of the estimate.
*   Review the estimate with the client and ensure they understand the terms.
*   Keep the client informed of any changes or updates to the estimate.
*   Use this form as a guide to create a comprehensive estimate for your veterinary practice.
