# Veterinary Digital Transformation Readiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Digital Transformation Readiness Survey is a tool designed to evaluate the current state of digital transformation in veterinary practices. This form helps veterinary professionals assess their readiness to adopt digital technologies and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Answer the questions honestly, and to the best of your knowledge.
2.  For questions where you are unsure or not applicable, please select "Not Applicable" or provide a brief explanation.
3.  For text fields, provide a brief explanation or example.
4.  If you have trouble accessing the form or encounter any issues, please contact <form@gic.mx>.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Are you currently using digital tools in your practice?** (`question_1`): This question is designed to determine your current level of digital tool usage. Select "Yes" if you use digital tools regularly, "False" if you do not use them, and "Not Applicable" if you are unsure or do not apply in your case.
*   **How often do you use digital tools in your practice? (`question_2`): This question is meant to gauge the frequency of digital tool usage. Select "Daily", "Weekly", "Monthly", "Rarely", or "Never" to express your usual digital tool usage.
*   **What are some of the main benefits of digital transformation in your practice? (`question_3`): This question is to gather information on the advantages of digital transformation. Please provide a brief description or example of how digital transformation has positively impacted your practice.
*   **What are some of the challenges or barriers to digital transformation in your practice? (`question_4`): This question aims to identify obstacles to digital transformation. Describe any challenges or difficulties you have faced or are facing in adopting digital technologies in your practice.
*   **What role do you play in your practice? (`question_5`): This question is to understand your position within the practice. Please describe your role, such as veterinary doctor, practice manager, or other.
*   **How long have you been in practice? (`question_6`): This question is to determine how long you have been working in the veterinary field. Select the corresponding option that best reflects your time in practice.
