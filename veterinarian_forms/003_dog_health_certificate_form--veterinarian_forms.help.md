# Dog Health Certificate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a health certificate for dogs, which is typically required for international travel or other official purposes. It ensures that the dog's health is verified and meets the necessary standards.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Contact Information**: Enter the dog's owner's contact information, including their name, address, phone number, and email.
2. Fill in the dog's **Name**, **Breed**, and **Age**.
3. Check the status of the dog's **Rabies**, **Distemper**, and **Parvovirus**.
4. Describe any **Medical Conditions** the dog may have.
5. Sign the form with your **Signature** and enter the **Date Signed**.
6. Enter your **Veterinary License Number**, **Veterinary Name**, **Veterinary Address**, **Veterinary Phone**, **Veterinary Email**, and **Veterinary Facility**.
7. Verify that you are a licensed veterinarian by checking the **Veterinary State License**.
8. Date and sign the form as a **Veterinarian**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`owner_information`):
	+ This field is required to collect contact information about the dog's owner.
* **Name** (`name`):
	+ Enter the dog's name.
* **Breed** (`breed`):
	+ Enter the dog's breed.
* **Age** (`age`):
	+ Enter the dog's age.
* **Rabies** (`rabies`):
	+ Check if the dog has been vaccinated against rabies.
* **Distemper** (`distemper`):
	+ Check if the dog has distemper.
* **Parvovirus** (`parvovirus`):
	+ Check if the dog has parvovirus.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`):
	+ Describe any medical conditions the dog may have.
* **Signature** (`signature`):
	+ Sign the form to authenticate the information provided.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`):
	+ Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Veterinarian Signature** (`veterinarian_signature`):
	+ Check that you are a licensed veterinarian.
* **Veterinary License Number** (`veterinary_license_number`):
	+ Enter your veterinary license number.
* **Veterinary Name** (`veterinary_name`):
	+ Enter your name as a veterinarian.
* **Veterinary Address** (`veterinary_address`):
	+ Enter your veterinary clinic's address.
* **Veterinary Phone** (`veterinary_phone`):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Veterinary Email** (`veterinary_email`):
	+ Enter your email.
* **Veterinary Facility** (`veterinary_facility`):
	+ Enter the name of your veterinary clinic or facility.
* **Veterinary State License** (`veterinary_state_license`):
	+ Verify that you are a licensed veterinarian.
