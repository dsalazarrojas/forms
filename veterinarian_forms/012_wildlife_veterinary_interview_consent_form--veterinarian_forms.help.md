# Wildlife Veterinary Interview Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wildlife Veterinary Interview Consent Form is a critical tool used to obtain informed consent from wildlife participants before conducting interviews. This form helps ensure that the participant is aware of the purpose, risks, and benefits of the interview and provides an opportunity for them to share any concerns or questions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Participant Name field with the name of the participant.
2. Enter the Participant Email address of the participant (optional).
3. Provide the Contact Number of the participant (required for follow-up communication).
4. Select the Date of Consent, when the participant agreed to give their consent for the interview.
5. Choose the Time of Consent, when the participant agreed to give their consent for the interview (note that this is an optional field).
6. Select the Reason for Interview, one of the options provided.
7. Enter the age of the participant.
8. Select the Gender of the participant.
9. Select the Ethnicity of the participant.
10. Select the language spoken by the participant.
11. Select the participant's education level.
12. Select the participant's occupation.
13. Select the location where the participant lives.
14. Enter any additional notes about the participant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the participant.
* **Participant Email** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the participant, this will be used for follow-up communication (if allowed).
* **Contact Number** (text, required): Enter a contact number of the participant, this will be used for follow-up communication.
* **Date of Consent** (date, required): Select the date when the participant agreed to give their consent for the interview.
* **Time of Consent** (time, optional): Select the time when the participant agreed to give their consent for the interview (note that this is an optional field).
* **Reason for Interview** (select_one, required): Select the reason why you want to interview the participant, one of the provided options.
* **Participant Age** (number, required): Enter the age of the participant.
* **Participant Gender** (select_one, required): Select the gender of the participant.
* **Participant Ethnicity** (select_one, required): Select the ethnicity of the participant.
* **Participant Language** (select_one, required): Select the language spoken by the participant.
* **Participant Education** (select_one, required): Select the participant's education level.
* **Participant Occupation** (select_one, optional): Select the participant's occupation.
* **Participant Location** (select_one, optional): Select the location where the participant lives.
* **Participant Note** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the participant.
