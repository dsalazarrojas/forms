# Veterinary Toxicology Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Toxicology Assessment Form is a tool designed to help collect information about a pet's medical history and exposure to potential toxins.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide information about the pet owner, including their name and contact details.
2. Describe the pet's medical history, including any previous illnesses or allergies.
3. Report any recent incidents that may have led to the pet's current condition.
4. Provide details about the poisoning or exposure to toxins.
5. Report any treatment outcomes and follow-up information.
6. Enter any lab test results.
7. Enter any follow-up information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Owner Info**: Enter the name and contact details of the pet owner.
* **Pet Info**: Describe the pet's medical history, including any previous illnesses or allergies.
* **Incident Info**: Report any recent incidents that may have led to the pet's current condition.
* **Poisoning Details**: Provide details about the poisoning or exposure to toxins.
* **Treatment History**: Describe any previous treatment or medication that the pet has received.
* **Clinical Findings**: Report any observations about the pet's physical condition.
* **Veterinary Recommendations**: Enter any recommendations made by a veterinarian.
* **Lab Info**: Enter any lab test results.
* **Follow Up**: Enter any follow-up information or next steps.
* **Animal Info**: Describe the pet's characteristics, such as age, weight, and gender.
* **Animal Type**: Select the type of animal (cat, dog, other).
* **Animal Age**: Enter the pet's age.
* **Animal Gender**: Select the pet's gender (male, female, other).
* **Animal Weight**: Enter the pet's weight.
* **Owner Age**: Enter the owner's age.
* **Owner Contact 2**: Enter additional contact information for the owner.
* **Animal Health Condition**: Describe the pet's health condition.
* **Incident Type**: Select the type of incident (poisoning, allergic reaction, other).
* **Exposure Path**: Enter details about the exposure path (e.g., ingestion, skin contact).
* **Treatment Outcome**: Enter any treatment outcomes or next steps.
* **Lab Test Results**: Enter any lab test results.
* **Owner Contact**: Enter any additional contact information for the owner.
* **Contact Details**: Enter any contact details or next steps.
* **Follow Up Info**: Enter any follow-up information or next steps.
* **Owner Contact 2**: Enter additional contact information for the owner.
