# pet_diary_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help pet owners keep a record of their pet's daily activities, health, and other relevant information. It is intended to be a simple and straightforward tool for tracking and monitoring your pet's progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your pet's details in the "Pet Details" field.
2. Describe your pet's daily activity in the "Daily Activity" field.
3. Select the date of the activity in the "Activity Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Details** (`Pet Details`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your pet's name, species, age, and any other relevant information about your pet.
* **Daily Activity** (`Daily Activity`, text, optional):
	+ Describe your pet's activity or behavior for the day, such as eating habits, exercise, or any notable events or milestones.
* **Activity Date** (`Activity Date`, date, optional):
	+ Select the date of the activity or event being recorded.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete the form regularly to keep an accurate and up-to-date record of your pet's activities.
- You can use this form as a template and modify it to suit your pet's needs and your personal preferences.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to contact us.
