# Animal Breeder Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for applicants interested in joining our animal breeding team. Please complete this form to provide information about yourself, your experience, and your qualifications. This will help us get to know you better and understand your fit for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Section 1: Contact Information**
	* **Full Name**: Enter your full name, as it appears on your ID or passport.
	* **Email Address**: Provide your primary contact email address.
	* **Phone Number**: Enter your best contact number.
	* **Home Address**: Enter your street address, city, state, and zip code.
2. **Section 2: Application Information**
	* **Application Introduction**: This section is not mandatory, but it's an opportunity to express your interest in the position and introduce yourself.
	* **Position Type**: Select the type of position you are applying for (Full-time, Part-time, Breeding Assistant, Kennel Manager, Breeding Consultant, or Apprentice).
	* **Available Start Date**: Enter the date you are available to start working.
3. **Section 3: Experience and Education**
	* **Experience Section**: This section is not mandatory, but it's an opportunity to describe your breeding and animal care experience.
	* **Years of Experience**: Select the number of years you have experience in animal breeding.
	* **Species Experience**: Select all the species you have worked with (Dogs, Cats, Horses, Cattle, Sheep, Goats, Pigs, Poultry, Exotic animals).
	* **Breed Specialization**: Enter specific breeds you specialize in (if applicable).
	* **Breeding Programs**: Describe your experience in previous breeding programs (if applicable).
	* **Education Section**: This section is not mandatory, but it's an opportunity to describe your educational background.
	* **Highest Education**: Select your highest level of education completed (High school or equivalent, Some college, Associate degree, Bachelor's degree, Master's degree, or Doctorate/DVM).
	* **Degree Field**: Enter the major or concentration of your degree (if applicable).
	* **Relevant Certifications**: Enter any relevant certifications you hold (if applicable).
4. **Section 4: Skills and Qualifications**
	* **Technical Skills**: Select all the technical skills you have (Artificial insemination, Pregnancy diagnosis, Whelping assistance, Neonatal care, Genetic testing, Pedigree analysis, Health screening, Nutrition planning, Record keeping).
	* **Areas of Expertise**: Select all the areas of expertise you have (Genetics and heredity, Reproductive physiology, Animal nutrition, Behavior and temperament, Disease prevention, Breed standards, Show preparation, Business management).
	* **Employment History**: Provide information about your previous employers in animal breeding (if applicable).
5. **Section 5: Application and Consent**
	* **Employment History**: Enter your reason for applying for this position and what makes you suitable for it.
	* **Career Goals**: Enter your career goals in animal breeding (if applicable).
	* **Background Check**: Confirm you are willing to undergo a background check.
	* **Drug Screening**: Confirm you are willing to undergo a drug screening (if required).
	* **Additional Information**: Enter any other information you would like to share.
6. **Section 6: Final Steps**
	* **Applicant Signature**: Type your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
	* **Date**: Enter the current date.

**Tips**

* Please ensure you complete all mandatory fields (marked as "Required") to avoid delays in processing your application.
* Use clear and concise language when answering questions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact information].
