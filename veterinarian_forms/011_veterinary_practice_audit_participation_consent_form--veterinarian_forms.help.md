# veterinary_practice_audit_participation_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the participation and consent of veterinary practices in a specific audit. It helps ensure that the practice is aware of and agrees to the terms of the audit.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and make sure you have all the required information before filling it out.
2. Confirm your participation and consent to the audit in the "Audit Participation and Consent" section.
3. Fill out the "Practice Details" section with accurate information about your practice.
4. Submit the form to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Do you agree** (1):
	+ This field asks if you agree to participate in the audit. Please select "Yes" to confirm your participation.
* **practice_npi_id** (2):
	+ This field is not applicable for this form.
* **practice_address** (3):
	+ This field is for the address of your veterinary practice.
* **practice_city** (4):
	+ This field is for the city of your veterinary practice.
* **practice_state** (5):
	+ This field is for the state of your veterinary practice.
* **practice_zip** (6):
	+ This field is for the zip code of your veterinary practice.
* **practice_name** (7):
	+ This field is for the name of your veterinary practice.
* **practice_phone** (8):
	+ This field is for the phone number of your practice.
* **practice_fax** (9):
	+ This field is for the fax number of your practice.
* **practice_email** (10):
	+ This field is for the email address of your practice.
* **audit_consent_date** (11):
	+ This field is for the date of the audit.
* **audit_consent_time** (12):
	+ This field is for the time of the audit.
* **auditors_name** (13):
	+ This field is for the name of the auditor.
* **auditors_title** (14):
	+ This field is for the title of the auditor.
* **auditors_phone** (15):
	+ This field is for the phone number of the auditor.
* **auditors_email** (16):
	+ This field is for the email address of the auditor.
* **auditors_facility** (17):
	+ This field is for the facility of the auditor.
* **auditors_facility_npi** (18):
	+ This field is for the facility NPI (National Provider Identifier) of the auditor.
* **auditors_facility_street** (19):
	+ This field is for the street address of the auditor's facility.
* **auditors_facility_city** (20):
	+ This field is for the city of the auditor's facility.
* **auditors_facility_state** (21):
	+ This field is for the state of the auditor's facility.
* **auditors_facility_zip** (22):
	+ This field is for the zip code of the auditor's facility.
* **auditors_facility_name** (23):
	+ This field is for the name of the auditor's facility.
* **auditors_facility_phone** (24):
	+ This field is for the phone number of the auditor's facility.
* **auditors_facility_email** (25):
	+ This field is for the email address of the auditor's facility.
