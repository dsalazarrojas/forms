# Veterinary Staff Training Session Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Staff Training Session Request Form is designed to be used by veterinary clinic staff to request a training session. This form will guide them in providing necessary information for the training session. The form helps to ensure that all required details are collected and submitted efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide clinic information on the first page. Enter the name of your clinic and any other necessary details.
2. Provide details about the training session on the second page. Describe what you want to learn or achieve from the training session.
3. On the third page, specify who requested the training session, their name and their contact information (Email and Phone number).
4. Select a date for the training session on the fourth page.
5. Choose a time for the training session on the fifth page.
6. On the sixth page, select a topic for the training session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Clinic Information** (`clinic_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your clinic name and any other necessary information.
* **Training Details** (`training_details`, `text`, required: false): Describe what you want to learn or achieve from the training session.
* **Requested By** (`requested_by`, `text`, required: false): Specify who requested this training session and their name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the person requesting the training session.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the person requesting the training session.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date for the training session.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Choose the time for the training session.
* **Session Topic** (`session_topic`, `text`, required: false): Select a topic for the training session.
