# veterinary_workshop_participant_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for workshop participants to provide their consent and contact information. It is used to collect data about their participation status, personal details, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select "Yes" or "No" for your workshop participation status.
2. Fill out the required fields for your personal details, including your name and email address.
3. For emergency contacts, please provide the name and contact information (phone number or email) for at least one contact person.
4. Select your preferred contact method (Phone, Email, or Other).
5. Fill out the fields for your emergency contact 1's phone and email (if applicable).
6. Specify your relationship with your emergency contact 1.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Participation** (`workshop_participation`, text, required): Indicate whether you will participate in the workshop by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, required): Fill out your name and email address.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, text, required): Provide a name for your emergency contact.
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, note, required): Sign this field to confirm your consent to participate in the workshop.
* **Contact Preference** (`contact_preference`, select_one, required): Select your preferred contact method (Phone, Email, or Other).
* **Emergency Contact 1** (`emergency_contact_1`, text, required): Provide the name for your emergency contact 1.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Specify your relationship with your emergency contact 1.
* **Emergency Contact 2** (`emergency_contact_2`, text, required): Provide the name for your emergency contact 2 (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact 2 Phone** (`emergency_contact_2_phone`, text, required): Fill out your emergency contact 2's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact 2 Email** (`emergency_contact_2_email`, email, required): Fill out your emergency contact 2's email address.
