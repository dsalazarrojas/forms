# Pet Breeding Ethics Certification Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Breeding Ethics Certification Application Form is designed to gather information from applicants seeking certification in pet breeding ethics. This form is used to verify the applicant's adherence to pet breeding ethics standards and to issue a certification that confirms their commitment to responsible and humane pet breeding practices.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that the organization's name and address are correctly formatted.
3. Enter the contact person's name and email address correctly.
4. Enter the phone number in the correct format (e.g., +1-123-456-7890).
5. Select the certification date and certification expiration date in the correct format (yyyy-mm-dd).
6. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Breeding Ethics Certification Application Form** (Page 1):
	+ This is the title of the form that will be displayed on the certification.
* **Organization Name** (Page 2, Required):
	+ Enter the name of your organization as it appears on your business cards or website.
* **Address** (Page 3, Required):
	+ Enter your organization's physical address or location where your business operations are conducted.
* **Contact Person** (Page 4, Required):
	+ Enter the name of the person to contact for more information.
* **Email** (Page 5, Required, Email):
	+ Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone** (Page 6, Required):
	+ Enter the contact person's phone number in the correct format (e.g., +1-123-456-7890).
* **Certification Date** (Page 7, Required):
	+ Enter the date when you first obtained your certification.
* **Certification Expiration Date** (Page 8, Required):
	+ Enter the date when your certification will expire.

## Tips
1. Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
2. Double-check the email address and phone number format for correctness.
3. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.
4. If you are unsure about any field, contact us for clarification.
