# Pet Microchip Study Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from pet owners on their pet's microchip status and other relevant details. This data will be used to support research on pet microchips.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your pet's details in the "Pet Details" section.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Owner Details" section.
3. Indicate if your pet has a microchip or not in the "Microchip Info" section.
4. If your pet has a microchip, provide more details in the "Microchip Research" section.
5. Describe your pet's health in the "Pet Health" section, if applicable.
6. Finally, provide your comments or any additional information in the "Veterinarian Info" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Details** (`Pet Details`, text, required/optional): Enter your pet's name, species, age, and any other relevant information.
* **Owner Details** (`Owner Details`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact information, including name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Microchip Info** (`Microchip Info`, text, required/optional): Indicate if your pet has a microchip. If yes, please provide the microchip number.
* **Microchip** (`Microchip`, select one, optional): Select 'True' if your pet has a microchip or 'False' if not.
* **Microchip Research** (`Microchip Research`, text, optional): If your pet has a microchip, provide any relevant information about the microchip, such as its brand, serial number, or location of implantation.
* **Pet Health** (`Pet Health`, note, optional): Describe your pet's health, including any medical conditions or allergies they may have.
* **Veterinarian Info** (`Veterinarian Info`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your pet's veterinary care.
