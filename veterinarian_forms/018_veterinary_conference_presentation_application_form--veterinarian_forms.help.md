# Veterinary Conference Presentation Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Veterinary Conference Presentation Application Form is designed to gather necessary information from speakers and presenters for a veterinary conference. This form helps organizers to collect details about the speaker, their abstract, and session proposal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including speaker name, email, phone number, and speaker organization (if applicable).
2. Provide a clear and concise abstract title and text.
3. Choose the session type (e.g., Conference, Workshop, Training, Symposium, Panel Discussion, Poster Presentation, or Other).
4. Enter the session duration and start and end dates.
5. Indicate whether you want to submit your presentation (Yes or No) and the date you intend to submit it.
6. Optionally, list your speaker interests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Speaker Name** (`speaker_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it would be printed in the conference schedule.
* **Speaker Organization** (`speaker_organization`, text, required): If you are affiliated with a university, research institution, or company, provide it here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for correspondence.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for calls or messages.
* **Abstract Title** (`abstract_title`, text, required): Enter a clear and concise title of your abstract.
* **Abstract Text** (`abstract_text`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your abstract.
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of session you propose (e.g., Conference, Workshop, Training, Symposium, Panel Discussion, Poster Presentation, or Other).
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of your session in minutes.
* **Session Start Date** (`session_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of your session in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Session End Date** (`session_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of your session in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required): Choose whether you want to submit your presentation (Yes or No).
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you plan to submit your presentation in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Speaker Interests** (`speaker_interests`, text, optional): List your interests, which may be helpful for future collaborations or networking.
