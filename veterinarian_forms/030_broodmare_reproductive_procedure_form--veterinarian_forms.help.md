# Broodmare Reproductive Procedure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Broodmare Reproductive Procedure Form is designed to record and document reproductive procedures for broodmares, ensuring accurate and efficient record-keeping for optimal breeding outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required contact information for the owner of the mare.
2. Enter the registered name of the mare.
3. Provide the age of the mare in years (if known).
4. Select the type of reproductive procedure requested.
5. Enter any pre-procedure veterinary notes and instructions.
6. Confirm that the owner has given consent for the procedure.
7. Provide post-procedure care instructions for the owner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Owner name** (`owner_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the mare's owner as provided by the owner or their authorized representative.
* **Owner contact phone** (`owner_contact`, text, required): Enter the phone number for the owner or their authorized representative.
* **Mare name** (`mare_name`, text, required): Enter the registered name of the mare as provided by the owner or their authorized representative.
* **Mare age** (`mare_age`, number, optional): If known, enter the age of the mare in years.
* **Procedure type** (`procedure_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of reproductive procedure requested from the available options:
	+ Artificial Insemination
	+ Embryo Transfer
	+ Caslick Procedure
	+ Other
* **Veterinary notes** (`veterinary_notes`, text, optional): Enter any pre-procedure notes and instructions from the veterinarian.
* **Consent given** (`consent_given`, select_one, required): Confirm that the owner has given consent for the procedure. Select 'True' if consent is given, 'False' otherwise.
* **Follow up instructions** (`follow_up_instructions`, text, optional): Provide post-procedure care instructions for the owner.

Note: The fields marked as optional are only required if the relevant information is known or applicable. Ensure you fill out only the required fields to minimize unnecessary work and maximize the form's utility.
