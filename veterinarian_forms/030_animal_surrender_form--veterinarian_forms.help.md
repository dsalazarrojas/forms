# Animal Surrender Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Surrender Form is a crucial tool for animal shelters to collect necessary information from animal owners who are surrendering their pets. The form is designed to gather information about the owner and the animal to ensure a smooth and safe transfer process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete the form in its entirety to ensure that all necessary information is provided.
2. Be honest and accurate when filling out the form, as it will help the shelter make informed decisions about the animal's care.
3. If you are unsure about any information, please do not hesitate to ask for assistance from shelter staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name as the owner of the animal.
* **Last Name**: Your last name as the owner of the animal.
* **Street Address**: Your residential address.
* **City**: The city of your residence.
* **State**: The state or province of your residence.
* **Zip Code**: The postal code of your residence.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact number.
* **Email Address**: Your contact email (optional).
* **Animal Name**: The name of the pet being surrendered.
* **Species**: The type of animal (e.g., Dog, Cat, Rabbit, etc.).
* **Breed**: The breed or mixed breed of the animal.
* **Age**: The age of the animal.
* **Color or Markings**: A description of the animal's appearance.
* **Weight**: The weight of the animal (optional).
* **Gender**: The gender of the animal (Male or Female).
* **Microchip Number**: The identification microchip number if available.
* **Known Medical Conditions**: Any health issues the animal has.
* **Current Medications**: Any medications the animal is taking.
* **Allergies or Dietary Restrictions**: Any known allergies or dietary restrictions.
* **Spay or Neuter Status**: Whether the animal has been spayed or neutered.
* **Current Vaccinations**: A list of completed vaccinations.
* **Behavioral Issues or Concerns**: Any aggression, anxiety, or other behavioral issues.
* **Good with Other Animals**: Whether the animal is good with other animals.
* **Good with Children**: Whether the animal is good with children.
* **Reason for Surrender**: The main reason for surrendering the animal (e.g., moving, financial hardship, allergies, etc.).
* **Additional Details**: More information about the reason for surrender.
* **Length of Time Owned**: How long the owner has had the animal.
* **Number of Previous Homes**: How many owners before this.
* **Special Instructions or Notes**: Any additional information that the shelter should know.
* **I Confirm All Information is Accurate**: A confirmation that all provided information is accurate.
