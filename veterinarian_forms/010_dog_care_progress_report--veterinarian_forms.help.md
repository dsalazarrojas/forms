# dog_care_progress_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dog care progress report is used to collect information about a dog's diet and water intake, which is crucial for monitoring their progress and making informed decisions about their care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information about the dog ready to be recorded.
2. Select the pet sitter responsible for the dog's care.
3. Enter the dog's details, including any relevant information about their diet and water intake.
4. Choose the dog's food type from the options provided (Kibble, Raw, or Homemade).
5. Select the dog's water source from the options provided (Tap or Well).
6. Provide any additional comments or observations about the dog's veterinary care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Sitter** (Pet Sitter, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or organization responsible for the dog's care.
* **dog_details** (dog_details, number, optional): Enter any relevant details about the dog, such as their age, weight, or breed.
* **dog_food** (dog_food, select_one, required): Choose the type of food the dog is consuming (Kibble, Raw, or Homemade). This is a critical field that helps monitor the dog's diet.
* **dog_water** (dog_water, select_multiple, optional): Select the source of the dog's water (Tap or Well). This field helps ensure the dog is getting access to safe and clean water.
* **Vet Comments** (Vet Comments, note, optional): Enter any comments or observations about the dog's veterinary care, including any notable events or concerns.
