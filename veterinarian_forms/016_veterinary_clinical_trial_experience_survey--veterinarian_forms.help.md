# Veterinary Clinical Trial Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Clinical Trial Experience Survey is a form designed to collect information from pet owners who have participated in a clinical trial related to veterinary care. This information will be used to evaluate the effectiveness and safety of new treatments, medications, or procedures for pets.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Start by reviewing the introduction page. This page provides background information about the clinical trial you participated in.
* Fill out the Owner Info form with your information. This includes your name, phone number, and email address.
* Provide details about your pet's information, such as breed and age.
* Share your experiences with the clinical trial, including any questions or concerns you had during the trial.
* Select any additional questions or concerns you experienced during the trial from the drop-down menu.
* Finally, provide any additional comments or observations about your experience with the clinical trial.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Introduction
* **Introduction**: This section provides background information about the clinical trial you participated in. Please review this page carefully before proceeding.

### 2. Owner Info
* **Owner's Name**: This is your name. Please enter your name as it appears on your driver's license or passport.
* **Phone Number**: This is your phone number. Please enter your phone number without any special characters.
* **Email Address**: This is your email address. Please enter your email address without any special characters.

### 3. Pet Info
* **Pet's Name**: This is the name of your pet. Please enter your pet's name as it appears on their medical records.
* **Breed**: This is your pet's breed. Please select from the list of common dog or cat breeds.
* **Age**: This is your pet's age. Please enter your pet's age in years.

### 4. Trial Info
* **Clinical Trial ID**: This is the unique identifier for the clinical trial you participated in. Please enter the ID number as provided by the clinical trial team.

### 5. Questions
* **Select Questions**: Select any questions or concerns you experienced during the clinical trial from the drop-down menu. You can select multiple options.

### 6. Communication
* **Additional Comments**: This is the space for any additional comments or observations about your experience with the clinical trial. Please provide any relevant information that may help us understand your experience.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and honest responses to each question.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* Thank you for participating in this clinical trial and for taking the time to complete this survey!
