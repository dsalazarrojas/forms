# Veterinary Public Safety Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Public Safety Training Registration form is designed to collect registration information from participants for veterinary public safety training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the registration form with your details, including participant information.
2. Select your training session and schedule date and time.
3. Provide experience details and select the animal control units and veterinary clinics you are associated with.
4. Review your information before submitting.
