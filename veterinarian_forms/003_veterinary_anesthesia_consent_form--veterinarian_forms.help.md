# Veterinary Anesthesia Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Anesthesia Consent Form is used to gather information from the owner of an animal that is undergoing anesthesia. This form ensures that the owner and the veterinarian are aware of the treatment options and procedures that will be performed on the animal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Owner Information**: Enter your name and contact information.
2. **Animal Information**: Enter the animal's name and breed.
3. **Anesthesia Information**: Describe the anesthesia procedure that will be performed.
4. **Consent**: Check the box indicating that you understand and agree to the treatment options and procedures.
5. **Veterinarian Signature**: Select the veterinarian performing the procedure.
6. **Owner Signature**: Choose "Owner" to indicate that you are signing on behalf of the owner.
7. **Date**: Enter the date of the procedure.
8. **Time**: Enter the time of the procedure.
9. **Treatments Options**: Select the procedure(s) to be performed.
10. **Note**: Add any additional notes about the procedure.
11. **Veterinarian Note**: Add any additional notes from the veterinarian.
12. **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
13. **Email**: Enter your email address.
14. **Owner Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
15. **Owner Email**: Enter your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Owner Information**: This field is used to gather your name and contact information. Please enter your full name, address, and phone number.
* **Animal Information**: Enter the animal's name and breed to ensure that the veterinarian has accurate information about the animal.
* **Anesthesia Information**: Describe the anesthesia procedure that will be performed, including any risks or complications.
* **Consent**: Check the box indicating that you understand and agree to the treatment options and procedures. This is a critical field that ensures you are aware of the risks and benefits of the procedure.
* **Veterinarian Signature**: Select the veterinarian performing the procedure. This ensures that the veterinarian is aware of your consent and will perform the procedure as agreed upon.
* **Owner Signature**: Choose "Owner" to indicate that you are signing on behalf of the owner.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the procedure to ensure that the record is accurate.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the procedure to ensure that the record is accurate.
* **Treatments Options**: Select the procedure(s) to be performed. This ensures that the veterinarian is aware of the treatment options and procedures you have agreed upon.
* **Note**: Add any additional notes about the procedure. This is an optional field for any additional information.
* **Veterinarian Note**: Add any additional notes from the veterinarian. This is an optional field for any additional information.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number in case the veterinarian needs to reach you.
* **Email**: Enter your email address in case the veterinarian needs to reach you.
* **Owner Phone**: Enter your contact phone number in case the veterinarian needs to reach you.
* **Owner Email**: Enter your email address in case the veterinarian needs to reach you.

**Tips**

* Ensure that you have read and understood the anesthesia procedure before signing this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask the veterinarian before signing this form.
