# Pet Hosting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Hosting Form is used to collect information about a pet's host family or individual.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the pet's name in the "pet" field.
2. Enter the host's name in the "host" field.
3. Enter the host's address in the "host_address" field.
4. Enter the host's city in the "host_city" field.
5. Select the host's state from the options provided in the "host_state" field.
6. Indicate whether the host has experience with pets in the "host_experience" field.
7. Enter the host's availability for hosting the pet in the "host_availability" field (date).
8. Enter a photo of the pet (if applicable) in the "pet_photo" field.
9. Enter the host's phone number in the "host_phone" field.
10. Enter the host's email address in the "host_email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **pet** (text): Enter the pet's name.
* **host** (text): Enter the host's name.
* **host_address** (text): Enter the host's address.
* **host_city** (text): Enter the host's city.
* **host_state** (select_one): Select the host's state from the provided options.
* **host_experience** (text): Indicate whether the host has experience with pets.
* **host_availability** (date): Enter the host's availability for hosting the pet.
* **pet_photo** (text): Enter a photo of the pet (if applicable).
* **host_phone** (text): Enter the host's phone number.
* **host_email** (email): Enter the host's email address.
