# veterinary_continuing_education_course_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and ratings from veterinarians or veterinary professionals who have attended continuing education courses. Your input will help us understand the quality and effectiveness of our courses, allowing us to continuously improve and better serve our attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer each question thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. If you have attended multiple courses, complete this form for each course separately.
3. Select the speaker type from the options provided.
4. Use the space provided for additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Welcome to the survey**: This is a text field where you can provide a brief introduction or context about your experience with the course.
* **Course Title**: This field is for you to enter the title of the course you attended.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the course took place.
* **Date**: Enter the date you attended the course. 
* **Time**: Enter the time of the course. 
* **Speaker**: Choose from the options provided to indicate who the speaker was. If you select "Other", you can enter the name of the speaker in the field below.
* **Other Speaker**: If you selected "Other" for the speaker, provide their name here.
* **Topic**: Enter the topic of the course you attended.
* **Rating**: Rate the course using the options provided (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the course you attended.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and thoroughly to help us improve our courses.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
