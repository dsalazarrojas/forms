# Pet Prescription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document pet medical information and provide instructions for follow-up care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your veterinary information, pet details, and prescription instructions.
2. Use the drop-down menu to select the pet type and health status.
3. Select the veterinary clinic where the follow-up appointment will be held.
4. Enter the date and time for the follow-up appointment.
5. Add any additional comments or medical history for the pet.
6. Select the follow-up status as completed, pending, or cancelled.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Name** (pet_name): Enter the name of the pet.
* **Pet Owner Name** (pet_owner_name): Enter the name of the pet's owner.
* **Pet Type** (pet_type): Select the type of pet (Dog, Cat, or Other).
* **Medication Name** (medication_name): Enter the name of any medication prescribed for the pet.
* **Prescription Notes** (prescription_notes): Add any special instructions or notes for the prescription.
* **Pet Health** (pet_health): Select the health status of the pet (Healthy, Sick, or Critical).
* **Veterinarian Signature** (veterinarian_signature): Enter your signature as the attending veterinarian.
* **Veterinary Clinic** (veterinary_clinic): Select the clinic where the follow-up appointment will be held.
* **Date** (date): Enter the date for the follow-up appointment.
* **Time** (time): Enter the time for the follow-up appointment.
* **Phone** (phone): Enter the phone number of the pet owner or contact person.
* **Email** (email): Enter the email address of the pet owner or contact person.
* **Medical History** (medical_history): Enter any relevant medical history for the pet.
* **Prescription Request** (prescription_request): Select the type of prescription required (Prescription 1, Prescription 2, or Prescription 3).
* **Veterinarian Notes** (veterinarian_notes): Add any notes for the veterinarian.
* **Additional Comments** (additional_comments): Add any additional comments or information.
* **Follow-up Instructions** (follow_up_instructions): Enter any follow-up instructions for the pet owner.
* **Follow-up Date** (follow_up_date): Enter the date for the follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-up Time** (follow_up_time): Enter the time for the follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-up Phone** (follow_up_phone): Enter the phone number of the contact person for the follow-up.
* **Follow-up Email** (follow_up_email): Enter the email address of the contact person for the follow-up.
* **Follow-up Status** (follow_up_status): Select the follow-up status (Completed, Pending, or Cancelled).
* **Follow-up Note** (follow_up_note): Add any follow-up notes.
* **Review Date** (review_date): Enter the date for the review of the follow-up appointment.

Note: This guide is for informational purposes only and is not intended to be a substitute for professional medical advice.
