# Vet Clinic Patient Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect essential information from pet owners during a veterinary visit. It includes fields for pet name, type, owner's name, phone number, pet breed, appointment date, visit time, medical notes, owner's email, and owner's address. This form helps the vet clinic staff to gather necessary details to provide efficient and effective care to the pets.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the pet's name in the "Pet Name" field.
* Select the pet's type from the options provided in the "Pet Type" field.
* Enter the owner's name in the "Owner Name" field.
* Enter the owner's phone number in the "Owner Phone" field.
* Select the pet's breed from the options provided in the "Pet Breed" field.
* Choose the appointment date in the "Appointment Date" field using the date picker tool.
* Choose the visit time in the "Visit Time" field using the time picker tool.
* Enter any relevant medical notes in the "Medical Notes" field.
* Enter the owner's email address in the "Owner Email" field.
* Enter the owner's address in the "Owner Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Name (text)**: Enter the name of the pet that is being checked in.
* **Pet Type (select_one)**: Select the type of pet (e.g., cat, dog, bird) from the options provided.
* **Owner Name (text)**: Enter the name of the pet's owner.
* **Owner Phone (text)**: Enter the owner's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Pet Breed (select_multiple)**: Select all applicable breeds for the pet (e.g., mixed, purebred).
* **Appointment Date (date)**: Choose the date for the appointment using the calendar tool.
* **Visit Time (time)**: Choose the time for the visit using the clock tool.
* **Medical Notes (note)**: Enter any relevant medical history or notes about the pet.
* **Owner Email (email)**: Enter the owner's email address for communication purposes.
* **Owner Address (text)**: Enter the owner's address for contact purposes.
