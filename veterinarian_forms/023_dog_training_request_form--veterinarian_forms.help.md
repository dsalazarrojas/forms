# Dog Training Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your dog's training request, including your contact information, dog details, and any additional comments or requests you may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the User Information section with your details.
2. Provide information about your dog in the Dog Information section.
3. Select the desired appointment date in the Appointment Date field.
4. Choose the type of training you require in the Training Type field.
5. Select the location where you would like the training to take place in the Training Location field.
6. Add any additional comments or requests in the Additional Comments field.
7. Enter your phone number in the User Phone field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required: false): This section is where you will enter your personal details, including your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Dog Information** (`dog_info`, text, required: false): In this section, please provide details about your dog, such as its name, age, breed, and any medical conditions it may have.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required: false): Select the date on which you would like to schedule the training appointment. Please ensure that this date is in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Training Type** (`training_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the type of training you require for your dog. Please select all the types that apply from the provided options, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, select_one, required: false): Select the location where you would like the training to take place. Please choose from the provided options, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required: false): If you have any additional comments or requests, please enter them here.
* **User Phone** (`user_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you with any further information or updates about your training appointment.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
* If you have any specific requirements or requests, please add them in the Additional Comments field.
