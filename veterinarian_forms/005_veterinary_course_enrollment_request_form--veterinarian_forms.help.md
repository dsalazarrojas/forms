# veterinary_course_enrollment_request_form--veterinarian_forms - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request veterinary courses and related information. The form is designed to help veterinarians provide their course interest, background details, contact information, and other relevant details for course enrollment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select your course interest by choosing from the options provided in the "What course interest" field.
* Provide any relevant background details in the "Background Details" field.
* Enter your contact information, including your name, email, phone number, and clinic address in the "Contact Information" field.
* Enter your school name and address in the "School Name" and "School Address" fields respectively.
* Choose the start and end dates for the course in the "Course Start Date" and "Course End Date" fields.
* Select the assigned tool for the course in the "Assigned Tool" field.
* Choose the course level and type in the "Course Level" and "Course Type" fields respectively.
* Select the course language in the "Course Language" field.
* Choose the payment option for the course in the "Payment Option" field.
* Add any additional notes about the course in the "Notes" field.
* Select who submitted the form in the "Submitted By" field.
* Enter the date the form was submitted in the "Submitted Date" field.
* Select who updated the form in the "Updated By" field.
* Enter the date the form was updated in the "Updated Date" field.
* Select who created the form in the "Created By" field.
* Enter the date the form was created in the "Created Date" field.
