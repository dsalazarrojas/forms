# questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "questionnaire" form is designed to gather information about a new puppy and its owner for veterinary purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

*   This form is divided into two main sections: New Puppy Information and Owner Information.
*   Please fill in the form fields according to the following steps:

    1.  **New Puppy Information:**
        Fill in the fields for New Puppy Owner, New Puppy Age, New Puppy Breed, New Puppy Color, New Puppy Age Group, and New Puppy Health Issues.
        For New Puppy Age, select the correct age group for the puppy.
        For New Puppy Breed and New Puppy Color, select the correct options from the list.
        For New Puppy Health Issues, select all applicable health issues the puppy may have.
*   **Owner Information:**
        Fill in the fields for Owner Name, Contact Number, Microchip Number, Microchip Type, Microchip Status, Vet Name, and Vet's License Number.
        If you have microchipped your puppy, enter the microchip number, microchip type, and microchip status accordingly.
        For Vet Name and Vet's License Number, enter the name and license number of the veterinarian who treated or cared for your puppy.
*   Once you have filled in all the required fields, click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **New Puppy Owner**: Enter the owner's name as it appears on the microchip.
*   **Owner Name**: Enter the owner's name as it appears on the microchip.
*   **Contact Number**: Enter the owner's phone number for easy contact.
*   **New Puppy Age**: Enter the age of the new puppy.
*   **New Puppy Age Group**: Select the correct age group for the new puppy.
*   **New Puppy Breed**: Select the breed of the new puppy.
*   **New Puppy Color**: Select the color of the new puppy.
*   **New Puppy Health Issues**: Select all health issues the new puppy may have.
*   **Microchip Number**: Enter the microchip number of the new puppy.
*   **Microchip Type**: Select the type of microchip used for the new puppy.
*   **Microchip Status**: Select the status of the microchip.
*   **Vet Name**: Enter the name of the veterinarian who treated or cared for the new puppy.
*   **Vet Prophets License Number**: Enter the license number of the veterinarian.
*   **Owner Signature**: Sign the form to confirm completion.

## Tips

*   Ensure that you fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please ask your veterinarian for guidance.
*   The information provided will be used to update the veterinary records of the new puppy.
*   This form is confidential and will not be shared with any third party.
