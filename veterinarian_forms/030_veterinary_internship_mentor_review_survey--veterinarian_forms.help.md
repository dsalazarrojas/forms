# Veterinary Internship Mentor Review Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey for veterinary professionals to provide feedback on their experiences with veterinary interns. We want to collect as much information as possible to improve the internship program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Make sure you are the supervisor of a veterinary intern.
2. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly to provide detailed feedback.
3. Fill in all the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`internship_mentors_first_name`, text, required):
	+ Enter the first name of the intern who worked with you.
* **Last Name** (`internship_mentors_last_name`, text, required):
	+ Enter the last name of the intern who worked with you.
* **First Name of Internship Supervisor** (`internship_supervisor_first_name`, text, optional):
	+ If you are not the supervisor, you can skip this field.
* **Last Name of Internship Supervisor** (`internship_supervisor_last_name`, text, optional):
	+ If you are not the supervisor, you can skip this field.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Comments** (`feedback_comments`, text, optional):
	+ Provide any additional comments or feedback about the intern.
* **Feedback Rating** (`feedback_rating`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Rate the intern's performance as "Excellent", "Good", or "Needs Improvement".
* **Internship Start Date** (`internship_start_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the start date of the internship.
* **Internship End Date** (`internship_end_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the end date of the internship.
* **Total Hours Contributed** (`total_hours_contributed`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the total hours the intern contributed.
* **Number of Days Attended** (`number_of_days_attended`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the number of days the intern attended.
* **Comments from Supervisor** (`internship_supervisor_comments`, text, optional):
	+ Provide any comments from the supervisor.
* **Email Sent to** (`email_sent_to`, email, optional):
	+ Enter the email address to which emails were sent.
* **Email CC** (`email_cc`, email, optional):
	+ Enter the email addresses to which emails were CC'd.
* **Comments from Supervisor** (`supervisor_comments`, text, optional):
	+ Provide any comments from the supervisor.
* **Rating Comments** (`rating_comments`, text, optional):
	+ Provide any additional comments about the rating.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, optional):
	+ Rate the intern's performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Internship Duration** (`internship_duration`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the duration of the internship.
* **Materials Supplied by Supervisor** (`supervisor_supplied_materials`, text, optional):
	+ Describe the materials you provided for the intern.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, text, optional):
	+ Describe the rating scale used.
* **Feedback Frequency** (`feedback_frequency`, text, optional):
	+ Describe the frequency of feedback provided.
* **Name of Supervisor** (`supervisor_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the supervisor.
* **Rating Scale 5** (`rating_scale_5`, text, optional):
	+ Describe the rating scale used for rating 5.
* **Rating Scale 6** (`rating_scale_6`, text, optional):
	+ Describe the rating scale used for rating 6.
