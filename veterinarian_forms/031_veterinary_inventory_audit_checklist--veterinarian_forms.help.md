# Veterinary Inventory Audit Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Inventory Audit Checklist is a tool designed to help veterinary clinics and hospitals maintain accurate and up-to-date records of their equipment, stock, and safety standards. This form is intended to be used by veterinarians and staff members to ensure that the necessary checks are performed regularly to maintain the clinic or hospital's equipment, stock, and safety standards.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page of the form carefully and answer each question according to your observations and experiences.
2. Be as accurate as possible when filling out each page. If you are unsure about any information, consult with your colleagues or supervisors.
3. For each field, provide as much detail as possible. If a field has multiple options, select the one that best describes your observation or action.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Clinic Stock** (`clinic_stock`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the current stock level of the clinic or hospital.
* **Equipment Checks** (`equipment_checks`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check the equipment and select 'Yes' if it is functioning correctly, 'No' if it is not. This will help identify any equipment that needs repair or replacement.
* **Safety Observations** (`safety_observations`, `text`, required/optional): Record any safety observations or concerns you have during the audit.
* **Stock Levels** (`stock_levels`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the current stock levels for each item, if applicable.
* **Equipment Condition** (`equipment_condition`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the condition of the equipment you have checked. Options include: Good, Fair, Poor, Needs Repair, Needs Replace.
* **Equipment Serial Numbers** (`equipment_serial_numbers`, `text`, required/optional): Record the serial numbers of any equipment that requires them.
* **Safety Standards** (`safety_standards`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check the safety standards and select 'Yes' if they are met, 'No' if they are not.
* **Stock Location** (`stock_location`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the location where the stock is stored.
* **Last Check** (`last_check`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the last check performed.
* **Last Checked By** (`last_checked_by`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the person who performed the last check.
* **Next Check** (`next_check`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date for the next scheduled check.
* **Checked By** (`checked_by`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the person performing the current check.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required/optional): Record any additional notes or recommendations.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address, if you wish to receive notifications or updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number, if you wish to receive notifications or updates.
* **Stock Status** (`stock_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the current stock status. Options include: Active, Inactive.

Note: All fields with "required/optional" indicate the field type and its required status.
