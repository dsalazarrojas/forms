# Sick Pet Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sick Pet Appointment Form is a user-facing form designed to collect information for scheduling a sick pet appointment at a veterinary clinic. This form is intended for pet owners to provide essential details about their pet's condition, appointment preferences, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Sick Pet Appointment Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the pet's identification number (if available).
2. Provide your name and contact information as the pet owner.
3. Enter any relevant information about the pet's condition, such as medical issues or allergies.
4. Select the appointment date and time that works best for you.
5. Choose the veterinarian you would like to see for the appointment.
6. Add any additional notes about the appointment.
7. Select the pet's condition(s) that apply to their current situation.
8. Provide your contact information as the form submitter.
9. Select who submitted the form (Form Submitter or Owner).
10. Enter your name, organization, and phone number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet ID** (`pet id`, Number, Required: false): Enter the pet's identification number, if available. This helps the clinic easily identify the pet and ensure accurate records.
* **Owner Name** (`owner name`, Text, Required: false): Provide your name as the pet owner. This ensures that you receive any necessary communications and updates about the appointment.
* **Pet Info** (`pet info`, Text, Required: false): Enter any relevant medical information about your pet, such as vaccination history, feeding habits, or previous medical conditions.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment date`, Date, Required: false): Select the date for the appointment. Choose a date that works best for you and your pet.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment time`, Time, Required: false): Choose the time slot for the appointment. Be mindful of the clinic's operating hours and availability.
* **Veterinarian** (`veterinarian`, Select One, Required: false): Select the veterinarian you would like to see for the appointment. This ensures that you receive consistent and personalized care.
* **Appointment Note** (`appointment note`, Note, Required: false): Add any additional comments or concerns you have about the appointment. This helps the veterinarian understand your pet's needs better.
* **Pet Condition** (`pet condition`, Select Multiple, Required: false): Select the condition(s) that apply to your pet's current situation. This helps the veterinarian understand the pet's needs and provide the best possible care.
* **Owner Contact** (`owner contact`, Text, Required: false): Enter your contact information, such as phone number or email address, so the clinic can easily reach you for updates.
* **Form Submitter** (`form submitted`, Select One, Required: false): Select who submitted the form (Form Submitter or Owner). This helps the clinic track who initiated the appointment request.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter name`, Text, Required: false): Enter your name as the person or organization submitting the form.
* **Submitter Organization** (`submitter organization`, Text, Required: false): Enter your organization's name or affiliation, if applicable.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter phone`, Text, Required: false): Enter your phone number for further communication.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to help the veterinarian provide the best possible care for your pet.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the clinic for assistance.
* The clinic will contact you to confirm the appointment and provide further instructions.
