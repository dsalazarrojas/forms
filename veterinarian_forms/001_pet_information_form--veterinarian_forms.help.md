# Pet Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pet Information Form is designed to collect essential details about a pet, ensuring accurate and efficient care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the pet's information, including its name, owner's contact details (name and phone number), and pet's breed, age, weight, and any medical conditions.
2. Select the pet's breed from the available options (Poodle, Golden Retriever, Labrador, Bulldog, or Cat).
3. Enter the pet's age and weight in their respective fields.
4. Provide any relevant medical information in the medical conditions field.
5. Add any additional medical notes in the medical notes field.
6. Click submit to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Information** (pet_info, text, optional): Enter the pet's identification or registration number, if applicable, or a brief description to help identify the pet.
* **Pet Name** (pet_name, text, optional): Enter the pet's name, including its nickname or alias if applicable.
* **Owner Name** (owner_name, text, optional): Enter the owner's name, which can be the primary caregiver or the person responsible for the pet's care.
* **Owner Phone** (owner_phone, text, optional): Enter the owner's contact phone number to ensure efficient communication.
* **Pet Breed** (pet_breed, select_one, optional): Choose the pet's breed from the available options (Poodle, Golden Retriever, Labrador, Bulldog, or Cat).
* **Pet Age** (pet_age, number, optional): Enter the pet's age in years.
* **Pet Weight** (pet_weight, number, optional): Enter the pet's weight in kilograms (kg).
* **Medical Conditions** (medical_conditions, text, optional): List any known medical conditions or health issues related to the pet.
* **Medical Notes** (medical_notes, note, optional): Add any additional medical information or treatment plans. 

## Tips
* Ensure the pet's information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Use the available breed options only to select the correct breed.
* If the pet has no medical conditions, leave the field blank.
* Use the medical notes field for additional information or follow-up notes.
