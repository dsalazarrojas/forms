# Veterinary Clinical Trial Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The veterinary clinical trial application form is a tool used to gather information from veterinarians and veterinary clinics to participate in clinical trials.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Application Form** section with a brief description of your practice or clinic.
2. Provide details about the **Animal Details** in the next section, including species, breed, age, weight, and other relevant information.
3. In the **Owner Contact** section, fill out the owner's contact name, phone number, and email address.
4. Specify the **Owner Contact Relationship** (e.g., Client, Family Member, Friend).
5. Add any additional **Owner Contact Notes** if necessary.
6. Provide the **Study Information** section with the study ID, study name, start date, end date, and study status.
7. Add any additional **Study Notes** if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form**: A brief description of your practice or clinic.
* **Animal Details**: Provide details about the animal, including species, breed, age, weight, and other relevant information.
* **Animal Species**: Select the species of the animal (e.g., Cat, Dog, Other).
* **Animal Breed**: The breed of the animal (if applicable).
* **Animal Age**: The age of the animal.
* **Animal Weight**: The weight of the animal.
* **Animal Gender**: Select the gender of the animal (Male, Female, Unknown).
* **Medical Condition**: A description of any medical conditions the animal has.
* **Medical Condition Notes**: Additional notes about the animal's medical condition.
* **Study ID**: A unique ID for the study.
* **Study Name**: The name of the study.
* **Study Start Date**: The start date of the study.
* **Study End Date**: The end date of the study.
* **Study Status**: The status of the study (Active, Completed, Inactive).
* **Study Notes**: Additional notes about the study.
* **Owner Contact Name**: The name of the owner or contact person.
* **Owner Contact Phone**: The phone number of the owner or contact person.
* **Owner Contact Email**: The email address of the owner or contact person.
* **Owner Contact Relationship**: Specify the relationship between the owner and the contact person (e.g., Client, Family Member, Friend).
* **Owner Contact Notes**: Additional notes about the owner or contact person.
* **Consent**: A text field for any additional consent or agreements.
* **Email**: A field for the contact person's email address.
* **Phone**: A field for the contact person's phone number.
* **Name**: A field for the contact person's name.
* **Consent**: A field for any additional consent or agreements.
* **Medical History**: A field for any additional medical history of the animal.

This form is for internal use only and should be used to avoid duplication of effort. Please ensure you have the necessary permissions and authorization before completing this form.
