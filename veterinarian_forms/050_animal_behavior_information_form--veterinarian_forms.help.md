# Animal Behavior Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an animal's behavior for diagnosis, analysis, or behavioral modification purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with the required information, following the instructions for each section.
2. For each field, please provide the most accurate and detailed information possible.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the field's explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Full Name (required)**: Please enter the full name of the animal's owner.
* **Animal Name (required)**: Enter the name of the animal being reported.
* **Species (required)**: Select the species of the animal (Canine, Feline, or Other).
* **What is your primary behavior concern? (required)**: Briefly describe the main issue you are experiencing with the animal's behavior.
* **Onset of Behavior (required)**: Select the onset of the behavior (Sudden, Gradual, or Always present).
* **Observed Behaviors (optional)**: Select all behaviors you have observed, such as Aggression towards people, Aggression towards animals, Separation Anxiety, Excessive Barking / Meowing, Destructive Chewing, or Inappropriate Elimination.
* **Average Intensity of Behavior (required)**: Select the intensity of the behavior (Mild, Moderate, or Severe).
* **Known Triggers for This Behavior (optional)**: List any known triggers or factors that contribute to the animal's behavior, such as loud noises, strangers, or other animals.
* **How do you currently respond to this behavior? (optional)**: Describe how you currently address the animal's behavior.
* **Previous Training Experience (required)**: Select your previous experience with training the animal (Professional Trainer, Home Training, or No Training).
* **What are your primary goals for behavior modification? (required)**: Describe your main goals for modifying the animal's behavior.
* **Form Completion Date (required)**: Enter the date you are completing this form.

Note: Please fill out the form with the most accurate and detailed information possible to ensure effective analysis and behavior modification.
