# Dog Registration Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dog Registration Form Template is designed to collect information about a dog's registration, including its owner's contact details, vet clinic visit, and emergency contact.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Camper Information in the first field. This is a general field to capture any relevant information about the dog.
2. Provide the Camper Name, which is the name of the dog.
3. Enter the Camper Email, which is the email address of the dog's owner or contact person.
4. Fill in the Owner Information field with any relevant details about the owner.
5. Enter the Owner Name, which is the name of the dog's owner.
6. Enter the Owner Email, which is the email address of the dog's owner.
7. In the Vet Records field, enter any relevant medical history or notes about the dog's visit.
8. Provide the Vet Clinic name and location.
9. Enter the Date of Visit, which is the date the dog visited the vet clinic.
10. If an emergency contact is required, enter their name and email address.
11. Enter the name and email of the person to contact in case of an emergency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Camper Information** (`campers_information`, `text`, optional): Enter any general information about the dog.
* **Camper Name** (`camper_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the dog.
* **Camper Email** (`camper_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the dog's owner or contact person.
* **Owner Information** (`owner_information`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant details about the dog's owner.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the dog's owner.
* **Owner Email** (`owner_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the dog's owner.
* **Vet Records** (`vet_records`, `text`, optional): Enter any medical history or notes about the dog's visit.
* **Vet Clinic** (`vet_clinic`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and location of the vet clinic.
* **Date of Visit** (`vet_date_of_visit`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the dog visited the vet clinic.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and email of the emergency contact person.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
