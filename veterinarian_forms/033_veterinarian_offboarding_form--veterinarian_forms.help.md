# Veterinarian Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinarian Offboarding Form is a crucial document that helps to collect vital information about a pet's medical history, client feedback, and clinic information. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that the pet receives the best care possible.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each section carefully and fill in the required information accurately.
- Answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
- Make sure to sign the form to confirm that you have provided the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Medical History** (`pet_medical_history`): Provide a brief overview of the pet's medical history, including any illnesses, allergies, or previous treatments.
* **Client Feedback** (`client_feedback`): Share your feedback about your interaction with the client, including any concerns or suggestions you may have.
* **Pet Name** (`pet_name`): Enter the pet's full name.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`): Indicate if the pet has any medical conditions such as diabetes, arthritis, or other chronic conditions.
* **Medication List** (`medication_list`): List any medications the pet is currently taking, including dosage and frequency.
* **Medical Tests** (`medical_tests`): Indicate if the pet has undergone any medical tests such as blood work, X-rays, or other diagnostic procedures.
* **Medical Procedure List** (`medical_procedure_list`): List any medical procedures the pet has undergone, including surgeries or other treatments.
* **Reason for Offboarding** (`reason_for_offboarding`): Describe the reason for offboarding the pet, if necessary.
* **Veterinary Clinic Information** (`veterinary_clinic_info`): Provide any relevant information about the pet's current veterinary clinic, such as their contact information or the name of the attending veterinarian.
* **Veterinarian Signature** (`veterinarian_signature`): Sign the form to confirm that you have provided the required information accurately and truthfully.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
- If you are unsure about any information, ask your veterinarian for guidance.
- Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
