# Veterinary Marketing Outreach Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from veterinarians about their marketing outreach and communication strategies. It will help us understand how they prefer to be contacted and what types of messages they find most effective.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select all the options that apply to you.
2. Choose the options that best describe your current practices or preferences.
3. Be honest and accurate in your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Marketing Channels** (`Marketing Channels`, select_multiple): Select all the channels through which you prefer to receive marketing messages, such as Email, Social Media, Print, and Online.
* **Campaign Awareness** (`Campaign Awareness`, number): Rate how aware you are of our marketing campaigns, from 0 (not at all) to 5 (very much).
* **Outreach Effectiveness** (`Outreach Effectiveness`, select_one): Rate how effective you find our outreach efforts, from Very Effective to Not Effective.
* **Clinic Participation** (`Clinic Participation`, select_multiple): Select all the areas where your clinic participates in marketing outreach, such as True and False.
* **Campaign Topics** (`Campaign Topics`, select_multiple): Select all the topics that you find most relevant to your practice, such as Animal Health, Product, and Service.
* **Target Audience** (`Target Audience`, select_multiple): Select all the target audiences that you prefer to reach, such as Pet Owners, Pet Care Professionals, and Veterinarians.
* **Marketing Channels Awareness** (`Marketing Channels Awareness`, number): Rate how aware you are of the marketing channels we use, from 0 (not at all) to 5 (very much).
* **Campaign Frequency** (`Campaign Frequency`, select_one): Rate how frequently you prefer to receive campaign messages, from Daily to Never.
* **Communication Methods** (`Communication Methods`, select_multiple): Select all the communication methods you prefer to use, such as Email, Phone, Social Media, and In-Person.
* **Campaign Messages** (`Campaign Messages`, select_multiple): Select all the types of messages you find most valuable, such as Promotional, Educational, and Both.
* **Campaign Message Channels** (`Campaign Message Channels`, select_multiple): Select all the channels through which you prefer to receive campaign messages, such as Email, Social Media, and Online Ads.
* **Campaign Message Topics** (`Campaign Message Topics`, select_multiple): Select all the topics that you find most relevant, such as Animal Health, Product, and Service.
* **Target Audience Awareness** (`Target Audience Awareness`, number): Rate how aware you are of our target audience, from 0 (not at all) to 5 (very much).
* **Communication Methods Awareness** (`Communication Methods Awareness`, number): Rate how aware you are of our communication methods, from 0 (not at all) to 5 (very much).
* **Message Content** (`Message Content`, select_multiple): Select all the types of content you find most valuable, such as Promotional, Educational, and Both.
* **Message Channels** (`Message Channels`, select_multiple): Select all the channels through which you prefer to receive messages, such as Email, Social Media, Online Ads, and In-Person.
* **Message Topics** (`Message Topics`, select_multiple): Select all the topics that you find most relevant, such as Animal Health, Product, and Service.
* **Message Frequency** (`Message Frequency`, number): Rate how frequently you prefer to receive messages, from 0 (not at all) to 5 (very much).
* **Follow-ups** (`Follow-ups`, select_one): Rate how frequently you prefer to receive follow-ups, from Daily to Never.
* **New Content** (`New Content`, select_multiple): Select all the frequencies at which you prefer to receive new content, such as Regularly, Occasionally, and Rarely.
* **Content Format** (`Content Format`, select_multiple): Select all the formats in which you prefer to receive content, such as Text, Image, and Video.
* **Content Topics** (`Content Topics`, select_multiple): Select all the topics that you find most relevant, such as Animal Health, Product, and Service.
* **Content Topics Awareness** (`Content Topics Awareness`, number): Rate how aware you are of our content topics, from 0 (not at all) to 5 (very much).

* **Follow-ups Frequency** (`Follow-ups Frequency`, number): Rate how frequently you prefer to receive follow-ups, from 0 (not at all) to 5 (very much).

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your answers.
