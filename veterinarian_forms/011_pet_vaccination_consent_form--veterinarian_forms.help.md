# Pet Vaccination Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain the pet owner's consent for vaccination. 
It is a critical step in ensuring the health and well-being of their pet. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and accurately. 
2. Provide detailed and clear answers in the text fields. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduce yourself** (`introduction`, `note`): This is where you can briefly introduce yourself and your pet.
* **Benefits** (`benefits`, `text`): Describe the benefits of vaccinating your pet. 
* **Risks** (`risks`, `text`): Explain the risks associated with not vaccinating your pet. 
* **Importance** (`importance`, `text`): Discuss the importance of vaccination for your pet's health. 
* **Consent to vaccination** (`consent`, `select_one`): Choose "Consent to vaccination" if you agree to vaccinate your pet, or "Do not consent to vaccination" if you do not. 
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`): Sign your name as proof of your consent. 
* **Date** (`date`, `date`): Enter the date of completion. 
* **Veterinary information** (`veterinary_information`, `text`): Provide details about your pet's current veterinary care, such as their current vet and contact information. 
* **Contact information** (`contact_information`, `text`): Enter your contact information so we can follow up with you about your pet's vaccination status.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately. 
* Make sure to provide detailed information for each field. 
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask a veterinarian or a medical professional for advice.
