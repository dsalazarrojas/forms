# Pet Confidence And Fitness Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the confidence and fitness level of a pet, which is essential for their well-being and performance in training. The form helps veterinarians and trainers evaluate a pet's behavior, fitness, and overall health to make informed decisions about their care and training.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the required information for the pet, including their age, type, and ID.
2.  Assess the pet's behavior, fitness, and health level using the provided fields.
3.  Select the training status of the pet, indicating whether they are not started, in progress, or have completed training.
4.  Enter any additional training notes if necessary.
5.  Choose a date for the assessment.
6.  Enter a fitness level score for the pet.
7.  Review and submit the form to finalize the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Behavior Score** (`behavior_score`, `number`, `required`: true): Enter a numerical score to assess the pet's behavior, with higher scores indicating better behavior.
* **Activity Level** (`activity_level`, `text`, `required`: false): Select a text field to describe the pet's current activity level, such as "low", "moderate", "high".
* **Confidence Level** (`confidence_level`, `text`, `required`: false): Select a text field to describe the pet's confidence level, such as "high", "moderate", "low".
* **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, `text`, `required`: true): Enter the type of pet (e.g., dog, cat, etc.).
* **Pet Age** (`pet_age`, `number`, `required`: true): Enter the age of the pet in years.
* **Pet Health** (`pet_health`, `text`, `required`: false): Enter a text description of the pet's health status.
* **Pet Weight** (`pet_weight`, `text`, `required`: false): Enter the weight of the pet.
* **Trainer ID** (`trainer_id`, `number`, `required`: true): Enter the ID of the trainer who assessed the pet.
* **Pet ID** (`pet_id`, `number`, `required`: true): Enter the ID of the pet being assessed.
* **Pet Breed** (`pet_breed`, `text`, `required`: false): Enter the breed of the pet (e.g., Labrador, Persian, etc.).
* **Training Status** (`training_status`, `select_one`, `required`: true): Select the training status of the pet, which can be "Not Started", "In Progress", or "Complete".
* **Training Notes** (`training_notes`, `text`, `required`: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about the pet's training status.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, `date`, `required`: true): Choose the date of the assessment.
* **Fitness Level** (`fitness_level`, `number`, `required`: true): Enter a numerical score to assess the pet's fitness level, with higher scores indicating better fitness.
