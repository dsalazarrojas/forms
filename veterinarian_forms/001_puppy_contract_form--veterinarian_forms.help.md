# Puppy Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from both buyers and sellers in a puppy adoption process. It ensures a smooth and secure adoption experience by gathering necessary details from both parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter your information in the **Buyer Information** field.
2. Make a **Buyer Pledge** to ensure you are committed to providing a safe and loving home for the adopted puppy.
3. Enter your **Seller Information** and **Seller Pledge** so we can get in touch with them.
4. Provide your **Contact Information** so we can keep you updated on any further communication.
5. Add a **Payment Information** section if applicable.
6. Read and agree to our **Terms and Conditions**.
7. Make a **Buyer Declaration** to confirm your adoption.
8. Sign the form digitally using **Buyer Signature** and **Seller Signature** fields.
