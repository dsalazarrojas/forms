# Veterinary Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from pet owners about their pets' medical history, current health status, and reason for visit to a veterinary clinic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your pet's name in the **Pet name** field.
2. Select your pet's type from the **Pet type** dropdown menu (e.g., Cat, Dog, Other).
3. Enter your name and contact information in the **Owner name** and **Owner contact** fields, respectively.
4. If your pet has any medical conditions, please enter them in the **Medical condition** field.
5. If your pet is currently taking any medication, please enter the details in the **Medication** field.
6. Describe your pet's medical history in the **Medical history** field.
7. Select the reason for your visit to the clinic from the **Reason for visit** dropdown menu (e.g., Consultation, Surgery, Check-up).
8. If you have any additional medical conditions or concerns, please enter them in the **Medical condition 2** field.
9. If your pet has had any previous surgery, please provide details in the **Previous surgery** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet name** (1): Enter your pet's name.
* **Pet type** (2): Select the type of pet you own (e.g., Cat, Dog, Other).
* **Owner name** (3): Enter your name.
* **Owner contact** (4): Enter your contact information (e.g., phone number, email).
* **Medical condition** (5): Enter any medical conditions your pet is currently experiencing.
* **Medication** (6): Enter any medication your pet is currently taking.
* **Medical history** (7): Describe your pet's medical history, including any previous illnesses or surgeries.
* **Reason for visit** (8): Select the reason for your visit to the clinic (e.g., Consultation, Surgery, Check-up).
* **Medical condition 2** (9): Enter any additional medical conditions or concerns.
* **Previous surgery** (10): Enter any details about your pet's previous surgery.

## Tips
* Please provide as much detail as possible for each field to ensure the veterinarian has a comprehensive understanding of your pet's health status.
* If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask your veterinarian for guidance.
* This form is intended to be a starting point for the consultation, and the veterinarian may ask additional questions or require further information during the visit.
