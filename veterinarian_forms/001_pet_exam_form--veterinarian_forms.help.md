# Pet Exam Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Exam Form is a tool used by veterinarians to gather information for a pet's examination. It is used to collect the pet's details, medical history, and examination results.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please fill out the form as thoroughly and accurately as possible.
2.  Start by entering the pet's name in the "name" field.
3.  Select the type of pet from the "pet_type" dropdown menu.
4.  Enter the pet's age in the "age" field.
5.  Choose the type of examination from the "exam_type" dropdown menu.
6.  If applicable, enter any medical conditions or notes in the "medical_condition" field.
7.  If the medical condition is not listed, you can describe it in the "medical_condition_other" field.
8.  If you have any additional medical findings, enter them in the "medical_findings" field.
9.  If you have any additional medical findings that are not listed, describe them in the "medical_findings_other" field.
10. If applicable, enter the diagnosis or notes in the "diagnosis" field.
11. Choose the treatment options from the "treatment" dropdown menu.
12. Enter the date and time for the follow-up appointment in the "follow_up" and "follow_up_time" fields.
13. Finally, enter any additional notes about the examination in the "note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **name (Text, Required):** Enter the pet's name.
- **pet_type (Select One, Required):** Select the type of pet from the menu (e.g., Pet, Cat, Dog, Other).
- **age (Number, Required):** Enter the pet's age.
- **exam_type (Select One, Required):** Select the type of examination from the menu (e.g., Basic, Advanced, Emergency).
- **medical_condition (Text, Optional):** Enter any existing medical conditions or notes.
- **medical_condition_other (Text, Optional):** Describe any medical conditions not listed.
- **medical_findings (Text, Optional):** Enter any medical findings or notes.
- **medical_findings_other (Text, Optional):** Describe any medical findings not listed.
- **diagnosis (Text, Optional):** Enter the diagnosis or notes.
- **treatment (Select Multiple, Optional):** Choose any applicable treatment options.
- **follow_up (Date, Required):** Enter the date for the follow-up appointment.
- **follow_up_time (Time, Required):** Enter the time for the follow-up appointment.
- **note (Text, Optional):** Enter any additional notes about the examination.
