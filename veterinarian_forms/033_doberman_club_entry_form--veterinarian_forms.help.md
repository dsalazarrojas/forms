# Doberman Club Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Doberman Club Entry Form is a simple registration process designed to collect basic information from individuals who wish to join or register with the Doberman Club. This information will be used to create a member profile and communicate with them regarding club events, activities, and membership benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information readily available before starting.
2. Fill out the following fields:
   - **First name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
   - **Last name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
   - **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you check regularly for club communications.
   - **Phone**: Enter your phone number for further contact purposes.
   - **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY (e.g., 01/01/20).
   - **Membership start date**: If applicable, select the date you started your membership with the club.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: The first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last name**: The last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: A valid email address where you can be contacted regarding club activities.
* **Phone**: Your phone number for further contact purposes.
* **Date of birth**: The date of birth as it appears on your identification documents, in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Membership start date**: The date you started your membership with the club, if applicable. 

## Tips

* Ensure the format for the **Date of birth** is correct.
* Double-check the accuracy of all information provided. 
* If you're not sure about a field, do not leave it blank; instead, use "Not Known/Provided" to indicate this in the notes.
* The information you provide will be used for internal club records and for sending club communications; do not hesitate to contact us for any corrections or updates to your data.
