# veterinary_continuing_education_recording_consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
The veterinary continuing education recording consent form is designed to obtain consent from veterinarians for recording their continuing education activities. This form ensures that participants' privacy and consent are respected during the recording process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your email address to ensure that you can be contacted for any further information or clarification.
2. Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
3. Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
4. Choose your title from the list of options provided (Dr, DVM, VMD, MV, or Other - specify if not listed).
5. If you are representing an organization, provide the name of your organization.
6. Check the box to confirm that you grant permission for your recording activity to be recorded.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Recording Consent** (`recording_consent`, `text`, required): This is a text field where you need to type your consent statement. Please type "yes" to confirm that you grant permission for your recording activity to be recorded.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to ensure that you can be contacted for any further information or clarification.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Veterinarian Title** (`veterinarian_title`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your title from the list of options provided (Dr, DVM, VMD, MV, or Other - specify if not listed).
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, optional): If you are representing an organization, provide the name of your organization.
* **Recording Permission** (`recording_permission`, `note`, required): Check this box to confirm that you grant permission for your recording activity to be recorded.
