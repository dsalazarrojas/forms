# Veterinary Employee Self-Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help veterinary employees self-assess their performance, strengths, and professional development goals. It's an opportunity for employees to reflect on their work and identify areas for improvement, and for management to assess employee performance and provide feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer all the questions honestly.
2. For the "Self-Assessment" section, type a brief description of your performance and accomplishments over the past year.
3. In the "Performance Evaluation" section, select one or more options that best describe your performance over the past year.
4. In the "Strengths Identification" section, list the skills and qualifications that set you apart as a veterinary professional.
5. In the "Professional Development Goals" section, describe what you hope to accomplish in the next 6-12 months.
6. In the "Comments" section, add any additional comments or feedback you'd like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Self-Assessment** (`Self-Assessment`, `note`, required/optional): A brief description of your performance and accomplishments over the past year.
* **Performance Evaluation** (`Performance Evaluation`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or more options that best describe your performance over the past year.
  * Option 1: Excellent performance
  * Option 2: Good performance
  * Option 3: Needs improvement
* **Strengths Identification** (`Strengths Identification`, `text`, required/optional): List the skills and qualifications that set you apart as a veterinary professional.
* **Professional Development Goals** (`Professional Development Goals`, `text`, required/optional): Describe what you hope to accomplish in the next 6-12 months.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or feedback you'd like to provide.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your assessment and comments.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, feel free to ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
* This form is meant to be a self-assessment, so don't hesitate to seek support or clarification if you need it.
* Remember to review and update your previous submissions if you've already completed a similar form recently.
