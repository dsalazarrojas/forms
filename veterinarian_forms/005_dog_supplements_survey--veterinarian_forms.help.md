# Dog Supplements Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about dog supplement use habits from pet owners.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully and truthfully.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for each question.
3. If you have any additional comments or concerns, feel free to add them in the "Additional Comments" field.
4. Once you have completed all the questions, review your answers carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dog Food Type** (`dog_supplements_survey_form`, text, optional): Please enter the type of dog food your pet eats (e.g. dry, wet, homemade).
* **Dog Supplements Used** (`dog_supplements_used`, text, optional): Please list the types of dog supplements you use for your pet.
* **Dietary Supplement** (`dietary_supplement`, text, optional): Please enter any dietary supplements you use for your pet.
* **Supplement Type** (`supplement_type`, text, optional): Please enter the type of supplement you use (e.g. vitamins, minerals, probiotics).
* **How Long Used** (`how_long_used`, number, optional): Please enter how long you have been using dog supplements for your pet.
* **Recommended By** (`recommended_by`, select_one, optional):
	+ Pet store
	+ Online
	+ Friend
* **Why Not Recommended** (`why_not_recommended`, text, optional): If you have not recommended dog supplements, please explain why.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Please add any additional comments or concerns you have.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address.

## Tips

* Please make sure to complete all required fields.
