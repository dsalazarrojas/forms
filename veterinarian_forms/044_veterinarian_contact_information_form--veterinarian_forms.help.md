# Veterinarian Contact Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information from veterinarians, which will be used for future communication and appointment scheduling.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This is the name you prefer to be addressed as in professional settings.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field. This is your surname.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the "Email" field. We will use this to communicate with you regarding appointments and updates.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field. This is an optional field for our staff to contact you directly.
5. Select a date for your **Appointment Date** in the "Appointment Date" field. This will help us schedule your appointment at the best time for you.
6. Choose how you would like to be contacted by selecting an option from the "Contact Method" dropdown. You can choose from Phone, Email, Text Message, or Video Chat.
7. Choose a time for your **Appointment Time** in the "Appointment Time" field. This will help us schedule your appointment at the best time for you.
8. In the "Emergency Information" field, select the emergency contact details that apply to you. You must include at least one option.
9. Enter your **Veterinary Practice** information in the "Veterinary Practice" field. This is the name of the practice where you work.
10. Enter your **Veterinarian Name** in the "Veterinarian Name" field. This is your professional name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text): Enter your preferred name to be addressed as in professional settings.
* **Last Name** (Text): Enter your surname.
* **Email** (Email): Provide your email address for future communication and updates.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number for our staff to contact you directly.
* **Appointment Date** (Date): Select a date for your appointment.
* **Contact Method** (Select One): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, Text Message, or Video Chat).
* **Appointment Time** (Time): Choose a time for your appointment.
* **Emergency Information** (Select Multiple): Select emergency contact details that apply to you (at least one option is required).
	+ **Emergency Contact Name**: Select your emergency contact's name.
	+ **Contact Phone**: Select your emergency contact's phone number.
	+ **Contact Email**: Select your emergency contact's email address.
	+ **Emergency Type**: Select the type of emergency information you want to disclose.
* **Veterinary Practice** (Text): Enter the name of your veterinary practice.
* **Veterinarian Name** (Text): Enter your professional name.
