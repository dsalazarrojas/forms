# Animal Health Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Health Declaration Form is a crucial tool for providing comprehensive information about an animal's health status. This form is designed to gather essential details from the animal's owner, which will help veterinarians or animal care professionals make informed decisions about the animal's care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary information about the animal and their owner before starting.
2. Fill out the form with the requested details, including the owner's full name and the animal's name, species, and date of the last veterinary check.
3. List any current medications or supplements the animal is taking.
4. Mention any known allergies the animal has, including food, environmental, or medication allergies.
5. Indicate the vaccination status of the animal.
6. Select any recent symptoms the animal is experiencing within the last 14 days.
7. Confirm the accuracy of the provided information by signing your name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Full Name**: Enter the full name of the animal's owner as it appears on official documents.
* **Animal Name**: Enter the name given to the animal by its owner or any other identifying name.
* **Animal Species**: Select the species of the animal from the given options (Canine, Feline, Equine, Bovine, Porcine, Avian, Other).
* **Date of Last Veterinary Check**: Enter the approximate date of the animal's last veterinary check-up.
* **Current Medications**: List all medications or supplements the animal is currently taking. Please be specific and include dosages if possible.
* **Known Allergies**: List any known food, environmental, or medication allergies the animal has.
* **Vaccination Status**: Select the status of the animal's core vaccinations. You can choose from Up to Date, Lapsed, Unknown, or Exempt.
* **Recent Symptoms**: Select all symptoms the animal is experiencing within the last 14 days. Please choose from the list of provided options or add any additional symptoms not listed.
* **Declaration Confirmation**: Confirm the accuracy of the provided information by signing your name as the owner of the animal.

## Tips
* Please ensure the information provided is accurate to ensure the animal receives proper care.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask your veterinarian or animal care professional for guidance.
