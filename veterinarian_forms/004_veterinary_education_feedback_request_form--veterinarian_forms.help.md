# veterinary_education_feedback_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from veterinarians on their educational sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the course you attended in the `course_title` field.
2. Provide the name of the course in the `course_name` field.
3. Fill in the date of the session you attended in the `session_date` field.
4. Answer the question about whether you received feedback in the `feedback` field.
5. Rate the quality of the session in the `rating` field.
6. Choose a rating scale for the session in the `rating_scale` field.
7. Identify areas of improvement in the `improvement_areas` field.
8. Provide any additional comments about the session in the `comments` field.
9. Select whether you attended school for the session in the `school` field.
10. Answer the question about whether the Continuing Education (CE) provider was involved in the session in the `ce_provider` field.
11. Choose the type of session you attended in the `session_type` field.
12. Fill in the number of participants who attended the session in the `participants_attended` field.
13. Enter the length of the session in minutes in the `session_length` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **course_title**: Enter the title of the course you attended.
* **course_name**: Enter the name of the course you attended.
* **session_date**: Enter the date of the session you attended.
* **feedback**: Answer whether you received feedback for the session (Yes/No).
* **rating**: Rate the quality of the session (Good/Fair).
* **rating_scale**: Choose a rating scale for the session (Good/Fair).
* **improvement_areas**: Identify areas of improvement for the session (Yes/No).
* **comments**: Provide any additional comments about the session.
* **school**: Select whether you attended school for the session (Yes/No).
* **ce_provider**: Answer whether the CE provider was involved in the session (Yes/No).
* **session_type**: Choose the type of session you attended (Yes/No).
* **participants_attended**: Fill in the number of participants who attended the session.
* **session_length**: Enter the length of the session in minutes.
