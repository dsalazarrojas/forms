# Veterinary Client Satisfaction Benchmark Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Client Satisfaction Benchmark Survey is designed to collect feedback from clients about their experiences at the clinic. This form will help us understand what you think about our services and what we can do to improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Please provide your contact information so that we can follow up with you if needed.
2. Answer the questions about your visit, including what you came in for, how long you waited, and what time you arrived and left.
3. Rate your satisfaction with our clinic on a scale of 1-5.
4. Share any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (1)**: Please provide your name, email, and phone number so that we can follow up with you if needed.
* **Introduction (2)**: This is a text field where you can describe your experience at the clinic.
* **Visit Information (3)**: Please select what you came in for (e.g. Initial Exam, Vaccination, etc.).
* **Likelihood to Recommend (4)**: Rate how likely you are to recommend our clinic to a friend or family member on a scale of 1-5.
* **Satisfaction with Clinic (5)**: Rate your satisfaction with our clinic on a scale of 1-5.
* **Visit Details (6)**: Select all that apply from the options provided.
* **Arrival Time (7)**: Enter the time you arrived at the clinic.
* **Departure Time (8)**: Enter the time you left the clinic.
* **Visit Length (9)**: Enter the length of your visit.
* **Staff Friendliness (10)**: Rate if the staff was friendly.
* **Additional Comments (11)**: Share any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Improvement Suggestions (12)**: Share any ideas for how we can improve our services.
* **Improvement Ideas (13)**: Share any other ideas you may have for how we can improve.
* **Waiting Time (14)**: Rate the waiting time as reasonable or not.
* **Quality of Care (15)**: Rate your satisfaction with the quality of care provided.
* **Return Likelihood (16)**: Rate how likely you are to return to our clinic for future visits.
* **Staff Helpfulness (17)**: Rate if the staff was helpful and knowledgeable.
* **Additional Comments (18)**: Share any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
