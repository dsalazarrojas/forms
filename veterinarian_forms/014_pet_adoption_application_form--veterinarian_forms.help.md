# Pet Adoption Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Adoption Application Form is a tool used to gather essential information from pet owners seeking adoption for their pets. This form helps us understand the pet's details and the owner's contact information to facilitate the adoption process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact information section with your current contact details.
2. Provide accurate and up-to-date information about your pet, including its type, name, date of birth, and any relevant health information.
3. Enter your owner contact information, including your name, date of birth, email, phone number, and address.
4. If applicable, enter the veterinarian's contact information, including their name, phone number, and email.
5. Finally, enter the adoption date for the pet.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info (page 1)**: This section is for you to provide your contact information, which is essential for us to get in touch with you.
	+ **Contact Method (page 3)**: Please choose how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, or Fax).
* **Pet Info (page 2)**: Enter accurate and up-to-date information about your pet, including its type, name, date of birth, and any relevant health information.
	+ **Pet Type (page 7)**: Choose the type of pet (Cat, Dog, Bird, Fish, or Other).
* **Owner Info (page 9-14)**: Enter your contact information, including your name, date of birth, email, phone number, and address.
	+ **Owner Name (page 10)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
	+ **Owner DOB (page 11)**: Enter your date of birth for identification purposes.
* **Owner Contact (page 12-13)**: Enter your email and phone number for us to contact you.
* **Owner Address (page 14)**: Enter your current address for us to send any necessary documents or information.
* **Veterinarian Info (page 15)**: If your pet has a veterinarian, please enter their contact information.
	+ **Vet Name (page 16)**: Enter the name of your veterinarian.
	+ **Vet Phone (page 17)**: Enter your veterinarian's phone number.
* **Adoption Date (page 20)**: Enter the date you are seeking to adopt your pet, if known.

## Tips
- Please ensure all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to contact us for assistance.
- This form is meant to facilitate the adoption process; please be honest and clear in your responses.
