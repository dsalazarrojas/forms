# veterinary_supplier_quality_rating_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The veterinary_supplier_quality_rating_survey form is used to collect feedback on the quality of service provided by suppliers. It helps management track and improve the services offered by suppliers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Supplier Name** (`Supplier Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the supplier you are rating.
2. **Rating** (rating, select multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the available ratings to reflect your overall rating of the supplier's service. The options are:
	* 1
	* 2
	* 3
	* 4
	* 5
3. **Comment** (Comment, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience with the supplier.
4. **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter the email address of the supplier, if available.
5. **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the supplier, if available.
6. **Quality** (Quality, select one, optional): Rate the quality of the service provided by the supplier.
	* Poor
	* Fair
	* Good
	* Excellent
7. **Communication** (Communication, select one, optional): Rate the communication with the supplier.
	* Poor
	* Fair
	* Good
	* Excellent
8. **Reliability** (Reliability, select one, optional): Rate the reliability of the supplier.
	* Poor
	* Fair
	* Good
	* Excellent

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Name**: Enter the name of the supplier you are rating.
* **Rating**: Choose one or more of the available ratings to reflect your overall rating of the supplier's service.
* **Comment**: Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience with the supplier.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the supplier, if available.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the supplier, if available.
* **Quality**: Rate the quality of the service provided by the supplier.
* **Communication**: Rate the communication with the supplier.
* **Reliability**: Rate the reliability of the supplier.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are filling out the correct fields with accurate and complete information.
* If you are unable to find the supplier in the list, please contact management to ensure you are accessing the correct form.
* Please be honest and objective when providing feedback. Your input helps us improve the services offered by suppliers.
