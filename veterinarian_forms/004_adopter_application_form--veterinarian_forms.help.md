# Adopter Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adopter Application Form is designed to collect necessary information from individuals or families interested in adopting pets from our organization. This form helps us ensure that the pet is placed in a suitable home and that all adopters meet our organization's adoption criteria.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Adopter Application Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Provide a valid phone number and email address where we can reach you for updates or further questions.
3. Enter your current residential address, including street, city, state, and postal code.
4. If applicable, provide the number of adults and children living in your household.
5. List any current pets in your home, including species and ages.
6. In the "Reason for Adopting a Pet" field, briefly explain why you want to adopt a pet from our organization.
7. Agree to a possible home visit if requested by our organization.
8. If you have personal references (e.g., friends, family members, or colleagues), provide their names and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Applicant full name**: Enter your first and last name in this field.
- **Phone number**: Enter your best contact number so we can reach you for updates or further questions.
- **Email address**: Enter a valid email address where we can send you updates or ask questions.
- **Residential address**: Enter your street address, city, state, and postal code to help us verify your home.
- **Household members**: If applicable, enter the number of adults and children living in your household.
- **Current pets**: List any pets you currently have at home, including their species and ages.
- **Reason for adopting a pet**: Briefly explain why you want to adopt a pet from our organization.
- **Agree to home visit**: Indicate whether you agree to a possible home visit by our organization.
- **Personal references**: Provide names and contact information of two personal references.
- **Signature date**: Date and sign the form to confirm that the information provided is accurate.

## Tips
- Please fill in the form accurately and completely.
- Make sure to include your current phone number and email address where we can reach you.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
- We may request additional information or documents before or after submitting the form.
- A home visit may be requested as part of the adoption process.
