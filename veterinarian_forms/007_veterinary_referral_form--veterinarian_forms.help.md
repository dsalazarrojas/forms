# Veterinary Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Referral Form is used for referring a patient from one specialist to another. This form helps the receiving specialist understand the patient's condition and make an informed decision about their care.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read all fields before filling out the form.
- Fill out the form accurately and completely.
- Double-check your answers for consistency and accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, required/optional): This section collects information about the patient, including any relevant medical history or conditions.
  - Enter the patient's name, age, and any other relevant details.
- **Referral Reason** (`referral_reason`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief reason for referring the patient to the specialist.
  - Be as detailed as possible about the patient's condition and why the referral is necessary.
- **Specialist Info** (`specialist_info`, text, required/optional): Add any additional information about the specialist making the referral.
  - This could include the referring specialist's name, title, or other relevant contact details.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Provide information about the contact person or the specialist's clinic.
  - This could include the contact person's name, phone number, or email address.
- **Specialist Clinics** (`specialist_clinics`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the specialist clinics that the patient has visited or will visit.
  - Choose all clinics that apply, as this will help the receiving specialist understand the patient's complete medical history.
- **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required/optional): Enter the name and other contact details of the person who will be handling the referral.
  - This could be the specialist's name, a clinic administrator, or a receptionist.
- **Contact EMail** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the person who will be handling the referral.
  - Ensure this is a valid email address that can receive messages.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the person who will be handling the referral.
  - Ensure this is a valid phone number that can receive calls.
- **Specialist Note** (`specialist_note`, note, required/optional): Add any additional information about the referral, such as medical notes or treatment plans.
  - Keep this section concise and focused on essential details.
- **Patient Note** (`patient_note`, note, required/optional): Add any additional information about the patient, such as medical history or allergies.
  - Keep this section concise and focused on essential details.
- **Referral Date** (`referral_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the referral.
  - Ensure this is in the correct format (DD/MM/YY) and is accurate.
- **Specialist Clinic** (`specialist_clinic`, select_one, required/optional): Select the specialist clinic that the patient will visit.
  - Choose the correct clinic for accurate referral.

## Tips
- Review the form before submission to ensure all fields are complete and accurate.
- Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
- Double-check for spelling and grammar mistakes.
- Ensure all fields are correctly formatted before submitting the form.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, please ask your supervisor or contact the IT department for assistance.
