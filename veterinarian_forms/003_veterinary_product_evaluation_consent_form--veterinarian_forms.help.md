# Veterinary Product Evaluation Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from pet owners for the purpose of evaluating veterinary products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your pet's details, including their age and breed.
2. Provide your contact information so we can reach out to you regarding the evaluation.
3. Indicate whether you consent to the evaluation of the product.
4. Add any additional product information.
5. Enter the start and end dates of the evaluation.
6. Provide any notes about the product.
7. Finally, sign the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Owner Details** (`pet_owner_details`, text, optional): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Provide your name, phone number, and/or email address to facilitate communication.
* **Pet Breed** (`pet_breed`, text, optional): Specify your pet's breed to help us better understand their needs and characteristics.
* **Pet Age** (`pet_age`, number, optional): Enter your pet's age for better understanding of their development stage.
* **Owner Consent** (`owner_consent`, select, optional): Select "Yes" if you consent to the evaluation of the product.
* **Product Info** (`product_info`, text, optional): Describe the product you are evaluating.
* **Evaluation Start Date** (`evaluation_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the evaluation starts.
* **Evaluation End Date** (`evaluation_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the evaluation ends.
* **Product Notes** (`product_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about the product, such as its dosage or any side effects.
* **Owner Signature** (`owner_signature`, text, optional): Sign this form to confirm your consent to the evaluation of the product.
