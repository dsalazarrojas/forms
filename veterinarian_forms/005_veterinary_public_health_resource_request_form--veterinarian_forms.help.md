# Veterinary Public Health Resource Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Veterinary Public Health Resource Request Form is a tool used to gather information and requests from resource requesters in the field of veterinary public health. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

- Fill out all required fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
- Select the correct options for each select and select_multiple field.
- Ensure that your contact information is up to date and accurate.
- Review and agree to the terms of service before submitting your request.
- Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Resource Requested** (`resource_request`, text, required)
Enter a detailed description of the resource you are requesting.

### 2. **Location** (`location`, text, required)
Enter the location where the resource is needed.

### 3. **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required)
Enter your name as the person requesting the resource.

### 4. **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required)
Enter your valid email address.

### 5. **Type of Animal** (`animal_type`, select_one, not required)
Choose the type of animal you are requesting resources for. Options include:
  - Dog
  - Cat
  - Other

### 6. **Type of Service Requested** (`service_request`, select_one, not required)
Choose the type of service you are requesting. Options include:
  - General Consultation
  - Emergency Service
  - Other

### 7. **Types of Resources Needed** (`resource_type`, select_multiple, not required)
Choose all types of resources you are requesting.

### 8. **Resource Description** (`resource_description`, text, required)
Enter a detailed description of the resources you are requesting.

### 9. **Resource Availability** (`resource_availability`, date, required)
Enter the date when the resource is available.

### 10. **Follow-up Needed** (`follow_up`, select_one, not required)
Choose if you need follow-up on your resource request. Options include:
  - Yes
  - No

### 11. **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, number, required)
Enter your valid phone number.

### 12. **Resource Requester Comment** (`resource_requester_comment`, text, required)
Enter any additional comments or information about your resource request.

### 13. **Resource Requester Agreement** (`resource_requester_agreement`, checkbox, required)
Check that you agree to the terms of service.

### 14. **Resource Requester Signature** (`resource_requester_signature`, radio, required)
Choose your agreement by selecting Option A or Option B.

### 15. **Resource Requester Date** (`resource_requester_date`, date, required)
Enter the date you are signing this form.

### 16. **Resource Requester Time** (`resource_requester_time`, time, required)
Enter the time you are signing this form.

### 17. **Resource Requester Notes** (`resource_requester_notes`, text, required)
Enter any additional notes or information about your resource request.
