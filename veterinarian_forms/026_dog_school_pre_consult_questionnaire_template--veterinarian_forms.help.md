# dog_school_pre_consult_questionnaire_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **dog_school_pre_consult_questionnaire_template** is a template used to gather information from pet owners before their dog's training session. This form helps trainers understand the dog's breed, age, medical conditions, and training goals, ensuring a safe and effective training experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in all the required fields.
2. For each field, provide the requested information.
3. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **dog_breed** (Required)
* **dog_breed** (Text, Required): Enter your dog's breed.

### 2. **prophets_name** (Required)
* **prophets_name** (Text, Required): Enter your dog's name.

### 3. **age** (Required)
* **age** (Number, Required): Enter your dog's age.

### 4. **owner_name** (Optional)
* **owner_name** (Text, Optional): Enter your name or the name of the person responsible for your dog's care.

### 5. **owner_phone** (Required)
* **owner_phone** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number for emergency contact.

### 6. **dog_name** (Required)
* **dog_name** (Text, Required): Enter your dog's name.

### 7. **dog_birthday** (Required)
* **dog_birthday** (Date, Required): Enter your dog's birthday (date of birth).

### 8. **owner_email** (Required)
* **owner_email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address for contact.

### 9. **medical_conditions** (Optional)
* **medical_conditions** (Text, Optional): If your dog has any medical conditions, please describe them here.

### 10. **health_insurance** (Required)
* **health_insurance** (Select One, Required): Select your dog's health insurance option.

### 11. **emergency_contacts** (Optional)
* **emergency_contacts** (Text, Optional): List any emergency contact information.

### 12. **training_experience** (Optional)
* **training_experience** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select any relevant training experience you've had with your dog.

### 13. **training_goals** (Required)
* **training_goals** (Text, Required): Describe your dog's training goals.

### 14. **training_frequency** (Required)
* **training_frequency** (Select One, Required): Select your desired training frequency.

### 15. **training_duration** (Optional)
* **training_duration** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the length of time you want to commit to training.

### 16. **training_schedule** (Required)
* **training_schedule** (Date, Required): Enter the date and time for your first training session.

### 17. **trainer** (Optional)
* **trainer** (Text, Optional): If you have a preferred trainer, enter their name here.

This guide should help you complete the **dog_school_pre_consult_questionnaire_template** with ease. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
