# veterinary_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The veterinary_release_form is a form designed to collect essential information from pet owners to facilitate veterinary care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the pet owner's name in the <owner_name> field.
2. Provide the pet owner's phone number in the <owner_phone> field.
3. Enter the pet owner's email address in the <owner_email> field.
4. Select the medical conditions of the pet in the <Select Medical Conditions> field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Owner Name**: <owner_name> (text, required: no)
	+ Enter the name of the pet's owner.
* **Owner Phone**: <owner_phone> (text, required: no)
	+ Enter the pet owner's phone number.
* **Owner Email**: <owner_email> (email, required: no)
	+ Enter the pet owner's email address.
* **Veterinarian Name**: <veterinarian_name> (text, required: no)
	+ Enter the name of the veterinarian or veterinary clinic.
* **Medical Conditions**: <Select Medical Conditions> (select multiple, required: no)
	+ Select the medical conditions affecting the pet, such as hypothyroidism, cancer, diabetes, etc.
* **Select Medical Conditions**: <Select Medical Conditions> (select one, required: no)
	+ Select a specific medical condition from the list provided.
	+ Note: This field is intended to be a secondary option, and if you select "Select Medical Conditions", you will be presented with a list of specific medical conditions to choose from.
