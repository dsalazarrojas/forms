# Animal Adoption Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to schedule an appointment for animal adoption. It allows visitors to provide their contact information, appointment details, and preferences for their visit.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Visitor Full Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Select the date and time you would like to schedule the appointment in the "Requested Appointment Date" and "Preferred Start Time" fields.
5. Choose the type of appointment you are scheduling (e.g., "Initial Meet and Greet", "Secondary Visit with Current Pets", or "Final Adoption Signing").
6. Enter the name of the animal you wish to meet in the "Name of Animal You Wish to Meet" field.
7. If you are bringing a current dog for an introduction, select "True" or "False" in the "Are you bringing a current dog for an introduction?" field.
8. If you would like to receive SMS appointment reminders, select "True" or "False" in the "Would you like to receive SMS appointment reminders?" field.
9. If you have any additional questions or concerns for the staff, you can enter them in the "Additional Questions for Staff" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Visitor Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address where you can be reached for appointment reminders and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for appointment reminders and updates.
* **Requested Appointment Date**: Select the date you would like to schedule the appointment.
* **Preferred Start Time**: Select the start time of your appointment.
* **Type of Appointment**: Choose the type of appointment you are scheduling (e.g., "Initial Meet and Greet", "Secondary Visit with Current Pets", or "Final Adoption Signing").
* **Name of Animal You Wish to Meet**: Enter the name of the animal you wish to meet.
* **Are you bringing a current dog for an introduction?**: If you are bringing a current dog for an introduction, select "True". If not, select "False" or "N/A" if you are unsure.
* **Would you like to receive SMS appointment reminders?**: If you would like to receive SMS reminders, select "True". If not, select "False".
* **Additional Questions for Staff**: If you have any questions or concerns for the staff, you can enter them here.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate information to ensure a smooth and successful appointment process.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact our staff for assistance.
* We will contact you via email and/or phone with appointment details and updates.
