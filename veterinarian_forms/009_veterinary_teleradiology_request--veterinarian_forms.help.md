# Veterinary Teleradiology Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting veterinary telemedicine services, allowing you to provide essential medical information and images to support remote diagnoses and treatments. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide detailed medical history, present your medical images, and add any additional information necessary for your veterinarian's evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
This form is divided into two pages. To complete it, follow the steps below:

1. **Page 1**: Provide general information about yourself and the medical issue.
	* **Form Info**: Briefly describe the reason for your visit.
	* **Name**: Enter your name, as it will appear on your records.
	* **Date**: Choose the date of your visit.
	* **Time**: Choose the time of your visit.
2. **Page 2**: Share more detailed medical information and images.
	* **Medical Info**: Describe your medical history, symptoms, and previous treatments.
	* **Medical Images**: Share medical images related to your condition.
	* **Additional Info**: Provide any additional context about your case.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Form Info
* **Form Info**: A description of your visit reason.
* **Required**: Required Field

### Name
* **Name**: Enter your name as it will appear on your veterinary records.
* **Required**: Required Field

### Date
* **Date**: Choose the date of your visit. This field is for selecting a date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Required**: Required Field

### Time
* **Time**: Choose the time of your visit. This field is for selecting a time of day in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Required**: Required Field

### File Attachments
* **File Attachments**: You can add any relevant files such as images, videos, or other attachments.
* **Required**: Optional Field

### Medical Info
* **Medical Info**: A detailed description of your medical history, symptoms, and previous treatments.
* **Required**: Required Field

### Medical Images
* **Medical Images**: Share any relevant medical images that can help with your diagnosis.
* **Required**: Optional Field

### Additional Info
* **Additional Info**: Any additional information you feel is important for your veterinarian's evaluation.
* **Required**: Optional Field

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out completely.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
