# Veterinary Clinical Placement Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Clinical Placement Application Form is designed to collect information from veterinary students, allowing them to apply for clinical placement opportunities at veterinary schools. This form helps schools and organizations to gather essential details about students' backgrounds, preferences, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your user information, including your name and contact details.
2. Share your student details, such as your student ID, grade level, and major.
3. Briefly describe your experience in the veterinary field.
4. Specify your preferred dates and time for the clinical placement.
5. Select your student status (e.g., Student, Active, Inactive).
6. Choose your preferred location for the clinical placement.
7. Enter your school information, including the school name, city, state, zip, and country.
8. Provide the address, phone number, and email of your school.
9. Add any additional school information or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info**: Enter your name and contact details.
	+ This field is used to collect your personal name and contact information.
* **Student Details**: Provide your student ID, grade level, and major.
	+ This field helps us understand your academic background and interests.
* **Experience**: Describe your experience in the veterinary field.
	+ Share your work experience, internships, or volunteer work in the veterinary field.
* **Preferred Dates**: Specify the dates you are available for the clinical placement.
	+ Enter the start and end dates you prefer for the clinical placement.
* **Preferred Time**: Choose your preferred time for the clinical placement.
	+ Select the time slot you prefer for the clinical placement.
* **Student Status**: Select your current student status (e.g., Student, Active, Inactive).
	+ Choose the status that reflects your current situation.
* **Preferred Location**: Choose one or more locations for the clinical placement.
	+ Select the locations you are interested in for the clinical placement.

	+ Provide your school's address, phone number, and email.
