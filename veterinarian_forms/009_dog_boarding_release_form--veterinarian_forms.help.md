# Dog Boarding Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dog Boarding Release Form is a document that requires pet owners to provide essential information about their dogs before they are boarded. This form ensures that the boarding facility has the necessary details to care for the pet and address any potential health or behavioral issues that may arise during the boarding period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the pet's information, including its name, age, breed, and any health conditions or allergies.
2. Provide your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
3. Describe the dog's behavior and any specific boarding instructions or requirements.
4. List any contact information for the person or organization responsible for picking up or visiting the dog during boarding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Info** (`Pet Info`, `text`, required: false): Please provide the dog's name, age, breed, and any health conditions or allergies.
* **Pet Owner Info** (`Pet Owner Info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as the pet's owner.
* **Boarding Details** (`Boarding Details`, `text`, required: false): Describe the dog's behavior, including any specific needs or requirements during boarding.
* **Boarding Contact** (`Boarding Contact`, `text`, required: false): List the contact information for the person or organization responsible for picking up or visiting the dog during boarding.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure the dog's safe and healthy boarding experience.
- If you have any concerns or questions, do not hesitate to contact the boarding facility directly.
- This form is an essential part of our boarding process, and your cooperation is greatly appreciated.

Note: The required/optional status of each field in the form YAML indicates that all fields are optional, as per the original design. However, please provide accurate and complete information for all fields to ensure the best possible care for your dog.
