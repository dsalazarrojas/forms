# Veterinary Research Footage Usage Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from pet owners or clients about the purpose and usage of footage collected during veterinary procedures. It ensures that all parties involved are aware of and agree to the usage of the footage.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Owner Information
To complete this form, please provide your personal information as a pet owner or client. This includes your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.

### Step 2: Animal Information
Provide details about the animal being treated, including its species, age, breed, and any relevant medical history.

### Step 3: Footage Details
Describe the footage collected during the procedure, including its purpose, location, and duration.

### Step 4: Consent
Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your consent for the usage of the footage.

### Step 5: Additional Details
Provide any additional information about the footage or the procedure, such as the date and time it was recorded, and the location where it was recorded.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Information**: Your personal details, including your name and contact information.
* **Animal Information**: Details about the animal being treated, including species, age, breed, and medical history.
* **Footage Details**: Description of the footage collected during the procedure, including its purpose, location, and duration.
* **Consent**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your consent for the usage of the footage.
* **Date Recorded**: The date when the footage was recorded.
* **Time Recorded**: The time when the footage was recorded.
* **Location Recorded**: The location where the footage was recorded.
* **Duration Recorded**: The duration of the recorded footage.
* **Footage Purpose**: The purpose of the footage, including Research, Training, Commercial, Educational, or Other.
* **Owner Note**: Any additional information you want to provide about the footage or procedure.
* **Veterinarian Note**: Any additional information the veterinarian may want to provide about the procedure or footage.

## Tips

* Please review the form carefully before signing it.
* If you are unsure about any section, please ask your veterinarian for clarification.
* Make sure to keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
