# Dog Pain Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dog Pain Assessment Form is designed to collect information about a dog's pain levels, type, duration, frequency, and severity to help veterinarians understand the dog's condition and provide appropriate treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of pain the dog is experiencing (e.g., Dog Arthritis, Dog Cancer, or Dog Injury).
2. Indicate the level of pain the dog is experiencing (e.g., Level 1, Level 2, Level 3, or Level 4).
3. Choose how long the dog has been experiencing pain (e.g., Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
4. Select the frequency at which the pain occurs (e.g., Daily, Weekly, or Rarely).
5. Indicate the severity of the pain (e.g., Mild, Moderate, or Severe).
6. If applicable, provide details about any medication the dog is taking.
7. Add any additional comments or notes about the dog's condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Pain Assessment Form
* **What Type of Pain** (`Pain Type`, Required): Select the type of pain the dog is experiencing, such as:
	+ Dog Arthritis
	+ Dog Cancer
	+ Dog Injury
	+ Other

### 2. Owner Information
* **Owner Information** (`Owner Information`, Optional): Enter any relevant information about the dog's owner or caregiver.

### 3. Pain Type
* **What Type of Pain** (Pain Type, Required): Select the type of pain the dog is experiencing.
* Hint: This field helps the veterinarian understand the underlying cause of the pain.

### 4. Pain Level
* **Pain Level** (Pain Level, Required): Select the level of pain the dog is experiencing.
* Hint: This field helps the veterinarian understand the intensity of the pain.

### 5. Pain Duration
* **Pain Duration** (Pain Duration, Optional): Enter the length of time the dog has been experiencing pain.
* Hint: This field helps the veterinarian understand the chronicity of the pain.

### 6. Pain Frequency
* **Pain Frequency** (Pain Frequency, Required): Select how often the dog experiences pain.
* Hint: This field helps the veterinarian understand how frequently the pain occurs.

### 7. Pain Severity
* **Pain Severity** (Pain Severity, Required): Select the severity of the pain.
* Hint: This field helps the veterinarian understand the intensity of the pain.

### 8. Medication
* **Medication** (Medication, Optional): If the dog is taking any medication, enter details here.
* Hint: This field helps the veterinarian understand any medication the dog is taking.

### 9. Notes
* **Notes** (Notes, Optional): Add any additional comments or information about the dog's condition.
* Hint: This field helps the veterinarian gather more information about the dog's situation.
