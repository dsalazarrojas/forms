# Grooming Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Grooming Release Form is used to grant consent for grooming services and provide essential information about your pet's medical history, grooming needs, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Read through the form carefully and answer each field according to your pet's needs.
2.  Fill in the required fields (indicated by *).
3.  Sign and date the form to confirm your consent for grooming services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **pet_info**: Enter your pet's name, age, breed, and any other relevant information.
* **owner_info**: Enter your name, address, and contact information (phone number and email).
* **pet_medical_history**: Briefly describe your pet's medical history, including any allergies, illnesses, or previous injuries.
* **grooming_services**: Select the services your pet requires, such as Bathing, Clipping, Nail Trimming, Shampooing, and Teeth Brushing.
* **groomer_release**: Check the box indicating that you grant permission for the pet to receive grooming services.
* **vet_info**: Enter any relevant medical information about your pet, such as vaccinations or previous treatments.
* **emergency_contact**: Enter the name and contact information of the person or organization to contact in case of an emergency.
* **vet_signature**: The veterinarian's signature confirms they have reviewed and understand the form.
* **owner_signature**: Your signature confirms you grant permission for the pet to receive grooming services.
* **date**: Enter the date you sign the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required fields (indicated by *).
* If you have any questions or concerns, ask your veterinarian for clarification.
* Keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
