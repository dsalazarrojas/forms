### Field-by-Field Explanation
* *Pet Name* (`pet_name`, required): This field should be filled out with the name of the dog being transferred.
* *Date Of Ownership Transfer* (`date_of_ownership_transfer`, required): This field should be filled out with the date of the transfer.
* *Reason For Transfer* (`reason_for_transfer`, optional): This field is optional but recommended for recording the reason for the transfer.
* *New Owner* (`new_owner`, optional): This field is for the name of the new owner.
* *Current Owner* (`current_owner`, optional): This field is for the name of the current owner.
* *Owner Signature* (`owner_signature`, required): This field should be signed by the current owner.
* *New Owner Signature* (`new_owner_signature`, required): This field should be signed by the new owner.
* *Witness Signature* (`witness_signature`, required): This field should be signed by a witness.
* *Veterinarian Signature* (`veterinarian_signature`, required): This field should be signed by the veterinarian.
* *Date Of Transfer* (`date_of_transfer`, optional): This field should be filled out with the date of the transfer.
* *Reason For Refusal* (`reason_for_refusal`, optional): This field is for recording the reason for refusal of the transfer.
