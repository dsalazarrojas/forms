# Pet Health Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Pet Health Record Form is designed to collect and store essential information about a pet's health status, vaccination history, and medical history. This form helps veterinarians and pet owners to keep track of a pet's health status, which is crucial for providing accurate and efficient care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of pet you are registering (Cat, Dog, or Bird).
2.  Indicate the pet's vaccination status (Vaccinated, Not Vaccinated, or Unknown).
3.  Select any medications the pet is currently taking (Flea/Tick, Heartworms, or Other).
4.  Identify any food allergies the pet has (Beef, Fish, or Other).
5.  Describe the pet's behavior (Aggressive, Affected, or Normal).
6.  Enter any medical notes or observations about the pet's health status.
7.  Enter any additional medical notes or observations about the pet's health status.
8.  Enter any further medical notes or observations about the pet's health status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **pet_name** (`pet_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the pet.
*   **pet_type** (`pet_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of pet (Cat, Dog, or Bird).
*   **vaccination_status** (`vaccination_status`, select_multiple, required): Indicate the pet's vaccination status (Vaccinated, Not Vaccinated, or Unknown).
*   **medications** (`medications`, select_multiple, required): Select any medications the pet is currently taking (Flea/Tick, Heartworms, or Other).
*   **food_allergies** (`food_allergies`, select_multiple, optional): Identify any food allergies the pet has (Beef, Fish, or Other).
*   **behavior** (`behavior`, select_multiple, required): Describe the pet's behavior (Aggressive, Affected, or Normal).
*   **medical_notes** (`medical_notes`, text, optional): Enter any medical notes or observations about the pet's health status.
*   **medical_notes_2** (`medical_notes_2`, text, optional): Enter any further medical notes or observations about the pet's health status.
*   **medical_notes_3** (`medical_notes_3`, text, optional): Enter any additional medical notes or observations about the pet's health status.
*   **medical_notes_4** (`medical_notes_4`, text, optional): Enter any further medical notes or observations about the pet's health status.
