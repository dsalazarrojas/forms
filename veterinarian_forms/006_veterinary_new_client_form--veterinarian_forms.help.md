# veterinary_new_client_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Client Form is a document that collects information about a new client, including their contact and medical history details. This form is designed to help veterinary professionals efficiently gather necessary information about new clients and their pets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the New Client Form with the client's basic contact information in section 1.
2. In section 2, provide the client's information, including their name, address, phone number, and email.
3. In section 3, gather any relevant medical history or allergies that the client would like to disclose.
4. In section 4, collect information about the pet's owner, such as their name, contact details, and emergency contact information.
5. In section 5, obtain payment information for future veterinary services.
6. In section 6, provide any additional veterinary-related information, such as veterinary care preferences.
7. Finally, include any additional notes that the veterinary clinic staff may find useful in caring for the new client's pet.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### New Client Form
* **New Client Form**: (`new_client_form`, `text`, not required): This is the initial form to fill out with the client's basic contact information. Please provide your name, address, phone number, and email to get started with our services.
* **Client Information**: (`client_info`, `text`, not required): Enter the client's information, including their name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Contact Information**: (`contact_info`, `text`, not required): If the client's information is different from the owner's, please enter their contact details here.
* **Medical History**: (`medical_history`, `text`, not required): Share any relevant medical history or allergies that the client would like to disclose.
* **Owner Information**: (`owner_info`, `text`, not required): Provide any relevant information about the pet's owner, such as their name, contact details, and emergency contact info.
* **Payment Information**: (`payment_info`, `text`, not required): Enter any details about payment methods and schedules for future veterinary services.
* **Veterinary Information**: (`veterinary_info`, `text`, not required): Share any additional veterinary-related information, such as veterinary care preferences.
* **Additional Notes**: (`additional_notes`, `text`, not required): Include any further information about the new client or their pet that you think the veterinary clinic staff would find useful.
