# Veterinary Media Appearance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to request media appearances for the veterinary clinic, such as interviews, TV shows, or other public events. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of media you are interested in (e.g., TV, radio, podcast). 
2. Enter the name of the media representative or publication. 
3. Enter the contact information for the media representative. 
4. Provide a brief description of the event or appearance. 
5. Enter the date and time of the event. 
6. Enter the location of the event. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Media Type**: Select the type of media you are interested in, such as TV, radio, or podcast. 
* **Media Name**: Enter the name of the media representative or publication. 
* **Veterinary Clinic Name**: Enter the name of your veterinary clinic. 
* **Media Contact Name**: Enter the name of the media contact person. 
* **Media Contact Number**: Enter the contact phone number of the media contact person. 
* **Media Email**: Enter the email address of the media contact person. 
* **Media Website**: Enter the website of the media contact person or publication. 
* **Event Description**: Provide a brief description of the event or appearance, including any relevant details. 
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event. 
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event. 
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event. 
* **Additional Media**: If you have any additional information about the media appearance, enter it here. 

**Tips**

* Please ensure that you have the necessary authorization to submit this form. 
* Double-check that you have entered all required information, including your contact details. 
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, contact the veterinary clinic administration.
