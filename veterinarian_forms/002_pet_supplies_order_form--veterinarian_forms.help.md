# Pet Supplies Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Supplies Order Form is a tool used to submit orders for pet supplies such as food, toys, and bedding. To use this form, follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow the steps below:
1. Select the type of pet you have.
2. Enter the name of your pet.
3. Choose the supplies you want to order (e.g. food, toys, bedding).
4. Enter the quantity of each supply you want to order.
5. Provide your contact information (phone number and email).
6. Add any additional comments or messages for the supplier.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Type** (`Pet Type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of pet you have (e.g. Pet, Cat, Dog, Other).
* **Pet Name** (`Pet Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your pet.
* **Supply Type** (`Supply Type`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the types of supplies you want to order (e.g. Food, Toys, Bedding).
* **Order Quantity** (`Order Quantity`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the quantity of each supply you want to order.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Message** (`Message`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or messages for the supplier.
* **Confirm Message** (`Confirm Message`, `note`, required/optional): Confirm that you have read and understood our terms and conditions.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select "Submit" to complete your order.
* **Error Message** (`Error Message`, `note`, required/optional): This field will display any errors or issues with your order.
