# dog_grooming_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "dog_grooming_application_form" is used to collect information from pet owners for scheduling a dog grooming appointment or related services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant information about yourself as the pet owner.
2. Choose the details about your pet.
3. Select the services you are interested in for your pet.
4. Enter your contact information for communication purposes.
5. Leave a note for the veterinarian (if necessary).
6. Schedule the appointment date and time.
7. Select the application status as "Active" or "Inactive".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **owner_info (Select Multiple)**: Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you are the pet owner.
* **pet_info (Select Multiple)**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the pet is available.
* **services (Select Multiple)**: Choose the services you are interested in for your pet.
* **contact_info (Select Multiple)**: Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you are willing to provide your contact information.
* **message (Text)**: Enter any additional comments or questions you have.
* **owner_note (Note)**: Leave a note for the veterinarian if necessary.
* **date (Date)**: Enter the date of the appointment.
* **time (Time)**: Enter the time of the appointment.
* **application_status (Select One)**: Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the status of your application.
