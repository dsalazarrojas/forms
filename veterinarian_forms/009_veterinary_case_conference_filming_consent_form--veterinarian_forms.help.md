# Veterinary Case Conference Filming Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information and consent from participants for filming in a veterinary case conference. It captures details about the participant, filming location, date, time, and other relevant parameters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's purpose and instructions.
2. Fill in the required fields as needed.
3. Ensure you understand each field's meaning and purpose.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Details
* **Participant Name**: (`participant_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter the name of the participant who will be filmed.
* **Email**: (`email`, `email`, **required**): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Contact Information**: (`contact_info`, `text`, **required**): Enter any additional contact information for the participant.

### Filming Details
* **Filming Location**: (`filming_location`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose the location where filming will take place.
* **Filming Date**: (`filming_date`, `date`, **required**): Select the date for filming.
* **Filming Time**: (`filming_time`, `time`, **required**): Select the time for filming.
* **Filming Duration**: (`filming_duration`, `number`, **required**): Enter the duration of filming.
* **Filming Equipment**: (`filming_equipment`, `text`, **required**): Specify the equipment to be used for filming.
* **Filming Scheduling**: (`filming_scheduling`, `date`, **required**): Select the scheduling date for filming.
* **Filming Scheduling Time**: (`filming_scheduling_time`, `time`, **required**): Select the time for scheduling.
* **Filming Scheduling Notes**: (`filming_scheduling_notes`, `note`, **required**): Add any additional filming scheduling notes.
* **Filming Conduct**: (`filming_conduct`, `text`, **optional**): Provide any filming conduct guidelines.
* **Filming Completion**: (`filming_completion`, `select_one`, **required**): Confirm if the filming is completed.
* **Filming Review**: (`filming_review`, `select_one`, **required**): Confirm if the filming has been reviewed.
* **Filming Status**: (`filming_status`, `select_one`, **required**): Specify the status of the filming.

### Consent and Additional Information
* **Filming Consent**: (`filming_consent`, `select_one`, **required**): Confirm if participant gives consent for filming.
* **Consent to Share**: (`consent_to_share`, `select_one`, **required**): Confirm if participant consents to sharing footage.
* **Use of Footage**: (`use_of_footage`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Specify the intended use of the footage.
* **Teaching Rounds**: (`teaching_rounds`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Confirm if teaching rounds are involved.
* **Professional Events**: (`professional_events`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Confirm if professional events are involved.
* **Consent to Recording**: (`consent_to_recording`, `select_one`, **required**): Confirm if participant consents to recording.
* **Participant Signature**: (`participant_signature`, `text`, **required**): Sign and date the form to confirm participation.
* **Additional Comments**: (`additional_comments`, `text`, **optional**): Provide any additional comments or information.
