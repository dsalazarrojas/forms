The purpose of the Veterinary Trivia Night Survey is to collect feedback and ratings from attendees about a veterinary-themed event. This survey aims to gauge the attendees' engagement, satisfaction, and understanding of the event content. The information collected will help improve future veterinary Trivia Nights, making them more engaging and informative for all participants.

## Veterinary Trivia Night Survey - Help Guide
### Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback and ratings from participants of the Veterinary Trivia Night event. Your responses will help us understand how you enjoyed the event and what we can do better in the future.

### How To Complete This Form
- Fill in all required fields to provide us with detailed and helpful feedback.
- You can answer all or some of the fields, depending on what you feel comfortable sharing.
- Use the available hints to guide your answers if you need help.

### Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (Event Name, text, optional): Please enter the name of the event you attended, if you remember it.
* **Event Date** (Event Date, date, optional): Enter the date of the event you attended, if you remember it.
* **Event Time** (Event Time, time, optional): Enter the time of the day the event took place, if you remember it.
* **Attendee Name** (Attendee Name, text, optional): This is your name, which will help us understand who you are and your preferences.
* **Feedback** (Feedback, text, optional): Share your thoughts, suggestions, and any other comments about the event. This is your chance to tell us what you liked and disliked.
* **Rating** (Rating, number, optional): Choose a number between 1 and 5 to rate your overall experience. A higher rating indicates a better experience.
* **Rating Reason** (Reason, text, optional): If you chose a lower rating, please explain why. If you chose a higher rating, tell us what made the event enjoyable for you.
* **1-5** (Rating Scale, select multiple, optional): Select one or multiple numbers that reflect your ratings for different aspects of the event. You can choose any or all of the numbers between 1 and 5. 

Note: If a field has no label, it means that no label has been specified in the YAML input schema for that field.
