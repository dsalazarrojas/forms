# Veterinary Risk Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate the current risk management practices within veterinary clinics, specifically focusing on staff awareness, protocols, safety, and quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant risk areas that apply to your clinic from the dropdown menu.
2. Rate your current awareness and understanding of risk management practices using the number scale.
3. Provide feedback on current protocols and safety procedures used in your clinic.
4. Indicate whether or not you have implemented safety and quality improvements.
5. Select the risk level and frequency that best describes your current risk exposure.
6. Answer "yes" or "no" for each risk area to indicate your current state.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Risk Areas** (`risk_areas`, select_multiple, required): Select all risk areas that apply to your clinic from the dropdown menu.
	+ This field allows you to identify the specific areas of risk within your clinic.
* **Staff Awareness** (`staff_awareness`, number, required): Rate your current awareness and understanding of risk management practices using the number scale (e.g., 1-10).
	+ This field measures your level of understanding of risk management concepts and practices.
* **Current Protocols** (`current_protocols`, text, required): Provide feedback on current protocols and safety procedures used in your clinic.
	+ This field is used to gather information about your current protocols and safety procedures.
* **Safety Improvements** (`safety_improvements`, select_one, required): Indicate whether or not you have implemented safety improvements in your clinic.
	+ This field checks for the presence or absence of safety improvements.
* **Quality Improvements** (`quality_improvements`, select_multiple, required): Select all quality improvements that are currently implemented in your clinic.
	+ This field identifies areas where quality improvements can be made.
* **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, number, required): Rate your current risk exposure using the number scale.
	+ This field measures the severity of risk in your clinic.
* **Risk Frequency** (`risk_frequency`, number, required): Indicate how frequently you experience risks in your clinic using the number scale.
	+ This field assesses the frequency of risk exposure.
* **Risk Urgency** (`risk_urgency`, time, required): Select the time frame within which you experience risks.
	+ This field measures the timing of risk exposure.
* **Risk Area 1-17** (`risk_area_1-risk_area_17`, select_one, required): Answer "yes" or "no" for each risk area to indicate your current state.
	+ This field assesses the presence or absence of specific risk areas.
