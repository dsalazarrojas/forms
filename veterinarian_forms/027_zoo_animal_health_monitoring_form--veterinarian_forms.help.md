# Zoo Animal Health Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Zoo Animal Health Monitoring Form is designed to gather information on the health status and treatment of animals in the zoo.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Ensure you have all necessary information and documentation about the animal before filling out the form.
*   Select the appropriate species from the list.
*   Choose the animal's current condition from the options provided.
*   Enter the animal's current weight.
*   Enter the animal's current temperature.
*   Enter the animal's current blood pressure.
*   If applicable, enter any medication being taken by the animal.
*   Add any additional notes about the animal's health status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Animal ID** (`animal_id`, number, optional): The unique identifier for the animal.
*   **Species** (`species`, select_one, optional): Select the species of the animal from the list.
*   **Condition** (`condition`, select_one, optional): Choose the animal's current condition from the options provided.
*   **Weight** (`weight`, number, optional): Enter the animal's current weight.
*   **Temperature** (`temperature`, number, optional): Enter the animal's current temperature.
*   **Blood Pressure** (`blood_pressure`, number, optional): Enter the animal's current blood pressure.
*   **Medication** (`medication`, text, optional): If the animal is taking any medication, enter the name and dosage of the medication.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about the animal's health status.
*   **Veterinarian** (`veterinarian`, text, optional): Enter the name of the veterinarian who last attended to the animal.

## Tips
*   Be as accurate and thorough as possible when filling out the form.
*   Ensure that all relevant information is included to help veterinary care professionals make informed decisions about the animal's care.
*   If you are unsure about any information, please contact the animal care team for guidance.
