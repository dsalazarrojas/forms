# Exotic Pet Owner Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for exotic pet owners to register their information and their pet's details. It will help us to keep a record of your pet's information for administrative, medical, or emergency purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Owner Information section with your personal details.
2. Provide your contact information in the Contact Information section.
3. Enter your exotic pet's details in the Pet Information section.
4. Sign and date the form with your owner's signature in the Owner Signature section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Owner Information (1)
* **Owner Name (Text)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Owner Email (Text)**: Provide your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Owner Phone Number (Text)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you easily.
* **Owner Address (Text)**: Enter your address for any potential home visits or deliveries.

### Contact Information (2)
* **Contact Name (Text)**: Who we should contact in case of an emergency or when we need additional information.
* **Contact Email (Text)**: The contact person's email address.
* **Contact Phone Number (Text)**: The contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Address (Text)**: The contact person's address.

### Pet Information (3)
* **Pet Name (Text)**: Your exotic pet's name.
* **Pet Type (Text)**: The type of exotic pet you own (e.g., snake, lizard, bird, etc.).
* **Pet Species (Text)**: The species of your pet (e.g., boa constrictor, green iguana, etc.).
* **Pet Age (Text)**: Enter your pet's age.
* **Pet Health Issue (Text)**: If your pet has any medical issues, please describe them.

### Owner Signature (4)
* **Owner Signature**: Please sign the form with your signature and date it.

## Tips
* Be sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* If you need any further assistance, we are here to help.
