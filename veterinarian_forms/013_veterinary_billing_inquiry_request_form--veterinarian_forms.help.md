# Veterinary Billing Inquiry Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help the veterinary clinic process your billing inquiry efficiently. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your inquiry is handled correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "What is your name?" field.
2. Describe your billing inquiry in the "What is your billing inquiry question?" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Select a date and time for your appointment in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
6. Answer "Yes" or "No" for "Select One" as required.
7. If you need to select multiple items, answer "Yes" or "No" for "Select Multiple".
8. Leave a note for the clinic staff to assist with processing your request in the "Note" field.
9. Enter your email address to confirm in the "Confirm Email" field.
10. Enter your phone number to confirm in the "Confirm Phone" fields.
11. Confirm your date of visit in the "Confirm Date" field.
12. Write a message for the clinic staff to process your request in the "Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name?** (user_name): Enter your name to help the clinic identify you.
* **What is your billing inquiry question?** (billing_inquiry_question): Describe your billing concern or question.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number): Enter your phone number to contact you.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address to send you updates.
* **Date** (date): Select a date for your appointment or visit.
* **Time** (time): Select a time for your appointment or visit.
* **Select One** (select_one): Choose "Yes" if your inquiry is for one specific reason, otherwise choose "No" or "Other".
* **Select Multiple** (select_multiple): Choose "Yes" if you have multiple items for your inquiry, otherwise choose "No" or "Other".
* **Note** (note): Leave a note to help the clinic staff process your request.
* **Confirm Email** (confirm_email): Enter your email address to confirm.
* **Confirm Phone** (confirm_phone): Enter your phone number to confirm.
* **Confirm Date** (confirm_date): Select a date to confirm your visit.
* **Message** (message): Write a message for the clinic staff to process your request.
* **Select Multiple 2** (select_multiple_2): Choose "Yes" if you have multiple items for your second inquiry, otherwise choose "No" or "Other". (Optional)
* **Select One 2** (select_one_2): Choose "Yes" if your second inquiry is for one specific reason, otherwise choose "No" or "Other". (Optional)
* **Message** (message): This field is required for the second set of multiple selection.
