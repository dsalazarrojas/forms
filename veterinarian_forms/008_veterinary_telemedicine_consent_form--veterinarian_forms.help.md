# Veterinary Telemedicine Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This Veterinary Telemedicine Consent Form is a crucial document that ensures pet owners understand the terms and conditions of telehealth services provided by our veterinary team. Completing this form correctly is essential for a smooth and secure telehealth experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review each field carefully and ensure you have filled out all required fields.
2.  For each question, please provide accurate and honest information.
3.  If you're unsure about any information, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet owner**: <label> (pet owner)</label>: Please fill in your name as the pet owner or caregiver responsible for your pet's care.
* **Owner info**: <label> (owner info)</label>: This field is for any additional information you'd like to provide about yourself or your pet. It could be anything from your contact info to medical history.
* **Telehealth agreement**: <label> (telehealth agreement)</label>:
	+ Please select all the options that apply to you. For example, if you've understood that our telehealth services are provided by licensed veterinarians, select that option.
* **Owner consent**: <label> (owner consent)</label>: Please select 'True' if you consent to participate in telehealth sessions.
* **Owner signature**: <label> (owner signature)</label>: Please provide your signature to indicate your consent.
* **Veterinarian signature**: <label> (veterinarian signature)</label>: This field is for the veterinarian or healthcare professional to sign once they've reviewed your telehealth agreement.
* **Date**: <label> (date)</label>: Please provide the date of completion of this form.
* **Time**: <label> (time)</label>: Enter the time of completion of this form.
* **Phone**: <label> (phone)</label>: Your contact phone number for follow-up or emergency purposes.
* **Email**: <label> (email)</label>: Your contact email address.

## Tips
- Ensure you have a working internet connection or reliable data connection for telehealth sessions.
- Respond promptly to your medical inquiries and emergencies during telehealth sessions.
- Ask questions during the sessions if you're unsure about anything.
