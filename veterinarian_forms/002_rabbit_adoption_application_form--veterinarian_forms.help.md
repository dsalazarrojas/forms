# Rabbit Adoption Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about you and your family to help us find the best match for your new furry friend. Please take a few minutes to fill out the form, and we'll use the information to make an informed decision about adopting a rabbit.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button once you have completed all the required fields.
2. Fill out the form in English, please.
3. Make sure to provide clear and accurate information for each field.
4. Review your submission before clicking "Submit".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (Required):
	+ Please enter your full name.
* **Address** (Required):
	+ Enter your complete address.
* **Email** (Required):
	+ Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (Required):
	+ Enter a contact number where we can reach you.
* **Species** (Required):
	+ Choose "Rabbit" if you are interested in adopting a rabbit.
	+ Choose "Other" if you are interested in adopting a different species.
* **Age** (Required):
	+ Enter your age.
* **Weight** (Required):
	+ Enter your weight in kilograms (kg).
* **Health Condition** (Required):
	+ Choose "Healthy" if you have no health conditions.
	+ Choose "Injured" if you have a health condition.
	+ Choose "Fur-bald" if you are unsure or have a fur-related condition.
* **Suitable for family with children** (Optional):
	+ Choose "Yes" if you have children and they are familiar with animals.
	+ Choose "No" if you do not have children or they are not familiar with animals.
	+ Choose "Unknown" if you are unsure or have no children.
* **Suitable for active lifestyle** (Optional):
	+ Choose "Yes" if you have an active lifestyle and can provide regular exercise for your pet.
	+ Choose "No" if you have a less active lifestyle.
	+ Choose "Unknown" if you are unsure.
* **Previous pet experience** (Optional):
	+ Choose "Yes" if you have experience with pets.
	+ Choose "No" if you have no experience with pets.
	+ Choose "Unknown" if you are unsure.
* **Previous pets lived with** (Optional):
	+ Choose "Yes" if you have previous pets.
	+ Choose "No" if you have no previous pets.
	+ Choose "Unknown" if you are unsure.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer each question truthfully.
- Provide accurate information to help us find the best match for you.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask us for help.
