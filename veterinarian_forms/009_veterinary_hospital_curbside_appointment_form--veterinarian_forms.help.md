# Veterinary Hospital Curbside Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Veterinary Hospital Curbside Appointment Form is designed to help our hospital staff schedule appointments for pet owners and their pets in a convenient and efficient manner. This form allows users to provide their contact information, pet details, and appointment preferences, enabling our staff to process and prepare for the appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields with the requested information. If you need assistance or have questions, do not hesitate to contact our staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Name**: <span class="required">Required</span> - Your name, which will be used for communication and processing.
  - Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
- **Pet Name**: <span class="optional">Optional</span> - The name of your pet, which helps our staff to identify and prepare for your pet's visit.
  - Enter the name of your pet as you have named it.
- **Pet Breed**: <span class="optional">Optional</span> - The breed of your pet, if known. This information helps our staff to better prepare for your pet's specific needs.
  - Enter your pet's breed if you know it, otherwise, you can skip this field.
- **Service Type**: <span class="optional">Optional</span> - Select the type of service you need for your pet, such as "Yes" for a regular check-up or "No" for an emergency visit.
  - Choose the option that best describes your pet's needs.
- **Service Time**: <span class="optional">Optional</span> - Enter the time you would like to schedule the appointment.
  - If you don't have a specific time in mind, our staff will contact you to schedule a convenient time.
- **Appointment Date**: <span class="optional">Optional</span> - Enter the date you would like to schedule the appointment.
  - If you don't have a specific date in mind, our staff will contact you to schedule a convenient time.
- **Pet Weight**: <span class="optional">Optional</span> - The weight of your pet, measured in pounds or kilograms.
  - Enter your pet's weight if you know it, otherwise, you can skip this field.
- **Phone Number**: <span class="optional">Optional</span> - Your contact phone number, where our staff can reach you for any updates or questions.
  - Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted.
- **Email**: <span class="optional">Optional</span> - Your email address, where our staff can send you updates or questions.
  - Enter your email address if you would like to receive email updates.
- **Address**: <span class="optional">Optional</span> - Your address, which helps our staff to prepare for your visit.
  - Enter your address if you would like to provide it, otherwise, you can skip this field.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a successful appointment.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our staff for assistance.
- Our staff will review and process your submission, and contact you with any further questions or requests.
