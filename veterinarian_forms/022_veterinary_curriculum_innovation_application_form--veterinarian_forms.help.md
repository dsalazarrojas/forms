# Veterinary Curriculum Innovation Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Curriculum Innovation Application Form is designed to gather proposals for innovative curriculum ideas from veterinarians, educators, or organizations. The form aims to collect detailed information about the proposal, including its title, description, and contact details. This form is used to encourage collaboration, innovation, and improvement in veterinary education and practice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the proposal details, including a clear and concise title and description of your innovative curriculum idea.
2. Provide additional details about your proposal in the second proposal details field.
3. Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number, so we can get in touch with you regarding your proposal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Proposal Details (Page 1)** (`proposal_details`, text, not required): Enter a clear and concise title and description of your innovative curriculum idea.
* **Proposal Details 2 (Page 2)** (`proposal_details_2`, text, not required): Provide additional details about your proposal to help us understand your innovative curriculum idea.
* **Contact Details (Page 3)** (`contact_details`, text, not required): Enter your name, email, and phone number so we can get in touch with you regarding your proposal.

## Tips
* Be specific and concise when filling out the proposal details fields.
* Use clear and descriptive language to help us understand your innovative curriculum idea.
* Make sure to provide all necessary contact information so we can get in touch with you.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
