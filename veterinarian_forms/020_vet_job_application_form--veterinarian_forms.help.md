# vet_job_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vet_job_application_form is designed to collect relevant information from applicants applying for veterinary jobs, allowing hiring managers and HR representatives to assess their qualifications and suitability for a position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, please follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form completely and accurately.
*   Ensure all required fields are filled out as this will help us assess your suitability for the role.
*   Please note that all fields are case sensitive.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, required: false): This section is meant to collect your contact information, such as your name, email address, phone number, and any other relevant details. Please provide the best contact method you have, so we can reach out to you easily.
*   **Personal Details** (Personal Details, text, required: true): This section is designed to collect information about your personal background, experience, and qualifications. Please provide a brief description of yourself, including any relevant experience or skills that align with the job requirements.
*   **Employment History** (Employment History, text, required: false): In this section, please provide information about your previous work experiences, including job titles, duration, and a brief description of your roles and responsibilities. Please note that this section is optional, but providing information here will help us assess your potential fit for the job.
*   **Education** (Education, text, required: true): This section requires information about your educational background, including degrees earned, institutions attended, and relevant courses or specializations. Please provide detailed information about your academic qualifications.
*   **Work Experience** (Work Experience, text, required: false): This section is meant to collect information about your work experience, including job titles, duration, and a brief description of your roles and responsibilities. Please note that this section is optional, but providing information here will help us assess your potential fit for the job.
*   **Professional Certifications** (Professional Certifications, text, required: true): This section requires information about any professional certifications you hold, including certification names, issuing bodies, and dates obtained. Please provide detailed information about your certifications and how they relate to the job requirements.
