# pet_owner_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about pet owners, including their demographics, habits, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of pet you have from the dropdown menu on page 1.
2. Enter your pet's age on page 2.
3. Provide your phone number on page 3.
4. Answer questions about your pet's habits, insurance, and additional comments on page 4.
5. Indicate the number of pets you have on page 5.
6. Enter your email address on page 6.
7. Add any additional comments or information on page 8.
8. Answer questions about your employment status and educational background on pages 9 and 10.
9. Enter your occupation on page 11.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What type of pet do you have** (`select_one`): Select one option from the dropdown menu to indicate the type of pet you have (cat, dog, or other).
* **What is your pet's age** (`number`): Enter the age of your pet.
* **Phone number** (`text`): Enter your phone number.
* **What is your pet's habits?** (`note`): Provide any additional information about your pet's habits.
* **Additional information** (`select_multiple`): Select all that apply:
	+ I have pet insurance
	+ I do not have pet insurance
	+ Other (please specify)
* **How many pets do you have** (`number`): Enter the number of pets you have.
* **Email address** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Additional comments** (`note`): Add any additional comments or information.
* **Do you work from home?** (`select_one`): Select one option from the dropdown menu to indicate whether you work from home (True or False).
* **What is your educational background?** (`select_multiple`): Select all that apply:
	+ High school
	+ Bachelor
	+ Master
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Occupation** (`text`): Enter your occupation.
* **What is your employment status?** (`select_one`): Select one option from the dropdown menu to indicate your employment status (True or False).

Note: This form is designed to be self-explanatory, so you should be able to fill it out without assistance. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
