# veterinary_client_record_copy_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The veterinary_client_record_copy_request_form is designed to help veterinarians collect information from pet owners about their requests for pet medical records or medical records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the pet owner's information in the **Pet Owner Info** field.
2. Provide details about the pet in the **Pet Info** field.
3. Select the type of records being requested in the **Request Type** field.
4. Enter the date of the request in the **Request Date** field.
5. Provide any relevant information about the veterinarian in the **Veterinarian Info** field.
6. Add any hospital-specific information in the **Hospital Info** field.
7. Enter the contact information for the pet owner in the **Contact Info** field.
8. Add any additional medical information about the pet in the **Pet Medical Info** field.
9. Provide detailed information about the request in the **Request Details** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Owner Info (pet_owner_info)** (Text, Required): Enter the name, address, phone number, and any other relevant information about the pet owner.
* **Pet Info (pet_info)** (Text, Optional): Enter any relevant information about the pet, such as the pet's age, breed, and medical history.
* **Request Type (request_type)** (Select One, Optional): Choose between **Pet Medical Record Request** or **Medical Records Request** to indicate the type of records being requested.
* **Request Date (request_date)** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the records are requested.
* **Veterinarian Info (veterinarian_info)** (Text, Optional): Enter any relevant information about the veterinarian who provided care to the pet.
* **Hospital Info (hospital_info)** (Text, Optional): Enter any relevant information about the hospital where the pet received treatment.
* **Contact Info (contact_info)** (Text, Optional): Enter the pet owner's contact information, such as their phone number and email address.
* **Pet Medical Info (pet_medical_info)** (Text, Optional): Enter any relevant medical information about the pet.
* **Request Details (request_details)** (Text, Optional): Provide detailed information about the request, including the reason for requesting the records and any other relevant context.
