# Pet Emergency Contact Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Pet Emergency Contact Information" form is designed to gather critical information about pets in emergency situations. This form is completed by pet owners or veterinarians to record contact information and details about their pets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the pet owner's information, including their name, contact phone and email (if applicable).
2.  Provide the veterinary clinic's details.
3.  Answer the question about emergency contacts. If yes, fill in the emergency contact's name, relation, phone, and email (if applicable).
4.  Fill in the pet's details such as name, type, breed, age, and weight.
5.  If necessary, provide medical information about the pet, including medical condition and medical history.
6.  If necessary, provide any special instructions for the pet's care.
7.  Finally, review and submit the form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Pet Owner Info
*   **Pet Owner Info** (`pet_owner_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who owns the pet.
*   **Veterinary Clinic** (`veterinary_clinic`, text, required/optional): Enter the details of the veterinary clinic that the pet visits.
*   **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, select multiple, required/optional): Select whether you have emergency contacts for the pet.
*   **Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_1_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the first emergency contact.
*   **Relation** (`emergency_contact_1_relation`, text, required/optional): Enter the relation of the first emergency contact (e.g., parent, guardian, etc.).
*   **Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_1_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the first emergency contact.
*   **Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_1_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email of the first emergency contact (if applicable).
*   **Contact Name 2** (`emergency_contact_2_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the second emergency contact.
*   **Relation 2** (`emergency_contact_2_relation`, text, required/optional): Enter the relation of the second emergency contact (e.g., parent, guardian, etc.).
*   **Contact Phone 2** (`emergency_contact_2_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the second emergency contact.
*   **Contact Email 2** (`emergency_contact_2_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email of the second emergency contact (if applicable).
*   **Pet Name** (`pet_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the pet.
*   **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, text, required/optional): Enter the type of the pet (e.g., dog, cat, etc.).
*   **Pet Breed** (`pet_breed`, text, required/optional): Enter the breed of the pet.
*   **Pet Age** (`pet_age`, number, required/optional): Enter the age of the pet.
*   **Pet Weight** (`pet_weight`, number, required/optional): Enter the weight of the pet.
*   **Medical Info** (`medical_info`, text, required/optional): Provide any medical information about the pet.
*   **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required/optional): Enter any medical conditions the pet has.
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required/optional): Enter any relevant medical history of the pet.
*   **Medications** (`vet_medication_allergies`, text, required/optional): Enter any medications the pet is allergic to.
*   **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, note, required/optional): Enter any special instructions for the pet's care.
*   **Vet Notes** (`vet_notes`, text, required/optional): Enter any notes from the veterinarian.

### Tips

*   Be sure to fill in all required fields.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it.
*   If you're unsure about any information, consult with the veterinarian or pet owner for clarification.
*   Make sure to update the form regularly to reflect any changes in pet information.
