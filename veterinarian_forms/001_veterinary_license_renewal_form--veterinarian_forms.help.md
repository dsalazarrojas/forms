# Veterinary License Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary License Renewal Form is designed to help update your veterinary license status. This form will ask for your personal and professional information, as well as your practice details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Start by filling out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your personal and professional information, including your first and last name, address, city, state, and zip code.
3. If applicable, select your veterinary school from the list of options.
4. Indicate whether you hold a veterinary technician license or a veterinarian license.
5. Enter your practice information, including practice name, address, city, state, and zip code.
6. Finally, review your form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **optional**): Enter your first name as it is written on your license.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **optional**): Enter your last name as it is written on your license.
* **Address** (`address`, text, **optional**): Enter your address as it is written on your license.
* **City** (`city`, text, **optional**): Enter your city as it is written on your license.
* **State** (`state`, select_one, **optional**): Select your state of residence from the list of options.
* **License Number** (`license_number`, number, **required**): Enter your veterinary license number.
* **License Expiration Date** (`license_expiration_date`, date, **optional**): Enter your license expiration date, if applicable.
* **Veterinary School Attended** (`veterinary_school_attended`, text, **optional**): Enter the name of the veterinary school you attended.
* **Veterinary Technician License** (`veterinary_technician_license`, select_one, **optional**): Indicate whether you hold a veterinary technician license or not.
* **Veterinarian License** (`veterinarian_license`, select_multiple, **optional**): Indicate your current veterinarian license status (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).
* **Renewal Date** (`renewal_date`, date, **optional**): Enter your license renewal date, if applicable.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, **optional**): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, **optional**): Enter your contact email address.
* **Address 2** (`address2`, text, **optional**): Enter your practice address 2, if applicable.
* **Zip** (`zip`, number, **optional**): Enter your practice zip code.
* **Practice Name** (`practice_name`, text, **optional**): Enter your practice name.
* **Practice Address** (`practice_address`, text, **optional**): Enter your practice address.
* **Practice City** (`practice_city`, text, **optional**): Enter your practice city.
* **Practice State** (`practice_state`, select_one, **optional**): Select your practice state from the list of options.
* **Practice Zip** (`practice_zip`, number, **optional**): Enter your practice zip code.
* **Practice Phone** (`practice_phone`, text, **optional**): Enter your practice phone number.
