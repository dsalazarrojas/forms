# Animal Transfer Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Animal Transfer Form" is a crucial document for the transfer of animals between parties. It captures essential information about the animal, the transfer reason, and other details related to the transfer process. This form ensures a smooth and transparent transfer process for all parties involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about the animal and the transfer process.
2. Fill in the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of Transfer**: Enter the date when the animal will be transferred to the new owner or location. (ID: 1, Required: True, Type: Date)
* **Transferring Party Information**: Provide the name and contact details of the party transferring the animal. (ID: 2, Required: False, Type: Note)
* **Full Name**: Enter the full name of the transferring party. (ID: 3, Required: True, Type: Text)
* **Address**: Enter the address of the party transferring the animal. (ID: 4, Required: True, Type: Text)
* **City**: Enter the city where the animal is being transferred from. (ID: 5, Required: True, Type: Text)
* **State**: Enter the state where the animal is being transferred from. (ID: 6, Required: True, Type: Text)
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the party transferring the animal. (ID: 7, Required: True, Type: Text)
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the party transferring the animal. (ID: 8, Required: False, Type: Email)
* **Receiving Party Information**: Provide the name and contact details of the party receiving the animal. (ID: 9, Required: False, Type: Note)
* **Receiving Party Name 2**: Enter the full name of the receiving party. (ID: 10, Required: True, Type: Text)
* **Receiving Party Address**: Enter the address where the animal will be transferred to. (ID: 11, Required: True, Type: Text)
* **Receiving Party City**: Enter the city where the animal will be transferred to. (ID: 12, Required: True, Type: Text)
* **Receiving Party State**: Enter the state where the animal will be transferred to. (ID: 13, Required: True, Type: Text)
* **Receiving Party Phone**: Enter the phone number of the party receiving the animal. (ID: 14, Required: True, Type: Text)
* **Receiving Party Email**: Enter the email address of the party receiving the animal. (ID: 15, Required: False, Type: Email)
* **Animal Name**: Enter the name of the animal being transferred. (ID: 16, Required: True, Type: Text)
* **Species**: Select the species of the animal being transferred (ID: 17, Required: True, Type: Select One)
* **Breed**: Enter the breed of the animal (ID: 18, Required: False, Type: Text)
* **Age**: Enter the age of the animal (ID: 19, Required: True, Type: Text)
* **Gender**: Select the gender of the animal (ID: 20, Required: True, Type: Select One)
* **Color or Markings**: Enter any notable color or markings on the animal (ID: 21, Required: False, Type: Text)
* **Weight**: Enter the weight of the animal (ID: 22, Required: False, Type: Number)
* **Microchip Number**: Enter the microchip number of the animal (ID: 23, Required: False, Type: Text)
* **Medical History**: Provide any medical history of the animal (ID: 24, Required: False, Type: Note)
* **Known Medical Conditions**: Enter any known medical conditions of the animal (ID: 25, Required: False, Type: Text)
* **Current Medications**: Enter any current medications the animal is on (ID: 26, Required: False, Type: Text)
* **Allergies or Dietary Restrictions**: Enter any allergies or dietary restrictions of the animal (ID: 27, Required: False, Type: Text)
* **Reason for Transfer**: Select the reason for the transfer (ID: 28, Required: True, Type: Select One)
* **Additional Details**: Provide any additional details about the transfer (ID: 29, Required: False, Type: Text)
* **Behavioral Information**: Provide any behavioral information about the animal (ID: 30, Required: False, Type: Note)
* **Behavioral Notes**: Enter any behavioral notes about the animal (ID: 31, Required: False, Type: Text)
* **Known Issues**: Enter any known issues with the animal (ID: 32, Required: False, Type: Text)
* **Special Care or Training Needs**: Enter any special care or training needs of the animal (ID: 33, Required: False, Type: Text)
* **Acknowledgment and Agreement**: This section is for the receiving party to acknowledge and agree to the transfer terms. (ID: 34, Required: False, Type: Note)
* **Receiving Party Acknowledges Receipt**: Select whether the receiving party acknowledges receipt of the animal (ID: 35, Required: True, Type: Select One)
* **Receiving Party Acknowledges Condition**: Select whether the receiving party acknowledges the animal's condition (ID: 36, Required: True, Type: Select One)
* **Transferring Party Released from Liability**: Select whether the transferring party has released from liability (ID: 37, Required: True, Type: Select One)
* **Additional Notes**: Provide any additional notes about the transfer (ID: 38, Required: False, Type: Text)

Please note that the fields marked as required must be filled in for the form to be valid. The form is designed to be completed in consultation with the transferring and receiving parties to ensure accuracy and a smooth transfer process.
