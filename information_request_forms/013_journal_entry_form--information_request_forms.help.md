# Journal Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Journal Entry Form is a critical tool for healthcare professionals to document and track patient interactions. This form captures essential details about each interaction, including patient information, service specifics, and submission details for the person submitting the form.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the patient's name in the `patient_name` field. This is a required field and must be filled out accurately to ensure correct patient identification.
2. Select the `date_of_service` and `time_of_service` fields to record the date and time of the interaction.
3. Enter the location where the service was performed in the `location_of_service` field.
4. In the `journal_entry` field, provide a detailed description of the patient interaction, including any notable events or discussions.
5. Select the patient's status as `Active` or `Inactive` in the `patient_status` field.
6. Indicate whether the patient has insurance in the `insurance_info` field.
7. Enter any billing information in the `billing_info` field (optional).
8. Provide any comments about the interaction in the `comments` field (optional).
9. Select the tool used to perform the service from the `assigned_tool` field.
10. Enter your name in the `submitted_by` field.
11. Enter the date of submission in the `submit_date` field.
12. Select the time of submission in the `submitted_time` field.
13. Enter your name in the `submitter` field.
14. Enter your email address in the `submitter_email` field.
15. Enter your phone number in the `submitter_phone` field.
16. Enter your fax number (optional).
17. Enter your address in the `submitter_address` field.
18. Enter your city in the `submitter_city` field.
19. Enter your state in the `submitter_state` field.
20. Enter your zip code in the `submitter_zip` field.
21. Enter your country in the `submitter_country` field.
22. Enter any additional notes about the submission in the `submitter_notes` field (optional).
23. Enter any comments about the submission in the `submitter_comments` field (optional).
