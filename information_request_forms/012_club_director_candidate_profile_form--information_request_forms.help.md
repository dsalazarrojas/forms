# Club Director Candidate Profile Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Club Director Candidate Profile Form is designed to gather essential information from candidates vying for the position of club director. It will help the selection committee evaluate their suitability, vision, leadership experience, and other relevant skills. Please fill out this form to showcase your qualifications and demonstrate your commitment to the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (marked as "required") in each section. These fields are essential for a comprehensive evaluation.
2. Provide accurate and honest information in all sections.
3. Ensure the form is filled out in a clear and legible manner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Full Name (1)** (`Candidate Full Name`, text, **required**):
  Provide your full name as you would like it to appear in official communications.
* **Email Address (2)** (`candidate_email`, email, **required**):
  Enter your official email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Phone Number (3)** (`candidate_phone`, text, **required**):
  Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Current Position in Club (4)** (`current_position`, text, **required**):
  Describe your current role or position within the club.
* **Years Involved with Club (5)** (`years_in_club`, number, **required**):
  Indicate the number of years you have been involved with the club.
* **Previous Leadership Experience (6)** (`leadership_experience`, text, **required**):
  Describe any leadership roles or positions you have held in the past.
* **Vision for Club Direction (7)** (`vision_statement`, text, **required**):
  Outline your vision for the club's future direction and goals.
* **Key Initiatives You Would Propose (8)** (`key_initiatives`, text, **required**):
  Describe the key initiatives or projects you would focus on as club director.
* **Key Strengths (9)** (`strengths`, text, **required**):
  List your top 3-5 strengths that would be beneficial as club director.
* **Areas for Development (10)** (`areas_development`, text, **optional**):
  Indicate areas where you would like to improve or develop your skills.
* **Available Time Commitment (11)** (`time_commitment`, number, **required**):
  Estimate the number of hours per week you can dedicate to the role.
* **Financial Management Experience (12)** (`financial_management`, select_one, **required**):
  Rate your financial management experience as No Experience, Basic, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert.
* **Conflict Resolution Skills (13)** (`conflict_resolution`, select_one, **required**):
  Rate your conflict resolution skills as Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent.
* **Communication Skills (14)** (`communication_skills`, select_one, **required**):
  Rate your communication skills as Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent.
* **References (15)** (`references`, text, **optional**):
  Provide names and contacts of 2-3 relevant references.
