# Business Agility Advisory Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is for individuals seeking business agility advice or services. It aims to gather information about your organization, your current state and goals, and your preferences for contact and engagement. The data collected will help us tailor our advice and services to your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each page carefully before proceeding.
2. Fill out all required fields.
3. For fields with multiple options, select the most relevant choice.
4. If a field is not relevant to your situation, leave it blank.
5. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (ID: 2): Your full name, including title and surname.
* **Position** (ID: 3): Your job title or position within your organization.
* **Organization** (ID: 4): The name of your organization.
* **Email Address** (ID: 5): Your work email address.
* **Phone Number** (ID: 6): Your best contact number.
* **Industry** (ID: 8): Select the industry sector that best describes your organization.
* **Organization Size** (ID: 9): Choose the number of employees in your organization.
* **Primary Location** (ID: 10): The location of your organization's headquarters or main office.
* **Agility Needs** (ID: 11): Briefly describe why you are seeking business agility advice or services.
* **Current Challenges** (ID: 12): Select all challenges your organization is currently facing.
* **Agility Goals** (ID: 13): Describe what you hope to achieve through business agility advice or services.
* **Current Methodology** (ID: 14): Select the methodologies you currently use within your organization.
* **Agility Transformation Stage** (ID: 15): Choose where you are in your organization's agility journey.
* **Services Interested In** (ID: 16): Select all services you are interested in or would like to inquire about.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (ID: 18): Choose how you would like to be contacted.
* **Preferred Contact Time** (ID: 19): Select the best time to reach you.
* **Best Days to Contact** (ID: 20): Choose the days you are available to be contacted.
* **Timeline for Engagement** (ID: 21): Select when you are looking to start the engagement process.
* **Budget Authority** (ID: 22): Indicate your authority to make purchasing decisions.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (ID: 23): Describe how you found out about this service.
* **Additional Comments** (ID: 24): Add any further information or clarification.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
* Avoid using jargon or technical terms not familiar to business agility professionals.
* If you are unsure about a question, choose the most likely option or leave it blank.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
