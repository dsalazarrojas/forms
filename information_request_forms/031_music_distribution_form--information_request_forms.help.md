# music_distribution_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from musicians who wish to distribute their music to various platforms, such as online streaming services, radio stations, or music libraries. The form helps musicians provide their contact details, album information, and song details to facilitate the distribution process.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form sections to ensure you're providing all the necessary information.
2. Fill in your musician information, including your name, contact details, and any additional notes.
3. Provide details about your album, including its title, artist name, and any relevant notes.
4. Enter information about your song, including its title, artist name, and any relevant notes.
5. Specify the publishing rights you're seeking, such as mechanical, master, or synchronization rights.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Musician Information** (`musician_info`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your name and contact details as a musician. This includes your name, email address, and phone number. This information will help us contact you about your music distribution.
* **Album Information** (`album_info`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide details about your album, including its title and artist name. This will help us understand the context of your music distribution request.
* **Song Information** (`song_info`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter information about your song, including its title and artist name. This will help us identify specific songs for distribution.
* **Publishing Rights** (`publishing_rights`, `text`, required: `false`): Specify the publishing rights you're seeking for your music. This can include mechanical, master, or synchronization rights, depending on your needs.
