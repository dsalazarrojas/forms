# ประก IV Flow Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "ประก IV Flow Log Form" is a medical record-keeping form used to document IV fluid administration events. It captures essential information about patients, IV treatment details, and related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the required information and permissions to access patient data.
2. Choose the correct fields to fill in, depending on your role and task.
3. Fill in your selections or inputs accordingly, following the guidelines below for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Patient ID
• **Patient ID (1)**: Enter the unique identifier of the patient being treated, if any.
### Patient Name
• **Patient Name (2)**: Enter the patient's name, if known.
### IV Date
• **IV Date (3)**: Input the date of the IV fluid administration event.
### Nurse ID
• **Nurse ID (4)**: Select the ID of the nurse responsible for administering the IV fluid, if applicable.
### IV Fluid
• **IV Fluid (5)**: Describe the type or composition of the IV fluid used.
### Rate
• **Rate (6)**: Record the administration rate of the IV fluid (e.g., rate of infusion).
### Volume
• **Volume (7)**: Log the total volume of IV fluid administered.
### Start Time
• **Start Time (8)**: Input the start time of IV fluid administration.
### Total
• **Total (9)**: Record the total volume of IV fluid administered, if required.
### Comments
• **Comments (10)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the IV treatment.
### Status
• **Status (11)**: Set the status of the IV flow record as Active, Inactive, or Pending.
### Comments
• **Comments (12)**: Enter any additional comments or remarks about the event.

## Tips
- Double-check your inputs for accuracy and completeness before saving the form.
- Ensure you have the necessary permissions and clearance to access patient data.
- If unsure about any field, refer to your institution's medical guidelines or seek guidance from a medical professional.
