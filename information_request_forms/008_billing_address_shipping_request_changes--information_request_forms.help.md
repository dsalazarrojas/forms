# Billing Address Shipping Request Changes - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request changes to a customer's billing address or shipping address. Please provide accurate and up-to-date information to facilitate a smooth address change process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required fields to request changes to your billing address or shipping address.
2. Review the form carefully to ensure all fields are complete and accurate.
3. Submit the form to initiate the address change process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Information
• **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
• **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
• **Email Address**: Enter the email associated with your account for verification and communication.
• **Phone Number**: Enter your contact number for verification purposes.
• **Account Number**: Enter your customer account number for identification.
### Address Change Details
• **Request Type**: Select the type of address change you are requesting.
• **Type of Change**: Choose the type of change you are making (Billing, Shipping, or both).
• **Reason for Change**: Select the reason for the address change (e.g., moved, incorrect address on file, temporary change).
• **Current Billing Address**: Enter the current billing address associated with your account.
• **New Billing Address**: Enter the updated billing address you wish to use.
• **New City, State, ZIP Code**: Enter the city, state, and ZIP code for the new billing address.
### Shipping Address Change
• **Current Shipping Address**: Enter the current shipping address associated with your account.
• **New Shipping Address**: Enter the updated shipping address you wish to use.
• **New City, State, ZIP Code**: Enter the city, state, and ZIP code for the new shipping address.
### Additional Information
• **Temporary End Date**: If this is a temporary address change, enter the date when the old address will be restored.
• **Affected Orders**: Select how this change should apply to pending orders (all, future, or specific order numbers).
• **Specific Order Numbers**: If applicable, list the order numbers affected by this change.
• **Notification Preference**: Choose how you would like to be confirmed of this change (email, SMS, phone call, or no confirmation).
• **Verification Code**: Enter the code sent to your phone or email for verification.
• **Authorization**: Confirm your authorization to make this change and accuracy of the information provided.
