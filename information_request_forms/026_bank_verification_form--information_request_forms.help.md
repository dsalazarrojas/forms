# Bank Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bank Verification Form is used to request verification of bank account information from a financial institution. This form is used for various purposes such as loan applications, mortgage applications, rental applications, and government benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the required information for the account holder, including their name, email, phone number, mailing address, date of birth, social security number, and verification type.
2.  Select the type of verification needed, such as account ownership or account balance.
3.  Enter the account number and type (e.g., checking, savings, etc.).
4.  Select the bank name and branch location (if applicable).
5.  Specify the verification purpose and the organization requesting the verification.
6.  Enter the contact information for the requesting organization, including their name, email, and phone number (if applicable).
7.  Choose the delivery method and authorization scope of the verification.
8.  Select the authorization duration and whether you authorize the verification.
9.  Provide any additional comments or information.
10.  Date the signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Account Holder Full Name** (`account_holder_name`, text, required): Enter the account holder's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`account_holder_email`, email, required): Enter the account holder's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`account_holder_phone`, text, required): Enter the account holder's phone number.
* **Mailing Address** (`account_holder_address`, text, required): Enter the account holder's mailing address, including street, city, state, and ZIP.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the account holder's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, text, required): Enter the last 4 digits of the account holder's social security number.
* **Verification Type** (`verification_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of verification needed, such as account ownership or account balance.
* **Account Number** (`account_number`, text, required): Enter the account number to be verified.
* **Account Type** (`account_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of account, such as checking or savings.
* **Bank Name** (`bank_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the financial institution.
* **Bank Branch** (`bank_branch`, text, not required): Enter the branch location of the financial institution (if applicable).
* **Verification Purpose** (`verification_purpose`, select_one, required): Select the purpose of the verification, such as loan application or rental application.
* **Requesting Organization** (`requesting_organization`, text, required): Enter the name of the organization requesting the verification.
* **Requesting Contact Name** (`requesting_contact_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the contact person for the requesting organization.
* **Requesting Contact Email** (`requesting_contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person for the requesting organization.
* **Requesting Contact Phone** (`requesting_contact_phone`, text, not required): Enter the phone number of the contact person for the requesting organization.
* **Verification Deadline** (`verification_deadline`, date, not required): Enter the deadline for the verification (if applicable).
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select_one, required): Select the method of delivery, such as direct to organization or to account holder.
* **Authorization Scope** (`authorization_scope`, select_multiple, required): Select the scope of authorization, such as account balance or transaction history.
* **Authorization Duration** (`authorization_duration`, select_one, required): Select the duration of the authorization, such as one-time use or 90 days.
* **Authorization Signature** (`authorization_signature`, select_one, required): Select whether you authorize the verification.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Date the signature.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, not required): Enter any additional comments or information.
