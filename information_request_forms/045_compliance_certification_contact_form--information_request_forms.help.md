# Compliance and Certification Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Compliance and Certification Contact Form" is designed for organizations seeking guidance or certification on compliance matters. This form will help us understand your needs and provide you with relevant information and support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your contact and organization details.
2. Provide information about your current compliance status and certification interests.
3. Specify your preferred contact method and available time slots.
4. Share any additional information or questions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inquiry Date** (`inquiry_date`): Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Type your full name, as we'll use it for communication.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your organization's email address where you'd like to be contacted.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Add your organization's phone number.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`): Enter your organization's name.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`): Provide your organization's mailing address.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`): Choose your organization's employee count:
	+ 1 to 10 employees
	+ 11 to 50 employees
	+ 51 to 200 employees
	+ 201 to 500 employees
	+ Over 500 employees
* **Industry** (`industry`): Select your industry sector:
	+ Technology
	+ Healthcare
	+ Finance
	+ Manufacturing
	+ Construction
	+ Retail
	+ Services
	+ Government
	+ Other
* **Type of Inquiry** (`inquiry_type`): Indicate what you're inquiring about:
	+ Compliance certification
	+ Compliance audit
	+ Compliance training
	+ Policy development
	+ Regulatory guidance
	+ Certification renewal
	+ General information
	+ Other
* **Certification of Interest** (`certification_interest`): Choose the certification you're interested in:
	+ ISO 9001 - Quality Management
	+ ISO 14001 - Environmental
	+ ISO 27001 - Information Security
	+ ISO 45001 - Occupational Health and Safety
	+ SOC 2
	+ HIPAA Compliance
	+ GDPR Compliance
	+ Industry-specific
	+ Not sure
	+ Other
* **Current Compliance Status** (`current_compliance_status`): Describe your current compliance status:
	+ Not started - need guidance
	+ Planning phase
	+ Implementation in progress
	+ Ready for audit
	+ Already certified - renewal
	+ Previously certified - lapsed
* **Desired Timeline** (`timeline`): Choose your preferred timeline for certification:
	+ Urgent - within 1 month
	+ Soon - 1 to 3 months
	+ Moderate - 3 to 6 months
	+ Flexible - 6 to 12 months
	+ Planning - over 12 months
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`): Select your estimated budget:
	+ Under $5000
	+ $5000 to $15000
	+ $15001 to $30000
	+ $30001 to $50000
	+ Over $50000
	+ Not determined
* **Previous Certifications** (`previous_certifications`): Indicate if you've been certified before:
	+ Yes - same certification
	+ Yes - different certification
	+ No - first time
	+ Not sure
* **Specific Requirements** (`specific_requirements`): Add any special needs or requirements.
* **Relevant Regulatory Bodies** (`regulatory_bodies`): List any relevant regulatory bodies.
* **Current Compliance Challenges** (`pain_points`): Describe any current compliance challenges.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`): Choose your preferred contact method:
	+ Email
	+ Phone call
	+ Video conference
	+ In-person meeting
	+ No preference
* **Best Time to Contact** (`best_contact_time`): Select the best time to contact you:
	+ Morning - 8am to 12pm
	+ Afternoon - 12pm to 5pm
	+ Evening - 5pm to 8pm
	+ Any time
	+ Specific time in comments
* **Are You the Decision Maker?** (`decision_maker`): Confirm if you're the decision maker:
	+ Yes - I decide
	+ Yes - with team input
	+ No - I am researching
	+ No - I will forward information
* **Other Stakeholders** (`other_stakeholders`): List any other stakeholders involved.
* **How Did You Hear About Us?** (`how_heard_about`): Choose where you heard about us:
	+ Website
	+ Referral
	+ Conference or event
	+ Social media
	+ Search engine
	+ Advertisement
	+ Other
* **Referral Source** (`referral_source`): Enter the referral source.
* **Additional Services of Interest** (`additional_services`): Select any additional services you might need:
	+ Compliance training
	+ Policy development
	+ Internal audits
	+ Gap analysis
	+ Consulting
	+ Software solutions
	+ Ongoing support
	+ Other
* **Your Questions** (`questions`): Add any questions you have.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Add any other information.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when answering each field to ensure we can better understand your needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please let us know.
* We'll be in touch as soon as we receive your submission.
