# Spray Tanning Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to provide information for spray tanning procedures. It is meant to be completed by customers in order to ensure that we have all the necessary details for a safe and effective service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as instructed. Make sure to provide accurate and truthful information, especially when it comes to medical history and allergies. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_information`, `text`, required: false): This is the section for you to provide your basic information.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your last name.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your address.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Please provide a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Please provide your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, `date`, required: false): Please provide the date you plan to visit us for the spray tanning procedure.
* **Contact Prefernce** (`contact_prefernce`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your contact preference (e.g. phone, email, or in-person).
* **Contact Preferred** (`contact_preferred`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select how you would like to be contacted (e.g. yes, no).
* **Spray Tanning Product** (`spray_tanning_product`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the type of spray tanning product you would like to use (e.g. bronze, dark, light).
* **Skin Type** (`skin_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select your skin type (e.g. fair, medium, dark, very dark).
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Please provide any relevant medical history or conditions.
* **Medication** (`medication`, `text`, required: false): Please list any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, required: false): Please list any allergies you have.
* **Tanning History** (`tanning_history`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select if you have any tanning history (e.g. yes, no).
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, required: false): Please provide any medical conditions you have.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select if you consent to the terms and conditions of our service.
* **Consent 2** (`consent_2`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is not clear, but please select the correct option (if it's a valid field, please clarify its purpose).

Note: This form is not a comprehensive medical history form, but it is used to collect some relevant information. If you have any concerns, please contact us instead of filling out this form.
