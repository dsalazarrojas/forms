# Marine Industry Advocacy Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Marine Industry Advocacy Form is used to report concerns or issues affecting the marine industry. Please fill out this form to provide us with information about a specific issue you are experiencing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant option for the `Contact Information` field to provide us with your contact details.
2. Briefly describe the issue in the `Briefly describe the issue` field.
3. Select all relevant `Stakeholder Perspectivs` that apply to your issue.
4. If applicable, upload any `Supporting Documents` that will help us better understand the issue.
5. Indicate the location of the issue in the `Issue Location` field.
6. Provide your contact details in the `Contact Details` field.
7. Enter the date and time of the issue in the `Issue Date` and `Issue Time` fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Marine Industry Advocacy Form** (`introduction`, text, required: false): This is the title of the form, and you do not need to fill out this field.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, select_one, required: false): Please select your contact information from the options provided. This will help us get in touch with you regarding your issue.
* **Briefly describe the issue** (`issue_brief`, text, required: false): Please provide a clear and concise description of the issue you are experiencing. This will help us understand the problem better.
* **Stakeholder Perspectivs** (`stakeholder_perspectives`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the stakeholder perspectivs that apply to your issue.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, note, required: false): If you have any documents that support your claim, please upload them here. This will help us better understand the issue.
* **Issue Location** (`issue_location`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate the location where the issue occurred.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): Please provide your contact details so we can get in touch with you regarding your issue.
* **Issue Date** (`issue_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the issue.
* **Issue Time** (`issue_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the issue.
