# Website Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from website planners, developers, and stakeholders to better understand their website planning needs and requirements. It will help us gather essential details about the website's purpose, target audience, and features. By completing this form, you'll be providing valuable insights that will inform our planning and development process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name**, which will help us address you personally.
2. Enter your **Last Name** to provide a full name for our records.
3. Enter your **Email** address to ensure we can contact you about your website planning.
4. Enter your **Website URL** if you already have a website, or leave this field blank if you're planning a new website.
5. Enter your **Company** name to provide context about your business or organization.
6. Enter your **Phone** number to facilitate communication and provide an additional contact method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: This is your personal name, which will be used to address you in our communication.
* **Last Name**: This is your family name, which will help us identify you in our records.
* **Email**: This is your email address, which we'll use to contact you about your website planning and development process.
* **Website URL**: If you already have a website, enter the URL so we can review its current state. If you're planning a new website, you can leave this field blank.
* **Company**: This is your company or organization name, which will help us understand your business context.
* **Phone**: This is your phone number, which we can use for communication and to provide additional contact options.

## Tips
* Please ensure you fill out all required fields (indicated by the "required" label).
* If you're unsure about any field, you can always ask us for clarification.
* If you need help with the form or have questions, feel free to contact us at your convenience.
