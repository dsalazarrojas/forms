# Citizen Profile Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Citizen Profile Form is designed to collect personal information from citizens to help the government better understand their needs and provide tailored services. This form is required for all citizens who wish to access government services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please ensure that you have the necessary information and documents ready before starting the form.**
2. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
3. Fill out each section carefully, providing accurate and truthful information.
4. Review and submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): This is the name given to you at birth. If you have a middle name, please include it if possible.
* **Middle Name** (`middle_name`, `text`, optional): If you have a middle name, please provide it.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): This is the surname given to you at birth.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): A valid email address is required to receive government updates and notifications.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Your phone number will be used to contact you for important updates and notifications.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): This is the date you were born.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required): Please select your gender (Male, Female, Other, or Prefer not to say).
* **Street Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Provide your current street address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): The city where you reside.
* **State or Province** (`state`, `text`, required): The state or province where you reside.
* **Zip or Postal Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): The zip or postal code of your address.
* **Country** (`country`, `text`, required): The country where you reside.
* **Citizen ID Number** (`citizen_id`, `text`, optional): If you have a citizen ID number, please provide it.
* **Nationality** (`nationality`, `text`, required): Your nationality or country of origin.
* **Marital Status** (`marital_status`, `select_one`, required): Please select your current marital status (Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Separated, Domestic partnership).
* **Highest Education Level** (`education_level`, `select_one`, required): Please select your highest education level achieved (High school, Some college, Associate degree, Bachelor's degree, Master's degree, Doctorate, Other).
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, `text`, required): Your current occupation or profession.
* **Employer Name** (`employer`, `text`, optional): If you are employed, please provide your employer's name.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): The name of the person you want to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): The phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `select_one`, required): Please select your relationship with the emergency contact person (Spouse, Parent, Sibling, Child, Friend, Other).
* **Government Services Needed** (`services_needed`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the government services you need (Voting registration, Tax services, License renewal, Benefits enrollment, Permit applications, Records request, Other).
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact`, `select_one`, required): Please select how you prefer to be contacted (Email, Phone, Mail, Text message).
* **Receive Government Updates Newsletter** (`newsletter_signup`, `select_one`, required): If you want to receive government updates and notifications, please select "True".
* **Any Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or information, please provide them here.
