# solar_system_self_survey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of the solar system self-survey form is to collect information from users about their personal details and solar system preferences. This form is used to understand user demographics and preferences within the context of the solar system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in the Personal Information page.
2. Provide your demographics, which may include details about your age, location, and other personal details.
3. Describe your solar system preferences, such as your favorite type of solar system and any specific features you enjoy.
4. Answer rating questions, which will help us understand your opinions about different aspects of the solar system.
5. You may also have the opportunity to provide additional information not covered in the previous questions.
6. If applicable, answer more rating questions for further understanding.
7. Share any additional text that you feel is relevant to your experience or thoughts about the solar system.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Information (Personal Information, text, optional)**: This page collects basic information about you, such as your name, address, and contact details.
* **Demographics (Demographics, text, optional)**: This page is used to gather information about your age, location, and any other relevant demographic details.
* **Solar System Preferences (Solar System Preferences, text, optional)**: This page is for you to describe your favorite type of solar system and any specific features you enjoy in a solar system.
* **Rating Questions (Rating Questions, text, optional)**: Answer rating questions that will help us understand your opinions about different aspects of the solar system.
* **Additional Information (Additional Information, text, optional)**: Use this page to provide any additional details that you feel are relevant to your experience or thoughts about the solar system.
* **More Rating Questions (More Rating Questions, text, optional)**: If applicable, answer more rating questions for further understanding.
* **More Text Sharing (More Text Sharing, text, optional)**: Share any additional text that you feel is relevant to your experience or thoughts about the solar system.
