# Mentorship Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mentorship Agreement Form is used to establish a clear understanding between a mentor and a mentee about the terms of their mentoring relationship. This form helps ensure that both parties are on the same page and understand their roles and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and discuss any questions or concerns with your mentee.
2. For each field, provide as much information as possible and be honest about your expectations and needs.
3. Make sure to sign and date the form to acknowledge that you have read, understand, and agree to the terms outlined in this form.
