# Step By Step Company Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about a company for internal use or external communication. It is meant to gather details such as name, address, contact information, and company status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the company name in the **Company Name** field.
2. Provide the company's address line 1 in the **Address Line 1** field.
3. Enter the company's address line 2 in the **Address Line 2** field.
4. Enter the company's email address in the **Email** field (in the correct format, e.g., example@example.com).
5. Enter the company's phone number in the **Phone** field.
6. Enter the company's fax number in the **Fax** field.
7. Enter the company's website URL in the **Website** field.
8. Select the type of company from the options provided in the **Company Type** field.
9. Select the current status of the company from the options provided in the **Company Status** field.
10. Enter the number of employees in the **Number of Employees** field.
11. Provide a brief description of the company in the **Company Description** field.
12. If applicable, enter the company's business hours in the **Business Hours** field (in 24-hour format, e.g., 09:00-17:00).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name**: Enter the company's official name.
* **Address Line 1**: Enter the first line of the company's address.
* **Address Line 2**: Enter the second line of the company's address.
* **Email**: Enter the company's email address (format: example@example.com).
* **Phone**: Enter the company's phone number.
* **Fax**: Enter the company's fax number.
* **Website**: Enter the company's website URL.
* **Company Type**: Select the type of company from the options provided (e.g., Company, Partnership, LLC, Corporation, or Other).
* **Company Status**: Select the current status of the company from the options provided (e.g., Active, Inactive, Merged, Dissolved, or Other).
* **Number of Employees**: Enter the total number of employees in the company.
* **Company Description**: Provide a brief description of the company.
* **Business Hours**: If applicable, enter the company's business hours in the 24-hour format (e.g., 09:00-17:00).
