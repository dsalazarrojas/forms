# Recruitment Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your interest in different areas of recruitment and gather contact information for potential recruitment opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field as follows:

1. **First Name**: Enter your first name.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
3. **Email**: Enter a valid email address.
4. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (optional, but helps us get in touch with you).
5. **Availability**: Select a date and time range of when you are available to work (not required).
6. **Start Time**: Select the start time you are available to work (not required).
7. **End Time**: Select the end time you are available to work (not required).
8. **Interest in Areas**: Select all the areas you are interested in (multiple selections allowed).
9. **Note**: Add any additional information or comments (not required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want us to get in touch with you.
* **Availability** (`availability`, date, optional): Select a date and time range when you are available to work (e.g., 2024-02-15 to 2024-02-17).
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Select the start time you are available to work (e.g., 08:00).
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Select the end time you are available to work (e.g., 17:00).
* **Interest in Areas** (`interest_in_areas`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the areas you are interested in:
	+ Area 1
	+ Area 2
	+ Area 3
	+ Area 4
	+ Area 5
	+ Area 6
	+ Area 7
	+ Area 8
	+ Area 9
	+ Area 10
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional information or comments.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have not already filled out this form in the past to avoid duplication.
* Enter accurate information to ensure we can get in touch with you.
* Select all areas that apply to you.
* Use the correct format for date and time selections (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD and HH:MM).
