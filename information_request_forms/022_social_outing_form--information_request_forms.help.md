# Social Outing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The social outing form is used for requesting and planning social events such as parties, meetups, or get-togethers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the social outing name in the "social outing name" field.
2. Select the date of the outing in the "date" field.
3. Choose the time of the outing in the "time" field.
4. Enter the location of the outing in the "location" field.
5. Enter the duration of the outing in the "duration" field.
6. Provide any relevant contact information in the "contact info" field.
7. Add any additional notes about the outing in the "notes" field.
8. Enter the contact phone number in the "contact phone" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Social Outing Name** (`social_outing_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the social outing, e.g. "Summer BBQ" or "Game Night".
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Select the date of the outing.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Choose the time of the outing.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of the outing, e.g. "123 Main St" or "Park XYZ".
* **Duration** (`duration`, number, required/optional): Enter the duration of the outing, e.g. 2 hours or 3 days.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Provide any contact information, e.g. "John Doe" or "johndoe@email.com".
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes or details about the outing.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact phone number for the outing (if applicable).

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are accurate and complete to ensure a smooth planning and execution of the social outing.
* You can use the information provided to share with others, such as organizers or attendees.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask the form owner for clarification.
