# Outdoor Lighting Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a client's or customer's outdoor lighting needs to provide accurate quotes or services for outdoor lighting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Start Form" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
3. For optional fields, choose the relevant information if you have it, and skip if you don't.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (Text, Optional)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name (Text, Optional)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email (Email, Optional)**: Enter your email address, which will be used for correspondence.
* **Street (Text, Optional)**: Enter the name of the street where the outdoor lighting will be installed.
* **City (Text, Optional)**: Enter the name of the city where the outdoor lighting will be installed.
* **State (Text, Optional)**: Enter the state where the outdoor lighting will be installed.
* **Zip (Text, Optional)**: Enter the zip code of the location where the outdoor lighting will be installed.
* **Type of Service (Select One, Optional)**: Choose the type of service you require:
	+ Yes: I need help with designing and installing outdoor lighting.
	+ No: I have already designed and installed my outdoor lighting.
* **Services Required (Select Multiple, Optional)**: Choose the services you require:
	+ Yes: I need assistance with design and installation of outdoor lighting.
	+ No: I already have a design and installation plan in place.
* **Project Details (Text, Optional)**: Provide as much detail as possible about your project, including the type of lighting you're interested in, and the specific area you'd like to be illuminated.

Note: Some fields may require additional information based on the user's selection in the "Type of Service" field. This will be indicated in the guide for those fields.
