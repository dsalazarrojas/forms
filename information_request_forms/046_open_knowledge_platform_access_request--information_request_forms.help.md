# Open Knowledge Platform Access Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Open Knowledge Platform Access Request form is designed to allow users to request access to the Open Knowledge Platform. This form is used to provide necessary information for access approval and to ensure that users understand the terms and conditions of using the platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Requester Name: This is the name of the person requesting access to the platform.
2. Enter your Email: This is the email address associated with your account.
3. Enter your Phone Number: This is your phone number, which can be used for verification purposes.
4. Describe Intended Use: Explain how you plan to use the platform and what you will be doing with the access you are requesting.
5. Agree to Terms and Conditions: This is the agreement that you must sign before accessing the platform.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Requester Name (ID 1, required)
* The Requester Name field is for your name. Please enter your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.

### Email (ID 2, required)
* The Email field is for your email address. Enter the email associated with your account.

### Phone (ID 3, required)
* The Phone field is for your phone number. This can be used for verification purposes.

### Intended Use (ID 4, required)
* The Intended Use field is for you to describe how you plan to use the platform. Be as specific as possible, so we can better understand your needs.

### Terms and Agreement (ID 5, required)
* The Terms and Agreement field is for you to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of using the platform. If you're unsure, please ask for clarification.
