# EUC Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact information from a person or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
Please complete the form by following these steps:

1. Enter your name in the name field.
2. Enter your email address in the email field.
3. Enter your phone number in the phone field.
4. Enter your company's name in the company name field.
5. Enter your address in the address field.
6. Enter your city in the city field.
7. Enter your state in the state field.
8. Enter your country in the country field.
9. Enter your company's name in the company name field (if you are filling out the form on behalf of a company).
10. Enter your zip code in the zip field.
</suggestion>

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_id** (Number, Optional): Enter a unique ID for this form, if required.
* **name** (Text, Optional): Enter your name or the name of the person filling out the form.
* **email** (Email, Optional): Enter your or your organization's email address.
* **phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your or your organization's phone number.
* **company name** (Text, Optional): Enter your company's name (if applicable).
* **address** (Text, Optional): Enter your or your organization's address.
* **address line 2** (Text, Optional): Enter an additional line of your or your organization's address.
* **city** (Text, Optional): Enter the city of your or your organization's address.
* **state** (Text, Optional): Enter the state of your or your organization's address.
* **zip** (Number, Optional): Enter the zip code of your or your organization's address.
* **country** (Text, Optional): Enter the country of your or your organization's address.
* **company name** (Text, Optional): Enter your company's name (if applicable).
