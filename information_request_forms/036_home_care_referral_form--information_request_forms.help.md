# home_care_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Care Referral Form is a tool to collect information for home care referrals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required fields and any additional information you think is relevant.
2. Ensure you are filling out the form for the correct client.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the client who needs home care services.
- **Referral Reason** (`referral_reason`, text, required): Provide the reason for the referral.
- **Referral Agency Name** (`referral_agency_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the agency making the referral.
- **Referral Date** (`referral_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the referral.
- **Client Address** (`client_address`, text, required): Enter the client's address.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter the client's contact number.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the client's email address.
- **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, text, required): Enter any relevant medical information about the client.
- **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, required): Provide any additional information about the client.
- **Home Care Agency** (`home_care_agency`, text, required): Enter the name of the home care agency making the referral.
- **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click to submit the form once you have filled out all the required fields.
