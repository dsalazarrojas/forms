> # Placenta Encapsulation Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients about their experiences with placenta encapsulation. It is meant to be completed by patients to provide healthcare providers with essential information about their medical history and treatment history.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to the best of your ability.
2. Provide detailed information about your medical history, including any pre-existing conditions such as hypertension, diabetes, or obesity.
3. Indicate whether you have undergone placenta encapsulation procedure.
4. Provide information about any complications you have experienced during or after the procedure.
5. Specify the medical provider you are seeing for the procedure (e.g. obgyn, doctor, healthcare).
6. Sign the form to indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions of the treatment.
7. Provide your patient name and date of service for record-keeping purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, required): Please provide your name, date of birth, and contact information in this field. This will help the healthcare provider identify you and reach out to you with any questions or concerns.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): Please list any pre-existing medical conditions you have, such as hypertension, diabetes, or obesity. This information will help the healthcare provider understand your medical history and provide better care.
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, optional): Please sign or type "yes" to indicate your consent for the treatment. This is a critical field that ensures you understand and agree to the treatment.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, select multiple, optional): Please select any pre-existing medical conditions you have experienced, such as hypertension, diabetes, or obesity. This will help the healthcare provider understand your medical history.
* **Placenta Procedure** (`placenta_procedure`, select one, optional): Please indicate whether you have undergone the placenta encapsulation procedure.
* **Placenta Encapsulation** (`placenta_encapsulation`, text, optional): Please provide any information about your experience with the placenta encapsulation procedure. This can include any side effects or complications you may have experienced.
* **Complications** (`complications`, text, optional): Please list any complications you have experienced during or after the procedure.
* **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, select multiple, optional): Please select the medical provider you will be seeing for the procedure, such as obgyn, doctor, healthcare.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Please sign this form to indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions of the treatment.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Please provide your name as the patient.
* **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, date, required): Please provide the date of the procedure.
* **Time of Service** (`time_of_service`, time, required): Please provide the time of the procedure.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, text, optional): Please provide your medical record number if you have one.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, required): Please sign this form to indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions of the treatment.
