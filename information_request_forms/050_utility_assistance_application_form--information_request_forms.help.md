# Utility Assistance Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Utility Assistance Application Form is designed to collect information from applicants seeking utility assistance. This form is intended to gather essential details about the applicant's needs and circumstances to provide support and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required and optional fields as instructed.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
3. Be accurate and clear in your responses to ensure accurate processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Info** (`applicant_info`, text, required: false): Please provide a brief description of yourself, including your name and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Provide your contact information, including your name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Service Request** (`service_request`, text, required: false): Describe the type of utility assistance you are seeking (e.g., emergency assistance or ongoing support).
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select multiple, required: false): Choose the type of service you are requesting, if applicable (e.g., 'Yes' for utility bill assistance).
* **Service Status** (`service_status`, select one, required: false): Select the status of your service (e.g., Active or Inactive).
* **Utility Bill** (`utility_bill`, number, required: false): If applicable, provide your current utility bill amount.
* **Service Start Date** (`service_start_date`, date, required: false): If applicable, provide the start date of your service.
* **Service End Date** (`service_end_date`, date, required: false): If applicable, provide the end date of your service.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Provide your physical address for delivery or communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number for communication.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Provide your email address for communication.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or details about your request.
* **Assigned Agent** (`assigned_agent`, select one, required: false): If applicable, indicate if an agent has been assigned to your case.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form.
* If unsure about any field, please consult with the form's administrator.
* Only complete this form for internal use within the organization.
