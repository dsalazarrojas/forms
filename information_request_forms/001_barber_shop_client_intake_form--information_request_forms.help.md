# Barber Shop Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help our barbershop team understand your preferences and needs. It will help us tailor our services to provide you with the best haircut and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your Full Name, Phone Number, and Email Address on pages 1-3.
2. If you have any medical concerns, list them on pages 4-6.
3. Select your preferred barber, hair type, and hair texture on pages 5-7.
4. Describe your current hairstyle and desired style on pages 8-10.
5. Choose your preferred service type on page 11.
6. List any hair products used and allergies/sensitivities on pages 12-13.
7. Share any previous barber experience and how you found us on pages 14-15.
8. Finally, add any special requests or notes on page 16.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your first and last name to help us get to know you better.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact number for appointment reminders and calls.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email for appointment confirmations and promotional messages.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your birthdate in the MM/DD/YYYY format. (Optional)
* **Preferred Barber**: Choose your favorite barber or select "No preference" to let us recommend someone.
* **Hair Type**: Select the option that best describes your hair type (Straight, Wavy, Curly, Coily, Textured).
* **Hair Texture**: Describe your hair thickness (Fine, Medium, Thick, Coarse).
* **Scalp Condition**: Select all that apply to your scalp conditions (Normal, Dry, Oily, Sensitive, Dandruff).
* **Current Hairstyle**: Briefly describe your current haircut to help us understand your style.
* **Desired Hairstyle**: Describe the style you're looking for today.
* **Service Type**: Choose the service you'd like to receive today (Haircut only, Haircut and beard trim, etc.).
* **Hair Products Used**: List any hair gels, pomades, or other products you currently use.
* **Allergies or Sensitivities**: If you have any, list them here.
* **Previous Barber Experience**: Share any positive or negative experiences with past barbers.
* **Referral Source**: Tell us how you found our shop (Friend, Social media, Google search, etc.).
* **Special Requests**: Add any additional information or requests for your barber.
* **Appointment Frequency**: Select how often you get haircuts (Every 2 weeks, Once a month, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of a person we can contact in case of an emergency during your visit.
* **Note**: This field is optional, but if you have any other information or requests, feel free to add them.
