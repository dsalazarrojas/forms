# gas_selection_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users who are seeking gas related services or supplies. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that we can assist you best.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, including all required and optional fields.
2. Be sure to enter your email address correctly, as it will be used to communicate with you.
3. If you select multiple contact methods, please separate them with commas.
4. If you are seeking specific gas supplies, please select them in the "Gas Supplies" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Gas Type** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the type of gas you are looking for (e.g. "Gasoline", "Diesel", etc.).
* **Contact Method** (`5`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, In-person).
* **Contact Method** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select multiple contact methods you are willing to be contacted through (Phone, Email, In-person).
* **Address** (`7`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your address where gas will be delivered or collected.
* **Zip Code** (`8`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the zip code of your address.
* **Date Of Visit** (`9`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date when you plan to visit the gas supplier.
* **Time Of Visit** (`10`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time when you plan to visit the gas supplier.
* **Message** (`11`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional information or instructions for the gas supplier.
* **Gas Supplies** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the gas supplies you are looking for (Fuel, Gas).
* **Contact Person Name** (`13`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person at the gas supplier.
* **Contact Phone** (`14`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`15`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Gas Suppliers** (`16`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the gas supplier you are considering.
* **Service Providers** (`17`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the gas service provider you are considering.
* **Company Name** (`18`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the company.
* **Address 2** (`19`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the company.
* **Zip Code 2** (`20`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the zip code of the company.
* **State** (`21`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the state of the company's location.
* **City** (`22`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the city of the company's location.
* **Country** (`23`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the country of the company's location.
* **Message** (`24`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional information or instructions.
* **Confirm Submission** (`25`, `select_one`, required/optional): Confirm that you have filled out the form correctly.

Please note that all fields are optional unless marked as required. Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that you receive the best possible service.
