# Education Verification Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify information related to an individual's education. It is intended to be completed by employees or individuals who are requesting education verification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the above section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your employee's name.
* **Employee Address** (`employee_address`, text, optional): Enter your employee's current address.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school your employee attended.
* **School Location** (`school_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the school.
* **Degree Name** (`degree_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your employee's degree.
* **Other Degree** (`degree_name_other`, text, optional): If your employee has completed a degree other than what is listed, enter the name of that degree.
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, date, optional): Enter the graduation date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Graduation Month** (`graduation_month`, select_one, required): Select the month of graduation from the dropdown list.
* **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`, number, optional): Enter the year of graduation.
* **School Attended Years** (`school_attended_years`, number, optional): If your employee attended school for a duration other than what is listed, select the duration of attendance from the dropdown list.
* **School Attended Month** (`school_attended_month`, select_multiple, optional): Select the months your employee attended school.
* **School Attended Year** (`school_attended_year`, number, optional): If your employee attended school for a duration other than what is listed, enter the year of attendance.
* **School Attended Duration** (`school_attended_duration`, select_multiple, optional): If your employee attended school for a duration other than what is listed, select the duration of attendance from the dropdown list.
* **Field 5** (`field_5`, select_one, optional): Select whether or not your employee was enrolled in school during the summer term.
* **Field 6** (`field_6`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether or not your employee was enrolled in school during the winter term.
* **Field 7** (`field_7`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your employee's education.
* **Field 8** (`field_8`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether or not your employee was enrolled in school during the spring term.
* **Field 9** (`field_9`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether or not your employee was enrolled in school during the fall term.
* **Field 10** (`field_10`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether or not your employee was enrolled in school during the other terms.
* **Field 11** (`field_11`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether or not your employee was enrolled in school during the other terms.
* **Field 12** (`field_12`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your employee's education.
* **Field 13** (`field_13`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether or not your employee was enrolled in school during the other terms.
* **Field 14** (`field_14`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether or not your employee was enrolled in school during the other terms.
