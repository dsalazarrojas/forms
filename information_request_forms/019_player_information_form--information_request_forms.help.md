# Player Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Player Information Form is designed to collect basic details about players, such as their name, contact information, and team affiliation. This information is likely used for registration, communication, or other related activities within a team or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your player name in the "Player Name" field.
2. Provide your player address in the "player address" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phone number" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
5. If you're a student-athlete, enter your school name in the "School Name" field.
6. If you hold a coaching position, enter your name and title in the "Coach" field.
7. (Optional) If you have a specific position on the team, enter it in the "Position" field.
8. Review and submit your information when ready.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Name** (Player Name, text, required): Enter your personal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Player address** (player address, text, optional): Enter your current address for communication purposes.
* **phone number** (phone number, text, optional): Enter your phone number for direct contact.
* **email** (email, text, optional): Enter your email address for secure communication.
* **School Name** (School Name, text, optional): If you're a student-athlete, enter your school name as a reference point.
* **Position** (Position, text, optional): If you have a specific role in the team, enter it here.
* **Coach** (Coach, text, optional): If you're a coaching staff member, enter your name and title.
