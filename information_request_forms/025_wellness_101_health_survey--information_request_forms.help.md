# Wellness 101 Health Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wellness 101 Health Survey is a form used to collect basic demographic information and in-depth health and wellness status from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Full Name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your Email in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Enter your Time of Birth in the "Time of Birth" field in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
6. Provide your Address in the "Address" field.
7. Enter your Zip Code in the "Zip Code" field.
8. Answer Question 1 in the "Question 1" field.
9. Answer Question 2 in the "Question 2" field.
10. Answer Question 3 in the "Question 3" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, required): Enter your time of birth in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your physical address.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, number, required): Enter your 5-digit zip code.
* **Question 1** (`question_1`, text, optional): Answer the first question in a clear and concise manner.
* **Question 2** (`question_2`, text, optional): Answer the second question in a clear and concise manner.
* **Question 3** (`question_3`, text, optional): Answer the third question in a clear and concise manner.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Use the correct format for date and time fields.
* Answer the questions in a clear and concise manner.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, feel free to contact us.
