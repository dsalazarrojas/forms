# Social Media Information Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Media Information Request form is used to collect information from users for social media purposes. This form helps to gather essential details such as the title, category, description, email, and phone number of the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter a clear and concise title** for your social media information request in the "Social Media Info Request Title" field.
2. **Select the category** of your request by choosing from the options provided in the "Category" field. If you do not need to select a category, you can leave this field blank.
3. **Provide a detailed description** of your request in the "Description" field.
4. **Enter your email address** in the "Email" field. This will be used as your point of contact for further communication.
5. **Enter your phone number** in the "Phone" field, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Social Media Info Request Title** (`social_media_info_request_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief and clear title of your social media information request.
* **Category** (`social_media_info_request_category`, select_one, optional): Choose the category for your social media information request from the provided options.
* **Description** (`social_media_info_request_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your social media information request.
* **Email** (`social_media_info_request_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`social_media_info_request_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if required.
