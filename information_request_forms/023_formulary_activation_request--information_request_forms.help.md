# Formulary Activation Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request the activation of a formulary, which is a system used to manage and track formulary-specific information.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out all required fields, marked as such.
2. Choose the correct options for select fields, such as approval status, reviewers, and status.
3. Enter any comments or additional information you think is relevant.
4. Review your submission carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
### Last Name
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
### Email
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
### Phone
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
### Formulary Name
* **Formulary Name**: Enter the name of the formulary you are requesting activation for.
### Formulary ID
* **Formulary ID**: Enter the ID number of the formulary you are requesting activation for.
### Approval Status
* **Approval Status**: Choose the current status of the formulary: Draft, Approved, or Rejected.
### Reviewers
* **Reviewers**: Choose the reviewers who have reviewed the formulary.
### Comments
* **Comments**: Enter any additional information or comments about the formulary.
### Reviewed By
* **Reviewed By**: Enter the name of the person who reviewed the formulary.
### Date Reviewed
* **Date Reviewed**: Enter the date the formulary was reviewed.
### Time Reviewed
* **Time Reviewed**: Enter the time the formulary was reviewed.
### Date Approved
* **Date Approved**: Enter the date the formulary was approved (if applicable).
### Time Approved
* **Time Approved**: Enter the time the formulary was approved (if applicable).
### Date Rejected
* **Date Rejected**: Enter the date the formulary was rejected (if applicable).
### Time Rejected
* **Time Rejected**: Enter the time the formulary was rejected (if applicable).
### Date Submitted
* **Date Submitted**: Enter the date the formulary was submitted.
### Time Submitted
* **Time Submitted**: Enter the time the formulary was submitted.
### Status
* **Status**: Choose the status of the formulary: Draft, Active, or Inactive.
### Form Status
* **Form Status**: Choose the form status of the formulary: Active, Inactive, or Draft.
### Activated By
* **Activated By**: Enter the name of the person who activated the formulary (if applicable).
### Date Activated
* **Date Activated**: Enter the date the formulary was activated (if applicable).
### Time Activated
* **Time Activated**: Enter the time the formulary was activated (if applicable).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your submissions for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your administrator.
