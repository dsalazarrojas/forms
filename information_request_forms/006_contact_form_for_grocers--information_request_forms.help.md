# Contact Form For Grocers - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is meant to provide a central point of contact for customers, vendors, or partners of the grocery store to inquire about various services or products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Choose whether you are a customer, vendor, or partner by selecting the appropriate contact type from the options provided.
2. Fill out your full name and email address in the corresponding fields. If you are a vendor or partner, provide your business name as well.
3. If you are a vendor, list the products you are interested in under the "Products interested in" field.
4. Choose your preferred contact method, if any.
5. Provide a brief description of your inquiry, including any relevant order numbers or details.
6. Indicate if you would like someone to follow up on your inquiry.
7. If you have any additional documentation or specifications, attach them via email.
8. Provide as much detail as possible about your inquiry in the "Inquiry details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Type (2)** (`<type: select_one>`, `required: true`): Select whether you are a customer, vendor, or partner of the store.
* **Full Name (3)** (`<type: text>`, `required: true`): Enter your full name as it appears on any official documents.
* **Business or Store Name (4)** (`<type: text>`, `required: false`): If you are a vendor or partner, enter your business name.
* **Email Address (5)** (`<type: email>`, `required: true`): Enter a valid email address for follow-up.
* **Phone Number (6)** (`<type: text>`, `required: false`): Enter a phone number for optional contact.
* **Products Interested In (7)** (`<type: text>`, `required: false`): If you are a vendor, list the products you are interested in.
* **Preferred Contact Method (8)** (`<type: select_one>`, `required: false`): Choose how you would like to be contacted, via email or phone, or an in-person meeting.
* **Order or Issue Reference (9)** (`<type: text>`, `required: false`): Provide a reference to a specific order or issue.
* **Attach Supporting Documents (10)** (`<type: note>`, `required: false`): Upload any relevant documents via email for further review.
* **Inquiry Details (11)** (`<type: text>`, `required: true`): Describe your inquiry as thoroughly as possible.
* **Store Location (12)** (`<type: text>`, `required: false`): If applicable, indicate your store location or branch.
* **Follow Up Needed (13)** (`<type: select_one>`, `required: true`): Indicate if you would like the store to follow up on your inquiry.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible in the "Inquiry details" field to ensure efficient communication.
* If you are a vendor, make sure to list the products you are interested in.
* Use a valid email address for follow-up communication.
* If you have any supporting documents or specifications, attach them via email for further review.
