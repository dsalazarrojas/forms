# Antique Appraisal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about an antique item to provide a comprehensive appraisal report.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Appraisal Date** (`appraisal_date`, date, required): Date of appraisal.
* **Appraiser Name** (`appraiser_name`, text, required): Full name of appraiser.
* **Appraisal Company** (`appraiser_company`, text, not required): Company name.
* **Appraiser Certification** (`appraiser_certification`, text, required): License or certification number.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Owner of the item.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, required): Contact email.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, required): Contact phone.
* **Purpose of Appraisal** (`appraisal_purpose`, select_one, required): Why is the appraisal needed.
	+ Insurance coverage
	+ Estate planning
	+ Sale or purchase
	+ Donation
	+ Divorce settlement
	+ Curiosity
	+ Other
* **Item Category** (`item_category`, select_one, required): Type of antique.
	+ Furniture
	+ Jewelry
	+ Ceramics or pottery
	+ Glassware
	+ Silver or metalware
	+ Paintings
	+ Prints or drawings
	+ Books or manuscripts
	+ Coins or currency
	+ Stamps
	+ Textiles
	+ Toys or dolls
	+ Other
* **Item Description** (`item_description`, text, required): Detailed description.
* **Item Dimensions** (`item_dimensions`, text, not required): Height x Width x Depth.
* **Item Weight** (`item_weight`, text, not required): If applicable.
* **Maker or Manufacturer** (`maker_or_manufacturer`, text, not required): If known.
* **Country of Origin** (`country_of_origin`, text, not required): Where was it made.
* **Approximate Age** (`approximate_age`, select_one, required): When was it made.
	+ Pre-1800
	+ 1800 to 1850
	+ 1850 to 1900
	+ 1900 to 1920
	+ 1920 to 1940
	+ 1940 to 1960
	+ 1960 or later
	+ Unknown
* **Period or Style** (`period_or_style`, text, not required): Victorian, Art Deco, etc.
* **Materials** (`materials`, select_multiple, required): What is it made of.
	+ Wood
	+ Metal
	+ Ceramic
	+ Glass
	+ Fabric or textile
	+ Paper
	+ Leather
	+ Stone
	+ Ivory or bone
	+ Other
* **Overall Condition** (`condition`, select_one, required): State of the item.
	+ Excellent
	+ Very good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Condition Details** (`condition_details`, text, required): Describe any damage or wear.
* **Repairs or Restoration** (`repairs_or_restoration`, select_one, required): Any work done.
	+ None
	+ Minor repairs
	+ Major repairs
	+ Full restoration
	+ Unknown
* **Markings or Signatures** (`markings_or_signatures`, text, not required): Any identifying marks.
* **Provenance** (`provenance`, text, not required): History of ownership.
* **Previous Appraisals** (`previous_appraisals`, select_one, required): Has it been appraised before.
	+ Yes, within 5 years
	+ Yes, over 5 years ago
	+ False
	+ Unknown
* **Previous Value** (`previous_value`, number, not required): If known.
* **Comparable Sales** (`comparable_sales`, text, not required): Similar items sold recently.
* **Valuation Section** (`valuation_section`, note, not required): This is an optional section for additional comments.
* **Replacement Value** (`replacement_value`, number, required): Retail replacement value.
* **Fair Market Value** (`fair_market_value`, number, not required): For estate or donation.
* **Auction Estimate Low** (`auction_estimate_low`, number, not required): Low estimate range.
* **Auction Estimate High** (`auction_estimate_high`, number, not required): High estimate range.
* **Basis of Valuation** (`valuation_basis`, text, required): How was value determined.
* **Market Analysis** (`market_analysis`, text, not required): Current market conditions.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, not required): Any other observations.
* **Photos Attached** (`photos_attached`, number, required): Number of photos.
* **Appraiser Signature** (`appraiser_signature`, text, required): Digital signature.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Date signed.
* **Disclaimer Acknowledged** (`disclaimer_acknowledged`, select_one, required): Client understands terms.
	+ Yes, acknowledged
	+ Other

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Use the field-by-field explanation as a reference to ensure you are filling out the form correctly.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the appraiser or support staff for assistance.
