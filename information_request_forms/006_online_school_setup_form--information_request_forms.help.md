# Online School Setup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online School Setup Form is designed to collect information about online schools for administrative or registration purposes. This form helps gather essential details such as school name, contact person, email, and social media links to create a comprehensive profile.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the school name in the "School Name" field.
2. Enter the contact person's name and email address in the "Contact Person" and "Email" fields, respectively.
3. Select the type of school (public, private, both, or other) in the "School Type" field.
4. Choose the school level (primary, secondary, or tertiary) in the "School Level" field.
5. Add the school address in the "School Address" field.
6. Enter the launch date and end date of the school in the "Launch Date" and "End Date" fields, respectively.
7. Provide a brief description of the school in the "Description" field.
8. Select the school status (active or inactive) in the "School Status" field.
9. Enter the school's social media links (Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, YouTube, and LinkedIn) in their respective fields.
10. Enter the school's website and email address in the "School Website" and "School Email" fields.
11. Add any additional contact information in the "Other Contact" and "Other Contact Info" fields.
12. Leave any comments or notes about the school in the "Comments" and "Notes" fields.
13. Attach any relevant documents or files to the "Attachments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the school.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the school's phone number.
* **School Type** (`school_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of school (public, private, both, or other).
* **School Level** (`school_level`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the school level (primary, secondary, or tertiary).
* **School Address** (`school_address`, note, required/optional): Enter the school's physical address.
* **Launch Date** (`launch_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the school was launched.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the school will end.
* **Description** (`description`, note, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the school.
* **School Status** (`school_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the school status (active or inactive).
* **School URL** (`school_url`, text, required/optional): Enter the school's website URL.
* **School Facebook** (`school_facebook`, text, required/optional): Enter the school's Facebook link.
* **School Twitter** (`school_twitter`, text, required/optional): Enter the school's Twitter link.
* **School Instagram** (`school_instagram`, text, required/optional): Enter the school's Instagram link.
* **School YouTube** (`school_youtube`, text, required/optional): Enter the school's YouTube link.
* **School LinkedIn** (`school_linkedin`, text, required/optional): Enter the school's LinkedIn link.
* **School Website** (`school_website`, text, required/optional): Enter the school's website URL.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the school's email address.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the school's phone number.
* **School Fax** (`school_fax`, text, required/optional): Enter the school's fax number.
* **Other Contact** (`school_other_contact`, text, required/optional): Enter any other contact information.
* **Other Contact Info** (`school_other_contact_info`, text, required/optional): Enter any other contact information.
* **Comments** (`school_comments`, note, required/optional): Leave comments about the school.
* **Notes** (`school_notes`, note, required/optional): Leave notes about the school.
* **Attachments** (`school_attachments`, note, required/optional): Attach relevant documents or files to the school's profile.
