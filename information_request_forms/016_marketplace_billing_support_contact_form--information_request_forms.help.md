# marketplace_billing_support_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers or users to contact the billing support team with questions, issues, or concerns related to our marketplace. Fill out the form with your information and a brief description of your question or issue, and we will get back to you shortly.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
- Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
- Enter your Email in the "Email" field (this will be used to contact you).
- Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
- Enter a brief description of your question or issue in the "Question" field.
- Select the Marketplace you are experiencing issues with in the "Marketplace" field.
- Select the SaaS platform you are experiencing issues with in the "SaaS" field.
- Click the "Submit" button to send your query to our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name to help us identify you.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name to help us identify you.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your issue.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Question** (`Question`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your question or issue briefly.
* **Marketplace** (`Marketplace`, `text`, required/optional): Select the marketplace you are experiencing issues with.
* **SaaS** (`SaaS`, `text`, required/optional): Select the SaaS platform you are experiencing issues with.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, `text`, required/optional): Click this button to send your query to our support team.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure we can assist you efficiently.
- If you are not sure about the Marketplace or SaaS field, just enter "Unknown" or ask our support team for guidance.
- If you have any attachments or files to share with us, you can include them in an email or message after submitting this form.
- Our support team will review your query and contact you shortly.
