# life_insurance_beneficiary_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from a beneficiary of a life insurance policy. It captures details such as the beneficiary's personal information, relationship to the insured, contact details, and witness information for verification purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the beneficiary's first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the beneficiary's last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter the beneficiary's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Select the relationship of the beneficiary to the insured in the "Relation to Insured" field.
5. Enter the insured's ID number in the "Insured ID" field.
6. Enter the policy number associated with the insured in the "Policy Number" field.
7. Provide the beneficiary's contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
8. Enter the beneficiary's contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
9. Enter the beneficiary's address in the "Beneficiary Address" field.
10. Sign the "Relationship Signature" field to confirm the relationship to the insured.
11. Have a witness sign and date the "Witness Signature" and "Witness Date" fields to verify the information provided.
12. Enter the witness's name in the "Witness Name" field.
13. Enter the witness's title in the "Witness Title" field.
14. Enter the witness's contact phone number in the "Witness Phone" field.
15. Enter the witness's email address in the "Witness Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter the beneficiary's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter the beneficiary's last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the beneficiary's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Relation to Insured**: Select the relationship of the beneficiary to the insured (e.g., Spouse, Child, Parent, Parent-in-law).
* **Insured ID**: Enter the ID number associated with the insured.
* **Policy Number**: Enter the policy number associated with the insured.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the beneficiary's contact phone number.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the beneficiary's contact email address.
* **Beneficiary Address**: Enter the beneficiary's address.
* **Relationship Signature**: Sign to confirm the relationship to the insured.
* **Witness Signature**: Sign to verify the information provided.
* **Witness Date**: Sign and date to verify the information provided.
* **Witness Name**: Enter the witness's name.
* **Witness Title**: Enter the witness's title or position.
* **Witness Phone**: Enter the witness's contact phone number.
* **Witness Email**: Enter the witness's email address.
