# Sustainable Interiors Advisor Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you get in touch with our sustainable interiors advisor service. We use the information you provide to determine the best way to contact you and to better understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, email address, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
2. Describe your inquiry or question in the "Describe your inquiry or question" field.
3. Select how you would like to be contacted (email, phone, or messaging app) in the "How would you like to be contacted?" field.
4. Indicate your estimated budget for the project.
5. If you have any additional comments or questions, you can type them in the "Do you have any additional comments or questions?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name?** (`name`, text, required): Please fill in your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **What is your email address?** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address where you can be reached.
* **What is your phone number?** (`phone_number`, number, required): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Describe your inquiry or question** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your inquiry or question.
* **How would you like to be contacted?** (`contact_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (email, phone, or messaging app).
* **What is your preferred design style or preference?** (`design_style`, text, optional): If you have a preferred design style or preference, please indicate it here.
* **Would you like to receive our monthly newsletter?** (`subscribe_newsletter`, checkbox, optional): Check this box if you would like to receive our monthly newsletter.
* **How did you hear about us?** (`how_found`, select_multiple, required): Indicate how you heard about us (social media, word of mouth, online search, or other).
* **Would you like to schedule a consultation?** (`schedule_consultation`, radio, optional): Check if you would like to schedule a consultation.
* **What is your estimated budget for the project?** (`budget`, number, required): Enter your estimated budget for the project.
* **Do you have any additional comments or questions?** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, please type them in here.
* **What is the best time to contact you?** (`best_time`, time, optional): Enter a time when it would be best to contact you. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
* Use your real email address and phone number for effective communication.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
