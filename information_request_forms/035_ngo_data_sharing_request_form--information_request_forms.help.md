# NGO Data Sharing Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs) to request data sharing from us. Fill it out carefully to help us understand your needs and ensure a smooth processing of your request.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a clear and concise description of the purpose of your data sharing request.
2. Specify how you intend to use the data.
3. Choose the correct policy agreement that applies to your organization.
4. Select the correct type of organization you represent.
5. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **NGO Data Sharing Request Form (text)**: This is the title of your request, which should give a brief overview of the purpose and scope of your data sharing request.
* **Purpose (text)**: Describe the reason for your data sharing request. Be specific about what data you're seeking and how it will be used.
* **Usage (text)**: Explain how you plan to use the data. This will help us understand the context and ensure that the data sharing is in accordance with our policies.
* **Policy Agreement (Select Multiple)**: Choose the policy agreement that applies to your organization. If none of the listed options apply, select "Other" and describe the specific policy that governs your request.
* **Nonprofit/Organization (Select One)**: Select the type of organization you represent. If not a nonprofit, government agency, or for-profit, select "Other" and describe your organization type in the text field.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted for updates on your request.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter a phone number where we can contact you for further information or clarification.

## Tips

- Make sure to review the form fields carefully before submitting your request to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.
- Submission of this form doesn't guarantee approval for data sharing. We will review your request according to applicable policies and procedures.
