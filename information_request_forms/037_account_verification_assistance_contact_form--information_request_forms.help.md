# Account Verification Assistance Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Account Verification Assistance Contact Form is used to report account verification issues and receive help from the support team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field. This is the name associated with your account.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field. This is the email address associated with your account.
3. Select your preferred contact method in the "Preferred Contact Method" field. You can choose from "Email", "Phone", or "Text message".
4. Enter your account username or ID in the "Account Username or ID" field. This helps us locate your account for assistance.
5. Select the type of account you have in the "Account Type" field. You can choose from "Personal", "Business", "Student", or "Other".
6. Describe the type of verification issue you are experiencing in the "Type of Verification Issue" field. Choose from the options provided, or select "Other" if your issue is not listed.
7. Provide a detailed description of the issue you are experiencing in the "Describe the Issue in Detail" field. Include any error messages you have received.
8. If you have already taken any steps to try and resolve the issue, select them in the "Steps Already Attempted" field. You can select multiple steps if necessary.
9. Indicate how urgent your request is in the "How Urgent Is This Request" field. Choose from "Low - Can wait a few days", "Medium - Need help within 24 hours", or "High - Need help immediately".
10. If you have any additional information that may help us resolve your issue, enter it in the "Additional Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your account.
- **Email Address**: Enter the email address associated with your account.
- **Preferred Contact Method**: Select how you would like to be contacted to discuss your verification issue. 
- **Account Username or ID**: Enter your account username or ID to help us locate your account.
- **Account Type**: Choose the type of account you have (e.g. Personal, Business, Student, etc.).
- **Type of Verification Issue**: Select the specific issue you are experiencing with verification.
- **Describe the Issue in Detail**: Provide a detailed description of the issue, including any error messages you have received.
- **Steps Already Attempted**: If you have already tried any steps to resolve the issue, select them here.
- **How Urgent Is This Request**: Indicate how urgently you need help with your verification issue.
- **Additional Information**: If you have any other details that may help us resolve your issue, enter them here.
