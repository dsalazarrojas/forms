# Student Climate Literacy Materials Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to allow students to request climate literacy materials, which include resources and information related to climate change, its effects, and its impact on society. The purpose of this form is to collect information about the specific resources and materials needed by students for their educational and research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form title with a brief description of the materials you are requesting.
2. Select the category of climate literacy materials you are requesting (e.g., articles, videos, books, etc.).
3. Provide a detailed description of the resources and materials you need.
4. Indicate the type of resources you are requesting (e.g., online, offline, etc.).
5. Specify the quantity of resources you need.
6. Enter your contact information, including your name, email address, and submitting school's information.
7. Upload any additional files or materials that support your request.
8. Agree to share your information with our team and have your name and email on file.
9. Submit the form to complete your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title**: A brief description of the materials you are requesting. This will help us understand the context of your request.
* **Category**: Select the category of climate literacy materials you are requesting (e.g., articles, videos, books, etc.). This will help us direct your request to the right resource provider.
* **Form Description**: Provide a detailed description of the resources and materials you need. Be specific about what you are looking for.
* **Requested Resources**: List the specific climate literacy resources you need.
* **Resource Type**: Indicate the type of resources you are requesting (e.g., online, offline, etc.). This will help us understand the availability and accessibility of the resources.
* **Quantity**: Specify the quantity of resources you need.
* **Requester Information**: Enter your name, email address, and submitting school's information. This will enable us to contact you and communicate about your request.
* **Additional Information**: Provide any additional information that may be relevant to your request.
* **Agree to Share**: Check the box to agree to share your information with our team and have your name and email on file.
* **Signature**: Date and sign the form to confirm your submission.
* **Upload File**: Upload any additional files or materials that support your request.
* **Agree to Sign**: Check the box to agree to have your name and email on file.
