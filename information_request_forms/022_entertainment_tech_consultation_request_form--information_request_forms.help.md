# Entertainment Tech Consultation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect consultation requests for entertainment technology projects from clients. It helps our team to understand client needs and requirements, allowing us to provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:
1. Ensure that you have all the required information ready before starting the form.
2. Enter your **Client First Name** and **Client Last Name** accurately in the corresponding fields.
3. Enter your project **Project Budget** in the required format.
4. Specify the **Project Location** where the technology will be used.
5. Provide the **Project Deadline** in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
6. Choose the correct **Tech Type** that best describes your project (Hardware, Software, or Other).
7. Enter a detailed **Tech Specs** description of the technology you need.
8. Select the **Tech Warranties** options that apply to your project (Yes, No, or Other, with a specific requirement for 'Other' if selected).
9. If you've selected 'Other' for **Tech Warranties**, provide a detailed description in the **Other (if applicable)** field.
10. Enter your **Client Email** in the correct format.
11. Enter your **Client Phone** number.
12. Add any further **Client Message** for our team to address your concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client First Name**: Your first name as a client.
* **Client Last Name**: Your last name as a client.
* **Project Budget**: The total budget allocated for this project.
* **Project Location**: Where the project will take place or where you need the technology.
* **Project Deadline**: The date by which you need the project completed.
* **Tech Type**: Choose the type of technology you need (Hardware, Software, or Other).
* **Tech Specs**: A detailed description of the technology you require.
* **Tech Warranties**: Choose which warranty options apply to your project.
* **Other (if applicable)**: If you selected 'Other' for Tech Warranties, describe the specific requirements here.
* **Client Email**: Your email address for contact and updates.
* **Client Phone**: Your phone number.
* **Client Message**: Any further message or details about your consultation request.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check the **Tech Warranties** options to ensure you've covered all necessary requirements.
* If you have any doubts or queries, use the **Client Message** field to communicate with our team.
* This form is designed to be completed by clients. If you need to submit on behalf of someone else, please provide their consent for us to process their request.
