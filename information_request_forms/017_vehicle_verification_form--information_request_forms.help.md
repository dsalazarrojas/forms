# Vehicle Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Verification Form is used to gather and confirm vehicle information from users or administrators. This form is intended to be a straightforward and efficient way to collect vehicle data for various purposes such as vehicle maintenance, sales, or rental purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections carefully and ensure you provide accurate information about the vehicle you are verifying.
2. Fill in each field according to the instructions provided below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Vehicle Information
* **Vehicle Information** (`vehicle_info`, text, required: false): Enter the vehicle's make, model, and year of manufacture.
* **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`, text, required: false): Describe the vehicle's condition, including any scratches, dents, or other notable features.
* **Vehicle Sharing** (`vehicle_sharing`, text, required: false): Check if the vehicle is shared or used for specific purposes.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, text, required: false): Enter the vehicle's type, such as sedan, SUV, or truck.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, required: false): Provide any additional details about the vehicle that is not covered in other fields.
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`, text, required: false): Enter the vehicle's color.
* **Vehicle Model Year** (`vehicle_model_year`, date, required: false): Enter the vehicle's model year.
* **Vehicle Type Year** (`vehicle_type_year`, number, required: false): Enter the number of years the vehicle has been in use.
* **Vehicle Color Year** (`vehicle_color_year`, text, required: false): Enter the year the vehicle's color was last changed.
* **Vehicle Model Year 2** (`vehicle_model_year_2`, text, required: false): Enter the model year of the vehicle (if different from the model year field).
* **Vehicle Model Color** (`vehicle_model_color`, text, required: false): Enter the color of the vehicle's model.
* **Vehicle Type Model** (`vehicle_type_model`, text, required: false): Enter the model type of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Type** and **Vehicle Model** are intended to be the same field in a different name.
*   If the field is not clear, try to provide the most accurate information you can.
*   If you are unsure, please leave the field blank and move on to the next field.

## Tips
* Be as accurate and thorough as possible when filling out the form.
* If you need to make any corrections or updates, please contact the relevant department or team.
* This form is for verification purposes only, and the information provided will be used for internal purposes.
