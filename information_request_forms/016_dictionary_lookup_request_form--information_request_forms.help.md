# Dictionary Lookup Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a dictionary lookup request. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure your request is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*). These fields are necessary for processing your request.
2.  Select the relevant options for "Definition Term," "Request Definition Language," "Translation Request," "Language," and "Follow-up" fields based on your specific needs.
3.  Provide your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
4.  Add any additional information or notes as needed in the "Note" field.
5.  Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Dictionary Lookup Request Form (\*)**: This is the title of the form. It is not a field that you need to fill out.
*   **Terminology Request Description (\*)**: Describe your dictionary lookup request in this text field.
*   **Definition Term (\*)**: Select the term for which you are requesting a dictionary lookup.
*   **Request Definition Language (\*)**: Select all languages for which you are requesting definitions.
*   **Definition (\*)**: Provide a detailed description of what you are looking for in the definition.
*   **Translation Request (\*)**: Specify whether you are requesting translations.
*   **Language (\*)**: Select all languages for which you are requesting translations.
*   **Follow-up (\*)**: Specify your follow-up preferences.
*   **Date**: Optional field for scheduling flexibility.
*   **Time**: Optional field for scheduling flexibility.
*   **Note (\*)**: Add any additional information or context about your request.
*   **Email Address (\*)**: Provide your email address for contact purposes.
*   **Phone Number (\*)**: Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Assigned Tool**: Specify the tool assigned to you for your request.
*   **Additional Info**: Add any additional information about your request.
*   **Submit**: Click to submit your form.
