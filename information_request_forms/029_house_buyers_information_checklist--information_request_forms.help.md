# House Buyers Information Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you gather and organize crucial information about a house you're considering buying. This information will be used to provide you with valuable insights and recommendations to make your decision-making process more efficient and informed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Enter your responses in the designated fields.
3. If you're unsure about any of the information or have any questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to our support team for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. House Location
* **House Location**: Enter the address or location of the house you're considering. This information will help us understand the property's geographical features and characteristics.

### 2. Number of Bedrooms
* **Number of Bedrooms**: Enter the number of bedrooms in the house, including any additional rooms that may be used as bedrooms (e.g., home office or playroom). This information will help us understand the property's layout and potential uses.

### 3. Square Footage
* **Square Footage**: Enter the total square footage of the house, including any additional areas like attics or garages. This information will help us calculate the property's value and assess its size.

### 4. Year Built
* **Year Built**: Enter the year the house was built. This information will help us understand the property's age and potential historical significance.

### 5. Lot Size
* **Lot Size**: Enter the size of the property's lot, including any additional land or features (e.g., gardens or parks). This information will help us understand the property's overall size and potential for expansion.

### 6. Property Type
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property (e.g., single-family home, condominium, or apartment building). This information will help us understand the property's ownership structure and potential restrictions or regulations.

### 7. Number of Floors
* **Number of Floors**: Enter the total number of floors in the house, including any additional features like basements or attic spaces. This information will help us understand the property's layout and potential uses.

### 8. Parking
* **Parking**: Indicate if the property has parking (yes or no). This information will help us understand the property's accessibility and convenience.

### 9. Yard Size
* **Yard Size**: Enter the size of the property's yard, including any additional features (e.g., gardens or pools). This information will help us understand the property's overall size and potential for outdoor activities.

### 10. Pool Size
* **Pool Size**: Enter the size of the property's pool, including its type and features (e.g., in-ground or above-ground). This information will help us understand the property's recreational features and potential for outdoor activities.

### 11. Lot Orientation
* **Lot Orientation**: Enter the orientation of the property's lot, including any features like north-south orientation. This information will help us understand the property's environmental features and potential for solar exposure.

### 12. Property Features
* **Property Features**: List any additional features or amenities of the property, such as fireplaces, vaulted ceilings, or solar panels. This information will help us understand the property's unique characteristics and potential for comfort and energy efficiency.

### 13. HOA Fees
* **HOA Fees**: Indicate if the property has HOA fees (yes or no). This information will help us understand the property's ownership structure and potential financial responsibilities.

### 14. HOA Dues
* **HOA Dues**: Select the type of HOA dues (yes or no), including any specific fees or assessments. This information will help us understand the property's financial obligations and potential restrictions or regulations.

### 15. Neighborhood
* **Neighborhood**: Enter a brief description of the property's neighborhood, including any notable features or characteristics. This information will help us understand the property's surroundings and potential for social interaction.

### 16. Schools
* **Schools**: List any nearby schools or educational institutions, including their distance and quality. This information will help us understand the property's potential for education and family-friendliness.

### 17. Parks
* **Parks**: List any nearby parks or recreational areas, including their size and features. This information will help us understand the property's potential for outdoor activities and community engagement.

### 18. Public Transportation
* **Public Transportation**: Indicate if the property has public transportation (yes or no), including any specific features or routes. This information will help us understand the property's accessibility and potential for convenience.

### 19. Amenities
* **Amenities**: List any additional amenities or features of the property, such as community pools, gyms, or spas. This information will help us understand the property's recreational features and potential for comfort and convenience.

### 20. Community Info
* **Community Info**: Enter any additional information about the property's community or neighborhood, including its reputation or notable events. This information will help us understand the property's social dynamics and potential for community interaction.

### 21. HOA Rules
* **HOA Rules**: Indicate if the property has HOA rules (yes or no), including any specific restrictions or regulations. This information will help us understand the property's ownership structure and potential restrictions or limitations.

### 22. Pet Policies
* **Pet Policies**: Indicate if the property has pet policies (yes or no), including any specific rules or restrictions. This information will help us understand the property's pet-friendliness and potential for pet ownership.

### 23. Homeowners Assoc Fees
* **Homeowners Assoc Fees**: Select the type of homeowners association fees (yes or no), including any specific fees or assessments. This information will help us understand the property's financial obligations and potential for community involvement.

### 24. HOA Board Meeting Frequency
* **HOA Board Meeting Freq**: Indicate if the HOA board has meetings (yes or no), including any specific meeting frequency. This information will help us understand the property's governance structure and potential for community engagement.

### 25. Home Warranty
* **Home Warranty**: Indicate if the property has a home warranty (yes or no), including any specific details or conditions. This information will help us understand the property's maintenance and potential for cost savings.

### Tips

* Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* Review each section carefully to ensure you're capturing all relevant information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to our support team for clarification.
