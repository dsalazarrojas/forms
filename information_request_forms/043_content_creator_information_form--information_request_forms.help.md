# Content Creator Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Content Creator Information Form is a tool used to gather essential information from content creators who are interested in collaborating with our organization. This form helps us to understand your background, social media presence, content style, and previous collaborations, which enables us to assess whether we can work together effectively. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in a quiet and private setting, without distractions.
2. Ensure you answer all questions truthfully and provide accurate information.
3. Double-check your answers for any errors before submitting.
4. Once you've completed all required fields, click the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Creator Full Name** (`creator_name`, **required**): Enter your full name as it appears on your government ID.
* **Creator Identification Number** (`creator_id`, **required**): Enter your identification number as per your government ID.
* **Email Address** (`creator_email`, **required**): Enter your official email address associated with your government ID.
* **Phone Number** (`creator_phone`, **required**): Enter your mobile number or office number where we can contact you for further discussions.
* **Primary Social Media Platform** (`primary_social_media_platform`, **required**): Select your primary social media platform (e.g., Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Twitter, LinkedIn, Facebook, or Other).
* **Primary Social Media Handle** (`primary_handle`, **required**): Enter your primary social media handle associated with your selected platform.
* **Follower Count on Primary Platform** (`follower_count`, **required**): Enter the number of followers you have on your primary social media platform.
* **Engagement Rate on Primary Platform (%)** (`engagement_rate_percentage`, **required**): Enter the engagement rate (likes, comments, shares, etc.) on your primary social media platform.
* **Description of your Content Style** (`content_style_description`, **required**): Describe your content style (e.g., vlogging, gaming, beauty tutorials, etc.).
* **Previous Brand Collaborations** (`previous_brand_collaborations`, **required**): List any previous brands you've collaborated with, including the platform and the specific project.
* **Information Form Completed?** (`information_form_completed`, **required**): Confirm that you've completed the form accurately.
* **I confirm that the information provided is accurate** (`signature_confirmation`, **required**): Agree that the information you've provided is accurate and true.
* **I have read and agree to the privacy policy regarding my data** (`privacy_agreement`, **required**): Confirm that you've read and agree to our privacy policy regarding your personal data.
* **Date of Application Submission** (`application_submission_date`, **required**): Enter the date you're submitting this form.

Please note that all fields are required, and you must submit the form to proceed. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
