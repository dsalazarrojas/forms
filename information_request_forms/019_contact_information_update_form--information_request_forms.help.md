# Contact Information Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to update your contact information for our records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). These fields are:
	* Student First Name
	* Student Last Name
	* Student ID Number
	* Grade Level
	* Parent or Guardian Name
	* Relationship to Student
	* Home Phone Number
	* Mobile Phone Number
	* Email Address
	* Home Address
	* City
	* State
	* ZIP Code
	* Emergency Contact Name
	* Emergency Contact Phone
	* Type of Update
2. Optional fields are:
	* Work Phone Number
	* Secondary Email Address
	* Additional Notes

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information Update Form** (`form_title`, note, not required): This is the title of the form.
* **Student First Name** (`student_first_name`, text, required): Please fill out your first name.
* **Student Last Name** (`student_last_name`, text, required): Please fill out your last name.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id`, text, required): Please enter your student ID number.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, required): Please select your current grade level.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, text, required): Please fill out the name of the parent or guardian.
* **Relationship to Student** (`relationship`, select_one, required): Please select your relationship to the student.
* **Home Phone Number** (`home_phone`, text, not required): Please enter your home phone number.
* **Mobile Phone Number** (`mobile_phone`, text, required): Please enter your mobile phone number.
* **Work Phone Number** (`work_phone`, text, not required): Please enter your work phone number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Secondary Email Address** (`secondary_email`, email, not required): Please enter your secondary email address.
* **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, required): Please fill out your home address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Please enter your city.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Please enter your state.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Please enter your zip code.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Please enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Type of Update** (`update_type`, select_multiple, required): Please select what you are updating (e.g., Phone Numbers, Email Addresses, etc.).
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, not required): Please enter any additional notes or information you want to include.
