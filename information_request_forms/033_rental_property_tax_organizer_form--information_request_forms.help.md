# Rental Property Tax Organizer Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information related to rental properties for tax purposes. It is intended to help users organize and upload relevant data to efficiently manage rental property taxes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete the form, first ensure you are on the correct page (Property Details) and click on the "Submit" button to navigate to the next page (Tax Year).
2. Fill out the required fields:
	* **Property Details**: Enter the name, address, or identifier of your rental property.
3. Proceed to the next page (Tax Year) and select the applicable tax year from the available options.
4. Enter the tax rate corresponding to the selected tax year.
5. If applicable, upload additional tax documentation using the "Tax Upload 1" and "Tax Upload 2" fields. You may also add a phone number and email for contact purposes.
6. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
7. Click the "Submit" button to complete and save the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Details** (Property Details, text, required): Enter a brief description of your rental property, including its name, address, or identifier.
* **Tax Year** (Tax Year, number, required): Select the applicable tax year for your rental property.
* **Tax Rate** (Tax Rate, number, required): Enter the tax rate corresponding to the selected tax year.
* **Tax Upload 1** (Tax Upload 1, text, optional): Upload additional tax documentation for further reference.
* **Tax Upload 2** (Tax Upload 2, text, optional): Upload further tax documentation as necessary.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Note** (Note, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes regarding your rental property.
