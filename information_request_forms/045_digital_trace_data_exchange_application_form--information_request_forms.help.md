# Digital Trace Data Exchange Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for the Digital Trace Data Exchange application. Please fill out this form with accurate and detailed information to help us understand your needs and determine the feasibility of your application.

## How To Complete This Form

- Ensure you have permission to fill out this form. You should be a representative from an eligible organization.
- Review the form carefully to ensure you have answered all required fields (marked with an asterisk (*)).
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you need help or have questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info**: This field is where you enter your name and other identifying information.
* **Data Description**: Provide a detailed description of the data you are requesting to access through our system. Please be as specific as possible.
* **Consent**: Please select one of the provided options to indicate your consent to our data usage policy.
* **Intended Use**: Describe how you plan to use the data you are requesting. This will help us understand your needs better.
* **Data Usage Policy**: Please enter any additional information about your organization's data usage policy.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your organization's contact information so we can reach out to you with further instructions.
* **Organization**: Enter your organization's name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your organization's address.
* **Additional Info**: If there is any other information you would like to provide, enter it here.
* **Upload File**: You can upload a file to support your application if necessary.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your application once you have completed the form.
* **Review**: Review your application before submitting it.
* **Cancel**: Cancel your application and start over if needed.
* **Save And Submit**: Save your progress and submit your application.
* **Save Draft**: Save your progress without submitting.
* **Save and Preview**: Save your progress and preview your application before submitting.
* **Clear Draft**: Clear any saved progress and start over.
* **Delete Draft**: Delete any saved progress.
* **View Draft**: View your saved progress.
* **Save**: Save your progress for later.
* **Confirm**: Confirm your application and make sure everything is correct.
* **Send Request**: Send your application for review.
* **Confirm Request**: Confirm that you are ready to receive our response.

Note: Please refer to the provided instructions for specific requirements and guidelines before filling out this form. If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to contact us for assistance.
