# Real Estate Lead Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Lead Intake Form is used to collect information from potential clients about their real estate inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete the form, please fill out each field accurately and completely.
- Start with the contact information section, where you will select your status as a home owner, real estate agent, or other.
- Next, select the type of property you are inquiring about, such as residential or commercial.
- Then, provide the specific type of property you are interested in, if applicable.
- After that, select the location of the property, such as city or state.
- Next, enter the location of the property, such as an address or specific geographic area.
- Choose how you would like to be contacted, such as by phone or email.
- Enter your preferred contact method, such as a phone number or email address.
- If necessary, provide any additional information about the lead.
- Finally, schedule a follow-up date and time with our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Information** (Contact Information, Select One, Required): This is where you select your status as a home owner, real estate agent, or other.
  • If you select "Home Owner", this form is intended for your personal real estate needs.
  • If you select "Real Estate Agent", this form is intended for your business real estate needs.
  • If you select "Other", please enter a brief description of your affiliation.
- **Property Type** (Property Type, Select Multiple, Required): This is where you select the type of property you are inquiring about.
  • Select one or multiple options that apply, such as residential or commercial.
- **Property Type Value** (Property Type Value, Text, Required): This is where you enter the specific type of property you are interested in, if not already selected above.
  • For example, if you selected "Residential", you might enter "single-family home" or "condo".
- **Location** (Location, Select One, Required): This is where you select the location of the property, such as city or state.
  • Choose the option that best describes the location of the property.
- **Location Value** (Location Value, Text, Required): This is where you enter the specific location of the property, if applicable.
  • For example, if you selected "City", you might enter "New York City" or "Los Angeles".
- **Contact Method** (Contact Method, Select One, Required): This is where you choose how you would like to be contacted.
  • Select one option that best fits your needs, such as phone or email.
- **Contact Method Value** (Contact Method Value, Text, Required): This is where you enter your preferred contact method, such as a phone number or email address.
  • For example, if you selected "Phone", you might enter "123-456-7890".
- **Lead Info** (Lead Info, Note, Required): This is where you provide any additional information about the lead, if necessary.
  • This field is for your own notes and reference.
- **Followup Info** (Followup Info, Text, Required): This is where you schedule a follow-up date and time with our team.
  • Enter the date and time that suits you best.
- **Notes** (Notes, Text, Required): This is where you enter any additional comments or notes about the lead.
  • This field is for your own reference.
- **Email** (Email, Email, Required): This is where you enter your email address.
  • This is the address where we will send any follow-up information.
- **Phone** (Phone, Text, Required): This is where you enter your phone number.
  • This is the number where we will call you for follow-up information.
- **Followup Date** (Followup Date, Date, Required): This is where you schedule a follow-up date with our team.
  • Enter the date you would like to be followed up on.
- **Followup Time** (Followup Time, Time, Required): This is where you schedule a follow-up time with our team.
  • Enter the time you would like to be followed up on.
