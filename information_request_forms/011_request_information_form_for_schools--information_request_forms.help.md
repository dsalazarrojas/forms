# Request Information Form For Schools - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Request Information Form For Schools is a general-purpose form designed to gather information from schools.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the first page, enter your school's information in the "Request Information" text field.
2. On the next page, select the options that apply to your school by checking the relevant boxes in the "Select A School" field.
3. Enter your school's email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your school's phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Request Information
**Request Information** (`request_information_form_for_schools_1`, text, required: false)

Enter a brief description of your school's information request.

### Select A School
**Select A School** (`request_information_form_for_schools_2`, select_multiple, required: false)

Indicate whether your school is a public or private institution.

* Select 'Yes' for public schools and 'No' for private schools.

### Email
**Email** (`request_information_form_for_schools_3`, email, required: false)

Enter your school's official email address.

### Phone
**Phone** (`request_information_form_for_schools_4`, text, required: false)

Enter your school's official phone number.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when filling out the form to ensure your school's information request is processed efficiently.
* If you need to submit additional information, please attach supporting documents with your form submission.
* Our team will review your request and respond promptly.
