# 10 Year Strategy Plan Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 10 Year Strategy Plan Questionnaire is designed to gather information about your personal and financial situation, goals, and investment preferences. This form will help us understand your needs and provide guidance on creating a tailored investment plan for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your personal information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Enter your date of birth and the date you are completing this form.
3. Provide your annual gross income, current investment portfolio value, and total debt outstanding.
4. Choose your primary financial goal and describe it if you select "Other".
5. Select the number of years you plan to invest and your risk tolerance level.
6. Choose your preferred investment categories.
7. Rate your satisfaction with your current investment strategy and provide a rating for your current strategy.
8. Rate your satisfaction with this questionnaire and provide an overall rating for this form.
9. Optionally, provide additional feedback or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your government-issued identification.
- **Email address** (`email_address`, email, required): This is the email address to which we will send the confirmation of your completed form.
- **Phone number** (`phone_number`, phone, required): Include your country code in your phone number.
- **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **Date of questionnaire** (`questionnaire_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are completing this form.
- **Annual gross income** (`annual_income`, decimal, required): Enter your annual gross income in your local currency.
- **Current investment portfolio value** (`current_investment_value`, decimal, required): Enter the total market value of your investment portfolio in your local currency.
- **Total debt outstanding** (`debt_total`, decimal, required): Include mortgages, loans, and credit cards.
- **Primary financial goal** (`primary_goal`, select_one, required): Choose your primary financial goal from the options provided. If you choose "Other", describe your goal.
- **Describe "Other" financial goal** (`other_goal_description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your financial goal if you selected "Other".
- **Desired time horizon** (`time_horizon_years`, integer, required): Enter the number of years you plan to invest.
- **Risk tolerance level** (`risk_tolerance`, select_one, required): Select the level of risk you are comfortable with.
- **Preferred investment categories** (`investment_preferences`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the investment categories that appeal to you.
- **Satisfaction with current strategy** (`satisfied_with_current_strategy`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with your current investment strategy.
- **Current strategy rating** (`current_strategy_rating`, integer, required): Rate your current investment strategy from 1 to 10.
- **Satisfaction with questionnaire** (`satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with this questionnaire.
- **Overall rating** (`overall_rating`, integer, required): Rate this form from 1 to 10.
- **Aspects applied to your experience** (`experience_aspects`, select_multiple, required): Select all aspects that applied to your experience with this form.
- **Additional feedback or suggestions** (`open_feedback`, text, required): Share any thoughts or suggestions you have.
- **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required): Use this space for any additional comments or information you would like to share with us.
