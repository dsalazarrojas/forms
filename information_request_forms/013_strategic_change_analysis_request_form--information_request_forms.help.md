# Strategic Change Analysis Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Strategic Change Analysis Request Form is a tool used to collect and evaluate information about proposed changes to the organization. It helps in assessing the feasibility of changes, identifying potential risks and benefits, and ensuring that all relevant stakeholders are informed and involved in the decision-making process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the "Name" and "email" fields, respectively.
2. Provide a brief description of the proposed change in the "proposal" field.
3. Explain the justification for the proposed change in the "justification" field.
4. Estimate the potential impact of the change on the organization in the "impact" field.
5. Identify potential risks associated with the change in the "risk" field.
6. Describe the proposed mitigation strategies for the identified risks in the "mitigation" field.
7. Outline the proposed implementation plan and timeline in the "implementation" field.
8. Specify the monitoring and review procedures for the change in the "monitoring" field.
9. Set the status of the change to "Approved", "Pending", or "Rejected" in the "Status" field.
10. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "notes" field.
11. Finally, submit the form and ensure that it is reviewed and updated by the "submitter", "reviewer", and "approver" fields, respectively.
