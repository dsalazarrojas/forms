# Open Access Courseware Distribution Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Open Access Courseware Distribution Request Form is used to request access to course materials for courses. This form helps administrators or support staff review and manage these requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Distribution Request` field with a brief description of the requested course materials.
2. Provide your `Name`, `Email`, and `Department` in their respective fields.
3. Select the `Access Type` you need for the course materials (Open Access or Internal Access).
4. Choose how you would like to be contacted for follow-up (Phone, Email, or Chat).
5. Select the method of distribution for the course materials (Automated or Human Review).
6. Choose the `Review Status` (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
7. If your request is rejected, provide the `Reason for Rejection` in the comments field.
8. Enter the `Request ID` and `User Request ID` (if provided).
9. Optionally, enter the `Date Requested` and `Submitted By` fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Distribution Request** (`Distribution Request`, text, required): A brief description of the requested course materials.
* **Name** (`Name`, text, required): Your full name.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Your institutional email address.
* **Department** (`Department`, text, required): The department you belong to.
* **Access Type** (`Access Type`, select_multiple, required): Select Open Access or Internal Access for the course materials.
* **Contact Method** (`Contact Method`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted for follow-up (Phone, Email, or Chat).
* **Course ID** (`Course ID`, number, required): The ID number of the course you are requesting access to.
* **Courseware Type** (`Courseware Type`, select_one, required): Select Open Access or Internal Access for the course materials.
* **Distribution Method** (`Distribution Method`, select_one, required): Choose Automated or Human Review for the course materials.
* **Review Status** (`Review Status`, select_one, required): Choose Pending, Approved, or Rejected for your request.
* **Reason for Rejection** (`Reason for Rejection`, note, optional): If your request is rejected, provide the reason here.
* **Request ID** (`Request ID`, number, required): A unique ID assigned to your request.
* **User Request ID** (`User Request ID`, number, optional): Your institution's unique ID (if provided).
* **Date Requested** (`Date Requested`, date, optional): The date you requested access to the course materials.
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, text, optional): Your name or the name of the person submitting the request on your behalf.
* **Submitted By Email** (`Submitted By Email`, email, optional): The email of the person submitting the request on your behalf.
* **Submitted By Phone** (`Submitted By Phone`, text, optional): The phone number of the person submitting the request on your behalf.
