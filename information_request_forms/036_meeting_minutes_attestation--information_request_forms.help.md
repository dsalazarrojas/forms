# meeting_minutes_attestation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The meeting minutes attestation form is used to validate the authenticity of meeting minutes. The form is completed by the person attesting to the meeting minutes, providing their information and signature to confirm the accuracy and authenticity of the meeting minutes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the attester_name field with your name.
2. Enter the name of the person or entity being attested to in the attestee_name field.
3. Enter the name of the person attesting to the meeting minutes in the attester_name field.
4. Enter the title of the person attesting to the meeting minutes in the attestor_title field.
5. Enter the organization of the person attesting to the meeting minutes in the attestor_organization field.
6. Enter your contact information, including email, phone, fax, address, etc.
7. Enter the country and zip code of the person attesting to the meeting minutes.
8. Enter the city and state of the person attesting to the meeting minutes.
9. If the organization is not listed, enter the other organization information.
10. Enter the type of organization the person attesting to the meeting minutes is associated with.
11. Enter any additional comments or information about the attestation.
12. Finally, enter the date and time of your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **attester_name** (text, required): Enter your name as the person attesting to the meeting minutes.
* **attester_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the person or entity being attested to.
* **attester_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the person attesting to the meeting minutes.
* **attested_by** (text, required): Enter the name of the person attesting to the meeting minutes.
* **attestor_title** (text, optional): Enter the title of the person attesting to the meeting minutes.
* **attestor_organization** (text, optional): Enter the organization of the person attesting to the meeting minutes.
* **attester_contact** (text, optional): Enter your contact information, including email, phone, fax, address, etc.
* **attester_country** (text, optional): Enter the country of the person attesting to the meeting minutes.
* **attester_zip** (text, optional): Enter the zip code of the person attesting to the meeting minutes.
* **attester_city** (text, optional): Enter the city of the person attesting to the meeting minutes.
* **attester_state** (text, optional): Enter the state of the person attesting to the meeting minutes.
* **attestor_organization_other** (text, optional): Enter other organization information if the organization does not have a listed name.
* **attestor_organization_type** (text, optional): Enter the type of organization the person attesting to the meeting minutes is associated with.
* **attestor_comments** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the attestation.
* **attested_by** (date, optional): Enter the date and time of your signature.
* **attested_by** (time, optional): Enter the date and time of your signature.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- Double-check your contact information and signature date and time for accuracy.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact the meeting minutes administrator for guidance.
