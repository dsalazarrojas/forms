# Mobile App Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit mobile apps to various app stores, including Apple, Google, and Amazon. It ensures that all necessary information is provided correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the app store where you want to submit your app (Apple, Google, or Amazon).
2. Fill in the app's name and version.
3. Choose the app type (Mobile, Web, or Desktop).
4. Select the app platform (iOS, Android, or Both).
5. Provide the app's category, description, URL, and URL category.
6. Enter the submitter's name, email, phone number, title, department, job title, address, and company.
7. Select whether to submit to each app store.
8. Review and confirm your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submit To**: Select the app store to which you want to submit your app (Apple, Google, or Amazon).
* **App Name**: Enter the name of your mobile app.
* **App Version**: Enter the version of your mobile app.
* **App Type**: Choose the type of your app (Mobile, Web, or Desktop).
* **App Platform**: Select the platform of your app (iOS, Android, or Both).
* **App Category**: Enter the category of your app.
* **App Description**: Describe your app in a few sentences.
* **App URL**: Enter the URL of your app's store page.
* **App URL Category**: Enter the category of your app's store page.
* **Submitter**: Provide your name, email, and other contact information.
* **Submit Date**: Enter the date you want to submit your app (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Submit Time**: Enter the time you want to submit your app (format: HH:MM:SS).
* **Submitter Agreement**: Select the agreements you agree to (I have read and agree to the terms and conditions, I have read and agree to the privacy policy).
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your submission.
* **Assigned To**: Choose who you want to assign your submission to (chatjimmy or Other).
* **Submitter Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your submission.

## Tips

* Ensure that you fill in all required fields correctly and efficiently.
* Double-check that you are submitting to the correct app store.
* Review your submission carefully before confirming.
