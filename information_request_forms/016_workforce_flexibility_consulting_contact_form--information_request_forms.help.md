# Workforce Flexibility Consulting Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect contact information from users seeking workforce flexibility consulting services. It helps our team to better understand your needs and preferences, allowing us to provide more effective support and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out this form with accurate and complete information about your needs.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you are providing the correct information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Work Location** (`work_location`, select_one): Choose how you work, whether you are **reported to** (managed directly), **office** (in an office setting), **remote** (working from home), or **other** (please specify).
* **Name** (`name`, text): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text): Enter your job title or role within your organization.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text): Provide any additional comments or context about your needs.
* **Location** (`location`, select_multiple): Choose multiple locations you are interested in working from, such as **New York**, **Los Angeles**, **Chicago**, or **Austin**.
* **Reported To** (`reported_to`, select_one): Select how you are reported to, whether it's your **Manager**, **Director**, or **Vice President**.
* **Date** (`date`, date): Enter the date you are interested in consulting our workforce flexibility services.
* **Time** (`time`, time): Enter the time you are interested in consulting our workforce flexibility services.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, number): Not required, but if you know your ID number, please enter it.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, time): Not required, but if you know the time you submitted, please enter it.
* **Lead Source** (`lead_source`, select_multiple): Choose how you learned about our workforce flexibility services, such as **Phone**, **Email**, **Social Media**, or **Referral**.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text): Provide any additional context or notes about your needs.
* **Lead Type** (`lead_type`, select_one): Select whether this is a **New**, **Existing**, or **Unknown** lead.
* **Submitted From** (`submitted_from`, select_one): Select where you submitted this form from, such as **Phone**, **Email**, **Social Media**, or **Referral**.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, number): Not required, but if you know your follow-up number, please enter it.
* **Follow-up By** (`follow_up_by`, number): Not required, but if you know the follow-up number by who, please enter it.
* **Lead Status** (`lead_status`, select_one): Select the status of your lead, whether it's **Qualified**, **Referred**, or **Not Qualified**.
* **Lead ID** (`lead_id`, number): Not required, but if you know your lead ID, please enter it.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, select_one): Select the person this form was submitted to, whether it's your **Manager**, **Director**, or **Vice President**.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date): Not required, but if you know the follow-up date, please enter it.
* **Submitted By Name** (`submitted_by_name`, text): Enter the name of the person submitting this form.

Please review your form carefully before submitting it. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us directly.
