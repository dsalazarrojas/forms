# Factory Sustainability Office Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Sustainability Office Contact Form is designed to gather contact information and sustainability-related inquiries from visitors to the Factory Sustainability Office.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Choose the correct category and department to best represent your concern.
3. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Message" field.
4. Review your submission for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (\*)
* **First Name** (`first_name`, *optional*): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, *required*): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, *optional*): Enter your email address.
* **Organization** (`organization`, *optional*): If you represent an organization, enter its name.
* **Message** (`message`, *required*): Enter a brief description of your sustainability-related inquiry, concern, or request.
* **Phone** (`phone`, *optional*): Enter a phone number where you can be reached.
* **Category** (`category`, *optional*): Choose the category that best represents your concern (e.g. Green, Blue, Yellow).
* **Department** (`department`, *optional*): Choose the department that best represents your concern (e.g. Engineering, Marketing, HR).
* **Date** (`date`, *optional*): Enter the date of your concern (if applicable).
* **Time** (`time`, *optional*): Enter the time of your concern (if applicable).
* **Note** (`note`, *optional*): Enter any additional comments or notes.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your email address is accurate to receive a response from us.
* If you have a specific request or inquiry, please provide as much detail as possible in the "Message" field.
* If you have any attachments or supporting documents, please attach them to your email or provide a link to them in your message.
