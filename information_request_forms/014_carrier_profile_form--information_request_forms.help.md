# Carrier Profile Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is intended for carriers to provide their carrier profile information for registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the required fields, indicated by the asterisk symbol (*).
2. Provide accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.
3. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Carrier ID Number** (`carrier_id`): A unique number assigned to your carrier by the company.
- **Registration Date** (`registration_date`): The date you first created your carrier profile.
- **Legal Company Name** (`company_name`): The registered name of your business.
- **DBA or Trade Name** (`dba_name`): The name you do business under, if different from the legal company name.
- **Company Type** (`company_type`): Select the type of business structure you have (e.g., Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, LLC, etc.).
- **DOT Number** (`dot_number`): Department of Transportation number assigned to your carrier.
- **MC Number** (`mc_number`): Motor carrier number assigned to your carrier.
- **EIN or Tax ID Number** (`ein_number`): Employer Identification Number or Tax ID Number.
- **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`): The number of years you've been operating your business.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): Your primary contact details, which should include your name, job title, email address, and phone number.
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Your full name.
- **Contact Title** (`contact_title`): Your job title.
- **Email Address** (`contact_email`): Your primary email address.
- **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`): Your primary phone number.
- **Fax Number** (`contact_fax`): Your fax number (optional).
- **Business Address** (`business_address`): The physical address of your business.
- **City** (`business_city`): The city where your business is located.
- **State** (`business_state`): The state or province where your business is located.
- **ZIP Code** (`business_zip`): The postal code of your business.
- **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`): The address for mail or package delivery (if different from the business address).
- **Operations Information** (`operations_info`): Any additional details about your operations (optional).
- **Primary Service Area** (`service_area`): The region where you operate (e.g., Local, Regional, National, International).
- **States Where You Operate** (`states_operated`): Select all states where you operate your business.
- **Primary Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`): The type of equipment you primarily use (e.g., Dry van, Refrigerated, etc.).
- **Number of Power Units** (`number_of_trucks`): The number of power units you have.
- **Number of Trailers** (`number_of_trailers`): The number of trailers you have (optional).
- **Types of Cargo Hauled** (`cargo_types`): The types of cargo you handle (select all that apply).
- **Hazmat Certified** (`hazmat_certified`): Whether you have a hazardous materials endorsement.
- **Insurance Information** (`insurance_info`): Any additional details about your insurance (optional).
- **Liability Coverage Amount** (`liability_coverage`): The liability coverage amount of your insurance policy.
- **Cargo Insurance Amount** (`cargo_insurance`): The cargo coverage amount of your insurance policy.
- **Insurance Carrier Name** (`insurance_carrier_name`): The name of your insurance carrier.
- **Policy Expiration Date** (`policy_expiry`): The date your insurance policy expires.
- **CSA Safety Rating** (`safety_rating`): Your CSA safety rating (e.g., Satisfactory, Conditional, etc.).
- **Banking Information** (`banking_info`): Information for payment setup (optional).
- **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_preference`): How you want to receive payments.
- **Factoring Company Name** (`factoring_company`): If you use a factoring company, provide their name (optional).
- **Business References Available** (`references`): Whether you have business references available to provide (yes, no, or attached).
- **Additional Information** (`additional_comments`): Any other information about your business (optional).

Note: This guide is for reference only and may not cover all possible scenarios. If you're unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or support team for clarification.
