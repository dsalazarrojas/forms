# Influencer Collaboration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Influencer Collaboration Form is a tool designed to collect information from potential collaborators in the influencer industry. This form helps us connect with professionals who have expertise or interest in a specific area, and it's intended to facilitate mutually beneficial collaboration opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. In the "Description" field, provide a brief overview of your work or expertise.
4. If you have any additional comments or questions, you can enter them in the "Description" field.
5. Finally, enter the category of your area of expertise in the "Category" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your full name, as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email**: Enter your professional email address. Please ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Category**: Select the category that best describes your area of expertise. This will help us understand your specialization.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of your work or expertise. This can include information about your social media presence, blog, or other relevant details.
* **Description (again)**: This is an optional field for any additional comments or questions you may have. You can use it to ask about collaboration opportunities or provide more context about your work.
* **Form Title**: This is a text field for you to enter a title that you would like to be known by. It's optional, but can be helpful for us to understand your brand or identity.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
* We'll review your submission and be in touch if we're interested in collaborating.
