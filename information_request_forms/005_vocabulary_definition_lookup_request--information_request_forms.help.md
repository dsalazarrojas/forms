# Vocabulary Definition Lookup Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about user preferences and submit a form for further processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Request Form" page with any relevant details.
2. Proceed to the "Submit Form" page and review your submission.
3. If you have any user preferences, such as language or formatting, you can specify them in the "User Preferences" page.
4. Click the "Submit Button" to submit your form for further processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Form** (`request_form`, `text`, **required**): Provide any relevant details about your vocabulary lookup request.
* **Submit Form** (`submit_form`, `text`, **required**): Confirm your submission by typing in the exact text as presented on the previous page.
* **User Preferences** (`user_preferences`, `text`, **optional**): Specify any language or formatting preferences for your submission.
* **Submit Button** (`submit_button`, `text`, **required**): Confirm your submission by clicking this button.
