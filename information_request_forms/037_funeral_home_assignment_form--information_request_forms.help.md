# Funeral Home Assignment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Funeral Home Assignment Form is used to collect information about a deceased person's funeral home assignment, including details about the deceased's identity, funeral home contact information, and assignment status. This form is used to update the assignment status in the system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the deceased's last name.
2. Fill in the deceased's first name.
3. Enter the deceased's date of birth, if known.
4. Enter the deceased's date of death.
5. Enter the name of the funeral home assigned to the deceased.
6. Enter the address of the funeral home assigned to the deceased.
7. Enter the name of the insurance company assigned to the deceased.
8. Enter the contact name for the funeral home or insurance company.
9. Enter the contact phone number for the funeral home or insurance company.
10. Enter the email address for the funeral home or insurance company.
11. Enter the assigned amount for the funeral home services.
12. Enter the date the funeral home was assigned to the deceased.
13. Enter the name of the person or organization that assigned the funeral home.
14. Select the status of the assignment (assigned, pending, or denied).
15. Enter any additional comments about the assignment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Deceased Last Name (deceased_last_name)** 
   Enter the deceased's last name.
* **Deceased First Name (deceased_first_name)**
   Enter the deceased's first name.
* **Deceased Date of Birth (deceased_date_of_birth)** 
   Enter the deceased's date of birth. If unknown, leave this field blank.
* **Deceased Date of Death (deceased_date_of_death)** 
   Enter the date of death using the date format: MM/DD/YY.
* **Funeral Home Name (funeral_home_name)**
   Enter the name of the funeral home assigned to the deceased.
* **Funeral Home Address (funeral_home_address)**
   Enter the address of the funeral home assigned to the deceased.
* **Assigned Insurance Company (assigned_insurance_company)**
   Enter the name of the insurance company that assigned the funeral home.
* **Contact Name (contact_name)**
   Enter the name of the contact person for the funeral home or insurance company.
* **Contact Phone (contact_phone)**
   Enter the phone number of the contact person for the funeral home or insurance company.
* **Contact Email (contact_email)**
   Enter the email address of the contact person for the funeral home or insurance company.
* **Assigned Amount (assigned_amount)**
   Enter the amount assigned for the funeral home services.
* **Assigned Date (assigned_date)**
   Enter the date the funeral home was assigned to the deceased.
* **Assigned By (assigned_by)**
   Enter the name of the person or organization that assigned the funeral home.
* **Assigned Status (assigned_status)**
   Select the status of the assignment (assigned, pending, or denied).
* **Additional Comments (additional_comments)**
   Enter any additional comments or information about the assignment.
