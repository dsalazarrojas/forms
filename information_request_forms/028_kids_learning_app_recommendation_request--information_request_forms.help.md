# Kids Learning App Recommendation Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their learning needs and preferences, in order to recommend suitable kids learning apps.

## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Fill in User Information
Provide your basic user information, such as name, username, or any other identifying information that you're comfortable sharing.

### Step 2: Choose Your Learning Preferences
Describe your learning preferences and needs on this page. This will help us better understand what you're looking for in a learning app.

### Step 3: Enter Contact Information
Provide your email address and phone number. This will allow us to contact you with personalized recommendations.

### Step 4: Select Suggested Apps
Choose the learning apps you're interested in or currently use. This will help us narrow down recommendations that fit your tastes.

### Step 5: Add App Comments (Optional)
If you have any feedback or comments about the apps you've tried, let us know! This will help us make more accurate recommendations.

### Step 6: Enter Contact Details
Provide your contact email address and phone number. This is crucial for us to get back to you with personalized recommendations.

### Step 7: Add Additional Notes
If you have any additional information that might be helpful for the recommendations, feel free to add it here!

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Information (Page 1)
* **User Information (Text)**: Enter your basic user information, such as name, username, or any other identifying information that you're comfortable sharing.

### Learning Preferences (Page 2)
* **Learning Preferences (Text)**: Describe your learning preferences and needs. What type of content do you enjoy learning? Do you prefer interactive or educational apps?

### Contact Information (Page 3)
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your contact email address, so we can get back to you with personalized recommendations.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number, so we can contact you with updates and recommendations.

### Suggested Apps (Page 4)
* **Suggested Apps (Select Multiple)**: Choose the learning apps you're interested in or currently use. This will help us narrow down recommendations that fit your taste.

### App Comments (Page 5)
* **App Comments (Text)**: If you have any feedback or comments about the apps you've tried, let us know! This will help us make more accurate recommendations.

### Additional Notes (Page 6)
* **Additional Notes (Note)**: If you have any additional information that might be helpful for the recommendations, feel free to add it here!
