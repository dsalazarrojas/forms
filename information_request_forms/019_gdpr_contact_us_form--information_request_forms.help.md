This form is for individuals to request information or provide feedback to our organization regarding the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). This form is a contact us page where users can provide their contact information and a detailed message, which will be reviewed and replied to by our team.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name: Please fill out your first name and last name in the first two fields.
2. Provide your email address: Enter your email address in the third field. This will help us respond to your query in a timely manner.
3. Add your contact number: If you want to be reached via phone, please fill in your phone number in the fourth field.
4. Mention your company (optional): If your query is related to your company or organization, provide a brief description of it in the fifth field.
5. Specify your job function: If you have a specific role within your company, please indicate it in the sixth field.
6. Select your department: If you are reaching out regarding a specific department within your company, provide it in the seventh field.
7. Leave a message: In the last field, type a detailed description of your reason for contacting us.

## gdpr-contact-us-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a contact us page for individuals to request information or provide feedback regarding the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to allow us to respond to your query in a timely manner.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you want to be reached via phone, enter your phone number.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, optional): If your query is related to your company or organization, provide a brief description of it.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, `text`, optional): If you have a specific role within your company, please indicate it.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, optional): If you are reaching out regarding a specific department within your company, provide it.
* **Message** (`message`, `note`, required): Type a detailed description of your reason for contacting us.

## Tips
* Please fill out all required fields to ensure we can respond to your query accurately.
* If you have any attachments or supporting documents, you can attach them via email after submitting the form.
* Our team will review and reply to your query in a timely manner.
