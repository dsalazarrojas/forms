# Workforce Communication Channel Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for employees to submit requests or concerns regarding workforce communication channels. It allows them to contact the relevant department or team for assistance or clarification. By using this form, employees can ensure that their messages are directed to the right people, facilitating timely and accurate responses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Contact Requestor" field with your name and title, as this information will be used to direct the communication to the correct person.
2. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field, so that the team can reach out to you for further discussions.
3. If you have a phone number, please enter it in the "Contact Phone" field. This will allow the team to contact you via phone.
4. Provide a brief description of your request or concern in the "Description" field.
5. Select the relevant department from the "Department" dropdown menu, so that the message is directed to the correct team.
6. Finally, click the "Submit" button to send your request or concern to the relevant department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Requestor** (`contact_requestor`, text, required): Your name and title, which will be used to direct the communication to the correct person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): The email address where the team can reach out to you for further discussions.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Your phone number, which will be used for phone contact.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): A brief description of your request or concern.
* **Department** (`department`, select one, optional): The department that will handle your request or concern.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to send your request or concern to the relevant department.
 
Note: Please ensure that you have filled out all required fields (Contact Requestor, Contact Email, and Submit) before submitting the form.
