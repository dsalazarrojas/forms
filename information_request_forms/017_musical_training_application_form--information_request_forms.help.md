## Musical Training Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather personal and contact information from users who wish to apply for musical training. The form aims to collect comprehensive details from applicants to facilitate a smooth and efficient training process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the form with the requested information**
   - Ensure that all required fields (if any) are filled in.
   - Use the available options for the "Slot" field to choose your preferred appointment time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Info** (Personal Info, text, required: false): Please fill in your personal information, including your name, contact number, and any other relevant details you'd like to share with us.
* **Contact** (Contact, text, required: false): Provide your contact information so that we can reach out to you about your training slot.
* **Skills and History** (Skills and History, text, required: false): Share your musical skills and experience to help us understand your background and what you're looking for in a training program.
* **Slot** (Slot, select_one, required: false): Choose your preferred appointment time from the options provided. This will help us schedule your training sessions accordingly.
* **Confirm Appointment** (Confirm Appointment, text, required: false): Confirm your chosen appointment slot by typing it in the provided field.

## Tips
