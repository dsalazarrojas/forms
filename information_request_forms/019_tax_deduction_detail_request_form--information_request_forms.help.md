# Tax Deduction Detail Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request detailed information about tax deductions from tax authorities. Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information to ensure your request is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Ensure you provide all required fields.
- Check the boxes for any supporting documents you have uploaded.
- Select the correct tax year, submitted to department, and submitted to unit from the options provided.
- Enter the tax deduction amount, description, and submitted on date accurately.
- Make sure to select the correct firm or firm type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tax Deduction Form** (`tax_deduction_form`, text, optional): This is the main content field where you can enter any additional information you have about your tax deduction request.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, optional): Upload any supporting documents relevant to your tax deduction request.
* **Tax Year** (`tax_year`, number, optional): Select the tax year for which you are requesting tax deduction.
* **Tax Deduction Amount** (`tax_deduction_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of tax deduction you are requesting.
* **Tax Deduction Description** (`tax_deduction_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the tax deduction you are requesting.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, optional): Enter the date you submitted your tax deduction request.
* **Requested By** (`requested_by`, select_one, optional): Select who requested the tax deduction (Requesting Firm, Finance Team, or Individual).
* **Requesting Firm** (`requested_by_firm`, select_multiple, optional): Select the firm that requested the tax deduction (Accounting Firm, Large Firm, or Small Firm).
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, select_one, optional): Select the department to which you submitted your tax deduction request (Accounting Department, Financial Department, or Tax Authority).
* **Submitted To Unit** (`submitted_to_unit`, select_multiple, optional): Select the unit to which you submitted your tax deduction request (Tax Authority, Unit A, or Unit B).
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, optional): Select who submitted the tax deduction request (Firm Representative, Team Member, or Individual).
* **Submitted By Firm** (`submitted_by_firm`, select_multiple, optional): Select the firm that submitted the tax deduction request (Accounting Firm, Financial Firm, or Other Firm).
* **Supporting Documents Description** (`supporting_documents_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the supporting documents you have uploaded.
* **Supporting Documents Uploaded** (`supporting_documents_uploaded`, select_multiple, optional): Check the box if you have uploaded supporting documents.
* **Submitted On Date** (`submitted_on_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you submitted your tax deduction request.
* **Submitted On Time** (`submitted_on_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you submitted your tax deduction request.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure you provide all required fields.
- If you have any doubts, contact the relevant department or firm for assistance.
