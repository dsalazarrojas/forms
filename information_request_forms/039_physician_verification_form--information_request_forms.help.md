# Physician Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physician Verification Form is used to collect information about a physician's credentials and contact details to verify their identity and validate their medical practice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and ensure you have the necessary information ready.
2. Enter your physician's First Name and Last Name in the designated fields.
3. Enter your physician's address in the Medical Facility Address field.
4. Enter your physician's title in the Physician Title field.
5. Enter your specialty and medical school in the respective fields.
6. Enter your graduation year if applicable.
7. Enter your medical license number, state, and postal code.
8. Enter your malpractice insurance start and end dates.
9. Review and confirm all the information provided before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your physician's first name.
  - Required: No
  - Type: text
  - Hint: e.g., John
- **Last Name**: Enter your physician's last name.
  - Required: No
  - Type: text
  - Hint: e.g., Smith
- **Address**: Enter your physician's medical practice address.
  - Required: No
  - Type: text
  - Hint: e.g., 123 Main St
- **Physician Title**: Enter your physician's title (e.g., MD, DO, etc.).
  - Required: Yes
  - Type: text
  - Hint: e.g., MD
- **Specialty**: Enter your area of specialization.
  - Required: No
  - Type: text
  - Hint: e.g., cardiology
- **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
  - Required: No
  - Type: text
  - Hint: e.g., 555-555-5555
- **Contact Email**: Enter your email address.
  - Required: No
  - Type: email
  - Hint: e.g., john.smith@example.com
- **Medical License Number**: Enter your medical license number.

  - Required: No
