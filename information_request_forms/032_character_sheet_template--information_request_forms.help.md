# Character Sheet Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you create a character sheet for your role-playing game character. It collects essential information about your character's identity, attributes, abilities, and other important details. Please fill out this form accurately to help you and others keep track of your character's progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the character's basic details: name, class or profession, and level or rank.
2. Choose your character's alignment system and attribute scores (strength, dexterity, constitution, intelligence, wisdom, and charisma).
3. Enter your character's combat and health stats: maximum hit points, current hit points, armor class value, and initiative modifier bonus.
4. List your character's primary and secondary equipped items, including primary and secondary weapons, armor, and other equipment.
5. Describe any miscellaneous items in your inventory.
6. List your character's abilities and special skills.
7. Confirm that you've accurately filled out the character sheet details.
8. Record the date of last update.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Character Full Name**: Enter your character's full name.
* **Character Class or Profession**: Enter your character's profession or class, for example, Fighter, Rogue, Wizard, or Medic.
* **Character Level or Rank**: Enter your character's current level or rank.
* **Character Race or Species**: Enter your character's race or species, for example, Human, Elf, Dwarf, or Alien.
* **Character Alignment System**: Select the most accurate description of your character's alignment.
* **Strength Attribute Score**: Enter your character's strength attribute score (physical power and carrying capacity).
* **Dexterity Attribute Score**: Enter your character's dexterity attribute score (agility, reflexes, and balance).
* **Constitution Attribute Score**: Enter your character's constitution attribute score (health, stamina, and vital force).
* **Intelligence Attribute Score**: Enter your character's intelligence attribute score (mental acuity, recall, and analytical skill).
* **Wisdom Attribute Score**: Enter your character's wisdom attribute score (perception, intuition, and willpower).
* **Charisma Attribute Score**: Enter your character's charisma attribute score (confidence, eloquence, and leadership).
* **Maximum Hit Points (HP)**: Enter your character's total maximum hit points.
* **Current Hit Points (HP)**: Enter your character's current hit points.
* **Armor Class (AC) Value**: Enter your character's defensive capability.
* **Initiative Modifier Bonus**: Enter your character's initiative modifier bonus.
* **Primary Weapon Equipped**: Describe your character's primary weapon.
* **Secondary Weapon or Shield**: Describe any secondary weapon or shield.
* **Armor Equipped Description**: Describe your character's armor.
* **Miscellaneous Items in Inventory**: List 2-3 significant items.
* **Gold and Currency Total**: Enter your character's gold and currency total.
* **Primary Ability or Feat**: Describe one major skill.
* **Secondary Ability or Feat**: Describe any other skills or abilities.
* **Language Proficiencies List**: List the languages your character can speak.
* **Confirmation of Character Sheet Details**: Confirm that these details accurately reflect your character.
* **Date of Last Update**: Enter the current date.
* **Additional Character Notes**: Enter any other relevant information or context.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure your character sheet is complete and up-to-date.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to your game's rules or consult with a game master.
* Double-check your character's details before confirming the accuracy of the character sheet.
* Update this form regularly to reflect changes in your character's abilities and circumstances.
