# post_office_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "post_office_claim_form" is used to report incidents of damaged parcels. Fill this form to provide details about the incident, and our team will assist you in resolving the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, providing all required and optional information.
2. Please ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
3. If you have any supporting documentation or images, please attach them to the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Address**: Provide your full address, including house number and street name.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number for further communication.
* **Incident Details**: Describe the incident that caused the damage to your parcel in as much detail as possible.
* **Description of damaged parcel**: Describe the type and condition of the damaged parcel.
* **Customer Signature**: Date and sign the form to confirm that the information provided is accurate and true.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you need help with filling out the form, do not hesitate to contact our support team.
* Ensure that you have attached any supporting documentation or images related to the incident.
