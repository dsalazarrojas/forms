# Document Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Document Delivery Form is a tool used to collect contact information and delivery details from users. It is meant to facilitate the delivery of documents, and by completing this form, users can provide their contact details and the address where they would like to receive their documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the form with your contact details**. This includes providing your name, email address, and phone number.
2. **Select the "Receiver Group"**. Choose the group or category under which you would like to receive the document.
3. **Enter your delivery address**. This is the address where you would like the document to be delivered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details (required)**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number so we can contact you regarding the document delivery.
* **Receiver Group (required)**: Select the category or group under which you would like to receive the document. This will help us ensure that the document is delivered to the correct group.
* **Delivery Address (required)**: Enter the address where you would like to receive your document. This is the exact address where we will deliver the document.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as required).
* Double-check your contact details and delivery address for accuracy.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, contact us at form@gic.mx.
* You can use this form to request delivery of documents from anywhere in the world.
