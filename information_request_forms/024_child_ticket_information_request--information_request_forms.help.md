# Child Ticket Information Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals or organizations requesting child tickets for various events. The information collected will help us understand your needs and preferences for the tickets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the date you are requesting the information for.
2. Provide your first and last name, as well as your email address and phone number.
3. Specify the name of the event you are inquiring about and its date.
4. Indicate the type of tickets you are requesting and the number of child and adult tickets needed.
5. Mention the age range of the children attending.
6. State the purpose of your request, such as personal attendance, school group, or charity event.
7. If applicable, specify the type of organization you represent.
8. Mention your approximate budget for the tickets and whether you need financial assistance.
9. Indicate if you have any seating preferences or accessibility requirements.
10. Provide any additional information or special requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Date** (1): Enter the date you are requesting the information for.
* **Requester First Name** (2): Enter your legal first name.
* **Requester Last Name** (3): Enter your legal last name.
* **Requester Email** (4): Enter your primary email address for contact.
* **Requester Phone** (5): Enter your primary phone number for contact.
* **Organization Name** (6): If you are representing an organization, enter its name.
* **Event Name** (7): Enter the name of the event you are inquiring about.
* **Event Date** (8): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Location** (9): Enter the location or venue of the event.
* **Ticket Type Requested** (10): Select the type of tickets you are requesting (e.g., General Admission, VIP, Group tickets).
* **Number of Child Tickets** (11): Enter the number of child tickets you need.
* **Number of Adult Tickets** (12): If applicable, enter the number of adult tickets you need.
* **Child Age Range** (13): Enter the age range of the children attending.
* **Purpose of Request** (14): Specify the purpose of your request (e.g., personal attendance, school group).
* **Organization Type** (15): If you represent an organization, select the type of organization (e.g., School, Nonprofit).
* **Budget Range** (16): Select your approximate budget for the tickets.
* **Financial Assistance Needed** (17): Indicate if you need financial assistance or full price is acceptable.
* **Seating Preferences** (18): If applicable, mention any seating preferences or requirements.
* **Accessibility Needs** (19): Select any accessibility requirements (e.g., Wheelchair accessible seating).
* **Special Accommodations** (20): If applicable, mention any special accommodations needed.
* **Payment Method** (21): Select how you plan to pay for the tickets.
* **Deadline for Response** (22): Enter the date by which you need a response.
* **Additional Information** (23): Provide any additional information or special requests.
