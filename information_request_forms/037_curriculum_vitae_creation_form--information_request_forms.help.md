# Curriculum Vitae Creation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Curriculum Vitae Creation Form is used to collect information about your work history, education, personal details, and professional experience. This form helps us to better understand your qualifications and create an accurate CV for your future needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill out each page of the form completely.
- Enter the required information for each field, marked as "Required" in the field label.
- You can add optional information in the fields not marked as "Required".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Work History
* **Work History** (`Work History`, `text`, Required): Enter a brief description of your work history, including your work experience and achievements.

### Education
* **Education** (`Education`, `text`, Required): Provide details about your educational background, including degrees earned and institutions attended.

### Personal Details
* **Personal Details** (`Personal Details`, `text`, Required): Enter your personal details, such as your name, address, and contact information.

### Work Experience
* **Work Experience** (`Work Experience`, `select_one`, Required): Select the type of work experience you have, e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.

### Skills
* **Skills** (`Skills`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select the skills you have, e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.

### References
* **References** (`References`, `text`, Optional): Enter any relevant references, e.g., people who can vouch for your skills and experience.

### Job Status
* **Job Status** (`Job Status`, `select_one`, Required): Select your current job status, e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.

### Job Type
* **Job Type** (`Job Type`, `select_one`, Required): Select the type of job you are applying for, e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.

### Work Organisation
* **Work Organisation** (`Work Organisation`, `text`, Required): Enter the name of your current or former work organisation.

### Work Position
* **Work Position** (`Work Position`, `text`, Required): Enter your current or former job title.

### Job Start Date
* **Job Start Date** (`Job Start Date`, `date`, Required): Enter your start date of employment.

### Job End Date
* **Job End Date** (`Job End Date`, `date`, Optional): Enter your end date of employment.

### Resume
* **Resume** (`Resume`, `text`, Required): Upload or enter your resume, if available.

### Photo
* **Photo** (`Photo`, `text`, Optional): Upload or enter a photo of yourself, if desired.

### Work Place
* **Work Place** (`Work Place`, `text`, Optional): Enter your workplace location, if desired.

### Job Title
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, `text`, Required): Enter your job title, e.g., Software Developer, Marketing Manager, etc.

### Job Description
* **Job Description** (`Job Description`, `text`, Required): Enter a brief description of your job responsibilities.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, Optional): Enter your work phone number, if desired.

### Email
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, Optional): Enter your work email, if desired.

### Job Location
* **Job Location** (`Job Location`, `select_one`, Required): Select the location where you work, e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.

### Job Salary
* **Job Salary** (`Job Salary`, `number`, Optional): Enter your job salary, if desired.

### Job Salary Currency
* **Job Salary Currency** (`Job Salary Currency`, `select_one`, Required): Select the currency of your job salary, e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.

### Job Salary Amount
* **Job Salary Amount** (`Job Salary Amount`, `number`, Optional): Enter the amount of your job salary, if desired.

### Job Salary Frequency
* **Job Salary Frequency** (`Job Salary Frequency`, `select_one`, Required): Select how often you are paid, e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
