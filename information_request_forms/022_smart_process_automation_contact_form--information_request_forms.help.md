# Smart Process Automation Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to submit a contact request, provide feedback or inquire about automation processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your First Name (ID: 1) and Last Name (ID: 2)
* Enter your Email (ID: 3) - please note that this is required for us to contact you.
* If you would like to provide your Phone Number (ID: 4), we encourage you to do so.
* Enter the name of your Company (ID: 5) (if applicable)
* Select the type of inquiry you would like to make (ID: 6):
	+ Automation: for questions about process automation
	+ Consultation: for general consultation services
	+ Other: for any other type of inquiry
* Provide a detailed Description (ID: 7) of your request or inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (ID: 1)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name (ID: 2)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email (ID: 3)**: Enter your business or personal email address.
* **Phone Number (ID: 4)**: Enter your business or personal phone number (optional).
* **Company (ID: 5)**: Enter the name of your company (if applicable).
* **Inquiry Type (ID: 6)**: Choose one of the following options:
	+ Automation: for questions about process automation
	+ Consultation: for general consultation services
	+ Other: for any other type of inquiry
* **Description (ID: 7)**: Provide a detailed description of your request or inquiry.

## Tips
* Please fill in the required fields to ensure we can contact you effectively.
* If you have any attachments or supporting documents, please attach them to your email.
* Our support team will contact you within 24-48 hours after receiving your form.
