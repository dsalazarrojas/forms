# HR Analytics Consultation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Analytics Consultation Request Form is designed for HR professionals or departments to request expert advice on data-driven human resources management. This form helps users inquire about analytics-related services, such as data interpretation, lead generation, or schedule planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the `analytics_needs` field with a brief description of your analytics requirements.
2. Choose a preferred date for the consultation in the `preferred_date` field.
3. Select a preferred time for the consultation from the `preferred_time` field.
4. Select one or multiple schedule follow-up options that suit your needs from the `schedule_followup` field.
5. Choose the analytics lead you need help with from the `analytics_leads` field.
6. Add any additional schedule details in the `schedule_details` field.
7. Finally, provide any additional comments or notes about your consultation needs in the `notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): A field for any additional information or comments about your analytics needs.
* **Analytics Needs** (`analytics_needs`, text, optional): Describe in brief the analytics needs or questions you have.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, optional): Choose a date for your consultation that suits you best.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, optional): Select a time slot for your consultation.
* **Schedule Follow-up** (`schedule_followup`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you want to schedule follow-up meetings or calls.
* **Analytics Leads** (`analytics_leads`, select_one, optional): Identify the specific analytics lead you need help with.
* **Schedule Details** (`schedule_details`, text, optional): Add any further details about your schedule preferences.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your consultation needs.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all fields that apply to your inquiry.
* If you need help with multiple analytics needs, select all relevant options in the "Schedule Follow-up" field.
* Use the "Notes" field to provide any additional context or information about your consultation.
