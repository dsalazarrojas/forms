# Infinity Marketing Onboarding Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for both new and existing marketing clients to gather essential information for a smooth onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the first question, "Are you a new marketing client?" with either "Yes" or "No".
2. If you are a new marketing client, fill in the details of your team members in the "Team Member Details" field.
3. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Enter your company name in the "Company" field.
7. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
8. Enter your city in the "City" field.
9. Select your state from the list in the "State" field.
10. Enter your zip code in the "Zip Code" field.
11. If necessary, add any additional notes about your onboarding process or requirements in the "Additional Notes" field.
12. Select the date if applicable, as the form will likely ask for this in the future.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Are you a new marketing client?** (`new_marketing_client`, select_one, required: false): This is the first question to determine whether you are a new client or not. Select "Yes" if you are a new client, and "No" if you already have an account with us.
* **Team Member Details** (`team_member_details`, text, required: false): If you selected "Yes" in the previous question, enter the details of your team members here.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number here.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required: false): Enter your company name here.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your full address here.
* **City** (`city`, text, required: false): Enter your city here.
* **State** (`state`, select_one, required: false): Select your state from the list.
* **Zip Code** (`zip`, text, required: false): Enter your zip code here.
* **Additional Notes** (`note`, note, required: false): If you have any additional notes or comments about your onboarding process or requirements, add them here.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): This field is for future reference and will likely be used to track the onboarding date.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all required questions accurately.
* If you are unsure about any information, please ask your point of contact for assistance.
* The onboarding process will begin once you submit this form.
