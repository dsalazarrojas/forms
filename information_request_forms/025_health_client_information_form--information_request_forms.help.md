# Health Client Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Health Client Information Form is designed to gather essential health-related details from clients for medical evaluation and treatment purposes. This form helps healthcare professionals assess a client's medical history, health conditions, medications, and other relevant information to provide appropriate care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Health Client Information Form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer all the fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Ensure that the form is completed for the correct client or patient.
3. If a field is not applicable or not known, please enter "N/A" or provide a brief explanation in the text field.
4. Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all fields are completed and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Medical History
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Describe the client's past medical history, including any previous illnesses, injuries, or medical procedures.

### Health Conditions
* **Health Conditions** (`health_conditions`, `select_one`, required: false): Select from the following options:
	+ Diabetes
	+ Hypertension
	+ Heart Conditions
	+ Other (Please specify in the text field)

### Medications
* **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, required: false): List all current medications, including dosages and frequencies.

### Medical Procedure Information
* **Medical Procedure Information** (`medical_procedure_info`, `text`, required: false): If the client has undergone any medical procedures, please provide details.

### Allergies
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, required: false): List any allergies or sensitivities the client may have.

### Health Insurance
* **Health Insurance** (`health_insurance`, `text`, required: false): Provide the client's health insurance information, including policy number and provider.

### Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.

Note: The form is designed to be completed by healthcare professionals for medical evaluation and treatment purposes. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your healthcare provider.
