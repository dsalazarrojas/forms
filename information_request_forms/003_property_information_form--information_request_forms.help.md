# Property Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Property Information Form is used to collect accurate and up-to-date information about a property's address, location, and other relevant details. This information is crucial for property management, maintenance, and other related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully review each field to ensure you understand its purpose and requirements.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information about the property.
3. Check your entries carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Street Address** (`address_line_1`, Text, Required): Enter the street address of the property.
* **City** (`city`, Text, Required): Enter the city where the property is located.
* **State** (`state`, Text, Required): Enter the state where the property is located.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, Text, Required): Enter the postal code of the property.
* **Country** (`country`, Text, Required): Enter the country where the property is located.
* **Latitude** (`latitude`, Number, Required): Enter the latitude of the property's geographic location.
* **Longitude** (`longitude`, Number, Required): Enter the longitude of the property's geographic location.

## Tips
- Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure that you are providing the most up-to-date information about the property.
- If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the property owner or representative.
