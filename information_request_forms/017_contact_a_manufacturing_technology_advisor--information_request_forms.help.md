# Contact A Manufacturing Technology Advisor - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for companies seeking advice or guidance on manufacturing technology. It gathers information about the company, their technology needs, and the advisor's routing information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your company information, including Company Name, Industry Sector, and Number of Facilities.
2. Provide your contact information, including Contact Name, Contact Email, and Contact Phone Number.
3. Describe your technology needs, including Primary Technology Interest and Description of Technology Needs.
4. Estimate the project budget and decision-making timeline.
5. Specify your urgency level and preferred advisor specialization.
6. Add any additional comments or information you would like to share.
7. Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company's full name.
* **Industry Sector** (`industry_sector`, select_one, required): Select your manufacturing sector, e.g., Aerospace and Defense, Automotive, Electronics, etc.
* **Number of Facilities** (`number_of_facilities`, number, optional): How many manufacturing plants do you operate?
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Your full name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Primary business email.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Best number to reach you.
* **Primary Technology Interest** (`primary_technology_interest`, select_multiple, required): What technology are you inquiring about, e.g., Robotics and Automation, Industrial IoT (IIoT), etc.
* **Description of Technology Needs** (`description_of_technology_needs`, text, required): Describe your current technology status and goals.
* **Estimated Project Budget** (`estimated_project_budget`, select_one, optional): Select a range, e.g., Under $100,000, $100,000-$500,000, etc.
* **Decision Making Timeline** (`decision_making_timeline`, select_one, required): When do you expect to make a decision, e.g., Immediately, Within 3 Months, etc.
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`, select_one, required): How quickly do you need a response, e.g., High-Urgent, Moderate-Normal, etc.
* **Preferred Advisor Specialization** (`preferred_advisor_specialization`, text, optional): Do you need an expert in a specific area?
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Anything else you would like to share.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Today's date.
