# Teacher Input For IEP - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from teachers about students under their care to support the development of an Individualized Education Plan (IEP). 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the requested information.
2. Select the correct assigned tool from the available options.
3. Provide additional information in the space provided.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's name.
* **Academic Needs** (`academic_needs`, `text`, required: false): Describe the student's academic needs.
* **Physical Needs** (`physical_needs`, `text`, required: false): Describe the student's physical needs.
* **Social Needs** (`social_needs`, `text`, required: false): Describe the student's social needs.
* **Emotional Needs** (`emotional_needs`, `text`, required: false): Describe the student's emotional needs.
* **Academic Needs Detail** (`academic_needs_detail`, `text`, required: false): Provide more detailed information about the student's academic needs.
* **Physical Needs Detail** (`physical_needs_detail`, `text`, required: false): Provide more detailed information about the student's physical needs.
* **Social Needs Detail** (`social_needs_detail`, `text`, required: false): Provide more detailed information about the student's social needs.
* **Emotional Needs Detail** (`emotional_needs_detail`, `text`, required: false): Provide more detailed information about the student's emotional needs.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about the student.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required: true): Select a tool for the student's IEP from the available options.
* **Other Tool Description** (`other_tool_description`, `text`, required: false): If you select "other" as the assigned tool, provide a description of the tool.
* **Other Tool** (`assigned_tool_other`, `text`, required: false): If you select "other" as the assigned tool, enter the name of the tool.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's ID number.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as the teacher.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, `text`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **School Year** (`school_year`, `text`, required: false): Enter the school year for the student.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's grade level.
* **Student Section** (`student_section`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's classroom or section assignment.
* **Student Subject** (`student_subject`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's subject or class being taken.
* **Student Room** (`student_room`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's room number.
* **Student Class** (`student_class`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's class or group assignment.
* **Student Group** (`student_group`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's group or team assignment.
* **Student Teacher** (`student_teacher`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's teacher's name.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as the person submitting the form.

Note: The fields marked as "required: false" are not required to be filled out, but it's recommended to fill them out for a more comprehensive IEP.
