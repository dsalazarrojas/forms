# Elementary School Records Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for record management purposes from parents or guardians of students attending an elementary school.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your child's **Student ID**, which is a unique identifier for your child.
2. Enter your child's **Student Name** as it appears on their official school documents.
3. Enter the name of your child's **School** (e.g., school name, school address).
4. Select the date for which you are requesting records using the **Request Date** field.
5. Confirm that you have **Parent Permission**, and provide a reason for requesting records using the **Why Records Requested** field.
6. Determine which **Records Requested** are needed (yes or no).
7. Choose the **Records for** you are requesting (yes or no).
8. Select the **Records Type** you require (yes or no).
9. Enter the **Records Location** where you would like to receive the records.
10. Provide any **Records Notes** you would like to include with the request.
11. Enter the name of your child's **School Name**.
12. Choose whether the school is located at **School Location** (yes or no).
13. Enter the school's **School Phone** number.
14. Enter the school's **School Email** address.
15. Enter the school's **School URL**.
16. Enter your child's **Student Email** address (if applicable).
17. Enter your child's **Student Phone** number (if applicable).
18. Provide any **Additional Notes** about the records request.
19. Enter your name and contact information as the **Submitter** (e.g., name, email, phone number).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID**: Enter your child's unique identifier for record management purposes. This is the first step in verifying student identity.
* **Student Name**: Enter your child's official name as it appears on their school documents.
* **Student School**: Enter the name of the school your child attends.
* **Request Date**: Select the date for which you are requesting records.
* **Parent Permission**: Confirm that you have given permission for records to be released.
* **Why Records Requested**: Explain why you are requesting records (e.g., for school transfer, graduation, or other reasons).
* **Records Requested**: Select which types of records you need (e.g., transcripts, diplomas, etc.).
* **Records for**: Choose whether the records are for you or someone else (e.g., another school, employer).
* **Records Type**: Select the type of records required (e.g., transcripts, diplomas, etc.).
* **Records Location**: Enter where you would like to receive the records.
* **Records Notes**: Provide any additional information about the records you need.
* **School Name**: Enter the official name of the school your child attends.
* **School Location**: Confirm the school's location (e.g., on or off campus).
* **School Phone**: Enter the school's phone number.
* **School Email**: Enter the school's email address.
* **School URL**: Enter the school's URL or website.
* **Student Email**: Enter your child's email address (if applicable).
* **Student Phone**: Enter your child's phone number (if applicable).
* **Additional Notes**: Provide any additional comments or information about the records request.
* **Submitter**: Enter your name as the person submitting the request.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter URL**: Enter your URL or website (if applicable).
* **Submitter Department**: Enter your department or organization (if applicable).
