# Extend Warranty Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Extend Warranty Form is used to gather information from customers to extend their warranty on a purchased product. This form is intended for customers who would like to extend their warranty beyond the standard warranty period. Please note that this form will only be available for customers who are eligible to extend their warranty based on the product type and other criteria.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. Review your answers carefully to ensure accuracy.
3. Submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, Optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or documentation.
* **Product ID** (`product_id`, number, Optional): Enter the ID number of the product you would like to extend the warranty on.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, select_one, Optional):
	+ If your product is one of the listed options, select the corresponding type.
	+ If your product is not listed, select "Other" and enter the type in the **Product Type (Other)** field.
* **Product Type (Other)** (`product_type_other`, text, Optional): Enter a description of your product type if it's not listed.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Company** (`customer_company`, text, Optional): If you are representing a company, enter the company name.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are eligible to extend your warranty before submitting this form.
* If you are unsure about your product type, please contact our customer service department for assistance.
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your request is processed correctly.
