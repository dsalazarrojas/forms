# Plagiarism Time, Writing Or Research? - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to understand why students plagiarize and identify the periods in which plagiarism is more common. Your input will help the school's academic community better address plagiarism and improve the writing and research environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read through the form carefully and ensure you understand each question.
2.  Select all the reasons why you plagiarize (if any).
3.  Identify the periods in which you plagiarize (if any).
4.  If you have any other thoughts or reasons not listed, please use the "Any Other Thoughts" field.
5.  Provide an email address or phone number if you'd like to be contacted by a faculty member or administrator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Plagiarism Time
This is a text field where you can briefly describe the context in which you've experienced plagiarism.

*   **Plagiarism Time** (text): Describe the time and place where you plagiarized, if applicable.

### Why Do Students Plagiarize?
Select all the reasons why you plagiarize:

*   **To get good grades** (checkbox): If you're looking for easier ways to get good grades.
*   **Because it's easier** (checkbox): If plagiarism is a quicker route for you.
*   **Don't want to do the work** (checkbox): If you're overwhelmed or simply don't want to put in the effort.
*   **Because I don't understand it** (checkbox): If you're struggling to grasp the material.
*   **Other (please specify)** (checkbox and text input): If none of the above apply, or if you have another reason.

### At What Periods Do Plagiarism Occur?
Select all the periods in which you plagiarize:

*   **Midterm 1-2** (checkbox): If you've plagiarized during this period.
*   **Midterm 3** (checkbox): If you've plagiarized during this period.
*   **Final** (checkbox): If you've plagiarized during this period.
*   **Other (please specify)** (checkbox and text input): If none of the above apply, or if you have another period in mind.

### Any Other Thoughts
If you have any other thoughts or reasons not listed above, use this field to express them.

*   **Any Other Thoughts** (text): Briefly describe any other reason not mentioned before.

### How Do You Want To Be Contacted?
If you'd like to be contacted by a faculty member or administrator about your feedback, provide your email address or phone number.

*   **How Do You Want To Be Contacted** (email and/or phone number): Your contact information.
