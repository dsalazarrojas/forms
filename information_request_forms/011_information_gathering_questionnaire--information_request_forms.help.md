# Information Gathering Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this questionnaire is to help us understand your experiences and needs. We value your feedback and will use it to improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully.
2. Answer each question honestly.
3. Make sure to select all relevant options for your contact details.
4. Choose the option that best represents your satisfaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Section
* **First Section**: This section is a general information section where you can provide any comments or feedback about our service.

### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (Select one or more of the following):
	+ **Email**: Your email address is collected to contact you for follow-up or to send additional information.
	+ **Phone**: Your phone number is collected for follow-up calls or to provide updates.
	+ **Postal**: Your postal address is collected for physical mail or packages.

### Satisfaction Feedback
* **Satisfaction Feedback** (Select one of the following):
	+ **Highly Satisfied**: You are very satisfied with our service.
	+ **Moderately Satisfied**: You are somewhat satisfied with our service.
	+ **Not Satisfied**: You are not satisfied with our service.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly, as your feedback is valuable to us.
* You can skip any section if you're not comfortable providing information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
