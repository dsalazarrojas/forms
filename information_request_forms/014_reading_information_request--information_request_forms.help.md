# Reading Information Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Reading Information Request" form is designed to gather information about your reading habits and preferences. This information will help us better understand your interests and provide you with personalized recommendations or services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant pages to complete, depending on the information you want to provide.
2. Fill in the required fields on each page.
3. Make sure to review your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reading Information Request Page 1** (Reading Information Request Page 1): This page is the first step in our reading information request process. It's where you can start providing information about your reading habits.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information Page 2): On this page, please select an option for your contact information. We'll use this to get in touch with you.
* **What do you read for pleasure?** (Reading Information Request Background Page 3): Here, you can select one or multiple options for what you enjoy reading about. This will help us understand your interests better.
* **Other** (Reading Information Request Other Page 4): If there's something else you'd like to share about your reading habits, this is the place to do so.
* **What is your educational background?** (Reading Information Request Education Page 5): Please select an option for your educational background to help us understand your level of knowledge.
* **Occupation** (Reading Information Request Occupation Page 6): Select an option for your occupation to help us understand your profession.
* **Phone Number** (Reading Information Request Phone Page 7): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Email** (Reading Information Request Email Page 8): Please enter a valid email address for us to get in touch with you.
* **Address** (Reading Information Request Address Page 9): Enter your address to help us understand your location better.
* **City** (Reading Information Request City Page 10): Select your city to provide more specific location information.
* **State** (Reading Information Request State Page 11): Choose your state to help us narrow down our recommendations.
* **Zip** (Reading Information Request Zip Page 12): Enter your zip code to provide more detailed location information.
* **Country** (Reading Information Request Country Page 13): Select your country to help us understand your location better.
* **Comments** (Reading Information Request Comments Page 14): If you have any additional comments or thoughts, feel free to share them with us.
* **Education Level** (Reading Information Request Education Level Page 15): Select an option for your level of education to help us understand your knowledge level.

Note: Please ensure that you've completed all required fields accurately and thoroughly to ensure that we can provide you with the best possible recommendations or services.
