# Building Health Specialist Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from clients about their building health needs and concerns. It is used to determine the urgency of the issue, gather details about the property and health issue, and schedule an assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your full name, job title, organization, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide the property name and address.
3. Classify your property type (residential, commercial, retail, industrial, or mixed-use).
4. Specify the type of building health issue you are concerned about.
5. Describe your primary concern.
6. If you have experienced health symptoms, list them.
7. Identify the areas affected by the issue.
8. Provide information on the duration of the issue and any previous assessments.
9. Indicate your urgency level and preferred assessment date.
10. Confirm your access availability and whether the building is occupied.
11. Mention any special access requirements.
12. Estimate your budget range for the assessment.
13. Specify how you heard about us.
14. Add any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Your full name and contact information.
* **Job Title** (`title`): Your position or role in the organization.
* **Organization** (`organization`): The name of your company or organization.
* **Email Address** (`email`): Your email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`): Your phone number for direct contact.
* **Contact Date** (`contact_date`): The date of your inquiry.
* **Property Name** (`property_name`): The name of the property.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`): The full address of the property.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`): Classification of the property (residential, commercial, retail, industrial, or mixed-use).
* **Building Size** (`building_size`): The square footage of the building (optional).
* **Year Built** (`year_built`): The year the building was constructed (optional).
* **Inquiry Type** (`inquiry_type`): The type of service needed (building health assessment, indoor air quality testing, mold inspection, or other).
* **Primary Concern** (`primary_concern`): The main issue affecting the building.
* **Concern Description** (`concern_description`): A detailed description of the issue.
* **Symptoms Reported** (`symptoms_reported`): Any reported health symptoms experienced by occupants.
* **Affected Areas** (`affected_areas`): The specific areas affected by the issue.
* **Duration of Issue** (`duration_of_issue`): The length of time the issue has existed.
* **Previous Assessments** (`previous_assessments`): Whether there have been previous assessments or not.
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency`): The level of urgency of the issue (emergency, high, medium, or low).
* **Preferred Assessment Date** (`preferred_assessment_date`): The desired date for the assessment (optional).
* **Access Availability** (`access_availability`): The availability of access to the property.
* **Occupants Present** (`occupants_present`): Whether the building is occupied or not.
* **Special Access Requirements** (`special_access_requirements`): Any special access requirements.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`): The estimated budget for the assessment (optional).
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_did_you_hear`): The source of your referral.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Any other relevant information.

## Tips
* Please fill in all required fields to ensure we can efficiently assist you.
* If there are multiple occupants in the building, please list each one.
* For the `symptoms_reported` field, please specify the symptoms experienced by each occupant.
* If there are any special access requirements, please describe them in detail.
* For the `budget_range` field, please choose an estimated budget range from the given options.
