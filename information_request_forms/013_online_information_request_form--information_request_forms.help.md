# Online Information Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect details from users who are requesting an online service or product. It helps us to understand their needs and preferences, which enables us to process their request efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Organization Name if applicable.
2. Enter your Contact Email address.
3. Select the services you are interested in from the Service Offered options.
4. Provide a brief description of the service you are requesting in the Service Details field.
5. Enter your Contact Phone number.
6. Choose a date and time for your request in the Request Date and Request Time fields.
7. Once you have filled in all the required and optional fields, click the submit button to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (Required: false)
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, Optional): Enter your first name for our reference. This field is optional, but it helps us to understand your identity.

### Organization Name (Required: false)
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, Optional): Enter your organization name if you are representing a company or institution. This field is optional and is used to differentiate between individual and institutional requests.

### Contact Email (Required: false)
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, Optional): Enter your email address where we can contact you to discuss your request. This field is optional but recommended for efficient communication.

### Service Offered (Required: false)
* **Service Offered** (`service_offered`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select the services you are interested in from the available options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3

### Service Details (Required: false)
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, `text`, Optional): Provide a brief description of the service you are requesting. This field helps us to understand your needs and preferences.

### Contact Phone (Required: false)
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, Optional): Enter your contact phone number so we can contact you for further clarification or details.

### Request Date (Required: false)
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, `date`, Optional): Choose a date for your request. You can select a specific date or choose 'Any' to indicate you need a service within a particular period.

### Request Time (Required: false)
* **Request Time** (`request_time`, `time`, Optional): Choose a time for your request.
