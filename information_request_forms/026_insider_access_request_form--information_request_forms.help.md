# insider_access_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The insider_access_request_form is a request form used to collect information from insiders who wish to access certain resources or information within an organization. The form helps the requester to provide necessary contact details and reasons for requesting access, and allows them to specify their preferred timing for the access request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contact information in the `contact_details` field.
2. Specify the reason for requesting access in the `reason_for_access` field.
3. Choose your preferred timing for the access request from the options in the `preferred_timing` field.
4. If you have any additional information to provide, you can enter it in the `additional_info` field.
5. Fill in any other relevant contact details in the `contact_details_1` and `contact_details_2` fields (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details**: (`contact_details`, `text`, required: `false`):
  This field is used to input your contact details for the request.
* **Reason for Access** (`reason_for_access`, `text`, required: `false`):
  This field is used to specify the reason for requesting access. Please provide a brief description of your request.
* **Preferred Timing** (`preferred_timing`, `select_one`, required: `false`):
  This field allows you to choose your preferred timing for the access request. Please select one of the available options:
  + 1 hour
  + 8 hours
  + 12 hours
  + 24 hours
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `text`, required: `false`):
  This field is used to provide any additional information that is relevant to your request.
* **Contact Details 1** (`contact_details_1`, `text`, required: `false`):
  This field is used to input any other relevant contact details for the request.
* **Contact Details 2** (`contact_details_2`, `text`, required: `false`):
  This field is used to input any other relevant contact details for the request.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill in all the required fields.
* You can adjust the timing of your request based on your needs.
* If you have any additional questions, please reach out to the contact person.
* Please note that some fields may be optional, but are still useful to provide more context and information to support your request.
