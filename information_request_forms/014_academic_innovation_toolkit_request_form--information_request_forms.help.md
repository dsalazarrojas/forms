# Academic Innovation Toolkit Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Innovation Toolkit Request Form is a tool used to gather information from users who are interested in requesting academic innovation toolkits. The form helps the toolkit development team understand the user's needs and preferences, allowing them to create effective toolkits that meet those needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in your details:
   * Your name and email address in the "Requester Name" and "Email Address" fields.
   * Your department and position in the "Department" and "Position/Title" fields.
2. Specify the type of toolkit you are requesting:
   * Select the category of toolkit you are interested in from the "Type of Innovation Toolkit Requested" dropdown menu.
   * If you have a specific toolkit in mind, enter its name in the "Specific Toolkit Name" field.
3. Explain how you plan to use the toolkit:
   * Describe how you intend to use the toolkit in the "Intended Use" field.
4. Identify your target audience:
   * Specify who will benefit from the toolkit in the "Target Audience" field.
5. Share your expected outcomes:
   * Describe the results you anticipate from using the toolkit in the "Expected Outcomes" field.
6. Set a timeline:
   * Choose when you need the toolkit from the "Timeline Requirement" dropdown menu.
7. Consider budget constraints:
   * If there are any budget constraints, enter them in the "Budget Considerations" field.
8. Share your experience:
   * Describe your previous experience with similar toolkits in the "Previous Experience" field.
9. Specify your training needs:
   * Choose how much training you require to effectively use the toolkit in the "Training Needed" field.
10. Share your collaboration interest:
   * Select how interested you are in collaborating with others using similar toolkits in the "Collaboration Interest" field.
11. Plan your evaluation:
   * Describe how you plan to evaluate the effectiveness of the toolkit in the "Evaluation Plan" field.
12. Additional resources:
   * If you need any additional resources to support the successful implementation of the toolkit, enter them in the "Additional Resources Needed" field.
