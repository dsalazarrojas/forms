# Animal Behavior Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps behaviorists understand your pet's behavior, habits, and concerns to provide effective assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring you answer all required fields.
2. Please provide your pet's information, including their species, age, sex, and medical history.
3. Describe any behavior concerns you have about your pet, including what triggers these behaviors and their frequency.
4. Specify your goals for addressing these behaviors and any additional context that may be helpful.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Owner Full Name** (`owner_name`, text, required): This field is where you'll enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Please enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up communication.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Animal Behavior Information Form** (`form_intro`, note, not required): This field is not required, but you can write about your pet's history and behavior here.
* **Pet's Name** (`pet_name`, text, required): Enter your pet's name.
* **Species** (`pet_species`, select_one, required): Choose your pet's species from the provided options.
* **Breed** (`pet_breed`, text, not required): Enter your pet's breed or mix.
* **Age** (`pet_age`, text, required): Enter your pet's age in years and months.
* **Sex** (`pet_sex`, select_one, required): Choose your pet's sex from the provided options.
* **Is your pet spayed or neutered** (`neutered_status`, select_one, required): Choose whether your pet is spayed or neutered.
* **How long has your pet lived with you** (`time_in_home`, select_one, required): Choose how long your pet has lived with you.
* **What are your primary behavior concerns** (`behavior_concerns`, select_multiple, required): Select all concerns that apply from the provided options.
* **Please describe the behavior concerns in detail** (`behavior_history`, text, required): Describe your pet's behavior concerns in detail.
* **What seems to trigger the behavior** (`behavior_triggers`, text, not required): Enter any situations or antecedents that may trigger your pet's behavior.
* **How often does the behavior occur** (`behavior_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how often the behavior occurs.
* **How intense is the behavior** (`behavior_intensity`, select_one, required): Choose the severity level of the behavior.
* **Has your pet received any training** (`previous_training`, select_one, required): Choose your pet's training history.
* **What training methods have you used** (`training_methods`, select_multiple, not required): Select all training methods you've tried.
* **Does your pet have any medical conditions** (`medical_history`, text, not required): Enter any health issues that may affect your pet's behavior.
* **Is your pet currently on any medications** (`medications`, text, not required): Enter any medications your pet is taking.
* **Describe your pet's typical daily routine** (`daily_routine`, text, not required): Describe your pet's daily routine.
* **Who lives in your household** (`household_members`, text, not required): Enter the ages of all household members.
* **Are there other pets in the home** (`other_pets`, text, not required): Describe the other pets in your home.
* **What are your goals for addressing the behavior** (`goals`, text, required): Specify your goals for addressing the behavior.
* **Any other information that might be helpful** (`additional_info`, text, not required): Enter any additional context that may be helpful.
