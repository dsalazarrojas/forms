# Digital Workforce Enablement Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Digital Workforce Enablement Contact Form" is used to gather information from users about their needs and requests for digital workforce enablement. This form is intended to collect contact details and preferences from users in order to provide them with relevant information and support. Please note that the form is not a formal support ticket, but rather a way for us to learn about your requirements and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Name.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Provide your Phone Number (optional).
4. Select your Department (optional).
5. Enter a brief Subject for your request.
6. Describe your Question or concern in the space provided.
7. If necessary, attach any relevant documents or files to support your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Name
* **User Name** (`user_name`, `text`, required/optional: Optional): Enter your name to help us get in touch with you.
### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional: Optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your request.
### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/optional: Optional): Provide your phone number if you would like us to contact you by phone.
### Department
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required/optional: Optional): Select your department to help us understand your specific needs.
### Subject
* **Subject** (`subject`, `text`, required/optional: Optional): Provide a brief title for your request.
### Question
* **Question** (`question`, `text`, required/optional: Optional): Describe your question or concern in this space.
### Attachments
* **Attachments** (`attachment`, `note`, required/optional: Optional): If you have any supporting documents or files to share, attach them here.

## Tips
* Please be as descriptive as possible when filling in the form to help us understand your needs.
* If you have any trouble filling in this form, feel free to contact us directly.
* This form will be reviewed by our team to learn about your requirements and preferences.
* Please do not submit duplicate requests, as this form is intended to gather new information only.
