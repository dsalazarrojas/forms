# credit_card_transaction_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The credit card transaction form is designed to collect information about a credit card transaction from a customer. This form is used for various purposes such as credit card applications, merchant inquiries, or other relevant transactions. Please ensure that you only fill out this form for legitimate transactions and not for unauthorized or duplicate purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Customer Name** in the field labeled "Customer Name". This is the name of the person associated with the credit card.
2. Enter the **Customer Email** in the field labeled "Customer Email". This is the email address of the person associated with the credit card.
3. Enter the **Customer Phone** in the field labeled "Customer Phone". This is the phone number of the person associated with the credit card.
4. Enter the **Billing Address** in the field labeled "Billing Address". This is the address where the credit card is billed.
5. Enter the **Card Number** in the field labeled "Card Number". This is the number of the credit card.
6. Enter the **Expiration Date** in the field labeled "Expiration Date". This is the expiration date of the credit card in mm/yy format.
7. Enter the **CVV** in the field labeled "CVV". This is the Card Verification Value, also known as the security code on the back of the card.
8. Select the **Card Type** from the dropdown menu. This is the type of credit card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, or Diners Club).
9. Enter the **Cardholder Name** in the field labeled "Cardholder Name". This is the name of the person associated with the credit card.
10. Enter the **Cardholder Address** in the field labeled "Cardholder Address". This is the address of the person associated with the credit card.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: A text field where you can enter the name of the person associated with the credit card.
* **Customer Email**: A text field where you can enter the email address of the person associated with the credit card.
* **Customer Phone**: A text field where you can enter the phone number of the person associated with the credit card.
* **Billing Address**: A text field where you can enter the address where the credit card is billed.
* **Card Number**: A text field where you can enter the number of the credit card.
* **Expiration Date**: A date field where you can enter the expiration date of the credit card in mm/yy format.
* **CVV**: A text field where you can enter the Card Verification Value, also known as the security code on the back of the card.
* **Card Type**: A dropdown menu where you can select the type of credit card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, or Diners Club).
* **Cardholder Name**: A text field where you can enter the name of the person associated with the credit card.
* **Cardholder Address**: A text field where you can enter the address of the person associated with the credit card.
