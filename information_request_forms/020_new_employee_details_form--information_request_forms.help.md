# New Employee Details Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The New Employee Details Form is used to capture information about a new employee's personal and professional details. This form is designed to be completed by the HR department or management to record and track employee data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: `name`, `email`, `category`, `start_date`, and `end_date` are not required.
2. Enter the employee's name in the `name` field.
3. Enter the employee's email address in the `email` field.
4. Select the employee's category from the `category` dropdown menu.
5. Enter the employee's start date of employment in the `start_date` field.
6. Enter the employee's end date of employment (if applicable) in the `end_date` field.
7. Enter the employee's address in the `address` field.
8. Enter the employee's department in the `department` field.
9. Enter the employee's manager's name in the `manager` field.
10. Add any additional notes or comments in the `notes` field.
11. Optionally, select the assigned tool for the employee in the `assigned_tool` dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **title** (text, required/optional): This is the title of the form, which is already pre-filled as "New Employee Details Form".
* **name** (text, required): Enter the name of the new employee.
* **email** (email, required): Enter the employee's email address.
* **phone** (text, optional): Enter the employee's phone number (if available).
* **category** (select_one, optional): Select the category of the new employee (e.g., "Full-time", "Part-time", etc.).
* **job_title** (text, optional): Enter the employee's job title.
* **start_date** (date, optional): Enter the employee's start date of employment.
* **end_date** (date, optional): Enter the employee's end date of employment (if applicable).
* **address** (text, optional): Enter the employee's address.
* **department** (text, optional): Enter the employee's department.
* **manager** (text, optional): Enter the employee's manager's name.
* **notes** (note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the employee.
* **assigned_tool** (select_one, optional): Select the assigned tool for the employee (e.g., "chatjimmy", "chatjimmy2", etc.).
