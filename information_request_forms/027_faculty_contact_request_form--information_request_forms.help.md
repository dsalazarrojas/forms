# Faculty Contact Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a request to be contacted by a faculty member. It helps to collect information about the faculty member's name, department, email, phone number, and any message you'd like to share. This information will be reviewed and followed up by the relevant contact person in the department.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a request, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First name.
2. Fill in your Last name.
3. Select your department from the dropdown list.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Type in your message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name here.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name here.
* **Department**: Select your department from the list. Please choose the option that best matches your affiliation.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Message**: Type in any additional information or questions you'd like to share with the faculty member.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to avoid errors.
* If you're unsure about your department, you can contact the relevant department directly for clarification.
* If you're not sure about any other field, just do your best to provide the most accurate information you can.
* Please use a valid email address and phone number that you actually check regularly.
