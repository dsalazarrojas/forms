# Inclusion Connection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Inclusion Connection Form is used to gather information from users about their inclusion connection requests. It helps the assigned team to understand the user's needs and provide necessary support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you are submitting a relevant request.
2. Fill in the required information, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Choose the correct options from the drop-down menus where applicable.
4. Double-check the accuracy of your input before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
Each field is designed to gather specific information to facilitate the inclusion process. Here's what each field is for:

* **Request Details** (`request_details`): A free-text field where you can describe the nature of your request.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`): Select the relevant program associated with your request.
* **Program Type** (`program_type`): Choose the type of program related to your request.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date relevant to your request (e.g., start date, deadline).
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time relevant to your request (e.g., start time, appointment time).
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): Provide any relevant contact information for follow-up.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number for follow-up calls.
* **Follow up Info** (`follow_up_info`): Additional information that may be needed for further clarification.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`): Any extra details that may be relevant to your request.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Select the tool assigned to support your request (e.g., Chat Jim).
* **Notes** (`notes`): A note-taking field for additional comments or reminders.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): Enter your name for tracking and accountability.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`): The date and time you submitted the request.
* **Submission ID** (`submission_id`): A unique ID for the submitted request.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`): A unique ID for the form itself.
* **Program Status** (`program_status`): Select the status of the program associated with your request (e.g., active, inactive).
