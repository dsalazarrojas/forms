# sport_trading_warranty_claim_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The sport trading warranty claim form is used to claim a warranty on a sporting goods item that you purchased. Please use this form to provide necessary details about your product and issue you experienced, so our team can process your warranty claim efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow the instructions below.

1. Select the "Customer Details" page and fill in your information.
2. Proceed to the "Product Details" page and provide details about the product you are claiming warranty on.
3. On the "Warranty Claim" page, describe the issue you are experiencing with your product.
4. On the "Contact Information" page, provide your contact number and email address.
5. On the "Product Information" page, enter the product serial number, purchase date, repair date, and issue date (if applicable).
6. Finally, review your information and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your name and contact information to help us get in touch with you.
* **Product Details** (`product_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the model number and other relevant details about the product.
* **Warranty Claim** (`warranty_claim`, text, required/optional): Describe the issue you are experiencing with your product.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, number, required/optional): Enter your contact number so we can contact you to discuss your claim.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you to discuss your claim.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your address to help us verify your identity.
* **Product Serial Number** (`product_serial_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the serial number of the product you are claiming warranty on.
* **Product Purchase Date** (`product_purchase_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you purchased the product.
* **Product Repair Date** (`product_repair_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the product was repaired.
* **Product Issue Date** (`product_issue_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the issue with the product started.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, required/optional): Describe the issue you are experiencing with your product.
* **Warranty Number** (`warranty_number`, number, required/optional): Enter the warranty number if you have it.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that your warranty claim is processed efficiently. If you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us.
