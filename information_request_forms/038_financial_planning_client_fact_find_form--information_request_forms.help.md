# Financial Planning Client Fact Find Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic and employment information from clients to facilitate financial planning and provide personalized recommendations. Please take a few minutes to fill it out accurately and completely.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the First name field.
2. Enter your last name in the Last name field.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
5. Enter your birth date in the Birth date field using the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
6. Choose your employment status from the Employment status dropdown list.
7. Choose your employment type from the Employment type dropdown list.
8. Select all relevant household occupations from the Household occupation select multiple field.
9. Enter the number of dependents you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Birth date** (`birth_date`, date, optional): Enter your birth date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Employment status** (`employment_status`, select_one, optional): Choose how you are currently employed:
	+ Married
	+ Single
	+ Separated
	+ Divorced
	+ Widowed
* **Employment type** (`employment_type`, select_one, optional): Choose how you currently work:
	+ Full time
	+ Part time
	+ Contract
	+ Retiree
	+ Student
	+ Unemployed
* **Household occupation** (`household_occupation`, select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant occupations from the list:
	+ Business Owner
	+ Employee
	+ Student
	+ Homemaker
	+ Military
	+ Unemployed
* **Dependents** (`dependents`, number, optional): Enter the number of dependents you have.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure the best possible service.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact the financial advisor or administrator for clarification.
* This form is subject to change as needed to improve its effectiveness.
* Your input will be reviewed and updated as necessary to ensure it remains relevant and effective.
