# Color Street Sample And Information Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for customers who are seeking color samples from us for their own use or demonstration purposes. It will help us understand their product needs and ensure that we provide the correct samples efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the "Requester name" in the first field.
2. Provide your contact email address in the "Contact email" field, where we will send the samples.
3. List the products or SKUs you are requesting samples for in the "Product samples requested" field.
4. Enter your full shipping address in the "Shipping address" field, where the samples will be delivered.
5. If applicable, provide us with any additional stylist information in the "Stylist information" field.
6. Choose how the samples will be used (Personal, Demonstration, or Event) in the "Sample use case" field.
7. If you'd like, select a preferred delivery date for the samples in the "Preferred delivery date" field.
8. Decide if you'd like to subscribe to our newsletter for updates and promotions in the "Subscribe to newsletter" field.
9. If you have any additional requests, please include them in the "Additional requests" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Requester name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the person requesting samples.
- **Contact email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address where we will send samples and updates.
- **Product samples requested** (`text`, `text`, required): List the specific products or SKUs you are requesting samples for.
- **Shipping address** (`text`, `text`, required): Enter your full shipping address where the samples will be delivered.
- **Stylist information** (`text`, `text`, optional): If you're a stylist or host, provide us with your details.
- **Sample use case** (`select_one`, `Personal`, `Demonstration`, `Event`, optional): Choose how the samples will be used.
- **Preferred delivery date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Select a preferred date for sample delivery.
- **Subscribe to newsletter** (`select_one`, `True`, `False`, optional): Decide if you'd like to subscribe to our newsletter for updates and promotions.
- **Additional requests** (`text`, `text`, optional): Include any additional requests or notes you have for your sample request.

## Tips
* Only submit one form per customer to prevent duplication of effort.
* Double-check your contact email address for accurate delivery of the samples.
* Be specific with your product samples requested to ensure we provide the correct items.
* If you have any additional requests, please include them in the "Additional requests" field.
