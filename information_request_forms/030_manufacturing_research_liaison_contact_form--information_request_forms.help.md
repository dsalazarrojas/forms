# Manufacturing Research Liaison Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manufacturing Research Liaison Contact Form is designed to gather essential information from potential collaborators, researchers, or organizations seeking to work with the manufacturing research team. This form helps the team to efficiently identify and contact potential collaborators and to understand their research interests, expertise, and any relevant files or information they might have.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill in your First Name, Last Name, Email, and other relevant details.
- If you are representing a company, please provide your Company and Job Title.
- If you have any other contact information, please provide it in the Other Contact Info field.
- Select 'Yes' if you are interested in collaborating on a specific research area.
- If you have a file to share with the team, please attach it in the File field.
- Finally, please fill in your Submission Date and Submitter's Name for the record.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: 
  * Type: text
  * Purpose: Enter your first name to help the research team understand who you are.

* **Last Name**: 
  * Type: text
  * Purpose: Enter your last name to complete your identity.

* **Email**: 
  * Type: email
  * Purpose: This will be used as contact information and to reach you if needed.

* **Company**: 
  * Type: text
  * Purpose: If you are representing a company, please provide the name of the company.

* **Job Title**: 
  * Type: text
  * Purpose: This will help us understand your role within your company.

* **Phone**: 
  * Type: text
  * Purpose: Enter your phone number for additional contact information.

* **Fax**: 
  * Type: text
  * Purpose: Enter your fax number for additional contact information.

* **Other Contact Info**: 
  * Type: text
  * Purpose: Provide any other contact information you might have (e.g., LinkedIn, website, etc.).

* **Research Area**: 
  * Type: select one
  * Purpose: Select 'Yes' if you are interested in collaborating on a specific research area.

* **File**: 
  * Type: text
  * Purpose: Attach any relevant file or document you might have to share with the research team.

* **Submission Date**: 
  * Type: date
  * Purpose: Enter the date of your submission for the record.

* **Submitter**: 
  * Type: text
  * Purpose: Fill in your name as the submitter of this form.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have permission to share any attached file.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the research team at [research email].
- The research team will review and confirm each submission before any further action is taken.
- Please fill out the form accurately and completely, as it will help us understand your background and interests correctly.
