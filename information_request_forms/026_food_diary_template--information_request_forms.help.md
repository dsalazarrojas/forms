# Food Diary Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Diary Template is a tool to help users track their food and drink intake over a period of time. It's designed to be simple and user-friendly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer "Yes" or "No" for each of the following questions:
	* Breakfast
	* Lunch
	* Dinner
	* Snacks
2. Provide any additional notes about your diet in the "Notes" field.
3. Record the time of each meal in the "Meal Time" field.
4. Enter the date of the meal in the "Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Breakfast** (`breakfast`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Answer "Yes" if you had breakfast, "No" otherwise.
* **Lunch** (`lunch`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Answer "Yes" if you had lunch, "No" otherwise.
* **Dinner** (`dinner`, `text`, required: false): Describe your dinner in detail, including the type and quantity of food consumed.
* **Snacks** (`snacks`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Answer "Yes" for each snack you had, "No" otherwise.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Type any additional comments or observations about your diet.
* **Meal Time** (`meal_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you finished eating each meal.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you had each meal.
