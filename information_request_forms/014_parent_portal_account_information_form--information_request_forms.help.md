# Parent Portal Account Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your parent portal account for the purpose of updating or verifying your account details. Please fill out this form carefully as the information you provide will be used to ensure seamless interaction with our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Student Name in the text field labeled "Student Name".
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field, ensuring it follows the standard email format (e.g., [name@example.com](mailto:name@example.com)).
3. Enter your Parent Name in the text field labeled "Parent Name".
4. Provide your Parent Email address in the "Parent Email" field, following the same email format as before.
5. Fill in the School ID, which is usually a unique identifier assigned to your school or institution.
6. Enter your Parent's Phone number in the "Parent Phone" field.
7. If you have any additional comments or messages, type them in the "Message" field.
8. Select one option from the dropdown menu labeled "Select One" for any applicable reasons or selections required.
9. In the "Select Multiple" field, choose all relevant options by clicking on each one individually.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name (Student Name, Text, Required)**: Enter your student's name.
* **Email (Email, Email, Required)**: Enter your email address in the standard format (e.g., [name@example.com](mailto:name@example.com)).
* **Parent Name (Parent Name, Text, Required)**: Enter your name.
* **Parent Email (Parent Email, Email, Required)**: Enter your parent's email address in the standard format (e.g., [name@example.com](mailto:name@example.com)).
* **School ID (School ID, Number, Required)**: Enter your school or institution's ID number.
* **Parent Phone (Parent Phone, Text, Required)**: Enter your parent's phone number.
* **Message (Message, Text, Optional)**: If you have a message for us, type it in this field.
* **Select One (Select One, Select One, Optional)**: Choose one option that applies to your situation.
* **Select Multiple (Select Multiple, Select Multiple, Optional)**: Choose all relevant options from the list below.

Note: Fields marked as "Optional" are only required for specific reasons or selections that you are choosing in the "Select One" field. If you're unsure, you can leave them blank or contact our team for guidance.
