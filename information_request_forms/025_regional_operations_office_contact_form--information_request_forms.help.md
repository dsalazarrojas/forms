# Regional Operations Office Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Regional Operations Office Contact Form is designed to collect information from users for Regional Operations Office purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Input your name in the "User Name" field.
2. Input your location or region in the "Location" field.
3. Write a message or provide details in the "Message" field.
4. Select your region in the "Region" field.
5. Select your office location in the "Office Location" field.
6. Select your preferred contact method in the "Contact Method" field.
7. Select an alternative contact method in the "Contact Method 2" field (optional).
8. Input your email address in the "Email" field.
9. Input your phone number in the "Phone" field.
10. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
11. Input your name or initials in the "Submitted By" field.
11. The form will automatically record the date and time of submission in the "Date Submitted" field.
