# information_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect basic information about a company and its contact person. This information will be used for sales and business purposes to facilitate communication and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Company Information section with your company's details.
2. Select the contact person from the dropdown list.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
5. If you have a specific Date and Time for the meeting or event, fill them out.
6. Add any additional notes in the Notes field.
7. Finally, provide any additional sales information in the Sales Information field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Company Information (Company Info, Text, Optional)
• **Company Information**: Enter your company's name and any other relevant details.

### Contact Person (Contact Person, Select Multiple, Optional)
• **Contact Person**: Select the name of the person responsible for the contact from the dropdown list.

### Email (Email, Email, Optional)
• **Email**: Enter your work email address that will be used for communication.

### Phone (Phone, Text, Optional)
• **Phone**: Enter your work phone number that will be used for communication.

### Date (Date, Date, Optional)
• **Date**: If you have a specific date for the meeting or event, enter it here.

### Time (Time, Time, Optional)
• **Time**: If you have a specific time for the meeting or event, enter it here.

### Notes (Notes, Note, Optional)
• **Notes**: Enter any additional information or comments that you would like to include.

### Sales Information (Sales Information, Text, Optional)
• **Sales Information**: Enter any additional sales information that you would like to provide.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
* If any of the fields are marked as optional, you do not have to fill them out if you do not have the information.
* This form is for business purposes only, and the information collected will not be shared with any other parties.
