# Free Art Book Download Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Free Art Book Download Request form is a tool for users to submit their interest in downloading free art books. By filling out this form, users will be able to download the book of their choice while also providing the necessary contact information and consent for the use of the book.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your **First Name** in the text field provided.
* Enter your **Last Name** in the text field provided.
* Enter your **Email** address in the format of `username@example.com`.
* Enter your **Phone** number if you prefer to be contacted via phone.
* Enter the **Art Book Title** you are interested in downloading.
* Select either **I agree to the terms and conditions** or **I do not agree** from the dropdown list.
* Optionally, you can add any additional **Comments** you'd like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (<code>first_name</code>, text, **required: false**): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (<code>last_name</code>, text, **required: false**): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email** (<code>email</code>, email, **required: false**): Enter your email address in the format of `username@example.com`.
* **Phone** (<code>phone</code>, text, **required: false**): Enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted via phone.
* **Art Book Title** (<code>art_book_title</code>, text, **required: false**): Enter the title of the art book you are interested in downloading.
* **Consent** (<code>consent</code>, select one, **required: true**): Select whether you agree or do not agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Comments** (<code>comments</code>, note, **required: false**): Optionally, enter any additional comments or information you'd like to provide.
