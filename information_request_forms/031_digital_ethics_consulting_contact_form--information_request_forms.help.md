# digital_ethics_consulting_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The digital ethics consulting contact form is designed to collect information from users seeking advice or guidance on digital ethics-related matters.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To start, ensure that you have a clear understanding of what you are seeking help with.
2.  Begin by selecting the correct options for "Organization" (Yes/No) and choose the categories relevant to your request (Ethics, AI, Data, or Other).
3.  Enter your contact information (Name, Email, Phone) and ensure it's accurate.
4.  Provide a detailed description of the issue you are experiencing or the question you have.
5.  Finally, add any additional comments you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Form Data** (Page 1):
    *   **Organization** (Select One):
        *   If you are contacting us on behalf of an organization, choose "Yes" to provide more information about your organization.
*   **Name** (Text):
    *   Enter your name as it appears on official documents or as you would like to be addressed.
*   **Email** (Email):
    *   Enter a valid email address that we can use to reply to your inquiry.
*   **Phone** (Text):
    *   Enter your phone number where we can reach you for follow-up questions or discussions.
*   **Category** (Select Multiple):
    *   Choose one or more categories related to your request (Ethics, AI, Data, or Other).
*   **Issue Description** (Text):
    *   Please provide a clear and detailed description of the issue you are experiencing or the question you have.
*   **Contact Name** (Text):
    *   Enter the name of the person we should contact regarding your request.
*   **Contact Email** (Email):
    *   Enter the email address of the person we should contact regarding your request.
*   **Contact Phone** (Text):
    *   Enter the phone number of the person we should contact regarding your request.
*   **Additional Comments** (Text):
    *   If you have any further information or comments regarding your request, enter them here.
 
## Tips
*   Be as clear and concise as possible when filling out this form.
*   Ensure that the contact information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
*   Attach any relevant documents or materials that can aid in our understanding of your request.
*   A prompt response will be provided within a reasonable timeframe.
