# Information Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for updating personal or business information, such as contact details or additional information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review and fill in the necessary fields as needed.
2. Fill out the "basic information" section with your basic details.
3. Enter your "contact details" in the respective fields.
4. Provide any "additional information" you'd like to include.
5. Submit the form to update your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **basic information** (`basic_info`, text, optional): Enter your basic contact information, such as your name, address, and email.
* **contact details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Add or update your contact details as needed, including phone numbers, email addresses, or any other relevant contact information.
* **additional information** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Provide any extra information you'd like to share about yourself or your business.

## Tips
* Be concise and accurate when filling out the form.
* Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
* If you need to update duplicate or incorrect information, please re-complete the form with the correct data.
