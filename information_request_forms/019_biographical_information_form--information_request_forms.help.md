# Biographical Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Biographical Information Form is a comprehensive collection of personal details that will be used to gather background information about you. This form is intended to collect your personal, professional, and contact information to ensure a thorough understanding of your identity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each field carefully and ensure you are providing accurate information.
2. Enter your personal details in the corresponding fields.
3. Fill out any additional fields that are relevant to your situation.
4. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your given name.
* **Middle Name** (`middle_name`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your middle name or initial if applicable.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your family name.
* **Suffix** (`suffix`, `text`, `optional`): Enter any suffix such as Jr, Sr, III, etc.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, `required`): Enter your birth date in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Place of Birth** (`place_of_birth`, `text`, `optional`): Enter city and state or country where you were born.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, `required`): Select your gender identity from the options provided.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `note`, `optional`): This is a note field for additional contact information.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, `required`): Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, `required`): Enter your primary contact number.
* **Alternate Phone** (`alternate_phone`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your secondary contact number.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, `text`, `required`): Enter your complete street address.
* **Apartment or Unit** (`apartment_unit`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your apartment or unit number if applicable.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, `required`): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State or Province** (`state`, `text`, `required`): Enter the state or province where you reside.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, `required`): Enter the postal code of your location.
* **Country** (`country`, `text`, `required`): Enter the country of your residence.
* **Identification** (`identification`, `note`, `optional`): This is a note field for government issued ID information.
* **Social Security Number** (`ssn`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your social security number or national ID if applicable.
* **Driver's License Number** (`drivers_license_number`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your driver's license number.
* **License State** (`license_state`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the state that issued your license.
* **License Expiration** (`license_expiration`, `date`, `optional`): Enter the expiration date of your license if applicable.
* **Citizenship Status** (`citizenship_status`, `select_one`, `optional`): Select your citizenship status from the options provided.
* **Education** (`education`, `note`, `optional`): This is a note field for your educational background.
* **Highest Education Level** (`highest_education`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the highest level of education completed from the options provided.
* **College or University** (`college_university`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the name of the institution where you studied.
* **Major or Field of Study** (`major_field_of_study`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your major or concentration.
* **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`, `number`, `optional`): Enter the year of graduation if applicable.
* **Employment** (`employment`, `note`, `optional`): This is a note field for your employment information.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, `required`): Select your current employment status from the options provided.
* **Current Employer** (`current_employer`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the name of your current employer if applicable.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your position or title.
* **Years Employed** (`years_employed`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the length of employment if applicable.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `note`, `optional`): This is a note field for emergency contact information.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, `required`): Enter your relationship to the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, `required`): Enter the contact number of your emergency contact.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, `note`, `optional`): This is a note field for any other relevant information.
* **Languages Spoken** (`languages_spoken`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the languages you speak.
* **Military Service** (`military_service`, `select_one`, `optional`): Select whether you have served in the military from the options provided.
* **Certifications or Licenses** (`certifications`, `text`, `optional`): List any professional certifications or licenses.
* **Authorization** (`authorization`, `select_one`, `required`): Confirm that all information provided is accurate and complete, or understand that false information may disqualify you.
* **Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date of this form.
