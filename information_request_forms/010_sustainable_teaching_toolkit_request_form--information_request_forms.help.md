# Sustainable Teaching Toolkit Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainable Teaching Toolkit Request Form is designed to provide educators and instructors with resources tailored to their specific teaching needs. To submit a request, please complete the form by selecting the relevant fields as instructed below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the **Primary Topic of Request**: Choose the core subject that best describes the topic of your request from the drop-down list (Math, Science, English, History, or Other).
2. Indicate whether you **Need Resources for this Topic?**: Please select either 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you require resources for the selected topic.
3. Choose your **Email Format Preference**: Select how you would like to receive emails about your resources (HTML, Plain, or Both).
4. Select the **Type of Resource Needed**: Choose the type of resource you require (Lesson Plans, Videos, Books, Weblinks, or Other).
5. Choose your **Resource Format Preference**: Select the format in which you would like to receive the resource (PDF, PPT, DOCX, or HTML).
6. Provide a specific **Deadline or Date**: Enter a date by which you need the resources.
7. Write a detailed **Request Description**: Provide a brief description of your request, explaining what you are looking for.
8. Enter your **Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your official records.
9. Select your **Institutional Affiliation**: Choose your institution from the drop-down list (High School, College/University, Community College, or Other).
10. Choose your **Contact Method**: Select how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or Text).
11. Indicate if you would like a **Follow-up Request**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you would like follow-up requests.
12. (Optional) Select how you would like to be contacted **Again**: Choose how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or Text).
13. Provide your preferred **Best Time of Day**: Select a time of day when you are most available to respond.
14. Add any **Additional Comments**: Please provide any additional information that may be helpful to us in processing your request.
