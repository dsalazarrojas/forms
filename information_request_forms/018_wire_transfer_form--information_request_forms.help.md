# wire transfer form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for wire transfer requests. It is likely used by financial institutions or banks to facilitate wire transfers. Please provide the required information to help process your transfer efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as prompted.
2. Make sure to enter all necessary information accurately.
3. Select the correct option for the transfer type, if applicable.
4. Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): This field is for your first name. Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Account Number** (`account_number`, `text`, required): This field is for your account number. Enter the correct account number as it appears on your account statement.
* **Amount** (`amount`, `number`, required): This field is for the transfer amount. Enter the exact amount you wish to transfer.
* **Routing Number** (`routing_number`, `text`, required): This field is for your routing number. Enter the correct routing number as it appears on your account statement.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): This field is for the transfer date. Enter the date you wish to make the transfer. Please use the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): This field is for the transfer time. Enter the time you wish to make the transfer. Please use 12-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, optional): This field is for a brief description or note about the transfer. This is not required, but it can be helpful for the transfer staff.
* **Wire Transfer Amount** (`wire_transfer_amount`, `number`, required): This field is for the wire transfer amount. Enter the exact amount to be transferred.
* **Account Holder** (`account_holder`, `text`, required): This field is for the account holder's name. Enter the name as it appears on the account.
* **Transfer Type** (`transfer_type`, `select_one`, required): This field is for selecting the type of transfer. Choose the correct option from the list (Option 1 or Option 2).
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): This field is for any additional notes or comments. This is not required, but it can be useful for the transfer staff.

Please note that you cannot submit the form without filling in all required fields (First name, Account Number, Amount, Routing Number, Date, Time, Wire Transfer Amount, Account Holder, and Transfer Type).
