# Pregnancy And Parenting Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information and consent from individuals who wish to participate in pregnancy and parenting-related programs or activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the `Student ID` number if you have one assigned.
2. Provide your name as it appears on your documents.
3. Enter your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pregnancy And Parenting** (`text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your interest in pregnancy and parenting related activities or programs.
* **Student ID** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your student ID number if assigned.
* **Student Name** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your documents.
* **Student Email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted. Please make sure to provide a valid email address that you actively use.

## Tips
* Please ensure the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review the provided fields carefully and ask for clarification if needed.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact form@gic.mx for assistance.
