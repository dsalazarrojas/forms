# Orchestra Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Orchestra Contract Form is used to record information about a musician's performance schedule, contract terms, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the musician's name in the **Musician** field.
2. Select the rehearsal date from the calendar or type it in manually in the **Rehearsal Date** field.
3. Enter the time of the performance in the **Time** field.
4. In the **Contract Terms** field, describe the terms of the contract, if any.
5. Check the **Performance Attendance** field to indicate whether the musician will be performing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contract Terms** (`contract_terms`, `text`, **required**): Describe any specific terms of the contract, if applicable.
