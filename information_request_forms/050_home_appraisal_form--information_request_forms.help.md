# home_appraisal_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and home details for the purpose of a home appraisal. It is a simple and straightforward form that helps gather essential data about the property and its owners. Fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that the appraiser has all the necessary information to provide an accurate and reliable appraisal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Contact Information** section with your name, phone number, and email address. This is optional but recommended to ensure that the appraiser can contact you with any questions or to provide updates on the appraisal process.
2. Provide detailed information about the **Home Details** section, including the address, type, and any other relevant features of the property. This will help the appraiser understand the property's value and make a more accurate assessment.
3. In the **Appraiser Information** section, enter the name and contact information of the appraiser you have assigned to the property. This is crucial to ensure that you and the appraiser can stay in touch throughout the appraisal process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, optional)
* This is a text field where you can enter your name, phone number, and email address.
### **Home Details** (Home Details, text, optional)
* Enter a detailed description of the property, including its address, type (e.g., single-family home, condo, etc.), and any other relevant features.
### **Appraiser Information** (Appraiser Information, text, optional)
* Provide the name and contact information of the appraiser you have assigned to the property. This will help ensure that you can communicate effectively with the appraiser throughout the appraisal process.
