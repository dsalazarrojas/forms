# Immigration Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Immigration Assessment Form is designed to collect relevant information from clients and assess their immigration needs. It's an essential tool for ensuring accurate and complete information for a smooth assessment process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the following sections:
	* **Client Details (Page 1)**: Provide general information about yourself, including your name, ID number, and email address.
	* **Assessment Questions (Page 2)**: Select one of the three available options for each assessment question.
	* **Client Information (Page 3)**: Provide any additional information you'd like to share about yourself.
	* **Assessment Result (Page 4)**: Select one of the three available options for the assessment result.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details** (`client_details`, text, required): Enter your full name, ID number, and email address to identify yourself.
* **Assessment Questions** (`assessment_questions`, select_one, required): Answer three assessment questions by selecting one option per question. The questions are designed to help us understand your immigration needs.
* **Client Information** (`client_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information you'd like to share about yourself.
* **Assessment Result** (`assessment_result`, select_one, required): Select one of the three available options for the assessment result.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required fields to ensure the best possible outcome.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authority or support team for guidance.
