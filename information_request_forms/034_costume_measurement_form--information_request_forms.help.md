# Costume Measurement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Costume Measurement Form is designed to collect detailed measurements for costume design and creation. Providing accurate measurements will help ensure a custom-fit costume that flatters your body type and complements your style.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page to ensure you have all the required information.
2. Fill out each page according to the instructions provided.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Body Type** (`body_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the body type that best describes your physique, such as athletic, pear-shaped, or round.
- **Height** (`height`, number, required): Enter your height in centimeters.
- **Bust Measurements** (`bust_measurements`, number, optional): If applicable, provide your bust measurements for a better fit.
- **Hip Measurements** (`hip_measurements`, number, optional): If applicable, provide your hip measurements for a better fit.
- **Waist Measurements** (`waist_measurements`, number, optional): If applicable, provide your waist measurements for a better fit.
- **Neck Collar Measurements** (`neck_collar_measurements`, number, optional): If applicable, provide your neck collar measurements for a better fit.
- **Shoulder Measurements** (`shoulder_measurements`, number, optional): If applicable, provide your shoulder measurements for a better fit.
- **Sleeve Length Measurements** (`sleeve_length_measurements`, number, optional): If applicable, provide your sleeve length measurements for a better fit.
- **Arm Circumference** (`arm_circumference`, number, optional): If applicable, provide your arm circumference for a better fit.
- **Sleeve Hem Allowance** (`sleeve_hem_allowance`, number, optional): If applicable, provide your sleeve hem allowance for a better fit.
- **Pant/Pant Leg Circumference** (`pant_pant_leg_circumference`, number, optional): If applicable, provide your pant/pant leg circumference for a better fit.
- **Pant/Pant Leg Length** (`pant_pant_leg_length`, number, optional): If applicable, provide your pant/pant leg length for a better fit.
- **Shoe Size** (`shoe_size`, text, optional): Enter your shoe size.
- **Coat Size** (`coat_size`, text, optional): Enter your coat size.
- **Top Size** (`top_size`, text, optional): Enter your top size.

- **Hair Color** (`hair_color`, text, optional): Select your hair color.
- **Eye Type** (`eye_type`, text, optional): Select your eye type.
- **Face Shape** (`face_shape`, text, optional): Select your face shape.
- **Hair Style** (`hair_style`, text, optional): Select your hair style.
- **Skin Tone** (`skin_tone`, text, optional): Select your skin tone.
