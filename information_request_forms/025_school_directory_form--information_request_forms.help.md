# School Directory Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information about a family's contact details and their child's educational information for school administration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Family Name" field with the name of the student's family.
2. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
3. Provide the street address of the family residence in the "Street" field.
4. Enter the city of the family residence in the "City" field.
5. Enter the state of the family residence in the "State" field.
6. Enter the zip code of the family residence in the "Zip Code" field.
7. Enter the email address of the family contact person in the "Email" field (this is optional).
8. Enter the phone number of the family contact person in the "Phone" field (this is optional).
9. Select the student's grade level from the "Student Grade" dropdown list.
10. Enter the student's date of birth in the "Student DOB" field.
11. Enter the student's age in the "Student Age" field (this is optional, but it will help with age-related calculations).
12. Enter the student's grade point average (GPA) in the "Student GPA" field (this is optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Family Name** (`family_name`, text, required/optional): The name of the student's family.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): The name of the student.
* **Street** (`street`, text, required/optional): The street address of the family residence.
* **City** (`city`, text, required/optional): The city of the family residence.
* **State** (`state`, text, required/optional): The state of the family residence.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required/optional): The zip code of the family residence.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): The email address of the family contact person (optional).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): The phone number of the family contact person (optional).
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, select_one, required/optional): The grade level of the student.
* **Student DOB** (`student_dob`, date, required/optional): The date of birth of the student.
* **Student Age** (`student_age`, number, required/optional): The age of the student (optional).
* **Student GPA** (`student_gpa`, number, required/optional): The grade point average of the student (optional).
