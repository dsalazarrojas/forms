# Virus Support Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Virus Support Contact Form is designed to provide a platform for customers to submit their virus-related issues, questions, or concerns to our support team. This form helps us gather essential information from you, allowing us to better understand and resolve your issues efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form title and description to understand the purpose.
2. Fill in the form fields as prompted. For each field, we'll provide a brief explanation of what information we need from you.
3. Double-check your submission before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_data** (`form_data`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you can provide any additional information about your issue or concern. Please be as detailed as possible to help us understand your problem better.
* **contact_details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required: false): If you'd like to share your contact information (e.g., email, phone number), this is the field to do so. This helps us reach out to you for further assistance or clarification.
* **device_info** (`device_info`, `text`, required: false): In this field, provide any relevant details about the device or system affected by the issue (e.g., device model, operating system).
