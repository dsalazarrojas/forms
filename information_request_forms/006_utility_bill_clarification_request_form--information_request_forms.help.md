# Utility Bill Clarification Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Utility Bill Clarification Request Form is used to provide details for utility bill clarification purposes. This form helps our team to understand your concerns and resolve any issues related to your utility bills. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that we can effectively assist you.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Fill out the form completely and accurately
To complete this form, please fill out each field with the required information. If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

### Step 2: Review and submit
Once you have filled out the form, review it carefully to ensure that all fields are filled out correctly. Then, submit the form to request clarification on your utility bill.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Utility bill request** (1): Provide a clear and concise description of the issue or question you are seeking clarification on.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false

- **Name** (2): Enter your name as it appears on your account.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false

- **Utility bill date received** (3): Enter the date you received your utility bill.
  - Type: date
  - Required: false

- **Account number** (4): Enter your account number.
  - Type: number
  - Required: false

- **Meter number** (5): Enter your meter number.
  - Type: number
  - Required: false

- **Amount overpaid** (6): If you have overpaid for your utility bill, enter the amount you overpaid.
  - Type: number
  - Required: false

- **Billing period** (7): Select the type of bill you are inquiring about (Electricity, Gas, Water, or Other).
  - Type: select_one
  - Required: false

- **Current reading** (8): Enter your current meter reading.
  - Type: number
  - Required: false

- **Previous reading** (9): Enter your previous meter reading.
  - Type: number
  - Required: false

- **Bill amount** (10): Enter the amount on your utility bill.
  - Type: number
  - Required: false

- **Payment method** (11): Select the method of payment you used for your utility bill (Cheque, Electronic fund transfer, Bank draft, Credit card, or Other).
  - Type: select_multiple
  - Required: false

- **Comments** (12): Provide any additional comments or information you would like to share about your inquiry.
  - Type: note
  - Required: false

- **Email** (13): Enter your email address.
  - Type: email
  - Required: false

- **Phone** (14): Enter your phone number.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false

- **Additional info** (15): Provide any additional information you would like to share about your utility bill inquiry.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
