# Wage Garnishment Assistance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request wage garnishment assistance from the system. You will need to provide detailed information to complete this form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Street Address in the Street Address field.
4. Enter your Apartment or Suite number in the Apartment or Suite field (if applicable).
5. Enter your City in the City field.
6. Enter your State in the State field.
7. Enter your ZIP in the ZIP field.
8. Enter your Email in the Email field (if you would like to be contacted).
9. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
10. Enter your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
11. Select the Type of Wage Garnishment (Bank, Wage garnishment, or Other) from the dropdown menu.
12. Enter your Employer's Name in the Employer Name field.
13. Enter your Employer's Address in the Employer Address field.
14. Enter your Employer's Phone number in the Employer Phone field.
15. Enter the Garnishment Amount in the Garnishment Amount field.
16. Enter the Start Date of Garnishment in the Start Date of Garnishment field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
17. Enter the End Date of Garnishment in the End Date of Garnishment field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
18. Enter the Reason for Wage Garnishment in the Reason for Wage Garnishment field.
19. If applicable, upload supporting documents in the Supporting Documents field.
20. Enter any Additional Comments in the Additional Comments field (if needed).
21. Enter your Employee's Name in the Employee Name field.
22. Enter your Employee's Address in the Employee Address field.
23. Enter your Employee's Phone number in the Employee Phone field (if applicable).
24. Enter your Employee's Date of Birth in the Employee Date of Birth field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
25. Enter the Employee's Termination Date in the Employee Termination Date field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, **text**, required): Enter your name as it is to be displayed.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, **text**, required): Enter your last name as it is to be displayed.
* **Street Address** (`address1`, **text**, required): Enter your street address where you reside.
* **Apartment or Suite** (`address2`, **text**, optional): Enter your apartment or suite number (if applicable).
* **City** (`city`, **text**, required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, **text**, required): Enter your state of residence.
* **ZIP** (`zip`, **text**, required): Enter your ZIP code.
* **Email** (`email`, **email**, optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (`phone`, **text**, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, **date**, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Type of Wage Garnishment** (`wage_garnishment_type`, **select_one, required): Select the type of wage garnishment (Bank, Wage garnishment, or Other).
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, **text**, required): Enter your employer's name.
* **Employer Address** (`employer_address`, **text, required): Enter your employer's address.
* **Employer Phone** (`employer_phone`, **text, required): Enter your employer's phone number.
* **Garnishment Amount** (`garnishment_amount`, **number, required): Enter the garnishment amount.
* **Start Date of Garnishment** (`garnishment_start_date`, **date, required): Enter the start date of garnishment in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **End Date of Garnishment** (`garnishment_end_date`, **date, required): Enter the end date of garnishment in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Reason for Wage Garnishment** (`garnishment_reason`, **text, required): Enter the reason for wage garnishment.
* **Supporting Documents** (`garnishment_documents`, **note, optional): Upload any supporting documents.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, **text, optional): Enter any additional comments.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, **text, required): Enter your employee's name.
* **Employee Address** (`employee_address`, **text, required): Enter your employee's address.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, **text, optional): Enter your employee's phone number.
* **Employee Date of Birth** (`employee_dob`, **date, required): Enter your employee's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Employee Termination Date** (`employee_termination_date`, **date, optional): Enter the employee's termination date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
