# Worker Certification Program Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Worker Certification Program Contact Form is designed to gather information from workers interested in obtaining certification in various areas, such as Basic Life Support (BLS) or Advanced Cardiovascular Life Support (ACLS). This form helps the program administrators to communicate with workers and facilitate the certification process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First name** in the first name field.
2. Enter your **Last name** in the last name field.
3. Type your **Email** in the email field.
4. Select your **Certification interest** from the dropdown list (e.g., Basic Life Support (BLS), Advanced Cardiovascular Life Support (ACLS), etc.).
5. Type any **Message** you would like to communicate to the program administrators in the message field (optional).
6. If you have a **Phone number**, please enter it in the phone number field (optional).
7. If you need to specify your **Date of birth** and **Time of birth**, please do so in the respective fields (optional).
8. If you have a preferred **Time of contact**, please specify it in the time of contact field (optional).
9. Enter your **Certification date** (if applicable) and **Certification time** (if applicable) in the respective fields (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Required): Enter a valid email address to ensure communication with the program administrators.
* **Certification interest** (Required): Select the type of certification you are interested in (e.g., Basic Life Support (BLS), Advanced Cardiovascular Life Support (ACLS), etc.).
* **Message** (Optional): Type any message you would like to communicate to the program administrators.
* **Phone number** (Optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Date of birth** (Optional): Enter your date of birth (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Time of birth** (Optional): Enter your time of birth (HH:MM).
* **Time of contact** (Optional): Specify the time you are available for contact.
* **Certification date** (Optional): Enter the date of your certification (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Certification time** (Optional): Enter the time of your certification (HH:MM).

**Tips:**

* Double-check your email address for accuracy.
* Ensure your certification interest matches the program's offerings.
* Enter your phone number and time of birth only if you are available for follow-up communication.
