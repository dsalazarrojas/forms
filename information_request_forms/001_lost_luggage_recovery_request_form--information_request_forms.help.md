# Lost Luggage Recovery Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lost Luggage Recovery Request Form is used to report lost luggage issues. Fill this form to report your lost luggage and provide relevant information for recovery efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name.
2. Provide your Email address for communication.
3. Enter your Contact Number for efficient contact.
4. Choose a Travel Date.
5. Enter Flight Number and confirm it.
6. Describe your baggage with Baggage Description.
7. Specify the Baggage Value if applicable.
8. Select the Luggage Type.
9. Provide the Airline and Flight Number 2, Flight Number 3, Flight Number 4 (if applicable).
10. Ensure that you have confirmed the Flight Number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name as per your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name as per your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for easy communication and updates.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your contact number for efficient contact.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of your travel.
* **Flight Number** (`flight_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the flight number related to the lost luggage.
* **Flight Number Confirmation** (`flight_number_confirmation`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Confirm if you are sure about the flight number.
* **Baggage Description** (`baggage_description`, `note`, required/optional): Describe the lost luggage in detail.
* **Baggage Value** (`baggage_value`, `number`, required/optional): Specify the value of the lost baggage if it has any monetary value.
* **Luggage Type** (`luggage_type`, `select_one`, required/true): Select the type of luggage.
* **Airline** (`airline`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the airline name associated with the lost luggage.
* **Flight Number 2** (`flight_number_2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the second flight number.
* **Flight Number 3** (`flight_number_3`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the third flight number.
* **Flight Number 4** (`flight_number_4`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the fourth flight number.
* **Baggage Type** (`luggage_type`, `select_one`, required/true): Select the type of luggage you are trying to recover (Suitcase, Bag, or Other)

Note:
- Please fill in the form accurately and honestly to help us assist you in recovering your lost luggage.
- Ensure that you have confirmed the flight number to avoid any confusion.
