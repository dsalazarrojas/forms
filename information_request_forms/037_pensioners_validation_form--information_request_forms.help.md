# Pensioners Validation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pensioners Validation Form is a required document used for verifying pensioners' information to avoid duplication and ensure accurate data entry. Please fill in the form accurately to avoid delays in processing your pensioner information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First name.
2. Fill in your Last name.
3. Enter your Date of birth.
4. Enter your Id number.
5. Enter your Email.
6. Enter your Phone number.
7. Enter your Address.
8. Select your Pensioner type.
9. Enter any dependant information (if applicable).
10. Fill in the dependant's name (if applicable).
11. Enter the dependant's Date of birth (if applicable).
12. Select your Pensioner status.
13. Enter the date you have been a pensioner (if applicable).
14. Enter any additional notes about yourself as a pensioner.
15. Select the user who submitted this form.
16. Enter the date this form was submitted.
17. Enter the user who submitted this form.
18. Enter the date this form was submitted by the user.
19. Enter the email of the user who submitted this form.
20. Enter the phone number of the user who submitted this form.
21. Enter the address of the user who submitted this form.
22. Enter the pensioner ID of the user who submitted this form.
23. Select the pensioner type of the user who submitted this form.
24. Select the status of the pensioner who submitted this form.
25. Enter the date the form was submitted by the pensioner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`First name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`Last name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Date of birth** (`Date of birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Id number** (`Id number`, number, required): Enter your ID number.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Pensioner ID** (`Pensioner ID`, number, required): Enter your pensioner ID number.
* **Pensioner type** (`Pensioner type`, select_one, required): Select your pensioner type (Pensioner, Pensioner with dependants, or Pensioner with dependant).
* **Dependant information** (`Dependant information`, text, optional): Enter any dependant information (if applicable).
* **Dependant name** (`Dependant name`, text, optional): Enter the dependant's name (if applicable).
* **Dependant date of birth** (`Dependant date of birth`, date, optional): Enter the dependant's date of birth (if applicable).
* **Pensioner status** (`Pensioner status`, select_one, required): Select your pensioner status (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).
* **Pensioner since** (`Pensioner since`, date, optional): Enter the date you have been a pensioner (if applicable).
* **Pensioner note** (`Pensioner note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about yourself as a pensioner.
* **Form submitted by** (`Form submitted by`, text, required): Select the user who submitted this form (e.g., yourself).
* **Form submitted on** (`Form submitted on`, date, required): Enter the date this form was submitted.
* **Form submitted by user** (`Form submitted by user`, text, required): Enter the user who submitted this form (e.g., yourself).
* **Form submitted on user** (`Form submitted on user`, date, required): Enter the date this form was submitted by the user.
* **Form submitted by email** (`Form submitted by email`, email, required): Enter the email of the user who submitted this form.
* **Form submitted by phone** (`Form submitted by phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the user who submitted this form.
* **Form submitted by address** (`Form submitted by address`, text, required): Enter the address of the user who submitted this form.
* **Form submitted by pensioner ID** (`Form submitted by pensioner ID`, number, required): Enter the pensioner ID of the user who submitted this form.
* **Form submitted by pensioner type** (`Form submitted by pensioner type`, select_one, required): Select the pensioner type of the user who submitted this form (Pensioner, Pensioner with dependants, or Pensioner with dependant).
* **Form submitted by pensioner status** (`Form submitted by pensioner status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the pensioner who submitted this form (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).
* **Form submitted by pensioner since** (`Form submitted by pensioner since`, date, required): Enter the date the form was submitted by the pensioner.
* **Form submitted by pensioner note** (`Form submitted by pensioner note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the user who submitted this form.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Make sure to enter the correct date formats (mm/dd/yyyy).
* Enter any relevant information for dependants (if applicable).
