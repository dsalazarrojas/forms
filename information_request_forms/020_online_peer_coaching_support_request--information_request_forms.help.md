# Online Peer Coaching Support Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Peer Coaching Support Request form is designed for individuals to seek support and guidance from their peers. This form will help you provide information about your request, and our team will use this information to schedule a coaching session with a peer who can best support you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely, including your name and email address.
2. Describe your support request in the "Description" field.
3. Select the type of support you are seeking (Coaching, Guidance, Support, or Other).
4. Choose the coaching program you are enrolled in (High School, College, University, Corporate, or Other).
5. Select the location where you would like to receive support (On-site, Off-site, or Remote).
6. Choose the frequency and format of follow-up reminders you would like to receive (Weekly, Biweekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Semi-Monthly).
7. If desired, add any additional follow-up message to be included in the reminders.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Request Information
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Description** (`Description`, `text`, required): Describe your request for peer coaching support.
* **Support Request** (`Support Request`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of support you are seeking.
* **Coaching Program** (`Coaching Program`, `select_one`, required): Select the coaching program you are enrolled in.
* **Coaching Coordinator** (`Coaching Coordinator`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any coaching coordinators you would like to work with.
* **School** (`School`, `select_one`, optional): Select the school you are affiliated with.
* **Location** (`Location`, `select_one`, optional): Select the location where you would like to receive support.
* **Preferred Date** (`Preferred Date`, `date`, optional): Select a preferred date for your coaching session.
* **Preferred Time** (`Preferred Time`, `time`, optional): Select a preferred time for your coaching session.
* **Follow-up Reminders** (`Follow-up Reminders`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether you would like to receive follow-up reminders.
* **Follow-up Frequency** (`Follow-up Frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Select the frequency of follow-up reminders.
* **Follow-up Message** (`Follow-up Message`, `text`, optional): Add any additional message to be included in the reminders.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible in your description to ensure our team can best support you.
* If you have any specific requirements or preferences for your coaching session, please let us know in the "Description" field.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, please feel free to ask for help.
