# Business Continuity Specialist Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide information about your organization's business continuity needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately, using your organization's information.
- Be as detailed as possible in your responses to help the specialist team understand your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name**: Enter your organization's name, such as XYZ Corporation.
* **Industry**: Enter the type of business or sector your organization operates in, such as finance or healthcare.
* **Company Size**: Select the number of employees in your organization, which will help the specialist team gauge the scope of the project.
* **Primary Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person to contact for this project.
* **Contact Title**: Enter the title of the contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the business email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the business phone number of the contact person.
* **Type of Service Needed**: Select the specific services you require from the specialist team, such as continuity planning, risk assessment, or plan testing.
* **Current Business Continuity Status**: Select where you are in the process of establishing business continuity, which will help the specialist team understand your current situation.
* **Main Continuity Challenge**: Briefly describe your top concern related to business continuity.
* **Scope Areas**: Select the areas that need focus for your organization's business continuity, such as data protection or staff communications.
* **Project Timeline**: Choose when you need to start the project, which will help the specialist team schedule accordingly.
* **Budget Range**: Select the budget range you are willing to invest, which will help the specialist team provide guidance on project scope and costs.
* **Preferred Engagement Model**: Choose how you prefer to work with the specialist team, such as a full project support or advisory support.
* **Additional Information**: Provide any other relevant details that might be helpful for the specialist team to understand your organization's business continuity needs.
* **Preferred Project Timeline**: If not selecting one of the provided options, provide a specific timeframe for when you would like the project to commence.

Note that the form is designed to be a flexible tool, and you can adjust it according to your organization's specific needs. The goal is to provide the specialist team with accurate and relevant information to provide the best possible support.
