# Home Value Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Value Estimate Form is used to collect information about a residential property's value and estimate its value based on various factors such as address, size, age, and other characteristics of the property.

## How To Complete This Form
This form is divided into multiple pages, each with a specific purpose. Please refer to the following explanations for details on each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What is the address?
* **What is the address?** (Text field, required) - This field is used to collect the address of the property. Please enter the physical address of the property, including the street name and number, city, state, and zip code.

### 2. How many bedrooms does the property have?
* **How many bedrooms?** (Select One field, not required) - This field is used to collect information about the number of bedrooms in the property. Please select from the options: "Yes", "No", or "Other" (if the property has a different number of bedrooms).

### 3. What is the square footage of the property?
* **Square Footage** (Number field, required) - This field is used to collect the total square footage of the property.

### 4. How old is the property?
* **Age of the Home** (Select One field, not required) - This field is used to collect information about the age of the property. Please select from the options: "Yes", "No", or "Other" (if the property has a different age).

### 5. What is the lot size of the property?
* **Lot Size** (Number field, required) - This field is used to collect the total lot size of the property.

### 6. Is the property a single-story or multi-story?
* **Is the Property a Single-Story or Multi-Story?** (Select One field, not required) - This field is used to collect information about the number of stories in the property. Please select from the options: "True" or "False".

### 7. How many stories is the property?
* **Number of Stories** (Select Multiple field, not required) - This field is used to collect information about the number of stories in the property. Please select all that apply from the options: "Yes", "No", or "Other" (if the property has a different number of stories).

### 8. What is the property type?
* **Is this Property a Single-Family or Multi-Family Property?** (Select One field, not required) - This field is used to collect information about the type of property. Please select from the options: "True" or "False".

### 9. Is this a mobile home?
* **Is this a Mobile Home?** (Select One field, not required) - This field is used to collect information about the type of property. Please select from the options: "True" or "False".

### 10. What is the property owner's email address?
* **Email of the Property Owner** (Text field, required) - This field is used to collect the email address of the property owner.

### 11. What is your name?
* **What is your Name?** (Text field, required) - This field is used to collect your name.

### 12. What is your address?
* **What is your Address?** (Text field, required) - This field is used to collect your address.

### 13. What is the square footage of the property?
* **What is the Square Footage of the Property?** (Number field, required) - This field is used to collect the total square footage of the property.

### 14. What is the lot size of the property?
* **What is the Lot Size of the Property?** (Number field, required) - This field is used to collect the total lot size of the property.

### 15. How old is the property?
* **Age of the Property** (Select One field, not required) - This field is used to collect information about the age of the property. Please select from the options: "Yes", "No", or "Other" (if the property has a different age).

### 16. What is the zip code of the property?
* **Zip Code of the Property** (Text field, required) - This field is used to collect the zip code of the property.

### 17. What is your email address?
* **What is your Email?** (Text field, required) - This field is used to collect your email address.

### 18. What is your phone number?
* **What is your Phone Number?** (Text field, required) - This field is used to collect your phone number.

### 19. What is the zip code of the property?
* **What is the Zip of the Property?** (Select One field, not required) - This field is used to collect information about the zip code of the property. Please select from the options: "Yes", "No", or "Other" (if the property has a different zip code).
