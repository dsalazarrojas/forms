# Patient Progress Notes Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be used by healthcare providers to document and track the progress of a patient's care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information in the "Patient Information" section.
2. Select the date and time of the visit in the "Date of Visit" and "Time of Visit" sections.
3. Document the patient's chief complaint and history of present illness.
4. Provide any general information relevant to the patient's care.
5. Record the patient's social history.
6. Describe the results of the physical examination.
7. Outline the assessment and plan for the patient's care.
8. List any allergies the patient has.
9. List any medications the patient is currently taking.
10. Document any test results.
11. Enter any other relevant information about the patient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, required): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and other identifying information.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, required): Enter the date of the patient's visit.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, required): Enter the time of the patient's visit.
* **Chief Complaint** (`chief_complaint`, text, required): Describe the patient's main reason for visiting the doctor.
* **History of Present Illness** (`history_of_present_illness`, text, required): Document the patient's medical history.
* **General Information** (`general_information`, text, required): Provide any other relevant information about the patient.
* **Social History** (`social_history`, text, required): Document the patient's social history.
* **Physical Examination** (`physical_examination`, text, required): Describe the results of the patient's physical examination.
* **Assessment and Plan** (`assessment_and_plan`, text, required): Outline the plan for the patient's care.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required): List any allergies the patient has.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, required): List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Test Results** (`test_results`, text, required): Document any test results.
* **Other Relevant Information** (`other_relevant_information`, text, required): Enter any other relevant information about the patient.
