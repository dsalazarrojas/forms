# Open Education Initiative Participation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Open Education Initiative Participation Request Form is used to gather information from individuals interested in participating in the Open Education Initiative. This form helps us understand your background, interests, and how you heard about the initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields:
	* First name
	* Last name
	* Email
2. Select your highest level of education completed
3. List your field of study
4. Enter your graduation date (if applicable)
5. Choose how you heard about the Open Education Initiative
6. Select any relevant projects or initiatives you are interested in or have already participated in

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (text, required): Your first name.
### Last Name
* **Last Name** (text, required): Your last name.
### Email
* **Email** (email, required): Your email address.
### Highest Level of Education Completed
* **highest_level_of_education_completed** (select one, required): Select your highest level of education completed from the options provided.
### Field of Study
* **field_of_study** (text, optional): List your field of study.
### Graduation Date
* **graduation_date** (date, optional): Enter your graduation date (if you have already graduated).
### Time of Day
* **time_of_day** (time, optional): Not applicable for this form.
### Note
* **note** (text, optional): Add any additional information you'd like to share about your interests or qualifications.
### How Did You Hear About Us
* **how_did_you_hear_about_us** (text, optional): Select how you heard about the Open Education Initiative from the options provided.
### Open Education Project 1-8
* **open_education_project_1-8** (select multiple, optional): Select one or more projects from the list provided that you are interested in or have already participated in.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about your highest level of education completed, select the closest option that matches your experience.
- If you are interested in multiple projects, select all relevant options.
- If you have already participated in projects not listed, select "Other" and provide details in the "Note" field. 

This guide provides a detailed explanation of the form's purpose, fields, and instructions to ensure that you can easily navigate and fill out the Open Education Initiative Participation Request Form. Please take a few moments to read through this guide, and then fill out the form to share your interests and qualifications with us!
