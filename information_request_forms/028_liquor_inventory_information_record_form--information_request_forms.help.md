# Liquor Inventory Information Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Liquor Inventory Information Record Form is a document used to record and manage liquor inventory data, including sales information and purchase history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: **Sales Data** and **Sales Price** (id: 1 and 3) with the relevant information.
2. If applicable, fill in **Purchase History** and **Purchases** (ids: 2 and 6) with the details of previous purchases.
3. Select the relevant **Liquor Type** (id: 4) from the options provided, if applicable.
4. Add any **Store Info** (id: 5) that is relevant to the sales data, if necessary.
5. Fill in **Sales Date** (id: 7) with the date of the sale.
6. If relevant, fill in **Sales Time** (id: 8) with the time of the sale.
7. Add any additional **Notes** (id: 9) about the sale or item.
8. Enter the **Contact Email** and **Contact Phone** (ids: 10 and 11) if you need to contact someone related to the sale.
9. Finally, fill in **Liquor Cost** (id: 12) and **Sales Count** (id: 13) if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sales Data** (1): A text field to fill in sales data, e.g., the description of the sale or item sold.
* **Purchase History** (2): A text field to fill in purchase history, if applicable.
* **Sales Price** (3): A number field to fill in the price of the sale.
* **Liquor Type** (4): A select field with options 'Yes' and 'No', used to indicate the type of liquor sold, if applicable.
* **Store Info** (5): A text field to fill in additional store information, if necessary.
* **Purchases** (6): A select multiple field with options 'Yes' and 'No', used to indicate if the sale is a purchase, if applicable.
* **Sales Date** (7): A date field to fill in the date of the sale.
* **Sales Time** (8): A time field to fill in the time of the sale, if necessary.
* **Notes** (9): A notes field for any additional comments or information about the sale or item.
* **Contact Email** (10): An email field to fill in the contact email of the person or organization related to the sale.
* **Contact Phone** (11): A text field to fill in the contact phone number of the person or organization related to the sale.
* **Liquor Cost** (12): A number field to fill in the cost of the liquor item.
* **Sales Count** (13): A number field to fill in the number of sales made for the item.
