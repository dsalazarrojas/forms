# Ministry Proposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Ministry Proposal Form is designed to gather information for project proposals from ministries within our organization. This form will help us evaluate and review each proposal based on the provided details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely.
2. Enter your project proposal title.
3. Provide a project description.
4. Choose whether your project has a leadership team.
5. Enter your project start and end dates.
6. Enter your email and phone number (if applicable).
7. Provide the location of your project.
8. Specify your project budget.
9. Select the supporting ministries involved in your project.
10. Briefly explain the justification for your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Ministry Proposal**: <label> (1, text, required) - This is the title of your project proposal.
* **Project Leadership**: <label> (2, select_one, required) - Select "Yes" if your project has leadership and "No" if it does not.
* **Project Name**: <label> (3, text, required) - A clear, concise name for your project.
* **Project Description**: <label> (4, text, required) - Describe your project in a few sentences.
* **Start Date**: <label> (5, date, required) - Enter the start date of your project.
* **End Date**: <label> (6, date, required) - Enter the end date of your project.
* **Contact Email**: <label> (7, email, required) - Enter your contact email.
* **Contact Phone**: <label> (8, text, optional) - Enter your contact phone number (if applicable).
* **Project Location**: <label> (9, text, required) - Provide the location of your project.
* **Project Budget**: <label> (10, number, required) - Enter your project budget.
* **Supporting Ministries**: <label> (11, select_multiple, required) - Select the ministries that will be supporting your project from the options provided.
* **Project Justification**: <label> (12, text, required) - Briefly explain the justification for your project.

Note: Please make sure to review your submission before submitting it for evaluation to ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly and that the information provided is accurate and complete.
