# Housing Support Services Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from residents who are seeking housing support services. The housing support team will use this information to assess and provide the necessary support to the residents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information (Contact Information section) so that the housing support team can get in touch with you.
2. Describe your housing needs (Housing Needs section) by selecting the relevant options that apply to you.
3. Indicate the level of urgency you require for housing support (Urgency section).
4. Enter the size of your household (Household Size section).
5. Provide any additional notes that may be helpful for the housing support team to understand your situation (Additional Notes section).
6. Enter your email address (Email section) so that the housing support team can communicate with you.
7. Enter your phone number (Phone section) so that the housing support team can contact you directly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, text, optional):
	+ This section is designed to collect your contact information, including your name and other details. Please fill in your name, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Housing Needs** (`Housing Needs`, select multiple, optional):
	+ Please select all the housing options that apply to your situation, such as housing needs or preferences.
* **Urgency** (`Urgency`, select one, optional):
	+ Indicate the level of urgency you require for housing support. Please select High, Medium, or Low, depending on your needs.
* **Household Size** (`Household Size`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the number of people in your household.
* **Additional Notes** (`Additional Notes`, note, optional):
	+ Please provide any additional information that may be helpful for the housing support team to understand your situation.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter your email address so that the housing support team can communicate with you.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number so that the housing support team can contact you directly.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the form is completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to the housing support team for clarification.
* Please provide complete and up-to-date information to enable the housing support team to effectively provide the necessary support.
