# Soundtrack Rights Usage Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting the rights to use a sound track. It is intended to be filled out by the person or project team who wishes to use the music.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **User Details**
	* Please fill out your name, email, phone, and contact organization information.
2. **Project Details**
	* Please provide the project title, start date, and end date.
3. **Music Licensing Details**
	* Please select the type of music you are licensing (e.g. commercial, non-commercial, etc.).
4. **Usage Terms**
	* Please specify the intended use of the music (e.g. background music, main theme, etc.).
5. **Payment Terms**
	* Please select the method of payment for the royalty fees (e.g. bank transfer, PayPal, etc.).
6. **Additional Comments**
	* Please provide any additional information that may be helpful for the music licensing process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details**: This is the contact information for the person or project team who is requesting the rights to use the music. Please fill out your name, email, phone, and contact organization information.
* **Project Details**: This is the project information that the music will be used for. Please provide the project title, start date, and end date.
* **Music Licensing Details**: This is the specific information about the music being licensed. Please select the type of music you are licensing (e.g. commercial, non-commercial, etc.).
* **Usage Terms**: This is the intended use of the music. Please specify the intended use of the music (e.g. background music, main theme, etc.).
* **Payment Terms**: This is the method of payment for the royalty fees. Please select the method of payment (e.g. bank transfer, PayPal, etc.).
* **Music License Terms**: This is any additional terms or conditions related to the music licensing.
* **Royalty Payment**: This is the amount of money paid for the royalty fees.
* **Payment Method**: This is the way the payment will be made (e.g. bank transfer, PayPal, etc.).
* **Additional Comments**: This is a field for any additional comments or information that may be helpful for the music licensing process.
* **Email**: This is the contact email for the person or project team who is requesting the rights to use the music.
* **Phone**: This is the contact phone number for the person or project team who is requesting the rights to use the music.
* **Contact Name**: This is the name of the contact person for the music licensing process.
* **Contact Organization**: This is the organization that the contact person represents.
* **Contact Title**: This is the title of the contact person within their organization.
* **Contact Email**: This is the email of the contact person for the music licensing process.
* **Contact Phone**: This is the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Address**: This is the address of the contact person.
* **Submitted By**: This is a field to note who submitted the request.
* **Submitter Info**: This is a field to provide any additional information about the submitter.
* **Submitter Email**: This is the email of the person or project team who submitted the request.
* **Submitter Phone**: This is the phone number of the person or project team who submitted the request.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Please double-check your information for accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the music licensing team for assistance.
* If you have already submitted a request for this specific sound track, please do not submit another.
