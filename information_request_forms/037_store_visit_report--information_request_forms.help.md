## Store Visit Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Store Visit Report form is intended to be used by employees to document their store visits, providing valuable information about their experiences and any issues that arose during their visits. This form helps gather data that can be used to improve customer service and employee training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the name of the store visited from the options provided in the "which store did you visit" field.
2. Describe what happened during your visit in the "what happened during your visit" field.
3. If applicable, select the purpose of your visit (e.g., shopping or meeting) from the options provided in the "what was your purpose of visit" field.
4. Enter any additional comments or notes about your visit in the "any other comments" field, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **which store did you visit** (`store_visit_location`, `select_one`, required):
The store visited is an important identifier for the report, so please select the correct store from the options provided.
* **what happened during your visit** (`store_visit`, `text`, required):
Describe the events or issues that occurred during your visit. Be as detailed as possible to provide valuable information.
* **what was your purpose of visit** (`store_visit_purpose`, `select_one`, required):
Choose the purpose of your visit from the provided options (e.g., shopping or meeting).
* **date of visit** (`store_visit_date`, `date`, required):
Enter the date of your visit in the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **time of visit** (`store_visit_time`, `time`, required):
Enter the time of your visit in the standard time format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **what was your purpose of visit** (`store_visit_purpose`, `select_one`, required):
( Duplicate label, select from options)
* **any other comments** (`store_visit_note`, `note`, required):
If you have any additional comments or observations about your visit, please enter them here.
* **store visit employee** (`store_visit_employee`, `text`, optional):
This field is for any other comments or observations about the employee who visited the store.

Note: All fields are required unless otherwise specified as optional. If you are unsure about any field, please contact the form owner for clarification.
