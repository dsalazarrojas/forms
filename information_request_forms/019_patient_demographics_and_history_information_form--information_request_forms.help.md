# Patient Demographics And History Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect patient demographics and medical history information for new patient registrations. Please fill out this form to provide us with essential information about the patient. If you have already registered a patient with similar information, please do not fill out this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "New Patient" page to enter the patient's basic demographic information.
2. Enter the patient's name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter the patient's date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter any available contact details for the patient, such as address or phone number.
5. Briefly describe any existing medical history or conditions for the patient.
6. List any current or past medical conditions that apply to the patient.
7. Enter any allergies or sensitivities for the patient.
8. Enter any ongoing medications for the patient.
9. Describe any recent or past medical procedures or surgeries.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (Name, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required/optional): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Details** (Contact Details, text, required/optional): Enter any available contact information for the patient, such as address or phone number.
* **Medical History** (Medical History, text, required/optional): Briefly describe any existing medical history or conditions for the patient.
* **Family Medicine** (Family Medicine, text, required/optional): List any family medical history or conditions.
* **Medical Conditions** (Medical Conditions, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any current or past medical conditions that apply to the patient.
* **Allergies** (Allergies, text, required/optional): Enter any allergies or sensitivities for the patient.
* **Medications** (Medications, text, required/optional): Enter any ongoing medications for the patient.
* **Medical Procedures** (Medical Procedures, text, required/optional): Describe any recent or past medical procedures or surgeries.
* **Surgical Procedures** (Surgical Procedures, text, required/optional): Describe any surgical procedures or surgeries the patient has had.
* **Hospital Visits** (Hospital Visits, text, required/optional): Describe any recent or past hospital visits for the patient.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check for spelling errors in any text fields.
* If a condition is not listed in the "Medical Conditions" dropdown, please describe it in the "Medical History" field.
* If a medical condition is a recurring or ongoing issue, please select it in the "Medical Conditions" dropdown.
* Please enter all relevant information for each field, as it will help us provide better care for the patient.
