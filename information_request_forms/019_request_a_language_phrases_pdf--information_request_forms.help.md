# request_a_language_phrases_pdf - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request language phrases or phrases in a PDF format for various purposes such as Personal, Professional, or Both.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the school name where you are affiliated with.
2. Select the languages you need phrases in (you can choose multiple languages).
3. Choose the purpose of your request (Personal, Professional, or Both).
4. Select the level of language phrases you need (Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced).
5. Choose the delivery method for the phrases (PDF, Excel, or Word).
6. Enter your email address.
7. Enter your phone number.
8. Add any additional notes about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### School Name (school_name)
* **School Name** (school_name): Enter the name of the school you are affiliated with. This will help us identify your request and provide the correct language phrases for your purpose.

### Language (language)
* **Language** (language): Select the languages you need phrases in (English, Spanish, French, German, or Chinese). You can choose multiple languages.

### Purpose (purpose)
* **Purpose** (purpose): Choose the purpose of your request (Personal, Professional, or Both). This will help us understand the context of your request.

### Level (level)
* **Level** (level): Select the level of language phrases you need (Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced).

### Delivery Method (delivery_method)
* **Delivery Method** (delivery_method): Choose how you want to receive the language phrases (PDF, Excel, or Word).

### Email (email)
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address where we can contact you with the phrases.

### Phone (phone)
* **Phone** (phone): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.

### Notes (notes)
* **Notes** (notes): Add any additional notes about your request. This is an optional field, but it can help us understand your specific needs.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are not duplicating a request that has already been made.
* Fill out the form accurately to ensure that the requested phrases are delivered correctly and meet your needs.
