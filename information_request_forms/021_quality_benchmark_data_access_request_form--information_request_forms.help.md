# Quality Benchmark Data Access Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request access to quality benchmark data for your team or department. It is used by employees to gather information and data from various sources for business purposes. Ensure that you are using this form for legitimate reasons and not for duplication of work.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and submit it to your approver. Please note that the form is divided into two sections: User Input and Submitter Information. The User Input section contains general information about your request, while the Submitter Information section contains details about the person submitting the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input**: 
  * **User Input (id: 1)** (`text`, required): This field is where you should provide a brief description of the quality benchmark data you are requesting.
* **Request Date (id: 2)** (`date`, optional): This field is where you can provide the date you are requesting access to the quality benchmark data. If left blank, it will default to the current date.
* **Data Format (id: 3)** (`text`, required): This field is where you can specify the format in which you would like to receive the data.
* **Contact Details (id: 4)** (`text`, required): This field is where you should provide your name, title, and department so that the submitter can contact you with any questions or issues.
* **Contact Email (id: 5)** (`email`, optional): This field is where you can provide your email address in case you need to be contacted.
* **Phone Number (id: 6)** (`text`, optional): This field is where you can provide your phone number in case you need to be contacted.
* **Company (id: 7)** (`text`, required): This field is where you should provide the name of your company.
* **Department (id: 8)** (`text`, required): This field is where you should provide the name of your department.
* **Job Title (id: 9)** (`text`, required): This field is where you should provide your job title.
* **Employee Name (id: 10)** (`text`, required): This field is where you should provide your name.
* **Purpose (id: 11)** (`text`, required): This field is where you should provide a brief description of the purpose of your request.
* **Approval Type (id: 12)** (optional): This field is where you can select the type of approval required for your request (Benchmark Approval, Manager Approval, Director Approval).
* **Approval Date (id: 13)** (`date`, optional): This field is where you can provide the date you need approval for, if required.
* **Comments (id: 14)** (`note`, required): This field is where you can add any additional comments about your request.
* **Submit (id: 15)** (optional): This field is used to submit your request. Select 'Yes' if you are ready to submit your request.
* **Submitter**: 
  * **Submitter (id: 16)** (`text`, required): This field is where you should provide your name.
  * **Submitter Email (id: 17)** (`email`, optional): This field is where you can provide your email address in case you need to be contacted.
  * **Submitter Phone (id: 18)** (`text`, optional): This field is where you can provide your phone number in case you need to be contacted.
  * **Submitter Department (id: 19)** (`text`, required): This field is where you should provide the name of your department.
  * **Submitter Job Title (id: 20)** (`text`, required): This field is where you should provide your job title.
  * **Submitter Employee Name (id: 21)** (`text`, required): This field is where you should provide your name.
  * **Submitter Purpose (id: 22)** (`text`, required): This field is where you should provide a brief description of the purpose of your request.
  * **Approver (id: 23)** (`text`, required): This field is where you should provide your approver's name.
  * **Approver Department (id: 24)** (`text`, required): This field is where you should provide your approver's department.
  * **Approver Job Title (id: 25)** (`text`, optional): This field is where you can provide your approver's job title.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form for accuracy and completeness.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, please contact your supervisor or HR department for assistance.
