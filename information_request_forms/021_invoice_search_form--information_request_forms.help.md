# Invoice Search Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Invoice Search Form is a tool for searching invoices based on specific criteria. It helps users quickly find invoices that match their search terms.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Search Criteria" page to enter your search terms.
2. Fill in the search criteria fields with relevant and specific information.
3. Click on the "Submit" button to initiate the search.
4. A new page will appear with additional information for you to provide, if needed.
5. Enter any additional information on this page if required.
6. Click on the "Submit" button to complete the search.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Search Criteria**: Enter the specific details of the invoice you want to search for. This could be a customer name, invoice date, or any other relevant information.
* **Additional Information**: Enter any additional details you think might be helpful for the search. This can include any relevant context or notes about the invoice.
