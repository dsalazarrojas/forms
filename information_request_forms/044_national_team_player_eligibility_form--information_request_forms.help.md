# National Team Player Eligibility Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from players regarding their eligibility to participate in national team events. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure the player's eligibility status is correctly assessed.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the player's details, including first name, last name, email, birth date, phone number, team name, player position, height, weight, and address.
2. Confirm the coach's and team manager's confirmation regarding the player's eligibility.
3. Indicate whether the player is eligible or not.
4. Submit the form to proceed with the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player First Name** (`player_first_name`, text, required): Enter the player's first name as it appears on their official identification documents.
* **Player Last Name** (`player_last_name`, text, required): Enter the player's last name as it appears on their official identification documents.
* **Player Email** (`player_email`, email, required): Enter the player's email address.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, required): Enter the player's birth date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Player Phone** (`player_phone`, text, optional): Enter the player's phone number, if available.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the team the player represents.
* **Player Position** (`player_position`, text, optional): Indicate the player's position in the team.
* **Player Height** (`player_height`, number, optional): Enter the player's height in centimeters.
* **Player Weight** (`player_weight`, number, optional): Enter the player's weight in kilograms.
* **Player Address** (`player_address`, text, optional): Enter the player's home address.
* **Coach Confirmation** (`coach_confirmation`, select_one, optional): Confirm the coach's confirmation regarding the player's eligibility. Please select one of the options: Coach1 or Coach2.
* **Team Manager Confirmation** (`team_manager_confirmation`, select_one, optional): Confirm the team manager's confirmation regarding the player's eligibility. Please select one of the options: Manager1 or Manager2.
* **Player Confirmation** (`player_confirmation`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm the player's own eligibility status. Please select one of the options: True or False.
* **National Team Eligibility** (`national_team_eligibility`, select_one, required): Indicate the player's national team eligibility status. Please select one of the options: Eligible or Not Eligible.

## Tips
* Ensure that the coach and team manager confirm the player's eligibility status.
* If you're unsure about any information, contact the national team administrator for clarification.
* Double-check the player's details before submitting the form to avoid errors.
