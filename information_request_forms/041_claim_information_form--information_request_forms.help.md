# Claim Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Claim Information Form is designed to collect essential details from requesters to process and fulfill their claims efficiently. It ensures that their needs are met and their claims are handled according to policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name in the "Requester name" field.
2. Provide the internal claim ID number if available.
3. Describe the documents or data needed in the "Information requested" field.
4. Explain the purpose of your request in the "Purpose of request" field.
5. If you have a preferred format for the information, select it from the dropdown menu in the "Preferred format for information" field.
6. Choose the confidentiality level required for your request from the dropdown menu in the "Confidentiality level required" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Contact email for delivery" field, so we can reach you for the information.
8. If you want to be contacted via phone, provide your phone number in the "Contact phone" field.
9. Select the date by which you need the information in the "Expected by date" field.
10. Finally, review and agree to the acknowledgement note at the bottom of the form, stating that your request will be processed according to policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester name (Required)**: Enter your full name to identify you as the requester.
* **Claim reference number (Optional)**: If you have a claim reference number, please provide it to help us identify your request.
* **Information requested (Required)**: Describe the documents or data needed to fulfill your claim.
* **Purpose of request (Required)**: Explain why you need the information.
* **Preferred format for information (Optional)**: Select the format you prefer to receive the information (PDF, CSV, Hard Copy, or Other).
* **Confidentiality level required (Optional)**: Choose the confidentiality level required for your request (Public, Internal, or Restricted).
* **Contact email for delivery (Required)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for the information.
* **Contact phone (Optional)**: Provide your phone number if you want to be contacted via phone.
* **Expected by date (Optional)**: Select the date by which you need the information.
* **Acknowledgement note (Required)**: Review and agree to the acknowledgement note, stating that your request will be processed according to policy.

## Tips
- Ensure you have provided all required fields (Requester name, Information requested, Purpose of request, Contact email for delivery, and Acknowledgement note).
- If you have any further questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
- You will receive an email or call from us once the information is ready for delivery, according to your requested format and confidentiality level.
