# Institution Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Institution Verification Form is a document that verifies the details of a student. It collects information about the student's name, date of birth, email, phone number, and other contact details. The form helps to ensure that the information provided by the student is accurate and up-to-date, and it serves as a reference for institutional purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student's first name in the `student_first_name` field.
2. Enter your student's last name in the `student_last_name` field.
3. Provide your date of birth in the `date_of_birth` field. Use the date format `mm/dd/yyyy` or `mm/dd/yy`.
4. Enter your student's email address in the `student_email` field. Make sure to enter a valid email address.
5. Enter your student's phone number in the `student_phone` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student First Name** (`student_first_name`, text, not required): Enter your student's first name as it appears on their official documents.
* **Student Last Name** (`student_last_name`, text, not required): Enter your student's last name as it appears on their official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, not required): Enter your student's date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy` or `mm/dd/yy`.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, not required): Enter a valid email address for your student.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, not required): Enter your student's phone number, including any necessary country codes.
