# Product Suggestion Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your feedback and suggestions for new or improved products. Your input helps us understand your needs and preferences, enabling us to develop and improve our products accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide a comprehensive suggestion, please follow these steps:

1.  Choose the type of product you're interested in suggesting (e.g., phone, laptop, etc.).
2.  Select your desired price range.
3.  Specify your preferred color options.
4.  Choose the display size(s) you are interested in.
5.  Select your preferred method(s) of payment (e.g., cash, credit).
6.  Provide any additional details about the product you have in mind (optional).
7.  Indicate if you would like to submit your suggestion.
8.  Enter your email address so we can get back to you.
9.  Enter your phone number in case we need to discuss your suggestion further.
10. Add any additional comments or thoughts about your product suggestion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Product Type** (`product_type`, select_one, **required**): Select the type of product you would like to suggest. This could be a phone, laptop, tablet, or any other product category.
- **Budget** (`budget`, number, **required**): Choose your desired price range for the product. This helps us understand your budget constraints.
- **Desired Price** (`desired_price`, number, **required**): Select the specific price you are looking for in the product.
- **Color** (`color`, select_multiple, **optional**): Choose the color(s) you would like the product to be. If no specific color is specified, we will consider all available options.
- **Display Size** (`display_size`, select_multiple, **required**): Select the display size(s) you prefer for the product.
- **Form of Payment** (`form_of_payment`, select_multiple, **required**): Choose the method of payment that best suits you (e.g., cash, credit).
- **Other Details** (`other_details`, note, **optional**): If you have additional thoughts or comments about your product suggestion, please write them here.
- **Submit Request** (`submit_request`, select_one, **required**): Indicate whether you would like to submit your suggestion for consideration.
- **Email** (`email`, email, **required**): Enter your email address so we can reply to your suggestion.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, **required**): Enter your phone number in case we need to discuss your suggestion further.
- **User Message** (`user_message`, note, **required**): Provide any additional comments or thoughts about your product suggestion.
