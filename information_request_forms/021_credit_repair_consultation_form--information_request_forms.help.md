# credit_repair_consultation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for credit repair consultations. Please provide detailed information about yourself and your financial situation to help identify potential areas for credit repair.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your client details (page 1)
2. Share your financial history (page 2)
3. Describe your financial problems (page 3)
4. Suggest debt solutions (page 4)
5. Summarize your current account (page 5)
6. Upload your credit report (page 6)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details (Client Details, text, required)**: Please provide your name, address, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Financial History (Financial History, text, required)**: Describe your past financial transactions, including loans, debts, and any other relevant financial events.
* **Financial Problems (Financial Problems, text, required)**: Explain any financial issues you are experiencing, such as late payments, high interest rates, or collection accounts.
* **Debt Solutions (Debt Solutions, text, required)**: Suggest any debt consolidation or other solutions you would like to consider.
* **Account Summary (Account Summary, text, required)**: Summarize your current financial account situation, including balances, interest rates, and payment history.
* **Credit Report (Credit Report, text, required)**: Upload your credit report or provide a link to it.

## Tips
- Please provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure effective credit repair.
- If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask your credit repair consultant for guidance.
- Keep your responses concise and clear, as this will help your credit repair consultant better understand your situation and provide more effective assistance.
