# Community Support Service Consultation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Support Service Consultation form is a tool for individuals or organizations seeking assistance with specific concerns. It is used to gather necessary information to provide tailored support.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, ensuring all required fields are completed.
- Select the correct options for each field, such as the type of support needed and the urgency of the request.
- If a field is not applicable, please indicate the reason for skipping it (e.g., not required for the specific request).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester full name** (`requester_name`, text, required): Enter your full name, including your title (if applicable).
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so that the support team can contact you for follow-up and scheduling.
* **Service requested** (`service_requested`, select_one, required): Choose the type of support you require from the available options.
* **Brief description of needs** (`brief_description`, text, required): Provide a concise description of the issue or concern you are facing.
* **Preferred contact time** (`preferred_contact_time`, text, optional): Suggest a preferred day and time for the support team to contact you.
* **Urgency of request** (`urgency`, select_one, required): Select the level of urgency for the support team to respond.
* **Consent to share information with partners** (`consent_info_sharing`, select_one, optional): Choose whether to share information with partners for coordinated support.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when describing your needs to ensure the support team understands your concern.
- If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to contact the support team for clarification.
- Please review your submission carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
