# Academic Resource Sharing Agreement Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Resource Sharing Agreement Request form is designed to gather information about a requesting institution's needs and intentions for accessing various academic resources. This form ensures that the requesting institution has the necessary details and agrees to the terms of use for the resource being requested.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  **Section 1: Requestor Details**
    - Fill in your institution's name, contact person, and contact email address.
    - Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
2.  **Section 2: Resource Details**
    - Select the primary category of the resource you are requesting (e.g., Laboratory Equipment, Datasets, etc.).
    - Provide a detailed description of the specific resource needed (e.g., serial numbers or dataset names).
    - Specify why you need the resource (e.g., Non-commercial Research, Educational or Instructional, etc.).
3.  **Section 3: Compliance and Security**
    - Acknowledge your institution's adherence to our data privacy policies.
    - Confirm that you acknowledge our institution's intellectual property rights over the resource.
4.  **Section 4: Authorization**
    - Verify that your institution has liability insurance for equipment use.
5.  **Signature**
    - Obtain the signature of an authorizing official from your institution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Name of Requesting Institution** (Required): The name of your institution that is requesting access to the academic resource.
*   **Primary Contact Person** (Required): The full name of the person responsible for the request.
*   **Contact Email Address** (Required): A valid email address that we can use to contact you.
*   **Category of Resource Requested** (Required): Select the primary type of resource you are requesting (e.g., Laboratory Equipment, Datasets, etc.).
*   **Specific description of the resource needed** (Required): Provide detailed information about the resource you are requesting (e.g., serial numbers or dataset names).
*   **Intended use of the resource** (Required): Select why you need the resource (e.g., Non-commercial Research, Educational or Instructional, etc.).
*   **Requested Start Date** (Required): The date when you would like access to the resource to begin.
*   **Requested End Date** (Required): The date when access to the resource should terminate.
*   **Will you adhere to our institutional data privacy policies?** (Required): Select "Yes - We Agree" to confirm your institution's adherence.
*   **Do you acknowledge our IP rights over the resource?** (Required): Confirm that you acknowledge our institution's intellectual property rights.
*   **Does your institution have liability insurance for equipment use?** (Required): Select "True" to confirm.
*   **Signature of Authorizing Official** (Required): Obtain the signature of an authorizing official from your institution.
