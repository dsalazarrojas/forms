# Customer And Contract Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect relevant details about a customer and their contract. It will help us update our records with accurate and up-to-date information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as instructed.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Double-check your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the full name of the customer as per your records.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the customer's primary contact person.
- **Contract Type** (`contract_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of contract the customer has with us (Domestic or International).
- **Client Address** (`client_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the customer's location.
- **City** (`city`, text, required/optional): Enter the city where the customer is located.
- **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the primary phone number of the customer.
- **Postal Code** (`client_postal_code`, text, required/optional): Enter the postal/zip code of the customer's location.
- **Country** (`client_country`, text, required/optional): Select the country where the customer is located.
- **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required/optional): Enter the employee ID of the customer's primary contact person.
- **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the customer's company.
- **Contract Start Date** (`contract_start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of the contract.
- **Contract End Date** (`contract_end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the end date of the contract.

## Tips
- Be accurate and precise with your entries to ensure correct records are kept.
- If unsure about any field, please consult with our team before proceeding.
- Double-check your form entries before submitting to avoid any errors.
