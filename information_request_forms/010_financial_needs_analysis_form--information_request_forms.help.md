# Financial Needs Analysis Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients to create a comprehensive financial plan tailored to their specific needs. It helps financial professionals understand a client's financial situation, risk tolerance, and goals to make informed investment recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer each question truthfully and in detail.
3. Provide as much information as requested for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name to help the financial professional identify you.
* **Client Contact Info** (`client_contact_info`, `text`, required): Please provide your contact information so the financial professional can reach out to you with any additional questions.
* **Income** (`income`, `number`, required): Enter your annual income to help the financial professional understand your financial capacity.
* **Expenses** (`expenses`, `number`, required): Report your monthly expenses to help the financial professional understand your financial obligations.
* **Financial Status** (`financial_status`, `select_one`, required): Please select your current financial status: Active, Passive, or Retired.
* **Financial Goals** (`financial_goals`, `text`, required): Describe your short-term and long-term financial goals, such as saving for a down payment on a house or funding your children's education.
* **Risk Tolerance** (`risk_tolerance`, `select_one`, required): Select your risk tolerance: Conservative, Moderate, or Aggressive.
* **Investment Experience** (`investment_experience`, `text`, required): Share your experience with investments, including any relevant certifications or training.
* **Financial Needs** (`financial_needs`, `text`, required): Specify what you're looking for in a financial plan, such as retirement planning or investment growth.
* **Income Source** (`income_source`, `text`, required): Describe your income source, such as employment or self-employment.
* **Investment Assets** (`investment_assets`, `text`, required): List any existing investments or assets you have, such as stocks, bonds, or real estate.
* **Financial Institutions** (`financial_institutions`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all financial institutions you've worked with, such as banks or brokers.
* **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose your risk level: Low, Medium, or High.
* **Financial Institutions Description** (`financial_institutions_description`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information about your experience with financial institutions.
* **Investment Assets Description** (`investment_assets_description`, `text`, optional): Explain any relevant details about your existing investments or assets.
* **Financial Goals** (`financial_goals`, `text`, required): Repeat this field from above, but this is a duplicate field. Please provide the same information here.
* **Investment Experience** (`investment_experience`, `text`, required): Repeat this field from above, but this is a duplicate field. Please provide the same information here.

Please note that this form is designed to gather information from clients for financial professionals. If you're an individual looking to create a general investment plan or seeking personal investment advice, please consider speaking with a financial advisor who can provide personalized guidance.
