# Project Charter Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a project from the project team. It should be filled out by the team lead or project manager to provide a brief overview of the project's goals, target outcomes, and any financial or risk considerations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide a brief description of your project, including its goals and objectives.
2. Enter any relevant financial details about the project.
3. Identify the key performance indicators (KPIs) you want to achieve during the project.
4. Estimate a budget for the project.
5. List any critical project milestones or deadlines.
6. Identify any potential risks or challenges that may impact the project.
7. Provide any additional information that may be helpful in understanding the project's impact and scope.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Project Information (1)
* **Project Information**: Enter a brief description of your project, including its goals and objectives.

### Financial Details (2)
* **Project financial details**: Enter any relevant financial information about the project, including estimated costs and any funding sources.

### Project Target KPI (3)
* **Project target KPI**: Enter the key performance indicators (KPIs) you want to achieve during the project. For example, "Increase revenue by 10%" or "Reduce time-to-market by 20%".

### Budget (4)
* **Budget**: Estimate a budget for the project. This will help us plan resources and funding.

### Project Milestones (5)
* **Project milestones**: List any critical project milestones or deadlines, such as "Complete project planning phase by June 1st" or "Launch product by November 15th".

### Project Risks (6)
* **Project risks**: Identify any potential risks or challenges that may impact the project. For example, "Delays in supplier delivery" or "Changes in market trends".

### Impact Details (7)
* **Impact details**: Provide any additional information that may be helpful in understanding the project's impact and scope. For example, "The project will impact 100 users" or "The project will have a direct impact on revenue".

### Project Name (8)
* **Project name**: Enter a brief name for your project. This will help us track and reference the project.
