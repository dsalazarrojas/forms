# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive business information request form used to collect detailed data about a business. It is used to gather information about a business's contact details, products or services, and other relevant data. This form is typically used by business development teams or marketing departments to get in touch with businesses for potential partnerships or collaborations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your business name and contact details in the "Client Name", "Business Name", "Contact Phone", "Contact Email", and "Contact Person" fields.
2. Provide your business website and social media handle in the "Business Website" and "Social Media" fields.
3. Specify your primary product or service in the "Primary Product" field.
4. Describe your product or service in the "Product Description" field.
5. Fill in your target market in the "Target Market" field.
6. If applicable, specify your business type and industry in the "Business Type" and "Industry" fields.
7. Provide your employee count and annual income in the "Employee Count" and "Annual Income" fields.
8. Fill in your address in the "Address 1" and "Address 2" fields.
9. Specify your city and state in the "City" and "State" fields.
10. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.
11. Fill in your years in business and company name if desired in the "Years in Business" and "Company Name" fields.
12. Fill in any additional information you'd like to provide in the "Address 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your business.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your business.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, text, optional): This field is not typically used, as it's not clear what it's intended for. Please ignore it.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, number, required): Enter your business contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your business contact email address.
* **Address 1** (`address_1`, text, optional): Enter your business's physical address. This field is not required.
* **Address 2** (`address_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional address information. This field is not required.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where your business is located.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state where your business is located.
* **Zip** (`zip`, number, required): Enter your business's zip code.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of business you have (e.g., corporation, LLC, partnership, etc.).
* **Industry** (`industry`, text, optional): Enter your business's industry.
* **Employee Count** (`employee_count`, number, required): Enter the number of employees in your business.
* **Annual Income** (`annual_income`, number, required): Enter your business's annual income.
* **Business Website** (`business_website`, text, required): Enter your business website URL.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, text, optional): Enter your social media handle.
* **Primary Product** (`primary_product`, text, required): Enter the primary product or service your business offers.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, optional): Describe your product or service.
* **Product/Service** (`product`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your product or service.
* **Target Market** (`target_market`, text, required): Enter the target market for your product or service.
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`, number, optional): Enter the number of years your business has been in operation.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your company (if different from "Business Name").
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, optional): Enter the website of your company (if different from "Business Website").
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the person to contact for your business.
* **Address 2** (`address_2`, text, optional): Fill in any additional address information.
* **Address 1** (`address_1`, text, optional): Enter your business's physical address.
