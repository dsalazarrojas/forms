# Property And Tenant Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about properties and tenants, including their addresses, employment histories, criminal backgrounds, and rental agreements. It will help property owners and managers to gather necessary details for tenant screening and property management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for each field as requested.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review and submit the form once you have filled in all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Address** (`property_address`, select_multiple, optional): Select the property address from the provided options.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of property (e.g. 'Yes' for yes, 'No' for no).
* **Employment History** (`employment_history`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the tenant's employment history.
* **Criminal Background** (`criminal_background`, text, optional): Provide any information about the tenant's criminal background (e.g. convictions, arrests).
* **Personal References** (`personal_references`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether you have personal references (e.g. 'Yes' for yes, 'No' for no).
* **Employment References** (`employment_references`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether you have employment references (e.g. 'Yes' for yes, 'No' for no).
* **Criminal References** (`criminal_references`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether you have criminal references (e.g. 'Yes' for yes, 'No' for no).
* **Rental Agreement** (`rental_agreement`, text, optional): Provide a copy of the rental agreement or a description of its terms.
* **Rental Agreement Term** (`rental_agreement_term`, number, optional): Enter the term of the rental agreement in months.
* **Property Owner** (`property_owner`, text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of the property owner.
* **Tenant Contact** (`tenant_contact`, text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of the tenant.
* **Property Details** (`property_details`, text, optional): Describe the property, including its condition and amenities.
* **Property Status** (`property_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of the property (e.g. 'Active' for active, 'Inactive' for inactive).
* **Rental Agreement Start Date** (`rental_agreement_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the rental agreement.
* **Rental Agreement End Date** (`rental_agreement_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the rental agreement.
* **Rent** (`rent`, number, optional): Enter the monthly rent amount.
* **Deposit** (`deposit`, number, optional): Enter the deposit amount.
* **Move In Date** (`move_in_date`, date, optional): Enter the move-in date of the tenant.
* **Lease Length** (`lease_length`, number, optional): Enter the length of the lease term in months.
* **Lease Start Date** (`lease_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the lease term.
* **Lease End Date** (`lease_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the lease term.
* **Lease Term** (`lease_term`, select_one, optional): Choose whether there is a lease term (e.g. 'Yes' for yes, 'No' for no).
* **Property Type Options** (`property_type_options`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the property type.
* **Employment History Options** (`employment_history_options`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the employment history.
* **Criminal Background Options** (`criminal_background_options`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the criminal background.
