# Per Diem Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Per Diem Request Form is used to request per diem, a daily allowance for travel expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your travel dates in the format MM/DD/YY to MM/DD/YY.
2. List the destinations you plan to visit in the format "Destination 1", "Destination 2", etc.
3. Enter a brief description of the purpose of your travel.
4. Enter the start and end dates of your trip in the format MM/DD/YY to MM/DD/YY.
5. Enter the location of your travel.
6. Enter the number of nights you will be traveling.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **travel_dates** (`travel_dates`, `date`, not required): Enter the dates of your travel in the format MM/DD/YY to MM/DD/YY.
- **destinations** (`destinations`, `text`, not required): List the locations you plan to visit.
- **purpose** (`purpose`, `text`, not required): Enter a brief description of the purpose of your travel.
- **start_date** (`start_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the start date of your travel in the format MM/DD/YY.
- **end_date** (`end_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the end date of your travel in the format MM/DD/YY.
- **travel_location** (`travel_location`, `text`, not required): Enter the location of your travel.
- **number_of_nights** (`number_of_nights`, `number`, not required): Enter the number of nights you will be traveling.
