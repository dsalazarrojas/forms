# Contractor Expense Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help contractors report their expenses related to a specific project. It ensures that all necessary information is collected accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details:
	* **Contractor Full Name**: Enter your legal name as it appears on your official documents.
	* **Contractor ID**: Enter your contractor identification number.
	* **Email Address**: Enter your primary email address for reimbursement.
	* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact number for any queries.
2. Select the project details:
	* **Period Start Date**: Enter the start date of the expense period.
	* **Period End Date**: Enter the end date of the expense period.
	* **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project these expenses relate to.
	* **Client Name**: (Optional) Enter the client name for whom the project is being done.
3. Report your expenses:
	* **Expense 1 Category**: Choose the category for your first expense from the list (e.g., Travel, Meals, etc.).
	* **Expense 1 Description**: Provide a brief description of the expense.
	* **Expense 1 Date**: Enter the date the expense was incurred.
	* **Expense 1 Amount**: Enter the cost of the expense.
	* You can add up to three more expenses using the same fields.
4. Calculate your total expenses:
	* **Total Expenses Claimed**: This will be automatically calculated based on your expense entries.
5. Select your payment information:
	* **Preferred Payment Method**: Choose how you would like to receive reimbursement (e.g., Direct Deposit, Check, etc.).
	* **Bank Name**: (Optional) Enter the name of your bank for direct deposit.
	* **Account Number**: (Optional) Enter your bank account number.
	* **Routing Number**: (Optional) Enter your bank routing number.
6. Confirm your submissions:
	* **I declare these expenses are accurate and business-related**: Confirm that the expenses reported are accurate and related to your business activities.
