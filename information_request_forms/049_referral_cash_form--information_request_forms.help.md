# Referral Cash Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Referral Cash Form is designed to be filled out by customers or users to refer new customers to your business. This form helps identify potential new customers and offers a reward to the referrer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the customer you wish to refer.
2. Select a reward amount from the options provided.
3. Enter your own name and any additional comments about the referral.
4. Enter the customer's name and contact information.
5. Choose whether to send an email to the customer.
6. If sending an email, write the subject and body of the email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Customer Referral
* **Referral Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the customer you are referring.

### 2. Reward Amount
* **Select a Reward** (select one, optional): Choose a reward amount to offer to the customer.
  - $10
  - $20
  - $50
  - $100

### 3. Your Referral Name
* **Your Referral Name** (text, optional): Enter your name, as the referrer.

### 4. Customer Information
* **Customer's Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the customer you are referring.
* **Customer's Phone** (text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer's Email** (email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.

### 5. Communication
* **Send Email** (select one, optional): Choose whether to send an email to the customer.
  - 'True'
  - 'False'
* **Sent Email** (select one, optional): If sending an email, indicate if it was sent or not.
  - 'Sent'
  - 'Not Sent'
* **Email Subject** (text, optional): Write the subject of the email to be sent to the customer.
* **Email Body** (text, optional): Write the body of the email to be sent to the customer.

### 6. Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (note, optional): Enter any additional comments about the referral.
