# Local Experience Coordination Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Local Experience Coordination Contact Form is used to collect information from customers or users who want to get in touch with our local experience coordination team. This form is meant to be used for people looking for assistance or guidance on local experiences, such as planning a trip, getting recommendations, or asking questions about a specific location.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Type your message in the "message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Local Experience Coordination Contact Form** (`form_title`, text, required: false): Enter a meaningful title for your message, if you'd like. This is optional.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name to help us get in touch with you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Provide your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by phone.
* **Message** (`message`, text, required: false): Type your question or message in this field. This is where you can provide as much detail as you need to get the help you're looking for. 

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible with your message, so we can better understand and assist you.
* If you have any attachments or supporting documents, consider including them with your message.
* Our team will be in touch with you within 24-48 hours of receiving your form submission. If you don't hear from us, feel free to follow up with a new message or call us. 

Please note that all fields are optional, but providing your name and email address will help us get in touch with you more efficiently.
