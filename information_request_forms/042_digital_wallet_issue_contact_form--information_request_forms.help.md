# digital-wallet-issue-contact-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help support teams gather information from users experiencing issues with digital wallets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose your preferred contact method for our team to reach out to you.
2. Describe the issue you are experiencing with your digital wallet.
3. Select the category that best describes the problem you are experiencing (e.g., Digital Wallets, Account Management, etc.).
4. If you have any additional information or context that may help us resolve the issue, please provide it in the text box.
5. If you have taken screenshots of the issue, please attach them to the form.
6. If you have information about your operating system or browser version, please include it in the text boxes provided.
7. If you have any other information that may be relevant, please include it in the text box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact**: Choose your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, or Live Chat).
	+ This will determine how the support team will contact you to discuss your issue.
* **Issue Description**: Describe the issue you are experiencing with your digital wallet.
	+ Please include as much detail as possible so that we can understand the problem.
* **Category**: Select the category that best describes the problem you are experiencing (e.g., Digital Wallets, Account Management, etc.).
	+ This will help us understand what area of the digital wallet you are experiencing issues with.
* **Operating System**: Please include information about your operating system and its version.
	+ This may help us narrow down the cause of the issue.
* **Browser Version**: Please include information about your browser version.
	+ This may help us narrow down the cause of the issue.
* **Issue Resolution**: If you have already found a solution or a workaround, please describe it here.
	+ This can save us time and help us understand your thought process.
* **Preferred Language**: Choose your preferred language for communication.
	+ This will help us communicate with you effectively.
* **Preferred Locale**: Choose your preferred locale (e.g., United States, Canada, Mexico).
	+ This will help us tailor our communication to your region.
* **Screenshots**: If you have taken screenshots of the issue, please attach them to the form.
	+ Screenshots can be helpful for us to understand the issue.
* **Additional Info**: If you have any additional information or context that may help us resolve the issue, please provide it here.
	+ This is an optional field for any extra details you want to share.
* **File for Screenshots**: Attach any relevant screenshots or files to this field.
	+ Please make sure the file is relevant to the issue you are experiencing.
* **Issue Resolution Note**: If you have already found a resolution to the issue, please describe it here.
	+ This can help us understand your thought process and may save us time.
* **Operating System Version**: Please include information about your operating system version.
	+ This may help us narrow down the cause of the issue.
* **Screen Resolution**: Please include information about your screen resolution.
	+ This may help us understand how the issue is affecting your user interface.

## Tips

* Please fill out as much information as possible to help us understand the issue.
* Make sure to attach relevant screenshots or files to help us understand the issue.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please let us know and we will do our best to assist you.
* If you have any other information that may be relevant, please include it in the text box.
