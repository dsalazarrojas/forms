# Dog Bio Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dog Bio Update Form is used to collect and update the bio and other relevant information of a dog. This form allows you to provide updates on the dog's behavior, energy level, and personality, as well as any veterinary and medical information, owner details, and additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the dog's information on the "dog_info" page.
2. Provide an update on the dog's behavior by selecting the corresponding option from the "behavior" field.
3. Update the dog's energy level by selecting the corresponding option from the "energy_level" field.
4. Add any additional details about the dog's personality in the "personality" field.
5. Provide any relevant training history in the "training" field.
6. Enter veterinary and medical information in the "veterinary_info" and "medical_info" fields, respectively.
7. Enter owner information, including the owner's name, phone number, and email address in the "owner_info" field.
8. Provide any additional contact information in the "contact_info" field.
9. Add any additional notes or comments about the dog's progress in the "additional_notes" field.
10. Upload any relevant images of the dog, including a photo and a video, in the "dog_image" and "owner_image" fields, respectively.
11. Select the relevant video for the dog from the available options in the "dog_video" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **dog_info**: Enter a brief description of the dog.
* **behavior**: Select how the dog behaves most of the time (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
* **energy_level**: Select the dog's energy level (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
* **personality**: Enter any additional details about the dog's personality.
* **training**: Enter any relevant training history or information about the dog's training.
* **veterinary_info**: Enter any veterinary visits or medical information about the dog.
* **medical_info**: Enter any medical information about the dog.
* **owner_info**: Enter the owner's name, phone number, and email address.
* **contact_info**: Enter any additional contact information.
* **additional_notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the dog.
* **dog_image**: Upload a photo of the dog.
* **owner_image**: Upload an image of the owner.
* **dog_video**: Select a video for the dog from the available options.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions and authorization to access this form.
* If you need to update multiple dogs, please submit each update individually to avoid duplication.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities or administrators.
