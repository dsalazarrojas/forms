# makeup_pre_procedure_questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form, titled "Makeup Pre Procedure Questionnaire," is designed to gather information from clients regarding their preferences and requests for makeup procedures. It is used to understand the client's wishes, procedure details, and other requests before commencing the makeup process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Review the form's layout and fields.
2. Enter your client's preferences and wishes in the "Client Wishes" field.
3. Specify the desired makeup procedure in the "Procedure Wishes" field.
4. Share any additional requests or comments in the "Other Requests" field.
5. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
6. Select your preferred makeup procedure from the options provided in the "Preferred Makeup Procedure" field.
7. Enter any additional details regarding the makeup procedure in the "Makeup Procedure Details" field.
8. Answer any follow-up questions in the "Follow Up Questions" field.
9. Sign the form with your client's signature in the "Client Signature" field.
10. Add any comments or remarks in the "Client Comments" field.
11. Leave any comments or notes for the staff in the "Staff Comments" field.
12. Select the tool assigned to the client in the "Assigned Tool" field.
13. Enter the name of the person who created the form in the "Created By" field.
14. Enter the name of the person who updated the form in the "Updated By" field.
15. Enter the date and time the form was created in the "Date Created" and "Time Created" fields.
16. Enter the date and time the form was last updated in the "Date Updated" and "Time Updated" fields.
17. Enter the form's ID and version number in the "Form ID" and "Form Version" fields.
18. Select the category for the form in the "Category" field.
19. Enter a brief description of the form in the "Description" field.
20. Select the status of the form in the "Form Status" field.
21. Select the output file associated with the form in the "Output File" field.
