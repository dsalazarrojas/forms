# New Account Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Account Information Form is designed to collect personal details and contact information from individuals who want to create a new account or update their existing one.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Type in your Company information.
4. Enter your Email address.
5. Enter your Phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your personal first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your personal last name.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Type in your company name or name of your organization.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address associated with your account.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
