# Smart HVAC Solution Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers or users to contact Smart HVAC Solution for inquiries, questions, or requests for service. The goal is to gather relevant information from customers to provide them with the best possible solution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **Email** field, following the standard format of `username@domain.com`.
4. Describe your service inquiry in the **Service Inquiry** field.
5. Add your phone number in the **Phone** field, if you would like to be contacted directly.
6. Enter your address in the **Address** field, if you would like to be contacted in person or for on-site services.
7. Type any additional message or comments in the **Message** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your first name is used to identify you as a customer.
* **Last Name**: Your last name is used to identify you as a customer.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to allow us to contact you regarding your inquiry or request.
* **Service Inquiry**: Describe the specific service you are inquiring about, such as a repair, maintenance, or new installation.
* **Phone**: If you would like to be contacted directly, enter your phone number here.
* **Address**: If you would like to be contacted in person or for on-site services, enter your address here.
* **Message**: Provide any additional information or comments about your inquiry or request.
