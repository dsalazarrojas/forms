# Government Benefit Change Of Address Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to notify changes to your address, which can impact your government benefits. Please fill out this form to update your address with the relevant government agencies or organizations providing your benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and ensure you are filling out the correct information.
2. Fill out the required fields (if any).
3. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **address_change_form_1_label** (Address Change 1, text, required): Enter your current address in this field.
* **address_change_form_2_label** (Address Change 2, text, required): Enter your new address in this field.
* **address_change_form_3_label** (Address Change 3, text, required): Enter any additional information about your address change in this field.
* **address_change_form_4_label** (Address Change 4, text, required): Enter your new address again to confirm.
* **address_change_form_5_label** (Address Change 5, text, required): Enter any other relevant information about your address change in this field.
* **address_change_form_6_label** (Address Change 6, text, required): Enter your reason for address change (e.g., moving, change of household, etc.).
* **submit_and_share** (Submit and Share, text, required): Fill out this field with any additional comments or requests for your address change (e.g., share with other departments).
* **Category** (Category, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" if you are updating your address for government benefits, otherwise, check "No".
* **category_option_1** (Category Option 1, select_multiple, optional): Select any other relevant categories related to your address change (e.g., "option 1", "option 2", etc.).
* **form_id** (Form ID, number, optional): Enter your reference number for this form, if provided.
* **assigned_tool** (Assigned Tool, text, optional): This field is intended to be used for internal purposes, please leave blank unless instructed otherwise.
