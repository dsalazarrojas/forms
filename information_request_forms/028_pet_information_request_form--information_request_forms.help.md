# pet_information_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and store information about your pet's identity, health status, and vaccination history. This information will help our team provide the best possible care for your pet.

## How To Complete This Form
Please fill out the form carefully, providing accurate and complete information about your pet. Each section is designed to help us understand your pet's unique needs and requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Name** (1): Enter your pet's name as it appears on their identification tag or vaccination records.
* **Pet Type** (2): Select the type of pet you're registering (e.g., Cat, Dog, Other).
* **Owner Name** (3): Enter your name, so we can verify your identity.
* **Owner Email** (4): Enter your email address, which we'll use to communicate with you.
* **Owner Phone** (5): Enter your phone number, so we can contact you in case of an emergency or question.
* **Pet Breed** (6): Enter your pet's breed (e.g., Labrador, Persian, Mixed).
* **Pet Age** (7): Enter your pet's age.
* **Pet Gender** (8): Select your pet's gender (Male, Female, Other).
* **Pet Weight** (9): Enter your pet's weight (in pounds or kilograms).
* **Pet Health Conditions** (10): Enter any health conditions or issues your pet may be experiencing.
* **Veterinarian Name** (11): Enter the name of your pet's veterinarian.
* **Veterinarian Phone** (12): Enter your veterinarian's phone number.
* **Veterinarian Email** (13): Enter your veterinarian's email address.
* **Pet Diet** (14): Enter your pet's diet (e.g., kibble, raw, grain-free).
* **Pet Allergies** (15): Enter any allergies or sensitivities your pet may have.
* **Pet Medical History** (16): Enter any medical history or previous injuries your pet has experienced.
* **Pet Medication** (17): Enter any medication your pet is currently taking.
* **Pet Vaccine History** (18): Enter your pet's vaccination history.
* **Pet Microchip Info** (19): Enter any microchip information, such as the serial number or activation status.
* **Pet Microchip Number** (20): Enter your pet's microchip number.
* **Pet Rabies Vaccination** (21): Enter the status of your pet's rabies vaccination.
* **Pet Microchip Activated** (22): Select whether your pet's microchip is activated or not.
* **Pet Microchip Lost or Stolen** (23): Select whether your pet's microchip has been lost or stolen.
* **Pet Microchip Serial Number** (24): Enter your pet's microchip serial number.
* **Pet Microchip Color** (25): Enter the color of your pet's microchip.
