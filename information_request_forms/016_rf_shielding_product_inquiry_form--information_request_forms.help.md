# RF Shielding Product Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The RF Shielding Product Inquiry Form is designed to gather information about the user's product inquiry needs. This includes specifying product categories, descriptions, and desired frequencies, as well as contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill in all required fields** to ensure your inquiry is processed accurately and efficiently.
2. **Choose the correct product category** from the provided options to help our team understand your product requirements.
3. **Provide a clear product description** to help our team understand your product needs.
4. **Enter your desired frequency** if you have a specific frequency in mind.
5. **Add any additional application notes** if necessary.
6. **Enter your contact information** so we can follow up with you.
7. **Choose the correct follow-up option** to inform us of your preferred follow-up method.
8. **Select the correct assigned engineer** if you would like to be assigned to a specific engineer.
9. **Enter any additional follow-up messages** if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **User Email (1)** 
User Email is the email address where we will contact you to discuss your product inquiry.

### **Product Category (2)** 
Select the category that best describes the product you are inquiring about.

### **Product Description (3)** 
Enter a detailed description of the product you are inquiring about.

### **Product Name (4)** 
Enter the name of the product you are inquiring about.

### **RF Type (5)** 
Select the type of RF (Radio Frequency) you are inquiring about.

### **Shielding Application (6)** 
Select the application for the shielding product you are inquiring about.

### **Frequency (7)** 
Enter the frequency of the product you are inquiring about.

### **Desired Frequency (8)** 
Optional: Enter a desired frequency if you have one.

### **Application Note (9)** 
Optional: Provide any additional application notes about your product.

### **Contact Name (10)** 
Enter the name of the person inquiring about the product.

### **Contact Phone (11)** 
Optional: Enter the phone number of the person inquiring about the product.

### **Contact Email (12)** 
Optional: Enter the email address of the person inquiring about the product.

### **Company (13)** 
Optional: Enter the name of the company inquiring about the product.

### **Department (14)** 
Optional: Enter the department of the person inquiring about the product.

### **Address (15)** 
Optional: Enter the address of the person inquiring about the product.

### **City (16)** 
Optional: Enter the city of the person inquiring about the product.

### **State (17)** 
Select the state of the person inquiring about the product.

### **Zip (18)** 
Optional: Enter the zip code of the person inquiring about the product.

### **Product Inquiry (19)** 
Select the type of product inquiry you are making.

### **Product Inquiry Message (20)** 
Optional: Enter a message about your product inquiry.

### **Product Inquiry Attachment (21)** 
Optional: Attach any relevant documents about your product inquiry.

### **Followup (22)** 
Select how you would like to be followed up on.

### **Assigned Engineer (23)** 
Optional: Select the engineer you would like to be assigned to your inquiry.

### **Assigned Engineer Email (24)** 
Optional: Enter the email address of the engineer you would like to be assigned.

### **Followup Message (25)** 
Optional: Enter any additional follow-up messages about your product inquiry.

## Tips

* Please ensure you fill out all required fields accurately to ensure efficient processing of your inquiry.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* Our team will follow up with you according to your selected follow-up method.
