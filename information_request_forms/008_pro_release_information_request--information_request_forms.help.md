# Pro Release Information Request - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Pro Release Information Request form is a structured request for information related to a Pro Release. It helps gather essential details from users, ensuring accurate and timely responses. This form is used to collect information from various stakeholders, facilitating effective tracking and management of Pro Releases.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, carefully read through each section to understand what information is needed.
2.  Enter a brief description of your request in the "Information Request" section.
3.  Clearly state the purpose of your request in the "Purpose" section.
4.  Select the urgency level for the release from the "Urgency" dropdown options (High, Medium, Low).
5.  Specify whether supporting documents are required for your request.
6.  Choose the current review status of your request.
7.  Enter the expected response date and time for your request.
8.  Enter the date you made the initial request.
9.  Select who is responsible for reviewing and responding to your request.
10. Enter any additional notes or comments for the request.
11. Enter your email address and phone number for easy contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Information Request** (`information_request`, text, required): This is the main description of your request. Please enter a clear and concise statement of what information you're seeking.
*   **Purpose** (`purpose`, text, required): Describe the purpose of your request. This helps the recipient understand the context and reason behind your request.
*   **Urgency** (`urgency`, select_one, required): Select the urgency level of your request: High, Medium, or Low. This helps the reviewer prioritize your request based on its level of importance.
*   **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether supporting documents are needed for your request. This could be files, images, or other attachments.
*   **Review Status** (`review_status`, select_one, required): Choose the current status of your request: Not Reviewed, Under Review, or Reviewed.
*   **Response Date** (`response_date`, date, required): Enter the expected date for a response to your request.
*   **Response Time** (`response_time`, time, required): Enter the expected time for a response to your request.
*   **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required): Enter the date you made the initial request.
*   **Reviewers** (`reviewers`, select_multiple, required): Select individuals or teams responsible for reviewing and responding to your request.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes related to your request.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for easy contact.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for easy contact.

By following these instructions, you'll be able to effectively complete the Pro Release Information Request form, ensuring that you get the information you need in a timely manner.
