# College Announcement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The College Announcement Request Form is used to request that an event or activity be announced on the college's website and other channels. This form should be used for events or activities that are open to the entire college community or a specific group within it.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a request for a college announcement, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the form completely and accurately**. Make sure to enter your real name and email address.
2. **Provide a clear and concise event description**. This will help ensure that your event gets the right attention.
3. **Choose the correct event type**. This will help the college's communication team to categorize and promote your event effectively.
4. **Select the correct target audience**. This will help the college to reach the right people with your announcement.
5. **Choose the correct posting frequency**. This will help the college to determine how often to post your announcement.
6. **Upload any necessary files or graphics**. This will help the college to create eye-catching announcements for your event.
7. **Submit the form**. Once you've filled out the form, click the "Submit" button to send in your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Full Name** (`requester_name`, **Required**): Enter your full name as it should appear in the announcement.
* **Email Address** (`requester_email`, **Required**): Enter your email address, which will be used by the college to contact you about your event.
* **Phone Number** (`requester_phone`, **Optional**): Enter your phone number, which can be used for further communication about your event.
* **Department/Organization** (`department`, **Required**): Enter the department or organization that is hosting the event.
* **Your Role** (`role`, **Required**): Enter your position or title within the department or organization.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, **Required**): Enter the date you are submitting this request.
* **Event Title** (`event_title`, **Required**): Enter the title of the event or activity.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, **Required**): Choose the category that best fits your event or activity (e.g. Academic Lecture, Workshop, etc.).
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, **Required**): Enter a clear and concise description of the event or activity.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, **Required**): Enter the date of the event or activity.
* **Event Start Time** (`event_start_time`, **Required**): Enter the start time of the event or activity.
* **Event End Time** (`event_end_time`, **Required**): Enter the end time of the event or activity.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, **Required**): Enter the location of the event or activity.
* **Building and Room Number** (`building_room`, **Optional**): Enter the specific location of the event or activity (e.g. room number, building name, etc.).
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, **Required**): Choose who this event is open to (e.g. All students, undergraduate students, etc.).
* **Expected Attendance** (`expected_attendance`, **Required**): Choose the expected number of attendees.
* **Where Should Announcement Be Posted** (`announcement_channels`, **Required**): Choose where you would like the announcement to be posted (e.g. college website, student portal, etc.).
* **Proposed Announcement Text** (`announcement_text`, **Required**): Enter the text that you would like to appear in the announcement.
* **Do You Have a Flyer** (`flyer_attached`, **Required**): Choose if you have a flyer for the event.
* **Upload Flyer** (`flyer_upload`, **Optional**): If you have a flyer, upload it here.
* **Do You Have Social Media Graphics** (`social_media_graphics`, **Optional**): Choose if you have social media graphics for the event.
* **Is Registration Required** (`registration_required`, **Required**): Choose if attendees need to register for the event.
* **Registration Link** (`registration_link`, **Optional**): If registration is required, enter the URL for registration.
* **Event Cost** (`cost`, **Required**): Enter the admission fee for the event.
* **Event Contact Person** (`contact_person`, **Required**): Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the event.
* **Event Contact Email** (`contact_email`, **Required**): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the event.
* **Accessibility Accommodations** (`accessibility_needs`, **Optional**): Enter any accessibility accommodations provided for the event.
* **Will Refreshments Be Provided** (`refreshments_provided`, **Optional**): Choose if refreshments will be provided.
* **Sponsorship Information** (`sponsorship_info`, **Optional**): Enter any sponsorship information for the event.
* **When Should Announcement First Appear** (`first_request_date`, **Required**): Enter the start date of when the announcement should appear.
* **When Should Announcement Stop Appearing** (`last_request_date`, **Required**): Enter the end date of when the announcement should stop appearing.
* **How Often Should It Be Posted** (`frequency`, **Required**): Choose how often you would like the announcement to be posted.
* **Has This Event Been Announced Before** (`previous_announcements`, **Optional**): Choose if this event has been announced before.
* **Does This Need Special Approval** (`approval_needed`, **Required**): Choose if this event requires special approval.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, **Optional**): Enter any additional information about the event.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your event is legitimate and for the benefit of the college community.
* Please check that you haven't already submitted a request for this event before.
* Be as specific as possible when filling out the form to ensure that the college's communication team can effectively promote your event.
