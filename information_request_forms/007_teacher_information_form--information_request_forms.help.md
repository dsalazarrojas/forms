# Teacher Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from teachers for administrative purposes, including department and school details. It is essential to fill out this form to help us better understand and respond to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Please fill out each field carefully**, making sure to double-check for accuracy and completeness.
2. **Start with the first field: First Name**. Enter your first name as it is commonly known. This field is not required.
3. **Enter your Last Name**. Enter your last name as it is commonly known. This field is not required.
4. **Enter your Email**. Please enter your work email address. This field is not required.
5. **Choose your Department**. Select 'Yes' if you belong to a department. This field is not required.
6. **Select your Position**. Choose 'Yes' if you hold a position. This field is not required.
7. **Enter School Year**. Enter the current school year. This field is not required.
8. **Enter School Name**. Type in your school's name as it is known. This field is not required.
9. **Enter Department Location**. Enter the department location (e.g., office number). This field is not required.
10. **Enter Room Number**. Enter your classroom or department room number. This field is not required.
11. **Enter Department Phone**. Enter your department phone number. This field is not required.
11. **Enter Department Email**. Enter your department email address. This field is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, Optional): Enter your commonly known first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, Optional): Enter your commonly known last name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, Optional): Enter your work email address.
- **Department** (`department`, select_one, Optional): Select 'Yes' if you belong to a department.
- **Position** (`position`, select_one, Optional): Select 'Yes' if you hold a position.
- **School Year** (`school_year`, text, Optional): Enter the current school year.
- **School Name** (`school_name`, text, Optional): Type in your school's name as it is known.
- **Department Location** (`department_location`, text, Optional): Enter the department location (e.g., office number).
- **Room Number** (`room_number`, text, Optional): Enter your classroom or department room number.
- **Department Phone** (`department_phone`, text, Optional): Enter your department phone number.
- **Department Email** (`department_email`, email, Optional): Enter your department email address.

## Tips
* Double-check for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the administrator.
* Please note that some fields are optional; however, providing all requested information will help us better understand and serve you efficiently.
