# Proof Of Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide proof of delivery for your products. It's essential to complete it accurately to facilitate efficient and secure delivery processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your information in the fields below.
2. Fill in your delivery date in the format `MM/DD/YYYY` (e.g., 02/01/2022).
3. Enter the time of delivery in the format `HH:MM AM/PM` (e.g., 08:00 AM).
4. Choose the location where the delivery was made from the provided options.
5. Enter the name of the person receiving the delivery.
6. Review your information for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information (Customer Information)** (`text`, optional):
	+ Enter your name, address, and contact information.
* **Delivery Date (Delivery Date)** (`date`, optional):
	+ Enter the date of delivery in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Delivery Time (Delivery Time)** (`time`, optional):
	+ Enter the time of delivery in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Delivery Location (Delivery Location)** (`select_one`, optional):
	+ Choose the location where the delivery was made from:
		- Customer location
		- Alternate location
		- Warehouse location
* **Delivery Person (Delivery Person)** (`text`, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the person receiving the delivery.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately to facilitate efficient and secure delivery processes.
- Double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you need assistance or have any questions, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
