# Alumni Contact Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect your contact information as an alumnus of the university. Please fill out the form to allow the university to stay in touch and provide you with updates on various programs, events, and news.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on "Submit" to start filling out the form.
2. Fill out each field with your details, starting from "Full name", "Preferred email", and "Update preferences".
3. If you have a secondary email or phone number, please fill those out as well.
4. Select your preference on how you want to receive updates.
5. Decide if you want to be contacted by the university and select "True" or "False".
6. Add any additional comments you'd like to mention in the "Additional comments" field.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (`name`, `text`, required): Fill out your full name as you'd like to be addressed.
* **Preferred email** (`preferred_email`, `email`, required): Enter your preferred email address where you'd like to receive updates.
* **Secondary email** (`secondary_email`, `email`, optional): If you'd like to provide an alternate email address for the university to contact you, enter it here.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to provide your mobile or landline number, enter it here.
* **Mailing address** (`mailing_address`, `text`, optional): Enter your full mailing address, including street and city, state.
* **Update preferences** (`update_preferences`, `select_one`, required): Select how you'd like to receive updates from the university. This can be by Email, Postal mail, or both.
* **Permission to contact** (`permission_contact`, `select_one`, required): Decide if you'd like the university to contact you. Select 'True' for yes and 'False' for no.
* **Additional comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or notes you'd like to add, please enter them here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Select a valid option for "Update preferences".
* If you'd like to be contacted, make sure to select "True" in the "Permission to contact" field.
