# New Client Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Client Information Form is a structured form used to collect necessary details from new clients for our organization's records and follow up purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the First Name field with the client's first name.
2. Fill in the Last Name field with the client's last name.
3. Enter the client's Email address in the Email field.
4. Enter the client's Phone number in the Phone field.
5. If applicable, provide a Reason for contact in the Reason for contact field.
6. If applicable, select the client's Company and Job Title fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required/optional): The first name of the client.
* **Last Name** (text, required/optional): The last name of the client.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): The client's email address.
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): The client's phone number.
* **Reason for contact** (text, required/optional): If applicable, please provide a brief reason for the client's contact.
* **Company** (text, required/optional): The client's company name or affiliation.
* **Job Title** (text, required/optional): The client's job title or position.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure proper processing and follow up with the client.
* Use a clear and concise format when providing the Reason for contact field.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
* Do not submit duplicate information for the same client.
