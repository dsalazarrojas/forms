# Transfer Of Dog Ownership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Transfer Of Dog Ownership Form is used to document the transfer of ownership of a dog from one individual to another. This form is essential for updating the dog's records and for both parties to be aware of their responsibilities and rights regarding the dog.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **User Input**: Provide your basic information, including your name and contact details.
2. **Pet Details**: Enter the dog's details, including its name, breed, age, and any relevant health or behavioral issues.
3. **Owner Details**: Provide information about the current owner, including their name, address, and contact details.
4. **New Owner Details**: Enter the new owner's information, including their name, address, and contact details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **User Input** (`user_input`, text, required): Enter your name and contact information to initiate the transfer process.
  - Please ensure that your information is accurate and up-to-date.
- **Pet Details** (`pet_details`, text, required): Enter the dog's name, breed, age, and any relevant health or behavioral issues.
  - Be as detailed as possible to ensure the new owner is aware of the dog's needs.
- **Owner Details** (`owner_details`, text, required): Enter the current owner's name, address, and contact details.
  - Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
- **New Owner Details** (`new_owner_details`, text, required): Enter the new owner's name, address, and contact details.
  - Ensure the information is accurate and up-to-date to facilitate the transfer process.

## Tips

- Please ensure all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- If there are any issues or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities or our support team.
- The new owner will receive a confirmation email once the form is submitted.
- Please review and sign the form before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
