# Industrial Policy Consulting Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from companies seeking consultation on industrial policy. It helps us understand your company's needs and provide personalized guidance to support your goals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your company's name to identify your organization.
2. Provide your email address so we can contact you.
3. Enter your phone number for further communication.
4. Type a message detailing your industrial policy needs and requirements.
5. Enter your name as a representative of your organization.
6. If applicable, provide your company's website for reference.
7. If applicable, enter your company's location for context.
8. If applicable, specify your company's industry for better understanding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your company's name as it appears on your business cards or letterhead.
  - **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your company's email address in the format "example@domain.com".
  - **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your company's phone number in the format "123-456-7890".
  - **Message** (`message`, `note`, required): Type a clear and concise message detailing your industrial policy needs and requirements.
  - **Name of Organization** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as a representative of your organization.
  - **Website** (`website`, `text`, optional): If your company has a website, provide the URL.
  - **Location** (`location`, `text`, optional): If relevant to your industrial policy needs, specify your company's location.
  - **Industry** (`industry`, `text`, optional): If applicable, specify your company's industry for better understanding.

## Tips
- Ensure the company name is accurate and up-to-date.
- Enter a valid email address that is checked regularly.
- A clear and concise message is crucial for efficient processing.
- If your company has a website, it may be helpful to provide it for reference.
