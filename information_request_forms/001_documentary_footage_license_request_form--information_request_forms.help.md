# Documentary Footage License Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a license for documentary footage from an archive. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the intended usage of the footage, distribution channels, license duration, and other relevant details from the requestor. This form helps the archive team to understand the request and provide the necessary guidance and approval for the license.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Answer the questions about your intended usage of the documentary footage.
2. Indicate the distribution channels you plan to use for the footage.
3. Specify the duration of the license you require.
4. Provide the details about the production company that produced the documentary.
5. Fill in the name of the archive that owns the documentary footage.
6. Enter your contact information, including your name, job title, department, company, and contact email address.
7. Specify your phone number.
8. Provide a brief description of your project.
9. Accept the license terms.
10. Sign the form with your name and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your intended usage of the documentary footage?** (`usage_purpose`): Please select one of the options provided:
	+ Non-commercial use
	+ Commercial use
	+ Personal project
	+ Research
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Which distribution channel(s) do you plan to use for the documentary footage?** (`distribution`): Please select all that apply:
	+ YouTube
	+ Vimeo
	+ TV broadcast
	+ Online streaming platform
	+ Other (please specify)
* **How long do you plan to license the footage for?** (`license_duration`): Please select one of the options provided:
	+ One-time use
	+ Ongoing use
	+ Unknown or open-ended
* **Which production company produced the documentary?** (`production_company`): Please enter the name of the company that produced the documentary.
* **What is the name of the archive that owns the documentary footage?** (`archive_name`): Please enter the name of the archive that owns the documentary footage.
* **Who is your contact person for this request?** (`media_contact`): Please enter the name of your contact person for this request.
* **What is your contact email address?** (`contact_email`): Please enter your email address.
* **What is your contact phone number?** (`contact_phone`): Please enter your phone number.
* **Can you provide a brief description of the project?** (`project_details`): Please provide a short description of your project.
* **Have you accepted the license terms?** (`license_accepted`): Please select `True` if you have accepted the license terms.
* **Please sign your name below** (`signature`): Please sign your name with a pen or a digital signature.
* **What date did you sign this form?** (`date_signed`): Please enter the date you signed this form.
* **What is your name?** (`user_name`): Please enter your name.
* **What is your job title?** (`job_title`): Please enter your job title.
* **Which department do you belong to?** (`department`): Please enter the name of your department.
* **Which company do you represent?** (`company`): Please enter the name of the company you represent.
* **What is your work address?** (`address`): Please enter your work address.
* **What is your work phone number?** (`phone`): Please enter your work phone number.
