# data_privacy_information_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals requesting access to their data, rights related to their data, or corrections to their data. Please fill out this form to provide a clear and concise overview of your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Fill out the **Privacy Inquiry Details** page (page 1) with a brief description of your request.
* On the **Request Type** page (page 2), select the type of request you are making (Data Subject Access Request, Subject Rights Request, or Data Correction Request).
* You can optionally add additional details in the **Description** page (page 3).
* If necessary, upload supporting documents in the **Supporting Documents** page (page 4).
* Fill out the **Contact Details** page (page 5) with your contact information.
* Provide a date and time for the request in the **Date** and **Time** pages (pages 6 and 7).
* Enter your email address and phone number in the **Email** and **Phone** pages (pages 8 and 9).
* Finally, fill out the **Address** and **Name** pages (pages 10 and 11) with your address and name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Privacy Inquiry Details**: A brief description of your request.
* **Request Type**: 
    + Data Subject Access Request: Request to access your own data.
    + Subject Rights Request: Request to know what rights you have related to your data.
    + Data Correction Request: Request to correct inaccuracies in your data.
* **Description**: Optional field for adding additional details.
* **Supporting Documents**: Upload any supporting documents related to your request.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your contact name, address, and email address.
* **Date**: The date you are requesting.
* **Time**: The time you are requesting.
* **Email**: Your email address.
* **Phone**: Your phone number.
* **Address**: Your address.
* **Name**: Your name.
* **Company**: Optional field for entering your company name.

Note: Please fill out as much information as you can, and if you have any questions or need help, do not hesitate to contact us.
