# Artist Offer Sheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Artist Offer Sheet form is a crucial document that helps organizers and promoters of events or performances understand the artist's terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details (Submitter First Name, Submitter Last Name, Submitter Email Address, Submitter Phone Number, Business Name or Organization, Type of Business, and Business Address).
2. Provide your business information (Business Name or Organization, Type of Business, and Business Address).
3. Enter your artistic details (Artist or Performer Name).
4. Offer details (Date of Offer, Event or Performance Date, Event Time, and Performance Duration - Hours).
5. Specify payment terms (Offer Amount or Fee and Currency).
6. Confirm the performance details (Event Capacity, if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1
* **Submitter First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Submitter Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Submitter Email Address**: Enter your email address (e.g., [name@email.com](mailto:name@email.com)).
* **Submitter Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Business Name or Organization**: Enter the name of your business or organization.
* **Type of Business**: Select the type of business you represent (e.g., venue, promoter, production company, etc.).
* **Business Address**: Enter the address of your business or organization.

### Page 2
* **Artist or Performer Name**: Enter your name as it should be displayed or credited.
* **Date of Offer**: Enter the date you are offering your services.
* **Event or Performance Date**: Enter the date of the event or performance.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event or performance.
* **Performance Duration - Hours**: Enter the duration of the performance in hours.
* **Offer Amount or Fee**: Enter the amount or fee you are offering.
* **Currency**: Select the currency of your offer.
* **Payment Terms**: Choose how you want to be paid (Full Payment Before Event, 50 Percent Deposit, Payment After Event, or Negotiable).
* **Expected Event Capacity**: If required, enter the expected capacity of the event.
* **Technical Requirements Available**: Describe any technical requirements you can provide (e.g., sound, lighting, etc.).

### Page 3
* **Accommodation or Hospitality Provided**: Select if you are providing accommodation or hospitality.
* **Marketing and Promotion Responsibility**: Specify who is responsible for marketing and promotion.
* **Contract Terms and Conditions Agreed**: Confirm if you have agreed to the contract terms and conditions.
* **Additional Terms or Conditions**: Enter any additional terms or conditions you would like to add.

Note: Please make sure to review all the fields carefully before submitting the form.
