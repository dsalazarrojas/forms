# Board Member Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect personal and professional information from prospective board members to evaluate their eligibility and suitability for a spot on the board.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each section accurately and thoroughly. The required fields are marked as such. If you have trouble with any of the fields, refer to the explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
### Preferred Name
* **Preferred Name** (`preferred_name`, `text`, optional): If your preferred name is different from your legal name, enter it here.
### Email Address
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your primary contact email address.
### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your primary contact phone number.
### Street Address
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, `text`, required): Enter your mailing address.
### City
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city where you live.
### State
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter the state where you live.
### ZIP Code
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your ZIP code.
### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth for record-keeping purposes.
### Board Position
* **Board Position** (`board_position`, `select_one`, required): Select your desired board position from the options provided.
### Term Start Date
* **Term Start Date** (`term_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when your term begins.
### Term End Date
* **Term End Date** (`term_end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when your term ends.
### Professional Title
* **Professional Title** (`professional_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title.
### Organization
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your company or organization.
### Industry
* **Industry** (`industry`, `select_one`, required): Select the industry sector you belong to from the options provided.
### Areas of Expertise
* **Areas of Expertise** (`expertise_areas`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the areas where you have expertise from the options provided.
### Previous Board Experience
* **Previous Board Experience** (`board_experience`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe any previous board experience you have.
### Committee Preferences
* **Committee Preferences** (`committee_preferences`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any committees you are interested in joining.
### General Availability
* **General Availability** (`availability`, `select_one`, required): Select when you are typically available.
### Emergency Contact Name
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
### Emergency Contact Phone
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
### Short Biography
* **Short Biography** (`biography`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief bio about yourself for organizational use.
### Photo Upload
* **Photo Upload** (`photo_upload`, `text`, optional): Upload a professional headshot (file name).

Note: Fields marked as `required` must be completed before submitting the form.
