# Demo Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Demo Request Form is designed to collect information from businesses interested in requesting a demo of our product. It is meant to be filled out by business representatives who want to schedule a demo of our product and gather more information about their interests and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form)
2. Select the interested product and contact method from the dropdown menus
3. Choose a date and time for the demo

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your company or business
* **Name Of Interested Party** (`name_of_interested_party`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person filling out this form on behalf of your business
* **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, number, optional): Enter your business phone number
* **Business Email** (`business_email`, email, optional): Enter your business email
* **Interested Business Product** (`interested_business_product`, text, optional): Select the product you are interested in learning more about
* **Interested In** (`interested_business_interest`, select_one, optional): Select the specific product demo you are interested in (e.g. Demo Product 1, Demo Product 2, Demo Product 3)
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g. Phone, Email, In-person)
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who will be contacting you
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, optional): Enter your business address
* **Demo Date** (`demo_date`, date, optional): Choose a date for the demo
* **Demo Time** (`demo_time`, time, optional): Choose a time for the demo

## Tips
* Please fill out as much information as possible to ensure we can follow up with you efficiently.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact our support team for assistance.
