# product_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The product information form is used to collect and store information about a product for various purposes, such as creating a product catalog or listing on a website or database.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **Product ID** (a unique identifier for the product).
2.  Enter the **Product Name** (a descriptive text for the product).
3.  Enter the **Product Price** (the cost of the product).
4.  Enter the **Product Description** (a detailed text about the product).
5.  Select a category from the **Product Category** dropdown menu.
6.  Select multiple tags from the **Product Tags** dropdown menu.
7.  Enter the URL of the **Product Image**.
8.  Enter the **Product Date** (the date the product was created).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **Product ID** (1): Enter a unique identifier for the product.
*  **Product Name** (2): Enter a descriptive text for the product.
*  **Product Price** (3): Enter the cost of the product.
*  **Product Description** (4): Enter a detailed text about the product.
*  **Product Category** (5): Select a category for the product from the dropdown menu.
*  **Product Tags** (6): Select multiple tags for the product from the dropdown menu.
*  **Product Image** (7): Enter the URL of the product image.
*  **Product Date** (8): Enter the date the product was created.
