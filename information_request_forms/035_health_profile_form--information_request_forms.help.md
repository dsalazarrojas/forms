# Health Profile Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Profile Form is designed to collect health-related information from individuals, providing a comprehensive view of a person's health profile.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the Personal Info page.
2. On the Personal Info page, enter your personal details as requested.
3. Proceed to the Medical History page.
4. On the Medical History page, enter any medical history information you have.
5. Select any medical conditions that apply to you from the Medical History Conditions dropdown menu.
6. List any allergies you have in the Allergies field.
7. Enter any medications you are currently taking in the Medications field.
8. List any vaccinations you have received in the Vaccinations field.
9. Provide information about your family medical history in the Family History field.
10. Lastly, share any relevant social history information in the Social History field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal Info
* **Personal Info (text)**: This is the starting point for your health profile form. Enter your personal details as requested.

### Medical History
* **Medical History (text)**: This field is for sharing any medical history information you have.

### Medical History Conditions
* **Medical History Conditions (select_one)**: Select any of the listed conditions that apply to you from the dropdown menu.

### Allergies
* **Allergies (select_multiple)**: Choose all the allergies that you have.

### Medications
* **Medications (text)**: List any medications you are currently taking.

### Vaccinations
* **Vaccinations (text)**: List any vaccinations you have received.

### Family History
* **Family History (text)**: Share information about your family's medical history.

### Social History
* **Social History (text)**: Provide any relevant social history information.

## Tips
* Please make sure to answer all questions carefully and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, consult your healthcare provider or a qualified medical professional.
* If you need help with any field, do not hesitate to contact the form administrators at form@gic.mx.
