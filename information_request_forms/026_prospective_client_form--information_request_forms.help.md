# Prospective Client Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for collecting initial information from prospective clients for the purposes of client management, lead follow-up, or potential partnership opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name.
2. Enter your last name.
3. Provide your email address.
4. Enter your contact number.
5. Select your current job title.
6. Choose the organization you are associated with.
7. Select the role you would like to be assigned as a prospective client.
8. (Optional) Enter the date of your birth.
9. (Optional) Select the time for the interview.
10. (Optional) Add a note if you have any additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your given name.
* **Last name**: Enter your family name.
* **Email**: Enter your professional email address.
* **Contact number**: Enter your phone number or other contact information.
* **Job title**: Select your current job title from the provided list.
* **Organization**: Choose the organization you are associated with from the list.
* **Role**: Select the role you would like to be assigned as a prospective client from the list.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Time of interview**: Select a time for the interview if you have scheduled one.
* **Note**: Add any additional information you want to share with our team.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any questions, feel free to contact us.
* Our team will review your submission and follow up with you accordingly.
