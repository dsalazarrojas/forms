# digital_ecosystem_strategy_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users who need to get in touch with us or have questions about our digital ecosystem strategy. We will use the information provided to better understand your needs and preferences, and provide personalized support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
3. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
4. Enter your company name (if applicable) in the Company field.
5. Enter your email address in the Email field.
6. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
7. Select your department from the Department field.
8. If you have a specific question or inquiry, type it in the Question field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, **required**:): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, **required**:): Enter your last name.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, **optional**:): Enter your company name (if applicable).
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **required**:): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, **required**:): Enter your phone number.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, **required**:): Select your department (IT, Marketing, Sales, or Support).
* **Question** (`question`, `note`, **optional**:): If you have a specific question or inquiry, type it here.

## Tips
* Please provide clear and concise information in each field to ensure we can assist you efficiently.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
