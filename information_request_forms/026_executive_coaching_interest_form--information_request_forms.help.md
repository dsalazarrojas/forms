# executive_coaching_interest_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The executive coaching interest form is a tool for executives to express their interest in executive coaching services. It helps our team understand your areas of improvement and availability for coaching sessions. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form accurately to ensure you receive relevant support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions as accurately as possible. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
Here is a detailed explanation of each field:

* **Executive Coaching Interest** (`1`, `text`, required: false): 
	+ This is a free-form text field where you can briefly describe your areas of interest for executive coaching. Please provide a short paragraph or bullet points explaining what you'd like to work on in a coaching engagement.
* **Category** (`2`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Select one or multiple categories that interest you for executive coaching. This form includes "Executive Coaching" and "Other" options. Please choose the one that best represents your interest.
* **Location** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Please select all the locations where you are available for coaching sessions. This form includes options for New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Austin, Boston, Seattle, Dallas, Houston, and Philadelphia, as well as Miami.
* **Executive Coaching Interests** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Choose one or multiple executive coaching interests from the provided options: Time Management, Career Development, Stress Management, and Work-Life Balance. These are just examples; you can also select "Other" and provide your own interests.
* **Email** (`5`, `email`, required: false):
	+ Please provide your email address so we can follow up with you about your interest in executive coaching and any upcoming sessions.
* **Phone** (`6`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Please provide your phone number so we can reach you more easily.
* **Date** (`7`, `date`, required: false):
	+ Please select a date you are available for coaching sessions.
* **Time** (`8`, `time`, required: false):
	+ Please select a time slot for coaching sessions.
* **Note** (`9`, `note`, required: false):
	+ If you have any specific comments or requests regarding coaching, please provide them here.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when selecting your coaching interests and locations.
- Make sure to provide accurate contact information so we can follow up with you.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
