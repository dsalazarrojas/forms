# Gun Transfer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gun Transfer Form is used to collect information on gun transfers, including the details of the gun, the person transferring the gun, and the method of transfer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2. Choose the correct transfer method for the gun transfer.
3. Enter the gun type and date of transfer.
4. Enter the person's name and contact information for follow-up.
5. Enter the date of birth and address of the person transferring the gun.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Gun Type (gun_type)**: Select the type of gun being transferred (Handgun, Rifle, Shotgun).
- **Transfer to (transfer_to)**: Choose whether the gun is being transferred to a person or not.
- **Reason for Transfer (reason_for_transfer)**: Enter a brief explanation for the gun transfer.
- **Phone Number (phone_number)**: Enter the phone number of the person transferring the gun for follow-up.
- **Email Address (email_address)**: Enter the email of the person transferring the gun for follow-up.
- **Address (address)**: Enter the address of the person transferring the gun for follow-up.
- **Date of Birth (date_of_birth)**: Enter the date of birth of the person transferring the gun.
- **Date of Transfer (date_of_transfer)**: Enter the date the gun is being transferred.
- **Transfer Date (transfer_date)**: Enter the date the gun is being transferred.
- **Transfer Method (transfer_method)**: Choose the method of gun transfer (Handover, Mail, Hand delivery).

Note: This form is not intended for commercial purposes and must be filled out for non-commercial use.
