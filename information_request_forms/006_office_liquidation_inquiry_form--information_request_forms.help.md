# Office Liquidation Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Office Liquidation Inquiry Form is used to request information about the liquidation of office assets. This form is to be filled out for each asset being liquidated.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of asset being liquidated (Asset or Other).
2. Provide a detailed description of the asset (asset_details).
3. Select the location where the asset is located (Building, Floor, or Other).
4. Enter the date and time of the inspection (inspection_date and inspection_time).
5. Select the contacts who should be notified (Contact 1, Contact 2, or Other).
6. Provide their name, phone number, and email address (contact_name, contact_phone, and contact_email).
7. Enter the value of the asset (asset_value).
8. Indicate if the asset is inspectable (inspection_location).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Liquidation Request** (`liquidation_request`): This field is used to request information about the liquidation of an office asset. Please provide a detailed description of the asset being liquidated.
* **asset_type** (`asset_type`): Select the type of asset (Asset or Other) to indicate what is being liquidated.
* **asset_details** (`asset_details`): Enter a detailed description of the asset being liquidated.
* **asset_location** (`asset_location`): Select the location where the asset is located (Building, Floor, or Other).
* **inspection_date** (`inspection_date`): Enter the date of the inspection in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **inspection_time** (`inspection_time`): Enter the time of the inspection in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **contact_details** (`contact_details`): Select the contacts who should be notified (Contact 1, Contact 2, or Other). This is a multiple selection field.
* **contact_name** (`contact_name`): If selecting "Other" in the contact_details field, enter the name of the contact.
* **contact_phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the contact.
* **contact_email** (`contact_email`): Enter the email address of the contact.
* **asset_value** (`asset_value`): Enter the value of the asset being liquidated.
* **inspection_location** (`inspection_location`): Indicate if the asset is inspectable (True or False).
* **inspection_notes** (`inspection_notes`): Enter any additional notes about the inspection (optional).
