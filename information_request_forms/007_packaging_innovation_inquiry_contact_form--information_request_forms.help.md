# packaging_innovation_inquiry_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for people interested in learning more about packaging innovation or seeking collaboration opportunities in the packaging industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply scroll down to the bottom and click on the "Submit" button after filling out all the required fields. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required): Enter your name, company (if applicable), and any other relevant contact information.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, required): Describe your project in a few sentences, including the type of packaging innovation you are interested in or the specific collaboration you are seeking.
* **Company Details** (`company_details`, text, optional): If you are representing a company, provide their name and any other relevant information.
* **Contact Request** (`contact_request`, text, required): Explain how the person filling out this form would like to be contacted (e.g., email, phone, etc.).
* **Innovation Request** (`innovation_request`, text, optional): Share any specific innovations you are interested in learning more about or collaborating on.
* **Collaboration Request** (`collaboration_request`, text, optional): Indicate if you are open to potential collaboration or specify any specific collaboration opportunities you are interested in.
 
## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your submission is successfully sent.
- Be as clear and concise as possible when providing your contact and project information. 
- If you have any attachments or further details, consider attaching them to your submission or including them in the "Project Details" field. 
- You can always reach out to the form administrators if you have any questions or need further clarification.
