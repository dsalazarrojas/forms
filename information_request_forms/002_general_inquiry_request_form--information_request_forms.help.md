# General Inquiry Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from end-users about general inquiries or requests they would like to submit. Please note that this form is not intended for urgent matters, as it is not a replacement for more formal channels (e.g., phone, email, or direct message). Ensure that you've checked our FAQ page, knowledge base, or reached out to your support channel first before submitting an inquiry via this form. This will help us process your inquiry efficiently and effectively. Before proceeding, also check that the information you're about to submit isn't already known to us or that you haven't submitted it previously, to avoid duplication of effort on our end.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information in the fields below.
2. Select the type of inquiry you wish to submit from the provided options.
3. Give a brief description of your inquiry.
4. Provide the contact person's name (if applicable).
5. Enter the contact person's email (if applicable).
6. Enter the contact person's phone number (if applicable).
7. Write a clear message about your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (Page 1)** (`general_inquiry_request_form_page_1`, text, Optional): Provide your name and contact details as the representative of your organization.
* **Inquiry Type (Page 2)** (`inquiry_type`, select_one, Optional): Select the type of inquiry you're submitting (e.g., Sales, Technical, General, etc.).
* **Description (Page 2)** (`inquiry_description`, text, Optional): Briefly explain your inquiry in 1-2 sentences.
* **Contact Person (Page 2)** (`inquiry_contact_person`, text, Optional): Enter the name of the contact person if your inquiry is on behalf of someone else.
* **Contact Email (Page 2)** (`inquiry_contact_email`, email, Optional): Enter the email address of the contact person if different from yours.
* **Contact Phone (Page 2)** (`inquiry_contact_phone`, text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person if different from yours.
* **Message (Page 2)** (`inquiry_message`, text, Optional): Clearly write a message about your inquiry, including any relevant details or questions you have.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Use clear and concise language when describing your inquiry.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification or contact our support channel directly.
* We will get back to you as soon as possible, usually within [insert timeframe].
