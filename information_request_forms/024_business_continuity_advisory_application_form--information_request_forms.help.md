# Business Continuity Advisory Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a tool to assist organizations in assessing and improving their business continuity and disaster recovery capabilities. By providing detailed information about their business operations, risks, and objectives, organizations can help experts provide targeted guidance on how to develop and maintain a robust business continuity program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and ensure you have the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Answer each question honestly and accurately based on your organization's current state and requirements.
3. Use the options provided to select the most relevant answers.
4. If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask for guidance or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Organization Information
* **Organization Name** (Organization Name, text, required): Enter the full legal name of your organization.
* **Organization Type** (Organization Type, select_one, required): Select the type of organization you represent (e.g., For-Profit Corporation, Non-Profit, Government Agency).
* **Number of Employees** (Number of Employees, number, required): Provide the total staff count of your organization.
* **Annual Revenue Range** (Annual Revenue Range, select_one, required): Choose an approximate annual revenue range for your organization.
* ...and so on for each field.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Person Name** (Contact Person Name, text, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Title** (Contact Title, text, required): Enter the job title or position of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, required): Enter the business email address of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, required): Enter the business phone number of the primary contact person.

### Business Continuity Status
* **Current Business Continuity Readiness** (Current Business Continuity Readiness, select_one, required): Indicate the current state of your organization's continuity planning (e.g., No plan in place, Plan in development).
* **Critical Functions Identified** (Critical Functions Identified, select_one, required): Confirm whether you have identified your organization's critical business functions.
* **Recovery Time Objective (RTO)** (Recovery Time Objective (RTO), number, optional): Specify the acceptable downtime in hours.
* **Recovery Point Objective (RPO)** (Recovery Point Objective (RPO), number, optional): Specify the acceptable data loss in minutes.

### Advisory Needs
* **Primary Advisory Need** (Primary Advisory Need, select_one, required): Select the main area requiring guidance (e.g., Business Continuity Plan Development).
* **Specific Challenges** (Specific Challenges, text, required): Describe any detailed challenges you're facing.
* **Regulatory Requirements** (Regulatory Requirements, text, optional): List any specific compliance needs.

### Advisory Timeline
* **Advisory Timeline** (Advisory Timeline, select_one, required): Specify when you need assistance (e.g., Immediately).

### Other
* **Budget Range** (Budget Range, select_one, optional): Select a budget range for the advisory services.
* **Expected Outcomes** (Expected Outcomes, text, required): Describe what success would look like for your organization.

### Tips
- Be honest and accurate in your answers to ensure we can provide tailored guidance.
- If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.
- Remember to review each section carefully before submitting the form.
