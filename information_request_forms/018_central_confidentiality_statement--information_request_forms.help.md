# Central Confidentiality Statement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document a confidential agreement between two parties, outlining what information is considered confidential and for how long.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the sections for Disclosing Party Name, Receiving Party Name, and Full Name of Signatory to provide the necessary identifying information for both parties.
2. Select the relationship between the parties in the "Relationship Between Parties" section.
3. Describe the purpose of the disclosure in the "Purpose of Disclosure" section.
4. Select the types of confidential information covered in the "Types of Information Covered" section.
5. Choose the term of confidentiality in the "Term of Confidentiality" section.
6. Confirm agreement to maintain confidentiality in the "Agreement to Maintain Confidentiality" section.
7. If necessary, list any additional non-disclosure requirements in the "Other Non-Disclosure Requirements" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Disclosing Party Name (Required)**: Enter the name of the entity or individual providing the confidential information.
* **Receiving Party Name (Required)**: Enter the name of the entity or individual receiving the confidential information.
* **Relationship Between Parties (Required)**: Select the relationship between the parties, such as Employer - Employee, Business Partners, etc.
* **Purpose of Disclosure (Required)**: Briefly describe why this information is being shared.
* **Types of Information Covered (Required)**: Select all categories that apply to the confidential information, such as Trade Secrets, Financial Data, etc.
* **Term of Confidentiality (Required)**: Choose the term of confidentiality, such as 1 Year, 2 Years, 5 Years, or Indefinite.
* **Agreement to Maintain Confidentiality (Required)**: Confirm that you agree to maintain confidentiality of the shared information.
* **Full Name of Signatory (Required)**: Type your full name, which serves as an electronic signature.
* **Date Signed (Required)**: Enter the date you signed this agreement.
* **Other Non-Disclosure Requirements (Optional)**: List any other specific requirements for this agreement if necessary.
