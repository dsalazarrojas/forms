# Property Automation Team Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to contact the Property Automation Team for assistance, feedback, or complaints related to a property. Please use this form to report any issues or inquiries you have about a property.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form completely**: Provide as much information as possible about the issue or inquiry you have.
2. **Be accurate and clear**: Ensure that your contact information and issue description are accurate and easy to understand.
3. **Choose the correct department**: Select the department that best matches your reason for contact (e.g., Support, Feedback, Complaint).
4. **Specify the property details**: If you're reporting an issue related to a specific property, please provide the property name and address.
5. **Select the correct property type**: Indicate the type of property (e.g., Residential, Commercial, Industrial, Mixed Use).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): This field is for your contact name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): This field is for your contact last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number so we can call you for further discussion.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Describe the issue or inquiry you have in as much detail as possible.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Choose the department that best matches your reason for contact (e.g., Support, Feedback, Complaint).
* **Property Name** (`property_name`, text, optional): If you're reporting an issue related to a specific property, enter the property name here.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the property related to your issue.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, optional): Describe the issue you're experiencing with the property.
* **Contact Reason** (`contact_reason`, select_one, optional): Indicate why you're contacting us (e.g., Support, Feedback, Complaint).
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of property (e.g., Residential, Commercial, Industrial, Mixed Use).

## Tips
- Be clear and concise in your message to ensure we understand your issue or inquiry accurately.
- If you're reporting an issue, provide as much detail as possible about the issue and any relevant background information.
- If you're requesting feedback or have a general inquiry, please provide context and background information that will help us understand your question or concern.
- If you have any attachments or additional information to support your issue, please attach them to the form or email them to us separately.
