**Property Planning Support Contact Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Property Planning Support Contact Form is a simple and intuitive form that allows users to submit their queries and requests related to property planning and support. This form is designed to gather essential information from users, enabling our team to provide prompt and efficient assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide a brief description of your property planning concern or query in the **property_details** field.
2. If you are contacting us for support, include your contact information in the **contact_info** field.
3. If necessary, provide additional property details in the **property_details_1** field.
4. If you wish to specify the type of property you are referring to, select one or multiple options from the **property_type** field.
5. Indicate the location of the property using the **property_location** field.
6. Choose the date of your submission from the calendar in the **submission_date** field.
7. For further support, answer 'Yes' or 'No' to the following questions:
	* Are you looking for property planning support? 
	* Do you have any other questions or concerns regarding your property planning? 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **property_details**: Provide a brief description of your property planning concern or query. This field is optional.
* **contact_info**: Please enter your contact information so our team can get in touch with you.
* **property_details_1**: If you have any additional property details, please enter them here.
* **property_type**: Choose one or multiple options to indicate the type of property you are referring to. Select from:
	+ option 1
	+ option 2
	+ option 3
	+ option 4
* **property_location**: Select one or multiple options to indicate the location of the property. Select from:
	+ option 1
	+ option 2
* **submission_date**: Choose the date of your submission from the calendar.
* **form_8**: Indicate if you are looking for property planning support by answering 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **form_9**: Indicate if you have any other questions or concerns regarding your property planning by answering 'Yes' or 'No'.
