# Patient Medication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect comprehensive information about a patient's medication history for better treatment planning and care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "patient_name" field.
2. Provide the patient's contact information (phone number and email) in the "contact_phone" and "contact_email" fields, respectively.
3. Write a brief description of the patient's medical history in the "medical_history" field.
4. List any allergies the patient has in the "allergies" field.
5. For each medication the patient takes, enter the name, dosage, frequency, start and end dates in the corresponding fields (e.g., "medication_1_name", "medication_1_dosage", etc.). You can add multiple medications by filling out the corresponding fields for each medication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_name** (required): Enter the patient's name.
* **contact_phone** (optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **contact_email** (optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **patient_address** (optional): Enter the patient's address.
* **medical_history** (optional): Describe the patient's medical history.
* **allergies** (optional): List any allergies the patient has.
* **medications** (optional): List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **medication_1_name** (optional): Enter the name of the first medication the patient takes.
* **medication_1_frequency** (optional): Enter the frequency of the medication (e.g., daily, weekly, etc.).
* **medication_1_dosage** (optional): Enter the dosage of the medication.
* **medication_1_start_date** (optional): Enter the start date of the medication.
* **medication_1_end_date** (optional): Enter the end date of the medication.
* **medication_1_comments** (optional): Add any comments about the medication.
* **medication_2_name** (optional): Enter the name of the second medication the patient takes.
* **medication_2_frequency** (optional): Enter the frequency of the second medication.
* **medication_2_dosage** (optional): Enter the dosage of the second medication.
* **medication_2_start_date** (optional): Enter the start date of the second medication.
* **medication_2_end_date** (optional): Enter the end date of the second medication.
* **medication_2_comments** (optional): Add any comments about the second medication.
* **medication_3_name** (optional): Enter the name of the third medication the patient takes.
* **medication_3_frequency** (optional): Enter the frequency of the third medication.
* **medication_3_dosage** (optional): Enter the dosage of the third medication.
* **medication_3_start_date** (optional): Enter the start date of the third medication.
* **medication_3_end_date** (optional): Enter the end date of the third medication.
* **medication_3_comments** (optional): Add any comments about the third medication.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the patient's information is up-to-date and accurate.
* If the patient has multiple medications, please fill out the corresponding fields for each medication.
* If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to the healthcare professional responsible for the patient's care.
* Please double-check your entries before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
