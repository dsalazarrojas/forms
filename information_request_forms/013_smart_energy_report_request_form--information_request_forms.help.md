# Smart Energy Report Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Energy Report Request Form is a tool used to gather necessary information about a user's energy consumption and generate a report about it. This form is designed to be completed by users to request a report about their own energy consumption.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Details section with your name and contact information.
2. Provide accurate details about your property in the Property Details section.
3. Choose your preferred method of receiving the report in the Delivery Preferences section.
4. If applicable, include information about the energy consultant who will be working on the report in the Consultant Details section.
5. Use the Additional Comments field to add any extra information you'd like to be included in the report.
6. Click submit to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Details
**Name** (`user_details`, text, required): Please fill out your name and contact information in this field.
### Property Details
**Property Details** (`property_details`, text, required): Enter the address and description of your property here.
### Delivery Preferences
**Delivery Preferences** (`delivery_preferences`, text, required): Choose how you'd like to receive the report: email, PDF, or another format.
### Consultant Details
**Consultant Details** (`consultant_details`, text, optional): If you have a specific energy consultant working on your report, provide their name and contact information.
### Additional Comments
**Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required): Use this field to include any extra information you'd like to be included in the report.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately to ensure the report is generated correctly.
- If you have already requested a report about this property, your request will be ignored to avoid duplication.
- You can use the form multiple times without any restrictions.
