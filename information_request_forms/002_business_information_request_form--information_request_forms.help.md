# Business Information Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Information Request Form is a tool used to gather detailed information from business users, ensuring that requests are managed efficiently and effectively. Fill out this form to send a formal request for business information.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name and contact email address to ensure we can communicate with you.
2. Provide your company name to help us identify which business you are inquiring about.
3. Specify the industry the business operates in to help us tailor our response.
4. Select the type of information you are requesting, such as company background, financial information, or contact details.
5. State the purpose of your request, including partnership evaluation, due diligence, or market research.
6. Indicate how quickly you need the information and your preferred follow-up method.
7. Optionally, provide any additional details or specifications about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (`requester_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Email Address** (`requester_email`, `email`, required): Enter your primary email contact address.
* **Company Name** (`requester_company`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`requester_phone`, `text`, required): Provide a best phone number to contact you.
* **Business Name You Are Inquiring About** (`target_business_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the business you are requesting information from.
* **Target Business Industry** (`target_business_industry`, `select_one`, required): Select the industry the business operates in from the provided options (Technology, Healthcare, Finance, Retail, Manufacturing, Services, or Other).
* **Type of Information Requested** (`information_needed`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the type of information you are requesting (Company Background, Financial Information, Products or Services, Contact Information, Certifications and Licenses, References, or Partnership Opportunities).
* **Purpose of Information Request** (`purpose_of_request`, `select_one`, required): Select the reason for your request from the provided options (Partnership Evaluation, Due Diligence, Vendor Assessment, Market Research, Business Development, or Other).
* **Intended Use of Information** (`intended_use`, `text`, optional): Describe how you plan to use the gathered information (no options provided).
* **Urgency Level** (`request_urgency`, `select_one`, required): Choose how quickly you need the information, from Immediate (within 24 hours), Urgent (within 1 week), Standard (within 2 weeks), or Non-urgent (flexible timeline).
* **Additional Details or Specifications** (`additional_details`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional context or information about your request.
* **I agree to Keep Information Confidential** (`confidentiality_agreement`, `select_one`, optional): Check if you agree to keep the information confidential (True or False).
* **Preferred Follow-up Method** (`follow_up_preference`, `select_one`, required): Select how you would like us to respond to your inquiry, from Email, Phone, Video Call, or In-Person Meeting.
* **Best Time to Contact You** (`best_contact_time`, `select_one`, optional): Choose when you are typically available for follow-up (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Any time).

## Tips
* Double-check your contact information to ensure we can reach you accurately.
* Be as specific as possible when selecting the type and purpose of information requested.
* If you need information within a tight deadline, select Urgent or Immediate.
* This form is a one-time request; if you need to follow up on a previous request, please resubmit the form with the new request details.
* You will receive a response based on your specified follow-up method.
