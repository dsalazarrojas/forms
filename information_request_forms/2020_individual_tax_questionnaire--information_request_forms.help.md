# 2020 Individual Tax Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 2020 Individual Tax Questionnaire is designed to collect information from taxpayers about their 2020 tax filing experience, including personal and contact information, tax return details, and feedback on the questionnaire itself. This form helps tax authorities to gather data on taxpayer satisfaction, identify areas for improvement, and make informed decisions to enhance tax services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your legal full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
2. Provide a valid email address.
3. Enter your phone number, including the area code.
4. If applicable, enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
5. Enter the last 4 digits of your Social Security Number (SSN).
6. Choose your filing status for the 2020 tax year.
7. Enter the number of dependents you claimed on your 2020 return.
8. If you received a copy of your 2020 return, select 'True' and enter the date received.
9. Enter the year of the tax return (2020).
10. Choose the preparer who completed your 2020 return.
11. If applicable, enter the full name of the preparer.
12. If applicable, provide the contact information of the preparer.
13. Select all tax documents you have received.
14. If you have received tax documents, note any missing or additional documents.
15. Rate your overall satisfaction with the questionnaire.
16. Rate your satisfaction with the questionnaire's design and functionality.
17. Select all aspects of your experience that applied to your feedback.
18. Share any suggestions or feedback for improvement.
19. Enter any additional comments or questions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (2): Enter your legal full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email address** (3): Provide a valid email address.
* **Phone number** (4): Enter your phone number, including the area code.
* **Date of birth** (5): If applicable, enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **SSN (last 4 digits)** (6): Enter the last 4 digits of your Social Security Number (SSN), no dashes.
* **Filing status** (7): Choose your filing status for the 2020 tax year.
* **Number of dependents claimed** (8): Enter the number of dependents you claimed on your 2020 return.
* **Did you receive a copy of your 2020 return?** (9): If applicable, select 'True' and enter the date received.
* **Date received** (10): If you received a copy of your 2020 return, enter the date received.
* **Tax year** (11): Enter the year of the tax return (2020).
* **Who prepared your 2020 return?** (12): Choose the preparer who completed your 2020 return.
* **Name of the preparer (if applicable)** (13): If applicable, enter the full name of the preparer.
* **Preparer contact information (if applicable)** (14): If applicable, provide the contact information of the preparer.
* **Tax documents received** (15): Select all tax documents you have received.
* **Tax documents received notes** (16): If you have received tax documents, note any missing or additional documents.
* **How satisfied are you with the 2020 Individual Tax Questionnaire?** (17): Rate your overall satisfaction with the questionnaire.
* **Overall rating** (18): Rate your satisfaction with the questionnaire's design and functionality.
* **Which aspects applied to your experience?** (19): Select all aspects of your experience that applied to your feedback.
* **Suggestions for improvement** (20): Share any suggestions or feedback for improvement.
* **Additional comments or notes** (21): Enter any additional comments or questions you have.
