# Client Stories Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Stories Form is designed to elicit a detailed account of a client's experience, allowing them to share their challenges, solutions, and outcomes in a compelling manner. This form serves as a valuable resource for learning, growth, and collaboration within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Begin by providing your contact information:
    *   Fill in your name in the "Your Name" field, as this is a required field.
    *   Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field, also required for communication.
2.  Share your company/Organization details:
    *   Enter your company name in the "Company/Organization Name" field, a mandatory field.
3.  Provide a detailed account of your story:
    *   Give your story a compelling title in the "Story Title" field.
    *   Briefly describe your story in the "Overview of your experience" field, keeping it concise to 2-3 sentences.
4.  Outline your challenge:
    *   Describe the original challenge in the "What was the original challenge?" field.
    *   Explain how it impacted your business in the "How did this challenge impact your business?" field.
5.  Share your solution:
    *   Explain how you solved the challenge in the "How did you solve the challenge?" field.
6.  Document your results:
    *   Specify the results you achieved in the "What results did you achieve?" field, focusing on specific improvements or metrics.
7.  Add context to your results:
    *   Mention the time it took to see results in the "How long did it take to see results?" field.
8.  Summarize your key takeaways:
    *   Provide your key lessons learned in the "Key lessons learned" field.
9.  (Optional) Add media:
    *   Choose whether your story can be used for testimonials or case studies in the "May we use your story for testimonials or case studies?" field.
    *   If allowed, choose whether your image can be used with your story in the "Can we use a photo of you with your story?" field.
10. Review and submit:
    *   Verify that you've completed all required fields and review your submission before submitting your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your Name**: Enter your full name in this field.
*   **Email Address**: Enter your work email address for communication and contact.
*   **Company/Organization Name**: Enter the name of your company or organization.
*   **Story Title**: Give your story a compelling and descriptive title.
*   **Overview of your experience**: Summarize your story in 2-3 sentences, providing context for others to understand your experience.
*   **What was the original challenge?**: Describe the challenge you faced, providing details for a better understanding.
*   **How did this challenge impact your business?**: Explain how this challenge affected your organization or operations.
*   **How did you solve the challenge?**: Outline the steps or solutions you took to address the challenge.
*   **What results did you achieve?**: Document specific improvements or metrics that resulted from your solution.
*   **How long did it take to see results?**: Provide the time frame for when you started seeing results from your solution.
*   **Key lessons learned**: Summarize the key takeaways or lessons from your experience.
*   **May we use your story for testimonials or case studies?**: Choose whether your story can be used for promotional purposes.
*   **Can we use a photo of you with your story?**: Decide whether your image can be associated with your story.
