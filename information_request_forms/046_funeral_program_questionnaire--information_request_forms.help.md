# Funeral Program Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Funeral Program Questionnaire is designed to collect information related to a funeral program. This form is used to gather essential details about the deceased, their family, and the funeral services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the Personal Information section (Page 1).
2. Next, fill out the Family Information section (Page 2).
3. Then, fill out the Funeral Date and Time of Funeral sections (Pages 3-4).
4. Proceed to the Burial Grounds, Location of Funeral, and Funeral Home sections (Pages 5-6).
5. Fill out the Contact Person, Contact Email, and Contact Phone sections (Pages 7-9).
6. Complete the Clergy Name, Officiant Information, and Obituary sections (Pages 10-11).
7. Finally, fill out the remaining sections, including Obituary Details, Burial Preferences, Casket, Casket Type, Headstone, Headstone Type, Urn, Urn Type, Cremation, Body Donation, Organ Donation, and Additional Comments (Pages 12-20).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Personal Information (Page 1)
* **Personal Information**: This section is used to collect the deceased's personal details. Please fill out their name, date of birth, and any other relevant information.

### Family Information (Page 2)
* **Family Information**: This section is used to collect information about the deceased's family. Please fill out the names, dates of birth, and relationships of family members.

### Funeral Date (Page 3)
* **Funeral Date**: This section is used to collect the date of the funeral service. Please enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### Time of Funeral (Page 4)
* **Time of Funeral**: This section is used to collect the time of the funeral service. Please enter the time in the format `HH:MM`.

### Burial Grounds (Page 5)
* **Burial Grounds**: This section is used to indicate whether the burial will take place in a specific location. Please select `True` if the burial will occur in a designated grounds, or `False` otherwise.

### Location of Funeral (Page 6)
* **Location of Funeral**: This section is used to collect the location of the funeral service. Please enter the address or venue where the service will be held.

### Contact Person (Page 7)
* **Contact Person**: This section is used to collect the name and contact information of the person responsible for coordinating the funeral. Please fill out their name and contact details.

### Contact Email (Page 8)
* **Contact Email**: This section is used to collect the email address of the contact person. Please enter their email address.

### Contact Phone (Page 9)
* **Contact Phone**: This section is used to collect the phone number of the contact person. Please enter their phone number.

### Funeral Home (Page 10)
* **Funeral Home**: This section is used to collect the name and address of the funeral home. Please fill out their name and contact details.

### Clergy Name (Page 11)
* **Clergy Name**: This section is used to collect the name of the clergy or officiant who will be conducting the funeral service. Please enter their name.

### Officiant Information (Page 12)
* **Officiant Information**: This section is used to collect any additional information about the officiant. Please fill out any relevant details.

### Obituary (Page 13)
* **Obituary**: This section is used to collect any obituary or death notice. Please fill out the text.

### Obituary Details (Page 14)
* **Obituary Details**: This section is used to collect any additional details about the obituary. Please fill out the text.

### Burial Preferences (Page 15)
* **Burial Preferences**: This section is used to collect any specific burial preferences. Please fill out any relevant details.

### Casket (Page 16)
* **Casket**: This section is used to collect the type of casket used for the funeral service. Please fill out the type of casket.

### Casket Type (Page 17)
* **Casket Type**: This section is used to collect any additional details about the casket. Please fill out any relevant details.

### Headstone (Page 18)
* **Headstone**: This section is used to collect the type of headstone used for the funeral service. Please fill out the type of headstone.

### Headstone Type (Page 19)
* **Headstone Type**: This section is used to collect any additional details about the headstone. Please fill out any relevant details.

### Urn (Page 20)
* **Urn**: This section is used to collect the type of urn used for the funeral service. Please fill out the type of urn.

### Urn Type (Page 21)
* **Urn Type**: This section is used to collect any additional details about the urn. Please fill out any relevant details.

### Cremation (Page 22)
* **Cremation**: This section is used to indicate whether cremation will take place. Please select `True` if cremation will occur, or `False` otherwise.

### Body Donation (Page 23)
* **Body Donation**: This section is used to collect whether body donation will take place. Please select `True` if body donation will occur, or `False` otherwise.

### Organ Donation (Page 24)
* **Organ Donation**: This section is used to collect whether organ donation will take place. Please select `True` if organ donation will occur, or `False` otherwise.

### Additional Comments (Page 25)
* **Additional Comments**: This section is used to collect any additional comments or information. Please fill out any relevant details.

### Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you encounter any issues or have questions, do not hesitate to ask for help.
* This form is for informational purposes only and should not be used as a legal document.
* Please review each section carefully and ensure that you have filled out all required fields.
* If you have already completed this form for the same funeral or individual, do not fill it out again.
