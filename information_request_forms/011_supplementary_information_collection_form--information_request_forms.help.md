# supplementary_information_collection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The supplementary information collection form is used to gather additional details about an individual or organization, which may be required for customer service or support purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the follow-up details, if any, in the "Follow Up Details" field.
2. If you have documents to upload, provide the document details in the "Document Uploads" field.
3. Select your preferred contact method from "Contact Preferences" field.
4. Specify the type of document from "Document Type" field.
5. Enter the size and weight of the document(s) in the "Document Size" and "Document Weight" fields.
6. If applicable, provide the URL of the document in the "Document URL" field.
7. Enter your email preferences in the "Email Preferences" field.
8. Provide phone preferences in the "Phone Preferences" field.
9. Fill in the contact details, including email, phone, name, title, department, office, street, city, state, postal, zip, and fax (if applicable).
10. Optionally provide follow-up comments in the "Follow Up Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Follow Up Details** (id: 1, text, optional): Enter any additional details regarding follow-up actions.
* **Document Uploads** (id: 2, note, optional): Upload any relevant documents.
* **Contact Preferences** (id: 3, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred contact methods. This field is optional.
* **Document Type** (id: 4, select_one, optional): Specify the type of document you are uploading.
* **Document Size** (id: 5, number, optional): Enter the size of the document.
* **Document Weight** (id: 6, number, optional): Enter the weight of the document.
* **Upload Documents** (id: 7, number, optional): This field is likely for the number of documents to upload.
* **Document Description** (id: 8, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the document.
* **Document Name** (id: 9, text, optional): Enter the name of the document.
* **Document URL** (id: 10, text, optional): Provide the URL of the document (if applicable).
* **Email Preferences** (id: 11, select_one, optional): Select your email preferences.
* **Phone Preferences** (id: 12, select_one, optional): Select your phone preferences.
* **Contact Email** (id: 13, email, optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (id: 14, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Name** (id: 15, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Contact Title** (id: 16, text, optional): Enter your title.
* **Contact Department** (id: 17, text, optional): Enter the department.
* **Contact Office** (id: 18, text, optional): Enter your office location.
* **Contact Street** (id: 19, text, optional): Enter the street address.
* **Contact City** (id: 20, text, optional): Enter the city.
* **Contact State** (id: 21, text, optional): Enter the state.
* **Contact Postal** (id: 22, text, optional): Enter the postal code (zip).
* **Contact Zip** (id: 23, text, optional): Enter the zip code.
* **Contact Fax** (id: 24, text, optional): Enter your fax number.
* **Follow Up Comments** (id: 25, text, optional): Optionally provide any follow-up comments.

Note: Some fields are marked optional, and you should only fill them in if applicable.
