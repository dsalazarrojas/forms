# Closing Property Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Closing Property Information Form" is designed to collect essential details about a property, including its address, ownership, and any known issues or damages. This form helps to provide accurate information for real estate transactions or property management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information about the property and its seller.
2. Review each section carefully to ensure all necessary fields are completed.
3. Double-check your responses for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Seller Name** (`seller_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full legal name of the seller.
- **Seller Email Address** (`seller_email`, `email`, required): Enter the seller's email address.
- **Seller Phone Number** (`seller_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the seller's primary contact number.
- **Property Address** (`property_address`, `text`, required): Enter the full street address of the property.
- **City** (`property_city`, `text`, required): Enter the city where the property is located.
- **State** (`property_state`, `text`, required): Enter the state where the property is located.
- **ZIP Code** (`property_zip`, `text`, required): Enter the ZIP code of the property.
- **Property Type** (`property_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of property (e.g., Single family home, Multi-family, Condo, etc.).
- **Year Built** (`year_built`, `number`, optional): Enter the approximate year the property was built.
- **Square Footage** (`square_footage`, `number`, optional): Enter the total living area of the property.
- **Number of Bedrooms** (`bedrooms`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of bedrooms in the property.
- **Number of Bathrooms** (`bathrooms`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of bathrooms in the property.
- **Ownership Duration** (`ownership_duration`, `text`, optional): Indicate how long you have owned this property.
- **Title Status** (`title_status`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if the title is clear and free of liens.
- **Title Company Name** (`title_company`, `text`, optional): If known, enter the name of the title company.
- **Is the Property a Single Family Home?** (`structural_issues`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if you are aware of any known structural issues or damages.
- **Describe Structural Issues** (`structural_description`, `text`, optional): Provide details about any known structural issues or damages.
- **History of Water Damage or Flooding?** (`water_damage_history`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if you are aware of any water damage or flooding history.
- **Known Pest or Termite Issues?** (`pest_issues`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if you are aware of any pest or termite issues.
- **Roof Age** (`roof_age`, `number`, optional): Enter the approximate age of the roof.
- **Any Known Utilities Issues?** (`section_utilities`, `note`, not applicable): While this section appears in the form, it does not have any input fields. This might be a placeholder or a section not requiring user input.

Note: This guide assumes the form is used for a single property. If multiple properties are being managed, ensure to complete each form separately to avoid confusion and potential errors.
