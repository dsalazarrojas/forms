# Autopsy Report Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Autopsy Report Request Form is designed to collect information from the requester to obtain an autopsy report for a deceased individual. This form is for internal use only and is intended to ensure that the report is processed and sent out in the agreed-upon manner.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your information, including your name, relationship to the decedent, email address, and phone number.
2. Verify your identity by confirming that you have attached a valid photo ID or will send it separately.
3. Provide the decedent's full name, date of birth, date of death, and location of death.
4. Specify the reason for requesting the autopsy report (e.g. insurance claim, personal closure, legal litigation, etc.).
5. Choose your preferred delivery method for the report (certified mail, secure email, or in-person pickup).
6. Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Requestor Information** (Section)
* **Requestor Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your valid photo ID.
* **Relationship to Decedent**: Select your legal relationship to the decedent (e.g. spouse, parent, child, etc.).
* **Requestor Email Address**: Enter your email address where the report will be sent.
* **Requestor Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.

### 2. **Decedent Information** (Section)
* **Decedent Full Name**: Enter the decedent's full name as it appears on their valid government-issued ID.
* **Decedent Date of Birth**: Enter the decedent's date of birth.
* **Decedent Date of Death**: Enter the decedent's date of death.
* **Location of Death**: Enter the city, county, and state where the decedent passed away.

### 3. **Report Logistics** (Section)
* **Reason for Request**: Explain why you need the autopsy report (e.g. insurance claim, personal closure, etc.).
* **Preferred Delivery Method**: Choose how you want to receive the report (certified mail, secure email, or in-person pickup).
* **Proof of Identity Verification**: Confirm that you have attached a valid photo ID or will send it separately.

### Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are accurate and complete before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues with the form or have questions, contact us directly.
* We will process your request in a timely and secure manner.
