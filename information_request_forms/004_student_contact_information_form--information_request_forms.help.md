# Student Contact Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Contact Information Form is used to collect contact information from students for academic or administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the student's address, phone number, and email address.
2. Select the type of phone number (mobile, landline, or other).
3. Enter the area code, exchange, and number of the phone number.
4. Enter any additional notes about the student.
5. If applicable, provide information about emergency contacts, including their name, relationship to the student, and phone number.
6. Enter any additional information about emergency contacts, such as their address.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_address**: Enter the student's full address, including the street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **student_phone_number**: Enter the student's phone number.
* **field_3**: Enter any additional information about the student (not required).
* **student_email**: Enter the student's email address.
* **student_notes**: Enter any additional notes about the student.
* **student_phone_number_type**: Select the type of phone number (mobile, landline, or other).
* **student_phone_number_area_code**: Enter the area code of the phone number.
* **student_phone_number_exchange**: Enter the exchange of the phone number.
* **student_phone_number_number**: Enter the number of the phone number.
* **student_phone_number_extension**: Enter the extension of the phone number.
* **student_phone_number_country_code**: Select the country code of the phone number.
* **emergency_contacts_name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **emergency_contacts_relationship**: Enter the relationship to the emergency contact.
* **emergency_contacts_relationship_address**: Select the address of the emergency contact.
* **emergency_contacts_relationship_phone**: Select the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **emergency_contacts_relationship**: Select the relationship type of the emergency contact.
* **emergency_contacts_relationship_notes**: Enter any additional notes about the emergency contact.
* **student_address_line_2**: Enter the student's second line of the address (if applicable).
* **student_address_city**: Enter the city of the student's address.
* **student_address_state**: Enter the state of the student's address.
* **student_address_zip**: Enter the zip code of the student's address.
* **student_address_country**: Enter the country of the student's address.
* **student_prophets**: Enter the country abbreviation of the student's address (not applicable).
