# Artist Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about artists participating in the program. Please fill out the form carefully, ensuring all required fields are completed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email Address for contact purposes.
3. Provide your Phone Number for easy communication.
4. Enter your Street Address, City, and State or Province for delivery and shipping purposes.
5. Enter your Zip or Postal Code for accurate delivery and shipping.
6. (Optional) If you have a Website or Portfolio URL, please provide it.
7. Write a short Artist Biography or Statement that summarizes your work.
8. Select your Primary Artistic Medium.
9. (Optional) If you have participated in programs for more than one year, enter the years since first participation.
10. (Optional) List the years you have participated in the program (comma-separated list).
11. (Optional) Share your exhibition or show history.
12. (Optional) List any awards or recognition you have received.
13. (Optional) Upload a professional portrait photo.
14. (Optional) Describe your current or recent artwork.
15. (Optional) Upload an artwork image or sample.
16. (Optional) Share your social media links (e.g. Instagram, Facebook, Twitter).
17. (Optional) Add any additional comments or notes about your participation in the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name (artist_first_name)
• **First Name** (artist_first_name, text, required): Enter your given name or the first name by which you are known.

### Last Name (artist_last_name)
• **Last Name** (artist_last_name, text, required): Enter your last name.

### Email Address (artist_email)
• **Email Address** (artist_email, email, required): Enter your valid email address for contact purposes.

### Phone Number (phone_number)
• **Phone Number** (phone_number, text, required): Enter your phone number for easy communication.

### Street Address (street_address)
• **Street Address** (street_address, text, required): Enter your street address for delivery and shipping purposes.

### City (city)
• **City** (city, text, required): Enter your city for delivery and shipping purposes.

### State or Province (state_province)
• **State or Province** (state_province, text, required): Enter your state or province for delivery and shipping purposes.

### Zip or Postal Code (zip_code)
• **Zip or Postal Code** (zip_code, text, required): Enter your zip or postal code for accurate delivery and shipping.

### Website or Portfolio URL (website_portfolio)
• **Website or Portfolio URL** (website_portfolio, text, optional): If you have a website or portfolio URL, please provide it.

### Artist Biography or Statement (artist_bio)
• **Artist Biography or Statement** (artist_bio, text, required): Write a short statement about your work and artistic experience.

### Primary Artistic Medium (artistic_medium)
• **Primary Artistic Medium** (artistic_medium, text, required): Select your main medium or material you work with (e.g., painting, sculpture, photography).

### Years Since First Participation (years_since_participation)
• **Years Since First Participation** (years_since_participation, number, optional): If you have participated in programs for more than one year, enter the number of years.

### Years Participated in Program (participation_years)
• **Years Participated in Program** (participation_years, text, optional): List the years you have participated in the program (comma-separated list).

### Exhibition or Show History (exhibition_history)
• **Exhibition or Show History** (exhibition_history, text, optional): Share your exhibition or show history.

### Awards or Recognition Received (awards_recognition)
• **Awards or Recognition Received** (awards_recognition, text, optional): List any awards or recognition you have received.

### Portrait Photo (portrait_photo)
• **Portrait Photo** (portrait_photo, text, optional): Upload a professional headshot or photo.

### Artwork Details and Descriptions (artwork_details)
• **Artwork Details and Descriptions** (artwork_details, text, optional): Describe your current or recent artwork.

### Artwork Image or Sample (artwork_image)
• **Artwork Image or Sample** (artwork_image, text, optional): Upload an image or sample of your artwork.

### Social Media Links (social_media_links)
• **Social Media Links** (social_media_links, text, optional): Share your social media links (e.g., Instagram, Facebook, Twitter).

### Additional Comments or Notes (additional_comments)
• **Additional Comments or Notes** (additional_comments, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your participation in the program.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete information.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact us for clarification.
* Please be concise and clear in your responses to ensure easy processing and review.
