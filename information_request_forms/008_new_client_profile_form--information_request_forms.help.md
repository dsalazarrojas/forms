# new_client_profile_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "new_client_profile_form" is a tool for collecting essential details about new clients to ensure we have accurate and up-to-date information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Ensure the client's information doesn't already exist in our system. If it does, please use the update form instead.
2. Fill in the first name, last name, and email of the client.
3. Enter the client's phone number.
4. Provide their date of birth.
5. Enter their address.
6. Specify their country of birth.
7. Enter their nationality.
8. Mention their occupation.
9. Choose their marital status (Active or Inactive).
10. Provide their education background.
11. Indicate their employment status (Active or Inactive).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter the client's first name.
- **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter the client's last name.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the client's email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter the client's phone number.
- **Date of birth** (`date_of`, `date`, required): Enter the client's date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter the client's address.
- **Country of birth** (`country_of_birth`, `text`, required): Enter the country where the client was born.
- **Nationality** (`nationality`, `text`, required): Enter the client's nationality.
- **Occupation** (`occupation`, `text`, required): Enter the client's occupation.
- **Marital status** (`marital_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the client's marital status (Active or Inactive).
- **Education** (`education`, `text`, required): Enter the client's education background.
- **Employment status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the client's employment status (Active or Inactive).

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check the client's information for accuracy before submitting.
- If you're unsure about any field, consult with your team lead or the client directly.
- Remember to update the form only if the client's information has changed.
