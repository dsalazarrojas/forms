# Pre Plan Funeral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for pre-planning a funeral. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure that the funeral services are tailored to your preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Provide information for the person who passed away (if applicable).
3. Answer all questions truthfully, as they will help us to provide the best possible service.
4. If you have any special requests or preferences, please let us know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Funeral Planner** (`funeral_planner`, text, required): The name and contact information of the person who will be coordinating the funeral services.
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): The first name of the deceased.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): The last name of the deceased.
- **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, required): The date you would like the funeral services to take place.
- **Time** (`time`, time, required): The time you would like the funeral services to start.
- **Burial Site** (`burial_site`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
  - Church
  - Cemetery
  - Home
- **Other Burial Site** (`burial_site_other`, text, optional): If you have a different burial site in mind, please specify.
- **Casket Style** (`casket_style`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following options:
  - Bronze
  - Wooden
  - Metallic
  - Other
- **Casket Color** (`casket_color`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following options:
  - White
  - Black
  - Brown
  - Other
- **Ebalming** (`embalming`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following options:
  - Yes
  - No
- **Flowers** (`flowers`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following options:
  - Yes
  - No
- **Viewing** (`viewing`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following options:
  - Yes
  - No
- **Visitation** (`visitation`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following options:
  - Yes
  - No
- **Obituary** (`obituary`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following options:
  - Yes
  - No
- **Headstone** (`headstone`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following options:
  - Yes
  - No
- **Headstone Description** (`headstone_description`, text, optional): A brief description of the headstone.
- **Headstone Type** (`headstone_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following options:
  - Flat
  - Monument
  - Gravestone
  - Cremation Urn
- **Cremation** (`cremation`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following options:
  - Yes
  - No
- **Death Certificate** (`death_certificate`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following options:
  - Yes
  - No
- **Death Certificate Note** (`death_certificate_note`, text, optional): Additional information regarding the death certificate.
- **Obituary Description** (`obituary_description`, text, optional): A brief description of the obituary.
- **Obituary Cost** (`obituary_cost`, number, optional): The estimated cost of the obituary.
- **Final Expense** (`final_expense`, text, optional): Additional expenses to be incurred after the funeral services.
- **Additional Expense** (`additional_expense`, text, optional): Any other expenses related to the funeral services.

Please contact us if you have any questions or concerns regarding this form.
