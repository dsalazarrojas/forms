# Case Management Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Case Management Form is designed to collect and store comprehensive case information for court proceedings. This form helps court personnel track and manage cases efficiently, ensuring accurate and complete data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by reviewing the form's pages**, each page is a separate section of the form that requires specific information.
2. **Fill out the fields as required**, indicated by the "Required" label. Make sure to provide accurate and concise information for each field.
3. **Use the hints provided** for each field to help guide you through the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Case Number**: Enter the unique identifier for the court case (required). This is the number assigned to the case by the court.
* **Filing Date**: Enter the date the case was filed (required). This is the date the case was initiated.
* **Court Information**: Enter any relevant details about the court (optional). This can include the court's address, division, or department.
* **Court Name**: Enter the specific court handling the case (required). This is the name of the court where the case is being heard.
* **Court Division**: Select the type of court (e.g., Civil, Criminal, Family, etc.) (required). This helps the court understand the case's nature.
* **Judge Assigned**: Enter the name of the presiding judge (optional). This is the judge assigned to the case.
* **Case Type**: Select the nature of the case (e.g., Personal injury, Contract dispute, etc.) (required). This helps the court understand the case's purpose.
* **Plaintiff or Petitioner Name**: Enter the name of the plaintiff or petitioner (required). This is the party filing the case.
* **Defendant or Respondent Name**: Enter the name of the defendant or respondent (required). This is the opposing party in the case.
* **Plaintiff Attorney Name**: Enter the name of the attorney representing the plaintiff (optional). This is the counsel for the plaintiff.
* **Defendant Attorney Name**: Enter the name of the attorney representing the defendant (optional). This is the counsel for the defendant.
* **Key Documents Filed**: Select the pleadings and motions filed in the case (optional). This includes documents like complaints, answers, and motions.
* **Discovery Deadline**: Enter the end of discovery date (optional). This is the deadline for discovery in the case.
* **Next Hearing Date**: Enter the scheduled hearing date (optional). This is the date of the next hearing.
* **Type of Hearing**: Select the purpose of the hearing (e.g., Initial conference, Motion hearing, etc.) (optional). This helps the court understand the hearing's purpose.
* **Trial Date**: Enter the trial date (optional). This is the date the case is set to be tried.
* **Statute of Limitations Deadline**: Enter the filing deadline (optional). This is the date by which the case must be filed.
* **Case Strategy Notes**: Enter any strategic considerations for the case (optional). This can include risk assessments and other relevant information.
* **Risks Assessment**: Enter any potential issues in the case (optional). This can include risks and challenges.
* **Additional Case Notes**: Enter any other relevant information about the case (optional). This can include additional details not covered in other fields.
* **Last Updated Date**: Enter the most recent update date (required). This is the date the form was last updated.
* **Updated By**: Enter the person who made the last update (required). This is the person who updated the form.
