# Property Innovation Hub Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Property Innovation Hub Contact Form is a tool that allows property owners or managers to provide information about their properties and inquires about innovation opportunities related to their properties. This form helps the Property Innovation Hub understand the properties' specifics and offer tailored support and resources.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Provide your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone number.
5. Choose the type of property (Apartment, House, or Other).
6. Enter your Property Address.
7. Enter a brief message describing your property and inquiry.
8. Select your preferred contact method (Phone Call, Email, or Mail).
9. Confirm whether you want to receive follow-up on your inquiry.
10. If applicable, select the innovation focus areas relevant to your property (Energy Efficiency, Water Conservation, or Sustainability).
11. If applicable, choose the date and time you prefer for follow-up.
12. Enter the Property City.
13. Enter the Property State.
14. Enter the Property Zip.
15. Enter the Property Country.
16. Enter the Property Website (if available).
17. (Optional) Enter any additional property notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Please enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to be contacted by the Property Innovation Hub team.
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property you're enquiring about (Apartment, House, or Other).
* **Property Address**: Enter your property's exact address.
* **Message**: Enter a brief description of your property and inquiry.
* **Contact Method**: Choose how you'd like to be contacted (Phone Call, Email, or Mail).
* **Follow Up**: Confirm whether you want to receive a follow-up on your inquiry.
* **Innovation Focus**: Select the areas of innovation relevant to your property (Energy Efficiency, Water Conservation, or Sustainability).
* **Date**: Enter the date you'd like to receive follow-up on your inquiry.
* **Time**: Enter the time you'd like to receive follow-up on your inquiry.
* **Location**: Enter your preferred location for follow-up.
* **Property Manager**: Enter your property manager's name (if applicable).
* **Property Owner**: Enter your name (if you're the property owner, or the property owner's name if you're a manager).
* **Property Contact**: Enter the person to contact at the property.
* **Property Address 2**: Enter any additional property address information.
* **Property City**: Enter the city of your property.
* **Property State**: Enter the state of your property.
* **Property Zip**: Enter the zip code of your property.
* **Property Country**: Enter the country of your property.
* **Property Website**: Enter your property's website (if available).
* **Property Notes**: Add any additional notes about your property.
