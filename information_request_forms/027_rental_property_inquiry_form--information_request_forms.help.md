# Rental Property Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rental Property Inquiry Form is designed to collect essential information about a rental property. This form helps the rental agent or property manager to gather details about the property, including its type, location, amenities, and rental terms. The form is used to provide an efficient and accurate way of communicating with clients who are interested in renting a property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Rental Property Inquiry Form, follow these steps:

1. Select the rental property type by choosing from the available options.
2. Provide the location of the property.
3. Specify the number of bedrooms in the property.
4. Select the amenities offered by the property.
5. Choose the preferred move-in date for the property.
6. Read and agree to the terms and conditions.
7. Select the rental agent's information.
8. Choose the client's information.
9. Select the property type details.
10. Provide a brief description of the property.
11. Enter the location details of the property.
12. Specify the terms and conditions of the rental.
13. Select the property area in square feet or acres.
14. Choose the price range of the property.
15. Enter the rent amount.
16. Select the property status.
17. Enter the rental terms.
18. Enter the rental agent's comments.
19. Enter the client's comments (optional).
20. Enter the client's email address (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rental Property Type (rental_property_type)**: Select the type of property you are inquiring about, either "Yes" or "No".
* **Location (location)**: Enter the location of the property.
* **Number of Bedrooms (number_of_bedrooms)**: Enter the number of bedrooms in the property.
* **Amenities (amenities)**: Select the amenities offered by the property, either "Yes" or "No".
* **Preferred Move-in Date (preferred_movein_date)**: Choose the desired move-in date for the property.
* **Terms and Conditions (terms_and_conditions)**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the rental.
* **Rental Agent Information (rental_agent_info)**: Select the rental agent's information, including their name, number, email, or phone number.
* **Client Information (client_info)**: Choose the client's information, including their name, email, or phone number.
* **Property Type (property_type)**: Select the type of property you are inquiring about, either "Yes" or "No".
* **Preferred City (preferred_city)**: Select the preferred city for the property.
* **Property Status (property_status)**: Select the status of the property, including "Available", "Lease Option", "Option to Rent", or "Under Contract".
* **Property Type Details (property_type_details)**: Enter a brief description of the property type.
* **Property Description (property_description)**: Provide a brief description of the property.
* **Location Details (location_details)**: Enter the location details of the property.
* **Terms and Conditions (terms_and_conditions)**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Property Area (property_area)**: Enter the area of the property in square feet or acres.
* **Property Price Range (property_price_range)**: Select the price range of the property.
* **Property Rent (property_rent)**: Enter the rent amount.
* **Property Status (property_status)**: Select the status of the property.
* **Rental Terms (rental_terms)**: Enter the terms and conditions of the rental.
* **Rental Agent Comments (rental_agent_comments)**: Enter the rental agent's comments about the property.
* **Client Comments (client_comments)**: Enter any comments or additional information from the client (optional).
* **Client Email (client_email)**: Enter the client's email address (optional).
