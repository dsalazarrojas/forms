# guest_communication_portal_access_request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from guests who wish to access the guest communication portal. It is used to assess and process their requests for access.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the guest's contact information, including their first and last name, email, phone number, and address.
2. Specify the purpose of their request for access.
3. Choose the duration of access required.
4. If necessary, provide additional comments.
5. Choose the type of access required (Full, Limited, or No Access).
6. Enter any additional information about the guest's company or role in the company.
7. Enter a guest portal password for the guest.
8. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Portal Access Request** (`guest_portal_access_request`, `text`, required): Describe the purpose of your request for access to the guest communication portal.
* **First Name** (`contact_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter the guest's first name.
* **Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter the guest's last name.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the guest's email address.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the guest's phone number.
* **Purpose of Request** (`purpose_of_request`, `text`, required): Specify the reason for the guest's request for access.
* **Access Duration** (`access_duration`, `time`, required): Choose the duration of access required.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments about the request.
* **Access Type** (`access_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of access required (Full, Limited, or No Access).
* **Address** (`contact_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the guest's address.
* **City** (`contact_city`, `text`, optional): Enter the guest's city.
* **State** (`contact_state`, `text`, optional): Enter the guest's state.
* **Zip** (`contact_zip`, `text`, optional): Enter the guest's zip code.
* **Country** (`contact_country`, `text`, optional): Enter the guest's country.
* **Date of Birth** (`contact_date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter the guest's date of birth.
* **Gender** (`contact_gender`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the guest's gender (Male, Female, or Other).
* **Language** (`contact_language`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the guest's language (English, Spanish, French, or Other).
* **Company** (`contact_company`, `text`, optional): Enter the guest's company or affiliation.
* **Position** (`contact_position`, `text`, optional): Enter the guest's position in the company.
* **Department** (`contact_department`, `text`, optional): Enter the guest's department in the company.
* **Manager** (`contact_manager`, `text`, optional): Enter the guest's manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (`contact_manager_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the guest's manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`contact_manager_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the guest's manager's phone number.
* **Additional Comments 2** (`additional_comments_2`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments about the request.
* **Guest Portal Password** (`portal_password`, `text`, required): Enter the guest portal password for the guest.
