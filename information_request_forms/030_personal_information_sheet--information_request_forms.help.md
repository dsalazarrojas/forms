# personal_information_sheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The personal information sheet is a document used to collect and store essential information about an individual and their dependents. This form is designed to ensure accuracy and completeness of personal data for various purposes, including insurance claims and administrative tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by reviewing the form carefully to ensure you understand each field's purpose.
2. Enter your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
3. Provide your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter your address and phone number accurately.
5. Enter your email address, if applicable.
6. Enter your occupation and insurance company information, if applicable.
7. Enter your policy number and coverage start and end dates, if applicable.
8. List any emergency contact names, phone numbers, and relationship, if applicable.
9. If applicable, select the dependents for whom you are responsible.
10. Enter the name and date of birth of each dependent, if applicable.
