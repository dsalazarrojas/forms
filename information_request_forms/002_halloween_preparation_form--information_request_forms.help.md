# Halloween Preparation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Halloween Preparation Form is designed to collect information about Halloween events. This form will gather details about the event, such as dates, times, locations, and contact information for attendees.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the date of the holiday event in the **Holiday Event Date** field.
2.  Enter the contact email in the **Contact Email** field.
3.  Provide a brief description of the business in the **Business Description** field.
4.  Enter the contact phone number in the **Contact Phone** field.
5.  Specify the event location in the **Event Location** field.
6.  Enter the start and end times of the event in the **Start Time** and **End Time** fields.
7.  Provide additional event details in the **Event Details** field.
8.  Specify the expected attendance in the **Expected Attendance** field.
9.  Enter the duration of the event in the **Event Duration** field.
10. Enter the contact first name in the **Contact First Name** field.
11. Enter the contact last name in the **Contact Last Name** field.
12. Enter the name of the business in the **Business Name** field.
13. Enter the business address in the **Business Address** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Holiday Event Date**: Enter the date of the holiday event.
*   **Contact Email**: Enter the contact email of the event organizers.
*   **Business Description**: Provide a brief description of the business hosting the event.
*   **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the event organizers.
*   **Event Location**: Specify the location of the event.
*   **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the event.
*   **End Time**: Enter the end time of the event.
*   **Event Details**: Provide additional details about the event.
*   **Expected Attendance**: Specify the expected number of attendees.
*   **Event Duration**: Enter the duration of the event.
*   **Contact First Name**: Enter the first name of the contact person.
*   **Contact Last Name**: Enter the last name of the contact person.
*   **Business Name**: Enter the name of the business hosting the event.
*   **Business Address**: Enter the address of the business hosting the event.
