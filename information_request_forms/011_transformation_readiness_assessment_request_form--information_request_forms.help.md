# Transformation Readiness Assessment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help organizations assess their readiness for a transformation and request guidance and support from a transformation expert.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Enter your organization's name in the first field.
2.  Select all the objectives that apply to your transformation, such as "Digitalization" or "Cloud Migration".
3.  If applicable, select a date for the transformation to take place.
4.  Provide details about the transformation in the fourth field.
5.  Enter the name and contact information of the consultant or expert who will assist with the transformation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **organization_name** (`organization_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your organization.
*   **objectives** (`objectives`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select all the objectives that apply to your transformation, such as "Digitalization" or "Cloud Migration".
*   **timing** (`timing`, `date`, required: false): If applicable, select a date for the transformation to take place. Note: This field is optional.
*   **transformation_details** (`transformation_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide details about the transformation, such as the goals and expected outcomes.
*   **consultant_contact** (`consultant_contact`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the consultant or expert who will assist with the transformation.

## Tips

*   Please note that this form is a request for guidance and support, not a comprehensive transformation plan.
*   The form is meant to be a starting point for discussion and consultation with a transformation expert.
*   Be as detailed as possible when providing transformation details to help the consultant understand your needs better.
*   If you have any questions or need further clarification, do not hesitate to contact us.
