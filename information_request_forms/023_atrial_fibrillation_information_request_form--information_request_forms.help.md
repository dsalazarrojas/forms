# Atrial Fibrillation Information Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Atrial Fibrillation Information Request Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in learning more about atrial fibrillation. This form will help us understand your needs and preferences for receiving information about the condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number (if you have one).
2. Indicate your relationship to atrial fibrillation (e.g., patient, family member, caregiver, or healthcare provider).
3. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the question of whether you have atrial fibrillation and provide the date of diagnosis (if applicable).
4. Select the type of atrial fibrillation you have (if diagnosed).
5. Choose the topics of interest to you, such as symptoms, treatment options, or lifestyle management.
6. Specify any specific questions you have.
7. Select your preferred format for receiving information (e.g., email newsletter, printed materials, or web resources).
8. Choose your preferred language for materials.
9. If applicable, list your current treatment and medications.
10. Indicate any other health conditions you may have.
11. Confirm that you consent to follow-up communications from us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`First Name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `email`, required): Enter your primary email address for receiving information and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, optional): Provide your phone number for optional communication.
* **Mailing Address** (`Mailing Address`, `text`, optional): Enter your mailing address if you prefer to receive printed materials.
* **City** (`City`, `text`, optional): Enter your city for address purposes.
* **State/Province** (`State/Province`, `text`, optional): Enter your state or province for address purposes.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (`ZIP/Postal Code`, `text`, optional): Enter your zip or postal code for address purposes.
* **Atrial Fibrillation Status** (`Atrial Fibrillation Status`, `note`, optional): This is a note section for you to describe your status.
* **Do you have Atrial Fibrillation?** (`Do you have Atrial Fibrillation?`, `select_one`, required): Answer "Yes", "No", or "Suspect I might".
* **Diagnosis Date** (`Diagnosis Date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your diagnosis (if applicable).
* **Relationship to AFib** (`Relationship to AFib`, `select_one`, required): Choose your relationship to atrial fibrillation (e.g., patient, family member, caregiver, or healthcare provider).
* **AFib Type (if diagnosed)** (`AFib Type (if diagnosed)`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of atrial fibrillation you have (if diagnosed).
* **Topics of Interest** (`Topics of Interest`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the topics you are interested in, such as symptoms, treatment options, or lifestyle management.
* **Specific Questions** (`Specific Questions`, `text`, optional): Enter any specific questions or concerns you have.
* **Preferred Information Format** (`Preferred Information Format`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your preferred format for receiving information, such as email newsletter or web resources.
* **Preferred Language** (`Preferred Language`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred language for materials (e.g., English, Spanish, or French).
* **Current AFib Treatment** (`Current AFib Treatment`, `text`, optional): Enter your current treatment for atrial fibrillation (if applicable).
* **Current Medications** (`Current Medications`, `text`, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Other Health Conditions** (`Other Health Conditions`, `text`, optional): Enter any other health conditions you may have.
* **May we contact you with additional resources?** (`May we contact you with additional resources?`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you consent to follow-up communications from us.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and accurately to help us provide relevant information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your healthcare provider or a trusted medical professional.
* This form is not intended for emergency use. If you are experiencing symptoms of atrial fibrillation or other medical emergencies, please call 911 or your healthcare provider immediately.
