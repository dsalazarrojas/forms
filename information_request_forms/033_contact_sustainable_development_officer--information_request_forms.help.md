# Contact Sustainable Development Officer - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about your sustainability initiatives and goals, allowing our team to better understand your needs and provide tailored support. Please fill out the form carefully to help us serve you more effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, start by selecting "Contact Sustainable Development Officer" in the form header. Then, fill in the required fields with your information. For fields with options, select all that apply. Finally, submit the form and our team will review your submission to provide guidance and resources.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Organization Name** (`organization`, text, required): Enter your organization's name.
* **Organization Type** (`organization_type`, select_one, required): Select one of the following types:
	+ Corporation
	+ Non-Profit
	+ Government Agency
	+ Educational Institution
	+ Small Business
	+ Individual
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter your city and country.
* **Topic of Inquiry** (`inquiry_topic`, select_multiple, required): Select all the topics that apply to your sustainability inquiry:
	+ Carbon Reduction
	+ Renewable Energy
	+ Waste Management
	+ Sustainable Supply Chain
	+ Green Building
	+ Water Conservation
	+ Biodiversity
	+ ESG Reporting
* **Project Stage** (`project_stage`, select_one, required): Select the current stage of your project:
	+ Idea Stage
	+ Planning Phase
	+ Implementation
	+ Monitoring and Reporting
	+ Looking to Improve
* **Organization Size** (`organization_size`, select_one, required): Select the number of employees in your organization:
	+ 1-50
	+ 51-200
	+ 201-500
	+ 501-1000
	+ Over 1000
* **Sustainability Goals** (`sustainability_goals`, text, required): Describe your sustainability goals.
* **Timeline for Implementation** (`timeline`, select_one, required): Select when you want to start implementing your sustainability initiatives:
	+ Immediate
	+ 1-3 months
	+ 3-6 months
	+ 6-12 months
	+ Long-term Planning
* **Budget Available** (`budget_available`, select_one, optional): Select your budget range (if applicable):
	+ Limited - Under $50000
	+ Moderate - $50000 to $200000
	+ Substantial - $200000 to $500000
	+ Significant - Over $500000
* **Internal Stakeholder Support** (`stakeholder_support`, select_one, optional): Select the level of internal stakeholder support:
	+ Full Executive Support
	+ Department Level Support
	+ Building Support
	+ Exploring
* **Current Sustainability Initiatives** (`current_initiatives`, text, optional): Describe any current sustainability initiatives you are undertaking.
* **Main Challenges** (`challenges`, text, optional): Describe the main obstacles you are facing with your sustainability initiatives.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any other information that might be helpful for our team to understand your needs.
