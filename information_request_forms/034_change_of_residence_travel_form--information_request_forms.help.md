# Change Of Residence Travel Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Change Of Residence Travel Form is used to gather essential information for an employee's change of residence. This form is used to update an employee's address, phone number, and email address in the organization's system.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps:

1. Fill in the "Form Date" field with the current date.
2. Provide your "Full Name".
3. Enter your "Employee ID" or "Reference Number" if applicable.
4. Enter your "Email Address".
5. Enter your "Phone Number".
6. Describe your "Current Residence Address".
7. Describe your "New Residence Address".
8. Select "Yes" or "No" if you are changing cities.
9. Select "Yes" or "No" if you are relocating internationally.
10. Enter the "Move Effective Date".
11. Select the reason for your "Relocation" from the list.
12. Provide your "Travel Dates" if applicable.
13. Enter "Temporary Address During Move" if applicable.
14. Select "Yes" or "No" if you want to request mail forwarding.
15. Select the duration for mail forwarding.
16. Select "Yes" or "No" to update all contact information.
17. Enter any special arrangements if needed.
18. Enter your new phone number (if changing).
19. Enter your new email address (if changing).
20. Confirm that the provided information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Date**: Date of submission.
* **Full Name**: Legal name.
* **Employee ID or Reference Number** (optional): Enter if applicable.
* **Email Address**: Personal email.
* **Phone Number**: Contact phone.
* **Current Residence Address**: Where you currently live.
* **New Residence Address**: Where you will be moving.
* **City Changing**: Are you changing cities.
* **Country Changing**: Are you relocating internationally.
* **Move Effective Date**: When the move takes effect.
* **Reason for Relocation**: Select from the list:
	+ Job Transfer
	+ Personal Preference
	+ Family Relocation
	+ Educational
	+ Retirement
	+ Other
* **Travel Dates**: When you will be traveling.
* **Temporary Address During Move** (optional): If applicable, address while in transit.
* **Request Mail Forwarding**: Select "Yes" or "No" if you want to forward mail to new address.
* **Mail Forwarding Duration**: Select from the list:
	+ 3 Months
	+ 6 Months
	+ 1 Year
	+ Permanent
* **Update All Contact Information**: Select "Yes" or "No" to update in all systems.
* **Special Arrangements Needed** (optional): Any special requests.
* **New Phone Number (if changing)** (optional): Your new phone number.
* **New Email Address (if changing)** (optional): Your new email.
* **I Confirm This Information is Correct**: Select "Yes, Confirmed" or "No" to verify accuracy.

## Tips

* Please be accurate when filling this form.
* Make sure to select the correct options for each field.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to your supervisor or HR department for further clarification.
* This form is not a duplicate or copy of any other form, and it's a unique form for change of residence.
