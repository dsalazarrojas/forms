# social_media_profile_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The social media profile information form is designed to gather information about your social media profiles across various platforms.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Enter your profile name in the "Profile Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. If applicable, enter the date you joined your social media profile in the "Date Joined (SM)" field.
5. Enter the URLs of your social media profile pictures in the "Profile Photo URL", "Profile Picture URL", and other platform-specific profile picture fields.
6. Enter the URLs of your social media profile links in the "Profile URL", "Profile LinkedIn URL", and other platform-specific profile link fields.
7. Add any additional information about yourself or your organization in the "About" field.
8. Select the platforms for which you have a social media profile from the "Social Media Platforms" dropdown.
9. If you have a Twitter profile, enter your Twitter username in the "Twitter Username" field.
10. If you have an Instagram profile, enter your Instagram username in the "Instagram Username" field.
11. If you have a YouTube profile, enter your YouTube username in the "YouTube Username" field.
12. If you have a Facebook profile, enter your Facebook username in the "Facebook Username" field.
13. If you have a TikTok profile, enter your TikTok username in the "Tiktok Username" field.
14. Select the type of profile you have in the "Profile Type" dropdown.
15. Finally, enter any additional information about your social media profile in the "Profile Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Profile Name** (`profile_name`, `text`, *): Enter your social media profile name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, *): Enter your email address.
* **Date Joined (SM)** (`date_joined_sm`, `date`, optional): If you joined your social media profile on a specific date, enter it here.
* **Profile Photo URL** (`profile_photo_url`, `text`, *): Enter the URL of your social media profile picture.
* **Profile Picture URL** (`profile_picture_url`, `text`, *): Enter the URL of your social media profile picture.
* **Profile URL** (`profile_url`, `text`, *): Enter the URL of your social media profile.
* **About** (`about`, `text`, optional): Add any additional information about yourself or your organization.
* **Bio** (`bio`, `text`, optional): Add any additional information about yourself or your organization.
* **Profile Link** (`profile_link`, `text`, *): Enter the URL of your social media profile.
* **Social Media Platforms** (`social_media_platforms`, `select_multiple`, *): Select the social media platforms you have profiles on.
* **Twitter Username** (`twitter_username`, `text`, optional): If you have a Twitter profile, enter your username here.
* **Instagram Username** (`instagram_username`, `text`, optional): If you have an Instagram profile, enter your username here.
* **YouTube Username** (`youtube_username`, `text`, optional): If you have a YouTube profile, enter your username here.

* **Tiktok Username** (`tiktok_username`, `text`, optional): If you have a TikTok profile, enter your username here.
* **Profile Type** (`profile_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of profile you have.
* **Profile Description** (`profile_description`, `text`, optional): Add any additional information about your social media profile.
* **LinkedIn URL** (`linkedin_url`, `text`, optional): If you have a LinkedIn profile, enter the URL here.
* **Profile LinkedIn URL** (`profile_linkedin_url`, `text`, optional): If you have a LinkedIn profile, enter the URL here.
