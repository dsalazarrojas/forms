# Puppy Clicker Training Guide Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Puppy Clicker Training Guide Request Form" is designed to collect essential information from end-users to create a personalized guide for their puppy's clicker training. This form will help us understand your specific needs and preferences for creating a tailored guide that suits your requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the required information. The form consists of multiple fields that cater to different aspects of your clicker training needs. Ensure you answer all the questions accurately to help us provide the best possible guide for your puppy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Puppy Clicker Training Guide Request Form
* **Puppy Clicker Training Guide Request Form** (`<text>`, `text`, required/optional): This field is a title field where you can type in the name of your puppy's clicker training guide request.
* Please enter a brief title for your request to help us identify your specific needs.

### 2. Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`<text>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact information so we can get back to you with updates or questions about your request.
* Please provide your name and any other relevant contact information.

### 3. Contact Email
* **Contact Email** (`<text>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can send you updates about your request.
* Please provide your email address.

### 4. Contact Phone
* **Contact Phone** (`<text>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can get in touch with you quickly.
* Please provide your phone number.

### 5. Contact Name
* **Contact Name** (`<text>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of any questions or updates.
* This field is optional, but please provide the name of the person we should contact.

### 6. Clicker Model
* **Clicker Model** (`<select_one>`, `Select One`, required/optional): Choose the clicker model you're interested in using for your puppy's training.
* Please select one of the available clicker models: Clicker 1, Clicker 2, or Clicker 3.

### 7. Training Level
* **Training Level** (`<number>`, `Number`, required/optional): Specify the level of training your puppy is at.
* Select the training level from the available options or enter a custom value.

### 8. Training Duration
* **Training Duration** (`<text>`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the duration of your training plan.
* Enter the duration of your training plan in text format.

### 9. Follow-up
* **Follow-up** (`<select_multiple>`, `Select Multiple`, required/optional): Check the follow-up options to indicate how you'd like to be contacted about your request.
* Please select one or both options to indicate whether you'd like to be contacted by phone or email.

### 10. Notes
* **Notes** (`<text>`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional notes about your request.
* This field is optional, but please provide any additional information that might be helpful for us to provide the best possible guide for your puppy.

# Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and consistently to ensure we can create a tailored guide for your puppy.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, don't hesitate to contact the system administrator.
* We'll get back to you soon with updates or questions about your request.
