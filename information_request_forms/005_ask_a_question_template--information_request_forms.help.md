# Ask A Question Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This template is a structured form to ask questions and seek assistance from our team. It helps us quickly understand your inquiry and provide relevant responses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

* Enter your name, email address, and a brief summary of your inquiry.
* Choose the relevant department to handle your request.
* Set the priority level of your request.
* Describe your inquiry in detail.
* Choose your preferred response method.
* (Optional) Provide a phone number and a best time for a call.
* (Optional) Provide a reference or order number if related to an existing case.
* Subscribe to our newsletter for updates and news.
* Confirm you are a human by verifying your choice.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`sender_name`, text, required): Enter your full name to help us address you personally.
* **Email Address** (`sender_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address so we can respond to you directly.
* **Company or Organization** (`company_name`, text, optional): If you're representing a company or organization, enter the name here.
* **Subject** (`subject`, text, required): Summarize your question or inquiry briefly.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Choose which department should handle your request:
	+ General Information
	+ Sales
	+ Customer Support
	+ Technical Support
	+ Billing
	+ Human Resources
	+ Management
* **Priority Level** (`priority`, select_one, required): Set the urgency of your request:
	+ Low
	+ Normal
	+ High
	+ Critical
* **Your Message** (`message`, text, required): Provide detailed information about your inquiry.
* **Attachment Reference** (`attachment_reference`, text, optional): Describe any attachments you'll be sending separately.
* **How Should We Respond** (`contact_preference`, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like us to respond:
	+ Email Reply
	+ Phone Call
	+ Either is Fine
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want a call back.
* **Best Time to Call** (`best_time_to_call`, select_one, optional): Choose a time slot for a phone call:
	+ Before 10am
	+ 10am to 12pm
	+ 12pm to 2pm
	+ 2pm to 5pm
	+ After 5pm
* **Reference or Order Number** (`reference_number`, text, optional): Enter a reference or order number if related to an existing case.
* **Subscribe to Newsletter** (`newsletter_signup`, select_one, optional): Choose to receive updates and news:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Verification** (`captcha_verification`, select_one, required): Confirm you're not a robot by selecting:
	+ I am human
	+ Other
* **Date Submitted** (`submit_date`, date, required): The date you're submitting this request, which will be today's date.
