# Building Access Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Building Access Authorization Form is used to request access to various areas of the building, such as main entrance, restricted areas, server room, executive offices, lab facilities, storage areas, and parking garage. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth processing of your request.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2.  Ensure that you have not already been authorized for access and that your request is not a duplicate.
3.  If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
4.  Provide all necessary documentation and supporting materials as requested.
5.  Submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full name** (Full name): Please enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID or other official documents.
*   **Employee ID or identification number** (Employee ID or identification number): Enter your employee ID or identification number as it appears on your employee ID card.
*   **Date of birth** (Date of birth): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Email address** (Email address): Enter your official email address that you use for work-related communication.
*   **Phone number** (Phone number): Enter your phone number that you use for work-related communication.
*   **Department** (Department): Select your department or work area from the list provided.
*   **Position title** (Position title): Enter your job title or position.
*   **Direct manager name** (Direct manager name): Enter the name of your direct manager or supervisor.
*   **Employment start date** (Employment start date): Enter your employment start date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Employment type** (Employment type): Select your employment type from the list provided (Full-time, Part-time, Contractor, Temporary, or Consultant).
*   **Type of access required** (Type of access required): Select the specific areas you need access to from the list provided (Main entrance, Restricted areas, Server room, Executive offices, Lab facilities, Storage areas, or Parking garage).
*   **Access level** (Access level): Select the level of access you need (Basic - Public areas only, Standard - Work area access, Extended - Multiple departments, Administrative - All areas except secure, or Full - All areas).
*   **Purpose of access** (Purpose of access): Briefly describe the reason for your access request.
*   **Duration of access needed** (Duration of access needed): Select the duration of access you require (Permanent, Temporary - specify end date, 30 days, 60 days, or 90 days).
*   **Access end date** (Access end date): If your access is temporary, enter the date when your access will expire.
*   **Badge type needed** (Badge type needed): Select the type of badge you need (Photo ID badge, Key card, Biometric access, Combination code, or Other).
*   **Existing access cards or badges to return** (Existing access cards or badges to return): Indicate if you have any existing access cards or badges that you need to return.
*   **Completed security training** (Completed security training): Select if you have completed the security training program.
*   **Agreement acknowledgment** (Agreement acknowledgment): Confirm that you understand the building security policies.
*   **Background check completed** (Background check completed): Indicate if you have completed the background check.
