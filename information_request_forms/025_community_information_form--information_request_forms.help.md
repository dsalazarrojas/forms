# Community Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Information Form is designed to gather information from individuals or groups requesting information from the community. This form helps ensure that the information is delivered efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester name**: Enter your full name or the name of the group requesting information.
* **Organization (Optional)**: If you are requesting information on behalf of a group, enter the name of the organization.
* **Contact email**: Enter your email address for sending the requested information.
* **Phone number (Optional)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Information requested**: Describe the information you need in a clear and concise manner.
* **Preferred format for response**: Choose how you would like to receive the response (e.g., Email document, Printed copy, Phone call, or In person meeting).
* **Urgency level (Optional)**: Indicate how soon you need the information (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Additional details**: Provide any context that may help locate the information.
* **Consent to store request details**: Check "True" if you consent to store your request details for contact and information purposes.
