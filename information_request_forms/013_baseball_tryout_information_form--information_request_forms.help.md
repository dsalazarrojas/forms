# Baseball Tryout Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from players who are seeking to try out for a baseball team or program. The form aims to gather data about the player's personal details, baseball background, and aspirations to help the tryout process.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Enter your date of birth.
3. Enter your height in inches.
4. Enter your weight in pounds.
5. Answer questions about your baseball background, including your primary and secondary positions, years playing baseball, and club team experience.
6. Indicate your throwing hand, batting stance, arm strength, and speed rating.
7. Answer questions about your academic information, including your current school name and grade level.
8. Finally, answer questions about your aspirations, including your collegiate goals and interest in professional baseball.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: Your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of birth**: Your date of birth, including month, day, and year.
* **Height (inches)**: Your height, measured in inches.
* **Weight (pounds)**: Your weight, measured in pounds.
* **Email address**: Your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone number**: Your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Current school name**: Your current school's name.
* **Grade level**: Your current grade level (Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, Senior, etc.).
* **Current GPA**: Your current grade point average (if applicable).
* **Primary position**: Your primary position on the field (Pitcher, Catcher, First Base, etc.).
* **Secondary position**: Your secondary position on the field (Pitcher, Catcher, First Base, etc.). This is optional.
* **Batting average**: Your batting average (if applicable).
* **Years playing baseball**: The number of years you have played baseball.
* **Club team experience**: Any club team experience you have (if applicable).
* **Throwing hand**: Your throwing hand (Right, Left, Ambidextrous).
* **Batting stance**: Your batting stance (Right, Left, Switch).
* **Arm strength rating**: Your arm strength rating, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Speed rating**: Your speed rating, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Collegiate goals**: Your goals for playing baseball in college (if applicable).
* **Interested in professional baseball**: Are you interested in playing professional baseball? (True, False, Maybe).

Note: Please answer all questions honestly and accurately. If you are unsure about any of the questions, please provide your best estimate or answer based on your understanding of the information being requested.
