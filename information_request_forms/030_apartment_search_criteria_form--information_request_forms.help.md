# Apartment Search Criteria Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Apartment Search Criteria Form is a tool to help you find your ideal apartment by specifying your search criteria.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal details and preferences.
2. Be as specific as possible to get accurate results.
3. Review and edit your answers before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Renter name** (`renter_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
* **Email address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Date of apartment search** (`search_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are looking to move in.
* **Minimum monthly budget** (`rental_budget_min`, number, required): Enter the minimum budget you are willing to spend per month.
* **Maximum monthly budget** (`rental_budget_max`, number, required): Enter the maximum budget you are willing to spend per month.
* **Preferred location or neighborhood** (`preferred_location`, text, required): Enter the neighborhood or location where you would like to live.
* **Location flexibility** (`location_flexibility`, select one, required): Choose how flexible you are with location:
	+ Must be in specified area
	+ Flexible but prefer area
	+ Very flexible on location
* **Maximum acceptable commute time** (`commute_time_acceptable`, number, optional): Enter the maximum time you are willing to spend commuting.
* **Minimum bedrooms** (`bedrooms_min`, number, required): Enter the minimum number of bedrooms you need.
* **Preferred number of bedrooms** (`bedrooms_preferred`, number, required): Enter the preferred number of bedrooms you prefer.
* **Minimum bathrooms** (`bathrooms_min`, number, required): Enter the minimum number of bathrooms you need.
* **Preferred number of bathrooms** (`bathrooms_preferred`, number, required): Enter the preferred number of bathrooms you prefer.
* **Minimum square footage** (`square_footage_min`, number, optional): Enter the minimum square footage you are looking for.
* **Preferred square footage** (`square_footage_preferred`, number, optional): Enter the preferred square footage you prefer.
* **Desired move-in date** (`move_in_date`, date, required): Enter the date you would like to move in.
* **Lease term preference** (`lease_term_preference`, select one, required): Choose your lease term preference:
	+ Month-to-month
	+ 3-6 months
	+ 1 year
	+ 2 years
	+ 3+ years
	+ Flexible
* **Furnished preference** (`furnished_preference`, select one, required): Choose your preference for furnished apartments:
	+ Must be furnished
	+ Prefer furnished
	+ Unfurnished preferred
	+ No preference
* **Number of occupants** (`number_of_occupants`, number, required): Enter the number of people occupying the apartment.
* **Pet requirements** (`pet_requirements`, select one, required): Choose your pet requirements:
	+ No pets
	+ Cats allowed
	+ Dogs allowed
	+ Any pets allowed
	+ Must allow pets
* **Parking requirement** (`parking_requirement`, select one, required): Choose your parking requirement:
	+ Parking essential
	+ Prefer parking
	+ Not necessary
* **Preference for utilities** (`utilities_inclusion`, select one, required): Choose your preference for utilities:
	+ Must be included
	+ Prefer included
	+ Separate bills acceptable
	+ No preference
* **Amenities desired** (`amenities_desired`, select multiple, optional): Choose the amenities you would like to have:
	+ Gym or fitness
	+ Pool
	+ Laundry in unit
	+ Laundry facilities
	+ Elevator
	+ Balcony or patio
	+ Hardwood floors
	+ Modern appliances
	+ Air conditioning
	+ Heating system
	+ Security system
	+ None required
* **Neighborhood priorities** (`neighborhood_priorities`, select multiple, optional): Choose the neighborhood priorities you value:
	+ Safe area
	+ Quiet area
	+ Young professional scene
	+ Family-friendly
	+ Close to shops
	+ Close to restaurants
	+ Good schools nearby
	+ Public transportation
	+ Walkable area
* **Must-have features** (`must_have_features`, text, optional): Enter any non-negotiable features you must have.
* **Nice-to-have features** (`nice_to_have_features`, text, optional): Enter any features you would prefer but are not essential.
* **Deal-breaker issues or conditions** (`deal_breakers`, text, optional): Enter any issues or conditions that would disqualify a property.

**Tips**

* Be as specific as possible when filling out the form to get the most accurate results.
* Review and edit your answers before submission to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact our team for support.
