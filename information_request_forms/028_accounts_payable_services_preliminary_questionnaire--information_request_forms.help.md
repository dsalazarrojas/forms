# Accounts Payable Services Preliminary Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather preliminary information from clients about their accounts payable (AP) services needs, to help the AP team provide a clear proposal for the services they require.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each section carefully and provide the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Organization Name**: Please enter the name of your organization.
* **Average Monthly Invoices**: Please estimate the approximate number of invoices processed by your organization.
* **Current AP Process**: Please select the level of automation in your current AP process. You can choose from:
	+ Manual / Paper-based
	+ Partially Automated
	+ Fully Automated
* **Software Compatibility Needs**: If you have any software systems that you need to integrate with, please select them. You can select all that apply:
	+ QuickBooks
	+ NetSuite
	+ SAP
	+ Xero
	+ Dynamics 365
* **Approval Workflow Complexity**: Please describe the number of steps in your typical approval process. You can choose from:
	+ Simple - 1 step
	+ Moderate - 2-3 steps
	+ Complex - 4 or more steps
* **Number of Active Vendors**: Please select the number of active vendors you have. You can choose from:
	+ 1-50
	+ 51-200
	+ 201-500
	+ 500 or more
* **AP Contact Email**: Please enter the email address of your main point of contact for AP matters.
* **AP Contact Phone**: (Optional) Please enter the phone number of your main point of contact for AP matters.
* **Key Pain Points in Current Process**: Please describe the biggest challenges you face in your current AP process. This is an optional field, but it will help us better understand your needs.
* **Services Interested In**: Please select all the services you are interested in. You can select all that apply:
	+ Invoice Scanning
	+ Data Entry
	+ Approval Management
	+ Payment Execution
	+ Vendor Onboarding

## Tips
* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately, as it will help us provide a better understanding of your AP needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
