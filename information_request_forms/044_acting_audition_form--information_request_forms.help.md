# Acting Audition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from actors who are interested in auditioning for a role. It is intended to gather details about the actor's background, preferences, and availability for the audition or shoot. By providing accurate and complete information, you can increase your chances of being considered for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a clear understanding of the role you are auditioning for.
2. Fill out the form with your information, making sure to provide accurate details about yourself.
3. If you have any relevant attachments (such as a headshot and resume), make sure to include the links or upload them accordingly.
4. Review your information carefully before submitting the form to ensure it is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (Full name): Enter your legal or stage name as you would like it to be seen in the credits.
* **Contact email** (Contact email): Enter your email address where you would like to receive communication from the casting director.
* **Phone number** (Phone number): Enter your mobile or best contact number for communication.
* **Age range** (Age range): Enter your age or the age range you fit into for the role.
* **Height and build** (Height and build): Enter a brief description of your height and general build.
* **Vocal type** (Vocal type): If you are a singer, enter the type of singing voice you have (e.g., soprano, alto).
* **Special skills** (Special skills): Enter any special skills you have that may be relevant to the role (e.g., stage combat, dialects).
* **Availability dates** (Availability dates): Enter the specific dates you are available for the audition or shoot.
* **Headshot and resume link** (Headshot and resume link): If you have a professional headshot and resume, enter the URL to where they can be accessed.
* **Audition role preferences** (Audition role preferences): Select the types of roles you are most suited for (e.g., lead, supporting, ensemble).
* **Consent for recording** (Consent for recording): Check the box indicating your permission to record your audition performance.
* **Additional notes** (Additional notes): Enter any other relevant information about yourself that is not covered by the other fields.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the casting director for clarification.
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form, as it will be used to consider you for the role.
