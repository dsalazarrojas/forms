# Dental Narrative Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dental narrative submission form is a tool to collect information from patients and doctors for dental submissions. It helps to gather necessary details for better dental care and collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your name as the patient.
2. Write a detailed narrative of your medical history, medical condition, or any other relevant information that may be helpful for your dental care.
3. Enter your address.
4. Provide your phone number.
5. Enter your email (if available).
6. Enter your date of birth (if applicable).
7. Enter your doctor's name.
8. Enter your doctor's phone number.
9. Enter your doctor's title.
10. Enter your doctor's address (if applicable).
11. Enter your patient's employer (if applicable).
12. Enter your doctor's department (if applicable).
13. Enter your work phone number (if applicable).
14. Enter your doctor's specialty (if applicable).
15. Enter any other relevant information.
16. Enter your doctor's license (if applicable).
17. Enter your medical condition (if applicable).
18. Enter your doctor's degree (if applicable).
19. Enter your medical history (if applicable).
20. Enter your doctor's insurance information (if applicable).
21. Enter an additional medical history (if applicable).
22. Enter another specialty of your doctor (if applicable).
23. Enter the name and relationship of the emergency contact.
24. Enter additional insurance information (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (required): Please enter your full name as the patient.
* **Narrative** (required): Write a detailed narrative of your medical history, condition, or any other relevant information for your dental care.
* **Patient Address**: Please enter your address for correspondence and billing purposes.
* **Patient Phone** (required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Patient Email** (optional): If you have an email address, please enter it for convenient communication.
* **Date of Birth** (optional): If applicable, please enter your date of birth.
* **Doctor Name** (required): Enter your doctor's name.
* **Doctor Phone** (required): Enter your doctor's phone number.
* **Doctor Title** (required): Enter your doctor's title.
* **Doctor Address** (optional): If applicable, enter your doctor's address.
* **Patient Employer** (optional): If applicable, enter your employer's name and address.
* **Doctor Department** (optional): If applicable, enter your doctor's department.
* **Work Phone** (optional): If applicable, enter your work phone number.
* **Doctor Specialty** (optional): If applicable, enter your doctor's specialty.
* **Other Info** (optional): Enter any additional relevant information.
* **Doctor License** (optional): If applicable, enter your doctor's license number.
* **Medical Condition** (optional): Enter your medical condition.
* **Doctor Degree** (optional): If applicable, enter your doctor's degree.
* **Medical History** (optional): Enter your medical history.
* **Doctor Insurance** (optional): If applicable, enter your doctor's insurance information.
* **Medical History 2** (optional): Enter any additional medical history.
* **Doctor Specialty 2** (optional): If applicable, enter your doctor's specialty.
* **Emergency Contact** (optional): If applicable, enter the name and relationship of the emergency contact.
* **Doctor Insurance 2** (optional): If applicable, enter your doctor's additional insurance information.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (optional): If applicable, enter the relationship of the emergency contact.

Note: Please fill out the form carefully, and if you are unsure about any field, please contact our office for guidance.
