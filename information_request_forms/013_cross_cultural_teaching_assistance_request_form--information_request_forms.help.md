# Cross Cultural Teaching Assistance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request support or assistance for cross-cultural teaching.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request form**: Enter a brief description of your request.
* **name** (Required): Enter your name.
* **Contact email** (Required): Enter your email address.
* **Contact phone**: Enter your contact phone number (optional).
* **Course details**: Provide a brief description of the course you are teaching.
* **Teaching location**: Mention the location where you will be teaching.
* **Teaching language**: Mention the language of instruction.
* **Teaching level**: Describe the level of your students.
* **Teaching subjects**: Select the subjects you need assistance with.
* **Class size**: Estimate the size of your class.
* **Teaching format**: Choose the format of your teaching.
* **Teaching duration**: Specify the duration of the teaching session.
* **Additional comments**: Add any additional comments or requests.
* **What type of support would you like to request?**: Choose the type of support you need.
* **Which subjects would you like to be assisted with?**: Select the subjects you need assistance with.
* **Request date** (Required): Set the date of your request.
* **Request time** (Required): Set the time of your request.
