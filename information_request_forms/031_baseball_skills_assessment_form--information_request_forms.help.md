# Baseball Skills Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the baseball skills of a player, providing a comprehensive picture of their overall ability and identifying areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the player's full name in the "Player Full Name" field.
2. Enter the player's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. If applicable, enter the player's current team name in the "Team Name" field.
4. Enter the date of the assessment in the "Assessment Date" field.
5. Enter the coach or evaluator's name in the "Evaluator Name" field.
6. Select the player's primary position in the "Primary Position" field.
7. Select the player's secondary positions in the "Secondary Positions" field.
8. Select the player's dominant hand for batting and throwing in the "Bats" and "Throws" fields.
9. Rate the player's hitting technique, estimated batting average, power hitting ability, plate discipline, baserunning speed, baserunning technique, fielding ability, throwing accuracy, throwing strength, pitching velocity, pitching control, game situation awareness, teamwork attitude, coachability, and overall skill rating using the provided options.
10. List the player's key strengths in the "Player Strengths" field.
11. List the areas for improvement in the "Areas for Improvement" field.
12. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Full Name**: Enter the player's full name as it is written on their ID, jersey, or record book. This is a required field.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the player's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This is a required field.
* **Team Name**: If the player is on a team, enter the name of the team they are currently playing for. This is an optional field.
* **Assessment Date**: Enter the date of the assessment in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This is a required field.
* **Evaluator Name**: Enter the name of the coach or evaluator completing the form. This is a required field.
* **Primary Position**: Select the player's primary position on the field, such as Pitcher, Catcher, First base, etc. This is a required field.
* **Secondary Positions**: Select all additional positions the player plays, such as Pitcher, Catcher, First base, etc. This is an optional field.
* **Bats**: Select the player's dominant hand for batting, either Right, Left, or Switch. This is a required field.
* **Throws**: Select the player's dominant hand for throwing, either Right, Left, or Switch. This is a required field.
* **Hitting Technique**: Rate the player's hitting technique, from Excellent to Needs work. This is a required field.
* **Estimated Batting Average**: Rate the player's estimated batting average, from Above 0.400 to Below 0.200. This is a required field.
* **Power Hitting Ability**: Rate the player's power hitting ability, from Excellent to Needs work. This is a required field.
* **Plate Discipline**: Rate the player's plate discipline, from Excellent to Needs work. This is a required field.
* **Baserunning Speed**: Rate the player's baserunning speed, from Excellent to Needs work. This is a required field.
* **Baserunning Technique**: Rate the player's baserunning technique, from Excellent to Needs work. This is a required field.
* **Fielding Ability**: Rate the player's fielding ability, from Excellent to Needs work. This is a required field.
* **Throwing Accuracy**: Rate the player's throwing accuracy, from Excellent to Needs work. This is a required field.
* **Throwing Strength**: Rate the player's throwing strength, from Excellent to Needs work. This is a required field.
* **Pitching Velocity**: Rate the player's pitching velocity, from Above 85 mph to Below 55 mph. This is a required field.
* **Pitching Control**: Rate the player's pitching control, from Excellent to Needs work. This is a required field.
* **Game Situation Awareness**: Rate the player's game situation awareness, from Excellent to Needs work. This is a required field.
* **Teamwork and Attitude**: Rate the player's teamwork and attitude, from Excellent to Needs work. This is a required field.
* **Coachability**: Rate the player's coachability, from Excellent to Needs work. This is a required field.
* **Overall Skill Rating**: Rate the player's overall skill rating, from Elite player to Beginner. This is a required field.
* **Player Strengths**: List the player's key strengths, such as speed, agility, or strong fielding skills. This is a required field.
* **Areas for Improvement**: List the player's areas for improvement, such as needing to work on their plate discipline. This is a required field.
* **Recommended Position**: If you have any thoughts on the player's best fit position, enter them here. This is an optional field.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the player. This is an optional field.
