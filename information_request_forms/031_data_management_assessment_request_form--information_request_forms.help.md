# Data Management Assessment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for data management assessment requests. It's essential to complete it accurately to ensure that the necessary details are provided.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the **Form Title** field with the title of your data management request.
2. Provide a brief description of the **Data Challenge** you are facing.
3. Mention your **Goals** for the data management request.
4. Identify your **IT Contact** and **Consulting Contact**.
5. Complete your **Form Submitter** information.
6. If necessary, select the **Submission Date** for the request.
7. Provide a **Form Description**.
8. Choose the **Form Status**, **Form Owner**, **Data Management Team**, **Project Status**, **Project Manager**, **Project Sponsor**, and **Priority** according to your request's specifics.
9. Add any **Submission Comments** or **Data Management Plan** and **Data Management Approach** that apply.
10. If needed, enter the **Data Management Schedule** and **Form Last Updated** dates.
11. Select the **Review Status**, add **Review Comments**, and choose the **Review Date** for review completion.
12. Finally, enter the **Submitted By**, and **Submitted Date** for the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required: false): A brief title of the data management request.
* **Data Challenge** (`data_challenge`, text, required: false): A description of the data management challenge.
* **Goals** (`goal`, text, required: false): A brief description of the goals for the data management request.
* **IT Contact** (`it_contact`, text, required: false): The contact information for the IT department.
* **Consulting Contact** (`consulting_contact`, text, required: false): The contact information for the consulting team.
* **Form Submitter** (`form_submitter`, text, required: false): Information about who submitted the form.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required: false): The date of submission for the request.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, required: false): A description of the request.
* **Form Status** (`form_status`, select_one, required: false): Choose the status of the form from Active, In Progress, Closed.
* **Form Owner** (`form_owner`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose one or more form owners from the provided options.
* **Data Management Team** (`data_management_team`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the data management team involved.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the project status from the available options.
* **Project Manager** (`project_manager`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more project managers involved.
* **Project Sponsor** (`project_sponsor`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose one or more project sponsors from the options.
* **Priority** (`priority`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the level of priority for the request from Low, High, Medium.
* **Submission Comments** (`submission_comments`, select_multiple, required: false): Select any submission comments.
* **Data Management Plan** (`data_management_plan`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose a data management plan that applies.
* **Data Management Approach** (`data_management_approach`, select_multiple, required: false): Select a data management approach that applies.
* **Data Management Schedule** (`data_management_schedule`, date, required: false): Enter the data management schedule dates.
* **Form Review Status** (`form_review_status`, text, required: false): Choose the review status of the form.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, text, required: false): Add any review comments.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, required: false): Choose the date of review completion.
* **Form Last Updated** (`form_last_updated`, date, required: false): The last update date of the form.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required: false): Information about who submitted the request.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, required: false): The date the request was submitted.
