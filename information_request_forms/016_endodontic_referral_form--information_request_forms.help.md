# endodontic_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The endodontic referral form is designed to collect information about a patient's condition and the referral process for endodontic treatment. This form is meant to be completed by a general dentist or a medical professional to refer a patient to an endodontist.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you are authorized to use this form.
2.  Fill in the relevant details about the patient.
3.  Ensure you have the necessary consent from the patient before completing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Info**: This field is used to gather information about the patient. Enter the patient's identifying details such as their name, date of birth, and contact information.
- **Patient Name**: This field is used to capture the patient's name. Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification document.
- **Dentist Info**: This field is used to gather information about the patient's dental condition. Describe the patient's condition in as much detail as possible.
- **Dentist Name**: This field is used to capture the name and contact information of the dentist referring the patient.
- **Patient Disease**: This field is used to describe the patient's disease or condition.
- **Patient History**: This field is used to gather information about the patient's medical or dental history. Describe any relevant medical conditions, surgeries, or previous treatments.
- **Dentist Signature**: This field is used to capture the signature of the referring dentist.
- **Endodontist Name**: This field is used to capture the name and contact information of the endodontist being referred to.

## Tips

*   Please ensure all fields are completed as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
*   This form is for internal use only, please do not share outside of your organization.
*   Please ensure you have the necessary consent from the patient before referring them.
