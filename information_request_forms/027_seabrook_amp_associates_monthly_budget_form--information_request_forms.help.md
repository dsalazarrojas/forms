# Seabrook & Associates Monthly Budget Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seabrook & Associates Monthly Budget Form is used to provide information about your monthly income and expenses. This data is essential for the organization to make informed decisions and create accurate budgets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your monthly income.
2. Fill in your total monthly expenses.
3. Break down your monthly expenses into categories such as rent, utilities, food, transportation, and miscellaneous expenses.
4. Calculate your average savings rate (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Monthly Income**: Enter your total monthly income from all sources.
* **Total Expenses**: Enter your total monthly expenses.
* **Monthly Expenses (Rent)**: Enter your monthly rent or housing costs.
* **Monthly Expenses (Utilities)**: Enter your monthly utility bills (e.g., electricity, water, gas).
* **Monthly Expenses (Food)**: Enter your monthly food expenses (e.g., groceries, dining out).
* **Monthly Expenses (Transportation)**: Enter your monthly transportation costs (e.g., car loan, fuel, parking).
* **Monthly Expenses (Miscellaneous)**: Enter any other monthly expenses not covered in the above categories.
* **Average Savings Rate**: Enter your average savings rate (if applicable).

## Tips
* Ensure that you are filling in accurate information to ensure that the financial data is reliable.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please contact the relevant department for assistance.
