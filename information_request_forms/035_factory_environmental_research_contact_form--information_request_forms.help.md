# factory-environmental-research-contact-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact information and details from visitors to a factory's environmental research department for general information requests.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter a title for your information request in the "Factory Environmental Research Contact Form" text field.
2.  Select the category that best describes your request from the "Category" drop-down menu.
3.  Provide a description of your request in the "Description" text field.
4.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6.  Add any additional notes or comments about your request in the "Note" text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Factory Environmental Research Contact Form** (`form_title`, `text`, required): Enter a title for your information request.
*   **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, optional): Select the category that best describes your request (e.g., Information Request Forms, Manufacturing Forms).
*   **Description** (`description`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of your request.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
*   **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your request.
