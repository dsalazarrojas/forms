# Client Profile Single - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Profile Single form is designed to collect essential information about a client's personal details, contact information, and any additional notes. This information will help us better understand the client's needs and tailor our services accordingly.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Start by scrolling down to the section titled "Personal Information".
2. Enter your **Full name** in the "Full name" field. This is a required field, so be sure to fill it in carefully.
3. Enter your preferred name (if applicable) in the "Preferred name" field.
4. Provide your contact email address in the "Email address" field. This is also a required field.
5. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone number" field.
6. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of birth" field. Please use the YYYY-MM-DD format.
7. If you are affiliated with an organization, enter it in the "Organization or company" field.
8. If applicable, enter your role or title in the "Role or title" field.
9. Provide a brief summary of your primary needs or reason for contact in the "Primary needs" field. This is a required field.
10. Finally, enter any additional notes or relevant details in the "Additional notes" field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (`Full name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents, with your first and last names separated by a space.
* **Preferred name** (`Preferred name`, `text`, optional): Enter any other names by which you would like to be known.
* **Email address** (`Email address`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone number** (`Phone number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number, including the country code if applicable.
* **Date of birth** (`Date of birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Organization or company** (`Organization or company`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your organization or company, if applicable.
* **Role or title** (`Role or title`, `text`, optional): Enter your job title or position.
* **Primary needs** (`Primary needs`, `text`, required): Briefly describe your primary needs or reason for contact.
* **Additional notes** (`Additional notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes or relevant details.
