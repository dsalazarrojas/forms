# Treasury Audit Schedule Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Treasury Audit Schedule Request Form is used to request a treasury audit schedule, which helps to identify and address any discrepancies or errors in financial records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Select the preferred date for the audit.
3. Enter the start and end dates of the audit period.
4. Indicate whether the audit is for a financial or compliance audit.
5. Provide the contact person's name and details.
6. Specify the audit location.
7. Choose your preferred contact method (email, phone, or mail).
8. Attach any necessary documents to support your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Preferred Date**: Select the date you would prefer to conduct the treasury audit. This will help in scheduling the audit accordingly.
  *Required: Optional*

- **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the audit period. This is the date when the audit will commence.
  *Required: True*

- **End Date**: Enter the end date of the audit period. This is the date when the audit will conclude.
  *Required: True*

- **Date Range**: Select 'Yes' if you want to schedule the audit over a specific date range, or 'No' if you want to schedule it on a single date.
  *Required: False*

- **Supporting Documents**: Attach any relevant documents to support your request.
  *Required: False*

- **Audit Details**: Provide a brief description of the audit, including any specific areas you want to focus on during the audit.
  *Required: True*

- **Audit Type**: Select 'Financial' for an audit focused on financial transactions, or 'Compliance' for an audit related to regulatory compliance.
  *Required: True*

- **Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information of the person the auditor should contact during the audit.
  *Required: True*

- **Audit Location**: Specify the location where the audit will take place.
  *Required: True*

- **Preferred Contact Method**: Choose how you want to be contacted during the audit (email, phone, or mail).
  *Required: False*

- **Audit Attachment**: Select the attachment option if you need to attach documents to your request.
  *Required: True*

- **Attachment Documents**: Attach any necessary documents to support your request.
  *Required: False*
