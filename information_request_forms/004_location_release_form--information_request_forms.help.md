# Location Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Location Release Form is used to gather information about a location, its contact details, and the company associated with it. This form is essential for administrative and communication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as needed.
2. Ensure that the "Contact Email" and "Contact Phone" fields are accurate and up-to-date.
3. Make sure the "Location Name" field is correctly labeled as it appears on your location's signage or maps.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Location Name** (`location_name`, Text, optional): Enter the name of the location.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, Text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person associated with the location.
* **Company** (`company`, Text, optional): Enter the name of the company associated with the location.
* **Address** (`address`, Text, optional): Enter the address of the location.
* **City** (`city`, Text, optional): Enter the city of the location.
* **State/Province** (`state_province`, Text, optional): Enter the state or province of the location.
* **Zip/Postal Code** (`zip_postal_code`, Text, optional): Enter the zip or postal code of the location.
* **Country** (`country`, Text, optional): Enter the country of the location.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, Note, optional): Enter any additional notes or agreements related to the location.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, Date, optional): Enter the date the agreement was signed.
* **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, Time, optional): Enter the time the agreement was signed.
* **Date Released** (`date_released`, Date, optional): Enter the date the location was released.
* **Time Released** (`time_released`, Time, optional): Enter the time the location was released.
* **Location Description** (`location_description`, Text, optional): Enter a brief description of the location.
* **Photo Directions** (`photo_directions`, Text, optional): Enter any directions or notes related to photos taken at the location.
* **Photo Credit** (`photo_credit`, Text, optional): Enter the credit or attribution for any photos taken at the location.
* **Release Terms** (`release_terms`, Text, optional): Enter any release terms or conditions related to the location.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, Email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person for the company.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, Text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person for the company.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, Text, optional): Enter the name of the company associated with the location.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, Text, optional): Enter the address of the company associated with the location.
* **Company City** (`company_city`, Text, optional): Enter the city of the company associated with the location.
* **Company State/Province** (`company_state_province`, Text, optional): Enter the state or province of the company associated with the location.

Note: All fields are optional unless specified as required.
