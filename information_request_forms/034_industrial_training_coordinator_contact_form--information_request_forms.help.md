# Industrial Training Coordinator Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from end users seeking industrial training. It will help the industrial training coordinator to respond to their inquiries and provide the necessary support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your inquiry details in the "Inquiry Details" field.
2. Select the type of training you are looking for from the options provided in "What type of training are you looking for".
3. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Details", "Email Address", and "Phone Number" fields.
4. If applicable, choose a date and time for the training in "Date and Time".
5. Add any additional notes about your inquiry or training needs in the "Notes" field.
6. Finally, click "Submit" to send your information to the industrial training coordinator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inquiry Details**: Enter a brief description of your inquiry or the specific details of your training needs.
* **What type of training are you looking for**: Select the type of training that best suits your needs, such as training for HR, educators, or providers.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your name and title (if applicable) to ensure the industrial training coordinator can get in touch with you.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address to allow the industrial training coordinator to communicate with you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number to allow the industrial training coordinator to contact you for further discussion.
* **Date and Time**: Choose a date and time for the training to ensure it fits your schedule.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information or comments about your inquiry or training needs.
* **Submit**: Click "Submit" to send your information to the industrial training coordinator.
