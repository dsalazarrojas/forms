# property_clean_energy_data_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a data collection tool for requesting clean energy data from building owners and property managers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Property Details section with your property's information.
2. Select the type of energy sources your property uses (e.g. Electricity, Gas, Solar).
3. Indicate your property's energy usage level (e.g. Low, Medium, High).
4. Identify your property type (e.g. Commercial, Industrial, Residential).
5. Choose your energy consumption (e.g. number of units).
6. Provide your contact information so we can get back in touch with you.
7. (Optional) Add a note if you have any additional information about your property.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Details**: Provide a brief description of your property, including its address and name.
* **Clean Energy Data**: Select all the types of clean energy your property uses.
* **Energy Usage**: Choose your energy usage level based on your property's energy consumption:
	+ Low: Your property uses less energy compared to similar properties in the area.
	+ Medium: Your property uses an average amount of energy compared to similar properties in the area.
	+ High: Your property uses more energy compared to similar properties in the area.
* **Building Type**: Choose the type of your property:
	+ Commercial: Your property is a business or office building.
	+ Industrial: Your property is a factory or production site.
	+ Residential: Your property is a private home or rental building.
* **Energy Sources**: Select all the types of energy sources your property uses (e.g. Solar, Wind, Coal).
* **Energy Consumption**: Enter the number of units your property consumes.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your email address so we can get back in touch with you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Address**: Choose the type of address your property has:
	+ Street: Your property is a street address.
	+ Building: Your property is a building number.
	+ Apartment: Your property is an apartment number.
* **Note**: Add any additional information about your property if needed.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have the relevant authority to fill out this form.
* If you are not sure which type of energy sources your property uses, select all that apply.
* If you have any additional information about your property, use the Note field.
