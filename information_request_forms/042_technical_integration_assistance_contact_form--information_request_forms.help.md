# technical_integration_assistance_contact_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The technical integration assistance contact form is designed to collect information from users who need technical integration assistance. This form helps our team to better understand your integration requirements and provide the necessary support to resolve your issues.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the integration type that best describes your issue.
2.  Choose the priority level of your request.
3.  Select the technical contact person or team that will handle your request.
4.  Attach any relevant files or screenshots to help us understand your issue.
5.  Provide a detailed description of your issue.
6.  If applicable, provide the integration URL, SaaS provider URL, and SaaS version URL.
7.  Fill in the additional comments section with any additional information that you think might be relevant to your request.
