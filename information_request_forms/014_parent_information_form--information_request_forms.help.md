# Parent Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parent Information Form is designed to gather essential information from the parents or guardians of a student. This form is used to collect details about the student's parent/guardian, including contact information, relationship, and any relevant medical conditions. The form is typically used for administrative or educational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **First Name** field with the parent's/guardian's first name.
2. Enter the **Last Name** field with the parent's/guardian's last name.
3. Provide the **Email** field with the parent's/guardian's email address.
4. Enter the **Phone Number** field with the parent's/guardian's phone number.
5. Fill out the **Birth Date** field with the student's birth date.
6. Select the **Relationship to Student** field with the correct relationship (Student, Parent, or Guardian).
7. Enter the **Address** field with the parent's/guardian's address (if applicable).
8. Fill out the **Additional Comments** field with any additional information about the student.
9. Enter the **School Name** field with the name of the school the student attends.
10. Select the **School Grade** field with the student's current grade level (Elementary School, High School, or Middle School).
11. Fill out the **Teacher Name** field with the student's teacher's name.
12. Select the **Parent Permission Level** field with the correct permission level (Parent Permission Level 1, 2, or 3).
13. Enter the **Emergency Contact Name** field with the emergency contact's name.
14. Select the **Emergency Contact Relationship** field with the relationship of the emergency contact (Mother, Father, Brother, Sister, Grandparent, or Other).
15. Enter the **Emergency Contact Phone** field with the emergency contact's phone number.
16. Provide the **Emergency Contact Email** field with the emergency contact's email address.
17. Fill out the **Medical Conditions** field with any relevant medical conditions.
18. Enter the **Medical Allergies** field with any allergies or medical conditions.
19. Fill out the **Medical Equipment** field with any necessary medical equipment.
20. Enter the **Medical Important Notes** field with any important medical notes.
21. Fill out the **Other Notes** field with any additional information about the student.
22. Select the **Consent Form** field with your consent level (True or False).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter the parent's/guardian's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter the parent's/guardian's last name.
* **Email**: Enter the parent's/guardian's email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the parent's/guardian's phone number.
* **Birth Date**: Fill out the student's birth date in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Relationship to Student**: Select the correct relationship to the student (Student, Parent, or Guardian).
* **Address**: Enter the parent's/guardian's address (if applicable).
* **Additional Comments**: Fill out any additional information about the student.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school the student attends.
* **School Grade**: Select the student's current grade level (Elementary School, High School, or Middle School).
* **Teacher Name**: Enter the student's teacher's name.

* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Select the relationship of the emergency contact (Mother, Father, Brother, Sister, Grandparent, or Other).
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the emergency contact's email address.
* **Medical Conditions**: Fill out any relevant medical conditions.
