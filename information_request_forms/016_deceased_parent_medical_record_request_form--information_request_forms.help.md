# Deceased Parent Medical Record Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to request medical records for a deceased parent. It's intended for individuals who want to access their parent's medical history, which may be necessary for various reasons such as estate planning, funeral arrangements, or other purposes. **Please note that this form is not intended for general public access. Only authorized individuals, such as family members or executors of the estate, can request medical records.**

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Deceased Parent's Name**: Enter the name of the deceased parent as it appears on their identification documents or birth certificate.
2. **Date of Birth**: Enter the deceased parent's date of birth in the format of Day (MM/DD/YYYY), Month, and Year (DD, MM, YYYY).
3. **Relationship to Requester**: Select the relationship between you and the deceased parent:
	* Requester is the deceased parent
	* Requester is a child of the deceased
	* Requester is the grandchild of the deceased
	* Requester is a relative
	* Requester is a friend (Please specify the type of relationship)
	* Requester is the executor of the estate
4. **Request Reason**: Provide a brief explanation for why you need the medical records.
5. **Contact Information**: Enter your name, phone number, and email address (if available) so we can contact you if needed.
6. **Medical Record Requestor Information**: Enter any additional information about your request, such as specific medical conditions or concerns.
7. **Signature**: Sign the form to confirm that you're the authorized individual making the request.
8. **Medical Record Requestor Agreement**: Confirm that you've read and agree with the terms of use for accessing the medical records, and that you understand the information will not be used for any other purpose.
9. **Medical Record Requestor Agreement Signed**: Confirm that you've signed the agreement.
10. **Medical Record Requestor Date and Time**: Enter the date and time you're making this request.
11. **Medical Record Requestor Phone, Email, Address, City, State, Postal Code, Country**: Enter your contact information for follow-up purposes.
12. **Medical Record Requestor Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your request.
13. **Medical Record Requestor Agreement Read**: Confirm that you've read the terms of use.
14. **Medical Record Requestor Agreement Signed**: Confirm that you've signed the agreement.
