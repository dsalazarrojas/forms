The purpose of this form is to request an accessibility audit for learning resources. The form is intended for users to submit their learning materials for review to ensure they meet accessibility standards. The form collects information such as the school name, date of request, resources to be audited, and other relevant details about the resources. The form will help ensure that the resources are accessible to all users, particularly those with disabilities.

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the school name where the audit is requested.
2. Select the date when you would like to request the audit.
3. Indicate whether the resources you are submitting are learning resources.
4. Provide any specific accessibility issues with the resources.
5. Enter your contact information, including your name and email address.
6. Select the platform on which the resources are hosted.
7. Enter the name of the course or resource being audited.
8. Choose the assigned tool or software used for the audit.
9. Select the category of the resource (e.g., video, image, document, etc.).
10. Specify who assigned the audit.
11. Enter a brief description of the resource.
12. Select whether the learning objectives are met.
13. Choose the type of audit to be performed (e.g., accessibility, usability, etc.).
14. Enter the name of the file containing the audit results.
15. Specify who the audit is assigned to.
16. Select whether categories A, B, C, D, E are applicable.
17. Enter your name as the person submitting the form.
18. Enter the date when you submitted the form.

## How To Complete This Form

To ensure the accuracy and completeness of your submission, please carefully review each field below and provide the requested information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **school_name**: Enter the name of the school where the audit is requested.
* **request_date**: Enter the date when you would like to request the audit.
* **learning_resources**: Indicate whether the resources you are submitting are learning resources.
* **accessibility_issues**: Provide any specific accessibility issues with the resources.
* **contact_information**: Enter your name and email address for contact purposes.
* **platform**: Select the platform on which the resources are hosted.
* **course_name**: Enter the name of the course or resource being audited.
* **resource_name**: Enter the name of the resource being audited.
* **assigned_tool**: Enter the assigned tool or software used for the audit.
* **category**: Select the category of the resource (e.g., video, image, document, etc.).
* **assigned_by**: Specify who assigned the audit.
* **description**: Enter a brief description of the resource.
* **learning_objectives**: Select whether the learning objectives are met.
* **audit_type**: Choose the type of audit to be performed (e.g., accessibility, usability, etc.).
* **output_file**: Enter the name of the file containing the audit results.
* **assigned_to**: Specify who the audit is assigned to.
* **category_1**: Select whether category A is applicable.
* **category_2**: Select whether categories B and C are applicable.
* **category_3**: Select whether category C is applicable.
* **category_4**: Select whether categories D and E are applicable.
* **category_5**: Select whether category E is applicable.
* **submitted_by**: Enter your name as the person submitting the form.
* **submitted_on**: Enter the date when you submitted the form.
* **submitted_by_name**: Enter your name.
* **submitted_by_email**: Enter your email address.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your answers for any errors before submission.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your administrator for assistance.
