# Travel Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Travel Request Form is designed to collect information about travel requests from employees, including travel dates, destinations, and reasons for travel. This form helps ensure that all travel requests are properly documented and submitted to the correct authorities for approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*) next to the field label.
2. Enter the destination of your travel in the "Destination" field.
3. Select the travel dates using the calendar icon or by typing in the date manually.
4. Choose the travel type from the options provided:
	* Inbound: Travel to a different city or country.
	* Outbound: Travel from one city or country to another.
	* Return: Travel to return to the same city or country.
	* Other: Specify a different travel type.
5. Enter a brief reason for your travel in the "Reason for Travel" field.
6. Add any additional travel details in the "Travel Details" field.
7. If you are traveling with an agency, enter the agency name in the "Agency" field.
8. If you are working with a travel agent, enter their name in the "Agent" field.
9. Enter your name as the "Requestor" of the travel.
10. Enter your agency ID (if applicable) in the "Agency ID" field.
11. Enter your contact email and phone number in the "Contact Email" and "Contact Phone" fields, respectively.
12. Add any additional travel notes in the "Travel Note" field.
13. Specify if this travel request is for business purposes in the "Business Use Case" field.
14. Add any comments about the travel request in the "Comments" field.
15. Select who the form should be submitted to:
	* Travel Agent
	* Travel Manager
16. Select the travel agency that will be handling your travel:
	* Yes
	* No

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Request Form** (Text): This is the title of the form.
* **Destination** (Text): Enter the city or country you are traveling to.
* **Travel Date** (Date): Select the date(s) of your travel using the calendar icon or type in manually.
* **Start Date** (Date): Select the start date of your travel using the calendar icon or type in manually.
* **End Date** (Date): Select the end date of your travel using the calendar icon or type in manually.
* **Travel Type** (Select One): Choose the type of travel from the options provided:
	+ Inbound: Travel to a different city or country.
	+ Outbound: Travel from one city or country to another.
	+ Return: Travel to return to the same city or country.
	+ Other: Specify a different travel type.
* **Reason for Travel** (Text): Enter a brief reason for your travel.
* **Travel Details** (Text): Add any additional travel details.
* **Agency** (Text): If you are traveling with an agency, enter their name here.
* **Agent** (Text): If you are working with a travel agent, enter their name here.
* **Requestor** (Text): Enter your name as the requestor of the travel.
* **Agency ID** (Text): Enter your agency ID (if applicable).
* **Contact Email** (Email): Enter your contact email.
* **Contact Phone** (Text): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Travel Note** (Note): Add any additional travel notes.
* **Business Use Case** (Text): Specify if this travel request is for business purposes.
* **Comments** (Note): Add any comments about the travel request.
* **Submit To** (Select One): Select who the form should be submitted to:
	+ Travel Agent
	+ Travel Manager
* **Assigned Agent** (Select Multiple): Select the travel agent assigned to handle your travel:
	+ Jimmy
	+ Other
* **Assigned Travel Agency** (Select Multiple): Select if the travel agency will be handling your travel:
	+ Yes
	+ No
