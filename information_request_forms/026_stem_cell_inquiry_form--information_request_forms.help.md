# Stem Cell Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for stem cell-related services or treatments.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your **Phone** number and **Contact Number 2** for follow-up purposes.
3. Select your **Department** of interest (Cardiology, Oncology, or Radiology).
4. Choose the **Medical Condition** you are inquiring about (Cancer, Diabetes, Heart Failure, Stroke, or Alzheimer's).
5. Select the **Inquiry Type** (In-person, Phone, Email, or Chat).
6. Provide **Additional Info** about your inquiry, if necessary.
7. Select the **Stem Cell Related Condition** (Stem Cell Therapies, Stem Cell Treatments, Stem Cell Procedures, or Stem Cell Research).
8. Enter the **Clinic Name** where you would like to inquire about.
9. Select the **Stem Cell Type** (Autologous, Allogeneic, or Isologeneic).
10. Select the **Stem Cell Source** (Bone Marrow, Adipose Tissue, or Peripheral Blood).
11. Enter the **Stem Cell Dose**.
12. Provide the **Stem Cell Protocol**.
13. Select **Public Inquiry** (Yes or No) to determine if your inquiry will be shared publicly.
14. Select **Consent Form** (Yes or No) to indicate if you have provided consent.
15. Enter the **Stem Cell Information**.
16. Select the **Stem Cell Protocol Type** (not specified in the YAML but implied).
17. Enter the **Inquiry Date** and **Time** for your inquiry.
18. Select **Submit** to finalize your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **Contact Number 2**: Enter an additional phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Address**: Enter your current address if you want to receive mailings.
* **Department**: Select the department you are inquiring about (Cardiology, Oncology, or Radiology).
* **Medical Condition**: Select the condition you are inquiring about (Cancer, Diabetes, Heart Failure, Stroke, or Alzheimer's).
* **Inquiry Type**: Select the method you would like to receive a response (In-person, Phone, Email, or Chat).
* **Message**: Enter a message about your inquiry, if necessary.
* **Public Inquiry**: Select if you want your inquiry to be shared publicly (Yes or No).
* **Consent Form**: Select if you have provided a consent form (Yes or No).
* **Stem Cell Related Condition**: Select the type of stem cell-related condition you are inquiring about (Stem Cell Therapies, Stem Cell Treatments, Stem Cell Procedures, or Stem Cell Research).
* **Clinic Name**: Enter the name of the clinic you are inquiring about.
* **Contact Number 2**: Enter an additional phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **Inquiry Date**: Enter the date you are inquiring about.
* **Time**: Enter the time you are inquiring about.
* **Stem Cell Type**: Select the type of stem cell (Autologous, Allogeneic, or Isologeneic).
* **Stem Cell Source**: Select the source of the stem cell (Bone Marrow, Adipose Tissue, or Peripheral Blood).
* **Stem Cell Dose**: Enter the dose of the stem cell.
* **Stem Cell Protocol**: Enter the protocol of the stem cell treatment.
* **Stem Cell Information**: Enter any additional information about the stem cell.
* **Stem Cell Protocol Type**: Select the type of protocol (not specified in the YAML).
* **Submit**: Click to finalize and submit your form.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* We will review your form and contact you separately if necessary.
