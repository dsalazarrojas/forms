# Gift Aid Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Gift Aid Declaration Form is used to declare gifts made to charities. This form helps charities understand that the donation is eligible for Gift Aid, a government scheme that allows charities to claim an enhanced rate of relief on certain donations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the Gift Aid Declaration Form Data section with the relevant details about the gift made to the charity.
2. Provide recipient details in the Recipient Details section.
3. Enter donor details in the Donor Details section.
4. Specify the gift value in the Gift Value section.
5. Select the gift type from the options provided (e.g., Donation of goods or services, Gift of money, etc.).
6. If applicable, provide a description of the gift in the Gift Description section.
7. Select the gift value currency (e.g., GBP).
8. If necessary, provide additional gift value details in the Gift Value section.
9. Enter the recipient's aid number in the Recipient Aid Number section.
10. Enter the declaration aid number in the Declaration Aid Number section.
11. Select the nature of the declaration from the options provided (e.g., Taxed at source, Taxed at source and relieved, etc.).
12. If applicable, provide additional declaration details in the Declaration Nature Other section.
13. Finally, provide any additional notes or comments about the declaration in the Declaration Notes section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gift Aid Declaration Form Data** (`gift_aid_declaration_form_data`, text, required: false): This section is for entering general information about the gift made to the charity. Enter the relevant details about the gift in this section.
* **Recipient Details** (`recipient_details`, text, required: false): Enter the details of the recipient of the gift, such as their name, address, and contact information.
* **Donor Details** (`donor_details`, text, required: false): Enter the details of the donor who made the gift, such as their name, address, and contact information.
* **Gift Aid Declaration Form Date** (`gift_aid_declaration_form_date`, date, required: false): This is the date the gift was made. Enter the date in the correct format (DD/MM/YY).
* **Gift Aid Declaration Form Time** (`gift_aid_declaration_form_time`, time, required: false): This is the time the gift was made. Enter the time in the correct format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Gift Value** (`gift_value`, number, required: false): Enter the value of the gift made to the charity.
* **Gift Type** (`gift_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of gift made, such as Donation of goods or services, Gift of money, etc.
* **Gift Description** (`gift_description`, select_multiple, required: false): Provide a description of the gift made, if necessary.
* **Gift Value Currency** (`gift_value_currency`, select_one, required: false): Select the currency of the gift value.
* **Gift Value** (`gift_value`, text, required: false): If necessary, provide additional details about the gift value.
* **Recipient Aid Number** (`recipient_aid_number`, text, required: false): Enter the recipient's aid number, if applicable.
* **Declaration Aid Number** (`declaration_aid_declaration_form_date`, date, required: false): Enter the declaration aid number, if applicable.
* **Declaration Aid Declaration Form Nature** (`declaration_aid_declaration_form_nature`, select_one, required: false): Select the nature of the declaration, such as Taxed at source, Taxed at source and relieved, etc.
* **Donor Aid Number** (`donor_aid_number`, text, required: false): Enter the donor's aid number, if applicable.
* **Declaration Aid Declaration Form Nature Other** (`declaration_aid_declaration_form_nature_other`, text, required: false): If the declaration nature is other, provide a description of the nature in this section.
* **Declaration Aid Declaration Form Value** (`declaration_aid_declaration_form_value`, text, required: false): If necessary, provide additional declaration value details.
* **Gift Aid Declaration Form Notes** (`gift_aid_declaration_form_notes`, text, required: false): Provide any additional notes or comments about the declaration.
