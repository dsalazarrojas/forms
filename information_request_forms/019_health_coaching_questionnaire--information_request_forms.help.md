# health-coaching-questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who are seeking health coaching services. The goal is to gather data on your health concerns, goals, habits, nutrition, physical activity, and stress levels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form by providing answers to the questions on each page.
2. Make sure to answer each question carefully, as it will help us understand your needs better.
3. You can save your progress and come back to the form later if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Health Coaching Questionnaire** (`Introduction`, required): A brief introduction to the form. Please don't worry about this field, just acknowledge that you're here for health coaching.
* **Health Concerns** (`health concerns`, optional): This field is to list any health concerns you have. You can type as many lines as you need to describe your health issues.
* **Goals** (`goals`, optional): In this field, describe your goals for health coaching. What do you want to achieve? What changes do you want to make?
* **Health Habits** (`health habits`, optional): This section is to describe your daily habits. What do you do regularly? What do you avoid?
* **Nutrition** (`nutrition`, optional): Here, you can list your dietary habits. What do you eat? What do you avoid?
* **Physical Activity** (`physical activity`, optional): Describe your physical activities. What do you do regularly? How often?
* **Stress** (`stress`, optional): How do you manage stress? Do you have any stressors that affect your well-being?

## Tips
* Be honest and detailed in your answers.
* If you're unsure about any question, just do your best and we'll discuss it later.
* You can always save your progress and come back to the form later if needed.
