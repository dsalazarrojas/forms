# Custom Color Match Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your skin type and preferences for a color matching application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following fields:

1. **Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
2. **Email**: Enter your email address to contact you about your application.
3. **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your application.
4. **Skin Type**: Select the type of skin you have from the dropdown menu.
5. **Skin Tone**: Enter your skin tone description.
6. **Freckles**: Enter any freckle-related information you'd like to share.
7. **Have Tan**: Indicate if you have a tan.
8. **Tan Type**: Enter any additional information about your tan.
9. **Tan Location**: Enter the location where you got your tan.
10. **Tan Duration**: Enter the duration of your tan in hours/days/weeks/months/years.
11. **Submit**: Select "Yes" to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address to contact you about your application.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your application.
* **Skin Type** (`skin_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of skin you have from the dropdown menu.
* **Skin Tone** (`skin_tone`, text, required): Enter your skin tone description.
* **Freckles** (`freckles`, text, required): Enter any freckle-related information you'd like to share.
* **Have Tan** (`has_tan`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have a tan.
* **Tan Type** (`tan_type`, text, required): Enter any additional information about your tan.
* **Tan Location** (`tan_location`, text, required): Enter the location where you got your tan.
* **Tan Duration** (`tan_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of your tan in hours/days/weeks/months/years.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to submit your application.

## Tips
Make sure to fill out all required fields and select a valid option for each field to ensure that your application is successful. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
