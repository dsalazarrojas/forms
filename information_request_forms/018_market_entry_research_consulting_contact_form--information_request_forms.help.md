# market_entry_research_consulting_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers who are interested in conducting market entry research consulting with our company. It is intended for customers who are looking to gain a deeper understanding of their market and potential entry points.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form header with a brief description of your project.
2.  Enter the name of your project in the "project_name" field.
3.  Enter the name of the project manager in the "project_manager" field.
4.  Enter the email address of the project manager in the "project_manager_email" field. Make sure to enter a valid email address.
5.  Enter the phone number of the project manager in the "project_manager_phone" field. This is optional, but recommended for quick follow-up.
6.  Enter the department or team of the project manager in the "project_manager_department" field. This is also optional.
7.  Enter your name in the "customer_name" field.
8.  Enter your email address in the "customer_email" field. Make sure to enter a valid email address.
9.  Enter your phone number in the "customer_phone" field. This is optional.
10.  Enter your department or team in the "customer_department" field. This is required.
11.  Fill out the form footer with any additional comments or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header** (1): A brief description of your project.
* **project_name** (2): The name of your project.
* **project_manager** (3): The name of the project manager.
* **project_manager_email** (4): A valid email address of the project manager, required for contact.
* **project_manager_phone** (5): The phone number of the project manager, optional.
* **project_manager_department** (6): The department or team of the project manager, optional.
* **customer_name** (7): Your name, required for our records.
* **customer_email** (8): A valid email address, required for us to contact you.
* **customer_phone** (9): Your phone number, optional.
* **customer_department** (10): The department or team you belong to, required.
* **Form Footer** (11): Any additional comments or information, optional.
