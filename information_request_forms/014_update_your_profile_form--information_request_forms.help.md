# Update Your Profile Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to update your profile information. This includes filling out fields such as first name, last name, email, phone number, date and time of birth, and other details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing each field carefully and making sure you're updating the correct information.
2. Fill out the required fields (if any).
3. Enter your first name in the "First name" field. This field accepts text input.
4. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field. This field accepts text input.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This field accepts email formats.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This field accepts text input.
7. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of birth" field. This field accepts date formats.
8. Enter your time of birth in the "Time of birth" field. This field accepts time formats.
9. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
10. Make selections for "Select one" and "Select multiple" fields as needed.
