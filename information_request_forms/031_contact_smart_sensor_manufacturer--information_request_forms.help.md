# Contact Smart Sensor Manufacturer - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant to provide a convenient way for customers to reach out to the smart sensor manufacturer with their inquiries, questions, or requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your basic contact information, including First Name, Last Name, Company Name, Email Address, and Phone Number.
2. Select the Type of Inquiry that best describes your reason for reaching out to us.
3. Choose the Sensor Type of Interest that you are enquiring about.
4. Specify the Intended Application for the sensor(s).
5. Select the Estimated Quantity Needed.
6. Choose the Project Timeline.
7. (Optional) If you have any specific Technical Requirements, please provide more details.
8. If you have a budget range in mind, select one from the options.
9. If you currently use sensors and looking to switch or look for alternative suppliers, please specify.
10. Finally, provide any Additional Information that you think would be helpful for us to understand your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Smart Sensor Manufacturer Contact** (`inquiry_header`): This is the title of the form that explains its purpose.
* **First Name** (`first_name`): Your first name for us to address you.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`): Your last name for us to address you.
* **Company Name** (`company`): Your organization's name if you represent a company or business.
* **Email Address** (`email`): Your business email address for us to reply to.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`): Your direct contact number for us to reach out.
* **Industry Sector** (`industry`): Select the industry sector that your company or project belongs to.
* **Type of Inquiry** (`inquiry_type`): Select one from the options to describe your reason for contacting us.
* **Sensor Type of Interest** (`sensor_type`): Select all the sensor types that you are enquiring about.
* **Intended Application** (`application`): Describe how you plan to use the sensor(s).
* **Estimated Quantity Needed** (`quantity_needed`): Select the estimated order volume.
* **Project Timeline** (`timeline`): Choose the project timeline that best fits your needs.
* **Technical Requirements** (`technical_requirements`): (Optional) If you have any specific technical requirements, provide more details here.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`): (Optional) If you have a budget range in mind, select one from the options.
* **Do You Currently Use Sensors?** (`current_supplier`): If you currently use sensors and looking to switch or look for alternative suppliers, please specify.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`): Any other details that you think would be helpful for us to understand your needs.

Please fill out this form to send us your inquiry, and we will get back to you as soon as possible.
