# offer_acknowledgment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "offer_acknowledgment_form" is used to provide detailed information about a job offer, including the offer's title, company name, job description, employment dates, salary, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill out the required fields with accurate and detailed information about the job offer.
- Ensure that you have permission to submit the form and that the information provided is valid.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **job_position_title** (`job_position_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the job offer.
* **company** (`company`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the company making the job offer.
* **job_description** (`job_description`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe the job offer, including the job responsibilities and requirements.
* **start_date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of the employment, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **end_date** (`end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of the employment, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **salary** (`salary`, `text`, optional): Enter the salary for the job offer, if applicable.
* **job_title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter the official job title, as it appears in the company's HR system.
* **work_location** (`work_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location where the job will be performed.
* **benefits** (`benefits`, `text`, optional): List any benefits included in the job offer, such as health insurance or retirement plan.
* **contact_person** (`contact_person`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact person's name and title.
* **contact_email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **contact_phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **job_status** (`job_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of the job offer, from the options: active, inactive, or pending.
* **job_status_reason** (`job_status_reason`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief explanation for the job status.
* **notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about the job offer.
* **job_type** (`job_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of job, from the options: full time, part time, or contract.
* **job_category** (`job_category`, `select_one`, optional): Select the category of the job, from the options: IT, Finance, Marketing.
* **job_level** (`job_level`, `select_one`, optional): Select the level of the job, from the options: junior, senior, lead.
* **job_role** (`job_role`, `select_one`, required): Select the role of the job, from the options: manager, team member, team lead.
* **job_education** (`job_education`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the education level required for the job, from the options: high school, college, university.
* **job_experience** (`job_experience`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the level of experience required for the job, from the options: 0-2 years, 2-5 years, 5-10 years.
* **job_seniority** (`job_seniority`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the seniority level of the job, from the options: less than 3 years, 3-10 years, more than 10 years.
* **job_tenure** (`job_tenure`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the tenure of the job, from the options: less than 3 years, 3-5 years, more than 5 years.
* **submission** (`submission`, `select_one`, required): Select whether to submit or decline the job offer.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form with accurate and detailed information about the job offer.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant contact person.
- Ensure that you have permission to submit the form and that the information provided is valid.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are completed correctly.
