# International Pet Travel Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for international pet owners to gather essential information about their pets' travel requirements and preparations before international travel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide general information about your pet, including its species, name, age, and type.
2. Enter owner information, including your name and contact details.
3. Describe your pet's health and any medical conditions or requirements.
4. Share your pet's medical history, including any previous illnesses or treatments.
5. Provide details about your pet's travel plans, including destination, travel dates, and mode of transport.
6. Attach any relevant travel documents, such as health certificates or vaccination records.
7. Describe your pet's accommodations during travel, including food and shelter arrangements.
8. Outline your pet's diet and feeding plans during travel.
9. Sign and date both the owner and pet sections to confirm your commitment to complete the form accurately and truthfully.
