# Lien Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lien Release Form is a document that confirms the release of a lien on a property. This form is used to provide evidence that a lien holder has received payment or satisfaction of their claim, and it is often required for various legal and financial transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the property address in the "property address" field, which is a required field.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the lien holder has received payment or satisfaction of their claim in the "property type" field.
3. Enter the loan amount paid in the "loan amount" field, which is also a required field.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the payment method was cash or another form of payment in the "payment method" field.
5. If a date of payment is relevant, enter it in the "date of payment" field. This field is not required.
6. Enter the date when the lien was placed in the "lien date" field. This field is not required.
7. Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm that the lien holder has signed the form in the "signed by" field.
8. If you are the borrower, enter your signature in the "borrower signature" field. This field is not required.
9. If you are the lien holder, enter your signature in the "lienholder signature" field. This field is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **lienholder**: This field is a note field where you can provide any additional information about the lien holder.
- **property address**: This is the address of the property that is subject to the lien. Enter the property address exactly as it appears on the lien document.
- **property type**: Select "Yes" if the lien holder has received payment or satisfaction of their claim, or "No" otherwise.
- **loan amount**: Enter the amount of the loan that was paid in full.
- **payment method**: Select "Yes" if the payment was made in cash, or "No" if it was made by another method.
- **date of payment**: Enter the date when the payment was made, if applicable.
- **lien date**: Enter the date when the lien was placed on the property, if applicable.
- **signed by**: Select "Yes" to confirm that the lien holder has signed the form, or "No" otherwise.
- **borrower signature**: If you are the borrower, enter your signature here. This field is not required.
- **lienholder signature**: If you are the lien holder, enter your signature here. This field is not required.
