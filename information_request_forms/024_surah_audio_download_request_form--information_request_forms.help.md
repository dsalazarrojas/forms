# Surah Audio Download Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Surah Audio Download Request Form is used to request audio materials from the library for educational purposes. This form helps library staff to understand the user's requirements and preferences for the requested audio materials. By filling out this form, users can provide accurate and detailed information about their needs, ensuring that they receive the correct audio materials for their educational activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **Name** in the "Name" field. This is the primary identifier for your request.
2.  Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field. This is the address where the library will contact you to follow up on your request.
3.  Choose your desired **Audio Type** from the dropdown list.
4.  In the "Purpose" field, describe the reason for your request, including the subject or topic you are researching or teaching.
5.  Enter the name of your **School** in the "School" field.
6.  Provide the name of the **Library** you are requesting from in the "Library" field.
7.  If you have any additional information or comments about your request, enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.
8.  Finally, click the **Submit** button to send your request to the library.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official ID.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications about your request.
* **Audio Type** (select_one, required): Select the type of audio material you are requesting (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Purpose** (text, required): Describe in detail the reason for your request, including the subject or topic you are researching or teaching.
* **School** (text, required): Enter the name of your school or institution.
* **Library** (text, required): Enter the name of the library you are requesting from.
* **Other** (text, required): Enter any other relevant information about your request.
* **Additional Comments** (note, optional): Enter any additional details or comments about your request.
* **Submit** (text, optional): Not applicable, as this is a submit button.
