# Adobe Sales Conference 2020 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Adobe Sales Conference 2020 form, explaining each field's purpose and how to complete it accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (indicated by *) carefully and accurately.
2. For fields with options, select the most relevant choice.
3. If you have any special needs or requests, such as dietary restrictions or accessibility needs, please let us know in the corresponding field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, *): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, *): Enter your last name.
* **Company Name** (3, text, *): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Job Title** (4, text, *): Enter your current role and position.
* **Email Address** (5, email, *): Enter your business email for conference materials.
* **Phone Number** (6, text, *): Enter your contact number during the conference.
* **Business Sector** (7, select_one, *): Select the industry or sector your company operates in.
* **Company Size** (8, select_one, *): Select the number of employees at your organization.
* **Adobe Product Experience** (9, select_multiple, ): Select the Adobe products you currently use.
* **Primary Areas of Interest** (10, select_multiple, *): Select the topics relevant to your role.
* **Confirmed Attendance** (11, select_one, *): Indicate whether you will be attending the conference.
* **Number of Additional Attendees** (12, number, ): Enter the number of colleagues attending with you.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (13, text, ): If you have any special meal requirements, enter them here.
* **Accessibility Needs** (14, text, ): If you need any accommodations, let us know here.
* **How Did You Hear About This Conference** (15, select_one, ): Select how you were led to register for the conference.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (16, text, ): Let us know any other comments or questions you may have.

This form is designed to be clear and easy to understand. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
