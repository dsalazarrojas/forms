# Radiation Dosimetry Information Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Radiation Dosimetry Information Request form is used to collect information about radiation exposure from individuals. This form is intended to be completed by facility staff or individuals who need to report their own exposure.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the facility where you were exposed.
	* **Facility ID**: Enter the ID number of the facility where you were exposed.
	* **Facility Type**: Select the type of facility (hospitals, labs, universities, or safety teams) where you were exposed.
	* **Contact Name**: Enter your name.
	* **Contact Role**: Enter your role in the facility.
	* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
	* **Submitted By Role**: Enter the role of the person who submitted the form.
	* **Submitted At**: Enter the date and time you submitted the form.
2. Provide details about your exposure:
	* **Location of Exposure**: Enter the location where you were exposed.
	* **Radiation Type**: Select the type of radiation you were exposed to (gamma radiation, alpha radiation, beta radiation, or neutron radiation).
	* **Exposure Level**: Enter the level of radiation you were exposed to.
	* **Dose Received**: Enter the dose of radiation you received.
	* **Dose Received Units**: Enter the units of the dose you received.
	* **Date of Exposure**: Enter the date of the exposure.
	* **Time of Exposure**: Enter the time of the exposure.
	* **Medical Condition/Treatment**: Enter any medical conditions or treatments related to your exposure.
	* **Dose Calculations**: Enter any additional comments or calculations related to your exposure.
3. Review and submit the form.
